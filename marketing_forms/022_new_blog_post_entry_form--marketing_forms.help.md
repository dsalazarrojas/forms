# New Blog Post Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Blog Post Entry Form is used to create new blog posts. It captures essential information about the post, including its title, description, tags, author, status, date, time, and image.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the `blog_post_title` field with the title of your new blog post.
2.  Enter the `blog_post_description` in the text field provided.
3.  Select the relevant tags for your blog post from the `blog_post_tags` field.
4.  Enter the author of the blog post in the `blog_post_author` field.
5.  Choose the status of your blog post (Active or Inactive) from the `blog_post_status` field.
6.  Select a date for the blog post from the `blog_post_date` field.
7.  Choose a time for the blog post from the `blog_post_time` field.
8.  Upload or enter the image for the blog post in the `blog_post_image` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Blog Post Title
*   `blog_post_title`: Enter the title of your new blog post.
*   This field is used to identify the title of the blog post.
*   Required: False

### Blog Post Description
*   `blog_post_description`: Enter a brief description of your blog post.
*   This field is used to capture the description of the blog post.
*   Required: False

### Blog Post Tags
*   `blog_post_tags`: Select the tags relevant to your blog post.
*   This field is used to categorize the blog post for ease of reference and searching.
*   Required: False

### Blog Post Author
*   `blog_post_author`: Enter the name of the author of the blog post.
*   This field is used to identify the author of the blog post.
*   Required: False

### Blog Post Status
*   `blog_post_status`: Choose the status of your blog post (Active or Inactive).
*   This field is used to indicate whether the blog post is live or not.
*   Required: False

### Blog Post Date
*   `blog_post_date`: Select the date for your blog post.
*   This field is used to set the date of the blog post.
*   Required: False

### Blog Post Time
*   `blog_post_time`: Select the time for your blog post.
*   This field is used to set the time of the blog post.
*   Required: False

### Blog Post Image
*   `blog_post_image`: Upload or enter the image for your blog post.
*   This field is used to add an image to the blog post.
*   Required: False

## Tips
*   Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly to ensure the form submission is valid.
*   Make sure to select relevant tags for your blog post for better categorization.
*   Choose the correct status and date for your blog post accurately.
*   Upload or enter the correct image for your blog post to display properly.
*   Double-check your entries before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
