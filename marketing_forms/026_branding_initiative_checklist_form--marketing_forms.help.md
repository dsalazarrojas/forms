# Branding Initiative Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Branding Initiative Checklist Form is a tool used to document and track progress on branding-related projects. This form helps project owners and team members to gather information about their projects, set clear objectives, and coordinate with stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Project name** in the first field. This should be a brief description of your branding initiative.
2. In the **Project owner** field, enter the name of the person or team responsible for managing the project.
3. In the **Objectives** field, list the key objectives of your project.
4. In the **Brand assets needed** field, list the logos, fonts, guidelines, and other assets required for your project.
5. In the **Timeline milestones** field, list the key dates and deadlines associated with your project.
6. In the **Approval checkpoints** field, list the stakeholders who need to approve your project.
7. In the **Completed items** field, list the tasks that have been completed so far.
8. In the **Next steps** field, describe the immediate next actions and owners for your project.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Be as specific as possible when listing objectives, brand assets, timeline milestones, and approval checkpoints.
* Use bullet points or numbered lists to organize your responses in the **Objectives**, **Brand assets needed**, **Timeline milestones**, and **Approval checkpoints** fields.
* Review your responses before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
