# Solar Program Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers about their experience with our solar program. The collected data will help us understand customer preferences, needs, and concerns, which will be used to improve our services. Please ensure that you have filled out the form honestly and accurately to provide valuable insights.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your survey details in the text field on **page 1**.
2. Answer the questions listed on **page 2**.
3. Enter your email address in the **email address** field on **page 3**.
4. Provide your customer service contact information, including phone number, email, and available hours on **page 4**.
5. Select the time of day when you would prefer to have customer service available on **page 5**.
6. Add any additional comments or feedback in the **additional comments** field on **page 6**.
7. Finally, write a brief **sales survey** on **page 7** to help us understand your sales experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **survey_details** (Page 1, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your experience with our solar program.
* **customer_survey_questions** (Page 2, text, optional): Answer any additional questions or concerns you have about our solar program.
* **email_address** (Page 3, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can reach out to you.
* **customer_service_number** (Page 4, text, optional): Enter your customer service contact number.
* **customer_service_name** (Page 4, text, optional): Enter your customer service contact name.
* **customer_service_email** (Page 4, email, optional): Enter your customer service contact email.
* **customer_phone** (Page 4, text, optional): Enter your customer service phone number.
* **customer_hours** (Page 5, time, optional): Select the time of day when you would prefer to have customer service available.
* **customer_hours_days** (Page 5, select_one, required): Select the days of the week when you would prefer to have customer service available.
* **additional_comments** (Page 6, text, optional): Add any additional comments or feedback about your experience with our solar program.
* **sales_survey** (Page 7, text, optional): Write a brief description of your sales experience with our solar program.
* **customer_hours_days** has a limited options list of days of the week. Select one or multiple days when customer service is available.
* **customer_hours_days** is a select_one field, meaning you can only select one option.

## Tips
- Please answer the questions honestly and accurately.
- If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to ask us.
- Your feedback is valuable to us, and we will use it to improve our services.
