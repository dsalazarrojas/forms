# Win A Beauty Treatment Contest Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from individuals who are interested in entering a beauty treatment contest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Salon Name** in the first field. This is the name of the salon where you currently receive or have received services from.
2. Enter your **First Name** in the second field. This is your personal name.
3. Enter your **Last Name** in the third field. This is your personal last name.
4. Enter your **Email** in the fourth field. This is your preferred method of contact.
5. Enter your **Phone** in the fifth field. This is your phone number.
6. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the sixth field. This is your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
7. Select all the **Salon Services** you have received or are interested in in the seventh field. You can select multiple services.
8. Choose your **Preferred Contact Method** in the eighth field. You can choose between Email, Phone, or Mail.
9. Finally, enter any additional **Message** you would like to share with us in the ninth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Salon Name**: Enter the name of the salon where you currently receive or have received services from.
* **First Name**: Enter your personal first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your personal last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Salon Services**: Select all the services you have received or are interested in from the provided list.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Choose your preferred method of contact between Email, Phone, or Mail.
* **Message**: Enter any additional information you would like to share with us.
