# Social Media Data Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for our internal team to collect information about your social media presence. 
Please fill it out only if we don't already have your data or if you're a current or past employee of our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the social media platforms you have a presence on.
2. Provide the handles associated with your presence on the selected platforms.
3. Enter your follower count for each platform.
4. Choose the purpose of your social media account.
5. Enter your email (if associated with your account).
6. Enter your phone number.
7. Provide your website (if associated).
8. Add any additional notes or comments about your account.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Social Media Platforms (select multiple)**: This is the list of available social media platforms you can choose from. Select all platforms you have a presence on.
* **Platform Handles (text)**: Enter the handles associated with your presence on the selected platforms (e.g. @username, [handle], etc.).
* **Follower Count (number)**: Enter your number of followers for each platform.
* **Account Purpose (select one)**: Choose the purpose of your social media account (Business, Personal, or Other).
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address associated with your account (if any).
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number associated with your account (if any).
* **Website (text)**: Enter your website associated with your account (if any).
* **Notes (text)**: Add any additional comments or notes about your social media account.
