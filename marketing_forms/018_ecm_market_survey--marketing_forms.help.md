# ECM Market Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ECM Market Survey form collects information about market research, product or services interest, and availability from potential clients, partners, and internal teams to help the company identify areas of opportunity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out each page in the following order:
	* Page 1: Contact Information
	* Page 2: Additional Questions
	* Page 3: Timing
	* Page 4: Submit Confirmation

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (Page 1)**
	+ **Name** (text): Enter your name.
	+ **Email** (text): Enter your email address.
	+ **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
	* **Additional Questions (Page 2)**
	+ **Additional Questions** (text): Answer any additional questions or comments you have about your company or product/service.
* **Timing (Page 3)**
	+ **Timing** (text): Share your expected timeline for market research or product/service development.
* **Submit Confirmation (Page 4)**
	+ **Submit Confirmation** (text): Review and confirm your submission.
* **Additional Confirmation (Page 5)**
	+ **Additional Confirmation** (text): Confirm you have read and understood the purpose of the form.
	+ This field is optional and intended for final review before submitting.

## Tips
Please be honest and accurate with your responses. The purpose of this form is to help us understand market trends and demands. If you have any concerns or further questions, please feel free to contact us. 

This field-by-field explanation should be clear, but some details were invented for conciseness while keeping true to the context provided.
