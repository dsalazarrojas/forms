# Digital Advertising Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential clients for the purpose of scheduling marketing activities such as meetings, phone calls, or demos. The information collected will help us determine the best time and date for the appointment, and assign the booking manager to ensure a smooth and successful appointment.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your client information, including the client's name, company name, and contact number.
2. Provide details about the appointment you would like to schedule, including the date, start and end time, and the duration of the appointment.
3. Choose the booking manager you would like to be assigned to your appointment.
4. Add any additional comments or notes that you would like to share with the booking manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information (client_info)**: This is the first page of the form where you will enter your name and company name.
* **Appointment Details (appointment_details)**: This section collects information about the appointment you would like to schedule. Please fill in the date, start and end time, and the duration of the appointment.
* **Available Time (available_time)**: Enter the available time for the appointment.
* **Appointment Duration (appointment_duration)**: Enter the duration of the appointment in hours.
* **Booking Manager (booking_manager)**: Select the booking manager you would like to be assigned to your appointment.
* **Client Comments (client_comments)**: Add any additional comments or notes about the appointment.
* **Contact Number (contact_number)**: Enter your contact number so we can reach out to you to confirm the appointment.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Be clear and concise in your comments and notes.
* If you are unsure about anything, feel free to ask your booking manager for clarification.
* The booking manager will review and confirm your appointment with you.
