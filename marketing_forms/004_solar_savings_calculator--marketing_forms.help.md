# Solar Savings Calculator - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Solar Savings Calculator form is designed to help users estimate the potential savings from installing solar panels based on their home energy usage and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To get started, simply fill out each field with the required information. 

1. Fill in your **Home Address**.
2. Provide the **Number of Homes** you own.
3. Select your **City**.
4. Enter your **Zip Code**.
5. Choose your **Energy Usage** options.
6. Enter your **Monthly Bill**.
7. Select your **Solar Panels** options.
8. You will not be able to edit or alter the **Estimated Savings** field as it's an automatically generated field.
9. Select the next **Next Steps** to guide you through the process.
10. Choose your **Submit** option to receive your estimated solar savings.
11. (Optional) Enter your **Email** and **Phone Number** for follow-up contact.
12. Choose the next **Follow-up** steps.
13. (Optional) Enter any **Additional Info** you'd like to share.
14. Enter your **Contact Name** for follow-up.
15. Enter your **Contact Phone** for follow-up.
16. Enter the **Email Follow-up** address.
17. Enter the **Lead Source**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Home Address**: Enter your home address as you would like it to appear on your utility bills.
  - **Type:** text
  - **Required:** true
  - **Hint:** Your home address is the physical address where you live.
- **Number of Homes**: Enter the total number of homes you own.
  - **Type:** number
  - **Required:** true
  - **Hint:** This will help us understand your overall energy usage better.
- **City**: Select the city where you live.
  - **Type:** select_one
  - **Required:** true
  - **Hint:** This will help us better understand your local energy market conditions.
- **Zip Code**: Enter your zip code.
  - **Type:** select_one
  - **Required:** true
  - **Hint:** This will help us better understand your local energy market conditions.
- **Energy Usage**: Choose your energy usage options.
  - **Type:** select_multiple
  - **Required:** false
  - **Hint:** This will help us understand how you use energy at home.
- **Monthly Bill**: Enter your estimated monthly energy bill.
  - **Type:** number
  - **Required:** false
  - **Hint:** This will help us understand your average energy consumption.
- **Solar Panels**: Choose your solar panel options.
  - **Type:** select_multiple
  - **Required:** false
  - **Hint:** This will help us understand your interest in solar panels.
- **Estimated Savings**: This field will be automatically generated based on your input data.
  - **Type:** number
  - **Required:** false
  - **Hint:** This is an automatically calculated field and cannot be edited.
- **Next Steps**: Select the next steps to guide you through the process.
  - **Type:** select_one
  - **Required:** false
  - **Hint:** These are suggestions on what to do next.
- **Submit**: Choose your submit option to receive your estimated solar savings.
  - **Type:** select_one
  - **Required:** true
  - **Hint:** This will generate your estimated solar savings report.
- **Email**: Enter your email address for follow-up contact.
  - **Type:** email
  - **Required:** false
  - **Hint:** This is for follow-up contact only.
- **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for follow-up contact.
  - **Type:** text
  - **Required:** false
  - **Hint:** This is for follow-up contact only.
- **Follow-up**: Choose the next follow-up steps.
  - **Type:** select_one
  - **Required:** false
  - **Hint:** These are suggestions on what to do next.
- **Additional Info**: Enter any additional information you'd like to share.
  - **Type:** text
  - **Required:** false
  - **Hint:** This is optional and helps us better understand your needs.
- **Contact Name**: Enter your name for follow-up contact.
  - **Type:** text
  - **Required:** false
  - **Hint:** This is for follow-up contact only.
- **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number for follow-up contact.
  - **Type:** text
  - **Required:** false
  - **Hint:** This is for follow-up contact only.
- **Email Follow-up**: Enter the email address to follow up with.
  - **Type:** text
  - **Required:** false
  - **Hint:** This is for follow-up contact only.
- **Lead Source**: Enter the source of your lead.
  - **Type:** text
  - **Required:** false
  - **Hint:** This helps us understand where you came from.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Be honest about your energy usage to get a more accurate estimated savings.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, contact us using the contact information provided.
