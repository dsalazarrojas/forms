# Community Promotion Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential partners about their interest in promoting their business or organization to our community. Please fill out this form to help us understand your needs and tailor our collaboration accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and detailed information about your contact, business, and promotional interests.
2. Choose the relevant options for your promotion type, goals, and timeline.
3. Be specific about your budget range and preferred channels for promotion.
4. Provide information about any previous promotional efforts and materials you have available.
5. Share any additional comments or questions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (Required)
	+ A brief overview of your contact details, including your name, email, phone number, and organization or business name (if applicable).
* **First Name** (Required)
	+ Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Required)
	+ Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (Required)
	+ Enter your professional email address.
* **Phone Number** (Required)
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Organization or Business** (Optional)
	+ Provide the name of your organization or business (if applicable).
* **Job Title** (Optional)
	+ Enter your job title.
* **Street Address** (Optional)
	+ Enter your street address.
* **City** (Optional)
	+ Enter your city name.
* **State** (Optional)
	+ Enter your state or province name.
* **ZIP Code** (Optional)
	+ Enter your ZIP code.
* **Promotion Information** (Optional)
	+ Briefly describe your interest in promoting your business or organization.
* **Type of Promotion Interest** (Required)
	+ Select one of the following options: Sponsorship, Advertising, Event Partnership, Media Coverage, Social Media Promotion, Community Outreach, or Other.
* **Describe Your Promotion Interest** (Required)
	+ Provide a detailed description of what you want to promote.
* **Target Audience** (Required)
	+ Specify who you're trying to reach with your promotion.
* **Promotion Goals** (Required)
	+ Select all relevant goals from the following options: Brand Awareness, Increase Sales, Community Engagement, Event Attendance, Lead Generation, Customer Loyalty, or Other.
* **Promotion Timeline** (Required)
	+ Choose your preferred timeline: Immediately, Within 1 Month, 1-3 Months, 3-6 Months, or Planning Ahead.
* **Promotion Duration** (Required)
	+ Select the duration of your promotion: One-Time Event, 1-4 Weeks, 1-3 Months, 3-6 Months, 6-12 Months, or Ongoing.
* **Budget Range** (Required)
	+ Choose your budget range: Under $500, $500-$1,000, $1,000-$5,000, $5,000-$10,000, $10,000-$25,000, or Over $25,000.
* **Preferred Promotion Channels** (Optional)
	+ Select all the channels you prefer for your promotion: Social Media, Email Marketing, Print Materials, Website, Events, Local Media, Outdoor Advertising, or Community Newsletter.
* **Have You Done Promotion Before** (Required)
	+ Select 'True' if you've done promotion before, or 'False' otherwise.
* **Previous Promotion Details** (Optional)
	+ Share any information about your previous promotional efforts, what worked or didn't work.
* **Who Are Your Main Competitors** (Optional)
	+ List your main competitors (if applicable).
* **What Makes You Unique** (Optional)
	+ Describe what makes your business or organization unique.
* **Marketing Materials Available** (Required)
	+ Select one of the following options: Yes - Complete Package, Yes - Some Materials, or No - Need Help Creating.
* **Who Approves Marketing Materials** (Required)
	+ Provide the name or title of the person who approves marketing materials.
* **How Will You Measure Success** (Required)
	+ Specify how you'll measure the success of your promotion (e.g., Key Performance Indicators).
* **Additional Comments** (Optional)
	+ Add any additional comments or questions you may have.

**Tips**

* Please ensure all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Be specific and detailed when providing information about your business or organization.
* Select only the relevant options for your promotion type, goals, and timeline.
* If you've done promotional work before, share any relevant information to help us better understand your needs.
