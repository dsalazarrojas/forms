# Customer Engagement Strategy Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to approve or disapprove customer engagement strategies. It collects information about the customer and the engagement strategy, and allows the approver to make a decision and add comments or notes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your company name in the "Company Name" field.
2. Enter the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
3. Enter the customer's email in the "Email" field.
4. Specify the department that the customer interacts with in the "Department" field.
5. Enter the customer's job title in the "Job Title" field.
6. Select the date that the strategy was approved in the "Date Approved" field.
7. Select the time that the strategy was approved in the "Time Approved" field.
8. Choose the person who approved the strategy in the "Approved By" field.
9. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
10. Select whether the strategy was approved or disapproved in the "Submit" field.
11. Add any comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your company.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the customer.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required: false): Specify the department that the customer interacts with.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): Enter the customer's job title.
* **Date Approved** (`date_approved`, date, required: false): Select the date that the strategy was approved.
* **Time Approved** (`time_approved`, time, required: false): Select the time that the strategy was approved.
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`, select_one, required: false): Choose the person who approved the strategy.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_multiple, required: false): Select whether the strategy was approved or disapproved.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Add any comments or feedback about the decision.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that the strategy is properly approved or disapproved.
* Only submit one form per customer engagement strategy.
* Use the "Notes" field to add any additional comments or feedback about the decision.
