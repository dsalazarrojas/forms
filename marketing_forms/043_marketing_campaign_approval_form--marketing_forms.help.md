# Marketing Campaign Approval Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the process of reviewing and approving marketing campaigns. It is meant to be used by marketing teams or managers to gather information and approvals for new marketing campaigns. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out all required fields.
2.  Enter the campaign details, including a brief description or title.
3.  Select the launch date for the campaign.
4.  Choose the current status of the campaign.
5.  Indicate whether the marketing team is involved or not.
6.  Add any review notes or comments for the campaign.
7.  Enter an email address for the review.
8.  Enter a phone number if needed for further communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Campaign details** (`campaign_details`, text, required): Enter a brief description or title of the marketing campaign.
* **Launch Date** (`launch_date`, date, required): Choose the date when the campaign will be launched.
* **Campaign status** (`campaign_status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of the campaign, such as "Approved", "Declined", or "On Hold".
* **Marketing team** (`marketing_team`, select_multiple, optional): Check if the marketing team is involved in the campaign.
* **Review notes** (`review_notes`, note, optional): Add any comments or notes for review.
* **Email to Review** (`email_to_review`, email, optional): Enter the email address for review.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter a phone number if needed for further communication.

## Tips

*  Make sure to fill out all required fields.
*  Be as detailed as possible when filling out campaign details.
*  Select the correct campaign status.
*  If the marketing team is involved, make sure to select all applicable options.
*  Use the review notes field for any comments or notes. 
*  Enter a valid email address for review. 
*  If a phone number is required, make sure to fill it out correctly.
