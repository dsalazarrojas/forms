# Christmas Giveaway Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This Christmas Giveaway Survey is designed to understand your interests and preferences for potential prizes and giveaways. It will help us tailor future promotions to better meet your needs and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name
2. Enter your Last Name
3. Enter your Email Address (make sure it's valid)
4. Choose your Customer Status: Existing customer, New customer, or Interested but not yet purchased
5. Select all that apply under What types of prizes interest you most? (e.g., Electronics, Fashion items, Home goods, etc.)
6. Choose your preferred Prize Value Range (e.g., Under $50, $50-$100, etc.)
7. Select all that apply under What motivates you to enter giveaways? (e.g., Chance to win prizes, Get to know the brand, etc.)
8. Choose how often you enter giveaways: Very frequently, Occasionally, Rarely, or Never
9. Select how you heard about this giveaway: Social media, Email newsletter, Website, etc.
10. If you'd like, add any Additional Comments
11. Finally, choose whether you'd like to receive updates about future promotions and whether you'd participate in future giveaways.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name, as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name, as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted about future promotions and giveaways.
* **Customer Status** (`customer_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you're an Existing customer, New customer, or Interested but not yet purchased.
* **What types of prizes interest you most?** (`prize_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply from the list, e.g., Electronics, Fashion items, Home goods, etc.
* **Preferred Prize Value Range** (`prize_value`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred prize value range (e.g., Under $50, $50-$100, etc.)
* **What motivates you to enter giveaways?** (`participation_reason`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply from the list, e.g., Chance to win prizes, Get to know the brand, etc.
* **How often do you enter giveaways?** (`entry_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Choose how often you participate in giveaways.
* **How did you hear about this giveaway?** (`giveaway_discovery`, `select_one`, required): Select how you found out about this giveaway.
* **What was most appealing about this giveaway?** (`most_appealing_aspect`, `text`, optional): Share your thoughts on what made this giveaway appealing.
* **How could we improve this giveaway?** (`improvement_suggestions`, `text`, optional): Suggest ways we can improve future giveaways.
* **Would you participate in future giveaways?** (`future_giveaways`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you'd like to participate in future giveaways.
* **Would you like to receive updates about future promotions?** (`newsletter_signup`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether you'd like to receive updates about future promotions.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Share any additional thoughts or comments you'd like to make.
