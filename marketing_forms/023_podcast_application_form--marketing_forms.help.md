# podcast_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The podcast application form is designed to collect comprehensive information about your podcast for a marketing or broadcasting purpose. This form is used to submit your podcast for consideration on a specific platform or campaign.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email**.
2. Provide a brief **Description** of your podcast.
3. Choose the relevant **Category** for your podcast.
4. Add the **Podcast Url** and upload your **Audio File** (if applicable).
5. Confirm the **File Type** and **File Size** of your audio file.
6. Choose the **Tool** used to create your podcast.
7. Enter the **Submitted By** information (usually your name or the name of the person submitting on your behalf).
8. Select the **Submitted Time** and **Submitted Date** for the submission.
9. Optionally, provide additional information in the **Category Options** field.
10. Click the **Submit** button to complete the form.
