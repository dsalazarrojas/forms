# lucky_draw_winner_profile_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture information about a lucky draw winner, including personal details, prize information, and draw-specific details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out the form header.
2. Enter the winner's name, email address, and phone number.
3. Select the lucky draw that this winner is associated with.
4. Enter the winner's date of birth and time of win.
5. If applicable, select the tools assigned to the winner.
6. Upload images, videos, or audio files as needed to showcase the winner's content.
7. Enter any messages or notes about the winner.
8. Select the winner status, whether they are a lucky draw winner or not.
9. Enter the lucky draw prizes if applicable.
10. Enter the lucky draw date and time.
11. Enter the lucky draw location.
12. Upload images, videos, or audio files to showcase the lucky draw.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Header**: This section provides a brief description of the lucky draw.
* **Name**: Enter the winner's name.
* **Email**: Enter the winner's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the winner's phone number.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the winner's date of birth.
* **Lucky Draw**: Select the lucky draw that this winner is associated with.
* **Address**: Enter the winner's address.
* **Postal Code**: Enter the winner's postal code.
* **Winner Date**: Enter the winner's date of win.
* **Winner Time**: Enter the winner's time of win.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool assigned to the winner.
* **Winner Image**: Upload an image of the winner.
* **Winner Video**: Upload a video of the winner.
* **Winner Audio**: Upload an audio file about the winner.
* **Winner Message**: Enter a message about the winner.
* **Lucky Draw Winner**: Select whether the winner is a lucky draw winner or not.
* **Lucky Draw Prizes**: Select whether there are prizes for the lucky draw.
* **Lucky Draw Date**: Enter the date of the lucky draw.
* **Lucky Draw Time**: Enter the time of the lucky draw.
* **Lucky Draw Location**: Enter the location of the lucky draw.
* **Lucky Draw Image**: Upload an image of the lucky draw.
* **Lucky Draw Video**: Upload a video of the lucky draw.
* **Lucky Draw Audio**: Upload an audio file about the lucky draw.
* **Lucky Draw Message**: Enter a message about the lucky draw.
* **Lucky Draw Status**: Enter a status about the lucky draw.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate and complete information.
* Use clear and concise language when entering text fields.
* Be careful when uploading files to ensure they are in the correct format.
* Review your entries before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* Take your time to fill out the form carefully, it may take a few minutes to complete.
