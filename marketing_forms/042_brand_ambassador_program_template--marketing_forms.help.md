# Brand Ambassador Program Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Brand Ambassador Program Template is a form designed to help potential brand ambassadors apply for the brand ambassador program. It collects information about the applicant's personal and professional background, including their contact information, location, and social media presence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Be honest and clear in your answers, especially when describing your motivation for joining the program.
3. Make sure to choose the correct options for each field, and do not leave any fields blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Your contact number.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): The city where you are located.
* **State or Country** (`state`, `text`, required): Your state or country.
* **Availability to Represent Brand** (`availability`, `select_one`, required): Choose how soon you can commit to representing the brand:
	+ Immediate
	+ Within 1 month
	+ Within 2-3 months
	+ Within 6 months
	+ Flexible
* **Primary Social Media Platform** (`primary_platform`, `select_one`, required): Select the main platform you use for promotion:
	+ Instagram
	+ YouTube
	+ TikTok
	+ Facebook
	+ Twitter
	+ LinkedIn
	+ Multiple platforms
* **Follower Count** (`follower_count`, `number`, required): The total number of followers on your main platform.
* **Your Audience Demographics** (`audience_demographics`, `text`, required): Describe your age range and interests.
* **Previous Brand Collaboration Experience** (`brand_experience`, `select_one`, required): Choose your level of experience with previous brand collaborations:
	+ Extensive experience
	+ Moderate experience
	+ Limited experience
	+ No experience
* **Content Creation Expertise** (`content_expertise`, `text`, required): Describe the types of content you excel at creating.
* **How You Learned About the Program** (`program_awareness`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you found this opportunity:
	+ Social media advertisement
	+ Word of mouth
	+ Brand website
	+ Influencer network
	+ Search engine
	+ Other
* **Why You Want to Join Our Ambassador Program** (`why_join`, `text`, required): Describe your motivation and passion for joining the program.
* **I submit this brand ambassador application** (`application_submit`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you want to submit your application:
	+ Submit application
	+ Save and continue later
	+ Cancel
