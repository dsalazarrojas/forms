# Loyalty Personalization AI Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Loyalty Personalization AI Registration Form is designed to collect information from businesses interested in integrating our Loyalty Personalization AI service. This form allows you to provide your contact information and specify your primary goal for using our service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your company name in the "Company Name" field.
4. Enter your company website in the "Website" field (optional).
5. Select your primary goal from the "What is your primary goal?" dropdown menu.
6. If you selected "Other (please specify)" in the previous step, provide a brief description in the "Other (please specify)" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
8. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
9. Check the box next to "Consent" to indicate that you have reviewed the terms of service.
10. Check the box next to "Terms of Service" to confirm that you have read and agree to the terms of service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (text, required)**: Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name (text, required)**: Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Company Name (text, required)**: Enter your company's name for identification purposes.
* **Website (text, optional)**: Enter your company website for reference purposes.
* **What is your primary goal? (select_one, required)**: Select one of the following options:
	+ I need a personalized experience for our customers
	+ I need AI-powered recommendations
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Other (please specify) (text, optional)**: If you selected "Other (please specify)" in the previous step, provide a brief description of your goal.
* **Email (email, required)**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone (text, required)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Consent (select_multiple, required)**: Check the box to indicate that you have reviewed the terms of service.
* **Terms of Service (select_multiple, required)**: Check the box to confirm that you have read and agree to the terms of service.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your submission is complete.
* If you have any issues or questions, please contact our support team for assistance.
