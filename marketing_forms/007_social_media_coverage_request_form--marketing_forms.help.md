# social_media_coverage_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The social media coverage request form is designed to collect information from users about their social media coverage requests. This form helps the marketing team to track and prioritize these requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address to ensure we can contact you for further information.
2. Provide your phone number so we can reach out to you if needed.
3. Describe your request in the "Request" field.
4. Select the category that best fits your request (Social Media, Marketing, or Other).
5. Add any additional details about your request in the "Description" field.
6. If you have a preferred person to be assigned to, select their name from the "Assigned to" field.
7. Finally, choose a date for your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Please enter your name so we can contact you about your request.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can reach out to you if needed.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Provide your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Request** (`request`, text, required: false): Describe your social media coverage request in this field.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required: false): Choose the category that best fits your request (Social Media, Marketing, or Other).
* **Description** (`description`, text, required: false): Add any additional details about your request.
* **Assigned to** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required: false): Select the person you would like to be assigned to for your request.
* **Date of Request** (`date_of_request`, date, required: false): Select the date you would like your request to be processed.
