# discount_wheel_participation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "discount wheel participation form" is a marketing promotion that allows users to participate in a reward program. To be eligible, users must have received a unique invitation code. This form is used to collect information about the user, including their contact details and campaign specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact information on the first page.
2. Enter your email address and phone number on separate lines.
3. Confirm your consent to participate in the campaign.
4. Enter your unique campaign ID, if provided.
5. Provide any additional campaign details, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details** (`user_details`, text, optional): Please enter your name to identify yourself as a participant.
* **Email** (`user_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you about your campaign details and rewards.
* **Phone** (`user_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, if you wish to be contacted by phone.
* **Consent** (`user_consent`, text, optional): Confirm that you have read and agreed to participate in the campaign.
* **Entry ID** (`entry_id`, number, optional): If you were provided with a unique campaign ID, enter it here to associate your entry with your campaign.
* **Campaign Details** (`campaign_details`, text, optional): If additional campaign information is required, enter it here to ensure we can process your entry correctly.
