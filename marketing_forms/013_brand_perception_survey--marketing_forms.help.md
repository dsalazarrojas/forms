# Brand Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on the respondent's perception of a brand.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the correct option for each question based on your personal experience with the brand.
2. Provide a text response if a question allows for it.
3. Answer all questions, as some are required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Status
* **Customer Status** (`customer_status`, select_one, required):
	+ Select your current status with the brand: Current Customer, Former Customer, or Never Purchased.
### First Impression
* **First Impression** (`first_impression`, text, required):
	+ Describe your first impression of the brand.
### Brand Descriptors
* **Brand Descriptors** (`brand_descriptors`, select_multiple, required):
	+ Select all words that describe this brand (e.g., Innovative, Traditional, Luxurious, etc.).
### Quality Perception
* **Quality Perception** (`quality_perception`, select_one, required):
	+ Select how you perceive the quality of this brand: Very High, High, Average, Below Average, or Low.
### Price Perception
* **Price Perception** (`price_perception`, select_one, required):
	+ Select how you perceive the pricing of this brand: Very Expensive, Somewhat Expensive, Fairly Priced, Good Value, or Very Affordable.
### Customer Service Experience
* **Customer Service Experience** (`customer_service_experience`, select_one, required):
	+ Rate your customer service experience: Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, or Very Poor.
### Brand Uniqueness
* **Brand Uniqueness** (`brand_uniqueness`, select_one, required):
	+ Select how unique this brand is compared to others: Very Unique, Somewhat Unique, Average, Not Very Unique, or Not Unique at All.
### Emotional Connection
* **Emotional Connection** (`emotional_connection`, select_one, required):
	+ Select how emotionally connected you feel with this brand: Strong Connection, Some Connection, Neutral, Little Connection, or No Connection.
### Social Status
* **Social Status** (`social_status`, select_one, required):
	+ Select how this brand affects your social image: Significantly Enhances, Somewhat Enhances, No Effect, Somewhat Negative, or Very Negative.
### Loyalty Level
* **Loyalty Level** (`loyalty_level`, select_one, required):
	+ Select how loyal you are to this brand: Very Loyal, Somewhat Loyal, Neutral, Not Very Loyal, or Not Loyal at All.
### Repurchase Intention
* **Repurchase Intention** (`repurchase_intention`, select_one, required):
	+ Select if you will purchase from this brand again: Definitely Will, Probably Will, Might or Might Not, Probably Will Not, or Definitely Will Not.
### Recommend to Others
* **Recommend to Others** (`recommend_to_others`, select_one, required):
	+ Select if you would recommend this brand: Definitely Would, Probably Would, Might or Might Not, Probably Would Not, or Definitely Would Not.
### Brand Strengths
* **Brand Strengths** (`brand_strengths`, text, optional):
	+ Describe what this brand does well.
### Brand Weaknesses
* **Brand Weaknesses** (`brand_weaknesses`, text, optional):
	+ Describe what this brand could improve.
### Overall Opinion
* **Overall Opinion** (`overall_opinion`, text, required):
	+ Summarize your overall opinion of this brand.
