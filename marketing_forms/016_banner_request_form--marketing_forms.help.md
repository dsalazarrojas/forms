# Banner Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Banner Request Form is designed to capture all the necessary information for creating or procuring a banner for marketing purposes. This form ensures that the required details are provided to ensure timely and correct banner creation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your request date to ensure your deadline is captured.
2. Enter your name, contact email, and contact phone number to allow for efficient communication.
3. Choose the department or division you belong to.
4. Specify the purpose of the banner.
5. Enter the name of the event or campaign.
6. Choose the date of the event.
7. Select the location where the banner will be displayed.
8. Choose the preferred banner size and format.
9. If you have custom dimensions, specify them.
10. Choose the banner material.
11. Indicate if you have a design ready or need help with it.
12. If you have a design, describe it or attach the file.
13. Enter the primary color scheme.
14. Enter the main text to appear on the banner.
15. Decide if the company logo should be included.
16. If applicable, enter the budget amount.
17. Specify when you need the banner by.
18. Choose the urgency level of your request.
19. If you have any additional requests or notes, enter them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Date** (1): Enter the date when you submitted this request.
* **Requester Name** (2): Enter your full name for contact purposes.
* **Email Address** (3): Enter your contact email for updates.
* **Phone Number** (4): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Department or Division** (5): Specify your department or division for reference.
* **Banner Purpose** (6): Choose the event or campaign type.
* **Event or Campaign Name** (7): Enter the name of the event or campaign.
* **Event Date** (8): Enter the date of the event.
* **Banner Placement Location** (9): Choose where the banner will be displayed.
* **Banner Dimensions** (10): Choose the preferred banner size and format.
* **Custom Dimensions** (11): If custom, enter exact dimensions.
* **Banner Material** (12): Choose the banner material.
* **Do You Have a Design?** (13): Indicate if you have a design ready or need help.
* **Design File Details** (14): Describe or attach design file.
* **Primary Colors** (15): Enter the main color scheme.
* **Key Message or Text** (16): Enter the main text to appear on the banner.
* **Include Logo?** (17): Decide if the company logo appears.
* **Budget Amount** (18): Enter the budget for the banner.
* **Required By Date** (19): Specify when you need the banner.
* **Urgency Level** (20): Choose the urgency level of your request.
* **Additional Requests or Notes** (21): Enter any special instructions or requests.

## Tips
* Double-check all fields before submitting to ensure accurate information.
* Be as detailed as possible in each field to avoid delays.
* If you're unsure about any field, consult with the banner team or your supervisor.
* Provide updates on any changes to your requests or deadlines.
