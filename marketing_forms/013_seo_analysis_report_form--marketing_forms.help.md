# seo_analysis_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The SEO Analysis Report Form is a tool for gathering and analyzing information about a website's performance and its potential impact on search engines like Google, Bing, and Baidu. This form helps in identifying the current state of a website's SEO strategies, the effectiveness of its content distribution methods, and the tools used for analytics.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant fields that pertain to your website's SEO analysis needs.
2. For each field, choose the options that best describe your website's characteristics and strategies.
3. Ensure that you fill in all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
4. Once you have filled out all the required fields, submit the form to generate your SEO analysis report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Website Performance**: Select all that apply from the options provided to describe your website's performance.
	+ Website speed: How fast does your website load?
	+ Page loading time: How long does it take for pages on your website to load?
	+ Page size: How much data does each page on your website use?
* **Keyword Usage**: Select the search engine where you want to analyze your website's keyword usage.
	+ Baidu: Do you use Baidu for your website?
	+ Bing: Do you use Bing for your website?
	+ Google: Do you use Google for your website?
* **SEO Strategies**: Choose the SEO strategy that best describes your website's current approach.
	+ Technical SEO: Do you focus on optimizing website's technical aspects, like page loading speed and mobile-friendliness?
	+ On-page SEO: Do you optimize on-page elements, such as titles, descriptions, and content?
	+ Off-page SEO: Do you focus on building links from other websites and social media platforms?
* **Content Distribution**: Select the method you use to distribute your content.
	+ Social media: Do you share your content on social media platforms?
	+ Influencer outreach: Do you partner with influencers to promote your content?
	+ Email marketing: Do you use email marketing campaigns to distribute your content?
* **Analytics Tools**: Select all the analytics tools you use to monitor your website's performance.
	+ Google Analytics: Do you use Google Analytics to measure your website's traffic and behavior?
	+ SEMrush: Do you use SEMrush to analyze your website's SEO performance?
	+ Ahrefs: Do you use Ahrefs for keyword research and link analysis?
* **Target Audience**: Select the type of audience your website is intended for.
	+ B2B: Is your website for business-to-business (B2B) purposes?
	+ B2C: Is your website for business-to-consumer (B2C) purposes?
	+ B2B/B2C: Does your website cater to both B2B and B2C audiences?
* **Website Technology**: Select the technologies used to build your website.
	+ HTML/CSS: Does your website use HTML and CSS for its design and layout?
	+ JavaScript: Does your website use JavaScript for interactive elements?
	+ PHP/MySQL: Does your website use PHP and MySQL for server-side scripting and database integration?
