# restaurant_food_photos - Help Guide
## Purpose
The restaurant_food_photos form is used to collect information about food photos from restaurant menus. It is designed to gather data for marketing and research purposes. Please fill out this form to provide accurate and relevant information about your food photos.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief title for the photo in the **Photo Title** field.
2. Provide the URL of the photo in the **Photo URL** field.
3. Indicate whether the photo is of a menu item or not by selecting **Yes** or **No** in the **Menu Item** field.
4. If applicable, enter the date the photo was taken in the **Date Taken** field.
5. If applicable, enter the time the photo was taken in the **Time Taken** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Photo Title** (text, required/optional): Enter a brief title for the photo to help identify it.
* **Photo URL** (text, required/optional): Enter the URL of the photo, if available.
* **Menu Item** (select multiple, required): Indicate whether the photo is of a menu item or not.
* **Date Taken** (date, required/optional): Enter the date the photo was taken, if applicable.
* **Time Taken** (time, required/optional): Enter the time the photo was taken, if applicable.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and relevant information about the photo to ensure its usefulness for marketing and research purposes.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the relevant authority for guidance.
