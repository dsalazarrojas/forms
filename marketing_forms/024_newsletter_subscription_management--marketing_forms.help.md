# newsletter_subscription_management - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users regarding their preferences for newsletters. It helps us understand how often and which newsletters you want to receive, so we can tailor our newsletters to meet your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Newsletters Preferences**: Enter your preferences for newsletters in this text field. You can write anything you'd like, and we'll consider it when creating our newsletters.
2. **Subscription Frequency**: Choose how often you want to receive newsletters from us. Options are Daily, Weekly, and Monthly.
3. **Newsletters List**: Select all or a specific list of newsletters that interest you.
4. **Date Frequency**: Unfortunately, this field is not currently used.
5. **Time Frequency**: Unfortunately, this field is not currently used.
6. **Email Address**: Enter your email address where we'll send our newsletters to.
7. **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number in the format of XXX-XXX-XXXX.
8. **Confirm Email**: Confirm your email address to ensure we have the correct information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Newsletters Preferences** (`Newsletters Preferences`, text, optional): Enter your preferences for newsletters in this text field. We'll use this information to create newsletters that cater to your interests.
* **Subscription Frequency** (`Subscription Frequency`, select_one, optional): Choose how often you want to receive newsletters from us. Options are Daily, Weekly, and Monthly.
* **Newsletters List** (`Newsletters List`, select_multiple, optional): Select all or a specific list of newsletters that interest you.
* **Date Frequency** (`Date Frequency`, date, optional): Unfortunately, this field is not currently used.
* **Time Frequency** (`Time Frequency`, time, optional): Unfortunately, this field is not currently used.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, email, optional): Enter your email address where we'll send our newsletters to.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number in the format of XXX-XXX-XXXX.
* **Confirm Email** (`Confirm Email`, email, optional): Confirm your email address to ensure we have the correct information.
