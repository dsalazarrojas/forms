**Interest In Upcoming Demos - Help Guide**

## Purpose
This form is designed to allow users to express interest in upcoming demos, which are presentations or live presentations of products or services. The purpose of the form is to gather information from interested individuals or representatives of companies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the demos you are interested in by choosing from the available options under "What is the upcoming demo you are interested in?"
2. Fill in your name and other relevant contact information.
3. Optionally, provide your company, job title, department, and phone number if relevant.
4. Add any additional message or details you'd like to share with the demo organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the upcoming demo you are interested in?** (`upcoming_demos`, select_multiple, required): This is a multiple-choice question where you select all the demos you are interested in. Please select all the options that apply.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in correspondence related to your interest in the demo.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to receive notifications and updates about the demo.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): If you're representing a company, enter your company name here. If not, you can leave this field blank.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title or position within your company, if relevant.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter your department name or team name, if relevant.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, which can be used for follow-up calls or meetings related to the demo.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): Provide any additional information or comments you'd like to share with the demo organizers. This can be questions, suggestions, or follow-up requests.
