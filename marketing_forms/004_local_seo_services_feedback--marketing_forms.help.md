# local_seo_services_feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Local SEO Services Feedback form is designed to gather information about our local SEO services from our customers. This form is intended for customers to provide feedback, rate their experience, and share their opinions about our services. It's essential to complete this form to help us improve our services and better understand our customers' needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and answer all the required fields.
2. Fill in the fields as requested, providing your genuine feedback and opinions.
3. Rate your experience with our local SEO services.
4. If you have any specific comments or feedback, use the "comment" field to express your thoughts.
5. Select all the services you used from the "what_services" field.
6. Enter your name and email address in the "name" and "email" fields respectively.
7. If you'd like to provide a testimonial, type it in the "testimonial" field.
8. Enter your contact phone number in the "phone" field.
9. Use the "message" field to leave a message or additional feedback.
10. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Welcome to Local SEO Services Feedback**:
	+ This is an introduction page that sets the context for the form.
* **Rate**: 
	+ Use the number field to rate your experience with our local SEO services.
* **Comment**:
	+ Type in your specific comments or feedback about our services.
* **What Services**: 
	+ Select all the services you used from the options provided.
* **Feedback**:
	+ Share your thoughts and opinions about our services.
* **Testimonial**:
	+ If you're happy with our services, consider leaving a testimonial to help us improve and promote our services.
* **Name**:
	+ Enter your name so we can address you personally.
* **Email**:
	+ Enter your email address so we can contact you for further clarification.
* **Phone**:
	+ Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further discussion.
* **Message**:
	+ Leave any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share with us.
 
## Tips
- Be honest and genuine in your feedback.
- Use clear and concise language in your comments and feedback.
- Select only the services you genuinely used to avoid errors.
- Make sure your contact information is accurate to ensure we can contact you back.
