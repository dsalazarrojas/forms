# Customer Awareness Surway - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect customer data for marketing purposes, including customer awareness and lead generation information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Customer Awarenes Surway" page to start entering customer information.
2. Enter customer lead generation details on the "Customer Lead" page.
3. Enter customer lead date on the "Customer Lead Date" page.
4. Enter customer lead time on the "Customer Lead Time" page.
5. Enter customer email address on the "Customer EMail" page.
6. Enter customer phone number on the "Customer Phone" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Awarenes Surway** (text): This is the first page of the form where you can enter any additional comments or information about the customer.
* **Customer Lead** (number): Enter the number of customer leads generated from the customer.
* **Customer Lead Date** (date): Enter the date when the customer was last seen or generated.
* **Customer Lead Time** (time): Enter the time when the customer was last seen or generated.
* **Customer EMail** (email): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (text): Enter the customer's phone number.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
- Double-check the entered data for accuracy and completeness.
- If you're unsure about any field, please contact your administrator for assistance.
