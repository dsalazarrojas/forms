# Marketing Collaboration Brief Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marketing Collaboration Brief Request Form is a tool used by marketing teams to request a collaboration brief from other teams or departments within the company. This form helps to gather necessary information about the brand name, goal of the collaboration, project timeline, and budget for the marketing project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the brand name that the collaboration brief is for.
2. Describe the goal of the collaboration in a few sentences.
3. Enter the project timeline (start and end dates).
4. Provide the expected budget for the project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Brand Name** (`brand_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the brand that this collaboration brief is for. This will help the team understand which brand's identity and guidelines they need to follow.
* **What is the Goal?** (`goal`, text, optional): Briefly describe the goal of the collaboration. This could be a specific marketing campaign, product launch, or other business objective.
* **Timeline** (`timeline`, date, optional): Enter the start and end dates of the project. This will help the team plan and schedule the project accordingly.
* **Budget** (`budget`, number, optional): Enter the expected budget for the project. This will help the team understand the financial constraints and plan accordingly.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out this form only when a collaboration brief is required.
- Be as specific as possible when filling out the fields.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact the relevant team or department for clarification.
