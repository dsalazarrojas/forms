# Digital Marketing SEO Interview Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about a candidate's experience and preferences in digital marketing. The form will help the hiring team evaluate the candidate's skills and experience, making it easier for them to make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling in your title and a brief description of this form.
2. Select the category that best fits this form.
3. Choose the marketing teams you are experienced in or familiar with.
4. Indicate how likely you are to fit the position.
5. Choose how you would organize this form.
6. Select how you would collect data.
7. Indicate if you would automate form collection.
8. Choose how you would submit this form.
9. Select if you would automate form submission.
10. Choose your preferred method of communication with clients.
11. Enter your email address.
12. Enter your phone number.
13. Add any additional notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (form_title, text, required: false): Enter a brief title of this form.
* **What is your candidate experience** (candidate_experience, text, required: false): Describe your experience in digital marketing.
* **Description of this form** (form_description, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of this form.
* **Category** (form_category, text, required: false): Select the category that best fits this form.
* **Marketing Teams** (marketing_teams, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the marketing teams you are experienced in or familiar with.
	+ Search Engine Optimisation (SEO)
	+ Content Marketing
	+ Social Media
	+ Email Marketing
	+ PPC (Pay-Per-Click)
	+ Affiliate Marketing
	+ Inbound Marketing
	+ Web Analytics
	+ Content Creation
	+ User Experience (UX)
* **How would you fit this position** (candidate_fit, select_one, required: false): Indicate how likely you are to fit the position.
	+ Highly Likely
	+ Moderately Likely
	+ Not Likely
* **How would you organize this form** (candidate_organised_form, select_one, required: false): Choose how you would organize this form.
	+ Well-Organized
	+ Moderately Organised
	+ Not Organised
* **How would you collect data** (candidate_data_collection, select_one, required: false): Select how you would collect data.
	+ Automated
	+ Manual
	+ Mixed
* **What is your automated form collection** (candidate_automated_collection, select_one, required: false): Indicate if you would automate form collection.
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **How would you submit this form** (candidate_organised_submissions, select_one, required: false): Choose how you would submit this form.
	+ Automated
	+ Manual
	+ Mixed
* **What is your automated form submission** (candidate_automated_submissions, select_one, required: false): Indicate if you would automate form submission.
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Communication** (candidate_communication, select_multiple, required: false): Choose your preferred method of communication with clients.
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ Text
	+ Social Media
	+ Instant Messaging
	+ Video
	+ In-Person
	+ Chat
* **Candidate Email** (candidate_email, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Candidate Phone** (candidate_phone, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Candidate Notes** (candidate_notes, note, required: false): Add any additional notes.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and relevant information to the best of your abilities.
* If unsure about any of the fields, try to provide a best-effort answer based on your experience and preferences.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
