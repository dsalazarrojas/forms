# pop_up_feedback_form_by_julie_potts - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from customers after a marketing event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions as accurately as possible.
2. Enter your contact information so we can get back to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **feedback** (1): Provide a brief description of your experience at the event.
* **contact_info** (2): Enter your name, email address, and phone number so we can get back to you.
* **follow_up** (3): If you would like us to follow up with you, please select this option.
* **event_info** (4): Share any additional information about the event that we may have missed.
* **other_info** (5): If you have anything else you'd like to share about your experience, please enter it here.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when answering the questions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We appreciate your feedback and will use it to improve our future events!
