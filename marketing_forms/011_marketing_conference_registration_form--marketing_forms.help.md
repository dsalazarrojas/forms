# Marketing Conference Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering attendees for a marketing conference. It gathers necessary information for conference organizers and staff to manage conference attendees, including contact information, registration and payment information, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Complete each field with accurate and up-to-date information.
2.  Ensure all required fields are filled out.
3.  For payment fields, select the correct payment option and amount.
4.  Review and confirm your submission before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Marketing Conference Details** (`marketing_conference_details`): Enter a brief description of the conference you are registering for.
*   **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`): Enter any additional details about the attendee, such as their role in the company or any special requests.
*   **Contact Information** (`contact_information`): Enter the attendee's name, address, and email address to facilitate communication and follow-up.
*   **Registration Information** (`registration_info`): Enter any additional registration information, such as special requests or dietary requirements.
*   **Payment Information** (`payment_info`): Enter the payment method and amount for the conference registration.
*   **Confirm Submission** (`confirm_submission`): Confirm that you have accurately filled out all required fields.
*   **Confirm Payment** (`confirm_payment`): Confirm that your payment information is accurate.
*   **Confirm Registration** (`confirm_registration`): Confirm your registration for the conference.
*   **Email** (`email`): Enter the attendee's email address to facilitate communication and follow-up.
*   **Phone** (`phone`): Enter the attendee's phone number to facilitate communication and follow-up.
*   **Name** (`name`): Enter the attendee's name to facilitate communication and follow-up.
*   **Address 1** (`address1`): Enter the attendee's street address.
*   **Address 2** (`address2`): Enter the attendee's apartment or suite number (if applicable).
*   **City** (`city`): Enter the attendee's city name.
*   **State** (`state`): Enter the attendee's state or province name.
*   **Zip** (`zip`): Enter the attendee's zip or postal code.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Enter the attendee's date of birth (if required).
*   **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`): Enter the attendee's time of birth (if required).
*   **Event Date** (`event_date`): Enter the date of the conference.
*   **Event Time** (`event_time`): Enter the time of the conference.
*   **Event Location** (`event_location`): Enter the location of the conference.
*   **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Select whether you will be paying online or offline.
*   **Payment Gateway** (`payment_gateway`): Select the payment gateway to be used for payment processing.
*   **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`): Select the payment amount from the available options.

## Tips
*   Always ensure that you have accurately filled out all required fields.
*   Double-check your payment information to ensure accuracy.
*   Review your submission before confirming.
*   If you have any special requests or requirements, please clearly state them in the `attendee_details` field.
