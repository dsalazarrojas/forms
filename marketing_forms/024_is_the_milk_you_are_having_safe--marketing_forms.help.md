# Is The Milk You Are Having Safe!!! - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the safety of milk being consumed. It helps ensure that the milk is free from adulterations and is safe for consumption.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please fill out the form in its entirety, including all eight fields.
* For each field, provide as much information as possible to ensure accurate results.
* You can skip any fields that are not relevant to your specific situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Detect Water Adulteration** (`water_detection`, text, required: false): Fill out this field if you suspect that the milk may have water adulteration.
* **Detect Starch Adulteration** (`starch_detection`, text, required: false): Fill out this field if you suspect that the milk may have starch adulteration.
* **Detect Detergent Adulteration** (`detergent_detection`, text, required: false): Fill out this field if you suspect that the milk may have detergent adulteration.
* **Detect Other Adulteration** (`other_detection`, text, required: false): Fill out this field if you suspect that the milk may have any other type of adulteration.
* **Video URL** (`video_url`, text, required: false): This field is not well-labeled, but it's likely a field for a URL of a video related to milk safety. If you have a video that provides more information on milk safety, please provide the URL.
* **Reference Source** (`reference_source`, text, required: false): This field is also not well-labeled, but it's likely a field for a reference source related to milk safety. If you have a reference that provides more information on milk safety, please provide the URL.
* **Is It Safe** (`is_safe`, select_one, required: false): Choose "Safe" if you are confident that the milk is safe, or "Not Safe" if you are not confident.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required: false): This field is likely for any additional tool or equipment used to detect adulteration. If you have used a tool, please provide its name or description.

## Tips
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with a milk safety expert or the form creator.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact the form creator at form@gic.mx.
