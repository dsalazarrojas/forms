# Newspaper Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to gather feedback and information from users about their experience with the newspaper. Your input will help us improve our content and services to better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "What is your name" field.
2. Answer how many times you have read our newspaper in the "How many times have you read our newspaper?" field.
3. Share your thoughts about our newspaper in the "What do you think of our newspaper?" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, if you wish to be contacted.
5. Choose your preferred contact method, whether it is "Call us" or "Email us".
6. Select a date and time for a meeting, if you wish to schedule one.
7. Write any additional comments or feedback in the "Additional comments" field.
8. Indicate how often you visit our website, by choosing one of the options in the "How often do you visit our website?" field.
9. Choose the types of content you usually read, by selecting multiple options in the "What type of content do you usually read?" field.
10. Answer how you heard about our newspaper in the "How did you hear about our newspaper?" field.
11. Indicate how long you have been reading our newspaper, by entering a number.
12. Choose the types of content you usually read, by selecting multiple options in the "What do you usually read?" field.
13. Answer how you like our newspaper, in the "Do you like our newspaper?" field.
14. Indicate how many times you have read our newspaper, by entering a number.
15. Write any additional comments or feedback in the "Additional comments" field.
16. Indicate if you want to be a part of our newspaper, by answering "yes" or "no".
17. Answer how often you want to read our newspaper, by entering a number.
18. Indicate if you read our newspaper on a computer, by typing "yes" or "no".
19. Indicate if you read our newspaper on a smartphone, by typing "yes" or "no".
