# Travel Deal Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Deal Submission Form is used by travel agencies to submit information about travel experiences they want to offer to customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the **Agency Name** to identify the travel agency submitting the deal.
2.  Select the **Type of travel** that best describes the experience you're offering (e.g., Beach, City, Cultural, Food and wine, Nature, Ski).
3.  Enter the **Start date** and **End date** of the travel experience.
4.  Enter the **Price (USD)** for the experience.
5.  Select the **Currency (USD)** for the price.
6.  Describe the experience in the **Description** field.
7.  Enter the **Image URL** where we can find a visual representation of the experience.
8.  Read and agree to our **Terms and Conditions** in multiple languages (English, French, Spanish, German).
9.  Enter any **Submission Notes** about the experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Agency Name**: Enter the name of your travel agency.
*   **Type of travel**: Choose the type of travel experience you're offering (e.g., Beach, City, Cultural, Food and wine, Nature, Ski).
*   **Start date**: Enter the start date of the travel experience.
*   **End date**: Enter the end date of the travel experience.
*   **Price (USD)**: Enter the price of the experience in USD.
*   **Currency (USD)**: Select the currency for the price (USD).
*   **Description**: Describe the travel experience in detail.
*   **Image URL**: Enter the URL of a visual representation of the experience.
*   **Terms and Conditions**: Read and agree to our terms and conditions in multiple languages.
*   **Submission Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the experience.

## Tips

*   Make sure the information is accurate and complete.
*   Be sure to proofread for spelling and grammar errors.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact us.
