# Marketing Role Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess a candidate's marketing role and provide a comprehensive evaluation of their skills, experience, and job history.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form by providing information for each field as relevant.
2. Be accurate and honest in your assessments.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled in.
4. Use the form as a starting point for further evaluation and discussion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Title** (`job_title`, select_one, required): Select the candidate's job title from the list provided.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_one, required): Select the job status of the candidate (Full-time, Part-time, Contract, or Intern).
* **Candidate Rating** (`candidate_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the candidate's performance as Not Rated, Highly Rated, Average, or Poorly Rated.
* **Review Status** (`review_status`, select_one, required): Select the review status (Approved, Rejected, or In Progress).
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): If the candidate has a manager, provide their name.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, text, optional): If the candidate has a manager, provide their email.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, optional): If the candidate has a manager, provide their phone number.
* **Manager Position** (`manager_position`, text, optional): If the candidate has a manager, provide their position.
* **Marketing Skills** (`marketing_skills`, select_multiple, required): Select the candidate's key marketing skills (Marketing Automation, Social Media Management, Content Creation, Brand Management).
* **Experience** (`experience`, text, optional): Provide information about the candidate's marketing experience.
* **Previous Company** (`previous_company`, text, optional): If applicable, provide the candidate's previous company name.
* **Job Duration** (`job_duration`, date, optional): If applicable, provide the candidate's job duration.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, text, optional): If applicable, provide the candidate's job type.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, optional): If applicable, provide the candidate's job description.
* **Job Responsibilities** (`job_responsibilities`, text, optional): If applicable, provide the candidate's job responsibilities.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, optional): If applicable, provide any additional information about the candidate.
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, text, optional): If applicable, provide the manager's comments.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, optional): If applicable, provide the next steps for the candidate.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, optional): If applicable, provide the review date.
* **Review Time** (`review_time`, time, optional): If applicable, provide the review time.
* **Previous Manager** (`previous_manager`, select_one, required): Select Yes or No to indicate if the candidate had a manager.
* **Additional Comments** (`candidate_comments`, text, optional): If applicable, provide additional comments about the candidate.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): If applicable, provide any additional notes.
