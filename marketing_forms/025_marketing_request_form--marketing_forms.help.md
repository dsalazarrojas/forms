# Marketing Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Marketing Request Form is used to collect information from customers and stakeholders for marketing activities. It helps us understand their needs and preferences, allowing us to tailor our marketing efforts to better serve them. **Please only submit one form per request, and ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.** If you've previously submitted a form for the same marketing activity, you can disregard this form. Duplicate submissions can cause confusion and delays in processing your request.

## How To Complete This Form
To submit a successful request, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the first name field with your full first name as it appears on your official identification.
2. Fill out the last name field with your full last name as it appears on your official identification.
3. Enter a valid email address that you check frequently.
4. Enter your phone number (optional but recommended for follow-up contact).
5. In the message field, provide a clear and concise description of your marketing request, including any relevant details or attachments.
6. Select the marketing activity that best matches your request from the dropdown menu.
7. Choose any relevant marketing tools or channels that you would like to be assigned to your request (optional but recommended for tailored support).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Enter your full first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last name**: Enter your full last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you check frequently.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for follow-up contact (optional but recommended).
* **Message**: Provide a clear and concise description of your marketing request, including any relevant details or attachments.
* **Marketing Activity**: Select the marketing activity that best matches your request from the dropdown menu.
  - Option 1: Social Media Campaign
  - Option 2: Email Marketing
  - Option 3: Event Promotion
