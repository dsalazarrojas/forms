# Referral Partner Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Referral Partner Signup Form is designed to collect information from referral partners to facilitate effective communication and collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the **First Name** field. This is a required field.
2. Fill in your Last Name in the **Last Name** field. This is a required field.
3. Enter your email address in the **Email** field. This is a required field.
4. Fill in your phone number in the **Phone** field. This is a required field.
5. Select a date for the **Date** field. This is a required field.
6. (Optional) If you have a preferred time, select it in the **Time** field.
7. If you have a referral URL, enter it in the **Referral URL** field.
8. If you have a description of the referral, enter it in the **Referral Description** field.
9. Select your referral partner from the options in the **Referral Partner** field. This is a required field.
10. Select your answer to the question "Do you want to be contacted by us?" from the options in the **Submit** field. This field is not required.
11. Add any additional notes about the referral in the **Notes** field. This field is not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`form_1`, `text`, required): Enter your first name, which will be used to address you in our communications.
* **Last Name** (`form_2`, `text`, required): Enter your last name, which will be used to address you in our communications.
* **Email** (`form_3`, `email`, required): Enter your email address, which will be used to contact you regarding the referral.
* **Phone** (`form_4`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number, which will be used for further communication about the referral.
* **Date** (`form_5`, `date`, required): Select a date for the referral.
* **Time** (`form_6`, `time`, optional): Select a time for the referral, if available.
* **Referral URL** (`form_7`, `text`, optional): If you have a URL related to the referral, enter it here.
* **Referral Description** (`form_8`, `text`, optional): Enter a description of the referral, if applicable.
* **Referral Partner** (`form_9`, `select_one`, required): Select your referral partner from the options provided.
* **Submit** (`form_10`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select "Yes" if you want to be contacted by us about the referral, or "No" otherwise.
* **Notes** (`form_11`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes about the referral, if desired.
