# PPC Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PPC Agreement Form is a document that outlines the terms and conditions for a specific campaign scope, including reporting expectations, timeline, and approval details. It is used to ensure that both the client and the agency are on the same page regarding the campaign's objectives, deliverables, and communication plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review the campaign scope and determine the specific objectives and deliverables for the campaign.
2.  Fill in the campaign scope field with a brief description of the campaign.
3.  Specify your reporting expectations, including how often you will be reporting results.
4.  Choose the marketing channels you will be using for the campaign.
5.  Enter your contact information and agency information.
6.  Provide your submitter's contact information.
7.  Review and sign the approval section to confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is the campaign scope** (`campaign_scope`, `text`, required: false): Describe the specific objectives and deliverables for the campaign.
* **reporting_expectations** (`reporting_expectations`, `text`, required: false): Specify how often you will be reporting results (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly).
* **What is the expected timeline** (`timeline`, `date`, required: false): Enter the start and end dates for the campaign.
* **approval** (`approval`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate whether you approve the terms and conditions of this agreement.
* **Which marketing channel will you be using** (`marketing_channel`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the marketing channels you will be using for the campaign (e.g., Google, Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, Snapchat).
* **How often will you be reporting results** (`reporting_frequency`, `select_one`, required: false): Specify the frequency of your reporting (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly).
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `note`, required: false): Enter any relevant contact information for yourself or your team.
* **Agency Information** (`agency_info`, `note`, required: false): Enter any relevant information about your agency or team.
* **Submitter Information** (`submitter_info`, `note`, required: false): Enter any relevant information about yourself or your team.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Submitter E-Mail** (`submitter_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your contact email address.
* **Client Information** (`client_info`, `note`, required: false): Enter any relevant information about the client.
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, `date`, required: false): If applicable, enter the date of approval for the campaign.
