# social_media_information_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The social media information request form is designed to collect basic contact information from individuals who are interested in engaging with our organization through social media channels. This form helps us to understand your identity, location, and preferred contact method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the field labeled "First Name".
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the field labeled "Last Name".
3. Enter your **Email** address in the field labeled "Email".
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the field labeled "Phone".
5. Select your **Country** of residence from the drop-down menu labeled "Country".
6. Select one or more **States** where you reside from the drop-down menu labeled "State".
7. Enter your **Zip** code in the field labeled "Zip".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: A text field to enter your given name.
- **Last Name**: A text field to enter your surname.
- **Email**: A field to enter your email address.
- **Phone**: A field to enter your phone number.
- **Country**: A select-one field to choose your country of residence.
  - Choose from: USA, Canada, Mexico
- **State**: A select-multiple field to choose one or more states where you reside.
  - Choose from: California, New York, Texas
- **Zip**: A text field to enter your zip code.
  - Enter a 5-digit code to complete this field.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your email address for accuracy.
- Be accurate when selecting your country and state of residence.
- Enter your zip code with the correct format (e.g., 12345).
