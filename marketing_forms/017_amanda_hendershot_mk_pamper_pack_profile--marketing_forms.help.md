# Amanda Hendershot MK Pamper Pack Profile - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Amanda Hendershot MK Pamper Pack Profile" form is designed to collect information about you, our valued customer, to provide a more personalized pamper pack experience. By filling out this form, you will help us better understand your needs and preferences, allowing us to tailor our services and products to make your experience with us unique and enjoyable.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please start by filling out the **Client name** field, where you can enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
2. Next, enter your **Email address** for correspondence and updates.
3. For our pamper pack, choose your **Skin type** from the dropdown menu, which will help us understand your skin needs.
4. If you have any **Preferred scents**, please list them in the text field provided.
5. If you have any **Known allergies**, list them in the text field to ensure our products are safe for you to use.
6. Enter your **Delivery address** where we will send your pamper pack.
7. Please indicate if you would like to **Opt in to promotions**, and we will keep you updated on offers and news.
8. Finally, if you have any **Additional comments** or special requests, feel free to let us know in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client name**: Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email address**: A valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Skin type**:
	+ **Dry**: Dry skin
	+ **Normal**: Normal skin
	+ **Oily**: Oily skin
	+ **Combination**: Combination skin
* **Preferred scents**: List any scents you enjoy or prefer (e.g., lavender, citrus, etc.).
* **Known allergies**: List any known allergies or sensitivities you have.
* **Delivery address**: Enter the address where we will send your pamper pack.
* **Opt in to promotions**: Choose 'Yes' to receive updates and offers or 'No' to not receive promotional emails.
* **Additional comments**: Any special requests or comments you'd like to share with us.
