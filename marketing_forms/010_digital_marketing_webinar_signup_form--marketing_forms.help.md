# Digital Marketing Webinar Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow users to sign up for a digital marketing webinar. Please fill out the required fields to confirm your spot in the webinar.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply click on each field to enter the required information. The form will guide you through the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Webinar Details** (`webinar_details`, `text`, required/optional: none): Enter the details about the webinar you are interested in, such as its title and description.
- **Registrants** (`registrants`, `select_multiple`, required/optional: none): Select which departments or teams within your organization are interested in the webinar. Choose from "Marketing", "Sales", or "Training".
- **Attendees** (`attendees`, `select_one`, required/optional: none): Confirm if you will be attending the webinar. Choose from "False" (no) or "True" (yes).
- **Confirmation Status** (`confirmation_status`, `select_one`, required/optional: none): Select the status of your registration. Choose from "Confirmed", "Pending", or "Replied".
- **Registrant Interests** (`registrant_interests`, `text`, required/optional: none): Enter any interests or special requests you have regarding the webinar.
- **Attendee Comments** (`attendee_comments`, `text`, required/optional: none): Leave any comments or questions you have about the webinar.
- **Registrant Details** (`registrant_details`, `text`, required/optional: none): Enter any additional details about yourself, such as your name and title.
- **Output File** (`output_file`, `text`, required/optional: none): Enter the name of the file to be outputted, such as a report or presentation.
- **Output File URL** (`output_file_url`, `text`, required/optional: none): Enter the URL of the output file to be used in the webinar.
- **Webinar Details 2** (`webinar_details_2`, `text`, required/optional: none): Enter any additional details about the webinar if needed.

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure smooth communication with our team.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
* The output file and URL will only be used for your personal use, so please make sure to keep sensitive information secure.
