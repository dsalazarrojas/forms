# Marine Boat Show Lead Generation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from attendees of the Marine Boat Show to generate leads for potential customers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your First Name, Email, and Sales Rep information correctly.
3. Choose your Company Role and Location from the available options.
4. Select the type of boat you are interested in (Sail, Motor, or Rowing).
5. Choose how you would like to be followed up (True or False).
6. Select the follow-up method you prefer (Phone, Email, or Mail).
7. Enter the follow-up date and time if you chose to be followed up.
8. Enter any additional comments or notes you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name. This field is required.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address. This field is required.
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of your company. This field is optional.
* **Company Role**: Choose your role within the company (Sales, Marketing, Product Development). This field is required.
* **Boat Type**: Select the type of boat you are interested in (Sail, Motor, Rowing). This field is optional.
* **Location**: Choose where you are based (France, Italy, Spain). This field is required.
* **Date of Visit**: Enter the date you plan to visit the Marine Boat Show. This field is optional.
* **Time of Visit**: Enter the time you plan to visit the Marine Boat Show. This field is optional.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes you have. This field is optional.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number. This field is optional.
* **Website**: Enter your website. This field is optional.
* **Follow up**: Indicate if you would like to be followed up (True or False). This field is required.
* **Follow up Method**: Choose how you would like to be followed up (Phone, Email, or Mail). This field is optional.
* **Follow up Date**: Enter the date you would like to be followed up. This field is optional.
* **Follow up Time**: Enter the time you would like to be followed up. This field is optional.
* **Follow up Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes for follow-up. This field is optional.
* **Follow up Phone Number**: Enter your follow-up phone number. This field is optional.
* **Follow up Email**: Enter your follow-up email. This field is optional.
* **Follow up Website**: Enter your follow-up website. This field is optional.
* **Sales Rep**: Enter the name of the sales representative you would like to be assigned. This field is required.
* **Sales Rep Email**: Enter the email of the sales representative you would like to be assigned. This field is required.
* **Sales Rep Phone**: Enter the phone number of the sales representative you would like to be assigned. This field is optional.
* **Sales Rep Follow up**: Enter the date you would like to be followed up by the sales representative. This field is optional.
* **Sales Rep Follow up Method**: Choose how you would like to be followed up by the sales representative (Phone, Email, or Mail). This field is optional.
