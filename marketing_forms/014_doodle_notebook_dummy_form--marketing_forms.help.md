# doodle_notebook_dummy_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "doodle_notebook_dummy_form" form is a tool used for customizing and purchasing doodle notebooks. It allows users to provide input on whether they want to customize their notebook or order one. The form is primarily used for internal purposes within the organization, and its completion helps the team in better understanding customer preferences and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Customise Your Doodle Notebook**: Enter your desired customization for your doodle notebook.
2. Select your preferred **Notebook Size**: Choose whether you want a small or large notebook.
3. Specify how many **Doodle Notebooks** you want to order.
4. (Optional) Enter your **Email** address to ensure we can contact you for further details.
5. (Optional) Provide your **Phone Number** if you would like to be contacted directly.
6. Enter your **Date of Birth** for identification purposes.
7. (Optional) Select your preferred **Time** for any additional information or follow-up.
8. (Optional) Add a **Note** for any additional comments or requirements.
9. Indicate whether you want to **Use Marketing Form**: Choose 'Yes' if you want to use the marketing form.
10. (Optional) Enter any **Customize Your Doodle Notebook Note** for further details.
11. Confirm your **Email** address again (if you didn't enter it in the previous step).
12. Choose your preferred **Notebook Size 1** (this field appears to be a redundant option; its purpose is not clearly defined in this example, but it seems to be a duplicate of "Select your preferred Notebook Size" in step 2).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customise Your Doodle Notebook (Text, Optional)**: Enter any desired customization for your doodle notebook.
* **Select your preferred Notebook Size (Select Multiple, Required)**: Choose whether you want a small or large notebook.
* **Specify how many Doodle Notebooks you want to order (Number, Required)**: Enter the number of notebooks you want to order.
* **Email (Email, Optional)**: Enter your email address for us to contact you.
* **Phone Number (Text, Optional)**: Provide your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Date of Birth (Date, Optional)**: Enter your date of birth for identification purposes.
* **Time (Time, Optional)**: Select your preferred time for any additional information or follow-up.
* **Note (Note, Optional)**: Add any additional comments or requirements for your order.
* **Use Marketing Form (Select One, Required)**: Choose 'Yes' if you want to use the marketing form.
* **Customise Your Doodle Notebook Note (Text, Optional)**: Enter any additional notes for your doodle notebook.
* **Email (Email, Optional)**: Enter your email address again (if not already entered).
* **Notebook Size 1 (Select One, Optional)**: Choose your preferred notebook size (this field seems to be redundant; please refer to "Select your preferred Notebook Size" in step 2).

## Tips
* Make sure to provide your email address for any follow-up or contact.
* Double-check your input for any errors or inconsistencies.
* If you have any additional questions or need further clarification, feel free to ask.
