# Content Marketing Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Content Marketing Communication Form is a tool used to streamline communication between team members, stakeholders, and clients about content marketing projects, campaigns, and feedback. It ensures that all necessary information is captured and recorded for reference and follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, ensuring all fields are filled and required fields are marked with an *.
2. Select the correct options for each field from the provided choices.
3. Review and proofread your input before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sender Name** (`sender_name`, `text`, *): Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Sender Email** (`sender_email`, `email`, *): Enter your email address associated with your department.
* **Sender Department** (`sender_department`, `select_one`, *): Select your department from the options provided.
* **Communication Date** (`communication_date`, `date`, *): Enter the date of this communication.
* **Recipient Name** (`recipient_name`, `text`): Enter the name of the person or team this communication is intended for.
* **Communication Type** (`communication_type`, `select_one`, *): Select the type of communication from the options provided.
* **Priority Level** (`priority_level`, `select_one`, *): Choose the level of urgency for this communication.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, `text`, *): Enter the name of the related project or campaign.
* **Content Type** (`content_type`, `select_one`, *): Select the type of content related to this communication.
* **Subject** (`subject`, `text`, *): Enter a brief subject line for this communication.
* **Message** (`message`, `text`, *): Enter your detailed message for the recipient.
* **Action Required** (`action_required`, `text`, *): Specify the action needed from the recipient.
* **Deadline** (`deadline`, `date`, ): Enter the deadline for response (optional).
* **Attachments Description** (`attachments`, `text`, ): Describe any attached files (optional).
* **Related Campaigns** (`related_campaigns`, `text`, ): List any related campaigns or projects (optional).
* **Other Stakeholders** (`stakeholders`, `text`, ): List any other stakeholders who should be informed (optional).
* **Previous Communications** (`previous_communications`, `text`, ): Reference any previous discussions or communications (optional).
* **Feedback Type** (`feedback_type`, `select_one`, *): Select the type of feedback needed.
* **Response Needed By** (`response_needed_by`, `date`, ): Enter the deadline for response (optional).
* **Follow-up Required** (`follow_up_required`, `select_one`, *): Indicate if this communication will need a follow-up meeting.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, `text`, ): Enter any other relevant information.

Note: The fields marked with an `*` are required.
