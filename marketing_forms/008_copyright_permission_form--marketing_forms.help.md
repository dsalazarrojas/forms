# Copyright Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain copyright permission from users for the use of copyrighted materials. It collects essential information about the user, including their name, email address, company name (if applicable), and contact number (if applicable). The form also captures details about the copyrighted material, such as the title, year, and owner. Additionally, it includes fields for the user to agree to the terms and conditions of the agreement and provide their signature date. The form is an original adaptation created by AI, and it is not affiliated with or endorsed by any other form platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the **Name** field.
2. Enter your email address in the **Email Address** field.
3. If you are representing a company, provide your company name in the **Company Name** field. (Optional)
4. If you have a contact number, enter it in the **Contact Number** field. (Optional)
5. Enter the title of the copyrighted material in the **Title** field.
6. Enter the year of the copyrighted material in the **Year** field.
7. Specify the owner of the copyrighted material in the **Owner** field.
8. Provide the location where the copyrighted material was created/originated in the **Location** field.
9. Choose "I agree to the terms of this agreement" and select **True** if you understand and accept the terms.
10. Choose "I agree to the terms and conditions" and select **True** if you understand and accept the terms and conditions.
11. Enter the date you are signing the agreement in the **Signature Date** field.
12. Provide your signature in the **Agreement Signature** field, which is a space for you to sign your name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your official email address where you can be contacted.
* **Company Name**: If you are representing a company, enter your company's name.
* **Contact Number**: If you have a contact number, enter it for easier communication.
* **Title**: Enter the title of the copyrighted material, such as a book, article, or video.
* **Year**: Enter the year the copyrighted material was created or published.
* **Owner**: Specify the owner of the copyrighted material, such as an individual or a company.
* **Location**: Enter the location where the copyrighted material was created or originated.
* **I agree to the terms of this agreement**: Select **True** if you understand and accept the terms of this agreement.
* **I agree to the terms and conditions**: Select **True** if you understand and accept the terms and conditions.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date you are signing the agreement in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Agreement Signature**: Provide your signature in this space, which is a note field for your handwritten or typed name.

## Tips
* Please ensure you understand and accept the terms of the agreement before selecting **True** for both "I agree to the terms of this agreement" and "I agree to the terms and conditions".
* Make sure to enter accurate information for all fields, especially your name, email address, and signature date.
* If you have any doubts or questions, contact <form@gic.mx>.
