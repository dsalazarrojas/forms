# Marketing And Public Relations Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture marketing and public relations requests from companies. It's intended for organizations looking to promote their brand, products, or services. The form should only be completed once per company, and any duplicate requests from the same company will be rejected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your company name in the **Company Name** field.
2.  Enter a valid email address in the **Email** field.
3.  Enter your company's phone number in the **Phone Number** field.
4.  Select the type of company from the **Type Of Company** dropdown menu.
5.  Enter the contact person's name in the **Contact Person** field.
6.  (Optional) Enter your company's address in the **Company Address** field.
7.  Describe your marketing and public relations request in the **Request** field.
8.  Enter any additional information related to your request in the **Additional Info** field.
9.  Click on the **Submit** button to finalize your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your company.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for your company.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your company's phone number.
*   **Type Of Company** (`company_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of company from the dropdown menu.
*   **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person.
*   **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, optional): Enter your company's address if necessary.
*   **Request** (`request`, text, optional): Describe your marketing and public relations request.
*   **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information related to your request.
*   **Submit** (`submit`, text, not required): Click this button to finalize your request.
