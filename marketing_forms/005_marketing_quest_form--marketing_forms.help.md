# Marketing Quest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for marketing-related purposes, such as tracking events, workshops, or other marketing activities. The form may be used to gather contact information, event details, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the **name** field.
2. Enter your email address in the **email** field.
3. Fill in your phone number in the **phone** field.
4. Select your workshop preferences from the **workshop** field.
5. Choose whether you are attending an **event**.
6. Select which graphics you would like to see or use from the **graphic** field.
7. Enter the website related to your activity (e.g., a website for your workshop or event) in the **website** field.
8. Indicate whether you would like to **update** information.
9. Choose whether you would like to **grant** access to your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name**: Enter your full name to help us identify you.
* **email**: Enter your email address to allow us to contact you about marketing activities.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number to allow us to contact you about marketing activities.
* **workshop**: Select which workshops you are interested in attending or participating in.
* **event**: Choose whether you are attending an event or not.
* **graphic**: Select which graphics you would like to see or use.
* **website**: Enter the website related to your activity (e.g., a website for your workshop or event).
* **update**: Indicate whether you would like to update information.
* **grant**: Choose whether you would like to grant access to your information.
