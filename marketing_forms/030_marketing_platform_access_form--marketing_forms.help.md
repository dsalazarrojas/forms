## Purpose

The Marketing Platform Access Form is designed to provide users and administrators with access to the marketing platform. This form helps in streamlining the access creation process and ensuring that users with different levels of access can be managed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start, fill in your user details in the User Details section. This includes providing your name and other necessary information.
2. Next, select your access level from the options provided. This will determine what features and tools you will have access to.
3. Enter your email address in the Email field. This is necessary for communication and authentication purposes.
4. Enter your phone number in the Phone field. This will be used for communication and other purposes.
5. In the Marketing Tools section, choose the tools you wish to access. This could include Google Ads, Facebook Ads, or other marketing tools.
6. Confirm your access request in the Confirm Access section. Select Accept to proceed with your access request.
7. Finally, if you have any additional notes or information, you can provide them in the Additional Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details (Text)**: This field is where you provide your name and other relevant information.
* **Select Access Level (Select One)**: Choose your access level from the options provided. This will determine what features and tools you will have access to.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address accurately. This is necessary for communication and authentication purposes.
* **Phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number in this field.
* **Marketing Tools (Select Multiple)**: Choose the marketing tools you wish to access. This could include Google Ads, Facebook Ads, or other marketing tools.
* **Confirm Access (Select One)**: Confirm that you wish to access the platform. Select Accept to proceed with your access request.
* **Additional Notes (Note)**: If you have any additional notes or information, you can provide them in this field.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out each field accurately and correctly.
* Double-check your selection in the Select Access Level and Marketing Tools fields.
* Be aware of the access level you select and the implications it has on your features and tool access.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the platform administrator for assistance.
