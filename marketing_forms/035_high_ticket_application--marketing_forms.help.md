# high_ticket_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for submitting applications for high-value services.

## How To Complete This Form
To submit a high-ticket application, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Contact Name.
2. Enter your Email address.
3. Enter your Phone number.
4. Select the Date and Time for the required service.
5. Write a Message describing your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, required: false): Enter your full name to help us identify you and communicate with you effectively.
* **Email** (`email`, required: false): Enter your email address to receive updates and notifications about your application.
* **Phone** (`phone`, required: false): Provide your phone number to allow us to contact you via phone (if needed).
* **Date** (`date`, required: false): Choose the date you require the service, keeping in mind that we can accommodate your requested date, subject to availability.
* **Time** (`time`, required: false): Select the time for the required service, ensuring it fits within your schedule and our availability.
* **Message** (`message`, required: false): Describe the details of your application to help us understand your requirements better.
