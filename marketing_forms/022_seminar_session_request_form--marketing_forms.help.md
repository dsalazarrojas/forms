# Seminar Session Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for seminar session requests. Please ensure that your request is accurate and complete to help us serve your needs better.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of session you are requesting (Seminar, Workshop, or Lecture).
2.  Provide a brief description of the session topic (Session Details).
3.  Choose a date and time for the session.
4.  Specify the location of the session.
5.  Indicate the session duration in minutes.
6.  Indicate your preferences for the session.
7.  Enter your email address so we can contact you back.
8.  Provide your phone number for easy communication.
9.  Add any additional notes about your session request.
10.  If applicable, specify the tool you need for the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Session Type**: Choose the type of session you are requesting: Seminar, Workshop, or Lecture.
*   **Session Details**: Briefly describe the topic of your session request.
*   **Date**: Select a date for the session.
*   **Time**: Specify the time for the session.
*   **Location**: Indicate the location where the session will take place.
*   **Duration**: Specify the duration of the session in minutes.
*   **Preferences**: Indicate your preferences for the session (yes or no).
*   **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you back.
*   **Phone**: Provide your phone number for easy communication.
*   **Additional Notes**: Add any additional notes or information about your session request.
*   **Assigned Tool**: If you need to specify a particular tool for the session, enter it here.
*   **Category**: Indicate whether this session is a yes or no category.
*   **Form Ids**: If this is part of a larger form, enter the relevant form IDs here.
*   **Output File**: If you need to specify an output file, enter it here.

## Tips

*   Be accurate and complete in your session request to ensure we can serve your needs better.
*   Use separate forms for multiple session requests.
*   Double-check your details for spelling and formatting errors.
*   Ensure your email and phone number are correct to facilitate communication.
