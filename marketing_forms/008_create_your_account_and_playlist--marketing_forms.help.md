# create_your_account_and_playlist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your account and playlist details for marketing purposes. Please fill out this form to create your account and provide necessary details about your playlist.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name on the first page.
2. Enter your Email address on the second page.
3. Provide details about your Playlist on the third page. This is a free-form text field where you can describe your playlist in your own words.
4. Select the media platforms where you have an account (e.g., Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, YouTube) on the fourth page.
5. Choose the Creator Community you belong to (e.g., Twitch, TikTok, TikTok Live, Discord) on the fifth page.
6. Enter the date for your Onboarding Details on the sixth page.
7. (Optional) Select the time for your Early Engagement on the seventh page.
8. (Optional) Provide any personalization details on the eighth page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`account_details`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Email** (`email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Playlist Details** (`playlist_details`, `note`, required): Describe your playlist in your own words.
* **Media Platforms** (`media_platforms`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all media platforms where you have an account (e.g., Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, YouTube).
* **Creator Community** (`creator_community`, `select_one`, required): Choose the community you belong to (e.g., Twitch, TikTok, TikTok Live, Discord).
* **Onboarding Details** (`onboarding_details`, `date`, required): Enter the date for your onboarding details.
* **Early Engagement** (`early_engagement`, `time`, optional): (Optional) Select the time for your early engagement.
* **Personalization** (`personalization`, `note`, optional): (Optional) Provide any personalization details.
