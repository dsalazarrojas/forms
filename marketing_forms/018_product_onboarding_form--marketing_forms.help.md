# Product Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from customers to ensure a smooth onboarding process for our product. Please fill out this form to help us understand your company and product requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your company and product information.
2. Select the relevant options for each field as per your requirements.
3. Review your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the person submitting this form.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to help us contact you for further questions or clarification.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number to help us contact you.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for us to contact you or your team.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter your company name if you have one.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, text, optional): Enter your company website URL.
* **Role** (`role`, select one, optional): Indicate if you're a customer representative, sales, account management, or IT representative.
* **Select a role** (`role_options`, select multiple, optional): Select all the applicable roles for you and/or your team.
* **Preferred Communication Channel** (`preferred_communication_channel`, select one, required): Choose how you prefer to communicate with us (phone, email, in-app messages, or other).
* **Timezone** (`timezone`, select one, required): Select your timezone from the provided options.
* **Select a timezone** (`timezone_options`, select multiple, required): Select all applicable timezones for your team.
* **Product Details** (`product_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about the product you're onboarding.
* **Product Preferences** (`product_preferences`, text, optional): Enter any preferences you have for the product.
* **Select product preferences** (`product_preferences_options`, select multiple, required): Select all applicable product preferences for your company.
* **Product Use Case** (`product_use_case`, text, optional): Enter the use case for the product.
* **Select product use case** (`product_use_case_options`, select multiple, optional): Select all applicable product use cases for your company.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about the onboarding process.
* **Select additional comments** (`additional_comments_options`, select multiple, optional): Select all applicable additional comments for your company.
* **Product Onboarding Form Comments** (`product_onboarding_form_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments about the onboarding form.
* **Select product onboarding form comments** (`product_onboarding_form_comments_options`, select multiple, optional): Select all applicable comments for the form.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter your company name.
* **Select company name** (`company_name_options`, select multiple, optional): Select all applicable company names for your team.
* **Company Type** (`company_type`, select one, optional): Indicate the type of your company.
* **Select company type** (`company_type_options`, select multiple, optional): Select all applicable company types for your team.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select one, optional): Indicate the size of your company.
* **Select company size** (`company_size_options`, select multiple, optional): Select all applicable company sizes for your team.

## Tips
* Please provide accurate and detailed information to help us understand your needs.
* If you have any questions or clarification required during the onboarding process, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* Review your entries carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
