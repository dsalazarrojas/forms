# Content Creation Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from respondents on the quality and effectiveness of content created by your team. It helps us understand what aspects of the content are working well and what areas need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the date you submitted this feedback.
3. Choose the type of content you reviewed.
4. Answer all the questions about the content's quality, message clarity, visual appeal, tone, engagement, and other attributes.
5. Provide specific suggestions for improvement if you have any.
6. Finally, share your overall satisfaction and willingness to recommend the content to others.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent Name (`respondent_name`, Text, Optional):** Enter your name so we can identify your feedback.
* **Respondent Email (`respondent_email`, Email, Optional):** Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Survey Date (`survey_date`, Date, Required):** Enter the date of your feedback submission.
* **Content Type Reviewed (`content_type_reviewed`, Select One, Required):** Choose the type of content you reviewed (e.g., Blog Post, Social Media Content, etc.).
* **Project Name (`project_name`, Text, Required):** Enter the name of the content project.
* **Content Quality Rating (`content_quality_rating`, Select One, Required):** Rate the overall quality of the content.
* **Message Clarity (`message_clarity`, Select One, Required):** Rate how clear the main message is.
* **Visual Appeal (`visual_appeal`, Select One, Required):** Rate how appealing the visuals are.
* **Tone Appropriateness (`tone_appropriateness`, Select One, Required):** Rate how well the tone aligns with the audience.
* **Engagement Level (`engagement_level`, Select One, Required):** Rate how engaging the content is.
* **Brand Alignment (`brand_alignment`, Select One, Required):** Rate how well the content aligns with your brand identity.
* **Call to Action Effectiveness (`call_to_action_effectiveness`, Select One, Required):** Rate how effective the call to action is.
* **Content Length (`content_length`, Select One, Required):** Rate the length of the content.
* **Would Share (`would_share`, Select One, Required):** Rate how likely you are to share the content.
* **Strengths (`strengths`, Text, Required):** Describe what you liked most about the content.
* **Areas for Improvement (`areas_for_improvement`, Text, Required):** Mention what could be improved.
* **Specific Suggestions (`specific_suggestions`, Text, Optional):** Share any specific suggestions.
* **Overall Satisfaction (`overall_satisfaction`, Select One, Required):** Rate your overall satisfaction.
* **Would Recommend (`would_recommend`, Select One, Required):** Rate how likely you are to recommend the content.
* **Additional Comments (`additional_comments`, Text, Optional):** Share any additional feedback you have.
