# Black Friday Deal Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Black Friday Deal Submission Form is designed for business users to submit their Black Friday deals for potential featured listing on our platform.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the first page of the form with your basic business information.
2. Click "Next" to proceed to the second page.
3. Fill out the deal-specific details on the second page.
4. Review your submission for accuracy and completeness before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter your official business name.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the primary contact person.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your business email address for correspondence.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your direct contact number.
* **Website URL** (`website_url`, `text`, required): Enter a link to your online store.
* **Product Category** (`product_category`, `select_one`, required): Select the main category for your deal.
	+ Electronics
	+ Fashion and Apparel
	+ Home and Kitchen
	+ Beauty and Health
	+ Sports and Fitness
	+ Toys and Kids
	+ Books and Media
	+ Food and Beverage
	+ Services
	+ Other
* **Deal Title** (`deal_title`, `text`, required): Enter a catchy title for your promotion.
* **Deal Description** (`deal_description`, `text`, required): Enter a detailed description of the offer.
* **Discount Type** (`discount_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of discount you are offering.
	+ Percentage Off
	+ Fixed Amount Off
	+ Buy One Get One
	+ Free Gift with Purchase
	+ Free Shipping
	+ Bundle Deal
* **Discount Value** (`discount_value`, `text`, required): Enter the discount percentage or amount.
* **Original Price** (`original_price`, `number`, required): Enter the regular price before discount.
* **Sale Price** (`sale_price`, `number`, required): Enter the price after discount.
* **Deal Start Date** (`deal_start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the start date of the deal.
* **Deal Start Time** (`deal_start_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the start time (leave blank if not applicable).
* **Deal End Date** (`deal_end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the end date of the deal.
* **Deal End Time** (`deal_end_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the end time (leave blank if not applicable).
* **Available Stock Quantity** (`stock_quantity`, `number`, required): Enter the number of available units.
* **Product Image URL** (`product_image_url`, `text`, optional): Enter a link to the main product image.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, `text`, optional): Enter any restrictions or special terms.
* **Interested in Featured Listing** (`featured_listing`, `select_one`, required): Select if you are interested in a premium placement.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any other information for our team.
