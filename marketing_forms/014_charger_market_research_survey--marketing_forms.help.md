# Charger Market Research Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather data on consumer behavior and preferences regarding phone chargers. The goal is to understand your demographics, charging habits, and preferences for different charger types, brands, and features.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name (Respondent Name) if you feel comfortable sharing it. This is optional.
2. Enter your email address for follow-up surveys and updates.
3. Select your age group from the options provided.
4. Rate your level of tech-savviness on a scale from "Very tech-savvy" to "Not tech-savvy at all".
5. Report how many phone chargers you own.
6. Choose all the types of chargers you own from the options provided (e.g., Wired USB-A, Wireless charging pad, etc.).
7. Select your preference for wireless or wired chargers and choose the reason for your preference.
8. Indicate how often you buy new chargers.
9. Choose a price range you usually spend on chargers.
10. Rate the importance of brand name in your purchasing decisions.
11. Select your favorite brands (if any).
12. Indicate how likely you are to buy aftermarket chargers.
13. Choose the features that matter most to you when selecting a charger.
14. List any frustrations you have with current chargers.
15. Let us know if you would pay more for eco-friendly chargers.
