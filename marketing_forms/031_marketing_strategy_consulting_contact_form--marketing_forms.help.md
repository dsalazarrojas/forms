# marketing_strategy_consulting_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a contact form for marketing strategy consulting inquiries. It collects contact information and a brief message from users interested in learning more about your marketing strategy consulting services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the first field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the second field.
3. Provide your Email address in the third field, formatted as a standard email address (e.g., `john.doe@example.com`).
4. Enter your Phone number in the fourth field, including the area code and the number (e.g., `123-456-7890`).
5. If you're representing a company, enter the name of your company in the fifth field.
6. Finally, type a brief message in the sixth field to let us know how we can assist you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your personal name as you would like it to be displayed in future communications.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your surname as you would like it to be displayed in future communications.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): This is where you can enter your work or personal email address. Ensure it's formatted as a standard email address (e.g., `john.doe@example.com`).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number, including the area code and the number (e.g., `123-456-7890`).
* **Company** (`company`, text, required: false): If you're representing a company, enter the full name of your company here.
* **Message** (`message`, text, required: false): Type a brief message explaining your interest in our marketing strategy consulting services. This is your opportunity to let us know how we can assist you.
