# Content Collaboration Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Content Collaboration Consent Form is designed to ensure that all parties involved in content creation are aware of their roles, responsibilities, and expectations. This form helps establish a clear understanding between collaborators, preventing potential misunderstandings or disputes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the name and email of the primary collaborator, who will be the main contact person for the collaboration.
2.  Enter the name and email of the secondary collaborator, who will be contributing to the content.
3.  Enter the name of the organization involved in the collaboration.
4.  Specify the start and end dates of the collaboration period.
5.  Select the type of content being created (e.g., Blog Posts, Video Content, etc.).
6.  Choose who will own the created content (e.g., Primary Collaborator, Secondary Collaborator, etc.).
7.  Determine the usage rights for the content (e.g., Exclusive Use, Non-Exclusive Use, etc.).
8.  Select the distribution channels for the content (e.g., Company Website, Social Media, etc.).
9.  Decide on revenue sharing, if applicable.
10.  Specify how collaborators will be credited.
11.  Determine who has the right to request revisions.
12.  Establish the conditions under which collaboration can be terminated.
13.  Confirm both parties agree to maintain confidentiality.
14.  Specify the method for resolving disputes.
15.  Confirm your consent to this collaboration agreement.
16.  Sign and date the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Primary Collaborator Name** (`primary_collaborator_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the primary content creator.
* **Primary Collaborator Email** (`primary_collaborator_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the primary collaborator.
* **Secondary Collaborator Name** (`secondary_collaborator_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the collaborating party.
* **Secondary Collaborator Email** (`secondary_collaborator_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the secondary collaborator.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the organization or company involved.
* **Collaboration Start Date** (`collaboration_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the collaboration period.
* **Collaboration End Date** (`collaboration_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the collaboration period, if applicable.
* **Type of Content** (`content_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of content being created (e.g., Blog Posts, Video Content, etc.).
* **Content Ownership** (`content_ownership`, select_one, required): Choose who will own the created content (e.g., Primary Collaborator, Secondary Collaborator, etc.).
* **Usage Rights** (`usage_rights`, select_one, required): Select the usage rights for the content (e.g., Exclusive Use, Non-Exclusive Use, etc.).
* **Distribution Channels** (`distribution_channels`, select_multiple, required): Select the distribution channels for the content (e.g., Company Website, Social Media, etc.).
* **Revenue Sharing** (`revenue_sharing`, select_one, required): Decide on revenue sharing, if applicable.
* **Revenue Split Percentage** (`revenue_split`, text, optional): Enter the revenue split percentage, if applicable.
* **Credit Attribution** (`credit_attribution`, text, required): Specify how collaborators will be credited.
* **Revision Rights** (`revision_rights`, select_one, required): Determine who has the right to request revisions.
* **Termination Clause** (`termination_clause`, text, required): Establish the conditions under which collaboration can be terminated.
* **Confidentiality Agreement** (`confidentiality_agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm both parties agree to maintain confidentiality.
* **Dispute Resolution Method** (`dispute_resolution`, select_one, required): Specify the method for resolving disputes.
* **Consent Given** (`consent_given`, select_one, required): Confirm your consent to this collaboration agreement.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Date of agreement.
* **Additional Terms** (`additional_terms`, text, optional): Enter any other terms or conditions.
