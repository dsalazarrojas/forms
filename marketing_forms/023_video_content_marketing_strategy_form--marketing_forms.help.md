# Video Content Marketing Strategy Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by marketing teams to document and strategize the creation and distribution of video content. It helps you think critically about the target audience, content type, marketing channel, and other key aspects of your video content marketing strategy.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form thoroughly, but only the fields that are required by your specific use case.
2. Choose the appropriate options for each field based on your team's current knowledge and research.
3. Review and revise your answers to ensure they are accurate and consistent with your marketing goals.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Video Content Marketing Strategy Form (id: 1)**:
  The title of this form is self-explanatory - it's the name of the form itself. Please don't change it.
* **target audience (id: 2)**:
  Please select the most relevant option that describes your target audience. This could be a specific age group, industry, or geographic region.
* **content type (id: 3)**:
  Choose all the options that apply to the type of content you plan to create. This could include videos for social media, product demos, explainer videos, etc.
* **marketing channel (id: 4)**:
  Select all the options that apply to the channels through which you plan to distribute your content. This could include social media, email, blog, or paid advertising.
* **marketing message (id: 5)**:
  This field is a text input for any additional marketing message or tagline you'd like to associate with your content.
* **content delivery (id: 6)**:
  Enter the date you plan to release your content. This helps you schedule and coordinate with your team.
* **content duration (id: 7)**:
  Enter the length of time your content will be available for viewing. This helps you plan for content updates and revisions.
* **content location (id: 8)**:
  Enter the location where your content will be hosted or distributed. This could be a website, social media platform, or mobile app.
* **content language (id: 9)**:
  Select the languages your content will be available in.
* **content cost (id: 10)**:
  Enter an estimated cost for creating and distributing your content. This helps you plan your budget and resource allocation.

## Tips
* Be honest about your current knowledge and research when filling out this form.
* Review and revise your answers to ensure they align with your marketing goals and strategies.
* Consult with your team to ensure everyone is on the same page with your content marketing strategy.
