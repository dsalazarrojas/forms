# Promotion Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Promotion Entry Form is designed to collect contact information from customers for promotional purposes. This form ensures that customers can provide necessary details quickly and easily for follow-up actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your first name in the customer_name field.
2. Enter your email address in the email_address field. Ensure it's a valid email address.
3. If you'd like to be contacted by phone, enter your phone number in the phone_number field.
4. Choose your preferred channel of communication from the preferred_channel select box. You can select one or multiple options.
5. If you'd like to be contacted via multiple channels, select all applicable options in the message_channel select box.
6. Read and agree to the terms and conditions by checking the i_agree_to_terms_and_conditions field. This is a mandatory step.
7. You can skip the terms field as it is not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name here.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter a valid email address to which you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted via phone.
* **Preferred Channel** (`preferred_channel`, select_one, required): Select your preferred channel of communication. You can only choose one option.
* **Message Channel** (`message_channel`, select_multiple, optional): Select all channels you'd like to be contacted via. You can choose multiple options.
* **Agree to Terms and Conditions** (`i_agree_to_terms_and_conditions`, note, required): This is a mandatory field. Read the terms and conditions, and check the box to agree.
* **Terms** (`terms`, date, optional): This field is not required. You can skip it.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter a valid email address and phone number for effective communication.
* Be cautious while selecting your preferred and message channels, as these determine how you'll be contacted.
* Ensure that you're agreeing to the terms and conditions before submitting the form.
