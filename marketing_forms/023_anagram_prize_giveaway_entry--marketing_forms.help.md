# Anagram Prize Giveaway Entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is intended to assist you in filling out the Anagram Prize Giveaway Entry form. 

## How To Complete This Form
Before filling out the form, make sure you have a valid email address and your answer to the anagram puzzle. The form consists of seven fields, each with a specific label and requirement. Start by filling out the required fields first. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Entrant name**: Enter your full name as it should be used for the prize notification.
* **Email address**: Enter the email address you want us to use to contact you about the prize.
* **Anagram answer**: Enter your answer to the anagram puzzle.
* **How did you hear about the giveaway**: Select how you heard about the giveaway. If you didn't hear about it through any of the provided options, select "Other".
* **Opt in to marketing**: Choose if you want to receive promotional emails from us.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional information you'd like to share with us. This field is optional.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out the required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check your email address for spelling and format errors.
* If you have any issues or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
