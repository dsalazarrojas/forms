# Ad Viewing Activity Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ad Viewing Activity Log Form is used to track and record user activity when viewing ads. This form helps to gather valuable insights into ad performance and optimize ad campaigns for better results.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by entering your observer name to identify yourself as the person logging the view.
2. Select the Ad campaign name to specify the campaign being viewed.
3. Choose the channel where the ad was viewed, such as Web, Mobile app, Social media, or TV.
4. Enter the date and time you viewed the ad.
5. Estimate the number of viewers for the ad.
6. Provide a unique identifier for the ad creative (ad version or creative id).
7. Add any notable notes on the placement and context of the ad.
8. If relevant, provide a link to a screenshot or log of the ad.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Observer name** (`observer_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as the person logging the view.
* **Ad campaign name** (`ad_campaign`, text, required): Choose the name or ID of the campaign being viewed.
* **Channel** (`channel`, select_one, required): Select the platform where the ad was seen, such as Web, Mobile app, Social media, or TV.
* **View date** (`view_date`, date, required): Enter the date you viewed the ad.
* **View time** (`view_time`, time, optional): Enter the approximate time of day you viewed the ad.
* **Audience estimate** (`audience_estimate`, number, optional): Estimate the number of viewers for the ad.
* **Ad version or creative id** (`ad_version`, text, optional): Enter a unique identifier for the ad creative.
* **Notes on placement and context** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any notable notes on the placement and context of the ad.
* **Attachment reference** (`attachment_reference`, text, optional): Provide a link to a screenshot or log of the ad, if relevant.
