# Customer Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers after their visit to a business or service provider. It helps us understand your experience and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Share your comments about your experience in the "Comments" field.
5. Rate our customer service by selecting an option from the "Customer Service Rating" field.
6. Choose how often you visit this business or service provider in the "Visit Frequency" field.
7. Provide ratings values for our customer service in the "Customer Service Rating Value" fields (fields 14-18).
8. Share any additional comments or ratings in the "Customer Comments" field (field 19).
9. Enter your ratings for each service area in the "Customer Rating" fields (fields 20-25).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Please enter your name so we can associate your feedback with your identity.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number for any follow-up communication.
* **Comments** (`comment`, note, optional): Share any comments or feedback you have about your experience.
* **Customer Service Rating** (`customer_service`, select_one, optional): Rate our customer service as Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Very Poor.
* **Visit Frequency** (`visit_frequency`, select_one, optional): Choose how often you visit this business or service provider (Often, Rarely, Never).
* **Customer Service Rating Value** (`customer_service_rating_value`, number, optional): Enter a value that corresponds to your customer service rating (1-5).
* **Customer Comments** (`customer_comments`, note, optional): Share any additional comments or feedback.
* **Customer Rating 1-6** (`customer_rating_1`, `customer_rating_2`, etc., number, optional): Enter ratings for each service area (1-6).
* **Customer Service Rating Value 1-5** (`customer_service_rating_value_1`, `customer_service_rating_value_2`, etc., text, optional): Enter rating values for each service area (1-5).

Please note that fields 19 and 20 are optional, but we encourage you to provide ratings values for each service area if possible.
