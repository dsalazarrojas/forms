# Dahua Marketing Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dahua Marketing Request form is used to collect marketing requests from customers or internal stakeholders. Please fill out this form to submit your marketing request and our team will review and follow up on your submission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Marketing Request Form** field with your request details.
2. Enter your **Phone** number so we can contact you for further discussion.
3. Provide your **Email** address to ensure we can reply to your submission.
4. Choose a **Date** for your marketing request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Marketing Request Form** (marketing_form_1, text, required): This field is where you can enter your marketing request. Please be as specific as possible with your request to help us understand your needs better.
* **Phone** (marketing_form_2, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted about your request, please enter your phone number.
* **Email** (marketing_form_3, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can reply to your submission.
* **Date** (marketing_form_4, date, optional): Select a date for your marketing request. This will help us schedule the follow-up process.
