# website_call_to_action_poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The website_call_to_action_poll form is designed to gather information from users about their interest in participating in a poll about website call-to-actions. This form is used for marketing research and analysis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form page by page, starting from **website_call_to_action_poll_page_1**.
2. For each page, answer the questions to the best of your abilities.
3. Review each page carefully before proceeding to the next one.
4. Once you have filled out all pages, submit the form.
