# cross_promotion_flyer_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The cross-promotion flyer order form is used to collect information from clients for marketing materials orders. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, click on the "Submit" button when you are ready to send your order request.
2. Fill out the required fields with your contact information.
3. Select the type of promotional material you are requesting.
4. Provide the desired delivery date for the promotional materials.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address. Please use a valid email address.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter your company name.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter your department name.
* **Role** (`role`, text, optional): Enter your role or position in the company.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location of your company.
* **Request Type** (`request_type`, select one, optional): Select the type of promotional material you wish to order, such as Flyer, Brochure, or Catalog.
* **Requested** (`requested_at`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to receive the promotional materials.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to receive the promotional materials.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or special requests you have for the promotional materials.
