# Familiarity Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their familiarity with our product or service. It's a quick and easy way for customers to provide insights into how well they know our product or service and what we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the prompts and answer the questions as they appear. You can choose from the provided options or provide a text response if required. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask and we'll do our best to help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **First Name** (<type:text>): Your name is requested to ensure that we can address any follow-up questions or provide feedback to you directly.
### 2. **Email** (<type:email>): Enter your email to enable us to contact you with follow-up questions or to provide updates about our product or service.
### 3. **How long have you been a customer?** (<type:select_one>): Choose one of the options to indicate how long you have been a customer.
### 4. **How did you hear about us?** (<type:select_multiple>): Select one or more of the options that best describe how you heard about us.
### 5. **How would you rate your familiarity with our product or service?** (<type:select_one>): Choose the option that best describes your familiarity with our product or service.
### 6. **How do you rate our product or service?** (<type:select_one>): Select the option that best describes your experience with our product or service.
### 7. **Would you recommend us to friends and family?** (<type:select_one>): Choose 'True' if you would recommend us to friends and family.
### 8. **What do you like most about our product or service?** (<type:text>): Provide a brief text response about what you like most about our product or service.
### 9. **What do you dislike most about our product or service?** (<type:text>): If you have any concerns or dislikes, provide a brief text response.
### 10. **Comments** (<type:text>): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them here.
### 11. **How might we improve?** (<type:text>): If you have any ideas for how we can improve our product or service, please provide them here.
### 12. **Would you like to receive news and updates?** (<type:select_one>): Choose 'True' if you would like to receive news and updates from us.

Note that some fields are not required, and you can choose to skip them if you don't feel comfortable providing the information. However, please keep in mind that providing as much information as possible will help us better understand your feedback.
