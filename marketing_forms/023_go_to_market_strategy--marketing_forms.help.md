# Go To Market Strategy - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help businesses and product teams develop a comprehensive go-to-market strategy for their product or service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your product's or service's key information.
2. Review and modify the optional fields as needed to fit your strategy.
3. Make sure to save your completed form for future reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **product-definition** (`product-definition`, `text`, optional): A brief description of the product or service you are going to market with. This should include its unique features, benefits, and competitive differentiators.
* **strategic-goals** (`strategic-goals`, `text`, optional): Outline your company's strategic goals for introducing this product or service to the market.
* **market-identification** (`market-identification`, `text`, optional): Describe the specific market segment your product or service is targeting.
* **market-strategy** (`market-strategy`, `text`, optional): Outline your overall approach to marketing and selling your product or service.
* **target-audience** (`target-audience`, `text`, optional): Define the specific audience you are trying to reach with your product or service, including demographics, interests, and behaviors.
* **pricing** (`pricing`, `text`, optional): Determine the pricing strategy for your product or service, including any discounts, promotions, or bundling offers.
* **marketing-channels** (`marketing-channels`, `text`, optional): Identify the marketing channels you plan to use to reach your target audience, such as social media, email, or trade shows.
