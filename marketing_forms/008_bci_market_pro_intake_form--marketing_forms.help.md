# BCI Market Pro Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The BCI Market Pro Intake Form is a crucial tool used to gather information about your company and marketing goals. This form helps our team understand your business needs and provide you with the best possible marketing services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with your company's and contact's information.
2. Select the relevant options for your company's industry type, company size, current marketing spend, marketing goals, marketing channels, marketing services needed, and project timeline.
3. Be as specific as possible when describing your target audience and current marketing challenges.
4. Ensure you select all relevant marketing services you're interested in and provide your expected start date for the project.
5. If you've worked with marketing agencies before, please mention that.
6. Lastly, provide your preferred metrics for measuring success and add any additional information that might help us better understand your marketing needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter your company's legal business name.
* **Primary Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of your primary contact person.
* **Contact Title** (`contact_title`, text, required): Enter your contact's job title or position.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your company's business email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your direct phone number.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, text, optional): Enter your company's website URL (optional, but recommended).
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, required): Enter your company's full address.
* **Industry Type** (`industry_type`, select_one, required): Select your primary industry sector. 
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one, required): Select the number of employees in your company.
* **Current Monthly Marketing Budget** (`current_marketing_spend`, select_one, required): Select your current marketing budget range.
* **Primary Marketing Goals** (`marketing_goals`, select_multiple, required): Select your main marketing objectives. 
* **Target Audience Description** (`target_audience`, text, required): Describe your ideal customer in as much detail as possible.
* **Current Marketing Challenges** (`current_challenges`, text, required): Mention any marketing obstacles you're facing.
* **Main Competitors** (`competitors`, text, optional): List your top competitors (optional but recommended).
* **Current Marketing Channels** (`marketing_channels`, select_multiple, required): Select all channels you currently use for marketing.
* **Marketing Services Needed** (`services_needed`, select_multiple, required): Select all marketing services you're interested in.
* **Project Timeline** (`timeline`, select_one, required): Select when you want to start the project.
* **Previously Worked with Marketing Agencies** (`previous_agencies`, select_one, required): Check if you've worked with marketing agencies before.
* **How Do You Measure Success** (`success_metrics`, text, optional): Mention your preferred metrics for measuring success.
* **Additional Notes or Questions** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional information that might help us better serve you.
