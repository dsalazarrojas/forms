# promotional_offer_enrollment_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect consent from users for promotional offers. It is a one-page form that captures basic contact information and promotional preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your **Email** address to receive promotional offers.
3. If you are associated with a company, provide your **Company Name** and **Job Title** for better communication.
4. Select your **Role** within the company (e.g., Sales, Marketing, Customer Support).
5. Choose your **Company Size** (e.g., Small, Medium, Large).
6. Select the **Company Type** (e.g., Private, Public, Partnership).
7. Check which **Promotional Offers** you are interested in (e.g., Newsletter, Special Offers, Loyalty Program).
8. Confirm your **Consent** to receive promotional offers by selecting the correct option.
9. Enter any additional **Comments** you may have.
10. If required, provide your **Date of Birth**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address to receive promotional offers.
- **Company** (`company`, text, required: false): If you are associated with a company, enter it here.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): Enter your job title within the company.
- **Role** (`role`, select_one, required: false): Select your role within the company (e.g., Sales, Marketing, Customer Support).
- **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_multiple, required: true): Select your company size (e.g., Small, Medium, Large).
- **Company Type** (`company_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Select your company type (e.g., Private, Public, Partnership).
- **Promotional Offers** (`promotional_offers`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose which promotional offers you are interested in (e.g., Newsletter, Special Offers, Loyalty Program).
- **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required: true): Confirm your consent to receive promotional offers.
- **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments you have.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): If required, enter your date of birth.

## Tips
- Please ensure that your email address is accurate to receive promotional offers.
- Be specific when selecting your company size and type to ensure accurate targeting of promotional offers.
- Confirm your consent to receive promotional offers before submitting the form.
- The Date of Birth field is optional and only required if it's a condition of promotion or service.
