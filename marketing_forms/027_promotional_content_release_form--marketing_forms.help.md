# Promotional Content Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for promotional content releases. Please provide the necessary details for the content you wish to release.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the content you wish to release in the **form_title** field.
2. Provide your **user_info**, such as your name or title.
3. Write a brief description of the content in the **content_description** field.
4. Indicate who owns the content by selecting the appropriate option in the **content_owner** field:
	* Me
	* Family Member
	* Friend
	* Family Friend
5. Add any additional contact information for the content owner in the **contact_info** field.
6. Enter the email address of the content owner in the **email** field.
7. Confirm your consent to release the content by selecting the appropriate option in the **consent** field:
	* True
	* False
8. If applicable, enter the date you wish to release the content on in the **date** field.
9. Add the time you wish to release the content on, if different from 12:00 AM in the **time** field.
10. Add any additional notes in the **notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form_title** (Text, Optional): Enter the title of the content you wish to release.
* **user_info** (Text, Optional): This field is for you to provide your information, such as your name or title.
* **content_description** (Text, Optional): Write a brief description of the content you wish to release.
* **content_owner** (Select One, Optional): Choose who owns the content:
	+ Me
	+ Family Member
	+ Friend
	+ Family Friend
* **contact_info** (Text, Optional): Add any contact information for the content owner, such as a phone number or address.
* **email** (Email, Optional): Enter the email address of the content owner.
* **consent** (Select One, Optional): Confirm your consent to release the content:
	+ True
	+ False
* **date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date you wish to release the content on.
* **time** (Time, Optional): If you wish to release the content on a specific time, enter it here.
* **notes** (Note, Optional): Add any additional notes about the content you wish to release.
