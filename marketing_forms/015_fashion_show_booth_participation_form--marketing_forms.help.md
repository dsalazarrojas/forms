# Fashion Show Booth Participation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from participants of the fashion show, including their personal details, booth information, and contact details. Please only fill out this form if you are participating in the fashion show.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out your first name and last name in the required fields.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. Provide your booth name and description in the "Booth Name" and "Booth Description" fields.
4. Enter the name and email of the contact person responsible for your booth in the "Contact Person" and "Contact Email" fields.
5. Add your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
6. Review your information and submit the form to complete your participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (Text, Required): Fill out your first name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Last Name** (Text, Required): Fill out your last name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Email Address** (Email, Required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for communication and updates.
- **Booth Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of your booth at the fashion show.
- **Booth Description** (Text, Optional): Provide a brief description of your booth and the products/services it will be showcasing.
- **Contact Person** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the person responsible for your booth.
- **Contact Email** (Email, Optional): Enter the email address of the person responsible for your booth.
- **Phone Number** (Text, Optional): Enter a valid phone number where the contact person can be reached.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the fashion show organizers.
* This form is for participants only. If you are not participating in the fashion show, please do not fill out this form.
