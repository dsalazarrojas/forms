# Marketplace Advertising Inquiry Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from advertisers who are interested in advertising on our platform. This form helps our team understand their campaign goals, duration, and other relevant details for review and approval.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Campaign Goals" field with the specific objectives you want to achieve through your ad campaign.
2. Specify the duration of your campaign in the "Campaign Duration" field.
3. Select the current status of your submission in the "Submission Status" field. This can be "pending" if your submission is still being reviewed, "approved" if it has been approved, or "rejected" if it has been declined.
4. You can optionally fill in the "Form Details" field with any additional information about your campaign.
5. You can optionally fill in the "Contact Preferences" field to specify how you would like to be contacted.
6. Once you have filled out all the required fields, click the submit button to send your submission to our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Campaign Goals** (`campaign_goals`, `text`, required): Describe the specific objectives you want to achieve through your ad campaign.
* **Campaign Duration** (`campaign_duration`, `number`, required): Specify the duration of your campaign in days or weeks.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the current status of your submission. This can be "pending" if your submission is still being reviewed, "approved" if it has been approved, or "rejected" if it has been declined.
* **Form Submission** (no label, `note`, optional): This is an optional field where you can provide any additional information about your submission.
* **Advertiser Details** (`advertiser_details`, `text`, optional): This is an optional field where you can provide any additional information about yourself as the advertiser.
* **Form Submission** (no label, `text`, optional): This is an optional field where you can provide any additional information about your submission.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields to ensure that your submission is complete and can be reviewed by our team.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, you can contact our support team for help.
* Our team will review your submission and contact you based on your contact preferences.
