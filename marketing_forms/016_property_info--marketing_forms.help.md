# Property Info - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Property Info" form is designed to collect property-related information from users. This form is used to gather details about a specific property, including its address, type, price, number of bedrooms and bathrooms, square footage, and location. This information will be used for internal purposes only and will not be shared publicly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the address of the property in the "Address" field.
2. Select the type of property (Apartment, House, Land, Office, or Other) from the "Property Type" dropdown menu.
3. Enter the price of the property in the "Price" field.
4. Enter the number of bedrooms in the "Bedrooms" field.
5. Enter the number of bathrooms in the "Bathrooms" field.
6. Enter the square footage of the property in the "Square Footage" field.
7. Enter the location of the property in the "Location" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter the address of the property, including the street number and name, city, and state.
- **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of property from the dropdown menu (Apartment, House, Land, Office, or Other).
- **Price** (`price`, number, required): Enter the price of the property.
- **Bedrooms** (`bedrooms`, number, required): Enter the number of bedrooms in the property.
- **Bathrooms** (`bathrooms`, number, required): Enter the number of bathrooms in the property.
- **Square Footage** (`square_footage`, number, required): Enter the square footage of the property.
- **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter the location of the property, including the city, state, and ZIP code.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure that the property details are correct.
- If the property type is "Other", please provide a brief description of the property type.
- If you have any questions or need help, contact us at [support email/phone number].
