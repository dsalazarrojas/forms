# Digital Artist Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Artist Booking Form is a tool used to gather information from digital artists who want to book a session with our company. Please fill out this form to provide us with the necessary details about yourself and your work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out each field with accurate and complete information.
* Make sure to select the correct social media platforms you have a presence on.
* Choose the type of media you will be providing for the booking (e.g. video, post, story, live stream, podcast).
* If you have a budget in mind for the media, please enter it.
* Select how you would like to be contacted (email, phone, message, or both).
* If you have any additional comments or notes about your booking, please include them in the "Booking Details" field.
* Finally, select the date you would like to book.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Social Media Platforms**: Select all the platforms where you have a presence (e.g. Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, TikTok, YouTube, Twitch).
* **Media Type**: Choose the type of media you will be creating for the booking (e.g. video, post, story, live stream, podcast).
* **Media Duration**: If you have a specific time frame for the media, enter it here.
* **Media Budget**: If you have a budget in mind for the media, enter it here.
* **Contact Method**: Select how you would like to be contacted (email, phone, message, or both).
* **Artist Agreement**: This field is not applicable for this form, as it seems to be a placeholder for a future feature.
* **Booking Details**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your booking.
* **Booking Date**: Select the date you would like to book.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your booking request is processed correctly.
* If you have any questions or need help with the form, contact us at [your contact email/phone number].
