# Loyalty Rewards Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Loyalty Rewards Form is used to track and manage customer loyalty program rewards. It captures data on customer interactions, rewards status, and balances to provide a comprehensive view of customer engagement and program performance.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all required fields filled in.
2. Fill out the customer's name, email, phone number, and address in the respective fields.
3. Enter the number of visits the customer has made to our store.
4. Select the type of reward the customer is eligible for (Loyalty points, Free Gift, Free Product, or Free Gift Card).
5. Choose the store location the customer visited.
6. Enter the points earned and reward status.
7. Provide the customer's last visit date and first visit date.
8. Enter the reward expiration date.
9. Enter the customer's total visits, reward balance, and reward balance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer's Name** (Text): Enter the customer's full name as they appear on their identification.
* **Customer's Email** (Email): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer's Phone Number** (Text): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Number of Customer Store Visits** (Number): Enter the number of times the customer has visited our store.
* **Reward Type** (Select Multiple): Choose the type of reward the customer is eligible for. Options include:
	+ Loyalty points
	+ Free Gift
	+ Free Product
	+ Free Gift Card
* **Store Location** (Text): Choose the location of the store the customer visited.
* **Points Earned** (Number): Enter the points earned by the customer.
* **Customer's Address** (Text): Enter the customer's address.
* **Reward Status** (Select One): Choose the status of the reward. Options include:
	+ Pending
	+ Awarded
	+ Expired
* **Customer's Last Visit Date** (Date): Enter the date of the customer's last visit.
* **Customer's First Visit Date** (Date): Enter the date of the customer's first visit.
* **Reward Expiration Date** (Date): Enter the date the reward expires.
* **Customer's Total Visits** (Number): Enter the total number of visits the customer has made to our store.
* **Customer's Reward Balance** (Number): Enter the current reward balance of the customer.
* **Customer Total Visits 2** (Number): Enter the total number of visits made by the customer. (Please note: This field is a redundant field. Please enter the same value as the **Customer's Total Visits** field.)
* **Reward Balance** (Number): Enter the current reward balance.
* **Customer's Phone** (Text): Enter the customer's phone number. (Please note: This field may have been a duplicate. Please enter the same value as the **Customer's Phone Number** field.)
* **Store** (Text): Choose the location of the store the customer visited. (Please note: This field may have been a duplicate. Please enter the same value as the **Store Location** field.)
