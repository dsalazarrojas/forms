# Influencer Campaign Quote Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a quote from an influencer for a marketing campaign.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, making sure to provide all required information.
2. Review the form for any errors before submitting it.
3. Submit the form and await further instructions from our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, \*): Please enter your full first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your last name if applicable.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, \*): Enter your valid email address.
* **Confirm Email** (`email_confirmation`, `select_one`, \*): Confirm your email address by selecting 'yes' or 'no' from the dropdown menu.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it here.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, optional): If you are representing a company, enter its name here.
* **Campaign Name** (`campaign_name`, `text`, \*): Enter the name of the campaign you are inquiring about.
* **Budget** (`budget`, `number`, \*): Enter your budget for the campaign.
* **Services Needed** (`services_needed`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the services you need for the campaign, such as video production, graphic design, or content creation.
* **Services Description** (`services_description`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief description of the services you need.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, optional): If you have a start date for the campaign, enter it here.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, `time`, optional): If you have a start time for the campaign, enter it here.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, optional): If you have an end date for the campaign, enter it here.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, `time`, optional): If you have an end time for the campaign, enter it here.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, optional): If the campaign will take place in a specific location, enter it here.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional notes or information about the campaign.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, optional): If you have an assigned tool for the campaign, enter it here.
* **Assigned Team** (`assigned_team`, `text`, optional): If you have an assigned team for the campaign, enter it here.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, `text`, optional): The person who created the campaign.
* **Created Date** (`created_date`, `text`, optional): The date the campaign was created.
* **Submited** (`submitted`, `select_one`, optional): Confirm if you have already submitted the form.
* **Updated** (`updated`, `select_one`, optional): Confirm if the form has already been updated.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `text`, optional): The person who submitted the form.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, `text`, optional): The person who updated the form.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, `text`, optional): A unique identifier for the form. 

## Tips
1. Make sure you have all necessary information before filling out the form.
2. Review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors.
3. If you are unsure about any field, contact our support team for assistance.
