# welcome_to_your_hc_color_street_wishlist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal details and preferences for an individual's profile, which can be used for marketing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email** in the first three fields.
2. Enter your **Phone Number**, **Date of Birth**, and **Time of Day** in the next three fields.
3. Choose your **Color Preference** by selecting one or multiple options from the dropdown list.
4. Enter your **Street Address**, **City**, and **Zip Code** in the next three fields.
5. Select your **State** and **Country** from the dropdown lists.
6. Choose your **Favorite Food**, **Favorite Drink**, and **Favorite Activity** from the dropdown lists.
7. Select your **First Language**, **Middle Language**, **Last Language**, and **Current Language** from the dropdown lists.
8. Choose your **Primary Education**, **Secondary Education**, and **Current Education** from the dropdown lists.
9. Select your **Highest Education** level from the dropdown list.
10. Choose your **Favorite Color** from the dropdown list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Time of Day** (Time, Required): Enter your time of day in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Color Preference** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose one or multiple colors that you prefer.
* **Street Address** (Text, Required): Enter your street address.
* **City** (Text, Required): Enter your city name.
* **Zip Code** (Number, Required): Enter your zip code.
* **State** (Select One, Required): Select your state from the dropdown list.
* **Country** (Select One, Optional): Select your country of origin.
* **Favorite Food** (Select One, Optional): Choose your favorite food from the dropdown list.
* **Favorite Drink** (Select One, Optional): Choose your favorite drink from the dropdown list.
* **Favorite Activity** (Select One, Optional): Choose your favorite activity from the dropdown list.
* **First Language** (Select One, Optional): Select your first language from the dropdown list.
* **Middle Language** (Select One, Optional): Select your middle language from the dropdown list.
* **Last Language** (Select One, Optional): Select your last language from the dropdown list.
* **Current Language** (Select One, Optional): Select your current language from the dropdown list.
* **Primary Education** (Select One, Optional): Choose your primary education level from the dropdown list.
* **Secondary Education** (Select One, Optional): Choose your secondary education level from the dropdown list.
* **Current Education** (Select One, Optional): Choose your current education level from the dropdown list.
* **Highest Education** (Select One, Optional): Choose your highest education level from the dropdown list.
* **Favorite Color** (Select One, Optional): Choose your favorite color from the dropdown list.
* **Notes** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about yourself.

Note: Please ensure that you fill in all the required fields and submit the form accurately.
