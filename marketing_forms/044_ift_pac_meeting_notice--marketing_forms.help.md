# IFT_PAC_Meeting_Notice - Help Guide

## Purpose
The IFT_PAC_Meeting_Notice form is used to collect and store meeting details for internal or external communication. It helps meeting organizers create and manage meeting notices, including the date, time, location, purpose, attendees, and reminders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields as per your meeting details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date of the meeting. This field is not required, so you can leave it blank if not applicable.
* **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, Time, Optional): Enter the time of the meeting. This field is also not required.
* **Location** (`location`, Text, Optional): Type in the meeting location. If there are multiple locations, you can enter them as needed.
* **Organizer** (`organizer`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the meeting organizer or contact person.
* **Purpose** (`purpose`, Note, Optional): Type in the purpose of the meeting. This field allows for longer text inputs.
* **Attendees** (`attendees`, Select Multiple, Optional): Check the boxes for who will be attending the meeting. Select "Yes" for attendees and "No" for those not attending.
* **Reminders** (`reminders`, Select Multiple, Optional): Check the boxes for reminder options. Select "Yes" if you want to send reminders and "No" if not.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter the email address for the meeting notice. If no email is required, leave this field blank.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required and optional fields as per your meeting details.
* Check the "Attendees" and "Reminders" fields carefully to ensure accuracy.
