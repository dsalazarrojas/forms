# Lifestyle Anniversary Giveaway Entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lifestyle Anniversary Giveaway Entry form is designed to gather information about your relationship history and preferences to create targeted marketing campaigns or to better understand your behavior in the context of a specific product or service.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your email address in the "User Email" field.
2. Enter your first and last name in the "first-name" and "last-name" fields, respectively.
3. If you want to share your phone number, enter it in the "User Phone" field.
4. Fill in your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
5. Enter the anniversary date and year for your relationship in the "Anniversary Date" and "Anniversary Year" fields.
6. If you'd like to provide a hint for your anniversary year, enter it in the "Anniversary Year Hint" field.
7. Select the gift you purchased for your partner from the "Gift Purchased" options.
8. Choose how you heard about the giveaway from the "How Did You Hear" options.
9. Enter your birthday in the "Birthday" field.
10. Enter your location in the "User Location" field.
11. Enter your occupation in the "User Occupation" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Email** (User Email, email, required: false): Enter your email address that you use to access your account.
* **first-name** (first-name, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **last-name** (last-name, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **User Phone** (User Phone, text, required: false): Enter your phone number if you want to share it.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required: false): Fill in your date of birth.
* **Anniversary Date** (Anniversary Date, date, required: false): Enter the date of your anniversary.
* **Anniversary Year** (Anniversary Year, number, required: false): Enter the year of your anniversary.
* **Anniversary Year Hint** (Anniversary Year Hint, text, required: false): If you want to provide a hint, enter it here.
* **Gift Purchased** (Gift Purchased, select_one, required: false): Select the gift you purchased for your partner from the options.
* **How Did You Hear** (How Did You Hear, select_multiple, required: false): Choose how you heard about the giveaway from the options.
* **Birthday** (Birthday, date, required: false): Enter your birthday.
* **User Location** (User Location, text, required: false): Enter your location.
* **User Occupation** (User Occupation, text, required: false): Enter your occupation.
