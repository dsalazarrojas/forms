# Business Directory Form - Jaleshwar - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect business information for inclusion in a directory. The goal is to gather details about a merchant, including their name, registration number, category, description, contact details, location, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Fill in the required fields with accurate information about your business.
2.  Use the text fields to provide clear and concise descriptions of your business and its offerings.
3.  Make sure to include all the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) in the label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Merchant Information --** (`1`, `note`, `false`): This section is a note to introduce the merchant information section.
* **Merchant Name** (`2`, `text`, `true`): Enter the name of your business.
* **Merchant Registration Number** (`3`, `text`, `true`): Enter your business registration number.
* **Business Category** (`4`, `text`, `true`): Enter the type of business you operate.
* **Business Description** (`5`, `text`, `true`): Describe what your business does.
* **-- Contact Details --** (`6`, `note`, `false`): This section is a note to introduce the contact details section.
* **Owner/Proprietor Name** (`7`, `text`, `true`): Enter the full name of the owner or proprietor.
* **Owner Phone Number** (`8`, `text`, `true`): Enter the primary contact phone number.
* **Business Phone** (`9`, `text`, `true`): Enter the business contact number.
* **Email Address** (`10`, `email`, `true`): Enter the business email address.
* **-- Location Details --** (`11`, `note`, `false`): This section is a note to introduce the location details section.
* **Street Address** (`12`, `text`, `true`): Enter the street address of your business.
* **Locality/Neighborhood** (`13`, `text`, `true`): Enter the area or neighborhood name.
* **City** (`14`, `text`, `true`): Enter the city name.
* **District** (`15`, `text`, `true`): Enter the district name.
* **Postal Code** (`16`, `text`, `true`): Enter the PIN code.
* **Region/State** (`17`, `text`, `true`): Enter the state or region.
* **-- Business Profile --** (`18`, `note`, `false`): This section is a note to introduce the business profile section.
* **Products or Services Offered** (`19`, `text`, `true`): List the main products or services offered by your business.
* **Business Hours** (`20`, `text`, `true`): Enter the operating hours of your business.
* **Payment Methods Accepted** (`21`, `text`, `false`): List the payment methods accepted by your business.
* **Website URL** (`22`, `text`, `false`): Enter the website URL of your business if available.
* **Social Media Profile** (`23`, `text`, `false`): Enter the social media profile URL if available.
* **Additional Information** (`24`, `text`, `false`): Enter any other relevant details about your business.
