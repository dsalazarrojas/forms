# digital_marketing_campaign_planning_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide provides an overview of the digital marketing campaign planning template, including its purpose, how to complete it, and a field-by-field explanation of each section.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the 'Campaign Name' field with a descriptive title for your campaign.
2. In the 'Goal' field, briefly describe the objective of your campaign.
3. In the 'Target Audience' field, specify the demographics or characteristics of the people you are targeting.
4. For the 'Channels Used' field, select all the channels you plan to use for your campaign (e.g. social media, email, etc.).
5. In the 'Budget' field, enter a realistic estimate of your campaign's financial resources.
6. Enter the 'Start Date' and 'End Date' fields with the start and end dates of your campaign.
7. In the 'Marketing Channels' field, select all the channels you plan to use for your campaign (e.g. social media, email, etc.).
8. In the 'Marketing Strategy' field, describe the general approach you will take to execute your campaign.
9. In the 'Key Performance Indicators' field, select all the metrics you will use to measure your campaign's success.
10. Finally, in the 'Target Outcomes' field, specify the specific results or outcomes you aim to achieve with your campaign.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Campaign Name** (`title`, `text`, required/optional): A descriptive title for your campaign.
  * Enter a brief and descriptive title for your campaign.
- **Goal** (`goal`, `text`, required/optional): A brief description of your campaign's objective.
  * Enter a brief description of what you aim to achieve with your campaign.
- **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, `text`, required/optional): The demographics or characteristics of the people you are targeting.
  * Specify the age range, location, interests, etc. of your target audience.
- **Channels Used** (`channels_used`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): The channels you plan to use for your campaign.
  * Select all the channels you plan to use, such as social media, email, etc.
- **Budget** (`budget`, `number`, required/optional): A realistic estimate of your campaign's financial resources.
  * Enter a realistic estimate of your campaign's budget.
- **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required/optional): The start date of your campaign.
  * Enter the date when your campaign will begin.
- **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required/optional): The end date of your campaign.
  * Enter the date when your campaign will end.
- **Marketing Channels** (`marketing_channels`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): The channels you plan to use for your campaign.
  * Select all the channels you plan to use, such as social media, email, etc.
- **Marketing Strategy** (`marketing_strategy`, `text`, required/optional): A general approach for executing your campaign.
  * Describe the overall approach you will take to execute your campaign.
- **Key Performance Indicators** (`key_performance_indicators`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): The metrics you will use to measure your campaign's success.
  * Select all the metrics you will use to measure your campaign's performance.
- **Target Outcomes** (`target_outcomes`, `text`, required/optional): The specific results or outcomes you aim to achieve with your campaign.
  * Specify the specific results or outcomes you aim to achieve with your campaign.

## Tips
- Make sure to review and refine your answers to ensure accuracy and completeness.
- Consider consulting with team members or experts if you're unsure about any field.
- Use this form as a starting point and adjust it as needed based on your campaign's evolving needs.
- Save your progress regularly to avoid data loss.
