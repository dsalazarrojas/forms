# Influencer Authenticity Validation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Influencer Authenticity Validation Form is a tool to validate the authenticity of an influencer's audience demographics, engagement metrics, diversity, content types, brand awareness, and favorability. This information helps brands understand their partnerships and make data-driven decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions accurately and honestly. You may need to refer to your social media analytics or other relevant data to provide accurate information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Audience Demographics
* **Audience Demographics**: Please describe your audience demographics, including age group, geographic location, and other relevant details.
* **Age Group**: Select the age group of your audience (13-18, 19-24, 25-34, 35-44, 45-54, or Other).
* **Geographic Location**: Select all the locations where your audience is located (USA, Canada, Mexico, or Other).

### Engagement Metrics
* **Followers count**: Please enter the number of followers for your social media channels.
* **Engagement Rate**: Please enter the engagement rate of your audience (e.g., likes, comments, shares).
* **Reach**: Please enter the reach of your audience (e.g., number of people exposed to your content).
* **Engagement Frequency**: Please enter the engagement frequency of your audience (e.g., number of interactions per week).

### Audience Diversity
* **Audience Diversity**: Please describe your audience diversity, including ethnicity and gender.
* **Audience Ethnicity**: Select the ethnicity of your audience (Asian, Black, Hispanic, White, or Other).
* **Audience Gender**: Select all the genders that represent your audience (Male, Female, or Other).

### Content Type
* **Content Type**: Please select the type of content you create (Video, Photo, or Other).
* **Post Type**: Please select the type of post you create (Post, Live Stream, or Other).
* **Content Duration**: Please enter the duration of your content (e.g., number of minutes).
* **Content Frequency**: Please enter the frequency of your content (e.g., number of posts per week).

### Brand Awareness
* **Brand Awareness**: Please describe your brand awareness, including recognition, top-of-mind, and association.
* **Brand Recognition**: Select the level of brand recognition (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Top-of-Mind**: Select the top-of-mind awareness of your brand (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Brand Association**: Select the association with your brand (Strong, Weak, or None).

### Brand Favorability
* **Brand Favorability**: Please describe your brand favorability, including positive, negative, and neutral sentiments.
* **Brand Positive**: Select the positive sentiment towards your brand (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Brand Negative**: Select the negative sentiment towards your brand (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Brand Neutral**: Select the neutral sentiment towards your brand (High, Medium, or Low).

## Tips
* Please answer all the questions accurately and honestly.
* You may refer to your social media analytics or other relevant data to provide accurate information.
* If you are unsure about any question, please contact us for clarification.
