# Wellness Product Collaboration Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help wellness industry professionals collaborate on new product ideas, share resources, and build relationships. It collects contact and partner information, product details, pricing, and submission workflow preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Provide your contact information**: Enter your name, email, and phone number in the "Contact Information" section.
2. **Share your partner's information**: Enter your partner's name, email, and phone number in the "Partner Information" section.
3. **Describe your product**: In the "Product Details" section, provide a brief description of your product, including its features and benefits.
4. **Outline your pricing strategy**: In the "Pricing Information" section, specify your pricing model, including any discounts or promotions.
5. **Submit your application**: In the "Submission Workflow" section, indicate how you would like to receive feedback or updates on your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Information
* **Name** (Contact Name, <name>, required): Enter your name as it appears on your business card or marketing materials.
* **Email** (Contact Email, <email>, required): Enter your business email address where you can be reached for collaboration opportunities.
* **Phone Number** (Contact Phone, <phone>, required): Enter your primary phone number for communication.
### Partner Information
* **Name** (Partner Name, <name>, required): Enter the name of your partner or collaborator.
* **Email** (Partner Email, <email>, required): Enter your partner's business email address or contact email.
* **Phone Number** (Partner Phone, <phone>, required): Enter your partner's primary phone number for communication.
### Product Details
* **Product Name** (Product Name, <name>, required): Enter a brief description of your product name.
* **Product Description** (Product Description, <text>, required): Describe your product in 1-2 sentences, focusing on its features and benefits.
### Pricing Information
* **Pricing Model** (Pricing Model, <name>, required): Specify your pricing model (e.g., subscription, one-time payment).
* **Discounts or Promotions** (Discounts or Promotions, <name>, optional): Describe any discounts or promotions you offer (e.g., student discount, referral program).
### Submission Workflow
* **Submission Preference** (Submission Preference, <name>, required): Choose how you would like to receive feedback or updates on your application (e.g., email, phone call).
