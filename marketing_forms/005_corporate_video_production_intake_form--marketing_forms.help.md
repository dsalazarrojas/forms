# corporate_video_production_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The corporate video production intake form is designed to collect essential information for video production projects. This form is used to gather project details, team information, and contact details, which are necessary for successful project execution.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you are completing it for a valid reason and that the project does not already exist in the system.
2. Fill out each field with accurate and relevant information.
3. Double-check your responses before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, not required):
	+ A short description of the project.
* **Team Details** (`team_details`, text, not required):
	+ Information about the team members involved in the project.
* **Production Details** (`production_details`, text, not required):
	+ Specific requirements and specifications for the video production.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, not required):
	+ Contact information for the project lead or team members.
