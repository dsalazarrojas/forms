# Video Idea Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Video Idea Log Form is designed to collect and track video ideas submitted by team members. The form helps project managers and team leaders to stay organized and ensure that all team members are on the same page.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Make sure to check for duplicates before submitting new ideas.
3. Provide detailed and accurate information to help project managers and team leaders make informed decisions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Video ID** (`video_id`, text, optional): Enter a unique identifier for the video idea, if you have one.
* **Video Date Submitted** (`video_date_submitted`, date, optional): Enter the date you submitted the video idea.
* **Video Status** (`video_status`, select_one, optional): Select the current status of the video idea (Active, Inactive, Completed).
* **Idea Description** (`idea_description`, text, optional): Describe the video idea in a few sentences.
* **Video Title** (`video_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the video.
* **Video Description** (`video_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the video.
* **Video Keywords** (`video_keywords`, text, optional): Enter relevant keywords for the video.
* **Idea Keywords** (`idea_keywords`, text, optional): Enter keywords related to the idea.
* **Team Contributors** (`team_contributors`, text, optional): List the team members involved in the idea.
* **Idea Status Change Date** (`idea_status_change_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the video status was last changed.
* **Idea Status Change Reason** (`idea_status_change_reason`, text, optional): Enter the reason for the status change.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter the tool assigned to the video idea.
* **Idea Category** (`idea_category`, select_one, optional): Select the category of the video idea (Comedy, Drama, Horror, Thriller, Other).
* **Idea Tags** (`idea_tags`, select_multiple, optional): Select the tags for the video idea (Tag 1, Tag 2, Tag 3, Tag 4).

## Tips

* Make sure to provide detailed and accurate information to help project managers and team leaders make informed decisions.
* Check the form regularly to see if a similar idea has been submitted.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask your team leader or project manager for clarification.
