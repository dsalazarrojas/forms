# Request A Brochure - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Request A Brochure" form is designed to allow customers to request a brochure from our marketing team. This form is used to collect information about the customer's brochure preferences and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the required information before submitting.
2. For each form section, fill in the required fields as prompted. If a field is not marked as required, you can skip it if it's not relevant to your request.
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Request A Brochure Form 1 (Optional)
* **Request A Brochure Form 1** (text, Optional): This field allows you to provide a brief description of the brochure content. Please type in a few sentences to help our marketing team understand your requirements.

### 2. Marketing Forms (Optional)
* **Marketing Forms (Select)** (Select One, Optional): Check yes if you want to receive a physical brochure, no if you don't. This will help us determine how to distribute your brochure.

### 3. Thhhgg (Optional)
* **Thhhgg** (Text, Optional): This field is currently not clearly labeled, but it is intended to ask for any additional comments or notes regarding your brochure request. Please use this space to add any relevant information that may help our marketing team better understand your needs.

### Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate and clear information in each field to help our marketing team create the best brochure possible.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification on any of the fields, please don't hesitate to contact our marketing team.
