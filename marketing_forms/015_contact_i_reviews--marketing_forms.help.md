# Contact I Reviews - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to allow users to contact us for review-related information, submission, or general questions. Please use this form for inquiries that are not urgent or time-sensitive. Our team will review and respond to your message according to your selected response time.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate subject of inquiry (e.g., Review Submission, Review Dispute, etc.) from the dropdown menu.
2. Choose whether your inquiry is related to a specific review or not.
3. Enter your name, email address, and phone number (if applicable).
4. Provide details about your inquiry in the message field.
5. If applicable, attach any supporting documents.
6. Select your preferred response time (if you have a specific time constraint).
7. Choose the best time to contact you.
8. Agree to our privacy policy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (2): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (3): Enter your active email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (4): Enter your primary phone number where we can contact you (optional, but recommended).
* **Subject of Inquiry** (6): Choose a specific subject that best describes the reason for your inquiry (e.g., Review Submission, Review Dispute, etc.).
* **Is This About a Specific Review** (7): Select 'True' if your inquiry is related to a specific review, otherwise select 'False'.
* **Review ID or Reference** (8): If your inquiry is related to a specific review, enter the review ID or reference number (optional).
* **Your Message** (9): Provide detailed information about your inquiry in the text area.
* **Attach Supporting Documents** (10): Attach any relevant documents that support your inquiry (optional).
* **Expected Response Time** (11): Select a response time that suits your needs (e.g., within 24 hours, within 48 hours, etc.).
* **Best Time to Contact** (12): Select the time of day that suits your schedule (e.g., morning, afternoon, etc.).
* **Privacy Policy Consent** (13): Select 'I Agree' to acknowledge our privacy policy.

**Tips**

* Please ensure you enter accurate and up-to-date information to facilitate efficient response time.
* If you have a specific review ID or reference number, please provide it for our reference.
* If you have any attachments, please ensure they are relevant and do not contain any sensitive information.
* Our team will review and respond according to your selected response time. If you select 'No Rush,' we will respond as soon as possible.
