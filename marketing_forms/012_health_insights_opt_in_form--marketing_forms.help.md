# health_insights_opt_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect demographic information and other relevant details from users for health insights purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To begin, read the welcome message on the "Welcome to our health insights form" page.
2. Fill out the form by entering your First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone, Date of Birth, and other details as requested.
3. Ensure you enter accurate information for each field.
4. Once you've completed the form, click the submit button to send your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is commonly known to others. (id: 2, type: text, required: false)
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is commonly known to others. (id: 3, type: text, required: false)
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address. This will be used to communicate with you. (id: 4, type: email, required: false)
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted. (id: 5, type: text, required: false)
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY or DD/MM/YY. (id: 6, type: date, required: false)
* **Resource**: Enter any relevant resources or references that might be helpful for your health insights. (id: 7, type: text, required: false)
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter the tool assigned to you for the health insights process. (id: 8, type: text, required: false)
* **Form IDs**: Enter the ID number for this form. (id: 9, type: text, required: false)
* **Category**: Enter the category for your health insights. (id: 10, type: text, required: false)
* **Description**: Enter a brief description of your health insights. (id: 11, type: text, required: false)
* **Assigned Tool 2**: Enter the second tool assigned to you for health insights. (id: 12, type: text, required: false)
* **Form Ids 2**: Enter the ID number for the second form. (id: 13, type: text, required: false)
* **Category 2**: Enter the category for the second form. (id: 14, type: text, required: false)
* **Description 2**: Enter a brief description of the second form. (id: 15, type: text, required: false)

## Tips
- Ensure you complete all required fields accurately and truthfully.
- Review your information before submitting the form to avoid errors.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
