# Graphic Designer Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a graphic designer from clients, colleagues, or friends. It helps evaluate the designer's performance, skills, and work quality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief message in the "Graphic Designer Referral" field to describe why you are referring this designer.
2. In the "Why are you referring this designer?" field, explain the reason you are recommending the designer.
3. Enter the designer's email address in the "What's the designer's email?" field.
4. If you have the designer's phone number, enter it in the "What's the designer's phone?" field (optional).
5. If you know the designer's company, enter it in the "What's the designer's company?" field (optional).
6. Enter the designer's name in the "What's the designer's name?" field.
7. Enter the designer's job title in the "What's the designer's job title?" field.
8. Describe the project details in the "What were the project details?" field.
9. Answer the question "Is this a good designer?" by selecting one of the provided options.
10. Choose your relationship to the designer from the available options (e.g., client, colleague, friend).
11. If you have any additional comments about the designer, enter them in the "Do you have any other details to share about this designer?" field (optional).
12. Rate the designer's work quality from Excellent, Good, or Fair.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Graphic Designer Referral** (`referral_message`, text, required): Briefly describe why you are referring this designer.
* **Why are you referring this designer?** (`form_1_referral_message`, text, required): Explain the reason you are recommending the designer.
* **What's the designer's email?** (`form_1_email`, email, required): Enter the designer's email address.
* **What's the designer's phone?** (`form_1_phone`, text, optional): Enter the designer's phone number (if available).
* **What's the designer's company?** (`form_1_company`, text, optional): Enter the designer's company name (if known).
* **What's the designer's name?** (`form_1_designer_name`, text, required): Enter the designer's full name.
* **What's the designer's job title?** (`form_1_designer_title`, text, required): Enter the designer's job title.
* **What were the project details?** (`form_1_project_details`, text, required): Describe the project details.
* **Is this a good designer?** (`form_1_referral_recommendation`, checkbox, required): Select one of the provided options to indicate the designer's performance.
* **What's your relationship to the designer?** (`form_1_relationship_to_designer`, select_multiple, required): Choose your relationship to the designer (e.g., client, colleague, friend).
* **Do you have any other details to share about this designer?** (`form_1_additional_comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments about the designer.
* **How do you rate the designer's work?** (`form_1_designer_work_quality`, radio, required): Select the rating for the designer's work quality (Excellent, Good, Fair).
