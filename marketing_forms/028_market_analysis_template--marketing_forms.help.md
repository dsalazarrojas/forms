# Market Analysis Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Market Analysis Template is a form designed to collect and analyze market-related information for a company's products or services. This form is used to gather data on market trends, competitors, and analysis results to help businesses make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Company" field with the name of the company you are analyzing.
2. Enter the name of the product or service you are analyzing in the "Product" field.
3. If applicable, check the box for "Competitors" if you are analyzing multiple competitors.
4. Describe the current market trends in the "Market Trends" field.
5. Write a brief analysis of the market and product in the "Market Analysis" field.
6. Enter your analysis result or conclusion in the "Analysis Result" field.
7. If you have any additional comments or notes, use the "Notes" field to add them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company** (text, required): Enter the name of the company you are analyzing.
* **Product** (text, required): Enter the name of the product or service you are analyzing.
* **Competitors** (select multiple, optional): Check this box if you are analyzing multiple competitors.
* **Market Trends** (text, optional): Describe the current market trends.
* **Market Analysis** (text, optional): Write a brief analysis of the market and product.
* **Analysis Result** (text, optional): Enter your analysis result or conclusion.
* **Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes you have.
