# Content Strategy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your content consumption habits, job roles, and preferences. The collected data will help our content team create targeted and effective content strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Scroll through each page of the form and fill in the required information.
2. Answer each question based on your personal experience and preferences.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please feel free to ask us for clarification.
4. Once you have filled in all required fields, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: About You
- **-- About You --**: This page is an introduction to the form and helps us understand your background information.
- **respondent_profile_header**: This is a note section to gather more about you and your experience.
### Page 2: Job Role and Industry
- **What is your current job role**: Please select your current job role from the options provided.
### Page 3: Content Consumption
- **Which industry do you work in**: Please select the industry you work in from the options provided.
- **When do you usually consume professional content**: Please select when you usually consume professional content from the options provided.
- **Which channels do you use most for learning**: Please select up to 3 channels you use most for learning from the options provided.
- **What content format do you find most valuable**: Please select the content format you find most valuable from the options provided.
### Page 4: Feedback and Suggestions
- **How satisfied are you with our current content**: Please select your level of satisfaction with our current content.
- **What topics would you like us to cover more**: Please provide any topic you feel we should cover more.
- **One thing we could do better with our content**: Please suggest one thing we could do better with our content.
### Page 5: Completion Information
- **Date of Completion**: Please enter the date you completed this form.
### Page 6: Submission
- **Please submit this form to complete the survey.**
