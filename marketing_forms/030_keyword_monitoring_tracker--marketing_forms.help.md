# Keyword Monitoring Tracker - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to monitor and track keywords related to marketing campaigns. It helps users understand the current state of their marketing strategy and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form to understand what information is being collected.
2. Fill in the relevant fields based on your keyword monitoring needs.
3. Select the options that apply to your keyword monitoring preferences.
4. Provide details about your requests, if any.
5. Confirm your keyword monitoring settings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **keyword_monitoring** (`keyword_monitoring`, text, optional): Enter the keyword(s) you want to monitor.
* **alert_preferences** (`alert_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how you want to receive alerts (Yes/No).
* **tracking_preferences** (`tracking_preferences`, select_one, optional): Select how you want to track your keywords (Yes/No).
* **keyword_status** (`keyword_status`, text, optional): Enter the current status of your keyword(s).
* **keyword_value** (`keyword_value`, number, optional): Enter a value for your keyword(s).
* **platform** (`platform`, select_multiple, optional): Select the platform(s) where your keyword(s) are monitored (Yes/No).
* **keyword_type** (`keyword_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of keyword you want to monitor (Yes/No).
* **request_status** (`request_status`, note, optional): Enter any request status updates.
* **request_details** (`request_details`, text, optional): Enter any request details.
* **keyword_monitoring_preferences** (`keyword_monitoring_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your keyword monitoring preferences (Yes/No).
* **request_date** (`request_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your request.
* **keyword_monitoring_type** (`keyword_monitoring_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of keyword monitoring you need.
* **keyword_monitoring_value** (`keyword_monitoring_value`, text, optional): Enter a value for your keyword monitoring.
* **keyword_monitoring_frequency** (`keyword_monitoring_frequency`, text, optional): Enter the frequency of your keyword monitoring.
* **keyword_monitoring_status** (`keyword_monitoring_status`, text, optional): Enter the status of your keyword monitoring.
* **platform_monitoring** (`platform_monitoring`, text, optional): Enter the platform(s) where your keyword(s) are monitored.
* **request_time** (`request_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your request.
* **request_frequency** (`request_frequency`, text, optional): Enter the frequency of your request.
* **keyword_monitoring_alert** (`keyword_monitoring_alert`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how you want to receive keyword monitoring alerts (Yes/No).
* **keyword_monitoring_type_frequency** (`keyword_monitoring_type_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how you want to track your keyword monitoring type frequency (Yes/No).
* **request_frequency_preferences** (`request_frequency_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your request frequency preferences (Yes/No).
* **keyword_monitoring_preferences** (`keyword_monitoring_preferences`, select_one, optional): Select your keyword monitoring preferences (Yes/No).

* **keyword_monitoring_preferences_4** (`keyword_monitoring_preferences_4`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your keyword monitoring preferences 4 (Yes/No).

## Tips
* Please fill in all the necessary fields to ensure accurate tracking and monitoring.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your administrator.
* Regularly review your keyword monitoring progress to stay updated on your marketing strategy.
