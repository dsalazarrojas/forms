# Name Our New Product - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Name Our New Product" form is used to collect information about a new product idea, including submitter details and product data. This form is meant for internal use only.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your company name from the list of options.
3. Provide a brief description of the product idea in the "Product Idea" field.
4. Select the people responsible for submitting the idea in the "Submitter" field.
5. Optionally, enter any additional details in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
* **Company Name** (select_one): Select your company from the list of options.
* **Product Idea** (text): Briefly describe your product idea.
* **Submitter** (select_multiple): Select who submitted the idea, one or multiple people.
* **Date Submitted** (date): Enter the date you submitted the idea.
* **Time Submitted** (time): Enter the time you submitted the idea.
* **Note** (note): Enter any additional details or comments about the product idea.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the product management team for guidance.
* Keep the "Product Idea" field concise and focused on the key aspects of the product.
* Use the "Submitter" field to identify all individuals involved in the submission.
* Review your submission carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.
