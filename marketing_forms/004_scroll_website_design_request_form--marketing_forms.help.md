# Scroll Website Design Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scroll Website Design Request Form is designed to collect information from users who need a website design created. This form helps our team understand your requirements and preferences to create a tailored website design that meets your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields (marked as "required") accurately and thoroughly.
2. Review each field carefully and make sure you have all the necessary information ready.
3. Double-check your inputs before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address to receive updates and communications from us.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number (optional, but recommended for follow-up communications).
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, optional): Enter your company name or affiliation (if applicable).
* **Website** (`website`, `text`, optional): Enter your website URL (if you have one).
* **Message** (`message`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information or requests you have for your website design.
* **Select One Option** (`select_one_option`, `select_one`, required): Choose one option from the available choices.
* **Select Multiple Options** (`select_multiple`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose multiple options from the available choices.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional): Enter a date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD` (if you're unsure, leave it blank).
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): Enter a time in the format `HH:MM` (if you're unsure, leave it blank).
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes for your website design project.
* **Assigned to** (`assigned_to`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person or team member assigned to your project.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required): Enter the tool or software used for your website design project.
* **Category** (`category`, `text`, required): Enter the category of your website design project.
* **Subcategory** (`subcategory`, `text`, optional): Enter the subcategory of your website design project (if applicable).
* **Created At** (`created_at`, `date`, optional): This field is automatically generated and not editable.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, `date`, optional): This field is automatically generated and not editable.
* **Deleted At** (`deleted_at`, `date`, optional): This field is automatically generated and not editable.
* **Status** (`status`, `text`, optional): Enter any status updates or changes (if applicable).
* **Status 1** (`status_1`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional status updates or changes (if applicable).
* **Status 2** (`status_2`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional status updates or changes (if applicable).
* **Status 3** (`status_3`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional status updates or changes (if applicable).
* **Status 4** (`status_4`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional status updates or changes (if applicable).
* **Status 5** (`status_5`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional status updates or changes (if applicable).

Please review each field carefully and make sure to fill out all the required fields to submit the form successfully. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
