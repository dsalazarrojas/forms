# Smart Marketing Service Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Smart Marketing Service Request form is a tool for customers to provide information about their marketing needs and preferences. This form helps the marketing team understand the customer's requirements and tailor their services accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your first name and last name to help the marketing team get in touch with you.
2.  Enter your organization or company name (if applicable) to help the marketing team understand your professional background.
3.  Enter your job title to assist the marketing team in tailoring their approach to your specific role.
4.  Enter a valid email address for the marketing team to contact you.
5.  Provide your phone number so the marketing team can reach out if needed.
6.  Describe your project details to enable the marketing team to offer you the best possible services.
7.  Specify your budget for the project (if known) to help the marketing team plan and price the services accordingly.
8.  Rate your expectations of the marketing strategy (Good, Fair) to inform the marketing team about your preferences.
9.  Choose your goals for the project (Yes, No) to let the marketing team know what to focus on.
10.  Select a follow-up option for the marketing team to schedule meetings or calls.
11.  Provide a start date for the project to help the marketing team plan and schedule services.
12.  Choose an end date for the project to help the marketing team know when to wrap up the project.
13.  Enter the duration of the project to help the marketing team plan and manage their time.
14.  Add any additional notes about your project to assist the marketing team in understanding your needs.
15.  Select a category for your project (Yes, No) to help the marketing team prioritize and categorize your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name to help the marketing team get in touch with you.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your last name (if different from your first name) to aid the marketing team in addressing you correctly.
*   **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, optional): Enter your organization or company name if you are representing a company or institution.
*   **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your job title to help the marketing team tailor their approach to your professional background.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter a valid email address for the marketing team to contact you.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number so the marketing team can reach out if needed.
*   **Project Details** (`project_details`, `text`, required): Describe your project in detail to enable the marketing team to offer you the best possible services.
*   **Budget** (`budget`, `number`, optional): Enter your budget for the project if known to help the marketing team plan and price their services.
*   **Marketing Strategy** (`marketing_strategy`, `select_one`, optional): Rate your expectations of the marketing strategy (Good, Fair) to inform the marketing team about your preferences.
*   **Goals** (`goals`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose your goals for the project (Yes, No) to let the marketing team know what to focus on.
*   **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, `date`, optional): Select a follow-up option for the marketing team to schedule meetings or calls.
*   **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, optional): Provide a start date for the project to help the marketing team plan and schedule services.
*   **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, optional): Choose an end date for the project to help the marketing team know when to wrap up the project.
*   **Duration** (`duration`, `time`, optional): Enter the duration of the project to help the marketing team plan and manage their time.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes about your project to assist the marketing team in understanding your needs.
*   **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, optional): Select a category for your project (Yes, No) to help the marketing team prioritize and categorize your request.
*   **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, optional): Enter your name to confirm and submit the form.
