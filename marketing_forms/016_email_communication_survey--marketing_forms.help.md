# email_communication_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess and analyze email communication by rating and trending various aspects of email performance. It helps marketing professionals gather data and insights to improve email marketing strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and select a rating or trend that best represents your perception.
2. Use a scale of 1-5 for ratings and select one of the three trends (increasing, decreasing, or unchanged) for each question.
3. Answer all required questions to ensure accurate data collection.
4. Review your answers to ensure they accurately reflect your perceptions.
