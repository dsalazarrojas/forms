# Sponsor Promotion Material Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from sponsors to request materials for promotion, such as banners, logos, and other visual elements.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of the material you are requesting (e.g., Sponsorship Material, Other).
2. Provide a brief description of the material you need.
3. Upload any supporting files, if necessary.
4. Specify the date and time you need the material by.
5. Enter your email address and phone number so we can contact you for further information.
6. Add any additional notes or comments about your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, required/optional): 
  Choose the category of the material you are requesting. This will help us understand the type of material you need.
* **Description** (`sponsor_promotion_material_request_form_description`, `text`, required/optional): 
  Provide a brief description of the material you need. This will help us understand the specific requirements.
* **Files** (`sponsor_promotion_material_request_form_files`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): 
  Upload any supporting files, such as images or documents, that will help us understand the material you need.
* **Date** (`sponsor_promotion_material_request_form_date`, `date`, required/optional): 
  Specify the date by which you need the material. This will help us prioritize your request.
* **Time** (`sponsor_promotion_material_request_form_time`, `time`, required/optional): 
  Specify the time by which you need the material. This will help us ensure we can provide it on time.
* **Email** (`sponsor_promotion_material_request_form_email`, `email`, required/optional): 
  Enter your email address so we can contact you with any questions or updates.
* **Phone** (`sponsor_promotion_material_request_form_phone`, `text`, required/optional): 
  Enter your phone number so we can contact you if we need more information.
* **Note** (`sponsor_promotion_material_request_form_note`, `note`, required/optional): 
  Add any additional notes or comments about your request. This will help us understand your specific needs.
