# Share A Testimonial Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Share A Testimonial Form is designed to collect feedback and testimonials from customers, partners, or users about their experiences with our company. This form helps us understand your satisfaction and opinions, which we use to improve our services and products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Share A Testimonial" field on page 1 and enter a brief description of your experience with our company.
2. On page 2, enter a detailed testimonial about your experience. Please be as specific as possible to help us understand your thoughts.
3. On page 3, select "True" to confirm that you accept the publishing terms and conditions. This ensures that your testimonial will be published on our website and marketing materials.
4. You can leave the last field (page 4) empty, as it is not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Share A Testimonial** (`share_a_testimonial_form_page_1`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your experience with our company. Please try to be as concise as possible.
* **Testimonial** (`share_a_testimonial_form_page_2`, text, required): Enter a detailed testimonial about your experience. Please be as specific as possible to help us understand your thoughts.
* **I accept the publishing terms** (`share_a_testimonial_form_page_3`, select_one, optional): Select "True" to confirm that you accept the publishing terms and conditions. This ensures that your testimonial will be published on our website and marketing materials.
* **Testimonial** (`share_a_testimonial_form_page_4`, text, optional): This field is not required, and you can leave it empty.
