# tv_commercial_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from viewers on their experience with TV commercials, including their effectiveness, future marketing strategies, and demographics.

## How To Complete This Form

- To complete this form, simply fill out the questions on each page, providing as much detail as possible.
- Make sure to review each section carefully before submitting the form.
- If you're unsure about any field, you can contact the administrator if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Viewer Insights**: This section provides space for any additional insights or comments you may have about your experience with the TV commercial.
  **Type: text**

- **Advertisement Effectiveness**: Rate how effective you found the commercial to be. Choose 'Yes' if you found it effective or 'No' if not.
  **Type: select_one, Options: 'Yes', 'No'**

- **Future Marketing Strategies**: Indicate how likely you are to consider watching a similar commercial in the future. Choose one or multiple options that apply.
  **Type: select_multiple, Options: 'Good', 'Fair'**

- **Feedback**: Add any additional comments or feedback about the commercial.
  **Type: text**

- **Commercial Rating**: Rate the commercial on a scale.
  **Type: number**

- **Watched**: Indicate the number of times you watched the commercial.
  **Type: number**

- **Age**: Did you find the commercial to be directed at your age group? Choose 'Yes' or 'No'.
  **Type: select_one, Options: 'Yes', 'No'**

- **Time Spent Watching**: Estimate the amount of time you spent watching the commercial.
  **Type: number**

- **Commercial Time**: Indicate how long the commercial was on air.
  **Type: time**

- **Watched Before**: When did you first watch the commercial?
  **Type: date**

- **Watched Again**: When did you watch the commercial again?
  **Type: date**

- **Watched Again Before**: When did you first watch the commercial again?
  **Type: date**

- **Watched Again Count**: Estimate the number of times you watched the commercial again.
  **Type: number**

- **Watched Again Time**: Estimate the amount of time you spent watching the commercial again.
  **Type: number**

- **Commercial Again**: Did you watch the commercial again? Choose 'Yes' or 'No'.
  **Type: select_one, Options: 'Yes', 'No'**

- **Gender**: Was the commercial targeted at your gender? Choose 'Yes' or 'No'.
  **Type: select_one, Options: 'Yes', 'No'**

- **Location**: Was the commercial targeted at your location? Choose 'Yes' or 'No'.
  **Type: select_one, Options: 'Yes', 'No'**

- **Watched Commercial**: Did you watch the commercial? Choose 'Yes' or 'No'.
  **Type: select_one, Options: 'Yes', 'No'**

- **Commercial Type**: Was the commercial a specific type? Choose 'Yes' or 'No'.
  **Type: select_one, Options: 'Yes', 'No'**

- **Feedback Important**: Indicate how important the feedback from the commercial was to you. Choose one or multiple options.
  **Type: select_multiple, Options: 'Very Important', 'Not Important'**

- **Age Group**: Was the commercial targeting an age group? Choose 'Yes' or 'No'.
  **Type: select_one, Options: 'Yes', 'No'**

- **Age Group Category**: Was the commercial targeting a specific age group category? Choose 'Yes' or 'No'.
  **Type: select_one, Options: 'Yes', 'No'**

- **Commercial Length**: Was the commercial of a suitable length? Choose 'Yes' or 'No'.
  **Type: select_one, Options: 'Yes', 'No'**

- **Time Watched**: Did you watch the commercial for a significant amount of time? Choose 'Yes' or 'No'.
  **Type: select_one, Options: 'Yes', 'No'**

- **Watched Commercial**: Did you watch the commercial? Choose 'Yes' or 'No'.
  **Type: select_one, Options: 'Yes', 'No'**
