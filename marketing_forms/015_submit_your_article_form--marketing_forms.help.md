# submit_your_article_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit articles for review, publication, or other purposes. Please fill it out carefully, ensuring you provide accurate information about your article.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field carefully, providing the required information.
2. Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Section 1**: This is the main section where you can provide your article content.
* **Form Section 2**: This section is currently empty and not used. Please ignore it for now.
* **Author Name**: Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed in the article.
* **Article Title**: Enter the title of the article you are submitting.
* **Article Body**: This is the main content of your article. Please provide a clear and concise description or text about your article.

## Tips
- Double-check your submission for accuracy and completeness.
- If you're unsure about any field or the submission process, contact us at form@gic.mx.
