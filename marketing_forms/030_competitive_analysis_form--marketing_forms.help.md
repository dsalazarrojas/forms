## Competitive Analysis Form - Help Guide

### Purpose
The Competitive Analysis Form is designed to gather information for a competitive analysis, providing insights on the competitive intensity, market trends, and strengths and weaknesses of key competitors.

### How To Complete This Form

1.  **Analysis Date**: The date on which the competitive analysis was conducted.
2.  **Analyst Name**: The name of the person conducting the analysis.
3.  **Analyst Email**: The contact email of the person conducting the analysis.
4.  **Department**: The department of the organization conducting the analysis.
5.  **Company Name**: The name of the organization conducting the analysis.
6.  **Industry Sector**: The industry or sector of the market being analyzed.
7.  **Analysis Scope**: The scope of the analysis, such as single competitor, multiple competitors, entire market, specific product category, or geographic market.
8.  **Primary Competitor Name**: The name of the primary competitor.
9.  **Competitor 1 Market Share**: The estimated market share of the primary competitor (optional).
10.  **Competitor 1 Strengths**: The strengths of the primary competitor (e.g., what they do well).
11.  **Competitor 1 Weaknesses**: The weaknesses of the primary competitor (e.g., where they are vulnerable).
12.  **Competitor 1 Products or Services**: The key offerings of the primary competitor.
13.  **Competitor 1 Pricing Strategy**: The pricing strategy of the primary competitor (e.g., premium, above average, market average, below average, low cost leader).
14.  **Secondary Competitor Name**: The name of the secondary competitor (optional).
15.  **Competitor 2 Strengths**: The strengths of the secondary competitor (optional).
16.  **Competitor 2 Weaknesses**: The weaknesses of the secondary competitor (optional).
17.  **Competitor 2 Pricing Strategy**: The pricing strategy of the secondary competitor (optional, not applicable if no secondary competitor is selected).
18.  **Our Market Position**: The position of the organization in the market (e.g., market leader, strong challenger, follower, niche player, new entrant).
19.  **Our Competitive Advantage**: What makes the organization unique and competitive.
20.  **Key Market Trends**: Industry trends affecting competition in the market.
21.  **Customer Preferences**: What customers value in the market.
22.  **Barriers to Entry**: How easy or difficult it is for new competitors to enter the market.
23.  **Threat of Substitutes**: The availability and attractiveness of alternative solutions in the market.
24.  **Competitive Intensity**: The level of competition in the market (e.g., very high, high, moderate, low, very low).
25.  **SWOT Analysis Summary**: A summary of the strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats of the organization and competitors.
26.  **Strategic Recommendations**: Recommended actions for the organization to stay competitive in the market.
27.  **Next Review Date**: The date to review and update the analysis (optional).
28.  **Additional Comments**: Any additional comments or observations about the market analysis.

To complete this form, please provide information on each field as instructed above. This form is designed to help gather comprehensive information for a competitive analysis, providing insights on the competitive intensity, market trends, and strengths and weaknesses of key competitors. 

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed.
* If a field is optional, provide information if relevant to the analysis.
* For select_one fields, select only one option that best describes the situation.
* For text fields, provide a brief description or explanation.
* For date fields, use the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* For email fields, use a valid email address.

By completing this form, you will help in gathering essential information for a comprehensive competitive analysis, providing insights on the competitive landscape of your market.
