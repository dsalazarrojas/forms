# Client Consultation Questionare - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential clients to understand their marketing challenges and interests.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Full Name and Business Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Business Website URL if applicable.
3. Describe your current marketing challenges in the text field.
4. Select the areas of marketing you are interested in (e.g. SEO, Social Media Management, etc.).
5. If applicable, enter your Monthly Marketing Budget in USD.
6. Provide a brief description of your target audience.
7. Define what metrics will measure success for this project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it should be used for communication.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, required): Enter your business name as it should be used for communication.
* **Business Website URL** (`website_url`, `text`, optional): If you have a website, enter the URL here.
* **What are your current marketing challenges?** (`current_challenges`, `text`, required): Describe your current marketing challenges.
* **-- Services Interested In --** (`section_services`, `note`, optional): Select all areas of marketing you are interested in. This field is a note, so you can enter as many or as few options as you like.
* **Interested in Search Engine Optimization (SEO)?** (`seo_services`, `select_one`, optional): Select 'True' if you are interested in SEO, 'False' otherwise.
* **Interested in Social Media Management?** (`social_media_mgmt`, `select_one`, optional): Select 'True' if you are interested in Social Media Management, 'False' otherwise.
* **Interested in Content Marketing?** (`content_marketing`, `select_one`, optional): Select 'True' if you are interested in Content Marketing, 'False' otherwise.
* **Interested in PPC Advertising?** (`ppc_advertising`, `select_one`, optional): Select 'True' if you are interested in PPC Advertising, 'False' otherwise.
* **Interested in Email Marketing?** (`email_marketing`, `select_one`, optional): Select 'True' if you are interested in Email Marketing, 'False' otherwise.
* **Monthly Marketing Budget** (`monthly_budget`, `number`, optional): Enter your monthly marketing budget in USD.
* **Please describe your target audience** (`target_audience`, `text`, optional): Describe your target audience.
* **What metrics will define success for this project?** (`success_metrics`, `text`, optional): Define what metrics will measure the success of this project.

Note: All fields with an asterisk (\*) are required. If a field is optional, it is indicated by 'optional'.
