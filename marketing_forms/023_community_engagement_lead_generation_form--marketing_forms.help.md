# Community Engagement Lead Generation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Engagement Lead Generation Form is a form used to collect contact information and preferences from individuals interested in engaging with our organization. This form helps us understand your interests, availability, and contact preferences, allowing us to tailor our communication and outreach efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields, respectively.
2. Provide your primary contact email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. Enter your primary phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Select the option that best describes the organization or institution you are associated with in the "Company or Organization" field.
5. Enter your job title and department (if applicable) in the "Job Title" field.
6. Enter your street address in the "Street Address" field.
7. Enter your city, state, and ZIP code in the "City, State, ZIP" field.
8. Select the area of interest that best describes your interests from the "Area of Interest" field.
9. Indicate your desired level of engagement in the "Desired Engagement Level" field.
10. Select the source where you heard about us in the "How Did You Hear About Us" field.
11. Select your preferred contact method (email, phone, text, or mail) in the "Preferred Contact Method" field.
12. Choose how often you would like to be contacted (weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, quarterly, or only for major events) in the "Preferred Contact Frequency" field.
13. Indicate your interest in volunteering and select any relevant skills you can offer in the "Interested in Volunteering" and "Skills You Can Offer" fields.
14. Select your availability for volunteering in the "Availability" field.
15. Check the box to sign up for our regular updates in the "Sign Up for Newsletter" field.
16. Enter your social media usernames in the "Social Media Handles" field.
17. Add any additional comments or information you would like to share in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your legal first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your legal last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Provide your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your primary phone number.
* **Company or Organization** (`company_organization`, text, optional): Select the organization or institution you are associated with.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title and department (if applicable).
* **Street Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your street address.
* **City, State, ZIP** (`city_state_zip`, text, required): Enter your city, state, and ZIP code.
* **Area of Interest** (`interest_area`, select_one, required): Select the area of interest that best describes your interests (Community Events, Volunteer Opportunities, Local Business, Education Programs, Health and Wellness, Arts and Culture, Environmental Initiatives, Youth Programs).
* **Desired Engagement Level** (`engagement_level`, select_one, required): Indicate your desired level of engagement (Very Active, Moderately Active, Occasionally Active, Just Information).
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_did_you_hear`, select_one, required): Select the source where you heard about us (Website, Social Media, Friend or Family, Event, Advertisement, Other).
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact`, select_one, required): Select your preferred contact method (Email, Phone, Text Message, Mail).
* **Preferred Contact Frequency** (`contact_frequency`, select_one, required): Choose how often you would like to be contacted (Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Only for Major Events).
* **Interested in Volunteering** (`volunteer_interest`, select_one, required): Indicate your interest in volunteering (Yes, Definitely, Maybe, False).
* **Skills You Can Offer** (`skills_to_offer`, text, optional): List any relevant skills you can offer.
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_multiple, optional): Select your availability for volunteering (Weekday Mornings, Weekday Afternoons, Weekday Evenings, Weekend Mornings, Weekend Afternoons, Weekend Evenings).
* **Sign Up for Newsletter** (`newsletter_signup`, select_one, required): Check the box to sign up for our regular updates.
* **Social Media Handles** (`social_media`, text, optional): Enter your social media usernames.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information you would like to share.
