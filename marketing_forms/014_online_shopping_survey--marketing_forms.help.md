# Online Shopping Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The online shopping survey is designed to collect your feedback and ratings to help us improve your online shopping experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as instructed. You can skip fields that are not relevant to you or your shopping experience. Make sure to provide clear and honest answers, and don't hesitate to reach out to us if you have any questions or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Store Type** (store_type)
* **Label: store_type**
* **Type: Select One**
* **Optional: Yes**
* Select the type of store you prefer to shop from.

### **Rating** (rating)
* **Label: rating**
* **Type: Number**
* **Optional: Yes**
* Please rate your current shopping experience from 1 to 5.

### **Rating Scale** (rating_scale)
* **Label: rating_scale**
* **Type: Select Multiple**
* **Optional: Yes**
* Choose the options that apply to your rating:
	+ 1
	+ 2
	+ 3
	+ 4
	+ 5

### **Comment** (comment)
* **Label: comment**
* **Type: Note**
* **Optional: Yes**
* Provide any comments or suggestions on how we can improve your shopping experience.

### **Email** (email)
* **Label: email**
* **Type: Email**
* **Optional: Yes**
* Please enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.

### **Phone** (phone)
* **Label: phone**
* **Type: Text**
* **Optional: Yes**
* If you prefer to be contacted by phone, please enter your phone number.

### **Feedback** (feedback)
* **Label: feedback**
* **Type: Text**
* **Optional: Yes**
* Any other comments or suggestions you'd like to share with us.

### **Assigned Tool** (assigned_tool)
* **Label: assigned_tool**
* **Type: Select One**
* **Optional: Yes**
* Select which tool or channel you were assigned to (chatjimmy, Other).

### **Other** (other)
* **Label: other**
* **Type: Text**
* **Optional: Yes**
* If your assigned tool is not listed, please let us know what it was.

### **Other Comments** (other_comments)
* **Label: other_comments**
* **Type: Text**
* **Optional: Yes**
* Any additional comments or information about your experience.

### **Form ID** (form_id)
* **Label: form_id**
* **Type: Number**
* **Optional: Yes**
* This field is not currently used and can be ignored.
