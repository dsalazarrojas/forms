# Automated Publication Scheduling - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Automated Publication Scheduling form is designed to collect information for the automated publication of content on various channels.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form carefully, following the instructions provided in each section.
2. Make sure to complete all required fields.
3. Review the form for accuracy before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Content Title**: A short title for the content.
* **Content Type**: The type of content (e.g., Blog Post, Video, Infographic, etc.).
* **Content Description**: A brief summary of the content.
* **Author Name**: The name of the author.
* **Author Email**: The email address of the author.
* **Department**: The department of the author (e.g., Marketing, Sales, Product, etc.).
* **Desired Publication Date**: The date on which the content should be published.
* **Desired Publication Time**: The time on which the content should be published (if selected).
* **Publication Channels**: The channels where the content should be published (e.g., Blog, LinkedIn, Twitter, etc.).
* **SEO Title**: A short title for the content on search engines (50-60 characters recommended).
* **Meta Description**: A short description of the content for search engines (150-160 characters recommended).
* **Keywords**: A comma-separated list of keywords related to the content.
* **Campaign Name**: The name of the campaign (if applicable).
* **Target Audience**: The target audience of the content (if applicable).
* **Call to Action**: What readers should do after viewing the content (if applicable).
* **Review Status**: The status of the review (Ready to Publish, Pending Review, Needs Revision, Approved).
* **Assigned Reviewer**: The name of the reviewer (if applicable).
* **Send notification on publish date**: Whether to send a notification on the publish date (yes or no).

## Tips
* Double-check all fields before submitting the form.
* Use the correct format for the desired publication date and time.
* Make sure to select the correct publication channels.
* Be as specific as possible when filling in the form to avoid any errors.
