# Office Supplies Purchase Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Office Supplies Purchase Request Form is used to submit a request for office supply purchases. This form helps gather relevant information to ensure that the purchases are correctly attributed and reviewed by the relevant department head or manager.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that your request is not a duplicate and is relevant to your department's needs.
2. Enter your employee information in the "Employee Info" field.
3. Select the department responsible for your request in the "Department" field.
4. Indicate whether you are requesting office supplies ("Yes" or "No") in the "Items Requested" field.
5. Select the date when you need the supplies in the "Date Needed" field.
6. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
7. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Info** (`employee_info`, `number`, required): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required): Select the department that will be using the office supplies. This will help ensure that the purchase is correctly attributed to your department.
* **Items Requested** (`items_requested`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate whether you are requesting office supplies, with "Yes" or "No".
* **Date Needed** (`date_needed`, `date`, required): Select the date when you need the office supplies.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `email`, required): Enter your email address or other contact information so that we can reach out to you about your request.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your request.
