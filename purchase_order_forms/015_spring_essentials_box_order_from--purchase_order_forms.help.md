# Spring Essentials Box Order From - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers purchase Spring Essentials Box, a curated selection of essential items for Spring cleaning and organization. The form gathers information on the customer's purchase amount, name, email, shipping address, shipping method, and any comments they may have. This data is used to process the order and ensure accurate delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the number of items you want to order from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter your name and email address.
3. Enter your shipping address.
4. Choose a shipping method from the available options (United States Postal Service, FedEx, UPS, or DHL).
5. Optionally, enter any comments you have about your order.
6. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Purchase Amount** (`purchase_amount`, `number`, **Required**): Enter the total amount you are willing to pay for the Spring Essentials Box.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, **Required**): Enter your name as it appears on your shipping address.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, **Optional**): Enter your email address for order updates and communications.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter your phone number for any additional communication.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, `text`, **Required**): Enter your shipping address where the Spring Essentials Box will be delivered.
* **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`, `select_multiple`, **Required**): Choose the shipping method for your order (United States Postal Service, FedEx, UPS, or DHL).
* **Items Ordered** (`items_ordered`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select the number of items you want to order from 1 to 10.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, **Optional**): Enter any comments or special requests you have about your order.
