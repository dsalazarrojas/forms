# vehicle_deposit_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vehicle_deposit_form is used to collect information from customers for vehicle deposits. This form is meant to be used when a customer wants to place an order and make a deposit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
* Review the form carefully before starting.
* Fill out each field accurately and completely.
* Make sure to select the correct payment method and terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Id**: Enter the ID of the vehicle you are depositing on. This is the unique identifier for the vehicle in our system.
* **Deposit Amount**: Enter the amount you are depositing on the vehicle. This can be a dollar amount or a percentage of the total price.
* **Deposit Date**: Enter the date you are making the deposit. This should be in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Payment Method**: Select how you would like to pay for the deposit. You can choose from:
	+ Cash
	+ Check
	+ Credit
	+ Other (Please specify in the comments field)
* **Payment Terms**: Select the payment terms that apply to your deposit. You can choose from:
	+ Net 30
	+ 30/60
	+ 60/90
	+ Other (Please specify in the comments field)
* **Vehicle Details**: Use this field to add any additional information about the vehicle you are depositing on. This can include features, model year, or other relevant details.
* **Comments**: Use this field to add any comments or notes about your deposit. This can include additional information about your payment method or terms.
* **Contact**: Enter your name and phone number so we can contact you about your deposit.
