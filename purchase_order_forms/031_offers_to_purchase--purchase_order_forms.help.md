# offers_to_purchase - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "offers_to_purchase" form is a tool for real estate agents or brokers to collect information from potential buyers about their offers on a property. It helps to gather details such as buyer info, property type, address, offer date and time, price, and terms to facilitate the buying and selling process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing your contact information as a buyer, including any relevant details such as your name, phone number, and email address.
2. Choose the type of property you are interested in (Residential, Commercial, or Other).
3. Enter the property address you are interested in purchasing.
4. Select the date and time you would like to make the offer.
5. Enter the price you are willing to pay for the property.
6. Provide any specific terms you would like to include in the offer, such as "as-is" conditions.
7. If you have any additional terms, enter them in the "Other terms" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Offer Submission** (`offer_submission`, text, optional): A description of your offer.
* **Buyer Info** (`buyer_info`, number, optional): Enter your buyer info (e.g., name, phone number, email).
* **Property Info** (`property_info`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of property you are interested in (Residential, Commercial, or Other).
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple types of property you are interested in (e.g., Residential and Commercial).
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, optional): Enter the property address you are interested in purchasing.
* **Offer Date** (`offer_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to make the offer.
* **Offer Time** (`offer_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you would like to make the offer.
* **Offer Price** (`offer_price`, number, optional): Enter the price you are willing to pay for the property.
* **Offer Terms** (`offer_terms`, note, optional): Provide any specific terms you would like to include in the offer (e.g., "as-is" conditions).
* **Other Terms** (`offer_terms_other`, text, optional): Enter any additional terms you would like to include in the offer.
* **Other Terms** (`offer_terms_other_text`, text, optional): Enter any additional terms you would like to include in the offer.
