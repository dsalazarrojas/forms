# Bison Premier Purchase Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and document the details of a purchase agreement between the seller and buyer.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the **Agreement Parties** section, which includes the seller's and buyer's name.
2. In the **Agreement Date**, enter the date of the agreement in a standard date format (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD).
3. In the **Purchase Details** section, provide a detailed description of the bison or products being purchased.
4. Enter the quantity of the product(s) being purchased.
5. Enter the unit price of the product(s).
6. Calculate the **Total Purchase Price** based on the quantity and unit price.
7. In the **Payment Terms**, specify the payment terms, such as e.g., Net 30, Cash on Delivery.
8. Enter the expected **Delivery Date**, when you expect to receive the purchased product(s).
9. Choose the desired **Delivery Method** (e.g., Pickup, Standard Shipping, Express Delivery).
10. In the **Signatures** section, type your name as the seller and buyer to confirm the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Seller Name**: Enter the name of the seller.
*   **Buyer Names**: Enter all the legal names of the buyer(s).
*   **Agreement Date**: Enter the date of the agreement in a standard date format (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD).
*   **Item Description**: Provide a detailed description of the bison or products being purchased.
*   **Quantity**: Enter the number of products being purchased.
*   **Unit Price**: Enter the price per unit of the product(s).
*   **Total Purchase Price**: Calculate the total price based on the quantity and unit price.
*   **Payment Terms**: Specify the payment terms, such as e.g., Net 30, Cash on Delivery.
*   **Expected Delivery Date**: Enter the date when you expect to receive the purchased product(s).
*   **Delivery Method**: Choose the desired delivery method (e.g., Pickup, Standard Shipping, Express Delivery).
*   **Seller Signature Name**: Type your name as the seller to confirm the agreement.
*   **Buyer Signature Name**: Type your name as the buyer to confirm the agreement.

**Tips**
*   Ensure you have all necessary information and approvals from both parties before filling out this form.
*   Follow the required formats for date fields (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD).
*   Be accurate when entering the quantity and unit price to avoid errors in the total purchase price.
*   Use the payment terms that apply to your specific agreement.
*   Choose the delivery method that best suits your needs.
*   Sign the form accurately and completely.
