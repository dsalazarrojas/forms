# Perfume Purchase Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a standard purchase order form designed to collect information from customers to complete a perfume purchase. The form is likely used within an e-commerce platform or a sales website.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the product(s) you would like to purchase using the "Select one product" or "Select multiple products" fields.
2. Enter the quantity of the selected product(s) if applicable.
3. Enter your name and contact details, including your email and phone number.
4. Review your order and submit it once complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product name**: Enter the name of the product(s) you would like to purchase. Please note that this field is not required but it's recommended to provide a clear and accurate product name.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of the selected product(s) you would like to purchase.
* **Price**: Enter the price of the selected product(s) you would like to purchase. Note: This field might not be visible or editable, depending on the form's configuration.
* **Customer name**: Enter your name or the name of the person placing the order.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to contact you for order updates and confirmation.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number for additional contact purposes.
* **Select one product**: Choose one product from the available options to add it to your order.
* **Select multiple products**: Choose multiple products from the available options to add them to your order. 
* **Select multiple products** (Note: This field is not directly related to "Select one product", as it's a separate and different field, likely used for bulk ordering) : This field is used to select multiple products to add them to your order.
