# Student Government Purchase Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request purchases made on behalf of the student government. Students must fill out this form to report any purchases made, ensuring accurate records are kept.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing a brief description of the item(s) you wish to purchase in the "Purchase Request" field.
2. Enter the date the item was purchased in the "Purchase Date" field. Use the mm/dd/yyyy format, where mm is the month, dd is the day, and yyyy is the year.
3. Enter the total cost of the item(s) in the "Total Cost" field.
4. Provide a detailed description of the item(s) purchased in the "Item Description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Purchase Request** (`student_government_purchase_request_form`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the item(s) you wish to purchase.
* **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`, date, required): Enter the date the item was purchased in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Total Cost** (`total_cost`, number, required): Enter the total cost of the item(s) purchased.
* **Item Description** (`item_description`, text, required): Enter a detailed description of the item(s) purchased.
