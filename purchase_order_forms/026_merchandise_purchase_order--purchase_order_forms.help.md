# Merchandise Purchase Order - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about merchandise purchases from vendors. It includes fields for vendor details, order details, and merchandise-specific information. The form is designed to facilitate communication and record-keeping between buyers and vendors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the vendor's name and email address in the respective fields.
2. Provide detailed information about the order, including the merchandise description, unit price, and quantity.
3. Select the merchandise being purchased from the list of options (Merchandise A, Merchandise B, or Merchandise C).
4. Enter the order date and time.
5. Update the order status (Pending, Confirmed, or Cancelled) and assign the order to a team member (Jim, John, or Jane).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Purchase Order**: This is the main form section where you can enter the vendor's name and a brief description of the order.
* **Vendor Name**: Enter the name of the vendor who is supplying the merchandise.
* **Order Details**: Describe the order in detail, including any special instructions or requirements.
* **Required fields**: This field is not applicable for users as all the fields are already defined and cannot be changed by users.
* **Merchandise ID**: Enter the ID number associated with the merchandise being purchased.
* **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the merchandise being purchased.
* **Unit Price**: Enter the price per unit of merchandise.
* **Total Cost**: The total cost is automatically calculated based on the quantity and unit price.
* **Merchandise Description**: Enter a brief description of the merchandise being purchased.
* **Services**: Select the services related to the merchandise being purchased (Merchandise A, Merchandise B, or Merchandise C).
* **Order Date**: Enter the date on which the order was placed.
* **Order Time**: Enter the time at which the order was placed.
* **Vendor Email**: Enter the email address of the vendor.
* **Vendor Phone**: Enter the phone number of the vendor.
* **Order Status**: Select the status of the order (Pending, Confirmed, or Cancelled).
* **Assigned To**: Select the team member assigned to handle the order (Jim, John, or Jane).
