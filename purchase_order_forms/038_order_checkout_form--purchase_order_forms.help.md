# Order Checkout Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect shipping and billing details, payment information, and other order-related data. It is meant to be filled out by customers during the checkout process on an e-commerce platform.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your shipping address details in the "Shipping Address" field.
2. Provide your shipping city and state in the corresponding fields.
3. Enter your shipping country and zip code in the "Shipping Country" and "Shipping Zip" fields, respectively.
4. Enter your billing address details in the "Billing Address" field.
5. Provide your billing city and state in the corresponding fields.
6. Enter your billing country and zip code in the "Billing Country" and "Billing Zip" fields, respectively.
7. Choose your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown menu.
8. Enter the card number, expiration date, and CVV for credit card payments.
9. Enter any order comments or notes you'd like to include with your order.
10. Enter any customer comments or feedback you'd like to provide.
11. Enter any shipping comments or notes you'd like to include.
12. Review the form for accuracy before submitting.

* **Shipping Address**: Enter your shipping address details here.

* **Shipping Country**: Enter the country of your shipping address.
* **Shipping Zip**: Enter the zip code of your shipping address.

* **Billing Address**: Enter your billing address details.

* **Billing Country**: Enter the country of your billing address.
