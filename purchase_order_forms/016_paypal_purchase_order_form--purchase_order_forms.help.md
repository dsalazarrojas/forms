# PayPal Purchase Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The PayPal Purchase Order Form is used to collect information for a transaction processed through PayPal.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your first name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Enter the amount for the purchase in the **Amount** field.
4. Select the **Currency** of the transaction from the dropdown menu.
5. Enter the name of the item or product in the **Item Name** field.
6. Describe the item or product in the **Item Description** field.
7. Enter the quantity of the item(s) being purchased in the **Quantity** field.
8. Enter the price of the item or product in the **Price** field.
9. Enter your shipping address in the **Shipping Address** field.
10. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
11. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
12. Select the payment method from the **Payment Method** dropdown menu.
13. If you have a valid PayPal token, enter it in the **PayPal Token** field.
14. Enter any order notes or comments in the **Order Note** field.
15. Enter the PayPal total, tax, shipping, and discount (if applicable) in the corresponding fields.
16. If you have a promo code, enter it in the **PayPal Discount** field.
17. Enter any additional notes for the PayPal payment in the **PayPal Note** field.
18. Confirm that you have reviewed the form and the information provided is correct by selecting **I have carefully reviewed the form and the information provided is correct** from the **Confirm** dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Amount** (Number, Required): Enter the total amount for the purchase.
* **Currency** (Select One, Not Required): Select the currency of the transaction.
* **Item Name** (Text, Not Required): Enter the name of the item or product being purchased.
* **Item Description** (Text, Not Required): Describe the item or product being purchased.
* **Quantity** (Number, Not Required): Enter the number of items being purchased.
* **Price** (Number, Not Required): Enter the price of the item or product.
* **Shipping Address** (Text, Not Required): Enter your shipping address for delivery.
* **Email** (Email, Not Required): Enter your email address for correspondence.
* **Phone** (Text, Not Required): Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Payment Method** (Select One, Not Required): Select the payment method for the transaction.
* **PayPal Token** (Text, Not Required): Enter the PayPal token provided by the service.
* **Order Note** (Note, Not Required): Enter any notes or comments for the order.
* **PayPal Total** (Number, Not Required): Enter the total amount for the PayPal payment.
* **PayPal Tax** (Number, Not Required): Enter the tax amount for the PayPal payment.
* **PayPal Shipping** (Number, Not Required): Enter the shipping amount for the PayPal payment.
* **PayPal Discount** (Text, Not Required): Enter a valid promo code for a discount.
* **PayPal Note** (Note, Not Required): Enter any additional notes for the PayPal payment.
* **Confirm** (Select Multiple, Required): Confirm that you have reviewed the form and the information provided is correct.
* **Confirm Note** (Text, Not Required): Enter any additional notes for the confirmation.
