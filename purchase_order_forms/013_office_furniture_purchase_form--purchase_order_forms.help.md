# Office Furniture Purchase Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for purchasing office furniture from our catalog. It's a quick and easy way to select the desired items and submit your order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Make sure to use a valid email format.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Provide your address in the "Address" field.
5. Select the office furniture products you want to purchase from the list below. Choose only one product from the "Products" field.
6. Choose the color of your preferred office furniture from the list below.
7. Finally, click on the "Submit" button to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your full name to help us process your order correctly.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): A valid email address to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Your phone number for further contact.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Your office address for delivery or billing purposes.
* **Products** (`products`, select_one, required: false): Select only one office furniture product that you want to purchase:
	+ Office Chair
	+ Office Desk
	+ Office Table
	+ Office Sofa
	+ Office Shelf
	+ Office File Cabinet
	+ Office Bookshelf
	+ Office Bookcase
* **Color** (`color`, select_one, required: false): Choose the color you prefer for your office furniture:
	+ Red
	+ Green
	+ Blue
	+ Yellow
	+ Purple
	+ Black
	+ White
	+ Grey
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required: false): Click to submit your order.

## Tips
* Please fill in all required fields to ensure your order is processed correctly.
* You can choose only one product and one color for each order.
* If you have any questions or issues with your order, contact us at your email or phone number provided.
