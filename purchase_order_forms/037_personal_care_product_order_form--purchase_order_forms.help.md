# Personal Care Product Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers or clients place a personal care product order. It collects information about the product selection, customer name, order details, and total amount.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select one of the available options for "Product Selection" from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter your customer name in the "Customer Name" field. This is the name by which your order will be processed.
3. If you have any special requests or instructions for your order, please include them in the "Order Details" field. This is an optional field, so feel free to leave it blank if not necessary.
4. If your order includes a payment, enter the total amount in the "Total Amount" field. This is optional, but helpful for our reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Selection (id: 1, select_one, optional)**: Please select the personal care product you would like to order. The options are "Yes" and "No", which correspond to different products or services.
* **Customer Name (id: 2, text, optional)**: Enter your name as you would like it to appear on your order.
* **Order Details (id: 3, note, optional)**: Add any special instructions or requests for your order, if needed.
* **Total Amount (id: 4, number, optional)**: Enter the total amount for your order, if applicable.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and complete, as it will be used to process your order.
- If you have any questions or issues with the form, do not hesitate to contact our support team.
- Please note that some fields are optional, but providing complete information will help us process your order more efficiently.
