To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the "Glamorous Nails With Beth On Hand Stock" order form completely, if possible.

3. Enter your shipping details in the "Shipping Details" field.
4. Enter your payment information in the "Payment Details" field.

* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your shipping address, including house or apartment number, city, state, and zip code.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your payment method and any other relevant payment information here.
