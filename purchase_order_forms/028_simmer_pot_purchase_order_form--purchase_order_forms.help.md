# Simmer Pot Purchase Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Simmer Pot Purchase Order Form is a tool for customers to place new orders with Simmer Pot. This form is used to capture essential information needed to complete the order accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Select the "Pot" option that best suits your needs.
3. Choose your preferred payment method.
4. Enter the total price of your order.
5. Provide your delivery address.
6. Select the desired time for delivery.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Simmer Pot Purchase Order Form** (Page 1, Text): This is the title of the form. You can leave this field blank as it's for identification purposes only.
* **Simmer Pot Purchase Order Form** (Page 1, Text, Optional): This field is not used in this form. Please ignore it.
* **Simmer Pot Purchase Order Form** (Page 1, Text): This field is not used in this form. Please ignore it.
* **Simmer Pot Delivery Preferences** (Page 2, Select Multiple): Select all the options that apply to your order preferences.
	+ Pot 1: Select this if you want to order a standard pot size.
	+ Pot 2: Select this if you want to order a large pot size.
	+ Pot 3: Select this if you want to order an extra-large pot size.
	+ Pot 4: Select this if you want to order a pot size that's not available as a standard option.
	+ Pot 5: Select this if you want to order a custom pot size.
* **Payment Details** (Page 3, Select One): Select how you would like to pay for your order. Please choose one of the options:
	+ Cash: Pay cash on delivery.
	+ Bank Transfer: Transfer the payment to our account.
	+ Credit Card: Pay using your credit card.
* **Payment Method** (Page 3, Text, Optional): If you're paying with a method other than Cash, Bank Transfer, or Credit Card, please describe the method you'll be using.
* **Order Total** (Page 4, Number, Optional): Enter the total price of your order.
* **Delivery Address** (Page 5, Text, Optional): Provide your delivery address for us to know where to send your order.
* **Delivery Time** (Page 6, Time, Optional): Select the desired delivery time for your order.
