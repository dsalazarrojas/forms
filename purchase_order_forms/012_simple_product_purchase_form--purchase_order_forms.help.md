# Simple Product Purchase Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Simple Product Purchase Form is a basic form designed to collect information from customers about their product purchases. It is intended for use in an e-commerce or retail context, such as a small business or startup.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your User Information, including your name and contact details.
2. Enter the product details in the Product Info section.
3. Fill in the Payment Information section with your payment method.
4. Summarize your order in the Order Summary section.
5. List the products you are purchasing in the Order Products section.
6. Review the Payment Terms and conditions.
7. Click the Submit button to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information (User Information)** (`user_info`, text, optional): Enter your name and contact details, such as email and phone number, to help us get in touch with you regarding your order.
* **Product Info (Product Info)** (`product_info`, text, optional): Provide details about the product(s) you are interested in purchasing, including product name, quantity, and any other relevant information.
* **Payment Information (Payment Information)** (`payment_info`, text, optional): Enter your payment method, including payment type (e.g., credit card, PayPal) and payment details (e.g., card number, expiration date).
* **Order Summary (Order Summary)** (`order_summary`, text, optional): Summarize your order to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* **Order Products (Order Products)** (`order_products`, text, optional): List the products you are purchasing, including product name, quantity, and any other relevant information.
* **Payment Terms (Payment Terms)** (`payment_terms`, text, optional): Review the payment terms and conditions before submitting your order.
* **Submit (Submit)** (`submit_btn`, text, optional): Click this button to submit your order and complete the purchasing process.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate processing of your order.
* Review your information carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
* Contact us if you have any questions or concerns about the form or your order.
