# Online Product Purchase Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Product Purchase Form is designed to collect necessary information for making online purchases from our platform. Please use this form to accurately provide your details for a smooth and hassle-free transaction experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your customer name in the customer_name field.
2. Provide your address in the address field.
3. Enter the purchase amount.
4. Upload or describe your payment proof in the payment_proof field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (customer_name, text, optional): Enter your full name as you want it to be displayed on our records.
* **Address** (address, text, optional): Type in your address where you'd like to receive your purchased product.
* **Purchase Amount** (purchase_amount, number, optional): Enter the amount you'd like to purchase.
* **Payment Proof** (payment_proof, text, optional): Upload a screenshot or describe your payment method (e.g., bank transfer, PayPal, etc.) to verify your payment.
