# Property Purchase Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential information for a property purchase order, including property details, buyer and seller information, price information, and more. Please complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth transaction process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing all required information.
- Review each section carefully before submitting the form.
- Ensure that all fields are correct, as this information will be used for the purchase order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Property Details (Field 1)
* **Property Details**: Please provide a brief description of the property, including its address, size, and any other relevant details.

### Buyer Information (Field 2)
* **Buyer Information**: Enter the name and contact information of the buyer.

### Buyer Contact (Field 3)
* **Buyer Contact**: Enter the contact information of the buyer, including phone number and email.

### Seller Information (Field 4)
* **Seller Information**: Enter the name and contact information of the seller.

### Price Information (Field 5)
* **Price Information**: Enter the price of the property, including any applicable taxes or fees.

### Property Location (Field 6)
* **Property Location**: Provide the location of the property, including city, state, or country.

### Property Status (Field 7)
* **Property Status**: Indicate the current status of the property, such as "Available for Sale" or "Under Contract".

### Property Notes (Field 8)
* **Property Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the property.

### Buyer Signature (Field 9)
* **Buyer Signature**: The buyer must sign this form to confirm their agreement to purchase the property.

### Seller Signature (Field 10)
* **Seller Signature**: The seller must sign this form to confirm their agreement to sell the property.

### Date Signed (Field 11)
* **Date Signed**: The date and time the form was signed.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as "required" in the form schema. Please fill out all required fields to ensure the form is complete and accurate.
