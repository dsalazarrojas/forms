# Souvenir Booklet Advertising Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for souvenir booklet advertising campaigns from business customers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your contact details, including your business address, phone number, and email address.
2. Choose the size and type of advertisement you want to order.
3. Select your payment method (Cash, Cheque, or Online Payment).
4. Provide your order date and total cost.
5. Specify the shipping method (Shipping via Email, Shipping via SMS, or Shipping via Phone).
6. Confirm your payment status (Unpaid, Paid, or Partial Paid).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`): Enter your business contact information, including your name, address, phone number, and email address.
* **Souvenir Ad Size** (`souvenir_ad_size`): Choose the size of the souvenir booklet you want to order.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`): Select your preferred payment method (Cash, Cheque, or Online Payment).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Confirm your preferred payment method (Bank Transfer, Cash, or Credit Card).
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`): Confirm your payment status (Unpaid, Paid, or Partial Paid).
* **Order Date** (`order_date`): Enter the date you want to order the booklet.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`): Enter the total cost of the order, including any applicable sales tax.
* **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`): Choose how you want to receive your order (Shipping via Email, Shipping via SMS, or Shipping via Phone).
* **Shipping Cost** (`shipping_cost`): Enter the shipping cost associated with your order.
* **Total Cost** (`total_cost`): Confirm the total cost of the order, including shipping and sales tax.
* **Business License** (`business_license`): Enter your business license number for tax purposes.
* **Sales Representative** (`sales_rep`): Enter the name of your sales representative.
* **Fax** (`fax`): Enter your fax number for communication.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`): Enter your business email address for communication.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`): Enter your shipping address to receive the order.
* **Shipping City** (`shipping_city`): Enter the city where you want to receive your order.
* **Shipping Country** (`shipping_country`): Enter the country where you want to receive your order.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`): Enter the date when you paid for the order.
* **Sales Tax** (`sales_tax`): Enter the sales tax applicable for your order.
* **Discount** (`discount`): Enter any discount applicable for your order.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`): Enter your business name as the customer.
* **Customer Contact** (`customer_contact`): Enter your contact information as the customer.
* **Payment Reference** (`payment_reference`): Enter any payment reference number for your order.

Note: Some fields may not be required for every order, so ensure you only fill out the necessary fields for your specific order.
