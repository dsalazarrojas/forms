# University Educational Tool Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to order educational tools for university students. Please fill out the form with your information to place an order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your first and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields, respectively.
2. Select the name of your university from the "university" dropdown list.
3. Choose your department(s) from the "department" multi-select list.
4. Select the type of tool you need (Software, Hardware, or Service) from the "tool_type" dropdown list.
5. Enter the quantity of tools you need in the "quantity" field.
6. Choose a date and time for the order delivery in the "date" and "time" fields, respectively.
7. If you have any special notes or instructions for your order, enter them in the "note" field.
8. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
9. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, optional): Your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, optional): Your last name.
* **university** (select_one, optional): Select the name of your university.
* **department** (select_multiple, optional): Choose the department(s) you belong to.
* **order_tool** (text, optional): Enter the name of the tool you are ordering.
* **tool_type** (select_one, optional): Select the type of tool you need (Software, Hardware, or Service).
* **quantity** (number, optional): Enter the quantity of tools you need.
* **date** (date, optional): Choose the date for order delivery.
* **time** (time, optional): Choose the time for order delivery.
* **note** (note, optional): Enter any special notes or instructions for your order.
* **email** (email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure your order is processed correctly.
* If you have any issues with your order, contact the university support team.
* If you need to make changes to your order, contact the support team as well.
