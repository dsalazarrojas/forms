# Network Device Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information for ordering network devices such as routers, switches, and other equipment. Please fill out the form with the required and optional information to ensure accurate tracking and management of your order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully and fill out the required information.
2. Select the type of router, switch, or other equipment you are ordering.
3. Enter the quantity of the equipment you are ordering.
4. Enter the unit price of the equipment.
5. Enter any additional details or notes about the order.
6. Select the status of the order (Active or Inactive).
7. Enter internal and external notes about the order.
8. Enter contact information for the person making the order.
9. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

* **General Information** (`general_info`): A field for entering any additional information about the order.
* **Router Order** (`router_order`): Select "Yes" if you are ordering a router, "No" otherwise.
* **Switch Order** (`switch_order`): Select "Yes" if you are ordering a switch, "No" otherwise.
* **Other Equipment Order** (`other_equipment_order`): Select "Yes" if you are ordering other equipment, "No" otherwise.
* **Device Description** (`device_description`): Enter a description of the device you are ordering.
* **Quantity Ordered** (`quantity_ordered`): Enter the quantity of the equipment you are ordering.
* **Unit Price** (`unit_price`): Enter the unit price of the equipment.
* **Total Price** (`total_price`): This field is not currently being used, but is included for future development.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`): Enter the date of the order.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`): Enter the time of the order.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`): Enter the date the order was requested.
* **Request Time** (`request_time`): Enter the time the order was requested.
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`): Enter the date the order was approved.
* **Approval Time** (`approval_time`): Enter the time the order was approved.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`): Select "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate the status of the order.
* **Internal Notes** (`internal_notes`): Enter any internal notes about the order.
* **External Notes** (`external_notes`): Enter any external notes about the order.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): Enter the name of the person making the order.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter the email of the person making the order.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter the phone number of the person making the order.
* **Orderer Name** (`orderer_name`): Enter the name of the person making the order.
* **Orderer Role** (`orderer_role`): Enter the role of the person making the order.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`): Click this button to complete the form.
