# spa_products_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "spa_products_order_form" is a form designed to capture essential details from customers when placing a spa order. This includes selecting preferred spa products and providing delivery information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct category of spa products you are interested in from the options provided in the "spa_products" section.
2. Choose whether you would like to receive the products or not in the "products" section.
3. Enter the quantity of the products you want, if applicable.
4. Provide the delivery date you prefer.
5. Add any special requests in the "special_requests" section.
6. Enter delivery information in the "delivery_info" section, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **spa_products** (`Spas and Wellness`, `Aesthetics`, `Massage`, required: false): Select the category of spa products you are interested in.
* **products** (`Yes`, `No`, required: false): Choose whether you would like to receive the products or not.
* **quantity**: Enter the quantity of the products you want, if applicable.
* **delivery_date**: Provide the delivery date you prefer.
* **special_requests**: Add any special requests you might have in this text field.
* **delivery_info**: Enter delivery information as required.
