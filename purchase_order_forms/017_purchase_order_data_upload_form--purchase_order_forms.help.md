# Purchase Order Data Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and process purchase order data for your organization. It is intended to be used for uploading purchase orders, providing essential information for efficient processing and tracking.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure that the provided data is relevant to your organization's purchase orders.
3. Review your submissions for accuracy before uploading.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Purchase Order Data** (`purchase_order_data`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for entering the purchase order data. It should include all relevant details about the order, such as order number, date, and status.
* **Vendor Information** (`vendor_info`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for entering information about the vendor involved in the purchase order, including their name, address, and contact details.
* **Delivery Information** (`delivery_info`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for entering details about the delivery of the purchase order, such as the delivery date and method.
* **Purchase Order Info** (`purchase_order_info`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for entering additional information about the purchase order, such as notes or comments.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_docs`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for uploading any supporting documents related to the purchase order, such as receipts or invoices.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* Double-check the provided data for consistency and correctness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the designated support team.
