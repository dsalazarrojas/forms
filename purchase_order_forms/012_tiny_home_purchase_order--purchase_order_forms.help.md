# Tiny Home Purchase Order - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a tiny home purchase order. It is used by buyers and sellers to create, manage, and track purchase orders for tiny homes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the Order Details section with information about the order, including the name of the buyer and seller.
2. Select the pricing options that apply to the order.
3. Choose the buyer's information, including their contact method and payment terms.
4. Enter the delivery details, including the date and time.
5. Provide any special instructions or notes about the order.
6. Select the payment method and amount.
7. Confirm the order status and add any comments or notes.
8. Enter the shipping address and payment terms details.
9. Add any buyer and seller comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Buyer Info** (`buyer_info`, select_one, optional): This section allows the buyer to select their contact method and payment terms.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, select_one, optional): This section allows the buyer to select their contact method and payment terms.
* **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`, note, optional): This section provides a space for the buyer to enter delivery details, such as the delivery date and time.

* **Order Terms** (`order_terms`, text, optional): This section allows the buyer to enter order terms and conditions.
