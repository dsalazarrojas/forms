# tickets_and_tables_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from customers for ticket and table order purposes, such as customer contact details and shipping requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide order details, including any specific requirements or requests.
2. Enter customer information, including name, email, and phone number.
3. Enter shipping details, such as shipping address and any special instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required: false): This field is used to collect any specific requirements or requests related to the order.
* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, required: false): Please enter your name, email address, and phone number in this field. This information will be used to contact you regarding your order.
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, text, required: false): Enter your shipping address and any special instructions related to delivery in this field.
