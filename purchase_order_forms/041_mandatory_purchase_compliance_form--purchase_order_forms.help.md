# Mandatory Purchase Compliance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and track purchases that require compliance. It captures essential information related to purchases, such as purchase dates, quantities, costs, and acknowledgments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant compliance options that apply to the purchase.
2. Enter the procurement number and purchase date and time.
3. Fill in the purchase amount and compliance acknowledgements.
4. Add any relevant notes or comments.
5. Update the status of the purchase form as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Purchase Compliance** (`purchase_compliance`): This field is used to select the relevant compliance options that apply to the purchase.
* **Required Purchases** (`required_purchases`): This field allows you to select multiple compliance options that are required for the purchase.
* **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`): This field is used to enter the date of the purchase.
* **Purchase Time** (`purchase_time`): This field is used to enter the time of the purchase.
* **Procurement Number** (`procurement_number`): This field is for the procurement number associated with the purchase.
* **Purchase Amount** (`purchase_amount`): This field is used to enter the amount spent on the purchase.
* **Compliance Acknowledgements** (`compliance_acknowledgements`): This field allows you to select which compliance acknowledgments apply to the purchase.
* **Notes** (`notes`): This field is for any additional notes or comments related to the purchase.
* **Email** (`email`): This field is for the contact email associated with the purchase.
* **Phone** (`phone`): This field is for the contact phone number associated with the purchase.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`): This field is for the person assigned to handle the purchase.
* **Assigned Date** (`assigned_date`): This field is used to enter the date the purchase was assigned to someone.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`): This field is for the person who submitted the purchase form.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`): This field is used to enter the date the purchase form was submitted.
* **Submitted Time** (`submitted_time`): This field is for the time the purchase form was submitted.
* **Status** (`purchase_order_forms_status`): This field is for the current status of the purchase form.
* **Status Date** (`purchase_order_forms_status_date`): This field is used to enter the date the status was updated.
* **Status Time** (`purchase_order_forms_status_time`): This field is for the time the status was updated.
* **Status Note** (`purchase_order_forms_status_note`): This field is for any additional notes or comments related to the status update.
* **Status Email** (`purchase_order_forms_status_email`): This field is for the contact email associated with the status update.
* **Status Phone** (`purchase_order_forms_status_phone`): This field is for the contact phone number associated with the status update.
* **Status Assigned To** (`purchase_order_forms_status_assigned_to`): This field is for the person assigned to handle the status update.
* **Status Assigned Date** (`purchase_order_forms_status_assigned_date`): This field is used to enter the date the status update was assigned to someone.
* **Status Submitted By** (`purchase_order_forms_status_submitted_by`): This field is for the person who submitted the status update.
* **Status Submitted Date** (`purchase_order_forms_status_submitted_date`): This field is used to enter the date the status update was submitted.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct compliance options that apply to the purchase.
* Double-check the procurement number and purchase date to ensure accuracy.
* Enter the correct purchase amount and compliance acknowledgments.
* Update the status fields as necessary to reflect any changes in the purchase process.
