# Video Recording Purchase Order - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for a video recording purchase order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Choose a customer from the list of options.
2. Select a recording session date.
3. Choose one or more service providers for the recording session.
4. Enter the payment information as required.
5. Submit the form to generate a purchase order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info** (`customer-info`, select_one, required: false): Select the customer for the video recording purchase order.
* **Recording Session** (`recording-session`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the recording session.
* **Service Provider** (`service-provider`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose one or more service providers for the recording session.
* **Payment** (`payment`, number, required: false): Enter the payment amount for the video recording purchase order.
* **Submit** (`submit-btn`, note, required: false): Click to submit the form and generate a purchase order.
