# Equipment Mounting Bracket Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers to process a purchase order for equipment mounting brackets.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer information in the "Customer Info" field.
2. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
3. Enter your delivery address in the "Delivery Address" field (this field is required).
4. Specify your delivery preferences in the "Delivery Preferences" field (this field is required).
5. If you wish to make a payment, enter the payment information in the "Payment Info" field (this field is optional).
6. Describe your mounting bracket preferences in the "Mounting Bracket Preferences" field (this field is optional).
7. Specify any additional equipment details in the "Equipment Details" field (this field is optional).
8. Add any order notes in the "Order Notes" field (this field is optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info** (`Customer Info`, text, optional): Please enter your company name, contact person, and other relevant customer information.
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, email, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Delivery Address** (`Delivery Address`, text, required): Enter your delivery address, including street, city, state, and ZIP code.
* **Delivery Preferences** (`Delivery Preferences`, text, required): Specify any special delivery requirements or instructions.
* **Payment Info** (`Payment Info`, text, optional): Enter your payment method (e.g. credit card, bank transfer).
* **Mounting Bracket Preferences** (`Mounting Bracket Preferences`, text, optional): Describe your preferences for the mounting bracket, including size, color, or any other specific requirements.
* **Equipment Details** (`Equipment Details`, text, optional): Provide any details about the equipment you are ordering, including model, serial number, or any other relevant information.
* **Order Notes** (`Order Notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
