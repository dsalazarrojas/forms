# Trends & Colours (For Resellers) - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trends & Colours (For Resellers) form is designed to collect information from resellers for a purchase order. This form is intended to be completed by resellers to provide details about their purchase order, including their name, email, phone number, order date, and order amount.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the page with the label "Purchase Order Form".
2. Fill in the "reseller_name" field with your name as it appears on your business card or invoice.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field. Please ensure that it matches the email address you use for your business.
4. Fill in your phone number in the "phone" field. Please format it as you would on your business card or invoice.
5. Select the order date for which you are submitting this purchase order.
6. Finally, enter the total amount for the order in the "order_amount" field. Please ensure that this is the accurate amount for the order you are placing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Purchase Order Form (1)**: This is the page where you will enter all the information for your purchase order.
* **reseller_name (2)**: This field is where you enter your business name as it appears on your business card or invoice.
* **Email (3)**: Enter the email address associated with your business to ensure accurate delivery of any communications regarding this purchase order.
* **Phone (4)**: Enter your business phone number in the format you would use on your business card or invoice.
* **Order Date (5)**: Select the date for which this purchase order is being placed.
* **Order Amount (6)**: Enter the total amount for this purchase order, ensuring accuracy in the amount entered.
