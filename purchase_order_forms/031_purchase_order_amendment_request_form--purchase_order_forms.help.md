# Purchase Order Amendment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a request for amendments to an existing purchase order. The form collects information related to the original purchase order, the desired changes, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully and answer all required fields. If a field is marked as "required", please make sure to fill it out.
2.  Fill in the original purchase order number and date.
3.  Describe the amendments you wish to make to the original purchase order.
4.  Provide any supporting documentation or evidence that may be required.
5.  Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Purchase Order Number** (`purchase_order_number`, text, required): This is the original purchase order number that you wish to amend.
* **Original Purchase Order Date** (`original_purchase_order_date`, text, required): This is the original date of the purchase order.
* **Changes to be Made** (`changes`, text, required): Describe the amendments you wish to make to the original purchase order.
* **Supporting Documentation** (`supporting_documentation`, text, optional): Please provide any supporting documentation or evidence that may be required.
* **Reason for Amendment** (`reason_for_amendment`, text, required): Explain the reason for making these amendments.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or information to provide, please enter them here.
* **Requestor's Name** (`requestor_name`, text, required): This is the name of the person requesting the amendment.
* **Requestor's Contact Information** (`requestor_contact_info`, text, required): Please provide your contact information so that we can get in touch with you regarding your request.
* **Purchase Order Status** (`purchase_order_status`, text, required): Please indicate the status of the original purchase order (e.g., pending, approved, cancelled).
* **Original Total Cost** (`original_total_cost`, text, required): This is the total cost of the original purchase order.
* **Amended Total Cost** (`amended_total_cost`, text, required): This is the total cost of the amended purchase order.
* **Amendment Reason Category** (`amendment_reason_category`, text, required): Please select the category of the reason for the amendment (e.g., quantity change, price change, delivery date change).
