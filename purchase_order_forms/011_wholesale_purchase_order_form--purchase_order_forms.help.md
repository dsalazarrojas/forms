# Wholesale Purchase Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Wholesale Purchase Order Form is used for submitting a purchase order to the wholesale team for approval and processing. This form is intended for authorized users to gather information about the items to be purchased, the contact information of the person submitting the order, and any additional details necessary for processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Contact Information section with your name, email, and phone number. This is necessary for the wholesale team to contact you with any questions or updates about your order.
2. In the Order Details section, provide a brief description of the order, including any relevant product codes or names.
3. In the Items To Purchase section, list the items you would like to purchase, along with their corresponding quantities.
4. In the Quantity field, enter the total quantity of each item to be purchased.
5. In the Item Number field, enter the item number associated with each item to be purchased.
6. In the Additional Information field, provide any further details or instructions that are relevant to your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required: false): This section collects your contact information, which includes your name, email, and phone number. Please ensure that this information is accurate and up-to-date so that the wholesale team can contact you efficiently.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required: false): This field is used to provide a brief description of the order, including any relevant product codes or names. Please be as specific as possible so that the wholesale team can quickly understand your requirements.
* **Items To Purchase** (`items_to_purchase`, text, required: false): In this field, list the items you would like to purchase, along with their corresponding quantities. Please separate each item with commas or use separate lines for clarity.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required: false): Enter the total quantity of each item to be purchased.
* **Item Number** (`item_number`, number, required: false): Enter the item number associated with each item to be purchased. This may be required for processing and inventory purposes.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, required: false): This field is used for providing any further details or instructions that are relevant to your order. Please be as specific as possible so that the wholesale team can process your order efficiently.
