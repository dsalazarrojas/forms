The form is designed to facilitate the ordering and management of mining equipment, capturing essential details such as order form information, equipment specifics, supplier data, delivery coordination, and logistical delivery details. This form will help ensure accurate and timely delivery of mining equipment to clients.

# Mining Equipment Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is meant for customers or clients to submit orders for mining equipment, which will be used to process and fulfill the order.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below.

1. Fill out the required and optional sections, depending on the fields provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Form** (`order_form`, Text, Optional): Provide a brief description of the order you want to place. This is a free-form text field where you can enter any relevant information about your order.
* **Equipment Details** (`equipment_details`, Text, Optional): Enter a detailed description of the equipment you are ordering, including any specific models or types.
* **Supplier Information** (`supplier_info`, Text, Optional): Enter any relevant information about the supplier you are using, such as their name, contact number, or other contact details.
* **Delivery Coordinator** (`delivery_coordinator`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the person responsible for coordinating the delivery, if applicable.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, Date, Optional): Select the date for the delivery of the equipment. This will help ensure timely delivery and scheduling.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, Time, Optional): Choose the time for the delivery of the equipment. This can be helpful for scheduling purposes.
* **Notes** (`notes`, Note, Optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the order or delivery.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate order processing.
* If you have any specific requirements or requests, include them in the "Notes" field.
* Review your order carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy.
