# Exempt Organization Purchase Certificate - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Exempt Organization Purchase Certificate form is used by exempt organizations to claim a purchase made from a vendor and to document the exempt organization's relationship with the vendor.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Info (1)**: This field is used to enter information about the vendor.
* **Purchase Date (2)**: Enter the date of the purchase.
* **Purchase Amount (3)**: Enter the amount of the purchase.
* **Exempt Status (4)**: Select the exempt status of the purchase from the options provided. This field is required.
* **Exempt Reason (5)**: Enter a brief explanation for the exempt status selected in the previous field.
* **Vendor Address (6)**: Enter the address of the vendor.
