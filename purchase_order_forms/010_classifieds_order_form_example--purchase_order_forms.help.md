# Classifieds Order Form Example - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Classifieds Order Form is designed to facilitate the creation and publication of classified advertisements in various newspapers and publications. This form collects necessary information for the advertisement, including advertiser details, advertisement content, and publication preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter Advertiser Information**: Fill in your full name or business name in the "Advertiser Name" field.
2. **Enter Contact Information**: Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field and your contact phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
3. **Select Ad Category**: Choose the primary category of your advertisement from the "Advertisement Category" dropdown list.
4. **Describe Your Ad**: Enter a brief title for your advertisement in the "Advertisement Headline" field, and provide a detailed description of what you are selling or offering in the "Advertisement Description" field.
5. **Add Additional Information (Optional)**: If applicable, enter the price of your item or service in the "Price (if applicable)" field, and select the geographic location relevant to your ad in the "Location or Service Area" field.
6. **Select Publication Preferences**: Choose the newspaper or publication where you'd like your ad to be published from the "Which Publication?" dropdown list.
7. **Specify Publication Date and Number of Issues**: Select the desired publication date and number of consecutive issues for your ad.
8. **Choose Pricing and Payment Options**: Select the pricing tier and payment method for your ad.
9. **Finalize Order**: Enter the approximate number of words in your ad, and select the order date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Advertiser Name**: Your full name or business name.
	+ Required field.
* **Email Address**: Email address for confirmation and contact purposes.
	+ Required field.
* **Phone Number**: Contact telephone number.
	+ Required field.
* **Advertisement Category**: Select the primary category for your advertisement.
	+ Required field.
* **Advertisement Headline**: Brief title for your advertisement (50 characters max).
	+ Required field.
* **Advertisement Description**: Detailed description of what you are selling or offering.
	+ Required field.
* **Price (if applicable)**: Cost or asking price of your item or service.
	+ Optional field.
* **Location or Service Area**: Geographic area relevant to the advertisement.
	+ Optional field.
* **Contact Name to Display**: Name to show in the classified ad.
	+ Required field.
* **Contact Phone to Display**: Phone number to show in the ad.
	+ Required field.
* **Contact Email to Display**: Email to show in the ad.
	+ Optional field.
* **Which Publication?**: Select the newspaper or publication where you'd like your ad to be published.
	+ Required field.
* **Desired Publication Date**: Date when you want your ad to appear.
	+ Required field.
* **Number of Issues**: Number of consecutive publications.
	+ Required field.
* **Pricing Tier**: Select pricing based on size and duration of your ad.
	+ Required field.
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you'd like to pay for your ad.
	+ Required field.
* **Word Count**: Approximate number of words in your ad.
	+ Required field.
* **Order Date**: The current date.
	+ Required field.
* **Special Formatting Requested**: Any special formatting requests for your ad.
	+ Optional field.
