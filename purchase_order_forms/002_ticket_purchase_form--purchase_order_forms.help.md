# Ticket Purchase Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers for ticket purchases. It includes fields for customer contact information, payment terms, ticket details, and notes for additional comments or notes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
2.  Provide your customer's email address in the "email" field.
3.  Enter your customer's phone number in the "customer_phone" field.
4.  Enter your customer's address in the "address" field.
5.  Enter your customer's postal code in the "postal_code" field.
6.  Enter your customer's city in the "city" field.
7.  Select your customer's state in the "state" field.
8.  Enter your customer's zip code in the "zip_code" field.
9.  Select the customer's country in the "country" field.
10. Check the "Terms and Conditions" field and select the correct option based on your agreement.
11. Enter the number of tickets you wish to purchase in the "Number of Tickets" field.
12. Enter the price of the tickets in the "Price" field.
13. Select the order date in the "Order Date" field.
14. Select the date and time you purchased the tickets in the "Ticket Purchased At" field.
15. Select the payment terms in the "Payment Terms" field.
16. Enter the total price of the tickets in the "Total Price" field.
17. Enter the ID of the ticket in the "Ticket ID" field.
18. Select the type of ticket from the "Ticket Type" field.
19. Add any additional notes for the customer in the "Customer Note" field.
20. Add any additional notes for the customer's phone in the "Customer Phone Note" field.
21. Add any additional notes for the ticket in the "Ticket Note" field.
22. Add any additional notes for the payment terms in the "Payment Terms Note" field.
23. Add any additional notes for the customer's address in the "Customer Address Note" field.
24. Add any additional notes for the customer's postal code in the "Customer Postal Code Note" field.
25. Select the status of the ticket from the "Ticket Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Enter the customer's name.
*   **email** (`customer_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the customer's email address.
*   **customer_phone** (`customer_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
*   **address** (`customer_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the customer's address.
*   **postal_code** (`customer_postal_code`, `text`, optional): Enter the customer's postal code (zip code).
*   **city** (`customer_city`, `text`, optional): Enter the customer's city.
*   **state** (`customer_state`, `text`, optional): Select the customer's state.
*   **zip_code** (`customer_zip_code`, `text`, optional): Enter the customer's zip code.
*   **country** (`customer_country`, `select_one`, optional): Select the customer's country.
*   **Terms and Conditions** (`customer_terms_and_conditions`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check the terms and conditions of the ticket purchase.
*   **Number of Tickets** (`number_of_tickets`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of tickets you wish to purchase.
*   **Price** (`ticket_price`, `number`, optional): Enter the price of the tickets.
*   **Order Date** (`order_date`, `date`, optional): Select the date you wish to order the tickets.
*   **Ticket Purchased At** (`ticket_purchased_at`, `time`, optional): Select the date and time you purchased the tickets.
*   **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `select_one`, optional): Select the payment terms.
*   **Total Price** (`total_price`, `number`, optional): Enter the total price of the tickets.
*   **Ticket ID** (`ticket_id`, `text`, optional): Enter the ID of the ticket.
*   **Ticket Type** (`ticket_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of ticket.
*   **Customer Note** (`customer_note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes for the customer.
*   **Customer Phone Note** (`customer_phone_note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes for the customer's phone.
*   **Ticket Note** (`ticket_note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes for the ticket.
*   **Payment Terms Note** (`payment_terms_note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes for the payment terms.
*   **Customer Address Note** (`customer_address_note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes for the customer's address.
*   **Customer Postal Code Note** (`customer_postal_code_note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes for the customer's postal code.
*   **Ticket Status** (`ticket_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the status of the ticket.
