# Crochet Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers who wish to place custom orders for crochet items.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the customer details section with your name and contact information.
2. Select the order type that best describes your order (Crochet or Other).
3. Enter the name of the item you wish to order.
4. Provide a brief description of the item.
5. Enter the price of the item.
6. Specify the quantity of the item you wish to order.
7. Enter any custom requests or special instructions for your order.
8. Choose a payment method (PayPal or Bank Transfer).
9. Add any customer notes or additional comments about your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, optional): Enter your name and contact information to ensure we can get in touch with you regarding your order.
* **Order Type** (`order_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of order you are placing (Crochet or Other).
* **Item Name** (`item_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the item you wish to order.
* **Item Description** (`item_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the item.
* **Item Price** (`item_price`, number, required): Enter the price of the item.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Specify the quantity of the item you wish to order.
* **Custom Request** (`custom_request`, text, required): Enter any custom requests or special instructions for your order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose a payment method (PayPal or Bank Transfer).
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
