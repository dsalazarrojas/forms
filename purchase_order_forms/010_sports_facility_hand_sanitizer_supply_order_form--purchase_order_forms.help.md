# Sports Facility Hand Sanitizer Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Facility Hand Sanitizer Supply Order Form is a document designed to gather essential information for the ordering of hand sanitizers for sports facilities. This form is intended for authorized personnel to provide details about the required hand sanitizers. If your facility already has an agreement with the company providing hand sanitizers, this form does not need to be completed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, follow these steps:

1. Enter all necessary information on the "Order Information" page.
2. Indicate whether you are the contact person for this order on the "Contact Person" page.
3. Provide your contact phone number, if applicable, on the "Phone Number" page.
4. Enter your email address on the "Email" page.
5. Specify the type of hand sanitizer required on the "Sanitizer Type" page.
6. Enter the quantity of hand sanitizers needed on the "Quantity Needed" page.
7. Select the delivery time on the "Delivery Time" page.
8. Enter the name of the facility on the "Facility Name" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Information** (`sports_facility_hand_sanitizer_supply_order_form_page_1`, text, required): This page collects general information about the order. Please fill out the form accurately with the required details.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, select_one, required): Please indicate if you are the contact person for this order.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): If you are the contact person, please provide your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address to ensure efficient communication about the order.
* **Sanitizer Type** (`sanitizer_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of hand sanitizer required.
* **Quantity Needed** (`quantity_needed`, number, required): Enter the amount of hand sanitizers needed for the facility.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, required): Select the desired delivery time for the hand sanitizers.
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the sports facility where the hand sanitizers are needed.
