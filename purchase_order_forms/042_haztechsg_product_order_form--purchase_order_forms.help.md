# haztechsg_product_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "haztechsg_product_order_form" is a form designed to collect details from customers for a specific product purchase. It helps us understand what the customer is looking to buy and ensures we can process their order accurately.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your **product_name** to help us identify what you are purchasing.
2.  Provide the **quantity** you wish to purchase.
3.  If you are purchasing at a **unit_price**, you can enter that here.
4.  Select if this purchase is **tax_exempt** or not.
5.  Choose how you would like to make your payment: **PayPal** or **Credit Card**.
6.  Enter the **order_date** and **order_time** for when you expect to receive the product.
7.  Add any additional **order_notes** about your order.
8.  Enter your **customer_email** so we can contact you about your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Input** (`user_input`, Text, Optional): This is an optional field for customers to add any additional comments or information about their purchase.
*   **Product Name** (`product_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the product you are interested in purchasing.
*   **Quantity** (`quantity`, Number, Optional): Indicate how many units you would like to purchase.
*   **Unit Price** (`unit_price`, Text, Optional): Enter the price per unit of the product, if different from the default.
*   **Tax Exempt** (`tax_exempt`, Select One, Optional): If your business is exempt from tax, select 'Yes'.
*   **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select one or more methods you plan to use for payment.
*   **Order Date** (`order_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date on which you expect to receive the product.
*   **Order Time** (`order_time`, Time, Optional): Indicate the specific time you expect the product to arrive.
*   **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, Note, Optional): Add any extra comments or information for the order.
*   **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, Email, Optional): Enter your email so we can contact you about your order status.

## Tips

*   Always enter accurate information to avoid any processing delays or misunderstandings.
*   If unsure about any field, feel free to contact us for clarification.
*   Ensure that your payment method is up to date.
*   Enter a valid email address for us to communicate with you about your order.
