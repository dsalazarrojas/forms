# Sample Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Sample Request Form is a simple form designed to collect general contact information from users. It is intended to be used for internal purposes, such as creating a contact list or storing information for follow-up communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Fill in your Last Name.
3. Enter your Email address. This field will be checked to ensure it's in the correct format.
4. Enter your Phone Number. This field will also be checked to ensure it's in the correct format.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name in this field.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address in this field. Note: The field will be checked for a valid email format.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number in this field.
