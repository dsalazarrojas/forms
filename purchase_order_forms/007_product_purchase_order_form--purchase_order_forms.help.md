# Product Purchase Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to guide customers through the process of creating a purchase order for products. The form collects necessary information for the purchase order process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required information for each field as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product** (`product_purchase_order_form_page_1`, Text, Optional): Enter the product details you wish to purchase.
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, Text, Optional): Enter your customer information such as name, contact number, and email.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, Text, Optional): Enter your shipping address for delivery.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, Text, Optional): Enter your payment method and details.
* **Product List** (`product_list`, Text, Optional): List the products you wish to purchase, one product per line.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled out accurately to avoid any delays in processing your purchase order.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our customer support team.
* We will review and process your form as soon as possible.
