**Solar System Purchase Order Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The Solar System Purchase Order Form is used to capture order information for a solar system, including supplier, customer, and order details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier ID (1)**: Type in the supplier's ID number.
* **Customer Name (2)**: Enter the customer's full name.
* **Order Date (3)**: Enter the date of the order in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Order Time (4)**: Enter the time of the order in 24-hour format.
* **Total Cost (5)**: Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Quantity (6)**: Enter the quantity of the order.
* **Product Type (7)**: Select the type of product being ordered from the dropdown menu:
	+ Solar Panel
	+ Inverter
	+ Mounting System
* **Payment Terms (8)**: Select the payment terms from the dropdown menu:
	+ Net 30
	+ Net 60
	+ Net 90
* **Email (10)**: Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone (11)**: Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Notes (9)**: Add any additional comments or remarks about the order.

## Tips
* Make sure to accurately enter all required fields.
* Review and verify all entered information before submitting the form.
* Use the notes field to capture any additional information or comments not captured in other fields.
