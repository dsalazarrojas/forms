# Weekly Delivery Preferences Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Weekly Delivery Preferences Survey is a form designed to collect feedback and preferences from users regarding the delivery schedule of a product or service. This form aims to gather information on the frequency and timing of deliveries, allowing us to improve our delivery process and better serve our customers.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form questions and instructions carefully.
2. Answer each question based on your actual preferences.
3. Select one or multiple options for each field, as applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Delivery Days** (`delivery_days`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check the days of the week you would like to receive deliveries. Select all applicable days from the list.
	+ If you want to receive deliveries on a particular day, choose "Yes" for that day. Leave "No" for days you do not want to receive deliveries.
* **Time Windows** (`time_windows`, `select_multiple`, required: false): If you select "Yes" for delivery_days, you will also be asked to choose the time windows for each selected day. Select all applicable time windows from the list.
	+ If you select a time window, choose the time of day you would prefer for the selected delivery day.
* **Instructions** (`instructions`, `text`, required: false): If you have any special instructions or comments about your deliveries, please enter them here. This will help our team understand your delivery needs better.
* **Satisfaction Feedback** (`satisfaction_feedback`, `text`, required: false): Share any feedback or suggestions you have about our delivery process. This will help us improve our services.
* **Satisfaction Rating** (`satisfaction_rating`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with our delivery service. Choose from "Excellent", "Neutral", or "Poor" to help us understand your experience.
* **Delivery Instructions** (`delivery_instructions`, `text`, required: false): If you have any specific instructions or requests for your deliveries, please enter them here. This will help our team prepare your delivery accordingly.
* **Satisfaction Suggestions** (`satisfaction_suggestions`, `text`, required: false): If you have any suggestions for improving our delivery service, please share them with us. This will help us make changes to better serve you.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the time of day you would prefer for your deliveries. Select from "Morning", "Afternoon", or "Evening" to indicate your preferred delivery time.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when answering each question to help us understand your delivery preferences.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact us and we will be happy to assist you.
* Please note that this form is for your convenience and is not a commitment. We will use your feedback to improve our services, but we cannot guarantee changes based on your input.
