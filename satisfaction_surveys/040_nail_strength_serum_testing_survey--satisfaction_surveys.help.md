# Nail Strength Serum Testing Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the effectiveness and usability of the nail strengthening serum. It helps us understand the experiences of users who have tried the serum, gather insights on its performance, and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions as honestly as possible, and choose the response that best describes your experience with the serum. Please make sure to fill out all the questions, even if you've had a negative experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: Introduce yourself and briefly describe your experience with the nail strengthening serum.
* **Nail Strength Serum Use**: How often do you use the nail strengthening serum? Please choose one of the options:
	+ Less than 1 time
	+ 1-2 times
	+ 3 or more times
* **Nail Strength Serum Effectiveness**: How effective do you feel the serum has been in strengthening your nails? Please choose all that apply:
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
* **Nail Strengthening**: Please rate the level of nail strengthening you felt after using the serum:
	+ Very Strong
	+ Strong
	+ Medium
	+ Weak
	+ None
* **Nail Clipping**: How many times have you had to clip your nails since using the serum?
	+ Less than 5 times
	+ 5-10 times
	+ 10-20 times
	+ 20+ times
* **Nail Breakage**: Please describe any breakage or lifting of the nail you experienced since using the serum.
* **Nail Strengthening Recommendation**: Do you have any nail strengthening recommendations for us?
* **Nail Strengthening Serum Recommendation**: Do you have any recommendations for the nail strengthening serum?
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Please rate your overall satisfaction with the serum:
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ None
* **Nail Strength Serum UGC (User Generated Content)**: Have you recommended the serum to anyone or written a review on social media?
	+ True
	+ False
* **Nail Clipping Difficulty**: How difficult was it to clip your nails since using the serum?
	+ Much easier
	+ Slightly easier
	+ No difference
	+ Slightly harder
	+ Much harder
* **Nail Growth**: Please rate your observed nail growth rate since using the serum:
	+ Much faster
	+ Slightly faster
	+ No difference
	+ Slightly slower
	+ Much slower
* **Serum Smell**: How would you describe the smell of the serum?
	+ Faint
	+ Medium
	+ Strong
* **Nail Sensitivity**: How sensitive have you felt while using the serum?
	+ None
	+ Slightly more sensitive
	+ Much more sensitive
* **Nail Strengthening Ingredient**: Which ingredient(s) do you think contribute to the nail strengthening effect of the serum? (Select all that apply)
	+ Vitamin E
	+ Calcium
	+ Biotin
	+ Other
* **Nail Strengthening Concentration**: How would you rate the concentration of the serum?
	+ Too concentrated
	+ Too dilute
	+ Just right
* **Nail Strengthening Side Effects**: Please describe any side effects you experienced while using the serum.
* **Nail Strengthening Side Effects Frequent**: How often did you experience side effects while using the serum?
