# Theme Park Attraction Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather feedback from theme park visitors, helping us to understand their satisfaction with our attractions, improve our services, and better understand our target audience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the attractions you visited from the list.
2. Rate your experience on a scale of 1-5.
3. If you'd like to share your thoughts or suggestions, type them in the "Comment" field.
4. If you're under 18, you can leave the "Age" field blank or answer with "Not Applicable".
5. Please select how you heard about the theme park.
6. If you have any email or phone number, please provide them for further contact.
7. In the "Feedback" field, you can share any additional comments or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attraction Visited**: 
	+ Please select all the attractions you visited during your trip.
* **Rating (1-5)**: 
	+ Rate your overall experience with our theme park attractions on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Comment**: 
	+ If you'd like to share your thoughts or suggestions, type them in here.
* **Age**:
	+ Please enter your age or select "Not Applicable" if you are under 18.
* **How did you hear about us?**:
	+ Please select how you heard about the theme park.
* **Email**:
	+ If you'd like to be contacted for further information or special offers, please enter your email address.
* **Phone**:
	+ If you'd like to be contacted via phone, please enter your phone number.
* **Feedback**:
	+ Share any additional comments or suggestions you have about your visit or the theme park.
