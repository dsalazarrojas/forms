# Hiring Manager Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hiring Manager Satisfaction Survey is designed to collect feedback from hiring managers on their overall satisfaction levels with various aspects of the hiring process. This survey helps us understand the strengths and weaknesses of our hiring practices and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please answer all questions honestly, as this feedback will help us understand your experience and improve our hiring process.
2. Use the provided options for questions that ask for a score or rating.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please explain your answer in the provided text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Title**: Enter the job title that you are currently working on or have recently worked on.
* **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`): Rate your overall satisfaction with the job title on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very Dissatisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
* **Communication Level** (`communication_level`): Rate your satisfaction with the communication level on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **Support Level** (`support_level`): Rate your satisfaction with the support level on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Unsatisfactory" and 5 is "Exceptional".
* **Training Level** (`training_level`): Rate your satisfaction with the training level on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very Low" and 5 is "Very High".
* **Job Satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`): Rate your overall satisfaction with your job on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very Dissatisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
* **Work Environment** (`work_environment`): Describe your work environment, including any factors that contribute to your satisfaction or dissatisfaction.
* **Job Knowledge** (`job_knowledge`): Rate your understanding of the job requirements and responsibilities on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Low" and 5 is "High".
* **Role Satisfaction** (`role_satisfaction`): Rate your overall satisfaction with your role on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very Dissatisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
