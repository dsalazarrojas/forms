# Movember Participation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from participants on their motivations, expectations, and feedback about Movember. It is essential to fill out the form accurately and honestly to help us better understand our community's interests and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the instructions and questions carefully, and make sure you are willing to participate in Movember.
2. Fill out each field accurately and honestly, following the guidelines below for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Introduction (Page 1)
* **introduction** (`note`, Required: False): A brief note introducing yourself, stating your name and/or team name is optional, but please keep it brief and relevant.

### What Motivated You (Page 1)
* **what_motivated_you** (Text, Required: False): Describe in a few words what motivated you to participate in Movember or what interests you about the event.

### What Motivation (Page 1)
* **what_motivation** (Text, Required: False): Please explain your motivation for participating in Movember, in a few words or sentences.

### Will You Participate (Page 1)
* **will_participate** (Select One, Required: False):
	+ 1: Yes, I will participate.
	+ 2: Yes, but I need more information.
	+ 3: No, I won't participate.

### Why Participate (Page 1)
* **why_participate** (Text, Required: False): If you selected 1 or 2 in the previous question, please explain why you want to participate in Movember.

### What Campaigns (Page 1)
* **what_campaigns** (Select Multiple, Required: False):
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3

### Next Steps (Page 1)
* **next_steps** (Text, Required: False): If you are planning to participate, please let us know what your next steps are, e.g., "join a team," "create a fundraising page," etc.

### Contact Info (Page 1)
* **contact_info** (Text, Required: False): Provide any additional contact information (e.g., phone number, email) you are willing to share with us.

### Email (Page 1)
* **email** (Email, Required: False): Enter your email address if you are willing to be contacted about Movember updates, news, and reminders.
