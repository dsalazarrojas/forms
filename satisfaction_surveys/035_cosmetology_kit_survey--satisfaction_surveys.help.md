# Cosmetology Kit Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Cosmetology Kit Survey is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with a specific cosmetology kit. This information will help us understand how our customers perceive and feel about the kit's effectiveness, usability, and overall satisfaction. 

This form is not mandatory and is intended for voluntary completion by customers who have used the kit. Your responses will help us improve and refine our products and services to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To get the most out of this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Choose the options that best fit your experiences and feelings about the cosmetology kit.
3. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the text field at the end of the form.
4. Click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Cosmetology Kit Survey (id: 1)**: This is the main survey title.
* **question_1 (id: 2)**: This question asks whether or not you experienced the desired outcome after using the kit. Please select "Yes" if you experienced the outcome, and "No" if not.
* **what_were_your_feelings_before (id: 3)**: Describe your feelings and expectations before using the cosmetology kit. This will help us understand your mental state and emotions when using the kit.
* **question_3 (id: 4)**: This date field is intended for you to provide the date you started using the kit, or the date you completed it.
* **question_4 (id: 5)**: Please enter the time you started using the kit, or the time you completed it.
* **question_5 (id: 6)**: Enter your email address to help us contact you for follow-up questions or to provide more information about our products and services.
* **question_6 (id: 7)**: Describe any thoughts, feelings, or experiences you had while using the kit.
* **question_7 (id: 8)**: Please enter a number that best represents your level of satisfaction with the kit's effectiveness.
* **question_8 (id: 9)**: Enter another number that best represents your level of satisfaction with the kit's usability.
* **question_9 (id: 10)**: This question asks whether or not you would recommend the kit to others. Please select "Yes" if you would recommend, and "No" if not.
* **question_10 (id: 11)**: Select all the reasons why you would recommend (or would not recommend) the kit.
* **question_11 (id: 12)**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the kit.
