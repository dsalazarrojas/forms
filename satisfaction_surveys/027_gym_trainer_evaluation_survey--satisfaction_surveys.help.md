# Gym Trainer Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback about your experience with a trainer, specifically on their level of professionalism, communication, motivation, overall satisfaction, and availability. Your input will help us improve our training services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Rate the trainer's professionalism, communication, motivation, overall satisfaction, and availability on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
2. If you have any additional feedback about the trainer, please provide it in the "Additional feedback" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, so we can get back to you with any questions or follow-ups.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field, in case we need to contact you directly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How would you rate the trainer's professionalism**: Rate on a scale of 1-5, where 1 indicates you were not impressed and 5 indicates you were highly impressed.
* **How would you rate the trainer's communication**: Rate on a scale of 1-5, where 1 indicates poor communication and 5 indicates excellent communication.
* **How would you rate the trainer's motivation**: Rate on a scale of 1-5, where 1 indicates low motivation and 5 indicates high motivation.
* **How would you rate your overall satisfaction with the trainer**: Rate on a scale of 1-5, where 1 indicates low satisfaction and 5 indicates high satisfaction.
* **How would you rate the trainer's availability**: Rate on a scale of 1-5, where 1 indicates poor availability and 5 indicates excellent availability.
* **Additional feedback about the trainer**: Share any additional thoughts or comments you have about your experience with the trainer. This is an optional field.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can get back to you with any questions or follow-ups.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you directly.
