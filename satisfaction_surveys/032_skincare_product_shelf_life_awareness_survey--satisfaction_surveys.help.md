# Skincare Product Shelf Life Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to understand your experience with skincare products, specifically your awareness of their shelf life.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the skincare product you are currently using from the options in the **product_used** field.
2. Enter the number of days since you first started using the product in the **shelf_life** field.
3. Describe any risk concerns you have about the product's shelf life in the **risk_concerns** field.
4. Rate your current level of awareness about the product's shelf life, using the scale provided in the **awareness** field.
5. If you have any habits that may affect your use of skincare products, such as regular checkups or product rotation, please mark them in the **habits** field.
6. If you think there are any risk factors associated with the product, such as packaging changes or ingredient degradation, please mark them in the **risk_factors** field.
7. If you have any other risk factors not listed, please describe them in the **risk_factors_other** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Used**: Please select the skincare product you are using from the provided options.
* **Shelf Life (days)**: Enter the number of days since you first started using the product.
* **Risk Concerns**: Describe any concerns you have about the product's shelf life.
* **Awareness**: Rate your current level of awareness about the product's shelf life, using the scale provided.
* **Habits**: If you have any habits that may affect your use of skincare products, such as regular checkups or product rotation, mark them here.
* **Risk Factors**: If you think there are any risk factors associated with the product, such as packaging changes or ingredient degradation, mark them here.
* **Risk Factors Other**: If you have any other risk factors not listed, describe them here.
* **Submit**: Once you have completed all the above fields, click the submit button to send the survey.

## Tips
- Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
- Make sure to select only the product you are currently using.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask for clarification.
