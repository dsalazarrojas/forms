# feedback_on_overcoming_challenges - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on challenges that individuals have overcome, with the aim of understanding how different strategies and approaches can be used to address these challenges.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide a reflective story about the challenges you have overcome, which will help us understand your experience and the strategies you used. This field is optional, but it will help us gain a deeper understanding of your situation.
2. Share your strategies for overcoming the challenges, which will help us understand the approaches and methods you used. This field is required.
3. Provide any advice or guidance you received during the program or coaching session, which will help us understand how you were supported.
4. Enter your email address, which will allow us to contact you if needed. This field is required.
5. Evaluate the impact of the program or coaching session, and provide any feedback on how it helped you. This field is optional.
6. Select whether you received coaching or organisational support during the program, which will help us understand the nature of your support. This field is required.
7. Share the impact of the program or coaching session, and describe any areas where you improved. This field is required.
8. Provide any additional comments or feedback on your experience. This field is optional.
9. If you were assigned a tool or resource during the program, select the name of the tool. If not, choose "Other" and describe the tool you used. This field is optional.
10. If you are evaluating a different program, please enter the name of that program. This field is optional.
11. Enter the date and time of submission, which will help us track the timing of your feedback. This field is required.
12. Enter the timezone of submission, which will help us understand your location. This field is required.
13. Finally, if you have any additional comments or feedback, please share them with us. This field is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Reflective Story** (`Reflective Story`, text, optional): Share a reflective story about the challenges you have overcome.
- **Strategies** (`Strategies`, text, required): Share your strategies for overcoming the challenges.
- **Advice** (`Advice`, text, optional): Provide any advice or guidance you received during the program or coaching session.
- **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
- **Program Evaluation** (`Program Evaluation`, text, optional): Evaluate the impact of the program or coaching session.
- **Coaching or Organisation** (`Coaching or Organisation`, select_one, required): Select whether you received coaching or organisational support.
- **Impact** (`Impact`, text, required): Share the impact of the program or coaching session.
- **Improvement Areas** (`Improvement Areas`, text, required): Describe any areas where you improved as a result of the program or coaching session.
- **Comments** (`Comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback on your experience.
- **Assigned Tool** (`Assigned Tool`, select_one, optional): If you were assigned a tool or resource during the program, select the name of the tool.
- **Other Program Evaluation** (`Other Program Evaluation`, text, optional): If you are evaluating a different program, please enter the name of that program.
- **Date of Submission** (`Date of Submission`, date, required): Enter the date of submission.
- **Time of Submission** (`Time of Submission`, time, required): Enter the time of submission.
- **Submitted By** (`Submitted By`, text, optional): Share any other relevant information about yourself.
- **Timezone of Submission** (`Timezone of Submission`, time, required): Enter the timezone of submission.

Note: This help guide is a general overview of the form and its fields. For specific questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
