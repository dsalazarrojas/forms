# Community Recreation Participation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Recreation Participation Survey aims to gather feedback and information from community members to enhance the quality of services offered by the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions and select the options that best fit your experience and opinion.
2. Be honest and provide as much information as you can.
3. If you have difficulty accessing a particular field, please contact us and we'll do our best to assist you.
4. Complete all required fields before submitting the form.
