# Salon Appointment No Show Behavior Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to help us understand the reasons behind missed appointments at the salon and identify areas where we can improve our operations and customer communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer each question to the best of your ability. If a question does not apply to you, please select the "Not applicable" or "Does not apply" option if available, or skip it altogether. Be as detailed as you can in your answers, as this will help us better understand your perspective.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What happened** (`salon_appointment_no_show_behavior_survey_page_1`, text): Describe what happened leading up to and during your missed appointment. Please be as brief or detailed as you'd like.
* **Why did not show** (`salon_appointment_no_show_survey_page_2`, select_multiple): Choose the reason(s) you did not show up for your appointment. You can select as many or few reasons as apply.
* **Reason for no show** (`salon_appointment_no_show_survey_page_3`, text): If none of the options in the previous question apply, describe your reason for not showing up in this field.
* **How likely do you think you will show** (`salon_appointment_no_show_survey_page_4`, select_one): On a scale of 1-4, how likely do you think you are to show up for your next appointment?
* **Would you like to reschedule?** (`salon_appointment_no_show_survey_page_5`, select_multiple): If you think you will show up, would you like to reschedule your appointment? Please select all options that apply.
* **How many times have you missed an appointment?** (`salon_appointment_no_show_survey_page_6`, text): If you have missed appointments before, how many times do you think you've missed them?
* **What is your preferred reschedule time frame** (`salon_appointment_no_show_survey_page_7`, time): If you would like to reschedule, what time frame would you prefer?
* **How often do you usually show** (`salon_appointment_no_show_survey_page_8`, select_one): On a scale of 1-5, how often do you usually show up for appointments?
* **What day do you usually arrive for appointments** (`salon_appointment_no_show_survey_page_9`, select_one): What time of day do you usually arrive for appointments?
* **How long do you usually stay** (`salon_appointment_no_show_survey_page_10`, select_multiple): How long do you usually stay for appointments? Please select all that apply.
* **How often do you book last minute** (`salon_appointment_no_show_survey_page_11`, select_multiple): How often do you book appointments at the last minute?
* **How often do you cancel last minute** (`salon_appointment_no_show_survey_page_12`, select_multiple): How often do you cancel appointments at the last minute?
* **How many appointments do you usually have per week** (`salon_appointment_no_show_survey_page_13`, select_one): How many appointments do you usually have per week?
* **How many minutes do you arrive early** (`salon_appointment_no_show_survey_page_14`, number): How many minutes before your appointment time do you usually arrive?
* **How many minutes do you arrive late** (`salon_appointment_no_show_survey_page_15`, number): How many minutes late are you usually for appointments?
* **What time do you usually arrive for appointments** (`salon_appointment_no_show_survey_page_16`, time): What time do you usually arrive for appointments?
* **How long do you usually stay** (`salon_appointment_no_show_survey_page_17`, select_multiple): How long do you usually stay for appointments? Please select all that apply.
* **What time do you usually leave** (`salon_appointment_no_show_survey_page_18`, time): What time do you usually leave after your appointments?
* **How long do you usually take for services** (`salon_appointment_no_show_survey_page_19`, number): How long do you usually take to complete services?
* **What time do you usually take your first service** (`salon_appointment_no_show_survey_page_20`, time): What time do you usually start your first service?
* **What time do you usually take your last service** (`salon_appointment_no_show_survey_page_21`, time): What time do you usually finish your last service?
* **Do you like to be on time?** (`salon_appointment_no_show_survey_page_22`, select_one): Do you prefer to be on time for appointments?
* **Do you like being early?** (`salon_appointment_no_show_survey_page_23`, select_one): Do you like to arrive early for appointments?
* **Do you like being late?** (`salon_appointment_no_show_survey_page_24`, select_one): Do you like to arrive late for appointments?
* **Additional comments** (`salon_appointment_no_show_survey_page_25`, text): Is there anything else you'd like to share about your appointment attendance or any suggestions for improvement?

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If a question does not apply to you, please skip it or select "Not applicable" or "Does not apply" if available.
* Be as detailed as you can in your answers, as this will help us better understand your perspective.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional comments" field.
