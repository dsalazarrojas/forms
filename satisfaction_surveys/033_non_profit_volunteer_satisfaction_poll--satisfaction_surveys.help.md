# Non Profit Volunteer Satisfaction Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Non Profit Volunteer Satisfaction Poll is a survey designed to collect feedback from non-profit volunteers about their experiences and perceptions. The survey is anonymous and non-attributional, ensuring that volunteers can provide honest and unbiased responses.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each page carefully and answer all questions relevant to you.
2. For each page, provide as much detail as possible in the answer boxes.
3. Ensure that you answer all required fields, as indicated by the asterisk (\*).
4. Submit your completed form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome Page (\*):**
	+ This page is designed to welcome you to the survey and provide context.
	+ Please take a moment to read through the page and familiarize yourself with the survey questions.
* **Volunteer Information (\*):**
	+ This page is for providing information about yourself as a volunteer.
	+ Please enter your name and any other relevant information about your volunteer role or position.
* **Feedback Page (\*):**
	+ This page is for providing feedback about your experiences as a volunteer.
	+ Please answer the questions thoughtfully and provide as much detail as possible.
* **Communication Page (\*):**
	+ This page is for providing feedback about communication within the organization.
	+ Please think about how you have interacted with staff, other volunteers, or clients.
* **Support Page (\*):**
	+ This page is for providing feedback about support within the organization.
	+ Please think about how well you have been supported during your volunteer work.
