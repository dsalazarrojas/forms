# Aquatic Facility Accessibility Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather data on respondents' experiences and perceptions of the accessibility features of aquatic facilities. The survey aims to improve the overall accessibility and satisfaction of visitors with aquatic facilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer the "Visit Frequency" question by selecting the option that best describes how often you visit the aquatic facility.
2. Answer the "Do You Have Any Mobility or Access Needs" question by selecting "Yes" if you have any mobility or access needs, "No" if you do not, or "Prefer Not to Say" if you do not want to disclose your needs.
3. Answer the "Accessibility Features" question by selecting the options that best describe your experiences with the parking, entrance, restrooms, seating, signage, and staff assistance at the aquatic facility.
4. If you have encountered any barriers or accessibility issues, describe them in the "Barriers or Accessibility Issues Encountered" text field.
5. Provide any suggestions for improvements in the "Suggestions for Improvement" text field.
6. Finally, answer the "Overall Accessibility Satisfaction" question by selecting the option that best describes your overall satisfaction with the accessibility features of the aquatic facility.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Visit Frequency**: Select the frequency of your visits to the aquatic facility. This information will help us understand how often the facility is being used.
	+ Weekly: You visit the facility once a week or more frequently.
	+ Bi-weekly: You visit the facility twice a week or less frequently.
	+ Monthly: You visit the facility once a month or less frequently.
	+ Few Times Per Year: You visit the facility occasionally.
	+ First Visit: This is your first visit to the facility.
* **Do You Have Any Mobility or Access Needs**: This question is intended to help us understand if you have any mobility or access needs that may require special accommodations.
	+ Yes: You have a disability or mobility issue that may affect your access to the facility.
	+ No: You do not have a disability or mobility issue.
	+ Prefer Not to Say: You do not want to disclose your needs.
* **Accessibility Features**: Select the options that best describe your experiences with the following accessibility features:
	+ **Parking**: Select the quality of the parking availability at the facility.
		- Excellent: The parking is well-maintained, easy to navigate, and accessible.
		- Good: The parking is adequate, but may have some issues.
		- Fair: The parking is somewhat limited or difficult to navigate.
		- Poor: The parking is very limited or difficult to access.
		- Not Applicable: The facility does not have a parking area.
	+ **Entrance**: Select the quality of the entrance accessibility at the facility.
		- Excellent: The entrance is well-maintained, easy to access, and accessible.
		- Good: The entrance is adequate, but may have some issues.
		- Fair: The entrance is somewhat limited or difficult to access.
		- Poor: The entrance is very limited or difficult to access.
		- Not Applicable: The facility does not have an entrance.
	+ **Restroom Facilities**: Select the quality of the restrooms at the facility.
		- Excellent: The restrooms are well-maintained, clean, and accessible.
		- Good: The restrooms are adequate, but may have some issues.
		- Fair: The restrooms are somewhat limited or difficult to access.
		- Poor: The restrooms are very limited or difficult to access.
		- Not Applicable: The facility does not have restrooms.
	+ **Seating and Rest Areas**: Select the quality of the seating and rest areas at the facility.
		- Excellent: The seating and rest areas are well-maintained, comfortable, and accessible.
		- Good: The seating and rest areas are adequate, but may have some issues.
		- Fair: The seating and rest areas are somewhat limited or difficult to access.
		- Poor: The seating and rest areas are very limited or difficult to access.
		- Not Applicable: The facility does not have seating or rest areas.
	+ **Signage and Navigation**: Select the quality of the signage and navigation at the facility.
		- Excellent: The signage and navigation are clear, easy to follow, and accessible.
		- Good: The signage and navigation are adequate, but may have some issues.
		- Fair: The signage and navigation are somewhat limited or difficult to follow.
		- Poor: The signage and navigation are very limited or difficult to follow.
		- Not Applicable: The facility does not have signage or navigation.
	+ **Staff Assistance**: Select the quality of the staff assistance at the facility.
		- Excellent: The staff are very helpful, knowledgeable, and assistive.
		- Good: The staff are helpful, but may not be as knowledgeable or assistive.
		- Fair: The staff may be somewhat limited in their assistance.
		- Poor: The staff are not very assistive or knowledgeable.
		- Not Applicable: The facility does not have staff assistance.
* **Barriers or Accessibility Issues Encountered**: If you have encountered any barriers or accessibility issues, please describe them in this field.
* **Suggestions for Improvement**: If you have any suggestions for improving the accessibility features of the facility, please provide them in this field.
* **Overall Accessibility Satisfaction**: Select the option that best describes your overall satisfaction with the accessibility features of the facility.
	+ Very Satisfied: You are very satisfied with the accessibility features of the facility.
	+ Satisfied: You are satisfied with the accessibility features of the facility.
	+ Neutral: You are neutral about the accessibility features of the facility.
	+ Dissatisfied: You are dissatisfied with the accessibility features of the facility.
	+ Very Dissatisfied: You are very dissatisfied with the accessibility features of the facility.

## Tips
- Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
- Be as specific as possible when describing barriers or accessibility issues.
- If you have any suggestions for improvement, please provide them in a clear and concise manner.
- Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey. Your feedback is valuable in helping us improve the accessibility features of the aquatic facility.
