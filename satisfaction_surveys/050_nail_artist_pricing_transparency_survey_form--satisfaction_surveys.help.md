# Nail Artist Pricing Transparency Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from clients on the pricing transparency of our services. The goal is to assess how clearly and effectively we present our pricing information to clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. Use the options provided to guide your response.
4. Submit the form once you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Salons and Independent Nail Artists**: This field is for you to list the salons or independent nail artists you have had interactions with. Please enter up to 3 responses.
* **Pricing Clarity (1-10)**: This field measures your level of satisfaction with the pricing clarity of the salon. Rate your experience on a scale of 1 (not clear at all) to 10 (very clear).
* **Services Offered**: Select all services that were offered by the salon during your interaction. You can choose multiple services.
* **Satisfaction with Pricing**: How satisfied are you with the pricing of our services? Choose from Satisfied, Dissatisfied, or Neutral.
* **Client Feedback**: Please share any additional feedback you have about our pricing. Type in your comments here.
* **Better Pricing (hours)**: If you think our pricing is better than average, please specify how many hours more than the average you think it is.
* **Pricing Decision**: Have you made a pricing decision based on our transparency? Choose from Yes, No, or Undecided.
* **Form Completion**: How clear was this form to you? Choose from Yes, No, or Undecided.

## Tips
* Be honest and provide feedback that is helpful for us to improve our pricing transparency.
* Please answer all questions truthfully, but feel free to skip fields if not applicable.
* Take your time and answer carefully to provide the most informative feedback.
