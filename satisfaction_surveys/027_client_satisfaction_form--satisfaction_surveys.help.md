# Client Satisfaction Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Client Satisfaction Form" is a crucial tool for gathering valuable feedback from our clients about their experiences with our services. This form will help us identify areas of improvement and measure the overall satisfaction level of our clients, allowing us to refine our processes and provide better services in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the form in its entirety**: Each section of the form is designed to gather specific information, so make sure to answer all questions and select the corresponding options.
2. **Be as detailed as possible**: The more information you provide, the more we can learn from your feedback.
3. **Select the correct options**: For questions with multiple choices (e.g., select_one fields), choose the option that best describes your experience or feelings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, text, required): Please enter your name so we can address you personally in our response.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you if we need more information or to provide updates.
* **What Service or Product Did You Use?** (`service_received`, text, required): Describe the specific service or product you used to get the best experience from our company.
* **Date You Received Service** (`date_of_service`, date, optional): If you can remember the date you received our service, please provide it. This will help us track your experience over time.
* **How Likely Are You to Recommend Us?** (`likelihood_recommend`, number, required): Rate your likelihood of recommending us to others, on a scale of 1-10.
* **Quality of Product or Service** (`product_service_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of our service or product you used:
	+ **Excellent**
	+ **Good**
	+ **Satisfactory**
	+ **Fair**
	+ **Poor**
* **Quality of Customer Service** (`customer_service_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of our customer service:
	+ **Excellent**
	+ **Good**
	+ **Satisfactory**
	+ **Fair**
	+ **Poor**
* **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, select_one, required): Rate the value for money of our service or product:
	+ **Excellent**
	+ **Good**
	+ **Fair**
	+ **Poor**
	+ **Very poor**
* **Timeliness of Service** (`timeliness_of_service`, select_one, required): Rate the timeliness of our service:
	+ **Excellent**
	+ **Good**
	+ **Satisfactory**
	+ **Fair**
	+ **Poor**
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, select_one, required): Rate your overall experience with us:
	+ **Excellent**
	+ **Good**
	+ **Satisfactory**
	+ **Fair**
	+ **Poor**
* **What Was the Biggest Strength?** (`biggest_strength`, text, optional): If you have a positive experience, tell us what we did well.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, text, optional): Share any areas where we can improve.
* **Would You Use Our Services Again?** (`would_return`, select_one, required): Indicate your willingness to use our services again:
	+ **Definitely yes**
	+ **Probably yes**
	+ **Uncertain**
	+ **Probably not**
	+ **Definitely not**
* **Additional Comments or Suggestions** (`comments`, text, optional): Share any additional thoughts or suggestions you might have.

Note: The form is divided into multiple sections to make it easier to provide feedback on specific aspects of your experience. Please feel free to skip sections that do not apply to you, but be sure to answer all required questions to give us a comprehensive understanding of your experience.
