# Checkout Process Shopper Satisfaction Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experience with the checkout process on this shop. Your input will help us improve the checkout process for future customers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the "Order ID" field to enter your order number from your receipt.
2. Choose the date of your purchase from the calendar.
3. For the "Checkout Experience Rating" section, select one of the options that best describes your experience.
4. Choose how satisfied you were with the payment options available.
5. Select how quickly you felt the checkout process was completed.
6. If you checked out on a mobile device, rate your experience with mobile checkout.
7. Let us know if you experienced any technical errors during checkout.
8. If you experienced technical errors, describe them in the "Describe any errors or frustrations" field.
9. Share any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
10. If you would like to be contacted for further discussion, select "True."
10. Finally, please rate how likely you are to recommend this shop based on your checkout experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order ID (text)** (Required, ID of your purchase): Please enter your order number from your receipt.
* **Date of Purchase (date)** (Required): Choose the date of your purchase from the calendar.
* **Order Context** (Note): A place to enter any context you would like to share about your order.
* **Checkout Experience Rating** (Select One, Optional):
	+ Very Easy
	+ Easy
	+ Neutral
	+ Difficult
	+ Very Difficult
* **Satisfaction with Payment Options** (Select One, Required):
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Unsatisfied
	+ Very Unsatisfied
* **Speed of Checkout** (Select One, Required):
	+ Very Fast
	+ Fast
	+ Average
	+ Slow
	+ Very Slow
* **Rating of Mobile Checkout (if applicable)** (Select One, Optional):
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
	+ N/A
* **Did you experience any technical errors?** (Select One, Required):
	+ True
	+ False
* **Describe any errors or frustrations** (Text, Optional): If you experienced technical errors, describe them here.
* **Any features missing from our checkout?** (Text, Optional): Share any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Likelihood to Recommend** (Number, Required): Rate how likely you are to recommend this shop to others based on your checkout experience (0-10 scale).
* **Additional Comments** (Text, Optional): Share any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
* **May we contact you for more details?** (Select One, Optional):
	+ True
	+ False

## Tips

* Please complete the form only for the shop from which you just made a purchase.
* If you're unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to reach out to the shop's customer support.
* Your input will help us improve the checkout process for future customers.
