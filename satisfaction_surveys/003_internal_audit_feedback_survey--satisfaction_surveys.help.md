# Internal Audit Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Internal Audit Feedback Survey is designed to collect opinions and experiences of internal auditors on the current audit process. The survey aims to identify areas that require improvement and enhance the overall quality of the audit process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Scroll through the form and read each question carefully.
* Answer each question truthfully, based on your experiences and opinions.
* For questions with multiple options, select one or more of the available choices that best fit your answer.
* Make sure to provide specific and detailed answers in the text fields.
* If a question is not applicable or does not apply to your situation, please select "N/A" (Not Applicable) or leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Page** (`first_page`, text, not required): This is the introductory page of the form. Please do not fill this field, as it is not applicable.
* **Risk Assessment** (`risk_assessment`, number, not required): This question asks about your perception of the overall risk associated with the audit process. Please answer with a numerical value between 0 and 100, where 0 indicates no risk and 100 indicates extreme risk.
* **Risk Level** (`risk_level`, select_one, not required): Select the level of risk you perceive in your audit experience, from High to Low.
* **Risk Description** (`risk_description`, text, not required): Provide a brief description of the risks you've identified during the audit process.
* **Audit Areas** (`audit_areas`, select_multiple, not required): Indicate which areas of the audit process you've had the most concerns, from Audit of financial statements, internal controls, or internal processes.
* **Finance Survey** (`finance_survey`, number, not required): This question asks about your perception of the overall finance-related risk. Please answer with a numerical value between 0 and 100, where 0 indicates no risk and 100 indicates extreme risk.
* **Audit Team Satisfaction** (`audit_team_satisfaction`, select_one, not required): Select the level of satisfaction you experience with the audit team, from Very Dissatisfied to Very Satisfied.
* **Process Satisfaction** (`process_satisfaction`, select_multiple, not required): Indicate your level of satisfaction with different aspects of the audit process, from Very Dissatisfied to Very Satisfied.
* **Improvement Recommendations** (`improvement_recommendations`, text, not required): Provide specific and detailed suggestions for improving the audit process.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, not required): Select the overall level of satisfaction you experience with the audit process, from Very Dissatisfied to Very Satisfied.

## Tips

* Answer truthfully and honestly, without bias or influence.
* Be specific and detailed in your answers.
* Do not provide duplicate or redundant information.
