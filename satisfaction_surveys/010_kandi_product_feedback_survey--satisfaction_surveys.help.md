# kandi_product_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experiences with Kandi. The goal is to understand how you use Kandi and how we can improve it for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each section and fill in the requested information. You will need to select from the provided options for most questions, and enter text or numbers as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How often do you use Kandi?** (`product_usage`, select_multiple): Select all the ways you use Kandi.
* **How satisfied are you with Kandi?** (`satisfaction_score`, number): Rate your satisfaction with Kandi on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Do you have any improvement ideas for Kandi?** (`improvement_ideas`, text): Share any ideas you have on how we can improve Kandi.
* **Have you encountered any issues with Kandi?** (`issue_report`, text): Report any problems you've had with Kandi.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter your phone number.
* **Company** (`company`, select_one): Select your company.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one): Select your department.
* **Job title** (`job_title`, select_one): Select your job title.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one): Select your role.
* **Location** (`location`, select_one): Select your location.
* **Language** (`language`, select_one): Select your language.
* **Time zone** (`time_zone`, select_one): Select your time zone.
* **Which Kandi product(s) do you use?** (`product_used`, select_multiple): Select all the Kandi products you use.
* **Please specify which Kandi product you use** (`product_used_other`, text): If you use a Kandi product not listed, tell us about it.
* **Any additional feedback about Kandi?** (`feedback`, text): Share any additional thoughts or comments about Kandi.
* **How did you find Kandi?** (`how_did_you_find`, select_one): Tell us how you initially found out about Kandi.
* **Social media** (`social_media`, select_one): Select how you found Kandi through social media.
* **Event** (`event`, select_one): Select how you found Kandi through an event.
* **Email marketing** (`email_marketing`, select_one): Select how you found Kandi through email marketing.
* **Online search** (`online_search`, select_one): Select how you found Kandi through online search.
* **Word of mouth** (`word_of_mouth`, select_one): Select how you found Kandi through word of mouth.

**Tips**

* Make sure to answer all the required questions.
* If you have any additional thoughts or comments, use the "Any additional feedback about Kandi?" field.
* If you use a Kandi product not listed, use the "Please specify which Kandi product you use" field.
