# Travel Service Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from travelers about their experience with a travel service. The goal is to gauge overall satisfaction and identify areas for improvement.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the level of satisfaction with the travel service using the options provided.
2. Provide your contact information if you wish to receive further communication about your experience.
3. If you feel that the travel service could improve, type in your suggestions in the "Travel Service Improvements" field.
4. Optionally, select the time of survey completion if it was completed during your travel.
5. Enter the name of the travel agent who assisted you during your travel.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Service Satisfaction** (`travel_service_satisfaction`): Select the level of satisfaction with the travel service using the options provided.
* **Travel Service Experience** (`overall_experience`): This field is optional and allows you to rate your overall experience with the travel service.
* **Travel Service Satisfaction** (`travel_service_satisfaction_survey_form_2`): Another question about your level of satisfaction with the travel service, this time as a multiple-choice option.
* **Travel Service Improvements** (`travel_service_satisfaction_survey_form_3`): If you have any suggestions for areas of improvement for the travel service, type them in this field.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address if you wish to receive further communication about your experience.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter your phone number if you wish to receive further communication about your experience.
* **Travel Service Survey Completed** (`travel_service_survey_completed`): Optionally, select the time of survey completion if it was completed during your travel.
* **Travel Service Survey Completed Time** (`travel_service_survey_completed_time`): Optionally, select the time of survey completion if it was completed during your travel.
* **Travel Service Agent** (`travel_service_agent`): Enter the name of the travel agent who assisted you during your travel.
* **Travel Service Agent 1** (`travel_service_agent_1`): Another field to enter the name of the travel agent who assisted you during your travel.
