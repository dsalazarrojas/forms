## Work Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Work Satisfaction Survey is designed to gather information about your current job satisfaction. It helps us understand your overall level of satisfaction with various aspects of your work, enabling us to identify areas for improvement and enhance your work experience.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
* Answer each question truthfully, using the numbers that best represent your level of satisfaction.
* Each question is self-explanatory, so simply select the number that best indicates your level of satisfaction with each aspect of your job.
* Make sure to submit your answers to ensure that your feedback is received.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Satisfaction** (1/5): This question asks about your overall job satisfaction. Please select a number between 1 and 5, where 1 means "very dissatisfied" and 5 means "very satisfied".
* **Pay Satisfaction** (2/5): This question asks about your satisfaction with your pay. Please select a number between 1 and 5, where 1 means "very dissatisfied" and 5 means "very satisfied".
* **Benefits Satisfaction** (3/5): This question asks about your satisfaction with the benefits you receive. Please select a number between 1 and 5, where 1 means "very dissatisfied" and 5 means "very satisfied".
* **Team Satisfaction** (4/5): This question asks about your satisfaction with your team. Please select a number between 1 and 5, where 1 means "very dissatisfied" and 5 means "very satisfied".
* **Overall Satisfaction** (5/5): This question asks about your overall satisfaction with your work. Please select a number between 1 and 5, where 1 means "very dissatisfied" and 5 means "very satisfied".

## Tips
* Be honest and truthful in your answers, as your feedback will help us improve your work experience.
* Please note that this survey is designed to be completed anonymously. Your feedback is valued, and we will use it to make informed decisions about your work environment. 
* If you have any comments or suggestions, feel free to include them in the "Comments" section (not shown in this template, but implied).
