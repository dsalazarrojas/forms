# Hair Texture Styling Comfort Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hair Texture Styling Comfort Survey aims to gather your input on your hair texture and comfort level when undergoing hair styling. Your responses will be used to better understand the comfort and preferences associated with different hair textures and styling frequencies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, navigate through each page and fill out the required fields. Each page contains a brief description of the questions and fields on that page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Hair Texture Styling Comfort** (`hair_texture_styling_comfort`, `note`, optional): This is a brief description of the survey's purpose.
  - **Hair Length** (`hair_length`, `number`, optional): Please select your hair length in inches.
  - **Hair Width** (`hair_width`, `number`, optional): Please select your hair width in inches.
  - **Texture Preferred** (`texture_preferred`, `select_one`, optional): Select the texture you generally prefer:
    • fine
    • medium
    • coarse
    • very coarse
    • extremely coarse
  - **Styling Frequency** (`styling_frequency`, `number`, optional): How frequently you style your hair:
    • 30 seconds
    • 1-2 minutes
    • 5-10 minutes
    • more than 10 minutes
  - **Styling Habits** (`styling_habits`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your styling habits:
    • regular
    • occasional
    • rare
  - **Comfort Level** (`comfort_level`, `select_one`, optional): How comfortable do you find your current styling process?
    • very comfortable
    • somewhat comfortable
    • somewhat uncomfortable
    • very uncomfortable
  - **Recommended Products** (`recommended_products`, `note`, optional): Please note any recommended products related to your hair texture and comfort level.
  - **Recommended Services** (`recommended_services`, `note`, optional): Please note any recommended services related to your hair texture and comfort level.
