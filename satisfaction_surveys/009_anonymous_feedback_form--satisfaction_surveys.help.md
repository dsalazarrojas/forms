# Anonymous Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect anonymous feedback from users to help us improve our services. Your responses will be kept confidential and used to identify areas of strength and areas that need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields on each page according to the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Overall Satisfaction (1)
**Overall Satisfaction (1)** (`number`, `required`): Please select a number from 1 to 5 to indicate your overall satisfaction with our services.

### How likely are you to recommend our services to others? (2)
**How likely are you to recommend our services to others? (2)** (`number`, `required`): Answer on a 0 to 10 scale, with higher numbers indicating greater likelihood of recommendation.

### Quality of Service (3)
**Quality of Service (3)** (`select_one`, `required`): Select one of the following options: Poor, Fair, Good, Excellent, Superior.

### Timeliness of Response (4)
**Timeliness of Response (4)** (`select_one`, `required`): Select one of the following options: Very Slow, Slow, Average, Fast, Instant.

### What are our key strengths? (5)
**What are our key strengths? (5)** (`text`, `optional`): Share your thoughts on what we do well. This is a optional question, but your feedback is valuable to us!

### What areas need improvement? (6)
**What areas need improvement? (6)** (`text`, `required`): Let us know what areas you think we can improve in. Be as specific as possible to help us address your concerns.

### How easy was it to use our platform? (7)
**How easy was it to use our platform? (7)** (`select_one`, `required`): Select one of the following options: Very Difficult, Difficult, Neutral, Easy, Very Easy.

### Any other specific feedback or comments (8)
**Any other specific feedback or comments (8)** (`text`, `optional`): Share any additional thoughts or ideas you have about our services. This is a optional question, but your feedback is always welcome!

### What features would you like to see in the future? (9)
**What features would you like to see in the future? (9)** (`text`, `optional`): Suggest any new features that you think would be useful to you or other users.

### How often do you use our services? (10)
**How often do you use our services? (10)** (`select_one`, `required`): Select one of the following options: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely.
