# Skincare Product Layering Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on your skincare routine and preferences. It will help us better understand your individual needs and provide you with personalized recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please read each question carefully and only answer the questions that apply to your current skincare routine.
2. Select the options that best describe your experiences or preferences.
3. Fill in any additional information that is not covered in the other questions.
4. Finally, submit your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (1): This is a note section where you can briefly explain your current skincare routine and any questions or concerns you may have.
* **Skincare Routine** (2): Select all the steps you currently perform in your skincare routine, such as cleansing, toning, exfoliating, etc.
* **Do you currently use a skincare routine?** (3/4/5/6/7): This is a multiple-choice question asking if you currently use a skincare routine. Select "Yes" if you do, and "No" if you do not.
* **Do you currently use multiple skincare products?** (8/9/10/11/12): This is a multiple-choice question asking if you currently use multiple skincare products. Select all the products that apply to your routine.
* **Do you currently pair any products together?** (13/14): This is a multiple-choice question asking if you currently pair products together in your routine. Select all the products that you pair together.
* **Additional Information** (14): This is a text field where you can provide any additional information that is not covered in the other questions.
* **Submit** (15): Please enter your name and email address so that we can contact you with your personalized recommendations.
