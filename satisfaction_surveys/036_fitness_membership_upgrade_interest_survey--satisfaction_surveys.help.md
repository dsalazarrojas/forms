# Fitness Membership Upgrade Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect information about your interest in upgrading your fitness membership. Please answer the questions to help us better understand your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and provide your input in the designated fields.
2. For select options, choose the relevant choices from the available options.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Info**: Enter a brief description about yourself (name, email, etc.).
* **Name**: Please enter your name.
* **Membership Length**: Select the length of membership you are interested in.
* **Upgrade Interest**: Select all the interests you have in upgrading your membership (e.g., more services, better pricing, etc.).
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth (format: YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Note**: Provide any additional comments or information about your interest in upgrading your membership.
* **Select One**: Select one option from the list (not specified in the options, please choose the most suitable option based on your interest).
* **Time**: Enter a time (format: 24-hour clock, e.g., 10:00).
* **Decimal**: Enter a decimal number (please specify the meaning or context of this field if needed).
* **Category**: Enter a category related to your interest in upgrading your membership.
* **Sub Category**: Enter a sub-category related to your interest in upgrading your membership.
* **Sub Category 2**: Enter a second sub-category related to your interest in upgrading your membership.
* **Sub Category 3**: Enter a third sub-category related to your interest in upgrading your membership.
* **Select Multiple 2**: Select all the options that apply to your interest in upgrading your membership (e.g., more services, better pricing, etc.).
* **Select One 2**: Select one option from the list (not specified in the options, please choose the most suitable option based on your interest).
* **Date**: Enter a date (format: YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Time 2**: Enter a time (format: 24-hour clock, e.g., 10:00).
* **Decimal 2**: Enter a decimal number (please specify the meaning or context of this field if needed).
* **Email 2**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone 2**: Enter your phone number.
* **Note 2**: Provide any additional comments or information about your interest in upgrading your membership.
* **Select One 3**: Select one option from the list (not specified in the options, please choose the most suitable option based on your interest).
