# Sponsorship Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sponsorship Satisfaction Survey is designed to collect feedback from sponsors about their experiences with our organization. This survey aims to gauge the effectiveness of our sponsorship initiatives and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the survey, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Survey questions" page to start the survey.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This is the contact information we'll use to keep you informed about your feedback.
3. Provide your comment in the "Comment" field. This is your opportunity to share any thoughts or suggestions about your experience with us.
4. Choose your type of sponsor from the "What type of sponsor?" select list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey questions**: This page contains our questions for you to answer. Please answer each question to the best of your ability.
* **Email**: Please enter the email address you want us to use to contact you about your feedback.
* **Comment**: This is your chance to provide any additional comments or suggestions about your experience with us.
* **What type of sponsor?**: Please choose one of the options that best describes the type of sponsor you are. This will help us understand your perspective.
