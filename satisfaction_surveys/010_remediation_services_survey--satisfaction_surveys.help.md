# Remediation Services Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Remediation Services Survey is designed to collect information from customers or clients about their experiences with remediation services. This form helps us gather feedback and ratings on the quality of service provided, which can aid in improving the overall quality and efficiency of the remediation services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Select the correct category from the dropdown menu.
* Provide a detailed description of your experience with the remediation services.
* Choose your level of satisfaction with the service (1-5).
* Record the date and time of service.
* Select the assigned agent who handled your case.
* Answer the follow-up questions regarding follow-ups sent via email or phone and provide additional notes if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category**: Select the category that best describes your experience with the remediation services. (Required)
* **Description**: Provide a detailed description of your experience with the remediation services. (Optional)
* **Satisfaction Rating**: Rate your level of satisfaction with the service (1-5). (Required)
* **Date of Service**: Record the date of service. (Required)
* **Time of Service**: Record the time of service. (Required)
* **Assigned To**: Select the agent who handled your case. (Required)
* **Email Sent**: Select whether follow-up was sent via email. (Required)
* **Follow Up Date**: Record the date of the follow-up (if sent). (Optional)
* **Follow Up Time**: Record the time of the follow-up (if sent). (Optional)
* **Follow Up Notes**: Provide additional notes about the follow-up. (Optional)
* **Phone Sent**: Select whether follow-up was sent via phone. (Required)
* **Phone Date**: Record the date of the follow-up (if sent). (Optional)
* **Phone Time**: Record the time of the follow-up (if sent). (Optional)
* **Phone Notes**: Provide additional notes about the follow-up. (Optional)
* **Follow Up Status**: Select the status of the follow-up (pending, completed, or cancelled). (Required)
* **Additional Notes**: Provide any additional comments or discussions. (Optional)
* **Email Sent**: Select whether email follow-up was sent. (Required)
* **Email Date**: Record the date of the email follow-up (if sent). (Optional)
* **Email Time**: Record the time of the email follow-up (if sent). (Optional)
* **Email Notes**: Provide additional notes about the email follow-up. (Optional)
* **Phone Number**: Record your phone number. (Optional)
* **Follow Up Status**: Select the status of the follow-up (pending, completed, or cancelled). (Required)
