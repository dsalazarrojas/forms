# Lash Glue Ingredient Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lash Glue Ingredient Awareness Survey is designed to gauge your awareness of different lash glue ingredients and their reactions to them. This survey aims to understand where you typically source information about these ingredients and gather your contact details for follow-up or further clarification.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your knowledge.
2. Select all the ingredients you are aware of from the list provided in the "ingredient_knowledge" field.
3. Choose the severity of your typical reaction to these ingredients from the "reaction" field.
4. Indicate the sources from which you typically gather information about lash glue ingredients from the "information_source" field.
5. If you select "Other" as your information source, please provide a brief description in the "other" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "email" field to ensure we can get in touch with you for further discussion.
7. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field to enable us to contact you for any follow-up questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **title**: The title of this survey is to help identify the form's purpose.
* **ingredient_knowledge**: Select all the ingredients you are aware of from the list below:
	+ Acrylic
	+ Cyanoacrylate
	+ Cyanoacrylate-based
	+ Latex
	+ Lash
	+ Polyurethane-based
	+ Polyvinylpyrrolidone-based
	+ Polyvinylpyrrolidone
	+ PVA
	+ PU-free
	+ Silicone
	+ Silicone-based
	+ Water-based
* **reaction**: Describe the severity of your typical reaction to the ingredients you selected above. Choose from:
	+ Mild
	+ Moderate
	+ Severe
* **information_source**: Indicate where you typically gather information about lash glue ingredients:
	+ Salon
	+ Brand website
	+ Social media
	+ Google
	+ Other (please describe in the "other" field)
	+ None
* **other**: If you selected "Other" as your information source, provide a brief description of where you typically gather information about lash glue ingredients.
* **note**: This field is for any additional comments or notes you may have.
* **email**: Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you for further discussion or clarification.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number to enable us to contact you for any follow-up questions.
