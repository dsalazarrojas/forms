# Housing Accessibility Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand the satisfaction level of our housing accessibility services. We're looking for your feedback on the effectiveness of our accessibility features. Your input will help us improve our services and better serve individuals with disabilities. Please answer honestly, and remember that all answers are anonymous.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction to understand the context of the survey.
2. Select the accessibility features that are currently working well for you from the "Accessibility Features" page.
3. Identify the accessibility barriers you're currently facing from the "Accessibility Barriers" page.
4. Rate your overall satisfaction with the housing accessibility services on the "Overall Satisfaction" page.
5. If you'd like to provide more information, you can add a comment on the "Accessibility Features 1" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Introduction**
(Introduction, note, false) 
This page provides a brief background on the survey and explains the importance of your feedback.

### 2. **Accessibility Features**
(Accessibility Features, select_multiple, false)
Select the accessibility features that are currently working well for you, such as wheelchair accessibility, audio description for visual aids, or braille signage. This helps us understand which features we're doing well and which ones need improvement.

*   0: None
*   1: Some
*   2: Excellent

### 3. **Accessibility Barriers**
(Accessibility Barriers, select_one, false)
Identify the accessibility barriers you're currently facing, such as lack of wheelchair access, inadequate sign language interpretation, or inaccessible website design. This helps us pinpoint areas where we can improve to make our services more accessible.

*   1: Minor issues affecting daily life
*   2: Moderate issues affecting daily activities
*   3: Major issues affecting daily activities

### 4. **Overall Satisfaction**
(Overall Satisfaction, text, false)
Rate your overall satisfaction with the housing accessibility services on a scale of 1 to 10, where 1 is "very dissatisfied" and 10 is "very satisfied". This will help us understand how well we're meeting your needs.

### 5. **Accessibility Features 1**
(Accessibility Features 1, number, false)
If you have any additional thoughts or suggestions on how we can improve our accessibility features, you can provide more information here. Please keep your answer concise and within 1-2 sentences.

Please remember to provide feedback honestly and help us improve our services. Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey!
