# Digital Carbon Footprint Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Carbon Footprint Assessment is designed to help us understand your digital habits and their impact on the environment. It's a self-assessment form that will guide you through a series of questions about your digital behavior, which will help us to identify areas where you can make positive changes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the form, which is divided into several sections.
2. For each section, answer the questions as thoroughly as possible.
3. Make sure to review each question carefully to ensure you're providing accurate and relevant information.
4. When you've completed all the questions, click the "Submit" button to submit your assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Intro Page (1)**: A brief introduction to the form, explaining its purpose and importance.
* **This form is for (2)**: A question to ensure you understand the form's objective and the type of information we're looking for.
* **What do you do? (3)**: Select all your digital habits, such as using social media, playing games, etc.
* **How aware are you of your impact? (4)**: A question to gauge your awareness of the environmental impact of your digital habits.
* **What is your digital footprint? (5)**: Report your estimated digital footprint, taking into account your daily habits and activities.
* **How do you track your efforts? (6)**: Select one way you track or keep a record of your digital habits.
* **Describe your habits (7)**: A text field for you to describe your daily digital habits in detail.
* **What impacts your habits? (8)**: Select all factors that influence your digital habits.
* **What goals and actions? (9)**: A text field for you to outline your goals and actions to reduce your digital footprint.
* **Next Steps (10)**: A note field for you to record any additional suggestions or next steps you'd like to take.
* **Additional Comments (11)**: A text field for any additional comments or feedback.
* **Next Steps (12)**: A select one field for you to choose the next step you'd like to take.
* **Email (13)**: A field for you to enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone (14)**: A field for you to enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Date (15)**: A field for you to enter the date you completed the form.
* **Time (16)**: A field for you to enter the time you completed the form.
* **Category (17)**: A field for you to select a category related to your digital habits.
* **Description (18)**: A text field for you to describe your digital habits in more detail.
* **Assigned Tool (19)**: A field for you to enter the tool or platform you used to complete the form.
* **Output File (20)**: A field for you to upload any output file related to your digital habits.

Note: Please make sure to fill out all required fields and submit the form completely to ensure accurate results.
