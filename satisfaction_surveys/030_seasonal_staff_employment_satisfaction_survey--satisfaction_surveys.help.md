# Seasonal Staff Employment Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from seasonal staff regarding their employment experience with the company. The goal is to gather data on the effectiveness of the company's seasonal staff programs and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the option that best describes your seasonal employer.
2. Enter your seasonal employment start and end dates in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Enter the number of hours you worked during your seasonal employment.
4. Select the rate at which you were paid for your seasonal employment.
5. Choose one of the four options that best describe your overall satisfaction with your seasonal employment.
6. Select all that apply for the seasonal employment questions.
7. Enter any comments about your seasonal employer in the text area provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Seasonal Employer** (text, optional): Enter the name of your seasonal employer.
* **Job Title** (text, optional): Enter the job title you held during your seasonal employment.
* **Seasonal Employment Start Date** (date, optional): Enter the start date of your seasonal employment in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Seasonal Employment End Date** (date, optional): Enter the end date of your seasonal employment in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Seasonal Employment Hours** (number, optional): Enter the total number of hours you worked during your seasonal employment.
* **Seasonal Employment Rate** (number, optional): Enter the hourly wage or rate of pay for your seasonal employment. Note: This field is intended to represent the hourly wage or rate of pay, but the label is not entirely clear.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (select one, optional): This question allows you to choose one of the four options that best describe your overall satisfaction with your seasonal employment. Options include:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
	+ Option 4
* **Seasonal Employment Recommend** (select multiple, optional): Select all that apply for the following questions:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
* **Seasonal Employer Comments** (text, optional): Enter any comments or feedback about your seasonal employer.
