# relationship_satisfaction_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about the relationship between you and another person, such as a coworker, family member, or friend.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. Provide your answers as you would in a personal conversation.
3. Use the provided text fields to describe your relationship and interactions.
4. Answer all required fields (none of them are marked as required in this form, but you are still expected to provide complete and thoughtful responses).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Relationship Survey 1** (`relationship_survey_1`, text, not required): Describe your relationship with this person, e.g., colleague, friend, family member.
* **Relationship Survey 2** (`relationship_survey_2`, text, not required): Describe a recent interaction or experience with this person that comes to mind.
* **Relationship Survey 3** (`relationship_survey_3`, text, not required): Reflect on how you would describe your relationship with this person to a close friend or family member.
* **Relationship Survey 4** (`relationship_survey_4`, text, not required): Describe a positive trait or characteristic you value in this relationship.
* **Relationship Survey 5** (`relationship_survey_5`, text, not required): Describe a challenging or negative aspect of this relationship.
* **Relationship Survey 6** (`relationship_survey_6`, text, not required): Think about a specific situation where you felt supported or connected with this person.
* **Relationship Survey 7** (`relationship_survey_7`, text, not required): Think about a specific situation where you felt unsupported or disconnected with this person.
* **Relationship Survey 8** (`relationship_survey_8`, text, not required): Consider how you would approach conflicts or difficult conversations with this person.
* **Relationship Survey 9** (`relationship_survey_9`, text, not required): Reflect on how you feel about the overall quality of your relationship with this person.
* **Relationship Survey 10** (`relationship_survey_10`, text, not required): Suggest a change or improvement you would like to see in this relationship.

Please note that the form's layout and field labels were created based on public metadata, so some fields might seem repetitive or redundant. However, each field is designed to help you provide a more complete and detailed description of your relationship.
