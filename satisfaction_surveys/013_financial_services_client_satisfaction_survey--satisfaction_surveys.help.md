# Financial Services Client Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Financial Services Client Satisfaction Survey is a form designed to collect feedback from clients about their experiences with our financial services. This survey aims to understand their satisfaction levels and gather suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please make sure to enter your correct name as a client. This information is required.
2. Enter your email address as it will be used for communication purposes.
3. Select the branch you interacted with us, as this will help us identify areas of improvement.
4. Indicate your level of satisfaction with our services. Choose from the provided options.
5. Choose the main reason for your interaction with us from the provided options.
6. Let us know if you would recommend our services to a friend or colleague. Select the appropriate option.
7. If you have any suggestions or recommendations, please type them in the provided field.
8. Please note that the submission date and time are not required, but will help us track feedback timelines.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name (client_name)**: This field is for your name. Please enter the name that corresponds to your account information.
* **Client Email (client_email)**: This field is for your email address that we may use for communication.
* **Branch (branch)**: This field is for the branch you interacted with us. Choose the correct option that corresponds to your interaction.
* **How satisfied are you with our service? (question1)**: Please select from the provided options to indicate your level of satisfaction with our services.
* **What was the main reason for your interaction with us? (question2)**: Select all reasons that apply from the provided options.
* **Would you recommend our services to a friend or colleague? (question3)**: Let us know if you would recommend our services or not.
* **Do you have any suggestions or recommendations? (suggestion)**: If you have any comments or suggestions, please type them in this field. This is optional.
* **Submission Date (submission_date)**: This field will automatically be filled with the current date. You don't need to worry about it.
* **Submission Time (submission_time)**: This field will automatically be filled with the current time. You don't need to worry about it.
* **Submission Status (submission_status)**: Please select the status of your submission. Choose from the provided options.
* **Client Feedback (client_feedback)**: Let us know if we've met your expectations or not.
