# Camper Satisfaction Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide will help you complete the Camper Satisfaction Survey Form, which is designed to gather feedback from campers about their overall experience during their camp session. This form aims to understand camper satisfaction, preferences, and opinions on various aspects of their stay.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin with the first page, "Respondent Information," where you will find questions that gather some basic information about yourself.
2. On the second page, "Camp Experience," you will be asked about your overall satisfaction, favorite activities, and other aspects of your camp experience.
3. On the third page, "Activities and Programming," you will find questions about the variety of activities offered and any you wish were available.
4. Move on to the "Counselors and Staff" page, where you will rate the quality and helpfulness of the staff.
5. The "Facilities and Accommodations" page is where you will provide feedback on the quality of the sleeping quarters, food, and cleanliness of facilities.
6. On the "Social Experience" page, you will be asked about making new friends and the sense of community at camp.
7. Finally, on the "Future Intentions" page, you will provide suggestions for improving the camp experience and indicate your likelihood of returning next year.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`camper_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as you would like to be referred to in the survey feedback.
* **Age** (`camper_age`, number, optional): Enter your age as a number (e.g., 15, 18, etc.).
* **Camp Session Attended** (`camp_session_attended`, text, required): Please specify the camp session you attended.
* **Cabin or Group Name** (`cabin_group`, text, optional): Enter the name of your cabin or group.
* **Overall, how satisfied were you with camp** (`overall_satisfaction`, select one, required): Choose how satisfied you were with the camp experience, using the following options:
	+ Extremely Satisfied
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Not Satisfied
* **What was the best part of your camp experience** (`best_part_of_camp`, text, optional): Describe your favorite aspect of the camp.
* **Variety of activities offered** (`activity_variety`, select one, required): Choose how satisfied you were with the variety of activities offered, using the following options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Below Average
	+ Poor
* **Your favorite activities or events** (`favorite_activities`, text, optional): List your favorite activities or events from the camp.
* **Were there activities you wished camp offered** (`activities_missed`, text, optional): If applicable, list activities you wished were available during your stay.
* **Quality of counselors and staff** (`counselor_quality`, select one, required): Choose how well you thought the counselors and staff performed their roles, using the following options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Below Average
	+ Poor
* **Counselors were helpful and supportive** (`counselor_supportive`, select one, required): Choose how much you agree that the counselors were helpful and supportive, using the following options:
	+ Strongly Agree
	+ Agree
	+ Neutral
	+ Disagree
	+ Strongly Disagree
* **Quality of sleeping accommodations** (`sleeping_accommodations`, select one, required): Choose how well you thought the sleeping quarters met your needs, using the following options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Below Average
	+ Poor
* **Quality of food and meals** (`food_quality`, select one, required): Choose how well you thought the food and meals were, using the following options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Below Average
	+ Poor
* **Cleanliness of facilities and bathrooms** (`facilities_cleanliness`, select one, required): Choose how clean you thought the facilities and bathrooms were, using the following options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Below Average
	+ Poor
* **I made new friends at camp** (`made_new_friends`, select one, required): Choose how much you agree that you made new friends, using the following options:
	+ Strongly Agree
	+ Agree
	+ Neutral
	+ Disagree
	+ Strongly Disagree
* **Strong sense of community at camp** (`sense_of_community`, select one, required): Choose how much you agree that there was a strong sense of community, using the following options:
	+ Strongly Agree
	+ Agree
	+ Neutral
	+ Disagree
	+ Strongly Disagree
* **Would you attend camp again** (`return_next_year`, select one, required): Choose how likely you are to attend the camp again, using the following options:
	+ Definitely Yes
	+ Probably Yes
	+ Maybe
	+ Probably No
	+ Definitely No
* **Suggestions for improving camp experience** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, optional): Share any suggestions you have for enhancing the camp experience.

Please answer these questions to provide valuable feedback and help us improve our camp for future campers!
