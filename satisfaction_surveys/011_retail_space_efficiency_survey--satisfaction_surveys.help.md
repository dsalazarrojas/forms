# Retail Space Efficiency Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your retail store's efficiency and operations. Your input will help us better understand your store's strengths and weaknesses, allowing us to provide more effective support and guidance to improve your store's performance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields, as marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How is your store layout organized?** (`store_layout`)
	+ Please describe your store's layout, including the arrangement of merchandise, checkout counters, and aisle spacing.
* **What is the size of your store in square feet?** (`store_size`)
	+ Provide the total square footage of your store, including any storage areas or backrooms.
* **Where is your store located?** (`store_location`)
	+ Provide the street address or geographic location of your store.
* **How many shelving units are in your store?** (`shelving_units`)
	+ Count the total number of shelving units in your store, including those on the floor and in stockrooms.
* **What are your stock levels currently?** (`stock_levels`)
	+ Choose from Low, Medium, or High to describe your current stock levels.
* **How many hours do you open during the week?** (`store_opening_hours`)
	+ Provide the total number of hours your store operates during the week.
* **How many hours do you open during each day?** (`store_hours_per_day`)
	+ Provide the number of hours your store operates each day.
* **How would you rate the flow of customers into your store?** (`customer_flow`)
	+ Rate the flow of customers as Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Is the store clutter-free?** (`store_clutter`)
	+ Choose True or False, indicating whether your store is clutter-free or not.
* **How would you rate the store's layout?** (`store_layout_2`)
	+ Rate the store's layout as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **What percentage of shelves are stocked?** (`store_shelving`)
	+ Provide the percentage of shelves that are currently stocked with merchandise.
* **Are there counts for each item?** (`stock_counts`)
	+ Choose True or False, indicating whether you have counts for each item in stock.
* **How often do you train your employees?** (`employee_training`)
	+ Rate the frequency of employee training as Often, Occasionally, Rarely, or Never.
* **How would you rate your customer service?** (`customer_service`)
	+ Rate your customer service as 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5.
* **How many employees does your store have?** (`employee_numbers`)
	+ Provide the total number of employees working at your store.
* **What are your inventory levels currently?** (`inventory_levels`)
	+ Choose from Low, Medium, or High to describe your current inventory levels.
* **What is your employee scheduling method?** (`employee_scheduling`)

* **What is your sales growth over the past 12 months?** (`sales_growth`)
	+ Provide the percentage of sales growth over the past 12 months.
* **What is your profit margin?** (`profit_margin`)
	+ Provide the profit margin as a percentage.
* **What are your labor costs as a percentage?** (`labor_costs`)
	+ Provide the labor costs as a percentage of total sales.
