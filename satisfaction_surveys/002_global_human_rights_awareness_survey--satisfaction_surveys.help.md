# Global Human Rights Awareness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from individuals about their level of awareness and attitude towards human rights. This form is designed to gather general information, awareness levels, attitude towards human rights, demographic information, and contact details from individuals. The form is used to gauge the community's understanding and perception of human rights and to gather feedback for future development of human rights-related initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form pages to understand what each page is asking for.
2. Fill out the general information page with any relevant comments or notes.
3. Rate your awareness levels towards human rights on the second page.
4. Express your attitude towards human rights on the third page.
5. Provide your demographic information on the fourth page.
6. Leave any additional comments on the fifth page.
7. Enter your email address and phone number on the sixth and seventh pages, respectively.
8. Review your form and submit it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_info`, `text`, required: false): This page contains space for any general comments or notes about human rights awareness.
* **Awareness Levels** (`awareness_levels`, `text`, required: false): Please rate your current level of awareness towards human rights on a scale from 0 to 10.
* **Attitude Towards Human Rights** (`attitude_towards_human_rights`, `text`, required: false): Express your feelings or thoughts about human rights in a few sentences.
* **Demographic Information** (`demographic_info`, `text`, required: false): This page contains space for you to provide any demographic information about yourself such as age, sex, or location.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Leave any additional comments or suggestions about human rights awareness.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: true): Please enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: true): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `note`, required: true): Click this button to submit your form once you have filled out all the required fields.
