# Sober Living House Manager Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sober Living House Manager Survey is designed to gather information from sober living house managers to track their progress and experiences. This form helps us better understand the challenges and needs of house managers, allowing us to provide more effective support and resources.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all the questions truthfully and to the best of your knowledge.
2. If you're unsure about any question, please use the "Comments" field to provide more information.
3. If you have any additional comments or concerns, please use the "Other" field.
4. Please complete all the questions on each page before submitting the form.
