# Power Layout Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to collect feedback on the power layout of a specific system or application. Please take a few minutes to complete this form and provide your honest feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. For the "satisfaction" field, select the option that best describes your overall satisfaction with the layout.
3. For the "how_do_you_use_layout" field, describe in detail how you use the layout, including any specific features or sections you use frequently.
4. For the "have_any_improvement_suggestions" field, select all the options that apply to you. You can also provide additional suggestions in the comments field.
5. If you want to provide any comments, please use the "comments" field.
6. If you want to be contacted for further questions or discussions, please provide your email address and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, `number`, required: false): Please rate your overall satisfaction with the layout on a scale of 1-5.
* **How Do You Use Layout** (`how_do_you_use_layout`, `text`, required: false): Describe how you use the layout, including any specific features or sections you use frequently. This will help us understand your needs and improve the layout accordingly.
* **Satisfaction Rating** (`satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the option that best describes your overall satisfaction with the layout.
* **Improvement Suggestions** (`have_any_improvement_suggestions`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the options that apply to you, or provide additional suggestions in the comments field.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required: false): Please use this field to provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the layout.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): If you want to be contacted for further questions or discussions, please provide your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): If you want to be contacted by phone, please provide your phone number.
