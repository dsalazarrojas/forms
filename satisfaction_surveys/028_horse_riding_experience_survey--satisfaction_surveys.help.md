# Horse Riding Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their horse riding experience. The survey includes various fields that ask about the quality and rating of their tour, instructor, and other aspects of their visit. The goal is to gather information that can be used to improve the riding school's services and ensure customer satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, please fill out each field with the requested information.
2. Start with the "Riding School Name" field, which is the name of the riding school where you took the tour.
3. Enter the name of the instructor who led your tour.
4. Rate the quality of the tour by selecting "Good" or "Fair" from the "Tour Rating" field.
5. Rate the quality of the tour by selecting a number between 1-10 in the "Tour Rating Score" field.
6. If you have any comments or feedback about the tour, type them in the "Comments" field.
7. Choose whether you had any riding experience or not by selecting from the "Riding Experience" field.
8. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
9. Select the date of the tour from the calendar or enter it manually in the "Tour Date" field.
10. Choose the time of the tour from the time selector or enter it manually in the "Tour Time" field.
11. Rate the quality of the instructor by selecting "Good" or "Fair" from the "Instructor Rating" field.
12. Provide a description of your rating in the "Instructor Rating Description" field.
13. Enter the name of the instructor who led your tour in the "Instructor Name" field.
14. Choose whether you had any riding experience or not by selecting from the "Riding Experience 2" field.
15. Enter the ID of the riding school in the "Riding School ID" field.
16. Enter the ID of the instructor in the "Instructor ID" field.
17. Enter the ID of the tour in the "Tour ID" field.
18. If you have any additional comments, type them in the "Comments 2" field.
19. Enter the score for the instructor's rating in the "Instructor Rating Score" field.
20. Provide a description of your rating in the "Instructor Rating Description 2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Riding School Name** (`riding_school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the riding school where you took the tour.
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the instructor who led your tour.
* **Tour Rating** (`tour_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of the tour by selecting "Good" or "Fair".
* **Tour Quality** (`tour_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of the tour by selecting "Good" or "Fair".
* **Experience** (`experience`, text, optional): Choose whether you had any riding experience or not by selecting from the "Riding Experience" field.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Tour Date** (`tour_date`, date, required): Select the date of the tour from the calendar or enter it manually.
* **Tour Time** (`tour_time`, time, required): Choose the time of the tour from the time selector or enter it manually.
* **Riding Experience** (`riding_experience`, select_multiple, required): Choose whether you had any riding experience or not by selecting from the options.
* **Tour Rating Score** (`tour_rating_score`, number, required): Rate the quality of the tour by selecting a number between 1-10.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): If you have any comments or feedback about the tour, type them here.
* **Comments 2** (`comments_2`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments, type them here.
* **Instructor Rating** (`instructor_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of the instructor by selecting "Good" or "Fair".
* **Instructor Rating Description** (`instructor_rating_description`, text, optional): Provide a description of your rating in this field.
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the instructor who led your tour.
* **Riding Experience 2** (`riding_experience_2`, select_multiple, required): Choose whether you had any riding experience or not by selecting from the options.
* **Comments 3** (`comments_3`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments, type them here.
* **Instructor Rating Score** (`instructor_rating_score`, number, required): Enter the score for the instructor's rating.
* **Instructor Rating Description 2** (`instructor_rating_description_2`, text, optional): Provide a description of your rating in this field.
* **Riding School ID** (`riding_school_id`, number, required): Enter the ID of the riding school.
* **Instructor ID** (`instructor_id`, number, required): Enter the ID of the instructor.
* **Tour ID** (`tour_id`, number, required): Enter the ID of the tour.
* **Comments 4** (`comments_4`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments, type them here.
