# Saddle Bench Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Saddle Bench Feedback Survey is designed to collect information about your experience with the Saddle Bench. This includes how comfortable you found the bench and if you have any ideas to improve it. The form is not mandatory and you can choose whether to answer each question.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by clicking on the first page of the form.
2. Read the label for each field to understand what information is being requested.
3. Enter your answer to each question, using the options provided for select multiple fields.
4. Click on the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback About Your Visit**: This field is for you to provide any comments or feedback about your recent visit with the Saddle Bench. You can enter any details you feel are relevant, such as what you liked or disliked about the bench.
* **How Comfortable Is Your Saddle Bench**:
	+ Very uncomfortable
	+ Somewhat uncomfortable
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat comfortable
	+ Very comfortable
This field asks you to rate your comfort level when using the Saddle Bench. You can select one or more of the options that apply to you.
* **Do You Have Any Improvement Ideas?**: This field is for you to share any ideas you have on how to improve the Saddle Bench. This could be suggestions for changes you would like to see or ideas for new features.

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback or improvement ideas.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
