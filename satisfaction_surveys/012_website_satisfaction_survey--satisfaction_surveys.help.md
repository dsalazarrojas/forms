# Website Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Website Satisfaction Survey is designed to gather feedback from users about different aspects of our website. This feedback will help us understand what we are doing well and what we can improve on. Please take a few minutes to complete this survey and share your honest thoughts about our website.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2. For the "Overall Satisfaction" question, please rate your satisfaction on a scale (e.g. 1-5).
3. If you want to follow up on your feedback, please provide your email address or phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Website Home Page** (`home_page`, text, required: false): Please describe your experience on our website's home page. What do you like or dislike about it?
* **Website Search** (`search_page`, text, required: false): How did you find our website's search function? What could we improve on?
* **Website Navigation** (`navigation_page`, text, required: false): How easy or difficult was it to navigate our website? What could we improve on?
* **Website Content** (`content_page`, text, required: false): What do you think about the content on our website? Is it informative, engaging, or could we improve on it?
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with our website on a scale (e.g. 1-5).
* **Feedback** (`feedback_page`, text, required: false): Is there anything else you'd like to share about your experience on our website? Please let us know!
* **Email** (`email_input`, email, required: false): If you want to follow up on your feedback, please provide your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): If you want to follow up on your feedback, you can also provide your phone number.

## Tips

* Please be honest and thorough in your answers.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* We value your feedback and appreciate your time in completing this survey!
