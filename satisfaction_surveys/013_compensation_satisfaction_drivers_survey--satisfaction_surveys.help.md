# Compensation Satisfaction Drivers Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information on your perception of your compensation package and what drives your satisfaction with it. Your responses will help us understand what you value most and where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your job role or title in the "Job Role or Title" field (optional).
2. Rate your overall satisfaction with your compensation on a scale of 1-5 in the "Overall Satisfaction with Compensation" field.
3. Choose the element of compensation that you value the most in the "Most Valued Element of Compensation" field.
4. If you have any suggestions for improvement, provide them in the "Comments on Compensation" field (optional).
5. Indicate if you're willing to be contacted by HR regarding your responses in the "Willing to Speak with HR" field (optional).
6. Submit your responses to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Role or Title** (`job role or title`, text, optional): Enter your current job title or role.
* **Overall Satisfaction with Compensation** (`satisfaction_level`, number, required): Rate your satisfaction with your compensation on a scale of 1 (low) to 5 (high).
* **Most Valued Element of Compensation** (`most_valued_element`, select_one, required): Choose the element of your compensation that you value the most. Options are: 
	+ Salary
	+ Benefits
	+ Bonuses
	+ Work life balance
	+ Career development
* **Highest Priority for Improvement** (`improvement_priority`, select_one, optional): Choose the area you'd like to see improved most. Options are: 
	+ Increase salary
	+ Improve benefits
	+ More transparency
	+ More recognition
* **Comments on Compensation** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional feedback or suggestions for improvement.
* **Willing to Speak with HR** (`willing_to_consult`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you're willing to be contacted by HR regarding your responses.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Date of your responses, automatically filled in by the system.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate with your responses, as they will help us understand your needs and preferences.
* If you have any additional feedback or suggestions, use the "Comments on Compensation" field to provide them.
* Please note that your responses will not be repeated or compared over time. This form is a one-time assessment.
