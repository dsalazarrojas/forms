# Job Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Job Satisfaction Survey** is designed to gather your feedback on your job experience at the company. Your input will help us understand your thoughts and feelings about various aspects of your job and company culture, enabling us to make informed decisions to improve your work environment. Please answer these questions honestly, and your responses will remain anonymous.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, simply follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and choose the option that best reflects your feelings or experiences.
2. For **select_one** questions, choose one option from the list provided.
3. For **select_multiple** questions, select all the options that apply.
4. For **note** questions, type your thoughts or comments in the space provided.
5. Make sure to answer all questions marked as **required**.
