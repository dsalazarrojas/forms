# Retail Employee Shift Satisfaction Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a satisfaction survey for retail employees to share their experiences about their shifts. It aims to gather feedback on shift length, break times, and manager interactions. Your input will help us understand your shift experiences and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Provide accurate information about your shift, including shift length, start and end times, and break times.
3. If you have any suggestions or comments, use the "Team Member Suggestions" or "Employee Suggestions" fields.
4. For "Break Status", select "Yes" if you took a break, "No" if you didn't, or "Other" if your situation was different.
5. Don't forget to include your manager's name and contact information if you have any feedback or issues.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Retail Employee Shift Satisfaction** (`employee_shift_satisfaction`, note, not applicable): This field is not required, but it's a placeholder for any notes or comments about your shift experience.
* **Shift Length** (`shift_length`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours you were on shift.
* **Break Length** (`break_length`, number, optional): Enter the number of minutes you took for break.
* **Shift Start Time** (`shift_start_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you started your shift.
* **Shift End Time** (`shift_end_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you ended your shift.
* **Break Start Time** (`break_start_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you took your break.
* **Break End Time** (`break_end_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you ended your break.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): If you have any feedback or issues, mention your manager's name.
* **Manager Contact** (`manager_contact`, text, optional): If you have any feedback or issues, mention your manager's contact information.
* **Team Member Suggestions** (`team_member_suggestions`, text, optional): Share any suggestions you have for improving your team or work experience.
* **Manager Response** (`manager_response`, text, optional): If you have any feedback or issues, mention your manager's response or any action taken.
* **Employee Suggestions** (`employee_suggestions`, text, optional): Share any suggestions you have for improving your own work experience or our company's policies.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or feedback about your shift experience.
* **Shift Date** (`shift_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you worked this shift.
* **Shift Time** (`shift_time`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your shift time or any relevant details.
* **Break Status** (`break_status`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you took a break, "No" if you didn't, or "Other" if your situation was different.
