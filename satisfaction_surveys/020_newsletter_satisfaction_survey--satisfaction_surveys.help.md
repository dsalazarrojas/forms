# Newsletter Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback on the company's newsletter to ensure it meets the needs and preferences of its users. This information will help the company to improve and adjust the newsletter content and format for better engagement and satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. On the first page, you will find a question about the rating of the newsletter. Please rate the newsletter on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
3. On the second page, you will have an opportunity to provide comments about the newsletter. Please share your thoughts and suggestions on how the company can improve it.
4. On the third page, you will be asked for your email address. This will help us to contact you if we need further information.
5. On the fourth page, you will choose your preferred format for the newsletter (Newsletter, Letter, or Email).
6. On the final page, you will select the frequency of reading the newsletter (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Never).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rating (1-5)** (`Rating`, `number`, required): This question asks about your level of satisfaction with the newsletter. Please answer on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Comments (optional)** (`Comments`, `text`, optional): This is an open-ended field where you can provide any suggestions or thoughts about the newsletter.
* **Email Address (required)** (`Email Address`, `email`, required): Please provide your email address so we can contact you if we need further information.
* **Preferred Format (multiple choices)** (`Preferred Format`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please select the format you prefer for the newsletter (Newsletter, Letter, or Email).
* **Frequency of Reading (single choice)** (`Frequency of Reading`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the frequency of reading the newsletter (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Never).

## Tips
- Answer the questions that apply to you the most, and feel free to skip the rest.
- Please be as specific as possible when answering the questions.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.

Note: The form structure and content may be adjusted or modified according to business needs. This help guide is subject to change.
