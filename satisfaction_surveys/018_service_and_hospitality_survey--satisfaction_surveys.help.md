# Service And Hospitality Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experience with our service and hospitality.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Choose your answers for each question from the provided options.
2. Enter your email address and phone number (if you want to be contacted for follow-up questions).
3. Provide any comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.
4. Click the submit button to send your responses.
