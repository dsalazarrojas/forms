# Hair Mask Usage Frequency Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to collect information about your habits and satisfaction level with using hair masks. The goal is to gather data on how often you use hair masks and your level of satisfaction with them. This will help us understand your preferences and habits.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please select your answer for the first question "How often do you use hair masks?".
2. Choose your level of satisfaction with the hair mask in the second question.
3. If you want to express additional preferences for hair masks, you can select them in the third question (you can select more than one option).
4. If you have other preferences that are not listed, you can type them in the "Other preferences" field.
5. Finally, add any additional comments you would like to share about your experience with hair masks in the last question "Additional comments".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How often do you use hair masks? (hair_mask_usage_frequency)**: This question asks about your frequency of using hair masks. Please select one of the options that best describes how often you use hair masks.
* **How satisfied are you with the hair mask? (satisfaction)**: This question is about your level of satisfaction with the hair mask. Please choose the option that best reflects your feelings.
* **What are your hair mask preferences? (hair_mask_preferences)**: This question is multiple choice. Select all the preferences that apply to you, such as "Natural ingredients", "Moisturizing", "Color protecting", or "Hydrating".
* **Other preferences (other_preferences)**: If you have any other preferences that are not listed, you can type them in this field.
* **Additional comments (note)**: If you want to share more information about your experience with hair masks, you can write it in this field.
