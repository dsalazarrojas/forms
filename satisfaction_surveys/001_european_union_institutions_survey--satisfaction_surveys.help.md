# european_union_institutions_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "European Union Institutions Survey" is a form used to collect feedback and information from individuals representing European Union institutions. This form helps to gather insights on the level of satisfaction with the European Union institutions, the frequency of contact, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked as *required*.
2. Select all the European Union bodies you work with in the "EU Bodies" field.
3. Choose your level of satisfaction with the institution in the "Satisfaction level" field.
4. Fill in your email address and phone number in the respective fields.
5. Optionally, provide general feedback in the "General feedback" field.
6. Select the type of institution you represent and its size, location, and language.
7. Finally, fill in any additional comments you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General feedback** (text, optional): A field to provide any general feedback or comments about the European Union institutions.
* **EU Bodies** (select_one, required): Select all the European Union bodies you work with.
* **Satisfaction level** (select_multiple, required): Choose your level of satisfaction with the institution from the options provided.
* **Email** (email, required): Fill in your email address.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Fill in your phone number.
* **Comments** (note, optional): Provide any additional comments you may have.
* **Additional comments** (text, optional): Any other comments you may have.
* **Name** (text, required): Fill in your name.
* **Institution** (text, required): Fill in the name of the institution you represent.
* **Department** (text, required): Fill in the department within the institution you represent.
* **Position** (text, required): Fill in your position within the institution.
* **Contact Name** (text, required): Fill in the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (email, required): Fill in the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (text, required): Fill in the phone number of the contact person.
* **Institution Address** (text, optional): Fill in the address of the institution.
* **Department Address** (text, optional): Fill in the address of the department.
* **Contact Address** (text, optional): Fill in the address of the contact person.
* **Date** (date, optional): Fill in the date of the feedback.
* **Time** (time, optional): Fill in the time of the feedback.
* **Institution type** (select_multiple, required): Select the type of institution you represent (European Institution or International Organisation).
* **Institution size** (select_one, required): Select the size of the institution you represent.
* **Region** (select_multiple, required): Select the region of the institution.
* **Language** (select_multiple, required): Select the languages spoken by the institution.
* **Comments** (note, optional): Any other comments you may have.
