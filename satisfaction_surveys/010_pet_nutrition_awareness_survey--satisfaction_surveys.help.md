# Pet Nutrition Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to gather information about pet nutrition awareness from pet owners. The goal is to collect data on pet feeding habits, nutritional knowledge, and contact information to better understand pet owners' behavior and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer them to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that apply to your pet's feeding habits, nutritional knowledge, and contact information.
3. Fill in any text fields with accurate and complete information.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feeding Habits** (`Feeding habits`, `select_multiple`): This question asks about how often you feed your pet. Select all the options that apply to your feeding habits.
* **Nutrient Knowledge** (`Nutrient Knowledge`, `select_one`): This question asks about what kind of nutrients you are aware of. Select the option that best describes your knowledge.
* **What is a healthy pet label?** (`What is a healthy pet label?`, `select_multiple`): This question asks about what you consider a healthy pet label. Select all the options that apply to your beliefs.
* **How often do you feed your pet?** (`How often do you feed your pet?`, `select_one`): This question asks about how often you feed your pet. Select the option that best describes your feeding frequency.
* **What is your pet's size?** (`What is your pet's size?`, `select_multiple`): This question asks about your pet's size. Select all the options that apply to your pet's size.
* **What is your pet's age?** (`What is your pet's age?`, `number`): This question asks about your pet's age. Fill in the exact age of your pet.
* **Age** (`Age`, `number`): This question is a duplicate of the previous one, it seems to be a redundant question.
* **Contact Method** (`Contact Method`, `select_multiple`): This question asks about how you would like to be contacted. Select all the options that apply to you.
* **Owner Name** (`Owner Name`, `text`): This question asks for your name.
* **Owner Email** (`Owner Email`, `email`): This question asks for your email address.
* **Owner Phone** (`Owner Phone`, `text`): This question asks for your phone number.
* **Owner Address** (`Owner Address`, `text`): This question asks for your address.
* **Owner City** (`Owner City`, `text`): This question asks for your city.
* **Owner State** (`Owner State`, `text`): This question asks for your state or region.
* **Owner Zip** (`Owner Zip`, `text`): This question asks for your zip code or postal code.
* **Owner Country** (`Owner Country`, `text`): This question asks for your country of residence.
* **Owner Notes** (`Owner Notes`, `text`): This question asks for any additional comments or notes you would like to provide.
