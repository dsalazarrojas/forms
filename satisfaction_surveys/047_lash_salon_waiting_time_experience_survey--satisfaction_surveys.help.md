# Lash Salon Waiting Time Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to help us understand your experience with our salon, specifically regarding the waiting time. Your feedback will help us to identify areas of improvement and to ensure that we provide the best possible experience for you and other clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your answer to each question, based on your experience with our salon.
2. Fill in your email and phone number if you wish to be contacted for any follow-up questions or comments.
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback you have in the overall experience with our salon.
4. Click submit to send your survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How would you rate the friendliness of your experience** (`client_info`, `select_one`, required): Please choose one of the following options to rate how friendly you found our staff to be:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
* **Waiting time was** (`waiting_time`, `select_one`, required): Please select one of the following options to indicate whether you had to wait:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Appointment time was** (`appointment_time`, `select_one`, required): Please select one of the following options to indicate whether your appointment time was kept:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **How would you rate the communication with your technician** (`communication`, `select_one`, required): Please choose one of the following options to rate the communication with your technician:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
* **Overall experience was** (`overall_experience`, `select_one`, required): Please select one of the following options to rate your overall experience:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Do you have any additional comments** (`feedback`, `text`, optional): Please use this field to provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Please enter your email address if you wish to be contacted for any follow-up questions or comments.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Please enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted for any follow-up questions or comments.
* **Rate our salon** (`rating`, `number`, required): Please rate our salon out of 5 stars, with 1 star being the lowest and 5 stars being the highest.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Please use this field to provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
