# Selfie Perception Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Selfie Perception Survey is designed to collect information about your selfie-taking habits, motivations, and perceptions. This survey aims to understand how you view and interact with selfies, as well as the frequency and frequency of your selfie-taking activities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the options that best fit your selfie-taking habits and attitudes for each field.
2. For fields with "Other, please specify" options, provide a brief description of your preference or behavior.
3. Fill out the survey carefully and honestly, as it will help us better understand the selfie culture and its impact on individuals.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Selfie Habits** (`selfie_habits`): How often do you take selfies?
	+ Frequent: I take selfies very often.
	+ Occasionally: I take selfies sometimes.
	+ Rarely: I rarely take selfies.
	+ Never: I never take selfies.

	+ Other, please specify: Provide a brief description of your reason.
