# post_presentation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from users after a presentation has been delivered. It helps identify areas of improvement for future presentations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your feedback in the "Survey Form" field.
2. Answer the "Questions" field.
3. Add any additional comments in the "Comments" field.
4. Rate your experience with the presentation in the "Rating" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
6. Add your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
7. Select the date and time of the presentation from the "Date" and "Time" fields.
8. Add any additional notes in the "Additional Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Form** (`survey_form`, text, optional): Enter your feedback about the presentation.
* **Questions** (`questions`, text, optional): Answer any questions related to the presentation.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about the presentation.
* **Rating** (`rating`, select_one, optional): Rate your experience with the presentation (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or No Opinion).
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for any follow-up communication.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Add your phone number for any follow-up communication.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select the date of the presentation.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select the time of the presentation.
* **Additional Notes** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the presentation.
