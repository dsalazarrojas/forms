# Neighborhood Satisfaction Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Neighborhood Satisfaction Questionnaire is designed to collect information about your experience and perceptions about your neighborhood. It will help us understand what you like and dislike about your neighborhood and how we can improve it for you and others.

## How to Complete This Form
The form has several sections to gather information from you about different aspects of your neighborhood. Please follow the steps below to complete the form:

1. For each question, select the corresponding answer options that best describe your feelings about your neighborhood.
2. If a question has multiple options, select all the options that apply to you.
3. For any question that has a text field, provide as much detail as possible about your thoughts and feelings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Neighborhood Satisfaction (1)** (`neighborhood_satisfaction`, text, required/optional): This question is where you can share your overall satisfaction with your neighborhood. Please type a brief description of what you like or dislike about your neighborhood.
* **Experience (2)** (`experience`, number, required/optional): This question asks about your level of experience with your neighborhood. Choose a number that represents how long you have lived in your neighborhood.
* **Cleanliness (3)** (`cleanliness`, number, required/optional): This question is about how clean and well-maintained you think your neighborhood is. Choose a number that reflects your perception.
* **Amenities (4)** (`amenities`, select_multiple, required/optional): Check all the amenities that you find useful or enjoyable in your neighborhood. This could include parks, community events, public restrooms, and security.
* **Safety (5)** (`safety`, number, required/optional): This question is about how safe you feel in your neighborhood. Choose a number that represents your level of comfort.
* **Communication (6)** (`communication`, select_one, required/optional): Select the option that best describes your level of satisfaction with communication in your neighborhood. Choose from Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Somewhat Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.
* **Engagement (7)** (`engagement`, select_multiple, required/optional): Check all the options that describe your level of engagement with your neighborhood. This could be attending community events, participating in neighborhood activities, or feeling a strong sense of belonging.
* **Community Feeling (8)** (`community_feeling`, note, required/optional): Use this text field to describe your feelings about your neighborhood community. Please provide as much detail as possible.
* **Community Involvement (9)** (`community_involvement`, select_multiple, required/optional): Check all the options that describe your level of involvement with your neighborhood community. This could be volunteering, participating in local events, or helping others.
* **Neighborhood Satisfaction Score (10)** (`neighborhood_satisfaction_score`, number, required/optional): This question asks you to provide a numerical score that represents your overall satisfaction with your neighborhood. Please choose a number between 1 and 10.
* **Overall Rating (11)** (`overall_rating`, number, required/optional): This question asks you to provide a numerical rating that represents your overall satisfaction with your neighborhood. Please choose a number between 1 and 10.
* **Comments (12)** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Use this text field to provide any additional comments or feedback about your neighborhood. Please be as specific as possible.
