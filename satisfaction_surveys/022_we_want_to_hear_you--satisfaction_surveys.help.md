# we_want_to_hear_you - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and information from end-users related to a satisfaction survey.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Start" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Choose a category for your entry (Schools, Nonprofits, or Businesses).
3. Provide a brief description of your entry.
4. Select your satisfaction rating (1-5 stars).
5. Optionally, you can attach files or documents to support your entry.
6. Leave any additional feedback or comments.
7. Enter your phone number and email address (if applicable).
8. Enter your name and organization name (if applicable).
9. Repeat the file upload steps if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **We Want To Hear You** (1): This is a title field for your entry, provide a brief and clear title that summarizes your description.
* **Category** (2): Choose a category from the dropdown menu that best describes the organization or entity that you are representing (e.g., Schools, Nonprofits, Businesses).
* **Description** (3): Provide a brief description of your organization or the reason for your entry (1000 characters max).
* **Satisfaction Rating** (4): Select a rating from 1 to 5 stars to indicate your satisfaction level with the survey.
* **Attachments** (5): Upload any relevant files or documents that support your entry (max 5 files, 5MB limit).
* **Feedback** (6): Leave any additional feedback or comments about the survey.
* **Phone** (7): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Email** (8): Enter your email address (if applicable).
* **Name** (9): Enter your name.
* **Organization** (10): Enter your organization name (if applicable).
* **Attachments 2** (11): Upload any additional files or documents (max 5 files, 5MB limit).
* **File Upload 3, 4, 5, 6** (12-15): Repeat the file upload steps as needed, up to 6 attachments in total.

## Tips
* Ensure that you are providing accurate and up-to-date information.
* Be clear and concise in your description and feedback.
* Attach relevant files that support your entry.
* Review your entry carefully before submitting.
