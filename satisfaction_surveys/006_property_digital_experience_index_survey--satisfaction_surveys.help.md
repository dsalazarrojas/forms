# Property Digital Experience Index Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Digital Experience Index Survey is designed to collect feedback on the property's digital experience, allowing us to identify areas for improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance the property's digital presence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Select the rating that best reflects your experience with the property's digital presence.
* Enter your property name in the text field.
* Select the department that was involved in your interaction with the property.
* Enter a rating score for your experience with the property.
* Optionally, provide any additional comments or feedback in the text field.
* Enter your email address.
* Enter your phone number (optional).
* Select a date and time for your rating.
* Enter the department name (if applicable).
* Enter the department email (if applicable).
* Enter the department phone number (if applicable).
* Select a rating score for the department's service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Page 1: Select Your Experience** (<type>: select_one, required: false)
	+ Select the rating that best reflects your experience with the property's digital presence. The options are:
		- 1 - Strongly Agree
		- 2 - Agree
		- 3 - Neutral
		- 4 - Disagree
		- 5 - Strongly Disagree
* **Property Name** (<type>: text, required: false)
	+ Enter the name of the property you visited or interacted with.
* **Department** (<type>: select_one, required: false)
	+ Select the department that was involved in your interaction with the property. The options are:
		- Front desk
		- Maintenance
		- Sales and Marketing
		- Management
* **Rating Score** (<type>: select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Enter a rating score for your experience with the property. The options are:
		- 5
		- 4
		- 3
		- 2
		- 1
* **Comments** (<type>: note, required: false)
	+ Optionally, provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the property.
* **Email** (<type>: email, required: false)
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (<type>: text, required: false)
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Rating Date** (<type>: date, required: false)
	+ Select a date for your rating.
* **Rating Time** (<type>: time, required: false)
	+ Select a time for your rating.
* **Department Name** (<type>: text, required: false)
	+ Enter the name of the department involved in your interaction with the property.
* **Department Email** (<type>: email, required: false)
	+ Enter the department's email address.
* **Department Phone** (<type>: text, required: false)
	+ Enter the department's phone number.
* **Department Rating Score** (<type>: select_one, required: false)
	+ Select a rating score for the department's service. The options are:
		- 5
		- 4
		- 3
		- 2
		- 1

## Tips

* Please be honest and thorough in your feedback.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please enter them in the comments field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at <insert contact information>.
