# Wood Pellet User Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wood Pellet User Survey is designed to collect information about your experiences and opinions on wood pellets.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Answer each question in a way that best describes your experiences and opinions.
2. Be as accurate as possible when filling out the form.
3. Make sure to review your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Wood Pellet User Survey Form**: This is the main section where you can provide feedback on your experiences with wood pellets.
* **Pellet Usage Habits**: Describe your typical usage of wood pellets, including how often you use them.
* **Pellet Purchasing Habits**: Share your experiences on how you purchase wood pellets, including where you buy them from.
* **Satisfaction**: Indicate your level of satisfaction with wood pellets, choosing from "Satisfied" or "Dissatisfied".
* **Pellet Type**: Tell us if you use wood pellets with or without additives.
* **Pellet Size**: Choose if you prefer larger or smaller pellets.
* **Pellet Color**: If you have a preference for the color of wood pellets, select from "Yes" or "No".
* **Pellet Brand**: If you have a preferred brand of wood pellets, type it here.
* **Pellet Weight**: If you have purchased wood pellets, type the weight of the pellets you purchased.
* **Pellet Cost**: If you have purchased wood pellets, type the cost of the pellets you purchased.
* **Pellet Frequency**: Indicate how often you use wood pellets, choose from "Yes" or "No".
* **Pellet Purchasing Channels**: Tell us if you purchase wood pellets from online or offline sources, choose from "Yes" or "No".

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when providing your responses.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, you can include them in the "Wood Pellet User Survey Form" section.
* If you have any questions or need help, feel free to contact us.
