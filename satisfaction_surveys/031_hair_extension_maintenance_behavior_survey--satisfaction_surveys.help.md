# Hair Extension Maintenance Behavior Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your behavior and satisfaction with hair extensions maintenance. Your responses will help us understand your needs and preferences, allowing us to provide a better experience for you.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer the questions as honestly as possible.
2. Select the options that best fit your experiences and feelings.
3. For open-ended questions, please provide as much detail as possible.
4. If you have trouble with any of the questions, please contact us and we will assist you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Habits**: Please describe your current habits and routines related to hair extension maintenance.
* **Salon Visit Frequency**: Please select the frequency of your visits to the salon for maintenance.
* **Satisfaction With Care**: Please select how satisfied you are with the care you receive from the salon staff.
	+ Hair: Very Good, Good, Average, Bad, Other
* **Salon Staff**: Please select the staff members you are most satisfied with.
	+ Very Good, Good, Average
* **Stylist Profession**: Please select whether you have had a stylist who is a professional.
	+ Yes, No
* **Brand Satisfaction**: Please select the level of satisfaction you have with the brand.
	+ Very Good, Good, Average, Bad
* **Communication Style**: Please select the communication style you prefer from the salon staff.
	+ Yes, No
* **Education Level**: Please describe your education level related to hair extension maintenance.
* **Training Hours**: Please describe the number of training hours you have received.
* **Prophets**: Please select the level of satisfaction you have with the prophets used in the salon.
	+ Very Good, Good, Average
* **Product Satisfaction**: Please select the level of satisfaction you have with the product.
	+ Satisfied, Dissatisfied
* **Product Quality**: Please select the level of quality of the product you use.
	+ Good, Poor
* **Salon Visit Frequency 2**: Please select the frequency of your visits to the salon for maintenance.
* **Salon Staff 2**: Please select the staff members you are most satisfied with.
	+ Very Good, Good, Average

## Tips

* Please answer the questions honestly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please contact us and we will assist you.
* This survey is intended to be completed by one person per client, so please do not complete it multiple times.
