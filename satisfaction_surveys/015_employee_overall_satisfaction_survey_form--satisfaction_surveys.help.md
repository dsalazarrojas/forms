# employee_overall_satisfaction_survey_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to measure employee overall satisfaction and understanding of the company's mission. It is meant to be completed by employees to provide feedback on their level of satisfaction and engagement with the company. By completing this form, you will be helping to gauge the company's strengths and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question thoroughly and honestly.
2. Choose the correct options for each question.
3. Use the text area for additional comments or feedback.
4. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Satisfaction** (`number`, **Required**): Rate your overall satisfaction with the company on a scale of 0-10.
* **How well do you understand our mission?** (`text`, **Required**): Describe how well you understand the company's mission.
* **Would you recommend the company to someone?** (`select_multiple`, **Required**): Choose all that apply:
	+ I strongly agree
	+ I somewhat agree
	+ I neither agree nor disagree
	+ I somewhat disagree
	+ I strongly disagree
	+ None
* **Additional Comments** (`note`, **Optional**): Provide any additional feedback or comments you have about the company.
