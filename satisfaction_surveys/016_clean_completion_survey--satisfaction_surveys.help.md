# Clean Completion Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from clients on their experience with the cleaning service. It's essential to provide honest and detailed feedback to help us improve our services and provide better experiences for our clients.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the date when the cleaning service was provided.
2. Enter the time when the service was completed.
3. Fill out the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
4. Select all areas that were cleaned from the list.
5. Choose the type of service provided and the duration of the service.
6. Rate your overall satisfaction, cleanliness, attention to detail, punctuality, and professionalism.
7. Answer the questions about value for money and whether you would recommend our services.
8. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the text field at the end of the form.
9. Finally, indicate whether you would like to be contacted for follow-up purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Date** (`service_date`, date, required): This field asks for the date when the cleaning service was provided. Please select the date from the calendar provided or enter it in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **Service Time** (`service_time`, time, required): This field asks for the time when the service was completed.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Please enter your name or the name of the company/organization you represent.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so that we can contact you for follow-up purposes.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you for follow-up purposes.
* **Service Location** (`service_location`, text, required): Please enter the address where the service was provided.
* **Type of Service Provided** (`service_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of service provided from the list.
* **Service Duration** (`service_duration`, select_one, required): Select the duration of the service from the list.
* **Cleaner or Team Name** (`cleaner_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the cleaner or team who performed the service.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the service from the list.
* **Cleanliness Rating** (`cleanliness_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the overall cleanliness after the service from the list.
* **Attention to Detail** (`attention_to_detail`, select_one, required): Rate the cleaner's attention to detail from the list.
* **Punctuality Rating** (`punctuality_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the punctuality of the cleaner from the list.
* **Professionalism Rating** (`professionalism_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the professionalism of the cleaner from the list.
* **Areas Cleaned** (`areas_cleaned`, select_multiple, required): Select all areas that were cleaned from the list.
* **Missed Areas or Concerns** (`missed_areas`, text, optional): If there were any areas that were not cleaned properly, please list them here.
* **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, select_one, required): Rate your opinion on the value for money from the list.
* **Would You Recommend Our Services** (`would_recommend`, select_one, required): Answer whether you would recommend our services to friends or family from the list.
* **Would You Use Our Services Again** (`would_use_again`, select_one, required): Answer whether you would use our services again in the future from the list.
* **Additional Comments or Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or feedback, please enter them here.
* **May We Contact You for Follow-Up** (`follow_up_permission`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you would like to be contacted for follow-up purposes from the list.
