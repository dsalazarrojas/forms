# Physician Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Physician Satisfaction Survey is a tool designed to collect feedback and satisfaction from physicians to improve their work experience. The form is not a mandatory form, allowing users to fill out the parts they feel are relevant to their situation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please navigate through its 7 fields, filling out each section as per your preference. You can skip any section if you don't feel it's relevant to your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate your satisfaction with your job, on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Work Life** (`work_life`, text, optional): Describe how work affects your life, providing any details that you feel are relevant.
* **Physician Survey** (`physician_survey`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the three provided options that best describe your experience with the medical staff.
* **Hospital Rating** (`hospital_rating`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the hospital's facilities and services.
* **Physician Comments** (`physician_comments`, note, optional): Use this field to provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, email, optional): Provide any contact information you'd like us to have in case we need to get back to you.
* **Physician Feedback** (`physician_feedback`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the feedback options that apply to your experience.
