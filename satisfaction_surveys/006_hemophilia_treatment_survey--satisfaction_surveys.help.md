# Hemophilia Treatment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to collect information on the treatment and satisfaction of individuals with hemophilia. It covers various aspects of their treatment regimens, bleeding frequencies, symptoms, and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start, please select the page with the corresponding section you would like to answer. The form is divided into two main sections: "Treatment Regimen" and "Satisfaction".
2.  In the "Treatment Regimen" section, you will be asked questions about your treatment history, including the type of treatments you have undergone and their frequency.
3.  In the "Satisfaction" section, you will be asked questions about your overall satisfaction with your treatment and how it has impacted your life.
