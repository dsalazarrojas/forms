# Receiving Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Receiving Management Survey is a form used to gather information about the efficiency and effectiveness of receiving management processes within the organization. It helps management identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions to optimize these processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the correct answer for **Receiving Management Survey Form**.
2. Enter the warehouse location in the **warehouse_location** field.
3. Fill in the **receiving_date** field with the date of the receiving management process you are surveying.
4. Provide the time of the receiving management process in the **receiving_time** field.
5. Choose the time of day when the process occurred in the **receiving_time_of_day** field.
6. Select the receiving shift in which the process occurred in the **receiving_shift** field.
7. Enter the area of the warehouse where the receiving management process took place in the **receiving_area** field.
8. Enter the name of the receiving manager responsible for the process in the **receiving_manager** field.
9. Add any additional notes about the process in the **receiving_notes** field.
10. Rate the receiving management process in the **receiving_rating** field using the provided rating scale (1-5).
11. Enter any comments or feedback about the process in the **comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Receiving Management Survey Form** (`select_one`): Select how well the receiving management process was executed (High, Medium, Low).
* **warehouse_location** (`text`): Enter the location within the warehouse where the receiving management process occurred.
* **receiving_date** (`date`): Enter the date of the receiving management process.
* **receiving_time** (`time`): Enter the time of the receiving management process.
* **receiving_time_of_day** (`time`): Select the time of day when the process occurred (Morning, Afternoon, Night).
* **receiving_shift** (`select_one`): Select the receiving shift in which the process occurred (Morning, Afternoon, Night).
* **receiving_area** (`text`): Enter the area of the warehouse where the process took place.
* **receiving_manager** (`text`): Enter the name of the receiving manager responsible for the process.
* **receiving_notes** (`note`): Add any additional notes or comments about the process.
* **receiving_rating** (`select_one`): Rate the receiving management process (1-5).
* **comments** (`text`): Enter any comments or feedback about the process.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions as accurately and truthfully as possible.
* If a field does not apply to the specific receiving management process, feel free to leave it blank or note the reason in the **comments** field.
* If you have any concerns or suggestions for improvements, use the **comments** field to provide feedback.
