# Service Non Renewal Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users who are not renewing their service. The purpose of this form is to understand the reasons behind their decision and provide an opportunity for them to share their thoughts and concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the reason for non-renewal from the options provided in the "Non Renewal Reason" field.
2. If applicable, select additional reasons for non-renewal from the "Non Renewal Why" field.
3. If you have any suggestions or additional feedback, please enter it in the "Suggestion" field (optional).
4. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
5. If you have any additional feedback or comments, please enter it in the "Feedback" field (optional).
6. Select your satisfaction level with the service in the "Satisfaction Level" field.
7. If you wish to provide more details on your satisfaction level, please enter it in the "Satisfaction Level Why" field (optional).
8. If you have any comments on your rating, please enter it in the "Rating Why" field (optional).
9. If you have any details on how your rating satisfaction changed, please enter it in the "Rating Satisfaction Difference" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Non Renewal Reason** (`Non Renewal Reason`, select_one, required):
	+ This field allows you to select the reason for non-renewal.
	+ Select one of the options provided: Not Satisfied, Not Needed, or Other (Please specify).
* **Non Renewal Why** (`Non Renewal Why`, select_multiple, required):
	+ This field allows you to select additional reasons for non-renewal.
	+ Select one or more of the options provided: Not Satisfied, Service not available, Price not competitive, or Other.
* **Suggestion** (`Suggestion`, note, optional):
	+ This field allows you to enter any suggestions or comments.
	+ Please keep your entry concise and clear.
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, text, required):
	+ This field is for you to enter your contact information.
	+ Please make sure to enter your name and any other relevant details.
* **Feedback** (`Feedback`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for any additional feedback or comments.
	+ Please keep your entry concise and clear.
* **Satisfaction Level** (`Satisfaction Level`, select_one, required):
	+ This field allows you to select your satisfaction level with the service.
	+ Select one of the options provided: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Not Satisfied, or Not Interested.
* **Rating** (`Rating`, number, optional):
	+ This field is for you to enter your rating of the service.
	+ Please enter a number between 1 and 5.
* **Non Renewal Employer** (`Non Renewal Employer`, select_one, optional):
	+ This field is for you to select your employment status.
	+ Select one of the options provided: Self Employed, Employed By Others.
* **Non Renewal Dependent** (`Non Renewal Dependent`, select_one, optional):
	+ This field is for you to select your dependent status.
	+ Select one of the options provided: Single, Married.
* **Non Renewal Income** (`Non Renewal Income`, select_one, optional):
	+ This field is for you to select your income level.
	+ Select one of the options provided: Less than 20,000, 20,000-40,000, 40,000-60,000, 60,000-80,000, More than 80,000.
* **Non Renewal Employed By** (`Non Renewal Employed By`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for you to enter your current employer.
* **Non Renewal Other** (`Non Renewal Other`, note, optional):
	+ This field is for any additional comments or details.
* **Satisfaction Level Why** (`Satisfaction Level Why`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for you to enter more details on your satisfaction level.
* **Rating Why** (`Rating Why`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for you to enter more details on your rating.
* **Rating Satisfaction Difference** (`Rating Satisfaction Difference`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for you to enter how much your rating satisfaction changed.
* **Non Renewal Other** (`Non Renewal Other`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for any additional comments or details.
* **Contact Info 2** (`Contact Info 2`, text, required):
	+ This field is for you to enter your contact information.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional):
	+ This field is for you to enter your email address.
* **Non Renewal Employed By Other** (`Non Renewal Employed By Other`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for you to enter your current employer.
* **Non Renewal Employed By Other Reason** (`Non Renewal Employed By Other Reason`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for you to enter the reason for not renewing.
* **Non Renewal Employed By Other Other** (`Non Renewal Employed By Other Other`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for any additional comments or details.

Please note that all fields with a `required` tag are compulsory and must be filled in for the form to be submitted successfully.
