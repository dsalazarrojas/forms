## Gym Accessibility For Disabled Members Survey - Help Guide

### Purpose
This survey aims to assess the accessibility of the gym facilities for members with disabilities, helping us understand the needs and experiences of our community members with disabilities. Your feedback is crucial in ensuring that our gym provides an accessible environment for everyone.

### How To Complete This Form

1. Review each field carefully and provide accurate information.
2. Please choose the options that best fit your experiences and needs.
3. Answer all required fields (marked with *).
