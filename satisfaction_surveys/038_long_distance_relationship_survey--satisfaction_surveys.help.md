# Long Distance Relationship Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your experiences and feelings in a long distance relationship. It will help us better understand the challenges and successes of relationships that are physically apart. Your input will be valuable for us to improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the survey, follow these steps:

1.  Select the correct options from the drop-down menus where indicated.
2.  Answer all questions in the text fields (e.g., partner_name, notes).
3.  Enter numerical values for questions asking for numbers (e.g., visits_frequency, emotional_intimacy).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Relationship Status** (`relationship_status`, select_one):
    *   Select one option from the drop-down menu to describe your current relationship status.
*   **Partner Name** (`partner_name`, text):
    *   Enter your partner's name.
*   **Relationship Length** (`relationship_length`, number):
    *   Enter the length of your relationship in months.
*   **Relationship Type** (`relationship_type`, select_multiple):
    *   Select all applicable methods from the drop-down menu (e.g., online gaming, social media).
*   **Visits Frequency** (`visits_frequency`, number):
    *   Enter the average number of visits you make to your partner per year.
*   **Communication Frequency** (`communication_frequency`, number):
    *   Enter the average number of messages/contacts you have with your partner per week.
*   **Emotional Intimacy** (`emotional_intimacy`, number):
    *   Rate your emotional intimacy with your partner on a scale (1-5).
*   **Emotional Intimacy 2** (`emotional_intimacy2`, number):
    *   Rate your emotional intimacy with your partner on a scale (1-5).
*   **Emotional Intimacy 3** (`emotional_intimacy3`, number):
    *   Rate your emotional intimacy with your partner on a scale (1-5).
*   **Emotional Intimacy 4** (`emotional_intimacy4`, number):
    *   Rate your emotional intimacy with your partner on a scale (1-5).
*   **Partner Satisfaction** (`partner_satisfaction`, number):
    *   Rate your satisfaction with your partner on a scale (1-5).
*   **Personal Satisfaction** (`personal_satisfaction`, number):
    *   Rate your personal satisfaction with your relationship on a scale (1-5).
*   **Relationship Satisfaction** (`relationship_satisfaction`, number):
    *   Rate your overall satisfaction with your relationship on a scale (1-5).
*   **Communication Satisfaction** (`communication_satisfaction`, number):
    *   Rate your satisfaction with your communication with your partner on a scale (1-5).
*   **Trust** (`trust`, number):
    *   Rate your level of trust with your partner on a scale (1-5).
*   **Intimacy** (`intimacy`, number):
    *   Rate your level of intimacy with your partner on a scale (1-5).
*   **Conflict** (`conflict`, number):
    *   Rate the level of conflict in your relationship on a scale (1-5).
*   **Conflict Frequency** (`conflict_frequency`, number):
    *   Enter the average frequency of conflicts in your relationship per year.
*   **Conflict Resolution Method** (`conflict_resolution_method`, text):
    *   Describe how you and your partner typically resolve conflicts.
*   **Conflict Resolution Method Other** (`conflict_resolution_method_other`, text):
    *   Describe other methods used to resolve conflicts that are not listed in the previous question.
*   **Communication Method** (`communication_method`, select_multiple):
    *   Select all applicable methods from the drop-down menu (e.g., phone, email, text messaging).
*   **Communication Method Other** (`communication_method_other`, text):
    *   Describe other methods used for communication that are not listed in the previous question.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, text):
    *   Enter any additional comments or observations about your relationship.

## Tips

*   Be honest and accurate when answering these questions.
*   Remember that your input will help improve our services for long distance relationships.
