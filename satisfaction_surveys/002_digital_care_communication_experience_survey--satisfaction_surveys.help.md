# Digital Care Communication Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the effectiveness and usability of digital care communication channels and services. It asks you about your preferences, opinions, and experiences with digital care communication, including your rating of its satisfactoryness and how you would rate various aspects of digital care services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly, and try to provide specific examples or reasons for your opinions.
2. Please answer all the required questions.
3. For multiple-choice questions, select one or more of the options that best match your answer.
4. For open-ended questions, provide detailed answers in the text boxes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What do you think about our digital care communication?** (`1`, `text`, required): This question asks for your general opinion about digital care communication. Please provide a brief answer about your thoughts and feelings.
* **How do you prefer to receive care messages?** (`2`, `select_multiple`, required): This question asks you to choose one or more channels you prefer to receive care messages, such as Care Coordinator, Doctor, Nurse, Phone, Email, SMS, or In-app.
* **Preferred message channels** (`3`, `select_multiple`, optional): Similar to the previous question, this one asks you to choose one or more channels you prefer to receive messages, but it's not required.
* **What time do you prefer to receive messages?** (`4`, `select_one`, required): This question asks you to choose a specific time of day you prefer to receive messages, such as Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.
* **How would you rate your experience with digital care communication?** (`5`, `number`, required): This question asks you to rate your experience on a scale, with higher numbers indicating a more positive experience.
* **Digital care communication satisfactory?** (`6`, `number`, required): Similar to the previous question, this one asks you to rate the satisfactoryness of digital care communication on a scale.
* **How would you rate digital care?** (`7`, `number`, required): This question asks you to rate the quality of digital care on a scale.
* **How would you rate digital communication?** (`8`, `number`, required): Similar to the previous question, this one asks you to rate the quality of digital communication on a scale.
* **How would you rate digital care team?** (`9`, `number`, required): This question asks you to rate the quality of the digital care team on a scale.
* **How would you rate digital care services?** (`10`, `number`, required): Similar to the previous question, this one asks you to rate the quality of digital care services on a scale.
* **Would you recommend digital care?** (`11`, `select_one`, required): This question asks you to decide if you would recommend digital care to others, with options "True" or "False".
* **Why didn't you recommend digital care?** (`12`, `text`, optional): If you chose "False" for the previous question, this one asks you to explain why in a text box.
* **Why didn't you recommend digital care to family member?** (`15`, `text`, optional): Similar to the previous question, this one asks you to explain why you didn't recommend digital care to a family member, if applicable.
* **Would you use digital care services?** (`17`, `select_one`, required): This question asks you to decide if you would use digital care services, with options "True" or "False".
* **How would you rate digital care services?** (`19`, `number`, required): This question asks you to rate the quality of digital care services on a scale.
* **Would you prefer to receive reminders?** (`20`, `select_multiple`, required): This question asks you to choose one or more channels you prefer to receive reminders, such as Phone, Email, SMS, or In-app.
* **How would you rate digital care services reminders?** (`21`, `number`, required): This question asks you to rate the quality of digital care services reminders on a scale.
* **How would you rate digital care services reminder frequency?** (`22`, `number`, required): This question asks you to rate the frequency of digital care services reminders on a scale.
* **Would you prefer reminder channels?** (`23`, `select_multiple`, required): This question asks you to choose one or more channels you prefer to receive reminders, similar to the previous question.
* **How would you rate digital care services reminders?** (`24`, `number`, required): This question asks you to rate the quality of digital care services reminders on a scale.
* **How would you rate digital care services reminder content?** (`25`, `number`, required): This question asks you to rate the quality of digital care services reminder content on a scale.

## Tips

* Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your abilities.
* If you have any difficulty in understanding a question, feel free to ask for help.
* This form is designed to gather feedback and opinions, so your input is valuable and helps us improve digital care communication.
