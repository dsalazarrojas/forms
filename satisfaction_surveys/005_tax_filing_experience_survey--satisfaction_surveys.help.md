# Tax Filing Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this survey is to gather your feedback about your experience with the tax filing process. This will help us understand what worked well and where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions honestly, and provide as much detail as possible.
2. You can skip any questions that don't apply to your experience.
3. If you have any comments or suggestions, please use the text fields provided for that purpose.
