# Resident Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Resident Satisfaction Survey is designed to collect feedback from residents on their satisfaction with various aspects of their living environment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each page carefully and ensure that you have understood what information is being requested.
2. Provide your honest feedback and ratings for each question.
3. Submit your responses when you have completed all the fields.


## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Resident Information (Page 1)**
  (Number, Not Required)
  Enter your resident ID or any other unique identifier.
* **Resident Satisfaction (Page 2)**
  (Text, Not Required)
  Describe your overall satisfaction with your living environment.
* **Comments (Page 3)**
  (Note, Not Required)
  Add any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Resident Satisfaction Score (Page 4)**
  (Number, Not Required)
  Rate your satisfaction with the quality of service (1-5).
* **Resident Contact Information (Page 5)**
  (Select One, Not Required)
  How do you prefer to be contacted (e.g. phone, email, mail).
* **Resident Contact Information 2 (Page 6)**
  (Select One, Not Required)
  If you have already answered the previous question, select this option to confirm how you want to be contacted.
* **Resident Contact Information 3 (Page 7)**
  (Select Multiple, Not Required)
  Select all the ways you are willing to be contacted.
* **Comments (Page 8)**
  (Note, Not Required)
  Add any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Resident Satisfaction Score 2 (Page 9)**
  (Number, Not Required)
  Rate your satisfaction with the overall quality of life (1-5).
* **Additional Comments (Page 10)**
  (Select One, Not Required)
  If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, select this option.
