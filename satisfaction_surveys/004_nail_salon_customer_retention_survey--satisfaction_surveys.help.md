# # Nail Salon Customer Retention Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from customers about their experience at our nail salon. Your input will help us improve and better serve you and our community.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2. Select the options that best fit your experiences and feelings.
3. Don't worry, your responses are anonymous.
4. If you need help or have questions, you can contact us through our contact form.
