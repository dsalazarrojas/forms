# Bishop Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bishop Feedback Survey is designed to collect feedback from the faithful about their experience with their bishop, providing valuable insights into their leadership, communication, and pastoral care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Respondent Information (Page 1)**: Please provide your contact details, including your full name and email address, so we can follow up with you if needed.
2. **Full Name**: Enter your complete name as it is written on your identification documents.
3. **Email Address**: Provide your email address so we can contact you if needed.
4. **Parish Name**: Enter the name of your parish community.
5. **Years in Parish**: Select how long you have been a member of your parish community.
6. **Overall Leadership Rating**: Rate the bishop's leadership effectiveness as Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
7. **Communication Effectiveness**: Rate the bishop's communication with the faithful as Very Effective, Effective, Neutral, Ineffective, or Very Ineffective.
8. **Homily Quality**: Rate the quality and relevance of the homilies as Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
9. **Pastoral Visits Frequency**: Select how often the bishop visits your parish as Regularly, Occasionally, Rarely, or Never.
10. **Community Engagement**: Rate your engagement with the parish community as Very Engaged, Engaged, Neutral, Disengaged, or Very Disengaged.
11. **Transparency in Decision Making**: Rate the level of transparency in diocesan decisions as Very Transparent, Transparent, Somewhat Transparent, Not Transparent, or Very Opaque.
12. **Bishop Strengths**: Describe the bishop's greatest strengths in a text field.
13. **Areas for Improvement**: Identify areas where the bishop could improve in a text field.
14. **Additional Comments**: Share any additional feedback you would like to provide in a text field.
15. **Recommend to Others**: Rate whether you would definitely, probably, or probably not recommend this bishop to other parishes or dioceses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent Information (Page 1)**: This field is to capture your contact details, which will enable us to follow up with you if needed.
* **Full Name**: This field is to record your complete name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Email Address**: This field is to gather your email address for future communication.
* **Parish Name**: This field is to identify the name of your parish community.
* **Years in Parish**: This field is to measure your length of service with your parish community.
* **Overall Leadership Rating**: This field is to evaluate the bishop's leadership effectiveness.
* **Communication Effectiveness**: This field is to assess the bishop's communication with the faithful.
* **Homily Quality**: This field is to rate the quality and relevance of the homilies.
* **Pastoral Visits Frequency**: This field is to measure how often the bishop visits your parish.
* **Community Engagement**: This field is to gauge your engagement with the parish community.
* **Transparency in Decision Making**: This field is to rate the level of transparency in diocesan decisions.
* **Bishop Strengths**: This field is to describe the bishop's greatest strengths.
* **Areas for Improvement**: This field is to identify areas where the bishop could improve.
* **Additional Comments**: This field is to share any additional feedback you would like to provide.

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide specific feedback to help us understand your experience with the bishop.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please share them in the "Additional Comments" field.
* If you have any questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
