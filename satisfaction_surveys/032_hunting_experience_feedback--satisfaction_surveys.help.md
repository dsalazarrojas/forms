# Hunting Experience Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their hunting experience with a guide. The purpose of this form is to collect feedback on various aspects of the guide's performance, such as their attitude, communication, and overall experience. The information collected will help us improve our services and ensure that our customers have a positive experience with our guides.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and provide honest and accurate feedback about your experience.
2. For numerical ratings (e.g., guide_rating), select a rating from 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
3. For text fields (e.g., guide_communication), provide a brief description or answer the question in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guide** (`guide_rating`, number, required): Rate the guide's overall performance from 1-5.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, text, required): Describe your overall experience with the guide.
* **Difficulty Level** (`difficulty_level`, text, optional): If you feel that the difficulty level of the hunt was not accurately represented, please let us know.
* **Scenery** (`scenery_rating`, text, optional): Comment on the scenery or environment of the hunt.
* **Guide Communication** (`guide_communication`, text, optional): Rate the guide's communication and ability to explain the hunt's details.
* **Facilities** (`facilities`, text, optional): Comment on the condition and availability of the facilities provided.
* **Guide Attitude** (`guide_attitude`, text, optional): Rate the guide's attitude and demeanor during the hunt.
* **Guide Knowledge** (`guide_knowledge`, text, optional): Rate the guide's knowledge about the hunt and the subject matter.
* **Equipment** (`equipment`, text, optional): Comment on the quality and availability of the equipment provided.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Provide any additional comments or feedback you would like to share with us.
* **Photo** (`photo`, note, required): Please upload a photo of the guide, if available.
* **Guide Name** (`guide_name`, text, required): Enter the guide's name.
* **Guide Phone** (`guide_phone`, text, required): Enter the guide's phone number.
* **Guide Email** (`guide_email`, text, optional): Enter the guide's email address.
 

This guide is designed to walk users through filling out this form, providing them the information they need to accurately and efficiently provide their feedback.
