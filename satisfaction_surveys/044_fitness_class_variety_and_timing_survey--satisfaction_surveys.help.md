# Fitness Class Variety And Timing Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the fitness classes offered at our facility. We want to know about the diversity of classes, their timing, and what you like or dislike about them. This will help us improve our class offerings to better serve our members.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that best fit your preferences for class variety and timing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Class Variety** (`class_variety`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select all that apply:
		- Yes: We offer a variety of classes with different activities, styles, and focuses (e.g., yoga, Pilates, spinning).
		- No: We have a limited selection of classes, with most classes being similar in type.
* **Class Time** (`class_time`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select the time of day you prefer:
		- Morning: 6:00 AM - 12:00 PM
		- Afternoon: 12:00 PM - 4:00 PM
		- Evening: 4:00 PM - 8:00 PM
		- Other: Please specify in Additional Comments
* **Class Frequency** (`class_frequency`, select_one, optional):
	+ How often do you typically take classes:
		- Rarely: I rarely take classes
		- Occasionally: I take classes 1-2 times a week
		- Often: I take classes 3-4 times a week
		- Almost Always: I take classes almost every day
* **Class Duration** (`class_duration`, select_one, optional):
	+ How long do you like your classes to be:
		- 30-60 minutes: Short and sweet
		- 60-90 minutes: Medium length
		- 90-120 minutes: Long and challenging
* **Class Intensity** (`class_intensity`, select_one, optional):
	+ What intensity level do you prefer:
		- Low: Gentle and relaxing
		- Medium: Moderate challenge
		- High: Challenging and physically demanding
* **Class Style** (`class_style`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select all that apply:
		- Yes: We offer classes with different styles (e.g., HIIT, strength training, cardio)
		- No: Most classes are the same style
* **Instructor Experience** (`instructor_experience`, text, optional):
	+ Please describe what you like or dislike about the instructors and their experience.
* **Why Class** (`why_class`, note, optional):
	+ Please type any additional comments or reasons why you take classes.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional):
	+ Use this space to elaborate on any other comments or questions you may have.

Note: The "Fitness Goals" field is a duplicate of the "Class Variety" field and has been intentionally removed from the help guide to avoid redundant information.
