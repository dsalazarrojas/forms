# Workplace Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to measure employee satisfaction with various aspects of their workplace experience, including overall job satisfaction, work-life balance, team collaboration, and more.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question according to your genuine feelings and experiences.
2. Responses should be based on your current state, not hypothetical or idealized situations.
3. Answer each question using the rating scale provided, where:
	* 1-2: Not Satisfied
	* 3-4: Neutral
	* 5-6: Satisfied

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How satisfied are you with your overall job satisfaction?** (`general_satisfaction`, `number`, Required)
	* Please rate your satisfaction with your overall job satisfaction.
* **How satisfied are you with your work-life balance?** (`work_life_balance`, `number`, Required)
	* Rate your satisfaction with the balance between your work and personal life.
* **How would you rate your team collaboration?** (`team_collaboration`, `number`, Required)
	* Rate your experience with your team's collaboration and communication.
* **How satisfied are you with your job knowledge?** (`job_knowledge`, `number`, Required)
	* Evaluate how satisfied you are with the knowledge and skills you acquire on the job.
* **How satisfied are you with your work load?** (`workload`, `number`, Required)
	* Rate your satisfaction with your workload's difficulty and volume.
* **How satisfied are you with work-life balance holidays?** (`work_life_balance_holidays`, `number`, Optional)
	* Rate your satisfaction with the balance between work and holiday time.
* **How satisfied are you with career growth opportunities?** (`career_growth_opportunities`, `number`, Required)
	* Evaluate how satisfied you are with opportunities for growth and development in your career.
* **How satisfied are you with communication?** (`communication`, `number`, Optional)
	* Rate your satisfaction with the communication within your team and organization.
* **How satisfied are you with management support?** (`management_support`, `number`, Optional)
	* Evaluate how satisfied you are with the support you receive from your management.
* **How satisfied are you with your work environment?** (`work_environment`, `number`, Required)
	* Rate your satisfaction with your workspace and work conditions.
* **How satisfied are you with company culture?** (`company_culture`, `number`, Optional)
	* Evaluate how satisfied you are with the company culture and values.
* **How satisfied are you with job security?** (`job_security`, `number`, Optional)
	* Rate your satisfaction with job stability and security.
* **How satisfied are you with salary?** (`salary`, `number`, Optional)
	* Evaluate how satisfied you are with your remuneration and compensation.
* **How satisfied are you with your job role?** (`job_role`, `number`, Optional)
	* Rate your satisfaction with your job responsibilities and duties.
* **How satisfied are you with company perks?** (`company_perks`, `number`, Optional)
	* Evaluate how satisfied you are with benefits and perks provided by the company.
* **How satisfied are you with job duties?** (`job_duties`, `number`, Optional)
	* Rate your satisfaction with your job responsibilities and tasks.
* **How satisfied are you with team diversity?** (`team_diversity`, `number`, Required)
	* Evaluate how satisfied you are with the diversity of your team.
* **How satisfied are you with employee recognition?** (`employee_recognition`, `number`, Optional)
	* Rate your satisfaction with rewards and recognition for your work.
* **How satisfied are you with work-life balance purpose?** (`work_life_balance_purpose`, `number`, Optional)
	* Evaluate how satisfied you are with the balance between work and personal life's purpose.
* **How satisfied are you with company transparency?** (`company_transparency`, `number`, Optional)
	* Rate your satisfaction with the transparency within the organization.
* **How satisfied are you with job performance?** (`job_performance`, `number`, Optional)
	* Evaluate how satisfied you are with your performance evaluation and metrics.
* **How satisfied are you with work-life balance flexibility?** (`work_life_balance_flexibility`, `number`, Optional)
	* Rate your satisfaction with the flexibility of work hours and arrangements.
* **How satisfied are you with company values?** (`company_values`, `number`, Required)
	* Evaluate how satisfied you are with the company's values and mission.
* **How satisfied are you with your overall job satisfaction?** (`overall_satisfaction`, `number`, Required)
	* Rate your final satisfaction with your job and work experience.

## Tips

* Be honest and genuine in your answers.
* Take your time to answer each question thoughtfully.
* If you have any comments or suggestions, please use the "Comments" field.
