# Marketing Campaign Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and satisfaction data about marketing campaigns. It aims to assess the effectiveness and success of marketing initiatives.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the Campaign Name field with the name of the marketing campaign in question.
* Select the duration of the campaign from the options provided (Less than 1 week, 1-2 weeks, 2-4 weeks, 1-3 months, More than 3 months).
* Enter the budget allocated for the campaign.
* Describe the target audience for the campaign.
* Specify the channels used for the campaign.
* Define the campaign goals.
* Answer the question about the campaign's success (Yes, No, Maybe).
* Provide any feedback or comments about the campaign.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Campaign Name (text)**: Enter the name of the marketing campaign.
* **Duration (select_one)**: Choose the length of time the campaign ran.
* **Budget (text)**: Enter the budget allocated for the campaign.
* **Target Audience (text)**: Describe the people the campaign was targeting.
* **Channels (text)**: Specify the communication channels used (e.g., social media, email, etc.).
* **Campaign Goals (text)**: Define the objectives of the campaign.
* **Success (select_one)**: Answer whether you believe the campaign was successful (Yes, No, Maybe).
* **Feedback (text)**: Provide any comments or suggestions about the campaign.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions truthfully and thoroughly to provide valuable feedback.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, you can leave them blank or enter "Not applicable" if relevant.
* This form is for your convenience, and your feedback is greatly appreciated!
