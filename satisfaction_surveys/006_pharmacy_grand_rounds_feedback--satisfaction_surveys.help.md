# Pharmacy Grand Rounds Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback and ratings from pharmacists regarding their experiences during the "Pharmacy Grand Rounds" sessions. It's designed to be completed by the pharmacists themselves, and it's meant to be a quick and straightforward way to collect their opinions and ratings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and honestly with your feedback and ratings.
2. Make sure to have your "Session ID" and "Pharmacist Name" ready, as they are required fields.
3. Provide a valid email address, in case we need to contact you for further discussion.
4. Choose your rating from the "Feedback" field, and type any additional comments you have in the "Comments" field.
5. Enter your rating for the pharmacist in the "Pharmacist Rating" field.
6. Fill out any additional fields as needed.
7. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session ID**: Please enter the ID of the session you are providing feedback on.
*   **Pharmacist Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on the "Pharmacy Grand Rounds" session list.
*   **Session Start Time**: If you recall the start time of the session, please enter it here.
*   **Pharmacist Name 2**: If you have multiple pharmacists participating in the session, please enter their name here.
*   **Feedback**: Please choose one of the options below to rate your overall experience.
*   **Comments**: Please provide any additional comments about the session.
*   **Pharmacist Rating**: Please enter a numerical rating for the pharmacist's performance.
*   **Email**: Please enter a valid email address, in case we need to contact you for further discussion.
*   **Phone**: Please enter a phone number, in case we need to contact you.
*   **Submit**: Click this button to complete the form and submit your feedback.
