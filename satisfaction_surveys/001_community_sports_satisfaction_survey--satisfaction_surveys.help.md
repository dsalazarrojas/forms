# Community Sports Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather your feedback on your experience with the Community Sports program. Your input is invaluable in helping us understand what we do well and what we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your first and last name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email Address" field. This is how we may contact you for follow-up questions.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field, if you'd like to be contacted.
4. Select your age group from the "Age Group" dropdown menu.
5. Choose the sports program you participated in from the "Which sports program did you participate in?" menu. Select all that apply.
6. Select the season you participated in from the "Program Season" dropdown menu.
7. Rate the quality of the sports facilities from the "How would you rate the quality of sports facilities?" menu.
8. Assess the quality of coaching from the "How would you rate the quality of coaching?" menu.
9. Rate your satisfaction with the equipment provided from the "How satisfied are you with the equipment provided?" menu.
10. Evaluate the organization of the program from the "How well organized was the program?" menu.
11. Rate the safety measures in place from the "How would you rate the safety measures in place?" menu.
12. Determine if you feel the program offered good value for the cost from the "Do you feel the program offered good value for the cost?" menu.
13. Express your willingness to recommend the program to others from the "Would you recommend this program to others?" menu.
14. Indicate your intention to participate in future programs from the "Do you plan to participate in future programs?" menu.
15. Specify what you enjoyed most about the program in the "What was the best aspect of the program?" text field.
16. Suggest areas that need improvement in the "What areas need improvement?" text field.
17. Share any additional comments or suggestions in the "Any additional comments or suggestions?" text field.
18. The date of survey completion will be auto-filled with today's date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Please enter your first and last name.
* **Email Address** (`participant_email`, email, required): We may contact you for follow-up questions.
* **Phone Number** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): Optional contact number.
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, select_one, required): Select your age range.
* **Which sports program did you participate in?** (`sport_program`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply.
* **Program Season** (`program_season`, select_one, required): When did you participate?
* **How would you rate the quality of sports facilities?** (`facility_rating`, select_one, required): Rate from poor to excellent.
* **How would you rate the quality of coaching?** (`coaching_quality`, select_one, required): Consider knowledge, communication, and support.
* **How satisfied are you with the equipment provided?** (`equipment_condition`, select_one, required): Rate from very dissatisfied to very satisfied.
* **How well organized was the program?** (`program_organization`, select_one, required): Consider scheduling, communication, and logistics.
* **How would you rate the safety measures in place?** (`safety_measures`, select_one, required): First aid, supervision, equipment safety.
* **Do you feel the program offered good value for the cost?** (`value_for_money`, select_one, required): Consider fees versus quality received.
* **Would you recommend this program to others?** (`would_recommend`, select_one, required): Your honest opinion helps us improve.
* **Do you plan to participate in future programs?** (`will_participate_again`, select_one, required): Let us know your intentions.
* **What was the best aspect of the program?** (`best_aspect`, text, optional): What did you enjoy most?
* **What areas need improvement?** (`areas_for_improvement`, text, optional): Please be specific about what could be better.
* **Any additional comments or suggestions?** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): We value your feedback.
* **Date of Survey Completion** (`survey_date`, date, required): Auto-filled with today's date.
