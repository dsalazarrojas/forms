# University Alumni Satisfaction Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Alumni Satisfaction Poll is a form designed to collect data on the overall satisfaction of university alumni with their alma mater.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Type in the name of the university where you graduated in the "University Name" field.
5. Select your academic program from the list in the "Academic Program" field.
6. Select one or more of the following graduation years in the "Graduation Year" field.
7. Rate your satisfaction with the following:
	* Overall Satisfaction
	* Satisfaction with Faculty
	* Satisfaction with Administrative Support
	* Satisfaction with Academic Support
	* Satisfaction with Resources

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it is known to you.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it is known to you.
* **Email** (Email, email, required: false): Enter your email address that you have associated with your university account.
* **University Name** (University Name, text, required: false): Type in the name of the university where you graduated.
* **Academic Program** (Academic Program, select_one, required: false): Select the academic program you were part of during your time at the university.
* **Graduation Year** (Graduation Year, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or more of the years you graduated.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (Overall Satisfaction, number, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with your university experience on a scale of 0 to 10.
* **Satisfaction with Faculty** (Satisfaction with Faculty, number, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with the faculty during your time at the university on a scale of 0 to 10.
* **Satisfaction with Administrative Support** (Satisfaction with Administrative Support, number, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with the administrative support you received during your time at the university on a scale of 0 to 10.
* **Satisfaction with Academic Support** (Satisfaction with Academic Support, number, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with the academic support you received during your time at the university on a scale of 0 to 10.
* **Satisfaction with Resources** (Satisfaction with Resources, number, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with the resources available to you during your time at the university on a scale of 0 to 10.
