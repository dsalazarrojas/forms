# Migrant Workforce Rights Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Migrant Workforce Rights Awareness Survey is designed to gather information about your understanding and experiences related to your rights as a migrant worker. This survey is a tool to help us better understand your needs and identify areas for improvement in the implementation of your rights.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and select the most accurate answer from the provided options.
2. For questions with a "date" or "time" type, provide the exact date or time of the issue or event.
3. For questions with a "note" type, provide any additional comments or explanations regarding the questions above.
4. Review all questions and ensure you have provided all relevant information.
5. Submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Do you know that you have the right to access clean drinking water?** (`migrant_workforce_rights_awareness_survey_questions`, select_one): This question aims to understand your understanding of your rights regarding access to clean drinking water. Please select "Migrant workers have the right to access clean drinking water" if you are aware of this right, "Migrant workers do not have the right to access clean drinking water" if you are not aware, or "Not sure" if you are unsure.
* **What is your primary occupation or industry?** (`migrant_workforce_rights_awareness_survey_questions_2`, select_multiple): Please select the most relevant option that describes your primary occupation or industry.
* **Have you experienced any issues with your employer related to your rights?** (`migrant_workforce_rights_awareness_survey_questions_3`, date): If you have experienced any issues, please provide the date of the incident or event.
* **Have you been provided with a clear understanding of your rights?** (`migrant_workforce_rights_awareness_survey_questions_4`, time): If yes, please provide the time when you received the information. If no, please provide more details.
* **Migrant Workforce Rights Awareness Survey Notes** (`migrant_workforce_rights_awareness_survey_notes`, note): This is an optional field for you to provide any further comments or explanations regarding the above questions.
* **Migrant Workforce Rights Awareness Survey Comments** (`migrant_workforce_rights_awareness_survey_comments`, text): This is an optional field for you to add any comments regarding your rights awareness.

## Tips
- Answer each question honestly, and to the best of your knowledge.
- Use the "notes" field to provide additional context or explanations if needed.
- Please review all questions to ensure you have provided all necessary information.
- Submit the form when finished.
