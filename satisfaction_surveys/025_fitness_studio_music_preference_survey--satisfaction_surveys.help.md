# fitness_studio_music_preference_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about your music preferences and habits, so we can better understand your tastes and provide more suitable music recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the options that best fit your preferences and habits.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Header**: This is a note field that provides an introduction to the survey and its purpose.
* **Music Genre**: Select all the music genres you enjoy or have an interest in (e.g., Jazz, Rock, Pop, etc.).
* **Favorite Artist**: Choose one artist you particularly enjoy or admire (e.g., The Beatles, Michael Jackson, etc.).
* **Music Channels**: Select all the music streaming channels you use (e.g., Apple Music, Spotify, Amazon Music, etc.).
* **Favorite Time of Day**: Choose the time of day when you typically listen to music (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Evening, etc.).
* **Music Preference**: Select all the times of day when you prefer to listen to music (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Evening, etc.).
* **Favorite Volume**: Please enter a number between 1 and 10 to indicate how loud you prefer your music (e.g., 5 for medium volume).
* **Music Preferred**: Choose whether you prefer music or not (e.g., True or False).
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any additional comments or preferences, please type them in here.
