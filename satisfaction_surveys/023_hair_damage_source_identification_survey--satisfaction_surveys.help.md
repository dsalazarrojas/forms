# Hair Damage Source Identification Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from users on the sources of hair damage. This includes chemical haircare products, physical damage, genetic predisposition, and other potential causes. The survey aims to help users identify the root cause of their hair damage and provide valuable feedback to the hair care industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the required questions: These are questions that are marked as required in the form. For this form, all questions are optional.
2. Select the relevant options for each question: For questions with multiple options, choose the correct answer that best describes your hair damage experience.
3. Enter any additional information: If you have any additional details about your hair damage experience, use the "Other" section to provide more information.
4. Confirm the accuracy of your answers: Before submitting the form, review your answers and confirm that they are accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hair Damage** (`id: 2`): This question asks about the type of hair damage you are experiencing. Select all the options that apply to your experience.
	+ Chemical Haircare Products: This option is for hair damage caused by haircare products such as shampoo, conditioner, and hair dye.
	+ Physical Damage: This option is for hair damage caused by physical means such as heat, sun exposure, or mechanical stress.
	+ Genetic Predisposition: This option is for hair damage caused by genetic factors that predispose you to hair damage.
* **Chemical Haircare Products** (`id: 3`): This question asks you to select one specific chemical haircare product that you think may be causing your hair damage.
	+ Shampoo
	+ Conditioner
	+ Hair Spray
	+ Hair Dye
	+ Hair Relaxer
* **Damage Level** (`id: 4`): This question asks you to rate the level of hair damage you are experiencing. However, there are no options provided for this field. It is assumed that this field is intended to be a number input for a numerical value.
* **How often do you use chemical haircare products?** (`id: 5`): This question asks you to input how often you use chemical haircare products. It is assumed that this is a number input field.
* **Hair Spray** (`id: 6`): This question asks for any additional information about your experience with hair spray.
* **Hair Dye** (`id: 7`): This question asks for any additional information about your experience with hair dye.
* **Hair Relaxer** (`id: 8`): This question asks for any additional information about your experience with hair relaxers.
* **Physical Damage** (`id: 9`): This question asks for any additional information about your experience with physical damage.
* **Genetic Predisposition** (`id: 10`): This question asks for any additional information about your genetic predisposition to hair damage.
* **Other** (`id: 11`): This question asks for any additional information about your hair damage experience that does not fit into the other categories.
* **Other Details** (`id: 12`): This question asks for any additional details about your hair damage experience.
* **Submit** (`id: 13`): This question confirms whether you are confident that the information you provided is correct.
* **Email** (`id: 14`): This question asks for your email address for further contact.
* **Phone** (`id: 15`): This question asks for your phone number for further contact.

Note: The fields "Damage Level" and "How often do you use chemical haircare products?" are assumed to be numerical fields but have no options. It is recommended to input a number value for these fields.
