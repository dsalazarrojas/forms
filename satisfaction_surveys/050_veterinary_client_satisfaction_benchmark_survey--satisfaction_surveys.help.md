# Veterinary Client Satisfaction Benchmark Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to help you complete the Veterinary Client Satisfaction Benchmark Survey form efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read through each section and question carefully to ensure you provide accurate and thorough feedback.
2. For fields that ask for specific information (Clinic Name, Contact Information, Date of Visit), provide as much detail as possible.
3. Answer questions that ask for ratings (Overall Satisfaction, Likely Recommend) with the provided options.
4. For open-ended questions (Staff Interactions, Comments), provide detailed and constructive feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Clinic Name**: The name of the clinic where you visited. This will help us identify which clinic you are providing feedback for.
* **Contact Information**: Your contact information, including name and contact number, to allow us to get back to you if needed.
* **Date of Visit**: The date you visited the clinic. This will help us track your visit history.
* **Service Provided**: A brief description of the service you received. This will help us understand the context of your visit.
* **Staff Interactions**: Describe your interactions with the staff, including any positive or negative experiences. This will help us evaluate staff performance.
* **Level of Service**: Rate the level of service you received. Choose between High, Low, or Other.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the clinic. Choose between Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Likely Recommend**: Answer with one of the provided options (Strongly Agree, Somewhat Agree, Neutral, Somewhat Disagree, Strongly Disagree).
* **Likely Recommend Why**: Provide reasons for your recommendation or lack thereof. This will give us insight into your thought process.
* **Likely Recommend Why Other**: If you selected "Strongly Disagree" or "Somewhat Disagree", provide an explanation for your choice.
* **Comments**: Any additional comments or thoughts about your visit.
* **Staff**: Indicate if you interacted with the following staff: Yes, No.
* **Doctor**: Indicate if you interacted with the doctor: Yes, No.
* **Nurse**: Indicate if you interacted with the nurse: Yes, No.
* **Receptionist**: Indicate if you interacted with the receptionist: Yes, No.
* **Other**: If you interacted with someone else, describe them and your experience.
* **Other Other**: If you have additional comments or thoughts about your interaction with other staff members.
* **Overall Clinic Rating**: Rate the overall clinic rating on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **Overall Clinic Rating Why**: Provide reasons for your rating.
* **Overall Clinic Rating Why Other**: If you selected a rating other than the provided options, explain your choice.
* **Likely Recommend Why 2**: Provide reasons for your recommendation or lack thereof.
* **Overall Satisfaction Why**: Provide reasons for your overall satisfaction.
* **Overall Satisfaction Why Other**: If you selected a rating other than the provided options, explain your choice.

Note: This guide is designed to be as concrete and concise as possible, providing you with a clear understanding of what each field means and how to complete it efficiently.
