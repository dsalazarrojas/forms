# Availability Survey For Observances - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Availability Survey For Observances" form is designed to gather information from employees regarding their availability to work on specific holidays or observances. This information will help management to make informed decisions about holiday scheduling and ensure that employees are not left without adequate support.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please provide the following information:
	* Full Name: Enter your full name as it appears on your official records.
	* Department or Team: Provide the name of your department or team.
	* Email Address: Enter your work email address.
	* Phone Number: Provide your work phone number for emergency contact.
	* Holiday or Observance Name: Specify the holiday or observance you are referring to.
	* Date of Holiday: Enter the date of the holiday or observance.
	* Availability Status: Choose whether you are available, unavailable, or have partial availability on this day.
	* Preferred Shift: Select all shifts that you would be available and willing to work on this day.
	* Preferred Compensation: Choose your preferred compensation for working on this holiday or observance.
	* On-call Availability: Indicate whether you would be willing to be on-call in case of staff shortages.
	* Direct Supervisor Name: Provide the name of your direct supervisor.
	* Special Requests or Constraints: If you have any special requests or constraints, please describe them briefly.
2.  Review your selection carefully before submitting the form.
3.  If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your official records.
* **Department or Team**: Provide the name of your department or team.
* **Email Address**: Enter your work email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Provide your work phone number for emergency contact.
* **Holiday or Observance Name**: Specify the holiday or observance you are referring to, so that management can better understand your availability.
* **Date of Holiday**: Enter the date of the holiday or observance.
* **Availability Status**: Choose whether you are available, unavailable, or have partial availability on this day. This will help management to plan for staffing needs.
* **Preferred Shift**: Select all shifts that you would be available and willing to work on this day. This can help management to schedule shifts that best fit your needs and preferences.
* **Preferred Compensation**: Choose your preferred compensation for working on this holiday or observance. This can help management to determine fair and reasonable compensation for your work.
* **On-call Availability**: Indicate whether you would be willing to be on-call in case of staff shortages. This can help management to plan for emergency situations.
* **Direct Supervisor Name**: Provide the name of your direct supervisor, so that they can be informed of your availability and preferences.
* **Special Requests or Constraints**: If you have any special requests or constraints, please describe them briefly. This can help management to understand your needs and make accommodations as necessary.
* **I understand**: Please review and acknowledge that final scheduling is at management's discretion. This is an important step to ensure that you understand the process and are aware of the final decisions made.
