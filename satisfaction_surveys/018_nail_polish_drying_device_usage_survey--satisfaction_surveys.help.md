# Nail Polish Drying Device Usage Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on the usage and satisfaction of nail polish drying devices. It's intended for users who have experience with these devices and can provide valuable insights on their usage frequency, satisfaction levels, and any issues they encounter.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Choose the type of nail polish drying device you usually use from the dropdown options.
2.  Indicate how often you use a nail polish drying device from the dropdown options.
3.  Rate your satisfaction level with the device from the dropdown options.
4.  Describe any issue you encounter with the device (e.g., speed, temperature, power).
5.  Choose the brand of the nail polish drying device you use from the dropdown options.
6.  Rate your user experience with the device from the dropdown options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Which nail polish drying device do you use?** (device_type): This field allows you to choose which type of nail polish drying device you usually use. Please select one of the options:
    *   Nail Polish Dryer
    *   LED Lamp
    *   Fan
    *   Other
*   **How often do you use a nail polish drying device?** (usage_frequency): This field lets you indicate how frequently you use a nail polish drying device. Choose one or multiple options that apply to your usage:
    *   Daily
    *   Weekly
    *   Rarely
    *   Never
*   **How satisfied are you with the nail polish drying devices you use?** (satisfaction): Rate your overall satisfaction with the devices you use. Please choose one of the options:
    *   Very Satisfied
    *   Somewhat Satisfied
    *   Neutral
    *   Somewhat Dissatisfied
    *   Very Dissatisfied
*   **Which type of issue do you usually encounter with your nail polish drying device?** (issue_type): This field allows you to select one or more types of issues you commonly encounter with your device. Choose the options that apply:
    *   Speed
    *   Temperature
    *   Power
    *   Other
*   **Please describe the issue with your nail polish drying device** (issue_description): Please provide a brief description of the issue you usually encounter with your device. Be as specific as possible.
*   **Which nail polish drying device brand do you use?** (brand): This field lets you choose the brand of the device you use. Please select one of the options:
    *   Brand A
    *   Brand B
    *   Brand C
    *   Other
*   **How would you rate your user experience with the nail polish drying device?** (user_experience): Rate your experience with the device. Please choose one of the options:
    *   Very Positive
    *   Somewhat Positive
    *   Neutral
    *   Somewhat Negative
    *   Very Negative

## Tips

*   Please answer honestly and as accurately as possible based on your actual experience.
*   If you're unsure or don't know the answer to a particular question, feel free to skip it or describe your situation in the issue description field.
*   Your feedback is valuable to us and will help improve future designs.

This form is designed to be completed within a reasonable time frame. If you have any questions or need clarification, please don't hesitate to contact us.
