# Service Customization Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to help us understand your preferences and needs for service customization. It's used to gather information on your role in the business, the services you use, and your satisfaction levels with different aspects of these services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select your role in the business from the options provided.
2. Choose the services you want to customize, as many or as few as you'd like.
3. For each service, select your usage frequency (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
4. If applicable, choose the time of day you usually use the service (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
5. Select the day of the week you usually use the service (if applicable).
6. If you're satisfied with the service, rate it on a scale (for fields with a numeric value).
7. Finally, if you have any preferred time for the service, select it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your role in the business?** (`user_role`): Please select all that apply (e.g., Client, Business Owner, Employee).
* **What service do you want to customize?** (`service_choice`): Choose all services you'd like to customize (e.g., Service A, Service B, Service C).
* **How often do you use this service?** (`service_frequency`): Select your usage frequency (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
* **What time of day do you usually use this service?** (`service_time`): Enter the time you usually use the service (if applicable).
* **What day of week do you usually use this service?** (`day_of_week`): If applicable, select the day of the week you usually use the service.
* **How satisfied are you with the service?** (`satisfaction`): Rate your satisfaction level on a scale (if applicable).
* **What time of day do you usually enjoy?** (`favorite_time`): Select the time of day you usually enjoy using the service (if applicable).
* **Service Satisfaction** (`service_satisfaction`): Rate your satisfaction level with the service (if applicable).
* **What time do you usually use this service?** (`service_time_2`): Enter the time you usually use the service (if applicable).
* **Service Satisfaction 2** (`service_satisfaction_2`): Rate your satisfaction level with the service (if applicable).
* **Service Time 3** (`service_time_3`): Enter the time you usually use the service (if applicable).
* **How important is service customization for you?** (`service_customization`): Rate the importance of service customization (if applicable).
* **What features would you like to see?** (`service_customization_2`): Choose all features you'd like to see (e.g., Feature A, Feature B, Feature C).
* **How satisfied are you with the service features?** (`service_satisfaction_2_2`): Rate your satisfaction level with the service features (if applicable).
* **Service Satisfaction 3** (`service_satisfaction_3`): Rate your satisfaction level with the service (if applicable).
* **Service Time 4** (`service_time_4`): Enter the time you usually use the service (if applicable).
* **Service Satisfaction 4** (`service_satisfaction_4`): Rate your satisfaction level with the service (if applicable).
* **Service Satisfaction 5** (`service_satisfaction_5`): Rate your satisfaction level with the service (if applicable).

Note: This form has several fields that are not applicable to all users, as indicated by the `required: false` label in the form YAML. These fields will only appear if they are relevant to your answers.
