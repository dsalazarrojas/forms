# Nail Polish Durability Expectation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gauge users' expectations about nail polish durability. It will help us understand their preferences and expectations for different types of nail polish products. Your feedback will be helpful in developing products that meet our customers' needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your options from the provided choices for each field.
2. If you have any additional comments or concerns, feel free to add them in the "User Expectation" field.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nail Polish Quality (1)** (`polish_quality`, select_multiple, required: false): 
  Choose the level of quality you expect from your nail polish products. Options: High, Low, Medium.

* **Wear Time (2)** (`wear_time`, select_one, required: false): 
  Select the wear time you expect from your nail polish products. Options: Less than 1 month, More than 1 month, More than 3 months, More than 6 months.

* **Polish Color (3)** (`polish_color`, select_multiple, required: false): 
  Select the color(s) you expect from your nail polish products. Options: Red, Blue, Pink, Purple, Yellow, Green, Silver, Brown, Black, White.

* **Polish Finish (4)** (`polish_finish`, select_multiple, required: false): 
  Choose the finish you expect from your nail polish products. Options: Matte, Glossy, Semi-gloss, Long-lasting Glossy, Holographic, Satin.

* **Wear Frequency (5)** (`wear_frequency`, number, required: false): 
  Enter the frequency of wear you expect from your nail polish products.

* **Polish Brand (6)** (`polish_brand`, select_one, required: false): 
  Select the brand you expect from your nail polish products. Options: OPI, Essie, Revlon, Sally Hansen, Other.

* **User Experience (7)** (`user_experience`, select_multiple, required: false): 
  Choose your experience with nail polish products. Options: Good, Fair, Poor.

* **Color Change (8)** (`color_change`, select_multiple, required: false): 
  Select your expectation for color change from your nail polish products. Options: No, Yes, Yes with chipping.

* **Nail Type (9)** (`nail_type`, select_multiple, required: false): 
  Select the type of nail you have. Options: Natural, Artificial.

* **User Expectation (10)** (`user_expectation`, note, required: false): 
  Enter any additional comments or concerns you have.

* **Polish Type (11)** (`polish_type`, select_one, required: false): 
  Select the type of nail polish you expect. Options: Nail Lacquer, Nail Enamel, Other.

* **Polish Condition (12)** (`polish_condition`, select_multiple, required: false): 
  Choose the condition of your nail polish products. Options: Good, Fair, Poor, Damaged.

* **User Rating (13)** (`user_rating`, number, required: false): 
  Enter your rating of nail polish products.

* **Polish Price (14)** (`polish_price`, number, required: false): 
  Enter the price range you expect from your nail polish products.

* **Polish Stickiness (15)** (`polish_stickiness`, select_multiple, required: false): 
  Select the stickiness level you expect from your nail polish products. Options: High, Low, Medium, Not sticky.

Please note that the fields marked as "required: false" are optional and can be left blank if not applicable.
