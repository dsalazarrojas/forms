# Static Question Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide helps you complete the "Static Question Survey" form, which collects feedback and suggestions from users on our services and product features.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options for each question to provide your feedback and suggestions.
2. Fill in all the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Date**: *Survey Date* (`survey_date`, `date`, *optional*): The date when you took the static question survey.
* **Survey Time**: *Survey Time* (`survey_time`, `time`, *optional*): The time when you took the static question survey.
* **Survey Rating**: *Survey Rating* (`survey_rating`, `number`, *required*): Rate your overall satisfaction with our service.
* **Survey Feedback**: *Survey Feedback* (`survey_feedback`, `text`, *required*): Provide any comments or feedback on your experience with our service.
* **Survey Suggestions**: *Survey Suggestions* (`survey_suggestions`, `text`, *optional*): Suggest any features or improvements you would like to see in our service.
* **Survey Recommendations**: *Survey Recommendations* (`survey_recommendations`, `text`, *optional*): Recommend any features or services you would like to see us offer in the future.
* **Survey Features**: *Survey Features* (`survey_features`, `text`, `optional*): Describe any features you would like to see in our service.
* **Liked**: *Liked* (`survey_liked`, `select_one`, *required*): Select 'Yes' if you liked our service.
* **Liked Features**: *Liked Features* (`survey_liked_features`, `select_multiple`, *required*): Select all the features you liked about our service.
* **Disliked**: *Disliked* (`survey_disliked`, `select_one`, *required*): Select 'Yes' if you disliked any part of our service.
* **Disliked Features**: *Disliked Features* (`survey_disliked_features`, `select_multiple`, *required*): Select all the features you disliked about our service.
