# Skill Utilization Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skill Utilization Feedback Survey is a tool for employees to provide feedback on their skill utilization in the workplace. The survey is designed to help assess how often and how important employees find utilizing their skills in their daily work.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Introduction**: This is a read-only page that serves as an introduction to the survey.
2. **Employee Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
3. **Job Title**: Enter your job title as it is reflected on your official documents.
4. **Job Description**: Provide a brief description of your job duties.
5. **Skill Utilization**: Select all the skills that you utilize in your daily work.
	* Managerially-oriented: You use your skills to make decisions or provide guidance to others.
	* Data-driven: You use data or statistics to inform your work.
	* Analytical: You analyze data or information to make decisions or recommendations.
	* Creative: You use your imagination or creativity to solve problems or come up with new ideas.
	* Collaborative: You work with others to achieve a common goal.
	* Adaptable: You adapt to changing circumstances or priorities.
6. **How Often Do You Utilize Your Skills**: Select how often you use your skills in your daily work.
	* Daily: You use your skills every day.
	* Weekly: You use your skills every week.
	* Monthly: You use your skills on a monthly basis.
	* Rarely: You rarely use your skills.
7. **How Important is Skill Utilization to Your Job**: Select the importance of skill utilization in your job.
	* Very Important: Skill utilization is crucial to your job.
	* Somewhat Important: Skill utilization is somewhat important to your job.
	* Not Very Important: Skill utilization is not very important to your job.
	* Not at All Important: Skill utilization is not important to your job.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: This is a text field where you will enter your name.
* **Job Title**: This is a text field where you will enter your job title.
* **Job Description**: This is a text field where you will enter a brief description of your job duties.
* **Skill Utilization**: This is a select multiple field where you will select how your skills are utilized in your job.
* **How Often Do You Utilize Your Skills**: This is a select one field where you will select how often you utilize your skills in your daily work.
* **How Important is Skill Utilization to Your Job**: This is a select one field where you will select the importance of skill utilization in your job.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about any of the options, please select the one that best describes your perspective.
* You can take as much time as you need to complete the survey, but please submit it once you have finished.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
