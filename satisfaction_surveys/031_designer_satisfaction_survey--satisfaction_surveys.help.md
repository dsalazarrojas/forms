# Designer Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Designer Satisfaction Survey form is designed to gather feedback and opinions from designers about their experience working on a project. This form is used to collect data that can be used to improve the project management process and overall design process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the introduction field with a brief comment about your experience.
2.  Provide detailed feedback in the feedback field.
3.  Rate your overall satisfaction with the project using the rating field.
4.  Describe the design process in the design process field.
5.  Select your overall satisfaction level from the dropdown menu.
6.  Provide any suggestions for improvement in the suggestions field.
7.  Fill out the contact information fields with your email, phone number, and contact information.
8.  Confirm whether or not you have submitted your feedback using the submission confirmation field.
9.  Add any additional comments in the submission comments field.
10. Fill out the date and time fields with the date and time you submitted your feedback.
11. Enter your IP address in the submission IP field.
12. Enter your region in the submission region field.
13. Enter your city in the submission city field.
14. Enter your street in the submission street field.
15. Enter your postal code in the submission postal code field.
16. Add any additional comments in the submission comments field.
17. Enter your latitude in the submission latitude field.
18. Enter your longitude in the submission longitude field.
19. Enter your accuracy in the submission accuracy field.
20. Enter your zip code in the submission zip field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Introduction**: This field allows you to provide a brief comment about your experience. This is a text field where you can type in as much or as little as you like.
*   **Feedback**: This field is for providing detailed feedback about your experience. Please be as specific as possible so that we can better understand your thoughts.
*   **Rating**: This field is for rating your overall satisfaction with the project. Use the number field to select a number that reflects your satisfaction level.
*   **Design Process**: This field is for describing the design process that took place during the project. Please be as detailed as possible so that we can learn from your experience.
*   **Overall Satisfaction**: This field is a dropdown menu where you can select your overall satisfaction level with the project.
*   **Suggestion**: This field is for providing suggestions for improvement. Please be as specific as possible so that we can understand your ideas.
*   **Contact Information**: This section is for providing your contact information, including email, phone number, and contact info. Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
*   **Submission Confirmation**: This field is for confirming whether or not you have submitted your feedback. Please select 'Yes' if you have submitted your feedback.
*   **Submission Comments**: This field is for adding any additional comments about your submission.
*   **Submission Date and Time**: This section is for filling out the date and time you submitted your feedback.
*   **Submission IP**: This field is for entering your IP address.
*   **Submission Region**: This field is for entering your region.
*   **Submission City**: This field is for entering your city.
*   **Submission Street**: This field is for entering your street.
*   **Submission Postal Code**: This field is for entering your postal code.
*   **Submission Comments**: This field is for adding any additional comments.
*   **Submission Latitude**: This field is for entering your latitude.
*   **Submission Longitude**: This field is for entering your longitude.
*   **Submission Accuracy**: This field is for entering your accuracy.
*   **Submission Zip**: This field is for entering your zip code.
