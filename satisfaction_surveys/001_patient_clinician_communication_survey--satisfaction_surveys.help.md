# Patient Clinician Communication Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and opinions from patients and clinicians on their communication experiences in the healthcare setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your category of response: Patient, Clinician, or Other
2. Choose the type of provider you interacted with: General Practitioner, Specialist, or Other
3. Select the channels you use for communication: Face-to-face, Telephone, Email, SMS, or multiple options
4. Indicate the frequency of your communication: Rarely, Sometimes, Often, or Very Often
5. Express your satisfaction level: Very Dissatisfied, Dissatisfied, Neutral, Satisfied, or Very Satisfied
6. Identify areas for improvement: Understanding, Responsiveness, Communication, or Other
7. Provide any additional comments
8. Specify the tool assigned to you for this form (if applicable)
9. Enter a brief description of this form
10. Enter a title for this form
11. If applicable, specify the tool assigned to you for this form
