# Funeral Home Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Funeral Home Survey is designed to gather information about your experience with our funeral home. Your feedback is crucial in helping us improve our services and provide the best possible care for our families in need. This survey is completely anonymous, and we value your opinions and suggestions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully to understand what we're asking.
2. Select your answer for each question.
3. If you have any comments or suggestions, please type them in the "Comments" field.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Funeral Home Experience
• **Funeral Home Experience** (select_one): This question asks about your overall experience at the funeral home. Please select "Yes" if you were satisfied with your experience, and "No" if you were not.

### Staff Interaction
• **Staff Interaction** (select_multiple): This question asks about the quality of interaction you had with the staff at the funeral home. Please select all the options that apply to your experience, such as "Yes" if you found the staff friendly and helpful, or "No" if you felt they were not.

### Services Quality
• **Services Quality** (number): This question asks you to rate the quality of services provided by the funeral home. Please select a number between 1 and 5 to rate your experience.

### Overall Satisfaction
• **Overall Satisfaction** (number): This question asks you to rate your overall satisfaction with the funeral home. Please select a number between 1 and 5 to rate your experience.

### Recommendations For Improvement
• **Recommendations For Improvement** (select_one): This question asks if you have any suggestions for how we can improve our services. Please select "Yes" if you have some suggestions, and "No" if you don't.

### Comments
• **Comments** (text): This is an open-ended field where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions about your experience with the funeral home.
