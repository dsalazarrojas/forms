# Spa Facility Comfort Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spa Facility Comfort Evaluation Survey is a form designed to gather feedback on the comfort level of a spa facility. It asks guests to rate their experience in various areas, providing the spa management with valuable insights to improve their services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your honest and detailed answers. Make sure to provide feedback on all areas listed, as it will help the spa management understand your experience better.
2. Select the options that best describe your experience in each category.
3. If you have any comments or suggestions, please type them in the Comments field.
4. Finally, sign and date the form to confirm that you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Spa Facilities**: This field asks you to select which facilities you used during your visit, such as massage or spa treatments.
* **Guest Name**: This is your name, which will be used to identify who completed the form.
* **Email**: Your email address, where the spa can contact you if needed.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number, in case the spa needs to contact you.
* **How would you rate your overall comfort?**: This field asks you to rate your overall comfort level, from 1 to 5.
* **Air Quality**: This field asks you to rate the air quality at the spa, from 1 to 5.
* **Temperature Control**: This field asks you to rate the temperature control at the spa, from 1 to 5.
* **Ambiance**: This field asks you to rate the ambiance of the spa, from 1 to 5.
* **Facilities**: This field asks you to rate the facilities of the spa, from 1 to 5.
* **Service**: This field asks you to rate the service at the spa, from 1 to 5.
* **Overall**: This field asks you to rate your overall comfort level, from 1 to 5.
* **Spa Facilities (2)**: This field asks you to select which facilities you used during your visit, such as massage or spa treatments. Please select all that apply.
* **Spa Staff**: This field asks you to select which spa staff members you interacted with during your visit. Please select all that apply.
* **Facilities (2)**: This field asks you to select which facilities you used during your visit, such as amenities or services. Please select all that apply.
* **Ambiance (2)**: This field asks you to select which ambiance you experienced during your visit, such as music or lighting. Please select all that apply.
* **Service (2)**: This field asks you to select which services you received during your visit, such as massages or treatments. Please select all that apply.
* **Spa Staff Friendly**: This field asks you to select which spa staff members you found friendly and helpful during your visit. Please select all that apply.
* **Time Spent**: This field asks you to estimate the time you spent at the spa.
* **Date**: This field asks you to enter the date of your visit.
* **Comments**: This field is for any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Signature**: This is a field for your digital signature.
* **Email Sent To**: This field is for the email address of the person you wish to receive updates or follow-ups from the spa.
* **Phone Sent To**: This field is for the phone number of the person you wish to receive updates or follow-ups from the spa.
* **Spa Sent To**: This field is for the spa's email address where you wish to receive updates or follow-ups.
* **Comments Sent To**: This field is for any comments or suggestions you wish to receive updates or follow-ups from the spa.

Note that some fields are mandatory, but most are optional. Please complete this form honestly and accurately, and the spa will use your feedback to improve their services.
