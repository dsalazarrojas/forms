# Interdisciplinary Learning Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This survey is designed to gather feedback from users about the Interdisciplinary Learning course they have taken. It is meant to help us understand their satisfaction with the course content, instructors, and overall experience. The survey should only be completed by users who have actually taken the course.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select all that apply.
3. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as possible.
4. Make sure to submit your response at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a note field to provide a brief introduction to the survey.
* **What type of courses did you take?**: Choose "Yes" if you took one or more courses, and "No" if you did not.
* **How many of the courses did you take?**: Enter the number of courses you took.
* **How would you rate the course content?**: Choose all that apply from the options "Good" and "Fair".
* **How would you rate the course instructors?**: Choose all that apply from the options "Good" and "Fair".
* **What did you find most useful in the course?**: Provide a detailed answer to this open-ended question.
* **What did you find least useful in the course?**: Provide a detailed answer to this open-ended question.
* **Additional comments?**: If you have any additional feedback or comments about the course, please provide them here.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Submitted by**: Choose "Yes" if you submitted this survey yourself, and "No" otherwise.
* **Date**: This field is automatically populated and cannot be changed.
* **Time**: This field is automatically populated and cannot be changed.
* **Submit**: Choose "Yes" to submit the survey, and "No" otherwise.
* **Submission Date**: This field is automatically populated and cannot be changed.
* **Submission Time**: This field is automatically populated and cannot be changed.
* **Submission**: Choose "Yes" to submit the survey, and "No" otherwise.
* **How did you find the online interface?**: Choose all that apply from the options "Yes" and "No".
* **How did you find the feedback process?**: Choose all that apply from the options "Yes" and "No".
* **Submission Status**: Choose from the options "Active" and "Inactive".
* **Comments**: Provide any additional feedback or comments about the course.
* **How did you find the overall quality of the course?**: Enter a number between 1 and 10 to rate the quality of the course.
* **How often do you want to take this type of course?**: Enter a number between 1 and 10 to indicate how often you want to take this type of course.
* **What did you find most useful in the course?**: Provide a detailed answer to this open-ended question.
* **What did you find least useful in the course?**: Provide a detailed answer to this open-ended question.

This guide is meant to help you complete the Interdisciplinary Learning Satisfaction Survey form. If you have any questions or need clarification, feel free to ask.
