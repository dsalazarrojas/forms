# Building Operations Transparency Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your experiences and perceptions regarding building operations, management, and communication. Your feedback will help us understand how we can improve transparency and communication in our building operations, ensuring that you have a better experience as a tenant or occupant.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the survey date from the calendar.
2. Enter the name of the building you occupy.
3. Choose your role in the building (e.g., tenant, owner, employee, etc.).
4. Select how long you have occupied this building.
5. Choose how often you hear from management.
6. Rate the clarity of communications from management.
7. Rate the completeness of information provided.
8. Rate the transparency of maintenance and repairs.
9. Rate the transparency of building budget and finances.
10. Rate the clarity of building policies.
11. Rate the clarity of emergency procedures.
12. Select your preferred communication channels.
13. Rate management's response time to concerns.
14. Rate your satisfaction with how concerns are resolved.
15. Choose how often management actively seeks your feedback.
16. Rate how often management acts on feedback.
17. If you have any suggestions for improving transparency, type them in the text field.
18. Finally, rate your overall satisfaction with transparency in the building.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are completing this survey.
* **Building Name** (`building_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the building you occupy.
* **Your Role** (`respondent_role`, select_one, required): Choose your role in the building (e.g., tenant, owner, employee, etc.).
* **How Long Have You Occupied This Building** (`occupancy_duration`, select_one, required): Select how long you have occupied this building.
* **Frequency of Building Communications** (`communication_frequency`, select_one, required): Choose how often you hear from management.
* **Clarity of Communications From Building Management** (`communication_clarity`, select_one, required): Rate the clarity of communications from management.
* **Clarity of Emergency Procedures** (`emergency_communication`, select_one, required): Rate the clarity of emergency procedures.
* **Preferred Communication Channels** (`communication_channels`, select_multiple, required): Select your preferred communication channels (e.g., email, phone call, in-person meeting).
* **Management Response Time to Concerns** (`response_time`, select_one, required): Rate management's response time to concerns.
* **Satisfaction With How Concerns Are Resolved** (`concern_resolution`, select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction with how concerns are resolved.
* **Management Actively Seeks Your Feedback** (`suggestion_request`, select_one, required): Choose how often management actively seeks your feedback.
* **Management Acts on Resident Feedback** (`action_on_feedback`, select_one, required): Rate how often management acts on feedback.
* **Suggestions for Improving Transparency** (`transparency_improvements`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions for improving transparency, type them in this text field.
* **Overall Satisfaction With Transparency** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with transparency in the building.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* You can select one or more options for "Preferred Communication Channels" as they may apply.
* If you have any suggestions for improving transparency, be as specific as possible so we can take your feedback seriously.
