# Property Energy Audit Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Energy Audit Feedback Survey is a form designed to gather feedback from property owners or managers on their energy audits. This survey helps assess the effectiveness of energy audits conducted on properties and gather additional comments or ratings from respondents.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing your property address.
2. Fill in your name.
3. Enter your contact email address.
4. Provide your contact phone number.
5. Rate your property's energy efficiency and provide comments on the rating.
6. Indicate if energy audits were conducted on your property.
7. Select all energy audits that have been completed on your property.
8. Add any general comments you have on the energy audits.
9. If you wish, submit the form with the date and time you completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Address**: Enter the address of the property you are providing feedback on.
* **Name**: Please enter your name as it will appear on the feedback form response.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your contact email address so we can reach out to you.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number so we can reach out to you.
* **Property Energy Rating**: Rate your property's energy efficiency out of 5. Please select one of the ratings below:
	+ 1: Very poor
	+ 2: Poor
	+ 3: Neutral
	+ 4: Good
	+ 5: Excellent
* **Rating Comments**: Add any comments about your property's energy rating.
* **Energy Audits Conducted**: Indicate if energy audits were conducted on your property.
* **Energy Audits Completed**: Select all energy audits that have been completed on your property.
* **Comments**: Add any general comments about the energy audits.
* **Date Submitted**: If you wish, provide the date and time you submitted this form.
