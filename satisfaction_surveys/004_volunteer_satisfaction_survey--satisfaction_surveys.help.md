# Volunteer Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Satisfaction Survey is designed to collect feedback and opinions from volunteers about their experiences and satisfaction with the project.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page of the form carefully before starting to fill it out.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Use the provided options for select_one and select_multiple questions to choose the best answer that fits your opinion.
4. If you have any comments or suggestions, use the text fields to provide detailed feedback.
5. Make sure to complete all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How would you rate the overall project satisfaction?** (`volunteer_satisfaction_survey`, `number`, required): This question asks for your overall satisfaction with the project. Please rate your satisfaction on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is Very Dissatisfied and 5 is Very Satisfied.
* **How satisfied are you with project communication?** (`project_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): This question asks about your satisfaction with project communication. Choose the option that best describes your level of satisfaction.
* **How satisfied are you with your role/position within the project?** (`volunteer_experience`, `select_one`, required): This question asks about your satisfaction with your role or position within the project. Choose the option that best describes your level of satisfaction.
* **Have you had any issues with project communication?** (`project_communication_issues`, `select_multiple`, required): This question asks if you have had any issues with project communication. Choose all that apply from the options.
* **What do you think is the most valuable thing that we could do to improve project communication?** (`improve_project_communication`, `text`, required): Please provide a detailed suggestion on how to improve project communication.
* **What do you think is the most valuable thing that we could do to improve your role/position within the project?** (`volunteer_satisfaction_improvement`, `text`, required): Please provide a detailed suggestion on how to improve your role or position within the project.
* **How satisfied are you with the overall project process?** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): This question asks about your overall satisfaction with the project process. Choose the option that best describes your level of satisfaction.
* **What do you think we could do to make the project more efficient?** (`project_efficiency`, `text`, required): Please provide a detailed suggestion on how to make the project more efficient.
* **What do you like or dislike about the project?** (`project_comments`, `text`, required): This question allows you to provide any comments or suggestions about the project.
* **What is the main reason for your participation in this project?** (`participation_goal`, `text`, required): This question asks about the main reason for your participation in the project.
* **Do you think your involvement in this project aligns with your personal goals?** (`involvement_alignment`, `select_one`, required): This question asks if you think your involvement in the project aligns with your personal goals. Choose the option that best describes your opinion.
* **How likely are you to participate in future projects like this?** (`future_participation`, `select_one`, required): This question asks about your likelihood of participating in future projects like this. Choose the option that best describes your opinion.
