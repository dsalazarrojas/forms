# Educational Kit Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on educational kits, helping us understand how well they meet your needs and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Select the options that best match your experience with the educational kit.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "comments" fields to provide more information.
4. Fill out the form completely, but do not worry if you are unable to answer every question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Introduction**: This is a note field to provide an overview of your experience with the educational kit. You can type as much or as little as you like to give us an idea of your general thoughts.
* **Usefulness**: Describe how useful you found the educational kit in your experience. For example, how well did it meet your needs?
* **Content Quality**: Rate the quality of the content provided in the educational kit. For example, was it well-structured and easy to follow?
* **Ease of Use**: Rate how easy or difficult you found the educational kit to use. For example, did it take a lot of time to understand or implement the concepts?
* **Rating - Usefulness**: Provide a numerical rating (1-5) of how useful you found the educational kit.
* **Rating - Content Quality**: Provide a numerical rating (1-5) of the content quality of the educational kit.
* **Rating - Ease of Use**: Provide a numerical rating (1-5) of how easy or difficult you found the educational kit to use.
* **Rating - Likely to Recommend**: Select how likely you are to recommend the educational kit to others. For example, would you recommend it to a friend or colleague?
* **Feedback**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about the educational kit.
* **Contact Name**: If you would like to be contacted regarding your feedback, please provide your name.
* **Contact Email**: If you would like to be contacted regarding your feedback, please provide your email address.
* **Contact Phone**: If you would like to be contacted regarding your feedback, please provide your phone number.
* **Contact Other**: If you would like to be contacted regarding your feedback, please provide any other contact information you have.
* **Contact Other Info**: If you would like to provide any additional contact information, please do so here.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about the educational kit.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about the educational kit.
* **Rating - Very Difficult to Use**: Rate how difficult you found the educational kit to use, with 1-3 representing less difficult and 7 or more representing more difficult.
* **Rating - Often Use**: Select how often you use the educational kit, with Never, Occasionally, Sometimes, Often, and Always representing different levels of usage.
* **Rating - Worthwhile**: Rate how worthwhile you found the educational kit, with Not Worthwhile, Not Very Worthwhile, Somewhat Worthwhile, Very Worthwhile, and Extremely Worthwhile representing different levels of satisfaction.
* **Rating - Satisfied**: Rate your level of satisfaction with the educational kit, with Not Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Very Satisfied, and Extremely Satisfied representing different levels of satisfaction.
* **Rating - Likely to Recommend to Other**: Select how likely you are to recommend the educational kit to others, with Not Recommended, Recommended, Not Recommended to Anyone, Recommended to a Few, Recommended to Many, and Recommended to All representing different levels of recommendation.
* **Rating - Very Helpful**: Select how helpful you found the educational kit, with Not Helpful, Somewhat Helpful, Very Helpful, Extremely Helpful, and Not Helpful to Anyone, Helpful to a Few, Helpful to Many, and Helpful to All representing different levels of helpfulness.
* **Rating - Improve**: Rate what area of the educational kit you would like to see improved, with Not Improve, Somewhat Improve, Very Improve, and Extremely Improve representing different levels of improvement.
* **Rating - Improve Area**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have on what area of the educational kit needs improvement.
* **Rating - Improve Other**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have on what area of the educational kit needs improvement.
* **Rating - Very Helpful**: Select how helpful you found the educational kit, with Not Helpful, Somewhat Helpful, Very Helpful, Extremely Helpful, and Not Helpful to Anyone, Helpful to a Few, Helpful to Many, and Helpful to All representing different levels of helpfulness.
