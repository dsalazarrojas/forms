# Financial Statement Review Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from reviewers about their experience with financial institution reviews. It will help us understand areas for improvement and ensure that our review process meets your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your name and department as the reviewer.
2. Select the date and time of the review you conducted.
3. Choose the financial institution you reviewed.
4. Rate your overall satisfaction with the review process (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Needs Improvement).
5. Provide any comments or notes about your experience.
6. Indicate if you submitted any of the review pages (Pages 1, 2, or 3).
7. Provide your email address and phone number.
8. If you answered "Yes" to any of the pages submitted, please specify which ones.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information**: This is where you can provide any relevant information about the client.
* **Review Experience**: Describe your experience with the review process, including any positive or negative aspects.
* **Financial Institution**: Choose the financial institution you reviewed.
* **Review Date**: Enter the date of the review.
* **Review Time**: Enter the time of the review.
* **Client Name**: This is where you can provide the client's name.
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Comments**: Provide any comments or notes about your experience.
* **Rating**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the review process.
* **Review Comments**: Provide any additional comments about the review process.
* **Form Page 1 Submitted**: Indicate if you submitted Form Page 1.
* **Form Page 2 Submitted**: Indicate if you submitted Form Page 2.
* **Form Page 3 Submitted**: Indicate if you submitted Form Page 3.
* **Financial Institution of Reviewer**: Choose the financial institution where you work.
* **Reviewer Notes**: Provide any notes or comments about your experience as a reviewer.
