# Tournament Entry Fee Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gauge your opinion on the fairness and value of the entry fee associated with a tournament. Your input will help us understand how we can improve the overall experience and make necessary changes to the entry fee structure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions on each page. You can select one of the options provided in each question. The form is split into three questions, each on a separate page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How do you feel about the entry fee fairness?** (`entry_fee_fairness`, select_one, required: false): Please choose one of the following options to describe your perception of the entry fee fairness:
	+ Fair: You feel that the entry fee is reasonable in comparison to the experience and services offered.
	+ Not Fair: You feel that the entry fee is higher than it should be or not worth the experience and services offered.
	+ Unfair: You feel that the entry fee is significantly higher than others in similar tournaments.
* **How do you feel about the value for money of this tournament?** (`value_for_money`, select_one, required: false): Please choose one of the following options to describe your perception of the value for money:
	+ Good value: You feel that the entry fee provides a good balance of quality and cost.
	+ Fair value: You feel that the entry fee is average, not too high or too low.
	+ Bad value: You feel that the entry fee is too high for the experience and services offered.
* **How likely is it that you will participate in the future?** (`future_participation`, select_one, required: false): Please choose one of the following options to describe your likelihood of participating in the tournament again:
	+ Highly likely: You are very likely to participate in the tournament in the future.
	+ Somewhat likely: You are somewhat likely to participate in the tournament in the future, but not as likely as you were in the past.
	+ Not likely: You are not very likely to participate in the tournament in the future.
	+ Not at all likely: You are not likely to participate in the tournament in the future.

## Tips
Please answer all questions honestly, as your feedback will help us make necessary improvements to the tournament entry fee structure.
