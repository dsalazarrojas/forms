# Makeup Technique Comfort Level Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to help us understand your comfort level with various makeup techniques. Your responses will help us improve our services and provide more effective training and resources for you and others in the industry. Please answer the questions honestly, and feel free to elaborate on your answers if you'd like.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by reading the introduction on the first page.
2. Answer the questions on each page, and make sure to fill in all required fields.
3. Select your preferred response options for select multiple and select one fields.
4. Type your answers in the text fields.
5. Submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Page 1, Required: No): This page introduces you to the survey and its purpose.
* **Makeup Technique Comfort Level** (Page 2, Required: Yes): Rate your comfort level with different makeup techniques on a scale of 1-10.
* **Makeup Technique Confidence Level** (Page 3, Required: Yes): Select the makeup techniques you are confident in using.
* **Skills Assessed** (Page 4, Required: Yes): List the skills that you have assessed or reviewed recently.
* **Class Planned** (Page 5, Required: Yes): Describe the class or workshop you are planning to attend or have attended.
* **Content Planners** (Page 6, Required: No): This field is optional and is for you to provide more details about the content you have planned.
* **Next Steps** (Page 7, Required: Yes): Describe your next steps or plans for improving your makeup skills.
* **Feedback** (Page 8, Required: No): This field is optional and is for you to provide any feedback or comments about the survey.
* **Makeup Technique Comfort Level 2** (Page 9, Required: No): Select your comfort level with different makeup techniques on a second scale.
* **Makeup Technique Confidence Level 2** (Page 10, Required: No): Select the makeup techniques you are confident in using on a second scale.
* **Learning Interests** (Page 11, Required: Yes, Multiple Select): Select the types of content you are interested in learning.
* **Class Planned Details** (Page 12, Required: No): This field is optional and is for you to provide more details about the class or workshop you are planning to attend.
* **Next Steps 2** (Page 13, Required: Yes): Describe your next steps or plans for improving your makeup skills.
* **Client Feedback** (Page 14, Required: No): This field is optional and is for you to provide feedback or comments about the survey.
* **Learning Interests 2** (Page 15, Required: No, Multiple Select): Select the types of content you are interested in learning on a second scale.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all required fields.
* Be as specific and honest as possible in your answers.
* You can select multiple options for select multiple fields.
* You can use as many lines as needed in text fields.
* If you have any feedback or comments, please use the feedback field.
* Don't hesitate to contact us if you need any assistance or clarification.
