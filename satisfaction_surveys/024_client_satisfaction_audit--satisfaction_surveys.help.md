# Client Satisfaction Audit - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on your experience with our products and services. It will help us identify areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance your experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out all required fields (marked as "required" in the form). This will help us gather the essential information we need to analyze and improve our services.
2. Answer each question honestly, providing as much detail as possible. Your input will be kept confidential, and we will use it solely for the purpose of improving our services.
3. If you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask or provide a brief explanation in the "Additional Comments or Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification or business card.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Customer Since** (`customer_since`, `date`, optional): Approximately when did you start using our products/services?
* **Primary Product or Service Used** (`primary_product_used`, `select_one`, required): Select the primary product or service you have been using from the options provided.
* **Quality of Product** (`product_quality_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the quality of the product you have been using from the options provided.
* **Does Product Meet Your Needs?** (`product_meets_needs`, `select_one`, required): Choose how well the product meets your needs from the options provided.
* **Quality of Service** (`service_quality_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the quality of the service from the options provided.
* **Customer Support Quality** (`customer_support_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the quality of our customer support from the options provided.
* **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate the value for money of our products and services from the options provided.
* **Delivery or Service Timeliness** (`delivery_timeliness`, `select_one`, required): Choose how often our delivery or services meet their timelines from the options provided.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Select how satisfied you are with our products and services from the options provided.
* **Likelihood to Recommend** (`likelihood_recommend`, `number`, required): Indicate how likely you are to recommend us to others on a scale of 1-5.
* **Strongest Aspects of Our Products** (`product_strengths`, `text`, optional): Please list the strengths of our products that you find most valuable.
* **Strongest Aspects of Our Services** (`service_strengths`, `text`, optional): Please list the strengths of our services that you find most valuable.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`improvement_suggestions`, `text`, optional): Provide any suggestions or ideas you have to improve our products and services.
* **Additional Comments or Feedback** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Share any other thoughts or concerns you may have about our products and services.

Please note that the "Additional Comments or Feedback" field is where you can provide any further comments or suggestions you might not have space for in the other fields. We appreciate any input, so don't hesitate to use this field if you have something to add!
