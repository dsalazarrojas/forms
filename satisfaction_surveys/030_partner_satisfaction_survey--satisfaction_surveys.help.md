# Partner Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Partner Satisfaction Survey is designed to help our organization understand the level of satisfaction our partners experience in working with us. This survey is meant to be a helpful tool for us to identify areas for improvement and provide a better service to our partners. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate response options for each question.
2. Enter any comments or feedback in the text boxes.
3. Fill in your contact information if you are willing to share it.
4. Submit the completed form and we will use this information to improve our services. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **partner_survey (1)**: Enter a brief description of your recent experience with our organization.
* **rating_question (2)**: Please rate your overall satisfaction with our services. Choose one of the provided options.
* **satisfaction_survey (3)**: How satisfied are you with our services? Select "Satisfied" or "Dissatisfied".
* **feedback_question (4)**: If you have any comments or feedback, please enter them here.
* **partner_rating (5)**: If applicable, enter a numeric rating of your satisfaction.
* **time_question (6)**: Enter the amount of time you spent on recent interactions with our organization.
* **date_question (7)**: Enter the date of your recent interaction with our organization.
* **contact_info (8)**: Enter your contact information, if you are willing to share it.
* **partner_survey_2 (9)**: Enter any additional comments or feedback you have.
* **partner_satisfaction (10)**: If applicable, enter a numeric rating of your satisfaction.
* **email (11)**: Enter your email address, if you are willing to share it.
* **phone (12)**: Enter your phone number, if you are willing to share it.
* **contact_person (13)**: Enter the name of the contact person you interacted with.
* **department (14)**: Have you had contact with our department? Select "Yes" or "No".
* **manager_name (15)**: If applicable, enter the name of your manager.
* **manager_title (16)**: If applicable, enter the title of your manager.
* **partner_survey_3 (17)**: Enter any additional comments or feedback.
* **partner_survey_4 (18)**: Enter any additional comments or feedback.
* **partner_survey_5 (19)**: Have we communicated with you effectively? Select "Yes" or "No".
* **partner_survey_6 (20)**: Enter any additional comments or feedback.
* **partner_survey_7 (21)**: Have we met your expectations? Select "Yes" or "No".
* **partner_survey_8 (22)**: Have we communicated with you effectively? Select "Yes" or "No". 

## Tips
* Please answer these questions as honestly as possible.
* If you need help or clarification on any question, you can reach out to us.
* We value your feedback and will use it to improve our services.
