# Program Member Satisfaction Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from program members to assess their satisfaction with our program. The goal is to understand how often they interact with us, their level of satisfaction, and gather suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions about how often you interact with us, choosing the option that best reflects your experience.
2. Rate your level of satisfaction with our program, selecting all that apply from the list of options.
3. If you have any suggestions for improvement, type them in the text field.
4. Enter your email address in the format `name@example.com`.
5. Enter your phone number for follow-up contact.
6. Provide any additional comments you have about your experience with our program.
7. If applicable, enter the date and time of your last interaction with us.
8. Rate your overall satisfaction with our program on a scale of 0-5.
9. Once you have completed the form, click the "Submit" button to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How often do you interact with us?** (`program_member_satisfaction_assessment_form`, select_one, required: false): This question asks about your frequency of interaction with our program. Select the option that best reflects your experience, such as "Never" or "Weekly".
* **How would you rate your level of satisfaction with our program?** (`program_member_satisfaction_level`, select_multiple, required: false): This question allows you to rate your level of satisfaction with our program. Select all that apply from the list of options, including "Very dissatisfied" to "Very satisfied".
* **What could we improve?** (`program_member_suggestions`, note, required: false): This is a text field where you can provide suggestions for improvement. Type in any comments or ideas you have about how we can better serve you.
* **Email** (`program_member_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address in the format `name@example.com`.
* **Phone** (`program_member_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for follow-up contact.
* **Comments** (`program_member_comments`, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments about your experience with our program.
* **Date** (`program_member_date`, date, required: false): If you have a specific date of your last interaction with us, enter it in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Time** (`program_member_time`, time, required: false): If you have a specific time of your last interaction with us, enter it in the format `HH:MM:SS`.
* **Rating** (`program_member_rating`, number, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with our program on a scale of 0-5.
* **Form submitted** (`program_member_form_submitted`, note, required: false): This field is used to confirm that you have submitted the form. Do not enter anything in this field.
