# Procurement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from users about their experiences with a product or service. Your feedback will help the procurement team to identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions about future purchases.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Please answer each question carefully and accurately.
2. Make sure to provide your contact information so that we can get back to you if needed.
3. You can skip any questions that are not relevant to your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, optional): This is a brief introduction to the form. Please read it carefully and understand the purpose of the form.
* **Product Category** (`product_category`, `text`, optional): Please select the category of the product or service you are providing feedback on.
* **Product Type** (`product_type`, `text`, optional): Please select the type of the product or service you are providing feedback on.
* **Rating Score** (`rating_score`, `number`, optional): Please rate your satisfaction with the product or service on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Not Satisfied" and 5 is "Satisfied".
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, optional): Please enter your email address so that we can get back to you if needed.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Please enter your phone number so that we can contact you if needed.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Please provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the product or service.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, `text`, optional): Please enter the name of the product or service you are providing feedback on.
