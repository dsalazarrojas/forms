# Wayfinder Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect your thoughts and feelings about your experience with the Wayfinder system. Your feedback will help us understand what works well and what needs improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. If you have any suggestions or comments, please type them in the "Suggestions" field.
3. If you're unsure about any of the questions, feel free to ask for clarification.
4. Please submit your form once you've completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### <i class="fa fa-circle"></i> 1. Navigation Experience
* **Navigation Experience**: Describe your overall experience with the navigation system. Please provide your honest feedback.

### <i class="fa fa-circle"></i> 2. Signage Clarity
* **Signage Clarity**: How clear were the signs and directions in the facility?
* Please rate the clarity of the signs and directions as clear or not clear.

### <i class="fa fa-circle"></i> 3. Wayfinding Experience
* **Wayfinding Experience**: Describe your experience with finding your way around the facility.

### <i class="fa fa-circle"></i> 4. Navigation Help
* **Navigation Help**: How often do you seek help from staff to navigate?
* Select one of the following: Often, Seldom, or Never.

### <i class="fa fa-circle"></i> 5. Overall Experience
* **Overall Experience**: Describe your overall experience with the Wayfinder system.

### <i class="fa fa-circle"></i> 6. Navigation Emotions
* **Navigation Emotions**: How do you feel when navigating?
* Select all that apply: Happy, Neutral, or Sad.

### <i class="fa fa-circle"></i> 7. Time Spent
* **Time Spent**: If you're comfortable sharing, please provide the date and time you spent navigating.

### <i class="fa fa-circle"></i> 8. Staff Helpfulness
* **Staff Helpfulness**: How helpful were the staff in assisting you with navigation?
* Select one of the following: Very Helpful or Not Helpful.

### <i class="fa fa-circle"></i> 9. Suggestions
* **Suggestions**: Do you have any suggestions for the facility or Wayfinder system?
* Please type your suggestions in the text area below.

# Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your feedback.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask.
* Your feedback is valuable to us, and it will help us improve the Wayfinder system.
