# Pet Training Program Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Training Program Satisfaction Survey is designed to collect feedback and satisfaction data from individuals who have participated in our pet training program. Your input will help us understand what aspects of the program you enjoyed and where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the program title from the list below that you participated in.
2. Fill out the required fields: Name, Email, and Phone.
3. Select your rating for the program (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
4. Provide your overall experience with the program (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
5. Select the staff interaction level you experienced (Extremely Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Extremely Dissatisfied).
6. Choose the aspects of the program content you found valuable (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
7. Select the level of communication you experienced (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
8. Choose the schedule of the program (On Time, Sometimes, Late).
9. Select the price level of the program (Reasonable, Expensive, Very Expensive).
10. If you recommend this program to friends, select Yes or No.
11. If you plan to attend another program, select Yes or No.
12. Fill out the comments section with any additional comments or questions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Title**: Please select the program title from the list below that you participated in.
* **Name**: Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed on your survey response.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you with any further questions or concerns.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number to enable us to contact you if needed.
* **Program Rating**: Select your overall rating of the program (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Overall Experience**: Select your overall experience with the program (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Staff Interaction**: Choose the level of staff interaction you experienced (Extremely Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Extremely Dissatisfied).
* **Program Content**: Select the aspects of the program content you found valuable (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Communication**: Select the level of communication you experienced (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Schedule**: Choose the schedule of the program (On Time, Sometimes, Late).
* **Price**: Select the price level of the program (Reasonable, Expensive, Very Expensive).
* **Recommend**: If you recommend this program to friends, select Yes or No.
* **Next Program**: If you plan to attend another program, select Yes or No.
* **Comments**: Fill out the comments section with any additional comments or questions you may have.
* **Program Questions**: Enter any questions or concerns you have about the program.
* **Program Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the program.
* **Phone of Contact**: Enter the name of the person who completed the survey.
* **Email of Contact**: Enter the email address of the person who completed the survey.
* **Phone of Contact**: Enter the phone number of the person who completed the survey. 

Note: Please fill out the required fields marked as required (asterisk: *) in order for the survey to be submitted successfully.
