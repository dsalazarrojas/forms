# Product Satisfaction Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Satisfaction Questionnaire is a tool used to measure the satisfaction level of customers with a product. It helps us gather data that can be used to improve the product and enhance customer experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully before answering the questions. Each question is designed to help us understand your thoughts and feelings about our product.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability, providing as much detail as possible.
3. If you're unsure about any question, please contact us and we'll be happy to assist you.
4. Once you've completed the form, click the submit button to send us your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Introduction
• **Introduction** (`introduction`, note, optional): This is a brief introduction to the form, explaining its purpose and importance. Please read it carefully before proceeding.

### Product Satisfaction
• **Product Satisfaction** (`product_satisfaction`, number, optional): This question asks you to rate your overall satisfaction with our product on a scale of 1-5. Please choose a number that best represents your level of satisfaction.

### Overall Satisfaction
• **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, optional): This question asks you to rate your overall satisfaction with our product on a scale of 1-5. Please choose a number that best represents your level of satisfaction.

### Rating
• **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): This question asks you to rate your satisfaction with specific aspects of our product. Please choose a number that best represents your level of satisfaction for each aspect.

### Comments
• **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): This is an open-ended question where you can provide any additional thoughts or comments about our product. Please use this space to share your feedback and suggestions.

### Overall Satisfaction 1-5
