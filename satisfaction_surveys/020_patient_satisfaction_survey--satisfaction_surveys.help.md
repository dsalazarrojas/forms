# Patient Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather patient feedback and satisfaction ratings on various aspects of their visit. Your responses will help healthcare providers understand their patients' needs and improve their services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read each question and answer accordingly.
2. Answer all questions accurately and completely.
3. Use the provided options for multiple-choice questions.
4. Enter text responses in the designated fields.
5. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction**: A brief description of your visit or experience with the healthcare provider.
  * This field is optional and serves as a context for your feedback.
- **Questions**: Select all that apply from the options provided.
  * We ask about your satisfaction with the following aspects:
    * Yes: I am satisfied with this aspect.
    * No: I am not satisfied with this aspect.
  * Select all that apply.
- **Rating**: Rate your satisfaction on a scale from 1 to 5.
  * Rate your overall satisfaction with a number from 1 to 5.
- **Comments**: Write any additional comments about your visit or experience.
  * This field is optional and provides more context for your feedback.
- **Name**: Enter your name as you'd like it to appear in our records.
  * This field is optional and helps us identify you as the respondent.
- **Email**: Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
  * This field is optional and helps us reach out to you for follow-up questions.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
  * This field is optional and helps us reach out to you for follow-up questions.
- **Time**: Choose the time of your visit.
  * Select the time of your visit for accurate tracking.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions accurately and completely.
* Use real email and phone numbers for contact purposes.
* Rate your satisfaction honestly.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting.
