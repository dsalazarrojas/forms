# Sweet Haven Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sweet Haven Feedback Form is designed to gather user satisfaction feedback from customers. This form helps us understand your experience and opinions about our service, and allows us to make improvements for future customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please answer all the questions honestly and provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
2. For the rating questions, select the option that best describes your experience with Sweet Haven.
3. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Additional Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Page 1** (`feedback_page_1`): This is the first page of the feedback form. Please answer the questions and provide as much detail as possible.
* **Rate Our Service** (`rating`): Please select the option that best describes your experience with Sweet Haven.
	+ **Excellent**: We exceeded your expectations.
	+ **Good**: You were satisfied with our service.
	+ **Fair**: We met your expectations, but there was room for improvement.
	+ **Poor**: We did not meet your expectations.
* **Share Your Thoughts** (`feedback`): This is a text field where you can provide your thoughts and opinions about your experience with Sweet Haven. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Share Your Email** (`email`): Please enter a valid email address if you would like to be contacted back about your feedback.
* **Share Your Contact Number** (`contact_number`): This is an optional field where you can enter your contact number if you would like to be contacted back about your feedback.
* **Submit** (`submit`): Please click this button to submit your feedback.

* **Feedback Page 2** (`feedback_page_2`): This is the second page of the feedback form. Please answer the questions and provide as much detail as possible.
* **Rate Our Product** (`rating_2`): Please select the option that best describes your experience with our product.
	+ **Delicious**: Our product exceeded your expectations.
	+ **Good**: You were satisfied with our product.
	+ **Fair**: Our product met your expectations, but there was room for improvement.
	+ **Poor**: Our product did not meet your expectations.
* **Share Your Thoughts 2** (`feedback_2`): This is a text field where you can provide your thoughts and opinions about your experience with our product. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Share Your Email 2** (`email_2`): Please enter a valid email address if you would like to be contacted back about your feedback.
* **Share Your Contact Number 2** (`contact_number_2`): This is an optional field where you can enter your contact number if you would like to be contacted back about your feedback.
* **Submit 2** (`submit_2`): Please click this button to submit your feedback.
* **Additional Feedback** (`additional_feedback`): If you have any additional comments or feedback that you would like to share, please use this field.
* **Submit Final** (`submit_final`): Please click this button to submit your final feedback.
