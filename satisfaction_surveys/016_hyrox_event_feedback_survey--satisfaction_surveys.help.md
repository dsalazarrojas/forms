# HYROX Event Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback and suggestions from participants after an event. It helps us understand their experience and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Rate your overall satisfaction with the event using the **Participant Score** field. 
2. Evaluate the event location by selecting a rating from **Location Rating**.
3. Share your thoughts on your experience in the **Feedback** field.
4. Provide specific suggestions on how we could improve future events in the **Improvement Suggestion** field.
5. Share general feedback or ideas in the **Participant Suggestion** field.
6. Enter your contact information in the **Email** field for follow-up or updates.
7. Provide your phone number in the **Phone** field for additional communication.
8. If you'd like to share the physical address or details of the event location, use the **Event Location** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Score**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the event on a numerical scale.
* **Location Rating**: Evaluate the event location by selecting one of the provided options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Feedback**: Share your thoughts on your experience with the event.
* **Improvement Suggestion**: Provide specific ideas on how we could improve future events.
* **Participant Suggestion**: Share general feedback or ideas on your experience.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for follow-up or updates.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for additional communication.
* **Event Location**: Note the physical address or details of the event location.
