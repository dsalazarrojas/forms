# IT Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The IT Satisfaction Survey is designed to collect information about your overall satisfaction with our IT services. Your feedback is valuable to us, and we use this survey to understand where we can improve our services to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

*   Fill in the required fields with the information requested:
    *   **Customer Information** (prophet): This is a free-form text field where you can enter your name and any other details you'd like to share.
    *   **Email**: Enter your valid email address. This information is required to ensure we can contact you about any issues or follow-ups.
    *   **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with our IT services on a scale of 1-10.
    *   **Why were you satisfied?**: Select all that apply to indicate why you are satisfied with our IT services.
    *   **Comments**: Share any additional comments or feedback you'd like to provide. This is a free-form text field where you can type as much or as little as you'd like.
*   Review each field carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Customer Information (prophet)**:
    *   A field to enter your name and any other details you'd like to share with us.
*   **Email**:
    *   Enter your valid email address to ensure we can contact you about any issues or follow-ups.
*   **Overall Satisfaction**:
    *   Rate your overall satisfaction with our IT services on a scale of 1-10. The higher the number, the more satisfied you are.
*   **Why were you satisfied?**:
    *   Select all that apply to indicate why you are satisfied with our IT services. Possible answers are 'Yes' and 'No'.
*   **Comments**:
    *   Share any additional comments or feedback you'd like to provide in this free-form text field.
