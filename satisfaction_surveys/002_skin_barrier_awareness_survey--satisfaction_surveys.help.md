# Skin Barrier Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form, titled "Skin Barrier Awareness Survey", is designed to collect information about individuals' skin types, concerns, habits, and preferences related to skin health.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your skin type from the options provided.
2. Choose your skin concerns from the options provided.
3. Select your current knowledge level about the skin barrier.
4. Describe your daily skincare habits.
5. Indicate how often you think about the skin barrier.
6. Choose how often you read or hear about skin barrier-related topics.
7. Select your level of education about skin barrier health.
8. Rate how important skin health is to you.
9. Choose where you usually get information about skin health.
10. Select the types of skincare products you use.
11. Indicate how often you apply skincare products.
12. Choose how important skin health influences your purchasing decisions.
13. Select the factors that influence your purchasing decisions.
14. Indicate how often you make skin-related purchases.
15. Choose where you usually make skin-related purchases.
16. Select the types of skincare products you prefer.
17. Choose how often you obtain skincare products.
18. Select the frequency of using skincare products.
19. Indicate if you have a skincare expert.
20. Choose your skincare goals.
21. Indicate how often you work towards your skincare goals.
22. Rate how important achieving skincare goals is to you.
23. Rate how important skin-related concerns are to you.
24. Indicate how you usually get skin-related products.
25. Finally, rate your satisfaction with this survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Skin Type** (`skin_type`, select_one): Select your skin type from the options provided (e.g., dry, oily, combination, sensitive, or normal).
- **Skin Concerns** (`skin_concerns`, select_multiple): Choose your skin concerns from the options provided (e.g., acne, aging, dryness, hyper-pigmentation, or sensitivity).
- **Knowledge Level** (`knowledge_level`, select_one): Select your current knowledge level about the skin barrier (e.g., basic, average, advanced, or extensive).
- **Daily Skincare Habits** (`habits`, text): Describe your daily skincare habits (e.g., morning and evening routines, products used).
- **How Often Do You Think About the Skin Barrier?** (`concerns_frequency`, select_one): Choose how often you think about the skin barrier (e.g., frequently, occasionally, rarely).
- **How Often Do You Read or Hear About Skin Barrier-Related Topics?** (`awareness_frequency`, select_one): Select how often you read or hear about skin barrier-related topics (e.g., frequently, occasionally, rarely).
- **Education Level** (`education_level`, select_one): Select your level of education about skin barrier health (e.g., basic, average, advanced, or extensive).
- **How Important is Skin Health to You?** (`skin_health_importance`, select_one): Rate how important skin health is to you (e.g., very, somewhat, not very, or not at all).
- **Where Do You Usually Get Information About Skin Health?** (`survey_source`, select_multiple): Choose where you usually get information about skin health (e.g., social media, friends, family, or online articles).
- **Which Type of Skincare Products Do You Use?** (`product_interest`, select_multiple): Select the types of skincare products you use (e.g., moisturizers, face cream, face masks, soaps, serums, or sunscreens).
- **How Often Do You Apply Skincare Products?** (`skincare_frequency`, select_one): Indicate how often you apply skincare products (e.g., daily, frequently, occasionally, or rarely).
- **How Important is Skin Health in Your Purchasing Decisions?** (`skin_health_influence`, select_one): Choose how important skin health influences your purchasing decisions (e.g., very, somewhat, not very, or not at all).
- **Which Factors Influence Your Purchasing Decisions?** (`influence_source`, select_multiple): Select the factors that influence your purchasing decisions (e.g., product ingredients, brand, price, or reviews).
- **How Often Do You Make Skincare Purchases?** (`purchase_frequency`, select_one): Indicate how often you make skincare purchases (e.g., frequently, occasionally, or rarely).
- **Where Do You Usually Make Skincare Purchases?** (`purchase_channel`, select_multiple): Choose where you usually make skincare purchases (e.g., online, offline, social media, or friends).
- **What Skincare Products Do You Prefer?** (`product_preferences`, select_multiple): Select the types of skincare products you prefer (e.g., natural, organic, or vegan products).
- **How Do You Usually Get Skincare Products?** (`product_availability`, select_multiple): Choose how you usually obtain skincare products (e.g., online, offline, social media, or friends).
- **How Often Do You Use Skincare Products?** (`product_frequency`, select_one): Indicate how often you use skincare products (e.g., daily, frequently, occasionally, or rarely).
- **Do You Have a Skincare Expert?** (`skincare_expert`, select_one): Indicate if you have a skincare expert (e.g., true or false).
- **What Are Your Skincare Goals?** (`skincare_goals`, select_multiple): Select your skincare goals (e.g., improve skin health, reduce acne, or improve skin tone).
- **How Often Do You Work Towards Your Skincare Goals?** (`skincare_frequency_goals`, select_one): Indicate how often you work towards your skincare goals (e.g., frequently, occasionally, or rarely).
- **How Important is Achieving Skincare Goals to You?** (`skincare_importance`, select_one): Rate how important achieving skincare goals is to you (e.g., very, somewhat, not very, or not at all).
- **How Important are Skincare Concerns to You?** (`skin_health_concerns_importance`, select_one): Rate how important skin-related concerns are to you (e.g., very, somewhat, not very, or not at all).
- **How Do You Usually Get Skincare Products?** (`product_availability`, select_multiple): Choose how you usually obtain skincare products (e.g., online, offline, social media, or friends).
- **How Often Do You Use Skincare Products?** (`product_frequency`, select_one): Indicate how often you use skincare products (e.g., daily, frequently, occasionally, or rarely).
- **Are You Satisfied with this Survey?** (`survey_satisfaction`, select_one): Rate your satisfaction with this survey (e.g., very, somewhat, not very, or not at all).

## Tips

- This survey aims to gather information about your skin health and preferences. Your input will help us improve our services.
