# provider_satisfaction_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "provider_satisfaction_survey" form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with the service provider. This feedback will help the provider to improve its services and better meet the needs of its customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer the questions honestly, and provide as much detail as possible. This will help the provider to get a better understanding of your experience.
2. Start with the questions on "page 1" and work your way through each page.
3. For each question, choose the best answer that describes your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **what_do_you_like (provider_satisfaction_survey_page2)** (`text`, optional): This field is where you can tell us about what you liked about your experience with the provider. Be specific and provide as much detail as possible.
* **what_do_you_dont_like (provider_satisfaction_survey_page2)** (`text`, optional): This field is where you can tell us about what you didn't like about your experience with the provider. Again, please be specific and provide as much detail as possible.
* **what_improvement (provider_satisfaction_survey_page3)** (`text`, optional): If you have any suggestions for improvement, please let us know in this field. This will help the provider to identify areas where it can improve its services.
* **would_you_recommend (provider_satisfaction_survey_page3)** (`select_one`, optional): Please select one of the options that best describes your willingness to recommend the provider to others.
	+ Option 1: Yes, I would definitely recommend the provider.
	+ Option 2: Yes, I would recommend the provider.
	+ Option 3: No, I do not recommend the provider.
	+ Option 4: Maybe, it depends on the context.
	+ Option 5: I'm not sure.
* **would_you_recommend (provider_satisfaction_survey_page3)** (`select_multiple`, optional): Please select all the options that best describe why you would or wouldn't recommend the provider to others.
	+ Option 1: The service provider's staff was friendly and helpful.
	+ Option 2: The service provider's service was of high quality.
	+ Option 3: The service provider's response time was timely.
	+ Option 4: The service provider's communication was good.
	+ Option 5: Other (please specify in the "additional_comments" field)
* **additional_comments (provider_satisfaction_survey_page4)** (`note`, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in this field. This is an optional field, and you can skip it if you don't have anything to add.
* **email (provider_satisfaction_survey_page4)** (`email`, optional): Please enter your email address so that we can contact you if we need more information from you.
* **phone (provider_satisfaction_survey_page4)** (`text`, optional): If you have a phone number that you'd like to share with us, please enter it here. This field is optional and only for users who would like to be contacted directly.
