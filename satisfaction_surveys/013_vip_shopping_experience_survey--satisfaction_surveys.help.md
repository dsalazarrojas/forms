# VIP Shopping Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to collect feedback from customers about their experience at our store. Your input will help us improve our services and products to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer name.
2. Enter the name of the store where you made your purchase.
3. Enter your contact number.
4. Enter the address of the store where you made your purchase.
5. Answer the questions about your experience with the product, staff, and store environment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **VIP Shopping Experience Survey** (`survey_header`, `note`, not required): A brief introduction to the survey.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, not required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Store Name** (`store_name`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the store where you made your purchase.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `number`, not required): Enter your phone number to contact you for follow-up questions.
* **Store Address** (`store_address`, `text`, not required): Enter the address of the store where you made your purchase.
* **Rating Stars** (`rating_star`, `select_multiple`, not required): Rate your experience with our store:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
* **Rating Value** (`rating_star_value`, `number`, not required): Rate your experience with our store out of 10.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the product you purchased.
* **Product Price** (`product_price`, `number`, not required): Enter the price of the product you purchased.
* **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`, `date`, not required): Enter the date you made your purchase.
* **Time Spent** (`time_spent`, `time`, not required): How much time you spent shopping at our store.
* **Visit Frequency** (`visit_frequency`, `select_one`, not required): How often you visit our store:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Customer Service** (`customer_service`, `select_one`, not required): Rate our customer service:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Product Quality** (`product_quality`, `select_one`, not required): Rate the quality of the product:
	+ Good
	+ Poor
* **Sales Person** (`sales_person`, `select_one`, not required): Rate the sales person who helped you:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Store Environment** (`store_environment`, `select_one`, not required): Rate the store environment:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Purchase Experience** (`purchase_experience`, `select_one`, not required): Rate your purchase experience:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, not required): Rate your overall satisfaction:
	+ Satisfied
	+ Dissatisfied
* **Will Recommend** (`will_recommend`, `select_one`, not required): Would you recommend our store to a friend or family member:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Will Return** (`will_return`, `select_one`, not required): Will you return to our store:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Will Buy** (`will_buy`, `select_one`, not required): Would you buy from our store again:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Will Share** (`will_share`, `select_one`, not required): Would you share your experience with friends or social media:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Store Name Suggestion** (`store_name_suggestion`, `text`, not required): Suggest a name for our store.
* **Customer Comments** (`customer_comments`, `note`, not required): Share any additional comments about your experience.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, not required): Enter your email address for follow-up questions.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Customer Comments" field.
* If you would like to be contacted for follow-up questions, please enter your contact number.
