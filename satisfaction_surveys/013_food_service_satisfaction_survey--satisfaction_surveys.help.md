# food_service_satisfaction_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the quality and overall satisfaction of a food service experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and select the option that best describes your experience.
2. Enter your Overall Rating of your experience.
3. Select the options that apply to your experience for Food Service Quality, Food Price, Food Portion, Food Appearance, and Service Friendliness.
4. Choose your level of satisfaction with Overall Satisfaction and Food Service Satisfaction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Overall Rating**: Enter a numerical value between 1 and 5 that represents your overall rating of the experience.
- **Food Service Quality**: Select the options that apply:
	+ **Poor**: The service was poor, and the quality was not up to par.
	+ **Average**: The service was average, and the quality was decent but not exceptional.
	+ **Good**: The service was good, and the quality was satisfactory.
	+ **Excellent**: The service was excellent, and the quality was exceptional.
	+ **Not Applicable**: This option is only relevant if you did not experience the service.
- **Food Price**: Select all options that apply:
	+ **Overpriced**: The price was higher than expected.
	+ **Average**: The price was reasonable.
	+ **Underpriced**: The price was lower than expected.
	+ **Not Applicable**: This option is only relevant if you did not experience the service.
- **Food Portion**: Select the option that best describes your experience:
	+ **Too Small**: The portion was smaller than expected.
	+ **Average**: The portion was satisfactory.
	+ **Too Large**: The portion was larger than expected.
	+ **Not Applicable**: This option is only relevant if you did not experience the service.
- **Food Appearance**: Select all options that apply:
	+ **Not Attractive**: The appearance was not appealing.
	+ **Average**: The appearance was decent but not exceptional.
	+ **Attractive**: The appearance was appealing.
	+ **Not Applicable**: This option is only relevant if you did not experience the service.
- **Service Friendliness**: Select all options that apply:
	+ **Not Friendly**: The service was not friendly.
	+ **Average**: The service was average.
	+ **Friendly**: The service was friendly.
	+ **Not Applicable**: This option is only relevant if you did not experience the service.
- **Overall Satisfaction**: Choose the option that best describes your level of satisfaction:
	+ **Not Satisfied**: You were not satisfied with your experience.
	+ **Somewhat Satisfied**: You were somewhat satisfied with your experience.
	+ **Satisfied**: You were satisfied with your experience.
	+ **Very Satisfied**: You were very satisfied with your experience.
	+ **Extremely Satisfied**: You were extremely satisfied with your experience.
- **Food Service Satisfaction**: Choose the option that best describes your level of satisfaction:
	+ **Not Satisfied**: You were not satisfied with the food service.
	+ **Somewhat Satisfied**: You were somewhat satisfied with the food service.
	+ **Satisfied**: You were satisfied with the food service.
	+ **Very Satisfied**: You were very satisfied with the food service.
	+ **Extremely Satisfied**: You were extremely satisfied with the food service.

## Tips
* Please answer each field honestly to provide accurate and helpful feedback.
* If you did not experience a particular aspect of the service, select the "Not Applicable" option.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please feel free to add them in the space provided.
