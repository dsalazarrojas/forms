# Cubing Performance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect data on your speedcubing performance. This information will be used to evaluate the overall skill level of our cubing community and identify areas for improvement.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1.  Choose your speedcubing skill level from the dropdown menu:
	* Beginner
	* Advanced
	* Expert
	* Novice
2.  If you have participated in a speedcubing event, please provide the event name and type.
3.  Enter the date and time of the event.
4.  Enter your score or time achieved in the event.
5.  Share any comments or suggestions you have about the event or our cubing platform.
6.  Rate your overall satisfaction with the event (on a scale of 1-5).
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your speedcubing skill level?** (`speedcubing_event`): This field determines your speedcubing skill level, from Beginner to Expert.
* **Event** (`speedcubing_event`): If you've participated in a speedcubing event, please enter the name of the event.
* **Event Type** (`speedcubing_event_type`): If you've participated in a speedcubing event, please select the type of event (e.g., 3x3, 3x4, 3x5, 3x6, 3x7, 4x4).
* **Date** (`speedcubing_date`): Enter the date of the speedcubing event.
* **Time** (`speedcubing_time`): Enter the time of the speedcubing event.
* **Score** (`speedcubing_score`): Enter your score or time achieved in the event.
* **Comments** (`speedcubing_comments`): Share any comments or feedback about the event or our cubing platform.
* **Suggestions** (`speedcubing_suggestions`): Share any suggestions or ideas for improvements on our platform.
* **Rating** (`speedcubing_rating`): Rate your overall satisfaction with the event (on a scale of 1-5).
## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* Make sure to provide any additional details that might help us improve our platform.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
