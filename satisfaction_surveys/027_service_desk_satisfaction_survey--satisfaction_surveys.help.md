# Service Desk Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Desk Satisfaction Survey is a form used to gather feedback from customers on their overall satisfaction with the service desk experience. Completing this form will provide valuable insights to the team to improve their service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer each question to the best of your ability. If you need help with any question, please contact the form administrator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, Number, Required: No): Rate your overall satisfaction with the service desk experience. Use the scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **Service Desk Performance** (`service_desk_performance`, Select Multiple, Required: No): Indicate how often you have received satisfactory service from the service desk team. Choose all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Communication Quality** (`communication_quality`, Select One, Required: No): Rate the quality of communication from the service desk team. Choose one of the following options:
	+ Good
	+ Poor
* **Service Quality** (`service_quality`, Select Multiple, Required: No): Indicate how often you have received services that met your needs. Choose all that apply:
	+ Good
	+ Poor
* **Problem Resolution** (`problem_resolution`, Select One, Required: No): Indicate whether the service desk team was able to resolve your problems. Choose one of the following options:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Communication Effectiveness** (`communication_effectiveness`, Select Multiple, Required: No): Indicate how effective the communication from the service desk team was. Choose all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, Text, Required: No): Provide any additional feedback on your experience with the service desk team.
* **Email Notification** (`email_notification`, Text, Required: No): If you would like to receive email notifications from the service desk team, enter your email address here.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, Select One, Required: No): If you would like to receive phone calls from the service desk team, choose your phone number from the list.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required: No): Enter your email address if you would like to receive email updates from the service desk team.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, Select Multiple, Required: No): Indicate how you would like to be contacted by the service desk team. Choose all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, Text, Required: No): If you would like to provide your contact name, enter it here.
* **Contact Title** (`contact_title`, Text, Required: No): If you would like to provide your contact title, enter it here.
* **Contact Department** (`contact_department`, Text, Required: No): If you would like to provide your department name, enter it here.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, Text, Required: No): If you would like to provide your phone number, enter it here.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, Text, Required: No): If you would like to provide your email address, enter it here.

## Tips
Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability. If you have any issues or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact the form administrator.
