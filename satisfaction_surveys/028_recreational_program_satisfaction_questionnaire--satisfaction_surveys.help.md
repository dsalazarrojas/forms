# ประกcreational Program Satisfaction Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Recreational Program Satisfaction Questionnaire" is a form designed to collect feedback on a recreational program. It aims to gauge the participant's satisfaction level and their likelihood of recommending the program to others.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by reading through the form and understanding each question.
2. For each question, provide honest and thoughtful responses.
3. If a question is not applicable to you, feel free to indicate this by selecting "N/A" or "Not applicable" in the relevant field.
4. Take as much time as you need to complete the form, and don't hesitate to ask for help if you need it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`1`, text, optional): Please start by reading the introduction and providing a brief explanation of your understanding of the program. This is a good opportunity to provide context or background information about your experience with the program.
* **program_name** (`2`, text, optional): This question asks for the name of the program you participated in. Try to be specific and accurate when providing the name.
* **program_location** (`3`, text, optional): Please provide the location where the program took place. This could be a specific venue, building, or even online platform.
* **satisfaction_level** (`4`, select_one, optional): How would you rate your overall satisfaction with the program? Choose from the following options:
	+ Very Good
	+ Somewhat Good
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Bad
	+ Very Bad
* **recommendation_level** (`5`, select_one, optional): How likely are you to recommend the program to others? Choose from the following options:
	+ Very Likely
	+ Somewhat Likely
	+ Unsure
	+ Somewhat Unlikely
	+ Very Unlikely
* **suggestions** (`6`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions or feedback for the program, please provide them here. Be specific and clear in your suggestions.
* **comments** (`7`, text, optional): This is a field for any additional comments or thoughts you may have about the program. Feel free to provide as much detail as you need.
* **contact_information** (`8`, email, optional): If you would like to be contacted for further discussion or follow-up, please provide your email address here. We will not share your contact information with anyone without your consent.
* **contact_phone** (`9`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted for further discussion or follow-up, please provide your phone number here. We will not share your contact information with anyone without your consent.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate in your responses.
* Take your time to complete the form carefully.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask for help.
