# International Student Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The International Student Satisfaction Survey is designed to collect feedback from international students on their overall experience at our institution. This form helps us understand how well we are meeting your needs and expectations, and identify areas where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely.
2. Be honest and provide feedback on your experience.
3. If you have any additional comments or concerns, please use the "Additional Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your first name as it is written on your student ID or passport.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your last name as it is written on your student ID or passport.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Please enter your phone number.
* **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, select_one, required: false): Please choose one of the following options to describe your current satisfaction level:
	+ Very Dissatisfied
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Very Satisfied
* **How would you describe your current satisfaction level?** (`satisfaction_level_hint`, text, required: false): Please provide more information on your satisfaction level (optional).
* **Additional Feedback** (`feedback`, note, required: false): Please share any additional comments or concerns you have.
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, required: false): Please enter the date of your last visit.
* **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, time, required: false): Please enter the time of your last visit.
* **Category** (`category`, text, required: false): Please enter the category of your visit (e.g. student life, academic, etc.).
* **Subcategory** (`subcategory`, text, required: false): Please enter the subcategory of your visit (e.g. language skills, cultural activities, etc.).
* **Question 1** (`question_1`, select_one, required: false): Please choose one of the following options:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
	+ Option 4
	+ Option 5
* **How would you describe this?** (`question_1_hint`, text, required: false): Please provide more information on your choice (optional).
* **Question 2** (`question_2`, select_multiple, required: false): Please choose one or more of the following options:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
	+ Option 4
	+ Option 5
* **How would you describe this?** (`question_2_hint`, text, required: false): Please provide more information on your choice (optional).
