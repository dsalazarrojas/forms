# Sports Facility User Satisfaction Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their overall satisfaction with a sports facility. The feedback will be used to help the facility's management identify areas of improvement and better serve the users.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question honestly, and provide detailed explanations where possible.
2. You may skip questions if they do not apply to your experience.
3. If you have any comments or suggestions, please use the "sports facility user satisfaction comment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sports Facility User Satisfaction Feedback** (`sports_facility_user_satisfaction_feedback`, text, optional): This is the main question where you can express your thoughts and opinions about the sports facility.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, optional): This field asks you to rate your overall satisfaction with the sports facility on a scale of 0 to 10. Please be honest and provide a number that best represents your experience.
* **Sports Facility User Satisfaction Score** (`sports_facility_user_satisfaction_score`, number, optional): This field is similar to "Overall Satisfaction" and asks you to rate your satisfaction with the sports facility on a scale of 0 to 10.
* **Sports Facility User Satisfaction Comment** (`sports_facility_user_satisfaction_comment`, text, optional): This field is for providing any additional comments or suggestions you might have about the sports facility.

## Tips
* Please remember that your feedback is valuable to us and will help us improve the sports facility.
* If you have any concerns or complaints, please address them in the "sports facility user satisfaction comment" field.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
