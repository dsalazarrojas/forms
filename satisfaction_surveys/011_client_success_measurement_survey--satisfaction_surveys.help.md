# Client Success Measurement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Success Measurement Survey is designed to measure the client's success in partnership with our organization. It aims to understand their satisfaction, engagement, and relationship with our services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please provide the required information, marked as "required" on the form. This includes your name, email address, account manager's name, engagement level, relationship health, value delivered, intended ROI, quality rating, timeliness rating, responsiveness rating, and overall satisfaction.
2. Answer each question honestly, and provide specific examples or details if possible.
3. Use the scale provided for each question to select the most appropriate response.
4. If you have any areas for improvement, please provide specific feedback in the text box.
5. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`Client Name`, `text`, required): Please enter your company's name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, required): Please enter your company's email address.
* **Account Manager** (`Your Account Manager`, `text`, optional): If you have an account manager assigned to you, please enter their name.
* **Overall Engagement Level with our Services** (`select_one`, required): Please select the level of engagement you have with our services (Very Low, Low, Moderate, High, Very High).
* **Health of our Business Relationship** (`select_one`, required): Please select the health of our business relationship (At Risk, Concerning, Stable, Strong, Excellent).
* **Frequency of Communication with Your Team** (`select_one`, optional): Please select how frequently you communicate with our team (Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, As needed).
* **Value Delivered by our Services** (`select_one`, required): Please select the value delivered by our services (Well Below Expectations, Below Expectations, Meets Expectations, Exceeds Expectations, Far Exceeds Expectations).
* **Have you Achieved your Intended ROI?** (`select_one`, required): Please select whether you have achieved your intended return on investment (No, Partially, Mostly, Completely).
* **What Success Metrics Matter Most to You?** (`text`, optional): Please enter specific metrics that matter most to you (Profitability, Efficiency, Growth, etc.).
* **Quality of Deliverables** (`number`, required): Please rate the quality of our deliverables on a scale of 1-10 (1=Poor, 10=Excellent).
* **Timeliness of Deliverables** (`number`, required): Please rate the timeliness of our deliverables on a scale of 1-10 (1=Poor, 10=Excellent).
* **Responsiveness and Support** (`number`, required): Please rate the responsiveness and support of our team on a scale of 1-10 (1=Poor, 10=Excellent).
* **Overall Satisfaction with Our Partnership** (`select_one`, required): Please select your overall satisfaction with our partnership (Very Dissatisfied, Dissatisfied, Neutral, Satisfied, Very Satisfied).
* **Likelihood to Continue This Partnership** (`select_one`, required): Please select how likely you are to continue this partnership (Very Unlikely, Unlikely, Neutral, Likely, Very Likely).
* **Areas for Improvement** (`text`, optional): Please provide specific feedback for areas of improvement.
* **Additional Services or Opportunities** (`text`, optional): Please suggest additional services or opportunities that you think would be beneficial.

Note: Please answer each question honestly, and provide specific examples or details if possible.
