# Airline Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects feedback from passengers about their experiences with our airline, covering aspects such as booking process, in-flight experience, and overall satisfaction. Your responses will help us identify areas of improvement and enhance our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name, if you wish to be identified as a passenger (optional).
2. Provide your email address, which will be used to validate your responses (optional).
3. Enter the flight number you traveled with.
4. Select the date of your travel.
5. Choose the cabin class you traveled in (e.g., Economy, Premium Economy, Business, or First Class).
6. Rate your experience with the booking process, check-in, and boarding process.
7. Rate your experience with the in-flight services, such as the friendliness and professionalism of the crew, quality of food and beverages, comfort of seating, and cleanliness of the aircraft.
8. Provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Passenger Name**: Enter your name, if you wish to be identified as a passenger (optional). This helps us understand who completed the survey.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address, which will be used to validate your responses (optional). This ensures that your feedback is associated with your email address for tracking purposes.
* **Flight Number**: Enter the flight number you traveled with. This helps us understand which flight your experience refers to.
* **Date of Travel**: Select the date of your travel. This helps us understand the period of your journey.
* **Cabin Class**: Choose the cabin class you traveled in, such as Economy, Premium Economy, Business, or First Class. This helps us understand your seating and service experience.
* **Ease of Booking Process**: Rate your experience with the booking process, with 1 being Difficult and 5 being Easy. This helps us understand how user-friendly our booking process is.
* **Check-in and Boarding Experience**: Rate your experience with check-in and boarding, with 1 being Poor and 5 being Excellent. This helps us understand how smooth our check-in and boarding processes are.
* **Friendliness and Professionalism of Crew**: Rate the friendliness and professionalism of the crew, with 1 being Poor and 5 being Excellent. This helps us understand the quality of our staff.
* **Quality of Food and Beverages**: Rate the quality of food and beverages served on board, with 1 being Poor and 5 being Excellent. This helps us understand the taste and variety of our in-flight offerings.
* **Comfort of Seating**: Rate the comfort of your seating, with 1 being Poor and 5 being Excellent. This helps us understand the comfort level of our aircraft.
* **Cleanliness of the Aircraft**: Rate the cleanliness of the aircraft, with 1 being Poor and 5 being Excellent. This helps us understand the hygiene standards of our aircraft.
* **Quality of In-flight Entertainment**: Rate the quality of in-flight entertainment, with 1 being Poor and 5 being Excellent. (Note: This field is optional, as not all flights may have in-flight entertainment).
* **Efficiency of Baggage Delivery**: Rate the efficiency of baggage delivery, with 1 being Poor and 5 being Excellent. (Note: This field is optional, as not all flights may have baggage delivery).
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the airline, with 1 being Poor and 5 being Excellent. This helps us understand your overall experience.
* **Net Promoter Score**: Rate how likely you are to recommend this airline, with 0 being Not at all likely and 10 being Extremely likely. This helps us understand our brand reputation.
* **Additional Feedback**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience. This helps us understand specific aspects of your journey that may not be captured in the previous questions.
