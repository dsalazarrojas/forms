# Client Retention Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Retention Effectiveness Survey is designed to gather your feedback on your experience with our company, helping us understand how we're doing in areas such as service quality, relationship strength, and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information in sections **Respondent Information** and **Respondent Details**.
2. Answer all required questions in the **Relationship Overview**, **Factors Contributing to Retention**, **Service Quality and Performance**, **Relationship Strength**, and **Value and ROI** sections.
3. Answer as many of the optional questions as you feel comfortable with, and provide any additional feedback or suggestions in the **Improvement Suggestions** and **Risk Factors** sections.
4. Review your answers for completeness and accuracy, and submit the form when ready.
