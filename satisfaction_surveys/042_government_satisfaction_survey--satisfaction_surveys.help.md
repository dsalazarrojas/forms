# Government Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect user feedback and satisfaction data about the government's services. By providing your input, you will be contributing to the improvement of government services and helping us better understand your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your title (Government Satisfaction Survey).
2.  Choose the category that best describes your experience (goals, public service, or government programs).
3.  Provide a brief description of what you think we do well.
4.  Enter your phone number (optional).
5.  Enter your email address (optional).
6.  Select the provinces where you have had experiences with the government (multiple selection allowed).
7.  Choose the government programs you have interacted with (multiple selection allowed).
8.  Enter the date of your experience (optional).
9.  Enter the time of your experience (optional).
10. If needed, provide additional comments or notes about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your title** (`government_satisfaction_survey_title`, text, optional): Enter a short description of yourself or the topic of your submission.
* **Category** (`government_satisfaction_survey_category`, select_one, optional): Choose the category that best describes your experience (goals, public service, or government programs).
* **What do you think we do well** (`government_satisfaction_survey_description`, note, optional): Provide a brief description of what you think the government does well.
* **Phone** (`government_satisfaction_survey_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Email** (`government_satisfaction_survey_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address (if applicable).
* **Province** (`government_satisfaction_survey_province`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the provinces where you have had experiences with the government.
* **Programs** (`government_satisfaction_survey_program`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the government programs you have interacted with.
* **Date** (`government_satisfaction_survey_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your experience (if applicable).
* **Time** (`government_satisfaction_survey_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your experience (if applicable).
* **Note** (`government_satisfaction_survey_note`, note, optional): Provide additional comments or notes about your experience.
