# resident_satisfaction_survey_1_3_beds - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from residents on their satisfaction with their living experience in a 1-3 bed property. This information will be used to identify areas of improvement and ensure that residents are satisfied with their living conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Make sure to provide clear and concise answers.
3. Use the options provided to select your response (e.g., "Yes", "No", etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. property_management (Page 1)
* **property_management** (`select_one`, required: false): This question asks about your experience with property management. Please select "Yes" if you are satisfied with the management of your property, and "No" if you are not.

### 2. leasing (Page 2)
* **leasing** (`select_multiple`, required: false): This question asks about your experience with leasing your property. Please select all the options that apply to you:
	+ "Yes" if you are satisfied with the leasing process
	+ "No" if you are not

### 3. communication (Page 3)
* **communication** (`text`, required: false): This question asks for any comments or concerns you have about communication with the property management or staff. Please provide your thoughts in this text field.

### 4. overall_experience (Page 4)
* **overall_experience** (`number`, required: false): This question asks for your overall satisfaction score out of 10. Please rate your experience on a scale from 1 to 10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.

### 5. property_conditions (Page 5)
* **property_conditions** (`number`, required: false): This question asks about the condition of your property. Please rate your satisfaction out of 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
