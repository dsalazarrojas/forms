# Policyholder Satisfaction Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from policyholders about their satisfaction with our services. Please take a few minutes to answer the questions below to help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Choose the best option that reflects your honest opinion.
3. Provide additional comments if you have any other feedback or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a brief explanation of the form's purpose and instructions.
* **Satisfaction with service quality** (`Satisfaction_with_service_quality`, Select One): How would you rate your satisfaction with our service quality?
	+ Choose one of the provided options (Satisfied, Dissatisfied).
* **Satisfaction with customer communication** (`Satisfaction_with_customer_communication`, Select One): How would you rate your satisfaction with our customer communication?
	+ Choose one of the provided options (Very good, Good, Neutral, Poor, Very poor).
* **Satisfaction with product features** (`Satisfaction_with_product_features`, Select Multiple): How would you rate your satisfaction with our product features?
	+ Choose one or multiple options (Very good, Good, Neutral, Poor, Very poor).
* **Satisfaction with policyholder service** (`Satisfaction_with_policyholder_service`, Select Multiple): How would you rate your satisfaction with our policyholder service?
	+ Choose one or multiple options (Very good, Good, Neutral, Poor, Very poor).
* **Overall satisfaction** (`Overall_satisfaction`, Select Multiple): How would you rate your overall satisfaction?
	+ Choose one or multiple options (Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied).
* **Feedback and comments** (`Feedback_and_comments`, Text): Please provide any additional comments or feedback you have.
* **Additional comments** (`Additional_comments`, Text): Please provide any additional comments or feedback you have.
* **Expected resolution time** (`Expected_resolution`, Select One): How long did you expect your issue to be resolved?
	+ Choose one of the provided options (Yes, No).
* **Actual resolution time** (`Actual_resolution`, Select One): How long did your issue take to be resolved?
	+ Choose one of the provided options (Yes, No).
* **Contact Information** (`Contact_Information`, Select Multiple): How did you want to be contacted?
	+ Choose one or multiple options (Phone, Email, Mail, None).

## Tips
* Be honest and honest in your answers.
* Please provide specific examples if possible.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
