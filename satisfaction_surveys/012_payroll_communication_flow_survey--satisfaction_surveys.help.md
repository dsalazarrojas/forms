# Payroll Communication Flow Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather feedback from employees on the communication flow of the payroll process. It will help management understand how often employees receive information on payroll channels, the clarity of the information, and areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
Please complete this form by selecting the options that best fit your experiences with our payroll communication flow. For multiple-choice questions, select all that apply. For text responses, provide your ideas and suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. How often do you get information on payroll channels?
 * **How often do you get information on payroll channels?** (`payroll_communication_channels`, select_one, Not required):
   + Please select the frequency at which you typically receive information on payroll channels. You can choose from: Very frequently, Somewhat frequently, Rarely, Never.

### 2. How clear is our payroll information?
 * **How clear is our payroll information?** (`payroll_clarity`, select_one, Not required):
   + Please select the level of clarity of the information you typically receive on payroll. You can choose from: Very clear, Somewhat clear, Somewhat unclear, Very unclear.

### 3. What is your satisfaction with our current payroll process?
 * **What is your satisfaction with our current payroll process?** (`satisfaction_with_payroll`, select_one, Not required):
   + Please select your level of satisfaction with our current payroll process. You can choose from: Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Somewhat dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied.

### 4. Do you have any ideas for improving our payroll process?
 * **Do you have any ideas for improving our payroll process?** (`payroll_improvement_ideas`, text, Not required):
   + If you have any suggestions or ideas to improve our payroll process, enter them here.

### 5. What are your preferred channels for payroll information?
 * **What are your preferred channels for payroll information?** (`communication_channels`, select_multiple, Not required):
   + Please select all the channels you prefer to receive information on payroll, such as Email, Phone, Mobile app, Team huddle, or Other (please specify).

### 6. Do you have any suggestions for improving the clarity of our payroll information?
 * **Do you have any suggestions for improving the clarity of our payroll information?** (`payroll_info_clarity_ideas`, text, Not required):
   + If you have any ideas or suggestions to improve the clarity of our payroll information, enter them here.

### 7. What is your satisfaction with our current payroll process?
 * **What is your satisfaction with our current payroll process?** (`payroll_process_satisfaction`, select_one, Not required):
   + Please select your level of satisfaction with our current payroll process. You can choose from: Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Somewhat dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied.

### 8. How would you rate your satisfaction with our current payroll channels?
 * **How would you rate your satisfaction with our current payroll channels?** (`payroll_info_channels_satisfaction`, select_one, Not required):
   + Please select your level of satisfaction with our current payroll channels. You can choose from: Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Somewhat dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied.

### 9. Do you have any ideas for improving our payroll process?
 * **Do you have any ideas for improving our payroll process?** (`payroll_process_improvement_ideas`, text, Not required):
   + If you have any suggestions or ideas to improve our payroll process, enter them here.

### 10. Do you think our current communication flow is effective?
 * **Do you think our current communication flow is effective?** (`communication_flow`, select_one, Not required):
   + Please select whether you think our current communication flow is effective or not. You can choose: True, False.

### 11. How would you prefer to get information about payroll changes?
 * **How would you prefer to get information about payroll changes?** (`email`, select_multiple, Not required):
   + Please select all the channels you prefer to receive information about payroll changes, such as Email, Phone, Mobile app, Team huddle, or Other (please specify).

### 12. How often would you like to get information about pay periods?
 * **How often would you like to get information about pay periods?** (`pay_period`, select_one, Not required):
   + Please select the frequency at which you would like to receive information about pay periods. You can choose from: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Other (please specify).
