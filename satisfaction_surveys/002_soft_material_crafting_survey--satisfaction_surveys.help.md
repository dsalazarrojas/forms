# Soft Material Crafting Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and data about your experience with soft materials in your projects. Your responses will help us understand your needs and preferences to improve the quality and availability of soft materials.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question truthfully to the best of your ability.
2. If you're unsure about any question, please ask your supervisor or team lead for guidance.
3. Ensure that you complete the form in one sitting, as saving and resuming may cause inconsistencies in data collection.
4. Use the provided fields and options to answer the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What type of fabric do you use?** (`fabric_type`, `select_one`, optional): Please choose the type of fabric that you used for this project. This could be something like wool, cotton, or synthetic.
* **How much fabric do you use for this project?** (`fabric_weight`, `number`, optional): This question is asking how much fabric you used for your project. Please provide a numerical value, e.g., 1 meter, 2 yards, etc.
* **What type of project is this for?** (`project_type`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the type of project this is for, such as home decor, clothing, or accessories.
* **How satisfied are you with the material?** (`satisfaction_level`, `select_multiple`, optional): Rate your satisfaction level with the material. You can select one or more of the following options: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Not Satisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`, optional): If you have any further thoughts or comments about the material, please share them here.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Please provide your work email address, so we can contact you for more information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Please provide your work phone number, so we can contact you for more information.
* **Project name** (`project_name`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to share the name of your project, please do so here.
* **Project description** (`project_description`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief description of your project, if you'd like.

Note: This form is for your feedback and suggestions, so please be as detailed and honest as possible. Your input is valuable to us, and we appreciate your time and effort in completing this form.
