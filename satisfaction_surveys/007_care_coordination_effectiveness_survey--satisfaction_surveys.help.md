# Care Coordination Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to evaluate the effectiveness of care coordination services. It is meant to be completed by individuals who have received care coordination services, such as patients, family members, caregivers, and healthcare providers. The survey aims to gather feedback on various aspects of care coordination, including communication between providers, appointment scheduling, medication management, and patient education. This information will help us identify areas of strength and opportunities for improvement in our care coordination programs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer each question based on your personal experience with care coordination services.
2. For questions that ask you to select an option from a list, choose the one that best describes your experience.
3. For questions that ask for additional feedback, you can type your thoughts in the provided space.
4. Be as specific as possible when answering each question, and try to provide concrete examples or situations that illustrate your point.
5. If you have any trouble navigating the form, do not hesitate to ask for help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, text, optional): This is the first question, asking for your name. You can provide your name if you wish, but it is not required.
* **Your Role** (`respondent_role`, select_one, required): This question asks about your role in the care coordination process. Please select the option that best describes your position or relationship to the care coordination services you received.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, optional): If you are affiliated with an organization, you can provide their name here. This is not required.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, required): Please select the date you completed this survey.
* **How Long Have You Been Receiving Coordinated Care** (`care_duration`, select_one, required): This question asks about the length of time you have been receiving care coordination services. Choose the option that reflects your experience.
* **How Would You Rate Communication Between Care Providers** (`communication_rating`, select_one, required): This question assesses your satisfaction with communication between care providers. Select the option that best describes your experience.
* **How Well Is Health Information Shared Among Your Providers** (`information_sharing`, select_one, required): This question asks about the quality of health information sharing among your providers. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
* **How Satisfied Are You With Appointment Scheduling Coordination** (`appointment_coordination`, select_one, required): This question evaluates your satisfaction with appointment scheduling coordination. Select the option that best reflects your experience.
* **How Well Are Your Medications Coordinated Across Providers** (`medication_management`, select_one, required): This question assesses your satisfaction with medication management across providers. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
* **How Clear Is Your Care Plan to You** (`care_plan_clarity`, select_one, required): This question asks about the clarity of your care plan. Select the option that best describes your understanding.
* **How Easy Is It to Access Needed Services** (`access_to_services`, select_one, required): This question evaluates your ease of access to needed services. Choose the option that best reflects your experience.
* **How Would You Rate Wait Times for Appointments** (`wait_times`, select_one, required): This question asks about your satisfaction with wait times for appointments. Select the option that best describes your experience.
* **How Smooth Are Transitions Between Care Settings** (`care_transitions`, select_one, required): This question assesses your experience with transitions between care settings, such as hospital to home or specialist to primary care provider. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
* **How Involved Do You Feel in Care Decisions** (`patient_involvement`, select_one, required): This question asks about your involvement in care decisions. Select the option that best describes your experience.
* **Are You Aware of Available Support Services** (`support_services_awareness`, select_one, required): This question evaluates your awareness of available support services. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
* **Have You Used Any Support Services** (`support_services_utilization`, select_multiple, required): Select all the support services you have used, if any.
* **Overall Satisfaction With Care Coordination** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): This question asks about your overall satisfaction with care coordination. Select the option that best describes your experience.
* **Have You Noticed Improvement in Your Health Outcomes** (`health_outcomes`, select_one, required): This question assesses your experience with improvement in health outcomes since receiving care coordination services. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
* **How Likely Are You to Recommend This Care Coordination Program** (`recommend_to_others`, select_one, required): This question asks about your likelihood of recommending this care coordination program. Select the option that best describes your experience.
* **What Is the Biggest Strength of the Care Coordination** (`biggest_strength`, select_one, required): This question asks about the biggest strength of the care coordination services. Select the option that best describes your experience.
* **What Areas Need the Most Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, select_multiple, required): Select all the areas that need improvement, if any.
* **Additional Comments or Suggestions** (`additional_feedback`, text, optional): If you have any additional thoughts or suggestions, please type them in this space.

## Tips

* Please answer honestly and based on your personal experience with care coordination services.
* If you have any difficulty navigating the form, do not hesitate to ask for help.
* Your feedback is valuable in helping us improve our care coordination services. Thank you for participating!
