# Sports Event Catering Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from sports event catering clients about their satisfaction with the quality of food, beverages, and service provided during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and choose the option that best represents your experience.
2. Provide feedback for each section based on your genuine experience.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Food Quality** (food_quality, select_one, required):
	+ Please select the option that best describes your experience with the food quality at the sports event.
* **Beverages Quality** (beverages_quality, select_one, required):
	+ Please select the option that best describes your experience with the beverages quality at the sports event.
* **Service Quality** (service_quality, select_one, required):
	+ Please select the option that best describes your experience with the service quality at the sports event.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (overall_satisfaction, select_multiple, required):
	+ Please select all options that best describe your overall satisfaction with the sports event catering service.
* **Comments** (comments, text, optional):
	+ If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them here.
* **Contact Information** (email, email, required):
	+ Please provide your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone Number** (phone_number, select_one, required):
	+ Please select the option that best represents your phone number type.
	+ Note: Please ensure that your phone number is correct and formatted correctly for our records.
