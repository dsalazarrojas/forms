# Organizational Trust And Accountability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Organizational Trust And Accountability Survey is designed to gather information about how employees perceive their workplace environment, specifically regarding trust, transparency, and support. This survey is meant to be completed by employees or team members to provide insights into the organizational culture and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Start" button to begin the survey.
2. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
3. Fill in the relevant fields with your thoughts and opinions.
4. Review your answers before submitting the survey.
5. Click on the "Submit" button to complete the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information (1)**: This is the first page of the survey where you can provide any general information about your role or position within the organization.
* **What is trust? (3)**: Please provide a brief description of what trust means to you in the context of your workplace.
* **How do you perceive the level of transparency in your organization? (4)**: Share your thoughts on how transparent you perceive your organization to be.
* **How do you perceive the level of accountability? (6)**: Describe your perception of accountability within your organization.
* **How often do you feel free to share your ideas and opinions? (7)**: Indicate how frequently you feel comfortable sharing your thoughts with your colleagues or superiors.
* **Do you feel supported in your role? (9)**: Share your thoughts on whether you feel supported in your position within the organization.

## Tips
* Take your time to answer each question carefully.
* Be honest in your responses.
* If you're unsure about a question, feel free to provide a brief explanation.
* You can use the "Submit" button to review your answers before finalizing the survey.
* Remember, this survey is designed to gather information to help improve the organizational culture, so your input is valuable!
