# Sports Event Parking Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey aims to gather feedback from participants about their experiences with parking at sports events. The survey assesses the accessibility, convenience, and overall satisfaction of the parking services provided.

## How To Complete This Form

### Introduction
Please review the instructions for this survey before starting. This survey has 25 questions, and you will be able to complete it in a few minutes. You can skip questions that do not apply to you or your experience.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`intro_page`, note, not required): This is a brief introduction to the survey. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **How easy was it to access the parking?** (`parking_accessibility`, select_one, not required): Rate the ease of accessing the parking on a scale of 5 (very easy) to 1 (not easy at all).
* **How did you access the parking?** (`parking_accessibility_how_did`, select_multiple, not required): Choose one or more of the options to describe how you accessed the parking (e.g., walking, taxi, Uber/Lyft, private vehicle, other).
* **How long did it take for you to find a parking spot?** (`parking_accessibility_time`, select_one, not required): Rate the time it took to find a parking spot on a scale of less than 2 minutes to more than 10 minutes.
* **How friendly was the parking staff?** (`parking_friendly`, select_one, not required): Rate the friendliness of the parking staff on a scale of extremely friendly to not at all friendly.
* **Did you encounter any parking staff during your visit?** (`parking_friendly_staff`, select_one, not required): Answer yes or no if you met parking staff during your visit.
* **If yes, what was their attitude towards you?** (`parking_friendly_staff_comments`, text, not required): Describe their attitude towards you if they were friendly or unfriendly.
* **Did the parking staff help you with parking?** (`parking_friendly_staff_help`, select_one, not required): Answer yes or no if they helped or did not help with parking.
* **Did the parking staff provide you with any parking tips?** (`parking_friendly_staff_tips`, select_one, not required): Answer yes or no if they provided tips or not.
* **Did they provide you with clear directions?** (`parking_friendly_staff_directions`, select_one, not required): Answer yes or no if they provided clear directions or not.
* **How convenient was parking?** (`parking_convenience`, select_one, not required): Rate the convenience of parking on a scale of very convenient to not at all convenient.
* **Any additional comments on parking convenience?** (`parking_convenience_comments`, text, not required): Provide any additional comments about parking convenience.
* **How important is parking to you?** (`parking_value`, select_one, not required): Rate the importance of parking on a scale of extremely important to not at all important.
* **How much did you pay for parking?** (`parking_parking_cost`, select_one, not required): Choose the amount you paid for parking on a scale of free to $16 or more.
* **Would you pay to park here again?** (`parking_value_parkingistency`, select_one, not required): Answer yes or no if you would or would not pay to park again.
* **Additional comments?** (`additional_comments`, text, not required): Provide any additional comments about your experience.
* **How likely are you to complete this survey?** (`parking_survey_completion`, select_one, not required): Rate your likelihood of completing the survey on a scale of extremely likely to not at all likely.
* **Why or why not?** (`parking_survey_completion_comments`, text, not required): Explain why you are likely or unlikely to complete the survey.
* **How long did it take to complete this survey?** (`parking_survey_length`, select_one, not required): Rate the time it took to complete the survey on a scale of less than 1 minute to more than 5 minutes.
* **Why?** (`parking_survey_length_comments`, text, not required): Explain why it took so long or so short to complete the survey.
* **How satisfied are you with the survey?** (`parking_survey_satisfaction`, select_one, not required): Rate your satisfaction with the survey on a scale of very satisfied to very dissatisfied.
* **Why?** (`parking_survey_satisfaction_comments`, text, not required): Explain why you are satisfied or dissatisfied with the survey.
* **Who administered the survey?** (`survey_admin`, select_one, not required): Choose the person who administered the survey (e.g., event staff, volunteers, self-administered).
* **Comments?** (`survey_admin_comments`, text, not required): Provide any comments about the survey administrator.
* **Were you satisfied with the survey administrator's support?** (`survey_admin_support`, select_one, not required): Rate your satisfaction with the survey administrator's support on a scale of extremely satisfied to not at all satisfied.
* **Why or why not?** (`survey_admin_support_comments`, text, not required): Explain why you are satisfied or dissatisfied with the survey administrator's support.
