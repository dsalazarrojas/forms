# Wealth Club Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Wealth Club Feedback Survey" is a form designed to gather feedback from users about their experience with the Wealth Club program. This survey aims to understand the level of satisfaction and engagement with the program and provide valuable insights to improve it.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin the survey.
2. Review each page to ensure you have answered all the questions.
3. Enter your answers according to the fields provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. <img src="feedback_form"> 
* **Feedback Form** (`survey_page`, text, required: false): This is the main form where you can provide your feedback about the Wealth Club program. Please be as detailed as possible with your thoughts and opinions.

### 2. 
* **Rating** (`rating_1`, number, required: false): This question asks for your rating of the program. Please rate your experience on a scale.

### 3. 
* **How often do you participate in our meetings?** (`rating_2`, number, required: false): This question asks about your frequency of participation in Wealth Club meetings. Please rate your participation on a scale.

### 4. 
* **Have you recommended this program to a friend or colleague?** (`rating_3`, select_one, required: false):
	+ **Yes**: You have recommended the program to someone.
	+ **No**: You have not recommended the program.
	+ **Maybe**: You might recommend the program in the future.

### 5. 
* **What suggestions do you have for improvement?** (`improvement_suggestion`, text, required: false): This is an open-ended question where you can provide any suggestions you have for improving the Wealth Club program.

### 6. 
* **Intent to Refer** (`referral_intent`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ **High**: You are very likely to refer someone.
	+ **Medium**: You might refer someone in the future.
	+ **Low**: You are not likely to refer someone.

### 7. 
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false): This is a text area where you can provide any additional comments or thoughts about the Wealth Club program.
