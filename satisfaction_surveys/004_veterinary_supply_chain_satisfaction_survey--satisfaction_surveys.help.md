# Veterinary Supply Chain Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Supply Chain Satisfaction Survey is used to gather information about the satisfaction of veterinary suppliers. It aims to collect feedback from users about their experiences with these suppliers, which will be used to identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant answers from the available options to provide your feedback on the following:
	* Supplier
	* Availability
	* Delivery
	* Quality
	* Service
	* Rating
2. Optionally, enter your Email address to provide a point of contact if your comments are requested.
3. Optionally, provide comments about your experience with the supplier.
4. Finally, enter your phone number to ensure we can reach you if your comments are requested.
5. Click submit to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier** (select_one, required/optional): This field allows you to select whether the supplier is 'Yes' or 'No'. This will indicate whether you have had a positive experience with the supplier.
* **Availability** (number, required/optional): This field asks you to rate the supplier's availability on a scale from 1 to 10. A rating of 1 means the supplier was not available, while a rating of 10 means they were very available.
* **Delivery** (select_multiple, required/optional): This field allows you to select whether you received the delivery from the supplier. This will help us understand whether the delivery was successful or not.
* **Quality** (select_one, required/optional): This field asks you to rate the quality of the supplier's products or services. You can select between 'Good' or 'Poor'.
* **Service** (number, required/optional): This field asks you to rate the service provided by the supplier. This will help us understand the level of service you received.
* **Rating** (select_one, required/optional): This field asks you to rate the overall experience with the supplier. You can select between 'Good', 'Fair', or 'Poor'.
* **Comments** (text, required/optional): This field allows you to provide any additional comments about your experience with the supplier.
* **Email** (email, required/optional): If you want to be contacted for further discussion about your feedback, enter your email address here.
* **Phone** (text, required/optional): This field is for you to enter your phone number if you want to be contacted for further discussion about your feedback.
