# Workplace Feedback Channel Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from employees through multiple feedback channels. The survey aims to gather constructive input on how employees experience the workplace and identify areas for improvement. The form ensures that the feedback is captured accurately and efficiently, allowing for informed decision-making and process optimization.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the first field. This is the first step in providing your feedback.
2. Select your **job_title**, which is the title you hold in the workplace.
3. Enter your **department**, which will help us understand the context of your feedback.
4. Choose your preferred **Feedback Channel** from the options available (Email, Manager, HR, Team Leader). This determines where your feedback will be sent.
5. Provide your **Feedback** in the text field. This is the core of your input, where you can describe your thoughts, ideas, and concerns.
6. If you have any specific **Suggestions**, you can type them in the dedicated field. This is an optional field, but it's an opportunity to provide actionable recommendations.
7. Enter your **Email Address** if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or discussions.
8. If needed, provide your **Phone Number** for easy contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Required): Your name as it appears on your name tag or ID badge.
* **job_title** (Optional): The title or role you hold in the organization.
* **department** (Optional): The team or section where you work.
* **Feedback Channel** (Optional): Select where you'd like your feedback sent (Email, Manager, HR, Team Leader).
* **Feedback** (Required): Describe your thoughts, concerns, and ideas. Be as detailed as you can.
* **Suggestions** (Optional): If you have specific recommendations, type them here.
* **Email Address** (Optional): Enter your official email address for follow-up communication.
* **Phone Number** (Optional): Your phone number for easier contact.

## Tips

* Be honest and open when providing feedback.
* Keep your suggestions concise and actionable.
* If you're unsure about a specific field, feel free to ask your HR or management for guidance.
* Feedback is an essential tool for creating a better workplace. Your input matters, and it's valued.
