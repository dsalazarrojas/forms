# Donation Channel Trustworthiness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your perception of the trustworthiness of different donation channels. Your input will help us identify areas where we can improve to increase transparency and reliability in our donation channels.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the options that best match your perception of the trustworthiness of each donation channel. Please choose the option that most closely aligns with your feelings.
2. Fill in the submission form if you have any additional thoughts or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Very Likely/Somehow Likely/Not Very Likely/Not At All
* **Likelihood of Trustworthiness**: How likely do you think it is that [donation channel] is trustworthy? Choose one of the options below:
	+ Very likely: I think [donation channel] is extremely trustworthy.
	+ Somewhat likely: I think [donation channel] is somewhat trustworthy.
	+ Not very likely: I think [donation channel] is not very trustworthy.
	+ Not at all: I do not think [donation channel] is trustworthy at all.

### Very Reliable/Not Very Reliable/Not Reliable At All/Not Reliable
* **Reliability of [donation channel]**: How reliable do you think [donation channel] is? Choose one of the options below:
	+ Very reliable: I think [donation channel] is extremely reliable.
	+ Somewhat reliable: I think [donation channel] is somewhat reliable.
	+ Not reliable at all: I do not think [donation channel] is reliable at all.
	+ Not reliable: I think [donation channel] is not reliable.

### Very Trustworthy/Not Very Trustworthy/Not Trustworthy At All/Not Trustworthy
* **Trustworthiness of [donation channel]**: How trustworthy do you think [donation channel] is? Choose one of the options below:
	+ Very trustworthy: I think [donation channel] is extremely trustworthy.
	+ Somewhat trustworthy: I think [donation channel] is somewhat trustworthy.
	+ Not trustworthy at all: I do not think [donation channel] is trustworthy at all.
	+ Not trustworthy: I think [donation channel] is not trustworthy.

### Submission
* **Additional comments or thoughts**: If you have any additional comments or thoughts about your perception of the trustworthiness of [donation channel], please type them in the space below.

## Tips
* Please be honest and objective when answering the questions.
* Your input will help us improve our donation channels for better transparency and reliability.
