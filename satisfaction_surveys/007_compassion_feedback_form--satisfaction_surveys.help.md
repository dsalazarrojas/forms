# Compassion Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Compassion Feedback Form is a tool used to collect feedback on compassionate behavior from individuals who have experienced or witnessed it within an organization or setting. This form helps to identify areas where compassionate behavior is more likely to happen and provides opportunities for growth and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. The form is divided into pages, each covering a different aspect of compassionate behavior. Please complete each page according to your experience or observation.
2. If you are completing this form anonymously, you can do so without providing your name or email.
3. Select all options that apply from the provided lists.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Compassion Feedback Form** (`feedback_intro`): This page serves as an introduction to the form and provides a brief overview of its purpose.
* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`): Your name is optional, and you can choose not to provide it if you prefer to remain anonymous.
* **Your Email** (`respondent_email`): Your email address is also optional and can be used for follow-up if needed.
* **Type of Organization** (`organization_type`): Please select where you experienced or witnessed compassionate behavior.
* **Date of Experience** (`experience_date`): Enter the date when the compassionate behavior occurred.
* **Did you receive compassionate behavior** (`compassion_received`): Select whether you received compassionate behavior or witnessed it toward others.
* **Source of Compassion** (`compassion_source`): Choose who showed compassion to you or others.
* **Type of Situation** (`situation_type`): Select the context in which compassionate behavior occurred.
* **How would you rate the compassion shown** (`compassion_rating`): Rate the compassion shown to you or others on a scale from 1 (poor) to 10 (exceptional).
* **Compassionate Actions** (`compassion_actions`): Choose all the actions that were shown to you or others.
* **Impact on You** (`impact_on_you`): Select the impact that compassionate behavior had on you.
* **How did it make you feel** (`emotional_response`): Choose how you felt as a result of the compassionate behavior.
* **Are there lasting effects** (`long_term_effect`): Select whether the compassionate behavior has had lasting effects on you.
* **Would you be willing to share your story** (`would_share_story`): Indicate whether you would be willing to share your story for promoting compassion initiatives.
* **Importance of Compassion** (`compassion_importance`): Rate the importance of compassion in the organization or setting.
* **How often do you experience compassion here** (`compassion_frequency`): Select how often you experience compassion in this organization or setting.
* **Suggestions for Fostering Compassion** (`suggestions_for_culture`): Provide any suggestions for creating a kinder culture.
* **Compassion Training Interest** (`training_interest`): Indicate your interest in compassion training programs or workshops.
* **Would you recommend this organization** (`recommend_to_others`): Select whether you would recommend this organization based on its compassionate culture.
* **Please describe your experience** (`story_description`): Share the details of your experience with compassionate behavior.
* **Someone to Acknowledge** (`person_to_acknowledge`): If you'd like to acknowledge someone who showed compassion, please provide their name.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have.

## Tips

* Please be as detailed and honest as possible in your responses.
* You can select all options that apply from the provided lists.
* If you have any additional feedback or comments, please use the "Additional Comments" field.
