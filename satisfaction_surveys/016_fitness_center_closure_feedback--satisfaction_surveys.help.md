# Fitness Center Closure Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about the impact of your fitness center's closure. It aims to help us understand how the closure affected your experience and help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the page corresponding to the closure event you want to provide feedback on.
2. Choose your response for each field, and be as specific as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Closure Impact** (`closure_impact`, select_multiple)
Choose all that apply to how the closure impacted your experience.

*   'Yes' - The closure had a significant impact on your experience.
*   'No' - The closure had little to no impact on your experience.

### **Communication Quality** (`communication_quality`, select_one)
Rate the quality of communication regarding the closure.

*   'poor' - The communication was inadequate or unclear.
*   'average' - The communication was average and left some room for improvement.
*   'good' - The communication was clear and helpful.
*   'excellent' - The communication was excellent and provided all necessary information.

### **Intend to Return** (`intend_to_return`, select_multiple)
Choose all that apply to whether you intend to return to our fitness center.

*   'Yes' - You intend to return to our fitness center.
*   'No' - You do not intend to return to our fitness center.

### **Reopen Decision** (`reopen_decision`, select_one)
Indicate your current stance on reopening.

*   'yes' - You are in favor of reopening.
*   'no' - You are against reopening.
*   'unsure' - You are unsure about the reopening decision.

### **Tips**
*   Be as specific as possible when answering each field.
*   If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the comments section.
