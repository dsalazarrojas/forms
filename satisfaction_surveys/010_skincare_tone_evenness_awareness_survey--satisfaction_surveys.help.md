# Skincare Tone Evenness Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skincare Tone Evenness Awareness Survey is designed to help users understand their skin tone evenness concerns and preferences. This survey gathers information about users' current skin tone concerns, skincare habits, and awareness of skincare products. The survey's goal is to identify areas of improvement for users and provide personalized recommendations for achieving even skin tone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page of the survey carefully.
2. Select the relevant options for each field.
3. Answer each question based on your personal experiences and preferences.
4. Don't hesitate to select "None" or "Not Applicable" if you don't have any concerns or habits related to the question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction**: This is a note field for you to read and understand the survey's purpose and instructions.
- **Skin Tone Evenness**: Select all the tone evenness issues that you are concerned about, such as Red, Yellow, or Green.
- **Current Tone Concerns**: Choose the option that best describes your current tone concerns. You can select more than one if you have multiple concerns.
- **Current Tone Concerns Why**: Provide a brief explanation of your current tone concerns. This will help us understand your concerns better.
- **Skincare Routine**: Select all the times you apply skincare products, such as Morning, Evening, or Both.
- **Skincare Products**: Choose all the types of products you use, such as Moisturizer, Serum, Mask, Exfoliator, or Sunscreen.
- **Skincare Frequency**: Select how frequently you apply skincare products.
- **Skincare Frequency Other**: If you have any other skincare frequency habit, provide a brief explanation.
- **Awareness Awareness**: Choose how aware you are of even skin tone.
- **Awareness Why**: Provide a brief explanation of why you are (or not) aware of even skin tone.
- **Concerns Concerns**: Select all the skin concerns that you have, such as Hyper-pigmentation, Aging, Dark Circles, or Pores.
- **Concerns Concerns Other**: If you have any other skin concerns, provide a brief explanation.
- **Education**: Choose all the sources where you learn about skincare, such as Online, Print, Social Media, or Word of Mouth.
- **Education Sources**: Select all the channels where you learn about skincare, such as YouTube, Google, Instagram, Facebook, or Twitter.
- **Education Sources Other**: If you have any other sources where you learn about skincare, provide a brief explanation.
- **Products**: Choose all the types of skincare products you use, such as Moisturizer, Serum, Mask, Exfoliator, or Sunscreen.
- **Products Other**: If you have any other skincare product, provide a brief explanation.
- **Routine**: Select all the times you apply skincare products.
- **Routine Other**: If you have any other skincare routine, provide a brief explanation.
- **Products Importance**: Choose how important you think skincare products are.
- **Products Importance Why**: Provide a brief explanation of why you think skincare products are (or not) important.
- **Products Importance Other**: If you have any other reason for the importance of skincare products, provide a brief explanation.
- **Recommendations**: Provide any specific recommendations you have for skincare products or routines.
- **Recommendations Other**: If you have any other recommendations, provide a brief explanation.
- **Next Steps**: Provide any next steps you plan to take to improve your skin tone.
- **Next Steps Other**: If you have any other next steps, provide a brief explanation.
