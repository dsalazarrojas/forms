# Skincare Fragrance Free Preference Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your skin type, skincare preferences, and concerns. The purpose of this form is to better understand your skin-related preferences and concerns to improve product offerings and recommendations. Please answer the questions honestly and accurately to help us create a more personalized experience for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the options that best describe your skin type and concerns.
2. Answer the questions with the information that best suits your skin preferences.
3. Submit your answers to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **skincare_preferences** (`skincare_preferences`, select_one, required: False): 
This field is used to determine how you feel about skincare products. Please select True if you are interested in skincare products, False if you are not interested, and Undecided if you are neutral.
* **skin_sensitivity** (`skin_sensitivity`, select_multiple, required: False): 
This field is used to determine your skin sensitivity level. Please select all that apply from the following options: Fragrance-free, Slightly sensitive, Somewhat sensitive, Very sensitive, Severely sensitive.
* **skin_sensitivity_2** (`skin_sensitivity_2`, text, required: True): 
This field is used to describe any sensitivities that are not listed in the previous question.
