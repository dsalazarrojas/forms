# Guest Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from guests about their experience at a recent event. The data collected will help us understand how to improve future events and provide better experiences for our guests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields as follows:

1. Go to the first page and select the "Event Details" field. Here, you can type any relevant information about the event you attended.
2. On the second page, select the "Overall Experience" field and rate your overall experience using the provided number.
3. On the third page, select the "Satisfaction" field and choose one of the three options to rate your satisfaction with the event.
4. On the fourth page, type any additional comments you'd like to make about your experience.
5. If you'd like to be contacted about your feedback, provide your contact email address, name, and phone number on the following pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (Event Details, text, required/optional): Please describe the event you attended. This field is optional, but providing more information will help us better understand your experience.
* **Overall Experience** (Overall Experience, number, required/optional): Rate your overall experience using the provided number, with higher numbers indicating a more positive experience.
* **Satisfaction** (Satisfaction, select_one, required/optional): Choose one of the three options to rate your level of satisfaction with the event.
* **Comments** (Comments, text, required/optional): Share any additional thoughts or comments about your experience.
* **Contact Info** (Contact Info, email, required/optional): If you'd like to be contacted about your feedback, provide your email address.
* **Contact Name** (Contact Name, text, required/optional): If you'd like to be contacted, please provide your name.
* **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone, text, required/optional): If you'd like to be contacted, provide your phone number.
* **Event Date** (Event Date, date, required/optional): This field will be used to track the date of the event you attended. 

## Tips
- Please provide as much detail as possible in the "Event Details" field to help us better understand your experience.
- If you'd like to provide feedback, but are not comfortable sharing your contact information, you can skip those fields.
- Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
