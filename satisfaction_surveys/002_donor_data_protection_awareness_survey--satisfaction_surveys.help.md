# Donor Data Protection Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from donors about their knowledge and attitudes towards data protection and collection practices within our organization. Your input will help us understand where we stand and make informed decisions to better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button at the end of the form to start.
2. Complete all required fields (marked as "required" in the form) to ensure we receive your feedback.
3. Enter your First name, Last name, and Email to help us identify you as a donor.
4. Provide your contact number for any follow-up communication.
5. Indicate your level of awareness about data protection and collection practices within our organization.
6. Select the options that best describe your understanding of our data collection practices.
7. Submit the form by clicking on the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): If you would like to provide your address for communication purposes, please enter it here.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to allow us to keep you updated on our activities and events.
* **Contact number** (`contact_number`, `text`, optional): If you have a contact number where we can reach you, please enter it here.
* **Data protection** (`data_protection`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your level of awareness about data protection within our organization. Please select "Data Protection" if you are aware, "Non-Data Protection" if you are not, or "Not sure" if you need more information.
* **Data collection** (`data_collection`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the options that best describe our data collection practices. Please select "Data Collection" if we collect your data for the purpose of, "No Data Collection" if we do not, or "Both" if we collect data for multiple purposes.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required): Click this button to submit your survey once you have completed all required fields.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and accurately to help us make informed decisions.
* If you have any concerns or questions about data protection and collection, please contact us.
* We appreciate your participation in this survey and look forward to serving you better in the future.
