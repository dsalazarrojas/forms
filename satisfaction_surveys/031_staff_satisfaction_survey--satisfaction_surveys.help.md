# Staff Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The purpose of this survey is to gather feedback from staff members about their experience at the company, including their overall satisfaction, management team, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

* To complete this form, simply answer each question based on your genuine experience and feelings about the company.
* Please be as specific as possible when providing your feedback.
* Remember that all answers are confidential and anonymous, so feel free to share your honest opinions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Staff Satisfaction Survey** (`1`, `Staff Satisfaction Survey`, text, required: false): This is the title of the survey.
* **What do you think about our company culture?** (`2`, text, required: true): Share your thoughts about the company culture, including what you like and dislike about it.
* **What do you think about our management team?** (`3`, select_one, required: true): Choose how you feel about our management team, with options ranging from Excellent to Bad.
* **What are some things we are doing well?** (`4`, text, required: false): Provide examples of things that the company is doing well.
* **What are some things we are doing poorly?** (`5`, text, required: false): Share examples of areas that need improvement.
* **What do you think could we do differently?** (`6`, text, required: false): Suggest ways to improve the company's practices and policies.
* **Is there anything you would like to add to this survey?** (`7`, text, required: false): If you have any additional thoughts or suggestions, share them here.
* **What do you think your manager could do for you?** (`15`, text, required: false): Share your expectations from your manager.
* **What do you think your HR could do for you?** (`16`, text, required: false): Share your expectations from HR.
* **What would you like your manager or HR to do for you?** (`17`, text, required: false): If you have any requests or suggestions for your manager or HR, share them here.
* **Is there anything else you would like to say?** (`25`, text, required: false): If you have any additional thoughts or comments, share them here.
* **What is your role at this company?** (`9`, select_one, required: true): Choose your role at the company, with options Admin, Manager, Team Member, or Other.
* **What is your role in this department?** (`10`, select_one, required: true): Choose your role in the department, with options Admin, Manager, Team Member, or Other.
* **What department do you work in?** (`11`, text, required: false): Share the name of the department you work in.
* **How many years have you been at this company?** (`12`, number, required: false): Share the number of years you have been at the company.
* **How many years have you been in your current role?** (`13`, number, required: false): Share the number of years you have been in your current role.
* **Would you like to be recognized for your contributions?** (`14`, select_one, required: true): Choose whether you would like to be recognized for your contributions, with options True or False.
* **Would you like to be contacted about this feedback?** (`19`, select_one, required: true): Choose whether you would like to be contacted about this feedback, with options True or False.
* **What is the best way to contact you?** (`20`, email, required: true): Share your preferred email address or phone number to contact you.
* **Do you have any questions or concerns?** (`21`, text, required: false): If you have any questions or concerns, share them here.
* **Is there anything we can do to improve your work experience?** (`22`, select_multiple, required: true): Choose from the options to share areas for improvement.
* **Is there anything else you would like to share?** (`23`, text, required: false): If you have any additional thoughts or comments, share them here.
* **Do you feel valued and appreciated at this company?** (`24`, select_one, required: true): Choose whether you feel valued and appreciated, with options True or False.
* **Is there anything we have not thought of?** (`18`, text, required: false): If you have any additional thoughts or suggestions, share them here.
