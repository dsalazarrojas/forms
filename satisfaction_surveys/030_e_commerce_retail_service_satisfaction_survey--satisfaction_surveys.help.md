# e_commerce_retail_service_satisfaction_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and satisfaction data from customers about their e-commerce retail service experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields below and click submit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Customer E Mail** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Please enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Customer Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Please enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Shipping Address** (`contact_shipping_address`, `text`, required): Please enter the address where you would like to have any shipments delivered.

## Tips
- Be as accurate as possible when filling out the fields to ensure that your feedback is properly recorded.
- Make sure to double-check your contact information for accuracy before submitting the form.
