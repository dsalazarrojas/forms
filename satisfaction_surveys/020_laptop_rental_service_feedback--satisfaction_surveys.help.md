# Laptop Rental Service Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Laptop Rental Service Feedback form is designed to gather user feedback on their experience with the laptop rental service. This form is intended for users who have rented a laptop from the service and have an opinion or experience to share.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have rented a laptop from the service before proceeding.
2. Navigate through each page, where you will find fields to fill out.
3. For fields with a scale of 1-10, select a number that best represents your opinion on the importance of that aspect.
4. For fields with multiple options, select all that apply.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Laptop Condition
* **Laptop Condition** (`laptop_condition`, text, required: false): Please describe the condition of the laptop you rented. This is an open-ended field where you can provide any comments or observations about the laptop's condition.

### Support Quality
* **Support Quality** (`support_quality`, text, required: false): Describe the quality of support you received from our staff. This is an open-ended field where you can provide any comments or feedback about the support you received.

### Overall Rental Experience
* **Overall Rental Experience** (`overall_rental_experience`, text, required: false): Please describe your overall experience with the laptop rental service. This is an open-ended field where you can provide any comments or feedback about your experience.

### Laptop was returned in good working condition
* **Laptop was returned in good working condition** (`laptop_returned_in_good_working_condition`, text, required: false): Please indicate whether the laptop was returned in good working condition. This field should only be filled out if you returned the laptop after renting it.

### Support was helpful
* **Support was helpful** (`support_was_helpful`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all that apply if the support you received was helpful. Options include:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3

### How important was support quality
* **How important was support quality** (`support_quality_important`, number, required: false): Rate the importance of support quality on a scale of 1-10. A higher number indicates that support quality was very important to you.

### How important was laptop condition
* **How important was laptop condition** (`laptop_condition_important`, number, required: false): Rate the importance of laptop condition on a scale of 1-10. A higher number indicates that laptop condition was very important to you.

### How important was overall rental experience
* **How important was overall rental experience** (`overall_rental_experience_important`, number, required: false): Rate the importance of overall rental experience on a scale of 1-10. A higher number indicates that overall rental experience was very important to you.

### Do you have any suggestion for our service?
* **Do you have any suggestion for our service?** (`support_suggestion`, text, required: false): Provide any suggestions or feedback you have for our service. This is an open-ended field where you can provide any comments or ideas you may have.

### Do you have any comments about our service?
* **Do you have any comments about our service?** (`support_suggestion_comment`, text, required: false): Provide any comments or feedback about our service. This is an open-ended field where you can provide any comments or feedback you may have.
