# National Security Expert Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from security industry experts. It aims to gather data on how security experts operate and interact with their customers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select your primary role in the security industry**:
	* Choose the option that best describes your main responsibility.
	* If none of the options match your role, select "Other" and provide a brief explanation.
2. **How often do you have contact with customers?**:
	* Enter a brief description of your contact frequency (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
3. **Email**:
	* Enter your work email address in the format "example@domain.com".
4. **Phone**:
	* Enter your work phone number in the format "123-123-1234".
5. **Date**:
	* Enter the date you are filling out this form in the format "2022-07-25".
6. **Time**:
	* Enter the time you are filling out this form in the format "0900".
7. **Best way to contact you**:
	* Select one method (Phone, Email, In-Person).
8. **You usually contact customers**:
	* Select one method (Phone, Email, In-Person).
9. **Do you usually contact customers at any time**:
	* Select "Yes" or "No".
10. **Average weekly hours spent with customers**:
	* Enter the average number of hours you spend working with customers per week.
11. **Question 3: How do you usually contact your customers?**:
	* Select one method (Phone, Email, In-Person).
12. **Question 5: On average how many hours do you usually spend with customers per week?**:
	* Enter the average number of hours you spend with customers per week.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a note field to provide any additional information about your role or experience.
* **Select your primary role in the security industry**: This question helps us understand your main area of expertise in the security industry.
* **How often do you have contact with customers?**: This question helps us understand your level of interaction with customers.
* **Email**: This is your work email address.
* **Phone**: This is your work phone number.
* **Date**: This is the date you are filling out this form.
* **Time**: This is the time you are filling out this form.
* **Best way to contact you**: This question helps us understand your preferred contact method.
* **You usually contact customers**: This question helps us understand your customer interaction habits.
* **Do you usually contact customers at any time**: This question helps us understand your customer interaction schedule.
* **Average weekly hours spent with customers**: This question helps us understand the average time you spend with customers per week.

## Tips

* Please ensure you fill in all required fields.
* You can skip questions that do not apply to your role or experience.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us.
