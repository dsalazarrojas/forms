# Financial Statement Clarity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gauge the clarity of financial statements provided to team leaders and finance team members. The survey is used to gather feedback on the quality of financial information presented, allowing for improvement and refinement in the financial reporting process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant team leaders and finance team members from the provided options.
2. Rate the clarity of the financial statement.
3. Provide feedback comments if you have any suggestions or concerns.
4. Select the best option for the financial institution and the financial team members.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Finance Statement** (`finance_statement`, `number`, required: false): This field is used to rate the clarity of the financial statement. Select a value from 1 to 5 to indicate how clear you find the financial statement.
- **Team Leaders** (`team_leaders`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all team leaders who have reviewed the financial statement.
- **Feedback Comments** (`feedback_comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any comments or suggestions you may have regarding the financial statement.
- **Financial Statement Rating** (`rating`, `number`, required: false): This field is used to rate the clarity of the financial statement. Select a value from 1 to 5 to indicate how clear you find the financial statement.
- **Prophets** (`prophets`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the best option for this field.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes regarding the financial statement.
- **Financial Institution** (`financial_institution`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the financial institution that provided the financial statement.
- **Finance Team Members** (`finance_team_members`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the finance team member who reviewed the financial statement.

Note: Fields marked as required: false can be filled in or left blank at your discretion.
