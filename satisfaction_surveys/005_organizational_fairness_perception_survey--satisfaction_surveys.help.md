# Organizational Fairness Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Organizational Fairness Perception Survey is designed to gather feedback on various aspects of your experience within the organization. Your honest responses will help us understand areas where we excel and where we need to improve to promote fairness and equity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and select the option that best reflects your perception of fairness in the corresponding area.
2. For questions that allow multiple selections, choose all applicable options.
3. Be honest and sincere in your responses, as your feedback is crucial for our improvement efforts.
4. Complete all sections, as every response is valuable to us.
