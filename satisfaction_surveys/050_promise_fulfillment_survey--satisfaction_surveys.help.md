# Promise Fulfillment Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gauge your satisfaction with an organization's performance in fulfilling their promises. It consists of three main questions that ask about the organization's communication, responsiveness, and overall satisfaction. By answering these questions, you'll be able to provide valuable feedback to help the organization improve its promise-fulfillment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction text, which provides some background information about the survey.
2. Answer the first question about how you feel about the organization's communication with you. Select one of the options (Very positive, Somewhat positive, Neutral, Somewhat negative, or Very negative).
3. Answer the second question about how you'd rate the organization's responsiveness. Select one of the options (Very responsive, Somewhat responsive, Neutral, Somewhat unresponsive, or Very unresponsive).
4. Answer the third question about your overall satisfaction with the organization. Select one of the options (Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, or Very dissatisfied).
5. Once you've answered all questions, click the "Submit" button to submit your feedback.
