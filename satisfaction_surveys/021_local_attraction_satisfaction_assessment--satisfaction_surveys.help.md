# Local Attraction Satisfaction Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Local Attraction Satisfaction Assessment form is used to collect feedback from visitors about their experience at a local attraction. This form helps us understand what visitors like or dislike about the attraction, which informs our efforts to improve the visitor experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly about your recent visit to the local attraction.
2. Rate the tour rating, staff interaction, and staff friendliness using numbers.
3. Select the attraction impression and overall experience using the dropdown menu.
4. Provide additional comments about your experience, if any.
5. Enter your contact information (email and phone number) if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or discussions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tour Rating** (`tour_rating`, number, required: false): Rate your overall experience with the tour, from 1 to 5.
* **Staff Interaction** (`staff_interaction`, text, required: false): Describe your experience with the staff, such as how friendly and helpful they were.
* **Attraction Impression** (`attraction_impression`, text, required: false): Share your impression of the attraction's quality, such as its cleanliness or entertainment value.
* **Staff Friendliness** (`staff_friendliness`, number, required: false): Rate how friendly and helpful the staff were, from 1 to 5.
* **Attraction Quality** (`attraction_quality`, select_one, options: Poor, Fair, Good, Great, Excellent, required: false): Choose how you would rate the attraction's overall quality, from Poor to Excellent.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, select_multiple, options: Poor, Fair, Good, Great, Excellent, required: false): Select all the ways in which your overall experience was, such as Good, Fair, Great, or Excellent.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, note, required: false): Share any recommendations or suggestions you have for improving the attraction.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false): Share any additional comments or thoughts about your experience.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can follow up with you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can follow up with you.
* **Visit Date** (`visit_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of your visit, if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions.
* **Visit Time** (`visit_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of your visit, if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions.
