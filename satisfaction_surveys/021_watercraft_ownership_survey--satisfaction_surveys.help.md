# This form is used to gather information about watercraft ownership. The purpose of this form is to gather data on various aspects of watercraft ownership, such as usage frequency, insurance coverage, and registration status. This information will help us understand the demographics of watercraft owners and provide valuable insights for future decision-making.

The form is designed to be completed by watercraft owners, and it consists of 25 questions that cover different aspects of watercraft ownership. The form includes questions on usage frequency, insurance coverage, boat characteristics, and owner information.

Before starting to fill out the form, please ensure that you have all the necessary information and documents required to complete it accurately and thoroughly.

## Purpose
The Watercraft Ownership Survey is designed to gather information about watercraft owners and their usage habits, insurance coverage, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please answer all the questions in the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2.  If you are unsure about any question, please select the "Not sure" option or provide additional comments if necessary.
3.  Make sure to provide your contact information so we can follow up with you if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Usage Frequency** (`usage_frequency`, `number`, required: false): This question asks about how often you use your watercraft. Please select the number that best describes your usage frequency.
* **Insurance Coverage** (`insurance_coverage`, `select_one`, required: true): This question asks if you have insurance coverage for your watercraft. Please select "True" if you have insurance, "False" if you don't, or "Not sure" if you are unsure.
* **Engine Oil** (`engine_oil`, `number`, required: false): This question is not applicable as it's not a select field in the original form.
* **Maintenance Habits** (`maintenance_habits`, `note`, required: false): This question is for you to provide any additional comments about your maintenance habits.
* **Boat Type** (`boat_type`, `select_multiple`, required: true): This question asks about the type of boat you own. Please select all that apply.
	+ **Sail**
	+ **Power**
	+ **Other** (please specify in additional comments)
* **Boat Age** (`boat_age`, `number`, required: true): This question asks about the age of your boat.
* **Registration** (`registration`, `date`, required: true): This question asks for the registration date of your boat.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, `date`, required: false): This question asks for the date of the survey.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): This question is for you to provide your contact information, so we can follow up with you if needed.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): This question is for you to provide your email address.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): This question is for you to provide your address.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`, required: false): This question is for you to provide any additional comments about your watercraft.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, `select_one`, required: true): This question asks about the insurance provider for your boat. Please select the provider from the list.
* **Boat Maintenance** (`boat_maintenance`, `select_multiple`, required: true): This question asks about the maintenance of your boat. Please select all that apply.
	+ **True**
	+ **False**
	+ **Not Sure**
* **Boat Warranty** (`boat_warranty`, `select_one`, required: true): This question asks about the warranty status of your boat. Please select "True" if you have warranty, "False" if you don't, or "Not sure" if you are unsure.
* **Boat License** (`boat_license`, `select_multiple`, required: true): This question asks about the license status of your boat. Please select all that apply.
	+ **True**
	+ **False**
	+ **Not sure**
* **Boat Registration Status** (`boat_registration_status`, `select_multiple`, required: true): This question asks about the registration status of your boat. Please select all that apply.
	+ **Registered**
	+ **Not registered**
	+ **Unknown**
* **Boat Insurance Status** (`boat_insurance_status`, `select_multiple`, required: true): This question asks about the insurance status of your boat. Please select all that apply.
	+ **Active**
	+ **Inactive**
	+ **Not insured**
* **Boat Safety Equipment** (`boat_safety_equipment`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question is not applicable as it's not a select field in the original form.
* **Boat Must Haves** (`boat_must_haves`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question is for you to list the must-haves for your boat.
* **Boat Worth** (`boat_worth`, `number`, required: false): This question is for you to estimate the worth of your boat.
* **Boat Size** (`boat_size`, `number`, required: true): This question asks about the size of your boat.
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, `text`, required: false): This question is for you to provide your name.
* **Owner Address** (`owner_address`, `text`, required: false): This question is for you to provide your address.
* **Owner Phone** (`owner_phone`, `text`, required: false): This question is for you to provide your phone number.

Please complete the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible, and if you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
