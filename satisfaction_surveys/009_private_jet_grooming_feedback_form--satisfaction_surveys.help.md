# private_jet_grooming_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with our private jet grooming service, to help us improve the cleanliness of our jets and overall service.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please start by filling out the "Cleanliness Ratings" page.
2. On this page, you will find the "Cleanliness Ratings" question, which asks you to rate the cleanliness of our private jet on a scale from 1 to 10. Please select the appropriate rating from the options provided.
3. After answering the cleanliness ratings, please confirm your ratings on the "Confirm Cleanliness Ratings" page.
4. On this page, you will find the same cleanliness ratings question, with the options you selected on the previous page. Please select the same options again to confirm your ratings.
5. Next, please fill out the "Cleanliness Score" page.
6. Here, you will be asked to enter a specific score for the cleanliness of our private jet, on a scale from 1 to 10.
7. Confirm your score on the "Confirm Cleanliness Score" page.
8. After completing the previous steps, please provide your email address on the "Email" page.
9. Enter your name for verification purposes on the "Name" page.
10. Please provide any additional comments or suggestions on the "Additional Comments" page.
11. If you have any final comments or suggestions, please enter them on the "Final Comments" page.
12. Finally, confirm your name and email address on the "Confirm Your Information" page.
13. Once you have completed all the previous steps, click the "Submit" button to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Cleanliness Ratings** (id: 2): Rate the cleanliness of our private jet using the scale provided. This will help us understand how well we have met your expectations.
* **Cleanliness Score** (id: 3): Enter a specific score for the cleanliness of our private jet, ranging from 1 to 10. This will help us understand the exact level of cleanliness you experienced.
* **Email** (id: 4): Enter your email address so we can contact you in case we need more information from you.
* **Name** (id: 6): Enter your name for verification purposes. We need this to confirm that you are the person who submitted the feedback.
* **Confirm Cleanliness Ratings** (id: 7): Confirm the cleanliness ratings you selected on the previous page.
* **Confirm Cleanliness Score** (id: 8): Confirm the score you entered for the cleanliness of our private jet.
* **Additional Comments** (id: 9): Share any additional comments or suggestions you may have about our service.
* **Final Comments** (id: 11): Enter any final comments or suggestions you may have about our service.
* **Submit** (id: 10): Click this button to submit your feedback form.
* **Confirm Your Information** (id: 12): Confirm your name and email address to ensure that we have the correct information for your feedback.
