# Tenant Complaint Resolution Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly.
2. Make sure to provide as much detail as possible in the complaint description field.
3. Select the correct answer for each multiple-choice question.
4. Leave comments in the manager comments and tenant comments fields to provide additional information.
5. Rate your satisfaction with a number (1-5) in the tenant satisfaction rating field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Tenant Complaint Resolution Survey** (`title`, `text`, required: false): This is the main title of the survey, which you are required to fill out.
- **Complaint Description** (`complaint_description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a detailed description of your complaint. This will help us understand the issue better.
- **Complaint Category** (`complaint_category`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the category of your complaint. This will help us group similar complaints together.
- **Complaint Subcategory** (`complaint_subcategory`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the subcategory of your complaint.
- **Resolution Method** (`resolution_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Select how your complaint was resolved.
- **Tenant Satisfaction** (`tenant_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with the resolution of your complaint.
- **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, `text`, required: false): Add any comments you'd like to share with the property management team.
- **Tenant Comments** (`tenant_comments`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional comments you'd like to share.
- **Manager Satisfaction** (`manager_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate the manager's satisfaction with the resolution.
- **Resolution Status** (`resolution_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the status of your complaint resolution.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional comments you'd like to share.
- **Tenant Satisfaction and Comments** (`tenant_complaint_resolution_survey_page_3`, `text`, required: false): This field is for additional comments.
- **Tenant Satisfaction Rating** (`tenant_satisfaction_rating`, `number`, required: false): Rate your satisfaction on a scale of 1-5.
- **Tenant Comment** (`tenant_comment`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional comments.
