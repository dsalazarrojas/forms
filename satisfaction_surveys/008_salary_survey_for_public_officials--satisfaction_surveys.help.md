# Salary Survey For Public Officials - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from public officials on their job satisfaction and performance. Please take a few minutes to complete this form and help us better understand your experiences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. You may select multiple options for questions that allow selection of multiple choices.
3. If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact your supervisor or HR representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your role in the public official position (job title)?**
	+ This is the title of your job, e.g., "Mayor", "City Councilor", etc.
* **What is your annual salary range?** 
	+ Enter your annual salary, e.g., 50,000 to 75,000.
* **Do you have any benefits with your job?**
	+ Select "Yes" if you have any benefits, such as health insurance, pension, or paid time off.
* **Are you satisfied with your current job?**
	+ Select "Yes" if you are generally satisfied with your job, or "No" if you are not.
* **What is your typical work schedule like (hours worked per week)?**
	+ Enter the average number of hours you work per week, e.g., 40.
* **Can you describe your typical day at work?**
	+ Type a brief description of your typical day, e.g., "Attend meetings, review documents, interact with the public".
* **What kind of training have you received for your job?**
	+ Select "Yes" if you have received any training, or "No" if you have not.
* **How many years do you have of experience in this position?**
	+ Enter the number of years you have been in this role.
* **Do you feel your job is secure?**
	+ Select "Yes" if you feel secure in your job, or "No" if you do not.
* **Are you able to balance work and family life?**
	+ Select "Yes" if you are able to balance work and family life, or "No" if you are not.
* **Do you have any job-related stress or anxiety?**
	+ Select "Yes" if you experience any stress or anxiety related to your job, or "No" if you do not.
* **How many weeks do you work per year?**
	+ Enter the number of weeks you work per year.

## Tips

* Please be honest and complete the form to the best of your ability.
* If you have any comments or suggestions, please include them in the "typical day at work" field.
* If you need assistance or have any questions, please contact your supervisor or HR representative.
