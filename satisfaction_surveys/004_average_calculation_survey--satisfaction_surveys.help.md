# Average Calculation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Average Calculation Survey is designed to collect feedback and preferences from respondents. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Choose your respondent type from the list.
2. Select the purpose of this survey.
3. Enter your name (if desired).
4. Enter your email address (if desired).
5. Enter your organization or school name (if desired).
6. Enter up to 10 numeric values.
7. Choose the type of calculation you need.
8. Decide if outliers should be included in the calculation.
9. Choose the decimal precision of the results.
10. Rate your satisfaction with the survey tool.
11. Rate how easy it was to use this tool.
12. Enter any feature requests.
13. Enter any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent Type** (Respondent Type, select_one, required): Choose what best describes you.
* **Survey Purpose** (Survey Purpose, select_one, required): Select the reason for this survey.
* **Your Name** (Respondent Name, text, optional): Enter your name if you wish.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, optional): Enter your email address for receiving results.
* **Organization or School Name** (Organization or School Name, text, optional): Enter your institution's name.
* **Data Point 1** (Data Point 1, number, required): Enter first numeric value.
* **Data Point 2** (Data Point 2, number, required): Enter second numeric value.
* **Data Point 3** (Data Point 3, number, required): Enter third numeric value.
* **Data Point 4** (Data Point 4, number, optional): Enter fourth numeric value.
* **Data Point 5** (Data Point 5, number, optional): Enter fifth numeric value.
* **Data Point 6** (Data Point 6, number, optional): Enter sixth numeric value.
* **Data Point 7** (Data Point 7, number, optional): Enter seventh numeric value.
* **Data Point 8** (Data Point 8, number, optional): Enter eighth numeric value.
* **Data Point 9** (Data Point 9, number, optional): Enter ninth numeric value.
* **Data Point 10** (Data Point 10, number, optional): Enter tenth numeric value.
* **Calculation Type Needed** (Calculation Type Needed, select_one, required): Choose the type of average calculation.
* **Include Outliers** (Include Outliers in Calculation, select_one, required): Decide if outliers should be included.
* **Decimal Precision** (Decimal Precision, select_one, required): Choose the decimal precision of the results.
* **Satisfaction with Survey Tool** (Satisfaction with Survey Tool, select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction with this tool.
* **Ease of Use Rating** (Ease of Use Rating, select_one, required): Rate how easy it was to use this tool.
* **Feature Requests** (Feature Requests, text, optional): Enter any feature requests.
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, text, optional): Enter any additional comments.
