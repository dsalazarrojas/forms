# Retail Product Delivery Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with the retail product delivery process. Your input will help us improve and refine our delivery process to better meet your needs.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each field carefully and answer the questions honestly.
2. Please answer all required fields (marked as "required").
3. Select the options that best match your experience.
4. If you have any additional comments or concerns, use the "Comments" field.
5. Once you have completed the form, click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Delivery Experience (1)**: Describe your overall experience with the delivery process. This can include any issues you encountered or pleasant surprises.
* **Product Quality (2)**: Rate the quality of the product you received. Please select one of the available options (1-5).
* **Delivery Time (3)**: Rate how timely your delivery was. Please select one of the available options (1-5).
* **Overall Satisfaction (4)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the product and the delivery process. Select one of the options:
	+ Very dissatisfied
	+ Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat satisfied
	+ Very satisfied
* **Communication (5)**: If you had any communication with our delivery team, describe your experience with their communication style.
* **Packaging (6)**: Rate the condition of the packaging of the product. Select all that apply:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
* **Product Condition (7)**: Rate the condition of the product. Select all that apply:
	+ New
	+ Gently used
	+ Used
* **Order Status (8)**: Rate the status of your order. Select one of the available options:
	+ On time
	+ Delayed
	+ Cancelled
* **Contact Method (9)**: If you had any contact with our delivery team, describe how you reached out to them.
* **Comments (10)**: If you have any additional comments or concerns, please share them here.
* **Email (11)**: If you would like to be contacted by our team, please provide your email address.
* **Phone (12)**: If you would like to be contacted by our team, please provide your phone number.
* **Date Received (13)**: If you have the date you received the product, please enter it in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Delivery Person (14)**: If you interacted with a delivery person, describe their service quality.
* **Delivery Company (15)**: If you had any interaction with the delivery company, describe their service quality.
* **Product Type (16)**: Describe the type of product you received (e.g., clothing, furniture, etc.).
* **Delivery Status (17)**: Rate the status of your delivery. Select one of the available options:
	+ Received
	+ Not received
* **Delivery Note (18)**: If you had any notes or comments about the delivery process, please share them here.
* **Order ID (19)**: If you have an order ID, please provide it here.
* **Customer ID (20)**: If you have a customer ID, please provide it here.
* **Delivery Person Note (21)**: If you interacted with a delivery person, describe their note or comment.
* **Delivery Person Phone (22)**: If you interacted with a delivery person, provide their phone number.
* **Delivery Person Email (23)**: If you interacted with a delivery person, provide their email address.
* **Delivery Person Comment (24)**: If you interacted with a delivery person, describe their service quality.
* **Delivery Person Company (25)**: If you interacted with a delivery person, describe their company or affiliation.
