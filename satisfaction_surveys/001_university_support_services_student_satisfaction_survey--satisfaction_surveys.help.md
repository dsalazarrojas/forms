# University Support Services Student Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather your feedback on the support services provided by the university. Your input will help us understand what we are doing well and where we can improve. Please take a few minutes to complete this survey and share your thoughts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Read through each page carefully.
* Answer each question by selecting one of the options provided.
* You can choose more than one option for select_multiple fields.
* Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction** (Introduction): This is a note section where we briefly explain the purpose of the survey and its importance in helping us improve our services.
- **Campus Support Services** (campus_support_services): This is a select_multiple field where you can choose which services you have used or not used on campus. You can select more than one option.
	+ Yes: You have used this service.
	+ No: You have not used this service.
- **Academic Advising** (academic_advising): This is a select_one field where you can choose one option regarding your experience with academic advising.
	+ Yes: You have had a positive experience with academic advising.
	+ No: You have not had a positive experience with academic advising.
- **Student Services** (student_services): This is a select_multiple field where you can choose which student services you have used or not used. You can select more than one option.
	+ Yes: You have used this service.
	+ No: You have not used this service.
- **Campus Resources** (campus_resources): This is a select_multiple field where you can choose which campus resources you have used or not used. You can select more than one option.
	+ Yes: You have used this resource.
	+ No: You have not used this resource.
