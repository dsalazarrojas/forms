# Life Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Life Satisfaction Survey is a form designed to collect information about your life satisfaction levels. This form is meant to be completed by individuals to provide feedback on their overall satisfaction with their lives. This form is used to track trends and patterns in user satisfaction, which can help identify areas of improvement for services and products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the first field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the second field.
3. Provide your age in the third field.
4. Select your gender in the fourth field. Please choose one of the following options: Male or Female.
5. Enter your phone number in the fifth field (this will help us reach out to you if needed).
6. Enter your email address in the sixth field (this will help us contact you for follow-up questions or surveys).
7. Select your happiness level in the seventh field. Choose one or more of the following options: High, Medium, or Low.
8. Select your satisfaction level in the eighth field. Choose one of the following options: High, Medium, or Low.
9. Select your life satisfaction score in the ninth field. Choose one of the following options: 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it is known to you.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it is known to you.
* **Age** (`age`, number, required/optional): Enter your current age.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required/optional): Select your gender (Male or Female).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Happiness Level** (`happiness_level`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select one or more of the following: High, Medium, or Low.
* **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, select_one, required/optional): Select one of the following: High, Medium, or Low.
* **Life Satisfaction Score** (`life_satisfaction_score`, select_one, required/optional): Select one of the following: 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5.
