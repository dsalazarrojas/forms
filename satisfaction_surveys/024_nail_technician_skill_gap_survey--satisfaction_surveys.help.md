# Nail Technician Skill Gap Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information on the current skills and proficiency level of nail technicians. This information will be used to create targeted training programs and materials to support your development as a nail technician.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the following steps:

1. Answer the questions truthfully, reflecting on your current experience and skills.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately.
3. For select multiple and select one questions, choose the options that best fit your current situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How long have you been practicing nails?** (`1`, `number`, required): Answer this question by selecting a number that represents the length of time you have been practicing nails.
* **Confidence level in performing nail services** (`2`, `number`, required): Rate your confidence level in performing nail services, with higher numbers indicating greater confidence.
* **What is your biggest need to improve your skills?** (`3`, `select_one`, required): Answer this question by selecting the option that best describes your biggest need to improve your skills.
* **What is your level of experience with nail services?** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required): Answer this question by selecting all the options that best describe your level of experience with nail services.
* **What type of training have you had?** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required): Answer this question by selecting all the options that describe the type of training you have had.
* **Confidence level in performing nail services** (`6`, `number`, required): Rate your confidence level in performing nail services, with higher numbers indicating greater confidence.
* **Which nail technique do you most frequently use?** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required): Answer this question by selecting all the options that describe the nail technique you most frequently use.
* **Which nail product do you most frequently use?** (`8`, `select_multiple`, optional): Answer this question by selecting all the options that describe the nail products you most frequently use.
* **Time management for nail technicians?** (`9`, `select_one`, required): Answer this question by selecting the option that best describes your time management skills.
* **Communication skills for nail technicians?** (`10`, `select_one`, optional): Answer this question by selecting the option that best describes your communication skills.
* **Problem solving for nail technicians?** (`11`, `select_one`, required): Answer this question by selecting the option that best describes your problem solving skills.
* **Nail proficiency for nail technicians?** (`12`, `select_multiple`, required): Answer this question by selecting all the options that describe your nail proficiency.
* **Additional nail products** (`13-24`, `select_multiple`, optional): Answer this question by selecting all the options that describe the additional nail products you use.
