# Delivery Conditions Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Delivery Conditions Satisfaction Survey" is designed to collect feedback from customers on their experience with the delivery process. This feedback will help us understand your satisfaction and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the options that best describe your experience with our delivery:
	* Timely Delivery: If the delivery was made at the scheduled time.
	* Late Delivery: If the delivery was made after the scheduled time.
	* No Delivery: If the delivery was not made.
2. Evaluate the condition of the package:
	* Good: If the package was in good condition.
	* Bad: If the package was damaged or not as expected.
3. Rate the professionalism of our staff:
	* Good: If you felt they were helpful and courteous.
	* Bad: If you felt they were not helpful or courteous.
4. Provide your overall satisfaction with the delivery:
	* Enter a numerical value between 0 and 10 to indicate your satisfaction level.
5. If applicable, provide the order details:
	* Order Number: The number assigned to your order.
	* Order Date: The date the order was placed.
	* Order Time: The time the order was placed.
6. If you have any other comments or feedback, please provide them in the "Other Comments" field.

* **Delivery Conditions**: Select the option that best describes your experience with the delivery.
	+ Timely Delivery: The delivery was made at the scheduled time.
	+ Late Delivery: The delivery was made after the scheduled time.
