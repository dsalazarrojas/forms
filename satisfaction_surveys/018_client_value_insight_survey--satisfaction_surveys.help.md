# Client Value Insight Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather insights on the client's perception of the value they receive from our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the required fields (marked as "true" in the form).
2. For optional questions, select the options that best apply to your experience with our services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: Please enter the name of the client company.
* **Email Address**: Please enter the email address of the client company.
* **Company Name**: Please enter the name of your company.
* **Contact Person**: Please enter the name of the contact person for the client company.
* **Perceived Value of our Products/Services**: Select the level of value you perceive our products/services provide (e.g., Poor value, Above average).
* **Value justifies the cost**: Select the level of agreement with the statement that the value we provide justifies the cost (e.g., Strongly Disagree, Strongly Agree).
* **Business impact from our services**: Select the level of business impact you have experienced from our services (e.g., Negative impact, Transformational impact).
* **Most valuable benefits received**: Select all the benefits you have received from our services (e.g., Increased revenue, Cost savings).
* **We have met your expectations**: Select the level of agreement with the statement that we have met your expectations (e.g., Well Below, Far Exceeded).
* **What needs are we not addressing?**: Please enter any unmet needs or expectations you have with our services (optional).
* **Achievement of ROI objectives**: Select the level of achievement of your ROI objectives (e.g., Not achieved, Exceeded objectives).
* **Estimated payback period**: Select the estimated payback period for the investment (e.g., Less than 3 months, Still evaluating).
* **Overall satisfaction with partnership**: Select the level of overall satisfaction with our partnership (e.g., Very Dissatisfied, Very Satisfied).
* **Likelihood to continue partnership**: Select the likelihood of continuing our partnership (e.g., Very Unlikely, Very Likely).
* **Interest in additional services**: Select the level of interest in additional services (e.g., Not interested, Very interested).

## Tips
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* We value your feedback and appreciate your time in completing this form.
* If you are unsure about any question, please select the most appropriate option based on your experience.
