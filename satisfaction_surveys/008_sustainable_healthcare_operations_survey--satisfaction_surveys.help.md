# Sustainable Healthcare Operations Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sustainable Healthcare Operations Survey aims to collect information from healthcare organizations about their sustainability practices and experiences. This data will help us understand the current state of sustainability in the healthcare sector and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully and make sure you are completing it as an authorized representative of your organization.
* Answer each page according to your organization's practices and experiences.
* For the "sustainability initiatives" page, please select the initiatives that apply to your organization from the provided options.
* For the "success stories" page, please share a brief description of a relevant success story.
* For the "challenges" page, please note any challenges your organization is currently facing related to sustainability.
* For the "staff feedback" page, please provide a number to indicate the level of agreement or disagreement with the statement provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Introduction
* **Introduction** (`note`, not required): This page serves as an introduction to the survey. Please read the page carefully and proceed to the next sections.

### Page 2: Sustainability Initiatives
* **What sustainability initiatives do you have in place at your organization?** (`select_one`, not required): Please select all the initiatives that apply to your organization. This includes, but is not limited to, renewable energy projects, sustainable supply chain practices, and environmentally-friendly waste management.

### Page 3: Success Stories
* **Can you tell us about a success story?** (`text`, not required): Please share a brief description of a success story related to sustainability within your organization. This could be a project or initiative that has had a positive impact on the environment.

### Page 4: Challenges
* **Challenges** (`note`, not required): Please note any challenges your organization is currently facing related to sustainability. This could include technical, financial, or operational hurdles.

### Page 5: Staff Feedback
* **staff-feedback** (`number`, not required): Please indicate the level of agreement or disagreement with the statement provided. A scale of 0-5, where 0 means "Strongly Disagree" and 5 means "Strongly Agree".
