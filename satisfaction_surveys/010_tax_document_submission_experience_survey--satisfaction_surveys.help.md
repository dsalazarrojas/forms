# tax_document_submission_experience_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The tax_document_submission_experience_survey form is designed to gather feedback on the tax document submission process. This form helps us understand your experience and opinions to improve the process for future users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant tax document type from the drop-down menu.
2. Rate the difficulty level of the upload process.
3. Enter the submission date and time in the format MM/DD/YYYY and HH:MM AM/PM.
4. Enter your tax firm name (if applicable).
5. Provide your email address and phone number (if applicable).
6. Add any comments or additional submission notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Tax Document Type** (`tax_document_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of tax document you submitted (e.g. Income Tax Return, W-2 Form, or 1099 Form).
- **Upload Process** (`upload_process`, select_multiple, optional): Rate the difficulty level of the upload process (e.g. Easy, Fair, or Difficult).
- **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you submitted the tax document in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you submitted the tax document in the format 12:30 PM.
- **Tax Firm Name** (`tax_firm_name`, text, optional): Enter your tax firm name (if applicable).
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address (if applicable).
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
- **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any comments or feedback about your submission experience.
- **Submission Notes** (`submission_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or details about your submission experience.
