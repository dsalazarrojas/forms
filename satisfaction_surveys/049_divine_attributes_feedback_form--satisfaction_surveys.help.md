# Divine Attributes Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Divine Attributes Feedback Form is a tool used to collect feedback on the divine attributes of a spiritual experience or service. This form helps us understand how users perceive the quality of these attributes, such as spiritual growth, guidance, and evaluations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your User Name in the first field.
2. Provide your User Email address in the second field.
3. For each of the attributes listed, select the rating that best describes your experience:
	* Spiritual Growth: Very Good, Good, Fair, or Bad
	* Spiritual Guidance: Very Good, Good, Fair, or Bad
	* Evaluations: Very Good, Good, Fair, or Bad
4. Enter your reflections in the third field. This is an open-ended space for you to share any additional thoughts or comments.
5. Select the correct Submission Date and Submission Time for your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name (1)**: Enter your name as it appears in your user account or identification.
* **User Email (2)**: Enter your email address associated with your user account.
* **Spiritual Growth (3)**: Rate your experience with spiritual growth, from Very Good to Bad.
* **Spiritual Guidance (4)**: Rate your experience with spiritual guidance, from Very Good to Bad.
* **Reflections (5)**: Enter any additional thoughts or comments about your experience.
* **Evaluations (6)**: Rate your overall evaluation of the experience, from Very Good to Bad.
* **Time (7)**: This field is currently not used. Please ignore it for now.
* **Submission Date (8)**: Select the date on which you submitted this form.
* **Submission Time (9)**: Select the time at which you submitted this form.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your User Name and User Email correctly to ensure your feedback is associated with your user account.
* Use the correct rating scale for the attributes listed, as they may have different implications.
* Be as specific as possible when completing the reflections field.
* Double-check the Submission Date and Submission Time fields for accuracy.
