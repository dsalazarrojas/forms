# feedback_and_memories_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and memories from users about their experiences with our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the title of the feedback and memories section.
2. Provide a rating of your experience using the experience_rating field.
3. Share a story about your experience in the story field.
4. Choose whether you want to share your feedback and memories publicly or privately in the sharing_status field.
5. Fill in the date and time of your experience in the experience_date and experience_time fields.
6. Share a photo of your experience in the experience_photo field.
7. Enter the name of the event or activity that you participated in.
8. Enter the name of the community or group that you were a part of.
9. Enter the name of the organization that hosted or sponsored the event.
10. Choose whether you want to share your feedback and memories in the sharing_status_2 field.
11. Fill in the feedback and memories about your experience.
12. Provide a testimonial about your experience in the testimonial field (optional).
13. Choose whether you want to share your feedback and memories publicly or privately in the sharing_status_2 field for the second part of the form.
14. Fill in the feedback 2 field.
15. Provide a testimonial 2 field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **title** (`feedback_and_memories_form`, text, required): This is the title of the feedback and memories section.
* **experience_rating** (`experience_rating`, number, required): This is the rating of your experience.
* **story** (`story`, text, required): This is the description of your experience.
* **sharing_consent** (`sharing_consent`, text, required): This is the sharing consent for your feedback and memories.
* **experience_date** (`experience_date`, date, required): This is the date of your experience.
* **experience_time** (`experience_time`, time, required): This is the time of your experience.
* **experience_photo** (`experience_photo`, text, optional): This is the photo of your experience.
* **event_name** (`event_name`, text, required): This is the name of the event or activity that you participated in.
* **community_name** (`community_name`, text, required): This is the name of the community or group that you were a part of.
* **organization_name** (`organization_name`, text, required): This is the name of the organization that hosted or sponsored the event.
* **sharing_status** (`sharing_status`, select_multiple, required): This is the sharing status for your feedback and memories.
* **feedback** (`feedback`, text, required): This is the feedback and memories about your experience.
* **testimonial** (`testimonial`, text, optional): This is the testimonial about your experience.
* **sharing_consent_2** (`sharing_consent_2`, text, optional): This is the sharing consent for the second part of the form.
* **consent_form** (`consent_form`, text, optional): This is the consent form.
* **sharing_consent_3** (`sharing_consent_3`, text, optional): This is the sharing consent 3.
* **event_id** (`event_id`, text, optional): This is the event ID.
* **community_id** (`community_id`, text, optional): This is the community ID.
* **organization_id** (`organization_id`, text, optional): This is the organization ID.
* **event_name_2** (`event_name_2`, text, optional): This is the event name 2.
* **community_name_2** (`community_name_2`, text, optional): This is the community name 2.
* **organization_name_2** (`organization_name_2`, text, optional): This is the organization name 2.
* **sharing_status_2** (`sharing_status_2`, select_multiple, optional): This is the sharing status 2.
* **feedback_2** (`feedback_2`, text, optional): This is the feedback 2.
* **testimonial_2** (`testimonial_2`, text, optional): This is the testimonial 2.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Please choose the correct sharing status for your feedback and memories.
* You can share photos and feedback and memories publicly or privately.
* If you have any issues or questions, please contact us.
