# Fitness Class Instructor Energy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from students about the energy and quality of instruction provided by fitness class instructors. The data collected will help us evaluate and improve the performance of our instructors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the options that best describe your experience with the instructor and provide your honest feedback. Each field is optional, but we appreciate your input.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Please enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for follow-up or to receive updates about our services.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for easy contact if needed.
* **Favorite Instructor**: Select how you feel about the instructor:
	+ Love them
	+ Like them
	+ Neutral
	+ Dislike them
	+ Hate them
* **Attitude**: Rate the instructor's attitude:
	+ Positive
	+ Negative
	+ Unsure
* **Energy Level**: Rate the instructor's energy level:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
* **Instruction Quality**: Describe the quality of the instructions provided by the instructor.
* **Communication Effectiveness**: Rate the instructor's communication style:
	+ Positive
	+ Negative
	+ Neutral
	+ Unsure
* **Class Environment**: Rate the class environment:
	+ Positive
	+ Negative
	+ Neutral
	+ Unsure
* **Equipment and Supplies**: Rate the availability of equipment and supplies:
	+ Well-stocked
	+ Moderately stocked
	+ Poorly stocked
* **Music and Sound System**: Rate the music and sound system:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Temperature and Air Quality**: Rate the temperature and air quality:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Water and Shower**: Rate the availability of water and shower facilities:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Water and Rm**: Rate the quality of the restrooms and shower facilities:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Parking Facilities**: Rate the parking facilities:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Facilities and Amenities**: Rate the overall facilities and amenities:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Overall Experience**: Rate your overall experience:
	+ Positive
	+ Negative
	+ Neutral
	+ Unsure
* **Recommend**: Would you recommend this instructor:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Unsure

## Tips
Please take your time to answer each question honestly, and feel free to add any additional comments or suggestions in the "Additional Comments" section (not provided in this form). Thank you for your feedback!
