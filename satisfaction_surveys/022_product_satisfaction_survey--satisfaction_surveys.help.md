# Product Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Product Satisfaction Survey" is designed to collect feedback from customers about their satisfaction with a product. This information will help us improve customer experience and product development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all the questions honestly and thoroughly.
2. Select the correct options for each question.
3. Enter your email address and phone number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **product_satisfaction_survey**: This is the main section of the form where you will be asked to provide feedback about the product. Please answer all the questions honestly and thoroughly.
* **product_name**: Enter the name of the product you are providing feedback about. This is the product you have been using or reviewing.
* **category**: Check "Yes" if the product meets your needs, and "No" if it does not.
* **subcategory**: Check "Yes" if the product has a subcategory that applies to you, and "No" if it does not.
* **question_1**: Answer this question honestly and thoroughly. What do you think about the product's features and functionality?
* **question_2**: Answer this question honestly and thoroughly. How easy was it to use the product?
* **question_3**: Answer this question honestly and thoroughly. How satisfied are you with the product's performance?
* **question_4**: Check "Yes" if the product has met your needs, and "No" if it has not.
* **rating_1**, **rating_2**, **rating_3**, **rating_4**, **rating_5**: Select the rating that best describes your satisfaction with different aspects of the product. For example, if you are very satisfied with the product's features, select "Good" for rating_1.
* **email_address**: Enter your email address so we can contact you for follow-up questions or to provide updates.
* **phone_number**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you for follow-up questions or to provide updates.
