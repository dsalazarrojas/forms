# event_follow_up_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback and satisfaction assessment on an event that has happened. The form contains multiple questions and text fields to collect both qualitative and quantitative data about the event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On the first page, please fill out your answers to any questions that apply to you.
2. Answer each question according to your experiences with the event.
3. If you have any additional comments or details about your experience, please use the text fields provided.
4. Review your answers and make sure you are comfortable with the information provided.
5. Once you are satisfied with your answers, submit the form.
