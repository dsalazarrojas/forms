# event_follow_up_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback and satisfaction assessment on an event that has happened. The form contains multiple questions and text fields to collect both qualitative and quantitative data about the event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On the first page, please fill out your answers to any questions that apply to you.
2. Answer each question according to your experiences with the event.
3. If you have any additional comments or details about your experience, please use the text fields provided.
4. Review your answers and make sure you are comfortable with the information provided.
5. Once you are satisfied with your answers, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **event_follow_up_survey_page_1** (`text`, not required): This is the first page of the form where you can provide your general feedback about the event.
* **follow_up_survey_question_1** (select multiple, not required): Please select all applicable answers that apply to your experience with the event. Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **follow_up_survey_question_1_answer** (text, not required): Please provide a more detailed answer to the previous question if you need to elaborate on your response.
* **follow_up_survey_question_2** (select one, not required): Please answer this question regarding your experience with the event. Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **follow_up_survey_question_2_answer** (text, not required): Similar to the previous question, please provide a more detailed answer if needed.
* **follow_up_survey_question_3** (select multiple, not required): Please select all applicable answers that apply to your experience with the event. Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **follow_up_survey_question_3_answer** (text, not required): Please provide a more detailed answer to the previous question if you need to elaborate on your response.
* **follow_up_survey_question_4** (select one, not required): Please answer this question regarding your experience with the event. Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **follow_up_survey_question_4_answer** (text, not required): Similar to the previous question, please provide a more detailed answer if needed.

## Tips

* Please be honest and thorough in your answers, as they will help us better understand your experience with the event.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* You can review and change your answers before submitting the form.
* Make sure to submit the form only when you are comfortable with the information provided.
