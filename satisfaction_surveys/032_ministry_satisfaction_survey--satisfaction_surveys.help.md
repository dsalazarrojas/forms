# Ministry Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This Ministry Satisfaction Survey is designed to collect feedback from ministry members, stakeholders, or users to understand their overall satisfaction levels with the services provided by the ministry. The survey aims to gather information on specific areas such as programs participated in, communication, service quality, community engagement, interaction with staff, and any comments or feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Read through the form carefully to understand each question and its relevance.
2.  Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3.  Provide your email address and phone number for further communication or follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Welcome to the Ministry Satisfaction Survey**: This is an introduction page that provides a brief overview of the survey.
- **How would you rate your overall satisfaction with the ministry?**: Rate your satisfaction on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
- **Which programs did you participate in?**: Choose all the programs that you participated in. Select "Yes" for each program that applies.
- **How did you like the communication from the ministry?**: Rate your satisfaction with the communication you received from the ministry.
- **How would you rate the quality of the ministry's services?**: Rate the quality of the services provided by the ministry on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
- **How would you rate the community engagement from the ministry?**: Rate the community engagement provided by the ministry on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
- **How would you rate your interaction with the ministry staff?**: Rate your interaction with the ministry staff on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
- **Any comments?**: Provide any comments or feedback you have about the ministry's services.
- **What is your email address?**: Provide your email address for further communication or follow-up.
- **What is your phone number?**: Provide your phone number for further communication or follow-up.
