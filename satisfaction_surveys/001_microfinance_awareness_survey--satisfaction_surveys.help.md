# Microfinance Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Microfinance Awareness Survey is designed to collect information about your knowledge, opinions, and experiences with microfinance. Your responses will help us better understand how to improve and develop microfinance programs and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the multiple-choice questions by selecting the option that best reflects your opinion or experience.
2. For text fields, provide a clear and concise answer in the space provided.
3. Make sure to answer all questions, as they are all optional.
4. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What about microfinance?** (Text field, Optional): Please provide a brief description of your thoughts or opinions about microfinance.
* **How do you know about microfinance?** (Select one, Optional): Choose how you know about microfinance. Options include:
	+ Very well
	+ Not very well
	+ Not at all
* **Where did you hear about microfinance?** (Select multiple, Optional): Choose how you heard about microfinance. Options include:
	+ Radio/TV
	+ Internet
	+ Newspapers
	+ Social media
	+ Word of mouth
	+ Other
	+ Don't know
* **How interested are you in microfinance?** (Select one, Optional): Choose how interested you are in microfinance. Options include:
	+ Very interested
	+ Somewhat interested
	+ Not very interested
	+ Not at all interested
* **What are the benefits of microfinance?** (Text field, Optional): Please provide a brief description of the benefits of microfinance.
* **What are the limitations of microfinance?** (Text field, Optional): Please provide a brief description of the limitations of microfinance.
* **Which microfinance institution do you know?** (Select multiple, Optional): Choose any microfinance institutions you are familiar with. Options include:
	+ MfA
	+ MfS
	+ Other
	+ None
* **What microfinance services are you interested in?** (Select multiple, Optional): Choose any microfinance services you are interested in. Options include:
	+ MFS
	+ MFB
	+ Other
	+ None
* **What is your opinion about microfinance?** (Select one, Optional): Choose your overall opinion about microfinance. Options include:
	+ Positive
	+ Negative
	+ Neutral
* **Would you like to contact us?** (Select one, Optional): Choose if you would like to be contacted by us. Options include:
	+ True

* **Email** (Email field, Optional): If you provide your email address, we may contact you for follow-up questions or to provide more information.
* **Phone** (Text field, Optional): If you provide your phone number, we may contact you for follow-up questions or to provide more information.
* **Your location** (Text field, Optional): Please provide your location (city, province, or country) if you would like to be contacted.
* **Comments** (Text field, Optional): Please provide any additional comments or thoughts about microfinance.
* **Gender** (Select one, Optional): Choose your gender. Options include:
	+ Male

* **Education** (Select one, Optional): Choose your level of education. Options include:

	+ Other
* **Occupation** (Text field, Optional): If applicable, please provide your occupation.
* **Income** (Number field, Optional): If applicable, please provide your annual income.

* **Family size** (Number field, Optional): If applicable, please provide the number of family members.

	+ Radio/TV

	+ Newspapers

	+ Word of mouth
	+ Other
	+ Don't know
* **How aware are you about microfinance?** (Select one, Optional): Choose how aware you are about microfinance. Options include:
	+ Very aware
	+ Somewhat aware
	+ Not very aware
	+ Not at all aware
