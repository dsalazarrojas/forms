# DIY Beauty Practice Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your beauty practices, including your routine frequency, product usage, and satisfaction levels. Please answer the questions honestly, and do not hesitate to describe any challenges you have faced with your routine if you choose to do so.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply choose the relevant answers for each field. Most fields are optional, so feel free to answer as many or as few questions as you like. If a field has a "Select all that apply" option, you can choose as many options as you like from the list. For text fields, simply type in your answer and we'll do our best to help you understand it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Beauty Practice** (Select all that apply): This field allows you to choose how you practice your beauty routine, whether it's at home or with a professional.
	+ At home: You practice your beauty routine at home.
	+ Professional: You practice your beauty routine with a professional.
* **Motivation** (Select one): Choose why you practice your beauty routine.
	+ I enjoy learning about new trends: You enjoy learning about new beauty trends.
	+ I want to improve my appearance: You practice your beauty routine to improve your appearance.
	+ I am not sure: You're not sure why you practice your beauty routine.
* **Challenges Faced** (Text): If you have faced any challenges with your beauty routine, please describe them in this field.
* **Length of Routine** (Time): Select the time of day you follow your beauty routine.
* **Satisfaction Level** (Select one): Choose how satisfied you are with your beauty routine.
	+ Very satisfied: You are very satisfied with your beauty routine.
	+ Somewhat satisfied: You are somewhat satisfied with your beauty routine.
	+ Neither satisfied nor dissatisfied: You are neither satisfied nor dissatisfied with your beauty routine.
	+ Somewhat dissatisfied: You are somewhat dissatisfied with your beauty routine.
	+ Very dissatisfied: You are very dissatisfied with your beauty routine.
* **Satisfaction Level Other** (Text): If you'd like to describe your satisfaction level with your beauty routine, please do so in this field.
* **Routine Frequency** (Select one): Choose how often you follow your beauty routine.
	+ Daily: You follow your beauty routine daily.
	+ Several times a week: You follow your beauty routine several times a week.
	+ Occasionally: You follow your beauty routine occasionally.
	+ Never: You never follow a beauty routine.
