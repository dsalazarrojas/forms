# Foreign Conflict Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Foreign Conflict Awareness Survey, a form designed to collect information about your awareness and perception of foreign conflicts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your knowledge.
2. Be as detailed as possible in your answers.
3. If you're unsure about any question, feel free to provide the best answer you can.
4. If you need to provide additional information, you can use the "Comments" field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Foreign Conflict Awareness Survey** (Page 1): This is the title of the survey and will not affect your answers.
* **What Do You Know** (Page 2, text field): Please provide a brief description of the foreign conflicts you are aware of, or any topics you think are relevant to the survey. 
* **Information Sources** (Page 3, select_multiple): Choose all the sources of information you use for news and updates on foreign conflicts. 
* **How Informed** (Page 4, select_one): Indicate how informed you feel about foreign conflicts, based on your knowledge and experience. 
* **Why Informed** (Page 5, text field): Please explain why you feel well- or not well-informed. 
* **Perceptions of Conflict** (Page 6, text field): Describe your perception of the foreign conflict, including any notable characteristics or concerns you have. 
* **How Concerned** (Page 7, text field): Describe how concerned you are about the foreign conflict, and why. 
* **How Confident** (Page 8, text field): Describe how confident you are in your understanding of the foreign conflict. 
* **Foreign Conflict Concerns** (Page 9, select_multiple): Choose all the concerns you have about the foreign conflict. 
* **Foreign Conflict Impact** (Page 10, select_one): Indicate how likely you think the foreign conflict will impact you or your community. 
* **Why Impact** (Page 11, text field): Explain why you think the foreign conflict will have an impact. 
* **Comments** (Page 12, text field): Use this field to provide any additional comments or information you think is relevant to the survey. 
* **E-mail** (Page 13, email field): If you want to be contacted regarding your answers, please provide your email address. 
* **Phone** (Page 14, text field): Provide your phone number if you want to be contacted regarding your answers. 
* **Name** (Page 15, text field): Please provide your name for reference. 
* **Foreign Conflict Awareness** (Page 16, time field): If you are concerned about foreign conflicts, please indicate how much time you spend reading about them. 
* **Follow-up Questions** (Page 17, text field): If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to provide them. 

Note that some fields are not mandatory and may be skipped, but be sure to complete all the required fields (marked with *).
