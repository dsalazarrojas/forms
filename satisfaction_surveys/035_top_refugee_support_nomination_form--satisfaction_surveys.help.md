# top_refugee_support_nomination_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The top_refugee_support_nomination_form is used to collect information about organizations and individuals who have made significant contributions to support refugees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the organization you wish to nominate.
2. Enter the name of the organization.
3. Enter the role played by the organization.
4. Enter the nomination date.
5. Enter the contact email of the organization.
6. Enter the contact phone number of the organization.
7. Enter a brief description of the organization's work.
8. Select the type of organization being nominated.
9. Enter the name of the individual being nominated.
10. Enter the title of the individual being nominated.
11. Enter the organization the individual is with.
12. Enter the role played by the individual.
13. Enter the contact email of the individual.
14. Enter the contact phone number of the individual.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **organization** (required): Enter the name of the organization you wish to nominate.
* **name** (required): Enter the name of the organization you selected.
* **role** (optional): Enter the role played by the organization.
* **nomination_date** (optional): Enter the date of the nomination.
* **contact_email** (optional): Enter the contact email of the organization.
* **contact_phone** (optional): Enter the contact phone number of the organization.
* **nomination_narrative** (optional): A brief description of the organization's work.
* **organization_type** (optional): Enter the type of organization being nominated.
* **nominee_name** (optional): Enter the name of the individual being nominated.
* **nominee_title** (optional): Enter the title of the individual being nominated.
* **nominee_organization** (optional): Enter the organization the individual is with.
* **nominee_role** (optional): Enter the role played by the individual.
* **nominee_contact_email** (optional): Enter the contact email of the individual.
* **nominee_contact_phone** (optional): Enter the contact phone number of the individual.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form with the relevant information for the organization and individual you wish to nominate.
* Only one organization and one individual should be nominated per submission.
* If you have already submitted a nomination for the same organization and individual, you will not be able to submit again.
