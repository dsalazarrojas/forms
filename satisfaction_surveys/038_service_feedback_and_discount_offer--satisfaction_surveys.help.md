# Service Feedback And Discount Offer - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from customers to improve the service provided. It's a way to show appreciation for their input and to identify areas that need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through the form carefully and fill out as many fields as you'd like.
2. If you have any comments or suggestions, please use the "service_feedback" field to type them out.
3. If you'd like to offer feedback about the service you received, use the "service_feedback" field.
4. If you have a suggestion for how we can improve the service, use the "service_improvement" field.
5. If you have a specific improvement idea, use the "service_improvement_ideas" field.
6. If you'd like to offer a discount or promotion, use the "discount_offer" field.
7. If you have a contact number, please enter it in the "contact_number" field.
8. If you have a contact email, please enter it in the "contact_email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Service Feedback And Discount Offer Form
This field is where you can type out any comments or feedback about the service you received.

### Service Feedback
This field is where you can provide specific feedback about the service you received.

### Discount Offer
This field is where you can offer a discount or promotion as a token of appreciation for our service.

### Customer Service Rating
This field is where you can rate your experience with our service, with higher numbers indicating better service.

### Customer Satisfaction
This field is where you can rate how satisfied you are with the service you received, with higher numbers indicating higher satisfaction.

### Service Improvement
This field is where you can suggest ways we can improve our service.

### Service Improvement Ideas
This field is where you can provide specific ideas for improving our service.

### Discount Coupon
This field is where you can enter a discount or promotion code.

### Contact Number
This field is where you can enter your contact number so we can follow up with you.

### Contact Email
This field is where you can enter your contact email so we can follow up with you.

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback or suggestions.
* If you have any attachments or images, you can attach them to your response.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at the email or number you entered.
* Your feedback is greatly appreciated and will help us improve our service.
