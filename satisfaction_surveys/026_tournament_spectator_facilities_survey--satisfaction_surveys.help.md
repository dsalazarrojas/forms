# Tournament Spectator Facilities Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the quality of facilities and services provided during a sports tournament event. The feedback collected will help event organizers understand what to improve to enhance the overall spectator experience. Please ensure you're filling out the form for the specific event you attended.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field according to your experience at the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tournament Spectator Facilities** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): A brief description of the facilities you experienced during the event, such as the condition of the seating, toilets, and other amenities.
* **Food and Beverages** (`2`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the food and beverages that were available during the event. You can choose from the following options: Water, Soft drinks, Coffee, Snacks, Food.
* **Facilities** (`3`, `select_one`, required): Choose the facilities that were available during the event. You can select only one option from: Seating, Toilets, Drinking fountains, Parking, Food court, Café.
* **Services** (`4`, `select_one`, required): Choose the services that were available during the event. You can select only one option from: Security, Staff, Information, First aid.
* **Facilities Rating** (`5`, `number`, required/optional): Rate the facilities on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "not good at all" and 5 is "excellent".
* **Services Rating** (`6`, `number`, required/optional): Rate the services on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "not good at all" and 5 is "excellent".
* **Overall Experience** (`7`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your overall experience at the event, including any positive or negative comments.
* **Venue** (`8`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the venue where the event took place, including any notable features or issues.
