# Tier And Interval Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about their satisfaction with different tiers and intervals. It will ask you to select the tiers and intervals that are relevant to you, and provide any comments or feedback you may have about each tier and interval.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the tiers that apply to you.
2. Choose the interval that best suits your feedback.
3. If you have any comments or feedback about each tier, type them in the corresponding section.
4. If you have any further comments, provide your email address and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tier Selection** (`tier_selection`, select_multiple, required=false): Select all the tiers that apply to you. This can be multiple selections.
* **Interval** (`interval_selection`, select_one, required=false): Choose the interval that best suits your feedback. This can be one selection.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, number, required=false): If you're satisfied with your current tier and interval, type a number between 1 and 10 to indicate your level of satisfaction.
* **Comments** (`tier_comments`, note, required=false): Provide any comments or feedback about the tiers you selected.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required=false): If you'd like to provide your email address, type it here.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required=false): If you'd like to provide your phone number, type it here.
* **Tier 1 Comment** (`tier_1_comment`, note, required=false): Provide any comments or feedback about Tier 1.
* **Tier 2 Comment** (`tier_2_comment`, note, required=false): Provide any comments or feedback about Tier 2.
* **Tier 3 Comment** (`tier_3_comment`, note, required=false): Provide any comments or feedback about Tier 3.
* **Interval 1 Comment** (`interval_1_comment`, note, required=false): Provide any comments or feedback about Interval 1.
* **Interval 2 Comment** (`interval_2_comment`, note, required=false): Provide any comments or feedback about Interval 2.
* **Interval 3 Comment** (`interval_3_comment`, note, required=false): Provide any comments or feedback about Interval 3.
