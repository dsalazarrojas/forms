# Jewelry Box Protection Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and satisfaction data from end users about their jewelry boxes. It helps us understand your experiences and preferences regarding jewelry box storage habits, protection features, customer satisfaction, and other aspects of your jewelry box.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Fill out the form completely, but feel free to skip questions that don't apply to your experience.
3. If you have any other comments or issues, please use the "Other Issues" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Storage Habits** (`storage_habits`, `text`, optional): Describe your current storage habits for your jewelry box.
* **Protection Features** (`protection_features`, `select_one`, optional): What feature do you value most in a jewelry box? (Secure, Water resistant, Other)
* **Customer Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, `select_one`, optional): How satisfied are you with your jewelry box? (Satisfied, Not Satisfied, Neutral)
* **Product Teams** (`product_teams`, `select_one`, optional): What do you think about the product teams behind your jewelry box? (Agree, Disagree, Neutral)
* **Storage Commissioner** (`storage_commissioner`, `select_multiple`, optional): Who is responsible for your jewelry box? (Box, Closet, Other)
* **Other Issues** (`other_issues`, `checkbox`, optional): Have you noticed any other issues with your jewelry box? (Option A, Option B)
* **Design** (`design`, `number`, optional): How would you rate the design of your jewelry box? (1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest)
* **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, `number`, optional): How would you rate the value for money of your jewelry box? (1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest)

## Tips

* Please be honest and thorough in your answers to help us better understand your jewelry box experience.
* If you have any suggestions or comments, please feel free to provide them in the "Other Issues" field.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
