# Legal Service Quality Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from clients on the quality of the legal services provided by your organization. It helps us to understand how we can improve our services, identify areas of strengths and weaknesses, and make necessary adjustments to provide better experiences for our clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Choose one of the available options for "Client Feedback" on page 1 of the form.
3. Fill in the "Service Quality" rating on page 2 of the form, using a number between 1 and 5 to indicate the level of satisfaction.
4. Enter your time taken for the process on page 3 of the form, using a number representing the hours spent.
5. Rate the "Communication Rating" on page 4 of the form, using a number between 1 and 5 to indicate the level of satisfaction.
6. Select the level of responsiveness of the "Response to Emergency" on page 5 of the form.
7. Rate the "Legal Team" on page 6 of the form, using a number between 1 and 5 to indicate the level of satisfaction.
8. Rate the "Communication Rating 2" on page 7 of the form, using a number between 1 and 5 to indicate the level of satisfaction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Feedback**: This field allows you to choose how satisfied you were with the service provided. Select "Highly Satisfied", "Somewhat Satisfied", or "Not Satisfied" to indicate your level of satisfaction.
* **Service Quality**: Rate the quality of the service provided, with higher numbers indicating higher satisfaction. For example, 5 is "Excellent" and 1 is "Poor".
* **Time Taken**: Enter the amount of time you spent on the process, in hours.
* **Communication Rating**: Rate the quality of communication from the legal team, with higher numbers indicating higher satisfaction. For example, 5 is "Excellent" and 1 is "Poor".
* **Response to Emergency**: Select how responsive the legal team was to your needs, with "Highly Responsive", "Somewhat Responsive", or "Not Responsive" options.
* **Legal Team**: Rate the performance of the legal team, with higher numbers indicating higher satisfaction. For example, 5 is "Excellent" and 1 is "Poor".
* **Communication Rating 2**: Rate the quality of communication from the legal team, with higher numbers indicating higher satisfaction. For example, 5 is "Excellent" and 1 is "Poor".
