# Civic Participation Readiness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Civic Participation Readiness Survey is designed to assess an individual's readiness to participate in civic activities, such as volunteering, attending public meetings, and engaging in community organizing. The survey aims to understand their level of knowledge about local government, voting habits, and willingness to engage in various civic activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name in the "Respondent Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field for follow-up communication.
3. Select your age group from the options provided in the "Age Group" field.
4. Choose the type of residence you currently live in from the options in the "Type of Residence" field.
5. Rate your knowledge of local government from the options in the "How would you rate your knowledge of local government" field.
6. Indicate how often you vote in local elections from the options in the "How often do you vote in local elections" field.
7. Answer the question about attending public meetings from the options in the "Have you attended a public meeting in the past year" field.
8. Indicate if you have ever contacted an elected official from the options in the "Have you ever contacted an elected official" field.
9. Choose how you currently volunteer in your community from the options in the "Do you currently volunteer in your community" field.
10. Indicate if you are a member of any community organizations from the options in the "Are you a member of any community organizations" field.
11. Select all the barriers that prevent your civic participation from the options in the "What barriers prevent your civic participation" field.
12. Choose the sources of information you use to stay informed about community issues from the options in the "How do you stay informed about community issues" field.
13. Indicate your interest in civic participation training from the options in the "Would you be interested in civic participation training" field.
14. Choose your preferred methods of getting involved in civic activities from the options in the "How would you prefer to get involved" field.
15. Rate your confidence in making a difference in your community from the options in the "How confident are you in making a difference" field.
16. Indicate the amount of time you can dedicate to civic activities from the options in the "How much time could you dedicate monthly" field.
17. Choose the skills you can offer to civic efforts from the options in the "What skills could you offer to civic efforts" field.
18. Share any additional comments or thoughts about civic participation in the "Additional Comments" field.
