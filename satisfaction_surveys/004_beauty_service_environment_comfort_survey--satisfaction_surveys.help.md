# Beauty Service Environment Comfort Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to help you complete the Beauty Service Environment Comfort Survey form. The form is used to collect feedback about your experience at the salon, and your input is greatly appreciated.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the corresponding answer to each question based on your experience at the salon. Make sure to be honest and provide detailed feedback where possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Date of Visit
* **Date of Visit** (`visit_date`, `date`, required): This is the date you visited the salon for your beauty service. Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### 2. Type of Service Received
* **Type of Service Received** (`service_type`, `select_one`, required): This question asks you to select the type of service you received at the salon. Choose the option that best describes your service. The options are:
	+ Hair Services
	+ Skincare
	+ Nail Services
	+ Massage
	+ Waxing
	+ Other

### 3. Salon Location/Branch
* **Salon Location/Branch** (`salon_location`, `text`, optional): If you want to provide more specific information about the location of the salon, you can enter the name or address of the branch here. This is an optional field.

### 4. Overall Cleanliness of the Salon
* **Overall Cleanliness of the Salon** (`cleanliness_overall`, `select_one`, required): This question asks you to rate the overall cleanliness of the salon. Choose the option that best describes your experience. The options are:
	+ Excellent
	+ Very Good

	+ Fair
	+ Poor
