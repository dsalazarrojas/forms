# Transport Service Route Satisfaction Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects feedback from passengers about their experiences with a particular transport service route. It aims to gauge overall passenger satisfaction, identify areas for improvement, and gather suggestions for enhancements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Choose the answer that best describes your experience with the route for each question.
2. For questions with options, select one or multiple answers depending on the type.
3. For questions without options, provide your comments in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **how_was_the_route_performance** (Select one or multiple from: Good, Poor, Fair)
	+ This question asks about your perception of the route's performance, such as punctuality, reliability, and efficiency.
* **how_useful_was_the_route** (Number)
	+ This question is intended to gauge how useful you found the route for your travel needs. Please provide a numerical value between 0 and 100, where 0 is not useful at all and 100 is very useful.
* **how_satisfied_are_you_with_the_route** (Select one from: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Not Satisfied, Very Dissatisfied)
	+ This question measures your overall satisfaction with the route, considering factors such as comfort, amenities, and overall experience.
* **how_frequently_do_you_use_this_route** (Select one from: Often, Occasionally, Rarely, Never)
	+ This question helps us understand how frequently you use this route, which informs our scheduling and resource allocation decisions.
* **would_you_recommend_this_route_to_a_friend** (Select one from: 'True', 'False')
	+ This question gauges your willingness to recommend the route to others, indicating its reputation and popularity.
* **do_you_have_any_suggestions_for_improvement** (Text)
	+ This question allows you to provide any suggestions or ideas for improving the route, such as new schedules, routes, or services.
* **any_comments_about_the_route** (Note)
	+ This text field is for any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share about your experience with the route.

## Tips
- Please answer each question based on your honest experience with the route.
- Be as specific as possible when providing suggestions or comments.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
