# Enterprise Healthcare Client Insights Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather insights from clients regarding their satisfaction with the enterprise healthcare services. We want to understand what you think about our services so that we can improve them to better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the required information such as your name and email address.
2. Rate your satisfaction level with our services.
3. Share any improvement ideas you have for our services.
4. Answer the referral questions to help us understand where our clients refer us from and why.
5. Fill in any additional comments you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enterprise Healthcare Client Insights Survey Form**: This is the title of the survey form.
* **Category**: Select the category for this survey.
* **Description**: Please describe your experience with our services.
* **Satisfaction Rating**: Rate your satisfaction level with our services on a scale of 1-5.
* **Improvement Ideas**: Share any ideas you have for improving our services.
* **Referral Intent**: Answer the question about whether you would refer us to someone else.
* **Referral Reasons**: Select the reasons why you would refer us to someone else.
* **Referral Source**: Select the source where you found out about us.
* **Referral Channels**: Select the channels you use to refer us to someone else.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments you have.
* **Submit**: Click on Submit to submit your survey.

## Tips
- Please fill in all the required fields.
- You can submit your survey at any time by clicking on the Submit button.
- You can save your survey and continue later by clicking on the Save and Continue button.
