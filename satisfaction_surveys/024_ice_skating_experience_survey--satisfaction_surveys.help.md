# Ice Skating Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their ice skating experience, aiming to improve the overall experience for customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question according to your ice skating experience and preferences.
2. For the "skater_type" field, choose one or more options that best describe your type of skating.
3. In the "what_are_your_goals_for_skating" field, select all goals that apply to you.
4. Ensure that you enter your email address, phone number, and name accurately for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
* **introduction**: This is an introductory page that explains the purpose of the form. Please read it carefully and proceed to the next question if you understand.

### Skating Experience
* **skating_experience**: This question asks about your level of satisfaction with your ice skating experience. Please select from the options:
	+ Very Dissatisfied
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Very Satisfied

### Skating Frequency
* **skating_frequency**: This question asks about how often you go ice skating. Please select from the options:
	+ Rarely
	+ Occasionaly
	+ Frequently
	+ Almost Always

### Type of Skater
* **skater_type**: This question asks about the type of skater you are. Please select one or more options that best describe your type of skating:
	+ Recreational Skater
	+ Competitive Skater
	+ Recreational/Competitive Skater

### Skating Frequency (Other)
* **skating_frequency_other**: If none of the above options match your frequency, please describe it here.

### Satisfaction
* **satisfaction**: This question asks about your satisfaction level. Please enter a number from 1 to 10, where 1 is very dissatisfied and 10 is very satisfied.

### Comments
* **comments**: Please share any additional comments or feedback about your ice skating experience.

### Goals for Skating
* **what_are_your_goals_for_skating**: This question asks about your goals for skating. Please select all options that apply:
	+ Improve my skills
	+ Get faster
	+ Have fun
	+ Meet new people

### Contact Information
* **email**: Please enter your email address for further contact or follow-up purposes.
* **phone**: Please enter your phone number for further contact or follow-up purposes.
* **name**: Please enter your name for further contact or follow-up purposes.
