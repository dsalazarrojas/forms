## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from customers about their hair care habits, brand loyalty, and purchasing behavior.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that you have already purchased a product from our brand and have a valid email address on file.
2. Review each question carefully and answer according to your personal experience.
3. Provide honest and accurate information.
4. Submit your response once you have completed all the questions.
