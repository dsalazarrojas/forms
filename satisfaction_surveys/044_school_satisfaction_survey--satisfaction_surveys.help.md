# School Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The School Satisfaction Survey is designed to collect feedback from students about their overall satisfaction with the school, teachers, and school facilities. The survey aims to gather opinions on these aspects to help identify areas of improvement and enhance student experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by reading the introduction page, which provides a brief context for the survey.
2. On the "Overall Satisfaction" page, rate your satisfaction with the school on a scale from 1 to 10.
3. On the "Teachers" page, rate your satisfaction with the teachers on a scale from 1 to 10.
4. On the "Facility" page, rate your satisfaction with the school facilities on a scale from 1 to 10.
5. On the "Main Reason" page, select the main reason that contributed to your overall satisfaction with the school from the available options.
6. On the "Comments" page, provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the school.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (1, note, not required): This page provides a brief introduction to the survey, explaining its purpose and the types of questions you will be answering.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (2, number, not required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the school on a scale from 1 to 10. A higher score indicates higher satisfaction.
* **Teachers** (3, number, not required): Rate your satisfaction with the teachers on a scale from 1 to 10. A higher score indicates higher satisfaction.
* **Facility** (4, number, not required): Rate your satisfaction with the school facilities on a scale from 1 to 10. A higher score indicates higher satisfaction.
* **Main Reason** (5, select multiple, not required): Select the main reason that contributed to your overall satisfaction with the school. You can choose one or more of the available options.
* **Comments** (6, note, not required): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the school. This is an optional field, but it will help us understand your thoughts and concerns.

## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* You can skip any questions that do not apply to you.
* Take as much time as you need to complete the survey.
* If you have any comments or feedback, please feel free to share them with us.
