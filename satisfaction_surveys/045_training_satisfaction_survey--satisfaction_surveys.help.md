# Training Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the effectiveness of training programs, aiming to improve the quality of our training content, materials, and overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply answer the questions honestly and accurately, and follow the instructions below:

* Select one option for each question, choosing the best description that fits your experience with the training.
* If you have any additional feedback or comments, you can type them in the "Additional feedback" field.
* You can also provide any additional tools or equipment that were assigned to you during the training.
* Finally, indicate whether you would take this training again and provide any further feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How satisfied are you with the training** (`training_satisfaction`): Please select one of the following options: Excellent, Very good, Good, Fair, or Poor. This question helps us understand your overall satisfaction with the training.
* **How engaging was the training content** (`training_content`): Please select one of the following options: Excellent, Very good, Good, Fair, or Poor. This question helps us evaluate the quality of the training content.
* **How good were the training materials** (`training_materials`): Please select one of the following options: Excellent, Very good, Good, Fair, or Poor. This question helps us assess the quality of the training materials provided.
* **How well did the trainer know the subject** (`trainer_knowledge`): Please select one of the following options: Excellent, Very good, Good, Fair, or Poor. This question helps us evaluate the expertise of the trainer.
* **How good was the training environment** (`training_environment`): Please select one of the following options: Excellent, Very good, Good, Fair, or Poor. This question helps us assess the quality of the training environment.
* **Overall training satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`): Please select one of the following options: Excellent, Very good, Good, Fair, or Poor. This question helps us evaluate your overall satisfaction with the training.
* **Would you take this training again** (`would_take_again`): Please select "True" if you would take this training again and "False" otherwise. This question helps us determine the effectiveness of the training.
* **Additional feedback** (`feedback`): If you have any additional feedback or comments, you can type them in this field. This is an opportunity to provide more detailed feedback about your experience.
* **Assigned tool(s)** (`assigned_tool` and `assigned_tool2`): If any tools or equipment were assigned to you during the training, please list them here. This helps us track the resources used during the training.
* **Additional feedback** (optional): If you have any further comments or suggestions, you can type them in this field.
