# health_product_satisfaction_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The health_product_satisfaction_survey is a form designed to collect customer feedback on their experiences with our health products. Fill this form to share your thoughts, suggestions, or concerns about our products.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have a genuine issue or suggestion related to a particular product.
2. Take a moment to review our other feedback channels.
3. Fill in the required and optional fields, as indicated below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **health_product_satisfaction_survey (Introduction)** (`introduction`, note, required: false): This is a short message to introduce yourself and explain the purpose of your feedback. Feel free to share your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **product_name (product_name)** (`product_name`, text, required: false): Type the name of the product you are providing feedback about.
* **product_rating (product_rating)** (`product_rating`, number, required: false): Rate your experience with the product, using a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **suggestion (suggestion)** (`suggestion`, text, required: false): Share any suggestions or ideas you have for improving our products or services.
* **rating_comment (rating_comment)** (`rating_comment`, text, required: false): Provide more details about your product rating, such as what you liked or disliked about the product.
* **Contact Us (contact_us)** (`contact_us`, email, required: false): Leave your email address so we can contact you for more information or clarification.
* **Contact Number (contact_number)** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): If you have a contact number, feel free to share it with us, so we can follow up if needed.

Please note that all fields are optional unless marked as "required". If you have any concerns or questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
