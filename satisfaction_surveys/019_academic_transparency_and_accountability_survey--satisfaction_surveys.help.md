# Academic Transparency And Accountability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Transparency And Accountability Survey is a tool designed to gather feedback from individuals in academic settings, focusing on measuring transparency, accountability, and satisfaction with decision-making processes. This guide will walk you through each section and field to help you complete the form effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your primary role within the institution (e.g., student, faculty member, staff).
2. Choose the department or division you belong to.
3. Rate your perception of how clearly decisions are communicated to you.
4. Evaluate the level of faculty involvement in strategic decision-making.
5. Assess how well students are represented in governance.
6. Assess the transparency of the institution's budget process.
7. Rate the fairness of resource allocation across departments.
8. Express your confidence in the institution's whistleblower protection policies.
9. (Optional) Suggest one way to improve transparency within the institution.
10. List any specific concerns you have regarding accountability.
11. Indicate your interest in participating in a focus group on this topic.
