# Operations Transformation Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Operations Transformation Feedback Survey is designed to gather feedback from employees on the operations transformation process. This survey aims to collect suggestions, challenges, and outcomes experienced by employees during the transformation process. The survey will help identify areas of improvement and provide insights for future transformations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. If a question is not applicable, please mark it as "Not Applicable" or leave it blank.
3. Select all relevant options for questions with multiple choices.
4. Ensure that you have filled out all required fields (if any).
5. Once you have completed the form, submit it for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What suggestions do you have to improve operations** (`employee_suggestions`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This field is an open-ended question that allows you to provide any suggestions or ideas you have for improving operations. Please write your thoughts and ideas here.
* **What challenges have you faced during the transformation** (`challenges_faced`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This field is for you to describe any challenges you faced during the transformation process. Be as detailed as possible to help us understand your experience.
* **What outcomes have you achieved during the transformation** (`outcomes`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This field is for you to describe any positive outcomes or results you have achieved during the transformation process. Please be specific and provide examples.
* **What areas of improvement do you see for future transformations** (`improvements`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This field is for you to suggest areas that could be improved for future transformations. Be as specific as possible to help us improve our processes.
* **Overall, how satisfied are you with the transformation** (`overall_satisfaction`, `number`, required: false)
	+ This field is a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest level of satisfaction. Please rate your overall satisfaction with the transformation process.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false)
	+ This field is for your email address. Please enter your email address if you want us to contact you for further clarification or discussion.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This field is for your phone number. Please enter your phone number if you want us to contact you for further discussion.
* **Time of day** (`time_of_day`, `time`, required: false)
	+ This field is to record the time of day you completed the survey. This is an optional field and will not affect your feedback.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false)
	+ This field is to record your date of birth. This is an optional field and will not affect your feedback.
* **Date of survey submission** (`date_of_survey_submission`, `date`, required: false)
	+ This field is to record the date and time you submitted the survey. This is an automated field and will be filled out by the system.
* **Which transformation initiatives have you experienced** (`transformation_initiatives`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ Select all initiatives that you have experienced during the transformation process. You can select multiple options.
* **How were outcomes measured** (`outcomes_measured`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select how outcomes were measured during the transformation process. You can only select one option.
* **How were challenges measured** (`challenges_measured`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select how challenges were measured during the transformation process. You can only select one option.
* **What suggestions do you have for improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This field is an open-ended question that allows you to provide any suggestions or ideas you have for improvement. Please write your thoughts and ideas here.
* **Overall satisfaction level** (`overall_satisfaction_level`, `number`, required: false)
	+ This field is a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest level of satisfaction. Please rate your overall satisfaction with the transformation process.
* **Operations team** (`operations_team`, `text`, required: false)

* **HR team** (`hr_team`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This field is for you to describe your experience with the HR team. If you have any positive or negative feedback, please provide it here.

	+ This field is for you to describe your experience with other departments. If you have any positive or negative feedback, please provide it here.
