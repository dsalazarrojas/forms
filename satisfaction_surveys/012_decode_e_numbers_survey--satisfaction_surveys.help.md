# Decode E Numbers Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey aimed at gathering information about how you perceive and interact with E-Numbers on products.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the page that best fits your current situation, or simply navigate through the pages to answer all questions.
2. Fill out the form by answering each page according to the instructions provided.
3. If you have any concerns or questions, please contact the survey administrators.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **E-Numbers Familiarity** (`e_numbers_familiarity`, select_one, required): Please select the E-Numbers you are familiar with. This will help us understand your level of knowledge about E-Numbers.
	+ Select one or more of the following E-Numbers: E-Number 1, E-Number 2, E-Number 3, E-Number 4, E-Number 5, E-Number 6, E-Number 7, E-Number 8.
* **Awareness Of E-Numbers** (`awareness`, select_one, required): This question gauges your level of awareness about E-Numbers. Please select the best option that describes your current awareness.
	+ Not at all aware
	+ Somewhat aware
	+ Aware
	+ Very aware
* **Concerns About E-Numbers** (`concerns`, select_multiple, required): We'd like to know which concerns you have about E-Numbers. Select all that apply.
	+ Health concerns
	+ Allergens concerns
	+ Environmental concerns
	+ Other
* **Behaviors When Seeing E-Numbers** (`behaviors`, select_multiple, optional): Please indicate what you typically do when you see E-Numbers on a product.
	+ Ignore
	+ Check the ingredients
	+ Look for alternative products
	+ Avoid
	+ Other
* **Country** (`country`, select_one, required): Please select your country of residence.
	+ United Kingdom
	+ Ireland
	+ United States
	+ Other
* **Age** (`age`, number, required): Please provide your age.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, select_one, optional): What is your highest educational level achieved?
	+ Secondary
	+ Tertiary
	+ University
	+ Other
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Please provide any additional comments or thoughts about E-Numbers.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, optional): Please provide your email address if you'd like to be contacted about your participation in this survey.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Please provide your phone number if you'd like to be contacted about your participation in this survey.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, select_one, required): Please confirm your email address.
	+ True
	+ False
* **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, select_one, required): Please confirm your phone number.
	+ True
	+ False

## Tips
* Please answer truthfully and carefully to provide accurate and useful data.
* If you're unsure about any question, it's okay to skip it or ask for help.
* If you'd like to be contacted for more information or clarification, please fill out the email and phone fields.
* Thank you for participating in this survey!
