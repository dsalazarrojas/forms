### Expense Workflow Efficiency Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to gather feedback on the efficiency of the expense workflow process within the organization. It is meant to be completed by users who have participated in or observed the expense process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Click on each page to access the text fields.
2. Answer each question to provide your feedback.
3. Scroll down to the next page to continue providing feedback.
4. When finished, click submit to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
Here is a detailed explanation of each field:

* **Page 1**: This page is the starting point for the survey. It is the first opportunity to provide feedback on the expense workflow process.
* **Page 2**: This page continues the survey, gathering more information about the process.
* **Page 3**: This page is the next step in the survey, where you can provide more detailed feedback.
* **Page 4**: This is the final page, where you can summarize your overall experience and provide any additional comments.

## Tips
* Take your time to answer each question carefully, as the feedback provided will help improve the expense workflow process.
* If you have any comments or concerns that are not mentioned in the survey, please include them in the final text field on Page 4.
* Your feedback is anonymous, and any information provided will be kept confidential.
