# facilitator_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "facilitator_evaluation_form" is a satisfaction survey used to evaluate the performance of facilitators in events, meetings, or workshops. This form helps assess the facilitator's communication skills, overall satisfaction, and other aspects of their performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and the facilitator's name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter the date and time of the session.
3. Rate the facilitator's communication skills using the provided scale.
4. Provide feedback on the facilitator's performance using the text fields.
5. Submit the form to provide your evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant_name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the participant of the session.
* **facilitator_name** (`facilitator_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the facilitator who led the session.
* **session_date** (`session_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the session.
* **session_time** (`session_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the session.
* **overall_satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the facilitator's performance on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **communication_skills** (`communication_skills`, text, optional): Provide feedback on the facilitator's communication skills.
