# Residential Soundproofing Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from homeowners or clients about their satisfaction with soundproofing services provided by contractors. Your input will help us improve our services and provide better experiences for future clients.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below.

1. **Residential Soundproofing Satisfaction Survey** (Page 1): Type in the name of the project as it is described in your contract or correspondence with the contractor.
2.  **Contractor** (Select One - Page 1): Select the name of the contractor who performed the soundproofing work on your project.
3.  **Contractor Phone Number** (Text - Page 1): Enter the phone number of the contractor, if you have it.
4.  **Contractor Email** (Email - Page 1): Enter the email address of the contractor, if you have it.
5.  **Project Location** (Text - Page 1): Type in the location where the soundproofing work was done (e.g., your home or building address).
6.  **Project Duration** (Select One - Page 1): Choose the duration of the project (less than 1 week, 1 to 4 weeks, or more than 4 weeks).
7.  **Overall Satisfaction** (Number - Page 1): Rate your overall satisfaction with the service on a scale of 1 to 5.
8.  **Specific Issues** (Text - Page 1): Describe any issues or problems you experienced with the project, if any.
9.  **Recommendations** (Text - Page 1): Share any suggestions or ideas for improvement.
10. **Contractor Comments** (Text - Page 1): Add any additional comments or thoughts about the contractor or their service.
11. **Contractor Rating** (Select One - Page 1): Rate the contractor's performance on a scale of 1 to 5.
