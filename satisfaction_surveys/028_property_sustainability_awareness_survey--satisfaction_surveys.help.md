# property_sustainability_awareness_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about the awareness and knowledge of sustainability features and barriers related to properties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the prompts for each question and select the options that best fit your responses.
