# Event Equipment Vendor Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from vendors who have provided equipment for events, helping us to gauge their satisfaction with the event and the support team.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Introduction**: Please read the introduction for context and understanding of the purpose of this form.
2. Fill out the form by providing the requested information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of your company or organization that provided the equipment for the event.
* **Vendor Contact Information** (`vendor_contact_info`, text, required): Please enter your contact information so we can get in touch with you after this form is submitted.
* **Satisfaction with Vendor** (`satisfaction_with_vendor`, number, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the vendor (e.g. 1-5).
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the event (e.g. 1-5).
* **Communication with Support Team** (`communication_with_support_team`, select_one, required): Rate your experience with our support team (e.g. Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Would Recommend Vendor** (`would_recommend_vendor`, select_multiple, required): Indicate if you would recommend this vendor to others (Yes or No).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or feedback, please provide them here.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required): Rate your overall experience with the event (e.g. 1-5).
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): You can leave this field blank if you don't want to provide a specific date.
* **Vendor Email** (`vendor_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you after this form is submitted.
* **Vendor Phone** (`vendor_phone`, text, optional): If you want to provide your phone number for follow-up, you can do so here.
* **Vendor Address** (`vendor_address`, text, required): Enter your company's address so we can get in touch with you after this form is submitted.
* **Vendor Website** (`vendor_website`, text, optional): If you have a website, you can provide it here for our reference.
