# Handbag Sewing Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gauge the interest of users in handbag sewing, specifically their preferences for frequency of attendance in workshops, techniques they wish to learn, schedule preferences, and any additional comments or notes they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below for each page:

Page 1: User Details
*   **User Details (text)**: Please enter your name, email, or any other details you wish to share.
Page 2: What is your workshop frequency?
*   **Workshop Frequency (select_one)**: Please select your frequency of attendance in workshops: Once a month, 2-3 times a month, Weekly, or Daily.
Page 3: What techniques do you want to learn?
*   **What techniques do you want to learn? (select_multiple)**: Choose from handbag design, pattern making, bag construction, embellishment, or pattern alteration.
Page 4: When do you prefer to schedule your classes?
*   **When do you prefer to schedule your classes? (select_multiple)**: Choose from Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.
Page 5: Additional Comments or Notes
*   **Additional Comments or Notes (note)**: You may provide any additional comments or notes that you wish to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **User Details**: This field is for any additional details about the user.
*   **What is your workshop frequency?**: This field is for selecting the frequency of attendance in workshops.
*   **What techniques do you want to learn?**: This field is for selecting the techniques you wish to learn in handbag sewing.
*   **When do you prefer to schedule your classes?**: This field is for selecting your schedule preference for classes.
*   **Additional Comments or Notes**: This field is for providing any additional comments or notes that you may have.

## Tips
Please be as accurate and specific as possible in your answers. Your input will help us understand your needs better and cater to them in future workshops.
