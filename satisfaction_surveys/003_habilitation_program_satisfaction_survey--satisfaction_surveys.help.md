# habilitation_program_satisfaction_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect satisfaction feedback from participants about our habilitation program. Please provide your honest opinion on the quality of our program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the first page to begin.
2. For each question, select an answer from the provided options.
3. If a question is optional, you can choose to leave it blank.
4. When you're finished, click submit to send your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How do you feel about your instructor?** (`instructor_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Select a rating from "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor" to indicate your level of satisfaction with your instructor.
* **What do you think about our facilities?** (`facility_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Choose from "Very Good", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor" to rate our facilities.
* **What is your overall satisfaction?** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction as "Very Satisfied", "Somewhat Satisfied", "Neutral", "Somewhat Unsatisfied", or "Very Unsatisfied".
* **How do you feel about our program?** (`program_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Choose "Very Satisfied", "Somewhat Satisfied", "Neutral", "Somewhat Unsatisfied", or "Very Unsatisfied" to rate our program.
* **Do you have any other feedback for us?** (`feedback`, note, optional): Write a message with any additional feedback or comments about our program.
* **How would you like to be contacted?** (`contact_info`, select_one, optional): Select how you would like to be contacted (phone, email, or both).
* **Your Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you selected "Email" as your preferred contact method.
* **Your Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you selected "Phone" or "Both" as your preferred contact method.

## Tips
- Please be honest and thorough in your responses.
- If you need help or clarification, feel free to contact your administrator or program leader.
- Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
