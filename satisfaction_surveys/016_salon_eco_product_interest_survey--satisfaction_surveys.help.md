# Salon Eco Product Interest Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Salon Eco Product Interest Survey is a form used to gather information about individuals interested in learning more about our salon products. This form helps us understand who is interested, what they are interested in, and what they are willing to consider. The data collected will help us tailor our marketing efforts and product offerings to better serve our customers.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions about your relationship with our salon.
2. Indicate which product categories you are interested in learning more about.
3. Select the date you are interested in learning more about these products.
4. Provide your contact information (phone number, email address, address, etc.).
5. Share any additional comments or notes about our products.
6. Agree or disagree with the terms presented. If you choose "No", please explain why in the text field provided.
7. Select "Other" and specify other terms you do not agree with.
8. Share additional comments if you choose "Other" for terms you do not agree with.
9. Finally, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your relationship with our salon? (1)** 
    This question helps us understand how you connect with our salon. Select all that apply: "Yes" if you are a customer, "No" if you are not.
* **What product category are you interested in? (2)** 
    This question helps us tailor our marketing efforts and product offerings to your interests. Select all that apply: "Yes" if you are interested, "No" if not.
* **When are you interested in learning more about these products? (3)** 
    This question helps us plan our communication and outreach efforts. Select a date on the calendar to indicate when you are interested in learning more about these products.
* **What is your phone number? (4)** 
    This information helps us get in touch with you to discuss your interests and questions.
* **What is your email address? (5)** 
    This information helps us send you relevant marketing messages and respond to your questions.
* **Do you have any notes about our products? (6)** 
    This field is for any additional comments or thoughts you may have about our products.
* **Name (7)** 
    This is your name for the purposes of follow-up communication.
* **Company (8)** 
    If you represent a company, provide the name here.
* **Job Title (9)** 
    If you represent a company, provide your job title here.
* **Address (10)** 
    Provide your physical address so we can send you information in the mail.
* **City (11)** 
    Provide the city where you reside or work.
* **State (12)** 
    Provide the state where you reside or work.
* **Zip (13)** 
    Provide your zip code for accurate mailing purposes.
* **Country (14)** 
    Provide the country where you reside or work.
* **Comments (15)** 
    This field is for any additional comments or questions you may have.
* **I agree with these terms (16)** 
    Select "Yes" if you agree with the terms and conditions of our product offerings.
* **I do not agree with these terms (17)** 
    Select "No" if you do not agree with the terms and conditions of our product offerings.
* **Undecided (18)** 
    Select "Yes" if you are not sure about agreeing with the terms.
* **Other (19)** 
    Select "Yes" if you do not agree with some but not all of the terms.
* **Other (please specify) (20)** 
    If you select "Yes" for "Other", specify which terms you do not agree with.
* **Please specify other terms that you do not agree with (21)** 
    Provide additional comments about why you do not agree with the terms you selected as "Other".
* **Other (please specify) (22)** 
    If you selected "Other", provide more detail about which terms you do not agree with.
* **Please explain why you do not agree with these terms (23)** 
    Provide a detailed explanation of why you do not agree with the terms.
* **Other (please specify) (24)** 
    If you selected "Other", specify which terms you do not agree with.
* **Form submitted by (25)** 
    This field is for display purposes only and will be automatically populated by the user submitting the form.

Please let us know if you have any questions or concerns. Thank you for taking the time to complete this form.
