# Wildlife Reserve Visitor Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and insights from visitors about their experiences at the wildlife reserve. Your input will help us understand how the reserve's efforts are impacting the visitor experience and inform future improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your visitor name in the "Visitor Name" field.
3. Select the area of the wildlife reserve you visited from the list of options.
4. Indicate your visit frequency using the available options.
5. Enter the amount of time you spent in the wildlife reserve.
6. Choose how the wildlife reserve impacted you (e.g., did it positively or negatively).
7. Provide your contact information (email or phone number) so we can follow up with any questions or for future communications.
8. Optionally, include any additional comments or feedback about your visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Visitor Name** (text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **wildlife-area-visited** (select_one, required): Select the specific area of the wildlife reserve you visited, such as "Main Loop Trail" or "Wildlife Viewing Platform".
* **Visit Frequency** (select_multiple, required): Choose how often you visit the wildlife reserve, e.g., "First Time", "Regularly", "Several Times", or "Never".
* **Time Spent in Park** (time, optional): Enter the amount of time you spent in the wildlife reserve in hours, e.g., "2 hours" or "5 hours".
* **Impact on Wildlife** (select_one, required): Choose how the wildlife reserve impacted you, e.g., "Positive Impact", "Negative Impact", "No Impact", or "Neutral".
* **Contact Information** (text, optional): Enter your email or phone number so we can follow up with any questions or for future communications.
* **Additional Comments** (note, optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments about your visit.
* **How likely are you to visit this park again?** (select_multiple, required): Choose how likely you are to visit the wildlife reserve again, e.g., "Very Likely", "Somewhat Likely", "Not Likely", or "Not at All".
