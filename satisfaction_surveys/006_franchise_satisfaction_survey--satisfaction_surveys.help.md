# Franchise Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is intended to help users understand the purpose and requirements of the Franchise Satisfaction Survey form.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined in the guide above.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Franchise Name**: Enter the name of your franchise.
* **How often do you contact your regional manager?**: Select how often you contact your regional manager.
* **How satisfied are you with your franchise relationship?**: Select the options that represent your level of satisfaction.
* **Which area of our organization do you feel is most lacking?**: Select the options that represent the area that is most lacking.
* **Date**: If you want to provide the date when you're completing the survey.
* **Time**: If you want to provide the time when you're completing the survey.
* **Note**: Add any additional comments or information about your experience with the franchise.
