# Parent Stress And Burnout Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parent Stress And Burnout Survey is designed to collect information about parent stress and burnout levels, coping strategies, self-care habits, support systems, and burnout symptoms.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
2. The form is divided into different sections, each focusing on a different aspect of parent stress and burnout.
3. For each question, please choose the best answer according to your understanding and feelings.
4. Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Information** (Parent Information, text, optional): This field is used to collect additional information from the parent, but it is not required.
* **How Stressful Do You Feel?** (Stress Level, number, optional): On a scale of 1-10, please indicate how stressed you feel.
* **Stress Coping Strategies** (Stress Coping Strategies, text, optional): Describe your current stress coping strategies.
* **Self Care Habits** (Self Care Habits, text, optional): Describe your self-care habits.
* **Support System** (Support System, text, optional): Describe your support system.
* **Burnout** (Burnout, select_one, required): Please select one of the options provided to indicate if you experience burnout or not.
	+ Burnout is a state of emotional exhaustion and cynicism.
	+ Burnout is not a state of emotional exhaustion and cynicism.
* **Stress Level Scale** (Stress Level Scale, number, optional): On a scale of 1-10, indicate how stressed you feel.
* **Emotional Expression** (Emotional Expression, text, optional): Please provide a description of your emotional expression.
* **Social Connections** (Social Connections, text, optional): Describe your social connections.
* **Self Care Habits** (Self Care Habits, text, optional): Describe your self-care habits.
* **Support System** (Support System, text, optional): Describe your support system.
* **Burnout Symptoms** (Burnout Symptoms, text, optional): Describe any burnout symptoms you are experiencing.
