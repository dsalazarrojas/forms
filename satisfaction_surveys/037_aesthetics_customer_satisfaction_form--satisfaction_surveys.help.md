# Aesthetics Customer Satisfaction Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your feedback about your recent visit to our clinic. Your input will help us understand how we can improve our services and provide better experiences for our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the form carefully, as some fields are required.
2. For select fields, choose the best option that describes your experience.
3. Be as detailed as possible in the "Your Feedback" section.
4. Don't hesitate to provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Visit Information --**: This section is for providing details about your visit, but you can skip it if you don't feel like filling it out.
* **Date of Visit**: This field is to capture the date of your visit to our clinic. Please enter the date in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Service / Treatment Received**: This field is to determine what treatment you received during your visit. Choose the option that best describes the primary service you received.
* **Provider Name**: This field is to acknowledge the staff member who provided your service. However, it's not required.
* **-- Satisfaction Ratings --**: This section is for rating your satisfaction with your visit. Please rate from 1 (Very Dissatisfied) to 5 (Very Satisfied) for each field.
* **Ease of Scheduling**: This field is to rate how easy or difficult you found the booking process.
* **Staff Friendliness**: This field is to rate how friendly and approachable you found the staff to be.
* **Wait Time**: This field is to rate whether your appointment started on time.
* **Satisfaction with Results**: This field is to rate whether you were satisfied with the outcome of your treatment.
* **Facility Cleanliness**: This field is to rate how clean and well-maintained you found our facility to be.
* **-- Your Feedback --**: This section is for providing any additional feedback or comments you may have.
* **What did you like most?**: This field is for telling us what you enjoyed most about your visit.
* **What could we improve?**: This field is for suggesting areas where we can improve.
* **How likely are you to recommend us?**: This field is to rate how likely you are to recommend our clinic to others.
* **Can we use your feedback as a testimonial?**: This field is to ask if you give us permission to use your feedback as a testimonial. 

Note: The clinic will use your first name only if you give us permission.
