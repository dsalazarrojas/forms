# Auditee Satisfaction Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from auditees about their experience with the audit process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Provide honest and detailed responses to each question.
3. Use the available options for select-one and select multiple questions.
4. For the "Suggestions for Improvement" field, provide as many suggestions as you can think of.
5. Make sure to fill out all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (*) on the right side of the field label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, `*`): Enter the date this survey is being completed.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, `*`): Enter the name of the organization audited.
* **Respondent Name** (`respondent_name`, text, `*`): Enter your name, the person completing the survey.
* **Respondent Title/Position** (`respondent_title`, text, optional): Enter your title or position within the organization.
* **Audit Date** (`audit_date`, date, `*`): Enter the date the audit was conducted.
* **Type of Audit Received** (`audit_type`, select_one, `*`): Choose the type of audit that was conducted (Financial, Operational, Compliance, Internal, or External).
* **Auditor Professionalism** (`auditor_professionalism`, select_one, `*`): Rate the professionalism of the audit team (Very Unprofessional, Unprofessional, Neutral, Professional, or Very Professional).
* **Auditor Competence and Expertise** (`auditor_competence`, select_one, `*`): Rate the technical knowledge of the audit team (Very Incompetent, Incompetent, Adequate, Competent, or Very Competent).
* **Fairness of Audit Process** (`audit_fairness`, select_one, `*`): Rate the fairness of the audit process (Very Unfair, Unfair, Neutral, Fair, or Very Fair).
* **Respect and Courtesy Shown** (`respect_shown`, select_one, `*`): Rate the respect and courtesy shown during the audit (Very Disrespectful, Disrespectful, Neutral, Respectful, or Very Respectful).
* **Communication Effectiveness** (`communication_effectiveness`, select_one, `*`): Rate the effectiveness of communication from the audit team (Very Ineffective, Ineffective, Neutral, Effective, or Very Effective).
* **Appropriateness of Findings** (`findings_appropriateness`, select_one, `*`): Rate the appropriateness of the findings (Very Inappropriate, Inappropriate, Somewhat Appropriate, Appropriate, or Very Appropriate).
* **Usefulness of Recommendations** (`recommendations_usefulness`, select_one, `*`): Rate the usefulness of the recommendations (Not Useful, Slightly Useful, Moderately Useful, Useful, or Very Useful).
* **Level of Disruption Caused** (`audit_disruption`, select_one, `*`): Rate the level of disruption caused by the audit (Severely Disruptive, Very Disruptive, Moderately Disruptive, Minimally Disruptive, or No Disruption).
* **Appropriateness of Audit Duration** (`audit_duration`, select_one, `*`): Rate the appropriateness of the audit duration (Much Too Long, Too Long, About Right, Too Short, or Much Too Short).
* **Auditor Time Management** (`time_efficiency`, select_one, `*`): Rate the efficiency of the auditor's time management (Very Inefficient, Inefficient, Neutral, Efficient, or Very Efficient).
* **Value Received for Cost** (`value_for_cost`, select_one, optional): Rate the value received for the cost (Very Poor Value, Poor Value, Average Value, Good Value, or Excellent Value).
* **Overall Satisfaction with Audit** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, `*`): Rate your overall satisfaction with the audit (Very Unsatisfied, Unsatisfied, Neutral, Satisfied, or Very Satisfied).
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, optional): Provide as many suggestions as you can think of for improvement.
* **Would Use This Auditor Again** (`would_use_again`, select_one, `*`): Rate your willingness to use this auditor again (Definitely Not, Probably Not, Unsure, Probably Yes, or Definitely Yes).
* **Would Recommend to Others** (`would_recommend`, select_one, optional): Rate your willingness to recommend this auditor to others (Definitely Not, Probably Not, Unsure, Probably Yes, or Definitely Yes).

## Tips
* Be honest and provide detailed responses to each question.
* Use the available options for select-one and select multiple questions.
* For the "Suggestions for Improvement" field, provide as many suggestions as you can think of.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
