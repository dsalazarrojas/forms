# Childhood Celebration Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Childhood Celebration Survey is designed to collect memories and reflections about your childhood celebrations. Your responses will help us understand how children experience and enjoy celebrations, and inform ways to create more enjoyable and meaningful celebrations for children.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question honestly and from your perspective.
2. Be as detailed as you like in your answers.
3. Rate questions with numbers where applicable.
4. Make sure to save your progress and submit the form when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **About You --** (`respondent_info`)
	+ This section is where you can briefly introduce yourself.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, required)
	+ Please enter your first name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, required)
	+ Enter your last name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Email Address** (`email`, required)
	+ Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Your Age Group** (`your_age_group`, required)
	+ Select your age group from 20-30, 31-40, 41-50, 51-60, or 60+.
* **Event Memories** (`event_memories`, optional)
	+ Share as much as you like about your favorite childhood celebration memories.
* **What is your favorite childhood celebration memory?** (`favorite_childhood_event`, required)
	+ Describe a memorable celebration or party from your childhood.
* **Type of Celebration** (`event_type`, required)
	+ Select the type of celebration this was (Birthday Party, Holiday Celebration, School Event, etc.).
* **How old were you during this memory?** (`event_age`, optional)
	+ Enter your approximate age during the celebration.
* **Approximate date of the event** (`event_date`, optional)
	+ Enter the approximate date of the celebration.
* **Where did the celebration take place?** (`location`, optional)
	+ Describe the location of the celebration.
* **About the Organizer** (`organizer_info`, optional)
	+ Share any information about the person or group that organized the celebration.
* **Who organized this celebration?** (`who_organized`, required)
	+ Select who organized the celebration (Parent, Grandparent, Other Family, etc.).
* **How much effort do you think went into organizing this event?** (`organizer_effort`, required)
	+ Rate the effort put into organizing the celebration (1-5).
* **Satisfaction and Details** (`satisfaction_section`, optional)
	+ Share any further thoughts or details about your satisfaction with the celebration.
* **How satisfied were you with the celebration?** (`satisfaction_level`, required)
	+ Rate your satisfaction level with the celebration (1-5).
* **What was the best part of the celebration?** (`best_part`, optional)
	+ Share what made the celebration enjoyable for you.
* **How important were the decorations?** (`decorations`, optional)
	+ Rate the importance of decorations to the celebration (1-5).
* **Do you remember any special food from the celebration?** (`food_memories`, optional)
	+ Share any memorable food from the celebration.
* **Ideas for Modern Celebrations** (`ideas_section`, optional)
	+ Share ideas you would include in a similar celebration today.
* **Would you include technology in modern celebrations?** (`tech_integration`, optional)
	+ Select whether you would include technology in modern celebrations.
* **May we share your memory in our collection?** (`share_permission`, required)
	+ Select whether you're willing to share your memory with us.
