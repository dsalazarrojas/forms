# Green Waste Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Green Waste Management Survey collects information on recycling practices, organic waste management, and waste collection and recycling efforts. This data will be used for internal analysis or reporting purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select or enter the required information for each field. Each field has a label that describes what it's for, and if you're unsure, you can refer to the field-by-field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Recycling Practices
* **Recycling Practices** (`green_waste_management_survey`, text, optional)
	+ Please select or describe your current recycling practices, such as regular recycling, composting, or other.

### 2. Organic Waste
* **Organic Waste** (`organic_waste_management`, text, optional)
	+ Describe your approach to managing organic waste, like food waste, yard waste, or other organic materials.

### 3. Green Waste Collected
* **Green Waste Collected** (`green_waste_collected`, number, optional)
	+ Enter the amount of green waste (e.g., paper, glass, metal) collected in your household or organization.

### 4. Green Waste Recycled
* **Green Waste Recycled** (`green_waste_recycled`, number, optional)
	+ Enter the amount of green waste (e.g., paper, glass, metal) that has been recycled.

### 5. Organic Waste Collected
* **Organic Waste Collected** (`organic_waste_collected`, number, optional)
	+ Enter the amount of organic waste (e.g., food waste, yard waste) collected.

### 6. Organic Waste Recycled
* **Organic Waste Recycled** (`organic_waste_recycled`, number, optional)
	+ Enter the amount of organic waste (e.g., food waste, yard waste) that has been recycled.

### 7. Awareness
* **Awareness** (`recycling_practices_awareness`, select_one, optional)
	+ Select one of the following levels of awareness:
		- 1: Very little
		- 2: Somewhat aware
		- 3: Aware
		- 4: Very aware

### 8. Other
* **Other** (`recycling_practices_awareness_other`, text, optional)
	+ If your level of awareness is not listed, describe it here. This field is for additional information or clarification.
