# Faith Based Youth Engagement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Faith Based Youth Engagement Survey" is a survey designed to gather feedback from youth about their relationship with their faith, their engagement with our ministry, and their participation in church activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your Youth ID (if provided).
2. Enter your Name.
3. Enter your Age.
4. Provide your Email address (if applicable).
5. Enter your Phone number (if applicable).
6. Answer the questions regarding your relationship with God, what it means to have faith, how you use your faith to guide your life, and your level of engagement with our ministry.
7. Indicate how often you attend a church service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Youth ID (number, optional)**: If you have a unique identifier for the youth program, please enter it here.
* **Name (text, optional)**: Enter your name to help us identify you in our records.
* **Age (number, optional)**: Please enter your age to help us understand your demographic.
* **Email (email, optional)**: If you have an email address associated with our ministry, please enter it here.
* **Phone (text, optional)**: If you have a phone number associated with our ministry, please enter it here.
* **How do you feel about your current relationship with God? (select_one, optional)**: Answer this question by selecting one of the following options:
	+ Closely related
	+ Somewhat related
	+ Not related
	+ Don't know
* **What does it mean to have faith? (text, optional)**: Describe what faith means to you.
* **How do you currently use your faith to guide your life? (select_multiple, optional)**: Answer this question by selecting one or more of the following options:
	+ Prayer and meditation
	+ Reading and study
	+ Service and mission
	+ Other (please specify)
* **How do you rate your current level of engagement with our ministry? (select_one, optional)**: Answer this question by selecting one of the following options:
	+ Highly engaged
	+ Somewhat engaged
	+ Not engaged
	+ Don't know
* **How often do you attend a church service? (select_multiple, optional)**: Answer this question by selecting one or more of the following options:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Rarely
	+ Never

## Tips

* Please answer these questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* This survey is designed to gather feedback and improve our outreach efforts. Your input is valuable to us!
