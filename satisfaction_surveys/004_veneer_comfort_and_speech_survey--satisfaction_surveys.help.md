# veneer_comfort_and_speech_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the comfort and speech outcomes of veneer treatments. It is used by dentists to gauge the effectiveness of their treatments and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  The first page of the form asks for overall satisfaction with the treatment. Select one of the available numbers to indicate your level of satisfaction.
2.  Next, you will be asked about your comfort level with the treatment. Select one of the options: "Pain", "Okay", "Uncomfortable".
3.  If you had any issues during the treatment, please list them below in the "Sensitivity Issues" field.
4.  If you have experienced any changes in your speech as a result of the treatment, please describe them in the "Speech Changes" field.
5.  Then, you will be asked about the clarity of your speech after the treatment. You can enter a numerical value to indicate your level of clarity.
6.  You will also be asked for the overall satisfaction level of your speech after the treatment, but this is not required.
7.  Next, you will be asked about the type of veneer treatment you received. Select one of the available options: "Anterior", "Posterior", "Full Molar", "Full Metal Crown".
8.  If you would like to estimate the frequency of your speech after the treatment, you can enter your response in the "Speech Frequency" field.
9.  You will also be asked for a follow-up appointment date, but this is not required.
10.  Please enter the name of the dentist who performed the treatment.
11.  Finally, you will be asked for your name and the date of the treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **overall_satisfaction** (Required): Select one of the available numbers to indicate your overall satisfaction with the treatment.
* **comfort_level** (Required): Select one of the options: "Pain", "Okay", "Uncomfortable" to indicate your comfort level with the treatment.
* **sensitivity_issues** (Required): If you had any issues during the treatment, list them below.
* **speech_changes** (Required): Describe any changes you have experienced in your speech as a result of the treatment.
* **speech_clarity** (Optional): Enter a numerical value to indicate your level of speech clarity after the treatment.
* **overall_satisfaction_level** (Optional): If desired, enter your overall satisfaction level of your speech after the treatment.
* **veneer_type** (Required): Select one of the available options: "Anterior", "Posterior", "Full Molar", "Full Metal Crown" to indicate the type of veneer treatment you received.
* **speech_frequency** (Optional): Estimate the frequency of your speech after the treatment.
* **follow_up_appointment** (Optional): Enter the date of your follow-up appointment.
* **dentist_name** (Optional): Enter the name of the dentist who performed the treatment.
* **patient_name** (Optional): Enter your name.
* **date_of_treatment** (Required): Enter the date of the treatment.
