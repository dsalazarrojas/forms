# Library Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Library Satisfaction Survey is designed for users to provide feedback and express their opinions about our library. We use this data to identify areas where we can improve our services and resources.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions that you have an opinion or experience about.
2. For select_one and select_multiple questions, select the option that best represents your opinion or experience.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to type them in the text fields.
4. Once you have filled out all the questions, submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction** (`page1`, text, optional): This is just a brief introduction to the survey. Please take a few minutes to read through the questions before answering.
- **How often do you visit the library?** (`page2`, select_one, optional): This question helps us understand your frequency of visits to our library. Please select one of the options provided:
	- Very often
	- Occasionally
	- Rarely
	- Never
- **What do you enjoy most about our library?** (`page3`, text, optional): This question helps us understand what you value most about our library. Please be as specific as possible in your answer.
- **Have you had any issues with our library?** (`page4`, select_one, optional): This question helps us identify any problems you have encountered with our library. Please select one of the options provided:
	- True
	- False
- **Why do you visit the library?** (`page5`, text, optional): This question helps us understand why you visit our library. Be as specific as possible in your answer.
- **How satisfied are you with our library?** (`page6`, select_one, optional): This question helps us understand your level of satisfaction with our library. Please select one of the options provided:
	- Very satisfied
	- Somewhat satisfied
	- Neutral
	- Somewhat dissatisfied
	- Very dissatisfied
- **How often do you experience problems with our library?** (`page7`, select_one, optional): This question helps us understand if you have experienced problems with our library. Please select one of the options provided:
	- Very often
	- Occasionally
	- Rarely
	- Never
- **What can we do to improve our library?** (`page8`, text, optional): This question invites you to provide any suggestions on how we can improve our library services and resources.
- **How likely are you to recommend our library to a friend or family member?** (`page9`, number, optional): This question helps us understand your willingness to recommend our library. Please type a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is "not likely at all" and 5 is "very likely".
- **How do you use our library resources?** (`page10`, select_multiple, optional): This question helps us understand how you use our library resources. Please select all the options that apply to you:
	- Books
	- Articles
	- Media
	- Other (please specify)
- **What do you value most when visiting our library?** (`page11`, select_multiple, optional): This question helps us understand what you value most when visiting our library. Please select all the options that apply to you:
	- Quiet and relaxing atmosphere
	- Availability of resources
	- Knowledgeable staff
	- Other (please specify)
- **Page12** (`page12`, text, optional): This is a text field for any other thoughts or comments you may have.
- **What is your favorite library service or feature?** (`page13`, text, optional): This question is an opportunity for you to let us know your favorite library service or feature.
- **How would you rate your overall experience with our library?** (`page14`, number, optional): This question invites you to rate your overall experience with our library. Please type a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is "very poor" and 5 is "excellent".
- **Any suggestions for library events or programs?** (`page15`, text, optional): This question allows you to suggest any events or programs you'd like to see us offer at our library.
