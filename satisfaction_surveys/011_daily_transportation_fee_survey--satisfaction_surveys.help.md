# Daily_TransportationFeeSurvey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your daily transportation habits and preferences. It will help us understand your experiences and concerns regarding transportation costs and modes of transportation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and accurately.
2. Use the provided options for select questions.
3. Do not skip any questions, as they are all optional but important for our analysis.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How many days do you commute to work?** (`commute_costs`): Please select the number of days per week that you commute to work.
* **How often do you travel to work?** (`travel_frequency`): Select how often you travel to work from among the provided options.
* **What mode of transportation do you typically use for work?** (`mode_of_transportation`): Choose the modes of transportation you use, selecting all that apply.
* **How would you rate your satisfaction with your commute?** (`satisfaction_level`): Select how satisfied you are with your commute from among the provided options.
* **What are your total weekly transportation costs?** (`total_cost_per_week`): Please enter your total weekly transportation costs in the provided format.
* **Does transportation cost affect your daily activities?** (`transportation_cost_impact`): Select how transportation costs impact your daily activities from among the provided options.
* **How does transportation cost impact your activities?** (`transportation_cost_impact_other`): Please describe in detail how transportation costs affect your daily activities.
* **How many minutes do you spend traveling to and from work?** (`commute_time`): Enter the total minutes you spend commuting to and from work per week.
* **What mode of transportation do you prefer?** (`transportation_mode_preferences`): Select your preferred mode of transportation from among the provided options.
