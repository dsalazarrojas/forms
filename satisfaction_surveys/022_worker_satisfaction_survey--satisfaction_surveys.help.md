# Worker Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Worker Satisfaction Survey is a form designed to gather feedback and opinions from workers on their job satisfaction, work-life balance, and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read the introduction page to understand the context and purpose of the survey.
* Answer the questions honestly, and provide as much detail as possible.
* Select the appropriate response for the "How satisfied are you with your job?" question.
* Rate your work-life balance on a scale of 1-5.
* Provide specific feedback on what we can do to improve the work environment.
* Share any feedback or suggestions you have for your manager.
* Share any feedback or suggestions you have for your department.
* Share any feedback or suggestions you have for your team.
* Provide any suggestions for the company.
* Finally, share any comments or questions you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: A brief overview of the survey and its purpose.
* **How satisfied are you with your job?**: Select one of the options to indicate how satisfied you are with your job.
	+ Very Dissatisfied
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Very Satisfied
* **How would you rate your work-life balance?**: Rate your work-life balance on a scale of 1-5.
	+ 1 (very bad)
	+ 2
	+ 3
	+ 4
	+ 5 (very good)
* **What can we do to improve?**: Provide specific feedback on what we can do to improve the work environment.
* **What feedback do you have for your manager?**: Share any feedback or suggestions you have for your manager.
* **What feedback do you have for your department?**: Share any feedback or suggestions you have for your department.
* **What feedback do you have for your team?**: Share any feedback or suggestions you have for your team.
* **Do you have any suggestions for the company?**: Provide any suggestions you have for the company.
* **Any comments or questions?**: Share any comments or questions you have.

Note: This form is not required, and you can choose to skip any questions or sections that do not apply to you.
