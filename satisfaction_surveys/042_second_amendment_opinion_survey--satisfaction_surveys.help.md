# Second Amendment Opinion Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Second Amendment Opinion Survey is designed to collect opinions on various aspects of gun rights and related policies. The form aims to gather demographic information and opinions on specific topics, allowing users to express their views in a structured and systematic way.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Select your age group from the list provided in the "Age Group" field.
3. Choose your region from the list in the "Region" field.
4. In the "Gun Rights" field, provide a brief note on your thoughts or opinions about gun rights.
5. In the "Safety" field, provide a brief note on your thoughts or opinions about safety related to gun rights.
6. In the "Policy" field, provide a brief note on your thoughts or opinions about gun policies.
7. Select your stance on gun policies from the list provided in the "Gun Policies" field.
8. Select your highest level of education from the list in the "Education Level" field.
9. If you own a firearm, rent a firearm, or do not own a firearm, select the corresponding option in the "Firearm Ownership" field.
10. Finally, click submit to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Age Group**: Select your age group from the options provided.
	+ 18-24: Select if you are between 18 and 24 years old.
	+ 25-34: Select if you are between 25 and 34 years old.
	+ 35-44: Select if you are between 35 and 44 years old.
	+ 45-54: Select if you are between 45 and 54 years old.
	+ 55-64: Select if you are between 55 and 64 years old.
	+ 65 or older: Select if you are 65 years old or older.
* **Region**: Choose your region from the options provided.
	+ Northeast: Select if you live in the Northeast region.
	+ Southwest: Select if you live in the Southwest region.
	+ Midwest: Select if you live in the Midwest region.
	+ West: Select if you live in the West region.
	+ East: Select if you live in the East region.
* **Gun Rights**: Enter a brief note about your thoughts or opinions about gun rights.
* **Safety**: Enter a brief note about your thoughts or opinions about safety related to gun rights.
* **Policy**: Enter a brief note about your thoughts or opinions about gun policies.
* **Gun Policies**: Select your stance on gun policies from the options provided.
	+ Strongly Support: Select if you strongly support gun policies.
	+ Somewhat Support: Select if you somewhat support gun policies.
	+ Somewhat Oppose: Select if you somewhat oppose gun policies.
	+ Strongly Oppose: Select if you strongly oppose gun policies.
* **Education Level**: Select your highest level of education from the options provided.
	+ High School: Select if you have a high school diploma or equivalent.
	+ Some college: Select if you have attended some college but not completed.
	+ Bachelor's: Select if you have a bachelor's degree.
	+ Master's: Select if you have a master's degree.
	+ PhD: Select if you have a doctorate degree.
* **Firearm Ownership**: Select how you own a firearm, if at all.
	+ Own: Select if you own a firearm.
	+ Rent: Select if you rent a firearm.
	+ Borrow: Select if you borrow a firearm.
	+ Other: Select if you own other types of firearms.
