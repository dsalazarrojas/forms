# Star Rating Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Star Rating Feedback Form is designed to gather your opinions and suggestions about your experience with our product or service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on "Start Form" to begin filling out the form.
2. On the first page, you will be asked to rate your experience with a star rating scale.
3. You will then have the opportunity to provide more in-depth comments about your experience.
4. On the second page, you will be asked to provide any additional comments or feedback.
5. You can choose your preferred contact method from the available options or select none at all.
6. Finally, fill out your contact information and name.
7. Press submit to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Star Rating** (1): Rate your experience with our product or service using a 1-5 star scale.
* **Comments** (2): Add more in-depth comments about your experience. Please provide a detailed description of your experience.
* **Additional Comments** (3): If you have any extra feedback or suggestions, add them here.
* **Contact Information** (4): Select one or more ways you would like to be contacted. Please note that providing accurate contact information will allow us to reach out to you directly.
* **Email** (5): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (6): Enter your phone number if you prefer to be contacted over the phone.
* **Name** (7): Provide your name, which will be used to identify you in our communication.
 
## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out all the required fields.
- Provide accurate and up-to-date contact information.
- If you have any issues or concerns about our product or service, use the "Additional Comments" section to let us know.
- If you have questions or need assistance, feel free to contact us directly at [support email].
