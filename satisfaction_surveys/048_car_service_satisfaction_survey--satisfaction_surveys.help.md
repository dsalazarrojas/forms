# Car Service Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with our car service. Your responses will help us understand what we're doing well and where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and honestly, one field at a time.
2. Use your real name and email address to ensure we can contact you for follow-up if needed.
3. For select questions, choose the option that best describes your experience.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, use the text fields to provide them.
5. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure everything is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information**: This field is optional, and you can use it for follow-up if needed. It will not be shared publicly.
* **First Name**: Your first name.
* **Last Name**: Your last name.
* **Email Address**: Your email address for follow-up if needed.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact number.
* **Service Information**: This field is optional, and you can use it for details about your service.
* **Service Date**: When was your service completed? (Required)
* **Service Location**: Where did you receive your service? (Required)
* **Service Type**: What service did you receive? (Required)
* **Vehicle Year**: The year of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make**: The manufacturer of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model**: The model of your vehicle.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: How satisfied were you overall with the service? (Required)
* **Service Quality**: Rate the quality of the service you received. (Required)
* **Staff Friendliness**: How friendly was the staff? (Required)
* **Staff Professionalism**: How professional was the staff? (Required)
* **Staff Knowledge**: How knowledgeable was the staff? (Required)
* **Service Advisor Rating**: Rate the service advisor. (Required)
* **Technician Skill**: Rate the technician's skill. (Required)
* **Communication**: How well did they communicate with you? (Required)
* **Explanation of Work**: How well was the work explained to you? (Required)
* **Wait Time**: How long did you wait? (Required)
* **Wait Time Acceptable**: Was the wait time acceptable? (Required)
* **Time to Complete**: Was it completed on time? (Required)
* **Facility Cleanliness**: Rate the cleanliness of our facility. (Required)
* **Waiting Area**: Rate the waiting area. (Optional)
* **Pricing**: How was the pricing? (Required)
* **Value for Money**: Was it worth the price? (Required)
* **Pricing Transparency**: Was pricing clear? (Required)
* **Vehicle Condition After Service**: How was your vehicle returned? (Required)
* **Problem Resolved**: Was the problem resolved? (Required)
* **Likelihood to Recommend**: Would you recommend us? (Required)
* **Likelihood to Return**: Will you return? (Required)
* **Competitor Comparison**: How do we compare to other shops? (Optional)
* **Best Aspect**: What did we do well? (Optional)
* **Areas for Improvement**: What could we improve? (Optional)
* **Additional Services Needed**: What other services would you like us to offer? (Optional)
* **Additional Comments**: Any other feedback. (Optional)
* **Survey Date**: Today's date. (Required)

This guide will help you complete the form in a few minutes. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us. We appreciate your feedback and look forward to serving you better.
