# Superannuation Contribution Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Superannuation Contribution Survey is used to gather information about superannuation contributions from employers. This form is intended to provide insights into the level of superannuation contributions made by different employers in order to identify areas for improvement and optimize superannuation benefits for employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page to the best of your ability.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for each field.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Superannuation Contribution Survey** (`superannuation_contribution_survey`, text, optional): This is the title of the form.
* **Employer** (`employer`, select_one, optional): Select the name of your employer from the options provided. This will help us understand which employer you are contributing to.
* **Employee Age** (`employee_age`, number, optional): Enter your age, which will help us understand your demographic information.
* **Employer Rating** (`employer_rating`, number, optional): Rate your satisfaction with your employer, on a scale of 1-5. This will help us understand your overall satisfaction level.
* **Superannuation Fund** (`superannuation_fund`, select_one, optional): Select the name of your superannuation fund from the options provided. This will help us understand your superannuation investment preferences.

## Tips
- Make sure to complete all required fields.
- Take your time to answer all questions accurately.
- If you are unsure about any question, please contact us for assistance.

Note: The fields and options are optional, so you can skip them if you are not comfortable providing this information. However, please note that the more information you provide, the more accurate the results will be.
