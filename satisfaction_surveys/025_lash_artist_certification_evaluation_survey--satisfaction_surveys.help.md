# Lash Artist Certification Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from students about the quality of a course taught by a lash artist. It helps to evaluate the performance of the instructor, course materials, support, and other aspects of the course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions in each page of the form.
2. Select the ratings or answers that best fit your opinion.
3. Provide additional comments or feedback in the "Do you have any additional comments or feedback about the course?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Instructor's Performance (`id: 2`)**: How would you rate the instructor's performance in this course?
	+ Excellent: The instructor was excellent in their performance.
	+ Good: The instructor was good in their performance.
	+ Fair: The instructor's performance was fair.
	+ Poor: The instructor was poor in their performance.
* **Course Quality (`id: 3`)**: How would you rate the overall quality of the course?
