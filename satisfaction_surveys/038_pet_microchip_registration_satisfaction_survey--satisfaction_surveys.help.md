# Pet Microchip Registration Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with the microchip registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Rate the quality of the microchip registration service using the "microchip_registration_service" field.
3. Provide any additional comments you'd like to share in the "comments" field.
4. Enter your contact information so we can follow up with you, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Microchip Registration Satisfaction Survey** (`pet_microchip_registration_satisfaction_survey`, text, optional):
	+ Please provide a brief description of your overall experience with the microchip registration process.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, optional):
	+ Rate your overall satisfaction with the microchip registration process, where 1 is very poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Clarity** (`clarity_satisfaction`, text, optional):
	+ Describe how clear and easy-to-understand the microchip registration instructions were for you.
* **Communication** (`communication_satisfaction`, text, optional):
	+ Rate your satisfaction with the communication received from our team during the microchip registration process.
* **Process Satisfaction** (`process_satisfaction`, text, optional):
	+ Describe your satisfaction with the flow and efficiency of the microchip registration process.
* **Microchip Registration Service** (`microchip_registration_service`, select_one, optional):
	+ Rate the quality of the microchip registration service received, where:
		- Excellent: The service was exceptional and met all my expectations.
		- Good: The service was satisfactory, but not exceptional.
		- Satisfactory: The service was adequate, but not impressive.
		- Poor: The service was below average.
		- Very Poor: The service was very disappointing.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional):
	+ Enter any additional comments or feedback you'd like to provide about the microchip registration process.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, email, optional):
	+ Enter your email address so we can follow up with you, if needed.

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough in your responses.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to us.
