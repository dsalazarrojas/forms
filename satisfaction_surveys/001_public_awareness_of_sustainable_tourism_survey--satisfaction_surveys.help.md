# public_awareness_of_sustainable_tourism_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey aims to understand public awareness and attitudes towards sustainable tourism, gathering information on demographics, travel habits, and opinions on sustainable tourism.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all questions honestly to provide valuable insights for the tourism industry.
2. Select only one option for questions where applicable.
3. If you're unsure or don't know the answer, please select "Other" or "Not at all informed" where relevant.
4. You can review and adjust your answers at any time before submission.
