# Parent Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parent Satisfaction Survey is a tool used to gather feedback from parents about their child's program or service, providing valuable input to program administrators to make improvements.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the survey in the "Parent Satisfaction Survey" field.
2. Provide your rating of the program in the "Parent Program Rating" field.
3. Enter your satisfaction score out of 100 in the "Parent Satisfaction Score" field.
4. If you have any comments about your experience, type them in the "Parent Satisfaction Comments" field.
5. If you think certain aspects of the program are particularly noteworthy, select your rating for "Parent Program Rating Important" field.
6. Indicate whether or not you would recommend the program to others by selecting the appropriate options in the "Parent Recommendation" field.
7. If you have any additional comments or concerns, type them in the "Parent Satisfaction Comments (Additional)" field.
8. Select the rating of the program's importance again in the "Parent Program Rating Important" field (this field is intended for comparison with the initial rating).
9. Enter the date of the survey completion in the "Parent Satisfaction Survey Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Parent Satisfaction Survey** (`parent_satisfaction_survey_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the survey.
- **Parent Program Rating** (`parent_program_rating`, number, optional): Rate the program with a number from 1 to 5.
  - 1: Very Poor
  - 2: Poor
  - 3: Fair
  - 4: Good
  - 5: Excellent
- **Parent Satisfaction Score** (`parent_satisfaction_score`, number, optional): Enter a satisfaction score out of 100.
- **Parent Satisfaction Comments** (`parent_satisfaction_comments`, note, optional): Provide any comments about your experience.
- **Parent Program Rating Important** (`parent_program_rating_important`, select_one, optional): Select one of the ratings for program importance.
  - 1: Excellent
  - 2: Good
  - 3: Fair
  - 4: Poor
  - 5: Very Poor
- **Parent Recommendation** (`parent_recommendation`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether or not you would recommend the program to others.
  - Very Likely
  - Somewhat Likely
  - Not Likely
  - Not at All Likely
- **Parent Satisfaction Comments (Additional)** (`parent_satisfaction_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or concerns.
- **Parent Program Rating Important (2)** (`parent_program_rating_important`, select_one, optional): Select one of the ratings for program importance (intended for comparison).
  - 1: Excellent
  - 2: Good
  - 3: Fair
  - 4: Poor
  - 5: Very Poor
- **Parent Satisfaction Survey Date** (`parent_satisfaction_survey_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the survey completion.

## Tips
- Please answer all questions truthfully and thoroughly.
- If you have any concerns or questions, contact us.
- The survey completion date is optional but recommended for tracking purposes.
