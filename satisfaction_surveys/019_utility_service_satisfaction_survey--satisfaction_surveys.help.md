# Utility Service Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Utility Service Satisfaction Survey" is designed to gather feedback from customers on their satisfaction with the utility service provided.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your overall satisfaction with the service on the scale provided.
2. Choose your preferred level of service reliability.
3. Select the service quality that best describes your experience.
4. Provide any suggestions or improvement areas you have identified.
5. Enter your contact information, including email, phone number, and address.
6. Select your preferred category and location.
7. Enter your full name, company name (if applicable), and department (if applicable).
8. Enter your employee ID (if applicable).
9. Select your role in the company (if applicable).
10. Select the type of service you received (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Satisfaction (0-100)** (`service_satisfaction`): Rate your overall satisfaction with the service provided.
* **Service Reliability** (`service_reliability`): Choose your preferred level of service reliability from the options provided.
	+ Very Likely
	+ Somewhat Likely
	+ Not Likely
	+ Not at All Likely
* **Service Quality** (`service_quality`): Select the service quality that best describes your experience.
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Suggestions/Improvement Areas** (`suggestions`): Provide any suggestions or improvement areas you have identified.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Utility Service** (`utility_service`): Enter a brief description of the utility service you received.
* **Improvement Areas** (`improvement_areas`): Provide any suggestions or improvement areas you have identified.
* **Follow-up Contact** (`follow_up`): Select whether you would like to be contacted for follow-up.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Provide any additional comments or information you would like to share.
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date you received the service.
* **Time** (`time`): Enter the time you received the service.
* **Category** (`category`): Select the category that best describes your interaction with the service.
* **Location** (`location`): Enter your address.
* **Name** (`name`): Enter your full name.
* **Company** (`company`): Enter your company name (if applicable).
* **Department** (`department`): Enter your department name (if applicable).
* **Role** (`role`): Enter your role in the company (if applicable).
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`): Enter your employee ID (if applicable).
* **Utility Number** (`utility_number`): Enter your utility number.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`): Select the type of service you received.
* **Meter Number** (`meter_number`): Enter your meter number.
* **Address** (`address`): Enter your address.
* **Reference** (`reference`): Enter any reference information you would like to provide.

Note: All fields are required, except where specified as optional.
