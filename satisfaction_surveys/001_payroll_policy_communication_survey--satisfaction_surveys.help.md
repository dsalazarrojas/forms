# Payroll Policy Communication Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the effectiveness of the payroll policy communication process. Your input will help us understand how clear and effective our communication is and where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page and select the relevant options for each field.
2. Fill in the fields with your honest feedback.
3. Click "Submit" to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Pay Period
**Pay Period** (`payperiod_label`, select_one, optional): 
This field asks about the pay period you are currently using. Please select the pay period that best describes your current situation.

### Pay Method
**Pay Method** (`paymethod_label`, select_one, optional): 
This field inquires about the method of payment you use. Please select the method that best applies to you.

### Salary Scale
**Salary Scale** (`salaryscale_label`, select_one, optional): 
This field asks whether you are familiar with our salary scale. Please select 'Yes' if you are aware of our salary scale, and 'No' otherwise.

### Department
**Department** (`department_label`, text, optional): 
This field is for you to provide any additional department-specific information related to your payroll policy experience. Please enter your answer clearly and concisely.

### Employee Count
**Employee Count** (`employeecount_label`, number, optional): 
This field is for you to provide the number of employees you are responsible for in terms of payroll policy. Please enter a numerical value for the employee count.

### Payroll Method
**Payroll Method** (`payrollmethod_label`, select_one, optional): 
This field inquires about our payroll method. Please select the method that best applies to you.

### Payroll Period
**Payroll Period** (`payrollperiod_label`, select_one, optional): 
This field asks whether we use a payroll period in your department. Please select 'Yes' if we do, and 'No' otherwise.

### Frequency
**Frequency** (`frequency_label`, select_one, optional): 
This field asks how frequently we update our payroll information. Please select 'Yes' if we do, and 'No' otherwise.
