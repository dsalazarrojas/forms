# employee_satisfaction_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from employees on their satisfaction with their job, manager, and overall work environment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your name and job title to identify yourself.
2. Provide your department and email address for contact purposes.
3. Share your phone number if you wish to be contacted for follow-up questions.
4. Indicate your length of employment to provide context for your feedback.
5. Rate your job satisfaction on a scale of 1-5.
6. Rate your manager's satisfaction on a scale of 1-5.
7. Rate your overall satisfaction on a scale of 1-5.
8. Provide any feedback or comments about your experience.
9. Add any additional comments or thoughts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official records.
* **Job Title** (text, required): Enter your current job title.
* **Department** (text, required): Enter the department you belong to.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your work email address.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your work phone number if you wish to be contacted for follow-up questions.
* **Date Employed** (date, required): Enter the date you started working with the company.
* **Job Satisfaction** (number, required): Rate your job satisfaction on a scale of 1-5.
	+ 1: Very dissatisfied
	+ 2: Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ 3: Neutral
	+ 4: Somewhat satisfied
	+ 5: Very satisfied
* **Manager Satisfaction** (number, required): Rate your manager's satisfaction on a scale of 1-5.
	+ 1: Very dissatisfied
	+ 2: Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ 3: Neutral
	+ 4: Somewhat satisfied
	+ 5: Very satisfied
* **Overall Satisfaction** (number, required): Rate your overall satisfaction on a scale of 1-5.
	+ 1: Very dissatisfied
	+ 2: Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ 3: Neutral
	+ 4: Somewhat satisfied
	+ 5: Very satisfied
* **Feedback** (note, optional): Share any feedback or comments about your experience.
* **Comments** (note, optional): Add any additional comments or thoughts.

Note: This form is anonymous, so you can be honest and open in your responses.
