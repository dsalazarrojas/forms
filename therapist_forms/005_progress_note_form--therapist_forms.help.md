# Progress Note Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Progress Note Form is a documentation tool used to record updates on employee and employer progress, project status, and other relevant notes. It helps in tracking and monitoring the progress of various activities, ensuring that all necessary information is documented and easily accessible.

## How To Complete This Form

- Start by selecting the relevant information that needs to be documented. The form consists of multiple sections, each with its own specific information to capture.
- Select the correct options for 'Projects Updated', 'Updated By', and 'Updated By Status' sections, based on your observations or actions taken.
- In the 'Comments' section, add any additional relevant information or comments that would be helpful for future reference.
- Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Updates** (progress_note_form_1, note, optional): This field is for capturing any updates or information about the employee's progress or performance.
* **Employer Updates** (progress_note_form_2, note, optional): This field is for capturing any updates or information about the employer's progress or performance.
* **Projects Updated** (progress_note_form_3, select_multiple, optional): This field is for selecting multiple projects that have been updated or completed.
* **Updated By** (progress_note_form_4, select_one, optional): This field is for selecting who has updated or made progress on the projects selected.
* **Date** (progress_note_form_5, date, optional): This field is for selecting the date of the update or completion of the project.
* **Date Updated** (progress_note_form_6, date, optional): This field is for selecting the date when the progress note was made.
* **Time** (progress_note_form_7, time, optional): This field is for capturing the time spent on the update or completion of the project.
* **Comments** (progress_note_form_8, text, optional): This field is for adding any additional comments or information about the update or completion of the project.
