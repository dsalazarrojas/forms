# Physiotherapy Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture the necessary information from a patient for physiotherapy treatment, ensuring they have provided informed consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your title (Physiotherapy Consent Form).
2. Indicate your interest in physiotherapy.
3. Provide your contact details.
4. Choose whether you want to receive emails or calls from us.
5. Enter a date for consent (if applicable).
6. Enter a time for consent (if applicable).
7. Add any additional notes about your consent.
8. Enter your email address.
9. Enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title**: This is the title of the form.
* **Patient Interest in Physiotherapy**: Please indicate your level of interest in physiotherapy.
* **Contact Details**: Please provide your contact information so we can reach out to you.
* **Contact Details Options**: You can choose to receive emails or calls from us.
* **Consent Date**: Enter a date for your consent (if applicable).
* **Consent Time**: Enter a time for your consent (if applicable).
* **Consent Notes**: Add any additional notes about your consent.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
