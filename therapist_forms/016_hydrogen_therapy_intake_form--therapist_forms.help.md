# Hydrogen Therapy Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hydrogen Therapy Intake Form is a collection of questions and fields that will help us understand you as a client better. We use this form to gather information about your details, health history, goals, and schedule an appointment for therapy. Please take your time to fill out this form accurately and honestly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please start by scrolling down to the first field, "Client Details". This is where you can provide basic information about yourself.
2. Move on to the "Health History" field, where you can describe any medical conditions or concerns you have.
3. Select one or more goals from the options provided in the "Goals" field that you would like to achieve through therapy.
4. In the "Consent" field, choose the option that best represents your understanding and agreement with our therapy terms and conditions.
5. If you have any specific dates in mind for your appointment, please select them in the "Appointment" field.
6. You can also specify a time for your appointment in the "Time" field.
7. The "Client Details 1" and "Client Details 2" fields are for any additional information you would like to provide about yourself.
8. Once you've filled out all the fields, please review your form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details**: This is the first field where you can provide your name, phone number, and any other basic information about yourself. Please make sure to fill this out accurately and honestly.
* **Health History**: Please describe any medical conditions or concerns you have that might be relevant to your therapy sessions.
* **Goals**: Select one or more goals that you would like to achieve through therapy. These goals can be as specific as losing weight or as broad as improving your mental well-being. Please be as specific as possible.
* **Consent**: This field is where you choose your understanding and agreement with our therapy terms and conditions. Please make sure to read and understand what you're agreeing to before submitting your form.
* **Appointment**: If you have any specific dates in mind for your appointment, please select them here.
* **Time**: Specify a time for your appointment if you prefer a particular time of day.
* **Client Details 1**: This field is for any additional information you'd like to provide about yourself.
* **Client Details 2**: Another field for any extra details.
* **Appointment**: This field is not used as it is the same as the "Appointment" field above, it seems like there is a duplicate field. Please ignore this field.
* **Time**: This field is not used as it is the same as the "Time" field above, it seems like there is a duplicate field. Please ignore this field.
* **Client Details 1** and **Client Details 2**: These fields are identical and seem to be duplicates of the "Client Details" field. Please ignore them.

Please note that the duplicate fields seem to be an error in the form design and will not be used. Please fill out the form with the understanding that these fields are not needed.
