# Psychological Appointment Scheduling - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule a psychological appointment with a therapist. It gathers information about the client, such as their name, contact details, appointment preferences, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields, which are marked as "required". The form is divided into several sections, each with its own set of fields. Please go through the form carefully and fill out each field as requested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address that you can be reached at.
* **Date** (date, required/optional): Select the date you would like to schedule your appointment.
* **Time** (time, required/optional): Select the time you would like to have your appointment.
* **Therapist Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of your assigned therapist or the name of the specific therapist you would like to see.
* **Notes** (note, required/optional): Any additional comments or information about your appointment.
* **Therapy Type** (select_one, required): Select the type of therapy you would like to have.
* **Appointment Frequency** (select_multiple, optional): Select the frequency of appointments you would like to have.
* **Phone Number** (text, required/optional): Enter a valid phone number where you can be reached.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (select_one, optional): Select your preferred method of contact.
* **Consent to Share** (text, required/optional): If you consent to share your information with your therapist, please enter "Yes".
* **Consent to Share with Therapist** (text, required/optional): If you consent to share your information with your therapist, please enter "Yes".
* **Emergency Contact** (text, required/optional): Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
* **Appointment Duration** (time, required/optional): Select the duration of your appointment.
* **Appointment Frequency Frequency** (select_multiple, optional): Select the frequency of appointments you would like to have.
* **Preferred Time** (time, required/optional): Select your preferred time for the appointment.
* **Therapist Location** (text, required/optional): Enter the location of your therapist's office or clinic.
* **Preferred Location** (text, required/optional): Enter your preferred location for the appointment.
* **Comments** (text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments about your appointment.
* **Appointment Type** (select_one, required): Select the type of appointment you would like to have.
* **Appointment Status** (select_one, required): Select the status of your appointment.
* **Submitted By** (text, required/optional): If you are submitting this form, enter your name.
* **Submitted On** (date, required/optional): If you are submitting this form, enter the date you are submitting it.
* **Updated By** (text, required/optional): If you are updating this form, enter your name.

Note: Some fields may not be required depending on the specific use case. Please review each field carefully to determine if it is required or optional.
