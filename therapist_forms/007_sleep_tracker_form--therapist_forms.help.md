# sleep_tracker_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track individual sleep patterns, including sleep dates, hours slept, wake times, bed times, sleep quality, wake frequency, sleep stages, and additional comments. It will be used in conjunction with a therapist's notes to provide comprehensive feedback and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start, select the date of your sleep session using the date field.
2. Next, enter the total number of hours you slept.
3. Enter your wake time and bed time, if applicable. These fields are optional and only required if you have specific data to report.
4. Choose your sleep quality, selecting the number of minutes you typically slept between 1-2, 3-4, 5-6, or 7-8.
5. Select how frequently you wake up during the night, choosing from "Once in a while," "Several times a week," "Most nights a week," or "Every night."
6. Select the sleep stage(s) you experienced, choosing from REM (90-120 minutes), NREM stage 1 (N1, 45-90 minutes), NREM stage 2 (N2, 60-120 minutes), or NREM stage 3 (N3, 60-90 minutes).
7. Enter any additional comments about your sleep session.
8. Your therapist will review and add their own notes to this section.
9. Set a sleep goal, specifying the number of hours you aim to sleep each night.
10. Enter the target date for your sleep goal, if applicable.
11. Finally, review and submit the form for feedback and guidance from your therapist.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sleep Date**: Enter the date of your most recent sleep session.
* **Sleep hours slept**: Enter the total number of hours you slept during the session.
* **Wake Time**: (Optional) Enter the time you woke up during the session, if applicable.
* **Bed Time**: (Optional) Enter the time you went to bed during the session, if applicable.
* **Sleep Quality**: Choose the number of minutes between 1-2, 3-4, 5-6, or 7-8 that you typically sleep.
* **Wake Frequency**: Choose how often you wake up during the night from "Once in a while," "Several times a week," "Most nights a week," or "Every night."
* **Sleep Stages**: Select the sleep stage(s) you experienced, choosing from REM (90-120 minutes), NREM stage 1 (N1, 45-90 minutes), NREM stage 2 (N2, 60-120 minutes), or NREM stage 3 (N3, 60-90 minutes).
* **Comments**: Enter any additional details about your sleep session, such as challenges or notable events.
* **Therapist Notes**: Enter any comments or notes from your therapist, which will be reviewed and discussed in your next session.
* **Sleep Goal**: Enter a specific number of hours you aim to sleep each night.
* **Sleep Goal Target Date**: Enter the target date for achieving your sleep goal, if applicable.

## Tips

* Be as accurate as possible when entering data, as this will help provide more effective feedback from your therapist.
* If you're unsure about a particular field, consult with your therapist for clarification.
* Review and submit the form regularly to monitor your progress and receive guidance from your therapist.
