# Sleep Quality Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Sleep Quality Survey is a tool designed to help you track your sleep habits, environment, and quality over time. This form is intended for users who have been diagnosed with sleep issues and are looking to monitor their progress.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **General Information**: Please enter your name and any other identifying information as prompted.
2. **Demographics**: Select your gender from the provided options.
3. **Sleep Habits**: Enter your average number of hours spent sleeping each night.
4. **Sleep Quality**: Enter a brief description of your sleep quality, including any difficulties you may be experiencing.
5. **Sleep Diagnostic**: Indicate which environmental factors you believe are affecting your sleep, such as noise or light level.
6. **Sleep Environment**: Select any rooms or environments you typically sleep in.
7. **Sleep Habituation**: Indicate whether you have habituated (gotten used to) sleeping well.
8. **Sleep Wake-up Time**: Enter the time you wake up each morning.
9. **Sleep Duration**: Enter how long you sleep each night.
10. **Sleep Latency**: Enter how quickly you fall asleep.
11. **Sleep Frequency**: Describe how often you sleep.
12. **Sleep Discomfort**: Rate your level of discomfort while sleeping.
13. **Sleep Diagnosed**: Indicate whether you have been diagnosed with a sleep issue.
14. **Sleep Habituation 2**: Repeat the same question as above.
15. **Sleep Wake-up Time 2**: Repeat the same question as above.
16. **Sleep Duration 2**: Repeat the same question as above.
17. **Sleep Latency 2**: Repeat the same question as above.
18. **Sleep Frequency 2**: Repeat the same question as above.
19. **Sleep Discomfort 2**: Repeat the same question as above.
20. **Sleep Diagnosed 2**: Repeat the same question as above.
21. **Sleep Habits 2**: Enter your average number of hours spent sleeping each night.
22. **Sleep Quality 2**: Enter a brief description of your sleep quality, including any difficulties you may be experiencing.
23. **Sleep Diagnostic 2**: Repeat the same question as above.
24. **Sleep Environment 2**: Select any rooms or environments you typically sleep in.
25. **Sleep Habituation 3**: Repeat the same question as above.

## Tips

* Please be as accurate as possible when answering the questions.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
* This form is intended to be completed over time, so please repeat the same questions on each occasion to help us track your progress.
