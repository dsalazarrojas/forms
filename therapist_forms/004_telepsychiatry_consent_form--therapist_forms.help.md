# Telepsychiatry Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Telepsychiatry Consent Form is a patient data collection form intended for mental health professionals. This form gathers patient personal data, medical insurance details, and emergency contact information. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Ensure that the fields "First name", "Last name", and "Email" are filled in with the patient's information.
3. Provide the "Date of birth" in the correct format (dd/mm/yyyy).
4. Select a valid "Consent date".
5. Sign the form with your signature in the space provided.
6. Fill in the "Patient information" field with any relevant details.
7. Enter the "Insurance information" with your insurance provider's details.
8. Enter the "Provider information" with your own details.
9. Fill in the "Patient address" field with the patient's address.
10. Enter the "Insurance plan" details.
11. Enter the "Policy number".
12. Enter the "Policy holder" name.
13. Enter the "Policy status".
14. Enter the "Policy type".
15. Fill in the "Policy notes" field.
16. Enter the "Emergency contact" name.
17. Enter the "Emergency phone" number.
18. Enter the "Emergency address".
19. Enter the "Emergency relationship".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (First name, text, required): Enter the patient's first name.
* **Last name** (Last name, text, required): Enter the patient's last name.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Date of birth** (Date of birth, date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth in the correct format (dd/mm/yyyy).
* **Consent date** (Consent date, date, required): Select a valid date for consent.
* **Date signed** (Date signed, date, required): Select the date you signed the form.
* **Signature** (Signature, note, required): Sign the form with your signature in the space provided.
* **Patient information** (Patient information, text, required): Enter any relevant patient information.
* **Insurance information** (Insurance information, text, optional): Enter your insurance provider's details.
* **Provider information** (Provider information, text, required): Enter your own details.
* **Patient address** (Patient address, text, optional): Enter the patient's address.
* **Insurance plan** (Insurance plan, text, optional): Enter your insurance plan details.
* **Policy number** (Policy number, text, optional): Enter your policy number.
* **Policy holder** (Policy holder, text, optional): Enter the policy holder's name.
* **Policy status** (Policy status, text, optional): Enter the policy status.
* **Policy type** (Policy type, text, optional): Enter the policy type.
* **Policy notes** (Policy notes, text, optional): Enter any policy notes.
* **Emergency contact** (Emergency contact, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency name** (Emergency name, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency phone** (Emergency phone, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency address** (Emergency address, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact's address.
* **Emergency relationship** (Emergency relationship, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact's relationship to you.
