# EMDR Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The EMDR Client Intake Form is a comprehensive form designed to collect information from clients to prepare for EMDR therapy sessions. This form helps the therapist understand the client's background, provide a safe and comfortable environment, and tailor the session to their specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Client Information section with your personal details.
2. Choose your consent status in the Consent section.
3. Provide session details in the Session Information section.
4. Select a date and time for your session in the Date and Time section.
5. Add any additional notes in the Notes section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information** (`client_info`, text, required/optional): Fill in your name, phone number, and any other relevant contact information in this field.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required/optional): Please select your consent status from the options provided.
	+ Select 'True' if you have given consent for the session.
	+ Select 'False' if you have not given consent.
	+ Select 'Maybe' if you are unsure or need more information.
* **Session Information** (`session_info`, text, required/optional): Briefly describe the purpose and goals of your EMDR sessions.
* **Date and Time** (`date_and_time`, time, required/optional): Select the date and time you are available for your EMDR session.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or questions you may have about your session.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your contact information for accuracy.
- If you are unsure about your consent status, please select 'Maybe' and discuss with your therapist.
- Choose a date and time that works best for you.
- Keep your notes concise and clear for your therapist's reference.
