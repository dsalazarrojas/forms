# Couples Therapy Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your relationship dynamics and experiences. Please provide as much detail as possible to help us better understand your situation.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out this form carefully and accurately.
- If you're unsure about any question, please ask your therapist for clarification.
- If you have any additional information or concerns, please share them with your therapist.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Relationship status**: Describe the current status of your relationship (e.g., committed, separated, divorced).
- **Partner Info**: Provide any relevant information about your partner (e.g., partner's name, age, occupation).
- **Length of relationship**: Specify how long you have been in your current relationship.
- **Conflicts**: Check "Yes" if you have had any conflicts or disagreements with your partner, or "No" otherwise.
- **Goals**: Describe any specific goals you hope to achieve through therapy.
- **Relationship type**: Check "Yes" if you are in a romantic relationship, "No" otherwise.
- **Relationship History**: Describe any relevant history or background information about your relationship.
- **Conflicts History**: Describe any conflicts or disagreements you've had in your relationship in the past.
- **Partner's Relationship Status**: Provide any relevant information about your partner's relationship status.
- **Client's Counseling History**: Describe any previous counseling or therapy you or your partner have had.
- **Therapist Info**: Provide any relevant information about your current or previous therapists.
- **Relationship Satisfaction**: On a scale of 1-10, rate your current relationship satisfaction level.
- **Conflicts Frequency**: Describe how often conflicts or disagreements arise in your relationship.
- **Communication Patterns**: Describe any regular communication patterns you and your partner have (e.g., frequent arguments, avoiding certain topics).
- **Emotional Issues**: Describe any emotional issues or topics that you and your partner struggle with.
- **Relationship Dynamics**: Describe the overall dynamics of your relationship (e.g., cooperative, competitive, dominant/submissive).
- **Partner's Counseling History**: Provide any relevant information about your partner's previous counseling or therapy experiences.
- **Communication Patterns Frequency**: Describe how often you communicate with your partner (e.g., daily, weekly, rarely).
- **Conflicts Frequency**: Describe how often conflicts or disagreements arise in your relationship.
- **Communication Patterns**: Describe any regular communication patterns you and your partner have (e.g., frequent arguments, avoiding certain topics).
