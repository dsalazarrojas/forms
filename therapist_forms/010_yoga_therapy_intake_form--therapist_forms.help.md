# Yoga Therapy Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Yoga Therapy Intake Form is designed to collect client information, medical history, and other relevant details to guide conversations and identify potential risks or complications during or after yoga therapy sessions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill out the sections below. Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information (Page 1)** (`yoga_therapy_intake_form_1`, `text`, optional):
	+ This is the first section of the form where you can provide your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself as a client.
* **Contact Information (Page 2)** (`yoga_therapy_intake_form_2`, `text`, optional):
	+ This section is for providing your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address, so we can reach you if needed.
* **Medical History (Page 3)** (`yoga_therapy_intake_form_3`, `text`, optional):
	+ This section is for describing any medical conditions, allergies, or health concerns that may impact your practice of yoga.

## Tips
- Please answer honestly and thoroughly to ensure accurate information is collected.
- If you have any medical conditions or concerns, please provide detailed information to help us provide the best possible care.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
- This form is not a formal medical document and is intended for informational purposes only.
