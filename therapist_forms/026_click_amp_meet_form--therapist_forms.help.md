# Click & Meet Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule appointments with therapists for clients to discuss their concerns and receive guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the time slot that works best for your preferred meeting time.
2. Provide your full name and email address.
3. Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
4. Choose your preferred therapist from the list.
5. Select a suitable meeting date and time.
6. Specify the type of session you require (e.g., initial consultation, follow-up, crisis intervention, or group session).
7. Decide on the session format (in-person, video call, or phone call).
8. If applicable, provide your insurance provider information.
9. Briefly describe your primary concerns or issues you'd like to discuss during your session.
10. Confirm that you understand and agree to the 24-hour cancellation policy.
11. Add any additional comments or notes for the therapist, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select Therapist** (`therapist_selection`, `select_one`): Select the therapist you would like to meet with. Choose from Dr. Smith (CBT), Dr. Johnson (Family Therapy), Sarah Williams (Trauma Specialist), or Michael Brown (General Counseling).
* **Primary Concerns** (`primary_concerns`, `text`): Briefly describe what brings you to therapy today.
* **Emergency Contact Name and Phone** (`emergency_contact_info`, `text`): Enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, `text`): If you have an insurance provider, please specify.
* **Intake Notes** (`intake_notes`, `text`): Add any additional comments or notes for the therapist.
* **Confirmation of Cancellation Policy** (`confirmation_policy`, `select_one`): Confirm that you understand and agree to the 24-hour cancellation policy.
* **Footnote** (`footer_note`, `note`): This is a placeholder for a friendly note from the therapist.
* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, `date`): Select a date for your meeting.
* **Preferred Time Slot** (`meeting_time`, `select_one`): Choose a preferred time slot for your meeting (morning, afternoon, late afternoon, or evening).
* **Session Type** (`session_type`, `select_one`): Select the type of session you require (initial consultation, follow-up, crisis intervention, or group session).
* **Session Format** (`session_format`, `select_one`): Decide on the session format (in-person, video call, or phone call).
* **Full Name** (`client_full_name`, `text`): Enter your full name for identification purposes.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, `email`): Enter your email address for secure communication.
* **Phone Number** (`client_phone`, `text`): Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
