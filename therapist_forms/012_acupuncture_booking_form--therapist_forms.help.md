# Acupuncture Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book an appointment for acupuncture services at our clinic. It collects contact information, service selection, and scheduling details.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the required fields: Full Name, Email Address, Mobile Phone, Select Service, Requested Booking Date, Requested Booking Time, and Preferred Confirmation Method.
2.  Enter your Mobile Phone number in the correct format, including the country code.
3.  Select the preferred service from the options provided (e.g., Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM), Cosmetic Acupuncture etc.).
4.  Choose a preferred method of confirmation (Email, SMS, or Phone Call).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): This is your complete name. Please fill in your first and last name.
*   **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): This is your email address. Please ensure it is in the correct format (e.g., example\@example.com).
*   **Mobile Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your mobile phone number in the correct international format (e.g., +1-1234567890).
*   **Select Service** (`acupuncture_service`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of service you are interested in, such as Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) or Stress and Anxiety Relief Session.
*   **Optional Add-on Services** (`add_on_services`, `select_multiple`, optional): If you wish, select any additional services you would like to have during your session, such as Cupping Therapy or Moxibustion.
*   **Requested Booking Date** (`booking_preference_date`, `date`, required): This is the date you would like to book your session. Please ensure the format is MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Requested Booking Time** (`booking_preference_time`, `time`, required): This is the time you would like to book your session. Please enter the time in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00).
*   **Clinic Location** (`location_preference`, `select_one`, required): Choose the clinic location where you would like to have your session.
*   **Additional Information** (`health_concerns`, `text`, optional): If you have any specific health concerns or goals you would like to share with our team.
*   **Current Medications or Supplements** (`medications`, `text`, optional): List any medications or supplements you are currently taking.
*   **Pacemakers or Metal Implants** (`pacemakers_metal`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you have a pacemaker or metal implants and please describe if applicable.
*   **Description of Implants** (`implants_description`, `text`, optional): Provide a description of your pacemakers or implants if you have them.
*   **Preferred Confirmation Method** (`booking_confirmation_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred method of confirmation, such as Email or SMS.

## Tips

*   Please ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
*   Select the preferred service and location based on your needs.
*   If you have any concerns or questions, please do not hesitate to contact us.
*   Our team will contact you after submitting this form with further instructions.
