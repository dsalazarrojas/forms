# Ketamine Therapy Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide is designed to help you understand and complete the Ketamine Therapy Consent Form. The form is a crucial document that outlines your agreement to participate in ketamine therapy, a type of treatment for mental health conditions such as depression, anxiety, or post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD). It ensures that both you and your healthcare provider have a clear understanding of the treatment terms and your medical history.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Read each field carefully** to ensure you understand the information requested.
2.  **Fill out the form completely** and accurately, as it is a legal document.
3.  **Sign and date the form** to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Consent** (`patient_consent`): Please write a clear and detailed statement agreeing to participate in ketamine therapy.
    *   Type a brief statement indicating your understanding of the treatment terms and your agreement to proceed.
    *   This field is required to ensure you've read and understood the treatment terms.
*   **Medical History** (`medical_history`): Provide a comprehensive description of your medical history, including any pre-existing conditions, allergies, or previous treatments.
    *   Be as detailed as possible, including any relevant medical records or test results.
    *   This field is required to help your healthcare provider understand your medical background.
*   **Patient Name** (`patient_name`): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
    *   This field is required to ensure accurate identification and record-keeping.
*   **Doctor's Name** (`doctor_name`): Enter your healthcare provider's name as it appears on their professional identification documents.
    *   This field is required to ensure accurate record-keeping and consent verification.
*   **Medical Facility** (`medical_facility`): Identify the medical facility where you will be receiving treatment.
    *   Be accurate to ensure correct billing and record-keeping.
*   **Date Signed** (`date_signed`): Enter the date you signed this form.
    *   This field is required to track the consent date and time.
*   **Time Signed** (`time_signed`): Enter the time you signed this form.
    *   This field is required to track the consent time.
*   **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`): Sign your name to confirm your agreement to the terms outlined on this form.
    *   This field is required to validate your consent.
*   **Doctor Signature** (`doctor_signature`): Your healthcare provider must sign their name to confirm their agreement to the terms outlined on this form.
    *   This field is required to validate your healthcare provider's consent.
*   **Consent Status** (`consent_status`): Select 'True' to indicate your agreement to ketamine therapy or 'False' to indicate otherwise.
    *   This field is required to track your consent status.

## Tips

*   **Ask questions or seek clarification** if you're unsure about any field or its purpose.
