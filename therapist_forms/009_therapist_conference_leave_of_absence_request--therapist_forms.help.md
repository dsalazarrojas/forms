# Therapist Conference Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Therapist Conference Leave Of Absence Request form is used to inform your supervisor and team about your absence during a specific period. This form helps maintain a smooth workflow and ensures that your work responsibilities are covered during your absence.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select whether you are requesting a conference or not.
2. Choose whether your supervisor has reviewed this request.
3. Enter the start date of your request.
4. Enter the end date of your request.
5. Enter the number of hours you are requesting to be off.
6. Provide a brief explanation for your leave request.
7. Select whether this request has been reviewed by the team.
8. Confirm who submitted this request (you or someone else).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Conference Request** (`conference_request`, select_multiple, required): Select whether you are requesting a conference or not. This field helps to confirm your intention for the absence.
* **Supervisor Review** (`supervisor_review`, select_one, required): Choose whether your supervisor has reviewed this request or not. This field ensures that your supervisor is aware of your leave request.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your request. This date should be in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`. If left blank, the system will assume the date is today.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your return. This date should be in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`. If left blank, the system will assume the date is the next available day.
* **Request Hours** (`request_hours`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours you are requesting to be off. This is used to plan your work responsibilities and cover during your absence.
* **Reason for Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, text, optional): Provide a brief explanation for your leave request. This helps your supervisor and team understand your absence and plan accordingly.
* **Team Review** (`team_review`, select_multiple, required): Select whether this request has been reviewed by your team or not. This ensures that your team is aware of your absence and can plan accordingly.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, required): Confirm who submitted this request (you or someone else). This is an administrative field to track who initiated the request.

## Tips
* Be accurate when entering dates to avoid conflicts with your work schedule.
* Ensure you've discussed your leave request with your supervisor and team before submitting this form.
* Use this form to inform your team and supervisor about your absence during a specific period.
* Review this form carefully before submitting it to avoid errors.
