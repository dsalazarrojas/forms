# Psychotherapy Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Psychotherapy Intake Form is a crucial document that helps us gather essential information about you, our client. This form is designed to help us understand your medical history, mental status, and emergency contact details. The information collected will aid in creating a safe and effective therapy plan tailored to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question carefully and honestly to the best of your ability.
2. Make sure to complete all required fields (marked with *) to ensure we have the necessary information to provide effective therapy.
3. If you're unsure about any question or field, do not hesitate to contact us for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Client (1)**: Enter your name and any other identifying information.
* **Contact Info (2)**: Provide your contact details, including phone number and email address, so we can reach you for any further information or to schedule appointments.
* **Medical History (3)**: Share any relevant medical history, including previous medical conditions, surgeries, or allergies.
* **Mental Status (4)**: Indicate your current mental status by selecting 'Active' if you're currently seeking therapy or 'Inactive' if you're not.
* **Medical Conditions (5)**: List any ongoing medical conditions, such as diabetes, hypertension, or other health issues.
* **Medications (6)**: List all your current medications, including prescription and non-prescription medications.
* **Insurance (7)**: Indicate if you have insurance coverage for therapy.
* **Emergency Contact (8)**: Provide the name, phone number, and relationship of an emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone (9)**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Address (10)**: Enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Relationship (11)**: Indicate the relationship between the emergency contact and you.
* **Emergency Info (12)**: Share any additional information about your emergency contact.
* **Message (13)**: Add any personal messages or instructions for your emergency contact.
* **Phone (14)**: Re-enter the phone number of your emergency contact ( duplicate of 9).
* **Email (15)**: Re-enter the email address of your emergency contact (duplicate of 2).
* **Address (16)**: Re-enter the address of your emergency contact (duplicate of 10).
* **Medical History 2 (17)**: Share any additional medical history not covered in the previous field.
* **Medical Conditions 2 (18)**: List any additional medical conditions not covered in the previous field.
* **Mental Status 2 (19)**: Indicate your current mental status in this specific context.
* **Mental Status 3 (20)**: Another question about your mental status.
* **Mental Status 4 (21)**: Yet another question about your mental status.
* **Mental Status 5 (22)**: Another question about your mental status.
* **Medical Conditions 3 (23)**: List any additional medical conditions.
* **Medical Conditions 4 (24)**: List any additional medical conditions.
* **Medical Conditions 5 (25)**: List any additional medical conditions.
