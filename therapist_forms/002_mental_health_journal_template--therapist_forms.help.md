# Mental Health Journal Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals track their mental health, emotions, and progress over time. It includes various sections to collect information on daily mood, emotions, physical symptoms, thoughts, and more. Completing this form will help users identify patterns and trends in their mental health, making it easier to reflect on their progress and seek help when needed.

## How To Complete This Form

To use this form, please follow these steps:

* Start by filling out the daily_mood field with a rating of your current mood, ranging from 1 (very bad) to 10 (very good).
* In the emotions field, jot down any emotions you are feeling at the moment, as specific and detailed as possible.
* For sleep_patterns, describe your sleep patterns and habits.
* In physical_symptoms, list any physical symptoms you are experiencing.
* In the thoughts field, write down any thoughts that are preoccupying your mind.
* For activities, list any activities you have engaged in during the day.
* In medications, list any medications you are currently taking.
* In mental_health_progress, describe any progress or setbacks you have experienced with your mental health.
* In feelings, describe your current feelings and emotions.
* For social_interactions, record any notable social interactions or conversations.
* Finally, in mental_health_goals, list any goals or objectives you have for your mental health.
* End each entry with notes, where you can record any additional information or observations.
