# Group Therapy Client Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from clients about their experience with a therapist. It helps therapists identify areas of improvement and provide better services to their clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your honest feedback.
2. Rate your satisfaction level with the 5-Star Rating field.
3. Choose how you would rate your experience in the Therapist Communicative Skills field.
4. Provide any comments or suggestions you have for improvement in the Comments field.
5. Fill in your name and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Feedback Form**: This is the main feedback section of the form. Please provide a clear and concise description of your experience with the therapist. (Text field, 255 characters max)
* **Therapist Rating**: Rate your satisfaction level with the services provided by the therapist on a scale of 1-5. (Number field)
* **5-Star Rating**: This is another field for rating your satisfaction level with the services provided by the therapist. Please use the same scale as the previous field. (Number field)
* **Group Dynamic**: Provide a description of the group dynamic, if applicable. (Text field, 255 characters max)
* **Therapist Availability**: Rate how available the therapist was during your sessions. (Number field)
* **Therapist Communicative Skills**: Rate how well the therapist communicated with you. (Select One field, options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, Option 4, Option 5)
* **Overall Experience**: Describe your overall experience with the therapist. (Text field, 255 characters max)
* **Suggestions for Improvement**: Provide any suggestions you have for how the therapist can improve. (Text field, 255 characters max)
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or feedback. (Text field, 255 characters max)
* **Client Name**: Your name. (Text field, 255 characters max)
* **Email**: Your email. (Email field)
* **Phone**: Your phone number. (Text field, 255 characters max)
* **Submitted On**: This field is automatically filled in when you submit the form and is not editable. (Date field)
* **Submitted By**: This field is automatically filled in when you submit the form and is not editable. (Text field)
* **Submitted By Name**: This field is automatically filled in when you submit the form and is not editable. (Text field)
* **Submitted By Email**: This field is automatically filled in when you submit the form and is not editable. (Email field)
* **Submitted By Phone**: This field is automatically filled in when you submit the form and is not editable. (Text field)
* **Submitted By IP**: This field is automatically filled in when you submit the form and is not editable. (Text field)
* **Submitted By IP Address**: This field is automatically filled in when you submit the form and is not editable. (Text field)
* **Submitted By User Agent**: This field is automatically filled in when you submit the form and is not editable. (Text field)
* **Submitted By User Agent**: This field is automatically filled in when you submit the form and is not editable. (Text field)
* **Submitted By Timezone**: This field is automatically filled in when you submit the form and is not editable. (Number field)
* **Submitted By Country**: This field is automatically filled in when you submit the form and is not editable. (Text field)
