# SLAA Recovery Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SLAA Recovery Questionnaire is a form designed to help clients seeking treatment for Sex and Love Addiction (SLAA) by gathering information about their recovery goals, feelings, relapse prevention strategies, support system, recovery process, and personal contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question carefully and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. If you are unsure about any question, please ask your therapist for clarification.
3. Fill out the form in one sitting, or save it for later and come back to it when you are ready.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Recovery Goals** (Text, Not Required): Please describe your goals for recovery, including any specific challenges you would like to overcome or changes you would like to make in your behavior.
* **Feelings About SLAA** (Text, Not Required): Describe your feelings about your addiction, including any emotions or thoughts that have been helpful or hindering your recovery.
* **Relapse Prevention** (Text, Not Required): Describe the strategies you use or plan to use to prevent relapse, including any specific triggers or warning signs.
* **Support System** (Text, Not Required): Describe your support system, including family, friends, or support groups that help you in your recovery.
* **Recovery Process** (Text, Not Required): Describe your current recovery process, including any successes, challenges, or areas of improvement.
* **Therapist Notes** (Text, Not Required): Additional comments or questions you would like to share with your therapist.
* **Additional Comments** (Text, Not Required): Any other comments or thoughts you would like to share with your therapist.
* **Client Name** (Text, Not Required): Please enter your name.
* **Client Email** (Email, Not Required): If you have an email address, please enter it.
* **Client Phone** (Text, Not Required): Please enter your phone number.
* **Therapist Name** (Text, Not Required): Please enter the name of your therapist.
