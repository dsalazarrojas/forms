# Grief Support Connection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Grief Support Connection Form is designed to help individuals seeking support connect with others who can offer assistance during difficult times. By filling out this form, you'll be able to provide your contact information and express your support preferences, allowing us to better understand how we can assist you.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply follow these steps:

*   Enter your first name in the **First name** field.
*   Enter your last name in the **Last name** field.
*   Provide your email address in the **Email** field.
*   Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
*   If you're part of a support group, enter the group's name in the **Support Group** field.
*   Indicate the size of your support group in the **Size of Support Group** field (choose one or more from the list).
*   Select your preferred method of support in the **Preferred Support** field (choose one from the list).
*   If necessary, provide an emergency contact person's name in the **Emergency contact** field.
*   If applicable, enter the emergency contact person's phone number in the **Emergency Phone** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First name (1)**: Please enter your first name.
*   **Last name (2)**: Please enter your last name.
*   **Email (3)**: Enter your email address.
*   **Phone (4)**: Enter your phone number.
*   **Support Group (5)**: If you're part of a support group, enter the group's name.
*   **Size of Support Group (7)**: Indicate the size of your support group (select one or more from the list):
    *   Small: 1-2 people
    *   Medium: 3-5 people
    *   Large: More than 5 people
*   **Preferred Support (8)**: Choose how you'd like to receive support:
    *   In person: In-person meetings or meetups
    *   Phone call: Support over the phone
    *   Video call: Support over video call (e.g., Zoom)
    *   Message: Support via messages (e.g., email, text)
*   **Emergency contact (9)**: If you have an emergency contact person, enter their name.
*   **Emergency Phone (10)**: If applicable, enter the emergency contact person's phone number.

Note to self: Please keep in mind that this form is relatively simple, so try to keep the explanations brief and focused on the most important information.
