# Wellness Center Therapy Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wellness Center Therapy Contact Form is a tool used to gather information from potential clients or patients who are interested in seeking therapy services. This form helps the staff of the Wellness Center to understand the client's needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the first field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the second field.
3. Provide your Email address in the third field. Make sure to enter a valid email address that you have access to.
4. Enter your Phone number in the fourth field.
5. Select the type of treatment you are interested in (Counseling, Physical Therapy, or Occupational Therapy) in the fifth field.
6. Choose the location where you would like to receive treatment (In-office, Online, or Both) in the sixth field.
7. Enter the number of sessions you are interested in committing to in the seventh field.
8. Enter the frequency of sessions you are interested in committing to in the eighth field.
9. If you have any additional comments or concerns, please enter them in the ninth field.
10. Select your preferred method of contact (Email, Phone, or Text) in the tenth field (optional).
11. If you were referred to the Wellness Center through a referral source, please select from the options provided in the eleventh field (optional).
12. If you were referred to the Wellness Center through a different referral source, please enter the source in the twelfth field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (required): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (required): Please enter a valid email address that you have access to.
* **Phone** (required): Please enter your phone number.
* **Treatment Type** (required): Select the type of treatment you are interested in (Counseling, Physical Therapy, or Occupational Therapy).
* **Treatment Location** (required): Choose the location where you would like to receive treatment (In-office, Online, or Both).
* **Treatment Duration** (required): Enter the number of sessions you are interested in committing to.
* **Treatment Frequency** (required): Enter the frequency of sessions you are interested in committing to.
* **Additional Comments** (optional): If you have any additional comments or concerns, please enter them here.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (optional): Select your preferred method of contact (Email, Phone, or Text).
* **Referral Source** (optional): If you were referred to the Wellness Center through a referral source, please select from the options provided.
* **Referral Source Other** (optional): If you were referred to the Wellness Center through a different referral source, please enter the source.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your email address correctly to ensure that we can contact you.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We will respond to your inquiry as soon as possible to discuss your treatment options.
