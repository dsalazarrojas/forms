# Clarity And Direction Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Clarity And Direction Questionnaire is a tool designed to help individuals gain clarity and direction in their lives. It is meant to be a starting point for self-reflection and exploration of one's goals, challenges, and strengths. This questionnaire is not a diagnostic tool, but rather a guide to help you identify areas where you may need support and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. For multi-select questions (e.g., "Areas where you feel most unclear"), select all that apply.
3. For numerical questions (e.g., "How clear are you about your current life direction" and "How much impact do these challenges have on your life"), use the scale provided (1-10, where 1 is minimal and 10 is major).
4. For text-based questions (e.g., "What are your primary goals for the next 6 months"), answer as concisely as possible, and provide as much detail as you need.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter your full name or the name of the person you are filling out this form on behalf of.
* **Date of Session or Questionnaire** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of the session or when you are completing this questionnaire.
* **How clear are you about your current life direction** (`clarity_rating`, number, required): Rate your level of clarity about your current life direction on a scale of 1-10.
* **Areas where you feel most unclear** (`clarity_areas`, select_multiple, required): Select all the areas where you feel most unclear.
* **What are your primary goals for the next 6 months?** (`primary_goals`, text, required): Be specific about your goals and aspirations.
* **Long-term goals for the next 2-5 years** (`long_term_goals`, text, optional): If you have long-term goals, share them here.
* **How clear are these goals to you?** (`goal_clarity`, select_one, required): Rate how clear your goals are to you on a scale of Very clear, Mostly clear, Somewhat clear, or Not clear at all.
* **What are your main challenges right now?** (`main_challenges`, text, required): Share the obstacles that are hindering your progress.
* **How much impact do these challenges have on your life?** (`challenge_impact`, number, required): Rate the impact of these challenges on your life on a scale of 1-10.
* **What obstacles do you perceive to achieving your goals?** (`perceived_obstacles`, text, optional): If you have any perceived obstacles, share them here.
* **What are your greatest personal strengths?** (`personal_strengths`, text, required): Share your greatest personal strengths.
* **Available resources or support systems** (`available_resources`, text, optional): If you have any resources or support systems that can help you achieve your goals, share them here.
* **How well are you utilizing your strengths?** (`strengths_utilization`, select_one, optional): Rate how well you are utilizing your strengths on a scale of Very well, Adequately, Could be better, or Not utilizing them.
* **What outcomes would you like from our work together?** (`desired_outcomes`, text, required): Be specific about what you hope to achieve from the process.
* **First action steps you plan to take** (`action_steps`, text, optional): Share the first steps you plan to take to achieve your goals.
* **Your readiness level for making changes** (`readiness_level`, select_one, required): Rate your readiness level for making changes on a scale of Very ready, Ready, Somewhat ready, or Not ready yet.
