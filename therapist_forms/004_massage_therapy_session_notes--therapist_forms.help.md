# Massage Therapy Session Notes - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather notes and information about massage therapy sessions, enabling therapists to maintain accurate records and provide better care to their clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Enter your name as the therapist in the "Therapist Name" field.
3. Choose the date of the session in the "Date" field.
4. Select the time of the session in the "Time" field.
5. Enter the duration of the session in the "Duration" field.
6. Provide any notable session notes in the "Session Notes" field.
7. List the areas of concern addressed during the session in the "Areas of Concern" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`<name>`): Enter the name of the client being seen by the therapist.
* **Therapist Name** (`<name>`): Enter your name as the therapist conducting the session.
* **Date** (`<date>`): Select the date of the massage therapy session using the date format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Time** (`<time>`): Choose the time of the session using the 12-hour clock format (e.g., 02:30 PM).
* **Duration** (`<text>`): Enter the length of the session, including minutes or hours.
* **Session Notes** (`<text>`): Record any important details or observations from the session.
* **Areas of Concern** (`<text>`): List any areas of concern addressed during the session, such as specific issues or conditions.
