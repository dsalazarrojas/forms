# Case Conceptualisation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help therapists and clinicians gather essential information for case conceptualization and treatment planning.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the client's personal information, including their full legal name.
2.  Enter the client's date of birth in the yyyy-mm-dd format.
3.  Describe the client's presenting problems and symptoms.
4.  Provide relevant history and contextual factors related to the client's mental health.
5.  Outline the client's current functioning, including their strengths and occupation.
6.  Assess the client's risk level for suicidal thoughts or harm to others, selecting Low, Moderate, or High.
7.  Write a brief summary of the client's biopsychosocial profile.
8.  List short and long-term goals of treatment.
9.  Select any recommended interventions, such as Cognitive Behavioural Therapy, Psychodynamic Therapy, Family Therapy, Medication Review, or Referral to a specialist.
10. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the client's full legal name.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the client's date of birth in the yyyy-mm-dd format.
*   **Presenting Problems** (`presenting_problems`, text, required): Describe the client's current concerns and symptoms.
*   **History and Contextual Factors** (`history_and_context`, text, optional): Provide relevant medical, social, and family history.
*   **Current Functioning and Strengths** (`current_functioning`, text, optional): Outline the client's daily functioning and occupation.
*   **Risk Assessment** (`risk_assessment`, select_one, required): Assess the client's risk level for suicidal thoughts or harm to others.
*   **Formulation Summary** (`formulation_summary`, text, required): Write a brief biopsychosocial summary.
*   **Goals of Treatment** (`goals_of_treatment`, text, optional): List short and long-term goals of treatment.
*   **Recommended Interventions** (`recommended_interventions`, select_multiple, optional): Select recommended therapies and referrals.

**Tips**

*   Be as specific as possible when answering each question.
*   Ensure you have the client's consent before completing this form.
*   Refer to the client's file for more information when necessary.
*   Use this form as part of a comprehensive assessment and treatment plan.

Please note that you should have the client's consent prior to completing this form.
