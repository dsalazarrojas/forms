# Reiki Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Reiki Consent Form is a tool used by therapists to gather information about a client's medical history, allergies, and other relevant health-related information.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Client Information: Fill in your information in this section, including your name and any other identifying details.
2.  Allergies: Select any allergies you have from the list.
3.  Medical History: Provide a detailed description of your medical history, including any chronic conditions, surgeries, and injuries.
4.  Medication: Mention any medications you are currently taking.
5.  Emergency Contact: If you have an emergency contact, select 'True' to indicate that you have one.
6.  Medical History Surgery: If you have any medical history that includes surgeries, describe them in this field.
7.  Medical History Injuries: If you have any medical history that includes injuries, describe them in this field.
8.  Medical History Fatigue: If you have any medical history that includes fatigue, describe it in this field.
9.  Medical History Other: If you have any other medical history that is not included in the previous fields, describe it in this field.
10.  Medical History Chronic: Select any chronic conditions you have from the list.
11.  Medical History Mental Health: If you have any medical history related to mental health, describe it in this field.
12.  Medical History Sleep: If you have any information about your sleep history, describe it in this field.
13.  Medical History Hygiene: If you have any information about your personal hygiene, describe it in this field.

## Tips

* Please provide as much detail as possible in each field.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask your therapist for guidance.
* Please fill in the form as accurately and honestly as possible to ensure the best possible outcome.
