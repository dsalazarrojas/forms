# Sleeping Habits Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect information about an individual's sleeping habits and patterns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions truthfully and accurately to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that apply to your sleeping habits and patterns.
3. Ensure that you complete all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Intro** (Page 1, Intro):
	+ This page serves as an introduction to the survey.
	+ Please read through this page to understand the purpose and instructions of the survey.
* **What is your wake up time** (Page 2, wake_time_page):
	+ Enter the time you usually wake up in the morning.
	+ Please format the time in 24-hour format (e.g., 06:00 AM).
* **AM or PM** (Page 2, wake_time_am_pm_page):
	+ Select the AM or PM of your wake-up time.
* **What is your bedtime** (Page 3, bedtime_page):
	+ Enter the time you usually go to bed at night.
	+ Please format the time in 24-hour format (e.g., 10:00 PM).
* **How many consecutive days do you sleep 8 hours or more each night?** (Page 4, wake_streak_page):
	+ Select the number of consecutive days you sleep 8 hours or more each night.
	+ Please select all the days that apply.
* **How would you rate the quality of your sleep?** (Page 5, sleep_quality_page):
	+ Rate the quality of your sleep from 0-1 hour to 11-12 hours.
* **Do you take naps?** (Page 6, naps_page):
	+ Select if you do or do not take naps.
* **How many times a week do you take naps?** (Page 6, naps_frequency_page):
	+ Select the number of times you take naps per week.
* **How long do naps usually last?** (Page 6, naps_duration_page):
	+ Select the length of your naps.
* **What is the environment where you sleep?** (Page 7, sleep_environment_page):
	+ Select the environment where you usually sleep.
* **What kind of bed do you sleep in?** (Page 7, bed_type_page):
	+ Select the type of bed you sleep in.
* **What position do you usually sleep in?** (Page 7, sleep_position_page):
	+ Select the position you usually sleep in.
* **Why do you wake up?** (Page 8, wake_reason_page):
	+ Select the reasons why you wake up.
* **What time do you usually go to bed?** (Page 9, sleep_schedule_page):
	+ Enter the time you usually go to bed at night.
* **AM or PM** (Page 9, sleep_schedule_am_pm_page):
	+ Select the AM or PM of your bedtime.
* **Do you usually sleep in during lunch?** (Page 10, sleep_schedule_lunch_page):
	+ Select if you usually sleep in during lunch.
* **How do you usually wake up?** (Page 10, wake_up_page):
	+ Select the way you usually wake up.
* **What time do you usually set your alarm clock to?** (Page 11, alarm_clock_page):
	+ Enter the time you usually set your alarm clock to.
* **What is the average length of your sleep?** (Page 12, sleep_duration_page):
	+ Select the average length of your sleep.
* **When was your longest consecutive wake-up streak?** (Page 13, wake_streak_start_page):
	+ Select the time when your longest consecutive wake-up streak started.
* **How long was your longest consecutive wake-up streak?** (Page 13, wake_streak_length_page):
	+ Enter the length of your longest consecutive wake-up streak.
* **What is your longest consecutive sleep streak?** (Page 14, sleep_streak_page):
	+ Select the length of your longest consecutive sleep streak.
* **When did your longest consecutive sleep streak start?** (Page 14, sleep_streak_start_page):
	+ Select the time when your longest consecutive sleep streak started.
* **When did your longest consecutive sleep streak end?** (Page 14, sleep_streak_end_page):
	+ Select the time when your longest consecutive sleep streak ended.
