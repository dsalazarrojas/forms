# Physical Therapy Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Physical Therapy Evaluation Form is a tool used by medical professionals to assess the physical condition of a patient. It is used to gather information about the patient's general information, physical evaluation, patient condition, pain level, physical function, range of motion, and muscle strengthening frequency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions in the sections below as accurately and completely as possible.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please refer to the field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (1)**: Provide any relevant medical history, allergies, or other relevant information that may affect your treatment.
* **Physical Evaluation (2)**: Describe your current physical condition, including any injuries or conditions that may affect your treatment.
* **Patient Condition (3)**: Answer the question about your patient condition by selecting one of the options:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
	+ Excellent
	+ Bad
* **Pain Level (4)**: Enter your current pain level on a numeric scale.
* **Physical Function (5)**: Describe your current physical function, including any limitations or difficulties you are experiencing.
* **Range of Motion (6)**: Answer the question about your range of motion by selecting one or more of the following options:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Limited
	+ Not Applicable
* **Muscle Strengthening (7)**: Answer the question about your muscle strengthening by selecting one or more of the following options:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Muscle Strengthening Frequency (8)**: Describe your muscle strengthening frequency, such as how often you perform exercises or activities to strengthen your muscles.

## Tips
- Please answer all questions accurately and completely.
- If you are unsure about a question, please consult the relevant medical professional.
- This form is for informational purposes only and should not be used to diagnose or treat any condition.
