# Therapist Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather feedback from therapists on their experience with a particular therapy session, program, or service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you have all necessary information before starting.
2. On each page, fill in the corresponding text field with your thoughts and feedback.
3. Ensure that all fields are filled in, but note that some fields are optional.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Information (Page 1)
**Client Name** (`<name>`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the client you are evaluating.
### Therapist Evaluation (Page 2)
**Feedback** (`<feedback>`, `text`, required): Provide a brief description of your experience with the client.
### Therapist Evaluation (Page 3)
**Feedback** (`<feedback>`, `text`, required): Share your thoughts on how the therapy session went.
### Additional Information (Page 4)
**Comments** (`<comments>`, `text`, optional): Share any additional thoughts or insights you have about the client or the therapy session.
