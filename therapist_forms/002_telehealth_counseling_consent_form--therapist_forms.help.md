# Telehealth Counseling Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain client consent for telehealth counseling services. It is a crucial document that ensures the client understands their rights, responsibilities, and expectations during the telehealth counseling process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the General Information section with your name, date of birth, and contact information.
2. Read and agree to the Client Consent section, which outlines the terms and conditions of the telehealth counseling service.
3. Review and understand the Telehealth Consent section, which describes the security measures and confidentiality of the online sessions.
4. Familiarize yourself with the HIPAA Information section, which explains your rights and responsibilities under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act.
5. Provide emergency contact information in case of any unforeseen circumstances.
6. Finally, sign the Client Signature section to acknowledge that you have read and understood the contents of this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information
* **Name** (`General Information`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`General Information`, text, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Contact Information** (`General Information`, text, required): Provide your email address and phone number where you can be reached for further communication.

### Client Consent
* **Client Consent** (`Client Consent`, text, required): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the telehealth counseling service. This section outlines your rights and responsibilities as a client.

### Telehealth Consent
* **Telehealth Consent** (`Telehealth Consent`, text, required): Review and understand the security measures and confidentiality of the online sessions. This includes the use of secure video conferencing tools and encryption methods to protect your identity and confidentiality.

### HIPAA Information
* **HIPAA Information** (`HIPAA Information`, text, required): Familiarize yourself with your rights and responsibilities under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act. This includes your right to access your medical records, request amendments to your records, and understand the limits of confidentiality.

### Emergency Contact
* **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, text, required): Provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person who can be reached in case of any unforeseen circumstances.

### Client Signature
* **Client Signature** (`Client Signature`, text, required): Sign this section to acknowledge that you have read and understood the contents of this form.
