# Mental Health Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about your mental health status, which will help us better understand your needs and provide you with appropriate support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete this form in its entirety, as indicated by the "required" field for each section.
2. For each field, please select or enter the most accurate and up-to-date information about your mental health status.
3. If you are unsure or have questions about any field, please do not hesitate to ask for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Intro Question** (<type>text, required:false):
	+ Please provide a brief description of your current mental health status.
* **Mood Level** (<type>number, required:true):
	+ Select your current overall mood level, where 0 is excellent, 1 is good, 2 is neutral, and 3 is poor.
* **Anxiety Level** (<type>number, required:true):
	+ Select your current anxiety level, where 0 is low, 1 is moderate, 2 is high, and 3 is very high.
* **Stress Level** (<type>number, required:true):
	+ Select your current stress level, where 0 is low, 1 is moderate, 2 is high, and 3 is very high.
* **Sleep Quality** (<type>select_one, required:true):
	+ Select your current sleep quality, where 0 is excellent, 1 is average, 2 is poor, and 3 is very poor.
* **Pain Level** (<type>select_multiple, required:false):
	+ Select all applicable pain levels you are experiencing, where 0 is none, 1 is mild, 2 is moderate, and 3 is severe.
* **Mental Health Condition** (<type>text, required:true):
	+ If you have a mental health condition, please provide a brief description.
* **Medication Usage** (<type>note, required:true):
	+ Please enter any relevant information about your medication usage.
* **Medical Notes** (<type>note, required:false):
	+ Enter any additional medical notes or information about your mental health status.
* **Follow Up Plan** (<type>note, required:false):
	+ If you have any follow-up plans or appointments, please enter the details.
