# treatment_plan_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain the patient's consent for their treatment plan. It's a document that ensures the patient understands their treatment and the expectations of the healthcare provider.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Form Header**: This page is a header for the form, providing an overview of what it's about.
2. **Patient Name**: Fill in your full name as per your identification. 
3. **Facility Name**: Enter your facility's name or address where treatment is being administered.
4. **Treatmend Plan**: Describe your treatment plan, including dates and times of sessions, expected outcomes and any medications to be administered.
5. **Patient Signature**: Sign this section with your full name to confirm you've read and understand this form.
6. **Date Signed**: Enter the date you've signed this form.
7. **Therapist Signature**: Sign this section if you're working with a therapist or healthcare provider. It's optional.
8. **Witness Signatures**: If you've had a witness present during this form signing, ask them to sign in this section. 
9. **Other Signatures**: If you have any other witnesses, choose their name from the dropdown list or add their names in the space provided.
10. **Facility Address**: Enter your facility's address for contact and billing purposes.
11. **Facility Phone**: Enter your facility's phone number for communication and billing purposes.
12. **Facility Email**: Enter your facility's email address for communication and billing purposes.
13. **Facility Fax**: Enter your facility's fax number if it's available.
14. **Facility Website**: Enter your facility's website if available.
15. **Facility License**: Enter your facility's license number for verification purposes.
16. **Facility Name**: Enter your facility's name for billing and contact purposes.
17. **Treatment Location**: Enter the location where treatment will occur.
18. **Facility Name 2**: Enter the name of your facility again for billing and contact purposes.
19. **Treatment Plan Date**: Enter the date of your treatment plan.
20. **Treatment Plan Time**: Enter the time of your treatment plan.
21. **Therapist Name**: Enter your therapist's name for billing and contact purposes.
22. **Therapist Title**: Enter your therapist's title for billing and contact purposes.
23. **Therapist Phone**: Enter your therapist's phone number for communication and billing purposes.
24. **Therapist Email**: Enter your therapist's email address for communication and billing purposes.

## Tips
- Always fill out the form carefully, as it's an important document for your treatment and healthcare details.
- If you're unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to reach out to your therapist or healthcare provider.
- Ensure you fill out the form correctly to avoid any delays or misunderstandings in your treatment process.
