# Depression Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Depression Questionnaire is a form designed to collect user information for the purpose of assessing and monitoring depression symptoms, scales, and notes. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure that the collected data is relevant and useful.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with your personal information, such as your email address and phone number.
2. Answer the question about your depression symptoms, choosing one of the options provided.
3. Enter your depression scale scores.
4. Provide additional notes about your depression.
5. Fill out any additional demographic information as requested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, `text`, required: false): Enter any relevant information about yourself that is not listed in the other fields.
* **User Email** (`user_email`, `email`, required: true): Enter your email address.
* **User Phone** (`user_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Depression Symptoms** (`depression_symptoms`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose one of the options provided to describe your depression symptoms.
* **Depression Scale** (`depression_scale`, `number`, required: true): Enter your depression scale score.
* **Depression Note** (`depression_note`, `note`, required: false): Provide additional notes about your depression.
* **User Date of Birth** (`user_date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth.
* **User Time of Day** (`user_time_of_day`, `time`, required: false): Enter your time of day.
* **Depression Scale 2** (`depression_scale_2`, `number`, required: false): Enter your second depression scale score.
* **Depression Scale 3** (`depression_scale_3`, `number`, required: true): Enter your third depression scale score.
* **Depression Note 2** (`depression_note_2`, `note`, required: false): Provide additional notes about your depression.
* **User Input 2** (`user_input_2`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional information about yourself.
* **User Email 2** (`user_email_2`, `email`, required: false): Enter your secondary email address.
* **User Phone 2** (`user_phone_2`, `text`, required: false): Enter your secondary phone number.
* **Depression Symptoms 2** (`depression_symptoms_2`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose one of the options provided to describe your secondary depression symptoms.
* **Depression Scale 4** (`depression_scale_4`, `number`, required: false): Enter your fourth depression scale score.
* **Depression Scale 5** (`depression_scale_5`, `number`, required: false): Enter your fifth depression scale score.
* **Depression Note 3** (`depression_note_3`, `note`, required: false): Provide additional notes about your depression.
* **User Input 3** (`user_input_3`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional information about yourself.
* **User Email 3** (`user_email_3`, `email`, required: false): Enter your tertiary email address.
* **User Phone 3** (`user_phone_3`, `text`, required: false): Enter your tertiary phone number.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that the collected data is relevant and useful.
* If you have filled out the first form, you do not need to fill out this one.
* Please make sure to fill out this form only once.
