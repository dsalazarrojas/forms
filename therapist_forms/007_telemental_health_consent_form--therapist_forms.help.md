# Telemental Health Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Telemental Health Consent Form is designed to obtain informed consent from patients for telemental health services. This form helps ensure that patients understand the services they are receiving and agree to participate in the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Review the form**: Take a moment to read through each field to ensure you understand what information is being requested.
2. Fill out the fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible, using the options provided.
3. **patient_info**: Enter the required patient information.
4. **consent_date**: Select the date you consent to the telemental health services.
5. **telemental_health_consent**: Provide a statement of consent for telemental health services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_info**: 
  + **label**: patient_info
  + **type**: text
  + **required**: false
  This field is for entering patient information, such as name, contact details, or other relevant personal data.
* **consent_date**:
  + **label**: consent_date
  + **type**: date
  + **required**: false
  This field is for selecting the date you consent to the telemental health services.
* **telemental_health_consent**:
  + **label**: telemental_health_consent
  + **type**: text
  + **required**: false
  This field is for providing a statement of consent for telemental health services, such as agreeing to participate in the program or receiving services.
