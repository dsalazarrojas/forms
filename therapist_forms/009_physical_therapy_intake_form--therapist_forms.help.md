# Physical Therapy Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Physical Therapy Intake Form is designed to collect information from patients about their personal and medical history, lifestyle, and other relevant details to help the physical therapist understand their needs and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer all questions honestly and accurately to provide the physical therapist with a clear understanding of your situation.
- You can type or write your answers in the space provided.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask your physical therapist for clarification.
- Please make sure to review and confirm your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient's Personal Info (id: 1)** (`text`, required: false)
	+ This field is for you to provide your name, contact information, and other identifying details.
* **Lifestyle (id: 2)** (`text`, required: false)
	+ Please describe your daily activities, habits, and lifestyle that may be relevant to your physical therapy treatment.
* **Family History (id: 3)** (`text`, required: false)
	+ Share any relevant family medical history that may affect your treatment.
* **Employment Info (id: 4)** (`text`, required: false)
	+ Provide details about your work or school schedule, job demands, and any related physical activities.
* **Past Medical History (id: 5)** (`text`, required: false)
	+ Share any past medical conditions, surgeries, or treatments that may impact your physical therapy.
* **Nature of Work (id: 6)** (`text`, required: false)
	+ Describe your work or activities that may be related to your physical therapy.
* **Medications (id: 7)** (`text`, required: false)
	+ List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Allergies (id: 8)** (`text`, required: false)
	+ Share any allergies or sensitivities you have.
* **Medical History (id: 9)** (`text`, required: false)
	+ Provide any relevant medical history, such as chronic conditions or past injuries.
* **Insurance Info (id: 10)** (`text`, required: false)
	+ Provide your insurance information, including policy number and provider name.
* **Emergency Contact (id: 11)** (`text`, required: false)
	+ List the contact information for someone who can reach you in case of an emergency.
* **Medical Conditions (id: 12)** (`text`, required: false)
	+ Share any medical conditions you are currently experiencing.
* **Surgical History (id: 13)** (`text`, required: false)
	+ List any previous surgeries or surgical procedures you have undergone.
* **Medical Procedure (id: 14)** (`text`, required: false)
	+ Describe any medical procedures or tests you have undergone.
* **Medical Tests (id: 15)** (`text`, required: false)
	+ List any medical tests or exams you have undergone.

## Tips
- Please be as detailed and thorough as possible when answering the questions.
- If you are unsure about any question, do not hesitate to ask your physical therapist for clarification.
- This form is designed to help your physical therapist understand your needs and conditions, so please provide honest and accurate information.
