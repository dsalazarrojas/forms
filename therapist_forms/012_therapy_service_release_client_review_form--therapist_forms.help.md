# Therapy Service Release & Client Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and ratings from clients who have received therapy services. This information helps therapists or therapy services evaluate the effectiveness of their work and make improvements for future clients.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please provide your rating of the therapy service you received. You can choose from the following options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Bad.
2. Provide a brief testimonial or testimonial consent statement, if applicable.
3. If you wish to provide additional feedback, please fill in the Client Feedback field.
4. If you are willing to share your contact information, please enter your email address and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rating**: This field is required and should reflect your overall impression of the therapy service. Choose from: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Bad.
* **Testimonial Consent**: This field is required, and you should select one of the following options if you are willing to share a testimonial about the therapy service.
* **Testimonial**: This field is required if you selected "Yes" for Testimonial Consent. Please provide a brief written statement about your experience with the therapy service.
* **Email**: This field is required and should be a valid email address.
* **Phone**: This field is required and should be a valid phone number.
* **Client Feedback**: This field is optional, but you can use it to provide additional feedback about your experience with the therapy service.
* **Rating Scale**: This field is required and should reflect the rating you selected in the "Rating" field. Please note that it is a pre-populated field that corresponds to your selected rating.
