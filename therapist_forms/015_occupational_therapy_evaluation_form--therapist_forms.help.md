# Occupational Therapy Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information on the patient's medical history, physical and cognitive function, as well as their treatment goals and follow-up schedules. It helps occupational therapists develop personalized treatment plans tailored to each patient's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the patient's name.
2. Enter the date of the visit.
3. Enter the patient's date of birth.
4. Describe the patient's chief complaint or reason for seeking treatment.
5. Briefly describe the patient's chief complaint or condition.
6. Describe the patient's medical history (if applicable).
7. Describe the results of the physical exam (if applicable).
8. Perform a cognitive functional screening to assess the patient's mental status.
9. Briefly describe the results of the cognitive functional screening.
10. Perform a physical functional screening to assess the patient's physical status.
11. Briefly describe the results of the physical functional screening.
12. Enter the patient's occupational therapy goals.
13. Briefly describe the patient's home visit details (if applicable).
14. List any other health conditions the patient may have.
15. Describe the patient's medication use.
16. Enter the patient's treatment goals.
17. Enter the discharge date.
18. Enter the follow-up date (if applicable).
19. Enter the next follow-up date (if applicable).
20. Have the patient sign and date the form.
21. Have the provider sign and date the form.
22. Add any additional notes or comments.
23. Enter the patient's phone number.
24. Enter the insurance provider (if applicable).
25. Enter the insurance card number (if applicable).
26. Enter the insurance payer (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name.
* **Date of Visit**: Enter the date of the visit.
* **Patient DOB**: Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Chief Complaint**: Describe the patient's chief complaint or reason for seeking treatment.
* **Chief Complaint Brief**: Briefly describe the patient's chief complaint or condition.
* **Medical History**: Describe the patient's medical history (if applicable).
* **Physical Exam**: Describe the results of the physical exam (if applicable).
* **Cognitive Functional Screening**: Perform a cognitive functional screening to assess the patient's mental status.
* **Cognitive Functional Screening Brief**: Briefly describe the results of the cognitive functional screening.
* **Physical Functional Screening**: Perform a physical functional screening to assess the patient's physical status.
* **Occupational Therapy Goals**: Enter the patient's occupational therapy goals.
* **Home Visit Details**: Briefly describe the patient's home visit details (if applicable).
* **Other Health Conditions**: List any other health conditions the patient may have.
* **Medication Use**: Describe the patient's medication use.
* **Treatment Goals**: Enter the patient's treatment goals.
* **Discharge Date**: Enter the discharge date.
* **Follow-up Date**: Enter the follow-up date (if applicable).
* **Next Follow-up**: Enter the next follow-up date (if applicable).
* **Patient Signature**: Have the patient sign and date the form.
* **Provider Signature**: Have the provider sign and date the form.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Insurance Provider**: Enter the insurance provider (if applicable).
* **Insurance Card Number**: Enter the insurance card number (if applicable).
* **Insurance Payer**: Enter the insurance payer (if applicable).

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure the best possible care for the patient.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with a colleague or supervisor before proceeding.
