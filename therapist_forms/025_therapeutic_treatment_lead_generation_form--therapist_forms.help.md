# Therapeutic Treatment Lead Generation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Therapeutic Treatment Lead Generation Form is a tool used to collect information from potential clients who are interested in seeking therapeutic treatment. This form helps us understand their needs and preferences to provide the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your name and contact information.
2. Answer any questions on the Lead Inquiry Form.
3. Provide any additional information you think is relevant to your therapeutic needs.
4. Enter your email address so we can get back to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your full name as you would like it to be used for communication.
* **Email**: Provide a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Lead Inquiry Form**:
	+ This section is used to ask us a few questions about your therapeutic needs and preferences.
	+ Please answer the questions as thoroughly and accurately as possible.
* **Additional Information**:
	+ This is a text field for you to provide any additional information you think is relevant to your therapeutic needs.
	+ This can include details about your goals, challenges, or concerns.
* **Therapist Name**:
	+ If you already have a preferred therapist, you can provide their name here.
	+ Otherwise, leave this field blank.
* **Address**:
	+ Provide your address for our records.
* **Contact Number**:
	+ Enter a phone number where you can be reached.
* **Phone**:
	+ This field is meant for you to provide a phone number where you can be reached.
	+ Please note that this field is not meant to be the same as the Contact Number field.
* **Lead Inquiry Form 2**:
	+ This section is similar to the Lead Inquiry Form 1, but is used to collect more information about your needs and preferences.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask for clarification before proceeding.
