# Psychiatry Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Psychiatry Feedback Form is used by therapists and healthcare professionals to provide feedback on patient interactions. This form ensures that a patient's medical record is accurately updated and any potential issues are documented.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the **Patient Name** field.
2. Provide feedback in the **Feedback** field.
3. Select the **Date Of Visit** and **Date Of Visit Time** fields to document the date and time of the patient's visit.
4. If applicable, enter the **Therapist Name** field.
5. Select a rating for the patient's feedback in the **Rating** field.
6. Choose the type of feedback provided in the **Feedback Type** field.
7. Add any additional comments or notes in the **Comments** field.
8. Enter any additional comments or notes in the **Additional Comments** field.
9. If necessary, fill in the **Contact Info** field for patient follow-up.
10. Enter the patient's email and phone number in the **Email** and **Patient Phone** fields, respectively.
11. If applicable, fill in the **Date Of Birth**, **Gender**, and **Age** fields.
12. Enter the **Medical Record Id**, **Therapist Id**, **Doctor Id**, and **Doctor Name** fields if applicable.
13. Select the **Medical Record Type** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name.
* **Feedback**: Provide feedback on the patient's interaction.
* **Date Of Visit**: Select the date of the patient's visit.
* **Therapist Name**: Enter the therapist's name (optional).
* **Date Of Visit Time**: Select the time of the patient's visit.
* **Rating**: Select a rating for the patient's feedback.
* **Feedback Type**: Choose the type of feedback provided.
* **Comments**: Add any comments or notes.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Contact Info**: Enter the patient's contact information (optional).
* **Email**: Enter the patient's email address.
* **Patient Phone**: Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Gender**: Select the patient's gender.
* **Age**: Enter the patient's age.
* **Medical Record Id**: Enter the patient's medical record ID.
* **Therapist Id**: Enter the therapist's ID (optional).
* **Doctor Id**: Enter the doctor's ID (optional).
* **Doctor Name**: Enter the doctor's name (optional).
* **Hospital Name**: Enter the hospital's name (optional).
* **Patient Location**: Enter the patient's location (optional).
* **Doctor Specialty**: Enter the doctor's specialty (optional).
* **Medical Record Type**: Select the type of medical record.
