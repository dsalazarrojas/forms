# teletherapy_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teletherapy Consent Form is used to gather essential information from patients before they start receiving teletherapy services. This form aims to collect the patient's basic contact details, informed consent for the telehealth services, and obtain their signature.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read each section carefully and ensure you understand what each field is for.
* Fill in your details accurately and thoroughly for each field.
* Sign the informed consent section, indicating your agreement to receive telehealth services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (`user_info`, text, required/optional): This section is used to collect the patient's basic contact information, such as name and any other relevant details.
* **Informed Consent** (`informed_consent`, text, required/optional): This is the informed consent for the telehealth services. Please sign this section to indicate your agreement to receive these services.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you regarding your treatment.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Add your phone number so we can reach you for any urgent matters.
* **eSignature** (`esignature`, text, required/optional): Sign this field with your electronic signature to confirm your agreement.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you're signing this form.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time you're signing this form.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required/optional): Sign this field with your handwritten signature to confirm your agreement.
* **eSignature 2** (`esignature_2`, text, required/optional): Another electronic signature for future reference.
* **eSignature 3** (`esignature_3`, text, required/optional): This is another electronic signature field.
* **eSignature 4** (`esignature_4`, text, required/optional): This electronic signature is for additional confirmation.
* **eSignature 5** (`esignature_5`, text, required/optional): This is the final electronic signature field.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required/optional): This section is used to add any additional notes or comments, if required.

Note: Some fields are optional but it's crucial to fill in every required field accurately.
