# Physical Therapy Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to obtain the informed consent of patients for physical therapy treatment. It helps to ensure that patients are aware of and agree to the treatment plan, associated risks, and their role in the treatment process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Please enter your name in the `patient_name` field.
2.  Enter your date of birth in the `date_of_birth` field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3.  Enter your home address in the `home_address` field.
4.  Enter your phone number in the `phone_number` field.
5.  Enter your email address in the `email_address` field. Ensure that it is in the correct format (e.g., `example@example.com`).
6.  In the `medical_history` field, please provide any relevant medical history that may impact your treatment.
7.  Enter the name of your physical therapist in the `therapist_name` field.
8.  If you have previously visited us for physical therapy, please enter the date of your last visit in the `date_of_last_visit` field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
9.  In the `treatment_plan` field, please describe your treatment plan and any concerns you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Patient Name**: The field for your name.
*   **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Home Address**: Enter your home address.
*   **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
*   **Email Address**: Enter your email address in the format example@example.com.
*   **Medical History**: Please provide any relevant medical history that may impact your treatment.
*   **Therapist Name**: Enter the name of your physical therapist.
*   **Date of Last Visit**: If you have previously visited us for physical therapy, enter the date of your last visit in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Treatment Plan**: Describe your treatment plan and any concerns you have.

## Tips

*   Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and thoroughly to ensure that your treatment plan is tailored to your needs.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask your physical therapist.
*   This form is a required step in the physical therapy process.
