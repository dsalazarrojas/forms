# Social Isolation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Isolation Survey is a tool used to measure an individual's level of social isolation and its impact on their physical and mental health.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. For each question, provide as much detail as possible.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please leave it blank and proceed to the next one.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **FirstName** (`FirstName`, text, required/optional): Please enter your first name.
* **LastName** (`LastName`, text, required/optional): Please enter your last name.
* **Address** (`Address`, text, required/optional): Please enter your address.
* **City** (`City`, text, required/optional): Please enter your city.
* **Zip** (`Zip`, number, required/optional): Please enter your zip code.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required/optional): Please enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required/optional): Please enter your email address.
* **DateLastIsolated** (`DateLastIsolated`, date, required/optional): Please enter the date you last felt isolated.
* **ReasonForIsolation** (`ReasonForIsolation`, text, required/optional): Please explain why you feel isolated.
* **IsolationFrequency** (`IsolationFrequency`, text, required/optional): Please describe how often you feel isolated.
* **TimeIsolated** (`TimeIsolated`, time, required/optional): Please estimate the amount of time you spend isolated.
* **FeelingsIsolated** (`FeelingsIsolated`, text, required/optional): Please describe how you feel when you are isolated.
* **PhysicalIsolation** (`PhysicalIsolation`, text, required/optional): Please describe your physical symptoms when feeling isolated.
* **EmotionalIsolation** (`EmotionalIsolation`, text, required/optional): Please describe your emotional symptoms when feeling isolated.
* **CognitiveIsolation** (`CognitiveIsolation`, text, required/optional): Please describe your cognitive symptoms when feeling isolated.
* **SocialConnection** (`SocialConnection`, text, required/optional): Please describe your social connections and relationships.
* **MentalHealth** (`MentalHealth`, text, required/optional): Please describe your mental health and well-being.
* **SelfCare** (`SelfCare`, text, required/optional): Please describe your self-care habits.
* **SupportNetwork** (`SupportNetwork`, text, required/optional): Please describe your support network and relationships.
* **FeelingsSupport** (`FeelingsSupport`, text, required/optional): Please describe your feelings of support from others.
* **SelfIsolationScore** (`SelfIsolationScore`, number, required/optional): Please rate your level of self-isolation.
* **SelfCareScore** (`SelfCareScore`, number, required/optional): Please rate your self-care habits.
* **MentalHealthScore** (`MentalHealthScore`, number, required/optional): Please rate your mental health.
* **FeelingsScore** (`FeelingsScore`, number, required/optional): Please rate your feelings of isolation.
* **PhysicalScore** (`PhysicalScore`, number, required/optional): Please rate your physical symptoms of isolation.

## Tips
- Answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
- Be as specific as possible when answering questions.
- If you are unsure about any question, please leave it blank and proceed to the next one.
- Please do not leave any blank fields without a clear explanation.
- If you need help or have questions, please contact the support team.
