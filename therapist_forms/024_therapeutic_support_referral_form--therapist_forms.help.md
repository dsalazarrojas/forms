# Therapeutic Support Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about a patient's treatment and care needs for therapeutic support services. It helps healthcare professionals determine the type and level of support required.

## How To Complete This Form
1. First, provide general patient information on page 2.
2. Explain the reason for referral on page 3.
3. Indicate the type of therapeutic services required on page 4.
4. Select the members of the patient's care team on pages 7 and 9.
5. Schedule appointments and services on pages 16 and 17.
6. Provide more detailed information about the patient's needs on page 24.
7. Add any additional comments or notes on page 25.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Patient Information** (page 2, required): Provide the patient's name, date of birth, and contact information.
- **Referral Reason** (page 3, required): Explain why the patient requires therapeutic support.
- **Therapeutic Services Required** (page 4, required): Select the type of support services the patient needs (e.g., counseling, medication management, etc.).
- **Therapeutic Services Required** (page 4, required): Select the type of support services the patient needs.
- **Patient Care Team** (page 7, optional): Choose the members of the patient's care team (e.g., social worker, psychiatrist, etc.).
- **Social Worker** (page 9, optional): Select whether a social worker is part of the patient's care team.
- **Family Member** (page 9, optional): Select whether a family member is part of the patient's care team.
- **Therapist** (page 9, optional): Select whether a therapist is part of the patient's care team.
- **Psychiatrist** (page 9, optional): Select whether a psychiatrist is part of the patient's care team.
- **Discharge Care Coordinator** (page 9, optional): Select whether a discharge care coordinator is part of the patient's care team.
- **Case Manager** (page 9, optional): Select whether a case manager is part of the patient's care team.
- **Other** (page 9, optional): Select whether other members are part of the patient's care team.
- **Date of Service Request** (page 16, optional): Schedule the appointment date for the service request.
- **Time of Service Request** (page 17, optional): Schedule the appointment time for the service request.
- **Date of Service** (page 18, required): Schedule the date of the service.
- **Time of Service** (page 19, required): Schedule the time of the service.
- **Follow-up Date** (page 20, optional): Schedule a follow-up date.
- **Follow-up Time** (page 21, optional): Schedule a follow-up time.
- **Service Requestor** (page 22, optional): Provide more information about the person requesting the service.
- **Contact Person** (page 23, optional): Provide contact information for the person responsible for the service.
- **Service Requested** (page 24, optional): Provide more detailed information about the service required.
- **Additional Comments** (page 25, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about the patient's situation.

Note: This help guide is based on the provided YAML structure and assumes that the form is used for referring patients for therapeutic support services.
