# Music Therapy Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this Music Therapy Intake Form is to gather essential information about a patient's medical history, music history, therapy goals, and other relevant details to provide personalized and effective music therapy sessions. This form helps us understand each patient's unique needs and develop a suitable treatment plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the form carefully and accurately** as it will be used to understand your needs for music therapy.
2. **Be as detailed as possible** in answering each question to ensure we have a complete understanding of your situation.
3. **If you are unsure about any section**, please do not hesitate to ask your music therapist for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (page 1)**:
	+ This section is used to collect basic information about you, such as your name and contact details.
* **Medical History (page 2)**:
	+ Describe any medical conditions you have or have had in the past. This will help us understand any potential physical or mental health implications for your music therapy sessions.
* **Music History (page 3)**:
	+ Share any relevant information about your musical background, including your favorite genres, instruments, or musical experiences. This will help us tailor your music therapy sessions to your preferences.
* **Music Therapy Goals (page 4)**:
	+ Specify what you hope to achieve through music therapy, such as stress relief, emotional expression, or physical rehabilitation. Be as specific as possible.
* **Therapist Information (page 5)**:
	+ If you have any specific preferences or requests for your music therapist, such as a certain music style or tempo, please let us know.
* **Patient/Caregiver Information (page 6)**:
	+ If you are a caregiver, please provide information about the patient's medical status, any relevant medical equipment they may require, or special instructions we should follow.
* **Contact Information (page 7)**:
	+ Provide your name, email, and phone number so we can keep in touch and arrange sessions.

## Tips
Please remember that this form is not a substitute for an in-person consultation, but it will help us start the process of preparing for your music therapy sessions. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask.
