# Physical Therapist Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and ratings from patients about their experiences with a physical therapist. It helps the therapist to improve their services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the patient's name.
2. Choose the date of the evaluation.
3. Rate the therapist's quality of care, communication skills, empathy, knowledge and expertise, professionalism, office environment, and waiting time.
4. Add any additional comments.
5. Sign the form to confirm that it has been read and understood.
6. Add the patient's signature and contact information.
7. Add any additional comments.
8. Sign the form again to confirm that it has been completed accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as the patient.
* **Therapist Name** (`therapist_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of the physical therapist who provided you with care.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required): Please enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Quality Of Care** (`therapist_quality_of_care`, select_multiple, not required): Rate the quality of care provided by the physical therapist. You can choose "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
* **Communication Skills** (`communication_skills`, select_one, not required): Rate the communication skills of the physical therapist. You can choose "Effective", "Ineffective", or "Neutral".
* **Empathy** (`empthy`, select_multiple, not required): Rate how much empathy the physical therapist showed during your visit. You can choose "Highly", "Somewhat", or "Not At All".
* **Time Spent Per Visit** (`time_spent_per_visit`, select_multiple, not required): Rate how much time was spent per visit. You can choose "Sufficient", "Not Sufficient", or "More than Sufficient".
* **Knowledge And Expertise** (`knowledge_expertise`, select_multiple, not required): Rate the knowledge and expertise of the physical therapist. You can choose "Highly", "Somewhat", or "Not At All".
* **Professionalism** (`professionalism`, select_multiple, not required): Rate the professionalism of the physical therapist. You can choose "Highly", "Somewhat", or "Not At All".
* **Office Environment** (`office_environment`, select_multiple, not required): Rate the office environment. You can choose "Clean", "Disorganized", or "Neutral".
* **Waiting Time** (`waiting_time`, select_multiple, not required): Rate the waiting time. You can choose "Short", "Long", or "Neutral".
* **Recommendations For Improvement** (`recommendations_for_improvement`, text, not required): Add any recommendations for improvement.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_multiple, not required): Rate your overall satisfaction. You can choose "Highly", "Somewhat", or "Not At All".
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, not required): Rate your satisfaction on a scale of 1 to 10.
* **Patient Comments** (`patient_comments`, text, not required): Add any comments from the patient.
* **Therapist Comments** (`therapist_comments`, text, not required): Add any comments from the therapist.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, not required): Add any additional comments.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, not required): Please sign the form to confirm that it has been read and understood.
* **Therapist Signature** (`therapist_signature`, text, not required): Please sign the form to confirm that it has been completed accurately.
* **Patient Agreement** (`patient_agreement`, text, not required): Please confirm that you have read and understood the form.
* **Therapist Agreement** (`therapist_agreement`, text, not required): Please confirm that you have completed the form accurately.
* **Form Completion** (`form_completion`, date, not required): Please enter the date of form completion.
* **Patient Name 2** (`patient_name_2`, text, not required): Please enter your name as the patient (again).
* **Patient Contact Info** (`patient_contact_info`, text, not required): Please enter your contact information.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, text, not required): Please sign the form to confirm that it has been completed accurately.
