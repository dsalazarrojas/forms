# Hypnosis Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for hypnosis therapy sessions to collect information about a patient's medical history, current complaints, and allergies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form completely and accurately as much as possible.
2. Review each page carefully and make sure to answer all relevant questions.
3. Do not leave any fields blank unless explicitly marked as optional.
4. If you are unsure about any question, consult with your hypnotherapist.
