# Chair Massage Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from clients about their massage preferences, medical conditions, and other relevant details for a safe and effective chair massage experience. Please fill out this form to ensure that our therapists are well-prepared to provide you with the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your full name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Provide your primary contact number in the "Phone Number" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field for appointment confirmations.
4. For "Date of Birth", enter your birth date if you wish to share this information.
5. In "Street Address", enter your home address.
6. In "City, State, Zip Code", enter your location information.
7. If you are a new client, select "Yes" in "First Time With Our Service".
8. Choose how you heard about us in "How Did You Hear About Us".
9. Select your preferred day of the week for the appointment in "Preferred Day for Appointment".
10. Choose your preferred time of day for the appointment in "Preferred Time for Appointment".
11. Select the areas of focus for the massage in "Areas of Focus for Massage".
12. Select the pressure preference for the massage in "Pressure Preference".
13. If applicable, enter any health conditions or injuries in "Health Conditions or Injuries".
14. In "Specific Pain or Problem Areas", describe any pain areas you experience.
15. If necessary, list any allergies or sensitivities to oils, lotions, or other products in "Allergies or Sensitivities".
16. If applicable, list your current medications for safety awareness in "Current Medications".
17. Select your preferred communication method in "Communication Preference".
18. Choose the payment method in "Payment Method".
19. Finally, if you have any special requests or notes, enter them in "Special Requests or Notes".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Provide your primary contact number for appointment scheduling.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address for appointment confirmations and communication.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your birth date if you wish to share this information.
* **Street Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your home address for location information.
* **City, State, Zip Code** (`city_state_zip`, text, optional): Enter your location details.
* **First Time With Our Service** (`first_time_client`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" if you are a new client.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_heard_about_us`, select_one, optional): Choose how you heard about our service (e.g., Word of Mouth, Social Media, Google Search, etc.).
* **Preferred Day for Appointment** (`preferred_appointment_day`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred day of the week for the appointment.
* **Preferred Time for Appointment** (`preferred_appointment_time`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred time of day for the appointment.
* **Areas of Focus for Massage** (`massage_areas`, select_multiple, required): Select areas you wish to focus on during the massage.
* **Pressure Preference** (`pressure_preference`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred pressure level for the massage.
* **Health Conditions or Injuries** (`health_conditions`, text, optional): If applicable, enter any health conditions or injuries.
* **Specific Pain or Problem Areas** (`pain_areas`, text, optional): Describe any pain areas you experience.
* **Allergies or Sensitivities** (`allergies`, text, optional): If necessary, list any allergies or sensitivities to oils, lotions, or other products.
* **Current Medications** (`medication_list`, text, optional): If applicable, list your current medications.
* **Communication Preference** (`communication_preferences`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred communication method (e.g., Phone, Email, Text Message).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred payment method (e.g., Cash, Credit Card, Debit Card, Check).
* **Special Requests or Notes** (`special_requests`, text, optional): If you have any special requests or notes, enter them here.
