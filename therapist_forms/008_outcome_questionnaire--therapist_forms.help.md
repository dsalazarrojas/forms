# Outcome Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Outcome Questionnaire is used to track client outcomes and feedback for program evaluation and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by carefully reading the form and understanding the sections and questions it contains.
- Answer each question according to your best understanding of your experience in the program.
- Make sure to provide as much detail as possible, but only answer questions that are relevant and applicable to your experience.
- Review your answers and ensure they accurately reflect your thoughts and feelings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction (Page 1)
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, required: false): This is a brief overview of the form and its purpose.

### Client Identification (Page 1)
* **Client Identification** (`client_identification`, `select_one`, required: false): Please indicate whether you are a client of the program. Choose 'Yes' if you are currently participating in the program or 'No' if you are not.

### Demographic Information (Page 1)
* **Demographic Information** (`demographic_info`, `text`, required: false): Please provide any relevant demographic information that may be helpful for the program to know about you.

### Program Participation (Page 1)
* **Program Participation** (`program_participation`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate how you have been participating in the program, for example, whether you are currently in the program or have completed it.

### Program Services (Page 1)
* **Program Services** (`program_services`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select which services you have received from the program.

### Outcomes (Page 1)
* **Outcomes** (`outcomes`, `date`, required: false): Please provide the date of the last time you experienced any outcomes from the program.

### Outcome Scale 1 (Page 1)
* **Outcome Scale 1** (`outcome_scale_1`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select which outcomes you have experienced from the program.

### Outcome Scale 2 (Page 1)
* **Outcome Scale 2** (`outcome_scale_2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select which outcomes you have experienced from the program.

### Program Evaluation (Page 1)
* **Program Evaluation** (`program_evaluation`, `text`, required: false): Please share your thoughts on how the program has helped or could be improved.

### Program Comments (Page 1)
* **Program Comments** (`program_comments`, `text`, required: false): Please provide any additional comments or feedback about the program.

### Clinician Comments (Page 1)
* **Clinician Comments** (`clinician_comments`, `text`, required: false): Please provide any additional comments or feedback from your clinician.

### Client Signature (Page 1)
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, `text`, required: false): Please sign to confirm your answers.

### Therapist Signature (Page 1)
* **Therapist Signature** (`therapist_signature`, `text`, required: false): Please sign to confirm your role in assisting the client.

### Program Services 2 (Page 1)
* **Program Services 2** (`program_services_2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select which additional services you have received from the program.
