# Animal Therapy Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animal Therapy Program Registration form is designed to collect necessary information from individuals who wish to enroll in the program. This form is required to be completed by all participants to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including first name, last name, email address, phone number, date of birth, and age.
2. If applicable, provide details about your parent or guardian, including their name, phone number, and email address.
3. Enter your address, including street address, city, state, and zip code.
4. If applicable, provide medical information, including primary physician's name, physician's phone number, any medical conditions, allergies, medications, and physical limitations.
5. Specify your prior experience with animals, if any.
6. Select your primary therapy goal and preferred animal therapy type.
7. Choose your preferred session frequency, session time, and specify which days you are available for sessions.
8. Confirm that your health information is accurate, and that you consent to animal contact and therapy treatment.
9. Provide emergency contact details, including name, phone number, and relationship to the emergency contact.
10. Finally, agree to the program terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (Required)**: Enter your first name as it is written on your official documents.
* **Last Name (Required)**: Enter your last name as it is written on your official documents.
* **Email Address (Required)**: Enter your active email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number (Required)**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth (Required)**: Enter your birth date in the MM/DD format (e.g., 01/01/2000).
* **Age (Optional)**: Enter your current age.
* **Parent or Guardian Name (Optional)**: If you are under 18, enter the name of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone (Optional)**: If you are under 18, enter the phone number of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Email (Optional)**: If you are under 18, enter the email address of your parent or guardian.
* **Street Address (Required)**: Enter your residential address, including street name and number.
* **City (Required)**: Enter the name of the city where you reside.
* **State (Required)**: Enter the name of the state or province where you reside.
* **Zip Code (Required)**: Enter the postal code of your area.
* **Primary Physician Name (Optional)**: Enter the name of your primary physician.
* **Physician Phone Number (Optional)**: Enter the phone number of your primary physician.
* **Medical Conditions or Disabilities (Optional)**: If you have any medical conditions or disabilities, describe them here.
* **Allergies or Sensitivities (Optional)**: If you have any allergies or sensitivities, describe them here.
* **Current Medications (Optional)**: If you are taking any medications, list them here.
* **Mobility or Physical Limitations (Optional)**: If you have any mobility or physical limitations, describe them here.
* **Animal Allergies (Optional)**: If you have any allergies to animals, describe them here.
* **Prior Experience with Animals (Required)**: Describe your experience with animals, if any.
* **Primary Therapy Goal (Required)**: Select the main reason you wish to participate in the program.
* **Preferred Animal Therapy Type (Required)**: Choose the type of animal therapy you prefer.
* **Preferred Session Frequency (Required)**: Choose how often you would like to attend sessions.
* **Preferred Session Time (Required)**: Choose the time of day you prefer to attend sessions.
* **Available Days (Required)**: Select the days you are available to attend sessions.
* **Confirm Health Information is Accurate (Required)**: Verify that your health information is accurate.
* **Consent to Animal Contact (Required)**: Give consent to direct contact with animals.
* **Consent to Therapy Treatment (Required)**: Give consent to therapy treatment.
* **Consent to Photography for Progress (Optional)**: If you agree to having photos taken for documentation.
* **Emergency Contact Name (Required)**: Enter the name of the person to contact in an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (Required)**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship (Required)**: Describe your relationship to the emergency contact.
* **Agree to Program Terms and Conditions (Required)**: Agree to the terms and conditions of the program.

## Tips
* Please make sure to answer all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact our support team for guidance.
* Make sure to review all information carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to us at [support email/phone number].
