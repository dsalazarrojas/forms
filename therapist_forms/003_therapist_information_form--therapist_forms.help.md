# Therapist Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Therapist Information Form is designed to collect essential details from therapists for internal purposes. It helps authorized personnel to verify and store therapist information in a centralized system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: You do not have to fill in all fields, but please complete the mandatory ones.
2. Enter your therapist name, work phone, and work email in the corresponding fields.
3. If applicable, provide your work address, work city, work state, and work zip for your location information.
4. Select your specialty(ies) from the provided options.
5. Enter your fax number (if available).
6. Fill in your therapist name suffix, first name, last name, and middle name as per your preference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Therapist Name** (`therapist_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as you prefer to be addressed.
* **Therapist Email** (`therapist_email`, email, optional): Enter your work email address if you have one.
* **Work Phone** (`work_phone`, number, optional): Enter your work phone number.
* **Work Email** (`work_email`, email, optional): Enter your work email address if you have one.
* **Therapist License Number** (`therapist_license_number`, text, optional): Enter your professional license number (if applicable).
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your work address.
* **Work Address** (`work_address`, text, optional): Enter your work address.
* **Work City** (`work_city`, text, optional): Enter your work city.
* **Work State** (`work_state`, select_one, optional): Select your work state.
* **Work Zip** (`work_zip`, number, optional): Enter your work zip code.
* **Work Country** (`work_country`, select_one, optional): Select your work country.
* **Fax Number** (`fax_number`, number, optional): Enter your fax number (if available).
* **Specialty** (`specialty`, select_multiple, optional): Select your areas of specialty.
* **Therapist Name Suffix** (`therapist_name_suffix`, text, optional): Enter any name suffix you prefer to be addressed with.
* **Therapist Name First** (`therapist_name_first`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Therapist Name Last** (`therapist_name_last`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Therapist Name Middle** (`therapist_name_middle`, text, optional): Enter your middle name.

## Tips

- Please ensure that you fill in the mandatory fields correctly.
- Use the provided options as reference for locations and specialties.
- If you are unsure about a particular field, you can contact your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
