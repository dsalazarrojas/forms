# Group Therapy Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is meant to inform and consent clients for group therapy sessions. It ensures that clients understand the therapy's nature, expectations, and payment terms.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully and honestly.
2. Review the form and make sure you understand the content.
3. Sign the form to confirm your consent.
4. Return the form to the therapist or organization providing the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. <label>Group Therapy Informed Consent Form</label> (`group_therapy_informed_consent_form`, `text`, required=false)

This is the title of the form, and it serves as a header for the rest of the form.

### 2. <label>Client Name</label> (`client_name`, `text`, required=false)

Please fill in your name as it appears on your identification.

### 3. <label>Client Address</label> (`client_address`, `text`, required=false)

Provide your address for contact and billing purposes.

### 4. <label>Date of Birth</label> (`client_dob`, `date`, required=false)

Enter your date of birth.

### 5. <label>Group Therapy Services</label> (`group_therapy_services`, `text`, required=false)

Describe the group therapy services you are interested in.

### 6. <label>Group Therapy Fees</label> (`group_therapy_fees`, `number`, required=false)

Indicate the frequency and cost of the group therapy sessions you are interested in.

### 7. <label>Payment Method</label> (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required=true)

Choose how you would like to pay for the group therapy services:

*   Cash
*   Check
*   Online

### 8. <label>Emergency Contact</label> (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required=false)

Provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.

### 9. <label>Client Agreement</label> (`client_agreement`, `text`, required=false)

Check the box to confirm that you have read and understand the group therapy services and policies.

### 10. <label>Group Therapy Fees Frequency</label> (`group_therapy_fees_frequency`, `text`, required=false)

Describe the frequency of the group therapy sessions you are interested in.

### 11. <label>Therapist Signature</label> (`therapist_signature`, `note`, required=false)

The therapist will sign this section after reading and discussing the form with you.

### 12. <label>Client Signature</label> (`client_signature`, `note`, required=false)

You must sign this section to confirm that you understand the form's content.

### 13. <label>Date</label> (`date`, `date`, required=false)

The date the form is completed.

## Tips

*   Make sure to carefully read and understand the group therapy services and policies.
*   Ask questions or seek clarification if you are unsure about any part of the form.
*   Return the completed form to the therapist or organization providing the service.
