# Therapist Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from therapists about a patient's mental health diagnosis, medical conditions, and goals/objectives. This information will be used for administrative or research purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide your phone number if you have one.
3. Describe the patient's mental health diagnosis in the "Mental Health Diagnosis" field.
4. List any relevant medical conditions in the "Medical Conditions" field.
5. Mention any current medications in the "Medication" field.
6. Outline the patient's goals and objectives in the "Goals" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Please enter your name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Provide your phone number if you have one.
* **Mental Health Diagnosis**: Describe the patient's mental health diagnosis, such as depression, anxiety, or post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD).
* **Medical Conditions**: List any relevant medical conditions that may be related to the patient's mental health diagnosis.
* **Medication**: Mention any current medications the patient is taking.
* **Goals**: Outline the patient's short-term and long-term goals, including any specific objectives or outcomes desired.
* **Objectives**: Outline any specific objectives or outcomes desired by the patient. (Note: This field is optional and may be used to gather more detailed information about the patient's goals and objectives.)
