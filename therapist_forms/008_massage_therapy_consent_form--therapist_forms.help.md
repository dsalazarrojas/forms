# Massage Therapy Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Massage Therapy Consent Form is used to collect information from clients who are receiving massage therapy services. This form helps the massage therapist understand the client's medical conditions, contact information, and preferences for their massage session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your full name and contact information, including a valid phone number and email address.
2. List any medical conditions you are currently experiencing or have experienced in the past.
3. If you have any allergies or medical allergies, please list them.
4. If you are currently taking any medications, please list them.
5. Provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
6. Choose the type of massage style you prefer (e.g., Swedish, Deep Tissue, Hot Stone).
7. Indicate the areas of your body you would like to be massaged.
8. Select how frequently you would like to receive massage sessions (e.g., daily, weekly, bi-weekly).
9. Choose how you would like to pay for your massage sessions (e.g., cash, credit card, insurance).
10. Add any additional notes or comments about your massage preferences.
