# Anger Management Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps therapists and counselors assess an individual's anger management skills, patterns, and motivations for change.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
2. Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
3. If a question does not apply to you, please select "Not applicable" or "None" as the answer.
4. Please do not leave any questions blank, as this can affect the accuracy of the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Client Full Name** (`text`, `Client Full Name`, `required`): Please enter your full legal name.
* **2. Date of Birth** (`date`, `Date of Birth`, `required`): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **3. Evaluation Date** (`date`, `Evaluation Date`, `required`): Enter the date of assessment.
* **4. Evaluator Name** (`text`, `Evaluator Name`, `required`): Enter the name of the professional conducting the evaluation.
* **5. Anger Management Evaluation** (`note`, `Anger Management Evaluation`, `optional`): This is a brief introduction to the evaluation. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **6. Reason for Evaluation** (`select_one`, `Reason for Evaluation`, `required`): Select the reason why this evaluation is being conducted.
	+ Self-referral: You referred yourself for evaluation.
	+ Court ordered: You were ordered by a court to undergo this evaluation.
	+ Employer referral: Your employer or school recommended this evaluation.
	+ School referral: Your school or school administration recommended this evaluation.
	+ Family recommendation: A family member recommended this evaluation.
	+ Therapist recommendation: A therapist or counselor recommended this evaluation.
* **7. How often do you experience Anger** (`select_one`, `How often do you experience Anger`, `required`): Select how often you experience anger episodes.
	+ Rarely - less than once a month
	+ Occasionally - 1-3 times per month
	+ Sometimes - 1-2 times per week
	+ Often - 3-5 times per week
	+ Very often - daily or multiple times daily
* **8. How long do your Anger episodes typically last** (`select_one`, `How long do your Anger episodes typically last`, `required`): Select the average duration of your anger episodes.
	+ Less than 5 minutes
	+ 5-15 minutes
	+ 15-30 minutes
	+ 30 minutes to 1 hour
	+ Several hours
	+ Most of the day
* **9. How intense is your Anger typically** (`select_one`, `How intense is your Anger typically`, `required`): Select the average intensity level of your anger.
	+ Mild - irritation or annoyance
	+ Moderate - clearly angry but controlled
	+ Strong - difficult to control
	+ Severe - often lose control
	+ Extreme - complete loss of control
* **10. What commonly triggers your Anger** (`select_multiple`, `What commonly triggers your Anger`, `required`): Select all that apply.
	+ Being criticized
	+ Feeling ignored or dismissed
	+ Traffic or delays
	+ Work or school stress
	+ Relationship conflicts
	+ Financial problems
	+ Feeling unfairly treated
	+ Technology failures
	+ Other
* **11. What physical signs do you notice when Angry** (`select_multiple`, `What physical signs do you notice when Angry`, `required`): Select all that apply.
	+ Racing heart
	+ Clenched fists or jaw
	+ Sweating
	+ Trembling
	+ Feeling hot
	+ Muscle tension
	+ Headache
	+ Stomach upset
* **12. How do you typically behave when Angry** (`select_multiple`, `How do you typically behave when Angry`, `required`): Select all that apply.
	+ Raise my voice
	+ Yell or scream
	+ Use harsh language
	+ Become silent or withdraw
	+ Leave the situation
	+ Make threats
	+ Break or throw things
	+ Physical aggression toward objects
	+ Physical aggression toward people
* **13. How has Anger affected your Relationships** (`select_one`, `How has Anger affected your Relationships`, `required`): Select the impact on personal relationships.
	+ No impact
	+ Minor strain
	+ Moderate problems
	+ Significant damage
	+ Lost important relationships
* **14. How has Anger affected your Work or School** (`select_one`, `How has Anger affected your Work or School`, `required`): Select the impact on professional or academic performance.
	+ No impact
	+ Minor issues
	+ Moderate problems
	+ Significant difficulties
	+ Job loss or academic consequences
* **15. Have you had Legal Issues related to Anger** (`select_one`, `Have you had Legal Issues related to Anger`, `required`): Select whether you have had legal issues related to anger.
	+ False
	+ Yes - minor incidents
	+ Yes - charges filed
	+ Yes - court-mandated treatment
* **16. What methods do you currently use to Manage Anger** (`select_multiple`, `What methods do you currently use to Manage Anger`, `required`): Select all that apply.
	+ Deep breathing
	+ Walking away
	+ Exercise
	+ Talking to someone
	+ Meditation or prayer
	+ Journaling
	+ Substance use
	+ None - no effective methods
* **17. Is there a Family History of Anger Issues** (`select_one`, `Is there a Family History of Anger Issues`, `optional`): Select whether you have a family history of anger issues.
	+ No known history
	+ Yes - one parent
	+ Yes - both parents
	+ Yes - other family members
	+ Unsure
* **18. Do you use Alcohol or Drugs** (`select_one`, `Do you use Alcohol or Drugs`, `required`): Select whether you use alcohol or drugs.
	+ False
	+ Occasionally
	+ Regularly
	+ Daily
* **19. How motivated are you to Change your Anger Patterns** (`select_one`, `How motivated are you to Change your Anger Patterns`, `required`): Select your level of motivation for change.
	+ Not motivated - here by requirement
	+ Slightly motivated
	+ Moderately motivated
	+ Very motivated
	+ Extremely motivated - ready to start now
* **20. What are your Goals for Anger Management** (`text`, `What are your Goals for Anger Management`, `required`): Enter your goals for anger management.
* **21. Have you received Anger Management Treatment before** (`select_one`, `Have you received Anger Management Treatment before`, `required`): Select whether you have received anger management treatment before.
	+ No - first time
	+ Yes - completed program
	+ Yes - did not complete
	+ Yes - currently in treatment
* **22. Evaluator Observations and Recommendations** (`text`, `Evaluator Observations and Recommendations`, `optional`): This is an optional field for professional notes and recommendations.
* **23. Assessed Risk Level** (`select_one`, `Assessed Risk Level`, `optional`): Select the assessed risk level for professional use only.
	+ Low risk
	+ Moderate risk
	+ High risk
	+ Requires immediate intervention

## Tips

* Answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
* If a question does not apply to you, please select "Not applicable" or "None" as the answer.
* Do not leave any questions blank, as this can affect the accuracy of the assessment.
* Please keep the answers concise and clear.
