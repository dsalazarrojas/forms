# neurosomatic_breath_client_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The neurosomatic_breath_client_evaluation_form is designed to collect information about a client's respiratory system and their breathing patterns. This includes details about the client's respiratory system, breathing difficulties, treatment plans, progress, goals, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your information accurately and thoroughly. Each field is described in detail below.
2. Make sure to answer all required fields (if any).
3. Review your work before submission to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details** (<label>Client Details</label>): This is the first field to fill out. Enter your client's details, including their name and any other relevant information.
* **Breath Patterns** (<label>Breath Patterns</label>): This field asks about your client's breathing patterns. Select all that apply from the provided options.
* **Evaluation** (<label>Evaluation</label>): Describe your current evaluation of the client's situation.
* **Additional Comments** (<label>Additional Comments</label>): Provide any additional comments or information about the client's condition.
* **Breathing Difficulties** (<label>Breathing Difficulties</label>): Select whether the client has breathing difficulties or not.
* **Treatment Plans** (<label>Treatment Plans</label>): This field allows you to select treatment plans that apply to the client.
* **Progress** (<label>Progress</label>): Describe any progress made by the client during their treatment.
* **Client Goals** (<label>Client Goals</label>): Describe the client's goals for their treatment.
* **Assessment Date** (<label>Assessment Date</label>): Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Next Visit** (<label>Next Visit</label>): Enter the date of the next visit.
* **Therapist** (<label>Therapist</label>): Enter the name of the therapist conducting the assessment.
* **Contact Info** (<label>Contact Info</label>): Enter any relevant contact information for the client.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all required fields accurately.
* Double-check your work before submission to ensure accuracy.
* If you're unsure about any field, consult with the therapist conducting the assessment.
