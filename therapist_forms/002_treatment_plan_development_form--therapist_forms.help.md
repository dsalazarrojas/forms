# treatment_plan_development_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather essential information from the patient, including their personal details, medical history, and treatment objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the patient's personal details (First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth, Email, Phone Number, Medical Record Number).
2. Enter the patient's medical information (Primary Diagnosis, Chief Complaint, Medical History, Family History, Social History).
3. Enter the treatment plan and treatment goals.
4. Enter review and progress notes.
5. Select treatment plans from the list.
6. (Optional) Enter additional information (Insurance, Other Notes, Medical Team, Other).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1): Enter the patient's first name.
* **Last Name** (2): Enter the patient's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (3): Enter the patient's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Email** (4): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Phone Number** (5): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Medical Record Number** (6): Enter the patient's medical record number.
* **Primary Diagnosis** (7): Enter the patient's primary diagnosis.
* **Chief Complaint** (8): Describe the patient's chief complaint.
* **Treatment Plan** (9): Outline the patient's treatment plan.
* **Medical History** (10): Describe the patient's medical history.
* **Family History** (11): Describe the patient's family history.
* **Social History** (12): Describe the patient's social history.
* **Review Date** (13): Enter the date of the review.
* **Review Time** (14): Enter the time of the review.
* **Assigned Provider** (15): Enter the name of the assigned provider.
* **Treatment Goals** (16): Enter the treatment goals.
* **Progress Notes** (17): Enter the patient's progress notes.
* **Treatment Plan Development** (18): Select from a list of treatment plans.
* **Insurance** (19): Enter any additional insurance information (optional).
* **Other Notes** (20): Enter any other notes (optional).
* **Medical Team** (21): Enter the name of the medical team (optional).
* **Other** (22): Enter any other information (optional).
* **Review Status** (23): Enter the review status.
* **Follow-up** (24): Enter follow-up information (optional).
* **Review Notes** (25): Enter review notes (optional).
