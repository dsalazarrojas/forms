# Session Note Documentation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The session_note_documentation form is used to record notes and details about a therapy session. It helps the therapist document the session's progress, client goals, and any follow-up actions required.

## How to Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the session_note field with a brief summary of the session.
2. Enter the client's goals and any other relevant information in the session_goals field.
3. Record any additional session notes in the session_notes field.
4. Indicate if there will be any follow-up actions in the followup_actions field by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
5. If follow-up actions are required, enter the follow-up date and time in the followup_date and followup_time fields, respectively.
6. Enter the client's email address in the client_email field.
7. Enter the client's phone number in the client_phone field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Note** (Session Note, text, optional): A brief summary of the session.
* **Client Goals** (Client Goals, text, optional): Enter the client's goals and any other relevant information.
* **Session Notes** (Session Notes, text, optional): Record any additional session notes.
* **Follow-up Actions** (Follow-up Actions, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if follow-up actions are required by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Session Time** (Session Time, time, optional): Enter the session's start and end times.
* **Client Email** (Client Email, email, optional): Enter the client's email address.
* **Client Phone** (Client Phone, text, optional): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Follow-up Date** (Follow-up Date, date, optional): Enter the follow-up date.
* **Follow-up Time** (Follow-up Time, time, optional): Enter the follow-up time.

## Tips
- Be concise when filling out the form to ensure accurate and relevant information is recorded.
- Use the follow-up actions field to track any necessary next steps for the client.
- Use the follow-up date and time fields to schedule appointments or send reminders.
