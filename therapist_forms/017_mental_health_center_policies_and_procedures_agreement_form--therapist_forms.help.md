# Mental Health Center Policies And Procedures Agreement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a mandatory agreement for patients to confirm their understanding and consent to the mental health center's policies and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and select the appropriate response for each field.
2. If a field is required, please select the correct answer and do not leave it blank.
3. Take your time and answer each question honestly and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Consent (Page 1)
* **Patient Consent** (`patient_consent`, `select_one`, required): Please select "Yes" to confirm that you have read and understand the mental health center's policies and procedures.

### Have You Read and Understood Our Policies (Page 2)
* **Have You Read and Understood Our Policies** (`policies_read`, `select_one`, required): Please select "Yes" to confirm that you have read and understood the mental health center's policies and procedures.

### Emergency Contact Information (Page 3)
* **Emergency Contact Information** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required): Please enter the name and contact information of the person to be contacted in case of an emergency.

### Emergency Contact Phone (Page 3)
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Please enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.

### Emergency Contact Email (Page 3)
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, `text`, required): Please enter the email address of the emergency contact person.

### Emergency Contact Phone Number (Page 3)
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`emergency_contact_phone_number`, `number`, required): Please enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.

### Signed Above (Page 4)
* **Signed Above** (`policies_signed`, `select_one`, required): Please select "Yes" to confirm that you have read and understood the mental health center's policies and procedures.

### As a Therapist, I Agree to Follow Our Policies (Page 4)
* **As a Therapist, I Agree to Follow Our Policies** (`therapist_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Please select "Yes" to confirm that you agree to follow the mental health center's policies and procedures.
