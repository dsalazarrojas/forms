# Virtual Counseling Experience Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather your feedback on your virtual counseling experience. Your input will help us improve the effectiveness of our services and ensure that you have the best possible outcome in our online sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
- To start, please read the instructions carefully and fill out each field accurately.
- Select the options that best describe your experiences.
- Write your answers in as much detail as possible.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for further assistance.
- Once you have completed the form, click the "Submit" button to send us your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client_feedback** (`client_feedback`, select_multiple, false):
  Please select "Yes" if you felt supported and engaged during the session, or "No" if you did not.
- **Session_type** (`session_type`, select_one, false):
  Select "Yes" if you felt that the session was conducted effectively, or "No" if you did not.
- **Technology_satisfaction** (`technology_satisfaction`, select_one, false):
  Select "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor" to describe your experience with the technology used during the session.
- **Improvement_areas** (`improvement_areas`, text, false):
  Please describe the areas where you think we could improve the service.
- **Technology_improvements** (`technology_improvements`, text, false):
  Suggest any technology improvements you would recommend for future sessions.
- **Session_effectiveness** (`session_effectiveness`, select_one, false):
  Select "Very Effective", "Somewhat Effective", "Not Very Effective", or "Not at All Effective" to describe how the session met your needs.
- **Technology_issues** (`technology_issues`, text, false):
  Please describe any technology issues you experienced during the session.
- **Next_steps** (`next_steps`, text, false):
  Suggest any next steps or actions you would like to take in the next session.
