# Sports Massage Consultation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your needs and preferences for sports massage therapy.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately.
2. Provide your name and contact information in the required fields.
3. Answer the questions truthfully, and make sure to provide any necessary medical documentation or information when prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: This field is where you'll enter your name for our records.
  - Type: text
  - Required: Yes
* **Contact Info**: This field is where you'll provide your contact information, such as phone number and email address.
  - Type: text
  - Required: Yes
* **Health Condition**: This field is where you'll describe any health conditions that may affect your massage experience.
  - Type: text
  - Required: No
* **Massage Needs**: This field is where you'll specify what you hope to achieve through your massage, e.g., relaxation, pain relief, or improved performance.
  - Type: text
  - Required: Yes
* **Treatment Plan**: This field is where you'll describe any previous massage experiences or preferences.
  - Type: text
  - Required: No
* **Client Notes**: This field is where you can provide any additional information about your massage experience or preferences.
  - Type: text
  - Required: No
* **Medical Conditions**: This field is where you'll select any medical conditions that may affect your massage experience.
  - Type: select_multiple
  - Required: No
* **Medications**: This field is where you'll list any medications you're currently taking.
  - Type: text
  - Required: No
* **Allergies**: This field is where you'll list any allergies or sensitivities.
  - Type: text
  - Required: No
* **Medical History**: This field is where you'll provide any relevant medical history.
  - Type: text
  - Required: No
* **Injury History**: This field is where you'll describe any past injuries or conditions that may affect your massage experience.
  - Type: text
  - Required: No
* **Massage Fees**: This field is where you'll select your preferred massage fee.
  - Type: number
  - Required: Yes
* **Massage Duration**: This field is where you'll select the desired duration of your massage.
  - Type: number
  - Required: Yes
* **Massage Frequency**: This field is where you'll specify how often you'd like to receive massages.
  - Type: text
  - Required: No
* **Massage Goals**: This field is where you'll specify what you hope to achieve through your massage.
  - Type: text
  - Required: No
* **Emergency Contact**: This field is where you'll provide an emergency contact's name and number.
  - Type: text
  - Required: No

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough when providing your information.
* If you have any concerns or questions, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We appreciate your cooperation in ensuring the best possible treatment outcome.
