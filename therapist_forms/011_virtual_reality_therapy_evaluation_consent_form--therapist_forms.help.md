# Virtual Reality Therapy Evaluation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect client information, ensure they understand the therapy process, and obtain their consent for virtual reality therapy sessions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please fill out each section as instructed and submit your answers for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Client Information** (`client_info`, `text`, required)
Please provide your contact information, including your name, address, and any other relevant details.

### 2. **Treatment Information** (`treatment_info`, `text`, required)
Provide details about your current treatment, including any previous therapy sessions or medications you are taking.

### 3. **Consent** (`consent_form`, `checkbox`, required)
Please indicate your agreement to participate in virtual reality therapy sessions by selecting one of the options: "I agree" or "I do not agree".

### 4. **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required)
Provide any relevant notes or observations about your therapy needs or concerns.

### 5. **Schedule Information** (`schedule_info`, `text`, required)
Provide your availability for sessions, including the days and time slots you are free.

### 6. **Schedule Time** (`schedule_time`, `time`, optional)
Specify a preferred time for sessions.

### 7. **Select One** (`select_one`, `select_one`, required)
Choose one of the options: "Yes" or "No".

### 8. **Schedule Date** (`schedule_date`, `date`, optional)
Specify a preferred date for sessions.

### 9. **Email** (`email`, `email`, required)
Please provide your email address for communication purposes.

### 10. **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required)
Provide your phone number for communication purposes.

### 11. **Phone2** (`phone2`, `text`, optional)
Please provide an alternate phone number for communication.

### 12. **Email2** (`email2`, `email`, optional)
Provide an alternate email address for communication.

### 13. **Phone3** (`phone3`, `text`, optional)
Please provide another alternate phone number for communication.

### 14. **Email3** (`email3`, `email`, optional)
Provide another alternate email address for communication.

### 15. **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Choose multiple options: "Yes" or "No".

### 16. **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional)
Please provide any additional notes or observations.

### 17. **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, optional)
Please provide your contact information for communication purposes.

### 18. **Contact Info 2** (`contact_info_2`, `text`, optional)
Please provide another set of contact information for communication purposes.

## Tips

- Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Make sure to provide complete and correct contact information.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your therapist for clarification.

This guide is intended to help you complete the Virtual Reality Therapy Evaluation Consent Form. If you need further assistance, please don't hesitate to ask.
