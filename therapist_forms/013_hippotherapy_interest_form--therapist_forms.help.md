# Hippotherapy Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hippotherapy Interest Form is designed to collect information about patients who are interested in participating in hippotherapy programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the patient information fields with your personal details.
2. Enter your contact information, including your phone number and email address.
3. If you have any medical conditions that may impact your participation in hippotherapy, please list them in the medical conditions fields.
4. If you have any additional information that you would like to share with our therapists, please include it in the therapist notes field.
5. Once you have completed all the fields, submit the form and a member of our team will contact you to discuss your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required/optional: false)
Please enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed on our records.

### 2. **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required/optional: false)
Enter your phone number so that we can contact you to discuss your application.

### 3. **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required/optional: false)
Enter your email address so that we can contact you to discuss your application.

### 4. **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional: false)
Enter your date of birth to help us understand your age.

### 5. **Therapist Notes** (`therapist_notes`, note, required/optional: false)
If you have any additional information that you would like to share with our therapists, please include it here.

### 6. **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, required/optional: false)
If you have any medical conditions that may impact your participation in hippotherapy, please list them here.

### 7. **Medical Conditions 2** (`medical_conditions_2`, text, required/optional: false)
Please list any additional medical conditions that are not already listed in the previous field.

### Tips
* Please be as thorough as possible when filling out this form to ensure that we have all the necessary information to process your application.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will review your application and contact you to discuss further.
