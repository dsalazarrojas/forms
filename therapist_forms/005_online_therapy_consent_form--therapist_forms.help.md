# Online Therapy Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect patient and next of kin information, obtain consent, and other details necessary for online therapy sessions.

## How To Complete This Form

*   **To start, ensure that all required fields are completed**. Some fields may not be required, but they are included for additional information or reference purposes.
*   **Provide accurate and detailed information**. Ensure that all provided details are up-to-date and true to your identity or next of kin's identity.
*   **Select the correct options** for consent and other fields as instructed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Information** (1): Enter your name, address, and any other relevant details about yourself.
*   **Consent** (2): Select one or more of the options to indicate your consent for online therapy sessions. This may include agreeing to receive emails, messages, or other forms of communication from your doctor.
*   **Next of Kin Information** (3): Provide details about your next of kin, such as their name, address, and contact information. This information is important for your doctor to communicate with them in case of emergencies or questions.
*   **Signature** (4): Sign this form to acknowledge that you understand and agree to the terms and conditions of online therapy.
*   **Date** (5): Provide the current date to confirm the time and date of the online therapy session.
*   **Time** (6): Specify the time for the online therapy session.
*   **Additional Comments** (7): Add any additional comments or details that might be helpful for your doctor to know.
*   **Patient Email** (8): Enter your email address for your doctor to contact you through.
*   **Doctor Name** (9): Enter the name of your doctor for reference purposes.
*   **Doctor Email** (10): Enter your doctor's email address for them to contact you through.
*   **Doctor Phone** (11): Enter your doctor's phone number for them to contact you through.
