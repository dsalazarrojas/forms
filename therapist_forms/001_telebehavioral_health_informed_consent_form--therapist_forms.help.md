# Telebehavioral Health Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information for telebehavioral health services, such as therapist or healthcare provider details, patient identification, and consent dates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information in the "User Info" field.
2. Choose a date for the consent in the "Consent Date" field. This date will be used for record-keeping purposes.
3. Enter your name in the "Provider Name" field.
4. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
5. Enter the patient's address in the "Patient Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info (Text)**: Enter your name and contact information for record-keeping purposes.
* **Consent Date (Date)**: Choose a date for the consent, which will be used for record-keeping purposes.
* **Provider Name (Text)**: Enter your name as the healthcare or therapy provider.
* **Patient Name (Text)**: Enter the patient's full name.
* **Patient Address (Text)**: Enter the patient's address for record-keeping purposes.

## Tips
* Ensure to fill out the form accurately and completely to provide reliable information for patient care.
* If you're unsure about any field, please reach out to [support email] for assistance.
* Remember to keep the patient's information private and confidential.
