# Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Intake Form is a mandatory document that helps us understand your needs and preferences for therapy. Please complete this form with accurate and clear information to ensure the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form carefully, answering all required fields.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.
- Make sure to save your progress if you need to take a break.
- Once completed, submit the form to schedule an appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 2. First Name
**First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Provide your first name.

### 3. Last Name
**Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Provide your last name.

### 4. Date of Birth
**Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.

### 5. Email Address
**Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.

### 6. Phone Number
**Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.

### 7. Emergency Contact Name and Phone
**Emergency Contact Name and Phone** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required): Provide the name and phone number of an emergency contact person.

### 8. Primary Reason for Seeking Therapy
**Primary Reason for Seeking Therapy** (`reason_for_therapy`, `text`, required): Briefly describe the reason why you're seeking therapy.

### 9. Have You Received Therapy Before?
**Have You Received Therapy Before?** (`previous_therapy_experience`, `select_one`, required): Answer if you have ever received therapy before.

### 10. Current Mental Health Concerns
**Current Mental Health Concerns** (`current_mental_health_concerns`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all that apply to your current mental health concerns.

### 11. Current Medications
**Current Medications** (`medications_currently_taking`, `text`, optional): If you're taking any medications, list them here.

### 12. Relevant Medical Conditions
**Relevant Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, optional): If you have any relevant medical conditions, describe them here.

### 13. Substance Use
**Do You Currently Use Alcohol or Recreational Drugs?** (`substance_use`, `select_one`, required): Answer if you regularly, occasionally, or never use alcohol or recreational drugs.

### 14. Therapy Goals
**What Are Your Goals for Therapy?** (`therapy_goals`, `text`, required): Describe your goals for therapy.

### 15. Preferred Session Type
**Preferred Session Type** (`preferred_session_type`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your preferred session type (in-person, video call, or phone call).

### 16. Availability
**Preferred Days and Times for Sessions** (`availability`, `text`, required): Provide your preferred days and times for sessions.

### 17. How Did You Hear About Our Practice?
**How Did You Hear About Our Practice?** (`how_did_you_hear_about_us`, `select_one`, optional): Tell us how you found out about our practice.

### 18. Insurance Provider
**Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, `text`, optional): If you have an insurance provider, list it here.

### 19. Additional Comments or Questions
**Additional Comments or Questions** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Share any additional comments or questions you have.

### 20. Consent to Treatment
**I Consent to Receive Therapy Services** (`consent_to_treatment`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your consent for therapy services.
