# Client Struggles Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Struggles Assessment form is a tool designed to help therapists and clinicians assess a client's primary challenges, assess the impact on their life, and prioritize treatment goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out each section carefully, providing as much detail as possible about your experiences and challenges.
2. Select all relevant categories that apply to your struggles.
3. Describe your goals and desired outcomes for treatment.
4. Provide information about your current support systems and any previous treatment or counseling experiences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Client Information (Page 1)
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Your full name.
* **Date of Assessment** (`date_of_assessment`, date, required): The date you are completing this assessment.
* **Clinician Name** (`clinician_name`, text, required): The name of the clinician completing this form (if applicable).

### Primary Challenges (Page 2)
* **What are your main struggles currently?** (`main_struggles`, text, required): Describe the areas causing you difficulty.
* **How long have you been experiencing these struggles?** (`struggle_duration`, select_one, required): Select the duration of your struggles.
* **Areas of struggle** (`challenge_categories`, select_multiple, required): Select all categories that apply to your struggles.
* **Additional concerns or safety issues?** (`additional_concerns`, text, optional): Describe any additional concerns or safety issues.

### Impact Assessment (Page 3)
* **Impact on work or school performance** (`work_impact`, select_one, required): Select the impact of your struggles on your work or school performance.
* **Impact on relationships** (`relationship_impact`, select_one, required): Select the impact of your struggles on your relationships.
* **Impact on daily functioning** (`daily_functioning`, select_one, required): Select the impact of your struggles on your daily life.

### Treatment Priorities (Page 4)
* **What would you like to work on first?** (`priority_goals`, text, required): List your priorities in order of importance.
* **How will you know you are getting better?** (`success_definition`, text, required): Describe your goals and desired outcomes for treatment.

### Support and Resources (Page 5)
* **Current support systems** (`current_support`, text, optional): Describe your current support systems.
* **Previous treatment or counseling** (`previous_treatment`, select_one, optional): Select if you have had previous treatment or counseling experiences.
* **Any additional concerns or safety issues?** (`additional_concerns`, text, optional): Describe any additional concerns or safety issues.
