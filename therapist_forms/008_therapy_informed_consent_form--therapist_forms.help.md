# Therapy Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from clients or patients for therapy sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your contact information (page 1)
	* `contact_info`: Enter your name and other contact information.
2. Enter your date of birth (page 1)
	* `date_of_birth`: Enter your date of birth.
3. Enter your time of birth (page 1)
	* `time`: Enter your time of birth.
4. Enter a note (page 1)
	* `note`: Enter a brief note about your therapy session.
5. Select your consent level (page 2)
	* `consent`: Select one of the three options to indicate your understanding of the purpose and risks of the therapy session.
6. Enter next steps (page 2)
	* `next_steps`: Enter the next steps or follow-up information.
7. Enter emergency contact information (page 2)
	* `emergency_contact`: Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
8. Enter emergency phone number (page 2)
	* `emergency_phone`: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
9. Enter emergency email (page 2)
	* `emergency_email`: Enter the email of the emergency contact person.
10. Enter therapist information (page 3)
	* `therapist_info`: Enter any necessary information about your therapist.
11. Enter therapy session notes (page 3)
	* `therapy_session_notes`: Enter notes about your therapy session.
12. Select next session (page 3)
	* `next_session`: Select the next session date.
13. Select follow-up (page 4)
	* `follow_up`: Select whether you need a follow-up session.
14. Enter other notes (page 4)
	* `other_notes`: Enter any additional notes about your therapy session.
15. Enter other phone number (page 4)
	* `other_notes_phone`: Enter a phone number for other contact information.
16. Enter other email (page 4)
	* `other_notes_email`: Enter an email for other contact information.
17. Enter other date (page 4)
	* `other_notes_date`: Enter a date for other contact information.
18. Enter other time (page 4)
	* `other_notes_time`: Enter a time for other contact information.
19. Enter other notes for other contact information (page 4)
	* `other_notes_notes`: Enter a note for other contact information.

* Be sure to review the form carefully before proceeding with the therapy sessions.
