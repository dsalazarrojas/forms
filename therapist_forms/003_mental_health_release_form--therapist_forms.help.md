# mental_health_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect the necessary information for a mental health release, ensuring that the patient's mental health status and treatment needs are documented and respected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, taking your time to ensure that all required fields are completed correctly.
2. Select the correct options for the "Mental Health Release" and "Mental Health Release" fields, as they are crucial for your treatment authorization.
3. Enter the patient's contact information, such as their email and phone number, to facilitate communication.
4. Review the form carefully before submitting it to the mental health provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Mental Health Provider**: Enter the name of the mental health provider you are seeking treatment from.
  - This field is not required, but it helps the mental health provider understand your treatment history.
- **Insurance Company**: Enter the name of your insurance company, if applicable.
  - This field is not required, but it helps the mental health provider understand your insurance coverage.
- **Mental Health Release**: Select "True" to release your mental health information.
  - This field is required, as it determines whether you authorize the release of your mental health information.
- **Mental Health Release**: Select all applicable options for the mental health releases you grant authorization for.
  - This field is required, as it grants authorization for specific mental health releases.
- **Date of Release**: Enter the date you are authorizing the release of your mental health information.
  - This field is not required, but it helps the mental health provider track the release authorization.
- **Time of Release**: Enter the time you are authorizing the release of your mental health information.
  - This field is not required, but it helps the mental health provider track the release authorization.
- **Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your treatment or authorization.
  - This field is not required, but it provides additional context for the mental health provider.
- **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
  - This field is not required, but it helps the mental health provider contact you efficiently.
- **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
  - This field is not required, but it helps the mental health provider contact you promptly.
- **Medical Insurance Number**: Enter your medical insurance number, if applicable.
  - This field is not required, but it helps the mental health provider understand your insurance coverage.
- **Patient Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your medical records.
  - This field is not required, but it helps the mental health provider identify you properly.
- **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth for identification purposes.
  - This field is not required, but it helps the mental health provider verify your identity.
- **Address**: Enter your home address for communication purposes.
  - This field is not required, but it helps the mental health provider contact you efficiently.
- **Date of Death**: Enter the date of your death, if applicable (this field is not applicable for most patients).
  - This field is not required, but it helps the mental health provider understand your treatment history.

## Tips
- Please review your treatment needs and ensure that you have the patient's consent to share their information.
- If you are unsure about any field, please consult the mental health provider or the patient directly.
- Ensure that you fill out the form completely and accurately to avoid any issues with treatment authorization.
