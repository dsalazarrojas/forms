# Initial Psychotherapy Consultation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information for an initial psychotherapy consultation. It aims to gather information about the patient's personal and medical history, current status, and contact details. Completing this form will help the therapist understand the patient's background and needs, ensuring a comprehensive and effective initial consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the sections as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Please answer all questions truthfully and honestly.
3. For multiple-choice questions, select the relevant option.
4. If a question is not applicable to your situation, please indicate this clearly.
