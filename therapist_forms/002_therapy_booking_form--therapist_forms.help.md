## Therapy Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Therapy Booking Form is used to book an appointment with a therapist for personal therapy sessions. This form gathers your basic information and appointment preferences to ensure a smooth and effective session.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First name.
2. Provide your Last name.
3. Enter a valid Email address associated with your contact information.
4. Enter your Phone number for communication purposes.
5. Choose a Date for your appointment.
6. Select a Time for your appointment.
7. Choose the type of appointment you would like to book (Consultation, Session, or Other).
8. Add any additional Notes about your appointment.
9. Click "Submit" to send your booking request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (text, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Last name** (text, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Email** (email, required: false): Enter a valid Email address associated with your contact information.
* **Phone** (text, required: false): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Date** (date, required: false): Choose a Date for your appointment. The date should be in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Time** (time, required: false): Select a Time for your appointment. The time should be in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Appointment type** (select_one, required: true): Choose the type of appointment you would like to book (Consultation, Session, or Other).
  * Consultation: A consultation is a brief, initial meeting to discuss your therapy needs and goals.
  * Session: A therapy session is a regular meeting for therapy work.
  * Other: If none of the above options apply, choose "Other" to describe your specific needs.
* **Notes** (note, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments about your appointment.
* **Submit** (text, required: false): Click "Submit" to send your booking request to the therapist.
