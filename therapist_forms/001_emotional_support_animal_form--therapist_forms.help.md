# Emotional Support Animal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the need for an emotional support animal (ESA) in an individual's life, particularly for people with mental disability. The form is a crucial tool for therapists and healthcare professionals to assess and provide support for clients who require an emotional support animal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the correct option for each question.
3. Provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Mental Disability**: Select "Yes" if you have a mental disability. If you do not have a mental disability, please proceed to the next question.
* **Pet Type**: Choose the type of pet you are requesting as an emotional support animal. You can select from Cat, Dog, Other, etc.
* **Pet Name**: If you have selected "Other" in the previous question, enter the name of your emotional support animal.
* **Type of Animal**: If you have selected "Other" in the previous question, choose the type of animal you are requesting as an emotional support animal.
* **Therapist Name**: Enter the name of your therapist who will be signing this form.
* **Therapist Title**: Enter the title of your therapist (e.g., LCSW, LPC, LMFT, etc.).
* **Date Start**: If you are requesting an emotional support animal for a specific start date, enter the date in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Date End**: If you are requesting an emotional support animal for a specific end date, enter the date in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Reason for Pet**: Explain why you need an emotional support animal in your life. Be as detailed as possible.
* **Signature**: Sign this form to indicate that the information provided is accurate and true.
* **Date Signed**: Enter the date you signed this form in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Name Signed**: Enter your name as it appears on your signature.

Note: The fields marked as "required" are mandatory and must be completed to submit the form.
