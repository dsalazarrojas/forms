# mental_health_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on an individual's mental health status and history.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer as honestly as possible.
2. Select the options that best match your mental health status or experience.
3. Fill out each field completely, if applicable.
