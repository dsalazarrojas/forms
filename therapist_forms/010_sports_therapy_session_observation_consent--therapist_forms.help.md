# sports_therapy_session_observation_consent - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from participants and record observation details for sports therapy sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your participant's details on page 1, including any necessary information about their involvement in the sports therapy session.
2. Describe the purpose of the observation on page 2.
3. Have the participant sign the form on page 3 to confirm their consent.
4. Sign and date the form on page 4 to confirm that you, as the therapist, have observed and recorded the session details.
5. Record the date of the observation on page 5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **participant_details** (page 1, not required): Enter any necessary information about the participant's involvement in the sports therapy session. This can include their name, age, and any relevant medical history.
* **observation_purpose** (page 2, not required): Describe the purpose of observing the participant during the therapy session. This could include noting any specific goals or objectives the participant is working towards.
* **participant_signature** (page 3, not required): Have the participant sign to confirm their consent to be observed during the therapy session.
* **therapist_signature** (page 4, not required): Sign and date the form to confirm that you, as the therapist, have observed and recorded the session details.
* **observation_date** (page 5, not required): Record the date of the observation.
