# Reiki Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reiki Registration Form is designed to collect registration information for individuals interested in participating in Reiki workshops, classes, or healing sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields (marked as "required") with the requested information.
2. Review the form to ensure that all fields are completed correctly.
3. Submit the form to submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Please enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Please enter your address.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Please enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Please select the time of the workshop or class you are interested in attending.
* **Reiki Level** (`reiki_level`, select_one, required): Please select your level of Reiki experience (Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3).
* **Workshop Type** (`workshop_type`, select_multiple, required): Please select the type of workshop or class you are interested in attending (Reiki Workshop, Reiki Class, or Healing Session).
* **Workshop Start Date** (`workshop_start_date`, date, required): Please enter the start date of the workshop or class you are interested in attending.
* **Workshop End Date** (`workshop_end_date`, date, required): Please enter the end date of the workshop or class you are interested in attending.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Please select the method of payment you will use to pay for the workshop or class (Cash or Credit/Debit Card).
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, required): Please select your current payment status (Paid or Pending).
* **Workshop Location** (`workshop_location`, select_multiple, required): Please select the location of the workshop or class you are interested in attending (New York, Los Angeles, or Chicago).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Please enter any additional comments or notes you would like to share with the workshop organizers.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Please enter any additional comments or notes you would like to share with the workshop organizers.
* **Reiki Master** (`reiki_master`, select_one, optional): Please indicate whether you are a Reiki master or not.
* **Reiki Practitioner** (`reiki_practitioner`, select_one, optional): Please indicate whether you are a Reiki practitioner or not.
* **Reiki Certified** (`reiki_certified`, select_one, optional): Please indicate whether you are Reiki certified or not.
* **Reiki Certification Level** (`reiki_certified_level`, select_one, optional): Please select the level of Reiki certification you have (Practitioner or Master/Teacher).
* **Workshop Attendance** (`workshop_attendance`, select_one, optional): Please select the type of workshop attendance you are interested in (Online or In-Person).
* **Language** (`language`, select_one, optional): Please select the language you are comfortable speaking (English or Spanish).
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, optional): Please select your gender (Male or Female).
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Please enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, optional): Please enter the phone number of your emergency contact.

## Tips
Please ensure that all required fields are completed correctly before submitting the form. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
