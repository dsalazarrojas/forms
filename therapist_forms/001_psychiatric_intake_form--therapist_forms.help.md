# psychiatric_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a patient's medical and mental health for therapeutic diagnosis and treatment.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Complete each field with accurate and relevant information about your medical and mental health.
2.  Ensure that you answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3.  If you are unsure about any question, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Patient Name (1)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Condition (2)**: Indicate whether you currently experience any medical conditions. Select one or more of the provided options to best describe your current health status.
*   **Symptoms (3)**: Describe any physical or mental symptoms you are experiencing, including their severity and duration.
*   **Diagnosis (4)**: Indicate whether you have been diagnosed with any medical conditions. Select one of the provided options to best describe your diagnosis status.
*   **Treatment Plan (5)**: Outline any existing treatment plans or medications you are currently following. Be as detailed as possible to help your healthcare provider understand your treatment history.
*   **Medication (6)**: Indicate whether you take any medications. Select one or more of the provided options to best describe your medication usage.
*   **Medication Notes (7)**: Provide any relevant details about your medications, including dosage, frequency, and any known side effects.
*   **Allergies (8)**: Indicate any known allergies or sensitivities you have to certain substances. Select one or more of the provided options to best describe your allergic reactions.
*   **Medical History (9)**: Describe your medical history, including any previous illnesses, surgeries, or hospitalizations.
*   **Family History (10)**: Provide information about your family medical history, including any genetic predispositions or inherited conditions.
*   **Mental Status (11)**: Indicate whether you are currently experiencing any mental health issues, such as anxiety or depression. Select one of the provided options to best describe your mental health status.
*   **Mental Status Notes (12)**: Provide any additional information about your mental health status, including any relevant treatment or coping strategies.

## Tips
*   Be as detailed and honest as possible when completing this form.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
*   This form is designed to be completed by the patient, but please ensure that a healthcare provider reviews and verifies the information before proceeding with treatment.
