# journal_entry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The journal entry form is used to document and record patient information and progress in a medical setting.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "patient_name" field.
2. Provide the patient's diagnosis in the "diagnosis" field.
3. Outline the treatment plan for the patient in the "treatment_plan" field.
4. Record any relevant medical notes in the "medical_notes" field.
5. If applicable, enter the insurance company information in the "insurance_company" field.
6. Enter the name of the healthcare professional submitting the form in the "submitted_by" field.
7. Finally, select the date of submission in the "submission_date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their medical records.
* **Diagnosis**: Describe the patient's current medical condition or diagnosis.
* **Treatment Plan**: Outline the proposed treatment or course of action for the patient's condition.
* **Medical Notes**: Record any additional information about the patient's medical history or progress.
* **Insurance Company**: If applicable, enter the patient's insurance company information for billing purposes.
* **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the healthcare professional submitting the form.
* **Submission Date**: Select the date of submission for accurate record-keeping purposes.
