## Hypnotherapy Intake and Consultation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The hypnotherapy intake and consultation form is designed to gather essential information from clients before and after a hypnotherapy session. This form helps the therapist understand the client's needs, preferences, and concerns, enabling them to tailor the session to provide the best possible results.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Complete this form in its entirety, providing as much information as possible.
2.  Double-check the accuracy of the information you provide.
3.  Review the form carefully before submitting it to your therapist.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, Required): Please enter your full first name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, Required): Enter your full last name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, Required): Enter a valid email address that you check regularly. This will be used for communication with your therapist.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, Required): Enter a working phone number where your therapist can contact you.
*   **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, `date`, Required): Enter the date of your scheduled appointment with your therapist.
*   **Consultation Insights** (`consultation_insights`, `text`, Optional): If you'd like to share any thoughts, feelings, or insights from your previous sessions or consultations, please provide them here.
*   **Therapist Name** (`therapist_name`, `text`, Required): Enter the name of your therapist, as provided by your healthcare provider.
*   **Client ID** (`client_id`, `text`, Required): Enter your client ID as provided by your healthcare provider.

## Tips

*   If you're unsure about any of the information asked, please ask your therapist for clarification.
*   Ensure the accuracy of your information to avoid any issues with your session.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting it to your therapist.

Please submit the form accurately and completely to help your therapist provide you with the best possible hypnotherapy experience. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to your therapist directly.
