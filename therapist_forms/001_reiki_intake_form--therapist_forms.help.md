The "Reiki Intake Form" is designed to gather information from clients before a Reiki session. This form aims to understand the client's background, preferences, and expectations to provide an effective and safe Reiki experience. It is essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure the therapist has a clear understanding of the client's needs and requirements.

The form will be used by the therapist to prepare for the session, ensure client safety, and establish a good connection with the client. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to get the most out of your Reiki session.

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather essential information from clients before their Reiki session. This information will help the therapist tailor the session to meet the client's specific needs and ensure a safe and effective experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field accurately and completely.
2. If a field is not applicable, please note "N/A" or "Not Applicable."
3. Review your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
