# Intuitive Energy Treatment Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain consent from patients for intuitive energy treatment. It's a crucial document that ensures patients understand the treatment's nature, process, and potential outcomes. Please complete this form carefully to ensure accurate and informed consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Gather patient information, including contact details and medical history.
2. Provide a clear treatment plan, including the expected outcomes and duration of the treatment.
3. Ask the patient about their medical condition, treatment preferences, and any allergies or medications.
4. Select the treatment outcome and duration options that best suit the patient's needs.
5. Provide insurance information (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Information
* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, text, required): This section asks for the patient's basic contact details, including name, contact number, and email address. Please ensure that all this information is accurate and up-to-date.

### Treatment Plan
* **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`, text, required): Here, describe the treatment process, including its purpose, expected outcomes, and potential risks or side effects. Be concise but thorough, and make sure to highlight any specific concerns or precautions the patient should be aware of.

### Medical Condition
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required): Briefly describe the patient's medical condition, focusing on its relevance to the treatment. This will help the practitioner understand the patient's situation better.

### Treatment Outcome
* **Treatment Outcome** (`treatment_outcome`, select_one, required): Select one of the three options: Satisfactory, Unsatisfactory, or Unknown. This field indicates the expected outcome of the treatment. Choose the option that best suits the patient's situation.

### Medical History
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, select_multiple, required): List any allergies, medications, or previous surgeries that the patient may have. This information helps the practitioner understand potential risks or interactions.

### Treatment Duration
* **Treatment Duration** (`treatment_duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of the treatment, measured in days or sessions.

### Treatment Frequency
* **Treatment Frequency** (`treatment_frequency`, select_one, required): Choose how often the treatment will be administered: Once, Twice, or Multiple sessions.

### Treatment Start Date
* **Treatment Start Date** (`treatment_start_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the treatment will commence.

### Treatment End Date
* **Treatment End Date** (`treatment_end_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the treatment is expected to conclude.

### Patient Agreement
* **Patient Agreement** (`patient_agreement`, note, required): This section is for the patient's signature, indicating their understanding and consent to the treatment.

### Practitioner Agreement
* **Practitioner Agreement** (`practitioner_agreement`, note, required): This section is for the practitioner's signature, confirming their agreement to follow the treatment plan and ensure patient safety.

### Insurance Information
* **Insurance Information** (`insurance_information`, text, required): If applicable, provide insurance details, such as policy number, provider, and contact information.

Tips:

* Make sure to read the instructions carefully before completing the form.
* Double-check the patient's information for accuracy.
* Clearly communicate with the patient about their treatment plan, expectations, and concerns.
* Review the form carefully before signing as a practitioner to ensure all necessary information is included.
