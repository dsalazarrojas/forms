# Client Pre Massage Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather essential information about you, the client, to ensure a safe and effective massage therapy session.

## How to Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Fill in your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Provide your email address in the "Email Address" field.
5. Answer "Yes" or "No" in the "Is this your first massage therapy session?" field.
6. Answer the question about how often you receive massage therapy in the "How often do you receive massage therapy?" field.
7. Indicate your type of preferred massage in the "Type of massage preferred" field.
8. List any areas of concern or pain in the "Do you have any current pain or injuries?" field.
9. List any medical conditions, recent surgeries, or ongoing treatments in the "Do you have any medical conditions we should know about?" field.
10. List any medications you are currently taking in the "Are you currently taking any medications?" field.
11. Indicate if you have any allergies to oils or lotions in the "Do you have any allergies to oils or lotions?" field.
12. Indicate your preferred pressure level in the "Preferred pressure level" field.
13. Answer the question about your current stress level in the "Current stress level" field.
14. Write a brief description of your goals for massage therapy in the "What are your goals for massage therapy?" field.
15. Answer "Yes", "No", or "Somewhat" in the "Are you comfortable providing feedback during the session?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (text): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (date): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Phone Number** (text): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further information or emergency contact.
* **Email Address** (email): Enter your email address where you can receive important information and appointment reminders.
* **Is this your first massage therapy session?** (select_one): Indicate if this is your first massage therapy session. If yes, it's essential to inform the therapist about any medical conditions or concerns.
* **How often do you receive massage therapy?** (select_one): Answer how often you receive massage therapy, from "First time" to "Multiple times per week".
* **Type of massage preferred** (select_one): Choose your preferred type of massage from "Sweedish", "Deep tissue", "Relaxation", or "Sports massage".
* **Do you have any current pain or injuries?** (text): List any areas of concern or pain that require special attention.
* **Do you have any medical conditions we should know about?** (text): Include any chronic conditions, recent surgeries, or ongoing treatments that may impact your treatment.
* **Are you currently taking any medications?** (text): List any medications that may interact with massage or affect treatment.
* **Do you have any allergies to oils or lotions?** (text): Include any skin sensitivities or allergies to oils or lotions.
* **Preferred pressure level** (select_one): Choose your preferred pressure level from "Light", "Medium", "Deep", or "No preference".
* **Current stress level** (select_one): Answer your current stress level from "Very low", "Low", "Moderate", "High", or "Very high".
* **What are your goals for massage therapy?** (text): Write a brief description of your goals for the massage therapy session.
* **Are you comfortable providing feedback during the session?** (select_one): Indicate if you're comfortable providing feedback during the session, from "True", "False", or "Somewhat".
