# new_client_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The new_client_intake_form is a crucial document that helps therapists and administrative staff collect essential information about new clients. This form ensures that all relevant details are collected accurately and consistently, making it easier for the therapist to provide the best possible care and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the first page with the client's first and last name, address, email, phone number, date of birth, and date of intake.
2. Enter the therapist's name and phone number for reference.
3. Select the client's status (Active, Inactive, On Leave, Retired, or Deceased).
4. Provide any additional client notes, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First and Last Name
* **First and Last Name** (`First and Last Name`, text, required): Enter the client's full name.
### Address
* **Address** (`Address`, text, required): Type in the client's address.
### Email
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter the client's email address.
### Phone
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter the client's phone number.
### Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, required): Enter the client's date of birth.
### Date of Intake
* **Date of Intake** (`Date of Intake`, date, required): Enter the date of the current intake.
### Therapist Name
* **Therapist Name** (`Therapist Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the therapist handling the client's case.
### Therapist Phone
* **Therapist Phone** (`Therapist Phone`, text, required): Enter the therapist's phone number.
### Client Status
* **Client Status** (`Client Status`, select_one, required): Select the client's status from the following options:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive
	+ On Leave
	+ Retired
	+ Deceased
### Client Notes
* **Client Notes** (`Client Notes`, text, required): Enter any additional client notes or comments, if necessary.
