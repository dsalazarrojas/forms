# Group Therapy Confidentiality Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to establish clear and mutual understanding of confidentiality agreements between patients and therapists in group therapy settings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your name as a patient and your therapist's name.
2. Check the emergency contact section if you have an emergency contact person you'd like to notify in case of any issue.
3. Review and agree to the confidentiality terms in each of the "Confidentiality Agreement" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Group Therapy Confidentiality** (Page 1):
	+ This field is a note field where you can type in any additional information or context about the group therapy sessions.
* **Patient Name** (Page 2):
	+ Please enter your name as a patient.
* **Therapist Name** (Page 2):
	+ Please enter your therapist's name.
* **Emergency Contact** (Page 3):
	+ If you have an emergency contact person, enter their name and relationship (e.g., parent, spouse, or friend).
* **Confidentiality Agreement 1-10** (Page 4-5):
	+ These fields are dedicated to establishing confidentiality agreements. You'll review and agree to each term, ensuring you understand the confidentiality boundaries in the group therapy setting.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship 1-11** (Page 6-7):
	+ If you've identified an emergency contact, describe your relationship with them (e.g., spouse, friend, or parent).
* **Confidentiality Agreement 11** (Page 8):
	+ Review and agree to the final confidentiality term in this field.

## Tips
- Please ensure to review each field carefully and ask questions if you're unsure about any information.
- This form is a guide, and your therapist may ask you to adapt it according to their specific needs.
- If you have any concerns or questions, don't hesitate to discuss them with your therapist.
