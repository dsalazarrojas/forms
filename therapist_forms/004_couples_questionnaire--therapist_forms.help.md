# Couples Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Couples Questionnaire is a tool designed to gather information about a couple's relationship dynamics, satisfaction, and goals. It's intended to be used in a professional setting, such as therapy or counseling, to help couples better understand each other's needs and concerns.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Answer each question carefully and honestly**. This will help you and your partner gain a better understanding of your relationship.
2. **Work together as a couple**. Fill out the form together, taking turns answering each question.
3. **Be as detailed as possible**. Use specific examples or anecdotes to answer open-ended questions.
4. **Review and discuss each other's answers**. This will help you both understand each other's perspectives and concerns.
