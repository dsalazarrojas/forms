# Mental Health Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Health Assessment Form is designed to help users assess their mental health status and identify potential areas of concern. This form is divided into several sections, each focusing on different aspects of mental health status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by selecting the most accurate and relevant options from the dropdown menus.
2. Complete all required sections first, as indicated by the "required" label.
3. Use the "None" option when no symptoms or mental health concerns are present.
4. If you need to report "Other" symptoms or mental health concerns, please use the provided text field to provide a clear and concise description.
5. Double-check your answers to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Mental Status** (`mental_status`, `select_one`, **Required**): Please select the mental health condition(s) that best describes your current mental health status. Choose one or more of the following options:
	+ Anxiety Disorder
	+ Bipolar Disorder
	+ Major Depressive Disorder
	+ Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder
	+ Schizophrenia
	+ Substance Use Disorder
* **What Happened** (`what-happened`, `select_multiple`, **Required**): Select all that apply to describe what happened that led to your current mental health status:
	+ None
	+ Other (please describe in the provided text field)
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select all that apply to describe your current mental health symptoms:
	+ None
	+ Other (please describe in the provided text field)
* **Symptoms 2** (`symptoms-2`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select all that apply to describe your current mental health symptoms:
	+ None
	+ Other (please describe in the provided text field)
* ... (continued for all other fields)

**Tips**

* Be honest and accurate when answering each question.
* If you are unsure about a particular question or option, feel free to ask for clarification.
* Take your time to complete the form carefully and thoroughly.
* If you need to revisit or update your answers, please contact the relevant personnel for assistance.
