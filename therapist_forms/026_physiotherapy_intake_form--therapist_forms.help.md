# Physiotherapy Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a patient's medical history, treatment goals, and progress, and to gather data for physiotherapy treatment planning.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please provide your name, date of birth, and contact information in the "Patient Information" section.
2. Answer the questions in the "Medical History" section, including any allergies, conditions, and surgeries.
3. Indicate your medical history, including chronic conditions and family history.
4. Choose the options that best describe your physiotherapy goals.
5. Report your vital signs and any other relevant medical information in the "Physical Assessment" section.
6. Provide any additional comments or recommendations in the "Physiotherapy Notes" and "Physiotherapy Recommendations" sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (text): Enter your name, date of birth, and contact information.
* **Medical History** (text): Describe any allergies, conditions, or surgeries.
* **Medical History Questions** (text): Answer any questions related to your medical history.
* **Medical History Conditions** (select multiple): Check "Yes" if you have any of the listed conditions.
* **Medical History Allergies** (select multiple): Check "Yes" if you have any of the listed allergies.
* **Medical History Surgeries** (select multiple): Check "Yes" if you have had any of the listed surgeries.
* **Medical History Medications** (select multiple): Check "Yes" if you are taking any of the listed medications.
* **Physiotherapy Goals** (select one): Choose the option that best describes your physiotherapy goals.
* **Physiotherapy Goals 2** (select one): This field is similar to "Physiotherapy Goals". Please choose the option that best describes your physiotherapy goals.
* **Physiotherapy Goals 3** (select one): This field is similar to "Physiotherapy Goals". Please choose the option that best describes your physiotherapy goals.
* **Physical Assessment** (text): Report your vital signs and any other relevant medical information.
* **Vital Signs** (text): Enter your blood pressure, pulse, and other relevant vital sign information.
* **Medical History Illnesses** (select multiple): Check "Yes" if you have any of the listed illnesses.
* **Medical History Chronic Conditions** (select multiple): Check "Yes" if you have any of the listed chronic conditions.
* **Medical History Family History** (select multiple): Check "Yes" if you have any of the listed family history.
* **Medical History Social History** (select multiple): Check "Yes" if you have any of the listed social history.
* **Physiotherapy Treatment** (text): Enter any relevant information about your physiotherapy treatment.
* **Physiotherapy Treatment Plan** (text): Enter any additional comments about your physiotherapy treatment plan.
* **Physiotherapy Treatment Progress** (select one): Choose the option that best describes your physiotherapy treatment progress.
* **Physiotherapy Treatment Progress 2** (select one): This field is similar to "Physiotherapy Treatment Progress". Please choose the option that best describes your physiotherapy treatment progress.
* **Physiotherapy Treatment Progress 3** (select one): This field is similar to "Physiotherapy Treatment Progress". Please choose the option that best describes your physiotherapy treatment progress.
* **Physiotherapy Treatment Progress 4** (select one): This field is similar to "Physiotherapy Treatment Progress". Please choose the option that best describes your physiotherapy treatment progress.
* **Physiotherapy Notes** (text): Enter any additional comments about your physiotherapy treatment.
* **Physiotherapy Recommendations** (text): Enter any additional recommendations for your physiotherapy treatment.
* **Physiotherapy Recommendations 2** (text): This field is similar to "Physiotherapy Recommendations". Please enter any additional recommendations for your physiotherapy treatment.
