# mental_health_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect patient and therapy information for mental health services. It is meant to be a comprehensive guide for patients to provide detailed information about their mental health history, medical history, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's basic information on **page 1**.
2. Provide a detailed medical history on **page 2**.
3. Attach any relevant supporting documents on **page 3**.
4. Confirm payment information on **page 4**.
5. Enter contact details on **page 5**.
6. Answer the question about the therapist's involvement on **page 6**.
7. Fill in any additional medical history on **page 7**.
8. Add any more supporting documents on **page 8**.
9. Confirm payment information again on **page 9**.
10. Enter contact details again on **page 10**.
11. Fill in any further medical history on **page 11**.
12. Add any supporting documents on **page 12**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_information** (Page 1): Please enter your basic information, including your name, address, and any other relevant details.
* **medical_history** (Page 2): Describe your medical history, including any previous illnesses, allergies, or medications.
* **supporting_documents** (Page 3): Attach any relevant documents, such as medical records or insurance cards, that support your medical history.
* **fee_payment** (Page 4): Confirm your payment information, including your payment method and amount.
* **contact_information** (Page 5): Enter your contact details, including your email and phone number.
* **therapist_information** (Page 6): Answer yes or no to whether you are currently working with a therapist.
* **medical_history_2** (Page 7): Fill in any additional medical information that may be relevant to your treatment.
* **supporting_documents_2** (Page 8): Attach any further supporting documents that may be necessary for your treatment.
* **fee_payment_2** (Page 9): Confirm your payment information again.
* **contact_information_2** (Page 10): Enter your contact details again.
* **medical_history_3** (Page 11): Fill in any more medical history information that may be relevant.
* **supporting_documents_3** (Page 12): Add any additional supporting documents that may be necessary.
* **fee_payment_3** (Page 13): Confirm your payment information one last time.
* **contact_information_3** (Page 14): Enter your contact details one last time.

## Tips
* Be thorough and honest when filling out this form to ensure accurate information is collected.
* Attach any supporting documents that may be relevant to your treatment.
* Confirm your payment information to avoid confusion.
