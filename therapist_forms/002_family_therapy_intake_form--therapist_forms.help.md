# Family Therapy Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about your family's background, concerns, and goals to aid in the creation of a personalized therapy plan.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form with as much detail as possible, providing answers to all the questions.
- If you are unsure about any question, please provide the best answer you can or indicate that you are unsure.
- If you have any additional information that you think is relevant to your family's therapy, please include it in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Family Therapy Intake** (`family_therapy_intake_form`, text, required: false)
	+ A brief description of your family's reasons for seeking therapy.
* **Family Information** (`family_information`, text, required: false)
	+ Provide general information about your family, such as your address, phone number, and email.
* **Family History** (`family_history`, text, required: false)
	+ Describe your family's background, including any significant events, traumas, or cultural influences that may impact your family dynamics.
* **Family Dynamic** (`family_dynamic`, text, required: false)
	+ Explain how you interact with each other, including your communication style, conflict resolution, and emotional support.
* **Family Culture** (`family_culture`, text, required: false)
	+ Share your family's cultural influences, traditions, and values.
* **Mental Health Information** (`mental_health_information`, text, required: false)
	+ Provide any relevant mental health information about your family members, including diagnosed conditions, medications, or therapy experiences.
* **Therapy Goals** (`therapy_goals`, text, required: false)
	+ Describe the specific goals you hope to achieve through therapy.
* **Family Member Information** (`family_member_information`, text, required: false)
	+ Provide basic contact information for each family member.
* **Family Member 1 First Name** (`family_member_1_first_name`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter the first name of the first family member.
* **Family Member 1 Last Name** (`family_member_1_last_name`, text, required: false)

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask your therapist.
