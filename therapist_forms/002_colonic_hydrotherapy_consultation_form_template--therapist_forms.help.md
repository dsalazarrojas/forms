# Colonic Hydrotherapy Consultation Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from clients seeking colonic hydrotherapy consultations. It helps the therapist understand the client's medical history, lifestyle habits, and preferences, ensuring a safe and effective treatment plan.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure you answer all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Client Full Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Please fill in your full name as it appears on your identification.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Please select your date of birth.
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Please enter your contact number for communication and follow-up.
- **Primary Health Concern** (`primary_health_concern`, text, required): Please specify the reason why you are seeking hydrotherapy (e.g., bloating, constipation, detox).
- **Current Bowel Movement Frequency** (`bowel_movement_frequency`, select_one, required): Please select how often you experience bowel movements.
- **Average Daily Water Intake (liters)** (`hydration_levels`, number, required): Please enter your average daily water intake in liters.
- **Contrainications Check** (`contraindications_check`, select_multiple, required): Please select any of the following contraindications that apply to you:
  - Severe Hemorrhoids
  - Heart Disease or Congestive Heart Failure
  - Severe Anemia
  - Abdominal Hernia
  - Recent Colon Surgery (less than 6 months)
  - Pregnancy
  - Kidney Insufficiency
  - Liver Disease
  - None of the above
- **List Current Medications or Supplements** (`medication_list`, text, optional): Please list any medications or supplements you are currently taking.
- **Known Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): If you have any known allergies, please list them.
- **How Often Do You Exercise?** (`exercise_frequency`, select_one, optional): Please select how often you exercise.
- **Current Stress Levels** (`stress_levels`, number, required): Please select your current stress levels from 1 (low) to 10 (high).
- **Informed Consent** (`informed_consent`, select_one, required): Please select "I Agree" to confirm you have provided accurate medical information and understand the nature of the procedure.
- **Signature of Client (Print Name)** (`signature_confirmation`, text, required): Please sign your name as printed above.
- **Date of Consultation** (`consultation_date`, date, required): Please select the date of your consultation.

Note: Please ensure you answer all required fields accurately and completely.
