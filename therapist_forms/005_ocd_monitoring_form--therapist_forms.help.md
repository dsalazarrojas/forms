# ocd_monitoring_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The OCD Monitoring Form is designed to collect information on a patient's Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder symptoms, frequency, severity, and treatment goals. This form is intended to be completed by a patient during their therapy sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Select the appropriate options for each field based on your current symptoms and treatment goals.
2.  Fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
3.  Double-check your answers to ensure they are consistent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the symptoms you are experiencing. Please note that the symptom list is not comprehensive and might not include all symptoms you are experiencing, if so, please add them in the text field `treatment_goals_2`.
*   **Frequency of symptoms** (`frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select how frequently you experience the symptoms.
    *   'Yes': Always
    *   'No': Never
    *   'Rarely': Occasionally
    *   'Sometimes': Sometimes
*   **Severity of symptoms** (`severity`, `number`, required): Select the severity of your symptoms. Please note that the number field might not accurately display all possible severities and might not be applicable to all symptoms, if so, please contact your therapist for further assistance.
*   **Treatment Goals** (`treatment_goals`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the goals you are looking to achieve through treatment. Please note that the treatment goals list might not be comprehensive and might not include all goals you are looking to achieve, if so, please add them in the text field `treatment_goals_other`.
*   **If not listed, what are other treatment goals?** (`treatment_goals_other`, `text`, required): If any of the treatment goals listed are not relevant to you, please describe your specific treatment goals here.
*   **Progress Notes** (`progress_notes`, `text`, required): Provide a brief description of your progress.
*   **Therapist Notes** (`therapist_notes`, `text`, required): Provide any notes or comments you think are relevant to your treatment.
*   **Patient Notes** (`patient_notes`, `text`, required): Provide any additional information you think would help your therapist understand your condition better.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
*   **Treatment Goals 2-16** (`treatment_goals_2`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the goals you are looking to achieve through treatment. Please note that the treatment goals list might not be comprehensive and might not include all goals you are looking to achieve, if so, please add them in the text field `treatment_goals_other`.
*   **If not listed, what are other treatment goals?** (`treatment_goals_other`, `text`, required): If any of the treatment goals listed are not relevant to you, please describe your specific treatment goals here.

## Tips
*   Make sure to review each field carefully and provide accurate information.
*   If you experience any symptoms not listed, please add them in `treatment_goals_2`.
*   If any of the treatment goals listed are not relevant to you, please add them in `treatment_goals_other`.
*   Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
