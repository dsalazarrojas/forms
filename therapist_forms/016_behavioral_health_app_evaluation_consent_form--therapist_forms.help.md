# Behavioral Health App Evaluation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Behavioral Health App Evaluation Consent Form is used to gather information from participants about the app they are using, their demographics, and their understanding of the evaluation process. This form is essential for evaluating the app and its effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Provide your full name as you would like it to appear on your evaluation report.
2. Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
4. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
5. Enter the name of the app being evaluated.
6. Select how long you plan to use the app for the evaluation period.
7. Check if you have used this app before.
8. Confirm that you understand the purpose of this evaluation.
9. Confirm that you understand how your data will be collected and used.
10. Confirm that you understand that your information will be kept confidential.
11. Confirm that you understand your participation is voluntary.
12. Confirm that you can withdraw from the study at any time.
13. Confirm that you will report any adverse events or concerns.
14. Select if you agree to have your data shared with researchers (please note that personally identifiable information will be removed).
15. Select if we can contact you for follow-up purposes (if this field is not filled, it will not affect your participation).
16. Confirm that all your questions have been answered.
17. Select if you agree to participate in the evaluation and provide your consent.
18. Finally, enter the date of your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (Participant Name, text, required): Enter your full name, as it should appear on your evaluation report.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, required): Enter your contact phone number for follow-up purposes.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Name of Behavioral Health App Being Evaluated** (Name of Behavioral Health App Being Evaluated, text, required): Enter the name of the app you are using for evaluation.
* **Evaluation Period Duration** (Evaluation Period Duration, select_one, required): Select how long you plan to use the app for the evaluation period.
* **Have You Used This App Before** (Have You Used This App Before, select_one, required): Check if you have used this app before.
* **I understand the purpose of this evaluation** (I understand the purpose of this evaluation, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand the purpose of this evaluation.
* **I understand how my data will be collected and used** (I understand how my data will be collected and used, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand how your data will be collected and used.
* **I understand my information will be kept confidential** (I understand my information will be kept confidential, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand your information will be kept confidential.
* **I understand my participation is voluntary** (I understand my participation is voluntary, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand your participation is voluntary.
* **I understand I can withdraw at any time** (I understand I can withdraw at any time, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand you can withdraw at any time.
* **I will report any adverse events or concerns** (I will report any adverse events or concerns, select_one, required): Confirm that you will report any adverse events or concerns.
* **My data may be shared with researchers** (My data may be shared with researchers, select_one, required): Select if you agree to share your data with researchers (please note that personally identifiable information will be removed).
* **May We Contact You for Follow-up** (May We Contact You for Follow-up, select_one, optional): Select if you agree to be contacted for follow-up purposes (if this field is not filled, it will not affect your participation).
* **All My Questions Have Been Answered** (All My Questions Have Been Answered, select_one, required): Confirm that all your questions have been answered.
* **I Consent to Participate in This App Evaluation** (I Consent to Participate in This App Evaluation, select_one, required): Select if you agree to participate in the evaluation and provide your consent.
* **Date of Consent** (Date of Consent, date, required): Enter the date of your consent in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

Note: Please fill in all required fields, as indicated by the asterisk (\*) symbol.
