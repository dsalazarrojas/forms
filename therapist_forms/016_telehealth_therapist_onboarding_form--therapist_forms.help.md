# Telehealth Therapist Onboarding Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a crucial step in the onboarding process for telehealth therapists. It helps us verify your credentials and ensure you have the necessary information to provide care on our platform.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form in its entirety. While some fields are optional, it's essential to complete all required fields to ensure a smooth onboarding process.
2. Double-check your email and phone number for accuracy, as these will be used for communication and verification purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required: false): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address that you use for professional purposes. This will be used for communication and verification.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Provide your phone number for us to contact you in case of any issues or updates.
* **Therapist Photo** (`therapist_photo`, text, required: false): Upload a clear photo of yourself for our records and to be displayed on our platform (if applicable).
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_multiple, required: false): Please confirm that you have read and understand our terms and conditions. Select "I have read and understand the terms and conditions" to continue with the onboarding process.
* **Documentation** (`documentation`, note, required: false): Please provide any relevant documentation (e.g., licenses, certifications, or insurance information) that supports your practice. This will help us verify your qualifications.
* **Onboarding Data** (`onboarding_data`, select_one, required: false): Select the option that best describes your onboarding preference:
	+ Option 1: Complete onboarding process with a scheduled call.
	+ Option 2: Complete onboarding process with a self-guided tutorial.
	+ Option 3: Complete onboarding process with a combination of both (e.g., initial call with a follow-up tutorial).

## Tips
* Ensure that you have a clear and recent photo of yourself.
* Double-check your email and phone number for accuracy.
* Complete all required fields to avoid delays in the onboarding process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
