## Rehabilitation Initial Documentation - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect essential information from patients for the rehabilitation process. This includes details about the patient's identity, medical history, and goals for rehabilitation. The data collected will help healthcare professionals tailor the rehabilitation plan to the patient's specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your patient's details in the "Patient Details" section.
2. Describe your patient's medical history in the "Medical History" section.
3. Select the rehabilitation goals that apply to your patient from the "Rehab Goals" dropdown menu.
4. Indicate your consent by selecting "True" in the "Consent" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Details** (`patient_details`, text, required: false): Enter your patient's name, date of birth, and any other relevant details.
- **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): Describe your patient's medical history, including any previous injuries or conditions that may impact their rehabilitation.
- **Rehab Goals** (`rehab_goals`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose one or more goals that apply to your patient from the list, such as Goal 1, Goal 2, or Goal 3.
- **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required: false): Select "True" to indicate that you consent to the rehabilitation process and the collection of your patient's data.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and honest information about your patient's medical history and goals.
- If you have any doubts or concerns about the form, consult with a healthcare professional before submitting the form.
- The data collected from this form will be used to create a personalized rehabilitation plan for your patient.
