# Animal Therapy Feedback Footage Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animal Therapy Feedback Footage Consent Form is designed to collect essential information from clients or their guardians regarding their experiences and feedback on animal therapy sessions. This form helps improve the effectiveness of the program by gathering ratings on comfort levels, progress, and consent for recordings and usage.
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have all necessary contact information and details about the therapy program.
2.  Fill out the client information section, including your name, email, and date of birth.
3.  Provide the required details about the therapy sessions, such as type, frequency, and start date.
4.  Rate your experience, comfort level, and progress in the provided rating fields.
5.  Consent to the recording of audio and video footage by selecting "Yes" from the options.
6.  Specify how the recorded footage can be used for therapy review, research, and education.
7.  Acknowledge confidentiality protections and agree to the terms.
8.  Finalize by confirming that you agree to the terms of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1.  **Client Name**: <span class="label">Client Name</span>
    *   Provide your full name.
    *   This is a required field.

### 2.  **Email Address**: <span class="label">Email Address</span>
    *   Enter your contact email.
    *   This is a required field for us to reach out to you for further questions or updates.

### 3.  **Phone Number**: <span class="label">Phone Number</span>
    *   If you have a contact phone number, please enter it here.
    *   This is not required but can be used for follow-up calls or messages.

### 4.  **Date of Birth**: <span class="label">Date of Birth</span>
    *   Enter your date of birth.
    *   This is a required field for age verification.

### 5.  **Parent or Guardian Name (if Applicable)**: <span class="label">Parent or Guardian Name (if Applicable)</span>
    *   If you're under 18, please enter the name of the parent or guardian.
    *   This is not required but may be needed for parental consent.

### 6.  **Parent or Guardian Email (if Applicable)**: <span class="label">Parent or Guardian Email (if Applicable)</span>
    *   If the above field is filled, please enter the email address of the parent or guardian.
    *   This field is not required but can be used for further communications.

### 7.  **Therapist Type**: <span class="label">Therapist Type</span>
    *   Choose the type of therapy you're participating in.
    *   This could be Equine, Canine, Feline, or another option if not listed.

### 8.  **Therapist Name**: <span class="label">Therapist Name</span>
    *   Enter the name of your current therapist.
    *   This is a required field to ensure we can provide the right support.

### 9.  **Therapy Start Date**: <span class="label">Therapy Start Date</span>
    *   Provide the date you started the therapy sessions.
    *   This is a required field for session tracking.

### 10. **Session Frequency**: <span class="label">Session Frequency</span>
    *   Tell us how often you attend sessions.
    *   Choose from Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, or As Scheduled.

### 11. **Session Date**: <span class="label">Session Date</span>
    *   Enter the date of the current session.
    *   This is a required field for tracking session history.

### 12. **Session Feedback**: <span class="label">Session Feedback</span>
    *   Share your thoughts and experiences from the current session.
    *   This is not required but helps us improve services.

### 13.  **Animal Interaction Comfort Level**: <span class="label">Animal Interaction Comfort Level</span>
    *   Rate how comfortable you feel with animal interaction.
    *   Choose from 1 (Very Uncomfortable), 2, 3, 4, or 5 (Very Comfortable).

### 14.  **Therapist's Skills**: <span class="label">Therapist's Skills</span>
    *   Rate your therapist's skills and knowledge.
    *   Choose from Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Excellent.

### 15.  **Perceived Progress**: <span class="label">Perceived Progress</span>
    *   Choose 'Yes' if you feel you are making progress.
    *   Choose 'No' if you don't feel any progress.
    *   Choose 'Uncertain' if you're not sure.

### 16.  **Progress Towards Goals**: <span class="label">Progress Towards Goals</span>
    *   Rate your progress towards therapy goals.
    *   Choose from No Progress, Minimal, Some, Good, or Excellent Progress.

### 17.  **Additional Comments**: <span class="label">Additional Comments</span>
    *   Share any additional thoughts or feedback about the session.
    *   This is not required but can be helpful for improvement.

### 18.  **Consent to Video Recording**: <span class="label">Consent to Video Recording</span>
    *   Agree or disagree with video recording of sessions.
    *   This is a required field for legal and consent purposes.

### 19.  **Consent to Audio Recording**: <span class="label">Consent to Audio Recording</span>
    *   Agree or disagree with audio recording of sessions.
    *   This is a required field for legal and consent purposes.

### 20.  **Recording Use for Therapy Review**: <span class="label">Recording Use for Therapy Review</span>
    *   Specify how recordings can be used for reviewing progress.
    *   Choose from 'Yes' or 'No'.

### 21.  **Recording Use for Research**: <span class="label">Recording Use for Research</span>
    *   Specify if recordings can be used for research purposes.
    *   Choose from 'Yes' or 'No'.

### 22.  **Recording Use for Education**: <span class="label">Recording Use for Education</span>
    *   Specify if recordings can be used for educational materials.
    *   Choose from 'Yes' or 'No'.

### 23.  **Anonymization for Public Use**: <span class="label">Anonymization for Public Use</span>
    *   Decide if identifying information will be removed if shared.
    *   Choose from 'Yes', 'No', or 'Not Applicable'.

### 24.  **Permission to Share on Social Media**: <span class="label">Permission to Share on Social Media</span>
    *   Agree or decline to share sessions or recordings on social media.
    *   This is a required field for social media sharing rights.

### 25.  **Confidentiality Agreement**: <span class="label">Confidentiality Agreement</span>
    *   Acknowledge and accept the confidentiality protections provided.
    *   This is a required field for legal and privacy reasons.

### 26.  **Agree to Terms**: <span class="label">Agree to Terms</span>
    *   Finalize by accepting all the terms of the form.
    *   This is a required field to confirm your agreement.

By carefully reading and understanding each field, you'll be well-equipped to complete the "Animal Therapy Feedback Footage Consent Form" effectively and confidently.
