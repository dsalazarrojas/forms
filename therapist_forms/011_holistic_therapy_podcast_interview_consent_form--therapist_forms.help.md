# Holistic Therapy Podcast Interview Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Holistic Therapy Podcast Interview Consent Form is a crucial document that helps us prepare for our conversation. It provides us with essential information about you and your preferences for the interview.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields, indicated by a red asterisk (\*) if applicable.
2. Select the correct options for each field where applicable.
3. Make sure to provide accurate and clear information for all fields.
4. Review your responses for completeness and clarity before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Please enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter your contact number.
* **Address 1** (`address_1`, text, required): Enter your address.
* **Address 2** (`address_2`, text, required): Enter your address (if applicable).
* **Address 3** (`address_3`, text, required): Enter your address (if applicable).
* **Address 4** (`address_4`, text, required): Enter your address (if applicable).
* **Address 5** (`address_5`, text, required): Enter your address (if applicable).
* **Type** (`select_type`, select_multiple, required): Please select the type(s) of therapy you are interested in.
* **Other** (`select_type_other`, text, required): If other, please describe the type of therapy you are interested in.
* **Date of Interview** (`date_of_interview`, date, required): Please select the date of the interview.
* **Time of Interview** (`time_of_interview`, time, required): Please select the time of the interview.
* **Duration of Interview** (`duration_of_interview`, number, required): Please select the duration of the interview.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Please provide any additional notes for your interview.
* **Therapist** (`therapist`, select_one, required): Please select the therapist you would like to interview with.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required): Please select the type of consent you grant for the interview.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, select_one, required): Please select the type of agreement you grant for the interview.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required): Please enter the relationship to the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, text, required): Please enter the emergency contact number.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, required): Please enter the emergency contact address.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, text, required): Please enter the emergency contact email.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Please sign this form to confirm your consent.
* **Submitted by** (`submitted_by`, select_one, required): Please select how you will be submitting the form.

Note: Please ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
