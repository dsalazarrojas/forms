# Post Massage Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and evaluation from clients after a massage session. Your input is essential in helping our therapists refine their skills and provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read through each section carefully to understand what information is required.
2. Answer each question thoroughly and honestly.
3. If you have no feedback for a particular question, simply leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Feedback** (`session_feedback`, text, optional): Describe your overall experience with the massage session. Share any positive or negative comments you have.
* **Massage Experience** (`massage_experience`, text, optional): Share your thoughts on how well the massage met your needs and expectations.
* **Areas Worked** (`areas_worked`, select_multiple, optional): Select the areas that were massaged during your session. This will help our therapists know what to focus on during future sessions.
	+ Options: Back of Neck, Back of Shoulders, Calf, Face, Feet, Hamstrings, Hands, Legs, Lower Back, Neck, Shoulders, Tight Muscle, Upper Back
* **Client Feedback** (`client_feedback`, text, optional): Share any feedback or comments you have about your experience.
* **Massage Tech Used** (`massage_tech_used`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of massage technique used during your session.
	+ Options: Swedish, Deep Tissue, Hot Stone
* **Areas to Focus On** (`areas_to_focus_on`, select_multiple, optional): Select areas that you feel need attention in future sessions.
	+ Options: Back, Feet, Hamstrings, Legs, Neck, Shoulders, Tight Muscle
* **Massage Time** (`massage_time`, time, optional): Enter the length of time your massage session lasted.
* **Date of Massage** (`date_of_massage`, date, optional): Enter the date of your massage session.
* **Time of Massage** (`time_of_massage`, time, optional): Enter the time of your massage session.
* **Massage Duration** (`massage_duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of your massage session.
* **Areas Worked Hands On** (`areas_worked_hands_on`, select_multiple, optional): Select areas that you felt the massage therapist worked on during the session.
	+ Options: Back of Neck, Back of Shoulders, Calf, Face, Feet, Hamstrings, Hands, Legs, Lower Back, Neck, Shoulders, Tight Muscle, Upper Back
