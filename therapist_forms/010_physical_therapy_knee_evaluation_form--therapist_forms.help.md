# Physical Therapy Knee Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a client's knee condition, medical history, and treatment goals. The therapist will use this information to create a comprehensive plan for treatment and evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form in the following order:
   - Client Knee Info (Page 1)
   - Medical History (Page 2)
   - Knee History (Page 3)
   - Current Condition (Page 4)
   - Knee Pain (Page 5)
   - Knee Pain Location (Page 6)
   - Knee Pain Intensity (Page 7)
   - Knee Pain Duration (Page 8)
   - Knee Pain Frequency (Page 9)
   - Knee Pain Characteristics (Page 10)
   - Knee Injury (Page 11)
   - Knee Surgery (Page 12)
   - Knee Medical History (Page 13)
   - Knee Medical Conditions (Page 14)
   - Knee Family History (Page 15)
   - Current Treatment (Page 16)
   - Knee Treatment Plan (Page 17)
   - Knee Evaluation (Page 18)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Knee Info** (`client_knee_info`, text, not required): Please describe your knee condition in a brief and concise manner.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, not required): This will include any relevant medical history that may be pertinent to your knee issue.
* **Knee History** (`knee_history`, text, not required): Describe how you are experiencing the issue with your knee.
* **Current Condition** (`current_condition`, text, not required): Describe how you are currently experiencing pain or symptoms.
* **Knee Pain** (`knee_pain`, number, not required): Please enter a number between 0-10 to indicate the intensity of your knee pain.
* **Knee Pain Location** (`knee_pain_location`, text, not required): Describe the location of the pain in your knee.
* **Knee Pain Intensity** (`knee_pain_intensity`, number, not required): Please enter a number between 0-10 to indicate the intensity of your knee pain.
* **Knee Pain Duration** (`knee_pain_duration`, number, not required): Please enter a number indicating how long you have been experiencing knee pain.
* **Knee Pain Frequency** (`knee_pain_frequency`, text, not required): Describe how often you experience knee pain.
* **Knee Pain Characteristics** (`knee_pain_characteristics`, text, not required): Describe any specific characteristics of your knee pain.
* **Knee Injury** (`knee_injury`, text, not required): Describe any knee injuries or traumas you have experienced.
* **Knee Surgery** (`knee_surgery`, text, not required): Describe any knee surgeries you have undergone.
* **Knee Medical History** (`knee_medical_history`, text, not required): This will include any other medical history that may be pertinent to your knee issue.
* **Knee Medical Conditions** (`knee_medical_conditions`, text, not required): Describe any medical conditions that may be contributing to your knee issue.
* **Knee Family History** (`knee_family_history`, text, not required): Describe any family history that may be contributing to your knee issue.
* **Current Treatment** (`knee_current_treatment`, text, not required): Describe any current treatments you are receiving for your knee issue.
* **Knee Treatment Plan** (`knee_treatment_plan`, text, not required): Describe any treatments or plans you have in place for your knee issue.
* **Knee Evaluation** (`knee_evaluation`, note, not required): This is where the therapist will take notes and make observations.
* **Knee Recommendations** (`knee_recommendations`, text, not required): Describe any recommendations or advice you have received from previous therapists or healthcare professionals.
