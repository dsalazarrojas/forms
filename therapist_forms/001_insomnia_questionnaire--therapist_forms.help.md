# insomnia_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a person's sleep habits and experiences. It's intended to help healthcare professionals, researchers, or individuals seeking help for sleep issues by gathering data on various aspects of sleep quality, duration, and satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review each page carefully to understand the questions and fields.
2.  Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3.  Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
4.  If you need help or clarification on any question, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **insomnia_questionnaire** (`title`, text, optional): This field is the title of the form, providing a brief description of what the form is about.
*   **sleep_history** (`sleep_history`, text, optional): This field is for providing a brief overview of your sleep history, including any relevant medical conditions or treatments that may affect your sleep.
*   **general_sleep_quality** (`general_sleep_quality`, text, optional): This field is for describing your overall sleep quality, including any factors that influence your sleep, such as environment, diet, or exercise.
*   **daytime_function** (`daytime_function`, text, optional): This field is for noting any changes in your daily functioning or energy levels since waking up.
*   **evening_winding_down** (`evening_winding_down`, text, optional): This field is for describing your evening wind-down activities, such as reading, relaxing, or other calming habits.
*   **sleep_latency** (`sleep_latency`, text, optional): This field is for estimating the time it takes you to fall asleep on average.
*   **sleep_efficiency** (`sleep_efficiency`, text, optional): This field is for describing how easily you can fall asleep and stay asleep.
*   **sleep_quality** (`sleep_quality`, text, optional): This field is for rating your sleep quality, including restfulness, refreshingness, and satisfaction.
*   **sleep_latency_sufficient** (`sleep_latency_sufficient`, text, optional): This field is for estimating the time it takes to fall asleep on sufficient quality sleep.
*   **wake_up_time** (`wake_up_time`, time, optional): This field is for noting the time you usually wake up.
*   **sleep_disrupted** (`sleep_disrupted`, select_one, optional): This field is for selecting how often you experience sleep disruptions, such as waking up during the night.
*   **difficulty_falling_asleep** (`difficulty_falling_asleep`, select_multiple, optional): This field is for rating how often you experience difficulty falling asleep, with options ranging from "Not at all" to "More than 120 minutes".
*   **sleep_stages** (`sleep_stages`, select_multiple, optional): This field is for rating how often you experience different stages of sleep, such as one, two, three, four, or five stages.
*   **sleep_latency_duration** (`sleep_latency_duration`, number, optional): This field is for estimating the duration of your sleep latency, with no options provided.
*   **sleep_latency_frequency** (`sleep_latency_frequency`, number, optional): This field is for estimating how often you experience sleep latency, with no options provided.
*   **sleep_latency_impact** (`sleep_latency_impact`, text, optional): This field is for describing the impact of your sleep latency on your daily functioning or well-being.
*   **sleep_latency_satisfactory** (`sleep_latency_satisfactory`, select_one, optional): This field is for rating the satisfaction of your sleep latency, with options ranging from "Very Satisfactory" to "Unsatisfactory".
*   **sleep_latency_satisfactory_frequency** (`sleep_latency_satisfactory_frequency`, select_one, optional): This field is for rating how often you experience satisfactory sleep latency, with options ranging from "Every Day" to "Rarely".
*   **sleep_latency_satisfactory_duration** (`sleep_latency_satisfactory_duration`, text, optional): This field is for describing the duration of your satisfactory sleep latency.
*   **sleep_latency_satisfactory_impact** (`sleep_latency_satisfactory_impact`, text, optional): This field is for describing the impact of your satisfactory sleep latency on your daily functioning or well-being.
*   **difficulty_sleeping** (`difficulty_sleeping`, text, optional): This field is for describing any challenges you face while sleeping, such as anxiety or insomnia.
*   **sleep_quality_felt** (`sleep_quality_felt`, select_multiple, optional): This field is for rating how you feel about your sleep quality, with options ranging from "Not at all" to "Extremely".
*   **sleep_quality_felt_impact** (`sleep_quality_felt_impact`, select_one, optional): This field is for rating the satisfaction of your sleep quality, with options ranging from "Very Satisfactory" to "Unsatisfactory".
*   **sleep_disruption** (`sleep_disruption`, text, optional): This field is for describing any sleep disruptions, such as snoring or restless leg syndrome.
*   **sleep_latency_disruption** (`sleep_latency_disruption`, text, optional): This field is for describing any sleep latency disruptions, such as difficulty falling asleep or waking up frequently.

## Tips

*   Be honest and thorough in your answers to help provide accurate data for analysis.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section or consult a healthcare professional.
*   This form is for general information gathering and may not be suitable for all purposes. Consult with a healthcare professional or researcher before using this form for clinical or research purposes.
