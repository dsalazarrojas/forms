# Coronavirus Intake Form For Massage Therapy - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the coronavirus intake form for massage therapy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Select the relevant options for each field.
3. Provide accurate and detailed information in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Client ID (`client_id`, text, required: false)
Enter your unique identifier or any other relevant information to help our staff identify you correctly.

### 2. Client Name (`client_name`, text, required: false)
Please provide your full name, as this will be used to address you during the massage therapy session.

### 3. Contact Method (`contact_method`, text, required: false)
Please indicate how you would like to be contacted (e.g., email, phone, or in-person).

### 4. Health Status (`health_status`, select_multiple, required: true)
Select the health status that best describes your current condition:

*   Healthy
*   Slightly unwell
*   Seriously unwell

### 5. Massage Goals (`massage_goals`, text, required: false)
Describe your goals for the massage therapy session, e.g., relaxation, pain relief, or stress reduction.

### 6. Massage Frequency (`massage_frequency`, select_one, required: true)
Choose the frequency of your massage therapy sessions:

*   First time
*   Regular
*   Frequent

### 7. Massage Area (`massage_area`, select_multiple, required: true)
Select the areas you would like to focus on during the massage therapy session:

*   Back
*   Neck
*   Shoulders
*   Legs
*   Feet

### 8. Massage Style (`massage_style`, select_one, required: true)
Choose the massage style you prefer:

*   Swedish
*   Deep Tissue
*   Shiatsu

### 9. Medical Conditions (`medical_conditions`, text, required: false)
List any medical conditions or concerns you have that may impact the massage therapy session.

### 10. Medication (`medication`, text, required: false)
Please list any medications you are currently taking.

### 11. Allergies (`allergies`, text, required: false)
List any allergies you may have.

### 12. Emergency Contacts (`emergency_contacts`, text, required: false)
Provide the contact information for a trusted emergency contact.

### 13. Next Appointment (`next_appointment`, date, required: false)
Schedule your next appointment (if applicable).

### 14. Appointment Time (`appointment_time`, time, required: false)
Select the time for your next appointment.

### 15. Therapist (`therapist`, text, required: false)
Name of the massage therapist who will be conducting the session.

### 16. Massage Duration (`massage_duration`, number, required: false)
Specify the length of your desired massage session.

### 17. Massage Frequency (`massage_frequency`, select_one, required: true)
Choose the frequency of your massage therapy sessions for this specific session:

*   10-15
*   30-60
*   90-120

### 18. Massage Area 2 (`massage_area_2`, select_multiple, required: false)
Select the areas you would like to focus on during the massage therapy session:

*   Back
*   Neck
*   Shoulders
*   Legs
*   Feet

### 19. Massage Style 2 (`massage_style_2`, select_one, required: false)
Choose the massage style you prefer for this session:

*   Swedish
*   Deep Tissue
*   Shiatsu

### 20. Medical Conditions 2 (`medical_conditions_2`, text, required: false)
List any medical conditions or concerns for this specific session.

### 21. Medication 2 (`medication_2`, text, required: false)
Please list any medications you are currently taking for this session.

### 22. Allergies 2 (`allergies_2`, text, required: false)
List any allergies you may have for this session.

### 23. Emergency Contacts 2 (`emergency_contacts_2`, text, required: false)
Provide the contact information for a trusted emergency contact for this session.

### 24. Next Appointment 2 (`next_appointment_2`, date, required: false)
Schedule your next appointment (if applicable).

### 25. Appointment Time 2 (`appointment_time_2`, time, required: false)
Select the time for your next appointment.

## Tips
