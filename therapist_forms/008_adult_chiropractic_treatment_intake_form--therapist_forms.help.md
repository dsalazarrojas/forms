# Adult Chiropractic Treatment Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about the patient's medical history, contact details, and consent for chiropractic treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information for each field. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (*). Make sure to provide accurate and clear information for each field.
2. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly. If you are unsure about any question or field, please ask the clinic staff for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient full name** (`patient_name`, text, **Required**): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, **Required**): Use the YYYY-MM-DD format to enter your date of birth.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, text, **Required**): Enter your preferred contact phone number.
* **Reason for visit** (`reason_for_visit`, text, **Required**): Describe your current complaint or injury that brought you to the chiropractor.
* **Pain level** (`pain_scale`, number, **Required**): Rate your pain level from 0 (no pain) to 10 (worst pain).
* **Relevant medical history** (`medical_history`, text, **Optional**): Share any relevant medical history, including surgeries, chronic conditions, or medications.
* **Previous chiropractic or physical therapy** (`previous_treatment`, text, **Optional**): If you have received previous chiropractic or physical therapy, provide the dates and names of providers.
* **Consent to treatment** (`consent_treatment`, select_one, **Required**): Select "I agree" or "I do not agree" to manual therapy and adjustments.
* **Emergency contact name and phone** (`emergency_contact`, text, **Optional**): If you have an emergency contact, enter their name and phone number.
* **Signature date** (`signature_date`, date, **Required**): Date and time of consent for treatment.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, **Required**): A digital signature to confirm consent.

Note: This guide is intended to assist you in understanding each field and its purpose. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the clinic staff for clarification.
