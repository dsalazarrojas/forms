# Adult Personal Analysis Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Full Name (Required)
* Label: Full Name
* Type: text
* Hint: Enter your full name

### 2. Age Group (Required)
* Label: Age Group
* Type: select_one
* Options: "18-25", "26-35", "36-45", "46-55", "56-65", "65+"
* Hint: Select your age range

### 3. Occupation (Optional)
* Label: Occupation
* Type: text
* Hint: Your current job or profession

### 4. -- Personal Information -- (Note)
* Label: -- Personal Information --
* Type: note
* Hint: This section is informational

### 5. Social Energy Level (Required)
* Label: Social Energy Level
* Type: select_one
* Options: "Extremely outgoing", "Mostly outgoing", "Balanced", "Mostly reserved", "Extremely reserved"
* Hint: How do you typically feel in social situations

### 6. Decision Making Style (Required)
* Label: Decision Making Style
* Type: select_one
* Options: "Rely on logic and facts", "Rely on emotions and values", "Seek advice from others", "Analyze all options thoroughly", "Go with gut instinct"
* Hint: How do you typically approach making decisions

### 7. Stress Management Approach (Required)
* Label: Stress Management Approach
* Type: select_one
* Options: "Face problems directly", "Take time to think", "Seek support from others", "Avoid the situation", "Physical activity or exercise"
* Hint: How do you typically handle stressful situations

### 8. Communication Preference (Required)
* Label: Communication Preference
* Type: select_one
* Options: "Face-to-face", "Phone calls", "Text messaging", "Email", "Written letters"
* Hint: How do you prefer to communicate with others

### 9. Life Values Ranking (Optional)
* Label: Life Values Ranking
* Type: text
* Hint: Rank the following in order of importance to you (1 being most important)

### 10. Relationship Patterns (Optional)
* Label: Relationship Patterns
* Type: text
* Hint: How would you describe your typical relationships with others

### 11. Personal Growth Goals (Optional)
* Label: Personal Growth Goals
* Type: text
* Hint: What areas of yourself would you like to develop or improve

### 12. Coping Strategies (Optional)
* Label: Coping Strategies
* Type: text
* Hint: What methods do you use to cope with challenges

Note: The optional fields (Occupation, Life Values Ranking, Relationship Patterns, Personal Growth Goals, and Coping Strategies) are intended to help provide more in-depth information about the user, but are not mandatory to complete the form.

This form is designed to be completed by adults and is not intended for minors. Please take your time to complete the form and provide accurate information to help us understand you better.
