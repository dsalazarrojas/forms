# Hypnotherapy Session Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and information from patients after a hypnotherapy session, helping the practice to refine and improve its services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Select the session outcome that best describes your experience.
3. Provide any additional feedback or comments about your session.
4. Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for further feedback or to receive updates about your session.
5. Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone.
6. Select the date and time of your hypnotherapy session.
7. Take a moment to add any additional notes about your session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Feedback** (`session_feedback`): Please provide a brief description of your overall experience with the session.
* **Session Outcome** (`session_outcome`): Select the outcome that best describes your experience after the session:
	+ **Positive**: You felt a significant improvement in your condition or symptoms after the session.
	+ **Negative**: Unfortunately, you did not experience any improvement in your condition or symptoms after the session.
	+ **Neutral**: Your experience was mixed or you are unsure about the effectiveness of the session.
* **Session Outcome Other** (`session_outcome_other`): If you did not select one of the above options, please specify what happened during the session.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`): Share any additional thoughts, comments, or suggestions you have about your session.
* **Email** (`email`): If you would like to be contacted for further feedback or to receive updates about your session, please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): If you would like to be contacted by phone, please enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`date`): Select the date of your hypnotherapy session.
* **Time** (`time`): Select the time of your hypnotherapy session.
* **Note** (`note`): Add any additional notes or comments about your session. 
* **Email Confirmation** (`email_confirmation`): Please enter your email address again to confirm that it is correct. 

## Tips
- Be honest and open when providing feedback.
- Please keep your feedback confidential and only share your thoughts and experiences relevant to the session.
- If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to add them to the "Note" field.
