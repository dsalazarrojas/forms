# Telehealth Physical Therapy Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Telehealth Physical Therapy Consent Form is a document that aims to gather essential information from patients before beginning telehealth physical therapy services. This form ensures that patients understand the terms and conditions of the treatment, including payment and confidentiality agreements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Telehealth Physical Therapy Consent Form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand all the questions and terms.
2. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm your understanding of the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (1)**: Please provide your name, address, and any other relevant contact information so that we can reach you for further communication.
* **I am interested in virtual physical therapy (2)**: This question is asking if you are interested in receiving physical therapy through telehealth (online) services. Please select 'Yes' if you want to proceed with virtual services, or 'No' if you prefer in-person services.
* **Payment Terms (3)**: Please read and understand the payment terms, which may include any applicable copayments, fees, or financial responsibilities.
* **Payment Information (4)**: If you are paying for services through your insurance, your provider's name, policy number, and any other relevant information should be provided. If you are paying out-of-pocket, please enter the payment amount and method (e.g., credit card, check).
* **Signature (5)**: This is the space for your digital signature, confirming that you have read and understood the terms and conditions. Please enter your name and date for verification.

## Tips

* Please ensure that your payment information is accurate and up-to-date to avoid any issues with your appointment or services.
* If you are unsure about any of the terms or conditions, do not hesitate to ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
* By signing this form, you are confirming your understanding and agreement to the terms and conditions of the telehealth physical therapy services.
