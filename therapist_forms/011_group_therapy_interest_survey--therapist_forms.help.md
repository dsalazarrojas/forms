# group_therapy_interest_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "group_therapy_interest_survey" form is designed to collect information about a potential group therapy participant's details, interests, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the "group_therapy_interest_survey" form, follow the instructions below.

1. Start by filling out the **User Details** page, which includes fields for your name, contact information, and other personal details.
2. Next, move on to the **Contact Info** page, where you will enter your contact information for emergency purposes.
3. On the **Session Details** page, provide more information about your availability for group therapy sessions.
4. On the **Therapy Goals** page, specify your goals for therapy.
5. On the **Current Issues** page, describe any issues you would like to discuss in group therapy.
6. On the **Previous Therapy Experience** page, share your experience with previous therapy sessions.
7. On the **Session Preferences** page, select the type of therapy sessions you prefer (Individual, Group, or Both).
8. Finally, complete the **Emergency Contact** and **Emergency Phone** and **Emergency Email** pages with the contact information of a person who can be reached in case of an emergency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details**: Enter your name, contact information, and other personal details.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your contact information for emergency purposes.
* **Session Details**: Provide more information about your availability for group therapy sessions.
* **Therapy Goals**: Specify your goals for therapy.
* **Current Issues**: Describe any issues you would like to discuss in group therapy.
* **Previous Therapy Experience**: Share your experience with previous therapy sessions.
* **Session Preferences**: Select the type of therapy sessions you prefer (Individual, Group, or Both).
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and contact information of a person who can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Email**: Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.

## Tips

* Please answer all fields truthfully and accurately to ensure the best possible match with a group therapy session.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the therapist.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the form's hints or ask the therapist for guidance.
