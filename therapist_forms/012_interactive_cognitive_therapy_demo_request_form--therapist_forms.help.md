# interactive_cognitive_therapy_demo_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for those interested in learning more about interactive cognitive therapy and is designed to collect contact information and any specific messages or inquiries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields (Last Name, Email, Message).
2. Provide your First Name (optional, but recommended for a more personalized response).
3. Enter your Phone number (optional, but recommended for follow-up communication).
4. Type your message clearly and concisely, so the therapy provider can best understand your needs and questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (First Name, text, optional)**: Enter your first name for more personalized communication.
* **Last Name (Last Name, text, required)**: Enter your last name to ensure accurate communication.
* **Email (Email, email, optional)**: Enter your email address to receive follow-up communication.
* **Phone (Phone, text, optional)**: Enter your phone number for more direct communication.
* **Message (Message, text, required)**: Clearly type your message or inquiry about interactive cognitive therapy.

## Tips
* Please fill in all required fields (Last Name, Message).
* If you are unsure about anything, do not hesitate to ask.
* This form is for demo purposes, so please do not submit personal or sensitive data unless you are certain it will not be shared outside of a controlled environment.
