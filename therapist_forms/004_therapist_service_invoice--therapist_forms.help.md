# Therapist Service Invoice - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to generate a detailed invoice for a therapist's services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the session date from the calendar or enter it manually.
2. Enter the client's name.
3. Enter the session duration in hours and minutes.
4. Enter the service fee for the session.
5. Select the payment status, either Unpaid or Paid.
6. Enter the payment date and amount if applicable.
7. Enter any session notes or comments.
8. Enter the client's phone number.
9. Enter the client's email address.
10. Select the assigned user who performed the session.
11. Enter the name of the therapist who provided the session.
12. Enter the name of the clinic where the session took place.
13. Select the type of session, either Individual or Family.
14. Enter the billing address (optional).
15. Enter the session duration in hours and minutes, if not already entered.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Date (1)**: Enter the date of the session. This field is not required but is helpful for tracking purposes.
* **Client Name (2)**: Enter the name of the client. This is a required field.
* **Session Duration (3)**: Enter the duration of the session in hours and minutes. This field is not required but helps to accurately calculate the service fee.
* **Service Fee (4)**: Enter the amount charged for the session. This field is not required but is necessary for accurate billing.
* **Payment Status (5)**: Select the payment status of the session. Choose Unpaid or Paid to indicate whether the client has paid or not.
* **Payment Date (6)**: Enter the date of payment if the payment status is Paid. This field is not required if the payment status is Unpaid.
* **Payment Amount (7)**: Enter the amount paid by the client if the payment status is Paid. This field is not required if the payment status is Unpaid.
* **Session Notes (8)**: Enter any relevant notes or comments about the session. This field is not required but helps to provide context for future references.
* **Client Phone (9)**: Enter the client's phone number. This field is not required but helps to facilitate communication.
* **Client Email (10)**: Enter the client's email address. This field is not required but helps to ensure accurate billing and communication.
* **Assigned User (11)**: Select the user who was assigned to perform the session. This field is not required but helps to track who provided the service.
* **Therapist Name (12)**: Enter the name of the therapist who provided the session. This field is not required but helps to track who provided the service.
* **Clinic Name (13)**: Enter the name of the clinic where the session took place. This field is not required but helps to provide context.
* **Session Type (14)**: Select the type of session, either Individual or Family. This field is not required but helps to categorize sessions.
* **Billing Address (15)**: Enter the billing address (optional). This field is not required but helps to facilitate payment processing.
* **Session Duration Hours (16)**: Enter the duration of the session in hours. This field is not required but helps to calculate the service fee.
* **Session Duration Minutes (17)**: Enter the duration of the session in minutes. This field is not required but helps to calculate the service fee.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter all required fields correctly to ensure accurate processing.
* If the payment status is Paid, ensure that the payment date and amount are entered correctly.
* If the session duration is entered, ensure that the session duration hours and minutes fields are also entered correctly.
* Review each field carefully to ensure that the information is accurate and complete.
