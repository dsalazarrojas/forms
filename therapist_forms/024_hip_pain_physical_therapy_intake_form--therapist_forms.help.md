# HipPainPhysicalTherapistForm - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from patients with hip pain, helping the physical therapist to assess their condition and provide proper treatment.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Answer the questions about your pain location, severity, and medical history.
2. Select the options that best describe your condition.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm that you have completed it accurately.
