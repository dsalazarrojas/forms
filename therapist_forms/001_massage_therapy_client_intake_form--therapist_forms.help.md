# Massage Therapy Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather relevant information from potential clients for a massage therapy session. 

## How To Complete This Form

This form can be completed in the following steps:
- Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the client is a new client.
- Provide any relevant medical history information in the text field.
- Select any relevant medical conditions from the given options.
- Choose whether or not the client has any allergies.
- Select whether or not the client is currently taking any medications.
- Select any relevant medical procedures the client has undergone.
- Describe the client's massage therapy goals in the text field.
- Provide any relevant physical history information in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Client** (`new_client`, select_one, optional): This field asks whether the client is a new client or not. Selecting "Yes" indicates that the client is new, while "No" indicates that the client has previously been a client.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): This field is for the client to provide any relevant medical history information that may be important for the massage therapist to know.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, select_multiple, optional): This field allows the client to select their preferred method of contact (e.g., phone, email, text). They can also select "Other" to provide a different contact method.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, select_multiple, optional): This field allows the client to select any relevant medical conditions they may have, such as high blood pressure, diabetes, etc. They can also select "Other" to provide a different medical condition.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_multiple, optional): This field asks whether the client has any allergies. They can select "Yes" if they have allergies or "No" if they do not.
* **Medications** (`medications`, select_multiple, optional): This field asks whether the client is currently taking any medications. They can select "Yes" or "No".
* **Medical Procedures** (`medical_procedures`, select_multiple, optional): This field allows the client to select any relevant medical procedures they may have undergone, such as surgeries, etc. They can select "Other" to provide a different medical procedure.
* **Massage Therapy Goals** (`massage_therapy_goals`, text, optional): This field is for the client to describe their goals for the massage therapy session.
* **Physical History** (`physical_history`, text, optional): This field is for the client to provide any relevant physical history information that may be important for the massage therapist to know.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and honest information to ensure the best possible experience and services for the client.
- If unsure about any of the fields, ask the massage therapist for guidance.
- The form is optional and can be completed at the client's discretion.
