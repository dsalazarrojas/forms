# Hypnotherapy Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain informed consent from patients before starting a hypnotherapy session. It is a standard clinical practice that ensures patients are aware of the therapy process, their rights, and the expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the patient's information, including their name and contact details.
2. Describe the hypnotherapy session, including the treatment plan and any relevant medical history.
3. Obtain the patient's consent by selecting "Yes" in the "Consent Given" field.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm that the patient has provided informed consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Hypnotherapy Informed Consent Form** (`1`, `text`, `required`):
	+ This is the title of the form, which should be filled in with the patient's name.
* **Patient Information** (`2`, `text`, `required`):
	+ Please provide the patient's name, contact details, and any relevant medical history.
* **Therapist** (`3`, `text`, `required`):
	+ Enter your name and contact details as the therapist providing the hypnotherapy service.
* **Contact Details** (`4`, `text`, `required`):
	+ Provide your contact details, including phone number and email address.
* **Therapy Details** (`5`, `text`, `required`):
	+ Describe the hypnotherapy session, including the treatment plan and any relevant medical history.
* **Consent Date** (`6`, `date`, `required`):
	+ Enter the date on which the patient provided informed consent.
* **Consent Time** (`7`, `time`, `required`):
	+ Enter the time at which the patient provided informed consent.
* **Consent Given** (`8`, `select_one`, `required`):
	+ Select "Yes" to confirm that the patient has provided informed consent.
* **Additional Comments** (`9`, `text`, `optional`):
	+ Add any additional comments or notes about the patient's consent.
* **Signed By** (`10`, `text`, `required`):
	+ Enter your name as the therapist providing the hypnotherapy service.
* **Therapist Signature** (`11`, `text`, `optional`):
	+ Add a signature of yourself as the therapist.
* **Patient Signature** (`12`, `text`, `optional`):
	+ Add a signature of the patient, if possible.
* **Date Signed** (`13`, `date`, `optional`):
	+ Enter the date on which the patient signed the form, if possible.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the patient understands the content of this form before they sign it.
* Make sure to keep a record of the informed consent provided by the patient.
* Review and update the form regularly to ensure it meets your clinical needs.
