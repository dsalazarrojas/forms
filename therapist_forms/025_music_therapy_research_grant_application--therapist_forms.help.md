# Music Therapy Research Grant Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for research studies focused on music therapy. It is used to gather details about your research proposal, budget, and supporting documents for evaluation by a committee or panel.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Provide clear and concise answers to each question.
3. Proofread your responses for spelling, grammar, and formatting errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Research Proposal
* **Research Proposal** (`research_proposal`, text, optional): Briefly introduce your research proposal, highlighting the research focus, goals, and objectives.

### Budget
* **Budget** (`budget`, number, true): Enter a valid budget number to indicate your total budget for the research project.

### Supporting Documents
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, text, optional): Attach any supporting documents relevant to your research proposal, and provide a brief description of each attached document.

### Research Justification
* **Research Justification** (`research_justification`, text, true): Provide a detailed justification for your research, explaining why it is relevant and valuable.

### Research Objectives
* **Research Objectives** (`research_objectives`, text, true): Clearly state the specific objectives of your research project.

### Budget Justification
* **Budget Justification** (`budget_justification`, text, true): Explain how your budget aligns with your research project's goals and objectives.

### Supporting Documents Description
* **Supporting Documents Description** (`supporting_documents_description`, text, false): If you have attached supporting documents, provide a brief description of each attached document to help the review committee understand their relevance.

## Tips
* Ensure your responses are accurate and truthful.
* Use clear and concise language throughout the form.
* Attach any additional information that supports your research proposal, if necessary.
