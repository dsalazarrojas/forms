# Client Intake Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Intake Consent Form is a crucial document that clients must complete before starting any treatment or product usage. This form ensures that clients are aware of their skin type, medical history, and the potential outcomes and risks associated with any treatment or product usage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. Enter your email address for communication purposes.
3. If applicable, provide your date of birth.
4. Answer the questions in the "Aesthetic Profile" section honestly.
5. Select your primary skin type from the options provided.
6. Indicate if you are currently seeing a dermatologist for any condition.
7. Answer whether you use Retin-A, Adapalene, or Glycolic Acid products.
8. Indicate if you have had a chemical peel or laser treatment in the last 4 weeks.
9. List any medications you are currently taking.
10. Read and understand the informed consent section carefully.
11. Check that you understand that results are not guaranteed and vary by individual.
12. Confirm that you have disclosed all known allergies and health conditions.
13. Sign your full name in the "Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`dob`, `date`, optional): If applicable, provide your date of birth.
* **Aesthetic Profile** (`section_profile`, `note`, optional): Answer the questions in this section honestly to help us understand your skin profile.
* **What is your primary skin type?** (`skin_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the following skin types: Dry, Oily, Normal, Sensitive, or Combination.
* **Are you currently seeing a dermatologist for any condition?** (`dermatologist_visit`, `select_one`, optional): Check if you are currently seeing a dermatologist.
* **Do you use Retin-A, Adapalene, or Glycolic Acid products?** (`active_ingredients`, `select_one`, required): Answer whether you use these products.
* **Have you had a chemical peel or laser treatment in the last 4 weeks?** (`recent_treatments`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you have had such treatments.
* **Please list any medications you are currently taking** (`medication_list`, `text`, optional): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Informed Consent** (`section_consent`, `note`, optional): Read and understand this section carefully.
* **I understand that results are not guaranteed and vary by individual** (`results_ack`, `select_one`, required): Check that you understand this.
* **I have disclosed all known allergies and health conditions** (`allergy_ack`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you have disclosed all known allergies and health conditions.
* **Signature - Type Full Name** (`signature`, `text`, required): Sign your full name in this field.
