# Client Progress Notes Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help healthcare professionals, such as physical therapists and counselors, document the progress of their clients after each session. It is a crucial tool for monitoring progress, identifying areas that require more attention, and informing future treatment strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter the client's name** in the `Client Name` field.
2. **Select the date** of the session using the `Date of Session` field.
3. **Enter the time** of the session using the `Time of Session` field.
4. **Enter your name** in the `Therapist Name` field.
5. Select the **type of session** using the `Type of Session` field (e.g., Massage therapy, Physical therapy, Mental health counseling, Medical treatment, or Other).
6. **Enter the chief complaint or focus area** in the `Chief Complaint or Focus Area` field.
7. **Describe what the client reports** in the `Subjective Notes -- What the client reports` field.
8. **Describe what you observed** in the `Objective Observations -- What you observed` field.
9. **Summarize your professional assessment** of the client's condition and progress in the `Assessment Summary` field.
10. **List the specific techniques or interventions** used in the `Treatment Provided` field.
11. **Describe the client's response to treatment** in the `Client Response to Treatment` field.
12. **Note any progress towards treatment goals** in the `Progress Towards Treatment Goals` field.
13. **Rate the client's pain level before and after the session** using the `Pain Level Before Session` and `Pain Level After Session` fields.
14. **Provide recommendations for the client** in the `Recommendations for Client` field.
15. **Plan the focus areas and treatment approach for the next session** in the `Plan for Next Session` field.
16. **Add any additional notes** in the `Additional Notes` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the client.
* **Date of Session** (`session_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the session.
* **Time of Session** (`session_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the session.
* **Therapist Name** (`therapist_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the therapist.
* **Type of Session** (`session_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of session (e.g., Massage therapy, Physical therapy, Mental health counseling, Medical treatment, or Other).
* **Chief Complaint or Focus Area** (`chief_complaint`, text, required): Enter the main issue addressed in this session.
* **Subjective Notes -- What the client reports** (`subjective_notes`, text, required): Describe what the client reports (e.g., pain levels, symptoms, concerns expressed).
* **Objective Observations -- What you observed** (`objective_observations`, text, required): Describe what you observed (e.g., range of motion, muscle tone, posture, observable symptoms).
* **Assessment Summary** (`assessment_summary`, text, required): Summarize your professional assessment of the client's condition and progress.
* **Treatment Provided** (`treatment_provided`, text, required): List the specific techniques or interventions used.
* **Client Response to Treatment** (`client_response`, text, optional): Describe the client's response to treatment (if applicable).
* **Progress Towards Treatment Goals** (`progress_towards_goals`, text, optional): Note any progress towards treatment goals (if applicable).
* **Pain Level Before Session** (`pain_level_before`, number, optional): Rate the client's pain level before the session on a scale of 1-10.
* **Pain Level After Session** (`pain_level_after`, number, optional): Rate the client's pain level after the session on a scale of 1-10.
* **Recommendations for Client** (`recommendations`, text, optional): Provide any recommendations for the client.
* **Plan for Next Session** (`next_session_plan`, text, optional): Plan the focus areas and treatment approach for the next session.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes_additional`, text, optional): Add any other relevant information.

## Tips

* Make sure to complete this form thoroughly and accurately for each client session.
* Review the client's progress over time to adjust treatment strategies as needed.
* Refer to this form to inform future sessions and adjust your approach based on the client's response.
