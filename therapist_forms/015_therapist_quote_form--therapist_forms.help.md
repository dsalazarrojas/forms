# Therapist Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Therapist Quote Form is a tool for therapists to provide quotes to clients for upcoming sessions or services. This form helps therapists gather important details and information to ensure a smooth and efficient quote process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the necessary information for the quote.
2. Select the services you provide from the list of options.
3. Enter the quote amount and any additional information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Quote Details** (`quote_details`, `text`, required: false): This is the main section where you will enter the quote details, including any relevant information about the services you provide.
* **Services** (`services`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the services you will be offering to the client from the provided options (Insurance, Sliding Scale, Self Pay, Military).
* **Therapist Info** (`therapist_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your personal information, such as your name and contact number.
* **Policies** (`policies`, `text`, required: false): This is where you will enter any policies or agreements that apply to the quote.
* **Costs** (`costs`, `text`, required: false): Enter any costs associated with the services, such as session fees.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter the client's contact information, including their name and contact number.
* **Payment Information** (`payment`, `text`, required: false): Enter the payment information, including payment method and due date.
* **Quote** (`quote`, `number`, required: false): Enter the quote amount.
* **Additional Information** (`quote`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional information you want to include in the quote. 

Note: Some fields are not required, but it's essential to fill in the required fields for the form to be complete and effective.
