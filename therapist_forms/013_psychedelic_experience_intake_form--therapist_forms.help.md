# Psychedelic Experience Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about a user's experiences with psychedelic substances.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer the questions as thoroughly and honestly as possible.
2. Describe your experiences in detail, including your feelings, visual and auditory changes, emotional changes, and social interactions.
3. Provide as much information as possible about your last experience, including the duration, intensity, and frequency of use.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Identifier** (`user_identifier`, `text`, `optional`): Please enter a unique identifier for yourself, such as your name or username.
* **Experience Description** (`experience_description`, `note`, `optional`): Describe your experience in as much detail as possible, including any notable events or sensations that occurred.
* **Feelings During Experience** (`feelings_during_experience`, `text`, `optional`): Describe your feelings during the experience, including any emotions, thoughts, or sensations that you experienced.
* **Visual Changes** (`visual_changes`, `text`, `optional`): Describe any visual changes you experienced, such as changes in perception or hallucinations.
* **Auditory Changes** (`auditory_changes`, `text`, `optional`): Describe any auditory changes you experienced, such as altered hearing or sound sensitivity.
* **Tactile Changes** (`tactile_changes`, `text`, `optional`): Describe any tactile changes you experienced, such as altered touch or sensitivity.
* **Temporal Changes** (`temporal_changes`, `text`, `optional`): Describe any changes in your sense of time or timing.
* **Emotional Changes** (`emotional_changes`, `text`, `optional`): Describe any emotional changes you experienced, such as altered mood or emotional state.
* **Social Impact** (`social_impact`, `text`, `optional`): Describe how your social interactions were affected by the experience.
* **Overall Intensity** (`overall_intensity`, `number`, `optional`): Rate the overall intensity of your experience on a scale of 1-10.
* **Peak Intensity** (`peak_intensity`, `number`, `optional`): Rate the peak intensity of your experience on a scale of 1-10.
* **Time of Day** (`time_of_day`, `text`, `optional`): Describe the time of day when you experienced the psychedelic substance.
* **Duration** (`duration`, `text`, `optional`): Describe the duration of your experience.
* **Setting** (`setting`, `text`, `optional`): Describe the setting in which you experienced the psychedelic substance.
* **Dosage** (`dosage`, `text`, `optional`): Describe the dosage or amount of the psychedelic substance you used.
* **Substance** (`substance`, `text`, `optional`): Describe the type of psychedelic substance you used.
* **Time with Substance** (`time_with_substance`, `text`, `optional`): Describe the time you spent with the substance.
* **Time Without Substance** (`time_without_substance`, `text`, `optional`): Describe the time you spent without the substance.
* **Number of Experiences** (`number_of_experiences`, `number`, `optional`): Rate the number of experiences you've had with the substance.
* **Last Experienced** (`last_experienced`, `text`, `optional`): Describe your last experience with the substance.
* **Last Experience Duration** (`last_experience_duration`, `text`, `optional`): Describe the duration of your last experience with the substance.
* **Last Experience Intensity** (`last_experience_intensity`, `text`, `optional`): Describe the intensity of your last experience with the substance.
* **Last Time** (`last_time`, `text`, `optional`): Describe the last time you experienced the substance.
* **Last Time Interval** (`last_time_interval`, `text`, `optional`): Describe the time interval between your last experience and your current time.
* **Last Time Type** (`last_time_type`, `text`, `optional`): Describe the type of experience you had during your last use of the substance.
* **Frequency** (`frequency`, `text`, `optional`): Describe how often you use the substance.
* **Last Used Time** (`last_used_time`, `text`, `optional`): Describe the time you last used the substance.
