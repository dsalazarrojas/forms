# Daily Subconscious Tracker Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Subconscious Tracker Form is designed to collect data on a daily basis to help individuals track their habits, activities, and emotional state. This form is meant to provide a comprehensive overview of an individual's daily routine, allowing them to identify patterns and make informed decisions about their well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
2. Select all applicable options that apply to you.
3. Enter any additional information or comments in the text fields.
4. Complete each field carefully, as it will help you better understand your daily habits and activities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Morning Meditation** (`morning_meditation`, select_multiple, Required): Select all that apply from the options provided to indicate if you meditated in the morning.
* **Sleep Patterns** (`sleep_patters`, date, Required): Enter your sleep schedule for the day, including the time you went to bed and woke up.
* **Dreams** (`dreams`, text, Required): Describe any dreams or thoughts you had during the day.
* **Daily Goals** (`daily_goals`, text, Required): List any goals or tasks you accomplished during the day.
* **Emotional State** (`emotional_state`, text, Required): Describe your emotional state during the day, including any challenging emotions you experienced.
* **Actions** (`actions`, text, Required): List any activities you completed or tasks you accomplished during the day.
* **Behaviors** (`behaviors`, text, Required): Describe any behaviors you exhibited during the day.
* **Sleep Quality** (`sleep_quality`, number, Required): Rate your sleep quality from 1-5, with 1 being poor and 5 being excellent.
* **Wake Time** (`wake_time`, time, Required): Enter the time you woke up.
* **Relaxation Methods** (`relaxation_methods`, select_one, Required): Select at least one method you used to relax during the day.
* **Other Relaxation Methods** (`relaxation_methods_other`, text, Required): Describe any other methods you used to relax during the day.
* **Relaxation Time** (`relaxation_time`, number, Required): Enter the amount of time you spent relaxing.
* **Exercise Time** (`exercise_time`, number, Required): Enter the amount of time you spent exercising.
* **Exercise Frequency** (`exercise_frequency`, text, Required): Describe how often you exercised during the day.
* **Sleep Duration** (`sleep_duration`, number, Required): Enter the amount of time you slept.
* **Wake Duration** (`wake_duration`, number, Required): Enter the amount of time you were awake.
* **Focus Time** (`focus_time`, number, Required): Enter the amount of time you focused on your activities.
* **Self-Care** (`self_care`, select_multiple, Required): Select all that apply from the options provided to indicate if you practiced self-care.
* **Other Self-Care** (`self_care_other`, text, Required): Describe any other self-care activities you completed during the day.
* **Self-Care Activities** (`self_care_activities`, text, Required): List any self-care activities you completed during the day.
* **Self-Care Time** (`self_care_time`, number, Required): Enter the amount of time you spent on self-care activities.
* **Self-Care Frequency** (`self_care_frequency`, text, Required): Describe how often you practice self-care.
* **Self-Care Quality** (`self_care_quality`, text, Required): Describe the quality of your self-care activities.
* **Self-Care Note** (`self_care_note`, text, Required): Add any notes or comments about your self-care activities.

Note: Please answer truthfully and thoroughly, and remember that your responses will help you better understand your daily habits and activities.
