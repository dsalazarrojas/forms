# sleep_consultation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sleep consultation form is a tool used to collect information about a patient's sleep habits and provide guidance on improving their sleep quality, schedule, and disorders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your sleep quality in the "sleep_quality" field. This field is required.
2. Provide your sleep schedule in the hours you usually sleep in the "sleep_schedule" field. This field is required.
3. Select any sleep disorders you are experiencing from the dropdown list in the "sleep_disorders" field. You can select multiple disorders. This field is required.
4. Enter the number of hours you usually sleep in the "sleep_duration" field. This field is required.
5. Choose when you typically experience difficulty falling asleep from the "sleep_onset_latency" field. You can select from "morning", "afternoon", "evening", or "none" options.
6. Provide any additional advice or insights related to your sleep quality in the "sleep_quality_advice" field. This field is required.
7. Share any advice or suggestions you have on your sleep schedule in the "sleep_schedule_advice" field. This field is required.
8. Share any advice or suggestions you have on your sleep disorders in the "sleep_disorders_advice" field. This field is required.
9. Enter any advice or insights you have on your sleep duration in the "sleep_duration_advice" field. This field is required.
10. Enter any advice or insights you have related to your sleep onset latency in the "sleep_onset_latency_advice" field. This field is optional.
11. Add any additional comments or feedback you would like to share in the "sleep_consultation_form_comments" field. This field is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **sleep_quality** (text, required): Enter a description of your sleep quality.
* **sleep_schedule** (number, required): Enter the hours you usually sleep.
* **sleep_disorders** (select multiple, required): Select any sleep disorders you are experiencing.
* **sleep_duration** (number, required): Enter the number of hours you usually sleep.
* **sleep_onset_latency** (select one, required): Choose when you typically experience difficulty falling asleep.
* **sleep_quality_advice** (text, required): Provide any advice or insights related to your sleep quality.
* **sleep_schedule_advice** (text, required): Share any advice or suggestions you have on your sleep schedule.
* **sleep_disorders_advice** (text, required): Share any advice or suggestions you have on your sleep disorders.
* **sleep_duration_advice** (text, required): Enter any advice or insights you have on your sleep duration.
* **sleep_onset_latency_advice** (text, optional): Enter any advice or insights you have related to your sleep onset latency.
* **sleep_consultation_form_comments** (note, optional): Add any additional comments or feedback.
