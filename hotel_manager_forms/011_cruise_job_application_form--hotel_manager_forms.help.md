## Cruise Job Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Cruise Job Application Form is a comprehensive form designed to help us understand your qualifications, experience, and preferences in the cruise industry. Your responses will be used to assess your eligibility for available job positions and ensure that we find the best match for you.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill in your personal details**: Start by providing your First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone Number. Please note that your email and phone number will be used to contact you for further communication.
2. **Select your job preferences**: Choose the Job Type and Job Status that match your current situation. This will help us understand your availability and work style.
3. **Describe your experience**: Provide a detailed description of your Work Experience in the cruise industry.
4. **Submit your portfolio**: Upload your Resume and Portfolio to showcase your skills and qualifications.
5. **Reference verification**: Provide at least one Reference contact to help us verify your work experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, **Required**): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, **Required**): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, **Required**): Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, **Required**): Indicate the job title you are interested in, e.g., Cruise Director, Hotel Manager, etc.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, `text`, **Required**): Provide a brief description of the job you are applying for, and how you fit the requirements.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, `text`, **Required**): Describe your relevant work experience in the cruise industry, highlighting your achievements and qualifications.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, `select_one`, **Required**): Choose the job type that matches your desired role (Cruise Director, Hotel Manager, or Cruise Officer).
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select the job status that reflects your current situation (Full-time, Part-time, Freelance, or Intern).
* **Available Date** (`available_date`, `date`, **Optional**): If applicable, provide the date you are available to start working.
* **Available Time** (`available_time`, `time`, **Optional**): If applicable, provide the specific time you are available to start working.
* **Resume** (`resume`, `text`, **Required**): Upload your resume as a PDF or docx file to showcase your qualifications and experience.
* **Portfolio** (`portfolio`, `text`, **Optional**): Upload your portfolio or any relevant work samples that demonstrate your skills and qualifications.
* **Reference 1** (`reference1`, `text`, **Required**): Provide a contact name and email for your first reference.
* **Reference 2** (`reference2`, `text`, **Optional**): Provide a contact name and email for your second reference (if applicable).
* **Reference 3** (`reference3`, `text`, **Optional**): Provide a contact name and email for your third reference (if applicable).
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, `text`, **Required**): Click to submit your application for review.

Note: Fields marked as **Optional** are not required but are still an important part of your application. Ensure you fill in all required fields to ensure a smooth evaluation process.
