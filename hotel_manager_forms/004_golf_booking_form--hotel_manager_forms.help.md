# Golf Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Golf Booking Form is used to collect information from golfers for booking a tee time at the golf course. This form is designed to gather essential details for efficient and effective processing of booking requests.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the text field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the text field.
3. Provide your **Email** address in the correct format (e.g., `john.doe@example.com`).
4. Enter your **Contact Number** in the format `123-456-7890`.
5. Choose a specific **Date** for your booking in the date format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
6. Select a specific **Time** for your booking in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
7. Answer whether you have played at the **Golf Course** before or not.
8. Choose the **Reservation Type** you are interested in (e.g., single, group, etc.).
9. Enter the number of **Players** in your group.
10. Select the number of **Holes** you plan to play.
11. Leave any additional **Message** for the golf course staff.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as you would like it to be displayed on your booking confirmation.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as you would like it to be displayed on your booking confirmation.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication about your booking.
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication about your booking.
- **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select a specific date for your booking in the `YYYY-MM-DD` format.
- **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select a specific time for your booking in the `HH:MM AM/PM` format.
- **Golf Course** (`golf_course`, select one, optional): Choose whether you have played at the golf course before.
- **Reservation Type** (`reservation_type`, select multiple, optional): Choose the type of reservation you are interested in (e.g., single, group, etc.).
- **Number of Players** (`num_players`, number, optional): Enter the number of players in your group.
- **Number of Holes** (`num_holes`, number, optional): Select the number of holes you plan to play.
- **Message** (`message`, note, optional): Leave any additional message for the golf course staff.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure smooth processing of your booking.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, do not hesitate to include them in the **Message** field.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact the golf course staff.
* For a more efficient booking process, it is recommended to provide as much information as possible.
