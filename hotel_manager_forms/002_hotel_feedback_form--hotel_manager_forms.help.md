# Hotel Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from hotel guests, allowing us to understand their experiences and identify areas for improvement. Each guest's feedback is checked for duplication to prevent multiple submissions from the same person.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select whether you have feedback about your stay (hotel_feedback_form_1).
2. Enter your name (hotel_feedback_form_2).
3. Provide your email address (hotel_feedback_form_3).
4. Enter your phone number (hotel_feedback_form_4).
5. Share your feedback about your stay (hotel_feedback_form_5).
6. Rate your overall experience (hotel_feedback_form_6).
7. Optional: Choose a date of your stay (hotel_feedback_form_7).
8. Optional: Mention the time of your stay (hotel_feedback_form_8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Hotel Feedback Form 1: Get Feedback
 * **Hotel Feedback Form 1** (`hotel_feedback_form_1`, Select Multiple, Required): 
Please select all that apply to indicate if you have feedback about your stay.

### Guest Name
 * **Guest Name** (`hotel_feedback_form_2`, Text, Required): 
Please enter your name so we can address your feedback personally.

### Email
 * **Email** (`hotel_feedback_form_3`, Email, Required): 
Please enter a valid email address so we can get in touch with you about your feedback.

### Phone
 * **Phone** (`hotel_feedback_form_4`, Text, Required): 
Please enter your phone number, so we can reach out to you if needed.

### Feedback
 * **Feedback** (`hotel_feedback_form_5`, Note, Required): 
Please share your thoughts about your stay, including any positive or negative experiences you had.

### Rating
 * **Rating** (`hotel_feedback_form_6`, Number, Required): 
Rate your overall experience from 1 to 5.

### Date (Optional)
 * **Date** (`hotel_feedback_form_7`, Date, Optional): 
If you remember the date of your stay, please enter it here.

### Time (Optional)
 * **Time** (`hotel_feedback_form_8`, Time, Optional): 
If you remember the time of your stay, please enter it here.

### Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and thoroughly to help us improve our service.
* If you have any additional comments or questions, please don't hesitate to contact us directly.
* We appreciate your feedback and look forward to making your next stay with us.
