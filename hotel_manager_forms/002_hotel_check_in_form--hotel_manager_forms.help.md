# Hotel Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Check In Form is designed to collect information from guests checking into a hotel for an event or stay. The form is used to gather contact details, check-in/check-out dates, and any special requests or notes related to the guest's stay.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the respective fields.
2. Enter your **Email** address for communication purposes.
3. Provide your **Phone** number for easy contact.
4. Select the **Check In Date** when you expect to arrive at the hotel.
5. Specify the **Check Out Date** when you plan to depart from the hotel.
6. Add any **Client Note** about your stay, such as special requests or requirements.
7. Choose the **Assigned Hotel** where you will be staying from the available options (Hotel A, Hotel B, or Hotel C).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Check In Date** (date, required/optional): Select the date you expect to arrive at the hotel.
* **Check Out Date** (date, required/optional): Specify the date you plan to depart from the hotel.
* **Client Note** (note, required/optional): Add any special requests or notes about your stay.
* **Assigned Hotel** (select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the hotel where you will be staying from the available options (Hotel A, Hotel B, or Hotel C).
