# RFID Hotel Connection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and store guest information, including their RFID keycard details, for hotel management purposes. It's essential to fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure that guest access is managed correctly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the guest's first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter the guest's last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Select the guest's date of birth from the calendar in the "Date of Birth" field.
4. Enter the guest's email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter the guest's phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Select "True" or "False" in the "RFID Keycard" field, indicating whether the guest has an RFID keycard.
7. Enter any additional access details in the "Access Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter the guest's first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter the guest's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (date, required): Select the guest's date of birth from the calendar.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter the guest's email address.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter the guest's phone number.
* **RFID Keycard** (select_one, required): Select "True" if the guest has an RFID keycard, "False" otherwise.
* **Access Details** (note, required): Enter any additional access details, such as room assignments or access expiration dates.
