# Guest Incident Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document and track incidents within the hotel, ensuring accurate and efficient reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the date and time of the guest's arrival.
2. Select the date and time of the guest's departure.
3. Enter the date of the incident, which may be the same as the guest's departure date if the incident occurred during their stay.
4. Enter the time of the incident, if known.
5. Briefly describe the incident in the "Incident Summary" field.
6. Specify the location where the incident occurred.
7. Provide the name of the person reporting the incident.
8. Select the status of the incident from the available options (Active, Inactive, or Closed).
9. Identify the type of incident (Damage, Loss, or Other).
10. Describe the incident in detail, choosing from the available options (Damage to Room, Injury or Illness, Lost or Stolen Property, or Other).
11. Enter the room number where the incident occurred, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Arrival** (`guest_arrival`, date, required: false): Enter the date and time of the guest's arrival.
* **Guest Departure** (`guest_deparature`, date, required: false): Enter the date and time of the guest's departure.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the incident, which may be the same as the guest's departure date if the incident occurred during their stay.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the incident, if known.
* **Incident Summary** (`incident_summary`, text, required: false): Briefly describe the incident in a few sentences.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, required: false): Specify the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Reporter** (`incident_reporter`, text, required: false): Provide the name of the person reporting the incident.
* **Incident Status** (`incident_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the status of the incident from the available options (Active, Inactive, or Closed).
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Identify the type of incident (Damage, Loss, or Other).
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, select_multiple, required: false): Describe the incident in detail, choosing from the available options (Damage to Room, Injury or Illness, Lost or Stolen Property, or Other).
* **Room Number** (`room_number`, text, required: false): Enter the room number where the incident occurred, if applicable.

## Tips

* Be as descriptive as possible when completing the "Incident Description" field.
* Use the available options to ensure accurate and efficient reporting.
* If unsure about the incident status, select "Active" to indicate it's still being investigated.
* Double-check the room number if applicable, as it helps with incident tracking.

By following this guide, you'll be able to complete the Guest Incident Report Form accurately and efficiently, ensuring that all relevant details are captured for internal and external auditing purposes.
