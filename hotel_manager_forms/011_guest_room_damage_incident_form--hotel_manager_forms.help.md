## Guest Room Damage Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Guest Room Damage Incident Form is used to report incidents of guest room damage to the hotel management. This form helps the staff to investigate and take necessary actions to resolve the issue.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with as much detail as possible.
2. Select the "Submit" option to indicate whether the incident has been reported or not.
3. Attach any relevant images or documents to support your report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Incident** (Guest Room Damage Incident Form - Page 1):
	+ This is the first page of the form where you can enter a brief description of the incident.
* **Incident Date** (Incident Date):
	+ Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (Incident Time):
	+ Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Location** (Incident Location):
	+ Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Room Number** (Room Number):
	+ Enter the room number affected by the incident.
* **Damage Description** (Damage Description):
	+ Provide a detailed description of the damage.
* **Guest Name** (Guest Name):
	+ Enter the name of the guest who reported the incident.
* **Contact Number** (Contact Number):
	+ Enter a contact number for the guest.
* **Witness Details** (Witness Details):
	+ If there were any witnesses, enter their names and contact information.
* **Submit** (Submit):
	+ Select "Yes" if you have already reported the incident or "No" if not.
* **Upload Attachment** (Upload Attachment):
	+ If you have any supporting documents or images, attach them here.

Note: This form is only used to report non-trivial incidents. If the incident is minor, such as a small stain, do not fill out this form.
