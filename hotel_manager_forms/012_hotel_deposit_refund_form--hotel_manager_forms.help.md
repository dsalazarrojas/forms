# Hotel Deposit Refund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Deposit Refund Form is used to request a refund for the deposit paid during a hotel stay. This form provides an efficient way for hotel guests to communicate their request for a refund to hotel management or staff.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you have the required information ready and accurate.
2. Enter your hotel's name.
3. Fill in the amount you paid as deposit.
4. Enter the date you paid the deposit.
5. Specify the refund amount you are asking for.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Hotel Deposit Refund Form** (`hotel_deposit_refund_form`, text, required/optional: false): This is the title of the form, it does not need to be filled in.
- **Amount Paid** (`amount_paid`, number, required/optional: false): Enter the total amount you paid as deposit when you booked your stay. This field is a numeric input for the deposit amount.
- **Deposit Date** (`deposit_date`, date, required/optional: false): Enter the date you paid the deposit, this can be in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Refund Amount** (`refund_amount`, number, required/optional: false): Enter the amount you are requesting as a refund, this can be a partial or full refund of your deposit.
