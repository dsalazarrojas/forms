# Hotel Guest Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Guest Registration Form is used to collect and verify information from hotel guests. This information is used to confirm their reservation, verify their identity, and provide a comfortable stay.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your Guest Name in the field labeled "Guest Name".
2. Enter your Email Address in the field labeled "Email Address".
3. Provide your Phone Number in the field labeled "Phone Number".
4. Select your Check In Date from the calendar and enter it in the field labeled "Check In Date".
5. Select your Check Out Date from the calendar and enter it in the field labeled "Check Out Date".
6. Choose your Room Type from the options: Single, Double, Triple, or Suite.
7. Enter the number of guests in your party in the field labeled "Guest Count".
8. Provide any additional notes or comments in the field labeled "Reservation Note".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Name (text)** (`guest_name`, required/optional: false): Enter your full name, as it appears on your passport or ID.
* **Email Address (email)** (`email_address`, required/optional: false): Enter your official email address associated with your reservation.
* **Phone Number (text)** (`phone_number`, required/optional: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Check In Date (date)** (`check_in_date`, required/optional: false): Select your planned check-in date using the calendar.
* **Check Out Date (date)** (`check_out_date`, required/optional: false): Select your planned check-out date using the calendar.
* **Room Type (select one)** (`room_type`, required/optional: false): Choose your preferred room type from the available options.
* **Guest Count (number)** (`guest_count`, required/optional: false): Enter the number of guests in your party.
* **Reservation Note (note)** (`reservation_note`, required/optional: false): Provide any additional comments or notes about your stay.
