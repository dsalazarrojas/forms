# Accommodation Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect primary contact and group/event information for accommodation registration, which is essential for accommodation arrangements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your primary contact information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Enter the name of your company or organization.
3. Enter the name and other details of the event or group you are attending.
4. Specify the total number of guests attending.
5. Enter the check-in and check-out dates for your stay.
6. Select any special room requirements, such as meal plan, meeting spaces, and transportation needs.
7. If necessary, provide any special requirements or requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Primary Contact Full Name** (2, contact_name, `required`): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (3, email, `required`): Enter your professional email address.
* **Phone Number** (4, phone_number, `required`): Enter your mobile or office phone number.
* **Company or Organization Name** (5, organization, `required`): Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Event or Group Name** (7, event_name, `required`): Enter the name of the conference, meeting, or group you are attending.
* **Total Number of Guests** (8, total_guests, `required`): Enter the total number of guests attending.
* **Check-In Date** (9, check_in_date, `required`): Enter the date you will be arriving at the accommodation.
* **Check-Out Date** (10, check_out_date, `required`): Enter the date you will be departing from the accommodation.
* **Room Requirements** (11, section_room_requirements, `required`): Select any special room requirements you have.
* **Single Rooms Needed** (12, single_rooms_needed, `optional`): Select the number of single rooms you need.
* **Double Rooms Needed** (13, double_rooms_needed, `optional`): Select the number of double rooms you need.
* **Suite Rooms Needed** (14, suite_rooms_needed, `optional`): Select the number of suite rooms you need.
* **Accessible Rooms Needed** (15, accessible_rooms_needed, `optional`): Select the number of accessible rooms you need.
* **Room Preferences** (16, room_preferences, `optional`): Select all applicable room preferences.
* **Meal Plan Required** (18, meal_plan, `required`): Select your meal plan requirements.
* **Meeting or Conference Rooms** (19, meeting_rooms, `required`): Select whether you need meeting or conference rooms.
* **Airport or Station Transfers** (20, transportation, `optional`): Select whether you need airport or station transfers.
* **Special Requirements or Requests** (21, special_requirements, `optional`): Enter any special requirements or requests.
* **Budget Per Room Per Night** (22, budget_per_night, `optional`): Enter your budget per room per night.
