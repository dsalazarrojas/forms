# Vacation Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from customers on their recent vacation experiences. It is meant to help hotel management identify areas of improvement and ensure that guests feel welcome and satisfied with their stay.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer all the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter your trip details in the "Trip Details" section.
3. Indicate if you stayed in a hotel accommodation by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Accommodation Type" section.
4. If you stayed in a hotel, enter the accommodation name in the "Accommodation Name" section.
5. Rate your trip experience in the "Trip Rating" section by selecting one or more of the available options.
6. Share any additional comments or feedback about your trip in the "Trip Comments" section.
7. If you worked with a travel agent, please enter their name in the "Travel Agent" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trip Details** (`trip_details`, note, required: false): Enter a brief description of your recent trip, including the destination, duration, and any notable events or activities you participated in.
* **Accommodation Type** (`accommodation_type`, select_one, required: false): Indicate whether you stayed in a hotel accommodation by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Accommodation Name** (`accommodation_name`, text, required: false): If you selected "Yes" in the "Accommodation Type" section, enter the name of the hotel where you stayed.
* **Trip Rating** (`trip_rating`, select_multiple, required: false): Rate your trip experience by selecting one or more of the available options: Good, Fair, or other options not listed.
* **Trip Comments** (`trip_comments`, text, required: false): Share any additional comments or feedback about your trip experience. This is a great opportunity to suggest improvements or special recognition for staff members who provided excellent service.
* **Travel Agent** (`travel_agent`, text, required: false): If you worked with a travel agent, please enter their name and any feedback about their performance.

## Tips
* Please be as specific and detailed as possible when answering the questions.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvements, feel free to share them with us.
* If you would like to remain anonymous, you can do so by leaving your name and other identifying information out of the form.
