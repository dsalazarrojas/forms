# Spa Guest Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from guests who have visited the spa, helping us improve the services we provide.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your name in the **Guest Name** field.
2. Provide your stay dates in the **Stay Dates** field.
3. Rate your overall experience in the **Overall Experience** field, using the provided scale (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
4. Rate the quality of service in the **Service Quality** field, choosing from (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
5. Select all that apply for the **Cleanliness** field, indicating how clean you found the facilities (e.g., Very Clean, Somewhat Clean, Not Very Clean, Not Clean At All).
6. Rate the interaction with the staff in the **Staff Interaction** field, using the provided scale (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
7. Choose all that apply for the **Facilities** field, indicating how satisfactory you found the facilities (e.g., Very Satisfactory, Somewhat Satisfactory, Not Very Satisfactory, Not Satisfactory At All).
8. In the **Recommendations** field, provide any suggestions or ideas you have for the spa.
9. In the **Comments** field, share any additional comments or thoughts about your stay.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Name**: This field is used to collect your name for the evaluation.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
* **Stay Dates**: Please enter the dates of your stay at the spa.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
* **Overall Experience**: Rate your overall experience at the spa, using the provided scale.
	+ Type: number
	+ Required: no
* **Service Quality**: Rate the quality of service you received, using the provided scale.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: no
* **Cleanliness**: Select all that apply regarding the cleanliness of the facilities.
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Required: no
* **Staff Interaction**: Rate the interaction with the staff, using the provided scale.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: no
* **Facilities**: Choose all that apply regarding the facilities' satisfaction.
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Required: no
* **Recommendations**: Share any recommendations or suggestions you have for the spa.
	+ Type: note
	+ Required: no
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or thoughts about your stay.
	+ Type: note
	+ Required: no
