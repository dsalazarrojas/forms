# Resort Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Resort Registration Form is designed to collect information from resort guests for registration and administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form with your accurate information.
2. Double-check your email address to ensure it matches the confirmation selection.
3. Select your gender and date of birth accurately.
4. Add any additional notes or comments if necessary.
5. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest First Name** (`guest_first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Guest Last Name** (`guest_last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address.
* **Confirm Email** (`email_confirmation`, select multiple, optional): Confirm your email address by selecting the correct option.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (dd/mm/yyyy).
* **Gender** (`gender`, select one, optional): Select your correct gender.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or information you wish to provide to the resort staff.

**Tips:** 
- Please make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to the resort staff.
- The information collected will be used for resort operations and communication purposes.
