# Hotel Sales Lead Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hotel sales lead form is used to collect information from potential clients and provide a structured format for the sales team to follow up and tailor their approach to each lead's specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the client's name, contact information, and sales information in the respective fields.
2. Select the services you are interested in from the options provided.
3. Enter the lead date and time in the format 'MM/DD/YYYY' (e.g., 01/15/2024).
4. Enter any additional notes or comments in the 'Note' field.
5. Ensure you have filled out all required fields and submitted the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information (id: 1)**: Enter the client's name and other contact details here.
* **Contact Information (id: 2)**: Enter the client's contact name, phone number, and email address here.
* **Sales Information (id: 3)**: Enter the details of the sales discussion or meeting here.
* **Services (id: 4)**: Select the services you are offering the client (e.g., accommodation, food and beverage, etc.).
* **Lead Date (id: 5)**: Enter the date for the lead in 'MM/DD/YYYY' format (e.g., 01/15/2024).
* **Lead Time (id: 6)**: Enter the time for the lead in 'HH:MM' format (e.g., 14:30).
* **Note (id: 7)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the client here.
* **Email (id: 8)**: Enter the client's email address here.
* **Phone (id: 9)**: Enter the client's phone number here.

## Tips
* Ensure you have filled out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check the services selected to ensure they align with the client's needs.
* The lead date and time should be accurately entered to schedule follow-up appointments or meetings.
