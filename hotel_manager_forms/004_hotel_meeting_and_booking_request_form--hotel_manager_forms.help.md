# Hotel Meeting And Booking Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Meeting And Booking Request Form is used by hotel staff to request meetings and bookings at the hotel. This form gathers all the necessary information to help the hotel management plan and coordinate meetings and events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the event information, including the meeting date and end date.
2. Choose a meeting time that suits your needs.
3. Select the meeting location within the hotel or outside of it.
4. Indicate whether you need a meeting room.
5. Choose the type of event or meeting you are planning.
6. Provide a description of the event in the "Event Details" field.
7. List the attendees, if any.
8. Enter your contact information, including your first name, last name, email, phone number, company, department, and title.
9. Provide any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Information**: This is the first page of the form where you enter the basic information about your meeting or event.
* **Meeting Date**: Enter the date of your meeting, following the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **End Date**: Enter the date when your meeting or event will end.
* **Meeting Time**: Choose a time that suits your needs for the meeting.
* **Meeting Location**: Select where your meeting will take place, within the hotel or outside of it.
* **Hotel Name**: Indicate whether you need a meeting room or not.
* **Event Type**: Choose the type of event or meeting you are planning.
* **Event Details**: Provide a brief description of the event or meeting.
* **Attendees**: List the names and contact information of the attendees, if any.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your contact information, including your first name, last name, email, phone number, company, department, and title.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Company**: Enter your company name.
* **Department**: Enter your department name.
* **Title**: Indicate whether you have a title.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional notes or comments about your meeting or event.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Use standard date and time formats (YYYY-MM-DD and HH:MM AM/PM).
* If you need any additional assistance, don't hesitate to contact the hotel management.
* Review your form before submitting it to ensure all information is correct.
