# Guest House Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for guest house owners to register their property with our system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your **Email** address for contact purposes.
3. Provide your phone number in the **Phone** field.
4. Enter your **Property Name** for identification.
5. Enter your **Address**, **City**, **Province**, and **Postal Code** for geographical location.
6. Choose your **Timezone** (e.g., America/New York) from the dropdown list.
7. Select your **Language** (e.g., English, French, etc.) from the dropdown list.
8. Choose your **Currency** (e.g., Euro, USD, etc.) from the dropdown list.
9. If required, choose your **Timezone 2** and **Timezone Name** for additional geographical data.
10. Enter any additional information in the **Country Iso Code**, **Currency Rate**, **Timezone Offset**, and **Timezone Offset (2)** fields (not required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Property Name**: Enter the name of your guest house property.
* **Address**: Enter the address of your guest house location.
* **City**: Enter the city of your guest house location.
* **Province**: Enter the province of your guest house location.
* **Postal Code**: Enter the postal code of your guest house location.
* **Postal Code 2**: Enter the second postal code (not used in this guide).
* **Country**: Enter the country of your guest house location (not used in this guide).
* **Latitude**: Enter the latitude of your guest house location (not used in this guide).
* **Longitude**: Enter the longitude of your guest house location (not used in this guide).
* **Timezone**: Choose your timezone from the dropdown list (e.g., America/New York).
* **Timezone Offset**: Enter your timezone offset (not used in this guide).
* **Timezone 2**: Choose your second timezone (not used in this guide).
* **Language**: Select your language preference (e.g., English, French, etc.).
* **Currency**: Choose your currency (e.g., Euro, USD, etc.).
* **Currency Symbol**: Enter the currency symbol (not used in this guide).
* **Timezone Name**: Enter the name of your timezone (not used in this guide).
* **Country Iso Code**: Enter the country ISO code (not used in this guide).
* **Currency Rate**: Enter the currency rate (not used in this guide).
* **Timezone Offset (2)**: Enter the second timezone offset (not used in this guide).
* **Timezone Offset (3)**: Enter the third timezone offset (not used in this guide).
* **Timezone 2**: Choose your second timezone (e.g., America/Los Angeles).
* **Timezone 4**: Enter the fourth timezone offset (not used in this guide).
* **Timezone 5**: Enter the fifth timezone offset (not used in this guide).
