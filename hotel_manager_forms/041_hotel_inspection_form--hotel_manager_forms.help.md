# hotel_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hotel_inspection_form is designed to collect information about a hotel's facilities, services, and overall experience. This form is intended to help you assess the hotel's quality and identify areas for improvement. It consists of six sections, each focusing on a different aspect of the hotel.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information for each section. The form is split into the following sections:

- **Hotel Facilities**: Describe the condition and cleanliness of the hotel's facilities, such as the lobby, rooms, and amenities.
- **Services**: Provide feedback about the hotel's services, including the quality of the food, staff, and overall service.
- **Cleanliness**: Rate the cleanliness of the hotel, including the rooms, common areas, and facilities.
- **Overall Cleanliness**: Rate the overall cleanliness of the hotel, taking into account the overall ambiance and maintenance.
- **Employee Courtesy**: Describe the behavior and attitude of the hotel staff, including their helpfulness and friendliness.
- **Check In/Check Out**: Record the check-in and check-out times, if applicable.
- **Date**: Note the date of your visit.
