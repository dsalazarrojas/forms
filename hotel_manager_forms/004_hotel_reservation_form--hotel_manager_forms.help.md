# Hotel Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Reservation Form is a tool designed to collect and process essential information from guests about their stay at the hotel. It is meant to streamline the reservation process, ensuring that all necessary details are collected and stored correctly, replacing any manual or ad-hoc systems that may be in place.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Select a check-in date for your stay. This is where you will arrive at the hotel.
2.  Choose a check-in time for your arrival. Please note that this may be subject to change based on hotel availability.
3.  Pick a check-out date for your stay. This will be your departure date from the hotel.
4.  Select your preferred room type, which can be Single, Double, or Suite.
5.  Specify the number of guests accompanying you during your stay.
6.  Provide your contact number so we can get in touch with you about your reservation.
7.  Enter your email address for any communication regarding your stay.
8.  If you have any special messages or requests for your stay, you can include them in the reservation message box.
9.  Finally, select your preferred hotel from our available options: Hotel A, Hotel B, or Hotel C.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Check In Date** (`id: 1`, `type: date`, required: false): Please enter the date when you plan to arrive at the hotel.
*   **Check In Time** (`id: 2`, `type: time`, required: false): Select the time of your arrival. Please be aware that this might be subject to change based on hotel availability.
*   **Check Out Date** (`id: 3`, `type: date`, required: false): Enter the date when you plan to depart from the hotel.
*   **Room Type** (`id: 4`, `type: select_multiple`, required: false): Choose your preferred room type from Single, Double, or Suite.
*   **Number of Guests** (`id: 5`, `type: number`, required: false): Specify the number of guests you will be bringing with you during your stay.
*   **Contact Number** (`id: 6`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter your contact number so we can get in touch with you about your reservation.
*   **Email Address** (`id: 7`, `type: email`, required: false): Please enter your email address for any communication regarding your stay.
*   **Reservation Message** (`id: 8`, `type: note`, required: false): If you have any special messages or requests for your stay, please include them here.
*   **Hotel Name** (`id: 9`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Select your preferred hotel from our available options.
*   **Form Submission Confirmation** (`id: 10`, `type: number`, required: false): This field is not intended for user input and is likely a placeholder for the hotel's internal use.

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when providing dates and times for your stay to avoid confusion.
- If you have any special requests or messages for your stay, do not hesitate to include them in the reservation message field.
- Ensure your contact number and email address are accurate and up-to-date to ensure smooth communication.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to us directly.
