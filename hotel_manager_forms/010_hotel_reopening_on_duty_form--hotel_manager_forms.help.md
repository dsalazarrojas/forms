# Hotel Reopening On Duty Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Reopening On Duty Form is a crucial tool to gather essential information for a successful hotel reopening after a closure due to COVID-19. This form helps hotel management to track and document the current status of the hotel's reopening plan, ensuring a smooth and safe reopening process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review and describe the current status of the hotel's reopening plan in the "hotel_reopening_status" field.
2.  Indicate whether the hotel has completed safety training for all employees in the "safety_training_completed" field.
3.  Describe the safety measures in place to prevent the spread of COVID-19 in the "safety_measures" field.
4.  Estimate the number of guests expected to arrive during the reopening period in the "number_of_guests" field.
5.  Estimate the number of employees expected to be on site during the reopening period in the "number_of_employees" field.
6.  Estimate the duration of the reopening time frame in the "reopen_time_frame_duration" field.
7.  Describe the goals of the safety measures in place in the "goals_of_safety_measures" field.
8.  Select whether any safety equipment or tools need to be purchased or updated in the "safety_equipment" field.
9.  Describe any specific safety protocols that need to be followed during the reopening period in the "safety_protocols" field.
10.  Estimate the number of guestrooms available during the reopening period in the "number_of_guestrooms" field.
11.  Describe the employee testing results in the "employee_testing_results" field.
12.  Provide the reopening date in the "reopen_date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### hotel_reopening_status (Page 1)
*   **Current Reopening Status:** `text` (Optional)
    *   Describe the current status of the hotel's reopening plan.
