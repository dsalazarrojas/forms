# Guest Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Guest Registration Form is used by hotel staff to collect information from arriving guests, ensuring accurate records and a smooth check-in process.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please ensure you have all necessary information ready, as some fields are required. Follow the steps below to fill out the form accurately:
1.  Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2.  Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field, using a valid format.
4.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5.  Choose the date you will check in with the "Checkin Date" field.
6.  Choose the date you will check out with the "Checkout Date" field.
7.  Select your country of origin from the "Country" dropdown.
8.  Select the type(s) of room you will be occupying from the "Room Type" dropdown.
9.  If applicable, enter any assigned tools or equipment in the "Assigned Tool" field.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required/optional): A field to input your first name, a personal identification.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required/optional): A field to input your last name, part of your personal identification.
* **Email** (Email, email, required/optional): Enter your email address in a valid format, for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number, including country code, for contact purposes.
* **Checkin Date** (Checkin Date, date, required/optional): Choose the date you will arrive at the hotel, for planning and preparation.
* **Checkout Date** (Checkout Date, date, required/optional): Choose the date you will depart from the hotel, for planning and preparation.
* **Country** (Country, select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether you have a connection to the country you are visiting, which may affect your stay or services available.
* **Room Type** (Room Type, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the type(s) of room you will be occupying, which may affect your stay or services available.
* **Assigned Tool** (Assigned Tool, text, required/optional): If assigned, enter any tools or equipment you have been given for your stay, for inventory management.
