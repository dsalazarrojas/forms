# Night Audit Review Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Night Audit Review Checklist is a form used to review and document the night audit process. It helps hotel managers and staff to ensure that all necessary steps are taken during the night audit, and that any discrepancies or issues are addressed and resolved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Night Audit Review Checklist, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Select the correct audit type from the options provided.
3. Choose the current status of the audit from the options provided.
4. Add any additional comments or notes in the employee notes and manager notes fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Audit Date** (`audit_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the night audit review.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the employee who completed the night audit review.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter the department responsible for the night audit review.
* **Audit Type** (`audit_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of audit that was performed (Night Audit, Daily Audit, or Monthly Audit).
* **Audit Status** (`audit_status`, select_one, required): Choose the current status of the audit (Not Started, In Progress, Completed, or Cancelled).
* **Completed By** (`completed_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the employee who completed the night audit review (if different from the employee who filled out the form).
* **Review Completed At** (`review_completed_at`, date, optional): Enter the date and time the night audit review was completed.
* **Employee Notes** (`employee_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes from the employee who completed the night audit review.
* **Manager Notes** (`manager_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes from the hotel manager or supervisor.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Use clear and concise language in the employee and manager notes fields.
* Save and submit the form once complete to ensure that all necessary steps are taken during the night audit review process.
