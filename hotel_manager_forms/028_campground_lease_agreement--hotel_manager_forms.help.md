# Campground Lease Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a comprehensive leasing agreement for a campground, capturing essential information from both the lessor and lessee. It ensures both parties understand and agree to the terms and conditions of the lease.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your details as a lessor:
	* Lessor Name or Company: Enter the name of your campground or company.
	* Email Address: Enter your email address.
	* Phone Number: Enter your phone number.
	* Business Address: Enter your business address.
2. Fill in your details as a lessee:
	* Lessee Name or Organization: Enter your name or the name of the organization renting the campsite.
	* Primary Contact Person: Enter the name of the primary contact person for the lessee.
	* Email Address: Enter your email address.
	* Phone Number: Enter your phone number.
	* Street Address: Enter the address of the lessee's campsite or location.
3. Fill in the lease details:
	* Lease Start Date: Select the start date of the lease.
	* Lease End Date: Select the end date of the lease.
	* Lease Duration (months): Enter the number of months for the lease.
	* Campground Location: Enter the address of the leased campground.
	* Number of Camping Sites: Enter the number of camping sites leased.
4. Fill in the financial details:
	* Monthly Lease Payment Amount: Enter the monthly lease payment amount.
	* Payment Due Date Each Month: Enter the payment due date for each month.
	* Security Deposit Amount: Enter the security deposit amount.
5. Confirm your agreement:
	* Maximum Group Size Allowed: Enter the maximum number of people allowed in the group.
	* Lessee agrees to all terms and conditions: Confirm your agreement to all terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Lessor Name or Company** (`lessor_name`, text, required): The name of the campground or company leasing the site.
* **Email Address** (`lessor_email`, email, required): The email address of the lessor.
* **Phone Number** (`lessor_phone`, text, required): The phone number of the lessor.
* **Business Address** (`lessor_address`, text, required): The business address of the lessor.
* **Lessee Name or Organization** (`lessee_name`, text, required): The name or name of the organization renting the campsite.
* **Primary Contact Person** (`lessee_contact_person`, text, required): The name of the primary contact person for the lessee.
* **Email Address** (`lessee_email`, email, required): The email address of the lessee.
* **Phone Number** (`lessee_phone`, text, required): The phone number of the lessee.
* **Street Address** (`lessee_address`, text, required): The address of the lessee's campsite or location.
* **Lease Start Date** (`lease_start_date`, date, required): The start date of the lease.
* **Lease End Date** (`lease_end_date`, date, required): The end date of the lease.
* **Lease Duration (months)** (`lease_duration_months`, number, required): The number of months for the lease.
* **Campground Location** (`campground_location`, text, required): The address of the leased campground.
* **Number of Camping Sites** (`number_of_sites`, number, required): The number of camping sites leased.
* **Monthly Lease Payment Amount** (`monthly_lease_payment`, number, required): The monthly lease payment amount.
* **Payment Due Date Each Month** (`payment_due_date`, number, required): The payment due date for each month.
* **Security Deposit Amount** (`deposit_required`, number, required): The security deposit amount.
* **Maximum Group Size Allowed** (`allowed_group_size`, number, required): The maximum number of people allowed in the group.
* **Lessee agrees to all terms and conditions** (`terms_acceptance`, select_one, required): Confirm your agreement to all terms and conditions.
