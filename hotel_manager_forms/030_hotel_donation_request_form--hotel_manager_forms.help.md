# Hotel Donation Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hotel Donation Request Form is a tool for hotel employees to submit a request for donations or contributions from guests, partners, or other stakeholders. This form is used to document and communicate the specific needs of the hotel, allowing the relevant parties to provide support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Name, ensuring it is accurate and up-to-date.
2. Enter your Email, a valid contact address for communication.
3. Provide your Phone number, so the relevant party can contact you for further discussion.
4. Describe the purpose and details of the donation or contribution you are requesting, in the Description field.
5. Specify the Amount of money or resources you are requesting.
6. Choose a Date and Time when the donation or contribution is needed.
7. Review your information and submit the form to send a request to the relevant parties.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donation Request Form** (`donation_request_form`): This field is the primary description of your request for donation or contribution. Enter a brief summary of the purpose and requirements of your request.
* **Name** (`name`): Your full name or a name that you would like to be associated with the request.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter a valid email address where the relevant parties can contact you for further discussion or information.
* **Phone** (`phone`): A phone number where you can be reached for additional questions or clarifications.
* **Description** (`description`): Provide a detailed description of the donation or contribution you are requesting. This field should explain why the donation or contribution is necessary and what it will be used for.
* **Amount** (`amount`): Specify the amount of money or resources you are requesting.
* **Date Needed** (`date_needed`): Choose a date when the donation or contribution is needed.
* **Time Needed** (`time_needed`): Select a time of day when the donation or contribution is required.

## Tips
* Ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* Review your form carefully before submitting it to avoid any errors or misunderstandings.
* Be clear and specific when describing your request, to ensure that the relevant parties understand your needs.
* If you need any further assistance or clarification, do not hesitate to contact the relevant parties directly.
