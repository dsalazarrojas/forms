# villa_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The villa booking form is used to create, manage, and book villas. It allows owners to add and update villa information, guests to make reservations, and internal teams to assign tasks and track progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
4. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
5. Select a date for the **Date** field.
6. Select a time for the **Time** field.
7. Enter a note or comment in the **Note** field.
8. Enter the name of the villa in the **Villa Name** field.
9. Enter the address of the villa in the **Villa Address** field.
10. Select a check-in date in the **Check In** field.
11. Select a check-out date in the **Check Out** field.
12. Select one option from the **Select One** dropdown menu.
13. Select multiple options from the **Select Multiple** dropdown menu.
14. Enter the category of the villa in the **Category** field.
15. Enter a description of the villa in the **Description** field.
16. Select a form category for the villa in the **Form Category** field.
17. Enter the name of the tool assigned to the villa in the **Assigned Tool** field.
18. Enter the name of the team assigned to the villa in the **Assigned Team** field.
19. Select a date for the assigned task in the **Assigned Date** field.
20. Select a time for the assigned task in the **Assigned Time** field.
21. Enter a note or comment for the assigned task in the **Assigned Note** field.
22. Enter the email of the person assigned to the villa in the **Assigned Email** field.
23. Enter the phone number of the person assigned to the villa in the **Assigned Phone** field.
24. Enter the name of the person who submitted the form in the **Submitted By** field.
25. Select a time for when the form was submitted in the **Submitted Time** field.
