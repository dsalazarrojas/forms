# Hotel Daily Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Daily Report Form is a tool for hotel managers to document their daily activities, hours worked, and notable situations that occurred during their shift. This form helps ensure that all relevant information is captured and recorded for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the start time of your shift.
2. Enter the end time of your shift.
3. Record the total hours you worked during your shift.
4. Provide a detailed activity summary, highlighting the key events and accomplishments of your shift.
5. Report any important situations that occurred during your shift, selecting the relevant options from the list provided.
6. If any other notable situations or events occurred, please describe them in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time you began your shift.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time you finished your shift.
* **Hours Worked** (`hours_worked`, `number`, required): Enter the total number of hours you worked during your shift.
* **Shift Details** (`shift_details`, `text`, required): Provide a brief description of your shift, including any notable events or challenges.
* **Important Situations** (`important_situations`, `select_multiple`, required): Select from the list of options provided to indicate the importance of situations that occurred during your shift.
* **Activity Summary** (`activity_summary`, `text`, required): Provide a detailed summary of your activities and accomplishments during the shift.
* **Situation 1-10** (`situation_1`-`situation_11`, `text`, optional): If any other notable situations or events occurred, please describe them in the space provided.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your shift.
* **Start Time Hours** (`start_time_hours`, `text`, optional): Enter the hour of your start time.
* **Start Time Minutes** (`start_time_minutes`, `number`, optional): Enter the minute of your start time.
* **End Time Hours** (`end_time_hours`, `text`, optional): Enter the hour of your end time.
* **End Time Minutes** (`end_time_minutes`, `number`, optional): Enter the minute of your end time.
* **Total Hours** (`total_hours`, `number`, optional): Enter the total number of hours you worked during your shift.
* **Start Time 24** (`start_time_24`, `text`, optional): Enter your start time in 24-hour format.
* **End Time 24** (`end_time_24`, `text`, optional): Enter your end time in 24-hour format.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- Use the provided options for "Important Situations" to accurately reflect the level of importance.
- If you have any additional comments or notes, please use the "Notes" field.
- If you need to report any other notable situations, please use the designated fields.
