# Vacation Rental Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a vacation rental booking or inquire about available rental options. Please fill it out to provide us with necessary information about your rental needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as prompted. You will need to provide the following information:

*   **Room Type**: Select the type of room you are interested in (e.g., Single Room, Double Room, Suite).
*   **Rental Location**: Choose the city where you would like to rent a property (e.g., New York, Los Angeles, Chicago).
*   **Checkin Date**: Enter the date you plan to check-in for your rental.
*   **Checkout Date**: Enter the date you plan to check-out for your rental.
*   **Customer Name**: Optionally, provide your name for identification and contact purposes.
*   **Email**: Enter your email address so we can communicate with you about your rental request.
*   **Phone**: Optionally, provide your phone number for further contact.
*   **Message**: Any additional comments or requests you may have about your rental.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Rental Request**: This is the main section of the form where you will find most fields. Please fill out each field as per the instructions above.
*   **Customer Name**: A field to optionally provide your name for identification and contact purposes.
*   **Checkin Date**: A date field where you will enter your planned check-in date for the rental.
*   **Checkout Date**: A date field where you will enter your planned check-out date for the rental.
*   **Room Type**: A select field where you will choose the type of room you are interested in (e.g., Single Room, Double Room, Suite).
*   **Room Quantity**: A number field where you will specify how many rooms you are interested in renting.
*   **Email**: An email field where you can enter your contact email address.
*   **Phone**: A text field where you can optionally provide your contact phone number.
*   **Message**: A note field for any additional comments or requests you may have about the rental.

## Tips
*   Be as specific as possible when filling out the room type and rental location fields to ensure we can provide you with accurate information on available rentals.
*   Use a real email address to ensure we can get back to you with updates on your rental request.
*   If you have any special requests, please mention them in the message field for our attention.
*   We will get in touch with you regarding your rental request as soon as possible after receiving this form.
