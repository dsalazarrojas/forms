# Front Desk Receptionist Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Front Desk Receptionist Evaluation Form is used to assess the performance of front desk receptionists. It's an essential tool for hotel management to evaluate individual characteristics and tasks performed by receptionists during a specified period.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form effectively, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name accurately.
2. Enter your Job Title as it appears in the hotel's organizational chart.
3. Select the Evaluation Date and Evaluation Period as per the given period.
4. Enter the Performance Rating, Skills Rating, and Effectiveness Rating based on the receptionist's performance during the evaluation period.
5. Include any relevant Comments regarding your observations.
6. Write down your Evaluation Comments to support your ratings and comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required/optional): Enter your job title as per the hotel's organizational chart.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the evaluation in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Evaluation Period** (`evaluation_period`, time, required/optional): Select a time period for the evaluation (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Night).
* **Performance Rating** (`performance_rating`, number, required/optional): Rate the receptionist's performance on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Skills Rating** (`skills_rating`, number, required/optional): Rate the receptionist's skills on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Effectiveness Rating** (`effectiveness_rating`, number, required/optional): Rate the receptionist's effectiveness on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Include any comments or observations regarding the receptionist's performance.
* **Evaluation Comments** (`evaluation_comments`, text, required/optional): Write a supporting evaluation comment based on the ratings and observations.

## Tips

* Be honest and objective when evaluating the receptionist's performance.
* Use the ratings and comments to support your observations and evaluation.
* Ensure that the form is completed for each receptionist being evaluated.
