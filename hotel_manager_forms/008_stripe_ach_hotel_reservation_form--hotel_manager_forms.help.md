# Stripe ACH Hotel Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stripe ACH Hotel Reservation Form is a simple form designed to collect essential information about a hotel reservation for payment processing via the ACH network.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the basic reservation information in the **Base Info** section, which includes guest details such as name, email, and phone number.
2. Select the room rate type from the **Room Rate** dropdown menu, which is essential for accurate payment processing.
3. Enter any additional room information in the **Room Info** field if necessary.
4. Fill in the **Card Holder** field with the card holder's name if different from the guest's name.
5. Enter the credit card details, including the card number, expiry date, and any other required information in the subsequent fields.
6. Leave the **Base Info** fields at the end empty, as they are redundant and not required for payment processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Base Info (Text)**: A text field for entering basic reservation information. 
  - Enter the guest's basic contact information (name, email, phone number).
  - Note: This field is not required for payment processing.
- **Guest Info (Text)**: Another text field for additional guest information. 
  - Enter any additional guest details as needed.
  - Note: This field is not required for payment processing.
- **Guest Name (Text)**: Enter the guest's name. 
  - This is a required field for identification purposes.
- **Guest Email (Email)**: Enter the guest's email address. 
  - This field is not required but helps with communication.
- **Guest Phone (Text)**: Enter the guest's phone number. 
  - This field is not required but helps with communication.
- **Guest Date of Arrival (Date)**: Enter the date the guest is arriving. 
  - This field is not required but helps with scheduling.
- **Guest Date of Departure (Date)**: Enter the date the guest is departing. 
  - This field is not required but helps with scheduling.
- **Room Rate (Select One)**: Select the room rate type (Single, Double, or Suite). 
  - This is a required field for accurate payment processing.
- **Room Rate (Select Multiple)**: Another select field for room rate type. 
  - This field is redundant and not required for payment processing.
- **Room Info (Text)**: Enter any additional room information if necessary. 
  - This field is not required but helps with room description.
- **Card Holder (Text)**: Enter the card holder's name if different from the guest's name. 
  - This field is not required but helps with identification.
- **Card Number (Text)**: Enter the credit card number. 
  - This field is required for payment processing.
- **Card Expiry (Text)**: Enter the credit card expiry date. 
  - This field is required for payment processing.
- **Base Info (Note)**: A redundant field for note-taking purposes. 
  - This field is not required and can be left empty.
- **Base Info (Note)**: Another redundant field for note-taking purposes. 
  - This field is not required and can be left empty.
- **Base Info (Note)**: A third redundant field for note-taking purposes. 
  - This field is not required and can be left empty.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly for accurate payment processing.
- If you are unsure about any field, refer to the form's label for guidance.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the hotel management team for assistance.
