# Hotel Reservation Form With Skrill - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from customers for hotel room reservations while utilizing Skrill as the payment method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your first and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter the date you would like to book your room in the "Date" field.
5. Select the time of your visit in the "Time" field.
6. Add any additional notes in the "Note" field if necessary.
7. Enter your Skrill ID in the "Skrill ID" field.
8. Choose the date of your booking from the "Booking Date" field.
9. Choose the date you will be checking out from the "Check Out Date" field.
10. Select the type of room you would like to book from the "Room Type" field.
11. Indicate the number of rooms you would like to book in the "Room Count" field.
12. Enter the price of the room in the "Room Price" field.
13. Select the payment method you would like to use from the "Payment Method" field.
14. Enter your credit card number in the "Card Number" field.
15. Enter the CVV of your credit card in the "CVV" field.
16. Enter the expiration date of your credit card in the "Exp. Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name here. This is a required field.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name here. This is a required field.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address here. This will be used for communication and booking confirmation.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number here. This will be used for further communication and contact.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date you would like to book your room.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Select the time of your visit.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments here if necessary.
* **Skrill ID** (`skrill_id`, text, required): Enter your Skrill ID here.
* **Booking Date** (`booking_date`, date, required): Choose the date of your booking.
* **Check Out Date** (`checkout_date`, date, required): Choose the date you will be checking out.
* **Room Type** (`room_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of room you would like to book.
* **Room Count** (`room_count`, number, required): Indicate the number of rooms you would like to book.
* **Room Price** (`room_price`, number, required): Enter the price of the room.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, required): Select the payment method you would like to use.
* **Card Number** (`card_number`, text, required): Enter your credit card number here.
* **CVV** (`cvv`, text, required): Enter the CVV of your credit card here.
* **Exp. Date** (`exp_date`, date, required): Enter the expiration date of your credit card here.
