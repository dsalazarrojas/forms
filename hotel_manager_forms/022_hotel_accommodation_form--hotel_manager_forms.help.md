# Hotel Accommodation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Accommodation Form is designed to gather information from guests to provide a comfortable and tailored stay experience. It is a crucial tool for the hotel's management team to process requests for accommodation, manage room preferences, and ensure a smooth stay for guests.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  To start, fill out the guest information fields with your correct details. This includes your email and phone number, which will be used to contact you about your booking.
2.  Select the correct room type and accommodation type that suits your requirements.
3.  Provide your check-in and check-out dates to ensure the hotel staff can prepare your room accordingly.
4.  Indicate your room rate preference, if any.
5.  If you have any special requests, such as extra bedding or amenities, please list them in the special requests field.
6.  Finally, add any additional information you feel is necessary for the hotel staff to know about your stay.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Guest Info** (`<name>`, `email`, `phone`): This section is used to collect the guest's name, email, and phone number. Please ensure these details are accurate and up-to-date.
*   **Room Type** (`<name>`, `select_one`): This field lets you choose between single, double, or suite room types to accommodate your preferences.
*   **Room Preferences** (`<name>`, `text`): If you have any specific room preferences, such as extra bedding or specific amenities, use this field to list them.
*   **Check-in Date** (`<name>`, `date`): Provide the date you intend to check-in to the hotel.
*   **Check-out Date** (`<name>`, `date`): Provide the date you plan to check-out from the hotel.
*   **Room Rate** (`<name>`, `number`): If you have a specific room rate in mind, enter it in this field.
*   **Special Requests** (`<name>`, `text`): If you have any special requests, such as extra towels or late check-out, write them down here.
*   **Email** (`<name>`, `email`): Enter your email address so the hotel can contact you about your booking.
*   **Phone** (`<name>`, `text`): Provide your phone number in case the hotel needs to reach you urgently.
*   **Additional Info** (`<name>`, `text`): This field is for any additional information you feel is necessary for the hotel staff to know about your stay.
*   **Accommodation Type** (`<name>`, `select_one`): Select single, double, or suite as your accommodation type preference.
*   **Accommodation Preferences** (`<name>`, `select_multiple`): Indicate if you have any specific accommodation preferences, such as smoking or non-smoking.

Note: Please fill out this form carefully and accurately. If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to the hotel staff for assistance.
