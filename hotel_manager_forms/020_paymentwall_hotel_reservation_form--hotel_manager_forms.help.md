# paymentwall hotel reservation form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The paymentwall hotel reservation form is used to collect information for hotel reservations, including checkin and checkout dates, room type, number of guests, and payment method. This form helps to provide a convenient way to book a hotel room with the desired amenities and payment options.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (check the form for required fields as they are marked with an asterisk \*).
2. Enter your checkin date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Enter your checkout date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Select your preferred payment method from the options provided.
5. Choose your room type and number of guests.
6. Enter your contact phone number.
7. Enter the hotel name.
8. Select the reservation status.
9. Enter any additional information about your stay.
10. Enter your email address.
11. Enter the hotel location.
12. Enter the hotel address.
13. Enter the hotel phone number.
14. Enter the hotel phone extension.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Hotel Reservation** (`paymentwall_hotel_reservation_form_1`, text, false): A text field to enter the hotel reservation details.
* **Checkin Date** (`paymentwall_hotel_reservation_form_2`, date, false): Enter your checkin date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Checkout Date** (`paymentwall_hotel_reservation_form_3`, date, false): Enter your checkout date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Phone Number** (`paymentwall_hotel_reservation_form_4`, text, false): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Payment Method** (`paymentwall_hotel_reservation_form_5`, select_one, false): Select your preferred payment method from the options provided.
	+ Options: Credit Card, Pay Pal, Bank Transfer
* **Room Type** (`paymentwall_hotel_reservation_form_6`, select_one, false): Choose your room type from the options provided.
	+ Options: Single, Double, Suite
* **Number of Guests** (`paymentwall_hotel_reservation_form_7`, number, false): Enter the number of guests.
* **Hotel Name** (`paymentwall_hotel_reservation_form_8`, text, false): Enter the hotel name.
* **Reservation Status** (`paymentwall_hotel_reservation_form_9`, select_one, false): Select the reservation status from the options provided.
	+ Options: Available, Not Available
* **Additional Info** (`paymentwall_hotel_reservation_form_10`, note, false): Enter any additional information about your stay.
* **Email** (`paymentwall_hotel_reservation_form_11`, email, false): Enter your email address.
* **Hotel Location** (`paymentwall_hotel_reservation_form_12`, text, false): Enter the hotel location.
* **Hotel Address** (`paymentwall_hotel_reservation_form_13`, text, false): Enter the hotel address.
* **Hotel Phone Number** (`paymentwall_hotel_reservation_form_14`, text, false): Enter the hotel phone number.
* **Hotel Phone Extension** (`paymentwall_hotel_reservation_form_15`, text, false): Enter the hotel phone extension.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* If you encounter any issues while filling the form, please contact our support team for assistance.
