# Swimming Pool Rental Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Swimming Pool Rental Form is designed to collect information from customers interested in renting a swimming pool for a specific period. This form helps pool owners or managers to gather essential details from customers, such as contact information, booking details, and payment information, which aids in efficient and secure pool rental management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide your contact information**: Fill in the customer_info field with your name, email, and phone number so that we can get in touch with you regarding your booking.
2. **Enter booking details**: In the booking_details field, specify the date and duration of your rental period. Be sure to include the type of swimming pool you're interested in renting (e.g., inflatable, above-ground, or in-ground).
3. **Share payment information**: In the payment_details field, provide the payment method you'd like to use (e.g., credit card, PayPal, or check).
4. **Add any additional information**: If you have any special requests or requirements, use the additional_info field to share them with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Customer Information (customer_info)**
* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name, email, and phone number to get in touch with you regarding your booking.

### **Booking Details (booking_details)**
* **Booking Details** (`booking_details`, `text`, required: false): Specify the date and duration of your rental period. Please include the type of swimming pool you're interested in renting.

### **Payment Information (payment_info)**
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your payment method (e.g., credit card, PayPal, or check).

### **Additional Information (additional_info)**
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, `text`, required: false): Share any special requests or requirements you may have for your rental.

## Tips
- Be sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us through the email or phone number provided in the customer_info field.
- We'll review your application and get back to you within 24-48 hours to confirm your booking.
