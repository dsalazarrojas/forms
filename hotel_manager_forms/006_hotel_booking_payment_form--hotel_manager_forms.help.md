# hotel_booking_payment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from guests booking a hotel room, including personal details, room preferences, and payment methods.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your personal details.
2. Select your room type and room number.
3. Enter your email address and phone number.
4. Choose your payment method(s).
5. Add any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required: false): Enter your first name as it is written on your official identification.
* **Last Name** (text, required: false): Enter your last name as it is written on your official identification.
* **Date Of Arrival** (date, required: false): Enter the date you plan to arrive at the hotel.
* **Date Of Departure** (date, required: false): Enter the date you plan to depart from the hotel.
* **Room Type** (select_one, required: false): Select the type of room you prefer (e.g. single, double, suite).
* **Room Number** (number, required: false): Enter the room number you would like to book (if available).
* **Room Price** (number, required: false): Enter the price you are willing to pay for the room.
* **Payment Method** (select_multiple, required: false): Choose the payment method(s) you would like to use (e.g. credit card, bank transfer).
* **Email** (email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Note** (note, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments about your booking.
* **Form Submission ID** (number, required: false): This is an internal field used for administrative purposes and should not be filled out by guests. 

Note: The form submission ID is not intended to be filled out by guests.
