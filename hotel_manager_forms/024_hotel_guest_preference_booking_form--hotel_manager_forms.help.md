# Hotel Guest Preference Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect preferences and information from hotel guests. It will be used to tailor their stay experience, ensuring that their needs are met and they can feel at home during their visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the following fields:

1. Enter your full name in the **name** field.
2. Provide your email address in the **email** field. This will be used to communicate with you about your stay.
3. Enter your phone number in the **phone** field. If you need to contact you urgently, this is how we will reach you.
4. Choose whether you have any **room_type** preferences, such as extra bedding or specific room features.
5. Select your **smoking preference**, which will help us ensure your comfort during your stay.
6. Choose your **bed_type** preference, such as extra pillows or specific bedding types.
7. If you have any **special_request**, please enter your request in the free-form text field below.
8. If you have any additional **additional_information**, please enter it in the free-form text field below.
9. Select any dietary **diet** requirements, such as vegan or gluten-free.
10. Choose any **restrictions** you have during your stay, such as mobility issues or allergies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name**: Enter your full name to associate with your booking.
* **email**: Enter your email address to receive communication about your stay.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number for urgent contact.
* **room_type**: Choose whether you have any room type preferences, such as extra bedding or specific room features.
* **smoking_preference**: Select whether you prefer a smoking or non-smoking room.
* **bed_type**: Choose your bed type preference, such as extra pillows or specific bedding types.
* **special_request**: Enter any special requests you may have, such as extra towels or a room change.
* **additional_information**: Enter any additional information you think is relevant to your stay, such as medical conditions or special circumstances.
* **diet**: Select any dietary requirements, such as vegan or gluten-free.
* **restrictions**: Choose any restrictions you have during your stay, such as mobility issues or allergies.
* **other**: Enter any other information you feel is necessary to share with us about your stay.

## Tips
- Please be as specific as possible with your requests and preferences.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
- We will do our best to accommodate your requests and preferences, but please note that some requests may not be possible due to hotel limitations.
