# Concierge Weekly Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate your knowledge and performance in the following areas:

* Customer service scenarios
* Local attractions knowledge
* Safety protocols
* Ability to provide examples of handling guest requests
* Identify areas where you'd like to improve through additional training

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your honest answers, considering the provided options for each question.
2. For each field, select the option that best aligns with your current knowledge or approach.
3. Provide clear and concise answers when prompted for text fields.
