# Pet Boarding Waiver And Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Boarding Waiver And Consent Form is used to obtain a waiver and consent from pet owners before boarding their pets. This form ensures that the pet owner's wishes and expectations are met and protects both the pet and the boarding facility.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Make sure to fill in all the required fields.
3. Review the form before submitting it to ensure that all the information is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Boarding Waiver** (`pet_boarding_waiver_and_consent_form`): This is the main section of the form where you will provide your consent for your pet to be boarded.
* **Pet Boarding Terms** (`pet_boarding_terms`): This section asks if you have read and understand the boarding terms. Please answer "Yes" if you have read and understood the terms.
* **Pet Boarding Owner Details** (`pet_boarding_owner_details`): This section is used to provide information about yourself as the owner of the pet. Please select "Yes" if you are the owner.
* **Consent** (`pet_boarding_consent`): This field is for you to provide your consent for your pet to be boarded. Please provide a brief explanation of your consent.
* **Boarding Dates** (`pet_boarding_boarding_dates`): This field is for you to provide the dates for which your pet will be boarded.
* **Boarding Type** (`pet_boarding_boarding_type`): This field is to indicate if you prefer a specific type of boarding facility, such as a dog park or a cat sanctuary.
* **Boarding Details** (`pet_boarding_boarding_details`): This field is for you to provide any additional information about your pet's boarding needs.
* **Owner Signature** (`pet_boarding_owner_signature`): This field is for you to provide your signature, acknowledging that you have read and understand the form.
* **Manager Sign Date** (`pet_boarding_manager_sign_date`): This field is for the manager of the boarding facility to sign and date, acknowledging that they have received your signed form.
* **Manager Sign** (`pet_boarding_manager_sign`): This field is for the manager of the boarding facility to sign, acknowledging that they have received your signed form.
* **Boarding Agreement** (`pet_boarding_boarding_aggrement`): This field is for you to agree on the boarding dates.
* **Owner Agreement** (`pet_boarding_boarding_owner_aggrement`): This field is for you to agree on the boarding dates as the owner.
* **Boarding Owners** (`pet_boarding_boarding_owners`): This field is to indicate if you are the primary owner of the pet or if there are other people involved.
* **Owner Name** (`pet_boarding_boarding_owner_name`): This field is for you to provide your name as the owner.
* **Owner Phone** (`pet_boarding_boarding_owner_phone`): This field is for you to provide your phone number.
* **Owner Email** (`pet_boarding_boarding_owner_email`): This field is for you to provide your email address.
* **Pet Type** (`pet_boarding_boarding_pet_type`): This field is to indicate the type of pet you are boarding.

Please note that this form is not intended to be used for other purposes. If you have any questions, please contact the boarding facility directly.
