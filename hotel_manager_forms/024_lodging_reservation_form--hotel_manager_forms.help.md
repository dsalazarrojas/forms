# Lodging Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from a police officer visiting a lodging to ensure their stay is comfortable and secure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form accurately and efficiently, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "name" field.
2. Provide your contact number in the "contact_number" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Choose a check-in date in the "checkin_date" field and a check-out date in the "checkout_date" field.
5. Describe the purpose of your visit in the "reason_for_visit" field.
6. Specify the number of guests in your party in the "number_of_guests" field.
7. If you have any special requests, please type them in the "special_request" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name**: Enter your personal name.
* **contact_number**: Provide your phone number.
* **email**: Enter your email address.
* **checkin_date**: Choose the date you plan to check-in.
* **checkout_date**: Choose the date you plan to check-out.
* **police_force_details**: Briefly describe any special details about your visit as a police officer.
* **police_force_number**: Enter your police force number.
* **police_rank**: Select your rank within the police force (Police Officer, Sergeant, or Commander).
* **police_force_location**: Specify the location of your police force.
* **reason_for_visit**: Describe the purpose of your visit.
* **number_of_guests**: Specify the number of guests in your party.
* **special_request**: If you have any special requests, please type them here.
 
## Tips
- Ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you have any special requests or needs, please let us know in the "special_request" field.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact us through the email provided.

This help guide aims to assist users in navigating and filling out the form efficiently, providing the required fields, and getting the most out of their stay without any confusion or errors.
