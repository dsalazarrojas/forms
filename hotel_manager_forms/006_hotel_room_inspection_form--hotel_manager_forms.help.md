# Hotel Room Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used for hotel room inspections to track the cleanliness, comfort, and maintenance of hotel rooms. It helps hotel management to identify and address any issues, assign maintenance tasks, and keep track of maintenance costs and schedules.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the **Inspect Date** field with the date of the inspection.
2. Enter the **Room Number** of the room being inspected.
3. Choose the **Room Type** (e.g., bedroom, suite, etc.).
4. Rate the **Cleanliness Score** on a scale of 0-10.
5. Select the **Amenities Provided** (e.g., TV, fridge, microwave, etc.).
6. Rate the **Comfort Score** on a scale of 0-10.
7. Describe any **Maintenance Issues** found during the inspection.
8. Add any additional **Comments** about the room.
9. Assign the inspection to a specific person or group (e.g., **Assigned To**).
10. Enter the **Email** and **Phone** contact information for the person or group assigned to the inspection (if applicable).
11. Describe the **Issue Description** of any issues found during the inspection.
12. Choose the **Room Status** (e.g., occupied, not occupied).
13. Choose the **Maintenance Status** (e.g., pending, completed).
14. Enter the **Next Check** date for the maintenance schedule.
15. Enter the **Last Check** date for the previous inspection.
16. Set the **Check Interval** for the maintenance schedule.
17. Add any additional **Notes** about the inspection.
18. Select any **Issues** found during the inspection (e.g., maintenance issues, etc.).
19. Create a **Maintenance Plan** for any issues found during the inspection.
20. Choose the **Maintenance Priority** (e.g., high, medium, low).
21. Enter the **Maintenance Cost** for any issues found during the inspection.
22. Choose the **Maintenance Type** (e.g., plumbing, electrical, etc.).
23. Select the **Assigned Tool** (e.g., chatjimmy, etc.).
24. Choose the tools used for the **Tool Usage** (e.g., chatjimmy, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspect Date** (required): Date of the inspection.
* **Room Number** (required): The number of the room being inspected.
* **Room Type** (required): Choose the type of room (e.g., bedroom, suite, etc.).
* **Cleanliness Score** (required): Rate the cleanliness of the room on a scale of 0-10.
* **Amenities Provided** (required): Select the amenities provided in the room (e.g., TV, fridge, microwave, etc.).
* **Comfort Score** (required): Rate the comfort of the room on a scale of 0-10.
* **Maintenance Issues** (required): Describe any maintenance issues found during the inspection.
* **Comments** (required): Add any additional comments about the room.
* **Assigned To** (required): Choose the person or group assigned to the inspection.
* **Email** (optional): Enter the email contact information for the person or group assigned to the inspection.
* **Phone** (optional): Enter the phone contact information for the person or group assigned to the inspection.
* **Issue Description** (optional): Describe the issue found during the inspection.
* **Room Status** (required): Choose the current status of the room (e.g., occupied, not occupied).
* **Maintenance Status** (required): Choose the maintenance status (e.g., pending, completed).
* **Next Check** (required): Enter the next check date for the maintenance schedule.
* **Last Check** (required): Enter the last check date for the previous inspection.
* **Check Interval** (required): Set the interval for the maintenance schedule.
* **Notes** (optional): Add any additional notes about the inspection.
* **Issues** (optional): Select any issues found during the inspection.
* **Maintenance Plan** (optional): Create a maintenance plan for any issues found during the inspection.
* **Maintenance Priority** (optional): Choose the maintenance priority (e.g., high, medium, low).
* **Maintenance Cost** (optional): Enter the maintenance cost for any issues found during the inspection.
* **Maintenance Type** (optional): Choose the type of maintenance (e.g., plumbing, electrical, etc.).
* **Assigned Tool** (optional): Choose the tool assigned for the maintenance.
* **Tool Usage** (optional): Choose the tools used for the maintenance.

Note: The optional fields are not required but can provide additional information for further analysis and planning.
