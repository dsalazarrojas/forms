# windsurf_equipment_rental_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users for windsurfing equipment rental purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the User Information section with your basic contact and demographic details.
2. In the Contact Information section, provide additional contact details.
3. In the Equipment Rental Information section, specify the equipment you wish to rent, any special requests, and other rental specifics.
4. In the Rental Terms section, read through and acknowledge the terms, or add any additional comments.
5. In the Emergency Contact section, add details of an emergency contact person.
6. In the Additional Info section, add any extra information you feel is relevant.
7. In the Payment Method section, specify how you will pay for the rental.
8. In the Payment Details section, add payment details such as amount and date.
9. Finally, sign the form electronically in the Signature section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Information** (`User Information`, text, optional): Please fill out this section with your basic contact and demographic details.
- **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, text, optional): Add any additional contact information you wish to provide.
- **Equipment Rental Information** (`Equipment Rental Information`, text, optional): Specify the equipment you wish to rent, any special requests, and other rental specifics.
- **Rental Terms** (`Rental Terms`, text, optional): Read through the rental terms carefully and either acknowledge them or add any comments.
- **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, text, optional): Add details of an emergency contact person.
- **Additional Info** (`Additional Info`, text, optional): Add any extra information you feel is relevant.
- **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, text, optional): Specify how you will pay for the rental.
- **Payment Details** (`Payment Details`, text, optional): Add payment details such as amount and date.
- **Signature** (`Signature`, text, optional): Sign the form electronically to confirm your rental application.
