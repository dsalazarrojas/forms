# Hotel Request For Proposal Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Request For Proposal Submission Form is a tool for hotel managers and stakeholders to submit and track hotel requests for proposal. This form serves as a centralized hub for collecting and managing all relevant information related to the proposal, from contact details to status updates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Select the correct options for each field, paying attention to the options available.
3. Review and submit your form to initiate the proposal process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Hotel Request for Proposal Submission** (Page 1, Field 1):
	* This field is a text area where you can input your hotel's proposal submission.
	* Ensure you provide a clear and concise title for your proposal.
- **Contact Details** (Page 1, Field 2):
	* Select all relevant contact information for your hotel, such as name, email, and phone number.
	* Choose all applicable options for your contact details.
- **Hotel Details** (Page 1, Field 3):
	* Select the correct hotel details, such as hotel type, location, or amenities.
* **Date Received** (Page 1, Field 4):
	* Input the date you received the proposal submission.
* **Date Reviewed** (Page 1, Field 5):
	* Input the date you reviewed the proposal submission.
* **Status** (Page 1, Field 6):
	* Select the current status of your proposal submission, such as "Pending", "Approved", or "Rejected".
* **Notes** (Page 1, Field 7):
	* Add any additional notes related to your proposal submission.
* **Email** (Page 2, Field 8):
	* Input the email address associated with your proposal submission.
* **Phone** (Page 2, Field 9):
	* Input the phone number associated with your proposal submission.
* **Submitted By** (Page 2, Field 10):
	* Select the person who submitted the proposal submission.
* **Review Notes** (Page 2, Field 11):
	* Add any additional review notes related to your proposal submission.
* **Review Status** (Page 2, Field 12):
	* Select the current review status of your proposal submission.
* **Reviewer** (Page 2, Field 13):
	* Select the person who reviewed your proposal submission.
* **Created at** (Page 3, Field 14):
	* Input the date the proposal submission was created.
* **Updated at** (Page 3, Field 15):
	* Input the date the proposal submission was last updated.
* **Submitted at** (Page 3, Field 16):
	* Input the date the proposal submission was submitted.
* **Review Completed at** (Page 3, Field 17):
	* Input the date the proposal submission was reviewed.
* **Proposal ID** (Page 4, Field 18):
	* Select the unique identifier for your proposal submission.
* **Form Status** (Page 4, Field 19):
	* Select the current status of your form submission, such as "Pending", "Approved", or "Rejected".
* **Review Comments** (Page 4, Field 20):
	* Add any additional review comments related to your proposal submission.
* **Reviewer Comments** (Page 5, Field 21):
	* Add any additional comments from the reviewer.
* **Submitted Comments** (Page 5, Field 22):
	* Add any additional comments from the person who submitted the proposal.
* **Review Completed By** (Page 5, Field 23):
	* Select the person who completed the review.
* **Submitted to** (Page 6, Field 24):
	* Select the person or department the proposal submission was submitted to.
* **Assigned to** (Page 6, Field 25):
	* Select the person or team assigned to review the proposal submission.

Note: The following fields are not currently being used and are not included in the field-by-field explanation. They are: 6, 9, 11, 14, 15, 17, 20, 22, 23, 24, 25.
