# Guest House Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from guests to make a reservation at a guest house. It is intended to be completed by guests who are interested in booking a room for a specific period.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*), with the requested information.
2. Select the preferred type of room you would like to book.
3. Enter any special requests or comments you may have.
4. Enter your contact information, including phone number and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest House Reservation Form** (`guest_house_reservation_form`, text, required): This is the title of the form, which is used for reference purposes.
* **Guest House Name** (`guest_house_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of the guest house you are interested in booking.
* **Checkin Date** (`checkin_date`, date, required): Enter the date you would like to check in.
* **Checkout Date** (`checkout_date`, date, required): Enter the date you would like to check out.
* **Number of Guests** (`guests_number`, number, required): Enter the number of guests in your party.
* **Type of Room** (`room_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of room you would like to book (Studio, One-bedroom, Two-bedroom, Three-bedroom).
* **Room Services** (`room_services`, text, optional): Enter any special services you would like to request (e.g. extra towels, etc.).
* **Special Request** (`special_request`, text, optional): Enter any special requests or comments you may have.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Guest Email** (`guest_email`, email, optional): Enter the guest's email address.

## Tips
* Please make sure to provide accurate information to avoid any confusion or delays in processing your reservation.
* If you have any special requests, please be as specific as possible so we can accommodate your needs.
