# Hotel Room Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users for hotel room reservations. The purpose is to provide the hotel management with the necessary details required to process the reservation request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the respective input fields.
2. Enter your Email address to help us contact you with any updates or concerns.
3. Provide your Phone number in case we need to reach you.
4. Select your preferred Check In date.
5. Select your preferred Check Out date.
6. Choose the type of Room you would like to reserve (Single, Double, Suite).
7. Indicate the number of Rooms you would like to reserve.
8. If you have any Special Requests, please specify them in the text field.
9. Select the Hotel Manager you would like to contact regarding your reservation.
10. Select the Room Status as Available or Occupied.
11. Add any additional Comments regarding your reservation.
12. Select the Check In date for the reservation.
13. Select the Check In Time.
14. Select the Status of your reservation (Pending, Approved, Rejected).
15. Fill out the fields under "Additional Information" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Required): Enter your first name to help us identify you.
* **Last Name** (Required): Enter your last name to help us identify you.
* **Email** (Optional): Enter your email address to enable us to contact you with updates or concerns.
* **Phone** (Optional): Enter your phone number in case we need to reach you.
* **Check In** (Optional): Select the date you would like to check in to the hotel.
* **Check Out** (Optional): Select the date you would like to check out of the hotel.
* **Room Type** (Optional): Choose the type of room you would like to reserve (Single, Double, Suite).
* **Room Quantity** (Optional): Indicate the number of rooms you would like to reserve.
* **Special Requests** (Optional): If you have any specific requests regarding your reservation, please specify them here.
* **Hotel Manager** (Optional): Select the hotel manager you would like to contact regarding your reservation.
* **Room Status** (Optional): Select the status of the room you would like to reserve (Available or Occupied).
* **Comments** (Optional): Add any additional comments regarding your reservation.
* **Date** (Optional): Select the date of the reservation.
* **Time** (Optional): Select the check in time for the reservation.
* **Status** (Optional): Select the status of your reservation (Pending, Approved, Rejected).
* **Additional Information** (Optional): Fill out the fields under this section as per your requirement.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (First Name, Last Name, etc.).
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all information is accurate.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the hotel management.
