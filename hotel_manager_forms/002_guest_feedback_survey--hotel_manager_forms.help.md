# Guest Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from guests after their stay at the hotel. Your response will help us understand your experience and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Guest Name
2. Enter your Email
3. Select your Stay Dates (start and end dates)
4. Choose your Check-in and Check-out Dates
5. Select the type of room you stayed in (Single Room, Double Room, or Suite)
6. Share your Suggestions for Improvement, if any

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name** (`guest_feedback_survey_name`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Guest Email** (`guest_feedback_survey_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Stay Dates** (`guest_feedback_survey_stay`, `date`, required/optional): Select the dates of your stay at the hotel.
* **Check-in Date** (`guest_feedback_survey_checkin`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date and time you checked in at the hotel.
* **Check-out Date** (`guest_feedback_survey_checkout`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date and time you checked out of the hotel.
* **Room Type** (`guest_feedback_survey_room`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the type of room you stayed in (Single Room, Double Room, or Suite).
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`guest_feedback_survey_suggestions`, `text`, required/optional): Share any suggestions you have for us to improve our services. 

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide constructive feedback.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please describe them clearly.
* If you would like to remain anonymous, please do not include your name or email.
* Your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your time in completing this form.
