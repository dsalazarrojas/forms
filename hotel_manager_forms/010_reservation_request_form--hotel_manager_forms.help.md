# reservation_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect customer contact information, reservation details, and specific requests for reservations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Customer Contact** information, which includes your name and any additional contact details you'd like to share with the hotel staff.
2. Provide your **Reservation** details, such as the type of accommodation you're interested in.
3. If you have any **Requests** or special requirements for your stay, please specify them in the **Message** field.
4. Select your preferred **Date** and **Time** for your reservation.
5. Choose the **Assigned To** field to specify which hotel staff member will be handling your request.
6. Review your submission and make sure everything is accurate before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Contact**: Enter your name and contact information to help the hotel staff get in touch with you.
* **Reservation**: Specify the type of accommodation or room you're interested in.
* **Request**: If you have any special requests or requirements for your stay, please describe them here.
* **Preferred Date**: Choose a date that works best for you. Please use the format **mm-dd-yyyy**.
* **Preferred Time**: Select a preferred time for your reservation.
* **Special Requests**: Add any additional comments or requests you might have.
* **Note**: Enter any further details or comments you'd like to add.
* **Confirm**: Double-check your submission for accuracy.
* **Customer ID**: If you're a repeat customer, enter your customer ID to help the hotel recognize you.
* **User**: Select the person making the request from the available options.
* **Form ID**: You can leave this field blank.
* **Created By**: You can leave this field blank.
* **Created At**: This field displays when the form was created.
* **Updated By**: This field displays when the form was last updated.
* **Updated At**: This field displays when the form was last updated.
* **Assigned To**: Select which staff member you'd like to handle your request.
* **Assigned User**: Enter the name of the staff member assigned to your request.
* **Assigned At**: This field displays when the form was assigned to a staff member.
* **Submitted At**: This field displays when you submitted the form.
* **Submitted By**: Enter your name or username.
* **Submitted User**: This field displays the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **Status**: Choose the current status of your request from the available options (Draft, Published, Archived).
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your request.
