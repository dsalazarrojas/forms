# Boutique Hotel Online Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Boutique Hotel Online Booking Form is used to collect guest information and reservation details for a hotel booking. This form helps the hotel management team to efficiently process and manage reservations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the guest information: Enter the guest's first and last name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the check-in and check-out dates.
3. Choose the preferred room type, bed type, and floor preference (if applicable).
4. Select any special amenities needed (e.g., baby chair, wheelchair accessibility, pet friendly).
5. Choose a preferred payment method.
6. Confirm that you agree to the booking terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Required): Enter the guest's first name.
* **Last Name** (Required): Enter the guest's last name.
* **Email Address** (Required): Enter the guest's email address for booking confirmation.
* **Phone Number** (Required): Enter the guest's phone number for contact during stay.
* **Country** (Optional): Enter the country of residence.
* **Check-in Date** (Required): Select the expected arrival date.
* **Check-out Date** (Required): Select the expected departure date.
* **Number of Nights** (Optional): Calculate the number of nights based on the selected check-in and check-out dates.
* **Total Number of Guests** (Required): Enter the total number of guests (adults and children combined).
* **Preferred Room Type** (Required): Select the preferred room type (e.g., Deluxe Double, Twin Room).
* **Bed Preference** (Required): Select the type of bed desired (e.g., King, Queen, Twin beds).
* **Floor Preference** (Optional): Select the preferred floor level (e.g., Ground floor, Mid-level, Upper floor).
* **Special Amenities Needed** (Optional): Select all special amenities needed (e.g., baby chair, wheelchair accessibility).
* **Additional Special Requests** (Optional): Enter any additional special requests (e.g., late arrival).
* **Preferred Payment Method** (Required): Select the preferred payment method (e.g., Credit card, Debit card, Bank transfer).
* **I agree to booking terms and conditions** (Required): Confirm that you agree to the booking terms and conditions.

Note: Required fields are marked as "Required" in the form and need to be filled out. Optional fields can be filled out if applicable.
