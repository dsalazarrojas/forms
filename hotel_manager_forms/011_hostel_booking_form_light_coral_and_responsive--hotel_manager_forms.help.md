# Hostel Booking Form Light Coral And Responsive - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user information for booking purposes, enabling users to provide their details for stay booking at a hostel.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the "Customer First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Customer Last Name" field.
3. Select your check-in date in the "Checkin" field.
4. Select your check-out date in the "Checkout" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, following the standard email format (e.g., user@example.com).
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. Select your country of origin from the "Country" drop-down list.
8. Select your preferred language from the "Language" drop-down list.
9. Optionally, add any additional comments or remarks in the "Customer Comment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer First Name** (`customer_first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Customer Last Name** (`customer_last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Checkin** (`checkin`, `date`, required: false): Select your check-in date.
* **Checkout** (`checkout`, `date`, required: false): Select your check-out date.
* **Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address in standard format (e.g., user@example.com).
* **Phone** (`customer_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Country** (`customer_country`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your country of origin.
* **Language** (`customer_language`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your preferred language.
* **Customer Comment** (`customer_comment`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional comments or remarks about your booking.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out as much information as possible for a smoother booking experience.
* If you have any dietary restrictions or special requests, consider adding a note in the "Customer Comment" field.
* Double-check your email and phone number for accuracy before submitting the form.
