# Hostel Booking Form Light Olive And Responsive - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hostel Booking Form Light Olive And Responsive is designed to collect essential information from guests for accommodation and booking purposes. This information enables our staff to efficiently process guest requests and ensure a smooth stay.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review the form carefully to understand each field's purpose.
2.  Ensure that all required fields are accurately filled in.
3.  Provide clear and concise answers to each field.
4.  Submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, **optional**): Enter the guest's full name.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, **optional**): Enter the guest's email address for contact purposes.
*   **Checkin Date** (`checkin_date`, date, **optional**): Enter the date of arrival in YYYY-MM-DD format.
*   **Checkout Date** (`checkout_date`, date, **optional**): Enter the date of departure in YYYY-MM-DD format.
*   **Room Type** (`room_type`, select one, **optional**): Select the type of room requested from the options provided.
*   **Adults** (`adult`, number, **optional**): Specify the number of adults staying.
*   **Children** (`children`, number, **optional**): Specify the number of children staying.
*   **Checkin Time** (`checkin_time`, time, **optional**): Enter the time of arrival in 24-hour format.
*   **Room Number** (`room_number`, text, **optional**): Enter the specific room number or type if available.
*   **Room Type Description** (`room_type_description`, text, **optional**): Provide any additional description of the requested room type.
*   **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, **optional**): Specify any special requests or requirements.
*   **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, **optional**): Provide any additional information that may be helpful for the booking.
*   **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select multiple, **optional**): Select the method of payment from the options provided.
*   **Other Payment Method** (`payment_method_other`, text, **optional**): If using an 'Other' payment method, specify it.
*   **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`, time, **optional**): Enter the time of arrival in 24-hour format.
*   **Departure Time** (`departure_time`, time, **optional**): Enter the time of departure in 24-hour format.
*   **Note** (`note`, note, **optional**): Add any additional comments or notes for the booking.
*   **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, text, **optional**): Review and agree to our terms and conditions.
*   **Confirmation** (`confirmation`, text, **optional**): Confirm the accuracy of the booking details.
*   **Terms and Conditions Agreement** (`terms_and_conditions_agreement`, select multiple, **optional**): Confirm your agreement to our terms and conditions.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, **optional**): Enter the guest's contact phone number.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, **optional**): Enter the guest's email address (this field should be ignored as it is already present in the form).
*   **Room** (`room`, text, **optional**): Specify the requested room type or number.
*   **Arrival Date** (`arrival_date`, date, **optional**): Enter the date of arrival in YYYY-MM-DD format.
*   **Departure Date** (`departure_date`, date, **optional**): Enter the date of departure in YYYY-MM-DD format.

## Tips
*   Ensure that all fields are accurately filled in.
*   Review the form before submitting to avoid errors.
*   If unsure about any field, contact our staff for assistance.
