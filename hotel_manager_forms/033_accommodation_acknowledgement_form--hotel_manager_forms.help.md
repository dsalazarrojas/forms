# Accommodation Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information about a guest's stay, including their identification details, accommodation preferences, and acknowledgement of the property's policies.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your personal details, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the correct check-in and check-out dates for your stay.
3. Choose the type of accommodation you have booked.
4. Provide your assigned room number.
5. Enter the property's name and address.
6. Acknowledge the property's cancellation policy, check-in and check-out times, smoking policy, pet policy, noise policy, amenity usage rules, liability disclaimer, housekeeping schedule, and emergency procedures.
7. Sign and date the form to confirm your acknowledgment of the property's policies and procedures.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Full Name**: Your full legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Guest Email**: Your email address for confirmation and communication purposes.
* **Guest Phone**: Your phone number for contact and verification purposes.
* **Check In Date**: The date you plan to arrive at the property.
* **Check Out Date**: The date you plan to depart from the property.
* **Accommodation Type**: The type of accommodation you have booked, such as Single Room, Double Room, Suite, Apartment, or Cabin.
* **Room Number**: The room number assigned to you by the property.
* **Property Name**: The name of the accommodation property.
* **Property Address**: The complete address of the accommodation property.
* **Cancellation Policy Acknowledged**: You must acknowledge and agree to the property's cancellation policy.
* **Check In Out Times Acknowledged**: You must acknowledge the property's check-in and check-out times.
* **Smoking Policy Acknowledged**: You must acknowledge the property's smoking policy.
* **Pet Policy Acknowledged**: You must acknowledge the property's pet policy.
* **Noise Policy Acknowledged**: You must acknowledge the property's noise policy.
* **Amenity Usage Acknowledged**: You must acknowledge the rules for using property amenities.
* **Liability Disclaimer Acknowledged**: You must acknowledge the property's liability disclaimer.
* **Housekeeping Schedule Acknowledged**: You must acknowledge the property's housekeeping schedule.
* **Emergency Procedures Acknowledged**: You must acknowledge the property's emergency procedures.
* **Guest Signature**: Please sign your name to confirm your acknowledgment of the property's policies and procedures.
* **Signature Date**: The date you sign this form.
