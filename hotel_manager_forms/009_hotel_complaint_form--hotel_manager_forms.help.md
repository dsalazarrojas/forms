# Hotel Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Complaint Form is designed to collect information about hotel complaints, which will help hotel management to identify and address issues promptly. Fill in this form accurately and completely to ensure that your complaint is handled effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the complaint title, describing the issue you're experiencing.
2. Provide a detailed description of the complaint, including what happened and when.
3. Specify the room number you were in when the incident occurred.
4. Select the date when the incident took place.
5. Enter your contact name and email address so we can get in touch with you.
6. Provide your contact phone number (optional).
7. Write a detailed account of the complaint in the complaint details field.
## Tips
- Please fill in the form accurately and completely.
- Make sure to include your room number to help us locate the issue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Complaint Title** (`complaint_title`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the issue you're experiencing.
* **Complaint Description** (`complaint_description`, text, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the complaint, including what happened and when.
* **Room Number** (`room_number`, number, required: false): Specify the room number you were in when the incident occurred.
* **Complaint Date** (`complaint_date`, date, required: false): Select the date when the incident took place.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required: false): Enter your full name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Provide your contact phone number (optional).
* **Complaint Details** (`complaint_details`, text, required: false): Write a detailed account of the complaint, including what happened and when.
