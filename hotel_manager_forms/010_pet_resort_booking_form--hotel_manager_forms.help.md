# Pet Resort Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Resort Booking Form is a form used to book and manage pet reservations at a resort.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information, marked with an asterisk (\*) in the label.
2. Enter the pet's name in the "pet_name" field.
3. Select the type of pet (dog, cat, or other) in the "pet_type" field.
4. Enter the owner's name in the "owner_name" field.
5. Enter the owner's email in the "owner_email" field.
6. Choose a date for the event or visit in the "event_date" field.
7. Select the number of participants (if applicable) in the "participant_count" field.
8. Choose the time of the event or visit in the "event_time" field.
9. Enter any additional comments in the "additional_comments" field.
10. Confirm that the participant(s) can attend by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "confirm_participant" field.
11. Enter the owner's phone number in the "owner_phone" field.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **pet_name** (pet_name, text, required): Enter the name of the pet booking.
* **pet_type** (pet_type, select_one, required): Select the type of pet (e.g., dog, cat, other).
* **owner_name** (owner_name, text, required): Enter the owner's name.
* **owner_email** (owner_email, email, required): Enter the owner's email address.
* **event_date** (event_date, date, required): Choose a date for the event or visit.
* **participant_count** (participant_count, number, optional): If applicable, select the number of participants.
* **event_time** (event_time, time, required): Choose the time of the event or visit.
* **additional_comments** (additional_comments, text, optional): Enter any additional comments.
* **confirm_participant** (confirm_participant, select_multiple, required): Confirm that the participant(s) can attend by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **owner_phone** (owner_phone, text, optional): Enter the owner's phone number.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check the entered information before submitting the form.
* If you need to book multiple participants, enter the number of participants in the "participant_count" field.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, enter them in the "additional_comments" field.
