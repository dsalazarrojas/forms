# Hotel Employee Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help hotel managers and supervisors collect feedback on employee performance.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the employee's name.
2. Select the job title that best reflects the employee's current role.
3. Rate the employee's performance as Excellent, Needs Improvement, or Unsatisfactory.
4. Provide specific feedback on the employee's performance.
5. Enter the date and time of the evaluation.
6. Type in the name of the person performing the evaluation.
7. Enter your name as the manager performing the evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee** (text): Enter the name of the employee being evaluated.
* **Job Title** (select_one): Select the job title that best reflects the employee's current role. 
	+ Options: Manager, Assistant Manager, Coordinator, Clerk
* **Performance Level** (select_one): Rate the employee's performance as Excellent, Needs Improvement, or Unsatisfactory.
* **Comments** (text): Provide specific feedback on the employee's performance. Be as detailed as possible.
* **Evaluation Date** (date): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Time** (time): Enter the time of the evaluation.
* **Evaluated by** (text): Type in the name of the person performing the evaluation.
* **Manager's Name** (text): Enter your name as the manager performing the evaluation.

## Tips
* Be specific and honest in the comments section to help the employee grow and improve.
* Ensure that all fields are filled in correctly to maintain accuracy and accountability.
* This form is meant to be a helpful tool for employee growth, so use it as a starting point for constructive feedback.
