# Accommodation Shift Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to report on the shift information and activities that occurred during a shift in the accommodation department of a hotel or similar establishment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the **Property Name** of the hotel or establishment you are working at.
2. Select the correct **Shift Date** from the calendar or clock.
3. Choose the correct **Shift Type** (Morning, Afternoon, Night, or Split shift).
4. Enter the start and end times of your shift using the **Shift Start Time** and **Shift End Time** fields.
5. Enter the name of the **Shift Manager** who was in charge during your shift.
6. Enter the number of **Staff On Duty** and **Staff Absent**.
7. Enter the **Total Check-Ins During Shift**, **Total Check-Outs During Shift**, **Total Rooms Occupied at End of Shift**, and **Total Rooms Available at End of Shift**.
8. Describe any **Maintenance Issues Reported**, **Guest Complaints Received**, and **Incidents or Unusual Occurrences**.
9. Enter the **Cash on Hand at End of Shift**.
10. Leave any **Handover Notes for Next Shift** for the incoming shift manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the property where you worked during this shift.
* **Shift Date** (Date, Required): Select the date of the shift you are reporting on.
* **Shift Type** (Select One, Required): Choose the type of shift you worked (Morning, Afternoon, Night, or Split shift).
* **Shift Start Time** (Time, Required): Enter the start time of your shift.
* **Shift End Time** (Time, Required): Enter the end time of your shift.
* **Shift Manager Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the shift manager who was in charge during your shift.
* **Number of Staff On Duty** (Number, Required): Enter the number of staff members on duty during your shift.
* **Number of Staff Absent** (Number, Optional): Enter the number of staff members absent during your shift.
* **Total Check-Ins During Shift** (Number, Required): Enter the total number of guests checked-in during your shift.
* **Total Check-Outs During Shift** (Number, Required): Enter the total number of guests checked-out during your shift.
* **Total Rooms Occupied at End of Shift** (Number, Required): Enter the total number of rooms occupied at the end of your shift.
* **Total Rooms Available at End of Shift** (Number, Required): Enter the total number of rooms available at the end of your shift.
* **Maintenance Issues Reported** (Text, Optional): Describe any maintenance issues reported during your shift.
* **Guest Complaints Received** (Text, Optional): Summarize any guest complaints received during your shift.
* **Incidents or Unusual Occurrences** (Text, Optional): Describe any safety, security, or notable events that occurred during your shift.
* **Cash on Hand at End of Shift** (Number, Optional): Enter the amount of cash on hand at the end of your shift.
* **Handover Notes for Next Shift** (Text, Required): Leave any important information for the incoming shift manager to know.
