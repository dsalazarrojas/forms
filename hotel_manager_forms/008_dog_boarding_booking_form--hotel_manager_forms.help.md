# Dog Boarding Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dog Boarding Booking Form is a digital form designed for customers to book a stay at a dog boarding facility. This form helps the facility gather necessary information from customers in an efficient and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide User Information**: Enter your name, address, and contact details in the "User Information" section.
2. **Enter Boarding Details**: In the "Boarding Details" section, provide as much information as possible about your dog's stay, including their breed, age, and any medical conditions.
3. **Share Boarding Preferences**: In the "Boarding Preferences" section, let us know about any special requests or requirements you have for your dog's boarding experience.
4. **Provide Payment Information**: Enter your payment details in the "Payment Information" section.
5. **Leave Review Information (Optional)**: If you have any feedback or reviews about your previous stay, you can share them in the "Review Information" section.
6. **Add Any Additional Notes**: If you have any other comments or requests, you can add them in the "Notes" section.
