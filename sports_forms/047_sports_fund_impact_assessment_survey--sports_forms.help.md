# sports_fund_impact_assessment_survey--sports_forms - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on the impact of sports funding on sports programs. It is crucial to complete this form accurately to ensure that your input is correctly recorded and analyzed.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that you have a clear understanding of the sport program you are assessing.
2. Enter the sport name in the <sport_name> field.
3. Enter the funding amount in the <funding_amount> field.
4. Select the correct program type in the <program_type> field.
5. Choose all applicable impact indicators in the <impact_indicators> field.
6. Select all applicable program evaluation indicators in the <program_evaluation> field.
7. Choose all applicable program improvement areas in the <program_improvement> field.
8. Select the correct resource allocation status in the <resource_allocation> field.
9. Enter the program goals in the <program_goals> field.
10. Enter the program success in the <program_success> field.
11. Leave any additional feedback in the <feedback> field.
12. Enter your contact information in the <contact> field.
13. Enter your email address in the <email> field.
14. Assign a unique tool ID to this form instance in the <assigned_tool> field.
15. Enter a unique ID for this form instance in the <form_id> field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `note`, required: false): This field is a note field where you can introduce your program and provide a brief overview.
* **Sport Name** (`sport_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the sport program you are assessing.
* **Funding Amount** (`funding_amount`, `number`, required: false): Enter the total funding amount allocated to the sport program.
* **Program Type** (`program_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of program you are assessing (e.g., grassroots, developmental, or competitive).
* **Impact Indicators** (`impact_indicators`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all applicable impact indicators that describe how the sport program has a positive or negative impact (e.g., increased participation, improved skills, or better facilities).
* **Program Evaluation** (`program_evaluation`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all applicable program evaluation indicators that assess the quality and effectiveness of the program (e.g., coaching, sports equipment, or sports facilities).
* **Program Improvement** (`program_improvement`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all applicable program improvement areas that describe what the program should improve (e.g., coaching, sports equipment, or sports facilities).
* **Resource Allocation** (`resource_allocation`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the status of resource allocation for the program (e.g., sufficient, insufficient, or no resources).
* **Program Goals** (`program_goals`, `text`, required: false): Enter the program's goals and objectives.
* **Program Success** (`program_success`, `text`, required: false): Describe the program's success and achievements.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `note`, required: false): Leave any additional feedback or comments.
* **Contact** (`contact`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact information (e.g., name, phone number, or address).
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required: true): Assign a unique tool ID to this form instance.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, `text`, required: true): Enter a unique ID for this form instance.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in to ensure accurate data collection.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the fields.
* Review your input before submitting to ensure it is correct.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the form administrator for assistance.
