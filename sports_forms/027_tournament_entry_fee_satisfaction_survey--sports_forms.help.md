# Tournament Entry Fee Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tournament Entry Fee Satisfaction Survey is a form designed to collect feedback from participants about their experience with the tournament entry fees. This survey aims to understand your opinion on the fairness and value of the fees associated with the tournament.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Select one of the options for each question based on your personal experience with the tournament entry fees.
2.  Be honest and provide as much detail as possible in the open-ended questions.
3.  Ensure you fill in all required fields, if any.
4.  Submit the form to help us improve your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Entry Fee Fairness (1)** (`entry_fee_fairness`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select 'Yes' if you feel the entry fee is fair, 'No' if you feel it's not fair, or leave it blank if you're unsure.
* **Value for Money (2)** (`value_for_money`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check all that apply if you feel the entry fee provides value for money:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Future Participation Intent (3)** (`future_participation_intent`, `number`, required: false): This is a numerical question to gauge your interest in participating in future events. Please enter the number that best represents your future participation intent.
* **Player Suggestions (4)** (`player_suggestions`, `note`, required: false): Share any suggestions you have for improving the tournament experience.
* **Comments (5)** (`comments`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check all that apply if you have any comments or feedback:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Email (6)** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for future events or communications.
* **Phone (7)** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact phone number if you'd like to be contacted via phone.
* **Name (8)** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name for future reference.
* **Organization (9)** (`organization`, `text`, required: false): Enter your organization or affiliation (if any).
* **Job Title (10)** (`job_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter your job title or position (if any).
* **Department (11)** (`department`, `text`, required: false): Enter your department or division (if any).
* **Location (12)** (`location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location of your office or department (if any).
* **Contact Number (13)** (`contact_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact number if you'd like to be contacted.
* **Contact Email (14)** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your contact email address if you'd like to be contacted.
* **Additional Comments (15)** (`additional_comments`, `note`, required: false): Share any additional comments or feedback you have.
* **Feedback (16)** (`feedback`, `select_one`, required: false): Check all that apply if you:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Satisfaction (17)** (`satisfaction`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check all that apply if you are:
	+ 'Satisfied'
	+ 'Dissatisfied'
* **Participation Interest (18)** (`participation_interest`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check all that apply if you are interested in participating in future events:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Future Participation (19)** (`future_participation`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you plan to participate in future events.
* **Date (20)** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the event you participated in.
* **Time (21)** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you participated in the event.
* **Event Type (22)** (`event_type`, `text`, required: false): Enter the type of event you participated in (e.g., competition, exhibition, seminar).
* **Event Name (23)** (`event_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the event you participated in.
* **Event Organizer (24)** (`event_organizer`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the event organizer or team (if any).
* **Event Location (25)** (`event_location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location of the event.
