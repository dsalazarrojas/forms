# tel_pre_training_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information for a telecommunications training program. You are expected to provide your contact details to enable us to get in touch with you during or after the training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and understand the purpose of each field.
2. Fill in all the required fields (none of the fields in this form are marked as required, but it's essential to fill in as much information as possible).
3. Enter your email address and phone number (you can enter multiple phone numbers if you have more).
4. Fill in the "tel address X" fields with any additional contact information you would like to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **date of birth** (`date`, required/optional): This field asks for your date of birth. Please enter your date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **email address** (`email`, required/optional): Enter your email address here. This will be used for communication and updates.
* **phone number** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number here. You can enter multiple numbers if you have more.
* **tel address X** (`note`, required/optional): These fields are for additional contact information. You can enter up to 20 different phone numbers or addresses. You can use these fields to provide any relevant contact information you would like to share.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you fill in all the fields truthfully and accurately.
* You can enter multiple phone numbers or addresses in the "tel address X" fields.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
