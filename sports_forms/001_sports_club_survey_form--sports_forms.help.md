# Sports Club Survey Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from sports club members on their experience and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and make sure you have answered all relevant questions.
2. Select your answers from the available options for each field.
3. If you have additional comments or suggestions, please use the text fields to provide more information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Food selection** (`survey_form_food_selection`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
This field allows you to select the types of food you would like to see on the menu. Please choose all that apply.
* **Frequency of Visit** (`frequency_of_visit`, `select_one`, required: false):
This field asks about how often you visit the sports club. Please select the option that best describes your visit frequency.
* **Preferred Beverage** (`preferred_beverage`, `select_one`, required: false):
This field asks about your preferred beverage at the sports club. Please select the option that you most enjoy.
* **Ideas to Improve Facility** (`ideas_improve_facility`, `text`, required: false):
If you have any suggestions on how to improve the sports club facility, please enter your ideas in this text field.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`, required: false):
If you have any other comments or suggestions, please enter them in this field.
* **Survey Form Beverage** (`survey_form_beverage`, `text`, required: false):
This field is for additional information about your beverage preferences. If you have any specific requests or dislikes, please enter them here.
* **Survey Form Foods** (`survey_form_foods`, `text`, required: false):
This field is for additional information about your food preferences. If you have any specific requests or dislikes, please enter them here.
* **Survey Form Frequency** (`survey_form_frequency`, `text`, required: false):
This field is for any additional information about your visit frequency. If you have any specific reasons for visiting more or less often, please enter them here.
* **Survey Form Ideas** (`survey_form_ideas`, `text`, required: false):
If you have any ideas or suggestions for improving the sports club, please enter them in this field.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when providing feedback and suggestions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the club administration.
* Your input is valuable and will help us improve the sports club experience.
