# Spring Tennis Challenge Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spring Tennis Challenge Registration Form is designed to gather essential information from participants for the competitive tennis event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Participant Email" field.
3. Enter the name of the challenge you are participating in in the "Challenge Name" field.
4. Select a date for the challenge in the "Challenge Date" field.
5. Enter the start time of the challenge in the "Challenge Start Time" field.
6. Enter the end time of the challenge in the "Challenge End Time" field.
7. Select your formation type, either "Single Player" or "Team Player", in the "Participant Formation" field.
8. Select the size of your team in the "Team Size" field.
9. Select the team members in the "Team Members" field (this field is only accessible if "Participant Formation" is set to "Team Player").
10. Select your level of tennis experience in the "Participant Tennis Experience" field.
11. Select your gender in the "Participant Gender" field.
12. Enter your age in the "Participant Age" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Participant Email**: Enter your valid email address.
* **Challenge Name**: Enter the name of the challenge you are participating in (e.g., "Spring Tennis Open" or "Summer Tennis Tournament").
* **Challenge Date**: Select a date for the challenge from the calendar.
* **Challenge Start Time**: Enter the start time of the challenge (e.g., "09:00").
* **Challenge End Time**: Enter the end time of the challenge (e.g., "12:00").
* **Participant Formation**: Select "Single Player" if you are participating alone, or "Team Player" if you are part of a team.
* **Team Size**: Select the number of players on your team (e.g., "2" for a team of two players).
* **Team Members**: Select each member of your team from the list of options (only accessible if "Participant Formation" is set to "Team Player").
* **Participant Tennis Experience**: Select your level of tennis experience (e.g., "1" for beginner, "2" for intermediate, "3" for advanced).
* **Participant Gender**: Select your gender.
* **Participant Age**: Enter your age.

Note: Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your registration is complete and accurate.
