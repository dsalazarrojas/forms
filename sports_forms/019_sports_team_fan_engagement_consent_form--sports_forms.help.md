# sports_team_fan_engagement_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sports team fan engagement consent form is designed to collect information from fans to create a more personalized experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your general information, such as your name and contact details.
2. Check the boxes for the types of marketing messages you are willing to receive from the team.
3. Enter your preferred communication channels, such as email or phone number.
4. Enter your email address.
5. Enter your phone number.
6. (Optional) Add any additional notes or comments.
7. Review and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (general_information, text, required): This is the first section of the form where you can provide your basic information. Please fill in your name and any other relevant details.
* **Consent to Marketing** (consent_to_marketing, select_multiple, required): This section allows you to choose the types of marketing messages you are willing to receive from the team. Please select the options that apply to you.
* **Contact Details** (contact_details, text, required): This section is used to collect your contact information, such as your address.
* **Preferred Communication Channels** (preferred_communication_channels, select_one, required): Select your preferred way of communication, such as email, phone, or social media.
* **Email** (email, email, required): Enter your email address that the team can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (phone_number, text, required): Enter your phone number that the team can use to contact you.
* **Note** (note, note, optional): This section is for any additional comments or notes you want to provide.
* **Terms and Conditions** (terms_and_conditions, text, required): Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the form. This is a required field to ensure you understand the terms of the form.
