# Exercise Goal Setting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to help you set and track your exercise goals. It's a comprehensive tool that will guide you in creating a tailored plan based on your specific needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
- Click on each page to proceed to the corresponding questions.
- Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*), to ensure you can save and access your completed form.
- Use the hints provided for guidance on each field.
- Once you've filled out all the required fields, click "Submit" to create your goal setting plan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Goal Setting** (`goal_setting`, text, optional): This is the title of your exercise goal, which will serve as a summary of your objectives.
* **Specific Goal** (`specific_goal`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Weight Loss
	+ Muscle Strengthening
	+ Flexibility
	+ Endurance
* **Target Weight** (`target_weight`, number, optional): The weight you aim to reach.
* **Workout Frequency** (`workout_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how frequently you plan to work out:
	+ High
	+ Moderate
	+ Low
* **Goal Duration** (`goal_duration`, date, optional): The start and end date for your goal period.
* **Start** (`goal_start`, date, optional): The start date for your goal period.
* **End** (`goal_end`, date, optional): The end date for your goal period.
* **Progress** (`goal_progress`, text, optional): Notes on your progress throughout the goal period.
* **Target Body Fat Percentage** (`target_body_fat_percentage`, number, optional): The body fat percentage you aim to reach.
* **Current Body Fat Percentage** (`current_body_fat_percentage`, number, optional): Your current body fat percentage.
* **Target Body Weight** (`target_body_weight`, number, optional): The weight you aim to reach.
* **Current Body Weight** (`current_body_weight`, number, optional): Your current weight.
* **Target Body Mass Index** (`target_body_mass_index`, number, optional): The body mass index you aim to reach.
* **Current Body Mass Index** (`current_body_mass_index`, number, optional): Your current body mass index.
* **Target Heart Rate** (`target_heart_rate`, number, optional): The heart rate you aim to reach.
* **Current Heart Rate** (`current_heart_rate`, number, optional): Your current heart rate.
* **Exercise Plan** (`exercise_plan`, text, optional): Notes on your exercise plan and routine.
* **Training Frequency** (`training_frequency`, text, optional): How frequently you train.
* **Training Plan** (`training_plan`, text, optional): Notes on your training plan.
* **Progress Notes** (`progress_notes`, text, optional): Notes on your progress.
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, text, optional): Any medical considerations or notes.
* **Exercise Frequency** (`exercise_frequency`, select_one, optional): The frequency of your exercise.
* **Rest Days** (`rest_days`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how often you take rest days:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Workout Type** (`workout_type`, select_one, optional): Choose one:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Workout Days** (`workout_days`, number, optional): The number of workout days per week.

Note: The hints provided are meant to guide you through the form, but please fill out fields according to your specific needs and preferences.
