# Netherton United Football Club Players Registration & Consent Form Test - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Netherton United Football Club Players Registration & Consent Form Test is a mandatory form for all players to register and provide their consent to the terms of the code of conduct.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:
1. Fill in your name, date of birth, contact number, and email.
2. If you have an emergency contact, fill in their name, relation, contact number, and email.
3. Check the box next to the "Consent" field to indicate your agreement to the terms of the code of conduct.
4. If you have any comments or concerns, you can fill in the "Code of Conduct" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Name**: This field is required to identify you as a player of the Netherton United Football Club. Please fill in your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date of Birth**: This field is required to verify your age and eligibility to play. Please fill in your date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YY).
* **Contact Number**: This field is required to contact you in case of emergency. Please fill in your mobile number or other contact number.
* **Email**: This field is required to contact you via email. Please fill in your email address.
* **Address**: This field is optional and only required if you have a different address than your registered address. Please fill in your current address.
* **Emergency Contact**: This field is optional and only required if you have an emergency contact. Please fill in the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relation**: This field is optional and only required if you have an emergency contact. Please fill in your relation with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Number**: This field is optional and only required if you have an emergency contact. Please fill in the contact number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: This field is optional and only required if you have an emergency contact. Please fill in the email of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: This field is optional and only required if you have an emergency contact. Please fill in the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Consent**: This field is required and indicates your agreement to the terms of the code of conduct. Please check the box next to "Agree" if you agree to the terms.
* **Code of Conduct**: This field is optional and only required if you have any comments or concerns about the code of conduct. Please fill in your comments or concerns.
