# Psa Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Psa Form is designed to collect information about the number and details of children from a user, including their names, dates of birth, assessment levels, and medical conditions. This information is likely used for educational or assessment purposes.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question for each child, starting from the first child and moving to the last child.
2. Enter the name of each child in the respective field.
3. Enter the date of birth for each child in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
4. Select the assessment level for each child from the options provided.
5. Select any medical conditions that apply to each child.
6. Confirm your consent to the collection and use of your child's information for assessment purposes.
7. Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the assessment.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How many children do you have?**: This field is used to determine the number of children the user has.
* **What is the name of your first child?**: This field is used to collect the name of the first child.
* **What is your first child's date of birth?**: This field is used to collect the date of birth of the first child in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **What is your first child's assessment level?**: This field is used to select the assessment level for the first child.
* **Does your first child have any medical conditions?**: This field is used to select any medical conditions that apply to the first child.
* **What is the name of your second child?**: This field is used to collect the name of the second child.
* **What is your second child's date of birth?**: This field is used to collect the date of birth of the second child in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **What is your second child's assessment level?**: This field is used to select the assessment level for the second child.
* **Does your second child have any medical conditions?**: This field is used to select any medical conditions that apply to the second child.
* ... (repeat for each child)
* **Do you consent to the collection and use of your child's information for the purpose of this assessment?**: This field is used to confirm the user's consent to the collection and use of their child's information.
* **Terms and Conditions**: This field is used to review and agree to the terms and conditions of the assessment.
* **Confirmation**: This field is used to confirm the user's understanding of their consent to the collection and use of their child's information.
