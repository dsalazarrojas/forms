# Volleyball Tournament Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volleyball Tournament Registration Form is used to collect information from teams registering for a volleyball tournament.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting, ensure you have all necessary information ready. This includes team name, team members, division, date of birth, email, contact phone, address, tournament name, location, date, time, registered by, and team ID.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Name**: The team name is the name of the team that will be participating in the tournament. Please input the name of your team.
* **Team Members**: Indicate whether your team has members. You can select 'Yes' or 'No' from the multiple-choice options.
* **Division**: Select the division your team will be competing in from the multiple-choice options.
* **Date of Birth**: Input the date of birth of your team members. This field is a date field.
* **Email**: Input the email address of your team contact.
* **Contact Phone**: Input the contact phone number of your team contact.
* **Address**: This field is for inputting any additional address information.
* **Tournament Name**: Input the name of the tournament.
* **Location**: Input the location of the tournament.
* **Date**: Input the date of the tournament. This field is a date field.
* **Time**: Input the time of the tournament. This field is a time field.
* **Registered By**: Select who registered your team from the multiple-choice options.
* **Team ID**: Input the team ID.
* **Notes**: This field is for inputting any additional notes about your team.

## Tips
Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely to avoid any issues during the registration process. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to the organizers of the tournament.
