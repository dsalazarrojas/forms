# Snowboard Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This Snowboard Survey form is designed to collect information from snowboarders about their preferences, experiences, and feedback. It's used to gather insights to improve the snowboarding community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the text field on the first page.
2. Enter your **Email** in the email field on the second page.
3. Enter your **Phone Number** in the text field on the third page.
4. Choose your favorite snow **Sport** from the dropdown options on the fourth page.
5. Select your **Satisfaction Level** with the options provided on the fifth page.
6. Enter the **Date** you last snowboarded in the date field on the sixth page.
7. Enter the time of the last **Event** you attended in the time field on the seventh page.
8. Write your feedback in the **Feedback 1** text field on the eighth page.
9. Write your **Feedback 2** in the text field on the ninth page.
10. Provide additional feedback in the **Feedback 3** text field on the tenth page.
11. Finally, provide any further feedback in the **Feedback 4** text field on the eleventh page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`snowboard_survey_1`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Email** (`snowboard_survey_2`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`snowboard_survey_3`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Favorite Sport** (`snowboard_survey_4`, select_one, required): Choose your favorite snow sport, such as snowboarding, skiing, skating, or snowshoeing.
* **Satisfaction Level** (`snowboard_survey_5`, select_multiple, required): Select how satisfied you are with the snowboarding experience, options are **High, Medium, Low**.
* **Date Last Snowboarded** (`snowboard_survey_6`, date, required): Enter the date you last went snowboarding.
* **Last Event** (`snowboard_survey_7`, time, required): Enter the time of the last event you attended.
* **Feedback 1** (`snowboard_survey_8`, text, required): Share your thoughts about your snowboarding experience.
* **Feedback 2** (`snowboard_survey_9`, text, required): Share another thought about your snowboarding experience.
* **Feedback 3** (`snowboard_survey_10`, text, required): Share more thoughts about your snowboarding experience.
* **Feedback 4** (`snowboard_survey_11`, text, required): Share any final thoughts about your snowboarding experience.

**Tips:** Ensure you complete all required fields before submitting the form.
