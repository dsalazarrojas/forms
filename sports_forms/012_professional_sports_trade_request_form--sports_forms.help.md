# Professional Sports Trade Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Professional Sports Trade Request Form is designed to gather information about trade requests between teams. It helps teams to communicate their trade interests and details efficiently, ensuring that all parties involved are on the same page.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Provide your team's name and details in the "Team Requesting Trade" field.
*   Enter the team offering the trade in the "Team Offering Trade" field.
*   Specify the proposed player(s) you would like to trade in the "Proposed Player(s)" field.
*   Mention the specific position(s) of the proposed player(s) in the "Proposed Player Position" field.
*   If the proposed player position is not listed, provide additional details in the "Proposed Player Position (Other)" field.
*   Enter the trade value and type in the "Trade Value" and "Trade Type" fields.
*   Specify the trade value type and amount in the "Trade Value Type" and "Trade Value Amount" fields.
*   If the trade value type is other than listed, provide more details in the "Trade Value Other" field.
*   Enter the trade deadline date and time in the "Trade Deadline Date" and "Trade Deadline Time" fields.
*   If the trade deadline is other than a date and time, provide additional details in the "Trade Deadline Other" field.
*   Select the trade status as "Active", "Inactive", or "Expired" from the options.
*   Choose the team requesting trade status as "Active" or "Inactive" from the options.
*   If the trade type is other than listed, provide more details in the "Trade Type Other" field.
*   Enter your contact email and phone number in the "Email" and "Phone" fields.
