# Wrestling Club Covid 19 Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from wrestling club members and their parents/guardians to ensure their safety and well-being during club activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Provide the necessary contact information for the parents or guardian.
* List any medical conditions that the member may have and provide details about the condition.
* Provide the member's date of birth, age, and grade completed.
* Record the emergency contact information, including the name and contact details of the person authorized to contact in case of an emergency.
* Sign and date the form as required.
