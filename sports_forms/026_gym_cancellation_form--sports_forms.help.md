# Gym Cancellation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gym Cancellation Form is designed to capture information from gym members who want to cancel their membership. It is essential that gym staff understand that this form is for cancellations only and not for other membership-related purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the date you are cancelling your membership in the `membership_date` field.
2. Provide the reason for your cancellation in the `reason_for_cancellation` field.
3. Confirm that you are cancelling your membership by selecting "Yes" in the `confirmation_of_cancellation` field.
4. Enter your gym name where you attended the gym.
5. Enter your member email address.
6. Enter your member phone number.
7. Enter the date of cancellation.
8. (Optional) Provide any additional cancellation reason if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Membership Date**: A field to enter the date you are cancelling your membership.
  - This field is a date field where you can enter the date you want your membership to end.
  - Enter the exact date of cancellation in `YYYY-MM-DD` format.
- **Reason for Cancellation**: A field to provide the reason for cancelling your membership.
  - This field is a text field where you can type in your reason.
  - Please keep in mind that providing a reason is optional but helpful for the gym management.
- **Confirmation of Cancellation**: A field to confirm that you are cancelling your membership.
  - This field is a dropdown menu where you select "Yes".
  - Select "Yes" if you are cancelling your membership.
- **Gym Name**: A field to enter the name of the gym where you are cancelling your membership.
  - This field is a text field where you enter your gym name.
  - Enter the exact gym name where you attend.
- **Member Email**: A field to enter your email address.
  - This field is an email field where you enter your email address.
  - Enter your actual email address associated with your membership.
- **Member Phone**: A field to enter your phone number.
  - This field is a text field where you enter your phone number.
  - Enter your actual phone number associated with your membership.
- **Cancellation Date**: A field to enter the date of cancellation.
  - This field is a date field where you can enter the date you want your membership to end.
  - Enter the exact date of cancellation in `YYYY-MM-DD` format.
- **Cancellation Reason**: An optional field to enter any additional reason for cancellation.
  - This field is a text field where you can add any additional reason for cancellation.
  - Keep it brief and to the point.

## Tips

- Ensure that you have filled out the form correctly before submitting it.
- Check that all required fields are filled out before submitting.
- Double-check that the date of cancellation is accurate.
