# Softball Player Finder Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about softball players for registration, organization, and communication purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Player Info** (`player_info`): Enter a brief description of the player, including their name and any other relevant details.
2. **Name** (`name`): Enter the player's name as it appears on their identification.
3. **Age Group** (`age_group`): Choose the player's age group from the options provided:
	* 9-12
	* 13-15
	* 16-18
4. **Height** (`height`): Enter the player's height in feet and inches. For example, 5'8" would be entered as "5 8".
5. **Position** (`position`): Select the player's primary position on the field:
	* Pitcher
	* Catcher
	* Infielder
	* Outfielder
6. **Contact Info** (`contact_info`): Enter any relevant contact information for the player, such as an email address or phone number.
7. **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`): Enter the name and phone number of the player's emergency contact.
8. **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`): If applicable, enter any medical conditions or special needs that the player may have.
9. **Availability** (`availability`): Check all days the player is available to play:
	* Monday
	* Tuesday
	* Wednesday
	* Thursday
	* Friday
	* Saturday
10. **Player Skills** (`player_skills`): Enter any notable skills or strengths the player has, such as batting, pitching, or throwing.
11. **Experience** (`experience`): Enter the number of years the player has played softball.
12. **Team Affiliations** (`team_affiliations`): Select the player's team affiliations:
	* None
	* Local Team
	* Regional Team
	* National Team
13. **Coach Name** (`coach_name`): Enter the name of the player's coach or mentor.
14. **Coach Phone** (`coach_phone`): Enter the coach's phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Info** (`player_info`): This field helps us understand the player's background and identity.
* **Name** (`name`): We need to know your name for identification and communication purposes.
* **Age Group** (`age_group`): We need to know your age group to ensure correct team assignments.
* **Height** (`height`): Accurate height helps ensure correct uniform and equipment sizing.
* **Position** (`position`): Select your primary position to help coaches make informed team decisions.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`): Share your contact information to facilitate communication and updates.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`): If you have an emergency contact, please provide their name and phone number.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`): If you have any medical conditions, please disclose them to ensure we can provide necessary accommodations.
* **Availability** (`availability`): Check all days you are available to play.
* **Player Skills** (`player_skills`): Share your skills to help coaches understand your strengths and abilities.
* **Experience** (`experience`): Share your years of experience to help coaches assess your skill level.
* **Team Affiliations** (`team_affiliations`): Indicate your team affiliations to help us know your team commitments.
* **Coach Name** (`coach_name`): Share your coach's name and contact information.
* **Coach Phone** (`coach_phone`): Share your coach's phone number for easy communication.
