# zumba_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from students or instructors regarding the Zumba program. It helps us evaluate and improve the program's effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct class date and report date for the feedback being provided.
2. Enter the name of the instructor who conducted the class.
3. Enter the name of the class or program being evaluated.
4. Choose the type of class (e.g., Zumba, Strength).
5. Provide any feedback or comments about the class.
6. Enter a rating for the class (on a scale, e.g., 1-5).
7. Enter the instructor's contact information (phone and email).
8. Enter your own name, email, and phone number (if you are not the instructor).
9. Select who submitted the form (e.g., Jim, Joe, Jane).
10. Select who the form was submitted to (e.g., John, Mary, David).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Class Date**: The date the class was conducted. 
* **Report Date**: The date the feedback is being provided. 
* **Instructor Name**: The name of the instructor who conducted the class.
* **Class Name**: The name of the class or program being evaluated.
* **Location**: The location where the class was conducted.
* **Class Type**: The type of class (e.g., Zumba, Strength).
* **Feedback**: Any comments or feedback about the class.
* **Comments**: Additional comments or suggestions about the class.
* **Rating**: A rating for the class (on a scale, e.g., 1-5).
* **Instructor Phone**: The phone number of the instructor.
* **Instructor Email**: The email of the instructor.
* **Student Name**: Your name (if you are a student).
* **Student Email**: Your email (if you are a student).
* **Student Phone**: Your phone number (if you are a student).
* **Instructor Comments**: Any comments or feedback from the instructor.
* **Submitted**: The date the form was submitted.
* **Form Submitted By**: Who submitted the form (e.g., Jim, Joe, Jane).
* **Form Submitted To**: Who the form was submitted to (e.g., John, Mary, David).

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to provide useful feedback.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the relevant person or department for guidance.
* The form is intended for students or instructors to provide feedback, so ensure you fill it out for the correct purpose.
