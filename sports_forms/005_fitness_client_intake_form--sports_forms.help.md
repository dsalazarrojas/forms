# Fitness Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather essential information from potential clients to provide them with an effective and tailored fitness coaching experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please enter your name, phone number, and email address in the **Contact Details** section.
2. In the **Health History** section, provide a brief description of your current health status, including any medical conditions, allergies, or previous injuries.
3. In the **Lifestyle Details** section, describe your current lifestyle habits, including your daily routine, diet, and any relevant habits or preferences.
4. In the **Availability Status** section, select whether you are available for coaching sessions.
5. In the **Training Goals** section, select one or more of the training goals that you are interested in achieving.
6. In the **Acknowledgement** section, select whether you agree or decline to participate in our coaching services.
