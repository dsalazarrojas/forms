# Gym Membership Verification Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gym Membership Verification Request Form is used to update or verify a member's details, ensuring that their information is accurate and up-to-date. This form is essential for maintaining accurate records and complying with regulatory requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Member Details section with your name and other required information.
2. Upload or provide your Member Documents, if requested.
3. Choose your consent option to share your personal data with the gym.
4. Enter your Member Email and Member Phone numbers.
5. Provide your Date Of Birth.
6. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Member Details (Member Name)** (`member_details`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Member Documents (Upload/Provide)** (`member_documents`, text, optional): Please upload or provide scanned copies of your official identification documents, such as a driver's license, passport, or ID card.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you agree, decline, or are unsure about sharing your personal data with the gym.
	+ If you choose "Member agrees", your data will be shared with the gym.
	+ If you choose "Member declines", your data will not be shared with the gym.
	+ If you choose "Member is not sure", your data will not be processed until your consent is confirmed.
* **Member Email** (`member_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address associated with your membership.
* **Member Phone** (`member_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number associated with your membership.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are accurate and up-to-date to avoid any issues with your membership.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult the gym staff for assistance.
* Remember to review and submit the form carefully before finalizing your changes.
