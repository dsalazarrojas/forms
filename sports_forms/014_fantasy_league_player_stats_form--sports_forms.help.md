# Fantasy League Player Stats Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fantasy League Player Stats Form is designed to collect player stats for a fantasy league. The form is intended for end-users to input information about their favorite players and provide stats for the fantasy league.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by entering the player's information in the "Player Info" section.
2. Next, enter the season information in the "Season Info" section.
3. Enter the player's stats in the "Stats" section.
4. Provide manager information in the "Manager Info" section.
5. Enter your name and email address in the "Form Submitter" section.
6. Finally, enter the submission date and time in the "Submission Date" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Info**: Enter the player's name, number, and position.
	+ Please note that you can enter the player's name, number, and position.
* **Season Info**: Enter the season number.
	+ Please note that this field requires the season number.
* **Stats**: Enter the player's stats, e.g., games played, goals scored, etc.
	+ Please note that this field requires specific stats for the player.
* **Manager Info**: Enter the manager's name and team name.
	+ Please note that this field requires the manager's name and team name.
* **Form Submitter**: Enter your name and email address.
	+ This field is required to track who submitted the form.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date and time of submission.
	+ Please note that this field is not required but recommended for tracking purposes.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that all necessary information is included.
* If you have any issues or questions, please contact us for assistance.
* This form is designed to be completed in a short amount of time, so please be efficient but thorough in your responses.
