# Competition Court Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Competition Court Agreement Form is a crucial document that ensures your team's compliance with court rules and regulations. This form requires information from team officials to verify your team's understanding of and commitment to follow the rules.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your team's official name in the "Team Name" field.
2. Provide your team's captain's name, phone number, and email address as the primary contact information.
3. Select your team's assigned competitive group from the "League or Division" dropdown menu.
4. Read and agree to the "Court Usage and Safety Terms" section, which outlines the rules and expectations for using the competition court.
5. Acknowledge your understanding and agreement to follow all court rules, including equipment usage and cleanup.
6. Confirm that you understand the risk of participation in sports and acknowledge your team's liability.
7. If your team has private liability insurance, select "Yes, fully covered." If not, select "No private insurance."
8. Choose up to three preferred weekly training slots for your team.
9. Provide the name and contact information of your team's emergency contact.
10. Verify the date of agreement by selecting today's date.
11. Finally, submit the form by digitally signing and acknowledging your team's understanding of the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Name** (`team_official_name`, text, required): Enter your team's official registered name.
* **Team Captain Name** (`captain_full_name`, text, required): Provide your team's primary point of contact, usually the team captain.
* **Team Captain Phone Number** (`captain_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your team's primary phone number for urgent court notifications.
* **League or Division** (`division_assigned`, select_one, required): Select your team's assigned competitive group.
* **Court Usage and Safety Terms** (`court_usage_section`, note, required): Read and agree to the terms and conditions for using the competition court.
* **I Agree to Adhere to All Facility and Court Rules** (`court_rules_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Select "Yes, I agree" to confirm your understanding and commitment to follow all court rules.
* **Liability and Injury Waiver** (`safety_waiver_sports`, select_one, required): Acknowledge your understanding of the risk of participation in sports and confirm your liability.
* **Does the Team Have Private Liability Insurance?** (`insurance_coverage_status`, select_one, required): Select "Yes, fully covered" if your team has private liability insurance, or "No private insurance" if not.
* **Preferred Weekly Training Slots** (`preferred_training_slots`, select_multiple, optional): Choose up to three preferred weekly training slots for your team.
* **Team Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_team`, text, required): Provide your team's emergency contact name.
* **Date of Agreement** (`captain_signature_date`, date, required): Select today's date to verify the agreement.
* **Digital Signature Acknowledgment** (`digital_sign_ack_court`, note, required): Confirm your digital signature by acknowledging your understanding of the agreement.
