# Covid Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This Covid waiver form is used to collect patient information, medical history, and consent for vaccinations or treatments in a medical setting.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Contact Information** page:
	* Provide your contact information, including name, phone number, and any other relevant details.
2. Complete the **Patient Information** page:
	* Enter your patient's name and age.
	* Provide details about any existing medical conditions.
3. Review the **Vaccine Eligibility** page:
	* Check if you are eligible for vaccination.
	* Select the type of vaccine administered (if applicable).
4. Fill out the **Consent and Signature** page:
	* Confirm your consent for vaccination or treatment.
	* Sign and date the form.
5. Review and update the **Medical Record** page:
	* Enter any relevant medical record information, such as your medical record number.
	* Confirm the medical provider's signature and details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, optional): Provides space for entering your contact information, such as your name and phone number.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your patient's name.
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, optional): Enter your patient's age.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `text`, optional): Provide details about any existing medical conditions.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact number.
* **Medical Organization** (`medical_organization`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your medical organization or provider.
* **Medical Provider** (`medical_provider`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your medical provider.
* **Vaccine Eligibility** (`vaccine_eligibility`, `date`, optional): Check if you are eligible for vaccination (not applicable for non-medical fields).
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, `date`, optional): The date of your consent.
* **Vaccine Dose** (`vaccine_dose`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose whether you have been administered the vaccine (Yes/No).
* **Vaccine Type** (`vaccine_type`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the type of vaccine administered (if applicable).
* **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your medical record number.
* **Vaccine Administered** (`vaccine_administered`, `select_one`, optional): Confirm if the vaccine was administered (Yes/No).
* **Vaccine Side Effects** (`vaccine_side_effects`, `select_multiple`, optional): Report any vaccine side effects (Yes/No).
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, `text`, optional): Sign and date the form.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional): The date of your signature.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): The time of your signature.
* **Medical Record Review Date** (`medical_record_review_date`, `date`, optional): The date of medical record review.
* **Medical Record Review Time** (`medical_record_review_time`, `time`, optional): The time of medical record review.
* **Patient Signature Date** (`patient_signature_date`, `date`, optional): The date of your patient signature.
* **Medical Provider Signature** (`medical_provider_signature`, `text`, optional): Sign and date the form as the medical provider.
* **Medical Provider Name** (`medical_provider_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the medical provider's name.
* **Medical Provider Title** (`medical_provider_title`, `text`, optional): Enter the medical provider's title.
* **Medical Provider License** (`medical_provider_license`, `text`, optional): Enter the medical provider's license number or details.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
* Review each field carefully to avoid any errors or discrepancies.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask your medical provider.
