# Survivor Pool Participation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for participants to register their interest in joining a survivor pool. It helps us understand who is interested in joining and their eligibility status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your registration form details (page 1).
2. Enter your name (page 2).
3. Enter your email address (page 3).
4. Enter your phone number (page 4).
5. Choose whether you participate in the season (page 5).
6. Confirm your eligibility status for the survivor pool (page 6).
7. Select your weekly picks options (page 7).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form** (page 1): This page allows you to submit your registration details.
* **Name** (page 2): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or other official documents.
* **Email** (page 3): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you about the pool.
* **Phone** (page 4): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Season Participation** (page 5): Choose whether you intend to participate in the season.
	+ 'Yes' to confirm your participation.
	+ 'No' to indicate you are not interested in participating in the season.
* **Eligibility Confirmation** (page 6): Confirm your eligibility status for the survivor pool.
	+ 'Yes' to confirm you meet the pool's requirements.
	+ 'No' to indicate you do not meet the pool's requirements.
* **Weekly Picks** (page 7): Select your weekly picks options.
	+ 'Yes' to choose your picks.
	+ 'No' to not choose your picks.
