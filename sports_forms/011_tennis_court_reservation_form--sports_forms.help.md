# Tennis Court Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tennis Court Reservation Form is a tool for managing and booking tennis court reservations at a tennis club.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the sport you wish to play.
2. Choose the court you want to reserve.
3. Choose the duration of your reservation.
4. Select the date and time of your reservation.
5. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sport** (`sport`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the sport you want to play (e.g. tennis, squash, etc.).
* **Court** (`court`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the tennis court you want to reserve (e.g. court 1, court 2, etc.).
* **Reservation Duration** (`reservation_duration`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the duration of your reservation (e.g. 30 minutes, 1 hour, etc.).
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of your reservation.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Choose the time of your reservation.
* **Reservation Date** (`reservation_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of your reservation.
* **Reservation Time** (`reservation_time`, `time`, required: false): Choose the time of your reservation.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required: false): Click this button to complete and submit your reservation form.

## Tips

* Make sure to select the correct sport and court for your reservation.
* Choose the correct duration, date, and time for your reservation.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please check with a staff member for assistance.
