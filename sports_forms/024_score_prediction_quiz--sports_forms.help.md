# score_prediction_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The score_prediction_quiz form is a sports prediction tool used to collect user's score predictions for a specific match. The goal of this form is to gather accurate and reliable predictions from users to be used in our sports prediction system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
2. For the "prediction" field, select one or more teams from the options provided.
3. Choose the score you believe will be achieved for the match from the options provided.
4. Select the outcome of the match (Win, Lose, or Draw).
5. Indicate your confidence level in your prediction by selecting "High", "Medium", or "Low".
6. Finally, click the "Submit" button to submit your prediction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **user_info** (`text`, required): Enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself that you'd like to share.
* **match_info** (`text`, optional): Provide any additional information about the match you'd like to share.
* **prediction** (`select_multiple`, required): Select one or more teams you believe will score in the match. You can select multiple teams by holding down the Ctrl key (or Command key on a Mac) while clicking on the teams.
* **score** (`select_one`, required): Choose the score you believe will be achieved for the match. The options are '0', '1', and '2'.
* **win_lose_draw** (`select_one`, required): Select the outcome of the match (Win, Lose, or Draw).
* **score_diff** (`number`, optional): If you'd like to provide a more detailed score difference, you can do so here. Note that this field is not required.
* **prediction_confidence** (`select_one`, required): Indicate your confidence level in your prediction by selecting "High", "Medium", or "Low".
* **submit** (`text`, required): Click this button to submit your prediction.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you are filling out the form for the intended purpose and do not duplicate your efforts.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask for clarification.
- Our system will use your prediction data to improve its accuracy, so please be as accurate as possible when filling out the form.
