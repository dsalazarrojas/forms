# General Mountaineering Camp Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The General Mountaineering Camp Application Form is used to gather information from potential camp attendees to assess their eligibility and suitability for the camp.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form in the following order: General Mountaineering Camp Application, First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone, Date of Birth, and Select One Experience.
2. Enter your first name in the First Name field. This is a required field.
3. Enter your last name in the Last Name field. This is a required field.
4. Enter your email address in the Email field. This is a required field.
5. Enter your phone number in the Phone field. This is not a required field.
6. Enter your date of birth in the Date of Birth field. This is a required field.
7. Select one of your mountaineering experiences in the Select One Experience field. This is not a required field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Mountaineering Camp Application** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): A description of the camp. Please provide a brief overview of the camp, including its location, dates, and activities.
* **First Name** (`2`, `text`, required/required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`3`, `text`, required/required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`4`, `email`, required/required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`5`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`6`, `date`, required/required): Enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Select One Experience** (`7`, `text`, required/optional): Select one of your mountaineering experiences. 

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have filled out the form correctly before submitting.
- Double-check your information before submitting to avoid errors.
- If you need assistance or have questions, contact the camp staff for help.
