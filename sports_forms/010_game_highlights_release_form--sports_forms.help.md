# Game Highlights Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect game highlights release information from participants in sports-related activities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter your participant's name and contact information accurately.
3. Select the correct distribution method for your game highlights.
4. Choose the distribution language for your game highlights.
5. Sign and date your consent form.
6. Enter the event ID number.
7. Provide any additional comments or notes for your participant's consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (*): This is the name of the participant who is releasing their game highlights. 
* **Participant Phone**: This is the contact phone number of the participant. 
* **Parent Name (if applicable)**: If the participant is a minor, enter their parent's name.
* **Email**: This is the participant's contact email address. 
* **Participant Consent** (*): Check "Yes" to confirm you have obtained the participant's consent. 
* **Distribution Method** (*): Choose how you want to distribute your game highlights, such as Radio/TV or Social Media. 
* **Distribution Location** (*): Select where you want to distribute your game highlights, such as National or Regional. 
* **Participant Photo**: Upload a photograph of the participant, if desired. 
* **Participant Signature** (*): Sign your name to confirm you are the participant. 
* **Parent Signature (if applicable)**: If the participant is a minor, sign your name as their parent or guardian. 
* **Date** (*): Enter the date of the consent form. 
* **Time** (*): Enter the time of the consent form. 
* **Distribution Date** (*): Enter the date you plan to distribute the game highlights. 
* **Distribution Time** (*): Enter the time of distribution. 
* **Distribution Location** (*): Enter where you plan to distribute the game highlights, such as at a specific location. 
* **Distribution Method (if Other)**: If you selected "Other" for the distribution method, enter the specific method. 
* **Distribution Method (if Other Specify)**: Provide details about the distribution method you chose as "Other". 
* **Parent Name (if not applicable)**: If the participant is an adult, enter your name if you are not a minor. 
* **Event ID** (*): Enter the event ID number. 
* **Participant Age**: Check "Yes" if the participant is above the age of majority. 
* **Participant Gender**: Select the participant's gender, such as Male or Female. 
* **Distribution Language** (*): Choose the language of the distribution, such as English or Spanish. 
* **Participant Consent Note**: Add any additional comments about the participant's consent. 
* **Participant Consent Signed** (*): Check "Yes" to confirm you have obtained the participant's consent. 
* **Participant Consent Date** (*): Enter the date you obtained the participant's consent. 

Note: This form requires all fields with an asterisk (\*) to be filled out.
