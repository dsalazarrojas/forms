# Youth Sports Tryout Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Youth Sports Tryout Evaluation" form is designed to collect information about a youth athlete's performance during a sports tryout. The form is used to assess an athlete's skills, level, attitude, and other relevant details to help coaches and team managers make informed decisions about their participation in a sport.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: * event_type, athlete_name, athlete_position, athlete_skill, and coach_name.*
2. Select the relevant options for *tryout_coach_comments, athlete_ability, tryout_level, athlete_attitude, and team_name.*
3. Enter the tryout date and time (if applicable) in the *tryout_date and tryout_time fields.*
4. Enter any additional comments or coach's comments in the *comments and coach_comments fields.*

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tryout info**: This field is for providing additional information about the tryout, such as the location and event name. (Note field)
* **Athlete Name**: Please enter the name of the athlete being evaluated. (Text field)
* **Tryout Date**: Enter the date of the tryout. (Date field)
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event where the tryout took place. (Text field)
* **Athlete Position**: Select the position or role the athlete played during the tryout. (Select One field)
* **Athlete Position Options:**

	+ Option 6
* **Athlete Ability**: Select the relevant abilities of the athlete. (Select Multiple field)
* **Tryout Coach Comments**: Add any additional comments from the tryout coach. (Note field)
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event where the tryout took place. (Date field)
* **Tryout Location**: Enter the location of the tryout. (Text field)
* **Coach Name**: Enter the name of the tryout coach. (Text field)
* **Tryout Time**: Enter the time of the tryout. (Time field)
* **Athlete Skill**: Select the relevant skills of the athlete. (Select One field)
* **Tryout Level**: Select the relevant level of the tryout. (Select Multiple field)
* **Athlete Attitude**: Select the relevant attitude of the athlete. (Select Multiple field)
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments about the athlete. (Text field)
* **Coach Comments**: Add any additional comments from the coach. (Note field)
* **Event Type**: Enter the type of event where the tryout took place. (Text field)
* **Team Name**: Enter the name of the team the athlete is trying out for. (Text field)
* **Tryout Date 2**: Enter the date of the second tryout (if applicable). (Date field)
* **Tryout Location 2**: Enter the location of the second tryout (if applicable). (Text field)
* **Coach Phone**: Enter the phone number of the coach. (Text field)
* **Coach Email**: Enter the email of the coach. (Email field)
* **Team Name 2**: Enter the name of the second team the athlete is trying out for. (Text field)
* **Coach Name 2**: Enter the name of the second coach. (Text field)
* **Coach Comments 2**: Add any additional comments from the second coach. (Note field)
