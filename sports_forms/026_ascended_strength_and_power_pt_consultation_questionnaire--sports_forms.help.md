# Ascended Strength And Power PT Consultation Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is a consultation questionnaire designed to help assess a client's fitness goals and current state. The purpose of this form is to gather essential information about a client's physical status, medical history, and personal goals to create a personalized fitness plan.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. Answer each question carefully, providing detailed information where necessary.
3. Select all applicable options for your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`client_full_name_pt`): Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Age** (`client_age_pt`): Enter your age as a numerical value.
* **Occupation** (`client_occupation_pt`): If applicable, enter your occupation or relevant information about your work or lifestyle.
* **Primary Fitness Goals** (`fitness_goals_pt`): Select all that apply:
	+ Weight Loss
	+ Muscle Gain/Hypertrophy
	+ Endurance/Stamina
	+ Flexibility/Mobility
	+ Strength/Power
	+ Sport-specific Training
* **Current Activity Level** (`current_activity_level_pt`): Rate your current activity level on a scale of 1-5, where:
	1 - Sedentary (little to no exercise)
	+ 5 - Very active (multiple hours of physical activity per day)
* **Medical History and Previous Injuries** (`medical_history_pt`): List any medical conditions or previous injuries that may affect your exercise routine.
* **General Nutritional Habits** (`nutritional_habits_pt`): Describe your typical daily diet, including eating habits and any dietary restrictions.
* **Average Hours of Sleep per Night** (`sleep_hours_pt`): If applicable, enter the number of hours you typically sleep each night.
* **Current Stress Level** (`stress_level_pt`): Rate your current stress level on a scale of 1-10, where:
	1 - Very low
	+ 10 - Extreme
* **Availability for Personal Training** (`training_availability_pt`): Enter the specific days and times you are available for personal training, e.g., Mon/Wed/Fri mornings.
* **Why Do You Want to Start Training Now?** (`motivation_pt`): Explain why you want to start training and what motivates you to make this change.
