# Player Interest Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect player interest in sports and create a player profile for team management purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "E-mail" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select one or more sports you are interested in from the "Which sports" dropdown list.
6. Select your preferred position from the "What position" dropdown list.
7. Select your level of experience from the "What level" dropdown list.
8. Explain why you want to join in the "Why join" field.
9. Finally, click the "Agree" checkbox to confirm your interest.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`player_interest_form_first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`player_interest_form_last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **E-mail** (`player_interest_form_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`player_interest_form_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Which sports** (`player_interest_form_which_sports`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more sports you are interested in:
	+ Soccer
	+ Basketball
	+ Baseball
	+ Volleyball
	+ Tennis
	+ Golf
* **What position** (`player_interest_form_what_position`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred position:
	+ Pitcher
	+ Catcher
	+ Point Guard
	+ Power Forward
* **What level** (`player_interest_form_which_level`, select_one, optional): Select your level of experience:
	+ Beginner
	+ Intermediate
	+ Advanced
* **Why join** (`player_interest_form_why_join`, text, optional): Explain why you want to join.
* **Agree** (`player_interest_form_agree`, text, optional): Check this box to confirm your interest.
