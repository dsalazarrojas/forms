# Sports Event Logistics Staff Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Event Logistics Staff Application Form is designed to gather information about applicants and their qualifications for sports event logistics staff positions. This form is used to collect data about the applicant's background, experience, and skills.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Staff Member" or "Crew Member" role that you are applying for.
2. Provide your contact information, including your name and email address.
3. Enter your availability dates for the event.
4. Describe your relevant experience and skills, including leadership, communication, and teamwork skills.
5. If applicable, provide referees' names and contact information.
6. List your references (name and contact information).
7. Upload any necessary documents and certifications.
8. Click "Submit" to send your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Info** (`applicant_info`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter your email address and any other relevant contact information.
* **Role Applications** (`role_applications`, select_one, required): Choose the role you are applying for (Staff Member or Crew Member).
* **Availability** (`availability`, date, required): Enter your available dates for the event.
* **Experience** (`experience`, note, required): Describe your relevant experience in 3-4 sentences, including any leadership, communication, and teamwork skills.
* **Skills** (`skills`, select_multiple, required): Select all relevant skills you possess, such as Leadership, Communication, and Teamwork.
* **Referees Info** (`referees_info`, text, not required): Enter the referees' names and contact information, if applicable.
* **References** (`references`, text, required): List the names and contact information of at least two referees who can vouch for your experience and qualifications.
* **Upload Documents** (`upload_documents`, text, not required): Upload any relevant documents, such as ID or qualification certificates.
* **Upload Certifications** (`upload_certifications`, text, not required): Upload any relevant certifications or diplomas.
* **Upload Additional Documents** (`upload_additional_documents`, text, not required): Upload any additional relevant documents.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click to send your application.
