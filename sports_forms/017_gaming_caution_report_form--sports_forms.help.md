# gaming_caution_report_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about incidents that have occurred on a gaming platform. It is intended to help moderators and administrators address potential issues and take appropriate actions. If you are reporting an incident, please fill out this form to provide as much detail as possible.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **User Name** in the first field. This is your username on the platform.
2. If you have a **User ID**, please enter it in the second field. This helps us identify you more accurately.
3. In the third field, select the date of the incident you are reporting.
4. In the fourth field, choose the time of the incident you are reporting.
5. In the fifth field, please enter the **Reason for Report**. This could be a brief explanation of what happened.
6. In the sixth field, provide any additional **Details about incident**. This is the main body of your report.
7. If you were involved in the incident, answer **User Involvement**. Choose 'Yes' if you were involved, and if you were not, select 'No'.
8. If you selected 'Yes' for **User Involvement**, you will be asked to choose from **User Involvement Options**. These are reasons why you may have been involved in the incident.
9. If a moderator was involved in the incident, answer **Moderator Involvement**. Choose 'Yes' if a moderator was involved, and if they were not, select 'No'.
10. If you selected 'Yes' for **Moderator Involvement**, you will be asked to choose from **Moderator Involvement Options**. These are reasons why a moderator may have been involved in the incident.
11. If action was taken against the user, answer **Action Taken**. Choose 'Yes' if action was taken, and if no action was taken, select 'No'.
12. If you selected 'Yes' for **Action Taken**, you will be asked to choose from **Action Taken Options**. These are actions that were taken against the user.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name**: Please enter your username on the platform.
* **User ID**: If you have a unique ID, please enter it to help us identify you more accurately.
* **Incident Date**: Choose the date that the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time**: Choose the time that the incident occurred.
* **Reason for Report**: Briefly explain why you are reporting this incident.
* **Details about incident**: This is the main body of your report, provide as much detail as possible.
* **User Involvement**:
	+ Choose 'Yes' if you were involved in the incident.
	+ Choose 'No' if you were not involved.
* **User Involvement Options**:
	+ If you selected 'Yes' for **User Involvement**, choose one or more of the following options:
		- User was being harassed
		- User was spamming
		- User was trolling
		- User was flaming
		- User was abusive
* **Moderator Involvement**:
	+ Choose 'Yes' if a moderator was involved in the incident.
	+ Choose 'No' if no moderator was involved.
* **Moderator Involvement Options**:
	+ If you selected 'Yes' for **Moderator Involvement**, choose one or more of the following options:
		- Moderator was present
		- Moderator was not present
		- Moderator was involved
		- Moderator was not involved
* **Action Taken**:
	+ Choose 'Yes' if action was taken against the user.
	+ Choose 'No' if no action was taken.
* **Action Taken Options**:
	+ If you selected 'Yes' for **Action Taken**, choose one or more of the following options:
		- Banned User
		- Warning Sent
		- Chat Ban
		- No action taken
