# premier_league_aal1_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Premier League AAL1 Assessment Form is designed to evaluate players' performance and skills in various aspects of football (soccer).

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete the form accurately:

* Read and understand the form's purpose and the sections it encompasses.
* Fill out the form in English, ensuring that all required fields are completed.
* Review your responses carefully before submission to ensure accuracy.
* Use specific examples or anecdotes to support your responses where possible.
* Ensure that all answers are within the allotted text boxes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Player Name (player_name, text, required)**: Enter your name as per your identification documents, or the player's name on your identification documents if you're a referee.
* **Referee Experience (referee_experience, text, optional)**: Describe your refereeing experience, including any relevant certifications, courses or training received.
* **Observation (observation, text, optional)**: Describe any observations you've made about the player's performance, including any notable events or actions.
* **Communication (communication, text, optional)**: Describe your experiences with the player's communication skills, including any interactions or incidents.
* **Game Conduct (game_conduct, text, optional)**: Describe your observations about the player's game conduct, including any issues or concerns.
* **Technical Skills (technical_skills, text, optional)**: Describe the player's technical skills, including their ball handling, passing, shooting, etc.
* **Physical Fitness (physical_fitness, text, optional)**: Describe the player's physical fitness, including their fitness level, endurance, etc.
* **Overall Performance (overall_performance, text, optional)**: Summarize the player's overall performance, highlighting their strengths and weaknesses.
* **Comments (comments, text, optional)**: Add any additional comments or feedback you may have about the player.
* **Additional Comments (additional_comments, text, optional)**: Provide any further comments or insights.
* **Referee Comments (referee_comments, text, optional)**: Share any observations, feedback or comments from the referee or other relevant individuals.

## Tips
* Be honest and objective when providing feedback and assessment.
* Provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your responses.
* Ensure that all answers are within the text box limits.
* Double-check your responses for accuracy and completeness before submission.
* If unsure or unclear about any field, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx) for guidance.
