# Athletic Participation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from students to ensure their participation in sports activities. It collects data on student demographics, medical information, sport selection, and parental consent, among other details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name accurately.
2. Provide your Student ID number (if applicable).
3. Enter your Date of Birth.
4. Select your Grade Level.
5. Enter your School Name.
6. Enter your Email Address and Phone Number (if applicable).
7. Select your Primary Sport and preferred Position or Role.
8. Choose your Years of Experience and current Skill Level.
9. Fill in your Medical Conditions, Current Medications, and Known Allergies (if applicable).
10. Describe any previous sports-related injuries (if applicable).
11. Enter your Parent/Guardian's Name, Email, and Phone Number.
12. Choose your Parent/Guardian's Consent.
13. Acknowledge and accept the liability waivers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, Text, required): Enter your first name accurately.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text, required): Enter your last name accurately.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, Text, optional): If you have a student ID number, enter it.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, Date, required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format.
* **Grade Level** (`grade`, Select One, required): Select your current grade level from the options provided.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, Text, required): Enter your school name accurately.
* **Email Address** (`student_email`, Email, required): Enter your school email address (if applicable).
* **Phone Number** (`student_phone`, Text, optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Primary Sport** (`primary_sport`, Select One, required): Choose the sport you wish to participate in.
* **Position Preference** (`position_preference`, Text, optional): Enter your preferred position or role in the sport.
* **Years of Experience** (`years_experience`, Select One, required): Choose how many years you've played the sport.
* **Skill Level** (`skill_level`, Select One, required): Assess your current skill level from the options provided.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, Text, optional): List any medical conditions or allergies.
* **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, Text, optional): List any medications you are taking.
* **Known Allergies** (`allergies`, Text, optional): List any known allergies.
* **Past Sports Injuries** (`past_injuries`, Text, optional): Describe any previous sports-related injuries.
* **Parent/Guardian Name** (`parent_name`, Text, required): Enter your parent/guardian's name accurately.
* **Parent/Guardian Email** (`parent_email`, Email, required): Enter your parent/guardian's email address (if applicable).
* **Parent/Guardian Phone** (`parent_phone`, Text, required): Enter your parent/guardian's phone number (if applicable).
* **Parent/Guardian Consent** (`parent_consent`, Select One, required): Choose your parent/guardian's consent.
* **Liability Waiver Acknowledgment** (`liability_waiver_acceptance`, Select One, required): Acknowledge and accept all liability waivers.
