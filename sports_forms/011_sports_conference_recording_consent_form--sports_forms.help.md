# Sports Conference Recording Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect participant information and recording details for a sports conference. It is essential to ensure that all participants have reviewed and agreed upon the terms of recording.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand the purpose and requirements.
2. Fill out the required fields accurately and completely.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Details
**Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, optional): This field is used to collect general participant information. Please provide your name and any other details as required.

### Recording Details
**Recording Details** (`recording_details`, text, optional): This field is used to collect details about the recording. Please provide any relevant information about the recording.

### I Consent
**I Consent** (`consent`, select_multiple, optional): Please select 'Yes' if you agree to the terms of recording. You can also select 'No' if you do not agree. This field is optional, but please ensure you have reviewed the terms carefully before selecting 'No'.

### Email
**Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you have an email address, please provide it for future contact purposes.

### Phone
**Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you have a phone number, please provide it for future contact purposes.

### Recording Date
**Recording Date** (`recording_date`, date, optional): Please select a date for the recording.

### Recording Time
**Recording Time** (`recording_time`, time, optional): Please select a time for the recording.

### Recording Location
**Recording Location** (`recording_location`, text, optional): If you have any information about the recording location, please provide it.

### Notes
**Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or notes, please provide them here.

### Consent Date
**Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, optional): Please select a date for your consent.

## Tips
* Please ensure you have reviewed the terms of recording carefully before filling out this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
* If you select 'No' for the 'I Consent' field, your recording will not be made.
