# Sports Fan Experience At Venue Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from sports fans about their experience at a venue after an event. It is essential to provide clear and honest feedback to help enhance the overall fan experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the questions carefully and choose the options that best fit your experience.
2. For text fields, provide a brief explanation of your experience or answer the question as accurately as possible.
3. Choose one option for fields with multiple options, such as event details and staff interactions.
4. Be honest and sincere in your feedback, as it will help us improve the event and venue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Experience
* **General Experience** (Text, Optional): Briefly describe your overall experience at the event. Was it enjoyable, entertaining, or memorable?

### Event Details
* **Event Details** (Select One, Required): Choose the best option that describes your experience at the event. Options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Bad

### Venue Details
* **Venue Details** (Text, Optional): Describe the venue and its surroundings. Was it clean, comfortable, and well-organized?

### Food and Drink
* **Food and Drink** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose the best options that describe your experience with food and drink at the event. Options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Bad

### Staff Interactions
* **Staff Interactions** (Text, Optional): Describe your interactions with the staff. Were they friendly, helpful, and professional?

### Event Value
* **Event Value** (Select One, Required): Choose the best option that describes your overall value for the event. Options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Bad

### Staff Friendly
* **Staff Friendly** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose the best options that describe your experience with the staff's friendliness. Options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Bad

### Communication
* **Communication** (Text, Optional): Describe the communication with the staff and organizers. Were they clear, concise, and prompt in their response?

### Overall Satisfaction
* **Overall Satisfaction** (Select One, Required): Choose the best option that describes your overall satisfaction with the event. Options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Bad

### Staff Professionalism
* **Staff Professionalism** (Text, Optional): Describe the staff's professionalism, including their attire, behavior, and attitude.

### Staff Knowledge
* **Staff Knowledge** (Text, Optional): Describe the staff's knowledge about the event, including their understanding of the schedule, rules, and procedures.

### Value for Money
* **Value for Money** (Text, Optional): Describe your experience with the value for money, including the ticket prices, concessions, and overall affordability.

### Staff Willingness

### Event Atmosphere

### Event Satisfaction

### Staff Courteousness
