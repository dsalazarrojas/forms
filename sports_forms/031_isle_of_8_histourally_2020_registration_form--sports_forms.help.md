# isle_of_8_histourally_2020_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The isle_of_8_histourally_2020_registration_form is used to register participants for the 2020 Isle of 8 Historically event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your name and contact information on page 1.
2. Select your event registration details on page 2.
3. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name (Page 1)
* **First Name** (`text`, required: false): Enter your first name.

### Last Name (Page 1)
* **Last Name** (`text`, required: false): Enter your last name.

### Email (Page 1)
* **Email** (`email`, required: false): Enter your email address.

### Phone (Page 1)
* **Phone** (`text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.

### Date of Birth (Page 1)
* **Date of Birth** (`date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth.

### Address (Page 1)
* **Address** (`text`, required: false): Enter your address.

### Event Type (Page 2)
* **Event Type** (`select_one`, required: false):
	+ Select the type of event you are participating in (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).

### Event Subtype (Page 2)
* **Event Subtype** (`select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Select one or more subtypes of the event type you chose above (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).

### Team Name (Page 2)
* **Team Name** (`text`, required: false): Enter your team name.

### Team Member (Page 2)
* **Team Member** (`select_one`, required: true):
	+ Select the member of your team (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).

### Registration Date (Page 2)
* **Registration Date** (`date`, required: false): Enter the date you registered for the event.

Note: The form is designed to be filled in multiple pages. Please ensure that you complete each section accurately and proceed to the next page for the event registration details.
