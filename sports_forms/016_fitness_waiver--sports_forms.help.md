# Fitness Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Fitness Waiver form is designed to collect vital information from individuals who engage in physical activities or participate in fitness programs. This form is intended to ensure that the individual is aware of and agrees to certain terms and conditions related to their participation, including medical information and emergency contact details. By completing this form, you will be providing essential details that help us provide you with a safe and healthy experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please carefully read the form instructions and questions carefully.
2.  Fill out the form completely and accurately, especially for the fields marked as "required".
3.  If you are unsure about any field, please ask for assistance or clarification.
4.  Review your completed form before submission to ensure it is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User info**: This is the first section of the form where you can enter your basic information.
    *   **User info** (`note`, required/optional): A brief description of the user information section is not provided as it is a note field.
*   **Emergency Contact**: Please enter your emergency contact's name and other relevant details.
    *   **Emergency Contact** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
*   **Name** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
*   **Relationship** (`select_one`, required/optional): Choose the relationship between you and the emergency contact (e.g., Mother, Father, Brother, Sister, Spouse, Friend, Other).
*   **Phone** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
*   **Address** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the address of the emergency contact.
*   **Email** (`email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the emergency contact.
*   **Other Contact Info** (`text`, required/optional): If you have other contact information, enter it here.
*   **Type of Other Contact Info** (`select_one`, required/optional): Choose the type of other contact information (e.g., Home, Work, Cell, Other).
*   **Other Contact Info Value** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the value of the other contact information.
*   **Other Contact Info 2** (`text`, required/optional): Enter any additional contact information.
*   **Medical Info**: Please provide any relevant medical information.
    *   **Medical Info** (`text`, required/optional): Enter any medical information you would like to share.
*   **Medical Condition** (`text`, required/optional): If you have any medical conditions, enter them here.
*   **Medical Procedure** (`text`, required/optional): If you have undergone any medical procedures, enter them here.
*   **Medical Insurance** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your medical insurance details.
*   **Medical Insurance Provider** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your medical insurance provider.
*   **Medical Info Provider**: Please provide details about your medical information provider.
    *   **Medical Info Provider** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your medical information provider.
*   **Type of Medical Info Provider** (`select_one`, required/optional): Choose the type of medical information provider (e.g., Doctor, Hospital, Clinic, Other).
*   **Other Medical Info Provider** (`text`, required/optional): Enter any additional medical information provider details.
*   **Other Medical Info Provider 2** (`text`, required/optional): Enter any further medical information provider details.
*   **Type of Other Medical Info Provider** (`select_one`, required/optional): Choose the type of other medical information provider (e.g., Doctor, Hospital, Clinic, Other).
*   **Other Medical Info Provider Value** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the value of the other medical information provider.
*   **Sign-up Date** (`date`, required/optional): Enter the date you signed up for the fitness activities or programs.
*   **Sign-up Time** (`time`, required/optional): Enter the time you signed up for the fitness activities or programs.

By following these instructions and filling out the form accurately, you will be providing us with necessary details to ensure your participation in our fitness activities or programs is safe and enjoyable.
