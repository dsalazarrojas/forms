# Personal Training Registration Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from clients interested in or already in a training program at the sports facility. Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a safe and effective training experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field with accurate and truthful information.
2. Be sure to check the required fields indicated by the asterisk (\*).
3. Submit the form once all fields have been completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name
• **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it is commonly known.
 
### Last Name
• **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your surname.

### Email
• **Email** (`email`, text, email, required): Enter your contact email address for communication purposes.

### Birth Date
• **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (DD-MM-YYYY or MM-DD-YYYY).

### Contact Phone
• **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number for emergency contact purposes if necessary.

### Emergency Contact Name
• **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.

### Emergency Contact Phone
• **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.

### Health Insurance Provider
• **Health Insurance Provider** (`health_insurance_provider`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your health insurance provider information.

### Health Insurance Provider Address
• **Health Insurance Provider Address** (`health_insurance_provider_address`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your health insurance provider's address.

### Fitness Goals
• **Fitness Goals** (`fitness_goals`, text, optional): Describe your fitness goals and objectives.

### Health History
• **Health History** (`health_history`, text, optional): Share any health history that may affect your training.

### Allergies
• **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): List any allergies or medical conditions.

### Meds
• **Meds** (`medications`, text, optional): List any medications or supplements you are currently taking.

### Relationship with Emergency Contact
• **Relationship with Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person.

### Medical Conditions
• **Medical Conditions** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): List any medical conditions that may affect your training.

### Emergency Contact Permission
• **Emergency Contact Permission** (`emergency_contact_permission`, text, optional): Grant permission to contact the emergency contact person.

### Emergency Contact Signature
• **Emergency Contact Signature** (`emergency_contact_signature`, text, optional): The emergency contact person signs to confirm permission.

### Client Signature
• **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, text, required): The client's signature confirms they have read and understood the form. 

Please ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
