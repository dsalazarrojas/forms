# Player Trading Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Player Trading Form is used to record and manage player trading activities within sports leagues, such as MLB, NFL, NBA, and NHL. This form is intended for players, coaches, or team administrators to report player trades, transfers, or other activities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the league and team involved in the trade from the respective drop-down menus.
2. Choose the players involved in the trade using the select multiple options.
3. Enter the contract length and trade price for the players involved.
4. Select the type of trade (e.g., trade, draft).
5. Enter the date of the trade.
6. Select the players for Team A and Team B.
7. Enter the trade value (optional).
8. Enter additional contract length and trade value fields (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Information (player_information)**: Enter the name or details of the player involved in the trade.
* **League (select_one_league)**: Select the league where the trade is taking place (e.g., MLB, NFL, NBA, or NHL).
* **Team (select_one_team)**: Choose the team trading or being traded away (e.g., Yankees, Dodgers, Lakers, or Celtics).
* **Players (select_multiple_players)**: Select the players involved in the trade using the multiple select options.
* **Position (position)**: Choose the position of the player (e.g., Quarterback, Running Back, or Wide Receiver).
* **Contract Length (contract_length)**: Enter the length of the contract for the player in years (e.g., 1 year, 2 years, 3 years).
* **Trade Price (trade_price)**: Enter the trade price of the player (optional).
* **Date of Trade (date)**: Enter the date of the trade.
* **Trade Type (trade_type)**: Select the type of trade (e.g., trade or draft).
* **Transfer Type (transfer_type)**: Not applicable in this form, please ignore.
* **Trade Details (trade_details)**: Enter additional details about the trade (e.g., trade conditions).
* **Team A (select_one_team_a)**: Select the team trading away the player (e.g., Yankees, Dodgers, Lakers, or Celtics).
* **Team B (select_one_team_b)**: Select the team trading for the player (e.g., Yankees, Dodgers, Lakers, or Celtics).
* **Players A (players_a)**: Select the players on Team A involved in the trade using the multiple select options.
* **Players B (players_b)**: Select the players on Team B involved in the trade using the multiple select options.
* **Date2 (date2)**: Enter the date of the trade (second instance, optional).
* **Trade Value (trade_value)**: Enter the trade value (optional).
* **Trade Value 2 (trade_value2)**: Enter the trade value (second instance, optional).
* **Contract Length 2 (contract_length2)**: Enter the contract length (second instance, optional).
* **Contract Length 3 (contract_length3)**: Enter the contract length (third instance, optional).
* **Date3 (date3)**: Not applicable in this form, please ignore.
* **Trade Value 3 (trade_value3)**: Not applicable in this form, please ignore.
* **Trade Price 2 (trade_price2)**: Not applicable in this form, please ignore.

Note: The additional fields (e.g., Contract Length 2, Trade Value 2, etc.) seem to be duplicates or alternatives, but their purpose is not clear. Please use caution when filling out these fields and refer to the team administrator or player representative for clarification.
