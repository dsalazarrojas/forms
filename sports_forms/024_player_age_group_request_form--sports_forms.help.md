# Player Age Group Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Player Age Group Request Form is designed to collect demographic information from players for team or team-building purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps listed above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Age Group** (`player_age_group`, select_one):
	+ Select the age group that best matches the player's age. The options include:
		- 8U
		- 9U
		- 10U
		- 11U
		- 12U
		- 13U
		- 14U
		- 15U
		- 16U
		- 17U
		- 18U
* **First Name** (`player_first_name`, text):
	+ Enter the player's first name.
* **Last Name** (`player_last_name`, text):
	+ Enter the player's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`player_date_of_birth`, date):
	+ Enter the player's date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Gender** (`player_gender`, select_one):
	+ Select the player's gender. Options include:
		- Male
		- Female
		- Non-binary
		- Prefer not to specify
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text):
	+ If the player has a parent or guardian, enter their name.
* **Email** (`email`, email):
	+ Enter the player's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text):
	+ Enter the player's phone number.
* **School** (`school`, text):
	+ Enter the player's school name.
