# Life Vest Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Life Vest Survey is designed to gather information about your experiences with life vests. This form helps us understand your preferences, habits, and satisfaction with life vests. Please answer the questions accurately and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each question.
2. Answer all questions accurately and honestly.
3. Provide as much detail as possible for text-based fields.
4. Select the correct options for select fields (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
5. Submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (first_name, text, optional): Please enter your first name as it is written on your official documents or ID.
* **Last Name** (last_name, text, optional): Please enter your last name as it is written on your official documents or ID.
* **Email** (email, email, optional): Please enter your email address.
* **Life Vest Type** (life_vest_type, select_one, optional): Please select the type of life vest you have used or would like to use:
	+ 'Yes'
* **Usage** (usage, select_multiple, optional): Please select all that apply regarding your usage of life vests:
	+ 'Yes'
* **Fit** (fit, select_one, optional): Please select your experience with the fit of your life vest:
	+ 'Yes'
* **Size** (size, select_one, optional): Please select your experience with the size of your life vest:
	+ 'Yes'
* **Color** (color, text, optional): Please describe the color of your life vest.
* **Brand** (brand, text, optional): Please enter the brand of your life vest.
* **Purchase Channel** (purchase_channel, select_one, optional): Please select how you purchased your life vest:
	+ 'Yes'
* **Purchase Year** (purchase_year, text, optional): Please enter the year you purchased your life vest.
* **Purchase Price** (purchase_price, text, optional): Please enter the price you paid for your life vest.
* **Purchase Place** (purchase_place, text, optional): Please enter where you purchased your life vest.
* **Use Vest** (use_vest, text, optional): Please describe how you use your life vest.
* **Like Vest** (like_vest, text, optional): Please describe what you like about your life vest.
* **Dislike Vest** (dislike_vest, text, optional): Please describe what you dislike about your life vest.
* **Why Not Like** (why_not_like, text, optional): Please explain why you do not like your life vest.
