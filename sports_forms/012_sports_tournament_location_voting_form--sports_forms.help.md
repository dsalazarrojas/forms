# sports_tournament_location_voting_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sports_tournament_location_voting_form is a tool used to collect user preferences for the location and schedule of a sports tournament.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review each field carefully and ensure you have provided accurate information.
2. Enter the name of the location you prefer for the tournament, if it is available in the provided options.
3. Select your preferred start time from the available options.
4. Confirm your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
5. Provide any additional notes or comments you would like to include.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tournament Location** (`tournament_location`, text, optional): Enter the name of the preferred location for the tournament. This could be a specific stadium, arena, or field.
* **Preferred Location** (`preferred_location`, text, optional): Enter the name of the specific location you prefer. If you are unsure about the location, you can enter the name of a nearby city or town.
* **Other Location Details** (`other_location_details`, text, optional): If you have any additional information about the preferred location, such as its address or specific features, enter it here.
* **Venue Capacity** (`venue_capacity`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the capacity of the venue you prefer. This could be the maximum number of attendees or spectators the venue can accommodate.
* **Preferred Venue Type** (`preferred_venue_type`, select multiple, optional): Select all the types of venues you would prefer for the tournament. This could include arenas, stadiums, or outdoor fields.
* **Date and Time** (`date_and_time`, date, optional): Enter the date and time of the tournament you prefer.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time you prefer for the tournament.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time you prefer for the tournament.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, select one, optional): Select your preferred start time for the tournament from the available options.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you if needed.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about your preferences.

## Tips
* Ensure you are entering accurate and up-to-date information.
* Please select all relevant options in the "Preferred Venue Type" field if you have multiple preferences.
* Use a standard format for dates and times (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY HH:MM AM/PM).
