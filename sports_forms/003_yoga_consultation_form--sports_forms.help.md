# Yoga Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from clients before a yoga consultation. It helps our team understand the client's medical conditions, allergies, and other important details to provide a safe and effective experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the form's submit button to begin.
2. Fill out all required fields (indicated by *).
3. Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
4. Submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Medical Conditions** (text, required): Describe any medical conditions you have, including past and current conditions.
* **Allergies** (text, optional): List any allergies or sensitivities you have.
* **Meditations** (text, optional): List any medications or supplements you are currently taking.
* **Emergency Contact** (text, optional): Provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
* **Studio Information** (text, optional): Share any additional information about your studio experience, such as previous classes taken or workshops attended.
* **Instructor Information** (text, required): Briefly describe your experience with our instructor, including the type of classes you've taken and any specific goals or objectives.
* **Class Information** (text, optional): Share any specific class preferences or goals you'd like to achieve in your consultation.
* **Consent** (text, required): Confirm that you have read and understand the terms of our studio's policies and procedures.
* **Medical Consent** (text, required): Confirm that you have read and understand the medical consent statement and any specific conditions or limitations you have.
* **Insurance Provider** (text, optional): If you have insurance, enter the name of your insurance provider.
* **Policy Number** (text, optional): If you have insurance, enter your policy number.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
