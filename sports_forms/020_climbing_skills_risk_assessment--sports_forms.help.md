# Climbing Skills Risk Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Climbing Skills Risk Assessment form is designed to evaluate climbers' skills, experience, and safety awareness to identify potential risks and ensure a safe climbing environment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth, Age, Email Address, and Phone Number. These fields are required to verify your identity.
2. Choose your Climbing Experience Level from the options provided, which range from Beginner - No Experience to Expert - Over 5 Years.
3. Select all the climbing disciplines you have experience in (e.g., Bouldering, Top Rope, Lead Climbing, etc.).
4. If you have any current injuries, select "True" and provide details in the "Injury Details" field.
5. If you have any relevant medical conditions, describe them in the "Relevant Medical Conditions" field.
6. Choose your Overall Fitness Level from the options provided.
7. Answer how often you climb (e.g., First Time, Less Than Once a Month, 1-3 Times Per Month, etc.).
8. Select your understanding of Climbing Risks from the options provided.
9. Choose your Knowledge of Safety Equipment from the options provided.
10. If you inspect equipment before use, select the frequency (e.g., Always, Usually, Sometimes, Rarely, Never).
11. Describe your Climbing Goals in the text field.
12. Select the Risk Score provided by the assessor.
13. If you have signed a liability waiver, select "True".
14. Provide any additional comments or information in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Age** (`age`, number, required): Enter your age.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Climbing Experience Level** (`climbing_experience`, select_one, required): Choose from Beginner - No Experience, Beginner - Less Than 6 Months, Intermediate - 6 Months to 2 Years, Advanced - 2 to 5 Years, Expert - Over 5 Years.
* **Climbing Disciplines Practiced** (`climbing_disciplines`, select_multiple, required): Select all the climbing disciplines you have experience in, e.g., Bouldering, Top Rope, Lead Climbing, etc.
* **Highest Bouldering Grade Completed** (`highest_grade_boulder`, select_one, optional): Choose the highest bouldering grade you have completed, e.g., V0-V1, V2-V3, etc.
* **Highest Sport Climbing Grade Completed** (`highest_grade_sport`, select_one, optional): Choose the highest sport climbing grade you have completed, e.g., 5.6-5.8, 5.9-5.10, etc.
* **Do You Have Belay Certification** (`belay_certification`, select_one, optional): Select from Yes Current, Yes Expired, False, Not Applicable.
* **Belay Devices You Can Use** (`belay_device_types`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the types of belay devices you can use, e.g., Tube Style (ATC), Assisted Braking (GriGri), etc.
* **Lead Climbing Experience** (`lead_climbing_experience`, select_one, required): Choose from No Experience, Beginner - Learning, Intermediate - Can Lead Indoors, Advanced - Can Lead Outdoors, Expert - Multi-Pitch Lead.
* **Have You Taken Significant Falls** (`number_of_falls`, select_one, optional): Select from Never, 1-2 Minor Falls, Multiple Minor Falls, 1-2 Significant Falls, Multiple Significant Falls.
* **Climbing-Related Injury History** (`injury_history`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the types of climbing-related injuries you have experienced, e.g., Finger Injuries, Shoulder Injuries, etc.
* **Do You Have Any Current Injuries** (`current_injuries`, select_one, required): Select from True, False.
* **Injury Details** (`injury_details`, text, optional): If you selected "True" in the previous field, describe your current injuries in this field.
* **Relevant Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): Describe any relevant medical conditions you may have.
* **Overall Fitness Level** (`fitness_level`, select_one, required): Choose from Sedentary, Lightly Active, Moderately Active, Very Active, Extremely Active.
* **How Often Do You Climb** (`climbing_frequency`, select_one, required): Choose from First Time, Less Than Once a Month, 1-3 Times Per Month, Once a Week, 2-3 Times Per Week, 4+ Times Per Week.
* **Do You Understand Climbing Risks** (`risk_awareness`, select_one, required): Select from Yes Completely, Yes Mostly, Somewhat, Not Really, False.
* **Knowledge of Safety Equipment** (`safety_equipment_knowledge`, select_one, required): Choose from Expert Level, Advanced, Intermediate, Basic, Beginner.
* **Do You Inspect Equipment Before Use** (`equipment_inspection`, select_one, required): Select from Always, Usually, Sometimes, Rarely, Never.
* **What Are Your Climbing Goals** (`climbing_goals`, text, optional): Describe your climbing goals.
* **Assessment Score** (`assessment_score`, select_one, optional): Choose from Low Risk, Moderate Risk, Elevated Risk, High Risk.
* **Assessor Name** (`assessor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the assessor who completed the assessment.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Liability Waiver Signed** (`waiver_signed`, select_one, required): Select from True, False.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately and honestly.
* Select all the applicable options for climbing disciplines and experience levels.
* Be as detailed as possible when describing your climbing goals and any injuries or medical conditions.
* Review the assessment score provided by the assessor and ensure it accurately reflects your climbing abilities and risks.
