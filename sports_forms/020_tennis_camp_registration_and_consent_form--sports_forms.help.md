# Tennis Camp Registration And Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for parents or guardians to register their child for a tennis camp.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately with your child's information.
2. Please ensure all required fields are completed before proceeding.
3. Use clear and legible handwriting for all fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your child's first name as they will be known at the tennis camp.
	+ Please use your child's first name in the required format (e.g., John, Mary).
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: true): Please provide your child's last name as they will be known at the tennis camp.
	+ Please use your child's last name in the required format (e.g., Smith, Johnson).
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Please provide your child's email address for communication purposes.
	+ Please use a valid email address that we can use to contact you and your child.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: true): Please provide your child's phone number for emergency contact purposes.
	+ Please use a valid phone number that we can use to contact you and your child.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: true): Please provide your child's address for emergency contact purposes.
	+ Please use the address in the required format (e.g., 123 Main St, Apt 101).
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): Please provide your child's date of birth for age verification purposes.
	+ Please use the date in the required format (e.g., 02/12/1999).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your emergency contact's name.
	+ Please use your name and contact information for emergency purposes.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, required: false): Please specify your relationship to the emergency contact.
	+ Please choose the relationship that best describes your connection (e.g., spouse, parent, guardian).
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, `note`, required: false): Please sign and date this field to confirm your consent.
	+ Please print your name and date in the required format (e.g., John Smith, 02/12/2023).
* **Camper Signature** (`camper_signature`, `note`, required: false): Please sign and date this field to confirm your camper's consent.
	+ Please print your child's name and date in the required format (e.g., John Smith, 02/12/2023).
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_condition`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select any medical conditions that apply to your child.
	+ Please choose from the provided options or add "None" if no conditions apply.
* **Allergies** (`allergy`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select any allergies that apply to your child.
	+ Please choose from the provided options or add "None" if no allergies apply.
* **Medical Conditions Note** (`medical_condition_note`, `text`, required: false): Please provide any additional information about your child's medical conditions.
	+ Please use clear and legible handwriting for this field.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your emergency contact's phone number.
	+ Please use a valid phone number that we can use to contact you and your child.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, `email`, required: false): Please provide your emergency contact's email address.
	+ Please use a valid email address that we can use to contact you and your child.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Note** (`emergency_contact_relationship_note`, `text`, required: false): Please provide any additional information about your emergency contact's relationship.
	+ Please use clear and legible handwriting for this field.
* **Camper Dietary Needs** (`camper_diet`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select your child's dietary needs.
	+ Please choose from the provided options or add "None" if no dietary needs apply.
* **Emergency Contact Permission** (`emergency_contact_permission`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select whether you give permission to contact your emergency contact.
	+ Please choose from the provided options or select "None" if no permission is given.
* **Parent Permission** (`parent_permission`, `select_one`, required: true): Please select whether you give permission for your child to attend the tennis camp.
	+ Please choose from the provided options or select "None" if no permission is given.
* **Medical Permission** (`medical_permission`, `select_one`, required: true): Please select whether you give permission for your child to receive medical treatment if needed.
	+ Please choose from the provided options or select "None" if no permission is given.
* **Medical Permission Note** (`medical_permission_note`, `text`, required: false): Please provide any additional information about your child's medical permission.
	+ Please use clear and legible handwriting for this field.
* **Camper Attendance** (`camper_attendance`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select your child's attendance frequency.
	+ Please choose from the provided options or add "None" if no attendance frequency applies.
* **Camper Start Date** (`camper_start_date`, `date`, required: false): Please provide your child's start date for the tennis camp.
	+ Please use the date in the required format (e.g., 02/12/2023).
* **Camper End Date** (`camper_end_date`, `date`, required: false): Please provide your child's end date for the tennis camp.
	+ Please use the date in the required format (e.g., 02/19/2023).
* **Camp Name** (`camp_name`, `text`, required: false): Please provide the name of the tennis camp your child will be attending.
	+ Please use clear and legible handwriting for this field.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form carefully and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask the camp staff for assistance.
* Please use clear and legible handwriting for all fields.
