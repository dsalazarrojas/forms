# Youth Sports Publicity Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Sports Publicity Consent Form is a document that allows parents or guardians to provide consent for their child's image and likeness to be used in publicity and promotional materials. This form ensures that the child's identity and image are not used without their parent's or guardian's permission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for the parent or guardian:
	* **Parent Name**: Enter the name of the parent or guardian.
	* **Parent Email**: Enter the email address of the parent or guardian.
	* **Parent Phone**: Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian.
	* **Date**: Enter the date on which the form is being submitted.
2. Provide information about the athlete:
	* **Athlete Name**: Enter the name of the athlete.
	* **Athlete Image**: Enter a description or reference to the image being used.
3. Choose the publicity permission and release:
	* **Publicity Permission**: Select one of the three options (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3) to indicate whether the parent or guardian gives permission for their child's image and likeness to be used in publicity and promotional materials.
	* **Publicity Permission Date**: Enter the date on which the parent or guardian grants the permission.
	* **Photo Release**: Select one of the three options (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3) to indicate whether the parent or guardian gives permission for photos to be taken and used in publicity and promotional materials.
	* **Photo Credit**: Enter the name of the photographer or person taking the photos.
	* **Publicity Release**: Select one or more of the three options (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3) to indicate whether the parent or guardian grants permission for the child's image and likeness to be used in publicity and promotional materials.
	* **Photo Copyright**: Select one or more of the three options (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3) to indicate the copyright status of the photos.
	* **Photo Use**: Select one or more of the three options (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3) to indicate the specific use of the photos.
	* **Photo Rights**: Select one or more of the three options (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3) to indicate the rights of the photos.
4. Choose the publicity details:
	* **Publicity Name**: Enter the name of the publicity agency or organization using the child's image and likeness.
	* **Publicity Position**: Enter the position or title of the person at the publicity agency or organization.
	* **Publicity Agency**: Enter the name of the publicity agency or organization.
	* **Publicity Email**: Enter the email address of the publicity agency or organization.
	* **Publicity Phone**: Enter the phone number of the publicity agency or organization.
	* **Publicity Date**: Enter the date on which the publicity materials will be used.
	* **Publicity Time**: Enter the time at which the publicity materials will be used.
	* **Publicity Note**: Add any additional notes or comments about the publicity materials.
5. Review and sign the publicity agreement:
	* **Publicity Agreement**: Select "Yes" to indicate that the parent or guardian has read, understands, and agrees to the publicity terms and conditions.
