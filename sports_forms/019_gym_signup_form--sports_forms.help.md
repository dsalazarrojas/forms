# Gym Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gym Signup Form is a simple, one-page form designed to collect basic information from users who want to sign up for a gym membership. This form includes fields for personal and emergency contact information, as well as details about the member's gym plan and membership duration.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the second field.
3. Provide your **Email** address in the third field (this should be a valid email address).
4. Enter your **Birth Date** (in the format MM/DD/YYYY) in the fourth field.
5. Enter your **Phone Number** in the fifth field (no special characters or symbols, please).
6. Select your preferred **Gym Plan** from the dropdown list (Basic, Premium, or Elite).
7. Choose the **Membership Duration** from the dropdown list (Month-to-Month, Month-to-Month (12 months), Month-to-Month (24 months), or Month-to-Month (36 months)).
8. Enter the **Emergency Contact Name** in the next field (this can be a family member, friend, or anyone else you'd like to have contact in case of emergencies).
9. Enter the **Emergency Contact Phone Number** in the following field (no special characters or symbols, please).
10. Select the **Emergency Contact Relation** from the dropdown list (Father, Mother, Brother, Sister, Spouse, Friend, or Family Member).
11. If you selected "Other" as your Emergency Contact Relation, enter the **Emergency Contact Relation Other** in the field below.
12. Enter your **Address**, including street number and apartment number (if applicable).
13. Enter your **City** in the next field.
14. Enter your **State** in the following field.
15. Enter your **Zip Code** in the field below.
16. For emergency contact, enter your **Emergency Contact Address** (including street number and apartment number, if applicable).
17. Enter your **Emergency Contact City** in the next field.
18. Enter your **Emergency Contact State** in the following field.
19. Enter your **Emergency Contact Zip Code** in the field below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your personal name. This is the first name that will be associated with your membership.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name. This is the surname associated with your membership.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for important updates and communications.
* **Birth Date**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/2000).
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number without special characters or symbols.
* **Gym Plan**: Choose your preferred gym plan from the dropdown list (Basic, Premium, or Elite).
* **Membership Duration**: Select the duration of your membership from the dropdown list (Month-to-Month, Month-to-Month (12 months), Month-to-Month (24 months), or Month-to-Month (36 months)).
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relation**: Select the relation of the emergency contact person from the dropdown list (Father, Mother, Brother, Sister, Spouse, Friend, or Family Member).
* **Emergency Contact Relation Other**: If you selected "Other" as your Emergency Contact Relation, enter the custom relation.
* **Address**: Enter your address, including street number and apartment number (if applicable).
* **City**: Enter the city where you live.
* **State**: Enter your state of residence.
* **Zip Code**: Enter your zip code.
* **Emergency Contact Address**: Enter the address of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact City**: Enter the city where the emergency contact person lives.
* **Emergency Contact State**: Enter the state where the emergency contact person lives.
* **Emergency Contact Zip Code**: Enter the zip code of the emergency contact person.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by \*\*asterisk\*\*).
* Ensure that your email address is valid and can be reached for important updates.
* For emergency contact information, enter accurate and up-to-date details.
* If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to ask our support team.
