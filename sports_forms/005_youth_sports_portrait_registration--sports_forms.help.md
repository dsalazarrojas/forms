# youth sports portrait registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The youth sports portrait registration form is designed to collect information from parents or guardians about their child's participation in youth sports. This information will be used to create a digital portrait of the athlete and provide insights for coaches, teams, and other stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the athlete's details in the "Athlete Details" section.
2. Provide the parent's contact information in the "Parent" section.
3. Select the photo package that suits your preference in the "Photo Package" section.
4. Check the box to confirm that you have obtained consent from the parents in the "Consent" section.
5. Enter the athlete's email address in the "Email" section.
6. Enter the school name in the "School Name" section.
7. Review and submit the form to complete the registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Details** (`athlete_details`, text, required: false): Enter the athlete's name, date of birth, and other relevant details to create a comprehensive profile.
* **Parent** (`parent_details`, text, required: false): Provide the parent's name, contact information, and consent to participate in the program. Include the parent's name, phone number, and address.
* **Photo Package** (`photo_package`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the photo package that suits your preference. The options are 8 x 10, 12 x 20, or 24 x 36.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required: false): Check the box to confirm that you have obtained consent from the parents to participate in the program.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the athlete's email address to enable communication and updates.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the school the athlete attends to create a comprehensive profile.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* Take your time to ensure that all information is correct.
* If you need help or have questions, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
