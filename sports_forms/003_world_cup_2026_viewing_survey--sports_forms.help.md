# World Cup 2026 Viewing Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about your viewing preferences and habits related to the World Cup 2026. Your responses will help us better understand the level of interest and engagement among sports fans. Your participation is voluntary and anonymous.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability, providing as much detail as needed.
2. Select your preferred options from the available choices.
3. Enter your email address and phone number if you are comfortable sharing them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How often do you plan to watch the World Cup? (viewing_plans)**:
	+ This field asks about your watching habits for the World Cup event. Choose one or more of the options to best describe your plans.
* **How excited are you about the World Cup? (excitement_level)**:
	+ This field measures your level of enthusiasm for the World Cup. Choose the option that best reflects your feelings.
* **Who is your favorite player? (favorite_player)**:
	+ If you have a favorite player, enter their name. If not, please leave this field blank.
* **Which team do you support? (favorite_team)**:
	+ Select the team that you are most interested in or feel loyal to.
* **If other, which team is it? (favorite_team_other)**:
	+ If your favorite team is not listed, enter the name of the team you support.
* **Who will you be watching with? (watching_with)**:
	+ Choose with whom you will be watching the World Cup matches.
* **If other, who is it? (favorite_player_other)**:
	+ If you will be watching with someone not listed, enter their name.
* **How old are you? (age)**:
	+ Enter your age to help us understand your demographic.
* **What country do you live in? (country)**:
	+ Select the country where you reside.
* **If other, which country do you live in? (country_other)**:
	+ If your country is not listed, enter the name of your country.
* **How often do you receive sports news updates via email? (email)**:
	+ This field measures your preference for sports news updates via email.
* **How often do you receive sports news updates via phone? (phone_number)**:
	+ This field measures your preference for sports news updates via phone.
* **How often do you use social media for sports news? (social_media)**:
	+ This field measures your preference for sports news updates on social media.
* **What is your email address? (email_address)**:
	+ Enter your email address if you are comfortable sharing it.
* **What is your phone number? (phone_number_number)**:
	+ Enter your phone number if you are comfortable sharing it.

**Tips**

* Be honest in your answers, and feel free to provide any additional information that you think is relevant to your responses.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
