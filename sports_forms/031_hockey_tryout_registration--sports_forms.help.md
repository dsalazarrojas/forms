# hockey_tryout_registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The hockey tryout registration form is designed to collect information from interested players and their families for a hockey tryout session.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your player's name and birth date in the corresponding fields.
3. Choose your player's position from the dropdown menu.
4. Enter any additional questions or comments in the "Questions or Comments" fields.
5. Review and submit your form.
