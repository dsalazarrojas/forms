# race_sponsor_booth_material_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Race Sponsor Booth Material Order Form" is used by race sponsors to order and manage booth material deliveries for events. This form ensures that all necessary details are captured and that materials are delivered correctly and on schedule.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the "Event Details" field with any relevant information about the event.
2. Select the "Booth Material" you would like to order from the provided options.
3. Provide your "Delivery Info" including the delivery date, time, address, state/province, country, and zip code.
4. Ensure all fields are correctly filled out and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details**: Enter any relevant information about the event you are ordering materials for.
* **Select Booth Material**: Choose the material you would like to order from the options provided.
* **Delivery Info**: Enter your delivery details, including date, time, address, state/province, country, and zip code.
* **Delivery Date**: Enter the date you would like the material to be delivered.
* **Delivery Time**: Enter the time you would like the material to be delivered.
* **Delivery Address**: Enter the address where you would like the material delivered.
* **Delivery City**: Enter the city where you would like the material delivered.
* **Delivery State/Province**: Enter the state or province where you would like the material delivered.
* **Delivery Country**: Enter the country where you would like the material delivered.
* **Delivery Zip**: Enter your zip code for delivery purposes.

## Tips
* Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you need to make any changes or additions to your order, please contact our support team directly.
