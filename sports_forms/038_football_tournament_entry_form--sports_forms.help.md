# Football Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for a football tournament, collecting information on team scores and rankings at each stage of the tournament.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the stage of the tournament you are registering for.
2. Enter your team's score for the selected stage.
3. Enter your team's ranking for the selected stage.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Stage 1** (`stage_1`, number, required/optional: false): Select the stage of the tournament you are registering for. This will be the first stage of the tournament.
* **Stage 2** (`stage_2`, number, required/optional: false): Enter your team's score for Stage 1.
* **Stage 3** (`stage_3`, number, required/optional: false): Enter your team's ranking for Stage 1.
* **Stage 4** (`stage_4`, number, required/optional: false): Select the stage of the tournament you are registering for. This will be the second stage of the tournament.
* **Stage 2** (`stage_2`, number, required/optional: false): Enter your team's score for Stage 2.
* **Stage 3** (`stage_3`, number, required/optional: false): Enter your team's ranking for Stage 2.
* **Stage 4** (`stage_4`, number, required/optional: false): Select the stage of the tournament you are registering for. This will be the third stage of the tournament.
* **Stage 4** (`stage_4`, number, required/optional: false): Enter your team's score for Stage 3.
* **Stage 3** (`stage_3`, number, required/optional: false): Enter your team's ranking for Stage 3.
* **Stage 5** (`stage_5`, number, required/optional: false): Select the stage of the tournament you are registering for. This will be the last stage of the tournament.
* **Stage 4** (`stage_4`, number, required/optional: false): Enter your team's score for Stage 4.
* **Stage 5** (`stage_5`, number, required/optional: false): Enter your team's ranking for Stage 4.

## Tips
- Make sure to select the correct stage number for each field to avoid confusion.
