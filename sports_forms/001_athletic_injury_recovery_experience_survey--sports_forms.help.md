# Athletic Injury Recovery Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects personal experiences and opinions about recovering from athletic injuries. The data collected will be used to better understand recovery methods, pain management, and overall satisfaction with treatment plans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal details, including your name and contact information.
2. Enter the date of your injury.
3. Choose the closest match for your injury type.
4. Select any recovery methods used.
5. Estimate the number of weeks it took to recover.
6. Rate your pain level during recovery (1-10).
7. Choose how satisfied you were with your recovery plan.
8. Add any additional comments about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name** (`Full name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name.
* **Contact email** (`Contact email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address, which we will use to follow up on your response.
* **Contact phone** (`Contact phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number, including the country code if you are outside your local region.
* **Date of injury** (`Date of injury`, `date`, required): Enter the approximate date of your injury.
* **Injury type** (`Injury type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the closest match for your injury type from the list:
	+ Sprain or strain
	+ Fracture
	+ Concussion
	+ Tendon or ligament injury
	+ Other
* **Recovery methods used** (`Recovery methods used`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any recovery methods you used during your recovery:
	+ Physical therapy
	+ Rest and ice
	+ Medication
	+ Surgery
	+ Alternative therapy
* **Recovery duration in weeks** (`Recovery duration in weeks`, `number`, optional): Estimate the number of weeks it took to recover from your injury.
* **Pain level during recovery** (`Pain level during recovery 1-10`, `select_one`, required): Rate your pain level during recovery (1 is minimal pain and 10 is worst pain).
* **Overall satisfaction with recovery plan** (`Overall satisfaction with recovery plan`, `select_one`, required): Choose how satisfied you were with your recovery plan:
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very dissatisfied
* **Additional comments or feedback** (`Additional comments or feedback`, `text`, optional): Provide any notes about your treatment or outcomes.
