# Sports Management Waiver Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the steps to complete the Sports Management Waiver Form Template.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the terms and conditions.
2. Enter your information in the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **player_information**: Enter your name and contact information as it appears on your official documents (e.g., ID, driver's license, or passport). This field is used to identify you as the player. (Required: False)
* **name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents. This field is used to verify your identity. (Required: False)
* **email**: Enter your email address. This field is used to contact you in case of emergencies. (Required: False)
* **contact_number**: Enter your phone number. This field is used to contact you in case of emergencies. (Required: False)
* **emergency_contact_name**: Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency. This field is used to notify them of your involvement in the sports activity. (Required: False)
* **emergency_contact_relationship**: Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person. (Required: False)
* **waiver_type**: Select the type of waiver you are signing (waiver1, waiver2, waiver3). This field is used to specify the type of protection you are seeking. (Required: False)
* **waiver_date**: Enter the date you signed this waiver. This field is used to track the effective date of the waiver. (Required: False)
* **waiver_expiration_date**: Enter the date the waiver expires. This field is used to track the expiration date of the waiver. (Required: False)
* **waiver_status**: Select the status of the waiver (active, inactive). This field is used to track the waiver's status. (Required: False)
* **player_signature**: Sign your name to indicate your agreement to the terms and conditions. This field is a legal requirement for waiving your rights. (Required: False)
* **emergency_contact_signature**: Sign the emergency contact person's name to acknowledge their agreement to be contacted in case of an emergency. This field is a legal requirement for emergency contacts. (Required: False)
* **waiver_issued_by**: Enter the name of the person issuing the waiver. This field is used to track who issued the waiver. (Required: False)
* **waiver_issued_date**: Enter the date the waiver was issued. This field is used to track the issue date of the waiver. (Required: False)
* **waiver_expiration_period**: Enter the period after which the waiver will expire. This field is used to track the expiration period. (Required: False)
* **waiver_renewal_date**: Enter the date to renew the waiver. This field is used to track the renewal date. (Required: False)
* **waiver_renewal_period**: Enter the period after which the waiver will be renewed. This field is used to track the renewal period. (Required: False)
* **waiver_status_change_date**: Enter the date the waiver status changed. This field is used to track the status change date. (Required: False)
* **waiver_status_change_reason**: Enter the reason for the waiver status change. This field is used to track the reason for the status change. (Required: False)
* **waiver_renewal_status**: Select the status of the waiver renewal (active, inactive). This field is used to track the renewal status. (Required: False)
* **emergency_contact_name_2**: Enter the name of the second emergency contact person. This field is used to notify them of your involvement in the sports activity. (Required: False)
* **emergency_contact_relationship_2**: Describe your relationship with the second emergency contact person. (Required: False)
* **waiver_status_2**: Enter the status of the second waiver. This field is used to track the status of the second waiver. (Required: False)
* **waiver_issuer**: Enter the name of the person issuing the waiver. This field is used to track who issued the waiver. (Required: False)
* **waiver_renewal_issuer**: Enter the name of the person renewing the waiver. This field is used to track who renewed the waiver. (Required: False)

Note: Some fields may not be required, but it's essential to review and understand the waiver carefully before signing it, as it may impact your rights and responsibilities as an athlete or team member.
