# gym_membership_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The gym membership form is used to collect information about a gym membership application. This form will help our gym staff to process membership applications efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in the "Membership Details" section with your name and any other relevant information.
2. Select your membership plan from the "Membership Plan" dropdown menu.
3. In the "Member Information" section, provide your address and any other personal details.
4. In the "Emergency Contact" section, provide the name and contact information of a person who can be contacted in case of an emergency.
5. In the "Emergency Details" section, provide any necessary medical information or other details about your emergency contact.
6. In the "Emergency Contact Relationship" section, select the relationship of the emergency contact person.
7. In the "Medical Information" section, provide any necessary medical information.
8. In the "Emergency Contact Person" section, provide the name and contact information of the person who will be contacted in case of an emergency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Membership Details** (`membership_info`, text, required/optional): This is the first section of the form where you need to provide your name and any other relevant information about your membership application.
* **Membership Plan** (`membership_plan`, select_one, required/optional): Choose your membership plan from the dropdown menu. Please note that this field is not mandatory.
* **Member Information** (`member_info`, text, required/optional): In this section, provide your address and any other personal details.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required/optional): Provide the name and contact information of a person who can be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Details** (`emergency_details`, text, required/optional): In this section, provide any necessary medical information or other details about your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the relationship of the emergency contact person.
* **Medical Information** (`medical_info`, text, required/optional): In this section, provide any necessary medical information.
* **Emergency Contact Person** (`emergency_contact_person`, text, required/optional): Provide the name and contact information of the person who will be contacted in case of an emergency.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all mandatory fields as they are required to process your membership application.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please provide them in the "Medical Information" section.
* Please note that providing false or incomplete information may delay or reject your membership application.
