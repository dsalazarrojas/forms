# Fitness Club Membership Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect membership information from individuals interested in joining the fitness club. It is used to gather basic details about the member, their fitness goals, and payment preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and any other required member details in the "Member Details" section.
2. Describe your fitness goals and what you hope to achieve by joining the fitness club.
3. Select your preferred payment method and frequency in the "Payment Details" and "Payment Method" sections.
4. Provide your payment start and end dates, if applicable.
5. Choose your membership type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Form Header
* **Form Header**: This section is informational and is not required. It serves as a heading for the form.

### Member Details (Member Details)
* **Member Details**: This is the section where you enter your basic member information, such as your name and any other required details.

### Fitness Goals (Fitness Goals)
* **Fitness Goals**: Describe your fitness goals, such as losing weight, gaining muscle, or improving overall health.

### Payment Details (Payment Details)
* **Payment Details**: This section is used to enter your payment information, including your preferred payment method and frequency.

### Payment Method (Payment Method)
* **Payment Method**: Choose your preferred payment method, such as Visa, Mastercard, or PayPal.

### Payment Frequency (Payment Frequency)
* **Payment Frequency**: Choose whether you want to pay monthly or not.

### Payment Start Date (Payment Start Date)
* **Payment Start Date**: Enter the date you would like your membership to start.

### Payment End Date (Payment End Date)
* **Payment End Date**: Enter the date you would like your membership to end.

### Membership Type (Membership Type)
* **Membership Type**: Choose whether your membership is "Yes" or "No". This is a required field. 
  Please note that you cannot save your membership information without selecting a membership type.
