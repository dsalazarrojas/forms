# Athletic Scholarship Tryout Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Athletic Scholarship Tryout Registration Form is a form designed to gather information from potential scholarship candidates who are interested in trying out for an athletic program. This form is used to collect demographic data, athletic background, and academic information to help scholarship committees evaluate candidates for potential scholarships.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the relevant information below to complete your athletic scholarship tryout registration.
2. Enter all required fields marked as "Required".
3. Enter any additional information that you feel is relevant to your scholarship tryout.
4. Once all required fields are filled in, submit the form.
5. Make sure to review your submission before finalizing it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tryout Event Name**: Enter the name of the scholarship tryout event you are interested in participating in.
* **Tryout Date**: Select the date of the tryout event.
* **Tryout Location**: Enter the city and venue where the tryout event will take place.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date of Birth**: Select your date of birth.
* **Current School/University**: Enter your current educational institution.
* **Grade Classification**: Select your current academic level (e.g., Freshman, Sophomore, etc.).
* **Primary Sport**: Select the sport you are interested in trying out for.
* **Position/Event**: Enter your specific position or event in your primary sport.
* **Years of Competitive Experience**: Enter the number of years you have competed in your primary sport.
* **Highest Level Competed**: Enter the highest level of competition you have participated in (e.g., University, national, etc.).
* **Current Club/Team**: Enter the name of your current club or team (if applicable).
* **Major Achievements and Honors**: Enter any notable achievements or honors you have received in your sport.
* **Height**: Enter your height in feet and inches.
* **Weight**: Enter your weight in pounds.
* **Current GPA**: Enter your current GPA.
* **Standardized Test Scores**: Enter your standardized test scores (if applicable).

## Tips

* Make sure to complete all required fields to ensure your submission is considered for the scholarship tryout.
* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact the scholarship committee for guidance.
