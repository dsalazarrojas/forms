# Nerf Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant details for a Nerf event. Please fill out this form carefully and accurately to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the participant details section, including name, age, contact information, and any relevant medical information.
2. Provide emergency contact information, including name, phone number, and email.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all information is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required/optional): This section is where you can provide any additional information about the participant, such as medical conditions or allergies.
* **Waiver** (`waiver`, text, required/optional): This is a space to provide any necessary waivers or consent forms.
* **Do you have any allergies or health issues?** (`waiver_agreement`, text, required/optional): Please provide any relevant medical information that may impact the participant's experience during the event.
* **Equipment Needs** (`equipment_needs`, text, required/optional): If the participant has any special equipment needs, please provide that information here.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required/optional): This section is for the participant's contact information, including phone number and email.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Provide your full name.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the participant.
* **Participant Age** (`participant_age`, number, required/optional): Enter the participant's age.
* **Participant Gender** (`participant_gender`, select_one, required/optional): Select the participant's gender.
* **Participant Contact Info** (`participant_contact`, text, required/optional): Provide the participant's contact information.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact`, text, required/optional): Provide the emergency contact's name, phone number, and email.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Participant Address** (`participant_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the participant's address.
* **Participant City** (`participant_city`, text, required/optional): Enter the participant's city.
* **Participant State** (`participant_state`, text, required/optional): Enter the participant's state.
* **Participant ZIP** (`participant_zip`, text, required/optional): Enter the participant's ZIP code.
