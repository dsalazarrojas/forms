# Gymnastics Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal details and emergency contact information from individuals who wish to participate in gymnastics activities, ensuring their safety and well-being during their involvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** (field 1) and **Last Name** (field 2) in the respective text fields.
2. Enter your **Date of Birth** (field 3) in the date format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
3. If you are a minor, enter your **Parent's First Name** (field 4) and **Parent's Last Name** (field 5) to provide emergency contact information.
4. Enter your **Guardian's First Name** (field 6) and **Guardian's Last Name** (field 7) if you are a minor and your parent/guardian is not the emergency contact.
5. Enter the **Emergency Contact First Name** (field 8) and **Emergency Contact Last Name** (field 9) of the person you have designated as your emergency contact.
6. Specify your **Relationship** (field 10) with the emergency contact (e.g., parent, guardian, friend, etc.).
7. Enter the **Emergency Contact Phone** (field 11) and **Emergency Contact Email** (field 12) of the person you have designated as your emergency contact.
8. If you have any **Medical Conditions** (field 13), enter them in the text box.
9. If you are taking any **Medication** (field 14), enter it in the text box.
10. If you have any **Allergies** (field 15), enter them in the text box.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (field 1): Enter your first and middle names in this field.
* **Last Name** (field 2): Enter your last name in this field.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Parent's First Name** (field 4): Enter the first name of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent's Last Name** (field 5): Enter the last name of your parent or guardian.
* **Guardian's First Name** (field 6): Enter the first name of your guardian.
* **Guardian's Last Name** (field 7): Enter the last name of your guardian.
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (field 8): Enter the first name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (field 9): Enter the last name of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (field 10): Specify your relationship with your emergency contact (e.g., parent, guardian, friend, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (field 11): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (field 12): Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Medical Conditions** (field 13): Enter any medical conditions you have in this text box.
* **Medication** (field 14): Enter any medication you are taking in this text box.
* **Allergies** (field 15): Enter any allergies you have in this text box.

This form is designed to be completed by you, so please ensure you provide accurate and clear information for the fields above. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us for support.
