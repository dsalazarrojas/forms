# Mum And Baby Fitness Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a registration form for parents and babies participating in a fitness program. It collects essential information to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for both parents and babies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your parent details (Section 1).
2. Provide information about your baby (Section 2).
3. Share your health information (Section 3).
4. Specify your session preferences (Section 4).
5. Grant your consent for participation (Section 5).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Details** (Section 1, `parent_details`):
	+ This section asks for your name, email, phone number, and other essential contact information.
	+ Please fill in your correct and up-to-date details to ensure we can contact you.
* **Baby Details** (Section 2, `baby_details`):
	+ Enter your baby's name, date of birth, age, and any medical conditions.
	+ Provide accurate and complete information to ensure your baby's safety and well-being.
* **Health Information** (Section 3, `health_info`):
	+ Share any relevant health concerns or medical conditions affecting you or your baby.
	+ This helps us understand any necessary accommodations or precautions to take during the fitness program.
* **Session Preferences** (Section 4, `session_preferences`):
	+ Choose your preferred dates and times for sessions.
	+ Specify any specific requirements or accommodations you need during sessions.
* **Consent** (Section 5, `consent`):
	+ Grant permission for your baby to participate in the fitness program.
	+ Please sign and date this section to confirm your consent.

## Tips

* Ensure you complete all required fields to register successfully.
* Double-check your details for accuracy before submission.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* We respect your privacy and will store your data securely.
