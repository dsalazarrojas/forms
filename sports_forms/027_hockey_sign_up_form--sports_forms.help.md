# hockey-sign-up-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for hockey players to sign up for a particular team or event. It collects essential information from players, including their first and last name, date of birth, and playing position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "first-name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last-name" field.
3. Enter your date of birth in the "day-of-birth" field in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
4. Select your playing position from the list provided in the "playing-position" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First-name
* **First-name** (`first-name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name in this field.

### Last-name
* **Last-name** (`last-name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name in this field.

### Day-of-birth
* **Day-of-birth** (`day-of-birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".

### Playing-Position
* **Playing-Position** (`position`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more playing positions from the list provided:
	+ Goalie
	+ Forward
	+ Defense
	+ Centre

## Tips
* Ensure that your date of birth is entered in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
