# SWOT Analysis For Sports Organizations - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help sports organizations conduct a SWOT analysis, a strategic planning tool used to identify internal and external factors that may impact an organization's performance. A SWOT analysis helps organizations identify their strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats, and develop strategies to maximize their strengths and minimize their weaknesses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions in each section, providing a thorough and detailed response.
2. Be honest and objective in your assessment of your organization's strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats.
3. Use the options provided in the "Implementation" section to select how you plan to conduct the SWOT analysis.
4. In the "Benefits" section, select all the benefits that you think will be achieved through the SWOT analysis.
5. In the "Goals" section, describe the specific goals of your SWOT analysis.
6. In the "Scope" section, define the scope of your SWOT analysis.
7. In the "Timeframe" section, specify how long you want the SWOT analysis to last.
8. In the "Resources" section, list the resources you will use to conduct the SWOT analysis.
9. In the "Threats details", "Opportunities details", "Implementation details", "Benefits details", "Goals details", "Scope details", "Timeframe details", "Resources details" sections, provide detailed descriptions of each.
10. In the "Attachments" section, upload any relevant documents or files that support your SWOT analysis.
11. In the "Next steps" section, describe the next steps you plan to take after completing the SWOT analysis.
12. In the "Additional comments" section, provide any additional information that may be helpful.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Strengths** (`swot_strengths`, text, optional): Identify your organization's internal strengths, such as your experience, skills, and resources.
* **Weaknesses** (`swot_weaknesses`, text, optional): Identify your organization's internal weaknesses, such as your limitations, weaknesses, and challenges.
* **Opportunities** (`swot_opportunities`, text, optional): Identify external opportunities, such as trends, market conditions, and competitor actions, that may impact your organization.
* **Threats** (`swot_threats`, text, optional): Identify external threats, such as competitor actions, market conditions, and regulatory factors, that may impact your organization.
* **Implementation** (`swot_implementation`, select_one, optional): Select how you plan to conduct the SWOT analysis, such as creating a task force, hiring a consultant, or using a SWOT analysis tool.
* **Benefits** (`swot_benefits`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the benefits that you think will be achieved through the SWOT analysis, such as improved decision-making, increased efficiency, and better resource allocation.
* **Goals** (`swot_goals`, text, optional): Describe the specific goals of your SWOT analysis.
* **Scope** (`swot_scope`, text, optional): Define the scope of your SWOT analysis.
* **Timeframe** (`swot_timeframe`, text, optional): Specify how long you want the SWOT analysis to last.
* **Resources** (`swot_resources`, text, optional): List the resources you will use to conduct the SWOT analysis.
* **Threats details** (`swot_threats_details`, text, optional): Provide detailed descriptions of each threat.
* **Opportunities details** (`swot_opportunities_details`, text, optional): Provide detailed descriptions of each opportunity.
* **Implementation details** (`swot_implementation_details`, text, optional): Provide detailed descriptions of your SWOT analysis implementation plan.
* **Benefits details** (`swot_benefits_details`, text, optional): Provide detailed descriptions of each benefit.
* **Goals details** (`swot_goals_details`, text, optional): Provide detailed descriptions of each goal.
* **Scope details** (`swot_scope_details`, text, optional): Provide detailed descriptions of each scope.
* **Timeframe details** (`swot_timeframe_details`, text, optional): Provide detailed descriptions of each timeframe.
* **Resources details** (`swot_resources_details`, text, optional): Provide detailed descriptions of each resource.
* **Attachments** (`swot_attachments`, text, optional): Upload any relevant documents or files that support your SWOT analysis.
* **SWOT Analysis** (`swot_analysis`, text, optional): Provide a summary of your SWOT analysis.
* **Recommendation** (`swot_recommendation`, text, optional): Provide a recommendation based on your SWOT analysis.
* **Next steps** (`swot_next_steps`, text, optional): Describe the next steps you plan to take after completing the SWOT analysis.
* **Additional comments** (`swot_additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or information.
* **SWOT Review** (`swot_review`, text, optional): Summarize your SWOT analysis and provide any updates or feedback.
