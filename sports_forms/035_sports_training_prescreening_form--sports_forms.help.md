# sports_training_prescreening_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sports training prescreening form is a crucial document that collects essential information from clients or participants enrolling in a sports training program. It helps trainers, coaches, or administrators gather contact details, emergency contact information, and client preferences for the effective operation of the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Use the provided options for the "Emergency Contact Relationship Type" field.
3. Ensure that you provide a valid email address for the "Emergency Contact Email" field.
4. Enter any additional notes for the "Emergency Contact Relationship Notes" field if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info** (`user_info`, text, optional): Please enter your name and any other relevant personal information.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your contact details, including your address, phone number, or any other relevant information.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact details of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address for your emergency contact person.
* **Client Preferences** (`client_preferences`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your preferences or requirements.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Type** (`emergency_contact_relationship_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of relationship you have with your emergency contact person from the provided options (Mother, Father, Spouse, Partner, Friend, Other).
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Notes** (`emergency_contact_relationship_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about your emergency contact relationship.
Note: All fields are optional unless marked as required.
