# # Athlete Apparel Design Brief Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect design briefs for athlete apparel. It is divided into different sections to make it easier for the customer to provide the necessary information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the official name of the team in the "Team Name" field.
2. Select the sport the apparel is for in the "Sport" field.
3. Select all the types of apparel needed in the "Type of Apparel" field.
4. Enter the approximate number of items needed in the "Total Quantity Needed" field.
5. Choose the primary color for the apparel in the "Primary Color" field.
6. If applicable, choose the secondary color for the apparel in the "Secondary Color" field.
7. Decide if you have a team logo to include, or if you will upload it.
8. Select your design style preference in the "Design Style Preference" field.
9. Provide the sizing breakdown in the "Sizing Breakdown" field.
10. Check any special features needed for the apparel in the "Special Features" field.
11. Enter any text to include on the apparel in the "Text to Include" field (optional).
12. Describe any design inspiration or references in the "Inspiration or References" field (optional).
13. Choose a budget range in the "Budget Range" field.
14. Enter the required delivery date in the "Required Delivery Date" field.
15. Enter the primary contact person's name in the "Contact Person Name" field.
16. Enter the contact email in the "Contact Email" field.
17. Enter the contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
18. Add any additional notes in the "Additional Notes" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Name (1)**: Enter the official name of the team.
* **Sport (2)**: Select the sport the apparel is for. This will help us understand the specific needs and requirements of the sport.
* **Type of Apparel (3)**: Select all the types of apparel you need. You can select multiple types.
* **Total Quantity Needed (4)**: Enter the approximate number of items needed.
* **Primary Color (5)**: Choose the main color for the apparel.
* **Secondary Color (6)**: If you have a secondary color in mind, choose it here.
* **Team Logo (7)**: If you have a team logo, you can choose "Yes - Will Upload" and upload it. If not, choose "Yes - Already on File" and provide the file, or "No - Need Design Help".
* **Design Style Preference (8)**: Choose the style you prefer for the design.
* **Sizing Breakdown (9)**: Provide the quantity needed for each size in the format of "Size - Quantity".
* **Special Features (10)**: Check any special features needed for the apparel.
* **Text to Include (11)**: Enter any text to include on the apparel, such as the team name or a slogan.
* **Inspiration or References (12)**: Describe any design inspiration or references you have.
* **Budget Range (13)**: Choose the estimated budget for the order.
* **Required Delivery Date (14)**: Enter the date by which you need the apparel delivered.
* **Contact Person Name (15)**: Enter the name of the primary contact person.
* **Contact Email (16)**: Enter the contact email.
* **Contact Phone (17)**: Enter the contact phone number.
* **Additional Notes (18)**: Add any additional notes or information about your design request.
