# Powerlifting Contest Entry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Powerlifting Contest Entry Form is designed to collect information from athletes who wish to participate in a powerlifting contest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Powerlifting Contest Entry Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "First Name" field with your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. Fill in the "Last Name" field with your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
3. Enter your age in the "Age" field.
4. Enter your weight in the "Weight" field.
5. Fill in the "Email" field with your email address for contact purposes.
6. Fill in the "Phone" field with your phone number for communication.
7. Review and submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: A field to input your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: A field to input your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Age**: A field to input your age.
* **Weight**: A field to input your weight.
* **Email**: A field to input your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: A field to input your phone number for communication.
* **Contest Details**: This is a note field, currently not used in the form. However, it might be a field for additional information or notes about the contest.
