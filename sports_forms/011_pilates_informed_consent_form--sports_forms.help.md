# Pilates Informed Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from participants before they begin their Pilates program. Please ensure you fill it out accurately to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out the form in its entirety.
2.  Answer all questions honestly and accurately.
3.  Ensure all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: Please enter your full name.
* **Goals**: State your goals for participating in the Pilates program.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your contact information, including your address and phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Details**: Provide the contact details of the person you would like to be notified in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relation**: Choose the relationship between you and the emergency contact person (e.g. Parent, Spouse, Partner, etc.).
* **Medical Condition**: Describe any medical conditions that may impact your participation in the program.
* **Medical Notes**: Provide any additional medical notes that may be relevant to your participation.
* **Participant Declaration**: Please declare that you have read and understand the terms and conditions of the program.
* **Waiver**: Check whether you have read and agreed to the waiver terms.
* **Signature**: Sign your name to confirm that you have read and understood the form.
* **Date**: Enter the current date.

Note: Failure to complete this form in its entirety may impact your participation in the program. Please contact us if you have any questions or concerns.
