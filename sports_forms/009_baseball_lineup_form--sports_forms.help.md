# Baseball Lineup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide helps you complete the Baseball Lineup Form efficiently. The form is used to gather information about a baseball game, including team names, game details, and player lineups.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the game information section**: Provide the team names, game date, and game type (Home or Away) in the respective fields.
2. **Add the starting lineup**: Enter the names and positions of the 9 players in the starting lineup, from the lead-off batter to the starting pitcher.
3. **Enter the starting pitcher and catcher**: Enter the names of the starting pitcher and catcher.
4. **List substitutes and bench players**: Optionally, list any substitutes and bench players.
5. **Coach's approval**: Enter the date when the coach approves the lineup.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the team playing the game.
* **Opponent** (`opponent_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the opposing team.
* **Game Date** (`game_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the game (mm/dd/yyyy format).
* **Game Type** (`home_or_away`, select_one, required): Select whether the game is Home or Away.
* 1. **Lead Off Batter** (`batter_1`, text, required): Enter the name and position of the first batter.
* 2. **Second Batter** (`batter_2`, text, required): Enter the name and position of the second batter.
* 3. **Third Batter** (`batter_3`, text, required): Enter the name and position of the third batter.
* 4. **Cleanup Batter** (`batter_4`, text, required): Enter the name and position of the cleanup batter.
* 5. **Fifth Batter** (`batter_5`, text, required): Enter the name and position of the fifth batter.
* 6. **Sixth Batter** (`batter_6`, text, required): Enter the name and position of the sixth batter.
* 7. **Seventh Batter** (`batter_7`, text, required): Enter the name and position of the seventh batter.
* 8. **Eighth Batter** (`batter_8`, text, required): Enter the name and position of the eighth batter.
* 9. **Ninth Batter** (`batter_9`, text, required): Enter the name and position of the ninth batter.
* **Starting Pitcher** (`starting_pitcher`, text, required): Enter the name and position of the starting pitcher.
* **Starting Catcher** (`starting_catcher`, text, required): Enter the name and position of the starting catcher.
* **Substitutes and Bench** (`substitute_players`, text, optional): List the names and positions of any substitutes and bench players.
* **Coach's Approval Date** (`coach_signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the coach approves the lineup (mm/dd/yyyy format).

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
* Double-check the names and positions of the players in the lineup.
* Make sure the coach's approval date is accurate.
