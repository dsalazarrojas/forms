# Player Coach Recommendation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Player Coach Recommendation Form is designed to help coaches provide detailed information about their players, aiding in the process of identifying and recommending players for various sports-related activities.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the player's name as it appears on their school ID or official records.
2. Provide the name of the player's school.
3. Specify the player's position or role in the team.
4. Describe the player's skills, strengths, and areas of improvement.
5. List the player's technical skills or areas of expertise.
6. Outline the player's personality traits, work ethic, or other relevant characteristics.
7. Briefly explain why you're recommending this player for this opportunity.
8. Provide any additional comments or suggestions about the player's performance or potential.
9. Suggest any ideas or recommendations for the player's development or improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Player's Name

- **Players Name** (`player_name`, text, required): Enter the player's full name as it appears on their school ID or official records.

### Player's School

- **Players School** (`player_school`, text, required): Provide the name of the player's school or the school they represent.

### Player's Position

- **Players Position** (`player_position`, text, required): Specify the player's position or role in the team.

### Player's Abilities

- **Players Abilities** (`player_abilities`, text, required): Describe the player's skills, strengths, and areas of improvement.

### Player's Skills

- **Players Skills** (`player_skills`, text, required): List the player's technical skills or areas of expertise.

### Player's Characteristics

- **Players Characteristics** (`player_characteristics`, text, required): Outline the player's personality traits, work ethic, or other relevant characteristics.

### Recommendation

- **Recommendation** (`recommendation`, text, required): Briefly explain why you're recommending this player for this opportunity.

### Feedback

- **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required): Provide any additional comments or suggestions about the player's performance or potential.

### Suggestions

- **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, required): Suggest any ideas or recommendations for the player's development or improvement.
