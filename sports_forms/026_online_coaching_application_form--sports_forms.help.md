# Online Coaching Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from individuals who are interested in receiving coaching services from a sports expert. The form will be reviewed by a coach who will provide guidance and advice based on the submitted information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow the steps below:
- Fill in the required fields marked as 'Required'
- Enter accurate and relevant information for all fields
- Double-check your information before submission

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Email** (email, Required): Enter your email address to ensure that we can contact you with updates about your coaching application.
* **Phone** (phone, Optional): Enter your phone number so that we can reach you for further questions or clarifications.
* **Address** (address, Optional): Enter your address for our records and for us to send any necessary materials or information.
* **Experience** (experience, Optional): Share your relevant experience in sports or related fields to help us understand your background.
* **Documents** (documents, Optional): Upload any necessary documents, such as medical certificates or proof of qualifications.
* **Name** (name, Required): Enter your full name to identify yourself on our records.
* **Contact** (contact, Optional): Enter a contact person's name and relationship in case we need to reach out to someone else regarding your coaching application.
* **Contact Number** (contact_number, Optional): Enter the phone number of your contact person.
* **Exercise Frequency** (exercise_frequency, Optional): Indicate how often you exercise per week (e.g., 1, 2-3 times, etc.).
* **Exercise Type** (exercise_type, Optional): Select the type of exercise you are interested in (e.g., running, weightlifting, etc.).
* **Exercise Duration** (exercise_duration, Optional): Enter the average duration of your exercise sessions.
* **Exercise Frequency per Week** (exercise_frequency_per_week, Optional): Indicate how many times you exercise per week (e.g., 1-2 times, etc.).
* **Additional Info** (additional_info, Optional): Provide any further information that you would like to share with us.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled in for a successful application.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
- We will review your application and respond to you within a few days.
- You will receive further instructions and details about your coaching process once we have reviewed your application.
