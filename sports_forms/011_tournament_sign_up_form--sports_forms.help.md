# Tournament Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information from individuals who wish to participate in a tournament. The purpose of this form is to provide a centralized way to gather data about the tournament participants, including their team details and contact information. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below.

1. Begin by entering a brief title for your team in the "Tournament Title" field. This will help us identify your team and display it on the tournament schedule.
2. Enter your team ID number in the "team_id" field. This is an optional field and can be left blank if not required.
3. Enter your name in the "player_name" field. This is your personal name and will be used for official communication and identification.
4. Enter your email address in the "player_email" field. This will be used for communication and updates about the tournament.
5. Enter your phone number in the "player_phone" field. This is optional and can be left blank if not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tournament Title** (`tournament_title`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief title for your team.
* **team_id** (`team_id`, number, required/optional): Enter your team ID number, if required.
* **player_name** (`player_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your personal name.
* **player_email** (`player_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for official communication and updates.
* **player_phone** (`player_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number, if required.
