# Swim Wear Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from swimmers on their experiences with swim wear. Please answer the questions honestly to help us better understand your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question honestly and accurately to the best of your ability.
2. Select the correct options for each question from the provided list.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
