# End of Season Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The End of Season Review Form is designed to gather information about the past season's performance and outcome. This form helps in evaluating and reflecting on the season's progress, identifying areas of improvement, and making informed decisions for the next season.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the relevant pages and questions that apply to your experience.
2. Fill out the form thoroughly and accurately, using the provided fields as a guide.
3. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
4. Provide clear and concise responses to each question.
5. Ensure that the form is complete before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Season Performance** (Page 1)
	+ This field is meant to capture a brief overview of the season's performance.
	+ Enter a short description of the season's performance, including any notable events or achievements.
* **Goals Scored** (Page 2)

	+ Enter the exact number of goals scored.
* **Teams Leagues Participated** (Page 3)

* **Top Players** (Page 4)
