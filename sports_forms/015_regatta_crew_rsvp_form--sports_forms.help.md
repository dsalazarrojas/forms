# regatta_crew_rsvp_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from regatta crew members about their participation and other details for a specific regatta event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
2. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Regatta Date** (`regatta_date`, date, required: false)
Enter the date of the regatta event.

### **Crew Name** (`crew_name`, text, required: false)
Enter your regatta crew name.

### **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required: false)
Enter the name of the regatta event.

### **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required: false)
Enter the start time of the regatta event.

### **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required: false)
Enter the end time of the regatta event.

### **Regatta Location** (`regatta_location`, text, required: false)
Enter the location of the regatta event.

### **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false)
Enter your contact email for the regatta event.

### **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false)
Enter your contact phone number for the regatta event.

### **Event Status** (`event_status`, select_one, required: false)
Select the current status of the event:
*   Pending
*   Confirmed
*   Cancelled

### **Crew Participation** (`crew_participation`, select_multiple, required: false)
Select your crew participation status:
*   Yes
*   No
*   Maybe

### **RSVP Comments** (`rsvp_comments`, text, required: false)
Enter any additional comments about your regatta event participation.

Note: This form is for a single event, and you should not fill it multiple times for the same regatta.
