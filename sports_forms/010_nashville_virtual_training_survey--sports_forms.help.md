# Nashville Virtual Training Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nashville Virtual Training Survey is designed to gather feedback and evaluation from participants in the training program. It helps in understanding the effectiveness, satisfaction, and areas for improvement of the training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the required fields and provide your honest feedback and opinions about the training program. Ensure that you answer all the questions truthfully, and provide specific details where possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Survey** (1): This field is optional but helps in identifying the training program you participated in.
* **Trainer Name** (3): Please provide the name of the trainer who conducted the training session.
* **Survey Date** (4): Enter the date you attended the training session.
* **Training Duration** (5): Enter the duration of the training session, ideally in hours or minutes.
* **Feedback** (6): Share your honest feedback about the training session, including any positive or negative experiences.
* **Suggestion** (7): Suggest areas for improvement for future training sessions.
* **Email** (8): Enter your email address, which will be used to contact you for follow-up questions or surveys.
* **Phone** (9): Enter your phone number, which will be used to contact you for follow-up questions or surveys.
* **Rating** (10): Rate your overall satisfaction with the training session, using a number scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **Training Goals** (11): Describe the training goals you achieved or would like to achieve through the training session.
* **Trainer Performance** (12): Evaluate the performance of the trainer, providing feedback on their teaching style, communication, and overall effectiveness.
* **Survey Goals** (13): Describe the goals you have for this survey, such as providing feedback on the training program.
* **Training Environment** (14): Describe the environment where the training session took place, including any notable features or challenges.
* **Trainer Support** (15): Evaluate the level of support you received from the trainer, including their availability, responsiveness, and overall support.
* **Participant Interest** (16): Describe your interest in participating in future training sessions.
* **Training Effectiveness** (17): Assess the effectiveness of the training session, including any positive or negative outcomes.
* **Participant Satisfaction** (18): Rate your overall satisfaction with the training session, using a number scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **Training Improvements** (19): Suggest areas for improvement for future training sessions.
* **Participant Comments** (20): Share any additional comments or thoughts about the training session.
* **Participant Recommendations** (21): Recommend any improvements or suggestions for future training sessions.
* **Trainer Evaluation** (22): Evaluate the overall performance of the trainer, providing feedback on their teaching style, communication, and overall effectiveness.
* **Evaluation Criterion** (23): Identify any evaluation criteria or standards that guided your assessment of the trainer.
* **Training Evaluation** (24): Assess the overall effectiveness of the training session, including any positive or negative outcomes.
* **Participant Feedback** (25): Share any feedback or comments about the training session.
