# Custom Sports Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Custom Sports Order Form is designed to collect customer information and order details for sports-related orders. This form can be used in various sports contexts, such as sports merchandise, equipment, or services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer details, including your name, email, and any other required information on the "Customer Details" page.
2. On the "Order Preferences" page, provide any additional information or notes about your order.
3. On the "Payment Info" page, enter your payment details, if applicable.
4. Review and confirm your order on the "Order Confirmation" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details (Customer Details)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your name, email, and any other relevant customer information.
* **Order Preferences (order_preferences)** (`text`, required/optional): Provide any additional information or notes about your order.
* **Payment Info (payment_info)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your payment details, if applicable.
* **Order Confirmation (order_confirmation)** (`text`, required/optional): Review and confirm your order details before submitting.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately to ensure a smooth ordering process.
* Double-check your order details on the "Order Confirmation" page before submitting.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact the form administrator at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
