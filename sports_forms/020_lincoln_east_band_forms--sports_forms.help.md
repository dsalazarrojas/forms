# Lincoln East Band Forms - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for students and their parents to provide information and consent for participation in a school band activity, including purchasing t-shirts and other materials.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information: This includes your name, email, and phone number. Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Select your t-shirt size: If you're interested in purchasing a t-shirt, please select your size from the options provided.
3. Choose any additional materials you'd like to purchase: Select any materials you'd like to buy from the options provided.
4. Choose your payment method: Select how you'd like to pay for the activity, such as cash, check, or online.
5. Enter your student's name and information: If you're filling out this form for yourself as a student, enter your name, email, and grade level. If you're a parent, enter your student's name, email, and grade level.
6. Enter your parent's name and information: As a parent, enter your own name and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Application and Consent Form (Text)**: This section is where you'll provide your consent for your student to participate in the activity. Please fill in your name and any other information as requested.
* **T-shirt size (Select One)**: Select the size of t-shirt you'd like to purchase, if applicable.
* **Other materials (Select Multiple)**: Select any additional materials you'd like to purchase for the activity.
* **Payments (Select One)**: Select how you'd like to pay for the activity, such as cash, check, or online.
* **Student Name (Text)**: Enter your student's name, or if you're a parent, enter your student's name.
* **Student Email (Email)**: Enter your student's email address.
* **Parent Name (Text)**: If you're a parent, enter your name.
* **Parent Email (Email)**: Enter your email address.
* **Student Grade (Select One)**: Select your student's grade level, if applicable.
* **Parent Phone (Text)**: If you're a parent, enter your phone number.
* **Other materials (Select Multiple)**: Select any additional materials you'd like to purchase for the activity, if applicable.

## Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Make sure to select the correct payment method and t-shirt size, if applicable.
- If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to the school's administration or band activity organizers.
- Complete this form carefully and thoroughly to ensure that your student's participation is recorded correctly.
