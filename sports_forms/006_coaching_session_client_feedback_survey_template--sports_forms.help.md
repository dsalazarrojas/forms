# Coaching Session Client Feedback Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from clients after a coaching session. It helps coaches evaluate their own performance and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your basic information, including your name and the date of the session.
2. Choose the method of delivery (in-person, online, hybrid, or phone).
3. Answer the questions in the sections about your experience, including how helpful the session was, whether the coach paid attention to your needs, and how clear the coach's communication was.
4. Rate the coach's performance in various areas, such as professionalism, knowledge, and communication.
5. Provide any additional feedback or suggestions for improvement.
6. Finally, indicate whether you would recommend this coach to a friend and how likely you are to attend another session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Basic Information
* **Client Full Name** (`client_full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name, as it appears on your identification.
* **Coach Name** (`coach_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the coach who provided the session.
* **Date of Session** (`session_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the coaching session.

### How was this session delivered?
* **How was this session delivered** (`delivery_method`, select_one, required): Choose how the session was delivered (in-person, online, hybrid, or phone).

### Your Experience
* **How helpful was this coaching session** (`session_helpfulness`, select_one, required): Rate how helpful you found the session (1 - not helpful to 5 - extremely helpful).
* **Coach paid attention to your needs and concerns** (`coach_attentiveness`, select_one, required): Rate how attentive the coach was to your needs (1 - strongly disagree to 5 - strongly agree).
* **Coach demonstrated professionalism** (`coach_professionalism`, select_one, required): Rate how professional the coach was (1 - strongly disagree to 5 - strongly agree).
* **Clear path forward** (`clear_path_forward`, select_one, required): Rate how clear the coach's guidance was (1 - strongly disagree to 5 - strongly agree).

### Value and Outcomes
* **Did you achieve the goals set for this session** (`achieved_goals`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you achieved the goals set for the session (Yes, fully, Yes, partially, or No).
* **You have actionable takeaways to implement** (`actionable_takeaway`, select_one, required): Rate how confident you are about the takeaways you have (1 - strongly disagree to 5 - strongly agree).
* **You have a clear path forward** (`clear_path_forward`, select_one, required): Rate how clear the coach's guidance was (1 - strongly disagree to 5 - strongly agree).

### Coach Quality
* **Coach demonstrated good knowledge** (`coach_knowledge_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the coach's knowledge level (1 - poor knowledge to 5 - expert knowledge).
* **Coach communicated clearly** (`communication_clarity`, select_one, required): Rate how clear the coach's communication was (1 - very unclear to 5 - very clear).

### Feedback
* **Coach strengths you noticed** (`strengths_noticed`, text, optional): Provide feedback on what the coach did well.
* **Areas coach could improve** (`areas_for_improvement`, text, optional): Provide suggestions for areas where the coach could improve.

### Future Sessions
* **Likelihood of attending another session** (`likely_to_return`, number, optional): Rate how likely you are to attend another session (0 to 100 percent).
* **Would you recommend this coach to a friend** (`recommend_to_friend`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you would recommend the coach to a friend (Yes, Maybe, or No).
