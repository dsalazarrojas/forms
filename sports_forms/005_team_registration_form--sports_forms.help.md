# Team Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Registration Form is a form used to register a team in the sports management system. It collects essential information about the team's parent contact and student(s) associated with the team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the parent contact information (parent_name).
2. Enter the email address of the parent contact (email).
3. Add the phone number of the parent contact (phone).
4. Enter the name of the student(s) associated with the team (student_name).
5. Enter the email address of the student(s) (student_email).
6. Add the phone number of the student(s) (student_phone).
7. Enter the name of the student's school (student_school).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **parent_name** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the name of the parent or guardian associated with the team.
* **email** (Email, Required/Optional): Enter a valid email address for the parent or guardian.
* **phone** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian.
* **student_name** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the name of the student(s) associated with the team.
* **student_email** (Email, Required/Optional): Enter a valid email address for the student(s).
* **student_phone** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the phone number of the student(s).
* **student_school** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the name of the student's school.
