# Sports Massage Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from clients about their medical history, health concerns, and massage preferences. Please complete this form to help the sports massage therapist understand your needs and tailor the massage experience accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. For fields with multiple options, select only one option.
3. If a field is marked as required, be sure to fill it out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional)
	+ Use this field to share any relevant information about your medical history, such as any injuries or surgeries.
* **Health Concerns** (`health_concerns`, text, optional)
	+ Describe any health concerns you may have that may impact your massage experience.
* **Massage Needs** (`massage_needs`, select_multiple, required)
	+ Indicate what type of massage you need, such as preventative maintenance, relaxation, or rehabilitation.
* **Medical History Details** (`medical_history_details`, text, optional)
	+ Provide more details about your medical history if applicable.
* **Current Conditions** (`current_conditions`, text, optional)
	+ If you have any current medical conditions, please describe them here.
* **Massage Frequency** (`massage_frequency`, select_one, required)
	+ Choose how often you typically get massages.
* **Massage Duration** (`massage_duration`, number, required)
	+ Indicate how long you want your massage to last.
* **Massage Intensity** (`massage_intensity`, select_one, required)
	+ Choose the level of intensity you prefer for your massage.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, email, required)
	+ Enter your email address so we can contact you about your massage appointment.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, required)
	+ Choose how you would like to be contacted about your massage appointment.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional)
	+ If you have any other information to share with us, please use this field.

# Tips

* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form to ensure the best possible massage experience.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
* We are committed to providing a safe and comfortable environment for your massage experience.
