# Horseracing Rider Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for horse racing authorities to verify or validate the credentials of potential jockeys applying to participate in horse racing events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Please provide your basic personal details and contact information on the **Personal Details** page.
- On the **Contact Information** page, provide your contact details so that we can reach out to you for further clarification or verification purposes.
- On the **Horse Racing Experience** page, select the category that best reflects your level of experience in horse racing:
  • Less than 1 year
  • 1-2 years
  • 2-5 years
  • 5-10 years
  • More than 10 years
- On the **Jockey Info** page, please provide any additional information that is relevant to your jockey credentials.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Personal Details** (`title`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide your name, surname, and any other personal details you would like to share with us.
- **Contact Information** (`label`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide your contact phone number, email address, or any other contact information that you are willing to share with us.
- **Horse Racing Experience** (`label`, `select_one`, required/optional): Please select the category that best reflects your level of experience in horse racing. This will help us understand your level of expertise and suitability for participating in our events.
- **Jockey Info** (`label`, `note`, required/optional): This is a note field where you can provide any additional information about yourself that is relevant to your jockey credentials. Please be as concise as possible.
