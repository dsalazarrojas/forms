# Rugby Championship Sponsorship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users interested in sponsoring the Rugby Championship. The goal is to gather data on the user's contact information, sponsorship preferences, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your email and phone number.
2. Choose your sponsorship package preferences.
3. Select a package that suits your needs.
4. Provide your sponsor information, including your name and contact details.
5. Confirm your submission and acknowledge your agreement to the sponsorship terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info** (1, text, required/optional): This field is for your contact information, including your name and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Info** (2, text, required/optional): This field is for your contact information, including your email and phone number.
* **Sponsor Packages** (3, select_multiple, required/optional): This field allows you to choose your sponsorship package preferences. Please select 'Yes' if you are interested in sponsoring the event.
* **Select A Package** (4, select_one, required): This field allows you to select a specific package that suits your needs.
* **Package Details** (5, select_one, required): This field is for you to confirm the package details you have selected.
* **Sponsor Info** (6, text, required): This field is for your sponsor information, including your name and contact details.
* **Payment Method** (7, select_multiple, required): This field is for you to choose how you will make the payment.
* **Payment Details** (8, text, required): This field is for you to provide the payment details.
* **Sponsorship Status** (9, select_one, required): This field is for you to confirm your sponsorship status.
* **Submission Date** (10, date, required): This field is for you to provide the submission date.
* **Submission Time** (11, time, required): This field is for you to provide the submission time.
* **Note** (12, note, required/optional): This field is for any additional comments or notes you may have.
* **Email** (13, email, required): This field is for your email address.
* **Phone** (14, text, required): This field is for your phone number.
* **Agreement** (15, select_one, required): This field is for you to confirm your agreement to the sponsorship terms.
* **Submission Status** (16, select_one, required): This field is for you to confirm your submission status.
* **Confirmation** (17, text, required): This field is for you to confirm that you have submitted your sponsorship application.
* **Submission File** (18, text, required/optional): This field is for you to attach any supporting documents or files.
* **User Acceptance** (19, text, required): This field is for you to confirm that you have accepted the terms and conditions.
* **Sponsor Name** (20, text, required/optional): This field is for your sponsor's name.
* **Sponsor Title** (21, text, required/optional): This field is for your sponsor's title.
* **Sponsor Logo** (22, text, required/optional): This field is for your sponsor's logo.
* **Sponsor Description** (23, text, required/optional): This field is for your sponsor's description.
* **Sponsor Address** (24, text, required/optional): This field is for your sponsor's address.
* **Sponsor Contact Number** (25, text, required/optional): This field is for your sponsor's contact number.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill in all mandatory fields to ensure your application is complete and submitted correctly.
* Double-check your details before submission to avoid errors.
* Ensure that your submission meets the sponsorship criteria and terms.
