# Sports Organization Online Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who wish to register with a sports organization. It gathers personal and sports-related details necessary for the organization to provide services and updates to registered members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Member Details section, including the member's name, phone number, and email address.
2. Provide the sports organization's name and a brief description of their interests.
3. Select the payment method you wish to use (e.g., Cash, Bank, PayPal, Credit, or Debit).
4. Enter any additional payment information, if necessary.
5. Choose the desired membership plan (e.g., Monthly, Yearly, One-time, Quarterly, Bi-annually, or Annually).
6. Fill out the Special Instructions section if you have any specific requirements.
7. Enter your address, city, state, zip, and country.
8. Review and agree to the member agreement and terms.
