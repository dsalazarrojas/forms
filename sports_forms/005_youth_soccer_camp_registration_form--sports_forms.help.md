# Youth Soccer Camp Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from parents or guardians of children registering for a youth soccer camp. The form includes fields to gather athlete and parent information, emergency contacts, and medical history. It also requires an agreement to the camp's terms and conditions and acknowledgment of the camp's safety and fun environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide athlete information (page 1).
2.  Enter parent or guardian information (page 2).
3.  If you have any emergency contacts, provide them (pages 3-4). Emergency contacts are not required.
4.  Choose your agreement (page 5).
5.  Acknowledge the camp's safety and fun environment (page 6).
6.  If you have more athlete information, you can provide it (page 7, optional).
7.  Enter payment information (page 8).
8.  Finally, provide any medical history (page 9).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Athlete Information** (`Athlete Information`, `text`, required): Enter the name, age, and contact information of the athlete registering for the camp.
*   **Parent or Guardian Information** (`Parent or Guardian Information`, `text`, required): Enter the name, contact information, and relationship to the athlete of the parent or guardian.
*   **Emergency Contact 1** (`Emergency Contacts`, `text`, required): If you have an emergency contact person for your child, enter their name, relationship to your child, and contact information.
*   **Emergency Contact 2** (`Emergency Contact 2`, `text`, optional): If you have a secondary emergency contact person for your child, enter their name, relationship to your child, and contact information.
*   **Agreement** (`Agreement`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you agree to all the camp's terms and conditions.
*   **Acknowledegment** (`Acknowledge`, `select_one`, required): Acknowledge that the camp is a fun and safe place for your child.
*   **More Athlete Information** (`More Athlete Information`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information about the athlete (e.g., special needs or allergies).
*   **Payment** (`Payment`, `text`, required): Enter the payment method and details (e.g., amount and date).
*   **Medical History** (`Medical History`, `text`, optional): Provide any medical history or conditions that may affect the athlete's participation in the camp.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out all required fields.
*   Use correct formatting and spell check before submitting.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact the camp organizers.
*   Review the camp's terms and conditions before agreeing.
