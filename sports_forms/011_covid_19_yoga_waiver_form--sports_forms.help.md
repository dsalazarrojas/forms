# Covid 19 Yoga Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from participants about their health, emergency contact, and waiver agreement before attending a yoga class at our studio. It is essential to read and understand the risks associated with COVID-19 and the studio's health and safety protocols.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant's name in the "participant_name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "phone" field.
4. Select "yes" or "no" in the "covid_19_symptoms" field if you have any symptoms of COVID-19.
5. In the "health_risks" field, select the health risks that you are aware of or willing to take on.
6. In the "waiver" field, select the statements that you understand and acknowledge.
7. Select the type of yoga class you would like to attend in the "yoga_class" field.
8. Enter your emergency contact's name in the "emergency_contact_name" field.
9. Select your relationship with the emergency contact in the "emergency_contact_relationship" field.
10. Enter your emergency contact's phone number in the "emergency_contact_phone" field.
11. Enter your emergency contact's email address in the "emergency_contact_email" field.
12. Enter your emergency contact's address in the "emergency_contact_address" field.
13. Select the statements that you understand and acknowledge regarding your emergency contact in the "emergency_contact_acknowledgement" field.
14. Select the statements that you understand and acknowledge regarding the studio's health and safety protocols in the "studio_acknowledgement" field.
15. Select whether you are aware of the risks associated with COVID-19 and the studio's participation in the "consent" field.
16. Sign your name in the "signature" field.
17. Enter the date in the "date" field.
18. Enter the timestamp in the "timestamp" field.
19. Enter a unique participant ID in the "participant_id" field.
20. Enter the studio ID in the "studio_id" field.
21. Select the version of the waiver agreement in the "waiver_version" field.
22. Enter the date of the waiver agreement in the "waiver_date" field.
23. Select the studio where you will be attending the yoga class in the "waiver_studio" field.
24. Enter the submitter's name in the "submitter" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **participant_name** (text): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or driver's license.
* **email** (email): Enter your email address where we can contact you.
* **phone** (text): Enter your phone number where we can contact you.
* **covid_19_symptoms** (select_one): Select "yes" if you have any symptoms of COVID-19, such as a fever, cough, or shortness of breath.
* **health_risks** (note): Select the health risks that you are aware of or willing to take on.
* **waiver** (select_multiple): Select the statements that you understand and acknowledge.
* **yoga_class** (select_multiple): Select the type of yoga class you would like to attend (e.g., Hot Yoga, Power Yoga, Hatha Yoga).
* **emergency_contact_name** (text): Enter your emergency contact's name.
* **emergency_contact_relationship** (select_one): Select your relationship with the emergency contact (e.g., Parent).
* **emergency_contact_phone** (text): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **emergency_contact_email** (email): Enter your emergency contact's email address.
* **emergency_contact_address** (text): Enter your emergency contact's address.
* **emergency_contact_acknowledgement** (select_multiple): Select the statements that you understand and acknowledge regarding your emergency contact.
* **studio_acknowledgement** (select_multiple): Select the statements that you understand and acknowledge regarding the studio's health and safety protocols.
* **consent** (select_one): Select whether you are aware of the risks associated with COVID-19 and the studio's participation.
* **signature** (text): Sign your name to confirm your agreement.
* **date** (date): Enter the date of the agreement.
* **timestamp** (time): Enter the timestamp of the agreement.
* **participant_id** (number): Enter a unique participant ID.
* **studio_id** (text): Enter the studio ID.
* **waiver_version** (select_one): Select the version of the waiver agreement.
* **waiver_date** (date): Enter the date of the waiver agreement.
* **waiver_studio** (select_one): Select the studio where you will be attending the yoga class.
* **submitter** (text): Enter the submitter's name.

## Tips
* Please fill in all fields carefully and accurately.
* Make sure to review and acknowledge the risks associated with COVID-19 and the studio's health and safety protocols.
* Ensure that you have emergency contact information up-to-date.
* Double-check your contact information before submitting the form.
