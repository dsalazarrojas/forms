# Registration Form For Training Children - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register children for training programs. It collects their personal and contact information to ensure their safe and efficient entry into our training programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your child's first name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter your child's last name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Provide your child's email address in the **Email** field.
4. Add your child's phone number in the **Phone** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required: false): Enter your child's first name.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required: false): Enter your child's last name.

### Email
* **Email** (Email, text, required: false): Enter your child's email address. This will be used as a contact method, so ensure it's accurate.

### Phone
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required: false): Enter your child's phone number. This can be used for emergency contact and updates.
