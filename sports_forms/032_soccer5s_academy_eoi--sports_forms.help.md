# Soccer5s Academy EOI - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Soccer5s Academy EOI form is a registration form designed to gather basic contact information and demographic details from interested individuals who want to participate in the Soccer5s Academy programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Start" button to begin filling the form.
2. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
3. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
4. Enter your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
5. Enter your Email in the "Email" field in the correct format (e.g., example@example.com).
6. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your full first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your full last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., example@example.com).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
- Make sure to use the correct format for your Date of Birth (MM/DD/YYYY).
- Use a valid email address that you have access to.
- Enter your phone number without any leading zeros or special characters.
