# Credential Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Credential Application Form is designed for individuals applying for sports-related credentials. It collects personal details, contact information, and other relevant data from applicants.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you are filling it out for the correct purpose.
2. Complete each section accurately and thoroughly, providing all required information.
3. Ensure that you are not filling out the form for a friend or colleague.
4. If you are unsure about any part of the form, ask for clarification before proceeding.
5. Review your completed form for accuracy and completeness before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Details**: This section is for providing your personal information, including your name and contact details. Please ensure that the information is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your contact information, including your address and phone number. This will help us reach you for any follow-up questions or concerns.
* **Email Address**: Select the correct email address from the list of options. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Select the correct phone number from the list of options. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the required format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Sport**: Select the sport you are applying for (e.g., Football, Basketball, Tennis).
* **Level Of Play**: Select the level of play you are applying for (e.g., Recreational, Competitive, Professional).
* **Years Of Experience**: Enter the number of years you have experience in the sport.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and relationship of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Relationship**: Select the relationship of your emergency contact (e.g., Mother, Father, Spouse).
* **Emergency Address**: Select the address of your emergency contact from the list of options.
* **Emergency Phone**: Select the phone number of your emergency contact from the list of options.
* **Emergency Email**: Select the email of your emergency contact from the list of options.
* **Medical Certification**: Select 'True' if you have a medical certification, 'False' otherwise.
* **Medical Certification Details**: If you have a medical certification, please provide details about it.
* **Medical Certification Upload**: If required, upload your medical certification documentation.
* **Additional Info**: Provide any additional information that may be helpful for your application.
* **Signature**: Sign your name, indicating that you have read and understood the form.
* **Signature Confirmation**: Confirm that you have signed the form.
* **Reason For Application**: Provide the reason for your application.
* **Terms And Conditions**: Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the application.
* **Submitted By**: Enter your name, indicating that you have submitted the form.
