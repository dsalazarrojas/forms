# sports_club_social_responsibility_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sports club social responsibility survey is a form designed to gather information about your club's social responsibility initiatives. This form is meant for sports clubs that are actively involved in community-focused activities, such as volunteer programs, community outreach, and social media presence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have permission to fill out this form on behalf of your sports club.
2. Answer all the questions thoroughly and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Make sure to select all the relevant options for each field.
4. Provide your sports club's contact information for us to reach out if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Info (Text)**: This field is meant for providing a brief overview of your sports club. Please write a short paragraph about your club's general information, such as its name, purpose, and any unique aspects.
- **Social Initiatives (Select Multiple)**: Select all the social initiatives that your club is involved in. These options include:
  • Community Involvement
  • Environmental Impact
  • Social Impact
  • Philanthropy
  • Community Outreach
- **Community Outreach Programs (Select Multiple)**: Select all the community outreach programs that your club is involved in. These options include:
  • Community Outreach Program
  • Community Development Program
  • Community Development Project
- **Volunteer Program (Select Multiple)**: Select all the volunteer programs that your club is involved in. These options include:
  • Volunteer Program
  • Community Volunteer Program
  • Community Volunteering
- **Social Media (Text)**: Provide a URL or a brief description of your sports club's social media presence.
- **Website (Text)**: Provide a URL or a brief description of your sports club's website.
- **Email (Email)**: Provide your sports club's email address.
- **Phone (Text)**: Provide your sports club's phone number (if applicable).
- **Feedback (Note)**: If you have any additional feedback or comments about your sports club, please provide them in this field.
- **Additional Comments (Note)**: If you have any further comments or information that you'd like to share about your sports club, please provide them in this field.
- **Comments (Note)**: If you have any comments or concerns about this form, please provide them in this field.
