# e_sports_team_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The e-sports team enrollment form is a tool for users to provide information about their personal details, gaming experience, and habits to be considered for team enrollment. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Fill in the personal details page with your name, email, and any other relevant information.
2. Select the game you are interested in joining and your level of experience in that game.
3. Choose how often you play the selected game and for how many hours you play each session.
4. Select which days of the week you usually play the game.
5. Confirm that you have read and agree to the team's rules and terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Details (id: 1)** (`text`, required: false): This field is for you to provide your name and contact information. Please fill in your name and email address to get in touch with the team management.
* **Gaming Experience (id: 2)** (`text`, required: false): This field is to describe your experience with the selected game. Please provide a brief description of what you have played and for how long.
* **Game (id: 3)** (`select_one`, required: false): This field is to select the game you are interested in joining. Choose one of the available games: game1, game2, or game3.
* **Game Level (id: 4)** (`select_one`, required: false): This field is to select your level of experience in the selected game. Choose one of the available levels: beginner, intermediate, or advanced.
* **Game Experience (id: 5)** (`text`, required: false): This field is for you to provide more details about your gaming experience. Please describe your achievements, wins, or any notable moments.
* **Gaming Frequency (id: 6)** (`select_one`, required: false): This field is to select how often you play the game. Choose one of the available options: daily, weekly, or monthly.
* **Gaming Hours (id: 7)** (`select_multiple`, required: false): This field is to select how many hours you play the game each session. Choose one or more of the available options: 1-2 hours, 2-4 hours, 4-6 hours, 6-8 hours, or 8+ hours.
* **Gaming Days (id: 8)** (`select_multiple`, required: false): This field is to select which days of the week you usually play the game. Choose one or more of the available options: monday, tuesday, wednesday, thursday, friday, saturday, or sunday.
* **Confirm Enrollment (id: 9)** (`select_one`, required: false): This field is to confirm that you have read and agree to the team's rules and terms. Please select "True" to confirm.
* **Submit (id: 10)** (`text`, required: false): This is the final submit button. Once you have filled out all the fields, click submit to send your application to the team management. 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields with accurate information.
* Provide as much detail as possible to increase your chances of being considered for team enrollment.
* Review your application carefully before submitting it to the team management. 

Note: The form requires users to confirm their understanding of the team's rules and terms before submitting. This ensures that users are aware of their responsibilities and obligations as a team member. 

Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure a smooth enrollment process.
