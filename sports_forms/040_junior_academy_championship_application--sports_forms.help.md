# junior_academy_championship_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants for the Junior Academy Championship event. The data collected will be used to create a safe and enjoyable experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. **User Input**: Fill in the text field with any information you think is necessary to provide about yourself or your team. This is an optional field and any information you submit here will be kept confidential.
2. **Parental Consent**: If you are under 18, please fill in the text field with a message to your parent or guardian giving their consent for you to participate in the event.
3. **Event Details**: Provide any relevant information about the event you are participating in, such as the event name, date, and location.
4. **Participant Details**: Enter any relevant information about yourself or your team, such as your name, age, and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input (1, text, optional)**: This field is optional and allows you to provide any additional information about yourself or your team that you think is relevant.
* **Parental Consent (2, text, optional)**: If you are under 18, please fill in this field with a message to your parent or guardian giving their consent for you to participate in the event.
* **Event Details (3, text, optional)**: Provide any relevant information about the event you are participating in, such as the event name, date, and location.
* **Participant Details (4, text, optional)**: Enter any relevant information about yourself or your team, such as your name, age, and contact information.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in the form accurately and honestly.
- If you are under 18, please make sure to get parental consent before participating in the event.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the event organizers.
- The data collected will be kept confidential and used only for the purpose of the Junior Academy Championship event.
