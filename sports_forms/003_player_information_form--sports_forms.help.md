# Player Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and store information about players in a sports-related context.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the player's first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter the player's last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter the player's address in the "Address" field.
4. Enter the player's email in the "Email" field.
5. Enter the player's phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Enter the player's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
7. Enter the player's height in the "Height" field.
8. Enter the player's weight in the "Weight" field.
9. Select the player's team from the "Team" dropdown.
10. Select the player's position from the "Position" dropdown.
11. If necessary, add any additional notes in the "Notes" field.
12. Select the player's category (Male, Female, Nonbinary) from the "Player Category" dropdown.
13. Select the player's level (Rookie, Experienced, Advanced) from the "Player Level" dropdown.
14. Select the player's creator from the "Created By" dropdown.
15. If applicable, select the player's updater from the "Updated By" dropdown.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Enter the player's first name.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Enter the player's last name.
* **Address** (`Address`, text, required): Enter the player's address.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter the player's email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter the player's phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, optional): Enter the player's date of birth.
* **Height** (`Height`, number, required): Enter the player's height in a numerical format (e.g. 1.80 m).
* **Weight** (`Weight`, number, required): Enter the player's weight in a numerical format (e.g. 70 kg).
* **Team** (`Team`, select_one, optional): Select the player's team.
* **Position** (`Position`, select_multiple, required): Select one or multiple positions the player plays.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the player.
* **Player Category** (`Player Category`, select_one, required): Select the player's category (Male, Female, Nonbinary).
* **Player Level** (`Player Level`, select_one, required): Select the player's level (Rookie, Experienced, Advanced).
* **Created By** (`Created By`, select_one, required): Select the player's creator.
* **Updated By** (`Updated By`, select_one, optional): Select the player's updater.
* **Address 2** (`Address 2`, text, optional): Enter the player's secondary address.
* **Email 2** (`Email 2`, email, optional): Enter the player's secondary email address.
* **Phone 2** (`Phone 2`, text, optional): Enter the player's secondary phone number.
* **Date of Birth 2** (`Date of Birth 2`, date, optional): Enter the player's secondary date of birth.
* **Height 2** (`Height 2`, number, optional): Enter the player's secondary height.
* **Weight 2** (`Weight 2`, number, optional): Enter the player's secondary weight.
