# Player Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and store information about players in a sports-related context.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the player's first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter the player's last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter the player's address in the "Address" field.
4. Enter the player's email in the "Email" field.
5. Enter the player's phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Enter the player's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
7. Enter the player's height in the "Height" field.
8. Enter the player's weight in the "Weight" field.
9. Select the player's team from the "Team" dropdown.
10. Select the player's position from the "Position" dropdown.
11. If necessary, add any additional notes in the "Notes" field.
12. Select the player's category (Male, Female, Nonbinary) from the "Player Category" dropdown.
13. Select the player's level (Rookie, Experienced, Advanced) from the "Player Level" dropdown.
14. Select the player's creator from the "Created By" dropdown.
15. If applicable, select the player's updater from the "Updated By" dropdown.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
