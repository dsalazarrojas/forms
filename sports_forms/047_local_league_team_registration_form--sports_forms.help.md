# local_league_team_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering a team in a local sports league. It collects basic information about the team and its members.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above:
1.  Team Details
2.  Team Members
3.  Team League Details
4.  Contact Information

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Team Details** (`team_details`, text, optional): Enter the team's name and any other relevant details.
*   **Team Members** (`team_members`, text, optional): Add information about each team member, including their name, position, and contact information.
*   **Team League Details** (`team_league_details`, text, optional): Enter the team's league name and division.
*   **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter the team's contact information, including name, email, and phone number.

## Tips
*   Be sure to double-check the league's requirements before registering your team.
*   Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information about your team and its members.
*   If you have any questions or need help with the registration process, contact the league administrators.
