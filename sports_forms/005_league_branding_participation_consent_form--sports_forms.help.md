# League Branding Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant's information and consent for league branding participation. It's used to inform participants about what their data will be used for and to obtain their consent for the use of their information in branding activities such as photos, videos, and social media promotions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Answer the questions about your willingness to participate in branding activities, such as taking photos or videos, and sharing your social media handle.
3. Check your consents to confirm your willingness to participate in different types of activities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information (1)**: This is the section where you can enter your name, email address, and phone number. Please fill in this section accurately to ensure we can contact you properly.
* **Branding Participants (2)**: Please select all the activities you are willing to participate in. This includes taking photos, videos, and sharing your social media handle.
* **Consent (3)**: This is a text field for you to provide any additional information or comments you'd like to share with us.
* **Name (4)**: This is your name, which will be used to address you properly.
* **Email (5)**: This is your email address, which we will use to contact you for future events or activities.
* **Phone Number (6)**: This is your phone number, which we may use to contact you for urgent matters.
* **Photo Consent (7)**: Please select whether you are willing to have your photo taken and published. This will help us create a positive image for the league.
* **Likeness Consent (8)**: This is similar to photo consent, but for likeness (e.g., a photo that captures your likeness or image).
* **Participant Image Consent (9)**: Please select whether you are willing to have your image used in our promotional materials (e.g., photos, videos, social media).
* **Participant Video Consent (10)**: This is similar to photo consent, but for video recordings.
* **Social Media Consent (11)**: Please select whether you are willing to share your social media handle with us, so we can tag you in our posts.
* **Social Media Handle (12)**: If you selected "Yes" to social media consent, please enter your social media handle here.
* **Additional Information (13)**: This is a text field for you to provide any additional information or comments you'd like to share with us.
