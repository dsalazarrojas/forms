# Torso Twist Exercise Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a Torso Twist exercise assessment, which is likely a medical or physical assessment related to the torso or spine.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form carefully and accurately.
2. Enter your First Name, Last Name, and Email address in the required fields.
3. Select the appropriate option for Date of Birth, Height, and Weight.
4. Describe any medical conditions you have in detail in the Medical Conditions field.
5. List any medications you are currently taking in the Medications field.
6. Indicate your exercise frequency (Low, Medium, High) and intensity (Low, Medium, High) in the Exercise Frequency and Exercise Intensity fields, respectively.
7. Select the Pain Level that best describes your current condition (None, Slight, Moderate, Severe).
8. Provide a brief description of your Medical History in the Medical History Notes field.
9. Enter your emergency contact information in the Emergency Contacts, Emergency Contact Relationship, and Emergency Contact Phone fields.
10. Provide the details of your medical insurance provider in the Medical Insurance Provider, Medical Insurance Plan, and Medical Insurance Provider Contact fields.
11. Enter any additional notes about your medical history in the Medical History Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Select your date of birth.
* **Height** (`height`, number, optional): Enter your height.
* **Weight** (`weight`, number, optional): Enter your weight.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): Describe any medical conditions you have.
* **Meditations** (`medications`, text, optional): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Exercise Frequency** (`exercise_frequency`, select_one, optional): Select the frequency of your exercise (Low, Medium, High).
* **Exercise Intensity** (`exercise_intensity`, select_one, optional): Select the intensity of your exercise (Low, Medium, High).
* **Pain Level** (`pain_level`, select_multiple, optional): Select the level of pain you are experiencing (None, Slight, Moderate, Severe).
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your medical history.
* **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact information.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the emergency contact email address.
* **Medical Insurance Provider** (`medical_insurance_provider`, text, optional): Enter the name of your medical insurance provider.
* **Medical Insurance Plan** (`medical_insurance_plan`, text, optional): Enter the name of your medical insurance plan.
* **Medical Insurance Provider Contact** (`medical_insurance_provider_contact`, text, optional): Enter the contact information of your medical insurance provider.
* **Medical Insurance Provider Phone** (`medical_insurance_provider_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your medical insurance provider.
* **Medical Insurance Provider Email** (`medical_insurance_provider_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of your medical insurance provider.
* **Medical History Notes** (`medical_history_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about your medical history.
* **Emergency Response** (`emergency_response`, text, optional): Describe the emergency response plan.
* **Emergency Procedure** (`emergency_procedure`, text, optional): Describe the emergency procedure.
* **Medical Insurance Provider Relationship** (`medical_insurance_provider_relationship`, text, optional): Describe your relationship with the medical insurance provider.
* **Medical Insurance Provider Contact** (`medical_insurance_provider_contact`, text, optional): Enter the contact information of your medical insurance provider.
