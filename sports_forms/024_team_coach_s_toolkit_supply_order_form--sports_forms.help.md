# Team Coaches Toolkit Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Coaches Toolkit Supply Order Form is designed to help team coaches order and manage their supplies. This form will collect information about the team coach, team name, and order date to facilitate the supply ordering process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. In the "Team Coach" field, enter your full name as it appears on your official documentation.
2. In the "Team Name" field, type in the name of the team you are ordering supplies for.
3. In the "Order Date" field, select the date you want to place the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Coach** (`team_coach`, text, optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documentation.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, optional): Type in the name of the team you are ordering supplies for.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Select the date you want to place the order.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate processing of your order.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact form@gic.mx.
