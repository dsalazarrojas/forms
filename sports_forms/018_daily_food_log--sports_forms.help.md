The Daily Food Log form is designed to help users track their daily food intake and activities over time. It captures essential information about the user's meals and experiences, allowing for better insight into their diet and lifestyle.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the designated fields.
2. Select the food item you consumed and enter the quantity.
3. Choose the date and time of consumption.
