# Mobile Sports Agency Online Footballers Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mobile Sports Agency Online Footballers Registration Form is a registration form for footballers to join the Mobile Sports Agency online platform. It allows users to fill out their details and potentially get opportunities for playing professional football.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Choose from the available registration options (Psl or Nfd) in the "Register to" field.
2.  Enter your age in the "Age" field.
3.  Select your identity document type from the "Identity document" field. This could be a passport, ID, or birth certificate.
4.  If you have paid the registration fee of R2 500.00, select "Yes" in the "Has paid registration fee" field.
5.  Indicate whether you have contacted the agency for more information in the "Contacted us for more information" field.
6.  Enter your name in the "Name" field.
7.  Enter your surname in the "Surname" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Register to** (Psl, Nfd, select_multiple): Choose from the available registration options to select the type of registration you are interested in.
*   **Age** (number): Enter your age.
*   **Identity document** (Passport, ID, select_one): Select your identity document type.
*   **Registration fee** (number): This is the registration fee of R2 500.00.
*   **Has paid registration fee** (number): If you have paid the registration fee, select "Yes".
*   **Contacted us for more information** (True, False, select_one): Indicate whether you have contacted the agency for more information.
*   **Name** (text): Enter your name.
*   **Surname** (text): Enter your surname.
