# Metabolic Bodybuilding Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Metabolic Bodybuilding Intake Form is designed to gather information about your workout habits to provide personalized recommendations for optimal metabolic bodybuilding.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the answers that apply to your current situation and provide any additional information you feel is relevant.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Workout frequency**
* **Workout frequency** (`Workout frequency`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the frequency of your current workouts.
	+ Choose from: Yes, No
### **Training intensity**
* **Training intensity** (`Training intensity`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the intensity of your current workouts.
	+ Choose from: Yes, No
### **Workout type**
* **Workout type** (`Workout type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of workouts you typically do.
	+ Choose from: Yes, No
### **Rest days per week**
* **Rest days per week** (`Rest days per week`, `number`, required: false): Enter the number of rest days you take per week.
	+ Enter a numeric value
### **Cardio days per week**
* **Cardio days per week** (`Cardio days per week`, `number`, required: false): Enter the number of cardio days you do per week.
	+ Enter a numeric value
### **Weightlifting days per week**
* **Weightlifting days per week** (`Weightlifting days per week`, `number`, required: false): Enter the number of weightlifting days you do per week.
	+ Enter a numeric value
### **Nutrition plan**
* **Nutrition plan** (`Nutrition plan`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your current nutrition plan.
	+ Choose from: Yes, No
### **Goals**
* **Goals** (`Goals`, `text`, required: false): Enter your short-term and long-term goals.
	+ Provide a brief description
### **Experience level**
* **Experience level** (`Experience level`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your level of experience.
	+ Choose from: High, Low
### **Training days per week**
* **Training days per week** (`Training days per week`, `number`, required: false): Enter the number of training days you do per week.
	+ Enter a numeric value
## Tips
* Be honest when answering the questions to receive accurate recommendations.
* If you have any additional information or context, provide it in the "Goals" field. 

This guide explains each field in a concrete and concise manner, ensuring that users understand what each field means and how to complete it accurately.
