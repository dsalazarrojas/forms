# Tournament Press Credential Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for journalists and media personnel to apply for press credentials for a tournament. The application allows you to provide necessary information about yourself and your media affiliation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the below steps:

1. Fill in the required fields for the **Journalist Details** section.
2. Select the correct options for **Media Accreditations Request**, **Media Type**, and **Credential Request**.
3. Enter your **Name**, **Email**, and **Phone Number**.
4. Select your **Media Category**.
5. Provide a brief description of your **Credential Request**.
6. Select the current **Credential Status**.
7. Add any additional notes in the **Credential Note** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Media Accreditations Request**: Check the box if you are requesting media accreditations for the tournament.
* **Media Type**: Select the type of media you represent (e.g., newspaper, radio, TV).
* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your business card or identification.
* **Email**: Enter your professional email address (e.g., @yourmediaorg.com).
* **Phone Number**: Enter your professional phone number (e.g., 123-456-7890).
* **Organization**: Enter the name of your media organization.
* **Media Category**: Select the category of media you represent (e.g., print, online, radio).
* **Credential Request**: Briefly describe the reason for your credential request.
* **Credential Status**: Select the current status of your credential (Active or Inactive).
* **Credential Note**: Add any additional information about your credential request.
* **Credential Rejected Reason**: If your credential has been rejected, provide a reason for the rejection.

## Tips

* Ensure that your contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
* Complete the form accurately and thoroughly to avoid delays in the application process.
* The completion of this form does not guarantee approval of your credential request.
* The approval process may involve additional steps and review by authorized personnel.
