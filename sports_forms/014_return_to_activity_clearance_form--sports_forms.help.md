# Return To Activity Clearance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Return To Activity Clearance Form is a simple form designed to gather information about individuals returning to activities after a certain period of time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your **Organization** (if applicable) in the "Organization" field.
4. Enter your **Role** (if applicable) in the "Role" field.
5. Select the **Date Of Event** from the date picker or enter it manually in the "Date Of Event" field.
6. Select the **Time Of Event** from the time picker or enter it manually in the "Time Of Event" field.
7. Provide a brief description of the **Event Details** in the "Event Details" field.
8. If you have a **Contact Number**, enter it in the "Contact Number" field.
9. If you have an **Email**, enter it in the "Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter the name by which you are known in your organization or team.
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname.
* **Organization**: If you are associated with a specific organization or team, enter its name here.
* **Role**: If you have a specific role within the organization or team, enter it here.
* **Date Of Event**: Select the date on which the event took place. You can use the date picker to select the date or enter it manually in the format dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Time Of Event**: Select the time at which the event took place. You can use the time picker to select the time or enter it manually in the format hh:mm.
* **Event Details**: Provide a brief description of the event or activity you participated in.
* **Contact Number**: If you have a contact number associated with your organization or team, enter it here.
* **Email**: If you have an email associated with your organization or team, enter it here.
