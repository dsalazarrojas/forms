# Swimming Race Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from swimmers participating in a swimming race. It's essential to provide accurate and complete answers to ensure a safe and enjoyable racing experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Swimmer Details" section with your relevant information.
2. Provide details about the race you are participating in.
3. Share your event preferences in the "Event Preferences" section.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Swimmer Details (text)**: Please enter your name, contact information, and any relevant swimming experience you'd like to share.
* **Race Details (text)**: Enter the name of the race, event date, and your expected participation status (e.g., confirmed, pending).
* **Event Preferences (text)**: Indicate your preferred event format (e.g., solo, team, relay), swimming style (e.g., freestyle, backstroke), and any specific needs or requirements you may have (e.g., accommodations, dietary restrictions).

## Tips

* Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We appreciate your participation and look forward to a fun and safe racing experience!
