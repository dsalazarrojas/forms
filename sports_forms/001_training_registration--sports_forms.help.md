# Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register interest or details about a training session. It helps to collect relevant information from users to process their registrations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact information (email and phone number) in the respective fields.
2. Choose your provider from the dropdown list.
3. Select your training status from the dropdown list.
4. If required, provide more details like date of birth, address, and description.
5. Select relevant subcategories for your training.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Name**
`name` (`text`, required/optional): Enter your name.

### 2. **Provider**
`provider` (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose one or multiple providers from the list.

### 3. **Email**
`email` (`email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.

### 4. **Phone**
`phone` (`text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.

### 5. **Date of Birth**
`date_of_birth` (`date`, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### 6. **Address**
`address` (`text`, required/optional): Enter your address.

### 7. **Description**
`description` (`text`, required/optional): Provide a brief description about the training.

### 8. **Category**
`category` (`select_one`, required/optional): Choose one category from the list.

### 9. **Subcategory**
`subcategory` (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select one or multiple subcategories.

### 10. **Training Status**
`training_status` (`select_one`, required/optional): Choose your training status from the list.

### 11. **Description**
`description` (`text`, required/optional): This field is for providing more details about your training. 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your selections for provider and training status.
* Be concise and accurate when providing details in the description field.
