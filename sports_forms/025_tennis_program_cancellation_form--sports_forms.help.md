# Tennis Program Cancellation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tennis Program Cancellation Form is used for players to cancel their scheduled tennis programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tennis Program Cancellation Form** (<input name="title">, text, required) 
    - Enter the title of the form. This field is required.
* **Date** (<input name="date">, date, required) 
    - Enter the date of the program cancellation.
* **Time** (<input name="time">, time, required) 
    - Enter the time of the program cancellation.
* **Reason** (<input name="reason">, text, required) 
    - Enter the reason for the cancellation.
* **Email** (<input name="email">, email, required) 
    - Enter your email address for further communication.
* **Phone** (<input name="phone">, text, required) 
    - Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Assigned User** (<input name="assigned_user">, select_one, required) 
    - Select the user who will be assigned to cancel your program.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields correctly to ensure that your cancellation request can be processed efficiently.
* If you have any difficulties filling out this form, please contact the program manager for assistance.
