# Sport Trading Warranty Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sport Trading Warranty Claim Form is used by customers to report issues with a product they have purchased from Sport Trading. The form helps us understand the nature of the problem, which enables us to evaluate and potentially resolve the issue efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your name and contact information.
2.  Enter the product ID number.
3.  Briefly describe the product.
4.  Explain the issue you are experiencing with the product.
5.  Provide any relevant warranty details.
6.  Select the reason for your warranty claim from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (customers_name): Your name as it appears on your identification or account information with Sport Trading.
* **Customer Email** (customers_email): Your email address associated with your Sport Trading account.
* **Product ID** (product_id): The unique number assigned to the product you are experiencing issues with.
* **Product Name** (products_name): A brief description of the product (e.g., model number, brand, etc.).
* **Product Description** (products_description): A more detailed description of the product.
* **Issue Details** (issue_details): Describe the problem you are experiencing with the product.
* **Warranty Details** (warranty_details): Any relevant information about the warranty that may be applicable to your claim.
* **Warranty Claim** (warranty_claim): Select the reason for your warranty claim from the options provided:

  * Option 1: Defective Product
  * Option 2: Not Functional
  * Option 3: Other (Please describe the reason for your claim in the issue_details field)

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have a clear description of the issue you are experiencing with the product.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can contact Sport Trading's customer support for assistance.
* If you have any additional documentation or evidence that may support your claim, please attach it to the form or send it to Sport Trading's customer support.
