# Goalkeeper_program_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **goalkeeper_program_inquiry_form** is designed to gather information for potential goalkeeping programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked as "required" in the field-by-field explanation below.
2. Ensure accuracy in filling out each field to help us provide the best assistance.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (1)** (`first_name`, **required**):
	+ This field is for your first name.
* **Last Name (2)** (`last_name`, **required**):
	+ This field is for your last name.
* **Experience Years (3)** (`experience`, **required**):
	+ This field is for your years of experience as a goalkeeper.
* **Availability Days (4)** (`availability_days`, **required**):
	+ This field is for your availability for the program. Please select all days you are available.
* **Contact Details (5)** (`contact_details`, **required**):
	+ This field is for your contact information, including any preferred contact method (e.g., phone, email).
* **Academy Details (6)** (`academy_details`, **optional**):
	+ This field is for any additional details about your academy or program.
* **Coach Name (7)** (`coach_name`, **required**):
	+ This field is for the name of your coach or primary contact.
* **Coach Email (8)** (`coach_email`, **required**):
	+ This field is for your coach's or primary contact's email address.
* **Coach Phone (9)** (`coach_phone`, **required**):
	+ This field is for your coach's or primary contact's phone number.
* **Academy Address (10)** (`academy_address`, **optional**):
	+ This field is for the address of your academy or program location.
* **Training Details (11)** (`training_details`, **optional**):
	+ This field is for any additional details about your training program or requirements.
* **Academy Contact Person (12)** (`academy_contact_person`, **optional**):
	+ This field is for the contact person's name at your academy or program.
* **Academy Contact Number (13)** (`academy_contact_number`, **optional**):
	+ This field is for the contact person's phone number at your academy or program.
* **Academy Website (14)** (`academy_website`, **optional**):
	+ This field is for the website of your academy or program.
* **Social Media Links (15)** (`social_media_links`, **optional**):
	+ This field is for any social media links related to your academy or program.
* **Training Hours (16)** (`training_hours`, **optional**):
	+ This field is for the hours of your training program.
* **Training Cost (17)** (`training_cost`, **optional**):
	+ This field is for the cost of your training program.
* **Training Cost Currency (18)** (`training_cost_currency`, **optional**):
	+ This field is for the currency of your training program's cost.
* **Payment Terms (19)** (`payment_terms`, **optional**):
	+ This field is for any additional payment terms or requirements.
* **Payment Terms Notes (20)** (`payment_terms_notes`, **optional**):
	+ This field is for any notes about payment terms.
* **Payment Method (21)** (`payment_method`, **required**):
	+ This field is for the payment method you prefer (e.g., bank transfer, card).
* **Payment Terms Date (22)** (`payment_terms_date`, **required**):
	+ This field is for the date of the payment terms or deadline.
* **Payment Terms Time (23)** (`payment_terms_time`, **required**):
	+ This field is for the time of the payment terms or deadline.
* **Notes (24)** (`notes`, **optional**):
	+ This field is for any additional comments or questions you have.
* **Submitted By (25)** (`submitted_by`, **required**):
	+ This field is for the person submitting the form (e.g., coach, academy representative).

Please note that some fields may have additional options or details not explicitly mentioned here. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
