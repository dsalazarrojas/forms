# Recreational Running Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Recreational Running Registration Form is designed to collect information from participants in a running event. The form is used to gather personal details, contact information, and preferences of the participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name
2. Enter your Email (for contact purposes only)
3. Enter Date Of Birth
4. Enter Parent Name (if applicable)
5. Select Event Date
6. Select Participant Gender
7. Enter Participant Age
8. Enter Contact Phone (for emergency contact)
9. Enter Emergency Contact Name
10. Enter Emergency Contact Phone
11. Enter School Name (if applicable)
12. Enter Teacher Name (if applicable)
13. Enter Student Name (if applicable)
14. Enter Student Grade (if applicable)
15. Enter Parent Email
16. Enter Student Interests (for fun activities or interests related to running)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes only. This will be used to send you information about the event and other updates.
* **Date Of Birth** (`Date Of Birth`, `date`, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Parent Name** (`Parent Name`, `text`, required/optional): If you are under 18 years old, please enter your parent's name.
* **Event Date** (`Event Date`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date of the event you plan to attend.
* **Participant Gender** (`Participant Gender`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your participant gender. Options: Male, Female, Other, Prefer not to say.
* **Participant Age** (`Participant Age`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your age.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`Emergency Contact Name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`Emergency Contact Phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **School Name** (`School Name`, `text`, required/optional): If you are a student, enter your school name.
* **Teacher Name** (`Teacher Name`, `text`, required/optional): If you are a student, enter your teacher's name.
* **Student Name** (`Student Name`, `text`, required/optional): If you are a student, enter your name.
* **Student Grade** (`Student Grade`, `number`, required/optional): If you are a student, enter your grade level.
* **Parent Email** (`Parent Email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your parent's email address for contact purposes only.
* **Student Interests** (`Student Interests`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your interests related to running and fun activities.
