# Daily Coaching Sheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Coaching Sheet is a form designed to capture essential information for each daily coaching session. This form helps coaches track progress, maintain accurate records, and provide effective feedback to students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Ensure you have all necessary information and resources before starting.
*   Fill in the required fields, marked with \*.
*   Use the available space for progress notes and comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Lesson Plan (1)** (`lesson_plan`, text, optional): Enter a brief summary of the coaching session's focus and objectives.
*   **Athlete Name (2)** (`athlete_name`, text, optional): Enter the athlete's name as recorded in their records.
*   **Date (3)** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the coaching session.
*   **Coach Name (4)** (`coach_name`, text, optional): Enter your name or your team's name.
*   **Student ID (5)** (`student_id`, number, optional): Enter the student's ID number, if applicable.
*   **Score (6)** (`score`, number, optional): Record the athlete's score in the current session.
*   **Progress Notes (7)** (`progress_notes`, text, optional): Add any progress notes or comments about the athlete.
*   **Coach Comments (8)** (`coach_comments`, note, optional): Add any comments or feedback for the coach.
*   **Next Lesson (9)** (`next_lesson`, date, optional): Enter the next scheduled lesson date.
*   **Coach Phone (10)** (`coach_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
*   **Coach Email (11)** (`coach_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
*   **Coach Signature (12)** (`coach_signature`, select_one, required): Select your name from the provided options.
*   **Student Signature (13)** (`student_signature`, select_one, required): Select the student's name from the provided options.
*   **Student Phone (14)** (`student_phone`, text, optional): Enter the student's phone number.
*   **Student Email (15)** (`student_email`, email, optional): Enter the student's email address.
*   **Additional Comments (16)** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about the coaching session.

**Tips:**

*   Ensure accuracy and thoroughness when filling out the form.
*   Keep your entries clear and concise for easy reference.
*   Review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors.
