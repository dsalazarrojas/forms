# Personal Trainer Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Trainer Request Form is designed to collect information from clients about their personal training needs and preferences. This form helps us assign a suitable personal trainer to them for an effective and enjoyable training experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Client Name** in the first field.
2. Provide your **Email Address** in the second field to ensure that we can contact you.
3. In the third field, enter your **Phone Number**, which will be used for communication.
4. For your **Preferred Contact Time**, select a suitable time that works best for you.
5. In the **Training Goals** field, describe your goals and objectives for the training sessions.
6. In the **Availability** field, select a date that you are available for training.
7. Choose your **Select Option 1** choices from the list to indicate your preferences.
8. Select a single option from **Select Option 2** for further specification.
9. In the **Additional Comments** field, enter any additional information or comments that you would like to share with your personal trainer.
10. Finally, select the **Assigned Tool** that you would like to use during the training process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Enter your name as it appears in your identification documents.
* **Email Address**: This field is required to ensure that we can contact you for updates and information.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for easier communication.
* **Preferred Contact Time**: Select a time that works best for you, such as morning, afternoon, or evening.
* **Training Goals**: Describe your objectives and goals for the training sessions, e.g., weight loss, fitness, or sports improvement.
* **Availability**: Select a date when you are available for training.
* **Select Option 1**: Choose your preferred options for training, e.g., one-on-one, group, or online sessions.
* **Select Option 2**: Select a specific option from the list to further specify your preferences.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional information or comments about your training needs and preferences.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool that you would like to use during the training process, e.g., chatjimmy or other.
