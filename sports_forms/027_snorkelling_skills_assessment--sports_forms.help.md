# Snorkelling Skills Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Snorkelling Skills Assessment form is designed to evaluate a person's proficiency in snorkelling skills and positioning, ensuring they have the necessary skills and experience to participate in snorkelling activities safely and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please read the form carefully and answer all questions honestly.
2. Ensure that you have sufficient knowledge and experience in snorkelling to complete the form accurately.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification or assistance.
4. Complete all required fields, as indicated by the required label next to each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Snorkelling Skills Rating** (`snorkelling_skills_rating`, number, required):
	+ This field asks you to rate your snorkelling skills on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "beginner" and 5 is "expert".
	+ Rate your skills based on your actual experience and practice in snorkelling.
* **Snorkelling Positioning Rating** (`snorkelling_positioning_rating`, number, required):
	+ This field asks you to rate your ability to position yourself while snorkelling on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "novice" and 5 is "experienced".
	+ Consider your experience in maintaining proper body position while snorkelling.
* **Snorkelling Skills Score** (`snorkelling_skills_score`, number, optional):
	+ This field is an optional rating of your overall snorkelling skills, on a scale of 1-10.
	+ Use this field to provide additional context about your skills, if applicable.

## Tips

* Answer all questions honestly and accurately, as this will help ensure that the assessment is fair and reliable.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please ask for clarification or assistance.
* Remember that this form is not a substitute for professional training or certification, but rather a tool to gauge your initial skills.
