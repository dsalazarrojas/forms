# Online Fitness Transformation Challenge Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect registration information from individuals interested in participating in the online fitness transformation challenge. Please answer the questions honestly and accurately to ensure a successful and safe experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your registration information in the "Registration Information" page (page 1).
2. Indicate whether you have any medical conditions that may impact your participation on page 2.
3. Choose your age category on page 3.
4. Enter your current email address, phone number, and address on pages 4, 7, and 8, respectively.
5. Select your current fitness level and goal for the challenge on pages 5 and 6.
6. Choose how you heard about the challenge and select your preferred activities on pages 9 and 14.
7. Confirm your agreement to the challenge's terms and conditions and take responsibility for your own safety on pages 15 and 16.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Information** (Page 1)
	+ A text field for entering your registration details.
* **Do you have any medical conditions that may impact your participation?** (Page 2)
	+ A multiple-choice question to indicate whether you have any medical conditions that may affect your participation.
* **Are you over 18 years old?** (Page 3)
	+ A checkbox for indicating your age category.
* **What is your current email address?** (Page 4)
	+ A field for entering your current email address.
* **What is your current fitness level?** (Page 5)
	+ A single-select question to choose your current fitness level.
* **What is your goal for the challenge?** (Page 6)
	+ A text field for entering your goal for the challenge.
* **What is your phone number?** (Page 7)
	+ A field for entering your phone number.
* **What is your address?** (Page 8)
	+ A text field for entering your address.
* **How did you hear about the challenge?** (Page 9)
	+ A single-select question to choose how you heard about the challenge.
* **What type of activities do you prefer during the challenge?** (Page 10)
	+ A multiple-select question to choose your preferred activities.
* **Do you have any questions or concerns about the challenge?** (Page 11)
	+ A text field for entering any questions or concerns.
* **Are you available to participate in the challenge on the specified dates?** (Page 12)
	+ A multiple-choice question to indicate your availability.
* **Please provide your emergency contact's name and phone number** (Page 13)
	+ A text field for entering your emergency contact's details.
* **Are you willing to pay a fee for the challenge?** (Page 14)
	+ A single-select question to choose whether you are willing to pay a fee.
* **I confirm that I have read and agreed to the challenge's terms and conditions** (Page 15)
	+ A checkbox to confirm your agreement to the challenge's terms and conditions.
* **I understand that I will be responsible for my own safety and well-being during the challenge** (Page 16)
	+ A checkbox to confirm your understanding of your responsibility for your own safety.

## Tips

* Please answer the questions honestly and accurately to ensure a successful and safe experience.
* If you have any questions or concerns, use the text field on page 10 to enter them.
* Make sure to enter your correct contact details, including your email address, phone number, and address.
* Confirm your agreement to the challenge's terms and conditions before proceeding.
