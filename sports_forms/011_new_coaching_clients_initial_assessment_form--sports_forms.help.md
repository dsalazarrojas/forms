# New Coaching Clients Initial Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from new coaching clients, helping the coaching business understand their needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide the date of your first contact with the coaching business.
3. Describe your coaching experience and goals.
4. List your emergency contact's name, phone number, and relationship with you.
5. Choose your preferred coaching style and training frequency.
6. Select your training location and duration.
7. Choose your emergency contact's relationship level.
8. Optionally, provide your emergency contact's address, phone type, phone number, and phone carrier.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email** (`email`, text, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of First Contact** (`date_of_first_contact`, text, required): Enter the date of your first contact with the coaching business.
* **Coaching Experience** (`coaching_experience`, text, optional): Describe your previous coaching experience.
* **Goals** (`goals`, text, optional): Describe your goals for coaching.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, text, optional): Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): Choose the relationship between your emergency contact and you.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Other** (`emergency_contact_relationship_other`, text, optional): Enter any other relationship details if not listed.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Other Details** (`emergency_contact_relationship_other_details`, text, optional): Enter any other details about your emergency contact relationship.
* **Coaching Style** (`coaching_style`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred coaching style.
* **Training Frequency** (`training_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Select your preferred training frequency.
* **Training Location** (`training_location`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred training location.
* **Training Duration** (`training_duration`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred training duration.
* **Training Schedule** (`training_schedule`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your preferred training schedule.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Level** (`emergency_contact_relationship_level`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the relationship level of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Type** (`emergency_contact_phone_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`emergency_contact_phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Extension** (`emergency_contact_phone_extension`, text, optional): Enter the extension of your emergency contact phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Carrier** (`emergency_contact_phone_carrier`, text, optional): Enter the phone carrier of your emergency contact.
