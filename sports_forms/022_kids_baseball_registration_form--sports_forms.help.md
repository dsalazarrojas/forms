# Kids Baseball Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Kids Baseball Registration Form is a crucial document that helps the baseball league's administrators gather information about each participant's registration for the upcoming season. This form is designed to collect essential details about the participant, their parents, and their interests in a concise and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the First Name, Last Name, and Date Of Birth sections accurately. These fields are mandatory to ensure proper identification and record-keeping.
2. Enter your email address in the Parent Email field. While not mandatory, this will help us contact you with updates, reminders, and important information.
3. Enter your phone number in the Phone field. This will help us reach you in case of any questions or updates.
4. Add any relevant notes or comments in the Parent Note section. This will help us understand your concerns or questions.
5. Select your consent in the Parent Consent field. Please choose from the provided options, "I consent" or "I do not consent." This is a mandatory field to ensure we have the necessary permissions to proceed with the registration.
6. If applicable, enter your child's name, school, and parent's occupation in their respective fields. These details will help us tailor the program to their needs and interests.
7. Select the sports your child is interested in from the Kids Sport field. Please choose one or more options from the available sports, such as Baseball, Soccer, or Football.

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your child's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your child's last name.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your child's date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact and updates.
* **Parent Note** (`parent_note`, note, optional): Add any relevant comments or questions you may have.
* **Parent Consent** (`parent_consent`, select_one, required): Select "I consent" to give us the necessary permissions to proceed.
* **Kids Interest** (`kids_interest`, text, optional): Enter your child's interests, which will help us tailor the program to their needs and preferences.
* **Parent Address** (`parent_address`, text, optional): Enter your parent's home address for contact and administrative purposes.
* **Kids Name** (`kids_name`, text, optional): Enter your child's name (if different from your name).
* **Kids School** (`kids_school`, text, optional): Enter your child's school name and grade level.
* **Parent Education** (`parent_education`, text, optional): Enter your parent's education level.
* **Kids Sport** (`kids_sport`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more sports your child is interested in, such as Baseball, Soccer, or Football.
