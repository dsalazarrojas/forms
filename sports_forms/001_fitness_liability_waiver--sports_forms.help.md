# Fitness Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document participant's consent, medical information, and emergency contact details prior to engaging in a physical activity or program. It's a crucial document to be filled out by all participants to protect themselves and the activity/program provider from potential legal liabilities.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. **Ensure you have all necessary information**: Before starting, ensure you have all the required details, such as participant's name, address, and emergency contact information.
2. **Fill in General Information**: Enter your name, date, and participant's name in the respective fields.
3. **Confirm Participation and Liability**: Select 'Yes' for both participant and witness signature fields, indicating your agreement to participate and acknowledge the risks involved.
4. **Provide Medical Information**: Enter the participant's address and medical conditions (if any).
5. **Emergency Contact**: Enter the emergency contact's name and phone number.
6. **Sign and Date**: Sign and date the form, indicating your agreement to the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (Required): Enter your name, date, and participant's name to identify yourself and the participant.
* **Participant Signature** (Required): Select 'Yes' to confirm that the participant has read, understands, and signs the waiver.
* **Witness Signature** (Required): Select 'Yes' to confirm that the witness has read, understands, and signs the waiver as a witness.
* **Liability Statement** (Required): Briefly describe any medical conditions, allergies, or concerns that may impact your participation.
* **Date** (Required): Enter the date of signing the waiver.
* **Signature** (Optional): Sign the form to confirm your agreement to the terms.
* **Participant Name** (Required): Enter the participant's full name.
* **Participant Address** (Required): Enter the participant's home or contact address.
* **Participant Phone** (Optional): Enter the participant's phone number for emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact** (Required): Enter the emergency contact's name and phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Optional): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled in correctly and accurately to avoid any issues during the activity or program.
* If you have any medical concerns or conditions, please list them in the Liability Statement field.
* The participant's emergency contact information should be up-to-date and accurate.
* The form should be completed on the specified date, not before or after.
* A witness should be present to sign and confirm the participant's signature.
