# Fitness Membership Card Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Membership Card Application Form is used to collect information for a fitness membership application. This form is intended for individuals, couples, or families who wish to join a fitness club or gym.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the relevant fields as follows:

* Select your membership period (Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly) on page 1.
* On page 2, select whether you have read and accepted the terms and conditions ('Yes' or 'No').
* On page 3, enter your membership start date and end date.
* On page 4, select your membership type (Individual, Couple, Family) and membership status (Active, Inactive).
* On page 5, enter your membership card number.
* On page 6, enter your membership card expiration date.
* On page 7, select whether you have accepted the terms and conditions ('Yes' or 'No').

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Membership Period (Page 1)
* **Membership Period** (Select multiple): Select your desired membership period (Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly) for your fitness membership.

### Terms and Conditions (Page 2)
* **Terms and Conditions** (Select multiple): Select 'Yes' if you have read and accepted the terms and conditions of the fitness club.

### Accept Terms (Page 3)
* **Accept Terms** (Note): Enter your acceptance of the terms and conditions. This field is not required.

### Membership Start Date (Page 3)
* **Membership Start Date** (Date): Enter the date you would like your membership to start.

### Membership End Date (Page 3)
* **Membership End Date** (Date): Enter the date you would like your membership to end.

### Membership Type (Page 4)
* **Membership Type** (Select one): Select your membership type (Individual, Couple, Family).

### Membership Status (Page 4)
* **Membership Status** (Select one): Select your membership status (Active, Inactive).

### Membership Card Number (Page 5)
* **Membership Card Number** (Number): Enter your membership card number.

### Membership Card Expiration Date (Page 5)
* **Membership Card Expiration Date** (Date): Enter the expiration date of your membership card.

### Accept Terms and Conditions (Page 7)
* **Accept Terms and Conditions** (Select one): Select 'Yes' if you have accepted the terms and conditions of the fitness club. This field is not required.
