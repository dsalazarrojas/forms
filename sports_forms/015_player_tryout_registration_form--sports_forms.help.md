# Player Tryout Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for players to register for a tryout session with a sports team. The information collected will be used to evaluate player suitability for the team.

## How To Complete This Form

To register for a tryout session, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Start Form" button to begin.
2. Select the tryout date from the provided date field.
3. Enter the player's name in the "Player Name" field.
4. Provide the player's contact information in the "Player Contact Info" field.
5. Select the player's team affiliation (if any) in the "Team Name" field.
6. Choose the player's position on the team in the "Player Position" field.
7. Select the player's height from the provided height options.
8. Select the player's weight from the provided weight options.
9. Select the player's speed from the provided speed options.
10. Choose the tryout location from the provided location options.
11. Enter the tryout time in the format "HH:MM".
12. Review and submit the form once all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tryout Date**: Enter the date of the tryout session you are registering for.
* **Player Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Player Contact Info**: Provide your contact information, such as phone number and email address.
* **Team Name**: If you are affiliated with a team, select your team name from the options provided.
* **Player Position**: Choose your position on the team, such as 1st, 2nd, or 3rd.
* **Height**: Select your height from the provided options (e.g., 6ft 0in, 6ft 1in, etc.).
* **Weight**: Select your weight from the provided options (e.g., 120-129, 130-139, etc.).
* **Speed**: Select your speed from the provided options (e.g., 5-5.9, 6-6.9, etc.).
* **Tryout Location**: Choose the location of the tryout session you are attending.
* **Tryout Time**: Enter the time of the tryout session in the format "HH:MM".

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and truthfully.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the team's administration for assistance.
* Review your form submission before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out.
