# Baseball Simulator Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Baseball Simulator Waiver Form is designed to collect personal and session-specific information from participants before entering a baseball simulator session. This form is required to be filled out accurately and completely to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form accurately and completely.
2.  For each field, ensure you provide the required information.
3.  Review the form carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required): Select your gender from the given options.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact and communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your best phone number for emergency contact.
* **Home Address** (`home_address`, text, required): Enter your complete street address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter your city.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter your state.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter your postal code.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required): Explain how you are related to your emergency contact.
* **Visit Date** (`visit_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your simulator session.
* **Visit Time** (`visit_time`, time, required): Enter the scheduled time of your visit.
* **Simulator Type** (`simulator_type`, select_one, required): Select which type of simulator you will be using.
* **Baseball Experience Level** (`experience_level`, select_one, required): Select your experience level with baseball.
* **Medical Conditions or Injuries** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions or injuries, please list them here.
* **Physical Limitations** (`physical_limitations`, text, optional): If you have any physical limitations, please list them here.
* **Medical Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, text, optional): If you have a medical insurance provider, please enter their name.
* **Assumption of Risk** (`assumption_of_risk`, select_one, required): Understand and accept the risks involved with simulator use.
* **Liability Release** (`liability_release`, select_one, required): Agree to release the facility from liability for ordinary negligence.
* **Equipment Use Agreement** (`equipment_use`, select_one, required): Agree to use equipment as instructed.
* **Facility Rules Agreement** (`facility_rules`, select_one, required): Agree to follow all facility rules.
* **Photo and Video Consent** (`photo_video_consent`, select_one, required): Agree to the use of photos or videos during your session.
* **Is Participant Under 18** (`minor_participant`, select_one, required): If you are under 18, select "Yes" and provide parental consent.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_guardian_name`, text, optional): If you are under 18, enter the name of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature** (`parent_guardian_signature`, text, optional): If you are under 18, enter the signature of your parent or guardian.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, text, required): Enter your full name as a signature.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your signature.
* **Staff Witness Initials** (`staff_witness`, text, optional): For facility use.

Please ensure you review and understand each field carefully before submission. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to the facility staff for clarification.
