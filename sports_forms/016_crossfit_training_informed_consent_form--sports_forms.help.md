# Crossfit Training Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential information from individuals participating in CrossFit training sessions. The data collected will be used to ensure your safety and provide emergency contact information in case of an injury.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the **First Name** field.
2. Provide your email address in the **Email** field.
3. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
4. If you have any medical conditions that may impact your ability to participate in CrossFit training, please list them in the **Medical Condition** field.
5. Enter the first name of your emergency contact person in the **Emergency Contact First Name** field.
6. Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person in the **Emergency Contact Relationship** field.
7. Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person in the **Emergency Contact Phone** field.
8. Provide the email address of your emergency contact person in the **Emergency Contact Email** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter a valid email address that you check frequently.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter a phone number that we can reach you at in case of an emergency.
* **Medical Condition** (Text, Optional): If you have any medical conditions that may impact your ability to participate in CrossFit training, list them here.
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the first name of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (Text, Optional): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person (e.g., spouse, parent, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (Email, Optional): Enter the email address of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone 2** - **Emergency Contact Phone 10** (Text, Optional): Enter additional phone numbers for emergency contact persons (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact Relationship 2** - **Emergency Contact Relationship 10** (Text, Optional): Describe the relationship with the additional emergency contact person (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact Email 2** - **Emergency Contact Email 10** (Email, Optional): Enter the email address of the additional emergency contact person (if applicable).

Note: Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely, especially in case of emergency contact information.
