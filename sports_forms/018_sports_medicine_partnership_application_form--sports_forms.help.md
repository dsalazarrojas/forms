# Sports Medicine Partnership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your interest in partnering with our sports medicine organization. By completing this form, you will be able to showcase your qualifications, services, and payment terms, making it easier for us to assess your partnership potential.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

* Fill out all the required fields (marked as required).
* For fields with multiple options, select all the services you offer.
* Enter accurate information in the text fields.
* Ensure you have filled out all sections thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Person Name** (contact_person_name): Enter the name of the person responsible for this application.
* **Contact Person Email** (contact_person_email): Enter the email address of the contact person. This will help us stay in touch with you.
* **Contact Person Phone** (contact_person_phone): Enter the phone number of the contact person. This is optional, but helps us to contact you quickly.
* **Organization Name** (organization_name): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Sports Medical License** (sports_medical_license): Select the type of sports medical license you hold (e.g., option 1, option 2, or option 3).
* **Services Offered** (services_offered): Select all the services you offer (e.g., option.report, option.training, option.consulting).
* **Report** (report): This is a note field for any additional information you want to share.
* **Partnership Start Date** (partnership_start_date): Enter the start date of your proposed partnership.
* **Partnership End Date** (partnership_end_date): Enter the end date of your proposed partnership.
* **Payment Terms** (payment_terms): Enter any payment terms or conditions related to your services.
* **Payment Frequency** (payment_frequency): Select how often you will make payments (e.g., weekly, monthly, or annually).
* **Payment Method** (payment_method): Select the payment method you will use (e.g., option.paypal, option.check, or option.bank transfer).
* **Bank Account Number** (bank_account_number): Enter your bank account number for payment processing.
