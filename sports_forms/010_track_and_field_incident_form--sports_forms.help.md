# Track And Field Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report incidents that occur during sports events. Please fill out the form as accurately and completely as possible.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Complete one page at a time**: The form is divided into several pages. Please complete one page before moving on to the next.
2. **Fill out required fields**: The fields marked as "required" must be completed before you can submit the form.
3. **Use the correct format**: Date fields should be in the format MM/DD/YYYY, while time fields should be in 24-hour format (HH:MM).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Incident Details
* **incident_details**: Describe the incident briefly in this field.
* **participant**: Enter the name of the participant involved in the incident.
* **incident_type**: Select the type of incident that occurred (injury, illness, or other).
* **incident_date**: Enter the date of the incident in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **incident_time**: Enter the time of the incident in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **incident_location**: Describe the location where the incident occurred.
* **incident_description**: Provide a detailed description of what happened.

### Page 2: Participant Details
* **participant_details**: Provide any additional details about the participant, such as medical conditions that may be relevant to the incident.
* **treatment**: Describe any treatment or medical care given to the participant.
* **follow_up**: Provide information on any follow-up actions taken.
* **reporting_user**: Enter your name and title (if applicable).
* **reporting_team**: Enter your team name or department.
* **reporting_date**: Enter the date you are reporting the incident, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **reporting_time**: Enter the time you are reporting the incident, in 24-hour format (HH:MM).

### Page 3: Incident Outcome and Reporting
* **incident_outcome**: Select the current status of the incident (resolved, unresolved, or pending).
* **incident_status**: Select the current status of the incident (active or closed).
* **incident_cause**: If the incident was an injury, enter "injury". If it was an illness, enter "illness". If other, select the type of incident.
* **incident_cause_description**: Provide a brief description of why the incident occurred.
* **incident_type_description**: Provide a description of the incident type selected.
* **incident_severity**: Select the severity of the incident (low, medium, or high).
* **notes**: Enter any additional comments or explanations.

### Additional Fields
* **follow_up_plan**: Provide information on any follow-up actions planned.
* **reporting_reason**: Explain why you are reporting this incident.
* **notes**: Enter any additional comments or explanations.
