# Weighted Dip Exercise Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Weighted Dip Exercise Form is designed to help track and document workout details for each participant. This form is intended for recording information about weighted dips, including the date of the workout, participant's name, exercise name, weight used, number of repetitions and sets, equipment used, and any additional notes or comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the workout in the "Workout Date" field. 
2. Type the participant's name in the "Participant Name" field.
3. Enter the name of the exercise (e.g., "Weighted Dips") in the "Exercise Name" field.
4. Enter the weight used for the exercise in the "Weight" field.
5. Enter the number of repetitions completed in the "Reps" field.
6. Enter the number of sets completed in the "Set" field.
7. Select the equipment used from the options provided in the "Equipment Used" field.
8. Add any additional comments or notes about the workout in the "Notes" field.
9. Enter any comments from the coach or trainer in the "Coach Comments" field.
10. Finally, enter the date when the form was submitted in the "Submission Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Workout Date
* **Workout Date** (date): Enter the date of the workout.

### 2. Participant Name
* **Participant Name** (text): Type the participant's name.

### 3. Exercise Name
* **Exercise Name** (text): Enter the name of the exercise, e.g., "Weighted Dips".

### 4. Weight
* **Weight** (number): Enter the weight used for the exercise.

### 5. Reps
* **Reps** (number): Enter the number of repetitions completed.

### 6. Set
* **Set** (number): Enter the number of sets completed.

### 7. Equipment Used
* **Equipment Used** (select multiple): Select the equipment used from the options provided.

### 8. Notes
* **Notes** (note): Add any additional comments or notes about the workout.

### 9. Coach Comments
* **Coach Comments** (text): Enter any comments from the coach or trainer.

### 10. Submission Date
* **Submission Date** (date): Enter the date when the form was submitted.

## Tips

* Use the same format for all dates entered in the form (e.g., MM/DD/YY).
* Ensure that you select the correct equipment used for the exercise.
* Use the "Notes" field for any additional comments or observations.
* Keep the "Coach Comments" field for any comments from the coach or trainer.
* Review and edit your form before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.
