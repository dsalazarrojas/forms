# Junior Team Tennis Medical Release And Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Junior Team Tennis Medical Release And Consent Form is a document that must be completed by parents or guardians of children participating in Junior Team Tennis programs. It is designed to collect medical information, obtain consent for medical treatment, and ensure that parents or guardians are aware of and agree to the terms of participation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately, making sure to provide all required information.
2. The form is divided into several sections, each with its own specific purpose:
	* **Medical Release and Consent**: This section requires parents or guardians to provide medical information about their child, including any medical conditions that may affect their participation in the program.
	* **Medical Info**: This section is used to gather any additional medical information that may be relevant to the child's participation in the program.
	* **Contact Info**: This section is used to provide contact information for the parent or guardian.
	* **Emergency Contact**: This section is used to provide emergency contact information for the child.
	* **Medical Conditions**: This section is used to list any medical conditions that may affect the child's participation in the program.
	* **Medications**: This section is used to list any medications the child is currently taking.
	* **Allergies**: This section is used to list any allergies the child may have.
	* **Medical Authorization**: This section requires parents or guardians to sign and date a statement authorizing medical treatment for their child.
	* **Parent/Guarantor**: This section requires parents or guardians to sign their name, indicating they are the parent or guarantor of the child.
	* **Date**: This section requires the date on which the form is signed.
	* **Medical Physician Info**: This section is used to provide information about the child's medical physician.
	* **Doctor Signature**: This section requires the medical physician to sign, indicating they have reviewed and authorized the form.
	* **Physician Signature**: This section is used to provide the signature of the medical physician.
	* **Emergency Contact Info**: This section is used to provide emergency contact information for the child.
	* **Emergency Contact Phone**: This section is used to provide the emergency contact phone number for the child.
	* **Emergency Contact Email**: This section is used to provide the emergency contact email for the child.
	* **Emergency Contact Address**: This section is used to provide the emergency contact address for the child.
