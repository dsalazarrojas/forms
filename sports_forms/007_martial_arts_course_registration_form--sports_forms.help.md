# Martial Arts Course Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Martial Arts Course Registration Form is designed to collect information from users interested in registering for a martial arts course. This form will guide you through the necessary steps to complete your registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal info (Section 1).
2. Provide your contact info (Section 2).
3. Select your current belt status (Section 3).
4. Choose your preferred payment plan (Section 4).
5. Enter your emergency contact information (Section 5).
6. Share your medical history (Section 6).
7. Select your preferred course package (Section 7).
8. Choose your preferred payment method (Section 8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Info (Section 1)**: Enter your name and any other personal details as requested.
* **Contact Info (Section 2)**: Provide your email address and phone number to ensure we can contact you about your registration.
* **Belt Status (Section 3)**: Select the current belt status you hold in your martial arts journey. This will help us understand your current level of expertise.
* **Payment Plan (Section 4)**: Choose your preferred payment plan to suit your needs. Select one or more options from the list.
* **Emergency Contact (Section 5)**: Enter the contact information for your emergency contact person.
* **Medical History (Section 6)**: Please share any relevant medical history to ensure we can accommodate any special needs.
* **Course Package (Section 7)**: Select the course package that suits your interests and goals.
* **Payment Method (Section 8)**: Choose your preferred payment method: Card, Cash, or Check.
