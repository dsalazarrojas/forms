# Prospect Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prospect Questionnaire Form is designed to collect essential contact information, preferences, and interests from potential customers or clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
5. Indicate your interests in the "What are your interests?" field.
6. Select an option from the dropdown menu in the "Why did you find this form?" field.
7. If you have any additional comments or questions, type them in the "Is there anything else you would like to ask us about?" field.
8. If you have any further information, you can add it to the "Additional Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Information
* **Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter your full name for our records.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for us to contact you.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter a phone number where we can reach you.
* **Address** (`contact_address`, text, required): Enter your physical address for our records.

### Additional Information
* **What are your interests?** (`prospect_interests`, text, optional): Select your interests from the options provided or type in your own answer.
* **Why did you find this form?** (`prospect_why_form`, select_one, optional): Choose an option from the dropdown menu that best answers this question.
* **Is there anything else you would like to ask us about?** (`prospect_message`, note, optional): If you have any questions or comments, type them in this field.
* **Additional Information** (`prospect_message_2`, note, optional): This field is for you to provide any additional context or information about your inquiry.

## Tips
Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that we can provide the best possible service to you.
