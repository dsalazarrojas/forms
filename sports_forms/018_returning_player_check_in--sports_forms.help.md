# Returning Player Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Returning Player Check In form is designed to collect information from returning players about their status and agreement to participate in the upcoming season.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your player information, including your name and contact details.
2. Acknowledge and agree to the team's policies and procedures by selecting "Yes" for each relevant question.
3. Confirm your returning player status by selecting "Active" or "Inactive" from the dropdown menu.
4. Confirm if you have the necessary documents required for the season, such as insurance cards, etc.
5. Confirm your consent to participate in the season by selecting "Yes".
6. Confirm if the team has acknowledged your agreement to the season's policies and procedures by selecting "Yes" from the dropdown menu.
7. Select the season start and end dates for the upcoming season.
