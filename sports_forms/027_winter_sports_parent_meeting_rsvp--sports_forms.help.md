# Winter Sports Parent Meeting RSVP - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect RSVPs from parents of winter sports participants. Please answer the questions below to provide your attendance status and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Choose whether you will be attending the parent meeting (`Parent Attendance`).
* Optionally, provide your name in the `What is your name?` field.
* Decide if you are bringing any guests (`Are you bringing any guests?`).
* If you are bringing guests, specify their names in the text field (not applicable in this guide).
* Indicate your arrival time in the correct format (24-hour time, e.g., "12:30 PM").

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Parent Attendance
• **Parent Attendance** (`parent_attendance`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose whether you will be attending the parent meeting. Options are `Yes` or `No`.

### Headcount
• **Headcount** (`headcount`, `number`, required: false): Enter the number of people in your party. Note: This field is currently empty and not used in the form.

### What is your name?
• **What is your name?** (`question_1`, `select_one`, required: false): Enter your name, or choose from the options provided.

### Are you bringing any guests?
• **Are you bringing any guests?** (`question_2`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose whether you are bringing any guests.

### What time do you arrive?
• **What time do you arrive?** (`question_3`, `time`, required: false): Enter your arrival time in the 24-hour format. For example, "12:30 PM".

## Tips
* Please ensure that your arrival time is accurate to ensure smooth attendance and planning.
* If you are bringing guests, please specify their names in the text field (not applicable in this guide).
