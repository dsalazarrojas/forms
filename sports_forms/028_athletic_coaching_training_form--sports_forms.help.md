# Athletic Coaching Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from athletes about their training goals, experience, and availability to provide effective coaching and guidance. It will help coaches understand your strengths, weaknesses, and needs to create a tailored training plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page and fill in the required fields. If you're unsure about any question, please ask your coach for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Full Name** (1, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Athlete ID Number** (2, text, optional): If you have a student or team identification number, please enter it here.
* **Date of Birth** (3, date, required): Enter your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format (e.g., 12-31-1995).
* **Gender** (4, select_one, required): Please select your gender from the options provided.
* **Sport** (5, select_one, required): Select your primary sport from the options listed.
* **Primary Position** (6, text, required): Enter the position you play in your sport.
* **Team Level** (7, select_one, required): Indicate the level of competition you participate in (e.g., Varsity, Junior Varsity, Club Team).
* **Years of Experience** (8, number, required): Enter the number of years you have played your sport.
* **Coach Name** (9, text, required): Enter the name of your primary coach.
* **Coach Email** (10, email, required): Enter your coach's email address.
* **Primary Training Goals** (11, text, required): Describe your main training goals and objectives.
* **Current Fitness Level** (12, select_one, required): Rate your current fitness level from the options provided.
* **Training Availability** (13, select_multiple, required): Select the times of day and days you are available for training.
* **Days Available Per Week** (14, number, required): Enter the number of days you are available for training per week.
* **Preferred Training Focus** (15, select_multiple, required): Choose the areas of focus for your training.
* **Injury History** (16, text, required): List any significant injuries you have had in the past.
* **Current Injuries or Limitations** (17, text, required): Describe any current issues or injuries you are dealing with.
* **Medical Clearance Status** (18, select_one, required): Indicate your current medical clearance status from the options provided.
* **Previous Training Experience** (19, text, optional): Describe any previous training programs or experiences.
* **Equipment Access** (20, select_multiple, required): Indicate the equipment you have access to for training.
* **Current Nutrition Habits** (21, text, optional): Describe your typical diet and nutrition habits.
* **Sleep Habits** (22, number, required): Enter the number of hours you sleep per night.
* **Stress Level** (23, select_one, required): Rate your current stress level from the options provided.
* **Motivation Level** (24, select_one, required): Rate your motivation level from the options provided.
* **Communication Preference** (25, select_one, required): Indicate your preferred method of communication with your coach.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (26, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone** (27, text, optional): If applicable, enter the contact number of your parent or guardian.
* **Additional Information** (28, text, optional): Provide any additional information that will help your coach understand you better.
