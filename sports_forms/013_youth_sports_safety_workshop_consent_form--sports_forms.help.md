# Youth Sports Safety Workshop Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect necessary contact and emergency information from parents or guardians for their child's safety during sports workshops. It is essential to complete this form accurately and honestly to ensure the well-being and safety of the child.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out this form for each child attending the workshop.
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information about the child's parents or guardians.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact the workshop organizers for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Permits** (`parent_permissions`, text, required): Enter the name(s) of the parent(s) permitted for this workshop. This is a crucial field as it ensures that only authorized individuals can pick up the child from the workshop.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact person for the child. This person will be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Health Details** (`health_details`, text, optional): If the child has any health issues or allergies, please provide a brief description.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): List any medical conditions or allergies the child has.
* **Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, text, optional): Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the emergency contact person.
* **Workshop** (`emergency_contact_workshop`, select_one, required): Select the workshop that the child is attending. This will ensure that the emergency contact is aware of the workshop details.
* **Coach** (`emergency_contact_coach`, select_multiple, optional): Select the coaches or instructors the child will be working with during the workshop. This is an optional field but helps identify the people who will be directly interacting with the child.
* **Other** (`emergency_contact_other`, text, optional): If there's any other relevant information about the emergency contact, please provide it here.
