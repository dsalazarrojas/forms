# Fitness And Health Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your fitness and health habits. The goal is to understand your perceptions of your general health, physical education participation, and habits.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form by selecting the answers that best describe your current situation.
2. Ensure that you complete all required fields to ensure accurate and complete data.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_information`, text, optional): Please provide your basic information, including your name, email, or any other relevant details you'd like to share with us.
* **General Health** (`general_health`, select_one, optional): Select how you perceive your current general health status:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Bad
* **Physical Education Participation** (`physical_education_participation`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate how often you participate in physical education:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Rarely
	+ Never
* **Health Habits** (`health_habits`, text, optional): Describe your daily habits, such as diet, exercise, or any other relevant habits you have.
* **Fitness Activities** (`fitness_activities`, select_multiple, optional): Select the activities you regularly perform for fitness:
	+ Running
	+ Biking
	+ Dancing
	+ Hiking
* **Health Status** (`health_status`, select_one, optional): Select how you perceive your overall health status:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Bad
* **Physical Education Classes** (`physical_education_classes`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate how often you attend physical education classes:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Rarely
	+ Never
* **Physical Education Frequency** (`physical_education_frequency`, select_one, optional): Select how often you participate in physical education:
	+ Less than once a week
	+ 1-2 times a week
	+ 3-4 times a week
	+ Almost every day
* **Physical Education Duration** (`physical_education_duration`, select_one, optional): Select the average duration of your physical education sessions:
	+ Less than 30 minutes
	+ 30-60 minutes
	+ 1-2 hours
	+ 2-4 hours
* **Physical Education Frequency Of Classes** (`physical_education_frequency_of_classes`, select_one, optional): Select how often you attend physical education classes:
	+ Never
	+ 1-2 per month
	+ 2-4 per month
	+ 5 or more per month
