# training-template-u13-u15-sports-forms - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information for training templates specific to the U13 and U15 sports age groups. It allows users to input data on training sessions, including the sport, date, time, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the training template type:
	* Choose "Yes" or "No" for the training template.
2. Select the sport:
	* Choose one of the available sports (Football, Basketball, Soccer, Baseball).
3. Enter the date:
	* Enter the date of the training session using the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
4. Enter the time:
	* Enter the time of the training session using the 24-hour clock format (HH:MM).
5. Enter note (optional):
	* Provide any additional comments or notes about the training session.
6. Enter email and phone (optional):
	* Enter contact information for further communication.
7. Select the assigned tool:
	* Choose "Chatjimmy" or "Other" as the assigned tool.
