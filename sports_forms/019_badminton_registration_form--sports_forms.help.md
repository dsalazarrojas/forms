# Badminton Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to register new players in the badminton club. It collects information about the player's personal details, membership preferences, playing schedule, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required information, such as full name, date of birth, email address, phone number, and other essential details.
2. Choose your membership type, playing experience level, and preferred playing schedule.
3. Select your preferred payment method and provide the annual membership fee.
4. If applicable, provide any medical conditions or injuries and acknowledge the liability waiver.
5. If required, select your preferred court/facility and provide any special requests or accommodations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (player_name)**: Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Date of Birth (date_of_birth)**: Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Email Address (email_address)**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number (phone_number)**: Enter a valid phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Emergency Contact Name (emergency_contact)**: Enter the name of a contact person to reach out in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (emergency_phone)**: Enter a valid phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Membership Type (membership_type)**: Choose your membership type from the provided options.
* **Playing Experience Level (experience_level)**: Select your playing experience level from the provided options.
* **Preferred Playing Days (preferred_days)**: Select all days you are available to play.
* **Preferred Time Slots (preferred_times)**: Select your preferred time slot to play.
* **Preferred Number of Sessions Per Week (sessions_per_week)**: Enter the number of sessions you prefer to play per week.
* **Preferred Court/Facility (preferred_court)**: Enter your preferred court/facility to play.
* **Annual Membership Fee (annual_fee)**: Enter the annual membership fee you are willing to pay.
* **Preferred Payment Method (payment_method)**: Choose your preferred payment method.
* **Medical Conditions or Injuries (medical_conditions)**: If you have any medical conditions or injuries, please let us know.
* **Liability Waiver (liability_waiver)**: Acknowledge that you agree to the liability waiver.
* **Medical Clearance Obtained (medical_clearance)**: If you are a high-risk individual, please provide medical clearance.
* **Special Requests or Accommodations (special_requests)**: If you have any special requests or accommodations, please let us know.

Note: All fields marked as required must be completed to submit the form.
