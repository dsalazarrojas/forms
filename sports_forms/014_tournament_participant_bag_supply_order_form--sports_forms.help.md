# Tournament Participant Bag Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tournament Participant Bag Supply Order Form is designed to collect information from participants regarding their bag supply orders. This information includes payment details, participant contact information, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your participant name and other relevant contact information in the "Participant Name", "Participant Email", "Participant Phone", "Participant Address", "Participant Zip", "Participant City", "Participant State", and "Participant Country" fields.
2.  Select the "Bag Type" (Standard, Premium, or VIP) that suits your preference.
3.  Choose the "Participant Order" (Bag A, Bag B, or Bag C) that you wish to order.
4.  Enter the quantity of bags you wish to purchase.
5.  If you have chosen a non-standard payment method, enter the corresponding payment details in the "Payment Method" field.
6.  Select the "Delivery Date" for the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the participant who is placing the order.
*   **Participant Bag Order** (`participant_order`, select_one, required): Select the type of bag that you wish to order (Bag A, Bag B, or Bag C).
*   **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Enter the number of bags you wish to purchase.
*   **Bag Type** (`bag_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of bag that you wish to order (Standard, Premium, or VIP).
*   **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required): Enter the date you wish to have the order delivered.
*   **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select the payment method you wish to use (Bank Transfer, Cash Payment, or Credit Card).
*   **Participant Contact Information** (`participant_email`, text, required): Enter the email of the participant who is placing the order.
*   **Participant Contact Information** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the participant who is placing the order.
*   **Participant Address** (`participant_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the participant who is placing the order.
*   **Participant Zip** (`participant_zip`, text, required): Enter the zip code of the participant who is placing the order.
*   **Participant City** (`participant_city`, text, required): Enter the city of the participant who is placing the order.
*   **Participant State** (`participant_state`, text, required): Enter the state of the participant who is placing the order.
*   **Participant Country** (`participant_country`, text, required): Enter the country of the participant who is placing the order.

This form is designed to be used by participants to place orders for tournament bags. By filling out the form, participants can select their preferred bag type, quantity, and delivery date. The form also requires payment details and participant contact information for accurate and timely delivery.
