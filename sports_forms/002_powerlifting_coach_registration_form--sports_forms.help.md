# Powerlifting Coach Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Powerlifting Coach Registration Form is designed for powerlifting coaches to register on our platform. It helps us verify their contact information and experience in the powerlifting industry to provide them with access to our services and platform resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address, ensuring it's accurate and valid.
3. Provide your Phone Number for easy contact.
4. Enter your Address for our records.
5. If you have any experience in powerlifting, please specify the Date Joined and Experience Years fields. This is optional but recommended.
6. Finally, review all your entered information and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (<type:text>, required: false): This field is used to input your first name.
* **Last Name** (<type:text, required: false): This field is used to input your last name.
* **Email** (<type:email, required: false): Please provide a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (<type:text, required: false): Enter your phone number for easier communication.
* **Address** (<type:text, required: false): Enter your physical address for our records.
* **Date Joined** (<type:date, required: false): If you have experience in powerlifting, enter the date you started.
* **Experience Years** (<type:number, required: false): If you have experience, provide the number of years you have been in the field.
