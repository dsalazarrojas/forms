# Adult Sports Roster Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adult Sports Roster Form is used to collect team information for adult sports teams, including the team's name, captain's name and email, and the names and emails of all team members. This form is designed to be filled out by team captains or representatives and is used to register their team for league participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Team Name" field with the name of your team.
2. Enter the name and email of the captain in the "Captain Name" and "Captain Email" fields, respectively.
3. Select the sport type that your team plays from the list of options in the "Sport Type" field.
4. Select the skill level of your team, such as competitive, recreational, or social, in the "League Division" field.
5. If you have more than 5 players on your team, enter their names and emails in the "Player 1 Name", "Player 2 Name", "Player 3 Name", "Player 4 Name", and "Player 5 Name" fields, and their corresponding email addresses in the "Player 1 Email", "Player 2 Email", "Player 3 Email", "Player 4 Email", and "Player 5 Email" fields.
6. If you have additional players on your team, list them in the "Additional Players List" field.
7. Check the "All Players Met Age Requirements" field to ensure that all team members meet the age requirements for league participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Name**: Enter the name of your team.
* **Captain Name**: Enter the name of the captain or the first player on your team.
* **Captain Email**: Enter the email address of the captain or the first player on your team.
* **Sport Type**: Select the sport type that your team plays from the list of options.
* **League Division**: Select the skill level of your team, such as competitive, recreational, or social.
* **Player 1 Name** :Enter the name of the second player on your team (if applicable).
* **Player 1 Email** :Enter the email address of the second player (if applicable).
* **Player 2 Name**: Enter the name of the third player on your team (if applicable).
* **Player 2 Email**: Enter the email address of the third player (if applicable).
* **Player 3 Name**: Enter the name of the fourth player on your team (if applicable).
* **Player 3 Email**: Enter the email address of the fourth player (if applicable).
* **Player 4 Name**: Enter the name of the fifth player on your team (if applicable).
* **Player 4 Email**: Enter the email address of the fifth player (if applicable).
* **Player 5 Name**: Enter the name of the sixth player on your team (if applicable).
* **Player 5 Email**: Enter the email address of the sixth player (if applicable).
* **Additional Players List**: List the names and email addresses of additional players on your team in this field.
* **All Players Met Age Requirements**: Select "True" if all team members meet the age requirements for league participation, or "False" if any team members do not meet the requirements.
