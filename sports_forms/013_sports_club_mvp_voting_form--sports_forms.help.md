# Sports Club MVP Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Club MVP Voting Form is a tool used to collect votes from club members to determine the Most Valuable Player in the sports club competition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your team rating for each of the three teams competing in the sports club competition.
2. Choose your favorite team in the "Vote 1" and "Vote 2" fields.
3. Provide your feedback or comments about the competition or teams, if desired.
4. Enter your email address and phone number for communication purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Team**: Enter the name of your team, e.g., Team A, Team B, Team C.
- **Team Rating 1**: Rate your team's performance on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is poor and 10 is excellent.
- **Team Rating 2**: Rate your team's performance on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is poor and 10 is excellent.
- **Team Rating 3**: Rate your team's performance on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is poor and 10 is excellent.
- **Vote 1**: Select your top choice for the MVP award. Choose one of the three teams competing in the sports club competition.
- **Vote 2**: Select your second choice for the MVP award. Choose one of the three teams competing in the sports club competition.
- **Feedback**: Provide any comments or feedback about the competition or teams, if desired.
- **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.

## Tips
- Be honest and objective when rating your team's performance.
- Choose your true favorites for the MVP award.
- Keep your feedback clear and concise.
