# Fitness Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect your feedback about your fitness experiences. Your input will help us understand how we can improve our services to better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, start by filling out the required fields first.
2. Make sure to provide your name and email address to help us contact you in case we need to follow up on your feedback.
3. Rate your experience by selecting a number between 1 and 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
4. Finally, type in your comments or suggestions in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your feedback.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required): Choose a rating between 1 and 5 to express your opinion about your fitness experience.
* **Comments** (`comment`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or suggestions you have about your experience.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible with your comments to help us understand your concerns.
* If you have any questions or need clarification about this form, you can always contact us directly.
