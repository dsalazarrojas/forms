# sports_team_volunteer_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a volunteer application form for sports teams or programs. It helps us understand your qualifications, interests, and availability to support our activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **What is your name?**: Enter your name in the text field. This is a required field.
2. **What is your availability for volunteering?**: Describe your availability in the text field. This is a required field.
3. **What sports teams or programs would you be interested in volunteering with?**: Select all the sports teams or programs that you are interested in from the multiple-choice list. This is a required field.
4. **Do you have any experience with this sport or in a similar role?**: Enter a brief description of your experience in the text field. This is a required field.
5. **What is your primary contact method (phone, email, or in person)?**: Select your preferred contact method from the multiple-choice list. This is a required field.
6. **What is your availability for volunteering in a 4-day workweek?**: Enter your availability in the time field (e.g., "Monday to Thursday 9am-5pm"). This is a required field.
7. **How many hours per week are you available to volunteer?**: Enter the number of hours you can dedicate to volunteering per week. This is a required field.
8. **Do you have any medical conditions that would affect your ability to participate in physical activities?**: Check the box if you have any medical conditions that would impact your ability to participate. This is a required field.
9. **Do you have any certifications or special skills that would be useful for volunteering with our team?**: Enter any relevant certifications or skills you have in the text field. This is not a required field.
10. **How did you hear about our team?**: Enter how you learned about our team. This is not a required field.
11. **Do you have a reliable means of transportation?**: Select your transportation method from the multiple-choice list. This is a required field.
12. **Are you a student or have you previously volunteered with our team before?**: Select your volunteer status from the radio button. This is a required field.
13. **Availability 4Day**: Enter your availability for a 4-day workweek in the time field. This is a required field.
14. **How do you want to be contacted?**: Select your preferred contact method from the multiple-choice list. This is a required field.
15. **Additional information about you or any specific volunteer experience?**: Enter any additional information you'd like to share. This is not a required field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name?** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your name so we can address you properly.
* **What is your availability for volunteering?** (`availability`, `text`, required): Describe your availability to volunteer with our team.
* **What sports teams or programs would you be interested in volunteering with?** (`volunteer_interest`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all sports teams or programs you are interested in volunteering with.
* **Do you have any experience with this sport or in a similar role?** (`experience`, `text`, required): Share your experience with the sport or a similar role.
* **What is your primary contact method (phone, email, or in person)?** (`contact_info`, `text`, required): Select your preferred contact method.
* **What is your availability for volunteering in a 4-day workweek?** (`availability_schedule`, `time`, required): Enter your availability for a 4-day workweek (e.g., "Monday to Thursday 9am-5pm").
* **How many hours per week are you available to volunteer?** (`volunteer_hours`, `number`, required): Enter the number of hours you can dedicate to volunteering per week.
* **Do you have any medical conditions that would affect your ability to participate in physical activities?** (`medical_conditions`, `checkbox`, required): Check the box if you have any medical conditions that would impact your ability to participate.
* **Do you have any certifications or special skills that would be useful for volunteering with our team?** (`certifications`, `text`, optional): Share any relevant certifications or skills you have.
* **How did you hear about our team?** (`referral_source`, `text`, optional): Tell us how you learned about our team.
* **Do you have a reliable means of transportation?** (`transportation`, `select_one`, required): Select your reliable transportation method.
* **Are you a student or have you previously volunteered with our team before?** (`previous_volunteer_status`, `radio`, required): Select your volunteer status.
* **Availability 4Day** (`availability_4day`, `time`, required): Enter your 4-day workweek availability.
* **How do you want to be contacted?** (`contact_method`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your preferred contact method.
* **Additional information about you or any specific volunteer experience?** (`additional_info`, `text`, optional): Share any additional information you'd like to share.
