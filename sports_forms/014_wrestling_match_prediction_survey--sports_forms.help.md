# wrestling_match_prediction_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect opinions and predictions on a specific wrestling match.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Predict the outcome of the match by selecting the options provided in the "Predicted Match Outcome" field.
2. Indicate your confidence level in your prediction by selecting a level between 0 and 10.
3. Provide a reason for your prediction in the "Reason for Outcome" field.
4. Select your picks for the match in the "Match Picks" field.
5. Provide a reason for your picks in the "Reason for Picks" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Predict the Match**: Select "Yes" if you think the match will occur, and "No" otherwise.
* **Confidence Level**: Select a value between 0 and 10 to indicate how confident you are in your prediction.
* **Reason for Prediction**: Explain why you made your prediction.
* **Predicted Match Outcome**: Select "Yes" if you think the match will be positive, and "No" otherwise.
* **Match Picks**: Select the options you think will win or lose the match.
* **Reason for Outcome**: Explain why you think the match will have the outcome you selected.
* **Confidence Reason**: Provide a reason for your confidence level (optional).
* **Match Picks 1**: Select the options you think will win or lose the match.
* **Match Picks 2**: Select the options you think will win or lose the match.
* **Match Picks 3**: Select the options you think will win or lose the match.
* **Match Picks 4**: Select the options you think will win or lose the match.

## Tips
* Please select only one option for each field.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact us.
