# court_rental_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a court for a specific sport or activity. Please fill out the form with your details to complete your booking request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your User Info to help us verify your identity.
2. Select the court you wish to book.
3. Choose the type of sport or activity you wish to play on the court.
4. Enter your preferred Booking Date and End Date for the session.
5. Enter your preferred Booking Time for the session.
6. Specify the duration of the booking in minutes.
7. Enter any Payment Details if required.
8. Enter the Total amount to be paid.
9. Select your preferred Payment Method.
10. Confirm the Payment Status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info (text, required/optional)**: Enter your name and any other relevant details to help us verify your identity.
* **Select Court (select_multiple, required/optional)**: Choose the court you wish to book. You can select multiple courts if available.
* **Booking Date (date, required/optional)**: Enter the date of the session in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **End Date (date, required/optional)**: Enter the end date of the session in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Court Type (select_one, required/optional)**: Choose the type of sport or activity you wish to play on the court (e.g. Tennis, Basketball, Football).
* **Booking Time (time, required/optional)**: Enter the start time of the session in the format HH:MM.
* **Duration (number, required/optional)**: Specify the duration of the booking in minutes.
* **Payment Details (text, required/optional)**: Enter any additional payment details if required.
* **Total (number, required/optional)**: Enter the total amount to be paid.
* **Payment Method (select_one, required/optional)**: Choose your preferred payment method (e.g. Credit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer).
* **Payment Status (select_one, required/optional)**: Confirm the status of your payment (e.g. Paid, Not Paid, Pending).

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are filled out to complete the booking request.
* Review and confirm your details before submitting the form.
* Payment details and status may require additional information or verification.
* The form will guide you through the booking process. If you have any issues, please contact support.
