# Sports Event Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect basic information about participants and their emergency contacts in a sports event. It is not a comprehensive medical form but rather a brief overview of the participant's details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information in the Participant Information section.
2. Provide your emergency contact's information.
3. Enter the event details.
4. Sign and date the form where indicated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information**
	+ <name> (text): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
	+ <risk_acknowledgement> (text): Acknowledge the risks associated with participating in the event.
	+ <emergency_contact> (text): Enter your emergency contact's name.
	+ <emergency_contact_relationship> (text): Specify your relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Information**
	+ <emergency_contact_name> (text): Enter your emergency contact's name.
	+ <emergency_contact_address> (text): Enter your emergency contact's address.
	+ <emergency_contact_phone> (text): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
	+ <emergency_contact_email> (text): Enter your emergency contact's email.

	+ <event_organizer> (text): Enter the event organizer's name.
