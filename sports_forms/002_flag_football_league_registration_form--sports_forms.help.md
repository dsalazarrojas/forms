# Flag Football League Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register players for the Flag Football League. Please fill out the required fields to complete the registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Provide accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
3. Ensure that each team manager's email address is unique and not already associated with another team manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Name** (`player_name`, text, required): Enter the player's name as it appears on their ID or license.
* **Player Email** (`player_email`, email, required): Enter the player's email address. This will be used to communicate with the player about league updates and information.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required): Enter the team name as registered with the league.
* **Team League** (`team_league`, text, required): Enter the team's league affiliation (e.g., "Local League" or "National League").
* **Division** (`division`, text, required): Enter the team's division (e.g., "A", "B", or "C").
* **Player Address** (`player_address`, text, required): Enter the player's address for billing and communication purposes.
* **Street** (`street`, text, required): Enter the street number and name for the player's address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city where the player resides.
* **State** (`state`, select_one, required): Select the state where the player resides from the list of options.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter the player's zip code for billing and communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, number, required): Enter the player's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Team Manager** (`team_manager`, select_one, required): Select the team manager's relationship with the player (e.g., "Player Manager", "Team Manager", or "Parent").
* **Team Manager Email** (`team_manager_email`, email, required): Enter the team manager's email address. This will be used to communicate with the team manager about league updates and information.
* **Team Manager Phone** (`team_manager_phone`, text, required): Enter the team manager's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Team Manager Address** (`team_manager_address`, text, required): Enter the team manager's address for billing and communication purposes.
* **Team Manager Street** (`team_manager_street`, text, required): Enter the team manager's street number and name.
* **Team Manager City** (`team_manager_city`, text, required): Enter the team manager's city.
* **Team Manager State** (`team_manager_state`, select_one, required): Select the team manager's state from the list of options.
* **Team Manager Zip Code** (`team_manager_zip_code`, text, required): Enter the team manager's zip code for billing and communication purposes.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select the payment method (e.g., "Cash", "Credit Card", or "Bank Transfer").
* **Payment Expiration** (`payment_expiration`, date, required): Enter the payment expiration date.
* **Payment Card Number** (`payment_card`, number, required): Enter the payment card number for credit card payments.
* **Payment Card Type** (`payment_card_type`, select_one, required): Select the payment card type (e.g., "VISA", "MC", or "Amex").
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, required): Enter any additional payment details.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Double-check your information for spelling and grammar errors before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the league administration for assistance.
