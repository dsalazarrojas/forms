# Football Match Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Football Match Incident Report Form is a tool to collect information about player injuries during a match. This form helps sports administrators or coaches to gather data on injuries, which will be used to make informed decisions about player treatment, game strategy, and potential medical attention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the player injury field with the type of injury that occurred during the match.
2. Enter the match date in the date format (mm/dd/yyyy) in the match date field.
3. Add any details about the event that led to the injury in the event details field.
4. Enter the Referee ID if available.
5. Select if the player was involved in the incident that led to the injury.
6. Enter the location where the injury occurred.
7. Provide a description of the injury.
8. Enter the time when the injury occurred.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Player Injury** (select_multiple): Select the type of injury that occurred to the player, such as Head, Neck, Arm, Back, Leg, or Other.
* **Match Date** (date): Enter the date of the match in the mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Event Details** (note): Add any details about what happened before or during the injury.
* **Referee ID** (number): Enter the ID of the referee who officiated the match, if available.
* **Player Involvement** (select_one): Check if the player was involved in the incident that led to the injury.
* **Match Location** (text): Enter the location where the match took place.
* **Injury Location** (text): Enter the specific location of the injury.
* **Injury Description** (text): Provide a detailed description of the injury.
* **Injury Time** (time): Enter the time when the injury occurred.
