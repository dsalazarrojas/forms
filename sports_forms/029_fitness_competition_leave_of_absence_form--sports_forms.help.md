# Fitness Competition Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Competition Leave Of Absence Form is designed to document participants' leave of absence from a fitness competition. It captures essential information about the participant, their reasons for leave, and the status of their leave.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your contact number to ensure we can reach you for further information.
3. Specify the competition name and date you were scheduled to participate in.
4. Provide a brief reason for your leave.
5. Select your current leave status (Active or Inactive).
6. Add any additional comments you'd like to share with the organizers.
7. If you'd like to add any comments for the organizer, please do so below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details** (participant_details, text, required: false): Please enter your name and any additional details you'd like to share about your participation in the competition.
* **Name** (name, text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (email, email, required: false): Enter your valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Contact Number** (contact_number, text, required: false): Enter your contact number where the organizer can reach you for further information.
* **Competition Name** (competition_name, text, required: false): Enter the name of the competition you were scheduled to participate in.
* **Competition Date** (competition_date, date, required: false): Select the date you were scheduled to participate in the competition.
* **Leave Reason** (leave_reason, text, required: false): Provide a brief explanation for your leave of absence.
* **Leave Status** (leave_status, select_one, required: false): Select your current leave status (Active or Inactive).
* **Comments** (comments, note, required: false): Add any additional comments you'd like to share with the organizers.
* **Organizer Comments** (organizer_comments, note, required: false): If you'd like to add any comments for the organizer, please do so below.
* **Approval Date** (approval_date, date, required: false): This field will be filled in by the organizer after reviewing your application.
