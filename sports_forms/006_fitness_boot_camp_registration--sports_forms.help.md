# Fitness Boot Camp Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a new account for a fitness boot camp member. It captures the required and optional information for the member's profile.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your **Email** in the "Email" field, following the format `username@example.com`.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Select your desired **Membership Type** from the dropdown menu. Choose "Basic" for standard membership or "Premium" for advanced features.
6. Enter your **Membership Start Date** in the "Membership Start Date" field.
7. Enter your **Membership End Date** in the "Membership End Date" field.
8. Select your **Membership Status** from the dropdown menu. Choose "Active", "Inactive", or "Expired" to reflect your current status.
9. Enter your **Assigned Tool** in the "Assigned Tool" field, if applicable.
10. Add any additional **Notes** about your account in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text): Enter your personal name.
* **Last Name** (text): Enter your surname.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address, e.g., `username@example.com`.
* **Phone Number** (text): Enter your phone number.
* **Membership Type** (select_one): Choose between "Basic" and "Premium" membership types.
* **Membership Start Date** (date): Enter your membership start date.
* **Membership End Date** (date): Enter your membership end date.
* **Membership Status** (select_multiple): Choose between "Active", "Inactive", or "Expired" membership status.
* **Assigned Tool** (text): Enter the tool assigned to your account, if applicable.
* **Notes** (note): Add any additional notes about your account.
