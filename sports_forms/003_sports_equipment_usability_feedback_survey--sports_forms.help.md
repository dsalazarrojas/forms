# Sports Equipment Usability Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather user feedback on the usability of sports equipment. Your input will help us improve the equipment and provide better experiences for users like you.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select one or more sports that you enjoy playing with this equipment.
2. Rate the overall usability of the equipment from 1 to 5.
3. Specify the reasons why you liked or disliked this equipment's overall usability.
4. Rate the overall comfort of the equipment from 1 to 5.
5. Specify the reasons why you liked or disliked this equipment's comfort.
6. Rate the overall performance of the equipment from 1 to 5.
7. Specify the reasons why you liked or disliked this equipment's performance.
8. Rate the overall price of the equipment from 1 to 5.
9. Specify the reasons why you liked or disliked this equipment's price.
10. Rate the overall ease of use of the equipment from 1 to 5.
11. Specify the reasons why you liked or disliked this equipment's ease of use.
12. Rate the overall value for money of the equipment from 1 to 5.
13. Specify the reasons why you liked or disliked this equipment's value for money.
14. Provide any overall improvement suggestions for the equipment.
15. (Optional) Specify the reasons why you liked or disliked this equipment.

## Tips

* Be honest and specific with your feedback.
* Provide examples or anecdotes to support your answers.
* Focus on the equipment's usability, comfort, performance, price, ease of use, value for money, and improvement suggestions.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Overall improvement suggestions" field.
