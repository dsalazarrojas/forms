# hockey_club_interest_uniform_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from hockey players to process their uniform orders accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Player Details** page with your general information.
2. On the **Experience Level** page, select your experience level in hockey.
3. On the **Positions** page, indicate which positions you play in hockey.
4. On the **Uniform Orders** page, select which type of uniform you are interested in purchasing.
5. On the **Payments** page, provide your payment information.
6. On the **Player Info** page, add any additional information you'd like to share about yourself.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Details** (`player_details`, text, optional): Please enter your name and contact information.
* **Experience Level** (`experience_level`, number, optional): Please select your level of experience in hockey.
	+ Level 1: Beginner
	+ Level 2: Intermediate
	+ Level 3: Advanced
* **Positions** (`positions`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the positions you play in hockey.
	+ Yes (Goalie)
	+ Yes (Defence)
	+ Yes (Forwards)
	+ No
* **Uniform Orders** (`uniform_orders`, select_multiple, optional): Please select which type of uniform you are interested in purchasing.
	+ Yes (Home Uniform)
	+ Yes (Away Uniform)
	+ No
* **Payments** (`payments`, text, optional): Please provide your payment information.
* **Player Info** (`player_info`, text, optional): Please add any additional information you'd like to share about yourself.
