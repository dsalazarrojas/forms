# wrestling_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for wrestling event registration, allowing users to record information about a wrestler's participation in an event. It collects details such as the wrestler's name, club, and event particulars.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required and optional information.
2. Ensure you enter accurate and correct information for fields like event date and match time.
3. For optional fields, provide relevant details if possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wrestler Name** (1, text, required): Enter the name of the wrestler participating in the event. 
* **Club Name** (2, text, optional): Enter the name of the club the wrestler is affiliated with. 
* **Event Date** (3, date, optional): Enter the date of the event in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`. 
* **Match Time** (4, time, optional): Enter the time of the match in the format `HH:MM:SS`. 
* **Wrestler Weight** (5, text, optional): Enter the weight of the wrestler. 
* **Event Weight** (6, text, optional): Enter the weight of the event. 
* **Event Type** (7, select multiple, optional): Choose one or more options for event type. 
* **Team Name** (8, text, optional): Enter the name of the team. 
* **Event Location** (9, text, optional): Enter the location of the event. 
* **Match Result** (10, select one, optional): Choose the result of the match. 
* **Assigned Tool** (11, text, optional): Enter the tool assigned to the wrestler. 
* **Notes** (12, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the event. 
* **Email** (13, email, optional): Enter the email address of the wrestler or team. 
* **Phone** (14, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the wrestler or team. 
* **Wrestler Age** (15, number, optional): Enter the age of the wrestler. 
* **Wrestler Height** (16, number, optional): Enter the height of the wrestler. 

## Tips
- For best results, double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness. 
- Ensure you have all necessary permissions or approvals to complete this form.
