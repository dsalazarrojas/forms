# south american tournament prediction form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The South American Tournament Prediction Form is designed to allow you to predict the outcome of a tournament in South America. By completing this form, you can make predictions for the tournament, including which teams will be part of the competition and which team will emerge victorious.

## How To Complete This Form

To use this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  For each field, select either "Yes" or "No" to indicate your prediction.
2.  Start by selecting "Club" to confirm whether or not the selected team plays in the club.
3.  Then, select "Team" to confirm whether or not the selected team plays in the team.
4.  Finally, select "Winner" to confirm which team you predict will win the tournament.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Club** (select_one):
    This field allows you to confirm whether or not the selected team plays in the club.
*   **Team** (select_one):
    This field allows you to confirm whether or not the selected team plays in the team.
*   **Winner** (select_one):
    This field allows you to confirm which team you predict will win the tournament.

## Tips

*   Please make sure to select the correct team for each field to ensure accurate predictions.
*   If you need more information or clarification, please consult the tournament schedule or other resources.
*   Remember to review your predictions carefully before submitting the form.
