# cricket_coach_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for coaches who want to apply to be a cricket coach. It collects necessary information to evaluate a coach's eligibility for a coaching position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the "personal_details" section.
2. Answer the "experience" question by selecting whether you have experience or not.
3. In the "cricket_background" section, provide a brief description of your cricket background.
4. For "sport_management", select whether you have experience in sports management or not.
5. Select whether you have "coaching_certification" or not.
6. For "emergency_contact", select whether you have emergency contact information or not.
7. Finally, provide any additional comments in the "additional_comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Personal Details** (`personal_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your personal details, including your name and contact information.
- **Experience** (`experience`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you have experience in coaching, "No" otherwise.
- **Cricket Background** (`cricket_background`, note, optional): Describe your cricket background, including any relevant experience or qualifications.
- **Sport Management** (`sport_management`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" if you have experience in sports management, "No" otherwise.
- **Coaching Certification** (`coaching_certification`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you have a coaching certification, "No" otherwise.
- **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" if you have emergency contact information, "No" otherwise.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments about your application.
