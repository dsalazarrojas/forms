# Fitness Instructor Transition Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to transfer client information, schedule coverage, and next steps in the client transition process from one instructor to another.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form in its entirety, ensuring all required fields are completed.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure accurate and clear information is provided.
3. Submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information (1)** (`Client Information`, `text`, required): This field is used to record the client's name, contact information, and any other relevant details. Please provide the client's full name, email address, and phone number.
* **Schedule Coverage (2)** (`Schedule Coverage`, `text`, optional): This field is used to describe any scheduling conflicts or changes that need to be communicated to the new instructor. If no conflicts are present, please provide a brief explanation.
* **Client Transfer (3)** (`Client Transfer`, `text`, required): This field is used to record any transfer notes from the previous instructor to the new instructor.
* **Notes (4)** (`Notes`, `note`, required): This field is used to record any additional notes or comments about the client.
* **Email (5)** (`Email`, `email`, required): This field is used to record the client's email address. Please ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Phone (6)** (`Phone`, `text`, required): This field is used to record the client's phone number. Please ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Schedule Changes (7)** (`Schedule Changes`, `text`, required): This field is used to record any changes to the client's schedule.
* **Next Steps (8)** (`Next Steps`, `text`, required): This field is used to record any next steps or instructions for the new instructor.
* **Coverage (9)** (`Coverage`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to record whether the client requires coverage during the transition period. Please select 'Yes' or 'No' accordingly.
* **Coverage Note (10)** (`Coverage Note`, `text`, required): This field is used to provide additional information about coverage if selected as 'Yes' in the 'Coverage' field.
* **Client (11)** (`Client`, `select_multiple`, required): This field is used to select the client's gender.
* **Client Transfer Date (12)** (`Client Transfer Date`, `date`, required): This field is used to record the date of the client transfer.
* **Client Transfer Time (13)** (`Client Transfer Time`, `time`, required): This field is used to record the time of the client transfer.
* **Client Transfer Notes (14)** (`Client Transfer Notes`, `text`, required): This field is used to record any additional notes about the client transfer.
* **Schedule Change Date (15)** (`Schedule Change Date`, `date`, required): This field is used to record the date of the schedule change.
* **Schedule Change Note (16)** (`Schedule Change Note`, `text`, required): This field is used to record any additional notes about the schedule change.
* **Next Steps Note (17)** (`Next Steps Note`, `text`, required): This field is used to record any additional notes about the next steps.
* **Next Steps Date (18)** (`Next Steps Date`, `date`, required): This field is used to record the date of the next steps.

## Tips

* Please ensure all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Review the form carefully before submission.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the [support team](support@domain.com) for clarification.
