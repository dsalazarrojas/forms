# Personal Training Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Training Informed Consent Form is a crucial document that outlines the terms and conditions of a training program, ensuring that participants understand their rights, responsibilities, and obligations. It's a necessary step in the onboarding process and should be filled out before starting the training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the Participant Name field with your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
2.  Enter your contact number and email address in the Contact Number and Contact Email fields, respectively.
3.  Provide your emergency contact's name and relationship with you in the Emergency Contact Name and Relationship fields, respectively.
4.  Sign and date the Participant Informed Consent field to indicate that you've read and understood the terms and conditions of the training program.
5.  Sign and date the Participant Signature field to confirm that you've completed the form accurately.
6.  If applicable, have your emergency contact sign and date the Emergency Contact Signature field to confirm that they've reviewed and agreed to the terms and conditions.
7.  Finally, your trainer will sign and date the Trainer Signature field to confirm that they've reviewed and explained the terms and conditions to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Form Header** (`form_header`, note, not applicable): This field serves as a header for the form and is not applicable for input.
*   **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, not required): Provide your contact number so that we can reach you in case of an emergency.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, not required): Enter your email address so that we can communicate with you.
*   **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, not required): Provide the name of a person to contact in case of an emergency.
*   **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, text, not required): Enter the contact number of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
*   **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, not required): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person.
*   **Participant Informed Consent** (`participant_informed_consent`, text, not required): Read and sign this field to confirm that you've understood the terms and conditions of the training program.
*   **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, text, required): Sign and date this field to confirm that you've completed the form accurately.
*   **Emergency Contact Signature** (`emergency_contact_signature`, text, not required): If applicable, have your emergency contact sign and date this field to confirm that they've reviewed and agreed to the terms and conditions.
*   **Trainer Signature** (`trainer_signature`, text, not required): Your trainer will sign and date this field to confirm that they've reviewed and explained the terms and conditions to you.
*   **Trainer Name** (`trainer_name`, text, not required): Enter your trainer's name who reviewed and explained the terms and conditions to you.
