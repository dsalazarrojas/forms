# Golf Handicap System Data Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture data related to a golfer's handicap system, including member name, handicap, course handicap, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the golf member's name in the **Member Name** field.
2. Enter the member's handicap index, which is a measure of their overall skill level.
3. Enter the member's course handicap, which is a measure of their performance at a specific course.
4. Enter the member's email address for communication purposes.
5. Select the member's gender from the options provided.
6. Enter any additional phone number for the member.
7. Add any additional notes about the member.
8. For each course played, enter the course rating and slope in the corresponding fields. This will help calculate the member's handicap index for that course.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Member Name**: This is the name of the golfer, used for identification and communication. 
* **Member Handicap**: A measure of the golfer's overall skill level, with lower numbers indicating better performance.
* **Member Handicap Index**: This is the number used to calculate the golfer's handicap for different courses. 
* **Course Handicap**: A measure of the golfer's performance at a specific course. 
* **Course Rating**: A number indicating the difficulty of the course, with higher numbers indicating more challenging courses.
* **Course Slope**: A number indicating the course's rating for a golfer's handicap calculation.
* **Gender**: The gender of the golfer, used for statistical purposes.
* **Member Email**: The contact email address for the golfer.
* **Phone Number**: An additional contact number for the golfer.
* **Member Notes**: Any additional information about the golfer that is not captured elsewhere on the form.
* **Course Rating 1-7**: Enter the course rating for each course the golfer has played.
* **Course Slope 1-7**: Enter the course slope for each course the golfer has played.

Note: This form captures data from up to 7 courses played by the golfer. If more courses need to be captured, additional rows can be added as needed.
