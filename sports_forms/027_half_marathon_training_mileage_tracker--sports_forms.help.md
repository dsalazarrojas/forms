# Half Marathon Training Mileage Tracker - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Half Marathon Training Mileage Tracker is a tool designed to help athletes track their progress and prepare for a half marathon event. It allows you to record your mileage, training plan, and performance metrics, as well as receive feedback and guidance from your coach.

## How To Complete This Form

To use this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your Date of Birth to ensure you are eligible to participate in the half marathon event.
3. Provide your Email address so that we can contact you with updates and information.
4. Input your Phone Number for emergency contact purposes.
5. Describe your Training Plan in the text field.
6. Record your Mileage Target and the Mileage Completed to track your progress.
7. Enter your Distance Completed to track your total distance covered.
8. Enter your Average Pace to calculate your performance.
9. Input your Max Altitude, Average Altitude, Max Temperature, Average Temperature, Max Humidity, Average Humidity, Max Weather Condition, Average Weather Condition, Max Wind Speed, Average Wind Speed, Max Snowfall, and Average Snowfall for environmental conditions.
10. Choose your Coach from the dropdown list to receive personalized guidance.
11. Add any additional notes for your coach or athlete in the Coach Note and Athlete Note fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so that we can contact you with updates and information.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Input your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **Training Plan** (`training_plan`, `text`, optional): Describe your training plan and goals for the half marathon event.
* **Mileage Target** (`mileage_target`, `number`, optional): Set your target mileage for the training period.
* **Mileage Completed** (`mileage_completed`, `number`, optional): Record the mileage completed during the training period.
* **Distance Completed** (`distance_completed`, `number`, optional): Enter the total distance completed.
* **Average Pace** (`average_pace`, `number`, optional): Calculate your average pace for the training period.
* **Max Altitude** (`max_altitude`, `number`, optional): Record the highest altitude reached during training.
* **Average Altitude** (`average_altitude`, `number`, optional): Calculate the average altitude for the training period.
* **Max Temperature** (`max_temperature`, `number`, optional): Record the highest temperature experienced during training.
* **Average Temperature** (`average_temperature`, `number`, optional): Calculate the average temperature for the training period.
* **Max Humidity** (`max_humidity`, `number`, optional): Record the highest humidity experienced during training.
* **Average Humidity** (`average_humidity`, `number`, optional): Calculate the average humidity for the training period.
* **Max Weather Condition** (`max_weather_condition`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the worst weather condition experienced during training.
* **Average Weather Condition** (`average_weather_condition`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the average weather condition for the training period.
* **Max Wind Speed** (`max_wind_speed`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the fastest wind speed experienced during training.
* **Average Wind Speed** (`average_wind_speed`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the average wind speed for the training period.
* **Max Snowfall** (`max_snowfall`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the heaviest snowfall experienced during training.
* **Average Snowfall** (`average_snowfall`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the average snowfall for the training period.
* **Coach Name** (`coach_name`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your coach from the list to receive personalized guidance.
* **Coach Note** (`coach_note`, `note`, optional): Add any notes for your coach.
* **Athlete Note** (`athlete_note`, `note`, optional): Add any notes for the athlete.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and honest information to get the most out of this form.
* Use the Training Plan field to describe your goals and plans for the half marathon event.
* Enter your actual mileage completed and target mileage to track your progress.
* Record your environmental conditions to gain insights into how they affected your performance.
* Review and update your form regularly to track your progress and receive feedback from your coach.
