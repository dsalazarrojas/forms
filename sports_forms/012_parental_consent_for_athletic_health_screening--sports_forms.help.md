# Parental Consent For Athletic Health Screening - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain parental consent for athletic health screening. As a parent, you are being asked to provide your consent for your child to participate in athletic activities that may involve health screenings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the appropriate response for "Guardian Authorization"
2. Enter your contact information (email and phone number) in the "Contact" field.
3. Enter any relevant medical notes in the "Medical Notes" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Provide any additional comments or information in the "Additional Comments" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guardian Authorization**: This field asks you to confirm that you are the parent or guardian of the athlete. Please select "Parental Consent for Athletic Health Screening" to show that you have given consent.
* **Contact**: This field is used to collect your contact information in case the school or organization needs to reach out to you with any questions or updates. Please enter your email address so that we can contact you.
* **Medical Notes**: If there is any relevant medical information about your child that the school or organization needs to know, please enter it here.
* **Email**: This field is used to collect your email address. Please enter your email address so that we can contact you.
* **Phone**: This field is used to collect your phone number. Please enter your phone number so that we can contact you.
* **Additional Comments**: This field is optional and can be used to provide any additional information about your child or your consent.
