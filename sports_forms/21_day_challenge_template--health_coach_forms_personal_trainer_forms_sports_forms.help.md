# 21 Day Challenge Participant Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 21 Day Challenge Participant Form is designed to help us personalize your experience and provide the best support on your journey to success. By completing this form, you'll be able to:

* Provide us with your basic information (name, email, phone number, and date of birth)
* Share your health and fitness profile (weight, height, current fitness level, medical conditions, and dietary restrictions)
* Set goals and expectations for the challenge (main challenge goal, motivation level, and target start date)
* Confirm your acknowledgment of risks and disclaimer

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Please carefully read each field's label, hint, and type to understand what information we're asking for.
2. Enter your information in the corresponding fields, making sure to follow any formatting instructions (e.g., date formats).
3. Select your answers from the available options for select-type fields.
4. Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official ID.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you want to receive challenge updates and resources.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number in case we need to reach you urgently. (Optional)
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (e.g., MM/DD/YY).
* **Gender Identity**: Select your identity from the available options (Female, Male, Non-binary, or Prefer not to say).
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of someone we can contact in an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Current Weight (kg)**: Enter your current weight in kilograms.
* **Height (cm)**: Enter your height in centimeters.
* **Current Fitness Level**: Select your current fitness level from the available options (Sedentary, Lightly Active, Moderately Active, or Very Active).
* **Medical Conditions or Physical Limitations**: List any relevant medical conditions or physical limitations we should be aware of.
* **Dietary Preferences or Restrictions**: Select all that apply to your dietary preferences or restrictions (e.g., Vegetarian, Vegan, Pescatarian, Gluten-Free).
* **Main Challenge Goal**: Enter your specific, measurable outcome for the 21-day challenge (e.g., 'Lose 3kg', 'Run 5k', 'Improve sleep').
* **Motivation Level**: Select your motivation level for the challenge from 1 (Low) to 5 (Extremely High).
* **Preferred Activities**: Select the types of physical activities you enjoy or wish to focus on (e.g., Strength Training, Cardio, Yoga, HIIT).
* **Target Challenge Start Date**: Enter the date you plan to officially begin your 21-day journey.
* **Acknowledgment of Risks**: Confirm that you have read and understood the risks and disclaimer associated with the challenge. Select 'Yes' if you agree.
* **Additional Comments or Questions**: Enter any additional information you'd like to share with your coach or ask any questions you have.

**Tips**

* Be accurate and honest when filling out the form to ensure we provide you with the best support.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask your coach or contact us for clarification.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* If you need to make changes or updates to your form, contact us with your changes and we'll assist you.
