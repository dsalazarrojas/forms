# Online Wellness Evaluation Form Gym Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Wellness Evaluation Form Gym Template is designed to gather information about your daily food intake, medicine dose, and exercise routine. This data will help you track your progress and make informed decisions about your overall wellness and fitness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Scroll down to the first page, "Food Intake", and enter your daily food intake in the text field. You can include any relevant details about your meals, such as the types of food you've consumed and the portion sizes.
2. Move on to the second page, "Daily Medicine Dose", and enter your daily medicine dose in the required field. This is a crucial section, so make sure to fill it out accurately.
3. On the third page, "Exercise Routine", enter your exercise routine for the day. Describe your activities, duration, and intensity to help you track your progress.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Food Intake** (`Food Intake`, text, optional): Enter your daily food intake in this text field. This is a free-form text field, so you can write as much or as little as you need to describe your meals and snacks.
* **Daily Medicine Dose** (`Daily Medicine Dose`, number, required): Enter your daily medicine dose in this field. Make sure to fill it out accurately, as this information is essential for your health and wellness tracking.
* **Exercise Routine** (`Exercise Routine`, text, optional): Describe your exercise routine for the day, including activities, duration, and intensity. This will help you track your progress and make informed decisions about your fitness goals.
