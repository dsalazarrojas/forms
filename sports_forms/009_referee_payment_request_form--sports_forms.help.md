# Referee Payment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for referees to request payment for their services in sports tournaments. It is used to collect and manage payment requests, track the status of these requests, and ensure accurate payment processing.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct payment method: Choose from Bank Transfer, PayPal, or Credit Card.
2. Enter the referees' name and email address.
3. Specify the pay amount and frequency: one-time, repeatedly, or ongoing.
4. Add any additional tournament name and referee name for the specific tournament.
5. Select the payment status: Paid, Pending, or Rejected.
6. Provide any additional payment notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Game Details:** Enter a brief description of the game or tournament the referee is requesting payment for. This helps us understand the context of the payment request. 
* **Payment Method:** Choose the standard payment method used in the industry. You can select from Bank Transfer, PayPal, or Credit Card. 
* **Referees Name:** Enter the name of the referee making the payment request. 
* **Referees Email:** Enter the email address of the referee for future communication. 
* **Pay Amount:** Enter the amount of payment the referee is requesting. Please ensure this matches the agreed-upon rate for the specific service. 
* **Pay Frequency:** Choose if the payment is one-time, repeatedly, or ongoing. 
* **Tournament Name:** Enter the name of the specific tournament the payment is for, if applicable. 
* **Tournament Referee:** Enter the name of the tournament referee, if applicable. 
* **Pay Date:** Enter the date you would like the payment to be processed. Please note that this date will not affect the payment processing timeline, but rather serve as a reference for our records. 
* **Payment Status:** Select the status of the payment request: Paid, Pending, or Rejected. 
* **Pay Request:** Enter any additional comments or requests regarding the payment. 
* **Notes:** Provide any further information or comments regarding the payment request.
