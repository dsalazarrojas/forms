# Soccer Coach Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Soccer Coach Evaluation Survey is a tool designed to help evaluate your coach's performance, communication style, and overall effectiveness. This survey is intended to gather feedback from players to provide valuable insights to coaches to improve their coaching techniques, communication, and team environment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the rating for each field based on your honest and thoughtful evaluation of your coach's performance.
2. Provide detailed feedback for fields that are marked as "text" type to give a thorough explanation of your thoughts and suggestions.
3. Be as concise as possible when filling out the form, and focus on providing actionable feedback for your coach to improve.
