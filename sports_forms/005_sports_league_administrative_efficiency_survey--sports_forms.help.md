# Sports League Administrative Efficiency Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports League Administrative Efficiency Survey is a questionnaire designed to evaluate the administration, communication, and staff support aspects of a sports league. This survey aims to identify areas of improvement and provide insights for sports organizations to become more efficient and effective in their operations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. The form is divided into three sections: League Administration, Communication, and Staff Support.
2. Please select the section that best aligns with your role or responsibility within the sports league.
3. For each section, provide honest and detailed answers to the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **League Administration** (Text): This section is about your experiences with the league's administrative systems. Please provide a brief description of your interactions with the league's administrative processes, including any challenges or successes you have had.
* **Communication** (Text): In this section, you will be asked about your communication experiences with the league, including any channels used (e.g., email, phone, team meetings) and any difficulties or successes you have encountered.
* **Staff Support** (Select One): This question inquires about the availability and effectiveness of staff support within the league. Please select "True" if you have received sufficient support from staff when needed, and "False" if not.

## Tips
* Be honest and provide detailed answers to help the league improve its administrative efficiency.
* If you are unsure about any section or question, feel free to ask for clarification or guidance.
* This survey is designed to be completed by individuals involved in the sports league, including administrators, coaches, players, or staff members.
