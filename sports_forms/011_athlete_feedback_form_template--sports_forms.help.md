# Athlete Feedback Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form template is designed to collect athlete feedback on their overall experience in the sports program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with your accurate and honest feedback.
2. Select the relevant options for the corresponding questions.
3. Answer the short-answer fields (like `parent_guardian_name`) if applicable.
4. Choose your preferences for additional services if you wish to see more in the program.
5. Submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Full Name**: Please provide your first and last name as registered in the program.
* **Athlete ID Number**: Your student or team identification number, if you have one.
* **Parent or Guardian Name**: If you have a parent or guardian, please provide their name here. This is optional.
* **Sport**: Select the primary sport you are participating in.
* **Team Level**: Choose the level of competition your team plays at, which can be Varsity, Junior Varsity, Freshman, Club Team, or Recreational.
* **Coach Name**: Please provide the name of your primary coach.
* **Feedback Period**: Choose the period that this feedback applies to, such as Fall Season, Winter Season, Spring Season, Full Year, or a specific Event.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the program, choosing from Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.
* **Coaching Quality**: Evaluate the quality of coaching provided, using the scale Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, or Poor.
* **Facility Quality**: Assess the quality of the training facilities provided, with the same scale.
* **Equipment Quality**: Rate the quality of the equipment provided, again using the same scale.
* **Communication Rating**: Evaluate the communication from the coaching staff, using the same scale.
* **Training Effectiveness**: Assess how effective the training sessions were, using the same scale.
* **Safety Measures**: Rate the safety measures in place during the program, using the same scale.
* **Program Highlights**: Describe what you enjoyed most about the program.
* **Areas for Improvement**: List any areas that you feel could be improved.
* **Additional Services Desired**: Select any additional services you would like to see offered, such as Strength and Conditioning, Nutrition Counseling, Mental Skills Training, Video Analysis, Injury Prevention Programs, More Practice Time, or Tournament Participation.
* **Would You Recommend This Program**: Choose how likely you are to recommend the program to other athletes.
* **Will You Return Next Season**: Indicate your plans to continue participating in the program.
* **Additional Comments**: Any other feedback or comments you have about the program.
* **Follow-Up Requested**: If you would like to be contacted about your feedback, choose Yes or No.

## Tips
- Please answer all required fields and provide accurate responses.
- Be honest and provide detailed feedback where relevant.
- Select the correct options for your preferences.
- Review your form before submission to ensure completeness.
- If you have any further inquiries, do not hesitate to contact us at `form@gic.mx`.
