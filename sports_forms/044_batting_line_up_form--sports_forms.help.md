# Batting Line Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a baseball team's lineup and game details. It is used for teams, coaches, or sports organizations to record and manage lineups for games, including the batting order, game metadata, and coaching staff.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the game information:
   - **Game and Team Information**: Fill in the team's name and game details in the first section.
   - **Official Batting Lineup**: Fill in the names and numbers of the players in the batting order.
   - **Defensive Assignments**: Enter the starting pitcher, catcher, and any substitutes and reserves.
   - **Coaching Staff**: Enter the head coach's name and sign off.
   - **Game Details**: Enter the game date and finalize the lineup.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **-- Game and Team Information --**
  - **Team Name**: The name of your team.
  - **Opponent**: The name of the opposing team.
  - **Date of Game**: The date of the game. Select the date in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
  - **Field or Venue Name**: The name of the field or venue where the game is being played.

- **-- Official Batting Lineup --**
  - **1. Lead Off Batter (Name and Number)**: The first batter in the batting order, including their name and number.
  - **2. Second Batter (Name and Number)**: The second batter in the batting order, including their name and number.
  - **3. Third Batter (Name and Number)**: The third batter in the batting order, including their name and number.
  - **4. Cleanup Batter (Name and Number)**: The cleanup batter in the batting order, including their name and number.
  - **5. Fifth Batter (Name and Number)**: The fifth batter in the batting order, including their name and number.
  - **6. Sixth Batter (Name and Number)**: The sixth batter in the batting order, including their name and number.
  - **7. Seventh Batter (Name and Number)**: The seventh batter in the batting order, including their name and number.
  - **8. Eighth Batter (Name and Number)**: The eighth batter in the batting order, including their name and number.
  - **9. Ninth Batter (Name and Number)**: The ninth batter in the batting order, including their name and number.

- **-- Defensive Assignments --**
  - **Starting Pitcher**: The pitcher who will start the game.
  - **Starting Catcher**: The catcher who will start the game.
  - **Substitutes and Reserves**: Any players who are not in the starting lineup but may be substituted or reserve players for the game.

- **-- Coaching Staff --**
  - **Head Coach Name**: The name of the head coach.
  - **Lineup Finalized Date**: The date the lineup was finalized.
