# Scouts COVID 19 Self Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scouts COVID 19 Self Assessment Form is designed to collect essential information about your child's health status and your contact details for the scouting event.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your child's name, contact number, email address, and other relevant details.
2. Provide your child's date of birth, medical conditions, and allergies (if any).
3. Confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions of the camp.
4. Enter your contact information and relationship with your child.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter your name and title.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, number, required): Enter your contact number for the scout's safety and communication.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address for easy communication.
* **Scout Name** (`scout_name`, text, required): Enter your child's name as it appears on their identification.
* **Scout's Date of Birth** (`scout_dob`, date, required): Enter your child's date of birth for age verification.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, note, required): Sign this form to confirm your agreement to the camp's terms and conditions.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the event name or program your child is participating in.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the event for the scout's safety and logistics.
* **Event Dates** (`event_dates`, date, required): If known, enter the dates of the event. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
* **Terms & Agreement** (`terms_agreement`, select_one, required): Choose to confirm your understanding and agreement to the camp's terms and conditions.
* **Terms & Agreement Confirmation** (`terms_agreement_confirmation`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have read and understood the terms and conditions of the camp.
* **Scout's COVID-19 Test Result** (`scout_covid_test_result`, select_multiple, required): Select the scout's COVID-19 test result (Negative, Positive, Pending, or Not Tested).
* **Parent's COVID-19 Test Result** (`parent_covid_test_result`, select_multiple, required): Select your own COVID-19 test result (Negative, Positive, Pending, or Not Tested).
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter the contact number of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, text, required): Enter the email address of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Scout's Medical Condition** (`scout_medical_condition`, text, optional): If your child has any medical conditions, provide details about them.
* **Medical Condition Information** (`medical_condition_info`, text, optional): If your child has any medical conditions, provide more information about them.
* **Medical Condition Treatment** (`medical_condition_treatment`, text, optional): If your child has any medical conditions, provide information about their treatment.
* **Scout's Allergies** (`scout_allergies`, text, optional): If your child has any allergies, provide details about them.
* **Allergies Information** (`allergies_info`, text, optional): If your child has any allergies, provide more information about them.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, text, required): Enter the contact number of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Relationship to Scout** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required): Enter your relationship to the scout (e.g., parent, guardian, etc.).
