# Calorie Count Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Calorie Count Form is designed to collect information about your dietary habits, health status, and preferences to help health professionals provide personalized guidance on maintaining a healthy balance of calories and nutrients.

## How To Complete This Form
### Completing the form will guide you through a series of questions that cover your:

* Physical characteristics (Client Name, Date of Birth, Height, Weight)
* Activity level and lifestyle (Activity Level, Primary Fitness Goal)
* Eating habits (Meals per day, Foods and Beverages for Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snacks, and Daily Beverage Consumption)
* Dietary restrictions and allergies (Dietary Restrictions or Allergies)
* Frequency of eating outside home (Eating out frequency)
* Health status (Existing Health Conditions, Medications or Supplements)
* Nutritional goals and preferences (Specific Nutrition Goals, Foods You Love, Foods You Dislike or Want to Avoid)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your birthdate in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Gender**: Select your gender from the provided options.
* **Height**: Enter your height in centimeters.
* **Weight**: Enter your current weight in kilograms.
* **Activity Level**: Select your typical level of physical activity from the provided options.
* **Primary Fitness Goal**: Choose your main objective (Weight loss, Muscle gain, Maintain current weight, etc.).
* **Meals per day**: Select how many meals you eat on a typical day.
* **Breakfast Foods and Beverages**: List typical foods and beverages you consume for breakfast.
* **Lunch Foods and Beverages**: List typical foods and beverages you consume for lunch.
* **Dinner Foods and Beverages**: List typical foods and beverages you consume for dinner.
* **Snacks Throughout the Day**: List your typical snacks.
* **Daily Beverage Consumption**: Describe your daily beverage consumption.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies**: Select all applicable restrictions or allergies you have.
* **Eating out frequency**: Choose how often you eat at restaurants.
* **Alcohol Consumption**: Select your average weekly alcohol intake.
* **Daily Water Intake**: Choose your daily water consumption.
* **Existing Health Conditions**: Select any existing health conditions you have.
* **Medications or Supplements**: List any medications or supplements you are currently taking.
* **Specific Nutrition Goals**: Describe any specific nutritional goals you have.
* **Foods You Love**: List foods you enjoy eating.
* **Foods You Dislike or Want to Avoid**: List foods you do not want to include in your meal plan.

# Tips
* Please answer these questions accurately and truthfully to ensure that the form's purpose is fully met.
* You can ask your healthcare professional or nutritionist for guidance if you're unsure about any question.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, please don't hesitate to ask.
* This form is designed to be completed once.
