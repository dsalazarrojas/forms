# Cricket For - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users who are interested in playing cricket. It will help us understand who is interested in cricket and their preferences for specific sports.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. Enter your response to "Who is interested" in the text field.
3. Select your answer to "sports_type" by choosing one or more of the options provided.
4. Select your answer to "cricket_team" by choosing one of the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Who is interested** (`user_input`, `text`, optional): Please enter your answer to "Who is interested in cricket".
- **sports_type** (`sports_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or more of the options provided to answer "Are you interested in other sports besides cricket?"
  - If you select "Yes", please specify which sports you are interested in.
- **cricket_team** (`cricket_team`, `select_one`, optional): Select your answer to "Do you have a favorite cricket team?" 

## Tips
- Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
