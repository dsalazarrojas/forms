# Tournament Participant Wristband Reorder Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tournament Participant Wristband Reorder Form is used to collect information from participants to facilitate the reordering process for tournament wristbands. This form is essential for ensuring accurate and efficient reordering.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form completely**: Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately.
2. **Provide accurate information**: Double-check your details to avoid errors.
3. **Submit the form**: Click submit to complete the reorder process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Tournament Name** (`tournament_name`, text, optional)
* This field is used to enter the name of the tournament you participated in.
* Enter the tournament name to ensure the correct order is processed.

### 2. **First Name** (`shipping_first_name`, text, optional)
* Enter your first name for accurate shipping purposes.

### 3. **Last Name** (`shipping_last_name`, text, optional)
* Enter your last name for accurate shipping purposes.

### 4. **Address** (`shipping_address`, text, optional)
* Enter your address for accurate shipping of reorder items.

### 5. **City** (`shipping_city`, text, optional)
* Enter the city where the reorder item will be shipped to.

### 6. **State** (`shipping_state`, text, optional)
* Enter the state where the reorder item will be shipped to.

### 7. **Zip** (`shipping_zip`, text, optional)
* Enter the zip code where the reorder item will be shipped to.

### 8. **Reorder Item ID** (`reorder_item_id`, number, optional)
* Enter the ID of the item you would like to reorder.

### 9. **Reorder Item Type** (`reorder_item_type`, select_one, optional)
* Select the type of reorder item you would like to reorder.
* Options:
	+ Item 1
	+ Item 2

### 10. **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, optional)
* Select the method you would like to use for payment.
* Options:
	+ PayPal
	+ Stripe

### 11. **Shipping Date** (`shipping_date`, date, optional)
* Enter the shipping date for the reorder item.
