# Senior Spotlight Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to highlight senior athletes who have made significant contributions to their school or community through sports.

## How To Complete This Form
### Steps to Complete the Form
To complete the form, follow the instructions above to fill out each field accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. First Name
* **First Name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the senior's first name.

### 2. Last Name
* **Last Name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the senior's last name.

### 3. Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth** (`date`, required/optional): Enter the senior's date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).

### 4. Email
* **Email** (`email`, required/optional): Enter the senior's email address.

### 5. Phone
* **Phone** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the senior's phone number.

### 6. School Year
* **School Year** (`number`, required/optional): Enter the senior's school year of graduation.

### 7. Event Name
* **Event Name** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the event where the senior will be spotlighted.

### 8. Event Description
* **Event Description** (`text`, required/optional): Describe the event where the senior will be spotlighted.

### 9. Event Location
* **Event Location** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the location of the event.

### 10. Event Date
* **Event Date** (`date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the event.

### 11. Event Time
* **Event Time** (`time`, required/optional): Enter the time of the event.

### 12. Photo URL
* **Photo URL** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the URL of the senior's photo.

### 13. Coach Name
* **Coach Name** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the coach's name.

### 14. Coach Email
* **Coach Email** (`email`, required/optional): Enter the coach's email address.

### 15. Coach Phone
* **Coach Phone** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the coach's phone number.

### 16. Team
* **Team** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the senior's team name.

### 17. Sport
* **Sport** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the sport associated with the senior.

### 18. Team ID
* **Team ID** (`number`, required/optional): Enter the team's ID.

### 19. Sport ID
* **Sport ID** (`number`, required/optional): Enter the sport's ID.

### 20. Team Name
* **Team Name** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the senior's team name.

### 21. Sport Name
* **Sport Name** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the sport name associated with the senior.

### 22. School Year ID
* **School Year ID** (`number`, required/optional): Enter the school year of the event.

### 23. School Year
* **School Year** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the school year of the event.

### 24. Assigned Tool
* **Assigned Tool** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the assigned tool.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check the information you enter to ensure accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your administrator for guidance.
* Please fill out the form carefully to ensure all required fields are completed accurately.
