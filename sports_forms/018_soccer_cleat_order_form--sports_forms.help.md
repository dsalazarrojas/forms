# Soccer Cleat Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for customers to place an order for soccer cleats, providing essential information to process their request efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your customer details, including your name, phone number, and email address in the "Customer Details" section.
2. Enter your cleat order in the "Cleat Order" section, specifying the type of cleats you're interested in purchasing.
3. Fill in your payment details in the "Payment Details" section.
4. Select your preferred delivery option from the available choices in the "Delivery Options" section.
5. If you have any additional comments or requests, you can include them in the "Customer Message" section.
6. Specify the current status of your order in the "Order Status" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, required): This is the section where you can enter your name, phone number, and email address to allow our team to contact you and process your order.
* **Cleat Order** (`cleat_order`, text, required): Please specify the type of cleats you're interested in purchasing, including the model and color if applicable.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, required): Enter your payment information, including credit card details or other relevant payment methods.
* **Delivery Options** (`delivery_options`, select_multiple, required): Choose from the available delivery options, such as "Delivery" or "Pickup".
* **Customer Message** (`customer_message`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or requests, you can include them in this section.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, select_multiple, required): Select the current status of your order from the available choices, such as "New", "In Progress", or "Completed".
