# personal_trainer_profile_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for personal trainers to submit their profile information to be listed in our directory.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the field marked "first_name."
2. Enter your last name in the field marked "last_name."
3. Enter your email address in the field marked "email."
4. Enter your address in the field marked "address."
5. Enter your phone number in the field marked "phone_number."

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name (first_name, Text, Required)**: This is the field to enter your first name.
* **Last name (last_name, Text, Required)**: This is the field to enter your last name.
* **Email (email, Email, Required)**: This is the field to enter your email address. Please ensure that this is a valid email address.
* **Address (address, Text, Required)**: This is the field to enter your physical address.
* **Phone Number (phone_number, Text, Required)**: This is the field to enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Please enter the information accurately and truthfully to ensure the completeness of your profile.
* If you encounter any issues with this form, please contact our support team for assistance.
* Make sure to review your submission carefully before submitting to ensure it is complete and accurate.
