# Kids Swimming Lessons Waiting List Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a waiting list form for kids swimming lessons. It's meant to be filled out by parents or guardians before the lessons start.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the child's first name, last name, and date of birth.
2. Enter the child's phone number.
3. Provide your email address.
4. Mention your child's swim level, swim frequency, swim goals, swim experience, and any medical conditions they may have.
5. Enter emergency contact information, including name, phone, email, and relationship.
6. If necessary, provide details about any medical equipment your child uses.
7. Mention any allergies your child has.
8. Write a brief message for the emergency contact in case we need to contact them.
9. Sign the form to confirm your agreement with the payment terms.
10. Select whether you agree or disagree with the payment terms.

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your child's first name.

* **Swim Level** (`swim_level`, `text`, required/optional): Please describe your child's swim level.
* **Swim Frequency** (`swim_frequency`, `text`, required/optional): Please mention how often your child swims.
* **Swim Goal** (`swim_goal`, `text`, required/optional): Please describe your child's swim goals.
* **Swim Experience** (`swim_experience`, `text`, required/optional): Please describe how long your child has been swimming.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, required/optional): Please describe your relationship to the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter the email address of the emergency contact.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, required/optional): Please mention any medical conditions your child has.
* **Medical Equipment** (`medical_equipment`, `text`, required/optional): Please describe any medical equipment your child uses.

* **Coach Signature** (`coach_signature`, `text`, required/optional): Please sign the form to confirm your agreement with the payment terms.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure we have accurate and up-to-date information about your child.
* If you're unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to ask our staff for help.
* Please sign the form to confirm your agreement with the payment terms.
* If you disagree with the payment terms, please explain your concerns in the "Payment Terms" field.
