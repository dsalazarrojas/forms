# Powerlifting Championships Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for athletes to apply for powerlifting championships. It gathers information about your contact details and physical characteristics to assess your eligibility and suitability for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling in your registrant's name in the "registrant_name" field.
- Provide your date of birth in the "date_of_birth" field.
- Enter your contact email address in the "contact_email" field.
- Enter your contact phone number in the "contact_phone" field.
- Select your weight class in the "weight_class" field. You can choose one or multiple options from the list.
- Measure and enter your height in the "height" field.
- Measure and enter your body measurements in the "body_measure_1", "body_measure_2", "body_measure_3", "body_measure_4", and "body_measure_5" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **registrant_name** (Text, Optional): Enter your name as you would like it to appear in the event program.
* **date_of_birth** (Date, Required): Your date of birth as per your passport or ID card.
* **contact_email** (Email, Required): A valid email address where we can contact you with event updates and instructions.
* **contact_phone** (Text, Optional): Your phone number where we can reach out to you with event updates and instructions.
* **weight_class** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the weight class you think you would compete in. This will help us assess your eligibility and suitability for the event.
* **height** (Number, Optional): Your height in meters (e.g., 1.75 m).
* **body_measure_1** (Number, Optional): Measure and enter your body measurement 1 (e.g., chest circumference).
* **body_measure_2** (Number, Optional): Measure and enter your body measurement 2 (e.g., waist circumference).
* **body_measure_3** (Number, Optional): Measure and enter your body measurement 3 (e.g., hip circumference).
* **body_measure_4** (Number, Optional): Measure and enter your body measurement 4 (e.g., arm length).
* **body_measure_5** (Number, Optional): Measure and enter your body measurement 5 (e.g., leg length).
