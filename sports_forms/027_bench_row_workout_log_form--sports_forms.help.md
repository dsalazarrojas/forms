# Bench Row Workout Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bench Row Workout Log Form is designed to help athletes track their progress in bench row exercises. This form allows users to record information about their workouts, including date, time, athlete name, and specific exercise data.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the athlete's name in the "Athlete Name" field.
2. Select the date of the workout from the calendar in the "Workout Date" field.
3. Optionally, enter the time of the workout in the "Time of Workout" field.
4. Record the duration of the workout in the "Workout Duration" field (in minutes).
5. For each set of the bench row exercise, enter the number of repetitions and weight (in pounds) in the corresponding fields.
6. Choose the difficulty level of each set from the drop-down menu.
7. Select your energy level before the workout from the drop-down menu.
8. Choose your energy level after the workout from the drop-down menu.
9. Rate your form quality and recovery rate from the drop-down menus.
10. If necessary, report any pain or discomfort experienced during the workout.
11. Enter any additional exercises performed during the workout in the "Additional Exercises Performed" field.
12. Record the rest periods between sets in seconds.
13. Enter any workout notes or progress observations in the "Workout Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Name** (`athlete_name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the athlete completing the workout.
* **Workout Date** (`log_date`, Date, Required): Select the date of the workout from the calendar.
* **Time of Workout** (`log_time`, Time, Optional): Optionally, enter the time of the workout.
* **Workout Duration** (`workout_duration`, Number, Optional): Record the duration of the workout in minutes.
* **Energy Level Before Workout** (`energy_level_pre`, Select One, Required): Choose your energy level before the workout (e.g., Very Low, Low, Moderate, High, Very High).
* **Set 1 - Repetitions** (`bench_row_set_1_reps`, Number, Required): Enter the number of repetitions in the first set.
* **Set 1 - Weight (lbs)** (`bench_row_set_1_weight`, Number, Required): Enter the weight (in pounds) of the first set.
* **Set 1 - Difficulty Level** (`bench_row_set_1_difficulty`, Select One, Optional): Choose the difficulty level of the first set (e.g., Easy, Moderate, Challenging, Very Challenging, Maximum Effort).
* **Set 2 - Repetitions** (`bench_row_set_2_reps`, Number, Required): Enter the number of repetitions in the second set.
* **Set 2 - Weight (lbs)** (`bench_row_set_2_weight`, Number, Required): Enter the weight (in pounds) of the second set.
* **Set 2 - Difficulty Level** (`bench_row_set_2_difficulty`, Select One, Optional): Choose the difficulty level of the second set (e.g., Easy, Moderate, Challenging, Very Challenging, Maximum Effort).
* **Set 3 - Repetitions** (`bench_row_set_3_reps`, Number, Optional): Enter the number of repetitions in the third set.
* **Set 3 - Weight (lbs)** (`bench_row_set_3_weight`, Number, Optional): Enter the weight (in pounds) of the third set.
* **Set 3 - Difficulty Level** (`bench_row_set_3_difficulty`, Select One, Optional): Choose the difficulty level of the third set (e.g., Easy, Moderate, Challenging, Very Challenging, Maximum Effort).
* **Total Repetitions Today** (`total_reps`, Number, Optional): Enter the total number of repetitions completed today.
* **Form Quality** (`form_quality`, Select One, Optional): Rate the quality of the form (e.g., Poor, Fair, Good, Excellent).
* **Additional Exercises Performed** (`additional_exercises`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional exercises performed during the workout.
* **Rest Between Sets** (`rest_periods`, Number, Optional): Enter the rest periods between sets in seconds.
* **Pain or Discomfort** (`pain_discomfort`, Select One, Optional): Report any pain or discomfort experienced during the workout (e.g., None, Mild, Moderate, Severe).
* **Recovery Rate** (`recovery_rate`, Select One, Optional): Rate your recovery rate (e.g., Fast, Normal, Slow, Very Slow).
* **Energy Level After Workout** (`energy_level_post`, Select One, Optional): Choose your energy level after the workout (e.g., Very Low, Low, Moderate, High, Very High).
* **Workout Notes** (`workout_notes`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional workout notes or progress observations.
