# Season Play Voting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed for users to provide feedback and information about a season-related form. It is intended to be used for voting and gathering data about sports seasons, specifically for Baseball, Basketball, Football, Hockey, and Soccer. The form is meant to help users share their opinions, experiences, and thoughts about a particular season of one or more of these sports.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the sport(s) you want to provide feedback about.
2.  Describe the season you are referring to.
3.  Provide a brief description of the season, including your feedback and any additional information you think is relevant.
4.  Identify the audience for your season feedback.
5.  Add any additional feedback or comments you have about the season.
6.  Finally, provide a description of the season play audience, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Season Category** (`season_category`, `select_one`, required): Select the sport for this form. Choose one of the following options: Baseball, Basketball, Football, Hockey, or Soccer.
*   **Season Description** (`season_description`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the season. You can include any details or experiences you've had during the season.
*   **Select a Sport** (`select_sport`, `select_one`, required): Choose one sport that this form is about.
*   **Select Multiple Sports** (`select_multiple_sports`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose multiple sports that this form is about, if applicable.
*   **Feedback** (`feedback`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments you think are relevant to the form.
*   **Voting Form Description** (`season_voting_form_description`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the form.
*   **Season Feedback** (`season_feedback`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional feedback you have about the season.
*   **Season Audience** (`season_audience`, `text`, required): Identify the audience for your season feedback.
*   **Season Play Audience Description** (`season_play_audience_description`, `text`, optional): Enter a description of the season play audience, if applicable.
*   **Feedback (2)** (`season_feedback`, `note`, required): Enter any additional feedback or comments you have about the season.
*   **Season Play Audience Feedback** (`season_play_audience_feedback`, `note`, optional): Enter any feedback or comments about the season play audience, if applicable.
*   **Season Play Audience Voting Form** (`season_play_audience_voting_form`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional details about the season play audience voting form, if applicable.
