# TNM Academy Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is the registration form for the TNM Academy, a platform for individuals to register or enroll in the academy's programs. To fill out this form, you will need to provide basic contact information, demographic details, your sport preferences, and a date of enrollment or birthdate. Please ensure all required fields are completed as this information is crucial for a smooth enrollment process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
- Fill out all the required fields marked as required in the form.
- If you need to select a sport, choose one or multiple from the list provided.
- Enter a valid date for your enrollment or birthdate.
- If you have any notes or comments, feel free to add them in the notes field.
- Once you have completed all the required fields, click the submit button.
- If you need to cancel or stop the registration process, click the cancel button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name here. This will be the first name by which you will be identified in our records.
- **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name here. Like the first name, this is a required field for identification purposes.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address here. This will be used for communication purposes.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number here. This will be used for additional contact methods.
- **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter your date of enrollment or birthdate here. This should be in a standard date format (DD/MM/YY or DD-MM-YY).
- **Gender** (`select_one`, select, required): Select your gender from the given options.
- **Sport** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or multiple sports you are interested in or have experience with.
  - This will help us understand your preferences and provide tailored opportunities or recommendations.
- **Notes** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your enrollment or preferences here. This is optional but can be helpful for further context.
  - Please keep in mind that this field is not for providing sensitive or personal details.
- **Submit** (`submit`, text, not required): Click this button once all required fields are filled out to proceed with the registration process.
- **Cancel** (`cancel`, text, not required): Click this button to stop and cancel the registration process.
