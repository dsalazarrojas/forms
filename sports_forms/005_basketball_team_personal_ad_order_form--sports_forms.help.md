# Basketball Team Personal Ad Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for placing personalized ads for basketball team members. It allows team representatives or players to input details for creating custom ads featuring individual team players.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer's name, email, phone number, and team affiliation in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the ad type and ad size from the available options.
3. Enter the player's name and jersey number (if applicable).
4. Choose the color option for the ad (black and white or full color).
5. Input the ad headline, ad message, and preferred publication date.
6. Select the payment method (credit card, debit card, check, or cash).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: Your full name as the representative or player.
* **Email Address**: Contact email for communication and ad delivery.
* **Phone Number**: Contact phone number for communication and updates.
* **Team Affiliation**: Which team you are associated with.
* **Ad Type**: Select the type of ad you want to create (Player Spotlight, Achievement Announcement, etc.).
* **Ad Size**: Choose the size of the ad (Small, Medium, Large, etc.).
* **Player Name**: Name of the player featured in the ad.
* **Player Number**: Jersey number of the player (if applicable).
* **Player Position**: Position played by the player (if applicable).
* **Ad Headline**: Main headline for the ad.
* **Ad Message**: Full message content for the ad.
* **Photo Upload**: Upload a photo of the player (if available).
* **Preferred Publication Date**: When you want the ad to be published.
* **Color Option**: Choose between black and white or full color for the ad.
* **Quantity**: Number of copies or prints needed (if applicable).
* **Special Requests**: Any additional requirements or requests.
* **Payment Method**: How you will pay for the ad (Credit Card, Debit Card, Check, etc.).

Note: Some fields are required to input information, while others are optional and allow for additional details or preferences.
