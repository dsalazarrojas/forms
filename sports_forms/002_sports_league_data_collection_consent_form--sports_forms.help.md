# sports league data collection consent form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a crucial part of the sports league's data collection process. It helps us gather necessary information from participants and their emergency contacts. The data collected will be stored securely and used for the purpose of contacting emergency contacts in case of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Start by filling in the participant details section, including the participant's name, age, and date of birth.
2. Enter the participant's email address and phone number.
3. Provide the details of an emergency contact, including their name, relationship to the participant, and contact information.
4. Confirm your relationship with the participant.
5. Sign and date the form to acknowledge that you have read and understand the terms of this consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details**
	+ **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, required/true): Enter the name of the participant.
	+ **Age** (`participant_age`, required/true): Enter the age of the participant.
	+ **Date of Birth** (`participant_dob`, required/true): Enter the date of birth of the participant.
	+ **Email** (`participant_email`, required/true): Enter the email address of the participant.
	+ **Phone** (`participant_phone`, required/true): Enter the phone number of the participant.
* **Emergency Contacts**
	+ **Emergency Name** (`emergency_name`, required/true): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
	+ **Relationship with the participant** (`emergency_relationship`, required/true): Choose the relationship between the emergency contact and the participant.
	+ **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, required/true): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
	+ **Emergency Email** (`emergency_email`, optional/true): Enter the email address of the emergency contact (if applicable).
	+ **Emergency Address** (`emergency_address`, optional/true): Enter the address of the emergency contact (if applicable).
* **Consent**
	+ **Consent** (`consent`, required/true): Choose to consent to the collection and processing of your participant information.
* **Signature**
	+ **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, required/true): Sign and date the form to acknowledge that you have read and understand the terms of this consent.
	+ **Emergency Signature** (`emergency_signature`, required/true): Sign and date the form to acknowledge that you have read and understand the terms of this consent.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled in correctly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask for clarification.
* Sign and date the form to ensure that you have provided consent.
* Keep a copy of the signed form for your records.
