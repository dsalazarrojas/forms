# Player Profile Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Player Profile Form is used to collect demographic and emergency contact information about a player, including their relationship with a school and contact details for emergency contacts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the first question, "Are you a coach at a school" with either "Coach" or "Non Coach" to indicate your relationship with the school.
2. If you are a parent or guardian, please enter your phone number in the "Parent's phone number" field.
3. If you want to report your height, enter it in the "Body measurement height" field.
4. If you want to report your weight, enter it in the "Body measurement weight" field.
5. If you have an emergency contact, please enter their phone number in the "Emergency contact phone number" field.
6. Provide the name of your emergency contact in the "Emergency contact name" field.
7. Indicate the relationship between you and your emergency contact in the "Emergency contact relationship" field by selecting one of the options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Are you a coach at a school** (`coach_at_sport`, select_one, required: false)
This question is used to determine whether you are a coach at a school or not.
* If you are a coach, select "Coach".
* If you are not a coach, select "Non Coach".

### 2. **Parent's phone number** (`parents_phone`, text, required: false)
If you are a parent or guardian, please enter your phone number in this field.

### 3. **Body measurement height** (`height`, number, required: false)
This field is used to report your height. Please enter a numeric value.

### 4. **Body measurement weight** (`weight`, number, required: false)
This field is used to report your weight. Please enter a numeric value.

### 5. **Emergency contact phone number** (`emergency_phone`, text, required: false)
If you have an emergency contact, please enter their phone number in this field.

### 6. **Emergency contact name** (`emergency_name`, text, required: false)
Provide the name of your emergency contact in this field.

### 7. **Emergency contact relationship** (`emergency_relationship`, select_one, required: false)
Indicate the relationship between you and your emergency contact by selecting one of the options.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all the questions to ensure that your player's profile is complete and accurate.
* If you are filling out this form for someone else, please make sure to get their consent before sharing their information.
* If you have any questions or concerns about this form, please contact us.
