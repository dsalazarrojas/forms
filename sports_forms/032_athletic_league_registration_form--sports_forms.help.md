# Athletic League Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for athletes to register for the sports league. Please fill it out completely and accurately to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each field's requirements.
2.  Answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
3.  If you are unsure about any field, contact the league administrator for clarification.
4.  Submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Full Name** (`athlete_name`, `text`, required): Please fill out your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required): Select your gender from the provided options.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_guardian_name`, `text`, required): If applicable, fill out the name of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Email** (`parent_guardian_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email of your parent or guardian for contact purposes.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone** (`parent_guardian_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of your parent or guardian.
* **Street Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your home address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State/Province** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter the state or province where you reside.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter your ZIP or postal code.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your current school.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, `select_one`, required): Select your current grade level from the provided options.
* **Sport Selection** (`sport_selection`, `select_one`, required): Select your primary sport of registration from the provided options.
* **Secondary Sport** (`secondary_sport`, `select_one`, optional): If applicable, select a secondary sport from the provided options.
* **League Division** (`league_division`, `select_one`, required): Select your league division based on age or skill level from the provided options.
* **Previous Experience** (`previous_experience`, `number`, required): Enter the years of experience you have in the selected sport.
* **Team Preference** (`team_preference`, `text`, optional): If desired, list any team preference you may have.
* **Friend or Carpool Request** (`friend_request`, `text`, optional): If desired, list the names of friends you wish to be placed with.
* **Coach Request** (`coach_request`, `text`, optional): If desired, list any specific coach request you may have.
* **Registration Type** (`registration_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of registration from the provided options.
* **Registration Fee** (`registration_fee`, `number`, required): Enter the registration fee amount in USD.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Select the method of payment from the provided options.
* **Scholarship or Financial Aid Needed** (`scholarship_needed`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you need financial aid.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Medical Conditions or Allergies** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, required): List any medical conditions or allergies you may have.
* **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, `text`, optional): If applicable, list any current medications.
* **Health Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your health insurance provider.
* **T-Shirt Size** (`t_shirt_size`, `select_one`, required): Select your T-shirt size from the provided options.
* **How Did You Hear About the League?** (`how_heard`, `select_one`, required): Select the source of your referral from the provided options.
* **Volunteer Interest** (`volunteer_interest`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your interest in volunteering from the provided options.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or information you may have.

## Tips

* Please ensure all required fields are completed accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the league administrator.
* Submit the form only once you are confident that all information is accurate.
