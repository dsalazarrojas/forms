# sports_program_complaint_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sports_program_complaint_form is used to report incidents or complaints related to sports programs. This form is designed to gather essential details for the proper handling and resolution of the issues raised.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in the Participant Name field with the person who experienced the issue.
2.  In the Complaint Description field, provide a detailed description of the incident or complaint.
3.  Specify the Complaint Subject field with the specific sport or sport program affected.
4.  Enter the contact phone number for the participant, which is optional.
5.  Enter the contact email address for the participant.
6.  Enter the email address for the person submitting the complaint.
7.  Enter the phone number for the person submitting the complaint.
8.  Select the complaint date when the incident occurred.
9.  Choose the complaint time in 24-hour format.
10. Enter the name of the person submitting the complaint.
11. Enter the name of the organization related to the sport program.
12. Enter the sport program name.
13. Enter the name of the coach associated with the sport program.
14. Enter the location where the incident occurred.
15. Enter the name of the participant affected (if different from the participant who submitted the complaint).
16. Enter the organization of the person submitting the complaint.
17. Leave the participant name field empty if it's not applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: The name of the person who experienced the issue. Please enter the full name as it appears on record.
* **Complaint Description**: A detailed description of the incident or complaint, including dates, times, and other relevant details.
* **Complaint Subject**: The specific sport or sport program affected by the issue.
* **Contact Phone Number**: The phone number of the participant. This is optional but recommended for further contact.
* **Contact E-mail Address**: The email address of the participant for correspondence.
* **Contact E-mail Address (Submitter)**: The email address of the person submitting the complaint.
* **Contact Phone Number (Submitter)**: The phone number of the person submitting the complaint.
* **Complaint Date**: The date when the incident occurred. Use the date format as provided.
* **Complaint Time**: The time when the incident occurred in 24-hour format.
* **Submitter Name**: The name of the person submitting the complaint.
* **Organization**: The name of the organization related to the sport program.
* **Sport Program**: The name of the sport program affected.
* **Coach Name**: The name of the coach associated with the sport program.
* **Complaint Location**: The location where the incident occurred.
* **Participant Name (Optional)**: The name of the participant affected. If not applicable, leave empty.
* **Submitter Organization**: The name of the organization of the person submitting the complaint.
