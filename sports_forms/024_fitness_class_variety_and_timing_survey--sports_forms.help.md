# Fitness Class Variety And Timing Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the variety and timing of fitness classes offered by your gym or fitness center.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Choose the types of fitness classes you are interested in (step 1).
2. Select the frequency of classes you would like to attend (step 2).
3. Indicate your preferred time of day for classes (step 3).
4. Select the duration of your desired classes (step 4).
5. Specify the start and end times for your classes (step 5).
6. Choose your preferred day of the week for classes (step 6).
7. Select your preferred time of day for classes (step 7).
8. Indicate the frequency of classes you would like to attend (step 8).
9. Specify the start and end times for your additional classes (steps 5-7).
10. Add any additional comments about your preferences (step 9).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Fitness Class Types**: Select all the types of classes you are interested in, such as yoga, weightlifting, cardio, etc.
* **Fitness Class Frequency**: Choose how often you would like to attend classes, such as daily, Monday and Tuesday, etc.
* **Time Of Day**: Select your preferred time of day for classes, such as morning, afternoon, or evening.
* **Class Duration**: Choose the length of your desired classes, such as 30-45 minutes, 45-60 minutes, etc.
* **Class Start Time**: Specify the start time for your classes.
* **Class End Time**: Specify the end time for your classes.
* **Preferred Day Of Week**: Choose your preferred day of the week for classes, such as Monday, Tuesday, etc.
* **Preferred Time Of Day**: Select your preferred time of day for classes, such as morning, afternoon, etc.
* **Class Frequency 2**: Select how often you would like to attend additional classes, such as Monday, Tuesday, etc.
* **Class Start Time 2**: Specify the start time for your additional classes.
* **Class End Time 2**: Specify the end time for your additional classes.
* **Class Frequency 3**: Select how often you would like to attend more additional classes, such as Monday, Tuesday, etc.
* **Class Start Time 3**: Specify the start time for your more additional classes.
* **Class End Time 3**: Specify the end time for your more additional classes.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or information about your preferences.

Note: All fields are optional, and you can choose as many or as few options as you like.
