# Community Engagement In Sports - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Community Engagement In Sports" form collects information from individuals who wish to participate in sports-related community engagement activities. This information helps organizers understand the needs and preferences of participants, ensuring a more inclusive and engaging experience for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form by providing personal information, such as your full name and contact details.
2. Select your age group and sports interests from the provided options.
3. Indicate your availability for participation and any interest in volunteering.
4. If applicable, provide an emergency contact name and phone number.
5. Add any additional comments or notes, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name (Participant's Name)** (`participant_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact email (For event updates and confirmations)** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to receive updates and notifications about sports events and activities.
* **Contact phone (Optional mobile or landline number)** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Provide your mobile or landline number for event organizers to contact you, if needed.
* **Age group** (`age_group`, `select_one`, required): Select the age group that best fits your age:
	+ Under 12
	+ 12 to 17
	+ 18 to 35
	+ 36 to 60
	+ Over 60
* **Sports interests** (`sports_interests`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the sports that interest you:
	+ Soccer
	+ Basketball
	+ Volleyball
	+ Running
	+ Swimming
	+ Other
* **Availability for participation** (`availability`, `select_one`, required): Indicate when you are available to participate:
	+ Weekdays
	+ Evenings
	+ Weekends
	+ Flexible
* **Interested in volunteering** (`volunteer_interest`, `select_one`, required): Check if you are interested in volunteering:
	+ 'True'
	+ 'False'
	+ Maybe
* **Emergency contact name and phone** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, optional): Provide the name and phone number of a contact person in case of an emergency.
* **Additional comments or accommodations** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Share any additional comments, notes, or accessibility needs you may have.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling out the form to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for all participants.
- Review the form carefully before submission to avoid any errors.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event organizers for support.
