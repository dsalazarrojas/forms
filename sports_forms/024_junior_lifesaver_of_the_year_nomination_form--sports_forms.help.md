# Junior Lifesaver Of The Year Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Junior Lifesaver Of The Year Nomination Form is designed to recognize and celebrate the bravery and lifesaving skills of junior individuals who have made a significant impact in their communities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary information and details about the junior lifesaver you wish to nominate.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
3. Review the form carefully to ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nomination Form** (text, required): Please provide a brief description of the nominee's lifesaving actions.
* **Youth Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the nominee.
* **Lifesaver** (text, required): Enter the name of the individual who performed the lifesaving actions.
* **Nominee Age** (number, required): Enter the age of the nominee.
* **Nominee School** (text, optional): Enter the name of the nominee's school (if applicable).
* **Nominee Grade** (text, optional): Enter the grade level of the nominee (if applicable).
* **Parent Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the nominee's parent or guardian (if applicable).
* **Nominee Contact** (text, optional): Enter a contact method for the nominee (if applicable).
* **Lifesaver Award Year** (number, required): Select the year for which the nominee is being nominated for the lifesaver award.
* **Lifesaver Achievement** (text, optional): Provide a brief description of the nominee's lifesaving achievement.
* **Lifesaver Nomination Reason** (text, optional): Provide a detailed explanation of why the nominee deserves the award.
