# sports_club_membership_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The sports club membership survey is designed to gather data from sports club members to improve the overall experience, inform future development, and tailor services to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select your overall satisfaction with the sports club.
2. Provide any comments you may have.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Choose your age group.
6. Select the duration of your membership.
7. Choose how often you visit the sports club.
8. Select your preferred activity.
9. Choose your preferred time of day.
10. Add any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): 
    Select how satisfied you are with the sports club.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required): 
    Please provide any comments or feedback you have about your experience with the sports club.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): 
    Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): 
    Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, required): 
    Please select your age group.
* **Membership Duration** (`membership_duration`, `select_multiple`, required): 
    Select the duration of your membership with the sports club.
* **Frequency of Visits** (`frequency_of_visits`, `select_multiple`, required): 
    Choose how often you visit the sports club.
* **Preferred Activity** (`preferred_activity`, `select_one`, required): 
    Select your preferred activity with the sports club.
* **Preferred Time of Day** (`preferred_time_of_day`, `select_one`, required): 
    Choose your preferred time of day to visit the sports club.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`, required): 
    Please add any additional comments or feedback about the sports club.
