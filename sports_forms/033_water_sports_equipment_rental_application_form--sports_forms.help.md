# Water Sports Equipment Rental Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Water Sports Equipment Rental Application Form is designed to provide information for renting water sports equipment. This form will help us track and manage the rental details, including user information, equipment type, and payment status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form by providing required and optional information, as marked on each page.
2. Select the correct equipment type and rental duration.
3. Choose the correct payment method and status.
4. Add any additional comments or notes, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information**: 
  * **ID**: Enter your unique user ID (if provided).
  * **Name**: Enter your name for record keeping purposes.
  * **Contact Method**: Select how you would like to be contacted (e.g., phone, email, or SMS).
  * **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about your rental.
* **Rental Date**: Enter the date you plan to rent the equipment.
* **Equipment Type**: Select the type of equipment you need to rent (e.g., kayak, paddleboard, etc.).
* **Rental Duration**: Specify the number of hours or days you plan to rent the equipment.
* **Rental Location**: Select where you plan to rent the equipment (e.g., lake, ocean, etc.).
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you would like to pay for the rental (e.g., cash, credit card, etc.).
* **Payment Status**: Select whether your payment is active or inactive (e.g., pending, paid, etc.).
* **Payment Date**: Enter the date you made the payment.
* **Payment Time**: Enter the time you made the payment.
* **Payment Duration**: Specify the duration of the payment (not required).
* **Rental Status**: Select whether your rental is active or inactive (e.g., pending, rented, etc.).
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter the tool assigned to you for the rental (if assigned).
* **Assigned Tool Type**: Select the type of tool assigned (if assigned).
* **Form Version**: Select whether this is the current version of the form (not required).
* **Form ID**: Enter a unique ID for the form (not required).
* **Form Category**: Enter the category of the form (not required).
* **Form Description**: Enter a brief description of the form (not required).
* **Form Created By**: Enter the person who created the form (not required).
* **Form Created At**: Enter the date the form was created (not required).
* **Form Last Updated At**: Enter the date the form was last updated (not required).
* **Form Last Updated By**: Enter the person who last updated the form (not required).
* **Form Last Updated Time**: Enter the time the form was last updated (not required).
