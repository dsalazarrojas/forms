# Personal Training Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Training Evaluation Form is a tool used to assess a client's progress, plan, and status in a personal training program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that you have filled out the form after each training session to avoid duplication of data.
2. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Review the form with the trainer before submitting it to ensure that all necessary information is captured.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Progress (1)**: A text area for the client to provide their progress, notes, or any comments.
* **Client Name (2)**: Enter the client's name as it appears on their profile or identification.
* **Training Days (3)**: Record the number of days the client has been training.
* **Workout Plan (4)**: Select the specific workout plan the client is following. 
* **Training Hours (5)**: Record the number of hours the client spends on training.
* **Training Minutes (6)**: Record the number of minutes the client spends on training.
* **Training Duration (7)**: Record the duration of each training session.
* **Training Frequency (8)**: Select how frequently the client trains (e.g., daily, weekly).
* **Training Intensity (9)**: Select how intense the client's training sessions are (e.g., high, medium, low).
* **Training Type (10)**: Select the type(s) of training the client is doing (e.g., cardio, weightlifting, stretching).
* **Client Comments (11)**: Provide any comments or feedback from the client.
* **Trainer Comments (12)**: Provide any comments or feedback from the trainer.
* **Client Feedback (13)**: Provide any feedback or comments from the client.
* **Evaluation Date (14)**: Record the date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Time (15)**: Record the time of the evaluation.
* **Assessment (16)**: Select the client's current assessment status (e.g., progressing, plateauing, not progressing).
* **Assessment Comments (17)**: Provide any comments on the client's assessment.
* **Overall Score (18)**: Record the client's overall score out of a predetermined range.
* **Evaluation Score (19)**: Record the client's evaluation score out of a predetermined range.
* **Training Status (20)**: Select the client's current training status (e.g., active, inactive, cancelled).
* **Next Steps (21)**: Provide any next steps or recommendations for the client's training plan. 

Note: The specific options for select fields will vary based on the specific settings and configuration of the form's implementation. Always refer to the form's settings and your training program's guidelines for specific guidance.
