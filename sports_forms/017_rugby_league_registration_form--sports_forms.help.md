# rugby_league_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is a rugby league registration form designed for players to register for a local or national rugby league program or event. The form collects player information, medical history, and emergency contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the personal details of the player on **Player Information** page.
2. Proceed to the **Contact Details** page to provide your contact information.
3. Then, move on to the **Emergency Contacts** page to list your emergency contacts.
4. Next, provide your medical history and any relevant medical notes on the **Medical History** page.
5. On the **Medical Certificate** page, provide any medical certificates or documents required for the player.
6. Finally, confirm your player information and emergency contact relationships on the **Player Info Confirmation** and **Emergency Contact Relationships** pages.
