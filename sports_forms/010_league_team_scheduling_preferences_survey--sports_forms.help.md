# league_team_scheduling_preferences_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your preferences for scheduling teams in a league. The information gathered from this form will be used to schedule games in a way that takes into account your availability and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page and answer the questions that apply to you. You can skip fields that are not relevant to your situation. For each field, provide as much information as you like, and don't worry if you're not sure about something - just answer to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Availability**: This page asks you to specify your team's availability, including preferred days and times for games.
	+ **Team Availability**: This field allows you to specify your team's availability, including preferred days and times for games. You can choose up to 5 days and 2 time slots for games.
* **Preferred Days**: Choose up to 5 days that you prefer for games.
* **Start Time**: Specify your preferred start time for games.
* **End Time**: Specify your preferred end time for games.
* **Blackout Dates**: Choose up to 3 dates when your team is not available for games.
* **Available Dates**: Choose up to 3 dates when your team is available for games.
* **Available Days**: Choose up to 5 days when your team is available for games.
* **Available Start Time**: Choose up to 2 start time slots when your team is available for games.
* **Available End Time**: Choose up to 2 end time slots when your team is available for games.
* **Preferred Time Slot**: Choose up to 3 time slots that you prefer for games.
* **Conflict Avoidance**: Specify your conflict avoidance strategy.
* **Conflict Avoidance Reason**: If you have a reason for your conflict avoidance strategy, provide it here.
* **Conflict Avoidance Preferred Days**: Choose up to 5 days that you prefer for games when avoiding conflicts.
* **Conflict Avoidance Preferred Time**: Choose up to 2 time slots that you prefer for games when avoiding conflicts.
* **Conflict Avoidance Preferred Slot**: Choose up to 3 time slots that you prefer for games when avoiding conflicts.
* **Conflict Avoidance Blackout**: Choose up to 3 dates when you prefer not to have games.
* **Conflict Avoidance Blackout Start**: Choose up to 2 start time slots when you prefer not to have games.
* **Conflict Avoidance Blackout End**: Choose up to 2 end time slots when you prefer not to have games.
* **Conflict Avoidance Blackout Dates**: Choose up to 3 dates when you prefer not to have games.
* **Blackout Start Time**: Specify your preferred start time for blackouts.
* **Blackout End Time**: Specify your preferred end time for blackouts.
* **Blackout Duration**: Specify the duration of your blackouts.
* **Blackout Frequency**: Specify how often you are available for blackouts.
* **Blackout Type**: Specify the type of your blackouts.
* **Blackout Notes**: Provide any additional information about your blackouts.

## Tips
* Be sure to answer all questions that apply to your situation.
* If you're unsure about a question, just answer to the best of your ability.
* You can provide as much or as little information as you like for each field.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact the league administrators.
