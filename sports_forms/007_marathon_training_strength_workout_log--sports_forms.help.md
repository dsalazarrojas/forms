# marathon_training_strength_workout_log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marathon Training Strength Workout Log form is a tool for recording information about strength workouts during a marathon training program. It's meant to track progress and provide insights for athletes and coaches.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, simply fill in the fields as described below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Exercise** (`Exercise`, text, optional): Enter the name of the exercise you performed during the workout.
* **session_date** (`session_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the workout session.
* **Weight** (`weight`, number, optional): Enter the weight used for the exercise.
* **Reps** (`reps`, number, optional): Enter the number of repetitions completed for the exercise.
* **Sets** (`Sets`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you did Sets, Reps, or RPE during the workout.
* **RPE** (`rpe`, select_one, optional): Select your perceived exertion rating (1-10).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the workout session.
* **Coach Notes** (`coach_notes`, text, optional): Add notes for your coach or training team.

## Tips
* Ensure you fill out each field for a complete record.
* Use the Sets field to indicate whether you completed a set-based workout, a rep-based workout, or a RPE-based workout.
* Remember that each form submission is a new record, so don't worry about duplicating records – start fresh each time!
