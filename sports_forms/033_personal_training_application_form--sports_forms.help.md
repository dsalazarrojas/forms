# Personal Training Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Training Application Form is designed to collect information about individuals interested in pursuing personal training. This form helps us match clients with suitable training programs that cater to their specific needs and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Provide your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your **Email Address** in the "Email" field. Please ensure it's a valid email address.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Phone Number" field.
5. If you have any **Medical Condition**, please describe it in the "Medical Condition" field.
6. State your **Goal** for pursuing personal training in the "Goal" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number where we can reach out to you.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required/optional): If you have any medical conditions that may impact your training, please describe them here.
* **Goal** (`goal`, text, required/optional): Describe your goal for pursuing personal training, such as weight loss, improved fitness, or injury rehabilitation.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the email address and phone number you provide are accurate and up-to-date.
- If you have any medical conditions, please describe them in detail to help us tailor the training program to your needs.
- Be as specific as possible when describing your goal, as this will help us create a more effective training plan for you.
