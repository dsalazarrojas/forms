# Fitness Event Water Station Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Event Water Station Supply Order Form is a tool for event organizers to provide information about the water station supplies needed for a fitness event. This form helps you to specify event details, hydration station requirements, water temperature, and number of water treats.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the details of your event in the "Event Details" field.
2. Specify the number of hydration stations required in the "Hydration Stations" field.
3. Choose the type of water station you need in the "Water Station Type" field.
4. Enter the temperature of the water in the "Water Temperature" field.
5. Indicate the number of water treats you need in the "Water Treats" field.
6. Submit your order by filling out the "Submission" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details** (text, required/optional): Enter the name and description of your event. This will help us understand the context of your order.
* **Hydration Stations** (text, required/optional): Specify the number of hydration stations you require for your event. This will help us determine the quantity of water and supplies needed.
* **Water Station Type** (text, required/optional): Choose the type of water station you need, e.g. water bottle filler, water fountain, etc.
* **Water Temperature** (text, required/optional): Enter the temperature of the water needed for your event. This will help us ensure we provide the correct equipment.
* **Water Treats** (text, required/optional): Indicate the number of water treats you need, e.g. cups, lids, etc.
* **Submission** (text, required/optional): Submit your order by providing your name, contact information, and any additional details about your order.
