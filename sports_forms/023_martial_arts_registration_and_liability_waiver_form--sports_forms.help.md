# Martial Arts Registration And Liability Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Martial Arts Registration and Liability Waiver Form is designed to gather essential information for your registration at a martial arts school and to obtain necessary consents and waivers for your safety and liability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps outlined above and fill in the required information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Martial Arts Registration and Liability Waiver Form**: This is the main title of the form.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Address**: Enter your current address.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Describe your relationship with the emergency contact (e.g., parent, spouse, friend).
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email of the emergency contact (if applicable).
* **Martial Arts School**: Enter the name of the martial arts school you are registering for.
* **Martial Arts Dojo**: Enter the name of the specific dojo or location where you will be training (if different from the school).
* **Instructor**: Enter the name of the instructor who will be training you.
* **Class Date Start**: Enter the date you plan to start classes.
* **Class Time**: Enter the time of your classes (if applicable).
* **Class Duration**: Enter the duration of your classes (if applicable).
* **Class Frequency**: Enter the frequency of your classes (e.g., daily, weekly, bi-weekly).
* **Payment Method**: Choose your preferred payment method (e.g., bank transfer, credit card).
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the amount you are paying for the classes.
* **Emergency Contact Consent**: Confirm that you have informed your emergency contact about your martial arts activities and that they are aware of their contact information.
* **Liability Waiver**: Confirm that you understand and agree to the school's liability waiver policy.
* **Photo Release**: Confirm that you grant permission for the school to take photos or videos of you during classes.
* **Media Release**: Confirm that you grant permission for the school to use your image or likeness for promotional purposes.
* **Emergency Medical Information**: Provide any relevant medical information that may affect your training (e.g., allergies, conditions).
* **Emergency Contact Information**: Provide your emergency contact's contact information (e.g., name, phone, email).
* **Student Information**: Enter any additional student information (e.g., previous martial arts experience).
* **Terms and Conditions**: Read and agree to the school's terms and conditions.

## Tips
- Please fill in the form carefully and accurately.
- Make sure to provide your emergency contact with a copy of this form.
- Review the terms and conditions carefully before agreeing to them.
- If you have any questions, feel free to ask your instructor or school staff.
