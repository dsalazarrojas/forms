# Registration Form For Wrestling Team - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect registration information from individuals interested in joining a wrestling team.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
3. Provide your Email address to ensure we can contact you for further instructions.
4. Enter your Birth Date (mm/dd/yyyy) if you are under 18 years old or if required by your team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: <label>: Enter your personal name that you would like to be known by on the team.
* **Last Name**: <label>: Enter your surname or the name by which you would like to be known on the team.
* **Email**: <label>: Provide your valid email address to ensure we can contact you for further instructions.
* **Birth Date**: <label>: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy if you are under 18 years old or if required by your team.

## Tips
- Ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you are under 18 years old, please provide your birth date to ensure age verification.
- If you have any issues with this form, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx)
