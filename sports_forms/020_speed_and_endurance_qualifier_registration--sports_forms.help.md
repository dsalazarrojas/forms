# Speed And Endurance Qualifier Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Speed And Endurance Qualifier Registration form is designed to collect information about an athlete's qualifications for speed and endurance events. This form helps to gather essential details about the athlete's performance, event category, and any necessary emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each field as instructed, using the information that applies to you.
2. Answer each question carefully, as the information will be used to assess your qualifications for speed and endurance events.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Details (id: 1)** (`Text`, **Required: No**): Enter your first and last name, date of birth, and contact information in the format "Name (Name, Date of Birth, Email, Phone)."
* **Emergency Contacts (id: 2)** (`Text`, **Required: No**): List any emergency contacts that may be reached in case of an unexpected event, such as a family member or friend.
* **Event Category (id: 3)** (`Select Multiple`, **Required: No**): Choose one or more categories that apply to your athletic qualifications:
	+ **Sprint**: Events that require short, fast runs or runs over short distances.
	+ **Distance**: Events that require longer, more endurance-based runs or runs over longer distances.
	+ **Endurance**: Events that require sustained effort over extended periods.
	+ **Trail**: Events that take place on trails or uneven terrain.
* **Event Details (id: 4)** (`Text`, **Required: No**): Provide any additional information about the event, such as the event name, location, or date.
* **Notes (id: 5)** (`Text`, **Required: No**): Enter any additional comments or notes about your qualifications or experience.
