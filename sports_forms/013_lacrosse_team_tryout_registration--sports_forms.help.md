# Lacrosse Team Tryout Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register for a lacrosse team tryout. It is intended for players to provide their contact information, player type, and tryout preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out all the required fields. The required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*).
* Enter your First Name and Last Name in the first two fields.
* Enter your email address in the Email field. Make sure it matches the email you use for your Lacrosse association account.
* In the Contact Info field, enter your address, phone number, or any other relevant contact information.
* Choose a Tryout Time that fits your schedule.
* Select your Player Type from the options provided (Goalie, Defenseman, Forward, or Other).
* Answer yes or no to indicate if you have Team Experience.
* Answer yes or no to indicate if a Parent Permission is required.
* Add any additional Notes about your tryout if needed.
* Click on the "Submit" field to submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address. This should match your Lacrosse association account email.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your address, phone number, or other contact information.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Choose the date for your tryout.
* **Tryout Time** (`tryout_time`, time, optional): Choose the tryout time that fits your schedule.
* **Player Type** (`player_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of player you are (Goalie, Defenseman, Forward, or Other).
* **Team Experience** (`team_experience`, select_multiple, optional): Answer yes or no if you have team experience.
* **Parent Permission** (`parent_permission`, select_one, optional): Answer yes or no if a parent permission is required.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your tryout.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click this to submit your tryout registration.
