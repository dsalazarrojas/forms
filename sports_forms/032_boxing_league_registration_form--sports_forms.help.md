# Boxing League Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from boxers who wish to register with the league for participation in a boxing competition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your first name, last name, date of birth, and email address on page 1.
2. Select your gender, weight class, and boxing experience on page 2.
3. Enter your address, phone number, and emergency contact information on page 3.
4. Provide your height, weight, medical clearance, and medical conditions on page 4.
5. Choose your league division and pay your registration fee on page 5.
6. Confirm you have read and agree to the league rules and liabilities on page 6.
7. Sign your signature and date of registration on page 7.
8. If the boxer is under 18 years old, their parent or guardian must sign on page 8.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your legal first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your legal last name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Gender**: Select your gender.
* **Address**: Enter your complete home address.
* **City**: Enter the city of your residence.
* **State**: Enter the state of your residence.
* **ZIP Code**: Enter your home ZIP code.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your primary contact number.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for league communications.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the full name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Describe your relationship with your emergency contact person.
* **Weight Class**: Select your competition weight class.
* **Current Weight**: Enter your current weight in pounds.
* **Height**: Enter your height in feet and inches.
* **Reach**: Enter your arm reach in inches (if known).
* **Boxing Experience**: Select your level of boxing experience.
* **Skill Level**: Select your skill level as a boxer.
* **Amateur Record**: Enter your amateur boxing record (if applicable).
* **Gym Affiliation**: Enter the name of your boxing gym.
* **Coach Name**: Enter the full name of your primary coach.
* **Coach Phone**: Enter your coach's contact number (optional).
* **Medical Insurance Provider**: Enter the name of your medical insurance provider.
* **Insurance Policy Number**: Enter your insurance policy number.
* **Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions we should know about.
* **Previous Head Injuries**: Select any history of head injuries or concussions.
* **League Division**: Select the division you wish to register for.
* **Registration Fee**: Enter the amount paid for registration.
* **Payment Method**: Select how you paid for your registration.
* **Liability Waiver**: Confirm you understand and agree to the risks of boxing.
* **Medical Clearance**: Confirm you have medical clearance to compete.
* **Rules Acknowledgment**: Confirm you have read and agree to the league rules.
* **Boxer Signature**: Enter your full legal name as a signature.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date of registration.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature**: Enter your parent or guardian's signature (if under 18 years old).

**Tips**

* Ensure all fields are completed thoroughly and accurately.
* Review your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the league administrator.
* Make sure to read and agree to the league rules and liabilities before proceeding.
* If you are under 18 years old, ensure a parent or guardian signs on your behalf.
