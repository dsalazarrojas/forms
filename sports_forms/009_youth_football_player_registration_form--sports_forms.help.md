# Youth Football Player Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register youth football players for a specific season. Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the designated field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the corresponding field.
3. If necessary, provide your **Date of Birth**.
4. Enter your **Contact Number** if you have one.
5. Provide your **Email** address if available.
6. Enter your **Address** if different from your contact information.
7. List the **Emergency Contact** if applicable.
8. Enter the **Emergency Contact First Name** and **Last Name** if applicable.
9. Enter the **Guardian First Name** and **Last Name** if applicable.
10. Enter the **Parent First Name** and **Last Name** if applicable.
11. Select the **Player Club** you are registered with.
12. Choose your **Player Position** on the team.
13. If necessary, provide **Medical History** details.
14. Provide a description of **Medical History Description** if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): If you are under 18, your parent or guardian will need to fill this information.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter your contact number for communication.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter your address if different from your contact information.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required): Name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (`emergency_contact_first_name`, `text`, required): First name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (`emergency_contact_last_name`, `text`, required): Last name of the emergency contact.
* **Guardian First Name** (`guardian_first_name`, `text`, required): First name of the guardian.
* **Guardian Last Name** (`guardian_last_name`, `text`, required): Last name of the guardian.
* **Parent First Name** (`parent_first_name`, `text`, required): First name of the parent.
* **Parent Last Name** (`parent_last_name`, `text`, required): Last name of the parent.
* **Player Club** (`player_club`, `text`, required): Select the club you are registered with.
* **Player Position** (`player_position`, `text`, required): Select your position on the team.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, optional): Provide any medical history relevant to your participation.
* **Medical History Description** (`medical_history_description`, `note`, optional): Provide a description of your medical history if required.

## Tips

- Be accurate and honest when filling out this form. Any false or misleading information may lead to disqualification.
- Make sure to provide up-to-date contact information.
- If you have any medical conditions, please inform the organization about it in the medical history section.
