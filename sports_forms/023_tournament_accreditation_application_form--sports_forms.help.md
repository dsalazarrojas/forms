# Tournament Accreditation Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect essential details from applicants for tournament accreditation. This includes general information, contact details, payment information, and payment terms. Completing this form will help us process your application efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your general information, including any relevant details about your tournament.
2. Enter your contact details so we can get in touch with you regarding your application.
3. If applicable, provide your payment information so we can process your application fee.
4. Read and agree to our payment terms to ensure you understand our policies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### General Information (Page 1)

* **General Information** (`title`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your tournament, including its name, type, and any other relevant details.

### Contact Details (Page 2)

* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, required/optional): Enter your name, email address, and phone number so we can get in touch with you.

### Payment Information (Page 3)

* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, required/optional): Enter your payment method and details, such as credit card number and expiration date.

### Payment Terms (Page 4)

* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, required/optional): Read and agree to our payment terms to ensure you understand our policies.
 
## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any field, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* This form is for general informational purposes only, and any information provided will be used to process your application efficiently.
