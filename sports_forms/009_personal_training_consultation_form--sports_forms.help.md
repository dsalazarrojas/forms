# Personal Training Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Training Consultation Form is designed to help trainers understand your personal fitness goals and preferences. Completing this form will help us provide you with tailored guidance and support to achieve your fitness objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
- To start, fill in your **First Name** in the "First name" field. This is the first and last name you were given at birth.
- Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last name" field.
- Provide your **Email** address in the "Email" field. This will be used to contact you for further consultation.
- Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Phone" field.
- Select a **Date** for your consultation in the "Date" field. This is the date you would like to have your consultation.
- Choose a **Time** for your consultation in the "Time" field. Please select from the available options.
- Provide any additional **Note** about your consultation in the "Note" field.
- Specify your **Training Goal** in the "Training goal" field. What specific area of fitness are you looking to achieve?
- Select your **Training Level** in the "Training level" field. This will help us understand your current fitness level.
- Select the **Days** you are available for your consultation in the "Frequency" field. You can choose multiple days for regular consultations.
- Finally, select a **Schedule** date for your consultation in the "Schedule" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
- **Last name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
- **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select a date for your consultation.
- **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select a time for your consultation.
- **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Provide any additional information about your consultation.
- **Training goal** (`training_goal`, text, optional): Specify your training goal.
- **Training level** (`training_level`, select_one, optional): Select your current fitness level.
- **Frequency** (`frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Select the days you are available for your consultation.
- **Schedule** (`schedule`, date, optional): Select a schedule date for your consultation.
