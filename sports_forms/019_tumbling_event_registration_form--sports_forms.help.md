# Tumbling Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tumbling Event Registration Form is designed to register athletes for a tumbling event. This form is used to gather essential information about the athlete, emergency contacts, and event details.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the Tumbling Event Registration Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked as required.
2. Leave optional fields blank unless you have relevant information to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tumbling Event Registration** (`athlete_details`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the event or activity.
* **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, text, required: false): Provide the name and contact information of the person responsible for the athlete's care in case of an emergency.
* **Parent Details** (`parent_details`, text, required: false): Enter the parent or guardian's contact information.
* **Consent** (`consent`, text, required: false): We value your safety and privacy; this consent will not affect your participation status.
* **Athlete Name** (`athlete_name`, text, required: false): Enter the athlete's name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter the athlete's date of birth in the correct date format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, text, required: false): Select the type of event or activity.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the event or activity.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the start date of the event in the correct date format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required: false): Enter the start time of the event in the correct time format (HH:MM:SS).
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the end date of the event in the correct date format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required: false): Enter the end time of the event in the correct time format (HH:MM:SS).
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of the event.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required: false): Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for the event.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter the contact phone number of the person responsible for the event.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the contact email of the person responsible for the event.
* **Emergency Name** (`emergency_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person responsible for emergency contact.
* **Emergency Email** (`emergency_email`, email, required: false): Enter the emergency contact email.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the emergency contact phone number.
* **Consent Type** (`consent_type`, select_one, required: false): Select whether you are the parent, guardian, or legal representative.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required: false): Confirm that you consent to this information being shared with our staff for the purposes of this event.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments.
