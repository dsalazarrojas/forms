# League Locker Room Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for coaches and staff to submit requests for supplies needed in the locker room. Please ensure that your request includes all required information to guarantee a successful delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the page to fill out.
2. Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
3. Select a delivery schedule.
4. Choose a delivery method.
5. Add any additional notes or comments.
6. Enter the contact person's name and phone number.
7. Enter the contact person's email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **League Locker Room Supply Requests** (`league_locker_room_supply_order_form_page_1`, Text, Required): Enter the requested supplies in the text box. Please include the quantity and type of supplies needed.
* **Delivery Schedule** (`delivery_schedule`, Date, Optional): Select the date for delivery. If no specific date is selected, the default date will be used.
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, Select One, Optional): Choose how the supplies will be delivered. You can select from Pickup, Delivery, or Drop-Off.
* **Notes** (`notes`, Text, Required): Add any additional comments or notes about your request.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, Text, Required): Enter the person's name who should be contacted regarding this order.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, Text, Optional): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, Email, Optional): Enter the contact person's email.
* **League Info** (`league_info`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional information about the league that is relevant to the order.
