# Fitness Transformation Starting Point Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you assess your current fitness level and provide essential information about your transformation goals and current workout routine. The insights gathered from this form will be used by our fitness professionals to offer you tailored guidance and support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your current fitness level from the provided options.
2. Choose your primary fitness goal(s) from the available options.
3. Enter your current body measurements (if applicable).
4. Assess your current fitness level in relation to the provided options.
5. Provide any additional comments or feedback about your current fitness state.
6. Enter the frequency of your current workouts.
7. Add any additional comments or feedback about your current workouts.
8. Enter any additional information about yourself (if required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Starting Point Info**: Enter a brief description of your current fitness level, goals, and any relevant medical or health information.
* **Fitness Level**: Select the best description of your current fitness level:
	+ Beginner: You are new to exercise or have been inactive for a while.
	+ Intermediate: You have some experience with exercise, but may not be consistent or regular.
	+ Advanced: You are physically fit and regularly active.
* **Fitness Goals**: Choose one or more goals for your transformation:
	+ Weight Loss: To lose weight and achieve a healthier body composition.
	+ Muscle Gain: To increase muscle mass and strength.
	+ Endurance: To improve cardiovascular fitness and stamina.
* **Body Measurements**: Enter your current body measurements (height, weight, body fat percentage, etc.).
* **Fitness Level Assessment**: Re-evaluate and update your current fitness level based on your goals and progress.
* **Fitness Level Comments**: Provide additional feedback or comments about your current fitness state.
* **Current Work Out Frequency**: Enter the frequency of your current workouts (number of times per week).
* **Form Completion Comments**: Add any additional comments or feedback about your form completion.
* **Client Info**: Enter any additional information about yourself that may be relevant to your fitness journey.
