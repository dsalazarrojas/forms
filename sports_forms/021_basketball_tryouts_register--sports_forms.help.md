# Basketball Tryouts Register - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Basketball Tryouts Register form is for players to sign up for a basketball tryout. To complete this form, you will need to provide personal and professional details about yourself, including your contact information, playing experience, and statistics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the first page labeled "Minor League Basketball Tryout Registration"
2. Fill out all required fields, including "Player Full Name", "Email Address", "Phone Number", "Date of Birth", "Height (Total Inches)", "Weight (Pounds)", "Primary Position", "Highest Level of Competition Played", "Most Recent Team and Year", and "Registration Fee Payment Status"
3. If you have professional representation, select "True" for the "Do you have professional representation (Agent)?" field
4. If you have a highlight reel, upload a link to it in the "Link to Highlight Reel" field
5. Select your experience level in the "Highest Level of Competition Played" field
6. Click on the "Submit" button to complete the form

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Player Full Name** (Text, Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (Email, Required): Enter your official email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Required): Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Height (Total Inches)** (Number, Required): Enter your total height in inches, e.g. 74 for 6 foot 2.
* **Weight (Pounds)** (Number, Required): Enter your weight in pounds.
* **Primary Position** (Select One, Required): Select your primary playing position, such as Point Guard, Shooting Guard, etc.
* **Highest Level of Competition Played** (Select One, Required): Select the highest level of competition you have played, such as Professional (International), G-League, etc.
* **Most Recent Team and Year** (Text, Required): Enter the name and year of your most recent team.
* **Registration Fee Payment Status** (Select One, Required): Select whether you have already paid the $150 registration fee or will pay at the door.
* **Do you have professional representation (Agent)?** (Select One, Required): Select whether you have professional representation, such as an agent.
* **Link to Highlight Reel** (Text, Optional): If you have a highlight reel, provide a link to it.
* **Career Statistics Summary** (Text, Optional): Enter any relevant career statistics, such as PPG, RPG, APG, etc.
* **Video Highlights URL** (Text, Optional): If you have a video of your playing highlights, provide a link to it.
* **Wingspan (Inches)** (Number, Optional): Enter your wingspan in inches.
* **Career Experience Header** (Note, Optional): This section is for providing any additional career experience.
* **Registration Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date you are registering for the tryout.

Please note that all fields marked as "Required" must be completed to register for the tryout.
