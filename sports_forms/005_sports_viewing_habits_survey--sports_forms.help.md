# Sports Viewing Habits Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information about your sports viewing habits and preferences to help us improve our sports content and services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions as honestly as possible.
2. Choose your responses from the provided options for select questions.
3. Leave a response in the text fields for questions that allow multiple answers or open-ended comments.
4. Review your answers before submitting to ensure they are accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How many sports do you like?**
	+ This question asks about the number of sports you enjoy watching or following.
	+ If you like multiple sports, select all that apply.
* **Which sports do you follow?**
	+ In this text field, list the sports you regularly follow or are interested in.
* **Favorite Sports 2**
	+ This text field is for listing any other sports you enjoy beyond your initial selection.
* **How often do you watch sports live?**
	+ Choose how frequently you watch sports events live, either in-person or through streaming services.
* **What sports channels do you have?**
	+ Select all sports channels you subscribe to or have access to.
* **What sports events do you watch?**
	+ Choose events you regularly watch, either live or recorded.
* **Which sports teams do you support?**
	+ In this text field, list the teams you actively root for or support.
* **Which sports leagues do you watch?**
	+ List the sports leagues you follow or are interested in.
* **Sports League**
	+ This text field is for listing any other sports leagues you enjoy.
* **Sports Channels 2**
	+ This text field is for listing any other sports channels you have access to.
* **Which sports events do you attend?**
	+ Describe the sports events you regularly attend, either in-person or through streaming services.
* **Which sports premier leagues do you watch?**
	+ Choose the premier leagues you follow or are interested in.
* **Which sports teams play in premier leagues?**
	+ List the sports teams that participate in premier leagues you watch.
* **How often do you watch sports games in leagues?**
	+ Choose how frequently you watch sports games in premier leagues.
* **How often do you watch sports games in premier leagues?**
	+ This question is a duplicate of the previous one, so feel free to answer it the same as before.

Note: Please be as specific as possible when answering the questions, and don't hesitate to ask if you have any questions or need clarification.
