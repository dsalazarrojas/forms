# Sports Coach Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Coach Onboarding Form is used to provide essential information about new sports coaches who have not yet been added to the system. This form helps us to onboard coaches efficiently and ensure we have accurate and up-to-date information about them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all the required information about yourself, such as your full name, email address, and availability.
* Fill in your full name, email address, and other relevant information in the corresponding fields.
* If you are not sure about the format or type of information required for a particular field, please refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Coach Full Name** (`coach_full_name`, `text`, required: false)
* This field is for entering your full name as a coach. Please include both your first and last names.

### **Coach Email** (`coach_email`, `email`, required: false)
* Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.

### **Coach License** (`coach_license`, `text`, required: false)
* If you have a coaching license, please enter the number or ID of your license. If you don't have a license, you can leave this field blank.

### **Coach Experience Years** (`coach_experience_years`, `number`, required: false)
* Enter the number of years you have been coaching.

### **Coach Education Level** (`coach_education_level`, `select_one`, required: false)
* Select the highest level of education you have achieved:
	+ High School
	+ Bachelor's Degree
	+ Master's Degree
	+ Doctoral Degree
	+ Other (please specify)

### **Coach Availability** (`coach_availability`, `time`, required: false)
* Select your regular working hours, e.g., 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

### **Coach Availability Notes** (`coach_availability_notes`, `note`, required: false)
* Provide any additional information about your availability, e.g., if you are available only on weekends or have limited hours during certain times of the year.

### **Coach Work Hours** (`coach_work_hours`, `text`, required: false)
* Enter any additional work hours information that you feel is relevant to your coaching services.
