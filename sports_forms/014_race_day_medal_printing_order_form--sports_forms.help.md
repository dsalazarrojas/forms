# Race Day Medal Printing Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Race Day Medal Printing Order Form is used to place orders for custom-made race day medals. It guides the user through the selection of medal design, quantity, shipping address, total cost calculation, shipping cost, payment method, and payment amount.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the design of the medal you want to order.
2. Choose the quantity of medals you need.
3. Enter your shipping address where the medals will be sent.
4. Review the total cost calculation, which is based on your selected options.
5. Select the shipping cost for your order.
6. Choose your payment method.
7. Enter the payment amount.
8. Click the "Submit" button to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **race day medal text input** (`race_day_medal_text_input`, text, optional): Enter a brief description or message for the medal.
* **medal design** (`medal_design_select`, select one, optional): Select the design of the medal from the available options (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **quantity** (`quantity_select`, select multiple, optional): Choose the quantity of medals you need (quantity1, quantity2, quantity3).
* **shipping address** (`shipping_address_input`, text, optional): Enter the shipping address where the medals will be sent.
* **total cost calculation** (`total_cost_calculation`, note, optional): This field displays the calculated total cost for your order based on your selected options.
* **shipping cost** (`shipping_cost_input`, number, optional): Enter the shipping cost for your order.
* **payment method** (`payment_method_select`, select one, optional): Choose the payment method (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **payment amount** (`payment_amount_input`, number, optional): Enter the payment amount for your order.
* **submit** (`submit_button`, note, optional): Click this button to complete and submit your order.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the total cost calculation before finalizing your order.
* Double-check the shipping address to ensure it is correct.
* Selecting the correct payment method and amount is crucial to completing the order.
