# Junior Golf Program Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to register individuals for the Junior Golf Program. The form is required for program participants to provide necessary information, including personal details and emergency contact information. It also helps the program administrators manage the registration process efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the `First Name` field.
2. Enter your last name in the `Last Name` field.
3. Enter your email address in the `Email` field.
4. Enter your phone number in the `Phone` field.
5. Enter your date of birth in the `Date Of Birth` field.
6. Enter your emergency contact's first name in the `Emergency Contact First Name` field.
7. Enter your emergency contact's last name in the `Emergency Contact Last Name` field.
8. Enter your emergency contact's phone number in the `Emergency Contact Phone` field.
9. Indicate your relationship with the emergency contact in the `Relationship` field.
10. Choose whether or not you are participating in the program in the `Program` field.
11. Choose whether or not you are having a session in the program in the `Session` field.
12. Choose your division in the `Division` field.
13. Select your level in the `Level` field.
14. Choose your payment plan in the `Payment Plan` field.
15. Optionally, enter any additional notes or comments in the `Notes` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your given name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your surname.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Date Of Birth** (`dob`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (`emergency_contact_first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your emergency contact's first name.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (`emergency_contact_last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your emergency contact's last name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you are related to your emergency contact (e.g., Parent, Spouse, Friend).
* **Program** (`program`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether or not you are participating in the program.
* **Session** (`session`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether or not you are having a session in the program.
* **Division** (`division`, `select_one`, required): Choose your division (e.g., Junior, Senior, Adult).
* **Level** (`level`, `select_one`, required): Choose your level (e.g., High, Low).
* **Payment Plan** (`payment_plan`, `select_one`, required): Choose your payment plan (e.g., Monthly, Quarterly).
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your registration.
