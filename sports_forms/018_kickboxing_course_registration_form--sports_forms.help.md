# Kickboxing Course Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kickboxing Course Registration Form is a crucial document that collects essential information from students registering for a kickboxing course. The form is designed to be completed by the student, and it's recommended that you review the fields carefully to ensure accurate and complete information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Enter the date you plan to attend the course in the "Date" field.
6. Enter the time you plan to attend the course in the "Time" field.
7. Enter the duration of the course in the "Course Duration" field.
8. Enter your student name as it appears on your identification in the "Student Name" field.
9. Select the registration option that applies to you in the "Student Registration" field.
10. If you have any additional comments or information to share, enter them in the "Additional Comments" field.
11. Provide the emergency contact's name in the "Emergency Contact Name" field (if applicable).
12. Select the relationship between you and the emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Relationship" field (if applicable).
13. Enter the emergency contact's phone number in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field (if applicable).
14. Enter the emergency contact's email address in the "Emergency Contact Email" field (if applicable).
15. Select the instructor who will be leading your course in the "Instructor" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date you plan to attend the course.
* **Time** (Time, Required): Enter the time you plan to attend the course.
* **Course Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date of the course you're registering for.
* **Course Time** (Time, Required): Enter the time of the course you're registering for.
* **Course Duration** (Number, Required): Enter the number of hours in the course.
* **Student Name** (Text, Required): Enter your student name as it appears on your identification.
* **Student Registration** (Select Multiple, Required): Select the registration option that applies to you.
* **Additional Comments** (Text, Note, Optional): Enter any additional comments or information you'd like to share.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select the relationship between you and the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (Email, Optional): Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Instructor** (Select One, Required): Select the instructor who will be leading your course.
* **Additional Comments** (Text, Note, Optional): Enter any additional comments or information you'd like to share.

## Tips

* Ensure that you fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to avoid any issues with your course registration.
* If you're registering multiple students, create a new form for each student.
* Review the fields carefully to ensure you provide the required information.
* Don't hesitate to reach out if you have any questions or need assistance with your course registration.
