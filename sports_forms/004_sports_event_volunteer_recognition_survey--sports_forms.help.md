# Sports Event Volunteer Recognition Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect data about volunteers involved in sports events to recognize and appreciate their contributions. This information will help us understand the impact of our volunteers and show our appreciation for their efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the volunteer's name in the **Volunteer Name** field.
2. Select the event the volunteer participated in from the **Event Name** field.
3. Answer the questions about the volunteer's role, feedback, and recognition methods, frequency, and amount.
4. Provide any additional comments or notes about the volunteer's experience in the **Comments** field.
5. Enter the volunteer's email and phone number for contact information.
6. Record the event date, time, and location.
7. Provide details about the volunteer's hours worked and the unit of measurement for those hours.
8. Answer the recognition frequency and amount questions to help us understand the impact of our volunteers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Name**: Enter the name of the volunteer you are reporting on.
* **Event Name**: Select the name of the event the volunteer participated in.
* **Volunteer Role**: Indicate if the volunteer had a role in the event, such as 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Feedback**: Provide any feedback or comments about the volunteer's experience.
* **Recognition Methods**: Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to questions about the methods used for recognition, such as gift cards, social media posts, etc.
* **Recognition Frequency**: Choose how often recognition was provided, answering 'Yes' or 'No' for each of the four questions.
* **Recognition Amount**: Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to questions about the amount of recognition provided for each method.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about the volunteer's experience.
* **Email**: Enter the volunteer's email address for contact information.
* **Phone**: Enter the volunteer's phone number for contact information.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event.
* **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the event.
* **Recognition Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about recognition provided.
* **Volunteer Hours**: Enter the number of hours the volunteer worked.
* **Volunteer Hours Unit**: Indicate the unit of measurement for the volunteer's hours, answering 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Volunteer Hours for Workshop**: Enter the number of hours the volunteer worked for the workshop.
* **Volunteer Hours for Event**: Enter the number of hours the volunteer worked for the event.
* **Recognition Date**: Enter the date recognition was provided.
* **Recognition Time**: Enter the time recognition was provided.
* **Recognition Frequency 1-4**: Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to questions about recognition frequency, repeating for each question.
