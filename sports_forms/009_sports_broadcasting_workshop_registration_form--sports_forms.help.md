# Sports Broadcasting Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather crucial information from participants for the sports broadcasting workshop. This information includes name, contact details, and preferences for the workshop. Completing this form will ensure that you have a seamless and enjoyable experience at the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
6. Select your preferred workshop location from the "Workshop Location" field.
7. Select one or more workshops from the "Selected Workshop" field.
8. Add any additional notes in the "Note" field.
9. Confirm that you have read and agreed to the "Terms and Conditions" by selecting the correct option.
10. Confirm that you are submitting the form by selecting the correct option in the "Submission Confirmation" field.
11. Confirm that you would like to receive email communications from us by selecting the correct option in the "Email Confirmation" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address here.
* **Phone Number** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number here.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Required): Enter your date of birth here.
* **Workshop Location** (Select One, Required): Select your preferred workshop location from the list.
* **Selected Workshop** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select one or more workshops from the list. You can choose up to [X] options.
* **Note** (Note, Optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your registration here.
* **Terms and Conditions** (Select One, Optional): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the workshop.
* **Submission Confirmation** (Select One, Optional): Confirm that you are submitting the form.
* **Email Confirmation** (Select One, Optional): Confirm that you would like to receive email communications from us.
