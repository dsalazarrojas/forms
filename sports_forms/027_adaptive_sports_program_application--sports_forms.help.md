# Adaptive Sports Program Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adaptive Sports Program Application is a form designed to collect athlete personal information, medical details, and consent for participation in the Adaptive Sports Program. The program is intended for athletes with various disabilities to engage in sports activities that are adapted to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

*   Fill in your personal details in the "Athlete Full Name", "Date of Birth", "Email Address", and "Phone Number" fields.
*   Select your primary sport of interest from the list provided in the "Primary Sport(s) of Interest" field.
*   Select your primary disability category from the list provided in the "Primary Disability Category" field.
*   Enter your equipment requirements, if any, in the "Equipment Requirements" field.
*   If you have a primary care physician, enter their name in the "Primary Care Physician" field.
*   If you have any known allergies or medical conditions, list them in the "Known Allergies / Medical Conditions" field.
*   Sign the "I hereby release the program from liability during participation" agreement.
*   Grant permission for photos to be used for program promotion in the "I grant permission for photos to be used for program promotion" field.
*   Finally, select a date for your application submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Athlete Full Name**: Enter your full name.
*   **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
*   **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
*   **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
*   **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
*   **Relationship to Athlete**: Describe your relationship to the athlete.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
*   **Primary Sport(s) of Interest**: Select all sports that interest you from the list provided.
*   **Primary Disability Category**: Select your primary disability category from the list provided.
*   **Equipment Requirements**: If you require any adaptive equipment, describe it here.
*   **Primary Care Physician**: Enter the name of your primary care physician.
*   **Known Allergies / Medical Conditions**: List any known allergies or medical conditions you have.
*   **I hereby release the program from liability during participation**: Sign the agreement to release the program from liability during participation.
*   **I grant permission for photos to be used for program promotion**: Select "True" to grant permission for photos to be used for program promotion.
*   **Date of Application**: Select the date of your application submission.
