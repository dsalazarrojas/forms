# Referee Course Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Referee Course Application form is designed to allow users to sign up for a sports referee course, providing them with the opportunity to obtain necessary training and certification to officiate sports games.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Referee Course Application form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective text fields.
2. Enter your Email address in the Email field, following the email format (e.g., example@email.com).
3. Provide your Phone number in the Phone field.
4. Select the sport you are interested in taking the referee course for (Basketball, Soccer, or Volleyball) in the Which referee course do you want to sign up for? field.
5. Enter the desired date for the Referee Course in the Referee Course Date field.
6. Specify the time you prefer for the Referee Course in the Referee Course Time field.
7. Select the duration of the Referee Course you are interested in (this option is usually numeric, e.g., 1 or 2 hours).
8. Enter the name of the Referee Course Instructor in the Referee Course Instructor field.
9. If you have any specific comments or requests regarding the Referee Course, provide them in the Referee Course Comments field.
10. Confirm that you have read and agree to the Course Agreement in the Course Agreement field.
11. Enter the date you agree to the Course Agreement in the Course Agreement Date field.
12. Select the time you agree to the Course Agreement in the Course Agreement Time field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (text)**: Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name (text)**: Please enter your last name.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address in the format example@email.com.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Which referee course do you want to sign up for? (select_one)**: Choose the sport you are interested in taking the referee course for (Basketball, Soccer, or Volleyball).
* **Referee Course Date (date)**: Enter the date you prefer for the Referee Course.
* **Referee Course Time (time)**: Enter the time you prefer for the Referee Course.
* **Referee Course Duration (number)**: Select the duration of the Referee Course you are interested in (usually numeric, e.g., 1 or 2 hours).
* **Referee Course Instructor (text)**: Enter the name of the Referee Course Instructor.
* **Referee Course Comments (note)**: If you have any specific comments or requests regarding the Referee Course, provide them here.
* **Course Agreement (select_one)**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the Course Agreement.
* **Course Agreement Date (date)**: Enter the date you agree to the Course Agreement.
* **Course Agreement Time (time)**: Enter the time you agree to the Course Agreement.

## Tips
* Please ensure you enter accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
* If you have any issues or concerns while filling out this form, feel free to reach out to the relevant authorities or support team.
