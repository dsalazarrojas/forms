# Youth Hockey Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Hockey Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from users for the registration process. This form ensures that all necessary details are captured accurately, and it helps to prevent duplicate registrations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
2. Ensure that you provide the correct payment terms and method.
3. Verify that you have entered the correct payment status.
4. Complete the emergency contact information accurately.
5. Fill in the medical information, if applicable.
6. Select the correct jersey size and color.
7. Enter your birthdate and contact information.
8. Fill in the coach's information accurately.
9. Review and submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.
