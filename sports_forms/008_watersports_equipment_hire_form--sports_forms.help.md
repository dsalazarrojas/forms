# Watersports Equipment Hire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Watersports Equipment Hire Form is designed to collect information from customers interested in hiring watersports equipment for their activities. Please fill in the required fields to ensure a smooth and efficient rental process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, contact number, and email address in the respective fields.
2. Select the preferred date and time for the equipment hire.
3. Choose the type of equipment you would like to hire (kayak, paddleboard, surfboard, wetsuit, or bike).
4. Indicate the number of adults and children who will be using the equipment.
5. Provide any special instructions or notes in the "Special Instructions" field.
6. Select the submit option to confirm your equipment hire details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Your name, which will be used as a reference point for the rental process.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Your contact number where you can be reached for any further information or inquiries.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Your email address where you can receive important updates and notifications regarding your rental.
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, date, required): Select the date you would like to hire the equipment. Please ensure it is in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, time, required): Choose the time slot you would like to hire the equipment. Please select from the available time slots.
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of equipment you would like to hire (kayak, paddleboard, surfboard, wetsuit, or bike).
* **Number of Adults** (`number_of_adults`, number, required): Indicate how many adults will be using the equipment.
* **Number of Children** (`number_of_children`, number, required): Indicate how many children will be using the equipment.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, note, required): Provide any special instructions or notes regarding your rental requirements.
* **Watersports Equipment Hire Form Submit** (`watersports_equipment_hire_form_submit`, select_multiple, required): Select the submit option to confirm your equipment hire details. Please choose either "confirm" or "agree" to proceed with the rental process.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate information to avoid any miscommunication or delay in the rental process.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please provide them in the "Special Instructions" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities or customer support team.
