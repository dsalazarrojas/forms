# okinawa_chess_championship_tournament - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Okinawa Chess Championship Tournament registration form is designed to collect information from players participating in the tournament.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name in the "name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
4. Select the start and end dates of the tournament in the "tournament_start_date" and "tournament_end_date" fields.
5. Enter your name as a player in the "player_name" field.
6. Enter your email address as a player in the "player_email" field.
7. Enter your phone number as a player in the "player_phone" field.
8. Enter your agreement as a player in the "player_agreement" field.
9. Enter your rating as a player in the "rating" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name**: This is the field to enter your name. (text, required)
* **email**: This is the field to enter your email address. (email, required)
* **phone**: This is the field to enter your phone number. (text, required)
* **tournament_start_date**: This is the field to select the start date of the tournament. (date, required)
* **tournament_end_date**: This is the field to select the end date of the tournament. (date, required)
* **player_name**: This is the field to enter your name as a player. (text, required)
* **player_email**: This is the field to enter your email address as a player. (email, required)
* **player_phone**: This is the field to enter your phone number as a player. (text, required)
* **player_agreement**: This is the field to enter your agreement as a player. (text, required)
* **rating**: This is the field to enter your rating as a player. (text, required)

## Tips
* Please ensure that you enter accurate and correct information in all fields.
* Make sure to check the dates for the tournament before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
