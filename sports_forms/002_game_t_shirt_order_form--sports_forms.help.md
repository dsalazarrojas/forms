# Game T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Game T Shirt Order Form is a simple form designed for users to place custom T-shirt orders. This form allows users to select their desired T-shirt size, color, and quantity, as well as provide their contact information and payment method.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Game T Shirt Selection

* **Game T Shirt** (`name`): Please enter your name as it would appear on the T-shirt. This field is not required.
* **T Shirt Size** (`t_shirt_size`): Choose your preferred T-shirt size from the following options: S, M, L, or XL.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`): Select the number of T-shirts you would like to order.

### Step 2: Color Selection

* **Color** (`color`): Choose one or more colors for your T-shirt from the following options: Blue, Red, or Green.

### Step 3: Contact Information

* **Shipping Address** (`shipping`): Please enter your shipping address in this text area.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address for contact purposes.

### Step 4: Payment

* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Choose your preferred payment method from the following options: Bank Transfer or Credit Card.

### Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields, even if they are not marked as required by the system.
* If you need to order multiple T-shirts, use the quantity field to specify the total number of T-shirts you would like to order.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us at your chosen email address.
