# Participation And Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect relevant information from participants for a sports-related event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each section carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for event attendance.
3. Enter your contact details in the participant contact section.
4. Set your fundraising goal in the specified format.
5. Provide any past experiences relevant to the event.
6. Share your medical history if necessary.
7. Choose "Agree" or "Disagree" for consent to terms and conditions.
8. Choose your assigned tool from the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event Attendance (<page: 1)
• Event Attendance (`event_attendance`, select_one, required: false): Choose "Yes" if you plan to attend the event, and "No" otherwise.

### Participant Contact (<page: 2)
• Participant Contact (`contact_details`, text, required: false): Enter your name, email address, and any other contact information you wish to provide.

### Fundraising Goal (<page: 3)
• Fundraising Goal (`fundraising_goal`, number, required: true): Enter your fundraising goal as a numerical value (e.g., 100.50).

### Past Experience (<page: 4)
• Past Experience (`past_experience`, text, required: false): Share any relevant past experiences that would be helpful for the event organizers to know.

### Medical History (<page: 5)
• Medical History (`medical_history`, text, required: false): If you have any medical conditions that may impact your participation, please share them with us.

### Consent to Terms and Conditions (<page: 6)
• Consent to Terms and Conditions (`consent_terms`, select_one, required: false): Choose "Agree" to confirm you have read and accept the terms and conditions.

### Assigned Tool (<page: 7)
• Assigned Tool (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required: false): Select the tool you are assigned to use during the event.

## Tips

* Ensure you fill out all required fields.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the event organizers for clarification.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
