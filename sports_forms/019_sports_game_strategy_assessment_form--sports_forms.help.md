# Sports Game Strategy Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Game Strategy Assessment Form is a tool used by sports teams to evaluate their performance, strategy, and communication during games. It helps teams identify areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions to optimize their game strategy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section and answer the questions accordingly.
2. Use the options provided for each field to select the most accurate response.
3. Provide detailed explanations for each field where possible.
4. Be honest and truthful in your responses, as the goal is to gain a clear understanding of your team's performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pre Game Preparation (Page 1)**
	+ <label>Pre Game Preparation</label> (`pre_game_preparation`, text, optional): This field is used to describe the team's preparation before the game, including any pre-game routines, warm-ups, or team meetings.
* **In Game Adjustments (Page 2)**
	+ <label>In Game Adjustments</label> (`in_game_adjustments`, text, optional): This field is used to document any adjustments made during the game, such as substitutions, formations, or timeouts.
* **Team Communication (Page 3)**
	+ <label>Team Communication</label> (`team_communication`, text, optional): This field is used to assess the team's communication during the game, including how well players and coaches were able to convey messages and instructions.
* **Team Performance (Page 4)**
	+ <label>Team Performance</label> (`team_performance`, select_one, optional): Select from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor to rate the team's overall performance during the game.
* **Coach Coaching (Page 5)**
	+ <label>C Coach Coaching</label> (`coach_coaching`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply from Encourage, Motivate, and Criticize to describe the coach's coaching style during the game.
* **Player Happiness (Page 6)**
	+ <label>Player Happiness</label> (`player_happiness`, number, optional): Use a numerical value between 1 and 100 to rate how happy the players were during the game.
* **Team Happiness (Page 7)**
	+ <label>Team Happiness</label> (`team_happiness`, number, optional): Use a numerical value between 1 and 100 to rate the team's overall happiness during the game.
* **Game Strategy (Page 8)**
	+ <label>Game Strategy</label> (`game_strategy`, text, optional): This field is used to describe the team's game strategy, including any key decisions made during the game.
* **Game Outcome (Page 9)**
	+ <label>Game Outcome</label> (`game_outcome`, text, optional): This field is used to report the final game outcome, including any notable events or results.
* **Post Game Debriefing (Page 10)**
	+ <label>Post Game Debriefing</label> (`post_game_debriefing`, text, optional): This field is used to document any post-game debriefing or discussions that took place after the game.

## Tips
* Be thorough and honest in your responses to gain a accurate understanding of your team's performance.
* Review the form before submission to ensure all necessary fields have been answered.
* Use concrete examples and descriptions to support your responses.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your coach or team management.
