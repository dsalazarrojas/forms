# scuba_diving_medical_clearance_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather medical information from divers for scuba diving medical clearance. The form is designed to ensure that divers are safe to participate in scuba diving activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide a detailed medical history, including any previous medical conditions or injuries that may impact your diving safety.
2. List any medications you are currently taking.
3. For each medication, provide the name of the medication, dosage, and frequency.
4. Choose the correct medical clearance option from the list provided.
5. Enter your doctor's name, address, and contact information.
6. Enter your doctor's license number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, `text`, required: false): Please provide a detailed description of your medical history, including any previous medical conditions or injuries that may impact your diving safety.
* **Medical History Condition** (`medical_history_condition`, `text`, required: false): List any medical conditions or injuries that you have experienced in the past.
* **Meditations** (`medication_list`, `text`, required: false): Please list any medications you are currently taking.
* **Meditations** (`medication_list_item`, `text`, required: false): For each medication, enter the name, dosage, and frequency.
* **Medical Clearance** (`medical_clearance`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the correct medical clearance option from the list provided.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of your doctor.
* **Doctor's Address** (`doctor_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your doctor's address.
* **Doctor's Phone** (`doctor_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your doctor's phone number.
* **Doctor's Email** (`doctor_email`, `text`, required: false): Enter your doctor's email address.
* **Doctor's License Number** (`doctor_license_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your doctor's license number (if applicable).
