# Girl Scout Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Girl Scout Registration Form is designed to collect information from potential members for the purpose of registration into the Girl Scout organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter your birth date in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **T-Shirt Size** (`tshirt_size`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the size of the t-shirt that you would like to wear. Options: Small, Medium, Large, X-Large, Extra Large.
* **Membership Type** (`membership_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose your membership type. Options: Full Membership, Free Membership, Trial Membership.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the person you would like to have contact with in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your relationship with the contact person you listed above.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, required/optional): List any medical conditions that you have.
* **Medical Procedures** (`medical_procedures`, `text`, required/optional): List any medical procedures that you have undergone.

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
