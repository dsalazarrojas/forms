# Team Player Medical Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Player Medical Screening Form is designed to collect medical information from team players to ensure their safety and well-being during sports activities. This form should be completed by the player themselves to provide accurate and complete information about their medical status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question carefully and accurately.
2. Provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
3. Select the correct options from the list for questions with multiple options.
4. If you have any medical conditions or concerns, please list them in the text fields provided.
5. Sign and date the form to indicate that you have read and understood the questions and answers provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Player Information** (`player_info_1`): Enter your full name as you would like it to appear on the team roster.
* **Date of Birth** (`player_info_2`): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address** (`player_info_3`): Enter your current address.
* **Contact Phone** (`player_info_4`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`player_info_5`): Enter your contact email address.
* **Name of Contact** (`emergency_info_1`): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_info_2`): Select the relationship between you and the emergency contact person.
* **Phone** (`emergency_info_3`): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Email** (`emergency_info_4`): Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Major Medical Condition** (`medical_history_1`): List any major medical conditions that may impact your ability to participate in sports activities.
* **Allergies** (`medical_history_2`): List any allergies you have.
* **Previous Medical Conditions** (`medical_history_3`): List any previous medical conditions or surgeries.
* **Last Medical Check-Up** (`medical_history_4`): Enter the date of your last medical check-up.
* **Last Medical Check-Up Result** (`medical_history_5`): Enter the result of your last medical check-up.
* **Current Medical Conditions** (`medical_history_6`): List any current medical conditions.
* **Player Consent** (`player_consent`): Sign and date this section to indicate that you have read and understood the questions and answers provided.
* **Do you have any medical conditions that may impact your ability to participate in the sport/activity?** (`player_consent_label`): Answer this question honestly and thoroughly.
* **Do you have any other medical conditions not listed above?** (`medical_history_7`): List any other medical conditions not mentioned earlier.
* **Medical History Information** (`medical_history_8`): Enter any additional medical history information.
* **Is there anything else you want to tell us?** (`medical_history_9`): Enter any additional medical information or concerns.
* **Is there any other information about you we should know?** (`player_info_6`): Enter any other information about yourself that may be relevant to your participation in sports activities.
