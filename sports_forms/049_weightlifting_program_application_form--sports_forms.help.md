# Weightlifting Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to apply for the weightlifting program. Fill out the form to register your interest and provide necessary information for your participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the required information and submit it. If you need any help or clarification, feel free to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main Information (Main Information)**: This field is for providing basic information about yourself.
* **Contact Information (Contact Information)**: Enter your contact details so we can get in touch with you.
* **Physical Information (Physical Information)**: Describe your current physical state.
* **Medical Information (Medical Information)**: Provide your medical history, if any.
* **Emergency Contact (Emergency Contact)**: Enter the contact information for your emergency contact.
* **Training Information (Training Information)**: Describe your current fitness level.
* **Medical History (Medical History)**: Provide any medical conditions that might affect your participation in the program.
* **Allergies (Allergies)**: List any allergies you have.
* **Medications (Medications)**: List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Training Level (Training Level)**: Select your current training level.
* **Height and Weight (Height and Weight)**: Enter your height and weight.
* **Age (Age)**: Enter your age.
* **Medical Condition (Medical Condition)**: Describe any medical condition that might affect your participation in the program.
* **Emergency Contact Name (Emergency Contact Name)**: Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (Emergency Contact Phone)**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship (Emergency Contact Relationship)**: Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Doctor Phone (Doctor Phone)**: Enter your doctor's phone number.
* **Medical Insurance (Medical Insurance)**: Provide your medical insurance information.
Note: Please ensure that all the fields marked as required are filled out correctly to avoid any issues with your application.
