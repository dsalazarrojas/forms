# Player Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect player information for registration purposes. It is used to gather details such as contact, demographic, and emergency contact information. Please note that this form is intended to be filled out by individual players, not teams. If you are trying to register a team, you will need to go to the Team Registration Form instead.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First name in the "First name" field.
2. Fill in your Last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your Date of birth in the "Date of birth" field in the correct format (yyyy-mm-dd).
6. Enter your current Address in the "Address" field.
7. Enter your Street in the "Street" field.
8. Enter your City in the "City" field.
9. Enter your State in the "State" field.
10. Enter your Zip Code in the "Zip" field.
11. Choose your Country of residence from the list.
12. Indicate if you are affiliated with a Team by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Team" field.
13. Indicate your role in the team by selecting "Yes" or "No" for each option in the "Role" field.
14. Enter the name of your emergency contact in the "Emergency contact name" field.
15. Enter the phone number of your emergency contact in the "Emergency contact phone" field.
16. Enter your relation with your emergency contact in the "Emergency contact relation" field.
17. Enter additional information about your emergency contact in the "Emergency contact info" field.
18. Enter the street of your emergency contact's address in the "Emergency contact info street" field.
19. Enter the city of your emergency contact's address in the "Emergency contact info city" field.
20. Enter the state of your emergency contact's address in the "Emergency contact info state" field.
21. Enter the zip code of your emergency contact's address in the "Emergency contact info zip" field.
22. Enter the country of your emergency contact's address in the "Emergency contact info country" field.
23. Enter the phone number of your emergency contact in the "Emergency contact info phone" field.
24. Enter the email of your emergency contact in the "Emergency contact info email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First name** (`text`, optional): Enter your first name here.
- **Last name** (`text`, optional): Enter your last name here.
- **Email** (`email`, required): Enter your email address to help us stay in touch with you.
- **Phone** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number to help us reach out to you.
- **Date of birth** (`date`, optional): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (yyyy-mm-dd).
- **Address** (`text`, optional): Enter your current address.
- **Street** (`text`, optional): Enter your street address.
- **City** (`text`, optional): Enter your city of residence.
- **State** (`text`, optional): Enter your state of residence.
- **Zip** (`text`, optional): Enter your zip code.
- **Country** (`text`, optional): Choose your country of residence from the list.
- **Team** (`select_one`, optional): Check if you are affiliated with a team.
- **Role** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select roles that apply to you, such as Coach, Player, or Referee.
- **Emergency contact name** (`text`, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
- **Emergency contact phone** (`text`, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
- **Emergency contact relation** (`text`, optional): Enter your relation with your emergency contact.
- **Emergency contact info** (`text`, optional): Enter additional information about your emergency contact.
- **Emergency contact info street** (`text`, optional): Enter the street of your emergency contact's address.
- **Emergency contact info city** (`text`, optional): Enter the city of your emergency contact's address.
- **Emergency contact info state** (`text`, optional): Enter the state of your emergency contact's address.
- **Emergency contact info zip** (`text`, optional): Enter the zip code of your emergency contact's address.
- **Emergency contact info country** (`text`, optional): Enter the country of your emergency contact's address.
- **Emergency contact info phone** (`text`, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
- **Emergency contact info email** (`email`, optional): Enter the email of your emergency contact.
