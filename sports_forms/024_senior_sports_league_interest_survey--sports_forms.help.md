# Senior Sports League Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from seniors about their interest and preferences regarding senior sports leagues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields, marked as "Required" in the field description.
2. Choose the correct options for each field, as described in the field-by-field explanations below.
3. Answer honestly and accurately to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. sports_league_interest (text, optional)
* **Sports League Interest**: Enter a brief description of your interest in participating in a senior sports league. Please be as detailed as possible.

### 2. sports_league_preferences (select_multiple, optional)
* **Sports League Preferences**: Select all the sports that interest you, such as bowling, golf, or other recreational sports. You can choose multiple options if applicable.

### 3. days_of_week (select_one, optional)
* **Days of the Week**: Select the day of the week that you are most available to participate in a senior sports league. Please choose one option that best suits your schedule.

### 4. start_time (time, optional)
* **Start Time**: If you selected a specific day of the week, please enter the start time that works best for you. This will help us determine the start time of the senior sports league.

### 5. end_time (time, optional)
* **End Time**: If you selected a specific day of the week and start time, please enter the end time of the senior sports league.

### 6. frequency (select_one, optional)
* **Frequency**: Select how often you would like to participate in a senior sports league. Please choose one option that suits your schedule and preferences.

### 7. interest_level (number, optional)
* **Interest Level**: Indicate your level of interest in participating in a senior sports league, on a scale of 1-10. Please be honest and accurate to the best of your ability.

### 8. email (email, optional)
* **Email**: Enter your email address, if you have one. This will help us contact you for follow-up information and updates.

### 9. phone (text, optional)
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number, if you have one. This will help us contact you for follow-up information and updates.

### 10. name (text, optional)
* **Name**: Enter your name, if you have one. This will help us personalize our communication and address you correctly.

### 11. additional_comments (note, optional)
* **Additional Comments**: Use this field to provide any additional comments or information about your interest in participating in a senior sports league. This is an opportunity to share any specific needs or concerns that you may have.
