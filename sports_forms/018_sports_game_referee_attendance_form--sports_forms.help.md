# Sports Game Referee Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for sports game referees to report their attendance and game details to sports organizations or leagues for administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Complete the **Game Details** section with the game details, such as game location and weather.
2. Report your **Attendance** by selecting "Yes" or "No" for the game.
3. Provide your **Referee Info**, including any relevant information about yourself as the referee.
4. Add any additional **Game Info**, **Team Info**, and **Other Info** related to the game.
5. Leave any comments or additional notes in the **Comments** section.
6. Enter the **Date/Time** of the game.
7. Select the **Game Type** (e.g., Yes or No).
8. Enter your **Referee Fee** (if applicable).
9. Select whether you received a **Referee Fee** (Yes or No).
10. Select the **Team League** and **Team Division** (if applicable).
11. Select your **Referee Level** (High or Low).
12. Enter the **Game Location** and **Team Location**.
13. Report the **Game Weather** (Yes or No).
14. Leave any additional **Game Notes**.
15. Enter the **Game Venue** and **Team Venue**.
16. Enter your contact information, including **Referee Phone**, **Referee E-mail**, and **Team Phone** and **Team E-mail**.
17. Finally, add any **Other Notes**.
