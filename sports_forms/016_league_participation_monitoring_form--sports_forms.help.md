# League Participation Monitoring Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to monitor and track participant information for a league. It helps administrators keep up-to-date records of who is participating in the league, which is useful for organizing events, tracking progress, and ensuring accurate participation tracking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "participant name" field with the name of the person participating in the league.
2. Provide the participant's "participant id" (if applicable) for accurate tracking.
3. Enter the name of the team or group participating in the league.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Participant Name**
participant name (text, required: false)
* Enter the name of the participant, such as "John Doe" or "Jane Smith".

### **Participant ID**
participant id (number, required: false)
* If you have a unique ID for the participant, enter it here. This is optional but helps with precise tracking.

### **Team Name**
team name (text, required: false)
* Enter the name of the team or group participating in the league.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out as much information as needed for accurate tracking.
* If you are not sure about a field, it is usually better to err on the side of caution and ask for help rather than leaving it blank.
* Double-check the entered information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* This form can be used for various types of leagues, such as sports tournaments or academic competitions.
