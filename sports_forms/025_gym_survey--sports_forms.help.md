# gym_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your fitness habits and preferences regarding home fitness equipment and gym membership.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Home Fitness Equipment" page to answer questions about the types of equipment you use at home.
2. Select the "Gym Membership" page to choose whether you have a gym membership and which type of membership you have.
3. Select the "Age" page to input your age.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Home Fitness Equipment** (`home_fitness_equipment`, text, required: false):
	+ This field is where you can list the types of home fitness equipment you have and use regularly. Please provide a brief description of the equipment you have, such as a treadmill, dumbbells, or a yoga mat.
* **Gym Membership** (`gym_membership`, select one, required: false):
	+ Please select one of the options below to indicate whether you have a gym membership. If you have a membership, choose the type of membership you have (e.g., monthly, yearly, etc.).
* **Age** (`age`, number, required: false):
	+ Please input your age to help us understand your demographic information.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* You can skip any questions that do not apply to you.
* If you have any questions or need help, please don't hesitate to contact us.
