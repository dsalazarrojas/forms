# match_performance_evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to evaluate the performance of a sports match, providing a structured and comprehensive way to collect data on various aspects of the match.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the match ID to identify the match being evaluated.
2. Select the player ID of the player being evaluated.
3. Enter the match details, including the match outcome (e.g., Team Won, Team Lost, Draw).
4. Enter the match outcome (a numerical value).
5. Enter the player's score.
6. Enter any match notes or observations.
7. Evaluate the team's performance by selecting the relevant options.
8. Evaluate the player's position on the field.
9. Enter the player's performance notes.
10. Enter any evaluation notes or comments.
11. Enter any recommended actions.
12. Enter any coach comments or feedback.
13. Enter any team comments or feedback.
14. Enter the team's rating.
15. Enter the match date and time.
16. Enter the match duration.
17. Enter the match location.
18. Select the weather conditions during the match.
19. Enter any match notes or observations.
20. Enter the team name.
21. Enter the player name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Match ID** (`match_id`, text, required/optional): Enter the unique ID of the match being evaluated.
* **Player ID** (`player_id`, text, required/optional): Select the ID of the player being evaluated.
* **Match Details** (`match_details`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the match.
* **Match Outcome** (`match_performance`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the outcome of the match (e.g., Team Won, Team Lost, Draw).
* **Match Outcome** (`match_outcome`, number, required/optional): Enter a numerical value representing the match outcome.
* **Player Score** (`player_score`, number, required/optional): Enter the player's score.
* **Match Notes** (`match_notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any notes or observations about the match.
* **Team Performance** (`team_performance`, text, required/optional): Evaluate the team's performance by selecting relevant options.
* **Player Position** (`player_position`, select_one, required/optional): Select the player's position on the field.
* **Player Performance** (`player_performance`, text, required/optional): Enter any notes or observations about the player's performance.
* **Evaluation Notes** (`evaluation_notes`, text, required/optional): Enter any evaluation notes or comments.
* **Recommended Actions** (`recommended_actions`, text, required/optional): Enter any recommended actions or changes.
* **Coach Comments** (`coach_comments`, text, required/optional): Enter any coach comments or feedback.
* **Team Comments** (`team_comments`, text, required/optional): Enter any team comments or feedback.
* **Team Rating** (`team_rating`, text, required/optional): Enter the team's rating.
* **Match Date** (`match_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the match.
* **Match Time** (`match_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the match.
* **Match Duration** (`match_duration`, text, required/optional): Enter the duration of the match.
* **Match Location** (`match_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location of the match.
* **Match Weather** (`match_weather`, select_one, required/optional): Select the weather conditions during the match.
* **Match Notes** (`match_notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any match notes or observations.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the team name.
* **Player Name** (`player_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the player's name.
