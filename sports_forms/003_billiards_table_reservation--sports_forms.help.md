# Billiards Table Reservation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for reserving a billiards table for a session of billiards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
- Enter your name and email address to confirm your reservation.
- Choose your experience level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Professional).
- Select a date and time for your session.
- Specify how long you'd like to use the table (in hours).
- Indicate whether you prefer a specific type of table (Pool Table, Snooker Table, Carom Table, No Preference).
- If you have a group, enter the number of players and the type of group (Casual Friends, Tournament, League Match, Corporate Event, Party, Other).
- If you're booking a tournament, enter the name of the tournament.
- If you have any special requests or need to rent equipment, let us know.
- Finally, review the terms and agreement by confirming you understand the 24-hour cancellation policy and agreeing to follow the house rules.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number for any updates.
* **Experience Level**: Choose your experience level:
	+ Beginner: Less than 6 months of experience.
	+ Intermediate: More than 6 months, but less than 2 years.
	+ Advanced: More than 2 years.
	+ Professional: Highly skilled player.
* **Reservation Date**: Select the date you'd like to reserve the table.
* **Start Time**: Select the start time for your session.
* **Duration in Hours**: Specify how long you'd like to use the table.
* **Table Type Preference**: Choose your preferred type of table:
	+ Pool Table: A classic, standard table used for most games.
	+ Snooker Table: A smaller, more challenging table.
	+ Carom Table: A large table with a specific type of cloth.
	+ No Preference: Any table available.
* **Table Size**: Choose the size of table you prefer:
	+ 7-foot: A standard, smaller table.
	+ 8-foot: A bit larger, but still a good size for most games.
	+ 9-foot: The largest size table, ideal for more competitive games.
	+ No Preference: Any table size available.
* **Number of Players**: Enter the number of players in your group.
* **Group Type**: Choose the type of group:
	+ Casual Friends: A group of friends looking for a fun session.
	+ Tournament: A competitive session with other players.
	+ League Match: A match for league points.
	+ Corporate Event: A corporate event or team-building activity.
	+ Party: A social gathering with friends.
	+ Other: Any other type of group.
* **Tournament Name**: If this is a tournament, enter the name of the event.
* **Special Requests or Accommodations**: If you have any special requests, enter them here.
* **Need to Rent Equipment**: Check if you need to rent any equipment (cues, balls, chalk, etc.).
* **Food and Beverage Order**: Indicate if you'd like to pre-order food or drinks.
* **I understand the Cancellation Policy**: Confirm you're aware of the 24-hour cancellation policy.
* **I agree to follow the House Rules**: Confirm you'll follow all house rules during your session.
