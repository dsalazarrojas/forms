# Covid 19 Pilates Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from clients for their safety and well-being during their workout sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Client Information**
	* Fill in your name and contact information in the "Client Information" section.
2. Enter your emergency contact's name and phone number in the "Emergency Contact" section.
3. Check if you have any medical conditions that may affect your workout in the "Medical Conditions" section.
4. If you have a doctor's contact information, please enter it in the "Doctor's Name", "Doctor's Phone", and "Doctor's Address" sections.
5. If you have insurance, please enter the insurance company and provider information in the "Insurance Company", "Insurance Provider", and "Insurance Number" sections.
6. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" section.
7. Check if you have any emergency declarations or acknowledgements in the "Emergency Declaration", "Acknowledgement", and "Terms and Conditions" sections.
8. Finally, sign and date the form in the "Signature" and "Date Signed" sections.
