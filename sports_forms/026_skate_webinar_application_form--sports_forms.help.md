# Skate Webinar Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Skate Webinar Application Form is designed to collect participant information and payment details for a webinar on skate-related topics. This form helps to ensure that participants are properly registered and informed about the webinar content.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Provide participant information**:
	* Enter your name, email address, and any other relevant details in the "Participant Information" field.
2. **Select webinar options**:
	* Choose your preferred payment method and webinar information in the "Webinar Information" field.
3. **Provide payment information**:
	* Select your preferred currency for payment (USD, CAD, or EUR) from the "Payment Information" field.
4. **Add any additional information**:
	* If applicable, choose any additional options or information relevant to your participation in the webinar.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Participant Information (Page 1)
- **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, text, optional): Enter your name and email address to register for the webinar.

### Webinar Information (Page 2)
- **Webinar Information** (`webinar_info`, text, required): This field is required to ensure you receive relevant information about the webinar content.

### Payment Information (Page 3)
- **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, select_multiple, required): Select your preferred currency for payment. This will determine the payment options for your participation in the webinar.

### Additional Information (Page 4)
- **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, select_multiple, optional): If applicable, choose any additional options or information relevant to your participation in the webinar.

## Tips
- Ensure that you complete all required fields to ensure proper registration and access to webinar content.
- If you experience any issues with the form, please contact form@gic.mx for assistance.
