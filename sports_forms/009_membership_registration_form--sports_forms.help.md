# Membership Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to gather information from users who want to register as a member of the organization. The collected data will be used to create a membership account and provide access to exclusive content and services. Please ensure that you have read and understood the form before proceeding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in your user information on page 1.
2. Provide your contact information on the second page.
3. Enter your email address on page 3, and confirm it on page 5.
4. Enter your phone number on page 4.
5. Select your membership type from the options on page 6.
6. Check the agreement box on page 7, indicating you have read and understood the terms.
7. Add any additional note if needed on page 8.
8. Confirm that you have read and agreed on the terms on page 9.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`user_input`, text, optional): Enter your name and other relevant details.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Fill in your address, phone number, or any other relevant contact details.
* **Email** (`email`, text, required): Enter a valid email address that you will use to access our services.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number to help us contact you.
* **Confirm Email** (`email_confirmation`, text, optional): Re-enter your email address to confirm it's correct.
* **Membership Type** (`membership_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of membership you are applying for. Choose from Individual, Corporate, or Other.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, text, optional): Check the box to indicate you have read and understood the terms.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your application.
* **Confirm Agreement** (`agreement_confirmation`, text, optional): Re-enter the agreement text to confirm you have read and understood it.
* **User Email** (`user_email`, text, required): Re-enter your email address to confirm your identity.
