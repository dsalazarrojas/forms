# Tournament Offer Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tournament Offer Form is a tool used by sports tournament organizers to gather essential information about a new tournament offer. It will help you create and submit a comprehensive description of your event, including all necessary details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the following required information:
	* **Name**: This field is used to provide a brief title for your tournament.
	* **Date**: Enter the date when the tournament is going to take place.
	* **Offer Type**: Select the type of tournament (Single-elimination Bracket, Double-elimination Bracket, Round-robin, or Swiss).
	* **Number of Games**: Specify the number of games played in the tournament.
2. Fill in the following optional information:
	* **Time**: Enter the expected time of the tournament.
	* **Location**: Provide the location of the tournament.
	* **Teams**: Indicate if teams are involved in the tournament (Yes/No).
	* **Rules**: Briefly describe the rules of the tournament.
	* **Description**: Give a detailed description of your tournament.
	* **Team Name**: Enter the name of the team participating.
	* **Team Email**: Enter the email of the team representative.
	* **Team Phone**: Provide the phone number of the team representative.
	* **Team Representative**: Enter the name of the team representative.
	* **Team Address**: Enter the address of the team.
	* **Prize Purse**: Specify the prize money or reward for the tournament.
	* **Entry Fee**: Specify the entry fee for the tournament.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter a brief title for your tournament.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date when the tournament is going to take place.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the expected time of the tournament.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Provide the location of the tournament.
* **Teams** (`teams`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if teams are involved in the tournament (Yes/No).
* **Rules** (`rules`, text, optional): Briefly describe the rules of the tournament.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Give a detailed description of your tournament.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the team participating.
* **Team Email** (`team_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the team representative.
* **Team Phone** (`team_phone`, text, optional): Provide the phone number of the team representative.
* **Team Representative** (`team_representative`, text, optional): Enter the name of the team representative.
* **Team Address** (`team_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the team.
* **Offer Type** (`offer_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of tournament (Single-elimination Bracket, Double-elimination Bracket, Round-robin, or Swiss).
* **Number of Games** (`number_of_games`, number, optional): Specify the number of games played in the tournament.
* **Game Duration** (`game_duration`, time, optional): Enter the duration of each game in the tournament.
* **Prize Purse** (`prize_purse`, text, optional): Specify the prize money or reward for the tournament.
* **Entry Fee** (`entry_fee`, text, optional): Specify the entry fee for the tournament.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check your information for accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please contact the tournament organization for assistance.
