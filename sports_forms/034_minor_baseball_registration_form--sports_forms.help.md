# Minor Baseball Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Minor Baseball Registration Form is used to register minor baseball players for a specific team, league, and season. It collects essential information about the player, their emergency contact, and team details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the player's name in the **Player Name** field.
2. Enter the player's birth date in the **Birth Date** field. Make sure to format the date in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
3. Provide the player's contact information in the **Contact Info** field.
4. List the player's emergency contact's name and contact information in the **Emergency Contact** field.
5. Check the **Parent Permission** field to indicate whether you have permission to register the player.
6. Select the player's type from the **Player Type** options (Player, Coach, or Umpire).
7. Enter the player's jersey number in the **Jersey Number** field.
8. Enter the player's position in the **Player Position** field.
9. Choose the preferred contact method (Email or Phone) in the **Contact Preference** field.
10. Enter the player's contact email in the **Contact Email** field.
11. Enter the player's contact phone number in the **Contact Phone** field.
12. Enter the team ID in the **Team ID** field.
13. Enter the team name in the **Team Name** field.
14. Enter the division ID in the **Division ID** field.
15. Enter the division name in the **Division Name** field.
16. Enter the league ID in the **League ID** field.
17. Enter the league name in the **League Name** field.
18. Enter the season ID in the **Season ID** field.
19. Enter the season name in the **Season Name** field.
20. Enter a nickname or alias for the player in the **Player Name Alias** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Name** (required): Enter the player's full name.
* **Birth Date** (required): Enter the player's birth date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Contact Info** (required): Enter the player's contact information (address, phone number, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact** (required): List the player's emergency contact's name and contact information.
* **Parent Permission** (required): Check the box to indicate that you have permission to register the player.
* **Player Type** (optional): Select the player's type (Player, Coach, or Umpire).
* **Jersey Number** (optional): Enter the player's jersey number.
* **Player Position** (optional): Enter the player's position.
* **Contact Preference** (optional): Choose the preferred contact method (Email or Phone).

* **Contact Phone** (optional): Enter the player's contact phone number.
* **Team ID** (optional): Enter the team ID.
* **Team Name** (optional): Enter the team name.
* **Division ID** (optional): Enter the division ID.
* **Division Name** (optional): Enter the division name.
* **League ID** (optional): Enter the league ID.
* **League Name** (optional): Enter the league name.
