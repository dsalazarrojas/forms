# Youth Sports Program Impact Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to help users understand how to complete the Youth Sports Program Impact Poll form, which is meant to gather feedback on youth sports programs in your area.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type(s) of youth sports programs offered in your area.
2. Choose how often you participate in sports activities in your free time.
3. Indicate your favorite type of sport or physical activity.
4. Specify how long you have been participating in sports activities.
5. Rate the quality of sports facilities and equipment in your area.
6. Choose how often you engage in physical activity for mental health benefits.
7. Provide a brief explanation on what motivates you to participate in sports activities.
8. Rate how much your overall well-being has improved since participating in sports activities.
9. (Duplicate question, please note that you will answer it again as question 10)
10. Rate how much your social connection has improved since participating in sports activities.
11. Rate how much your self-confidence has improved since participating in sports activities.
12. Briefly describe any benefits you have gained from participating in sports activities.
13. Share any recommendations for improving sports programs in your area.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What type of youth sports program is offered in your area?** (Local recreation centers, Community sports leagues, School sports teams, Private sports clubs, Other (please specify))
	+ This question aims to gather information on the types of programs available in your area.
* **How often do you participate in sports activities in your free time?** (Every day, Several times a week, About once a week, Less than once a week, Rarely)
	+ This question seeks to understand your level of engagement with sports activities.
* **What is your favorite type of sport or physical activity?** (Team sports, Individual sports, Dance or movement classes, Fitness or exercise classes)
	+ This question helps identify your preferred activities.
* **How long have you been participating in sports activities?** (Less than 1 year, 1-2 years, 2-5 years, 5-10 years, More than 10 years)
	+ This question determines your level of experience with sports activities.
* **How would you rate the overall quality of sports facilities and equipment in your area?** (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor)
	+ This question assesses your satisfaction with the infrastructure supporting sports activities.
* **How often do you engage in physical activity for mental health benefits?** (Every day, Several times a week, About once a week, Less than once a week, Rarely)
	+ This question explores your physical activity habits for mental health.
* **What motivates you to participate in sports activities?** (Please provide a brief explanation)
	+ This question seeks to understand your reasons for engaging in sports activities.
* **How much has your overall well-being improved since participating in sports activities?** (Much better, Slightly better, About the same, Slightly worse, Much worse)
	+ This question measures the impact on your well-being due to sports participation.
* **How much has your social connection improved since participating in sports activities?** (Much stronger, Slightly stronger, About the same, Slightly weaker, Much weaker)
	+ This question assesses how sports activities have affected your social relationships.
* **How much has your self-confidence improved since participating in sports activities?** (Much higher, Slightly higher, About the same, Slightly lower, Much lower)
	+ This question evaluates the effect of sports on your self-confidence.
* **What benefits have you gained from participating in sports activities?** (Please provide a brief description)
	+ This question seeks to gather information on the positive outcomes from sports participation.
* **Do you have any recommendations for improving sports programs in your area?** (Please provide a brief description)
	+ This question invites suggestions for enhancing sports programs in your area.

Note: Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability, as your feedback will contribute to the development of more effective youth sports programs.
