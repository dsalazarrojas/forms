# Athlete Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for athletes to register themselves for a sports-related program or facility. It collects essential details such as personal, medical, and payment information to create your athlete profile and determine your eligibility for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your full name in the "Athlete Full Name" field.
2.  Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field using the date format (dd/mm/yyyy).
3.  Select your gender from the "Gender" dropdown menu.
4.  Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
5.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
6.  Provide the name and contact information of your emergency contact in the corresponding fields.
7.  Select your primary sport from the "Primary Sport" dropdown menu.
8.  Choose your current competition level from the "Current Competition Level" dropdown menu.
9.  Select all the days you'd like to train on in the "Preferred Training Schedule" field.
10.  If you have any known medical conditions or allergies, list them in the "Known Medical Conditions" or "Known Allergies" fields.
11.  Consent to emergency medical treatment in the "I consent to emergency medical treatment if required" field.
12.  Choose your preferred payment method from the "Preferred Payment Method" dropdown menu.
13.  Select your membership type from the "Membership Type" dropdown menu.
14.  Sign the form electronically using your full name in the "Electronic Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Athlete Full Name** (Required): Your full name.
*   **Date of Birth** (Required): Enter your date of birth in the date format (dd/mm/yyyy).
*   **Gender** (Required): Select your gender from the dropdown menu.
*   **Email Address** (Required): Enter your email address.
*   **Phone Number** (Required): Enter your phone number.
*   **Emergency Contact Name** (Optional): Name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone** (Optional): Phone number of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
*   **Relationship to Athlete** (Optional): Relationship to the athlete (e.g., Parent, Spouse, Guardian).
*   **Primary Sport** (Required): Select your primary sport.
*   **Current Competition Level** (Required): Choose your current competition level.
*   **Preferred Training Schedule** (Required): Select all the days you'd like to train on.
*   **Known Medical Conditions** (Optional): List any known medical conditions.
*   **Known Allergies** (Optional): List any known allergies.
*   **I consent to emergency medical treatment if required** (Required): Select to consent to emergency medical treatment.
*   **Membership Type** (Required): Select your membership type.
*   **Preferred Payment Method** (Required): Choose your preferred payment method.
*   **I have read and agree to the Waiver of Liability** (Required): Select to agree to the waiver.
*   **Date of Registration** (Required): Enter the date of registration.
*   **Electronic Signature** (Required): Type your full name for electronic signature.
