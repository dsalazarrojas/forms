# Internal Championship Results Submission - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide explains how to complete the **Internal Championship Results Submission** form, which is used to report the results of internal championships or competitions within a company or organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the necessary fields with the required information. If you're unsure about any field, refer to the **Field-by-Field Explanation** section for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event Details (`event_details`, text, optional)
*   **Event Details**: Enter a brief description of the championship or competition, including the name, date, and location. This field is not required, but providing this information helps to provide context for the results.

### Competition Results (`competition_results`, text, optional)
*   **Competition Results**: Report the results of the championship or competition, including the outcomes, scores, or any other relevant information. This field is not required, but providing this information helps to record the performance of competitors.

### Competitor Details (`competitor_details`, text, optional)
*   **Competitor Details**: Enter any additional information about the competitors, such as their names, teams, or affiliations. This field is not required, but providing this information can help to provide context for the results.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and clear information in each field.
- If you're reporting results for multiple events or competitions, you can submit separate forms for each.
- If you're unsure about any field, feel free to contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx) for assistance.
