# Volleyball Recruitment Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us recruit new members for our volleyball club. We are looking for individuals, families, and teams interested in joining our club. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form and we will be in touch with more information on how to join us.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields (first name, last name, email, and membership type).
2. Make sure to enter your correct email address as we will use it to contact you.
3. Select the type of membership you are interested in (Individual, Family, or Team).
4. Review your answers and make sure they are accurate before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter your email address. This is how we will contact you with more information on how to join us.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Please enter a phone number where you can be reached.
* **Membership Type** (`member_type`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options:
	+ Individual: This is for one person who wants to join our club.
	+ Family: This is for multiple people living in the same household who want to join together.
	+ Team: This is for groups of people who want to join our club as a team.
