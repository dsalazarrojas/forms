# Fielding Skills Clinic Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fielding Skills Clinic Registration form is designed to collect information from players who are interested in attending the Fielding Skills Clinic. The form helps us to gather essential details and ensures that players are properly registered for the clinic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your **Player Details** (page 1):
  	* Enter your name and contact information.
2. Fill out your **Contact Details** (page 2):
  	* Provide your contact information to get in touch with you.
3. Select your **Waiver** (page 3):
  	* Choose one of the provided options regarding your attendance or waiver.
4. Optionally, fill out your **Player Information** (page 4):
  	* Add any additional details about yourself as a player.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Player Details** (Player Details, text, not required): Enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself as a player.
* **Contact Details** (Contact Details, text, not required): Provide your email address, phone number, or other contact information to help us get in touch with you.
* **Waiver** (Waiver, select_one, required): This is a required field where you must select one of the provided options. Please choose "Option 1" if you are willing to attend the clinic and understand the rules, "Option 2" if you are not able to attend, or "Option 3" for other reasons.
* **Player Information** (Player Information, text, not required): If you'd like to share more information about yourself, please fill out this section. This can include any relevant details that might help us better understand your needs and preferences as a player.

## Tips
* Please double-check your entries before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you select the correct waiver option.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
