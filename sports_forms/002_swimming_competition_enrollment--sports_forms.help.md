# Swimming Competition Enrollment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for participants to enroll in swimming competitions. It captures essential information about the swimmer, including their name, age, contact details, and event preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the participant information page, including your name, age, and contact details.
2. Select your preferred event (50m, 100m, or 200m) and swim style (Freestyle, Backstroke, Breaststroke, or Butterfly).
3. Choose your preferred event date and time.
4. Indicate whether you will be participating in multiple events.
5. Provide any additional comments or information about your participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Participant Info
* **participant info**: Enter your basic information, including your name and age.
* **swimmer name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **swimmer age**: Enter your age.
* **swimmer gender**: Select your gender (Male, Female, or Nonbinary).

### Contact Information
* **swimmer contact**: Enter your contact information (email or phone number).
* **swimmer email**: Enter your email address.
* **swimmer phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **swimmer address**: Enter your address.

### Event Selection
* **event**: Select the event you want to participate in (50m, 100m, or 200m).
* **swimmer event type**: Select the swim style you prefer (Freestyle, Backstroke, Breaststroke, or Butterfly).
* **event date**: Select the date you want to participate in.
* **event time**: Select the time of your event.
* **swimmer event day**: Select the day of your event (Monday, Tuesday, or Wednesday).

### Event Details
* **event entrants**: Indicate whether you are participating in multiple events (Yes, No, or Other).
* **event entrants 2**: Indicate whether you are participating in multiple events for this event (Yes, No, or Other).

### Additional Information
* **swimmer event date**: Enter the date of your event.
* **swimmer event time 2**: Enter the time of your event.
* **swimmer event time 3**: Enter the time of your event.
* **swimmer notes**: Enter any additional comments or information about your participation.
* **swimmer additional info**: Enter any additional information about your participation.
* **swimmer comments**: Enter any comments or feedback about your experience.
* **swimmer info 2**: Enter additional information about yourself.
