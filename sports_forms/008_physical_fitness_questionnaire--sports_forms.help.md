# Physical Fitness Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Physical Fitness Questionnaire is a self-reporting form designed to help track your physical fitness level over time. This form should be completed honestly, and it's recommended to fill it out on a weekly basis.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form at your leisure, but ideally on a weekly basis for tracking purposes.
- Answer the questions to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **physical_fitness_questionnaire (Page 1)** (`physical_fitness_questionnaire`, text, optional):
  This is a text box where you can enter your name, your fitness goal, or any additional information about your physical fitness journey.

* **section_1 (Page 2)** (`section_1`, select_multiple, optional):
  Select all that apply from the options provided. This section is designed to gather information about your current physical fitness level. Choose all the options that best describe your current state.

* **section_1_question_1 (Page 2)** (`section_1_question_1`, select_one, optional):
  Select the option that best describes your current level of physical fitness. This question is designed to help you gauge your current fitness level.

* **section_2 (Page 3)** (`section_2`, text, optional):
  Enter any relevant information about your physical fitness journey, such as your workout routine, diet, or any notable milestones.

* **section_2_question_1 (Page 3)** (`section_2_question_1`, select_multiple, optional):
  Select all that apply from the options provided. This question is designed to help you track your progress and identify areas of improvement.

* **section_3 (Page 4)** (`section_3`, text, optional):
  Enter any additional information about your physical fitness journey, such as your workout routine, diet, or any notable milestones.

* **section_3_question_1 (Page 4)** (`section_3_question_1`, select_one, optional):
  Select the option that best describes your current level of physical fitness. This question is designed to help you gauge your current fitness level.

* **note (Page 5)** (`note`, text, optional):
  Enter any additional information you'd like to note about your physical fitness journey.

* **email (Page 6)** (`email`, email, optional):
  Enter your email address if you'd like to receive any updates or follow-ups.

* **phone (Page 7)** (`phone`, text, optional):
  Enter your phone number if you'd like to receive any updates or follow-ups.

* **date (Page 8)** (`date`, date, optional):
  Enter the current date to track your progress over time.

* **time (Page 9)** (`time`, time, optional):
  Enter the current time to track your progress over time.

Note: This is a personal, non-medical form and should not be used as a substitute for professional medical advice. Always consult a medical professional if you have concerns about your physical fitness.
