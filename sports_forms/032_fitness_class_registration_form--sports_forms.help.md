# Fitness Class Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Class Registration Form is a tool used to collect essential information from participants who want to register for a fitness class. This form helps our staff get in touch with you and provide a tailored experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Registration Date** in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
2. Fill in your **Participant Name** as you would like it to appear on our records.
3. Enter your **Contact Email**, which we use to send you important updates and confirmations.
4. Provide your **Contact Phone Number**, so we can get in touch with you if needed.
5. Choose the **Program Name** you are interested in.
6. Select your **Participant Gender** from the provided options: **Female**, **Male**, or **Other**.
7. Once you have filled out all the necessary information, click the submit button to send your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Date**: Enter the date you would like to register for the fitness class.
* **Participant Name**: This is your name as we will use it to address you in our communication and in our records.
* **Contact Email**: Provide your email address so we can send you important updates and confirmations about your registration.
* **Contact Phone**: This is your phone number, which we will use to contact you if needed.
* **Program Name**: Choose the fitness program you are interested in registering for.
* **Participant Gender**: Select your gender to help us tailor our services to your needs.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you if necessary.
