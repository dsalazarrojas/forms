# runningClubMembershipForm - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect membership information from potential members of a running club. It helps the club's administration to verify the member's identity and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Member Information** page with your personal details.
2. Enter your **Name** to identify yourself as a member.
3. Provide your **Email Address** so we can contact you about upcoming events and news.
4. Enter your **Address** where you can be reached by the club's volunteers.
5. Enter your **Phone Number** in the international format (e.g., +123-456-7890).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Member Information**:
	+ **Member Information** (`member_info`, text, optional): This page serves as the starting point for the form, where you can provide any additional information about yourself that you think is relevant to your membership.
* **Name**:
	+ **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Please enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email**:
	+ **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you about club events and news.
* **Address**:
	+ **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address where you can be reached by the club's volunteers.
* **Phone**:
	+ **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number in the international format (e.g., +123-456-7890).
