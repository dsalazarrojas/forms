# Player Endorsement Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for player endorsement and consent purposes. It collects personal and contact information from players and their guardians to ensure their involvement in the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your personal information.
2. Choose your endorsement options for Endorsement 1 and Endorsement 2.
3. Provide your guardian's consent by selecting "I agree" in the Guardian Consent field.
4. Provide your own consent by selecting "I agree" in the Player Consent field.
5. Add any additional comments in the Additional Comments field.
6. Enter your email address in the Email field.
7. Enter your phone number in the Phone Number field.
8. Provide your emergency contact information, including their name, contact number, and address.
9. If you have any medical conditions, select "Yes" in the Medical Condition field and provide a description in the Medical Condition Description field.
10. Enter your player address in the Player Address field.
11. Enter your guardian's address in the Guardian Address field.
12. Enter your coach's information, including their name, phone number, and email address.
13. Enter the date and time you are available for participation.
