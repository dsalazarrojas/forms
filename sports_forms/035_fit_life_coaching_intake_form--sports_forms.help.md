# Fit Life Coaching Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients or individuals to help fit life coaches or professionals understand their background and provide guidance or services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out each field accurately and thoroughly. Ensure that you have permission from the emergency contact person before providing their information. If you are unsure about any field, please ask your fit life coach or professional for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name** (Text, Optional): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **email** (Email, Optional): Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **phone** (Text, Optional): Provide your phone number that we can use to contact you or your emergency contact.
* **goal** (Text, Optional): Share with us your goals and objectives that you want to achieve through our coaching services.
* **medical_condition** (Text, Optional): List any medical conditions you are currently managing.
* **medications** (Text, Optional): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **allergies** (Text, Optional): List any allergies you have.
* **emergency_contact** (Text, Optional): Provide the name and contact information of your emergency contact person.
* **emergency_phone** (Text, Optional): Provide the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **emergency_relationship** (Text, Optional): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact person.
* **emergency_permission** (Text, Optional): Confirm that you have permission from your emergency contact person to share their information with us.
* **medical_provider** (Text, Optional): Provide the name and contact information of your current medical provider.
* **medication_history** (Text, Optional): Share with us any significant events or history related to your medication use.
* **health_insurance** (Text, Optional): Share with us your current health insurance information.
* **injury_history** (Text, Optional): Share with us any significant events or history related to your injuries.
* **medical_notes** (Text, Optional): Share with us any relevant medical notes or observations.
* **medication_allergies** (Text, Optional): Share with us any medication allergies you have.
* **injury_notes** (Text, Optional): Share with us any injury notes or observations.
* **emergency_notes** (Text, Optional): Share with us any emergency notes or observations.
* **next_steps** (Text, Optional): Describe the next steps you would like to take with our coaching services.
* **medication_history_details** (Text, Optional): Share with us any significant events or history related to your medication use.
* **injury_history_details** (Text, Optional): Share with us any significant events or history related to your injuries.
* **health_insurance_details** (Text, Optional): Share with us your current health insurance information.
* **emergency_history_details** (Text, Optional): Share with us any emergency history details.

Note: Please ensure you have permission from your emergency contact person before providing their information. This form is for general guidance and information sharing purposes only, and all information shared will be kept confidential.
