# Youth Sports Coach Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from youth sports coaches to register and verify their identity, contact details, and coaching experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your first and last name.
2. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
3. Select the sport you coach.
4. Choose your coaching level (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced).
5. If applicable, provide your coaching experience in years.
6. Provide information about your emergency contact, including their name, relationship, and contact details.
7. Specify your coach license status and license number (if applicable).
8. Add any additional comments or information about your coaching experience.
9. If applicable, select the level of relationship for your emergency contact.
10. Select a date for your coaching experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address to receive notifications and updates from us.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Sport** (`sport`, select_one, required): Choose the sport you coach (e.g., Football, Basketball, etc.).
* **Coaching Level** (`coaching_level`, select_one, required): Select your coaching level (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced).
* **Coaching Experience** (`experience_years`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the number of years you have coached.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): Specify your relationship with your emergency contact (e.g., parent, spouse, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Level** (`emergency_contact_relationship_level`, select_multiple, optional): Select the level of relationship for your emergency contact (e.g., Parent, Spouse, etc.).
* **Coach License Status** (`coach_license_status`, select_one, required): Choose whether you have a coach license (True, False, or Pending).
* **Coach License Number** (`coach_license_number`, text, optional): Enter your coach license number (if applicable).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information about your coaching experience.
* **Coaching Experience** (`coach_experience`, date, optional): If applicable, select a date for your coaching experience.
* **Coach Education** (`coach_education`, text, optional): Enter any relevant education or certifications you have as a coach.
