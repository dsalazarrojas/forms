# Gym Class Participation Motivation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gym Class Participation Motivation Survey is a survey designed to gather information about your motivation and preferences regarding gym class participation. This form is intended for students or staff members to provide their honest answers and contribute to the development of programs and resources that meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Select your response to each question using the provided options.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. You can complete this form multiple times, as the questions are not time-sensitive.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your instructor or administrator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Gym Class Participation** (`gym_class_participation`, Select Multiple, required: false):
This question asks whether you participate or don't participate in gym class. Please select all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No

* **Motivation** (`motivation`, Number, required: false):
This question asks about your motivation or reason for participating or not participating in gym class. Please answer with a number that represents your level of motivation (e.g., 1-10).

* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, Number, required: false):
This question asks about your level of satisfaction with gym class. Please answer with a number that represents your level of satisfaction (e.g., 1-10).

* **Attendace and Frequency** (`attendance`, Date, required: false):
This question asks about your gym class attendance and frequency. Please enter a date or dates when you attend gym class.
