# COVID 19 Financial Assistance Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "COVID 19 Financial Assistance Application Form" is a tool designed to aid individuals who have been affected by the COVID-19 pandemic and need financial assistance. This form will help us understand their situation and provide the necessary support to alleviate their financial distress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal details, including your full name, date of birth, email address, and phone number.
2. Enter your address, including your street address, city, state, and zip code.
3. Share your professional background, highlighting your professional title, years of experience, and primary source of employment.
4. Describe how COVID-19 has impacted your employment and your current annual income.
5. Specify your financial needs, including your annual income before COVID-19, current annual income, percentage of income lost, and the amount of assistance requested.
6. Explain how you plan to use the assistance, such as for living expenses, rent, or medical bills.
7. If applicable, mention any other forms of assistance you've received, like unemployment benefits or grants.
8. Describe your financial hardship, explaining how COVID-19 has affected your financial situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`applicant_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email Address** (`applicant_email`, `email`, required): Provide your valid email address for communication and verification purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`applicant_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your active phone number where we can reach you.
* **Street Address** (`street_address`, `text`, required): Enter your street address where you reside.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter the state where you reside.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter your postal zip code.
* **Professional Title** (`profession`, `text`, required): Describe your professional title or job description.
* **Years in Profession** (`years_experience`, `number`, required): Enter the number of years you've been in your profession.
* **Primary Source of Employment** (`primary_employment`, `text`, required): Specify your primary source of employment.
* **Annual Income Before COVID-19** (`income_before_covid`, `number`, required): Enter your annual income before the COVID-19 pandemic.
* **Current Annual Income** (`current_annual_income`, `number`, required): Enter your current annual income.
* **Percentage of Income Lost** (`income_loss_percentage`, `number`, required): Describe the percentage of your income lost due to COVID-19.
* **How COVID-19 Impacted Your Employment** (`impact_details`, `text`, required): Describe how COVID-19 has affected your employment.
* **Amount of Assistance Requested** (`assistance_amount`, `number`, required): Specify the amount of financial assistance you need.
* **Purpose of Assistance** (`assistance_purpose`, `text`, required): Explain how you plan to use the financial assistance.
* **Other Assistance Received** (`other_assistance_received`, `text`, optional): If applicable, mention any other forms of assistance you've received.
* **Describe Your Financial Hardship** (`financial_hardship`, `text`, required): Describe your financial hardship due to COVID-19.
* **Application Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you're submitting this application.
