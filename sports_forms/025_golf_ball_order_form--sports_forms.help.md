# Golf Ball Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Golf Ball Order Form is a tool used to gather information from customers for their golf ball orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer name.
2. Choose the date for your order.
3. Select the type of ball you want to order.
4. Enter the number of balls you need.
5. Provide your contact number.
6. Enter your email address.
7. Enter your physical address.
8. Select your payment method.
9. Enter your payment details.
10. Provide any order instructions or special requests.
11. Confirm that the information is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, **Required**): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, **Required**): Select the date for your order using the calendar.
* **Ball Type** (`ball_type`, select_one, **Required**): Choose the type of ball you wish to order.
* **Ball Count** (`ball_count`, number, **Required**): Enter the number of balls you need to purchase.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, **Required**): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, **Required**): Enter your email address.
* **Address** (`address`, note, **Required**): Enter your physical address.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, **Required**): Choose your payment method (e.g., credit card, bank transfer, etc.).
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, **Required**): Enter your payment information (e.g., account number, card number, etc.).
* **Order Instructions** (`order_instructions`, text, **Required**): Provide any special instructions for your order.
* **Confirmation** (`confirmation`, text, **Required**): Confirm that the information is correct and accurate.
* **Order Comments** (`order_comments`, text, **Optional**): Enter any additional comments about your order.
* **Order Confirmation** (`order_confirmation`, text, **Optional**): Enter any additional comments after placing your order.

Note: This form requires all fields to be filled out except for the "Order Comments" and "Order Confirmation" fields. Please ensure that all necessary information is provided to complete the form accurately and efficiently.
