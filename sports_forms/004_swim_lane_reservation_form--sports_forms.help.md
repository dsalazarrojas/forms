# Swim Lane Reservation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Swim Lane Reservation Form is used to record and manage swim lane reservations for a pool or aquatic facility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the swim lanes you wish to reserve. This field allows you to select the swim lanes you need for your reservation.
2. Choose the start and end times for your reservation.
3. Provide your swimmer's details, including their name, email, phone number, and other information as required (see below).
4. Enter any additional comments about the reservation.
5. Select the second swim lane reservation details if needed.
6. Enter any additional swimmer details for the second swimmer if necessary.
7. Enter any other swimmer details as requested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Swim Lanes**: Select the swim lanes you wish to reserve.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of your reservation.
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of your reservation.
* **Swimmer Name**: Enter the name of the swimmer who will be using the swim lanes.
* **Swimmer Email**: Enter the email address of the swimmer.
* **Swimmer Phone**: Enter the phone number of the swimmer.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the reservation.
* **Swim Lane 2**: Select the second swim lane reservation details.
* **Reservation Date**: Enter the reservation date.
* **Swimmer Name 2**: Enter the name of the second swimmer.
* **Swimmer Email 2**: Enter the email address of the second swimmer.
* **Swimmer Phone 2**: Enter the phone number of the second swimmer.
* **Swimmer Age**: Enter the age of the swimmer.
* **Swimmer Height**: Enter the height of the swimmer.
* **Swimmer Weight**: Enter the weight of the swimmer.
* **Swimmer Gender**: Enter the gender of the swimmer.
* **Swimmer Country**: Enter the country of the swimmer.
* **Swim Lane 2**: Select the second swim lane reservation details.
* **Reservation Date 2**: Enter the reservation date.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments about the reservation.
* **Swimmer Name 3**: Enter the name of the third swimmer.
* **Swimmer Email 3**: Enter the email address of the third swimmer.
* **Swimmer Phone 3**: Enter the phone number of the third swimmer.
* **Swimmer Age 2**: Enter the age of the second swimmer.
* **Swimmer Height 2**: Enter the height of the second swimmer.
* **Swimmer Weight 2**: Enter the weight of the second swimmer.
* **Swimmer Gender 2**: Enter the gender of the second swimmer.
* **Swimmer Country 2**: Enter the country of the second swimmer.
