# sports_injury_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sports injury report form is used to collect detailed information about sports injuries, allowing athletic teams and medical staff to track and analyze injuries in their athletes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the athlete's email address in the `athlete` field.
2. Select the type of injury (if known) in the `injury_type` field.
3. Enter the date of the injury in the `injury_date` field.
4. Describe the injury location using the `injury_location` field.
5. Provide a detailed description of the injury using the `injury_description` field.
6. Specify whether the injury occurred during training or competition using the `training_or_competition` field.
7. Indicate the level of medical attention required in the `medical_attention` field.
8. Note any treatment or care received for the injury in the `treatment` field.
9. Enter the date of any follow-up or follow-up appointments in the `follow_up` field.
10. Provide any additional information that may be relevant to the injury report in the `additional_info` field.

* **injury_date**: Enter the date on which the injury occurred. Format the date as per your organization's standards.
