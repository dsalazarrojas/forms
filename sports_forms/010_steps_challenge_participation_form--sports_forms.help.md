# Steps Challenge Participation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Steps Challenge Participation Form is used to collect participant information and challenge data for the Steps Challenge program.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out all the required fields, marked as *required*.
* Provide accurate and complete information in each field.
* Review your submission before hitting the *Submit* button.
