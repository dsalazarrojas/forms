# Swim Tryout Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Swim Tryout Form" is a tool used by coaches or staff to document and evaluate swim tryout tests. It collects data on swimmer performance and provides a consistent way to record results.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Swim Tryout Form Data" field with any relevant information you'd like to record about the swim tryout (e.g., notes, comments, or other context).
2. Select the "Swim Tryout Test Type" field to indicate whether the test is a tryout or not.
3. Enter the "Swim Tryout Test Date" field with the date of the test.
4. Enter the "Swim Tryout Test Time" field with the time of the test.
5. Enter a score in the "Swim Tryout Score" field, if applicable.
6. Choose the "Swim Tryout Result" field to indicate whether the swimmer passed, failed, or the result is unknown.
7. Add any comments or observations in the "Swim Tryout Comment" field.
8. If desired, include your signature in the "Swim Tryout Coach Signature" field.
