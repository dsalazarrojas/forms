# Futsal Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Futsal Registration Form is used to collect information from teams and players participating in the Futsal tournament. This form is designed to gather details such as team and player information, event details, and contact person's information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required information on each page:
   * Team Details
   * Player Details
   * Contact Info
   * Event Details
   * Team Info
   * Event Info

The following fields are optional and can be filled out based on your preference:

* Player Info
* Contact Person
* Player (Yes/No)
* Team (Yes/No)
* Date

Please ensure that you fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to avoid any delays or errors in processing your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Details** (Team Details, Text, required): Enter your team's name, abbreviation, and description.
* **Player Details** (Player Details, Text, required): Enter each player's name, position, and date of birth.
* **Contact Info** (Contact Info, Text, required): Enter your name, email, and phone number.
* **Event Details** (Event Details, Text, required): Enter the event title, date, and location.
* **Team Info** (Team Info, Text, optional): Enter any additional team information you would like to provide.
* **Event Info** (Event Info, Text, optional): Enter any additional event information you would like to provide.
* **Player Info** (Player Info, Text, optional): Enter any additional player information you would like to provide.
* **Contact Person** (Contact Person, Text, optional): Enter your name and role in the event.
* **Player** (Player, Select One, optional): Choose "Yes" if you will be bringing players, "No" otherwise.
* **Team** (Team, Select One, optional): Choose "Yes" if you will be bringing a team, "No" otherwise.
* **Date** (Date, Date, optional): Enter the date you will be attending the event.

Note: Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to avoid any issues with your registration.
