# rugby_football_club_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a new rugby football club member. It's essential to fill it out accurately and completely to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by entering your general information in the first field.
2. Provide your contact information, including your email and phone number, in the second field.
3. Enter your emergency contact information, such as their name and relationship to you, in the third field.
4. In the fourth field, provide details about your membership application.
5. Read and agree to the terms and conditions in the fifth field.
6. In the last field, provide any additional comments or notes about your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Info** (`general_info`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to collect general information about the member.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact information, including your email and phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required: false): Provide the name and relationship of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, required: false): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Membership Application** (`membership_application`, `text`, required: false): Enter details about your membership application.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, `text`, required: false): Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the club.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required: false): Click this button to submit your registration.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes about your registration.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Select the date of the event or membership start date.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Select the time of the event or membership start time.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose one option from the list.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose multiple options from the list.
* **Note Text** (`note_text`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes about your registration.

## Tips
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* Make sure to agree to the terms and conditions.
* Be as thorough as possible when providing your contact and emergency contact information.
* If you have any additional comments or notes, please include them in the "Notes" field.
* If you're unsure about any part of the form, don't hesitate to contact the club administration.
