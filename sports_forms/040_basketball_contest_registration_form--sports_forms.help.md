# Basketball Contest Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Basketball Contest Registration Form is a form designed to gather information from basketball teams to register for a basketball tournament. This form is typically used by teams to provide their team details, including team name, contact information, division, and payment method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Select the correct division and age bracket for your team.
3. Choose the payment method for the entry fee.
4. Agree to the tournament rules and regulations.
5. Confirm that all information is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team name** (`team_name`, text, required): Enter the official name of your team, including the team name and any additional identifying information such as "Team Name - Team Number".
* **Team code or number** (`team_code`, text, optional): Enter a unique code or number assigned to your team.
* **Home city** (`home_city`, text, required): Enter the city where your team is based.
* **Coach name** (`coach_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the coach or team representative.
* **Coach email** (`coach_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the coach or team representative.
* **Coach phone** (`coach_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the coach or team representative.
* **Division** (`division`, select_one, required): Select the division your team belongs to (Youth, High School, College, Amateur, or Professional).
* **Age bracket** (`age_bracket`, select_one, optional): Select the age bracket of the players on your team (Under 12, 12-15, 16-18, 19-30, 30-40, or 40+).
* **Team skill level** (`skill_level`, select_one, required): Select the skill level of your team (Recreational, Competitive, or Elite).
* **Tournament name** (`tournament_name`, text, required): Enter the official name of the tournament.
* **Tournament date** (`tournament_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the tournament.
* **Tournament location** (`tournament_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the tournament.
* **Number of players on roster** (`number_of_players`, number, required): Enter the total number of players on your team's roster.
* **Entry fee amount** (`entry_fee`, number, optional): Enter the amount you are willing to pay as an entry fee.
* **Payment method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select the payment method you will use to pay the entry fee (Credit Card, Check, Cash, Bank Transfer, or PayPal).
* **Agree to tournament rules and regulations** (`rules_agreement`, select_one, required): Select "I Agree" to confirm you have read and understood the tournament rules and regulations.
* **Acknowledge and accept liability waiver** (`liability_agreement`, select_one, required): Select "I Agree" to confirm you have read and understood the liability waiver.
* **Confirm all information is accurate** (`registration_confirmation`, select_one, required): Select "I Confirm" to confirm that all information is accurate and complete.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter the correct division and age bracket for your team.
* Double-check the accuracy of all information before confirming.
* If you are unsure about any field or option, contact the tournament organizers for clarification.
