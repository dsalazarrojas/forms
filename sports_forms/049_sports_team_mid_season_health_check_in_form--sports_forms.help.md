# Sports Team Mid Season Health Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help teams monitor and assess the health and well-being of their players during the mid-season period. It gathers essential information about player injuries, medical conditions, and treatment plans, ensuring that your team's athlete's health is taken care of.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Make sure to answer all required fields.
3. Review and update your answers as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Name** (`player_name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the player you are filling out the form for.
* **Player Date Of Birth** (`player_date_of_birth`, Date, Required): Enter the player's date of birth.
* **Injury Details** (`injury_details`, Text, Required): Describe any injuries or medical conditions the player is experiencing.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, Text, Optional): List any ongoing medical conditions the player has.
* **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`, Text, Optional): Outline any planned treatments or therapies for the player's condition.
* **Concussion Symptoms** (`concussion_symptoms`, Text, Optional): Describe any symptoms of a possible concussion the player is experiencing.
* **Player Position** (`player_position`, Text, Required): Specify the player's position on the team.
* **Coach Concerns** (`coach_concerns`, Text, Optional): List any concerns or issues you have regarding the player's health or well-being.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the team the player belongs to.
* **Player Height** (`player_height`, Number, Required): Enter the player's height.
* **Player Weight** (`player_weight`, Number, Required): Enter the player's weight.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter the player's email address (if applicable).
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Optional): Enter the player's phone number (if applicable).
* **Player Grade** (`player_grade`, Number, Required): Enter the player's grade or performance level.
* **Game Injury** (`game_injury`, Number, Optional): Enter the number of games the player has missed due to injury.
* **Game Suspension** (`game_suspension`, Number, Optional): Enter the number of games the player has been suspended from.
* **Game Suspension Ending Date** (`game_suspension_ending_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date the suspension will end.
* **Game Suspension Ending Date Start Date** (`game_suspension_ending_date_start_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the start date of the suspension period.
* **Game Suspension Start Date** (`game_suspension_start_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the start date of the suspension period.
* **Game Suspension Ending Date** (`game_suspension_end_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the end date of the suspension period.
* **Concussion Protocol Completed** (`concussion_protocol_completed`, Select One, Required): Indicate whether the concussion protocol has been completed or not.
* **Concussion Protocol Status** (`concussion_protocol_status`, Select Multiple, Required): Select the current status of the concussion protocol (Not Started, In Progress, Completed).
* **Medical Provider** (`medical_provider`, Text, Optional): Enter the name and contact information of the medical provider treating the player.
* **Relationship With Medical Provider** (`medical_provider_relationship`, Select Multiple, Optional): Specify the relationship with the medical provider (Physician, Trainer, Other).
* **Follow Up Plan** (`follow_up_plan`, Text, Optional): Outline any planned follow-up appointments or meetings with the player or medical provider.
