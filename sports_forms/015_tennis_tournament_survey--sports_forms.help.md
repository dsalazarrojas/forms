# Tennis Tournament Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants in a tennis tournament. The goal is to gather data such as names, email addresses, tournament names, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form in its entirety, ensuring all required fields are filled in.
* Enter your first and last name in the first two fields.
* Enter your email address in the third field. This is optional, but recommended for communication purposes.
* Enter the name of the tournament in the fourth field.
* If you are aware of the date and time of the tournament, you can enter this information in the fifth and sixth fields, respectively.
* In the final field, provide any additional comments or information you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address. If you do not have an email address, you can leave this field blank.
* **Tournament Name** (`tournament_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the tournament you are participating in.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the tournament. If you do not know the date, you can leave this field blank.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the tournament. If you do not know the time, you can leave this field blank.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required): Enter any additional comments or information about the tournament.
