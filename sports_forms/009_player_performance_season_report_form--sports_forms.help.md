# Player Performance Season Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Player Performance Season Report Form" is a tool to collect data on a player's performance during a specific season of a sport-related activity, event, or competition. This form will help coaches, trainers, or administrators evaluate a player's progress, identify areas of improvement, and provide feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the player whose performance you are reporting on and enter their name in the "Player Name" field.
2. Enter the name of the team the player belongs to in the "Team Name" field.
3. Select the season being evaluated from the date field "Season".
4. For each question, select the relevant answer based on the player's performance:
   * For "Evaluation", choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the player met the expected standards.
   * For "Position", select one or more positions the player played during the season.
   * For "Performance Level", choose "High" or "Low" based on the player's overall performance.
5. Enter any goals scored by the player in the "Goals Scored" field.
6. Enter any assists received by the player in the "Assists Received" field.
7. Provide any additional comments about the player's performance in the "Player Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Player Name** (`player_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the player being evaluated.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the team the player belongs to.
* **Season** (`season`, date, required: false): Select the season being evaluated.
* **Evaluation** (`evaluation`, select_one, required: false): Check "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the player met the expected standards.
* **Position** (`position`, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or more positions the player played during the season.
* **Performance Level** (`performance_level`, select_one, required: false): Choose "High" or "Low" based on the player's overall performance.
* **Goals Scored** (`goals_scored`, number, required: false): Enter any goals scored by the player.
* **Assists Received** (`assists_received`, number, required: false): Enter any assists received by the player.
* **Player Comments** (`player_comments`, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments about the player's performance.
