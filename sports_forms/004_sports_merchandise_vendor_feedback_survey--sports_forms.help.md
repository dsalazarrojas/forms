# Sports Merchandise Vendor Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback on your experience with our sports merchandise sales and events. Your input will help us improve our services and better serve you and other vendors in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have the correct event ID, product ID, and vendor ID related to your experience.
2. Fill in all required fields (marked with *) and any additional comments you'd like to share.
3. Answer the rating questions using the provided options (e.g., Very Satisfied, Somewhat Likely, etc.).
4. Provide detailed answers in the text fields for each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Id** (1, number, required): Enter the ID of the event you participated in.
* **Product Id** (2, number, required): Enter the ID of the product you were selling or promoting.
* **Vendor Id** (3, number, required): Enter your vendor ID to help us track your feedback.
* **Name** (4, text, optional): Enter your name or alias for anonymity.
* **Rating** (5, number, optional): Rate your experience out of 5 stars. (1-5)
* **Comment** (6, text, optional): Provide any additional comments about your experience.
* **Email** (7, email, optional): Enter your email for any follow-up questions or concerns.
* **Phone** (8, text, optional): Enter your phone number for any follow-up calls.
* **Rating Satisfied** (9, select_one, required): Rate how satisfied you were with your overall experience (Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
* **Rating Recommended** (10, select_one, required): Rate how likely you are to recommend our services (Very Likely, Somewhat Likely, Neutral, Somewhat Unlikely, Very Unlikely).
* **Would Recommend Again** (11, select_one, required): Rate how likely you are to recommend us again (Very Likely, Somewhat Likely, Neutral, Somewhat Unlikely, Very Unlikely).
* **Overall Experience** (12, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your overall experience.
* **Feedback** (13, text, optional): Share any additional feedback or comments about your experience.
* **Additional Comments** (14, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you'd like to share.
* **Event Date** (15, date, optional): Enter the date of the event you participated in (if known).
* **Event Time** (16, time, optional): Enter the time of the event you participated in (if known).
* **Event Location** (17, text, optional): Enter the location of the event you participated in (if known).
* **Vendor Rating** (18, select_one, required): Rate our service quality (1, 2, 3, 4, 5).
* **Vendor Recommendation** (19, select_one, required): Would you recommend us to other vendors (True or False).
* **Vendor Satisfaction** (20, select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction with our vendor services (Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
* **Vendor Quality** (21, text, optional): Provide feedback on the quality of the merchandise sold or promoted.
* **Vendor Service** (22, text, optional): Provide feedback on the service you received from our team.
* **Vendor Value** (23, text, optional): Provide feedback on the value of our services to you and other vendors.
* **Vendor Complaints** (24, text, optional): Provide any feedback on complaints or issues you experienced.
* **Vendor Remarks** (25, text, optional): Provide any additional remarks or comments about your experience.

Please note that all required fields are marked with *. We appreciate your time and feedback!
