# Sports Facility Accessibility Improvement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and suggestions from users on improving accessibility at sports facilities. Your input will help identify areas where the facility can be made more inclusive and enjoyable for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly, as your feedback is crucial in creating a better experience for all users.
- You can select multiple options for each question where applicable.
- Make sure to provide concrete improvement ideas or comments where possible.
- Don't hesitate to share your thoughts on any aspect of the sports facility.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Introduction
- **Introduction** (label: Introduction, type: note, required: false): This is a brief introduction to the survey, explaining its purpose and importance.

### Accessibility Challenges
- **Accessibility Challenges** (label: Accessibility Challenges, type: select_multiple, required: false): Choose all the challenges you've faced or observed at the sports facility that affect accessibility, such as:
  - Wheelchair accessibility: Issues related to mobility aids, pathways, or entrance/exit routes that make it difficult for people with physical impairments to navigate.
  - Sign language interpretation: Difficulty communicating with staff or other users due to a lack of sign language interpretation services.
  - Assistive technology: Issues with assistive technology such as audio description systems or screen readers that don't work properly.
  - Other (please specify): Any other challenges you've faced or observed that don't fit the above categories.

### Improvement Ideas
- **Improvement Ideas** (label: Improvement Ideas, type: text, required: false): Share your thoughts on how the sports facility can be improved to make it more accessible. Please be specific and provide concrete suggestions.

### Feedback
- **Feedback** (label: Feedback, type: select_one, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the sports facility's accessibility:
  - Very satisfied
  - Somewhat satisfied
  - Neutral
  - Somewhat dissatisfied
  - Very dissatisfied

### Comments
- **Comments** (label: Comments, type: text, required: false): If you have any additional thoughts or comments on the sports facility's accessibility, share them here.

## Tips
- Please take the survey seriously and provide constructive feedback.
- If you're unsure about any question, feel free to contact the facility administrators for guidance.
- Your input will directly contribute to creating a more accessible sports environment.
