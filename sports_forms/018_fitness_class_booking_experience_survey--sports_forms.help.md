# Fitness Class Booking Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about their experience with a fitness class booking system.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select all that apply for each question by clicking on the corresponding checkbox or select option.
2. Please answer each question truthfully, as we value your honest feedback.
3. Do not hesitate to provide as much detail as you like in any of the open-ended fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Class Booking** (`class_booking`, `select_multiple`, required): Check all classes you have booked with us. If none, select 'No'.
* **Instructor Rating** (`instructor_rating`, `number`, required): Rate the quality of our instructors from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Facility Rating** (`facility_rating`, `number`, required): Rate the quality of our facilities from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required): Share any feedback or observations you have about your experience with us.
* **Trainer Rating** (`trainer_rating`, `number`, required): Rate the quality of our trainers from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Class Booking Experience** (`class_booking_experience`, `select_one`, required): Have you had a positive experience booking classes with us? Select 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Class Booking Difficulty** (`class_booking_difficulty`, `select_multiple`, required): How difficult was it to book a class with us? Select all that apply.
* **Class Booking Frequency** (`class_booking_frequency`, `select_one`, required): How often do you book classes with us? Select 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Instructor Feedback** (`instructor_feedback`, `select_multiple`, required): Were you satisfied with the instructor's feedback during your workout? Select all that apply.
* **Facility Feedback** (`facility_feedback`, `select_multiple`, required): Were you satisfied with the facilities provided during your workout? Select all that apply.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, `select_one`, required): Were you satisfied with your overall experience at our fitness center? Select 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Class Scheduling** (`class_scheduling`, `select_one`, required): Were the class schedules provided accurate and reliable? Select 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Class Length** (`class_length`, `select_one`, required): Were the class lengths suitable for your needs? Select 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Class Intensity** (`class_intensity`, `select_one`, required): Was the class intensity suitable for your needs? Select 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Instructor Quality** (`instructor_quality`, `select_multiple`, required): How satisfied were you with the instructor's quality during your workout? Select all that apply.
* **Facility Quality** (`facility_quality`, `select_multiple`, required): How satisfied were you with the facility's quality during your workout? Select all that apply.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Were you satisfied with your overall satisfaction with our services? Select 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Trainer Scheduling Availability** (`trainer_scheduling_availability`, `select_multiple`, required): Were the trainer's scheduling availability suitable for your needs? Select all that apply.
* **Class Booking Availability** (`class_booking_availability`, `select_multiple`, required): Were the class booking availability suitable for your needs? Select all that apply.
* **Facility Availability** (`facility_availability`, `select_multiple`, required): Were the facility availability suitable for your needs? Select all that apply.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, `number`, optional): Rate your overall experience with us from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Class Booking Satisfaction** (`class_booking_satisfaction`, `select_multiple`, optional): How satisfied were you with the satisfaction of booking classes with us? Select all that apply.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `note`, optional): Share any additional feedback or suggestions you have for us.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Provide your email address if you'd like us to contact you back.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Provide your phone number if you'd like us to contact you back.

## Tips
* Please fill out all required fields truthfully and thoroughly.
* If you have any additional feedback or suggestions, please use the 'Feedback' field.
* If you'd like us to contact you back, please provide your email or phone number in the corresponding fields.
