# sports_event_parking_satisfaction_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sports_event_parking_satisfaction_survey is designed to collect feedback from attendees of sports events about their parking experiences. It aims to gather opinions on the quality and effectiveness of the event parking services, allowing organizers to improve and optimize their parking management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by reading the introduction**: This is a free-form text where you can provide any comments or thoughts about your parking experience at the event.
2. **Fill in your name**: Provide your name as you would like it to be displayed in any follow-up communication regarding your feedback.
3. **Enter your email (optional)**: If you'd like to receive responses or updates from the event organizers, you can provide your email address here. However, please note that this field is optional.
4. **Add your phone number (optional)**: If you prefer to be contacted via phone, you can provide your number here. Again, this is optional.
5. **Submit your feedback**: Once you've filled all the required fields, click the submit button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: A free-form text where you can provide any comments or thoughts about your parking experience at the event.
* **Name**: A required field where you can provide your name as you'd like it to be displayed in any follow-up communication.
* **Email**: An optional field for your email address if you'd like to receive updates or responses from the event organizers.
* **Phone**: An optional field for your phone number if you prefer to be contacted via phone.

## Tips
- Please be as specific as possible with your feedback to help the event organizers improve their services.
- If you have any photos or videos that can support your feedback, consider including links to them.
- You can also use this form to ask questions or request assistance related to the parking services.
- Remember, all fields are optional, and you can choose to provide as much or as little information as you feel comfortable with.
