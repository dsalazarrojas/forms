# Player Registration And Equipment Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect player information for registration and equipment ordering purposes. Please fill out the form accurately and completely for a smooth process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully, ensuring that all required fields are completed.
2. Review the form before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (1) 
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your personal name (e.g., John).
### Last Name (2) 
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name (e.g., Doe).
### Equipment Type (3)
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the type of equipment you need or want to order (e.g., Ball, Net, etc.).
### Equipment Size (4)
* **Equipment Size** (`equipment_size`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the size of the equipment you need or want to order (e.g., Small, Medium, Large).
### Payment Method (5)
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Select the payment method you intend to use for payment (e.g., Credit Card, Check).
### Payment Amount (6)
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the amount you wish to pay for the equipment.
### Date of Birth (7)
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth (MM/DD/YYYY).
### Phone (8)
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number (e.g., 123-456-7890).
### Email (9)
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address (e.g., john.doe@example.com).
### Emergency Contact (10)
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, optional): Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact person (if applicable).
### Accept Terms (11)
* **Accept Terms** (`accept_terms`, `text`, required): Type 'Yes' to accept the terms and conditions.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant personnel.
