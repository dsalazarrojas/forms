# Fitness Center Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow users to contact the Fitness Center with their inquiry or feedback. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required information: First Name, Last Name, and Email (if provided).
2. Fill in any additional information in the Phone field.
3. Type in your message in the Message field.
4. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required:false): This field is an optional field where you can enter your first name if you want to be recognized by name in the Fitness Center's response.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required:false): This field is an optional field where you can enter your last name if you want to be recognized by name in the Fitness Center's response.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, required:false): This field is an optional field where you can enter your email address if you want to be contacted via email by the Fitness Center.
* **Phone** (`4`, `text`, required:false): This field is an optional field where you can enter your phone number if you want to be contacted via phone by the Fitness Center.
* **Message** (`5`, `text`, required:false): This field is a text box where you can type in your message or inquiry to the Fitness Center.
