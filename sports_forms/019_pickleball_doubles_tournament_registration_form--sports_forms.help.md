# Pickleball Doubles Tournament Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pickleball Doubles Tournament Registration Form is designed to collect team and player information for the Pickleball Doubles Tournament.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Team Name field with your team's name.
2. Enter the name of Player One in the Player One field.
3. Enter the name of Player Two in the Player Two field.
4. Enter your team's email address in the Team Email field.
5. Enter your team's phone number in the Phone Number field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your team, e.g. "Paddle Power".
* **Player One** (`player_one`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the first player on your team.
* **Player Two** (`player_two`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the second player on your team.
* **Team Email** (`team_email`, email, required: false): Enter your team's email address, which will be used for communication and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your team's phone number, which will be used for communication and updates.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form with accurate and complete information to ensure that your team is correctly registered for the tournament.
* If you are experiencing issues while filling out the form, please contact the tournament organizers for assistance.
