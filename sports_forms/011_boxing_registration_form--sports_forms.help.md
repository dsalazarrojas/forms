# Boxing Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a registration form for boxing participants, collecting essential information such as name, date of birth, weight class, emergency contact, medical conditions, club affiliation, and parental consent. It is designed to ensure accurate and complete participant data for safe and regulated competition.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as it appears on your official identification documents.
2. Enter your date of birth to confirm your age for competition purposes.
3. Select your current weight class in kilograms.
4. Provide a phone number of an emergency contact in case of an urgent situation.
5. If you have any known medical conditions or are taking medications, list them in this field.
6. If you are affiliated with a boxing club or gym, enter the name of your club.
7. If you are under 18 years old, confirm that you have obtained parental or guardian consent.
8. Confirm whether you have already paid the registration fee or not.
