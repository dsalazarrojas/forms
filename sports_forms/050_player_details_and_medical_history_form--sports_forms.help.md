# Player Details And Medical History Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect player details and medical history for sports-related events or activities. Please fill in the required information to provide a safe and healthy environment for everyone involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Player Name in the first field.
2. Enter your Date Of Birth in the correct format (dd/mm/yyyy).
3. Provide your Address in the second field.
4. Select any allergies you have from the list (Peanuts, Shellfish, Tree Nuts, or Soy).
5. Describe any medical conditions you may have in the third field.
6. List any medications you are currently taking in the fourth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Player Name** (1, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date Of Birth** (2, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (dd/mm/yyyy).
* **Address** (3, text, required): Provide your full address for contact and medical emergency purposes.
* **Allergies** (4, select_multiple, required): Check any allergies you have from the list:
	+ Peanuts
	+ Shellfish
	+ Tree Nuts
	+ Soy
* **Medical Conditions** (5, text, required): Describe any existing medical conditions you may have.
* **Medications** (6, text, required): List any medications you are currently taking.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill in all required fields.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask for assistance.
- If you have any additional medical information not listed here, please describe it in the "Medical Conditions" field.
