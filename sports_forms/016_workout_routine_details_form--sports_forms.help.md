# Workout Routine Details Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect detailed information about a user's workout routine.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* **Exercise Name** (`exercise_name`): Enter the name of the exercise you performed.
	* **Routine Name** (`routine_name`): Enter the name of the workout routine (e.g., "Legs Day" or "Chest and Triceps").
2. Fill in the optional fields:
	* **Exercise Description** (`exercise_description`): Provide a brief description of the exercise, including any specific instructions or details.
	* **Muscle Group** (`muscle_group`): Select the muscle group associated with the exercise (e.g., "Legs" or "Chest and Triceps").
	* **Duration** (`duration`): Enter the duration of the workout in minutes.
	* **Frequency** (`frequency`): Enter the frequency of the workout (e.g., "Daily", "3 times a week", etc.).
	* **Date** (`date`): Select the date of the workout.
	* **Time** (`time`): Select the time of the workout.
	* **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional notes or comments about the workout.
