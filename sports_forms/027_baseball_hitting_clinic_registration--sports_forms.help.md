## Purpose
The Baseball Hitting Clinic Registration form is designed to gather essential information from participants and their guardians for the purpose of registration and preparation for the clinic. This form helps us ensure that we have accurate and up-to-date information about each participant, which is crucial for a safe and enjoyable experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out your name, contact information, and other required details.
2. Provide information about your child's basic contact information, such as date of birth, parent/guardian name, and contact details.
3. Choose the correct clinic date and session that suits your child's preferences.
4. Select your child's age group, school name, and grade level (if applicable).
5. Answer questions about your child's baseball experience level, primary position, and batting hand.
6. Indicate which areas you would like to improve with the help of the clinic.
7. List any medical conditions or allergies your child may have.
8. Provide information about your child's current medications, insurance provider, and emergency contact details.

10. Indicate how you heard about the clinic.
