# Sports Covid 19 And Release Of Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to capture the release of liability for individuals who wish to participate in sports activities despite the risks associated with COVID-19.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the "Sportscovid19_and_releaseliabilitywaiver" page and fill in the required information.
2. Fill in your **name** in the "name" field.
3. Enter your **email** in the "email" field.
4. Fill in your **phone** number in the "phone" field.
5. Answer the question about **release_of_liability** by selecting the appropriate option.
6. In the **acknowledge_risks** field, please select one of the options that best reflects your current understanding of the risks associated with the COVID-19 vaccine.
7. In the **acknowledge_liability** field, select the option that best reflects your agreement to the release of liability terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sportscovid19_and_releaseliabilitywaiver**: A unique identifier for the form page.
* **name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **email**: Please enter your email address where you can be reached in case of an emergency or if further information is required.
* **phone**: Please enter your phone number where you can be reached in case of an emergency or if further information is required.
* **release_of_liability**: Please select "I agree to the release of liability terms." to acknowledge that you understand and agree to the terms of this form.
* **acknowledge_risks**: Please select one of the options to acknowledge your understanding of the risks associated with the COVID-19 vaccine. If you select "I have not read and do not understand the risks associated with the COVID-19 vaccine." we may not be able to allow you to participate in sports activities.
* **acknowledge_liability**: Please select "I agree to the release of liability terms." to acknowledge that you understand and agree to the terms of this form.
