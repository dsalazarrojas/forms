# Player Readiness Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Player Readiness Check In form is a tool used to determine a player's readiness to participate in a particular activity or event. It helps to ensure that players are accurately informed and prepared for their scheduled activities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Select your player ID from the dropdown menu.
*   Choose your availability status from the options provided.
*   If you are unavailable, provide a brief reason for your unavailability in the text field.
*   Indicate whether or not you will be participating in the activity by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' from the dropdown menu.
*   Enter the scheduled start time of the activity.
*   Enter the scheduled end time of the activity.
*   Indicate whether the activity will be held at your location or not.
*   Add any additional notes you'd like to include.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Player ID**: 
    *   <label>Player ID</label> (`player_id`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): The form requires you to select your player ID from a list of options. Please choose the correct ID corresponding to your player name.
*   **Availability Status**: 
    *   <label>Availability Status</label> (`availability_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field asks you to indicate your availability status for the activity. Choose 'Available' if you are available to participate, 'Unavailable' if you are not, and 'Late' if you will be late.
*   **Reason for Unavailability**: 
    *   <label>Reason for Unavailability</label> (`reason_for_unavailability`, `text`, required/optional): If you are unavailable, please provide a brief explanation for your unavailability in this text field.
*   **Activity Name**: 
    *   <label>Activity Name</label> (`activity_name`, `select_one`, required/optional): Indicate whether or not you will be participating in the activity by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' from the dropdown menu.
*   **Start Time**: 
    *   <label>Start Time</label> (`start_time`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the scheduled start time of the activity.
*   **End Time**: 
    *   <label>End Time</label> (`end_time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the scheduled end time of the activity.
*   **Location**: 
    *   <label>Location</label> (`location`, `select_one`, required/optional): Indicate whether the activity will be held at your location or not.
*   **Notes**: 
    *   <label>Notes</label> (`notes`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional notes or comments you'd like to include about the activity.
