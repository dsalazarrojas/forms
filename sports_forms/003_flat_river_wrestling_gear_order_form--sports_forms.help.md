## Flat River Wrestling Gear Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers who want to order wrestling gear. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure that your order is processed accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Customer Name** in the "customer name" field.
2. Enter your **Customer Email** in the "customer email" field.
3. Provide your **Shipping Address**, including city and **Zip Code**.
4. Select the **Order Items** you want to purchase from the list.
5. Specify the **Order Quantity** for each selected item.
6. Choose your preferred **Shipping Method** (USPS, FedEx, UPS, or Local Pickup).
7. Enter the **Shipping Cost** for your selected shipping method.
8. Enter the **Order Total** to ensure accuracy.
9. Add any **Order Comments** if necessary.
10. Select the **Order Date** and **Order Time** for tracking purposes.
11. If applicable, select the **Assigned Tool** and **Assigned User** for order fulfillment.
12. Finally, review your order and submit it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter your physical address where you want the order to be shipped.
* **City**: Enter the city where you reside.
* **Zip Code**: Enter your postal code for shipping purposes.
* **Order Items**: Select the wrestling gear items you want to purchase from the list.
* **Order Quantity**: Enter the quantity of each selected item.
* **Shipping Method**: Choose how you want your order shipped (USPS, FedEx, UPS, or Local Pickup).
* **Shipping Cost**: Enter the cost of shipping for your selected method.
* **Order Total**: Enter the total cost of your order.
* **Order Comments**: Add any comments or instructions related to your order.
* **Order Date**: Enter the date you want to place the order.
* **Order Time**: Enter the time you want to place the order.
* **Assigned Tool**: If you have a specific tool assigned to fulfill your order, enter it here.
* **Assigned User**: If you have a specific user assigned to fulfill your order, enter it here.
* **Created At**: Display only, this field is not editable.
* **Updated At**: Display only, this field is not editable.
* **Deleted At**: Display only, this field is not editable.

Note: This form will be submitted after all required fields are filled out. Review your order carefully before submission to avoid errors.
