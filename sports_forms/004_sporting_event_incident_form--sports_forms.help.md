# Sporting Event Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sporting Event Incident Form is used to report and document incidents that occur during sports events. This form helps to gather relevant information about the incident, including its details, location, and status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Incident Details
	* Incident Location
	* Incident Date
	* Incident Time
	* Incident Severity
	* Incident Status
	* Incident Resolution
	* Incident Reporter
2. Provide additional information (if applicable):
	* Incident Notes
	* Follow-up Information
	* Safety Improvements
	* Incident Recommendations
	* Incident Investigation Status
	* Incident Closed Date
	* Incident Closed Time
	* Incident Followers
	* Incident Investigators
	* Incident Victims
	* Incident Injuries
	* Incident Damages
	* Incident Investigation Notes
	* Incident Investigation Status

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Details** (Required): A brief description of the incident.
* **Incident Location** (Required): The location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Date** (Required): The date of the incident.
* **Incident Time** (Required): The time of the incident.
* **Incident Severity** (Required): Select from "Yes" or "No" to indicate the severity of the incident.
* **Incident Status** (Required): Select from "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate the status of the incident.
* **Incident Resolution** (Required): A description of the resolution or outcome of the incident.
* **Incident Reporter** (Required): The person reporting the incident.
* **Incident Notes** (Optional): Additional information or comments about the incident.
* **Follow-up Information** (Optional): Any follow-up information or updates about the incident.
* **Safety Improvements** (Optional): Suggestions for safety improvements related to the incident.
* **Incident Recommendations** (Optional): Recommendations for preventing similar incidents in the future.
* **Incident Investigation Status** (Optional): Select from "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate the status of the incident investigation.
* **Incident Closed Date** (Optional): The date the incident was closed.
* **Incident Closed Time** (Optional): The time the incident was closed.
* **Incident Followers** (Optional): A list of individuals who have been informed about the incident.
* **Incident Investigators** (Optional): A list of individuals involved in the investigation.
* **Incident Victims** (Optional): A list of individuals or groups affected by the incident.
* **Incident Injuries** (Optional): A description of any injuries sustained during the incident.
* **Incident Damages** (Optional): A description of any damages or losses resulting from the incident.
* **Incident Investigation Notes** (Optional): Additional information or notes about the investigation.
* **Incident Investigation Status** (Optional): Select from "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate the status of the incident investigation.
