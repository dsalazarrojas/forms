# Athlete Travel Grant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Athlete Travel Grant Application Form is designed to help athletes apply for financial support to participate in events or competitions. The form collects information about the athlete, the event, and the financial aspects of the trip, allowing the organization to assess the athlete's need and provide support accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Complete the athlete's personal details, including their full name, date of birth, primary sport, current competition level, and team or club affiliation.
2.  Enter the event or competition details, including the event name, location, dates, and primary purpose of attendance.
3.  Provide evidence of selection or eligibility for the event, such as a link to an official selection letter.
4.  Estimate the total cost of the trip, including all travel, lodging, and fees.
5.  Specify the grant amount requested and provide a detailed breakdown of expenses.
6.  If applicable, list any other sources of funding, such as grants or sponsorships.
7.  Explain why the grant is essential for your participation in the event.
8.  Describe how this event will support your long-term athletic development.
9.  If you have received previous travel grants, list them.
10.  Provide your email address, phone number, and certify that the costs and information provided are accurate.
11.  Finally, sign and date the application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Athlete Full Name** (`athlete_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID.
*   **Date of Birth** (`athlete_dob`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
*   **Primary Sport** (`primary_sport`, text, required): Choose your primary sport or discipline.
*   **Current Competition Level** (`competition_level`, text, required): Describe your current competition level, such as national, international, or professional.
*   **Team or Club Affiliation** (`team_affiliation`, text, required): Enter your team or club affiliation, if applicable.
*   **Event or Competition Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event or competition you are applying for.
*   **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required): Enter the city and country of the event or competition.
*   **Event Dates** (`event_dates`, text, required): Enter the dates of the event or competition in the format `mm/dd/yyyy` to `mm/dd/yyyy`.
*   **Primary Purpose of Attendance** (`attendance_purpose`, select_one, required): Choose the primary purpose of your attendance at the event or competition.
*   **Evidence of Selection or Eligibility** (`selection_evidence`, text, required): Describe your qualification or provide a link to the official selection letter.
*   **Total Estimated Cost of Trip** (`total_trip_cost`, number, required): Estimate the total cost of the trip, including all travel, lodging, and fees.
*   **Grant Amount Requested** (`grant_requested_amount`, number, required): Specify the amount of grant you are requesting.
*   **Detailed Breakdown of Expenses** (`expense_breakdown`, text, required): Provide a detailed breakdown of expenses, such as flights, accommodation, and fees.
*   **Other Sources of Funding** (`other_funding_sources`, text, optional): List any other grants, sponsorships, or personal contributions.
*   **Statement of Financial Need** (`financial_need_statement`, text, required): Explain why this grant is essential for your participation in the event.
*   **How will this event support your long-term athletic development?** (`athletic_development_impact`, text, required): Describe how this event will support your long-term athletic development.
*   **List any previous travel grants received in the last 2 years** (`previous_grants_received`, text, optional): If applicable, list any previous travel grants received in the last 2 years.
*   **Email Address** (`athlete_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
*   **Phone Number** (`athlete_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
*   **I certify that all estimated costs and information provided are accurate** (`accuracy_certification`, select_one, required): Choose to certify that all estimated costs and information provided are accurate.
*   **Athlete Electronic Signature** (`athlete_signature`, text, required): Type your full name as it appears on your ID.
*   **Parent or Guardian Electronic Signature** (`guardian_signature`, text, optional): If you are under 18, your parent or guardian must sign this field.
*   **Date of Application** (`application_date`, date, required): Enter the date you submitted this application.

## Tips

*   Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
*   Use the format `mm/dd/yyyy` for dates and `e.g., March 10 to 15, 2026` for event dates.
*   Attach any supporting documents, such as selection letters, to the application.
*   Be honest and transparent in your responses to ensure a smooth review process.
