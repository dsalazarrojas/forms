# Physical Activity Readiness Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate an individual's physical activity level and gather other relevant details such as age, height, weight, email, phone, and readiness for service. Completing this form will help you determine your suitability for physical activities and services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select the page you wish to complete from the form's available pages.
2.  For each field, select the appropriate value or input your information as required.
3.  Ensure that all required fields are filled in, if applicable.
4.  Review and submit your answers when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Customer Details (customer_details)** (`text`, required: false): This is a free text field where you can input any information you feel is relevant to your activity level or other details.
*   **Physical Activity Level (physical_activity_level)** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select the level that best describes your current physical activity level. Options include High, Low, and Medium.
*   **Age (age)** (`number`, required: false): Enter your age to help us better understand your overall health and fitness level.
*   **Height (height)** (`number`, required: false): Input your height in centimeters or meters.
*   **Weight (weight)** (`number`, required: false): Enter your weight in kilograms or pounds.
*   **Email (email)** (`email`, required: false): Input your email address.
*   **Phone (phone)** (`text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
*   **Ready for Service (service_ready)** (`select_one`, required: false): Select yes if you are ready for service, and no otherwise.

## Tips

*   Be as accurate as possible when inputting your information.
*   If you have any issues or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
*   Review your answers carefully before submitting your form.
