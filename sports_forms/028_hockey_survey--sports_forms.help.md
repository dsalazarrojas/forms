# Hockey Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to collect information about hockey enthusiasts' experiences and preferences, including their hockey experience, training habits, equipment preferences, game play frequency, team participation, practice frequency, feedback, contact information, and any additional notes they may want to provide.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer all the questions accurately and honestly, based on your experiences and preferences.
2. For questions with multiple-choice options, select the one that best represents your response.
3. If you have additional comments or observations, please use the "note" field to provide any additional information.
4. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Hockey Experience** (`hockey_experience`, text, optional): This field is where you can describe your level of involvement with hockey, e.g., player, coach, fan, etc.
* **Training Habits** (`training_habits`, text, optional): Please share any regular training or practice routines you follow.
* **Equipment Preferences** (`equipment_preferences`, text, optional): Describe any specialized equipment you prefer or use during hockey games or practice.
* **Game Played** (`game_played`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you have played hockey recently or in the past.
* **Team Participation** (`team_participation`, select_multiple, optional): Check the boxes of any teams you've played for or participated in.
* **Practice Frequency** (`practice_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate how often you engage in hockey-related practice or training.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share any thoughts, suggestions, or comments you have about the form or hockey-related topics.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or observations you'd like to share.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* If you're unsure about any question or have additional comments, use the "note" field.
* If you've already filled out this form, you can disregard this guide; the form is designed to be user-friendly.
