# Golf Team Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Golf Team Registration form is designed to gather information from golf teams and their representatives. This form is used to collect team details, player information, and financial data for the golf tournament.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the golf team's name in the "Golf Team Name" field.
2. Enter the names and handicaps of your team's players in the fields labeled "Player 1 Name", "Player 2 Name", "Player 3 Name", and "Player 4 Name". Each player's handicap should be entered in the corresponding "Player X Handicap" field.
3. For teams with more than four players, you can enter the number of players in your team by selecting the correct option in the "Player Count" field.
4. If you have additional tickets or financial information, you can enter that in the "Additional Tickets" field. This field is used to track any extra tickets or funds associated with your team.
5. Finally, enter your team's bank details, including the "Bank Account", "Bank IBAN", and "Bank BIC" fields. This information is essential for financial transactions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Golf Team Name** (1, text, required): Enter the name of your golf team.
* **Player 1 Name** (2, text, required): Enter the name of your first player.
* **Player 1 Handicap** (3, number, required): Enter your first player's handicap.
* **Player 2 Name** (4, text, required): Enter the name of your second player.
* **Player 2 Handicap** (5, number, required): Enter your second player's handicap.
* **Player 3 Name** (6, text, required): Enter the name of your third player.
* **Player 3 Handicap** (7, number, required): Enter your third player's handicap.
* **Player 4 Name** (8, text, required): Enter the name of your fourth player (if applicable).
* **Player 4 Handicap** (9, number, required): Enter your fourth player's handicap.
* **Additional Tickets** (10, number, optional): Enter any additional tickets you have or need for your team.
* **Player Count** (11, select_one, required): Select the correct number of players in your team from the options provided.
* **Bank Details** (12, text, required): Enter your team's bank details for financial transactions.
* **Host Name** (13, text, optional): Enter your team's host name (if applicable).
* **Bank Account** (14, text, required): Enter your team's bank account information.
* **Bank IBAN** (15, text, optional): Enter your team's bank IBAN (if applicable).
* **Bank BIC** (16, text, optional): Enter your team's bank BIC (if applicable).

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to avoid any delays or issues with financial transactions.
- If you're unsure about any of the fields, please contact our support team for assistance.
- This form is designed to be completed by the team representative or the team's manager.
