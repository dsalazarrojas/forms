# Girls Football Match Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from female football players and their teams for a girls' football match.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) and found on pages 1-2.
2. Enter your team name in the "team_name" field.
3. Select your team position using the "team_position" field.
4. Provide the match date in the "match_date" field, using the YYYY-MM-DD format.
5. Enter the match time in the "match_time" field, using the 24-hour clock format HH:MM.
6. Enter your player's name in the "player_name" field.
7. Enter your player's age in the "player_age" field.
8. Enter your player's height in the "player_height" field.
9. Enter your player's preferred position in the "player_position" field.
10. Enter your player's email address in the "email" field.
11. Enter your player's phone number in the "player_phone" field.
12. Select your team(s) using the "team" field.
13. Enter your coach's name in the "coach" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Player Information (Page 1)**
	+ **Player Name** (`player_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
	+ **Player Age** (`player_age`, number, required): Enter your age.
	+ **Player Height** (`player_height`, text, required): Enter your height.
	+ **Player Position** (`player_position`, text, required): Select your preferred position.
* **Team Information (Page 2)**
	+ **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required): Enter your team's name.
	+ **Team Position** (`team_position`, text, required): Select your team's position.
	+ **Match Date** (`match_date`, date, required): Enter the match date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
	+ **Match Time** (`match_time`, time, required): Enter the match time in 24-hour format HH:MM.
* **Contact Information**
	+ **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
	+ **Phone Number** (`player_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Team Selection**
	+ **Team** (`team`, select_multiple, required): Select your team(s) from the list.
* **Coach**
	+ **Coach** (`coach`, text, required): Enter your coach's name.
