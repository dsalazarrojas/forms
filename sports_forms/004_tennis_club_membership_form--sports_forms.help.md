# tennis_club_membership_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential members of a tennis club. It gathers data on member details, emergency contacts, payment information, and other relevant data for administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Member Details** section with your personal information.
2. Provide **Emergency Contacts** in case we need to reach out to you or your family members.
3. Enter **Payment Details** for your membership fees.
4. Sign the **Member Declaration** to agree to the club's terms and conditions.
5. Add **Tennis Club Details**, if applicable.
6. If you wish to add a signature for your membership, do so in the **Member Signature** field.
7. Provide **Staff Details**, if applicable.
8. Optionally, you can add **Payment Reminders** and **Payment History**.
9. Finally, enter **Tennis Club Admin Details** and **Membership Status** if applicable.
10. Select your preferred **Payment Method** and agree to the **Payment Terms**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Member Details** (Page 1)
	+ This is the first page of the form. Enter your name, email, phone number, and other relevant details to get started.
* **Emergency Contacts** (Page 2)
	+ Please provide the names, emails, and phone numbers of people who should be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Payment Details** (Page 3)
	+ Enter the payment method, amount, and any other relevant payment information.
* **Member Declaration** (Page 4)
	+ Read and agree to the tennis club's terms and conditions.
* **Tennis Club Details** (Page 5)
	+ If you are a member of a specific tennis club, enter the club's name and details.
* **Member Signature** (Page 6)
	+ If you wish to add a signature for your membership, do so here.
* **Staff Details** (Page 7)
	+ Provide details about any staff members you are working with.
* **Payment Reminders** (Page 8)
	+ Optionally, add any payment reminders you would like to receive.
* **Payment History** (Page 9)
	+ Enter any relevant payment history if applicable.
* **Tennis Club Admin Details** (Page 10)
	+ Enter any relevant details about the tennis club administration.
* **Membership Status** (Page 11)
	+ Indicate your membership status with the tennis club.
* **Payment Method** (Page 12)
	+ Select your preferred payment method.
* **Payment Terms** (Page 13)
	+ Agree to the payment terms and conditions of the tennis club.

Note: This form is for information purposes only and does not collect any sensitive or financial information.
