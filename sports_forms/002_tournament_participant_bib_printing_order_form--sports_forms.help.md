# tournament_participant_bib_printing_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Tournament Participant Bib Printing Order Form" is designed to collect information from participants in a sports tournament, ensuring that their bib details are accurate and printed correctly for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Participant Form" section with your name and contact information.
2. Provide your bib details in the "Bib Details" section, including any additional information that needs to be printed on your bib.
3. If necessary, specify your printing requirements for your bib in the "Printing Requirements" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Form**: 
  * `participant_form` (text, required): Enter your name and contact information to confirm your identity and provide a way to contact you.
* **Bib Details**:
  * `bib_form` (text, required): Enter any relevant details about your bib, such as your bib number, name, or any other information that needs to be printed on it.
* **Printing Requirements**:
  * `printing_requirements` (text, optional): If you have any special printing requirements, such as color or font preferences, enter them here.
