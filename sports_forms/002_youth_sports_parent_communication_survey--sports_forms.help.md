# Youth Sports Parent Communication Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from parents or guardians about their communication preferences and expectations with the school or league, as well as their feedback on the current level of communication.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select all the communication preferences and expectations you have for your child's school or league. For example, if you want to receive information about your child's progress, grades, and upcoming events, select "Yes".
2. Choose your preferred method of communication with the school or league (e.g., email, phone, text message).
3. Add any additional notes or comments about your communication preferences.
4. If you have any other comments or suggestions, please specify them in the "Other" field.
5. Provide your name as a parent or guardian.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Parent Communication Quality
* **Parent Communication Quality (1)**: This question assesses how you rate the quality of communication between you and your child's school or league. Please rate it on a scale from 1 (low) to 5 (high).

### Communication Preferences
* **Communication Preferences (2)**: What information would you like to receive from your child's school or league? Please select all that apply:
	+ Yes, I want to receive information about my child's progress and achievements.
	+ Yes, I want to receive information about upcoming events and schedules.
	+ Yes, I want to receive information about my child's grades and attendance.
	+ No, I don't want to receive any information.

### Communication Improvements
* **Communication Improvements (3)**: Do you think there is an area where the school or league could improve in communicating with you? Please select "Yes" or "No":
	+ Yes, I think there is an area where the school or league could improve in communicating with me.
	+ No, I don't think there is an area where the school or league could improve.

### Parent Communication Style
* **Parent Communication Style (4)**: How do you prefer to communicate with your child's school or league?
	+ Yes, I prefer to communicate via phone calls.
	+ Yes, I prefer to communicate via email.
	+ Yes, I prefer to communicate via text message.
	+ No, I don't have a preferred method of communication.

### Email
* **Email (5)**: Please enter your preferred email address where you would like to receive information from your child's school or league.

### Phone
* **Phone (6)**: Please enter your phone number where you would like to receive information from your child's school or league.

### Note
* **Note (7)**: Add any additional comments or suggestions for improving communication with your child's school or league.

### Other (please specify)
* **Other (8)**: If you have any other comments or suggestions that are not mentioned above, please specify them here.

### Parent Name
* **Parent Name (9)**: Please enter your name as a parent or guardian.

## Tips
* Make sure to select all that apply for your communication preferences.
* If you are unsure about any question, please don't hesitate to ask the school or league for clarification.
* Please enter your email address and phone number correctly to ensure you receive information from your child's school or league.
