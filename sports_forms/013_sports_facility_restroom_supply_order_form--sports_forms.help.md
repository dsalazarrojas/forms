# Sports Facility Restroom Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to enable sports facilities to order restrooms supplies quickly and efficiently. It captures essential information about the facility, the order details, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Facility Name in the "Facility Name" field.
2. Select the date you would like to order the supplies in the "Supply Order Date" field.
3. Choose the type of supplies you would like to order by selecting the corresponding option in the "Order Type" field.
4. Enter the quantity of each supply type you would like to order.
5. Add any additional comments or notes about your order in the "Order Comments" field.
6. Enter the contact number of the person responsible for the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, not required): Enter the title of your order. This can be a unique identifier for your order or a descriptive title.
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of your facility. This helps us to understand which facility is making the order.
* **Supply Order Date** (`supply_order_date`, date, not required): Select the date you would like to order the supplies. This will help us to check if the order has been created recently.
* **Order Type** (`order_type`, select_multiple, not required): Choose the type of supplies you would like to order. Select multiple options if you need different types of supplies.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, not required): Enter the quantity of each supply type you would like to order.
* **Order Comments** (`order_comments`, note, not required): Add any additional comments or notes about your order. This can be helpful for our customer service team to understand your needs.
* **Facility Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, not required): Enter the contact number of the person responsible for the order. This ensures that we can contact you if needed.

## Tips

* Double-check the "Supply Order Date" field to avoid creating duplicate orders.
* Be as specific as possible when selecting the "Order Type" options.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* We appreciate your feedback and suggestions to improve this form.
