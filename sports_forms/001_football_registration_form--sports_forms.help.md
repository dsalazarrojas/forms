# Football Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Football Registration Form is used to collect information about a player's personal and medical details, as well as team and contact information for registration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by filling out the Player Info section.
2. Provide the player's name and other personal details.
3. Enter the player's age and height in the respective fields.
4. Enter the emergency contact's name and phone number.
5. Select the medical clearance status for the player.
6. Provide additional medical clearance information if applicable.
7. Fill out the team information fields, including team name and coach details.
8. Review and sign the registration form to complete the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Name** (`player_name`, text, required): Enter the player's full name as it appears on their ID or passport.
* **Age** (`player_age`, number, required): Enter the player's age in years.
* **Height** (`player_height`, number, required): Enter the player's height in inches.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, required): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Medical Clearance 1** (`medical_clearance`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the player's medical clearance status.
* **Medical Clearance 2** (`medical_clearance_2`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the player's medical clearance status for another condition.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required): Enter the team's name.
* **Coach Info** (`coach_info`, text, required): Enter the coach's contact information.
* **Player Position** (`player_position`, text, required): Enter the player's position on the team.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Sign the form to complete the registration process.
* **Signature Info** (`signature_info`, text, required): Enter additional information about the signature.
* **Medical Clearance Info** (`medical_clearance_info`, text, required): Enter any additional medical clearance information.
