# Sports Team Participation Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from parents and guardians of athletes participating in sports teams. It ensures that the necessary permissions and medical information are obtained to guarantee the athlete's safety and well-being during team activities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each section carefully and provide detailed information for each field.
2. Complete the form in a quiet and private setting to ensure accuracy and confidentiality.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parental Consent** (`parent_consent`, text, required/optional): This field asks for the parent or guardian's signature indicating their consent for their child to participate in team activities. Please provide a detailed written statement indicating that you, as the parent or guardian, have reviewed and agreed to the terms and conditions of team participation.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required/optional): Provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person. This person will be responsible for picking up your child from the location where they are participating in team activities in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Insurance Information** (`insurance_info`, text, required/optional): If your child has any insurance coverage, provide details about the type and provider of the insurance.
* **Doctor Information** (`doctor_info`, text, required/optional): If your child has any ongoing medical conditions, provide the name and contact information of their doctor or medical professional.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, required/optional): List any known allergies that your child may have.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required/optional): If your child has any medical conditions that may impact their participation in team activities, provide a detailed description.
* **Medical Information** (`medical_info`, text, required/optional): If your child has any medical conditions that may impact their participation in team activities, provide a detailed description.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have provided accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the team manager or coach for guidance.
* Please review the terms and conditions of team participation before signing the form.
* This form must be completed for each athlete participating in the team.
