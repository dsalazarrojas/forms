# Racquetball Partner Finder Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Racquetball Partner Finder Form is a tool to connect you with potential racquetball partners, helping you find someone with similar playing interests and schedules.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select your interests by checking the boxes next to the options that apply to you.
2. Choose your availability by entering a date.
3. Indicate the number of racquetballs you have for playing.
4. Select whether you have a court available.
5. Choose a preferred time for playing.
6. Indicate how frequently you want to play.
7. Select a preferred day for playing.
8. Enter your start and end times for playing.
9. Add any additional notes about your playing style or preferences.
10. Provide your contact information, including email and phone number.
11. Enter your name and location.
12. Enter your partner's contact information, including email and phone number.
13. Enter your partner's name and location.
14. Enter any additional notes about your partner.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Partner Interests** (`Partner Interests`, Select Multiple, required): Select all the options that apply to your interests in playing racquetball.
* **Availability** (`Availability`, Date, required): Enter the date you are available to play.
* **Racquets** (`Racquets`, Number, required): Indicate how many racquetballs you have for playing.
* **Court Size** (`Court Size`, Select One, required): Check 'Yes' if you have access to a court.
* **Time Preference** (`Time Preference`, Time, required): Enter the time you prefer to play.
* **Frequency** (`Frequency`, Select One, required): Select how frequently you want to play.
* **Preferred Day** (`Preferred Day`, Select One, required): Choose a day you prefer to play.
* **Start Time** (`Start Time`, Time, required): Enter your start time for playing.
* **End Time** (`End Time`, Time, required): Enter your end time for playing.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, Note, required): Add any additional information about your playing style or preferences.
* **Email** (`Email`, Email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, Text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Name** (`Name`, Text, required): Enter your name.
* **Partner ID** (`Partner ID`, Text, required): Enter your partner's ID.
* **Partner Email** (`Partner Email`, Email, required): Enter your partner's email address.
* **Partner Phone** (`Partner Phone`, Text, optional): Enter your partner's phone number.
* **Partner Name** (`Partner Name`, Text, required): Enter your partner's name.
* **Partner Location** (`Partner Location`, Text, optional): Enter your partner's location.
* **My ID** (`My ID`, Text, required): Enter your partner's ID.
* **My Email** (`My Email`, Email, required): Enter your email address.
* **My Phone** (`My Phone`, Text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **My Location** (`My Location`, Text, optional): Enter your location.
* **My Interests** (`My Interests`, Select Multiple, optional): Select all the options that apply to your interests in playing racquetball.
* **Partner Interests 2** (`Partner Interests 2`, Select Multiple, optional): Select all the options that apply to your partner's interests in playing racquetball.
* **Notes 2** (`Notes 2`, Note, optional): Add any additional information about your match.
* **Partner Location 2** (`Partner Location 2`, Text, optional): Enter your partner's location.

Note: Please double-check your partner's details before submitting the form.
