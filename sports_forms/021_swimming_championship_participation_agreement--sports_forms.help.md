# Swimming Championship Participation Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from individuals participating in swimming championships. It helps event organizers and administrators gather essential details, including medical information, emergency contacts, and consent, to ensure a safe and smooth experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your swimmer details, including your name, club, and school affiliation.
2.  Provide your medical information, including any relevant medical conditions or allergies.
3.  List your emergency contacts, including their names and contact information.
4.  Give your consent to participate in the swimming championship.
5.  Fill in the event organizer's details, including their name and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Swimmer Details** (`swimmer_details`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Medical Information** (`medical_information`, text, required): Disclose any medical conditions, allergies, or other relevant health information that may affect your participation in the championship.
* **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, text, required): Provide the names and contact information (phone number, email, or address) of at least two emergency contacts, such as a parent or guardian.
* **Consent** (`consent`, text, required): Check the box to indicate your consent to participate in the swimming championship.
* **Club Details** (`club_details`, text, optional): If you are a club member, provide your club's name and contact information.
* **School Details** (`school_details`, text, optional): If you are a school team member, provide your school's name and contact information.
* **Event Organizer Details** (`event_organizer_details`, text, optional): If you are representing a club or school, provide the event organizer's name and contact information.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event organizer or administrator.
* This form is confidential and will only be used to ensure a safe and successful championship experience.
