# Cutting Phase Weight Training Plan Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from athletes about their cutting phase weight training plan. The form will be used to track and monitor the athlete's progress during the cutting phase, which is a period of intense fat loss and weight reduction. This form will help athletes to set realistic goals, track their workout frequency, focus on specific strength exercises, and receive guidance on their progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **User ID**, which is a unique identifier for you as an athlete.
2. Enter your **Athlete Name**, which should be your legal name as it appears on your ID.
3. Choose the date of your **Training Plan Date**, which should be the date you plan to start or have started your cutting phase.
4. In **Strength Goals**, describe your short-term and long-term strength goals, e.g., "Lose 10 pounds of body fat" or "Increase squats by 50 pounds".
5. Choose how often you will be **Working Out Frequency** - Daily, Weekly, or Bi-Weekly.
6. Select the specific **Strength Focus** areas you will be targeting with your workout plan - Chest, Back, Shoulders, Legs, Core, or Cardio.
7. List the **Exercises Performed** during your workout, e.g., "Squats, deadlifts, bench press, etc."
8. Add any additional **Notes** about your progress, tips, or challenges you're facing.
9. Enter your **Email** address, which will be used to communicate with you regarding your progress.
10. Lastly, provide your **Phone** number, which will be used for any follow-up questions or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User ID** (`number`, optional): Your unique identifier as an athlete.
* **Athlete Name** (`text`, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your ID.
* **Training Plan Date** (`date`, optional): Date you plan to start or have started your cutting phase.
* **Strength Goals** (`text`, optional): Describe your short-term and long-term strength goals.
* **Workout Frequency** (`select_one`, optional): Choose how often you will be working out - Daily, Weekly, or Bi-Weekly.
* **Strength Focus** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select the specific strength focus areas you will be targeting - Chest, Back, Shoulders, Legs, Core, or Cardio.
* **Exercises Performed** (`text`, optional): List the exercises you performed during your workout.
* **Notes** (`note`, optional): Add any additional notes about your progress, tips, or challenges.
* **Email** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`text`, optional): Provide your phone number for follow-up questions or concerns.

This form is designed to be a quick and efficient way to collect information from athletes about their cutting phase weight training plan. Please fill it out as accurately and thoroughly as possible to ensure you receive the best guidance and support during this phase.
