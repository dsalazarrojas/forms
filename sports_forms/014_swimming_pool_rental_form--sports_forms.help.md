# swimming_pool_rental_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The swimming pool rental form is a crucial document that helps us understand your needs and preferences when renting a pool for your swimming activities. Please answer the questions accurately to help us provide you with the best possible experience.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each section carefully and fill in the relevant information accordingly.
- Make sure to answer all required fields (indicated by an asterisk *).
- Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, *)
Please provide your basic contact information to help us identify you.

### **Swimmer Information** (`swimmer_info`, text, *)
Please provide the information of the swimmer who will be using the pool.

### **Pool Booking** (`pool_booking`, date, *)
Please select the date and time for the pool booking.

### **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, *)
Please select a payment method that suits your needs. We support Option 1, Option 2, and Option 3.

### **Swimmer Contact** (`swimmer_contact`, email, *)
Please provide an email address where we can contact you regarding any pool information or updates.

### **Swimmer Phone** (`swimmer_phone`, text, *)
Please provide a phone number where we can contact you in case of emergencies or last-minute updates.

### **Pool Address** (`pool_address`, select_multiple, *)
Please select the pool location that suits your needs from the available options: Location 1, Location 2, Location 3, and Location 4.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer all required fields accurately.
- Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.

Note: The form fields and options are subject to change based on the pool's availability and policies.
