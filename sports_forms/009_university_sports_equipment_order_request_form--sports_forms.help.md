# University Sports Equipment Order Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The University Sports Equipment Order Request Form is used by university staff or students to request sports equipment for a specific sport. This form helps to gather the necessary information for the equipment order, ensuring that the correct equipment is ordered and delivered to the correct location and person.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the sport you are requesting equipment for.
2. Specify the quantity of equipment you need.
3. Provide the location where you would like to pick up the equipment.
4. Choose a date to pick up the equipment.
5. Enter your name and email address so we can contact you.
6. Add your phone number so we can reach you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sport Requested** (Select One: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3): Select the sport for which you are requesting equipment.
* **Quantity Requested** (numeric value): Enter the number of pieces of equipment you need.
* **Pickup Location** (text field): Enter the location where you would like to pick up the equipment (e.g., department name, building).
* **Pickup Date** (date field): Choose a date to pick up the equipment.
* **Contact Name** (text field): Enter your name so we can contact you.
* **Contact Email** (email field): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your request.
* **Contact Phone** (text field): Enter your phone number so we can reach you.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure a successful order.
* Check your email for confirmation of your order.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact [support email] or [support phone number].
