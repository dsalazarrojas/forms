# Sports Facilities Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about sports facilities, allowing facility administrators to understand user experiences and make informed decisions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Read through the form carefully**: Before starting, take a moment to review the form and understand what information is being requested.
2. **Choose the correct feedback type**: Select the type of feedback you want to provide: General Feedback, Suggestion, or Complaint.
3. **Rate the facilities**: Rate the facilities on a scale, indicating your level of satisfaction.
4. **Rate the facilities overall**: Rate the facilities overall, using the same scale as above.
5. **Provide contact information (optional)**: If you want to be contacted for further discussion, enter your contact number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Feedback
* **Feedback**: A text field where you can provide a brief description of your experience with the sports facilities.

### Facilities
* **Facilities**: A multiple select field where you can choose which facilities you would like to provide feedback on (e.g. 'Yes', 'No').

### Rating
* **Rating**: A field where you can rate your satisfaction with the facilities, using a numerical scale.

### Facilities Rating
* **Facilities Rating**: Another field where you can rate your satisfaction with the facilities overall, using a numerical scale.

### Type of Feedback
* **Type of Feedback**: A select field where you can choose the type of feedback you would like to provide: General Feedback, Suggestion, or Complaint.

### Contact Number
* **Contact Number**: A text field where you can enter your contact number if you'd like to be contacted for further discussion.

## Tips
* Be honest and clear in your feedback to help facility administrators make informed decisions.
* Use the provided rating system to rate your satisfaction accurately.
* If you have multiple facilities to provide feedback on, use the 'Facilities' field to select all applicable options.
