# Membership Cancellation Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request the cancellation of a membership. Please fill out this form if you would like to cancel your membership.

## How To Complete This Form
To cancel your membership, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Cancel Membership" text field with a short description of your reason for canceling your membership.
2. Select the correct option from the "Are you a member?" field to indicate your current membership status.
3. If you have selected "Other (please specify)" in the "Are you a member?" field, please provide a brief explanation in the "Reason for Cancellation" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Cancel Membership** (Membership Cancellation Request Form, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your reason for canceling your membership.
* **Are you a member?** (Member Type, select_one, optional):
	+ Select "True" if you are a current member.
	+ Select "False" if you are not a current member.
	+ Select "Other (please specify)" if you are a member but your membership type is not listed.
* **Reason for Cancellation** (Reason for Cancellation, text, optional): If you selected "Other (please specify)" in the "Are you a member?" field, provide a brief explanation of your reason for cancellation.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have a valid reason for canceling your membership before submitting this form.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact our support team for assistance.
