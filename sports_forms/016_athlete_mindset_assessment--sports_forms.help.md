# Athlete Mindset Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Athlete Mindset Assessment is a tool designed to help athletes evaluate their mental strengths and weaknesses, identify areas for improvement, and develop strategies to enhance their performance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your full name and athlete ID number in the respective fields.
2. Select your primary sport from the options provided.
3. Choose your current team level.
4. Enter the date of the assessment.
5. Answer the questions regarding your motivation, confidence, goal commitment, pressure handling, focus, and resilience.
6. Select the sources of stress that apply to you.
7. Describe your mental strengths and areas for improvement.
8. Indicate what support you would like to receive to improve your mental skills.
9. Provide any additional comments you may have about your mindset.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Full Name**: This field is required and asks for your full name as an athlete.
* **Athlete ID Number**: Enter your student or team identification number if applicable. This field is not required but can be helpful for reference purposes.
* **Sport**: Select your primary sport from the options provided. This will help us understand the context of your answers.
* **Team Level**: Choose your current team level from the options provided.
* **Assessment Date**: Enter the date of the assessment. This will help us track your progress over time.
* **Overall Motivation Level**: Rate your current motivation level as Very High, High, Moderate, Low, or Very Low.
* **Confidence Level**: Rate your confidence in your abilities as Very Confident, Confident, Neutral, Not Confident, or Very Unconfident.
* **Goal Commitment**: Rate your goal commitment as Extremely Committed, Very Committed, Moderately Committed, Slightly Committed, or Not Committed.
* **Pressure Handling Ability**: Rate how well you handle competitive pressure as Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, or Poor.
* **Focus and Concentration**: Rate your ability to stay focused during competition as Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, or Poor.
* **Resilience Rating**: Rate your ability to bounce back from setbacks as Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, or Poor.
* **Self-Talk Quality**: Rate the positivity of your internal dialogue as Very Positive, Mostly Positive, Neutral, Mostly Negative, or Very Negative.
* **Visualization Practice**: Choose how often you use visualization techniques, such as Daily, Several Times a Week, Once a Week, Rarely, or Never.
* **Pre-Competition Anxiety Level**: Rate your typical anxiety before competition as None, Low, Moderate, High, or Very High.
* **Anxiety Management**: Rate how well you manage competition anxiety as Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, or Poor.
* **Teamwork Mindset**: Rate your attitude toward team collaboration as Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, or Poor.
* **Coachability**: Rate how open you are to feedback and coaching as Very Open, Open, Neutral, Resistant, or Very Resistant.
* **Competitive Drive**: Rate your desire to win and compete as Extremely High, High, Moderate, Low, or Very Low.
* **Mental Preparation Routine**: Choose whether you have a mental preparation routine, including whether it is detailed or basic.
* **Stress Sources**: Select all sources of stress that apply to you from the options provided.
* **Mental Strengths**: Describe your greatest mental strengths.
* **Mental Areas for Improvement**: Describe the mental skills you would like to improve.
* **Mental Skills Support Needed**: Choose from the options provided what support would help you improve your mental skills.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any other thoughts about your mindset.

Please complete this form honestly and thoroughly, and it will provide valuable insights into your mental strengths and weaknesses.
