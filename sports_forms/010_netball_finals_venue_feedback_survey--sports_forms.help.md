# Netball Finals Venue Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants about their overall experience at the Netball Finals event. Your feedback is crucial in helping us improve future events and create a better experience for everyone. Please take a few minutes to complete this survey and provide your honest feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each section as prompted. Start by rating your overall experience, and then proceed to answer questions about the venue's facilities, event scheduling, and other aspects of the event. If you have any suggestions or comments, please provide them in the text areas provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Rating**: Rate your overall experience at the event on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Venue Facilities**: Describe the condition of the venue's facilities, such as cleanliness, maintenance, and availability of amenities.
* **Event Scheduling**: Enter the date of the event, if you attended.
* **Event Scheduling Start**: Enter the start time of the event, if you attended.
* **Event Scheduling End**: Enter the end time of the event, if you attended.
* **Venue Environment**: Describe the condition of the venue's environment, such as temperature, lighting, and overall feel.
* **Communication**: Select all that apply to describe your experience with communication during the event. You can choose one or more of the following options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Netball Equipment**: Select one option to describe your experience with the netball court, balls, and shoes. You can choose one of the following options: Netball Court, Netball Balls, Netball Shoes, Other.
* **Netball Officials**: Select one option to rate your experience with the netball officials, such as referees, umpires, and scorers. You can choose one of the following options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Netball Officials Dress**: Select one option to rate your experience with the appearance of the netball officials' uniform. You can choose one of the following options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Overall Experience**: Select one option to rate your overall experience with the event. You can choose one of the following options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Suggestions**: Type any suggestions you have for improving the event or service provided.
* **Comments**: Share any comments or feedback you have about the event or experience.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if we need more information.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number, if you would like to be contacted.
* **Name**: Enter your name, so we can address you in any communication.
* **Netball Officials Suggestions**: Provide any suggestions you have for improving the netball officials' performance or service.

Please note that all fields are optional, and you can provide as much or as little information as you like.
