# Highlands Football Club Pre Tryout Club Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Highlands Football Club Pre Tryout Club Registration Form is designed to collect information from players for their registration and trial process. This form is essential for the club to gather accurate data and facilitate the registration process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by providing your name and contact details in the "Name," "Contact Number," and "Email" fields. Ensure that your contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
2. Upload a copy of your identity or birth certificate in the "Identity Copy" field.
3. Enter your registration details, including the trial date and start date, in the "Trial Date" and "Start Date" fields. Make sure to enter the correct dates in the correct format.
4. Provide your age in the "Age" field, ensuring it is between 9 and 30 years old.
5. Select the correct year for your trial date in the "Year" field.
6. Fill in the "Registration Fee" field with the required amount (R2,500.00).
7. If you have any additional notes or comments, provide them in the "Notes" field.
8. Check the "Accept" field to confirm your acceptance of the terms and conditions.
9. If applicable, enter the duration of your trial in the "Duration" field.
10. If you have any other contact information, provide it in the "Contact Name" field.
11. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
12. Fill in your address in the "Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **registration_details**: This is the first page of the form, which requires you to provide your registration details. Please fill in this section accurately.
* **name** (Name): Enter your name as it should appear on your registration documents.
* **identity_copy** (Identity Copy): Upload a copy of your identity or birth certificate.
* **registration_fee** (Registration Fee): This is the amount you need to pay for registration, which is R2,500.00.
* **trial_date** (Trial Date): Enter the date of your trial with the Highlands Football Club. Please ensure it is in the correct format.
* **start_date** (Start Date): Enter the date you would like to start playing with the club.
* **contact_number** (Contact Number): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **age** (Age): Please enter your age, which should be between 9 and 30 years old.
* **year** (Year): Select the year you are currently in or would like to start playing.
* **email** (Email): Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **notes** (Notes): If you have any additional comments or notes, provide them here.
* **accept** (Accept): Check the box to confirm your acceptance of the terms and conditions.
* **accept_date** (Accept Date): This field is not required, but please enter the date you accepted the terms and conditions if applicable.
* **duration** (Duration): If you know the duration of your trial, provide it here.
* **registration_fee_2** (Registration Fee 2): This is the second registration fee, which is R2,500.00. Please fill this field if you need to pay an additional registration fee.
* **contact_name** (Contact Name): If you have any additional contact information, enter it here.
* **phone** (Phone): Enter any other phone number we can use to contact you.
* **address** (Address): Enter your address if you have any additional contact information.
* **accept_time** (Accept Time): This field is not required and is used to track the time of acceptance.
* **accept_date_2** (Accept Date 2): This field is not required and is used to track the date of acceptance.
* **duration_2** (Duration 2): If you know the duration of your trial, enter it here.
* **trial_date_2** (Trial Date 2): This field is not required and is used to track the trial date.
* **registration_fee_3** (Registration Fee 3): This is the third registration fee, which is R2,500.00. Please fill this field if you need to pay an additional registration fee.
* **contact_number_2** (Contact Number 2): If you have any additional contact information, enter it here.
* **name_2** (Name 2): This field is not required and is used to track any additional contact name.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the Highlands Football Club.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid any errors.
* If you need to update any information, please contact the Highlands Football Club directly.
