# football_league_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The football_league_registration_form is designed for individuals or teams to register for a football league. It gathers essential information to facilitate the registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the required details in the "football_league_details" field.
2. Select your "team_name" if you're registering as a team, or your name if you're registering individually.
3. Choose your "sport" to confirm it's football.
4. Select your age range from the options provided in the "age_range" field.
5. Indicate if you're registering as an "Individual Registration" or "Team Registration" in the "registration_type" field.
6. Enter your "contact_email" for communication purposes.
7. Enter your "contact_phone" number for contact.
8. Enter the registration "date" and "time" if applicable.
9. Review your entries and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **football_league_details**: A text field for entering details about the league you're registering for. This is a required field.
* **team_name**: A text field for entering your team name if you're registering as a team, or your name if you're registering individually. This field is not required.
* **sport**: A text field for selecting the sport, which should be football. This field is not required but helps us confirm the correct sport.
* **age_range**: A select-one field with options for age ranges (18-25, 26-35, 36-45, 46-55, 56-65). Choose the age group you belong to. This field is not required.
* **registration_type**: A select-multiple field for choosing whether you're registering as an Individual Registration or Team Registration. This field is not required.
* **contact_email**: A text field for entering your email address. This field is not required but helps us get in touch with you.
* **contact_phone**: A text field for entering your phone number. This field is not required.
* **date**: A date field for entering the date of registration. This field is not required.
* **time**: A time field for entering the time of registration. This field is not required.
* **time**: A time field for entering the time of registration. This field seems to be a duplicate and is not required. 

Note: The "time" field at id: 9 seems to be a duplicate of the "time" field at id: 8. To correct this, you can remove or merge the duplicate fields.
