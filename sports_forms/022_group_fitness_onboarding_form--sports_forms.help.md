# Group Fitness Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Group Fitness Onboarding Form is designed to collect essential information from new members, including their personal details, emergency contact information, and agreement to the membership terms. This form is a crucial step in the onboarding process, ensuring that new members have the necessary information and agreement in place before starting the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1.  **New Member Details**: Fill out the "New Member Details" page with your personal information.
    *   **Title:** Enter your title (e.g., Mr., Mrs., Ms., Dr.)
    *   **First Name:** Enter your first name
    *   **Last Name:** Enter your last name
2.  **Emergency Contact**: Select the emergency contact method that best describes you:
    *   **First Name**
    *   **Last Name**
    *   **Phone**
    *   **Email**
3.  **Member Agreement**: Confirm your understanding of the membership terms by selecting at least one of the options.
    *   **I have read and agree to the terms and conditions**
    *   **I understand the membership terms and conditions**
    *   **I have not read and do not agree to the terms and conditions**
4.  **Emergency Contact Details**: If you selected "Emergency Contact" as your contact method, please provide the corresponding details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **New Member Details:**
    *   **New Member Details** (Page 1)
        +   **New Member Details** (Text)
            -   **Title** (`title`, text): Enter your title (e.g., Mr., Mrs., Ms., Dr.)
            -   **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name
            -   **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name
*   **Emergency Contact:**
    *   **Emergency Contact** (Page 2)
        +   **Select one:** (`emergency_contact`, select_one, required)
            -   **First Name**
            -   **Last Name**
            -   **Phone**
            -   **Email**
*   **Member Agreement:**
    *   **Member Agreement** (Page 3)
        +   **Select one or more:** (`member_agreement`, select_multiple, required)
            -   **I have read and agree to the terms and conditions**
            -   **I understand the membership terms and conditions**
            -   **I have not read and do not agree to the terms and conditions**
*   **Emergency Contact Details:**
    *   **Emergency Contact Details** (Page 4)
        +   **Emergency Contact Details** (Text)
            -   **(If selected as emergency contact)** (`emergency_details`, text, required): If you selected "Emergency Contact" as your contact method, please provide the corresponding details.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that you understand the membership terms and conditions before selecting the "Member Agreement" options.
*   If you select "Emergency Contact" as your contact method, please provide accurate and up-to-date emergency contact details.
