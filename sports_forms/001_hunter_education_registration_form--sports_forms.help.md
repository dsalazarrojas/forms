# Hunter Education Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hunter Education Registration Form is a crucial document that allows individuals to register for Hunter Education, a program that promotes hunting safety and regulations awareness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.
4. Enter your Address in the "Address" field.
5. Enter the City in the "City" field.
6. Enter the State in the "State" field.
7. Enter the Zip Code in the "Zip" field.
8. Provide your Phone Number in the "Phone" field.
9. Enter your Email Address in the "Email" field (if applicable).
10. Check or select your Hunter Education Registration Certificate status in the "Hunter Education Registration Certificate" field.
11. Specify whether you have a Utah License in the "Utah License" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your full first name (e.g., John).
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your full last name (e.g., Doe).
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter your complete address where you reside.
* **City** (`city`, text, required: false): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (`state`, text, required: false): Enter the state where you reside.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required: false): Enter the zip code of your residence.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address (if applicable).
* **Hunter Education Registration Certificate** (`hunter_education_registration_certificate`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the available options for your Hunter Education Registration Certificate.
	+ **Utah Resident Hunter Education Registration Certificate**: If you are a resident of Utah, select this option.
	+ **Non-Resident Hunter Education Registration Certificate**: If you are not a resident of Utah, select this option.
	+ **Other Hunter Education Registration Certificate**: Select this option if none of the other options apply.
	+ **Unknown**: If you are unsure, select this option.
* **Utah License** (`utah_license`, select_multiple, required: false): Check or select whether you have a Utah License.
	+ **True**: If you hold a Utah License, select this option.
	+ **False**: If you do not hold a Utah License, select this option.
