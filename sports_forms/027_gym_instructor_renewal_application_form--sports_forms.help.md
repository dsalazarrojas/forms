# Gym Instructor Renewal Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect necessary information from gym instructors for the purposes of renewal. Please ensure that the information is accurate and up-to-date to avoid any issues with your certification or licenses.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address.
2. Provide your Phone number for contact purposes.
3. Enter your Renewal Date, which is the date you are seeking to renew your certification or licenses.
4. Indicate your License status (Active/Inactive) and whether you have licenses renewal ('Yes' or 'No').

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Your first name should match the name on your official records.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Your last name should match the name on your official records.
* **Email** (Email, text, required): This is your contact email address that the gym uses for communication.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required): This is your contact phone number that the gym uses for communication.
* **Renewal Date** (Renewal Date, date, required): Enter the date you are seeking to renew your certification or licenses.
* **License** (License, select_one, required): Check 'Valid' if your license is current and 'Invalid' otherwise.
* **Address** (Address, text, required): Enter your current address that is used on your records.
* **License Status** (License Status, select_multiple, required): Check 'Active' if your licenses are active and 'Inactive' otherwise.
* **Licenses Renewal** (Licenses Renewal, select_multiple, required): Check 'Yes' if you have licenses renewal, 'No' otherwise.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and completely.
- Double-check your information before submission to avoid any discrepancies.
- If you have any issues with your submission, contact the gym administration for assistance.
