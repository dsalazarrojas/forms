# Primary School Swimming Carnival Nomination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for nominating students for the school swimming carnival. Teachers and parents can use this form to provide essential details about the student and their medical conditions, ensuring that the student can participate safely in the swimming events.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Student**: Enter the name and year level of the student.
2. **Class**: Select the year level of the student from the dropdown menu.
3. **Event**: Select one or multiple swimming events the student can participate in.
4. **Teacher Approval**: Confirm if you (the teacher) have approved the student's nomination.
5. **Additional Notes**: Provide any additional information about the student, such as previous swimming experience or any other relevant details.
6. **Email**: Enter the student's email address for communication purposes.
7. **Phone**: Enter the student's phone number for contact purposes.
8. **Date of Birth**: Enter the student's date of birth in the correct format (dd/mm/yyyy).
9. **Gender**: Select the student's gender.
10. **Medical Condition**: If the student has any medical conditions, select them from the list (e.g., Asthma, Diabetes, Epilepsy). If the student has a different medical condition, select "Other" and provide more details in the "Additional Notes" field.
11. **Allergies**: If the student has any allergies, enter them in the text field.
12. **Emergency Contacts**: Provide emergency contact details for the student, including name, relationship, phone number, and email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student** (`swim_carnival_student`, text, required): Enter the name of the student who is nominating for the swimming carnival.
* **Class** (`swim_carnival_class`, select_one, required): Select the year level of the student from the dropdown menu.
* **Event** (`swim_carnival_event`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or multiple swimming events the student can participate in.
* **Teacher Approval** (`swim_carnival_teacher_approval`, select_one, required): Confirm if you, as the teacher, have approved the student's nomination.
* **Additional Notes** (`swim_carnival_notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the student that may be helpful.
* **Email** (`swim_carnival_email`, email, required): Enter the student's email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`swim_carnival_phone`, text, required): Enter the student's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`swim_carnival_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the student's date of birth in the correct format (dd/mm/yyyy).
* **Gender** (`swim_carnival_gender`, select_one, required): Select the student's gender.
* **Medical Condition** (`swim_carnival_medical_condition`, select_multiple, optional): If the student has any medical conditions, select them from the list or choose "Other" and provide more details.
* **Allergies** (`swim_carnival_allergies`, text, optional): If the student has any allergies, enter them in the text field.
* **Emergency Contacts** (`swim_carnival_emergencies`, text, optional): Provide emergency contact details for the student, including name, relationship, phone number, and email.

## Tips

- Double-check the information provided before submitting the form.
- If the student has a medical condition or allergy, please ensure that you have provided accurate and up-to-date information.
- If you are unsure about any of the information, please contact the school administration or event organizers for guidance.
