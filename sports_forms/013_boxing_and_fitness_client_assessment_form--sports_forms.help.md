# Boxing And Fitness Client Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a crucial part of the onboarding process at the gym, and it's essential to complete it thoroughly and accurately. The information collected will help create a personalized training plan tailored to your needs and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form carefully, answering all questions as accurately as possible.
2. Review each question before submitting the form to ensure you've provided all the necessary information.
3. If you're unsure about any question, please ask your trainer for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, **required**): Your legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, **required**): Your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, **required**): Your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, **required**): Your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Current Fitness Level** (`current_fitness_level`, `select_one`, **required**): Please select how active you are in your daily life:
	+ Sedentary
	+ Lightly active
	+ Moderately active
	+ Very active
	+ Athlete
* **How Often Do You Exercise** (`exercise_frequency`, `select_one`, **required**): How often do you exercise in a typical week?
	+ Never
	+ 1-2 days per week
	+ 3-4 days per week
	+ 5-6 days per week
	+ Daily
* **Boxing or Combat Sport Experience** (`boxing_experience`, `select_one`, **required**): Please select your level of experience:
	+ No experience
	+ Beginner (less than 3 months)
	+ Intermediate (3-12 months)
	+ Advanced (1-3 years)
	+ Expert (3+ years)
* **Previous Injuries** (`injuries_history`, `text`, **optional**): List any prior injuries you've experienced.
* **History of Head Injuries or Concussions** (`head_injury_history`, `select_one`, **required**): Please answer this question carefully:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Unsure
* **Current Medications** (`medication_use`, `text`, **optional**): List any current prescriptions or supplements you're taking.
* **Primary Fitness Goal** (`primary_goal`, `select_one`, **required**): What is your primary fitness goal:
	+ Weight loss
	+ Muscle gain
	+ Cardiovascular fitness
	+ Stress relief
	+ Competition training
	+ General fitness
	+ Confidence building
* **Areas of Focus** (`area_focus`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Select the areas of focus for your training:
	+ Upper body strength
	+ Core strength
	+ Cardio endurance
	+ Speed and agility
	+ Technical boxing skills
	+ Self-defense
	+ Flexibility
* **Physician has Cleared You for Physical Activity** (`clearance_to_participate`, `select_one`, **required**): Please answer this question carefully:
	+ Yes, cleared
	+ No clearance
	+ Not applicable
	+ Under medical review
* **I Acknowledge the Risks of Boxing and Fitness** (`liability_acknowledgment`, `select_one`, **required**): Please read carefully and agree:
	+ I fully acknowledge and accept risks
	+ I need more information
	+ I do not accept risks
* **I Consent to this Fitness Assessment** (`assessment_consent`, `select_one`, **required**): Please agree:
	+ I fully consent
	+ I do not consent

## Tips
* Please answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
* If you're unsure about any question, please ask your trainer for clarification.
* This form is a required part of the onboarding process and will be reviewed by your trainer before creating a customized training plan.
