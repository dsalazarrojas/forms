# Fitness Center Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is to collect personal and contact information from individuals visiting or using the Fitness Center for liability or emergency purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the respective fields.
2. Provide your **Email**, which will be used for communications and notifications.
3. Enter your **Address**, which is your current location.
4. Enter your **Phone**, which will be used for emergency contact and communication.
5. If you have an **Emergency Contact**, provide their name, phone number, and relationship to you.
6. If you have an **Emergency Contact**, provide their email address.
7. Repeat steps 5-6 for up to 6 emergency contacts.
8. You may add a note for each emergency contact, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, `required`): Enter your active and valid email address for communications and notifications.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, `required`): Enter your current address, which may be your home, work, or gym address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, `required`): Enter your active and valid phone number for emergency contact and communication.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, `optional`): If you have an emergency contact, enter their name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, `optional`): If you have an emergency contact, enter their phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `select_multiple`, `optional`): If you have an emergency contact, select their relationship to you (e.g., Mother, Father, Spouse, Friend, Partner, Child).
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, `text`, `optional`): If you have an emergency contact, enter their address.
* **Emergency Contact Phone 2-3** (`emergency_contact_phone_2-3`, `text`, `optional`): Repeat for up to 6 emergency contacts.
* **Emergency Contact Note** (`emergency_contact_note`, `note`, `optional`): Add any additional notes for each emergency contact.

Note: The form appears to be designed for liability or emergency purposes, with a focus on collecting contact information in case of emergencies or incidents. The form is extensive but seems to be redundant, suggesting some fields could be removed or combined for a more efficient and user-friendly experience.
