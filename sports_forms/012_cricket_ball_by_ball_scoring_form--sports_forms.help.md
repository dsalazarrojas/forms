# Cricket Ball By Ball Scoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the score of a cricket match by ball. It's essential to provide accurate and consistent data for umpires, scorers, and analysts to track the game's progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have access to real-time data during the match.
2.  Fill out the required fields as accurately as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, required: false)
This field is for any introductory information or notes related to the match.

### 2. **Batsman Details** (`batsman_details`, `text`, required: false)
Enter any relevant details about the batsman, such as their name, team, or position.

### 3. **Bowler Details** (`bowler_details`, `text`, required: false)
Enter any relevant details about the bowler, such as their name, team, or position.

### 4. **Score** (`score`, `number`, required: false)
Enter the total score of the batsman for the ball.

### 5. **Over** (`over`, `number`, required: false)
Enter the current over being bowled.

### 6. **Ball** (`ball`, `number`, required: false)
Enter the current ball being bowled.

### 7. **Runs** (`runs`, `number`, required: false)
Enter the number of runs scored on the current ball.

### 8. **Wickets** (`wickets`, `number`, required: false)
Enter the number of wickets fallen on the current ball.
### 9. **Wickets Lost** (`wickets`, `number`, required: true)
Enter the total number of wickets lost so far in the match.

## Tips
- Always ensure you are filling out the form accurately and consistently.
- Use the notes field for any additional information that may aid in scoring.
- Double-check the data for correctness before submitting it.
