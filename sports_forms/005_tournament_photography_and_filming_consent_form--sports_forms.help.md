# Tournament Photography and Filming Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain consent from participants, parents or guardians for media coverage during a sports tournament. This includes photography and filming.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined below:

1. Review each field carefully and fill out the required information accurately.
2. Ensure you fill out all required fields (indicated by a asterisk *).
3. If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tournament Host Name** (`1`, `text`, *required*): Enter the name of the tournament host.
* **Participant Name** (`2`, `text`, *required*): Enter the name of the participant.
* **Parent/Guardian Consent** (`3`, `select_multiple`, *required*): Check the box to confirm that you are the parent/guardian and have given consent for the participant to be photographed or filmed.
	+ Options: Yes/No
* **Email** (`4`, `email`, *required*): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`5`, `text`, *required*): Enter a valid phone number.
* **Filming Consent** (`6`, `select_multiple`, *required*): Check the box to confirm that you consent to filming.
	+ Options: Yes/No
* **Photography Consent** (`7`, `select_multiple`, *required*): Check the box to confirm that you consent to photography.
	+ Options: Yes/No
* **Media Permissions** (`8`, `select_one`, *required*): Select one of the media options to grant or deny media coverage.
	+ Options: Tournament Photography and Filming, Social Media, Both
* **Additional Comments** (`9`, `note`, *optional*): Enter any additional comments or remarks about the form.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please ask for clarification.
* Ensure that you have the consent of the parent/guardian for participants under 18 years old.
