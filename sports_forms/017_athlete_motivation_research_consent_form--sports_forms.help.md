# Athlete Motivation Research Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Athlete Motivation Research Consent Form is designed to collect information from athletes about their motivations, behaviors, and experiences in sports. This form helps researchers understand athlete motivations and develop insights to improve performance and well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the following fields:

1. **Athlete Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
2. **Athlete ID Number**: If applicable, enter your student or team identification number.
3. **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format (e.g., 12-25-1999).
4. **Sport**: Select the primary sport you participate in.
5. **Team Level**: Indicate the level of competition you are participating in (e.g., Varsity, Junior Varsity, Club Team).
6. **Research Study Title**: Enter the name of the research study you are participating in.
7. **Principal Investigator**: Enter the name of the lead researcher conducting the study.
8. **Research Institution**: Enter the university or organization conducting the research.
9. **Research Purpose**: Describe the goal of the research study in a few words.
10. **Participation Activities**: Select the activities you will participate in during the study (e.g., Surveys, Interviews, Psychological Assessments).
11. **Time Commitment**: Estimate the total time required for participation (e.g., Less Than 1 Hour Total, 1 to 5 Hours Total).
12. **Potential Risks**: Indicate if you are aware of any potential risks associated with the study.
13. **Potential Benefits**: Select the benefits you may gain from participating in the study (e.g., Better Understanding of Motivation, Improved Performance Insights).
14. **Confidentiality Understanding**: Confirm that you understand how your data will be protected.
15. **Data Usage**: Select how your data can be used (e.g., Research Analysis Only, Publication in Academic Journals).
16. **Voluntary Participation Understanding**: Confirm that you understand participation is voluntary and you can withdraw at any time.
17. **Withdrawal Rights**: Confirm that you understand you can withdraw from the study at any time.
18. **Compensation**: Indicate if you will receive monetary or other compensation for participating.
19. **Questions Contact Information**: Confirm if you have received contact information for questions or concerns.
20. **Primary Motivation Factors**: Select the factors that motivate you most in sports (e.g., Winning, Personal Improvement).
21. **Parent or Guardian Name** (if under 18): Enter the name of your parent or guardian.
22. **Athlete Signature**: Sign your full name as a confirmation of understanding.
23. **Parent or Guardian Signature** (if under 18): Sign your parent or guardian's full name as a confirmation of understanding.
24. **Date of Consent**: Sign and date this form as confirmation of your understanding and consent.
