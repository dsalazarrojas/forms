# sports_clinic_enrollment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for enrolling in a sports clinic. Users will select a course and provide their first name, last name, email, and phone number to complete the registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the course you wish to enroll in from the available options in the **Course Selection** field.
2. Enter your **First Name** in the field provided.
3. Enter your **Last Name** in the field provided.
4. Enter your valid **Email Address** in the field provided.
5. Enter your **Phone Number** in the field provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Selection** (`course_selection`, **select_one**, optional): Select the course you wish to enroll in. This field allows you to choose from available options.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, **text**, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, **text**, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email** (`email`, **email**, optional): Enter a valid email address to contact you regarding sports clinic updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, **text**, optional): Enter a valid phone number for contact purposes.
