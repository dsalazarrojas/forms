# Parental Travel Consent For Away Games Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for parents/guardians to provide travel information and obtain necessary consent for their child's away game events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide travel details for your child, including travel date, time, duration, location, and transport arrangement.
2. List any medical conditions that may impact your child's participation in the away game.
3. Provide the name and phone number of an emergency contact person.
4. Specify the medical contact person and any relevant medical information.
5. Fill out the travel notes and medical administration sections as necessary.
6. Select "Parent has given permission" from the "Parent Permission" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Travel Details (1)**: Enter the date, time, and duration of the away game.
* **Parent Permission (2)**: Select "Parent has given permission" to confirm that you have obtained consent from the parent/guardian.
* **Emergency Contact (3)**: Enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact person who can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Medical Contact (4)**: Enter the name and contact information of the medical contact person, if any.
* **Medical Conditions (5)**: List any medical conditions that may impact your child's participation in the away game.
* **Travel Location (6)**: Enter the location of the away game.
* **Travel Transport (7)**: Specify the transport arrangement for the away game (e.g., car, bus, etc.).
* **Travel Notes (8)**: Enter any additional travel-related information, such as travel itinerary or special instructions.
* **Medical Administration (9)**: Provide any necessary medical administration information for the away game.
* **Administration Form (10)**: Enter any additional administration information, if necessary.
* **Travel Date (7)**: Enter the date of the away game.
* **Travel Time (8)**: Enter the time of the away game.
* **Travel Duration (9)**: Enter the duration of the away game.
* **Tips**
	+ Please ensure that you have obtained the necessary consent from the parent/guardian before submitting this form.
	+ Keep the emergency contact information up-to-date in case of an emergency.
	+ List any medical conditions or administration information that may impact your child's participation in the away game.
