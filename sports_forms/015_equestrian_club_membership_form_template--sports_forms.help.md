# Equestrian Club Membership Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from individuals who wish to join the Equestrian Club. This form is designed to gather necessary details for membership registration, which will be reviewed and processed by the club's administration team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Ensure that your email address is valid and in the correct format (e.g., `example@example.com`).
3. Enter your contact number (phone or mobile) for easy communication.
4. Double-check your details before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First name** (text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Last name** (text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Address** (text, optional): Provide your current address for membership purposes.
- **Email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
- **Contact number** (text, optional): Enter your phone or mobile number for easy communication with the club.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
- If you have any special requests or requirements, kindly note them in the additional comments section (not provided in this template).
- Our team will review and process your membership application after receiving this form.
