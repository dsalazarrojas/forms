# Virtual Running Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Running Event Registration Form is used by event organizers to collect participant information for virtual running events.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with the following information:
2. Enter your First Name.
3. Enter your Last Name.
4. Provide your Email address.
5. Enter your Phone number (optional).
6. Enter your Date of Birth.
7. Select the type of event (Virtual or In-person Event).
8. Enter the Event Date.
9. Enter the Event Time.
10. Specify the Number of Participants.
11. If necessary, enter Additional Information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **First Name** (`form_first_name`, text, required)
Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.

### **Last Name** (`form_last_name`, text, required)
Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.

### **Email** (`form_email`, email, required)
Enter your valid email address for communication and updates.

### **Phone** (`form_phone`, text, optional)
Enter your phone number for additional contact.

### **Date of Birth** (`form_dob`, date, required)
Enter your Date of Birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY.

### **Event Type** (`form_event_type`, select_one, required)
Select the type of event you are registering for (Virtual or In-person Event).

### **Event Date** (`form_event_date`, date, required)
Enter the date of your registered event.

### **Event Time** (`form_event_time`, time, required)
Enter the time of your registered event.

### **Number of Participants** (`form_number_of_participants`, number, required)
Enter the number of participants you are registering for.

### **Additional Information** (`form_additional_info`, note, optional)
Enter any additional information about your registration or event.
