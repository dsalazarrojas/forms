# sports_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sports_registration_form is a user registration form for capturing personal and sports-specific information for sports teams. It is used to collect information from users who wish to register for sports activities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Birth Date in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Phone number, Email, and Registration Date, if applicable.
3. Select your type of registration (New player or Returning player) in the Select Type field.
4. Choose your preferred Sport (Soccer, Basketball, or Baseball) in the Select Position field.
5. Select your Sport of interest (Soccer, Basketball, or Baseball) in the Sport field.
6. Add any additional Note if required or desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Birth date** (date, required: false): Enter your date of birth.
* **Phone** (text, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (email, required: false): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Select Type** (select_one, required: false): Select New player or Returning player.
* **Select position** (select_multiple, required: false): Select one or more of the following sports: Soccer, Basketball, Baseball.
* **Sport** (select_one, required: false): Select one of the following sports: Soccer, Basketball, Baseball.
* **Registration Date** (date, required: false): Enter your date of registration.
* **Note** (note, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments.
