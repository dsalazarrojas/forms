# Uniform Distribution Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect details about staff members' uniform requirements, ensuring accurate allocation and minimizing errors. It is designed to prevent duplication of uniform requests and make it easier for staff members to receive the correct uniform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your staff member's details:
	* **Staff Member Details**: Enter your staff member's name and staff number in the respective fields.
2. Select your team and uniform type:
	* **Team Name**: Choose your team name from the list of options.
	* **Uniform Type**: Select the type of uniform you require (e.g., Baseball, Basketball, etc.).
3. Specify your size and color preferences:
	* **Size**: Choose your size preference from the available options (e.g., Small, Medium, Large, etc.).
	* **Color**: Select your preferred color (e.g., Red, Blue, Green, etc.).
4. Enter the date you require the uniform:
	* **Date**: Enter the date you need the uniform by.
5. Add any additional notes:
	* **Notes**: Enter any additional information about your uniform requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Staff Member Details** (text)
	+ Enter your staff member's name and staff number in this field.
* **Staff Number** (text)
	+ Enter your staff member's staff number.
* **Staff Name** (text)
	+ Enter your staff member's staff name.
* **Team Name** (text)
	+ Choose your team name from the list of options.
* **Number of Uniforms** (number)
	+ Enter the number of uniforms you require.
* **Uniform Type** (select_one)
	+ Select the type of uniform you require (e.g., Baseball, Basketball, etc.).
	+ Note: Choose one type of uniform only.
* **Size** (select_multiple)
	+ Select one or more size preferences (e.g., Small, Medium, Large, etc.).
* **Color** (select_multiple)
	+ Select one or more color preferences (e.g., Red, Blue, Green, etc.).
* **Date** (date)
	+ Enter the date you need the uniform by.
* **Notes** (note)
	+ Enter any additional information about your uniform requirements.
	+ Note: This is a free-form field, so you can enter as much or as little information as needed.
