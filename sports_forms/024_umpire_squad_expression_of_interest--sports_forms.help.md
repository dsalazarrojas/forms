# Umpire Squad Expression Of Interest - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals interested in being part of an umpire squad.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, Text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, optional): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Note** (`umpire_squad_expression_of_interest`, Note, optional): This field is not currently active, so you can ignore it for now.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Ensure your email address is accurate and active.
- This form is for general interest in being part of an umpire squad; if you have any further questions, feel free to contact form@gic.mx.
