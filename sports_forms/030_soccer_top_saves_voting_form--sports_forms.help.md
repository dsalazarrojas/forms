# Soccer Top Saves Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about the top saves made by soccer players during a game or season. Users will vote for their top saves from a list of options, and provide additional information such as dates, times, and notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your top saves from the list provided in the "Soccer Save" fields.
2. Enter the date and time of the save(s) you selected.
3. Provide a brief note about the save(s) if desired.
4. Enter any additional contact information if you want to be contacted for further discussion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Soccer Save 1 (text)**: Select your top save from the list of provided options.
* **Soccer Save 2 (select one)**: Choose one of the top saves from the list, and select it from the provided options.
* **Soccer Save 4 (number)**: Enter a numerical value to represent the save's impact or rating.
* **Soccer Save 5 (number)**: Enter another numerical value to represent the save's impact or rating.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the save(s) you selected.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the save(s) you selected.
* **Note**: Enter a brief note about the save(s) you selected.
* **Email**: Enter your email address if you want to be contacted for further discussion.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted for further discussion.
* **Date 2 (date)**: Enter the date of another save if you have another one to report.
* **Time 2 (time)**: Enter the time of another save if you have one to report.
* **Note 2 (note)**: Enter a brief note about another save if you have one to report.
* **Email 2 (email)**: Enter another email address if you want to be contacted for further discussion about this save.
* **Phone 2 (text)**: Enter another phone number if you want to be contacted for further discussion about this save.
