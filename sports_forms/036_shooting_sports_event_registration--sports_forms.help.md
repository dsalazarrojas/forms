# Shooting Sports Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for participants to register for a shooting sports event. It collects information about the participant, their discipline choices, emergency contact details, safety acknowledgement, organizer's details, and shooting event details. Please use this form to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a successful registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form in sections, starting with participant details.
- Answer each field carefully and to the best of your ability.
- Make sure to review your submission before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required: false): Please enter your name and any other relevant personal information.
- **Discipline Choices** (`discipline_choices`, select multiple, required: false): Select one or more disciplines you will be participating in (e.g., Pistol, Rifle, Shotgun, Archery).
- **Emergency Contact Details** (`emergency_contact_details`, text, required: false): Enter the name and contact information of a person to be contacted in case of an emergency.
- **Participant Safety Acknowledgement** (`participant_safety_acknowledgement`, note, required: false): Read and acknowledge that you understand the safety guidelines and risks associated with the event.
- **Organizer Details** (`organiser_details`, text, required: false): Enter the name and contact information of the person or organization hosting the event.
- **Shooting Event Details** (`shooting_event_details`, text, required: false): Enter any additional information about the shooting event, such as date, time, location, and other relevant details.

## Tips
- Please double-check your information before submitting.
- If you have any questions or issues, contact the event organizers for assistance.
