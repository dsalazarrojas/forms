# Adult Co Ed Softball League Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register players for an adult co-ed softball league. It gathers essential information about the player, including their contact details, player experience, and preferred positions. The form also includes a liability waiver consent that players must agree to before registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*). These fields include:
    *   Full Name: Your full name as you would like to be referred to in the league.
    *   Age: Your age, which must be 18 or older to participate.
    *   Gender: Your gender, which is used for league balancing purposes.
    *   Contact Phone: Your primary phone number for communication.
    *   Email Address: Your primary email address for schedule updates and other communications.
    *   Registration Type: You must select whether you are registering as a free agent, team captain, or team member.
    *   Liability Waiver Consent: You must agree to the liability waiver, which is standard practice in many sports leagues.
*   Select your preferred positions from the list.
*   If you are registering as a team member or captain, enter your team name in the "Team Name if Applicable" field.
*   Review and agree to the liability waiver consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Player Full Name** (`player_full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as you would like to be referred to in the league.
*   **Age** (`age`, number, required): Your age, which must be 18 or older to participate.
*   **Gender** (`gender`, select\_one, required): Select your gender, which is used for league balancing purposes.
*   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your primary phone number for communication.
*   **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your primary email address for schedule updates and other communications.
*   **Registration Type** (`registration_type`, select\_one, required): Select whether you are registering as a free agent, team captain, or team member.
*   **Team Name if Applicable** (`team_name_if_applicable`, text, optional): If you are registering as a team member or captain, enter your team name.
*   **Skill Level** (`skill_level`, select\_one, required): Select your skill level, which can inform team captain decisions.
*   **Preferred Positions** (`preferred_positions`, select\_multiple, optional): Select the positions you enjoy playing.
*   **T-Shirt Size** (`t_shirt_size`, select\_one, required): Select your t-shirt size for your team jersey.
*   **Liability Waiver Consent** (`liability_waiver_consent`, select\_one, required): You must agree to the liability waiver, which is standard practice in many sports leagues.
