# membership_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect membership applications from interested individuals who wish to join a sports-related organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your first name
2. Enter your email address
3. Enter your contact number
4. Enter your home address
5. Enter your date of birth
6. Select the sport you are interested in
7. Choose your membership type (individual, team, or club)
8. If applicable, select any additional members who wish to join
9. Enter the date you submit this application
10. Enter the name of the tool assigned to you (if any)
11. Select the category of this form (sports or entrepreneur)
12. Enter any additional comments or notes
13. Enter the form ID (if required for any reason)
14. Select the output file format

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### first name
* **first name** (`member_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
### email
* **email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
### contact
* **contact** (`contact_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
### home address
* **home address** (`member_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your home address for our records.
### date of birth
* **date of birth** (`birth_date`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
### sport
* **sport** (`sport`, select_one, required/optional): Select the sport you are interested in (e.g., soccer, basketball, baseball, football).
### membership
* **membership** (`membership_type`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose your desired membership type (individual, team, or club).
### additional members
* **additional members** (`additional_members`, select_multiple, required/optional): If you wish to join with additional members, select them from the list provided.
### submit date
* **submit date** (`submit_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you submit this application.
### assigned tool
* **assigned tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the tool assigned to you if any.
### category
* **category** (`category`, select_one, required/optional): Select the category of this form (sports or entrepreneur).
### description
* **description** (`description`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your application.
### notes
* **notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any extra information you feel is necessary to process your application.
### form id
* **form id** (`form_id`, text, required/optional): Enter the form ID if required by any authority.
### output file
* **output file** (`output_file`, text, required/optional): Select the output file format you prefer.

## Tips

- Please ensure that you fill out the form correctly to avoid any processing delays.
- If you are submitting with additional members, ensure they also fill out this form accurately.
- If you have any difficulties with the form, contact our support team for help.
