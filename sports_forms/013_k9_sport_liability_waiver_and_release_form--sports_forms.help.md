# K9 Sport Liability Waiver And Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The K9 Sport Liability Waiver And Release Form is a document that requires participants to acknowledge and agree to certain rules and guidelines before engaging in K9 sports-related activities. It's essential to complete this form carefully to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Event Details**: This section is for you to provide information about the event, such as the date, time, location, and any other relevant details. Please ensure that the information is accurate and up-to-date.
2.  **Event Host**: This section is for you to provide information about the event host, such as their name, contact number, and email. This information will be used in case of emergencies or any other issues that may arise during the event.
3.  **Participant**: This section is for you to provide your personal details, such as your name, address, and contact number.
4.  **Participant Information**: This section is for you to provide more information about yourself, such as your age, sex, and any other relevant details.
5.  **Emergency Contact Information**: This section is for you to provide information about a contact person that can be reached in case of an emergency.
6.  **Emergency Contact Relationship**: This section is for you to describe your relationship with the emergency contact, such as "son, mother, friend," etc.
7.  **Medical Conditions**: If you have any medical conditions that may affect your ability to participate in the event, please list them in this section.
8.  **Allergies**: If you have any allergies, please list them in this section.
9.  **Insurance**: If you have any insurance that may be relevant to the event, please provide the details in this section.
10. **Release Of Holds**: This section is for you to acknowledge and release any potential holds or liabilities that may arise from participating in the event.
11.  **Medical Release**: This section is for you to grant permission for medical treatment in case of an emergency.
12.  **Waiver**: This section is for you to acknowledge and agree to the rules and guidelines of the event.
13.  **Participant Signature**: Finally, please sign this section to acknowledge that you have read and understood the form.
