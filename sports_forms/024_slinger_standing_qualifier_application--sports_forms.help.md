# Slinger Standing Qualifier Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This application is used to determine whether you meet the qualifications to be a Slinger Standing Qualifier. If you're unsure if you qualify, refer to the application instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to answer every question that applies to your situation.
3. Double-check your information before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Slinger Standing Qualifier Application Form** (`main_info`, text, optional): This is the title of the form.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your available times (6am-10am, 10am-2pm, 2pm-6pm).
* **Experience** (`experience`, text, optional): Enter your experience in the field.
* **Consent** (`consent`, note, optional): You must be 18 or older to participate in the Slinger Standing Qualifier program.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, optional): Choose "I am a qualified event organizer" if you meet the qualifications, otherwise select "I am not a qualified event organizer".

## Tips
* Make sure to answer truthfully and accurately.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the application instructions above.
* You can always reach out to us if you have questions or concerns.
