# Tennis Course Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to apply for a tennis course. It collects information about the user's availability, practice habits, and other relevant details to determine the best-suited tennis course for them. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information, marked as "required" in the form.
2. Select the relevant options for each field, as prompted.
3. Enter your available dates and practice schedule in the corresponding fields.
4. Enter your emergency contact information.
5. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (id: 1, text, required: false): Enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Select Tennis Course** (id: 2, select_one, required: false): Choose whether you are interested in a tennis course.
* **Schedule Your Class** (id: 3, date, required: false): Enter the date you would like to start your class.
* **Practice Frequency** (id: 4, select_one, required: false): Select how often you would like to practice.
* **Available Dates** (id: 5, text, required: false): Enter the dates you are available to practice.
* **Emergency Contact** (id: 6, email, required: false): Enter the contact information of an emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Phone** (id: 7, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of an emergency contact person.
* **Tennis Facility** (id: 9, select_one, required: false): Indicate whether you have access to a tennis facility.
* **Class Level** (id: 10, select_one, required: false): Select the level of your tennis class (High or Low).
* **Available Time** (id: 11, time, required: false): Enter the time you are available to practice.
* **Practice Duration** (id: 12, text, required: false): Enter the length of time you want to practice each session.
* **Practice Frequency 2** (id: 13, select_one, required: false): Select how often you would like to practice.
* **Submit** (id: 8, text, required: false): Submit your application.

Please note: 

* All fields are optional unless marked as required in the form.
* You can enter your answers in any format that is convenient for you.
* Please review and submit the form carefully before sending it in.
