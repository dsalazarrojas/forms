# Soccer Experience Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to collect feedback from soccer participants and coaches about their experience during soccer sessions. It will help us identify areas of improvement and ensure that our training camps, events, and matches are enjoyable and safe for everyone involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:
1. Select the relevant options for each field that best describe your experience.
2. Fill in the text fields with your thoughts and comments.
3. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `text`, optional)
	+ This field is a brief description of your experience. Please keep it concise and to the point.
* **Session Type** (`session_type`, `select_one`, optional)
	+ Select the type of session you attended (Training Camp, Event, or Match).
* **Training Camp Details** (`training_camp_details`, `text`, optional)
	+ If you attended a training camp, please provide any relevant details about the session, such as the date, time, and location.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `number`, optional)
	+ Rate your overall satisfaction with the session on a scale from 1-5.
* **Training Camp Rating** (`training_camp_rating`, `number`, optional)
	+ Rate the quality of the training camp you attended on a scale from 1-5.
* **Trainers' Performance** (`trainers_performance`, `select_multiple`, optional)
	+ Rate the performance of the trainers during the session. Select all that apply.
* **Trainers' Knowledge** (`trainers_knowledge`, `select_one`, optional)
	+ Rate the knowledge and expertise of the trainers during the session.
* **Facilities Condition** (`facilities_condition`, `select_one`, optional)
	+ Rate the condition of the facilities used for the session.
* **Event Safety** (`event_safety`, `select_one`, optional)
	+ Rate the safety of the event.
* **Communication** (`communication`, `select_one`, optional)
	+ Rate the quality of communication from the organizers.
* **Food and Drinks** (`food_drinks`, `select_one`, optional)
	+ Rate the quality of food and drinks provided.
* **Overall Environment** (`overall_environment`, `select_one`, optional)
	+ Rate the overall environment and atmosphere of the session.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, `text`, optional)
	+ Provide any suggestions or feedback you have about the session.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional)
	+ Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the session.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, `date`, optional)
	+ Enter the date of the session.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, `time`, optional)
	+ Enter the time of the session.
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, `number`, optional)
	+ Enter the duration of the session.
* **Attendees Count** (`attendees_count`, `number`, optional)
	+ Enter the number of attendees at the session.
* **Staff Count** (`staff_count`, `number`, optional)
	+ Enter the number of staff members present at the session.
* **Form Submitter** (`form_submitted_by`, `text`, optional)
	+ Enter your name or the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional)
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional)
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional)
	+ Enter any notes or additional information about the session.
* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, optional)
	+ Select the category of the session (Training Camp, Event, or Match).
* **Form Submitted On** (`form_submitted_on`, `date`, optional)
	+ Enter the date the form was submitted.

Note: If a field is marked as "required", please make sure to fill it in before submitting the form.
