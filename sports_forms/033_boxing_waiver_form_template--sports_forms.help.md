# Boxing Waiver Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect demographic information and ensure participants understand the risks and rules associated with boxing. It's essential for participants to fill this form carefully, as it will be used to assess their understanding and consent.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the required fields marked as "required" (denoted by **Required** in the field name).
2. Select the correct options for each question, taking into account the hints provided.
3. Type your full legal name as the Participant Signature.
4. Enter the Date of signing in the Signature Date field.
5. If applicable, enter the Witness Name and Witness Signature.

  **Required: true**
