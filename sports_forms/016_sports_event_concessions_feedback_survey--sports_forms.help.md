# sports_event_concessions_feedback_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and information from attendees about the quality of food and beverage options and services provided at sports events. The information will be used to improve the concessions services and overall attendee experience at future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the relevant options for each field and provide any additional comments or feedback you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What was the quality of food and beverage options?** (`concession_quality`, select_multiple, required: false): This question allows you to rate the quality of the food and beverage options at the event, selecting as many options as apply. You can choose from: Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **How was the service from the vendors?** (`concession_service`, select_one, required: false): This question asks you to rate the service from the vendors at the event, choosing one of the following options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **What is your future purchase intent for concessions at this event?** (`future_purchase_intent`, select_one, required: false): This question helps us understand your interest in purchasing concessions at future events, with the options: True, False, or Undecided.
* **Which events have you attended?** (`event_attendance`, select_multiple, required: false): This question helps us understand the types of events you have attended in the past, selecting as many options as apply. You can choose from: Sports events, Music events, Theater events, or Other events.
* **Additional comments or feedback** (`feedback`, note, required: false): This is an open-ended field where you can provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience at the event.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Please enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted with any follow-up questions or if we'd like to share future event information with you.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): If you'd like to be contacted via phone, please enter your phone number.
