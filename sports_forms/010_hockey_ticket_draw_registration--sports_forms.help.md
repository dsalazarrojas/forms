# Hockey Ticket Draw Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register players for a hockey ticket draw. It collects personal and team information to ensure accurate and complete data.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Make sure to enter your first name, last name, email address, phone number, address, team name, jersey number, division, position, date of birth, and date of registration.
- Select the correct division and position from the drop-down menus.
- If applicable, provide any additional team reektions in the provided text area.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (Email, text, required): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your phone number.
- **Address** (Address, text, required): Enter your address.
- **Team Name** (Team Name, text, required): Enter your team name.
- **Player Name** (Player Name, text, required): Enter your player name.
- **Jersey Number** (Jersey Number, number, required): Enter your jersey number.
- **Division** (Division, select_one, required): Select your division from the provided options.
  - Options: Defense, midfield, attack, goals
- **Position** (Position, select_multiple, required): Select your position from the provided options.
  - Options: Forward, Goalkeeper, Defense, Midfielder
- **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Date of Registration** (Date of Registration, date, required): Enter the current date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Team Reektions** (Team Reektions, note, optional): Enter any additional team reektions in the provided text area.
  - Note: This field is optional, and it's not required.
- **Team Reektions 2** (Team Reektions 2, note, optional): This field is optional and not required. You can enter any additional team reektions in this field.
