# Demo Ride Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain liability waiver consent from demo ride riders. It's a mandatory step for all riders who participate in demo ride events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the introduction page to understand the purpose and context of the form.
2. Provide your participant information, which should include your name, email, and phone number.
3. Fill out the emergency contact information with the details of your emergency contact person.
4. Select the waiver consent options that apply to you. You must choose "Accept" and at least one other option to agree to the terms and conditions.
5. Sign the E-Signature page to confirm your consent.
6. Sign the Terms and Conditions Signature page to confirm that you have read and understood the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Page 1, Text, Optional): This is a brief overview of the form and its purpose. Please read it carefully to understand what you are about to do.
* **Participant Information** (Page 2, Select One, Optional): Please choose one of the options to provide your contact information. This will be used to reach out to you or your emergency contact in case of an emergency.
	+ **Name**: Your full name.
	+ **Email**: Your email address where we can reach you.
	+ **Phone**: Your phone number where we can contact you.
* **Emergency Contact** (Page 3, Text, Optional): Enter the details of your emergency contact person, including their name, email, and phone number.
* **Waiver Consent** (Page 4, Select Multiple, Optional): Select all the options that apply to you to agree to the terms and conditions.
	+ **Accept**: Indicate that you agree to the terms and conditions.
	+ **Terms and Conditions**: Indicate that you have read and understood the terms and conditions.
	+ **Emergency Contact Details**: Confirm that you understand the emergency contact information you provided.
* **E-Signature** (Page 5, Time, Optional): Sign this page to confirm that you have agreed to the terms and conditions.
* **Terms and Conditions Signature** (Page 6, Time, Optional): Sign this page to confirm that you have read and understood the terms and conditions.

## Tips
* Make sure to review each page carefully before proceeding to the next.
* Ensure that you have selected all the necessary options for waiver consent.
* Sign both the E-Signature and Terms and Conditions Signature pages to complete the form.
