# outdoor_camp_equipment_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for ordering outdoor camp equipment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): Enter the customer's name.
* **Email** (`customer_email`, email, required: false): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Enter the customer's contact number.
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the type of outdoor camp equipment being ordered (e.g. Camping Equipment, Outdoor Gear, etc.).
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required: false): Enter the number of items being ordered.
* **Price** (`price`, number, required: false): Enter the price of the ordered items.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date for delivery of the ordered items.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time for delivery of the ordered items.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes about the order.
* **Total Price** (`total_price`, select_one, required: false): Select the total price of the order from the options ($1-$10, $11-$20, $21-$30).
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required: false): Enter the assigned tool for the order.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, text, required: false): Enter the output file related to the order.
* **Category** (`category`, text, required: false): Enter the category of the ordered items.
* **Form Ids** (`form_ids`, text, required: false): Enter any form IDs related to the order.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required: false): Enter a description of the ordered items.
* **Category** (`category`, text, required: false): Enter the category of the ordered items. **Note:** This field is identical to the previous "Category" field. Please ensure that only one category is selected.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required: false): Enter the assigned tool for the order.
