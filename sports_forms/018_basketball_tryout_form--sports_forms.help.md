# Basketball Tryout Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for basketball tryouts, aiming to collect relevant information from athletes and their parents/guardians. It ensures the required details are provided for the tryout process.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: **Athlete Full Name**, **Date of Birth**, **Current Grade**, **Parent or Guardian Name**, **Contact Phone Number**, **Contact Email Address**, **Emergency Contact Name**, and **Emergency Contact Phone**.
2. Select the correct options for **I give Consent** and **Liability Waiver and Release**.
3. Confirm that you have completed the tryout fee payment by selecting **Yes, I am ready to pay**.
4. Select the correct option for **Have you completed the fee payment?**.
5. Finally, submit the form and follow the instructions for payment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Full Name**: Please enter the full name of the athlete as it appears on their official documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the athlete's date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YY).
* **Current Grade**: Enter the grade level of the athlete in their current school year.
* **Parent or Guardian Name**: Enter the name of the parent or guardian who is responsible for the athlete.
* **Contact Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian.
* **Contact Email Address**: Enter the email address of the parent or guardian.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **I give Consent**: Please select **I Consent** to confirm that you understand and agree to participate in the tryout.
* **Liability Waiver and Release**: Please select **I Agree** to confirm that you understand and agree to the waiver and release of liability.
* **Have you completed the fee payment?**: Select **Yes, I am ready to pay** to confirm that you have made the required payment.
* **Date Signed**: This field will be auto-populated with the date and time of submission.

Remember to submit the form carefully, as the accuracy and completeness of the information provided will impact the tryout process. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
