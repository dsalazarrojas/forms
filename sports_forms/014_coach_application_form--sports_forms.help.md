# Coach Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Coach Application Form is used for coaches or individuals interested in coaching to apply for a position or opportunity. This form helps us understand your background, experience, and availability to determine the best fit for your coaching skills.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by filling out the required fields marked as "required" in the form.
2. Enter your full legal name in the "Applicant name" field.
3. Provide a valid email address in the "Email address" field.
4. Enter your mobile phone number in the "Phone number" field.
5. Select the number of years of coaching experience in the "Coaching experience years" field.
6. List the teams or programs you have recently coached in the "Teams coached" field.
7. If applicable, list relevant licenses or certifications in the "Certifications" field.
8. Choose the preferred age group to coach in the "Preferred age group to coach" field.
9. Enter your availability in the "Availability" field.
10. If applicable, provide references with their names and contact information in the "References" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant name**: Your full legal name.
* **Email address**: A valid contact email address where we can reach you.
* **Phone number**: Your mobile phone number where we can contact you.
* **Coaching experience years**: The number of years you have coaching experience.
* **Teams coached**: List recent teams or programs you have coached.
* **Certifications**: List relevant licenses or certifications you hold.
* **Preferred age group to coach**: Select the age group you are most interested in coaching.
* **Availability**: Enter your availability in terms of days and times you are available to coach.
* **References**: Provide names and contact information of individuals who can vouch for your coaching skills and experience.
