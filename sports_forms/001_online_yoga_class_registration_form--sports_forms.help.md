# Online Yoga Class Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for online yoga class registration. It requires personal details such as name, contact information, and payment method, as well as course-related information like class type, duration, and schedule.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details:
	* Enter your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the respective fields.
	* Provide your **Email**, **Phone**, and **Payment Date** and **Payment Time**.
2. Confirm your **Class Type**, **Class Level**, **Instructor**, **Student Name**, and **Student Name** preferences.
3. Select your preferred **Payment Method** and **Student Payment Method**.
4. Choose the **Location** and **Class Schedule** that suit you best.
5. Enter the **Instructor Fee** and **Student Fee** (if applicable).
6. Add any **Additional Notes** or **Student Notes** for the instructor's reference.
7. Finally, confirm your **Submitter Name** and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Please enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date**: Choose the date of the class you want to attend.
* **Time**: Choose the time of the class you want to attend.
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments for the instructor.
* **Class Type**: Select the type of class you want to attend (e.g., Hatha, Vinyasa, etc.).
* **Class Level**: Select your class level (e.g., beginner, intermediate, advanced).
* **Instructor**: Confirm if you are an instructor.
* **Student Name**: Confirm if you are a student.
* **Instructor/Student**: Confirm your relationship with the instructor (e.g., yes, no).
* **Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method (e.g., credit card, PayPal, etc.).
* **Student Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method as a student (e.g., bank transfer, etc.).
* **Course Duration**: Choose the duration of the course you want to attend.
* **Class Schedule**: Choose the schedule of the class you want to attend (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, etc.).
* **Location**: Choose the location of the class you want to attend (e.g., online, physical studio, etc.).
* **Instructor Fee**: Enter the instructor's fee for the class.
* **Student Fee**: Enter the student fee for the class (if applicable).
* **Student Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments for the student.
* **Student Payment Method**: Confirm your student payment method.
* **Submitter Name**: Enter your name as the person submitting the form.
