# Athlete Mentorship Program Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Athlete Mentorship Program Feedback Survey is designed to gather valuable insights from athletes who have participated in the program. Your feedback will help us improve the program and provide better support for future participants.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the required fields, marked as * (asterisk). These fields are essential for providing a comprehensive overview of your experience in the program.
2. For multiple-choice fields, select the answer that best fits your experience.
3. For open-ended fields (e.g., "Additional Comments" and "Challenges Faced"), please provide detailed and specific feedback.
4. Review your answers before submitting to ensure they accurately reflect your experiences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Athlete Full Name:** This is the full name of the athlete, as per your identification. Please ensure it is correct and accurate.
* **Athlete ID Number (optional):** This is your student or team identification number. If you don't have one, please leave this field blank.
* **Sport:** Select the sport you primarily participated in the program.
* **Team Level:** Indicate the competition level of your team.
* **Your Role in Mentorship Program (Mentor/Mentee/Both):** Choose the role you played in the program.
* **How Long Have You Been in the Program:** Select the duration of your participation in the program.
* **How Often Do You Meet (Daily/Weekly/Bi-Weekly/Monthly/As Needed):** Select the frequency of your meetings in the program.
* **Meeting Format (In Person/Video Call/Phone Call/Text or Email/Mixed Format):** Choose the format of your meetings.
* **Mentor-Mentee Relationship Quality (Excellent/Good/Average/Below Average/Poor):** Rate the quality of your relationship with your mentor.
* **Communication Effectiveness (Excellent/Good/Average/Below Average/Poor):** Rate how well you communicate with your mentor.
* **Goal Setting Support (Excellent/Good/Average/Below Average/Poor):** Rate the support you received in setting goals.
* **Skill Development Support (Excellent/Good/Average/Below Average/Poor):** Rate the support for developing athletic skills.
* **Personal Growth Support (Excellent/Good/Average/Below Average/Poor):** Rate the support for personal development.
* **Academic Support (Excellent/Good/Average/Below Average/Not Applicable):** Rate the support for academic success.
* **Biggest Benefits of the Program:** Describe the most valuable aspect of the program for you.
* **Challenges Faced (optional):** If you experienced any difficulties, please describe them here.
* **Suggested Improvements (required):** Share your ideas on how the program could be improved.
* **Satisfaction with Mentor-Mentee Match (Very Satisfied/Satisfied/Neutral/Dissatisfied/Very Dissatisfied):** Rate your satisfaction with your mentor-mentee pairing.
* **Will You Continue in the Program (Definitely Yes/Probably Yes/Not Sure/Probably No/Definitely No):** Indicate your intention to continue participating in the program.
* **Would You Recommend to Other Athletes (Definitely Yes/Probably Yes/Not Sure/Probably No/Definitely No):** Share your recommendation to other athletes.
* **Additional Resources Needed (Select Multiple):** If there are any additional resources you feel would be helpful for future participants, select them here.
* **Additional Comments (optional):** Share any other feedback or suggestions you have.
* **May We Contact You for Follow-Up (Yes/No):** Indicate if you are willing to be contacted for further feedback.

By following this guide, you should be able to complete the survey efficiently and provide valuable feedback that will help improve the Athlete Mentorship Program for future participants.
