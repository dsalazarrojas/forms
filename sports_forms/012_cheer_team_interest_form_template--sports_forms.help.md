# Cheer Team Interest Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a template to help us get to know you better and determine whether you would be a good fit for our cheer team. Please fill out this form as completely and honestly as possible.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all the required fields. Each field has a hint to help you understand what to fill in.
2. Select relevant options for your skills and experience.
3. Choose the options that best match your availability and preferences.
4. Double-check that all fields are filled before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (1)
 * **First Name**: A text field to enter your first name.
 * Hint: Enter your first name, e.g., John.

### Last Name (2)
 * **Last Name**: A text field to enter your last name.
 * Hint: Enter your last name, e.g., Doe.

### Date of Birth (3)
 * **Date of Birth**: A date field to enter your date of birth.
 * Hint: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY, e.g., 01/01/1999.

### Age (4)
 * **Age**: A number field to enter your current age.
 * Hint: Enter your current age, e.g., 20.

### School Name (5)
 * **School Name**: A text field to enter your school name.
 * Hint: Enter the name of your school, e.g., ABC High School.

### Grade Level (6)
 * **Grade Level**: A select_one field to choose your grade level.
 * Hint: Select the grade level you are currently in, e.g., 9th Grade.

### Email Address (7)
 * **Email Address**: An email field to enter your email address.
 * Hint: Enter your email address, e.g., johndoe@example.com.

### Phone Number (8)
 * **Phone Number**: A text field to enter your phone number.
 * Hint: Enter your phone number, e.g., 123-456-7890.

### Parent or Guardian Name (9)
 * **Parent or Guardian Name**: A text field to enter your parent or guardian's name.
 * Hint: Enter the name of your parent or guardian, e.g., Jane Doe.

### Parent or Guardian Phone (10)
 * **Parent or Guardian Phone**: A text field to enter your parent or guardian's phone number.
 * Hint: Enter your parent or guardian's phone number, e.g., 123-456-7890.

### Cheerleading Experience (11)
 * **Cheerleading Experience**: A select_one field to choose your cheerleading experience level.
 * Hint: Select the level of your cheerleading experience, e.g., 3-4 years.

### Previous Team (12)
 * **Previous Team**: A text field to enter the name of your previous team if applicable.
 * Hint: Enter the name of your previous team, e.g., ABC Cheer Team.

### Position Interest (13)
 * **Position Interest**: A select_multiple field to choose the positions you are interested in.
 * Hint: Select all the positions you are interested in, e.g., Flyer, Base.

### Skills You Can Perform (14)
 * **Skills You Can Perform**: A select_multiple field to choose the skills you can perform.
 * Hint: Select all the skills you can perform, e.g., Cartwheel, Back handspring.

### Tumbling Level (15)
 * **Tumbling Level**: A select_one field to choose your tumbling level.
 * Hint: Select your current tumbling level, e.g., Intermediate - Back handspring.

### Dance Experience (16)
 * **Dance Experience**: A select_one field to choose your dance experience level.
 * Hint: Select the level of your dance experience, e.g., 4+ years training.

### Availability (17)
 * **Availability**: A select_multiple field to choose your practice availability.
 * Hint: Select all the days and times you are available for practice.

### Competition Availability (18)
 * **Competition Availability**: A select_one field to choose your competition availability.
 * Hint: Select how often you can attend competitions.

### Medical Conditions (19)
 * **Medical Conditions**: A text field to enter any medical conditions.
 * Hint: Enter any medical conditions we should know about, e.g., knee injury.

### Emergency Contact (20)
 * **Emergency Contact**: A text field to enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
 * Hint: Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact, e.g., Jane Doe 123-456-7890.

### Why Do You Want to Join (21)
 * **Why Do You Want to Join**: A text field to enter why you want to join the team.
 * Hint: Enter why you want to join the team, e.g., I love cheerleading and want to improve my skills.

### Goals (22)
 * **Goals**: A text field to enter your goals.
 * Hint: Enter your goals, e.g., To improve my jumping ability.

### Team Commitment (23)
 * **Team Commitment**: A select_one field to choose your understanding of the team commitment.
 * Hint: Select whether you understand the time commitment required.

### Code of Conduct (24)
 * **Code of Conduct**: A select_one field to choose whether you agree to the team code of conduct.
 * Hint: Select whether you agree to follow the team code of conduct.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form carefully and honestly.
- Make sure to double-check all fields before submitting.
- If you have any questions, feel free to ask.
