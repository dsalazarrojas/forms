# Volleyball Skills Clinic Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from players who want to register for the Volleyball Skills Clinic. The form helps us to get to know you better, understand your preferences, and prepare for the clinic.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to select the correct session time that suits you.
3. Provide your consent to participate.
4. Enter your contact details, including your contact number and email.
5. Enter your player information, including your name, date of birth, height, and weight.
6. Enter any medical information we should know about you.
7. Enter your coach information, including their name, phone number, and email.
8. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Player Details**: Please enter your name and any other information you'd like to share with us. (Required: False)
* **Session Preferences**: Please select the time slot that you'd like to attend the clinic. You can choose from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. (Required: False)
* **Player Consent**: Please indicate whether you give your consent to participate in the clinic. (Required: False)
* **Contact Details**: Please enter your contact number and email so we can reach out to you. (Required: False)
* **School**: Please enter the name of your school. (Required: False)
* **Grade Level**: Please enter your grade level. (Required: False)
* **Coach Information**: Please enter your coach's name, phone number, and email. (Required: False)
* **Coach Name**: Please enter your coach's name. (Required: False)
* **Coach Phone**: Please enter your coach's phone number. (Required: False)
* **Coach Email**: Please enter your coach's email. (Required: False)
* **Coach Address**: Please enter your coach's address. (Required: False)
* **Player Information**: Please enter any additional information about you. (Required: False)
* **Player Name**: Please enter your name. (Required: False)
* **Player Date of Birth**: Please enter your date of birth. (Required: False)
* **Player Height**: Please enter your height. (Required: False)
* **Player Weight**: Please enter your weight. (Required: False)
* **Player Gender**: Please enter your gender. (Required: False)
* **Player Phone**: Please enter your phone number. (Required: False)
* **Player Email**: Please enter your email. (Required: False)
* **Player Medical Info**: Please enter any medical information we should know about you. (Required: False)
* **Player Medical Info 2**: Please enter any additional medical information. (Required: False)
* **Player Medical Info 3**: Please enter any further medical information. (Required: False)
* **Player Medical Info 4**: Please enter any other medical information. (Required: False)

Note: All fields are optional, except for Player Consent, which is required to participate in the clinic.
