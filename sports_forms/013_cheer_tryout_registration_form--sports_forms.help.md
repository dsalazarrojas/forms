# Cheer Tryout Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cheer Tryout Registration Form is designed for participants to register for tryouts with a cheer team. It gathers essential information about the participant's personal details, medical history, cheer experience, and skills. This form ensures that participants understand their commitment to the team's code of conduct, medical release, and liability waiver.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal details, including your first name, last name, date of birth, and age.
2. Provide your school name and grade level.
3. Enter your contact information, including email address and phone number.
4. Select your tryout date from the available dates.
5. Choose your years of cheer experience.
6. List your tumbling skills and stunting skills.
7. Select your jump skills.
8. Choose your T-Shirt size.
9. If applicable, list any medical conditions and current medications.
10. Enter your insurance provider (if applicable).
11. Provide the name and phone number of your emergency contact person.
12. Confirm that you authorize emergency medical treatment.
13. Select whether you accept the liability waiver and code of conduct.
14. Confirm your registration fee payment status.
15. Indicate how you heard about tryouts.
16. Provide additional comments or feedback (if any).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Participant First Name (<name> First Name, <type> text, required)
Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.

### 2. Participant Last Name (<name> Last Name, <type> text, required)
Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.

### 3. Date of Birth (<name> Date of Birth, <type> date, required)
Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.

### 4. Age (<name> Age, <type> number, required)
Enter your current age.

### 5. Gender (<name> Gender, <type> select_one, required)
Select your gender identification from the given options.

### 6. School Name (<name> School Name, <type> text, required)
Enter the name of your school.

### 7. Grade Level (<name> Grade Level, <type> select_one, required)
Select your current grade level from the given options.

### 8. Email Address (<name> Email Address, <type> email, required)
Enter your email address.

### 9. Phone Number (<name> Phone Number, <type> text, required)
Enter your phone number.

### 10. Parent or Guardian Name (<name> Parent or Guardian Name, <type> text, required)
Enter the full name of your parent or guardian.

### 11. Parent or Guardian Email (<name> Parent or Guardian Email, <type> email, required)
Enter the email address of your parent or guardian.

### 12. Parent or Guardian Phone (<name> Parent or Guardian Phone, <type> text, required)
Enter the phone number of your parent or guardian.

### 13. Tryout Date (<name> Tryout Date, <type> select_one, required)
Select the tryout date you intend to attend from the given options.

### 14. Years of Cheer Experience (<name> Years of Cheer Experience, <type> select_one, required)
Select the number of years you've been cheering.

### 15. Previous Team Name (<name> Previous Team Name, <type> text, required)
Enter the name of your previous team (if applicable).

### 16. Tumbling Skills (<name> Tumbling Skills, <type> select_multiple, required)
Select all tumbling skills that you can perform.

### 17. Stunting Experience (<name> Stunting Experience, <type> select_multiple, required)
Select all stunting skills you have experience with.

### 18. Jump Skills (<name> Jump Skills, <type> select_multiple, required)
Select all jump skills you have experience with.

### 19. T-Shirt Size (<name> T-Shirt Size, <type> select_one, required)
Select your T-Shirt size for tryout events.

### 20. Medical Conditions or Allergies (<name> Medical Conditions or Allergies, <type> text, required)
List any medical conditions or allergies you have.

### 21. Current Medications (<name> Current Medications, <type> text, required)
List any current medications you're taking.

### 22. Insurance Provider (<name> Insurance Provider, <type> text, required)
Enter the name of your insurance provider (if applicable).

### 23. Emergency Contact Name (<name> Emergency Contact Name, <type> text, required)
Enter the full name of your emergency contact person.

### 24. Emergency Contact Phone (<name> Emergency Contact Phone, <type> text, required)
Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.

### 25. Medical Release (<name> Medical Release, <type> select_one, required)
Confirm that you authorize emergency medical treatment.

### 26. Liability Waiver (<name> Liability Waiver, <type> select_one, required)
Confirm that you accept the liability waiver.

### 27. Code of Conduct (<name> Code of Conduct, <type> select_one, required)
Confirm that you agree to follow the team conduct rules.

### 28. Registration Fee Payment Status (<name> Registration Fee Payment, <type> select_one, required)
Confirm your registration fee payment status.

### 29. How Did You Hear About Tryouts (<name> How Did You Hear About Tryouts, <type> select_one, required)
Select how you heard about tryouts.

### 30. Additional Comments (<name> Additional Comments, <type> text, required)
Enter any additional comments or feedback.

Please ensure that you fill out all required fields and provide accurate information to facilitate a smooth and efficient tryout process.
