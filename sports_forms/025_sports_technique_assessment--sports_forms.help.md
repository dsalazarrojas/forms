# Sports Technique Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Sports Technique Assessment form is designed to help coaches and trainers evaluate an athlete's skills and performance in a specific sport. This form helps to gather detailed information about the athlete's technique, progress, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the athlete's name in the "Athlete Name" field.
2. Select the sport the athlete competes in from the "Sport" dropdown menu.
3. If necessary, enter additional comments or notes about the athlete's progress or technique in the "Coach Comments" field.
4. Choose an assessment type from the "Assessment Type" dropdown menu (if applicable).
5. Enter the date you assessed the athlete in the "Date Assessed" field.
6. If required, enter the time spent observing or coaching the athlete in the "Time Spent" field.
7. Enter the athlete's progress or evaluation in the "Athlete Progress" field (in date format).
8. Enter the athlete's email address in the "Email" field.
9. Enter the athlete's phone number in the "Phone" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Name**: Enter the athlete's full name, as it appears on their ID, passport, or official documents.
* **Sport**: Select the sport the athlete competes in from the list of options provided. This helps us understand the athlete's specific skills and performance.
* **Coach Comments**: If you have any additional comments or observations about the athlete's progress or technique, enter them here. This is an optional field, but it can be helpful for providing more detailed feedback.
* **Assessment Type**: Choose the type of assessment that best fits the evaluation you are providing. This is an optional field, but it helps to categorize and track different types of assessments.
* **Date Assessed**: Enter the date you assessed the athlete. This helps to keep track of when the assessment was conducted.
* **Time Spent**: If you spent a specific amount of time observing or coaching the athlete, enter it here. This helps to understand the context and scope of the assessment.
* **Athlete Progress**: Enter the athlete's progress or evaluation in date format. This is where you can record any notable events, dates, or milestones in the athlete's career or performance.
* **Email**: Enter the athlete's email address. This is an optional field, but it can be helpful for keeping in touch or sending reminders.
* **Phone**: Enter the athlete's phone number. This is an optional field, but it can be helpful for scheduling follow-up meetings or calls.
* **Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the athlete's progress or technique. This is an optional field, but it can be helpful for referencing later.
