# Lawn Bowls Participation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a participation form for lawn bowls events or sessions, designed to collect essential information from potential participants.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with your accurate and up-to-date information:
	* Participant Name
	* Email Address
	* Phone Number
2. Choose your session preferences by selecting one or more options that suit your interests.
3. If applicable, indicate your skills, experience, and formation related to lawn bowls.
4. Provide your consent to participate in the selected sessions.
5. Enter any additional notes or comments about the session in the Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted for session updates and communications.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted for session updates and emergencies.
* **Session Preferences** (`session_preferences`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more sessions you are interested in, and we will tailor the sessions accordingly.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand and agree to the terms and conditions of the session.
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, note, optional): Enter any additional details about yourself that may be helpful for the organizers.
* **Participant Skills** (`participant_skills`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if you have any relevant skills or experience related to lawn bowls.
* **Participant Experience** (`participant_experience`, select_multiple, optional): Select any relevant experience you have in lawn bowls.
* **Participant Formation** (`participant_formation`, select_multiple, optional): Select any formations you are familiar with in lawn bowls.
* **Session Preferences** (`session_preferences_formation`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more preferences for the sessions, such as day, time, or venue.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the session.
