# Sports Training Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a liability waiver that requires individuals to provide their personal details and acknowledge their understanding of the risks and responsibilities associated with participating in sports training programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the Member's Name field with your name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
2. Enter your Member's Email address in the Member's Email field. This is the email address we will use to communicate with you.
3. Enter your Member's Phone number in the Member's Phone field. This is the phone number we will use to contact you in case of emergencies or important updates.
4. Fill out the Member's Address field with your current address. This is the address we will use to provide you with updates or instructions related to the training.
5. Carefully read and understand the terms of this waiver. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Member's Name (Text, Required)**: Please fill out your name exactly as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Member's Email (Email, Required)**: Enter your email address that we will use to communicate with you about the training.
* **Member's Phone (Text, Required)**: Enter your phone number that we will use to contact you in case of emergencies or important updates.
* **Member's Address (Text, Optional)**: Please fill out your current address, including your street address, city, state, and zip code. This field is optional, but it will help us provide you with more accurate and timely updates.
* **Note**: Please review this waiver carefully and acknowledge your understanding of the risks and responsibilities associated with participating in the sports training program. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us before proceeding.
