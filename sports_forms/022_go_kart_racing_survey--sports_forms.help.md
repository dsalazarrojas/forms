# Go Kart Racing Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from go-kart racing facility users on the staff friendliness, cleanliness, and safety of the facility. Your input will help us improve our services and create a better experience for you and other users.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question according to your recent experience with a go-kart racing facility.
2. Select the relevant options from the provided lists for each question.
3. Make sure to submit the form when you have finished answering all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, text, optional): Please briefly describe your recent experience with a go-kart racing facility.
* **Go Kart Racing Experience** (`go_kart_racing_experience`, number, optional): How many years of experience do you have with go-kart racing?
* **Staff Friendliness** (`staff_friendlyness`, select_one, required): Rate the friendliness of the staff you interacted with. Choose between Very friendly, Average, and Not friendly at all.
* **Facilities Cleanliness** (`facilities_citeness`, select_multiple, optional): Rate the cleanliness of the facilities. Choose between Excellent, Good, and Fair. Note: If you have any feedback on specific areas of the facility, please include it in the introduction.
* **Facilities Safety** (`facilities_safety`, select_multiple, optional): Rate the safety of the facilities. Choose between Excellent, Good, and Fair. Note: If you have any feedback on specific safety concerns, please include it in the introduction.
* **Facilities Price** (`facilities_price`, number, optional): If applicable, please report any prices you were charged for services or equipment rental.

Note: Please keep in mind that this form is for your opinion and feedback. Be honest and thorough in your answers, and remember to submit the form when you have finished answering all questions.
