# Sports Psychology Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Psychology Referral Form is designed to collect information from athletes, helping sports psychologists or coaches understand their needs and provide guidance or referrals as needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the athlete's contact information, including their name and email address.
2. Provide the athlete's school, club, or practice name.
3. Specify the referral reasons for seeking sports psychology support.
4. Choose the type of referral (School/Club, Practice, or Other).
5. Enter the contact person's name.
6. Enter the follow-up date and time.
7. Specify the follow-up person.
8. Add any additional comments or next steps.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Athlete Name**: Enter the athlete's full name.
* **Contact Information**: Provide the athlete's contact information, including their email address.
* **School/Club/Practice Name**: Enter the name of the athlete's school, club, or practice.
* **Referral Reasons**: Describe the reasons for seeking sports psychology support.
* **Referral Type**: Choose one or more of the following:
	+ School/Club
	+ Practice
	+ Other
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person athletes will contact for follow-up.
* **Follow-up Date**: Enter the date for the follow-up meeting or call.
* **Follow-up Time**: Enter the time for the follow-up meeting or call.
* **Follow-up Person**: Enter the name of the person who will follow up with the athlete.
* **Next Steps**: Describe any next steps or actions needed for the athlete.
* **Referral Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes for the follow-up.
* **Follow-up Notes**: Add any final follow-up notes or comments.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or information.
* **Referral Form Comments 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8**: These fields are for adding further comments or notes.
* **Submission**: Use this field to submit the form.

Note: Each field is optional unless marked as required.
