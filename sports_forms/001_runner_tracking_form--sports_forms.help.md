# runner-tracking-form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to track running activities, including user information, workout details, and follow-up schedules.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Start by filling out the user information page, which includes the user's name and contact information.
2. Select the type of activity (e.g. Running, Walking, Cycling) and indicate where the activity was performed (e.g. Home, Gym, Outdoors).
3. Enter the duration of the workout in minutes.
4. Input the pace of the workout in minutes per mile or kilometer.
5. Enter the distance covered during the workout.
6. Complete the follow-up schedule by selecting the follow-up duration (e.g. 30, 60, 90 minutes) and the next follow-up date.
7. Select the representative who will follow up with the client (e.g. Rep 1, Rep 2, Rep 3).
8. Finalize the form by adding any additional notes or instructions for the representative to follow up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info**: Provide your name and contact information (e.g., email, phone number) so we can get in touch with you.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the workout, in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the workout in HH:MM format.
* **Distance**: Enter the distance covered during the workout in miles or kilometers.
* **Pace**: Enter the pace of the workout in minutes per mile or kilometer.
* **Runner-type**: Select the type of activity performed (e.g. Running, Walking, Cycling).
* **Location**: Select where the activity was performed (e.g. Home, Gym, Outdoors).
* **Workout-duration**: Select the duration of the workout in minutes.
* **Running-notes**: Enter any notes or comments about the workout.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for further communication.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for quick contact.
* **Workout-frequency**: Select the frequency of your workouts (e.g. 1x, 2x, 3x).
* **Customer-notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the client.
* **Follow-up-schedule**: Select the follow-up schedule (e.g. 30, 60, 90 minutes) for the representative to follow up.
* **Follow-up-time**: Enter the follow-up time in HH:MM format.
* **Follow-up-notes**: Enter any additional notes or instructions for the representative to follow up.
* **Next-follow-up**: Select the next follow-up date.
* **Assigned-rep**: Select the representative who will follow up with the client.
* **Follow-up-rep**: Select the representative who will follow up with the client (same as Assigned-rep).
* **Follow-up-date**: Enter the follow-up date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Follow-up-duration**: Select the follow-up duration in minutes.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or instructions for the representative to follow up.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for quick contact.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for further communication.
* **Follow-up-rep**: Select the representative who will follow up with the client (same as Assigned-rep).
* **Follow-up-date**: Enter the follow-up date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Next-follow-up**: Enter the next follow-up date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Workout-duration**: Select the workout duration in minutes.
