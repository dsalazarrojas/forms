# Sport Class Registration Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information from participants for the sport class event. Please ensure you only register once for this event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Enter your phone number.
4. Enter your address.
5. Select the sport you are interested in.
6. Choose the class you are registering for.
7. Select the date and time you registered for the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your address.
* **Sport** (`sport`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the sport you are interested in.
	+ Select 'Yes' to confirm you are registered for this sport.
* **Class** (`class`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the class you are registering for.
	+ Select 'Yes' to confirm you are registered for this class.
* **Registered Date** (`registered_date`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date you registered for the event.
* **Registered Time** (`registered_time`, `time`, required/optional): Select the time you registered for the event.

## Tips
Please ensure you only register once for this event. If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact the event organizers.
