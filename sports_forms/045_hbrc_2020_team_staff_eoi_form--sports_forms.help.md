# HBRC 2020 Team Staff EOI Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HBRC 2020 Team Staff EOI Form is used to nominate or apply for staff positions in a team for the 2020 edition of the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact number in the "Coach Name" and "Contact Number" fields, respectively.
2. Fill in your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Describe your experience in the "Experience" field, specifying the number of years you have been playing or coaching rugby.
4. Select your position on the team from the options provided in the "Position" field (e.g. Head Coach, Assistant Coach, Manager).
5. Choose your team from the options provided in the "Team" field (e.g. Team A, Team B, Team C).
6. Indicate whether you have referee certification by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Referee Certification" field.
7. If you have referee certification, enter the date you obtained it in the "Referee Cert Date" field.
8. Select your level of referee certification from the options in the "Referee Cert Level" field (e.g. Level 1, Level 2, Level 3).
9. Specify the status of your referee certification in the "Referee Cert Status" field (e.g. Active, Inactive, Expired).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Coach Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number that you can be reached at.
* **Email**: Enter your email address that you can be reached at.
* **Experience**: Specify the number of years you have been playing or coaching rugby.
* **Position**: Choose your role within the team from the options provided.
* **Team**: Select the team you are nominating or applying for.
* **Referee Certification**: Indicate whether you have referee certification. Select "True" if you have it, "False" otherwise.
* **Referee Cert Date**: If you have referee certification, enter the date you obtained it.
* **Referee Cert Level**: If you have referee certification, select your level of certification from the options provided.
* **Referee Cert Status**: If you have referee certification, specify its status from the options provided.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
- Double-check that all fields are correct before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, consult with the event organizers or staff.
