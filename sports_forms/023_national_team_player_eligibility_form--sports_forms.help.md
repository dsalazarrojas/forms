# National Team Player Eligibility Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to help national teams collect player information for eligibility checks and team management.

The form is used to gather data about a player's name, team affiliation, sport, date of birth, nationality, sport experience, sport level, and emergency contact information.

It ensures that all necessary data is captured for a thorough assessment of the player's eligibility to represent the national team.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill in the requested fields with the relevant information about the player. Each section should be filled out as per the guidelines below:

- Fill in the `player_name` field with the player's name as it appears on their official identification documents.
- Enter the `team_id` field with the team the player is currently affiliated with or plans to be affiliated with.
- Select the country of origin of the player in the `nationality_id` field.
- Enter the player's date of birth in the `date_of_birth` field in the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
- Choose the player's sport experience level from the options provided in the `sport_experience` field.
- Select all relevant sport levels from the options provided in the `sport_level` field.
- Enter the name and contact information of the player's emergency contact person in the `emergency_contact_person` field.
- Describe the relationship between the player and their emergency contact person in the `emergency_contact_relationship` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enter player name (player_name)**: Enter the player's name as it appears on their official identification documents.
* **Team (team_id)**: Enter the team the player is currently affiliated with or plans to be affiliated with.
* **Sport (sport_id)**: Select the sport the player competes in.
* **Date of birth (date_of_birth)**: Enter the player's date of birth in the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Nationality (nationality_id)**: Select the player's country of origin.
* **Sport experience (sport_experience)**: Choose the player's sport experience level.
* **Sport level (sport_level)**: Select all relevant sport levels the player has achieved.
* **Emergency contact person (emergency_contact_person)**: Enter the name of the player's emergency contact person.
* **Relationship with emergency contact (emergency_contact_relationship)**: Describe the relationship between the player and their emergency contact person.

## Tips

- Make sure to double-check all entered information for accuracy.
- If a field is required, it is essential to fill it out.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authorities for guidance.
