# we_quality_u_can_trust_asking_for_donation_for_mask_food_face_shields_for_our_health_care - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a donation request form for mask and food face shields for health care, specifically for nursing homes and individuals in need. It is used to collect information for categorization and description purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter a title for the donation in the **Donation For Mask Food Face Shields For Our Health Care** field.
2. Enter a unique identifier for the form in the **Form Id** field.
3. Enter a description of the donation in the **We @ Quality U Can Trust Asking for Donation For Mask, Food for them and for Food Banks ,Face Shields For Our Health Care & Nursing Homes we can get Many of them and we can get Mask for the littles ones** field.
4. Choose a category for the donation from the **Category** dropdown menu:
	* **Sports Forms**
	* **Other**
5. (Optional) Enter a description of the donation in the **Description** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Donation For Mask Food Face Shields For Our Health Care** (`mask_food_face_shields`, text, optional)
This is the title of the donation. Enter a brief description of what you are requesting, e.g. "Masks for nursing homes" or "Food face shields for schools".

### **Form Id** (`form_id`, number, optional)
This is a unique identifier for the form. Enter a number to distinguish your form from others.

### **We @ Quality U Can Trust Asking for Donation For Mask, Food for them and for Food Banks ,Face Shields For Our Health Care & Nursing Homes we can get Many of them and we can get Mask for the littles ones** (`form_description`, text, optional)
This is an optional description field. Enter a more detailed description of the donation, e.g. "We are requesting masks for nursing homes because they are in need".

### **Category** (`form_category`, select_one, optional)
This field allows you to categorize the donation. Choose the relevant category:
	* **Sports Forms**
	* **Other**

### **Description** (text, optional)
This is an optional description of the donation. Enter a brief description, e.g. "Masks and food face shields for nursing homes".
