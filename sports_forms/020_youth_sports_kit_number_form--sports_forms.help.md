# Youth Sports Kit Number Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Sports Kit Number Form is used to track and manage youth sports kits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out your personal details: Provide your first name and last name in the designated fields.
* Enter your birth date: Select your birth date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* Identify your sport and kit: Select the sport and kit type for which you are updating the form.
* Enter the kit details: Enter the kit number and the quantity of kits you need.
* Confirm allocation: Select whether the kits are allocated or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Birth Date**: Enter your birth date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Sport ID**: Enter the unique ID for the sport you are participating in.
* **Kit ID**: Enter the unique ID for the kit you are updating.
* **Kit Type**: Select the type of kit you are updating (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Kit Number**: Enter the kit number as assigned by the system.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of kits you need.
* **Allocated**: Select whether the kits are allocated (1) or not (0).

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure correct tracking and management of youth sports kits.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with the relevant administrator or staff member.
* This form should not be completed multiple times for the same kit, as it can lead to duplicate records and errors.
