# Player Eligibility Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Player Eligibility Form is designed to help verify player eligibility for sports teams or other sports-related activities. This form collects personal and contact information from players to assess their eligibility for participation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **Player Info** (Page 1).
2. Provide your **Personal Info** (Page 2).
3. Enter your **Contact Info** (Page 3).
4. Share your **Health History** (Page 4).
5. Describe your **Sports History** (Page 5).
6. Read and agree to our **Contact And Consent** (Page 6).
7. Review our **Contact And Consent Terms** (Page 7).
8. Provide any required **Medical Info** (Page 8).
9. Share your **Sports History Info** (Page 9).
10. Enter your **Address** (Page 10).
11. Add a **Contact Number** (Page 11).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Info**: Enter your full name, player ID (if applicable), and any other relevant player details. (Page 1)
* **Personal Info**: Provide your full name, date of birth, and any other personal details that may be relevant to your eligibility. (Page 2)
* **Contact Info**: Enter your name, email, and other contact details that we can use to reach you. (Page 3)
* **Health History**: Share any medical conditions, allergies, or other health concerns that may impact your participation. (Page 4)
* **Sports History**: Describe your past sports experience, including any injuries or medical issues. (Page 5)
* **Contact And Consent**: Acknowledge that you agree to be contacted by our team regarding your eligibility. (Page 6)
* **Contact And Consent Terms**: Review and agree to the terms of our contact and consent policy. (Page 7)
* **Medical Info**: Provide any medical documentation required by our team. (Page 8)
* **Sports History Info**: Share further details about your sports experience, if required. (Page 9)
* **Address**: Enter your current address for our records. (Page 10)
* **Contact Number**: Add a contact number that we can reach you at. (Page 11)
