# Little League Player Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register players for a local Little League. Please fill out the required information accurately to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in its entirety, including all required fields.
2. Ensure all information is accurate and up-to-date.
3. Submit the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Player Info** (1, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the player, if desired.
* **Player Name** (2, text, optional): Enter the player's name.
* **Player Age** (3, number, optional): Enter the player's age.
* **Player Phone** (4, text, optional): Enter the player's phone number.
* **Player Email** (5, email, optional): Enter the player's email address.
* **League Info** (6, text, optional): Enter any additional league information.
* **Team Name** (7, text, optional): Enter the team name.
* **Player Position** (8, text, optional): Enter the player's position.
* **Player Number** (9, text, optional): Enter the player's number.
* **Parent Permission** (10, select_one, optional): Choose whether the parent has given permission for the player to participate.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are completed accurately.
* If a field is not required, you may leave it blank, but be aware that missing information may cause delays in the registration process.
