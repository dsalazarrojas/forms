# E Sport Team Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help potential team members apply for membership in an e-sports team. It collects information about the team, the tournament they are applying for, and team members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your team's name in the "Team Name" field.
2. Enter the name of the tournament you are applying for in the "tournament_name" field.
3. List the members of your team in the "Team Members" field.
4. If your team has a Discord server, provide the link in the "Discord Link" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of your team. This is the name that will be displayed to the team management and other teams.
* **Tournament Name** (`tournament_name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the tournament you are applying for. This will help the team management to understand which tournament you are applying for.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, Text, Required): List all members of your team, including their usernames, Discord IDs, or any other identifier that will help the team management to verify your team's membership.
* **Discord Link** (`discord_link`, Text, Optional): If your team has a Discord server, provide the link so that the team management can easily invite and communicate with your team members.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate information about your team and its members.
- If you are applying for a specific tournament, ensure that your team is eligible for it.
- If you are applying for multiple teams, fill out one form per team.
- You can always contact the team management if you have any questions or need further clarification on the application process.
