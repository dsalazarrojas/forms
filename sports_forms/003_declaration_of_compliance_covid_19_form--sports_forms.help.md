# Declaration Of Compliance Covid 19 Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information on team members' vaccination status for COVID-19.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Complete all required fields marked with a * (asterisk)**.
2. Fill in the **Team ID**, which is a unique identifier for your team.
3. Enter the **Team Name**, which should match your team's official name.
4. Enter your **Employee ID** and **Employee Name** to uniquely identify yourself.
5. If you have been vaccinated, enter the **Date of Vaccination** in the format dd/mm/yyyy.
6. If applicable, enter the **Time of Vaccination**, e.g., 08:00 or 12:00.
7. Select the **Vaccination Status** to indicate whether you are vaccinated or not.
8. If vaccinated, enter the **Vaccine Name** and **Vaccine Dose Number**.
9. For vaccinated individuals, select the **Vaccine Dose Status** to indicate whether you have completed, are in progress, or not started with your vaccination doses.
10. Add any **Additional Comments** about your vaccination status if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Team ID (1)
* Team ID: *required* - Unique identifier for your team.
### Team Name (2)
* Team Name: *required* - Team name as per official records.
### Employee ID (3)
* Employee ID: *required* - Unique identifier for yourself.
### Employee Name (4)
* Employee Name: *required* - Your name as per official records.
### Date of Vaccination (5)
* Date of Vaccination: *required* - Date when you were vaccinated, in dd/mm/yyyy format.
### Time of Vaccination (6)
* Time of Vaccination: *optional* - Time when you were vaccinated, e.g., 08:00 or 12:00.
### Vaccination Status (7)
* Vaccination Status: *required* - Select 'True' if vaccinated or 'False' if not.
### Vaccine Name (8)
* Vaccine Name: *required if vaccinated* - Name of the vaccine you took.
### Vaccine Dose Number (9)
* Vaccine Dose Number: *required if vaccinated* - Number of doses you have taken.
### Vaccine Dose Status (10)
* Vaccine Dose Status: *required if vaccinated* - Select 'Completed', 'In Progress', or 'Not Started'.
### Additional Comments (11)
* Additional Comments: *optional* - Any additional comments about your vaccination status.
