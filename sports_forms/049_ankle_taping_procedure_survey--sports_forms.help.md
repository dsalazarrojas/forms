## Purpose
The "Ankle Taping Procedure Survey" is a tool used to collect feedback and gather information about the effectiveness and usability of ankle taping procedures. This form is designed to be completed by athletes who have undergone ankle taping for a specific procedure, and is intended to help practitioners refine their taping techniques and identify areas for improvement.

### How To Complete This Form

1. **Athlete Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification or medical records.
2. **Athlete ID Number**: If you have a unique ID number, please enter it. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
3. **Date of Taping Service**: Enter the date of the taping procedure you are reporting on.
4. **Time of Service**: If applicable, enter the time of the taping procedure.
5. **Practitioner Name**: Enter the name of the practitioner who performed the taping procedure.
6. **Practitioner Role**: Select the role of the practitioner who performed the taping procedure (e.g. Athletic Trainer, Physical Therapist, Sports Medicine Physician, etc.).
7. **Ankle Taped**: Select the ankle that was taped (e.g. Left Ankle, Right Ankle, Both Ankles).
8. **Reason for Taping**: Select the reason why the ankle was taped (e.g. Injury Prevention, Acute Injury Support, etc.).
9. **Type of Injury**: If applicable, select the type of injury that was treated (e.g. Ankle Sprain, Strain, etc.).
10. **Injury Grade**: If applicable, select the grade of the injury (e.g. Grade 1 - Mild, Grade 2 - Moderate, etc.).
11. **Taping Method Used**: Select the method used for taping the ankle (e.g. Closed Basket Weave, Open Basket Weave, etc.).
12. **Type of Tape Used**: Select the type of tape used for taping (e.g. Rigid Athletic Tape, Elastic Athletic Tape, etc.).
13. **Tape Brand**: If known, select the brand of tape used.
14. **Pre-Tape or Underwrap Used**: Select if pre-tape or underwrap was used.
15. **Skin Preparation**: Select all skin preparation methods used (e.g. Shaved, Cleaned with Alcohol, etc.).
16. **Comfort Rating During Application**: Rate your comfort level during the application of the tape (e.g. 1-10 scale).
17. **Fit Rating**: Rate how well the tape fit during the procedure (e.g. Excellent, Good, etc.).
18. **Support Level**: Select the level of support provided by the taping (e.g. Excellent Support, Good Support, etc.).
19. **Mobility Rating**: Rate how the taping affected your mobility (e.g. No Restriction, Slight Restriction, etc.).
20. **Activity Performed**: Select the activity you performed while taped (e.g. Practice, Competition, etc.).
21. **Duration of Activity**: If applicable, enter the duration of the activity in minutes.
22. **Tape Performance During Activity**: Rate how the tape performed during the activity (e.g. Stayed Secure, Minor Loosening, etc.).
23. **Skin Reaction**: Select any skin reaction experienced.
24. **Pain Level During Activity**: Rate your pain level during the activity (e.g. 0-10 scale).
25. **Tape Removal**: Select how the tape was removed (e.g. Self-Removed, Practitioner Removed, etc.).
26. **Removal Difficulty**: Rate the difficulty of tape removal (e.g. Very Easy, Easy, etc.).
27. **Post-Removal Skin Condition**: Select the skin condition after tape removal (e.g. Normal, Mild Redness, etc.).
28. **Would Repeat**: Select if you would use this taping method again.
29. **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the taping method.
30. **Complications Experienced**: Select any complications experienced during the taping procedure.
31. **Complications Details**: If any complications were experienced, describe them.
32. **Improvement Suggestions**: Suggest any improvements for the taping method.
33. **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional feedback or comments about the taping procedure.
