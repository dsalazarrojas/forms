# Girl Scout Event Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from Girl Scout leaders and participants after an event, to help the organization gauge its success and make improvements for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the event name in the `event_name` field.
2. Select the date and time of the event in the `event_date` and `event_time` fields, respectively.
3. Enter the location of the event in the `event_location` field.
4. Enter any additional notes about the event in the `event_notes` field.
5. List the troop leaders who were present during the event in the `troop_leaders_present` field.
6. Enter your satisfaction level with the event in the `event_satisfaction` field.
7. Enter any recommendations for future events in the `event_recommendation` field.
8. Enter any additional comments about the event in the `event_comments` field.
9. Select the date and time of the evaluation in the `evaluation_date` and `evaluation_time` fields, respectively.
10. Enter any additional notes about the evaluation in the `evaluation_notes` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event that took place. This will help us identify the event and associate it with your feedback.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the day when the event took place, in the format HH:MM.
* **Event Location**: Enter the location where the event was held.
* **Event Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the event that you would like to share.
* **Troop Leaders Present**: List the troop leaders who were present during the event.
* **Event Satisfaction**: Enter your level of satisfaction with the event, e.g. "Very Satisfied", "Satisfied", "Neutral", "Dissatisfied", or "Very Dissatisfied".
* **Event Recommendation**: Enter any recommendations for future events that you think would be helpful.
* **Event Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the event.
* **Evaluation Date**: Select the date of this evaluation.
* **Evaluation Time**: Select the time when you are completing this evaluation.
* **Evaluation Notes**: Enter any additional notes about this evaluation.
* **Event Comments (Additional)**: Enter any additional comments about the event.
* **Troop Leaders Present (List)**: Enter the names of the troop leaders who were present during the event.
* **Event Satisfaction (Text)**: Enter your level of satisfaction with the event.
