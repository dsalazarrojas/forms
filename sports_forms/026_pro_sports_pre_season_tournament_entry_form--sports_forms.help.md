# pro_sports_pre_season_tournament_entry_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The pro_sports_pre_season_tournament_entry_form is a registration form for teams to provide their details before participating in a professional sports pre-season tournament. This form helps the tournament organizers collect essential information from each team, ensuring a smooth and organized event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Team Details section with your team's name and division.
2. Select the players from your team that will be participating in the tournament.
3. Enter the details of the athlete(s) who will be representing your team, including their name, contact information, and phone number.
4. Provide contact information for the team coach, including their email and phone number.
5. Select the team manager(s) who will be responsible for your team during the tournament.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Details**: Enter your team's name and choose the division you will be participating in.
* **Team Name**: Enter the name of your team, which will be displayed in the tournament standings.
* **Team Division**: Select the division you will be competing in (e.g., Division 1, Division 2, Division 3).
* **Team Players**: Select up to six players from your team who will be participating in the tournament.
* **Athlete Details**: Enter the details of the athlete(s) who will be representing your team, including their name.
* **Athlete Name**: Enter the name of the athlete who will be representing your team.
* **Athlete Contact**: Enter the contact information for the athlete, including their email address.
* **Athlete Phone**: Select the primary phone number for the athlete.
* **Team Coach**: Enter the name of your team coach.
* **Team Coach Email**: Enter the email address of your team coach.
* **Team Coach Phone**: Select the primary phone number for your team coach.
* **Team Manager**: Select the team manager(s) who will be responsible for your team during the tournament.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields (indicated by a *) to ensure your team's registration is complete.
* Double-check the team and athlete details for accuracy before submitting the form.
