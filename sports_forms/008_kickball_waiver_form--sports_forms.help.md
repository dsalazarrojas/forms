# Kickball Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kickball Waiver Form is a mandatory document for all players participating in the Kickball league. It's used to capture crucial information for player safety, emergency contacts, and liability reasons.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details accurately and completely.
2. Provide your emergency contact's information, including their name, phone number, and relationship to you.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Describe any medical conditions or injuries that may affect your participation in the league.
5. Choose the correct waiver version (if multiple versions are available).
6. Enter your date of birth.
7. Optionally, provide your contact phone number and form ID (if required by the Kickball staff).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Player Name** (`player_name`, text, required: false): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required: false): Provide the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required: false): Describe your relationship to your emergency contact person (e.g., mother, father, sister, etc.).
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Player Address** (`player_address`, text, required: false): Enter your home address.
* **Player Injury** (`player_injury`, note, required: false): Describe any injuries or medical conditions that may affect your participation in the Kickball league.
* **Player Medical Condition** (`player_medical_condition`, text, required: false): Enter any medical conditions that may impact your participation in the league.
* **Waiver Version** (`waiver_version`, select_one, required: false): Choose the correct waiver version (if multiple versions are available).
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter a phone number where you can be reached.
* **Formid** (`formid`, text, required: false): Enter a unique form ID (if required by the Kickball staff).
* **Assignedtool** (`assignedtool`, text, required: false): This field is likely for internal staff use and can be ignored by players.
