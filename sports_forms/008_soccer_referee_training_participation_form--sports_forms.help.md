# soccer_referee_training_participation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The soccer_referee_training_participation_form is designed to collect and track information from soccer referees for training participation, including personal details, training specifics, and certification information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information and details before starting.
2. Fill in your Participant Name, Email, Phone, and Referee Licence Number fields.
3. Enter the Training Date and Training Time for the relevant session.
4. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for Training Session ID and Referee Association Member, and choose the correct Training Status (Active/Inactive).
5. Enter the Referee Licence Expiry Date, Referee Training Course Hours, and Referee Training Certifications Status.
6. If applicable, fill in the Emergency Contact Name and Emergency Contact Relationship.
7. Review and submit the form once all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (text, required): Enter your name as a soccer referee.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Training Date** (date, required): Enter the date of the training session.
* **Training Time** (time, required): Enter the time of the training session.
* **Training Session ID** (select_one, required): Indicate whether you participated in the session (Yes/No).
* **Training Course Name** (text, required): Enter the course name or details.
* **Referee Experience Years** (number, required): Record your years of referee experience.
* **Referee Licence Number** (text, required): Enter your referee licence number.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (text, required): Describe your relationship to the emergency contact person (e.g., relative, family member, friend, etc.).
* **Referee Certificate Number** (text, required): Enter your referee certificate number.
* **Referee Association Member** (select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether you're a member of a referee association (Yes/No).
* **Referee Licence Issue Date** (date, optional): Enter the issue date of your referee licence (if applicable).
* **Referee Licence Expiry Date** (date, required): Enter the expiry date of your referee licence.
* **Referee Training Course Hours** (number, required): Record the hours completed in the training course.
* **Referee Training Certifications Status** (select_one, required): Select your current certification status (Active/Inactive).
* **Training Status** (select_one, optional): Select your current training status (Active/Inactive).
* **Training Location** (text, optional): Enter the location of the training session (if applicable).
* **Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Assigned Tools** (text, optional): List any tools assigned to you for use during the training session.
* **Form ID** (text, optional): Enter a unique form ID (if required).
* **Created By** (text, optional): Record who created the form or generated it.
* **Updated By** (text, optional): Record who last updated the form or generated it.
* **Created Date** (date, optional): Record the date the form was created or generated.
