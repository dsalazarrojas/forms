# Golf Course Player Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for golf course players to check in with course staff and provide necessary information for their tee time.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately, as your information will be used by course staff to prepare for your tee time.
- Enter your player name and email address, as this is required information.
- Enter your 10-digit phone number in the player phone field, as this will be used for communication with the staff.
- Select your tee time from the options provided.
- Enter the size of your playing group.
- Check any additional service needs from the options provided.
- Enter any additional notes about your group or special requests.
- Enter the name of the staff member that will be working with you.
- Enter your staff contact phone number.
- Enter the email of the staff member that will be working with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Player Name** (`player_name`, text, optional): Enter your name for your own reference and for the staff to know who you are.
* **Player Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so that the staff can contact you with any important information.
* **Player Phone** (`player`, text, optional): Enter a 10-digit phone number in case the staff needs to contact you during your tee time.
* **Tee Time** (`tee_time`, time, required): Select your scheduled tee time from the options provided.
* **Group Size** (`group_size`, number, required): Enter the number of players in your group.
* **Service Needs** (`service_needs`, select_multiple, optional): Check any additional services your group may need, such as cart or caddy service.
* **Player Notes** (`player_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or requests your group may have.
* **Staff Name** (`staff_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the staff member that will be working with you.
* **Staff Phone** (`staff_phone`, text, optional): Enter a 10-digit phone number of the staff member that will be working with you.
* **Staff Email** (`staff_email`, email, required): Enter the email of the staff member that will be working with you.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the information provided is accurate, as this will help the staff provide better service to you.
- If you are unsure of any of the information, please ask the staff for assistance.
- If you have any special requests, please enter them in the player notes field.
- The staff will use this information to provide you with the best possible experience at the golf course.
