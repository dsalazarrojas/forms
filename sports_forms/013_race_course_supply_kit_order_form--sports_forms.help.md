# Race Course Supply Kit Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Race Course Supply Kit Order Form is a form used to order supplies for race courses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form fields and make sure you understand what is being asked.
2.  Fill in the relevant information in each field as required.
3.  Review the form before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled in.
4.  Submit the form to place your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Order Supplies
*   **Order Supplies**: (`race_course_supply_kit_order_form_1`, text, required: false)
    Enter a brief description of the supplies you would like to order.

### Delivery Schedule
*   **Delivery Schedule**: (`delivery_schedule`, text, required: false)
    Provide the delivery schedule for your order, including any specific dates or timeframes you would like to receive the supplies.

### Special Instructions
*   **Special Instructions**: (`special_instructions`, text, required: false)
    Enter any special instructions for the delivery of your supplies, such as delivery to a specific location or any other requirements.

### Race Type
*   **Race Type**: (`race_type`, select_one, required: true)
    Select the type of race for which you are ordering supplies:
        5K
        Marathon
        School run
        Other

### Delivery Address
*   **Delivery Address**: (`delivery_address`, text, required: false)
    Enter the address where the supplies should be delivered.

### Special Instructions
*   **Special Instructions**: (`special_instructions`, text, required: false)
    Enter any additional notes or instructions regarding the order.

### Race Course Supply Kit
*   **Race Course Supply Kit**: (`race_course_supply_kit`, text, required: false)
    Enter the specific Race Course Supply Kit you would like to order.

### Event Date
*   **Event Date**: (`event_date`, date, required: false)
    Enter the date of the event.

### Event Time
*   **Event Time**: (`event_time`, time, required: false)
    Enter the time of the event.

### Email
*   **Email**: (`email`, email, required: false)
    Enter your email address for communication.

### Phone
*   **Phone**: (`phone`, text, required: false)
    Enter your phone number for contact.

Please review this guide carefully and let me know if you have any questions or need further clarification.
