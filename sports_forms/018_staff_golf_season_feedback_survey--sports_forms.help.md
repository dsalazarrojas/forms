# Staff Golf Season Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Staff Golf Season Feedback Survey is a form used to collect feedback and ratings on the golf season events organized by the staff. This form is completed by staff to provide their input on the events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Choose your answer for each field by selecting from the available options or typing in the text fields.
2. Make sure to fill out all fields that are required.
3. If you have comments or suggestions for improvement, please enter them in the "Comments" and "Suggestions" text fields.
4. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
5. Once you have filled out all the fields, click the submit button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Staff Golf Season Feedback** (`staff_golf_season_survey`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all that apply to indicate your overall satisfaction with the golf season events.
	+ Yes: I was satisfied with the events.
	+ No: I was not satisfied with the events.
* **Event Organizers** (`event_organizer`, text, required/optional): Enter the name or title of the event organizer(s) who organized the golf season events.
* **Golf Season Rating** (`golf_season_rating`, select_one, required/optional): Select one of the following ratings to indicate your overall satisfaction with the golf season events:
	+ Good: I was satisfied with the events.
	+ Fair: I was not satisfied with the events.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required/optional): Enter any comments you have on the golf season events.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, note, required/optional): Enter any suggestions you have for improving the golf season events.
* **Staff Satisfaction** (`staff_satisfaction`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all that apply to indicate the level of staff satisfaction with the golf season events.
	+ Satisfied: Staff was satisfied with the events.
	+ Dissatisfied: Staff was not satisfied with the events.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, text, required/optional): Enter a rating for the golf season events.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required/optional): Select the department that you belong to:
	+ Yes: This department participated in the golf season events.
	+ No: This department did not participate in the golf season events.
