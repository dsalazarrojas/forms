# Sports Media Content Partnership Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for media content partners to submit their partnership proposals to the sports media platform. The goal is to collect all necessary information for review and decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Partner Info** section with your contact details.
2. Provide detailed information about your proposal in the **Proposal Info** section.
3. Review and complete the remaining sections as needed.
4. Submit your form when you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Partner Info** (`partner_info`, text, required): Provide your name, email, and other contact details here.
* **Proposal Info** (`proposal_info`, text, required): Describe your partnership proposal in detail, including the type of content you'd like to partner on and the reasons why you're a good fit.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required): Enter your contact information for follow-up communication.
* **Agreement Terms** (`agreement_terms`, text, required): Describe the terms of your proposed partnership, including any relevant agreements or contracts.
* **Submission Review** (`submission_review`, text, required): Describe your submission review process, including how you will review and assess proposals.
* **Submission Reviewer** (`submission_reviewer`, text, required): Identify the person or team responsible for reviewing your proposals.
* **Submission Review Status** (`submission_review_status`, text, required): Update the status of your proposal during the review process.
* **Submission Review Comments** (`submission_review_comments`, text, required): Provide comments on the status of your proposal.
* **Submission Decisions** (`submission_decisions`, text, required): Record the decisions made on your proposal, including approval or rejection.
* **Submission Decision Reasons** (`submission_decision_reasons`, text, required): Explain the reasons behind your decisions.
* **Submission Decisions Date** (`submission_decisions_date`, date, required): Date of your proposal decision.
* **Submission Decisions Time** (`submission_decisions_time`, time, required): Time of your proposal decision.
* **Partner Intake** (`partner_intake`, text, required): Complete any additional questions related to partnership intake.
* **Partner Agreement** (`partner_agreement`, text, required): Describe the terms of your partnership agreement.
* **Agreement Acceptance** (`agreement_acceptance`, text, required): Record the agreement acceptance or rejection.
* **Form Submission** (`form_submission`, text, required): Confirm that you have submitted your form.
* **Partner Intake Form Submited** (`partner_intake_form_submitted`, text, required): Confirm that you have submitted your partner intake form.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, text, required): Update the status of your proposal during the review process.
* **Submission Comments** (`submission_comments`, text, required): Provide additional comments on your proposal.
* **Submission Status Updated** (`submission_status_updated`, text, required): Update the status of your submission.
* **Form Submission Time** (`form_submission_time`, time, required): Confirm the time of your form submission.
* **Submission Decision Reasons** (`submission_decision_reasons`, text, required): Provide reasons for any decisions made on your proposal.
* **Agreement Terms Accepted** (`agreement_terms_accepted`, text, required): Record the acceptance or rejection of agreement terms.
* **Partner Intake Submited** (`partner_intake_submitted`, text, required): Confirm that you have submitted your partner intake form.

## Tips
* Please fill out each field carefully and accurately, as this information will be used to review and decide on your proposal.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, feel free to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
