# Sports Trading Card Rookie Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Trading Card Rookie Checklist is designed to collect information about a sports trading card, specifically for a rookie card. This form will help maintain a comprehensive database of sports trading cards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the first name of the sports trading card player in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter the last name of the sports trading card player in the "Last Name" field.
3. If you have the birthdate of the sports trading card player, select it in the "Birthdate" field.
4. Select the sports trading card set from the list of available options in the "Card Set" field.
5. Enter the grade of the sports trading card (0-10) in the "Grade" field.
6. Enter the series of the sports trading card in the "Series" field.
7. Enter the number of the sports trading card in the "Number" field.
8. Select the type of the sports trading card (Rookie, Veteran, or Parallel) in the "Card Type" field.
9. Select the status of the sports trading card (Active, Inactive, or Missing) in the "Card Status" field.
10. Enter any additional notes about the sports trading card in the "Notes" field.
11. Enter the email of the sports trading card owner in the "Email" field.
12. Enter the phone number of the sports trading card owner in the "Phone" field.
13. Enter the address of the sports trading card owner in the "Address" field.
14. Enter the city of the sports trading card owner in the "City" field.
15. Enter the state of the sports trading card owner in the "State" field.
16. Enter the zip of the sports trading card owner in the "Zip" field.
17. Select whether the card was shipped to the owner in the "Shipped To" field.
18. Select the date the card was shipped to the owner in the "Shipped Date" field.
19. Enter the number of cards shipped to the owner in the "Cards Shipped" field.
20. Enter the number of cards remaining in the "Cards Remaining" field.
21. Enter the total value of the sports trading card in the "Total Value" field.
22. Enter the total value amount of the sports trading card in the "Total Value Amount" field.
23. Enter any additional notes about the sports trading card in the "Notes Extra" field.
24. Select the date the sports trading card was added in the "Date Added" field.
25. Select the date the sports trading card was updated in the "Date Updated" field.
