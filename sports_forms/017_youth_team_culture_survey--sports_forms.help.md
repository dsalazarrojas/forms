# Youth Team Culture Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Team Culture Survey is designed to gather feedback on the team culture of youth teams, aiming to create a more positive and productive team environment. This survey will help identify strengths and weaknesses, providing actionable insights for team members, leaders, and coaches to improve team culture.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out each section carefully, considering your honest opinion about each aspect of your team culture.
- Select ratings or responses according to your genuine feelings and experiences.
- Provide additional comments in the "Team Culture Comments" section if you have more to share or want to elaborate on any of the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Team Culture Form** (text): This is the beginning of the survey, which asks you to rate your overall team culture.
  • **Team Morale** (number): Rate your team's morale, with higher numbers indicating a more positive atmosphere.
  • **Team Communication** (text): Describe how you rate your team's communication, focusing on how well team members interact with each other.
  • **Team Belonging** (text): Rate how much you feel a part of your team, with more positive ratings indicating a stronger sense of belonging.
  • **Team Leadership** (select_one): Choose one of the options to rate the performance of your team's leadership.
  • **Team Leadership Hands On** (select_multiple): Select all the options that apply to how much hands-on approach you receive from your team's leadership.
  • **Team Leadership Style** (select_one): Choose one of the options to rate the leadership style of your team.
  • **Team Leadership Support** (select_one): Select one of the options to rate the support you receive from your team's leadership.
  • **Team Culture** (text): Describe your team culture, providing any additional comments or insights.
  • **Team Culture Comments** (text): Add any final comments or thoughts about your team culture.
