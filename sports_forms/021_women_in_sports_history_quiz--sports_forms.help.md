# Women In Sports History Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about their personal preferences related to women in sports history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please use the standard email format (example: `username@example.com`).
4. Enter your date of birth in the "Date Of Birth" field. Please use the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
5. Select your favorite athlete from the "Favorite Athlete" dropdown menu.
6. Select all the sports you enjoy watching or participating in from the "Favorite Sport" and "Sport Participated" dropdown menus. You can select multiple sports.
7. Enter your score out of 10 in the "Score" field. This will help us gauge your knowledge on women's sports history.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address in the standard format (example: `username@example.com`).

### Date Of Birth
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the standard format (YYYY-MM-DD).

### Favorite Athlete
* **Favorite Athlete** (`favorite_athlete`, `select_one`, required): Select your favorite athlete from the dropdown menu. Our options include:
  * Serena Williams
  * Maria Sharapova
  * Martina Navratilova
  * Naomi Osaka

### Favorite Sport
* **Favorite Sport** (`favorite_sport`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the sports you enjoy watching or participating in. Our options include:
  * Tennis
  * Basketball
  * Football
  * Baseball
  * Volleyball

### Sport Participated
* **Sport Participated** (`sport_participated`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the sports you have participated in. Our options include:
  * Tennis
  * Basketball
  * Football
  * Baseball
  * Volleyball
  * Golf

### Score
* **Score** (`score`, `number`, required): Enter your score out of 10, indicating your level of knowledge on women's sports history.

### Tips
* Please answer fields with the correct format to ensure accurate results.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
* Thank you for participating in this form! Your feedback and preferences will help us improve our sports-related content.
