# Fitness Football Activity Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from football players about their fitness and training activities. It is intended to gather data that will help identify potential issues or areas for improvement in the fitness programs being offered.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the relevant options for each field and provide any necessary additional comments or details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sports** (`sports-forms`): This field allows you to select all the sports you participate in, such as Football, Hockey, or Basketball. Please choose all the sports that apply to you.
* **Health Conditions** (`health-conditions`): Select any health conditions you currently have or are concerned about, such as Chest, Back, or Leg issues. This will help us understand if any specific conditions may be affecting your training.
* **Fitness Activities** (`fitness-activities`): Select all the activities you regularly do to stay fit, like Cardio, Strength, or Flexibility exercises. This will help us identify your exercise habits.
* **Fitness Activities 2** (`fitness-activities-2`): Select the specific activity you are most active in, such as Running, Swimming, or Cycling. This will help us understand your specific fitness goals.
* **Exercise Frequency** (`exercise-freq`): Enter a number to indicate how frequently you exercise per week. For example, if you exercise 3 times a week, enter 3.
* **Training Days** (`training-days`): Enter the number of days you train per week. This is a critical field to provide accurate information for our training programs.
* **Start Date** (`start-date`): Enter the date you began your current fitness program. This will help us track your progress over time.
* **End Date** (`end-date`): Enter the date you plan to end your current fitness program. This will help us plan for future programs.
* **Comments** (`comments`): If you have any additional comments or feedback, please provide them here.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned-tool`): If you have been assigned a specific tool or program, please select it from the list. If not, select "Other" or "None".
* **Form ID** (`form-id`): This is a unique number that helps us track your submission. Please do not change this value.

## Tips
* Please answer all fields truthfully and completely.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to contact us for assistance.
* Make sure to save your progress as you fill out the form, in case you need to refer back to it later.
