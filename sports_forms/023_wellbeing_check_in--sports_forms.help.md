# Wellbeing Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wellbeing Check In form is designed to help users assess their current wellbeing status and habits related to physical activity and exercise.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **User Name** in the text box provided.
2. Provide your **Email** address to receive any relevant updates or reminders.
3. Enter your **Phone** number (optional).
4. Rate your current **Wellbeing Score** on a scale of 1-10.
5. Add any additional **Wellbeing Comments** in the text box provided.
6. Select the **Date** you last exercised or engaged in physical activity.
7. Enter the **Time** of day when you last exercised or engaged in physical activity.
8. Choose how often you **Exercise**:
	* Option 1: Daily
	* Option 2: Several times a week
	* Option 3: Rarely
9. Select the **Reason for Exercise** (Choose all that apply):
	* Option 1: For overall health and wellbeing
	* Option 2: To improve physical performance
10. Choose your preferred **Location**:
	* Option 1: Home
	* Option 2: Gym
11. Select the **Tools** you use:
	* Option 1: Running shoes
	* Option 2: Weights
12. Enter any additional **Goals** you have for wellbeing.
13. Add any **Notes** about your wellbeing progress.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (Text): Enter your name to help us identify your wellbeing progress.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address to receive updates and reminders.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Wellbeing Score** (Number): Rate your current wellbeing score on a scale of 1-10.
* **Wellbeing Comments** (Text): Add any additional comments about your wellbeing progress.
* **Date** (Date): Select the date you last exercised or engaged in physical activity.
* **Time** (Time): Enter the time of day when you last exercised or engaged in physical activity.
* **How often do you exercise?** (Select One):
	+ Option 1: Daily
	+ Option 2: Several times a week
	+ Option 3: Rarely
* **Why do you exercise?** (Select Multiple):
	+ Option 1: For overall health and wellbeing
	+ Option 2: To improve physical performance
* **Location** (Select One):
	+ Option 1: Home
	+ Option 2: Gym
* **Tools** (Select Multiple):
	+ Option 1: Running shoes
	+ Option 2: Weights
* **Goals** (Text): Enter any additional wellbeing goals you have.
* **Notes** (Text): Add any additional wellbeing notes.

## Tips
* Please complete this form regularly to maintain a record of your wellbeing progress.
* Be honest and accurate when rating your wellbeing score.
* Choose the options that best fit your circumstances.
* Use the form to identify areas for improvement and track your progress over time.
