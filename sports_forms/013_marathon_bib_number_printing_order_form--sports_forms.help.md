# Marathon Bib Number Printing Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marathon Bib Number Printing Order Form is designed to collect necessary information for printing marathon bib numbers. This form is used for events, participants, and shipping details. It is crucial to fill out this form carefully to ensure accurate bib number printing and delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Event Title** (`event_title`, text, required/optional): Enter the title of the event you are participating in.
2. **Event Design** (`event_design`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the design options for your bib number from the given options.
3. **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your bib number.
4. **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional shipping instructions or notes.
5. **Payment Type** (`payment_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the payment method you prefer.
6. **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose your preferred shipping method.
7. **Shipping Cost** (`shipping_cost`, number, required/optional): Enter the shipping cost, if applicable.
8. **Upload Design File** (`upload_design`, text, required/optional): Upload any additional design files for your bib number.
9. **Upload Additional Design File** (`upload_design_2`, text, required/optional): Upload any extra design files for your bib number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Title**: Enter the title of the event you are participating in.
* **Event Design**: Select the design options for your bib number from the given choices.
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your bib number.

* **Shipping Method**: Choose your preferred shipping method.
* **Shipping Cost**: Enter the shipping cost, if applicable.
* **Upload Design File**: Upload any additional design files for your bib number.
* **Upload Additional Design File**: Upload any extra design files for your bib number.

## Tips
* Double-check your event title and participant name for accuracy.
* Carefully select your event design and shipping method to avoid delays or errors.
* Make sure to upload any required design files to ensure correct bib number printing.
* Review your submission carefully before submitting to ensure all fields are filled out correctly.
