# Sports League Expansion Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Sports League Expansion Application Form is a tool designed to help sports leagues expand their reach and grow their operations. This form gathers information about the division, teams, and expansion plans to ensure that the necessary details are collected accurately.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please ensure that you have all the necessary information and approvals before filling out this form.
2. Carefully review each section and ensure that you provide accurate and complete information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Division Name** (`division_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the division you are applying to expand.
* **Division Location** (`division_location`, text, required): Provide the location where the division will be located.
* **Division Status** (`division_status`, select_one, required): Choose the status of the division:
	+ New
	+ Expanding
	+ Established
* **Division Notes** (`division_notes`, text, optional): If necessary, provide any additional notes about the division.
* **Team 1 Organization** (`team_1_organization`, text, required): Enter the name of the first team's organization.
* **Team 1 History** (`team_1_history`, text, required): Provide a brief history of the first team.
* **Team 1 League Affiliation** (`team_1_league_affiliation`, select_one, required): Choose the league affiliation:
	+ Affiliated
	+ Unaffiliated
	+ Independent
* **Team 2 Organization** (`team_2_organization`, text, required): Enter the name of the second team's organization.
* **Team 2 History** (`team_2_history`, text, required): Provide a brief history of the second team.
* **Team 2 League Affiliation** (`team_2_league_affiliation`, select_one, required): Choose the league affiliation:
	+ Affiliated
	+ Unaffiliated
	+ Independent
* **Expansion Plans** (`expansion_plans`, text, required): Describe the expansion plans for the division.
* **Expansion Location** (`expansion_location`, text, required): Provide the location where the expansion will take place.
* **Expansion Date** (`expansion_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the expansion.
* **Expansion Time** (`expansion_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the expansion.
* **Expansion Note** (`expansion_note`, text, optional): If necessary, provide any additional notes about the expansion.
* **Team 1 League Name** (`team_1_league_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the first team's league.
* **Team 2 League Name** (`team_2_league_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the second team's league.
* **Team 1 Owners** (`team_1_owners`, text, required): Enter the name of the first team's owners.
* **Team 2 Owners** (`team_2_owners`, text, required): Enter the name of the second team's owners.
* **Team 1 Contact** (`team_1_contact`, text, required): Enter the name of the first team's contact person.
* **Team 2 Contact** (`team_2_contact`, text, required): Enter the name of the second team's contact person.
* **Team 1 Email** (`team_1_email`, email, required): Enter the email of the first team.
* **Team 2 Email** (`team_2_email`, email, required): Enter the email of the second team.
* **Team 1 Phone** (`team_1_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the first team.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by individuals with authority and knowledge about the division and teams. Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
