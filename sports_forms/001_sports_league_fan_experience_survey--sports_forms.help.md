# sports_league_fan_experience_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "sports_league_fan_experience_survey" form is designed to gather feedback from users about their experiences with the sports league, including demographic information, attendance habits, and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by carefully reading each field label to understand what information is requested.
2. Complete each field accurately and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure the form is properly submitted.
