# Online Training Disclaimer - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect basic contact information and ensure that users agree to the terms and conditions of the online training program.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start, fill in your User Name, which is a required field. This will help the training program administrators know who you are.
2.  Next, enter your Email address in the Email field, also a required field. This will enable us to keep in touch with you.
3.  If you have a Date of Birth (not required), please enter it in the Date of Birth field.
4.  If you have a Phone Number (not required), please enter it in the Phone Number field.
5.  In the Consent field, select the option that applies to you: "I have read and agreed to all the terms and conditions." or "I have not read the terms and conditions."
6.  If you have an Emergency Contact (not required), enter their name in the Emergency Contact field.
7.  If you have an Emergency Phone Number (not required), enter it in the Emergency Phone Number field.
8.  Finally, in the Agree to Terms field, select whether you agree or do not agree to the terms and conditions.
9.  In the Agree to Privacy field, select whether you agree or do not agree to the privacy policy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Name** (text, required): Enter your full name as you want it to be displayed in the training program.
*   **Email** (email, required): Enter your valid email address to receive updates and notifications from the training program.
*   **Date of Birth** (date, optional): If you're unsure or do not want to provide your date of birth, you can skip this field.
*   **Phone Number** (number, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted via phone calls.
*   **Consent** (select one, optional): Select one of the options to indicate whether you have read and agreed or not agreed to the terms and conditions.
*   **Emergency Contact** (text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of a person who can reach you in case of an emergency.
*   **Emergency Phone Number** (number, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
*   **Agree to Terms** (select one, optional): Select whether you agree or do not agree to the terms and conditions of the online training program.
*   **Agree to Privacy** (select one, optional): Select whether you agree or do not agree to the privacy policy of the online training program.
