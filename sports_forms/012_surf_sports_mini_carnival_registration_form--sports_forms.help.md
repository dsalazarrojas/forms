# surf_sports_mini_carnival_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering participants for a mini surf sports event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Enter the Event ID, if required.
- Fill in the participant's name, gender, age, contact number, email, and address.
- Select the participant's gender and country of residence.
- Provide the event date and time.
- Enter the event location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Id (Number)**: Enter the ID of the event you are registering for. If you do not know your event ID, you might need to contact the organizers.
* **Participant Name (Text)**: Fill in your name as it should be displayed on event materials.
* **Participant Gender (Select One)**: Choose your gender from the available options. This will help organizers understand your demographic.
* **Participant Age (Number)**: Enter your age in years.
* **Participant Contact Number (Text)**: Provide your phone number for event organizers to contact you.
* **Participant Email (Email)**: Enter your email for event organizers to communicate with you.
* **Participant Address (Text)**: Fill in your current address for event organizers' reference.
* **Participant City (Text)**: Enter your city of residence.
* **Participant State (Select One)**: Select your country of residence from the available options.
* **Participant Country (Text)**: Enter the name of your country of residence.
* **Event Type (Select Multiple)**: Select all event types that apply to this registration. This field might not be clearly labeled; it seems to be asking for the type of event (e.g., competition, festival, etc.).
* **Event Date (Date)**: Enter the date of the event you are registering for.
* **Event Time (Time)**: Enter the time of the event you are registering for.
* **Event Location (Text)**: Enter the location of the event you are registering for.

## Tips
- Ensure you fill in all required fields.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact the event organizers for clarification.
- Review your form submission before submitting to ensure it is accurate.
