# sports_fan_video_recording_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a consent form for sports fans to record video content. It is used to collect personal data and to obtain consent from the users to record their video content.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the field labeled "First Name".
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the field labeled "Last Name".
3. Enter your **Email Address** in the field labeled "Email Address".
4. If you are under 18 years old, enter your **Date of Birth** in the field labeled "Date of Birth".
5. Select one of the options for **Parent Consent** from the dropdown menu.
6. Enter your **Parent Name** in the field labeled "Parent Name".
7. If you have a contact number, enter it in the field labeled "Contact Number".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **First Name** (Required, text): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Last Name** (Required, text): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Email Address** (Required, email): Enter your email address, and ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
- **Date of Birth** (Optional, date): If you are under 18 years old, enter your date of birth to confirm your age.
- **Parent Consent** (Required, select_one): Select one of the options for parent consent to confirm that you have your parent or guardian's consent.
- **Parent Name** (Required, text): Enter your parent or guardian's name.
- **Contact Number** (Optional, text): Enter your contact number if you have one.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are filled correctly before submitting the form.
* If you are under 18 years old, make sure to enter your date of birth to confirm your age.
* If you have any issues with the form, please contact the support team for assistance.
