# Sports Tournament Security Pass Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to order security passes for staff, vendors, and media personnel involved in a sports tournament. It captures their roles, contact information, and any special notes to ensure that their passes are issued correctly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Choose the type of personnel you are ordering a security pass for by selecting "Staff", "Vendor", or "Media" from the "Staff Role", "Vendor Role", or "Media Role" dropdown menu, respectively.
2. Enter the name of the personnel member, e.g., "John Doe" for a staff member.
3. Enter the contact email and phone number for the personnel member, if applicable.
4. Enter any special notes or instructions for the personnel member, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Staff Name**: Enter the name of the staff member who is being issued a security pass. (Required)
* **Vendor Name**: Enter the name of the vendor who is being issued a security pass. (Required)
* **Media Name**: Enter the name of the media member who is being issued a security pass. (Required)
* **Staff Role**: Choose the role of the staff member from the dropdown menu. (Required)
* **Vendor Role**: Choose the role of the vendor from the dropdown menu. (Required)
* **Media Role**: Choose the role of the media member from the dropdown menu. (Required)
* **Staff Email**: Enter the email address of the staff member. (Required)
* **Vendor Email**: Enter the email address of the vendor, if applicable. (Optional)
* **Media Email**: Enter the email address of the media member, if applicable. (Optional)
* **Staff Phone**: Enter the phone number of the staff member, if applicable. (Optional)
* **Vendor Phone**: Enter the phone number of the vendor, if applicable. (Optional)
* **Media Phone**: Enter the phone number of the media member, if applicable. (Optional)
* **Staff Notes**: Enter any special notes or instructions for the staff member, if necessary. (Optional)
* **Vendor Notes**: Enter any special notes or instructions for the vendor, if necessary. (Optional)
* **Media Notes**: Enter any special notes or instructions for the media member, if necessary. (Optional)
* **Submit**: Once you have completed all the required fields, click "Submit" to process the order.
