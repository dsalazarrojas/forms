# Martial Arts Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to assess your understanding and experience in various aspects of martial arts. It is a comprehensive guide to help you reflect on your knowledge and progress in martial arts training.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read each section carefully and answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Be honest and accurate in your responses.
3. Use the available space to provide detailed explanations or comments.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide any additional information or comments about your martial arts experience or knowledge. It's an opportunity to share your thoughts or thoughts on your training or teaching experience.
* **martial_arts_experience** (`martial_arts_experience`, text, optional): This field is for you to describe your martial arts experience, including how long you've been training and what styles you've learned or taught.
* **rank** (`rank`, select_one, optional): This field asks you to select your current rank in martial arts, from beginner to black belt.
* **martial_arts_concepts** (`martial_arts_concepts`, select_multiple, optional): This field allows you to select all the martial arts concepts you have knowledge or experience in, such as basics, footwork, hand-to-hand, or theory.
* **class_placement** (`class_placement`, select_one, optional): This field asks you to select your current class placement in martial arts training, from beginner to instructor-led.
* **additional_comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): This field is for you to provide any additional comments or thoughts about your martial arts training or experience.
* **instructor_name** (`instructor_name`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide the name of your instructor or coach.
* **instructor_title** (`instructor_title`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide the title or designation of your instructor or coach.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): This field is for you to provide your email address.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide your phone number.
* **training_program** (`training_program`, text, optional): This field is for you to describe the training program you are or have been part of.
* **additional_comments_2** (`additional_comments_2`, note, optional): This field is for you to provide any additional comments or thoughts about your martial arts training or experience.
