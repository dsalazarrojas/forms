# Golf Practice Session Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Golf Practice Session Log is a tool for golf instructors or coaches to track and review practice sessions with their students. It helps to capture essential details such as practice dates, session types, focus areas, self-assessment, coach and golfer comments, session length, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form for each new practice session.
2. Ensure that each field is completed accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Practice Date** (`practice_date`, `date`, Optional): Enter the date of the practice session.
* **Session Type** (`session_type`, `select_one`, Optional): Select the type of session (e.g., 'Yes' for a practice session).
* **Focus Area** (`focus_area`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select areas of focus during the session (e.g., 'Yes' for focus on a specific area).
* **Self-Assessment** (`self_assessment`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select areas where you self-assessed your progress (e.g., 'Yes' for improvement in a specific area).
* **Coach Comments** (`coach_comments`, `text`, Optional): Enter any comments or observations from your coach.
* **Golfer Comments** (`golfer_comments`, `text`, Optional): Enter any comments or insights from the golfer.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, Optional): Enter any additional notes or observations about the session.
* **Session Length** (`session_length`, `number`, Optional): Enter the length of the session (in hours).
* **Session Length Type** (`session_length_type`, `select_one`, Optional): Select the type of session length (e.g., 'Yes' for a standard session).

## Tips

* Be thorough when filling out the form to ensure accurate record-keeping and review.
* Use the form for each new practice session to avoid duplication of effort.
* Review and update the form regularly to track progress and identify areas for improvement.
