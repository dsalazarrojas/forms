# Soccer Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a friendly soccer survey designed to gather information about your favorite soccer-related preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, simply fill in the fields with your information.
2. Please answer all the questions that apply to you.
3. If you're unsure about a field, use your best judgment and try to answer as accurately as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address associated with your soccer identity.
* **Favorite Team** (`favorite_team`, select_one, optional): Select your favorite soccer team from the list:
	+ Manchester United
	+ Barcelona
	+ Bayern Munich
	+ Chelsea
* **Birthdate** (`birthdate`, date, optional): Enter your birthdate in the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Height** (`height`, number, optional): Enter your height in centimeters (cm).
* **Weight** (`weight`, number, optional): Enter your weight in kilograms (kg).
* **Jersey Number** (`jersey_number`, number, optional): Enter the number on your favorite soccer jersey.
* **Favorite Player** (`favorite_player`, select_one, optional): Select your favorite soccer player from the list:
	+ Cristiano Ronaldo
	+ Lionel Messi
	+ Neymar
	+ Robert Lewandowski
* **Favorite Position** (`favorite_position`, select_one, optional): Select your favorite soccer position:
	+ Goalkeeper
	+ Defender
	+ Midfielder
	+ Forward
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time you usually watch soccer or play.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional information you'd like to share about your soccer preferences.
