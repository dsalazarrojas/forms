# Sports Advisory Panel Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users who want to join the Sports Advisory Panel. It's a tool used to assess your qualifications and experience in sports, so we can determine whether you're a good fit for our team. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to increase your chances of being considered for membership.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. (Optional) Enter the name of the sport you're interested in or have experience with in the "Sport" field.
3. (Optional) Enter the location where you have sports experience or are interested in sports in the "Location" field.
4. (Optional) Describe your sports experience in the "Experience" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please ensure it's a valid address.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. Confirm your email address in the "Confirm Email" field. This should match your email address in step 5.
8. Confirm your phone number in the "Confirm Phone" field. This should match your phone number in step 6.
9. Enter any additional information or message about yourself in the "Message" field.
10. Select the sports discipline you're interested in or have experience with in the "Confirm" field.
11. Select all that apply from the "Review and Confirm" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents or as you prefer to be known.
* **Sport** (text, optional): If you have experience or interest in a specific sport, enter its name here (e.g., "Soccer", "Basketball", etc.).
* **Location** (text, optional): If you have sports experience or are interested in sports in a specific location, enter the name here (e.g., "New York", "Los Angeles", etc.).
* **Experience** (text, optional): Describe your sports experience, including any relevant education or certifications.
* **Availability** (text, optional): If you're able to participate in activities or events, describe your availability (e.g., weekends, weekdays, etc.).
* **Email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Confirm Email** (email, required): Enter the same email address you entered in the "Email" field to confirm it.
* **Confirm Phone** (text, required): Enter the same phone number you entered in the "Phone" field to confirm it.
* **Message** (text, optional): Enter any additional information or message about yourself.
* **Confirm** (select_one, required): Select "Sports" if you're interested in sports. If you're not interested in sports, select "Other".
* **Review and Confirm** (select_multiple, required): Select all that apply from the options provided.
