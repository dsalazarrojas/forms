# Soccer Player Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about a soccer player for record-keeping and administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Be sure to provide all necessary details for each field to ensure proper completion.
3. Review your information before submitting to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter the soccer player's first name as it appears on their government-issued ID.
* **Last Name**: Enter the soccer player's last name as it appears on their government-issued ID.
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter the player's date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 1990-01-01).
* **Position**: Select the player's position on the team (e.g., goalkeeper, defender, midfielder, forward).
* **Height**: Enter the player's height in meters (e.g., 1.75 m).
* **Weight**: Enter the player's weight in kilograms (e.g., 70 kg).
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Enter the relationship between the player and the emergency contact person (e.g., parent, guardian, coach).
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Address**: Enter the player's address for contact and record-keeping purposes.

## Tips
- Be accurate when filling out the form to avoid errors.
- Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the team administrator.
