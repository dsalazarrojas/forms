# Wrestling Event Guest Pass Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a registration application for guests to attend a wrestling event. It helps event organizers verify your identity, affiliation, and role, while ensuring you are aware of the event rules and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your Email address for contact purposes.
3. Select your Affiliation from the provided options.
4. Choose your Role from the options (Coach/Trainer, Athlete, Official, or Other).
5. Enter your Attend Start and End Dates for the event.
6. Read and acknowledge the rules and regulations by checking the corresponding boxes.
