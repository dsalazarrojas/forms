# One Eighty Basketball Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for users to register for a basketball tournament or event with a team or as an individual. The form collects contact information and team details to allow the organizers to manage the competition effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **User Name**, which can be your actual name or a pseudonym.
2. Provide your **Email**, which will be used as a contact point for communication.
3. Enter your **Password**, which is required for security purposes. Please note that this form does not store the password securely; it's recommended that you use a unique and secure password.
4. Enter your **Team Name**, which can be your team's actual name or a temporary name for the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (text, optional): Enter your actual name or a pseudonym you'd like to use.
* **Email** (email, optional): Type in your contact email address for communication purposes.
* **Password** (text, optional): Enter a unique and secure password for your account. Please be cautious with this field as it is not stored securely on the server.
* **Team Name** (text, optional): Enter your team's actual name or a temporary name for the event.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask the organizers for clarification.
* If you're registering as an individual, you can leave the **Team Name** field blank.
