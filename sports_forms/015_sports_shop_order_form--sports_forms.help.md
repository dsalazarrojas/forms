# Sports Shop Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Shop Order Form is a form designed to collect customer information and order details for sports-related orders. This form is used to gather essential data to process and fulfill orders accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal details in the Customer Information section.
2. Provide your contact information in the Contact Info section.
3. Specify your shipping details in the Shipping section.
4. Choose your preferred payment gateway in the Payment Gateway section.
5. Enter your customer address in the Customer Address section.
6. List the items you want to order in the Order Items section.
7. Add any additional customer notes or comments in the Customer Note section.
8. Enter your email address in the Customer Email section.
9. Provide your contact phone number in the Customer Phone section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, optional): Enter your name, phone number, and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Provide your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
* **Shipping** (`shipping`, text, optional): Specify the shipping details, including the address and any special instructions.
* **Payment Gateway** (`payment_gateway`, text, optional): Choose your preferred payment method, such as credit card or bank transfer.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, optional): Enter your customer address for efficient shipping.
* **Order Items** (`order_items`, text, optional): List the items you want to order, including product names, quantities, and any additional instructions.
* **Customer Note** (`customer_note`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Provide your contact phone number for easy communication.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Use a valid email address and phone number to ensure timely communication.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
