# Public Gym Event Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Public Gym Event Participation Consent Form is designed to collect information from participants who are planning to take part in a public gym event. This form helps event organizers ensure the participants' safety and well-being during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully review each field and its corresponding explanation.
2. Fill out the form accurately and honestly.
3. Make sure to sign the form to confirm your participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Information:
* **Participant Info**: <i>This is where you should enter your personal details, such as your name and contact information.</i>
* **Health Status**: <i>This field asks about your health status to ensure your safety during the event. Select all applicable options from the drop-down list.</i>
* **Contact Details**: <i>Please enter your contact information, including your address and phone number, so we can reach you in case of an emergency.</i>
* **Signed Consent**: <i>Select the option that best represents your willingness to participate in the event. This is a mandatory field, so make sure to choose one of the options.</i>

## Tips
* Please make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask the event organizers.
* If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to seek clarification.
