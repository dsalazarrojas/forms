# Sports Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Registration Form is used to collect essential details from athletes to register them for a sports-related program or event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Base Form with your athlete's information.
2. Read and understand the disclaimer and terms and conditions.
3. Indicate your preferred position in the sport.
4. Provide your guardian's contact information.
5. Confirm that your guardian has given their consent for you to participate.
6. Sign and date the form to confirm your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Base Form
* **Base Form** (`Base Form`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself as an athlete.

### Disclaimer
* **Disclaimer** (`how_the_program_works`, `note`, required: false): This is a note field intended to inform you about the program's rules and policies. Please read carefully before proceeding.

### Athlete Details
* **Athlete Details** (`athlete_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional details about yourself as an athlete.

### Athlete Position
* **Athlete Position** (`athlete_position`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your preferred position in the sport, such as 'Yes' for participation or 'No' for non-participation.

### Guardian Contact Information
* **Guardian Contact Information** (`guardian_contact_information`, `text`, required: false): Enter your guardian's name, contact information, and any other relevant details about them.

### Guardian Consent
* **Guardian Consent** (`guardian_consent`, `select_one`, required: false): Confirm that your guardian has given their consent for you to participate in the program or event. Select 'True' to indicate consent or 'False' otherwise.

### Terms and Conditions
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, `note`, required: false): Read and understand the terms and conditions of the program or event.

### Athlete Signature
* **Athlete Signature** (`athlete_signature`, `number`, required: false): Sign and date the form to confirm your registration. Enter the number of the current year.
