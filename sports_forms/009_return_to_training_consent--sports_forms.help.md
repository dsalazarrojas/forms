# return_to_training_consent - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "return_to_training_consent" form is used to obtain consent from participants before returning to training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your name and training facility information.
3. Specify the date you returned to training.
4. Read through and agree to the rules and guidelines of the training program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Return to Training Consent** (`return_to_training_consent`, text, required): This is the main field where you can enter your consent statement.
- **Training Facility** (`training_facility`, text, optional): Please enter the name of the training facility you are returning to.
- **Participant** (`participant`, text, optional): This is your name, which will be used for contact and identification purposes.
- **Date Returned** (`date_returned`, date, optional): Enter the date you returned to training. Use the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Rules and Guidelines** (`rules_and_guidelines`, text, optional): Read through and agree to the rules and guidelines of the training program.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your information accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the training facility directly.
* Please review the rules and guidelines carefully before agreeing to them.
