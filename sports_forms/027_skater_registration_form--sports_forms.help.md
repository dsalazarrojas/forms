# Skater Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skater Registration Form is designed to collect information from skaters for a specific event or competition. This form helps event organizers understand who is participating and their skill level, location, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (optional).
5. Select the date for the event from the calendar provided.
6. Select the time of the event from the clock provided.
7. Select the type of skating event you will be participating in from the "Event" dropdown menu.
8. Select your skill level from the "Skensity" dropdown menu (optional).
9. Enter the location of the event in the "Location" field.
10. Provide any additional notes or comments about your participation in the "Notes" field (optional).
11. Select the category of skating you will be participating in from the "Category" dropdown menu (optional).
12. Select the subcategory for your selected category (optional).
13. Select any tools assigned to you from the "Assigned Tool" dropdown menu (optional).
14. Select the user(s) assigned to you from the "Assigned User" dropdown menu (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (`First Name`, text, required)
* Enter your first name.

### Last Name (`Last Name`, text, required)
* Enter your last name.

### Email (`Email`, email, required)
* Enter your email address.

### Phone (`Phone`, text, optional)
* Enter your phone number, if you want to provide it.

### Date (`Date`, date, required)
* Select the date for the event from the calendar provided.

### Time (`Time`, time, required)
* Select the time of the event from the clock provided.

### Event (`Event`, select_one, required)
* Select the type of skating event you will be participating in:
	+ Roller Skating
	+ Ice Skating
	+ Skateboarding

### Skensity (`Skensity`, select_multiple, optional)
* Select your skill level:
	+ Beginner
	+ Intermediate
	+ Advanced

### Location (`Location`, text, required)
* Enter the location of the event.

### Notes (`Notes`, note, optional)
* Provide any additional notes or comments about your participation.

### Category (`Category`, select_one, optional)
* Select the category of skating you will be participating in:
	+ Ice Skating

	+ Skateboarding

### Subcategory (`Subcategory`, select_multiple, optional)
