# Football Recruiting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Football Recruiting Form is a tool designed to collect and manage information from players, coaches, or recruiters in the sports industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you are filling it out for a player or coach who is part of the sports industry.
2. Fill in the required information for each field, starting from the top of the form.
3. Answer each question carefully, as the information will be used for internal purposes.
4. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Player Information
* **player_info** (`player_info`, text, required/optional): Enter the player's name.
* **contact_info** (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): Enter the player's contact information, including phone number and email address.
* **contact_details** (`contact_details`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional contact details for the player.
* **player_highlights** (`player_highlights`, text, required/optional): Enter the player's highlights, achievements, or notable skills.
* **player_info_2** (`player_info_2`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional information about the player.
* **additional_player_info_2** (`additional_player_info_2`, text, required/optional): Enter any further information about the player.
* **football_position** (`football_position`, select_one, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if the player has played football, 'No' otherwise.
* **football_position2** (`football_position2`, select_one, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if you want to add another football position for the player, 'No' otherwise.
* **football_position3** (`football_position3`, select_one, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if you want to add another football position for the player, 'No' otherwise.
* **football_position4** (`football_position4`, select_one, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if you want to add another football position for the player, 'No' otherwise.
* **player_recruitment_status** (`player_recruitment_status`, select_one, required/optional): Select 'committed', 'not committed', or 'pending' to indicate the player's recruitment status.

### Coach Information
* **coach_info** (`coach_info`, text, required/optional): Enter the coach's name.
* **contact_preferences** (`contact_preferences`, text, required/optional): Enter the coach's contact preferences, including phone number and email address.
* **coach_preference** (`coach_preference`, select_one, required/optional): Select 'high school', 'college', or 'travel' as the coach's preference.
* **coach_recruitment_status** (`coach_recruitment_status`, select_one, required/optional): Select 'committed', 'not committed', or 'pending' to indicate the coach's recruitment status.
* **additional_coach_info** (`additional_coach_info`, text, required/optional): Enter any further information about the coach.
* **football_recruiter_info** (`football_recruiter_info`, text, required/optional): Enter any further information about the coach's recruiting activities.
