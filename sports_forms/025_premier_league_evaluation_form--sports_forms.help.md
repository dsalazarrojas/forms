# premier-league-evaluation-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The premier-league-evaluation-form is a tool for sports instructors and evaluators to provide detailed feedback and evaluate the performance of Premier League players.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, which include:
   - Player Name
   - Email
   - Phone Number
   - Evaluation Score
   - Overall Evaluation (if selected)

2. Fill in any other optional fields, such as:
   - Player Position
   - Evaluation Date
   - Evaluation Time
   - Notes
   - Player Level

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Player Name** (`player_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the player being evaluated.
- **Player Position** (`player_position`, `select_one`, optional): Select the position played by the player (e.g., Football, Basketball, Soccer, Volleyball).
- **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the evaluation (format: YYYY-MM-DD).
- **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the evaluation (format: 24-hour clock, e.g., 12:00).
- **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or observations about the player's performance.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the player or the person being evaluated.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the player or the person being evaluated.
- **Evaluation Score** (`evaluation_score`, `number`, required): Enter a numerical value between 1 and 5 to evaluate the player's performance.
- **Overall Evaluation** (`overall_evaluation`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or more overall evaluations (e.g., Excellent, Average, Below Average).
- **Player Level** (`player_level`, `select_one`, optional): Select the level of the player's skill (e.g., Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* If you need help or have any questions, contact your sports instructor or the person in charge of Premier League evaluations.
* Review and edit your responses carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
