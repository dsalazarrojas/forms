# Team T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for team t-shirt orders, including team name, t-shirt color, size, and other relevant details. It's intended for teams to submit their order requests to the organization managing the t-shirt production and distribution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of your team to ensure accurate processing and tracking of the order.
2. Choose the desired color for the t-shirt(s) to be ordered.
3. Select the preferred size for the t-shirt(s).
4. Select any additional design option(s) you'd like on the t-shirt.
5. Enter your email address so we can contact you for any further information or updates.
6. Provide your phone number for any potential follow-up communication.
7. Enter the cost and price of the t-shirt order.
8. Select the payment method you prefer (Credit Card, Cash, or PayPal).
9. Choose the date and time you'd like to schedule the order.
10. Enter any additional notes or comments regarding the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **team_name**: Enter the name of your team. This helps us identify and track your order accurately.
* **tshirt_color**: Select the desired color for the t-shirt(s). This will affect the appearance of the shirt.
* **shirt_size**: Choose the preferred size for the t-shirt(s). This will impact the fit and comfort of the shirt.
* **tshirt_design**: Select any additional design option(s) you'd like on the t-shirt, such as the team logo, player number, or player name.
* **email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you for any updates or further information.
* **phone**: Provide your phone number for any potential follow-up communication.
* **team_tshirt_cost**: Enter the total cost of the t-shirt order.
* **team_tshirt_price**: Enter the price of the t-shirt(s) per unit.
* **team_tshirt_tax**: Enter any applicable tax rate for the t-shirt order.
* **team_tshirt_discount**: Enter any applicable discount for the t-shirt order.
* **team_tshirt_total**: This field is automatically calculated based on the other fields.
* **customer_name**: Enter the name of the customer or person placing the order (if different from the team name).
* **customer_email**: Enter the email address of the customer or person placing the order.
* **customer_phone**: Enter the phone number of the customer or person placing the order.
* **customer_address**: Enter the address of the customer or person placing the order.
* **customer_city**: Enter the city where the customer or person placing the order resides.
* **customer_state**: Select the state where the customer or person placing the order resides.
* **customer_zip**: Enter the zip code of the customer or person placing the order.
* **customer_country**: Enter the country of the customer or person placing the order.
* **order_notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes regarding the order.
* **payment_method**: Select the preferred payment method for the order (Credit Card, Cash, or PayPal).
* **order_date**: Enter the desired date for the order to be processed.
* **order_time**: Enter the desired time for the order to be processed.
* **order_total**: This field is automatically calculated based on the other fields.
