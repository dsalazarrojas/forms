# Horse Riding Indemnity Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Horse Riding Indemnity Form is a document that collects information from horse riders, their horses, and emergency contacts. This form is designed to gather essential details for safe and successful horse riding experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details, including your name, contact information, and any relevant medical history.
2. Provide information about the horse, including its name, age, breed, and any medical conditions.
3. Share the emergency contact information for the rider and any other relevant contacts.
4. Agree to the rider agreement and emergency procedure terms.
5. Share any relevant medical certifications, medical conditions, or medications for the rider or horse.
6. Report any horse-related information, such as its training, equipment, and experience.
7. Indicate your own riding experience and the horse's experience.
8. Confirm your insurance coverage and employment status.
9. Finally, provide emergency contact information and relationship details for emergency contacts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Details**: Enter your name, address, phone number, and any other relevant contact information for emergency purposes.
- **Horse Details**: Write down the horse's name, age, breed, and any medical conditions or relevant information.
- **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name, phone number, and relationship of the emergency contact person.
- **Rider Agreement**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the rider agreement terms.
- **Emergency Procedure**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the emergency procedure terms.
- **Medical History**: List any relevant medical history, including allergies, illnesses, or injuries.
- **Medical Certifications**: List any relevant medical certifications, such as first aid or riding certifications.
- **Medical Condition**: Indicate any medical condition that may affect your riding performance or horse safety.
- **Medical Certification**: Enter any relevant medical certification numbers or expiration dates.
- **Horse Information**: Share any relevant horse information, such as training, age, or experience.
- **Horse Medication**: List any medications used on or by the horse.
- **Horse Equipment**: List any equipment used on or by the horse.
- **Horse Training**: Indicate the horse's training level or experience.
- **Rider Experience**: Share your riding experience, including certifications, courses, or hours spent riding.
- **Horse Experience**: Indicate the horse's experience, including training, age, or breed.
- **Insurance Coverage**: Confirm your insurance coverage for horse riding, including policy numbers and expiration dates.
- **Employment Agreement**: Confirm your employment status, including work hours and job role.
- **Employment Status**: Indicate your employment status, including student or retired.
- **Emergency Contact Information**: Enter the emergency contact's name, phone number, and other contact information.
- **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Confirm the relationship with the emergency contact person.

Note: This form is a sample and may need to be adjusted based on specific business needs and requirements. Always review and test the form before sharing it with users.
