# Watersports Liability Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a liability waiver designed to be used in watersports activities. It collects participant information and operator details to ensure that both parties are aware of the risks associated with the activity.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to understand the questions and fields that need to be filled out.
2. For each field, follow the instructions provided below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Info**: Enter your name, age, and contact information.
* **Participant Contact**: Enter your emergency contact person's name, phone number, and relationship with you.
* **Participant Consent**: Provide your consent to participate in the watersports activity.
* **Participant Agreement**: Read and agree to the liability waiver terms.
* **Participant Medical Info**: Disclose any medical conditions that may affect your participation.
* **Risk Acknowledgement**: Acknowledge that you are aware of the risks associated with the watersports activity.
* **Operator Info**: Enter the operator's name, address, and contact information.
* **Operator Agreement**: Provide the operator's agreement to the liability waiver terms.
* **Liability Waiver**: Read and sign the liability waiver document.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the emergency contact person's name, phone number, and relationship with you.
* **Participant Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.

## Tips
* Please review the form carefully before signing it.
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, ask the operator for clarification.
* This form is not a substitute for medical advice, so if you have any health concerns, please discuss them with your doctor.
* Keep a copy of the signed form for your records.
* The operator is responsible for ensuring that the watersports activity is conducted in a safe and controlled environment.
