# sports_club_member_experience_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sports_club_member_experience_survey is designed to gather feedback from sports club members about their experience with the club. This feedback will help the club's management understand what they are doing well and what they need to improve.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2.  Make sure to select all that apply for multi-select fields (e.g., "programs", "facilities", etc.).
3.  For "select_one" fields, please choose only one option that best describes your experience.
4.  Fill out the form completely before submitting it.
