# Horsemanship Challenge Log Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Horsemanship Challenge Log Submission form is designed to collect data about horse riding challenge sessions. This form is used to track and analyze challenge sessions, allowing organizers and participants to monitor progress, identify trends, and provide feedback to riders and trainers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form title: Enter the title of the challenge session.
2. Enter the rider's name: Provide the name of the rider participating in the challenge session.
3. Record the session date: Enter the date of the challenge session.
4. Record the session time: Enter the time of the challenge session.
5. Enter session duration: Enter the duration of the challenge session.
6. Record session distance: Enter the distance covered during the challenge session.
7. Select session reflections: Choose one or more reflections to describe the session experience.
8. Attach media: If relevant, upload or link to media related to the challenge session.
9. Enter media URL: If media is attached, enter the URL where it can be accessed.
10. Add rider notes: Provide any additional notes about the rider's performance.
11. Add trainer notes: Provide any additional notes about the trainer's performance.
12. Add organizer notes: Provide any additional notes about the challenge session or organizer.
13. Record submission date: Enter the date the form was submitted.
14. Record submitted by: Enter the person who submitted the form.
15. Select assigned to: Choose who the form will be assigned to for review.
16. Select assigned by: Choose who assigned the form for review.
17. Select session type: Choose the type of challenge session (e.g., competition, training).
18. Select challenge level: Choose the difficulty level of the challenge session.
19. Select rider score: Choose the rider's score for the session.
20. Select trainer score: Choose the trainer's score for the session.
21. Enter session location: Enter the location where the challenge session took place.
22. Enter session weather: Describe the weather conditions during the challenge session.
23. Add session notes: Provide any additional notes about the challenge session.
24. Enter submitted IP: Enter the IP address of the person who submitted the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Horsemanship Challenge Log Submission**: The title of the form, which should be entered to identify the challenge session.
* **Rider**: The name of the rider participating in the challenge session.
* **Session Date**: The date of the challenge session.
* **Session Time**: The time of the challenge session.
* **Session Duration**: The length of the challenge session.
* **Session Distance**: The distance covered during the challenge session.
* **Session Reflections**: Choose one or more reflections to describe the session experience (e.g., challenging, enjoyable, etc.).
* **Media Attachment**: If relevant, attach media (e.g., images, videos) related to the challenge session.
* **Media URL**: If media is attached, enter the URL where it can be accessed.
* **Rider Notes**: Provide any additional notes about the rider's performance.
* **Trainer Notes**: Provide any additional notes about the trainer's performance.
* **Organizer Notes**: Provide any additional notes about the challenge session or organizer.
* **Submission Date**: The date the form was submitted.
* **Submitted By**: The person who submitted the form.
* **Assigned To**: Choose who the form will be assigned to for review.
* **Assigned By**: Choose who assigned the form for review.
* **Session Type**: Choose the type of challenge session (e.g., competition, training).
* **Challenge Level**: Choose the difficulty level of the challenge session.
* **Rider Score**: Choose the rider's score for the session.
* **Trainer Score**: Choose the trainer's score for the session.
* **Session Location**: Enter the location where the challenge session took place.
* **Session Weather**: Describe the weather conditions during the challenge session.
* **Session Notes**: Provide any additional notes about the challenge session.
* **Submitted IP**: Enter the IP address of the person who submitted the form.

**Tips**

* Please ensure that you are submitting accurate and relevant information.
* Do not resubmit the same challenge session.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the challenge session organizer or administrator.
