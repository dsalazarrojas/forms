# Soccer Player Transfer Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to be used by soccer clubs to request transfer of players from one club to another. It's a crucial part of the transfer process, which requires accurate and clear information to facilitate a smooth transaction.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Enter the player's name as it is recorded on their official documents.
2. Select the player's position on the team.
3. Enter the name of the player's current club.
4. Enter the name of the club to which you're requesting the transfer.
5. (Optional) Select the status of the transfer request from the options provided.
6. Enter any comments related to the transfer request.
7. Enter the reason for the transfer request.
8. Enter the name of the person requesting the transfer.
9. Update the player's status in the system.
10. Enter the name of the player's current club.
11. Enter the name of the player's previous club.
12. Enter the amount of the transfer request (in the selected currency).
13. Select the currency of the transfer request.
14. Enter the amount of the transfer request in the selected currency.
15. Enter the player's age.
16. Enter the player's height.
17. Enter the player's weight.
18. Select the player's experience level.
19. Enter the type of contract the player has.
20. Enter the length of the player's contract.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Player Name**: Enter the full name of the player as it is recorded on their official documents.
* **Player Position**: Select the position the player plays on the team.
* **Player Club**: Enter the name of the player's current club.
* **Requested Club**: Enter the name of the club you're requesting the transfer for.
* **Transfer Request Status**: Select the status of the transfer request (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments related to the transfer request.
* **Reason for Transfer Request**: Enter the reason for the transfer request.
* **Requested By**: Enter the name of the person requesting the transfer.
* **Player Status**: Update the player's status in the system.
* **Current Club**: Enter the name of the player's current club.
* **Previous Club**: Enter the name of the player's previous club.
* **Transfer Request Amount**: Enter the amount of the transfer request in the selected currency.
* **Transfer Request Currency**: Select the currency of the transfer request (USD, EUR, or GBP).
* **Currency Amount**: Enter the amount of the transfer request in the selected currency.
* **Player Age**: Enter the age of the player.
* **Player Height**: Enter the height of the player.
* **Player Weight**: Enter the weight of the player.
* **Player Experience**: Select the player's experience level (Novice, Experienced, or Professional).
* **Player Contract Type**: Enter the type of contract the player has.
* **Player Contract Length**: Enter the length of the player's contract.
* **Transfer Request Amount**: Enter the amount of the transfer request.
* **Transfer Request Currency**: Select the currency of the transfer request.
* **Transfer Request Reason**: Enter the reason for the transfer request.
