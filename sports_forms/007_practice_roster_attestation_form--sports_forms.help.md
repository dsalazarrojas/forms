# Practice Roster Attestation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Practice Roster Attestation Form is designed to collect information about practice rosters, including player details, practice names, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the player's information in the "Player 1 Info" section.
2. Select the type of practice (Baseball, Soccer, or Hockey) in the "Roster Type" field.
3. Choose the status of the roster (Confirmed, Approved, or Rejected) in the "Roster Status" field.
4. Enter the date of the practice in the "Date of Practice" field.
5. Select the attestation status (Confirmed or Unconfirmed) in the "Practice Attestation" field.
6. Choose the team name (Team A, Team B, or Team C) in the "Team Name" field.
7. Enter the player's name in the "Player Name" field.
8. Enter contact information (including name, number, and email) in the "Contact Information" section.
9. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player 1 Info** (`player_info_1`, text, required): Enter the name and other relevant details of the player.
* **Practice Name** (`practice_name_1`, text, required): Enter the name of the practice being attested.
* **Roster Type** (`roster_type_1`, select_one, required): Choose the type of practice (Baseball, Soccer, or Hockey).
* **Roster Status** (`roster_status_1`, select_multiple, required): Choose the status of the roster (Confirmed, Approved, or Rejected).
* **Date of Practice** (`date_practice_1`, date, required): Enter the date of the practice.
* **Practice Attestation** (`roster_attestation_1`, select_one, required): Choose the attestation status (Confirmed or Unconfirmed).
* **Team Name** (`team_name_1`, select_one, required): Choose the team name (Team A, Team B, or Team C).
* **Player Name** (`player_name_1`, text, required): Enter the name of the player.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info_1`, text, required): Enter the contact information for the player, including name, number, and email.
* **Name** (`contact_name_1`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Number** (`contact_number_1`, text, required): Enter the contact number.
* **Email** (`contact_email_1`, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Note** (`contact_note_1`, text, required): Enter any additional notes or comments.
