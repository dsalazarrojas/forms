This form is a crucial tool for registering teams for a Futsal league. It collects essential information from teams, including their name, contact details, and participation data. To complete this form, follow the steps below, carefully understanding each field's requirements and implications.

## Purpose
The Futsal League Team Registration Form is designed to gather necessary data from teams participating in the Futsal league. This form helps the administrators to process and manage the teams effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by entering the team's name in the **Team Name** field (id: 1). This is a required field.
2.  Enter the team's email address in the **Team Email** field (id: 2). This is a required field.
3.  In the **Team Contact Person Name** field (id: 3), provide the name of the person responsible for the team (if applicable).
4.  The **Team Phone** field (id: 4) requires you to enter the team's contact phone number.
5.  Enter the team's physical address in the **Team Address** field (id: 5). This field is optional.
6.  For the **Team City** field (id: 6), enter the city where the team is based (if applicable).
7.  Enter the team's province (or state) in the **Team Province** field (id: 7). This field is optional.
8.  In the **Team Postal Code** field (id: 8), enter the team's postal code or zip code (if applicable).
9.  The **Team Website** field (id: 9) is for entering the team's website or online presence (if applicable).
10. In the **Team Social Media** field (id: 10), enter any relevant social media links or handles (if applicable).
11. Enter the League Event ID in the **League Event ID** field (id: 11). This is a required field.
12. In the **Number of Players** field (id: 12), provide the number of players that will be participating in the league event.
13. For the **Number of Teams** field (id: 13), enter the number of teams that will be participating in this event.
14. Select the division for the team from the options provided in the **Division** field (id: 14). This is a required field.
15. Enter the date of the league event in the **Date** field (id: 15). This field is optional.
16. Enter the time of the league event in the **Time** field (id: 16). This is a required field.
17. In the **Additional Notes** field (id: 17), add any further information or remarks regarding the team's participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Team Name** (id: 1, Text, required): Enter the name of your team as you want it to be displayed in the league.
*   **Team Email** (id: 2, email, required): Enter the team's email address for communication purposes.
*   **Team Contact Person Name** (id: 3, Text, optional): Provide the name of the person responsible for your team (if applicable).
*   **Team Phone** (id: 4, Text, required): Enter the team's contact phone number for communication.
*   **Team Address** (id: 5, Text, optional): Enter the team's physical address (if applicable).
*   **Team City** (id: 6, Text, optional): Enter the city where your team is based.
*   **Team Province** (id: 7, Text, optional): Enter the team's province or state.
*   **Team Postal Code** (id: 8, Text, optional): Enter the team's postal code or zip code (if applicable).
*   **Team Website** (id: 9, Text, optional): Enter the team's website or online presence (if applicable).
*   **Team Social Media** (id: 10, Text, optional): Enter any relevant social media links or handles for your team.
*   **League Event ID** (id: 11, Text, required): Enter the ID for the league event you are registering for.
*   **Number of Players** (id: 12, Number, required): Enter the number of players that will participate in this event.
*   **Number of Teams** (id: 13, Number, required): Enter the total number of teams participating in this event.
*   **Division** (id: 14, Select One, required): Select the division for your team from the provided options.
*   **Date** (id: 15, Date, optional): Enter the date of the league event (if applicable).
*   **Time** (id: 16, Time, required): Enter the time of the league event.
*   **Additional Notes** (id: 17, Note, optional): Add any further information or remarks regarding your team's participation.
