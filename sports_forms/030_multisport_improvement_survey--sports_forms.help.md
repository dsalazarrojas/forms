# Multisport Improvement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from users about various sports. It is meant to be a tool to aid in making improvements to different sports and gather insights on how to better serve users' needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the sports you are interested in by choosing from the list provided.
2. Provide your thoughts on how to improve the selected sports in the "Feedback" field.
3. Add any additional comments about the sports in the "Comments" field.
4. Rate the sports on a scale from 1-10 in the "Rating" field.
5. Select the type of entity that you are submitting your feedback to (Coach, Team, Parent, Friend, or Other) in the "Submission" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "Email" field (if you wish to be contacted about your feedback).
7. Optionally, you can provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sport** (`sport`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the sport you are interested in from the list.
	+ Please select the sports that you are interested in by checking the box next to the sport.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false): Share your thoughts on how to improve the selected sport.
	+ This is an opportunity to provide detailed feedback and suggestions on how to enhance the sports.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional comments or thoughts about the sport.
	+ Use this field to provide any additional context or information that may be helpful in understanding your feedback.
* **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, required: false): Rate the sport from 1-10, where 1 is very poor and 10 is excellent.
	+ A rating system to help quantify your opinion on the sports.
* **Submission** (`submission`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the type of entity that you are submitting your feedback to (Coach, Team, Parent, Friend, or Other).
	+ Choose the type of entity that you are submitting your feedback to.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address if you wish to be contacted about your feedback.
	+ If you wish to be contacted about your feedback, please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Provide your phone number if you wish to be contacted about your feedback.
	+ If you wish to be contacted about your feedback, please enter your phone number.

Note: All fields are optional, and users can fill out only the fields they feel necessary.
