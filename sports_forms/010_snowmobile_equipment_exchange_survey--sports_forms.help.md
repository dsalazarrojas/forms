# Snowmobile Equipment Exchange Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Snowmobile Equipment Exchange Survey is a tool used to collect information about snowmobile equipment being offered for exchange. It helps users to accurately and efficiently list their equipment for trade.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your contact details, including your name and contact information.
2. Select the type of snowmobile equipment you are exchanging.
3. Indicate the condition of your snowmobile equipment.
4. Add photos of the equipment (up to 8 photos).
5. Provide detailed information about the equipment, including its price and size.
6. Review and submit your form.

- **Contact Details (id: 1)**: Enter your name, phone number, and email.
- **Equipment Type (id: 2)**: Choose the correct type of snowmobile equipment from the provided options.
- **Condition (id: 3)**: Select the condition of your equipment from the provided options.
