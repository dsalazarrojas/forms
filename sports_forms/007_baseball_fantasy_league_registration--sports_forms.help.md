# Baseball Fantasy League Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Baseball Fantasy League Registration form is used to collect information from potential league participants. This form helps us understand your preferences and requirements for a successful fantasy baseball experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your manager information, including your first name, last name, and email address.
2. Enter your fantasy team name and abbreviation (3-4 characters).
3. Select your years of fantasy baseball experience.
4. Choose your preferred draft format (Snake Draft, Auction Draft, or Salary Cap).
5. Select your preferred draft timing (Spring Training, Opening Day, Anytime Before Season Starts, or No Preference).
6. Specify if you authorize auto-drafting if you're unable to attend (True, False, Use League Settings).
7. Choose your preferred scoring format (Points-Based, Head-to-Head, Rotisserie, or Custom).
8. Confirm that you've read and agree to the league rules.
9. Select your preferred communication method (Email, Text Message, Phone Call, League Website, Mobile App).
10. Decide if you want to receive league updates and notifications (True, False, Weekly Digest Only).
11. Confirm your registration fee payment status (Paid, Will Pay Later, Invoice Needed).
12. Finally, confirm that your registration information is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Manager first name** (`manager_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as the manager of your fantasy baseball team.
* **Manager last name** (`manager_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as the manager of your fantasy baseball team.
* **Email address** (`manager_email`, email, required): Enter your email address to receive important league updates and notifications.
* **Phone number** (`manager_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication purposes (not required).
* **Fantasy team name** (`team_name`, text, required): Enter a unique name for your fantasy baseball team.
* **Team abbreviation** (`team_abbreviation`, text, optional): Enter a 3-4 character abbreviation for your fantasy baseball team (not required).
* **Years of fantasy baseball experience** (`league_experience`, number, optional): Enter your years of experience in fantasy baseball (not required).
* **Preferred draft format** (`draft_preference`, select_one, required): Select your preferred draft format (Snake Draft, Auction Draft, or Salary Cap).
* **Preferred draft timing** (`draft_timing`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred draft timing (Spring Training, Opening Day, Anytime Before Season Starts, or No Preference).
* **Authorize auto-draft** (`autodraft_allowed`, select_one, optional): Specify if you authorize auto-drafting if you're unable to attend (True, False, Use League Settings).
* **Preferred scoring format** (`scoring_preference`, select_one, required): Select your preferred scoring format (Points-Based, Head-to-Head, Rotisserie, or Custom).
* **League rules agreement** (`league_rules_agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm that you've read and agree to the league rules.
* **Preferred communication method** (`communication_method`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred communication method (Email, Text Message, Phone Call, League Website, Mobile App).
* **Receive updates and notifications** (`email_updates`, select_one, optional): Decide if you want to receive league updates and notifications (True, False, Weekly Digest Only).
* **Registration fee payment status** (`registration_fee_paid`, select_one, required): Confirm your registration fee payment status (Paid, Will Pay Later, Invoice Needed).
* **Registration confirmation** (`agreement_confirmation`, select_one, required): Confirm that your registration information is accurate.
