# Horseback Riding Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from horseback riders, including their personal details, horse information, emergency contact, medical conditions, and other relevant data. Please review the form carefully and ensure you provide accurate and complete answers.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your rider information, including your name and contact details.
2. Enter the horse's name, breed, and any other relevant details.
3. In the Emergency Contact section, provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
4. If you have any medical conditions that may affect your riding abilities, list them in the Medical Conditions field.
5. Read and agree to the Waiver Terms, which will help ensure you understand the terms and conditions of your horseback riding.
6. Acknowledge the risks associated with horseback riding and the actions you'll take to minimize them.
7. Finally, provide your signature and date to confirm your agreement.
