# Diver Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Diver Registration Form is a crucial tool for collecting and maintaining accurate information about divers. It ensures that divers' records are up-to-date and complete, which is essential for providing the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out your participant name, contact number, and email address.
2. Enter your date of birth, which is required for age verification purposes.
3. Provide your current address, which is essential for emergency contact purposes.
4. Enter the name and contact number of your emergency contact.
5. If applicable, enter your date of last diving certification, which is important for safety and certification purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter your primary contact number, which will be used for communication and emergency contact purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your personal email address, which will be used for communication and record-keeping purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your current residential or mailing address, which will be used for emergency contact purposes.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, text, required): Enter the contact number of your emergency contact person.
* **Date of Last Diving Certification** (`date_of_last_diving_certification`, date, optional): If you have a diving certification, enter the date of your last certification. This information is optional but recommended for safety and certification purposes.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* Check your existing record before registering to prevent duplication.
* If you are unsure about any information, contact our team for assistance.
