# Personal Training Par Q Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Training Par Q Form is designed to gather information on your physical and medical readiness for personal training. This form will help you assess your ability to safely participate in physical activities, ensuring that you're well-prepared for a training program that suits your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review each field carefully to understand the information being asked.
2.  Answer all fields truthfully to provide accurate information.
3.  Select the relevant options for each field, paying attention to the labels and hints provided.
4.  Ensure you answer all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Has trouble taking deep breath** (physical_readiness): Check yes if you have difficulty breathing deeply, which could be a sign of poor lung function or other respiratory issues.
* **Do you have a medical condition** (medical_condition): Check yes if you have a medical condition that affects your health or well-being.
* **Have you recently had an injury** (recent_injury): Check yes if you've experienced a recent injury that may impact your training.
* **Have you recently had surgery** (recent_surgery): Check yes if you've undergone surgery in the past that may impact your training.
* **Blood pressure** (blood_pressure): Enter your blood pressure reading, if you have one.
* **Resting heart rate** (resting_heart_rate): Enter your resting heart rate in beats per minute.
* **Height** (height): Enter your height in centimeters or meters.
* **Weight** (weight): Enter your weight in kilograms or pounds.
* **Date of Birth** (date_of_birth): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Do you have any medical history** (medical_history): Check yes if you have a known medical history.
* **Medical history details** (medical_history_details): Provide additional information about your medical history if you have a known medical history.

## Tips
* Please answer all fields honestly to ensure your safety and the effectiveness of the training program.
* If you're unsure about any field, consult with your healthcare provider or training staff.
* This form is not a substitute for medical evaluation or consultation.
