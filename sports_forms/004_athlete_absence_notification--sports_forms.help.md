> **Athlete Absence Notification - Help Guide**
======================================

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to notify the relevant parties of an athlete's absence from an event or activity. This form ensures that the team receives accurate and timely information about an athlete's absence, allowing them to make necessary arrangements and adjustments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the athlete's personal information in the **Personal Information** section.
2. Provide the athlete's name and team name in the **Athlete Name** and **Team** fields, respectively.
3. If applicable, provide the coach's name and contact information in the **Coach Name** and **Parent or Guardian Contact Number** fields.
4. Specify the date and time of absence in the **Date of Absence** and **Time of Absence** fields.
5. Choose the reason for the athlete's absence from the available options in the **Reason for Absence** field.
6. If necessary, provide an estimated date for the athlete's return in the **Expected Return Date** field.
7. Enter any additional details or information about the absence in the **Additional Details** field.
8. Choose the preferred notification channels (Email, Phone, or Text) in the **Notify Via** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Personal Information --** (Note)
	+ This section serves as a header for the athlete's basic details.
* **Athlete Name** (text, required)
	+ Enter the full name of the athlete.
* **Team** (text, required)
	+ Enter the name of the athlete's team or squad.
* **Coach Name** (text, optional)
	+ Enter the primary coach or manager's name. If the athlete is not under a coach, leave this field blank.
* **Date of Absence** (date, required)
	+ Enter the date the athlete will be absent.
* **Time of Absence** (time, optional)
	+ If applicable, enter the start time of the athlete's absence.
* **Reason for Absence** (select one, required)
	+ Select the reason that best matches the athlete's situation. Options are:
		+ Illness
		+ Injury
		+ Personal
		+ Academic
		+ Other
* **Expected Return Date** (date, optional)
	+ Enter an estimated date for the athlete's return. If unsure, leave this field blank.
* **Parent or Guardian Contact Number** (text, optional)
	+ Enter the parent or guardian's contact number, including country code if applicable.
* **Additional Details** (text, optional)
	+ Provide any other relevant information about the absence.
* **Notify Via** (select multiple, optional)
	+ Choose the preferred notification channels for the team:
		+ Email
		+ Phone
		+ Text
