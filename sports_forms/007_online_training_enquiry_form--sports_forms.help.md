# Online Training Enquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Training Enquiry Form is used to collect information from individuals interested in training programs in the sports domain. It helps to assess an individual's suitability for the training programs and collects necessary information for their safety and well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the requested information. Make sure to double-check your details for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): If you're interested in our training programs for minors, please enter their date of birth.
* **Height** (`height`, `number`, optional): Enter your height in meters.
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your gender from the options provided.
* **Sport Interests** (`sport_interests`, `select_one`, optional): Choose one or more sports that interest you.
* **Personal Details** (`personal_details`, `note`, optional): Enter any personal details that you feel are important to share with us.
* **Contact Preference** (`contact_preference`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your preferred contact method (phone, email, or SMS).
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, optional): Enter the name and contact information for your emergency contact.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter your address for contact purposes.
* **Training Expectations** (`training_expectations`, `text`, optional): Tell us about your training expectations and goals.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, `text`, optional): If you have any medical conditions or concerns, please enter them here.
* **Medical Certificate** (`medical_certificate`, `text`, optional): If you have a medical certificate or clearance, please upload or paste the relevant document.
* **Medical Certificate URL** (`medical_certificate_url`, `text`, optional): If you have a digital medical certificate, please paste the URL where we can access it.
* **Medical Certificate Date** (`medical_certificate_date`, `date`, optional): If you have a medical certificate with a valid date of issue, please enter it.
* **Medical Certificate Number** (`medical_certificate_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the medical certificate number (if applicable).
* **Medical Certificate Issuer** (`medical_certificate_issuer`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the medical issuer (if applicable).
* **Medical Certificate Issue Date** (`medical_certificate_issue_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the medical certificate was issued (if applicable).
* **Medical Certificate Issue Number** (`medical_certificate_issue_number`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the medical certificate was issued (if applicable).
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required): Please submit your form to complete your registration.

Note: All fields marked as required are mandatory and must be filled out to complete the form.
