# Coach Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Coach Report Form is a comprehensive report designed to capture essential information about a team's performance, injuries, and resources during a match or training session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information about the team and the event/match/session before starting the form.
2. Enter your name and contact information in the "Coach name" field.
3. Enter the date of the report in the "Report date" field.
4. Provide a brief summary of the team's performance, including outcomes, in the "Team performance summary" field.
5. List any injuries reported during the event/match/session in the "Injuries reported" field.
6. Identify standout players and the reasons for their outstanding performance in the "Standout players" field.
7. Describe the required next steps for follow-up actions in the "Follow up actions" field.
8. Specify any resources needed for future events or sessions in the "Resources needed" field.
9. Add any additional comments or information that are not captured in the other fields in the "Additional comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Coach Report --**: This page is the header of the form, providing a general overview of the report.
* **Coach name**: Enter your full name as the coach.
* **Report date**: Enter the date of the report, in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Event or match name**: Enter the name of the event or match/session being reported on.
* **Team performance summary**: Provide a brief summary of the team's performance, including outcomes.
* **Injuries reported**: List any injuries reported during the event/match/session.
* **Standout players**: Identify standout players and the reasons for their outstanding performance.
* **Follow up actions**: Describe the required next steps for follow-up actions.
* **Resources needed**: Specify any resources needed for future events or sessions.
* **Additional comments**: Add any additional comments or information that are not captured in the other fields.
