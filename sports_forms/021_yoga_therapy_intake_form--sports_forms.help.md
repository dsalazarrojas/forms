# yoga_therapy_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The yoga therapy intake form is designed to collect basic contact information and any medical issues or concerns that may affect the client's participation in yoga therapy sessions. Please use this form to provide your first name, last name, date of appointment, time of appointment, email, phone number, any medical issues, and a brief note about your visit. This form should only be filled out once for each new client.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Select a date for your appointment in the "Date" field.
4. Choose a time for your appointment in the "Time" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. If you have any medical issues or concerns, type them in the "Medical Issues" field.
8. If you have any additional information or comments, type them in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional: false): Enter your last name.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional: false): Select a date for your appointment.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional: false): Choose a time for your appointment.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Medical Issues** (`medical_issues`, text, required/optional: false): If you have any medical issues or concerns, type them here.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required/optional: false): If you have any additional information or comments, type them in this field.
