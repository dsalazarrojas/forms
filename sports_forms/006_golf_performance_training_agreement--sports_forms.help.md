# Golf Performance Training Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Golf Performance Training Agreement is a form that collects information from participants for golf performance training. This form is meant to be completed by the participant and is used to establish a clear understanding of the training program's terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the requested information. Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Name (<participant_name>)
* A text field for the participant's name.
* Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Participant Email (<participant_email>)
* A text field for the participant's email address.
* Please enter a valid email address where you can be reached.

### Participant Phone (<participant_phone>)
* A text field for the participant's phone number.
* Please enter a valid phone number where you can be reached.

### Participant Address (<participant_address>)
* A text field for the participant's address.
* Please enter your current address.

### Participant Date of Birth (<participant_dob>)
* A date field for the participant's date of birth.
* Please enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Participant Signature (<participant_signature>)
* A text field for the participant's signature.
* Please sign below to acknowledge that you have read and understood the terms and conditions of the training program.

### Training Programs (<training_programs>)
* A select multiple field for selecting training programs.
* Please select all applicable programs that you will be participating in.

### Training Coach (<training_coach>)
* A text field for the training coach's name.
* Please enter the name of your assigned coach.

### Training Start Date (<training_start_date>)
* A date field for the training start date.
* Please enter the start date of your training program.

### Training End Date (<training_end_date>)
* A date field for the training end date.
* Please enter the end date of your training program.

### Training Duration (<training_duration>)
* A text field for the training duration.
* Please enter the duration of your training program.

### Training Location (<training_location>)
* A text field for the training location.
* Please enter the location of your training program.

### Participant Agreement (<participant_agreement>)
* A note field for the participant's agreement.
* Please read and sign below to acknowledge that you have read and understood the terms and conditions of the training program.

### Coach Agreement (<coach_agreement>)
* A note field for the coach's agreement.
* Please read and sign below to acknowledge that you have read and understood the terms and conditions of the training program.

### Training Coach Agreement (<training_coach_agreement>)
* A note field for the training coach's agreement.
* Please read and sign below to acknowledge that you have read and understood the terms and conditions of the training program.

### Emergency Contact (<emergency_contact>)
* A text field for the emergency contact's name.
* Please enter the name of an emergency contact person.

### Emergency Contact Relationship (<emergency_contact_relationship>)
* A text field for the emergency contact's relationship.
* Please enter the relationship of the emergency contact person to you.

### Emergency Contact Phone (<emergency_contact_phone>)
* A text field for the emergency contact's phone number.
* Please enter a valid phone number where the emergency contact can be reached.

### Emergency Contact Address (<emergency_contact_address>)
* A text field for the emergency contact's address.
* Please enter the address of the emergency contact person.

### Emergency Contact Email (<emergency_contact_email>)
* A text field for the emergency contact's email address.
* Please enter a valid email address where the emergency contact can be reached.

### Emergency Contact Agreement (<emergency_contact_agreement>)
* A note field for the emergency contact's agreement.
* Please read and sign below to acknowledge that you have read and understood the terms and conditions of the training program.

### Participant Emergency Contact Agreement (<participant_emergency_contact_agreement>)
* A note field for the participant's emergency contact agreement.
* Please read and sign below to acknowledge that you have read and understood the terms and conditions of the training program.

### Participant Medical Info (<participant_medical_info>)
* A text field for the participant's medical information.
* Please enter any medical information that may be relevant to your training program.

### Participant Medical Info Agreement (<participant_medical_info_agreement>)
* A note field for the participant's medical information agreement.
