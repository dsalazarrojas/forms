# Injury Investigation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Injury Investigation Form is a tool to gather information and document the investigation of workplace incidents.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the Incident Date.
2.  Select the Injury Type.
3.  Enter the Incident Location.
4.  Enter the Employer Name.
5.  Enter the Investigator Name.
6.  Select the Incident Type.
7.  Enter the Incident Description.
8.  Enter the Date of Injury.
9.  Enter the Time of Injury.
10. Select the Investigation Status.
11. Enter the Investigation Date.
12. Enter the Investigation Time.
13. Enter the Investigation Notes.
14. Enter the Employer Awareness.
15. Enter the Investigator Recommendation.
16. Enter the Incident Report Number.
17. Enter the Incident Report Date.
18. Enter the Investigation Report Number.
19. Enter the Investigation Report Date.
20. Enter the Incident Reporter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date on which the injury occurred.
*   **Injury Type** (`injury_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of injury from the options provided.
*   **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, `text`, required): Enter the specific location where the incident occurred.
*   **Witness Name** (`witness_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the witness, if applicable.
*   **Witness Statement** (`witness_statement`, `note`, optional): Enter a statement from the witness about what they saw or heard.
*   **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the employer involved in the incident.
*   **Investigator Name** (`investigator_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person conducting the investigation.
*   **Job Title** (`investigator_title`, `text`, optional): Enter the job title of the investigator, which is not currently used in the form.
*   **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of incident from the options provided.
*   **Incident Location Details** (`incident_location_details`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional details about the incident location.
*   **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, `text`, required): Enter a description of the incident.
*   **Date of Injury** (`incident_date_of_injury`, `date`, required): Enter the date on which the injury occurred.
*   **Time of Injury** (`incident_time_of_injury`, `time`, required): Enter the time at which the injury occurred.
*   **Investigation Status** (`investigation_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the current status of the investigation from the options provided.
*   **Investigation Date** (`investigation_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date on which the investigation was completed.
*   **Investigation Time** (`investigation_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time at which the investigation was completed.
*   **Investigation Notes** (`investigation_notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or observations about the incident.
*   **Employer Awareness** (`employer_awareness`, `text`, optional): Enter the status of the employer's awareness of the incident.
*   **Investigator Recommendation** (`investigator_recommendation`, `text`, optional): Enter the investigator's recommendation, which is not currently used in the form.
*   **Incident Report Number** (`incident_report_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the incident report number, which is not currently used in the form.
*   **Incident Report Date** (`incident_report_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the incident report date, which is not currently used in the form.
*   **Investigation Report Number** (`investigation_report_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the investigation report number, which is not currently used in the form.
*   **Investigation Report Date** (`investigation_report_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the investigation report date, which is not currently used in the form.
*   **Incident Reporter** (`incident_reporter`, `text`, optional): Enter the person who reported the incident.
*   **Witness Signature** (`witness_signature`, `text`, optional): Enter the witness's signature, which is not currently used in the form.
