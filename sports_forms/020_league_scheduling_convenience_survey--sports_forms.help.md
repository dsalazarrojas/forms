# League Scheduling Convenience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The League Scheduling Convenience Survey is a form designed to collect information from users about their availability and preferences for league scheduling. This form will help us better understand your schedule and preferences, allowing us to create a more convenient and enjoyable league experience for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out this form with your information, following the instructions for each field.
2. Ensure that you complete all required fields (indicated by an asterisk *) and any other fields that you wish to provide information for.
3. Once you have completed the form, click the "Submit" button to submit your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as you would like it to be displayed in the league.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as you would like it to be displayed in the league.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you about league information and updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes only.
* **Availability** (`availability`, select multiple, required: false):
	+ Select all the times of the week when you are available to play.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, time, required: false):
	+ Enter a time when you would like to play, in 24 hour format (HH:MM).
* **Available Days** (`available_days`, select multiple, required: false):
	+ Select all the days of the week when you are available to play.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required: false):
	+ Enter the date you are available to start playing.

## Tips

* Please be honest about your availability to ensure that we can schedule games that work for everyone.
* If you have any special requests or conflicts, please let us know in the notes section.
* We will use this information to create a convenient and enjoyable league experience for everyone.
