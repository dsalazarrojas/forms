# Fitness Class Series Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers who are interested in purchasing a fitness class series. It will ask questions about their schedule preferences, participant details, and payment details to help us complete the preorder process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Schedule Preferences**: Select your preferred schedule for the fitness class series. You can choose from the options provided, and select all that apply.
2. **Participant Details**: Enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
3. **Fitness Class**: Choose the fitness class you are interested in purchasing.
4. **Payment Details**: Enter your payment information. This is a numeric field where you can enter any value to indicate your payment method.
5. **Notes**: Add any additional information you think is necessary for the preorder process.
6. **Participant Confirmation**: Choose whether or not you would like to be contacted about your preorder.
7. **Participant Confirmation 2**: This is another confirmation question to ensure that you are aware of the preorder details.
8. **Schedule Preferences 2**: Select your preferred schedule for the class series.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Schedule Preferences**: This field allows you to select your preferred schedule for the fitness class series. You can choose from multiple options, and select all that apply. This will help us determine which schedule is best for you.
* **Participant Details**: This field is for you to enter any other relevant details about yourself. You can add any information you think is necessary for the preorder process.
* **Payment Details**: This is a numeric field where you can enter any value to indicate your payment method. Please enter the required numeric value for your payment method.
* **Notes**: This is a field where you can add any additional information you think is necessary for the preorder process.
* **Participant Confirmation**: This field will ask you to confirm whether or not you would like to be contacted about your preorder. Please select the option that best applies to your preference.
* **Participant Confirmation 2**: This is another confirmation question to ensure that you are aware of the preorder details.
* **Schedule Preferences 2**: This field will allow you to select your preferred schedule for the class series. Please choose the option that best suits your needs.
* **Fitness Class**: This field will ask you to choose the fitness class you are interested in purchasing. You can select from the provided options.
