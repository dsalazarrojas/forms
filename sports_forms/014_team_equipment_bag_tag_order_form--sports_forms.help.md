# Team Equipment Bag Tag Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to order team equipment bags. It collects user input, select the type and size of the equipment bag, and other relevant information to ensure accurate fulfillment of the order.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely, ensuring all required fields are completed.
2. Select the type of equipment bag you need from the "Bag Type" dropdown menu.
3. Choose the size of the equipment bag that you require.
4. Enter the date and time you would like to pickup the equipment bag.
5. Add any additional notes or comments about your order in the "Note" field.
6. Enter your contact email address so we can get in touch with you about your order.
7. Enter your contact phone number so we can get in touch with you about your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, `text`, required: false): This is the text field where you can enter any additional information about your order.
* **Bag Type** (`bag_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of equipment bag you need. This field is a dropdown menu with two options: "Bag A" and "Bag B".
* **Bag Size** (`bag_size`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the size of the equipment bag you require. This field is a multiple select menu with three options: "Small", "Medium", and "Large".
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you would like to pickup the equipment bag.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you would like to pickup the equipment bag.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments about your order.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your order.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can get in touch with you about your order.
