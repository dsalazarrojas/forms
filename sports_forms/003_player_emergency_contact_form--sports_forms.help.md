# Player Emergency Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect emergency contact information for players, including their own contact information and the contact information for up to two emergency contacts. The form helps ensure that the team has the necessary contact information in case of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

* Enter your personal contact information:
	+ First Name: Enter your given name (e.g., John).
	+ Last Name: Enter your surname (e.g., Doe).
	+ Phone: Enter your phone number (e.g., 123-456-7890).
	+ Address: Enter your address (e.g., 123 Main St).
	+ Email: Enter your email address (e.g., [johndoe@example.com](mailto:johndoe@example.com)).
* Enter your emergency contact information:
	+ Emergency Contact Name: Enter the name of the person to be contacted in case of an emergency (e.g., Mother).
	+ Emergency Contact Phone: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact (e.g., 987-654-3210).
	+ Emergency Contact Address: Enter the address of the emergency contact (e.g., 456 Elm St).
	+ Emergency Contact Email: Enter the email address of the emergency contact (e.g., [mothersmail@example.com](mailto:mothersmail@example.com)).
	+ Relationship: Describe your relationship with the emergency contact (e.g., Mother).
* If you have a second emergency contact, please enter their information below:
	+ Emergency Contact Name 2: Enter the name of the second emergency contact.
	+ Emergency Contact Phone 2: Enter the phone number of the second emergency contact.
	+ Emergency Contact Address 2: Enter the address of the second emergency contact.
	+ Emergency Contact Email 2: Enter the email address of the second emergency contact.
	+ Emergency Contact Relationship 2: Describe your relationship with the second emergency contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your given name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your address.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address**: Enter the address of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the emergency contact.
* **Relationship**: Describe your relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Name 2**: Enter the name of the second emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone 2**: Enter the phone number of the second emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address 2**: Enter the address of the second emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email 2**: Enter the email address of the second emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship 2**: Describe your relationship with the second emergency contact.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Emergency Contact Sport**: Select the sport you are involved in.
* **Emergency Contact Sport 2**: Select the sport of the second emergency contact.
* **Date of Birth Other**: Select the other date of birth option.
* **Emergency Contact Address 2**: Enter the address of the second emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Select the relationship type for the second emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Sport Other**: Select whether the emergency contact is involved in a sport.
* **Emergency Contact Email 2**: Enter the email address of the second emergency contact.

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter your email address and phone number correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the team administrator.
* If you need to add additional emergency contacts, please contact the team administrator for assistance.
