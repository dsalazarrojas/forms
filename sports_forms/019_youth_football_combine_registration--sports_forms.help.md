# youth_football_combine_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for registering youth football players.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the player's details in the "Player Details" section.
2. Provide the player's contact details in the "Contact Details" section.
3. Obtain parental consent by filling out the "Parental Consent" section.
4. If applicable, provide the emergency contact information in the "Emergency Contact" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Player Details
* **Player Details** (text): Enter the player's name, age, and other relevant information.

### Contact Details
* **Contact Details** (text): Enter the player's address, phone number, and other contact information.

### Parental Consent
* **Parental Consent** (text): Sign this section to confirm that the parent or guardian has reviewed and agreed to the terms and conditions of the football program.

### Emergency Contact
* **Emergency Contact** (text): Provide the name and contact information of the person to be contacted in case of an emergency.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check the information provided for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant authorities or administrators.
