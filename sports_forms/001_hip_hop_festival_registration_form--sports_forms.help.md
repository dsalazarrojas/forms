# Hip Hop Festival Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register your interest in attending the Hip Hop Festival. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to help us better understand your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as follows:
- Fill out your personal details (page 1)
- Enter your name, email address, phone number, and any other required information (fields: Full Name, Email, Phone Number)
- Select your area of interest (Hip Hop, Rap, Electronic, Pop, or Rock) (Area Of Interest)
- Rate us, if you choose to do so (Rating)
- Provide any other contact information and address you'd like to share (Contact Info, Address)
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Enter your complete name, including any middle names.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number, including any relevant extension (e.g., 123-456-7890 ext. 123).
* **Area Of Interest**: Choose the type of music that you are most interested in. Please select all that apply.
  * Hip Hop
  * Rap
  * Electronic
  * Pop
  * Rock
* **Rating**: If you feel inclined, provide us with a rating of your experience with our previous events. 
  1 = Poor
  2 = Average
  3 = Good
  4 = Very Good
  5 = Excellent
* **Contact Info**: Provide any additional contact information you'd like to share, such as a preferred email or phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your address to help us plan logistics and provide you with an optimal experience. 

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your registration is complete and we can best accommodate your needs.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please include them in the "Contact Info" field.
* You are welcome to share your music preferences, but it is not mandatory.
