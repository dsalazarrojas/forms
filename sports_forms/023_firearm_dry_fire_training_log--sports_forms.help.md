# Firearm Dry Fire Training Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and track data on dry fire training sessions for firearm users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the dry fire training session in the "Dry Fire Date" field.
2. Select the firearm used and enter the caliber in the corresponding fields.
3. Enter the number of rounds fired in the "Rounds Fired" field.
4. Enter the time spent practicing in the "Time Spent Practicing" field.
5. Optionally, enter the target distance and rounds stripped if applicable.
6. Select whether an assigned coach was present during the training session.
7. Enter any additional notes on the session.
8. Enter your email and phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Dry Fire Date** (`dry_fire_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the dry fire training session.
- **Firearm Used** (`firearm_used`, text, required): Select the firearm used during the training session.
- **Caliber Used** (`caliber_used`, text, optional): Enter the caliber used during the training session.
- **Rounds Fired** (`rounds_fired`, number, required): Enter the number of rounds fired during the training session.
- **Dry Fire Type** (`dry_fire_type`, text, optional): Optionally, select the type of dry fire training session (e.g., "Basic", "Advanced", etc.).
- **Target Distance** (`target_distance`, number, optional): Enter the target distance during the training session, if applicable.
- **Rounds Stripped** (`rounds_stripped`, number, optional): Enter the number of rounds stripped during the training session, if applicable.
- **Time Spent Practicing** (`time_spent_practicing`, text, required): Enter the total time spent practicing during the training session.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes on the session.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Assigned Coach** (`assigned_coach`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether an assigned coach was present during the training session.
