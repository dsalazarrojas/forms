# Research Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

This Research Questionnaire form is designed to collect data from individuals for research purposes. It is intended to help researchers gather information on various topics, including physical activity, aging, and other relevant factors.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please review each section carefully and provide accurate information.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and completely.
3. If you're unsure about any question, please ask the researcher or study coordinator for clarification.
4. If you have any comments or additional information to provide, please use the Comments section at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (`section_w`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a title for the form, which will help identify the research project or study.
* **Physical Inactivity** (`section_a`, `select_one`, required): Answer whether you are physically inactive or not.
* **Aging** (`section_b`, `text`, required): Provide information about your age, including your birthdate.
* **Physical Activity** (`section_c`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all physical activities you're involved in.
* **Conclusion** (`section_d`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to provide any additional comments or insights, please use this section.
* **Email** (`section_e`, `text`, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`section_f`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date** (`section_g`, `date`, required): Provide your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Time** (`section_h`, `time`, required): Provide your time of birth in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **Address** (`section_i`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose all locations where you've lived.
* **Comments** (`section_j`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or insights.
* **Researcher** (`section_k`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to provide the name or contact information of the researcher or study coordinator.
* **Assigned Tool** (`section_l`, `text`, optional): If you're using any specific tools or resources for the study, please describe them.
* **Category** (`section_m`, `text`, optional): Enter any relevant categories or tags for the study.
* **Form ID** (`section_n`, `text`, optional): Enter any unique identifier for the form.
* **Description** (`section_o`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief description of the study or project.
* **Section Q** (`section_p`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information or questions not covered in other sections.
* **Output file** (`section_q`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to upload any output files, please use this section.
* **Section S** (`section_s`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information or notes.
* **Section T** (`section_t`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information or notes.
* **Form** (`section_u`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information or notes.
* **Section V** (`section_v`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information or notes.
* **Section W** (`section_w`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information or notes.
* **Section X** (`section_x`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information or notes.
