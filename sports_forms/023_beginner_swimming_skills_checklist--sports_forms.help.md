# Beginner Swimming Skills Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the swimming skills of a beginner swimmer, evaluating their comfort level, abilities, and safety knowledge in various aspects of swimming.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully before filling it out to ensure you understand each question and its relevance to the swimmer's abilities.
2. Answer each question based on your observation and the swimmer's self-reporting, if applicable.
3. For each question, select the answer that best describes the swimmer's performance.
4. Fill in the "Instructor Notes" field with additional comments or recommendations if necessary.
5. Once complete, submit the form to document the swimmer's progress and to help identify areas where they need improvement.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when assessing the swimmer's abilities.
- Refer back to the swimmer's previous lessons or experiences if unsure about any question.
- Use your best judgment based on the assessment criteria.
- Provide clear and specific feedback for the swimmer to improve.
- Consider suggesting areas of focus or recommended next lessons for the swimmer.
