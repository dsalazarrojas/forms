# Edit Volleyball Match Score - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Edit Volleyball Match Score" form is used to correct or update the score of a volleyball match. This form helps sports administrators or officials accurately record match scores for record-keeping, statistics, or dispute resolution purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the unique ID of the match in the "Match Id" field.
2.  Select the date of the match in the "Date" field.
3.  Enter the time of the match in the "Match Time" field.
4.  Input the initial score of Team 1 in the "Score Team 1" field.
5.  Input the initial score of Team 2 in the "Score Team 2" field.
6.  If necessary, provide a reason for the correction in the "Reason" field.
7.  Enter the corrected score for Team 1 in the "Score Corrected To" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Match Id (1)**: Enter the unique ID of the match being edited.
* **Date (2)**: Select the date of the match.
* **Match Time (3)**: Enter the time of the match.
* **Score Team 1 (4)**: Input the initial score of Team 1.
* **Score Team 2 (5)**: Input the initial score of Team 2.
* **Reason (6)**: Provide a reason for correcting the score, if necessary.
* **Score Corrected To (7)**: Enter the corrected score for the specified team.

## Tips
- Make sure you have the correct match ID to avoid confusion.
- Ensure you input accurate scores and dates to maintain records' integrity.
- Use the "Reason" field to provide context for future reference.
