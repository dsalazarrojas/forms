# online-training-consultation-questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users who want to receive guidance on online training consultations. It collects general information to help the consultant understand the user's needs and preferences.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Provide general information about yourself, including your contact details.
2. Answer the health and diet information questions truthfully to help the consultant provide relevant guidance.
3. Share your fitness progress and personal goals with the consultant to get tailored advice.
4. If you have any medical conditions, provide a note with the required information.
5. Select the relevant options for your preferences.
6. Enter your email address and phone number if you want to be contacted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information**: This field is for providing your name, address, or any other general information that you want to share with the consultant.
* **Health and Diet Info**: Please answer this field truthfully as it will help the consultant provide relevant guidance on your health and diet.
* **Fitness Progress**: Enter your current fitness progress, e.g., your age, height, and weight.
* **Personal Goals**: Be specific about your goals, e.g., losing weight, building muscle, or improving flexibility.
* **Medical Info**: If you have any medical conditions, please provide a note with the required information. This will help the consultant understand any limitations or precautions that need to be taken.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address if you want to be contacted by the consultant.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted by the consultant.
* **Select One**: Select one option from the list that best represents your preference.
* **Select Multiple**: Select multiple options from the list that best represent your preferences.
* **Date**: Enter your preferred date for the consultation.
* **Time**: Enter your preferred time for the consultation.
* **Medical Condition**: If you have any medical conditions, please provide a brief note with the required information.

## Tips
* Be honest and detailed in your answers to help the consultant provide the best guidance.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can ask for clarification or leave it blank.
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to get the most accurate guidance.
