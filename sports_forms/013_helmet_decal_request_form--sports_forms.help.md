# Helmet Decal Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users requesting helmet decals for their teams or schools. It helps us understand their requirements, preferences, and needs for custom decals. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your team or school name in the "Team or School Affiliation" field.
2. Describe your preferred artwork design in the "Artwork Description" field.
3. Select the size of your decal from the options in the "Decal Size" field.
4. Choose the color(s) you prefer for your decal in the "Color(s) Choice" field.
5. Enter the quantity of decals you want to order in the "Quantity" field.
6. (Optional) You can select your preferred print shop from the "Print Shop" field.
7. If you'd like to add any additional artwork, enter it in the "Additional Artwork" field.
8. Finally, provide any additional notes or requirements in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team or School Affiliation** (`team`, `text`, required: false): Enter your team or school name, so we can identify your group.
* **Artwork Description** (`artwork`, `text`, required: false): Describe the design or artwork you have in mind for your decals.
* **Decal Size** (`size`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose a size for your decal: Small, Medium, or Large.
* **Color(s) Choice** (`color`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more colors for your decal: Black, White, or Red.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, required: true): Enter the number of decals you'd like to order.
* **Print Shop** (`print_shop`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select your preferred print shop: Local, National, or Online.
* **Additional Artwork** (`artwork_2`, `text`, required: false): If you'd like to add any extra artwork, enter it here.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any extra notes or requirements for your order.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and clear information, especially for artwork design and color selection.
* You can add as many additional artworks as you like, but keep in mind that each will be a separate decal.
* For quantity, please enter a whole number, not fractions or decimals.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
