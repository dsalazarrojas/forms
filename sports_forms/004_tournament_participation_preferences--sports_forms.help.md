# Tournament Participation Preferences - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your participation in a tournament. This information will be used to create a schedule and manage teams for the tournament.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details and availability on **Page 1: User Details**.
2. Select your preferences for the tournament on **Page 2: Tournament Preferences**.
3. Enter your team's details, including name and lead, on **Page 3: Team Details**.
4. Select your team's position on **Page 4: Team Position**.
5. Enter the number of team members on **Page 5: Team Members**.
6. Enter the name of the tournament on **Page 6: Tournament Name**.
7. Select the date and time of the tournament on **Page 7: Tournament Date and Time**.
8. Enter any additional details about the tournament on **Page 8: Tournament Details**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details** (Text, **user_details**, required: false): Enter your name, email, and any other relevant personal details.
* **Availability** (Date, **availability**, required: false): Select a date when you are available to participate in the tournament.
* **Tournament Preferences** (Select One, **tournament_preferences**, required: false): Choose your preference for the tournament, such as "I want to participate" or "I want to watch".
* **Team Details** (Text, **team_details**, required: false): Enter your team's name and any other relevant details.
* **Team Lead** (Text, **team_lead**, required: false): Enter the name of the person leading your team.
* **Team Name** (Text, **team_name**, required: false): Enter your team's name.
* **Team Position** (Select Multiple, **team_position**, required: false): Select your team's position in the tournament, such as "winner" or "runner-up".
* **Team Members** (Number, **team_members**, required: false): Enter the number of team members participating in the tournament.
* **Tournament Name** (Text, **tournament_name**, required: false): Enter the name of the tournament.
* **Tournament Date** (Date, **tournament_date**, required: false): Select the date of the tournament.
* **Tournament Time** (Time, **tournament_time**, required: false): Select the time of the tournament.
* **Tournament Details** (Text, **tournament_details**, required: false): Enter any additional details about the tournament, such as rules or requirements.
