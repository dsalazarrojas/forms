# Snow Sports Staff Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Snow Sports Staff Application form is designed to gather information from applicants interested in joining a snow sports team. By filling out this form, you will be helping us to better understand your qualifications, skills, and experience, which will be reviewed for potential staff positions within our organization.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form accurately and efficiently, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out all required fields (indicated by a red star):** Ensure you fill out all fields with a red star to increase your chances of being considered for staff positions.
2. **Be concise in your answers:** Provide brief and relevant answers to each field.
3. **Proofread your form:** Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting.
