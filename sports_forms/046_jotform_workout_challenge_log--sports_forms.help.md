# Jotform Workout Challenge Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Jotform Workout Challenge Log is a form designed to help users track and record their workout progress over time. 
It allows users to input their workout details, including date, exercises performed, duration, and calories burned, helping them make informed decisions about their workout routine.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your User Name to identify your workout session.
2. Select the Date of your workout.
3. Describe the Exercises Performed in the space provided.
4. Enter the Duration of your workout in minutes.
5. Choose your Workout Type (Strength, Cardio, or Yoga).
6. Select the Intensity of your workout (Low, Medium, or High).
7. Enter the Weight Used (if applicable).
8. Record the Weight Lifted (if applicable).
9. Calculate the Calories Burned based on your workout duration and intensity.
10. Add any Comments about your workout.
11. Enter your submission details (Submitted By).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Name (User Name, text, optional):** Enter your name to identify your workout session.
- **Date (Date, date, optional):** Enter the date of your workout.
- **Exercises Performed (Exercises Performed, text, optional):** Describe the exercises you performed during your workout.
- **Duration (Duration, number, optional):** Enter the duration of your workout in minutes.
- **Workout Type (Workout Type, select_one, optional):** Choose the type of workout you did (Strength, Cardio, or Yoga).
- **Intensity (Intensity, select_multiple, optional):** Select the intensity of your workout (Low, Medium, or High).
- **Weight Used (Weight Used, text, optional):** If you used weights during your workout, enter the weight you used.
- **Weight Lifted (Weight Lifted, number, optional):** If you lifted weights during your workout, enter the weight lifted.
- **Calories Burned (Calories Burned, number, optional):** Calculate the calories burned based on your workout duration and intensity.
- **Comments (Comments, text, optional):** Add any comments about your workout.
- **Submitted By (Submitted By, text, optional):** Enter your submission details.
