# Volunteer Form For Race Event - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for volunteers who want to help out at a race event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out your first name and last name in the respective fields.
2. Enter a valid email address in the email field.
3. If you have a phone number, enter it in the phone field. If not, you can leave this field blank.
4. Select your preferred shift: Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.
5. If you have availability for specific times, you can select them in the Availability field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Please enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): If you have a phone number, please enter it. If not, you can leave this field blank.
* **Shift** (`shift`, `select_one`, required): Please select your preferred shift for the event. We have options for Morning, Afternoon, and Evening.
* **Availability** (`availability`, `time`, optional): Please select any specific times you are available to work. You can choose multiple times if needed.

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are completed to the best of your ability.
* If you are unable to work a certain shift, please select the "I don't have a preference" option for that shift.
* If you need to provide any additional information, please include it in the email field or in a separate message to us.
* We appreciate your time and effort in helping out at the race event!
