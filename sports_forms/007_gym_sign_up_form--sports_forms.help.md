# Gym Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect basic contact information and account credentials for users to create a gym membership account or log in.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Type in your **Email** address in the "Email" field. Note that this is the address that will be used for account verification and correspondence.
4. Enter your **Password** in the "Password" field. This is required to secure your account.
5. Confirm your **Password** in the "Confirm Password" field to ensure accuracy.

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter a valid email address that we can use to verify your account.
