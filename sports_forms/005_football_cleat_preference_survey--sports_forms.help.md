# Football Cleat Preference Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather user preferences and feedback on football cleats, which will be used to inform product development and marketing decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select the type of cleat that best describes your preference (Stud or Non-Stud) on Page 2.
* Choose your preferred brand of football cleats from the list on Page 3.
* Select the features that are most important to you from the list on Page 4.
* If you want to provide additional feedback, enter it in the text box on Page 5.
* If you wish to contact us for any reason, enter your email address in the text box on Page 6.
* You can leave the satisfaction and comments fields blank, as they are optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Football Cleat Type** (Page 2):
	+ This field asks what type of cleat you prefer (Stud or Non-Stud).
* **Football Cleat Brand** (Page 3):
	+ This field asks which brand of cleats you prefer (Adidas, Nike, Under Armour, Reebok, New Balance, or Other).
* **Football Cleat Features** (Page 4):
	+ This field allows you to select the features that are most important to you. You can choose from Studs, Cushioning, Support, or Other.
* **Satisfaction** (Page 5):
	+ This field is optional and asks how satisfied you are with your current cleats (on a scale of 1-10).
* **Comments** (Page 5):
	+ This field is optional and allows you to provide any additional feedback or comments about your cleats.
* **Contact Info** (Page 6):
	+ This field is optional and asks for your email address so that we can contact you if needed.
