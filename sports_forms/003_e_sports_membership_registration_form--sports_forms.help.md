# e_sports_membership_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The e_sports_membership_registration_form is a form designed to gather information from potential members of our e-sports organization. This form will help us understand your interests, preferences, and membership plans, ensuring we can provide the best experience for you as a member.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant information on **Page 1: Participant Information**.
2. Provide your contact information on **Page 2: Contact Information**.
3. Choose your preferred membership plan on **Page 3: Membership Plan**.
4. Select your game preferences on **Page 4: Game Preferences**.
5. Enter your email address on **Page 5: Email**.
6. Enter your phone number on **Page 6: Phone**.
7. Enter your birth date on **Page 7: Birth Date**.
8. Optionally, provide your team information on **Page 8: Team Information**.
9. Enter your team name on **Page 9: Team Name**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information (Page 1)**: This field is for providing your personal details such as name and username.
* **Contact Information (Page 2)**: Enter your contact email address and other relevant contact details.
* **Membership Plan (Page 3)**: Choose one of the three membership plans offered by our organization: Basic, Premium, or Advanced.
* **Game Preferences (Page 4)**: Select multiple games you're interested in playing with our organization.
* **Email (Page 5)**: Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone (Page 6)**: Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Birth Date (Page 7)**: Enter your birthdate for age verification purposes.
* **Team Information (Page 8)**: Provide details about your team, if applicable.
* **Team Name (Page 9)**: Enter your team name, if you're part of a team.

## Tips
* Please ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask our support team for help.
* We'll review your form submission and get back to you soon after.
