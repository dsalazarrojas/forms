# running_course_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "running_course_feedback_form" is designed to collect feedback from participants after a running course. This form helps us evaluate the effectiveness of the course and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your Participant Name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Provide your Feedback about the course in the "Feedback" field.
3. Rate your Satisfaction with the course by selecting a number in the "Satisfaction Rating" field.
4. Rate your Overall Experience with the course by selecting a number in the "Overall Experience" field.
5. Select the Trainer you worked with from the list in the "Trainer" field.
6. If desired, provide additional Comments about your experience.
7. (Optional) Rate the Program by selecting a number in the "Program Rating" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required): Share your thoughts and opinions about the course.
* **Satisfaction Rating** (`satisfaction_rating`, number, optional): Rate how satisfied you are with the course. 
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, number, required): Rate your overall experience with the course.
* **Trainer** (`trainer`, select_one, required): Choose the Trainer you had during the course.
* **Program Rating** (`program_rating`, number, optional): Rate the Program. 
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions about the course.
* **Program Rating** (`program_rating`, number, optional): (this field is not actually needed as it's the same as the one on page 1)

Please note that the "Program Rating" field has been removed as it's a duplicate of the "Program Rating" field on page 1.
