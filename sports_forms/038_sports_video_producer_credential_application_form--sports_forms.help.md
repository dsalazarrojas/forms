# Sports Video Producer Credential Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for sports video producers to apply for a credential that certifies their skills and experience in producing sports-related content.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your sports form name (e.g., sports-forms).
2.  Enter your videographer form name (e.g., videographer-forms).
3.  Provide a brief description of your work as a sports video producer.
4.  Enter your customer's name.
5.  Enter your email address.
6.  Enter your phone number.
7.  Enter your work address.
8.  Choose the media credential that best describes your experience (e.g., Option A, Option B, or Option C).
9.  Select the sports media access you have (e.g., Option A, Option B, or Option C).
10. Select the sports media submissions you are able to make (e.g., Option A, Option B, or Option C).
11. Enter the name of your agency.
12.  Enter the URL of your agency.
13. Enter the name of the project you are working on.
14.  Enter the URL of the project.
15. Read and agree to the agreement.
16.  Choose the agreement date (if applicable).
17.  Choose the agreement time (if applicable).
18.  Enter the duration of the agreement (in years).
19.  Enter any additional remarks about the agreement.
20.  If applicable, enter the file name of the agreement.
21.  If applicable, enter the URL of the agreement file.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **sports-forms (Text)**: Enter the name of your sports form (e.g., sports-forms).
* **videographer-forms (Text)**: Enter the name of your videographer form (e.g., videographer-forms).
* **description (Text)**: Provide a brief description of your work as a sports video producer.
* **customer-name (Text)**: Enter the name of your customer.
* **email (Text)**: Enter your email address.
* **phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number.
* **address (Text)**: Enter your work address.
* **media-credential (Text)**: Choose the media credential that best describes your experience (e.g., Option A, Option B, or Option C).
* **credential-type (Select One)**: Select the type of credential you have (e.g., Option A, Option B, or Option C).
* **agree (Select Multiple)**: Select the agreements you have read and agree to (e.g., Option A, Option B, or Option C).
* **date-of-birth (Date)**: Enter your date of birth.
* **time-of-birth (Time)**: Enter your time of birth.
* **sports-media-access (Select One)**: Choose the sports media access you have (e.g., Option A, Option B, or Option C).
* **sports-media-submission (Select Multiple)**: Select the sports media submissions you can make (e.g., Option A, Option B, or Option C).
* **agency-name (Text)**: Enter the name of your agency.
* **agency-url (Text)**: Enter the URL of your agency.
* **project-name (Text)**: Enter the name of the project you are working on.
* **project-url (Text)**: Enter the URL of the project.
* **agreement (Text)**: Read and agree to the agreement.
* **agreement-date (Date)**: Enter the agreement date (if applicable).
* **agreement-time (Time)**: Enter the agreement time (if applicable).
* **agreement-duration (Number)**: Enter the duration of the agreement in years.
* **agreement-remark (Text)**: Enter any additional remarks about the agreement.
* **agreement-file-name (Text)**: If applicable, enter the file name of the agreement.
* **agreement-file-url (Text)**: If applicable, enter the URL of the agreement file.
