# par-q-loughborough - Help Guide

## Purpose
The par-q-loughborough form is designed to collect user information for various purposes. This form may be used for registering or updating user data, submitting event information, or other relevant purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your first name in the field labelled "first_name".
2. Enter your last name in the field labelled "last_name".
3. Type in your email address in the field labelled "email".
4. Input your phone number in the field labelled "phone".
5. Provide a date in the format of MM/DD/YYYY for the field labelled "date".
6. Enter a time in the format HH:MM:SS for the field labelled "time".
7. If required, provide any additional notes or comments in the field labelled "note".
8. Select one option from the field labelled "select_one" with the label "category".
9. If required, select multiple options from the field labelled "select_multiple" with the label "multiple_choice_1".

* **note** (note): Provide any additional comments or notes.
