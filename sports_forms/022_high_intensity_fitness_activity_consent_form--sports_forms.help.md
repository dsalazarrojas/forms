# High Intensity Fitness Activity Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information and consent from participants before they engage in high intensity fitness activities. It is intended to ensure the participant's safety and well-being during the activity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please carefully review each field and provide accurate and truthful information. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your instructor for clarification. Complete all required fields, and provide as much information as possible for all other fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information**: This field is used to collect general information about the participant. Please enter your name, as it appears on your official identification.
* **Participant Name**: This field is used to collect your name as you would like it to be used for emergency contact purposes.
* **Participant Email**: This field is used to collect your email address. Please enter a valid email address where you can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Participant Phone**: This field is used to collect your phone number. Please enter a valid phone number where you can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Consent Given**: This field is used to indicate whether or not you have provided consent to participate in the high intensity fitness activity. Please select 'True' to indicate that you have given consent, and 'False' otherwise.
* **Health Status**: This field is used to collect information about your current health status. Please select your health status from the options provided:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Risk Factors**: This field is used to collect any risk factors that may impact your safety during the activity. Please enter any relevant information about your health or physical condition that may affect your ability to participate.
* **Emergency Contact**: This field is used to collect information about an emergency contact person. Please enter the name of the person who can be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: This field is used to collect the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: This field is used to collect the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship to Participant**: This field is used to collect the relationship between the participant and the emergency contact person.
* **Participant Signature**: This field is used to collect a signature from the participant, indicating that they have read and understood the consent form.
* **Instructor Signature**: This field is used to collect a signature from the instructor, indicating that they have explained the consent form and the participant has understood it.

## Tips
Remember to carefully review each field and provide accurate and truthful information. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to ask your instructor.
