# Golf Scorecard Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Golf Scorecard Template form is designed to record and track golf scores for an individual golfer's performance in a specific game.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Title**: Enter the title of the golf scorecard template.
2. **Golfer**: Enter the golfer's name.
3. **Golf Course**: Enter the name of the golf course where the game was played.
4. **Scorecard ID**: Enter a unique identifier for the scorecard (optional, but recommended for tracking purposes).
5. **Scorecard Date**: Enter the date of the game (in MM/DD/YYYY format).
6. **Golfer Scorecard**: Enter the golfer's performance in the game (text format, e.g., "72" for a score of 72).
7. **Total Score**: Enter the total score achieved by the golfer.
8. **Par**: Enter the par value for the game (number of strokes a professional golfer would take to complete the course).
9. **Golf Score**: Enter the golfer's performance for specific holes, using the corresponding par value (e.g., "4" for a par 4 hole).
10. **Hole Number**: Enter the hole number (starting from 1 for the first hole).
11. **Par 4, Par 5, Par 6, Par 7, Par 8, Par 9, Par 10, Par 11, Par 18, Par 19, Par 20, Par 21, Par 22, Par 23, Par 24**: Enter the par values for each corresponding hole type.
