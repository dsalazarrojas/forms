# Sports League Fan Wristband Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from sports league fans to facilitate the ordering of fan wristbands. It captures personal details, wristband preferences, and any additional comments or messages.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your fan's name and number in the designated fields.
2. Choose the preferred color for the wristband from the options provided.
3. Select any additional options or comments you'd like to provide.
4. Confirm your order submission and provide a message if needed.
5. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fan Name** (`fan_name`, Text): Enter your fan's name in this field.
* **Fan Number** (`fan_number`, Number): Enter your fan's number in this field.
* **Wristband Color** (`wristband_color`, Select One): Choose the preferred color for the wristband from the options: Red, Blue, Green, or Yellow.
* **T-Shirt Size** (`tshirt_size`, Select Multiple): Select any additional options or comments you'd like to provide.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, Date): This field is not currently required.
* **Email** (`email`, Email): Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you about your order.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text): Enter your phone number in this field.
* **Address** (`address`, Note): This field is not currently required.
* **Comments** (`comments`, Text): Provide any additional comments or messages you'd like to share.
* **Message to Fan** (`fan_wristband_message`, Text): Write a message for your fan.
* **Message Hint** (`fan_wristband_message_hint`, Text): This field is not currently required.
* **Form Submission** (`form_submission`, Select Multiple): Choose the type of form submission you'd like to provide.
* **Submit** (`submit`, Select One): Confirm your order submission by selecting 'Yes'.
* **Confirm Submit** (`confirm_submit`, Select One): Confirm that you have submitted your order by selecting 'True'.
* **Fan Wristband Order Message** (`fan_wristband_order_message`, Text): Provide any additional comments or messages you'd like to share.
* **Fan Wristband Order Message Hint** (`fan_wristband_order_message_hint`, Text): This field is not currently required.

* **Message Hint 3** (`fan_wristband_message_3_hint`, Text): This field is not currently required.
* **Message to Fan 4** (`fan_wristband_message_4`, Text): Write a message for your fan 4.

* **Message to Fan 5** (`fan_wristband_message_5`, Text): Write a message for your fan 5.
