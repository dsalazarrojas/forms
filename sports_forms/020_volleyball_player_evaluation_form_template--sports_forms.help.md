# Volleyball Player Evaluation Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volleyball Player Evaluation Form Template is designed to assess the skills, progress, and training needs of a volleyball player. This form helps coaches, managers, or evaluators to evaluate a player's current level of skills, identify areas for improvement, and create a tailored training plan to enhance their performance.

### How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the player's **first_name** and **last_name** in the respective text fields on page 1.
2. Enter the player's **age** in the numeric field on page 1.
3. Evaluate the player's **skills_assessment** by selecting one of the options provided on page 1.
4. Select the player's current **skill_level** by choosing one or more of the options on page 2.
5. Provide a brief description of the player's **progress** in the text area on page 2.
6. Select the areas where the player needs training or improvement by choosing one or more of the options on page 3.
7. Choose a training plan for the player from the options provided on page 3.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_name** (Text Field, **required: false**): Enter the player's first name.
* **last_name** (Text Field, **required: false**): Enter the player's last name.
* **age** (Number Field, **required: false**): Enter the player's age.
* **skills_assessment** (Select One, **required: false**):
	+ This field assesses the player's overall skills in volleyball.
	+ Choose the option that best describes the player's current level of skills.
* **skill_level** (Select Multiple, **required: false**):
	+ Select one or more options that represent the player's current skill level in volleyball.
	+ For example, if the player is a beginner, beginner, intermediate, or advanced player.
* **progress** (Note Field, **required: false**):
	+ Provide a brief description of the player's current progress in the sport.
	+ This can include any notable achievements, milestones, or areas of improvement.
* **training_needs** (Select Multiple, **required: false**):
	+ Choose one or more options that represent areas where the player needs improvement or training.
	+ For example, if the player needs to work on their serve, forehand, or backhand shots.
* **training_plan** (Select One, **required: false**):
	+ Select a training plan that suits the player's needs and goals.
	+ This plan can include specific exercises, drills, or practice regimens to help the player improve their skills.

### Tips

* Take note of any specific comments or remarks about the player's skills, progress, and training needs.
* Consider consulting with the player themselves or other coaches to get a more accurate assessment of their abilities.
* Use this form as a guide to create a comprehensive and personalized plan for the player's development and improvement.
