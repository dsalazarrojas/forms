# Coaching Weekly Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Coaching Weekly Feedback Form is a tool for coaches to provide feedback on an athlete's performance during a specific week. It's used to assess progress, identify areas of improvement, and set goals for the next week.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the session information section with the relevant details about the athlete's training sessions.
2. Provide feedback on the athlete's performance, focusing on skill development, consistency, and overall rating.
3. Evaluate the athlete's attitude, focus, and coachability.
4. Set goals and objectives for the next week based on the athlete's progress and needs.
5. If necessary, note any concerns or issues that need attention.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Name** (`athlete_name`, text, required): Enter the athlete's full name.
* **Coach Name** (`coach_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the coach providing feedback.
* **Week of - date** (`week_of`, date, required): Select the start date of the week being reviewed.
* **Number of sessions this week** (`number_sessions`, number, required): Enter the number of coaching sessions completed with the athlete this week.
* **Overall performance rating for the week** (`performance_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the athlete's overall performance from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Consistency of effort and practice** (`consistency`, select_one, required): Rate the athlete's consistency of effort and practice from 1 (very inconsistent) to 5 (highly consistent).
* **Progress toward stated goals** (`progress_toward_goals`, select_one, required): Evaluate the athlete's progress toward their stated goals from 1 (no progress) to 5 (exceeding goals).
* **Focus areas and goals for next week** (`goals_for_next_week`, text, required): Outline the athlete's focus areas and goals for the next week.
* **Athlete attitude during sessions** (`attitude_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the athlete's attitude during sessions from 1 (poor - negative) to 5 (excellent - highly positive).
* **Coachability - willingness to receive feedback** (`coachability`, select_one, required): Rate the athlete's willingness to receive feedback from 1 (resistant) to 5 (highly receptive).
* **Positive highlights from this week** (`positive_highlights`, text, optional): Record any positive highlights from the week.
* **Concerns or issues to address** (`concerns_issues`, text, optional): Note any concerns or issues that need attention.
* **Does parent communication need to happen?** (`parent_communication_needed`, select_one, optional): Determine if parent communication is necessary, with options for positive updates, concerns to discuss, or routine week.
* **Message or talking points for parent** (`parent_comment`, text, optional): If parent communication is needed, provide a message or talking points for the parent.

This form is designed to be a helpful tool for coaches to provide structured feedback and support athlete development. Please ensure you fill it out thoroughly and accurately to help athletes improve and achieve their goals.
