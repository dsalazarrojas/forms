# sports_team_promotion_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for the promotion of sports teams, capturing essential details about the team, athletes, parents, and school information. It helps in efficiently and accurately sharing the team's information with relevant parties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review each field carefully to avoid any errors or inconsistencies.
3. Ensure that all fields are filled out as per the required and optional options provided in the field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Info** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the athlete(s) involved in the sports team promotion.
* **Parent Info** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter information about the parent(s) related to the athlete(s).
* **Promotion Terms** (`3`, `text`, required/optional): Explain the terms and conditions of the sports team promotion.
* **Consent** (`4`, `select_one`, required/optional): Indicate whether you consent to the promotion of the sports team. Choose from 'True' or 'False'.
* **Athlete Name** (`5`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the athlete involved in the sports team promotion.
* **Athlete Image** (`6`, `text`, required/optional): Share an image of the athlete related to the promotion (if any).
* **Parent Name** (`7`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the parent(s) related to the athlete(s).
* **Parent Email** (`8`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the parent(s) related to the athlete(s).
* **School Name** (`9`, `text`, required/optional): Provide the name of the school related to the athlete(s) and promotion.
* **School Address** (`10`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the address of the school related to the athlete(s) and promotion.
* **School Phone** (`11`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the school related to the promotion.
* **School Email** (`12`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the school related to the promotion.
* **League Info** (`13`, `text`, required/optional): Share information about the sports league associated with the promotion.
* **League Name** (`14`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the sports league.
* **Team Name** (`15`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the sports team involved in the promotion.
* **Club Name** (`16`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the club related to the sports team and promotion.
* **Social Media** (`17`, `text`, required/optional): Share social media information about the sports team and promotion.
* **Social Media Url** (`18`, `text`, required/optional): Provide the URL of the social media platform used.
* **Social Media Handle** (`19`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the social media handle used for the promotion.
* **Image Release** (`20`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the image release information related to the promotion.
* **Image Usage** (`21`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose how the image will be used in the promotion. Select from 'Team Website', 'Social Media', or 'Both'.
* **Release Date** (`22`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date for the release of the promotion.
* **Photo Release** (`23`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the photo release information related to the promotion.
* **Photo Usage** (`24`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose how the photo will be used in the promotion. Select from 'Team Website', 'Social Media', or 'Both'.
* **School Info** (`25`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any other relevant school information for the promotion.

Please fill out this form carefully and thoroughly, providing accurate and up-to-date information to ensure efficient and successful promotion of your sports team.
