# Bring A Friend Special - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for members to bring a friend to the gym as part of a promotion or incentive program.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form in sections, starting with the member information and moving on to the friend's details.
- Ensure each field is filled out accurately and completely, as this information will help the gym staff tailor the friend's experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Member Information
- **Member Name** (`member_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as a member of the gym.
- **Member Email Address** (`member_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address as a member of the gym.
- **Member Phone Number** (`member_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number as a member of the gym.
- **Membership ID** (`membership_id`, `text`, required): Enter your membership ID number.

### Friend Information
- **Friend Name** (`friend_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the friend you are bringing.
- **Friend Email Address** (`friend_email`, `email`, required): Enter your friend's email address.
- **Friend Phone Number** (`friend_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your friend's phone number.
- **Friend Age** (`friend_age`, `number`, required): Enter your friend's age.
- **Friend Fitness Level** (`friend_fitness_level`, `select_one`, required): Select your friend's current fitness level: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Unsure.

### Visit Details
- **Preferred Visit Date** (`preferred_visit_date`, `date`, required): Select your preferred date for the friend's visit.
- **Preferred Time Slot** (`preferred_time`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred time slot for the visit: Early Morning (6-9 AM), Mid-Morning (9-12 PM), Afternoon (12-5 PM), or Evening (5-9 PM).

### Activities and Goals
- **Activities Interested In** (`activities_interested`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all activities your friend is interested in, such as Gym Equipment, Group Fitness Classes, Swimming Pool, Personal Training Session, Yoga, or Cycling Classes.
- **Fitness Goals** (`friend_goals`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the primary fitness goals of your friend: Weight Loss, Muscle Building, General Fitness, Flexibility, Cardiovascular Health, or Sports Training.
- **Previous Gym Experience** (`previous_gym_experience`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if your friend has previous gym experience: Yes - Regularly, Yes - Occasionally, or No - Never.
- **How Heard About** (`how_heard`, `select_one`, optional): Select how your friend learned about this promotion: Email, Social Media, In-Club Signage, Staff Referral, or Friend Referral.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or questions about the promotion.

Note: The optional fields are only necessary if there are specific requirements or special circumstances that need to be addressed.
