# Training Instructor Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals interested in becoming a training instructor for a sports-related program or organization. It collects essential information about your personal details, contact information, physical capabilities, health conditions, and emergency contact.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully, ensuring all required fields are completed.
2. Be accurate and honest in your responses.
3. Save the form and revisit it at any time.
4. If you need help or have questions, contact the relevant department or support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Details (personal_details)**: Enter your full name, address, and any other relevant personal details as required or requested.
* **Contact Details (contact_details)**: Provide your email, phone number, and any other contact information you'd like to share.
* **Physical Capabilities (physical_capabilities)**: Describe your physical abilities, including any relevant sports experience or qualifications.
* **Health Conditions (health_conditions)**: List any health conditions that may impact your ability to perform as a training instructor.
* **Emergency Contact (emergency_contact)**: Enter the name and contact information of a person to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Medical Conditions (medical_conditions)**: List any medical conditions that may impact your ability to perform as a training instructor.

## Tips
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submission.
* Be prepared to provide additional information or clarify any points as needed.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant department or support team. 

Note: The fields "health_conditions", "physical_capabilities", and "medical_conditions" may seem similar but are not redundant. "physical_capabilities" focuses on your physical abilities, while "health_conditions" and "medical_conditions" pertain to pre-existing health conditions that might affect your performance.
