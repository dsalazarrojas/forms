# Batting Lesson Scheduling Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from players to schedule batting lessons with a coach. It gathers details about the player, their background, and their preferences to provide a tailored experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details such as Player Name, Parent or Guardian's Name, and Email Address.
2. Provide your contact phone number.
3. Enter your age to indicate your current grade level.
4. Choose your skill level from Beginner to Competitive.
5. Indicate how many years you have played baseball or softball.
6. Select your primary position.
7. Choose the type of lesson you want to have (Private One-on-One, Small Group, Team Session, etc.).
8. Select your preferred date and time for the lesson.
9. If you have an alternate date or time, enter it here.
10. If you have a specific coach preference, enter their name.
11. Select the areas you want to focus on during the lesson (Swing Mechanics, Bat Speed, etc.).
12. Choose the equipment you will need for the lesson (Bring Own Bat and Helmet, Rent Equipment, etc.).
13. Enter any relevant previous injuries.
14. Describe your goals for the lesson.
15. Choose a lesson package that suits your needs (Single Lesson, 5 Lesson Package, etc.).
16. Add any additional comments or information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Player Name** (`player_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_guardian_name`, `text`, required): Enter your parent or guardian's full name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your contact email.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Player Age** (`player_age`, `number`, required): Enter your age to indicate your current grade level.
* **Grade Level** (`player_grade`, `select_one`, required): Select your current grade level (Elementary, Middle School, High School, College, Adult).
* **Skill Level** (`skill_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose your current skill level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Competitive).
* **Years Playing** (`years_playing`, `select_one`, required): Indicate how many years you have played baseball or softball (Less than 1 year, 1-2 years, 3-4 years, 5+ years).
* **Primary Position** (`primary_position`, `select_one`, optional): Select your primary position (Pitcher, Catcher, Infielder, Outfielder, Designated Hitter, Multiple Positions).
* **Lesson Type** (`lesson_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of lesson you want to have (Private One-on-One, Small Group, Team Session, etc.).
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, `date`, required): Select your preferred date for the lesson.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, `time`, required): Select your preferred time for the lesson.
* **Alternate Date** (`alternate_date`, `date`, optional): If you have an alternate date, enter it here.
* **Coach Preference** (`coach_preference`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of a specific coach if you have a preference.
* **Areas to Focus On** (`focus_areas`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the areas you want to focus on during the lesson (Swing Mechanics, Bat Speed, etc.).
* **Equipment Needed** (`equipment_needed`, `select_one`, required): Choose the equipment you will need for the lesson (Bring Own Bat and Helmet, Rent Equipment, etc.).
* **Previous Injuries** (`previous_injuries`, `text`, optional): Enter any previous injuries or medical conditions that may impact the lesson.
* **Goals for Lessons** (`goals`, `text`, required): Describe what you hope to achieve during the lesson.
* **Lesson Package** (`package_selection`, `select_one`, required): Choose a lesson package that suits your needs (Single Lesson, 5 Lesson Package, etc.).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional information or comments about your lesson preferences.
