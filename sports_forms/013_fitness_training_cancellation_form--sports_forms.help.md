# Fitness Training Cancellation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Training Cancellation Form is used to notify the trainer of the cancellation of a fitness training session. This form is designed to be completed by the trainee, providing necessary information to process the cancellation and communicate with the trainee and instructor.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name or the name of the trainee that needs to be cancelled in the "Training Cancellation" field.
2. In the "Training Session Details" section, provide a brief description of the training session you wish to cancel.
3. Choose a valid "Reason For Cancellation" from the dropdown menu.
4. If you have any additional feedback or suggestions, please provide them in the "Suggestion or Suggestion" text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Training Cancellation (text)**: Enter the name of the person cancelling the training session or the name of the person to be cancelled.
* **Training Session Details (note)**: Provide a short description of the cancelled training session, including details like date, time, or location.
* **Reason For Cancellation (select_one)**: Choose one of the provided options for why the training session is being cancelled. For example, "Choice 1" or "Choice 2".
* **Suggestion or Suggestion (text)**: If you have any additional comments or feedback, please provide them here. This helps us improve our services.

## Tips
- Fill out the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
- Make sure to provide a valid reason for cancellation.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, contact us at form@gic.mx.
