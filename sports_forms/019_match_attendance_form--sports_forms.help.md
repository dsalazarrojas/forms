# Match Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Match Attendance Form is designed to collect information from participants and teams about their attendance at a sports match. This form is intended to be used for internal purposes, such as recording attendance, tracking participation, and gathering feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the team name in the "Team Name" field.
2. Enter the participant's name in the "Participant Name" field.
3. Select the date of attendance in the "Attendance Date" field.
4. Choose the match time in the "Match Time" field.
5. Enter any additional match details in the "Match Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Name**: Enter the name of the team you are representing (e.g., "Red Devils" or "Green Team").
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as a participant in the match (e.g., "John Doe" or "Jane Smith").
* **Attendance Date**: Select the date you attended the match (e.g., "2022-02-20" or "February 20, 2022").
* **Match Time**: Choose the time of the match (e.g., "10:00 AM" or "10:00 AM EST").
* **Match Details**: Enter any additional information about the match, such as the location or the opponents (e.g., "The Red Devils vs. The Blue Team at Stadium X").

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate tracking of match attendance.
* If you have any issues while completing the form, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx) for assistance.
