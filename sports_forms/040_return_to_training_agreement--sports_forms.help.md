# return to training agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Return to Training Agreement" form is used to capture athlete information and consent to return to training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Fill in your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Provide your address in the "address" field.
4. Select your preferred contact method from the dropdown menu: Phone, Email, or Text.
5. Enter your phone number (if selected as the contact method) in the "phone1" field.
6. Enter your phone number (if selected as the contact method) in the "phone2" field.
7. Enter the date you plan to return to training.
8. Add any additional notes about your return to training in the "note" field.
9. Enter your email address (if selected as the contact method) in the "email" field.
10. Enter your email address (if selected as the contact method) in the "email2" field.
11. Enter your email address (if selected as the contact method) in the "email3" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name as it is written on your official documentation.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name as it is written on your official documentation.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your physical address where you can be contacted.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your preferred contact method from Phone, Email, or Text.
* **Phone Number 1** (`phone1`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number if you selected Phone as your contact method.
* **Phone Number 2** (`phone2`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number if you selected Phone as your contact method.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you plan to return to training.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional notes about your return to training.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address if you selected Email as your contact method.
* **Email 2** (`email2`, `text`, required: false): Enter your email address if you selected Email as your contact method.
* **Email 3** (`email3`, `text`, required: false): Enter your email address if you selected Email as your contact method.
