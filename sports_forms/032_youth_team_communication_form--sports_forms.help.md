# Youth Team Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential information from parents/guardians or caregivers for communication and emergency contact purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your first and last name.
2. Provide your email address.
3. Enter your phone number.
4. Select your preferred method of communication (text, email, or phone).
5. If you would like to receive team updates and messages, check the "Receive team updates and urgent messages" checkbox.
6. Enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact person.
7. Review and submit your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First name
**First name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your first name.

### Last name
**Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your last name.

### Email
**Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address for communication.

### Phone
**Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.

### Preferred communication method
**Preferred communication method** (`preferred_communication_method`, `select_one`, required: true):
	* Select one of the following options:
		+ Text
		+ Email
		+ Phone

### Receive team updates and urgent messages?
**Receive team updates and urgent messages** (`consent`, `note`, required: false):
	* Check this box if you want to receive updates about practice schedules, game reminders, and other important team news.
	* This field does not require a specific value.

### Emergency contact name
**Emergency contact name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.

### Emergency contact phone
**Emergency contact phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
