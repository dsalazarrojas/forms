# Trials Gala Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trials Gala Entry Form is designed for athletes and teams to register their participation in the Trialing Gala event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Enter your swimmer information in the Swimmer Information field.
	* Provide event information in the Event Information field.
	* Enter event information in the Event Information 2 field.
	* Confirm your agreements in the Agreements field.
	* Click the Submit button to complete the form.
2. Ensure that all fields are filled in before submitting. A blank or incomplete form cannot be submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Swimmer Information
**Swimmer Information** (`Swimmer Information`, `text`, required)
Enter your swimmer information, including your name, team, and contact details, in this field. This information will help us to identify you and your team during the event.

### Event Information
**Event Information** (`Event Information`, `text`, required)
Provide information about the event you are participating in, including event type, date, and other relevant details. This field is crucial for us to understand the context of your participation.

### Event Information 2
**Event Information 2** (`Event Information 2`, `text`, required)
Enter additional event information, if necessary. This field is optional but can be used to provide more specific details about the event.

### Agreements
**Agreements** (`Agreements`, `text`, required)
Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the Trialing Gala event. This field is essential to ensure that you understand and accept the terms of the event.

### Submit
**Submit** (`Submit`, `text`, required)
Click this button to submit your form once you have filled in all the required fields. A submitted form will be reviewed by our staff before the event.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all fields correctly and accurately.
* Review your form before submitting to avoid any errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
