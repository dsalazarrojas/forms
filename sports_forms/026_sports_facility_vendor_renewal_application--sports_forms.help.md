# Sports Facility Vendor Renewal Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Facility Vendor Renewal Application is used to manage the renewal of vendor relationships for sports facilities. This form helps you keep track of important vendor details, renewal periods, and required documents to ensure a smooth and efficient renewal process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the application process.
2. Fill in the vendor details, including the name and any other relevant information.
3. Select the renewal period for the vendor.
4. Attach any required documents for the vendor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Details**: Enter the vendor's name and any other relevant information such as address, contact person, etc.
* **Renewal Period**: Select the number of months or years the vendor's contract is set to renew. Enter the number of months or years to calculate the total renewal period.
* **Vendor Documents**: Attach any documents required for the renewal process, such as contracts, invoices, or certificates of insurance.
