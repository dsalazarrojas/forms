# Youth Athlete Confidence Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Athlete Confidence Survey is a form designed to collect information from young athletes about their confidence levels in various aspects of sports and competitions. This survey aims to collect data to improve coaching and support services for young athletes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each section carefully, and make sure you understand the questions and what we are asking you to do.
2. Start at the beginning and work your way through each field.
3. Answer each question accurately and truthfully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Name** (1): What is your name? Please enter your full name, as it is registered with your sport organization or school.
* **Age Group** (2): Please select the correct age group that best describes your age. The options are:
	+ U11
	+ U12
	+ U13
	+ U14
	+ U15
	+ U16
	+ U17
	+ U18
* **Events Competed** (3): Please select the events that you have competed in. You can select multiple events. The options are:
	+ Running
	+ Swimming
	+ Cycling
	+ Basketball
	+ Soccer
	+ Tennis
	+ Volleyball
	+ Baseball
	+ Softball
	+ Golf
	+ Other
* **Sports Competed** (4): Please select the sports that you have competed in. You can select multiple sports. The options are the same as above.
* **Gender** (5): Please enter your gender (male or female).
* **Other Events** (6): If you have competed in other events, please enter them here.
* **Other Sports** (7): If you have competed in other sports, please enter them here.
* **Overall Confidence** (8): Please rate your overall confidence level in competitions on a scale of 1-10.
* **Event Confidence** (9): Please rate your confidence level in your performance in events on a scale of 1-10.
* **Competition Confidence** (10): Please rate your confidence level in competition on a scale of 1-10.
* **Overall Training Confidence** (11): Please rate your confidence level in your overall performance on a scale of 1-10.
* **Performance Event** (12): Please rate your confidence level in your performance in events on a scale of 1-10.
* **Training Performance** (13): Please rate your confidence level in your training on a scale of 1-10.
* **Competition Training Confidence** (14): Please rate your confidence level in competitions on a scale of 1-10.
* **Performance Competition** (15): Please rate your confidence level in competitions on a scale of 1-10.
* **Overall Training Confidence** (16): Please rate your confidence level in your training on a scale of 1-10.
* **Performance Training** (17): Please rate your confidence level in your performance in training on a scale of 1-10.
* **Competition Training Confidence** (18): Please rate your confidence level in competitions on a scale of 1-10.

Note: All answers should be given on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is "not confident at all" and 10 is "very confident".
