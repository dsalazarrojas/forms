# personal_trainer_injury_waiver_and_media_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain information and consent from individuals who wish to participate in sports-related activities or trainings, including their waiver of liability and consent for media coverage.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the general information section with your basic details.
2. In the waiver section, please indicate if you have any injuries or medical conditions that may affect your participation in the activity.
3. In the media consent section, select whether you agree to be photographed, videoed, or interviewed by the media.
4. Provide your emergency contact information, including their name, relationship to you, and phone number.
5. Fill in your emergency contact's name, relationship to you, and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`general_info`, text, optional): Enter your basic details, such as your name, address, and contact information.
* **Waiver** (`waiver`, text, optional): Indicate if you have any injuries or medical conditions that may affect your participation in the activity.
* **Consent** (`consent`, text, optional): Select whether you agree to be photographed, videoed, or interviewed by the media.
* **Media Consent** (`media_consent`, select_multiple, optional): Choose from the options to indicate your consent for media coverage.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Provide the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
