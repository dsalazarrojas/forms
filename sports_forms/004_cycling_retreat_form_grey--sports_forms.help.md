# Cycling Retreat Form Grey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Cycling Retreat Form Grey is designed to collect data from participants in a cycling-related event or activity. It gathers information about the participants' waiver, contact details, and activity status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Waiver section with your signature, name, and any other required information.
2. Provide your full name in the Full Name field.
3. Enter your valid email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
5. Select your activity status for the cycling event (Yes or No).
6. If applicable, choose a date for the cycling event.
7. Select a start time for the cycling event (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Waiver** (id: 1, text, not required): Please sign the waiver to indicate your agreement to participate in the cycling event.
* **Full Name** (id: 2, text, not required): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email** (id: 3, email, not required): Provide a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (id: 4, text, not required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Activities** (id: 5, select_multiple, not required): Select Yes if you plan to participate in the cycling event, or No if you do not.
* **Date** (id: 6, date, not required): Choose a date for the cycling event (if applicable).
* **Start Time** (id: 7, time, not required): Select a start time for the cycling event (if applicable).

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields (if any).
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask an administrator for clarification.
- This form is intended for internal use only. Do not share your information with anyone outside the cycling community.
