**Rhythmic Gymnastics Ribbon Quiz - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The "Rhythmic Gymnastics Ribbon Quiz" is a tool used by coaches and officials to assess athletes' knowledge and skills in the sport of rhythmic gymnastics. This form helps to collect and review data about athletes' performances, providing valuable insights for coaches to make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate page by choosing from the options provided.
2. Fill in the required information for each field. The fields are marked as "required" and can be completed by selecting an option or typing in a response.
3. Take note of any additional information that can be included in the "Data Collection and Review" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rhythmic Gymnastics Ribbon Quiz** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): This is the main section of the form where you can enter any comments or notes about the athlete's performance.
* **Athletes' Ribbon Knowledge** (`2`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select one of the four options to indicate the athlete's level of knowledge in rhythmic gymnastics:
	+ option 1: Novice
	+ option 2: Intermediate
	+ option 3: Advanced
	+ option 4: Expert
* **Coaches' Assessment** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all applicable options to indicate the areas where the athlete excels or needs improvement:
	+ option 1: Ribbon manipulation
	+ option 2: Movement coordination
	+ option 3: Artistic expression
* **Data Collection and Review** (`4`, `note`, required/optional): This field is for any additional comments or notes about the athlete's performance.
