# Rising Sports Star Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect nominations for rising sports stars. Your input will help identify and recognize exceptional athletes in their respective sports and schools.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your nomination's first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your nomination's last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Select the sport that your nomination is associated with from the "Sport" dropdown menu.
4. Enter your nomination's school in the "School" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the first name of the sports star being nominated.
- **Last Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the last name of the sports star being nominated.
- **Sport** (Select one, Optional): Choose the sport that the nomination is associated with.
  - Baseball
  - Basketball
  - Football
  - Golf
  - Soccer
- **School** (Text, Optional): Enter the school that the sports star attends or is affiliated with.
