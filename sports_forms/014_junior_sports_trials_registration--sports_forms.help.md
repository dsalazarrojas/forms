# junior_sports_trials_registration - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used for registering junior athletes for sports trials. It is intended for parents or guardians to provide information about their child's participation in the trials.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of trial your child is participating in from the options provided.
2. Enter your child's name.
3. Enter your child's age.
4. Enter your child's date of birth.
5. Enter your child's email address.
6. Enter your child's phone number.
7. Enter your child's home address.
8. Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
9. Enter the phone number of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
10. Enter the name of your child's parent or guardian.
11. Enter the phone number of your parent or guardian.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trial Type** (Required: false): Select the type of trial your child is participating in.
* **Name** (Required: false): Enter your child's name.
* **Age** (Required: false): Enter your child's age.
* **Date of Birth** (Required: false): Enter your child’s date of birth.
* **Email** (Required: false): Enter your child’s email address.
* **Phone** (Required: false): Enter your child’s phone number.
* **Address** (Required: false): Enter your child’s home address.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Required: false): Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Required: false): Enter the phone number of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Parent Name** (Required: false): Enter your child’s parent or guardian’s name.
* **Parent Phone** (Required: false): Enter your child’s parent or guardian’s phone number.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our support team.
* Make sure to review all fields carefully before submitting the form.
