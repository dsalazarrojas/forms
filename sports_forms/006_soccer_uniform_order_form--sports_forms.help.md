# Soccer Uniform Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Soccer Uniform Order Form is designed to gather information for fulfilling soccer uniform orders. This form helps us ensure all necessary details are captured for accurate and efficient order processing.

## How To Complete This Form
### Step 1: Enter Your Order Details
- First, provide your name and any additional details about the order in the **player name**, **shirt color**, **pant color**, and **sleeve length** fields. This helps us understand your specific order requirements.
- Then, choose your preferred **jersey size** and **order date** and **order time** for the uniform order.
- Next, add any specific **order notes** about your order in the provided field.
- Finally, select your preferred **payment method** (PayPal, Square, or Stripe) for processing the order.

### Step 2: Confirm Your Contact Information
- Enter your **order email** and **order phone** so we can get in touch with you about your order.
- In **team** and **team name** fields, provide the name of the team related to your order if applicable.

### Step 3: Review and Submit
- Review all the fields to ensure you've provided accurate information.
- Click the submit button to send your order details to us for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Name**: Please enter your name as it should appear on the uniform.
* **Jersey Size**: Choose your preferred jersey size for the uniform.
* **Shirt Color**: Enter your preferred shirt color.
* **Pant Color**: Enter your preferred pant color.
* **Sleeve Length**: Specify the desired sleeve length for the uniform.
* **Order Date**: Enter the date you'd like to have your order delivered or processed by.
* **Order Time**: If you have a specific time in mind for your order delivery, enter it here.
* **Order Note**: Add any additional details or comments about your order here.
* **Order Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your order.
* **Order Phone**: Provide your phone number for any further contact.
* **Team**: Enter the name of your team if you're placing an order on behalf of a team.
* **Team Name**: Enter the name of the team associated with your order.
* **Order Status**: This field will display the status of your order once it's been processed.
* **Payment Method**: Select the method through which you'd like to make the payment for your order (PayPal, Square, or Stripe).
* **Order Total**: The total cost for your order will be displayed here after you've submitted the form.
* **Player Position**: If your role within the team, enter it here for specific order processing.
* **Order Status**: Displays the current status of your order.

### Tips
- If you have any specific requirements or requests for your uniform, please note them in the **order note** field.
- Ensure your **order email** and **order phone** are accurate to enable smooth communication about your order.
- Review all fields before submitting to ensure you've provided all necessary details.

Please fill out the form accurately to ensure we fulfill your order efficiently and effectively.
