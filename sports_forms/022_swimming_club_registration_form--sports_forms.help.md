# swimming_club_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from individuals interested in joining the swimming club.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your first and last name.
2. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
3. Provide your date of birth for age verification.
4. Select your swimmer level (High or Low) and gender (Yes or No).
5. Enter your age as a numeric value.
6. Answer the question about your swimmer level and gender.
7. Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact person.
8. Answer the question about your relation with the emergency contact person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Enter your surname.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required): Provide your date of birth.
* **Swimmer Level** (Swimmer Level, select_multiple, not required): Select your swimmer level (High or Low).
* **Swimmer Gender** (Swimmer Gender, select_one, not required): Answer if you are a male or female swimmer (Yes or No).
* **Ages** (Ages, number, not required): Enter your age as a numeric value.
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (Emergency Contact First Name, text, not required): Enter your emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (Emergency Contact Last Name, text, not required): Enter your emergency contact's surname.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Emergency Contact Phone, text, not required): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Relation** (Emergency Contact Relation, select_one, not required): Answer if you are related to the emergency contact person (Yes or No).

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure your registration is complete.
* If you have any issues or concerns, don't hesitate to contact the club administration.
* The information collected will be used for club administration and safety purposes only.
