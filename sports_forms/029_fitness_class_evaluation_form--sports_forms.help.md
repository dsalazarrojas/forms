# Fitness Class Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate a fitness class, providing an opportunity for instructors and participants to give feedback on the class's quality, value, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with your honest opinions and experiences about the fitness class. You may choose not to complete the form if you're a participant who has already evaluated the class previously. For each field, you can provide a brief response, keeping in mind that more detailed explanations are welcome but not necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Class Name** (`class_name`, Text, Optional): Provide the name of the fitness class you're evaluating.
* **Instructor** (`instructor`, Select One, Optional): Choose 'Yes' if the instructor was present, 'No' if they were not.
* **Instructor Rating** (`instructor_rating`, Number, Optional): Rate the instructor's performance on a scale from 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Class Rating** (`class_rating`, Number, Optional): Rate the class's overall quality on a scale from 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Class Value** (`class_value`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the level of value you experienced in the class. Options include Excellent, Good, Average, and Poor.
* **Class Improvement** (`class_improvement`, Text, Optional): Suggest any improvements the class could make to better serve its participants.
* **Instructor Improvement** (`instructor_improvement`, Text, Optional): Suggest any improvements the instructor could make to better serve the class.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, Text, Optional): Offer any suggestions or comments you have about the class or its instructor.
* **Comments** (`comments`, Text, Optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the class or instructor.
