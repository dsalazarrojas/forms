# Athlete Council Election Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from students running for the Athlete Council election. The form captures the candidate's details, their motivations, and their vision for the Athlete Council. Completing this form is the first step in the election process for students interested in holding a position on the Athlete Council.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the election year field with the year of the election.
2. Select the position you are running for from the list of options.
3. Provide your full name, student ID number, email address, and academic major.
4. List the sports you participate in.
5. Choose your year of study from the list of options.
6. Write a motivation statement explaining why you are running for the position.
7. Describe your previous leadership experience, both within and outside of sports.
8. Share your vision for the Athlete Council in this position.
9. Identify a key issue you plan to address if elected.
10. Provide a reference name and contact information from a faculty member or coach.
11. Agree to abide by all election rules and guidelines.
12. Sign your full name electronically.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Election Year**: Enter the year of the election. Please ensure you enter the correct year to ensure your application is valid.
* **Position Applied For**: Select the position you are running for. Please choose one position only.
* **Candidate Full Name**: Enter your full name. This will be displayed on the ballot and in all election-related materials.
* **Student ID Number**: Enter your university-issued student ID number. This is used to verify your identity.
* **Email Address**: Enter your university email address. This will be used for communication and election-related purposes.
* **Academic Major**: Enter your academic major. This will help the Athlete Council understand your area of study and interest.
* **Sport - Participate In**: List all the sports you participate in. This will help the Athlete Council understand your experience and qualifications.
* **Year of Study**: Choose your year of study from the list of options. This will help the Athlete Council understand your level of experience and involvement.
* **Why are you running for this position?**: Write a brief statement explaining why you want to hold this position on the Athlete Council. This will help the voting body understand your motivations.
* **Previous Leadership Experience**: Describe your previous leadership experience, both within and outside of sports. This will help the Athlete Council understand your leadership skills and qualifications.
* **Vision for the Athlete Council**: Share your vision for the Athlete Council if elected. This will help the voting body understand your goals and objectives.
* **Key Issue You Plan to Address**: Identify a key issue you plan to address if elected. This will help the voting body understand your priorities and focus.
* **Faculty or Coach Reference Name**: Enter the name of a faculty member or coach who can vouch for your qualifications and character.
* **Reference Contact Information**: Enter the contact information of your reference, such as their email or phone number.
* **I consent to abide by all election rules and guidelines**: Select this option to indicate that you agree to follow all election rules and guidelines.
* **Candidate Electronic Signature**: Type your full name to sign this application electronically.
* **Date of Application**: The date you complete this form will be automatically populated.
