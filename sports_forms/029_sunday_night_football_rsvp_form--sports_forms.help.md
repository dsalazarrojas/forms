# sunday_night_football_rsvp_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sunday Night Football RSVP form is used by players to inform the team management of their availability and position for the game.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information for each field. You can submit the form once you have filled out all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sunday Night Football RSVP** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name to identify yourself.
* **player_name** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name to let others know who you are.
* **availability** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose your availability for the game. Please select all that apply.
	+ 'Yes': You will be available for the game.
	+ 'No': You will not be available for the game.
* **will_play** (`4`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose your willingness to play in the game. 
	+ 'Yes': You will play in the game.
	+ 'No': You will not play in the game.
* **game_time** (`5`, `time`, required/optional): Enter your expected start time for the game. Please use standard 12-hour format (e.g., 8:00 AM).
* **position** (`6`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your position on the team (e.g., quarterback, wide receiver).
* **phone** (`7`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you have any special requests, feel free to include them in the phone field.
