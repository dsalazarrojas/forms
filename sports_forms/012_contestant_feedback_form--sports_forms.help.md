# Contestant Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from contest participants on their experience with the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Contestant Name" field.
2. Enter your contact email in the "Email Address" field.
3. Select the contest you participated in from the "Contest Name" field.
4. Select the date of the contest event from the "Contest Date" field.
5. Select your category of participation from the "Competition Category" field.
6. Rate your overall experience with the contest from the "Overall Experience" field.
7. Rate the registration process from the "Registration Process" field.
8. Rate the quality of communication from the "Communication Quality" field.
9. Rate the facilities of the venue from the "Venue Facilities" field. (Note: This field is optional.)
10. Rate the friendliness and helpfulness of the staff from the "Staff Friendliness" field.
11. Rate the fairness of the judging process from the "Judging Fairness" field.
12. Rate the organization of the event from the "Event Organization" field.
13. Rate the value for money from the "Value for Money" field.
14. Select what you enjoyed most about the event from the "Best Aspect" field (optional).
15. Select what could be improved about the event from the "Areas for Improvement" field (optional).
16. Select if you would recommend the contest to others from the "Would Recommend" field.
17. Select if you would participate again from the "Participate Again" field.
17. Enter any additional comments you may have in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contestant Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address associated with your account.
* **Contest Name**: Select the name of the contest you participated in.
* **Contest Date**: Select the date of the contest event.
* **Competition Category**: Select the category of competition you participated in (e.g., Individual Sports, Team Sports, etc.).
* **Overall Experience**: Rate your overall experience with the contest from Excellent to Poor.
* **Registration Process**: Rate the registration process from Excellent to Poor.
* **Communication Quality**: Rate the quality of communication from Excellent to Poor.
* **Venue Facilities**: Rate the facilities of the venue from Excellent to Poor. (Note: This field is optional.)
* **Staff Friendliness**: Rate the friendliness and helpfulness of the staff from Excellent to Poor.
* **Judging Fairness**: Rate the fairness of the judging process from Excellent to Poor.
* **Event Organization**: Rate the organization of the event from Excellent to Poor.
* **Value for Money**: Rate the value for money from Excellent to Poor.
* **Best Aspect**: Select what you enjoyed most about the event (optional).
* **Areas for Improvement**: Select what could be improved about the event (optional).
* **Would Recommend**: Select if you would recommend the contest to others.
* **Participate Again**: Select if you would participate again.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
