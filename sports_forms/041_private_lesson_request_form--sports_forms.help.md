# Private Lesson Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to request a private lesson with one of our instructors. Please use this form to provide information about the student, lesson date, time, and details so we can efficiently schedule and prepare for the lesson.

## How To Complete This Form
- Click on the "Private Lesson Request Form" to access the form.
- Fill out the form with the required fields (if any).
- Review and verify the information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lesson Date (Date)** (`lesson_date`, date, required: false): Select the date for the private lesson. You can choose any available date from our scheduling calendar. Please ensure that you have chosen a date that our instructor is available.
* **Lesson Time (Lesson Time)** (`lesson_time`, time, required: false): Choose the time slot for the private lesson. Please ensure that you select a time that is convenient for the student and our instructor.
* **Lesson Duration (Lesson Duration)** (`lesson_duration`, number, required: false): Specify the duration of the private lesson. This will help us ensure that we have sufficient time for the lesson. Please enter a whole number of minutes.
* **Instructor (Instructor)** (`instructor_id`, select_one, required: false): Select the instructor you would like to schedule the private lesson with. Please review our instructors' availability and choose someone with expertise in the selected lesson type.
* **Student Name (Student Name)** (`student_name`, text, required: false): Enter the student's full name as they will be appearing on our records.
* **Lesson Topic (Lesson Topic)** (`lesson_topic`, text, required: false): Briefly describe the lesson topic or area of focus for the private lesson.
* **Lesson Type (Lesson Type)** (`lesson_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of lesson that suits the student's needs. Please review our available options to select the most suitable type.
* **Student Email (Student Email)** (`student_email`, email, required: false): Enter the student's email address so we can contact them with lesson details.
* **Student Phone (Student Phone)** (`student_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the student's phone number so we can reach them in case of any last-minute changes or cancellations.
