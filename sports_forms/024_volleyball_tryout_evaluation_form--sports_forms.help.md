# Volleyball Tryout Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by coaches or evaluators to assess a player's performance during a volleyball tryout. It helps to evaluate the player's skills, speed, agility, endurance, and other qualities that are relevant to their position on the team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields according to the player's performance during the tryout. 

1. Answer "yes" or "no" to the "player_selection" field, indicating whether or not the player was selected for the team.
2. For the "skills", "speed", "agility", "endurance", "team_compatibility", "plays_ball_control" and "ball_distribution" fields, rate the player's performance using the numbers provided.
3. For the "team_fit" field, rate how well the player fits in with the team's culture and style of play, using the numbers provided.
4. In the "overall" field, provide an overall assessment of the player's performance.
5. Use the "comments" fields to provide any additional comments or feedback from the evaluator or the player.
6. Fill out the "player_name", "player_email", "player_phone", "coach_name" fields with the player's and coach's contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **player_selection (1)**: Answer "yes" or "no" to indicate whether or not the player was selected for the team.
* **skills (2)**: Note any skills the player demonstrated during the tryout, such as serving, hitting, or blocking.
* **speed (3)**: Rate the player's speed, using the numbers provided.
* **agility (4)**: Rate the player's agility, using the numbers provided.
* **endurance (5)**: Rate the player's endurance, using the numbers provided.
* **team_compatibility (6)**: Answer "yes" or "no" to indicate whether or not the player's personality and style fit in with the team's culture.
* **plays_ball_control (7)**: Rate the player's ability to control the ball, using the numbers provided.
* **ball_distribution (8)**: Rate the player's ability to distribute the ball, using the numbers provided.
* **team_fit (9)**: Rate how well the player fits in with the team's culture and style of play, using the numbers provided.
* **overall (10)**: Provide an overall assessment of the player's performance.
* **comments (11)**: Provide any additional comments or feedback from the evaluator or the player.
* **coach_comments (12)**: Provide any comments or feedback from the coach.
* **evaluator_comments (13)**: Provide any comments or feedback from the evaluator.
* **player_comments (14)**: Provide any comments or feedback from the player.
* **other_comments (15)**: (Not applicable)
* **player_name (16)**: Enter the player's name.
* **player_email (17)**: Enter the player's email address.
* **player_phone (18)**: Enter the player's phone number.
* **coach_name (19)**: Enter the coach's name.
