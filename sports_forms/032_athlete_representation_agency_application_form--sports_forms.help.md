# Athlete Representation Agency Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from athletes who wish to be represented by a sports agency.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to the best of your ability.
2. Make sure to select all the required fields (marked as "required").
3. For fields with "select all that apply" options, select all relevant options that apply to your situation.
4. If you have any questions or concerns while filling out the form, please don't hesitate to reach out to the agency's support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Full Name**: Your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Nationality**: Your country of citizenship.
* **Current Residential City and Country**: Your current city and country of residence.
* **Primary Sport**: The primary sport you compete in.
* **Position or Specialization**: Your position or specialization in your primary sport.
* **Current Team or Organization**: The team or organization you are currently a part of.
* **Years Competing at Professional Level**: The number of years you have been competing at a professional level.
* **Major Career Achievements and Milestones**: A brief summary of your major career achievements and milestones.
* **Current World or National Ranking**: Your current world or national ranking (if applicable).
* **Current Agent or Manager Name**: The name of your current agent or manager (type "N-A" if not currently represented).
* **Expiration Date of Current Representation Contract**: The expiration date of your current representation contract (if applicable).
* **Primary Goals for New Representation**: Select all relevant options for your primary goals for new representation (e.g. contract negotiation and renewals, sponsorship and endorsement deals, etc.).
* **Instagram Handle**: Your Instagram handle (e.g. @athlete_name).
* **Twitter or X Handle**: Your Twitter or X handle (if applicable).
* **Link to Highlight Reel or Portfolio**: A link to your highlight reel or portfolio (e.g. YouTube or Vimeo link).
* **Email Address**: Your email address (e.g. example@domain.com).
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number (including country code).
* **I Certify**: I certify that I am not under any conflicting representation contracts.
* **Electronic Signature**: Your electronic signature (type your full name).
* **Date of Application**: The date you are submitting this application.

Note: This form is not intended for current agency representatives to fill out. Please do not fill out this form if you are currently represented by another agency.
