# Hockey Youth Waitlist Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hockey Youth Waitlist Application Form is used to register your child for hockey programs and provide essential contact information for the child's care and safety.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your child's first name, last name, and address in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide your child's phone number and email address (if applicable).
3. Enter your contact information (name, email, and phone number) as the parent or guardian.
4. Add child and parent hockey experience, school name (if applicable), and birthdate.
5. Specify the emergency contact's name, relationship, phone number, and email address.
6. Ensure all required fields are completed, as indicated by the asterisk (\*) symbol.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your child's given name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your child's surname.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your child's home address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your child's contact phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your child's email address (if applicable).
* **Parent Contact Name** (`parent_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent Phone Number** (`parent_phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian.
* **Child Birthdate** (`child_birthdate`, date, optional): Enter the child's birthdate.
* **Child School Name** (`child_school_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the child's school (if applicable).
* **Child Hockey Experience** (`child_hockey_experience`, text, optional): Describe your child's hockey experience (if applicable).
* **Parent Work Status** (`parent_work_status`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the parent's work status (if applicable).
* **Parent Job Title** (`parent_job_title`, text, optional): Enter the parent's job title (if applicable).
* **Parent Employment Type** (`parent_employment_type`, text, optional): Enter the parent's employment type (if applicable).
* **Parent Hockey Experience** (`parent_hockey_exp`, text, optional): Describe the parent's hockey experience (if applicable).
* **Siblings** (`child_siblings`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the names of other siblings participating in hockey programs.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_relationship`, text, optional): Describe the relationship of the emergency contact to the child (e.g., parent, guardian, or other relative).
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`emergency_phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the emergency contact.

## Tips

* Please ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Use a valid email address format (e.g., `example@example.com`) for email fields.
* Double-check the entered information for accuracy and completeness.
* Submitting this form will initiate the waitlist application process for your child.
