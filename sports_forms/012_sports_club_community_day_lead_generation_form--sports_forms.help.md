# sports_club_community_day_lead_generation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sports club community day lead generation form is used to collect information about potential attendees' interests and availability to participate in the club's Community Day event. This form helps the club's management to determine who to follow up with via phone or email to invite them to attend the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select your interest in the Community Day event by choosing one or more of the options in the "What is your interest in Community Day" section.
2. Provide your name, email address, and phone number if you would like to be contacted by the club.
3. Choose a date and time that you are interested in attending the event.
4. If you have any notes or special requests, please include them in the "Notes" section.
5. Decide whether you would like the club to follow up with you via phone or email.
6. Choose your preferences for follow-up contact in the "Follow-up question" and "Follow-up contact" sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your interest in Community Day** (`community_day_interest`, `select_multiple`): Choose one or more of the options to express your interest in the Community Day event.
	+ Select 'Yes' if you are interested in attending the event.
	+ Select 'No' if you are not interested in attending the event.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in our records.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, `email`): Enter your email address where you can be reached for follow-up communications.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for follow-up calls.
* **Date of Interest** (`date_of_interest`, `date`): Choose the date you would be available to attend the event.
* **Time of Interest** (`time_of_interest`, `time`): Choose the time you would be available to attend the event.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`): Enter any additional notes or requests you have regarding your attendance.
* **Follow-up question** (`follow_up_question`, `select_one`): Choose whether you would like the club to follow up with you after submitting this form.
* **Follow-up contact** (`follow_up_contact_email`, `select_one`): Choose your preference for follow-up contact (phone or email).
* **Follow-up contact phone** (`follow_up_contact_phone`, `select_one`): Choose whether you would like to receive follow-up calls via phone.
