# online_fifa_tournament_setup_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The online FIFA tournament setup checklist is a form designed to help organizers collect necessary information for setting up an online FIFA tournament. It consists of 7 fields, each with its own purpose and requirements.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
* Complete each field based on your tournament's specific needs and requirements.
* Double-check your answers to ensure accuracy and consistency throughout the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Info** (`title`, `type: text`, required): This field is for providing a title for the tournament. It should be a concise and descriptive title that reflects the tournament's name and purpose.
* **Teams** (`teams`, `type: text`, required): Enter the names of the teams participating in the tournament. You can list each team on a new line, and be as detailed as necessary.
* **Games** (`games`, `type: text`, required): Enter details about the games, such as schedules, dates, or any specific rules or requirements for the games.
* **Players** (`players`, `type: text`, required): List the names of the players participating in the tournament.
* **Tournament Rules** (`tournament_rules`, `type: text`, required): Enter any specific rules or regulations for the tournament that might affect gameplay, scheduling, or other essential aspects.
* **Tournament Details** (`tournament_details`, `type: text`, required): Provide any additional details about the tournament that might not fit into the other categories, such as location, dates, or specific requirements.
* **Referees** (`referees`, `type: select_one`, required): Choose the referees who will be responsible for the tournament, selecting from the available options: Team A, Team B, Team C, or Team D.
* **Game Settings** (`game_settings`, `type: select_multiple`, required): Select the game settings that apply to your tournament, choosing from the available options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.

## Tips
* Double-check all fields for accuracy and consistency.
* Make sure to keep the form concise and complete, ensuring you've covered all necessary information.
* If any field doesn't fit your specific needs, consider modifying the form to suit your requirements.
