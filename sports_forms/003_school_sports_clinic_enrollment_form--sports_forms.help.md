# School Sports Clinic Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect enrollment information for your child to participate in a school sports clinic. Please provide accurate and complete details to ensure a smooth enrollment process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the student's information (1-8).
2. Choose your payment method (9).
3. Select your child's team and sport (10-11).
4. Provide your contact and emergency contact information (12-15).
5. Enter payment details (16-18).
6. Confirm your child's details (19-22).
7. Select your child's school (23-25).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Information (1-8)**: This section collects the student's name, email, date of birth, address, and other essential details.
	+ **Student Name**: Enter your child's name as it appears on their ID or passport.
	+ **Student Email**: Enter your child's email address for communication purposes.
	+ **Date of Birth**: Enter your child's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
	+ **Student Address**: Enter your child's home address.
* **School Info (23-25)**: This section selects the student's school.
	+ **Student School**: Select the student's school from the provided options.
* **Contact Information (12-15)**: This section collects your contact and emergency contact information.
	+ **Contact Name**: Enter your name.
	+ **Contact Email**: Enter your email address.
	+ **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
	+ **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Payment Method (9)**: Choose how you want to make the payment.
	+ **Payment Method**: Select "Pay Online" or "Pay Offline".
* **Team and Sport (10-11)**: This section selects the team and sport your child will be participating in.
	+ **Team Name**: Select the team your child will be part of.
	+ **Sport Name**: Select the sport your child will be participating in.
* **Payment Details (19-20)**: This section collects payment and status information.
	+ **Payment Amount**: Enter the payment amount.
	+ **Payment Status**: Select the payment status.
* **Parent/Guardian Information (16-18)**: This section collects parent/guardian contact and relationship information.
	+ **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Enter the relationship between the emergency contact and your child.
