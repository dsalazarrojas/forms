# Team Travel Organization Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will help teams understand the purpose and use of the Team Travel Organization Survey form. The form is designed to collect important information about team travel dates and times to ensure organization and coordination of team activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select a suitable date for your team's travel in the **travel_date** field.
2. Enter the time of your team's travel in the **travel_time** field.
3. Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to enable smooth organization and coordination of team activities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Team Name (team_name)**: Enter your team's name to ensure that your information is associated with the correct team.
  - This field is optional but recommended to provide a clear identity for your team's travel data.
- **Travel Date (travel_date)**: Select a date for your team's travel to schedule activities and meetings.
  - This field is optional but recommended for accurate planning and organization.
- **Travel Time (travel_time)**: Enter the time of your team's travel for precise scheduling.
  - This field is optional but recommended to ensure smooth coordination and organization.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to avoid any issues with scheduling and planning.
