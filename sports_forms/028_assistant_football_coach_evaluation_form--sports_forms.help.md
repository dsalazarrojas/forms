# Assistant Football Coach Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the performance of an assistant football coach. It assesses various aspects of the coach's performance, including game strategy, player development, team management, and overall leadership.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your name and position.
2. Enter the date of the evaluation.
3. Enter the name of the head coach being evaluated.
4. Select the season and year for which the evaluation is being conducted.
5. Rate the coach's performance in each of the following areas:
	* Game Strategy and Planning
	* Player Development and Coaching
	* Team Management and Discipline
	* Communication Skills
	* In-Game Decision Making and Management
	* Team Motivation and Leadership
	* Offensive Strategy and Coaching
	* Defensive Strategy and Coaching
	* Special Teams Coaching
	* Player Recruitment and Retention
6. Identify the coach's key strengths and areas for improvement.
7. Provide recommendations for the coach's development.
8. Provide an overall rating of the coach's performance.
9. Add any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Evaluator Name** (`evaluator_name`, `text`, required): Your name and position.
	+ This field is required and should be filled out with your name and position.
* **Your Position** (`evaluator_position`, `text`, required): Your coaching position.
	+ This field should be filled out with your current coaching position.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, `date`, required): Date of the evaluation.
	+ This field should be filled out with the date of the evaluation.
