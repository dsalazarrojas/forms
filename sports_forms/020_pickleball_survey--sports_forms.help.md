# Pickleball Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about pickleball players' preferences and habits. The data collected will help us better understand the game and identify areas of improvement for players.

## How To Complete This Form
- To begin, fill out the form with your player information, including your name, age, and gender.
- Scroll down to the "How often do you play?" section and select your frequency of play.
- If you play with others, select the frequency with which you play with them.
- Select your favorite surface, tool, and net type.
- If playing with an opponent, select their gender, age, frequency of play, favorite surface, and favorite net type.
- Finally, select the level of play you have with your opponent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official ID or passport. (Text, Required)
* **Player Age**: Enter your age in years. (Number, Required)
* **Gender**: Select your gender. (Select Multiple, Required)
* **How often do you play?**: How often do you play pickleball? (Select One, Required)
* **Time of Day**: Enter the time you usually play. (Time, Optional)
* **Favorite Surface**: Enter your favorite surface to play on. (Text, Optional)
* **Favorite Tool**: Enter your favorite tool or equipment. (Text, Optional)
* **Favorite Net**: Enter your favorite net type. (Text, Optional)
* **Net Type**: Select your favorite net type. (Select One, Optional)
* **Net Maintenance**: How often do you maintain your net. (Select One, Optional)
* **Player Rating**: Enter your player rating. (Number, Optional)
* **Rating Level**: Select your rating level. (Select One, Optional)
* **Play With**: How often do you play with others. (Select One, Optional)
* **Opponent Type**: Select the type of opponent you usually play with. (Select One, Optional)
* **Opponent Gender**: Select the gender of your opponent. (Select Multiple, Optional)
* **Opponent Age**: Enter the age of your opponent. (Number, Optional)
* **Opponent Frequency**: Select the frequency with which you play with your opponent. (Select Multiple, Optional)
* **Opponent Skill Level**: Select the skill level of your opponent. (Select One, Optional)
* **Opponent Surface**: Select the surface you play with your opponent on. (Select One, Optional)
* **Opponent Net**: Select the net type you play with your opponent on. (Select One, Optional)
* **Opponent Play Frequency**: Select the frequency with which you play with your opponent. (Select One, Optional)

Note: All fields are optional, but required fields are marked as such. Please fill out as much information as you can to help us better understand the data.
