# Online Fitness Coach Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for individuals seeking personalized fitness coaching services. It collects necessary information to match you with a suitable coach and ensure a smooth coaching experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your details in the "Client Details" section.
2. Provide your contact and emergency contact information in the "Contact Details" and "Emergency Contact" sections, respectively.
3. Share your medical history to ensure safe and effective coaching.
4. Specify your fitness goals, including what you aim to achieve through coaching.
5. Attach any relevant client contracts you've signed or agreed upon.
6. Describe your ideal training setup, including equipment and facilities you need.
7. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details** (`client_details`, text, required): Provide your full name, contact number, and email address.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required): Enter your preferred contact method (e.g., phone, email, or in-person).
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): Disclose any medical conditions or health concerns that may impact your fitness journey.
* **Fitness Goals** (`fitness_goals`, text, required): Clearly state your objectives for seeking fitness coaching, such as weight loss, increased endurance, or improved overall fitness.
* **Client Contracts** (`client_contracts`, text, optional): Attach any relevant contracts, including client agreements or releases, if applicable.
* **Training Setup** (`training_setup`, text, optional): Specify your preferred training environment, equipment, and facilities requirements.
* **Form Footer** (note, required): Leave any additional comments or questions for your coach to consider.

## Tips
- Be honest and clear in your responses to provide the most accurate coach match.
- If you have any attachments, such as contracts or medical documents, you can upload them separately.
- If you're unsure about any section, consult with your coach or contact the form support team.
