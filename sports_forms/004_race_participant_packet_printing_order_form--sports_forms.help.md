# race-participant-packet-printing-order-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to take orders for participant packets for sports events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **order-quantity** field with the number of packets you want to order.
2. Select the **packet-type** that you want to order from the dropdown menu.
3. Enter the **delivery-details-1** and **delivery-details-2** fields with any specific delivery instructions.
4. If you have any further payment details, you can enter them in the **payment-1**, **payment-2**, and **payment-3** fields.
5. You can select multiple options for **delivery-details-3**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **race-participant-packet-printing-order-form** (Text): A field to enter the title of your order. This field is not required.
- **order-quantity** (Number): A field to enter the quantity of packets you want to order. This field is required if you select any options for packet-type.
- **delivery-details-1** (Text): A field to enter any delivery instructions for your order. This field is optional.
- **delivery-details-2** (Text): A field to enter any additional delivery instructions for your order. This field is optional.
- **packet-type** (Select One): A dropdown menu to select the type of packet you want to order. You must select a packet type.
- **order-quantity-2** (Number): A field to enter the quantity of packets you want to order for the previously selected packet type. This field is optional.
- **payment-1** (Text): A field for any payment details. This field is optional.
- **payment-2** (Number): A field for any further payment details. This field is optional.
- **order-quantity-3** (Number): A field to enter the quantity of packets you want to order for the previously selected packet type. This field is optional.
- **payment-3** (Number): A field for any further payment details. This field is optional.
- **delivery-details-3** (Select Multiple): A dropdown menu to select any additional delivery instructions for your order. This field is optional.
