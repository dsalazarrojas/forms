# Wing Walk Participation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wing Walk Participation Survey is designed to gather feedback and information from participants of wing walks, a type of adventure activity. This survey aims to collect data on the participant's experience, ratings, and suggestions for future improvements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the questions carefully and answer them accordingly.
2. Fill in the required fields, indicated by a red asterisk (\*) in the label.
3. For select multiple options (such as wing walkers and experienced walkers), select all relevant answers.
4. Enter your comments and feedback in the text fields (such as comments, suggestions, and safety concerns).
5. Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (event_details, text, required): Please provide a brief description of the wing walk event, including the location, date, and any relevant details.
* **Event Date** (event_date, date, required): Enter the date of the wing walk event.
* **Event Time** (event_time, time, required): Enter the start and end time of the wing walk event.
* **Start Time** (start_time, time, required): Enter the start time of the wing walk event.
* **Duration** (duration, text, required): Provide the duration of the wing walk event.
* **Wing Walkers** (wing_walkers, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether you are a wing walker or not.
* **Experienced Walker** (experienced_walker, select_multiple, required): Indicate your level of experience as a wing walker.
* **Rating** (rating, number, required): Rate your experience on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Rating-2** (rating_2, text, required): Provide additional comments or ratings for the wing walk event.
* **Comments** (comments, note, required): Share your thoughts and comments about the wing walk event.
* **Feedback** (feedback, note, required): Provide feedback on how to improve the wing walk event.
* **Email** (email, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (phone, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Safety Rating** (safety_rating, number, required): Rate the safety of the wing walk event on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Safety Concerns** (safety_concerns, note, required): Share any safety concerns you had during the wing walk event.
* **Suggestions** (suggestions, text, required): Provide suggestions for future improvements.
* **Contact** (contact, text, required): Enter any additional contact information.
* **Name** (name, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Email2** (email2, text, required): Enter your email address again for confirmation.
* **Contact Phone** (contact_phone, text, required): Enter your phone number again for confirmation.
* **Event Name** (event_name, text, required): Enter the name of the wing walk event.
* **Team ID** (team_id, text, required): Enter the team ID or number of the wing walk event.
* **Team Name** (team_name, text, required): Enter the name of the team that organized the wing walk event.
* **Team Website** (team_website, text, required): Enter the website of the team that organized the wing walk event.
* **Event Website** (event_website, text, required): Enter the website of the wing walk event.
