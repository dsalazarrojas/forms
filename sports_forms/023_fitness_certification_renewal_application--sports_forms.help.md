# Fitness Certification Renewal Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help certified fitness professionals renew their certifications. This form gathers necessary information to support the certification renewal process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your Date of Birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
3. Enter your Email address for contact purposes.
4. If applicable, provide your Phone number for communication.
5. Select "Yes" if you are renewing your certification, "No" otherwise.
6. If you have any Supporting Documents, provide a clear description in the text field.
7. Confirm that you have provided true and accurate information by selecting "Yes" or "No" for each statement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you use to receive important updates and notifications.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted by our team.
* **Renewing Certification**: Select "Yes" if you are renewing your certification, "No" if this is a new application.
* **Supporting Documents**: If you have any supporting documents, provide a clear description of them.
* **Confirmation**: Confirm that you have provided true and accurate information by selecting "Yes" or "No" for each statement.
* **Submit**: Click the submit button to complete and submit your application.
