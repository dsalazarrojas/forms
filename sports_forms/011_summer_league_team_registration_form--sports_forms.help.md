# Summer League Team Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for team registration for the Summer League, a sports league where teams can register and manage their team information. The form is used to collect team details, contact information, and other relevant data for the league administrators to manage teams effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the team name and contact name sections.
2. Enter the email address and phone number of the contact person.
3. List the team members in the format "Name, Name, Name".
4. Select the division for the team (1, 2, or 3).
5. Choose the practice days for the team (if available).
6. Provide the availability dates for the team.
7. Select the team's experience (0, 1, or 2).
8. Select the coach's experience (0, 1, or 2).
9. Enter the emergency contact information (name and phone number).
10. Enter the insurance provider (if applicable).
11. Enter the insurance number (if applicable).
12. Describe any medical history or requirements.
13. Enter the doctor's name, phone number, and address (if applicable).
14. Enter the doctor's email and relationship to the team.
15. Enter the team league name and phone number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Name (1)**: Enter the name of your team.
* **Contact Name (2)**: Enter the name of the person who will be the contact for your team.
* **Email Address (3)**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone (4)**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Team Members (5)**: List the names of all team members, separated by commas.
* **Division (6)**: Select the division for your team (1, 2, or 3).
* **Practice Days (7)**: Select the practice days for your team. You can choose multiple days.
* **Availability (8)**: List the dates of availability in MM/DD format.
* **Team Experience (9)**: Select the experience level of your team (0, 1, or 2).
* **Coach Experience (10)**: Select the experience level of your coach (0, 1, or 2).
* **Emergency Contact (11)**: Enter the name and phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Insurance Provider (12)**: Enter the name of the insurance provider (if applicable).
* **Insurance Number (13)**: Enter the insurance number (if applicable).
* **Medical History (14)**: Describe any medical history or requirements for your team.
* **Doctor Name (15)**: Enter the name of the doctor (if applicable).
* **Doctor Phone (16)**: Enter the phone number of the doctor (if applicable).
* **Doctor Address (17)**: Enter the address of the doctor (if applicable).
* **Doctor Email (18)**: Enter the email of the doctor (if applicable).
* **Doctor Relation (19)**: Enter the relationship of the doctor to the team.
* **Medical Insurance (20)**: Enter the medical insurance information (if applicable).
* **Medical Notes (21)**: Enter any additional medical notes (if applicable).
* **Team League Name (22)**: Enter the name of the team league.
* **Team League Phone (23)**: Enter the phone number of the team league (if applicable).

## Tips

* Make sure to complete all required fields.
* Double-check the information provided before submission.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the form administrators.
