# Player Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Player Sign Up Form is designed to collect basic information from players, such as their name, contact details, and team affiliation. This form helps us gather essential data to manage player registrations and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Player Name** in the designated field. This is the name by which you'll be known in the team.
2. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the format of mm/dd/yy (e.g., 12/25/1999).
3. Provide your **Phone Number** as your preferred contact method. This will help us reach you with important updates and information.
4. Choose your **Team Name** from the list provided. This will help us assign you to the correct team for games and events.
5. Select your **Player Position** from the list. This will help us understand your role in the team and assign you to the correct positions in games.
6. Enter your **Email** address for secure communication. This will be used for sending team news, updates, and important notifications.
7. Enter your **Player Number** (optional, but recommended for identification purposes).
8. Enter your **Address** in the space provided. This will help us contact you in case of emergencies or important events.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Name** (text, required): This is the name by which you'll be known in the team.
* **Date of Birth** (date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yy (e.g., 12/25/1999).
* **Phone Number** (text, optional): Provide your preferred contact method for important updates and information.
* **Team Name** (select_multiple, optional): Choose your team name from the list provided.
* **Player Position** (select_one, optional): Select your position in the team from the list.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address for secure communication and updates.
* **Player Number** (number, optional): Enter your unique player number for identification purposes.
* **Address** (note, optional): Enter your address for emergency or important events contact.

## Tips

* Ensure you fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* For **Team Name**, select only one option from the list.
* For **Player Position**, select only one option from the list.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact us for assistance.
