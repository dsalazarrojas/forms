## Sports Seminar Feedback Survey - Help Guide
### Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and insights from participants about a sports seminar. It will help the seminar organizers and facilitators to better understand the quality and effectiveness of the seminar, and identify areas for improvement. The feedback collected through this form will be used to refine and enhance future seminars.

### How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by selecting the date of the seminar you are providing feedback on.
2.  Enter the name of the seminar session you attended.
3.  If you attended the seminar at a specific location, provide the details in the 'Session Location' field. If not, you can leave this field blank.
4.  Briefly describe the topic of the seminar you attended.
5.  Rate your experience of the seminar outcomes, choosing one of the four options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
6.  Indicate whether the seminar facilities met your needs, choosing one of the two options: Yes or No.
7.  Provide your contact email address.
8.  If you have any comments or additional feedback, you can type them in the 'Other Comments' field.

### Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Seminar Date** (`seminar_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the seminar you are providing feedback on.
* **Session Name** (`session_name`, text, required: true): Enter the name of the seminar session you attended.
* **Session Location** (`session_location`, text, required: false): If you attended the seminar at a specific location, enter the address or name of the location. If not, you can leave this field blank.
* **Seminar Topic** (`seminar_topic`, text, required: false): Briefly describe the topic of the seminar.
* **Seminar Outcomes** (`seminar_outcomes`, select_one, required: false): Rate your experience of the seminar outcomes, choosing one of the four options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Seminar Facilities** (`seminar_facilities`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate whether the seminar facilities met your needs, choosing one of the two options: Yes or No.
* **Seminar Organisation** (`seminar_organisation`, date, required: false): This field is currently not in use, please ignore it.
* **Seminar Notes** (`seminar_notes`, note, required: false): Type any additional comments or notes about the seminar you attended.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Other Comments** (`other_comments`, text, required: false): Type any other comments or feedback you have about the seminar.

Note: Please be as detailed and honest as possible while filling out this form to help us improve our seminars.
