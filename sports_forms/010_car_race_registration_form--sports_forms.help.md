# Car Race Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Car Race Registration Form is designed for participants to register for a car racing event. This form collects personal and vehicle information from drivers, ensuring a safe and successful race.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review each field carefully and ensure you have the necessary information before submitting.
2.  Fill in your personal details (driver's first name, last name, date of birth, email address, phone number, and driver's license number).
3.  Add your vehicle information (year, make, model, color, vehicle identification number (VIN), and engine size).
4.  Confirm your racing experience, category, distance, and entry fee amount.
5.  Confirm your health insurance coverage and safety equipment.
6.  Review your submission carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Participant Information (1)** (Required) - This is the header for your personal details.
*   **Driver first name (2)** (Required) - Enter your first name as it appears on your driver's license.
*   **Driver last name (3)** (Required) - Enter your last name as it appears on your driver's license.
*   **Date of birth (4)** (Required) - Enter your date of birth in the format of month/day/year (MM/DD/YYYY).
*   **Email address (5)** (Required) - Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
*   **Phone number (6)** (Required) - Enter your phone number with the country code and area code.
*   **Driver license number (7)** (Required) - Enter your driver's license number as it appears on your license.
*   **Years of racing experience (8)** - If you have any racing experience, enter the number of years you've been racing.
*   **Racing license number (9)** (Optional) - If you have a racing license, enter the number here.
*   **Emergency contact name (10)** (Required) - Enter the name of the person you would like us to contact in case of an emergency.
*   **Emergency contact phone (11)** (Required) - Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
*   **Vehicle Information (12)** (Optional) - This is the header for your vehicle details.
*   **Vehicle year (13)** (Required) - Enter the year of your vehicle.
*   **Vehicle make (14)** (Required) - Enter the make of your vehicle (e.g., 'Chevrolet', 'Ford', etc.).
*   **Vehicle model (15)** (Required) - Enter the model of your vehicle.
*   **Vehicle color (16)** (Optional) - Enter the color of your vehicle (e.g., 'Red', 'Blue', etc.).
*   **Vehicle VIN (17)** (Required) - Enter the Vehicle Identification Number of your vehicle.
*   **Vehicle engine size (18)** (Optional) - If you have any modifications to your vehicle's engine size, enter it here.
*   **Vehicle modifications (19)** (Optional) - If you've made any modifications to your vehicle, list them here.
*   **Registration Details (20)** (Optional) - This is the header for your race details.
*   **Race category (21)** (Required) - Choose your racing category (e.g., 'Beginner', 'Intermediate', 'Advanced', etc.).
*   **Race distance (22)** (Required) - Choose the racing distance (e.g., 'Sprint', 'Medium Distance', 'Long Distance', etc.).
*   **Entry fee amount (23)** (Required) - Enter the amount of money you're paying as an entry fee.
*   **Health insurance confirmation (24)** (Required) - Confirm if you have health insurance coverage.
*   **Safety equipment checklist (25)** (Required) - Select all the safety equipment you're aware of and will be using during the race.
