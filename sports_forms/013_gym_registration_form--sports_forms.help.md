# gym_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The gym registration form is used to collect basic contact information from individuals interested in joining the gym for membership or services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your first name in the **first_name** field.
2. Fill in your last name in the **last_name** field.
3. Enter your email address in the **email** field.
4. Enter your phone number in the **phone** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`text`, required: false): Enter your first name here. This is an optional field, but it will help us get to know you better.
* **Last Name** (`text`, required: false): Enter your last name here. This is an optional field, but it will help us get to know you better.
* **Email** (`email`, required: false): Enter your email address here. This will be used as your contact email address for communication from us. You should enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`text`, required: false): Enter your phone number here. This will be used as your contact phone number for communication from us. You should enter a valid phone number.
