# Sports Facility Marketing Event Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant consent and emergency contact information for sports facility marketing events. It's essential to fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure your participation in the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the participant details section, which includes information about yourself as a participant.
2. Next, provide emergency contact information, including details about your emergency contacts.
3. Review and select your marketing preferences in the participant details section.
4. Submit your completed form to confirm your participation in the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details**: This section is where you provide information about yourself as a participant. Please fill out your details accurately and completely.
* **Emergency Contact Information (Participant 1)**: This section is where you provide contact information for your emergency contact. Please ensure they can reach you in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Information (Participant 2)**: This is the second part of the emergency contact information. Fill out this section with details about your second emergency contact.
* **Marketing Permissions**: This section allows you to choose how you'd like to receive marketing communication from us. Please select 'True' to consent to marketing, or 'False' to opt out.
* **Participant (Confirmation)**: This is a duplicate field of participant details. Please fill it out to confirm your participation in the event. 

**Tips**
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our event staff.
* Make sure to provide up-to-date information for both emergency contacts.
