# Skating Club Membership Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from skating club members for administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the General Information section with your basic details.
2. Provide your contact details and emergency contact information.
3. Specify the relationship of the emergency contact person to you.
4. Choose your membership status, whether active, non-active, or trial member.
5. Select your membership type (skater, club, or member).
6. Fill out any additional sections as required for your specific membership status and type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **General Information**: Enter your name and other general details as requested.
- **Contact Details**: Provide your contact details, including phone number and email address.
- **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name of the person you've designated as your emergency contact.
- **Name of Contact Person**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
- **Relationship to Skater**: Choose how the emergency contact person is related to you (Parent or Guardian, Spouse or Domestic Partner, or Relative).
- **Skater Details**: Select the skater(s) you are affiliated with (Skater 1, Skater 2, or Skater 3).
- **Phone number of Emergency Contact**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
- **Email address of Emergency Contact**: Enter the email address of your emergency contact person.
- **Membership Status**: Choose your current membership status (Active Member, Non-Active Member, or Trial Member).
- **Date of Membership**: Enter the date you joined the club.
- **Date Membership Expires**: Enter the date your membership is set to expire.
- **Skater Experience**: Describe your experience as a skater.
- **Skater Interest**: Enter your interests as a skater.
- **Skater Skills**: List any relevant skills you possess as a skater.
- **Skater Equipment**: List any equipment you use for skating.
- **Skater Club Affiliations**: Enter any affiliations with other skating clubs.
- **Type of Skater Club Affiliation**: Select the type of affiliation you have with other clubs (Skater, Club, or Member).
- **Other Club Affiliations**: Enter any other club affiliations.
- **Membership Fees**: Enter any membership fees you pay.
- **Fees Frequency**: Describe the frequency of your membership fees.
- **Membership Dues**: Enter any membership dues you pay.
- **Dues Frequency**: Describe the frequency of your membership dues.
- **Membership Notes**: Enter any notes about your membership status.
- **Membership Comments**: Enter any comments about your membership experience.
- **Submitted By**: Enter your name or username.
