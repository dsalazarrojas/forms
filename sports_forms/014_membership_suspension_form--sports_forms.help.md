# Membership Suspension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Membership Suspension Form is used to suspend a user's membership. This form is used to gather relevant information to temporarily or permanently suspend a user's access to a membership-related resource or service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Review the membership status and the reason for suspension carefully.
2. Choose the correct options for each field, ensuring that you select the right answers for your specific situation.
3. Double-check your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
