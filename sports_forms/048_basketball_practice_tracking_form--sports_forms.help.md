# Basketball Practice Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for coaches and practice administrators to track basketball team practices. The form aims to collect essential information about each practice, including date, time, location, team, and player details. This information helps coaches and administrators maintain accurate records and identify trends in team performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select a practice date from the calendar input field.
2. Choose the practice time using the time input field.
3. Enter the practice location in the text field.
4. Select the team name and coach's name from the respective text input fields.
5. For each player, enter their name, select their attendance status, and enter any additional performance notes.
6. Confirm the accuracy of the attendance record by selecting "I Confirm" or "I Need to Review" from the tracking confirmation dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Practice Date**: Enter the date of the practice in the format MM/DD/YYYY. This will help you track practices over time.
* **Practice Time**: Select the time of the practice, ensuring everyone knows when the session started and ended.
* **Practice Location**: Enter the location where the practice took place, such as a specific court or gym.
* **Team Name**: Enter the name of the team that attended the practice.
* **Coach Name**: Enter the name of the coach who led the practice.
* **Player Name**: Enter each player's name, as they attended the practice.
* **Player Number**: Enter the player's number, if applicable (e.g., for tracking player IDs).
* **Attendance Status**: Select the attendance status of each player from the options: Present, Absent, Late, Excused Absence, or Injured.
* **Effort Level**: Rate the player's effort level during practice on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Focus and Concentration**: Rate the player's focus and concentration during practice on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Performance Notes**: Leave additional comments or notes about each player's performance, if necessary.
* **Practice Focus or Drills**: Describe the focus or drills of the practice session, if desired.
* **Injuries or Concerns**: Report any injuries or concerns that occurred during the practice, if applicable.
* **Tracking Confirmation**: Confirm the accuracy of the attendance record by selecting "I Confirm" or "I Need to Review".
