# Gaming Sponsorship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help game players apply for sponsorship for their gaming activities. It collects information about the player's game playing experience, goals, and online presence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Game Player Data**: Fill in your personal information.
* **What are your gaming goals?**: Describe your goals and aspirations as a gamer.
* **What are your gaming accomplishments?**: List your notable gaming achievements.
* **Consent to Terms and Conditions**: Select "I agree to the terms and conditions" to confirm that you have read and understood the terms.
* **Game Player Image**: Upload an image showcasing your gaming activities.
* **Game Player Video**: Upload a video showcasing your gaming activities.
* **Game Player Clips**: Upload clips of your gaming activities.
* **Game Player Streams**: Upload streams of your gaming activities.
* **Game Player Social Media**: Upload links to your social media profiles.
* **Game Player Links**: Upload links to your gaming-related websites or platforms.
* **Game Player Other Data**: Upload any additional information about your gaming activities.
* **Sponsor Information**: Enter any information about the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Message**: Enter a message from the sponsor.
* **Game Player Agreement**: Type a brief statement confirming that you agree with the terms and conditions.
* **Game Player Declaration**: Type a brief statement declaring your identity and gaming experience.
* **Game Player Signature**: Sign your name to confirm your identity.
* **Sponsor Signature**: Sign the name of the sponsor.
* **Game Player Date**: Fill in the current date.
* **Game Player Time**: Fill in the current time.
* **Game Player Location**: Enter the location where you are gaming.
* **Game Player Other Info**: Enter any additional information about your gaming activities.
* **Game Player Terms Agree**: Select "Yes" to confirm that you agree with the terms and conditions.
* **Game Player Terms Decline**: Not applicable if you select "Yes" in the previous field.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Review the terms and conditions carefully before selecting "I agree" in the Consent to Terms and Conditions field.
