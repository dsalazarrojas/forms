# coronavirus self assessment form heath ryan coaching orange - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from users about their experiences and actions regarding the COVID-19 pandemic. It's essential to provide honest and accurate information to help with health coaching or evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and truthfully.
2. Select one option for the "symptoms" question.
3. Provide as much detail as possible in the text fields, especially when asked about what happened, what you do now, what you've tried so far, and what you think will happen.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What happened** (`what_happened`, text, required: false): Please describe the recent events or experiences you've had related to COVID-19.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, select_one, required: false): Choose one of the options provided to select the symptoms you're experiencing:
	+ Headache
	+ Cough
	+ Fever
* **What do you do now** (`what_do_you_do_now`, text, required: false): Describe any actions you're taking or plan to take due to your experiences with COVID-19.
* **What have you tried so far** (`what_have_you_tried_so_far`, text, required: false): Share any measures or strategies you've already taken to address your experiences with COVID-19.
* **What do you think will happen** (`what_do_you_think_will_happen`, text, required: false): Share your expectations or predictions about the future regarding your experiences with COVID-19.
* **Medical history** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): Describe your medical history, which can be relevant to your experiences with COVID-19.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when providing information.
- If you're unsure about any question, please ask for clarification or guidance.
- This form is designed to support health coaching or evaluation, so please provide as much detail as possible to help with that purpose.
