## match_official_send_off_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for submitting official match reports and send-offs, helping us keep track of match details and communicate with team officials.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the Team from the provided options.
2. Choose the Player's Position, selecting all that apply.
3. If necessary, enter a Reason for the send-off.
4. Fill in the Match Date and Match Time of the event.
5. Specify the Venue where the match took place.
6. Enter the Email address of the person or official to Send To, and add a Message if needed.
7. Finally, select the Report Type (Match Report, Event Report, or Both).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player** (`number`, required/optional): Enter the Player's ID number, which can be found on the player's ID card or in the team's database.
* **Team** (`select_one`, required): Choose the Team involved in the match from the dropdown list.
* **Player Position** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select all applicable positions for the Player involved in the match.
* **Reason** (`text`, optional): Provide a clear and concise Reason for the send-off, describing the incident or issue.
* **Match Date** (`date`, optional): Enter the Date of the match, in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Match Time** (`time`, optional): Enter the Time of the match, in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Venue** (`text`, optional): Specify the location where the match took place.
* **Send To** (`email`, optional): Enter the Email address of the person or official to send the report to.
* **Message** (`text`, optional): Add any additional Message or information related to the match or report.
* **Report Type** (`select_one`, optional): Choose the type of Report to be submitted (Match Report, Event Report, or Both).
