# Sports Medicine Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the discharge process for patients treated in a sports medicine setting. It collects information on patient details, medical conditions, and treatment plans to ensure continuity of care and proper follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Review the form carefully** to ensure all required fields are completed accurately.
2. Ensure you have the patient's informed consent and obtain their signature on the "Patient Signature" field.
3. Complete the "Provider Name" field with your name and title.
4. Enter the start date of the visit in the "Visit Start Date" field (if applicable).
5. For patients who are being discharged, enter the "Discharge Date" field.
6. Provide guidance on returning to activity in the "Return to Activity Guidance" field (if applicable).
7. Offer instructions for rehabilitation in the "Instructions for Rehabilitation" field (if applicable).
8. Schedule the next appointment in the "Next Appointment" field (if applicable).
9. Record details on the medical condition(s) in the "Medical Condition Description" field (if applicable).
10. Verify insurance information with the patient and enter the insurance provider's name in the "Insurance Provider Name" field (if applicable).
11. Document the patient's medical history and medication list in the "Medical History" and "Medications List" fields (if applicable).
12. Record any allergies in the "Allergy List" field (if applicable).
13. Record any medical notes in the "Medical Notes" field (if applicable).
14. Sign the form to confirm you've reviewed and verified the information.
15. Obtain the staff's signature to confirm they've reviewed and processed the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name (1)**: Enter the patient's full name, as recorded on their medical chart.
* **Condition (2)**: Describe the medical condition(s) being treated.
* **Visit Start Date (3)**: Enter the start date of the visit, if applicable.
* **Provider Name (4)**: Enter your name and title.
* **Discharge Date (5)**: Enter the date the patient is being discharged.
* **Return to Activity Guidance (6)**: Provide guidance on returning to activity, if applicable.
* **Instructions for Rehabilitation (7)**: Offer instructions for rehabilitation, if applicable.
* **Next Appointment (8)**: Schedule the next appointment, if applicable.
* **Follow-up Visit (9)**: Record details on the follow-up visit, if applicable.
* **Medical Condition Description (10)**: Describe the medical condition(s) in detail.
* **Patient Phone (11)**: Verify the patient's phone number.
* **Insurance Provider Name (12)**: Verify the insurance provider's name.
* **Medical History (13)**: Document the patient's medical history.
* **Medications List (14)**: Record the patient's list of medications.
* **Allergy List (15)**: Record any allergies.
* **Medical Notes (16)**: Record any medical notes.
* **Patient Signature (17)**: Obtain the patient's signature to confirm they've reviewed and agreed to the information.
* **Provider Signature (18)**: Sign to confirm you've reviewed and verified the information.
* **Date Completed (19)**: Enter the date the form was completed.
* **Time Completed (20)**: Enter the time the form was completed.
* **Staff Signature (21)**: Obtain the staff's signature to confirm they've reviewed and processed the form.
* **Clinic Visit Type (22)**: Check if the visit was a clinic visit.
* **Sports Injury Type (23)**: Check if the visit was for a sports injury.
* **Medical Condition Type (24)**: Check if the visit was for a medical condition.
* **Insurance Provider (25)**: Check if the patient has an insurance provider.

Note: The fields with "required: true" are mandatory and must be filled out.
