# wrestling_medical_release_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather medical information and emergency contact details from a wrestler. It is a requirement for wrestlers to complete this form before participating in wrestling activities. The form is used to:

- Record a wrestler's medical conditions and allergies
- Gather medical provider information
- Establish emergency contact details
- Obtain consent for medical treatment
- Collect signatures from the wrestler and/or parent/guardian

## How To Complete This Form

1. Complete each section carefully and accurately. If you're unsure about any information, ask a coach or administrator for assistance.
2. Please ensure that all fields are filled out completely, as incomplete forms may delay or prevent your participation in wrestling activities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Wrestler Information (Page 1)
**Wrestler Info** (text, required: false)
Enter your full name and any other names you are commonly known as.

### Medical Conditions (Page 1)
**Medical Conditions** (text, required: false)
List any medical conditions, such as injuries, illnesses, or allergies, that may affect your ability to participate in wrestling activities.

### Concussion/Concussion (Page 1)
**Concussion/Concussion** (select_multiple, required: false)
Indicate if you have ever experienced a concussion or head injury.

### Head Injury (Page 1)
**Head Injury** (select_one, required: false)
Indicate if you have ever experienced a head injury.

### Knee Injury (Page 1)
**Knee Injury** (select_multiple, required: false)
Indicate if you have ever experienced a knee injury.

### Medical Providers (Page 2)
**Medical Providers** (text, required: false)
List any medical providers you have seen in the past 12 months.

### Allergies (Page 2)
**Allergies** (select_multiple, required: false)
Indicate any allergies you have.

### Medical Conditions (Page 2)
This field is not used in the form.

### Emergency Contact (Page 2)
**Emergency Contact** (text, required: false)
Enter the name of the person you have designated as your emergency contact.

### Emergency Name (Page 2)
**Emergency Name** (text, required: false)
Enter the name of your emergency contact.

### Emergency Phone (Page 2)
**Emergency Phone** (text, required: false)
Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.

### Emergency Address (Page 2)
**Emergency Address** (text, required: false)
Enter the address of your emergency contact.

### Emergency Relationship (Page 2)
**Emergency Relationship** (text, required: false)
Describe your relationship with your emergency contact (e.g., parent, guardian, friend).

### Emergency Other (Page 2)
**Emergency Other** (text, required: false)
Enter any additional information about your emergency contact.

### Signature (Page 3)
**Signature** (text, required: false)
Sign this form to confirm that you have completed it accurately and truthfully.

### Medical Providers (Page 3)
**Medical Providers** (text, required: false)
List any medical providers you have seen in the past 12 months.

### Medical Provider (Page 3)
This field is not used in the form.

### Medical Notes (Page 3)
This field is not used in the form.

### Consent (Page 3)
**Consent** (select_one, required: false)
Indicate if you consent to medical treatment.

### Parent/Guardian Signature (Page 3)
**Parent/Guardian Signature** (select_one, required: false)
Indicate if you are a parent or guardian and sign this form.

### Emergency Parent/Guardian Signature (Page 3)
**Emergency Parent/Guardian Signature** (select_one, required: false)
Indicate if you are a parent or guardian and sign this form for your emergency contact.

## Tips

- If you have any medical conditions or allergies that affect your ability to participate in wrestling, please list them in the "Medical Conditions" field.
- If you have any medical providers that you have seen in the past 12 months, list them in the "Medical Providers" field.
- Ensure that all fields are filled out completely and accurately to avoid any issues with your participation in wrestling activities.
- If you are unsure about any information, ask a coach or administrator for assistance.
