# JumpStart Personal Training Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The JumpStart Personal Training Assessment Form is designed to gather essential information from clients about their personal training needs, fitness level, and medical conditions. This form helps coaches or trainers create a tailored training plan that suits the client's goals and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the client's information:
	* **Client ID**: A unique identifier for the client.
	* **Client Name**: The client's name.
	* **Client Email**: The client's email address (optional).
2. Select the client's **Training Level**: Choose between Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced to describe the client's current fitness level.
3. Indicate the client's **Training Frequency**: Select how often the client trains, such as Once a week, Twice a week, or Three or more times a week.
4. Specify the client's **Goals**: Choose one or more goals for training, such as Increase Strength, Improve Flexibility, Increase Endurance, or Lose Weight.
5. Describe the client's **Current Level of Activity**: Select how active the client currently is, such as None, Low, or High.
6. Indicate the client's **Exercise Background**: Choose between None, Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced to describe the client's exercise experience.
7. List any **Medical Conditions**: Select one or more medical conditions that may impact the client's training, such as High Blood Pressure, Diabetes, or Other (please specify).
8. Provide the **Emergency Contact Name**: The name of a person the client would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
9. Choose the **Relationship** between the client and the emergency contact, such as Spouse, Parent, Friend, or Family Member.
10. Enter the **Emergency Contact Phone**: A phone number for the emergency contact.
11. Indicate if the client has a **Medical Alert**: Select Yes, please provide a medical alert or No, I am not aware of any medical conditions.
12. Enter any additional **Medical Conditions** the client would like to specify.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client ID** (`1`, number, required): This field is where you enter the client's unique identifier.
* **Client Name** (`2`, text, required): This is where you enter the client's name.
* **Client Email** (`3`, email, optional): This field is where you enter the client's email address, if they have one.
* **Training Level** (`4`, select_one, required): Choose between Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced to describe the client's current fitness level.
* **Training Frequency** (`5`, select_multiple, required): Select how often the client trains, such as Once a week, Twice a week, or Three or more times a week.
* **Goals** (`6`, select_one, required): Choose one or more goals for training, such as Increase Strength, Improve Flexibility, Increase Endurance, or Lose Weight.
* **Current Level of Activity** (`7`, select_multiple, required): Select how active the client currently is, such as None, Low, or High.
* **Exercise Background** (`8`, select_one, required): Choose between None, Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced to describe the client's exercise experience.
* **Medical Conditions** (`9`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more medical conditions that may impact the client's training, such as High Blood Pressure, Diabetes, or Other (please specify).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`10`, text, required): The name of a person the client would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Relationship** (`11`, select_one, required): Choose the relationship between the client and the emergency contact, such as Spouse, Parent, Friend, or Family Member.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`12`, text, required): A phone number for the emergency contact.
* **Medical Alert** (`13`, select_multiple, required): Select Yes, please provide a medical alert or No, I am not aware of any medical conditions.

## Tips

* Make sure to complete each field accurately and thoroughly to ensure the coach or trainer has a clear understanding of the client's needs.
* If the client has a medical condition, please specify it in the Medical Conditions field.
* If the client has a medical alert, please select the Yes option and provide further information in the field above.
* Make sure to enter the correct phone number for the emergency contact.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask the coach or trainer for guidance.
