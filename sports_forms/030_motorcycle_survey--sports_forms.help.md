# Motorcycle Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Motorcycle Survey is a short and simple form designed to gather information about motorcyclists' preferences and experiences with their motorcycles. This form will help us improve the motorcycling experience by understanding what type of motorcycles people like, what they prefer, and their ownership experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and truthfully.
2. Select all applicable options for each question.
3. Make sure to fill in all required fields.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What kind of motorcycle do you drive?**
	+ This is the first question to help us understand what type of motorcycle you are familiar with.
	+ Answer this question with the type of motorcycle you own or ride frequently.
* **What do you like about your motorcycle?**
	+ In this text field, please describe what you like or enjoy about your motorcycle.
	+ Feel free to be as specific or general as you prefer.
* **Do you own a motorcycle?**
	+ This is a yes/no question to determine if you own a motorcycle.
* **Who does the motorcycle belong to?**
	+ Select all people who own or ride the motorcycle you are answering for.
* **What year was the motorcycle purchased?**
	+ Answer this question with the year the motorcycle was purchased.
* **What type of motorcycle do you have?**
	+ Select all the types of motorcycles that describe your bike.
* **What color is your motorcycle?**
	+ Answer this question with the color of your motorcycle.
* **Why do you like your motorcycle?**
	+ In this text field, describe why you enjoy riding or owning your motorcycle.
* **Who is the pilot of the motorcycle?**
	+ Select all people who ride the motorcycle you are answering for.
* **What year was the motorcycle last serviced?**
	+ Answer this question with the year the motorcycle was last serviced.
* **What color was the motorcycle last painted?**
	+ Answer this question with the color the motorcycle was last painted.
* **Motorcycle Preferences Why 2**
	+ In this text field, describe why you like or enjoy your motorcycle.
* **Motorbike Possessions What Type 2**
	+ Select all the types of motorcycles that describe your bike.
* **What model is your motorcycle?**
	+ Answer this question with the model of your motorcycle.
