# Athletic Questionnaire Recruitment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Athletic Questionnaire Recruitment Form is a comprehensive tool used to collect personal and athletic information from potential recruits for various sports programs. This form will help us get to know you better and assess your fit for our programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Please fill out the form carefully, ensuring that all required fields are completed accurately.
*   For fields marked as "text", please enter your response in the provided text box.
*   For fields marked as "select_multiple", please choose all the options that apply.
*   For fields marked as "number", please enter a numerical value.
*   Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Personal Information**
    -   -- Personal Information --
        *   **First Name**: Enter your first name here.
        *   **Last Name**: Enter your last name here.
        *   **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
        *   **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number here.
        *   **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the given format (YYYY-MM-DD).
        *   **Current School**: Enter the name of your current school or college.
        *   **Grade or Graduation Year**: Enter your current grade or graduation year.
        *   **City, State**: If applicable, enter your current city and state of residence.
*   **Athletic Background**
    -   -- Athletic Background --
        *   **Sport Interested In**: Choose all the sports you are interested in from the provided options.
        *   **Position/Event Played**: Enter your primary position or event played.
        *   **Current Club or Travel Team**: If applicable, enter the name of your current club or travel team.
        *   **Jersey Number**: Enter your current jersey number, if applicable.
*   **Physical Statistics**
    -   -- Physical Statistics --
        *   **Height**: Enter your height in inches or cm.
        *   **Weight**: Enter your weight in pounds or kg.
        *   **Current GPA**: Enter your current academic grade point average.
*   **Motivation and Interest**
    -   -- Motivation and Interest --
        *   **Why are you interested in our program?**: Tell us about your interest in joining our program.
*   **Additional Information**
    -   **Additional Information**: If you have any other information you would like to share, enter it here.

**Tips**

*   Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.
*   We appreciate your honesty and transparency in filling out this form.
