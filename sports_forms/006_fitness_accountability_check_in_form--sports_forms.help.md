# Fitness Accountability Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to provide a comprehensive overview of your fitness journey, allowing you to track your progress, set goals, and stay accountable to your workout routine.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by entering your General Information, including any relevant details about your current fitness level, goals, and preferences.
2.  Next, provide information about the Exercise you performed, including the type and description.
3.  Track your progress by entering the Frequency of your workouts and the Progress Tracking date.
4.  Create a Workout Plan by selecting the appropriate exercise type and entering the relevant details.
5.  Indicate your Goal and whether you aim to achieve it.
6.  Provide any Comments or feedback you have about your workout session.
7.  Select the Type of Exercise you performed.
8.  Enter a brief Description of the Exercise, including any notable aspects or observations.
9.  Add any Exercise Notes that are relevant to your workout session.
10. Enter the Workout Time spent on the exercise.
11. Specify the Workout Date of the exercise.
12. Record any Trainer Comments or feedback received during the session.
13. Enter your Email address for communication purposes.
14. Enter your Phone number for contact purposes.
15. Select the Assigned Trainer who guided you during the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **General Info** (`General Info`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of your current fitness level, goals, and preferences.
*   **Exercise** (`Exercise`, text, required: false): Provide information about the exercise you performed during the session.
*   **Frequency** (`Frequency`, text, required: false): Record the frequency of your workouts.
*   **Progress Tracking** (`Progress Tracking`, date, required: false): Enter the date of your last progress tracking.
*   **Workout Plan** (`Workout Plan`, number, required: false): Create a plan for your workouts by selecting an appropriate number.
*   **Goal** (`Goal`, select_one, required: false): Indicate whether you aim to achieve a goal.
*   **Comments** (`Comments`, text, required: false): Enter any comments or feedback about your workout session.
*   **Type of Exercise** (`Type of Exercise`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the type of exercise you performed.
*   **Exercise Description** (`Exercise Description`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the exercise, including notable aspects or observations.
*   **Exercise Notes** (`Exercise Notes`, text, required: false): Add any exercise notes that are relevant to your workout session.
*   **Workout Time** (`Workout Time`, time, required: false): Enter the time spent on the workout.
*   **Workout Date** (`Workout Date`, date, required: false): Specify the date of the exercise.
*   **Trainer Comments** (`Trainer Comments`, text, required: false): Record any comments from your trainer.
*   **Email** (`Email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
*   **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Assigned Trainer** (`Assigned Trainer`, select_one, required: false): Select the trainer assigned to you during the session.

Note: The fields are listed in the order they appear in the form. Please ensure you complete each field according to the provided instructions.
