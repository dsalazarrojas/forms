# summer_sports_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The summer sports registration form is a crucial document that helps us collect relevant information from users who want to participate in sports activities during the summer season.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form by following the steps below.
2. Start by entering your basic information on **page 1: Basic Information**.
3. Proceed to **page 2: Personal** to provide your personal details.
4. On **page 3: Sports Experience**, share your sports-related experience.
5. Move on to **page 4: Emergency Contact**, where you can provide information about your emergency contact.
6. On **page 5: Medical Info**, share any relevant medical details.
7. **Page 6: Emergency Details** is where you can provide additional information about your emergency contact.
8. **Page 7: Consent** is where you can express your agreement or disagreement with our terms and conditions.
9. **Page 8: Email** is where you can provide your email address.
10. **Page 9: Phone** is where you can enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Basic Information (Page 1)**: This is the starting point of the form where you can enter your name, etc.
* **Personal (Page 2)**: Here, you can provide more about yourself.
* **Sports Experience (Page 3)**: Share your sports experience to help us better understand your background.
* **Emergency Contact (Page 4)**: Please provide information about who to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Medical Info (Page 5)**: Share any medical details that might be relevant to your participation in sports activities.
* **Emergency Details (Page 6)**: Provide any additional emergency contact information.
* **Consent (Page 7)**: Select either "I agree with the terms and conditions" or "I do not agree with the terms and conditions" to confirm your agreement.
* **Email (Page 8)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone (Page 9)**: Enter your phone number for easy communication.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have filled out all required fields correctly.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* Make sure to review our terms and conditions before expressing your consent.
