# midsummer_viking_challenge_2020 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The midsummer_viking_challenge_2020 form is intended for interested participants to sign up for the Midsummer Viking Challenge. The form is designed to collect basic information such as name, reason for participation, and email for communication purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter Your Name or Nickname**: In the **midsummer-viking-challenge-2020** field, type your name or nickname as you would like to be known by in the challenge.
2. **Describe Your Interest**: In the **sign-up-for-midsummer-viking-challenge** field, provide a brief description of why you are interested in participating in the Midsummer Viking Challenge.
3. **Provide Your Email**: In the **email** field, enter your email address for the organizers to contact you with updates, rules, and other important information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **midsummer-viking-challenge-2020**:
	+ A text field for you to enter your name or nickname.
	+ Required: No
* **sign-up-for-midsummer-viking-challenge**:
	+ A text field where you can describe your interest in participating in the challenge.
	+ Required: No
* **email**:
	+ A field for you to enter your email address for communication purposes.
	+ Required: Yes
