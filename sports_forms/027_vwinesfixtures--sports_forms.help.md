# vwinesfixtures - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "vwinesfixtures" form is designed to collect information about sports fixtures, specifically the home and away teams, score, date, and score time of a match.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the home and away teams from the options provided in the "Home and Away" field.
2. Enter the score of the match in the "Score" field.
3. Enter the date of the match in the "Date" field.
4. Enter the score time of the match in the "Score Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Home and Away** (`Home and Away`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the two teams involved in the match from the available options.
* **Score** (`Score`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the final score of the match as a numerical value.
* **Date** (`Date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the match in the standard date format (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Score Time** (`Score Time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time the match started or finished, or the score time for a specific segment of the match.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct home and away teams to ensure accurate match information.
* Use the standard date format (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD) when entering the date.
* Enter the score time accurately, considering the start or end time of the match, or a specific segment of the match.
