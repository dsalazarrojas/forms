# Rimfire Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rimfire Registration Form is designed to gather information from shooters and participants about their event registration details. This form is used to collect relevant information for event planning and management purposes. Please ensure that you are eligible and have the necessary permissions to complete this form, as it is intended for internal use within the shooting sports community.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your accurate information.
2. Ensure that you have filled out all the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
3. Double-check your responses for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shooter Information (Shooter Information, text, optional)**: Enter your name and any other identifying information about yourself.
* **Firearm Information (Firearm Information, text, optional)**: Describe your firearm in detail, including make, model, caliber, and any other relevant information.
* **Safety Agreement (Safety Agreement, text, optional)**: Read and agree to our safety guidelines, ensuring that you have read and understood the terms and conditions.
* **Participant Details (Participant Details, text, optional)**: Enter any relevant details about yourself as a participant, such as your experience level or any medical conditions.
* **Event Information (Event Information, text, optional)**: Enter any additional event-specific information, such as the event name or date.
* **Contact Information (Contact Information, text, optional)**: Enter your contact information, including phone number and email address.
* **Emergency Contacts (Emergency Contacts, text, optional)**: List any emergency contacts, including their phone number and relationship to you.
* **Event Sponsor (Event Sponsor, text, optional)**: If applicable, list the event sponsor and their contact information.
* **Event Date (Event Date, date, optional)**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Start Time (Event Start Time, time, optional)**: Enter the start time of the event.
* **Event End Time (Event End Time, time, optional)**: Enter the end time of the event.
* **Event Duration (Event Duration, number, optional)**: Enter the duration of the event.
* **Event Location (Event Location, text, optional)**: Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Type (Event Type, select_one, optional)**: Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if this is a practice or competition event.
* **Match Details (Match Details, text, optional)**: Enter any match-specific details, such as the match format or number of rounds.
* **Match Format (Match Format, select_one, optional)**: Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if this is a match format event.
* **Match Length (Match Length, number, optional)**: Enter the length of the match in minutes or hours.
* **Match Rounds (Match Rounds, number, optional)**: Enter the number of rounds in the match.
* **Match Stage (Match Stage, select_one, optional)**: Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if this is a match stage event.
* **Match Division (Match Division, select_one, optional)**: Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if this is a match division event.
* **Event Aims (Event Aims, text, optional)**: Enter any event-specific aims or objectives.
* **Participant Age (Participant Age, number, optional)**: Enter your age as a participant.
* **Firearm Information 2 (Firearm Information 2, text, optional)**: Describe your second firearm in detail, including make, model, caliber, and any other relevant information.
* **Membership Type (Membership Type, select_one, optional)**: Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you are a member of our organization.

Please note that while this form is designed to be user-friendly, it is still a requirement that you fill out all the required fields accurately and completely. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
