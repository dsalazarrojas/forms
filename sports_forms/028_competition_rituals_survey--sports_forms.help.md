# Competition Rituals Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on pre-competition rituals from athletes and individuals involved in various sports and activities. The survey aims to understand the types of rituals people have before competing, how they impact performance, and how they vary depending on the competition type.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. For fields that do not require a specific answer, you can choose any option that best fits your situation.
3. Please answer all questions to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, text): Share your name with us (optional).
* **Your Email** (`respondent_email`, email): Provide your email for follow-up purposes (optional).
* **Sport or Activity** (`sport_or_activity`, text): Type the name of the sport or activity you compete in.
* **Competition Level** (`competition_level`, select_one): Select your level of competition:
	+ Recreational
	+ Amateur
	+ Collegiate
	+ Professional
	+ Elite or Olympic
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Years of Competition Experience** (`years_competing`, select_one): Select how long you have competed:
	+ Less than 1 year
	+ 1 to 3 years
	+ 4 to 6 years
	+ 7 to 10 years
	+ More than 10 years
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, select_one): Select your age category:
	+ Under 18
	+ 18 to 24
	+ 25 to 34
	+ 35 to 44
	+ 45 to 54
	+ 55 and over
* **Pre-Competition Meal Ritual** (`pre_competition_meal`, select_one): Select your pre-competition meal preference:
	+ Yes - always the same meal
	+ Yes - similar meals
	+ Sometimes
	+ Rarely
	+ No - eat whatever
* **Meal Ritual Details** (`meal_details`, text): Describe your pre-competition meal routine.
* **Warm-up Routine** (`warmup_routine`, select_one): Select your warm-up preference:
	+ Yes - exact same routine
	+ Yes - similar routine
	+ Sometimes
	+ Rarely
	+ No - vary each time
* **Warm-up Routine Details** (`warmup_details`, text): Describe your warm-up routine.
* **Music Usage** (`music_usage`, select_one): Select your music preference:
	+ Yes - always same playlist
	+ Yes - specific songs
	+ Sometimes
	+ Rarely
	+ No - never
* **Music Details** (`music_details`, text): Type the specific music you listen to before competing.
* **Clothing or Gear Rituals** (`clothing_ritual`, select_one): Select your clothing or gear preference:
	+ Yes - lucky items
	+ Yes - specific order
	+ Sometimes
	+ Rarely
	+ No - never
* **Clothing or Gear Rituals Details** (`clothing_details`, text): Describe your clothing or gear ritual.
* **Mental Preparation Rituals** (`mental_preparation`, select_one): Select your mental preparation preference:
	+ Yes - meditation
	+ Yes - visualization
	+ Yes - prayer
	+ Yes - other mental routine
	+ No - no mental ritual
* **Mental Preparation Details** (`mental_details`, text): Describe your mental preparation routine.
* **Superstitions** (`superstitions`, select_one): Select how many superstitions you have:
	+ Yes - multiple
	+ Yes - one or two
	+ Maybe - not sure
	+ Rarely
	+ No - not superstitious
* **Superstition Details** (`superstition_details`, text): Describe your superstition.
* **Social Rituals** (`social_rituals`, select_one): Select your social preference:
	+ Yes - team huddle
	+ Yes - talk with coach
	+ Yes - talk with family
	+ Yes - avoid social contact
	+ No - no social ritual
* **Arrival Time Ritual** (`arrival_time_ritual`, select_one): Select your arrival preference:
	+ Yes - always early
	+ Yes - exactly on time
	+ Yes - specific timing
	+ Sometimes
	+ No - varies
* **Ritual Importance** (`ritual_importance`, select_one): Rate the importance of your rituals:
	+ Extremely important - must do
	+ Very important
	+ Somewhat important
	+ Slightly important
	+ Not important at all
* **Ritual Impact on Performance** (`ritual_impact`, select_one): Select the effect of your rituals on performance:
	+ Yes - significant positive impact
	+ Yes - slight positive impact
	+ No impact
	+ Yes - negative impact
	+ Not sure
* **Effect of Missing a Ritual** (`missed_ritual_effect`, select_one): Select how you feel when you miss a ritual:
	+ Very anxious - affects performance
	+ Somewhat anxious
	+ Slightly bothered
	+ No effect
	+ Never missed
* **Origin of Rituals** (`ritual_origin`, select_one): Select how your rituals started:
	+ Developed over time naturally
	+ Learned from coach or mentor
	+ Copied from role model
	+ Started after a good performance
	+ Family tradition
	+ Not sure
* **Rituals by Competition Type** (`rituals_by_competition_type`, select_one): Select how your rituals vary by competition type:
	+ Yes - very different
	+ Yes - slightly different
	+ No - same for all
	+ Not sure
* **Willingness to Share Rituals** (`would_share_rituals`, select_one): Select how willing you are to share your rituals:
	+ Yes - happy to share
	+ Maybe - with some people
	+ No - prefer private
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text): Share any other thoughts about your rituals.
