# Gym Registration Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gym Registration Form Template is designed to collect personal and health information from individuals interested in joining a gym.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the Personal Info page with your personal details.
2. On the Body Measure page, provide your current height, weight, and other relevant body measurements.
3. On the Fitness Goals page, describe your goals and expectations from the gym experience.
4. Finally, read and agree to the Acceptance of Terms page, which includes our gym's rules and regulations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Personal Info
* **Personal Info (Personal Info, text, optional):** This page is for filling out your name, email, and any other contact information you'd like to share with us.

### Body Measure
* **Body Measure (Body Measure, text, optional):** This page is for recording your height, weight, and any other body measurements you're comfortable sharing with us. This information will help us understand your fitness needs and provide you with better recommendations.

### Fitness Goals
* **Fitness Goals (Fitness Goals, text, optional):** This page is for describing your fitness goals and expectations from the gym experience. Please be as specific as possible to help us tailor our services to your needs.

### Acceptance of Terms
* **Acceptance of Terms (Acceptance of Terms, text, optional):** This page is for reading and agreeing to our gym's rules and regulations. Please read the terms carefully before submitting your registration.
