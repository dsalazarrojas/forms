# Sports Competition Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Competition Information Collection Form is used to gather information about sports competitions, including details about the competition, participants, and events. This form helps the organizers and administrators of the competition to better understand the participant's needs and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Competition Name field with the name of the sports competition you are participating in.
2. Select the type of competition (e.g., Football, Basketball, Baseball, Tennis) from the Competition Type dropdown menu.
3. Enter your name in the Participant Name field.
4. Enter your age in the Participant Age field.
5. Enter your contact information (e.g., phone number, email) in the Participant Contact field.
6. Enter your date of birth in the Participant Date of Birth field.
7. Select your gender from the Participant Gender dropdown menu.
8. Select your school from the Participant School dropdown menu (e.g., High School, Middle School, Elementary School, University).
9. Indicate if you are participating in a league by selecting the Participant League option.
10. Indicate if you are a participant in a competition by selecting the Participant Category option.
11. Indicate if you have any subcategories or additional information by selecting the Participant Subcategory option.
12. Enter your address in the Participant Address field.
13. Enter your city in the Participant City field.
14. Select your state from the Participant State dropdown menu.
15. Enter your zip code in the Participant Zip field.
16. Enter the address of the competition in the Competition Address field.
17. Enter the city of the competition in the Competition City field.
18. Select if the competition is located in the same state as the participant by selecting the Competition State option.
19. Enter the zip code of the competition in the Competition Zip field.
20. Enter any additional notes about your participation or the competition in the Participant Notes field.
