# Pole Bending Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pole Bending Event Registration form is used to register participants for a pole bending event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Provide your participant details (name, email, and phone number).
* Choose your participant type (Rider, Driver, or Spectator).
* Enter the payment amount (if applicable).
* Select the payment gateway (Stripe, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
* Enter the event date and time.
* Choose the event venue.
* Enter the number of participants.
* Select the event status (Pending, Confirmed, or Cancelled).
* Choose the payment status (Paid, Unpaid, or Partially Paid).
* Select whether a tool has been assigned to you.
* Add any additional notes.
* Enter the date the form was created and updated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`Name`, text, required): Enter your name as you want it to appear in the registration.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Participant Type** (`Participant Type`, select_multiple, required): Choose whether you are a Rider, Driver, or Spectator.
* **Payment Amount** (`Payment Amount`, number, required): Enter the amount paid for the event (if applicable).
* **Payment Gateway** (`Payment Gateway`, select_one, required): Select the payment method used (Stripe, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
* **Event Date** (`Event Date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`Event Time`, time, required): Enter the time of the event.
* **Event Venue** (`Event Venue`, note, required): Enter the venue of the event.
* **Participant Count** (`Participant Count`, number, required): Enter the number of participants.
* **Event Status** (`Event Status`, select_one, required): Select the event status (Pending, Confirmed, or Cancelled).
* **Payment Status** (`Payment Status`, select_one, required): Select the payment status (Paid, Unpaid, or Partially Paid).
* **Assigned Tool** (`Assigned Tool`, select_one, required): Select whether a tool has been assigned to you.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, note, required): Enter any additional notes about your registration.
* **Created at** (`Created at`, date, required): Displays the date the form was created.
* **Updated at** (`Updated at`, date, required): Displays the date the form was updated.
* **Form ID** (`Form ID`, text, required): Displays the unique ID of the form.
* **Category** (`Category`, select_one, required): Select the category of the event (Sports Forms, Events, or Other).
* **Description** (`Description`, note, required): Enter a brief description of the event.
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, select_one, required): Select the payment method used (Cash, Credit Card, or Online Payment).
* **Assigned Form** (`Assigned Form`, select_one, required): Select whether a form has been assigned to you.
* **Created by** (`Created by`, select_one, required): Select who created the form.
* **Updated by** (`Updated by`, select_one, required): Select who updated the form.
* **Updated at** (`Updated at`, date, required): Displays the date the form was last updated.
