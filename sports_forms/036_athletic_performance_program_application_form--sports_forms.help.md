# Athletic Performance Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Athletic Performance Program Application Form is designed to collect information from athletes or their guardians to assess their suitability for the program. This information will be used to tailor a comprehensive training regimen to their needs and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out the form completely and honestly.
2.  Ensure all required fields are filled in (marked as `required` in the field explanations).
3.  Be as specific as possible when answering questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Full Name (1)** (`Applicant Full Name`, `text`, required): Enter the athlete's first and last name.
* **Date of Birth (2)** (`Date of Birth`, `date`, required): Enter the athlete's date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
* **Gender (3)** (`Gender`, `select_one`, required): Select the athlete's gender from the provided options.
* **Email Address (4)** (`Email Address`, `email`, required): Enter the athlete's contact email address.
* **Phone Number (5)** (`Phone Number`, `text`, required): Enter the athlete's contact phone number.
* **Street Address (6)** (`Street Address`, `text`, required): Enter the athlete's home street address.
* **City (7)** (`City`, `text`, required): Enter the city where the athlete resides.
* **State (8)** (`State`, `text`, required): Enter the state or province where the athlete resides.
* **ZIP Code (9)** (`ZIP Code`, `text`, required): Enter the athlete's ZIP or postal code.
* **School Name (10)** (`School Name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the athlete's current school.
* **Grade Level (11)** (`Grade Level`, `select_one`, required): Select the athlete's current grade level.
* **Primary Sport (12)** (`Primary Sport`, `select_one`, required): Select the athlete's primary sport.
* **Primary Position (13)** (`Primary Position`, `text`, required): Enter the athlete's main position played.
* **Years of Experience (14)** (`Years of Experience`, `number`, required): Enter the number of years the athlete has played their primary sport.
* **Current Team (15)** (`Current Team`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the athlete's current team.
* **Coach Name (16)** (`Coach Name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the athlete's current coach.
* **Coach Contact (17)** (`Coach Contact`, `text`, required): Enter the coach's contact email or phone number.
* **Height (18)** (`Height`, `text`, required): Enter the athlete's height in feet and inches or cm.
* **Weight (19)** (`Weight`, `number`, required): Enter the athlete's weight in pounds or kg.
* **Athletic Achievements (20)** (`Athletic Achievements`, `text`, required): List any notable awards, honors, or records the athlete has achieved.
* **Personal Records or Stats (21)** (`Personal Records or Stats`, `text`, optional): List any personal statistics or records the athlete has.
* **Program Selection (22)** (`Program Selection`, `select_one`, required): Select the athletic program the athlete is applying for.
* **Primary Training Goals (23)** (`Primary Training Goals`, `text`, required): Enter the athlete's primary training goals or objectives.
* **Training Availability (24)** (`Training Availability`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the athlete's available training times.
* **Days Available Per Week (25)** (`Days Available Per Week`, `number`, required): Enter the number of days the athlete is available to train per week.
* **Injury History (26)** (`Injury History`, `text`, required): List any significant injuries the athlete has experienced.
* **Current Injuries or Limitations (27)** (`Current Injuries or Limitations`, `text`, required): List any current injuries or limitations the athlete has.
* **Medical Clearance Status (28)** (`Medical Clearance Status`, `select_one`, required): Select the athlete's current medical clearance status.
* **Previous Training Experience (29)** (`Previous Training Experience`, `text`, optional): Describe any previous training experiences or programs the athlete has participated in.
* **Why Do You Want to Join This Program (30)** (`Why Do You Want to Join This Program`, `text`, required): Enter the athlete's motivation for joining the program.
* **How Did You Hear About Us (31)** (`How Did You Hear About Us`, `select_one`, required): Select the source that referred the athlete to the program.
* **Parent or Guardian Name (32)** (`Parent or Guardian Name`, `text`, optional): Enter the parent or guardian's name if applicable.
* **Parent or Guardian Contact (33)** (`Parent or Guardian Contact`, `text`, optional): Enter the parent or guardian's contact email or phone number if applicable.
* **Application Date (34)** (`Application Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the athlete's application was submitted.
* **Additional Comments (35)** (`Additional Comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or information the athlete wants to share.
