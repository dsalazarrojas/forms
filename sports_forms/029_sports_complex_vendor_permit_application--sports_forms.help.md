# Sports Complex Vendor Permit Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to apply for a vendor permit to operate at a sports complex. The purpose of this form is to collect information from vendors who wish to sell their products or services at the complex. Vendors must provide their name, address, contact number, and other relevant details to ensure a smooth and secure business operation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name, address, and contact number to ensure we can get in touch with you.
2. Choose the correct start and end dates for your permit, if applicable.
3. Indicate your permit status (Active, Inactive, or Cancelled).
4. Confirm your insurance status (True or False).
5. Accept the terms and conditions of doing business at the sports complex.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (Text, Required): Enter your business name as it will be represented at the sports complex.
* **Address** (Text, Required): Provide your business address for communication and logistical purposes.
* **Contact Number** (Text, Required): Enter your business contact number for efficient communication.
* **Start Date** (Date, Optional): If your permit is not immediate, please provide the start date for your permit.
* **End Date** (Date, Optional): If your permit has an end date, please provide it.
* **Status** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose the status of your permit (Active, Inactive, or Cancelled).
* **Insurance Status** (Select One, Optional): Indicate whether your business is insured or not.
* **Terms Acceptance** (Select Multiple, Optional): Confirm that you have accepted the terms and conditions of doing business at the sports complex.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields (Name, Address, Contact Number, and Status).
* Double-check your insurance status to ensure accuracy.
* Confirm your terms acceptance to avoid any disputes.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, don't hesitate to contact us.
