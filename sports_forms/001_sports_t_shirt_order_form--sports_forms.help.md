# Sports T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports T Shirt Order Form is a tool for teams or individuals to place orders for sports T-shirts. This guide will walk you through how to fill out the form to ensure your order is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully, entering all required information.
2. Review each field to ensure accuracy before submitting your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required: false): Enter your team's name, which will be used to identify your order.
* **Team Color** (`team_color`, text, required: false): Enter your team's color or preferred T-shirt color.
* **Player Size** (`player_size`, text, required: false): Specify the size of the T-shirt you need, which is usually based on your team's jersey size or your preferred fit.
* **Player Color** (`player_color`, text, required: false): Select the T-shirt color you prefer, which should match your team's or preferred color.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you'd like to place your order by.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, required: false): Select the time you'd like to place your order by.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Order Email** (`order_email`, email, required: false): Enter a contact email address for us to reply to regarding your order.
* **Order Phone** (`order_phone`, text, required: false): Enter a contact phone number for us to reach you regarding your order.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, text, required: false): Specify which team member or staff member this order is for.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required: false): Specify which equipment or T-shirt style is assigned for this order.
* **Team ID** (`team_id`, text, required: false): Enter your team ID, if applicable.

## Tips

* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to reach out to our support team for clarification.
* Ensure your email address and phone number are accurate for efficient communication about your order.
