# Sports Co Op Registration And Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants in a sports cooperation or team. It asks for personal details, emergency contacts, and activity participation, which suggests that it's used for registration and waiver purposes for events or programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the participant details, such as Participant Details 1, 2, 3, and 4.
* Indicate your participation in activities with the "Activities" field, where you can select multiple options.
* Add any additional information about your activities in the "Activity Details" field.
* Provide emergency contact information in the "Emergency Contacts" field.
* Check the "Signed Consent" field to indicate that you've read and agreed to the terms and conditions.
* Finally, review your answers and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (Participant Details 1, 2, 3, 4) (text, required: false)
This field is for entering personal details about yourself, such as name, email, and other relevant information.
* **Activities** (select_multiple, required: false)
Choose the activities you will be participating in, where you can select multiple options.
* **Activity Details** (text, required: false)
Add any additional information about your activities.
* **Emergency Contacts** (text, required: false)
List any emergency contacts that should be notified in case of an emergency.
* **Signed Consent** (note, required: false)
Check this field to indicate that you've read and agreed to the terms and conditions.
* (Optional) The last four fields are similar to "Participant Details" and seem to be duplicates; however, they might be required for specific purposes or might have slightly different requirements. Please use these fields as necessary, following the instructions given above.
