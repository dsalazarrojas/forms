# Cricket Tournament Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cricket Tournament Registration Form is a form designed to collect information from teams and players who wish to participate in a cricket tournament. The form is required to be completed by all teams and players to register for the tournament.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Provide your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Enter your contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.
4. Provide your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
5. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
6. Enter your team's name in the "Team Name" field.
7. Select the type of team you are representing (e.g. Cricket Club, Cricket Association, Other) in the "Team Type" field.
8. If you have a team logo, you can provide it in the "Team Logo" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Name** (1, text, required: false)
Enter your name as the team representative.

### **Date of Birth** (2, date, required: false)
Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### **Contact Email** (3, email, required: false)
Enter your contact email address so we can reach you regarding the tournament.

### **Contact Phone** (4, text, required: false)
Provide your contact phone number for communication purposes.

### **Address** (5, text, required: false)
Enter your address for logistical purposes.

### **Team Name** (6, text, required: false)
Enter your team's name.

### **Team Type** (7, select_one, required: false)
Select the type of team you are representing (e.g. Cricket Club, Cricket Association, Other).

### **Team Logo** (8, text, required: false)
If you have a team logo, you can provide it here.

## Tips
- Please ensure all fields are completed accurately and honestly.
- Make sure to check the "Team Type" field to select the correct team type.
- If you have any issues or questions, contact us for assistance.
