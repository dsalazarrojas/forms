# Fleece Show Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fleece Show Entry Form is designed to record and showcase information about a dog's characteristics and performance in a dog show competition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with accurate and relevant information about the dog.
2. Review the form carefully to ensure all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Show Details** (show_details, text, optional): Enter a brief description or notes about the dog's show performance.
* **Exhibitor** (exhibitor, text, optional): Enter the name of the person or organization showing the dog.
* **Email** (email, email, optional): Enter the email address of the exhibitor.
* **Phone** (phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the exhibitor.
* **Fleece Info** (fleece_info, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the dog's fleece.
* **Breed** (breed, text, optional): Enter the dog's breed.
* **Sex** (sex, select_one, optional): Select the dog's sex (Female, Male, or None).
* **Age** (age, number, optional): Enter the dog's age.
* **Color** (color, text, optional): Enter the dog's color.
* **Weight** (weight, number, optional): Enter the dog's weight.
* **Judge 1** (judge1, time, optional): Enter the time of the first judge's evaluation.
* **Judge 2** (judge2, time, optional): Enter the time of the second judge's evaluation.
* **Judge 1 Score** (judge1_score, select_one, optional): Select the score given by Judge 1 (1-10).
* **Judge 2 Score** (judge2_score, select_one, optional): Select the score given by Judge 2 (1-10).
* **Judge 1 Notes** (judge1_notes, text, optional): Enter any notes or comments from Judge 1.
* **Judge 2 Notes** (judge2_notes, text, optional): Enter any notes or comments from Judge 2.
* **Class Num** (class_num, text, optional): Enter the class number or category of the dog's performance.

Tips:
* Double-check the information entered in each field to ensure accuracy.
* Make sure to enter all required fields, if applicable.
* If you're unsure about any field, please consult with the form's administrator or developer.
