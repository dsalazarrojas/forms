# Youth Baseball Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Baseball Evaluation Form is designed to collect information from youth baseball players for evaluation purposes. It aims to capture their skills, experience, and background to provide a comprehensive assessment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review each section carefully and answer the questions truthfully.
2.  Fill out each page completely, as required.
3.  Ensure you have the necessary permissions or consents before disclosing sensitive information.
4.  Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Player Information (Page 1)
**Player Information** (`player_info`, text, optional)
This field is for players to provide their basic contact information, such as name, address, and contact details.

### Player Contact (Page 1)
**Player Contact** (`player_contact`, text, optional)
This field collects the player's contact information, including phone numbers, email addresses, and emergency contacts.

### Player Skills (Page 1)
**Player Skills** (`player_skills`, text, optional)
This field is for players to describe their baseball skills, such as batting, pitching, and fielding abilities.

### Player Experience (Page 1)
**Player Experience** (`player_experience`, text, optional)
This field is for players to detail their baseball experience, including years played, teams participated in, and notable achievements.

### Player Emotional Intelligence (Page 1)
**Player Emotional Intelligence** (`player_emotional_intelligence`, text, optional)
This field is for players to describe their emotional intelligence, including self-awareness, empathy, and social skills.

### Player Concentration (Page 1)
**Player Concentration** (`player_concentration`, text, optional)
This field is for players to indicate their concentration levels during games and practices.

### Player Physical Fitness (Page 1)
**Player Physical Fitness** (`player_physical_fitness`, text, optional)
This field is for players to describe their physical fitness level, including cardiovascular endurance and strength.

### Player Character (Page 1)
**Player Character** (`player_character`, text, optional)
This field is for players to showcase their character traits, such as sportsmanship, teamwork, and respect for rules.

### Player Family Background (Page 2)
**Player Family Background** (`player_family_background`, text, optional)
This field is for players to provide information about their family background, including parents, siblings, and any relevant family dynamics.

### Player Medical History (Page 2)
**Player Medical History** (`player_medical_history`, text, optional)
This field is for players to disclose any significant medical conditions or injuries that may impact their baseball performance.

### Player Mental Health (Page 2)
**Player Mental Health** (`player_mental_health`, text, optional)
This field is for players to discuss any mental health concerns or issues that may affect their baseball participation.

### Player Sports Involvement (Page 2)
**Player Sports Involvement** (`player_sports_involvement`, text, optional)
This field is for players to detail their involvement in other sports or physical activities.

### Player Coach Observations (Page 2)
**Player Coach Observations** (`player_coach_observations`, text, optional)
This field is for coaches or evaluators to provide observations and feedback about the player's abilities and performance.

### Player Tournaments (Page 2)
**Player Tournaments** (`player_tournaments`, text, optional)
This field is for players to record their tournament participation and performance.

### Player References (Page 3)
**Player References** (`player_references`, text, optional)
This field is for players to provide references from coaches, teachers, or other relevant individuals.

### Player Notes (Page 3)
**Player Notes** (`player_notes`, text, optional)
This field is for players to add any additional comments or information that may be relevant to their evaluation.

### Player Signature (Page 3)
**Player Signature** (`player_signature`, text, optional)
This field is for players to sign and date the form, indicating their consent and agreement to the terms.

### Player Consent (Page 3)
**Player Consent** (`player_consent`, text, optional)
This field is for players to confirm their consent to the evaluation process and terms.

### Admin Info (Page 4)
**Admin Info** (`admin_info`, text, optional)
This field is for administrators to record any additional information or notes about the player's progress.

### Admin Comment (Page 4)
**Admin Comment** (`admin_comment`, text, optional)
This field is for administrators to provide feedback or comments about the player's performance or progress.

Please note that some fields may have specific guidance or requirements that are not explicitly mentioned here. If you are unsure about any field or have questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to the relevant authorities or administrators.
