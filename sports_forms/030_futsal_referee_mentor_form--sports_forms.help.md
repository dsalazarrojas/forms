# Futsal Referee Mentor Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help the organization assess potential Futsal Referee Mentors' qualifications and experience. It will be reviewed to determine whether you are a good fit for the mentorship program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your first name, last name, and contact information (email, phone number, and date of birth).
2. Select your experience level in Futsal (Rookie, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert).
3. Choose your sport of preference (Soccer, Futsal, Basketball, Football, or Volleyball).
4. Fill in your additional contact information (email, phone number, date of birth, time, experience level, and sport) for up to four mentors.
5. Provide a note for each of your additional mentors if you wish.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your work or personal email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date of birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Time**: Enter your time preference (not used in this form).
* **Sport**: Select your preferred sport (Soccer, Futsal, Basketball, Football, or Volleyball).
* **Experience level**: Select your experience level in Futsal (Rookie, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert).
* **Note**: Enter a note for this application (not used in this form).
* **Email 2**: Enter your email address for your second mentor.
* **Phone 2**: Enter your phone number for your second mentor.
* **Date of birth 2**: Enter your date of birth for your second mentor.
* **Time 2**: Enter your time preference for your second mentor.
* **Sport 2**: Select your preferred sport for your second mentor (Soccer, Futsal, Basketball, Football, or Volleyball).
* **Experience level 2**: Select your experience level for your second mentor (Rookie, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert).
* **Note 2**: Enter a note for your second application (not used in this form).
* **Email 3**: Enter your email address for your third mentor.
* **Phone 3**: Enter your phone number for your third mentor.
* **Date of birth 3**: Enter your date of birth for your third mentor.
* **Time 3**: Enter your time preference for your third mentor.
* **Sport 3**: Select your preferred sport for your third mentor (Soccer, Futsal, Basketball, Football, or Volleyball).
* **Experience level 3**: Select your experience level for your third mentor (Rookie, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert).
* **Note 3**: Enter a note for your third application (not used in this form).
* **Email 4**: Enter your email address for your fourth mentor.
* **Phone 4**: Enter your phone number for your fourth mentor.
* **Date of birth 4**: Enter your date of birth for your fourth mentor.
* **Time 4**: Enter your time preference for your fourth mentor.
* **Sport 4**: Select your preferred sport for your fourth mentor (Soccer, Futsal, Basketball, Football, or Volleyball).
* **Experience level 4**: Select your experience level for your fourth mentor (Rookie, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert).
* **Note 4**: Enter a note for your fourth application (not used in this form).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all fields accurately and thoroughly.
* You can select multiple experience levels for your additional mentors if desired.
* You can enter any additional information for each of your mentors if you wish.
* If you have any issues with this form, please contact us.
