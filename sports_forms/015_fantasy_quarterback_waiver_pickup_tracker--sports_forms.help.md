# Fantasy Quarterback Waiver Pickup Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fantasy Quarterback Waiver Pickup Tracker is a form used to process fantasy football waiver requests. It helps to streamline and track waiver requests, ensuring that they are processed efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of waiver request from the options provided.
2. Enter the player's ID, team name, and other relevant information in the designated fields.
3. Set the priority level of the request, reflecting its urgency.
4. Add any additional notes to provide context and details about the request.
5. Submit the form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Football Quarterback Waiver Request Form** (`page 1`): Select the type of waiver request you are submitting.
* **Player ID** (`id: 2`): Enter the ID of the player you are requesting.
* **Team Name** (`id: 3`): Enter the name of the team requesting the waiver.
* **Waiver Type** (`id: 4`): Select the type of waiver request (Trade or Drop).
* **Waiver Reason** (`id: 5`): Provide a brief explanation for the waiver request.
* **Request Date** (`id: 6`): Enter the date of the waiver request.
* **Waiver Date** (`id: 7`): Enter the date the waiver was granted or denied.
* **Priority** (`id: 8`): Set the priority level of the request (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Notes** (`id: 9`): Add any additional comments or details about the request.
* **Player Name** (`id: 10`): Enter the name of the player involved in the waiver request.
* **Team Name** (`id: 11`): Enter the name of the team involved in the waiver request.
* **Waiver Status** (`id: 12`): Select the current status of the waiver request (Claimed or Not Claimed).
* **Waiver Owner** (`id: 13`): Enter the name of the player who submitted the waiver request.
* **Waiver Owner Position** (`id: 14`): Enter the position of the player who submitted the waiver request.
* **Waiver Owner Salary** (`id: 15`): Enter the salary of the player who submitted the waiver request.
* **Owner Name** (`id: 16`): Enter the name of the owner who submitted the waiver request.
* **Owner Team** (`id: 17`): Enter the name of the team of the owner who submitted the waiver request.
* **Owner Salary** (`id: 18`): Enter the salary of the owner who submitted the waiver request.
* **Waiver Owner Salary Earned** (`id: 19`): Enter the total salary earned by the waiver owner.
* **Player Salary** (`id: 20`): Enter the salary of the player involved in the waiver request.
* **Team Salary** (`id: 21`): Enter the total salary of the team involved in the waiver request.
* **Owner Salary Cap** (`id: 22`): Enter the salary cap of the team involved in the waiver request.
* **Waiver Cap** (`id: 23`): Enter the waiver cap of the team involved in the waiver request.
* **Waiver Cap Remaining** (`id: 24`): Enter the remaining waiver cap of the team involved in the waiver request.
* **Waiver Cap Used** (`id: 25`): Enter the total waiver cap used by the team involved in the waiver request.
