# fitness_performance_training_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The fitness_performance_training_evaluation_form is designed to track and evaluate an athlete's or client's physical performance during a fitness training session. This form collects data on the client's physical state, workout details, and trainer notes to provide a comprehensive understanding of their progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief description of the training session or client information.
2. Provide your client ID or name.
3. Enter the date of the training session.
4. Choose the type of workout that was performed (e.g., cardio, strength training, etc.).
5. Record your client's weight.
6. Record your client's height.
7. Enter your client's body fat percentage.
8. Measure and record your client's resting and maximum heart rates.
9. Enter your client's blood pressure.
10. Add any relevant notes about the workout (optional).
11. Record any notes from the trainer (optional).
12. Enter any goals or objectives for the client (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form description** (1, text, optional): A brief description of the training session or client information.
* **Client ID** (2, text, required): Enter your client's ID or name.
* **Training date** (3, date, required): The date of the training session.
* **Workout type** (4, select_one, required): Choose the type of workout that was performed (e.g., cardio, strength training, etc.).
* **Weight** (5, number, required): Record your client's weight.
* **Height** (6, number, required): Record your client's height.
* **Body fat percentage** (7, number, required): Record your client's body fat percentage.
* **Resting heart rate** (8, number, required): Measure and record your client's resting heart rate.
* **Max heart rate** (9, number, required): Measure and record your client's maximum heart rate.
* **Blood pressure** (10, number, required): Record your client's blood pressure.
* **Workout notes** (11, text, optional): Add any relevant notes about the workout.
* **Trainer notes** (12, text, optional): Record any notes from the trainer.
* **Client goals** (13, text, optional): Enter any goals or objectives for the client.

Note: This guide is for the fitness performance training evaluation form.
