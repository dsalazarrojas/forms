# Youth Sports Video Story Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain informed consent from parents or legal guardians for recording video stories of their child participating in sports events. The purpose of this form is to ensure that parents have been informed and agree to their child's video recording.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please read each field carefully and provide the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consent to Record Video** (Consent to Record Video, required): Please indicate your consent for us to record video of your child participating in this sports event.
* **Share Video** (Share Video, required): Select how you would like us to share the recorded video with others, such as Parent's Email, Coach/Assistant Coach, or Organization.
* **Consent to Share Video Details** (Consent to Share Video Details, required): Please select how much information you want to be shared with others about your child's video recording, such as Parent's Email, Coach/Assistant Coach, or Organization.
* **Consent to Share with Analytics** (Share with Analytics, required): Please select whether you agree to share the video recording with analytics, which helps us to better understand how to improve our services.
* **Consent to Share on Social Media** (Share on Social Media, optional): If you agree, please select which social media platforms you would like us to share the video recording on, such as Facebook, Instagram, or Twitter.
* **Video Storage** (Video Storage, required): Please indicate where you would like the video recording to be stored.
* **Contact Preference** (Contact Preference, required): Please select how you would like to be contacted about the recorded video, such as Email or Phone.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (Emergency Contact Info, required): Please provide the contact information of an emergency contact person that we should notify in case of an accident or injury.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Emergency Contact Phone, required): Please provide the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (Emergency Contact Email, required): Please provide the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (Emergency Contact Phone Number, optional): Please provide any additional phone number for the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Extension** (Emergency Contact Phone Extension, optional): Please provide any phone extension for the emergency contact person.
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, optional): Please provide any additional comments or notes about the video recording.
* **Signature** (Signature, required): Please sign this form to confirm your consent.
* **Recorded Video ID** (Recorded Video ID, required): This is a unique identifier for the recorded video.
* **Recorded Video URL** (Recorded Video URL, required): Please provide the URL of the recorded video.
* **Record Video** (Record Video, required): Please indicate whether you agree to record video of your child participating in this sports event.
* **Record Audio** (Record Audio, required): Please indicate whether you agree to record audio of your child participating in this sports event.
* **Record Image** (Record Image, required): Please indicate whether you agree to record image of your child participating in this sports event.
* **Audio Description** (Audio Description, required): Please provide a description of the audio recording.
* **Video Description** (Video Description, required): Please provide a description of the video recording.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (Emergency Contact Info, required): Please provide the contact information of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Emergency Contact Phone, required): Please provide the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (Emergency Contact Email, required): Please provide the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (Emergency Contact Phone Number, optional): Please provide any additional phone number for the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Extension** (Emergency Contact Phone Extension, optional): Please provide any phone extension for the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Ext 2** (Emergency Contact Phone Ext 2, optional): Please provide any additional phone extension for the emergency contact person.
* **Record Video File** (Record Video File, required): This field is for internal use only.
* **Record Audio** (Record Audio, required): Please indicate whether you agree to record audio of your child participating in this sports event.
* **Record Image** (Record Image, required): Please indicate whether you agree to record image of your child participating in this sports event.
