# tel-academy-training-log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and track details of a specific training or workout session.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the date and time of the workout.
2. Enter the athlete's name, email, and phone number.
3. Enter the coach's name, email, and phone number.
4. Enter the emergency contact's email and phone number.
5. Select the type of exercise performed and the exercise name.
6. Enter the number of sets and reps performed.
7. Enter the weight and distance covered.
8. Indicate whether wellness monitoring was performed (yes/no).
9. Select the athlete's fatigue level (high/low).
10. Add any notes about the workout.
11. Indicate whether the athlete's wellness score is yes/no.
12. Enter the training date and time (separate fields).
13. Indicate the number of rest days since the last workout.
14. Select whether the workout frequency is yes/no.
15. Enter the athlete's email and phone number (if not already filled in).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **training log**: A text area for notes about the workout.
* **date**: The date of the workout.
* **time**: The time of the workout.
* **athlete name**: The name of the athlete participating in the workout.
* **athlete email**: The email of the athlete participating in the workout.
* **athlete phone**: The phone number of the athlete participating in the workout.
* **coach name**: The name of the coach or trainer.
* **coach email**: The email of the coach or trainer.
* **coach phone**: The phone number of the coach or trainer.
* **emergency contact**: The name of the emergency contact person.
* **emergency email**: The email of the emergency contact person.
* **emergency phone**: The phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **training type**: The type of exercise performed during the workout.
* **exercise name**: The name of the specific exercise performed during the workout.
* **sets**: The number of sets performed during the workout.
* **reps**: The number of reps performed during the workout.
* **weight**: The weight lifted or used during the workout.
* **distance**: The distance covered during the workout.
* **wellness monitoring**: Indicates whether wellness monitoring was performed (yes/no).
* **fatigue level**: Selects the athlete's fatigue level (high/low).
* **notes**: A text area for notes about the workout.
* **wellness score**: Indicates whether the athlete's wellness score is yes/no.
* **training date**: The date of the training or workout.
* **training time**: The time of the training or workout.
* **rest days since last workout**: The number of rest days since the last workout.
* **workout frequency**: Indicates whether the workout frequency is yes/no.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Enter accurate and relevant information for each field.
* Review and edit the form before submitting it.
* Use the form to track and analyze progress over time.
