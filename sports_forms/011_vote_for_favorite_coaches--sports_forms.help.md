# Vote For Favorite Coaches - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your favorite coaches in a sports-related context. You can select up to three coaches from the provided options and provide additional feedback for each of them.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your three favorite coaches from the provided options by choosing one coach per "Favorite Coach" page.
2. For each selected coach, provide additional feedback or comments in the corresponding "Coach <number> Feedback" field.
3. If you want to provide more information about a coach, you can do so in the "Coach <number> Bio" field.
4. Enter a valid email address in the "Email" field, so we can get back to you with any updates or news.
5. If you have any additional comments or feedback for this form, you can enter them in the "Additional Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Favorite Coach** (`favorite_coach`, select_one, required): Select up to three coaches from the provided options.
* **Coach 1 Info** (`coach_1_info`, text, required): Enter any additional information you have about Coach 1.
* **Coach 2 Info** (`coach_2_info`, text, required): Enter any additional information you have about Coach 2.
* **Coach 3 Info** (`coach_3_info`, text, required): Enter any additional information you have about Coach 3.
* **Vote For Coach** (`vote_for_coach`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to vote for your favorite coach.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address so we can get back to you.
* **Additional Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback about this form.
* **Coach 1 Feedback** (`coach_1_feedback`, note, optional): Enter any feedback or comments about Coach 1.
* **Coach 2 Feedback** (`coach_2_feedback`, note, optional): Enter any feedback or comments about Coach 2.
* **Coach 3 Feedback** (`coach_3_feedback`, note, optional): Enter any feedback or comments about Coach 3.
* **Coach 1 Name** (`coach_1_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of Coach 1.
* **Coach 1 Team** (`coach_1_team`, text, optional): Enter the team for Coach 1.
* **Coach 1 Bio** (`coach_1_bio`, text, optional): Enter a brief biography of Coach 1.
* **Coach 2 Name** (`coach_2_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of Coach 2.
* **Coach 2 Team** (`coach_2_team`, text, optional): Enter the team for Coach 2.
* **Coach 2 Bio** (`coach_2_bio`, text, optional): Enter a brief biography of Coach 2.
* **Coach 3 Name** (`coach_3_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of Coach 3.
* **Coach 3 Team** (`coach_3_team`, text, optional): Enter the team for Coach 3.
* **Coach 3 Bio** (`coach_3_bio`, text, optional): Enter a brief biography of Coach 3.
