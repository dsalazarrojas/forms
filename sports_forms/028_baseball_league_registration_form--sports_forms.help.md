# Baseball League Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for registering a player in a baseball league. It collects player information, medical details, and contact information for emergency purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the player's personal information, including their first name, last name, date of birth, and contact information.
2. Select the player's gender and division or age group.
3. Provide the player's school information, if applicable, and grade level.
4. Indicate the player's previous baseball experience.
5. Choose the player's position(s) of interest.
6. Specify the player's handedness (right, left, or both).
7. Disclose any medical conditions or allergies.
8. List any current medications.
9. Enter the player's medical insurance provider.
10. Provide emergency contact information, including name, phone number, and relationship.
11. Indicate if the player has any transportation needs.
12. Express interest in volunteering for the league.
13. Choose a jersey size for the player.
14. Confirm payment method for the registration fee.
15. Indicate how the player heard about the league.
16. Confirm understanding of the liability waiver and code of conduct agreements.
17. Sign and date the form to complete the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player First Name** (Text, Required): Enter the player's first name.
* **Player Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter the player's last name.
* **Player Date of Birth** (Date, Required): Enter the player's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Player Gender** (Select One, Required): Select the player's gender from the options provided.
* **Parent or Guardian Full Name** (Text, Required): Enter the player's parent or guardian's full name.
* **Parent or Guardian Email** (Email, Required): Enter the parent or guardian's email address.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone** (Text, Required): Enter the parent or guardian's phone number.
* **Home Address** (Text, Required): Enter the player's home address.
* **City** (Text, Required): Enter the city of the player's residence.
* **State** (Text, Required): Enter the state of the player's residence.
* **ZIP Code** (Text, Required): Enter the player's ZIP code.
* **Division or Age Group** (Select One, Required): Select the division or age group the player will be joining.
* **School Name** (Text, Optional): If applicable, enter the player's current school name.
* **Grade Level** (Select One, Required): Select the player's current grade level.
* **Previous Baseball Experience** (Select One, Required): Indicate the number of years the player has played baseball.
* **Positions of Interest** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose the player's preferred baseball positions.
* **Bats** (Select One, Required): Specify the player's handedness for batting.
* **Throws** (Select One, Required): Specify the player's handedness for throwing.
* **Medical Conditions or Allergies** (Text, Optional): Disclose any medical conditions or allergies the player has.
* **Current Medications** (Text, Optional): List any current medications the player is taking.
* **Medical Insurance Provider** (Text, Required): Enter the player's medical insurance provider.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Text, Required): Enter the full name of the player's emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Text, Required): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (Text, Required): Indicate the relationship between the player and the emergency contact.
* **Transportation Needs** (Select One, Required): Indicate if the player requires transportation assistance.
* **Volunteer Interest** (Select One, Required): Express interest in volunteering for the league.
* **Uniform Jersey Size** (Select One, Required): Choose the player's jersey size.
* **Registration Fee Payment** (Select One, Required): Confirm payment method for the registration fee.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (Select One, Required): Indicate how the player heard about the league.
* **Liability Waiver Agreement** (Select One, Required): Confirm understanding of the liability waiver agreement.
* **Code of Conduct Agreement** (Select One, Required): Confirm understanding of the code of conduct agreement.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature** (Text, Required): Sign the form with the player's parent or guardian's full name.
* **Signature Date** (Date, Required): Date the form with the signature.

Note: This form is for registration purposes only and is required for participation in the baseball league. Please ensure all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly to avoid any issues with registration.
