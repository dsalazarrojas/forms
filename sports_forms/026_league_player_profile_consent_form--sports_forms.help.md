# League Player Profile Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The League Player Profile Consent Form is used to collect information from the player and their emergency contacts, including their consent for emergency contacts and medical conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
4. If you have an emergency contact, enter their first name in the "Emergency Contact First Name" field and their last name in the "Emergency Contact Last Name" field.
5. Enter your emergency contact's phone number in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field.
6. Specify your relationship with your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Relationship" field.
7. If you have any medical conditions, enter them in the "Medical Conditions" field.
8. List any allergies in the "Allergies" field.
9. List any medications in the "Medications" field.
10. If you have any additional player information, enter it in the "Player Information" field.
11. Check if you consent to being saved in the "Player Consent" field.
12. Check if you consent to emergency contacts in the "Emergency Contact Consent" field.
13. If you have an emergency contact, enter their name in the "Emergency Contact Name" field.
14. Enter your emergency contact's email address in the "Parent/Guarantor Email" field.
15. Enter your player's date and time in the "Player Date" and "Player Time" fields.
16. Enter your emergency contact's alternate phone number in the "Emergency Contact Alternate Phone" field.
17. Enter your emergency contact's address in the "Emergency Contact Address" field.
18. Specify your relationship with your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Relationship" field.
19. Enter any additional notes about your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Note" field.
20. Choose whether or not to save your player profile in the "Form Submission" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your given name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your surname.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (`emergency_contact_first_name`, text, required): Enter your emergency contact's first name.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (`emergency_contact_last_name`, text, required): Enter your emergency contact's last name.
* **Parent/Guarantor First Name** (`parent_guarantor_first_name`, text, required): Enter your emergency contact's first name.
* **Parent/Guarantor Last Name** (`parent_guarantor_last_name`, text, required): Enter your emergency contact's last name.
* **Parent/Guarantor Phone** (`parent_guarantor_phone`, text, required): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Player Consent** (`player_consent`, select_one, required): Check if you consent to being saved.
* **Emergency Contact Consent** (`emergency_consent`, select_multiple, required): Check if you consent to emergency contacts.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required): Specify your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, required): Enter any medical conditions you have.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, required): Enter any allergies you have.
* **Medications** (`medications`, text, required): Enter any medications you are taking.
* **Player Information** (`player_information`, text, required): Enter any additional player information.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter your emergency contact's name.
* **Parent/Guarantor Email** (`parent_guarantor_email`, email, required): Enter your emergency contact's email address.
* **Player Date** (`player_date`, date, required): Enter your player's date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Player Time** (`player_time`, time, required): Enter your player's time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Alternate Phone** (`emergency_contact_alternate_phone`, text, required): Enter your emergency contact's alternate phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, required): Enter your emergency contact's address.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required): Specify your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Note** (`emergency_contact_note`, note, required): Enter any additional notes about your emergency contact.
* **Player Profile** (`form_submission`, select_one, required): Choose whether or not to save your player profile.

Note that this form is designed to collect information that is sensitive in nature, and it is essential to fill it out carefully and accurately.
