# Tournament Spectator Facilities Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about the spectator facilities available during a tournament, ensuring that event organizers and organizers can better understand what amenities are available for spectators, which can help improve the overall spectator experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the following questions about the spectator facilities available during the tournament.
2. Please fill in the required information as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
3. You can skip any fields that do not apply to the facilities provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Please enter your first name to help us identify you.
* **Last name**: Please enter your last name to help us identify you.
* **Email**: Please enter your email address to contact you with any further questions or updates.
* **Phone**: Please enter your contact phone number to contact you with any further questions or updates.
* **Venue**: Please enter the name of the venue where the tournament is taking place.
* **Facilities**: Please select whether the following facilities are available: 
  - True: Yes, they are available.
  - False: No, they are not available.
* **Services**: Please select which of the following services are available:
  - Food and drinks: Yes, they are available.
  - Merchandise: Yes, they are available.
  - Parking: Yes, they are available.
  - Security: Yes, they are available.
*   **Comments**: Please provide any additional comments about the facilities or services available.
* **Date and time**: Please select a date and time from the calendar to help us understand when the facilities are available.
* **Time**: Please select a time of day from the available options.
* **Additional Comments**: This is a space for any further comments you may have about the facilities or services provided.

## Tips

* Please be as accurate as possible when filling out the form.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact us.
* We will use the information provided to better understand and improve the spectator experience.
