# Sports Participation Grade Reporting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Participation Grade Reporting Form is designed to collect data on student participation in sports activities. This form helps track and evaluate the effectiveness of sports programs, providing valuable insights for coaches, administrators, and students.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the relevant sport.
2. Enter the grade level of the student.
3. Indicate whether the student participated in the sport (yes/no).
4. Provide the student ID number.
5. Enter the student's name.
6. Provide the coach ID number.
7. Enter the coach's name.
8. Provide the school ID number.
9. Enter the school name.
10. Enter the year the student participated.
11. Select the semester the student participated (yes/no).
12. Enter the sport ID number.
13. Enter the date the student participated in the sport.
14. Enter the time the student participated in the sport.
15. Provide the location where the student participated in the sport.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **sports (Page 1)**: Enter the name of the sport the student participated in.
* **grade (Page 1)**: Enter the grade level of the student (e.g., 9, 10, 11, 12).
* **participation (Page 1)**: Indicate whether the student participated in the sport (yes/no).
* **sport participation (Page 1)**: Select all sports the student participated in.
* **student id (Page 2)**: Enter the student ID number.
* **student name (Page 2)**: Enter the student's name.
* **coach id (Page 2)**: Enter the coach ID number.
* **coach name (Page 2)**: Enter the coach's name.
* **school id (Page 3)**: Enter the school ID number.
* **school name (Page 3)**: Enter the school name.
* **year (Page 3)**: Enter the year the student participated.
* **semester (Page 3)**: Select the semester the student participated (yes/no).
* **sport id (Page 4)**: Enter the sport ID number.
* **date of participation (Page 4)**: Enter the date the student participated in the sport.
* **time of participation (Page 4)**: Enter the time the student participated in the sport.
* **location of participation (Page 4)**: Provide the location where the student participated in the sport.
