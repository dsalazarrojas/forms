The purpose of this form is to gather information from snowmobile tour participants, including their contact details, medical conditions, and emergency contact information. This form ensures that tour operators can effectively manage participant safety and provide necessary care during the tour. 

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name as the snowmobile tour operator.
2. Enter your participant's name.
3. Enter your participant's phone number.
4. Enter your emergency contact's name.
5. Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
6. Provide any relevant medical details about your participant (e.g., allergies, medical conditions).
7. Enter your participant's email address.
8. Accept or decline the waiver agreement.
9. Specify your relationship with the emergency contact (e.g., parent, sibling, spouse, other).
10. Enter the emergency contact's address.

This form is used for the snowmobile tour check-in process. It is crucial that you fill it out carefully and accurately to ensure your safety and the safety of the tour participants.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **snowmobile tour operator** (`tour_operator`, text, required): Enter your name as the tour operator responsible for this snowmobile tour.
* **participant name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person participating in the snowmobile tour.
* **participant phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the tour participant.
* **emergency contact name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **emergency contact phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **medical details** (`medical_details`, text, optional): Provide any relevant medical information about the tour participant, such as allergies or medical conditions.
* **participant email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the tour participant.
* **waiver acceptance** (`waiver_acceptance`, select_one, required): Accept or decline the waiver agreement for the tour participant.
* **relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_one, required): Specify the relationship between you as the emergency contact and the tour participant (e.g., parent, sibling, spouse, other).
* **emergency contact address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the emergency contact.
