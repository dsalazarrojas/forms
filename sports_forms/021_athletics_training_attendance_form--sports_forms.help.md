# Athletics Training Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Athletics Training Attendance Form is a tool for recording and reporting on athletics training sessions. It helps coaches and trainers gather data on athlete performance, provide feedback, and track progress over time.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the training session details: date, start time, end time, location, and type.
2. Enter the athlete's name and confirm their attendance status.
3. Rate the athlete's participation level, effort, focus, technique, physical condition, and attitude.
4. Report any injuries or incidents that occurred during the session.
5. Leave additional comments or notes for the coach or trainer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Date** (`training_date`): Enter the date of the training session in the format `MM/DD/YY`.
* **Session Start Time** (`training_time_start`): Enter the start time of the training session in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **Session End Time** (`training_time_end`): Enter the end time of the training session in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **Training Type** (`training_type`): Select the type of training session, such as Strength Training, Conditioning, Skill Development, Team Practice, Individual Training, Recovery, or Other.
* **Location** (`location`): Enter the location where the training session was held.
* **Coach/Instructor Name** (`coach_name`): Enter the name of the person conducting the training session.
* **Athlete Name** (`athlete_name`): Enter the name of the athlete participating in the training session.
* **Attendance Status** (`attendance_status`): Select the athlete's attendance status, such as Present, Absent, Late, or Excused Absence.
* **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`): Enter the time the athlete arrived for the training session.
* **Participation Level** (`participation_level`): Rate the athlete's participation level, such as Full Participation, Partial Participation, Limited Participation, or Observer Only.
* **Effort Rating** (`effort_rating`): Rate the athlete's effort during the session, such as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Focus and Concentration** (`focus_concentration`): Rate the athlete's focus and concentration during the session, such as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Technique Quality** (`technique_quality`): Rate the quality of the athlete's technique during the session, such as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Needs Improvement.
* **Physical Condition** (`physical_condition`): Rate the athlete's physical condition after the session, such as Excellent, Good, Fair, Fatigued, or Injured.
* **Attitude and Behavior** (`attitude_behavior`): Rate the athlete's attitude and behavior during the session, such as Positive, Cooperative, Neutral, Negative, or Disruptive.
* **Injury/Incident Report** (`injury_incident`): Report any injuries or incidents that occurred during the session.
* **Any Injuries or Incidents?** (`any_injury_incidents`): Select yes or no to indicate if there were any injuries or incidents.
* **Injury/Incident Description** (`injury_description`): If yes, describe what happened.
* **Medical Attention Needed?** (`medical_attention_needed`): Select yes or no to indicate if medical attention is needed.
* **Coach Comments** (`coach_comments`): Enter any additional observations or notes from the coach.
* **Follow-up Actions Needed** (`follow_up_actions`): Enter any follow-up actions required for the athlete.
* **Additional Comments** (`comments_notes`): Enter any additional comments or notes for the coach or trainer.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
* Double-check the athlete's name and attendance status.
* Rate the athlete's performance honestly and accurately.
* Report any injuries or incidents promptly and thoroughly.
