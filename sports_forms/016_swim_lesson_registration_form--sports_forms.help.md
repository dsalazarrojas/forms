# Swim Lesson Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Swim Lesson Registration Form is designed for users to register for swim lessons. It collects various information from the user, including name, contact information, swim lesson details, and additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the User Input section with your name and any other details you would like to provide.
2. Enter your email address and phone number in the respective fields.
3. Provide your address and the address of your instructor in the respective fields.
4. Enter your student's age, the number of lessons per week, and the duration of each lesson.
5. Select your student's swim level, lesson frequency, and the instructor's level.
6. Choose any assigned tools for the lesson.
7. Add any additional notes you would like to include.
