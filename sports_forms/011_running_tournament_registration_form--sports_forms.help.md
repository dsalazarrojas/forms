# running_tournament_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The running tournament registration form is designed to gather information from participants for a successful event. This includes getting basic details about the participant and event specifics to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for all.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the participant info field with your basic details.
2. Provide information about the event in the event info field.
3. If necessary, add additional event specifics in the registration details field.
4. Add any extra information about yourself in the participant info 2 field.
5. Confirm that the participant info you provided is correct by selecting 'Yes' in the confirm participant info field.
6. Confirm that the event info you provided is correct by selecting 'Yes' in the confirm event info field.
7. Confirm that the registration details you provided are correct by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' in the confirm registration details field.
8. Finally, click the submit button to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Info**: This field is required to get basic information about the participant. Please fill in your name, age, and any other relevant details.
- **Event Info**: This field is required to get information about the event. Please provide details about the event you are registering for, such as the date, time, and location.
- **Registration Details**: This field is optional, but provides additional event specifics that are useful for the organizers. If you have any special requests or requirements, please add them here.
- **Additional Info**: This field is optional and provides extra space for you to add any additional information about yourself.
- **Confirm Participant Info**: This field is a multiple-choice field to confirm that the participant info you provided is correct. Please select 'Yes' if the information is accurate.
- **Confirm Event Info**: This field is a multiple-choice field to confirm that the event info you provided is correct. Please select 'Yes' if the information is accurate.
- **Confirm Registration Details**: This field is a multiple-choice field to confirm that the registration details you provided are correct. Please select 'Yes' or 'No' based on your response.
- **Submit**: This field is required to submit your form. Please select 'Submit' to complete the registration process.
