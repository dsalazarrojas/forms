# soccer_shots_park_program_evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The soccer_shots_park_program_evaluation form is designed to gather feedback and evaluation from coaches and parents who have participated in the Shots Park Program. This information will help us understand their experiences and improve the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have a valid evaluation ID. If you do not have one, please contact our support team.
2. Select the "Coach Evaluation" page and fill in your evaluation.
3. Move on to the "Coach Information" page and provide your name, phone number, and email.
4. On the "Parent Evaluation" page, provide your evaluation and move on to the "Parent Information" page.
5. Fill in your name, phone number, and email.
6. Finally, click "Submit" to send your evaluation and feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Coach Evaluation** (Coach Evaluation, note, required): This section is where you can provide your overall evaluation of the Shots Park Program. Please provide any feedback, suggestions, or concerns you may have.
* **Coach Name** (Coach Name, text, optional): If you want to identify yourself as a coach, please fill in your name here.
* **Coach Phone** (Coach Phone, text, optional): If you want to provide your phone number for contact, please fill in the required format (###-###-####).
* **Coach Email** (Coach Email, email, optional): If you want to provide your email address for contact, please fill in the correct format (####@####.####).
* **Evaluation Comments** (Evaluation Comments, text, optional): This is the space to provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the program.
* **Parent Evaluation** (Parent Evaluation, note, optional): If you are a parent, please provide your evaluation of the Shots Park Program here.
* **Parent Name** (Parent Name, text, optional): If you are a parent, please fill in your name here.
* **Parent Phone** (Parent Phone, text, optional): If you are a parent, please fill in your phone number here (###-###-####).
* **Parent Email** (Parent Email, email, optional): If you are a parent, please fill in your email address here (####@####.####).

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when providing your evaluation and feedback.
* If you are a coach, please focus on providing feedback about the program's coaching aspects.
* If you are a parent, please focus on providing feedback about the program's parent-coach interaction and overall experience.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please contact our support team.
