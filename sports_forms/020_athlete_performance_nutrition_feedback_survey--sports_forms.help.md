# Athlete Performance Nutrition Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your athletic performance and nutrition habits. By completing this form, you will be able to provide detailed information about your current diet, hydration habits, and preferences. This data will help sports teams and nutritionists provide tailored guidance and support for optimal performance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all required questions to the best of your ability.
2. Select the correct options for each question.
3. Use the "Additional Feedback" field for any further comments or questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Full Name**: Enter your first and last name.
* **Athlete ID Number**: Enter your student or team identification number. (Optional)
* **Sport**: Select your primary sport (e.g., Football, Basketball, Soccer, etc.).
* **Team Level**: Select your current competition level (e.g., Varsity, Junior Varsity, Freshman, etc.).
* **Survey Date**: Enter the date of the survey.
* **Current Nutrition Plan Status**: Select whether you are following a formal nutrition plan from a nutritionist or not.
* **Meals Per Day**: Enter your average number of meals per day.
* **Snack Frequency**: Select how often you snack (e.g., 1-2 times daily, 3-4 times daily, etc.).
* **Daily Water Intake**: Enter your average glasses of water per day.
* **Pre-Workout Nutrition**: Select whether you usually eat before training (e.g., Always, Usually, Sometimes, etc.).
* **Post-Workout Nutrition**: Select whether you usually eat after training (e.g., Always, Usually, Sometimes, etc.).
* **Overall Nutrition Satisfaction**: Select how satisfied you are with your current nutrition (e.g., Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, etc.).
* **Energy Levels During Training**: Select your typical energy levels during training (e.g., Excellent, Good, Average, etc.).
* **Recovery Quality**: Select how well you recover between training sessions (e.g., Excellent, Good, Average, etc.).
* **Nutrition Challenges**: Select any challenges you face with nutrition (e.g., Lack of Time, Lack of Knowledge, etc.).
* **Supplement Use**: Select whether you use any supplements.
* **Supplements Taken**: If you use supplements, list them here.
* **Nutritionist Consultation**: Select whether you have worked with a sports nutritionist.
* **Nutrition Education Topics of Interest**: Select topics you would like to learn more about (e.g., Meal Planning, Pre-Competition Nutrition, etc.).
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies**: If you have any dietary restrictions or allergies, list them here.
* **Nutrition Goals**: Enter your current nutrition goals.
* **Nutrition Support Needed**: Select what type of support would help you (e.g., Individual Sessions, Group Workshops, etc.).
* **Additional Feedback**: Enter any additional comments or questions you have.

Please note that all fields marked as "required" are mandatory to complete the form. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your team or coach for assistance.
