# Fitness Club Re Opening Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Club Re Opening Survey is a form used to gather information from club owners to re-open a fitness club after a period of closure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the category of your fitness club.
2. Provide a description of your club.
3. Decide whether you want to embed your club's URL on our website or not.
4. Choose whether to send email updates to us about your club's re-opening.
5. Decide whether to embed your club's URL on our website or not.
6. Choose the option for sending emails about your club.
7. Confirm whether to embed your club's website on our website or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required: false): Select the category of your fitness club.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required: false): Provide a clear and concise description of your fitness club.
* **Embedded URL** (`embedded_url`, select_multiple, required: false): Decide whether to embed your club's URL on our website or not. Select 'Yes' to embed your URL on our website.
* **Email Addresses** (`email_addresses`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose whether to send email updates to us about your club's re-opening. Select 'Yes' to send email updates.
* **Website URL** (`website_url`, text, required: false): Provide the URL of your club's website.
* **Send Email** (`send_email`, select_one, required: false): Choose the option for sending emails about your club's re-opening.
* **Embedded on Website** (`embedded_on_website`, select_one, required: false): Confirm whether to embed your club's website on our website or not. Select 'Yes' to embed your club's website on our website.
