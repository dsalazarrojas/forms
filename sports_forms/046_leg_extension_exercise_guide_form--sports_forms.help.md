# Leg Extension Exercise Guide Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Leg Extension Exercise Guide Form is a tool for collecting and documenting participant information and exercise data to facilitate research and documentation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the participant information section with your personal details.
2. Answer the exercise information section with your exercise type, frequency, duration, and intensity.
3. Provide feedback on your exercise experience by answering the feedback questions.
4. Enter your participant email address for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Intake Form (Text)**: This field is used to provide a brief description of your exercise routine or experience.
* **Feedback Form (Text)**: This field is used to provide any additional feedback or comments about your exercise experience.
* **Participant Info (Text)**: This field is used to enter a brief description of yourself as a participant.
* **Participant Name (Text)**: Enter your name as it should be used for documentation and communication.
* **Participant Age**: Enter your age as it will be used to help us better understand your demographic information.
* **Exercise Info (Text)**: This field is used to describe the exercise being performed.
* **Exercise Type (Select)**: Choose the type of exercise you are performing from the options provided.
* **Exercise Frequency (Number)**: Enter the frequency with which you perform this exercise.
* **Exercise Duration (Number)**: Enter the duration of the exercise.
* **Exercise Intensity (Select)**: Choose the intensity level of the exercise from the options provided.
* **Feedback Question 1 (Text)**: Provide your feedback on your experience with the exercise.
* **Feedback Question 2 (Text)**: Provide any additional feedback or comments.
* **Feedback Question 3 (Text)**: Provide any further feedback or comments.
* **Participant Email (Email)**: Enter your email address for contact and communication purposes.
