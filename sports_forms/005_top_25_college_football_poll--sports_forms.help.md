# top_25_college_football_poll - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for ranking the top 25 colleges for football. The form collects data on your poll rankings for each week of the college football season. Please provide your rankings for each week, and optionally include any comments about your poll.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select your top 25 college football rankings for each week from the provided options.
2. Enter the name of the team for the week you are ranking.
3. Provide any comments about your poll in the form comments field.
