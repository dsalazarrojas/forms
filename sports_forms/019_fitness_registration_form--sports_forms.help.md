# fitness_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and personal details for individuals interested in joining a fitness program or club. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Provide your personal details in the Personal Info section.
2. Share your motivation for joining the fitness program or club in the What Motivates You To Join section.
3. Enter your contact information, including phone number, email, note, and address details in the Contact Info section.
4. Describe your health background, sports background, and nutrition background in the respective sections.
5. Specify your fitness goals in the What Are Your Fitness Goals section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Info**: Enter your name and any other relevant personal details.
* **Why Do You Want To Join**: Describe why you want to join the fitness program or club.
* **Health Background**: Enter any health issues or concerns you have.
* **Sports Background**: Enter any relevant sports experience or background.
* **Nutrition Background**: Enter any relevant nutrition information or background.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Note**: Enter any additional note or information.
* **Address**: Enter your address.
* **City**: Enter your city.
* **State**: Enter your state.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **Country**: Enter your country.
* **What Are Your Fitness Goals**: Enter your fitness goals.
* **What Motivates You To Join**: Enter what motivates you to join the fitness program or club.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate processing of your form submission.
- Be as detailed as possible when answering the questions, it will help us to better understand your needs and preferences.
- If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to contact us.
