# return_to_training_waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to confirm the waiver status of an individual for a training or exercise program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer truthfully.
2. For the "medical_condition" questions (Fields 2-5), provide a description of any medical condition that may affect your participation in the training or exercise program.
3. For the "training_waiver" questions (Fields 6-25), select "Waiver" or "No Waiver" to confirm your waiver status for each training or exercise program listed.
