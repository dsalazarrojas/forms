# Football Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Football Registration Form is used to collect information from players registering for a football team. The form helps to gather necessary details for player registration, including personal information, medical conditions, and contact details. This form is required for all players to ensure accurate and efficient registration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in your personal details, including your first name, last name, email, and phone number.
2. Select your position on the team, as this is a required field.
3. If you are a returning player, select "Returning" from the registration type dropdown menu.
4. If you are a new player, select "New" from the registration type dropdown menu.
5. Provide emergency contact information, including the first name, last name, relationship, phone number, and address.
6. If you are a coach, provide your name, phone number, and email.
7. If necessary, provide any medical conditions you may have.
8. Finally, fill in your player height and weight.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text): Your first name is a required field. Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (text): Your last name is an optional field. Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (email): Your email address is a required field for communication purposes. Please enter a valid email address that you have access to.
* **Phone** (text): Your phone number is an optional field. Please enter a valid phone number that you have access to.
* **Birthdate** (date): Your birthdate is an optional field. Please enter your birthdate in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Position** (select_one): This field is a required field. Select your position on the team, which could be Coach, Defense, Goalie, Kicker, or Offense.
* **Team** (select_multiple): This field is an optional field. Select any teams you are registered with, if any.
* **Registration Type** (select_one): This field is a required field. Select whether you are a returning or new player.
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (text): This field is an optional field. Enter the first name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (text): This field is an optional field. Enter the last name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (select_one): This field is a required field. Select your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (text): This field is an optional field. Enter a valid phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (text): This field is an optional field. Enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Coach Name** (text): This field is an optional field. Enter the name of your coach, if any.
* **Coach Phone** (text): This field is an optional field. Enter the phone number of your coach, if any.
* **Coach Email** (email): This field is an optional field. Enter a valid email address of your coach, if any.
* **Player Height** (number): This field is an optional field. Enter your height in centimeters or meters.
* **Player Weight** (number): This field is an optional field. Enter your weight in kilograms.
* **Player Position** (text): This field is an optional field. Enter your position on the team, if different from your selected position.
* **Medical Conditions** (text): This field is an optional field. Enter any medical conditions you may have.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (text): This field is an optional field. Enter your emergency contact's address.
* **Registration Type** (select_one): This field is a required field. Select whether you are a returning or new player.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (text): This field is an optional field. Enter a valid phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (text): This field is an optional field. Enter the first name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (text): This field is an optional field. Enter the last name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (select_one): This field is a required field. Select your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (text): This field is an optional field. Enter the address of your emergency contact.

## Tips
* Please ensure you have completed all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check your details before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
* If you have any doubts or issues, contact your team administrator for assistance.
