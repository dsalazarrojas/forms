# Tournament Venue Experience Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Tournament Venue Experience Survey is a tool used to collect feedback from event organizers, participants, and attendees on the overall experience at a tournament venue. This survey aims to assess various aspects of the venue's facilities, staff, and services, providing valuable insights for future event planning and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. The survey is divided into several sections, each focusing on a specific aspect of the tournament venue experience.
2. Answer each question based on your genuine experience and perceptions.
3. Rate each question using a numeric scale, where:
	* 1: Very Dissatisfied
	* 2: Somewhat Dissatisfied
	* 3: Neutral
	* 4: Somewhat Satisfied
	* 5: Very Satisfied
