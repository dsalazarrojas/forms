# Concussion Physical Exam Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on the physical examination of an athlete or patient who has suffered a concussion. The purpose of this form is to gather a comprehensive medical history, assess the extent of the injury, and provide a recommended plan for treatment or return to play.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully read the form instructions and each section before starting.
- Ensure you are completing the form for the correct patient or athlete.
- Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and date of the exam.
- Describe the mechanism of injury and symptoms reported by the patient.
- Select and list any symptoms reported by the patient.
- Document any neurological exam findings.
- Perform a balance test and select the result.
- Record the cognitive assessment notes.
- Provide a recommended treatment or return to play plan.
- Sign and date the form with your clinician name and credentials.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient name** (`patient_name`, `text`, required): The full name of the athlete or patient.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): The date of birth of the patient.
* **Exam date** (`exam_date`, `date`, required): The date of the physical exam.
* **Mechanism of injury** (`mechanism_of_injury`, `text`, required): A brief description of how the injury occurred.
* **Symptoms reported** (`symptom_list`, `select_multiple`, required): A list of symptoms reported by the patient, such as headache, dizziness, nausea, etc.
* **Neurological exam findings** (`neurological_findings`, `text`, optional): The results of the neurological exam, including cranial nerves, reflexes, and gait.
* **Balance test outcome** (`balance_test`, `select_one`, optional): The result of the balance test, such as normal, abnormal, or not performed.
* **Cognitive assessment notes** (`cognitive_assessment`, `text`, optional): Brief notes on the cognitive assessment, such as results of a cognitive screen.
* **Recommended treatment or return to play plan** (`recommended_plan`, `text`, required): A recommended plan for treatment or return to play.
* **Clinician name and credentials** (`clinician_signature`, `text`, required): The name and credentials of the clinician completing the form.

## Tips
- Complete each section carefully and thoroughly.
- Use the patient's or athlete's own words when describing the mechanism of injury.
- Be sure to include all symptoms reported by the patient.
- Document any neurological findings that may indicate concussion.
- Select the correct outcome of the balance test.
- Record any cognitive assessment notes if performed.
- Ensure the recommended plan is specific to the patient's or athlete's needs.
- Sign and date the form with your name and credentials.
