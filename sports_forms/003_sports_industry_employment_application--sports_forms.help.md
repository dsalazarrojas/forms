# sports-industry-employment-application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect employment-related information from candidates in the sports industry, specifically from referees and officials. It includes fields for candidate information, contact details, employment history, education and credentials, skills and qualifications, work experience, referee information, referee certifications, referee availability, and referee specialites.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the Candidate Information section, where you will provide basic details about yourself, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Next, move on to the Contact Information section, where you will provide more detailed contact information, including your address and any other relevant contact methods.
3. Proceed to the Employment History section, where you will describe your previous employment experiences, including job titles, dates of employment, and reason for leaving.
4. In the Education and Credentials section, list your educational background, including degrees, certifications, and any other relevant qualifications.
5. The Skills and Qualifications section is where you will describe your relevant skills and qualifications, including languages, software, and other relevant expertise.
6. In the Work Experience section, describe your work experience, including job titles, dates, and a brief description of your role and responsibilities.
7. The Referee Information section is where you will provide detailed information about your refereeing experience, including the sports you referee, your level of experience, and any relevant certifications.
8. The Referee Certifications section is where you will list any relevant certifications or qualifications you hold related to refereeing.
9. Finally, in the Referee Availability section, indicate your availability for refereeing assignments, including your preferred dates and times.
10. In the Referee Specialties section, list any specific sports or disciplines you specialize in as a referee.
