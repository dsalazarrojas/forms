# Volunteer Sports Official Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Sports Official Application is a form used to find suitable volunteers for sports officiating and coaching positions. It is intended for individuals with relevant sports experience, certification, and training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill in all the required fields and provide as much detail as possible. Make sure to accurately represent your experience, certifications, and availability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Form** (Text): A field to enter your name and contact details.
* **Name** (Text): Enter your name as it appears on official documents.
* **Role** (Select One): Choose your role (Official, Coach, or Player).
* **School** (Text): Enter the name of the school you are associated with.
* **Level** (Select One): Choose the level of your sports experience (High School, Middle School, or College).
* **Sport** (Select Multiple): Select the sports you have officiated or coached experience in.
* **Game Experience** (Date): Enter the date of your last game experience.
* **Availability** (Time): Enter your availability for the sports event.
* **Availability Notes** (Text): Provide additional information about your availability.
* **Certification** (Text): Enter any certification or licenses you hold.
* **Referee Experience** (Number): Enter the number of games you have refereed.
* **Referee Certification** (Text): Enter your referee certification or license details.
* **Training Hours** (Number): Enter the number of training hours you have completed.
* **Training Hours Notes** (Text): Provide additional information about your training hours.
* **Medical Certificate** (Note): Enter any relevant medical certifications you hold.
* **Medical Certificate Notes** (Text): Provide additional information about your medical certification.
* **First Aid Training** (Select Multiple): Choose whether you have completed first aid training or not.
* **Certification Expiration** (Date): Enter the date your certification expires.
* **Availability 3** (Time): Enter your additional availability for the sports event.
* **Availability 4** (Time): Enter your another additional availability for the sports event.
* **First Aids** (Text): Enter any first aid certifications you hold.
* **Medical Cert** (Text): Enter any medical certifications you hold.
* **Notes** (Text): Provide additional information about yourself that is not covered in other fields.

Note: Some fields are optional, but please fill in all required fields accurately and truthfully.
