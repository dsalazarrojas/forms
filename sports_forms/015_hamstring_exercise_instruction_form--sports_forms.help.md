# Hamstring Exercise Instruction Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record instructions for hamstring exercises conducted by coaches for participants. The form collects information about the participant, the exercise performed, and the details of the workout.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your participant name.
2. Enter the description of the exercise performed.
3. Choose who conducted the coaching (Options 1, 2, or 3).
4. Enter the date of the exercise.
5. Enter the time of the exercise (in 24-hour format).
6. Provide the location of the exercise.
7. Confirm your participation in the exercise (Option 1, 2, or 3).
8. (Optional) Enter the coach's signature.
9. (Optional) Enter your participant signature.
10. Enter your participant email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name**: (`participant_name`, text, required) This field collects the name of the participant who performed the hamstring exercise.
* **Exercise Description**: (`exercise_description`, text, required) This field is used to describe the exercise performed in detail.
* **Coached by**: (`coach_conducted`, select, required) This field allows you to select who conducted the coaching during the exercise.
* **Exercise Date**: (`exercise_date`, date, required) This field requires the date of the exercise in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Exercise Time**: (`exercise_time`, time, required) This field requires the time of the exercise in 24-hour format (`HH:MM`).
* **Training Location**: (`training_location`, text, required) This field is used to specify the location of the exercise.
* **Participant Confirmation**: (`participant_confirmation`, select, required) This field is used to confirm your participation in the exercise.
* **Coach Signature**: (`coach_signature`, text, optional) This field is an optional field for the coach to sign and date their name and title.
* **Participant Signature**: (`participant_signature`, text, optional) This field is an optional field for you to sign and date your name.
* **Participant Email**: (`participant_email`, email, required) This field is used to enter your email address as the participant.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled accurately and truthfully.
* If a coach conducted the exercise, ensure to select the correct option for coach_conducted field.
* Enter dates and times in the correct format as specified.
