# Athlete Medical History Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Athlete Medical History Form is a critical document that helps coaches, trainers, and medical staff gather essential information about an athlete's medical history, ensuring their safety and well-being during sports activities. This form is required for all athletes to fill out before participating in sports programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
2. Fill out the form completely, as required fields are marked.
3. If you are unsure about a specific question, do not hesitate to consult with the coaching staff or a medical professional.
4. Once you have completed the form, review it carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Athlete Full Name**: This field is for your full name, first and last name.
* **Date of Birth**: Please enter your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
* **Gender**: Select your gender from the provided options.
* **Athlete ID Number**: If you have an athlete ID number, please enter it here. (Note: This is not always required.)
* **Sport**: Choose your primary sport from the options listed.
* **Parent or Guardian Name**: Please provide the full name of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone**: Enter the emergency contact phone number of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Email**: Enter the contact email of your parent or guardian.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Provide the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the emergency contact phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Describe your relationship to the emergency contact.
* **Health Insurance Provider**: Enter the name of your health insurance provider.
* **Insurance Policy Number**: Enter your insurance policy or member number.
* **Primary Physician Name**: Provide the name of your regular doctor.
* **Primary Physician Phone**: Enter the doctor's contact phone number.
* **Blood Type**: Select your blood type from the provided options.
* **Current Medications**: List all current medications and their dosages.
* **Medication Allergies**: List any medication allergies.
* **Food Allergies**: List any food allergies.
* **Other Allergies**: List any other allergies, such as latex or insect stings.
* **Chronic Medical Conditions**: List any ongoing medical conditions, like asthma or diabetes.
* **Previous Sports Injuries**: List any significant sports injuries.
* **Previous Surgeries**: List any surgeries and their dates.
* **Previous Hospitalizations**: List any hospitalizations and reasons.
* **Family History of Heart Conditions**: Check 'Yes' if there is a family history of heart problems or sudden cardiac events.
* **History of Concussions**: Check 'Yes' if you have had a concussion.
* **Concussion Details**: Provide details about any concussions, if applicable.
* **Symptoms During Exercise**: Check 'Yes' if you experience chest pain, dizziness, or fainting during exercise.
* **Additional Medical Information**: Provide any other medical information that the coaching staff should know.
* **Information Accuracy Confirmation**: Confirm that the information provided is accurate and complete.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature**: Sign your name as the parent or guardian.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date of signature.

Remember, this form is a critical document that ensures your safety and well-being during sports activities. Please fill it out carefully and accurately, and do not hesitate to ask if you are unsure about any question.
