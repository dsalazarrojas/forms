# Club Membership Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from you for your membership registration. It includes personal details, membership preferences, and medical information to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for everyone at the club.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out all required fields, denoted by an asterisk (*).
2. Enter your personal information, including your first name, last name, email address, phone number, and date of birth.
3. Select your preferred membership type and start date.
4. Choose your payment method for membership fees.
5. Indicate your interest in various sports and select your experience level in each sport.
6. If applicable, provide information about any medical conditions you have that may impact your participation in club activities.
7. Provide emergency contact information.
8. Accept the club's liability waiver and terms of service.
9. Optionally, provide additional comments or questions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your given name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your family name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for club communications.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your best contact number for the club.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth.
* **Membership Type** (`membership_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose from Basic, Standard, Premium, or Family membership levels.
* **Preferred Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required): Select the date you would like your membership to begin.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Select how you will pay your membership fees from Credit Card, Debit Card, Bank Transfer, or Direct Debit.
* **Which sports are you interested in** (`sports_interests`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all sports that you are interested in from Basketball, Tennis, Swimming, Volleyball, Fitness, or Badminton.
* **Experience Level** (`experience_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose your skill level from Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Competitive.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **I accept the club liability waiver and terms of service** (`waiver_acceptance`, `select_one`, required): Select Yes, I accept to complete the registration.
* **How did you hear about our club** (`how_heard_about_us`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how you heard about the club from Friend or Family, Social Media, Internet Search, Advertisement, or Other.
* **Any additional comments or questions** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Use this field to provide any additional comments or questions you have about the club.
