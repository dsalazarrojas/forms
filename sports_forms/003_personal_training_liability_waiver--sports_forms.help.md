# Personal Training Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather personal information from individuals who will be participating in personal training activities to secure a waiver, releasing the training organization from liability in case of any accidents or injuries during the activity.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Ensure you have read and understood the form carefully before signing it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1): Enter your personal name as it is spelled.
* **Last Name** (2): Enter your surname as it is spelled.
* **Date of Birth** (3): Enter your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (4): Enter the first name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (5): Enter the last name of the emergency contact person.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and truthfully.
* Review the form carefully before signing it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
