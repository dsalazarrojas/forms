# Race Finish Line Arch Rental Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Race Finish Line Arch Rental Order Form is designed to collect information from customers interested in renting a finish line arch for their sports event. This form helps to gather all necessary details to process the rental request efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select the relevant options for the customer information.
2.  Choose the preferred arch rental details for your event.
3.  Fill in the customer contact information.
4.  Specify the date and time for the arch rental.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **customer_info** (`customer_information`, `select_one`, *optional*): Choose the correct customer information that best describes your event. Select one of the available options: Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
* **arch_rental_details** (`arch_rental_details`, `select_multiple`, *optional*): Select the preferred arch rental details for your event. You can choose one or more of the available options: Arch 1, Arch 2, or Arch 3.
* **customer_name** (`customer_name`, `text`, *optional*): Enter the customer's name as it appears on their official identification.
* **customer_email** (`customer_email`, `email`, *optional*): Enter the customer's email address to ensure efficient communication.
* **customer_phone** (`customer_phone`, `text`, *optional*): Enter the customer's phone number for any follow-up communication.
* **customer_zip** (`customer_zip`, `text`, *optional*): Enter the customer's zip code for location information.
* **arch_rental_date** (`arch_rental_date`, `date`, *optional*): Specify the date you wish to rent the arch for.
* **arch_rental_time** (`arch_rental_time`, `time`, *optional*): Choose the time you would like to rent the arch. Please note that some time slots may have availability restrictions. 

## Tips
- Please ensure to fill in all required fields for a successful rental process.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact our team for assistance.
- For any urgent requests, please contact us via email or phone to expedite the process.
