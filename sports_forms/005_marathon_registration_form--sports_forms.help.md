# Marathon Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect registration information for a marathon event. Fill out the form with your details to participate in the marathon.

## How To Complete This Form
To register for the marathon, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*): first name, last name, email, address, and finisher t shirt options.
2. Select your t shirt size.
3. Choose your favorite t shirt color.
4. Answer the finisher t shirt questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first name** (text): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **last name** (text): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **email** (email): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **phone** (text): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **address** (text): Enter your address, including street, city, state, and zip code.
* **event date** (date): Enter the date of the marathon event.
* **t shirt size** (select_one): Choose your t shirt size from the available options.
* **t shirt color** (select_multiple): Select your preferred t shirt color(s) from the available options.
* **Finisher T-shirt Options**:
  * **finisher_tshirt_option1** (select_one): Indicate if you want a finisher t shirt.
  * **finisher_tshirt_option2** (select_one): Choose if you want a finisher t shirt with the True option.
  * **finisher_tshirt_option3** (select_one): Choose if you want a finisher t shirt with the Maybe option.
  * **finisher_tshirt_option4** (select_one): Choose if you want a finisher t shirt with the Yes, Please option.
  * **finisher_tshirt_option5** (select_one): Choose if you want a finisher t shirt with the No Thank You option.
  * **finisher_tshirt_option6** (select_one): Choose if you want a finisher t shirt with the Unsure option.
  * **finisher_tshirt_option7** (select_one): Choose if you want a finisher t shirt with the No Preference option.
  * **finisher_tshirt_option8** (select_one): Choose if you want a finisher t shirt with the Not Interested option.
  * **finisher_tshirt_option9** (select_one): Choose if you want a finisher t shirt with the False option.
  * **finisher_tshirt_option10** (select_one): Choose if you want a finisher t shirt with the Unknown option.
  * **finisher_tshirt_option11** (select_one): Choose if you want a finisher t shirt with the Other option.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [insert contact info].
