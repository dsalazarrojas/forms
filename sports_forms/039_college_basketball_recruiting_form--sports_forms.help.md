# College Basketball Recruiting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The College Basketball Recruiting Form is designed to collect information from high school basketball players interested in being recruited by colleges for their basketball team. This form gathers essential details about the player's athletic and academic achievements, interests, and contact information. The collected data will help college coaches and recruiters to evaluate and potentially offer scholarships or recruit the player to their team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
6. Provide your street address in the "Street Address" field.
7. Enter the name of your city in the "City" field.
8. Enter the name of your state or province in the "State" field.
9. Enter your zip code in the "ZIP Code" field.
10. Enter the name of your high school in the "High School Name" field.
11. Enter the address of your high school in the "High School Address" field.
12. Enter the year you plan to graduate in the "Graduation Year" field.
13. Enter your current GPA in the "Current GPA" field.
14. If applicable, enter your class rank in the "Class Rank" field.
15. Enter your SAT or ACT score, if available, in the "SAT Score" and "ACT Score" fields.
16. Enter your height in feet and inches in the "Height" field.
17. Enter your weight in pounds in the "Weight" field.
18. Select your primary position played in "Primary Position" field.
19. If applicable, enter your secondary position played in the "Secondary Position" field.
20. Select your dominant hand in the "Dominant Hand" field.
21. Enter your jersey number in the "Jersey Number" field, if applicable.
22. Enter the name of your current team in the "Current Team Name" field.
23. Enter the name of your coach in the "Head Coach Name" field.
24. Enter your coach's email address in the "Coach Email" field.
25. Enter your coach's phone number in the "Coach Phone" field.
26. Enter your points per game average in the "Points Per Game" field.
27. Enter your rebounds per game average in the "Rebounds Per Game" field.
28. Enter your assists per game average in the "Assists Per Game" field.
29. Enter your steals per game average in the "Steals Per Game" field.
30. Enter your blocks per game average in the "Blocks Per Game" field, if applicable.
31. Enter your three-point percentage, if available, in the "Three-Point Percentage" field.
32. Enter your free throw percentage, if available, in the "Free Throw Percentage" field.
33. Enter any basketball honors and awards in the "Basketball Honors and Awards" field.
34. Enter any team achievements, if applicable, in the "Team Achievements" field.
35. Enter the link to your highlight video in the "Highlight Video Link" field.
36. If available, enter the link to your game schedule in the "Game Schedule Link" field.
37. List the colleges you are interested in applying to in the "Colleges You Are Interested In" field.
38. Enter your current recruiting status in the "Current Recruiting Status" field.
39. Enter the name of your parent or guardian in the "Parent or Guardian Name" field.
40. Enter your parent or guardian's email address in the "Parent Email" field.
41. Enter your parent or guardian's phone number in the "Parent Phone" field.
42. Enter your academic interests in the "Academic Interests" field.
43. If interested, select "Yes Very Interested" or "Yes Somewhat Interested" in the "Interested in Campus Visit" field.
44. Enter any additional comments you'd like to share in the "Additional Comments" field.

* **First Name** (`player_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`player_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address as your primary contact.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Street Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your street address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the name of your city.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter the name of your state or province.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip`, text, required): Enter your zip code.
* **High School Name** (`high_school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your current high school.
* **High School Address** (`high_school_address`, text, required): Enter the address of your high school.
* **Graduation Year** (`graduation_year`, number, required): Enter the year you plan to graduate.
* **Current GPA** (`gpa`, number, required): Enter your current GPA.
* **Class Rank** (`class_rank`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your class rank.
* **SAT Score** (`sat_score`, number, optional): If applicable, enter your SAT score.
* **ACT Score** (`act_score`, number, optional): If applicable, enter your ACT score.
* **Height** (`height`, text, required): Enter your height in feet and inches.
* **Weight** (`weight`, number, required): Enter your weight in pounds.
* **Primary Position** (`primary_position`, select_one, required): Select the primary position you play.
* **Secondary Position** (`secondary_position`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your secondary position.
* **Dominant Hand** (`dominant_hand`, select_one, required): Select your dominant hand.
* **Jersey Number** (`jersey_number`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your jersey number.
* **Current Team Name** (`current_team_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your current team.
* **Head Coach Name** (`coach_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your coach.
* **Coach Email** (`coach_email`, email, required): Enter your coach's email address.
* **Coach Phone** (`coach_phone`, text, required): Enter your coach's phone number.
* **Points Per Game** (`ppg`, number, required): Enter your points per game average.
* **Rebounds Per Game** (`rpg`, number, required): Enter your rebounds per game average.
* **Assists Per Game** (`apg`, number, required): Enter your assists per game average.
* **Steals Per Game** (`spg`, number, optional): If applicable, enter your steals per game average.
* **Blocks Per Game** (`bpg`, number, optional): If applicable, enter your blocks per game average.
* **Field Goal Percentage** (`fg_percentage`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your field goal percentage.
* **Three-Point Percentage** (`three_point_percentage`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your three-point percentage.
* **Free Throw Percentage** (`ft_percentage`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your free throw percentage.
* **Basketball Honors and Awards** (`honors_awards`, text, optional): Enter any basketball honors and awards.
* **Team Achievements** (`team_achievements`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your team achievements.
* **Highlight Video Link** (`video_highlights`, text, required): Enter the link to your highlight video.
* **Game Schedule Link** (`schedule_link`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the link to your game schedule.
* **Colleges You Are Interested In** (`colleges_you_are_interested_in`, text, required): List the colleges you are interested in applying to.
* **Current Recruiting Status** (`recruiting_status`, text, optional): Enter your current recruiting status.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, required): Enter your parent or guardian's email address.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, required): Enter your parent or guardian's phone number.
* **Academic Interests** (`academic_interests`, text, optional): Enter your academic interests.
* **Interested in Campus Visit** (`campus_visit_interest`, select_one, required): If interested, select "Yes Very Interested" or "Yes Somewhat Interested".
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments you'd like to share.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your entered information for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your coach or parent/guardian for assistance.
* If you are unsure about a field, try to enter the best-effort answer possible.
* This form is for your information and the colleges you are interested in.
