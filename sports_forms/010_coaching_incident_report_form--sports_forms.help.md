# Coaching Incident Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Coaching Incident Report Form is designed to report incidents that occurred during sports activities, such as injuries, misconduct, or other safety concerns. This form helps coaches, team managers, and other relevant parties to gather information and take necessary actions to prevent future incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your name and role as the reporter in the "Reporter Information" section.
3. Provide detailed information about the incident, including the date, time, location, and type of incident.
4. List any witnesses and describe what happened in detail.
5. Indicate if any injuries were reported and describe their severity.
6. Report if medical attention was provided or needed.
7. Outline follow-up actions required to prevent recurrence.
8. Finally, submit the form by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reporter Name** (`reporter_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as the reporter of this incident.
* **Your Role** (`reporter_role`, select_one, required): Select your role as the reporter (Coach, Assistant Coach, Team Manager, Parent, Athlete, or Other).
* **Email Address** (`reporter_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`reporter_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for additional contact.
* **Date of Incident** (`incident_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the incident (in MM/DD/YYYY format).
* **Time of Incident** (`incident_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the incident (in HH:MM AM/PM format).
* **Location of Incident** (`incident_location`, text, required): Describe the location where the incident occurred.
* **Type of Incident** (`incident_type`, select_one, required): Select the most accurate category (Injury, Misconduct or Violation, Property Damage, Safety Concern, Medical Emergency, Conflict Between Athletes, or Other).
* **Athlete or Person Involved** (`athlete_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the athlete or person involved.
* **Age or Date of Birth** (`athlete_age`, number, optional): Enter the age or date of birth of the athlete or person involved.
* **Witnesses** (`witnesses`, text, optional): List the names of witnesses who saw the incident.
* **Detailed Description** (`detailed_description`, text, required): Provide a clear and detailed account of what happened during the incident.
* **Immediate Actions Taken** (`immediate_actions`, text, required): Describe the immediate actions taken after the incident.
* **Injuries Reported** (`injuries_reported`, select_one, required): Indicate if any injuries were reported (True or False).
* **Severity of Injury** (`injury_severity`, select_one, optional): Select the severity of the injury (Minor, Moderate, or Severe).
* **Medical Attention Provided or Needed** (`medical_attention`, select_one, required): Indicate if medical attention was provided or needed (Yes, Provided; Yes, Referred to Hospital; or No, Not Needed).
* **Follow-up Actions Required** (`followup_actions`, text, optional): Describe the follow-up actions required to prevent recurrence.
* **Report Date** (`report_date`, date, required): Enter the date of this report.
