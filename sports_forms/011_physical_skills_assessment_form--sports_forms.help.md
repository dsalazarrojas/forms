> # Physical Skills Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess an individual's physical skills, specifically their strength, endurance, agility, and speed. It is intended to provide a snapshot of the participant's current physical condition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant details in the first field.
2. Provide your contact details in the second field.
3. Select your strength level (High, Medium, or Low) in the third field.
4. Select your endurance level (High, Medium, or Low) in the fourth field.
5. Select your agility level (High, Medium, or Low) in the fifth field.
6. Select your speed level (High, Medium, or Low) in the sixth field.
7. Enter any additional comments about your assessment in the seventh field (optional).
8. Enter the date and time of submission in the eighth and ninth fields, respectively.
9. Sign and date the form in the final field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required): Enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required): Enter your contact information, including your phone number and email address.
* **Strength** (`strength`, select_one, required): Select the level of strength you possess, where:
	+ High: You possess high strength and can perform physical tasks with ease.
	+ Medium: You possess medium strength and may need some effort to perform physical tasks.
	+ Low: You possess low strength and may struggle with physical tasks.
* **Endurance** (`endurance`, select_one, required): Select the level of endurance you possess, where:
	+ High: You can perform physical activities for an extended period without getting tired.
	+ Low: You may get tired quickly and need regular breaks.
* **Agility** (`agility`, select_one, required): Select the level of agility you possess, where:
	+ High: You can move quickly and easily through physical spaces.
	+ Low: You may struggle with rapid movements or changes in direction.
* **Speed** (`speed`, select_one, required): Select the level of speed you possess, where:
	+ High: You can perform physical activities quickly and efficiently.
	+ Low: You may take longer than average to complete physical tasks.
* **Assessment Comments** (`assessment_comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your assessment.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date you submitted this form.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you submitted this form (optional).
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, note, required): Sign and date this form to confirm your participation.
