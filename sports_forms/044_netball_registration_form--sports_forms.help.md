# Netball Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for registering participants for netball sessions, providing details about each participant to ensure a smooth and enjoyable netball experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in the form completely and accurately, ensuring all required fields are filled out correctly.
2. For each field, follow the explanation below to understand what information is required and what kind of input is expected.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant name**: Enter the full name of the participant, as it appears on their identification.
* **Participant age**: Enter the participant's age as of the current date. This is used to assess participant suitability for certain sessions.
* **Participant email**: Enter the participant's email address. This is used for communication and sending session reminders.
* **Participant phone**: Enter the participant's phone number for emergencies and contact purposes.
* **Participant session preferences**: Select all sessions that the participant is interested in, using the available options (Session 1, Session 2, Session 3).
* **Participant emergency contact**: Enter the name of the participant's emergency contact person.
* **Emergency contact relationship**: Describe the relationship between the participant and their emergency contact (e.g., parent, guardian, spouse).
* **Emergency contact phone**: Enter the phone number of the participant's emergency contact person.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are completed.
- Double-check the participant's session preferences for accuracy.
- If you're unsure about any field, contact us for clarification.

Please note that this form is for registration purposes only. Once you've completed the form, you will be contacted by our team for further processing and to confirm your registration details.
