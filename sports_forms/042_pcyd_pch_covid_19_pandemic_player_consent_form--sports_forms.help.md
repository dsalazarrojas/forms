# Pcyd/ Pch Covid 19 Pandemic Player Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for players to confirm their understanding and acceptance of risks related to COVID-19. It gathers information about their symptoms, acknowledgment of the contagious nature of COVID-19, and assumption of responsibility for any risks or consequences. The form also includes sections for releasing any claims and confirming the absence of symptoms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Ensure that you have a clear understanding of what each question is asking.
3. If you need clarification on any question, do not hesitate to reach out to the contact provided.
