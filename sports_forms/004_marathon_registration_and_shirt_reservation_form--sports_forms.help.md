# Marathon Registration And Shirt Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marathon Registration And Shirt Reservation Form is designed for athletes to register for a marathon event and reserve a shirt. This form collects information about the athlete, including their name, contact information, and preferences for the marathon event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form in its entirety, ensuring that all required fields are filled in correctly.
2. Enter your first and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields, respectively.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Select your preferred shirt size and color in the "Shirt Size" and "Shirt Color" fields, respectively.
5. Choose the marathon date and time you will be participating in.
6. Enter your finisher bib number in the "Finisher Bib Number" field.
7. Review and submit the form once all fields have been filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address here.
* **Shirt Size** (`shirt_size`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred shirt size from the available options: XS, S, M, L, XL, XXL.
* **Marathon Date** (`marathon_date`, date, required): Choose the date you will be participating in the marathon.
* **Marathon Time** (`marathon_time`, time, required): Choose the time you will be participating in the marathon.
* **Shirt Color** (`shirt_color`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred shirt color from the available options: Blue, Red, Green, Yellow.
* **Finisher Bib Number** (`finisher_bib_number`, number, required): Enter your finisher bib number here.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us for clarification.
* We will review and process your form submission once all information is accurate and complete.
