# Fitness Weekly Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather weekly fitness updates from users. Please complete this form to report your progress, goals, and other relevant information to help us track your progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Weekly Goal** (text field) with your specific goal for the week.
2. Report your **Exercise Frequency** (number field) for the week.
3. Note down your **Rest Days** (number field).
4. Select your **Workout Type** (select_one field) from the provided options.
5. Enter the number of **Exercises Completed** (number field) this week.
6. If applicable, report the distance you've **Run** (number field).
7. Report the weight you've **Lifted** (number field).
8. Enter the **Workout Time** (time field) for your workout session.
9. Enter the total **Workout Time in Minutes** (number field) for your workout session.
10. Provide any additional **Comments** (note field) about your progress or any challenges you faced.
11. Enter your **Email** (email field) to ensure we can get in touch with you about your progress.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Weekly Goal** (Weekly Goal): A brief description of your weekly goal, e.g., "Complete 3 workouts this week."
* **Exercise Frequency** (Exercise Frequency): The number of times you've exercised this week.
* **Rest Days** (Rest Days): The number of rest days you've had this week.
* **Workout Type** (Workout Type): Select one of the provided options to indicate the type of exercise or activity you did this week.
* **Exercises Completed** (Exercises Completed): The total number of exercises you've completed this week.
* **Run Distance** (Distance Run): If you've been running, report the distance you've covered this week.
* **Weight Lifted** (Weight Lifted): If you've been weightlifting, report the weight you've lifted this week.
* **Workout Time** (Workout Time): Enter the start and end time of your workout session.
* **Workout Time in Minutes** (Workout Time in Minutes): The total duration of your workout session in minutes.
* **Comments** (Comments): Any additional comments or challenges you'd like to share.
* **Email** (Email): Your email address for us to get in touch with you about your progress.
