# Gym Trainer Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gym Trainer Evaluation Survey is designed to gather feedback from clients about their experiences with gym trainers, helping the gym management team to evaluate and improve the quality of services provided.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select all relevant options that apply to your experience with the trainer.
3. Provide detailed feedback in the text fields.
4. Submit the form when you have completed all the sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trainer Professionalism** (Page 1, Field 1): Please rate the trainer's professionalism, from 1 (not professional) to 5 (very professional).
* **Trainer Communication** (Page 1, Field 2): Describe the trainer's communication style, including how well they explained exercises, provided feedback, and kept you engaged.
* **Motivation** (Page 1, Field 3): Describe what motivated you to come to the gym and start a training program. This can include your fitness goals, reasons for joining the gym, or any specific goals you wanted to achieve.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (Page 1, Field 4): Rate your overall satisfaction with the training session, from 1 (very dissatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).
* **Would Recommend Trainer** (Page 1, Field 5): Indicate whether you would recommend this trainer to a friend, with options for "True", "False", or "maybe".
* **Comments** (Page 1, Field 6): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the trainer or your experience.
* **Gym Name** (Page 1, Field 7): Please enter the name of the gym you visited.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (Page 1, Field 8): This field is optional and allows you to provide any additional details about your overall satisfaction.

Note: Please answer all questions in the context of your most recent training session. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact [insert contact information].
