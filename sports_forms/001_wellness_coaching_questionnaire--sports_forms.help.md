# wellness_coaching_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The wellness coaching questionnaire is designed to gather information on your physical and mental well-being, helping you identify areas for improvement and providing a starting point for guidance and support from wellness coaches.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and answer the questions based on your current state.
2. Be honest and accurate in your responses.
3. Fill out each page, moving from top to bottom.
4. Once you have completed all pages, click submit to proceed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Introduction 
* **Introduction** (Page 1): This is an optional note section where you can provide any additional information or context about yourself that you feel is essential for the coaching process.

### Physical Activity Level 
* **Physical Activity Level** (Page 2): Choose one or more of the following options that best describe your current level of physical activity:
	+ High: You regularly engage in intense or strenuous physical activities.
	+ Medium: You have a moderate level of physical activity, such as walking or light exercise.
	+ Low: You have a sedentary lifestyle, with minimal physical activity.

### Sleep Habit 
* **Sleep Habit** (Page 3): Select one option that best describes your current sleep habit:
	+ True: You consistently sleep well and wake up feeling rested.
	+ False: You have difficulty sleeping or experience disrupted sleep.
	+ Sometimes: You have irregular sleep patterns or sleep quality varies.

### Water Intake 
* **Water Intake** (Page 4): Choose one option that best describes your current water intake:
	+ High: You drink plenty of water throughout the day.
	+ Medium: You have a moderate water intake.
	+ Low: You often forget to or don't drink enough water.

### Exercise Frequency 
* **Exercise Frequency** (Page 5): If applicable, please enter the number of days you engage in physical activity per week.

### Stress Level 
* **Stress Level** (Page 6): Select one option that best describes your current stress level:
	+ High: You often feel stressed or overwhelmed.
	+ Medium: You experience occasional stress.
	+ Low: You generally feel calm and relaxed.

### Physical Pain 
* **Physical Pain** (Page 7): Choose one or more of the following options that best describe your current physical pain levels:
	+ True: You experience consistent or occasional physical pain.
	+ False: You don't experience physical pain.
	+ Sometimes: You experience occasional or mild physical pain.

### Mental Pain 
* **Mental Pain** (Page 8): Select one option that best describes your current mental pain level:
	+ High: You experience intense mental pain or emotional distress.
	+ Medium: You experience occasional mental pain or emotional discomfort.
	+ Low: You generally feel mentally well.

### Overall Feeling 
* **Overall Feeling** (Page 9): Choose one option that best describes your current overall feeling:
	+ High: You feel energized, motivated, and positive.
	+ Medium: You feel neutral or average.
	+ Low: You feel tired, unmotivated, or negative.

### Exercise Frequency Days 
* **Exercise Frequency Days** (Page 10): If applicable, please enter the number of days you engage in physical activity per week.

Note: The answers to these questions will be treated confidentially and used solely for coaching purposes. Please answer honestly and accurately to ensure the best possible guidance and support.
