# Youth Sports Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Youth Sports Registration Form is used to collect information from students and their guardians for sports-related activities. This form is typically used by schools, sports teams, or organizations to gather relevant data for registration, safety, and other administrative purposes. 

To ensure accurate and efficient completion of the form, please review the following guide carefully.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form in sections**: The form is divided into several sections, each corresponding to a different category of information. Complete each section according to the instructions provided.
2. **Use the correct format for date**: When entering the date of birth, please use the standard format of MM/DD/YYYY.
3. **Enter valid email addresses**: Make sure to enter a valid email address for both the student and parent, as this will be used for communication purposes.
4. **Select only relevant sports options**: Only select the sports that the student is registering for, as this will help with team assignment and other administrative tasks.
5. **Enter accurate school information**: Please ensure that you enter the correct school name and school year, as this will help with record-keeping and reporting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your student's first name.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your student's last name.

### Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Enter your student's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address, which will be used for communication purposes. This should be a valid email address.

### Parent Phone
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact number, which will be used for communication purposes. Please ensure that you enter a valid phone number.

### Sports
* **Sports** (`sports`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the sports that the student is registering for. Please select only the sports that apply to the student.

### School Name
* **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your school.

### School Year
* **School Year** (`school_year`, `select_one`, optional): Select the school year that the student is currently in.

### Grade Level
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, `select_one`, optional): Select the grade level that the student is currently at.

### Student Name
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the student, which will be used for record-keeping purposes.

### Student Email
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, `text`, optional): Enter a valid email address for the student, which will be used for communication purposes.

### Student Phone
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter a valid phone number for the student.

### Guardian Name
* **Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the guardian or parent.

### Guardian Phone
* **Guardian Phone** (`guardian_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the guardian or parent.

### Emergency Contact Name
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact.

### Emergency Contact Phone
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
