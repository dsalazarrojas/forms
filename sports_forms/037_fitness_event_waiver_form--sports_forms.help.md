# Fitness Event Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for individuals participating in fitness events, such as workouts or classes, to provide information about their medical history, emergency contacts, and other relevant details. It ensures that the participant has provided informed consent and acknowledges certain risks associated with the event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Please fill out the form accurately and honestly**. Make sure to enter the required information (marked as `required` in the form) to ensure a complete and accurate submission.
2. **Use the correct format for date fields**. When entering dates, use the standard `mm/dd/yyyy` format.
3. **Double-check your answers** before submitting the form. Ensure that all fields are complete and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your surname as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the standard `mm/dd/yyyy` format.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of an emergency contact person, such as a family member or friend.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, note, required): If you have any medical conditions or concerns, please provide details in this field. If you don't have any medical conditions, simply leave this field blank.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, required): List any allergies you may have, such as food or medication.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): Provide a brief summary of your medical history, including any previous injuries or surgeries.
* **Insurance Company** (`insurance_company`, text, required): If you have insurance, please enter the name of your insurance company.
* **Insurance Number** (`insurance_number`, text, required): Enter your insurance number or policy number.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the emergency contact person.
* **Next of Kin Name** (`next_of_kin_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of a next of kin or contact person.
* **Next of Kin Relationship** (`next_of_kin_relationship`, text, required): Describe your relationship with the next of kin contact person.
* **Next of Kin Phone** (`next_of_kin_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the next of kin contact person.
* **Next of Kin Address** (`next_of_kin_contact_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the next of kin contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Signature** (`emergency_contact_signature`, text, required): This field is not used for submission. Please sign and date this field to confirm that you have completed and understood the form.
* **Waiver** (`waiver`, select_one, required): Select `Yes` or `No` to acknowledge that you have read and understood the terms and conditions of the fitness event.

Please note that while this form is designed to help ensure your safety, failure to complete it accurately or provide required information may affect your participation in the fitness event.
