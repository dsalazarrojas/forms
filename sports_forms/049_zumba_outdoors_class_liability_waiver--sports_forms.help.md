# Zumba Outdoors Class Liability Waiver - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Zumba Outdoors Class Liability Waiver is a form designed to gather essential information and obtain consent for outdoor Zumba classes. It's meant to protect both the participant and the organization hosting the class.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your name, contact information, and emergency contact information in the designated fields.
2.  For participants under 18, provide the emergency contact name, relationship, and phone number.
3.  Check the boxes to grant or decline photo and video releases.
4.  If necessary, fill in the medical information fields for any pre-existing medical conditions or allergies.
5.  Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Class Date (1)**: Record the date of the class you're participating in.
*   **Name (2)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
*   **Contact Info (3)**: Provide your email address or phone number where you can be reached in case of an emergency.
*   **Emergency Contact Name (4)**: Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
*   **Emergency Contact Relationship (5)**: Specify your relationship to the emergency contact (e.g., Mom, Dad, etc.).
*   **Emergency Contact Phone (6)**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
*   **Parent Signature (7)**: Sign below if you are a parent or guardian of a participant under 18.
*   **Participant Signature (8)**: Sign below if you are the participant themselves.
*   **Emergency Contact (9)**: Select the emergency contact from the dropdown options (e.g., Mom, Dad, etc.).
*   **Photo Release (10)**: Check the box to grant or decline photo release. If granted, it allows your photo to be taken and shared.
*   **Video Release (11)**: Check the box to grant or decline video release. If granted, it allows your video to be recorded and shared.
*   **Medical Info (12-20)**: Fill in any pre-existing medical conditions or allergies for your own health and safety.
*   **Medical Info 10-14 (12-14)**: These fields are for any additional medical information that may be needed.
*   **Medical Info 15-21 (15-21)**: More medical information fields for specific medical conditions or allergies.
*   **Medical Info 22-25 (22-25)**: Additional medical information fields for any other medical conditions or allergies.

Please note that some fields may require clarification or validation, so ensure you have the necessary permissions or approvals before completing this form.
