# Dual Meet Participation Interest Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help event organizers and coaches collect information from interested participants to determine their interest in attending a dual meet event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the following questions to help us understand your interest in attending the dual meet event:
2. Select the options that apply to you for each question.
3. Be as specific as possible in your answers to help us better serve you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dual Meet Participation Interest Survey Form** (1, text, required): This is the title of the form, please do not fill out this field.
* **Club Name** (2, text, required): Please enter the name of your swim club or team.
* **School Team** (3, text, required): Please enter your school team or squad.
* **Available Dates** (4, select_multiple, required): Select the dates you are available to attend the dual meet event. Please choose one or more of the following options:
	+ Option 1: Select Option 1
	+ Option 2: Select Option 2
	+ Option 3: Select Option 3
* **Consent** (5, select_one, required): Please select which option best represents your understanding of the event:
	+ Option 1: I understand and agree to the event terms
	+ Option 2: I am unsure of the event terms
	+ Option 3: I do not understand the event terms
* **Email** (6, email, optional): Please enter your email address if you would like to be contacted by us with event updates.
* **Phone** (7, text, optional): Please enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by us with event updates.
* **Notes** (8, note, optional): If you have any additional information or comments, please enter them here.
* **Additional Info** (9, text, optional): If you have any other comments or information, please enter them here.
* **Submit** (10, text, optional): Please click the submit button to send your application.

## Tips

* If you have any issues or errors while filling out the form, please contact our event organizers.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
* This form is intended for individuals who are interested in participating in the dual meet event. If you are completing this form and are not interested in participating, please do not submit it.
