# Soccer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for players to provide their details for registration with our soccer organization. It collects player's basic information to facilitate their participation in soccer activities and events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Review the form carefully and fill in the required fields.
2. Ensure you provide accurate and complete information for each field.
3. If you need help with any field, don't hesitate to contact our support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Details (1)**: Enter your name and any other details you'd like to share about yourself.
* **Preferred Sport (2)**: Select your preferred sport (e.g., soccer, basketball, etc.).
* **Preferred Formation (3)**: Choose your preferred soccer formation (e.g., 4-4-2, 4-3-3, etc.).
* **Date of Birth (4)**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Height (5)**: Enter your height in inches. Please ensure you enter a valid number.
* **Notes (6)**: Add any additional information you'd like to share about yourself or your soccer preferences.

## Tips
- Please ensure all fields are filled correctly to avoid rejection of your registration.
- If you're unsure about any field, please ask our support team for help.
- If you have any questions or need further assistance, feel free to contact us.
