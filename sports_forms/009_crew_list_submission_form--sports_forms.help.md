# crew_list_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather data from crew members regarding their status, position, and other relevant details. The information collected will be used to update the crew list and facilitate administrative tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information about the crew members ready.
2. Fill in the required fields with the correct details.
3. Review the form carefully to ensure accuracy.
4. Submit the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Crew List**: Enter the list of crew members in this text field. Please ensure that you have all the necessary information about each crew member.
* **Crew Member Name**: Enter the name of the crew member. This field is used to identify the individual.
* **Crew Number**: Enter the number of the crew member. This is a unique identifier for each crew member.
* **Crew Status**: Select the status of the crew member from the dropdown menu. Please choose between "Active" and "Inactive".
* **Crew Position**: Select the position(s) of the crew member from the options provided. Please choose one or more options as relevant.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date of submission. Use the date format as prompted.
* **Submission Time**: Enter the time of submission.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the crew member.
* **Email**: Enter an email address (if applicable).
* **Note**: Enter any additional notes about the crew member.

## Tips
- Ensure that you fill in all the required fields.
- Double-check the accuracy of the information entered.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
