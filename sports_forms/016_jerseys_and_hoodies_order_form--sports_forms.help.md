# Jerseys And Hoodies Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow users to order jerseys and hoodies from the sports store. To ensure accurate order processing, please fill out this form completely and correctly. If you have any questions, feel free to contact our customer support team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field as prompted, making sure to select the correct options for your order. You can navigate through the form using the "Continue" and "Back" buttons. If you need to correct or change any information, click on the field's edit icon.

* **Order Form (1)** (`text`, optional): Please enter any additional notes or comments regarding your order in this field.
* **Shipping Address (2)** (`text`, optional): Enter your shipping address where you would like your order to be delivered.
* **Order Summary (3)** (`note`, optional): This is a text area where you can provide a brief overview of your order.
* **Select One (4)** (`select_one`, optional): Select one of the options provided to make a specific selection for your order.
	+ Options:
		- Option 1
		- Option 2
		- Option 3
* **Select Multiple (5)** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select multiple options to include in your order.
	+ Options:
		- Option 1
		- Option 2
		- Option 3
* **Order Date (6)** (`date`, optional): Enter the date you would like your order to be processed.
* **Order Time (7)** (`time`, optional): Enter the time of day you would like your order to be processed.
* **Order Note (8)** (`note`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments regarding your order.

## Tips
* Please double-check your information before submitting your order to avoid errors.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact our customer support team for assistance.
* Make sure to enter a valid date and time for your order date and time fields.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please include them in the "Order Note" field.
