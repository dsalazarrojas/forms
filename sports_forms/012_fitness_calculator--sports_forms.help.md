# Fitness Calculator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Calculator form is designed to gather information about your physical condition and activity level. Please provide accurate data for each field to get a comprehensive analysis of your fitness level.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form with your personal details and health metrics.
2. Select the correct unit of measurement for each field as per the provided options.
3. Ensure you answer each question truthfully and accurately.
4. Submit the form for analysis.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Age
* **Age** (`Age`, number, required): Enter your age in numbers.
*   Please fill this in to get the analysis of your age factor.

### Height
* **Height** (`height`, number, required): Enter your height in centimeters or meters.
*   You can use any unit, but ensure you select the correct unit in the field below.

### Weight
* **Weight** (`weight`, number, required): Enter your weight in kilograms.
*   This is an essential factor in calculating your body mass index.

### Resting Heart Rate
* **Resting Heart Rate** (`resting_heart_rate`, number, required): Enter your resting heart rate in beats per minute.
*   This measures how fast your heart beats when you're at rest.

### Max Heart Rate
* **Max Heart Rate** (`max_heart_rate`, number, required): Enter your maximum heart rate in beats per minute.
*   This measures how fast your heart beats at its maximum.

### Systolic Blood Pressure
* **Systolic Blood Pressure** (`systolic_blood_pressure`, number, required): Enter your systolic blood pressure in mmHg, mmmHg, or kPa.
*   You will select the correct unit for this field below.

### Diastolic Blood Pressure
* **Diastolic Blood Pressure** (`diastolic_blood_pressure`, number, required): Enter your diastolic blood pressure in mmHg, mmmHg, or kPa.
*   Ensure you select the correct unit for this field as well.

### Resting Blood Pressure
* **Resting Blood Pressure** (`resting_blood_pressure`, number, required): Enter your resting blood pressure in mmHg, mmmHg, or kPa.
*   This measures the pressure in your blood vessels at rest.

### Max Blood Pressure
* **Max Blood Pressure** (`max_blood_pressure`, number, required): Enter your maximum blood pressure in mmHg, mmmHg, or kPa.
*   This measures the pressure in your blood vessels at its maximum.

### Body Temperature
* **Body Temperature** (`body_temperature`, number, required): Enter your body temperature in C, F, or K.
*   Please select the correct unit for this field.

### Resting Oxygen Saturation
* **Resting Oxygen Saturation** (`resting_oxygen_saturation`, number, required): Enter your resting oxygen saturation in %.
*   This measures the oxygen levels in your blood at rest.

### Max Oxygen Saturation
* **Max Oxygen Saturation** (`max_oxygen_saturation`, number, required): Enter your maximum oxygen saturation in %.
*   This measures the oxygen levels in your blood at its maximum.

### Respiratory Rate
* **Respiratory Rate** (`respiratory_rate`, number, required): Enter your respiratory rate in beats per minute.
*   This measures how fast you breathe.

### Systolic Blood Pressure Unit
* **Systolic Blood Pressure Unit** (`systolic_blood_pressure_unit`, select_one, required): Select the unit for this field from mmHg, mmmHg, or kPa.
*   Ensure you select the correct unit for this field.

### Diastolic Blood Pressure Unit
* **Diastolic Blood Pressure Unit** (`diastolic_blood_pressure_unit`, select_one, required): Select the unit for this field from mmHg, mmmHg, or kPa.
*   Select the correct unit for this field as well.

### Resting Blood Pressure Unit
* **Resting Blood Pressure Unit** (`resting_blood_pressure_unit`, select_one, required): Select the unit for this field from mmHg, mmmHg, or kPa.
*   Ensure you select the correct unit for this field.

### Max Blood Pressure Unit
* **Max Blood Pressure Unit** (`max_blood_pressure_unit`, select_one, required): Select the unit for this field from mmHg, mmmHg, or kPa.
*   Select the correct unit for this field.

### Body Temperature Unit
* **Body Temperature Unit** (`body_temperature_unit`, select_one, required): Select the unit for this field from C, F, or K.
*   Ensure you select the correct unit for this field.

### Exercise Minutes
* **Exercise Minutes** (`exercise_minutes`, number, required): Enter the duration of your exercise in minutes.
*   This is essential for analysis.

### Exercise Intensity
* **Exercise Intensity** (`exercise_intensity`, select_one, required): Select how intense your exercise is from Lightly, Moderately, or Highly.
*   This is a key factor in calculating your fitness level.

### Exercise Frequency
* **Exercise Frequency** (`exercise_frequency`, select_one, required): Select how frequent your exercise is from Rarely, Occasionally, Frequently, or Often.
*   This is also essential for analysis.

### Exercise Duration
* **Exercise Duration** (`exercise_duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of your exercise in minutes.
*   This measures the overall exercise time.
