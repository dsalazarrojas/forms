# new_member_fitness_assessment_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The new_member_fitness_assessment_form is designed to collect essential information from new members, including personal and medical details. This form helps us ensure that we have accurate and up-to-date information about each member's fitness and medical status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
2. Provide your emergency contact's name in the "Emergency Contact Name" field.
3. Enter your emergency contact's phone number in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field.
4. Specify your relationship to your emergency contact in the "Relationship to Emergency Contact" field.
5. Enter your height and weight in their respective fields.
6. Provide any relevant medical information in the "Medical Info" fields (Medical Info 1-10).
7. Enter your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
8. Enter your Date of Membership in the "Date of Membership" field.
9. Provide any emergency information in the "Emergency Info" field.
10. List any medical conditions you have in the "Medical Conditions" field.
11. Declare any allergies you have in the "Allergies" field.
12. Leave any additional notes in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact information, including your address, phone number, and other relevant details.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`Emergency Contact Name`, `text`, required): Provide the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`Emergency Contact Phone`, `text`, required): Enter your emergency contact person's phone number.
* **Relationship to Emergency Contact** (`Relationship to Emergency Contact`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you are related to your emergency contact person.
	+ Options: Mom, Dad, Brother, Sister, Other
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`Emergency Contact Address`, `text`, optional): Enter your emergency contact's address.
* **Height** (`Height`, `number`, optional): Enter your height in inches or centimeters.
* **Weight** (`Weight`, `number`, required): Enter your weight in pounds or kilograms.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Date of Membership** (`Date of Membership`, `date`, required): Enter the date you joined the fitness club.
* **Emergency Info** (`Emergency Info`, `text`, optional): Provide any emergency contact information.
* **Medical Conditions** (`Medical Conditions`, `text`, optional): List any medical conditions you have.
* **Allergies** (`Allergies`, `text`, optional): Declare any allergies you have.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `text`, optional): Leave any additional notes about your health or fitness status.
* **Medical Info 1-10** (`Medical Info 1-10`, `text`, optional): Provide additional medical information, if necessary.
* **Medical Conditions** (`Medical Conditions`, `text`, optional): List any medical conditions you have.
