# Volleyball Open Gym Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a registration form for the Volleyball Open Gym program, designed to collect essential details from participants to ensure their safe and successful participation in the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Participant Information page, providing your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select your session date and time in the Session Date and Time page.
3. On the Additional Information page, enter your participant name, emergency contact name, and relationship with the contact person. You can also add any medical conditions and notes you want to share with the program staff.
4. Finally, review and sign the form to confirm your participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Participant Information
* **Participant Name**: Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed in the program.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to receive important updates and notifications from the program.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number to enable easy contact with the program staff.
* **Gender**: Select your gender to help us better understand your needs and preferences.
* **Experience**: Describe your volleyball experience, including any previous experience playing volleyball or training in a specific area.

### Session Information
* **Session Date**: Select the date you plan to participate in the session.
* **Session Time**: Choose the time slot you would like to attend.

### Emergency Contact Information
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person you have designated as your emergency contact.
* **Relationship**: Select how you are related to the emergency contact (e.g., Parent, Spouse, Child, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Notes**: Add any additional notes or instructions for the emergency contact.

### Medical Information
* **Participant Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions or allergies you have that we should be aware of.
* **Participant Medical Notes**: Add any additional medical notes or instructions for the program staff.
* **Participant Signature**: Select how you would like to sign the form (in person, digitally, or no signature required).

### Tips
* Please be accurate and honest when filling out this form to ensure your safe and successful participation in the program.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
