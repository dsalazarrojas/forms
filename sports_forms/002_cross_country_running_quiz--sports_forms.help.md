# Cross Country Running Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from athletes who have participated in cross-country running events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer the questions honestly and accurately, as they will help us understand your experience and preferences.
2. Choose the correct options for each field from the provided lists.
3. Enter any additional comments, notes, or information in the text fields.
