# Athlete Travel Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Athlete Travel Experience Survey is designed to collect feedback from athletes on their experiences with travel arrangements for sports-related events or tournaments.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, fill in your Athlete Full Name in the "Athlete Full Name" field.
2.  If you have an athlete ID number, please enter it in the "Athlete ID Number" field. This is optional, but it helps sports organizers identify your responses.
3.  Select your primary sport from the "Sport" field.
4.  Enter the name of the team you are affiliated with in the "Team Name" field.
5.  Choose a date for the survey from the calendar in the "Survey Date" field.
6.  Describe the destination of your recent trip in the "Recent Trip Destination" field.
7.  Enter the date of your trip in the "Trip Date" field.
8.  Select the purpose of your trip from the "Trip Purpose" field.
9.  Choose your method of travel from the "Method of Travel" field.
10. Rate your travel comfort in the "Travel Comfort Rating" field.
11. Select your accommodation type from the "Type of Accommodation" field.
12. Rate the quality of your accommodation in the "Accommodation Quality" field.
13. Choose how many people you shared a room with from the "Room Sharing Situation" field.
14. Select how meals were arranged for you from the "Meal Arrangements" field.
15. Rate the quality of the meals provided in the "Meal Quality" field.
16. Rate your sleep quality in the "Sleep Quality During Trip" field.
17. Select all that apply from the "Travel Issues Experienced" field.
18. Select all that apply from the "Accommodation Issues Experienced" field.
19. Describe the best part of your travel experience in the "Best Part of Travel Experience" field.
20. Describe the worst part of your travel experience in the "Worst Part of Travel Experience" field.
21. Suggest improvements for travel arrangements in the "Suggested Travel Improvements" field.
22. Suggest improvements for accommodation in the "Suggested Accommodation Improvements" field. (Optional)
23. Suggest improvements for meals in the "Suggested Meal Improvements" field. (Optional)
24. Rate your overall satisfaction with the travel experience in the "Overall Travel Satisfaction" field.
25. Select whether or not you would travel with similar arrangements again in the "Willingness to Travel Similar Arrangements" field.
26. Provide any additional comments about your travel experience in the "Additional Comments" field. (Optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Athlete Full Name**: Your full name as an athlete.
    \*   **Athlete ID Number (Optional)**: Your student or team identification number, if you have one.
    \*   **Sport**: Primary sport you participate in.
    \*   **Team Name**: Name of the team you are affiliated with.
    \*   **Survey Date**: Date of survey completion.
    \*   **Recent Trip Destination**: Destination of your recent trip.
    \*   **Trip Date**: Date of the trip.
    \*   **Trip Purpose**: Reason for travel.
    \*   **Method of Travel**: How you traveled to the destination.
    \*   **Travel Comfort Rating**: Rate your travel comfort.
    \*   **Type of Accommodation**: Where you stayed during the trip.
    \*   **Accommodation Quality**: Rate your lodging experience.
    \*   **Room Sharing Situation**: How many people shared a room with you.
    \*   **Meal Arrangements**: How meals were arranged for you.
    \*   **Meal Quality**: Rate your meal quality.
    \*   **Sleep Quality During Trip**: Rate your sleep quality during the trip.
    \*   **Travel Issues Experienced**: Select all travel issues you experienced.
    \*   **Accommodation Issues Experienced**: Select all accommodation issues you experienced.
    \*   **Best Part of Travel Experience**: Describe the best part of your travel experience.
    \*   **Worst Part of Travel Experience**: Describe the worst part of your travel experience.
    \*   **Suggested Travel Improvements**: Suggest improvements to travel arrangements.
    \*   **Suggested Accommodation Improvements**: Suggest improvements to accommodation. (Optional)
    \*   **Suggested Meal Improvements**: Suggest improvements to meals. (Optional)
    \*   **Overall Travel Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with travel.
    \*   **Willingness to Travel Similar Arrangements**: Indicate your willingness to travel with similar arrangements again.
    \*   **Additional Comments**: Any other feedback about your travel experience. (Optional)

## Tips

*   Please answer honestly and as thoroughly as possible.
*   Ensure you fill in all required fields.
*   You can provide feedback about any aspect of your travel experience.
*   You can suggest improvements for travel, accommodation, and meal arrangements.
