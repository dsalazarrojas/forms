# Metaphysical Consultation Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Metaphysical Consultation Feedback form is used to collect post-session insights, satisfaction ratings, and recommendations from clients after a metaphysical consultation. This form helps us understand our clients' experiences and gather feedback to improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on "Submit" to access the form.
2. The form is divided into two pages. Please complete each page according to the instructions below.
3. On each page, fill in the required fields and provide as much detail as possible for the optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Post-Session Insights (Page 1)
* **Post-Session Insights** (`metaphysical_consultation_feedback_page_1`, text, optional): This field is for any observations, notes, or insights you want to share about your experience during the consultation. Please be as detailed as possible to help us improve our services.

### Overall Satisfaction Rating (Page 1)
* **Overall Satisfaction Rating** (`satisfaction_rating`, number, optional): Select a number between 1 and 5 to rate your overall satisfaction with the consultation. This rating will help us understand the level of satisfaction you experienced.

### Recommendations (Page 1)
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, please share them with us here.

### Improvement Notes (Page 2)
* **Improvement Notes** (`improvement_notes`, text, optional): If you have any specific areas you would like us to improve in future consultations, please provide detailed notes here.

### Overall Satisfaction Rating (Page 2)
* **Overall Satisfaction Rating** (`satisfaction_rating`, number, optional): Select a number between 1 and 5 to rate your overall satisfaction with the consultation. This rating will help us understand the level of satisfaction you experienced.

### Tips
* Please be honest and thorough in your feedback. Your input is invaluable in helping us provide better services.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We appreciate your time and effort in providing feedback.
