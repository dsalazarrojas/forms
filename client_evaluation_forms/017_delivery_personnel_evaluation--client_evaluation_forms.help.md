# Delivery Personnel Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Delivery Personnel Evaluation" form is used to assess the performance of delivery personnel in several key areas. This guide will walk you through the form's purpose, how to complete it, and a field-by-field explanation of each section.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the questions and sections.
2. Answer each question honestly and accurately based on your observation and experience with the delivery personnel.
3. Ensure you complete all required fields.
4. If any section is not applicable, please mark it as "Not Applicable" or "N/A".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Punctuality (1)
* Punctuality: Evaluate the delivery personnel's punctuality, with higher scores indicating better time management skills. 

### Professionalism (2)
* Professionalism: Assess the delivery personnel's professionalism, considering their attitude, behavior, and work habits. A high score indicates strong work habits and a positive attitude.

### Communication (3)
* Communication: Rate the delivery personnel's communication skills, including their ability to communicate effectively with clients, colleagues, and superiors. A high score reflects excellent communication skills.

### Overall Delivery Satisfaction (4)
* Overall Delivery Satisfaction: Evaluate the delivery personnel's overall performance, taking into account their punctuality, professionalism, and communication skills. This score is required to be completed.

### Comments (5)
* Comments: Provide any additional comments or observations about the delivery personnel's performance. This is an optional field for any additional feedback.

### Email (6)
* Email: This field is for the delivery personnel's email address. Please ensure it's accurate and up-to-date.

### Phone (7)
* Phone: This field is for the delivery personnel's contact phone number. Please ensure it's accurate and up-to-date.

## Tips

* Ensure you answer each question accurately and honestly based on your experience and observation.
* Complete all required fields to ensure an accurate evaluation.
* If any section is not applicable, please mark it as "Not Applicable" or "N/A".
* Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
