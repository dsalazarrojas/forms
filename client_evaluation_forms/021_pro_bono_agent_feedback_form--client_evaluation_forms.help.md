# Pro Bono Agent Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pro Bono Agent Feedback Form is a tool used to gather feedback from clients about their experience with a Pro Bono program. This feedback will help the Pro Bono team understand the effectiveness of their services and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully to understand the questions and their relevance.
2. Answer each question based on your experience with the Pro Bono program.
3. Ensure that you provide honest and constructive feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Feedback Form** (Page 1, Text, Optional): This page is a text area where you can provide any comments or feedback about your experience with the Pro Bono program.
* **Service Quality** (Page 2, Select One, Optional): Choose one of the following options to rate the quality of service you received:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Bad
* **Outcomes** (Page 3, Select Multiple, Optional): Select all the outcomes that you achieved through the Pro Bono program:
	+ Outcome 1
	+ Outcome 2
	+ Outcome 3
* **Training** (Page 4, Text, Optional): This is a text field where you can describe any training or guidance provided by the Pro Bono team.
* **Additional Comments** (Page 5, Text, Optional): This is an open-ended text field for any additional comments or feedback you may have about the Pro Bono program.
* **Email** (Page 6, Email, Required): Enter your email address so that the Pro Bono team can contact you for further discussion.
* **Phone** (Page 7, Text, Optional): This is a text field for any phone number you may want to provide.
* **Submit** (Page 8, Date, Optional): This field is not currently used, but it may be a future development.
* **Time** (Page 9, Time, Optional): This field is not currently used, but it may be a future development.
* **Date** (Page 10, Date, Optional): This field is not currently used, but it may be a future development.
* **Page 11** (Page 11, Number, Optional): This field is not currently used, and it's not clear what it's intended for.
* **Page 12** (Page 12, Number, Optional): This field is not currently used, and it's not clear what it's intended for.

## Tips

* Be honest and constructive in your feedback.
* Ensure that your email address is up-to-date and correct.
* If you're unsure about any question, please ask the Pro Bono team for clarification.
* Review each page carefully to ensure that you're providing relevant feedback.
