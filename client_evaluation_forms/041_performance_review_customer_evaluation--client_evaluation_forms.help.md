# Performance Review Customer Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the performance of a customer over a specific period of time. It is used to gather feedback on the customer's overall performance, representative performance, and customer satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete the form:

1. Choose the representative who completed the performance review.
2. Select the performance level of the customer.
3. Enter the rating for the customer's performance.
4. Enter any comments about the customer's performance.
5. Choose the date and time the form was completed.
6. Enter the form ID.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Representative** (Select One): Choose the representative who completed the performance review.
* **Performance Level** (Select Multiple): Select the performance level of the customer.
* **Rating** (Number): Enter a rating for the customer's performance, on a scale from 1 to 4.
* **Comments** (Note): Enter any comments about the customer's performance.
* **Date Completed** (Date): Enter the date the form was completed.
* **Time Completed** (Time): Enter the time the form was completed.
* **Form ID** (Text): Enter a unique ID for the form.
* **Customer Evaluation** (Text): This field is not used.
* **Representative Performance Level** (Select Multiple): Select the performance level of the representative.
* **Representative Rating** (Select Multiple): Enter a rating for the representative's performance.
* **Representative Comments** (Note): Enter any comments about the representative's performance.
* **Overall Rating** (Select Multiple): Enter an overall rating for the customer's performance.
* **Completed By** (Select One): Choose the person who completed the form.
* **Completed Date** (Date): Enter the date the form was completed.
* **Completed Time** (Time): Enter the time the form was completed.
* **Customer Rating** (Select Multiple): This field is not used.
* **Customer Comments** (Note): This field is not used.
* **Customer Email** (Email): This field is not used.
* **Customer Phone** (Text): This field is not used.
* **Assigned Representative** (Select One): Choose the representative assigned to the customer.
* **Customer Rating Options** (Select Multiple): Select the options for the customer's performance.
* **Overall Performance Level** (Select Multiple): This field is not used.
* **Customer Rating Options** (Select Multiple): This field is not used.
