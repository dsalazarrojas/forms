# Biopsychosocial Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Biopsychosocial Assessment Form is a comprehensive evaluation tool used to gather information about a patient's biopsychosocial history. This form helps clinicians assess the patient's physical health, mental health, and social factors to identify potential risks and develop personalized treatment plans.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the questions and sections.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Be thorough in providing all necessary information, especially regarding medical and mental health history.
4. If you are unsure about any question, ask for clarification or guidance from the clinician.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (1): Enter patient details, including name, date of birth, and contact information.
* **First Name** (2): Enter the patient's first name.
* **Last Name** (3): Enter the patient's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (4): Enter the patient's birth date in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Gender** (5): Select the patient's gender identity.
* **Marital Status** (6): Select the patient's current marital status.
* **Phone Number** (7): Enter the patient's contact phone number.
* **Emergency Contact** (8): Enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact.
* **Assessment Date** (9): Enter the date of this assessment in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Biological Factors** (10): Provide a brief description of any relevant physical health issues.
* **Current Medical Conditions** (11): List any ongoing medical issues or conditions.
* **Chronic Illnesses** (12): List any chronic health conditions.
* **Current Medications** (13): List all current medications.
* **Sleep Patterns** (14): Select your typical sleep quality and patterns.
* **Appetite Changes** (15): Select any recent changes in appetite.
* **Energy Level** (16): Rate your typical energy levels.
* **Substance Use** (17): Provide information about any alcohol or drug use.
* **Alcohol Use** (18): Select the frequency of alcohol use.
* **Tobacco Use** (19): Select your current tobacco use status.
* **Recreational Drug Use** (20): Select any recreational drug use.
* **Psychological Factors** (21): Provide a brief description of any mental health issues or concerns.
* **Current Mood** (22): Select how you would describe your current mood.
* **Anxiety Level** (23): Rate your anxiety levels over the past 2 weeks.
* **Depression Level** (24): Rate your feelings of depression over the past 2 weeks.
* **Stress Level** (25): Rate your current stress levels.
* **Mental Health Diagnoses** (26): List any previous mental health diagnoses.
* **Previous Therapy or Counseling** (27): Select whether you have had therapy or counseling before.
* **Suicidal Thoughts** (28): Select whether you have had suicidal thoughts.
* **Coping Mechanisms** (29): Describe how you typically cope with stress or difficulties.
* **Social Factors** (30): Provide a brief description of any social or environmental factors that may impact your health.
* **Living Situation** (31): Select your current living situation.
* **Employment Status** (32): Select your current employment status.
* **Work Satisfaction** (33): Rate your satisfaction with your work.
* **Financial Stress** (34): Rate your level of financial concern.
* **Social Support System** (35): Select the strength of your support system.
* **Quality of Relationships** (36): Rate the quality of your relationships.
* **Family History** (37): Provide any relevant family medical or mental health history.
* **Cultural or Religious Factors** (38): Provide any relevant cultural or religious factors that may impact your health.
* **Recent Life Changes** (39): List any significant recent life changes.
* **Assessment Summary** (40): This field is for the clinician's use.
* **Risk Level** (41): Select the overall risk assessment.
* **Immediate Intervention Needed** (42): Select whether urgent care is required.
* **Treatment Recommendations** (43): Suggest any interventions or recommendations.
* **Clinician Name** (44): Enter the name of the assessing clinician.
* **Clinician Signature** (45): Confirm the assessment completion by the clinician.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your responses to ensure accurate assessment.
* If you have any questions or concerns, ask the clinician for guidance.
* Review the form carefully to ensure you have answered all necessary questions.
* Do not leave any section blank without explanation or clarification.
* The clinician will review and discuss your responses with you to ensure accurate and personalized care.
