# Supervisor And Consultant Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supervisor and Consultant Evaluation form is used for supervisors and consultants to evaluate each other's performance in a working relationship. This helps to gather feedback on the effectiveness of the partnership.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `supervisor_name` field with the name of the supervisor evaluating the consultant.
2. Enter the `consultant_name` field with the name of the consultant being evaluated.
3. Rate the performance of the supervisor and consultant using the `supervisor_rating` and `consultant_rating` fields, respectively. These fields are numerical and allow for a rating from 1 to 5.
4. Provide any additional comments in the `comments` field.
5. Click the `Submit` button to submit the form.
6. Fill in the `date_submitted` field with the date the form was submitted.
7. Fill in the `time_submitted` field with the time the form was submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### supervisor_and_consultant_evaluation_form
* **Form Header**: This is the title of the form, which is automatically generated and not editable.
### supervisor_name
* **Supervisor's Name**: Enter the name of the supervisor evaluating the consultant.
### consultant_name
* **Consultant's Name**: Enter the name of the consultant being evaluated.
### supervisor_rating
* **Supervisor's Rating**: Rate the performance of the supervisor using a number between 1 and 5.
### consultant_rating
* **Consultant's Rating**: Rate the performance of the consultant using a number between 1 and 5.
### comments
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback on the performance of the supervisor and consultant.
### submit
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit the form.
### date_submitted
* **Date Submitted**: Enter the date the form was submitted.
### time_submitted
* **Time Submitted**: Enter the time the form was submitted.
### supervisor_comments
* **Supervisor's Comments**: Provide any additional comments from the supervisor.
### consultant_comments
* **Consultant's Comments**: Provide any additional comments from the consultant.
### submitted_by
* **Submitted By**: This field is automatically populated with the user who submitted the form.
### supervisor_rating_score
* **Supervisor's Rating Score**: This field is automatically populated with the supervisor's rating score.
### consultant_rating_score
* **Consultant's Rating Score**: This field is automatically populated with the consultant's rating score.
### supervisor_and_consultant_evaluation_form_submission_details
* **Submission Details**: This field is automatically populated with submission details.
### submitted_to
* **Submitted To**: This field is automatically populated with the name of the supervisor who submitted the form.

Tips
- Ensure that the form is used correctly to avoid duplication or misallocation of feedback.
- Review and follow up on the feedback provided to ensure continuous improvement.
