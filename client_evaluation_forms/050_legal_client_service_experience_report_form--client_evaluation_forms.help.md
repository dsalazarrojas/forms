# Legal Client Service Experience Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from clients on their experience with your organization, helping us to improve our service delivery.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out the form for every client interaction, regardless of its success or failure.
2.  Ensure that you provide your name and contact number so that we can get in touch with you to discuss your experience and gather more information if needed.
3.  Answer every question thoroughly, as it will help us to better understand your experience and improve our services accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client Name** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide your name as it appears on your contract or records.
*   **Contact Number** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide your contact number so that we can get in touch with you to discuss your experience and gather more information if needed.
*   **Service Type** (`3`, `select_one`, required/optional): Please select the type of service you received from our team.
*   **Service Provided** (`4`, `text`, required/optional): Please describe the service you received from our team.
*   **Date of Service** (`5`, `date`, required/optional): Please provide the date on which the service was provided.
*   **Time of Service** (`6`, `time`, required/optional): Please provide the time of service.
*   **Location of Service** (`7`, `text`, required/optional): Please describe the location where the service was provided.
*   **Service Rating** (`8`, `number`, required/optional): Please rate the service provided, from 1 to 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
*   **Feedback** (`9`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide any feedback or comments about your experience with our team.
*   **Comments** (`10`, `note`, required/optional): Please provide any additional comments or suggestions about your experience with our team.
*   **Assigned to** (`11`, `select_one`, required/optional): Please select who was assigned to you for the service.
*   **Client Satisfaction** (`12`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Please select how satisfied you are with the service provided, from options 1 to 3.

Note: Please ensure that you are filling out this form for a specific interaction with our team, and not for general feedback or suggestions.
