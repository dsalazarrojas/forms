# Modeling Consultant Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from clients about the services provided by modeling consultants, capturing details about the consultant, project, and evaluation date. It aims to assess the performance of consultants in various areas and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the consultant's performance level for each area (communication skills, work style, time management, and overall satisfaction).
2. Enter the project title and other relevant details (consultant name, project title 2, and submission date).
3. Provide feedback about the consultant's performance and any comments (Feedback Comment).
4. Enter the submission date (evaluation date, submission date).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consultant** (`consultant_id`): Select the consultant you are evaluating. The options are: Highly Satisfactory, Satisfactory, and Not Satisfactory.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`): Enter the date of the evaluation. Format: MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Consultant Name** (`consultant_name`): Enter the name of the consultant being evaluated.
* **Project Title** (`project_title`): Enter the title of the project being evaluated.
* **Feedback** (`feedback_comment`): Enter any comments or feedback about the consultant's performance.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`): Rate the overall satisfaction of the consultant's performance. The options are: Excellent, Very Good, Fair, Poor, and Very Poor.
* **Communication Skills** (`communication_skills`): Rate the consultant's communication skills. The options are: Highly Satisfactory, Satisfactory, and Not Satisfactory.
* **Work Style** (`work_style`): Rate the consultant's work style. The options are: Highly Satisfactory, Satisfactory, and Not Satisfactory.
* **Time Management** (`time_management`): Rate the consultant's time management. The options are: Highly Satisfactory, Satisfactory, and Not Satisfactory.
* **Project Manager Skills** (`project_manager_skills`): Rate the consultant's project management skills. The options are: Highly Satisfactory, Satisfactory, and Not Satisfactory.
* **Problem Solving** (`problem_solving`): Rate the consultant's problem solving skills. The options are: Highly Satisfactory, Satisfactory, and Not Satisfactory.
* **Team Collaboration** (`team_collaboration`): Rate the consultant's team collaboration. The options are: Highly Satisfactory, Satisfactory, and Not Satisfactory.
* **Feedback Comment** (`feedback_comment`): Enter any additional comments about the consultant's performance.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`): Enter the name of the person submitting the evaluation.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`): Enter the date of the submission.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`): Rate the overall satisfaction of the consultant's performance. The options are: Highly Satisfactory, Satisfactory, and Not Satisfactory.
