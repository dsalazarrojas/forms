# post_case_resolution_client_feedback_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect client feedback after a case resolution has been achieved, helping our team to refine our processes and provide better service in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer all the questions honestly.
2. Provide your name and any relevant contact information.
3. Rate your satisfaction with our service, using the scale of 1-5.
4. Identify any areas for improvement.
5. Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
6. Submit the form to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction (Note)**: A brief introduction to your feedback. Please feel free to write as much or as little as you'd like.
* **Name (Text)**: Please enter your name so we can personally address your feedback.
* **Feedback (Text)**: Describe the experience you've had with our service, including the resolution of your case. Be as detailed as possible.
* **Rating (Number)**: Rate your satisfaction with our service, using the scale of 1-5. 1 means not satisfied, 5 means highly satisfied.
* **Improvement Areas (Text)**: Identify any areas where we could improve our service.
* **Comments (Text)**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have.
* **Contact Information (Text)**: Enter any contact information you'd like us to have, so we can personally address your feedback.
* **Email (Text)**: Enter your email address so we can follow up with you directly.
* **Phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number so we can follow up with you directly.
* **Submit (Text)**: Click the "Submit" button to send your feedback to us.
