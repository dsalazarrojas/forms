# Daily Cleaning Staff Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Cleaning Staff Evaluation form is designed to assess the performance of cleaning staff on a daily basis. This form helps to identify areas of improvement, recognize good work, and provide feedback to staff members. It's an essential tool for maintaining high-quality cleaning services and promoting a positive work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the evaluation date from the calendar provided.
2. Enter the name of the cleaning staff member being evaluated.
3. Choose the overall performance of the staff member (Excellent, Good, or Poor).
4. Evaluate the staff member's communication skills by entering any relevant feedback.
5. Assess the staff member's time management by providing a brief description.
6. Rate the staff member's workmanship (Select "On Time" or "Ahead of Schedule").
7. Select the staff member's attendance status for the day (On Time, Late, or Absent).
8. Provide feedback on safety precautions taken by the staff member.
9. Add any suggestions for improvement or additional comments.
10. Enter any evaluation comments about the staff member's performance.
11. Suggest a recommended action for the staff member's development.
12. Enter the recommended date for the action to be taken.
13. Mark the completed status of the evaluation (Completed or Not Completed).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Evaluation Date** (`Evaluation Date`, Date, Required: False): The date of the evaluation. Select the date when this evaluation was conducted.
* **Staff Name** (`cleaning_staff_name`, Text, Required: False): The name of the cleaning staff member being evaluated.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, Date, Required: False): The date of the last evaluation (for reference).
* **Overall Performance** (`overall_performance`, Select One, Required: False): A subjective rating of the staff member's overall performance (Excellent, Good, or Poor).
* **Communication Skills** (`communication_skills`, Text, Required: False): Any feedback or comments about the staff member's communication skills.
* **Time Management** (`time_management`, Text, Required: False): A brief description of how well the staff member managed their time.
* **Workmanship** (`workmanship`, Select Multiple, Required: False): Rate the staff member's workmanship as either "On Time" or "Ahead of Schedule".
* **Attendance** (`attendance`, Select Multiple, Required: False): Select the staff member's attendance status for the day (On Time, Late, or Absent).
* **Safety Precautions** (`safety_precautions`, Text, Required: False): Feedback on any safety precautions taken by the staff member.
* **Suggestion** (`suggestion`, Text, Required: False): Any suggestions or comments for the staff member's improvement.
* **Comments** (`comments`, Text, Required: False): Additional comments or feedback about the staff member's performance.
* **Evaluation Comments** (`evaluation_comments`, Text, Required: False): Comments or feedback about the staff member's performance.
* **Recommended Action** (`recommended_action`, Text, Required: False): Suggested actions for the staff member's development.
* **Recommended Date** (`recommended_date`, Date, Required: False): The date for the recommended action to be taken.
* **Completed Status** (`completed_status`, Select One, Required: False): Mark whether the evaluation has been completed or not.
