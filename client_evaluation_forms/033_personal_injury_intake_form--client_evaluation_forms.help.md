# personal_injury_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information related to personal injuries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your relevant information. You can find each field's description below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Info (1)**: Provide your name, address, and any other identifying information.
* **Case Details (2)**: Describe the circumstances surrounding your personal injury, including the date, time, and location of the incident.
* **Medical Info (3)**: Share your medical history, including any pre-existing conditions or previous injuries.
* **Witness Info (4)**: If applicable, provide information about witnesses, including their names and contact details.
* **Vehicle Info (5)**: If the incident involved a vehicle, provide details about the vehicle and any damage it sustained.
* **Contact Info (6)**: Enter your contact information, including phone number and email address.
* **Medical Provider Info (7)**: If you have received medical treatment, provide information about your healthcare provider.
* **Witness List (8)**: If you have identified witnesses, list their names and contact details.
* **Witness Statement (9)**: If you have statements from witnesses, provide them here.
* **Vehicle Details (10)**: If the incident involved a vehicle, provide more details about the vehicle and its condition.
* **Vehicle Damage (11)**: If the vehicle sustained any damage, describe it here.
* **Medical Expertise (12)**: If applicable, describe any specialized medical expertise you have or require.
* **Medical Treatment (13)**: If you have received treatment for your injury, describe it here.
* **Insurance Info (14)**: If you have insurance, provide information about your policy.
* **Medical Records (15)**: If you have medical records that are relevant to your case, upload them here.
* **Contact Info 2 (16)**: Enter any additional contact information.
