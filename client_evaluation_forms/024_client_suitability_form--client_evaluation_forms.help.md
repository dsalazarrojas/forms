# Client Suitability Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form helps our financial advisors assess your suitability for various financial products or services based on your demographic details, financial situation, and investment history.

## How To Complete This Form

### Section 1: Client Information

- **Full Name**: Please provide your full name, as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
- **Email Address**: Enter your current email address.
- **Phone Number**: Enter your current phone number.
### Section 2: Financial Information

- **Employment Status**: Please select your current employment status:
  - Employed
  - Self-Employed
  - Retired
  - Unemployed
  - Student
  - Other
- **Annual Income Range**: Please choose your annual income range:
  - Under $25,000
  - $25,000 - $50,000
  - $50,000 - $100,000
  - $100,000 - $250,000
  - $250,000 - $500,000
  - Over $500,000
- **Estimated Liquid Assets**: Please select the range of your estimated liquid assets:
  - Under $10,000
  - $10,000 - $50,000
  - $50,000 - $100,000
  - $100,000 - $250,000
  - $250,000 - $500,000
  - Over $500,000
- **Estimated Net Worth**: Please choose the range of your estimated net worth:
  - Under $100,000
  - $100,000 - $500,000
  - $500,000 - $1,000,000
  - $1,000,000 - $5,000,000
  - Over $5,000,000
- **Investment Experience**: Please select your investment experience level:
  - No Experience
  - Limited Experience
  - Moderate Experience
  - Extensive Experience

### Section 3: Investment Goals and Objectives

- **Primary Financial Goal**: Please select your primary financial goal:
  - Capital Preservation
  - Income Generation
  - Growth
  - Retirement Planning
  - Education Planning
  - Estate Planning
  - Other
- **Investment Timeline**: Please choose your investment timeline:
  - Less than 1 year
  - 1-3 years
  - 3-5 years
  - 5-10 years
  - 10+ years

### Section 4: Risk Assessment

- **Risk Tolerance**: Please select how you would describe your risk appetite:
  - Very Conservative
  - Conservative
  - Moderate
  - Aggressive
  - Very Aggressive
- **Risk Comfort**: Please choose how you would react to a 20% decrease in your investment value:
  - Would not tolerate this
  - Would be uncomfortable
  - Neutral
  - Could accept this
  - Could tolerate this

### Section 5: Current Investments and Liabilities

- **Do you currently have any investments?**: Please answer:
  - Yes
  - No
- **Types of investments currently held**: Select all that apply:
  - Stocks
  - Bonds
  - Mutual Funds
  - ETFs
  - Real Estate
  - Retirement Accounts
  - Insurance Products
  - Commodities
  - Other
- **Approximate current investment portfolio value**: Please choose your approximate current investment portfolio value:
  - Under $50,000
  - $50,000 - $100,000
  - $100,000 - $250,000
  - $250,000 - $500,000
  - Over $500,000

### Section 6: Liabilities and Obligations

- **Do you have a mortgage?**: Please answer:
  - Yes
  - No
- **Other outstanding debts**: Please select:
  - No
  - Under $50,000
  - $50,000 - $100,000
  - Over $100,000

### Section 7: Product Suitability

- **Do you understand the products or services being proposed?**: Please answer:
  - Yes
  - No
  - Partially
- **Have all your questions been answered to your satisfaction?**: Please select:
  - Yes
  - No
- **I acknowledge that this assessment was conducted and I received appropriate suitability advice**: Please answer:
  - Yes
  - No
