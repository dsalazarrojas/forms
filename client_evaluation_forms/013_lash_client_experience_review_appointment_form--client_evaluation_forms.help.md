# Lash Client Experience Review Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and evaluation from clients after a service appointment at the salon. It helps to ensure that the services provided meet the client's expectations and to identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please select the correct response for each question based on your experience with the service provided.
2. If you have any notes or comments about your experience, please type them in the text boxes provided.
3. Please submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Lash Services** (`lash_services`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Please select "Yes" if you received good service and met your expectations.
	+ Please select "No" if you did not receive good service or did not meet your expectations.
* **Technician Rating** (`technician_rating`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Please rate the quality of the service provided by selecting "Good" or "Fair".
* **Technician Name** (`technician_name`, text, required: false):
	+ Please enter the name of the technician who provided your service.
* **Technician Notes** (`technician_notes`, text, required: false):
	+ Please enter any notes or comments you may have about your experience with the technician.
  Note: The notes field is a free-text field where you can enter any additional information you'd like to share with the salon staff.
