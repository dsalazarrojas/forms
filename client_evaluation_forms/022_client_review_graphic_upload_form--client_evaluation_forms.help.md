# Client Review & Graphic Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Review & Graphic Upload Form is a critical component of our design process. It captures client feedback, review, and approval status for designs submitted to them. This form helps maintain consistency and ensures that all stakeholders have a clear understanding of the project's progress and status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have reviewed the designs and provided detailed and specific feedback.
2. Fill out the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
3. Select the correct options for each field as they apply to your feedback.
4. If additional comments or notes are needed, please provide them in the "Additional Comments or Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Name**: This field is used to identify the project name associated with the review. Enter the name of the project as it appears on the project management system.
* **Client Name**: This field is used to capture the client's name. Enter the name of the client as it appears in the project management system.
* **Client Email**: This field is used to capture the client's email address. Enter the email address associated with the client's account.
* **Review Round Number**: This field is used to indicate which review round the design is for (e.g., First, Second, Third, etc.). Select the correct review round from the options provided.
* **Design Submission Date**: This field is used to capture the date the design was submitted to the client. Enter the date in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **Number of Concepts Submitted**: This field is used to indicate the number of design concepts submitted to the client. Enter the number of concepts submitted.
* **Design Style Preferences**: This field is used to capture the client's design style preferences. Select all applicable options that apply to the client's preferences.
* **Quality of Feedback Provided**: This field is used to indicate the quality of the feedback provided by the client. Select the option that best describes the feedback.
* **Strengths of Design(s)**: This field is used to capture the strengths of the design. Enter a brief description of what worked well.
* **Revisions Needed**: This field is used to capture any revisions needed for the design. Enter a brief description of the revisions required.
* **Overall Direction and Feedback**: This field is used to capture any general comments or feedback about the design direction. Enter a brief description of any comments or feedback.
* **Design Alignment with Brand**: This field is used to capture how well the design aligns with the brand's vision. Select the option that best describes the alignment.
* **Approval Status**: This field is used to indicate the current approval status of the design. Select the correct option that best describes the current status.
* **Preferred Timeline for Revisions**: This field is used to capture the client's desired timeline for revisions. Enter the date when the revisions are required.
* **Upload Revised Artwork File**: This field is used to upload the revised artwork file. Please ensure that the file is in a valid format and is related to the review round number.
* **Additional Comments or Notes**: This field is used to capture any additional comments or notes about the design process. Enter any relevant information that may be helpful for the designer's understanding.
