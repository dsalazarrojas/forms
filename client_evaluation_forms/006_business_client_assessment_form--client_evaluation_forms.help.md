# Business Client Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Business Client Assessment Form is designed to gather information about a client's business, goals, and challenges. This form helps us understand their needs and tailor our services to provide the best support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through each page and fill in the required and optional fields as instructed. Make sure to select the correct options for each field and provide clear information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: A required text field for the client's full name, including business or individual names.
* **Email Address**: A required text field for the client's primary contact email.
* **Phone Number**: A required text field for the client's business contact number.
* **Company Name**: A required text field for the client's organization name.
* **Industry Type**: A select-one field for the client's industry type, choose one from the options (Technology, Healthcare, Finance, Retail, Manufacturing, Services, or Other).
* **Company Size**: A select-one field for the client's company size, choose one from the options (Startup, Small, Medium, or Large).
* **Annual Revenue Range**: A select-one field for the client's approximate annual revenue, choose one from the options (Under 100k, 100k - 500k, 500k - 1M, 1M - 5M, 5M+, or Prefer Not to Say).
* **Years in Business**: A required number field for how long the client has been established.
* **Key Business Strengths**: A select-multiple field for the client's key business strengths, choose one or more from the options (Customer Service, Product Quality, Innovation, Team Expertise, Market Position, Brand Reputation).
* **Growth Challenges**: A select-multiple field for the client's main growth challenges, choose one or more from the options (Market Competition, Limited Budget, Staffing Issues, Operations Efficiency, Customer Acquisition, Technology Integration).
* **Primary Business Goals**: A select-multiple field for the client's primary business goals, choose one or more from the options (Revenue Growth, Market Expansion, Product Development, Customer Retention, Cost Reduction, Team Development).
* **Services of Interest**: A select-multiple field for the services the client is interested in, choose one or more from the options (Strategy Consulting, Operations Improvement, Financial Planning, Marketing Strategy, Technology Solutions, Training Programs).
* **Implementation Timeline**: A select-one field for when the client wants to start, choose one from the options (Immediate, 1-3 Months, 3-6 Months, 6+ Months).
* **Estimated Budget Range**: A select-one field for the client's investment capability, choose one from the options (Under 10k, 10k - 25k, 25k - 50k, 50k+, To be determined).
* **Additional Comments**: A text field for any other relevant information the client would like to provide.
