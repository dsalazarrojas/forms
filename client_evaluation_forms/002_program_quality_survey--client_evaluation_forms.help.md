# Program Quality Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Program Quality Survey is designed to collect feedback on the quality of services provided by your organization. It is intended for employees or agents familiar with the services offered, who can accurately assess and report on the quality of these services. This form helps your organization understand how well it is meeting the needs of its service recipients and areas of operation, and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form effectively, follow these steps:

- Read the form carefully and understand the purpose and fields below.
- Select the service area that best describes the service you are evaluating.
- Choose how you rate the quality of services for each selected service area using the provided scale.
- Enter your name and contact information if you have any comments or feedback.
- Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the services provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Quality Survey (Page 1)**
	+ This is the main title of the form, which you should read and understand before proceeding.
* **Service Area**
	+ Select the service area that best describes the service you are evaluating. This will help the organization understand which specific service is being rated.
	+ Options: Health and Human Services, Economic Development, Social and Environmental, Education, Community and Culture, Transportation
* **Service Quality**
	+ Choose how you rate the quality of the selected service area using the provided scale.
	+ Options: Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor
* **Survey Date**
	+ This field is not required, but if you do enter a date, it will help the organization understand when the survey was completed.
* **Survey Time**
	+ This field is not required, but if you do enter a time, it will help the organization understand when the survey was completed.
* **Contact Name**
	+ If you have any comments or feedback, enter your full legal name.
* **Contact Email**
	+ Enter your email address, which will be used to contact you if the organization needs more information.
* **Contact Phone**
	+ Enter your phone number, which will be used to contact you if the organization needs more information.
* **Comments**
	+ Enter any additional comments or feedback you have about the services provided. This is an optional field, and you can use it to provide more detail or context to your feedback.

## Tips
- Please be honest and accurate when rating the quality of services.
- If you have any comments or feedback, provide as much detail as possible.
- Remember, this form is designed to help the organization improve its services, so your input is valuable!
