# water_safety_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess an organization or facility's safety practices and procedures related to water activities. It aims to gather information on various aspects of water safety, including emergency contacts, first aid, and rescue techniques.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Read the introduction and understand the purpose of the survey.
2.  For each section, select the relevant options or answers based on your organization's safety practices and procedures.
3.  Provide any other relevant comments or information in the comments section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This section is an overview of the survey. Please read it carefully and proceed with the survey.
* **Safety Knowledge**: Please select all the safety knowledge categories that apply to your organization or facility. These include:
	+ 'Yes' for areas where your organization has a good understanding of safety practices and procedures.
	+ 'No' for areas where your organization does not have a good understanding of safety practices and procedures.
* **Emergency Contact**: Provide the name and contact information of the person to be contacted in case of an emergency. This can be a phone number, email, or physical address.
* **Emergency Contact 2**: Provide the name and contact information of a secondary contact person in case the primary contact is unavailable.
* **Lifeguard Knowledge**: Select whether your organization or facility has a good understanding of lifeguard procedures and practices. This includes:
	+ 'True' for areas where your organization has a good understanding of lifeguard procedures and practices.
	+ 'False' for areas where your organization does not have a good understanding of lifeguard procedures and practices.
* **Safety Equipment**: List all the safety equipment available at your organization or facility. These can include:
	+ 'Yes' for areas where your organization has the necessary safety equipment.
	+ 'No' for areas where your organization does not have the necessary safety equipment.
* **Rescue Techniques**: Select all the rescue techniques used by your organization or facility. These can include:
	+ swimming
	+ diving
	+ treading water
* **First Aid**: List all the first aid procedures available at your organization or facility. These can include:
	+ 'Yes' for areas where your organization has the necessary first aid procedures.
	+ 'No' for areas where your organization does not have the necessary first aid procedures.
* **Emergency Procedures**: List all the emergency procedures available at your organization or facility. These can include:
	+ 'Yes' for areas where your organization has the necessary emergency procedures.
	+ 'No' for areas where your organization does not have the necessary emergency procedures.
* **Water Safety Practices**: Select all the water safety practices used by your organization or facility. These can include:
	+ 'Yes' for areas where your organization has a good understanding of water safety practices.
	+ 'No' for areas where your organization does not have a good understanding of water safety practices.
* **Other Safety Practices**: Provide any other relevant safety practices or procedures not mentioned in the previous fields.
* **Other Safety Practices 2**: Provide any other relevant safety practices or procedures not mentioned in the previous fields.
* **Comments**: Provide any other relevant comments or information about your organization's or facility's safety practices and procedures.
* **Survey Completed**: If you have completed the survey, mark the date you completed it.

Please note that this form is not a test, and the answers selected are for informational purposes only.
