# Story Evaluation Scorecard - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and scores from evaluators on various aspects of a story. It is used to evaluate a story based on its alignment with specific criteria.

## How To Complete This Form
1. The form is divided into two main sections: the Intro Section and the Criteria Sections.
2. The Intro Section is optional and can be filled out if desired.
3. The Criteria Sections consist of 6 criteria that you must rate as true or false, indicating whether the story meets the specific criterion or not.

5. Finally, you may add any additional comments or feedback in the Review field.
