# 10 Point Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 10 Point Inspection Form is a comprehensive evaluation tool designed to assess the condition and compliance of various assets, systems, or facilities. It covers 10 key areas, including structural integrity, operational functionality, safety features, cleanliness, documentation, environmental impact, maintenance status, power connections, access and egress routes, and security vulnerabilities.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Inspector/Auditor Name**: Enter your full name and contact information for future reference.
2.  **Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the inspection to track progress and follow-up activities.
3.  **Location / Site Inspected**: Enter the specific location or site being inspected, including any relevant identifying information.
4.  **Type of Asset/Subject**: Choose the type of asset or subject being inspected from the provided options (e.g., Vehicle, Equipment, Facility, etc.).
5.  **Asset ID/Serial Number**: Enter the unique identifier or serial number for the inspected item.
6.  **Client or Department**: Enter the relevant client or department associated with the inspected item (optional).
7.  **1. Structural Integrity / Physical Condition**: Rate the inspected item's structural integrity on a scale of 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent) or select N/A if not applicable.
8.  **2. Operational Functionality**: Rate the inspected item's operational functionality on a scale of 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent) or select N/A if not applicable.
9.  **3. Safety Features & Guards**: Rate the presence and functionality of safety features and guards on a scale of 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent) or select N/A if not applicable.
10.  **4. Cleanliness & Housekeeping**: Rate the cleanliness and organization of the inspected area or asset on a scale of 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
11.  **5. Documentation & Records**: Rate the quality and availability of documentation and records on a scale of 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
12.  **6. Environmental Impact**: Rate the presence and severity of environmental concerns such as leaks or emissions on a scale of 1 (none) to 3 (severe).
13.  **7. Maintenance Status**: Rate the maintenance status of the inspected item on a scale of 1 (up-to-date) to 3 (overdue).
14.  **8. Power / Utility Connections**: Rate the security and integrity of power and utility connections on a scale of 1 (secure & intact) to 3 (hazardous).
15.  **9. Access & Egress Routes**: Rate the clarity and safety of access and egress routes on a scale of 1 (clear) to 3 (blocked).
16.  **10. Security Vulnerabilities (Physical)**: Rate the presence and severity of security vulnerabilities on a scale of 1 (secure) to 3 (compromised).
