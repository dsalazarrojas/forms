# Client Project Satisfaction Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather client feedback on a project, assessing their satisfaction with the project's outcome, quality of work, and overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your honest feedback. Each section has a label and a corresponding field where you can provide your input. You can type your feedback directly into the fields provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Name** (`project_name`, required): Please enter the name of the project you are providing feedback on.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, required): Please enter your name or company name as the client who completed the project.
* **Project Completion Date** (`completion_date`, required): Please select the date when the project was completed.
* **Project Manager Name** (`project_manager`, optional): If you would like to provide the name of the project manager who oversaw your project, you can do so here.
* **Overall Satisfaction with the Project** (`overall_satisfaction`, required): Please select how satisfied you are with the project's outcome. This will help us understand how we met your expectations.
* **How would you rate the quality of work delivered?** (`quality_of_work`, required): Please rate the quality of work delivered by selecting from the provided options. This will help us assess the quality of our services.
* **The project met or exceeded your expectations** (`met_expectations`, required): Please select how strongly you agree or disagree with the statement that the project met or exceeded your expectations.
* **How well did the project team adhere to the timeline?** (`timeline_adherence`, required): Please rate the project team's adherence to the timeline by selecting from the provided options. This will help us evaluate our project management skills.
* **How well was the budget managed?** (`budget_adherence`, optional): If you'd like to provide feedback on how well we managed the budget for your project, you can do so here.
* **Quality of communication during the project** (`communication_quality`, required): Please rate the quality of communication you experienced during the project by selecting from the provided options. This will help us improve our communication with you and future clients.
* **Team professionalism and responsiveness** (`team_professionalism`, required): Please rate the team's professionalism and responsiveness by selecting from the provided options. This will help us evaluate our team's behavior.
* **How effectively were issues or concerns addressed?** (`problem_resolution`, optional): If you have any issues or concerns that you would like to share, you can do so here. This will help us understand what we can improve on.
* **Would you recommend our firm to others?** (`recommend_firm`, required): Please select whether you would recommend our firm to others, based on your experience with the project.
* **Would you consider working with us on future projects?** (`future_work`, required): Please select whether you would consider working with us again on future projects, based on your experience with the project.
* **What could we improve for future engagements?** (`improvement_areas`, optional): If you have any feedback on areas we could improve, please provide it here.
* **What did you appreciate most about this project?** (`positive_feedback`, optional): If you'd like to share what you appreciated most about the project, you can do so here.
* **What additional services would interest you?** (`additional_services`, optional): If you would like to share what additional services you would be interested in, you can do so here.

# Tips
* Please be honest and provide detailed feedback to help us improve our services.
* If you are unsure about a question or answer, feel free to ask us or provide an explanation.
* Your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your time in taking the survey.
