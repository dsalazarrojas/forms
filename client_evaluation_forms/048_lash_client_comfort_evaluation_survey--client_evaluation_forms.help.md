# Lash Client Comfort Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lash Client Comfort Evaluation Survey is designed to gather feedback from clients about their experiences and comfort level during a session at a laser hair removal clinic.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please complete this form carefully, ensuring all required fields are filled out.
- Review each section thoroughly before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Intro** (`intro_page`, note, required: false): This is the introduction page of the form, which provides background information about the client's session.
- **Artists Info** (`artist_info_page`, text, required: false): This field is intended for the client to provide information about the artist or technician who assisted them during the session.
- **Services** (`service_page`, select_multiple, required: false):
  - Select all services performed during the session, such as "Lash application" or "Pain relief techniques."
- **Comfort Level** (`comfort_level_page`, select_one, required: false):
  - Choose one of the two options: "High" for comfortable or "Low" for uncomfortable.
- **Pain Level** (`pain_page`, select_one, required: false):
  - Choose one of the two options: "High" for high pain level or "Low" for low pain level.
- **Suggestions** (`suggestions_page`, text, required: false): 
  - Provide any suggestions you may have for improvement in future sessions.
- **Comments** (`comments_page`, text, required: false): 
  - Share any comments or feedback about your experience, positive or negative.
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info_page`, email, required: false): 
  - Enter your email address or other contact information so that we can follow up with you.
- **Review** (`review_page`, text, required: false): 
  - Share your overall experience with the service, including any positive or negative comments.
- **Submission** (`submission_page`, date, required: false): 
  - The date and time this form was submitted.
- **Follow Up** (`followup_page`, time, required: false):
  - The time when we should follow up with you about your feedback.
- **Submission Date** (`submission_date_page`, date, required: false): 
  - The date when this form was submitted.
- **Submission Time** (`submission_time_page`, time, required: false):
  - The time when this form was submitted.
- **Submission Status** (`submission_status_page`, note, required: false): 
  - This field is likely for internal use, but it may display the status of your submission.
- **Submission Note** (`submission_note_page`, text, required: false): 
  - Add any additional comments or notes about your submission.
- **Artist Note** (`artist_note_page`, text, required: false): 
  - The artist or technician may leave notes about your session here.
- **Service Note** (`service_note_page`, text, required: false): 
  - Additional comments or notes about your service, such as any complications that may have arisen.
- **Review** (`review_page`, text, required: false): 
  - Share your thoughts on the quality of the service provided.

## Tips

- Take your time to complete this form carefully and accurately.
- If a field is unclear, do not hesitate to ask for assistance.
- Submit the form once you have completed all required fields. 
- If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to our staff.
