# Client Relationship Evaluation Form - Help Guide
 
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the client relationship with your organization. It helps assess the overall satisfaction, quality of service, and relationship strength between your team and the client. The form includes questions that gauge the client's perception of your services, responsiveness, professionalism, and communication quality. The collected feedback will be used to identify areas for improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance the client relationship.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name and click "Submit" to start.
2. Select the relevant time period for how long you have been a client from the dropdown menu.
3. Choose your level of overall satisfaction with our services from the options provided.
4. Rate our communication quality, responsiveness, professionalism, value for money, and consistency in service quality.
5. Indicate whether your expectations were met during the relationship.
6. Assess the overall strength of the relationship.
7. Express your likelihood to recommend our services to others.
8. If applicable, describe what you like most about working with us and areas that need improvement.
9. Indicate if you are interested in expanding our services in the future.
10. Add any additional feedback or comments you have about your experience with us.
