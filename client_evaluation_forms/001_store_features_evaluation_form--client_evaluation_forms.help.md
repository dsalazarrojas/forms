# Store Features Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers on various aspects of a store's features, such as overall appearance, cleanliness, products quality, and staff helpfulness. The goal is to help the store's management to understand customer perceptions and improve the shopping experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the store for which you would like to provide feedback.
2. Fill out the form fields with your honest opinions.
   - For the **Overall Rating** field (10), please rate on a scale of 0 to 10.
   - For the **Rating Fields** (1, 3, 4, 5, 8, 9), please rate on a scale of 0 to 5.
   - For the **General Comments** field (6), please enter any additional thoughts or observations you have about the store.
   - For the **Satisfaction with Prices** field (7), please select one of the three options provided.
3. Review your answers carefully and make sure they accurately reflect your opinions.
4. Click the submit button to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Appearance** (1, required): This field asks for your overall impression of the store's appearance. Please rate how visually appealing you found the store, its layout, and its atmosphere.
* **Cleanliness** (2, required): Rate the store's cleanliness, including its floors, walls, and restrooms.
* **Products Quality** (3, required): Rate the quality of the products offered by the store, including their selection and presentation.
* **Staff Helpfulness** (4, required): Rate how helpful the store's staff were in assisting you with your purchases or questions.
* **Location Convenience** (5, required): Rate the store's location, including its accessibility, parking, and proximity to other amenities.
* **General Comments** (6, optional): Enter any additional comments or suggestions you have about the store.
* **Satisfaction with Prices** (7, required): Indicate your level of satisfaction with the store's prices.
* **Service Quality** (8, required): Rate the store's service quality, including the staff's knowledge, friendliness, and efficiency.
* **Product Variety** (9, required): Rate the variety of products offered by the store.
* **Overall Rating** (10, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the store, on a scale of 0 to 10.

## Tips

* Be honest and objective in your evaluations.
* Try to provide specific examples or details to support your ratings.
* Take your time to complete the form carefully.
* Review your answers before submitting to ensure they accurately reflect your opinions.
