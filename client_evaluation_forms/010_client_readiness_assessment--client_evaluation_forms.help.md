# Client Readiness Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a Client Readiness Assessment, designed to evaluate the readiness of a client to embark on a project or engagement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Enter the client's company name in the "Company Name" field.
3. Select the date of the assessment from the calendar or enter it manually in the "Date of Assessment" field.
4. Choose the type of project or engagement from the options provided in the "Type of Project or Engagement" field.
5. Answer the questions in the "Do you have clearly defined objectives?" to "Unknown" for "Technical readiness level".
6. Answer the question "What are potential obstacles or risks?" and "What are your key success factors?" in the text fields.
7. Select the level of experience for "Experience with similar initiatives" from the options.
8. Finally, choose one of the options for "Overall readiness to proceed" from "Highly recommended" to "Requires significant preparation".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the client's name to whom the project or engagement is intended.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the client's company name.
* **Date of Assessment** (`assessment_date`, date, required): Select the date of the assessment or enter it manually.
* **Type of Project or Engagement** (`project_type`, text, required): Choose the type of project or engagement.
* **Do you have clearly defined objectives?** (`clear_objectives`, select_one, required): Select one of the options: Very clear, Clear, Somewhat clear, Unclear, or Not defined.
* **Are all key stakeholders aligned on the project?** (`stakeholder_alignment`, select_one, required): Select one of the options: Strongly aligned, Aligned, Mostly aligned, Misaligned, or Very misaligned.
* **Do you have necessary resources committed?** (`resource_availability`, select_one, required): Select one of the options: Fully committed, Mostly committed, Partially committed, Limited commitment, or No commitment.
* **Is the project budget approved and allocated?** (`budget_approved`, select_one, required): Select one of the options: Yes, fully approved, Mostly approved, Partially approved, Pending approval, or Not approved.
* **Is your timeline realistic and achievable?** (`timeline_realistic`, select_one, required): Select one of the options: Very realistic, Realistic, Somewhat realistic, Unrealistic, or Very unrealistic.
* **Do you have strong executive/leadership buy-in?** (`leadership_buy_in`, select_one, required): Select one of the options: Strong support, Good support, Moderate support, Weak support, or No support.
* **Do you have a change management plan?** (`change_management`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options: Comprehensive plan, Basic plan, In development, No plan yet, or Not applicable.
* **Assessment of team capability to execute** (`team_capability`, select_one, required): Select one of the options: Highly capable, Capable, Reasonably capable, Limited capability, or Insufficient capability.
* **Technical readiness level** (`technical_readiness`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options: Fully ready, Mostly ready, Partially ready, Not ready, or Unknown.
* **What are potential obstacles or risks?** (`potential_obstacles`, text, optional): Answer this question in the text field.
* **What are your key success factors?** (`success_factors`, text, optional): Answer this question in the text field.
* **Experience with similar initiatives** (`previous_experience`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options: Extensive experience, Moderate experience, Some experience, Limited experience, or No experience.
* **Overall readiness to proceed** (`readiness_recommendation`, select_one, required): Select one of the options: Highly recommended, Recommended, Conditionally recommended, Not recommended at this time, or Requires significant preparation.

## Tips

* Make sure to answer each question honestly and to the best of your knowledge.
* If you are unsure about any question, select the option that best represents your current state.
* If you need more information or have additional comments, use the "What are potential obstacles or risks?" and "What are your key success factors?" text fields to provide more context.
* This form is designed to provide an initial assessment of the client's readiness to proceed with a project or engagement. It is not a substitute for a thorough project planning and execution plan.
