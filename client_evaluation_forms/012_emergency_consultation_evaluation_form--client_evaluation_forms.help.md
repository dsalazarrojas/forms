## Field-by-Field Explanation
## Tips

In "Field-by-Field Explanation", use one bullet per page/field:
* **Client Name** (Client Name, text, required): Enter the client's full name as per the official records or the name you are using for consultation purposes.
* **Contact Number** (Contact Number, text, required): Enter the client's contact number for communication purposes.
* **Contact Email** (Contact Email, email, optional): Enter the client's contact email address, if available.
* **Emergency Status** (Emergency Status, text, required): Describe the emergency status of the client, as per your professional assessment.
* **Medical Condition** (Medical Condition, text, required): Provide an accurate description of the client's medical condition.
* **Emergency Department** (Emergency Department, text, required): Specify the emergency department or unit where the consultation took place or is scheduled to take place.
* **Emergency Visit Date** (Emergency Visit Date, date, required): Enter the date of the emergency consultation visit.
* **Emergency Visit Time** (Emergency Visit Time, time, required): Enter the time of the emergency consultation visit.
* **Medical History** (Medical History, text, optional): If applicable, provide relevant medical history that might be useful for further evaluation or treatment.
* **Medication** (Medication, text, optional): If the client is taking any medication, describe it here.
* **Medical Condition 2** (Medical Condition 2, text, required): Describe the second medical condition of the client.
* **Emergency Department 2** (Emergency Department 2, text, required): Specify the second emergency department or unit where the consultation took place or is scheduled to take place.
* **Emergency Visit Date 2** (Emergency Visit Date 2, date, required): Enter the date of the second emergency consultation visit.
* **Emergency Visit Time 2** (Emergency Visit Time 2, time, required): Enter the time of the second emergency consultation visit.
