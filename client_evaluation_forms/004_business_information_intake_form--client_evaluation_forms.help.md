# Business Information Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about your business, including contact details, industry, size, and challenges faced by the business. This information will help us understand your needs and provide you with the best possible guidance and support for your business.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through each page and fill out the required fields. Ensure that you provide accurate and complete information for each section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Company Name** (`client_company_name`, text, required): Enter the full legal name of your company.
* **Primary Contact Name** (`primary_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the main point of contact.
* **Primary Contact Title** (`primary_contact_title`, text, required): Enter the job title of the primary contact.
* **Primary Contact Email** (`primary_contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the primary contact.
* **Primary Contact Phone** (`primary_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the direct phone number of the primary contact.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, text, optional): Enter the full website URL of your company.
* **Company Industry** (`company_industry`, select_one, required): Select the primary industry sector your company operates in:
	+ Technology
	+ Healthcare
	+ Finance
	+ Manufacturing
	+ Retail
	+ Education
	+ Hospitality
	+ Professional services
	+ Government
	+ Non-profit
	+ Other
* **Years in Business** (`years_in_business`, number, required): Enter how long your company has been operating.
* **Number of Employees** (`employee_count`, number, required): Enter the total number of employees.
* **Annual Revenue Range** (`annual_revenue`, select_one, required): Select the approximate annual revenue:
	+ Under $100K
	+ $100K - $500K
	+ $500K - $1M
	+ $1M - $5M
	+ $5M - $10M
	+ $10M - $50M
	+ $50M - $100M
	+ Over $100M
* **Number of Business Locations** (`business_locations`, number, required): Enter the number of locations your business operates.
* **Target Market** (`target_market`, select_one, required): Select the primary customer demographic:
	+ Consumers (B2C)
	+ Businesses (B2B)
	+ Government agencies
	+ Non-profits
	+ Mixed
* **Current Business Challenges** (`current_challenges`, select_multiple, required): Select all challenges your business is currently facing:
	+ Revenue growth
	+ Cost management
	+ Employee retention
	+ Market competition
	+ Technology adoption
	+ Regulatory compliance
	+ Customer acquisition
	+ Cash flow
	+ Supply chain
	+ Other
* **Primary Improvement Goals** (`improvement_goals`, select_multiple, required): Select the areas that need improvement most urgently:
	+ Operations efficiency
	+ Sales and marketing
	+ Customer service
	+ Financial management
	+ Human resources
	+ Technology infrastructure
	+ Product development
	+ Strategic planning
* **Previous Consulting Experience** (`previous_consulting_experience`, select_one, required): Select your experience with business consultants:
	+ Yes - multiple times
	+ Yes - once
	+ No - first time
* **Budget Range for Improvement Initiatives** (`budget_range`, select_one, optional): Enter the available budget for business improvement projects:
	+ Under $10K
	+ $10K - $25K
	+ $25K - $50K
	+ $50K - $100K
	+ $100K - $250K
	+ Over $250K
* **Expected Timeline for Results** (`timeline_expectations`, select_one, required): Select the expected timeline for seeing improvements:
	+ Immediate (within 1 month)
	+ Short-term (1-3 months)
	+ Medium-term (3-6 months)
	+ Long-term (6-12 months)
	+ Ongoing (12+ months)
* **Key Stakeholders Involved** (`key_stakeholders`, text, required): List the key decision-makers who will be involved in the business.
* **Main Competitors** (`competitors`, text, optional): List the primary competitors in your market.
* **Unique Value Proposition** (`unique_value_proposition`, text, required): Describe what makes your business unique in the market.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, optional): Provide any other information that would be helpful to know.
