# Client Relationship Management Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to assess the overall health and performance of client relationships, providing insights to inform business decisions and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the requested information for the corresponding client account.
2. Ensure that all fields marked as required are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Assessment Date
**Assessment Date** (1, date): 
The date on which this assessment is taking place. This is a required field and should be completed in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Client Account Name
**Client Account Name** (2, text): 
The name of the client's account. This is a required field and should be a brief description of the client's account.

### Assigned Account Manager
**Assigned Account Manager** (3, text): 
The person responsible for managing the client's account. This is a required field and should be the name of the assigned account manager.

### Overall Relationship Health
**Overall Relationship Health** (4, select_one): 
An assessment of the overall health of the client relationship, with options Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or At risk.

### Engagement Level
**Client Engagement Level** (5, select_one): 
An assessment of the client's engagement level, with options Very high, High, Moderate, Low, or Very low.

### Communication Frequency
**Communication Frequency** (6, select_one): 
An assessment of how often you interact with this client, with options Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or As needed.

### Contract Status
**Contract Status** (7, select_one): 
An assessment of the client's contract status, with options Active and current, Expiring soon, Renewal pending, Discontinued, or On hold.

### Client Satisfaction Level
**Client Satisfaction Level** (8, select_one): 
An assessment of the client's satisfaction level, with options Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very dissatisfied.

### Upsell or Cross-sell Potential
**Upsell or Cross-sell Potential** (9, select_one): 
An assessment of the potential for selling additional products or services to the client, with options High potential, Good potential, Some potential, Low potential, or No potential. (Note: This field is not required.)

### Risk of Client Churn
**Risk of Client Churn** (10, select_one): 
A likelihood assessment of losing this client, with options Very low, Low, Moderate, High, or Very high. This is a required field and should be completed.

### Top Concerns
**Top Concerns** (11, text): 
A description of the top concerns or issues with the client. This field is not required.)

### Key Contact Person
**Key Contact Person** (12, text): 
The primary point of contact at the client's organization. This is a required field and should be completed.

### Annual Account Revenue
**Annual Account Revenue** (13, number): 
The annual revenue generated by the client. (Note: This field is not required.)

### Strategic Importance
**Strategic Importance** (14, select_one): 
An assessment of the importance of this client to our business strategy, with options Critical, High, Medium, Low, or Minimal. (Note: This field is not required.)

### Action Plan for Relationship Improvement
**Action Plan for Relationship Improvement** (15, text): 
Specific steps to strengthen the relationship. (Note: This field is not required.)

### Next Review Date
**Next Review Date** (16, date): 
The date on which this assessment should be revisited. (Note: This field is not required.)

# Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Review the form regularly to ensure that the information is up-to-date and accurate.
* Use the collected data to inform business decisions and identify areas for improvement in the client relationship.
