# Client Evaluation Form For Lash Extensions - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from clients about their experience with a lash extension service. It helps the salon or service provider to evaluate the quality of service, identify areas for improvement, and make data-driven decisions to enhance customer satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely, answering all questions to the best of your ability.
2. Be honest and provide detailed responses to each question, including any comments or suggestions you may have.
3. Make sure to review your answers before submitting the form to ensure they accurately reflect your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`client_first_name`, text, required): This is the first step in identifying yourself. Please enter your legal first name.
* **Last Name** (`client_last_name`, text, required): This is the next step in identifying yourself. Enter your legal last name.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, email, required): This is your primary contact email. Please enter a valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (`client_phone`, text, required): This is your best contact number. Enter a valid phone number.
* **Date of Service** (`service_date`, date, required): This is the date of your appointment. Choose the correct date from the calendar or enter it manually.
* **Lash Technician Name** (`lash_technician`, text, required): This is the name of the technician who performed your service. Please enter their name as you recall it.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of service you received from the options below:
	+ Classic Lash Extensions
	+ Volume Lash Extensions
	+ Hybrid Lash Extensions
	+ Mega Volume Lash Extensions
	+ Lash Lift
	+ Lash Tint
	+ Lash Removal
	+ Fill or Refill
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Lash Style** (`lash_style`, select_one, required): Choose the style of lash extensions applied:
	+ Natural
	+ Cat Eye
	+ Doll Eye
	+ Squirrel
	+ Kim K
	+ Wispy
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Lash Length** (`lash_length`, select_one, required): Select the length of the lash extensions:
	+ Short (6-9mm)
	+ Medium (10-12mm)
	+ Long (13-15mm)
	+ Extra Long (16mm+)
	+ Mixed Lengths
* **Lash Thickness** (`lash_thickness`, select_one, optional): Select the thickness of the lash extensions (if applicable):
	+ 0.03mm
	+ 0.05mm
	+ 0.07mm
	+ 0.10mm
	+ 0.12mm
	+ 0.15mm
	+ Not Sure
* **Lash Curl Type** (`lash_curl`, select_one, optional): Select the curl type of the lash extensions (if applicable):
	+ J Curl
	+ B Curl
	+ C Curl
	+ D Curl
	+ L Curl
	+ Mixed Curls
	+ Not Sure
* **Comfort Level During Application** (`comfort_level`, select_one, required): Choose how comfortable you felt during the application:
	+ Very Comfortable
	+ Comfortable
	+ Neutral
	+ Uncomfortable
	+ Very Uncomfortable
* **Application Time Satisfaction** (`application_time`, select_one, required): Choose how long you felt the application time was:
	+ Too Fast
	+ Just Right
	+ Too Long
	+ Much Too Long
* **Satisfaction With Final Appearance** (`final_appearance`, select_one, required): Choose how you feel about the final appearance:
	+ Excellent
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **How Do the Lashes Feel?** (`lash_weight`, select_one, required): Choose how the lashes feel:
	+ Very Light and Natural
	+ Comfortable
	+ Slightly Heavy
	+ Too Heavy
	+ Uncomfortable
* **Did You Experience Any Eye Irritation?** (`eye_irritation`, select_one, required): Choose your level of eye irritation:
	+ No Irritation
	+ Mild Irritation
	+ Moderate Irritation
	+ Severe Irritation
* **How Well Are Lashes Retaining?** (`lash_retention`, select_one, required): Choose how well the lashes are retaining:
	+ Excellent Retention
	+ Good Retention
	+ Average Retention
	+ Poor Retention
	+ First Time Service
* **Were Aftercare Instructions Clear?** (`aftercare_instructions`, select_one, required): Choose how clear the aftercare instructions were:
	+ Very Clear
	+ Clear
	+ Somewhat Clear
	+ Unclear
	+ No Instructions Given
* **Are You Following Aftercare Instructions?** (`aftercare_followed`, select_one, required): Choose how well you're following aftercare instructions:
	+ Yes, Completely
	+ Mostly
	+ Somewhat
	+ False
* **Technician Skill Level** (`technician_skill`, select_one, required): Choose the skill level of the technician:
	+ Expert
	+ Very Skilled
	+ Skilled
	+ Average
	+ Below Average
* **Technician Professionalism** (`technician_professionalism`, select_one, required): Choose the level of professionalism exhibited by the technician:
	+ Excellent
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Salon Cleanliness** (`salon_cleanliness`, select_one, required): Choose the level of cleanliness of the salon:
	+ Excellent
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, select_one, required): Choose how you feel about the value for money:
	+ Excellent Value
	+ Good Value
	+ Fair Value
	+ Poor Value
	+ Very Poor Value
* **How Likely Are You to Return?** (`likely_to_return`, select_one, required): Choose how likely you are to return to the salon:
	+ Very Likely
	+ Likely
	+ Neutral
	+ Unlikely
	+ Very Unlikely
* **How Likely Are You to Recommend Us?** (`recommend_to_others`, select_one, required): Choose how likely you are to recommend the salon to others:
	+ Very Likely
	+ Likely
	+ Neutral
	+ Unlikely
	+ Very Unlikely
* **What Did You Like Most?** (`favorite_aspect`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of what you liked most about the service.
* **What Could We Improve?** (`improvement_areas`, text, optional): Enter any areas that could be improved.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any other feedback or comments about your experience.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required): This is the date of your evaluation. Please enter the correct date.
