# Babysitting Service Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather your feedback about the babysitting service you have utilized for your child. Your input will help us to identify areas of strength and weakness, enabling us to continue to maintain the highest standards of care for our babysitters.

## How To Complete This Form

### General Guidance
Please fill out this form to provide your honest feedback about the babysitting service. Make sure to answer each question thoroughly, and provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Babysitting Service Quality Survey --** (Note): This is a heading to guide you through the form.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name_feedback`, `text`, required): Please enter the name of the parent who has used the babysitting service.
* **Date of Babysitting Session** (`date_of_service`, `date`, required): Indicate the date the babysitting service was provided for the child.
* **Name of Babysitter** (`babysitter_name_feedback`, `text`, required): Type in the name of the babysitter who provided the service.
* **Was the babysitter on time?** (`punctuality_rating`, `select_one`, required): Answer this question with one of the following options:
	+ Yes, exactly on time
	+ Arrived early
	+ Slightly late (less than 10 mins)
	+ Late
* **Effectiveness of communication before and during session** (`communication_rating`, `number`, required): Rate the effectiveness of the babysitter's communication with you. Please use the scale:
	+ 1 = Poor
	+ 2
	+ 3
	+ 4
	+ 5 = Excellent
* **Did your child enjoy their time with the sitter?** (`child_enjoyment_level`, `select_one`, required): Answer this question based on your child's reaction or comments:
	+ Loved it
	+ Had a good time
	+ It was okay
	+ Did not enjoy it
* **Was the house left in a satisfactory condition?** (`house_cleanliness_return`, `select_one`, required): Rate the cleanliness of the house after the babysitting session. Please choose one of the following:
	+ Very clean and tidy
	+ Satisfactory
	+ Messy
* **Did the sitter engage the child in activities?** (`activity_engagement`, `select_one`, optional): If the sitter provided a variety of activities for the child, answer:
	+ Highly engaged
	+ Somewhat engaged
	+ Mostly screen time
	+ No activities were provided
* **Would you hire this babysitter again?** (`rehire_likelihood`, `select_one`, required): Answer with one of the following options:
	+ Definitely
	+ Likely
	+ Unlikely
	+ False
* **Overall satisfaction with the service** (`overall_experience_rating`, `number`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the service. Please use the scale:
	+ 1 = Unsatisfied
	+ 2
	+ 3
	+ 4
	+ 5 = Highly satisfied
* **Any additional comments or areas for improvement?** (`constructive_feedback`, `text`, optional): If you have any further comments or suggestions for improvement, please provide them here.
