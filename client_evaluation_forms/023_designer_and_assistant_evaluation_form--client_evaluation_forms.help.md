# Designer And Assistant Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Designer and Assistant Evaluation Form is used to assess the performance of designers and assistants. This form is meant to provide feedback on their skills, work habits, and communication style, which can be used to improve their performance and provide guidance for future projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as "required".
2. For fields that allow multiple selections, select all applicable options.
3. Use the "Comments" fields to provide additional feedback or comments about the designer or assistant's performance.
4. Once you have completed the form, submit it for review and action.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Designer And Assistant Name** (`designer_assistant_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the designer or assistant being evaluated.
* **Designer Rating** (`designer_rating`, `number`, required): Rate the designer's overall performance, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Assistant Rating** (`assistant_rating`, `number`, required): Rate the assistant's overall performance, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief description of the project.
* **Design Work** (`design_work`, `select_one`, required): Rate the quality of the designer's work, as excellent, average, or below average.
* **Communication Skills** (`communication_skills`, `select_multiple`, required): Rate the designer's communication skills, with options excellent, average, below average, not at all, or somewhat.
* **Time Management** (`time_management`, `select_multiple`, required): Rate the designer's time management skills, with options excellent, average, below average, not at all, or somewhat.
* **Meeting Commitments** (`meeting_commitments`, `select_one`, required): Rate the designer's commitment to meetings, as almost always, most of the time, some of the time, rarely, or not at all.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the overall performance of the designer and assistant, with options excellent, average, below average, or poor.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Provide additional feedback or comments about the designer or assistant's performance.
* **Designer Rating Comments** (`designer_rating_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide feedback about the designer's rating.
* **Assistant Rating Comments** (`assistant_rating_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide feedback about the assistant's rating.
* **Communication Skills Comments** (`communication_skills_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide feedback about the designer's communication skills.
* **Time Management Comments** (`time_management_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide feedback about the designer's time management skills.
* **Meeting Commitments Comments** (`meeting_commitments_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide feedback about the designer's meeting commitments.
* **Overall Rating Comments** (`overall_rating_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide feedback about the overall rating.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief description of the project.
* **Work Style** (`work_style`, `select_one`, optional): Rate the designer's work style, as collaborative, independent, or autonomous.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, optional): Provide additional feedback or comments about the designer or assistant's performance.
* **Designer and Assistant Name** (`designer_assistant_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the designer or assistant.
* **Project Title** (`project_title`, `text`, optional): Provide the title of the project.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, `select_one`, optional): Rate the project status, as active, completed, or paused.
* **Design Phase** (`design_phase`, `select_multiple`, optional): Rate the design phase of the project, with options concept, design, testing, launch, maintenance, or finished.
* **Designer and Assistant Email** (`designer_assistant_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the designer or assistant.
* **Designer and Assistant Phone** (`designer_assistant_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the designer or assistant.

## Tips
* Be specific and concise in your feedback and comments.
* Use clear and objective criteria when rating the designer or assistant's performance.
* Avoid making personal attacks or criticism.
* Keep your feedback focused on the designer or assistant's performance and work habits.
* Use this form to provide guidance and support for future projects.
