# Bespoke Interpretation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather feedback from clients on their experience with our interpretation services, including ratings and comments on various aspects of our service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name or organization name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email Address" field, as we will send survey confirmation here.
3. If you have an organization, enter it in the "Organization or Company" field.
4. Select the date you received interpretation services in the "Date of Interpretation Service" field.
5. Choose the type of interpretation service you received in the "Type of Interpretation Service" field.
6. Select the language pair that was interpreted in the "Language Pair" field.
7. Rate the interpreter's performance in the "Interpreter Performance Rating" field.
8. Rate the accuracy of the interpretation in the "Accuracy of Interpretation" field.
9. Rate the interpreter's professionalism in the "Professionalism Rating" field.
10. Rate the interpreter's punctuality in the "Punctuality Rating" field.
11. Rate the communication quality in the "Communication Quality" field.
12. Rate the interpreter's cultural sensitivity in the "Cultural Sensitivity" field.
13. Rate whether the service met your expectations in the "Did Service Meet Expectations" field.
14. Answer whether you would recommend our services to others in the "Would You Recommend Our Services" field.
15. Indicate your interest in using our services again in the "Interest in Future Services" field.
16. Select the strongest aspect of our service in the "Strongest Aspect of Service" field (optional).
17. Provide any areas for improvement in the "Areas for Improvement" field (optional).
18. Add any additional comments or feedback in the "Additional Comments or Feedback" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (Text, Required): Enter your full name or organization name to identify yourself in the survey.
* **Email Address** (Email, Required): Enter your email address to receive survey confirmation and any follow-up communications.
* **Organization or Company** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of your organization if applicable.
* **Date of Interpretation Service** (Date, Required): Select the date you received interpretation services.
* **Type of Interpretation Service** (Select One, Required): Choose the type of interpretation service you received, such as simultaneous, consecutive, or written translation.
* **Language Pair** (Select One, Required): Select the language pair that was interpreted, such as English to Spanish or English to French.
* **Interpreter Performance Rating** (Select One, Required): Rate the interpreter's performance overall.
* **Accuracy of Interpretation** (Select One, Required): Rate the accuracy of the interpretation.
* **Professionalism Rating** (Select One, Required): Rate the interpreter's professionalism.
* **Punctuality Rating** (Select One, Required): Rate the interpreter's punctuality.
* **Communication Quality** (Select One, Required): Rate the communication quality.
* **Cultural Sensitivity** (Select One, Required): Rate the interpreter's cultural sensitivity.
* **Did Service Meet Expectations** (Select One, Required): Rate whether the service met your expectations.
* **Would You Recommend Our Services** (Select One, Required): Answer whether you would recommend our services to others.
* **Interest in Future Services** (Select One, Required): Indicate your interest in using our services again.
* **Strongest Aspect of Service** (Select One, Optional): Select the strongest aspect of our service, such as interpreter quality or booking process.
* **Areas for Improvement** (Text, Optional): Provide any areas for improvement.
* **Additional Comments or Feedback** (Text, Optional): Add any additional comments or feedback.
