# Employer Of Record Service Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employer Of Record Service Evaluation Form is used to gather feedback from clients about their experience with our employer of record service. The form consists of multiple sections that cover different aspects of the service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the options that best match your experience with our employer of record service.
2. Make sure to complete all required fields.
3. Provide additional comments if you have any other feedback or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Feedback** (`client_feedback`, text, required): Describe your overall impression of our employer of record service.

* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Choose the option that best describes your overall satisfaction with our service:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied

* **Service Quality** (`service_quality`, select_multiple, required): Select all options that apply to describe the quality of our service:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor

* **Communication** (`communication`, select_one, required): Choose the option that best describes our communication:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor

* **Timeliness** (`timeliness`, select_one, required): Choose the option that best describes how timely our service was:
	+ Very Quick
	+ Somewhat Quick
	+ Average
	+ Somewhat Slow
	+ Very Slow

* **Support** (`support`, select_multiple, required): Select all options that apply to describe the support you received:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor

* **Frequency of Support** (`frequency_of_support`, select_multiple, required): Select all options that apply to describe how often you received support:
	+ Always
	+ Most of the Time
	+ Some of the Time
	+ Rarely

* **Employer of Record** (`employer_of_record`, select_one, required): Choose the option that best describes the service you used:
	+ Our Services
	+ Another Service

* **Employer of Record Service** (`employer_of_record_service`, select_one, required): Choose the option that best describes the service you used:
	+ Our Services
	+ Another Service

* **Overall** (`overall`, select_multiple, not required): Select all options that apply to describe your overall impression:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor

* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, not required): Provide any additional comments or suggestions about our employer of record service.

Note: This form does not include an option for "Overall (Repeated)", as it is not required and seems to be a duplicate of the "Overall" field. If it's intended for a specific use case, please clarify.
