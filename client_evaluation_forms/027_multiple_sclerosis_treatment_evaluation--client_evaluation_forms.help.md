# Multiple Sclerosis Treatment Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the treatment experience of patients with Multiple Sclerosis (MS). It collects information on symptoms, treatment history, treatment outcomes, satisfaction, and other relevant details to help healthcare providers better understand the effectiveness of treatment options.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Review each page carefully and select the options that apply to your experience with MS treatment.
* For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose one or multiple options from the list.
* Enter text in the text fields (e.g., Patient Name, Healthcare Provider, Medical Record Number).
* Enter email addresses in the email field (e.g., Patient Email).
* Use a number to rate your satisfaction level in the ms_satisfaction field.
