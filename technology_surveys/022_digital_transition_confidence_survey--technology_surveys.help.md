# Digital Transition Confidence Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gauge employees' confidence and readiness for digital transformation within their organization. It assesses their perceptions on the use and impact of digital technologies on their work processes and performance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the relevant options for each question that best describe your feelings and experiences.
2. Answer questions about your confidence in your digital skills, adaptability to new technologies, and perception of data analytics' role in decision-making and productivity.
3. Complete the survey honestly, as your responses will help your organization better understand the needs and challenges of its employees regarding digital transformation.
