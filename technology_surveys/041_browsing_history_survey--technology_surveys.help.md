# Browsing History Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Browsing History Survey is a collection of questions designed to gather information about users' browsing habits, helping us to better understand their behavior and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the appropriate age group that best describes you.
2. Choose the browser you use most often for browsing.
3. Enter the average number of hours you spend browsing per day.
4. Select the level of concern you have about browsing privacy.
5. Indicate the device you most commonly use for browsing.
6. Choose how often you clear your browsing history.
7. Select how often you use private browsing modes.
8. Add any additional comments or feedback you have about your browsing habits.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Respondent age group**: Select the age range that best describes you.
* **Primary browser**: Choose the browser you use most often for browsing. This will help us understand which browsers our users prefer.
* **Average daily browsing hours**: Enter the average number of hours you spend browsing per day.
* **Privacy concerns**: This question helps us understand your level of concern about your online privacy.
* **Common device**: Indicate the device you most often use for browsing. This could be a Desktop, Laptop, Mobile, or Tablet.
* **Cleared history frequency**: Select how often you clear your browsing history.
* **Use of private mode**: Choose how often you use private browsing modes, such as Incognito or Private Browsing.
* **Additional comments**: Add any feedback or comments you have about your browsing habits.
