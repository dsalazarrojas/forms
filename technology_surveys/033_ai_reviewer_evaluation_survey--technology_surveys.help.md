# AI Reviewer Evaluation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the performance of AI reviewers, gathering information about the context of the review, the reviewer's name and review date, and their ratings for accuracy, clarity, and overall satisfaction. The collected data will help in improving the quality of AI reviewer outputs and providing feedback to the reviewers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the appropriate review type from the options provided.
2. Enter your name as the reviewer.
3. Choose the date of the review.
4. Rate the accuracy and clarity of the reviewer output on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
5. Select all that apply regarding observed bias or fairness issues.
6. Provide practical steps for improvement, if any.
7. Rate your overall satisfaction with the reviewer's output.
8. Indicate if follow-up is required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Review Context --**: This section is a brief description of the context in which the review was conducted. It's not required but it helps in providing background information.
* **Reviewer name**: Enter your name as the reviewer who conducted the assessment.
* **Review date**: This is the date when the review was conducted.
* **Review type**: Choose the type of review you conducted, such as accuracy assessment or user satisfaction.
* **Accuracy rating**: Rate the accuracy of the reviewer output on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Clarity rating**: Rate the clarity of the reviewer output on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Observed bias or fairness issues**: Select all that apply regarding observed bias or fairness issues. This includes options like gender, racial, socioeconomic biases.
* **Recommendations for improvement**: Provide any practical steps to improve the reviewer. This is an optional field.
* **Overall satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the reviewer output on a scale from Very satisfied to Very dissatisfied.
* **Follow up required**: Indicate whether follow-up is required based on your feedback.
