# Pet Service Innovation Study Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about pet owners' experiences with pet services, including their satisfaction with these services, the time spent with them, and their contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please select the relevant options for each field and provide any additional information requested. The form is designed to be completed by pet owners who have used or are interested in using pet services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Pet Business Information (Page 1, Question 1)
* **Pet Business Information (pet_business_info)** (Note): This field is for you to provide any relevant information about your pet business.

### Services (Page 1, Question 2)
* **Services (pet_services)** (Select One): Have you used pet services before?
	+ 'Yes' - You have used pet services.
	+ 'No' - You have not used pet services.

### New Services (Page 1, Question 3)
* **New Services (new_services)** (Select Multiple): Which of the following new services are you interested in?
	+ 'Yes' - You are interested in these new services.
	+ 'No' - You are not interested in these new services.

### Satisfaction (Page 2, Question 4)
* **Satisfaction (satisfaction)** (Number): On a scale of 1-5, how satisfied are you with the services you used?
	+ 1 - Very dissatisfied
	+ 2 - Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ 3 - Neutral
	+ 4 - Somewhat satisfied
	+ 5 - Very satisfied

### Satisfaction Details (Page 2, Question 5)
* **Satisfaction Details (satisfaction_details)** (Text): Please provide any additional details about your satisfaction with the services you used.

### Service Quality (Page 3, Question 6)
* **Service Quality (service_quality)** (Number): On a scale of 1-5, how would you rate the quality of the services you used?
	+ 1 - Very poor
	+ 2 - Somewhat poor
	+ 3 - Neutral
	+ 4 - Somewhat good
	+ 5 - Very good

### Time Spent With Services (Page 3, Question 7)
* **Time Spent With Services (time_spent_with_services)** (Number): How much time do you spend with the services each week?
	+ 1 - Less than 1 hour
	+ 2 - 1-2 hours
	+ 3 - 2-4 hours
	+ 4 - 4-6 hours
	+ 5 - More than 6 hours

### Email (Page 4, Question 8)
* **Email (email)** (Email): If you would like to be contacted by us, please provide your email address.

### Phone Number (Page 4, Question 9)
* **Phone Number (phone_number)** (Text): If you would like to be contacted by us, please provide your phone number.

### Pet Type (Page 5, Question 10)
* **Pet Type (pet_type)** (Select One): What type of pet do you own?
	+ 'Yes' - You own a pet of this type.
	+ 'No' - You do not own a pet of this type.

### Pet Age (Page 5, Question 11)
* **Pet Age (pet_age)** (Select Multiple): Which of the following age ranges apply to your pet?
	+ 'Yes' - Your pet is of this age range.
	+ 'No' - Your pet is not of this age range.

### Pet Gender (Page 6, Question 12)
* **Pet Gender (pet_gender)** (Select Multiple): Which of the following genders apply to your pet?
	+ 'Yes' - Your pet is of this gender.
	+ 'No' - Your pet is not of this gender.

### Pet Weight (Page 6, Question 13)
* **Pet Weight (pet_weight)** (Select Multiple): Which of the following weight ranges apply to your pet?
	+ 'Yes' - Your pet is of this weight range.
	+ 'No' - Your pet is not of this weight range.

### Pet Breed (Page 7, Question 14)
* **Pet Breed (pet_breed)** (Select Multiple): Which of the following breeds apply to your pet?
	+ 'Yes' - Your pet is of this breed.
	+ 'No' - Your pet is not of this breed.

### Services Used (Page 8, Question 15)
* **Services Used (services_used)** (Select Multiple): Which of the following services have you used?
	+ 'Yes' - You have used these services.
	+ 'No' - You have not used these services.

### Service Location (Page 9, Question 16)
* **Service Location (service_location)** (Select Multiple): At which locations do you typically use pet services?
	+ 'Yes' - This location.
	+ 'No' - Not this location.

### Additional Comments (Page 10, Question 17)
* **Additional Comments (comments)** (Note): Do you have any additional comments about your experiences with pet services?

### Contact Information (Page 11, Question 18)
* **Contact Information (contact_info)** (Select Multiple): How would you like to be contacted?
	+ 'Yes' - By email.
	+ 'No' - Not by email.
	+ 'Yes' - By phone.
	+ 'No' - Not by phone.

### Follow-up (Page 12, Question 19)
* **Follow-up (follow_up)** (Select Multiple): Would you like to be contacted for follow-up surveys?
	+ 'Yes' - Yes, I would like to be contacted.
	+ 'No' - No, I do not want to be contacted.

## Tips
