# knowledge_assessment_on_erp_and_itil - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess users' knowledge on ERP and ITIL systems, their familiarity, and current knowledge level.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill up this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the relevant ERP system you are familiar with from the dropdown list.
2. Choose your level of knowledge on ITIL from the dropdown list.
3. Check if you have used any ERP system in the past.
4. Check if you have used any ITIL framework in the past.
5. Select the knowledge areas you have knowledge about.
6. (Optional) Enter the number of hours you have spent on ERP system training.
7. (Optional) Enter the number of hours you have spent on ITIL training.
8. Enter any additional comments you have.
9. Enter your email address.
10. (Optional) Enter your phone number.
11. Enter the date and time you submitted this form.
12. Enter your name.
13. Enter your company name (optional).
14. Enter your job title (optional).
15. Enter your job function (optional).
16. Enter your location (optional).
17. Enter your department (optional).
18. Enter your team (optional).
19. (Optional) Enter any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **technology_surveys (1)** (text, required): Enter the relevant ERP system you are familiar with.
* **erp_systems (2)** (select_one, required): Choose the ERP system you have experience with from the dropdown list.
* **itil (3)** (select_multiple, required): Choose your knowledge level on ITIL from the dropdown list.
* **knowledge_level (4)** (select_one, required): Select your current knowledge level on ITIL from the dropdown list.
* **erp_system_used (5)** (select_multiple, required): Check if you have used any ERP system in the past.
* **itil_used (6)** (select_multiple, required): Check if you have used any ITIL framework in the past.
* **knowledge_areas (7)** (select_multiple, required): Select the knowledge areas you have knowledge about.
* **erp_system_training_hours (8)** (number, optional): Enter the number of hours you have spent on ERP system training.
* **itil_training_hours (9)** (number, optional): Enter the number of hours you have spent on ITIL training.
* **comments (10)** (text, optional): Enter any additional comments you have.
* **email (11)** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **phone (12)** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **date_submitted (13)** (date, required): Enter the date and time you submitted this form.
* **time_submitted (14)** (time, optional): Enter the time you submitted this form.
* **submitter_name (15)** (text, optional): Enter your name.
* **submitter_email (16)** (email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **submitter_phone (17)** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **submitter_comments (18)** (text, optional): Enter any comments you have.
* **submitter_company (19)** (text, optional): Enter your company name.
* **submitter_job_title (20)** (text, optional): Enter your job title.
* **submitter_job_function (21)** (text, optional): Enter your job function.
* **submitter_location (22)** (text, optional): Enter your location.
* **submitter_department (23)** (text, optional): Enter your department.
* **submitter_team (24)** (text, optional): Enter your team name.
* **submitter_notes (25)** (text, optional): Enter any additional notes you have.
