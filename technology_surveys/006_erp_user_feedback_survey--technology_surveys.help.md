# ERP User Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide will walk you through the steps to complete the ERP User Feedback Survey form. Please follow it carefully to ensure you provide accurate and relevant feedback.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Read and review the form carefully** before submitting it, making sure you understand each field and provide complete and accurate information.
2. **Complete each field according to the instructions** provided, and don't skip or leave blank any required fields.
3. **Be thorough and detailed in your feedback**, providing clear and concrete comments where possible.
4. **Only submit the form once**, avoiding duplication of effort.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. feedback
* **Feedback** (optional): This field is a text area where you can provide any comments or feedback on the ERP system. Be as detailed as possible, and explain any issues or concerns you have.

### 2. how_satisfied
* **How Satisfied** (optional): Rate your satisfaction level with the ERP system, with options 'Yes' or 'No'. This will help us understand your overall experience.

### 3. erp_adopted
* **ERP Adopted** (optional): Indicate whether you've adopted the ERP system, with options 'Yes' or 'No'. This will help us understand who has and hasn't adopted the system.

### 4. improvement_priorities
* **Improvement Priorities** (optional): Identify areas that need improvement, with options 'Yes', 'No', or select multiple options. Be specific and provide detailed comments where possible.

### 5. department
* **Department** (optional): Indicate whether you're associated with a department, with options 'Yes', or 'No'. This will help us understand which departments are using the system.

### 6. email
* **Email** (optional): Enter your email address if you're willing to receive follow-up questions or additional information.

### 7. contact_number
* **Contact Number** (optional): Enter your contact number if you have one and are willing to provide it for follow-up contact.

### 8. additional_comments
* **Additional Comments** (optional): Add any additional comments or thoughts about the ERP system, providing further insight into your experience.

# Tips

* If you're unsure about any field, please ask for clarification before submitting the form.
* Remember to be honest and detailed in your feedback, and provide examples or concrete issues where possible.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your department administrator or the survey administrator.
