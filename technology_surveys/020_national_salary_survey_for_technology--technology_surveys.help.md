# national-salary-survey-for-technology - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about national salaries for technology professionals.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
2. Provide accurate and honest answers to each question.
3. Make sure to select the correct options for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **National Salary Survey For Technology** (`national_salary_survey_form`, text, required): This is the form title, which you can leave blank.
* **Experience Level** (`experience_level`, select_one, required): Select your current experience level:
	+ Junior Developer
	+ Mid-Level Developer
	+ Senior Developer
	+ Leader
* **Average Salary** (`average_salary`, number, required): Enter your average annual salary.
* **Tech Stack** (`tech_stack`, select_multiple, required): Select all the technologies you are experienced with:
	+ Backend
	+ Frontend
	+ DevOps
	+ Data Science
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, required): Select your current job type:
	+ Full-time
	+ Part-time
	+ Freelance
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter your current location (city, state, or country).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your current job title.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_multiple, required): Select the size of your current or previous company:
	+ Small
	+ Medium
	+ Large
	+ Enterprise
* **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, number, required): Enter your years of experience.
* **Education** (`education`, text, required): Enter your current education level.
* **Industry** (`industry`, text, required): Enter the industry you currently work in.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your current or previous job.

Note: You can skip this form if you have filled it out recently, or if you are not a technology professional. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
