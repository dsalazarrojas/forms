# Financial Forecast Accuracy Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This survey is designed to gather feedback and insights from finance teams and leaders on their experiences, challenges, and suggestions for improving financial forecasting accuracy.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please answer the questions and provide any additional comments or feedback as you see fit. The form is divided into several sections, each focusing on a specific aspect of financial forecasting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**:
* **finance_teams**:
	+ Please provide any information or comments about your finance team.
* **finance_leaders**:
	+ Please provide any information or comments about your finance leaders.
* **last_quarters_forecasts**:
	+ Share any insights or observations about previous forecasts.
* **causes_of_variance**:
	+ Select all that apply from the list of common challenges in financial forecasting:
		- Inaccurate Data
		- Inadequate Analysis
		- Lack of Visibility
		- Poor Communication
		- Ineffective Processes
	+ Specify "Other (please describe)" if you have a different reason for variance.
* **ideas_to_improve**:
	+ Share any ideas you have on how to improve forecast accuracy.
* **comments**:
	+ Add any additional comments or feedback you might have.
* **finance_teams_comments**:
	+ Please provide any information or comments about your finance team.
* **finance_leaders_comments**:
	+ Please provide any information or comments about your finance leaders.

## Tips

* Please be as specific and detailed as possible when answering the questions.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please share them in the "comments" field.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, feel free to ask for clarification.
