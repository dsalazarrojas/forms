# hospital_digital_innovation_readiness_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the digital innovation readiness of a hospital.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select the answer that best represents your hospital's current state.
3. If you are unsure of an answer, select "Other" and provide a brief explanation in the comments section.
4. Make sure to complete all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).

* **What is the current state of digital transformation in your hospital?** (1) - This question assesses the current state of digital transformation in your hospital.
