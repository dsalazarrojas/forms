# digital_humanities_practice_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your experience and practices in the Digital Humanities field.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with your relevant information. You can save your progress and come back to the form later if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (1)
• **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
### Email (2)
• **Email**: Enter your institutional email address (e.g., `[name]@institution.edu`).
### Academic Institution (3)
• **Academic Institution**: Enter the name of the institution where you completed your studies or work.
### Job Title (4)
• **Job Title**: Enter your job title or position within your institution or organization.
### Department (5)
• **Department**: Enter the name of the department or unit within your institution or organization where you work.
### Year of Completion (6)
• **Year of Completion**: Enter the year you completed your studies or project.
### Time Spent (7)
• **Time Spent**: Enter a brief description of the time you've spent on Digital Humanities projects or activities.
### Fieldwork (8)
• **Fieldwork**: Enter a brief description of your fieldwork experience in the Digital Humanities field.
### Digital Humanities Area of Focus (9)
• **Digital Humanities Area of Focus**: Enter a brief description of the area of focus within the Digital Humanities field (e.g., DH theory, DH pedagogy, DH tools).
### Technology Used (10)
• **Technology Used**: Enter a list of technologies you've used in your Digital Humanities work or projects.
### Research Questions (11)
• **Research Questions**: Enter a brief description of the research questions you've explored in your Digital Humanities work or projects.
### Methods (12)
• **Methods**: Enter a brief description of the research methods you've employed in your Digital Humanities work or projects.
### Tools (13)
• **Tools**: Enter a list of tools you've used in your Digital Humanities work or projects.
### Data Management (14)
• **Data Management**: Enter a brief description of how you've managed data in your Digital Humanities work or projects.
### Data Storage (15)
• **Data Storage**: Enter a brief description of where and how you've stored data in your Digital Humanities work or projects.
### Digital Humanities Practices (16)
• **Digital Humanities Practices**: Enter a brief description of the Digital Humanities practices you've implemented or adopted in your work or projects.
### Digital Humanities Project (17)
• **Digital Humanities Project**: Enter a brief description of your Digital Humanities project or work.
### Project URL (18)
• **Project URL**: Enter the URL of your Digital Humanities project or work (if applicable).
### Collaborators (19)
• **Collaborators**: Enter a list of collaborators you've worked with on your Digital Humanities projects or work.
### Collaborators URL (20)
• **Collaborators URL**: Enter the URL of your collaborators' projects or work (if applicable).
### Funding (21)
• **Funding**: Enter a brief description of the funding sources for your Digital Humanities projects or work.
### Collaborators Funding (22)
• **Collaborators Funding**: Enter a brief description of the funding sources for your collaborators' work or projects.
### Project Duration (23)
• **Project Duration**: Enter a brief description of the duration of your Digital Humanities projects or work.

Please note that the fields marked with a "(required: false)" are optional, and you may choose not to fill them out.
