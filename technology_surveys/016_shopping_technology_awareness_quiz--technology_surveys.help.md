# Shopping Technology Awareness Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to assess a retail employee's awareness, skills, and needs regarding modern retail technology. It helps gather information on their role, department, and current usage of technology within the retail environment, which will be used to provide targeted training and support for employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required personal details such as First Name, Last Name, E-mail, Department, and Phone.
2. Answer the questions regarding your role, technology usage, and awareness by selecting the best fit option from the provided choices.
3. Complete any other optional questions that you feel relevant to your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Enter your first name as it is written on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Enter your last name as it is written on your official documents.
* **E-mail** (E-mail, email, required): Enter your work or personal email address.
* **Department** (Department, select_one, required): Select the department you belong to, which can be Retail, Technology, or Sales.
* **Role** (Role, select_multiple, required): Select the roles that describe your current position, which can be Sales Associate, Manager, or Leader.
* **Date** (Date, date, optional): This field is not required. You can leave it blank.
* **Time** (Time, time, optional): This field is not required. You can leave it blank.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **What is your role within a retail environment?** (What is your role within a retail environment?, select_one, required): Select the role that describes your position, which can be Sales Associate, Manager, or Leader.
* **How do you currently use technology in your job?** (How do you currently use technology in your job?, select_multiple, optional): Select the technologies you currently use for your job, which can be Mobile, Tablet, or Laptop.
* **How often do you use technology in your daily work?** (How often do you use technology in your daily work?, select_one, required): Select how often you use technology in your daily work, which can be Always, Frequently, or Rarely.
* **What technology do you use most often?** (What technology do you use most often?, select_multiple, optional): Select the technologies you use most often for your job, which can be Mobile, Tablet, or Laptop.
* **What is your level of awareness about modern retail technology?** (What is your level of awareness about modern retail technology?, select_one, required): Select your level of awareness, which can be Very High, High, Medium, or Low.
* **Which of the following technologies do you currently use?** (Which of the following technologies do you currently use?, select_multiple, optional): Select the technologies you are currently using, which can be EPOS, EPoS, MPOS, or OMS.
* **How would you rate your confidence about modern retail technology?** (How would you rate your confidence about modern retail technology?, select_one, required): Select your level of confidence, which can be Very High, High, Medium, or Low.
* **What do you feel most comfortable doing?** (What do you feel most comfortable doing?, select_multiple, required): Select how you feel most comfortable, which can be Self-directed, Guided, or Directed.
* **How does your current role influence your ability to use modern retail technology?** (How does your current role influence your ability to use modern retail technology?, select_multiple, required): Select how your role influences your ability to use technology, which can be 'True', 'False', or Undecided.
* **What technology skills do you have?** (What technology skills do you have?, select_multiple, optional): Select the technology skills you possess, which can be Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced.
* **What technology skills do you need more training on?** (What technology skills do you need more training on?, select_multiple, required): Select the technology skills you require additional training on, which can be Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced.
* **What technology skills do you currently lack?** (What technology skills do you currently lack?, select_multiple, required): Select the technology skills you are missing, which can be Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced.
* **What technology skills do you think you will need in the future?** (What technology skills do you think you will need in the future?, select_multiple, required): Select the technology skills you anticipate requiring in the future, which can be Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced.
* **What technology skills do you think are required for your role?** (What technology skills do you think are required for your role?, select_multiple, required): Select the technology skills you believe are necessary for your role, which can be Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced.
* **Do you currently use a mobile device for work?** (Do you currently use a mobile device for work?, select_one, optional): Select yes or no.
* **Feedback** (Feedback, text, optional): You can provide any additional feedback about the form or your experience with modern retail technology.
