# Software Usability Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the usability of a software application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Read each question and rating prompt carefully.
2. Answer the multiple-choice questions to the best of your ability.
3. Rate your experience with specific features using the scale provided.
4. Provide comments for each rating, if desired.
5. Share any additional thoughts or suggestions about the software's usability.
6. Submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
* **Introduction**: A brief overview of your experience with the software.

### Feedback
* **Feedback**: Share your thoughts and opinions about the software.

### Usability Questions
* **Is this feature intuitive?**: Consider whether the feature was easy to use and understand.
* **Is this feature useful?**: Think about whether the feature met your needs and expectations.
* **Is this feature well-documented?**: Evaluate whether the documentation was clear and sufficient.

### Feature Ratings
* **Feature Ratings**: Rate your experience with each feature on a scale of 1 to 5.
*   Use the scale to express your satisfaction level with each feature.

### Feature Ratings Comments
* **Feature Ratings Comments**: Provide comments about your ratings, if desired.

### Technology Survey Questions
* **How often do you use the software?**:
	+ Select how often you use the software (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly).
* **Have you used the software for a specific task?**:
	+ Indicate whether you used the software for a specific purpose or task.
* **What do you like about this software?**:
	+ Share your positive experiences or aspects of the software you enjoy.
* **What do you dislike about this software?**:
	+ Share your negative experiences or aspects of the software you dislike.

### Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments**: Share any additional thoughts or suggestions about the software's usability.

### Submit
* **Submit**: Click to submit your completed form and share your feedback.
