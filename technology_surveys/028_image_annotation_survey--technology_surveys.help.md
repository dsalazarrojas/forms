# image_annotation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **image_annotation_survey** form is designed to collect information from users on image annotation, including upload, details, and additional context.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Upload the image you want to annotate by clicking on the "Upload Image" field.
2. Provide detailed information about the image in the "Annotation Details" field by selecting one or more options from the list.
3. If required, sign the "Consent" field to acknowledge that you have read and understood the terms.
4. Enter the time spent annotating the image in the "Annotation Time" field.
5. Enter the date of annotation in the "Annotation Date" field.
6. If available, enter your email address in the "Email" field.
7. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
8. Provide any additional comments about the image in the "Additional Comments" field.
9. Describe the uploaded image in the "Upload Description" field.
10. Select relevant tags for the image from the "Upload Tags" field.
11. Choose one or more categories for the image from the "Upload Categories" field.
12. Select additional files related to the image from the "Upload Files" field.
