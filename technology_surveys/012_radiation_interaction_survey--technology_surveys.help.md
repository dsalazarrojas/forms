# radiation_interaction_survey_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about radiation exposure frequencies, durations, symptoms, and experiences related to radiation interactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

* Answer each question truthfully and accurately, based on your own experiences and knowledge.
* If you have not had any radiation exposure or interactions, you can skip the form entirely.
* Do not repeat questions that are identical or similar in nature, as this can lead to inaccurate or incomplete data.
