# PC Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PC Management Survey is designed to gather information about the quality of service provided by the PC team, including support, communication, and overall experience. Your feedback will help us understand your needs and concerns, enabling us to improve our services and better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully, and provide as much detail as possible in your answers.
2. Answer all questions, but you are not required to answer every question. The fields marked as "optional" can be skipped.
3. Select the most accurate response for each question, based on your experience with the PC team.
4. If you are unsure about any question, please provide a brief explanation or additional information to help us understand your concern.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Survey (1)**: This question is an open-ended field where you can provide any comments or feedback about your experience with the PC team. Be as detailed as possible.
* **Computer Type (2)**: Select the type of computer you use, which is essential for us to understand your needs and preferences.
* **Operating System (3)**: Choose the operating system(s) your computer runs on, as this information helps us identify potential compatibility issues.
* **Age of Computer (4)**: Please provide an estimate of how old your computer is. This information helps us gauge the level of support required for your device.
* **Support Issues (5)**: Describe any problems or concerns you have with computer support, including hardware, software, or other issues.
* **Support Rating (6)**: Rate the quality of support you receive from the PC team, based on your experience.
* **Email Issues (7)**: Select any issues you have experienced with emails related to PC support, such as slow responses or missing information.
* **Email Support (8)**: Choose the methods you would prefer for email support, such as phone calls, emails, or in-person visits.
* **Follow-up (9)**: Indicate whether you prefer follow-ups from the PC team, which helps us understand your preferences for communication.
* **Follow-up Frequency (10)**: Select how frequently you would like to receive follow-ups from the PC team.
* **Communication (11)**: Choose the aspects of communication you find most helpful from the PC team, such as clear and concise messages or timely responses.
* **IT Team (12)**: Select the qualities you appreciate most about the IT team, such as being helpful, friendly, or knowledgeable.
* **Communication Method (13)**: Choose the preferred method of communication with the PC team, such as email, phone, or video calls.
* **Contact Method (14)**: Select the methods you prefer for contacting the PC team, including phone numbers, emails, or in-person visits.

Please note that all questions are optional, but providing detailed and honest answers will greatly assist the PC team in delivering better services to you. If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to contact the PC team directly. Thank you for taking the time to help us improve our services.
