# Foreign Conflict Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Foreign Conflict Awareness Survey is designed to collect opinions and perceptions on current foreign conflicts. Your input will help us understand public awareness and sentiment, guiding our internal decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Survey Questions** (`survey_questions`, text, **optional**): Please provide your thoughts and feelings regarding the current foreign conflict situation. You can write as much or as little as you'd like.
2. **Information Sources** (`information_sources`, select_multiple, **required**): Choose all the sources that you have used to gather information about the conflict. Select all that apply.
	* Option 1
	* Option 2
	* Option 3
3. **Conflict Perceptions** (`conflict_perceptions`, text, **required**): Share your personal perception of the current conflict. Describe your understanding of the situation.
4. **Public Awareness** (`public_awareness`, number, **required**): On a scale of 1-10, indicate your assessment of the level of public awareness about the conflict.
5. **Form Submissions** (`form_submissions`, note, **optional**): If you have any additional comments or observations, you can include them here.

## Tips
- Be as concise as possible when answering the survey questions.
- Select all relevant information sources that apply to your understanding.
- Provide a clear and detailed description of your conflict perceptions.
- Rate public awareness accurately, based on your understanding.
- Use the "Form Submissions" field for any additional comments or observations that don't fit in the other fields.
