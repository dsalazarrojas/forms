# Marine Engine Performance Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data about marine engines for research or analysis purposes. It is not for creating or editing a marine engine.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Page 1 - Marine Engine Performance**: This is the first page of the form. It serves as an introduction to the rest of the form.
2. Select the **Engine Model** that best describes the marine engine you're surveying. Choose from the available options.
3. Enter the **Horsepower** of the engine.
4. Enter the **Year Manufactured** of the engine.
5. Select the **Operating Conditions** for the engine, if applicable.
6. Enter the **Engine Performance** data.
7. **Continue to the next page** for more questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Engine Model** (`Engine Model`, `select_one`, required): Select the model of the marine engine being surveyed.
* **Horsepower** (`Horsepower`, `number`, required): Enter the horsepower of the engine.
* **Year Manufactured** (`Year Manufactured`, `date`, required): Enter the year the engine was manufactured.
* **Operating Conditions** (`Operating Conditions`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the operating conditions for the engine (Normal, Abnormal, Critical).
* **Engine Performance** (`Engine Performance`, `number`, required): Enter the engine performance data.
* **Fuel Type** (`Fuel Type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the fuel type used by the engine (Diesel, Gasoline, Hybrid).
* **Emissions Impact** (`Emissions Impact`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the emissions impact of the engine (Low, Medium, High).
* **Engine Maintenance** (`Engine Maintenance`, `select_one`, optional): Select the engine maintenance status (Regular, Intermittent, Critical).
* **Operating Hours** (`Operating Hours`, `time`, optional): Enter the operating hours of the engine.
* **Engine Weight** (`Engine Weight`, `number`, optional): Enter the weight of the engine.
* **Engine Height** (`Engine Height`, `number`, optional): Enter the height of the engine.
* **Engine Width** (`Engine Width`, `number`, optional): Enter the width of the engine.
* **Engine Length** (`Engine Length`, `number`, optional): Enter the length of the engine.
* **Engine Power** (`Engine Power`, `number`, optional): Enter the engine power.
* **Engine Speed** (`Engine Speed`, `number`, optional): Enter the engine speed.
* **Fuel Capacity** (`Fuel Capacity`, `number`, optional): Enter the fuel capacity of the engine.
* **Fuel Consumption** (`Fuel Consumption`, `number`, optional): Enter the fuel consumption of the engine.
* **Engine Oil** (`Engine Oil`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of engine oil used (Synthetic, Regular, Diesel).
* **Transmission** (`Transmission`, `select_one`, optional): Select the transmission type (Manual, Automatic).
* **Drive Type** (`Drive Type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the drive type (Front wheel, Rear wheel, All wheel drive).
* **Engine Noise** (`Engine Noise`, `number`, optional): Enter the engine noise level.
* **Engine Emissions** (`Engine Emissions`, `number`, optional): Enter the engine emissions level.
* **Engine Warranty** (`Engine Warranty`, `select_one`, optional): Select the engine warranty status (Valid, Invalid).
* **Submit** (`Submit`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or submit the form.
