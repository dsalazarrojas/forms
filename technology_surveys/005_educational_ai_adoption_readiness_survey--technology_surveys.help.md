# Educational AI Adoption Readiness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to assess the readiness of educational institutions to adopt AI technology. This survey is designed to collect data on your institution's current state of AI adoption, including your understanding of AI, your willingness to invest in AI technology, and your current challenges and concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer all questions honestly and accurately.
2. Please note that some fields are optional and can be left blank if not applicable.
3. Make sure to submit the form only once you have finished answering all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Technology Surveys** (<type: text>): This field is a free-form text field where you can describe your institution's current technology surveys or assessments that you have conducted or are planning to conduct in the near future.
* **Institutional Readiness** (<type: number>): This field is a numerical field where you can input a score between 0 and 10 to indicate your institution's current state of AI readiness. A score of 0 means your institution is not ready for AI adoption, while 10 means your institution is highly ready.
* **AI Readiness Score** (<type: number>): This field is also a numerical field where you can input a score between 0 and 10 to indicate your institution's current state of AI readiness in terms of AI-specific metrics. A score of 0 means your institution is not ready for AI adoption, while 10 means your institution is highly ready.
* **Educational AI Adoption Readiness Survey** (<type: note>): This field is a free-form text field where you can describe your institution's current AI adoption plans, including any AI-related projects or initiatives you have or are planning to start in the near future.
