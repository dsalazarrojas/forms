# Education Technology Product Demo Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for participants to provide their details, consent, and agreement for a product demo. It is essential to fill in all the required fields to ensure that we can proceed with the demo and communicate with you effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant details, including your name, company name, and contact information.
2. Choose the correct option for your participation consent.
3. Indicate your preferred product demo date and time.
4. Select your preferred participant signature and company signature date.
5. Finally, click the "Submit" button to confirm your information.

* **Participant Details** (`1`): Please fill in your name and contact details. This will help us keep in touch with you after the demo.

* **Product Demo Date** (`5`): Choose the date and time you would like to have the product demo.
* **Participant Consent** (`6`): Please select the option that best represents your agreement to participate in the demo.
* **Participant Comments** (`7`): If you have any additional comments or questions, please provide them here.
* **Participant Signature** (`8`): Choose the signature that best represents your company or organization.
* **Company Information** (`9`): Provide a brief description of your company or organization.
* **Company Name** (`10`): Enter your company name as it appears on your business card or ID.
* **Company Address** (`11`): Provide your company address for us to contact you effectively.
* **Company Phone** (`12`): Enter your company phone number.
* **Company Email** (`13`): Provide your company email address.
* **Company Website** (`14`): Enter your company website.
* **Participant Agreement** (`15`): Please select the option that best represents your agreement to the terms and conditions of the demo.
* **Company Agreement** (`16`): Select the option that best represents your company's agreement to the terms and conditions of the demo.
* **Participant Declaration** (`17`): Provide a brief explanation of your declaration and agreement to the terms and conditions.
