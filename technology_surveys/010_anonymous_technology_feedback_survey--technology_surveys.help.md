**Anonymous Technology Feedback Survey - Help Guide**

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather anonymous feedback from users about their experience with various products or features. Your input will help us understand areas for improvement and make informed decisions about future development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*) in the label.
2. Answer each question as thoroughly as possible.
3. If you have additional comments or questions, feel free to provide them in the open-ended fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product or tool name** (*required*): This is the name of the product or feature you are reviewing. Please type the exact name as it appears in the product or feature.
* **Overall satisfaction 0 to 10** (*required*): Rate your overall satisfaction with the product or feature, with 0 being very dissatisfied and 10 being very satisfied.
* **Biggest issue encountered** (optional): Describe the primary problem you faced while using the product or feature. This can be a bug, a feature that didn't work as expected, or any other issue that hindered your experience.
* **Feature requests or improvements** (optional): Suggest any changes that would most improve your experience with the product or feature.
* **Usability rating** (optional, but recommended): Rate how easy or difficult it is to complete core tasks with the product or feature. Choose from Very difficult, Somewhat difficult, Neutral, Somewhat easy, or Very easy.
* **Environment used** (optional): Describe the device and platform where you used the product or feature (e.g., desktop, laptop, mobile phone, etc.).
* **Allow follow up if we need clarification** (optional): Check the box if you would be okay with us reaching out to you for further information or clarification on your feedback. This will allow us to contact you without knowing your identity, if you leave it blank, your identity will not be shared.
