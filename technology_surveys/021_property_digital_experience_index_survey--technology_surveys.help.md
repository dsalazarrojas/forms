# Property Digital Experience Index Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about the digital experience of properties. The data collected will help us understand the current state of properties' online presence and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required information in the respective fields. Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
2. Select the relevant options for the "digital_experience" field from the dropdown menu.
3. Enter your name, email, and other required fields.
4. Submit the form once you have filled in all the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **property_id** (text, required): Enter the unique identifier for the property.
* **contact_number** (text, required): Enter the contact number for the property.
* **contact_email** (email, required): Enter the contact email for the property.
* **property_name** (text, required): Enter the name of the property.
* **digital_experience** (select_multiple, required): Select the time frame when you last experienced the property's digital presence:
	+ Digital Experience (1-2 months ago)
	+ Digital Experience (2-6 months ago)
	+ Digital Experience (6-12 months ago)
	+ Digital Experience (over 12 months ago)
* **property_score** (number, required): Enter a rating for the property's digital experience.
* **comments** (text, required): Enter any comments or feedback about the property's digital experience.
* **rating** (number, required): Enter a rating for the property's digital experience.
* **submit_form** (text, required): Enter the form submission type:
	+ Submission via Jotform Mobile App
	+ Submission via Jotform Web App
	+ Submission via Email
* **name** (text, required): Enter your name.
* **email** (email, required): Enter your email.
* **form_submission_type** (select_one, required): Select the method through which you submitted this form:
	+ Submission via Jotform Mobile App
	+ Submission via Jotform Web App
	+ Submission via Email
* **date** (date, required): Enter the date you completed this form.
* **time** (time, required): Enter the time you completed this form.
* **property_location** (text, required): Enter the location of the property.
* **property_country** (text, required): Enter the country of the property.
* **property_region** (text, required): Enter the region of the property.
* **property_postal** (text, required): Enter the post code of the property.
* **property_state** (text, required): Enter the state of the property.
* **property_city** (text, required): Enter the city of the property.
* **property_zip** (text, required): Enter the zip code of the property.
* **property_address** (text, required): Enter the address of the property.
* **property_notes** (text, required): Enter any additional information about the property.
* **property_feedback** (text, required): Enter any feedback about the property.
* **form_completed** (date, required): Enter the date you completed this form.
