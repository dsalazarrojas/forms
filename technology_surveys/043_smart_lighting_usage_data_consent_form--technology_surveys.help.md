# Smart Lighting Usage Data Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Lighting Usage Data Consent Form is a document that asks participants to give their consent to collect and use their smart lighting usage data for research purposes. This form is intended to ensure that participants understand how their data will be used and are willing to provide it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form title and description to understand the purpose and use of the data.
2. Answer the question "Do you give us consent to collect your smart lighting usage data?" with either "True" or "False".
3. If you give your consent, provide your contact details, including your first name, last name, email, and phone number.
4. Add any additional participant notes if required or desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information (Page 1)** (<participant_information>, text, optional): This field is a general information section where you can provide any additional details about yourself as a participant. This is entirely optional.
* **Do you give us consent to collect your smart lighting usage data?** (<consent, select_one, optional): This question asks you to give your consent to collect your smart lighting usage data. Please select "True" if you are comfortable with your data being collected, and "False" if you are not.
* **Contact Details** (<contact_details, text, optional): This section is where you will provide your contact details. Please fill in your first name, last name, email, and phone number.
* **First Name** (<contact_first_name, text, optional): This is the field for your first name.
* **Last Name** (<contact_last_name, text, optional): This is the field for your last name.
* **Email** (<contact_email, email, optional): This is the field for your email address.
* **Phone** (<contact_phone, text, optional): This is the field for your phone number.
* **Participant Notes** (<participant_notes, text, optional): This field is for any additional participant notes you may want to provide.

## Tips

* Please fill in all the required fields to ensure that your consent and contact details are complete.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form or the data collection process, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* The data collected will be handled in accordance with data protection regulations.
* If you are not comfortable providing your consent, please do not fill in the form.
