# Web Services Expertise Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your expertise in web services, including your experience with APIs, web services expertise, current usage patterns, proficiency in APIs, tools used, and common usage patterns.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct answers for each field based on your actual experience and expertise.
2. Fill in any text fields (e.g., common usage patterns) with specific details about your experience.
3. Make sure to provide accurate and honest answers as this will help us refine our services to better meet your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Introduction
* **Introduction (Note)**: This is a brief introduction to the form and is required to be filled but does not require a specific answer.

### Experience with APIs
* **Experience with APIs (Select Multiple)**: Choose all the APIs you have experience with:
	+ REST
	+ GraphQL
	+ SOAP
	+ Other (please specify)

### Expertise in Web Services
* **Expertise in Web Services (Select One)**: Choose your level of expertise:
	+ Beginner
	+ Intermediate
	+ Advanced
	+ Expert

### Current Usage of Web Services
* **Current usage of Web Services (Select Multiple)**: Choose how you currently use web services:
	+ Internal
	+ External
	+ Cloud
	+ On-premises

### Proficiency in APIs
* **Proficiency in APIs (Select Multiple)**: Choose your proficiency levels with APIs:
	+ REST
	+ GraphQL
	+ SOAP
	+ Other (please specify)

### Tools Used for Web Services
* **Tools used for Web Services (Select Multiple)**: Choose the tools you use for web services:
	+ Postman
	+ cURL
	+ SoapUI
	+ Postman/SoapUI
	+ Other (please specify)

### Common Usage Patterns
* **Common usage patterns (Text)**: Fill in specific details about your common usage patterns.

### Web Services Experience
* **Web Services Experience (Select Multiple)**: Choose your level of experience:
	+ Less than 1 year
	+ 1-2 years
	+ 2-5 years
	+ 5-10 years
	+ More than 10 years

### Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments (Text)**: Provide any additional comments or details about your experience with web services.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide honest and accurate answers as this will help us refine our services to better meet your needs.
* If you have any specific questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
* The survey is completely anonymous and any information will be treated confidentially.
