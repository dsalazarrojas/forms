# Cryptographic Communication Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Cryptographic Communication Survey is designed to gather feedback and opinions from various stakeholders about their experiences and perceptions with cryptographic communication technologies. This guide will walk you through each section of the form, ensuring you complete it accurately and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review each page carefully and answer every question that applies to you.
2. Select the options that best match your thoughts and experiences.
3. Enter your answers in the required fields, marked as "required".
4. You may skip questions that do not apply to you.
5. Use the "Note" fields to provide any additional comments or explanations.
