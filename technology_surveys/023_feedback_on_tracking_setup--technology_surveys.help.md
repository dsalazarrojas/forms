# feedback_on_tracking_setup - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback on tracking setup, helping us understand how users interact with our system and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Tracking Setup Form" page from the menu.
2. Review each question carefully and provide accurate information about your goals and objectives.
3. Use the "What Are Your Other Goals" and similar fields to describe any additional goals or objectives you may have.
4. Submit your feedback by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tracking Setup Form** (`Tracking Setup Form`, `text`, required): 
    This is the main section of the form, where you will specify your goals and objectives for tracking.
* **What are your goals** (`What are your goals`, `text`, required): 
    Please describe your main goals and objectives related to tracking.
* **What are your other goals** (`What are your other goals`, `text`, optional): 
    If you have additional goals or objectives, describe them here.
* **Other Goals** (`Other Goals`, `text`, optional): 
    This is another field for specifying any other goals or objectives.
* **More Other Goals** (`More Other Goals`, `text`, optional): 
    Use this field to add even more secondary goals or objectives.
* **Even More Other Goals** (`Even More Other Goals`, `text`, optional): 
    Add more details about your secondary goals or objectives.
* **Extremely More Other Goals** (`Extremely More Other Goals`, `text`, optional): 
    This field is intended to capture any additional goals or objectives that are not already listed.
* **Extremely More Other Goals 2** (`Extremely More Other Goals 2`, `text`, optional): 
    Another field for specifying more details about your goals or objectives.
* **Extremely More Other Goals 2** (`Extremely More Other Goals 2`, `text`, optional): 
    Use this field to add even more details about your goals or objectives.
* **Extremely More Other Goals 4** (`Extremely More Other Goals 4`, `text`, optional): 
    The last field for specifying any additional goals or objectives.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when describing your goals and objectives.
* Use the "What Are Your Other Goals" fields to provide additional context.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
