# Smart Home Feature Value Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Smart Home Feature Value Survey aims to gauge the value and priorities of various features within the smart home domain. This form helps us understand which features are most important or relevant to users, which ones have high interest levels, and how users value them.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the smart home feature you are interested in from the options provided on the "What is the smart home feature you are interested in?" page.
2. On the "How important is pricing for this feature?" page, use the number to indicate how important the pricing is to you.
3. On the "What is the price expectation for this feature?" page, enter the price you expect to pay for this feature.
4. On the "How interested are you in this feature?" page, select the level of interest you have for the feature.
5. On the "How important is this feature to you?" page, select all options that apply.
6. On the "What is the name of this feature?" page, enter the name of the feature.
7. (Optional) On the "How do you expect to use this feature?" and "Other expectation" pages, provide any additional information about your expectations.
8. (Optional) If you have any additional expectations not listed, use the "More Expectation" page to provide details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the smart home feature you are interested in?** (`title`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the smart home feature you are interested in.
* **How important is pricing for this feature?** (`price_importance_page`, `number`, required): Use the number to indicate how important the pricing is to you.
* **What is the price expectation for this feature?** (`price_pricing_page`, `number`, required): Enter the price you expect to pay for this feature.
* **How interested are you in this feature?** (`interest_page`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of interest you have for the feature.
* **How important is this feature to you?** (`importance_page`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all options that apply to indicate how important the feature is to you.
* **What is the name of this feature?** (`feature_page`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the feature.
* **How do you expect to use this feature?** (`expectation_page`, `text`, optional): Provide details on how you expect to use the feature.
* **Other expectation** (`other_expectation_page`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional expectations not listed.
* **More Expectation** (`expectation_page_10`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information.
