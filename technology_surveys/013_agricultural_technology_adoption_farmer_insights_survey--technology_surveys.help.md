# Agricultural Technology Adoption Farmer Insights Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to collect information on your experiences, perceptions, and opinions on the adoption of new agricultural technologies. Your input is invaluable in helping us understand the challenges and opportunities associated with these technologies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form by providing the required and optional information as prompted.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select one of the available options that best reflects your current situation or attitude.
3. For text fields, provide a brief description or answer as prompted.
4. You can skip questions that do not apply to you or your farm.
5. When you have completed the form, click the submit button to send your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Farmer name**: Optional for anonymous responses. If you prefer not to share your name, you can leave this field blank.
* **Region**: Enter the geographic region or district where your farm is located.
* **Farm size in hectares**: Approximate cultivated area of your farm.
* **Awareness of new agricultural technologies**: Indicate if you are aware of recent technologies. Please select 'True' if you are aware, and 'False' if you are not.
* **Adoption status**: Select the option that best describes your current use of new technology. Please choose from:
	+ Not aware
	+ Aware but not using
	+ Using partially
	+ Fully using
* **Main barriers to adoption**: If you have difficulty adopting new technologies, please describe the main challenges you face. (e.g., cost, training, availability, trust, or other)
* **Perceived benefits**: If you are using new technologies, please list the benefits you perceive from their use (e.g., productivity, cost savings, labor reduction, or quality)
* **Interest in training or demonstration**: Indicate if you would be interested in hands-on training or demonstration of new technologies. Please select:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Maybe
* **Interest in financing options for technology**: Indicate if you would consider accessing credit to adopt new technologies. Please select:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Additional comments**: Provide any other feedback or comments you may have on technology adoption.

## Tips
- Please be as honest and detailed as possible in your responses.
- If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask for clarification.
- Your input will be kept confidential and used to improve the development and deployment of new agricultural technologies.
