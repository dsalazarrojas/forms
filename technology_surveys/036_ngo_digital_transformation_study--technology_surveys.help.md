# NGO Digital Transformation Study - Help Guide
## Purpose
The NGO Digital Transformation Study is a survey designed to assess the digital transformation readiness of NGOs. This study will help us understand the tools used, skills needed, and overall satisfaction levels among NGOs. Your input is valuable in helping us shape the digital transformation landscape for the sector.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer each question carefully, taking into account your current digital transformation status.
- For select multiple and select one questions, select all that apply.
- For number and text questions, enter your response in the corresponding format (e.g., number for tool usage frequency, text for tool evaluation criteria).
- Please answer all questions honestly and accurately, as your responses will help us better understand the sector's needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This is an introduction page to get familiarized with the form.
* **Tools Used**: Select all the tools you use for your NGO's digital transformation.
	+ Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **Skills Needed**: Select all the skills you need for your NGO's digital transformation.
	+ Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **Digital Literacy**: Enter your current level of digital literacy.
* **Digital Abilities**: Enter your current digital abilities.
* **Needs Assessment**: This is a notes section for you to add any specific needs or areas that require attention in your digital transformation journey.
* **Data Analysis**: Enter the date of the data analysis you have performed.
* **Tool Satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction level with the tools you use for digital transformation.
	+ Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **Skill Satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction level with the skills you need for digital transformation.
	+ Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **Digital Skills**: Select all the digital skills you have.
	+ Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **Digital Literacy Score**: Enter your digital literacy score.
* **Skills Training**: Select all the skills you received training for.
	+ Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **Training Satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction level with the training received.
	+ Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **Digital Tools**: Select all the digital tools you have used.
	+ Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **Tool Evaluation Criteria**: Enter your criteria for evaluating digital tools.
* **Tool Usage Frequency**: Enter the frequency of tool usage.
* **Digital Skills Training**: Select all the digital skills you received training for.
	+ Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **Training Type**: Select the type of training you received.
	+ Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **Training Provider**: Select all the training providers you received training from.
	+ Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **Tool Cost**: Enter the cost of the digital tools used.
* **Tool Implementation Time**: Enter the time taken to implement digital tools.
* **Tool Evaluation Criteria**: Enter the criteria used for evaluating digital tools.
* **Digital Skills Assessment**: Enter your assessment of digital skills.
* **Skills Assessment Criteria**: Select all the criteria you use to assess digital skills.
	+ Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction level with the digital transformation process.
	+ Options: 'Yes', 'No'
