# Dynamic Content Code Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dynamic Content Code Survey is designed to collect feedback on the dynamic content code used in our applications. This survey aims to gather information about how you use the dynamic content code, what you like or dislike about it, and any suggestions you may have for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2. Make sure to provide your name and email address so we can contact you for follow-up questions or to confirm your submission.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
4. If you have any additional comments or concerns, please use the "Additional Comments" field to share them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Questions**: Provide a brief description of the dynamic content code you were using when you answered this survey.
* **Dynamic Content Code Usage**: Describe how you used the dynamic content code, what was its purpose, and in what context.
* **Improvement Priorities**: Please indicate whether "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the following items are priorities for improvement:
	+ Yes/No
* **Time Spent on Dynamic Content Code**: Provide the approximate time you spent using the dynamic content code.
* **Satisfaction Rating**: Rate your satisfaction with the dynamic content code on a scale of 1-5:
	+ Label 1: Very dissatisfied
	+ Label 2: Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ Label 3: Neutral
	+ Label 4: Somewhat satisfied
	+ Label 5: Very satisfied
* **Sentiment Over Time**: If applicable, provide the date when you first used the dynamic content code and when you last used it.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or concerns you have about the dynamic content code.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you for follow-up questions or to confirm your submission.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you for follow-up questions or to confirm your submission.
* **Team Members**: Indicate whether you are a team member:
	+ Yes/No
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any additional comments or concerns that do not fit in the other fields, please provide them here.
* **Rating**: Enter a numerical rating of your experience with the dynamic content code.
* **Time Spent on Task**: If applicable, provide the time you spent using the dynamic content code.
* **Survey Completed At**: Date and time of completion.
* **Dynamic Content Code Survey ID**: A unique identifier for the survey.
* **User ID**: A unique identifier for the user.
* **User Name**: Your name.
* **Department**: Enter your department name.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments.
* **Department Name**: Enter your department name.
* **Job Title Name**: Enter your job title name.
* **Team Member**: Indicate whether you are a team member:
	+ Yes/No
