# usage_context_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your usage habits and behaviors, helping us understand how you interact with tools, platforms, and services. Your input will aid in improving our services and providing more efficient solutions for our users.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by clicking on the "Submit" button to begin the survey.
2. The form is divided into several sections, each focused on a specific aspect of your usage habits.
3. Answer each question honestly and concisely, providing as much detail as possible.
4. For the "How often do you use" question, select all applicable options that apply to your usage habits.
5. For the "Why do you use" and "Why don't you use" questions, provide a brief explanation in the text field.
6. If you have any improvement suggestions or feedback, please type them in the "Improvement Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **When do you usually use** (`usage_context`, date): This field is used to track when you typically use a particular tool, platform, or service. Please select a date that represents your usual usage pattern.
- **Where do you usually use** (`where_do_you_usually_use`, select_one): This field is used to determine the location where you usually use a particular tool, platform, or service. Select the option that best describes your typical usage location.
- **How do you use** (`how_do_you_use`, text): This field is used to gather information about your usage habits. Please provide a brief description of how you use a particular tool, platform, or service.
- **Why do you use** (`why_do_you_use`, text): This field is used to understand your motivations and reasons for using a particular tool, platform, or service. Provide a brief explanation.
- **How often do you use** (`frequency`, select_multiple): This field is used to determine the frequency of your usage. Select all applicable options that apply to your usage habits.
- **Why don't you use** (`why_don_t_use`, text): This field is used to gather information about your limitations or constraints in using a particular tool, platform, or service. Please provide a brief explanation.
- **Improvement Feedback** (`improvement_feedback`, note): This field is used to gather any suggestions or feedback you may have for improving our services. Type in your thoughts, and we will take them into consideration.
- **Assisted Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one): This field is used to determine which tool or platform is assisting you in your usage. Select the option that best describes the tool you're using.
- **Note**: If you have any additional information or comments, please provide them in the "Improvement Feedback" field. This is a dedicated space for your thoughts and suggestions.
