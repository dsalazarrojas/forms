# Student AI Tools Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect your experiences and opinions on AI tools used in your studies or work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Start" button to begin.
2. Fill out each field according to the instructions below.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, required): Enter a unique number to identify your response.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Choose the category that best describes the AI tool you are answering about:
	+ Technology Surveys
	+ Other
	+ Unknown
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Describe the AI tool you are answering about in a few words.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the specific tool's name or identifier.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): This is a duplicate field for you to add additional details about the AI tool.

Note: You must complete both "Description" fields if you choose to fill them out.
