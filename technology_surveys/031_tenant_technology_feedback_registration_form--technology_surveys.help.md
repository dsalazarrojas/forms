# Tenant Technology Feedback Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from tenants about the technology provided by their housing units.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each field as accurately as possible.
2. Be as detailed as you can be when providing feedback.
3. Ensure that you are providing unique and relevant comments.

* Be specific and detailed when providing feedback.
