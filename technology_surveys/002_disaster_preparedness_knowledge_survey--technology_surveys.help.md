# Disaster Preparedness Knowledge Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the steps to complete the Disaster Preparedness Knowledge Survey, which is designed to assess your household's disaster preparedness knowledge and capabilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions on each page to the best of your ability. Each question will help us understand your household's experience and knowledge regarding disaster preparedness. Please answer the questions as they apply to your household situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your role in the household?** (<name> `role`, required/optional: true)
	+ Please select one of the following options that best describes your role within the household:
		- Parent: A responsible adult caring for one or more children.
		- Spouse: A partner or adult family member in a marital relationship.
		- Adult Child: An adult who is a child of the household head.
		- Other: Any other adult living within the household.
* **Do you have an Emergency Plan?** (<name> `has_plan`, required/optional: true)
	+ Please select one of the following options:
		- Yes: Your household has a plan in place for emergency situations.
		- No: Your household does not have a plan in place.
* **Have you had to evacuate due to a disaster?** (<name> `evacuated`, required/optional: true)
	+ Please select one of the following options:
		- Yes: You or your household have had to evacuate due to a disaster.
		- No: You or your household have not had to evacuate due to a disaster.
* **Have you experienced a disaster in the past?** (<name> `experienced_disaster`, required/optional: true)
	+ Please select one of the following options:
		- Yes: You or your household have experienced a disaster in the past.
		- No: You or your household have not experienced a disaster in the past.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (<name> `emergency_contact_info`, required/optional: true)
	+ Please select one of the following options:
		- On a list in a visible location (e.g., fridge, near phone): You have a list of emergency contact information in a visible location.
		- In a separate notebook or binder: You have a notebook or binder with emergency contact information.
		- On a phone app (e.g., phone contacts): You have emergency contact information saved on a phone app.
* **Have you taken any training on disaster preparedness?** (<name> `disaster_preparedness_training`, required/optional: true)
	+ Please select one of the following options:
		- Yes: You or your household have taken disaster preparedness training.
		- No: You or your household have not taken disaster preparedness training.
* **Safe Room or Area** (<name> `safe_room`, required/optional: false)
	+ Please select one of the following options:
		- Yes, I have a safe room/area: You have designated a specific area for emergency shelter.
		- No, but I know what to do in case of a disaster: You have not designated a safe room/area but know the steps to take in case of a disaster.
