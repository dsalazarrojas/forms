# Lease Digitalization Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather feedback from users on their experiences with lease digitalization processes.
## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, please select the relevant options for each section.
2. Answer each question truthfully and provide detailed information where possible.
3. Be sure to review the explanations of each field below for more information on what is expected.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What type of property do you rent?** (`property_type`): Select the type of property you rent from the options provided.
* **How big is the property?** (`property_size`): Choose all that apply from the options provided.
* **How long is your lease?** (`lease_length`): Select the duration of your lease from the options provided.
* **How often do you lease?** (`lease_frequency`): Choose the frequency of your lease from the options provided.
* **How do you usually lease?** (`lease_method`): Select all that apply from the options provided.
* **What pain points do you have with current lease processes?** (`lease_pain_points`): Provide detailed information on your experiences with lease processes.
* **What areas could be improved?** (`lease_improvement_areas`): Choose all that apply from the options provided.
* **Do you have any other suggestions?** (`lease_suggestions`): Provide detailed information on any additional suggestions you may have.
* **Who is the property owner?** (`property_owner`): Select the status of the property owner.
* **Are you an employee?** (`employee`): Select the status of your employment.
* **What is your contact number?** (`contact_number`): Enter your contact number for follow-up purposes.
* **What is your email address?** (`email_address`): Enter your email address for follow-up purposes.
* **How would you rate your satisfaction with digital lease processes?** (`lease_survey_satisfaction`): Rate your satisfaction on a scale of 0 to 10.
* **How would you rate your improvement ideas?** (`lease_improvement_score`): Rate the importance of your improvement ideas on a scale of 0 to 10.

Note: Please provide detailed information for fields where possible. This will help us better understand your experiences and suggestions.
