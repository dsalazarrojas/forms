# Digital Learning Readiness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to gather information about your readiness to adopt digital learning solutions. It's a one-time survey that will help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through the form carefully before starting.
2. Fill in all required fields, marked as [required].
3. If you're unsure about an answer, provide the best answer you can or skip it if you don't know.
4. Don't worry about grammar or spelling errors.
5. Submit the form once you've completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Introduction** 
- **Introduction** (`text`, required: false): This is a text field where you can briefly describe yourself or introduce your background. It's not required, so feel free to skip it if you don't want to answer this question.

### **Demographic Info** 
- **Demographic_info** (`text`, required: false): This field is for providing any additional demographic information about yourself. It's not required, so you can skip it if you don't want to answer this question.

### **Learning Goals** 
- **Learning goals** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all that apply. This question is about your goals related to learning. You can choose one or more options:

  • Yes
  • No

### **Learning Preferences** 
- **Learning preferences** (`text`, required: false): This field is for providing any additional information about your learning preferences. It's not required, so feel free to skip it if you don't want to answer this question.

## Tips
- If you have any technical difficulties or questions, please contact us.
- If you've already completed this survey, please don't submit it again.
- Your input will help us improve our services.
