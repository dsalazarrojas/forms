# Pet Technology Demonstration Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users for a pet technology demonstration request. It is intended for internal use within the organization and is not intended for external use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the purpose of the form and ensure that you are using it for an internal purpose.
2. Select the pet type(s) from the options provided on Page 2.
3. Enter the date of birth for the pet, including the month and year of birth.
4. Enter the contact name, email address, and phone number of the person requesting the demonstration.
5. Provide any additional comments or requests for the demonstration.
6. Indicate if you are interested in any additional demonstrations.
7. Submit the form to complete the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Type (Page 2)** (`page_2_pet_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or more pet types from the options provided.
* **Contact Name (Page 1)** (`page_1_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person requesting the demonstration.
* **Contact Email (Page 1)** (`page_1_contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the person requesting the demonstration.
* **Contact Phone (Page 1)** (`page_1_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the person requesting the demonstration.
* **Comments (Page 1)** (`page_1_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or requests for the demonstration.
* **Demos Wanted (Page 1)** (`page_1_demos_wanted`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate if you are interested in any additional demonstrations.
* **Date of Birth (Page 2)** (`page_2_dob`, `date`, required): Enter the date of birth for the pet, in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Month (Page 2)** (`page_2_dob_month`, `select_one`, required): Select the month of the pet's birth from the options provided.
* **Year (Page 2)** (`page_2_dob_year`, `select_one`, required): Select the year of the pet's birth from the options provided.
* **Day (Page 2)** (`page_2_dob_day`, `select_one`, required): Select the day of the pet's birth from the options provided.

## Tips
* Ensure that you are using the form for an internal purpose.
* Be thorough in completing the form to provide accurate information.
* Contact the organization's support team if you have any questions or concerns.
