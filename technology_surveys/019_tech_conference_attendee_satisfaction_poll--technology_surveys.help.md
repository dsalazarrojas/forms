# Tech Conference Attendee Satisfaction Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from conference attendees on their overall experience at the conference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the name of the conference you attended.
2. Provide the date of the conference.
3. Select whether you attended a session.
4. Enter the name of the speaker(s) who gave the session.
5. Rate the speaker(s) who gave the session.
6. Rate the quality of the session.
7. Select the speaker(s) who gave the session.
8. Rate the relevance of the content presented.
9. Rate the value provided by the speaker.
10. Rate the quality of the food provided.
11. Rate the service provided with the food.
12. Enter the name of your organization.
13. Enter your contact email.
14. Enter your contact phone number.
15. (Optional) Add any additional comments you have about the conference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the conference you attended.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the conference.
* **Session Attended** (`session_attended`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" if you attended a session, "No" otherwise.
* **Speaker** (`speaker_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the speaker(s) who gave the session you attended.
* **Speaker** (`speaker_topic`, text, required): Enter a brief description or topic of the speaker's session.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the conference on a scale of 1-5.
* **Session Quality** (`session_quality`, number, required): Rate the quality of the session on a scale of 1-5.
* **Speaker Reported** (`speaker_reported`, select_multiple, required): Select the speaker(s) who gave the session you attended.
* **Relevance of Content** (`content_relevance`, number, required): Rate the relevance of the content presented on a scale of 1-5.
* **Speaker Value** (`speaker_value`, select_one, required): Rate the value provided by the speaker on a scale of 1-5.
* **Food Quality** (`food_quality`, number, required): Rate the quality of the food provided on a scale of 1-5.
* **Food Service** (`food_service`, select_multiple, required): Select the level of service provided with the food.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments you have about the conference.
* **Session Attended Venue** (`session_attended_venue`, select_one, optional): Select the venue where the session was held.
* **Session Attended Room** (`session_attended_room`, select_multiple, optional): Select the rooms where the session was held.
* **Speaker Attended Rating** (`speaker_attendee_rating`, select_multiple, optional): Rate the speaker(s) who gave the session.
* **Session Quality Rating** (`session_quality_rating`, number, optional): Rate the quality of the session on a scale of 1-5.
* **Speaker Content** (`speaker_content`, select_multiple, optional): Rate the content presented by the speaker(s).
* **Overall Attendee Satisfaction** (`overall_attendee_satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the conference on a scale of 1-5.
* **Overall Speaker Satisfaction** (`overall_speaker_satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate the overall satisfaction with the speaker(s) on a scale of 1-5.
* **Session Venue** (`session_venue`, select_multiple, optional): Rate the venue where the session was held.
* **Overall Venue Satisfaction** (`overall_venue_satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate the overall satisfaction with the venue on a scale of 1-5.

## Tips
* Please provide detailed and honest feedback to help us improve future conferences.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please add them in the "Additional Comments" field.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your feedback is submitted correctly.
