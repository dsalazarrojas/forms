# user_interface_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their interface experience. The information gathered will help the development team identify areas for improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance the user interface.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select all the issues you have encountered with the user interface by choosing "Yes" or "No" for each option.
2.  On the "satisfaction Level" field, rate your overall satisfaction with the user interface on a scale of 1-5.
3.  If you have any suggestions for improvement, please type them in the "Suggestion for Improvement" field.
4.  Provide your contact information (email address or phone number) so that we can follow up with you regarding your feedback.
5.  Select the issue(s) that you experienced by choosing one of the provided options in the "Feedback" field.
6.  If you have taken a screenshot of the issue, please upload it as a "Note" under the "Screenshot" field.
7.  Briefly describe the issue you encountered in the "Screenshot Description" field.
8.  If you want to provide your email address, please type it in the "Email" field.
9.  If you want to provide your phone number, please type it in the "Phone" field.
10.  If you want to confirm your email address, please type it in the "Email Confirmation" field.
11.  If you want to confirm your phone number, please type it in the "Phone Confirmation" field.
12.  If you want to provide your email address for follow up, please type it in the "Email Address" field.
13.  If you want to confirm your email address, please type it in the "Email Verification" field.
14.  If you want to provide your phone number for follow up, please type it in the "Phone Number" field.
15.  If you want to confirm your phone number, please type it in the "Phone Verification" field.
16.  If you have taken multiple screenshots of the issue, please upload them as "Notes" under the "Screenshot 1", "Screenshot 2", etc. fields.
17.  Briefly describe what each screenshot shows in the corresponding "Screenshot Description" field.
18.  Please review the information you provided to ensure it is accurate before submitting the form.
19.  Click "Submit" to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Interface Survey** (Page 1, select_multiple): Select all the issues you have encountered with the user interface.
	+ "Yes" or "No" for each option.
* **Satisfaction Level** (Page 1, number): Rate your overall satisfaction with the user interface on a scale of 1-5.
* **Suggestion for Improvement** (Page 1, text): Type any suggestions for improvement you may have.
* **Contact Information** (Page 1, email): Provide your email address or phone number for follow up.
* **Feedback** (Page 1, select_one): Select the issue you experienced.
	+ Choose one of the provided options.
* **Screenshot** (Page 1, note): Upload any screenshots of the issue.
* **Screenshot Description** (Page 1, text): Briefly describe what the screenshot shows.
* **Email** (Page 1, email): Provide your email address.
* **Phone** (Page 1, text): Provide your phone number.
* **Email Confirmation** (Page 1, email): Confirm your email address.
* **Phone Confirmation** (Page 1, text): Confirm your phone number.
* **Email Address** (Page 1, email): Provide your email address for follow up.
* **Phone Number** (Page 1, text): Provide your phone number for follow up.
* **Email Verification** (Page 1, email): Confirm your email address.
* **Phone Verification** (Page 1, text): Confirm your phone number.
* **Screenshot 1** (Page 1, note): Upload a screenshot of the issue.
* **Screenshot 2** (Page 1, note): Upload another screenshot of the issue.
* ...
* **Report Screenshot 1** (Page 1, note): Upload a screenshot to report the issue.
* **Report Screenshot 2** (Page 1, note): Upload another screenshot to report the issue.
