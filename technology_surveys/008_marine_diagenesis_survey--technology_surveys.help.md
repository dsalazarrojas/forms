# Marine Diagenesis Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Marine Diagenesis Survey form is a tool used to collect information about marine diagenesis samples. It helps researchers gather data on various aspects of marine diagenesis, including project details, sample types, collection methods, and supporting files. This form aims to provide a structured approach to collecting comprehensive information about marine diagenesis samples.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Marine Diagenesis Survey form, follow these steps:

1. Read through the introduction page (Page 1) to understand the purpose and objectives of the survey.
2. Select the relevant options for your project details (Page 2).
3. Choose the correct sample type(s) that apply to your sample (Page 3).
4. Enter the sample location and collection method (Page 4 and 5).
5. Provide any supporting files or information (Page 6).
6. Select any diagenetic features present in your sample (Page 7).
7. Enter the method used to collect your sample (Page 8).
8. Enter your sample ID (Page 9).
9. Select the file type and upload any relevant files (Page 10 and 11).
10. Provide a brief description of your sample (Page 12).
11. Add any additional notes about your sample (Page 13).
12. Enter the file size (Page 14).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Page 1): This page provides an overview of the Marine Diagenesis Survey form and its objectives.
* **Project Details** (Page 2): Select one of the provided options to describe your project details. This helps in categorizing your project for better organization and analysis.
