# ServiceNow Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and information from end-users regarding their experience with ServiceNow.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you are answering based on your actual experience with ServiceNow.
2. Fill out the form honestly and accurately, selecting the options that best reflect your experience.
3. If you have already submitted a similar assessment, do not resubmit. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction** (note): This is a placeholder section where you can provide any additional comments or information that you think would be helpful for our team to understand your experience with ServiceNow.
  
- **Adoption** (select_multiple): This section is used to gauge your perception of ServiceNow's adoption level within your organization. Please select all that apply.

  • Yes: You have used or are aware of the product's features and capabilities.

  • No: You have not used or do not know about the product's features and capabilities.
  
- **User Experience** (number): Please rate your overall experience with ServiceNow. A higher number indicates a more positive experience.
  
- **Skills** (select_one): This section is to gauge your understanding of ServiceNow's skill requirements and your confidence level in those areas. 

  • Yes: You are familiar with or feel confident in these skills.

  • No: You are not familiar or do not feel confident in these skills.
  
- **Training and Support** (select_multiple): This section is used to evaluate the quality and adequacy of ServiceNow's training and support. 

  • Yes: You have received adequate support or training from ServiceNow.

  • No: You have not received adequate support or training from ServiceNow.
  
- **Challenges** (select_multiple): This section is to identify any challenges you have faced while using ServiceNow. 

  • Yes: You have faced these challenges while using ServiceNow.

  • No: You have not faced these challenges while using ServiceNow.
  
- **Feedback** (text): Use this section to provide any additional comments or feedback that you think would be helpful for us to know about your experience with ServiceNow. This can include suggestions, ideas, or concerns.
 
# Tips
- Please be honest and accurate in your responses. Your feedback is crucial for us to understand your needs and make improvements.
