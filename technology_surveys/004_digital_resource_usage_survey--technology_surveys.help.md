# Digital Resource Usage Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information on how users access and use digital resources. Your input will help us understand your preferences and experiences with digital resources, which will inform future development and improvement of these resources.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and honestly, as your input will help us understand your needs and preferences.
2. For each question, select the option that best represents your answer.
3. If you select "Other" for any question, please provide more information in the text field below.
4. You can save your progress and return to the form later if needed.
5. Once you have completed all questions, click "Submit" to finalize your survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### School Library Website Usage (school_library_usage)
* **School Website**: This option is for users who access the school's website for digital resources.
* **Library Website**: This option is for users who access the library's website for digital resources.
* **Both**: This option is for users who access both the school and library websites for digital resources.
* **Other**: This option is for users who access other websites for digital resources. If you select "Other", please describe the website in the text field below.
* **None**: This option is for users who do not access any of the above websites.

### Training Program Usage (training_program_usage)
* **Online Courses**: This option is for users who have accessed online courses for digital training.
* **In-person Courses**: This option is for users who have accessed in-person courses for digital training.
* **Online tutorials**: This option is for users who have accessed online tutorials for digital training.
* **In-person tutorials**: This option is for users who have accessed in-person tutorials for digital training.
* **Both**: This option is for users who have accessed both online and in-person courses or tutorials for digital training.
* **Other**: This option is for users who have accessed other types of training programs. If you select "Other", please describe the program in the text field below.
* **None**: This option is for users who have not accessed any of the above training programs.

### How Do You Usually Access (technology_usage)
* **Mobile**: This option is for users who usually access digital resources using a mobile device.
* **Desktop**: This option is for users who usually access digital resources using a desktop computer.
* **Laptop**: This option is for users who usually access digital resources using a laptop.
* **Tablet**: This option is for users who usually access digital resources using a tablet.
* **Other**: This option is for users who usually access digital resources using other devices. If you select "Other", please describe the device in the text field below.
* **None**: This option is for users who do not usually access digital resources using any of the above devices.

### Access to Resources (digital_resource_access)
* **Always**: This option is for users who always have access to digital resources.
* **Most of the time**: This option is for users who have access to digital resources most of the time.
* **Sometimes**: This option is for users who sometimes have access to digital resources.
* **Rarely**: This option is for users who rarely have access to digital resources.
* **Never**: This option is for users who never have access to digital resources.

### Satisfied with Resources (resource_quality)
* **Very Satisfied**: This option is for users who are very satisfied with the quality of digital resources.
* **Somewhat Satisfied**: This option is for users who are somewhat satisfied with the quality of digital resources.
* **Neutral**: This option is for users who are neutral about the quality of digital resources.
* **Somewhat Dissatisfied**: This option is for users who are somewhat dissatisfied with the quality of digital resources.
* **Very Dissatisfied**: This option is for users who are very dissatisfied with the quality of digital resources.

### Areas for Improvement (resource_improvement)
This field is for you to provide any suggestions or feedback on how digital resources can be improved.

### Comments (comments)
This field is for you to provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with digital resources.

### Contact Information (email)
This field is for you to provide your contact information so we can follow up with you if needed. Please enter your email address.

### Phone Number (phone)
This field is for you to provide your phone number so we can follow up with you if needed. Please enter your phone number.

Note: This guide is for the Digital Resource Usage Survey form, if you have any questions or need further clarification, please do not hesitate to contact us.
