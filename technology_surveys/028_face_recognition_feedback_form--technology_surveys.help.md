# Face Recognition Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Face Recognition Feedback Form is designed to collect user feedback on their experience with the face recognition system. Your input will help us improve the system's performance, accuracy, and user interface.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Review each page carefully and answer all the questions accordingly.
2. Click "Submit" to send your feedback to us once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Face Recognition Experience
* **Face Recognition Experience** (`face_recognition_experience`, text, required: false): Please describe your experience with the face recognition system. What was it like for you?

### Face Recognition Feedback 
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false): Share your thoughts and opinions about the face recognition system. Your feedback will help us identify areas of improvement.

### Suggestions
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, required: false): If you have any suggestions on how we can improve the face recognition system, please share them here.

### Face Recognition System Performance 
* **Face Recognition System Performance** (`face_recognition_system_performance`, select_one, required: false): How would you rate the performance of the face recognition system?
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor

### Face Recognition Accuracy 
* **Face Recognition Accuracy** (`face_recognition_accuracy`, number, required: false): Rate the accuracy of the face recognition system on a scale of 1 to 10, where 1 is low and 10 is high.

### Face Recognition Speed 
* **Face Recognition Speed** (`face_recognition_speed`, number, required: false): Rate the speed of the face recognition system on a scale of 1 to 10, where 1 is slow and 10 is fast.
