# Marine Technology Research Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This marine technology research survey is designed to gather information on various aspects of marine technology, including research interests, roles, areas of focus, common challenges, and tool usage. The survey aims to collect valuable insights that will help improve marine technology planning and research processes. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the "category" that best describes your role in marine technology (e.g., marine engineer, marine technologist, researcher).
2. List your research interests and areas of focus in marine technology, choosing multiple options as necessary.
3. Describe the common challenges you face in your marine technology work.
4. Provide any suggestions for marine technology planning.
5. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
6. Select the "assigned tool" used in your marine technology work, if applicable.
7. List any other tools you use in your marine technology work.
8. Add any comments or feedback about the survey.
9. Fill in the date and time of the survey completion.
10. Optionally, add any additional comments.
11. Select the output file format for your survey responses.
