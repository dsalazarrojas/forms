# Application Architect Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your experience and perspectives on application architecture, including your current role, company size, and technology stack. The data collected will help us better understand the needs and challenges of application architects and inform our development of resources and services for this community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select a valid date for the survey completion in the "Survey Date" field.
2.  Enter your full name in the "Respondent Name" field.
3.  Provide your professional email address in the "Respondent Email" field.
4.  Enter your current job title in the "Current Title" field.
5.  Enter the name of your organization in the "Company Name" field.
6.  Choose the number of employees in your organization in the "Company Size" field.
7.  Select your total years of experience in application architecture in the "Years of Experience" field.
8.  Choose your primary industry in the "Primary Industry" field.
9.  Select the methodologies you use in the "Architecture Methodologies" field.
10. Select the programming languages you work with most in the "Primary Programming Languages" field.
11. Choose the cloud platforms you have experience with in the "Cloud Platforms" field.
12. Select the database technologies you use in the "Database Technologies" field.
13. Choose the container orchestration tools you use in the "Container Orchestration" field.
14. Select the API styles you implement in the "API Architecture" field.
15. Choose the security practices you implement in the "Security Practices" field.
16. Select the DevOps tools you use in the "DevOps Tools" field.
17. Describe the biggest challenges you face as an application architect in the "Biggest Challenges" field.
18. (Optional) Describe how you handle scalability concerns in the "Scalability Concerns" field.
19. (Optional) Choose your annual technology budget range in the "Technology Budget" field.
20. Select the size of your architecture team in the "Team Size" field.
21. (Optional) Describe technologies you plan to adopt in the future in the "Future Technology Focus" field.
22. (Optional) Provide any additional comments or insights in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, required): The date when the survey is completed.
* **Respondent Name** (`respondent_name`, text, required): The full name of the survey respondent.
* **Respondent Email** (`respondent_email`, email, required): The professional email address of the respondent.
* **Current Title** (`current_title`, text, required): The current job title of the respondent.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): The name of the organization.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one, required): The number of employees in the organization.
* **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, select_one, required): The total years of experience in application architecture.
* **Primary Industry** (`primary_industry`, select_one, required): The industry sector the respondent works in.
* **Architecture Methodologies** (`architecture_methodologies`, select_multiple, required): The methodologies used by the respondent.
* **Primary Programming Languages** (`primary_programming_languages`, select_multiple, required): The programming languages worked with most.
* **Cloud Platforms** (`cloud_platforms`, select_multiple, required): The cloud platforms with experience.
* **Database Technologies** (`database_technologies`, select_multiple, required): The database technologies used.
* **Container Orchestration** (`container_orchestration`, select_multiple, required): The container and orchestration tools used.
* **API Architecture** (`api_architecture`, select_multiple, required): The API styles implemented.
* **Security Practices** (`security_practices`, select_multiple, required): The security practices implemented.
* **DevOps Tools** (`devops_tools`, select_multiple, required): The DevOps tools used.
* **Biggest Challenges** (`biggest_challenges`, text, required): The biggest challenges faced as an application architect.
* **Scalability Concerns** (`scalability_concerns`, text, optional): How scalability is handled.
* **Technology Budget** (`technology_budget`, select_one, optional): The annual technology budget range.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, select_one, required): The size of the architecture team.
* **Future Technology Focus** (`future_technology_focus`, text, optional): Technologies to adopt in the future.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Additional comments or insights.

Please note that fields marked as optional are not required for completion.
