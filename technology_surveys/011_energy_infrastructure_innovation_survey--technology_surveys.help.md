# energy_infrastructure_innovation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about energy infrastructure innovation. It's intended for gathering perceptions and ideas about the state of innovation in energy infrastructure, which is crucial for the industry's development and improvement. Please fill out this form if you're involved in or interested in energy infrastructure projects or research.

## How To Complete This Form
This form consists of 8 questions with multiple-choice, select multiple, and text input types. Please read each question carefully and select the most relevant options. If you have any questions or doubts, please refer to the field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, required: false): This field is a brief description of the innovation in energy infrastructure that you're familiar with or would like to discuss. Please keep it concise.
- **Company** (`company`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all companies that you're familiar with or are relevant to your innovation in energy infrastructure.
- **Company List** (`company_list`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of company you're associated with.
- **Innovation Priorities** (`innovation_priorities`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the top priorities for innovation in energy infrastructure that you're familiar with.
- **Barriers** (`barriers`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Identify the challenges that you believe hinder innovation in energy infrastructure.
- **Successful Projects** (`successful_projects`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Name a few examples of successful projects or initiatives that showcase innovation in energy infrastructure.
- **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, required: false): If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions, please provide your name, email, or phone number.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): If you'd like to be contacted by email, please provide your email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): If you'd like to be contacted by phone, please provide your phone number.

## Tips
- Please fill out this form accurately and completely, as it will help us better understand your perspectives on energy infrastructure innovation.
- If you have any additional comments or ideas, please feel free to include them in the "Contact Details" field.
- If you're unsure about any option, you can refer to the provided explanations above.
- Thank you for taking the time to complete this form and help us improve energy infrastructure innovation!
