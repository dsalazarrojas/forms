# Smart TV App Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart TV App Feedback Survey is designed to collect information about your experience with the Smart TV App. This form will help us understand your satisfaction with the app and identify areas that need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the question "How do you rate the overall experience" on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
2. If you encountered any issues during your session, select all that apply from the list provided. This will help us understand the problems you faced.
3. If you have any suggestions for improvement, type them in the text box provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How do you rate the overall experience**: This question asks you to rate your overall satisfaction with the Smart TV App on a scale of 1-5. Please select the number that best represents your experience.
* **What issues did you encounter during your session**: This question asks you to select all issues that you faced during your session. Please choose one or more of the options provided to help us identify areas that need improvement.
* **Do you have any suggestions for improvement**: This question is an opportunity for you to share any ideas you have on how we can improve the Smart TV App. Please type your suggestions in the text box provided.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions truthfully to help us understand your experience and identify areas that need improvement.
* If you have any issues that are not listed, please let us know so we can consider them for future improvements.
* If you have any suggestions, please keep them concise and focused on specific features or functionalities.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide us with your feedback! Your input is invaluable to us.
