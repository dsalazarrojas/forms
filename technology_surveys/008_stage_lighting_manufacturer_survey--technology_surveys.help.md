# stage_lighting_manufacturer_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from stage lighting manufacturers about their products, market trends, capacity, and research partnerships or industry analysis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Start by filling out the fields on the first page, which includes the stage lighting manufacturer's name and other basic information.
2. Move on to the next page where you will select the types of stage lighting products your company offers.
3. On the following pages, provide information about market trends, capacity, research partnerships, and industry analysis.
4. Make sure to fill out each field only once to avoid duplication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Stage Lighting Manufacturer**: (<label>: `stage_lighting_manufacturer`, <type>: `text`, required: `false`): Please provide the name of the stage lighting manufacturer.
- **Capacity**: (<label>: `capacity`, <type>: `number`, required: `false`): If applicable, please provide the maximum capacity of your stage lighting system.
- **Stage Lighting Markets**: (<label>: `stage_lighting_markets`, <type>: `text`, required: `false`): Please describe the markets that your stage lighting systems are used in.
- **Stage Lighting Challenges**: (<label>: `stage_lighting_challenges`, <type>: `text`, required: `false`): If you have experienced any challenges with your stage lighting system, please describe them here.
- **Stage Lighting Trends**: (<label>: `stage_lighting_trends`, <type>: `text`, required: `false`): Please describe any trends you have observed in the stage lighting industry.
- **Stage Lighting Products**: (<label>: `stage_lighting_products`, <type>: `select_one`, required: `false`): Please select the type of stage lighting products your company offers.
  - Stage Lighting
  - Dimmer Systems
  - LED
  - Other
- **Market Trends**: (<label>: `market_trends`, <type>: `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Please select the current market trends affecting the stage lighting industry.
  - Market Trends 1
  - Market Trends 2
  - Market Trends 3
- **Market Capacity**: (<label>: `market_capacity`, <type>: `number`, required: `false`): If you have any information on the market capacity, please enter it here.
- **Research Partnerships**: (<label>: `research_partnerships`, <type>: `text`, required: `false`): If you have any research partnerships or collaborations, please describe them here.
- **Industry Analysis**: (<label>: `industry_analysis`, <type>: `text`, required: `false`): If you have any insights or analysis on the stage lighting industry, please share them here.
