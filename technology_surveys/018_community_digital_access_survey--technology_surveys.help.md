# Community Digital Access Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your household's digital access, including the availability and affordability of internet services, your digital skills confidence, and interest in digital training. The goal of this survey is to better understand your community's needs and challenges in accessing digital technologies and to inform strategies to address digital divides.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the prompts on each page. Please answer all questions, as indicated by the "Required" label on each field. For questions with options, select the choice that best reflects your situation. You can also submit feedback or comments if you have any additional information you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name (Optional)** (`respondent_name`, `text`, Required: false): Enter your name if you'd like to share it with us.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, Required: true): Enter your ZIP code, which will help us understand your geographic location.
* **Household Size** (`household_size`, `number`, Required: true): Enter the number of people in your household.
* **Number of Children Under 18** (`children_count`, `number`, Required: false): Enter the number of children under 18 in your household.
* **Do You Have a Computer or Laptop** (`device_computer`, `select_one`, Required: true): Select "Yes, Multiple" if you have more than one computer or laptop, "Yes, One" if you have only one, or "False" if you don't have a computer or laptop.
* **Do You Have a Tablet** (`device_tablet`, `select_one`, Required: true): Select "True" if you have a tablet, "False" if you don't.
* **Do You Have a Smartphone** (`device_smartphone`, `select_one`, Required: true): Select "Yes, Multiple" if you have more than one smartphone, "Yes, One" if you have only one, or "False" if you don't have a smartphone.
* **Do You Have Internet at Home** (`internet_access`, `select_one`, Required: true): Select "Yes, High Speed" for a high-speed internet connection, "Yes, Basic" for a basic connection, "Yes, Mobile Hotspot Only" for a mobile hotspot, or "False" if you don't have internet.
* **Internet Service Provider** (`internet_provider`, `text`, Required: false): Enter the name of your current internet service provider, if applicable.
* **Monthly Internet Cost** (`internet_cost`, `select_one`, Required: false): Select the approximate monthly cost of your internet service. Please select from the options provided.
* **Is Your Internet Reliable** (`internet_reliability`, `select_one`, Required: true): Rate your internet connection reliability, selecting "Very Reliable" for consistent and fast connections, "Mostly Reliable" for mostly consistent connections, "Sometimes Unreliable" for occasional outages, "Often Unreliable" for frequent outages, or "Very Unreliable" for frequent and consistent outages.
* **Is Your Internet Speed Adequate** (`internet_speed`, `select_one`, Required: true): Select "More Than Adequate" for connections that meet your needs, "Adequate" for connections that meet your needs, "Barely Adequate" for connections that barely meet your needs, "Inadequate" for connections that don't meet your needs, or "Not Sure" if you're not sure.
* **Primary Internet Uses** (`primary_use`, `select_multiple`, Required: true): Select all the ways you use the internet, such as "Work from Home", "School or Education", "Email", "Social Media", "Video Streaming", "Online Shopping", "Telehealth", or "Job Searching".
* **Digital Skills Confidence** (`digital_skills`, `select_one`, Required: true): Select how confident you are with technology, choosing "Very Confident" for high confidence, "Somewhat Confident" for moderate confidence, "Not Very Confident" for low confidence, or "Not Confident at All" if you're not confident.
* **Interested in Digital Training** (`training_interest`, `select_one`, Required: true): Select how interested you are in digital training, choosing "Yes, Very Interested" for high interest, "Somewhat Interested" for moderate interest, or "Not Interested" if you're not interested.
* **Training Topics of Interest** (`training_topics`, `select_multiple`, Required: false): Select the topics you're interested in learning, such as "Basic Computer Skills", "Internet Safety", "Email Usage", "Video Conferencing", "Online Job Applications", "Social Media", "Online Banking", or "Telehealth Usage".
* **Is Cost a Barrier to Access** (`affordability_barrier`, `select_one`, Required: true): Select whether cost is a barrier to access, choosing "Yes, Major Barrier" for a significant barrier, "Yes, Minor Barrier" for a minor barrier, or "False" if cost is not a barrier.
* **Are You Aware of Low-Income Internet Programs** (`low_income_program`, `select_one`, Required: true): Select whether you're aware of low-income internet programs, such as the Affordable Connectivity Program, choosing "Yes, Currently Enrolled" for current enrollment, "Yes, But Not Enrolled" for not currently enrolled, or "No, Not Aware" if you're not aware.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly to help us better understand your community's digital access challenges.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, you can provide them in the text box below.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey! Your input is valuable to us.
