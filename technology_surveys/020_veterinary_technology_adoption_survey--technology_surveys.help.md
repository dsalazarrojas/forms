# Veterinary Technology Adoption Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information on the veterinary sector's adoption of new technologies, including their current uses, benefits, challenges, and future interests. Completing this survey will help us understand your experiences and opinions on these topics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the appropriate answers for each question and fill out the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Is your veterinary practice ownership?** (`survey_practice_ownership`): Please select one of the options provided:
	+ Practice
	+ Clinic
	+ Hospital
	+ Other
* **What are the benefits of adopting new technology?** (`technology_adoption_benefits`): Please describe in a few sentences the benefits you have seen or anticipate from adopting new technology in your veterinary practice.
* **What are common barriers to adopting new technology?** (`technology_adoption_barriers`): Please describe in a few sentences the challenges you face or anticipate when adopting new technology in your veterinary practice.
* **Future interest in new technologies?** (`future_interest_in_new_technologies`): Please select all technologies that interest you for future adoption:
	+ AI/Artificial Intelligence
	+ Telemedicine/Telehealth
	+ Mobile/Remote Access/Assessment
	+ 3D Printing
	+ Other
* **Technology usage?** (`technology_usage`): Please enter a numerical value representing the level of technology usage in your practice (e.g., 0 for no technology usage, 10 for heavy technology usage).
* **Technology adoption barriers?** (`technology_adoption_barriers`): Please describe any additional challenges or barriers you face when adopting new technology in your veterinary practice.
* **Practice size?** (`practice_size`): Please select the size of your veterinary practice:
	+ Small
	+ Medium
	+ Large
	+ Very Large
* **Years in practice?** (`years_in_practice`): Please enter a numerical value representing the number of years you have been in practice (e.g., 1-50 years). 

## Tips

* Please be as specific and honest as possible when answering these questions.
* If you are unsure of an answer, please provide the best answer you can based on your current experience.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to include them in the "Technology adoption barriers" field.
