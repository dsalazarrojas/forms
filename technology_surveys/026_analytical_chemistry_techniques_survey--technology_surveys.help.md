# Analytical Chemistry Techniques Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from respondents about their primary analytical chemistry techniques used in their work.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, follow these steps:
   - Review each field carefully and fill in the required information.
   - For fields marked as "optional," you may skip them or provide information if you wish.
   - If a field is marked as "required," ensure you fill in the information accurately and completely.
   - Use the provided options for select fields, and select all that apply for fields like "Primary techniques used" or rate accordingly for "Equipment satisfaction rating".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. -- Respondent Information --
* **Respondent name**: This field is optional for anonymous responses. Please feel free to fill it in if you'd like to be recognized for your contribution.
* **Organization**: This field is optional and allows you to specify your laboratory or institution.

### 2. Respondent name
* **Respondent name**: This is an optional field. If you'd like to remain anonymous, simply leave it blank.

### 3. Organization
* **Organization**: This field is optional and is used to identify your laboratory or institution.

### 4. Primary techniques used
* **Primary techniques used**: Select all the techniques you use. This includes HPLC, Mass spectrometry, Spectroscopy, and Chromatography.
*   Ensure you select all the techniques that apply to your work.

### 5. Frequency of use
* **Frequency of use**: This is a required field. Select the frequency with which you use these techniques in your work:
  * Daily
  * Weekly
  * Monthly
  * Rarely

### 6. Training needs
* **Training needs**: This field is optional. If there are areas where you'd like more training, provide details here.

### 7. Equipment satisfaction rating
* **Equipment satisfaction rating**: This field is optional. Rate your satisfaction with your equipment from 1 to 5:
  * 1
  * 2
  * 3
  * 4
  * 5

### 8. Additional comments
* **Additional comments**: This field is optional. If you have any other feedback or suggestions, please share them here.

## Tips

* Please answer honestly and accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
* Your input will greatly help us improve our services and training programs.
