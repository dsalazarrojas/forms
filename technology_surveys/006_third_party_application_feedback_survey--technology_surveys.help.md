# Third Party Application Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect feedback from users about their experiences with third-party applications. Your input will help us understand how to improve our relationships with these vendors and provide better services to you.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Introduction** (`intro`, `text`, optional): Please feel free to provide a brief introduction about your experience with the third-party application.
2. **How would you rate your satisfaction with the third-party software?** (`rating_satisfaction`, `number`, optional): Rate your satisfaction on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "very dissatisfied" and 5 is "very satisfied".
3. **What features do you find most useful?** (`feedback`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the features that you find most useful in the third-party application (e.g., "Yes" or "No" for each feature).
   - **Yes** / **No**
4. **Additional comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please share them here.
5. **What features do you most frequently use?** (`usage_patters`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the features that you most frequently use in the third-party application (e.g., "Yes" or "No" for each feature).
   - **Yes** / **No**
6. **What features should be improved?** (`improvement_prioirites`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the features that you think should be improved in the third-party application (e.g., "Yes" or "No" for each feature).
   - **Yes** / **No**
7. **How did you get here?** (`contact`, `text`, optional): If you could provide more information about how you got to this form, it will help us to understand your experience better.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: A brief introduction about your experience with the third-party application.
* **How would you rate your satisfaction with the third-party software?**: Rate your satisfaction on a scale of 1-5.
* **What features do you find most useful?**: Select all the features that you find most useful.
* **Additional comments**: Share any additional comments or suggestions you have.
* **What features do you most frequently use?**: Select all the features that you most frequently use.
* **What features should be improved?**: Select all the features that you think should be improved.
* **How did you get here?**: Provide more information about how you got to this form, if possible.
