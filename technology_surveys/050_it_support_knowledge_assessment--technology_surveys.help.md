# IT Support Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to assess the IT support knowledge, skills, and experience of the respondent.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, select the options that apply to your skills and experience.
*   Please read each field carefully and choose the options that best match your knowledge and skills.
*   You can select multiple options for fields marked as "select_multiple".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **troubleshooting skills** (`troubleshooting skills`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field asks about your troubleshooting skills, such as resolving technical issues with Help desk software, network protocols, operating systems, computer hardware, or operating system troubleshooting, network troubleshooting, hardware troubleshooting, and mobile device troubleshooting.
*   **experience** (`experience`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field inquires about your years of experience, which can be 1-2 years, 2-5 years, 5-10 years, or more than 10 years.
*   **confidence** (`confidence`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field measures how confident you are in your knowledge, with options Very confident, Somewhat confident, Not very confident, or Not at all confident.
*   **problem resolution** (`problem resolution`, `text`, required/optional): This field is a text box where you can describe your approach to resolving technical problems.
*   **knowledge base** (`knowledge base`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field asks about the platforms or operating systems you are familiar with, including Windows, Linux, MAC OS, Cisco, Microsoft, or Other.
*   **technical skills** (`technical skills`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field inquires about your technical skills, such as Windows operating systems, Network protocols, Operating systems, Computer hardware, Network security, or Mobile device troubleshooting.
*   **support providers** (`support providers`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field asks about the companies you provide support to, including Microsoft, Cisco, Dell, or Other.
*   **technical cert** (`technical cert`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field is about your technical certifications, such as CCNA, CCNP, MCITP, MCSA, or Other.
*   **knowledge update** (`knowledge update`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field inquires about how often you update your knowledge, which can be 1-2 years, 2-5 years, 5-10 years, or more than 10 years.
*   **areas** (`areas`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field asks about the areas you are familiar with, such as Networking, Operating System, Hardware, Software, or Other.
*   **training programs** (`training programs`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field is about the programs you participate in for training, such as Windows, Linux, Cisco, or Other.
*   **support team** (`support team`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field inquires about the platforms or operating systems you support, such as Windows, Linux, MAC OS, or Other.
*   **support providers** (`support providers`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field asks about the companies you provide support to, including Microsoft, Cisco, or Other.
*   **communication skills** (`communication skills`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field is about your communication skills, such as Verbal, Non-verbal, Email, or Phone.
*   **team leadership** (`team leadership`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field asks if you have team leadership experience, with options True or False.

## Tips

*   Take your time to read each field carefully and choose the options that best match your skills and experience.
*   If you're unsure about an option, choose the one that best describes your skills or experience.
*   You can select multiple options for fields marked as "select_multiple".
