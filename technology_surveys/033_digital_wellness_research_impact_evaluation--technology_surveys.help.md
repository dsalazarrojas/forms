# Digital Wellness Research Impact Evaluation - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback and ratings from participants about their experiences with the "Digital Wellness Research" program, providing valuable insights to researchers and program administrators to improve future iterations of the program.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully, ensuring you answer all required questions and provide any feedback or ratings.
2. Enter your participant name in the "Participant Name" field.
3. Describe your pre-program digital habits in the "Digital Habits" field.
4. Rate your experience in the program and provide feedback in the "Ratings Participant Experience" and "Feedback Participant Experience" fields.
5. Rate the program overall in the "Ratings Program" field and provide feedback in the "Feedback Program" field.
6. Set the date and time for any follow-up meetings or discussions in the "Follow Up" and "Follow Up Time" fields, if applicable.
7. Enter your contact information (email and/or phone number) to facilitate communication.
8. Rate the researcher's performance in the "Ratings Researcher" field and provide feedback in the "Feedback Researcher" field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Name**: Enter the name of the program you participated in.
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as a participant.
* **Digital Habits**: Describe your pre-program digital habits, such as your daily screen time, social media usage, or other digital activities.
* **Ratings Participant Experience**: Rate your experience in the program using the options "Improved my life", "Made no difference", or "Worsened my life".
* **Feedback Participant Experience**: Provide any feedback or comments about your experience in the program.
* **Ratings Program**: Rate the program overall using the options "Excellent", "Good", or "Poor".
* **Feedback Program**: Provide any feedback or comments about the program.
* **Follow Up**: Enter the date for any scheduled follow-up meetings or discussions.
* **Follow Up Time**: Enter the time for any scheduled follow-up meetings or discussions.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Ratings Researcher**: Rate the researcher's performance using the options "Excellent", "Good", or "Poor".
* **Feedback Researcher**: Provide any feedback or comments about the researcher's performance.
