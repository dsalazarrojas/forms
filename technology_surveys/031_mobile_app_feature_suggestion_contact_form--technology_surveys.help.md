# mobile_app_feature_suggestion_contact_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feature suggestions for mobile apps. It allows users to submit their ideas, provide additional information such as screenshots and feedback, and contact details for the submitter. The form is used for internal purposes, and it's essential to understand that the information collected will be reviewed and potentially used by our development team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief description of the suggested feature in the **Feature Name** field.
2. Add a more detailed description of the feature in the **Description** field.
3. If possible, attach a screenshot of the suggested feature in the **Screenshot URL** or **Screenshot** field.
4. Select a **Category** for the feature, such as Mobile App, Game, or Productivity.
5. Indicate the **Priority** of the feature, which can be Critical, High, Medium, or Low.
6. Enter your **Email** and **Phone Number** for us to contact you.
7. Provide any additional **Comments** about the feature suggestion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feature Name** (`feature_name`, text, optional): Briefly describe the feature you would like to suggest.
* **Description** (`feature_description`, text, optional): Provide a more detailed description of the feature.
* **Screenshot URL** (`feature_screenshot_url`, text, optional): If you have a screenshot of the feature, provide the URL where it can be found.
* **Screenshot** (`feature_screenshot`, text, optional): Upload a screenshot of the feature.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Choose a category for the feature, such as Mobile App, Game, or Productivity.
* **Priority** (`priority`, select_one, optional): Select the priority of the feature, which can be Critical, High, Medium, or Low.
* **Email** (`submitter_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for us to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`submitter_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Additional Comments** (`submitter_comment`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments about the feature suggestion.
* **Submit** (`submit`, date, optional): This field is not used in this form, it seems to be an error in the YAML.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, text, optional): This field is not used in this form, it seems to be an error in the YAML.

Note: Please make sure to fill in the form accurately and completely, as it will help our development team to review and potentially implement your feature suggestions.
