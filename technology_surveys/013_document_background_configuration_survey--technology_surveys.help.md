# Document Background Configuration Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Document Background Configuration Survey is designed to gather information about the visual presentation of documents. This survey helps us understand how you want your documents to be configured.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and select the options that best fit your document's configuration needs.
2. For each field, select one option from the provided list.
3. Make sure to fill out all fields that are marked as required.
4. If a field is optional, you can skip it if it's not applicable to your document.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Logo Position** (`logo_position`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the position of your logo on the document. Select one of the following options: Left, Right, Center.
* **Background Color** (`background_color`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the background color of your document from the following options: White, Black, Gray, Blue, Red.
* **Document Orientation** (`document_orientation`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the orientation of your document. Select one of the following options: Portrait, Landscape.
* **Document Size** (`document_size`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the size of your document. Choose one of the following options: A4, Letter, Legal.
* **Footer Position** (`footer_position`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the position of your footer on the document. Select one or more of the following options: Left, Right, None.
* **Footer Color** (`footer_color`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the color of your footer. Choose one or more of the following options: White, Black, Gray, Blue, Red.
* **Footer Content** (`footer_content`, `note`, required: false): You can add any additional content to the footer here (e.g., terms of service, copyright information). This field is a text area for free-form input.
* **Header Position** (`header_position`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the position of your header on the document. Select one of the following options: Top, Bottom, None.

## Tips

* Take your time to carefully review each field and make informed decisions about your document's configuration.
* If you're unsure about any setting, you can always contact the form owner or administrator for clarification.
* This survey is designed to be simple and quick to fill out. If you have any further questions or need assistance, don't hesitate to reach out!
