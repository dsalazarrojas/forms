# Webhook Delivery Platform Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Webhook Delivery Platform Evaluation Form is a tool used to gather information about the performance and suitability of different webhooks for our business. This form is used by our team to make informed decisions about which platform to use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the **Vendor Information** section with details about the webhook delivery platform you are evaluating.
2.  Describe your **Evaluation Methodology** in the designated field.
3.  Select all the **Evaluation Criteria** that you used during your evaluation (you can select multiple options).
4.  Provide a **Satisfaction Score** for the platform, on a scale from 1 (least satisfied) to 5 (most satisfied).
5.  Enter your **Platform Score** as a numerical value.
6.  Answer **Decision-Ready Platform?** with either 'True' or 'False' to indicate if you think the platform is decision-ready.
7.  Leave any **Vendor Feedback** in the designated text field.
8.  Add any **Additional Comments** about your evaluation in the designated text field.
9.  Enter your **Email Address** for contact purposes.
10.  Enter your **Phone Number** for further contact.
11.  Leave any **Comments** about your experience with the platform in the designated text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Vendor Information**: This field is for entering details about the webhook delivery platform you are evaluating.
*  **Evaluation Methodology**: Describe the steps and criteria you used during your evaluation.
*  **Evaluation Criteria**: Select all the criteria you used for evaluation. These criteria are used to assess the performance and suitability of the platform.
*  **Satisfaction Score**: Rate your satisfaction with the platform on a scale from 1 (least satisfied) to 5 (most satisfied).
*  **Platform Score**: Enter a numerical value for your overall assessment of the platform.
*  **Decision-Ready Platform?**: Answer with 'True' or 'False' to indicate if you think the platform is decision-ready.
*  **Vendor Feedback**: Leave any comments or feedback from the platform vendor.
*  **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments about your evaluation.
*  **Email Address**: Enter your email address for further contact.
*  **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for further contact.
*  **Comments**: Leave any comments about your experience with the platform.
