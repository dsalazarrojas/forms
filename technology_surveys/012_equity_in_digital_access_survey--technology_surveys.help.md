# Equity in Digital Access Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your experiences with digital access. We want to understand your current situation and identify areas where support is needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply work through each page and answer the questions accordingly. You can submit your answers at any time and continue later if needed. If you have any comments or suggestions, feel free to add them to the last question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the primary reason you use the Bible?** (`question_1`, select_one, required/optional): This question helps us understand your motivation for using the Bible. Please select the option that best describes why you use the Bible.
* **What are the goals of your organization?** (`question_2`, select_multiple, required/optional): This question is about the main objectives of your organization. If you have multiple goals, select all that apply.
* **What type of equipment would you like to see your organization support?** (`question_3`, select_one, required/optional): This question is about the kind of equipment you would like to use for digital access. Select the option that best describes your preference.
* **What is the current quality of your internet connection?** (`question_8`, number, required/optional): This question is about your current internet connection's speed. Please enter the number that best describes your connection's speed.
* **What is the current speed of your internet connection?** (`question_10`, number, required/optional): This question is about the speed of your current internet connection. Please enter the number that best describes your connection's speed.
* **Do you have any further comments or suggestions?** (`question_11`, note, required/optional): This is the last question and a chance to share any additional thoughts or ideas you have about digital access.
