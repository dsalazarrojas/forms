# Technology Transformation Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users on the implementation and outcomes of technology transformation initiatives. The form is meant to be completed by users who have undergone or are currently undergoing a technology transformation process and provides a structured way to report on their experiences and insights.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate options for each field as requested.
2. Provide detailed and thoughtful text responses where applicable, such as in the "transformation_implementation", "implementation_benefits", "implementation_challenges", "implementation_outcome", "technology_transformation", "implementation_tools", "submission_comments", "submission_notes", "submission_comments_2", and "submission_comments_3" fields.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Transformation Implementation** (`transformation_implementation`, select_one, required): Select the option that best describes the implementation status of the transformation process.
* **Implementation Status** (`implementation_status`, select_one, required): Select the option that best describes the implementation status of the transformation process.
* **Implementation Outcome** (`implementation_outcome`, select_one, required): Select the option that best describes the outcome of the transformation process.
* **Implementation Benefits** (`implementation_benefits`, text, required): Describe the benefits realized from the transformation process.
* **Implementation Challenges** (`implementation_challenges`, text, required): Describe the challenges faced during the transformation process.
* **Implementation Technology** (`implementation_technology`, text, required): Describe the technology used in the transformation process.
* **User Insights** (`user_insights`, text, optional): Provide any additional insights or comments on the transformation process.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to receive updates on this submission.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number to receive follow-up calls or updates.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name to be credited as the submitter.
* **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable departments that are involved in the transformation process.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, text, optional): Describe the team members involved in the transformation process.
