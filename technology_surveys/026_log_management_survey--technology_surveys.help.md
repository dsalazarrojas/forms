# Log Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your log management practices and preferences. It will help us understand your understanding and usage of logs, log types, retention period, and frequency.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form by answering the questions and selecting options as prompted.
2. Ensure to provide accurate and honest answers to help us improve our services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is a log?** (text): Describe your understanding or concept of a log.
* **Log types** (select_multiple): Select all the log types you have or have not used. You will see two options for each type.
* **How often do you log?** (number): Rate how frequently you log on a scale.
* **Primary log type** (select_one): Identify your most important log type.
* **What is the retention period?** (select_one): Choose whether you retain or do not retain logs.
* **Log retention period** (number): Input the time period you retain logs for.
* **How would you rate your log frequency?** (number): Rate how frequently you log on a scale.
* **Log management practices** (note): Describe your current log management practices.
* **Log types you log** (select_multiple): Select all the log types you have or have not used. You will see two options for each type.
* **What is the log date?** (date): Input the date of the log if available.
* **How often do you practice log management?** (number): Rate how frequently you practice log management on a scale.
* **Number of log entries** (number): Input the amount of logs you have maintained.
* **Log retention period frequency** (number): Input the retention period frequency.
* **Log frequency** (number): Rate how frequently you log on a scale.
* **What is the log frequency?** (number): Rate or input the log frequency.
