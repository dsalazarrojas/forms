# media_usage_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your digital media consumption habits and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions according to your genuine experiences and habits.
2. Be as specific as possible when selecting your options.
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Additional comments" field.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How often do you consume digital media content?**: Choose the frequency with which you access digital media content.
	+ **Multiple times daily**: You visit or view digital media content multiple times in a 24-hour period.
	+ **Daily**: You check or view digital media content once a day.
	+ **A few times a week**: You access digital media content sporadically, less than once a week.
	+ **Weekly**: You check or view digital media content once a week.
	+ **Rarely**: You seldom or rarely access digital media content.
* **What are your primary sources for news and information?**: Select all that apply from the following:
	+ **Social media**: News and information from social media platforms.
	+ **News websites/apps**: News and information from news websites or mobile apps.
	+ **TV/Radio**: News and information from television or radio broadcasts.
	+ **Podcasts**: News and information from audio podcasts.
	+ **Print media**: News and information from print publications.
	+ **Friends/Family**: News and information from friends or family members.
* **What devices do you primarily use to consume media?**: Select all that apply:
	+ **Smartphone**: News and information from mobile phones.
	+ **Laptop/PC**: News and information from personal computers or laptops.
	+ **Tablet**: News and information from tablets.
	+ **Smart TV**: News and information from smart TVs.
	+ **Gaming Console**: News and information from gaming consoles.
	+ **Smart Speaker**: News and information from smart speakers.
* **What types of digital content do you engage with most?**: Select all that apply:
	+ **Video**: News and information from video content, such as videos or live streams.
	+ **Articles/Blogs**: News and information from written articles or blogs.
	+ **Music**: News and information from music or audio content.
	+ **Podcasts**: News and information from audio podcasts.
	+ **Social media posts**: News and information from social media posts.
	+ **E-books**: News and information from e-books or digital books.
* **What is your biggest challenge or frustration with current media consumption?**: Describe the main difficulty you face when accessing or interacting with digital media content.
* **Additional comments or feedback**: Share any other information or insights about your media usage that you think may be helpful to us.
