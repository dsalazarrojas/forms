# Student Technology Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Technology Survey is a collection of questions designed to gather information about students' usage of technology tools, devices, and activities for classwork and personal use. This data will help educators understand students' tech habits and how they impact their learning experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
- Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
- Answer each question based on your actual usage or access to technology.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Technology Usage Home** (`technology_usage_home`, text, optional): Please describe the technology you use at home, such as devices, software, or tools.
* **Technology Tools Used** (`tech_tools_used_for_classwork`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the tools you use for classwork, such as calculators, computers, tablets, or smartphones.
* **Technology Help At Home** (`technology_help_at_home`, select_one, optional): If you need help with technology at home, select who you usually get help from, such as your father, mother, teacher, or parent.
* **Student Computer Access** (`student_computer_access`, number, optional): Please enter the number of computers you have access to at home.
* **Student Internet Access** (`student_internet_access`, number, optional): Please enter the number of hours you spend on a device per day.
* **Classroom Computer Access** (`classroom_computer_access`, number, optional): Please enter the number of computers available in your classroom.
* **Technology Used For Classwork** (`technology_used_for_classwork`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the subjects you use technology for in classwork, such as math, reading, science, or writing.
* **Time Spent On Devices** (`time_spent_on_devices`, number, optional): Please enter the number of hours you spend on a device per day.
* **Devices In Bedroom** (`devices_in_bedroom`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the devices you have in your bedroom, such as TVs, computers, tablets, or video game consoles.
* **Parental Controls** (`parental_controls`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether your parents or guardians have parental controls on your devices, such as true or false.
* **Technology Used For Fun** (`technology_used_for_fun`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the activities you use technology for, such as games, social media, videos, or music.
