# Gearbox Design Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gearbox Design Feedback Form is a tool for providing feedback on the design of a gearbox. This form is intended to collect feedback from users on the design of a gearbox, including its usability, functionality, and overall performance. This feedback will help us improve the design of the gearbox and make it more efficient and effective for users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the page where you are providing feedback (currently page 1).
2. Rate the design of the gearbox using the rating field.
3. Provide comments on your feedback in the comments field.
4. If you have an attachment, please attach it to the attachment field.
5. Optionally, provide a rating and comment in the rating & comment field.
6. Provide any additional comments on your trial experience in the trial comments field.
7. If you have any ideas for continuous improvement, please provide them in the continuous improvement field.
8. Finally, provide a rating of your trial experience in the trial rating field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Design Rating
• **Design Rating** (`design_rating`, `number`, required: false): Rate the design of the gearbox on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "very poor" and 5 is "excellent".

### Comments
• **Comments** (`design_comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any comments or feedback you have on the design of the gearbox.

### Attachment
• **Attachment** (`attachment`, `text`, required: false): If you have an attachment (such as a photo or document) related to your feedback, please upload it here.

### Rating & Comment
• **Rating & Comment** (`rating_comment`, `note`, required: false): This is an optional field for providing a rating and comment in one place.

### Trial Comments
• **Trial Comments** (`trial_comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any comments on your trial experience with the gearbox.

### Continuous Improvement
• **Continuous Improvement** (`continuous_improvement`, `text`, required: false): Share any ideas you have for continuous improvement of the gearbox design.

### Trial Rating
• **Trial Rating** (`trial_rating`, `number`, required: false): Rate your trial experience with the gearbox on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "very poor" and 5 is "excellent".
