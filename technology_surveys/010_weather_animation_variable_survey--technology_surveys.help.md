# Weather Animation Variable Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from experts in the field on the animation variables that are most important for weather-related projects.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each page of the form carefully and answer the questions according to your expertise.
2. Select the relevant options for each field, and provide additional information as required.
3. Make sure to fill in all required fields. If a field is not marked as required, it is optional and your answer will not affect the overall survey results.
