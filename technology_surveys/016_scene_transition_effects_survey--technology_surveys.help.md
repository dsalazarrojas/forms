# Scene Transition Effects Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Scene Transition Effects Survey is designed to collect data on the usage and satisfaction of scene transition effects in various projects. This survey aims to gather information on the types of effects used, the degree of satisfaction with these effects, and the areas of improvement needed for future development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all that apply for each of the fields on each page.
2. For select_multiple fields, choose as many options as are relevant to your experience.
3. For select_one fields, choose only one option that best represents your response.
4. Fill in the numerical fields (if present) with the corresponding numerical values.
5. Submit the form once you have filled out all the necessary fields.
