# Building Energy Transition Readiness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Building Energy Transition Readiness Survey is designed to evaluate your building's readiness for energy upgrades and transitions. This form helps us understand your building's current energy status, transition goals, and barriers to energy transition. By completing this form, you'll help us identify areas for improvement and provide valuable insights for your energy transition journey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer the questions that apply to your building.
2. Be as specific as possible when providing information, especially when answering yes or no questions.
3. If a question doesn't apply, leave it blank.
4. Use the provided options to select the most accurate answer for each question.
5. Ensure you provide all required information (indicated by an asterisk *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Building owner or facility manager name** (*): Enter the name of the person responsible for the building's energy management.
* **Organization name**: Enter your organization's name, if applicable.
* **Email address** (*): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone number** (*): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Building name** (*): Enter the name of the building being assessed.
* **Building address** (*): Enter the address of the building being assessed.
* **Building type**: Select the building type from the provided options (Office, Residential, Educational, etc.).
* **Total square footage** (*): Enter the total square footage of the building.
* **Year built**: Enter the year the building was constructed.
* **Current energy status**: This section is a note field; you can add any relevant information about your building's current energy status.
* **Energy audit completed**: Select whether an energy audit has been completed recently, 1-3 years ago, or over 3 years ago.
* **Current energy efficiency rating**: Select the current energy efficiency rating of your building (Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, or Unknown).
* **Current energy and fuel sources used**: Select all the energy and fuel sources currently used in your building (Natural gas, Oil, Electricity, etc.).
* **Current renewable energy systems**: Select all the renewable energy systems currently in use (Solar photovoltaic, Solar thermal, Wind, etc.).
* **Estimated annual energy cost**: This section is a note field; you can add any relevant information about your building's estimated annual energy cost.
* **Energy cost trend**: Select the energy cost trend of your building (Increasing significantly, Increasing moderately, Stable, Decreasing, or Unknown).
* **Energy monitoring system in place**: Select whether an energy monitoring system is in real-time, monthly, minimal, or not present.
* **Energy transition goals**: Select all the energy transition goals for your building (Reduce energy consumption, Decarbonization, etc.).
* **Transition timeline target**: Select the target date for your building's energy transition (Net zero by 2030, 2040, 2050, etc.).
* **Infrastructure readiness**: This section is a note field; you can add any relevant information about your building's infrastructure readiness.
* **Building electrical capacity for increased load**: Select whether your building has sufficient electrical capacity, minor upgrades needed, or insufficient capacity.
* **Grid connection capability**: Select the strength of your building's grid connection capability (Strong, Adequate, Limited, Unknown).
* **Building suitability for on-site renewables**: Select the suitability of your building for on-site renewable energy systems (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Unknown).
* **Building envelope improvements**: Select the condition of your building's envelope (Recently improved, Adequate, Needs improvement, Significant work needed, Unknown).
* **Financial readiness**: This section is a note field; you can add any relevant information about your building's financial readiness for energy transition.
* **Budget available for energy transition** (*): Select whether a budget is fully funded, partially funded, or not identified.
* **Interest in financing options**: Select all the financing options you're interested in (Rebates and grants, Low-interest loans, etc.).
* **Expected payback period preference** (*): Select the expected payback period for your building's energy transition.
* **Primary barriers to energy transition**: Select all the primary barriers to your building's energy transition (High upfront costs, Limited financing options, etc.).
* **Needed support or resources**: Select all the support or resources you need for energy transition (Technical expertise, Financing assistance, etc.).
* **Capacity to implement transition**: Select the capacity of your building to implement energy transition (High, Moderate, Limited, Very limited).
* **Additional comments or questions**: Enter any additional comments or questions you have about your building's energy transition readiness.

Please answer each question carefully and thoroughly to ensure accurate assessment of your building's energy transition readiness. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us for support.
