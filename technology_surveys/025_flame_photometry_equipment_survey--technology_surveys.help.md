# flame_photometry_equipment_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and insights from users who have interacted with flame photometry equipment. Your input will help us improve and develop the equipment for better performance and user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form title and category.
2. Provide a brief description of your experience with the flame photometry equipment.
3. Select the type of problem or issue you encountered with the equipment.
4. Indicate whether the problem occurred once or frequently.
5. Describe the type of help you required to resolve the issue.
6. Indicate the type of help that was provided or attempted to be provided.
7. Provide a brief description of the outcome.
8. Describe the action taken to resolve the issue.
9. Describe the outcome of that action.
10. Indicate whether you would like to see any changes to the flame photometry equipment in the future.
11. Finally, provide any additional comments or feedback you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief title for your submission.
* **Category** (`category`, text, required/optional): Select the category related to your submission.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required/optional): Describe your experience with the flame photometry equipment.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required/optional): Select the tool assigned to you for the equipment.
* **How would you rate your satisfaction with the flame photometry equipment?** (`question1`, select_multiple, required/optional): Rate your satisfaction with the equipment on a scale of Very good, Fair, Neutral, or Bad.
* **What type of problem or issue did you encounter with the equipment?** (`question2`, text, required/optional): Describe the type of problem or issue you encountered.
* **Is this a problem that occurred once or repeatedly?** (`question3`, select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether the problem occurred once or frequently.
* **What type of help was required to resolve the issue?** (`question4`, text, required/optional): Describe the type of help you required.
* **What type of help was provided or attempted to be provided?** (`question5`, text, required/optional): Describe the type of help that was provided or attempted to be provided.
* **What was the outcome?** (`question6`, text, required/optional): Describe the outcome of the issue.
* **What action was taken to resolve the issue?** (`question7`, text, required/optional): Describe the action taken to resolve the issue.
* **What was the outcome of that action?** (`question8`, text, required/optional): Describe the outcome of the action.
* **Would you like to see any changes to the flame photometry equipment in the future?** (`question9`, select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether you would like to see any changes to the equipment.
* **Do you have any other comments or feedback?** (`question10`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have.
* **This form has been used for 1 submissions** (`this_form_has_been_used_for_1_submissions`, note, required/optional): This is a note field indicating the number of submissions made with this form.
