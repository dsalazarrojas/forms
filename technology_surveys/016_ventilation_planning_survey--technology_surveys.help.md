# Ventilation Planning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ventilation Planning Survey form is designed to collect and organize essential information about ventilation systems and their associated personnel for planning and coordination purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

* Review each page to understand the type of information that needs to be collected.
* Fill in the **Project Details** page (Page 1) with your project's name and any other relevant details.
* Move on to the **Space Description** page (Page 2) and enter a detailed description of the space or area being surveyed.
* On the **Space Size** page (Page 3), input the size of the area, including height.
* Continue to the **System Description** page (Page 4) and provide a description of the ventilation system in question.
* Choose the **System Type** from the options on Page 5 to indicate the type of system being planned.
* Enter the **System Capacity** on Page 6, which is likely the capacity or scale of the system.
* Provide the **System Location** on Page 7, specifying where the system will be installed.
* If necessary, add **System Notes** on Page 8 to include any additional information about the system.
* Enter the **Designer Name**, **Designer Email**, and **Designer Phone** on Pages 9, 10, and 11 to identify the designer(s) involved in the project.
* Add **Designer Notes** on Page 13, if needed.
* Enter the **Engineer Name**, **Engineer Email**, and **Engineer Phone** on Pages 14, 15, and 16 to identify the engineer(s) involved in the project.
* Add **Engineer Notes** on Page 17, if needed.
* Enter **Other Names** on Page 18 if there are other individuals or teams involved.
* Add **Other Notes** on Page 19, if necessary.
* Provide any additional comments on Page 20.
* Identify who **Submitted By** and on which date on Pages 21 and 22.
* Select who the **Assigned To** should be on Page 23 and choose one or multiple **Project Status** options on Page 24.
* Finally, input the **Review Date** on Page 25.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Details** (Page 1):
	+ **Project Details**: Enter a brief description of your project.
* **Space Description** (Page 2):
	+ **Space Description**: A detailed description of the space or area being surveyed.
* **Space Size** (Page 3):
	+ **Space Size**: Enter the size of the area in square units (e.g., meters, feet).
* **Space Height** (Page 4):
	+ **Space Height**: Input the height of the space in units (e.g., meters, feet).
* **System Description** (Page 5):
	+ **System Description**: A description of the ventilation system in question.
* **System Type** (Page 6):
	+ **System Type**: Choose the type of ventilation system from the provided options.
* **System Capacity** (Page 7):
	+ **System Capacity**: Enter the capacity or scale of the system.
* **System Location** (Page 8):
	+ **System Location**: Specify where the system will be installed.
* **System Notes** (Page 9):
	+ **System Notes**: Add any additional information about the system.
* **Designer Name** (Page 10):
	+ **Designer Name**: Enter the name of the designer.
* **Designer Email** (Page 11):
	+ **Designer Email**: Input the designer's email address.
* **Designer Phone** (Page 12):
	+ **Designer Phone**: Enter the designer's phone number.
* **Designer Notes** (Page 13):
	+ **Designer Notes**: Add any notes or comments from the designer.
* **Engineer Name** (Page 14):
	+ **Engineer Name**: Enter the name of the engineer.
* **Engineer Email** (Page 15):
	+ **Engineer Email**: Input the engineer's email address.
* **Engineer Phone** (Page 16):
	+ **Engineer Phone**: Enter the engineer's phone number.
* **Engineer Notes** (Page 17):
	+ **Engineer Notes**: Add any notes or comments from the engineer.
* **Other Names** (Page 18):
	+ **Other Names**: List any other names or teams involved.
* **Other Notes** (Page 19):
	+ **Other Notes**: Add any other comments or information.
* **Comments** (Page 20):
	+ **Comments**: Provide any additional comments about the project.
* **Submitted By** (Page 21):
	+ **Submitted By**: Identify who submitted the form.
* **Submitted Date** (Page 22):
	+ **Submitted Date**: Enter the date when the form was submitted.
* **Assigned To** (Page 23):
	+ **Assigned To**: Choose who the form should be assigned to from the options.
* **Project Status** (Page 24):
	+ **Project Status**: Select one or multiple options to indicate the project's status.
* **Review Date** (Page 25):
	+ **Review Date**: Enter the date when the form should be reviewed.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate and complete data collection.
* Double-check each field to ensure accuracy before moving on.
* Save your progress and come back to the form as needed.
