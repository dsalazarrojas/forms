# public-awareness-of-sustainable-tourism-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to gauge your understanding and attitudes towards sustainable tourism. It consists of multiple questions that will help us better understand your level of awareness, attitudes, and preferences related to sustainable tourism.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Fill out all the required fields (indicated by an *).
3. Select the options that best match your preferences.
4. Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What do you know about sustainable tourism?** (`introduction`, `text`, optional)
	+ This question is an open-ended question that allows you to share your current understanding of sustainable tourism.
* **What level of awareness do you have about sustainable tourism?** (`awareness`, `select_one`, optional)
	+ Choose the option that best describes your level of awareness, from "Very High" to "Very Low".
* **Which of the following do you believe is the most important aspect of sustainable tourism?** (`attitudes`, `select_multiple`, optional)
	+ Select all the options that you consider most important, from "Environmental", "Social", "Economic", or "Cultural".
* **Do you have any feedback or suggestions for sustainable tourism practices?** (`feedback`, `note`, optional)
	+ Share any comments or suggestions you have about sustainable tourism practices.
* **E-mail** (`email`, `email`, optional)
	+ Enter your email address (not required).
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional)
	+ Enter your phone number (not required).
* **Date of Birth** (`date`, `date`, optional)
	+ Enter your date of birth (not required).
* **Time of Day** (`time`, `time`, optional)
	+ Enter the time (not required).
* **What level of education do you have?** (`education`, `select_one`, optional)
	+ Choose your level of education, from "Bachelor" to "Others".
* **What is your occupation?** (`occupation`, `text`, optional)
	+ Enter your occupation (not required).
* **Which company/organization do you represent?** (`company`, `text`, optional)
	+ Enter the name of your company or organization (not required).
* **What is your role in the company/organization?** (`contact`, `text`, optional)
	+ Enter your role in the company or organization (not required).
* **What is your location?** (`location`, `text`, optional)
	+ Enter your location (not required).
* **Additional Comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional)
	+ Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have.
* **How would you rate your level of interest in sustainable tourism?** (`rating`, `select_one`, optional)
	+ Rate your level of interest, from 1 to 5.
* **What is your level of knowledge about sustainable tourism?** (`knowledge`, `select_one`, optional)
	+ Rate your level of knowledge, from 1 to 5.
* **Do you agree or disagree with the following statements about sustainable tourism?** (`agreement`, `select_multiple`, optional)
	+ Select all the options that best match your stance, from "I agree" to "I disagree".
* **How satisfied are you with sustainable tourism?** (`satisfaction`, `select_one`, optional)
	+ Choose how satisfied you are with sustainable tourism, from "Very Satisfied" to "Very Unsatisfied".
* **How often do you travel?** (`travel`, `select_one`, optional)
	+ Choose how often you travel, from "1-5 times a year" to "More than 30 times a year".

Note: The fields marked with an asterisk (\*) are not required, but it is highly recommended to fill them for a more accurate representation of your opinions.
