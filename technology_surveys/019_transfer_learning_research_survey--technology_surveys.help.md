# Transfer Learning Research Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about research on transfer learning, a subfield of machine learning that allows models to use knowledge gained from one task to improve performance on another related task. The goal of this survey is to collect feedback and ideas on transfer learning research, collaboration, and tools used in this field.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form and make sure you are familiar with all the fields.
2. Fill in your answers in the relevant fields according to your experience and knowledge on transfer learning.
3. If you have any additional comments or thoughts, use the "additional_comments" field.
4. If you have published any research or papers on transfer learning, provide the names of the publications in the "publication" field.
5. If you have collaborated with others on transfer learning research, select the "collaboration" options that best fit your experience.
6. If you have used any collaboration tools, list them in the "collaboration_tools_used" field.
