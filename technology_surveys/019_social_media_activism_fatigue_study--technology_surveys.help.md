# Social Media Activism Fatigue Study - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form aims to gather information about individuals' social media usage, participation, and experiences related to social media activism. The data collected will be used to better understand the effects of social media activism on users' emotional and psychological well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the option that best describes your social media participation.
2. Choose how much time you spend on social media platforms.
3. Select the social media platforms you actively use.
4. Rate how often you engage in activism on your personal social media accounts.
5. Answer questions about your emotional experiences related to social media.
6. Rate the level of emotional exhaustion you feel when using social media.
7. Choose how frequently you engage with different types of content on social media.
8. Rate how often you share information on social media platforms.
9. Indicate how often you participate in surveys like this one.
10. Choose the primary purpose of this survey for you.
11. Select the social media platforms you use for activism.
12. Rate the impact social media activism has on your life.
13. Assess your emotional engagement level with social media content.
14. Evaluate the style of content on social media you find most engaging.
15. Choose how often the content style on social media varies for you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Participation** (`survey_participation`, number, required: false):
	+ This question asks about your level of participation in social media activities.
* **Social Media Usage** (`social_media_usage`, select_one, required: false):
	+ This question inquires about the amount of time you spend on social media platforms.
	+ Please select the option that best describes your usage habits.
* **Social Media Platforms** (`social_media_platforms`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Choose all the social media platforms you actively use.
* **Social Media Activism on Personal Accounts** (`activism_on_social_media`, select_one, required: false):
	+ This question asks about how often you engage in activism on your personal social media accounts.
* **Emotional Ex Exhaustion** (`emotional_exhaustion`, number, required: false):
	+ Please rate the level of emotional exhaustion you feel when using social media.
* **Emotional Engagement** (`emotional_engagement`, number, required: false):
	+ This question is about your emotional engagement level with social media.
* **Content Type** (`content_type`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Choose the type of content you most engage with on social media (e.g., informational, advocacy, entertainment).
* **Content Awareness** (`content_awareness`, number, required: false):
	+ Please rate how aware you are of the content on social media.
* **Survey Exposure** (`survey_exposure`, select_one, required: false):
	+ This question inquires about how often you participate in surveys like this one.
* **Survey Frequency** (`survey_frequency`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Please select how often you participate in surveys.
* **Survey Conductors** (`survey_conductors`, text, required: false):
	+ If you know who conducts this survey, please provide their name or description.
* **Survey Purpose** (`survey_purpose`, select_one, required: false):
	+ This question is about the primary purpose of this survey for you.
* **Social Media Platforms Used for Activism** (`social_media_platforms_used`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Choose all the social media platforms you use for activism.
* **Social Media Activism Impact** (`activism_impacts`, number, required: false):
	+ This question assesses the impact social media activism has on your life.
* **Engagement Level** (`engagement_level`, number, required: false):
	+ Rate your emotional engagement level with social media content.
* **Content Style** (`content_style`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Please choose the style of content on social media that you find most engaging.
* **Content Style Variation** (`content_style_variation`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Choose how often the content style on social media varies for you.

## Tips
