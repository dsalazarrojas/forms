# Sync Settings Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Sync Settings Survey aims to gather user feedback on their experiences with data syncing, helping us to identify areas of improvement and ensure a seamless syncing experience for all users.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please answer each question honestly, and choose the options that best describe your experiences with data syncing.
2.  For the select options (How often do you sync your data, What issues do you encounter with data syncing, etc.), select all the options that apply to you.
3.  In the note fields (Describe the issues you've encountered, What are the details of these issues?), provide as much detail as possible about your experiences with data syncing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **How often do you sync your data?** (`sync_behavior`, select_multiple): This question asks about your data syncing frequency. Select all the options that best describe how often you sync your data.
*   **Sync Frequency** (`sync_frequency`, select_one): This question is about the frequency of your data syncing. Choose the option that best describes your syncing frequency.
*   **What issues do you encounter with data syncing?** (`issues`, select_multiple): This question is about the types of issues you've experienced with data syncing. Select all the options that apply to you.
*   **Describe the issues you've encountered** (`issue_details`, note): Please provide as much detail as possible about the issues you've experienced with data syncing.
*   **How often do these issues impact your business?** (`sync_frequency_urgent`, select_one): This question is about the urgency of the issues you've experienced. Choose the option that best describes how often these issues impact your business.
*   **Which of the above issues impact your business?** (`issues_urgent`, select_multiple): This question is about the issues that have a significant impact on your business. Select all the options that apply to you.
*   **What are the details of these issues?** (`sync_issues_details`, note): Please provide as much detail as possible about these issues that have a significant impact on your business.
