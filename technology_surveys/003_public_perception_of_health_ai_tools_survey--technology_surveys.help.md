# public_perception_of_health_ai_tools_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on public perception of health AI tools used in the past 6 months.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions honestly, and provide as much detail as possible.
2. Make sure to select the correct options that apply to your experiences.
3. You can provide additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field if you have any further thoughts or insights.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info** (`User Info`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for you to provide some basic information about yourself. Please enter your job title or role.
* **What is your job title or role?** (`question_1`, select_one, required: true)
	+ Please select the option that best describes your job title or role. This will help us understand your professional context when using AI-powered health tools.
* **Have you used AI-powered health tools in the past 6 months?** (`question_2`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ If you've used AI-powered health tools in the past 6 months, select 'yes'. If not, select 'no'.
* **How often do you use AI-powered health tools in your work?** (`question_3`, select_one, required: true)
	+ Please select how often you use AI-powered health tools in your work. Options are 'daily', 'weekly', 'monthly', or 'rarely'.
* **How would you rate the overall usability of AI-powered health tools?** (`question_4`, number, required: false)
	+ Rate the overall usability of AI-powered health tools on a scale of 1 (very poor) to 5 (very good). This will help us understand your experience with the tools.
* **What is the main purpose of AI-powered health tools for you?** (`question_5`, select_one, required: true)
	+ Please select the option that best describes the primary purpose of AI-powered health tools for you.
* **How would you rate the accuracy of AI-powered health tools?** (`question_6`, select_one, required: true)
	+ Rate the accuracy of AI-powered health tools on a scale from 1 (very poor) to 5 (very good).
* **How would you rate the ease of use of AI-powered health tools?** (`question_7`, select_one, required: true)
	+ Rate the ease of use of AI-powered health tools on a scale from 1 (very poor) to 5 (very good).
* **Have you experienced any issues with AI-powered health tools?** (`question_8`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ If you've experienced any issues with AI-powered health tools, select the options that apply.
* **What do you expect from AI-powered health tools in the future?** (`question_9`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ If you have any expectations or suggestions for future AI-powered health tools, select the options that apply.
* **Additional Comments** (`note`, note, required: false)
	+ Provide any additional comments or insights you have about your experience with AI-powered health tools.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false)
	+ If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up or additional information, enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false)
	+ If you'd like to be contacted by phone, enter your phone number.
