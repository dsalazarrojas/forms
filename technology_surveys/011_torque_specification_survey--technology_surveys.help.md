# Torque Specification Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Torque Specification Survey is a tool designed to collect information about your experience with torque specifications and standards. Your input will help us better understand your needs and preferences when it comes to torque standardization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you have a good understanding of the questions being asked.
2. Select the correct units used for torque specification (e.g., "Yes" for units like in-lb, N·m, etc.)
3. Provide any common challenges you face when dealing with torque specification (type in your answer).
4. Describe these common challenges in more detail (type in your answer).
5. Indicate if you use any specific torque standards (e.g., "Yes" for standards like SAE or ASME).
6. Enter the range of torque values you typically work with (e.g., 1-100 in-lb).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Torque Specification Values**
* `torque_specification_values`: Please enter the range of torque values you typically work with.

### **Units Used**
* `torque_units`: Please select the units you use for torque specification. If your organization uses multiple units, select all that apply. 
  * 'Yes' for units like in-lb, N·m, etc.
  * 'No' for units not used in your work.

### **Common Challenges**
* `common_challenges`: Describe any common challenges you face when dealing with torque specification. This could be issues with data consistency, communication, or something else entirely.

### **Description of Common Challenges**
* `common_challenges_description`: Please provide more details about the common challenges you described above.

### **Standards Used**
* `torque_standards`: Please indicate if you use any specific standards for torque specification. If your organization uses multiple standards, select all that apply.
  * 'Yes' for standards like SAE or ASME.
  * 'No' for standards not used in your work.

### **Torque Value Range**
* `torque_value_range`: Please enter the range of torque values you typically work with (e.g., 1-100 in-lb).

## Tips
* Make sure to answer honestly and accurately, as your input will help inform our understanding of torque specifications and standards.
* If you have any suggestions or comments, please feel free to include them in the "Common Challenges" and "Description of Common Challenges" sections.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions or answers, please ask your supervisor or contact person for clarification.
