# Treasury Risk Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Treasury Risk Awareness Survey is a tool designed to help identify and document potential risks that may affect the organization's financial operations. This form gathers information about risks related to company-wide concerns, risk areas, risk impact, frequency, and severity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the company's name in the "Company Name" field.
2. Indicate the specific risk areas that are relevant to the reported risk in the "Risk Areas" field.
3. Describe the risk in the "Risk Description" field.
4. Assess the risk impact on a scale of 1-100 in the "Risk Impact" field.
5. Select the frequency of the risk occurrence in the "Risk Frequency" field.
6. Select the severity of the risk in the "Risk Severity" field.
7. If desired, provide any additional comments in the "Comments" field.
8. Submit the form by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Submit" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the company or entity related to the reported risk.
* **Risk Areas** (`risk_areas`, select_multiple, required): Select all relevant risk areas that are associated with the reported risk.
* **Risk Description** (`risk_description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the risk.
* **Risk Impact** (`risk_impact`, number, required): Assess the risk impact on a scale of 1-100.
* **Risk Frequency** (`risk_frequency`, select_one, required): Select the frequency of the risk occurrence from the provided options.
* **Risk Severity** (`risk_severity`, select_multiple, required): Select all relevant severity levels of the risk.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or explanations related to the risk.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether the risk report is submitted or not.
