# Workspace Login Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback on your experience with logging into the workspace. Your input will help us improve the login experience for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Make sure to select all that apply from the provided options.
3. If you've experienced issues during login, please describe them in detail in the "issue_description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **login_frequency**
**login_frequency** (select_one): How often do you log in to the workspace?
* Yes: You log in regularly.
* No: You don't log in frequently.

### 2. **days_logged_in**
**days_logged_in**: How many days have you logged in to the workspace?
* Please enter a number.

### 3. **devices_used**
**devices_used** (select_multiple): Which devices do you use to access the workspace?
* Yes: You use devices for login.
* No: You don't use devices for login.

### 4. **browsers_used**
**browsers_used** (select_multiple): Which browsers do you use to access the workspace?
* Yes: You use browsers for login.
* No: You don't use browsers for login.

### 5. **issues_encountered**
**issues_encountered** (select_multiple): Have you encountered any issues during login?
* Yes: You've experienced issues.
* No: You haven't experienced issues.

### 6. **satisfaction**
**satisfaction** (select_one): How satisfied are you with the login experience?
* Satisfied: You're satisfied.
* Dissatisfied: You're not satisfied.

### 7. **device_type**
**device_type** (select_one): What type of device do you use for login?
* Laptop: A laptop.
* Desktop: A desktop.
* Tablet: A tablet.
* Mobile: A mobile device.
* Other: Other devices.

### 8. **device_os**
**device_os** (select_one): What operating system is on your device?
* Windows: Windows OS.
* Mac: Mac OS.
* Linux: Linux OS.
* Unknown: Other operating systems.

### 9. **browser_type**
**browser_type** (select_one): What browser do you use for login?
* Chrome: Chrome.
* Firefox: Firefox.
* Safari: Safari.
* Edge: Edge.
* Internet Explorer: Internet Explorer.
* Other: Other browsers.

### 10. **browser_os**
**browser_os** (select_one): What operating system is on your browser?
* Windows: Windows OS.
* Mac: Mac OS.
* Linux: Linux OS.
* Chrome OS: Chrome OS.
* Other: Other operating systems.

### 11. **issue_type**
**issue_type** (select_multiple): What types of issues have you experienced during login?
* Login required: Login required.
* Password required: Password required.
* Access denied: Access denied.
* Other: Other issues.

### 12. **issue_description**
**issue_description** (select_multiple): Can you describe the issues you've experienced during login?
* Yes: Please describe the issues.
* No: You haven't experienced issues during login.

Note to the user: Please provide honest and detailed feedback on your experience with the login experience. Your input will help us improve the workspace for everyone.
