# Cloud Systems Analyst Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information about your experiences and opinions as a Cloud Systems Analyst. Your input will help us understand your needs and challenges in the field and inform future development and support for cloud-based systems.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your full name as it appears on official documents (e.g., passport, driver's license).
2. Enter your email address that is used to contact you (we will not share your email address).
3. Type the name of your current company.
4. Indicate how many years you have been working as a Cloud Systems Analyst.
5. Select all the cloud platforms you currently use for your work.
6. Rate your overall job satisfaction on a scale of 1-5.
7. Rate your satisfaction with the current tools you use on a scale of 1-5.
8. Choose the biggest challenges you face in your role (select all that apply).
9. If you're not using any tools, you may skip this field.
10. Rate your team's effectiveness on a scale of 1-5.
11. If you're not interested in any emerging technologies, you may skip this field.
12. Briefly describe your career goals for the next 2-3 years.
13. If you have any suggestions for improvement, please type them here.
14. If you're willing to mentor others, select 'True'. Otherwise, select 'False' or 'Maybe'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name** (`respondent_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Your Email Address** (`respondent_email`, email, required): Enter your email address that is used to contact you.
* **Current Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Type the name of your current company.
* **Years as Cloud Systems Analyst** (`years_in_role`, number, required): Indicate how many years you have been working as a Cloud Systems Analyst.
* **Primary Cloud Platforms Used** (`primary_cloud_platform`, select_multiple, required): Select all the cloud platforms you currently use for your work.
* **Overall Job Satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your overall job satisfaction on a scale of 1-5.
* **Satisfaction with Current Tools** (`tools_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction with the current tools you use on a scale of 1-5.
* **Biggest Challenges in Role** (`biggest_challenges`, select_multiple, required): Choose the biggest challenges you face in your role (select all that apply).
* **Training Needed** (`training_needed`, select_multiple, optional): If you're interested in any of the listed topics, select all that apply. If not, you may skip this field.
* **Workflow and Process Satisfaction** (`workflow_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction with the workflow and process of your role on a scale of 1-5.
* **Team Effectiveness Rating** (`team_effectiveness`, select_one, optional): Rate your team's effectiveness on a scale of 1-5.
* **Interest in Emerging Technologies** (`emerging_technologies`, text, optional): If you're interested in any emerging technologies, please describe them briefly.
* **Career Goals for Next 2-3 Years** (`career_goals`, text, optional): Briefly describe your career goals for the next 2-3 years.
* **Suggestions for Team Improvement** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions for improvement, please type them here.
* **Willing to Mentor Others** (`mentor_availability`, select_one, optional): If you're willing to mentor others, select 'True'. If not, select 'False' or 'Maybe'.
