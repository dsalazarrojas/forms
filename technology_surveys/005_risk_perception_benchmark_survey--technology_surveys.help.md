## Purpose
This form is designed to gauge the risk perception of various stakeholders in the industry, particularly in technology sectors. Your input will help us understand the risk perception landscape in the industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Select your preferred technology sector from the options provided in the technology_sector field.
2. Choose the subsector that best describes your work or industry from the options in industry_subsector.
3. Indicate the size of your company using the options in company_size.
4. Select the type of company you represent using the options in company_type.
5. Select the region where your company operates from the options in region.
6. Choose the level of industry experience you have using the options in industry_experience.
7. Indicate the level of risk perception you have using the options in risk_perception.
8. Select the frequency of risk occurrence in your industry using the options in risk_frequency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **risk_perception_scale**: This is a scale to gauge your risk perception level, which is not required.
* **risk_level**: This field is optional and measures the level of risk perceived in your industry.
* **technology_sector**: Choose the technology sector that best describes your work or industry, such as Information Technology, Internet of Things, Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity, or Cloud Computing.
* **industry_subsector**: Select the subsector that best describes your work or industry, such as Finance, Healthcare, Retail, Automotive, Education, or Other.
* **company_size**: Indicate the size of your company, which can be Small, Medium, or Large.
* **company_type**: Select the type of company you represent, which can be Private, Public, or Non-Profit.
* **region**: Choose the region where your company operates, which can be Asia, Europe, North America, Latin America, Africa, or Oceania.
* **industry_experience**: Measure the level of industry experience you have using the options Less than 1 year, 1-5 years, 5-10 years, or More than 10 years.
* **risk_perception**: Select the level of risk perception you have, which can be Low, Medium, or High.
* **risk_frequency**: Choose the frequency of risk occurrence in your industry, which can be Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Yearly, or Never.
