# Electric Vehicle Readiness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information from users about their current or future plans for owning an electric vehicle. By completing this form, we aim to understand your needs and preferences, and tailor our outreach efforts to better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please complete this form in its entirety. If you need assistance or clarification, don't hesitate to ask.
2. Fill in your information accurately and truthfully.
3. For select multiple or one fields, you can select multiple or single values from the provided options.
4. For text fields, please enter your response and do not exceed the character limit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name** (`name`, text, required): Please enter your full name so that we can address you correctly in our communication.
* **Are you an electric vehicle owner or interested in buying one?** (`name`, select_one, true): Check one of the three options to indicate your status.
	+ True: You are already an electric vehicle owner or have already committed to buying one.
	+ False: You have no plans for owning an electric vehicle.
	+ Maybe: You have some interest or are considering it, but no concrete plans.
* **How many Electric Vehicles do you own or are you interested in purchasing?** (`name_of_ev`, number, true): Please enter the number of electric vehicles you currently own or expect to own, or are interested in buying.
* **What kind of charging options do you currently have or are looking for?** (`charging_interest`, select_multiple, false): Check the options that apply to you:
	+ Workplace charging
	+ Home charging
	+ Public charging
	+ Other
* **How often do you charge your EVs?** (`charging_frequency`, select_one, true): Please select one of the four options:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Never
* **How many miles do you drive per week?** (`driving_frequency`, number, false): If applicable, please enter the number of miles you drive per week.
* **What is your primary location for charging?** (`charging_location`, select_one, true): Select one option:
	+ Home
	+ Workplace
	+ Public
	+ Other
* **What is the make of your current or desired electric vehicle?** (`vehicle_make`, text, false): Please enter the make of your current or desired electric vehicle, e.g. "Tesla", "Toyota".
* **What is the model of your current or desired electric vehicle?** (`vehicle_model`, text, false): Enter the model number or name, e.g. "Model 3", "Leaf" for the Nissan Leaf.
* **What is the range of your current or desired electric vehicle?** (`vehicle_range`, number, false): Enter the vehicle range in miles, e.g. 200, 300.
* **What do you need help with regarding electric vehicle outreach?** (`outreach_needs`, select_multiple, false): Check the options that apply:
	+ Education and awareness
	+ Test drive opportunities
	+ Workplace charging infrastructure
	+ Vehicle pricing and incentives
	+ Other
* **Do you have any additional comments about electric vehicle adoption?** (`comments`, note, false): Please provide any additional thoughts or comments you have about electric vehicle adoption.
* **Email address** (`email`, email, false): Enter your email address so that we can contact you for follow-up or to provide information.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, text, false): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you for follow-up or to provide information.
* **What year do you expect to purchase an electric vehicle?** (`vehicle_year`, number, false): Enter the expected year of purchase.
* **What is your current estimated budget for an electric vehicle?** (`vehicle_budget`, number, false): Enter your current budget for an electric vehicle.
* **Are you considering leasing an electric vehicle?** (`vehicle_leasing`, select_one, true): Check one of the three options:
	+ True: You are considering leasing an electric vehicle.
	+ False: You are not considering leasing an electric vehicle.
	+ Maybe: You are unsure or open to the idea.
* **Do you have any additional comments?** (`comments_additional`, note, false): Please provide any additional thoughts or comments you have.
* **Name** (`name_of_contact`, text, true): Please enter your name so that we can address you correctly.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, false): Please enter your contact information such as phone number, email or social media handle so that we can follow up with you.
* **What type of electric vehicle are you interested in?** (`vehicle_type`, select_one, true): Select one of the options:
	+ Sedan
	+ SUV
	+ Truck
	+ Van
	+ Motorcycle
* **What size electric vehicle are you interested in?** (`vehicle_size`, select_one, true): Select one of the four options:
	+ Compact
	+ Full-size
	+ Large
	+ Extra Large
	+ Other
* **What color electric vehicle do you prefer?** (`vehicle_color`, select_multiple, true): Check one or more options for your preferred color:
	+ Black
	+ White
	+ Gray
	+ Silver
	+ Other
* **What type of vehicle do you prefer?** (`vehicle_type_of_vehicle`, select_one, false): Select one of the options:
	+ Hatchback
	+ SUV
	+ Wagon
	+ Van
	+ Other
* **Other vehicle options?** (`vehicle_other`, text, false): Enter any other vehicle options or preferences you have.
