# mobile_app_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with a mobile application. The purpose of this form is to gather insights into user satisfaction, usage frequency, and experience with the app.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions based on your actual experience with the mobile app.
2. Select the options that best describe your experience.
3. Provide specific feedback and comments in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### How Satisfied Are You with Your Overall Experience Using the Mobile App?
* **Very Satisfied** (`Very satisfied`, Single): How satisfied are you with your overall experience with the mobile app, with the highest level of satisfaction being "Very Satisfied".
* **Somewhat Satisfied** (`Somewhat satisfied`, Single): How satisfied are you with your overall experience with the mobile app, with the middle level of satisfaction.
* **Neutral** (Neutral, Single): How satisfied are you with your overall experience with the mobile app, with the middle level of satisfaction.
* **Somewhat Dissatisfied** (`Somewhat dissatisfied`, Single): How dissatisfied are you with your overall experience with the mobile app, with the lowest level of satisfaction.
* **Very Dissatisfied** (`Very dissatisfied`, Single): How dissatisfied are you with your overall experience with the mobile app, with the lowest level of satisfaction.

### How Often Do You Typically Use the Mobile App?
* **Daily** (`Daily`, Single): How often do you use the mobile app on a daily basis.
* **Several Times a Week** (`Several times a week`, Single): How often do you use the mobile app on a weekly basis.
* **Weekly** (`Weekly`, Single): How often do you use the mobile app on a weekly basis.
* **Monthly** (`Monthly`, Single): How often do you use the mobile app on a monthly basis.
* **Rarely** (`Rarely`, Single): How often do you use the mobile app.
* **Never** (`Never`, Single): How often do you use the mobile app.

* **Easy to Use** (`Easy to use`, Multiple): This option best describes your experience with the app's user interface and ease of use.

* **Reliable** (`Reliable`, Multiple): This option best describes your experience with the app's reliability and stability.

* **Lacking Features** (`Lacking features`, Multiple): This option best describes your experience with the app's feature set and functionality.

### What Is the Primary Reason You Use the Mobile App?
* **Browsing Content** (`Browsing content`, Single): This option best describes the primary reason you use the app for browsing content.
