# online_collaboration_fatigue_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect feedback about online collaboration and virtual meetings from end-users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Read through the questions carefully and answer them to the best of your ability.
2. Select your response for each question using the provided options.
3. Enter any additional comments or feedback in the "comments" field.
4. Click "Submit" to send your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This is a text area where you can read a brief introduction about the purpose and goals of this survey.
* **How do you feel about virtual meetings**: This question asks you to select all that apply from the provided options. Please choose "Yes" if you have positive feelings about virtual meetings, "No" if you have negative feelings, or select both if you have a mix of feelings.
* **What is your role in the meeting**: This question asks you to select the role you play in virtual meetings. Please choose the option that best describes your position in the meeting.
* **How long have you been participating in virtual meetings**: This question asks you to select the length of time you have been participating in virtual meetings.
* **Time in virtual meetings**: This question asks you to select the amount of time you spend in virtual meetings.
* **How much do you prefer video in virtual meetings**: This question asks you to select how much you prefer video in virtual meetings. Please choose "Always", "Sometimes", "Rarely", or "Never" based on your preference.
* **Satisfaction level**: This question asks you to select your overall satisfaction level with virtual meetings. Please choose "Satisfied", "Neutral", or "Dissatisfied".
* **How do virtual meetings affect your productivity**: This question asks you to select all that apply from the provided options. Please choose "Yes" if virtual meetings affect your productivity positively, "No" if they have no effect, or both if they have a mix of effects.
* **Comments**: This is a text area where you can enter any additional comments or feedback about virtual meetings.
