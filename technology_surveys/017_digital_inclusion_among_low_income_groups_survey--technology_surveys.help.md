# digital_inclusion_among_low_income_groups_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your experiences and perceptions on digital inclusion, internet access, and digital literacy among low-income groups.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer each question carefully and to the best of your ability.
- If you are unsure or do not have access to a particular option, select the option that best represents your situation.
- You may skip fields that are not applicable or relevant to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

This field is a header that provides an introduction to the survey.
