# Digital Identity Awareness In Academia Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to collect demographic and professional information from academics in academia to better understand digital identity awareness within the academic community. This information will help in identifying areas where academics may need more assistance or support in developing a secure digital presence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Choose the correct options for each field from the provided lists.
3. Enter any additional information required in the text fields.
4. Review your information before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1
* **Form title** (`survey_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title for your survey response.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your institutional email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter your academic department.
* **Role in institution** (`role_in_institution`, text, optional): Describe your role within the institution.
* **Role in research** (`role_in_research`, text, optional): Describe your role in research projects.
* **Role in research** (`role_in_research`, text, optional): This field is duplicate, please disregard.

### Page 2

* **Age** (`age`, number, optional): Enter your age.
* **Highest education level completed** (`highest_education_level_completed`, select_one, optional): Select your highest education level (Less than high school, High school, Some college, Bachelor's degree, Master's degree, Doctoral degree, or Other).
* **Average years of experience** (`average_years_of_experience`, number, optional): Enter your average years of experience.
* **Average hours of labor worked** (`average_hours_of_labor_worked`, number, optional): Enter your average hours of labor worked per week.
* **Average hours in labor per week** (`average_hours_labor_per_week`, number, optional): This field is duplicate, please disregard.
* **Average months of labor** (`average_months_of_labor`, number, optional): This field is duplicate, please disregard.
* **Average months in work per year** (`average_months_worked_per_year`, number, optional): This field is duplicate, please disregard.
* **Average years of employment** (`average_years_of_employment`, number, optional): Enter your average years of employment.
* **Average years of service** (`average_years_of_service`, number, optional): This field is duplicate, please disregard.
* **Job title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Departmentalization** (`departmentalization`, text, optional): Enter your departmentalization (not sure what this means, please refer to institutional definitions).
* **Research area** (`research_area`, text, optional): Enter your research area.
* **Research experience** (`research_experience`, text, optional): Enter your research experience.
* **Publication count** (`publication_count`, number, optional): Enter your publication count.
