# Robotics Onboarding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to onboard new members into the robotics team. It collects essential information to help the team understand your background, skills, and availability. Completing this form ensures you're properly introduced to the team and helps us tailor the onboarding process to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Answer each question in the required or optional fields as directed. Each page is designed to guide you through the form.
2. Review and ensure you've completed all required fields (indicated by a red star *).
3. When finished, submit the form to begin your onboarding process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

**Introduction (Page 1)** 
* This is a brief welcome message about the form and the robotics team. Please read carefully to understand what the form is about and what the team is all about.

* **Name (Page 2)** 
  * Enter your full name as you'd like to be known within the team.
  * This field is optional, but it helps us get to know you better.

* **Experience Level** 
  * Select your level of experience with robotics from:
    + Robotics Team Member: You're already part of a robotics team or have experience in robotics.
    + New Member: You're new to robotics or the team.
    + Experienced Member: You have significant experience in robotics, but not necessarily within a team.
    + Other: Select this if you have a different level of experience.

* **Role** 
  * Choose your role within the team from:
    + Team Member: You contribute to the team's projects and activities.
    + Team Leader: You lead the team or have a leadership role.
    + Other: If you have a different role in mind, please select this option.

* **Skills** 
  * Select the skills you have or are interested in developing from:
    + Programming: Coding languages, algorithms, software development.
    + Engineering: Mechanical, electrical, and systems engineering.
    + Design: Designing, prototyping, and product development.
    + Other: If you have a specific skillset not listed, please select this option.

* **Interests** 
  * Choose your interests from:
    + Robotics: Robotics-related topics or projects.
    + Programming: Programming languages, software development, etc.
    + Engineering: Mechanical, electrical, and systems engineering.
    + Design: Designing, prototyping, and product development.
    + Other: If you have a specific interest not listed, please select this option.

* **School or University** 
  * Select where you've studied from:
    + High School: Your educational background.
    + University: Your university or college education.
    + Online Course: Online courses or certifications.
    + Other: If you've attended a different educational institution, please select this option.

* **Goals** 
  * This field is for you to describe your short and long-term goals in the team or with robotics, if any.

* **Contact Info** 
  * Enter your preferred contact information for team communication (email or phone number).

* **Email** 
  * Enter your email address for team communication.

* **Time Commitment** 
  * Describe how many hours per week you're willing or available to dedicate to the team.

* **Availability** 
  * Choose a date for your first team meeting or availability for team activities.

* **Additional Info** 
  * If there's anything else you'd like to share about yourself or your goals, please use this field.

* **Training Needs** 
  * If you need any specific training or resources, describe them here.

* **Emergency Contact** 
  * Enter contact information for a family member or emergency contact.

This guide is designed to help new team members understand the form's purpose and how to complete it. The form is divided into sections for easy navigation, and each field is explained in detail to ensure clarity. Completing this form ensures a smooth onboarding process and helps the team tailor their support to your needs.
