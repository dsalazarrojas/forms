# IT Autonomy Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess an employee's level of autonomy in performing various IT tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer each question to the best of your ability. Select the option that best describes your level of autonomy for each task. If you are unsure or have no experience with a particular task, select "Undecided".
