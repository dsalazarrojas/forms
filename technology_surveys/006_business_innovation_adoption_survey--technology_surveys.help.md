# Business Innovation Adoption Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information about employees' awareness, comfort level with new technologies, and perceived barriers to innovation adoption within the company. The data collected will help the organization improve its innovation initiatives and provide targeted support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions honestly, as your input will help improve the company's innovation initiatives.
2. Select one response for each question, even if you are not entirely sure.
3. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
4. You may skip questions that don't apply to you or are not relevant to your role.
5. If you have any additional feedback or suggestions, please use the "Additional Feedback" field at the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name (1)** (`employee_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your company ID badge or on your email signature.
* **Employee ID (2)** (`employee_id`, text, optional): If you have a unique employee ID, please enter it.
* **Department (3)** (`department`, select_one, required): Select your department from the list.
* **Job Level (4)** (`job_level`, select_one, required): Select your current job level from the list.
* **Years with Company (5)** (`tenure_years`, number, required): Enter the number of years you have worked with the company.
* **Awareness of Current Innovation Initiatives (6)** (`innovation_awareness`, select_one, required): Select how aware you are of current innovation initiatives within the company.
* **Technology Comfort Level (7)** (`technology_comfort_level`, select_one, required): Select how comfortable you are with adopting new technologies.
* **Barriers to Innovation Adoption (8)** (`innovation_barriers`, select_multiple, required): Select all barriers you experience in adopting new technologies or innovations.
* **Training Needs (9)** (`training_needs`, select_multiple, required): Select all training types that would help you adopt new technologies or innovations.
* **Innovation Communication Effectiveness (10)** (`communication_effectiveness`, select_one, required): Select how effectively leadership communicates about innovation within the company.
* **Resource Availability for Innovation (11)** (`resource_availability`, select_one, required): Select how often adequate resources are provided for innovation within your team or department.
* **Innovation Incentives (12)** (`innovation_incentives`, select_one, required): Select how strong or weak incentives are for adopting innovations within your organization.
* **Peer Support for Innovation (13)** (`peer_support`, select_one, required): Select how supportive or unsupportive your colleagues are of innovation adoption within your team or department.
* **Management Support for Innovation (14)** (`management_support`, select_one, required): Select how supportive or unsupportive your direct manager is of innovation adoption within your team or department.
* **Awareness of Innovation Success Stories (15)** (`innovation_success_stories`, select_one, required): Select how many successful innovation implementations you are aware of within the company.
* **Personal Readiness for Change (16)** (`change_readiness`, select_one, required): Select how ready or hesitant you are to accept organizational change.
* **Innovation Suggestions (17)** (`innovation_suggestions`, select_one, required): Select if you have ever submitted or planned to submit innovative suggestions to the company.
* **Collaboration Tools Usage (18)** (`collaboration_tools_usage`, select_multiple, required): Select all collaboration tools you regularly use within your team or department.
* **Innovation Priorities for Organization (19)** (`innovation_priorities`, select_multiple, required): Select all areas the company should prioritize for innovation adoption.
* **Additional Feedback on Innovation (20)** (`additional_feedback`, text, optional): Share any additional thoughts or suggestions on innovation adoption within the company.
