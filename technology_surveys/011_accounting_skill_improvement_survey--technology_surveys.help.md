# Accounting Skill Improvement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your perspective on accounting skills improvement. The goal is to gather information that will help us identify areas where you need more training and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Select your primary role in the accounting field from the options provided.
2. Choose your years of experience in accounting from the options provided.
3. Rate your current proficiency level in accounting fundamentals from the options provided.
4. Choose your comfort level with accounting software and technology from the options provided.
5. Select all areas where you feel you need more training.
6. Choose how you prefer to learn new accounting skills from the options provided.
7. Indicate how much time you can dedicate to training per week from the options provided.
8. Select all accounting technology tools you currently use regularly.
9. Choose the top priority for your training from the options provided.
10. Select all expectations you have from a training program.
11. If you have a budget for training, choose an approximate range from the options provided.
12. If you have specific goals for improving your accounting skills, enter them in the text box provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Role in Accounting** (`respondent_role`, select_one, required): Select your primary role in the accounting field from the options provided.
* **Years of Experience** (`years_experience`, select_one, required): Choose your years of experience in accounting from the options provided.
* **Current Overall Proficiency Level** (`current_proficiency_level`, select_one, required): Rate your current proficiency level in accounting fundamentals from the options provided.
* **Comfort Level with Accounting Technology** (`technology_comfort_level`, select_one, required): Choose your comfort level with accounting software and technology from the options provided.
* **Areas Where You Feel You Need Improvement** (`skill_gaps_identified`, select_multiple, optional): Select all areas where you feel you need more training.
* **Preferred Learning Method** (`preferred_learning_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you prefer to learn new accounting skills from the options provided.
* **Time Available for Training Per Week** (`time_available_for_training`, select_one, required): Indicate how much time you can dedicate to training per week from the options provided.
* **Technology Tools Currently Used** (`technology_tools_used`, select_multiple, optional): Select all accounting technology tools you currently use regularly.
* **Top Priority for Training** (`training_priority`, select_one, required): Choose the top priority for your training from the options provided.
* **What Would You Expect From a Training Program** (`training_expectations`, select_multiple, optional): Select all expectations you have from a training program.
* **Budget for Training** (`budget_for_training`, select_one, optional): If you have a budget for training, choose an approximate range from the options provided.
* **Specific Goals for Skill Improvement** (`improvement_goals`, text, optional): If you have specific goals for improving your accounting skills, enter them in the text box provided.
