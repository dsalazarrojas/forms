# Barber Shop Technology Usage Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Barber Shop Technology Usage Survey is designed to collect information about the technology usage and preferences of barber shops. This form will help us understand how barbers shop owners currently manage their businesses, what technology they use, and their needs and interests in technology solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please fill out each field with the most accurate information about your barber shop.
2. Select the options that best fit your shop's situation.
3. Be honest and detailed in your responses, especially for the open-ended questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name**: Your Name is a required field, but you may choose to remain anonymous. Please enter your name as it appears on your business license or ID.
* **Shop Name**: This is the name of your barber shop. Please enter the name as it appears on your business license or ID.
* **Years in Business**: Select the number of years your shop has been operating.
* **Number of Barbers Employed**: Choose the number of barbers working in your shop, including yourself.
* **Current Booking System**: Select how you currently manage appointments: Phone calls, Walk ins, Paper appointment book, Basic digital calendar, Dedicated booking software, or Online booking platform.
* **Point of Sale System**: Choose the POS system you use: Cash register only, Basic card reader, Square or similar, Full POS system, or No POS system.
* **Satisfaction with Current Software**: Rate your overall satisfaction with your current software: Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, or Very dissatisfied.
* **Biggest Technology Challenges**: Select all the technology challenges you face: Cost of software, Learning curve, Lack of features, Poor customer support, Integration issues, Reliability problems, or No major challenges.
* **Interest in Online Booking**: Choose how interested you are in online booking: Already have it, Very interested, Somewhat interested, Not interested.
* **Social Media Usage**: Select all the social media platforms you use for your business: Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Twitter or X, YouTube, or None.
* **Digital Marketing Tools Used**: Select all the digital marketing tools you currently use: Email marketing, SMS reminders, Social media ads, Google ads, Loyalty programs, or None.
* **Inventory Management Method**: Choose how you track products and supplies: Manual tracking, Spreadsheet, Basic software, Advanced inventory system, or No tracking system.
* **Monthly Technology Budget**: Select the amount you spend on technology each month: Less than 50 dollars, 50 to 100 dollars, 101 to 200 dollars, 201 to 500 dollars, or More than 500 dollars.
* **Most Desired Features**: Select the features you would like to see in your current technology solutions: Automated reminders, Online payments, Client database, Analytics and reporting, Marketing automation, Staff scheduling, or Inventory alerts.
* **Mobile App for Business Management**: Choose if you use a mobile app to manage your shop: Yes, extensively, Yes, occasionally, No, but interested, or No, not interested.
* **Technology Training Needed**: Choose if you would benefit from technology training: Yes, definitely, Maybe, or No, I am comfortable.
* **Planning Technology Investment**: Select when you plan to invest in new technology: Yes, within 3 months, Yes, within 6 months, Yes, within a year, or No plans currently.
* **Additional Comments**: This is an optional field where you can add any other thoughts or comments about technology in your shop.
