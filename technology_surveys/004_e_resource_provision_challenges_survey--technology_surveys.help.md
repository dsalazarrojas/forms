# E Resource Provision Challenges Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to collect feedback on the challenges you face when accessing resources related to the "E Resource Provision" category. Your input will help us better understand your needs and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out each section completely, as indicated by the required field status (required or optional).
2. Select the most relevant options for each field from the available choices.
3. Provide detailed responses to the text fields, such as "What challenges do you face when accessing resources?".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **E Resource Provision Challenges Survey** (`survey_title`, note, optional): This is the title of the survey, and you can leave this field blank if you prefer.
* **Institution Type** (`institution_type`, select_one, optional): Please select the type of institution you are accessing resources from (e.g. University, College, etc.).
* **Institution Size** (`institution_size`, select_one, optional): Indicate the size of the institution you are accessing resources from (e.g. Large, Medium, Small).
* **Resource Type** (`resource_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of resource you are trying to access (e.g. Books, Articles, etc.).
* **Digital Delivery Methods** (`digital_delivery`, select_multiple, optional): Check all the methods through which you prefer or are accustomed to receiving digital resources (e.g. Email, Portal, etc.).
* **Access Level** (`access_level`, select_one, optional): Indicate the level of access you have to the resource (e.g. Public, Restricted, Private).
* **Access Restrictions** (`access_restrictions`, select_multiple, optional): Check any restrictions you face when trying to access resources (e.g. Time of day, Location, etc.).
* **What challenges do you face when accessing resources?** (`feedback`, text, optional): Describe the specific challenges you face when trying to access resources.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or thoughts about accessing resources.
* **What are some pain points or issues with accessing resources?** (`pain_points`, text, optional): List or describe any pain points or issues you encounter when accessing resources.
* **What improvements would you like to see in terms of resource provision?** (`improvement_priorities`, text, optional): Suggest any improvements you'd like to see in the way resources are provided.
* **Any additional suggestions or comments?** (`suggestions`, text, optional): Share any additional thoughts or comments you have about accessing resources.
