# Impact Of Heat Wave Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about the impact of heat waves on individuals and households. The purpose of this form is to gather data that can be used to inform decision-making and policies related to heat waves.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page of the form carefully.
2. Answer each question honestly and accurately.
3. Select all that apply from the options provided.
4. If you're unsure about any question, please use the available options or add your own answer in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `note`, required: false)
	+ This is a note field where you can write any additional information about your experience with heat waves.
* **Survey Part 1** (`Survey Part 1`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ Please select all that apply from the options provided:
		- Heat Wave Impacts
		- Heat Waves
* **Coping Strategies** (`Coping Strategies`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Please select one coping strategy that you have used to deal with heat waves.
	+ Options:
		- Air conditioner
		- Fan
		- Window Shutter
		- None
		- Other (please specify)
* **Local Needs** (`Local Needs`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ Please select all that apply from the options provided:
		- Air Conditioner
		- Fan
		- Window Shutter
		- Other (please specify)
* **Demographic Info** (`Demographic Info`, `number`, required: false)
	+ Please select a number:
		+ Number of people in your household.
* **Household Info** (`Household Info`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Please select the number of people in your household:
		+ 1 or 2
		+ 3 or 4
		+ 5 or 6
		+ 7 or 8
		+ 9 or 10
		+ Other (please specify)
* **Survey Part 2** (`Survey Part 2`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ Please select all that apply from the options provided:
		- Heat Wave Impacts
		- Heat Waves
