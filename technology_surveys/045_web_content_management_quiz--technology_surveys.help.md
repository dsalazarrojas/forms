# Web Content Management Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Web Content Management Quiz is designed to collect feedback and ratings from users about the effectiveness of the Web Content Management Quiz.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the category of the quiz you are providing feedback on.
2. Choose one or more of the reasons you are providing feedback for (e.g. onboarding, internal enablement, classroom learning).
3. Enter your feedback in the designated field.
4. Select a date and time for the feedback.
5. Add any notes or comments as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Web Content Management Quiz** (`web_content_management_quiz`, text, optional): A brief title or description of the quiz.
* **CMS Knowledge** (`cms_knowledge`, number, optional): A rating of your level of understanding of the content management system.
* **Quiz Category** (`web_content_management_quiz_category`, select_one, optional): The category of the quiz (e.g. Technology Surveys).
* **Quiz Description** (`web_content_management_quiz_description`, text, optional): A brief description of the quiz.
* **Onboarding Learning Purpose** (`onboarding_learning_purpose`, select_multiple, optional): The reasons you are providing feedback for (e.g. onboarding, internal enablement, classroom learning).
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): The date you completed the quiz.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): The time you completed the quiz.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Any additional comments or notes about the quiz.
