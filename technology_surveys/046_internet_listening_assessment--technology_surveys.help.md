# internet_listening_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about your online habits and experiences. It will help us understand how you use the internet and what challenges you face when online.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select all the answers that apply to you.
2. For multiple-choice questions, choose one of the options that best describes your situation.
3. If a question does not apply to you, you can leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Internet Listening Habits** (`internet_listening_habits`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ This question asks about your listening habits when using the internet.
	+ Do you often listen to podcasts, audiobooks, or online lectures? Select all that apply.
* **Online Communication Skills** (`online_communication_skills`, select_one, required: false):
	+ This question asks about your skills in communicating online.
	+ Which of the following best describes your online communication skills?
	+ Yes: You are comfortable expressing yourself online.
	+ No: You are less comfortable expressing yourself online.
* **Online Listening Skills** (`online_listening_skills`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ This question asks about your listening skills when interacting with others online.
	+ Do you often listen actively while interacting with others online? Select all that apply.
* **Online Community Involvement** (`online_community_involvement`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ This question asks about your involvement in online communities.
	+ Do you participate in online forums, social media groups, or online communities? Select all that apply.
* **Online Content Consumption** (`online_content_consumption`, select_one, required: false):
	+ This question asks about your online content consumption habits.
	+ How often do you consume online content (e.g. videos, articles, podcasts)?
	+ Yes: You often consume online content.
	+ No: You rarely consume online content.
* **Online Security Challenges** (`online_security_challenges`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ This question asks about your online security concerns.
	+ Do you often worry about online security issues (e.g. hacking, identity theft)? Select all that apply.
* **Online Safety Concerns** (`online_safety_concerns`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ This question asks about your online safety concerns.
	+ Do you often worry about online safety issues (e.g. scams, online harassment)? Select all that apply.
* **Online Privacy Concerns** (`online_privacy_concerns`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ This question asks about your online privacy concerns.
	+ Do you often worry about online privacy issues (e.g. data collection, surveillance)? Select all that apply.
* **Internet Speed** (`online_internet_speed`, number, required: false):
	+ This question asks about your internet speed.
	+ What is your average internet speed (e.g. 10 Mbps, 50 Mbps, 100 Mbps)?
