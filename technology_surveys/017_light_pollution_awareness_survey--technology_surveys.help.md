# Light Pollution Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Light Pollution Awareness Survey is designed to gather information about your experiences and opinions on nighttime lighting. This form is for personal feedback and not for any other use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer each question to the best of your ability and truthfully.
2. Make sure to fill in all the required fields.
3. If a question is not applicable to you, feel free to skip it.
4. Enter any additional comments or information you feel is relevant.
5. Review your form carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### What do you see in the night sky? (Page 1)
**What do you see in the night sky?** (`survey_start`, text, optional): Describe what you usually see in the night sky, such as stars, moon, city lights, or other objects. 

### What do you use for lighting in your home? (Page 2)
**What do you use for lighting in your home?** (`energy_use`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the light sources you use in your home, such as LED bulbs, incandescent bulbs, or smart lighting systems. 

### How much nighttime lighting do you use for outdoor activities? (Page 3)
**How much nighttime lighting do you use for outdoor activities?** (`nighttime_lighting`, number, optional): On a scale of 1-5, rate how often you use nighttime lighting for activities like walking, reading, or watching movies outside. 

### How much does skyglow affect your life? (Page 4)
**How much does skyglow affect your life?** (`skyglow`, select_one, optional): Choose the option that best describes how much skyglow, or light pollution, affects your daily life. 

### What can we do to improve nighttime lighting in our community? (Page 5)
**What can we do to improve nighttime lighting in our community?** (`community_feedback`, note, optional): Share your ideas and suggestions on how to make nighttime lighting more beneficial and less harmful to our community. 

### How much time have you spent on this form? (Page 6)
**How much time have you spent on this form?** (`submission_confirmation`, time, optional): This is a check-in to ensure you've spent the right amount of time completing the form. 

### Email (Page 7)
**Email** (`email_input`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for any follow-up questions or surveys. 

### Phone (Page 8)
**Phone** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted for any follow-up questions or surveys. 

### Additional comments (Page 9)
**Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any further comments or thoughts on the topic, please share them here. 

Tips:

* Be honest and accurate in your responses.
* If you're unsure about a question, try to answer based on your best judgment.
* Don't worry if you can't answer a question - you can skip it if it's not applicable.
