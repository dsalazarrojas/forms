# Camera Demo Request Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from potential customers for camera demo requests. It helps us understand your needs and requirements for a camera solution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal information, including your name, company name, job title, and email address.
2. Select your industry and company size to help us better understand your needs.
3. Choose the type of camera solution you need and its primary use case.
4. Specify your room size and current camera equipment status.
5. Select your budget range and preferred demo date and time.
6. Choose how you would like to receive the demo and the number of attendees.
7. If you have any specific technical requirements or questions, provide them in the text field.
8. Select when you plan to make a decision and who is the decision maker.
9. Finally, add any additional questions or comments you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your first and last name.
* **Company or Organization** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your business or organization.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your role or position within your company.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your business email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact phone number (optional).
* **Industry** (`industry`, `select_one`, required): Select your industry from the options provided:
	+ Technology
	+ Healthcare
	+ Education
	+ Media and Entertainment
	+ Corporate Business
	+ Government
	+ Non-profit
	+ Retail
	+ Other
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, `select_one`, required): Select your company size from the options provided:
	+ 1-10 employees
	+ 11-50 employees
	+ 51-200 employees
	+ 201-500 employees
	+ 501-1000 employees
	+ Over 1000 employees
* **Type of Camera Solution You Need** (`camera_type_interest`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply:
	+ Conference room camera
	+ PTZ camera
	+ Webcam for desktop
	+ 360-degree camera
	+ Security camera
	+ Broadcast camera
	+ Action camera
	+ Other
* **Primary Use Case** (`primary_use_case`, `select_one`, required): Select your primary use case from the options provided:
	+ Video conferencing
	+ Live streaming
	+ Recording presentations
	+ Security monitoring
	+ Content creation
	+ Surveillance
	+ Training and education
	+ Other
* **Room Size for Camera Installation** (`room_size`, `select_one`, optional): Select the typical room size from the options provided:
	+ Small huddle room (1-4 people)
	+ Medium conference room (5-10 people)
	+ Large conference room (11-20 people)
	+ Auditorium or large venue (20+ people)
	+ Multiple rooms
* **Do you currently have camera equipment** (`current_equipment`, `select_one`, required): Select your current status from the options provided:
	+ No - first time purchase
	+ Yes - looking to upgrade
	+ Yes - need additional units
	+ Yes - replacing faulty equipment
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, `select_one`, optional): Select your estimated budget range from the options provided:
	+ Under $500
	+ $500-$1000
	+ $1001-$2500
	+ $2501-$5000
	+ $5001-$10000
	+ Over $10000
* **Preferred Demo Date** (`preferred_demo_date`, `date`, required): Select your preferred demo date.
* **Preferred Demo Time** (`preferred_demo_time`, `time`, required): Select your preferred demo time.
* **Demo Location Preference** (`demo_location`, `select_one`, required): Select how you would like to receive the demo from the options provided:
	+ Virtual demo via video call
	+ In-person at our office
	+ Visit your showroom
	+ Webinar or group demo
* **Number of Attendees for Demo** (`attendees`, `select_one`, optional): Select how many people will attend the demo:
	+ 1-2 people
	+ 3-5 people
	+ 6-10 people
	+ More than 10 people
* **Specific Technical Requirements** (`technical_requirements`, `text`, optional): Provide any specific features you need or questions you have.
* **When do you plan to make a decision** (`decision_timeline`, `select_one`, required): Select when you plan to make a decision from the options provided:
	+ Immediate - within 2 weeks
	+ 1 month
	+ 2-3 months
	+ 4-6 months
	+ Just researching
* **Are you the decision maker** (`decision_makers`, `select_one`, required): Select your role from the options provided:
	+ Yes - I make the final decision
	+ I am part of the decision team
	+ I am researching for my team
	+ Other
* **Questions or Comments** (`additional_questions`, `text`, optional): Add any additional questions or comments you may have.
