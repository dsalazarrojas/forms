# Beauty Tech Device Adoption Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather insights on how individuals use and perceive beauty technology devices. Your responses will help us understand your preferences and needs, which will inform the development of future beauty technology products and services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions honestly, and select all the options that apply to your preferences.
2. Make sure to fill out the form completely, as indicated by the required fields.
3. When selecting from multiple options, choose the ones that best fit your experience or expectations.
4. For open-ended questions, provide detailed and clear answers.
5. Please note that you can only fill out this form once.
