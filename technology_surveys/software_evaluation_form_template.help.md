# Software Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a feedback collection tool designed to help us improve our software application. Please take a few minutes to share your honest opinions about your recent experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Choose one of the options for the "Overall satisfaction" question, selecting the one that best describes your feelings.
2. Select how often you use the software from the provided options.
3. Check all the boxes that apply to the "Describe your experience" question, selecting the options that best reflect your thoughts about the software.
4. Enter any comments or suggestions you may have in the text area.
5. Add any additional comments or suggestions you may have in the text area.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall, how satisfied are you with the software?** (`Overall Satisfaction`, `single`, required): This question measures your general satisfaction level with the software. Select the option that best describes your feelings.
* **How often do you typically use this software?** (`Frequency of Use`, `single`, required): This question helps us understand how often you use the software, which can help us tailor our development efforts.
* **Which of the following describe your experience with the software?** (`Describe your experience`, `multiple`, required): This question gives you the opportunity to select all the options that accurately reflect your experience with the software's usability and feature set.
* **What features or aspects would you improve or change?** (`Suggestions`, `text`, required): This question is an open-ended invitation to share any specific features or aspects of the software that you'd like to see changed or improved.
* **Do you have any additional comments or suggestions?** (`Additional Comments`, `text`, optional): This is a final chance to share any extra thoughts or ideas you may have about the software.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and accurately to help us improve the software.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please feel free to add them in the text area.
* If you've already submitted feedback, you can submit it again, but please make sure to update your answers to reflect any changes you've experienced since your last submission.
