# Mobile Network Connectivity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mobile Network Connectivity Survey is designed to collect feedback about your mobile network experience. This form helps us understand your level of satisfaction, coverage, and any issues you may have encountered.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "General Info" page to begin.
2. On the "General Info" page, enter a brief description of your mobile network issue or experience.
3. On subsequent pages, you will be presented with additional questions related to your mobile network experience.
4. For each question, select one of the provided options that best describes your experience.
5. Once you have answered all questions, click "submit" to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Info (Text)** (`general_info`, Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of your mobile network issue or experience.
* **Area (Select)** (`area`, Select One, Optional): Choose one of the following options for your mobile network coverage:
	+ 3G: You are covered by 3G network.
	+ 4G: You are covered by 4G network.
	+ 5G: You are covered by 5G network.
* **Network Speed (Select)** (`network_speed`, Select One, Optional): Rate your mobile network speed:
	+ Slow: My mobile network is slow.
	+ Average: My mobile network speed is average.
	+ Fast: My mobile network is fast.
* **Coverage (Select Multiple)** (`coverage`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose all that apply for your mobile network coverage:
	+ Strong: You have strong coverage.
	+ Weak: You have weak coverage.
	+ No: You have no coverage.
* **Reliability (Select)** (`reliability`, Select One, Optional): Rate how often you experience issues with your mobile network:
	+ Often: I experience issues often.
	+ Sometimes: I experience issues sometimes.
	+ Rarely: I experience issues rarely.
* **Satisfaction (Select)** (`satisfaction`, Select One, Optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with your mobile network:
	+ Very Satisfied: I am very satisfied.
	+ Satisfied: I am satisfied.
	+ Neutral: I am neutral.
	+ Dissatisfied: I am dissatisfied.
	+ Very Dissatisfied: I am very dissatisfied.
* **Issue Description (Text)** (`issue_description`, Text, Optional): Provide a detailed description of the issue you are experiencing.
* **Additional Info (Text)** (`additional_info`, Text, Optional): If you have any additional information about your experience, please provide it here.
* **Submit (Text)** (`submit`, Text, Optional): Click here to submit your feedback.
* **Email (Email)** (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for follow-up questions.
* **Phone (Text)** (`phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted for follow-up questions.
* **Date (Date)** (`date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date of your experience, if applicable.
