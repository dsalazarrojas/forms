# Treasury Data Visualization Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather user feedback and suggestions on how to improve data visualization in the Treasury department.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Answer the questions that are relevant to you and your experience with data visualization.
* If you're unsure or don't have experience with a particular topic, feel free to skip that question.
* Make sure to review your answers before submitting the form.
