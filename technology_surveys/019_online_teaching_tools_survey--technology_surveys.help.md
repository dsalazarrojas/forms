# Online Teaching Tools Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and opinions from instructors and educators about their experiences with online teaching platforms such as JotForm, Kahoot, Google Classroom, and Canvas. The primary purpose of this form is to gather information on users' satisfaction with these platforms, their level of satisfaction, and areas that need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select the option that best represents your opinion.
3. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as you feel is necessary to express your thoughts.
4. You can skip any question that does not apply to your experience.
5. Please be honest and constructive in your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Online Teaching Tools Survey Form**: This is the main section of the form where you will answer the questions.
* **Platform**: Select the online teaching platform you are most familiar with (e.g., JotForm, Kahoot, Google Classroom, Canvas). (Required: false)
	+ Choose the platform you use most frequently or have the most experience with.
* **Satisfaction with Platform**: Rate your satisfaction with the platform on a scale of 1-10. (Required: false)
	+ This question helps us understand your overall satisfaction with the platform.
* **Challenges Faced**: Select the challenges you faced while using the platform. (Required: false)
	+ Choose all that apply to you.
	+ Difficulty with technology
	+ Difficulty with learning management system
	+ Difficulty with content creation
	+ Difficulty with engagement
	+ Difficulty with other
* **Platform Satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with the platform on a scale of 1-10. (Required: false)
	+ This question helps us understand your overall satisfaction with the platform.
* **Platform Liking**: Rate your liking for the platform on a scale of 1-10. (Required: false)
	+ This question helps us understand your overall liking for the platform.
* **Platform Improvement**: Select the area that needs improvement in the platform. (Required: false)
	+ Choose one of the following:
		- User interface
		- Features
		- Support
		- Other
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about the platform. (Required: false)
	+ This is an open-ended question where you can provide any additional thoughts or suggestions about the platform.

Tips:

* Please be honest and constructive in your feedback.
* You can skip any question that does not apply to your experience.
* Your feedback is valuable and will help us improve these online teaching tools.
