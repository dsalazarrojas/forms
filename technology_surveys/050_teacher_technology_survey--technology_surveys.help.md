# Teacher Technology Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The purpose of this survey is to gather information about teachers' experiences and perceptions of technology use in the classroom. This form will help us better understand the current state of technology adoption and support in our school, identify areas of need, and inform future professional development opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form

This form is divided into several sections, each with specific questions that aim to gather different types of information. Please read each question carefully and answer according to your experiences and opinions.

*   Fill in the numerical fields with whole numbers.
*   Select all applicable options for select\_multiple, select\_one, and select\_two fields.
*   Enter your answer in the text field for text questions.
*   Indicate whether you have or have not had an issue in the checkbox field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your experience level with technology in the classroom? (<name>: teacher_experience_with_technology)**: This question asks about your level of experience with technology in the classroom. Please answer with a number that corresponds to:
	1. Novice (little to no experience)
	2. Basic (some experience, but not extensive)
	3. Intermediate (moderate experience)
	4. Advanced (substantial experience)
	5. Expert (high level of experience)
* **What technology platforms do you use for communication with students and parents? (<name>: technology_used_for_communication)**: This question asks about the platforms you use for communication with students and parents. Please select all that apply from:
	* Email
	* Online forum
	* Social media
* **How many hours do you spend per week on professional development using technology? (<name>: hours_spent_on_professional_development)**: This question asks about the amount of time you spend on professional development using technology. Please answer with a whole number of hours.
* **What type of technical skills do you think are essential for teachers to have? (<name>: technical_skills_needed)**: This question asks about the technical skills you consider essential for teachers. Please select all that apply from:
	* Programming
	* Data analysis
	* Multimedia design
* **Have you received any technical training or certification in the past 2 years? (<name>: technical_training)**: This question asks about your experience with technical training or certification in the past 2 years. Please answer with:
	* Yes
	* No
* **What are the top 3 technologies that you use in your classroom? (<name>: top_technologies_used)**: This question asks about the top 3 technologies you use in your classroom. Please select all that apply from:
	* Computer
	* Tablet
	* Laptop
* **How many hours of technical support do you receive from your school's IT department per week? (<name>: technical_support)**: This question asks about the amount of technical support you receive from your school's IT department. Please answer with a whole number of hours.
* **What are the top 3 technical skills that teachers in your school lack? (<name>: lacking_skills)**: This question asks about the technical skills you think your colleagues lack. Please select all that apply from:
	* Programming
	* Data analysis
	* Multimedia design
* **Have you had any issues with technology in the past year? (<name>: technical_issues)**: This question asks about your experience with technology issues in the past year. Please answer with:
	* Yes
	* No
* **How many hours do you spend on technical issues per week? (<name>: technical_support_hours)**: This question asks about the amount of time you spend on technology issues per week. Please answer with a whole number of hours.
* **What would you like to see in the future regarding technology usage in school? (<name>: future_technology_usage)**: This question asks about your vision for the future of technology usage in your school. Please enter your thoughts and ideas in the text field.
* **Any additional comments? (<name>: additional_comments)**: This question is an opportunity for you to share any additional thoughts or comments you have.
* **What technical skills do you need to improve your teaching practice? (<name>: technical_skills)**: This question asks about the technical skills you would like to acquire to improve your teaching practice. Please select all that apply from:
	* Presentation design
	* Data analysis
	* Online resources
* **What technical skills and knowledge would you like to acquire? (<name>: technical_skills_and_knowledge)**: This question asks about the technical skills and knowledge you would like to acquire. Please select all that apply from:
	* Programming
	* Data analysis
	* Multimedia design

Please note that this form is designed to be completed anonymously, and all answers will be treated confidentially.

**Tips**

*   Be honest and honest when answering the questions.
*   Please do not leave any fields blank.
*   If you need help or have questions, feel free to ask.
