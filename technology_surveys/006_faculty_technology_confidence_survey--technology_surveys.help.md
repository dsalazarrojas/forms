# Faculty Technology Confidence Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Faculty Technology Confidence Survey is designed to gather your thoughts and opinions on technology use, confidence, and readiness in the academic setting. Your input will help us better understand your technology needs and preferences, enabling us to provide targeted support and resources.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Select the most appropriate option for each question, even if it's not explicitly listed.
3. If you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask your administrator or support team for guidance.
4. Please submit the form once you've completed all sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, required): This is the name of the faculty member completing the survey.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): The department where you are currently employed.
* **Technology Confidence** (`technology_confidence`, number, required): This question assesses your overall technology confidence level, with higher scores indicating greater confidence.
* **Confidence Scale** (`confidence_scale`, text, optional): If you'd like to elaborate on your confidence level, please provide more details in this text field.
* **Technology Use** (`technology_use`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the technology tools or platforms you frequently use in your work.
* **Other Technology Use** (`other_technology_use`, text, optional): If there's a technology tool not listed, please specify it here.
* **Technology Readiness** (`technology_readiness`, select_one, required): This question gauges your technology readiness level, with higher scores indicating greater preparedness.
* **Technology Readiness 2** (`technology_readiness_2`, select_one, required): Another question assessing your technology readiness level.
* **Technology Confidence Score** (`technology_confidence_score`, number, required): This is a numerical representation of your technology confidence level.
* **Technology Confidence Score 2** (`technology_confidence_score_2`, number, optional): Another score to gauge your confidence level.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): If you have any comments or suggestions for technology support or resources, please share them here.
* **Comments 2** (`comments_2`, note, optional): Another opportunity to provide comments or suggestions.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Your departmental email address, used to contact you for support or follow-up.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Your departmental phone number, if applicable.
* **Technology Survey Comments** (`technology_survey_comments`, text, optional): Any additional comments about the survey or technology use.
* **Technology Survey Comments 2** (`technology_survey_comments_2`, text, optional): More comments or suggestions.
* **Technology Confidence Score 5** (`technology_confidence_score_5`, number, optional): Another score to gauge your confidence level.
* **Technology Confidence Score 6** (`technology_confidence_score_6`, number, optional): Yet another score.
* **Technology Confidence Score 7** (`technology_confidence_score_7`, number, optional): Another score.
* **Technology Confidence Score 8** (`technology_confidence_score_8`, number, optional): And another score.
* **Technology Confidence Score 9** (`technology_confidence_score_9`, number, optional): Another score.
