# ISP Sampling Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ISP Sampling Survey is a form designed to collect feedback and opinions from internet service providers (ISPs) regarding their satisfaction and experiences with various aspects of their services. This form is used to gauge the overall quality and satisfaction of their services, providing valuable insights for improvement and enhancement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and answer the questions as accurately as possible.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select one or multiple options that apply to your experience.
3. Use the text fields to provide detailed and descriptive answers.
4. Ensure you fill out all required fields, marked as "required" in the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Page 1 (text)**: This is the introductory page where you can provide any additional comments or context about your experience with your ISP's services.
* **Satisfaction Levels (select_multiple)**: Please select one or more of the satisfaction levels that apply to your experience:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **Page 3 (text)**: This field is for any additional comments about your services experience.
* **Page 4 (text)**: This field is for any specific issues or concerns you have with your services.
* **Page 5 (date)**: If you have any specific dates or timeframes for events or issues, please provide them here.
* **Page 6 (time)**: If you have any specific time-related comments or experiences, enter them here.
* **Page 7 (email)**: This field is not applicable for this form.
* **Page 8 (text)**: This field is for any further comments or thoughts.
* **Page 9 (select_one)**: Please select one of the options that best describes your experience:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Page 10-25 (text)**: These fields are for detailed and specific comments or answers to questions that will help us better understand your experience with your ISP's services.
* **Page 10-25 (text)**: These fields are required and should be filled out as accurately and thoroughly as possible.

Note: Please make sure to fill out all required fields as marked with "required" in the form.
