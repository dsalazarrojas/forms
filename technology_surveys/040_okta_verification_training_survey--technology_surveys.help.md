# Okta Verification Training Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Okta Verification Training Survey is a form used to collect feedback and comments from users about their training experience. The survey helps to identify areas of improvement and provide insights for future training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the corresponding options for each field according to your experience and knowledge.
2. Fill in the required fields accurately and thoroughly.
3. Review your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Training Quality
* **what_was_the_training_quality**: This field asks about the quality of the training you received. Please rate the training on a scale of 1 (low) to 3 (high).

### Page 2: Training Clarity
* **how_clear_was_the_training**: This field asks about how clear the training was. Please rate the training on a scale of 1 (low) to 3 (high).

### Page 3: Confidence Level
* **how_confident_did_you_feel_at_the_end**: This field asks about how confident you felt at the end of the training. Please select one or more options from the list.

### Page 4: Participant Information
* **prophets_name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your company's employee directory or ID card.

### Page 5: Job Title
* **job_title**: This field asks for your job title as it appears on your company's employee directory or ID card.

### Page 6: Department
* **department**: Please select your department from the list.

### Page 7: Supervisor Name
* **prophets_supervisor_name**: Please select your supervisor's name from the list.

### Page 8: Supervisor Title
* **supervisor_title**: This field asks for your supervisor's title as it appears on their company ID card.

### Page 9: Okta Training
* **prophets_okta_training**: This field asks about your Okta training experience. Please select one option.

### Page 10: Training Length
* **training_length**: This field asks about the duration of the training session. Please enter the length of the session.

### Page 11: Session Date
* **training_session_date**: This field asks for the date of the training session. Please enter the date.

### Page 12: Okta Email
* **prophets_email**: Please enter your company email address.

### Page 13: Okta Phone
* **prophets_phone**: Please enter your company phone number.

### Page 14: Comments
* **prophets_comments**: Please enter any additional comments you have about the training session.

### Page 15: Okta Session ID
* **prophets_okta_session_id**: This field asks for the Okta session ID as it appears on your company ID card.

### Page 16: Session Notes
* **prophets_session_notes**: Please enter any notes about the training session.

### Page 17: Training Notes
* **prophets_training_notes**: Please enter any notes about the training session.

### Page 18: Comments
* **prophets_comments**: Please enter any additional comments about the training session.

### Page 19: Okta Survey ID
* **prophets_okta_survey_id**: This field is not required.
