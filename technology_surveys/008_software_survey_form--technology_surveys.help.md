# Software Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your company and your experience with software tools. By completing this form, you will help us understand your company's and software usage, which will aid our development team in creating better software solutions for you and others.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
    *   Read each field carefully and answer according to your company's and software usage.
    *   Ensure that all required fields are completed. Required fields are marked as such.
    *   If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to contact us.
    *   Double-check your form for any errors before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Software Survey Form** (`software_survey_form`, text, required: false): This field is a text area where you can enter any additional comments or information you think would be useful for us to know about your company and software usage.
*   **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required: false): Enter your company name here.
*   **Company URL** (`company_url`, text, required: false): Enter your company website URL here.
*   **Company Type** (`company_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the best option that describes your company type from the list. 
    +   Company type 1
    +   Company type 2
    +   Company type 3
*   **Software Survey** (`software_survey`, number, required: false): This field is meant to capture the number of software you use for your company.
*   **Time Used Software** (`time_used_software`, number, required: false): Enter the amount of time your company uses software.
*   **How Much Time** (`how_much_time`, time, required: false): Choose a time period that best describes how much time your company uses software.
*   **How Satisfied** (`how_satisfied`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose all the options that best describe how satisfied you are with our software.
    +   Very satisfied
    +   Somewhat satisfied
    +   Not satisfied
    +   Not at all
*   **Contact** (`contact`, text, required: false): Enter your name and any other contact information you'd like to share with us.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your company email address here.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your company's phone number here.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false): Use this field to add any additional comments or suggestions you have about our software.
*   **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false): Use this field to provide any feedback you have about our software.
Please fill out the form completely and accurately to help us better understand your company's and software usage. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
