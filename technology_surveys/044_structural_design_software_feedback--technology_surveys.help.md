# Structural Design Software Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from customers on the structural design software provided by the company. The feedback will help us understand your experience with the software and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide a brief description of your overall satisfaction level with the software in the "Satisfaction Level" field.
2. In the "What you like least about structural design software" field, please describe the feature or aspect of the software that you are least satisfied with.
3. In the "Feature requests or improvement ideas" field, please provide any suggestions or ideas you have for improving the software.
4. If you would like to provide your email address or phone number, you can do so in the "Email" and "Phone" fields, respectively. This is optional.
5. If you would like to report the date and time you last used the software, you can provide this information in the "Date" and "Time" fields, respectively. This is also optional.
6. Click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Satisfaction Level** (required):
	+ Please provide a brief description of your overall satisfaction level with the software.
* **What you like least about structural design software** (required):
	+ Please describe the feature or aspect of the software that you are least satisfied with.
* **Feature requests or improvement ideas** (required):
	+ Please provide any suggestions or ideas you have for improving the software.
* **Email** (optional):
	+ If you would like to be contacted about your feedback, please provide your email address.
* **Phone** (optional):
	+ If you would like to be contacted about your feedback, please provide your phone number.
* **Date** (optional):
	+ If you would like to report the date you last used the software, please provide the date.
* **Time** (optional):
	+ If you would like to report the time you last used the software, please provide the time.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when describing your satisfaction level, least satisfied feature, and feature requests or improvement ideas.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas, please keep them concise and easy to read.
* If you would like to be contacted about your feedback, please make sure to provide a valid email address or phone number.
