# Youth Climate Policy Understanding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information on the youth perspective on climate policy, including their awareness, understanding, attitudes, and the impact of climate policy on their daily lives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the spaces provided, being as thorough as possible.
2. For each question, select the relevant option from the list provided.
3. Make sure to review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **youth_climate_policy_understanding_survey** (`youth_climate_policy_understanding_survey`, text, optional): This is the main question of the survey. Answer this question in a concise and clear manner, providing your thoughts and opinions on youth climate policy.
* **climate_policy_awareness** (`climate_policy_awareness_label`, text, optional): Describe your level of awareness about climate policy. This can include any relevant knowledge or experiences you have had with climate policy.
* **climate_policy_attitudes** (`climate_policy_attitudes_label`, text, optional): Share your attitudes towards climate policy. This can include any personal or societal values, feelings, or thoughts you associate with climate policy.
* **ideas_about_climate_policy** (`ideas_about_climate_policy_label`, text, optional): What are your thoughts and opinions on climate policy? What do you think is being done well and what can be improved?
* **climate_policy_understanding_impacts** (`climate_policy_understanding_impacts_label`, text, optional): Describe how you think climate policy affects your daily life.
* **climate_policy_action_impacts** (`climate_policy_action_impacts_label`, text, optional): How does climate policy impact your daily actions and decisions?
* **climate_policy_action_opportunities** (`climate_policy_action_opportunities_label`, text, optional): What opportunities do you see for climate policy to create positive change in your community?
* **climate_policy_understanding_implementation** (`climate_policy_understanding_implementation_label`, text, optional): Describe any experiences or knowledge you have about the implementation of climate policy.
* **climate_policy_understanding_implementation_status** (`climate_policy_understanding_implementation_status_label`, text, optional): What is the status of climate policy implementation in your community?
* **climate_policy_understanding_implementation_status_importance** (`climate_policy_understanding_implementation_status_importance_label`, text, optional): How important is the status of climate policy implementation in your community?
* **climate_policy_understanding_implementation_status_frequency** (`climate_policy_understanding_implementation_status_frequency_label`, select_one, optional): Select the frequency of climate policy implementation in your community (Active, Inactive)

## Tips
* Answer questions honestly and thoroughly.
* Provide as much detail as possible.
* Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
