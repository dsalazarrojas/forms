# Health Tech Integration Readiness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Tech Integration Readiness Survey is used to assess the integration readiness of health technology in a specific context. It aims to evaluate whether a particular health technology is ready for integration and what steps can be taken to ensure a smooth and successful integration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and provide answers that reflect your situation.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for questions that are relevant to your current integration status.
3. Choose all that apply for questions that require multiple answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Main**
 * This field is a placeholder for the main page.
 
### 2. **Readiness**
 * Answer "Yes" if your health technology is ready for integration, otherwise answer "No".
 
### 3. **Barriers**
 * List all barriers that may hinder the integration of your health technology. 
 * Examples: lack of technical expertise, regulatory issues, financial constraints, etc.
 
### 4. **Contact**
 * Answer "Yes" if you need to be contacted for further integration discussions, otherwise answer "No".
 
### 5. **Method**
 * Answer "Yes" if you are ready for integration, otherwise answer "No".
 
### 6. **Integration**
 * Answer "Yes" if you are ready for integration, otherwise answer "No".
 
### 7. **Next Steps**
 * Describe the next steps you plan to take for integration.
 * Examples: training, pilot project, etc.
 
### 8. **Additional**
 * Provide any additional information that may be relevant to the integration process.
 
### 9. **Submit**
 * Click this button to submit your answers and complete the form.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly, your answers will help us better understand your integration readiness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this form.
