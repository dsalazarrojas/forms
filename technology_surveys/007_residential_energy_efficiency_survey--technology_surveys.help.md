# Residential Energy Efficiency Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about residential energy efficiency, energy sources, energy upgrades, and other related data from homeowners. This data will be used to assess the energy efficiency of a residential property and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Answer all required questions truthfully and accurately.
* For fields with multiple options, select all applicable choices.
* For fields with text input, provide clear and concise answers.
* For fields with date input, enter the date in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* Make sure to review and edit your answers carefully before submitting the form.
