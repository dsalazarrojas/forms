# Market Readiness For Digitalization Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Market Readiness For Digitalization Survey is designed to assess an organization's readiness for digitalization. It focuses on the organization's transformation, attitude, and technology usage to gauge its level of digitalization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all the questions honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for each question, as they are relevant to the digitalization process.
3. Ensure that you are aware of the consequences of selecting certain options, as they may impact your organization's digitalization status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Main Page (Page 1)
* **(No field label)** (`name`: `main_page`, `type`: `text`, `required`: `false`): This page serves as an introduction to the survey. It is not required to fill out, but it may contain context about the form's purpose and instructions.

### Digital Transformation (Page 2)
* **Digital Transformation (`name`: `page_2`, `type_: `select_one`, `required`: `true`): This question asks about the transformation of traditional processes into digital processes. Select the option that best describes your organization's current status.
	+ Option 1: Our organization has fully transformed most of its traditional processes into digital ones.
	+ Option 2: Our organization has partially transformed some traditional processes into digital ones.
	+ Option 3: Our organization has not transformed any traditional processes into digital ones.

### Digitalization (Page 3)
* **Digitalization (`name`: `page_3`, `type_: `select_multiple`, `required`: `false`): This question explores the use of digital technologies within your organization. Select all that apply:
	+ Option 1: We have fully implemented digital technologies across the organization.
	+ Option 2: We have partially implemented digital technologies across the organization.
	+ Option 3: We have not implemented digital technologies at all.

### Organization (Page 4)
* **Organization (`name`: `page_4`, `type_: `text`, `required`: `false`): This field is for providing additional information about your organization. Please enter any relevant details.

### Transformation (Page 5)
* **Transformation (`name`: `page_5`, `type_: `date`, `required`: `false`): This field is for recording the date of the last transformation of a traditional process into a digital process. If no transformation has occurred, please enter a date of '0000-00-00'.

### Technology (Page 6)
* **Technology (`name`: `page_6`, `type_: `time`, `required`: `false`): This field is for recording the time spent on digital activities. Please enter the time spent in hours and minutes.
