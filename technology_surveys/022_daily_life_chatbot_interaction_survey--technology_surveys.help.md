# Daily Life Chatbot Interaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Life Chatbot Interaction Survey is designed to gather information about your interactions with our chatbot. This survey aims to understand your preferences, habits, and experiences with the chatbot, helping us to improve its functionality and provide better services to you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form questions carefully and answer them based on your honest experiences with the chatbot.
2. Select the relevant options for each question, as they pertain to your interactions with the chatbot.
3. Fill out all required fields, denoted by the asterisk (\*) symbol next to the field label.
4. If a question is not applicable to your situation, please indicate this by selecting "None" or "Never" (if available).
5. You can skip optional fields, but your feedback is valuable to us, so please consider sharing any improvement suggestions.
