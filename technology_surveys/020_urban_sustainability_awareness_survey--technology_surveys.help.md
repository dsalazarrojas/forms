# Urban Sustainability Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from end-users on their current and future practices related to urban sustainability. It is not a mandatory form, and users can answer as much or as little as they like.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully review each section to provide the most accurate information possible.
- Fill out each section according to your current and future practices.
- Answer questions as much or as little as you like, but try to be as honest and thorough as possible.
- Be aware that your responses will be kept confidential and anonymous.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome** (`intro_page`, note, required: false): This is an introduction page to the survey. You can read and understand the purpose and guidelines of the form.
* **How aware are you about urban sustainability?** (`awareness_level`, select_one, required: false): This question helps us understand your current level of awareness about urban sustainability. You can select either "Yes" or "No" based on your current understanding.
* **What are you currently doing to contribute to urban sustainability?** (`current_sustainability_practices`, select_multiple, required: false): This question asks about your current practices related to urban sustainability. You can select multiple options that apply to you.
* **What do you plan to do in the future to contribute to urban sustainability?** (`future_sustainability_practices`, select_multiple, required: false): This question is similar to the previous one, but asks about your future plans and intentions.
* **Which of the following areas of knowledge about urban sustainability are you familiar with?** (`knowledge_areas`, select_multiple, required: false): This question helps us understand your knowledge and familiarity with various aspects of urban sustainability. You can select multiple options that apply to you.
* **What behaviors do you currently exhibit to contribute to urban sustainability?** (`behaviors`, select_multiple, required: false): This question is similar to "What are you currently doing..." but focuses on your personal behaviors.
* **Which areas of urban sustainability do you have an interest in?** (`interest_areas`, select_multiple, required: false): This question helps us understand your areas of interest related to urban sustainability. You can select multiple options that apply to you.
* **Demographic Information** (`demographic_info`, note, required: false): This section is about your personal demographic information. It is not mandatory and you can choose to fill it out or skip it.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): This field is for your email address. Please enter a valid email address if you want to provide it.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): This field is for your phone number. Please enter a valid phone number if you want to provide it.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required: false): This field is for submitting your survey. Please fill it out carefully and honestly.
* **Urban Area** (`demographic_urban_area`, select_one, required: false): This question helps us understand where you live in the urban area. You can select "Yes" or "No" based on your current location.
* **Specify** (`demographic_urban_area_other_input`, text, required: false): If you selected "No" for the previous question, please specify the urban area you live in.
* **Gender** (`demographic_gender`, select_one, required: false): This question is about your gender. You can select "Yes" or "No" based on your current gender.
* **Demographic Gender Other** (`demographic_gender_other`, text, required: false): If you selected "No" for the previous question, please specify your gender.
* **Demographic Gender Other Input** (`demographic_gender_other_input`, text, required: false): If you selected "Other" for the previous question, please enter a description of your gender.
* **Education Level** (`demographic_education_level`, select_one, required: false): This question is about your education level. You can select "High" or "Low" based on your current education level.
* **Demographic Education Level Other** (`demographic_education_level_other`, text, required: false): If you selected "Low" for the previous question, please specify your education level.
* **Demographic Education Level Other Input** (`demographic_education_level_other_input`, text, required: false): If you selected "Other" for the previous question, please enter a description of your education level.
* **Occupation** (`demographic_occupation`, text, required: false): This field is for your occupation. Please enter a valid occupation if you want to provide it.
* **Employment Status** (`demographic_employment_status`, select_one, required: false): This question is about your employment status. You can select "Active" or "Inactive" based on your current employment status.
* **Demographic Employment Status Other** (`demographic_employment_status_other`, text, required: false): If you selected "Inactive" for the previous question, please specify your employment status.
* **Demographic Employment Status Other Input** (`demographic_employment_status_other_input`, text, required: false): If you selected "Other" for the previous question, please enter a description of your employment status.
* **Monthly Income** (`demographic_income`, number, required: false): This field is for your monthly income. Please enter a valid number if you want to provide it.
