# Smart Storage Technology Pilot Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from organizations interested in participating in a Smart Storage Technology Pilot, a testing phase for new storage technologies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name
2. Provide your Company Name and Contact Email
3. Specify your Company Size
4. Select your Business Type
5. Choose the Technology Type your company uses
6. Select the Technology Level of your current solution
7. Specify the Project Goals you expect to achieve
8. Provide the Pilot Start Date and End Date (if applicable)
9. Describe the Technology Features you use
10. Describe the Technology Description and Expected Outcomes
11. Provide Lessons Learned and Recommendations
12. Specify Next Steps for your company
13. Add any Comments you have about the pilot project
14. Confirm your name and email for submission

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please fill in your first name as it will be used for internal communication.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please fill in your last name as it will be used for internal communication.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Please enter your company's name as it will be used for internal record-keeping.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Please enter your company's contact email for internal communication.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Please enter your company's contact phone number for internal communication.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_multiple, required): Please select the size of your company: Small, Medium, or Large.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, select_one, required): Please select the type of business your company is: Retail, Service, or Other.
* **Technology Type** (`technology_type`, select_one, required): Please select the type of technology your company uses: Data Storage, Data Processing, Data Analysis, Data Security, Data Management, or Other.
* **Technology Level** (`technology_level`, select_one, required): Please select the level of technology your company uses: Pilot, Beta, Alpha, or Other.
* **Pilot Location** (`pilot_location`, text, required): Please specify where you are planning to pilot the technology.
* **Project Goals** (`project_goals`, select_multiple, required): Please select the goals you expect to achieve: Improve efficiency, Increase revenue, or Other.
* **Pilot Start Date** (`pilot_start_date`, date, required): Please enter the date you plan to start the pilot.
* **Pilot End Date** (`pilot_end_date`, date, required): Please enter the date you plan to end the pilot.
* **Technology Features** (`technology_features`, text, required): Please describe the features of the technology you are using or planning to use.
* **Technology Description** (`technology_description`, text, required): Please describe your company's technology use and goals.
* **Expected Outcomes** (`expected_outcomes`, text, required): Please describe the expected outcomes of the pilot project.
* **Actual Outcomes** (`actual_outcomes`, text, required): Please describe the actual outcomes of the pilot project.
* **Lessons Learned** (`lessons_learned`, text, required): Please describe any lessons learned from the pilot project.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, text, required): Please provide recommendations for future development.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, required): Please specify next steps for your company.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Please add any comments you have about the pilot project.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required): Please confirm your name for submission.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, date, required): Please enter the date of submission.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, time, required): Please enter the time of submission.
* **Submitted By Email** (`submitted_by_email`, email, required): Please enter your email for submission.

This form will help us better understand your company's needs and goals, and will be reviewed internally to determine if your company is eligible for pilot participation.
