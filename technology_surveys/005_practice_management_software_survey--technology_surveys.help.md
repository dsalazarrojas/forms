# Practice Management Software Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your experience with practice management software. Your responses will help us improve the software and create a better experience for future users.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each question carefully and provide honest answers based on your experience with practice management software.
- Please answer all questions, even if it takes some time.
- If you are unsure of any question, please ask for clarification or skip the question.
- Submit your answers when you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Practice Management Software Used Today** (`practice_management_software_used_today`): Describe the practice management software you use today.
- **Practice Management Software Used in the Past 2 Years** (`practice_management_software_used_in_the_past_2_years`): List the practice management software you have used in the past 2 years.

  - Yes: The feature is used.

  - Yes: The feature is planned to be used.
