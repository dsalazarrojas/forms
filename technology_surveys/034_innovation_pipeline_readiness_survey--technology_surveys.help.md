# innovation_pipeline_readiness_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to assess an organization's innovation readiness, helping the Innovation Pipeline Team and the Innovation Pipeline Manager to identify areas of improvement and develop targeted strategies for innovation growth.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, please select the correct organization type from the options provided:
    *   Organization: 
        *   Select the type of organization you represent:
            - Innovation Pipeline Team
            - Innovation Pipeline Manager
            - Other
2.  Next, choose the department(s) related to your role in the organization:
    *   Department: 
        *   Select all departments that apply:
            - Marketing
            - Product Development
            - IT
            - Finance
            - Operations
            - Other
3.  Rate your organization's current innovation maturity level:
    *   innovation_maturity: 
        *   Rate your innovation maturity level from 0 to 100.
4.  Assess your organization's current innovation readiness score:
    *   readiness_for_innovation: 
        *   Rate your innovation readiness score from 0 to 100.
5.  Evaluate the current state of your innovation pipeline:
    *   innovation_pipeline_score: 
        *   Rate the current state of your innovation pipeline from 0 to 100.
6.  Determine the level of your innovation pipeline:
    *   innovation_pipeline_level: 
        *   Select the level of your innovation pipeline from:
            - Level 1 - Awareness
            - Level 2 - Understanding
            - Level 3 - Development
            - Level 4 - Deployment
            - Level 5 - Sustainability
7.  Identify the current process used in your organization:
    *   current_process: 
        *   Select the process currently used in your organization:
            - Agile
            - Waterfall
            - Hybrid
8.  Calculate your readiness for innovation score:
    *   readiness_for_innovation_score: 
        *   Rate your readiness for innovation score from 0 to 100.
9.  Estimate the potential impact of innovation on your organization:
    *   readiness_for_innovation_impact: 
        *   Rate the potential impact of innovation on your organization from 0 to 100.
10. Describe the potential impact of innovation on your organization:
    *   readiness_for_innovation_impact_description: 
        *   Enter a brief description of the potential impact of innovation on your organization.
11. Provide a comment on your organization's innovation pipeline readiness:
    *   innovation_pipeline_readiness_comment: 
        *   Enter any comments or additional information regarding your organization's innovation pipeline readiness.
12. Calculate your overall innovation pipeline readiness score:
    *   innovation_pipeline_readiness_score: 
        *   Rate your overall innovation pipeline readiness score from 0 to 100.
13. Review and submit your answers:
