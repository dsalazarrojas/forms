# phone_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user insights on phone usage and satisfaction with current mobile technology.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer the questions as they appear below. You can select one or multiple options for the questions that allow multiple selections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How satisfied are you with your current smartphone experience?** (`Very satisfied`, `Somewhat satisfied`, `Neutral`, `Somewhat dissatisfied`, `Very dissatisfied`): This question asks about your level of satisfaction with your current smartphone experience.
* **How often do you upgrade your smartphone?** (`Annually`, `Every 2-3 years`, `Every 4+ years`, `Only when broken`, `Never`): This question inquires about the frequency of upgrading your smartphone.
* **Which factors are most important when choosing a new smartphone?** (select all that apply): This question allows you to choose the factors that are most important to you when selecting a new smartphone. You can select multiple options from the list below:
	+ **Battery life**
	+ **Camera quality**
	+ **Price**
	+ **Brand**
	+ **Screen size**
	+ **Performance**
	+ **Operating system**
* **What is your primary use for your smartphone?** (`Communication`, `Social media`, `Web browsing`, `Gaming`, `Work/Productivity`, `Entertainment`): This question asks about your primary use for your smartphone.
* **What would you improve about your current smartphone or mobile service?** (free text): This question allows you to provide any improvements or changes you would like to see in your current smartphone or mobile service.
* **Do you have any additional comments or feedback about mobile technology?** (free text): This question allows you to share any further comments or feedback about mobile technology.
