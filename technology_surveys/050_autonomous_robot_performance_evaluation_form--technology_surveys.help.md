# Autonomous Robot Performance Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect data on your autonomous robot's performance during a test. This information will help us to better understand your robot's capabilities and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the date and time of the test.
2. Enter the location where the test was conducted.
3. Choose the duration of the test in minutes.
4. Specify the model and serial number of the robot.
5. Record the initial battery status.
6. Note the ambient temperature and humidity levels during the test.
7. Assess the lighting conditions during the test (e.g. Bright, Moderate, Dim, Dark, or Variable).
8. Evaluate the robot's navigation accuracy rating (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Failed).
9. Rate the robot's obstacle avoidance performance (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Failed).
10. Rate the robot's speed consistency (Very Consistent, Mostly Consistent, Variable, or Inconsistent).
11. Record the task completion rate as a percentage.
12. Rate the LiDAR sensor performance (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Not Used).
13. Rate the camera performance (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Not Used).
14. Describe any issues or failures encountered during the test.
15. Give an overall performance rating (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Failed).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Test Information --**
    + **Test Date**: The date on which you conducted the test. (Required)
    + **Test Time**: The time of day when you conducted the test. (Required)
    + **Test Location**: The location where the test was conducted. (Required)
    + **Test Duration**: The length of the test in minutes. (Required)
* **-- Robot Information --**
    + **Robot Model**: The model of the robot you tested. (Required)
    + **Robot Serial Number**: The serial number of the robot. (Required)
    + **Software Version**: The version of the software installed on the robot. (Required)
    + **Initial Battery Status**: The battery status of the robot at the start of the test. (Required)
* **-- Environmental Conditions --**
    + **Ambient Temperature (F)**: The temperature of the environment during the test. (Required)
    + **Humidity Level**: The humidity level of the environment during the test. (Optional)
    + **Lighting Conditions**: The lighting conditions during the test (e.g. Bright, Moderate, Dim, Dark, or Variable). (Required)
* **-- Performance Metrics --**
    + **Navigation Accuracy Rating**: The accuracy of the robot's navigation system. (Required)
    + **Obstacle Avoidance Performance**: The robot's ability to avoid obstacles. (Required)
    + **Speed Consistency**: The consistency of the robot's speed. (Required)
    + **Task Completion Rate (%)**: The rate at which the robot completed tasks. (Required)
* **-- Sensor Performance --**
    + **LiDAR Sensor Performance**: The performance of the LiDAR sensor. (Required)
    + **Camera Performance**: The performance of the camera. (Required)
* **-- Issues and Failures --**
    + **Issues or Failures Encountered**: Describe any issues or failures encountered during the test. (Optional)
* **-- Overall Performance --**
    + **Overall Performance Rating**: A general assessment of the robot's performance. (Required)
