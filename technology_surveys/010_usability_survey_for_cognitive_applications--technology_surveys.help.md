# Usability Survey For Cognitive Applications - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback and ratings from users about the usability of cognitive applications. It is meant to be filled out by users who have used a cognitive application and want to provide their input on its usability.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply follow the instructions below.

1. Choose the type of cognitive application you interacted with (Mobile, PC, or Web)
2. Rate the usability features of the application
3. Provide your overall rating of the application
4. Enter any comments or suggestions you have about the application
5. Enter any additional comments or suggestions
6. Enter your user ID (optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Overall User Rating** (`user_rating`, number, optional): Rate the overall usability of the application on a scale of 1-5.
  - Choose a number between 1 and 5 to indicate your overall impression of the application's usability.
- **What do you like about this tool?** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share your positive experiences and features you enjoyed about the application.
  - Write a few words about what you liked about the application.
- **What features could be improved?** (`feature_improvement_suggestions`, text, optional): Suggest any features that could be improved or added to the application.
  - Share your thoughts on what could be better about the application.
- **Usability Features Rating** (`usability_features_rating`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the features you think are important for the application's usability.
  - Select all the features that you think are important for the application.
- **Score (1-5)** (`usability_features_rating_score`, number, optional): Rate the application's usability features on a scale of 1-5.
  - Choose a number between 1 and 5 to rate the features' usability.
- **Comments** (`usability_features_rating_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or thoughts about the application's features.
  - Share any additional thoughts or comments about the features.
- **Improvement Suggestions** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, optional): Suggest any improvements or changes to the application.
  - Share any thoughts on what could be improved about the application.
- **Name of the Cognitive Application** (`cognitive_application_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the application.
  - Enter the name of the application you rated.
- **Type of the Cognitive Application** (`cognitive_application_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of cognitive application you interacted with.
  - Select the type of application (Mobile, PC, or Web).
- **Version of the Cognitive Application** (`cognitive_application_version`, number, optional): Enter the version of the application.
  - Enter the version number of the application.
- **Developer of the Cognitive Application** (`cognitive_application_developer`, text, optional): Enter the name of the application's developer.
  - Enter the name of the developer.
- **Developer Link** (`cognitive_application_developer_link`, text, optional): Enter the link to the application's developer.
  - Enter the link to the developer's website or contact information.
- **User ID** (`user_id`, number, optional): Enter your user ID (optional).
  - Enter your user ID if you have one, otherwise leave it blank.
- **Additional Comments** (`user_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or thoughts.
  - Share any additional comments or thoughts about the application.
- **Rating Time** (`user_rating_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you rated the application.
  - Enter the time you submitted the form.
- **Rating Date** (`user_rating_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you rated the application.
  - Enter the date you submitted the form.
- **Overall Rating** (`cognitive_application_rating_score`, number, optional): Enter your overall rating of the application on a scale of 1-5.
  - Choose a number between 1 and 5 to rate the application.
- **Cognitive Application Rating Comments** (`cognitive_application_rating_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or thoughts about the application.
  - Share any additional comments or thoughts about the application.
- **Version** (`cognitive_application_version_2`, number, optional): Enter the version number of the application.
  - Enter the version number of the application.
- **Cognitive Application Rating Date** (`cognitive_application_rating_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you rated the application.
  - Enter the date you submitted the form.
- **Cognitive Application Rating Time** (`cognitive_application_rating_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you rated the application.
  - Enter the time you submitted the form.
- **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, optional): Enter the form ID (optional).
  - Enter the form ID if you have one, otherwise leave it blank.
- **Form Version** (`form_version`, text, optional): Enter the version number of the form.
  - Enter the version number of the form.
- **Form Developer** (`form_developer`, text, optional): Enter the name of the form developer.
  - Enter the name of the form developer.
- **Form Developer Link** (`form_developer_link`, text, optional): Enter the link to the form developer.
  - Enter the link to the form developer's website or contact information.

## Tips
- Please rate the application based on your actual experience.
- Be honest and provide constructive feedback.
- If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to share them.
