# CI/CD Process Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from users about the Continuous Integration/Continuous Delivery (CI/CD) process. Your responses will help us identify areas for improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance the process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely, answering all required questions.
2. For optional questions, feel free to provide additional feedback or comments.
3. Select the relevant options for each question, using the rating scale or select options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official documentation or ID.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your work email address.
* **Your Role** (`team_role`, `select_one`, required): Select your primary role on the team:
	+ Developer
	+ DevOps Engineer
	+ QA Engineer
	+ Release Manager
	+ Tech Lead
	+ Platform Engineer
	+ Other
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your team.
* **How often do you use the CI/CD pipeline?** (`pipeline_usage_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select how often you use the CI/CD pipeline:
	+ Daily
	+ Several times per week
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Rarely
* **Overall, how effective is our CI/CD pipeline?** (`pipeline_effectiveness`, `select_one`, required): Rate the effectiveness of the CI/CD pipeline on a scale of 1-5:
	+ 1 - Not Effective
	+ 2 - Somewhat Ineffective
	+ 3 - Neutral
	+ 4 - Effective
	+ 5 - Very Effective
* **How satisfied are you with deployment speed?** (`deployment_speed`, `select_one`, required): Rate your satisfaction with deployment speed on a scale of 1-5:
	+ 1 - Very Dissatisfied
	+ 2 - Dissatisfied
	+ 3 - Neutral
	+ 4 - Satisfied
	+ 5 - Very Satisfied
* **How are failure handling and error reporting?** (`failure_handling`, `select_one`, required): Rate failure handling and error reporting on a scale of 1-5:
	+ 1 - Very Poor
	+ 2 - Poor
	+ 3 - Acceptable
	+ 4 - Good
	+ 5 - Excellent
* **How easy is the pipeline to use?** (`tool_usability`, `select_one`, required): Rate the ease of use of the pipeline on a scale of 1-5:
	+ 1 - Extremely Difficult
	+ 2 - Difficult
	+ 3 - Neutral
	+ 4 - Easy
	+ 5 - Very Easy
* **Is the documentation clear and helpful?** (`documentation_quality`, `select_one`, optional): Rate the quality of the documentation on a scale of 1-5:
	+ 1 - Not Helpful
	+ 2 - Somewhat Helpful
	+ 3 - Neutral
	+ 4 - Helpful
	+ 5 - Very Helpful
* **How would you rate the support for pipeline issues?** (`support_quality`, `select_one`, optional): Rate the support for pipeline issues on a scale of 1-5:
	+ 1 - Very Poor
	+ 2 - Poor
	+ 3 - Acceptable
	+ 4 - Good
	+ 5 - Excellent
* **What are the main pain points with the current pipeline?** (`main_pain_points`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply:
	+ Slow build times
	+ Unclear error messages
	+ Difficult to debug failures
	+ Lack of visibility
	+ Limited notifications
	+ Poor documentation
	+ Difficult approval process
	+ Other
* **What improvements would you suggest?** (`improvement_suggestions`, `text`, optional): Describe your top 1-3 improvement ideas.
* **What features are missing from the pipeline?** (`missing_features`, `text`, optional): Describe the features you feel are missing from the pipeline.
* **Would additional training be helpful?** (`training_needed`, `select_one`, optional): Select how helpful you think additional training would be:
	+ Yes, definitely
	+ Yes, somewhat
	+ No, not needed
	+ Not sure
* **How well does the pipeline support team collaboration?** (`team_collaboration`, `select_one`, optional): Rate the pipeline's support for team collaboration on a scale of 1-5:
	+ 1 - Very Poor
	+ 2 - Poor
	+ 3 - Acceptable
	+ 4 - Good
	+ 5 - Excellent
* **Would you recommend this pipeline to other teams?** (`recommend_pipeline`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether you would recommend the pipeline:
	+ Definitely
	+ Probably
	+ Maybe
	+ Probably not
	+ Definitely not
* **Additional Comments or Feedback** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Any other thoughts about our CI/CD process?
