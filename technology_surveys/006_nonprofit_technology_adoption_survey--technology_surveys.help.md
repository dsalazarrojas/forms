# nonprofit_technology_adoption_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The nonprofit_technology_adoption_survey form is designed to gather information on nonprofit organizations' technology adoption levels to improve operational efficiency and service delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Form Title" field with a brief description of your technology adoption survey.
2. Select the tools you currently use (e.g., Microsoft Office, Google Workspace, Other).
3. List any challenges you face in your technology adoption.
4. Choose the level of training you require for staff.
5. (Optional) Enter your submission management system details, such as how you manage form submissions.
6. Enter your email address (if you wish to receive follow-up emails).
7. Enter your phone number (if you wish to receive follow-up calls).
8. Add any additional notes about your technology adoption.
9. Enter the date and time you complete the form.
10. Enter the time it takes you to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title (form_title, text, required)**: A brief description of your technology adoption survey.
* **Tool Used (tool_used, select_multiple, required)**: Select the tools you currently use for your work (e.g., Microsoft Office, Google Workspace, Other).
* **Challenges (challenges, text, required)**: List any obstacles you face in adopting and using technology.
* **Training Needs (training_needs, select_one, required)**: Choose the level of training your staff requires (e.g., Basic, Intermediate, Advanced, None).
* **Submission Management (submission_management, text, optional)**: Enter details about your form submission management (e.g., use the no-code form builder, drag-and-drop interface).
* **Email (email, email, optional)**: Enter your email address if you wish to receive follow-up emails.
* **Phone (phone, text, optional)**: Enter your phone number if you wish to receive follow-up calls.
* **Notes (notes, note, optional)**: Add any additional comments or notes about your technology adoption.
* **Form Completion (form_completion, date, optional)**: Enter the date you complete the form.
* **Form Completion Time (form_completion_time, time, optional)**: Enter the time it takes you to complete the form.
