# Pet Tech App Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather your experience and feedback about the Pet Tech App. The survey will help us understand how you use the app, what you like or dislike about it, and what features you would like to see in the future. Please answer the questions as honestly as possible, and select all that apply to provide a complete picture of your experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. For each question, select the answer that best describes your experience.
2. If a question is not applicable to you, you can select "None" or leave the field blank.
3. Take your time and answer each question carefully to ensure you provide the best possible feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: App Usage
- **How Often Do You Use The App?** (`<title>`, `number`, optional):
  * This question is designed to understand how frequently you use the app.
  * Please select a number from the scale to indicate how often you use the app (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly).
  
### Page 1: App Usage
- **How Satisfied Are You With The App's Performance?** (`<satisfaction_level>`, `number`, optional):
  * This question is designed to gauge your satisfaction level with the app's performance.
  * Please select a number from the scale to indicate your level of satisfaction.

### Page 1: App Usage
- **Have You Encountered Any Issues While Using The App?** (`<issues_encountered>`, `select_multiple`, optional):
  * This question is designed to identify any issues you have encountered while using the app.
  * Select all that apply from the list:
    * None
    * Bug in the app
    * Feature not working as expected
    * Slow performance
    * Other

### Page 2: Future Features
- **What Features Would You Like To See In The Future?** (`<feature_requests>`, `select_one`, optional):
  * This question is designed to gather your ideas for future app features.
  * Select one or more from the list:
    * None
    * Integration with calendar apps
    * Integration with other pet-related apps
    * Personalized experience
    * Other

### Page 2: Feedback
- **Why Do You Feel About The App?** (`<satisfaction_why>`, `note`, optional):
  * This question is designed to gather your thoughts and reasons for your satisfaction level.
  * Please provide a brief explanation or note about your satisfaction level.

### Page 2: Contact
- **What Is Your Preferred Contact Method?** (`<contact_method>`, `select_one`, optional):
  * This question is designed to gather your preferred method of contact.
  * Select one:
    * Phone
    * Email
    * Chat
    * None
