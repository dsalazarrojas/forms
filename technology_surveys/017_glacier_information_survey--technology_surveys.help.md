# Glacier Information Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about glaciers, which is crucial for research and monitoring purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct options for each field based on your knowledge of the glacier.
2. Enter your responses in the text fields, such as "Glacier Information", "Conditions", and "Comments".
3. For fields with multiple options, select the most accurate choice from the list.
4. Ensure all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Glacier Information** (`glacier_information_survey_form_page`, text, required: false): This field is used to enter any additional information about the glacier, such as its name, location, or unique features.
* **Conditions** (`conditions_page`, text, required: false): Describe any specific conditions that might affect the glacier, such as weather patterns or environmental factors.
* **Features** (`features_page`, text, required: false): Enter any notable features of the glacier, such as its size, shape, or notable events.
* **Glacier Condition** (`conditions_1`, text, required: false): Specify the current state of the glacier, such as its size, shape, or any changes observed.
* **Features** (`features_1`, text, required: false): Enter any notable features of the glacier, such as its size, shape, or notable events.
* **Length** (`glacial_length`, number, required: false): Enter the length of the glacier in meters or kilometers.
* **Width** (`glacial_width`, number, required: false): Enter the width of the glacier in meters or kilometers.
* **Height** (`glacial_height`, number, required: false): Enter the height of the glacier in meters or kilometers.
* **Depth** (`glacial_depth`, number, required: false): Enter the depth of the glacier in meters or kilometers.
* **Surface Area** (`glacial_surface_area`, number, required: false): Enter the surface area of the glacier in square meters or square kilometers.
* **Volume** (`glacial_volume`, number, required: false): Enter the volume of the glacier in cubic meters or cubic kilometers.
* **Aspect Ratio** (`glacial_aspect_ratio`, number, required: false): Enter the aspect ratio of the glacier, which is the ratio of its width to its height.
* **Slope** (`glacial_slope`, select_one, required: false): Select the slope of the glacier from the available options: 5, 10, 20, 25, 30, 40, or 50.
* **Orientation** (`glacial_orientation`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the orientation of the glacier from the available options: North, South, East, West, or Flat.
* **Color** (`glacial_color`, select_one, required: false): Select the color of the glacier from the available options: Blue, White, Brown, Black, or Green.
* **Terminus** (`glacial_terminus`, select_one, required: false): Select whether the glacier has a terminus or not from the available options: True, False, Unknown, or None.
* **Comments** (`glacial_comments`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes about the glacier.
* **Researcher Information** (`researcher_information`, note, required: false): Enter any information about the researcher who collected this data or their affiliation.
