# Global Human Rights Awareness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This survey aims to measure your level of awareness and understanding on various aspects of human rights, including definition, impact, and consequences of human rights violations.

## How to Complete This Form

Please answer all the questions based on your honest opinion and understanding.

1. Read each question carefully and choose the relevant option that best describes your view.
2. Fill out every question, even if you are unsure. Your answer will help improve our understanding of human rights awareness globally.
3. Select all applicable options for multiple-choice questions, if applicable.
