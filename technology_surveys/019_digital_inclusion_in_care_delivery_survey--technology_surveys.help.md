# Digital Inclusion In Care Delivery Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to understand the experiences and challenges of clinicians when using technology in care delivery settings. Your responses will help to identify areas where technology can be improved to better support clinicians in their work.

## How To Complete This Form
This form is divided into 7 sections, each with a specific question or field. Please answer each question to the best of your ability, and make sure to follow the instructions for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Digital Inclusion In Care Delivery Survey** (`survey_title`, `note`, required): This section is the title of the survey, and it should not be changed. Please do not enter any text here.
* **Device Access in the Hospital** (`clinician_device_access_hospital`, select_one, required): Please select how often you access devices while providing care in the hospital:
	+ Very often
	+ Somewhat often
	+ Rarely
	+ Never
* **Device Access in the Home** (`clinician_device_access_home`, select_one, required): Please select how often you access devices while providing care in the home:
	+ Very often
	+ Somewhat often
	+ Rarely
	+ Never
* **Comfort with Technology** (`clinician_comfort_with_technology`, select_one, required): Please select how comfortable you feel when using technology:
	+ Very comfortable
	+ Somewhat comfortable
	+ Not very comfortable
	+ Not at all comfortable
* **Barriers to Technology Use** (`clinician_barriers_1`, select_one, required): Please select the main barrier you face when using technology:
	+ Technical difficulties
	+ Lack of training
	+ Fear of data security risks
	+ Other
* **Other Barriers** (`clinician_other_barriers_1`, text, optional): If you selected "Other" in the previous question, please provide a brief explanation of any other barriers you face when using technology.
* **Comments** (`clinician_comments`, text, optional): Please provide any comments or additional information about your experience with technology in care delivery settings.

## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about any question, you can always ask for clarification or guidance.
* Make sure to follow the instructions for each field to ensure accurate and complete responses.
