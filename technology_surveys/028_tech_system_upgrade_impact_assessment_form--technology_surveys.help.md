# Tech System Upgrade Impact Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tech System Upgrade Impact Assessment Form is designed to collect data about the potential effects and implications of a tech system upgrade. This form should be filled out by users who are directly involved in or affected by the upgrade. The information gathered will help stakeholders assess and prepare for the upgrade.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out each field as instructed below, providing as much detail as possible for each section.
2.  Ensure that all required fields (marked as "required" in the form) are filled out.
3.  If you are unsure about any field or its purpose, contact the assigned team member for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Expected Impact
* **Expected Impact** (text, optional): Describe the expected impact of the tech system upgrade on your work or business operations. This field is not required but provides valuable information for stakeholders to assess the upgrade's effects.

### Business Risks
* **Business Risks** (text, optional): List potential business risks or challenges that the tech system upgrade might pose. This field helps identify and plan for any potential business-related obstacles.

### Technical Risks
* **Technical Risks** (text, optional): Describe any technical risks or challenges associated with the upgrade, such as data migration, compatibility issues, or other technical considerations.

### Readiness
* **Readiness** (text, required): Provide information on any pre-implementation preparations necessary for a smooth upgrade process. This includes any steps or actions that need to be taken before the upgrade is executed.

### Technical Readiness
* **Technical Readiness** (text, required): Detail any technical preparations or measures that should be taken before the upgrade. This could include hardware, software, or system configuration adjustments.

### Business Readiness
* **Business Readiness** (text, optional): Describe any business process or workflow adjustments that should be made to accommodate the upgrade, ensuring that all affected teams are informed and prepared.

### Risks
* **Risks** (select multiple, required): Select all applicable risks that you believe the tech system upgrade might pose. This helps in identifying and prioritizing potential issues.

### Date
* **Date** (date, optional): If you're aware of a specific date for the upgrade, please provide it in the format MM/DD/YYYY. This helps in planning and scheduling for the upgrade.

### Time
* **Time** (time, optional): If you know the specific time for the upgrade, please provide it in the format HH:MM AM/PM. This aids in scheduling the upgrade and any related preparations.

### Email
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your contact email address. This is crucial for any further communication regarding the upgrade.

### Phone
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number. This ensures you can be contacted for any last-minute questions or urgent matters.

### Comments
* **Comments** (note, optional): Share any additional comments or concerns you have about the upgrade. This field is for personal notes and doesn't affect the upgrade's success, but provides valuable insights to the assigned team.

### Assigned
* **Assigned** (select one, required): Choose who is assigned to lead or handle the upgrade process. This ensures that the correct team member is informed and in charge of the upgrade's progress.

### Tech System Upgrade Form Comments
* **Tech System Upgrade Form Comments** (note, optional): This is another field for any additional comments or information you'd like to share about the upgrade. Like the previous comments field, this does not affect the upgrade's success but provides valuable background information.

Please fill out the form accurately, and if you're unsure about any field or its purpose, do not hesitate to contact the assigned team member for guidance.
