## Purpose
The Keyword Research Survey is designed to gather information about your keyword research process. This form will help us understand how you currently perform keyword research and what tools you use to achieve your goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions truthfully and thoroughly, as this will help us better understand your current behavior and provide more accurate recommendations.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by the required label).
3. If you're using a tool that's not listed in the "Tools Used" dropdown, you can select "Other" and provide the tool name in the "Tools Used Other" field.
4. For the "Frequency Goals" and "Frequency Challenges" questions, please respond with a simple numerical value (e.g., "daily", "weekly", etc.).
5. For the "Habits" field, respond with a brief description of your current behavior.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Keyword Research Form** (`keyword_research_form`): This is the main field for describing your current keyword research process. Please provide a short description of how you currently perform keyword research.
- **Tools Used** (`tools_used`): Select one or more tools you use for keyword research, such as Google Search, Keyword Clustering, SEMrush, or Ahrefs. If you're using a different tool, choose "Other" and provide the tool name in the "Tools Used Other" field.
  - **Tools Used Other** (`tools_used_other`): This field is for providing the name of a tool you're using that's not listed in the "Tools Used" dropdown.
- **Tools Used Why** (`tools_used_why`): This field is not currently used in the form, but it's kept for future development purposes.
- **Frequency Goals** (`frequency_goals`): Respond with a numerical value indicating how frequently you perform keyword research, such as "daily", "weekly", etc.
- **Frequency Goals Frequency** (`frequency_goals_frequency`): This field is not currently used in the form, but it's kept for future development purposes.
- **Challenges** (`challenges`): Describe any difficulties or obstacles you face during your keyword research process.
- **Challenges Frequency** (`challenges_frequency`): Respond with a numerical value indicating how frequently you face challenges in your keyword research process.
- **Habits** (`habits`): Describe your current keyword research habits or routines.
- **Habits Frequency** (`habits_frequency`): Respond with a numerical value indicating how frequently you perform your keyword research habits.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when answering the questions.
* If you're unsure about a question or need clarification, please contact the project administrator.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey! Your feedback is greatly appreciated.
