# Cybersecurity Analyst Exit Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Cybersecurity Analyst Exit Survey is designed to gather information from departing employees on their experience with the company's security tools, processes, and overall work environment.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please answer the questions honestly to provide valuable feedback.
2.  If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the comments section.
3.  Fill in your contact information to help us contact you if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **How did you feel about our security tools** (`security_analyst_survey_title`, Text, Optional)
    This question asks about your overall satisfaction with the security tools provided by the company.
*   **How would you rate the overall tools provided** (`tools_rating`, Select One, Optional)
    This question asks you to rate the overall quality of the security tools provided by the company. The options are Excellent, Good, Fair, and Poor.
*   **How would you rate our security processes** (`processes_rating`, Select One, Optional)
    This question asks you to rate the quality of the security processes provided by the company. The options are Strongly Agree, Somewhat Agree, Neutral, Somewhat Disagree, and Strongly Disagree.
*   **How would you rate your overall work environment** (`work_environment_rating`, Select One, Optional)
    This question asks you to rate the overall work environment provided by the company. The options are Excellent, Good, Fair, and Poor.
*   **What were the primary reasons for your resignation** (`reasons_for_leaving`, Text, Optional)
    This question asks you to provide the main reasons for your resignation.
*   **What would make you more likely to stay** (`retention_recommendations`, Text, Optional)
    This question asks for any suggestions on what the company could do to make you more likely to stay.
*   **Do you have any additional comments or feedback** (`comments`, Text, Optional)
    This question is for any additional comments or feedback you may have.
*   **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional)
    Please enter your email address so that we can contact you if needed.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Optional)
    Please enter your phone number so that we can contact you if needed.
*   **Job Title** (`job_title`, Text, Optional)
    This question asks for your job title.
*   **Department** (`department`, Text, Optional)
    This question asks for your department.
*   **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, Text, Optional)
    This question asks for your manager's name.
*   **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, Email, Optional)
    Please enter your manager's email address.
*   **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, Text, Optional)
    Please enter your manager's phone number.
*   **How many members are on the IT team** (`it_team`, Number, Optional)
    This question asks for the number of members on the IT team.
*   **What is the size of the IT team** (`it_team_size`, Number, Optional)
    This question asks for the size of the IT team.
*   **What is your manager's title** (`manager_title`, Text, Optional)
    This question asks for your manager's title.
*   **Job Description** (`job_description`, Text, Optional)
    This question asks for your job description.
*   **Job Category** (`job_category`, Text, Optional)
    This question asks for your job category.
*   **Job Function** (`job_function`, Text, Optional)
    This question asks for your job function.
*   **How many years have you worked with the company** (`years_worked`, Number, Optional)
    This question asks for the number of years you have worked with the company.
*   **How many months have you worked with the company** (`months_worked`, Number, Optional)
    This question asks for the number of months you have worked with the company.
*   **How many weeks have you worked with the company** (`weeks_worked`, Number, Optional)
    This question asks for the number of weeks you have worked with the company.
*   **What was your salary range** (`salary_range`, Number, Optional)
    This question asks for your salary range.
*   **What was your overall experience with the company** (`overall_experience`, Text, Optional)
    This question asks for your overall experience with the company.
