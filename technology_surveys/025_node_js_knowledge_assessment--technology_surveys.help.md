# node-js-knowledge-assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your knowledge and experience with Node.js. It will help us understand your goals, experience level, and proficiency with the technology. Your input will guide our support and training efforts to better serve you and our community.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Read each field's description carefully and select the most accurate option or fill in the requested information.
2.  Answer the questions honestly to ensure accurate assessment of your Node.js knowledge.
3.  Use the provided fields to describe your projects, experience, and skills in detail.
4.  If you're unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor or contact us for guidance.
