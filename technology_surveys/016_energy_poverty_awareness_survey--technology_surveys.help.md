# Energy Poverty Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather data on energy poverty awareness among households, focusing on income, energy consumption patterns, and energy saving incentives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer all questions thoroughly and accurately.
2. Ensure you are filling out the form on behalf of your household, not as an individual.
3. If you're unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.
