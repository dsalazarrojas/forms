# internal-systems-feedback-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from internal users on our internal systems and tools. This feedback will help us identify areas for improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance our internal systems.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Internal Systems Feedback Survey** (`survey_title`, text, optional): This is the title of your feedback message. Feel free to provide a brief description of the topic you'd like to discuss.
  - Please keep it concise and clear, as it will help us understand the context of your feedback.
- **Tool Name** (`tool_name`, text, optional): If your feedback is related to a specific tool, please mention its name so we can direct your feedback to the correct area.
  - This will help us to focus our improvement efforts on the relevant tool.
- **Feedback Question** (`feedback_question`, text, optional): If you have a specific question or concern, please describe it in this field.
  - This is an opportunity to ask clarifying questions or seek help on a particular aspect of our internal systems.
- **Feedback Answer** (`feedback_answer`, text, optional): If you have a response or resolution to the feedback question, please provide it here.
  - This is a chance to offer solutions, ideas, or additional information that might help us understand the issue better.
- **Suggestion** (`suggestion`, text, optional): If you have a suggestion or idea for improvement, please share it with us.
  - Your input is invaluable in helping us enhance our internal systems and tools.

## Tips
- Please be as clear and concise as possible in your responses.
- If you have already provided feedback on this topic, do not submit duplicate feedback.
- If you have any questions or need help, please don't hesitate to reach out to us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
