# Educational Technology Access For Rural Students Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about the educational technology access of rural students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, follow the instructions below:

1. Carefully read each question and answer it truthfully based on your experience.
2. Choose the best possible option for each question from the provided options.
3. Make sure to answer all questions, as some are marked as required.
4. If you are unsure of any question, choose "Don't know" or "None" as an option.
5. Submit your responses at the end of the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
