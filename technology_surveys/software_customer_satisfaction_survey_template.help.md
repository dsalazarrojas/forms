# software_customer_satisfaction_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The software customer satisfaction survey aims to gather feedback on your experience with our software. This survey helps us understand what works well and what needs improvement, allowing us to provide better products and services in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer all questions to the best of your abilities.
2. Choose the most appropriate answer for each question.
3. Provide detailed and specific suggestions for improvement in the "What specific improvements would you suggest?" text field.
4. If you have trouble finding what you are looking for, use the "Help" button to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How satisfied are you with our software overall?** (Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied)
	+ This question helps us understand your overall experience with our software.
* **How would you rate the software's ease of use?** (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor)
	+ This question gauges your experience with the software's usability.
* **Which features do you find most valuable?** (Select all that apply: Core functions, Reporting, Integrations, Customization, Support resources)
	+ Choose all the features you have used and value the most.
* **How would you rate the responsiveness of our customer support?** (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Not applicable)
	+ This question assesses the effectiveness of our support services.
* **What specific improvements would you suggest for the software?** (Text)
	+ Provide detailed and specific suggestions to help us improve the software.
* **How likely are you to recommend our software to others?** (Very likely, Likely, Neutral, Unlikely, Very unlikely)
	+ This question helps us understand our software's reputation and potential for growth.

## Tips
- Be honest and provide constructive feedback to help us improve our software.
- Use the "What specific improvements would you suggest?" field to share your thoughts on areas for improvement.
- If you have any trouble or questions, use the "Help" button to contact us.
