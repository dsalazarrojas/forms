# VoIP Security Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to help organizations assess the security posture of their Voice over Internet Protocol (VoIP) systems. It's meant to be a starting point for identifying potential vulnerabilities and weaknesses in your VoIP systems.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the VoIP systems used in your organization.
2. Identify the existing protections in place.
3. Report any recent incidents or issues experienced with your VoIP systems.
4. Specify any concerns you have about your VoIP systems.
5. List any current protections used to secure your VoIP systems.
6. Plan improvements to strengthen your VoIP systems' security.
7. Be as accurate as possible when filling out this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Which VoIP systems are used
* **Which VoIP systems are used** (`voip_systems_used`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field asks you to select all the VoIP systems used in your organization. This can include VoIP servers, Voice-activated phone systems, or any other type of VoIP system.
### 2. What existing protections exist
* **What existing protections exist** (`protection_in_place`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field asks you to choose one option that describes the protections currently in place to secure your VoIP systems. This can be firewalls, routers, or other protections.
### 3. Recent incidents or issues experienced
* **Recent incidents or issues experienced** (`recent_incidents`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field allows you to report any recent incidents or issues experienced with your VoIP systems. This can include network connection issues, security concerns, or other issues.
### 4. What concerns do you have
* **What concerns do you have** (`concerns`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field asks you to specify any concerns you have about your VoIP systems. This can include security, compliance, or other concerns.
### 5. Current protections
* **Current protections** (`current_protections`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field lists the current protections used to secure your VoIP systems. This can include firewalls, intrusion detection systems, or other protections.
### 6. Plan improvements
* **Plan improvements** (`planning_improvements`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field allows you to plan improvements to strengthen your VoIP systems' security. This can include network segmentation, regular security audits, or other improvements.

## Tips

* Be as accurate as possible when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any of the options, please provide a clear and detailed explanation in the "Other" field.
* This form is not intended to be a comprehensive security assessment, but rather a starting point for organizations to gauge their current security posture and identify areas for improvement.
