# Social Media Ethics Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Social Media Ethics Survey is a form designed to gather information on users' attitudes, behavior, and experiences with social media. Your responses will help researchers understand social media ethics and user perspectives.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and choose the most accurate response.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select the answer that best reflects your experience or attitude.
3. For questions that require a numerical response, enter your age in the format specified.
4. Answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
5. Ensure that you have answered all required questions before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: A brief explanation of the purpose and objectives of this survey.
* **Survey**: Please answer this question based on your experiences with social media. Choose 'Yes' if you have experienced any of the following: 
  + Yes: I have experienced social media-related issues or concerns
  + No: I have not experienced social media-related issues or concerns
* **Demographics**: Please select 'Yes' if you are:
  + Yes: A current student
  + No: Not a current student
* **Age**: Enter your age in the format "XX", e.g., 25 for 25 years old.
* **Education**: Please select 'Yes' if you have a degree in a field related to social media or technology.
  + Yes: I have a degree in a field related to social media or technology
  + No: I do not have a degree in a field related to social media or technology
* **Social Media Platforms**: Please answer this question based on your experiences with social media. Choose 'Yes' if you have experienced any of the following: 
  + Yes: I use multiple social media platforms
  + Yes: I use a specific social media platform (e.g., Facebook, Twitter, Instagram)
  + No: I do not use social media platforms
* **Attitudes**: Answer this question based on your feelings about social media.
  + Yes: I have positive feelings about social media
  + No: I have negative feelings about social media
* **Ethical Concerns**: Please answer this question based on your experiences with social media. Choose 'Yes' if you have experienced any of the following: 
  + Yes: I have experienced online harassment
  + Yes: I have experienced online hate speech
  + No: I have not experienced online harassment or hate speech
* **Behavior**: Please select 'Yes' if you have engaged in any of the following online behaviors:
  + Yes: I have posted content that is not true
  + Yes: I have engaged in online arguments or conflicts
  + No: I have not engaged in online arguments or conflicts
* **Review**: Please provide a brief review of your experiences with this form.
* **Submit**: Please confirm that you have completed the form accurately and honestly.
