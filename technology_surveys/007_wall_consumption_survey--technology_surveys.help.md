# Wall Consumption Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your experiences with walls, specifically your material preferences, usage volume, and purchasing channels.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the type(s) of wall material(s) you have used in the past (e.g., Brick, Concrete Block, Glass, Stone, Wood, Metal).
2. If you have used a specific volume of wall material, please enter the corresponding quantity in the "Usage Volume" field.
3. Select all the channels through which you have purchased wall materials in the past (e.g., Online, Offline, Mobile App, Social Media).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Wall Material Types** (`wall_material_types`, `select_one`, optional): Please choose the type(s) of wall material you have used in the past.
* **Usage Volume** (`usage_volume`, `number`, optional): If applicable, enter the quantity of wall material you have used. This will help us understand your consumption patterns.
* **Purchasing Channels** (`purchasing_channels`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the channels through which you have purchased wall materials in the past, such as online retailers, local stores, or through a mobile app.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, `note`, optional): This field is for any additional comments or notes you have about your experiences with walls or this form. Please use this space to provide any further information that may be helpful to us.
