# Inhibition Kinetics Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Inhibition Kinetics Survey is designed to gather information about inhibition kinetics experiments. This form is meant to collect data for analysis and comparison across different experiments.

## How To Complete This Form

1. The form is divided into several sections, each with a specific set of questions. Please complete each section thoroughly and accurately.
2. You can fill out the form online or print it out for manual submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Experiment Title** (`experiment_title`, text, required): Please enter a brief description of the experiment, including the title, date, and any other relevant details.
* **Enzyme Name** (`enzyme_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the enzyme being studied, including the species and any other relevant details.
* **Inhibitor Concentration** (`inhibitor_concentration`, number, required): Enter the concentration of the inhibitor used in the experiment.
* **Substrate Concentration** (`substrate_concentration`, number, required): Enter the concentration of the substrate used in the experiment.
* **Temperature** (`temperature`, number, required): Enter the temperature at which the experiment was conducted.
* **Reaction Time** (`reaction_time`, number, required): Enter the duration of the experiment.
* **Kinetic Parameters** (`kinetic_parameters`, select multiple, required): Select one or more of the following kinetic parameters: AUC, kcat, kobs, Ki, kobs/ki.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the experiment, including any issues or concerns.

Note: The form fields are labeled with their corresponding field names, which should be referenced in the explanations above.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter all required fields to ensure that your data is complete and accurate.
* Double-check your entries to ensure that they are correct and consistent.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact the administrator.
* This form is for internal use only and the data collected will be used for research and development purposes.
