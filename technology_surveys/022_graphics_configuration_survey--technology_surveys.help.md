# graphics_configuration_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The graphics configuration survey is designed to collect feedback on your graphics settings, display preferences, and overall satisfaction with our product. This will help us improve and tailor our graphics configuration options to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*) or a red asterisk.
2. Select your answer from the dropdown menus for questions that require a selection.
3. Enter your thoughts and comments in the text fields as needed.
4. Submit your feedback when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (Text)
	+ This field is for general information, such as your name and any other relevant details you'd like to share.
* **Graphics Settings** (Text)
	+ Describe your current graphics settings and any issues you're experiencing.
* **Display Preferences** (Text)
	+ Tell us about your display preferences, such as resolution and refresh rate.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (Text)
	+ Share your overall satisfaction with our product and provide any comments or suggestions.
* **GPU Model** (Select One)
	+ Select your graphics processing unit (GPU) model from the list: AMD Radeon, NVIDIA GeForce, or Other.
* **GPU Usage Level** (Select Multiple)
	+ Choose the level of GPU usage you experience with our product: Low, Medium, High.
* **Display Resolutions** (Select Multiple)
	+ Select your preferred display resolution: 1080p, 1440p, 2160p.
* **Display Refresh Rate** (Select Multiple)
	+ Choose your preferred display refresh rate: 30, 60, 120.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (Select One)
	+ Rate your overall satisfaction with our product: Very Dissatisfied, Neutral, Very Satisfied.
* **Comments** (Text)
	+ Share any comments or feedback you have about our product.
* **Email** (Email)
	+ Enter your email address for us to contact you (if needed).
* **Phone** (Text)
	+ Provide your phone number (if you'd like to be contacted).
* **User Comments** (Text)
	+ Share any additional comments or feedback about our product.
* **Comments on GPU usage** (Text)
	+ Provide any comments or suggestions about GPU usage.
* **Comments on Display Needs** (Text)
	+ Share any comments or feedback about display needs or settings.
* **Comments on Overall Satisfaction** (Text)
	+ Share any comments or feedback about overall satisfaction.
* **Other Comments** (Text)
	+ Add any other comments or suggestions for improvement.
