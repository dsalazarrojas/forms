# Cloud Backup Configuration Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to collect information about your current cloud backup configuration. This will help us identify potential candidates for a backup solution upgrade and inform our product development and sales teams about your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each section and field as instructed. Make sure to answer each question carefully, as your input is crucial for our understanding of your current backup situation. If you need help or clarification, don't hesitate to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your business card or ID.
* **Organization Name**: Enter the name of your organization, as it is registered in our system.
* **Email Address**: Enter your official email address associated with your organization.
* **Department - Team**: Enter the name of your department or team that handles backup configurations.
* **Current backup solution provider**: Enter the name of your current backup solution provider.
* **Years using current solution**: Enter the number of years you have been using your current backup solution.
* **Approximate data volume backed up - TB**: Enter the approximate amount of data you are currently backing up.
* **Backup frequency**: Select how often you back up your data:
	+ Hourly
	+ Daily
	+ Multiple times daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Custom
* **Recovery Time Objective - RTO**: Select your maximum acceptable downtime for backup recovery:
	+ Minutes
	+ Hours
	+ Less than 1 day
	+ 1-7 days
	+ No specific requirement
* **Recovery Point Objective - RPO**: Select your maximum acceptable data loss for backup recovery:
	+ Real-time
	+ Under 1 hour
	+ 1-24 hours
	+ 1-7 days
	+ No specific requirement
* **Overall satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with your current backup solution from 1 (very unsatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).
* **Reliability and uptime rating**: Select your satisfaction with your current backup solution's reliability and uptime:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Poor
	+ Very poor
* **Ease of use and management**: Select your satisfaction with the ease of use and management of your current backup solution:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Poor
	+ Very poor
* **Quality of vendor support**: Select your satisfaction with the quality of support from your current backup solution vendor:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Poor
	+ Very poor
* **Main challenges with current backup**: Select all that apply:
	+ Cost
	+ Complexity
	+ Performance
	+ Scalability
	+ Support
	+ Compliance
	+ Recovery speed
	+ None
* **Describe main challenges in detail**: Enter a brief description of your main challenges with your current backup solution.
* **Considering backup solution upgrade?**: Select whether you are considering upgrading your current backup solution:
	+ Yes, definitely
	+ Maybe
	+ No
	+ Undecided
* **Upgrade timeline**: If you answered "Yes" to the previous question, select a timeline for your potential upgrade:
	+ Next 3 months
	+ 3-6 months
	+ 6-12 months
	+ 12+ months
	+ No specific timeline
* **Most important features for new solution**: Enter the features that are most important to you in a new backup solution.
* **Tips**:
	+ Be honest and accurate in your answers, as this will help us provide better services to you.
	+ If you need help or clarification, don't hesitate to ask.
