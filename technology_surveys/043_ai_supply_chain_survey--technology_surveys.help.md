# AI Supply Chain Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather insights on the adoption and impact of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in supply chain management. The goal is to understand the current state of AI use in supply chains, the challenges faced, and the expected benefits. Your participation will help us better understand the trends and opportunities in the field.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Click on each field to start typing or select an option.
2. Fill out all required fields (marked as "required: true" in the form).
3. For fields marked as "select_multiple", select all that apply from the list.
4. You can leave optional fields blank if you prefer, but please be aware that they might impact the quality of the survey data.
5. Click submit to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent name** (`respondent_name`, text, required/optional): 
Please fill in your name as it appears on your business card or your name as you would like it to be displayed in the survey results.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required): 
This field is for your company or unit name. Please provide the official name of your organization.
* **Role in supply chain** (`role`, text, required): 
Please describe your role in the supply chain, e.g., Procurement, Logistics.
* **AI use cases in supply chain** (`ai_use_cases`, select_multiple, required): 
Select all the AI use cases that apply to your organization:
	+ Demand forecasting
	+ Inventory optimization
	+ Route planning
	+ Supplier risk scoring
	+ Quality inspection
* **Adoption stage of AI in supply chain** (`adoption_stage`, select_one, required): 
Choose the current stage of AI adoption in your supply chain:
	+ Experimentation
	+ Early adoption
	+ Operational
	+ Scaling
* **Expected benefits from AI** (`expected_benefits`, select_multiple, required): 
Select all the expected benefits from AI adoption in your supply chain:
	+ Cost reduction
	+ Improved service levels
	+ Faster decision making
	+ Reduced stockouts
* **Estimated ROI timeframe** (`estimated_roi`, select_one, optional): 
Select the estimated timeframe for the ROI from AI adoption in your supply chain:
	+ Under 6 months
	+ 6 to 12 months
	+ 12 to 24 months
	+ Over 24 months
* **Key challenges to adoption** (`key_challenges`, select_multiple, optional): 
Select all the key challenges faced during AI adoption in your supply chain:
	+ Data quality
	+ Integration complexity
	+ Change management
	+ Vendor maturity
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): 
Leave any additional comments or insights you'd like to share about AI adoption in your supply chain.

## Tips
- Please answer honestly and to the best of your knowledge.
- This form is designed to collect general trends, so your individual responses will not be shared publicly.
