# Software Update Effectiveness Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Software Update Effectiveness Audit Form is designed to gather information about the success or failure of a software update. This form helps identify areas where the update process can be improved and provides valuable insights for future updates. It should only be used to report on one software update per form submission.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the User Name field.
2. Enter the version of the software that was updated.
3. Enter the date the update was released (YYYY-MM-DD format).
4. Provide a feedback score between 1 and 5 in the Feedback Score field.
5. Describe any issues encountered in the Issues Encountered field.
6. Choose the outcome of the update from the dropdown menu in the Update Outcome field.
7. If any issues were encountered, describe them in detail in the Issues Detailed field. This is optional.
8. Identify the department affected by the issues (e.g., IT, Operations, Sales, Marketing, Engineering) in the Issues Department field. This is optional, but please select all that apply.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (text, required): Enter your name to associate your submission with your identity.
* **Software Version** (text, optional): Enter the version of the software that was updated.
* **Release Date** (date, required): Enter the date the software update was released in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Feedback Score** (number, required): Provide a score between 1 and 5 to rate the effectiveness of the update. A score of 1 means the update was completely unsuccessful, while a score of 5 means it was very successful.
* **Issues Encountered** (text, required): Describe any issues that arose during or after the update.
* **Update Outcome** (select one, required): Choose the outcome of the update from the dropdown menu:
	+ Success
	+ Partial Success
	+ No Change
	+ Failure
	+ Unknown
* **Issues Detailed** (note, optional): If you encountered any issues, provide a detailed description in this field.
* **Issues Department** (select multiple, optional): If any issues were encountered, identify the department(s) affected (e.g., IT, Operations, Sales, Marketing, Engineering).
