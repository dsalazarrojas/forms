# ESG Consulting Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This help guide will walk you through the ESG Consulting Awareness Survey form, which is designed to gather information about your company's or organization's awareness and understanding of Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) issues.

## How To Complete This Form

The form consists of several questions that will guide you in providing the necessary information. Here's a step-by-step guide to help you complete the form:

1. Read through each question carefully and answer the ones that apply to your experience.
2. For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose the options that best match your situation.
3. Provide a brief explanation for text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. esg_survey (Select One)**: This question asks if you are familiar with ESG principles. Choose "Yes" if you have a good understanding of ESG issues, and "No" if you are not sure or need more information.
* **2. esg_topics (Select Multiple)**: This question asks about the ESG topics you have addressed or are familiar with. Choose all the topics that apply to your experience.
* **3. esg_challenges (Select One)**: This question asks about the ESG challenges you face or are familiar with. Choose "Yes" if you face these challenges, and "No" if you don't.
* **4. esg_familiarity_level (Text)**: Provide a brief explanation of your level of familiarity with ESG principles.
* **5. esg_sustainability (Select One)**: This question asks about your familiarity with ESG sustainability issues. Choose "Yes" if you are familiar with these issues, and "No" if you are not.
* **6. esg_environment (Select One)**: This question asks about your familiarity with ESG environmental issues. Choose "Yes" if you are familiar with these issues, and "No" if you are not.
* **7. esg_financial (Select One)**: This question asks about your familiarity with ESG financial issues. Choose "Yes" if you are familiar with these issues, and "No" if you are not.
* **8. esg_governance (Select One)**: This question asks about your familiarity with ESG governance issues. Choose "Yes" if you are familiar with these issues, and "No" if you are not.
* **9. esg_intangibles (Select One)**: This question asks about your familiarity with ESG intangibles issues. Choose "Yes" if you are familiar with these issues, and "No" if you are not.
* **10. esg_social (Select One)**: This question asks about your familiarity with ESG social issues. Choose "Yes" if you are familiar with these issues, and "No" if you are not.
* **11. esg_other (Select Multiple)**: This question asks about any other ESG-related issues you are familiar with. Choose all the issues that apply.
* **12. esg_survey_comments (Text)**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about the ESG Consulting Awareness Survey.
* **13. esg_survey_recommendations (Text)**: If you have any recommendations for improving our ESG consulting services, please share them here.

## Tips

* Answer each question truthfully based on your experience and knowledge.
* If you are unsure about a question, choose the option that best matches your situation.
* Provide detailed explanations for text fields to help us understand your feedback better.
