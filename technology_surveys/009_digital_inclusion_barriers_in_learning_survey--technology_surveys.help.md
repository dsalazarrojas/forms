# Digital Inclusion Barriers In Learning Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Digital Inclusion Barriers in Learning Survey is designed to help us understand the obstacles people face when accessing digital technology for educational purposes. Your input will contribute to a more comprehensive understanding of these challenges and aid in developing inclusive solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out this form thoroughly and accurately. You can save your progress and come back later to finish it.
- Select the correct options for each field according to your experiences.
- If you're unable to answer a question, please select "No" or "Other (please specify)" for select questions, and provide a brief explanation in the text fields.
- For any field requiring a date/time input, use the date/time picker or input manually in the format 'DD/MM/YYYY'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user_survey** (select multiple): This question is about your current access to digital technologies (e.g., computers, mobile devices, internet).
* **device_access** (text): Describe your device's performance, reliability, and any issues you've faced while using it for learning.
* **internet_access** (text): Share your experiences with internet connectivity and its impact on your learning process.
* **learning_skills** (select one): Indicate if you have the necessary skills to use digital technologies for learning.
* **learning_barriers** (select multiple): Select the barriers you face when using digital technologies for learning (e.g., language, accessibility, cost).
* **institutional_support** (select one): Rate your institution's support for digital inclusion in learning.
* **device_problems** (select multiple): List any problems you've faced with your device.
* **internet_problems** (select multiple): Mention any internet-related issues you've encountered.
* **skills_problems** (select multiple): Indicate any skills you lack or find challenging to develop for digital learning.
* **institutional_barriers** (select one): Rate your institution's overall support for digital inclusion.
* **institutional_support_barriers** (select one): Identify any barriers you face with institutional support.
* **other_barriers** (text): Describe any other barriers not listed above.
* **other_solutions** (text): Share any alternative solutions you've found to overcome digital inclusion barriers.
* **comments** (note): Provide any additional comments or thoughts you have about your experiences with digital inclusion barriers.
* **submission_comments** (text): If you have any comments about this survey, please share them here.
* **surveyor_comments** (text): If you have any questions or need further clarification, please address them here.
* **date_completed** (date): Input the date you completed this survey.
* **time_completed** (time): Enter the time you completed this survey.
* **time_spent** (number): Estimate the time you spent completing this survey.
* **age** (number): Please input your age.
* **gender** (select one): Select your gender.
* **occupation** (text): Describe your occupation or current role.
* **education** (select one): Indicate your educational level.
* **institution** (text): If you're affiliated with an institution, please mention it.
* **surveyor** (text): Provide any comments or feedback about the surveyor.
