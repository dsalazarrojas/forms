# Radar Technology Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Radar Technology Assessment form is used to collect detailed information about radar technologies. This includes its name, primary application, future use, strengths, limitations, performance ratings, and benefits and risks. 

## How To Complete This Form

- Fill in the form with the relevant information about the radar technology you are assessing.
- Ensure that the form is filled out accurately and completely.
- Review the form before submission to ensure all required information is provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the name of the technology?**: Enter the name of the technology being assessed.
* **What is the primary application of the technology?**: Describe the main purpose or use of the technology.
* **How will the technology be used in the future?**: Predict how the technology will be used in the future, if at all.
* **What are the strengths of the technology?**: Select the strengths of the technology from the options provided.
* **What are the limitations of the technology?**: Select the limitations of the technology from the options provided.
* **How would you rate the performance of the technology?**: Enter a numerical rating (if applicable) to rate the performance of the technology.
* **What are the benefits of the technology?**: Enter the benefits of the technology.
* **What are the risks of the technology?**: Enter the risks of the technology.
* **What is the current level of the technology?**: Select the current level of the technology from the options provided.

## Tips

- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please refer to the guidelines above or contact your supervisor/manager for guidance.
- If you are filling out the form for a technology that is not in your area of expertise, please ensure you are providing the most accurate information possible.
- Please be thorough in providing information and avoid duplication of assessments.
