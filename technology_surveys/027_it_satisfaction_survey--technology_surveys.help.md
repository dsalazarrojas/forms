# it_satisfaction_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from individuals about their overall satisfaction with the organization's technology services. The data collected will help identify areas of improvement and provide insight into the overall sentiment towards our technology services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **first name** in the field labeled "first name".
2. Enter your **email** address in the field labeled "email".
3. Provide your **department** name in the field labeled "department".
4. Enter your **job title** in the field labeled "job title".
5. Rate your **satisfaction score** from 1 to 10 in the field labeled "satisfaction score".
6. Provide any additional **comment** in the field labeled "comment".
7. Enter your **phone number** in the field labeled "phone".
8. Enter your **company** name in the field labeled "company".
9. Enter your **location** in the field labeled "location".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first name**: Enter your first name, as this will help us understand who is providing feedback.
* **email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you if needed.
* **department**: If you have a department, please enter it here.
* **job title**: Enter your job title or position.
* **satisfaction score**: Rate your satisfaction level with our technology services, from 1 (very dissatisfied) to 10 (very satisfied).
* **comment**: Provide any additional feedback or comments you have about your experience with our technology services.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number, if you would like to be contacted directly.
* **company**: Enter the name of your company.
* **location**: Enter your location or office.

## Tips

* Please answer the questions as accurately as possible.
* You can rate your satisfaction score based on your overall experience with our technology services.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact us using the email address provided in the email field.
