# Robot Vacuum Product Comparison Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and insights from users about their experiences with different robot vacuum brands and models. We aim to understand your preferences, satisfaction, and any issues you may be experiencing with these products.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions truthfully, based on your personal experiences with your robot vacuum.
2. You can select only one option for most of the questions, but for some questions, you can select multiple options.
3. For open-ended questions, please provide detailed and concrete answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Which robot vacuum brand do you own or have used?** (`robot_vacuum_brand`): This question helps us understand which brand you are familiar with. Please select the brand that you currently own or have used in the past.
* **What feature is most important to you when choosing a robot vacuum?** (`feature_importance`): This question helps us understand your top priority feature when selecting a robot vacuum. Please select the feature that is most important to you.
* **What feature is second most important to you?** (`feature_importance_2`): This question is similar to the previous one, but this time, you can select multiple features that are equally important to you.
* **How satisfied are you with your purchase?** (`purchase_satisfaction`): This question assesses your overall satisfaction with your robot vacuum purchase.
* **Why did you purchase your robot vacuum?** (`purchase_driver`): This question helps us understand what drove your purchasing decision.
* **What is your preferred price range for a robot vacuum?** (`price_range`): This question determines your price sensitivity when it comes to robot vacuums.
* **What feature would you prefer?** (`feature_preference`): This question is similar to the first feature question, but this time, you can select only one feature that you would prefer.
* **What is your preferred navigation system for a robot vacuum?** (`preferred_navigation`): This question helps us understand your preference for navigation systems in robot vacuums.
* **What is your preferred model of robot vacuum?** (`preferred_model`): This question helps us understand your preferred model of robot vacuum.
* **How long have you been using your robot vacuum?** (`usage_time`): This question is about your overall usage experience with your robot vacuum.
* **What is your level of satisfaction with your robot vacuum?** (`satisfaction`): This question is similar to the first satisfaction question, but this time, it's about your overall satisfaction with your robot vacuum.
* **What drives your purchasing decision for a robot vacuum?** (`purchase_driver_2`): This question is about factors that drive your purchasing decision. Please note that this question is optional.
* **Additional reasons that drive your purchasing decision for a robot vacuum?** (`additional_purchasing_driver`): This question allows you to provide any additional reasons that may influence your purchasing decision.
* **Are you experiencing any issues with your current robot vacuum?** (`robot_vacuum_issues`): This question helps us understand if you are experiencing any issues with your current robot vacuum.
* **Please describe the issues you are experiencing with your current robot vacuum.** (`issues_description`): This question is for you to elaborate on any issues you are experiencing with your current robot vacuum.
