# Click Speed Configuration Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Click Speed Configuration Survey is designed to gather information about your current click speed settings, satisfaction with the current responsiveness, and your preferences for changes in click speed. Your feedback will help us improve device usability and accessibility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your primary device type from the "Primary device you are configuring" dropdown.
2. Choose your operating system from the "Operating System" dropdown.
3. Choose your current double-click speed setting from the number scale, where 1 is slowest and 10 is fastest.
4. Select how satisfied you are with the current responsiveness from the dropdown menu.
5. Choose how often you experience accidental double-clicks or missed clicks from the dropdown menu.
6. If you require specific click speed adjustments for accessibility reasons, select "True" from the dropdown menu.
7. Choose your primary use case for this device from the dropdown menu.
8. If you could change the click speed setting, select how much slower or faster you would prefer it to be from the dropdown menu.
9. If you have any specific feature requests, type them in the text box.
10. Share any additional technical observations or feedback in the text box.
11. Rate your technical expertise with device configuration from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Primary device you are configuring** (`device_category`, Select, Required): This field asks you to select the type of device you are configuring. Please choose from the dropdown menu the type of device you are using, such as a Desktop Mouse, Laptop Trackpad, or Touchscreen Device.
* **Operating System** (`operating_system`, Select, Required): This field asks you to select the operating system you are using, such as Windows, macOS, or Linux.
* **Your current double-click speed setting** (`current_click_speed`, Number, Required): This field asks you to rate your current double-click speed setting on a scale of 1 (slowest) to 10 (fastest).
* **How satisfied are you with the current responsiveness?** (`satisfaction_with_speed`, Select, Required): This field asks you to rate how satisfied you are with the current responsiveness of your device.
* **How often do you experience accidental double-clicks or missed clicks?** (`issue_frequency`, Select, Required): This field asks you to report how often you experience accidental double-clicks or missed clicks.
* **Do you require specific click speed adjustments for accessibility reasons?** (`accessibility_needs`, Select, Required): This field asks you to report whether you have specific click speed requirements for accessibility reasons.
* **Primary use case for this device** (`usage_context`, Select, Required): This field asks you to select the primary use case for your device, such as General Productivity, Competitive Gaming, or Creative Work (Design/Editing).
* **If you could change the setting, would you prefer it to be...** (`preference_adjustment`, Select, Required): This field asks you to select how much slower or faster you would prefer the click speed setting to be.
* **Are there any specific click-related features you'd like to see?** (`feature_requests`, Text, Optional): This field is for you to type any specific feature requests you have for the click speed setting.
* **Any additional technical observations or feedback?** (`technical_feedback`, Text, Optional): This field is for you to share any additional technical observations or feedback you have.
* **How would you rate your technical expertise with device configuration?** (`user_expertise`, Select, Optional): This field asks you to rate your technical expertise with device configuration.
* **Date of Evaluation** (`survey_date`, Date, Optional): This field is for you to record the date of your evaluation.
* **-- Thank you for helping us improve device usability --** (`footer_note`, Note, Optional): This is a note of appreciation for your feedback.
