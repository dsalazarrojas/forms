# Process Automation Insight Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather insights from users on their current process automation efforts, aiming to understand their pain points and potential solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
This form consists of multiple pages, each with different types of questions. Please read through each page carefully to ensure you provide accurate and comprehensive answers.
You can select multiple options for questions that allow it, and provide as much detail as possible in text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. What is the primary workflow you're trying to automate? (Required)
 * This is the main process or task you're trying to automate. Describe the workflow in a few sentences, mentioning key steps and involved parties.

### 2. What tools do you currently use for this workflow?
 * Select all tools you use for this workflow, such as software, hardware, or other resources.
 * If none, select "None".

### 3. What are you trying to achieve with this automation?
 * Briefly describe the benefits or improvements you aim to achieve with this automation.

### 4. What are some challenges you're currently facing with this workflow?
 * Describe any difficulties or obstacles you're experiencing with this process.

### 5. What are some areas that could be improved?
 * Suggest potential areas that could be optimized or enhanced in this workflow.

### 6. What would you recommend as a potential solution?
 * Suggest any solutions you've found helpful or think could be helpful for others facing similar challenges.

### 7. Additional Feedback
 * If there's anything else you'd like to share or mention, please use this field.

### 8. Your Email (Optional)
 * If you'd like to be contacted or receive updates on new features or improvements, please provide your email address.

### 9. Team Members Involved (Optional)
 * Select all team members involved in this workflow.

### 10. Team Size (Optional)
 * If applicable, enter the number of team members involved.

### 11. Status of Automation (Optional)
 * Select the status of your current automation efforts.

Tips:

* Be as concise and clear as possible in your answers.
* Select all options that apply for multiple-choice questions.
* Provide details for text fields.
* If you have any further questions or need assistance, feel free to ask.
