# photography_equipment_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "photography_equipment_survey" form is designed to collect information about your photography equipment preferences and plans. It will help us better understand your needs and preferences for photography gear, enabling us to tailor our services and recommendations to your specific requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

1. Please read the form carefully to understand what each question is asking.
2. For each question, select the option that best reflects your current or future plans for your photography equipment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, required: false): This is an optional note-taking field. If you have any questions or comments about the form, feel free to write them here.
* **how_satisfied** (`satisfaction_survey`, `number`, required: false): This field asks about your current level of satisfaction with your current photography equipment. Please select a number to indicate your satisfaction level, where 0 is extremely dissatisfied and 100 is extremely satisfied.
* **purchase_plan** (`purchase_intent`, `select_one`, required: false): This field asks about your plans for purchasing new photography equipment. Select 'True' if you plan to buy new gear in the near future, 'False' if you don't, or 'Undecided' if you're still considering it.
* **satisfaction_score** (`satisfaction_score`, `number`, required: false): This field asks about your satisfaction with your current photography equipment. Please select a number to indicate your satisfaction level, where 0 is extremely dissatisfied and 100 is extremely satisfied.
* **satisfaction_score** ( duplicate field, `number`, required: false): This field is a duplicate of the previous one and seems to be an error in the form design. Please disregard this field.
* **purchase_score** (`purchase_score`, `number`, required: false): This field asks about your current satisfaction with the price range of your current photography equipment. Please select a number to indicate your satisfaction level.
* **camera_body_type** (`camera_body_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field asks about the type of camera body you use. Select all the types that apply to you, such as DSLR, Mirrorless, Action, or Other.
* **purchase_camera_body_type** (`purchase_camera_body_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field asks about the type of camera body you plan to purchase. Select all the types that apply to you, such as DSLR, Mirrorless, Action, or Other.
* **lens_type** (`lens_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field asks about the type of lens you use. Select all the types that apply to you, such as Prime, Zoom, or Other.
* **purchase_lens_type** (`purchase_lens_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field asks about the type of lens you plan to purchase. Select all the types that apply to you, such as Prime, Zoom, or Other.
* **purchase_date** (`purchase_date`, `date`, required: false): This field asks for the date you plan to purchase your new photography equipment.
* **purchase_time** (`purchase_time`, `time`, required: false): This field asks for the time of day you plan to purchase your new photography equipment.
* **price_range** (`price_range`, `number`, required: false): This field asks for the price range you are looking for in your new photography equipment.
* **purchase_price_range** (`purchase_price_range`, `number`, required: false): This field is a duplicate of the previous one and seems to be an error in the form design. Please disregard this field.
