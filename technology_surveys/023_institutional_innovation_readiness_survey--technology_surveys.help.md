# Institutional Innovation Readiness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to assess the institutional readiness for innovation within your organization, providing valuable insights to help you better understand your current innovation capacity and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of institution you represent.
2. Choose the innovation culture your institution fosters.
3. Choose the innovation capacities your institution has (select all that apply).
4. Select the digital transformation capabilities your institution has (select all that apply).
5. Choose the institutional structure that best describes your organization.
6. Indicate your institution's innovation readiness level on a scale.
7. Calculate your institution's innovation readiness score.
8. Rate your institution's capacity for innovation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Institution Type** (`institution_type`, `select_one` required)
Choose the type of institution you represent: 
* **Innovation Hub**: A hub for innovation activities and services.
* **Research Institute**: An organization focused on research and development.
* **Other**: Other (please specify in the comments if applicable).

### 2. **Innovation Culture** (`innovation_culture`, `select_one` required)
Select the level of innovation culture your institution fosters:
* **Encourage**: Encourage and support innovation within the institution.
* **Foster**: Foster and promote innovation within the institution.
* **Support**: Have a basic level of innovation culture.

### 3. **Innovation Capabilities** (`innovation_capabilities`, `select_multiple` required)
Choose the innovation capabilities your institution has:
* **Data Science**: Data analysis, machine learning, etc.
* **Digital Transformation**: Cloud computing, data analytics, etc.
* **Artificial Intelligence**: AI and AI-powered tools.

### 4. **Digital Transformation Capabilities** (`digital_transformation`, `select_multiple` required)
Select the digital transformation capabilities your institution has:
* **Cloud Computing**: Cloud infrastructure, cloud services, etc.
* **Data Analytics**: Data analysis tools and services.
* **Cybersecurity**: Data security and protection services.

### 5. **Institutional Structure** (`institutional_structure`, `select_multiple` required)
Choose the structure of your institution:
* **Flat**: A flat organizational structure.
* **Hierarchy**: A hierarchical organizational structure.
* **Matrix**: A matrix organizational structure.

### 6. **Innovation Readiness Level** (`innovation_readiness`, `number` required)
Rate your institution's innovation readiness level on a scale (no explanation provided).

### 7. **Institutional Innovation Readiness Score** (`institutional_innovation_readiness_score`, `number` required)
Calculate your institution's innovation readiness score based on your answers.

### 8. **Institutional Capacity for Innovation** (`institutional_capacity_for_innovation`, `select_multiple` required)
Rate your institution's capacity for innovation:
* **High**: High capacity for innovation.
* **Medium**: Medium capacity for innovation.
* **Low**: Low capacity for innovation.

### Tips
* Please answer based on your actual institutional capabilities and culture.
* If you need more information or clarification, please use the comments section.
