# online_exam_platform_evaluation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about their experience with our online exam platform. It aims to evaluate the platform's usability, reliability, satisfaction, and overall performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the questions carefully and answer them honestly.
2. Provide concrete examples or descriptions to support your answers.
3. Fill in all required fields. If a field is not required, feel free to provide information if you have any comments or suggestions.
4. Submit your feedback to help us improve the platform.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction**: This field is optional and allows you to provide any initial comments or explanations about your experience with the platform.
  - If you are new to the platform, please describe how you encountered the platform.
  - If you have been using the platform for a while, please provide any notable improvements or changes you have observed.
- **Usability**: This field asks you to rate the platform's usability on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is 'very difficult to use' and 5 is 'very easy to use'. Please consider factors such as navigation, user interface, and overall user experience.
- **Reliability**: This field also asks you to rate the platform's reliability on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is 'often fails to work as expected' and 5 is 'always works as expected'. Please consider factors such as data accuracy, system response time, and overall stability.
- **Satisfaction**: This field asks you to rate your satisfaction with the platform on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is 'very dissatisfied' and 5 is 'very satisfied'. Please consider your overall experience with the platform, including features and support.
- **Comments**: This field is optional and allows you to provide any additional comments or suggestions about the platform. Please be specific and provide concrete examples or ideas for improvement.
