# programming_languages_proficiency_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your experience and familiarity with various programming languages. By completing this survey, you will be able to express your proficiency levels, years of experience, and frequency of use for each language, as well as share your favorite languages and reasons for learning new ones.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the list of languages provided and select your known languages.
2. For each language, rate your proficiency level (beginner, intermediate, or advanced).
3. Answer questions about your years of experience, frequency of use, and last project completed with each language.
4. Choose your favorite languages, including the one you like best and the second language you enjoy using.
5. Share any other languages you know and list them.
6. Consider your interest in learning new languages and choose from the list of options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Which language do you know?** (`survey_language`, `select_one`, required)
    This question asks you to select one language from the list provided that you have knowledge of.
* **How proficient are you in this language?** (`language_proficiency`, `select_multiple`, required)
    Rate your level of proficiency in this language as beginner, intermediate, or advanced.
* **How many years of experience do you have with the language?** (`years_of_experience`, `number`, required)
    Share the number of years you have experience with this language.
* **How frequently do you use the language?** (`frequency_of_use`, `select_one`, required)
    Indicate how often you use this language, such as never, rarely, occasionally, frequently, or always.
* **When did you last use the language?** (`last_used`, `date`, required)
    Share the date of your last project completion with this language.
* **What was your last project that you worked on with the language?** (`last_project`, `text`, required)
    Describe your last project that you completed with this language.
* **Which language do you like best?** (`favorite_language`, `select_one`, required)
    Choose one language from the list provided that you enjoy using the most.
* **Why is Python your favorite language?** (`why_language`, `text`, required)
    Share your reasons for liking Python as your favorite language.
* **Which of the following is the second favorite language that you enjoy using?** (`favorite_language_2`, `select_multiple`, required)
    Choose up to two languages that you enjoy using from the list provided.
* **How many years of experience do you have with your second favorite language?** (`years_of_experience_2`, `number`, required)
    Share the number of years you have experience with your second favorite language.
* **How frequently do you use your second favorite language?** (`frequency_of_use_2`, `select_one`, required)
    Indicate how often you use this language, such as never, rarely, occasionally, frequently, or always.
* **When did you last use your second favorite language?** (`last_used_2`, `date`, required)
    Share the date of your last project completion with this language.
* **What was your last project that you worked on with your second favorite language?** (`last_project_2`, `text`, required)
    Describe your last project that you completed with this language.
* **Which other languages do you know?** (`other_languages`, `text`, required)
    Share any other languages you know that are not listed.
* **List of other languages** (`other_languages_list`, `text`, required)
    Write a list of other languages you know.
* **Do you have any other languages that you would like to learn?** (`other_languages_2`, `select_one`, required)
    Choose if you are interested in learning new languages.
* **List of languages to learn** (`other_languages_2_list`, `text`, required)
    Write a list of languages you would like to learn.
* **Which other language would you like to learn next?** (`other_languages_3`, `select_one`, required)
    Choose one language from the list provided that you would like to learn next.
* **Would you like to work on a project with the next language you want to learn?** (`other_project_1`, `select_one`, required)
    Share your interest in working on a project with the next language.
* **What type of project would you like to work on with the next language you want to learn?** (`other_project_2`, `text`, required)
    Describe the type of project you would like to work on with the next language.
* **What skills do you think you would need to learn to be proficient in the next language you want to learn?** (`other_project_3`, `text`, required)
    Share the skills you think you need to learn for the next language.
* **What resources do you think you would need to learn the next language you want to learn?** (`other_project_4`, `text`, required)
    Describe the resources you think you need to learn the next language.
* **What would you do if you had a chance to work on a project with the next language you want to learn?** (`other_project_5`, `text`, required)
    Share what you would do if given the opportunity.
* **Why would you like to learn the next language you want to learn?** (`other_project_6`, `text`, required)
    Explain why you want to learn the next language.
