# Digital Health Adoption Strategy Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Digital Health Adoption Strategy Evaluation Form is designed to help organizations assess their current level of digital health adoption and identify areas for improvement. This form will evaluate your organization's digital health adoption strategy and provide valuable insights to help you better understand your current state and make informed decisions for future development.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Select the correct options for each question, based on your organization's current situation.
3. Be sure to answer all questions, even if you're not sure or don't know the answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your role in the organization?**
	+ A select-one question to identify your role within the organization.
	+ Please choose one of the following options:
		- CEO/Owner
		- Project Manager
		- IT Director
* **What is the size of your organization?**
	+ A select-multiple question to identify the size of your organization.
	+ Please choose one or more of the following options:
		- Less than 10
		- 10-50
		- 51-100
		- More than 100
* **What digital health initiatives has your organization implemented?**
	+ A text field to describe the digital health initiatives your organization has implemented.

* **What is your current stage of digital health adoption?**
