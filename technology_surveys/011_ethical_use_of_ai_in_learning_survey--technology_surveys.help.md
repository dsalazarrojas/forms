# Ethical Use Of AI In Learning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This help guide is designed to assist users in completing the Ethical Use Of AI In Learning Survey form. The form is intended to gather opinions and insights from users on the use of AI in learning, focusing on the ethical aspects of its adoption.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your title for the survey in the "Survey Title" field.
2. Read and agree with the introduction.
3. Select one or more benefits of AI in learning from the "Benefits of AI" field.
4. Select one or more concerns about AI in learning from the "Concerns about AI" field.
5. Specify any other benefits or concerns not listed in the "Other benefits of AI" and "Other concerns about AI" fields, if applicable.
6. Provide any additional comments in the "Comments" field, if applicable.
7. Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted.
8. Enter your name, organization, and role in the "Name", "Organization", and "Role in the organization" fields.
9. If you have any other comments, enter them in the "Other comments" field, if applicable.
10. Click the "Submit" button to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Title** (survey_title):
	+ This field is where you enter your title for the survey.
* **Introduction** (introduction):
	+ This field is where you read and agree with the introduction.
* **Benefits of AI** (ai_benefits):
	+ This field allows you to select one or more benefits of AI in learning.
	+ Options include AI helps teachers to automate tedious tasks, AI helps to personalize learning, AI improves learning outcomes, AI increases accessibility, and Other (please specify).
* **Concerns about AI** (ai_concerns):
	+ This field allows you to select one or more concerns about AI in learning.
	+ Options include AI raises concerns about privacy, AI increases bias, AI raises concerns about accountability, AI can be too expensive, and Other (please specify).
* **Most Important Benefits** (benefits_important):
	+ This field allows you to select the most important benefits of AI in learning from the options provided.
* **Most Important Concerns** (concerns_important):
	+ This field allows you to select the most important concerns about AI in learning from the options provided.
* **Other benefits of AI** (benefits_other):
	+ This field is for specifying any other benefits of AI not listed in the previous field.
* **Other concerns about AI** (concerns_other):
	+ This field is for specifying any other concerns about AI not listed in the previous field.
* **Comments** (comments):
	+ This field is for providing any additional comments or thoughts you may have.
* **Email** (email):
	+ This field is where you enter your email address if you would like to be contacted.
* **Name** (name):
	+ This field is where you enter your name.
* **Organization** (organization):
	+ This field is where you enter your organization's name.
* **Role in the organization** (role):
	+ This field is where you enter your job title or position in the organization.
* **Other comments** (comments_other):
	+ This field is for providing any other comments you may have.
* **Submit** (submit):
	+ This field is the button to complete and submit the form.

## Tips

* Please select only one or more benefits or concerns if the options provided.
* If you have any additional comments or thoughts, please provide them in the "Comments" or "Other comments" fields.
* Make sure to enter your email address if you would like to be contacted.
* Enter your name, organization, and role in the correct fields.
