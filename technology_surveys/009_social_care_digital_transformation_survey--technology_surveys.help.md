# Social Care Digital Transformation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to collect information from users about their current digital tool usage, skills required for their role, and priorities for the next quarter. It will help us understand the current state of digital transformation in social care and identify areas where support is needed.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the survey.
2. Answer each question in the space provided, taking care to ensure the correct information is entered.
3. Select any relevant options for each question.
4. Enter a valid email address.
5. Fill out any additional information required.
6. Click on the "Submit" button to complete the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Main Page
* **Email** (`email`, text, required): Enter a valid email address that will be used to identify and prevent duplicate entries.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for any additional contact information.
* **Additional Notes** (`note`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes related to your experience.

### Tools Used
* **Which digital tools do you use?** (`tools_used`, select_multiple, optional): Select all digital tools you currently use. Options include:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3

### Skills Required
* **What skills do you think are required for the role?** (`skills_required`, text, optional): Provide a list of skills you think are necessary for your role.

### Priorities
* **What are your priorities for the next quarter?** (`priorities`, select_one, optional): Select your primary priority for the next quarter. Options include:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3

### Date and Time
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are submitting the survey.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time you are submitting the survey.

### Submit
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Confirm your submission and complete the survey.

Note: Please ensure you submit the survey only once. If you have already filled out the survey with the same email address, you will not be able to complete this survey again.
