# edtech_app_feature_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about the edtech app features. The data collected will help the development team to improve and enhance the app.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User ID, as it is required to identify your entry.
2. Select the feature you want to provide feedback about, by choosing 'Yes' or 'No' from the dropdown list.
3. If you want to provide feedback for a specific feature, rate it using the scale (Very Poor, Poor, Neutral, Good, Excellent).
4. If you have any suggestions for improvement, type them in the text field provided.
5. Enter your Email Address, which is required to contact you.
6. If you want to provide a contact number, you can do so in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User ID** (`user_id`, number, required): This is your unique identifier that helps us to track your feedback. It is a required field.
* **What feature do you want to provide feedback about?** (`user_feedback`, select_multiple, required): Please choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the features you want to provide feedback about.
* **How would you rate this feature?** (`rating`, select_one, optional): If you want to provide feedback for a specific feature, rate it on the scale (Very Poor, Poor, Neutral, Good, Excellent).
* **What are your suggestions for improvements?** (`suggestions`, text, required): Please provide any suggestions you have for improving the features you selected.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address, so we can contact you to discuss your feedback.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): If you have a contact number, please enter it here.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields (User ID, feature selection, rating, and Email Address).
* If you are unsure about what feature to select, you can always choose 'No' for all features.
* If you have any suggestions, make sure to type them clearly in the text field.
* If you have any questions or need help, please contact us at your provided email address.
