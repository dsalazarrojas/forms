# Educational Platform Beta Testing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Platform Beta Testing Consent Form is designed to gather information from participants in the beta testing process of our educational platform. This form is intended to ensure that all participants understand their roles and responsibilities during the testing phase.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the required fields marked as "Required" (indicated by *).
2. For the "Role" field, select the appropriate role from the provided options.
3. For the "Consent" field, select "I understand and agree" to confirm your participation.
4. Provide accurate and detailed information in the "Participant information," "School information," and "Contact information" fields.
5. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (id: 1, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Last name** (id: 2, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Email** (id: 3, required): Enter your official email address.
* **School name** (id: 4, optional): Enter the name of your educational institution. If you are not affiliated with a school, you can leave this field blank.
* **School email** (id: 5, required): Enter your school's official email address.
* **Role** (id: 6, required): Select the role you are taking on for this beta testing:
	+ Option 1: Participant
	+ Option 2: School representative
	+ Option 3: Other (please specify)
* **Consent** (id: 7, required): Select "I understand and agree" to confirm your participation in the beta testing process.
* **Expectations** (id: 8, optional): Provide any additional expectations you have for your participation in the beta testing process.
* **Participant ID** (id: 9, optional): Enter any identifying information for you as a participant.
* **School ID** (id: 10, optional): Enter any identifying information for your school.
* **Date** (id: 11, required): Enter the date of your participation in the beta testing process.
* **Time** (id: 12, optional): Enter the time of your participation in the beta testing process.
* **Location** (id: 13, optional): Enter the location of your participation in the beta testing process.
* **Contact number** (id: 14, optional): Enter any contact information for you as a participant.
* **School location** (id: 15, optional): Enter any location information for your school.
* **Participant information** (id: 16, optional): Select any relevant participant information:
	+ Option 1: Option 1
	+ Option 2: Option 2
	+ Option 3: Option 3
* **School information** (id: 17, optional): Select any relevant school information:
	+ Option 1: Option 1
	+ Option 2: Option 2
	+ Option 3: Option 3
* **Other information** (id: 18, optional): Enter any additional information about your participation in the beta testing process.
* **School other** (id: 19, optional): Enter any other information about your school.
* **Participant other** (id: 20, optional): Enter any other information about you as a participant.
* **School other info** (id: 21, optional): Enter any other information about your school.
* **Participant other info** (id: 22, optional): Enter any other information about you as a participant.
* **Date time** (id: 23, optional): Enter the date and time of your participation in the beta testing process.
* **Date time note** (id: 24, optional): Enter any additional notes about the date and time of your participation.
* **Contact information** (id: 25, optional): Enter any contact information for you or your school.
