# Healthcare Technology Adoption Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Healthcare Technology Adoption Survey is a form designed to gather information about the primary goal of the organization, the type of organization, the role within the organization, level of experience with the technology, and other relevant questions to better understand the current state of healthcare technology adoption in healthcare settings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To begin, select the title of the organization from the dropdown menu.
2. Next, answer the following questions:
   - What is the primary goal of your organization? (Select one option: Hospital, Clinic, Research Team, or Other)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, `text`, required): Enter a brief title for your organization.
* **What is the primary goal of your organization?** (`form_question_1`, `select_one`, required): Select the primary goal of your organization (Hospital, Clinic, Research Team, or Other).
* **Which type of organization do you belong to?** (`form_question_2`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all types of organizations that apply (Hospital, Clinic, Research Team, or Other).
* **What is your role within your organization?** (`form_question_3`, `select_one`, required): Select your role within your organization (Doctor, Nurse, IT Manager, or Other).
* **What is your level of experience with the technology?** (`form_question_4`, `number`, required): Enter a number from 0 to 10, where 0 represents no experience, and 10 represents a high level of experience.

## Tips
- Be as accurate and honest as possible when answering the questions.
- If unsure about any question, please feel free to ask for help.
