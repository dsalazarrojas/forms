# Digital Consulting Adoption Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to understand your usage and preferences regarding digital tools within our organization. Your responses will help us better understand the current state of digital tool adoption and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for questions that ask about digital tool adoption.
3. For questions that ask about when you plan to adopt digital tools, select a date or time that best represents your intended action.
4. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
5. If you have any comments or feedback about digital tools, please use the space provided for notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Digital Tool Adoption** (1, text): This is the introduction to the survey.
* **What kind of tools do you use?** (2, select_multiple): Please select all the digital tools you use from the provided options.
* **Digital Tool 1** (3, note): This is a field for taking notes, please feel free to write anything relevant to this tool.
* **Digital Tool 2** (4, note): Another field for taking notes about this tool.
* **Digital Tool 3** (5, note): Another field for taking notes about this tool.
* **...** (6-25, select_one): These questions ask you to confirm if you plan to adopt each digital tool. Please select "Yes" for tools you plan to adopt and "No" for tools you do not plan to adopt.
* **When do you plan to adopt the digital tools?** (13, date): If you have selected "Yes" for any of the previous questions, please provide a date when you plan to adopt the digital tool.
* **Time** (14, time): Please provide a time when you plan to adopt the digital tool.
* **Email** (15, email): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (16, text): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Digital Tool 11** (17, note): Another field for taking notes about this tool.
* **...** (18-25, note): More fields for taking notes about additional tools.

**Tips**

* Please answer all questions truthfully, as your responses will help us improve digital tools and services.
* If you have any feedback or suggestions about digital tools, please use the space provided for notes.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
