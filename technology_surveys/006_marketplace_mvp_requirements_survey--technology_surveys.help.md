# Marketplace MVP Requirements Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and requirements from end users about the new marketplace MVP. It's essential for our team to understand your needs and expectations to refine and improve the feature set of the marketplace.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Review each page carefully, and fill out the required fields (marked with *Required*).
2. Choose the options that best match your preferences and needs.
3. Provide honest and accurate information about your expectations.
4. Click "Submit" to send your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Page 2: Select an Option* (Required):** This field asks you to choose the best option for a particular requirement. Please select the option that best fits your needs and expectations.
* **Page 4: Date* (Required):** This field is for selecting a specific date. Please enter the exact date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Page 7: Email* (Required):** This field is for entering your email address. Please type your email address as it appears in your account.
* **Page 14: Date* (Required):** This field is similar to Page 4, for selecting a specific date. Please enter the exact date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Page 8: Text (Optional):** This field is for entering any additional comments or information about your preferences and needs.
* **Page 10: Select Multiple Options (Optional):** This field allows you to choose multiple options from the list. Please select the options that best match your needs and expectations.
* **Page 16: Time (Optional):** This field is for selecting a specific time. Please enter the exact time in 24-hour format (`HH:MM`).
* **Page 18: Select Multiple Options (Optional):** This field is similar to Page 10, allowing you to choose multiple options from the list. Please select the options that best match your needs and expectations.
