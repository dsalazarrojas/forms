# Student Technological Readiness Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Technological Readiness Assessment is a form designed to evaluate a student's technological readiness, which is crucial for effective learning in today's digital age.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select "No" or "Yes" for each question that applies to you.
2. Choose "None", "Some", or "Yes" for questions that ask about access to resources or support.
3. For questions that ask about device usage, select the types of devices you use for learning.
4. Enter your thoughts or comments in the "digital_skills" and "device_maintenance" questions.
5. Once you've completed all the questions, click submit to calculate your technological readiness score.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Home Internet Access**: Please select "Yes" if you have access to the internet at home, and "No" otherwise.
* **Digital Confidence**: This question assesses your confidence in using digital tools and resources. Please enter a brief description of your confidence level (e.g., "high", "medium", or "low").
* **Device Reliability**: Describe the reliability of the devices you use for learning.
* **Device Access**: Please select all the devices you have access to for learning (e.g., Laptop, Tablet, Smartphone).
* **School Wi-Fi**: Select "Yes" if you have access to Wi-Fi at school, and "No" otherwise.
* **School Device Access**: Please select all the devices you have access to at school (e.g., Laptop, Tablet, Computer Lab).
* **Online Support**: Select "None", "Some", or "Yes" to indicate the level of support you have for online learning.
* **Blended Learning Support**: Select "None", "Some", or "Yes" to indicate the level of support you have for blended learning.
* **Digital Skills**: This question is for your thoughts or comments about your digital skills. Please enter any relevant information.
* **Device Quality**: Describe the quality of the devices you use for learning.
* **Device Maintenance**: Describe how you maintain your devices for learning.
* **Online Reliability**: Select "None", "Some", or "Yes" to indicate the reliability of online resources.
* **Blended Learning Confidence**: Select "None", "Some", or "Yes" to indicate your confidence in blended learning.
* **Device Security**: Select "None", "Some", or "Yes" to indicate the level of security on your devices.
* **Digital Confidence**: This question is similar to "Digital Confidence" and assesses your confidence in using digital tools and resources. Please enter a brief description of your confidence level.
* **Student Device Usage**: Select all the devices you use for learning (e.g., Laptop, Tablet, Smartphone).
* **Teacher Device Usage**: Select all the devices used by your teacher (e.g., Laptop, Tablet, Smartphone).
* **Other Device Usage**: Select all other devices used for learning (e.g., e-reader, tablet, etc.).
* **Device Usage Policy**: This question is for your thoughts or comments about your school's device usage policy.
* **Teacher Training**: Select "None", "Some", or "Yes" to indicate the level of training your teachers have for using technology.
* **Student Support**: Select "None", "Some", or "Yes" to indicate the level of support you receive from teachers and peers.
* **Teacher Support**: Select "None", "Some", or "Yes" to indicate the level of support your teachers provide.
* **School Support**: Select "None", "Some", or "Yes" to indicate the level of support your school provides.
* **Blended Learning Plan**: Select "None", "Some", or "Yes" to indicate whether your school has a blended learning plan.
* **Student Technological Readiness Score**: This is a calculated score based on your answers, indicating your level of technological readiness.
