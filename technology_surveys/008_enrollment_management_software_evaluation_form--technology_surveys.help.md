# Enrollment Management Software Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback about enrollment management software systems. Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly to provide valuable insights for stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Read each question carefully and answer it to the best of your ability.
2.  If a question is not applicable, please indicate that in the corresponding field.
3.  If you need help or clarification about a question, please ask your administrator.
4.  Make sure to review your answers before submitting the form.
