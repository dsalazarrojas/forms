## software_freedom_principles_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The software freedom principles survey aims to assess individuals' understanding and attitudes towards free and open-source software development. The survey assesses respondents' awareness of the benefits and values associated with open-source software, as well as their involvement in open-source communities.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please ensure you have read and understood the purpose of this survey before proceeding.
2.  Select the correct answer for each question, based on your understanding and experiences.
3.  Fill in any additional comments you may have in the text box at the end.
