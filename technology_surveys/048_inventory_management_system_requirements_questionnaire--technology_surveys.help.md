# inventory_management_system_requirements_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about inventory management system requirements from users.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out your contact information, including your email and phone number.
2. Provide a note if you have any additional information to share about your contact information.
3. Answer the system requirements section with specific details about the system you are looking for.
4. If applicable, answer the business objectives, functional requirements, and non-functional requirements sections with specific details.
5. Choose the evaluation criteria that best matches your requirements.
6. Choose the evaluation scale for the selected evaluation criteria.
7. Provide the implementation team and implementation plan.
8. Choose the implementation schedule and implementation cost.
9. Select the evaluation method for the system.
10. Choose the evaluation scope and evaluation frequency.
11. Select the system ownership and system usage.
12. Provide any other information about the system scope.
13. Add any note about the system requirements.
14. Add any implementation note.
15. Add any evaluation note.
16. Add any other note.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, required/optional): Fill in your name, phone number, and other relevant details about yourself or your organization.
* **Email** (`contact_information_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`contact_information_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Note** (`contact_information_note`, `text`, optional): Add any additional information about your contact information.
* **System Requirements** (`system_requirements`, `text`, required): Describe the specific requirements you are looking for in an inventory management system.
* **Business Objectives** (`business_objectives`, `text`, optional): If applicable, describe the business objectives of the system.
* **Functional Requirements** (`functional_requirements`, `text`, optional): If applicable, describe the functional requirements of the system.
* **Non-functional Requirements** (`non_functional_requirements`, `text`, optional): If applicable, describe the non-functional requirements of the system.
* **Evaluation Criteria** (`evaluation_criteria`, `select_one`, required): Choose one from the options: Performance, Cost, Usability, Security.
* **Evaluation Scale** (`evaluation_scale`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose one or more from the options: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5.
* **Implementation Team** (`implementation_team`, `text`, optional): Describe the team that will implement the system.
* **Implementation Plan** (`implementation_plan`, `text`, optional): Describe the implementation plan.
* **Implementation Schedule** (`implementation_schedule`, `date`, required): Choose the implementation schedule.
* **Implementation Cost** (`implementation_cost`, `number`, required): Enter the implementation cost.
* **Implementation Success Criteria** (`implementation_success_criteria`, `text`, optional): Describe the criteria for implementation success.
* **Evaluation Method** (`evaluation_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose one from the options: Cost-Benefit Analysis, Return on Investment (ROI), Net Present Value (NPV), Payback Period.
* **Evaluation Frequency** (`evaluation_frequency`, `date`, required): Enter the evaluation frequency.
* **Evaluation Scope** (`evaluation_scope`, `text`, optional): Describe the scope of evaluation.
* **System Ownership** (`system_ownership`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose one or more from the options: Company, Department, Individual.
* **System Usage** (`system_usage`, `text`, optional): Describe the system usage.
* **System Scope** (`system_scope`, `text`, optional): Describe the scope of the system.
* **System Requirements Note** (`system_requirements_note`, `text`, optional): Add any additional information about system requirements.
* **Implementation Note** (`implementation_note`, `text`, optional): Add any implementation note.
* **Evaluation Note** (`evaluation_note`, `text`, optional): Add any evaluation note.
* **Other Note** (`other_note`, `text`, optional): Add any other note.
