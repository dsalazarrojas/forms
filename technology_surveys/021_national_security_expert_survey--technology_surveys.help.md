# National Security Expert Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The National Security Expert Survey is designed to gather information from national security experts on their experience and views on various security topics. This data will be used to inform policy and decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you understand the purpose and requirements.
2. Answer the questions to the best of your ability and provide accurate information.
3. Ensure all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### National Security Expert Survey (Page 1)
* **National Security Expert Survey** (`national_security_expert_survey_page_1`, text, optional): This is a field to enter your name as a national security expert.

### Security Experts (Page 2)
* **Security Experts** (`security_experts_survey_page_2`, select_multiple, required/optional): Please select all the security areas you are an expert in.
  - 'Yes' indicates you have expertise in this area.
  - Multiple selections are allowed, as experts often have multiple areas of expertise.

### Contact Details (Page 3)
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details_page_3`, text, required): This is a field to enter your contact details, including your name, email, and phone number.

### Survey Submission (Page 4)
* **Survey Submission** (`survey_submission_page_4`, date, required): Please enter the date of your last survey submission.

### National Security Expert Survey (Page 5)
* **National Security Expert Survey** (`national_security_expert_survey_page_5`, number, optional): This field is not currently used. Please leave it blank.

### Security Experts (Page 6)
* **Security Experts** (`security_experts_survey_page_6`, select_one, required/optional): Please select one area of security expertise that best describes you.
  - 'Yes' indicates you have expertise in this area.
  - Only one selection is allowed, as it is assumed that each expert has one primary area of expertise.

### Additional Comments (Page 7)
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments_page_7`, text, optional): This field is for any additional comments or information you may want to provide about your security expertise.
