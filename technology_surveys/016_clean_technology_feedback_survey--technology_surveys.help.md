# Clean Technology Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback on clean technologies to drive innovation and make them more effective and accessible to everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps above and answer each question honestly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Which clean technology are you evaluating?**
	+ This question helps us understand which technology you are providing feedback on.
	+ Please select the technology that best matches your experience, such as solar power, wind energy, or electric vehicles.
* **How long have you been using this technology?**
	+ This question helps us assess the effectiveness of the technology over time.
	+ Choose the duration that best reflects your experience, from less than 6 months to more than 3 years.
* **Overall Satisfaction Level**
	+ This question asks for your honest opinion on your experience with the technology.
	+ Rate your satisfaction level, from very satisfied to very dissatisfied.
* **Performance and Reliability Rating**
	+ This question evaluates the technology's ability to perform consistently.
	+ Rate its performance and reliability, from excellent to poor.
* **Cost-Effectiveness**
	+ This question assesses the technology's value for money.
	+ Choose whether you find the technology cost-effective, not cost-effective, or neutral.
* **Ease of Use and Operation**
	+ This question measures the technology's ease of use and maintenance.
	+ Rate its ease of use, from very easy to difficult.
* **Environmental Impact Rating**
	+ This question examines the technology's effect on the environment.
	+ Rate its environmental impact, from very positive to not sure.
* **Primary Benefit**
	+ This question seeks the main advantage you've experienced from using the technology.
	+ Provide a clear and concise answer, describing the benefit in a few sentences.
* **Major Challenges or Drawbacks**
	+ This question asks for any significant difficulties or drawbacks you've encountered.
	+ Share your honest experience, describing the challenges in a few sentences.
* **Improvement Suggestions**
	+ This question invites you to suggest ways to enhance the technology.
	+ Provide constructive feedback, describing specific improvements in a few sentences.
* **Likelihood to Recommend**
	+ This question asks how likely you are to recommend the technology to others.
	+ Choose whether you are highly likely, likely, unlikely, or highly unlikely to recommend.
* **Future Adoption Interest**
	+ This question explores your interest in adopting other clean technologies.
	+ Choose when you might consider adopting other technologies, from within one year to not considering adopting other technologies.
* **Email Address (optional)**
	+ If you're interested in receiving the survey results, provide your email address.
* **Thank you for your feedback!**
	+ This final note thanks you for your time and input, which helps drive innovation in clean technologies.

## Tips

* Answer each question honestly, providing as much detail as needed.
* If a question doesn't apply to your experience, choose "Not sure" or "Not applicable."
* If you have any trouble or need help, please contact us for assistance.

I hope this helps guide you through the clean technology feedback survey form!
