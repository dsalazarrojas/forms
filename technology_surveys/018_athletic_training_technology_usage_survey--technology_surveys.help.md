# Athletic Training Technology Usage Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This survey is designed to gather information about the current state of technology usage in athletic training settings. The goal is to understand the adoption and perceived benefits of various technologies and identify areas where more training or support is needed.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please read through each question carefully and answer the options that best describe your current situation.
2. Select the options that apply to you and your organization.
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Additional Comments" field.
