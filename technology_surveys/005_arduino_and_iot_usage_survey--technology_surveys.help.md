# Arduino And IoT Usage Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Arduino And IoT Usage Survey is designed to gather information from users about their experience with Arduino boards and IoT platforms.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the survey questions in order, from top to bottom.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select all the options that apply.
3. If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask us for clarification.
4. Please answer the questions honestly and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (2, text, required): Please enter your full name.
* **Organization or Company** (3, text, optional): If you are affiliated with an organization or company, please enter it here.
* **Job Title or Role** (4, text, optional): Please enter your job title or role.
* **Experience Level with Arduino** (5, select_one, required): Choose your level of experience with Arduino:
	+ Beginner
	+ Intermediate
	+ Advanced
	+ Expert
* **Which Arduino boards have you used** (7, select_multiple, required): Choose all the Arduino boards you have used:
	+ Arduino Uno
	+ Arduino Nano
	+ Arduino Mega
	+ Arduino MKR boards
	+ Arduino Leonardo
	+ Others (please specify)
* **Primary use case for Arduino projects** (8, select_one, required): Choose the primary use case for your Arduino projects:
	+ Education or learning
	+ Hobbyist projects
	+ Commercial development
	+ Industrial automation
	+ Research
* **IoT platforms or ecosystems you use** (9, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the IoT platforms or ecosystems you use:
	+ Arduino IoT Cloud
	+ AWS IoT
	+ Google Cloud IoT
	+ Microsoft Azure IoT
	+ Raspberry Pi ecosystem
	+ Custom solutions
* **Connectivity protocols used** (10, select_multiple, required): Choose all the connectivity protocols you use:
	+ WiFi
	+ Bluetooth
	+ LoRaWAN
	+ Cellular LTE
	+ Zigbee
	+ NB-IoT
* **Types of sensors typically integrated** (11, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the types of sensors you typically integrate:
	+ Temperature and humidity
	+ Motion and proximity
	+ Light and color
	+ Sound and vibration
	+ Air quality
	+ Pressure and altitude
* **How often do you work on Arduino projects** (12, select_one, required): Choose how often you work on Arduino projects:
	+ Daily
	+ Several times a week
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Occasionally
* **Main challenges encountered** (14, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the main challenges you have encountered:
	+ Hardware compatibility
	+ Network connectivity
	+ Power management
	+ Data security
	+ Software debugging
	+ Scaling solutions
* **Satisfaction with available documentation and support** (15, select_one, required): Choose your level of satisfaction with available documentation and support:
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very dissatisfied
* **Areas for improvement in Arduino ecosystem** (16, text, optional): Please provide any suggestions or areas for improvement in the Arduino ecosystem.
* **Email Address** (17, email, optional): Please enter your email address for follow-up questions or responses.
* **May we contact you about future surveys** (18, select_one, required): Choose whether you are available for future surveys:
	+ True
	+ False

## Tips

* Please answer the questions honestly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask us for clarification.
* We will keep your responses anonymous and confidential.
* Please take your time to answer the questions carefully.
