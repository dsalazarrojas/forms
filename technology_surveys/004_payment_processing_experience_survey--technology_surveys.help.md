# Payment Processing Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experience with our payment processing services. Your input will help us improve our services and provide a better experience for our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the corresponding answers based on your experience with our payment processing services. Make sure to answer all the questions honestly to help us better understand your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How satisfied are you with your payment processing experience?** (`payment_processing_satisfaction`, select_one, required: false): This question helps us understand your level of satisfaction with our payment processing services. Please select the option that best describes your feelings.
* **How easy was the payment process?** (`payment_processing_ease`, select_one, required: false): This question helps us measure the ease of use of our payment processing services. Select the option that best describes your experience.
* **How long did it take to complete the payment?** (`payment_processing_time`, number, required: false): This question helps us understand the time it took to complete the payment. Please enter the actual time it took, if available.
* **How much did you pay?** (`payment_processing_amount`, number, required: false): This question helps us understand the amount you paid. Please enter the actual amount you paid, if available.
* **Were you charged any fees?** (`payment_processing_fees`, select_one, required: false): This question helps us understand if you were charged any fees during the payment process. Select the option that best describes your experience.
* **Please describe any fees you were charged?** (`payment_processing_fees_details`, text, required: false): This question is a follow-up question to the previous one, where you can describe any fees you were charged in more detail.
* **What issues, if any, did you encounter during checkout?** (`issues_encountered`, select_multiple, required: false): This question helps us understand any issues you faced during the checkout process. Select all the issues that apply to your experience.
* **Comments about your payment processing experience** (`payment_processing_comments`, text, required: false): This question is an open-ended question where you can share any additional comments or feedback about your experience with our payment processing services.

## Tips
- Please answer all the questions honestly to help us improve our services.
- Make sure to select the correct options that best describe your experience.
- If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the comments section to share them.
