# Remote Teaching Tools Faculty Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Remote Teaching Tools Faculty Inquiry Form is designed to collect feedback from faculty members on their experience with remote teaching tools. This form aims to gather information on the faculty's satisfaction with these tools, their support needs, and any other comments they may have.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to access the form.
2. Select your answer to each question, either "Yes" or "No", for the question about remote teaching technology.
3. Choose your satisfaction rating for the remote teaching tools (Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Somewhat Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied).
4. Select any additional support needs you may have (Technology, Support, Others, or Not Listed).
5. Type any comments you have about your experience with remote teaching tools in the "Comments" field.
6. Provide your email address so that we can contact you with follow-up questions or to provide further support.
7. Enter your phone number so that we can contact you if needed.
8. Enter your faculty name and title for identification purposes.
9. Enter the name of your department for identification purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Remote Teaching Technology**: This question asks whether you have used any remote teaching tools.
	+ Choose "Yes" if you have used any remote teaching tools.
	+ Choose "No" if you have not used any remote teaching tools.
* **Satisfaction Rating**: This question asks how satisfied you are with the remote teaching tools.
	+ Choose "Very Satisfied" if you are very pleased with the tools.
	+ Choose "Somewhat Satisfied" if you are somewhat pleased with the tools.
	+ Choose "Somewhat Dissatisfied" if you are somewhat dissatisfied with the tools.
	+ Choose "Very Dissatisfied" if you are very dissatisfied with the tools.
* **Support Needs**: This question asks what additional support you may need for remote teaching tools.
	+ Choose "Technology" if you need technical support.
	+ Choose "Support" if you need general support.
	+ Choose "Others" if you need something else.
	+ Choose "Not Listed" if none of the above applies.
* **Comments**: This field allows you to provide any additional comments about your experience with remote teaching tools.
* **Email**: This field is for you to enter your email address so that we can contact you with follow-up questions or provide further support.
* **Phone**: This field is for you to enter your phone number so that we can contact you if needed.
* **Faculty Name**: This field is for you to enter your faculty name for identification purposes.
* **Faculty Title**: This field is for you to enter your faculty title for identification purposes.
* **Department**: This field is for you to enter the name of your department for identification purposes.
