# Metals And Their Properties Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Metals And Their Properties Survey is a form designed to collect information about metals and their properties from respondents. It aims to gather knowledge about metals and opinions on metal-related topics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the introduction page (page 1) for an overview of the form's purpose and instructions.
2. On the "Respondent Details" page (page 2), enter your details as prompted, if applicable.
3. On the "Metal Knowledge" page (page 3), select one of the options that best describes your level of knowledge about metals.
4. On the "Metal Opinion" page (page 4), select all that apply to express your opinion on the given metal-related topics.
5. Finally, on the "Submission" page (page 5), enter any additional comments or notes, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent Details (page 2)**: This field is used to collect your personal information, such as your name, email, or other relevant details. Please enter your information accurately and completely.
* **Metal Knowledge (page 3)**: This field asks you to select one of the options that best describes your level of knowledge about metals. Please choose the option that most accurately reflects your understanding.
* **Metal Opinion (page 4)**: This field is used to collect your opinion on metal-related topics. Select all that apply to express your thoughts and opinions.
* **Submission (page 5)**: This field is for any additional comments or notes you want to provide. Please enter your thoughts or questions related to the form's purpose.
