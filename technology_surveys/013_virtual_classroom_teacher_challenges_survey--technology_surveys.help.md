# Virtual Classroom Teacher Challenges Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather insights from virtual classroom teachers on their experiences, challenges, and suggestions for improvement in teaching in a virtual environment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your response to the initial question by choosing from the available options.
2. Enter your name and title as they appear on your official records.
3. Rate your level of satisfaction with teaching in a virtual classroom on a scale of 1-5.
4. Rate your satisfaction with support services on a scale of 1-5.
5. Provide any suggestions or comments about your experience in the virtual classroom.
6. If you have any additional suggestions, enter them in the box provided.
7. Finally, confirm your completion of the survey by selecting the appropriate option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Start of Survey** (Select One):
  * This is the first question, which helps us understand your initial experience in the virtual classroom.
* **Teacher Name** (Text):
  * Enter your official name as it appears on your records.
* **Teacher Title** (Text):
  * Enter your official title, e.g., 'Ms.', 'Dr.', or 'Professor'.
* **Teaching Challenge** (Number):
  * Rate your level of challenge in teaching a virtual classroom on a scale of 1-5.
* **Teaching Satisfaction** (Number):
  * Rate your level of satisfaction with teaching in a virtual classroom on a scale of 1-5.
* **Support Satisfaction** (Number):
  * Rate your level of satisfaction with support services on a scale of 1-5.
* **Suggestions** (Text):
  * Provide any general comments or suggestions about your experience.
* **Other Suggestions** (Text):
  * Enter any additional suggestions that are not already mentioned.
* **End of Survey** (Select One):
  * Confirm that you have completed the survey.

Note: The fields with a 'Select One' type are multiple-choice questions with the provided options.
