# system_usability_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your experience with the system, aiming to understand how well the system meets your needs and expectations. Your feedback will help us improve the system's usability and overall user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide a clear and comprehensive feedback, follow these steps:

1. Answer all questions honestly, selecting the best option from the available choices.
2. For questions with "select all that apply" options, choose all that apply to your experience.
3. When answering "How easy was it to learn to use the system?" and "What specific aspects would you improve or change about the system?", be as detailed and concrete as possible.
4. For "Do you have any additional comments or feedback?", feel free to share any thoughts, suggestions, or concerns you might have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How satisfied are you with the system's overall usability?**
	+ Type: single, Required
	+ Description: This question asks for your general satisfaction level with the system's overall usability. Select one of the five options: Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, or Very dissatisfied.
* **How often do you interact with the system?**
	+ Type: single, Required
	+ Description: This question asks about your interaction frequency with the system. Choose one of the seven options: Daily, Several times a week, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely, or Never.
* **Which of the following best describes your experience with the system?** (Select all that apply)
	+ Type: multiple, Required
	+ Description: Choose all the options that best describe your experience with the system. Options include: Easy to use, Intuitive, Fast performance, Reliable, Lacks features, or Confusing interface.
* **How easy was it to learn to use the system?**
	+ Type: single, Required
	+ Description: This question asks about your experience learning to use the system. Select one of the five options: Very easy, Somewhat easy, Neutral, Somewhat difficult, or Very difficult.
* **What specific aspects would you improve or change about the system?**
	+ Type: text, Required
	+ Description: This question is an open-ended opportunity for you to provide specific and concrete suggestions on how to improve the system.
* **Do you have any additional comments or feedback?**
	+ Type: text, Required
	+ Description: This is your chance to share any additional thoughts, suggestions, or concerns you might have about the system.
