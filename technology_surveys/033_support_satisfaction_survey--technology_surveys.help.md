# Support Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Support Satisfaction Survey is designed to gather feedback from customers on their experience with our support team. This survey is intended to help us understand how well we're meeting our customers' needs and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the survey, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Rate your experience using the numbers provided.
3. Select the correct option for the "What type of issue did you experience?" question.
4. Add any additional comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Intro** (`intro_page`, note, required: false): This is the introduction to the survey. Please read it carefully to understand what we're asking you to do.
* **How would you rate the support you received?** (`support_rating`, number, required: false): Rate your experience with our support team on a scale of 1-10.
* **On a scale of 1-10, how would you rate the support you received?** (`support_rating_score`, number, required: false): This question is similar to the previous one. Please rate your experience again, using the same scale.
* **How much time did it take for support to resolve your issue?** (`time_to_resolution`, number, required: false): Estimate the amount of time it took for our support team to resolve your issue.
* **How would you rate the resolution of the issue?** (`resolution_rating`, number, required: false): Rate how well we resolved your issue, using the same 1-10 scale.
* **How would you rate the communication from support?** (`communication_rating`, number, required: false): Rate the quality of communication from our support team.
* **How would you rate the support agent's knowledge on the issue?** (`agent_knowledge`, number, required: false): Rate how well our support agent knew about the issue.
* **What type of issue did you experience?** (`issue_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of issue you experienced (Technical, Non-technical, or Other).
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or feedback about your experience with our support team.
