# Hand Tracking Feature Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from users about the hand tracking feature of a system or application. Your input will help us understand what works and what doesn't, and aid us in making improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Rate your feature: Choose a rating from 1 to 5 to express your overall satisfaction with the feature.
2. Select your overall satisfaction: Choose a level of satisfaction from 1 (Not Satisfied) to 5 (Extremely Satisfied) to help us understand your overall experience.
3. Add any comments: If you have any thoughts or suggestions about the feature, please share them in the comment box.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feature Rating (Feature Rating)**: Rate the feature from 1 (Not Satisfied) to 5 (Extremely Satisfied) based on your experience.
* **Overall Satisfaction (Overall Satisfaction)**: Select a level of satisfaction from 1 (Not Satisfied) to 5 (Extremely Satisfied) to express your overall satisfaction with the feature. 
* **Comment (Comment)**: Share your thoughts, suggestions, or feedback about the feature.

## Tips
- Please be honest and thorough in your feedback.
- If you have any suggestions or ideas, we'd love to hear them.
- You can add as much or as little information as you like in the comment field.
