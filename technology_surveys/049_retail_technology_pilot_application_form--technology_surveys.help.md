# retail-technology-pilot-application-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help retailers apply for the retail technology pilot program. It gathers information about their current technology needs and pain points, as well as details about the solutions they are interested in implementing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure that you are eligible to participate in the pilot program.
2. Answer each question based on your own experiences and opinions as a retailer.
3. Provide clear and concise information for each question.
4. Do not hesitate to reach out if you have any questions or need clarification on any of the questions.
