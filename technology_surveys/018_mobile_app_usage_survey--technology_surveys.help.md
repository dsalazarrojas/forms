# Mobile App Usage Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mobile App Usage Survey is designed to collect data on users' mobile app usage habits, including the frequency of app usage, time spent on the app, and user behavior. The survey aims to gather information on various aspects of app usage, such as how often users access the app, how much time they spend on it, and how they usually find out about new features or updates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Mobile App Usage Survey, follow the instructions below:

1. Begin by answering the questions on the first page. These questions cover the basic information about your mobile app usage habits.
2. On the second page, answer the additional questions about more specific aspects of your app usage behavior.
3. Make sure to answer all the required questions, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
4. For each question, select the most accurate option that best describes your behavior or preference.
5. If you have any additional comments or feedback, you can type them in the "Comments" field.
6. Once you have completed all the questions, review your answers to ensure accuracy.
7. Click "Submit" to submit your survey.
