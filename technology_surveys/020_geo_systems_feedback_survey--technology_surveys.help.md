# Geo Systems Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect user feedback on their experiences with geo systems, specifically their usage of mapping platforms.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the mapping platform(s) you have used from the provided options.
3. Rate your experience with the selected platform.
4. Enter any comments or feedback you may have.
5. Enter your email address and phone number (if applicable).
6. Enter the date and time you used the mapping platform (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Feedback** (`id: 1`): Please enter your honest feedback about your experience with the geo system.
* **Mapping Technology** (`id: 2`): Select the mapping platform you used from the options provided.
* **Rating** (`id: 3`): Rate your experience with the selected platform on a scale of 1-5.
* **Comment** (`id: 4`): Enter any comments or feedback you may have about your experience.
* **Email Address** (`id: 5`): Enter your email address for follow-up purposes (if applicable).
* **Phone Number** (`id: 6`): Enter your phone number for follow-up purposes (if applicable).
* **Date Used Mapping Platform** (`id: 7`): Enter the date you used the mapping platform (if applicable).
* **Time Used Mapping Platform** (`id: 8`): Enter the time you used the mapping platform (if applicable).
* **Mapping Platforms Used** (`id: 9`): Select any additional mapping platforms you used from the options provided.
* **Submit** (`id: 10`): Review your answers and submit the form.

Note: Fields with `id: 11-20` are duplicates of the original fields, and should not be filled out unless specifically instructed to do so. 

**Tips**

* Please be honest and accurate in your feedback.
* If you're having trouble with a particular field, please contact us for assistance.
* Your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your input.
