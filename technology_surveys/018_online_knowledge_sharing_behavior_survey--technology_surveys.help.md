# Online Knowledge Sharing Behavior Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your online knowledge sharing behavior. Your responses will help us understand how you engage with online platforms and resources, and will inform the development of more effective online tools.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form questions and options carefully before starting.
2. Answer each question honestly and accurately based on your online behavior.
3. You will be asked about your online activities, barriers to sharing, and the frequency of your sharing behavior.
4. You can select multiple options for questions that allow it.
5. For text fields, provide a brief description in your own words.
6. When finished, review your answers carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `note`, required): This is a brief introduction to the form. Please read it carefully before starting.
- **Online Knowledge Sharing** (`Online Knowledge Sharing`, `select_multiple`, required): This question asks about the types of online activities you participate in, such as online courses, forums, or communities. Select all that apply.
- **Barriers** (`Barriers`, `select_one`, required): This question asks about the main barrier you face when sharing online. Choose one that best describes your situation.
- **Reasons to Share** (`Reasons to Share`, `select_multiple`, required): This question asks about your reasons for sharing online. Select all that apply.
- **How much time do you spend on online activities** (`How much time do you spend on online activities`, `number`, required): This question asks about the average time you spend on online activities.
- **Most frequent platforms** (`Most frequent platforms`, `select_multiple`, required): This question asks about the platforms you most frequently use for online activities. Select all that apply.
- **Frequency of sharing** (`Frequency of sharing`, `select_one`, required): This question asks about how often you share online. Choose one that best describes your situation.
- **Why don't you share** (`Why don't you share`, `select_multiple`, required): This question is about your reasons for not sharing online. Select all that apply (if you don't share at all).
- **What barriers you face** (`What barriers you face`, `text`, optional): This question is about any other barriers you face when sharing online. Provide a brief description if you have any additional information.
- **Additional comments** (`Additional comments`, `text`, optional): This is an optional field for any additional comments or thoughts you may have about your online knowledge sharing behavior.
