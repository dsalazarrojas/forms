# Grid Plotting Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and insights on experiences, tools, and training related to grid plotting, with the goal of understanding what users find most challenging or useful in these areas.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the answers to each question based on your true experience and opinions.
2. Choose one or multiple options for each question, as indicated by the field type (e.g., select_multiple or select_one).
3. Fill in the text fields with your thoughts and suggestions.
4. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
