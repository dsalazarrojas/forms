# Mobile App User Experience Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the mobile app's user experience, helping developers understand areas for improvement and what features are missing or needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review each field carefully, ensuring you're providing relevant feedback.
2. Fill out the fields as instructed, providing your honest opinion on each aspect of the app.
3. Take your time to ensure you've covered all the important points.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Mobile App User Experience Assessment** (`id: 1`, `title`, `note`, required: false): A brief introduction to the form, providing context for the subsequent questions.
- **app usability** (`id: 2`, `app usability`, `number`, required: false): Rate the app's overall usability, with a scale of 1-5.
- **app satisfaction** (`id: 3`, `app satisfaction`, `number`, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the app, with a scale of 1-5.
- **feature needs** (`id: 4`, `feature needs`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the additional feature(s) you feel are missing or needed in the app.
	* Choose one or more of the options: Additional feature(s) not listed or Feature suggestion.
- **How would you rate the overall usability of the app?** (`id: 5`, `app usability 1`, `number`, required: false): Rate the app's usability, with a scale of 1-5.
- **How would you rate your overall satisfaction with the app?** (`id: 6`, `app satisfaction 1`, `number`, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the app, with a scale of 1-5.
- **How would you rate the ease of navigation in the app?** (`id: 7`, `app usability 2`, `number`, required: false): Rate the app's navigation, with a scale of 1-5.
- **How would you rate the responsiveness of the app?** (`id: 8`, `app usability 3`, `number`, required: false): Rate the app's responsiveness, with a scale of 1-5.
- **Additional feature(s) needed in the app** (`id: 9`, `feature needs 1`, `text`, required: false): Describe any additional features you feel are missing or needed in the app.
- **Feature suggestion** (`id: 10`, `feature needs 2`, `text`, required: false): Suggest any features you'd like to see added to the app.
- **How would you rate the overall experience in the app?** (`id: 11`, `feature needs 3`, `number`, required: false): Rate the overall experience in the app, with a scale of 1-5.
- **How would you rate the overall satisfaction with the app?** (`id: 12`, `feature needs 4`, `number`, required: false): Rate the overall satisfaction with the app, with a scale of 1-5.
- **How likely are you to continue using this app?** (`id: 13`, `feature needs 5`, `number`, required: false): Rate how likely you are to continue using the app, with a scale of 1-5.

## Tips
- Be honest and detailed in your feedback, providing concrete examples or suggestions.
- If you're unsure about a field or question, skip it or provide a brief explanation for why you're uncertain.
- Take your time, as this form is designed to gather in-depth feedback.
