# Accounting Technology Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This survey is designed to gather information about the usage and preferences of accounting technology among organizations. This will help us understand how different systems are being used, what challenges are faced, and what priorities are for the next year.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please complete all fields that are marked as "Required" to ensure that your response is complete.
2. Enter your organization's name, if you feel comfortable providing it.
3. Indicate your primary accounting software used.
4. Select the hosting model for your accounting systems (Cloud, On Premise, or Hybrid).
5. Choose how often you upgrade your accounting software.
6. Select the level of integration with other systems.
7. Indicate the areas where automation is used.
8. Enter your main concerns about data security.
9. If you have an annual budget for accounting technology, enter it here.
10. Indicate if your organization provides formal training for new tools.
11. Select up to three priorities for the next year.
12. Add any additional comments or feedback about accounting technology.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent Information** (Respondent information, text, optional): Please enter your name and any other information about yourself that is relevant to this survey.
* **Organization name** (Organization name, text, optional): Enter your organization's name. This will help us understand the context of your responses.
* **Primary accounting software** (Primary accounting software, text, required): Enter the name of your main accounting software used.
* **Cloud vs On Premise** (Cloud vs On Premise, select_one, required): Choose the hosting model for your accounting systems. Options are Cloud, On Premise, and Hybrid.
* **Frequency of upgrades** (Frequency of upgrades, select_one, optional): Indicate how often you upgrade your accounting software. Options are Quarterly, Annually, and Rarely.
* **Integration Maturity** (Integration maturity, select_one, required): Select the level of integration with other systems. Options are Fully integrated, Partially integrated, and Not integrated.
* **Automation use cases** (Automation use cases, select_multiple, optional): Indicate the areas where automation is used. Options are Invoicing, Bank reconciliation, Payroll, and Reporting.
* **Data Security Concerns** (Data security concerns, text, optional): Enter your main concerns about data security.
* **Budget for technology** (Budget for technology, number, optional): Enter your annual budget for accounting technology.
* **Training Provisions** (Training provisions, select_one, optional): Indicate if your organization provides formal training for new tools. Options are True and False.
* **Top Priorities** (Top priorities, text, optional): Select up to three priorities for the next year.
* **Additional comments** (Additional comments, text, optional): Add any other feedback or comments about accounting technology.

Note: This survey is meant to be completed by individuals with knowledge of their organization's accounting technology usage and decisions. Please ensure that each individual completing the survey is not duplicating efforts, such as by surveying the same people or teams.
