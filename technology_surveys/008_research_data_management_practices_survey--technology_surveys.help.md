# research_data_management_practices_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about research data management practices to better understand the current state of data utilization, security, and compliance in your organization.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form carefully, providing accurate information about your research data management practices.
- Make sure to select all the relevant options that apply to your organization.
- If unsure about a particular section, consult with your team or superiors for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
