# Layout Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and layout information from users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. **Enter your layout feedback**: In the first field, please describe your experience with the layout or design you are providing feedback on.
2. **Rate your experience**: Rate your experience with the layout on a scale of 1-5.
3. **Add any additional comments**: If you have any other comments or suggestions, please add them in the text field.
4. **Enter your contact information**: You can optionally enter your email address and phone number, which will help us get in touch with you for further clarification or follow-up.
5. **Select categories and subcategories**: If you are providing feedback on a specific technology, select the relevant categories and subcategories from the drop-down menus.
6. **Submit your feedback**: Once you have completed all the fields, click the "Submit" button to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **layout_feedback_survey**: A text field where you can provide your feedback on the layout or design. This is a required field.
- **feedback**: A text field where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions.
- **rating**: A field to rate your experience with the layout on a scale of 1-5.
- **email**: An optional field to enter your email address.
- **phone**: An optional field to enter your phone number.
- **submit**: This is a submit button that sends your feedback to us.
- **category**: A drop-down menu to select the category related to the technology used.
- **technology_category**: A multiple-select field to select the relevant categories related to the technology used.
- **technology_subcategory**: A single-select field to select the subcategory related to the technology used.
- **technology_subcategory2**: Another single-select field to select the subcategory related to the technology used.
- **date**: A date field to enter the date when you submitted your feedback.
- **time**: A time field to enter the time when you submitted your feedback.
- **note**: A text field where you can add any additional notes or comments.
- **assigned_to**: A text field to enter the name of the person assigned to review your feedback.
- **created_by**: A single-select field to select the person who created the layout or design.
- **date_created**: A date field to enter the date when the layout or design was created.
- **time_created**: A time field to enter the time when the layout or design was created.
- **date_submitted**: A date field to enter the date when you submitted your feedback.
- **time_submitted**: A time field to enter the time when you submitted your feedback.
- **note_created**: A text field where you can add any additional notes or comments created by you.
- **note_submitted**: A text field where you can add any additional notes or comments submitted by you.
- **note_updated**: A text field where you can add any additional notes or comments updated by you.
- **assigned_tool**: A single-select field to select the tool assigned to review your feedback.
