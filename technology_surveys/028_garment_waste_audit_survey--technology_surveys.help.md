# Garment Waste Audit Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect data on garment waste audits, which is used to identify and address the challenges and opportunities in the garment industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. **Read through the form carefully** and make sure you are familiar with the questions and fields.
2. **Answer each question carefully and honestly**. If you are unsure or don't have information on a particular field, you can leave it blank or provide a comment.
3. **Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Waste Location** (`waste_location`, `text`, required): This field is for entering the location where the waste is being produced. This can be a specific factory, warehouse, or store.
- **Description** (`waste_description`, `text`, required): Provide a brief description of the waste you are reporting on. This can include information on the type of garments, materials, and quantities involved.
- **Waste Quantities** (`waste_quantities`, `number`, required): Enter the quantities of waste being produced. This can be measured in pounds, kilograms, or other units.
- **Disposal Method** (`disposal_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the following methods:
  - Textile Reuse: Recycling of textile materials.
  - Downcycling: Recycling of materials into a lower quality product.
  - Upcycling: Recycling of materials into a higher quality product.
  - Incineration: Burning of waste.
  - Recycling: Recycling of materials into a new product.
  - Landfill: Disposal of waste in a landfill.
  - Other: Choose this option if none of the above apply.
- **Challenges** (`challenges`, `text`, optional): Describe any challenges you are facing in dealing with garment waste.
- **Improvement Ideas** (`improvement_ideas`, `text`, optional): Provide any ideas or suggestions on how to improve the garment waste audit process.
- **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the following:
  - In Progress: You are in the process of submitting the form.
  - Complete: You have finished submitting the form.
  - Not Started: You have not started submitting the form.
