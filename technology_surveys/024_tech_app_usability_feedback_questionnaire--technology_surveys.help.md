# tech_app_usability_feedback_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback about the usability of a technical application (app). Please provide your honest and constructive thoughts about your experience with the app.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your knowledge and experience with the app.
2. Be honest and provide concrete examples when possible.
3. You can submit your feedback anonymously, but please provide your email address and/or phone number to help us contact you if we need more information.
4. If you have any suggestions or requests for new features, please provide them in the text field "tech_app_improvement".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. What are your thoughts about the app? 
**What are your thoughts about the app?** (`tech_app_usability`, text, optional)
Please describe your overall impression of the app. What did you like or dislike about it? 

### 2. What is your satisfaction with the app? 
**What is your satisfaction with the app?** (`user_satisfaction`, select_one, optional)
Choose one of the following options:
	* Very Satisfied
	* Satisfied
	* Neutral
	* Dissatisfied
	* Very Dissatisfied

### 3. How easy was it to use the app? 
**How easy was it to use the app?** (`app_usability`, select_multiple, optional)
Choose all that apply:
	* Very Easy
	* Somewhat Easy
	* Neutral
	* Somewhat Difficult
	* Very Difficult

### 4. What features would you add or remove from the app? 
**What features would you add or remove from the app?** (`tech_app_improvement`, text, optional)
Please provide any suggestions you have for new features or features you would like to remove.

### 5. What do you think about this feedback questionnaire? 
**What do you think about this feedback questionnaire?** (`user_feedback`, note, optional)
Please provide any additional comments or suggestions about this form.

### 6. Your email address (optional) 
**Your email address** (`email_address`, email, optional)
Please provide your email address if you would like to be contacted about your feedback.

### 7. Your phone number (optional) 
**Your phone number** (`phone_number`, text, optional)
Please provide your phone number if you would like to be contacted about your feedback.

### 8. Your name (optional) 
**Your name** (`user_name`, text, optional)
Please provide your name if you would like to be contacted about your feedback.
