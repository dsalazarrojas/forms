> # Editing Tools Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your team's editing tools needs and preferences. The goal is to assess how well your team's editing tools are meeting their requirements and identify areas where improvements can be made. The form will help us better understand your team's needs and provide guidance on tools that would be beneficial for your team.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your company name in the "Company Name" field.
2. List the software tools your team is currently using in the "Software Used" field.
3. Indicate the number of team members in the "Team Members" field.
4. Evaluate your team's satisfaction with the current software tools in the "Team Satisfaction" field.
5. Specify the training needs for your team in the "Training Needs" field.
6. Identify the features needed for your team in the "Features Needed" field.
7. Select the team size in the "Team Size" field.
8. Rate your satisfaction with the current software tools in the "Software Satisfaction" field.
9. Choose the features that your team would like to have in the "Feature Requests" field.
10. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
11. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
12. Select the tools currently assigned to your team in the "Assigned Tool" field.
13. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name (1)**: Enter your company name.
* **Software Used (2)**: List the software tools your team is currently using.
* **Team Members (3)**: Enter the number of team members.
* **Team Satisfaction (4)**: Evaluate your team's satisfaction with the current software tools. (Optional, but can help inform our recommendations.)
* **Training Needs (5)**: Specify the training needs for your team.
* **Features Needed (6)**: Identify the features needed for your team.
* **Team Size (7)**: Select the team size.
* **Software Satisfaction (8)**: Rate your satisfaction with the current software tools.
* **Feature Requests (9)**: Choose the features that your team would like to have.
* **Email (11)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (12)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Assigned Tool (13)**: Select the tools currently assigned to your team.
* **Notes (14)**: Enter any additional comments or notes.
