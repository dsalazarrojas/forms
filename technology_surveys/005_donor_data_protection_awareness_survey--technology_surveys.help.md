# Donor Data Protection Awareness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form aims to gather information about the awareness of donors regarding data protection practices at our institution. The survey is designed to help us understand your understanding and expectations around how we handle your personal data, including collection, retention, and sharing of data with third parties. Please take a few minutes to go through the questions and provide honest answers to help us improve our data protection practices.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply answer the questions as they appear. Most fields are optional, and you can skip any question that doesn't apply to you. If you need any clarification or have any questions, feel free to contact us for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **1. Donor Data Protection Awareness Survey** (Text)
This is the title of the survey, which is displayed at the top of the page. You can read the introduction below to understand what this survey is about.

### **2. Introduction** (Note)
This is a brief introduction to the survey. Please read it carefully to understand the purpose and context of the questions that follow.

### **3. Do you consent to our data collection practices?** (Select One)
Do you agree or decline to our data collection practices? This question is optional, but it helps us understand your willingness to share your data with us.

### **4. What is the purpose of your data collection?** (Select Multiple)
We collect data for various purposes. Please select all that apply to help us understand why we are collecting your data. The options include:

*   Marketing
*   Fundraising
*   Research
*   Other (please specify)

### **5. What do you understand about our data protection practices?** (Text)
This question is for you to describe your understanding of our data protection practices. Feel free to answer in a few sentences.

### **6. Do you share your data with third parties?** (Select One)
This question is about whether you share your data with third parties. Please answer with either "True" or "False".

### **7. How secure is your data?** (Select One)
We ask about your perception of the security of your data. Please select one of the options:

*   Very secure
*   Somewhat secure
*   Not secure at all

### **8. Do you have control over your data?** (Select One)
This question is about your level of control over your data. Please select one of the options:

*   Complete control
*   Limited control
*   No control

### **9. How long do we retain your data?** (Select One)
We ask about the duration of data retention. Please select one of the options:

*   Indefinite
*   Finite ( specify duration)

### **10. How long do we delete your data?** (Select One)
This question is about data deletion. Please select one of the options:

*   Never
*   On request
*   1 year

### **11. Access to your data?** (Select Multiple)
We ask about who has access to your data. Please select all that apply. The options include:

*   You
*   Staff
*   Third party

### **12. Data transfer?** (Select Multiple)
This question is about data transfer. Please select all that apply. The options include:

*   False
*   True

### **13. Compliance?** (Select Multiple)
We ask about the compliance of our data protection practices. Please select all that apply. The options include:

*   GDPR
*   CCPA
*   HIPAA

### **14. Do you want us to use data for analysis?** (Select One)
This question is about your consent to use data for analysis. Please answer with either "True" or "False".

### **15. Do you want automation of our data practices?** (Select One)
This question is about your consent to automate our data practices. Please answer with either "True" or "False".

### **16. What do you think is the most important security measure?** (Text)
This question is for you to describe the most important security measure in your opinion. Feel free to answer in a few sentences.

### **17. What do you think is the next most important security measure?** (Text)
This question is for you to describe the next most important security measure in your opinion. Feel free to answer in a few sentences.

### **18. Comments** (Text)
This is a space for you to provide any additional comments or suggestions about our data protection practices.
