# Solar Forecasting Location Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect data on a specific location's solar forecasting capabilities. This information will help us better understand the site's solar potential and optimize solar forecasting models for improved accuracy.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please provide the location name in the text field labeled "Location Name".
2. In the "Site Details" text field, enter any relevant information about the site, such as its description, features, or notable points.
3. Enter the site's zip code in the numeric field labeled "Zip Code". Ensure to enter only numerical characters (e.g. 10001 or 30000).
4. For "Local Time Zone", enter the relevant time zone for the location (e.g. Eastern Time Zone).
5. In the "Elevation MSL" field, enter the location's mean sea level elevation.
6. Select the orientation of the site by choosing one or more of the available options in the "Orientation" field (e.g. north, south, east, west).
7. Enter the solar altitude of the location in the "Solar Altitude" field.
8. Enter the slope angle of the location in the "Slope Angle" field.
9. Enter the azimuth (compass direction) of the location in the "Azimuth" field.
10. If available, select the solar forecast data date range in the "Solar Forecast Data" field.
11. In the "Additional Notes" text field, enter any additional information or comments about the location.
12. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, so we can contact you with any further questions or updates.
13. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field, for any follow-up conversations or clarifications.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Location Name** (`location_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the location.
* **Site Details** (`site_details`, note, optional): Enter any relevant information about the site.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, number, optional): Enter the site's zip code.
* **Local Time Zone** (`local_time_zone`, text, optional): Enter the relevant time zone for the location.
* **Elevation MSL** (`elevation_msl`, number, optional): Enter the location's mean sea level elevation.
* **Orientation** (`orientation`, select_multiple, required): Select the orientation of the site (e.g. north, south, east, west).
* **Solar Altitude** (`solar_altitude`, number, optional): Enter the solar altitude of the location.
* **Slope Angle** (`slope_angle`, number, optional): Enter the slope angle of the location.
* **Azimuth** (`azimuth`, number, optional): Enter the azimuth (compass direction) of the location.
* **Solar Forecast Data** (`solar_forecast_data`, date, optional): If available, select the solar forecast data date range.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional information or comments about the location.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.

## Tips

* Double-check the information you enter to ensure accuracy.
* If unsure about any field, refer to the instructions above.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, contact us at the email address provided above.
