# Compression Settings Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Compression Settings Survey, a form designed to gather information about your compression settings and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reviewing the form and understanding its purpose.
2. Read each section carefully and select the answers that best fit your situation.
3. Fill out the form by selecting or typing in the required fields.
4. Review your completed form for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Primary use case** (`primary_use_case`): This field is required and asks about your typical use case for compressing files. Please select the option that best describes your main purpose for compressing files.
* **Preferred compression level** (`preferred_compression_level`): This field is required and allows you to select your typical compression settings. Choose from the options: High compression, Balanced, or Fast compression.
* **File types compressed** (`file_types_compressed`): This field is optional and asks you to select the types of files you commonly compress. You can select multiple options by holding down the Ctrl key (or Command key on a Mac) and clicking on multiple items.
* **Satisfaction with results** (`satisfaction_with_results`): This field is required and asks you to rate your satisfaction with your current compression tools. Select from the options: Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, or Dissatisfied.
* **Issues encountered** (`issues_encountered`): This field is optional and allows you to describe common problems or limitations you've faced when compressing files.
* **Desired features** (`desired_features`): This field is optional and asks you to describe features that would improve your workflow when compressing files.
* **Respondent role** (`respondent_role`): This field is optional and asks about your role or job function.
* **Usage Context** (`section_usage_context`): This field is optional and is a note section where you can provide additional information about your usage context.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all required fields to ensure your feedback is complete and accurate.
* If you're unsure about a field, refer back to the form instructions and review the field explanation.
* Take your time to provide thoughtful and honest answers to help us better understand your compression settings and preferences.
