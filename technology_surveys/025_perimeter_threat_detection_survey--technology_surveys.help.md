# perimeter_threat_detection_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The perimeter threat detection survey is a form designed to collect information about an organization's perimeter threat detection systems and processes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Existing Systems** (`existing_systems`, text): This field is used to report on the existing systems and technologies used for perimeter threat detection.
2. **Incident History** (`incident_history`, text): Describe the history of incidents related to perimeter threat detection within your organization.
3. **Concerns** (`concerns`, text): Identify any concerns or issues related to perimeter threat detection within your organization.
4. **Detection Capabilities** (`detection_capabilities`, text): Describe the capabilities of your organization's perimeter threat detection systems.
5. If you have filled out similar surveys before, please ensure that you provide new, relevant information. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Existing Systems**: This field asks about the systems and technologies you use for detecting and responding to threats outside your organization's perimeter. For example, you can describe your use of network intrusion detection systems, firewalls, or other security measures.
* **Incident History**: Describe any significant incidents related to perimeter threats that have occurred within your organization. This includes attacks, security breaches, or other events that required response and mitigation efforts.
* **Concerns**: Identify any concerns or issues you have with your current perimeter threat detection systems or processes. This can include technical difficulties, performance issues, or concerns about detection capabilities.
* **Detection Capabilities**: This field is for describing the capabilities of your perimeter threat detection systems, including their strengths and weaknesses.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
* Ensure that you have access to relevant information before filling out this form.
* Provide concrete examples and specific details when answering questions.
* Review and update your answers as needed to ensure they reflect your organization's current situation.
* If you have any questions or concerns about this survey, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
