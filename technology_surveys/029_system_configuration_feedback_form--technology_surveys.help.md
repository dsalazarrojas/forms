# System Configuration Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the system's configuration, specifically on its performance, user interface, and overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. Select the "System Setup" options that apply to your experience with the system.
3. Answer each page's text fields with as much detail as possible.
4. Fill out as many pages as you have experience with.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **System Config** (`system_config_form`, text, optional): Enter your thoughts or comments on the system's configuration.
- **System Setup** (`system_setup`, select multiple, optional): Select the options that apply to your experience with the system's setup.

  - 2 - High: The system's setup is high in quality.
  - 3 - Medium: The system's setup is medium in quality.
  - 4 - Low: The system's setup is low in quality.
