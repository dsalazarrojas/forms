# Scrum Task Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your team's Scrum process and experiences. Your input will help us understand your team's strengths and areas for improvement, enabling us to provide better support and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions truthfully, and provide as much detail as possible.
2. For numerical questions, please respond on a scale from 1 to 10, where 1 is "Not Satisfied" and 10 is "Extremely Satisfied".
3. For text questions, please provide brief but clear responses.
4. If you are unsure about any question, please ask your team lead or scrum master for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

This is a brief introduction to the survey, explaining its purpose and importance.
