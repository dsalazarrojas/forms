# Responsible Technology Use Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering the responsible individuals for technology use. It helps us to track who is using our technology and ensures that the responsible individuals have the necessary training and qualifications to use it securely.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
2. Be accurate when filling out the fields, especially the user type and training level.
3. Make sure to submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (text, required): Your name is the first field in this form. Please enter your name as it appears on your official documentation.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address that is associated with your organization or user account.
* **Phone Number** (text, required): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **User Type** (select, required): Choose your user type from the available options: School, Nonprofit, Training Team.
* **Date** (date, required): Enter the date you have completed the required training for our technology.
* **Hours Trained** (number, optional): If you have additional training hours beyond what's required, please enter them here.
* **Program Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the program you are registered for.
* **Program Description** (text, optional): Briefly describe the program you are registered for.
* **Training Level** (select, required): Choose the level of training you have completed from the available options: Basic, Intermediate, Advanced, Expert, Master.
* **Certification Status** (select, required): Choose your current certification status from the available options: Pending, Active, Expired, Not Available.
* **Notes** (text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your registration.
* **Submitted By** (text, optional): Enter the name of the person submitting this form.
* **Submitted On** (date, optional): Enter the date this form was submitted.
* **Submitted By Email** (email, optional): Enter the email address of the person submitting this form.
* **Submitted By Phone** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person submitting this form.

Note: Please make sure to review all fields carefully before submitting the form. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
