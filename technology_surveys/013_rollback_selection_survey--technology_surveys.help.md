# Rollback Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rollback Selection Survey is a crucial tool for gathering information on rollbacks, which are critical incidents where a project or process is reverted or undone. The data collected through this form will help teams evaluate the reasons behind these incidents and make improvements to minimize their occurrence in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form effectively, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections and familiarize yourself with the fields and their labels.
2. Select the relevant options for each field as instructed.
3. Provide clear and accurate information where required.
4. Double-check your responses for consistency and coherence.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Main** (`1`, `text`, optional)
  This field is a text box where you can provide a brief description of the main topic or context of the rollback.

- **Rollback Decisions** (`2`, `select_one`, not required)
  Choose one of the provided options that best describes the reason for the rollback.

- **Reasons** (`3`, `select_multiple`, not required)
  Select all applicable reasons for the rollback from the provided list.

- **Business Impact** (`4`, `text`, not required)
  Describe the business impact or consequences of the rollback in detail.

- **Comments** (`5`, `note`, not required)
  This field is for any additional comments or information you'd like to provide.

- **Email** (`6`, `email`, not required)
  If you're providing a response on behalf of someone else, please enter their email address here.

- **Phone** (`7`, `text`, not required)
  Enter your phone number or the phone number of the person providing this information.

- **Assigned To** (`8`, `select_multiple`, not required)
  Select the person(s) assigned to follow up on this rollback.

- **Submitted By** (`9`, `select_one`, not required)
  Choose who provided this submission from the list of options.
