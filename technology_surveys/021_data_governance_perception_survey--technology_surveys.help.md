# data_governance_perception_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The data governance perception survey is designed to gather information about data governance practices and perceptions within your organization. The survey will help us to understand your understanding and current practices related to data governance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read each field carefully and enter your answers accordingly.
2. Fill in all required fields (indicated as "required" in the field description).
3. Enter your answers in the format requested for each field.
4. Click submit to send your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Introduction** (1)
* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `text`, required: false): This field is a brief introduction to the survey.

### **organization_name** (2)
* **organization_name** (`organization_name`, `text`, required: false): This field is to capture the name of your organization.

### **question_1** (3)
* **question_1** (`Do you have a data governance strategy in place?`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  * This question is asking about the existence of a data governance strategy within your organization.
  * Select one of the following options: "True", "False", "Partially".

### **question_2** (4)
* **question_2** (`What types of data is your organization responsible for governing?`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
  * This question is asking about the types of data your organization is responsible for governing.
  * Select all that apply from the following options: "Financial data", "Customer data", "Product data".

### **name** (5)
* **name** (`Name`, `text`, required: false): This field is to capture your name.

### **email** (6)
* **email** (`Email`, `email`, required: false): This field is to capture your email address.

### **phone** (7)
* **phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required: false): This field is to capture your phone number.

### **question_3** (8)
* **question_3** (`How does your organization currently track and manage data?`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  * This question is asking about how your organization currently tracks and manages data.
  * Select one of the following options: "Centralized database", "Dispersed databases", "No formal tracking system".

### **note** (9)
* **note** (`Additional information`, `note`, required: false): This field is for any additional comments or information you'd like to provide.

### **date** (10)
* **date** (`Date`, `date`, required: false): This field is to capture the date you completed the survey.

### **time** (11)
* **time** (`Time`, `time`, required: false): This field is to capture the time you completed the survey.

### **organization_size** (12)
* **organization_size** (`Organization Size`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  * This question is asking about the size of your organization.
  * Select one of the following options: "Small", "Medium", "Large".

### **location** (13)
* **location** (`Location`, `text`, required: false): This field is to capture your location.

### **comments** (14)
* **comments** (`Comments`, `text`, required: false): This field is for any additional comments or information you'd like to provide.
