# Institutional Digital Ethics Awareness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Institutional Digital Ethics Awareness Survey is designed to gauge participants' awareness and understanding of digital ethics within their institution. This survey aims to gather information on digital ethics practices, policies, and procedures, as well as the level of awareness and confidence of participants in relation to digital ethics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions honestly and accurately.
2. Select the appropriate options that best reflect your understanding and experiences.
3. Complete all the questions in the survey.
4. Submit the form once you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey**: This is the title of the survey.
* **Introduction**: A brief introduction to the survey and its purpose.
* **Participant Information**: This section is not required and is optional for you to provide any additional information about yourself.
* **What does digital ethics mean to you?**: This question is an open-ended text field for you to describe your understanding of digital ethics.
* **How important is digital ethics in your daily work?**: Please select a number that best reflects how important digital ethics is in your daily work.
* **Are you aware of any digital ethics policies in your institution?**: Select 'Yes' if you are aware of digital ethics policies in your institution.
* **Have you received any digital ethics training?**: Select 'Yes' if you have received digital ethics training.
* **Have you received any digital ethics resources on campus?**: Select all options that apply if you have received digital ethics resources on campus.
* **Do you have a clear understanding of digital ethics in your role?**: Select 'Yes' if you have a clear understanding of digital ethics in your role.
* **Have you reported any digital ethics concerns to your supervisor or colleague?**: Select 'Yes' if you have reported digital ethics concerns to your supervisor or colleague.
* **Have you ever faced a digital ethics related problem?**: Select 'Yes' if you have ever faced a digital ethics related problem.
* **Are there digital ethics guidelines on your work processes and activities?**: Select 'Yes' if there are digital ethics guidelines on your work processes and activities.
* **Would you like to learn more about digital ethics?**: Select 'Yes' if you would like to learn more about digital ethics.
* **Do you feel confident that your institution values digital ethics?**: Select 'Yes' if you feel confident that your institution values digital ethics.
* **Do you believe that digital ethics should be a priority in the institution?**: Select 'Yes' if you believe digital ethics should be a priority in the institution.
* **Do you have access to digital ethics resources on campus?**: Select all options that apply if you have access to digital ethics resources on campus.
* **Have you ever faced any digital ethics related consequences?**: Select 'Yes' if you have ever faced a digital ethics related consequence.
* **Are digital ethics guidelines and procedures well understood and followed in your work group?**: Select 'Yes' if digital ethics guidelines and procedures are well understood and followed in your work group.
* **Do you feel that digital ethics is well understood and communicated throughout the institution?**: Select 'Yes' if digital ethics is well understood and communicated throughout the institution.
* **Has your supervisor or colleagues ever talked to you about digital ethics?**: Select 'Yes' if your supervisor or colleagues have ever talked to you about digital ethics.
* **Have you received any digital ethics related feedback or guidance?**: Select 'Yes' if you have received digital ethics related feedback or guidance.
* **Do you feel that digital ethics is well integrated into your work processes and activities?**: Select 'Good' or 'Fair' if digital ethics is well integrated into your work processes and activities.
* **Are you aware of any digital ethics policies or guidelines that are not well understood or followed?**: Select 'Yes' if you are aware of any digital ethics policies or guidelines that are not well understood or followed.
* **Have you been part of any digital ethics related projects or initiatives?**: Select 'Yes' if you have been part of digital ethics related projects or initiatives.
* **Do you believe that digital ethics should be a part of your daily work?**: Select 'Yes' if you believe digital ethics should be a part of your daily work.
