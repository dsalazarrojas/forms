# AI Knowledge Extraction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your experience with AI Knowledge Extraction. It will help us understand your needs and challenges in extracting knowledge from various data sources.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please answer all the questions that apply to your situation.
2.  For select multiple questions, you can choose one or more options.
3.  If you have any additional feedback or notes, please use the "Additional feedback or notes" field.
4.  Please be as accurate and detailed as possible in your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Respondent name** (`respondent_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name.
*   **Organization** (`organization`, text, required): Enter the name of your organization or team.
*   **Role or job title** (`role`, text, required): Describe your primary role related to AI work.
*   **Primary AI use cases** (`use_cases`, select_multiple, required): Select all the primary AI use cases that apply to you.
	+ Data extraction: Extracting relevant information from unstructured data sources.
	+ Document summarization: Summarizing long documents to get the main points.
	+ Knowledge base construction: Building a knowledge base from various data sources.
	+ Question answering: Using AI to answer questions from users or systems.
	+ Automation of workflows: Automating tasks using AI to improve efficiency.
*   **Primary data sources** (`data_sources`, select_multiple, required): Select up to three main data sources that you are using for AI extraction tasks.
	+ Internal documents: Documents within your organization.
	+ Databases and tables: Structured data stored in databases or tables.
	+ Support tickets: Customer support tickets.
	+ Email archives: Email communications.
	+ Web pages: Web pages or web applications.
*   **Extraction frequency** (`extraction_frequency`, select_one, required): Indicate how often you perform AI extraction tasks.
	+ One time: Rarely, only once.
	+ Daily: Daily or frequent tasks.
	+ Weekly: Regular tasks.
	+ Monthly: Monthly tasks.
	+ On demand: As needed.
*   **Target accuracy or confidence threshold** (`extraction_accuracy_goal`, number, optional): Enter a target accuracy or confidence threshold (e.g., 90 for 90%).
*   **Common challenges encountered** (`common_challenges`, select_multiple, required): Choose all the common challenges you face during AI extraction tasks.
	+ Noisy data: Data with irrelevant or distracting information.
	+ Unstructured formats: Data in non-standard or unstructured formats.
	+ Data privacy concerns: Protecting sensitive or private data.
	+ Lack of annotations: Missing or incomplete metadata.
	+ Integration complexity: Integrating AI extraction with other systems or processes.
*   **Desired output formats** (`desired_outputs`, select_multiple, required): Choose all the desired output formats from your AI extraction tasks.
	+ Structured records: Data in a structured format for easy retrieval.
	+ Summaries: Brief summaries of the extracted data.
	+ Knowledge graph: A knowledge graph representing relationships between data entities.
	+ Search index: A searchable index of extracted data.
	+ API responses: Data in a format suitable for API consumption.
*   **Additional feedback or notes** (`additional_feedback`, text, optional): Share any additional feedback or notes about your experience with AI Knowledge Extraction.
