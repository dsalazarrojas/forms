# NGO Technology Capacity Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your organization's technology capacity and needs. It will help us understand your current technology resources, staff skills, and plans for implementing new technologies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question to the best of your ability. You can provide as much or as little information as you have available.
2. If you are unsure about any question, please feel free to ask for clarification.
3. Be as specific as possible when providing answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Intro** (`intro`, text, required: false): A brief introduction to your organization and its current state of technology capacity.
- **Org Information** (`org information`, text, required: false): Information about your organization, such as its name, location, or mission.
- **Tech Resources** (`tech res`, text, required: false): Describe your current technology resources and tools, such as software, hardware, and equipment.
- **Staff Digital Skills** (`staff skills`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate the level of digital skills of your staff members, such as programming languages, software proficiency, or data analysis skills.
- **Support Needs** (`support needs`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the types of technical support your organization needs, such as help desk, email, or online resources.
- **Budget** (`budget`, number, required: false): Provide an estimate of your budget for technology investments.
- **Timeframe** (`timeframe`, date, required: false): Indicate your expected timeframe for implementing new technologies, such as short-term, medium-term, or long-term.
- **Timeframe Details** (`timeframe details`, text, required: false): Provide more details about your expected timeframe, such as specific deadlines or milestones.
- **Org Size** (`org size`, text, required: false): Indicate the number of staff members in your organization.
- **Tech Plan** (`tech plan`, select_one, required: false): Select whether you have a technology plan in place or not, such as a document, a process, or no plan.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when providing information about your organization's technology capacity and needs.
- If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.
- You can provide additional comments or explanations in the "Timeframe Details" field if you have more information about your expected timeframe.
