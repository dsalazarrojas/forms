# Voice To Invoice Automation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to help us understand your current workflow and experiences with invoice automation. Your input will guide the development and deployment of invoice automation technology that caters to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please read the survey carefully and answer each question honestly.
2. Select the options that best describe your current practices and experiences.
3. Fill out the form with your feedback and observations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, text, optional): This field is a space for you to provide a brief introduction or context about your current workflow and any relevant background information.
* **Workflow Description** (`workflow_description`, text, optional): Describe your current workflow related to invoicing and automation.
* **Billing Method** (`billing_method`, select_one, optional): Select the method used for invoicing from the options: Automated, Manual, Other. For example, you might choose Automated if you use automatic invoicing systems.
* **Invoice Frequency** (`invoice_frequency`, select_one, optional): Select the frequency at which you currently issue invoices from the options: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Other. Choose the option that best describes your current practice.
* **Invoice Amount** (`invoice_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of the invoices you issue.
* **Automation Readiness** (`automation_readiness`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate your level of readiness for invoice automation from the options: High, Medium, Low, Undecided. This will help us understand your level of comfort with automation.
* **Adoption Concerns** (`adoption_concerns`, select_multiple, optional): Select any concerns you have about adopting automation from the options: True, False, Undecided. This will help us understand your reservations and potential challenges.
* **Internal Process Planning** (`internal_process_planning`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you have a plan in place for automation from the options: True, False, Undecided. This will help us understand your preparedness for automation.
* **Product Research** (`product_research`, select_one, optional): Indicate the level of research you have done on product offerings related to automation from the options: True, False, Undecided. This will help us gauge your awareness and engagement with available options.
* **Customer Discovery** (`customer_discovery`, select_one, optional): Indicate your level of engagement with customers regarding automation from the options: True, False, Undecided. This will help us understand your customer-centric approach to automation.
* **Planning** (`planning`, time, optional): Provide a rough estimate of the time you have dedicated to planning for automation, if any.
