# Emerging Technology Integration Voting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback on the current state and future plans for Emerging Technology within your organization. It helps to identify areas of strength, opportunities for improvement, and potential challenges. Please be honest and thorough in your answers to provide valuable input.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each field carefully, taking into account the field's purpose and requirements.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3. You can skip optional fields, but filling them out can provide additional valuable insights.
4. Review your answers to ensure accuracy and consistency before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Please describe the current state of Emerging Technology in your organization**: Please provide a detailed description of the current state of Emerging Technology in your organization, including its adoption, benefits, and limitations.
* **What is your role in the organization (e.g., developer, manager, user)?**: Please select your role in the organization to help us understand your perspective and level of interaction with Emerging Technology.
* **How familiar are you with Emerging Technology (on a scale of 1-10)?**: Please rate your familiarity with Emerging Technology to gauge your level of understanding and expertise.
* **What are the key benefits of Emerging Technology (e.g., automation, security, scalability)?**: Please list the key benefits of Emerging Technology that you've observed or experienced in your organization.
* **What are some key use cases for Emerging Technology (e.g., data analytics, AI, IoT)?**: Please provide examples of how Emerging Technology is being used or could be used within your organization.
* **How important is Emerging Technology for our business (on a scale of 1-10)?**: Please rate the importance of Emerging Technology for your organization.
* **Which Emerging Technology is the most relevant to our business (select one)?**: Please select the Emerging Technology that you believe is most relevant to your organization.
* **Which Emerging Technologies are most relevant to our business (select multiple)?**: Please select all Emerging Technologies that you believe are relevant to your organization.
* **What is the expected date of adoption for Emerging Technology in our organization?**: Please enter the expected date of adoption for Emerging Technology.
* **What time of day do you think Emerging Technology will be widely adopted in our organization?**: This field is optional, but providing an estimate can help us gauge the timing of its adoption.
* **Do you have any additional comments or concerns regarding Emerging Technology?**: Please use this field to provide any further thoughts or concerns you may have about Emerging Technology.
