# IT Awareness And Trust Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This survey is designed to collect your feedback and insights on your awareness and trust of IT-related topics.

## How To Complete This Form

1. For each question, select the option that best answers your response.
2. Fill in all required fields (marked as 'required' in brackets).
3. You can submit your responses at any time, but keep in mind that your responses will be kept confidential and will not be used for any purpose other than to improve our services.
