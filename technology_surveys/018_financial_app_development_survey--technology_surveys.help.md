# Financial App Development Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback and ratings from users of financial apps to help us improve our product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions below to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name.
* **Age** (`age`, number, optional): Please select your age group.
* **Email** (`email`, text, optional): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number.
* **Financial Institution** (`financial_institution`, select_one, optional): Please select your financial institution from the list.
* **Products Used** (`product_used`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the financial products you have used.
* **Product Type** (`product_type`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the type of financial products you have used.
* **How Long Have You Used** (`how_long_have_used`, select_one, optional): Please select how long you have used the financial products.
* **How Often Do You Use** (`how_often_use`, select_one, optional): Please select how often you use the financial products.
* **Rate Experience** (`rate_experience`, select_one, optional): Please rate your experience with the financial products.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, optional): Please provide any additional feedback about your experience with the financial products.
* **Feature Priorities** (`feature_priorities`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the features that are most important to you.
* **Usability Improvements** (`usability_improvements`, select_multiple, optional): Please suggest areas for usability improvement.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): Please provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Financial Institution Rating** (`financial_institution_rating`, number, optional): Please rate your financial institution.
* **Product Reliability** (`product_reliability`, number, optional): Please rate the reliability of the financial product.
* **Product Feasibility** (`product_feasibility`, number, optional): Please rate the feasibility of the financial product.
* **Product Usability** (`product_usability`, number, optional): Please rate the usability of the financial product.
* **Product Ease of Use** (`product_ease_of_use`, number, optional): Please rate the ease of use of the financial product.
* **Product Overall** (`product_overall`, number, optional): Please rate your overall satisfaction with the financial product.
* **Financial Institution Service** (`financial_institution_service`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the services offered by your financial institution.
* **Product Suggestion** (`product_suggestion`, select_one, optional): Please suggest a feature or improvement for the financial product.
