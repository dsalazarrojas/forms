# email_service_provider_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Email Service Provider Survey is designed to collect feedback and data about your experience with email services such as Gmail, Outlook, Office 365, Yahoo, and Hotmail. This information will help us better understand your needs and preferences when it comes to email services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select the email service provider you currently use**: From the dropdown menu, choose the email service provider that you currently use for your email needs.
2. **Rate the satisfaction with your current service**: Use the number rating scale to rate how satisfied you are with your current service provider.
3. **Choose your feature preferences**: Select all the features that you would like to have in your ideal email service.
4. **Describe your pain points**: Provide specific issues or difficulties you face while using your current email service.
5. **Indicate reasons for choosing your current service**: Tick the box if you are currently using your selected email service.
6. **Compare features and provide a breakdown**: In the text field, describe your experience with your current service and how it compares with other services.
7. **Describe your ideal email service**: In the text field, describe your ideal email service provider and what features you would like to see.
8. **Compare services and provide a breakdown**: In the text field, describe how you compare your current service with other services.
9. **Describe your pain points in detail**: In the text field, provide specific details about your pain points with your current service.
10. **Prioritize features**: Select all the features that are most important to you.
11. **Confirm completion of the form**: Please type 'yes' to confirm that you have completed the form.
