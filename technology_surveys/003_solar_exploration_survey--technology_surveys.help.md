# solar_exploration_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects user feedback and data for the solar exploration survey.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form by providing the required information for each field.
2. Select all applicable options for the demographics, education, department, affiliation, role, experience, and comments 2 fields.
3. Answer the rating question.
4. Provide a comment or feedback in the comments field.
5. Select the consent options.
6. Submit the form.
