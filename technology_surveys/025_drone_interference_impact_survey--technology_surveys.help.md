# Drone Interference Impact Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about drone incidents, including the date and location of the incident, the type of drone involved, and the contact details of the person reporting the incident. This data is used to help us better understand and address the impact of drone interference.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the first page of the form.
2. Fill in the following fields:
	* Incident Date: The date of the incident.
	* Drone Type: The type of drone involved (A, B, or C).
3. Continue to the next page of the form.
4. Fill in the following fields:
	* Incident Location: The location where the incident occurred.
	* Incident Description: A brief description of the incident.
	* First Contact?/Second Contact?/Third Contact?: Indicate if the contact was made with the drone's pilot or a representative.
	* Incident Image: Attach any relevant image(s) to the incident.
5. Fill in the following fields on the second page of the form:
	* Contact Phone: The phone number of the person reporting the incident.
	* Contact Email: The email address of the person reporting the incident.
	* Additional Information: Any additional information about the incident.
6. Fill in the following fields on the third page of the form:
	* Category: The category of the incident (e.g. "Drone Incident").
	* Subcategory: The subcategory of the incident (e.g. "Drone Interference").
	* Description: A brief description of the incident.
	* Drone Location: The location where the incident occurred.
	* Contact Name: The name of the person reporting the incident.
	* Contact Number: The phone number of the person reporting the incident.
	* Contact Address: The address of the person reporting the incident.
	* Contact City: The city of the person reporting the incident.
	* Contact State: The state of the person reporting the incident.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Attach relevant images to help us better understand the incident.
* If you have any additional information, please use the "Additional Information" field.
* Make sure to fill in your contact information accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for help.
