# Agricultural Innovation Recognition Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your name and organization name (if applicable).
2. Enter a short title for the innovation.
3. Briefly describe the innovation and its purpose.
4. List the expected benefits of the innovation for agriculture.
5. Choose the development stage of the innovation (Research, Prototype, Pilot, or Commercial).
6. If applicable, estimate the impact scale of the innovation (Local, Regional, National, or International).
7. Describe any supporting evidence or trials related to the innovation.
8. Enter your contact email for follow-up questions or further information.
9. Confirm your permission to publish a summary of the innovation in reports or awards.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Respondent name
**Respondent name** (`respondent_name`, text, optional): This is the name of the individual completing the form. If you prefer to remain anonymous, you can leave this field blank.

### Organization
**Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): This is the name of the organization that has developed or is promoting the innovation.

### Innovation title
**Innovation title** (`innovation_title`, text, true): This is a short name for the innovation.

### Innovation summary
**Innovation summary** (`innovation_summary`, text, true): Briefly describe the innovation and its purpose.

### Innovation benefits
**Innovation benefits** (`innovation_benefits`, text, true): Describe the expected benefits of the innovation for agriculture.

### Readiness level
**Readiness level** (`readiness_level`, select_one, true): Choose the development stage of the innovation from the following options: Research, Prototype, Pilot, or Commercial.

### Impact scale
**Impact scale** (`impact_scale`, select_one, false): Estimate the impact scale of the innovation, if applicable. Choose from the following options: Local, Regional, National, or International.

### Supporting evidence
**Supporting evidence available** (`supporting_evidence`, text, false): Describe any trials, data, or case studies that support the innovation.

### Contact email
**Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, false): Enter your contact email for follow-up questions or further information.

### Consent to publish
**Consent to publish** (`consent_publish`, select_one, true): Confirm your permission to publish a summary of the innovation in reports or awards.

Note: Make sure to review the field explanations and requirements before completing the form to ensure that you have provided accurate and complete information.
