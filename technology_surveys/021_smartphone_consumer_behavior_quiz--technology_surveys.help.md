# Smartphone Consumer Behavior Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form, "Smartphone Consumer Behavior Quiz", is designed to collect insights into smartphone user preferences and behavior. The goal is to gather information about how users interact with their smartphones, their preferred features, and their overall experience with the device. This data will help us better understand the needs and expectations of smartphone users, which can inform product development and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Select the options that best fit your preferences and behavior.
2. Enter your age, phone number, and email address (if applicable).
3. Answer the questions about your phone usage, phone type, and other related topics.
4. Provide additional comments if you have any other information you'd like to share.
5. Review your responses carefully to ensure they accurately reflect your preferences and experiences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, not required): Enter your phone number if you wish to participate in follow-up studies or surveys.
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, not required): Select your age to help us understand the demographics of our audience.
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, not required): Choose your gender to help us understand the diversity of our audience.
* **How often do you use your phone?** (`phone_usage`, `select_multiple`, not required): Choose how often you use your phone, such as Daily, Several times a week, Rarely, or Never.
* **What size screen do you prefer?** (`screen_size`, `select_one`, not required): Select the size screen you prefer for your phone, such as Small, Medium, Large, or Extra Large.
* **What operating system do you use on your phone?** (`phone_os`, `select_one`, not required): Choose the operating system you use on your phone, such as iOS, Android, or other.
* **How do you prefer to use social media?** (`social_media`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select how you prefer to use social media, such as Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, or Other.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, not required): Enter your email address if you wish to participate in follow-up studies or surveys.
* **Location** (`location`, `note`, not required): Share any additional information about your location (e.g., city, country) to help us better understand your context.
* **How often do you use a smartphone?** (`device_usage`, `select_multiple`, not required): Choose how often you use a smartphone, such as Always, Often, Sometimes, or Rarely.
* **What percentage of battery do you use daily?** (`battery_life`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select the percentage of battery you use daily, such as 0-10, 10-20, 20-50, or More than 50.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, not required): Share any additional comments or thoughts about your smartphone experience.
* **Confirm Phone Number** (`confirm_phone_number`, `text`, not required): Confirm your phone number to ensure accuracy (optional).
