# tech_product_user_experience_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user experience reports for tech products. It aims to gather information about the user's experience with a product, including the product's performance, any issues encountered, and suggestions for improvement. By filling out this form, users can help us understand their needs and make the product better suited for their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing a brief **Introduction** to your experience with the product.
2. Next, describe the **Product** you are reporting about, including its name and any relevant details.
3. Share your **User Experience**, including any issues you have encountered while using the product.
4. Suggest improvements to the product, if any.
5. Enter your **Email** address so that we can contact you for further clarification, if needed.
6. If applicable, provide your **Phone** number for easy contact.
7. Indicate whether the **Tech** product **Info** is available or not.
8. Choose whether this experience occurred on a **Tech** **Page** or not.
9. Select whether you have encountered an **Issue** during the experience.
10. Enter your **Product Info 2**, if you have more product details to share.
11. Select whether you have more **Product** details to share.
12. Enter any **Technical Details** about the product.
13. Enter your **Email 2** address for further contact, if needed.
14. If you have more details, enter your **Phone 2** number.
15. Finally, provide any additional **Feedback 2** and click on **Submit** to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title**: Tech Product User Experience Report Form
* **Introduction (1)**: This is the initial page where users can provide a brief introduction to their experience with the product.
* **Product (2)**: Enter the name of the product you are reporting about.
* **User (3)**: Share your experience with the product, including any issues you have encountered.
* **Issues (4)**: Describe any issues you have encountered while using the product.
* **Improvement (5)**: Suggest improvements to the product.
* **Email (6)**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you for further clarification, if needed.
* **Phone (7)**: If applicable, provide your phone number for easy contact.
* **Tech (8)**: Indicate whether the product is tech-related or not.
* **Page (9)**: Choose whether the experience occurred on a product page or not.
* **Issue (10)**: Select whether you have encountered an issue during the experience.
* **Product Info 2 (11)**: Enter any additional product information, if you have more details to share.
* **Product (12)**: Select whether you have more product details to share.
* **Technical Details (13)**: Enter any technical details about the product.
* **Email 2 (14)**: Enter your email address for further contact, if needed.
* **Phone 2 (15)**: If you have more details, enter your phone number.
* **Feedback 2 (16)**: Provide any additional feedback.
* **Submit (17)**: Click on this button to complete and submit the form.
