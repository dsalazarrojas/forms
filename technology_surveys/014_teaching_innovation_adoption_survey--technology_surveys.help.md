# Teaching Innovation Adoption Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Teaching Innovation Adoption Survey is designed to collect feedback from educators on the adoption and implementation of new teaching methods and technologies in their educational settings. This survey aims to understand the challenges and barriers faced by educators in adopting innovative teaching practices, as well as their support needs and preferences. The data collected will help inform the development and improvement of support services for educators.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Review the form to ensure you have all the necessary information and questions to answer.
2. Select the options that best describe your current situation as an educator.
3. Fill in the required fields, if any.
4. Provide additional comments or information if you have any.
5. Submit your survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Introduction** (Note)
This is an introductory page that provides background information about the survey.
No action is required for this field.

### 2. **Educator Stages** (Select One)
Select the stage that best describes your current level of experience with the innovative teaching method or technology:
* Option 1: Just started exploring
* Option 2: Currently implementing
* Option 3: Fully integrated into my teaching practice

### 3. **Barriers** (Select Multiple)
Select all the barriers that you are currently facing while adopting the innovative teaching method or technology:
* Option 1: Lack of resources
* Option 2: Limited time
* Option 3: Technical issues
* Option 4: Other (please specify in the "Additional Comments" field)

### 4. **Support Needs** (Select Multiple)
Select all the types of support you need to adopt the innovative teaching method or technology:
* Option 1: Training or professional development
* Option 2: Access to resources or funding
* Option 3: Other (please specify in the "Additional Comments" field)

### 5. **Comments** (Note)
Provide any additional comments or feedback you have on the innovative teaching method or technology.

### 6. **Email** (Email)
Please enter your email address to contact you if we need to follow up with any additional information.

### 7. **Phone** (Text)
Please enter your phone number to ensure we can reach you if needed.

### 8. **Submission** (Select One)
Select how you would prefer to submit your survey response:
* Option 1: Online submission
* Option 2: Phone submission
* Option 3: In-person submission

### 9. **Additional Comments** (Note)
Provide any additional comments or information you think are relevant to the survey.
