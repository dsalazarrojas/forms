# Faculty Technology Adoption Study Application - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Faculty Technology Adoption Study Application form is designed to collect data from faculty members about their technology adoption habits and experiences. The data collected will be used to analyze technology adoption patterns among faculty members and to identify areas for further investigation and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Information" field.
2. Provide your faculty information in the "Faculty Information" field.
3. Enter the course details in the "Course Information" field.
4. Select the types of technology used for your course in the "Technology Usage" field.
5. If applicable, provide any information about your participation in technology programs in the "Participation Program" field.
6. Finally, submit your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, Optional): Enter your contact information, including your name and email address.
* **Faculty Information** (`faculty_information`, `text`, Optional): Provide information about your faculty position and department.
* **Course Information** (`course_information`, `text`, Optional): Enter details about the course you are teaching, including the course name and number.
* **Technology Usage** (`technology_usage`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select all the types of technology used for your course, such as laptop/computer, smartphone, or tablet.
* **Participation Program** (`participation_program`, `text`, Optional): If you are participating in any technology programs, please provide details about your involvement.
* **Submission** (`submission`, `text`, Optional): Enter any additional comments or information you would like to submit.
