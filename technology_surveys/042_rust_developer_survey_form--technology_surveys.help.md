# Rust Developer Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rust Developer Survey Form is designed to collect data from Rust developers about their experiences and opinions on the language and its ecosystem.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the form, one page at a time.
2. For each field, follow the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **introduction** (text): This is a brief introduction to the form. Please provide a short statement explaining your experience and understanding of the Rust language.
* **rust_adoption** (select_one): How do you currently use Rust? (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, Option 4, Option 5, Option 6, Option 7)
* **team_size** (number): How many people are in your team that use Rust? Please provide the number of team members.
* **years_of_experience** (number): How many years of experience do you have with Rust? Please provide the number of years.
* **satisfaction** (select_multiple): How satisfied are you with the following aspects of Rust? (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, Option 4, Option 5, Option 6, Option 7, Option 8)
* **tool_usage** (select_one): Which tools do you use for Rust development? (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, Option 4, Option 5)
* **community_involvement** (text): Are you involved in any Rust-related communities, such as forums, social media, or mailing lists? If so, please describe your involvement.
* **product_research** (text): Are you involved in any research related to Rust? If so, please describe your research area and goals.
* **other_comments** (text): Do you have any other comments or suggestions about Rust or this survey?
* **email** (email): If you would like to be contacted for follow-up questions or to be added to a mailing list, please provide your email address.
* **phone** (text): If you would like to be contacted by phone, please provide your phone number.
