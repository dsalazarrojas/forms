# Smart Home Feature Trust Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and opinions on the features of smart home systems.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please select the options that best fit your experience and opinions about smart home features. Each question is designed to be answered based on your actual experience and feelings.
