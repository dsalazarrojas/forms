This form is designed to collect feedback from visitors to the computer lab, gathering information on their experiences, satisfaction, and areas for improvement. The form aims to ensure that the lab provides a positive and productive environment for users. This form is intended for students, faculty, staff, community members, and visitors who have visited the lab. It is not a mandatory form, but rather a voluntary way for users to provide feedback and suggestions. The form is designed to be completed once per visit, and the responses will be used to evaluate and improve the lab's services and facilities.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with asterisks (*). These include:
	* Survey ID
	* Survey Date
	* Affiliation (Student, Faculty, Staff, Community Member, or Visitor)
	* Visit Date
	* Visit Time
	* Visit Duration (Less than 30 Minutes, 30 Minutes to 1 Hour, 1 to 2 Hours, 2 to 4 Hours, or More than 4 Hours)
	* Purpose of Visit (select up to 3 options)
	* Workstation Condition (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Very Poor)
	* Software Availability (Yes, All Software Available, Most Software Available, Some Software Missing, or Required Software Not Available)
	* Hardware Functionality (Yes, Everything Worked, Minor Issues, Significant Issues, or Hardware Not Working)
	* Internet Connectivity (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or No Connection)
	* Lab Cleanliness (Very Clean, Clean, Acceptable, Dirty, or Very Dirty)
	* Noise Level (Very Quiet, Quiet, Acceptable, Noisy, or Very Noisy)
	* Overall Satisfaction (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied)
	* Frequency of Visits (Daily, Several Times a Week, Once a Week, Few Times a Month, or First Visit)

2. Select your affiliation and purpose of visit from the dropdown menus.
3. Rate your satisfaction with the workstations, software, hardware, internet connectivity, lab cleanliness, noise level, and staff helpfullness.
4. Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have for the lab.
5. Submit the form.

**Field-by-Field Explanation**

* **Survey ID**: A unique identifier for each survey.
* **Survey Date**: The date the survey was completed.
* **Participant Name**: Your full name (optional).
* **Participant ID**: Your student or member ID (optional).
* **Email Address**: Your email address (optional).
* **Affiliation**: Choose how you are affiliated with the lab (Student, Faculty, Staff, Community Member, or Visitor).
* **Department or Major**: Your department or major (optional).
* **Visit Date**: The date of your lab visit.
* **Visit Time**: The time of your lab visit.
* **Visit Duration**: How long you stayed at the lab (Less than 30 Minutes, 30 Minutes to 1 Hour, 1 to 2 Hours, 2 to 4 Hours, or More than 4 Hours).
* **Purpose of Visit**: Select up to 3 reasons why you visited the lab (Coursework, Research Project, Personal Study, Group Project, Software Access, Printing Services, Technical Support, Internet Access, or Other).
* **Workstation Used**: The workstation you used during your visit (optional).
* **Workstation Condition**: Rate the condition of the workstation you used (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Very Poor).
* **Software Availability**: Rate the availability of software you needed (Yes, All Software Available, Most Software Available, Some Software Missing, or Required Software Not Available).
* **Hardware Functionality**: Rate the functionality of hardware you used (Yes, Everything Worked, Minor Issues, Significant Issues, or Hardware Not Working).
* **Internet Connectivity**: Rate the internet connection speed and reliability (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or No Connection).
* **Lab Cleanliness**: Rate the cleanliness of the lab (Very Clean, Clean, Acceptable, Dirty, or Very Dirty).
* **Noise Level**: Rate the noise level in the lab (Very Quiet, Quiet, Acceptable, Noisy, or Very Noisy).
* **Staff Helpfulness**: Rate the helpfulness of lab staff (Very Helpful, Helpful, Neutral, Unhelpful, or Very Unhelpful).
* **Availability of Workstations**: Rate the availability of workstations when you arrived (Yes, Many Available, Yes, Some Available, Limited Availability, or No Workstations Available).
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with your visit (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied).
* **Frequency of Visits**: Rate how often you visit the lab (Daily, Several Times a Week, Once a Week, Few Times a Month, or First Visit).
* **Improvements Suggested**: Provide any suggestions for improvements (optional).
* **Additional Comments**: Any other feedback or comments you have (optional).

**Tips**

* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* If you select "Other" for any of the purpose of visit questions, please specify the reason in the "Additional Comments" section.
* If you have any suggestions or comments, please provide them in the "Improvements Suggested" or "Additional Comments" fields.
* You can submit this form once per visit, but it's recommended to do so after each visit to provide more accurate feedback.
