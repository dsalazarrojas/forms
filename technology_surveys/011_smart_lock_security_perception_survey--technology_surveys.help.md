# Smart Lock Security Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and opinions from users about smart locks, specifically on a company's security perception. Your input will help us understand your trust level in smart locks, concerns, and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Answer the questions truthfully to help us understand your perception of smart locks.
2.  Select up to 3 concerns that you have about smart locks.
3.  Choose your level of satisfaction with your current smart lock.
4.  Add any additional comments you'd like to share with us.
5.  Fill in your company's information, including your name, email, phone number, and address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Smart Lock Security Perception** (`survey_form`, number, optional): This field is not directly related to the provided YAML fields. However, we can assume it's a placeholder or a required question that asks about the user's overall perception of smart locks.
*   **Introduction** (`introduction`, note, optional): This field is a space for you to provide any additional information or context about your company or your experience with smart locks.
*   **Trust Level** (`trust`, number, optional): On a scale of 1-5, please rate your level of trust for smart locks. This will help us understand your confidence in the security of smart locks.
*   **Concerns** (`concerns`, select_multiple, optional): Select up to 3 concerns you have about smart locks. Concerns are:
    *   Security
    *   Cost
    *   Aesthetics
*   **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, select_one, optional): How satisfied are you with your current smart lock? Please choose one of the options:
    *   Very Satisfied
    *   Somewhat Satisfied
    *   Neutral
    *   Somewhat Dissatisfied
    *   Very Dissatisfied
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or thoughts about smart locks, feel free to share them with us.
*   **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Please enter your company's name.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your company email address.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your company phone number.
*   **Company Type** (`company_type`, select_one, optional): What type of company do you represent?
    *   Private
    *   Public
*   **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one, optional): What is your company's size?
    *   1-10
    *   11-50
    *   51-200
    *   201-1000
    *   1001-5000
    *   5001-10000
    *   10001
    *   10000+
*   **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter your department name.
*   **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title.
*   **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
*   **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, optional): Enter your company's address.
*   **Zip** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter your company's zip code.
*   **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter your city name.
*   **Country** (`country`, text, optional): Enter your country name.
*   **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter your company name (if different from the company name above).
*   **Phone** (`company_phone`, text, optional): Enter your company phone number (if different from the phone number above).

Note: Please fill in the required information as accurately as possible, and don't hesitate to contact us if you have any questions or concerns!
