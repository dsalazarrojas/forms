# Data Utilization Capability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate your organization's data utilization capability. It will help you understand your team's or department's data sources, access to data, data training, collaboration tools, data governance, data scientists, data owners, data users, data management, data security, and data privacy capabilities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select all relevant data sources for your organization.
3. Indicate how many data users and data scientists are available in your organization.
4. Rate your organization's data governance, data management, data security, and data privacy.
5. Provide any additional information for each section if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name of your team or department.
* **Data Sources** (`data_sources`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select all data sources used by your team or department, such as Google Drive, Google Analytics, and Google Sheets.
* **Additional Data Sources** (`data_sources_additional`, text, optional):
	+ Provide any additional data sources not listed above.
* **Access to Data** (`data_access`, select_one, optional):
	+ Indicate how often your team or department has access to data: Always, Sometimes, Rarely, or Never.
* **Additional Information** (`data_access_additional`, text, optional):
	+ Provide any additional information about access to data.
* **Data Training** (`data_training`, select_one, optional):
	+ Rate your organization's data training: High, Medium, or Low.
* **Data Training Additional** (`data_training_additional`, text, optional):
	+ Provide any additional information about data training.
* **Collaboration Tools** (`collaboration_tools`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select all collaboration tools used by your team or department, such as Google Drive, Google Sheets, Trello, and Asana.
* **Additional Collaboration Tools** (`collaboration_tools_additional`, text, optional):
	+ Provide any additional collaboration tools not listed above.
* **Data Governance** (`data_governance`, select_one, optional):
	+ Rate your organization's data governance: High, Medium, or Low.
* **Data Governance Additional** (`data_governance_additional`, text, optional):
	+ Provide any additional information about data governance.
* **Data Scientists** (`data_scientists`, number, optional):
	+ Indicate how many data scientists are available in your organization.
* **Data Scientists Additional** (`data_scientists_additional`, text, optional):
	+ Provide any additional information about data scientists.
* **Data Owners** (`data_owners`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select all data owners in your team or department, such as High, Medium, and Low.
* **Data Owners Additional** (`data_owners_additional`, text, optional):
	+ Provide any additional information about data owners.
* **Data Users** (`data_users`, number, optional):
	+ Indicate how many data users are available in your organization.
* **Data Users Additional** (`data_users_additional`, text, optional):
	+ Provide any additional information about data users.
* **Data Management** (`data_management`, select_one, optional):
	+ Rate your organization's data management: High, Medium, or Low.
* **Data Management Additional** (`data_management_additional`, text, optional):
	+ Provide any additional information about data management.
* **Data Security** (`data_security`, select_one, optional):
	+ Rate your organization's data security: High, Medium, or Low.
* **Data Security Additional** (`data_security_additional`, text, optional):
	+ Provide any additional information about data security.
* **Data Privacy** (`data_privacy`, select_one, optional):
	+ Rate your organization's data privacy: High, Medium, or Low.
* **Data Privacy Additional** (`data_privacy_additional`, text, optional):
	+ Provide any additional information about data privacy.
* **Form Footer** (`form_footer`, note, optional):
	+ This is a note field for any additional comments.

Please provide any other comments or suggestions about this form in the form footer.
