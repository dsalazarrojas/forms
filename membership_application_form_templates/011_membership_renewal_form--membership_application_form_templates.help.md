# Membership Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Membership Renewal Form is a tool for members to update their membership information, including their name, contact details, and membership status. This form helps the organization to maintain accurate records and ensure members have access to the latest benefits and opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Double-check your email address and phone number for correctness.
3. Select the correct membership status (Active or Inactive).
4. Choose the correct month and year since you have been a member.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Member Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documentation (e.g. driver's license, passport).
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address that you use to receive important updates and notifications from the organization.
* **Phone**: Enter your mobile or landline phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Address**: Enter your current address that you would like to be listed in our records.
* **Membership Start Date**: Enter the date you first became a member of the organization. This will help us track your membership history.
* **Membership End Date**: Enter the date your current membership is set to expire or has already expired.
* **Membership Status**: Choose the current status of your membership (Active or Inactive).
* **Member Since Date**: Enter the date you became a member of the organization. This will help us track your membership history.
* **Member Since Month**: Choose the month you joined the organization.
* **Member Since Year**: Choose the year you joined the organization.

## Tips

* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Make sure to update your membership status if it has changed.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
* Once you submit the form, you will be notified of any changes or updates to your membership status.
