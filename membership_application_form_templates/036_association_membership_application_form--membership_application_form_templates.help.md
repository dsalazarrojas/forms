# Association Membership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Association Membership Application Form is a tool used to collect information from individuals interested in joining the Association. This guide will walk you through the process of completing the form and provide explanations for each field.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your personal details, including your name, contact information, and professional background.
2. Select your membership type and goals for joining the Association.
3. Indicate your preferred payment method for dues.
4. Confirm that you agree to the membership terms and conditions.
5. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Date** (id: 1): Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Membership Type** (id: 2): Choose which type of membership you are interested in:
	+ Individual
	+ Student
	+ Family
	+ Corporate
	+ Sustaining
	+ Lifetime
* **First Name** (id: 3): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (id: 4): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth** (id: 5): Enter your birth date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Email Address** (id: 6): Enter your primary contact email.
* **Phone Number** (id: 7): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Mailing Address** (id: 8): Enter your full address.
* **City** (id: 9): Enter the city of your residence.
* **State/Province** (id: 10): Enter your state or province of residence.
* **ZIP/Postal Code** (id: 11): Enter your postal code.
* **Country** (id: 12): Enter your country of residence.
* **Professional Title or Occupation** (id: 13): Enter your current job or role.
* **Organization or Company** (id: 14): Enter your current employer or affiliation.
* **Years Experience in Field** (id: 15): Enter the number of years you have been in your field.
* **Educational Background** (id: 16): Enter your educational background, including degrees and qualifications.
* **Professional Interests** (id: 17): Describe what interests you in this field.
* **Goals for Membership** (id: 18): Describe why you want to join the Association.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (id: 19): Select how you heard about the Association:
	+ Website
	+ Social Media
	+ Friend or Colleague
	+ Professional Conference
	+ Email Newsletter
	+ Search Engine
	+ Print Advertisement
	+ Other
* **Prior Membership** (id: 20): Indicate if you have been a member before:
	+ No
	+ Yes
* **Referred By** (id: 21): If applicable, enter the name of the member who referred you.
* **Interested In** (id: 22): Check all that apply:
	+ Newsletters and Publications
	+ Professional Development
	+ Networking Events
	+ Conference Access
	+ Job Board Access
	+ Mentorship Program
	+ Committee Participation
* **Payment Method** (id: 23): Select your preferred payment method:
	+ Credit Card
	+ Debit Card
	+ Bank Transfer
	+ Check
	+ PayPal
* **I Agree to the Membership Terms and Conditions** (id: 24): Select to confirm your agreement:
	+ I Agree
	+ I Do Not Agree
* **Form Submission Date** (id: 25): The date this form was completed.

Note: This guide is based on the provided YAML fields and has been adapted to provide clear and practical explanations for each field.
