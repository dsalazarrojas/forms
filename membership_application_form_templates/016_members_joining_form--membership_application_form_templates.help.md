# members_joining_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who want to join our membership program. It helps us to understand their details and provide a smooth onboarding experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the correct format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Make sure it's a valid email address.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
6. Enter your address in the "Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address to receive updates and notifications.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address for delivery and communication purposes.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* You can contact us if you have any questions or need help with filling out this form.
* Once you submit the form, you will receive a confirmation email with further instructions on the membership process.
