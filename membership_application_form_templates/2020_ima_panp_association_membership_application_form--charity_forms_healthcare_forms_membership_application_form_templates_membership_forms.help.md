# 2020 IMA PANP Association Membership Application - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information from individuals interested in becoming members of the IMA PANP Association. The form is intended to collect personal and professional information from applicants, including their contact details, professional credentials, and membership preferences. The data collected will be used to facilitate the membership application process, verify credentials, and provide benefits to members.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by entering your personal information, including your full legal name, primary email address, and primary phone number. 
2. Next, provide your date of birth, which will be used for membership verification and benefits eligibility. 
3. Enter your complete mailing address, including street, city, state/province, and postal code. 
4. If you have professional credentials, provide your medical license or NPI number. 
5. Select your primary professional designation from the list provided. 
6. Choose your primary specialty area from the list provided. 
7. If you are currently employed, enter the name of your practice or employer. 
8. If you wish to be involved in committees or working groups, select all that interest you. 
9. If you have a referral source, select how you heard about IMA PANP. 
10. If you have an emergency contact, enter their name and phone number. 
11. If you require any accessibility accommodations, describe your needs. 
12. Finally, enter any additional comments or questions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full legal name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full legal name as it appears on your professional license.
* **Primary email address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter the email address you would like to use for official communications and dues renewal.
* **Primary phone number** (`phone_number`, `phone`, required): Enter your mobile phone number for urgent updates.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth to be used for membership verification and benefits eligibility.
* **Complete mailing address** (`mailing_address`, `text`, required): Enter your complete mailing address, including street, city, state/province, and postal code.
* **Medical license or NPI number** (`professional_license_number`, `text`, required): Enter your state license number or National Provider Identifier (NPI) number.
* **Professional designation** (`professional_designation`, `select_one`, required): Select your primary professional designation from the list provided.
* **Primary specialty area** (`primary_specialty`, `select_one`, required): Choose your primary specialty area from the list provided.
* **Current practice or employer name** (`employer_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your practice or employer.
* **Primary practice setting** (`practice_setting`, `select_one`, optional): Select your primary practice setting from the list provided.
* **Years since initial licensure** (`years_in_practice`, `integer`, optional): Enter the number of years since your initial licensure (0 for new graduates).
* **Membership category** (`membership_preferences`, `select_one`, required): Select your membership category from the list provided.
* **Committees or working groups of interest** (`committee_interests`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the committees or working groups that interest you. 
* **Referral source** (`referral_source`, `select_one`, optional): Select how you heard about IMA PANP.
* **Emergency contact name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency contact phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `phone`, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Accessibility needs** (`accessibility_needs`, `text`, optional): Describe any accessibility requirements you may have for meetings or events.
* **Additional comments or questions** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information relevant to your application.
