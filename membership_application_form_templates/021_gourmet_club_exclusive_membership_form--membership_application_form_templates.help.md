# Gourmet Club Exclusive Membership Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Gourmet Club Exclusive Membership Form is a simple application form designed for potential members to provide their details and preferences to join the Gourmet Club. This form helps the club's administration review and process membership applications efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your First Name in the designated field.
2. Provide your Last Name in the corresponding field.
3. Enter your Email address for contact purposes.
4. If you have a Phone number, please add it for further contact.
5. Select your desired Membership Type (Individual or Club Membership).
6. Type your Address for delivery or meeting purposes.
7. Mention your preferred Cuisine in the designated field.
8. Choose your Membership Start Date.
9. If you have any Special Requests, type them in the designated field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter a valid Email address that can be used for correspondence and club updates.
* **Phone**: If you have a phone number, please enter it for further contact.
* **Membership Type**: Select Individual for personal membership or Club Membership for a group membership.
* **Address**: Enter your address for delivery or meeting purposes.
* **Cuisine Preferred**: Mention your favorite cuisine or type of food.
* **Membership Start Date**: Choose the date you'd like your membership to start.
* **Special Request**: If you have any special requirements or requests, please type them in this field.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for the club to contact you.
* **Membership Start Date**: Choose your preferred membership start date.
