# Membership Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect membership information from applicants, ensuring a smooth and safe membership experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your contact details in the "contact_info" section.
2. Provide your name, email, and phone number.
3. Enter your preferred date of joining the membership.
4. If required, indicate your membership type.
5. For each question about emergency contact, answer "Yes" if the emergency contact is applicable. If "Yes" is selected, provide additional information as required.
6. Fill in any other necessary information in the provided text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (contact_info, text, required: false): This section captures your basic contact details.
* **Name** (Name, text, required: false): Enter your full name.
* **Email** (email, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (phone_number, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (date_of_birth, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth.
* **Date of Joining** (date_of_joining, date, required: false): Enter your preferred date of joining the membership.
* **Membership Type** (membership_type, select_one, required: false): Indicate your membership type preference. You can choose either "Yes" or "No".
* **Address** (address, text, required: false): Enter your address.
* **Emergency Contact** (emergency_contact, select_multiple, required: false): If applicable, indicate if you have an emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (emergency_contact_relationship, text, required: false): If you selected "Yes" for emergency contact, provide a relationship reason.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (emergency_contact_phone, text, required: false): If you selected "Yes" for emergency contact, provide their phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (emergency_contact_address, text, required: false): If you selected "Yes" for emergency contact, provide their address.
* **Other Emergency Contact Relationship** (emergency_contact_relationship_other, text, required: false): If you have an emergency contact that does not fit the provided options, provide a reason.
* **Medical History** (medical_history, text, required: false): Provide any relevant medical history.
* **Allergies** (allergies, text, required: false): If you have any allergies, list them here.
* **Relationship with Emergency Contact** (emergency_contact_relationship_reason, text, required: false): If you selected "Yes" for emergency contact, provide the relationship reason.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have filled out all necessary fields accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to reach out to our membership team.
* Once you have completed the form, review it carefully before submitting it.
* If you need to correct any errors or changes, please contact our membership team for assistance.
