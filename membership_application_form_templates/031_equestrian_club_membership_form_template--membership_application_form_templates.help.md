# Equestrian Club Membership Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Equestrian Club Membership Form Template is used to collect essential information from potential new members for the approval process of joining the club.
## How To Complete This Form
Please complete the form carefully, filling in all the relevant fields. If you are unsure about any field or have questions, feel free to contact our membership administration.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Please fill in your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): Please fill in your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, required/optional): Please provide your personal email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`4`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide your phone number for emergency contact.
* **Date of Birth** (`5`, `date`, required/optional): Please enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Address** (`6`, `text`, required/optional): Please fill in your current address for record purposes.
* **Emergency Name** (`7`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Phone** (`8`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship** (`9`, `text`, required/optional): Please describe your relationship with the emergency contact person.
* **Years of Experience** (`10`, `number`, required/optional): Please fill in the number of years you have been riding horses.
* **Equestrian License** (`11`, `text`, required/optional): If you have an equestrian license, please provide the number or certification.
* **Riding Experience** (`12`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your riding experience, including type of riding and any notable achievements.

## Tips
Please review your answers carefully before submitting the form to avoid errors. If you need any assistance or have doubts, do not hesitate to contact our membership administration.
