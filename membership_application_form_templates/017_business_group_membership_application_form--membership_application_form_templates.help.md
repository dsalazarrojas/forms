# Business Group Membership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from businesses or individuals applying for membership in our business group. The purpose of this form is to facilitate the evaluation of your eligibility and suitability for membership.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your profile information, including your full name, professional title, business or organization name, and years in business.
2. Select your industry sector and years in business.
3. Choose your primary reason for joining the group.
4. Describe how you can contribute to the group.
5. If applicable, provide a professional reference name and business website URL.
6. Select your preferred membership level.
7. Acknowledge and agree to the Membership Terms and Code of Conduct.
8. Finally, submit your application by providing the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: This is the full name of the applicant, and is required for our records.
* **Professional Title**: This is your professional title, such as CEO, Founder, or Director. Please enter your current title.
* **Business or Organization Name**: This is the name of your business or organization. If you are an individual, please enter "None" if you do not have a business.
* **Years in Business**: This is the number of years you have been in business. Please enter a valid number.
* **Industry Sector**: Select the primary industry of your business or organization from the dropdown list.
* **Primary Reason for Joining**: This is the main reason you want to join the group. Choose from the options provided.
* **Proposed Contribution**: Describe how you can contribute to the group. This can be a brief statement about your expertise, experience, or services you can offer.
* **Professional Reference Name**: If you have a colleague or peer who can vouch for your professional standing, please provide their name.
* **Business Website URL**: If you have a website for your business, please provide the URL. This is optional.
* **Preferred Membership Level**: Select your desired membership level from the options provided.
* **Membership Terms Acknowledgment**: Please acknowledge and agree to the Membership Terms and Code of Conduct.
* **Application Date**: This is the date you submit your application. This will be automatically populated.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have reviewed the Membership Terms and Code of Conduct before submitting your application.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions or fields, feel free to ask for clarification.
* Make sure to provide accurate and honest information to ensure a smooth evaluation process.
