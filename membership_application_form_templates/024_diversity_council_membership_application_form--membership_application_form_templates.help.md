# Diversity Council Membership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Diversity Council Membership Application Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in becoming a member of the Diversity Council. This form helps us understand your qualifications and interests and ensures that applicants have the right information to be considered for membership.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly. 
2. Make sure to select the correct options for each field.
3. Double-check your responses for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your physical address for membership eligibility purposes.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, optional): Enter the city where you reside.
* **Zip** (`zip`, `text`, required): Enter your zip code for membership eligibility purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required): Select your gender from the available options.
* **Ethnicity** (`ethnicity`, `text`, required): Enter your ethnicity for diversity purposes.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all relevant education levels you have achieved.
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, `text`, required): Enter your occupation or current job title.
* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your current or previous organization.
* **Position** (`position`, `text`, required): Enter your current or previous position.
* **Diversity Interest** (`diversity_interest`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all relevant diversity interests.
* **Volunteer Experience** (`volunteer_experience`, `text`, required): Enter any relevant volunteer experience.
* **Availability** (`availability`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all relevant availability times of day.
* **Availability Days** (`availability_days`, `number`, required): Enter the number of hours you are available per week.
* **Availability Time** (`availability_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time you are available in the 12-hour clock format (HH:MM).
* **Availability Frequency** (`availability_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select all relevant availability frequency.
* **Availability Note** (`availability_note`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional availability information.
* **Why Join** (`why_join`, `text`, required): Enter why you are interested in joining the Diversity Council.
* **How Know** (`how_know`, `text`, required): Enter how you heard about the Diversity Council.
* **Other Info** (`other_info`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information about yourself.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required): Click to submit your application.

Please note that some fields are required for membership eligibility purposes. Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
