# member_id_card_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The member_id_card_application_form is used to collect information from new members who are applying for a membership ID card. This form helps to ensure that the provided data is accurate and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
- Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
- Enter a valid Email address for contact purposes.
- Provide a Phone number for easy communication.
- Select your Date of Birth accurately.
- Enter your Membership Number (if applicable).
- Provide your Address for record keeping.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address for contact purposes, ensuring it is not already in use by the system. This will help us get in touch with you.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for easy communication, including call, text, or email.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Select your Date of Birth accurately from the provided calendar or manually enter it.
- **Member Number** (`membership_number`, `text`, optional): If you are an existing member or have an existing membership number, please enter it to help us verify your identity.
- **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Provide your full address to help us update your records accurately.
