# Mentoring Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mentoring Program Application Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in participating in the mentoring program. This form helps us understand your personal and educational background to provide a suitable mentoring experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form sections as instructed.
2. Enter accurate and complete information where required.
3. Review and edit your answers before submitting.
4. Attach any required documents or information as requested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Start**: This is the beginning of the form, and you can start filling out the subsequent sections from here.
* **Personal Information**:
	+ This section is used to gather basic information about you. Please fill out your name and contact details accurately.
* **Full name**:
	+ Provide your full name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Email**:
	+ Enter your valid email address to ensure we can contact you.
* **Date of birth**:
	+ Enter your date of birth in the correct format (DD-MM-YYYY).
* **Address**:
	+ Provide your current address to help us contact you for any necessary arrangements.
* **Phone**:
	+ Enter your contact number to ensure we can reach you.
* **A little about you**:
	+ Write a brief introduction about yourself to help us understand your interests and preferences.
* **Education**:
	+ Select all relevant educational options from the list to provide us with your educational background.
* **Financial Information**:
	+ Choose your financial situation from the list to help us assess your needs.
* **A little about yourself**:
	+ Write a more in-depth introduction about your interests, goals, and expectations from the mentoring program.
* **Attachments**:
	+ Upload any necessary documents or information to support your application.
