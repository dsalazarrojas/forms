# Crew List Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Crew List Submission Form is used to submit crew lists for management and tracking purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
2. Review the fields below for specific instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (2, text, required): Enter the name of the crew member being submitted.
* **Job Title** (3, text, required): Enter the job title of the crew member being submitted.
* **Email** (4, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person for the crew member being submitted.
* **Contact Information** (5, text, required): Enter any additional contact information for the crew member being submitted.
* **Contact Information Note** (6, note, optional): Enter any additional details about the contact information.
* **Submit Date** (7, date, optional): Enter the date when the submission was made.
* **Submit Time** (8, time, optional): Enter the time when the submission was made.
* **Assigned Tool** (9, select_one, optional): Select the tool assigned to the crew member being submitted.
* **Submission Type** (10, select_multiple, optional): Select the types of submissions being made (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).

## Tips

* Double-check your submission for accuracy before submitting.
* If you need help or have questions, contact your supervisor or support team.
