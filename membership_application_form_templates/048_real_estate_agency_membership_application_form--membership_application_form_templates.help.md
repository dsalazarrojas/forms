# real_estate_agency_membership_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide real estate agencies with a membership application process. Please fill out this form to start the membership application process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read and understand each field's requirements before filling out the form.
- Ensure all required fields are completed accurately to avoid delays in the processing of your application.
- Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Please enter your address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address. Ensure it is correctly formatted as [yourname@example.com](mailto:yourname@example.com).
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Please enter your date of birth in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **Date of Application** (`date_of_application`, date, optional): Please enter the date you submitted this application.
* **Real Estate License Number** (`real_estate_license_number`, number, optional): Please enter your real estate license number.
* **Real Estate License Type** (`real_estate_license_type`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select one of the following options: Residential, Commercial, or Industrial.
* **Agency Address** (`agency_address`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select all that apply from: Suite 300, 123 Main St, 456 Elm Rd.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of the agency's manager.
* **Manager Position** (`manager_position`, text, optional): Please enter the manager's position.
* **Agency Type** (`agency_type`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select one of the following options: Single Agent, Multi Agent, or Corporation.
* **Broker Agreement** (`broker_agreement`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select all that apply from: Broker 1, Broker 2, Broker 3.
* **Agency License Number** (`agency_license_number`, number, optional): Please enter the agency's license number.
* **Manager License Number** (`manager_license_number`, number, optional): Please enter the manager's license number.
* **Manager License Type** (`manager_license_type`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select one of the following options: Residential, Commercial, or Industrial.

## Tips

- Double-check your information before submitting the form.
- Ensure all required fields are completed accurately to avoid delays in the processing of your application.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our association's support team.
