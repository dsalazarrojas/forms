# Airline Alliance Membership Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to assist airlines in completing the membership application form for the airline alliance of their choice. The purpose of this form is to gather essential information about your airline's current status and goals, ensuring a smooth membership application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review each section carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2.  Enter the required information for each field, including your airline's details and goals.
3.  Select the correct options for each field, such as the alliance you are applying for and your financial stability.
4.  Attach any required supporting documents, if applicable.
5.  Verify your digital signature and title.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Airline Name** (`airline_name`, text, required): This is the full name of your airline.
*   **Airline Code** (`airline_code`, text, required): Enter your IATA code.
*   **ICAO Code** (`icao_code`, text, required): Enter your IATA code identifier.
*   **Country of Registration** (`country_of_registration`, text, required): Enter the country where your airline is registered.
*   **Headquarters Address** (`headquarters_address`, text, required): Enter your main office address.
*   **Website** (`website`, text, required): Enter your airline's website address.
*   **CEO Name** (`ceo_name`, text, required): Enter your Chief Executive Officer's name.
*   **CEO Email** (`ceo_email`, email, required): Enter your CEO's contact email address.
*   **Primary Contact Name** (`primary_contact_name`, text, required): Enter your primary contact's name.
*   **Primary Contact Title** (`primary_contact_title`, text, required): Enter your primary contact's job title.
*   **Primary Contact Email** (`primary_contact_email`, email, required): Enter your primary contact's email address.
*   **Primary Contact Phone** (`primary_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your primary contact's contact number.
*   **Application Date** (`application_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your application.
*   **Alliance Applied For** (`alliance_applied`, select_one, required): Select which alliance you are applying for: Star Alliance, SkyTeam, Oneworld, or Other.
*   **Membership Type** (`membership_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of membership you are applying for: Full member, Associate member, Affiliate member, or Connecting partner.
*   **Fleet Size** (`fleet_size`, number, required): Enter the total number of aircraft.
*   **Destinations Served** (`destinations_served`, number, required): Enter the total number of destinations you serve.
*   **Countries Served** (`countries_served`, number, required): Enter the number of countries you serve.
*   **Annual Passengers (millions)** (`annual_passengers`, number, required): Enter your yearly passenger count.
*   **Year Established** (`year_established`, number, required): Enter the year your airline was founded.
*   **IATA Member** (`iata_member`, select_one, required): Check if you are an IATA member.
*   **IOSA Certified** (`iosaa_certified`, select_one, required): Select your IATA Operational Safety Audit status: Yes, current, Yes, expired, or False.
*   **Safety Rating** (`safety_rating`, select_one, required): Select your airline's safety rating: 7-star, 6-star, 5-star, 4-star, 3-star, or Not rated.
*   **Current Codeshare Agreements** (`codeshare_agreements`, number, optional): Enter the number of codeshare agreements you have.
*   **Existing Alliance Memberships** (`existing_alliance_memberships`, select_multiple, required): Select the alliances you are currently a member of: None, Star Alliance, SkyTeam, Oneworld, or Other regional.
*   **Frequent Flyer Program** (`frequent_flyer_program`, text, optional): Enter the name of your frequent flyer program.
*   **FFP Members (millions)** (`ffp_members`, number, optional): Enter the size of your loyalty program.
*   **Airport Lounges** (`lounge_count`, number, optional): Enter the number of airport lounges you have.
*   **Hub Airports** (`hub_airports`, text, required): Enter the primary airports that serve as hubs.
*   **Strategic Fit Rationale** (`strategic_fit`, text, required): Explain why you want to join this alliance.
*   **Expected Benefits** (`benefits_expected`, text, required): Describe the benefits you hope to gain from joining this alliance.
*   **Financial Stability** (`financial_stability`, select_one, required): Select your current financial status: Profitable, Break-even, Loss, recovering, or Restructuring.
*   **Alliance References** (`references`, text, optional): Enter the names of current members who support your application.
*   **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, select_one, required): Check if you are attaching supporting documents.
*   **Document Types** (`document_types`, select_multiple, optional): Select the types of documents you are attaching: Financial statements, Safety certificates, Operating license, Fleet information, Route map, or Other.
*   **Airline Authorized Signature** (`airline_signature`, text, required): Enter your digital signature.
*   **Signatory Title** (`signature_title`, text, required): Enter your title.
*   **Application Status** (`application_status`, select_one, required): Select your current application status: Submitted, Under review, Pending documentation, Approved, or Denied.
