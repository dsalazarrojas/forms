# Parent Organization Membership Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals or organizations interested in joining a parent organization. It gathers general details, contact information, membership information, and other relevant details required for the membership application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the general information section with your name, title, and any other relevant details.
2.  Provide your contact details, including your email address, phone number, and any other contact information.
3.  Enter your membership information, such as your membership type and status.
4.  Share information about your parent organization, including its name, address, and contact person.
5.  Enter details about the members of your parent organization, including their name, title, and member type.
6.  Describe your relationship with your parent organization, such as your role or position.
7.  Finally, provide payment information, such as the membership fee and payment method.
8.  Add any additional information that you think is relevant to your membership application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **General Info (1)** (`title`, `text`, **required**):
    *   Enter your name and title to identify yourself or your organization.
*   **Contact Details (2)** (`contact_details`, `text`, **required**):
    *   Provide your email address, phone number, and any other contact information for you or your organization.
*   **Membership Info (3)** (`membership_info`, `text`, **required**):
    *   Specify your membership type and status to ensure we have accurate information.
*   **Parent Organization Info (4)** (`parent_org_info`, `text`, **required**):
    *   Enter your parent organization's name, address, and contact person to help us get in touch with the right person.
*   **Member Details (5)** (`member_details`, `text`, **required**):
    *   Enter details about the members of your parent organization, including their name, title, and member type.
*   **Member Relationships (6)** (`member_relationships`, `text`, **required**):
    *   Describe your relationship with your parent organization, including your role or position.
*   **Payment Info (7)** (`payment_info`, `text`, **required**):
    *   Provide payment information, such as the membership fee and payment method.
*   **Additional Info (8)** (`additional_info`, `text`, **required**):
    *   Add any additional information that you think is relevant to your membership application.
