# Clan Membership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your personal and gaming details to help us get to know you better and see if you're a good fit for our clan. Please answer all required fields to ensure a successful submission.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to proceed with the application.
2. Fill out all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*), carefully.
3. Be honest and accurate in your responses, as this will help us assess your fit for our clan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`Full Name`, Required): Please enter your real name for verification purposes.
* **Age** (`Age`, Required): Select your age category from the provided options.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, Required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, Optional): If you'd like to be contacted via phone, provide your number. (Optional)
* **Phone Number**: This field is optional, so feel free to skip it if you don't want to be contacted via phone.
* **City or Region** (`City or Region`, Optional): If you'd like to share your location, enter it here. (Optional)
* **Primary Gaming Username** (`Primary Gaming Username`, Required): Enter the username associated with your main gaming account.
* **Gaming Platforms** (`Gaming Platforms You Use - select all`, Required): Select all platforms you use for gaming.
* **Favorite Games** (`Favorite Games`, Required): List your top 3 favorite games.
* **Years of Gaming Experience** (`Years of Gaming Experience`, Required): Select the number of years you've spent gaming.
* **Gaming Skill Level** (`Gaming Skill Level`, Required): Choose your gaming skill level from the options.
* **Previous Clan or Team Experience** (`Previous Clan or Team Experience`, Optional): If you have prior experience, share it here. (Optional)
* **Roles You've Held** (`Roles you have held in clans or teams`, Optional): If you've held roles, describe them here. (Optional)
* **Interest in Leadership Roles** (`Interest in leadership roles`, Optional): Check if you have an interest in leadership roles.
* **Average Hours per Week** (`Average hours per week available for gaming`, Required): Enter the average hours you dedicate to gaming per week.
* **Timezone** (`Timezone`, Required): Select your timezone.
* **Best Times to Play** (`Best times to play`, Optional): If you have a preferred schedule, share it here. (Optional)
* **Comfort with Voice Chat** (`Comfortable with voice chat communications`, Required): Choose how comfortable you are with voice chat communications.
* **Agree to Code of Conduct** (`I agree to follow clan code of conduct and sportsmanship rules`, Required): Confirm your commitment to our code of conduct and sportsmanship rules.
* **Why Want to Join** (`Why do you want to join this clan`, Required): Explain why you'd like to join our clan.
