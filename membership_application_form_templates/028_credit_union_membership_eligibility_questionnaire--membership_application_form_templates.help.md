# Credit Union Membership Eligibility Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to determine the eligibility for credit union membership.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details such as name, address, date of birth, phone number, and email.
2. Provide your employment details including employer name, address, and employment status.
3. Select your member type and annual income frequency.
4. If applicable, provide emergency contact details such as name, relationship, phone number, and address.
5. Finally, review and confirm all the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (text, required): Your first name as per your identification document.
- **Last Name** (text, required): Your last name as per your identification document.
- **Address** (text, required): Please enter your current address.
- **Date of Birth** (date, required): Please enter your date of birth in the format dd/mm/yyyy.
- **Social Security Number** (number, required): Please enter your Social Security Number.
- **Phone Number** (text, required): Please enter your phone number.
- **Email** (email, required): Please enter your email address.
- **Emergency Contact Name** (text, optional): Name of an emergency contact person. This can be a family member, relative, or friend.
  - **Emergency Contact Relationship** (select_one, optional): Relationship of the emergency contact (spouse, parent, child, relative, other).
  - **Emergency Contact Phone** (text, optional): Phone number of the emergency contact person.
  - **Emergency Contact Address** (text, optional): Address of the emergency contact person.
- **Member Type** (select_one, required): Please select your member type (full member, junior member, share member).
- **Member Since** (date, required): Date you have been a member.
- **Annual Income** (number, required): Please enter your annual income.
- **Employment Status** (select_one, required): Please select your employment status (full time, part time, self employed, retired, student, unemployed).
- **Occupation** (text, required): Your occupation.
- **Income Frequency** (text, required): Please select how often you get paid (weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, quarterly, etc.).
- **Other** (text, optional): If your income frequency is other, please describe it.
- **Employer** (text, optional): Name of your employer.
- **Employer Address** (text, optional): Address of your employer.
- **Branch** (text, optional): Name of your branch.
- **Branch Address** (text, optional): Address of your branch.
- **Branch Phone** (text, optional): Phone number of your branch.
- **Branch Email** (email, optional): Email of your branch.
