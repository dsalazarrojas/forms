# Hockey Youth Waitlist Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to apply for a spot on our hockey program's waitlist. Please fill out the form below to provide your information and help us better understand your interest in joining our program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the requested information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1): Please enter the child's first name as they will be known in the program.
* **Last Name** (2): Please enter the child's last name as they will be known in the program.
* **Age** (3): Enter the child's age. This will help us understand their eligibility for our program.
* **Email** (4): Enter your email address. This will be used as the primary contact method for communication.
* **Phone** (5): Enter your phone number. This will help us reach out to you if we need more information.
* **Address** (6): Enter your home address. This will help us understand where to send any necessary documents.
* **Father's Name** (7): If applicable, enter your father's name as they would like to be known in the program.
* **Mother's Last Name** (8): If applicable, enter your mother's last name as they would like to be known in the program.
* **Father's Email** (9): If applicable, enter your father's email address.
* **Father's Phone** (10): If applicable, enter your father's phone number.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields (not marked as optional). This will ensure we can process your application correctly.
* If you're having trouble accessing the form, try clearing your browser's cache or using a different device.
* If you've already applied for the program, you don't need to fill out this form again. You can contact the program directly to ask about your application status.
