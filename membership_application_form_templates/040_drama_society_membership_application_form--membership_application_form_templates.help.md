# Drama Society Membership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Drama Society Membership Application Form is designed for individuals to apply for membership in the Drama Society. To be eligible, applicants must complete all required fields and submit the form for review.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the requested information, ensuring that all required fields are completed.
2. Enter a valid email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide a complete and accurate address in the "Address" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "PhoneNumber" field.
5. Describe your interest in the "Interest" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **FirstName**: Enter your given name.
- **LastName**: Enter your surname.
- **Email**: Enter a valid email address associated with your account.
- **Address**: Enter your full address, including street address, city, state, and zip code.
- **PhoneNumber**: Enter your phone number with the area code and prefix.
- **Interest**: Describe your interest in joining the Drama Society, e.g. "Acting, directing, or backstage crew."

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- Double-check your email address, address, and phone number for accuracy.
