# Membership Information 1 Hope Chapel - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is part of the membership application process for Hope Chapel. It collects information about the applicant, including their name, contact details, and date of birth.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Fill in your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email in the "Email" field. This should be a valid email address.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field. This should be in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required: false): Enter your given name.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required: false): Enter your surname.
* **Email** (Email, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you enter your contact information accurately and completely.
* If you need help or have any issues with the form, contact us.
* If you have already submitted an application, please do not re-submit this form. We will process your previous application.
