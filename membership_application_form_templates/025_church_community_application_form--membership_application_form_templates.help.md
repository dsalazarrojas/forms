# Church Community Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Church Community Application Form is designed to collect information from individuals who are interested in joining the church community. This form ensures that the applicant's personal details, spiritual history, and membership intentions are well-understood by the church leadership.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your full name and date of birth in the required fields.
* Provide your primary phone number and email address.
* Select your marital status and occupation (if applicable).
* If you have been previously baptized, select "True" or "False".
* Share a brief testimony about your faith journey.
* If applicable, provide the name and age of your children.
* Indicate if you are interested in the next membership orientation class.
* Agree with the church's Statement of Faith and Bylaws.
* Sign and date the application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your complete name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Primary Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your primary phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Marital Status** (`marital_status`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options: Single, Married, Widowed, Divorced.
* **Occupation/Profession** (`occupation`, text, optional): Enter your current occupation or profession.
* **Have you been baptized?** (`baptism_status`, select_one, required): Select "True" if you have been baptized or "False" otherwise.
* **Briefly share your faith journey** (`personal_testimony_brief`, text, required): Share a brief testimony of how you came to know Christ.
* **Name of Previous Church (if any)** (`previous_church_membership`, text, optional): If you are transferring from another church, provide the name of your previous church.
* **Reason for leaving previous church** (`reason_for_leaving`, text, optional): If you are transferring from another church, provide a brief reason for your decision.
* **Why do you want to join our community?** (`reason_for_joining`, text, required): Share your reason for wanting to join our church community.
* **Skills or Talents** (`skills_talents`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the skills or talents you would like to use in ministry: Teaching, Music/Worship, Children/Youth, Tech/Media, Administration, Hospitality, Outreach.
* **Children's Names and Ages** (`children_names_ages`, text, optional): If you are applying as a family, list the names and ages of your children.
* **Are you interested in the next Membership Orientation Class?** (`membership_class_interest`, select_one, required): Indicate if you are interested in attending the next orientation class.
* **I agree with the church Statement of Faith and Bylaws** (`statement_of_faith_agree`, select_one, required): Select "I Agree" if you agree with the church's Statement of Faith and Bylaws.
* **Electronic Signature** (`signature_confirmation`, text, required): Enter your electronic signature by typing your name in the provided text field.
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, date, required): Enter the date you completed this application form.
