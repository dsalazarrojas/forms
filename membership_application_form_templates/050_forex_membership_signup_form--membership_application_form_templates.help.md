# Forex Membership Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect basic information from users who are interested in joining a membership service for Forex traders. The form will help you to provide your details and get started with the service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your name: Enter your first and last name in the "First name" and "Last name" fields.
2. Enter your email: Provide your valid email address in the "Email" field.
3. Set up a password: Create a strong and unique password for your account in the "Password" field. 
4. Confirm your password: Re-enter your password in the "Confirm-password" field to ensure accuracy.
5. Submit: Once you have filled in all the required fields, click the submit button to send your information to our system.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Last name** (`last-name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that you have access to and check regularly.
* **Password** (`password`, text, required): Create a strong and unique password for your account. A strong password should be at least 8 characters long and contain a mix of characters, numbers, and special characters.
* **Confirm-password** (`confirm_password`, text, required): Re-enter your password to confirm that it is correct.

## Tips
* Make sure to use a strong and unique password to secure your account.
* Use the same email address you have access to and check regularly to avoid missing important updates.
* If you experience any issues while filling the form, please contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
