# Zoo Family Membership Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Zoo Family Membership Application form is a tool for families to apply for membership at the zoo. Completing this form will help us understand your family's information and confirm your agreement to the zoo's rules and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Application Form" with any relevant information.
2. Review and confirm your agreement to the zoo's terms by selecting "I Agree" or "Decline" on the "Confirm Agreement" page.
3. If you have any additional information you'd like to share, please provide it in the "Additional Notes" field.
4. Finally, submit your application by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Application Form**: Please fill out this field with any relevant information about your family. This will help us understand your membership needs and provide a smooth application experience. (Required: false)
- **Agreement Form**: This field is not meant to be filled out. It's an empty field for you to acknowledge that you've read the agreement. However, if you'd like to provide any additional comments or notes, you can do so in the "Additional Notes" field. (Required: false)
- **Additional Notes**: If you have any additional information you'd like to share about your application, please provide it here. This is an optional field, and you can use it to ask any questions or provide any supporting documents. (Required: false)
- **Confirm Agreement**: Please select "I Agree" to confirm that you have read and agreed to the zoo's rules and regulations. If you decline, your application will not be processed. (Required: true)
- **Submit**: This is the final step to complete your application. Please review your information carefully before submitting. (Required: true)

## Tips
- Please make sure to review your information carefully before submitting your application.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
- We'll process your application as soon as possible after we receive your submission.
