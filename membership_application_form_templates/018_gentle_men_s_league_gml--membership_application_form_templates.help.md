# Gentle_Mens_League_Gml - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gentle_Mens_League_Gml form is used for new member registration to provide personal details and contact information for processing membership eligibility and application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Double-check your input before submitting the form.
3. Make sure to review and agree to the membership terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Header** (`form_header`, `text`, not required): This is a placeholder text for the form header.
* **Full Name** (`name`, `text`, not required): Please enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed in the membership roster.
* **Surname** (`surname`, `text`, not required): Please enter your surname as you would like it to be displayed in the membership roster.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, not required): Enter your valid email address for future communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, not required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for membership updates.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, not required): Provide your current address for membership eligibility verification.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, not required): Enter the city where you currently reside.
* **Post Code** (`post_code`, `text`, not required): Enter the post code of your current address.

## Tips

* Make sure to use a valid email address as it will be used for membership updates and notifications.
* Please double-check your input for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
