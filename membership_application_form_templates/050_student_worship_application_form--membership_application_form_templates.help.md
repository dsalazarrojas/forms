# Student Worship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Worship Application Form is used to collect information from students and their guardians for the purpose of enrolling the student in a worship program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out your student information:
	- Enter your student name in the "Student Name" field.
	- Enter your contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.
	- Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
	- Select your preferred communication method from the "Preferred Communication Method" field.
2. Confirm your preferred communication method:
	- Select your preferred communication option(s) in the "Contact Preferred Communication Option" field.
3. Enter your student grade level and grade level option:
	- Select your grade level (Elementary, Middle School, High School) in the "Grade Level" field.
	- Enter your grade level option in the "Student Grade Level Option" field.
4. Enter your student school attendance information:
	- Enter your school attendance start date in the "School Attendance" field.
	- Enter your school attendance end date in the "School Attendance End" field.
5. Confirm your student covenant:
	- Sign the "Student Covenant Signed" field to confirm you have read and agreed to the terms of the covenant.
6. Fill out your guardian information (if applicable):
	- Enter your guardian name in the "Parent Name" field.
	- Enter your guardian email address in the "Parent Email" field.
	- Enter your guardian phone number in the "Parent Phone" field.
	- Select your preferred communication method from the "Parent Preferred Communication Method" field.
	- Select your preferred communication option in the "Parent Preferred Communication Option" field.
	- Enter your age in the "Parent Age" field.
	- Select your gender in the "Parent Gender" field.
	- Enter your guardian name in the "Guardian Name" field.
	- Enter your guardian email address in the "Guardian Email" field.
	- Enter your guardian phone number in the "Guardian Phone" field.
	- Select your preferred communication method from the "Guardian Preferred Communication Method" field.
	- Select your preferred communication option in the "Guardian Preferred Communication Option" field.
7. Review and sign the form:
	- Review your information carefully before submitting the form.
	- Sign the "Student Worship Leaders Signature" field to confirm you have reviewed and agreed to the terms of the covenant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the student.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Preferred Communication Method** (`contact_preferred_communication`, select_one, required): Select your preferred communication method (Email, Phone, Text).
* **Contact Preferred Communication Option** (`contact_preferred_communication_option`, select_multiple, required): Select your preferred communication options (Email, Phone, Text).
* **Grade Level** (`student_grade_level`, select_one, required): Select your grade level (Elementary, Middle School, High School).
* **Student Grade Level Option** (`student_grade_level_option`, text, required): Enter your grade level option.
* **School Attendance** (`student_school_attendance`, date, required): Enter your school attendance start date.
* **School Attendance End** (`student_school_attendance_end`, date, required): Enter your school attendance end date.
* **Student Covenant Signed** (`student_covenant_signed`, note, required): Sign this field to confirm you have read and agreed to the terms of the covenant.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter your guardian name.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, required): Enter your guardian email address.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, required): Enter your guardian phone number.
* **Parent Preferred Communication Method** (`parent_preferred_communication`, select_one, required): Select your preferred communication method (Email, Phone, Text).
* **Parent Preferred Communication Option** (`parent_preferred_communication_option`, select_multiple, required): Select your preferred communication options (Email, Phone, Text).
* **Parent Age** (`parent_age`, number, required): Enter your age.
* **Parent Gender** (`parent_gender`, select_one, required): Select your gender (Male, Female, Non-Binary).
* **Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, text, required): Enter your guardian name.
* **Guardian Email** (`guardian_email`, email, required): Enter your guardian email address.
* **Guardian Phone** (`guardian_phone`, text, required): Enter your guardian phone number.
* **Guardian Preferred Communication Method** (`guardian_preferred_communication`, select_one, required): Select your preferred communication method (Email, Phone, Text).
* **Guardian Preferred Communication Option** (`guardian_preferred_communication_option`, select_multiple, required): Select your preferred communication options (Email, Phone, Text).
* **Student Worship Leaders Signature** (`student_worship_leaders_signature`, text, required): Sign this field to confirm you have reviewed and agreed to the terms of the covenant.
