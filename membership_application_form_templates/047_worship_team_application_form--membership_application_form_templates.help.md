# Worship Team Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Worship Team Application Form is a crucial tool for the church's worship team selection process. It helps the team leaders to evaluate potential team members' qualifications, experience, and fit for the team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal information, including your name, contact details, and date and time you are available to start.
2. Provide details about your spiritual life, including your spiritual background and any relevant spiritual experiences.
3. Share your music training and experience, including any relevant music education or training you have received.
4. List any personal references who can vouch for your character and abilities.
5. Upload a recent photo of yourself (optional).
6. Share your music experience, including any previous bands or groups you have been part of.
7. List any music directors or leaders you have worked with in the past.
8. Share any music achievements or awards you have received.
9. List any church affiliations or leadership positions you hold.
10. Share any music leadership experience you have.
11. Describe your music ministry experience and any relevant accomplishments.
12. Provide details about your family information, if applicable.
13. Add any additional information that you feel is relevant to your application.
14. Finally, sign and date the form to confirm your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Info** (`Personal Info`, `text`, required): Fill out your full name, email address, and phone number.
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, `text`, required): Provide your contact information, including your address and any other relevant contact details.
* **Spiritual Life** (`Spiritual Life`, `text`, optional): Share your spiritual background, including your denomination, church affiliation, and any relevant spiritual experiences.
* **Music Training** (`Music Training`, `text`, optional): List any music education or training you have received, including diplomas, certifications, or degrees.
* **Personal References** (`Personal References`, `text`, optional): List at least two personal references who can vouch for your character and abilities.
* **Applicant Photo** (`Applicant Photo`, `text`, optional): Upload a recent photo of yourself for team leaders to review.
* **Music Experience** (`Music Experience`, `text`, optional): Share your music experience, including any previous bands or groups you have been part of.
* **Music Abilities** (`Music Abilities`, `text`, optional): List any music abilities or skills you possess, including instruments, languages, or other relevant qualifications.
* **Previous Employment** (`Previous Employment`, `text`, optional): Share any previous employment or work experience, including job titles and dates of employment.
* **Education** (`Education`, `text`, optional): List any educational background or credentials you hold.
* **Spiritual Background** (`Spiritual Background`, `text`, optional): Share your spiritual background, including your denomination, church affiliation, and any relevant spiritual experiences.
* **Previous Music Groups** (`Previous Music Groups`, `text`, optional): List any previous music groups you have been part of.
* **Music Directors** (`Music Directors`, `text`, optional): List any music directors or leaders you have worked with in the past.
* **Music Achievements** (`Music Achievements`, `text`, optional): Share any music achievements or awards you have received.
* **Church Affiliations** (`Church Affiliations`, `text`, optional): List any church affiliations or leadership positions you hold.
* **Church Leadership** (`Church Leadership`, `text`, optional): Share any church leadership experience you have.
* **Music Leadership** (`Music Leadership`, `text`, optional): Share any music leadership experience you have.
* **Music Ministry** (`Music Ministry`, `text`, optional): Describe your music ministry experience and any relevant accomplishments.
* **Family Info** (`Family Info`, `text`, optional): Provide details about your family information, if applicable.
* **Additional Info** (`Additional Info`, `text`, optional): Add any additional information that you feel is relevant to your application.
* **Signature** (`Signature`, `text`, required): Sign and date the form to confirm your application.
* **Date** (`Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are applying to join the worship team.
* **Time** (`Time`, `time`, required): Enter the time you are available to start serving on the worship team.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
