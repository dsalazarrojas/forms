# Student Leadership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students applying for leadership positions within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field accurately and completely.
2. Make sure to sign and agree with the "Expectation and Agreement" section.
3. Double-check your email and contact number for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your school records or ID card.
* **Student School** (`student_school`, text, not required): If applicable, enter the name of your school.
* **Expectation and Agreement** (`expectation_and_agreement`, note, required): Read and carefully agree with the terms and conditions listed below. Please initial each point to indicate your understanding and acceptance.
* **Student Background** (`student_background`, text, not required): Briefly describe your relevant experience and qualifications for the leadership position.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Have You Read and Agreed with Expectation and Agreement** (`expectation_and_agreement_agreed`, select_multiple, required): If you have read and agreed with the "Expectation and Agreement" section, select "Yes". If not, select "No".
