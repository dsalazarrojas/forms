# Film Association Membership Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to apply for or update membership status in the Film Association.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out your First Name in the first field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the second field, which is a required field.
3. Provide your email address in the third field. This is also a required field.
4. If you have a phone number, enter it in the fourth field.
5. If you have relevant film experience, describe it in the fifth field.
6. Check the Film Association Member status in the sixth field.
7. If you have years of experience with the Film Association, enter them in the seventh field.
8. Lastly, select the date you are submitting your membership application in the eighth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name for our records.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): This is required for us to identify you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted, enter your phone number.
* **Film Experience** (`film_experience`, text, optional): Describe any relevant film-related experience.
* **Film Association Member** (`film_association_member`, select_one, required): Check if you are already a member or not.
* **Years of Film Association Experience** (`film_association_years`, number, optional): If you've been a member, tell us how many years.
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you're submitting your application.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you only fill out each field once and accurately.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, contact the Film Association for guidance.
* We'll be in touch soon after submitting your application.
