# Signup Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect personal information from users for membership purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with your personal information. You can skip any fields that are not relevant to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address that you have access to.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your physical address.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter the state where you reside.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, optional): Enter your zip code for address verification.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure that your membership application is processed smoothly.
* If you are having trouble with any field, please contact the form administrator for assistance.
