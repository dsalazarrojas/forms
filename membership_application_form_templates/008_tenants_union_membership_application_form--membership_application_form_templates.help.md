# Tenants Union Membership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help potential members of the Tenants Union to provide their information and apply for membership. It is intended to gather contact and personal details, as well as preferences and consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name and other contact information in the "Applicant Information" section.
2.  Provide your contact preferences and consent in the "Contact Preferences" and "Consent" sections.
3.  Share your position applying for in the "Position Applying" section.
4.  Enter any additional relevant information about yourself or your application in the "Additional Info" section.
5.  Finally, review and submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Information (1)**: Enter your name and any other relevant contact information (e.g., email address, phone number).
* **Contact Information (2)**: Provide your preferred method of contact, such as email or phone number.
* **Membership Terms (3)**: Please describe why you are interested in joining the Tenants Union and what you hope to achieve as a member.
* **Position Applying (4)**: Indicate the role or position you are interested in applying for within the union.
* **Personal Info (5)**: Share any personal details that you believe are relevant to your application.
* **Contact Preferences (6)**: Select your preferred method of communication (e.g., email, phone, or mail).
* **Consent (7)**: Confirm that you have read and understood the union's membership terms and conditions.
* **Additional Info (8)**: Add any additional information that you think is essential to your application.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* Be clear and concise in your responses.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, feel free to contact us.
