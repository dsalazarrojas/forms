# Music Club Members Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for individuals interested in joining the Music Club to provide their contact and demographic information, musical interests, and emergency contact details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the **Name** field.
2. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
3. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
4. Enter your date of birth (mm/dd/yyyy) in the **Date of Birth** field.
5. Choose if you have any **Music Interest** by selecting one of the options.
6. Enter the number of years you have **Music Experience**.
7. Choose multiple music styles that interest you in the **Music Style** field.
8. Select multiple music instruments you are interested in in the **Music Instruments** field.
9. Enter the name of your emergency contact person in the **Emergency Contact** field (if applicable).
10. Enter your address in the **Address** field.
11. Enter the name of your city in the **City** field.
12. Choose your country of residence.
13. Enter your zip code.
14. Agree to the **Terms of Service** by selecting "Yes" from the dropdown list.
15. Agree to the second **Agreement 2**.
16. Choose your preferred music genre(s) from the **Music Genre** field.
17. Choose if you are interested in participating in music venues by selecting multiple options in the **Music Venues** field.
18. Choose the type of music club that interests you.
19. Choose the size of the music club that interests you.
20. If you have an emergency contact, enter their name.
21. If you have an emergency contact, enter their email address.
22. If you have an emergency contact, enter their phone number.
23. If you have an emergency contact, describe your relationship with them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to be contacted by the music club.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number to be contacted by the music club.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Music Interest**: Choose if you have any interest in music.
* **Music Experience**: Enter the number of years you have experience with music.
* **Music Style**: Choose multiple music styles that interest you.
* **Music Instruments**: Choose multiple music instruments you are interested in.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Address**: Enter your address.
* **City**: Enter the name of your city.
* **Country**: Choose your country of residence.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **Terms of Service**: Agree to be a member of the music club by selecting "Yes" from the dropdown list.
* **Agreement 2**: Agree to the additional terms and conditions of the music club.
* **Music Genre**: Choose multiple music genres that interest you.
* **Music Venues**: Choose multiple music venues that interest you.
* **Music Club Interest**: Choose the type of music club that interests you.
* **Music Club Size**: Choose the size of the music club that interests you.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email address of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Enter your relationship with your emergency contact person.

Please review each field carefully and fill it in according to the instructions. If you have any questions or concerns, contact the music club administrators.
