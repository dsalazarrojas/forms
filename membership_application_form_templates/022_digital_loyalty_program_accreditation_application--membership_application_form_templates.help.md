# Digital Loyalty Program Accreditation Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for organizations that want to join the digital loyalty program and have not previously applied.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required") with accurate and truthful information.
2. Ensure that you have filled in all necessary fields before submitting the form.
3. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (required): This is the name of your organization.
* **Program Name** (required): This is the name of the digital loyalty program you are applying for.
* **Program Type** (required, select_one): Select 'Yes' if you are applying for the digital loyalty program.
*   • Yes: I am applying for the digital loyalty program.
* **Program Description** (required): Briefly describe your digital loyalty program.
* **Contact Person** (required): Enter the name of the person responsible for the application.
* **Contact Email** (required): Enter the email address of the person responsible for the application.
* **Phone Number** (required): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the application.
* **Organization Address** (required): Enter the address of your organization.
* **Program URL** (optional): If you have a website for your digital loyalty program, enter the URL here. This is not a required field.
* **Certification Body** (required, select_one): Select 'Yes' if your organization has been certified by a certification body.
*   • Yes: I have been certified by a certification body.
* **Certification Date** (required): Enter the date of your certification.
* **Certification Expiration Date** (required): Enter the expiration date of your certification.
* **Renewal Date** (optional): If your certification requires renewal, enter the renewal date here. This is not a required field.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact our support team.
