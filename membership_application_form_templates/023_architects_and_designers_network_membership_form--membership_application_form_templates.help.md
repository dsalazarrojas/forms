# Architects And Designers Network Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide the Architects And Designers Network with information about you and your firm to help us verify your credentials and determine your membership level.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

* Fill in your personal and professional information, including your name, email address, phone number, and mailing address.
* Select your professional discipline from the list provided.
* Enter your firm's name and address, if applicable.
* Specify your years of professional experience.
* Choose your areas of specialization.
* If you have professional licenses or registrations, provide your status and license number.
* Indicate your desired membership level (Individual, Student, Firm, or Corporate).
* Select your preferred method of payment for the membership fee (Check, Credit card, Bank transfer, or Invoice me).
* Check the areas of interest you are interested in (Networking events, Professional development, Project showcases, or Mentoring programs).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: <code>Member Name</code> (Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address**: <code>Member Email</code> (Required): Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number**: <code>Member Phone</code> (Required): Enter your phone number.
* **Mailing Address**: <code>Member Mailing Address</code> (Required): Enter your mailing address.
* **Professional Discipline**: <code>Profession</code> (Required): Select one or more of the following disciplines:
	+ Architect
	+ Designer
	+ Interior designer
	+ Landscape architect
	+ Urban planner
	+ Engineer
	+ Other
* **Firm or Company Name**: <code>Firm Name</code> (Required): Enter the name of your firm or company.
* **Firm Address**: <code>Firm Address</code> (Optional): Enter your firm's address.
* **Firm Phone Number**: <code>Firm Phone</code> (Optional): Enter your firm's phone number.
* **Years of Professional Experience**: <code>Years of Experience</code> (Required): Enter the number of years you have been working in your professional discipline.
* **Areas of Specialization**: <code>Areas of Specialization</code> (Optional): Select one or more of the following areas of specialization:
	+ Commercial architecture
	+ Residential design
	+ Interior design
	+ Landscape design
	+ Urban design
	+ Sustainable design
	+ Heritage restoration
* **Professional License Status**: <code>Licensure Status</code> (Required): Select your current professional license status:
	+ Licensed
	+ Registered
	+ Certified
	+ Not licensed
	+ In progress
* **License or Registration Number**: <code>License Number</code> (Optional): Enter your professional license or registration number.
* **Issuing State or Country**: <code>Issuing State or Country</code> (Optional): Enter the state or country that issued your license.
* **Membership Level Desired**: <code>Membership Level</code> (Required): Select one of the following membership levels:
	+ Individual
	+ Student
	+ Firm
	+ Corporate
* **Membership Fee Payment Method**: <code>Membership Fee Confirmed</code> (Optional): Select your preferred method of payment for the membership fee:

* **Interested In**: <code>Interested In</code> (Optional): Check one or more of the following areas of interest:
