# Nurses Welfare Member's Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a registration form for new members to join the Nurses Welfare organization. It collects essential information for membership purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields, marked as `true`.
2. Double-check your information before submission.
3. Review the form carefully before completing it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts_list_page`, select multiple, required):
	+ Select all your emergency contacts from the given options (John and Jane Doe).
* **Address** (`address_list_page`, select multiple, required):
	+ Choose the type of address you would like to register (Home, Work, Other).
* **Medical License Number** (`medical_license_number_page`, text, required):
	+ Enter your medical license number.
* **Medical License Type** (`medical_license_type_page`, select one, required):
	+ Select your medical license type (MD, RN, NP).
* **Date Issued** (`medical_license_issued_date_page`, date, required):
	+ Enter the date your medical license was issued (in YYYY-MM format).
* **Date Expires** (`medical_license_expires_date_page`, date, required):
	+ Enter the date your medical license expires (in YYYY-MM format).
* **Medical School Attended** (`medical_school_attended_page`, text, required):
	+ Enter the name of your medical school.
* **Medical License Number** (`medical_license_number_page_2`, text, required):
	+ Enter any additional information about your medical license number.
* **State** (`medical_license_state_page`, text, required):
	+ Enter the state your medical license is issued in.
* **Address Line 1** (`address_line_1_page`, text, required):
	+ Enter your address line 1.
* **Address Line 2** (`address_line_2_page`, text, required):
	+ Enter your address line 2.
* **City** (`city_page`, text, required):
	+ Enter the city of your address.
* **Zip** (`zip_page`, text, required):
	+ Enter the zip of your address.
* **Medical License Number** (`medical_license_number_page_3`, text, required):
	+ Enter any additional information about your medical license number.
* **Phone** (`phone_page`, text, required):
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email_page`, email, required):
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Membership Status** (`membership_status_page_2`, select one, required):
	+ Select your membership status (Active, Inactive, Pending).
