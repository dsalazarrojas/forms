# Rotary Club Membership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rotary Club Membership Application Form is a tool for individuals to apply for membership in the Rotary Club. This form helps club administrators review and assess your application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Email**, **Phone**, and **Address** fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Provide a brief explanation for **Reason for Joining**.
3. List any **Previous Affiliations** you have with other organizations or Rotary clubs.
4. Describe your understanding of **Privileges and Responsibilities** as a member of the Rotary Club.
5. Once you have completed all fields, click the **Submit** button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Please enter your first name as it is listed on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Please enter your last name as it is listed on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your primary email address where you can be contacted by the club administrators.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached by the club administrators.
* **Address**: Enter your current address where you reside.
* **Reason for Joining**: Provide a brief explanation of why you are interested in joining the Rotary Club. This can include your personal goals, values, or how you think you can contribute to the club.
* **Previous Affiliations**: List any other organizations or Rotary clubs you have been a part of in the past.
* **Privileges and Responsibilities**: Describe what you understand as privileges and responsibilities of being a member of the Rotary Club. This is your opportunity to express your commitment to the club's values and principles.
* **Submit**: Once you have reviewed and completed all fields, click this button to submit your application for review.
