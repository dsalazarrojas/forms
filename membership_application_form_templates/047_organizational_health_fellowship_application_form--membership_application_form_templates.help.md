1. Start by filling out the "Main Application" fields, which ask for the most essential information about your organization.
2. Move on to the "Main Application Context" fields, where you will describe the context in which your organization operates.
3. Continue to the "Main Application Experience" fields, which ask about your experience and skills.
4. Next, fill out the "Main Application Motivation" fields, where you will explain why you are applying.
5. Fill out the "Main Application Organization" fields, where you will describe your organization and its mission.
