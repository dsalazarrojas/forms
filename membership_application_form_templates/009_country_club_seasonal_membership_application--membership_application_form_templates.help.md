# Country Club Seasonal Membership Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Country Club Seasonal Membership Application is a form used to collect information from applicants for seasonal membership at a country club. To complete this form, please follow the steps below.

## How To Complete This Form
To ensure a smooth and accurate application process, please note the following:

* Please fill out all required fields.
* Select the correct membership type from the "Membership Selection" field.
* Sign the "E-Signature" field with your electronic signature.
* Enter a valid email address in the "Email" field.
* Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
* Provide any additional notes in the "Notes" field.
* Choose the correct season and date for your membership in the "Season" and "Season Start" fields.
* Enter the correct time for your membership in the "Time Start" and "Time End" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information (1)**: This section is where you should enter your name.
* **Membership Selection (2)**: Choose the type of membership you wish to apply for: Basic, Premium, or VIP.
* **E-Signature (3)**: Enter your electronic signature in this field.
* **Email (4)**: Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone (5)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Notes (6)**: Add any additional notes or comments about your application.
* **Date (7)**: Enter the date for your seasonal membership.
* **Time (8)**: Enter the time for your seasonal membership.
* **Agreement (9)**: This field is for any additional notes about your agreement.
* **Submit (10)**: This is the submit button to complete your application.
* **Name (11)**: Enter your name.
* **Season (12)**: Choose the correct season for your membership.
* **Country (13)**: Enter your country of residence.
* **Address (14)**: Enter your address.
* **City (15)**: Enter the city where you reside.
* **State (16)**: Enter the state where you reside.
* **Zip (17)**: Enter your zip code.
* **E-Signature Required (18)**: If required, enter your electronic signature again.
* **Signature (19)**: Enter your signature.
* **Agreement Signed (20)**: If required, sign this field to indicate that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions.
* **Season Start (21)**: Choose the start date of your season.
* **Season End (22)**: Choose the end date of your season.
* **Time Start (23)**: Choose the start time of your season.
* **Time End (24)**: Choose the end time of your season.
* **Agreement Checked (25)**: Select "I have read and agreed to the terms and conditions" if you have indeed read and agreed to the terms and conditions, or choose "Other" if this does not apply to you.

Please note that some fields are required and must be filled out accurately to ensure a successful application. Ensure that you have read and understood the terms and conditions before completing the agreement fields.
