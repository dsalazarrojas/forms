# Best Sellers Club Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Best Sellers Club Membership Form is designed to help the Best Sellers Club onboard successful sellers onto our platform. This form collects essential information about sellers, their business operations, and sales strategies to ensure that only high-quality businesses can join the club.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Seller Information**: Provide your full name, business name, and email address to identify yourself and your business.
2. **Sales Profile**: Select your primary sales platform, categorize your goods, and state your years in business to describe your sales operations.
3. **Performance and Reputation**: Share your average customer rating, order fulfillment speed, and top-selling product to showcase your performance.
4. **Membership Application**: Explain why you want to join the Best Sellers Club, what you can contribute to the community, and finally, agree to the code of conduct and ethics of the club.
5. **Final Details**: Submit your application date and agree to the terms of membership.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Provide your full name as it appears on your business cards or identification.
* **Business Name**: Enter the registered name of your business or enterprise.
* **Business Email**: Enter your business email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number for communication and verification purposes.
* **Business Website**: Enter the URL of your online store or profile for reference.
* **Primary Sales Platform**: Select where you conduct most of your business (e.g., Amazon, eBay, Etsy, Shopify, etc.).
* **Category of Goods**: Select your primary niche or category of goods sold (e.g., Electronics and Tech, Fashion and Apparel, etc.).
* **Years in Business**: Choose how long you've been selling (e.g., Less than 1 year, 1 to 3 years, etc.).
* **Average Monthly Sales**: Select your average monthly sales range (e.g., Under $1,000, $5,001 - $10,000, etc.).
* **Number of SKUs or Products**: Enter the total number of unique products listed.
* **Average Customer Rating**: Enter your average customer rating out of 5 stars.
* **Order Fulfillment Speed**: Select your order fulfillment speed (e.g., Under 24 hours, 1 to 2 days, etc.).
* **Top-Selling Product**: Describe your top-selling product and its unique features.
* **Customer Return Policy**: Describe your return and refund process in detail.
* **Club Membership Application**: Explain your reason for joining the club, what you can contribute to the community, and your goals for membership.
* **Code of Conduct Agreement**: Agree to the club's code of conduct and ethics.
* **Date of Application**: Set the date of your membership application submission.
* **Final Details**: Review your form before submission and ensure all fields are accurate and complete.
