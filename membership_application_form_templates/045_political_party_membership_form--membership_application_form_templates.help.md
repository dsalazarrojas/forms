## Purpose
This form is intended for individuals who want to express their interest in joining or learning more about the political party. It is not a formal membership application, but rather a way for the party to collect contact information and general details about potential members or supporters.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. Make sure to provide accurate contact information.
3. Answer all questions to the best of your ability.
4. Review the form before submitting it.
