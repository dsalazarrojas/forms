# Labor Union Membership Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Labor Union Membership Intake Form is used to collect information about a member's work history and union employment status.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name, last name, and email address.
2. Provide your work history, including the start and end dates of your employment.
3. Select the type of union you are a member of.
4. Enter your job title and job status.
5. Enter your work hours and union employment information.
6. Enter your work address, union address, phone numbers, and manager's contact information.
7. Enter your work and union IBANs, and tax IDs.
8. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address associated with your work or union.
* **Work History** (`work_history`, `text`, required): Briefly describe your work history, including your job title, work status, and employment dates.
* **Union Type** (`union_type`, `text`, required): Select the type of union you are a member of (e.g., labor union, union local, etc.).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your current or most recent job title.
* **Work Start** (`work_start`, `date`, required): Enter the start date of your most recent employment.
* **Work End** (`work_end`, `date`, required): Enter the end date of your most recent employment.
* **Union Years** (`union_years`, `number`, required): Enter the number of years you have been a union member.
* **Work Status** (`work_status`, `text`, required): Describe your current or most recent work status (e.g., full-time, part-time, etc.).
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, `text`, required): Describe your current or most recent job status (e.g., active, inactive, etc.).
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, `number`, required): Enter the number of hours you work per week.
* **Union Employment** (`union_employment`, `text`, required): Describe your union employment status (e.g., union member, union officer, etc.).
* **Work Type** (`work_type`, `text`, required): Enter your work type (e.g., salaried, hourly, etc.).
* **Work Address** (`work_address`, `text`, required): Enter your work address.
* **Union Address** (`union_address`, `text`, required): Enter your union's address.
* **Work Phone** (`work_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your work phone number.
* **Union Phone** (`union_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the union's phone number.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, `text`, required): Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, `email`, required): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Union Status** (`union_status`, `text`, required): Describe your union status (e.g., active, inactive, etc.).
* **Work IBAN** (`work_iban`, `text`, required): Enter your work IBAN (International Bank Account Number).
* **Union IBAN** (`union_iban`, `text`, required): Enter the union's IBAN (International Bank Account Number).
* **Work Tax ID** (`work_tax_id`, `text`, required): Enter your work tax ID number.
* **Union Tax ID** (`union_tax_id`, `text`, required): Enter the union's tax ID number.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, contact the union office for guidance.
* This form is for informational purposes only, and your union will use the information to update their records and membership database.
