# Volunteers Hub Liberia New Volunteer SignUp Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Volunteers Hub Liberia New Volunteer SignUp Form" is designed to collect information from potential volunteers who are interested in joining the organization. By filling out this form, you'll help us get to know you better and ensure that you're a good fit for our volunteer opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. For fields with multiple options, select all that apply.
3. Be honest and thorough in your responses, especially when answering the "How did you find us?" question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
• **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, Required/Optional): Your first name will help us get to know you better. Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Last Name
• **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, Required/Optional): Your last name will help us confirm your identity and connect with your family members or contacts. Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Email
• **Email** (`email`, `email`, Required/Optional): Your email address will be used to contact you for further information about volunteer opportunities. Please use a valid email address that you check regularly.

### Phone
• **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, Required/Optional): Your phone number will allow us to reach you for urgent volunteer opportunities or schedule changes. Please enter your phone number in the international format (e.g., +231 20XXXXXXX).

### Date of Birth
• **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, Required/Optional): This field is for your date of birth, which helps us understand your age group and preferences.

### Gender
• **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, Required/Optional): Please select your gender to help us understand your identity and preferences.

### Volunteer Type
• **Volunteer Type** (`volunteer_type`, `select_multiple`, Required/Optional): You can select all that apply to indicate your areas of interest and expertise. Please choose from:
	+ Volunteer Coordinator
	+ Volunteer

### Available Days
• **Available Days** (`available_days`, `select_multiple`, Required/Optional): Indicate which days you are available to volunteer. Please select all applicable days from the list:
	+ Monday
	+ Tuesday
	+ Wednesday
	+ Thursday
	+ Friday
	+ Saturday
	+ Sunday

### Available Time
• **Available Time** (`available_time`, `time`, Required/Optional): If applicable, please provide your available time slots for volunteering. This will help us schedule you for opportunities that fit your schedule.

### Bio
• **Bio** (`bio`, `note`, Required/Optional): This is your opportunity to tell us a little about yourself, your motivations, and why you're interested in volunteering with us.

### How did you find us?
• **How did you find us?** (`how_did_you_find_us`, `select_one`, Required/Optional): Please select how you came across Volunteers Hub Liberia:
	+ Social Media
	+ Website
	+ Referral
	+ Other

Note: Please answer this question honestly, as it helps us understand how to improve our outreach and communication.
