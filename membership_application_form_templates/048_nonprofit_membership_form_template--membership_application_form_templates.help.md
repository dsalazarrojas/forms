# Nonprofit Membership Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nonprofit Membership Form Template is designed to collect membership information from individuals interested in joining a nonprofit organization. The form gathers contact and membership details to facilitate the process of becoming a member.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your User Information in the User Information field.
2. Provide your Contact Information in the Contact Information field.
3. Select your preferred Membership Plan from the options available.
4. Choose your preferred Payment Method from the available options.
5. Specify your current Membership Status.
6. Enter your Membership Date (if applicable).
7. Enter your Membership Expiration Date (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`user_info`, `text`, `required: false`): Enter your name and contact details for our records.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, `required: false`): Provide your contact information for future communication.
* **Membership Plan** (`membership_plan`, `select_one`, `required: false`): Select one of the available membership plans: Basic, Premium, or Enterprise.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`): Choose your preferred payment method: Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer.
* **Membership Status** (`membership_status`, `select_one`, `required: false`): Specify your current membership status: Active, Inactive, or Suspended.
* **Membership Date** (`membership_date`, `date`, `required: false`): Enter your membership start date (if applicable).
* **Membership Expiration** (`membership_expiration`, `date`, `required: false`): Enter your membership expiration date (if applicable).
