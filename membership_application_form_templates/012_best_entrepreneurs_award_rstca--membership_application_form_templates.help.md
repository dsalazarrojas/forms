# Best Entrepreneurs Award RSTCA - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Best Entrepreneurs Award RSTCA is a form designed to collect information from potential award recipients. This form helps the award committee evaluate businesses for their achievements and future goals. It is essential to provide accurate and truthful information to increase the chances of being selected for the award.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the form to begin.
2.  Fill in your personal information, including your full name, date of birth, email, and phone number.
3.  Provide your residential address.
4.  Enter your business details, including your business name, position, and industry category.
5.  Specify your business's revenue range and employee count.
6.  Select the award category you are applying for.
7.  Provide a brief executive summary of your business model and mission.
8.  List your key achievements and future goals.
9.  Provide a professional reference's name, contact information, and LinkedIn profile URL (if available).
10.  Certify that all information provided is true and accurate.
11.  Finally, submit the form by providing the date of application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (`applicant_full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your passport or government-issued ID.
*   **Date of Birth** (`applicant_dob`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Email Address** (`applicant_email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
*   **Phone Number** (`applicant_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
*   **Residential Address** (`applicant_residential_address`, text, required): Enter your current residential address.
*   **Business Name** (`business_legal_name`, text, required): Enter the registered name of your business.
*   **Position** (`applicant_position`, text, required): Describe your role within the business.
*   **Industry Sector** (`business_industry_category`, select_one, required): Select the category your business falls under (e.g., Technology and Software, Healthcare and Wellness, etc.).
*   **Year Business was Founded** (`year_founded`, number, required): Enter the year your business was founded.
*   **Number of Full-time Employees** (`employee_count`, number, required): Enter the current number of full-time employees in your company.
*   **Annual Revenue Range** (`annual_revenue_bracket`, select_one, required): Select the revenue range of your business (e.g., Under $100,000, $100,000 - $500,000, etc.).
*   **Category for Award** (`award_category_choice`, select_one, required): Select the category for which you are applying (e.g., Young Entrepreneur of the Year, Social Entrepreneurship Award, etc.).
*   **Executive Summary** (`business_executive_summary`, text, required): Provide a brief description of your business model and mission.
*   **Recent Key Achievements** (`recent_key_achievements`, text, required): List your key achievements and successes in the last 12 months.
*   **Future Business Goals** (`future_business_goals_desc`, text, required): Describe your plans for your business in 3-5 years.
*   **Professional Reference Name** (`professional_reference_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your professional reference.
*   **Reference Contact Information** (`reference_contact_info`, text, required): Enter your professional reference's contact information (email or phone number).
*   **LinkedIn Profile URL** (`linkedin_profile_url`, text, optional): If you have a LinkedIn profile, provide the URL.
*   **I certify that all information provided is true and accurate** (`information_accuracy_cert`, select_one, required): Confirm that all information provided is true and accurate.
*   **Date of Application** (`application_submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date you submit this application.
