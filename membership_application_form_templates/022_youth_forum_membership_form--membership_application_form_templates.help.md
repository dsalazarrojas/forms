# youth_forum_membership_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect contact information from individuals interested in joining the youth forum.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the first name field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the last name field.
3. Enter your **Address** in the address field.
4. Enter your **City** in the city field.
5. Make sure to fill out all the fields as accurately and completely as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false)
* Your first name will help us know your identity better.
### **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false)
* Your last name will help us know your identity better.
### **Address** (`address`, text, required: false)
* Your address will help us know where to contact you.
### **City** (`city`, text, required: false)
* Your city will help us know where you live.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the fields accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions, feel free to ask us.
