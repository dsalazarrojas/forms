# Business Marketing New Member Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for businesses to apply for membership in the Business Marketing New Member Application Form. This form is intended for businesses that want to join the membership program and gain access to exclusive benefits. The membership program is open to individual businesses, small businesses, and corporations. To ensure that each member is well-represented, please fill out this application form accurately and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each page from top to bottom. The form is divided into different sections, each with its own set of fields that require your information. Make sure to fill out all required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant name** (`applicant_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Company name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter your company name or organization name.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address that will be used for membership communications.
* **Membership type** (`membership_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of membership that best suits your business needs:
	+ Individual: For individual business owners or representatives.
	+ Small business: For small business owners or representatives.
	+ Corporate: For corporate businesses or large organizations.
* **Industry** (`industry`, `text`, required): Enter your primary industry sector.
* **Marketing focus areas** (`marketing_focus_areas`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the areas where you would like to see support:
	+ Digital marketing
	+ Content creation
	+ Lead generation
	+ Branding
* **Agree to membership terms** (`agree_terms`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you have read and agree to the membership terms by selecting "True."
* **Referral source** (`referral_source`, `text`, optional): If you were referred to the membership program by someone else, please enter their name or the name of their organization.
* **Additional information** (`additional_information`, `text`, optional): Enter any other details that you think would be helpful for the membership team to know.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your email address for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the membership team for assistance.
* Membership applications will be reviewed and processed within 3-5 business days.
