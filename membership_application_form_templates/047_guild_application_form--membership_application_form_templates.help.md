# Guild Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your interest in joining a guild on our platform. It helps us understand your background, motivations, and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the following fields, providing accurate and detailed information. Note that some fields are optional, marked as such. Ensure you fill out all required fields to increase your chances of being considered for a guild membership.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, note, optional): Briefly introduce yourself and your background. This can be a short paragraph or a few sentences.
* **name** (`Name`, text, optional): Provide your name, either as an individual or as a representative of your organization or company.
* **guild name** (`Guild Name`, text, optional): Indicate your interest in joining a specific guild or suggest a potential new guild name if you wish to create a new one.
* **location** (`Location`, text, optional): Mention your primary work location or a location you're interested in working with.
* **role** (`Role`, text, optional): Describe the role you're interested in playing within the guild or your current occupation.
* **experience** (`Experience`, text, optional): Summarize your relevant experience or skills that align with the guild's goals.
* **why join** (`Why Join`, text, optional): Explain why you're interested in joining this guild, highlighting your motivations and expectations.
* **availability** (`Availability`, text, optional): Specify your availability for guild activities, meetings, or commitments.
* **contact info** (`Contact Info`, email, optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached for further communication.
* **submit** (`Submit`, select multiple, optional): Check the boxes to indicate your willingness to proceed with the application process (Yes) or not (No).
