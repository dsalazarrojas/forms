# Membership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect membership information from individuals who want to become part of an organization. It includes fields for personal details, membership type, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** fields with your personal details.
2. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Select your **Membership Type** from the options provided.
4. Provide the **Emergency Contact Name** and **Emergency Contact Relationship** of someone who can be contacted in case of emergency.
5. Enter your **Email** and **Phone Number** for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Your personal first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Your personal last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Membership Type** (`membership_type`, select_one, required): The type of membership you are applying for (Full, Junior, or Social).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): The name of the person you have designated as your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required): Your relationship with the emergency contact (e.g., spouse, parent, etc.).
* **Email** (`email`, text, required): A valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): A valid phone number for contact purposes.
