# Startup Hub Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Startup Hub Membership Form is a one-page form designed to collect basic information from applicants for membership in the Startup Hub community. The form is intended to be simple and easy to use, with clear labels and instructions to guide users through the application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Application Information** section with your name, email address, and any other relevant details about your application.
2. In the **Membership Details** section, select the type of membership you are interested in (e.g. Basic, Premium, etc.).
3. In the **Contact Information** section, provide your contact name and phone number.
4. In the **Address Information** section, enter your address for billing or shipping purposes.
5. In the **Application** section, provide a brief description of your startup.
6. In the **Notes** section, add any additional comments or questions you have.
7. If applicable, upload a file related to your application.
8. Finally, review your submission and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Information**: 
  * **Name**: Enter your name.
  * **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
  * **Application Details**: Enter a brief description of your application.
* **Membership Details**:
  * **Membership Type**: Select the type of membership you are interested in (e.g. Basic, Premium, etc.).
* **Contact Information**:
  * **Contact Name**: Enter your name as the contact person.
  * **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Address Information**:
  * **Address**: Enter your address for billing or shipping purposes.
* **Application**:
  * **Application Description**: Provide a brief description of your startup.
* **Notes**:
  * **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or questions you have.
* **Upload File**: If applicable, upload a file related to your application.
* **Confirmation**: Review your submission before submitting.
* **Submission**: Submit your application.
