# young-member-group-expression-of-interest - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for young members to express their interest in joining a community group. The form allows users to provide their contact details, list their interests, share their motivations for joining, and include any additional notes about themselves and their interest in the group. The goal is to provide a clear and concise way for young members to introduce themselves and demonstrate their fit with the group's values and goals.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your contact details in the `Contact Details` section. This includes your name and any additional information you'd like to share with the group.
2.  Select your interests from the list in the `Interests` section. Choose all that apply to help the group understand your motivations and goals.
3.  Write a brief description of your motivations for joining the group in the `Motivations` section. This will help the group understand why you're interested in joining and what you can bring to the community.
4.  Enter your email address in the `Email` field. This will help the group stay in touch with you and keep you informed about upcoming events and activities.
5.  Enter your phone number in the `Phone` field. This is optional, but it will help the group contact you if needed.
6.  If you have any additional comments or notes about yourself or your interests, you can include them in the `Note` field.
7.  Finally, add a brief description of your expression of interest in the `Expression Of Interest` field. This is a chance to tell the group about your passions, goals, and any specific projects or activities you'd like to get involved with in the group.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required/optional): This is the first section where you'll enter your name, contact address, phone number, or any other additional contact information you'd like to share with the group. Please enter your information clearly and accurately so that the group can get in touch with you.
*   **Interests** (`interests`, select_multiple, required/optional): In this section, please select all that apply from the list of interests that resonate with you. This will help the group understand your motivations and goals.
*   **Motivations** (`motivations`, text, required/optional): Write a brief description of your motivations for joining the group. This should include your thoughts on why you'd like to join and what you can bring to the community.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address so that the group can stay in touch with you and keep you informed about upcoming events and activities.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): This is an optional field, but please enter your phone number so that the group can contact you if needed.
*   **Note** (`note`, note, required/optional): If you have any additional comments or notes about yourself or your interests, you can include them here.
*   **Expression Of Interest** (`expression_of_interest`, note, required/optional): Write a brief description of your expression of interest, including your passions, goals, and any specific projects or activities you'd like to get involved with in the group.
