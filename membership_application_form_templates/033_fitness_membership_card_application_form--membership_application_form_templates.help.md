# Fitness Membership Card Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you apply for a fitness membership card. Please fill out the form with your information to create a new membership application.

## How To Complete This Form

- **Before starting**: Ensure you have all the necessary information to fill out this form. This includes your preferred membership period, card type, membership card number, start and end dates, and membership status.
- **Submit your application**: Once you have filled out all the required fields, submit the form to complete your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Membership Period
* **Membership Period (Monthly, 3-Month, 6-Month, 12-Month)**: Choose the length of time you would like your membership to be active.
  * Please select the option that fits your needs best.
  * This will determine when your membership will expire.

### Membership Card Type
* **Membership Card Type (Type 1, Type 2, Type 3)**: Choose the type of membership card that suits your needs.
  * Please select the option that fits your needs best.
  * This will help determine the benefits and services you will have access to.

### Membership Card Number
* **Membership Card Number**: Enter a unique number for your membership card.
  * This is a required field and can be a number, letter or any combination of them.
  * Make sure it is not already taken by someone else.

### Membership Start Date
* **Membership Start Date**: Enter the date you want your membership to start from.
  * This can be any date in the past or future.
  * Ensure it is in the correct format (yyyy-mm-dd).

### Membership End Date
* **Membership End Date**: Enter the date you want your membership to end.
  * This can be any date in the future.
  * Ensure it is in the correct format (yyyy-mm-dd).

### Membership Status
* **Membership Status (Active, Inactive, Expired)**: Choose the status of your membership.
  * This will determine how your membership is seen by the system.
  * Active will give you access to all services, inactive will mean you will not have access to services until you activate it, and expired will mean you are no longer active.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check all the fields for correct information.
* You can always contact us if you have questions or concerns.
* We will review your application after submission and get back to you with the results.
* Membership cards will be delivered to you once approved.
