# Wabc | Membership Form 2020 2021 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wabc | Membership Form 2020 2021 is a template used by Wabc to collect information from applicants interested in joining their non-profit organization. This form is designed to gather relevant information from applicants and streamline the membership application process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, select the correct "Membership Application" option from the "Membership Application" dropdown list. This will help us understand your interest in joining Wabc.
2. Please enter your name and any other relevant information in the "Membership Form" field.
3. If you are applying on behalf of a non-profit organization, please enter the name of the organization in the "Non-Profit Organisation" field.
4. If you are applying as a volunteer or on behalf of a volunteer organization, please enter the relevant information in the "Volunteers or Volunteer Organisation" field.
5. Finally, if you would like to share this form with others, you can specify this in the "Share Form amongst other" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Membership Application** (`Membership Application`, select_one, required):
  + This field allows you to select the type of membership application you are submitting. Please choose "Boxing Club Members 2020-2021" if you are interested in joining as a member or "Other" if your application is for a different type of membership.
* **Membership Form 2020 2021** (`Membership Form 2020 2021`, text, required):
  + Please enter your name and any other relevant information in this field.
* **Membership Application Form Templates** (`Membership Application Form Templates`, text, optional):
  + This field is not required, but you can provide additional information about your application if you need to.
* **Non-Profit Organisation** (`Non-Profit Organisation`, text, optional):
  + If you are applying on behalf of a non-profit organization, please enter the name of the organization.
* **Volunteers or Volunteer Organisation** (`Volunteers or Volunteer Organisation`, text, optional):
  + If you are applying as a volunteer or on behalf of a volunteer organization, please enter the relevant information in this field.
* **Share Form amongst other** (`Share Form amongst other`, text, optional):
  + If you would like to share this form with others, please specify how you would like to share it.
