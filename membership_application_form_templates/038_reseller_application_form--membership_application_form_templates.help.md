# reseller_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is intended to assist users in completing the reseller application form, which is used to apply for a reseller account with the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Click on the "Start" button to begin the application process.
2. Fill out each page of the form, working your way through the questions and fields from top to bottom.
3. Be sure to review each page carefully and ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
4. Once you have completed all the pages, click the "Submit" button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`page_1`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your business.
* **Email Address** (`page_2`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (`page_3`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`page_4`, `text`, required): Enter your address.
* **Date of Birth** (`page_5`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth.
* **Time Zone** (`page_6`, `time`, required): Enter your time zone.
* **Reseller Account** (`page_7`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" to apply for a reseller account.
* **Reseller Account Options** (`page_8`, `select_multiple`, required): Select multiple options for your reseller account.
* **Second Time Zone** (`page_9`, `time`, required): Enter your second time zone.
* **Second Email Address** (`page_10`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Reseller Account Options** (`page_11`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" to apply for a reseller account.
* **Select Multiple Options** (`page_12`, `select_multiple`, required): Select multiple options for your reseller account.
* **Second Phone Number** (`page_13`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Second Address** (`page_14`, `text`, required): Enter your address.
* **Second Date of Birth** (`page_15`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth.
* **Second Notes** (`page_16`, `note`, required): Enter your notes.
* **Reseller Account** (`page_17`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" to apply for a reseller account.
* **Select Multiple Options** (`page_18`, `select_multiple`, required): Select multiple options for your reseller account.

Note: This guide is intended to be a general guide and is not specific to any particular business or organization. The fields and options listed are based on the provided YAML fields and may not be exhaustive or up-to-date.
