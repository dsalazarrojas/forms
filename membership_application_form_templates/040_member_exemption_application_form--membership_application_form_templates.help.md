# Member Exemption Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Member Exemption Application Form is designed to gather information from members of an organization who need to apply for an exemption. This form helps ensure that all required information is collected accurately, and it is used to evaluate and process exemption requests.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please ensure you have all the necessary information and documentation before starting to fill out this form.
2.  Fill in the organization name where you are a member.
3.  Provide the contact person's name and email address.
4.  Enter your contact phone number.
5.  Specify the reason for your exemption request.
6.  Select your member type (Student, Faculty, or Member).
7.  Enter the start and end dates of your exemption period.
8.  Choose the current exemption status (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
9.  Add any additional comments if required.
10. Enter your organization's address.
11. Enter the exemption application number (if available).
12. Fill in the exemption application date.
13. Select the current exemption application status (Approved, Rejected, or Pending).
14. Select the files to submit as part of your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the person you would like to be contacted about your application.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Exemption Reason** (`exemption_reason`, text, required): Clearly describe the reason for your exemption request.
* **Member Type** (`member_type`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more of the following options: Student, Faculty, Member.
* **Exemption Start Date** (`exemption_start_date`, date, required): Enter the date when your exemption period starts.
* **Exemption End Date** (`exemption_end_date`, date, required): Enter the date when your exemption period ends.
* **Exemption Status** (`exemption_status`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options: Active, Inactive, Pending.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, required): Add any additional comments that are not covered in the other fields.
* **Organization Address** (`organization_address`, text, required): Enter your organization's address.
* **Exemption Application Number** (`exemption_application_number`, number, required): Enter the number assigned to your exemption application.
* **Exemption Application Date** (`exemption_application_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your exemption application submission.
* **Exemption Application Status** (`exemption_application_status`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more of the following options: Approved, Rejected, Pending.
* **Submission** (`submission`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more of the following options: File1, File2, File3.
