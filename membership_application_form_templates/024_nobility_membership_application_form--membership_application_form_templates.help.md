# Nobility Membership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for applicants seeking membership in a nobility order. The purpose of this form is to gather information about the applicant and their motivation for joining the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately. This form serves as the primary means of communication between the applicant and the nobility order.
2.  Make sure to enter your contact information accurately, including your email address and phone number.
3.  If you have any affiliations with other noble orders, please indicate them accurately.
4.  Provide clear and concise statements for all declarations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Details** (`user_details`, text, Required): Enter your full name and any other relevant information that may be requested by the nobility order.
*   **Membership Details** (`membership_details`, text, Required): Enter your motivation for joining the nobility order. This is the reason why you are interested in joining this order.
