# Political Party Membership Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Political Party Membership Application form is used to gather information from individuals who wish to join or maintain their membership status with the political party. This form is essential for processing membership applications and ensuring accurate and up-to-date member information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Select your membership type in the "membership_type" field.
6. Indicate your membership status in the "membership_status" field.
7. If you're a current member and leaving the party, provide the reason for leaving in the "reason_for_leaving" field. Note: This field is only visible if you have already provided your current membership status.
8. Once you have filled out all required fields, click "Submit" to complete your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name (text, required)**: Enter your first name.
* **last_name (text, required)**: Enter your last name.
* **email (text, email, required)**: Enter your valid email address.
* **phone (text, required)**: Enter your phone number.
* **address (text, optional)**: Enter your address. This field is not required.
* **membership_type (select_one, required)**: Select one of the following membership types:
	+ full member
	+ affiliate member
	+ supporting member
* **membership_status (select_one, required)**: Select one of the following membership statuses:
	+ active
	+ inactive
* **reason_for_leaving (text, optional)**: If you are a current member leaving the party, provide the reason for leaving. This field is only visible if you have already provided your current membership status.
## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
- Enter your valid email address in the "email" field.
- If you are having trouble selecting your membership type or status, refer to the party's membership information on our website or contact the party's administration office.
- If you are a current member leaving the party, please provide the reason for leaving in the "reason_for_leaving" field.
