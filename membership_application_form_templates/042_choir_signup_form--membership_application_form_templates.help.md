## Choir Signup Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from potential members who wish to join the choir. It's a step-by-step guide to provide us with essential details about you as a singer, including your contact information, singing experience, and availability for rehearsals. By filling out this form, you'll be able to showcase your skills, goals, and motivations for joining our choir.

## How To Complete This Form

To ensure a smooth and accurate completion of this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Review each field carefully**, making sure to answer all required questions and provide honest answers.
2. **Fill out the form from top to bottom**, starting with your name and moving on to the subsequent fields.
3. **Take your time and be thorough**, providing as much detail as possible.
4. **Save your progress** if you need to leave the form temporarily.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Applicant Information (Page 1)

* **Applicant Information (Page 1)**: This section is for your general contact details.
	+ **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
	+ **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
	+ **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY (e.g., 02/12/2000).
	+ **Age**: Enter your age as of your current age.

### 2. Date of Birth and Age (Page 1)

* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY (e.g., 02/12/2000).
* **Age**: Enter your age as of your current age.

### 3. Gender and Contact Information (Page 2)

* **Gender**: Choose your gender identity from the provided options.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address for correspondence.

### 4. Singing Experience and Availability (Page 3)

* **Vocal Range**: Select your vocal range from the provided options.
* **Years of Singing Experience**: If applicable, enter the number of years you've been singing.
* **Availability**: Select your available days for rehearsals.
* **Preferred Rehearsal Time**: Choose a time slot you prefer for rehearsals.
* **Schedule Conflicts**: If you have any regular conflicts, describe them here.

### 5. Membership Interest and Goals (Page 4)

* **Membership Interest**: Explain why you want to join our choir.
* **Why Do You Want to Join**: Provide your personal goals for joining our choir.
* **Musical Goals**: Share your musical aspirations and objectives.

### 6. Performance Commitment and Additional Skills (Page 5)

* **Can you commit to performances?**: Indicate your availability for concerts and events.
* **Additional Skills**: If you have any other talents or skills, describe them here.

### 7. Referral and Agreement (Page 6)

* **Referral**: If applicable, provide the name of the person who referred you.
* **How Did You Hear About Us**: Choose how you heard about our choir.
* **I agree to the choir terms and conditions**: Accept our terms and conditions.
* **Digital Signature**: Enter your digital signature as proof of acceptance.

## Tips

* Please ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Be honest and accurate in your responses.
* If you have any concerns or questions, don't hesitate to reach out.
* This form is for your benefit, and your responses will help us tailor our choir to your needs.
