# Gaming Club Membership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gaming Club Membership Application Form is a tool used to collect information from potential members about themselves and their availability for club events. This information is used to facilitate the membership application process and ensure that members are aware of their responsibilities and commitments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user information (page 1).
2. Select your gaming hobby (page 2).
3. Enter your contact details (page 3).
4. Enter your email address (page 4).
5. Enter your phone number (page 5).
6. Select a specific date you are available (page 6).
7. Select a specific time you are available (page 7).
8. Add any additional notes about yourself or your membership application (page 8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, not required): This is a text field where you can enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself. Please enter your full name and any other details you feel are important for us to know.
* **Gaming Hobby** (`gaming_hobby`, select_multiple, not required): This is a multiple-select field where you can choose one or more of the following options:
	+ Gaming
	+ Esports
	+ PC Gaming
	+ Console Gaming
	+ Tabletop Gaming
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, not required): This is a text field where you can enter your address, phone number, or any other contact information you feel is relevant.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Please enter your email address in the format [example@example.com](mailto:example@example.com).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): This is a text field where you can enter your phone number.
* **Date Available** (`date_available`, date, not required): Please select a specific date when you are available to be contacted or attend club events.
* **Time Available** (`time_available`, time, not required): Please select a specific time when you are available to be contacted or attend club events.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, note, not required): This is a text field where you can enter any additional notes or information about yourself or your membership application.
