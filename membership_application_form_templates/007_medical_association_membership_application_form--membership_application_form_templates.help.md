# Medical Association Membership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for medical professionals to apply for membership in a medical association. It collects information about the applicant's medical degree, years of practice, license, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your basic information (page 1).
2. Answer questions about your medical degree and years of practice.
3. Fill out your medical practice details, including your practice name, address, phone number, and email.
4. Confirm your medical specialty and board certification status.
5. Enter the name, address, phone number, and email of the medical association you are applying to.
6. Review and submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Info**: Provide your name, title, and contact information.
	+ This field is used to identify you as the applicant.
	+ Your name and title will be used to address your membership application.
* **Medical Degree**: Describe your completed medical degree (e.g., MD, PhD, DO).
	+ This field is used to understand your medical qualifications.
	+ Please be specific and detailed about your degree.
* **Years of Practice**: Enter the number of years you have been in practice.
	+ This field helps the association assess your level of experience.
* **Medical License**: Enter your medical license number or a description of your licensure status (e.g., active, inactive, or pending).
	+ This field is used to verify your licensure status.
* **Medical Practice Info**: Describe your medical practice, including its location and type (e.g., private practice, hospital, clinic).
	+ This field helps the association understand your current practice setting.
* **Medical Practice Name**: Enter the name of your medical practice.
* **Medical Practice Address**: Enter the address of your medical practice.
* **Medical Practice Phone**: Enter the phone number of your medical practice.
* **Medical Practice Email**: Enter the email address of your medical practice.
* **Medical Specialty**: Check "Yes" if you have a medical specialty (e.g., cardiology, pediatrics).
	+ This field helps the association understand your areas of expertise.
* **Medical Board Certification**: Check "Yes" if you are board certified.
	+ This field is used to verify your advanced training and qualifications.
* **Medical Association Name**: Enter the name of the medical association you are applying to.
* **Medical Association Address**: Enter the address of the medical association.
* **Medical Association Phone**: Enter the phone number of the medical association.
* **Medical Association Email**: Enter the email address of the medical association.
* **Submit**: Review and submit your application.
	+ This field will submit your application for review.
