# technology_forum_membership_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The technology_forum_membership_application form is designed to collect necessary information from users who want to become members of our online technology forum. This form helps us to process your membership application and ensure that you have the necessary information to get started.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, Email, and Password. These are all required fields and must be filled in.
2. Choose your Membership Type from the list of options provided.
3. Enter any additional information you'd like to share about your Technology interests (e.g. Technology 1, Technology 2, etc.). This is optional and can be left blank if not relevant.
4. Choose a date and time you'd like to submit your application.
5. Review and submit your application.
