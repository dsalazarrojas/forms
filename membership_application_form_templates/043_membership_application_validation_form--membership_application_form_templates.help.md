# Membership Application Validation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from individuals applying for membership in a club or organization. It is a crucial step in the membership application process, and it helps us understand who you are and how we can contact you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your contact details, including your name, organization (if applicable), and address.
2. Then, provide your contact preferences, such as your preferred method of contact.
3. Next, enter your email address and phone number, making sure to format them correctly (e.g., `john.doe@example.com` for email and `123-456-7890` for phone).
4. Finally, enter your name and any additional comments or information you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details**: This field is where you can provide your name, organization (if you have one), and address.
* **Organization Details**: If you are applying as part of an organization, please enter the name and address of your organization.
* **Member Details**: Please enter your name as it should appear on your membership documents.
* **Contact Preferences**: Here, you can specify how you would like to be contacted (e.g., email, phone, mail).
* **Email**: Enter your email address, making sure it is correctly formatted (e.g., `john.doe@example.com`).
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number in the format `123-456-7890`.
* **Address**: Enter your physical address, including your street address and postal code.
* **Name**: Please enter your name as it should appear on your membership documents.
* **Tips**: You can attach any additional comments or information you'd like to share with us.
 
Note: All fields are optional unless otherwise specified.
