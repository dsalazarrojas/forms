# Gym Franchise Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gym Franchise Application Form is a document designed to gather information from potential gym franchisees about their background, investment capacity, and experience in order to assess their suitability as a gym franchisee.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the main page with your basic information.
2. On the second page, select either "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you have certain inquiries.
3. On the third page, provide your investment capacity.
4. On the fourth page, enter your relevant work experience.
5. On the fifth page, add any additional information you believe is relevant to your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main Page (Main Page, text, required: false)**: This is the initial page where you can provide a brief introduction about yourself and your interest in the gym franchise opportunity.
* **Applicant Inquiries (Select Multiple, required: true)**: Choose either "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you have had the following inquiries:
	+ Have you been contacted by a gym franchise consultant or representative?
	+ Have you been a member of a gym or fitness center?
* **Investment Capacity (Number, required: true)**: Enter your available investment capacity for the gym franchise, including any relevant financial information.
* **Applicant Experience (Date, required: true)**: Enter your relevant work experience in the form of dates and a brief description of your role and responsibilities.
* **Additional Inquiries (Text, required: false)**: If you have any additional information that you believe is relevant to your application, you can add it here.
