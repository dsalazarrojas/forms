# Art Gallery Membership Tier Application - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Art Gallery Membership Tier Application form is used to apply for membership at our art gallery. Fill out this form to provide your information and select your desired membership tier.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely, ensuring all required fields are filled in.
2. Provide accurate and up-to-date information for the applicant's name, email address, and contact information.
3. Select your desired membership tier from the options provided.
4. Choose your payment method (credit card, bank transfer, or invoice).
5. If you have a billing address or organization affiliation, provide it in the relevant field.
6. Consent to receive newsletters from the gallery by selecting "True" in the "Receive newsletter" field.
7. Provide an emergency contact person's name, if applicable.
8. Add any special member notes or requests.
9. Type your name as an electronic signature at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant name**: Enter your full name as it should be displayed on our membership records.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: Yes
* **Email address**: Enter your email address that you'd like to use for gallery communication.
	+ Type: email
	+ Required: Yes
* **Membership tier**: Select from one of our four tiers:
	+ Basic: For general membership benefits
	+ Supporting: For additional benefits and discounts
	+ Patron: For exclusive benefits and recognition
	+ Student: For student membership benefits
* **Payment method**: Choose how you'd like to pay for your membership:
	+ Credit card: Online payment through our secure payment gateway
	+ Bank transfer: Payment through a bank transfer or check
	+ Invoice: We'll send you an invoice for payment
* **Billing address**: Enter your address for membership billing purposes. (Optional)
* **Organization affiliation**: If you're affiliated with an organization, enter it here. (Optional)
* **Receive newsletter**: Consent to receive newsletters from the gallery.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: Yes
* **Emergency contact**: Enter an emergency contact person's name, if applicable.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Member notes**: Add any special membership requests or notes.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Signature**: Type your name as an electronic signature.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: Yes
