# Auto Club Membership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This help guide is designed to walk you through the process of filling out the Auto Club Membership Application Form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out your personal information, including first and last name.
2. Enter your date of birth.
3. Provide your email address and phone number.
4. Enter your mailing address.
5. Select your preferred membership tier and payment frequency.
6. Enter your vehicle's make, model, and year.
7. Provide the contact information for an emergency contact person.
8. Indicate whether you authorize auto-renewal of your membership.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your legal first name.
* **Last Name**: Your legal last name.
* **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Email Address**: The email address where you can receive digital membership updates and notifications.
* **Phone Number**: Your primary contact number.
* **Mailing Address**: Your physical address, including street, city, state, and zip code.
* **Membership Tier**: Select the level of coverage you need:
	+ Basic
	+ Plus
	+ Premier
	+ Family
* **Payment Frequency**: Choose your preferred payment frequency:
	+ Monthly
	+ Annually
* **Vehicle Make**: Enter the make of your vehicle (e.g. Nissan).
* **Vehicle Model**: Enter the model of your vehicle (e.g. Altima).
* **Vehicle Year**: Enter the year of your vehicle.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person to contact in an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Authorize Auto-Renewal**: Select whether to automatically renew your membership each period:
	+ 'True'
	+ 'False'
