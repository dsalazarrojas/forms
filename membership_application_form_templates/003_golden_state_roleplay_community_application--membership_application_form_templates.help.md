# Golden State Roleplay Community Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Golden State Roleplay Community Application is a form designed for interested individuals to express their interest in joining the community. It collects essential information to help our team evaluate applications and ensure a good fit for the community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with accurate and honest information.
2. For each field, make sure to provide a detailed and thoughtful response, as this will help our team evaluate your application.
3. Review and submit your form when you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details** (`user_details`, text, required: false)
  This is the section for you to provide basic information about yourself. This may include your name, username, or other relevant details that help us understand who you are.
* **Character Details** (`character_details`, text, required: true)
  In this section, please describe your character in the roleplay world, including their name, background, and any relevant attributes or abilities.
* **Roleplay Experience** (`roleplay_experience`, text, required: false)
  Share your experience with roleplaying, including any relevant stories, scenarios, or events where you've engaged in roleplay activities.
* **Why Join** (`why_join`, text, required: true)
  Explain why you're interested in joining the Golden State Roleplay Community. Share your motivations, goals, or expectations for participating in the community.
* **Availability** (`availability`, time, required: true)
  Please provide your availability for roleplaying, including any regular schedules or times when you're most active in the roleplay world.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, select_multiple, required: true)
  Select up to two emergency contact options that we can reach out to in case of an unexpected situation.
* **Submission** (`submission`, text, required: false)
  This is a final section to provide any additional thoughts, comments, or questions that you'd like to share with us.
