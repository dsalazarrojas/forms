# Mug Club Membership Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mug Club Membership Application form is intended for users who want to become members of the Mug Club. This includes new applicants and existing members looking to update their membership information or agree to new rules.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have read and understood the membership application process and what information is required.
2. Fill in your First name, Last name, and Email address in the corresponding fields. Please note that your email address will be used to contact you about club events and news.
3. If you have a preferred drink, select it from the options provided.
4. Read and agree to the Membership Agreement, which outlines the expectations and responsibilities of being a club member.
5. For rules 1, 2, 3, and 4, indicate your agreement by selecting "Agree" or "Disagree" accordingly.
6. Review your application before submitting it to ensure you've completed all required fields and agreed to the membership agreement and rules.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as you would like it to be known by other members.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as you would like it to be known by other members.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you about club events and news.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number if you'd like to receive text updates from the club.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter your address if you'd like to receive physical mail from the club.
* **Preferred drink** (`preferences`, select_multiple, required: false): Select your favorite drink from the options provided. You can select multiple options if applicable.
* **Membership Agreement** (`membership_agreement`, select_one, required: false): Check the box to confirm you've read and agreed to the membership agreement.
* **Rule 1** (`rule1`, select_one, required: false): Indicate your agreement or disagreement with rule 1.
* **Rule 2** (`rule2`, select_one, required: false): Indicate your agreement or disagreement with rule 2.
* **Rule 3** (`rule3`, select_one, required: false): Indicate your agreement or disagreement with rule 3.
* **Rule 4** (`rule4`, select_one, required: false): Indicate your agreement or disagreement with rule 4.

## Tips
* Please ensure you've carefully read and agreed to the membership agreement and rules before submitting your application.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* We'll review your application and contact you to confirm your membership status.
