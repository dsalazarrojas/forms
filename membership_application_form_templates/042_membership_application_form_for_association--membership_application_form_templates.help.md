# Membership Application Form For Association - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals who are interested in joining our association. It is intended for new members to provide their details and preferences for our association's records. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Start by filling in the required fields.
2. Select the type of membership you are interested in applying for.
3. Provide any additional information you think would be helpful for us to know.
4. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Membership Application Form For Association (Page 1)
* **First Name** (Membership Application Form For Association First Name): A text field to input your first name.
* **Last Name** (Membership Application Form For Association Last Name): A text field to input your last name.
* **Email** (Membership Application Form For Association Email): A field to input your email address.
* **Phone Number** (Membership Application Form For Association Phone): A text field to input your phone number.
### Membership Application Form For Association (Page 2)
* **Membership Type** (Membership Application Form For Association Membership Type): Select the type of membership you are interested in applying for:
	+ Regular: This membership category is for individuals who are not business owners or students.
	+ Business: This membership category is for business owners.
	+ Student: This membership category is for students.
* **Additional Information** (Membership Application Form For Association Additional Info): A text field to provide any additional information about yourself that would be helpful for us to know.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* Make sure to select the correct membership type for your interest.
* Be as brief and clear as possible when providing additional information.
* If you have any questions, contact us at [association contact info].
