# Trucking Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trucking Booking Form is a simple form used to submit requests for trucking services. It is likely used by customers or clients to send their transportation or logistics needs to the trucking company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name or company name in the "Trucking Company" field.
2. Provide the contact person's name and email address in the "Contact Person" and "Contact Email" fields respectively.
3. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
4. Select a date for the trucking service booking in the "Trucking Booking Date" field.
5. Enter the time of the service in the "Trucking Booking Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trucking Company** (text, required): Enter your company name or the name of the company you represent.
* **Contact Person** (text, optional): Enter the name of the person who will be handling the booking.
* **Contact Email** (email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (text, optional): Enter the contact phone number of the person or company.
* **Trucking Booking Date** (date, optional): Select a date for the desired trucking service booking.
* **Trucking Booking Time** (time, optional): Select the time for the desired trucking service booking.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information to avoid any confusion or delay in processing your request.
* If you need any special instructions or requests, you can include them in the "Trucking Company" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the trucking company directly.
