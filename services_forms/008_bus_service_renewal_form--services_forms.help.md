# Bus Service Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bus Service Renewal Form is used to update and renew the contract of a bus service operator. This form requires accurate and complete information about the operator, their service, and their current and planned fleet sizes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Gather all necessary information and documents as required by the form fields.
* Ensure that you have the authority to sign and submit this form, as it may require signatures and approvals from your company.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Operator Company Name** (`operator_name`): This is the legal name of your bus service operator.
* **Operator License ID** (`operator_id`): This is the state license or permit number assigned to your operator.
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_person`): This is the name of the primary contact person for your company.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): This is the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): This is the email address for renewal correspondence.
* **Type of Bus Service** (`service_type`): Choose the category of transportation service provided by your company. Options include School Bus Service, Public Transit, Charter Bus Service, Shuttle Service, Intercity Bus Service, and Tour/Travel Service.
* **Current Contract Number** (`current_contract_number`): Enter the existing contract identifier.
* **Current Contract Start Date** (`contract_start_date`): Enter the start date of the current contract.
* **Current Contract End Date** (`contract_end_date`): Enter the expiration date of the current contract.
* **Requested Renewal Term** (`renewal_term`): Choose the length of time for the renewal period. Options are 1 Year, 2 Years, 3 Years, and 5 Years.
* **Current Fleet Size** (`current_fleet_size`): Enter the number of buses currently operating.
* **Planned Fleet Size for Renewal** (`planned_fleet_size`): Enter the expected number of buses during the renewal period.
* **Vehicle Maintenance Status** (`vehicle_maintenance`): Choose the overall condition of your fleet. Options are Excellent, Good, Fair, Needs Attention, and Poor.
* **Safety Compliance Record** (`safety_compliance`): Choose the status of your safety standards compliance. Options are Fully Compliant, Generally Compliant, Needs Improvement, and Non-compliant.
* **Annual Revenue** (`annual_revenue`): Enter the estimated annual revenue in USD.
* **Current Insurance Status** (`insurance_current`): Choose whether your insurance is current and valid.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`): Enter the name of the insurance company.
* **Pending Insurance Claims** (`pending_claims`): If you have any outstanding claims or liabilities, list them here.
* **Operational Changes** (`operational_changes`): If there are any planned changes for the renewal period, describe them here.
* **Authorized Signature** (`renewal_signature`): Enter the name of the person authorized to sign this form.

Note: Please ensure that you submit the form accurately and thoroughly, as the information gathered will be used to update and renew your contract.
