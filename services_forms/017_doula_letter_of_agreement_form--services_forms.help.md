# Doula Letter Of Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to outline the terms of a Doula service, including client details, service duration, and payment terms. It helps establish a clear understanding between the Doula and the client.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your name and email address to identify yourself as the client.
2. Select the type of service you are seeking (e.g., "Yes" for prenatal, postpartum, or postpartum overnight support).
3. Enter the duration of the service you require (number of hours or days).
4. Choose the start and finish dates for the service.
5. Describe the payment terms and frequency (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, etc.).
6. Select the payment method (e.g., "Yes" for in-person sessions, "No" for virtual sessions).
7. Write a detailed agreement outlining the scope of work, responsibilities, and expectations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Doula Letter Of Agreement Form (form_title)**:
	+ This is the title of the form. Enter a descriptive title that summarizes the document.
* **Client Name (client_name)**:
	+ Enter your name or your partner's name if you are the client's representative.
* **Client Email (client_email)**:
	+ Enter your valid email address to facilitate communication with the Doula.
* **Service Type (service_type)**:
	+ Select the type of service you require (e.g., prenatal, postpartum, or postpartum overnight).
* **Service Duration (service_duration)**:
	+ Enter the number of hours or days for the service you need.
* **Service Start Date (service_start_date)**:
	+ Choose the start date for the service.
* **Service Finish Date (service_finish_date)**:
	+ Choose the finish date for the service.
* **Payment Terms (payment_terms)**:
	+ Describe the payment terms and conditions, including amount, frequency, and method.
* **Payment Terms Frequency (payment_terms_frequency)**:
	+ Specify the payment frequency (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, etc.).
* **Payment Method (payment_method)**:
	+ Select whether you prefer in-person sessions or virtual sessions.
* **Doula Agreement (doula_agreement)**:
	+ Write a detailed agreement outlining the scope of work, responsibilities, and expectations. This is an opportunity to clarify any important details or special requests.

## Tips

* Make sure to clearly outline the scope of work, payment terms, and expectations to avoid misunderstandings.
* Be open and honest about your needs and requirements.
* Review the form carefully before signing to ensure it accurately reflects your needs and agreement.
