# Wholesale Account Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wholesale Account Application Form is designed to gather essential information from wholesale accounts to facilitate efficient interaction with our business.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing the required information for your wholesale account. The system will automatically skip or suggest additional questions based on your answers.
2. Select the relevant information for each field as accurately as possible.
3. If you're unsure about any field, refer to our explanations below for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Wholesale Account Form 1** (wholesale_account_form_1, text, optional): A brief description of your wholesale account, helping us understand your business needs.
  Please provide a short summary of your wholesale account, including your company name, description, and any relevant details.
- **Business Information** (business_information_form_2, text, optional): A detailed overview of your business, including your company profile, history, and any other essential details.
- **Wholesale Contact** (wholesale_contact_form_3, select_multiple, optional): Select from the provided options (Wholesale, Business, Contact) to indicate who will be the primary contact for your account.
- **Wholesale Address** (wholesale_address_form_4, select_one, optional): Choose one from the options (Street, Number, Postal, Code) to specify the type of address you're providing.
- **Contact Form 5** (contact_form_5_label, text, optional): Additional information for the contact person, such as their job title or department.
- **Phone** (phone_form_6, text, optional): Enter your contact person's phone number for efficient communication.
- **Email** (email_form_7, email, optional): Provide your contact person's email address for secure communication.
- **Date** (date_form_8, date, optional): Enter the date you're submitting this application.
- **Time** (time_form_9, time, optional): Enter the time you're submitting this application, if applicable.
