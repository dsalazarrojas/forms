# Pet Care Service Partnership Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from potential partners for a pet care service. It collects contact details, partner preferences, and additional information about the partnership request. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review each field carefully and ensure you have selected the correct options for your partnership request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Title** (`title`, `text`, required): Enter a brief title that describes the partnership request.
- **Pet Care Service Partnership Request Form 1** (`pet_care_service_partnership_request_form`, `text`, required): Enter a detailed description of the partnership request.
- **Pet Type** (`pet_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of pet you are interested in partnering with (Cat, Dog, or Other).
- **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name or the name of the contact person responsible for the partnership request.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address or the email address of the contact person responsible for the partnership request.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number or the phone number of the contact person responsible for the partnership request.
- **Street Address** (`street_address`, `text`, required): Enter your street address or the address of the contact person responsible for the partnership request.
- **State/Province** (`state_province`, `select_one`, required): Select the state or province where you are located (Alabama, Alaska, or Other).
- **Country** (`country`, `select_one`, required): Select the country where you are located (United States or Other).
- **Postal Code** (`zip_postal_code`, `text`, required): Enter your postal or zip code.
- **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are requesting the partnership.
- **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Enter the time you are requesting the partnership.
