# easter_meal_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for an Easter meal order. Users will need to provide details about the meal, delivery address, payment method, and other relevant information to complete the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the details of the Easter meal in the "Easter Meal Details" field.
2. Enter the delivery address in the "Easter Meal Delivery Address" field.
3. Choose a payment method in the "Easter Meal Payment Method" dropdown menu.
4. If required, enter any special requests in the "Easter Meal Special Requests" field.
5. Select a delivery date and time in the "Easter Meal Delivery Date" and "Easter Meal Delivery Time" fields.
6. Enter a phone number for the delivery in the "Easter Meal Delivery Phone" field.
7. Enter an email address for the delivery in the "Easter Meal Delivery Email" field.
8. If necessary, enter any special instructions in the "Easter Meal Special Instructions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Easter Meal Details** (1) (`text`, Required): Enter the details of the Easter meal you wish to order.
* **Easter Meal Delivery Address** (2) (`text`, Optional): Enter the address where the Easter meal should be delivered.
* **Easter Meal Payment Method** (3) (`select_one`, Optional): Choose how you plan to pay for the Easter meal (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
* **Easter Meal Special Requests** (4) (`text`, Optional): If you have any special requests for your Easter meal, enter them here.
* **Easter Meal Delivery Date** (5) (`date`, Optional): Select the date you would like to have your Easter meal delivered.
* **Easter Meal Delivery Time** (6) (`time`, Optional): Select the time you would like to have your Easter meal delivered.
* **Easter Meal Delivery Phone** (7) (`text`, Optional): Enter a phone number where you can be contacted for the Easter meal delivery.
* **Easter Meal Delivery Email** (8) (`email`, Optional): Enter an email address where you can be contacted for the Easter meal delivery.
* **Easter Meal Special Instructions** (9) (`text`, Optional): If you have any special instructions for your Easter meal delivery, enter them here.
