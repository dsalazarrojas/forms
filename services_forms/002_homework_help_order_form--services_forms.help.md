# Homework Help Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The homework help order form is a tool designed to collect information from students seeking help with their homework assignments. By filling out this form, you can request assistance from our support team, and they will do their best to provide guidance and resources to help you complete your assignments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name, student ID, assignment name, subject, and due date.
2. Provide a brief description of the help you need.
3. If you have a phone number, please include it so we can contact you for further clarification.
4. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your student ID or other official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your student ID or other official documents.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, optional): Your student ID number, if you have one.
* **Assignment Name** (`assignment_name`, text, optional): The name of the assignment you're seeking help with.
* **Subject** (`subject`, text, optional): The subject or course related to the assignment.
* **Due Date** (`due_date`, date, optional): The date by which the assignment is due.
* **Description** (`description`, note, optional): A brief description of the help you need, such as "I need help with problem 3 on page 12" or "I need clarification on the concept of calculus."
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Your phone number where we can contact you for further clarification.
