# mimal_vehicle_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a vehicle.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the "Vehicle Type" field and select "Car", "Bike", or "Truck" from the dropdown menu.
2.  If applicable, enter the vehicle's mileage.
3.  Enter the year the vehicle was produced.
4.  Add any relevant notes about the vehicle in the "notes" field.
5.  Provide the owner's name, phone number, and email address in their respective fields.
6.  Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) of the vehicle.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Type** (`Vehicle Type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of vehicle you are entering information for. This field has three options: Car, Bike, or Truck.
* **mileage** (`mileage`, `number`, optional): Enter the current mileage of the vehicle.
* **Year** (`Year`, `number`, optional): Enter the year the vehicle was produced.
* **notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any relevant notes about the vehicle.
* **owner_name** (`owner_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the vehicle's owner.
* **owner_phone** (`owner_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the vehicle's owner.
* **owner_email** (`owner_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the vehicle's owner.
* **vin** (`vin`, `text`, optional): Enter the Vehicle Identification Number of the vehicle.
* **make** (`make`, `text`, optional): This field does not appear to have any significance, but it is likely intended for a vehicle manufacturer. If you know the make of the vehicle, you can enter it here, but it is not required for the form's function.
