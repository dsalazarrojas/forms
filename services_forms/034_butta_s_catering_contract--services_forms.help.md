# Butta's Catering Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Butta's Catering Contract is a form used to gather necessary information for catering services, including the client's name and contact details, event details (name, date, time, location, and type), and specific requirements for services such as menu, staff, and equipment rentals. This guide will walk you through each field to ensure you have a successful and stress-free catering experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your client's name, email address, and phone number to ensure we can contact you efficiently.
2. Provide the event name, date, start and end times, and location to give us a clear understanding of your event needs.
3. Select the type of event (e.g., Wedding, Corporate event, etc.) to help us tailor services accordingly.
4. Indicate the expected number of guests to plan for sufficient supplies and staff.
5. Choose the preferred cuisine type and menu items.
6. Specify your beverage service requirements.
7. List any dietary restrictions or requirements.
8. Select the service style (e.g., buffet, plated, etc.) and staff required.
9. Indicate if any equipment or rentals are needed.
10. Estimate the total contract cost and provide a deposit if required.
11. Make any special arrangements or notes regarding the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (1, text, required): Enter the full name of the client who will be hosting the event.
* **Email Address** (2, email, required): Provide your contact email address so we can get in touch with you efficiently.
* **Phone Number** (3, text, required): Enter your phone number for easy communication.
* **Event Name or Occasion** (4, text, required): Describe the type of event (e.g., Wedding, Birthday party, etc.).
* **Type of Event** (5, select_one, required): Choose the type of event from the provided options (e.g., Wedding, Corporate event, etc.).
* **Event Date** (6, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Start Time** (7, time, required): Specify the time services should begin.
* **Event End Time** (8, time, required): Specify the time services should end.
* **Event Location** (9, text, required): Provide the address or location of the event.
* **Expected Number of Guests** (10, number, required): Indicate the expected number of guests.
* **Cuisine Preference** (11, select_multiple, required): Choose one or multiple preferred cuisine types (e.g., Southern, Seafood, etc.).
* **Preferred Menu Items** (12, text, required): List the specific menu items you would like to have served.
* **Beverage Service Included** (13, select_one, required): Specify if you want non-alcoholic, alcoholic, or a combination of beverages included.
* **Dietary Requirements or Restrictions** (14, text, optional): List any dietary restrictions or requirements (e.g., gluten-free, vegetarian, etc.).
* **Service Style** (15, select_one, required): Choose the service style (e.g., buffet, plated, etc.).
* **Catering Staff to Be Provided** (16, select_multiple, required): Select the staff required (e.g., servers, bartender, chef, etc.).
* **Equipment or Rentals Needed** (17, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if any equipment or rentals are required (e.g., tables, chairs, etc.).
* **Estimated Contract Total** (18, number, required): Enter an estimated cost for catering services.
* **Deposit or Down Payment Required** (19, number, optional): Provide a deposit or down payment amount if required.
* **Special Arrangements or Notes** (20, text, optional): Make any special arrangements or notes regarding the event.

**Tips:**

* Make sure to double-check each field for accuracy before submitting.
* Be as detailed as possible for better service quality.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
