# test_drive_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used for booking a test drive for a vehicle. It gathers necessary information from customers to facilitate the booking process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Booking Date** (1, date, required): Provide the date for the test drive booking.
- **Vehicle Select** (2, select_one, required): Select the vehicle you would like to book a test drive for.
- **Customer Name** (3, text, required): Enter your name as the customer.
- **Customer Phone** (4, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Customer Email** (5, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
- **Time** (6, time, required): Select the time for the test drive booking.
- **Notify Me** (7, select_multiple, required): Indicate if you would like to be notified about any updates or changes regarding your booking.
- **Additional Notes** (8, note, optional): Provide any additional information you would like to include in the booking.
- **Customer ID** (9, number, required): Enter your customer ID for identification purposes.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
