# Food Bank Voucher Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Bank Voucher Form is a streamlined template designed to simplify the process of distributing essential goods. It is intended for use by food banks and their staff to efficiently manage and track their services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
- Fill out the form title.
- Select a category for the food bank (Food Bank, Services, or Forms).
- Provide a description of the food bank's services.
- (Optional) Add additional descriptions to further elaborate on the form's purpose and use.
- (Optional) Add a form description to provide more details about the form's features and functionality.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Food Bank Voucher Form** (`form_title`, text, required/optional): This field is the title of the form, which should be a brief and descriptive name of the food bank's voucher form.
- **Category** (`category`, select, optional): Select the category for the food bank (Food Bank, Services, or Forms).
- **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a description of the food bank's services, including details about what they provide and how they operate.
- **A Food Bank Voucher Form is a streamlined template that simplifies the process of distributing essential goods.** (`description`, note, optional): This field provides additional context and explanation about the form's purpose and use. However, it is not required to be filled out.
- **It helps food banks manage and track their services efficiently, eliminating any documentation chaos.** (`description`, note, optional): Like the field above, this note provides additional context, but it is not required.
- **Become more organized and tackle hunger effectively with this customizable tool.** (`description`, note, optional): Another note that offers additional information about the form's benefits.
- **A Food Bank Voucher Form is a streamlined template that simplifies the process of distributing essential goods.** (`form_description`, text, optional): This field is meant to provide a more detailed description of the form's purpose, but it is not required.
- **Food Bank Voucher Form** (`form_title`, text, optional): This field is the form's title, and it should be a descriptive name for the food bank's voucher form.
- **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Another field for a more in-depth description of the food bank's services.
