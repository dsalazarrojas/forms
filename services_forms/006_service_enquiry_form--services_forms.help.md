# Service Enquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from users about their interest in our services. This form is used to collect contact information and service-related details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form with your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Select your answer to the service enquiry questions, including whether you are interested in a service and whether you have provided contact information.
3. Enter any additional comments or messages about your service enquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Enquiry Form** (`service_enquiry_form`, text, optional): This is the main form title.
* **Service Name** (`service_name`, select_one, optional): Select whether you are interested in a service by choosing 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Enquiry Details** (`enquiry_details`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your service enquiry.
* **Enquiry Contact** (`enquiry_contact`, select_multiple, optional): Check whether you have provided contact information.
* **Enquiry Email** (`enquiry_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Enquiry Phone** (`enquiry_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Service Enquired** (`service_enquired`, select_multiple, optional): Check whether you have enquired about a service.
* **Service Enquiry Message** (`service_enquiry_message`, select_one, optional): Select whether you have provided a service enquiry message.
