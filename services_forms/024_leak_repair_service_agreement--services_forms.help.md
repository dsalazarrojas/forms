# Leak Repair Service Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to collect information from customers who need leak repair services. It helps the business understand the customer's needs and schedule the repair accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your service address where the leak repair is needed.
2. Provide some details about yourself, even though this field is not required.
3. Describe the issue in detail, including the type of repair needed.
4. Choose a date and time for the repair.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Address**: Enter the address where you need the leak repair service.
* **Customer Details**: Enter any additional details about yourself, such as your name, phone number, or email address.
* **Issue Description**: Describe the issue in detail, including the type of repair needed. For example, "Toilet leak", "Pipe leak under sink", or "Burst pipe in the attic."
* **Repair Date**: Choose a date and time for the repair. Please format the date as it appears on your computer or mobile device.
