# Consulting Client Intake Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a template designed to help you gather essential information from clients seeking consulting services. It's intended to facilitate the consultation process by providing a structured approach to capturing client needs and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
2. Review and provide a short description of the service you're requesting.
3. Outline your project goals and desired outcomes (if applicable).
4. Specify your expected start and end dates (if applicable).
5. Indicate your approximate budget range (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Client Overview (Page 1)
* **-- Client Overview --** (`section_client_overview`, `note`, required: false): This is an introductory section meant to provide some context about the client and their engagement. You can leave this section blank if you don't have any additional information to add.

### Client Name (Page 1)
* **Client name** (`client_name`, `text`, required: true): Enter the name of the company or individual seeking consulting services.

### Contact Person (Page 1)
* **Contact person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required: true): Identify the primary contact person for the engagement.

### Contact Email (Page 1)
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: true): Provide the email address for follow-up and proposal communications.

### Service Requested (Page 1)
* **Service requested** (`service_requested`, `text`, required: true): Briefly describe the consulting service you're seeking.

### Project Goals (Page 1)
* **Project goals** (`project_goals`, `text`, required: false): This field is optional. If you have specific project goals or desired outcomes, you can outline them here.

### Timeline (Page 1)
* **Timeline** (`timeline`, `text`, required: false): This field is also optional. If you have expected start and end dates, you can specify them here.

### Budget Range (Page 2)
* **Budget range** (`budget_range`, `number`, required: false): Enter your approximate budget for the engagement if you know it. This is an optional field, but it helps us better understand your financial expectations.
