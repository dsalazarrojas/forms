# josh_dampf_events_post_event_feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on events hosted by Josh Dampf. Your input will help us improve future events and ensure that we meet your expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

*   Fill out the form fields as prompted, and make sure to select the correct options for each field.
*   Be as detailed as possible in your comments and feedback, but keep in mind that some fields are optional.
*   If you're unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Event Name** (`Event Name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the event you're providing feedback on.
*   **Event Date** (`Event Date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the event.
*   **Event Time** (`Event Time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of the event.
*   **Feedback Rating** (`Feedback Rating`, `number`, required/optional): Rate your overall experience at the event, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
*   **Comments** (`Comments`, `note`, required/optional): Share your comments and thoughts about the event. Be as detailed as possible.
*   **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address. This will be used to follow up on your feedback.
*   **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number. This will be used to follow up on your feedback, if needed.
*   **Event Location** (`Event Location`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the location of the event.
*   **Attendee Count** (`Attendee Count`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the number of attendees at the event.
*   **Attendee Names** (`Attendee Names`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the names of attendees who attended the event. The options are:
    *   John Doe
    *   Jane Doe
    *   Joe Doe
*   **Assigned Tool** (`Assigned Tool`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the tool assigned to you during the event. The options are:
    *   chatjimmy
    *   other
*   **Event Duration** (`Event Duration`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the duration of the event in hours.
*   **Event Type** (`Event Type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of event:
    *   meeting
    *   conference
    *   workshop
*   **Event Status** (`Event Status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the status of the event:
    *   active
    *   pending
    *   cancelled
*   **Feedback** (`Feedback`, `text`, required/optional): Share any additional feedback or comments about the event.
*   **Followup** (`Followup`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select whether you want to be followed up on:
    *   True
    *   False
*   **Event Notes** (`Event Notes`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about the event.
*   **Followup Notes** (`Followup Notes`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any follow-up notes for the event.

Note: Some fields are optional, so feel free to skip them if they don't apply to your event.
