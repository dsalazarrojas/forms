# Maintenance Inspection Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to request maintenance inspections for equipment or machinery. Please use this form to provide detailed information about the condition of the equipment, including visual descriptions and any relevant photos or notes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the fields with accurate and clear information about the equipment or machinery you are requesting maintenance inspection for.
2. Use the `maintenance_inspection_request` field to provide a brief description of the issue or concern you are requesting inspection for.
3. Upload photos of the tank and pump conditions to help the maintenance team better understand the issue. 
4. Add any additional notes or comments regarding the equipment or inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **maintenance_inspection_request** (Text, Optional): A brief description of the maintenance issue or concern you are reporting.
* **tank_photos** (Text, Optional): Upload photos of the tank's condition to aid in the inspection process.
* **tank_photos_copy** (Text, Optional): A duplicate field for uploading additional tank photos.
* **visual_condition** (Text, Optional): A detailed description of the tank's visual condition.
* **pump_photos** (Text, Optional): Upload photos of the pump's condition to aid in the inspection process.
* **notes** (Text, Optional): Additional comments or notes regarding the equipment or inspection.
* **engineer_name** (Text, Optional): The name of the engineer assigned to the maintenance request.
* **assigned_to** (Text, Optional): The name of the person or team assigned to the maintenance request.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and truthfully to provide the best possible outcome.
* If additional information is required, please attach supporting documents or notes as needed.
* This form is for maintenance inspection requests only, and should not be used for general feedback or comments.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the maintenance team for guidance.
