# release_of_liability_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "release_of_liability_template" form is used to gather essential information from submitters applying for services or programs that require a release of liability. This form helps streamline the process by collecting demographic data and relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Provide Submitter Info: Enter your personal details, including any relevant contact information.
2. Choose your Citizenship: Select your citizenship status (US or Non-US).
3. Select your Race: Choose your racial identification (white, black, or Asian).
4. Select your Ethnicity: Choose your ethnic identification (white, black, or Asian).
5. Specify your Educational Status: Select your level of education (High School Diploma or Higher, Some High School, or Less than High School).
6. Enter Test Scores: Provide your test scores for SAT, ACT, ASVAB, or LSAT (if applicable).
7. Review and Agree to the Submission Agreement: Select your agreement to the submission terms.
8. Give Consent: Confirm your consent to the submission terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submitter Info (Page 1)**:
	+ **Name**: Enter your personal name.
	+ **Email**: Enter your email address.
	+ **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Info (Page 1)**:
	+ **Address**: Enter your address.
	+ **City**: Enter your city.
	+ **State**: Enter your state.
	+ **Zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **Citizenship (Page 1)**:
	+ **US Citizen**: Select 'US Citizen' if you are a US citizen.
	+ **Non-US Citizen**: Select 'Non-US Citizen' if you are not a US citizen.
* **Race (Page 1)**:
	+ **white**: Select 'white' if you identify as white.
	+ **black**: Select 'black' if you identify as black.
	+ **Asian**: Select 'Asian' if you identify as Asian.
* **Ethnicity (Page 1)**:
	+ **white**: Select 'white' if you identify as white.
	+ **black**: Select 'black' if you identify as black.
	+ **Asian**: Select 'Asian' if you identify as Asian.
* **Education Status (Page 1)**:
	+ **High School Diploma or Higher**: Select this if you have a high school diploma or higher.
	+ **Some High School**: Select this if you have some high school education.
	+ **Less than High School**: Select this if you have less than a high school education.
* **Test Scores (Page 2)**:
	+ **SAT Score**: Enter your SAT score.
	+ **ACT Score**: Enter your ACT score.
	+ **ASVAB Score**: Enter your ASVAB score.
	+ **LSAT Score**: Enter your LSAT score.
* **Submission Agreement (Page 2)**:
	+ **I agree to the Submission Agreement**: Select this if you agree to the submission terms.
	+ **I do not agree to the Submission Agreement**: Select this if you do not agree to the submission terms.
* **Submitter Consent (Page 2)**:
	+ **True**: Select 'True' to confirm your consent.
	+ **False**: Select 'False' to decline your consent.
