# Auto Repair Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Auto Repair Release Form is a document that outlines the details of a vehicle's repair, including customer information, vehicle details, repair service details, and condition of the vehicle upon pickup. This form is used to ensure that both the customer and the repair shop have a clear understanding of the work performed and the conditions of the vehicle upon release.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Enter your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
* Provide your vehicle's information, including the year, make, model, and Vehicle Identification Number (VIN).
* Describe the repairs performed on your vehicle.
* Indicate the total cost of the repairs.
* Choose the warranty period for the parts and labor.
* Indicate your satisfaction with the work performed.
* Acknowledge receipt of the vehicle and accept the repairs.
* Release the shop from liability for the repairs completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name (Text):** Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address (Email):** Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number (Text):** Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address (Text):** Enter your home or business address.
* **Vehicle Year (Number):** Enter the year of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make (Text):** Enter the make of your vehicle (e.g., Toyota, Honda, etc.).
* **Vehicle Model (Text):** Enter the model of your vehicle (e.g., Camry, Civic, etc.).
* **Vehicle VIN (Text):** Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) of your vehicle.
* **License Plate Number (Text):** Enter the license plate number of your vehicle.
* **Odometer Reading (Number):** Enter the current odometer reading of your vehicle.
* **Repair Date (Date):** Enter the date of the repair service.
* **Description of Repairs Performed (Text):** List all the repairs that were completed on your vehicle.
* **Total Repair Cost (Number):** Enter the total cost of the repairs.
* **Parts Warranty (Select):** Choose the warranty period for the parts (30 days, 90 days, 6 months, 1 year, No Warranty).
* **Labor Warranty (Select):** Choose the warranty period for the labor (30 days, 90 days, 6 months, 1 year, No Warranty).
* **Vehicle Condition Upon Pickup (Select):** Indicate the condition of your vehicle upon pickup (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Are you satisfied with the work performed (Select):** Indicate your level of satisfaction with the work performed (Yes, No, Partial).
* **I acknowledge receipt of the vehicle and accept the repairs (Select):** Confirm that you have received your vehicle and accept the repairs.
* **I release the shop from liability for repairs completed (Select):** Confirm that you release the shop from liability for the repairs completed.
* **Signature Date (Date):** Enter the date of your signature.
