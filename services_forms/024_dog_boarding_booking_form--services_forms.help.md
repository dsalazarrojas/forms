# Dog Boarding Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for pet owners to book a dog boarding service. It collects information about the pet, owner, and schedule for boarding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your pet's name in the "Pet Name" field.
2. Enter your name and contact information in the "Owner Name" field.
3. Choose the start date for your pet's boarding in the "Start Date" field.
4. Choose the end date for your pet's boarding in the "End Date" field.
5. Select the boarding start time for your pet in the "Boarding Start Time" field.
6. Select the boarding end time for your pet in the "Boarding End Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Pet Name** (text, required/optional): Please enter your pet's name.
- **Owner Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your name and contact information.
- **Start Date** (date, required/optional): Select the date you want to start boarding your pet.
- **End Date** (date, required/optional): Select the date you want to end boarding your pet.
- **Boarding Start Time** (time, required/optional): Choose the time you want to start boarding your pet.
- **Boarding End Time** (time, required/optional): Choose the time you want to end boarding your pet.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check the dates and times you select to ensure they accurately reflect your pet's boarding schedule.
- If you have any special requests or instructions for your pet's care, please include them in the "Notes" field.
- If you have any questions or concerns, you can contact us at form@gic.mx.
