# Photography Session Agreement LuLaRoe Addendum - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture an agreement for a photography session, specifically the LuLaRoe addendum. It's a contract between the photographer and the client, detailing the services provided, contract terms, payment information, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Title** field with the title of the agreement, which is the LuLaRoe addendum.
2. In the **Services** field, list the services provided by the photographer.
3. In the **Services details** field, provide additional details about the services provided.
4. The **Contract** field is for general contract information.
5. The **Contract terms** field is for specific terms and conditions of the contract.
6. In the **Contract term date** field, enter the date of the contract term.
7. Enter the start and end dates of the contract term in the **Contract term start** and **Contract term end** fields, respectively.
8. In the **Contract term duration** field, enter the duration of the contract term.
9. In the **Payment** field, enter payment information, including the amount and method.
10. In the **Payment method** field, select the payment method used (e.g. Cash, PayPal, Stripe, Bank Transfer).
11. In the **Client** field, select the client's name.
12. In the **Services price** field, enter the price of the services provided.
13. In the **Terms** field, enter any general terms and conditions.
14. In the **Signature** field, sign this document to confirm agreement.
15. In the **Date** field, enter the date this agreement was signed.
16. In the **Time** field, enter the time this agreement was signed.
17. In the **Location** field, enter the location of the photography session.
18. In the **Notes** field, enter any additional notes or comments about the agreement.
