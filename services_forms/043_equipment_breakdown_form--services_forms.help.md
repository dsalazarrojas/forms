## Equipment Breakdown Form - Help Guide
### Purpose
This form is used to report equipment malfunctions or issues affecting the functionality or availability of equipment.

### How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your user name to identify yourself as the person reporting the issue.
2. Provide the equipment's item ID to help track and identify the affected equipment.
3. Describe the issue or malfunction in detail, including any relevant details about the equipment.
4. Select the category and sub-category of the equipment that is affected.
5. Choose the location where the issue occurred.
6. Enter the contact information of the person reporting the issue, including their name, email, and phone number.
7. Add any additional notes or updates about the status of the equipment.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required): This field is for you to enter your name, so we can identify who reported the issue.
* **Item ID** (`item_id`, number, optional): Enter the item ID of the equipment that is affected. This helps us track and identify the issue.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Describe the issue or malfunction in detail, including any relevant details about the equipment.
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, optional): Select the category of the equipment that is affected, such as "Equipment" or "Supplies".
* **Sub-Category** (`sub_category`, select_multiple, optional): Select the sub-category of the equipment that is affected, such as "Category" or "Type".
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the issue occurred.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time when the issue occurred.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the issue occurred.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person reporting the issue.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the person reporting the issue.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person reporting the issue.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or updates about the status of the equipment.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who submitted the report.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, date, optional): Enter the date when the report was submitted.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who updated the report.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, date, optional): Enter the date when the report was last updated.

### Tips

* Please provide as much detail as possible about the issue, as this will help us resolve the problem more efficiently.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, you can ask for help or clarification.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure the report is complete and useful for issue resolution.
