# Spray Tanning Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The spray tanning intake form is used to gather essential client information for a safe and successful tanning experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact information on pages 1-5.
2. On page 6, select 'Yes' or 'No' for medical conditions that affect your skin or allergies.
3. On page 7, select 'Yes' or 'No' for skin types that may impact the tanning process.
4. On page 8, select 'Yes' or 'No' for tanning oil requirements.
5. In pages 9 and 11, provide any relevant medical history and allergies in the provided text boxes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Page 1): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Page 2): Enter your last name.
* **Age** (Page 3): Enter your age in numeric format (e.g., 25).
* **Contact Number** (Page 4): Enter your contact number.
* **Email** (Page 5): Enter your email address.
* **Medical Conditions** (Page 6): Check 'Yes' if you have any medical conditions that may impact your skin. Check 'No' otherwise.
* **Skin Type** (Page 7): Select 'Yes' if you have any skin type that may impact the tanning process. Select 'No' otherwise.
* **Tanning Oil** (Page 8): Check 'Yes' if you require tanning oil, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Medical History** (Page 9): Describe any relevant medical history that may impact your tanning experience in this text box.
* **Allergies** (Page 10): Check 'Yes' if you have any allergies that may impact the tanning process. Check 'No' otherwise.
* **Allergies Details** (Page 11): Provide any relevant details about your allergies in this text box.
* **Medical Notes** (Page 12): Enter any other relevant medical information that may impact your tanning experience in this text box.

## Tips

* Be honest about any medical conditions, skin types, or allergies that may impact your tanning experience.
* Provide accurate contact information so the technician can reach you before or after the session.
* If you have any concerns, don't hesitate to discuss them with your technician.

Note: Some fields are optional, but accurate completion of all fields is crucial for a safe and successful tanning experience.
