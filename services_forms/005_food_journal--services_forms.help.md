# Food Journal - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Journal form is designed to help individuals track their food consumption for a specific number of days. It allows users to select the days they wish to journal and record details about their meals, including whether they consumed each meal or not, and add notes if necessary. This form is useful for tracking eating habits and making informed dietary decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Select Number of Days to Track
- Select the number of days you want to track your food consumption for by choosing a number from 1 to 7.

### Step 2: Record Meal Consumption
- For each meal type (up to 8 options), choose whether you consumed a meal or not by selecting either 'Yes' or 'No'.

### Step 3: Add Date and Note (Optional)
- Enter the date for which you are tracking your meals.
- Optionally, add a note about this meal if you wish to record additional information.

### Step 4: Provide Contact Information (Optional)
- Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
