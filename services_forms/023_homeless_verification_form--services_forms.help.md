# Homeless Verification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Homeless Verification Form is used to collect information about an individual's homeless status, including their contact and housing details.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Complete each field accurately and truthfully to the best of your ability.
2.  Please fill out the form in its entirety.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Name** (`user_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
    *   Example: John Doe
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
    *   Example: johndoe@example.com
*   **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your current address.
    *   Example: 123 Main Street, Anytown, USA 12345
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
    *   Example: 123-456-7890
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
    *   Example: 1990-01-01
*   **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have an emergency contact or not.
    *   Options: Yes, No, Undisclosed
*   **Housing Benefit Type** (`housing_benefit_type`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable housing benefit types.
    *   Options: Type 1, Type 2, Type 3, Other
*   **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional information that may be relevant to your homeless status.
*   **Income** (`income`, number, optional): Enter your current income.
*   **Housing Status** (`housing_status`, select_one, required): Indicate your current housing status.
    *   Options: Owner Occupier, Tenant, Other
*   **Verification Date** (`verification_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of this form submission.
    *   Example: 2022-01-01

## Tips

*   Please ensure all fields are completed accurately to avoid delays in processing your information.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please contact our support team for assistance.
*   If you are unable to complete this form, please contact our support team for assistance.
