# hotel_room_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hotel room booking form is used to book and manage room reservations at a hotel. This form provides a structured and organized way to collect essential details from guests, including room number, arrival and departure dates, room type, and contact information. By completing this form, hotel staff can efficiently process and confirm bookings, ensuring a smooth and pleasant experience for both guests and hotel staff.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the hotel room booking form, follow these steps:

1. Select the room type and arrival date.
2. Enter the departure date.
3. Choose the room price (if available).
4. Provide the guest's name and contact phone number.
5. Enter the guest's email address (if available).
6. Check the boxes for the extra services required (if any).
7. Add any special requests or booking notes.
8. Confirm the guest's arrival and departure times (if required).
9. Add any guest arrival and departure notes (if required).
10. Enter any hotel staff notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Room Number** (1, Number, Required/Optional): This field is used to specify the room number assigned to the guest. Enter the correct room number to ensure accurate room assignment.
* **Room Type** (2, Select Multiple, Required/Optional): This field is used to select the type of room booked (e.g., single, double, suite). Choose the relevant option for the guest's room preferences.
* **Arrival Date** (3, Date, Required/Optional): Enter the date the guest plans to arrive at the hotel.
* **Departure Date** (4, Date, Required/Optional): Enter the date the guest plans to depart from the hotel.
* **Room Price** (5, Number, Required/Optional): If available, enter the agreed-upon room price for the booking.
* **Guest Name** (6, Text, Required/Optional): Enter the guest's name for hotel staff reference and communication.
* **Contact Phone** (7, Text, Required/Optional): Enter the guest's contact phone number for hotel staff to reach out.
* **Contact Email** (8, Email, Required/Optional): Enter the guest's email address for hotel staff to send confirmations and updates.
* **Room Service** (9, Select One, Required/Optional): Check the box if the guest requires room service.
* **Extra Services** (10, Select Multiple, Required/Optional): Select any extra services required by the guest (e.g., extra bed, extra towel).
* **Booking Note** (11, Note, Required/Optional): Add any notes or comments about the booking for hotel staff reference.
* **Special Requests** (12, Select Multiple, Required/Optional): Select any special requests or services required by the guest.
* **Guest Arrival Time** (13, Time, Required/Optional): Enter the expected arrival time of the guest.
* **Guest Arrival Date** (14, Date, Required/Optional): Enter the expected arrival date of the guest.
* **Guest Contact Number** (15, Text, Required/Optional): Enter the guest's contact phone number.
* **Guest Email** (16, Email, Required/Optional): Enter the guest's email address.
* **Room Status** (17, Select One, Required/Optional): Check the room's status (Active or Inactive).
* **Room Status Note** (18, Note, Required/Optional): Add any notes about the room status for hotel staff reference.
* **Booking Reference** (19, Text, Required/Optional): Enter a reference number or code for the booking.
* **Guest Comment** (20, Text, Required/Optional): Add any comments or feedback from the guest.
* **Guest Arrival Note** (21, Note, Required/Optional): Add any notes about the guest's arrival.
* **Guest Departure Note** (22, Note, Required/Optional): Add any notes about the guest's departure.
* **Hotel Staff Note** (23, Note, Required/Optional): Add any comments or notes from hotel staff for reference.
* **Booking Status** (24, Select One, Required/Optional): Check the booking status (Active or Inactive).

Note: This form is designed to be flexible, so some fields might not be required in certain situations. Always check with hotel staff or management if you're unsure about any field's requirement.
