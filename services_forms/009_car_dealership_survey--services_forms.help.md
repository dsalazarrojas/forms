# Car Dealership Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experience at the car dealership. It will help us understand what we're doing well and where we can improve to better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Introduction

1. This is an optional form, and you can choose to participate at your discretion.
2. Please take a few minutes to answer the questions honestly, and provide as much detail as possible.

### Step 2: Customer Information (Optional)

1. **Customer Information**: This section is optional, and you can skip it if you don't want to share your contact information.
2. **Full Name**: Your full name, as you would like it to be displayed on the survey.
3. **Email Address**: For follow-up questions or comments, we may contact you at this email address. You can leave it blank if you don't want us to contact you.
4. **Visit Date**: The date you visited our dealership.
5. **Visit Purpose**: Why did you visit our dealership? (Select one of the options: Purchased a vehicle, Test drove a vehicle, Service/Maintenance, Just browsing, Parts/Accessories, Finance inquiry)

### Step 3: Experience

1. **Overall Experience**: How would you rate your overall experience at our dealership? (Select one of the options: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor)
2. **Staff Greeting**: Were you greeted promptly when you arrived? (Select one of the options: Yes - immediately, Yes - within 5 minutes, Yes - but took longer, No - had to find someone)
3. **Staff Knowledge**: How knowledgeable was the staff you interacted with? (Select one of the options: Very knowledgeable, Knowledgeable, Somewhat knowledgeable, Not very knowledgeable, Poor)
4. **Staff Professionalism**: How professional was the staff you interacted with? (Select one of the options: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor)

### Step 4: Facilities and Services

1. **Facility Cleanliness**: How clean was the dealership? (Select one of the options: Very clean, Clean, Acceptable, Needs improvement, Poor)
2. **Waiting Area Comfort**: How comfortable was the waiting area? (Select one of the options: Very comfortable, Comfortable, Acceptable, Uncomfortable, Poor)
3. **Vehicle Selection**: Were you satisfied with the vehicle selection? (Select one of the options: Excellent selection, Good selection, Adequate selection, Limited selection, Poor selection)
4. **Pricing Transparency**: Was the pricing clearly explained? (Select one of the options: Very clear, Clear, Somewhat clear, Confusing, Not clear at all)

### Step 5: Finance and Purchase

1. **Finance Process**: How was your finance experience? (Select one of the options: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor, Did not use finance)
2. **Purchase Process**: How smooth was the purchase process? (Select one of the options: Very smooth, Smooth, Acceptable, Complicated, Very difficult)

### Step 6: Additional Questions

1. **Time Spent**: How long were you at the dealership? (Select one of the options: Under 30 minutes, 30 minutes to 1 hour, 1-2 hours, 2-3 hours, Over 3 hours)
2. **Wait Time Rating**: Was the wait time acceptable? (Select one of the options: Yes - very reasonable, Yes - acceptable, Somewhat long, Too long, Unacceptable)
3. **Likelihood to Recommend**: Would you recommend us to others? (Select one of the options: Definitely yes, Probably yes, Maybe, Probably not, Definitely not)
4. **Return Likelihood**: Would you return to our dealership for future purchases? (Select one of the options: Definitely yes, Probably yes, Maybe, Probably not, Definitely not)

### Step 7: Final Comments

1. **Best Aspect**: What did we do well? (Please provide a brief description)
2. **Areas for Improvement**: What could we improve? (Please provide a brief description)
3. **Additional Comments**: Any other feedback or suggestions? (Please provide any additional comments or suggestions)

Note: Please answer the questions honestly, and provide as much detail as possible. Thank you for taking the time to participate in this survey!
