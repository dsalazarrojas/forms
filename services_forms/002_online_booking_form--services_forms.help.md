# online_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will help you fill out the "online_booking_form" for your online booking request. Please follow the instructions below to ensure a successful submission.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Start" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Enter the "form_title" field with a brief description of your booking request.
3. Fill out the "form_name" field with your name.
4. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
5. Add your phone number in the "phone" field.
6. Choose a date for your appointment from the calendar in the "date" field.
7. Select a time for your appointment from the dropdown menu in the "time" field.
8. Enter the "location" field with the address of the appointment venue.
9. (Optional) Add a note about your appointment in the "note" field.
10. If you have a preferred tool or equipment assigned to you, enter it in the "assigned_tool" field.
11. If you have a preferred location for your appointment, enter it in the "suggested_location" field.
12. If you have a preferred date for your appointment, enter it in the "suggested_date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form_title** (Text, Optional): A brief description of your booking request.
* **form_name** (Text, Optional): Your name.
* **email** (Email, Optional): Your email address.
* **phone** (Text, Optional): Your phone number.
* **date** (Date, Optional): Choose a date for your appointment from the calendar.
* **time** (Time, Optional): Select a time for your appointment from the dropdown menu.
* **location** (Text, Optional): Enter the address of the appointment venue.
* **note** (Note, Optional): Add a note about your appointment.
* **assigned_tool** (Text, Optional): If you have a preferred tool or equipment assigned to you, enter it here.
* **suggested_location** (Text, Optional): If you have a preferred location for your appointment, enter it here.
* **suggested_date** (Date, Optional): If you have a preferred date for your appointment, enter it here.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure a successful submission.
- If you have any additional requests or comments, feel free to add them in the "note" field.
- Double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid any mistakes.
