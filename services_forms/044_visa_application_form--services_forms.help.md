# visa-application-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The visa-application-form is used to gather information for a visa application. It includes fields for the applicant's nationality, date of birth, passport number, passport expiry date, purpose of trip, address, contact number, email, and emergency contact.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and valid information.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Apply For Visa
* **application_form**: Enter the purpose of your visa application.

### What is your nationality
* **nationality_form**: Enter your nationality.

### Date of Birth
* **date_of_birth_form**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Passport Number
* **passport_number_form**: Enter your passport number.

### Passport Expiry Date
* **passport_expirey_date_form**: Enter your passport expiry date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Purpose of Trip
* **purpose_of_trip_form**: Enter the purpose of your trip.

### Address
* **address_form**: Enter your current address.

### Contact Number
* **contact_number_form**: Enter your contact number.

### Email
* **email_form**: Enter your email address.

### Emergency Contact
* **emergency_contact_form**: Enter your emergency contact information.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and valid information in all fields.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
