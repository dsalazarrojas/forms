# it_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information for support requests or issues related to IT services. It's used to provide necessary details for the support team to address the issue efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with the requested information.
2. Make sure to include your contact email address and phone number for easy communication.
3. Provide clear and concise descriptions of the issue.
4. Choose the correct categories for software and/or hardware issues.
5. Specify the urgency and priority level of the request.
6. Confirm your request by clicking the "Submit" button.
