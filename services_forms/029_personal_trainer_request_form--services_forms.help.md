# personal_trainer_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users who are interested in requesting a personal trainer. It is designed to provide the personal training team with the necessary details to tailor their services according to the user's needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your **Email** address in the "Email" field, ensuring it is accurate and formatted correctly.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Phone" field.
5. Select the services you are interested in from the "Services" dropdown menu. You can select more than one service if applicable.
6. Choose which services you would like to receive from the specific service options below, selecting "Yes" or "No" for each service. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that you regularly check.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Services** (`Services`, select_multiple, required): Select all the services that interest you, separated by a comma. Options: Yes, No.
	+ Select all services that interest you to ensure the personal training team understands your needs.
* **Personal Training** (`Personal Training`, select_one, required): If you are interested in personal training, select "Yes". If not, select "No".
* **Weight Loss** (`Weight Loss`, select_one, required): If you are interested in weight loss, select "Yes". If not, select "No".
* **Nutrition Planning** (`Nutrition Planning`, select_one, required): If you are interested in nutrition planning, select "Yes". If not, select "No".

## Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Ensure your email address and phone number are up-to-date and reachable.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the personal training team for assistance.
