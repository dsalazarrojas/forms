# Third Party Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request authorization from a third-party entity to perform specific actions or access certain resources on their behalf.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked as "required".
2. For fields not marked as "required", you can enter any relevant information you think is necessary.
3. Make sure to select the correct "Authorization Type" from the dropdown menu.
4. Enter any additional comments or context in the "Note" field if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Third Party Authorization** (`third_party_authorization_form`, text, required): Enter the name of the third-party entity.
* **Name** (name, text, optional): Enter the name of the individual or organization requesting authorization.
* **Email** (email, email, optional): Enter the email address of the individual or organization requesting authorization.
* **Phone** (phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the individual or organization requesting authorization.
* **Authorization Type** (authorization_type, select_one, optional): Select the type of authorization being requested.
* **Date** (date, date, optional): Enter the date for which the authorization is valid.
* **Time** (time, time, optional): Enter the time for which the authorization is valid.
* **Note** (note, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or context about the authorization request.
* **Account Name** (account_name, text, optional): Enter the name of the account associated with this authorization.
* **Account Number** (account_number, text, optional): Enter the account number associated with this authorization.
* **Third Party Email** (third_party_email, text, optional): Enter the email address of the third-party entity.
* **Account Type** (account_type, text, optional): Enter the type of account associated with this authorization.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure the form is complete and accurate.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to your department's guidelines and procedures for third-party authorization.
