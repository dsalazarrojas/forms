# School Bus Driver's Daily Pre-Trip Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to record your vehicle's condition before a trip, ensuring that you identify and address any issues or concerns.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the status for each of the following categories:
	* Vehicle Status: Choose if the vehicle is safe to travel.
	* Trip Number: Enter the trip number assigned to you.
	* Driver Info: Enter your name and any other necessary information.
2. Inspect the vehicle's equipment:
	* Check each item and choose the corresponding status:
	* Seat belts: Normal, with minor defects, or not working.
	* All lights: Normal, with minor defects, or not working.
	* All reflective devices: Normal, with minor defects, or not working.
	* Safety chains: Normal, with minor defects, or not working.
	* All mirrors: Normal, with minor defects, or not working.
3. Check the bus's systems:
	* Bus Body: Choose the status (Normal, Normal with minor defects, or Not Normal).
	* Tires: Normal, with minor defects, or Not Normal.
	* Brakes: Normal, with minor defects, or Not Normal.
	* Lights System: Normal, with minor defects, or Not Normal.
	* Suspension System: Normal, with minor defects, or Not Normal.
	* Steering System: Normal, with minor defects, or Not Normal.
4. Inspect additional items:
	* Horn: Normal or not working.
	* Mirrors: Normal or not normal.
	* Parking Brakes: Normal or not working.
	* Fire Suppression System: Normal or not working.
5. Enter your driver's log and any vehicle status notes in the provided fields.

* **Vehicle Status**: Select the status of the vehicle, whether it's safe to travel or not.
* **Trip Number**: Enter the trip number assigned to you.

* **Tires**: Select Normal, with minor defects, or Not Normal.
* **Brakes**: Select Normal, with minor defects, or Not Normal.
* **Lights System**: Select Normal, with minor defects, or Not Normal.
* **Suspension System**: Select Normal, with minor defects, or Not Normal.
