# equipment_breakdown_summary_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The equipment breakdown summary report form is used to report and track equipment downtime. This form captures information about the equipment, its status, maintenance frequency, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by providing a brief **Summary** of the equipment breakdown.
2.  Enter the **Equipment details**, including the model number and location of the equipment.
3.  Describe the **Failure description** in detail.
4.  Provide **Repair details** and estimated **Repair cost**.
5.  Enter **Incident history**, if applicable.
6.  Select the **Status** of the equipment (Functional, Non-functional, or Offline).
7.  Indicate the **Maintenance frequency** (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
8.  Enter the **Date of last maintenance** and **Last inspection date**, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Summary**: A short description of the equipment breakdown (e.g., a brief report of the issue).
* **Equipment details**: Enter the model number and location of the affected equipment.
* **Failure description**: A detailed description of the failure.
* **Repair details**: Enter the details of the repair process and estimated cost.
* **Incident history**: Enter any relevant incident history related to the equipment.
* **Status**: Select the current status of the equipment (Functional, Non-functional, or Offline).
* **Maintenance frequency**: Indicate how often maintenance is performed on the equipment (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Last maintenance**: Enter the date of the last maintenance performed.
* **Last inspection**: Enter the date of the last inspection performed (if applicable).

## Tips
- Make sure to complete each field accurately and thoroughly.
- Double-check the form for completeness before submitting.
- If you're unsure about any field, please contact the relevant personnel for guidance.
