# House Painting Estimate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool for customers to provide information for the house painting estimate. Fill in the form with accurate details to get a quick and reliable estimate of the total cost of the service.

## How To Complete This Form

- Read each question carefully and fill in the fields accordingly.
- Select the relevant options for each field from the dropdown menu.
- Enter numerical values in the required fields (e.g., Number of Rooms).
- Use the text field for special requests or notes.
- Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **House Type** (`House Type`, required/true): Select the type of house you have. This will help us understand your house's specific needs.
* **Painting Area** (`Painting Area`, required/true): Choose the areas of the house that need painting. Be as specific as possible (e.g., Living room, Kitchen, Dining room, etc.).
* **Color Scheme** (`Color Scheme`, required/true): Select the color scheme you want for your house. This will influence the type and quantity of paint needed.
* **Number of Stories** (`Number of Stories`, required/true): Select the number of stories your house has. This will affect the amount of paint needed.
* **Number of Rooms** (`Number of Rooms`, required/true): Count the total number of rooms in your house. This will influence the total area to be painted.
* **Special Requests** (`Special Requests`, optional/false): Add any special requests or notes about your painting needs.
* **Painting Service** (`Painting Service`, required/true): Select whether you need painting service. If yes, we'll take care of everything.
* **Paint Color** (`Paint Color`, required/true): Select the color of paint you need. This will influence the type and quantity of paint needed.
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, required/true): Select how you want to pay for the service. This will help us process your payment efficiently.
* **Confirm** (`Confirm`, required/true): Double-check your answers to ensure they are accurate.
* **Total Cost** (`Total Cost`, required/true): Enter the estimated total cost of the painting service. This will serve as a reference for your final payment.
