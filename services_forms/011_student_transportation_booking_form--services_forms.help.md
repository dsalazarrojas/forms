# Student Transportation Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students who need transportation services. It helps the organization or institution providing these services to process and manage bookings efficiently.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your **Student Information** (Field 1). This includes your name and any other relevant student details.
2. Provide **Parent Information** (Field 2). This is where you can input your parent's or guardian's contact details.
3. Enter **Travel Details** (Field 3). This includes information about your travel dates, times, and destinations.
4. Complete **Payment Details** (Field 4). This is where you'll enter your payment method and any other financial information required.
5. Finally, add any **Other Details** (Field 5) that might be relevant to your booking request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information** (Field 1, Text, Required): Fill in your name and any other relevant student details to help us serve you better.
* **Parent Information** (Field 2, Text, Required): Enter your parent's or guardian's name, contact number, and any other relevant details.
* **Travel Details** (Field 3, Text, Required): Include information about your travel dates, times, and destinations to ensure we can plan your transportation accordingly.
* **Payment Details** (Field 4, Text, Required): Provide your payment method and any other financial information required for the booking.
* **Other Details** (Field 5, Text, Optional): Add any additional information that might be helpful for us to know about your booking request.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled correctly to avoid delays or errors in processing your booking.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
