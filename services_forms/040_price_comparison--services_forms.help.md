# Price Comparison - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Price Comparison" form is designed to help customers or prospects compare prices of products or services across different currencies. This form is intended to assist users in selecting a price range and currency pair for comparison.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your price range for AUD, EUR, and USD by entering a value into the respective fields.
2. Choose whether or not to compare prices in each currency pair by selecting the desired option from the dropdown menus.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your price range for AUD?** (`price_range_aud`, number, required): Enter the price range you are looking for in Australian Dollars.
* **What is your price range for EUR?** (`price_range_eur`, number, required): Enter the price range you are looking for in Euros.
* **What is your price range for USD?** (`price_range_usd`, number, required): Enter the price range you are looking for in US Dollars.
* **Do you want to compare prices in AUD?** (`compare_aud`, checkbox, required): Check this option if you want to compare prices in AUD.
* **Do you want to compare prices in EUR?** (`compare_eur`, checkbox, required): Check this option if you want to compare prices in EUR.
* **Do you want to compare prices in USD?** (`compare_usd`, checkbox, required): Check this option if you want to compare prices in USD.
* **Do you want to compare prices in AUD and EUR?** (`compare_aud_eur`, checkbox, required): Check this option if you want to compare prices in both AUD and EUR.
* **Do you want to compare prices in AUD and USD?** (`compare_aud_usd`, checkbox, required): Check this option if you want to compare prices in both AUD and USD.
* **Do you want to compare prices in EUR and USD?** (`compare_eur_usd`, checkbox, required): Check this option if you want to compare prices in both EUR and USD.
* **What is your price point for AUD?** (`price_point_aud`, number, optional): If you want to compare a specific price point, enter it here.
* **What is your price point for EUR?** (`price_point_eur`, number, optional): If you want to compare a specific price point, enter it here.
* **What is your price point for USD?** (`price_point_usd`, number, optional): If you want to compare a specific price point, enter it here.

## Tips
* Make sure to select a price range for each currency you want to compare.
* If you only want to compare prices in one currency pair, select only that option.
* Use this form to compare prices of products or services across different currencies.
