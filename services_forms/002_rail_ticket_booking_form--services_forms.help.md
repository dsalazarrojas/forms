# rail_ticket_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The rail ticket booking form is designed to collect basic information from customers or users for booking a rail ticket.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name.
2. Fill in your last name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Select one option from the "Select One" dropdown menu.
6. Select one or more options from the "Select Multiple" dropdown menu.
7. Enter the date of your trip.
8. Enter the time of your trip.
9. Add any additional notes about your booking.
10. Confirm your details by clicking the "Confirm" button.
11. Review your information and click the "Submit" button to complete your booking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, optional): Select one option from the dropdown menu.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more options from the dropdown menu.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your trip.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your trip.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your booking.
* **Confirm Label** (`confirm_label`, text, optional): This field is not intended to be filled in by the user.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click this button to complete your booking.
* **Confirm** (`confirm`, text, optional): Confirm your details before submitting the form.
* **Confirm Label** (`confirm_label`, text, optional): This field is not intended to be filled in by the user. 

Note: The "Confirm Label" field is likely not needed in the form and can be removed. It is included in the form data but does not serve any purpose. 

Tips:
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate booking information.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues with the form, contact the relevant authorities for assistance.
