# Vehicle Take Over Report Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Take Over Report Form Template is a document used to record the details of a vehicle being taken over. This form helps to ensure that all necessary information is captured accurately and consistently, making it easier to track and manage vehicle take overs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by selecting the vehicle type from the options provided. This will help identify the specific vehicle being taken over.
2.  If necessary, indicate if the vehicle was taken over due to the owner's consent or if it was taken over without consent.
3.  Choose the vehicle's make from the available options.
4.  Select the vehicle's model from the provided options.
5.  Enter the current odometer reading of the vehicle.
6.  Specify the current fuel level of the vehicle, if necessary.
7.  Select the type of fuel in the vehicle, if applicable.
8.  Describe any existing damage to the vehicle, if applicable.
9.  Specify the overall condition of the vehicle, if necessary.
10. If the vehicle has a fuel card, enter the information in the corresponding field.
11. Provide a description of any damage to the vehicle, if applicable.
