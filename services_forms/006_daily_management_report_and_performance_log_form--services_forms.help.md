# Daily Management Report And Performance Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Management Report And Performance Log Form is a tool to provide a daily report and performance log for your assigned tools and tasks. This form helps you track your progress, update your team's status, and report on your work. It's essential to fill this form accurately and thoroughly to provide valuable insights for your daily management and performance analysis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the assigned tool from the dropdown menu (assigned_tool_label).
2. Enter the name of the task you are working on (task_name_label).
3. Fill in the daily management report and performance log form (daily_management_report_form_label) on the first page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### daily_management_report_form_label (`daily_management_report_form_label`, text, optional)

*   The daily management report form is a field where you can enter any relevant information or comments about your daily work. This is an optional field, but it's recommended to fill it in to provide more details about your work.

### assigned_tool_label (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required)

*   The assigned tool is a dropdown menu where you select the tool you are working with. You can choose between "chatjimmy" and "another tool".

### task_name_label (`task_name`, text, optional)

*   The task name is a field where you can enter the name of the task you are working on. This is an optional field, but it's recommended to fill it in to provide more context about your task.
 
## Tips

*   Make sure to select the correct tool and task name to ensure accurate reporting and performance analysis.
*   Be concise and detailed in your daily management report and performance log.
*   Review and update your form regularly to track your progress and performance over time.
