# Outdoor Care Services Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register your information for outdoor care services. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure your registration is successful.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone Number, and Property Address.
2. Select the type of service you require from the Service Type dropdown menu.
3. Choose a date and time for the scheduled appointment from the Calendar icon.
4. Optionally, provide additional notes or authorization details in the Service Details and Authorization fields.
5. Select the follow-up duration for any scheduled appointment from the Follow Up dropdown menu.
6. Assign the service to a specific team member from the Assigned to dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number where we can reach you.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one, required): Choose from Landscaping, Property Care, or Other to select the type of service you require.
* **Schedule Date** (`schedule_date`, date, required): Select the date you would like to schedule the service.
* **Schedule Time** (`schedule_time`, time, required): Select the time of day you would like to schedule the service.
* **Authorization** (`authorization`, note, optional): Provide any necessary authorization or clearance information for the service.
* **Service Details** (`service_details`, text, required): Describe the specific details of the service you require.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, required): Enter the exact address of the property requiring service.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments for the service.
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the follow-up duration for any scheduled appointment from 1 day, 3 days, or 7 days.
* **Assigned to** (`assigned_to`, select_one, optional): Select the team member to assign the service to from the dropdown menu.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask the administrator for clarification.
* Double-check your registration before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
