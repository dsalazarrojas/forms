## Gas Boiler Replacement Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients seeking gas boiler replacement or installation services. It will help our team to quickly and accurately assess the client's needs and provide necessary assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all the required fields, as indicated by the asterisk (*) symbol.
2. For multiple-choice fields, select the option that best represents your situation.
3. Be as detailed as possible when providing information in text fields.
4. If you have any special requests or additional information, please provide it in the "Additional Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`general_info`, `text`, required): This field is used to collect your contact information, which will be used to communicate with you regarding your gas boiler replacement or installation request.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required): Please provide the name and title/job title of the contact person in charge of the boiler replacement or installation project.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your company or organization's address where the gas boiler is located.
* **Gas Boiler Info** (`gas_boiler_info`, `select_multiple`, required): Please indicate if the gas boiler has been replaced or is currently operational. Select all that apply.
	+ Yes: The gas boiler has been replaced.
	+ No: The gas boiler is currently operational.
* **Reason for Replacement** (`reason_for_replacement`, `text`, required): Provide a brief explanation for why you are requesting a gas boiler replacement.
* **Boiler Age** (`boiler_age`, `number`, required): Enter the age of the gas boiler.
* **Boiler Condition** (`boiler_condition`, `text`, required): Describe the current condition of the gas boiler, e.g., new, old, good, or bad.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter a contact phone number where you can be reached.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter a contact email address where you can be reached.
* **Preferred Installation Date** (`preferred_installation_date`, `date`, required): Please provide a preferred date for the gas boiler installation.
* **Special Request** (`special_request`, `note`, required): If you have any special requests or requirements, please provide them here.
* **Gas Provider** (`gas_provider`, `select_one`, required): Please indicate if you would like us to arrange for the gas boiler gas provider or if you have already chosen one.
	+ Yes: Please arrange for us to find a gas provider.
	+ No: We have already chosen a gas provider.
* **Gas Boiler Model** (`gas_boiler_model`, `text`, required): Enter the model of the gas boiler to be replaced or installed.
* **Gas Boiler Brand** (`gas_boiler_brand`, `text`, required): Enter the brand of the gas boiler to be replaced or installed.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, `text`, required): Please provide any additional information that may be helpful for our team to assist you.
* **Installation Time** (`installation_time`, `time`, required): Please select the installation time that is most convenient for you.
* **Installation Date** (`installation_date`, `date`, required): Provide the installation date for the gas boiler.
* **Installation Location** (`installation_location`, `text`, required): Enter the location where the gas boiler is to be installed.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, `select_one`, required): Please indicate if you would like to be followed up after the installation.
	+ Yes: Yes, please follow up with me after the installation.
	+ No: No follow up required.
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, `date`, required): If you selected "Yes" for follow-up, please provide a specific date for the follow-up.
* **Contact Telephone** (`contact_telephone`, `text`, required): Enter a contact telephone number where you can be reached.
* **Contact Fax** (`contact_fax`, `text`, required): Enter a contact fax number where you can be reached.
* **Contact Cell** (`contact_cell`, `text`, required): Enter a contact cell number where you can be reached.
* **Contact Alternate** (`contact_alt`, `text`, required): Enter an alternate contact name and phone number.
* **Contact Other** (`contact_other`, `text`, required): Enter any other contact information.
