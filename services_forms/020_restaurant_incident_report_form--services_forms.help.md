# Restaurant Incident Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Restaurant Incident Report Form is a crucial tool for documenting and tracking incidents that occur within the restaurant. This form helps restaurant staff, management, and other stakeholders to report incidents such as food poisoning, accidents, or other events that affect the establishment's operations and reputation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*), to ensure you are providing the most accurate and informative report.
2. Enter the date and time the incident occurred in the "Incident Date and Time" field.
3. Provide the location of the incident in the "Location" field.
4. Indicate if there were witnesses by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Witnesses" field.
5. If applicable, attach any supporting documents in the "Supporting Documents" field.
6. Enter a description of the incident in the "Incident Description" field.
7. Select the type of incident from the "Incident Type" field, which includes options like Food Poisoning, Food Allergy, Fire, and Other.
8. Enter the date the report was submitted in the "Date Submitted" and "Time Submitted" fields.
9. Select the category of the incident from the "Incident Category" field, which includes options like Restaurant, Kitchen, Customer, and Other.
10. Select the subcategory of the incident from the "Incident Subcategory" field, which includes options like Food Handling, Food Preparation, Food Storage, and Customer Behavior.
11. Enter the location details, including address, city, state, postal code, and country, in the corresponding fields.
12. Select who reported the incident from the "Incident Reported By" field, which includes options like Restaurant, Customer, Kitchen Staff, and Other.
13. Select the status of the incident from the "Incident Status" field, which includes options like New, In Progress, Resolved, and Closed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date and Time** (`incident_date_and_time`, date, *): Enter the date and time the incident occurred.
* **Location** (`location`, text, *): Provide the location of the incident, including the address, city, state, and postal code.
* **Witnesses** (`witnesses`, select_multiple): Indicate if there were witnesses by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, note): Attach any relevant documents that support your report.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note): Enter any additional notes or comments about the incident.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, *): Select who the incident should be assigned to.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select_one, *): Select the type of incident, such as Food Poisoning, Food Allergy, Fire, or Other.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, *): Enter a detailed description of the incident.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date): Enter the date you submitted the report.
* **Time Submitted** (`time_submitted`, time): Enter the time you submitted the report.
* **Incident Category** (`incident_category`, select_one, *): Select the category of the incident, such as Restaurant, Kitchen, Customer, or Other.
* **Incident Subcategory** (`incident_subcategory`, select_one, *): Select the subcategory of the incident, such as Food Handling, Food Preparation, Food Storage, or Customer Behavior.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, *): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Location Type** (`location_type`, select_one, *): Select the type of location, such as On Premises, Off Premises, or Other.
* **Location Address** (`location_address`, text): Enter the address of the location.
* **Location City** (`location_city`, text): Enter the city of the location.
* **Location State** (`location_state`, text): Enter the state of the location.
* **Location Postal Code** (`location_postal_code`, text): Enter the postal code of the location.
* **Location Country** (`location_country`, text): Enter the country of the location.
* **Incident Reported By** (`incident_reported_by`, select_one, *): Select who reported the incident, such as Restaurant, Customer, Kitchen Staff, or Other.
* **Incident Type Details** (`incident_type_details`, text): Enter any additional details about the incident type.
* **Incident Date Submitted** (`incident_date_submitted`, date): Enter the date you submitted the report.
* **Incident Status** (`incident_status`, select_one, *): Select the status of the incident, such as New, In Progress, Resolved, or Closed.

Note: The fields marked with an asterisk (*) are required fields.
