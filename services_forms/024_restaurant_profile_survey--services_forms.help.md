# Restaurant Profile Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form aims to collect feedback on various aspects of your restaurant's experience, including its attributes and services. Your input will help us understand your overall satisfaction and provide valuable insights for us to improve.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you answer each question as accurately as possible.
2. Fill in the required fields with your honest and thoughtful feedback.
3. Take your time to provide detailed answers in the text fields.
4. Select the rating options for each attribute that best describes your experience.
5. When prompted, provide a review message sharing your thoughts on your visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Restaurant Information**: This field is to collect the name or identifier of the restaurant you visited.
* **Address**: In this field, please provide the exact location of the restaurant, including the building or street name, number, city, and state/region.
* **Contact Number**: This field is for you to provide a phone number or any other contact information of the restaurant.
* **Description**: Share a brief description of the restaurant, including its type (e.g., Italian, Chinese, etc.), cuisine, and any notable features.
* **Rating 1**: Rate the quality of food on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Rating 2**: Rate the quality of service on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Rating 3**: Rate the cleanliness of the restaurant on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Rating 4**: Rate the value for money on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is overpriced and 5 is reasonably priced.
* **Rating 5**: Rate the ambience of the restaurant on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Overall Rating**: Provide an overall rating from 1 to 5 that reflects your experience with the restaurant.
* **Recommendation**: Share if you would Highly Recommend, Somewhat Recommend, or Not Recommend the restaurant.
* **Review Message**: Write a message sharing your thoughts on your visit, including any praise or criticism.
* **Service Quality**: Describe the quality of service you experienced during your visit.
* **Food Quality**: Describe the quality of food you had during your visit.

* **Ambience**: Rate the ambience of the restaurant on a scale of 1-5.
* **Service Attentiveness**: Describe the service attentiveness you experienced during your visit.
