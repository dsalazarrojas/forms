# Customer Service Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Service Survey is designed to gather valuable feedback from customers to improve our customer service process. We would like to hear about your experience with our team, and your responses will help us to identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately to provide your honest feedback.
2. Please answer all required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (*).
3. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the text fields provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for future correspondence.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, `select_one`, optional): Select how you usually contact us (phone, email, or chat).
* **Service Rating** (`service_rating`, `number`, required): Rate your experience with our customer service team from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Time of Day** (`time_of_day`, `date`, optional): Select a date when you usually contact us.
* **Time Of Day 2** (`time_of_day_2`, `time`, optional): Select a time when you usually contact us.
* **How often do you contact us?** (`question`, `text`, required): Please type how often you contact us.
* **Contact Frequency** (`contact_frequency`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select how often you contact us (once a month, twice a month, once a week).
* **Customer Service Comments** (`customer_service_survey_comments`, `text`, required): Enter any additional feedback or comments about your experience with our customer service.
* **Contact Method 2** (`contact_method_2`, `select_one`, optional): Select how you usually contact us (phone, email, or chat).
* **Time Of Day 3** (`time_of_day_3`, `time`, required): Select a time when you usually contact us.
* **How long do you usually contact us?** (`question_2`, `select_one`, optional): Select how long you usually contact us (less than 30 minutes, 30 to 60 minutes, more than 60 minutes).
* **Customer Service Survey Comments 2** (`customer_service_survey_comments_2`, `text`, required): Enter any additional feedback or comments about your experience with our customer service.
* **Email 2** (`email_2`, `email`, optional): Enter an email address where you can be reached.
* **Do you usually contact us during office hours?** (`question_3`, `select_one`, optional): Select yes or no to whether you usually contact us during office hours.
* **Office Hours** (`office_hours`, `time`, optional): Select a time when our office hours are.
* **Question 4** (`question_4`, `select_one`, optional): Select yes or no to whether you are satisfied with our customer service.
* **Customer Service Survey Comments 3** (`customer_service_survey_comments_3`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional feedback or comments about your experience with our customer service.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): Select a time when you contact us.
* **How long did it take for you to contact us?** (`question_5`, `text`, optional): Please type how long it took for you to contact us.
* **Contact Time** (`contact_time`, `number`, optional): Enter a time it took for you to contact us.
* **How long did it take for you to get help?** (`question_6`, `text`, optional): Please type how long it took for you to get help.
* **Resolution Time** (`resolution_time`, `number`, optional): Enter a time it took for you to get help.
* **Question 7** (`question_7`, `text`, optional): Please type any additional feedback or comments.
* **Would you like to provide any additional comments or feedback?** (`question_8`, `text`, required): Please use this field to provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with our customer service.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* Your feedback is invaluable to us, and we appreciate your time and effort.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
