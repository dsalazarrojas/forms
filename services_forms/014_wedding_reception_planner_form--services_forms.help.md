# Wedding Reception Planner Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Reception Planner Form is designed to collect essential information from couples to create a comprehensive plan for their wedding reception.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through the form carefully to understand each field's purpose.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
3. Choose options for select fields, such as the wedding reception style and professional services.
4. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Bride & Groom Information** (`bride_groom_information`, `text`, required: false): Enter the names of the couple getting married.
* **Wedding Ceremony** (`wedding_ceremony`, `text`, required: false): Briefly describe the wedding ceremony, including location and details.
* **Wedding Reception Venue** (`wedding_reception_venue`, `text`, required: false): Specify the venue for the wedding reception.
* **Wedding Reception Style** (`wedding_reception_style`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose from options: Classic, Modern, Rustic, Beach, Country, Outdoor, Theme Park.
* **Guest Information** (`guests_information`, `text`, required: false): Enter any special guest information, such as dietary restrictions or special needs.
* **Guest Number** (`guests_number`, `number`, required: false): Provide an estimate of the number of guests attending the reception.
* **Guest List** (`guests_list`, `text`, required: false): List the guests attending, separated by commas.
* **Food and Beverages** (`food_and_beverages`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose from options: Food, Wedding Cake, Hors d'oeuvres, Canapés.
* **Entertainment** (`entertainment`, `text`, required: false): List any entertainment or special activities planned for the reception.
* **Other Professional Services** (`other_professional_services`, `text`, required: false): List any other professional services required, such as photographers or videographers.
* **Additional Services** (`additional_services`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose from options: DJ, Photographer, Videographer, Hairdresser, Makeup Artist, Wedding Planner.
* **Reception Date** (`reception_date`, `date`, required: false): Specify the date of the wedding reception.
* **Reception Time** (`reception_time`, `time`, required: false): Specify the time of the wedding reception.
* **Venue Details** (`venue_details`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional venue details, such as layout or parking information.
* **Parking Information** (`parking_info`, `text`, required: false): Provide parking information, such as availability and locations.
* **Parking Fee** (`parking_fee`, `number`, required: false): Specify the parking fee, if applicable.
* **Accommodations** (`accommodations`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional accommodations information, such as hotel or shuttle details.
* **Accommodations Details** (`accommodations_details`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional accommodation details, such as room types or capacities.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, `text`, required: false): List any special requests for the reception.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `email`, required: false): Provide the contact information for the couple or planner.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, `text`, required: false): The couple's signature for verification and record-keeping purposes.
* **Wedding Planner Signature** (`planner_signature`, `text`, required: false): The wedding planner's signature for verification and record-keeping purposes.
* **Wedding Planner Signature Date** (`planner_signature_date`, `date`, required: false): The date of the wedding planner's signature.
* **Wedding Planner Signature Time** (`planner_signature_time`, `time`, required: false): The time of the wedding planner's signature.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Leave any additional comments or notes for the wedding planner.
