# Fire Rescue Aerosolized Procedure Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fire Rescue Aerosolized Procedure Report Form is used to report the details of a rescue procedure when the fire chief or another officer is not available.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the Lead Medic Info section with the name, title, and other relevant information of the lead medical officer.
2. Enter the Email address of the Lead Medic in the Lead Medic Email field.
3. In the Hidden EMS Chief section, enter the name, title, and other relevant information of the hidden EMS chief.
4. Enter the Email address of the Hidden EMS Chief in the Hidden EMS Chief Email field.
5. Enter the phone number of the Hidden EMS Chief in the Hidden EMS Chief Phone field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Lead Medic Info** (`lead_medical`): Enter the name, title, and other relevant information of the lead medical officer.
* **Lead Medic Email** (`lead_medical_email`): Enter the email address of the lead medical officer.
* **Hidden EMS Chief Info** (`hidden_ems_chief`): Enter the name, title, and other relevant information of the hidden EMS chief.
* **Hidden EMS Chief Email** (`hidden_ems_chief_email`): Enter the email address of the hidden EMS chief.
* **Hidden EMS Chief Name** (`hidden_ems_chief_name`): Enter the name of the hidden EMS chief.
* **Hidden EMS Chief Title** (`hidden_ems_chief_title`): Enter the title of the hidden EMS chief.
* **Hidden EMS Chief Phone** (`hidden_ems_chief_phone`): Enter the phone number of the hidden EMS chief.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have the correct permissions to access and use this form.
* Double-check that you have filled in all required fields.
* This form is for reporting the details of a rescue procedure, so ensure you have the correct information to capture.
* If the form is incomplete or contains incorrect information, please revisit the fields and complete them accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your supervisor or the relevant department.
