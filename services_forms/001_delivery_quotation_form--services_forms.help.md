# Delivery Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Delivery Quotation Form is a simple form that collects information about a customer's purchase request. The form helps us gather essential details to process and deliver the requested product efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the product ID, if provided.
2. Enter the product name. If not provided, try to enter a description or a known name related to the product.
3. Enter the quantity you wish to purchase.
4. Choose how you want to receive your delivery: select one option from the available methods.
5. Enter your contact information: your name, a valid email address, and a phone number.
6. Click on the "Submit" button to complete and send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. product_id (<name>): 
* This field is used to collect the unique identifier of the product you are interested in.

### 2. product_name (<name>): 
* This is where you enter the name of the product you want to buy.

### 3. quantity (<name>): 
* Enter how many units or items you wish to purchase.

### 4. delivery_method (<name>): 
* Select one of the available delivery methods to receive your order.

### 5. customer_name (<name>): 
* Enter your full name as the customer.

### 6. customer_email (<name>): 
* Enter a valid email address for correspondence.

### 7. customer_phone (<name>): 
* Enter your phone number for any further communication or delivery updates.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check the product ID for accuracy.
* Enter valid contact information for efficient communication.
* Review all the fields before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you need help or have questions, please feel free to contact us for assistance.
