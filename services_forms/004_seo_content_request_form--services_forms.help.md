# seo_content_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SEO Content Request Form is used to collect information about Search Engine Optimization (SEO) content requests. It helps gather details about the type of content, length, format, and target audience required for the content.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have filled out all the required fields.
2. Fill in the Client Information section with your details, including your name, email, phone number, company, job title, and any additional notes.
3. In the Seo Request Details section, choose the type of content you need, select the content length and format, and specify if you have any target audience in mind.
4. Provide any additional information about the content you need, such as keywords, description, link, image, video, file, and additional notes.
5. Finally, fill in your requester's information, including their name, email, phone number, company, job title, and status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information:**
	+ **Client Information** (`client_information`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name, email, phone number, company, job title, and any additional notes about yourself.
* **Seo Request Details:**
	+ **Seo Request Details** (`seo_request_details`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the SEO content you need.
	+ **Seo Content Type** (`seo_content_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of content you need (e.g., text, image, video, file).
	+ **Seo Content Length** (`seo_content_length`, `number`, required: false): Specify the length of the content you need (e.g., number of words, characters).
	+ **Seo Content Format** (`seo_content_format`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the format of the content you need (e.g., blog post, article, social media post).
	+ **Seo Target Audience** (`seo_target_audience`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Specify if you have any target audience in mind (e.g., age, location, interest).
* **Seo Keywords and Description:**
	+ **Seo Keywords** (`seo_keywords`, `text`, required: false): Enter relevant keywords for the content.
	+ **Seo Description** (`seo_description`, `note`, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the content you need.
* **Links and Media:**
	+ **Seo Link** (`seo_link`, `text`, required: false): Provide a link to any relevant website or resource.
	+ **Seo Image** (`seo_image`, `text`, required: false): Upload an image related to the content.
	+ **Seo Video** (`seo_video`, `text`, required: false): Upload a video related to the content.
	+ **Seo File** (`seo_file`, `text`, required: false): Upload a file related to the content.
* **Requester Information:**
	+ **Seo Requester Name** (`seo_requester_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name.
	+ **Seo Requester Email** (`seo_requester_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
	+ **Seo Requester Phone** (`seo_requester_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
	+ **Seo Requester Company** (`seo_requester_company`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company name.
	+ **Seo Requester Job** (`seo_requester_job`, `text`, required: false): Enter your job title.
	+ **Seo Requester Notes** (`seo_requester_notes`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional notes about yourself.
	+ **Seo Requester Comments** (`seo_requester_comments`, `text`, required: false): Enter any comments about the request.
* **Agreement and Status:**
	+ **Seo Requester Agreement** (`seo_requester_agreement`, `text`, required: false): Sign an agreement with the requester.
	+ **Seo Requester Status** (`seo_requester_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Specify the status of the request (e.g., Active, Inactive).
* **User Information:**
	+ **Seo Requester User** (`seo_requester_user`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Specify if you have any user information (e.g., Yes, No).

## Tips
1. Make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure accurate data collection.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting it.
3. If you're unsure about any field, refer to this guide for clarification.
4. Use relevant keywords and descriptions for accurate content creation.
5. Attach any relevant images, videos, or files to support your request.
