# Repair Estimation Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect necessary information for repair estimation and quote purposes. It helps gather details about the customer, job specifics, and technician involvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that you receive an accurate repair estimation quote.
2. Enter your name and contact information in the "Customer Information" section.
3. Provide a brief description of the job and any relevant details in the "Job Details" section.
4. If you have any comments or notes about the job, please enter them in the "Customer Comments" section.
5. In the "Technician Information" section, enter the technician's name and contact information who will be performing the repair.
6. Enter the repair quote information, including any necessary details, in the "Repair Quote Information" section.
7. Select the services needed for the repair from the options provided in the "Selected Services" section.
8. Enter the cost of the selected services in the "Service Cost" field.
9. Once you have reviewed and agreed on the quote, please sign and date the document in the "Customer Signature" and "Technician Signature" sections.
10. Finally, enter the date and time the job is scheduled for completion in the "Job Date" and "Job Time" sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, not required)
	+ Enter your name and contact information for us to contact you about your repair.
* **Job Details** (`job_details`, text, not required)
	+ Provide a brief description of the job and any relevant details.
* **Customer Comments** (`customer_comments`, text, not required)
	+ If you have any additional comments or notes about the job, please enter them here.
* **Technician Information** (`technician_info`, text, not required)
	+ Enter the technician's name and contact information who will be performing the repair.
* **Repair Quote Information** (`repair_quote_info`, text, not required)
	+ Enter the repair quote details, including any necessary information.
* **Selected Services** (`services_selected`, select_multiple, not required)
	+ Select the services needed for the repair from the options provided.
* **Service Cost** (`service_cost`, number, not required)
	+ Enter the cost of the selected services.
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, text, not required)
	+ Once you have reviewed and agreed on the quote, please sign and date this section to confirm acceptance.
* **Technician Signature** (`technician_signature`, text, not required)
	+ Sign and date this section to confirm that the repair has been completed and accepted.
* **Job Date** (`job_date`, date, not required)
	+ Enter the date the job is scheduled for completion.
* **Job Time** (`job_time`, time, not required)
	+ Enter the time the job is scheduled for completion.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have all the required information before proceeding with the form.
- Double-check that you have entered all necessary details and signatures before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
