# Concierge Service Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Concierge Service Agreement form is a contract between the client and the concierge service provider. It outlines the services to be provided, the payment terms, and other essential details for the agreement. This form ensures both parties understand their roles and responsibilities before commencing the services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **client name** (full legal name) in the "Client name" field.
2. Provide your **email address** in the "Client contact email" field for agreement correspondence.
3. Enter the **start date** of the service in the "Service start date" field.
4. Describe the **services provided** under the agreement in the "Services provided" field.
5. Enter the **service fee** for the agreed-upon services.
6. Choose the **billing schedule** (Monthly, Weekly, or Per service) that suits your needs.
7. If required, provide the **emergency contact name and phone** for urgent service issues.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client name (1)**: Your full legal name as stated on your official government-issued ID.
* **Client contact email (2)**: Your email address where you want to receive agreement-related communications.
* **Service start date (3)**: The date when the services will begin.
* **Services provided (4)**: A description of the services included under this agreement.
* **Service fee (5)**: The agreed-upon payment amount for the services.
* **Billing schedule (6)**: Choose how often you want to be billed (Monthly, Weekly, or Per service).
* **Cancellation policy (7)**: This is where you can summarize your agreement's cancellation terms, if applicable.
* **Emergency contact name and phone (8)**: Provide information about who can be contacted in case of urgent matters.
* **Client signature instructions (9)**: This field will guide you on how to sign on the validated platform.
* **Additional terms or notes (10)**: Use this field to include any special conditions or terms that aren't covered in the agreement.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submission to avoid any errors.
* Double-check your contact information to ensure you can receive important agreement-related communications.
* Be clear and concise in your description of the services provided.
* If you're unsure about any part of the form, don't hesitate to reach out to the concierge service provider.
* Keep your additional terms or notes field clean and focused on essential details only.
* Once submitted, the form serves as an enforceable contract; make sure you're comfortable with the terms before signing.
