# Damage Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Damage Claim Form is a document used to report and manage damage claims. It is designed for businesses and individuals to submit a claim for reimbursement or compensation due to damage or loss of assets.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields as needed.
2. Enter your Business Name and Contact Name to identify yourself or your company.
3. Provide a detailed Description of the damage or loss.
4. Specify the Location of the incident.
5. Enter the Date and Time of the incident.
6. Choose the Damage Type.
7. Add a description of the Asset that was damaged or lost (Asset Description).
8. Enter the monetary Value of the damaged or lost item.
9. Provide a Contact Number and Email where you can be reached.
10. Optionally, you can specify the tools and equipment used or required (Assigned Tool).
11. Select the Category and Status of the claim.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name** (`Business Name`, text, required): Enter your business or company name.
* **Contact Name** (`Contact Name`, text, required): Enter your personal or company contact name.
* **Description** (`Description`, note, required): Provide a detailed description of the damage or loss.
* **Location** (`Location`, text, required): Specify the location of the incident.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, required): Enter the date of the incident.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, required): Enter the time of the incident.
* **Damage Type** (`Damage Type`, text, required): Choose the type of damage or loss.
* **Asset Description** (`Asset Description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the asset that was damaged or lost.
* **Value** (`Value`, number, required): Enter the monetary value of the damaged or lost asset.
* **Contact Number** (`Contact Number`, text, required): Enter a contact number where you can be reached.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Assigned Tool** (`Assigned Tool`, text, optional): Specify any tools or equipment used or required for the claim.
* **Category** (`Category`, select_one, optional): Choose the category of the asset (Assets, Property, Items).
* **Status** (`Status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the status of the claim (Pending, Resolved, In Progress).
* **Comments** (`Comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about the incident.

## Tips
Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and detailed, as this will help us process your claim efficiently. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
