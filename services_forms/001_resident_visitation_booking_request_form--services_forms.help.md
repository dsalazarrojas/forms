# resident_visitation_booking_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Resident Visitation Booking Request Form" is a document used to collect information about a visitation request, including the guest's details, visit type, reason, and staff involved.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*) as they are critical for processing your request.
2. Provide accurate information about the date and time of the visit.
3. Enter the details of the guest, including their name and contact information.
4. Choose the type of visit and reason for the visit.
5. List any special instructions or requirements for the visit.
6. Enter the details of the resident, including their ID number.
7. Specify the staff members involved in the visit.
8. Choose the status of the resident.
9. Enter any comments or notes about the visit.
10. Finally, submit the form for review and processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Resident Visitation Booking Request Form (title)**: This is the title of the form.
* **Visitation Request (1)**: Enter a brief description of the visit request. (\*)
* **Date and Time (2)**: Enter the date and time of the visit. (Optional)
* **Guest Details (3)**: Enter the guest's name and contact information. (\*)
* **Guest Name (4)**: Enter the guest's name. (\*)
* **Contact Info (5)**: Enter the guest's contact information. (Optional)
* **Visit Type (6)**: Choose the type of visit. (\*)
* **Visit Reason (7)**: Choose the reason for the visit. (\*)
* **Special Instructions (8)**: Enter any special instructions or requirements for the visit. (Optional)
* **Resident Details (9)**: Enter the resident's details, including their ID number. (\*)
* **Staff Involved (10)**: Specify the staff members involved in the visit. (\*)
* **Staff Notes (11)**: Enter any notes or comments about the staff. (Optional)
* **Staff ID (12)**: Enter the staff's ID number. (Optional)
* **Resident Status (13)**: Choose the status of the resident. (\*)
* **Comments (14)**: Enter any comments or notes about the visit. (Optional)
* **Submitted By (15)**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form. (Optional)
* **Submitted By ID (16)**: Enter the ID number of the person submitting the form. (Optional)
* **Submitted On (17)**: Enter the date and time the form was submitted. (Optional)
* **Submitted By Staff (18)**: Enter the name of the staff member submitting the form. (Optional)
* **Created By (19)**: Enter the name of the person creating the form. (Optional)
* **Created By ID (20)**: Enter the ID number of the person creating the form. (Optional)
* **Created On (21)**: Enter the date and time the form was created. (Optional)
* **Updated By (22)**: Enter the name of the person updating the form. (Optional)
* **Updated By ID (23)**: Enter the ID number of the person updating the form. (Optional)
* **Updated On (24)**: Enter the date and time the form was last updated. (Optional)
