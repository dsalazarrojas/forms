# Service Plan Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Plan Assessment Form is a questionnaire designed to collect information about your preferences, habits, and needs related to our services. This information will help us better understand how to tailor our services to individual needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by selecting the service you are interested in from the options provided in the "What service are you interested in?" field.
2.  Choose your preferred method of communication for our services (e.g., Email, Phone, Text).
3.  If you have a next birthday, provide the date. Otherwise, you can leave it blank.
4.  Indicate how many years you have been using our service.
5.  In the "What would you like to say?" field, provide any additional thoughts or comments about our services.
6.  For "Email Address" and "Phone number", simply enter your contact information.
7.  In "Additional Information", provide any further details about your preferences or needs.
8.  In "How do you plan to use our services?", describe how you plan to utilize our services.
9.  For "What is the name of your pet?", enter the name of any pet you may have.
10.  In "How do you take care of your pet?", describe your pet care routine.
11.  Finally, "What is your favorite food?" is a field for you to share any food preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Service Plan Assessment** (Text): This is where you can enter any general comments or thoughts about your service plan.
*   **What service are you interested in?** (Select One): Choose the service that best aligns with your needs from the provided options.
*   **How do you prefer to communicate?** (Select Multiple): Indicate which communication method (Email, Phone, Text) you prefer for our services.
*   **What date is your next birthday?** (Date): If you have a next birthday, enter the date; otherwise, leave blank.
*   **Time of day?** (Time): Enter the time you are most likely to engage with our services.
*   **How many years do you have in the service?** (Number): Enter the number of years you have been using our service.
*   **What would you like to say?** (Text): This field is for any additional comments or thoughts you have about our services.
*   **Email Address** (Email): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
*   **Phone number** (Text): Enter your phone number for any communication.
*   **Additional Information** (Note): Provide any further details about your preferences or needs.
*   **How do you plan to use our services?** (Text): Describe how you intend to utilize our services.
*   **What is the name of your pet?** (Text): Enter the name of any pet you may have.
*   **How do you take care of your pet?** (Text): Describe your pet care routine.
*   **What is your favorite food?** (Text): Share your favorite food.
*   **How do you feel about our services?** (Text): Enter any comments or thoughts about our services.
