# RFQ Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The RFQ Form is designed to gather information from customers or potential customers about their business needs and preferences. This information will be used to assess their requirements and determine the best course of action for the business.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Provide your contact details, such as name, email, and phone number.
3. Choose the correct business type and size.
4. Select the location and services offered by your business.
5. Write about your business in the "About Business" field.
6. Choose how you heard from us and how you would like to be contacted.
7. Provide additional comments if necessary.
8. Select the lead information options (lead type, lead value, lead priority, lead quality, lead source, lead channel, marketing channels, sales channels, target audience, target location, and lead time frame).
9. Save or submit your form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name** (1, text, required): Enter the name of your business.
* **Email** (2, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (3, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Business Type** (4, select_one, required): Choose the type of business you have (Business, Service, or Product).
* **Business Size** (5, select_multiple, optional): Choose the size of your business (1-10, 11-50, 51-100, 101-500, 501-1000, or >1000).
* **Location** (6, text, required): Enter the location of your business.
* **Services Offered** (7, text, required): List the services offered by your business.
* **About Business** (8, text, required): Write a brief description of your business.
* **How Did You Hear from Us** (9, select_one, required): Choose how you heard from us (Social Media, Word of Mouth, Online Search, or Other).
* **How Would You Like to be Contacted** (10, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, or Text Message).
* **Additional Comments** (11, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or information.
* **Contact Preference** (12, select_one, required): Choose whether you would like to be contacted by True or False.
* **Follow up** (13, select_one, optional): Choose whether you would like to be followed up on by True, False, or Only if Requested.
* **Lead Source** (14, select_one, optional): Choose the source of the lead (Online or Offline).
* **Lead Channel** (15, select_one, optional): Choose the channel of the lead (Website, Social Media, or Referral).
* **Marketing Channels** (16, select_multiple, optional): Choose the marketing channels used (Google Ads, Social Media, Referral, or Word of Mouth).
* **Sales Channels** (17, select_multiple, optional): Choose the sales channels used (Online, Offline, or Phone).
* **Target Audience** (18, select_multiple, optional): Choose the target audience (B2B, B2C, or Consumer).
* **Target Location** (19, select_multiple, optional): Choose the target location (Local, National, or Global).
* **Lead Time Frame** (20, select_multiple, optional): Choose the lead time frame (Immediately, Short-Term, Medium-Term, or Long-Term).
* **Lead Value** (21, number, required): Enter the value of the lead.
* **Lead Priority** (22, number, required): Enter the priority of the lead.
* **Lead Quality** (23, number, required): Enter the quality of the lead.
* **Lead Type** (24, select_one, optional): Choose the type of lead (New Lead or Follow up).
* **Notes** (25, text, optional): Provide any additional notes or comments.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact us for assistance.
