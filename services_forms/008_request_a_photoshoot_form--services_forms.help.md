# Request A Photoshoot Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Request A Photoshoot Form is designed to collect essential information from clients or potential clients about their interest in requesting a photoshoot service. This form helps the business understand client needs and preferences, making it easier to tailor their services and quote accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the name of your photographer's website in the "Photographer Website" field. This is the website where you can find more information about the photographer's services and style.
2. Select 'Yes' or 'No' in the "Get A Quote" field, indicating whether you would like to receive a quote for your photoshoot. If you select 'Yes', we will contact you to discuss your specific needs and preferences.
3. In the "Services Forms Description" field, provide a brief description of the services you are interested in. This will help us better understand your requirements and preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request A Photoshoot** (`request_a_photoshoot_form`, text, optional): Enter the name of the photoshoot you are interested in.
* **Photographer Website** (`photographer_website`, text, optional): Enter the name of your photographer's website.
* **Get A Quote** (`get_a_quote`, select one, optional): Select 'Yes' to receive a quote for your photoshoot, or 'No' if you don't want to receive one.
* **Services Forms Description** (`services_forms_description`, note, optional): Enter a brief description of the services you are interested in, if any.
