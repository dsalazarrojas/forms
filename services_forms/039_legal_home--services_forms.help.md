# Legal Home - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Legal Home" form is designed to collect information about your home and family services. This form will help us understand your requirements and provide personalized recommendations and services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select "Yes" or "No" for each service you are using or have experience with.
2. For "Legal Practitioner" fields, select all practitioners that apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service 1** (`legal_home_service_1`, select_one, required: false): Choose "Yes" if you are currently using or have used Service 1 in the past.
* **Service 2** (`legal_home_service_2`, text, required: false): Enter any additional information about Service 2 you would like to provide.
* **Service 3** (`legal_home_service_3`, text, required: false): Enter any additional information about Service 3 you would like to provide.
* **Legal Practitioner 1** (`legal_home_legal_practitioner_1`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose all legal practitioners that apply to you.
* **Legal Practitioner 2** (`legal_home_legal_practitioner_2`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose all legal practitioners that apply to you.

## Tips
* Ensure you are answering these questions based on your current and past experiences.
* You can select "Yes" for multiple services if applicable.
* For select_multiple fields, select all options that apply to you.
