# Handyman Service Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Handyman Service Request Form is used to collect information from customers or clients to provide handyman services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your Client Info, which includes your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
2. Describe your Service Request, providing a clear and concise summary of the issue or problem you need help with.
3. Provide Detailed Info about the job, including any relevant context or background information.
4. Choose your preferred Contact Method to reach you with the status of your request.
5. Enter your Contact Number, if you prefer to be contacted by phone.
6. Enter your Email, if you prefer to be contacted by email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Info
* **Client Info** (`client_info`, text, required: false): Please fill in your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself.

### Service Request
* **Service Request** (`service_request`, text, required: false): Describe the issue or problem you need help with in a clear and concise way.

### Detailed Info
* **Detailed Info** (`detailed_info`, text, required: false): Provide any relevant context or background information about the job.

### Contact Method
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, required: false): Choose how you would like to be contacted:
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ Chat

### Contact Number
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): If you prefer to be contacted by phone, enter your contact number here.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): If you prefer to be contacted by email, enter your email address here.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed and clear as possible in your Client Info, Service Request, and Detailed Info fields.
* Choose your preferred contact method and enter the correct contact information.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask for help or clarification.
