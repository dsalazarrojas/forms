# Community Service Hours Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Community Service Hours Form is a tool designed for students to report their community service hours completed outside the classroom. This form helps track and verify volunteer work completed for school requirements, personal interest, court mandates, and other purposes. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the program type that best describes why you are completing these hours (e.g., School Requirement, Court Mandated, Personal Interest, Corporate Social Responsibility, or Other).
2. Fill in your volunteer name, email, and phone number.
3. Enter the start and end times of your volunteer service.
4. Record the date of your service.
5. Provide a brief description of your duties.
6. Enter the number of hours completed (e.g., 4.5 hours).
7. Select "I Verify" for supervisor verification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Volunteer Name** (`Volunteer Name`, text, **required**): Enter your name as it appears on your student ID or driver's license.
- **Volunteer Email** (`Volunteer Email`, email, **required**): Enter your email address where your service hours will be sent or viewed.
- **Volunteer Phone Number** (`Volunteer Phone Number`, text, **required**): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for verification.
- **Program Type** (`Program Type`, select_one, **required**): Choose why you are completing these hours (e.g., School Requirement, Court Mandated, Personal Interest, Corporate Social Responsibility, or Other).
- **Name of Organization Served** (`Name of Organization Served`, text, **required**): Enter the name of the non-profit where you volunteered.
- **Date of Service** (`Date of Service`, date, **required**): Enter the date of your volunteer work.
- **Start Time** (`Start Time`, time, **required**): Enter the start time of your volunteer work.
- **End Time** (`End Time`, time, **required**): Enter the end time of your volunteer work.
- **Number of Hours Completed** (`Number of Hours Completed`, number, **required**): Enter the number of hours you worked (e.g., 4.5 hours).
- **Brief Description of Duties** (`Brief Description of Duties`, text, **required**): Describe the tasks or activities you performed during your volunteer work.
- **Supervisor Name** (`Supervisor Name`, text, **required**): Enter the supervisor's name at the organization where you volunteered.
- **Supervisor Title** (`Supervisor Title`, text, **required**): Enter the supervisor's job title or position.
- **Supervisor Phone** (`Supervisor Phone`, text, **required**): Enter the supervisor's phone number.
- **Supervisor - I verify these hours are correct** (`Supervisor - I verify these hours are correct`, select_one, **required**): Select "I Verify" to confirm the hours are accurate.
- **Verification Date** (`Verification Date`, date, **required**): Enter the date you verified the hours.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out your information accurately and completely.
- Review your form carefully before submitting.
- If you have any issues or concerns, contact your school or organization for assistance.
