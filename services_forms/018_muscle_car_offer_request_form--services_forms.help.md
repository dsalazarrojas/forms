# Muscle Car Offer Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow customers to submit their muscle car interests and contact details to the platform team, so we can provide relevant muscle car offers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Make sure you are ready to provide all required fields.** This form is designed to collect information about your muscle car needs, so please fill in all fields marked as required.
2. **Enter a brief description of the muscle car you are interested in.** In the "Muscle Car Make" field, enter the make of the muscle car you are interested in.
3. **Enter the specific model of the muscle car.** In the "Muscle Car Model" field, enter the exact model of the muscle car you are interested in.
4. **Provide the year of the muscle car.** In the "Muscle Car Year" field, enter the year of manufacture of the muscle car.
5. **Explain what you are looking for in an offer.** In the "Muscle Car Description" field, describe what you are hoping to get in return, e.g., a test drive, price quote, etc.
6. **Choose the type of offer you are looking for.** In the "Offer Type" field, select the type of offer you are interested in (e.g., test drive, price quote, etc.).
7. **Provide your contact information.** In the "Contact Email" and "Contact Phone" fields, enter your email and phone number so we can get in touch with you.
8. **Add any additional message.** In the "Offer Message" field, add any additional information you'd like to share with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Muscle Car Make (<type> text)**: Enter the make of the muscle car you are interested in.
* **Muscle Car Model (<type> text)**: Enter the exact model of the muscle car you are interested in.
* **Muscle Car Year (<type> number)**: Enter the year of manufacture of the muscle car.
* **Muscle Car Description (<type> text)**: Describe what you are looking for in an offer.
* **Offer Type (<type> select_one)**: Select the type of offer you are interested in.
* **Contact Email (<type> email)**: Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Contact Phone (<type> text)**: Enter your phone number so we can get in touch with you.
* **Offer Message (<type> text)**: Add any additional information you'd like to share with us.
