# Initial Client Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Initial Client Interview Form is used by our team to gather essential information from new clients before providing services. This form will guide you in collecting the necessary details to facilitate a successful relationship with our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. **Please fill out the form below with the required information.**
2. Ensure that you answer all the questions truthfully and thoroughly.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information** (client_info): Enter the client's name and any other relevant details.
* **Services** (services): Select all the services you are interested in, by clicking on the options provided. You can select multiple services.
* **Contact Information** (contact_info): Provide your contact details, such as email address and phone number.
* **Notes** (notes): Share any additional information that you think is relevant to our services, such as your goals and expectations.
* **Phone Number** (phone): Enter your preferred phone number for communication. This will help us stay in touch and provide the most efficient service.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly to ensure we can tailor our services to your needs.
* If you have any doubts or questions, feel free to contact us for clarification.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* If you need to make changes to your submission, please contact us, and we will assist you in updating your information.
