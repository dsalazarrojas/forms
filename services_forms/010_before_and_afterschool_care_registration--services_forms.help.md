# Before And Afterschool Care Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Before And Afterschool Care Registration form is used to register a student for before and after school care services. It is a necessary step to ensure the student can receive the required care. Please fill out this form with accurate and up-to-date information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the Student Information section with the student's details.
2.  Enter the Parent/Guardian Information section with your details and relationship to the student.
3.  Provide emergency contact information for the student.
4.  Enter the Physician Information section with the student's physician's details.
5.  List the student's allergies, medical conditions, and current medications in the Health History section.
6.  In the Program Enrollment section, select the requested before and after school care services.
7.  Finally, select the desired program start date and number of days per week.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Student First Name**: Enter the student's first name.
*   **Student Last Name**: Enter the student's last name.
*   **Date of Birth**: Enter the student's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Current Grade Level**: Select the student's current grade level.
*   **School Name**: Enter the name of the student's school.
*   **Parent/Guardian Full Name**: Enter your full name.
*   **Relationship to Student**: Select how you are related to the student (Parent, Guardian, Guardian, or Other).
*   **Home Address**: Enter your home address.
*   **Home Phone**: Enter your home phone number.
*   **Work Phone**: Enter your work phone number (optional).
*   **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
*   **Primary Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the primary emergency contact's name.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the primary emergency contact's phone number.
*   **Secondary Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the secondary emergency contact's name (optional).
*   **Secondary Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the secondary emergency contact's phone number (optional).
*   **Physician Name**: Enter the student's physician's name.
*   **Physician Phone**: Enter the student's physician's phone number.
*   **Allergies**: List any allergies the student has (food, medication, environmental).
*   **Medical Conditions or Chronic Illnesses**: List any medical conditions or illnesses the student has (asthma, diabetes, etc.).
*   **Current Medications**: List any medications the student is currently taking (name, dosage, frequency).
*   **Before School Care Requested**: Select if before school care is requested.
*   **After School Care Requested**: Select if after school care is requested.
*   **Program Start Date**: Enter the desired program start date.
*   **Number of Days Per Week**: Select the number of days per week the student will be enrolled in the program.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that all information is accurate and up-to-date.
*   If the student has any medical conditions or allergies, please provide detailed information.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
