# oxygeneoconsult - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "oxygeneoconsult" form is designed to gather information from users for a skincare consultation and related services. This form is typically used for users to schedule an appointment, provide contact details, and express their interest in different skincare services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all required information ready.
2. Select the desired services you are interested in (Yes/No) for each option.
3. Fill in your contact information (name, email, and appointment details).
4. Review your selections and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main Information** (`maininformation`, text, not required): This field is optional and allows you to provide any additional information about your visit or consultation.
* **Contact Information** (`contactinformation`, text, not required): This field is optional and allows you to provide your name and any other relevant contact details.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointmenttime`, date, not required): Select a specific date and time for your appointment, if preferred.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointmentdate`, date, not required): Select a preferred date for your appointment.
* **Services** (`services`, select multiple, not required): Choose any of the available skincare services you are interested in from the drop-down options.
* **Skincare Consultation** (`skincareconsultation`, select one, not required): Select if you want to proceed with a skincare consultation.
* **Facial Treatment** (`facialtreatment`, select one, not required): Select if you want to proceed with a facial treatment.
* **Chemical Peel** (`chemicalpeel`, select one, not required): Select if you want to proceed with a chemical peel.
* **Microdermabrasion** (`microdermabrasion`, select one, not required): Select if you want to proceed with microdermabrasion.

## Tips
- Be sure to review all fields carefully before submitting the form.
- If you have any special requests or requirements, you can include them in the "Main Information" field.
- You can select multiple services for the "Services" field.
- You can choose only one option for each of the "Skincare Consultation", "Facial Treatment", "Chemical Peel", and "Microdermabrasion" fields.
