# RBT Fidelity Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The RBT Fidelity Checklist is a tool to evaluate the quality and completeness of the RBT service provided. It helps ensure that the service meets the required standards and criteria.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter the **Service Name** of the service provided.
*   Choose the **RBT Name** associated with the service.
*   Enter the **Date of Service** when the service was provided.
*   Select the **Time of Service** when the service was provided.
*   Enter the **Client Name** if it's not already available.
*   Choose the **RBT Fidelity** level (Yes/No) to indicate whether the service met the required standards.
*   Enter any additional **Comments** about the service.
*   Enter a **Score** out of 10 to evaluate the quality of the service.
*   Enter **Additional Comments** about the service (if any).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Service Name** (`service_name`): Enter the name of the service provided.
*   **RBT Name** (`rbt_name`): Select the name of the RBT associated with the service.
*   **Date of Service** (`date_of_service`): Enter the date when the service was provided.
*   **Time of Service** (`time_of_service`): Select the time when the service was provided.
*   **Client Name** (`client_name`): Enter the name of the client if it's not already available.
*   **RBT Fidelity** (`rbt_fidelity`): Choose 'Yes' if the service met the required standards, 'No' otherwise.
*   **Comments** (`comments`): Enter any additional comments about the service.
*   **Score** (`rbt_fidelity_score`): Enter a score out of 10 to evaluate the quality of the service.
*   **Additional Comments** (`rbt_fidelity_comments`): Enter any further comments about the service.

# Tips

*   Be honest and objective when evaluating the fidelity of the service.
*   Use the score to rate the service based on its quality and completeness.
*   Keep the comments clear and concise.
*   The form is designed to be completed after the service has been provided, not during the service delivery process.
*   If you're unsure about any field, contact the relevant person for clarification.
*   This form is meant to be a quality control check on the service provided.
