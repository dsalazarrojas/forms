# CREATE Contact Form With Image Upload - Help Guide
## Purpose
This contact form is designed to gather information from users to efficiently address their inquiries and provide personalized support. By filling out this form, you will be able to provide the necessary details about your inquiry, and our team will be able to respond accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the required fields: Your Name, Email Address, Subject of Inquiry, Type of Inquiry, and Consent to Being Contacted.
2. If applicable, fill out optional fields: Phone Number, Company or Organization Name, Preferred Contact Method, Priority Level, and Reference Number.
3. Upload relevant images or documents that support your inquiry, if necessary.
4. Scroll down and review your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: Please enter your full name.
* **Your Email Address**: Enter your email address where we will reply to your inquiry.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number is optional, but it can help us contact you more efficiently.
* **Company or Organization Name**: If you represent a company or organization, please enter it here.
* **Subject of Inquiry**: Briefly describe the topic of your message.
* **Type of Inquiry**: Choose the category that best suits your inquiry.
* **Consent to Being Contacted**: We will not contact you unless you give us your consent.
* **Primary Image or Photo**: Upload a relevant image or photo that supports your inquiry.
* **Secondary Image or Document**: If necessary, upload additional images or documents that support your inquiry.
* **Additional Comments or Notes**: If you have any additional information that you think is relevant, please enter it here.

## Tips

* Please ensure that your email address is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have an existing reference number or ticket ID, please enter it.
* Be specific and clear in your message and image uploads.
* Do not hesitate to contact us if you need any clarification or assistance.
