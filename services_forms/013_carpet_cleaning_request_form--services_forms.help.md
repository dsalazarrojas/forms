# Carpet Cleaning Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your carpet cleaning request, including your contact details, service location, and specific requirements for the cleaning service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*) symbol.
2. Select all options that apply for each field.
3. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Carpet Cleaning Service Request** (`request_header`): A brief description of your carpet cleaning request.
* **Request Number** (`request_number`): A reference number for your booking.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`): The date of your booking.
* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`): Your contact details, including your name and address.
* **Full Name** (`customer_name`): Your legal name.
* **Email Address** (`customer_email`): A valid email address for appointment confirmation.
* **Phone Number** (`customer_phone`): A valid phone number for contact.
* **Service Address** (`service_address`): The address where the cleaning service will be provided.
* **City** (`service_city`): The city where the service will be provided.
* **ZIP Code** (`service_zip`): The postal code of your service address.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`): The type of property where the cleaning service will be provided.
* **Areas Requiring Cleaning** (`cleaning_areas`): Select all areas that require cleaning.
* **Carpet Information** (`carpet_info`): Additional details about your carpets.
* **Approximate Square Footage** (`total_square_footage`): The total area to be cleaned.
* **Carpet Type** (`carpet_type`): The type of carpet to be cleaned.
* **Approximate Age of Carpet** (`carpet_age`): The age of your carpet.
* **Reason for Cleaning** (`cleaning_reason`): The primary motivation for the cleaning.
* **Types of Stains Present** (`stain_types`): Select all types of stains present.
* **Do You Have Pets** (`pets_in_home`): Whether you have pets in your home.
* **Are There Children in the Home** (`children_in_home`): Whether there are children in your home.
* **Do You Prefer Eco-Friendly Products** (`eco_friendly`): Your preference for eco-friendly cleaning products.
* **Type of Service Needed** (`service_type`): The type of cleaning service required.
* **Additional Services** (`additional_services`): Select any additional services required.
* **Scheduling Preferences** (`scheduling`): Your scheduling preferences.
* **Preferred Service Date** (`preferred_date`): Your preferred service date.
* **Preferred Time of Day** (`preferred_time`): Your preferred time of day for the service.
* **Is Your Schedule Flexible** (`flexible_schedule`): Your schedule flexibility.
* **Access Instructions** (`access_instructions`): Instructions on how to access your property.
* **Parking Information** (`parking_info`): Information on where to park.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Your preferred payment method.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_heard`): The source of your referral.
* **Special Requests or Concerns** (`special_requests`): Any special requests or concerns you may have.

Note: The required fields are marked with an asterisk (*) symbol. Please make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
