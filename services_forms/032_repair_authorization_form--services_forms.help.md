# Repair Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Repair Authorization Form is a document used to collect information from customers who need to authorize repairs on their equipment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information as the authorized representative for the customer.
2. Provide the equipment ID number for the item that needs repair.
3. Describe the model of the equipment that needs repair.
4. Provide a detailed description of the issue you are experiencing with the equipment.
5. Select the method you would like to use to communicate with the repair center (phone call, on-site repair, email, mail, or visit in-person).
6. Choose a delivery date for the repaired equipment, if applicable.
7. Select the technician you would like to be assigned to the repair, or select "Other" if you would like to be assigned a different technician.
8. Indicate the status of the repair (open, in progress, or complete).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information**: Enter your name and contact information as the authorized representative for the customer.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the phone number or other contact information of the authorized representative.
* **Equipment ID**: Enter the equipment ID number for the item that needs repair.
* **Equipment Model**: Describe the model of the equipment that needs repair.
* **Issue Description**: Provide a detailed description of the issue you are experiencing with the equipment.
* **Repair Method**: Select the method you would like to use to communicate with the repair center (phone call, on-site repair, email, mail, or visit in-person).
* **Estimated Delivery Date**: Provide a date for when you would like to receive the repaired equipment, if applicable.
* **Assigned Technician**: Select the technician you would like to be assigned to the repair, or select "Other" if you would like to be assigned a different technician.
* **Repair Status**: Indicate the status of the repair (open, in progress, or complete).

## Tips
* Be accurate and complete in your descriptions and selections to ensure the repair center can efficiently process your request.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact the repair center for assistance.
* Please note that the repair center will contact you via the communication method you select.
