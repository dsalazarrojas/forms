# Maltese English Reservation & Booking - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for Maltese English reservations and bookings. It's a crucial tool for gathering necessary details for successful events, parties, or meetings. The form helps in streamlining the process of booking and confirming events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by selecting the type of event you are booking for (Hotel, Party, or Event) from the options provided in the "Event Type" field.
2. Enter the date of the event in the "Event Date" field.
3. Choose a time for the event in the "Event Time" field.
4. Enter the guest's name in the "Guest Name" field.
5. Provide the guest's email address in the "Guest Email" field.
6. Enter the guest's phone number in the "Guest Phone" field.
7. Add any additional notes about the guest in the "Notes" field.
8. Confirm your reservation by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Confirm" field.
9. If you are booking on behalf of someone else, select "User" in the "Confirm User" field and enter the other person's name in the "Other User" field.
10. Enter the booking date and time in the "Booking Date" and "Booking Time" fields respectively.
11. Specify the number of guests attending in the "Guest Count" field.
12. Enter the location of the event in the "Event Location" field.
13. Add any additional comments about the event in the "Guest Comment" field.
14. Enter a unique reservation ID in the "Reservation ID" field.
15. Select the user ID (if applicable) and confirm the booking by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Confirm Admin" field.
16. Finally, select the booking status (Approved, Rejected, or Pending) in the "Booking Status" field.
