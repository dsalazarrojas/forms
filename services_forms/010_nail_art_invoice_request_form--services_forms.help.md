# Nail Art Invoice Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from customers for creating a nail art invoice. Please fill it out carefully and thoroughly to ensure accurate and efficient processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Select the design choice that best represents your desired nail art style.
2. Choose the style that matches your design choice.
3. Enter your contact details, including name, email, and phone number.
4. Enter any additional notes you'd like to share with our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. customer_design_choice (Select Multiple)
* **Customer Design Choice** (`customer_design_choice`, Select Multiple, Required/Optional)
	+ Please select the design(s) you are interested in for your nail art.
### 2. customer_style_choice (Select One)
* **Customer Style Choice** (`customer_style_choice`, Select One, Required/Optional)
	+ Choose a style that matches your design choice.
### 3. customer_contact_details (Note)
* **Customer Contact Details** (`customer_contact_details`, Note, Required/Optional)
	+ Please provide any additional contact information you'd like our team to have on file.
### 4. customer_contact_name (Text)
* **Customer Contact Name** (`customer_contact_name`, Text, Required)
	+ Enter your name for our records.
### 5. customer_email (Email)
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, Email, Required)
	+ Enter your email address for easy communication.
### 6. customer_phone (Text)
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, Text, Required)
	+ Enter your phone number for our team to contact you.
### 7. customer_address (Text)
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, Text, Required)
	+ Enter your address for shipping and communication purposes.
### 8. customer_notes (Text)
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, Text, Required/Optional)
	+ Provide any additional notes or comments about your order.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form carefully and thoroughly to ensure accurate and efficient processing.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to our team for assistance.
