# Pest Control Service Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a request for pest control services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email Address in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your Phone Number for easy contact.
3. Select the type of service you require (Yes/No).
4. Choose whether you need interior or exterior services (Yes/No).
5. Provide your address.
6. Choose a date for the service.
7. Select a start time for the service.
8. Choose an end time for the service.
9. Specify the type of pest (Yes/No).
10. Add any additional information or instructions in the Notes field.
11. Indicate whether you want a specific staff member assigned to you (Yes/No).
12. If assigned a staff member, enter their name in the Label field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted for further communication.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Service Type**: Select "Yes" if you need pest control services, and "No" otherwise.
* **Interior or Exterior**: Choose "Yes" if the services are needed for interior areas, and "No" for exterior areas.
* **Address**: Enter your address for accurate service delivery.
* **Service Date**: Choose a date for the service to be conducted.
* **Start Time**: Select a start time for the service.
* **End Time**: Choose an end time for the service.
* **Pest Type**: Select "Yes" if you can identify the type of pest, and "No" otherwise.
* **Notes**: Add any additional information or instructions for the service request.
* **Assigned To**: Select "Yes" if you want a specific staff member assigned to you, and "No" otherwise.
* **Label**: If a staff member is assigned, enter their name in this field.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional information or instructions for the staff member assigned.
