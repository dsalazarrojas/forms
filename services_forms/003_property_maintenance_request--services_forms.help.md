# Property Maintenance Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Maintenance Request form is designed for property owners and managers to submit requests for property maintenance, repairs, or upgrades. This form helps streamline the process of reporting necessary work and ensures that the maintenance team is informed and equipped to complete the tasks on time.

## How To Complete This Form
To submit a Property Maintenance Request, follow these steps:

1.  Select the category of the maintenance request (Maintenance, Repairs, or Upgrades).
2.  Enter a brief description of the work required.
3.  Choose a priority level for the request (High, Medium, or Low).
4.  If necessary, assign the task to a specific person or team member.
5.  Enter the due date for the task.
6.  Provide the property address and contact information for the property owner or manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Category** (Maintenance, Repairs, Upgrades): Select the type of work required from the options provided. This will help the maintenance team understand the nature of the request.
*   **Description** (BRIEFLY describe the work required): Enter a concise description of the work needed to complete the task. This will help the maintenance team understand what needs to be done.
*   **Priority** (High, Medium, Low): Choose a priority level for the request based on its urgency and importance. This will help the maintenance team prioritize tasks and allocate resources accordingly.
*   **Assigned To** (Yes, No): If you want to assign the task to a specific person or team member, select 'Yes' and choose from the available options.
*   **Assigned Person** (Yes, No): If you want to assign the task to a specific person, select 'Yes' and choose from the available options.
*   **Property Address**: Enter the address of the property where the maintenance request is for.
*   **Property Contact** (Name, Phone, Email): Provide the contact information for the property owner or manager, so the maintenance team can reach out for clarification or updates.
