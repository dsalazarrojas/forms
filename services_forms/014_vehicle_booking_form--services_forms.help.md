# Vehicle Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Booking Form is designed to collect information from customers for vehicle bookings, allowing your team to efficiently manage bookings and provide excellent customer service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete the form, start by selecting the vehicle you wish to book using the 'Select Vehicle' field.
2. Next, choose the start and end dates for your booking using the 'Start Date' and 'End Date' fields, respectively.
3. Enter the pick-up location in the 'Pick Up Location' field and the drop-off location in the 'Drop Off Location' field.
4. Select the type of vehicle you wish to book in the 'Vehicle Type' field.
5. Provide your customer name, email, and phone number in the 'Customer Name', 'Customer Email', and 'Customer Phone' fields, respectively.
6. Select the booking status as either 'Active' or 'Inactive' in the 'Booking Status' field.
7. If necessary, add any notes for your booking in the 'Booking Notes' field.
8. Finally, review and submit the form to complete your booking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Vehicle** (`vehicle_selection`, select_one, required): Choose the vehicle you wish to book from the available options.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you wish to begin your booking.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you wish to end your booking.
* **Pick Up Location** (`pick_up_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the vehicle will be picked up.
* **Drop Off Location** (`drop_off_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the vehicle will be dropped off.
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of vehicle you wish to book.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name as the customer.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Booking Status** (`booking_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of your booking as 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
* **Booking Notes** (`booking_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes for your booking.
* **Form Submission Count** (`form_submission_count`, number, optional): This field is for tracking purposes and should not be edited.
* **Form Submission Timestamp** (`form_submission_timestamp`, time, optional): This field is for tracking purposes and should not be edited.
* **Vehicle Booking Form Submission Data** (`vehicle_booking_form_submission_data`, text, optional): This field is for internal tracking purposes and should not be edited.

**Tips**
* Make sure to review all fields carefully before submitting the form.
* Ensure that all fields are filled correctly to avoid errors.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact our support team for assistance.
