# Automotive Consultant Estimate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from a client for an estimate of a service or maintenance job on their vehicle.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name, email address, and phone number in the respective fields.
2. Provide the vehicle's year, make, model, current mileage, and Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) (if applicable).
3. Describe the issue or problem with the vehicle.
4. Select the type of service required (e.g., repair, maintenance, diagnostic, inspection, restoration, upgrade, customization).
5. List any parts or components affected by the issue.
6. Enter the estimated labor hours and labor rate.
7. Enter the estimated parts cost.
8. If applicable, enter any additional costs or fees.
9. Enter the total estimated cost.
10. Select the estimated timeline for the work to be completed.
11. If applicable, enter any warranty information.
12. Enter the date of the estimate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (2): Enter the client's name.
* **Email Address** (3): Enter the client's email address.
* **Phone Number** (4): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Address** (5): If desired, enter the client's address.
* **Vehicle Year** (7): Enter the year of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make** (8): Enter the make of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model** (9): Enter the model of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle VIN** (10): If applicable, enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) of the vehicle.
* **Current Mileage** (11): Enter the current mileage of the vehicle.
* **Issue or Problem Description** (13): Describe the issue or problem with the vehicle.
* **Type of Service** (14): Select the type of service required (e.g., repair, maintenance, diagnostic, inspection, restoration, upgrade, customization).
* **Parts or Components Affected** (15): List any parts or components affected by the issue.
* **Estimated Labor Hours** (17): Enter the estimated labor hours required.
* **Labor Rate Per Hour** (18): Enter the labor rate per hour.
* **Estimated Parts Cost** (19): Enter the estimated parts cost.
* **Additional Costs or Fees** (20): If applicable, enter any additional costs or fees.
* **Total Estimate** (21): Enter the total estimated cost.
* **Estimated Work Timeline** (23): Select the estimated timeline for the work to be completed.
* **Warranty on Work** (24): If applicable, enter any warranty information.
* **Estimate Date** (25): Enter the date of the estimate.
