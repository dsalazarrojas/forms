# service_invoice - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a service invoice for a company and client, capturing key details for financial or administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the company's name in the "Company Name" field.
2. Enter the company's address in the "Company Address" field.
3. Enter the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
4. Enter the client's address in the "Client Address" field.
5. Enter the email of the company or client in the "Email" field.
6. Enter the phone number of the company or client in the "Phone" field.
7. Enter the date of service in the "Date" field.
8. Enter the invoice number in the "Invoice Number" field.
9. Enter a description of the service provided in the "Description" field.
10. Enter the total fees for the service in the "Fees" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name (text, optional)**: Enter the name of the company providing the service.
* **Company Address (text, optional)**: Enter the address of the company providing the service.
* **Email (email, optional)**: Enter the email address of the company or client.
* **Phone (text, optional)**: Enter the phone number of the company or client.
* **Client Name (text, optional)**: Enter the name of the client receiving the service.
* **Client Address (text, optional)**: Enter the address of the client receiving the service.
* **Date (date, optional)**: Enter the date of service.
* **Invoice Number (number, optional)**: Enter the invoice number for the service provided.
* **Description (text, optional)**: Enter a description of the service provided.
* **Fees (number, optional)**: Enter the total fees for the service provided.
