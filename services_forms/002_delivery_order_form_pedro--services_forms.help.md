## delivery_order_form_pedro - Help Guide

## Purpose
The **delivery_order_form_pedro** is a form designed to collect customer contact information and delivery details for orders placed through our services. This form helps us gather essential data to ensure efficient delivery of your orders and provide excellent customer service. Please note that this form is used to update and verify customer information, so accuracy is crucial.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields (marked as "required") with accurate and up-to-date information.
* Leave blank fields that are not applicable or not required for your order.
* Ensure that you fill out all the fields that are specific to your order and delivery.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Contact Information (contact_information, text, optional)**: This field is for collecting the customer's contact details. Please fill in the customer's name, address, and other relevant contact information if available.
* **Delivery Instructions (delivery_instructions, note, optional)**: Here, describe any specific delivery instructions you have, such as delivery location or any special requests.
* **Delivery Address (delivery_address, text, optional)**: Provide the delivery address for the order. Include any details like apartment or suite numbers, if applicable.
* **Delivery Date (delivery_date, date, optional)**: Enter the date you would like the order to be delivered on. Please use the date format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Delivery Time (delivery_time, time, optional)**: Select the time you would like the order to be delivered. Please choose from the available time slots.
* **Customer Name (contact_name, text, optional)**: Fill in the customer's name as it appears on their identification or as they would like it to be displayed on the order.
* **Customer Email (contact_email, email, optional)**: Enter the customer's email address for communication purposes.
* **Customer Phone (contact_phone, text, optional)**: Enter the customer's phone number for easy contact and confirmation.

## Tips

* Be accurate and thorough when filling out this form to ensure smooth delivery and efficient customer service.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us and we will be happy to help.
* Please fill out this form for each order you would like to update or verify.
