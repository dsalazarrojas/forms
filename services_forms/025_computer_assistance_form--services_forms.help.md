# Computer Assistance Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to report computer-related issues and request assistance from our technical team. By filling out this form, you will be able to provide detailed information about your issue, which will help us to resolve it efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your ticket ID to identify your support request.
2. Provide the submission date of your request.
3. Enter your name and email address to contact you about your issue.
4. Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
5. Select the department related to your issue.
6. Choose the category of your issue.
7. Indicate the priority of your issue.
8. Select the affected device.
9. Enter the device ID or asset tag (if applicable).
10. Select the operating system of the affected device.
11. If applicable, enter the name of the software involved.
12. Describe the issue in detail.
13. If applicable, enter any error messages displayed.
14. Enter the date when you first noticed the problem.
15. Indicate the frequency of the issue.
16. Describe any steps you have taken to try and fix the issue.
17. Indicate the impact of the issue on your work.
18. Choose whether remote access is allowed.
19. Select your preferred contact method.
20. Enter your availability for support.
21. Add any additional information that may be helpful to us.
22. Indicate the current status of your ticket.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Ticket ID** (`ticket_id`, `text`, required): Unique support ticket identifier.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): Date of support request.
* **Requester Name** (`requester_name`, `text`, required): Your full name.
* **Requester Email** (`requester_email`, `email`, required): Your email address.
* **Requester Phone** (`requester_phone`, `text`, required): Your contact phone number.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required): Select your department.
* **Issue Category** (`issue_category`, `select_one`, required): Select the category of your issue.
* **Issue Priority** (`issue_priority`, `select_one`, required): Indicate the priority of your issue.
* **Affected Device** (`affected_device`, `select_one`, required): Select the affected device.
* **Device ID or Asset Tag** (`device_id`, `text`, optional): Device identification number.
* **Operating System** (`operating_system`, `select_one`, optional): Select your operating system.
* **Software Involved** (`software_involved`, `text`, optional): Name of software with issues.
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, `text`, required): Describe the problem in detail.
* **Error Message** (`error_message`, `text`, optional): Copy any error messages displayed.
* **When Did Issue Start** (`when_did_issue_start`, `text`, required): Date when you first noticed the problem.
* **Frequency of Issue** (`frequency_of_issue`, `select_one`, required): Indicate how often this occurs.
* **Steps Already Taken** (`steps_taken`, `text`, optional): Describe any steps taken to try and fix the issue.
* **Impact on Work** (`impact_on_work`, `select_one`, required): Indicate the impact of the issue on your work.
* **Remote Access Allowed** (`remote_access_allowed`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether remote access is allowed.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred contact method.
* **Availability** (`availability`, `text`, required): When are you available for support.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, `text`, optional): Any other relevant details.
* **Ticket Status** (`ticket_status`, `select_one`, required): Current ticket status.

Please note that all fields marked as `required` are mandatory and must be filled out accurately to ensure efficient support.
