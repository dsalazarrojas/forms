# Skilled Trade Service Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skilled Trade Service Request form is designed to collect information about a service request from a customer or contact person. It helps our skilled trade team to understand the nature of the request and provide timely and effective service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the Service Type by choosing from the available options (Electrician, Plumber, HVAC).
2. Enter the Contact First Name and Contact Last Name of the person requesting the service.
3. Enter the Contact Email address of the person requesting the service.
4. Enter the Contact Phone number of the person requesting the service.
5. Provide the Service Location where the service is needed.
6. Enter the Service Date when the service is scheduled to take place.
7. Enter the Service Time when the service is scheduled to take place.
8. Add a brief Service Description of the requested service.
9. If necessary, attach a file related to the service request.
10. Select the Scheduling Date when the service is scheduled to take place.
11. Select the Scheduling Time when the service is scheduled to take place.
12. If applicable, select the Assigned Tools and Trades that will be used for the service.
13. Choose how you want to submit the form: Submit, Save for Later, or Cancel.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Type** (`service_type`): Select from three available services: Electrician, Plumber, or HVAC.
* **Contact First Name** (`contact_first_name`): Enter the first name of the person requesting the service.
* **Contact Last Name** (`contact_last_name`): Enter the last name of the person requesting the service.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter the email address of the person requesting the service.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter the phone number of the person requesting the service.
* **Service Location** (`service_location`): Provide the location where the service is needed.
* **Service Date** (`service_date`): Enter the date when the service is scheduled to take place.
* **Service Time** (`service_time`): Enter the time when the service is scheduled to take place.
* **Service Description** (`service_description`): Add a brief description of the requested service.
* **Attachment** (`attachment`): Attach a file related to the service request, if necessary.
* **Scheduling Date** (`scheduling_date`): Enter the date when the service is scheduled to take place.
* **Scheduling Time** (`scheduling_time`): Enter the time when the service is scheduled to take place.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): If applicable, select the tools that will be used for the service.
* **Assigned Trades** (`assigned_trades`): If applicable, select the trades that will be assigned to the service.
* **Submit** (`submit`): Choose how you want to submit the form: Submit, Save for Later, or Cancel.
* **Upload File** (`upload_file`): Attach a file related to the service request, if necessary.

## Tips
* Please fill out all required fields to ensure that your service request is processed accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to this guide for clarification.
