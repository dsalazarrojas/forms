# home_care_caregiver_availability_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "home_care_caregiver_availability_form" is a tool used to collect information from caregivers about their availability to provide home care services. This form helps home care agencies or organizations to onboard new caregivers, schedule care shifts, or to assess their capacity to work with clients.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Caregiver Info** in the "Caregiver Info" text field. This is a general description of yourself.
2. Indicate your **Availability** in the "Availability" text field. Describe when you are available to work.
3. Choose the **Services Offered** you are willing to provide from the options listed. Select all that apply.
4. Enter your **Address** in the "Address" text field.
5. Enter your **Email** in the "Email" text field. This is your contact email address.
6. Enter your **Phone** number in the "Phone" text field.
7. Enter your **Experience** level in the "Experience" field. This can be a number to indicate how many years of experience you have.
8. Select your **Availability Time** in the time field. Indicate when you are available to work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Caregiver Info** (1, text, required/optional):
	+ Enter a brief description of yourself.
* **Availability** (2, text, optional):
	+ Describe when you are available to work.
* **Services Offered** (3, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose from options: Bathing, Dressing, Feeding, Medication Management, Personal Care, Transportation.
* **Address** (4, text, optional):
	+ Enter your home or office address for contact purposes.
* **Email** (5, email, optional):
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (6, text, optional):
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Experience** (7, number, optional):
	+ Enter the number of years of experience you have as a caregiver.
* **Availability Time** (8, time, optional):
	+ Select a time of day when you are available to work.

## Tips
* Ensure you are honest about your availability and services offered.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* If you have any questions, feel free to contact the administering organization for assistance.
