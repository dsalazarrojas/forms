# weekly_lunch_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The weekly lunch order form is a tool for employees to place orders for lunch services. It is designed to be easy to use and understand, with clear labels and instructions to ensure accurate and efficient orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Add any special instructions or notes in the "Special Instructions" field.
5. Select your options for "Select One", "Select Multiple", and "Special Instructions 2" fields as needed.
6. Enter the date and time of your preferred order in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
7. Enter the price and quantity of your order in the "Price" and "Quantity" fields.
8. Repeat steps 4-7 for any additional orders or sections of the form.
9. Finally, click the "Submit" button to complete and submit your order.
