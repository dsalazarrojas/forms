# industrial_design_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect data for industrial design and production processes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have access to the necessary data and information for each field.
2. Fill out each field with the relevant information.
3. Review your answers for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Product Name** (text)
Enter the name of the product being designed or produced.

### 2. **Product Type** (select_one)
Select the type of product being designed or produced.

### 3. **Product Description** (text)
Enter a brief description of the product.

### 4. **Production Method** (select_multiple)
Select all methods used in the production process.

### 5. **Production Process** (text)
Enter a detailed description of the production process.

### 6. **Production Capacity** (number)
Enter the production capacity of the equipment or process.

### 7. **Production Frequency** (text)
Enter the production frequency of the equipment or process.

### 8. **Production Cycle Time** (time)
Enter the production cycle time for the equipment or process.

### 9. **Production Status** (select_one)
Select the current production status.

### 10. **Production Notes** (note)
Enter any additional notes about the production process.

### 11. **Product ID** (number)
Enter the product ID.

### 12. **Production Cost** (number)
Enter the production cost.

### 13. **Production Price** (number)
Enter the production price.

### 14. **Production Weight** (number)
Enter the production weight.

### 15. **Production Length** (number)
Enter the production length.

### 16. **Production Width** (number)
Enter the production width.

### 17. **Production Height** (number)
Enter the production height.

### 18. **Production Depth** (number)
Enter the production depth.

### 19. **Production Volume** (number)
Enter the production volume.

### 20. **Production Color** (select_one)
Select the production color.

### 21. **Production Size** (number)
Enter the production size.

### 22. **Production Weight Unit** (select_one)
Select the unit of weight used.

### 23. **Production Length Unit** (select_one)
Select the unit of length used.

### 24. **Production Width Unit** (select_one)
Select the unit of width used.

### 25. **Production Height Unit** (select_one)
Select the unit of height used.
