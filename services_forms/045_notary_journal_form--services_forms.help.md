# Notary Journal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Notary Journal Form is a tool designed to record the details of notarization for documents. This form is used to document the parties involved, the property signed, and other relevant information in a clear and concise manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the first party involved in the notarization in the `first_party` field.
2. Enter the name of the second party involved in the notarization in the `second_party` field.
3. Describe the property that was signed in the `property_signed` field.
4. Enter the date of the notarization in the `date_of_notarization` field.
5. Enter the location where the notarization took place in the `location_of_notarization` field.
6. Add any additional notes or comments in the `note` field.
7. If a signature was obtained, enter the date and time of the signature in the `signature_date` and `signature_time` fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Party**: Enter the name of the first party involved in the notarization. (Text, not required)
* **Second Party**: Enter the name of the second party involved in the notarization. (Text, not required)
* **Property Signed**: Describe the property that was signed. (Text, not required)
* **Date of Notarization**: Enter the date of the notarization. (Date, not required)
* **Location of Notarization**: Enter the location where the notarization took place. (Text, not required)
* **Note**: Add any additional notes or comments about the notarization. (Note, not required)
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date of the signature. (Date, not required)
* **Signature Time**: Enter the time of the signature. (Time, not required)
