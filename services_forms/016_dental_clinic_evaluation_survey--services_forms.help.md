# dental_clinic_evaluation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from patients about their recent visit to a dental clinic.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Select the options that best describe your experience with the clinic in the "Clinic Information" field.
3. Rate the service you received in the "How do you rate the service?" field.
4. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.
5. Enter your contact information so we can follow up with you.
6. Finally, click "Submit" to submit your feedback.
