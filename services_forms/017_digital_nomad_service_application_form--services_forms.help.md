# Digital Nomad Service Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Digital Nomad Service Application Form is designed for digital nomads who want to access services tailored to their remote work and travel lifestyle. This form helps us understand your requirements and provide personalized assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the first two fields.
2. Enter your **Email** address to allow us to contact you regarding your application.
3. Provide your **Phone** number for any follow-up communication.
4. Select the type of **Service Type** you require, such as Digital Nomad or Other.
5. Indicate how frequently you would like to access the service (**Service Frequency**).
6. Describe the **Service Description** you need, including any specific requirements or requests.
7. Specify the **Service Hours** you require, such as daily, weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly.
8. Review your application carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name is the name you go by, and it's required for our records.
* **Last Name**: Your last name is used for our reference, and it's also required for our records.
* **Email**: Your email address is used for communication and will be used to send updates about your application.
* **Phone**: Provide your phone number for any follow-up communication or confirmation.
* **Service Type**: Select the type of service you require, whether it's Digital Nomad or Other.
* **Service Frequency**: Choose how frequently you'd like to access the service, such as daily, weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly.
* **Service Description**: Describe the specific service you need, including any specific requirements or requests.
* **Service Hours**: Specify the number of hours you require for the service.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and completely to avoid any delays in processing your application.
* Double-check your email and phone number for accuracy to ensure effective communication.
