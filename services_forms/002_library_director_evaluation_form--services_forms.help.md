# library_director_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from library directors, patrons, and employees about the services provided by the library.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps listed above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **form_title** (Text, Optional)
    Enter a brief title for your evaluation in this field.
*   **category** (Text, Optional)
    Select the category or type of evaluation you are providing in this field.
*   **description** (Text, Optional)
    Enter a description of the service or issue being evaluated in this field.
*   **feedback_from_patrons** (Text, Optional)
    Provide feedback from patrons in this field.
*   **feedback_from_employees** (Text, Optional)
    Provide feedback from employees in this field.

## Tips
Remember to provide as much detail as possible in each field to ensure that the evaluation is thorough and informative.
