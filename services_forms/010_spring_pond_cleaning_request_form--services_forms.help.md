# spring_pond_cleaning_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The spring pond cleaning request form is a collection of information required to assess and address the cleaning needs of a customer's spring pond. This form is meant to be filled out by customers who have requested services from our team.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that your request is processed efficiently.
2. Enter the address of your pond in the "Pond Address" field.
3. Provide a clear and concise description of the problem in the "Description of Problem" field.
4. Select the correct size of your pond in the "Pond Size" field.
5. Indicate the type of spring you have (small or large) in the "Spring Type" field.
6. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Person" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Spring Pond Cleaning Request Form** (`spring_pond_cleaning_request_form_1`, text, optional): This field is intended to be filled out by customers who have already discussed the form with our team.
* **Pond Address** (`spring_pond_cleaning_request_form_2`, text, required): Enter the physical address of the pond that needs cleaning.
* **Description of Problem** (`spring_pond_cleaning_request_form_3`, text, optional): Describe the issue with your spring pond, such as debris, algae, or other concerns.
* **Pond Size** (`spring_pond_cleaning_request_form_4`, number, required): Select the size of your pond from the options provided.
* **Spring Type** (`spring_pond_cleaning_request_form_5`, select_one, required): Indicate whether your spring is small or large to help us assess the cleaning needs.
* **Contact Person** (`spring_pond_cleaning_request_form_6`, text, optional): Enter your name and contact information so we can get in touch with you regarding your request.

## Tips
- Review your form before submission to ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
- If you are unsure about any information, contact us for clarification.
- We will follow up with you regarding your request once we receive it.
