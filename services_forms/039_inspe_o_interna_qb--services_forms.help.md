Inspecao Interna QB - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Inspecao Interna QB form is used to gather information about the internal service type, installation date, status, internal inspection, and any additional notes related to internal inspections. This form is designed to collect data in a structured and organized manner, making it easier to analyze and manage internal services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Inspecao Interna QB form, follow these steps:

1. Select the service type from the options provided in the "Service Type" field.
2. Enter the installed date in the "Installed Date" field in the format of a standard date (MM/DD/YYYY).
3. Choose the installation status from the options provided in the "Installation Status" field.
4. Enter any internal inspection details in the "Internal Inspection" field.
5. Add any additional notes or comments about the internal inspection in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Type (Optional)** (`service_type`, Select, Optional): This field allows you to select the type of service being inspected.
* **Installed Date (Optional)** (`installed_date`, Date, Optional): This field is used to enter the date when the service was installed.
* **Installation Status (Optional)** (`installation_status`, Select Multiple, Optional): This field allows you to choose the status of the installation.
* **Internal Inspection (Optional)** (`internal_inspection`, Text, Optional): This field is used to enter any details about the internal inspection.
* **Notes (Optional)** (`notes`, Note, Optional): This field is for adding any additional comments or notes about the internal inspection.

## Tips
- Make sure to select the correct service type and installation status to ensure accurate data collection.
- Enter the installed date in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
- Use the internal inspection field to provide a detailed description of the internal inspection.
- The notes field is for adding any additional comments or observations that may be helpful in managing the internal service.
 
Please note that this form is an internal document and should be treated as such. Please ensure that all sensitive information is kept confidential and is only shared with authorized personnel. 

You can contact us at `form@gic.mx` for any questions or concerns regarding this form.
