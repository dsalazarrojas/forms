# Laundry Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Laundry Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experiences with our laundry services. This form helps us identify areas for improvement and ensures that we continue to meet or exceed our customers' expectations. By completing this form, you'll be helping us provide better services in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Customer Name
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your name as you would like to be addressed.

### 2. Order Number
* **Order Number** (`order_number`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of your recent laundry order, if applicable.

### 3. Date
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Select the date of your recent laundry order, if applicable.

### 4. Time
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Select the time of your recent laundry order, if applicable.

### 5. Service Quality
* **Service Quality** (`service_quality`, select_one, required/optional): Choose how you would rate our service:
	+ **Excellent**
	+ **Good**
	+ **Poor**

### 6. Service Quality Options
* **Excellent** (`service_quality_option_1`, text, required/optional): If you selected "Excellent", enter specific reasons why our service was excellent.
* **Good** (`service_quality_option_2`, text, required/optional): If you selected "Good", enter specific reasons why our service was good.
* **Poor** (`service_quality_option_3`, text, required/optional): If you selected "Poor", enter specific reasons why our service was poor.

### 7. Laundry Service
* **Laundry Service** (`laundry_service`, select_one, required/optional): Select the laundry service you received:
	+ **'Yes'**
	+ **'No'**

### 8. Laundry Service Options
* **Dry Cleaning** (`laundry_service_option_1`, text, required/optional): If you selected "Yes" for laundry service, enter "Dry Cleaning" as an example.
* **Laundry** (`laundry_service_option_2`, text, required/optional): If you selected "Yes" for laundry service, enter "Laundry" as an example.
* **Alterations** (`laundry_service_option_3`, text, required/optional): If you selected "Yes" for laundry service, enter "Alterations" as an example.

### 9. Order Type
* **Order Type** (`order_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of order you made:
	+ **'Yes'**
	+ **'No'**

### 10. Order Type Options
* **Pick-up** (`order_type_option_1`, text, required/optional): If you selected "Yes" for order type, enter "Pick-up" as an example.
* **Drop-off** (`order_type_option_2`, text, required/optional): If you selected "Yes" for order type, enter "Drop-off" as an example.
* **Both** (`order_type_option_3`, text, required/optional): If you selected "Yes" for order type, enter "Both" as an example.

### 11. Staff Helpfulness
* **Staff Helpfulness** (`staff_helpfulness`, select_one, required/optional): Choose how helpful our staff was:
	+ **'Yes'**
	+ **'No'**

### 12. Staff Helpfulness Options
* **Very Helpful** (`staff_helpfulness_option_1`, text, required/optional): If you selected "Yes" for staff helpfulness, enter specific reasons why our staff was very helpful.
* **Somewhat Helpful** (`staff_helpfulness_option_2`, text, required/optional): If you selected "Somewhat Helpful", enter specific reasons why our staff was somewhat helpful.
* **Not Helpful** (`staff_helpfulness_option_3`, text, required/optional): If you selected "Not Helpful", enter specific reasons why our staff was not helpful.

### 13. Price
* **Price** (`price`, number, required/optional): Enter the price you paid for your recent laundry order, if applicable.

### 14. Rating
* **Rating** (`rating`, select_one, required/optional): Choose your rating:
	+ **Good**
	+ **Fair**

### 15. Rating Options
* **5** (`rating_option_1`, text, required/optional): If you selected "Good", enter specific reasons why you gave us a 5-star rating.
* **4** (`rating_option_2`, text, required/optional): If you selected "Fair", enter specific reasons why you gave us a 4-star rating.
* **3** (`rating_option_3`, text, required/optional): If you selected "Fair", enter specific reasons why you gave us a 3-star rating.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when answering the service quality, staff helpfulness, and rating questions.
* If you have any further comments or suggestions, feel free to include them in the "Additional Comments" or "Feedback" fields (not shown in this guide).
