# Wedding Expense Details Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Wedding Expense Details Form is designed to collect information about wedding expenses. This form will help users track and categorize expenses related to wedding events, services, and vendors. By filling out this form, users will be able to easily access and review expense records, making it simpler to manage their finances related to the wedding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the **category** that best describes the expense (e.g., "Yes" for expenses related to the wedding venue or "No" for expenses related to the wedding favors).
2. Enter the **amount** of the expense in numerical format.
3. Choose the **vendor** that is providing the service or item related to the expense (or select "No" if the expense is not from a vendor).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category**: Choose the category that best describes the expense. This will help us understand the type of expense you are tracking. If you are unsure, choose "Yes" for expenses related to services or events, and "No" for expenses related to items or products.
* **Amount**: Enter the total amount of the expense in numerical format. For example, if you spent $100 for an item, enter 100.
* **Vendor**: Choose the vendor that provided the service or item related to the expense. If the expense is not from a vendor, select "No".
* **Wedding Expense Details Form**: This field is not a field to fill out, but rather the title of the form itself.

## Tips
* Be specific when selecting the category to ensure accurate expense tracking.
* Double-check the amount to ensure accuracy.
* If you are unsure about a vendor, try to recall or research the vendor's name.
* Review and update your expense records regularly to stay on top of your finances.
