# Music Track Mastering Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Music Track Mastering Request Form is a tool for artists and their representatives to provide essential details about their music tracks being mastered. This form ensures that accurate and efficient communication can be maintained with the mastering team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select "Yes" or "No" for "submit_audio_files" to indicate whether you will be submitting audio files.
2.  Choose your desired mastering preferences (e.g., 0 for no preference, 1 for some preference, 2 for high preference, etc.).
3.  Select "Yes" or "No" for "share_mastering_deadlines" to indicate whether you would like to share your mastering deadlines.
4.  Select "Yes" or "No" for "share_mastering_preferences" to indicate whether you would like to share your mastering preferences.
5.  Enter your name as the artist.
6.  Enter your email address as the artist.
7.  Enter your studio name.
8.  Enter your studio email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **submit_audio_files (Yes/No)**: This field indicates whether you will be submitting audio files for mastering. Please select "Yes" if you will be submitting audio files, and "No" otherwise.
- **mastering_preferences (0, 1, 2)**: This field represents your desired level of mastering preference. Please select "0" for no preference, "1" for some preference, or "2" for a high level of preference.
- **share_mastering_deadlines (Yes/No)**: This field is used to indicate whether you would like to share your mastering deadlines. Please select "Yes" if you would like to share deadlines, and "No" otherwise.
- **share_mastering_preferences (Yes/No)**: This field is used to indicate whether you would like to share your mastering preferences. Please select "Yes" if you would like to share preferences, and "No" otherwise.
- **artist_name**: This field requires you to enter your name as the artist.
- **artist_email**: This field requires you to enter your email address as the artist.
- **studio_name**: This field requires you to enter your studio name.
- **studio_email**: This field requires you to enter your studio email address.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate information in the "artist_name" and "studio_name" fields to ensure correct communication.
- Enter your actual email addresses in the "artist_email" and "studio_email" fields for efficient communication.
