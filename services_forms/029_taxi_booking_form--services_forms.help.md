# Taxi Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to book a taxi service, providing essential information for the booking process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first and last name in the "First and last name" field.
2. Select the date for the booking in the "Date" field.
3. Choose the time of the booking in the "Time" field.
4. Enter the pickup location in the "Pickup location" field.
5. Enter the dropoff location in the "Dropoff location" field.
6. Select the type of vehicle you prefer in the "Vehicle type" field.
7. Enter the vehicle number (if applicable).
8. Provide your contact information in the "Customer phone" and "Customer email" fields.
9. Add any special instructions or additional information in the "Special instructions" field (optional).
10. Specify if you have any additional info to add in the "Additional info" field (optional).
11. Confirm your booking in the "Booking confirmation" field.
12. Indicate if you require payment information in the "Payment info" field.
13. Select your preferred payment method in the "Payment method" field.
14. Enter the payment amount (if applicable).
15. Add any notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
16. Specify the booking status in the "Booking status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First and last name** (text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date** (date, required): Select the date for the booking.
* **Time** (time, required): Choose the time of the booking.
* **Pickup location** (text, required): Enter the address or location where you will be picked up.
* **Dropoff location** (text, required): Enter the address or location where you would like to be dropped off.
* **Vehicle type** (select_one, required): Choose the type of vehicle you prefer (e.g., sedan, taxi, etc.).
* **Vehicle number** (text, required): Enter the vehicle number if you have a specific vehicle number.
* **Customer phone** (text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Customer email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Special instructions** (text, optional): Add any special instructions or requests you may have.
* **Additional info** (note, optional): Add any additional information you'd like to share with the taxi service.
* **Booking confirmation** (select_multiple, required): Confirm that you are booking the taxi service.
* **Payment info** (select_multiple, required): Indicate if you require payment information.
* **Payment method** (select_one, required): Select the payment method you prefer.
* **Payment amount** (number, required): Enter the payment amount (if applicable).
* **Notes** (note, required): Add any notes or comments about your booking.
* **Booking status** (select_one, required): Specify the status of your booking (e.g., active, inactive, etc.).
