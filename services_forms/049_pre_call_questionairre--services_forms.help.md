# pre-call-questionairre - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect basic contact information from users before a call or meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and any other relevant information in the "user-input" field.
2. Provide your phone number in the "phone" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User-Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Enter any relevant information about yourself that you would like to share with us before our call or meeting.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you easily.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can follow up with you after our call or meeting.

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure smooth communication.
* If you have any questions, feel free to ask and we'll be happy to help!
