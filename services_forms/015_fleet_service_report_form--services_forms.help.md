# fleet_service_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Fleet Service Report Form" is used to collect information about vehicle services. This form helps maintenance teams or service providers gather details about a vehicle's condition, maintenance needs, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by entering the **Vehicle Details** field. This field is required and should contain a brief description of the vehicle being serviced.
2. Next, enter the **Vehicle Year** field, which is also required. This field should contain the year of manufacture of the vehicle.
3. Enter the **Mileage** field, which is required. This field should contain the current mileage of the vehicle.
4. In the **Maintenance Needs** field, select one or more of the options that apply to the vehicle's maintenance needs (e.g., Oil Change, Tire Change, Battery Check).
5. In the **Maintenance Items** field, select all the maintenance items that are required for the vehicle (e.g., Oil, Tire, Battery).
6. Enter the **Report Date**, which is required and should contain the date the form is being submitted.
7. Optionally, add notes about the vehicle's service history or other relevant information in the **Notes** field.
8. If the vehicle's maintenance team is not directly assigned, select "Not Assigned" in the **Submitted By** field. Otherwise, select "Assigned" to indicate that the maintenance team is handling the vehicle's maintenance.
9. Finally, optionally, enter the email and phone number of the person submitting the form. These fields are not required but can be useful for follow-up or communication purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Details**: A brief description of the vehicle being serviced. This field is required.
* **Vehicle Year**: The year of manufacture of the vehicle. This field is required.
* **Mileage**: The current mileage of the vehicle. This field is required.
* **Maintenance Needs**: Select one or more of the options that apply to the vehicle's maintenance needs (e.g., Oil Change, Tire Change, Battery Check). This field is required.
* **Maintenance Items**: Select all the maintenance items that are required for the vehicle (e.g., Oil, Tire, Battery). This field is required.
* **Report Date**: The date the form is being submitted. This field is required.
* **Notes**: Any additional notes or comments about the vehicle's service history or other relevant information.
* **Email**: The email address of the person submitting the form (optional).
* **Phone**: The phone number of the person submitting the form (optional).
* **Submitted By**: Indicate if the vehicle's maintenance team is directly assigned or not. This field is required.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the instructions above or contact the maintenance team for clarification.
* The form is designed to be completed by the person submitting the vehicle for service, not by the maintenance team.
