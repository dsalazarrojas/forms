# Utility Service Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers who are interested in applying for a utility service. The form is designed to gather essential details such as customer contact information, service requirements, and preferences. Please fill out this form carefully to ensure that your application is processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Read the form instructions carefully.
- Fill in your customer information, including your name and contact phone number.
- Provide your email address to enable us to contact you for further information.
- Select the type of service you are interested in.
- Fill in the service address and date you are requesting for.
- Choose the time of day you would like to receive the service.
- Enter a brief description of the service you need.
- Add any additional comments you would like to share with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Information
**Customer Name** (text): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
### Service Information
**Service Type** (select_one): Select 'Yes' if you are interested in our service, 'No' otherwise.
### Customer Contact
**Customer Email** (email): Enter your active email address so we can reach out to you with updates and information.
### Customer Contact
**Customer Phone** (text): Enter your active phone number so we can contact you for further information.
### Service Details
**Service Address** (text): Enter the address where you would like to receive the service.
### Service Schedule
**Service Date** (date): Enter the date you would like to receive the service.
### Service Schedule
**Service Time** (time): Choose the time of day you would like to receive the service.
### Service Description
**Service Description** (text): Enter a brief description of the service you require.
### Customer Comments
**Customer Comments** (text): Enter any additional comments or questions you have about the service.
