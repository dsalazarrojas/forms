# Pet Boarding Waiver And Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain a pet owner's consent and agreement to board their pet. It includes fields to collect relevant information about the pet, the boarding schedule, and the pet owner's contact details. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your pet's information**:
	* Enter the name of your pet (pet_dog_name)
	* Select the breed of your pet (pet_breed)
	* Enter your pet's weight (pet_weight)
	* Enter your pet's date of birth (pet_dob)
2. **Enter boarding schedule**:
	* Select the start date for boarding (pet_boarding_start_date)
	* Select the end date for boarding (pet_boarding_end_date)
	* Enter the boarding cost (pet_boarding_cost)
3. **Agree to terms and conditions**:
	* Agree to the terms of service (pet_boarding_agreed_terms)
	* Select the time format for terms agreement (pet_boarding_agreed_terms_time_format, 12-hour or 24-hour)
	* Select the AM/PM format for terms agreement (pet_boarding_agreed_terms_am_pm)
4. **Sign and date the form**:
	* Enter your signature (pet_boarding_signature)
	* Date the signature (pet_boarding_signature_date)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **pet_boarding_waiver_and_consent_form_label**: The form label. This is a text field where the pet owner can enter their own label for the form.
* **pet_owner_name_label**: The name of the pet owner. Enter the name of the person boarding the pet.
* **pet_owner_email_label**: The email of the pet owner. Enter the email address of the person boarding the pet.
* **pet_dog_name_label**: The name of the pet. Enter the name of the pet being boarded.
* **pet_breed_label**: The breed of the pet. Select the breed of the pet from the list.
* **pet_weight_label**: The weight of the pet. Enter the weight of the pet.
* **pet_dob_label**: The date of birth of the pet. Enter the date of birth of the pet.
* **pet_boarding_start_date_label**: The start date for boarding. Select the start date for boarding.
* **pet_boarding_end_date_label**: The end date for boarding. Select the end date for boarding.
* **pet_boarding_cost_label**: The boarding cost. Enter the cost of boarding the pet.
* **pet_boarding_agreed_terms_label**: Agreement to terms of service. Select "Terms of service", "Agreement and acknowledgement", or "Liability release" from the list.
* **pet_boarding_signature_label**: The signature of the pet owner. Enter your signature.
* **pet_boarding_signature_date_label**: The date of the signature. Enter the date of the signature.
* **pet_boarding_agreed_terms_date_label**: The date of the agreement to terms. Enter the date of the agreement to terms.
* **pet_boarding_agreed_terms_time_label**: The time of the agreement to terms. Select 12-hour or 24-hour format.
* **pet_boarding_agreed_terms_am_pm_label**: The AM/PM format for the agreement to terms. Select AM or PM.
* **pet_boarding_notes_label**: Additional notes about the pet boarding. Enter any additional notes.
* **pet_boarding_agreed_terms_2_label**: Agreement to terms 2. Select "Yes" or "No" from the list.
* **pet_boarding_waiver_agreed_terms_2_label**: Waiver agreement to terms 2. Select "Yes" or "No" from the list.

Note: The fields marked as required must be filled out for the form to be considered complete.
