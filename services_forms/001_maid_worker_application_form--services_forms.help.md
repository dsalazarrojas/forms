# Maid Worker Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Maid Worker Application Form is used to collect information from individuals interested in becoming a maid worker. It's essential to fill out the form correctly to ensure that your application is processed efficiently and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your contact information, including your name and email address.
2. Provide your phone number to facilitate communication.
3. Enter your address for logistical purposes.
4. Describe your experience as a maid worker.
5. Indicate your availability to work, including specific days and periods.
6. Select the type of work you're interested in (full-time, part-time, or temporary).
7. Choose your notice period for termination of employment.
8. Enter the number of notice period days, if applicable.
9. Specify any tools or equipment you're familiar with or prefer to use.
10. Add any additional notes or comments about yourself.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (id: 1): Enter your name and contact details.
* **Name** (id: 2): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (id: 3): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (id: 4): Enter your phone number to facilitate communication.
* **Address** (id: 5): Enter your residential or work address for logistical purposes.
* **Experience** (id: 6): Describe your experience as a maid worker, including relevant skills and qualifications.
* **Availability** (id: 7): Select when you are available to work. This will help us schedule appointments and assignments.
* **Work Type** (id: 8): Choose the type of work you're interested in (full-time, part-time, or temporary).
* **Availability Days** (id: 9): Select the specific days you are available to work.
* **Notice Period** (id: 10): Select your preferred notice period for termination of employment.
* **Notice Period Days** (id: 11): Enter the number of days you would like to give as a notice period.
* **Assigned Tool** (id: 12): If applicable, specify any tools or equipment you're familiar with or prefer to use.
* **Notes** (id: 13): Add any additional comments or information about yourself that you'd like to share.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form carefully and thoroughly to ensure that your application is processed efficiently.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, refer to this guide for clarification.
* Ensure that you've entered accurate and up-to-date information to avoid any issues during the application process.
