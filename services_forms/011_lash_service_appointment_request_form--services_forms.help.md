# Lash Service Appointment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lash Service Appointment Request Form is designed to collect essential information from clients requesting lash services. This form helps service providers prepare for the appointment and ensures a smooth and efficient experience for both parties.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **appointment_request** field with a brief description of your desired lash service (e.g., lash extension, lash lift, or lash refill).
2. Provide your **client_info**, which includes your name, email, and contact number in the corresponding fields.
3. Select the **appointment_time** and **appointment_date** according to your preferred schedule.
4. Choose your preferred **payment_method** and **payment_method_type** for the services.
5. Select the **services** and **available_services** you are interested in receiving during the appointment.
6. Enter the **service_price** for the selected services.
7. Add any **notes** you would like to share with the service provider.
8. Confirm your **service_options** by selecting "Yes" or "No" according to your preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **appointment_request**: A brief description of your desired lash service.
* **client_info**: Provide your name, email, and contact number for communication and follow-up.
* **appointment_time**: Select a preferred time for your appointment.
* **appointment_date**: Choose a preferred date for your appointment.
* **contact_number**: Your contact number to ensure smooth communication.
* **email**: Your email address for further communication and updates.
* **notes**: Any additional information or comments you'd like to share with the service provider.
* **service_details**: A brief description of the services you are interested in (not applicable as it's not a required field).
* **service_options**: Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm your preferences for services such as lash lift, lash refill, or lash extension.
* **services**: Select the services you are interested in receiving during the appointment.
* **available_services**: Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm your preferences for available services.
* **service_price**: Enter the price for the selected services.
* **payment_method**: Select a preferred payment method, such as cash, credit card, or bank transfer.
* **payment_method_type**: Select the type of payment method, such as online or in-person.
* **service_options**: Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm your preferences for services such as lash lift, lash refill, or lash extension.
