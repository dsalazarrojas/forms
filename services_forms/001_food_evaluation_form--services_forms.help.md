# Food Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your feedback on the food you have purchased or consumed from our service. Your responses will help us understand your experience and make improvements to our service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer the questions on each page of the form, providing your honest feedback on the food quality, taste, appearance, customer service, value for money, and overall satisfaction.
2. You can rate each question using the number scale provided.
3. Once you have completed all the pages, click the "Submit" button to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Food Evaluation Form Data
* **Food Evaluation Form Data** (`food_evaluation_form_data`, text, Optional): This is the main text field where you can provide any additional comments or feedback about your food experience. You can enter any information you think is relevant to your feedback.

* **Food Quality** (`food_quality`, number, Optional): How would you rate the quality of the food you received? You can rate it on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".

* **Food Taste** (`food_taste`, number, Optional): How would you rate the taste of the food you received? You can rate it on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".

* **Food Appearance** (`food_appearance`, number, Optional): How would you rate the appearance of the food? You can rate it on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".

* **Customer Service** (`customer_service`, number, Optional): How would you rate the customer service you received? You can rate it on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".

* **Value For Money** (`value_for_money`, number, Optional): How would you rate the value for money? You can rate it on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".

* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, Optional): How would you rate your overall satisfaction with the food and service? You can rate it on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".

* **Food Evaluation Form Completion** (`food_evaluation_form_completion`, note, Optional): This is a note field where you can provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with our service.

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide as much detail as possible in your feedback.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, please include them in the "Food Evaluation Form Data" field.
* Your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your time in filling out this form.
