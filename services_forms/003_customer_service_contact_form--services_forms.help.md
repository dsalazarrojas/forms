# Customer Service Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a convenient way for customers to contact the service team with their questions, issues, or comments.

## How To Complete This Form

### Before Submitting the Form

- Please take a few minutes to review the form and make sure you are comfortable with the information you are providing.
- Use a valid email address for the email field.
- If you have a phone number, please use a standard format (e.g., ###-#####).

### Submitting the Form

1. Fill in the form with your contact information and a brief description of your issue or question.
2. Make sure to fill in all the required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
3. Review your submission for completeness and accuracy.
4. Click the "Submit" button to send your contact information and issue or question to the service team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **contact_info** (`text`): This is the first page of the form, where you can enter your contact information, such as your name, address, etc.
* **customer_name** (`text`): Please enter your name so that we can address you personally.
* **email** (`email`): Enter a valid email address that we can use to reply to your query.
* **phone** (`text`): If you have a phone number, please enter it in the standard format (e.g., ###-#####).
* **description** (`text`): Describe your issue or question as briefly as possible, so we can understand the problem you are facing.

## Tips

- Please be concise and clear in your description, as this will help us to respond promptly and effectively.
- If you are unsure about anything, feel free to ask, and we will do our best to help.
- If you have any further questions or need clarification, you can always reach out to us directly.
- We appreciate your feedback and will review and address it as soon as possible.
