# Veterinarian Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from clients and their pets to schedule a veterinary appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Animal Information section with details about the animal you are scheduling an appointment for.
2. Provide information about the visit details in the Visit Details section.
3. Enter the pet's details in the Pet Details section.
4. In the Schedule Visit section, provide a brief description of when you would like to schedule the appointment.
5. Enter the animal's owner details in the Animal Owner Details section.
6. If you have any additional comments or requests, you can type them in the Additional Comments section.
7. Lastly, provide the veterinarian's information in the Veterinarian Info section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Animal Information** (`animal_info`, `text`, required: false): Please fill in the details about the animal you are scheduling an appointment for, such as its name, breed, and any relevant medical information.
* **Visit Details** (`visit_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter the type of visit you are scheduling, such as check-up, vaccination, or consultation.
* **Pet Details** (`pet_details`, `text`, required: false): Fill in the pet's age, weight, and any other relevant details that would be helpful for the veterinarian to know.
* **Schedule Visit** (`schedule_visit`, `text`, required: false): In this section, provide the date and time you are available to schedule the visit.
* **Animal Owner Details** (`animal_owner_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about who the animal's owner is.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false): If you have any questions, concerns, or special requests, type them in this section.
* **Veterinarian Info** (`veterinarian_info`, `text`, required: false): Please provide the veterinarian's name, phone number, or email address so we can contact them for your appointment.
