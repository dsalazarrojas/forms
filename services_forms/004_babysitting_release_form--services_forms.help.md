# Babysitting Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Babysitting Release Form is used to document and agree on details for babysitting services, including parent and child information, service duration, and payment terms.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all required fields to ensure accurate and complete information.
2. Provide accurate parent and child information in the relevant fields.
3. Select the correct service date and duration for the babysitting service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent/Guardian Full Name (required)**: Provide the full name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent Email Address (required)**: Enter the email address of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent Phone Number (required)**: Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian.
* **Babysitter Name (required)**: Provide the name of the babysitter.
* **Child Name(s) (required)**: List all children to be cared for.
* **Babysitting Date (required)**: Choose the date for the babysitting service.
* **Start Time (required)**: Select the start time of the service.
* **End Time (required)**: Select the end time of the service.
* **Parent Acknowledges the Babysitter is Not Liable for Accidents or Incidents Beyond Reasonable Care (required)**: Confirm that the parent understands the babysitter is not liable for accidents or incidents beyond reasonable care.
* **Parent Confirms Emergency Contact Information Has Been Provided (required)**: Confirm that the parent has provided emergency contact information.
* **Parent Confirms Child Care Instructions Have Been Clearly Communicated (required)**: Confirm that child care instructions have been clearly communicated to the babysitter.
* **Parent Agrees Children Will Follow Babysitter's Rules (required)**: Confirm that the parent agrees children will follow the babysitter's rules.
* **Any Additional Terms or Conditions (optional)**: Leave blank if there are no additional terms or conditions.
* **Agreed Compensation Amount (required)**: Enter the agreed compensation amount.
* **Payment Method (required)**: Select the payment method.
* **Cancellation Policy (optional)**: Describe the notice period and refund terms.
* **Date of Release (required)**: Select the date of the release form.
* **I Acknowledge That I Have Read and Agree to This Release Form (required)**: Confirm that the parent has read and agrees to the release form.

Note: Please fill out the form completely and accurately to avoid any issues during service.
