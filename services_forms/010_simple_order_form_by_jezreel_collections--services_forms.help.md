# Simple Order Form By Jezreel Collections - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to place orders with Jezreel Collections. Please fill out the form accurately to submit a valid order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all required fields filled out (marked with *).
2. For the "products" field, choose one or more options from the list.
3. For the "details" field, select only one option.
4. Fill out any additional comments or notes in the "note" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name** (Text, Required): Enter your name to help us identify you.
* **address** (Text, Required): Enter your address so we can deliver your order correctly.
* **products** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose one or more products to be ordered.
* **details** (Select One, Required): Provide additional details about your order.
* **note** (Text, Optional): Leave any additional comments or notes.
* **email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
- Double-check that you have selected the correct products and details for your order.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at the email or phone number provided.
