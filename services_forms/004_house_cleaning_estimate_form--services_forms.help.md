# House Cleaning Estimate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The House Cleaning Estimate Form is a tool used to gather information from clients for a house cleaning service estimate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter a brief description of the services needed.
* Estimate the total cost of the services.
* Specify whether certain areas of the house will be cleaned.
* Choose the start and end dates for the cleaning service.
* Choose the start and end times for the cleaning service.
* Specify whether any additional service areas will be cleaned.
* Select the payment method (if applicable).
* Enter the client's address where the service will be performed.
* Enter the client's name and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **House Cleaning Estimate** (`house_cleaning_estimate_form`, text, optional): A brief description of the services needed.
* **Estimated Cost** (`estimated_cost`, number, optional): Enter an estimated total cost for the cleaning service.
* **Cleaning Type** (`cleaning_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose which areas of the house will be cleaned (e.g. "Yes", "No").
* **Start Date** (`estimated_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date for the cleaning service.
* **End Date** (`estimated_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date for the cleaning service.
* **Start Time** (`estimated_start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time for the cleaning service.
* **End Time** (`estimated_end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time for the cleaning service.
* **Areas Served** (`service_areas`, select_one, optional): Choose which areas will be serviced (e.g. "Yes", "No").
* **Total Cost** (`estimated_total_cost`, number, optional): Re-enter the total cost (this field is a duplicate of the estimated cost field, use at your discretion).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the payment method (e.g. "Yes", "No").
* **Service Area Address** (`service_area_address`, text, optional): Enter the address where the service will be performed.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Enter the client's name.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, optional): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, optional): Enter the client's email address.
* **Client Message** (`client_message`, text, optional): Enter any additional information or comments for the client.
