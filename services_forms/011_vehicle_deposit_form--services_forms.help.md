# Vehicle Deposit Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Vehicle Deposit Form is used to collect information from customers about their vehicle deposit. It is essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the vehicle make in the text field labeled "vehicle_make".
2. Enter the deposit amount in the text field labeled "deposit_amount".
3. Select the payment method from the options provided in the "payment_method" field, which may be "Yes" or "No".
4. Fill in the contact person's information in the text field labeled "contact_person".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **vehicle_make** (`vehicle_make`, text, optional): Enter the make of the vehicle being deposited, for example, "Toyota" or "Ford".
* **deposit_amount** (`deposit_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of the deposit, including any necessary currency symbols (e.g., "$1,000").
* **payment_method** (`payment_method`, select multiple, optional): Select "Yes" if you are paying through a specific method, or "No" if not applicable.
* **contact_person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Fill in the contact person's name and information, for example, "John Doe, Sales Manager".

## Tips
* Double-check the information you enter to ensure accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask the person handling the vehicle deposit for clarification.
* Be as detailed as possible when providing information to avoid any issues with the deposit process.
