## service_appointment_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a service appointment request. It will ask you for your personal details and preferences for the appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer details as requested.
2. Select one or more services that you would like to inquire about.
3. Choose a date and time that suits you for the appointment.
4. Submit the form once you have filled in all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details**: This is where you can enter your personal details, including your name and any other relevant information. This field is not required, so you can leave it blank if you don't want to provide your details.
* **Service Request**: This is where you can select one or more services that you would like to inquire about. Select 'Yes' for each service that interests you.
* **Appointment Preferences**: This is the date field where you can choose a date for your appointment. Please format it as YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Appointment Time**: This is the time field where you can choose a time for your appointment. Please format it as HH:MM.
