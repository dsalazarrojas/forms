# travel_expenses_fest_team_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The travel expenses fest team form is designed to record and report travel-related expenses for team members. This form helps to streamline the process of documenting and tracking team travel expenses, ensuring accurate and transparent financial reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in sections, starting from page 1.
2. Ensure you provide the required fields (Payment Method, Departure Date, Flight Number, and Travel Agency) and fill them accurately.
3. For optional fields, provide only necessary information.
4. Review your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **payment_method** (Select One): Select your mode of payment for the travel expenses (Cash, Credit Card, Debit Card).
* **payment_method_details** (Text): Provide additional details about your payment method (e.g., card number, expiration date).
* **departure_date** (Date): Enter the date you departed for the trip.
* **flight_number** (Text): Enter the flight number you took for the trip.
* **travel_agency** (Text): Enter the name of the travel agency used for booking.
* **travel_agent** (Text): Enter the name of the travel agent who assisted with booking.
* **travel_agency_phone** (Text): Enter the phone number of the travel agency.
* **personal_preferences** (Select Multiple): Select any personal preferences you had during the trip (Accommodation, Food, Transportation, Activity, Other).
* **accommodation** (Text): Enter details about your accommodation during the trip (e.g., hotel name, address).
* **flight_number_details** (Text): Enter any additional details about your flight number.
* **travel_agent** (Text): Enter details about the travel agent's assistance (e.g., helpful or not).
* **payment_method_details** (Text): Enter any additional details about your payment method.

Note: Ensure you review your submission before submitting the form for accurate and complete reporting.
