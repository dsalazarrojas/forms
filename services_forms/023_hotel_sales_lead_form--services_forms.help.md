# Hotel Sales Lead Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hotel Sales Lead Form is a simple form designed to collect basic information from potential customers interested in booking a hotel stay. This form helps the sales team to follow up with leads and offer customized sales pitches.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form accurately, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your **Email** address in the "Email" field. This will allow us to contact you with more information about our offers.
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the "Phone" field. This will help us to contact you with updates about your inquiry.
5. Select the services you are interested in from the "Services" dropdown menu.
6. Choose the **Date of Stay** you are planning to visit us.
7. Choose the **Check In** and **Check Out** times for your stay.
8. Enter a brief **Message** to let us know how we can assist you.
9. If you've already spoken to someone from our team, select who you were assigned to from the "Assigned to" dropdown menu.
10. If applicable, enter the name of your **Company**.
11. If applicable, enter your **Address**.
12. Enter your **Country** of residence.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: This field is for your personal first name.
* **Last Name**: This field is for your personal last name.
* **Email**: This field is for your email address, which we will use to contact you about your stay.
* **Phone**: This field is for your phone number, which we will use to contact you with updates.
* **Services**: Select one or more services you are interested in, such as Room Types or Amenities.
* **Date of Stay**: Choose a date you plan to visit us.
* **Check In**: Choose the time you plan to check in.
* **Check Out**: Choose the time you plan to check out.
* **Message**: This field is for any additional information or requests you have.
* **Assigned to**: If you've already spoken to someone from our team, select who they were.
* **Company**: If you're contacting us on behalf of your company, enter the name here.
* **Address**: If you're contacting us from an office or other location, enter the address here.
* **Country**: Enter the country you're from.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure we can follow up with you effectively.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask our team for clarification.
* We appreciate your interest in our hotel services, and we look forward to assisting you with your stay!
