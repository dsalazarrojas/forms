# Request An Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to request an appointment with a doctor. It is intended for new clients or existing clients who need to schedule a new appointment. Please note that the form is not intended for repeat clients who are already scheduled for an appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select "New Client" to confirm you are a new client or do not have a prior appointment scheduled.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Select Doctor" to indicate whether you are interested in selecting a specific doctor.
3. Choose a date from the list provided in "Select Date" to schedule your appointment.
4. Choose a time from the list provided in "Select Time" to schedule your appointment.
5. Select a duration for your appointment in "Select Duration".
6. Enter your medical history in the "Medical History" field.
7. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Select Practice" to indicate whether you are interested in scheduling with a specific practice.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **New Client**
* **New Client** (1, text, required: false): Please select "New Client" if you are a new client or if you do not have a prior appointment scheduled. If you are an existing client, please do not select this option.

### **Select Doctor**
* **Select Doctor** (2, select_one, required: false): Choose "Yes" if you would like to select a specific doctor for your appointment. If you are unsure or would like to be assigned a doctor, choose "No".

### **Select Date**
* **Select Date** (3, select_multiple, required: false): Choose a date for your appointment from the provided list. Please note that the available dates are subject to change and are not guaranteed.

### **Select Time**
* **Select Time** (4, select_one, required: false): Choose a time for your appointment from the provided list. Please note that the available times are subject to change and are not guaranteed.

### **Select Duration**
* **Select Duration** (5, select_one, required: false): Choose the duration of your appointment from the provided list.

### **Medical History**
* **Medical History** (6, text, required: false): Please enter your medical history in the provided field. This will help the doctor understand your medical background and provide better care.

### **Select Practice**
* **Select Practice** (7, select_one, required: false): Choose "Yes" if you are interested in scheduling with a specific practice. If you are unsure or would like to be assigned a practice, choose "No".
