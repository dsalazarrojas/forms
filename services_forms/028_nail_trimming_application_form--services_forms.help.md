# Nail Trimming Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers for a nail trimming service.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. For select fields, choose one of the provided options.
3. Enter your information accurately and clearly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`nail_artist_form_data`, text, required/optional): Please enter your pet's name.
* **Pet Name** (`pet_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your pet's name.
* **Pet Type** (`pet_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of pet you are scheduling for (Cat, Dog, Other).
* **Nail Length** (`nail_length`, number, required/optional): If possible, provide the length of your pet's nails in inches.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, required/optional): Choose the date you would like to schedule the appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, required/optional): Choose the time you would like to schedule the appointment.
* **Nail Trimming Frequency** (`nail_trimming_frequency`, text, required/optional): Describe how often you would like your pet's nails trimmed (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, etc.).
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, note, required/optional): If there are any special instructions or considerations for your pet's nail trimming, please include them here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): If you would like to be contacted about your appointment or to receive reminders, please enter your email address here.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted about your appointment or to receive reminders.
* **Nail History** (`nail_history`, select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate if your pet has had any previous nail trimming experiences (True or False).
* **Confirm Nail History** (`confirm_nail_history`, select_one, required/optional): Confirm your pet's nail history status (True or False).

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* We appreciate your cooperation in ensuring the best possible experience for your pet.
