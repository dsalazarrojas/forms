# Missed Recycling Collection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to report and record instances of missed recycling collections. Please use this form to document the issue with the collection and provide essential details to help resolve it.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
2. Enter your report date in the "Report Date" field, which is used to track when the issue was reported.
3. If the collection was missed, enter the date it was missed in the "Collection Missed" field.
4. Provide a brief description of the reason for the missed collection in the "Reason" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, so that we can contact you for further information or clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Date** (`collection_date`, Date, required/optional): This field is used to track when the issue was reported. Enter the date you are filling out the form, as it will help us keep accurate records.
* **Collection Missed** (`missed_collection_date`, Date, required/optional): If the collection was missed, enter the date it was originally scheduled for.
* **Reason** (`reason`, Text, required/optional): Please provide a brief description of why the collection was missed. This will help us understand the cause of the issue and prevent future mistakes.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, Email, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further clarification or to follow up on the issue.
