# Client Satisfaction Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from clients about their experience with the service they received. Your input will help us understand what we do well and what we can improve on.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with your personal details, including your name and email address.
2. Enter the date of the service you received.
3. Select the type of service you received.
4. Rate your overall satisfaction with the service and provide comments on the quality, professionalism, and timeliness of the service.
5. Choose how you would rate the quality of the service provided.
6. Rate the professionalism of the service provider.
7. Choose how you would rate the service provider's communication throughout the service.
8. Choose how you would rate if the service provider understood your needs.
9. Provide feedback on what you liked most about the service (optional).
10. Suggest areas for improvement (optional).
11. Indicate whether you would recommend this service to others.
12. Choose how likely you would be to use this service again.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (2): Please enter your name.
* **Email Address** (3): Enter the email address you used to contact us.
* **Date of Service** (4): Enter the date you received the service.
* **Type of Service Received** (5): Select the type of service you received.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (7): Rate your overall satisfaction with the service, choosing from: Very Dissatisfied, Dissatisfied, Neutral, Satisfied, Very Satisfied.
* **Quality of Service Provided** (8): Rate the quality of the service from 1 to 5.
* **Professionalism of Service Provider** (9): Rate the service provider's professionalism from: Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, Excellent.
* **Communication throughout the Service** (12): Rate the service provider's communication from: Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, Excellent.
* **Service Provider Understood Your Needs** (13): Rate how well the service provider understood your needs from: Not at all, Somewhat, Mostly, Completely.
* **What did you like most about the service?** (15): Provide a brief description of what you liked most about the service (optional).
* **What could be improved?** (16): Suggest areas where we can improve (optional).
* **Would you recommend this service to others?** (17): Choose whether you would recommend this service to others.
* **Likelihood of using this service again** (18): Rate how likely you are to use this service again, from: Very Unlikely, Unlikely, Neutral, Likely, Very Likely.
