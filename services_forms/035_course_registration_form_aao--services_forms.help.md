# Course Registration Form (AAO) - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a new course, collecting relevant user information such as name, contact details, course specifics, and course status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Make sure to enter your course start date and status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (id: 1)** (`First Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your personal documents or ID.
* **Last Name (id: 2)** (`Last Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your personal documents or ID.
* **Date of Birth (id: 3)** (`Date of Birth`, `date`, required: true): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Email (id: 4)** (`Email`, `email`, required: true): Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number (id: 5)** (`Phone Number`, `text`, required: true): Enter your phone number without any special characters or symbols.
* **Address (id: 6)** (`Address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your physical address for correspondence or other purposes.
* **Zip Code (id: 7)** (`Zip Code`, `text`, required: false): Enter your zip code for geographic location purposes.
* **Course Name (id: 8)** (`Course Name`, `text`, required: true): Enter the name of the course you are registering for.
* **Course Description (id: 9)** (`Course Description`, `text`, required: false): This field is intended for a brief description of the course.
* **Course Start Date (id: 10)** (`Course Start Date`, `date`, required: true): Enter the start date of the course you are registering for.
* **Course End Date (id: 11)** (`Course End Date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the end date of the course you are registering for (if applicable).
* **Course Status (id: 12)** (`Course Status`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the current status of the course (Active, Inactive, Completed, or Cancelled).
* **Assigned Tool (id: 13)** (`Assigned Tool`, `text`, required: false): Enter the tool assigned to the course or leave blank if not applicable.

## Tips
Please ensure all required fields are filled in accurately and with valid information. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact our support team.
