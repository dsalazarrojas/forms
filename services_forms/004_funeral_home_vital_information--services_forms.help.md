# Funeral Home Vital Information - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential information about a funeral or death process for administrative and record-keeping purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to understand what each field is asking for.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
3. Ensure you answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Death Certificate Form** (`death_certificate_form`, text, optional): This field is for entering the death certificate number, if applicable.
* **Cremation Authorization Form** (`cremation_authorization_form`, select_multiple, optional): Choose 'Yes' if cremation is authorized, 'No' otherwise.
* **Cremation Details** (`cremation_details`, number, optional): Enter the cremation details, if applicable.
* **Death Certificate Number** (`death_certificate_number`, text, optional): Enter the death certificate number, if applicable.
* **Date of Death** (`date_of_death`, date, optional): Enter the date of death.
* **Time of Death** (`time_of_death`, time, optional): Enter the time of death.
* **Place of Death** (`place_of_death`, text, optional): Enter the location where the death occurred.
* **Name of Deceased** (`name_of_deceased`, text, optional): Enter the name of the deceased person.
* **Date of Death Certified** (`date_of_death_certified`, date, optional): Enter the date the death was certified.
* **Time of Death Certified** (`time_of_death_certified`, time, optional): Enter the time the death was certified.
* **Place of Death Certified** (`place_of_death_certified`, text, optional): Enter the location where the death was certified.
* **Cremation Authorized By** (`cremation_authorized_by`, text, optional): Enter the name or title of the person authorizing cremation.
* **Funeral Director** (`funeral_director`, text, optional): Enter the name or title of the funeral director.
* **Death Certificate Issued By** (`death_certificate_issued_by`, text, optional): Enter the name or title of the person or organization issuing the death certificate.
* **Date of Death Certificate Issued** (`date_of_death_certificate_issued`, date, optional): Enter the date the death certificate was issued.
* **Date of Cremation** (`date_of_cremation`, date, optional): Enter the date of cremation.
* **Time of Cremation** (`time_of_cremation`, time, optional): Enter the time of cremation.
* **Place of Cremation** (`place_of_cremation`, text, optional): Enter the location of cremation.
* **Authorized to Remove Body** (`authorized_to_remove_body`, select_one, optional): Choose 'Yes' if authorized to remove the body, 'No' otherwise.
* **Authorization Signature** (`authorization_signature`, text, optional): Enter the signature of the person authorizing removal of the body.

## Tips
- Double-check the accuracy of each field before submission.
- Ensure you answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
- If you are unsure about any field, consult with a funeral director or administrator for guidance.
