# BRS Quote Sheet - Help Guide

## Purpose
The BRS Quote Sheet is a form used to collect information and create a quote for a service or product. This form helps in providing accurate and detailed quotes to customers.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

* Please select the quote sheet and fill out the relevant information.
* Ensure to fill out all required fields to get an accurate quote.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Quote Date** (`quote_date`, date, required): The date when the quote is being issued.
* **Quote Number** (`quote_number`, text, required): Unique identifier for this quote.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): The full name of the customer.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): The email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): The phone number of the contact person.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): The full street address of the customer.
* **City, State, ZIP** (`city_state_zip`, text, optional): The city, state, and ZIP code of the customer.
* **Service Description** (`service_description`, text, required): A brief description of the service or product being quoted.
* **Scope of Work** (`scope_of_work`, text, required): A detailed description of the work to be performed.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): The amount of service or units being quoted.
* **Unit Price** (`unit_price`, number, required): The price per unit of the service or product.
* **Estimated Labor Hours** (`labor_hours`, number, optional): The estimated hours of labor required for the service or product.
* **Labor Rate per Hour** (`labor_rate`, number, optional): The rate per hour of labor.
* **Materials Cost** (`materials_cost`, number, optional): The cost of materials used for the service or product.
* **Subtotal** (`subtotal`, number, optional): The subtotal before taxes and fees.
* **Tax Rate** (`tax_rate`, number, optional): The tax rate applied to the quote.
* **Tax Amount** (`tax_amount`, number, optional): The tax amount calculated on the subtotal.
* **Total Amount** (`total_amount`, number, required): The total amount of the quote.
* **Validity Period** (`validity_period`, date, optional): The expiration date of the quote.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_conditions`, text, optional): Any additional terms or conditions that apply to the quote.
