# home_care_timesheet_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to record the time spent by home care staff on caring activities, including time spent caring for patients and time spent traveling to and from patient locations. The form helps to track and monitor staff work hours, ensuring accurate record-keeping and reporting. It is intended for home care staff to document their daily activities and time spent providing care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's ID number.
2. Enter the date on which the care activities were performed.
3. Record the total time spent caring for patients during the day.
4. Record the total time spent traveling to and from patient locations.
5. Describe the services performed for the patient (e.g., bathing, dressing, etc.).
6. Enter your name and ID number for staff identification.
7. Provide any additional comments about the day's activities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Id**: Enter the unique ID number assigned to the patient you provided care to.
* **Date**: Enter the date on which you provided care to the patient (mm/dd/yyyy format).
* **Time Spent Caring**: Enter the total hours and minutes spent caring for the patient.
* **Time Spent Traveling**: Enter the total hours and minutes spent traveling to and from patient locations.
* **Services Performed**: Describe the services you provided to the patient (e.g., bathing, dressing, etc.).
* **Staff Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your staff ID badge.
* **Staff Id**: Enter your staff ID number for identification purposes.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the day's activities.

## Tips
* Be as accurate as possible when recording time spent caring and traveling.
* If you need to make an error correction, please use the "Comments" field to explain the reason.
* If you have any questions or concerns about this form, please contact your supervisor or administrator.
