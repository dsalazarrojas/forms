# Asset Activity Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and report asset activities, including asset additions, disposals, and transactions. It is essential to ensure that all required fields are filled in to accurately represent the asset activities within the specified report period.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Company Name** of the legal business entity.
2. Select the **Report Period** (Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-Annual, or Annual) covered by the form.
3. Enter the **Period Start Date** and **Period End Date** of the report period.
4. Enter the name of the **Preparer** who is submitting the form.
5. Select the **Department** responsible for the asset activities.
6. Select the **Asset Category** (Current Assets, Fixed Assets, Intangible Assets, Financial Assets, or All Categories).
7. Enter the **Opening Balance** (value at the start of the period).
8. Enter the **Additions During Period** (new assets acquired).
9. Describe the **Additions During Period** in the **Additions Description** field.
10. Enter the **Disposals During Period** (assets sold or removed).
11. Describe the **Disposals During Period** in the **Disposals Description** field.
12. Enter the **Depreciation Amount** (total depreciation).
13. Enter the **Impairment Losses**, **Revaluation Gains**, and **Transfers In/Out** (if any).
14. Enter the **Closing Balance** (value at period end).
15. Enter the **Number of Asset Activities** (total transactions).
16. Describe any **Significant Changes** in the asset activities.
17. Note any **Variance from Budget**.
18. List any **Supporting Documents Attached**.
19. Enter the name of the **Reviewer** who has reviewed the form.
20. Enter the **Review Date** when the form was reviewed.
21. Select the **Approval Status** (Draft, Pending Review, Approved, or Rejected).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name**: Enter the legal business name of the organization submitting this form.
* **Report Period**: Select the time period covered by this asset activity report (Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-Annual, or Annual).
* **Period Start Date** / **Period End Date**: Enter the start and end dates of the report period.
* **Preparer**: Enter the name of the person preparing this report.
* **Department**: Enter the department responsible for the asset activities.
* **Asset Category**: Select the category of the asset (Current Assets, Fixed Assets, Intangible Assets, Financial Assets, or All Categories).
* **Opening Balance**: Enter the value of the assets at the start of the period.
* **Additions During Period**: Enter the value of new assets acquired during the period.
* **Additions Description**: Describe the new assets acquired during the period.
* **Disposals During Period**: Enter the value of assets sold or removed during the period.
* **Disposals Description**: Describe the assets sold or removed during the period.
* **Depreciation Amount**: Enter the total depreciation amount for the period.
* **Impairment Losses**, **Revaluation Gains**, and **Transfers In/Out**: Enter any impairment losses, revaluation gains, or transfers in/out that occurred during the period.
* **Closing Balance**: Enter the value of the assets at the end of the period.
* **Number of Asset Activities**: Enter the total number of transactions for the period.
* **Significant Changes**: Describe any significant changes in the asset activities.
* **Variance from Budget**: Note any variances from the budget.
* **Supporting Documents Attached**: List any supporting documents attached to this form.
* **Reviewer**: Enter the name of the person reviewing this form.
* **Review Date**: Enter the date when the form was reviewed.
* **Approval Status**: Select the current status of the form (Draft, Pending Review, Approved, or Rejected).
