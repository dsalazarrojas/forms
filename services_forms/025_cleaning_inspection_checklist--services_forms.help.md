# Cleaning Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Cleaning Inspection Checklist is a form designed to assess the cleanliness and standards compliance of a property or facility. It evaluates the overall cleanliness and condition of various areas, including entrance and common areas, individual rooms, kitchens, restrooms, and overall property. The form is used to identify areas of concern and recommend corrective actions to maintain or improve cleanliness standards.

<thinking>

This form is intended for use by facilities management, property managers, or maintenance personnel to conduct routine inspections and ensure compliance with established cleaning standards. It is not designed for other purposes, such as customer satisfaction surveys or marketing research.

The form is designed to be completed in sections, with each section focusing on a specific area or aspect of the property. The sections include:

* Entrance and Common Areas
* Individual Rooms
* Kitchen Areas
* Restrooms
* Overall Assessment

Each section is designed to be completed by answering a series of questions, such as rating the cleanliness of various areas or identifying areas of concern. The form also includes fields for inspector comments and signature, as well as fields for follow-up information.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Complete Each Section

1. Begin by completing each section in the order presented.
2. For each question or field, carefully read the description and select the response that best matches your observation.
3. If you find an area or item not listed, please add it to the "Areas of Concern" field.
4. For the "Inspectors Comments" field, provide any additional information you think is relevant to the inspection.
5. Once you have completed all sections, review your answers to ensure accuracy and completeness.
6. Finally, review the "Overall Cleanliness Rating" and "Compliance with Cleaning Standards" fields to provide a summary assessment of the property.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Name** (1): Enter the name of the property being inspected.
* **Property Address** (2): Enter the full address of the property.
* **Inspection Date** (3): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Inspection Time** (4): Enter the time of the inspection.
* **Inspector Name** (5): Enter your name.
* **Inspector Title** (6): Enter your job title or position.
* **Property Type** (7): Select the type of property (e.g., residential home, office building, etc.).
* **Inspection Type** (8): Select the type of inspection (e.g., routine, pre-service, post-service, etc.).
* **Entrance Area Cleanliness** (10): Rate the cleanliness of the entrance area (excellent, good, fair, poor).
* **Floors Condition** (11): Rate the condition of the floors (excellent, good, fair, poor).
* **Walls and Baseboards Condition** (12): Rate the condition of the walls and baseboards (excellent, good, fair, poor).
* **Windows and Glass Condition** (13): Rate the condition of the windows and glass (excellent, good, fair, poor).
* **Lighting Fixtures Condition** (14): Rate the condition of the lighting fixtures (excellent, good, fair, poor).
* **Trash Receptacles Condition** (15): Select the condition of the trash receptacles (excellent, good, fair, poor).
* **Dusting Quality in Rooms** (17): Rate the dusting quality in the rooms (excellent, good, fair, poor).
* **Room Floors Cleanliness** (18): Rate the cleanliness of the room floors (excellent, good, fair, poor).
* **Furniture Cleanliness** (19): Rate the cleanliness of the furniture (excellent, good, fair, poor).
* **Kitchen Counters** (21): Rate the cleanliness of the kitchen counters (excellent, good, fair, poor).
* **Kitchen Appliances** (22): Rate the cleanliness of the kitchen appliances (excellent, good, fair, poor).
* **Kitchen Sink** (23): Rate the cleanliness of the kitchen sink (excellent, good, fair, poor).
* **Kitchen Floors** (24): Rate the cleanliness of the kitchen floors (excellent, good, fair, poor).
* **Restroom Toilets** (26): Rate the cleanliness of the restrooms (excellent, good, fair, poor).
* **Restroom Sinks and Counters** (27): Rate the cleanliness of the restrooms (excellent, good, fair, poor).
* **Mirrors** (28): Rate the cleanliness of the mirrors (excellent, good, fair, poor).
* **Showers or Tubs** (29): Rate the cleanliness of the showers or tubs (excellent, good, fair, poor).
* **Restroom Floors** (30): Rate the cleanliness of the restroom floors (excellent, good, fair, poor).
* **Restroom Supplies** (31): Select the level of restroom supplies (excellent, good, fair, poor).
* **Overall Cleanliness Rating** (33): Rate the overall cleanliness of the property (excellent, good, fair, poor).
* **Compliance with Cleaning Standards** (34): Select whether the property meets cleaning standards (fully compliant, mostly compliant, partially compliant, non-compliant).
* **Areas of Concern** (35): List any areas of concern or issues identified during the inspection.
* **Corrective Actions Needed** (36): Describe any corrective actions needed to address areas of concern.
* **Follow-Up Required** (37): Select whether a follow-up inspection is required (yes, yes - immediate re-cleaning needed, no).
* **Follow-Up Date** (38): Enter the date for the follow-up inspection (if applicable).
* **Inspector Comments** (39): Enter any additional comments or observations from the inspector.
* **Inspector Signature** (40): Enter your electronic signature, including your full name.

### Tips

* Ensure the form is completed accurately and thoroughly to provide a fair and reliable assessment.
* Use the "Areas of Concern" field to identify any issues or areas not listed in the standard sections.
* Review and consider the overall cleanliness rating and compliance with cleaning standards in your assessment.
* Take corrective actions as necessary and document them in the "Corrective Actions Needed" field.
* Schedule follow-up inspections as needed using the "Follow-Up Date" field.
* Provide accurate and detailed comments using the "Inspector Comments" field.
* Ensure your electronic signature is complete and accurate in the "Inspector Signature" field.
