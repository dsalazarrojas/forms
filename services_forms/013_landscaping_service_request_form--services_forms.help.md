# Landscaping Service Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Landscaping Service Request Form is designed to gather information from users who wish to request landscaping services. This form is used to ensure that our team has all the necessary details to provide the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on "Contact" and select your name, phone number, and email address.
2.  Click on "Service" and select the type of service you are interested in.
3.  In the "Details" field, please provide a brief description of your request.
4.  Finally, review and submit your request by selecting "Option 1" or "Option 2" from the "Submit" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Contact** (`Contact`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Please provide your name, phone number, and email address so that our team can contact you about your request.
*   **Service** (`Service`, `text`, required/optional): Select the type of service you are interested in, such as lawn maintenance or tree trimming.
*   **Details** (`Details`, `text`, required/optional): Briefly describe your request, including any specific needs or requirements.
*   **Submit** (`Submit`, `select_one`, required): Review your request and submit it by selecting "Option 1" or "Option 2" to confirm.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that you have provided all necessary information before submitting your request.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, our team will contact you after receiving your request.
*   Please be patient as our team will review your request and contact you as soon as possible to discuss your services.
