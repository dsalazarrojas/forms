# LetterOfAgreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the agreement between a doula and a client, outlining the terms and conditions of the doula's services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form header with a brief title.
2. Enter the client's details.
3. Select the doula's details.
4. Select the agreement terms.
5. Enter the payment terms.
6. Select the signature date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header** (1): Enter a brief title for the letter of agreement.
* **Form Content** (2): Enter a description of the letter of agreement.
* **Client Details** (3): Select whether the client's information is publicly available or not. Options: "Yes" or "No".
* **Doula Details** (4): Select whether the doula's information is publicly available or not. Options: "Yes" or "No".
* **Agreement Terms** (5): Select the agreement terms. Options: "Yes" or "No".
* **Payment Terms** (6): Enter the payment amount (e.g., "500" or "1000").
* **Signature Date** (7): Enter the date of the signature. Format: MM/DD/YYYY.

## Tips

* Make sure to carefully review each field before submitting the form.
* Double-check the client and doula details to ensure they are accurate.
* Enter the payment amount carefully, as it affects the terms of the agreement.
* Enter the signature date correctly, as it is a date field.
* Save the form regularly to avoid losing your progress.
