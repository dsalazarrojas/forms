# Police Report Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Police Report Template is a form used to generate a police report for reporting crimes or incidents to the police department.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please follow these steps:
  1. Select the type of crime that occurred from the list provided.
  2. Enter the date of the incident.
  3. Provide the name of the suspect.
  4. Enter the address of the suspect (if applicable).
  5. Describe the crime in as much detail as possible.
  6. Write a statement about what you witnessed (if you are a witness).
  7. Enter your name and contact information (if you are a witness).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Crime Type** (`crime_type`, `select_multiple`, **Required**): Please select the type of crime that occurred from the options provided. This will help the police department understand the nature of the incident.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, `date`, **Optional**): Enter the date the crime occurred.
* **Suspect Name** (`suspect_name`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the name of the suspect.
* **Suspect Address** (`suspect_address`, `text`, **Optional**): If the suspect has an address, enter it here.
* **Crime Description** (`crime_description`, `text`, **Required**): Describe the crime in as much detail as possible.
* **Witness Statement** (`witness_statement`, `text`, **Required**): If you are a witness, please write a statement about what you saw.
* **Witness Name** (`witness_name`, `text`, **Optional**): If you are a witness, enter your name.
* **Witness Email** (`witness_email`, `email`, **Optional**): If you are a witness, enter your email address.
* **Witness Phone** (`witness_phone`, `text`, **Optional**): If you are a witness, enter your phone number.
