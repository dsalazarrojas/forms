# Free Wellness Evaluation - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from individuals who are seeking free wellness services. Our healthcare professionals will use this information to provide better service and tailor their approach to each individual's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your **User Name** in the first field. This is the name you would like to be known by our healthcare professionals.
2.  Enter your **E-mail** in the second field. This is the email address where you would like to receive updates and notifications from us.
3.  Enter your **Phone** number in the third field. This is the phone number where you can be reached for any further questions or concerns.
4.  Enter your **Date of Birth** in the fourth field. This is your date of birth.
5.  Enter your **Age** in the fifth field. This is your current age.
6.  In the sixth field, please list any **Medical Conditions** you may have. This will help our healthcare professionals understand your health situation.
7.  In the seventh field, please list any **Current Medications** you are taking. This will help our healthcare professionals avoid any potential interactions.
8.  In the eighth field, please list any **Allergies** you may have. This will help our healthcare professionals take necessary precautions.
9.  In the ninth field, please describe your **Medical History**. This will help our healthcare professionals understand your medical history and provide better service.
10. In the tenth field, please select your **Health Insurance Provider**. This will help our healthcare professionals bill your insurance company for any services provided.
11. In the eleventh field, please enter your **Medical Insurance Number**. This is the number associated with your health insurance.
12. In the twelfth field, please enter the **Emergency Contact Name**. This is the name of a person who can be reached in case of an emergency.
13. In the thirteenth field, please enter the **Emergency Contact Phone** number. This is the phone number of the person listed in the previous field.
14. In the fourteenth field, please select your relationship with the emergency contact person. This will help our healthcare professionals reach the right person in case of an emergency.
15. You may have additional **Medical History 1** to **10** fields. Please list any additional medical history that may be relevant to your situation.
