# Service Listing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Listing Form is a tool for service providers to list their services and manage their business information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of your service in the "Service" field.
2. Write a brief description of your service in the "Description" field.
3. Enter your service's address in the "Address" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
6. Select the days your service is available in the "Hours" field.
7. Set your service's rate in the "Rate" field.
8. Choose the status of your service in the "Status" field.
9. Select the category of your service in the "Category" field.
10. Select the subcategory of your service in the "Subcategory" field.
11. Enter any tags associated with your service in the "Tags" field.
12. Choose whether to list tags in the "Tags List" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service** (`service_listing_form_service`, text, required): Enter the title of your service.
* **Description** (`service_listing_form_description`, text, optional): Write a brief description of your service.
* **Address** (`service_listing_form_address`, text, optional): Enter your service's address.
* **Phone** (`service_listing_form_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`service_listing_form_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Hours** (`service_listing_form_hours`, select_multiple, optional): Select the days your service is available.
* **Rate** (`service_listing_form_rate`, number, optional): Set your service's rate.
* **Status** (`service_listing_form_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the status of your service.
* **Category** (`service_listing_form_category`, select_one, optional): Select the category of your service.
* **Subcategory** (`service_listing_form_subcategory`, select_one, optional): Select the subcategory of your service.
* **Tags** (`service_listing_form_tags`, text, optional): Enter any tags associated with your service.
* **Tags List** (`service_listing_form_tags_list`, select_multiple, optional): Choose whether to list tags.

## Tips
* Be sure to fill out as much information as possible to ensure accurate service listings.
* Use the "Tags List" field to indicate whether to display tags for your service.
* Double-check your service's status to ensure accuracy.
