# permission_to_enter_apartment_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request permission to enter an apartment for maintenance, repairs, or other services. It is intended for apartment owners or their representatives to provide access to service providers for a specific date and time.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form as accurately as possible.
2. Make sure to fill out all the fields that are marked as required.
3. If a field is not required, you can skip it if it's not relevant to your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Apartment Owner**: <label> (apartment_owner)</label>
  Enter the name of the apartment owner or person requesting permission.
  This field is a text field where you can enter the name of the person who has ownership or control over the apartment.

* **Service Provider**: <label> (service_provider)</label>
  Enter the name of the service provider (e.g. maintenance worker, repair technician).
  This field is a text field where you can enter the name of the person or company providing the service.

* **Entry Date**: <label> (entry_date)</label>
  Enter the date you want to access the apartment.
  This field is a date field where you can select a date when you want to enter the apartment for maintenance or services.

* **Entry Time**: <label> (entry_time)</label>
  Enter the time you want to access the apartment.
  This field is a time field where you can select the time you want to enter the apartment for maintenance or services.

* **Permission Granted**: <label> (permission_granted)</label>
  Confirm if the permission has been granted.
  This field is a select one field where you can choose "Yes" if permission has been granted, or "No" if it hasn't.

* **Notes**: <label> (notes)</label>
  Add any additional comments or notes about the access request.
  This field is a note field where you can enter any additional information about the access request.

* **Service Provider Phone**: <label> (service_provider_phone)</label>
  Enter the phone number of the service provider.
  This field is a text field where you can enter the phone number of the person or company providing the service.

* **Service Provider Email**: <label> (service_provider_email)</label>
  Enter the email of the service provider.
  This field is an email field where you can enter the email address of the person or company providing the service.

Note: This form is for internal use only and should not be used for external purposes.
