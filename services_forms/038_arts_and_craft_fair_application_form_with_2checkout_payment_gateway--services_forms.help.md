**Form Purpose:**
This form is intended for applicants to the Arts and Crafts Fair, an event that showcases local artisans and craftspeople. The form collects information about the vendor or business, contact person, and relevant details about their craft or art. Additionally, it asks about the vendor's participation in past fairs, how they heard about the event, and their payment method via 2Checkout. This form is essential for the fair organizers to evaluate and process applications.

**How To Complete This Form:**
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your vendor or business name, contact person's name, and email address.
2. Enter your phone number and physical business address.
3. Provide your city and state or province.
4. Specify your zip or postal code.
5. Select the type of craft or art you specialize in.
6. Describe your craft or art in detail.
7. Attach images or samples of your work.
8. Choose your preferred booth size.
9. Enter the fair or event date.
10. Select your experience with participating in past fairs.
11. Specify how you heard about this event.
12. Agree to the fair's terms and payment policy.
13. Choose your payment method via 2Checkout.
14. Add any additional notes or requests.

**Field-by-Field Explanation:**

* **Vendor or Business Name** (`vendor_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter the name of your business or vendor name.
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_person`, `text`, **required**): Enter the name of the contact person for your business.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, **required**): Enter your business's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, **required**): Enter your business's phone number.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, `text`, **required**): Enter your business's physical address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, **required**): Enter the city where your business is located.
* **State or Province** (`state`, `text`, **required**): Enter the state or province where your business is located.
* **Zip or Postal Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, **required**): Enter the zip or postal code of your business.
* **Type of Craft or Art** (`craft_type`, `text`, **required**): Select the type of craft or art you specialize in.
* **Detailed Craft Description** (`craft_description`, `text`, **required**): Describe your craft or art in detail.
* **Product Images or Samples** (`product_images`, `text`, **required**): Attach photos of your work or samples of your craft.
* **Booth Size Preference** (`booth_size_preference`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose the size of the booth you prefer (4x4 feet, 6x6 feet, 8x8 feet, or 10x10 feet).
* **Booth Fee (displayed for payment)** (`booth_cost`, `number`, **required**): Enter the booth fee that will be displayed for payment.
* **Electricity or Water Needed** (`electricity_needed`, `select_one`, **required**): Select whether you need electricity or water at your booth.
* **Fair or Event Date** (`fair_date`, `date`, **required**): Enter the date of the fair or event.
* **Previous Experience** (`previous_experience`, `select_one`, **required**): Select how often you've participated in past fairs.
* **How Did You Hear About This Fair** (`how_heard`, `select_one`, **required**): Specify how you heard about this event.
* **I Agree to Fair Terms and Payment Policy** (`agree_terms`, `select_one`, **required**): Agree to the fair's terms and payment policy.
* **Payment Method via 2Checkout** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose your payment method via 2Checkout (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
* **Additional Notes or Requests** (`additional_notes`, `text`, **optional**): Enter any additional notes or requests.

**Tips:**

* Make sure to provide accurate and clear information to ensure a smooth application process.
* Attach clear and high-quality images or samples of your work to showcase your craft or art.
* Double-check your booth size preference and payment method before submitting.
* Review the fair's terms and payment policy carefully before agreeing.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the fair organizers for assistance.
