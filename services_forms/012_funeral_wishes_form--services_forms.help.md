# Funeral Wishes Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a person's funeral wishes, preferences, and arrangements in the event of their passing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, paying attention to each field's required status.
2. Ensure that you are authorized to complete the form on behalf of the deceased or their representative.
3. Review and edit your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Funeral Wishes** (Field 1: funeral_wishes): This field is where you can write down any specific wishes or requests you have for your funeral or memorial service.
* **Family Preferences** (Field 2: family_preferences): Here, you can select the family members who will be involved in your funeral arrangements. Please choose all applicable family members.
* **Funeral Services** (Field 3: funeral_services): Select the type of funeral services you would like to have, such as cremation, burial, or donation.
* **Donation Details** (Field 4: donation_details): If you have chosen to donate your body for medical research or education, please provide more information about your wishes for this option.
* **Contact Info** (Field 5: contact_info): Please provide the contact information for your next of kin or representative who can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Executor Details** (Field 6: executor_details): Here, you can provide more information about your executor or representative, such as their name and contact details.
* **Final Wishes** (Field 7: final_wishes): This field is where you can express any final wishes or requests you have, such as your favorite music or readings to be played during the service.
* **Medical Info** (Field 8: medical_info): If you have any medical conditions or concerns that your family should be aware of, please provide details here.
* **Funeral Arrangements** (Field 9: funeral_arrangements): Select the type of funeral arrangements you would like to have, such as traditional, green, or cremation.
* **Burial Location** (Field 10: burial_location): Please provide the location where you would like to be buried or have your ashes scattered.
* **Burial Date** (Field 11: burial_date): Choose the date for your burial.
* **Burial Time** (Field 12: burial_time): Select the time for your burial.
* **Final Message** (Field 13: final_message): Here, you can express a final message or wish to be shared with your loved ones.
* **Funeral Home** (Field 14: funeral_home): Indicate whether you would like to use a funeral home or not.
* **Organ Donation** (Field 15: organ_donation): Choose whether you would like to donate your organs for medical research or education.
* **Autopsy** (Field 16: autopsy): Select whether you would like to have an autopsy performed on your body after death.
* **Embalming** (Field 17: embalming): Choose whether you would like your body to be embalmed or not.
* **Burial Certificate** (Field 18: burial_certificate): Indicate whether you would like to obtain a burial certificate or not.
* **Viewing** (Field 19: viewing): Select whether you would like to have a viewing or not.
* **Funeral Services Cost** (Field 20: funeral_services_cost): Please estimate the cost of your funeral services.
* **Executor Benefits** (Field 21: executor_benefits): Here, you can select any benefits you would like your executor or representative to have, such as access to your financial information or authority to make decisions on your behalf.
* **Funeral Location** (Field 22: funeral_location): Please provide the location where you would like to have your funeral.
* **Funeral Date** (Field 23: funeral_date): Choose the date for your funeral.
* **Final Fund** (Field 24: final_fund): Select the type of final fund you would like to set up, such as traditional or green.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have discussed your funeral wishes with your family members or close relatives to ensure everyone is on the same page.
* Review and edit your responses before submitting the form.
* If you have any specific requests or wishes that are not covered by this form, please include them in the final message field.
