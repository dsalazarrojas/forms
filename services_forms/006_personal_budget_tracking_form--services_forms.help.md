# personal budget tracking form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals track their personal income and expenses, setting a target budget, and defining their financial goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your monthly income in the "monthly income" field.
2. Enter your monthly expenses in the "monthly expenses" field.
3. Set your target budget in the "target budget" field.
4. Describe your financial goals in the "financial goals" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Monthly Income** (`1`, number, optional): Enter the total income you receive on a monthly basis.
* **Monthly Expenses** (`2`, number, optional): Enter the total monthly expenses you incur.
* **Target Budget** (`3`, number, optional): Enter your target budget for the month or year.
* **Financial Goals** (`4`, note, optional): Describe your short-term or long-term financial objectives.

## Tips
- Be sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
- Use the "Financial Goals" field to set specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound (SMART) goals.
- You can adjust your target budget as needed, based on your income and expenses.
- This form is for personal use, so don't worry about formatting or styling; just focus on inputting your data accurately.
