# Gym Cleaning Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gym Cleaning Checklist is used to gather information about the cleanliness and maintenance of a fitness center. The purpose of this form is to help you ensure that your gym is clean, safe, and well-maintained for your members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Select the relevant information for each field:
  - **Fitness Center Information (Page 1)**: Enter the name and any additional information about the fitness center.
  - **Facilities (Page 2)**: Choose which facilities you would like to clean, such as Locker Rooms, Showering Areas, and Sauna.
  - **Equipment (Page 2)**: Select which equipment you have available at your gym, such as Treadmill, Exercise Bike, and Weights.
  - **Cleaning Frequency (Page 3)**: Choose how frequently you would like to clean your gym (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly).
  - **Cleaning Standards (Page 4)**: Select which cleaning standards you would like to follow (Sanitation, Hygiene, Organization).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Fitness Center Information (Page 1)** (`fitness_center_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional information about the fitness center that you would like to record.
* **Facilities (Page 2)** (`facilities`, `select_one`, required: false): Select which of the following facilities you would like to clean: 
  - Locker Rooms
  - Showering Areas
  - Sauna
  - Restrooms
  - Exercise Rooms
* **Equipment (Page 2)** (`equipment`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select which of the following equipment you have available:
  - Treadmill
  - Exercise Bike
  - Weights
  - Resistance Machines
* **Cleaning Frequency (Page 3)** (`cleaning_frequency`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how frequently you would like to clean your gym:
  - Daily
  - Weekly
  - Monthly
  - Quarterly
  - Yearly
* **Cleaning Standards (Page 4)** (`cleaning_standards`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select which cleaning standards you would like to follow:
  - Sanitation
  - Hygiene
  - Organization

## Tips
* Make sure to select all facilities and equipment that apply to your gym.
* Choose a cleaning frequency that suits your gym's needs.
* Select the cleaning standards that are relevant to your gym's operations.
* Double-check your selections before submitting the form.
