# Crowd Funding Forms - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information from project creators and backers for a crowd funding campaign. It consists of two main sections: "Project Information" and "Backer's Information".

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Project Information" section with the project's details.
2. Provide the contact information for the project creator in the "Main Project Contact Information" fields.
3. Confirm that the contact information is accurate in the "Backer's Confirmation" field.
4. Read and agree to the terms and conditions in the "Backer's Agreement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Information (1)**: Please provide your project's title and details.
* **Main Project Contact Name (3)**: Enter the name of the project's main contact person.
* **Main Project Contact Email (4)**: Enter the email address of the project's main contact person. We will use this for communication.
* **Main Project Contact Phone Number (5)**: Enter the phone number of the project's main contact person. We will use this for communication.
* **Main Project Deadline (6)**: Enter the deadline for the project.
* **Main Project Timeframe (7)**: Enter the timeframe for the project.
* **Backer's Information (8)**: Provide any additional information you'd like to share about yourself.
* **Backer's Contact Email (9)**: Enter your email address. We will use this for communication.
* **Backer's Contact Phone Number (10)**: Enter your phone number. We will use this for communication.
* **Backer's Notes (11)**: Add any additional notes about yourself.
* **Backer's Confirmation (12)**: Confirm that the contact information you provided is accurate.
* **Backer's Agreement (13)**: Read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Backer's Notes 2 (14)**: Add any additional notes.
