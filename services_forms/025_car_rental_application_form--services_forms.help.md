### # Car Rental Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for car rental applications. It is designed to gather essential details from renters to facilitate the rental process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Step 1: Renter Information**
   - First Name: Enter your first name.
   - Last Name: Enter your last name.
   - Date of Birth: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
   - Email Address: Enter your email address.
   - Phone Number: Enter your contact phone number.
2. **Step 2: Address**
   - Street Address: Enter your street address.
   - City: Enter the city you want to pick up or return the vehicle in.
   - State: Enter the state that issued your driver's license.
   - ZIP Code: Enter your zip code.
3. **Step 3: Driver's License**
   - Driver's License Number: Enter your driver's license number.
   - License State: Enter the state that issued your driver's license.
   - License Country: Enter the country that issued your driver's license.
   - License Expiration: Enter your driver's license expiration date in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
4. **Step 4: Rental Details**
   - Pickup Location: Select the pickup location from the options provided.
   - Pickup Date: Enter the date you want to pick up the vehicle in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
   - Pickup Time: Enter the time you want to pick up the vehicle in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
   - Return Location: Select the return location from the options provided. If it's different from the pickup location, enter the return address.
   - Return Date: Enter the date you want to return the vehicle in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
   - Return Time: Enter the time you want to return the vehicle in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
   - Rental Duration: Select the duration of the rental from the options provided.
5. **Step 5: Vehicle Preference**
   - Vehicle Preference: Select the type of vehicle you prefer.
6. **Step 6: Insurance and Payment**
   - Personal Insurance: Select your insurance coverage type.
   - Payment Method: Select how you want to pay.
   - Card Type: Select the type of card you will use for payment.
   - Cardholder Name: Enter the name on the card.
   - Billing Address: Enter the address on the card.
7. **Step 7: Additional Information**
   - Purpose of Rental: Select the reason you are renting the vehicle.
   - Estimated Mileage: Select how many miles you expect to drive.
   - Special Requests: Enter any special requirements.
8. **Step 8: Terms and Agreement**
   - How Did You Hear About Us: Select how you heard about us.
   - Loyalty Program: Select if you are a member of our loyalty program.
   - Loyalty Number: Enter your loyalty number if applicable.
   - Terms Agreement: Agree to the rental terms and conditions.
   - Application Date: Enter the current date in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
