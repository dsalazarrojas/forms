# Astrology Chart - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for clients to provide information about themselves so that an astrology chart can be generated for their benefit. The chart will depend on the client's birth date, time, location, and any other relevant details they provide. This form is to be filled out completely and accurately to ensure the creation of an accurate and meaningful chart.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
2. Provide as much information as possible, but do not worry if some fields are not known or available.
3. Choose the correct chart type and focus from the provided options.
4. Specify the preferred consultation type and duration.
5. Provide any prior experience with astrology for better understanding and guidance.
6. Optionally, provide any specific questions or concerns you may have.
7. Choose a preferred payment method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (client_first_name, text, required): Provide your given name as it is spelled on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (client_last_name, text, required): Provide your surname as it is spelled on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (client_email, email, required): Enter a valid email address for communication.
* **Phone Number** (client_phone, text, required): Enter your phone number for communication.
* **Date of Birth** (date_of_birth, date, required): Enter your birth date in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Time of Birth** (birth_time, time, not required): If known, enter the time of your birth in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Birth City** (birth_location_city, text, required): Enter the city where you were born.
* **Birth State/Province** (birth_location_state, text, required): Enter the state or province where you were born.
* **Birth Country** (birth_location_country, text, required): Enter the country where you were born.
* **Birth Latitude (if known)** (birth_latitude, number, not required): If known, enter your birth latitude.
* **Birth Longitude (if known)** (birth_longitude, number, not required): If known, enter your birth longitude.
* **Sun Sign (calculated or known)** (sun_sign, text, not required): Select your zodiac sun sign or enter it if calculated.
* **Moon Sign (if known)** (moon_sign, text, not required): If known, select your zodiac moon sign.
* **Rising Sign or Ascendant (if known)** (rising_sign, text, not required): If known, select your zodiac rising sign.
* **Type of Chart Requested** (chart_type_requested, select_one, required): Select the type of chart you need (Birth, Transit, Progressed, etc.).
* **Focus or Purpose of Chart** (chart_focus, select_multiple, required): Choose the area of focus for your chart (Career, Love, Health, etc.).
* **Additional Person Name (for synastry)** (additional_person_name, text, not required): Enter the name of another person for relationship comparison.
* **Additional Person Birth Date** (additional_person_birthdate, date, not required): Enter the birth date of the additional person for relationship comparison.
* **Consultation Type** (consultation_type, select_one, required): Select the type of consultation you want (Chart Interpretation, Telephone, Email, etc.).
* **Preferred Consultation Duration** (consultation_duration, select_one, required): Select the length of time you want the consultation to be.
* **Prior Experience with Astrology** (prior_astrology_experience, select_one, required): Indicate your level of experience with astrology.
* **Specific Questions or Concerns** (specific_questions, text, not required): Enter any questions or concerns you have.
* **Preferred Reading Date** (preferred_reading_date, date, not required): Choose a preferred date for the reading.
* **Payment Method** (payment_method, select_one, required): Select the payment method you prefer (Credit Card, Debit, PayPal, etc.).
* **Request Date** (request_date, date, required): The system will fill out this field automatically.

**Tips**
* Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting.
* If unsure about any field, please contact us for guidance.
* Be as specific as possible for accurate chart creation.
* The more information you provide, the better the chart will be.
* We appreciate your honesty about your experience with astrology to better serve you.
* We will contact you for further details if needed.
