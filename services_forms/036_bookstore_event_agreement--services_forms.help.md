# Bookstore Event Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about upcoming events hosted by the bookstore. It helps the bookstore staff understand the event's requirements, ensuring a smooth and successful execution of the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the "Event Organizer Name", "Email Address", and "Phone Number" fields with the relevant information.
2. Enter the "Company or Business Name" if it is applicable.
3. Provide the "Event Title", which is the name of the event.
4. Enter the "Event Date" and "Event Time" for the event schedule.
5. Choose the expected duration of the event from the provided options (1 hour, 2 hours, 3 hours, 4 hours, 5 hours, or "All day").
6. Estimate the number of expected attendees for the event.
7. Select the "Event Format" according to the event type (e.g., Author reading, Book signing, Workshop, etc.).
8. Describe the "Space Requirements" for the event in the designated field.
9. Choose who provides equipment for the event (Bookstore provides, Organizer provides, Split responsibility, or To be determined).
10. Acknowledge the liability terms by selecting "I agree" or requesting clarification.
11. Agree to the cancellation policy by selecting "I accept" or requesting review.
12. Date and sign the "Agreement Date" field with the current date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Organizer Name** (`event_organizer_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the event organizer.
* **Email Address** (`organizer_email`, email, required): Enter the contact email of the event organizer.
* **Phone Number** (`organizer_phone`, text, required): Enter the contact phone number of the event organizer.
* **Company or Business Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the company or business associated with the event, if applicable.
* **Event Title** (`event_title`, text, required): Enter the name of the event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of the event.
* **Expected Duration** (`event_duration`, select_one, required): Select the expected duration of the event from the provided options (1 hour, 2 hours, 3 hours, 4 hours, 5 hours, or "All day").
* **Expected Number of Attendees** (`expected_attendees`, number, required): Estimate the approximate number of guests attending the event.
* **Event Format** (`event_format`, select_one, required): Choose the type of event (e.g., Author reading, Book signing, Workshop, etc.).
* **Space Requirements** (`space_requirements`, text, required): Describe the space requirements for the event.
* **Who Provides Equipment** (`equipment_responsibility`, select_one, required): Choose who provides equipment for the event (Bookstore provides, Organizer provides, Split responsibility, or To be determined).
* **Liability Acknowledgement** (`liability_acknowledgement`, select_one, required): Acknowledge the liability terms by selecting "I agree" or requesting clarification.
* **Cancellation Policy Agreement** (`cancellation_policy`, select_one, required): Agree to the cancellation policy by selecting "I accept" or requesting review.
* **Agreement Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Date and sign the agreement with the current date.
