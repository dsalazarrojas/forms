# Work Order Close Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Work Order Close Out Form is used to formally conclude the work or services provided to a customer. This form captures key information about the customer, job, and closure status to ensure accurate and complete records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields as follows:
1. Enter the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter the job number in the "Job Number" field.
3. Select the close out date for the work order from the calendar in the "Close Out Date" field.
4. Enter the close out time for the work order in the "Close Out Time" field.
5. Record the number of hours worked on the job in the "Hours Worked" field.
6. List any materials used for the job in the "Materials Used" field.
7. Enter the total cost of the job in the "Total Cost" field.
8. Provide any additional notes or comments about the job in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the customer who was served.
* **Job Number** (`job_number`, text, required: false): Enter the unique identifier for the job.
* **Close Out Date** (`work_order_close_out_date`, date, required: false): Select the date the job was closed out.
* **Close Out Time** (`work_order_close_out_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time the job was closed out.
* **Hours Worked** (`hours_worked`, number, required: false): Record the number of hours worked on the job.
* **Materials Used** (`materials_used`, text, required: false): List any materials used for the job.
* **Total Cost** (`total_cost`, number, required: false): Enter the total cost of the job.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Provide any additional notes or comments about the job.
