# Service Directory Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Directory Submission Form is designed for service providers to submit their information to be listed in the service directory. This guide will walk you through the steps to complete the form correctly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Submit your service information**:
Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information about your service provider. Make sure to review your submission before submitting it for review.
2. **Double-check your contact details**:
Ensure that your email, phone number, and address are correct and easily accessible.
3. **Describe your service**:
In the description field, provide a clear and concise explanation of the services you offer.
4. **Set your review status**:
Indicate the current status of your submission, whether it's awaiting review, approved, rejected, or removed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Directory Submission Form**: This is the title of the submission form. Please fill it out accurately.
* **Name of Organization**: Enter the name of your organization.
* **Name of Service**: Enter the name of your service or the specific service you are offering.
* **Category**: Select the category that best describes your service from the options provided:
	+ Association
	+ Non-Profit
	+ Network
* **Email**: Enter your organization's email address in the format `name@domain.com`. This should be the primary contact method for your organization.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your organization's phone number for easy communication.
* **Address**: Enter your organization's address for accurate location mapping.
* **Description**: Describe your service in a clear and concise manner. Explain what your service offers and how it benefits users.
* **Review Status**: Select the current status of your submission:
	+ Awaiting Review
	+ Approved
	+ Rejected
	+ Removed

## Tips

* Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure a successful submission.
* Double-check your contact details for correctness and accessibility.
* Use clear and concise language in the description field to make it easy for users to understand your service.
* Keep your review status up-to-date to avoid confusion and ensure timely feedback.
* If you're unsure about any field or have questions, feel free to contact us for support.
