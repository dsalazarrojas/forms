# Moving Forward Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Moving Forward Survey is a tool designed to gather information from users about their past experiences, current status, and future goals and plans. This survey aims to help the organization gain valuable insights and feedback to improve its services and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully to ensure you understand what information is being requested.
2. Fill out the form in a quiet and private setting, free from distractions.
3. Answer each question honestly and accurately to the best of your ability.
4. If you're unsure or have questions about any part of the form, don't hesitate to reach out to the relevant personnel for guidance.

* **past_year**: A note field where you can provide any additional comments or information about the past year.
* **goals**: A single-select question where you can choose one of the following options:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Other (please provide additional details in the "future_focus" field)

* **focus**: A text field where you can provide any relevant comments or information about your current focus.
* **past_focus**: A text field where you can provide any additional comments or information about your past experiences and focus.
* **challenges**: A multiple-select question where you can choose all relevant options:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Other (please provide additional details in the "future_achieve" field)
* **lessons_learned**: A multiple-select question where you can choose all relevant options:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Other (please provide additional details in the "future_achieve" field)
* **future_achieve**: A text field where you can provide any additional comments or information about your future goals and plans.
* **future_focus**: A text field where you can provide any additional comments or information about your future focus.
