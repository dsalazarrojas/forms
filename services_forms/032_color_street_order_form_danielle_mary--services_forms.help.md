# Color Street Order Form Danielle Mary - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Color Street Order Form is a form used to capture customer orders for Color Street products. To complete this form, you will need to provide the following information:

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your full name in the "Customer name" field.
2. Enter your contact email in the "Contact email" field.
3. Select the product you would like to order in the "Product" field.
4. Choose the quantity of the product you would like to order in the "Quantity" field.
5. Select your preferred delivery method in the "Delivery method" field.
6. (Optional) Enter a delivery address if you have selected delivery as your preferred delivery method.
7. Enter a preferred date for delivery or pickup in the "Preferred date" field.
8. Add any additional instructions or special requests in the "Additional instructions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer name** (Customer name, text, required): Enter your full name to identify yourself as the customer.
* **Contact email** (Contact email, email, required): Enter your email address for confirmation and to receive any order updates.
* **Product** (Product, text, required): Choose the Color Street product you would like to order.
* **Quantity** (Quantity, number, required): Enter the number of units you would like to order.
* **Delivery method** (Delivery method, select_one, required): Select your preferred delivery method: "Pickup" or "Delivery".
* **Delivery address** (Delivery address, text, optional): If you selected "Delivery" as your preferred delivery method, enter your delivery address.
* **Preferred date** (Preferred date, date, optional): Enter a date for delivery or pickup, using the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Additional instructions** (Additional instructions, text, optional): Add any special requests or instructions for your order.
* **Service option** (Service option, select_one, optional): Select a service type (Standard, Premium, Express) if desired.
Note: The "Service option" field is optional but allows you to choose a service type if you wish.
