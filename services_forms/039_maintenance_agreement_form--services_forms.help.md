# Maintenance Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Maintenance Agreement Form is used to document and standardize the maintenance agreement process, ensuring that all relevant information is collected and stored properly.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Fill out the form sections accordingly, providing accurate and complete information for each field.
* Make sure to select the correct options for each field as required.
* Review and agree to the terms of agreement.
* Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Maintenance Agreement Form** (id: 1, label: Maintenance Agreement Form, type: text, required: false)
    This is the title of the form and will be used to identify the document.

* **Company Name** (id: 2, label: company_name, type: text, required: false)
    Enter the name of the company involved in the maintenance agreement.

* **Maintenance Service Firm** (id: 3, label: maintenance_service_firm, type: select_one, required: false)
    Select one of the options: Firm, Individual, or Both.

* **Maintenance Service Provider** (id: 4, label: maintenance_service_provider, type: select_one, required: false)
    Select one of the options: Firm, Individual, or Both.

* **Agreement Terms** (id: 5, label: agreement_terms, type: text, required: false)
    Enter any terms or conditions specific to the agreement.

* **Maintenance Agreement Form Version** (id: 6, label: maintenance_agreement_form_version, type: text, required: false)
    Enter the version of the maintenance agreement form being used.

* **Date** (id: 7, label: date, type: date, required: false)
    Enter the date the agreement was made or revised.

* **Service Description** (id: 8, label: service_description, type: text, required: false)
    Enter a brief description of the service provided.

* **Payment Details** (id: 9, label: payment_details, type: text, required: false)
    Enter payment details, if applicable.

* **Signature** (id: 10, label: signature, type: text, required: false)
    Sign your name to agree to the terms.

* **Comments** (id: 11, label: comments, type: text, required: false)
    Enter any comments or notes about the agreement.

* **Signed Date** (id: 12, label: agreed_by, type: date, required: false)
    Enter the date the agreement was signed.

* **Agreed By** (id: 13, label: agreed_by, type: select_one, required: false)
    Select one of the options: Firm, Maintenance Service Firm, or Both.

* **Signed By** (id: 14, label: signed_by, type: select_one, required: false)
    Select one of the options: Firm, Maintenance Service Provider, or Both.

* **Terms of Agreement** (id: 15, label: terms_of_agreement, type: text, required: false)
    Enter a summary of the agreement terms.

* **Agreed To Terms** (id: 16, label: agreed_to_terms, type: text, required: false)
    Enter a confirmation that you understand and agree to the terms.

* **Service Provider** (id: 17, label: service_provider, type: text, required: false)
    Enter the name or description of the service provider.

* **Maintenance Company** (id: 18, label: maintenance_company, type: text, required: false)
    Enter the name of the maintenance company.

* **Agreement Revision Date** (id: 19, label: agreement_revision_date, type: date, required: false)
    Enter the date the agreement was revised.

* **Next Agreement Revision Due Date** (id: 20, label: next_agreement_revision_due_date, type: date, required: false)
    Enter the date the next revision is due.

* **Last Agreement Revision Date** (id: 21, label: last_agreement_revision_date, type: date, required: false)
    Enter the date of the last revision.

* **Agreement Revision Number** (id: 22, label: agreement_revision_number, type: text, required: false)
    Enter the number of the agreement revision.

* **Agreement Revision Status** (id: 23, label: agreement_revision_status, type: text, required: false)
    Enter the status of the agreement revision.

* **Service Fees** (id: 24, label: service_fees, type: text, required: false)
    Enter any service fees associated with the agreement.

* **Payment Terms** (id: 25, label: payment_terms, type: text, required: false)
    Enter payment terms or conditions.

## Tips

* Make sure to review and agree to the terms before signing the agreement.
* Ensure that all fields are filled out correctly and accurately.
* Date and sign the agreement to confirm your agreement.
* Keep a copy of the agreement for your records.
* Update the agreement as needed and store the revisions properly.
