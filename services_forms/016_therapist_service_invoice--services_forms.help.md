# Therapist Service Invoice - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to create an invoice for a Therapist Service session. It helps to document the details of the session, including client name, session date, session duration, session description, total session fees, payment status, payment method, and session notes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Client Name** (Field 1) to identify the client who received the session.
2. Select the **Session Date** (Field 2) of the session.
3. Enter the **Session Duration** (Field 3) in minutes.
4. Provide a detailed description of the **Session Details** (Field 4) to document the session.
5. Enter the **Total Session Fees** (Field 5) that the client needs to pay.
6. Indicate whether **Payment Received** (Field 6) or not.
7. Select the **Payment Method** (Field 7) used by the client (Check or Bank Transfer).
8. Add any **Session Notes** (Field 8) about the session, such as any important information or follow-up tasks.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name (Field 1, text)**: Enter the name of the client who received the session.
* **Session Date (Field 2, date)**: Enter the date of the session.
* **Session Duration (Field 3, number)**: Enter the duration of the session in minutes.
* **Session Details (Field 4, text)**: Provide a detailed description of the session to document its progress.
* **Total Session Fees (Field 5, number)**: Enter the total fees for the session.
* **Payment Received (Field 6, select_one)**: Indicate whether payment has been received or not.
* **Payment Method (Field 7, select_multiple)**: Select the payment method used by the client (Check or Bank Transfer).
* **Session Notes (Field 8, note)**: Add any important notes or follow-up tasks about the session.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check the client's name and session date to ensure accuracy.
* Be specific and detailed when describing the session details.
* Update the payment status and method as soon as the payment is received.
* Keep the session notes concise and focused on important information or follow-up tasks.
