# Pre Plan Funeral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pre Plan Funeral Form is designed to help individuals and families pre-plan funeral arrangements before the loss of a loved one. This form gathers essential information to ensure that the funeral process is handled smoothly and respectfully when the time comes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide your name**: In the "Name" field, enter the name of the deceased.
2. **Enter age**: In the "Age" field, enter the age of the deceased at the time of passing.
3. **Specify place of death**: In the "Place Of Death" field, enter the location where the deceased passed away.
4. **Cause of death**: In the "Cause Of Death" field, enter the medical condition or circumstances leading to the death.
5. **Time of death**: In the "Time Of Death" field, enter the time of day the deceased passed away.
6. **Place of service**: In the "Place Of Service" field, enter the location of the funeral service or memorial service.
7. **Casket type**: In the "Casket Type" field, specify the type of casket you would like to use.
8. **Urn**: In the "Urn" field, select or specify an urn for the deceased's ashes.
9. **Obits**: In the "Obits" field, enter any obituary notices or publications.
10. **Flowers**: In the "Flowers" field, list any flower arrangements or tributes you would like.
11. **Headstone**: In the "Headstone" field, specify the type of headstone or monument you would like.
12. **Viewing**: In the "Viewing" field, indicate if you would like a viewing or visitation service.
13. **Burial**: In the "Burial" field, specify if you would like a burial service.
14. **Cremation**: In the "Cremation" field, indicate if you would like cremation instead of burial.
15. **Memorial**: In the "Memorial" field, specify any memorial services or events.
16. **Reception**: In the "Reception" field, enter any reception or gathering details.
17. **Obituary**: In the "Obituary" field, enter any obituary notices or publications.
18. **Autopsy**: In the "Autopsy" field, enter any autopsy reports or results.
19. **Death Certificate**: In the "Death Certificate" field, enter any death certificate details.
20. **Service**: In the "Service" field, specify the type of service or ceremony.
21. **Reception Details**: In the "Reception Details" field, enter any reception or gathering details.
22. **Obituary Details**: In the "Obituary Details" field, enter any obituary notices or publications.
23. **Funeral Details**: In the "Funeral Details" field, enter any funeral or memorial service details.
24. **Pre Plan Funeral**: In the "Pre Plan Funeral" field, enter any additional comments or preferences for the funeral.
