# Industrial Equipment Health Log Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Industrial Equipment Health Log Report is a tool used to collect data on the status of industrial equipment within a facility. The report helps maintenance personnel report and track issues with equipment, facilitating prompt resolution and resource allocation.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, please follow these steps:

    1.  Review the equipment in your area of responsibility.
    2.  Check the status of each piece of equipment, considering its condition, and any issues that have arisen recently.
    3.  Fill out the report for each piece of equipment, providing accurate and detailed information about its condition and any issues.
    4.  Submit the completed form for review by facility management.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Title (1)** (text, Optional): This is the title of the form, which should be filled out with the name of the equipment being reported on.
*   **Equipment Details (2)** (text, Optional): Provide a brief description of the equipment, including its type, model, and any relevant specifications.
*   **Issue Reported (3)** (text, Optional): Describe the issue that has been reported with the equipment, including any symptoms or problems that have arisen.
*   **Issue Description (4)** (text, Optional): Provide a detailed explanation of the issue, including any factors that may have contributed to it.
*   **Issue Cause (5)** (text, Optional): Describe the probable cause of the issue, if known.
*   **Issue Effect (6)** (text, Optional): Outline the impact of the issue on operations, including any downtime or disruptions caused.
*   **Repair Status (7)** (select, Optional): Select the current status of the issue, which should be one of the following:
    *   Active: The issue is currently being repaired or addressed.
    *   Inactive: The issue has been resolved.
*   **Issue Priority (8)** (select, Optional): Indicate the priority of the issue, which should be either:
    *   Yes: The issue is urgent and requires immediate attention.
    *   No: The issue is not urgent and can be addressed in the future.
*   **Issue Category (9)** (select, Optional): Identify the category of the issue, which should be:
    *   Yes: The issue is related to the equipment's functionality or performance.
    *   No: The issue is not related to the equipment's functionality or performance.
*   **Issue Resolution (10)** (select, Multiple): Select all relevant options to describe how the issue was resolved, which may include:
    *   Yes: The issue was resolved.
    *   No: The issue was not resolved.
*   **Issue Reporter (11)** (select, Optional): Indicate whether or not you are reporting the issue, which should be:
    *   Yes: You are reporting the issue.
    *   No: You are not reporting the issue.

## Tips
*   When reporting an issue, please be as detailed and accurate as possible.
*   Be sure to review and update the report regularly to ensure that it accurately reflects the status of the equipment.
