# Virtual Assistant Online Business Manager Service Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential clients or customers who are interested in receiving virtual assistant services from the online business manager service.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of service you are looking for (Service Type).
2. Enter your business name and contact information (Name, Email, Phone).
3. Specify what service you need help with (Service Needed).
4. Provide details on the service you inquired (Service Inquired).
5. Choose your preferred communication method (Preferred Communication).
6. If desired, select a follow-up schedule (Follow-Up Schedule).
7. Add any additional notes about your request.
8. Provide your business working hours (Business Hours).
9. Choose a best contact time (Best Contact Time).
10. If you have any meeting details (Meeting Details), date (Meeting Date), and time (Meeting Time) please specify them.
11. If you prefer, you can also specify a meeting location (Meeting Location).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Type** (Select One):
	+ Please select the type of service you are looking for.
* **Name** (Text):
	+ Enter your business name.
* **Email** (Email):
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Text):
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Service Needed** (Select One):
	+ Please select the service you need help with.
* **Service Inquired** (Text):
	+ Provide details on the service you inquired.
* **Preferred Communication** (Select One):
	+ Choose how you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone, Text, or Chat).
* **Follow-Up Schedule** (Select Multiple):
	+ If you want to be contacted at regular intervals, please select the schedule (1 hour, 2 hours, 3 hours, 4 hours, or 5 hours).
* **Additional Notes** (Note):
	+ Add any additional information about your request.
* **Business Hours** (Text):
	+ Enter your business working hours.
* **Best Contact Time** (Time):
	+ Choose a time when you are best available to be contacted.
* **Meeting Details** (Text):
	+ Provide any meeting details if you prefer to meet in-person or virtually.
* **Meeting Date** (Date):
	+ If you have a meeting date in mind, please enter it.
* **Meeting Time** (Time):
	+ If you have a meeting time in mind, please enter it.
* **Meeting Location** (Select One):
	+ If you prefer, please select a meeting location (Virtual, In-person, or Online Office).
* **Meeting Location Details** (Text):
	+ If you have specific meeting location details, please provide them.
* **Meeting Follow-Up** (Text):
	+ This field is not currently used in the form.

Note: The form is designed to be flexible and adaptable to the user's needs, so please feel free to adjust the fields according to your requirements.
