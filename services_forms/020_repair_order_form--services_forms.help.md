# Repair Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Repair Order Form is designed to collect information from customers who need to place a repair order. This form will help to understand their order details, customer information, and any additional notes they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Go to the first page of the form, labeled "Order Details".
2. Fill out the "Order Details" section with any relevant information about the order you are placing.
3. Move on to the next page, "Customer Info", to fill out the customer's contact information.
4. On the "Order Items" page, select the relevant options for the items that need to be repaired.
5. Fill out the "Customer Address" and "Customer Phone" sections with the customer's contact information.
6. Enter the customer's email address in the "Customer E-mail" section.
7. Add any additional notes in the "Customer Notes" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details**: 
  This section is for providing the details of the repair order. Please enter any relevant information about the order.
  (required: no)

* **Customer Info**:
  This is the section where you will enter the customer's information.
  - **Customer Address**: 
    * A text area for entering the customer's address.
    * Please enter the customer's address in this field.
    (required: no)
  - **Customer Phone**:
    * A text field for entering the customer's phone number.
    * Please enter the customer's phone number in this field.
    (required: no)
  - **Customer E-mail**:
    * An email field for entering the customer's email address.
    * Please enter the customer's email address in this field.
    (required: no)
  - **Customer Notes**:
    * A text field for entering any additional notes about the customer.
    * Please enter any additional notes about the customer in this field.
    (required: no)

* **Order Items**:
  This section is for selecting the items that need to be repaired.
  - **Order Item 1**:
    * A text field for entering the details of the first item to be repaired.
    * Please enter the details of the first item to be repaired.
    (required: no)
  - **Order Item 2**:
    * A text field for entering the details of the second item to be repaired.
    * Please enter the details of the second item to be repaired.
    (required: no)

* **Customer Address**:
  This is a note field for entering the customer's address.
  Please enter the customer's address in this field.
  (required: no)

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields correctly.
- Double-check the information you enter before submitting the form.
- If you need help or have any questions, contact the support team.
