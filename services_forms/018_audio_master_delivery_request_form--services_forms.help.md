# Audio Master Delivery Request Form - Help Guide

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please enter the artist's name in the "Artist Name" field. This is the name of the artist or band you are working with.
2. Next, provide the contact person's full name in the "Contact Full Name" field. This will be the primary contact for communication and delivery.
3. Enter the contact person's email address in the "Email Address" field. This will be used for communication and updates.
4. Enter the contact person's phone number in the "Phone Number" field. This will be used to contact you for any questions or concerns.
5. In the "Label or Company" field, provide the name of the record label or company if applicable.
6. Enter the date of the delivery request in the "Request Date" field.
7. Enter the project title (album, EP, or single) in the "Project Title" field.
8. Select the type of project from the "Project Type" dropdown: "Single Track", "EP (3-6 tracks)", "Full Album (7+ tracks)", "Compilation", "Podcast Series", "Audiobook", or "Other".
9. Enter the total number of tracks to be mastered in the "Number of Tracks" field.
10. Select the primary musical genre from the "Genre" dropdown: "Rock", "Pop", "Hip Hop", "Electronic", "Classical", "Country", "R and B", "Metal", "Folk", or "Other".
11. If you have any reference tracks for mastering style guidance, list them in the "Reference Tracks" field.
12. Choose the mix format for delivery from the "Mix Format" dropdown: "WAV 24-bit", "WAV 16-bit", "AIFF", "MP3", or "Other".
13. Select the sample rate from the "Sample Rate" dropdown: "44.1 kHz", "48 kHz", "88.2 kHz", "96 kHz", or "Other".
14. Choose the preferred delivery format from the "Delivery Format" multiple select: "WAV 24-bit", "WAV 16-bit", "MP3 320kbps", "MP3 256kbps", "AAC", "FLAC", or "DDP (for CD)".
15. Select the delivery method from the "Delivery Method" dropdown: "Digital download link", "Cloud storage link", "Physical USB drive", "Physical CD", or "FTP transfer".
16. If applicable, enter the delivery address in the "Delivery Address" field.
17. Enter the delivery deadline in the "Deadline" field.
18. Select whether you have ISRC codes ready in the "ISRC Codes" dropdown: "Yes, will provide", "No, need assistance", or "Not applicable".
19. Choose whether CD text is required for physical release in the "CD Text Required" dropdown: "True", "False", or "Not applicable".
20. If you have any special instructions or notes, enter them in the "Special Instructions" field.
21. Select the number of revision rounds from the "Revision Rounds" dropdown: "1 round", "2 rounds", "3 rounds", or "Unlimited".
22. Indicate whether a rush service is required in the "Rush Service" dropdown: "Yes, rush service needed", "No, standard timeline".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist Name** (`artist_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the artist or band.
* **Contact Full Name** (`contact_full_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the primary contact person.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the contact person's email address for communication and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the contact person's phone number for communication and updates.
* **Label or Company** (`label_or_company`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the record label or company.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the delivery request.
* **Project Title** (`project_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the project (album, EP, or single).
* **Project Type** (`project_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of project from the available options: "Single Track", "EP (3-6 tracks)", "Full Album (7+ tracks)", "Compilation", "Podcast Series", "Audiobook", or "Other".
* **Number of Tracks** (`number_of_tracks`, number, required): Enter the total number of tracks to be mastered.
* **Genre** (`genre`, select_one, required): Select the primary musical genre from the available options: "Rock", "Pop", "Hip Hop", "Electronic", "Classical", "Country", "R and B", "Metal", "Folk", or "Other".
* **Reference Tracks** (`reference_tracks`, text, optional): If you have any reference tracks for mastering style guidance, list them here.
* **Mix Format** (`mix_format`, select_one, required): Choose the mix format for delivery from the available options: "WAV 24-bit", "WAV 16-bit", "AIFF", "MP3", or "Other".
* **Sample Rate** (`sample_rate`, select_one, required): Select the sample rate from the available options: "44.1 kHz", "48 kHz", "88.2 kHz", "96 kHz", or "Other".
* **Delivery Format** (`delivery_format`, select_multiple, required): Choose the preferred delivery format from the available options: "WAV 24-bit", "WAV 16-bit", "MP3 320kbps", "MP3 256kbps", "AAC", "FLAC", or "DDP (for CD)".
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, select_one, required): Select the delivery method from the available options: "Digital download link", "Cloud storage link", "Physical USB drive", "Physical CD", or "FTP transfer".
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the delivery address for physical delivery.
* **Deadline** (`deadline`, date, required): Enter the delivery deadline.
* **ISRC Codes** (`isrc_codes`, select_one, required): Select whether you have ISRC codes ready from the available options: "Yes, will provide", "No, need assistance", or "Not applicable".
* **CD Text Required** (`cd_text_required`, select_one, required): Choose whether CD text is required for physical release from the available options: "True", "False", or "Not applicable".
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, optional): If you have any special instructions or notes, enter them here.
* **Revision Rounds** (`revision_rounds`, select_one, required): Select the number of revision rounds from the available options: "1 round", "2 rounds", "3 rounds", or "Unlimited".
* **Rush Service** (`rush_service`, select_one, required): Indicate whether a rush service is required in the available options: "Yes, rush service needed", "No, standard timeline".

Please ensure to fill out all required fields accurately to ensure efficient processing of your request.
