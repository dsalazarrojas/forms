# Straight Truck Pre Trip Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Straight Truck Pre Trip Inspection Form is a critical tool for truck drivers to inspect their vehicle before embarking on a trip. This form helps drivers to identify potential issues with their vehicle and ensure it is in good working condition, reducing the risk of accidents and damage to the vehicle or cargo.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the driver details section with your name, truck number, and date.
2. For each section, check the corresponding checkbox or select the option that best describes the condition of the vehicle's system or component.
3. If a system or component has any issues, describe the problem in the provided text field.
4. Review the form carefully to ensure you have completed all the necessary sections.
5. Sign and date the form to confirm that you have inspected the vehicle.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Straight Truck Pre Trip Inspection Form**: This is the title of the form, which serves as a heading.
* **Driver Details**: Fill in your name, truck number, and date to identify yourself as the driver.
* **Vehicle Details**: Check the options that best describe the condition of the vehicle's systems and components.
* **Vehicle Condition**: Select all that apply from the options, or describe any other issues in the text field.
* **Brake System**: Assess the condition of the brake system, selecting Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Brake Warning**: If you have any concerns about the brake system, describe them in this text field.
* **Suspension System**: Evaluate the condition of the suspension system, choosing Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Steering System**: Check the condition of the steering system, selecting Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Transmission System**: Assess the condition of the transmission system, selecting Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Wheel Brakes**: Evaluate the condition of the wheel brakes, choosing Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Tire Inflation**: Enter the current tire inflation levels for the vehicle.
* **Tire Condition**: Select all that apply from the options, or describe any other issues with the tires in the text field.
* **Tire Size**: If different from the standard size, enter the correct size in this field.
* **Tire Type**: If the tires are not standard, describe the type in this field.
* **Load Rating**: If you're unsure about the load rating, you can research it or consult with a mechanic.
* **Tire Pressure**: If the tire pressure is not at the recommended level, describe the issue in this field.
* **Tire Tread Depth**: If the tread depth is below the recommended level, describe the issue in this field.
* **Tire Side Walls**: If any issues with the tire side walls, describe them in this field.
* **Tire Center Tires**: If any issues with the center tires, describe them in this field.
* **Vehicle Equipment**: If any equipment is missing or not functioning correctly, list it in this field.
* **Vehicle Deflection**: Evaluate the vehicle's deflection, selecting Less than 10 degrees, Between 10 and 20 degrees, Between 20 and 30 degrees, or More than 30 degrees.
* **Vehicle Alignment**: If the vehicle is not properly aligned, describe the issue in this field.
* **Vehicle Condition Note**: If any additional comments about the vehicle's condition, add them in this field.
* **Vehicle Deflection Note**: If any further information about the vehicle's deflection, add it in this field.
* **Vehicle Equipment Note**: If any additional comments about the vehicle's equipment, add them in this field.

## Tips

* Always inspect your vehicle thoroughly before each trip.
* Check the vehicle's condition regularly to prevent issues from becoming major problems.
* If you're unsure about any aspect of the vehicle's condition, consult with a mechanic or supervisor.
* Keep this form with you during the trip, in case you need to refer to it during an inspection or audit.
