# Detailed Real Estate - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed information about real estate transactions, including contact details, follow-up information, and notes related to the agent's interactions with the client. The form is used to document and track the progress of real estate transactions, ensuring that all relevant information is captured accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the contact type (e.g., First Contact, Re-Opening Contact, etc.) and provide the first contact date if applicable.
2. Choose the contact frequency (e.g., Daily, Weekly, etc.) and the source of the first contact (e.g., Email, Phone, etc.).
3. Enter the phone number and email of the contact person or realtor.
4. Provide notes related to the initial contact, contact person, and follow-up activities.
5. Select the follow-up type (e.g., First Follow-up, Second Follow-up, etc.) and follow-up frequency (e.g., Weekly, Monthly, etc.).
6. Choose the source and method of follow-up (e.g., Email, Phone, etc.).
7. Enter notes related to the follow-up activities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Initial Contact** (1) (Text, Required: False): Enter the initial contact details, including the contact type and date.
* **Contact Type** (2) (Select One, Required: False): Select the type of contact (e.g., First Contact, Re-Opening Contact, etc.).
* **First Contact Date** (3) (Date, Required: False): Enter the date of the first contact if applicable.
* **Contact Frequency** (4) (Select One, Required: False): Choose the frequency of contact (e.g., Daily, Weekly, etc.).
* **First Contact Source** (5) (Select One, Required: False): Select the source of the first contact (e.g., Email, Phone, etc.).
* **Phone Number** (6) (Text, Required: False): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Email** (7) (Email, Required: False): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Contact Method** (8) (Select One, Required: False): Select the method of contact (e.g., Voice, Email, etc.).
* **Lead Source** (9) (Select One, Required: False): Select the source of the lead (e.g., Realtor Website, Realtor Social Media, etc.).
* **Agent Name** (10) (Text, Required: False): Enter the realtor's name.
* **Agent Email** (11) (Email, Required: False): Enter the realtor's email address.
* **Agent Phone Number** (12) (Text, Required: False): Enter the realtor's phone number.
* **Contact Person** (13) (Text, Required: False): Enter the contact person's name.
* **Contact Person Phone Number** (14) (Text, Required: False): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Agent Note** (15) (Text, Required: False): Enter notes related to the realtor's interactions with the client.
* **Contact Note** (16) (Text, Required: False): Enter notes related to the contact activities.
* **Realtor Note** (17) (Text, Required: False): Enter notes related to the realtor's interactions with the client.
* **Follow-up Type** (18) (Select One, Required: False): Select the follow-up type (e.g., First Follow-up, Second Follow-up, etc.).
* **Follow-up Frequency** (19) (Select One, Required: False): Choose the follow-up frequency (e.g., Weekly, Monthly, etc.).
* **Follow-up Source** (20) (Select One, Required: False): Select the source of follow-up (e.g., Email, Phone, etc.).
* **Follow-up Method** (21) (Select One, Required: False): Select the method of follow-up (e.g., Voice, Email, etc.).
* **Follow-up Source** (22) (Select One, Required: False): Select the source of follow-up (e.g., Realtor Website, Realtor Social Media, etc.).
* **Follow-up Person** (23) (Text, Required: False): Enter the follow-up person's name.
* **Follow-up Person Phone Number** (24) (Text, Required: False): Enter the follow-up person's phone number.
* **Follow-up Note** (25) (Text, Required: False): Enter notes related to follow-up activities.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and relevant information for each field.
* Review and update the form regularly to ensure that all fields are up-to-date and accurate.
* Use the form as a reference guide to track and document real estate transactions.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the form instructions or consult with a realtor or team member.
