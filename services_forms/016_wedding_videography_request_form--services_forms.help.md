# Wedding Videography Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed for couples to request wedding videography services from our team. It's intended to capture the essential details needed for us to prepare and deliver an exceptional wedding video. Please take a few minutes to complete this form carefully, as it helps us better understand your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

Before filling out the form, make sure you have all the necessary information ready. This includes:

* Choosing the package that suits your wedding needs
* Selecting your preferred videographer(s)
* Adding any desired add-ons
* Providing your wedding date and ceremony, reception times
* Sharing any special requests or messages
* Providing contact information for us to reach out to you

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Package Choice (Basic, Premium, or Elite)**: Please select one of our three offered packages based on your wedding vision and budget. Our packages offer varying levels of service, so choose the one that best fits your expectations.
* **Videographer Preference (John, Jane, or Jim)**: Select up to three videographers who you would like to work with for your wedding. This will allow us to ensure that your preferred team is assigned to your event.
* **Add-ons (Tilting, Editing, Montage, or Photo Booth)**: Choose any additional services you'd like to include in your package. These add-ons can enhance your wedding video.
* **Wedding Date**: Enter the date of your wedding (e.g., 2024-02-15).
* **Ceremony Time**: Enter the start time of your ceremony (e.g., 10:00 AM).
* **Reception Time**: Enter the start time of your reception (e.g., 6:00 PM).
* **Special Request**: If there's anything specific you'd like to mention or any unique requirements for your wedding video, please share them here.
* **Contact Information**: Please provide your contact information so we can reach out to you with any questions or updates.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where we can contact you.
* **Message**: If there's anything else you'd like to share or discuss with us, please include it here.
