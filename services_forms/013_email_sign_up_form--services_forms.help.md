# email_sign_up_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The email_sign_up_form is used for new users to sign up for our service. It collects user information such as email, phone number, and organization for registration and verification purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
4. Confirm your Email address in the Confirm Email field.
5. Enter your Service Id in the Service Id field.
6. Confirm your Service Id in the Confirm Service Id field.
7. (Optional) Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
8. (Optional) Enter your Organization in the Organization field, and confirm it in the Confirm Organization field.
9. Click Submit to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address. This field is required.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, email, optional): Confirm your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): Enter your organization (if any).
* **Confirm Organization** (`confirm_organization`, text, optional): Confirm your organization.
* **Service Id** (`service_id`, text, required): Enter your service id. This field is required.
* **Confirm Service Id** (`confirm_service_id`, text, optional): Confirm your service id.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you encounter any issues, please contact our support team.
* The form is designed to be simple and easy to understand, with clear labels and instructions for each field.
