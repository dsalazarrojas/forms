# dog-walking-training-pet-sitting--homestay-agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for pet-related services, specifically for dog walking, pet sitting, and homestay agreements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form with the necessary information.
2. Select the services you offer (Dog Walking, Pet Sitting, Homestay, or Other) in the "Services Offered" field.
3. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Information" field if desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form**:
This is the main form where you will enter your information.
- **Pet Sitting Information**:
Enter any relevant information about pet sitting services you offer (e.g., duration of visits, frequency, etc.).
- **Pet Sitting Rules**:
Describe any rules or policies you have for pet sitting services (e.g., vaccination requirements, emergency procedures, etc.).
- **Homestay Agreement**:
Provide any information about homestay agreements you have established (e.g., terms and conditions, payment options, etc.).
- **Services Offered** (Dog Walking, Pet Sitting, Homestay, or Other):
Select the services you offer from the available options. This is a required field, so choose one or more of the provided options.
- **Contact Information**:
Enter your contact information if you want to be reached by clients or customers.
- **Email**:
Enter your email address.
- **Phone**:
Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct services you offer.
* Be as detailed as possible in the "Pet Sitting Information" and "Homestay Agreement" fields.
* Keep your contact information up-to-date and accurate.
* If you have any specific rules or policies for services, include them in the "Pet Sitting Rules" field.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
