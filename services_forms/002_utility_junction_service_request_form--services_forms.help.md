# Utility Junction Service Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by customers to submit service requests for utility services such as electric, gas, or water. It is a straightforward and easy-to-use form that allows customers to provide their information and submit a service request.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your **Service Request** in the "Service Request" field.
2. Select the type of service you require from the **Service Type** dropdown menu.
3. Provide a **Service Description** of your request.
4. Enter your **Customer Name**.
5. Enter your **Phone Number**.
6. Enter your **Email Address**.
7. Enter your **Address**.
8. Select the **Service Request Date** and **Service Request Time** for the required service.
9. Choose who you would like the service to be assigned to from the **Assigned To** dropdown menu.
10. Choose the date and time when the service is assigned to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Request**: This field is where you can provide a brief description of your service request.
* **Service Type**: This is a dropdown menu that allows you to select the type of service you require (electric, gas, or water).
* **Service Description**: This field is where you can provide more details about your service request.
* **Customer Name**: This field is used to capture your name.
* **Phone Number**: This field is used to store your phone number.
* **Email**: This field is used to store your email address.
* **Address**: This field is used to store your address.
* **Service Request Date**: This field is used to select a date for the service request.
* **Service Request Time**: This field is used to select a time for the service request.
* **Assigned To**: This is a dropdown menu that allows you to select who you would like the service to be assigned to from the options provided.
* **Assigned At**: This field is used to select a date and time when the service is assigned to you.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you provide accurate and complete information in all fields.
* If you have any issues or concerns while filling out this form, please reach out to the service provider for assistance.
