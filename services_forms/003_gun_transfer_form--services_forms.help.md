# Gun Transfer Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gun Transfer Form is used to document the transfer of firearms, ensuring accurate and secure recording of ownership and possession changes. This form is a crucial tool for law enforcement, firearms dealers, or other authorized personnel to maintain compliance with regulations and ensure public safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the person transferring the firearm.
2. Enter the name of the person receiving the firearm (transferor).
3. Select the relationship between the transferor and the person you are transferring the gun to.
4. Describe the reason for the transfer.
5. Enter the date and time of the transfer (optional).
6. Enter the transfer time zone (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the name of the person transferring the firearm.

### 2. **Transferor's Name** (`transferor_name`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the name of the person receiving the firearm.

### 3. **Relationship** (`relationship`, `select_one`, required: false)
Select how you know the person you are transferring the gun to:
	* First
	* Last
	* Family Member
	* Friend
	* Other

### 4. **Reason for Transfer** (`reason_for_transfer`, `text`, required: false)
Describe the reason for transferring the firearm.

### 5. **Transfer Date** (`transfer_date`, `date`, required: false)
Enter the date of the transfer (optional).

### 6. **Transfer Time** (`transfer_time`, `time`, required: false)
Enter the time of the transfer (optional).

### 7. **Transfer Time Zone** (`transfer_time_zone`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the time zone of the transfer (e.g., EST, CST, etc.).

### Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure accurate records.
- If you are unsure about any information, please ask for assistance.
- This form is for internal use only; do not share or distribute it without permission.
