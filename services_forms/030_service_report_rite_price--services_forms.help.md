# service_report_rite_price - Help Guide
## Purpose
The service report form is used to capture and report details about rite prices for services rendered. This form helps in maintaining accurate and up-to-date records, enabling efficient service tracking, billing, and reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill in the **Service Report Rite Price** title to provide a brief description of the service report.
- Select the **Type** of the rite from the options provided (1, 2, or 3) to categorize the service.
- Choose the **Location** where the service was provided, selecting one or more from the options (One, Two, or Three).
- Optionally, provide a note about **History** related to the service in the available text area.
- Enter the **Price** for the service in the designated numerical field.
- Enter the **Date** of service in the calendar format (DD/MM/YYYY).
- Select the **Time** of the service from the available options in the format (HH:MM AM/PM).
- Choose the **Assigned User** who performed the service from the options provided (chatjimmy or chatjimmy2).
- Select the **Service Report Status** from the options provided (1, 2, or 3).
- Optionally, add any **Comments** about the service in the text field.
- Finally, review your submission to ensure all fields are accurately filled before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Title**: This is the title of the service report. A brief description of the service report should be provided in this field.
- **Price**: This field is for recording the price of the rite service provided. Ensure it is a numerical value that accurately reflects the service's cost.
- **Type**: Select the type of rite service from the options provided to categorize the service.
- **Location**: Choose one or more locations where the service was provided.
- **History**: This field allows for any notes or observations about the service's history.
- **Comments**: Add any comments or remarks about the service in this text field.
- **Date**: Enter the date the service was provided using the calendar format (DD/MM/YYYY).
- **Time**: Select the time of service from the available options in the format (HH:MM AM/PM).
- **Assigned User**: Select the user assigned to perform the service from the options provided.
- **Service Report Status**: Choose the status of the service report from the options provided.
- **Notes**: This field is currently not in use in this form but is available for future development and customization. Please ignore it for now. 

Please note that this form is designed to be user-friendly and comprehensive, and each field has been explained in detail for your convenience.
