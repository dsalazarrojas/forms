# product_photography_briefing_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the product's photography needs and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have a clear understanding of the product's photography needs and requirements.
2. Fill out the form with the requested information, making sure to select the correct options for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. product_photography_briefing_form_1
* **product_photography_briefing_form_1_label** (text, required/optional): This field is for the product's title or name. Please enter the product's title in this field.

### 2. product_photography_briefing_form_2
* **product_photography_briefing_form_2_label** (select_one, required/optional): This field is for selecting the type of photography required for the product. Please choose one of the options: "product photography briefing form 2 label 1" or "product photography briefing form 2 label 2".

### 3. product_photography_briefing_form_3
* **product_photography_briefing_form_3_label** (date, required/optional): This field is for scheduling the photography shoot. Please enter the date for the shoot in this format: YYYY-MM-DD.

### 4. product_photography_briefing_form_4
* **product_photography_briefing_form_4_label** (time, required/optional): This field is for scheduling the start time of the photography shoot. Please enter the time in 24-hour format: HH:MM.

### 5. product_photography_briefing_form_5
* **product_photography_briefing_form_5_label** (note, required/optional): This field is for any additional notes or comments about the photography shoot. Please enter any relevant information here.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact the relevant team or contact person.
* This form is designed to be filled out once, and it should provide a comprehensive overview of the product's photography requirements.
