# Daycare Cleaning Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to be used by daycare centers to assess the cleanliness and hygiene of various areas within their facilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select the status for each area by choosing between "Yes" or "No" from the provided options.
* Make sure to answer honestly and thoroughly, as this form is designed to help identify areas that need improvement.
* If an area does not apply to your daycare center, select "No" for that particular field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Facility** (`daycare_facility_cleanliness`, select_one, required): Indicate the overall cleanliness of your facility. Choose "Yes" if your facility is clean and well-maintained, or "No" if it is not.
* **Bathroom Hygiene** (`bathroom_hyggiene`, select_multiple, required): Select "Yes" if your bathrooms are clean and well-maintained, including soap, toilet paper, and towels. Select "No" if they are not.
* **Kitchen Cleanliness** (`kitchen_cleanliness`, select_multiple, required): Choose "Yes" if your kitchen is clean and well-maintained, including appliances, counters, and sinks. Select "No" if it is not.
* **Outdoor Space Cleanliness** (`outdoor_space_cleanliness`, select_multiple, required): Select "Yes" if your outdoor spaces are clean and well-maintained, including playgrounds, parking areas, and walkways. Select "No" if they are not.
* **Laundry Facilities** (`laundry_facility_cleanliness`, select_multiple, required): Choose "Yes" if your laundry facilities are clean and well-maintained, including washers, dryers, and laundry areas. Select "No" if they are not.
* **Toy Area** (`toy_area_cleanliness`, select_multiple, required): Select "Yes" if your toy area is clean and well-maintained, including toys, furniture, and floors. Select "No" if it is not.
* **Office** (`office`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes" if your office area is clean and well-maintained, including desks, chairs, and files. Select "No" if it is not.
* **Dining Room** (`dining_room_cleanliness`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" if your dining room is clean and well-maintained, including tables, chairs, and appliances. Select "No" if it is not.
* **Outdoor Play Area** (`outdoor_play_area_cleanliness`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes" if your outdoor play area is clean and well-maintained, including playground equipment and play surfaces. Select "No" if it is not.
* **Storage Areas** (`storage_areas_cleanliness`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" if your storage areas are clean and well-maintained, including shelves, closets, and storage containers. Select "No" if they are not.

## Tips
* Remember to be thorough and honest when completing this form, as it will help you identify areas that need improvement.
* If you are unsure about a particular area, select "No" and make a note of the specific issues you need to address.
* Complete the form regularly to maintain a clean and safe environment for the children in your care.
