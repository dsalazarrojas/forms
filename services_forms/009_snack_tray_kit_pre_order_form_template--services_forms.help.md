# Snack Tray Kit Pre Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers pre-order a Snack Tray Kit, a convenient collection of snacks for special events or gatherings. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand what information is required.
2. Enter your details in the relevant fields.
3. Submit your pre-order request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Pre Order Details** (pre_order_details)
* This field is for customers to provide more information about their pre-order request. Enter a brief description of the Snack Tray Kit you are interested in, including the quantity and any special requests or preferences.

### 2. **Customer Information** (customer_info)
* This field is for customers to provide their contact information, including their name and email address. This is necessary for us to process your pre-order request and follow up with any updates or notifications.

### 3. **Payment Information** (payment_info)
* This field is for customers to provide payment details, including the method of payment and any other relevant payment information. Please ensure that you have the necessary payment information ready to complete this form accurately.

## Tips
* Please review each field carefully before submitting your pre-order request to ensure accurate and complete information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
