# Roefabulous, Products Are Natural Chemical Dye Free, Biohazard Safe Products With Amazing Essential - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help Roefabulous understand customer preferences for natural, chemical-dye-free, and biohazard-safe products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below for each field:

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **soy_wax_candle** (`soy_wax_candle`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your soy wax candle preferences. This field is optional.
* **product_choice** (`product_choice`, select multiple, optional): Select all the products you are interested in from the list below:
	+ Apple
	+ Lemon
	+ Green
	+ Pineapple
	+ Orange
* **scent_choise** (`scent_choise`, select one, optional): Choose your preferred scent:
	+ Peppermint
	+ Eucalyptus
	+ Lavender
	+ Vanilla
	+ Cinnamon
* **biohazard_safe** (`biohazard_safe`, select one, optional): Please indicate if the product is biohazard safe:
	+ 'True'
	+ 'False'
* **more_products** (`more_products`, note, optional): This field is for additional comments or requests.
* **description** (`description`, text, optional): Enter any additional description or details about the products you are interested in.

## Tips
Be sure to answer all questions to the best of your ability, and feel free to provide additional information in the "more_products" field.
