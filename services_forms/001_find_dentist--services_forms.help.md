# Find Dentist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users find and filter dentists based on their location, date of birth, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To find a dentist using this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Enter your search query in the "Search Query" field. This field is optional but helps narrow down your search results.
2. If you have more specific information, such as additional details or requirements, you can enter them in the "More Information" field.
3. Select your desired location from the "Location" field. This field is a list of cities across the United States.
4. If you have a specific date of birth, you can enter it in the "Date of Birth" field. This will help filter the search results to include only dentists who match this date range.
5. If you have a preferred dentist, you can select them from the "Dentist Information" field.
6. Finally, if you have a contact number for the dentist, you can enter it in the "Contact Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Search Query** (`search_query`, `text`, required: false): Enter any specific information you'd like to search for in the dentists' profiles.
* **More Information** (`search_query_2`, `text`, required: false): If you have more specific details about the dentist you're looking for, enter them here. This can include requirements or preferences.
* **Location** (`location`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select your desired location from the list of cities across the United States.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth to filter the search results based on age range.
* **Dentist Information** (`dentist_info`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the preferred dentist from the available options.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required: false): If you have the contact number of the dentist, enter it here.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out as many fields as you can to get more accurate search results.
* If you have any questions or issues, feel free to contact our support team.
* This form is designed to be user-friendly and efficient, so don't hesitate to explore and find the right dentist for your needs.
