# Medical Alert Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Medical Alert Form is used to collect and communicate medical alert information to the medical staff in a timely and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's information, including their name, contact information, and any relevant medical conditions.
2. Provide a brief description of the medical alert reason.
3. Select the alert level (High or Low) and specify the alert location.
4. Choose the contact person and their relationship to the patient (e.g., Emergency Contact, Family Member, etc.).
5. Specify the follow-up plan and contact information.
6. Choose the follow-up frequency and time.
7. Indicate whether medical staff should be notified and who is on the medical team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (1)** (`Patient Information`, text, required): Fill in the patient's name, contact information, and any relevant medical conditions.
* **Medical Alert Reason (2)** (`Medical Alert Reason`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the medical alert reason.
* **Medical Condition (3)** (`Medical Condition`, text, optional): Specify any relevant medical conditions the patient may be experiencing.
* **Contact Information (4)** (`Contact Information`, text, optional): Enter the contact person's name and contact information.
* **Contact Person (5)** (`Contact Person`, text, optional): Choose the contact person from the options listed (e.g., Emergency Contact, Family Member, etc.).
* **Alert Level (6)** (`Alert Level`, select_one, optional): Choose between High and Low alert levels.
* **Alert Location (7)** (`Alert Location`, text, optional): Specify the location where the alert needs to be communicated.
* **Alert Date (8)** (`Alert Date`, date, optional): Enter the date the alert needs to be communicated.
* **Medical Facility (9)** (`Medical Facility`, text, optional): Specify the medical facility where the alert needs to be communicated.
* **Emergency Contact (10)** (`Emergency Contact`, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact's information.
* **Contact Relationship (11)** (`Contact Relationship`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how the contact person is related to the patient (e.g., Yes, No, etc.).
* **Alert Details (12)** (`Alert Details`, text, optional): Provide any additional details about the alert.
* **Follow-up Plan (13)** (`Follow-up Plan`, text, optional): Specify the follow-up plan for the patient.
* **Follow-up Contact (14)** (`Follow-up Contact`, text, optional): Enter the contact information for follow-up.
* **Follow-up Frequency (15)** (`Follow-up Frequency`, select_one, optional): Choose whether follow-up is necessary (Yes or No).
* **Follow-up Time (16)** (`Follow-up Time`, time, optional): Enter the follow-up time.
* **Medical Staff (17)** (`Medical Staff`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether medical staff should be notified (Yes or No).
* **Medical Team (18)** (`Medical Team`, select_multiple, optional): Specify who is on the medical team.
