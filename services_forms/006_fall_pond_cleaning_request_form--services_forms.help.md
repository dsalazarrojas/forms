# Fall Pond Cleaning Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fall Pond Cleaning Request Form is a service request form for customers to submit their requests for pond cleaning services. The form is designed to collect necessary information to schedule and process the service request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your contact information (name, phone number, and email).
2. Provide your address so we can locate the pond to be cleaned.
3. Select the services you need (e.g., pond cleaning, pond aeration).
4. Add any comments or notes about the service request.
5. Choose who you would like to be assigned to for the service (ChatJimmy or Other).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information**: Enter your name, phone number, and email address so we can contact you to confirm your service request and provide updates.
* **Address**: Provide the address of the pond to be cleaned. This is the location of your pond.
* **Services Needed**: Select which services you require for your pond. You can choose one or multiple options, and the available services are: Pond Cleaning and Pond Aeration.
* **Comments**: If you have any additional information or notes about your service request, please add them here.
* **Date and Time**: Provide the date and time you would like the service to be performed.
* **Assigned To**: Choose who you would like to be assigned to for the service. You can choose ChatJimmy or Other.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled out completely and accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us before submitting the form.
* We will review your request and contact you to confirm the details.
