# Parking Permit Cancellation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to cancel a parking permit. Please provide accurate information to facilitate a smooth cancellation process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Parking Permits Cancellation**: Enter a brief description of the parking permit you wish to cancel.
2. **Parking Permit Number**: Provide the parking permit number issued to you. This information can be found on the original parking permit document.
3. **Issuer Name**: Enter the name of the person or organization that issued the parking permit.
4. **Reason for Cancellation**: Explain the reason for the cancellation in a few words or sentences.
5. **Date**: Enter the date you wish to cancel the parking permit.
6. **Date Optional**: This field is optional and should only be filled if the date of cancellation is different from the current date.
7. **Time**: Enter the time you wish to cancel the parking permit. If the time is not specified, you can leave this field blank.
8. **Time Optional**: This field is optional and should only be filled if the time of cancellation is different from the current time.
9. **Phone**: Enter your phone number where the issuer can contact you for further information.
10. **Email**: Enter your email address where the issuer can contact you for further information.
11. **Note**: Add any additional comments or information regarding the cancellation.
12. **Issuer Note**: Enter any notes from the issuer regarding the cancellation.
13. **Agree to Cancel**: Select 'Yes' to confirm you agree to cancel the parking permit.
14. **Agreed By**: Enter the name of the person agreeing to cancel the permit.
15. **Issuer Agreed By**: Enter the name of the person or organization issuing the cancellation agreement.
16. **Issuer Agreed By Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person or organization issuing the cancellation agreement.
17. **Issuer Agreed By Email**: Enter the email address of the person or organization issuing the cancellation agreement.
18. **Issuer Agreed By Note**: Enter any notes from the issuer regarding the agreed by.
19. **Agreed At**: Enter the date and time you agreed to cancel the parking permit.
20. **Issuer Agreed At**: Enter the date and time the issuer agreed to cancel the parking permit.
21. **Agreed At Time**: Enter the time you agreed to cancel the parking permit.
22. **Issuer Agreed At Time**: Enter the time the issuer agreed to cancel the parking permit.
23. **Agreed At Note**: Enter any notes regarding the agreed at.
24. **Issuer Agreed At Note**: Enter any notes from the issuer regarding the agreed at.
25. **Submit**: Enter your name and submit the form.
