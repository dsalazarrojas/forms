# Skid Steer Equipment Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information about the inspection of a skid steer equipment. The goal is to gather data on the inspection date, status, and result, as well as any issues that may have arisen during the inspection.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the inspection date from the calendar or enter it manually.
2. Choose the current inspection status from the dropdown menu.
3. Choose the inspection result from the dropdown menu.
4. Enter a description of the equipment's condition.
5. Provide any notes about the inspection.
6. List any issues that were identified during the inspection.
7. Describe the location of each issue.
8. Choose the severity of each issue.
9. Record the date of each issue.
10. Choose the status of each issue from the dropdown menu.
11. Choose the resolution date of each issue from the dropdown menu.
12. Select maintenance activities required for the equipment.
13. Choose maintenance frequency from the dropdown menu.
14. Enter any additional notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **inspection_date**: The date of the skid steer equipment inspection.
* **inspection_status**: The current status of the skid steer equipment inspection (e.g., "Complete", "Incomplete", " Pending").
* **inspection_result**: The outcome of the skid steer equipment inspection (e.g., "Pass", "Fail", "Needs Repair").
* **equipment_condition**: A brief description of the equipment's condition.
* **notes**: Additional comments or observations about the inspection.
* **issues**: A list of any issues identified during the inspection.
* **issue_location**: The location of each issue.
* **issue_severity**: The severity of each issue (e.g., "High", "Medium", "Low").
* **issue_date**: The date each issue was identified.
* **issue_status**: The status of each issue (e.g., "Open", "Closed", " Pending").
* **issue_resolution_date**: The date each issue was resolved.
* **issue_resolution_status**: The status of each issue resolution (e.g., "Resolved", "Reopened", " Pending").
* **maintenance_required**: A list of maintenance activities required for the equipment.
* **maintenance_frequency**: The frequency of maintenance required for the equipment.
* **notes**: Additional comments or observations about the equipment or inspection.
* **skid_steer_equipment_inspection_form_16** to **skid_steer_equipment_inspection_form_25**: These fields are for additional comments or notes about the equipment or inspection. They should be used to enter any relevant information not covered by other fields.
