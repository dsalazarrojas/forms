# inspection_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The inspection registration form is used to collect information from clients regarding their visits. This includes their details, contact information, and any notes or comments about their visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the client's details.
2.  Enter their email address.
3.  Enter their contact phone number.
4.  Select the price range for the service provided.
5.  Enter any additional notes or comments about the visit.
6.  Enter details about the service provided.
7.  Set a date for the inspection.
8.  Choose the time of the inspection.
9.  Assign a tool to the inspection, either "chatjimmy" or "Other".
10.  Decide whether a user is assigned to the inspection, either "Yes" or "No".
11.  Choose the group assigned to the inspection, either "admin", "manager", or "team".
12.  Set a date for the inspection to be assigned.
13.  Set a time for the inspection to be assigned.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details** (id: 1, text, required: false): Enter the client's details.
* **client name** (id: 2, text, required: false): Enter the client's name.
* **client email** (id: 3, email, required: false): Enter the client's email address.
* **contact phone** (id: 4, text, required: false): Enter the client's contact phone number.
* **price range** (id: 5, select one, required: false): Select the price range for the service provided. Options are $0-$500, $500-$1000, $1000-$2000, or more than $2000.
* **notes** (id: 6, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments about the visit.
* **service details** (id: 7, text, required: false): Enter details about the service provided.
* **inspection date** (id: 8, date, required: false): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **inspection time** (id: 9, time, required: false): Enter the time of the inspection.
* **assigned tool** (id: 10, select one, required: false): Assign a tool to the inspection, either "chatjimmy" or "Other".
* **assigned user** (id: 11, select one, required: false): Decide whether a user is assigned to the inspection, either "Yes" or "No".
* **assigned group** (id: 12, select one, required: false): Choose the group assigned to the inspection, either "admin", "manager", or "team".
* **assigned date** (id: 13, date, required: false): Set a date for the inspection to be assigned.
* **assigned time** (id: 14, time, required: false): Set a time for the inspection to be assigned.
