# Incident Complaint Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The incident complaint form template is a standardized form designed to collect and document information about incidents or complaints. It helps to ensure that all necessary details are captured and recorded in a structured and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Select the correct options from the provided dropdown lists.
3. Enter any additional details that are not covered in the standard fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **incident_complaint_form** (Text): Enter a brief description of the incident or complaint.
* **issue_description** (Text): Provide a detailed description of the issue or problem that occurred.
* **incident_date** (Date): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **incident_time** (Time): Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **incident_location** (Text): Specify the location where the incident took place.
* **incident_description** (Text): Enter a detailed description of the incident.
* **witness_info** (Text): Provide any relevant information about witnesses.
* **resolution_status** (Select One): Indicate if the resolution status is Active or Inactive.
* **resolution_details** (Text): Enter any additional details about the resolution.
* **action_taken** (Select Multiple): Check the actions taken in relation to the incident, such as Yes or No.
