# translator_availability_reminder_2 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record a translator's availability for a specific period. It helps us to track their capacity and schedule translations efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name in the `translator_name` field. This is the name of the translator.
2.  Enter the start date of your availability in the `start_date` field.
3.  Enter the end date of your availability in the `end_date` field.
4.  Enter the number of days you are available for translation work in each of the `availability_1`, `availability_2`, and `availability_3` fields.
5.  Enter the number of words you can translate per day in the `words_per_day` field.
6.  Optionally, enter any notes about your availability in the `notes` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **translator_name** (`translator_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as a translator.
* **start_date** (`availability_start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the start date of your availability period.
* **end_date** (`availability_end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the end date of your availability period.
* **availability_1** (`availability_1`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of days you are available for translation work (1-31).
* **availability_2** (`availability_2`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of days you are available for translation work (1-31).
* **availability_3** (`availability_3`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of days you are available for translation work (1-31).
* **words_per_day** (`words_per_day`, `number`, required): Enter the number of words you can translate per day.
* **notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes about your availability.
