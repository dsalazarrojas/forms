# Cleaning Client Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect essential information from clients to provide tailored cleaning services. By filling out this form, you will be able to:

* Provide accurate and efficient cleaning services
* Inform our team about any special requirements or allergies
* Ensure our team can access your property safely
* Schedule the best cleaning frequency and time for your needs
* Confirm your emergency contact information

Please carefully fill out this form to ensure we can provide excellent service.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start with the "Client Information" section, where you'll provide basic contact information such as First Name, Last Name, and Email Address.
2. Continue with the "Property Information" section, where you'll provide details about the property to be cleaned, including the Property Address, City, and Postal or ZIP Code.
3. Next, select the type of property and approximate Square Footage.
4. Move on to the "Service Preferences" section, where you'll choose the type of cleaning service you require (e.g., Regular Recurring, One-Time Deep Cleaning).
5. Schedule your cleaning frequency and preferred day and time of service.
6. If applicable, provide information about pets in the home and any special instructions regarding pets.
7. Confirm your emergency contact information.
8. Finally, accept our Terms and Conditions of service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`client_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`client_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your primary contact phone number.
* **Alternate Phone Number** (`alternate_phone`, text, optional): Provide an alternate contact phone number, if applicable.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): For identification purposes, enter your date of birth (not required for most services).
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, required): Enter the full street address of the property to be cleaned.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city or town where the property is located.
* **State or Province** (`state_province`, text, required): Enter the state or province where the property is located.
* **Postal or ZIP Code** (`postal_code`, text, required): Enter the postal or ZIP code of the property.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of property (e.g., Single Family Home, Apartment or Condo).
* **Approximate Square Footage** (`property_size`, select_one, required): Select the approximate square footage of the property.
* **Number of Bedrooms** (`number_of_bedrooms`, select_one, optional): If applicable, select the number of bedrooms in the property.
* **Number of Bathrooms** (`number_of_bathrooms`, select_one, optional): If applicable, select the number of bathrooms in the property.
* **Access Instructions** (`access_instructions`, text, required): Provide instructions on how our team should access the property (e.g., front door, garage).
* **Parking Information** (`parking_information`, text, optional): Provide information on where our team should park.
* **Service Type Needed** (`service_type_needed`, select_one, required): Select the type of cleaning service required (e.g., Regular Recurring, One-Time Deep Cleaning).
* **Cleaning Frequency** (`cleaning_frequency`, select_one, required): Select how often you want the cleaning service (e.g., Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly).
* **Preferred Day of Week** (`preferred_day`, select_multiple, required): Select the preferred day of the week for service.
* **Preferred Time of Day** (`preferred_time`, select_one, required): Select the preferred time of day for service.
* **Do You Have Pets** (`pets_in_home`, select_one, required): Indicate if there are pets in the home.
* **Pet Instructions** (`pet_instructions`, text, optional): Provide special instructions regarding pets.
* **Special Requirements or Requests** (`special_requirements`, text, optional): Enter any special cleaning requirements or requests.
* **Allergies or Chemical Sensitivities** (`allergies_or_sensitivities`, text, optional): Enter any allergies or chemical sensitivities that our team should be aware of.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_did_you_hear`, select_one, optional): Indicate how you learned about our services (e.g., Website, Social Media).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the emergency contact phone number.
* **Terms and Conditions Acceptance** (`terms_acceptance`, select_one, required): Confirm that you accept our terms and conditions of service.
