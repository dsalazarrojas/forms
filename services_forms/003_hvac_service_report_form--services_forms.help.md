# hvc_service_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report services performed by a company in the HVAC sector. It collects information about the service provided, including technician details, client information, and service details.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter the name of the technician who performed the service.
2.  Select the date of service from the calendar.
3.  Choose whether the system is heating or not.
4.  Choose whether the cooling system is present or not.
5.  Describe the status of the equipment.
6.  Describe the condition of the system.
7.  If applicable, enter the temperature setting for the system.
8.  Enter any additional notes about the service.
9.  Enter the email address of the technician.
10. Enter the phone number of the technician.
11. Enter the name of the assigned technician.
12. Enter the service address.
13. Enter the city of the service.
14. Select the state where the service was provided.
15. Enter the zip code of the service location.
16. Enter any comments about the service.
17. Select the date the service was completed.
18. Enter any notes from the technician.
19. Enter the name of the client.
20. Enter the email address of the client.
21. Enter the phone number of the client.
22. Enter any notes from the client.
23. Select the date the service was provided.
24. Enter the tool assigned to the technician.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Technician Name**: Enter the name of the technician who performed the service. This field is required.
*   **Date of Service**: Select the date the service was provided from the calendar. This field is required.
*   **Heating System**: Choose whether the heating system is present or not. This field is required.
*   **Cooling System**: Choose whether the cooling system is present or not. This field is required.
*   **Equipment Status**: Describe the status of the equipment. This field is required.
*   **System Condition**: Describe the condition of the system. This field is required.
*   **Temperature Setting**: If applicable, enter the temperature setting for the system. This field is not required.
*   **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the service. This field is not required.
*   **Email**: Enter the email address of the technician who performed the service. This field is required.
*   **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the technician who performed the service. This field is not required.
*   **Assigned Technician**: Enter the name of the technician assigned to the service. This field is required.
*   **Service Address**: Enter the address where the service was provided. This field is required.
*   **City**: Enter the city where the service was provided. This field is required.
*   **State**: Select the state where the service was provided. This field is not required.
*   **Zip**: Enter the zip code of the service location. This field is required.
*   **Comments**: Enter any comments about the service. This field is not required.
*   **Date Completed**: Select the date the service was completed from the calendar. This field is not required.
*   **Technician Notes**: Enter any notes from the technician. This field is not required.
*   **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client. This field is required.
*   **Client Email**: Enter the email address of the client. This field is required.
*   **Client Phone**: Enter the phone number of the client. This field is not required.
*   **Client Notes**: Enter any notes from the client. This field is not required.
*   **Service Date**: Select the date the service was provided. This field is not required.
*   **Assigned Tool**: Enter the tool assigned to the technician. This field is not required.
