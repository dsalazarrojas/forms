# forex_membership_signup_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Forex membership signup form is used to sign up for a Forex membership, requiring users to provide their personal and contact information, select a membership plan, choose their membership level, and verify their email address.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal information (Personal Info page).
2. Enter your contact details (Contact Info page).
3. Select a membership plan and level from the options on the Membership Plan page.
4. Enter a valid email address to be verified (Email field).
5. Enter your phone number (Phone field).
6. Verify your email address by confirming it in the Confirm Email field.
7. Click the Submit button to complete your membership signup.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Personal Info** (Personal Info, text, optional): This page collects your personal details, but it's not a required field.
- **Contact Info** (Contact Info, text, required): Please enter your contact details here.
- **Membership Plan** (Membership Plan, select_one, required): Choose a membership plan that suits your needs, which are Free, Premium, or Standard.
- **Membership Level** (Membership Level, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more membership levels that match your preferences, which are Basic, Advanced, or Premium.
- **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we'll use for communication.
- **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your phone number.
- **Confirm Email** (Confirm Email, email, required): Verify your email address by confirming it here.
- **Submit** (Submit, note, required): Click this button to complete your membership signup.

## Tips
- Ensure that you enter accurate and valid information in each field.
- If you encounter any issues, contact our support team for assistance.
- Double-check your email address for verification before submitting the form.
