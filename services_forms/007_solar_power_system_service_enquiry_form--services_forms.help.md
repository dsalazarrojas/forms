# Solar Power System Service Enquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Solar Power System Service Enquiry Form is a way for customers to get in touch with our team about their solar power system needs. This form is designed to collect essential information about the customer and their requirements, allowing us to provide accurate and timely services.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Customer Name** in the text field provided.
2. Enter your **Email Address** and **Phone Number** in the respective text fields.
3. Select your preferred **Service Type** from the dropdown menu. Our services include:
	* Installation
	* Maintenance
	* General Services
4. You may optionally provide more details about your installation in the **Installation Details** field.
5. If you have a specific solar power system in mind, please provide more information about it in the **Solar Power System Details** field.
6. Let us know your **Preferred Time** for the service in the time format (e.g., 08:00 or 08:00 AM).
7. Enter your **Enquiry Date** to schedule the service on a specific date.
8. You may add any additional **Enquiry Details** or comments about your service requirements.
9. Finally, type in your **Customer Message** to provide further information about your service needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (Required): Enter your full name as the customer representative.
* **Email Address** (Required): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (Required): Enter a valid phone number where we can reach you.
* **Service Type** (Optional): Select one of the services we offer: Installation, Maintenance, or General Services.
* **Installation Details** (Optional): Provide more information about your installation requirements.
* **Solar Power System Details** (Optional): Describe your solar power system in detail, including its make and model.
* **Preferred Time** (Optional): Enter a time of day when we can schedule the service (e.g., 08:00 or 08:00 AM).
* **Enquiry Date** (Optional): Enter the date you would like to schedule the service.
* **Enquiry Details** (Optional): Add any additional information or comments about your service requirements.
* **Customer Message** (Required): Provide any further information about your service needs.
