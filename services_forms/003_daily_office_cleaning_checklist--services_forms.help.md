# Daily Office Cleaning Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Daily Office Cleaning Checklist is a form designed to collect information about the daily office cleaning tasks and their completion status. It's intended to be completed by facility staff or designated personnel to maintain accuracy and transparency in office cleaning procedures.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, please make sure to have a clear understanding of the office locations, cleaning areas, and tasks that need to be completed.
*   Select the office location from the provided options (if any).
*   Choose all the areas that require cleaning for that location.
*   Select the tasks that were completed for each area.
*   Optionally, add any additional notes or comments about the cleaning process.
*   Enter your email address or phone number for reference (if necessary).
*   Select a date and time for submission.
*   Choose the person responsible for submitting this form (i.e., their name).
*   Finally, select who actually submitted this form (i.e., their name).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Office Location** (`office_location`, text, optional): This is the location where the cleaning tasks are being performed. Please select the correct office location from the provided options.
*   **Cleaning Areas** (`cleaning_areas`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the areas that require cleaning for the selected office location. This could include carpets, furniture, windows, and other areas that need attention.
*   **Tasks Completed** (`tasks_completed`, select_one, required): Select the tasks that were completed for each area. This helps to track progress and identify any areas that still require cleaning.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): If you want to add any additional comments or notes about the cleaning process, you can do so here.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you want to be contacted regarding this form, please provide your email address.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): If you prefer to be contacted via phone, please provide your phone number.
*   **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select the date on which the cleaning was completed.
*   **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select the time on which the cleaning was completed.
*   **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, select_one, required): This is the person responsible for the completion of the tasks, so please select their name.
*   **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, required): This is who actually submitted this form, so please select their name.
*   **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, date, optional): This indicates the date on which the form was submitted.
*   **Submitted Time** (`submitted_time`, time, optional): This indicates the time on which the form was submitted.

This form is designed to collect relevant information about office cleaning tasks and their completion status, making it easier to track progress and maintain a clean and organized work environment.
