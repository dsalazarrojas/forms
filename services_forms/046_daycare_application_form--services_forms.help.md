# Daycare Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a crucial part of our daycare application process. It captures the essential details about the parent and child, as well as their preferences and schedules. Please provide accurate and clear information to help us assess your eligibility for our daycare services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out the "Parent Information" page, which requests basic details about yourself and your contact information.
2. On the next page, provide details about your child, including their name, date of birth, and any relevant medical information.
3. The "Contact Information" page is where you can enter your address, phone number, and email.
4. Select your service preferences, such as Early Childhood Program, Preschool Program, or School Age Program.
5. Enter your availability for daycare services, including start and end dates.
6. Choose a schedule for your child's daycare, including the days and times they need to be picked up and dropped off.
7. Add any additional notes or comments that would be helpful for our staff to know.
8. Finally, enter your contact information, including your email and phone number.
9. Review your entire application carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Information** (`parent_info`, text, required: false): This field collects basic details about you, the parent or guardian of the child.
* **Child Information** (`child_info`, text, required: false): Please provide your child's name, date of birth, and any relevant medical information.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter your address, phone number, and email so we can contact you about your application.
* **Service Preferences** (`service_preferences`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of service you are applying for, such as Early Childhood Program or Preschool Program.
* **Availability** (`availability`, date, required: false): Enter your availability for daycare services, including start and end dates.
* **Schedule** (`schedule`, time, required: false): Choose a schedule that suits your needs, including the days and times your child will be picked up and dropped off.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional information or comments that would be helpful for our staff to know.
* **Contact** (`contact`, text, required: false): Enter your contact information, including your phone number and email.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your application.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required: false): Please enter your name or the name of the person submitting the application.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, date, required: false): Enter the date and time you submit this application.
