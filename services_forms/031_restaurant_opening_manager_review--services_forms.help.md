# Restaurant Opening Manager Review - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Restaurant Opening Manager Review form is used to evaluate and provide reviews for new restaurants that have recently opened. This form helps assess the performance and overall rating of the restaurant, as well as collect specific details like restaurant name, manager and employee IDs, and location.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the overall rating of the restaurant, selecting one of the provided options (Very poor, Poor, Average, Good, Excellent).
2. If you have any specific feedback or comments about the restaurant's performance, enter them in the Manager Notes field.
3. Enter the restaurant name, manager ID, and location ID in their respective fields.
4. Enter the dates related to the restaurant's opening and review, if available.
5. Click the "Submit" button to save your review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### New Opening Performance (id: 1)
* **New Opening Performance**: A note-taking field to capture any specific details or observations about the restaurant's opening performance.

### Overall Rating (id: 2)
* **Overall Rating**: This field allows you to rate the restaurant's overall performance using one of the following options:
	+ Very poor
	+ Poor
	+ Average
	+ Good
	+ Excellent

### Restaurant Name (id: 3)
* **Restaurant Name**: Enter the name of the restaurant being reviewed.

### Manager Notes (id: 4)
* **Manager Notes**: A text field for you to add any specific feedback or comments about the restaurant's performance.

### Opening Date (id: 5)
* **Opening Date**: Enter the date the restaurant opened.

### Closing Date (id: 6)
* **Closing Date**: Enter the date the restaurant closed (if applicable).

### Review Date (id: 7)
* **Review Date**: Enter the date of the review.

### Manager ID (id: 8)
* **Manager ID**: Enter the manager ID associated with the restaurant.

### Restaurant ID (id: 9)
* **Restaurant ID**: Enter the ID of the restaurant being reviewed.

### Location ID (id: 10)
* **Location ID**: Enter the ID of the location where the restaurant is situated.

## Tips

* Be thorough in your evaluation to provide an accurate assessment of the restaurant's performance.
* Ensure you are using the correct IDs associated with the restaurant and manager.
* Use the Manager Notes field to provide specific feedback and suggestions for improvement.
* Submit your review once you have completed all the required fields.
