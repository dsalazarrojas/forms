# Catering Equipment Report - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to record information about equipment maintenance, repair, and other services performed on behalf of a customer. The form captures details about the equipment being serviced, the service performed, and the outcome of the service. The information collected will be used to track and analyze the maintenance history of equipment, enabling efficient resource allocation and planning.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Select the correct options for fields with dropdown or check boxes.
3. Use the text fields to provide detailed descriptions when necessary.
4. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Engineer Name**: Enter the name of the service engineer who performed the service.
* **Engineer ID**: Enter the employee identification number of the service engineer.
* **Report Date**: Enter the date when the service report was created or updated.
* **Site Name**: Enter the location where the service was performed.
* **Site Address**: Enter the complete address of the location.
* **Site Contact Person**: Enter the name and contact information of the person at the location.
* **Site Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the site contact person.
* **Order or Reference Number**: Enter the job or order reference number.
* **Equipment Category**: Select the type of equipment being serviced.
* **Equipment Name**: Enter the specific equipment being serviced.
* **Equipment Model**: Enter the model number of the equipment.
* **Equipment Serial Number**: Enter the serial number of the equipment.
* **Service Type**: Select the type of service performed (e.g., installation, maintenance, repair).
* **Arrival Time**: Enter the time of arrival at the site.
* **Completion Time**: Enter the time when the service was completed.
* **Work Performed**: Provide a detailed description of the work done.
* **Parts Used**: List any parts replaced or used during the service.
* **Parts Cost**: Enter the cost of any parts used.
* **Labor Hours**: Enter the total hours spent on the service.
* **Equipment Status After Service**: Select the status of the equipment after the service.
* **Recommendations**: Any recommendations or follow-up actions.
* **Follow-up Needed**: Indicate if additional service is required.
* **Recommended Follow-up Date**: Enter the date when to return for follow-up service.
* **Customer Satisfaction**: Rate the customer satisfaction level.
* **Engineer Signature Date**: Enter the date of the engineer's signature.
* **Customer Signature Date**: Enter the date of the customer's signature.
* **Additional Notes**: Any other relevant information.

Note: The form is designed to capture all relevant information about the service performed. Please make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accuracy and completeness of the report.
