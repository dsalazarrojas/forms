# Fencing Permit Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a fencing permit application. It helps us to understand your property details and provide a permit for your fencing needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the **User Input** section (page 1).
2. Enter your property address in the **Property Address** section (page 2).
3. Indicate the size of your property in the **Property Size** section (page 3).
4. Select the type of permit you need from the **Permit Type** dropdown menu (page 4).
5. If required, enter the start and end dates for your permit in the **Start Date** and **End Date** sections (pages 5 and 6).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (page 1, required: false)
	+ **User Input**: Enter your personal details, such as your name and contact information.
* **Property Address** (page 2, required: false)
	+ **Property Address**: Enter the address of your property, including house number and street name.
* **Property Size** (page 3, required: false)
	+ **Property Size**: Enter the size of your property in square meters or feet.
* **Permit Type** (page 4, required: true)
	+ **Permit Type**: Choose the type of permit you need for your fencing project. This will be either:
		- **Permit A**: For residential property owners.
		- **Permit B**: For commercial property owners.
		- **Permit C**: For agricultural property owners.
* **Start Date** (page 5, required: false)
	+ **Start Date**: Enter the start date for your permit if required.
* **End Date** (page 6, required: false)
	+ **End Date**: Enter the end date for your permit if required.

## Tips

* Ensure you provide accurate information for all fields to avoid delays or errors in processing your permit application.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, please don't hesitate to contact us.
