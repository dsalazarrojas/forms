# Municipality Self Analysis And Transition Plan Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for municipalities to submit their self-analysis and transition plans. It gathers information about their infrastructure, contacts, and other necessary details to support their application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the application form with your municipality's unique details.
2. Provide your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
3. Upload or describe the documents required for your application.
4. Select the types of infrastructure relevant to your application.
5. Describe the conditions of your infrastructure.
6. Identify the authority in your municipality responsible for your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Application Form**: A text field where you can provide a brief description of your municipality's self-analysis and transition plan.
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name, which will be used as the primary contact point for communication.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address, which will be used for communication and correspondence.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number, which will be used for additional contact and communication.
* **Documents**: Upload or describe the documents required for your application, such as plans, reports, or other relevant supporting materials.
* **Infrastructure Types**: Select the types of infrastructure relevant to your application from the options provided.
* **Infrastructure Conditions**: Describe the conditions of your infrastructure, such as current state, planned upgrades, or other relevant details.
* **Municipality Authority**: Identify the authority in your municipality responsible for your application, which could be a department, agency, or other relevant entity.
