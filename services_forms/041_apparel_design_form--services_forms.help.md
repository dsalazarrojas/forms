# Apparel Design Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for the apparel design project. It is intended for clients to provide their details, design requirements, and project specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Select the type of apparel or item from the dropdown menu for "Type of Apparel or Item".
3. Enter a brief description of the design concept in the "Design Concept and Description" field.
4. Choose the preferred material or fabric for the project.
5. Select the preferred printing or production method from the dropdown menu.
6. Enter the approximate size distribution and any other details that may be relevant.
7. Set the required delivery date for the project.
8. Enter the estimated budget for the project, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Name** (*): Enter the name of the person or company contacting us.
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company or organization associated with this project (if applicable).
* **Email Address** (*): Enter the email address of the contact person or company.
* **Type of Apparel or Item** (*): Select the type of apparel or item that the design is for, such as T-Shirt, Hoodie, or Hat.
* **Design Concept and Description** (*): Briefly describe the design concept and any details that are relevant to the project.
* **Preferred Material / Fabric**: Enter the preferred material or fabric for the project (e.g., 100% cotton, tri-blend, or vinyl for banners).
* **Garment or Base Color** (*): Enter the base color of the apparel or item.
* **Approximate Size Distribution**: Enter the approximate size distribution of the project (e.g., 10S-20M-10L).
* **Preferred Printing / Production Method** (*): Select the preferred printing or production method, such as screen printing or embroidery.
* **Number of Colors in Design**: Enter the number of colors used in the design.
* **Required Delivery Date** (*): Enter the date by which the project needs to be delivered.
* **Estimated Budget**: Enter the estimated budget for the project (if applicable).
