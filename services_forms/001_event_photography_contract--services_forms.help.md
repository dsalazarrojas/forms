# Event Photography Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create and manage event photography contracts, ensuring a clear understanding between the client and photographer of their responsibilities and expectations. The form is divided into several sections, each collecting specific information necessary for a smooth and successful event experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  **Title**: This is the name of the contract, which should be a descriptive title of the event.
2.  **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client, who is the event organizer or host.
3.  **Event Details**: Provide a brief description of the event, including its date, location, and any other relevant details.
4.  **Photographer Name**: Enter the name of the photographer assigned to cover the event.
5.  **Contract Date**: Select the date the contract was signed.
6.  **Contract Terms**: Describe the terms of the contract, including responsibilities, expectations, and any specific requirements.
7.  **Contract Status**: Choose the current status of the contract, which can be Draft, Active, or Cancelled.
8.  **Client Signature**: The client must sign this field to confirm they have read and agreed to the contract terms.
9.  **Photographer Signature**: The photographer must sign this field to confirm they have read and agreed to the contract terms.
10.  **Client Notes**: Any additional notes or comments from the client.
11.  **Photographer Notes**: Any additional notes or comments from the photographer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Event Photography Contract** (`event_photography_contract`, `text`, required): A descriptive title of the event being photographed.
*   **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required): The name of the event organizer or host.
*   **Event Details** (`event_details`, `text`, required): A brief description of the event, including its date, location, and any other relevant details.
*   **Photographer Name** (`photographer_name`, `text`, required): The name of the photographer assigned to cover the event.
*   **Contract Date** (`contract_date`, `date`, required): The date the contract was signed.
*   **Contract Terms** (`contract_terms`, `text`, required): A description of the contract, including responsibilities, expectations, and any specific requirements.
*   **Contract Status** (`contract_status`, `select_one`, required): The current status of the contract, which can be Draft, Active, or Cancelled.
*   **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, `text`, required): The client must sign this field to confirm they have read and agreed to the contract terms.
*   **Photographer Signature** (`photographer_signature`, `text`, required): The photographer must sign this field to confirm they have read and agreed to the contract terms.
*   **Client Notes** (`client_notes`, `text`, optional): Any additional notes or comments from the client.
*   **Photographer Notes** (`photographer_notes`, `text`, optional): Any additional notes or comments from the photographer.
