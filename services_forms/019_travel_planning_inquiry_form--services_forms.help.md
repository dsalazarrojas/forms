# travel_planning_inquiry_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for customers to express their interest in travel planning services. It will help us understand their needs and provide a personalized experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all that apply from the "travel_planning_inquiry" section.
2. Provide your travel services interest in the "travel_planning_services" field.
3. Enter your travel date in the "travel_planning_date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **travel_planning_inquiry**:
	+ Select all that apply (Yes, No): This field is a multiple-choice selection where you can choose whether or not you are interested in travel planning services.
* **travel_planning_services**:
	+ Text Field: This field is for you to describe the type of travel planning services you are interested in.
* **travel_planning_date**:
	+ Date: This field is for you to enter the date you plan to travel. Please use the date format MM/DD/YYYY.
