# Customer Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Satisfaction Survey is a form designed to gather feedback from customers to help us better understand their experience with our services, products, and website. This form is meant to be a quick and easy way for customers to share their thoughts and suggestions, so we can make informed decisions about how to improve.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each question carefully and think about your experience with our services, products, and website.
2. Choose a response for each question based on your honest feedback.
3. Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have for us.

* **What do you think about our products**:
	+ This is the place to describe your feelings about our products. Please tell us about your likes, dislikes, and any areas where we could improve.
* **What do you think about our services**:
	+ This is the place to describe your feelings about our services. Please tell us about your likes, dislikes, and any areas where we could improve.
* **What do you think about our website**:

* **How would you rate the quality of our service**:
	+ This question asks you to rate the quality of our service on a scale (e.g., 1-5). Please choose a number that best represents your experience.
* **How would you rate the quality of our product**:
	+ This question asks you to rate the quality of our product on a scale (e.g., 1-5). Please choose a number that best represents your experience.
* **Do you have any suggestions for us?**:
	+ This is the place to provide any suggestions or ideas you have for us to improve our services, products, and website.
* **Do you have any comments?**:
	+ This is the place to add any additional thoughts or comments you have about your experience with our services, products, and website.
