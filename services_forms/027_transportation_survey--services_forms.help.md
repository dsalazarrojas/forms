# Transportation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Transportation Survey is designed to collect information about your transportation habits, preferences, and priorities. This will help us better understand your needs and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Read the introduction to understand the context and purpose of the survey.
2.  Select all applicable transportation methods that you use.
3.  Choose your preferred transportation method.
4.  Answer questions about the frequency and importance of transportation in different contexts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Transportation Methods** (`transportation_methods`, `select_multiple`): Select all the transportation methods you use, such as public transportation or private vehicles.
*   **Preferred Transportation Method** (`preferred_transportation_method`, `select_one`): Choose one method you prefer.
*   **How Often Do You Use Public Transportation?** (`how_often_use_public_transportation`, `select_one`): Select how often you use public transportation.
*   **What is Your Usual Daily Travel Distance?** (`travel_distance`, `number`): Enter the distance you usually travel each day.
*   **How Important Is Transportation to You?** (`how_important_transportation_to_you`, `select_one`): Select how important transportation is to you.
*   **How Important Is Transportation to Your Friends?** (`how_important_transportation_to_friends`, `select_one`): Select how important transportation is to your friends.
*   **How Important Is Transportation to Work?** (`how_important_transportation_to_work`, `select_one`): Select how important transportation is to your work.
*   **How Important Is Transportation to School?** (`how_important_transportation_to_school`, `select_one`): Select how important transportation is to your school.
*   **Other** (`other`, `text`): If none of the above options apply to you, please describe your transportation methods and priorities.
