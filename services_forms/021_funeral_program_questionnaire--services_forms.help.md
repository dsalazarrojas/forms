# Funeral Program Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a funeral program. It is a collection of questions that will help you gather the necessary details to create a well-planned and organized funeral program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read through each question carefully and answer accordingly.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed, as indicated by the "required" label.
* You can skip fields that are not required.
* You can save your progress and return to the form later if needed.
* Make sure to fill in all the necessary fields to provide a complete answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal info** (personal_info_section, note, required/optional): This section is used to gather personal information about the individual or group that the funeral program is for. Please fill in the name, address, phone number, and email of the person in charge of the funeral program.
* **Program details** (program_details, note, required/optional): This section is used to collect information about the funeral program. Please provide as much detail as possible about the program, including its purpose, date, time, location, and any special instructions or requests.
* **Contact info** (contact_info, note, required/optional): This section is used to collect contact information for the funeral program. Please provide the name, phone number, and email of the person to contact for any questions or concerns. 

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the designated contact person.
* If you need to make changes or updates, you can return to the form and revise your answers as needed.
