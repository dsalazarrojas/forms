# Shift Close - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Shift Close form is used to record and report information related to sales shifts, including daily sales, total sales, start and end times, inventory details, refunds, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant page from the drop-down menu.
2. Fill in the required and optional fields as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Daily Sales (1) (`daily_sales`, number, optional)
Enter the daily sales figures for the shift.

### Sales Total (2) (`sales_total`, number, optional)
Enter the total sales for the shift, including any applicable discounts or promotions.

### Shift Start (3) (`shift_start`, date, optional)
Enter the start time of the shift.

### Shift End (4) (`shift_end`, time, optional)
Enter the end time of the shift.

### Inventory Details (5) (`inventory_details`, text, optional)
Enter any relevant details about inventory levels or stock during the shift.

### Refunds (6) (`refunds`, number, optional)
Enter any refunds made during the shift.

### Notes (7) (`notes`, note, optional)
Add any additional notes or comments about the shift.

### Employee Name (8) (`employee_name`, text, optional)
Enter the name of the employee working on the shift.

### Employee ID (9) (`employee_id`, number, optional)
Enter the employee's ID number.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all relevant fields accurately and honestly.
- If you're unsure about any field, try to use your best judgment.
- Review your entries before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
