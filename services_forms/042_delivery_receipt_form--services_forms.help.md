# Delivery Receipt Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Delivery Receipt Form is a tool for tracking delivery-related activities. It collects information from the customer, delivery details, and payment information to ensure efficient processing of delivery receipts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the **Delivery Receipt** field with a brief description of the delivery receipt.
2. Provide the customer's **Customer Name** in the respective field.
3. Enter the customer's **Customer Address** in the field provided.
4. Select the **Delivery Date** of the delivery receipt from the date picker or enter it manually.
5. Describe the **Item Description** of the delivery in the field provided.
6. Enter the **Quantity** of items delivered.
7. Enter the **Unit Price** of each item.
8. Calculate the **Amount per Item** by multiplying the quantity by the unit price.
9. In the **Delivery Details** field, you can provide any additional information related to the delivery.
10. Enter the **Receiver Name** of the delivery recipient.
11. Enter the **Delivery Address** where the delivery was made.
12. Enter the **Tax Percentage** rate applied to the delivery (if applicable).
13. Calculate the **Subtotal** by multiplying the amount per item by the quantity.
14. Finally, enter the **Total Amount** for the entire delivery.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Delivery Receipt** (`delivery_receipt_form_title`): A brief description of the delivery receipt.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`): The name of the person or entity receiving the delivery.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`): The address of the customer where the delivery was made.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`): The date of the delivery.
* **Item Description** (`delivery_details_item_description`): A description of the items delivered.
* **Quantity** (`delivery_details_quantity`): The number of items delivered.
* **Unit Price** (`delivery_details_unit_price`): The price per unit of the delivered items.
* **Amount per Item** (`delivery_details_amount_per_item`): The total amount for each item (quantity x unit price).
* **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details_matrix_table`): Any additional information related to the delivery.
* **Receiver Name** (`receiver_name`): The name of the person or entity receiving the delivery.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`): The address where the delivery was made.
* **Tax Percentage** (`tax_percentage`): The tax percentage rate applied to the delivery (if applicable).
* **Subtotal** (`subtotal`): The total amount before tax.
* **Total Amount** (`total_amount`): The final total amount including tax (if applicable).

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate processing of the delivery receipt.
* If you're unsure about any information, refer to your delivery records or contact your delivery team for assistance.
* Double-check your calculations for the subtotal and total amount.
