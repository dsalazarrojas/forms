# Advanced Facial Treatments And Chemical Peels Client Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from clients before undergoing advanced facial treatments and chemical peels. It helps ensure a safe and smooth treatment experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your legal full name.
2. Enter your date of birth.
3. Provide your contact phone number for appointment reminders.
4. Enter your email address for communication.
5. List any current medications.
6. Select any existing skin conditions.
7. Confirm whether you have experienced any adverse reactions to skincare treatments.
8. Confirm your pregnancy status.
9. Select the treatment type you are receiving.
10. Choose the treatment area.
11. Select the depth of the chemical peel (if applicable).
12. Acknowledge potential risks associated with the treatment.
13. Confirm understanding of common risks.
14. Confirm understanding of rare risks.
15. Give consent to proceed with the treatment.
16. Sign and date the consent form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Full Name** (Client Full Name, text, required): Enter your legal full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required): Provide your date of birth for medical consideration.
* **Contact Number** (Contact Number, text, required): Enter a phone number where you can be reached for appointment reminders.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, required): Enter your email address for communication with our team.
* **Current Medications** (Current Medications, text, optional): If you are taking any medications, list them here.
* **Skin Conditions** (Skin Conditions, select_multiple, required): Select any existing skin conditions you have.
* **Previous Adverse Reactions** (Previous Adverse Reactions, select_one, required): Confirm if you have experienced any adverse reactions to skincare treatments.
* **Pregnancy Status** (Pregnancy Status, select_one, required): Confirm your pregnancy status.
* **Treatment Type** (Treatment Type, select_one, required): Select the type of treatment you are receiving.
* **Treatment Area** (Treatment Area, select_one, required): Choose the area to be treated.
* **Peel Depth** (Peel Depth, select_one, required): If applicable, select the depth of the chemical peel.
* **Risk Acknowledgment** (Risk Acknowledgment, select_one, required): Acknowledge the potential risks associated with the treatment.
* **Common Risks Acknowledged** (Common Risks Acknowledged, select_one, required): Confirm understanding of common risks (redness, peeling, sensitivity).
* **Rare Risks Acknowledged** (Rare Risks Acknowledged, select_one, required): Confirm understanding of rare risks (scarring, infection, hyperpigmentation).
* **Consent to Proceed** (Consent to Proceed, select_one, required): Give consent to proceed with the treatment.
* **Signature Date** (Signature Date, date, required): Date and sign this consent form to confirm your consent.
