# Favorite Food Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about a person's preferences regarding their favorite foods. This survey aims to help us better understand your tastes and interests.

## How To Complete This Form

1. The form consists of two sections:
	* The first section, "Favorite Food Survey", allows you to select which foods you enjoy (you can choose multiple options).
	* The second section, "Other Food", is for you to provide any other foods that you like.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Favorite Food Survey** (`select_multiple`, required: false):
	* This field allows you to select which foods you enjoy. You can choose one or multiple options from the list.
* **Favorite Food** (`select_one`, required: false):
	* This field asks you to choose a single food that you particularly enjoy.
* **Other Food** (`text`, required: false):
	* This field is for you to enter any other food that you like that is not listed in the "Favorite Food Survey" section.
* **Other Food** (`text`, required: false):
	* If you mentioned a specific food in the "Other Food" field, you can elaborate on it in detail here.
