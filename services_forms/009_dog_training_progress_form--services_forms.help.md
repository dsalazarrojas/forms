# Dog Training Progress Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track the progress of dog training sessions. It captures essential information about the dog's owner, dog's details, and training session specifics. This data will help trainers and administrators evaluate progress, identify areas of improvement, and provide a platform for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields (if any) and provide as much detail as possible for the optional fields.
2.  Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
3.  Save your work frequently to avoid losing any input.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Dog Training Progress Form** (1)
*   **Dog Training Progress Form** (`dog_training_progress_form`, text, optional)
    *   This field is the title of the form, which is already pre-filled.

### 2. **Dog Owner Info** (2)
*   **Dog Owner Info** (`dog_owner_info`, text, optional)
    *   Enter the dog owner's name and contact information.

### 3. **Dog Info** (3)
*   **Dog Info** (`dog_info`, select_one, optional)
    *   Choose the appropriate category from:
        *   **Puppy**
        *   **Adult**
        *   **Senior**

### 4. **Training Date** (4)
*   **Training Date** (`training_date`, date, optional)
    *   Enter the date of the training session in the required format (yyyy-mm-dd).

### 5. **Training Time** (5)
*   **Training Time** (`training_time`, time, optional)
    *   Enter the time of the training session in the required format (HH:MM).

### 6. **Training Duration** (6)
*   **Training Duration** (`training_duration`, number, optional)
    *   Enter the length of the training session in minutes.

### 7. **Trainer Info** (7)
*   **Trainer Info** (`trainer_info`, text, optional)
    *   Enter the trainer's name and any additional information.

### 8. **Notes** (8)
*   **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional)
    *   Add any notes about the training session.

### 9. **Dog’s Name** (9)
*   **Dog’s Name** (`dog_name`, text, optional)
    *   Enter the dog's name.

### 10. **Dog’s Breed** (10)
*   **Dog’s Breed** (`dog_breed`, text, optional)
    *   Enter the dog's breed.

### 11. **Training Location** (11)
*   **Training Location** (`training_location`, text, optional)
    *   Enter the location where the training session took place.

### 12. **Training Level** (12)
*   **Training Level** (`training_level`, select_multiple, optional)
    *   Choose the level of difficulty from:
        *   **Beginner**
        *   **Intermediate**
        *   **Advanced**

### 13. **Training Outcome** (13)
*   **Training Outcome** (`training_outcome`, text, optional)
    *   Enter a brief description of the training outcome.

### 14. **Notes 2** (14)
*   **Notes 2** (`notes_2`, note, optional)
    *   Add any additional notes about the training session.

### 15. **Trainer’s Name** (15)
*   **Trainer’s Name** (`trainer_name`, text, optional)
    *   Enter the trainer's name.

### 16. **Contact Number** (16)
*   **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional)
    *   Enter the trainer's contact number.

### 17. **Email** (17)
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional)
    *   Enter the trainer's email address.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by the trainer after each training session. The data collected will help evaluate progress and provide valuable feedback for future trainings.
