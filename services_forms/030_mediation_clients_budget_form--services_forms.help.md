# mediation_clients_budget_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about a mediation client's financial situation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Review the form carefully and answer each question truthfully.
2. For fields with a "select" type, choose the option that best describes your situation.
3. For fields with a "number" type, enter a numerical value (e.g., dollars).
4. For fields with a "text" type, provide a brief description of your financial obligations.
5. If a question does not apply to you, simply skip it and move on.
