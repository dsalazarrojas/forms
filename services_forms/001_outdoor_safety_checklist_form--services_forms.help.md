# Outdoor Safety Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Outdoor Safety Checklist Form is a simple text-based checklist designed to help users prepare for outdoor activities. It's a quick and easy way to ensure that you've covered all essential safety items before heading out.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Open the form and start from the first item.
2. For each item, type the name of the safety measure or task you've completed.
3. If you've completed the task, type "done" in the corresponding text field.
4. If you haven't completed the task, describe why it's not applicable or what you need to do next.
5. Repeat this process for all 25 checklist items.
6. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Checklist 1** (checklist_1): List the type of clothing you're wearing for the activity (e.g., hat, gloves, boots).
* **Checklist 2** (checklist_2): Describe your plan for staying hydrated during the activity (e.g., bring water, drink regularly).
* **Checklist 3** (checklist_3): Mention your strategy for dealing with emergencies, such as having a first aid kit or a means of communication.
* **Checklist 4** (checklist_4): Specify your emergency contact information, including phone numbers and names.
* **Checklist 5** (checklist_5): Describe your plan for navigating, including a map or GPS coordinates.
* **Checklist 6** (checklist_6): List any medications you're taking, including their names and dosages.
* **Checklist 7** (checklist_7): Mention any health conditions that may affect your safety during the activity.
* **Checklist 8** (checklist_8): Describe your plan for dealing with wildlife, such as bears or other animals.
* **Checklist 9** (checklist_9): Specify your plan for finding shelter in case of bad weather.
* **Checklist 10** (checklist_10): List any essential items you're bringing, such as a first aid kit or a knife.
* **Checklist 11** (checklist_11): Describe your plan for staying warm or cool during the activity.
* **Checklist 12** (checklist_12): Specify your plan for dealing with water sources, such as rivers or lakes.
* **Checklist 13** (checklist_13): Mention any potential hazards, such as steep slopes or loose rocks.
* **Checklist 14** (checklist_14): Describe your plan for using navigation tools, such as a compass or GPS.
* **Checklist 15** (checklist_15): List any necessary permits or permissions for the activity.
* **Checklist 16** (checklist_16): Specify your plan for staying on designated trails.
* **Checklist 17** (checklist_17): Describe your plan for dealing with inclement weather, such as snow or extreme heat.
* **Checklist 18** (checklist_18): Mention any support resources, such as phone numbers or locations.
* **Checklist 19** (checklist_19): Specify your plan for dealing with altitude sickness.
* **Checklist 20** (checklist_20): Describe your plan for staying visible, such as using headlamps or flashlights.
* **Checklist 21** (checklist_21): List any necessary equipment, such as bear spray or a fire starter.
* **Checklist 22** (checklist_22): Specify your plan for dealing with unexpected events, such as an injury or lost person.
* **Checklist 23** (checklist_23): Mention any local regulations or rules for the activity.
* **Checklist 24** (checklist_24): Describe your plan for staying informed, such as checking the weather forecast.
* **Checklist 25** (checklist_25): Specify your plan for exiting the activity area, including your expected return time.

Note: I've assumed the "type: text" fields are meant to hold brief descriptions or answers to the questions, and the "required: false" fields are not necessarily meant to be required but rather provide options for users to fill out if relevant.
