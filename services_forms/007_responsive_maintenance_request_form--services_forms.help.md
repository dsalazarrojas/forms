# Responsive Maintenance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Responsive Maintenance Request Form is used to request maintenance for equipment, machines, or other assets that require repair, replacement, or adjustment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details (Name, Email, and Phone).
2. Provide a brief description of the issue or problem you are experiencing.
3. Choose the priority level (Low, Medium, or High) that best describes the urgency of the maintenance request.
4. Select the category (Maintenance Request or Other) that best describes the type of maintenance request.
5. Enter the date and start and end times for the maintenance request.
6. Enter the location where the maintenance request is needed.
7. Click the "Submit" button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification or business card.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address where the maintenance team can contact you.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number where the maintenance team can reach you.
* **Date** (date, required): Enter the date for the maintenance request.
* **Start Time** (time, required): Enter the start time for the maintenance request.
* **End Time** (time, required): Enter the end time for the maintenance request.
* **Description** (text, optional): Provide a brief description of the issue or problem you are experiencing.
* **Priority** (select_one, required): Choose the priority level that best describes the urgency of the maintenance request (Low, Medium, or High).
* **Category** (select_multiple, required): Choose the category that best describes the type of maintenance request (Maintenance Request or Other).
* **Location** (text, required): Enter the location where the maintenance request is needed.
* **Comments** (text, optional): Add any additional information or comments about the maintenance request.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when describing the issue or problem.
* Choose the priority level that accurately reflects the urgency of the maintenance request.
* Make sure to enter accurate contact information so the maintenance team can reach you.
* If you have any additional information or comments, use the comments field to provide more context.
