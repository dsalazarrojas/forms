# moving_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "moving_form" is a form used to collect information about a moving, including its description, address, type, square footage, moving date, weight, phone number, email, and any additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the fields as prompted. Each field is described below.
2. Ensure you have filled out all the required fields (indicated by a "required" label).
3. Review your answers for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Moving Description** (`moving_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the moving.
* **Address** (`moving_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the item being moved.
* **Type** (`moving_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of moving, such as Local, Long Distance, or International.
* **Square Footage** (`square_footage`, number, optional): Enter the square footage of the item being moved.
* **Moving Date** (`moving_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the moving.
* **Weight** (`moving_weight`, number, optional): Enter the weight of the item being moved.
* **Phone Number** (`moving_phone`, text, optional): Enter a phone number for further contact.
* **Email** (`moving_email`, email, optional): Enter an email address for further contact.
* **Additional Notes** (`moving_note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the moving.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible in your answers.
* Ensure you have filled out all required fields.
* If you're unsure about a field, refer to the provided explanations above for guidance.
