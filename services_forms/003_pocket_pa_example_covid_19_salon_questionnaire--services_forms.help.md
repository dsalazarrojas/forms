# pocket_pa_example_covid_19_salon_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

The pocket pa example COVID-19 salon questionnaire is a form designed to gather essential information from customers visiting medical facilities during the COVID-19 pandemic. This form helps medical staff to understand the customers' contact information, medical history, and other relevant details for providing better services and taking necessary precautions.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling out the contact information section: "Contact Information", "Name", "Email", "Phone", and "Date of Visit" are all required fields. Please make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for these fields.
2.  In the "Symptoms" section, select all the symptoms that apply to your current condition. If you have any other symptoms not listed, you can describe them in the "Other Symptoms" field.
3.  If you have any medical history or allergies, please describe them in the "Medical History", "Allergies", "Medical Condition", and "Other Medical Condition" fields.
4.  In the "Emergency Contact" section, provide the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
5.  Select "Yes" or "No" for "Emergency Contact Relationship" and "Medical Provider" as per your situation.
6.  Describe the reason for your medical visit in the "Medical Visit Reason" field.
7.  If you have any specific instructions or notes from your previous medical visit, type them in the "Medical Visit Notes" field.
8.  In the "Follow-up" section, select "Yes" or "No" as per your follow-up needs, and if you want to specify any other follow-up requirements, use the "Other Follow-up" field.
9.  Finally, provide the time of your next follow-up visit in the "Follow-up Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, required): Please enter your name, email, and phone number. This information will help us to contact you for follow-ups or any other necessary communication.
* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required): Type your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you for any updates or information.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number where we can reach you.
* **Date of Visit** (`Date of Visit`, `date`, required): Type the date you plan to visit us.
* **Time of Visit** (`Time of Visit`, `time`, required): Type the time you plan to visit us.
* **Symptoms** (`Symptoms`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the symptoms you are experiencing from the list. If you have any other symptoms, please describe them in the "Other Symptoms" field.
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, `text`, optional): If you have any medical history, please describe it here.
* **Allergies** (`Allergies`, `text`, optional): If you have any allergies, please describe them here.
* **Medical Condition** (`Medical Condition`, `text`, optional): If you have any medical conditions, please describe them here.
* **Other Medical Condition** (`Other Medical Condition`, `text`, optional): If you have any other medical conditions, please describe them here.
* **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, `text`, optional): Please provide the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`Emergency Contact Relationship`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the relationship with your emergency contact (e.g., spouse, parent, friend).
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`Emergency Contact Phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Other Medical History** (`Other Medical History`, `text`, optional): If you have any other medical history, please describe it here.
* **Other Symptoms** (`Other Symptoms`, `text`, optional): If you have any other symptoms, please describe them here.
* **Time of Call** (`Time of Call`, `time`, optional): Type the time you will be calling us back.
* **Medical Provider** (`Medical Provider`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" if you are currently seeing a medical provider.
* **Other Medical Provider** (`Other Medical Provider`, `text`, optional): If your medical provider is not listed, please describe them here.
* **Medical Visit Reason** (`Medical Visit Reason`, `text`, optional): If you have any specific reason for your medical visit, please describe it here.
* **Medical Visit Notes** (`Medical Visit Notes`, `text`, optional): If you have any specific instructions or notes from your previous medical visit, please type them here.
* **Follow-up** (`Follow-up`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" if you need any follow-up after your visit.
* **Other Follow-up** (`Other Follow-up`, `text`, optional): If you need any other follow-up requirements, please describe them here.
* **Follow-up Time** (`Follow-up Time`, `time`, optional): If you have any follow-up requirements, please type the time you plan to follow up in this field.

**Tips**

* Please ensure that you fill out all the required fields to help us provide better services.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* This form is designed to help us understand your situation better, so please be as accurate and detailed as possible.
