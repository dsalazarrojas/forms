# covid_19_waiver_linda_mitchell - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form "covid_19_waiver_linda_mitchell" is designed to collect information from individuals or institutions regarding their experience with massage therapy services, their Covid-19 status, and other relevant details. This data will help gather insights on the impact of massage therapy on health outcomes during the Covid-19 pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select the appropriate option for each field that best describes your experience or situation.
2.  Enter your details accurately and truthfully in each field.
3.  Review your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Massage Therapy** (`massage_therapy`, text, required: false)
*   Describe your experience or knowledge about massage therapy.

### **Hands On Services** (`hands_on_services`, text, required: false)
*   List any hands-on services you have experienced or received, if any.

### **Covid 19 Status** (`covid_19_status`, select_multiple, required: false)
*   Indicate your current Covid-19 status:
    *   **True**: You are currently experiencing Covid-19.
    *   **False**: You are not currently experiencing Covid-19.
    *   **Unknown**: You are unsure or have not been tested.

### **Date of Symptoms** (`date_of_symptoms`, date, required: false)
*   Enter the date you started experiencing symptoms.

### **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, text, required: false)
*   Provide the name of your treating doctor, if any.

### **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, text, required: false)
*   Enter the name of your insurance provider, if applicable.

### **Signature** (`signature`, text, required: false)
*   Sign your name in the signature field.

### **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, required: false)
*   Enter the date you signed this form.

### **Time Signed** (`time_signed`, time, required: false)
*   Enter the time you signed this form.

### **Time Zone** (`time_zone`, time, required: false)
*   Enter the time zone you are in.

**Tips:**

*   Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
*   Review your submission carefully before submitting it.
*   If you have any questions or need help, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
