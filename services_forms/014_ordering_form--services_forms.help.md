> # Ordering Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers for an ordering process, specifically for an online ordering system. It's intended to gather details about the products being ordered, customer contact information, order status, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required") and the optional fields you feel are necessary.
2. Enter the product number in the "Product Number" field.
3. Provide the product name in the "Product Name" field.
4. Enter the order number in the "Order Number" field.
5. Enter the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
6. Enter the customer's email address in the "Customer Email" field. Please note that this is a required field.
7. Enter the customer's phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
8. Select the order date in the "Order Date" field using the calendar icon.
9. Select the order status from the options provided in the "Order Status" field.
10. Enter the order total in the "Order Total" field.
11. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
12. Select the payment method used from the options provided in the "Payment Method" field.
12. Enter any assigned tool information in the "Assigned Tool" field.
13. Finally, enter the form ID in the "Form ID" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Number** (text): Enter the product number as required.
* **Product Name** (text): Enter the product name as required.
* **Order Number** (text): Enter the order number as required.
* **Customer Name** (text): Enter the customer's name as required.
* **Customer Email** (text): Enter the customer's email address as required.
* **Customer Phone** (text): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Order Date** (date): Select the order date using the calendar icon.
* **Order Status** (select one): Select the order status from the provided options.
* **Order Total** (number): Enter the order total.
* **Order Total Hint** (text): Enter any additional notes or comments about the order total.
* **Payment Method** (select one): Select the payment method used from the options provided.
* **Notes** (text): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Assigned Tool** (text): Enter any assigned tool information.
* **Form ID** (text): Enter the form ID.

## Tips

* Please enter all required fields to ensure that the form is completed accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field label or this help guide for clarification.
* If you need to add any additional comments or notes, use the "Notes" field.
* Remember to enter a valid email address in the "Customer Email" field.
* If you have any questions or issues, contact our support team for assistance.
