# Health & Fitness Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and details from individuals seeking health and fitness services or support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief title or description of your inquiry or request.
2. Provide your first name.
3. Enter your last name.
4. Enter your contact number (phone or mobile).
5. Enter your email address.
6. Select a date for your inquiry or request (if applicable).
7. Choose the area of focus for your inquiry or request (health, fitness, or physical).
8. Provide any additional details about your request or inquiry.
9. Select either "Submit" or "Cancel" to finalize your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **health_fitness_contact_form** (`form_title`, text, false): Enter a brief title or description of your inquiry or request.
* **first_name** (`first_name`, text, false): Provide your first name.
* **last_name** (`last_name`, text, false): Enter your last name.
* **contact_number** (`contact_number`, text, false): Enter your contact number (phone or mobile).
* **email** (`email`, email, false): Enter your email address.
* **date** (`date`, date, false): Select a date for your inquiry or request (if applicable).
* **advisor** (`advisor`, select_one, false): Choose the area of focus for your inquiry or request (health, fitness, or physical).
* **problematic_area** (`problematic_area`, select_multiple, true): Select the area(s) you are concerned about (health, fitness, or physical).
* **details** (`details`, note, false): Provide any additional details about your request or inquiry.
* **submit** (`submit`, select_one, true): Select "Submit" to finalize your submission, or "Cancel" to discard.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
- If you need help or clarification on any field, contact the organization's support team.
