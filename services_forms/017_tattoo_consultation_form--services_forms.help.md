# tattoo_consultation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tattoo Consultation Form is designed to provide a comprehensive platform for tattoo artists to consult with clients about their tattoo needs. This form helps collect essential information from clients, including their contact details, preferences, and requirements, to ensure a smooth and successful tattoo session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form title, which should describe the consultation in a concise and clear manner.
2. Enter the website URL where the consultation will take place (if applicable).
3. Customize the form as needed for your specific use case (if required).
4. Create a Trello board and card for the consultation (if required).
5. Configure Zapier integration settings (if required).
6. Set up Google Calendar integration settings (if required).
7. Select the appointment list type (New, Consulting, or Done).
8. Add a description to the Trello card (if required).
9. Decide whether to move the Trello card to the appointment list (if required).
10. Book a consultation time slot (if required).
