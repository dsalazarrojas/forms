# puppy_training_purchase_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from clients or customers regarding their puppy training requests, including contact and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the purchase request field with the relevant details, which is a number field.
2. Select one or multiple options for the training schedule field, depending on the client's needs.
3. Enter the payment details, which is a number field.
4. Provide contact information, including email address, in the contact information field.
5. Optionally, add client comments in the client comments field, which is a text area.
6. Select "Yes" or "No" for the pet details field, depending on whether the client has a pet.
7. Choose the payment terms, which can be payable upon completion of training or upon initial consultation.
8. Enter the trainer's information in the trainer information field.
9. Select a number for the business logo field.
10. Choose the contact person, which can be the owner, trainer, or other.
11. Enter the client's phone number in the client phone field.
12. Provide the client's address in the client address field.
13. Select the payment status, which can be pending, completed, or declined.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Purchase Request (Purchase Request)** (`purchase_request`, number, required: false): Enter the request details as a number.
* **Training Schedule (Training Schedule)** (`training_schedule`, select multiple, required: false): Select one or multiple options for the client's training schedule.
* **Payment Details (Payment Details)** (`payment_details`, number, required: false): Enter the payment details as a number.
* **Contact Information (Contact Information)** (`contact_information`, email, required: false): Enter the client's contact information, including email address.
* **Client Comments (Client Comments)** (`client_comments`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about the client.
* **Pet Details (Pet Details)** (`pet_details`, select one, required: false): Select "Yes" or "No" depending on whether the client has a pet.
* **Payment Terms (Payment Terms)** (`payment_terms`, select one, required: false): Choose the payment terms, which can be payable upon completion of training or upon initial consultation.
* **Trainer Information (Trainer Information)** (`trainer_info`, text, required: false): Enter the trainer's information.
* **Business Logo (Business Logo)** (`business_logo`, number, required: false): Select a number for the business logo.
* **Contact Person (Contact Person)** (`contact_person`, select one, required: false): Choose the contact person, which can be the owner, trainer, or other.
* **Client Phone (Client Phone)** (`client_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Client Address (Client Address)** (`client_address`, text, required: false): Provide the client's address.
* **Payment Status (Payment Status)** (`payment_status`, select one, required: false): Select the payment status, which can be pending, completed, or declined.
