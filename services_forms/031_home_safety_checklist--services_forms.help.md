# home_safety_checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Home Safety Checklist is a form designed to assess the safety features and certification of a property. It is used to collect information about the address, safety features, and contact details of the property owner or contact person.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the address of the property.
2. Select the number of electrical outlets in the property.
3. Select the type of plumbing fixtures installed in the property (e.g., Kitchen, Bathroom, or Laundry Room).
4. Specify the type of heating system used in the property.
5. Specify the type of cooling system used in the property.
6. Select the type of fire system used in the property (e.g., Fire Sprinkler, Fire Alarm, or Fire Extinguisher).
7. Select any safety certifications or standards applicable to the property (e.g., UL, NFPA, or ASME).
8. Enter the last date and time of inspection, if applicable.
9. Select the name of the staff member assigned to the property.
10. Enter any additional notes or comments about the property.
11. Enter the contact email and phone number of the property owner or contact person.
12. Enter the name and address of the contact person.
13. Enter the contact city and state of the contact person.
14. Enter the contact zip and country of the contact person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the address of the property.
* **Home Size** (`home_size`, `number`, `optional`): Enter the number of bedrooms in the property.
* **Electrical Outlets** (`electrical_outlets`, `select_multiple`, `optional`): Select the number of electrical outlets in the property (e.g., 1, 2, or 3).
* **Plumbing Fixtures** (`plumbing_fixtures`, `select_one`, `optional`): Select the type of plumbing fixtures installed in the property (e.g., Kitchen, Bathroom, or Laundry Room).
* **Heating System** (`heating_system`, `text`, `optional`): Specify the type of heating system used in the property (e.g., natural gas, electricity, or oil).
* **Cooling System** (`cooling_system`, `text`, `optional`): Specify the type of cooling system used in the property (e.g., air conditioning, central air, or window units).
* **Fire System** (`fire_system`, `select_one`, `optional`): Select the type of fire system used in the property (e.g., Fire Sprinkler, Fire Alarm, or Fire Extinguisher).
* **Safety Certification** (`safety_certification`, `select_multiple`, `optional`): Select any safety certifications or standards applicable to the property (e.g., UL, NFPA, or ASME).
* **Last Inspection Date** (`last_inspection_date`, `date`, `optional`): Enter the last date of inspection, if applicable.
* **Last Inspection Time** (`last_inspection_time`, `time`, `optional`): Enter the last time of inspection, if applicable.
* **Assigned Staff** (`assigned_staff`, `select_one`, `optional`): Select the name of the staff member assigned to the property (e.g., Chatjimmy, John, or Jane).
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, `optional`): Enter any additional notes or comments about the property.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, `optional`): Enter the contact email of the property owner or contact person.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the contact phone number of the property owner or contact person.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, `optional`): Enter any additional comments about the property.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the signature of the person completing the form.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the email of the emergency contact person.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the address of the emergency contact person.
* **Contact City** (`contact_city`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the city of the contact person.
* **Contact State** (`contact_state`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the state of the contact person.
* **Contact Zip** (`contact_zip`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the zip code of the contact person.
* **Contact Country** (`contact_country`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the country of the contact person.
