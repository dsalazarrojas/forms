# Mastercraft Homewrights Pool Inspection - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of filling out the Mastercraft Homewrights Pool Inspection form.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully read each field explanation and fill out the form accordingly.
- Fill out all required fields to ensure accurate reporting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pool Location** (`pool_location`, text, Required: false): Enter the pool's location in the property, e.g., address or location description.
* **Pool Features** (`pool_features`, select_multiple, Required: false): Select all relevant features of the pool, such as Spa, Pool, or Water Feature. This field allows multiple selections.
* **Inspection Results** (`inspection_results`, note, Required: false): Provide a detailed description of the inspection results, including any notable findings or comments.
* **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, text, Required: false): Enter the name of the inspector who conducted the inspection.
* **Inspector Phone** (`inspector_phone`, text, Required: false): Enter the phone number of the inspector.
* **Inspector Email** (`inspector_email`, email, Required: false): Enter the email address of the inspector.
* **Realtor Name** (`realtor_name`, text, Required: false): Enter the name of the realtor handling the property listing.

## Tips
- Be thorough when describing the pool location to ensure accurate mapping.
- If the inspector is not the same as the realtor, ensure to fill out both fields correctly.
- Use proper formatting for the inspector's phone number and email address.
- Fill out the form according to your knowledge and understanding of the inspection process.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy.
