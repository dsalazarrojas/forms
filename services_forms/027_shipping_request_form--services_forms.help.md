# Shipping Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Shipping Request Form is designed to collect information about shipping requests from customers. It's used to gather details about shipping methods, contact information, and any special instructions for the shipping process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Shipping Details**: Enter a brief description of your shipping request, including any relevant product or package information.
2. **Shipping Method**: Choose a shipping method that suits your needs, or type a custom option if it's not listed.
3. **Contact Info**: Provide your contact name and email for us to get in touch with you regarding your shipping request.
4. **Special Instructions**: Add any specific instructions or notes about your shipping preferences.
5. **Additional Info**: If there's anything else you'd like to share or mention about your shipping request, please use this field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_request_form_page_1`, text, required): A brief description of your shipping request, including relevant product or package information.
* **Shipping Method** (`shipping_request_form_page_2`, text, required): A shipping method that suits your needs. You can select an option or type a custom method.
* **Contact Info** (`shipping_request_form_page_3`, text, required): Your contact name and email for us to get in touch with you.
* **Special Instructions** (`shipping_request_form_page_4`, text, optional): Any specific instructions or notes about your shipping preferences.
* **Additional Info** (`shipping_request_form_page_5`, text, optional): If there's anything else you'd like to share or mention about your shipping request.

## Tips

* Please ensure you provide accurate and clear information in each field to help us process your shipping request efficiently.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us at form@gic.mx.
* If you need to submit the same shipping request multiple times, please refrain from doing so to avoid confusion and duplication of efforts.
