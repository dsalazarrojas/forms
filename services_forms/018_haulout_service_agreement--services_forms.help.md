# haulout_service_agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The haulout_service_agreement form is used to document the service agreement for a haulout, including details about the vessel, haulout location, date, and services required. This form helps ensure that all parties involved are clear on the terms and conditions of the haulout service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the form heading in the **Form Heading** field.
2. Select the type of vessel from the **What Type of Vessel** field.
3. Enter the length of the vessel in the **Vessel Length** field.
4. Select the draft of the vessel from the **Draft** field.
5. Enter the width of the vessel in the **Vessel Width** field.
6. Enter the draft displacement of the vessel in the **Vessel Draft Displacement** field.
7. Enter the location of the haulout in the **Haulout Location** field.
8. Enter the date of the haulout in the **Haulout Date** field.
9. Select the duration of the haulout in the **Haulout Duration** field.
10. Enter the rate of the haulout in the **Haulout Rate** field.
11. Select the haulout services required from the **Haulout Services** field.
12. Enter any additional comments in the **Haulout Comments** field.
13. Enter the name of the contact person in the **Contact Name** field.
14. Enter the email of the contact person in the **Contact E-mail** field.
15. Enter the phone number of the contact person in the **Contact Phone** field.
16. Enter the address of the contact person in the **Contact Address** field.
17. Enter any additional services required in the **Additional Services** field.
18. Select whether the service agreement is applicable or not.
19. Select the status of the vessel from the **Vessel Status** field.
20. Select the status of the haulout from the **Haulout Status** field.
21. Enter any comments about the haulout in the **Haulout Comments** field.
22. Select the type of vessel from the **Vessel Type** field.
23. Enter the width of the vessel in the **Vessel Width** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Heading** (`form_heading`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the form.
* **What Type of Vessel** (`vessel_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of vessel being hauled out, such as Boat, Yacht, Sailboat, Ship, or Vessel.
* **Vessel Length** (`vessel_length`, number, required): Enter the length of the vessel.
* **Draft** (`vessel_draft`, select_multiple, required): Select the draft of the vessel from the options provided.
* **Vessel Width** (`vessel_width`, number, required): Enter the width of the vessel.
* **Vessel Draft Displacement** (`vessel_draft_displacement`, number, required): Enter the draft displacement of the vessel.
* **Haulout Location** (`haulout_location`, text, required): Enter the location where the haulout will take place.
* **Haulout Date** (`haulout_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the haulout.
* **Haulout Time** (`haulout_time`, time, required): Select the time of the haulout.
* **Haulout Duration** (`haulout_duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of the haulout.
* **Haulout Rate** (`haulout_rate`, number, required): Enter the rate of the haulout.
* **Haulout Services** (`haulout_services`, select_multiple, required): Select the services required for the haulout, such as Haulout and Launching, Haulout and Launching and Painting, etc.
* **Haulout Comments** (`haulout_comments`, note, required): Enter any comments about the haulout.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact E-mail** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the contact person.
* **Additional Services** (`additional_services`, text, required): Enter any additional services required.
* **Haulout Service Agreement** (`haulout_service_agreement`, select_one, required): Select whether the service agreement is applicable or not.
* **Vessel Status** (`vessel_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the vessel.
* **Haulout Status** (`haulout_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the haulout.
* **Haulout Comments** (`haulout_comments`, note, required): Enter any comments about the haulout.
* **Vessel Type** (`vessel_type_2`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of vessel.
* **Vessel Width** (`vessel_width_2`, number, required): Enter the width of the vessel.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information for each field.
* Check the options provided for each select fields.
* Use the **Form Heading** field to clearly describe the form.
* Ensure that the contact information is accurate and complete.
* Review the **Haulout Comments** field for any special notes or comments about the haulout.
* Check the **Vessel Status** field for accurate information about the vessel.
* Verify that the **Haulout Status** field is accurately selected.
* Check the **Haulout Services** field for required services.
* Ensure that the **Haulout Rate** field is accurately selected.
