# Vacation Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers about their vacation plans in order to provide a quote for their stay. Please fill out this form if you are a customer looking for a quote for your vacation plans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the requested information and submit the form when you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any relevant information about yourself or your group that we should know when providing a quote.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): In this field, please provide any contact information for the person we should reach out to for further questions or to confirm the quote.
* **Vacation Details** (`vacation_details`, text, optional): Describe the type of vacation you are planning, including dates, destinations, and any other relevant details.
* **Travel Preferences** (`travel_preferences`, text, optional): Indicate any specific travel preferences or requirements you have, such as dietary restrictions or special equipment needs.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): If there are any special requests you'd like to make regarding your stay, such as early check-in or late check-out, please list them here.
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, number, optional): If you're traveling with a group, please indicate how many people will be staying with you.
* **Room Preferences** (`room_preferences`, text, optional): Specify any specific room preferences or requests you have, such as a room with a view or a king-size bed.
* **Transportation Requests** (`transportation_requests`, text, optional): If you need transportation to or from the destination, describe your requirements here.

## Tips
* Please fill out each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible to ensure we can provide the best possible quote for your stay.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us directly.
