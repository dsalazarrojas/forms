# GhostHouse Haunted Tours - Help Guide
## Purpose
The GhostHouse Haunted Tours form is a tool to collect information from customers to provide a customized and successful tour experience. Please fill out the form below to reserve your tour.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select a date for the tour from the calendar provided.
2. Choose the time you would like to begin the tour.
3. Enter the number of guests that will be attending the tour.
4. Enter your contact email so we can reach out to you with any further details.
5. Enter your contact phone number so we can call you if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tour Date** (`tour_date`, date, required: false): Choose the date for the tour from the calendar provided.
* **Tour Time** (`tour_time`, time, required: false): Select the time you would like to begin the tour.
* **Number of Guests** (`num_guests`, number, required: false): Enter the number of guests that will be attending the tour.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter your contact email so we can reach out to you with any further details.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your contact phone number so we can call you if needed.
