# Billing Address Shipping Request Changes - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to update customer billing and shipping information. Customers can use this form to make changes to their billing and shipping addresses, including their names, addresses, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields with your current account information:
    * `Account Number`: Your customer account number.
    * `Email Address`: Email associated with your account.
    * `Phone Number`: Contact phone number.
2.  Select the type of change you would like to make:
    * `Type of Change`: Select `Billing Address`, `Shipping Address`, `Both Addresses`, `Billing Name`, or `Contact Information`.
3.  Enter the new information for the selected type of change:
    * `New Billing Name`: New name for billing address (if selecting `Billing Address` or `Both Addresses`).
    * `New Billing Address`: New street address for billing (if selecting `Billing Address` or `Both Addresses`).
    * `New Billing City`: City for billing address (if selecting `Billing Address` or `Both Addresses`).
    * `New Billing State`: State or province for billing address (if selecting `Billing Address` or `Both Addresses`).
    * `New Billing ZIP/Postal Code`: Billing area postal code (if selecting `Billing Address` or `Both Addresses`).
    * `New Billing Country`: Country for billing address (if selecting `Billing Address` or `Both Addresses`).
    * `New Shipping Name`: New name for shipping recipient (if selecting `Shipping Address` or `Both Addresses`).
    * `New Shipping Address`: New street address for shipping (if selecting `Shipping Address` or `Both Addresses`).
    * `New Shipping City`: City for shipping address (if selecting `Shipping Address` or `Both Addresses`).
    * `New Shipping State`: State or province for shipping address (if selecting `Shipping Address` or `Both Addresses`).
    * `New Shipping ZIP/Postal Code`: Shipping area postal code (if selecting `Shipping Address` or `Both Addresses`).
    * `New Shipping Country`: Country for shipping address (if selecting `Shipping Address` or `Both Addresses`).
4.  Confirm the changes:
    * `I confirm the above information is accurate`: Check to verify the information is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Account Number (required)**: Your customer account number.
* **Email Address (required)**: Email associated with your account.
* **Phone Number (required)**: Contact phone number.
* **Type of Change (required)**: Select what information you want to update:
    + `Billing Address`: Update billing address.
    + `Shipping Address`: Update shipping address.
    + `Both Addresses`: Update both billing and shipping addresses.
    + `Billing Name`: Update billing name.
    + `Contact Information`: Update contact information.
* **New Billing Name (optional)**: New name for billing address.
* **New Billing Address (optional)**: New street address for billing.
* **New Billing City (optional)**: City for billing address.
* **New Billing State (optional)**: State or province for billing address.
* **New Billing ZIP/Postal Code (optional)**: Billing area postal code.
* **New Billing Country (optional)**: Country for billing address.
* **New Shipping Name (optional)**: New name for shipping recipient.
* **New Shipping Address (optional)**: New street address for shipping.
* **New Shipping City (optional)**: City for shipping address.
* **New Shipping State (optional)**: State or province for shipping address.
* **New Shipping ZIP/Postal Code (optional)**: Shipping area postal code.
* **New Shipping Country (optional)**: Country for shipping address.
* **Effective Date for Changes (optional)**: When should these changes take effect.
* **I confirm the above information is accurate (required)**: Check to verify information is correct.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your changes are processed correctly.
* Double-check the information you enter to avoid errors.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask for assistance from our customer support team.
