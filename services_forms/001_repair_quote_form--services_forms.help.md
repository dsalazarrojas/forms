# Repair Quote Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Repair Quote Form is used by customers to request and obtain a quote for repair services. This form gathers information about the customer's repair needs and provides an opportunity for the customer to communicate their requirements and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Repair Quote Form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required information, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2.  Provide a detailed description of the repair work needed in the **Description** field.
3.  Enter the cost of the repair in the **Cost** field.
4.  Select the start and end dates of the repair work in the **Start Date** and **End Date** fields respectively. If the end date is not applicable, please leave this field blank.
5.  Add any additional notes or comments in the **Notes** field.
6.  Enter your email address in the **Customer Email** field, so we can contact you about your repair quote.
7.  Enter your phone number in the **Customer Phone** field, in case we need to contact you.
8.  Enter your address in the **Customer Address** field, so we can send you the quote and any necessary documentation.
9.  Select whether you **Agreed** to the repair quote in the **Work Agreed** field.
10. Enter a description of the repair work in the **Work Description** field.
11. Enter the price of the repair work in the **Work Price** field.
12. Add any additional notes or comments in the **Work Notes** field.
13. Sign the form with your signature in the **Customer Signature** field to confirm your agreement.
14. Lastly, the **Technician Signature** field should be signed by a representative from our company to confirm we received your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **What do you need repaired** (1): This is the main description of the repair work you need done.
*   **Description** (2): Use this field to provide a detailed explanation of the issue you want us to repair.
*   **Cost** (3): Enter the cost of the repair work.
*   **Start Date** (4): Select the date when the repair work should start.
*   **End Date** (5): Select the date when the repair work should finish. If not applicable, leave this field blank.
*   **Notes** (6): Add any additional comments or notes you'd like to share with us.
*   **Customer Email** (7): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your repair quote.
*   **Customer Phone** (8): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
*   **Customer Address** (9): Enter your address so we can send you the quote and any necessary documentation.
*   **Work Agreed** (10): Select whether you agree to the repair quote.
*   **Work Description** (11): Enter a description of the repair work.
*   **Work Price** (12): Enter the price of the repair work.
*   **Work Notes** (13): Add any additional notes or comments about the repair work.
*   **Customer Signature** (14): Sign the form with your signature to confirm your agreement.
*   **Technician Signature** (15): This field should be signed by a representative from our company to confirm we received your agreement.
