# Equipment Maintenance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Equipment Maintenance Request Form is used to submit a request for equipment maintenance. To complete this form, follow the steps below.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Submit Equipment Maintenance Request**: Enter a unique identifier for the maintenance request, which will be used to track the status of your request.
2. **Equipment Type**: Select the type of equipment that requires maintenance. Please choose the correct option from the list of available equipment types.
3. **Equipment Description**: Provide a brief description of the equipment that needs maintenance.
4. **Maintenance Requester**: Choose the person or team submitting the request from the options provided.
5. **Maintenance Request Date**: Enter the date when the maintenance request is being submitted.
6. **Maintenance Request Time**: Enter the time when the maintenance request is being submitted.
7. **Maintenance Requester Email**: Enter the email address of the person or team submitting the request.
8. **Maintenance Requester Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person or team submitting the request.
9. **Maintenance Requester Note**: If necessary, enter any additional notes or details about the maintenance request.
10. **Submit Request**: Click the "Submit Request" button to send in the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submit Equipment Maintenance Request**: Enter a unique identifier for the maintenance request.
* **Equipment Type**: Choose the type of equipment that requires maintenance. This will help us track the correct type of maintenance needed.
* **Equipment Description**: Give us a brief description of the equipment that needs maintenance.
* **Maintenance Requester**: Select the person or team submitting the request from the provided options.
* **Maintenance Request Date**: Enter the date the maintenance request is being submitted.
* **Maintenance Request Time**: Enter the time the maintenance request is being submitted.
* **Maintenance Requester Email**: Enter the email address of the person or team submitting the request.
* **Maintenance Requester Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person or team submitting the request.
* **Maintenance Requester Note**: If additional notes or details are necessary, enter them here.
* **Submit Request**: Click the "Submit Request" button to send in the form.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled accurately and completely.
* Review your submission carefully before submitting the form.
* If you need to submit multiple maintenance requests, use a unique identifier for each request.
* If you have any doubts or questions about the form, contact your supervisor or maintenance team for assistance.
