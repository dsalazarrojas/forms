# Smoke Alarm Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for smoke alarm authorizations, ensuring that all necessary details are captured for maintenance or inspection purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the property owner's name in the **Property Owner** field.
2. Provide the building's address in the **Building Address** field.
3. Enter the contact email for the property owner or authorized representative in the **Contact Email** field.
4. Select the type of smoke alarm from the drop-down list in the **Smoke Alarm Type** field.
5. Enter the location of the smoke alarm in the **Smoke Alarm Location** field.
6. (Optional) Enter the date of authorization in the **Authorization Date** field.
7. If applicable, enter the smoke alarm's serial number in the **Smoke Alarm Serial Number** field.
8. (Optional) Attach any relevant certificates or documentation related to the smoke alarm in the **Smoke Alarm Certificate** field.
9. If additional information is required, provide it in the **Additional Comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Owner**: Enter the name of the person responsible for the property or facility.
* **Building Address**: Provide the full address of the building or facility.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the property owner or authorized representative for contact purposes.
* **Smoke Alarm Type**: Choose the type of smoke alarm from the provided drop-down list (e.g., Hard Wired 1-10).
* **Smoke Alarm Location**: Enter the location of the smoke alarm within the building or facility.
* **Authorization Date**: If authorization has been previously granted, enter the date of the initial authorization. (Optional)
* **Smoke Alarm Serial Number**: If applicable, enter the serial number of the smoke alarm.
* **Smoke Alarm Certificate**: Attach any relevant documentation or certificates for the smoke alarm. (Optional)
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional information that may be helpful for the smoke alarm authorization process.
