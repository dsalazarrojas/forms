# Passenger Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from passengers for a waiver of liability, which is a legal document that releases an individual or entity from any liability for damages or injuries. This form is intended to be used by passengers, airlines, or travel agencies to gather essential details about the passenger's identity, travel dates, and flight information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents (e.g., passport, driver's license, or government ID).
3. Enter the date of your passenger flight, following the format `MM/DD/YYYY` (e.g., 02/15/2022).
4. Enter the flight number or code associated with your travel.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Passenger Name** (`passenger_name`, text, **not required**)
Enter your full name, as it appears on your identification documents.

### **Date of Passenger** (`date_of_passenger`, date, **not required**)
Enter the date of your passenger flight, using the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.

### **Flight Number** (`flight_number`, text, **not required**)
Enter the flight number or code associated with your travel.

Note: This form is for gathering information only and is not intended to be a substitute for any official travel documentation or identification. If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact your travel agency or airline representative.
