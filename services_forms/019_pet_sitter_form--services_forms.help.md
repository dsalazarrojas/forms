# Pet Sitter Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Sitter Form is designed to collect essential information about your pet to ensure we can provide the best care and attention during their stay.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each field carefully and answer accurately.
2. Fill in all the required fields (marked as *required*).
3. Choose one or multiple options from select options fields.
4. Double-check your answers to ensure they are accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Name (required)** (pet_sitter_form_13, text, required): Enter your pet's name.
* **Date of Birth (pet_sitter_form_2, number, required)**: Enter your pet's age in months.
* **Age (in years) (pet_sitter_form_3, text, optional)**: Enter your pet's age in years (if different from their age in months).
* **Age Group (pet_sitter_form_4, select_one, optional)**: Select your pet's age group from the options: 1-6 months, 6-12 months, 1-2 years, 2-5 years, 5-10 years, 10+ years.
* **Size (pet_sitter_form_5, select_multiple, optional)**: Select the size of your pet from the options: Small, Medium, Large.
* **Date (pet_sitter_form_6, date, optional)**: Enter your pet's date of birth or a special date (e.g., adoption date).
* **Time (pet_sitter_form_7, time, optional)**: Enter a specific time for your pet's regular routine.
* **Vaccinated (pet_sitter_form_8, select_one, optional)**: Select True if your pet is vaccinated or False if not.
* **Microchipped (pet_sitter_form_9, select_multiple, optional)**: Select True if your pet is microchipped or False if not.
* **Pet Type (pet_sitter_form_10, select_one, optional)**: Select the type of your pet from the options: True, False.
* **Pet Breed (pet_sitter_form_11, select_one, optional)**: Select the breed of your pet from the options: True, False.
* **Pet Color (pet_sitter_form_12, select_one, optional)**: Select the color of your pet from the options: True, False.
* **Has a collar (pet_sitter_form_13, text, required)**: Enter the name of your pet's collar (e.g., name tag).
* **Emergency contact info (pet_sitter_form_14, email, optional)**: Enter your email address or phone number for emergency contact.
* **Additional Information (pet_sitter_form_15, text, optional)**: Enter any additional information about your pet.
* **Special Requests (pet_sitter_form_16, text, optional)**: Enter any special requests for your pet's stay.
* **Medical History (pet_sitter_form_17, text, optional)**: Enter your pet's medical history.
* **Allergies (pet_sitter_form_18, text, optional)**: Enter any allergies your pet may have.
* **Diet (pet_sitter_form_19, select_one, optional)**: Select your pet's diet from the options: True, False.
* **Feeding Schedule (pet_sitter_form_20, text, optional)**: Enter your pet's feeding schedule.
* **Medication (pet_sitter_form_21, text, optional)**: Enter any medication your pet may need.
* **Special Habits (pet_sitter_form_22, text, optional)**: Enter any special habits or routines your pet has.
* **Housebroken (pet_sitter_form_23, select_one, optional)**: Select True if your pet is housebroken or False if not.
* **Additional Services (pet_sitter_form_24, text, optional)**: Enter any additional services you would like to request during your pet's stay.
* **Your Email (pet_sitter_form_25, email, optional)**: Enter your email address for contact and updates.
