# web_development_inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers about their web development project inquiries. Please fill out the form with your project details to help us better understand your needs and provide you with a suitable solution.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the Customer Details page with your name, email, and phone number. This is the first step in our inquiry process.
2. On the second page, select your development skills and services offered to give us an idea of your project's complexity and requirements.
3. On the third page, select your company size and project deadline to help us plan and schedule your project accordingly.
4. Finally, fill out your project budget and provide us with your email and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details (id: 1)**: This is the first step in our inquiry process. Please fill out your name, email, and phone number to get started.
* **Customer Website (id: 2)**: If you have a website, please provide the URL so we can get a better understanding of your project's scope.
* **Development Skills (id: 3)**: Select the skills you require for your project. This will help us determine the complexity of your project and allocate the right resources.
* **Services Offered (id: 4)**: Select the services you would like us to offer for your project. This could be web design, development, or maintenance.
* **Company Size (id: 5)**: Select the size of your company to help us better understand your project's scope and resources.
* **Project Deadline (id: 6)**: Please select a date for your project's deadline. This will help us plan and schedule your project accordingly.
* **Project Budget (id: 7)**: Fill out your project budget to give us an idea of your project's financial scope.
* **Email (id: 8)**: Please provide us with your email address to contact you back.
* **Phone (id: 9)**: Please provide us with your phone number to contact you back.

Note: Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that we can provide you with the best possible solution for your project. If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to contact us.
