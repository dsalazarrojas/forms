# Graphic Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Graphic Submission Form is a simple, AI-generated form designed to gather information from customers for graphic-related purposes. This form is not affiliated with or endorsed by any form platform, and you are free to use, modify, and even sell it, as long as you give proper credit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the details on the **First Page** field.
2. Read and acknowledge the **Agreement Form**.
3. Upload an image for the **Image Upload** field.
4. Add a customer image for the **Customer Image** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Page** (First Page): Enter the details of the first page of the submission.
* **Agreement Form** (Agreement Form): Please read and agree to the terms and conditions of the submission.
* **Image Upload** (Image Upload): Upload a relevant image related to the submission.
* **Customer Image** (Customer Image): Upload a relevant image of the customer related to the submission.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and truthfully.
* Upload only relevant and allowed image formats.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact form@gic.mx.
