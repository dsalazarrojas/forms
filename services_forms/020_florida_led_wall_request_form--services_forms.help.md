# Florida LED Wall Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Florida LED Wall Request Form is designed to collect information from clients about their LED wall requests in Florida.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your client information in the Client Information field.
2. Provide details about the event in the Event Info field.
3. Specify the wall information in the Wall Info field.
4. Enter event details in the Event Details field.
5. Share rental information in the Rental Info field.
6. Enter your contact information in the Contact Info field.
7. Specify billing information in the Billing Info field.
8. Choose your payment terms in the Payment Terms field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information (Client Information, text, required)**: Enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself or your organization.
* **Event Info (Event Info, text, required)**: Describe the event for which you are requesting the LED wall.
* **Wall Info (Wall Info, text, required)**: Specify the wall details, such as location and size.
* **Event Details (Event Details, text, required)**: Provide more details about the event, such as date and time.
* **Rental Info (Rental Info, text, required)**: Share rental information, including the duration of the rental.
* **Contact Info (Contact Info, text, required)**: Enter your contact name, email, and phone number.
* **Billing Info (Billing Info, text, required)**: Provide billing information, including your address and any additional details.
* **Payment Terms (Payment Terms, text, required)**: Choose your payment terms, such as payment method and due date.

## Tips
Please double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
