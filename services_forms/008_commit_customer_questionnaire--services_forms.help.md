# Commit Customer Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed information about you, the customer, to help us tailor a personalized health coaching program to suit your needs. Your inputs will enable us to understand your goals, habits, and preferences, allowing us to create a plan that suits you best.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Answer all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (*) in the label.
2. Provide accurate and detailed information, as this will help us create a precise coaching plan.
3. Select only one option for multi-choice questions.
4. Enter your email address to help us contact you for further communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: This is the name you prefer to be addressed.
* **Current Age**: Please enter your age at the time of completing this form.
* **Gender**: Please select your gender from the provided options.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address where you would like to receive further communication about your coaching plan.
* **Primary Contact Phone Number** (optional): If you would like to be contacted via phone, please enter your primary contact number.
* **Current Activity Level**: Please select one that best describes your current physical activity level.
* **Average Daily Water Intake (in ounces)**: Please enter your average daily water intake for better health assessment.
* **Average Hours of Sleep per Night**: Please select the number of hours you sleep on average per night.
* **Energy Levels**: Rate your average energy levels throughout the day, from 1 (Lowest) to 10 (Highest).
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** (optional): If you have any dietary restrictions or allergies, please enter them here.
* **Primary Health or Fitness Goal**: Select the primary goal you aim to achieve through the coaching program.
* **Commitment Level**: Rate your commitment level to achieving this goal, from 1 to 10.
* **Biggest Challenge**: Please describe the biggest challenge you face when it comes to health and fitness.
* **Preferred Communication Method**: Select the method(s) you would prefer to communicate with your coach.
* **Coaching Style**: Select the coaching style that you respond best to.
* **Date Completed**: Enter the date you completed this form.
* **Additional Comments** (optional): If there is anything else you would like to share with us, please enter it here.
