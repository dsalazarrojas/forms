# Hotel Guest Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by hotel staff to gather information from guests upon check-in, allowing for efficient and accurate record keeping.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the guest's first and last name in the first and second fields, respectively.
2. Enter the guest's email address in the third field. Ensure it is a valid email address.
3. Enter the guest's phone number in the fourth field. This can be a cell phone number or a landline number.
4. Select the date of the guest's check-in date in the fifth field.
5. Select the date of the guest's check-out date in the sixth field.
6. For the room type, choose the type of room the guest is booked into from the available options (Single, Double, Suite) in the seventh field.
7. Finally, enter the room number in the eighth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Enter the guest's first name as it is typically written.
- **Last Name**: Enter the guest's last name as it is typically written.
- **Email**: Enter the guest's email address in the format `example@example.com`. Ensure it is a valid email address.
- **Phone**: Enter the guest's phone number in the format `123-456-7890`. This can be a cell phone number or a landline number.
- **Checkin Date**: Select the date of the guest's check-in date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
- **Checkout Date**: Select the date of the guest's check-out date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
- **Room Type**: Select the type of room the guest is booked into from the available options.
- **Room Number**: Enter the number of the room assigned to the guest.

## Tips
- Be sure to double-check the guest's information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If the guest's email address or phone number is not valid, please enter `N/A` in the respective fields.
- If the guest's check-in and check-out dates do not match the dates on their booking confirmation, please contact the booking department for assistance.
