# event_medical_cover_enquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The event_medical_cover_enquiry_form is used to gather information from event organizers about their events to determine the need for medical coverage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are an event organizer or have a legitimate reason for requesting medical coverage.
2. Fill in the required fields, marked as such, and provide any optional information you believe is relevant.
3. Review your submission before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Organizer** (`event_organizer`, text, required):
    Provide the name and contact information of the person responsible for the event.
* **EventType** (`event_type`, select_one, not required):
    Indicate whether your event requires medical coverage (e.g. 'Yes' for a high-risk activity or 'No' for a standard event).
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, not required):
    Provide the date of the event, if applicable.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, not required):
    If your event has a specific time, provide the time of the event, if applicable.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, not required):
    Provide the location of the event, if applicable.
* **Medical Coverage** (`medical_coverage`, select_multiple, not required):
    Indicate the type(s) of medical coverage required for your event (e.g. 'Yes' for insurance, 'Yes' for medical assistance, etc.).
* **Medical Coverage Level** (`medical_coverage_level`, text, not required):
    Provide a brief description or level of medical coverage needed (e.g. basic, advanced, etc.).
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, not required):
    Provide a contact number where you can be reached for further information.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required):
    Provide an email address where you can be reached for further information.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, not required):
    Add any additional comments or information about your event that might be helpful.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text, not required):
    If this form is being submitted on behalf of someone else, provide the name and contact information of the person or team assigned to the event.
* **Assigned Date** (`assigned_date`, date, not required):
    If the event has been assigned to someone or a team, provide the date it was assigned.

## Tips
- Ensure accuracy and completeness of your submission.
- Review your submission before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled out.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant party for clarification.
- Only submit this form if you have a legitimate reason for requesting medical coverage.
