# Cemetery Mapping Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cemetery Mapping Form is designed to collect essential information for the burial and maintenance of a deceased's grave in a cemetery. This form will help families and individuals coordinate funeral services and arrangements for their loved ones.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the deceased's name and date of birth, death, and any other relevant details.
2. Provide the primary family contact name, phone number, and email address.
3. Choose the preferred burial location within the cemetery.
4. Select the type of burial plot (single, companion, family, or estate).
5. Select the type of burial (in-ground, cremation with urn, mausoleum, or columbar niche).
6. Indicate whether there are existing family plots in the cemetery, and if so, provide their locations.
7. Choose the type of grave marker (flat bronze plaque, granite monument, marble stone, simple headstone, or custom design).
8. (Optional) Specify any inscription or epitaph for the marker.
9. (Optional) Mention any religious or cultural considerations.
10. (Optional) Mention any floral or plant preferences for the grave site.
11. (Optional) Indicate the anticipated frequency of visits to the grave.
12. (Optional) Select the type of burial service (full, graveside, private, or no service planned).
13. (Optional) Select any additional services required (opening and closing, flower arrangement, monument installation, perpetual care, or vault service).
14. (Optional) Choose the payment method (prepaid, insurance, payment plan, or at time of need).
15. (Optional) Make any special requests or provide additional instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name of Deceased**: Enter the full legal name of the deceased.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the deceased's date of birth.
* **Date of Death**: Enter the deceased's date of death.
* **Primary Family Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person coordinating the funeral services.
* **Family Contact Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the primary family contact.
* **Family Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the primary family contact (optional).
* **Preferred Burial Location**: Choose the section or plot type within the cemetery where the burial will take place.
* **Plot Size Preference**: Select the type of burial plot (single, companion, family, or estate).
* **Type of Burial**: Choose the method of burial (in-ground, cremation with urn, mausoleum, or columbar niche).
* **Existing Family Plots**: Indicate if there are other family members already buried in the cemetery.
* **Location of Existing Plots**: If applicable, provide the section and plot numbers of existing family plots.
* **Grave Marker Type Preference**: Choose the type of marker (flat bronze plaque, granite monument, marble stone, simple headstone, or custom design).
* **Inscription or Epitaph Preference**: (Optional) Specify any inscription or epitaph for the marker.
* **Religious or Cultural Considerations**: (Optional) Mention any special requirements or customs.
* **Floral or Plant Preferences**: (Optional) Mention any preferences for flowers or plants at the grave site.
* **Anticipated Frequency of Visits**: (Optional) Indicate how often the family plans to visit the grave (weekly, monthly, several times per year, holidays only, or infrequently).
* **Type of Burial Service**: (Optional) Select the type of burial service (full, graveside, private, or no service planned).
* **Additional Services Requested**: (Optional) Select any additional services required (opening and closing, flower arrangement, monument installation, perpetual care, or vault service).
* **Payment Method**: (Optional) Choose the payment method (prepaid, insurance, payment plan, or at time of need).
* **Special Requests or Instructions**: (Optional) Make any special requests or provide additional instructions.
