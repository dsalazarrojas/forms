# Individualized Service Plan Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create an individualized service plan for each client. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the client's name.
2. Choose the service provider name.
3. Select the service type.
4. Set the service start date and target date.
5. Choose the frequency and status of the service.
6. Add any comments (optional).
7. Enter the service provider's contact information and the client's contact information.
8. Set the service start and target times (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client for whom the service plan is being created.
* **Service Provider**: Enter the name of the service provider who will be delivering the service.
* **Service Type**: Select the type of service being provided.
* **Goals**: Describe the goals of the service.
* **Service Start Date**: Enter the date when the service will start.
* **Target Date**: Enter the date when the service is expected to be completed or finished.
* **Frequency**: Select how often the service will be provided.
* **Status**: Choose the status of the service.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about the service.
* **Service Provider Phone**: Enter the phone number of the service provider.
* **Client Phone**: Enter the phone number of the client.
* **Service Provider Email**: Enter the email address of the service provider.
* **Client Email**: Enter the email address of the client.
* **Service Start Time**: Enter the time when the service will start.
* **Target Time**: Enter the time when the service is expected to be completed or finished.

Note: The time fields (Service Start Time and Target Time) are only visible if you are completing this form for a client who has opted in for time-sensitive services. 

Remember to review and validate the information before submitting the form.
