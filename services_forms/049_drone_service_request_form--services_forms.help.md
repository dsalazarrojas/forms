# Drone Service Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your drone service request. Please fill it out carefully to ensure that our team can provide you with the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Choose the type of service you need from the available options (Aerial Photography, Drone Surveying, or Event Coverage).
2.  Enter the location where the service will be performed.
3.  Select a date for the service.
4.  Choose the time for the service.
5.  Indicate if you need any additional services (Yes/No).
6.  Enter contact information for the person responsible for the service.
7.  Enter the contact email address.
8.  Enter the contact phone number.
9.  Briefly describe the service you need.
10.  Enter any additional information about the service.
11.  Select the type of drone that will be used for the service (Camera Drone, Thermal Drone, or Obstacle Avoidance Drone).
11.  Fill in any other required information as needed, but be aware that it is not mandatory to fill out all fields.


## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Drone Service Type**: Choose the type of service you need from Aerial Photography, Drone Surveying, or Event Coverage.
*   **Service Location**: Enter the location where the service will be performed.
*   **Service Date**: Select a date for the service. Please ensure you enter a valid date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
*   **Service Time**: Choose the time for the service. Please ensure you enter a valid time in the format `HH:MM:SS`.
*   **Additional Services**: Indicate if you need any additional services. Select Yes if you need it, otherwise select No.
*   **Contact Person**: Enter the contact person's name.
*   **Contact Email**: Enter the contact email address.
*   **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number.
*   **Service Request**: Briefly describe the service you need. 
*   **Service Description**: Enter any additional information about the service.
*   **Assigned Tool**: This field is currently not required to be filled out.
*   **Drone Type**: Choose the type of drone that will be used for the service. Select from Camera Drone, Thermal Drone, or Obstacle Avoidance Drone.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that you fill out the form only once for each service request.
*   Be as accurate as possible while filling out the form to ensure our team can provide you with the best possible service.
*   If you have any questions, please contact us and we will be happy to assist you.
