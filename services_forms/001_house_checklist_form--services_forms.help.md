# House Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The House Checklist Form is a tool designed to gather information about a house's status, basic systems, and service requirements. It is intended to help users document and assess the overall condition and needs of a house. This form aims to provide a comprehensive overview of the key aspects of a house's condition, allowing users to track and compare data effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question thoroughly and concisely.
2. Ensure you provide clear and accurate information about your house's status.
3. If you're unsure about any of the questions, try to answer based on your best judgment or available information.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and consistency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **House Status** (`house_status`): Please provide a brief description of your house's current status, such as "Excellent", "Fair", "Poor", or "Uninhabitable".
* **Workability of Basic Systems** (`workability_of_basic_systems`): Describe the workability of basic systems in your house, including plumbing, electrical, and HVAC.
* **Workability of Engines** (`workability_of_engines`): Assess the workability of engines and mechanical systems in your house, such as boilers, pumps, and generators.
* **Service Requirements** (`service_requirements`): List any ongoing or upcoming service requirements for your house, including but not limited to, maintenance, repairs, or inspections.
