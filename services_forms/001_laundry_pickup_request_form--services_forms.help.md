# Laundry Pickup Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Laundry Pickup Request Form is used to request laundry pickup services for customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Choose the preferred pickup date and time for your laundry.
3. Select the type of service you require (e.g., dry cleaning or hand washing).
4. Enter your contact information, including phone number and email.
5. Choose your preferred contact method (email, phone, or text message).
6. Select your preferred pickup method (e.g., pickup at home or drop off at location).
7. Enter any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer* (\`: customer, Text, Required)**: Please enter your name and contact information.
- **Pickup Date* (\`: pickup_date, Date, Required)**: Choose the date for laundry pickup.
- **Pickup Time* (\`: pickup_time, Time, Required)**: Choose the time for laundry pickup.
- **Pickup Location (\`: pickup_location, Text, Optional)**: Enter the location where you would like to have your laundry picked up.
- **Wash Service Type (\`: wash_service_type, Select One, Optional)**: Choose the type of service you require (e.g., dry cleaning or hand washing).
- **Pickup Address (\`: pickup_address, Text, Optional)**: Enter the address where you would like to have your laundry picked up.
- **Contact Number (\`: contact_number, Text, Optional)**: Enter your contact number.
- **Email (\`: email, Email, Optional)**: Enter your email address.
- **Message (\`: message, Note, Optional)**: Enter any additional comments or requests.
- **Preferred Contact Method (\`: preferred_contact_method, Select One, Optional)**: Choose how you would like to be contacted (e.g., email, phone, or text message).
- **Service Type (\`: service_type, Select One, Optional)**: Choose the type of service you require (e.g., pickup at home or drop off at location).
- **Pickup Time Frame (\`: pickup_time_frame, Time, Optional)**: Choose the time frame for laundry pickup.
- **Pickup Time Slot (\`: pickup_time_slot, Time, Optional)**: Choose the specific time slot for laundry pickup.
- **Notes (\`: notes, Note, Optional)**: Enter any additional comments or requests.
- **Pickup Date Earliest (\`: pickup_date_earliest, Date, Optional)**: Choose the earliest date for laundry pickup.
- **Pickup Date Latest (\`: pickup_date_latest, Date, Optional)**: Choose the latest date for laundry pickup.
