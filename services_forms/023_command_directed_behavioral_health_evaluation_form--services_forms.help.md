# Command Directed Behavioral Health Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess a service member's behavioral health, focusing on areas such as mental health, substance use, and relationships. The evaluations conducted using this form will inform decisions related to the service member's behavior and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully before starting to ensure you understand each question.
2. Answer each question honestly and accurately.
3. Complete the form in a quiet, private setting, free from distractions and interruptions.
4. Please consult with the evaluating officer or a supervisor if you are unsure about any part of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Member Name** (`service_member_name`, `text`, required): Enter the service member's full legal name.
* **Rank/Grade** (`rank_grade`, `text`, required): Enter the service member's current military rank.
* **Service Branch** (`service_branch`, `select_one`, required): Select the service branch the service member belongs to (Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, Coast Guard, Space Force).
* **Unit/Command** (`unit_command`, `text`, required): Enter the service member's current unit assignment.
* **Date of Evaluation** (`date_of_evaluation`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **-- Evaluation Type --** (`section_header_evaluation_type`, `note`, required): This section is used to identify the type of evaluation being conducted.
* **Reason for Evaluation** (`evaluation_reason`, `select_one`, required): Select the reason for the evaluation (Routine Assessment, Post-Deployment, Incident-Related, Command Directed, Self-Referral, Medical Referral, Other).
* **Previous Behavioral Health Evaluations** (`previous_evaluations`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you have had prior evaluations (True or False).
* **-- Mental Health Assessment --** (`section_header_mental_health`, `note`, required): This section is used to assess the service member's mental health.
* **Current Stress Level** (`current_stress_level`, `select_one`, required): Select the service member's current stress level (Very Low, Low, Moderate, High, Very High).
* **Sleep Quality** (`sleep_quality`, `select_one`, required): Select the service member's sleep quality (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor).
* **Mood Changes** (`mood_changes`, `select_one`, required): Select whether the service member has experienced mood changes (No Changes, Mild Changes, Moderate Changes, Significant Changes, Severe Changes).
* **Anxiety Level** (`anxiety_level`, `select_one`, required): Select the service member's current anxiety level (None, Mild, Moderate, Severe, Overwhelming).
* **Depression Symptoms** (`depression_symptoms`, `select_one`, required): Select whether the service member is experiencing depression symptoms (None, Mild, Moderate, Severe).
* **-- Trauma and PTSD --** (`section_header_trauma`, `note`, required): This section is used to assess the service member's trauma and PTSD history.
* **Trauma Exposure** (`trauma_exposure`, `select_one`, required): Select whether the service member has experienced traumatic events (True, False, Prefer Not to Answer).
* **PTSD Symptoms** (`ptsd_symptoms`, `select_one`, required): Select the service member's current PTSD symptom level (None, Mild, Moderate, Severe).
* **Nightmares or Flashbacks** (`nightmares`, `select_one`, required): Select the frequency of nightmares or flashbacks (Never, Rarely, Sometimes, Often, Daily).
* **-- Substance Use --** (`section_header_substance_use`, `note`, required): This section is used to assess the service member's substance use.
* **Alcohol Use** (`alcohol_use`, `select_one`, required): Select the service member's frequency of alcohol use (None, Occasional, Moderate, Heavy, Daily).
* **Substance Use** (`substance_use`, `select_one`, required): Select the service member's substance use (None, Past Use Only, Occasional, Regular, Daily).
* **-- Relationships and Functioning --** (`section_header_relationships`, `note`, required): This section is used to assess the service member's relationships and functioning.
* **Relationship Status** (`relationship_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the service member's current relationship status (Single, Married, Divorced, Separated, Widowed, In a Relationship).
* **Family Relationships** (`family_relationships`, `select_one`, required): Select the quality of the service member's family relationships (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Strained).
* **Work Performance** (`work_performance`, `select_one`, required): Select the service member's work performance (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor).
* **-- Support and Treatment --** (`section_header_support`, `note`, required): This section is used to assess the service member's support system and treatment.
* **Currently in Treatment** (`current_treatment`, `select_one`, required): Select whether the service member is receiving mental health treatment (True, False).
* **Medication** (`medication`, `select_one`, required): Select whether the service member is currently taking psychiatric medication (True, False).
* **Support System** (`support_system`, `select_one`, required): Select the service member's level of support (Strong Support, Some Support, Limited Support, No Support).
* **Suicidal Thoughts** (`suicidal_thoughts`, `select_one`, required): Select the service member's current level of suicidal ideation (False, Past Only, Rarely, Sometimes, Frequently).
* **-- Evaluator Use Only --** (`section_header_recommendations`, `note`, required): This section is used to provide the evaluator's summary and treatment recommendations.
* **Overall Assessment** (`overall_assessment`, `text`, required): Enter the evaluator's summary of the service member's behavior and needs.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, `text`, required): Enter the evaluator's treatment recommendations for the service member.
* **Follow-Up Required** (`follow_up_required`, `select_one`, required): Select whether follow-up is required (True, False).
* **Evaluator Name** (`evaluator_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the evaluating professional.
* **Evaluator Signature** (`evaluator_signature`, `text`, required): Enter the signature of the evaluating professional.

## Tips

* Complete the form carefully and accurately, as the information gathered will inform decisions related to the service member's behavior and treatment.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, please consult with the evaluating officer or a supervisor.
* Please ensure that the form is completed in a quiet, private setting, free from distractions and interruptions.
* Review the form carefully before starting to ensure you understand each question.
* Enter all required fields.
* Do not skip any questions or sections.
* Enter your answers honestly and accurately.
* If you need help or guidance, please ask for it.
* The form is designed to be completed in conjunction with other relevant documentation and information.
* The evaluating officer or supervisor will review and use this form to inform decisions related to the service member's behavior and treatment.
