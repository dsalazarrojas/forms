# Funeral Planning Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information for funeral planning and organization. It helps individuals and families gather the necessary details for a respectful and meaningful funeral or memorial service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to understand the different sections and fields.
2. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, using the space provided for each field.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields, as indicated by the "required" flag.
4. For select fields (e.g., "Headstone Selection" and "Burial Site"), choose the relevant option that best describes your preference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (page 1)
	+ This field is an optional note section for you to add any initial thoughts or ideas about the funeral planning process.
* **Funeral Planning Goals** (page 1)
	+ Describe your goals for the funeral or memorial service, such as honoring the deceased's wishes or providing support for mourners.
* **Planning Process** (page 1)
	+ Outline the steps you plan to take in the funeral planning process, from preparation to the actual service.
* **Family Involvement** (page 1)
	+ Discuss how you plan to involve family members or close friends in the funeral planning process.
* **Funeral Director** (page 2)
	+ Identify the funeral director or service provider you have selected for the funeral or memorial service.
* **Death Certificate** (page 2)
	+ This field is not explicitly defined, please enter any relevant details about the death certificate, if applicable.
* **Body Donation** (page 2)
	+ Confirm whether you plan to arrange for body donation or not.
* **Casket Selection** (page 3)
	+ Choose 'Yes' if you plan to select a casket for the funeral or memorial service.
* **Headstone Selection** (page 3)
	+ Choose 'Yes' if you plan to select a headstone (gravesite marker) for the funeral or memorial service.
* **Burial Site** (page 4)
	+ Select all relevant burial sites where the service will take place.
