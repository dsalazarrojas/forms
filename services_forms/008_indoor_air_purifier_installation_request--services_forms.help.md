# indoor-air-purifier-installation-request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to request indoor air purifier installation services for a specific unit.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information, including name, phone number, and email.
2. Provide unit information, including unit type and number.
3. Specify your preferred appointment time and date.
4. Describe the service request, including any specific features or requirements.
5. Confirm that you are the authorized person submitting the form.
6. Add any additional comments or questions.
7. Click the submit button to send the form to the service provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`contact_name`): Enter your name.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`): Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`): Enter your address.
* **Property Manager** (`property_manager`): Enter the property manager's name.
* **Property Management Company** (`property_management_company`): Enter the property management company name.
* **Unit Type** (`unit_type`): Select the type of unit (e.g., apartment, house, condo).
* **Unit Number** (`unit_number`): Enter the unit number.
* **Appointment Availability** (`appointment_availability`): Choose your preferred appointment time.
* **Installation Date** (`installation_date`): Select the date for the installation.
* **Installation Time** (`installation_time`): Choose the installation time.
* **Installation Technician** (`installation_technician`): Enter the name of the installation technician.
* **Installation Status** (`installation_status`): Confirm the installation status.
* **Installation Comments** (`installation_comments`): Enter any comments about the installation.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Enter any additional comments or questions.
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`): Confirm the date the form was submitted.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`): Confirm who submitted the form.
* **Submitted To** (`submitted_to`): Confirm who the form was submitted to.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`): Confirm the date the form was submitted.
* **Submitted Status** (`submitted_status`): Confirm the submitted status.
* **Submitted Comment** (`submitted_comment`): Enter any submitted comments.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`): Confirm who was assigned to handle the request.
* **Assigned Date** (`assigned_date`): Confirm the date the request was assigned.
* **Assigned Status** (`assigned_status`): Confirm the assigned status.
* **Assigned Comment** (`assigned_comment`): Enter any assigned comments.
* **Completed Date** (`completed_date`): Confirm the completion date.
* **Completed Status** (`completed_status`): Confirm the completed status.
* **Completed Comment** (`completed_comment`): Enter any completed comments.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are submitting this form for the correct purpose and that you are the authorized person submitting it on behalf of the customer.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the form administrator.
* The form will only allow one submission per customer to prevent duplicate entries.
