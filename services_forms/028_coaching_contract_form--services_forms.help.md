# Coaching Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for creating a comprehensive coaching contract between the client and the coach. It is designed to gather necessary information for the coaching process to begin, including client details, coaching preferences, and agreements for the coaching terms.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the client's personal information, including their full name, email address, phone number, and mailing address.
2. Choose the type of coaching service you require (e.g. Life Coaching, Career Coaching, etc.).
3. Select the start and end dates of the coaching contract.
4. Choose how often you would like to meet with the coach (e.g. weekly, bi-weekly, etc.).
5. Select the preferred appointment time for coaching sessions.
6. Choose the format of the coaching sessions (e.g. in person, video conference, etc.).
7. Select the number of coaching sessions included in the contract.
8. Choose the fee per session and the total contract value.
9. Select the payment schedule (e.g. upfront full payment, 50% deposit, etc.).
10. Choose the method of payment (e.g. credit card, debit card, etc.).
11. Describe your coaching objectives and any relevant health information.
12. Confirm that you understand the cancellation policy, refund terms, confidentiality agreement, liability waiver, and terms and conditions.
13. Sign and date the contract.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Full Name**: Your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email Address**: Your contact email address.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number.
* **Mailing Address**: Your mailing address.
* **Type of Coaching**: Choose the type of coaching service you require (e.g. Life Coaching, Career Coaching, etc.).
* **Contract Start Date**: The date when coaching will begin.
* **Contract End Date**: The date when coaching will end (leave blank if not applicable).
* **Session Frequency**: How often you would like to meet with the coach (e.g. weekly, bi-weekly, etc.).
* **Preferred Appointment Time**: The time of day that works best for you for coaching sessions.
* **Session Format**: The format of the coaching sessions (e.g. in person, video conference, etc.).
* **Number of Sessions**: The total number of coaching sessions included in the contract.
* **Fee Per Session**: The cost per coaching session.
* **Total Contract Value**: The total cost of all coaching sessions.
* **Payment Schedule**: The payment schedule you have chosen (e.g. upfront full payment, 50% deposit, etc.).
* **Payment Method**: The method of payment you will use (e.g. credit card, debit card, etc.).
* **Coaching Objectives**: Describe what you hope to achieve through the coaching process.
* **Relevant Health Information**: Provide any relevant health information that the coach should know.
* **I Understand Cancellation Terms**: Confirm that you understand the cancellation policy.
* **Cancellation Notice Required**: The amount of notice required to cancel the contract.
* **I Understand Refund Terms**: Confirm that you understand the refund policy.
* **Confidentiality Agreement**: Confirm that you agree to keep the coaching relationship confidential.
* **Liability Waiver**: Confirm that you agree to release the coach from certain liabilities.
* **I Accept Terms and Conditions**: Confirm that you agree to all contract terms.
* **Client Signature Date**: Date of your signature.
* **Coach Signature Date**: Date of the coach's signature.
* **Additional Notes or Terms**: Any additional agreements or terms that need to be discussed.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure that the coaching contract is comprehensive and effective.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to the coach.
* This form is a template and can be modified to suit your specific coaching needs.
