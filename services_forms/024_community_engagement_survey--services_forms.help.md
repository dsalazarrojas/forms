# Community Engagement Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Community Engagement Survey is designed to gather feedback and perceptions from community members about their community. The survey is used to identify areas of strength and weakness within the community, allowing for informed decision-making and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. For select fields, choose one of the provided options.
3. For optional fields, feel free to add any additional comments or information.
4. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name (Optional)** (1): Enter your name if you wish to provide feedback anonymously or if we may reach out to you for follow-up questions.
* **Email Address** (2): Enter your email address for follow-up purposes. This is an optional field.
* **ZIP Code** (3): Enter your ZIP code for geographic reference.
* **Years in Community** (4): Select how many years you have lived in the community.
* **Sense of Community Pride** (5): Choose one of the provided options to indicate your level of pride in living in the community.
	+ Very Proud: You feel extremely proud to call this community your home.
	+ Proud: You feel proud to live in this community.
	+ Neutral: You don't feel strongly about your community pride.
	+ Not Very Proud: You don't feel very proud to live in this community.
	+ Not Proud at All: You don't feel proud to live in this community.
* **Level of Community Involvement** (6): Choose how involved you are in community activities.
	+ Very Involved: You participate regularly in community activities.
	+ Somewhat Involved: You occasionally participate in community activities.
	+ Not Very Involved: You rarely participate in community activities.
	+ Not Involved at All: You never participate in community activities.
* **Activities You Participate In** (7): Select all the activities you regularly participate in. You may choose multiple options.
	+ Community Events: Events hosted by the community or community organizations.
	+ Local Meetings: Meetings that take place within the community.
	+ Volunteer Work: Volunteering your time to help others in the community.
	+ Neighborhood Associations: Participating in neighborhood organizations or activities.
	+ Sports Leagues: Participating in sports or recreation leagues.
	+ Arts and Culture: Engaging in arts or cultural activities.
	+ None: None of the above activities.
* **Community Services Rating** (8): Rate the quality of community services.
	+ Excellent: Very good services.
	+ Good: Average services.
	+ Fair: Below average services.
	+ Poor: Poor services.
* **Safety Rating** (9): Choose how safe you feel in the community.
	+ Very Safe: You feel extremely safe in the community.
	+ Safe: You feel safe in the community.
	+ Neutral: You don't feel strongly about safety.
	+ Unsafe: You don't feel safe in the community.
	+ Very Unsafe: You feel extremely unsafe in the community.
* **Quality of Life Rating** (10): Rate the quality of life in the community.
	+ Excellent: Very good quality of life.
	+ Good: Average quality of life.
	+ Fair: Below average quality of life.
	+ Poor: Poor quality of life.
* **Community Communication Rating** (11): Rate how well the community communicates with each other.
	+ Excellent: Good communication among community members.
	+ Good: Fair communication among community members.
	+ Fair: Poor communication among community members.
	+ Poor: Very poor communication among community members.
* **Favorite Aspects of Community** (12): Describe what you love most about the community.
* **Least Favorite Aspects** (13): Describe what you would like to see improved in the community.
* **Barriers to Engagement** (14): Select all the reasons that prevent you from being more involved in the community. You may choose multiple options.
	+ Lack of Time: You don't have enough time to participate.
	+ Lack of Information: You don't know enough about community activities.
	+ Lack of Interest: You are not interested in community activities.
	+ Transportation Issues: You face transportation challenges.
	+ Childcare Issues: You have childcare responsibilities.
	+ Health Issues: You have health issues that prevent participation.
	+ None: None of the above reasons.
* **Interest in Future Involvement** (15): Choose if you would like to be more involved in the community in the future.
	+ Yes, Definitely: You would like to be more involved.
	+ Maybe: You might be more involved in the future.
	+ No: You are not interested in being more involved.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (16): Provide any suggestions you have for improving the community.
