# Building Systems Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Building Systems Checklist is a form used to inspect and report on the status of various building systems, including security, safety, and external systems. This form helps ensure that the building's systems are functioning correctly and that any issues or maintenance needs are identified.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and make sure you understand what each field is asking for.
2. Answer each field accurately and thoroughly, taking into account the options available.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields.
4. Use the provided options for each field to ensure consistency in your responses.
5. If you have any issues or concerns, describe them clearly in the "Issues or Deficiencies Found" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, required): The date of the building systems inspection.
* **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, required): The name of the person conducting the inspection.
* **Building Location** (`building_location`, required): The address or identifier of the building being inspected.
* **Inspection Shift** (`shift_time`, required): The shift during which the inspection is conducted (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Night).
* **Security Cameras Operational** (`security_cameras`, required): Status of security cameras, with options for Operational, Partially Operational, Non-functional, or Not Applicable.
* **Door and Window Contacts** (`door_contacts`, required): Status of door and window contact sensors, with options for Operational, Partially Operational, Non-functional, or Not Applicable.
* **Entry Gate System** (`entry_gate`, required): Status of entry gate system functionality.
* **Exit Gate System** (`exit_gate`, required): Status of exit gate system functionality.
* **Fire Extinguishers Status** (`fire_extinguishers`, required): Status of fire extinguishers, with options for Fully Stocked and Accessible, Present but Needs Inspection, Missing or Non-functional, or Not Applicable.
* **Fire Alarm Systems** (`fire_alarms`, required): Status of fire detection and alarm systems, with options for Operational, Partially Operational, Non-functional, or Not Applicable.
* **Emergency Lighting** (`emergency_lights`, required): Status of emergency exit lights, with options for All Operational, Some Non-functional, None Operational, or Not Applicable.
* **Emergency Exits Accessible** (`emergency_exits`, required): Status of emergency exits, with options for Clear and Accessible, Partially Blocked, Blocked, or Not Applicable.
* **External Sign Lighting** (`external_lighting`, required): Status of external lighting, with options for Operational, Partially Operational, Non-functional, or Not Applicable.
* **Landscaping and Grounds** (`landscaping_maintenance`, required): Status of exterior grounds, with options for Good Condition, Acceptable, Needs Maintenance, or Poor Condition.
* **Parking Areas Condition** (`parking_areas`, required): Status of parking areas, with options for Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Issues or Deficiencies Found** (`issues_found`, optional): Describe any issues or concerns you have about the building's systems.
* **Action Required** (`action_required`, required): The type of maintenance or repair needed for any issues identified.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, optional): Any other observations or comments you have about the inspection.

Note: Please fill out this form thoroughly and accurately to ensure that the building's systems are functioning correctly and that any issues or maintenance needs are identified. If you are unsure about any field, please review the options or seek clarification from your supervisor or colleague.
