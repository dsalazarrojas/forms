# Event Risk Management Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Risk Management Checklist is used to identify and assess potential risks associated with events, ensuring that all necessary risk control measures are in place to mitigate them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the sections and fill out the form in the order presented.
2. Start with the "Introduction" section, which provides background information about the event.
3. Move on to the "Risk Identification" section, where you'll list potential risks associated with the event.
4. In the "Risk Categories" section, select the categories of risk that apply to the identified risks.
5. In the "Risk Probability" section, rate the likelihood of each risk.
6. Move on to the "Risk Impact" section and rate the potential impact of each risk.
7. In the "Risk Control Measures" section, describe the measures in place to mitigate each risk.
8. The "Emergency Contacts" section is for identifying emergency services and contacts that will be used in case of an emergency.
9. In the "Incident Reporting" section, indicate whether the risk of incident reporting is present.
10. In the "Review and Update" section, confirm if the review and update process is in place.
11. Finally, review and sign the form, providing your comments and review date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction (1)**: This section provides background information about the event.
* **Risk Identification (2)**: List potential risks associated with the event.
* **Risk Categories (3)**: Select the categories of risk that apply to the identified risks (e.g., Risk of Injury or Illness, Property Damage, Financial Loss).
* **Risk Probability (4)**: Rate the likelihood of each risk on a scale (e.g., Low, Medium, High).
* **Risk Impact (5)**: Rate the potential impact of each risk (e.g., Low, Medium, High).
* **Risk Control Measures (6)**: Describe the measures in place to mitigate each risk.
* **Emergency Contacts (8)**: Identify emergency services and contacts that will be used in case of an emergency.
* **Incident Reporting (9)**: Indicate whether the risk of incident reporting is present.
* **Review and Update (10)**: Confirm if the review and update process is in place.
* **Review and Signature (13)**: Confirm that you have reviewed the form and sign it to acknowledge your completion.
* **Review Date (14)**: Enter the date of your review.
* **Reviewer Signature (15)**: Enter your signature to confirm your review.
* **Reviewer's Name (16)**: Enter your name.
* **Date (17)**: Enter the date of the review.
* **Time (18)**: Enter the time of the review.
* **Event Details (19)**: Provide additional event details, if necessary.
* **Review Period (20)**: Enter the review period for the event.
* **Review Frequency (21)**: Enter the review frequency for the event.
* **Emergency Procedure (22)**: Enter any emergency procedures, if necessary.
* **Risk Assessment (23)**: Rate the risk assessment for each risk (e.g., Low, Medium, High).
* **Risk Assessment Remarks (24)**: Enter any additional remarks about the risk assessment.
* **Comments (25)**: Enter any comments about the form or the event.
* **Signature (12)**: Enter your signature to acknowledge your completion of the form.
* **Review and Signature (13)**: Confirm that you have reviewed the form and sign it to acknowledge your completion.
