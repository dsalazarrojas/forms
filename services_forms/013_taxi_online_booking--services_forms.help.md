# Taxi Online Booking - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to book a taxi service online. Fill in the form with your details and preferences to make an online booking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. If you have any special instructions or requests, type them in the "Instructions" field.
4. Choose the start date of your journey in the "Start Date" field.
5. Select the start time of your journey in the "Start Time" field.
6. Choose the type of journey you want to book (e.g., Taxi, Private).
7. Choose the type of vehicle you prefer (e.g., Car, Bus, Tram).
8. Specify the route for your journey in the "Route" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Instructions**: If you have any special instructions or requests, type them here.
* **Start Date**: Select the start date of your journey.
* **Start Time**: Select the start time of your journey.
* **Journey Type**: Choose the type of journey you want to book (e.g., Taxi, Private).
* **Vehicle Type**: Choose the type of vehicle you prefer (e.g., Car, Bus, Tram).
* **Route**: Specify the route for your journey.
