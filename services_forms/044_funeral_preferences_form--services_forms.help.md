# Funeral Preferences Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Funeral Preferences Form is designed to help users express their personal and practical preferences for the funeral service. The form is meant to gather information about the user's coffin type, description, date, time, color, shape, size, location, and type of service, allowing funeral planners to create a tailored experience that reflects the user's wishes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully, paying attention to the label and options provided.
2. Select the preferred coffin type, shape, and color by choosing from the available options.
3. Enter a brief description of your desired coffin, if applicable.
4. Choose a date and time for the funeral service.
5. Select one or multiple locations where you would like the service to be held.
6. Indicate your preference for the type of funeral service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Coffin Type** (Coffin Type, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of coffin that best suits your preferences. Options include Casket, Wooden Coffin, and Eco-Friendly Coffin.
* **Coffin Description** (Coffin Description, text, required: false): If you have a specific description or wishes for your coffin, enter it here.
* **Funeral Date** (Funeral Date, date, required: false): Select a date for the funeral service. This can be a specific date or an open-ended date (e.g., "within the next 2 weeks").
* **Funeral Time** (Funeral Time, time, required: false): Choose a time for the funeral service.
* **Coffin Color** (Coffin Color, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or multiple colors for your coffin.
* **Coffin Shape** (Coffin Shape, select_one, required: false): Choose the shape of the coffin that suits your style.
* **Coffin Size** (Coffin Size, number, required: false): Enter the size of the coffin you prefer.
* **Location** (Location, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or multiple locations where you would like the service to be held.
* **Funeral Service** (Funeral Service, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of funeral service you prefer, such as Traditional, Memorial, or Cremation.
