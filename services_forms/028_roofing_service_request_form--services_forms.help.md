# Roofing Service Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Roofing Service Request Form is a comprehensive tool for customers to request roofing services. This form captures essential information to ensure that the requested services are properly handled and fulfilled.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your **First name**: The first name field is used to collect the first name of the person requesting the service. Please enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
2. Enter your **Last name**: The last name field is used to collect the last name of the person requesting the service. Please enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
3. Enter your **Email**: The email field is used to collect your contact email address. This will be used for internal communication and follow-up.
4. Enter your **Phone**: The phone field is used to collect your contact phone number. Please enter your phone number in the format (123) 456-7890.
5. Choose the **Service type**: Select the type of service you are requesting from the dropdown menu. This will help us to better understand your needs.
6. Select the **Service date**: Choose the date for the requested service. This will help us to schedule the service accordingly.
7. Provide a **Request description**: Describe the reason for your service request. Please be as detailed as possible to ensure that we can provide the best possible service.
8. Enter your **Location**: Enter the location where the service will be provided. This will help us to understand where to send the service team.
9. You may choose to provide a **Service request ID**: If you have a pre-existing service request ID, please enter it here.
10. If applicable, select the **Assigned tool**: Choose the tool that you would like to be assigned to your service request.
11. If applicable, enter the **Created by**: Enter the person who created the service request.
12. You do not need to enter the **Updated at** and **Created at** fields as they are automatically generated.
13. If you want to identify the **Form ID**, you can enter it here.
14. If you want to, enter the **Category** where the service request belongs.
15. If you have a **Subform name**, you can enter it here.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that we can provide the best possible service.
- If you are unsure about any field, feel free to contact us.
- If you would like to add any additional information, please attach a note or comment to the form.
