# Custom Tailoring Inquiry Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Custom Tailoring Inquiry Contact Form is designed to collect inquiries and requests from customers for custom tailoring services. This form helps us understand their needs and respond accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the **Customer Name** field.
2. Enter your email address in the **Customer Email** field. This will help us contact you directly.
3. Add your phone number in the **Customer Phone** field, if you have one.
4. In the **Customer Message** field, provide a brief description of your custom tailoring needs.
5. In the **Custom Tailoring Inquiry Type** field, select the type of service you require:
	* Alteration
	* Alteration - Alter
	* Alteration - Add
	* Repair
	* Repair - Alter
	* Other (if none of the above apply)
6. If you selected "Other", please provide a brief description in the **Custom Tailoring Inquiry Type Other** field.
7. If you need to attach any files or documents to support your request, upload them in the **Custom Tailoring Inquiry Attachment** field.
8. Provide a brief comment about the attached file in the **Custom Tailoring Inquiry Attachment Comment** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Custom Tailoring Inquiry Contact Form** (Text): This is the form title.
* **Customer Name** (Text, Required): Enter your name to help us understand who is inquiring about our services.
* **Customer Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you directly.
* **Customer Phone** (Text, Optional): Provide your phone number if you have one.
* **Customer Message** (Text, Optional): Describe your custom tailoring needs in a brief message.
* **Custom Tailoring Inquiry Type** (Select One, Optional): Select the type of service you require:
	+ Alteration
	+ Alteration - Alter
	+ Alteration - Add
	+ Repair
	+ Repair - Alter
	+ Other
* **Custom Tailoring Inquiry Type Other** (Text, Optional): If you selected "Other", provide a brief description of your needs.
* **Custom Tailoring Inquiry Attachment** (Text, Optional): Upload files or documents to support your request.
* **Custom Tailoring Inquiry Attachment Comment** (Text, Optional): Provide a brief comment about the attached file.

## Tips
* Be sure to fill in all required fields (Customer Name and Custom Tailoring Inquiry Type).
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, feel free to contact our customer support team for assistance.
* Please note that we will respond to your inquiry within 24-48 hours.
