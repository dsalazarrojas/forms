# Online Taxi Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to provide information for their online taxi booking. It's intended to make the booking process more efficient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
2. Choose your preferred taxi fare from the "Taxi Fare" dropdown menu.
3. Select the type of trip you're booking (Airport, City, or Station).
4. Enter your phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
5. Enter your email in the "Customer Email" field.
6. In the "Address 2" field, enter your destination address (if it's different from your original address).
7. Optionally, include a message in the "Message" field for any special requests.
8. Select "Submit" to confirm your booking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Address**: Enter your starting address where you would like to be picked up.
* **Taxi Fare**: Choose from options: Taxi 1, Taxi 2, Taxi 3.
* **Trip Type**: Select one or more of the options: Airport, City, Station.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your booking.
* **Address 2**: If your destination address is different from your original address, enter it here.
* **Message**: Optionally, include any special requests or instructions for your booking.
* **Submit**: Review and submit your booking request.
