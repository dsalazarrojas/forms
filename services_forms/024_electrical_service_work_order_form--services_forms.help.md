# Electrical Service Work Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Electrical Service Work Order Form is used to collect information for electrical service work orders from customers or internal teams.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Job Number (if applicable).
2. Provide the Customer Name and any relevant details about the customer.
3. Describe the Work Order, including the specific services required.
4. Enter the Service Date for the scheduled service.
5. Click Submit to submit the form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Number**: Enter the unique identifier for the job, if available. This can be a reference number or any other relevant identifier assigned by the customer or internal team.
* **Customer Name**: Provide the name of the customer or the individual requesting the service. This will help us to address the request and communicate with them effectively.
* **Work Order**: Describe the specific services required for the job, including any additional details or instructions. Be as detailed as possible to ensure accurate processing and completion of the service.
* **Service Date**: Enter the date when the service is scheduled to be completed. This will help us to plan and prepare for the service efficiently.
