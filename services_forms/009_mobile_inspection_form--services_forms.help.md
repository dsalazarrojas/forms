# Mobile Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mobile Inspection Form is designed to collect information about the condition of mobile units. It is used by inspectors to document their observations and findings during an inspection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the relevant category for the inspection form from the drop-down options.
2. Enter your name as the inspector in the `inspector_name` field.
3. Enter your phone number in the `inspector_phone` field.
4. Enter your email address in the `inspector_email` field.
5. Choose the date of the inspection from the calendar.
6. Select the inspection time from the time dropdown.
7. Provide a detailed observation in the `observation` field.
8. Enter any additional notes about the inspection in the `notes` field.
9. Sign the form with your signature in the `inspector_signature` field.
10. If applicable, have the client sign the form with their signature in the `client_signature` field.
11. Once you've completed all the required fields, click the "Form Completed" checkbox to confirm that the form is complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **inspection_form_page_1**: This field is the first page of the form, which is a text field to collect any additional information about the inspection.
* **mobile_inspection_form_category_1**: Select the correct category for the inspection from the dropdown options.
* **mobile_inspection_form_category_2**: Select multiple categories for the inspection from the dropdown options.
* **inspector_name**: Enter your name as the inspector conducting the inspection.
* **inspector_phone**: Enter your phone number in case the client needs to contact you.
* **inspector_email**: Enter your email address in case the client needs to contact you.
* **inspection_date**: Choose the date of the inspection from the calendar.
* **inspection_time**: Select the time of the inspection from the dropdown options.
* **observation**: Provide a detailed description of what you observed during the inspection.
* **notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the inspection.
* **inspector_signature**: Sign the form with your signature to confirm that you conducted the inspection.
* **client_signature**: If applicable, have the client sign the form to confirm that they received the inspection.
* **form_completed**: Check the "Form Completed" checkbox to confirm that the form is finished.
