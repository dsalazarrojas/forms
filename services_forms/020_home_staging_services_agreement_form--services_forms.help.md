# Home Staging Services Agreement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to collect necessary information for the provision of home staging services. It serves as a binding agreement between the client and the agent involved in the staging process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields. Note that some fields may not be required but are suggested for the best possible understanding of the service agreement.
2. Enter accurate and clear information for all fields, as they will be reviewed and used as part of the agreement.
3. Review and acknowledge the provided terms and conditions before signing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Home Staging Services Agreement Form (Page 1)** (`home_staging_services_agreement_form_page_1`, text, required: false): This is the title of the form, which should not be filled out as it is a label.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required: false): Please enter your contact information here, including your name, phone number, and email address.
* **Client Details** (`client_details`, text, required: false): This field is intended for you to provide any additional information about yourself as a client.
* **Property Details** (`property_details`, text, required: false): Provide any relevant property-related information here.
* **Service Details** (`service_details`, text, required: false): Describe the services you require from the agent.
* **Contract Terms** (`contract_terms`, text, required: false): Review and acknowledge the terms and conditions of the service agreement.
* **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, date, required: false): This field is intended for the agent to record the date of the agreement.
* **Agreement Time** (`agreement_time`, time, required: false): The time of the agreement recording.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, text, required: false): This field is for the client's signature, confirming acceptance of the service agreement.
* **Agent Signature** (`agent_signature`, text, required: false): This field is for the agent's signature, indicating agreement and acceptance of the terms.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required: false): Your name as the client.
* **Agent Name** (`agent_name`, text, required: false): The name of the agent providing the service.
* **Client Company** (`client_company`, text, required: false): If applicable, the name of your company.
* **Agent Company** (`agent_company`, text, required: false): The name of the company the agent works for.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, required: false): Your phone number.
* **Agent Phone** (`agent_phone`, text, required: false): The agent's phone number.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, required: false): Your email address.
* **Agent Email** (`agent_email`, email, required: false): The agent's email address.
* **Client Address** (`client_address`, text, required: false): Your address.
* **Agent Address** (`agent_address`, text, required: false): The agent's address.
* **Client City** (`client_city`, text, required: false): The city you are located in.
* **Agent City** (`agent_city`, text, required: false): The city of the agent's location.
* **Client State** (`client_state`, text, required: false): The state you are located in.
* **Agent State** (`agent_state`, text, required: false): The state of the agent's location.
* **Contract Number** (`contract_number`, text, required: false): A unique identifier for the service agreement.
