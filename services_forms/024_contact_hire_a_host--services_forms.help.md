# Contact Hire A Host - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request hosting services for an event. Fill out this form to provide essential details about your event and get in touch with our team for booking confirmation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields marked as "required".
2. Be as specific as possible with your answers.
3. If you have any special requests, please provide detailed information.
4. Double-check your email address for correct formatting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requester name** (2): This is the full name of the person requesting service.
* **Email address** (3): Enter your email address for booking confirmation.
* **Phone number** (4): Optional, but recommended for scheduling purposes.
* **Event date** (5): The date you need hosting services for.
* **Event location** (6): Provide venue address or online details.
* **Event type** (7): Select the type of event you need hosting for (Corporate, Social, Virtual, or Other).
* **Number of guests** (8): Estimated headcount for your event.
* **Special requirements** (9): Any special requests or accommodations.
* **Additional notes** (10): Any other information for the booking.

## Tips
* Please double-check your answers for accuracy.
* Be specific about your needs and requirements.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us.
