# Consent To Conduct Background Check - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain the applicant's consent to conduct a background check as part of the hiring process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Complete each section of the form carefully and accurately.
2. Make sure to provide your full name, date of birth, social security number, phone number, and current residential address.
3. Check the boxes to authorize the background check and understand its purpose.
4. Sign the form electronically.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Legal Name** (text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (date, required): Please enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Social Security Number** (text, required): Please enter your Social Security number for background verification purposes.
* **Phone Number** (text, required): Please enter your phone number.
* **Current Residential Address** (text, required): Please enter your current residential address.
* **I give The Youth Connection permission to conduct a background check** (select one, required): Please select "I give The Youth Connection permission to conduct a background check" to authorize the check.
* **Types of checks authorized** (select multiple, required): Please select all the types of checks you authorize The Youth Connection to conduct, including:
	+ Criminal Records
	+ Sex Offender Registry
	+ Employment History
	+ Education Verification
* **I understand this check is a requirement of the process** (select one, required): Please select "I understand" to acknowledge that the background check is a requirement of the process.
* **Applicant Electronic Signature (Type Full Name)** (text, required): Please sign your name electronically.
* **Date** (date, required): Please enter the date you are signing this form.
* **Witness Name (if applicable)** (text, optional): Please enter the name of a witness who can attest to your identity.
