# Brand Identity Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect comprehensive data about your business's brand identity. Completing this form will help you establish a strong brand presence and aid in marketing and branding efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Brand Identity Questionnaire, follow these steps:

* Review each field carefully to ensure you understand what is required.
* Answer all questions truthfully and in detail.
* Use the provided hints for guidance if needed.
* Take your time to complete each field thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, required): Enter the official name of your business.
- **Contact Person Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required): Provide the name of the primary contact person for this project.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address for communications.
- **Business Description** (`business_description`, `text`, required): Describe what your business does.
- **Years in Business** (`years_in_business`, `select_one`, required): Select how long your business has operated.
	+ Startup (Less than 1 year)
	+ 1-3 years
	+ 4-10 years
	+ 10+ years
- **Mission Statement** (`mission_statement`, `text`, required): State your business's mission.
- **Unique Value Proposition** (`unique_value_proposition`, `text`, required): Define what makes your business different from competitors.
- **Target Market** (`target_market`, `text`, required): Describe your ideal customer.
- **Customer Demographics** (`customer_demographics`, `text`, required): Specify age, gender, location, income level, or other relevant characteristics of your target audience.
- **Brand Personality** (`brand_personality`, `text`, required): If your brand were a person, describe them.
- **Brand Attributes** (`brand_attributes`, `select_multiple`, required): Select words that describe your brand:
	+ Innovative
	+ Trustworthy
	+ Friendly
	+ Professional
	+ Luxurious
	+ Affordable
	+ Sustainable
	+ Bold
	+ Creative
	+ Reliable
- **Primary Competitors** (`primary_competitors`, `text`, required): Identify your main competitors.
- **Competitor Differentiation** (`competitor_differentiation`, `text`, required): Explain what sets you apart from competitors.
- **Existing Brand Elements** (`existing_brand_elements`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you have existing logos or colors:
	+ None
	+ Logo Only
	+ Colors Only
	+ Full Brand Guidelines
- **Preferred Colors** (`preferred_colors`, `text`, optional): Enter colors you like for your brand.
- **Colors to Avoid** (`colors_to_avoid`, `text`, optional): Specify colors you do not want to associate with your brand.
- **Brand Applications** (`brand_applications`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all places where your brand will be used:
	+ Website
	+ Social Media
	+ Business Cards
	+ Signage
	+ Packaging
	+ Advertising
	+ Merchandise
	+ Presentations
- **Design Inspiration** (`design_inspiration`, `text`, optional): Provide inspiration from brands or designs you admire.
- **Project Goals** (`project_goals`, `text`, required): Define what you want to achieve with this branding project.
- **Timeline Expectations** (`timeline_expectations`, `text`, required): Specify when you need this project completed.
