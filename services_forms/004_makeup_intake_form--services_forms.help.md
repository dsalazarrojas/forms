# Makeup Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients to better understand their needs and preferences, ensuring they receive the best possible service during their visit to a salon or spa.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields as instructed below.
2. Be as detailed as possible when answering the questions to ensure accurate information is collected.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Preferences** (`client_preferences`, text, optional): Please share any personal preferences you have for your service, such as your favorite makeup style or any specific services you're interested in.
- **Makeup Details** (`makeup_details`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your makeup requirements, including your desired look, any specific products you'd like to use, or any areas of your face you'd like to focus on (e.g., eyes, lips, etc.).
- **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): If there are any special requests or accommodations you'd like to make for your service, such as requests for specific lighting or equipment, please include them here.
- **Allergy Info** (`allergy_info`, text, optional): Please list any allergies or medical conditions that may affect your treatment, such as skin allergies or sensitivities. This will help us take necessary precautions to ensure your safety and comfort during the service.
- **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): If you have any pre-existing medical conditions that may impact your service, such as skin conditions or injuries, please list them here.
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Please provide your contact information so we can reach out to you about your service appointment, any updates, or to gather more information if needed.
- **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, optional): If you have any specific payment requests or arrangements, such as payment plans or methods, please include them here.

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible when answering the questions to ensure we can provide the best service for you.
- If you're unsure about any of the fields, please ask a staff member for assistance.
- If you have any additional information or requests, you can attach a file or note to your submission.
- We appreciate your honesty and cooperation in helping us provide the best experience for you.
