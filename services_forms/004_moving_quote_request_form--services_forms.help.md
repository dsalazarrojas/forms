# moving_quote_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The moving quote request form is used to request a moving quote from the user, gathering relevant information to facilitate the quote process.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your submitter information: Enter your name and email address in the "submitter info" field.
2. Describe your move: Enter the details of your move, such as date and time, in the "moving details" field.
3. Specify moving time: Enter the time of your move in the "moving time" field.
4. Indicate if you have files to upload: Select "Yes" or "No" in the "files" field if you have files associated with your move.
5. Provide file information (if applicable): If you have selected "Yes" in the previous field, enter the name, description, and size of your file(s) in the "file name", "file description", and "file size" fields respectively.
6. Enter your contact email and phone number: Enter your email address and phone number in the "submitter email" and "submitter phone" fields.
7. Identify your company (if applicable): Enter your company name in the "submitter company" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submitter info**: Enter your name and email address to enable us to contact you regarding your quote.
* **Moving details**: Describe the details of your move, including date and time.
* **Moving time**: Specify the time of your move.
* **Files**: Indicate if you have files associated with your move. Select "Yes" if you have files to upload.
* **File name**: If you have selected "Yes" in the previous field, enter the name of your file.
* **File description**: If you have selected "Yes" in the previous field, describe the contents of your file.
* **File size**: If you have selected "Yes" in the previous field, enter the size of your file.
* **File type**: If you have selected "Yes" in the previous field, describe the type of your file.
* **Submitter email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your quote.
* **Submitter phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you regarding your quote.
* **Submitter company**: If you have a company, enter your company name for our records.
