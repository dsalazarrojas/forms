# cyber_security_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cyber Security Checklist form is designed to assess an organization's cybersecurity posture by evaluating their adherence to various best practices and protocols for responding to and managing incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Review each section carefully and answer all questions honestly.
2. Select the most accurate response for each question.
3. Be specific and provide clear information to ensure accurate scoring.
4. If you are unsure about a question, please consult with your security team or refer to your organization's policies and procedures.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Best Practices**
  + **General Best Practices** (`general_best_practices`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief overview of your organization's best practices for security, such as their approach to patching, monitoring, and incident response.
* **Password Security** (`password_security`, `number`, optional): Indicate the number of password rotations performed within the last 6 months.
* **Network Security** (`network_security`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' if your organization has a network security policy in place.
* **Access Controls** (`access_controls`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all applicable access controls, such as Firewalls, Virtual Private Network, and Network segmentation.
* **Incident Response** (`incident_response`, `select_one`, optional): Select the incident response approach your organization uses, such as 24/7, 6/24, or 24/7 with rotation.
* **Backup Procedures** (`backup_procedures`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all applicable backup procedures, such as Daily, Weekly, or Monthly backups.
* **Patching** (`patching`, `text`, optional): Provide information about your organization's patching process and schedule.
* **Incident Reporting** (`incident_reporting`, `text`, optional): Describe your organization's incident reporting process and procedures.
* **Security Testing** (`security_testing`, `text`, optional): Explain your organization's security testing process and procedures.
* **Security Training** (`security_training`, `text`, optional): Describe your organization's security training and awareness programs.
* **Incident Response Plan** (`incident_response_plan`, `select_one`, required): Select the tier of incident response planning your organization uses.
* **Incident Triage** (`incident_triage`, `text`, optional): Explain your organization's incident triage process and procedures.
* **Incident Triage Team** (`incident_triage_team`, `text`, optional): Describe your organization's incident triage team composition and procedures.
* **Backup Frequency** (`backup_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select the backup frequency used in your organization, such as Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
* **Backup Location** (`backup_location`, `text`, optional): Specify the location(s) where backups are stored.
* **Data Classification** (`data_classification`, `text`, optional): Explain your organization's data classification and handling procedures.
* **Access Logs** (`access_logs`, `text`, optional): Explain your organization's access logs collection and analysis procedures.
* **Incident Classification** (`incident_classification`, `text`, optional): Describe your organization's incident classification process and procedures.
* **Incident Classification Categorization** (`incident_classification_categorization`, `text`, optional): Explain your organization's incident classification categorization procedures.
* **Incident Classification Description** (`incident_classification_description`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief description of your organization's incident classification procedures.
* **Incident Classification Level** (`incident_classification_level`, `select_one`, required): Select the classification level of an incident, such as Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3.
* **Incident Classification Urgency** (`incident_classification_urgency`, `select_one`, required): Select the urgency level of an incident, such as High, Medium, or Low.
* **Incident Classification Priority** (`incident_classification_priority`, `select_one`, required): Select the priority level of an incident, such as High, Medium, or Low.
* **Incident Classification Certainty** (`incident_classification_certainty`, `select_one`, required): Select the certainty level of an incident, such as Certain, Probable, or Remote.
* **Incident Classification Severity** (`incident_classification_severity`, `select_one`, required): Select the severity level of an incident, such as Critical, High, or Low.

Note: The required fields are marked with an asterisk (*).
