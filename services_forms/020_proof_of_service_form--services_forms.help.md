# Proof Of Service Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Proof Of Service Form is used to document the provision of services to a party, which is crucial for maintaining accurate records and ensuring that all parties involved are informed and satisfied.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the relevant fields with accurate and clear information.
2. Ensure that all fields marked as required are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Proof Of Service Form** (`proof_of_service_form`, text, optional): Fill in a brief description of the service provided.
* **Served Party** (`served_party`, select_one, optional): Select the party that received the service.
* **Date Served** (`date_served`, date, optional): Enter the date the service was provided.
* **Time Served** (`time_served`, time, optional): Enter the time the service was provided.
* **Method Of Service** (`method_of_service`, select_multiple, optional): Select the methods used to provide the service (e.g., Personal Service, Certified Mail, Email, Fax, Mail Box).
* **Service Completed** (`service_completed`, select_one, required): Indicate whether the service has been completed.
* **Service Form Comments** (`proof_of_service_form_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments about the service provided.
* **Additional Documents** (`proof_of_service_form_additional_documents`, text, optional): Attach any additional documents or supporting evidence related to the service.
* **Attachment** (`proof_of_service_form_attachment`, text, optional): Attach any relevant files or evidence related to the service.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out fields like "Service Form Comments" and "Additional Documents".
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to the relevant authority for guidance.
