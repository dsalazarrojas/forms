# Service Provider Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to provide information about your service provider account. Please fill out this form to create a new service provider account.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field. This is where you will receive important updates about your account.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Enter your date of birth in the "date_of_birth" field.
6. Enter your address in the "service_provider_address_1" and "service_provider_address_2" fields.
7. Enter your city in the "service_provider_city" field.
8. Select "Yes" or "No" for whether you are a service provider from the "service_provider_state" field.
9. Enter your zip code in the "service_provider_zip" field.
10. Select "Yes" or "No" for whether you hold a license from the "license_number" field.
11. Select "Yes" or "No" for whether you have certifications from the "certifications" field.
12. Enter a brief description of your services in the "service_description" field.
13. Select "Yes" or "No" for the type of services you provide from the "service_type" field.
14. Select "Yes" or "No" for the sub-type of services you provide from the "service_sub_type" field.
15. Select "Yes" or "No" for the type of license you hold from the "license_type" field.
16. Select "Yes" or "No" for whether you have licenses from the "licenses" field.
17. Select "Yes" or "No" for the type of certifications you hold from the "certifications_type" field.
18. Enter your website in the "service_provider_website" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **last_name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
* **email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **date_of_birth** (date, required): Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **service_provider_address_1** (text, required): Enter your service provider address line 1.
* **service_provider_address_2** (text, required): Enter your service provider address line 2.
* **service_provider_city** (text, required): Enter your city.
* **service_provider_state** (select_one, required): Select "Yes" to indicate you are a service provider.
* **service_provider_zip** (text, required): Enter your zip code.
* **license_number** (select_multiple, required): Select "Yes" to indicate you hold a license.
* **certifications** (select_multiple, required): Select "Yes" to indicate you have certifications.
* **service_description** (text, required): Enter a brief description of your services.
* **service_type** (select_one, required): Select "Yes" to indicate the type of services you provide.
* **service_sub_type** (select_multiple, required): Select "Yes" to indicate the sub-type of services you provide.
* **license_type** (select_one, required): Select "Yes" to indicate the type of license you hold.
* **licenses** (select_multiple, required): Select "Yes" to indicate you have licenses.
* **certifications_type** (select_multiple, required): Select "Yes" to indicate the type of certifications you hold.
* **service_provider_website** (text, required): Enter your website.
