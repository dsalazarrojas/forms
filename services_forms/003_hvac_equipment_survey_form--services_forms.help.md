# HVAC Equipment Survey Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The HVAC (Heating, Ventilation, and Air Conditioning) Equipment Survey Form is designed to collect information about the equipment used in various facilities or locations. This form is used to gather data about the equipment, its status, and its maintenance history. The collected data will be used to improve the management of HVAC equipment and ensure that it is running efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct options for each field, ensuring you provide accurate information about the equipment.
2. Fill out the required fields with the relevant details.
3. Review and submit the form once you have completed all the necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Heating and Ventilating**: Enter a brief description of the heating and ventilating systems in your facility.
* **Hvac Units**: Select 'Yes' if the HVAC unit is currently in use, and 'No' if it is not.
* **Equipment Status**: Select the status of the equipment from the options 'Active' or 'Inactive' to indicate whether it is currently in use or not.
* **Maintenance Status**: Enter a brief description of the current maintenance status of the equipment.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional information or comments about the equipment that you think are relevant.
* **Email**: Enter the contact email address for the person responsible for the equipment.
* **Phone**: Enter the contact phone number for the person responsible for the equipment.
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter the tool or device number assigned to the equipment.
* **Facility ID**: Enter the unique identifier for the facility where the equipment is located.
* **Date Installed**: Select the date the equipment was installed.
* **Last Updated**: Select the last time the equipment was updated.
* **Model Number**: Enter the model number of the equipment.
* **Manufacturer Name**: Enter the name of the manufacturer of the equipment.
* **Serial Number**: Enter the serial number of the equipment.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional information about the equipment that you think are relevant.
* **Equipment Location**: Enter the location where the equipment is installed.
* **Equipment Type**: Enter the type of equipment (e.g., air conditioner, boiler, etc.).
* **Installed By**: Enter the person or company that installed the equipment.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about the equipment.
* **Survey Status**: Select the status of the survey from the options 'Active' or 'Inactive' to indicate whether it is currently being surveyed or not.
