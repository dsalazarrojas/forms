# Lawn Care/Landscaping New Client Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from new clients to provide personalized lawn care and landscaping services. It will help the service provider understand your lawn's size, condition, and preferences, ensuring you receive the best services tailored to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and fill out the required information.
2. Ensure you provide accurate and complete answers to each question.
3. Take a moment to read through the "Field-by-Field Explanation" section for clarification on any unclear fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Client Questionnaire** (`new_client_questionnaire`, note, required: false): This is a brief introduction to the form. Please feel free to write anything you'd like here.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required: false): Enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Lawn Preferences** (`lawn_preferences`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of lawn you have (Lawn Type or Other).
* **Lawn Size** (`lawn_size`, number, required: false): Enter the approximate size of your lawn in square feet.
* **Lawn Condition** (`lawn_condition`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the current condition of your lawn (Dry, Normal, or Damaged).
* **Landscaping Taste** (`landscaping_taste`, text, required: false): Describe your desired style or theme for your lawn landscaping.
* **Services Requests** (`services_requests`, select_one, required: false): Choose the services you require (Aeration, Mowing, Trimming, Pruning, Fertilization, or Weeding).
* **Schedule Preferences** (`schedule_preferences`, date, required: false): Enter your preferred schedule for the services (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, monthly).
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, required: false): Choose how you'd like to be contacted (Phone, Email, or SMS).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false): Add any extra information you think is necessary for the service provider to know.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Service Area** (`service_area`, text, required: false): Specify the service area or location where the services will be performed.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, required: false): Enter your zip code for location identification.

## Tips
- Ensure you provide clear and accurate information to ensure a smooth service provision.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact the service provider.
