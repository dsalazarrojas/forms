## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from users about their desired Facebook Likes and Twitter Followers services. This information is used to determine the best package and service plan for the user's business.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the available "Likes and Followers Package" options and select the one that suits your needs.
2. Choose the type of service you need, whether it's Facebook Likes and Twitter Followers, Facebook Likes Only, Twitter Followers Only, or Custom Likes and Followers.
3. Fill out the required fields for "Contact Info", "Phone number", "Business Name", and "Business Link" to provide a way for us to contact you.
4. Input the number of orders or services you want, along with any additional notes.
5. Finally, fill out any additional fields as required for your specific services.
