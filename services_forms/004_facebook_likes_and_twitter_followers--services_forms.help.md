# Facebook Likes And Twitter Followers - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from users about their desired Facebook Likes and Twitter Followers services. This information is used to determine the best package and service plan for the user's business.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the available "Likes and Followers Package" options and select the one that suits your needs.
2. Choose the type of service you need, whether it's Facebook Likes and Twitter Followers, Facebook Likes Only, Twitter Followers Only, or Custom Likes and Followers.
3. Fill out the required fields for "Contact Info", "Phone number", "Business Name", and "Business Link" to provide a way for us to contact you.
4. Input the number of orders or services you want, along with any additional notes.
5. Finally, fill out any additional fields as required for your specific services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Likes and Followers Package**: Select the package that matches your desired number of likes and followers, e.g., 100 likes, 500 likes, 1000 likes, 2000 likes, 5000 likes.
* **Likes and Followers Type**: Select the type of service you need, whether it's Facebook Likes and Twitter Followers, Buy Facebook Likes Only, Buy Twitter Followers Only, or Custom Likes and Followers.
* **Likes and Followers Price**: Input the price you are willing to pay for the service.
* **Likes and Followers Delivery Time**: Select the time frame within which you want the likes and followers delivered.
* **Time units**: Select the time unit for the delivery time, whether it's Within 24 hours, Within 48 hours, Within 72 hours, or Within 1 week.
* **Contact Info**: Input your contact information to enable us to contact you.
* **Phone number**: Input your phone number.
* **Business Name**: Input your business name.
* **Business Link**: Input the link to your business.
* **Order**: Input the number of orders or services you want.
* **Order Note**: Input any additional notes for your order.
* **Order 2**: Input the number of orders or services you want for the second order.
* **Order Note 2**: Input any additional notes for the second order.
* **Client Facebook Likes**: Input the number of Facebook likes for your client.
* **Client Twitter Followers**: Input the number of Twitter followers for your client.
* **Client Facebook Likes 2**: Input the number of Facebook likes for your client 2.
* **Client Twitter Followers 2**: Input the number of Twitter followers for your client 2.
* **Order Notes**: Input any additional notes for your order.
* **Order Note 3**: Input any additional notes for your order 3.
* **Client Facebook Likes 3**: Input the number of Facebook likes for your client 3.
* **Client Twitter Followers 3**: Input the number of Twitter followers for your client 3.
