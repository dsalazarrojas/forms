# Vendor Creation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Creation Form is designed to collect essential information about a vendor for our records. This form helps us gather and store contact, address, and other relevant details about the vendor.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your first name in the **First Name** field.
2.  Enter your last name in the **Last Name** field.
3.  Provide your company name in the **Company** field (if applicable).
4.  Enter your email address in the **Email** field. Please use the format `your-email@example.com`.
5.  Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
6.  Enter your address in the **Address** field.
7.  Enter your city in the **City** field.
8.  Enter your state in the **State** field.
9.  Enter your zip code in the **Zip** field.
10. Enter your country in the **Country** field.
11. Enter the contact person's name in the **Contact Person** field.
12. If applicable, enter the contact person's email address in the **Contact Email** field.
13. If applicable, enter the contact person's phone number in the **Contact Phone** field.
14. Enter any additional notes about the vendor in the **Note** field.
15. If the vendor is assigned to a specific user, select "Yes" or "No" in the **Assigned User** field.
16. If the vendor is associated with a category, select "Yes" or "No" in the **Category** field.
17. If the vendor is associated with a specific tool, select "Yes" or "No" in the **Assigned Tool** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, **Required**): Enter the first name of the vendor.
* **Last Name** (text, **Required**): Enter the last name of the vendor.
* **Company** (text, **Optional**): Enter the company name of the vendor.
* **Email** (email, **Optional**): Enter the vendor's email address in the format `your-email@example.com`.
* **Phone** (text, **Optional**): Enter the vendor's phone number.
* **Address** (text, **Required**): Enter the vendor's address.
* **City** (text, **Required**): Enter the city where the vendor is located.
* **State** (text, **Required**): Enter the state where the vendor is located.
* **Zip** (text, **Required**): Enter the zip code of the vendor's location.
* **Country** (text, **Required**): Enter the country where the vendor is located.
* **Contact Person** (text, **Required**): Enter the name of the contact person for the vendor.
* **Contact Email** (email, **Optional**): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Contact Phone** (text, **Optional**): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Note** (note, **Optional**): Enter any additional notes about the vendor.
* **Assigned User** (select_one, **Optional**): Select "Yes" if the vendor is assigned to a specific user, "No" otherwise.
* **Category** (select_multiple, **Optional**): Select "Yes" if the vendor is associated with a category, "No" otherwise.
* **Assigned Tool** (select_multiple, **Optional**): Select "Yes" if the vendor is associated with a specific tool, "No" otherwise.
