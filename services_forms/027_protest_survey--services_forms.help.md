# Protest Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Protest Survey form is designed to collect information about a protest event. The form allows users to provide details about the protest, including the description, contact email, location, participants, objectives, outcomes, media coverage, and follow-up information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Protest Survey form, follow these steps:

1.  **Protest Description**: Enter a brief description of the protest in the `Protest Description` field.
2.  **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person associated with the protest in the `Contact Email` field.
3.  **Protest Participation**: Check the box next to `Protest Participation` if you are participating in the protest.
4.  **Survey Participation**: Check the box next to `Survey Participation` if you are participating in the survey.
5.  **Protest Date**: Enter the date of the protest in the `Protest Date` field if applicable.
6.  **Survey Time**: Enter the time of the survey in the `Survey Time` field if applicable.
7.  **Protest Location**: Enter the location of the protest in the `Protest Location` field.
8.  **Survey Location**: Enter the location where the survey was conducted in the `Survey Location` field.
9.  **Protest Participants**: Enter a brief description of the participants in the `Protest Participants` field.
10. **Survey Participants**: Enter a brief description of the participants in the `Survey Participants` field.
11. **Protest Objectives**: Enter a brief description of the objectives of the protest in the `Protest Objectives` field.
12. **Survey Objectives**: Enter a brief description of the objectives of the survey in the `Survey Objectives` field.
13. **Protest Outcomes**: Enter a brief description of the outcomes of the protest in the `Protest Outcomes` field.
14. **Survey Outcomes**: Enter a brief description of the outcomes of the survey in the `Survey Outcomes` field.
15.  **Protest Media**: Enter a brief description of any media coverage of the protest in the `Protest Media` field.
16.  **Survey Media**: Enter a brief description of any media coverage of the survey in the `Survey Media` field.
17.  **Protest Message**: Enter a brief message about the protest in the `Protest Message` field.
18.  **Survey Message**: Enter a brief message about the survey in the `Survey Message` field.
19.  **Protest Follow Up**: Enter a brief description of any follow-up information about the protest in the `Protest Follow Up` field.
20.  **Survey Follow Up**: Enter a brief description of any follow-up information about the survey in the `Survey Follow Up` field.
21.  **Protest Additional Info**: Enter any additional information about the protest in the `Protest Additional Info` field.
22.  **Survey Additional Info**: Enter any additional information about the survey in the `Survey Additional Info` field.
23.  **Protest Suggestions**: Enter any suggestions or comments about the protest in the `Protest Suggestions` field.
24.  **Survey Suggestions**: Enter any suggestions or comments about the survey in the `Survey Suggestions` field.
