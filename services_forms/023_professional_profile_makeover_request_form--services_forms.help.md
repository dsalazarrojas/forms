# Professional Profile Makeover Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Professional Profile Makeover Request Form is used to collect information from users for their profile makeover request. Please use this form to submit your request and we'll do our best to update your profile as soon as possible.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your basic information in the "User Information" section.
2. Select your preferred profile picture in the "Profile Picture" section.
3. Choose your profile background image in the "Profile Background" section.
4. Select your contact method in the "Contact Method" section.
5. Indicate your expected outcome from the profile makeover in the "Expected Outcome" section.
6. Provide any additional comments or information in the "Additional Comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, optional): This section allows you to provide your basic information, such as your name and contact details.
* **Profile Picture** (`profile_picture`, select multiple, optional): Choose your preferred profile picture from the available options.
* **Profile Background** (`profile_background`, text, optional): Select a background image for your profile.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, select one, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted for the profile makeover request.
* **Services** (`services`, select multiple, optional): Select the services you require for your profile makeover.
* **Makeover Request** (`makeover_request`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your profile makeover request.
* **Expected Outcome** (`expected_outcome`, text, optional): Indicate your expected outcome from the profile makeover.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select one, optional): Select how you would like to be contacted for follow-up regarding your profile makeover.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, date, optional): Schedule a follow-up time for further discussion about your profile makeover.
* **Follow-up Time** (`follow_up_time`, time, optional): Specify the time for your follow-up meeting.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or information to help us better understand your profile makeover request.
* **Assignee** (`assigned_to`, select one, optional): Select who you would like to be assigned to your profile makeover request.
