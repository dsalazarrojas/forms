# Service Payment Receipt - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to record and manage payment information for services rendered to clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the Payment Date for the service payment.
2. Enter the Client Name, which is the name of the client who made the payment.
3. Enter the Client Email, which is the email address of the client.
4. Enter the Client Phone number, if available.
5. Enter the Payment Amount, which is the amount paid for the service.
6. Select the Payment Method used for the payment (e.g., Credit Card, Cash, Online Transfer).
7. Select the Payment Status (Paid, Pending, Partially Paid) to indicate the current state of the payment.
8. Enter any additional Service Details, if applicable.
9. Enter any Payment Notes, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date): Enter the date of the payment.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text): Enter the name of the client who made the payment.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email): Enter the email address of the client.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text): Enter the client's phone number, if available.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number): Enter the amount paid for the service.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one): Select the payment method used (e.g., Credit Card, Cash, Online Transfer).
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one): Select the current state of the payment (e.g., Paid, Pending, Partially Paid).
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text): Enter any additional payment terms or conditions.
* **Service Details** (`service_details`, text): Enter any additional details about the service provided.
* **Payment Notes** (`payment_notes`, text): Enter any additional notes about the payment, if necessary.
