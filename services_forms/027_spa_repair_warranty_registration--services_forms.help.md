# Spa Repair Warranty Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a spa repair, collecting essential details for warranty processing and future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the client details (name, address, phone number, etc.).
2. Provide a detailed description of the repair.
3. Select the type of warranty you would like to apply for.
4. Enter the dates for the warranty start and end.
5. Fill out the technician's details (if applicable).
6. Enter the spa's name, address, and phone number.
7. Fill out the submitter's details (name, contact information, etc.).
8. Select whether the repair was submitted by the spa or another individual.
9. Enter the date and time of submission.
10. Finally, confirm the submission is being made to the correct party.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details** (`client_details`, `text`, required): Enter the client's name, address, phone number, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Repair Description** (`repair_description`, `text`, required): Provide a detailed description of the repair performed on the spa.
* **Warranty Type** (`warranty_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of warranty you are applying for (e.g., 'Yes' for yes, 'No' for no).
* **Warranty Start Date** (`warranty_date`, `date`, required): Enter the start date of the warranty period.
* **Warranty End Date** (`warranty_end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the end date of the warranty period.
* **Technician Details** (`technician_details`, `text`, optional): If applicable, provide the technician's details (name, contact information, etc.).
* **Spa Name** (`spa_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the spa where the repair was performed.
* **Spa Address** (`spa_address`, `text`, required): Enter the address of the spa where the repair was performed.
* **Spa Phone** (`spa_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the spa where the repair was performed.
* **Spa Email** (`spa_email`, `text`, optional): Enter the email address of the spa where the repair was performed.
* **Submitter Details** (`submitter_details`, `text`, required): Fill out the submitter's details (name, contact information, etc.).
* **Submitter Contact** (`submitter_contact`, `text`, optional): Enter the contact information for the submitter.
* **Submitter Notes** (`submitter_notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments from the submitter.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `select_multiple`, required): Select whether the repair was submitted by the spa or another individual.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, `date`, required): Enter the date and time of submission.
* **Submitted To** (`submitted_to`, `text`, required): Confirm the submission is being made to the correct party.
