# thanksgiving_bake_sale_pre_order_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow customers to pre-order baked goods for the Thanksgiving bake sale. This form gathers information about the customer, their order details, and any special instructions they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "thanksgiving_bake_sale" page.
2. Enter your **name** in the "Name" field.
3. Enter your **email address** in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your **phone number** in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter the **quantity** of items you wish to order in the "Quantity" field.
6. Enter any **special instructions** you have in the "Special Instructions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **thanksgiving_bake_sale** (Page 1): This is the main page of the form where you can select the "thanksgiving_bake_sale" option.
* **Name** (Page 2): Enter your name to associate it with your order.
* **Email** (Page 2): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you about your order.
* **Phone** (Page 3): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you with any questions or to confirm your order.
* **Quantity** (Page 4): Enter the number of items you would like to order.
* **Special Instructions** (Page 5): If you have any special instructions or requests for your order, enter them here, such as "Please make sure to include no nuts" or "Add extra sugar to the cake".
