# facialConsultationForm - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about individuals seeking facial consultation services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Fill in your Last Name.
3. Enter your Email address.
4. Confirm your Email address.
5. Enter your Contact Number.
6. Enter your Birth Date.
7. Select your Gender.
8. If applicable, provide any Medical Condition.
9. Enter the name of your Emergency Contact.
10. Enter the Emergency Contact Number.
11. Specify the relation of your Emergency Contact.
12. Confirm your Emergency Contact Number.
13. If applicable, enter the name of your Medical Provider.
14. Enter the address of your Medical Provider.
15. Enter the city of your Medical Provider.
16. Enter the state of your Medical Provider.
17. Enter the zip of your Medical Provider.
18. Enter the phone number of your Medical Provider.
19. Enter the NPI of your Medical Provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Please fill in your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Please fill in your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Confirm Email** (`email_confirmation`, `email`, required: false): Confirm your email address for verification purposes.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact number.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter your birth date in the format 'MM/DD/YYYY'.
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select your gender.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `text`, required: false): If you have any medical condition, please provide it.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter the number of your emergency contact.
* **Relation** (`emergency_contact_relation`, `text`, required: false): Specify the relation of your emergency contact.
* **Confirm Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number_confirmation`, `text`, required: false): Confirm your emergency contact number for verification purposes.
* **Medical Provider** (`medical_provider`, `text`, required: false): If you have a medical provider, please enter their name.
* **Medical Provider Address** (`medical_provider_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address of your medical provider.
* **Medical Provider City** (`medical_provider_city`, `text`, required: false): Enter the city of your medical provider.
* **Medical Provider State** (`medical_provider_state`, `text`, required: false): Enter the state of your medical provider.
* **Medical Provider Zip** (`medical_provider_zip`, `text`, required: false): Enter the zip of your medical provider.
* **Medical Provider Phone** (`medical_provider_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of your medical provider.
* **Medical Provider NPI** (`medical_provider_npi`, `text`, required: false): Enter the NPI of your medical provider.
