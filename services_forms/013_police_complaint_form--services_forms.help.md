# Police Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Police Complaint Form is used to report and manage information related to police complaints, providing a structured approach for users to input necessary details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form instructions and guidelines carefully before beginning.
2. Ensure you have all required information and supporting documents ready for input.
3. Fill out the form with the requested information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Description (Page 1)** (`description`, text, required)
	+ A brief description of the incident being reported is necessary for the officer to understand the context of the complaint.
* **Address (Page 1)** (`address`, text, required)
	+ Enter the exact address where the incident occurred, or the last known address of the individual involved.
* **Contact Number (Page 1)** (`contact_number`, text, required)
	+ Enter the contact information of the person reporting the incident, including their phone number.
* **Witness Details (Page 1)** (`witness_details`, note, required)
	+ Provide a list of witness names, contact information, and any relevant descriptions.
* **Incident Date (Page 1)** (`incident_date`, date, required)
	+ Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time (Page 1)** (`incident_time`, time, required)
	+ Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Suspect Details (Page 1)** (`suspect_details`, note, required)
	+ Enter the names and descriptions of individuals involved in the incident.
* **Crime Description (Page 1)** (`crime_description`, text, required)
	+ A detailed description of the crime or incident is necessary for the officer to investigate properly.
 
## Tips
- Ensure accuracy and completeness when filling out the form.
- Use clear and concise language when providing descriptions.
- Attach any supporting documentation or evidence as instructed by the officer.
 
This guide is designed to be a quick reference for users to complete the form accurately and efficiently. By following these steps and explanations, you can ensure that your complaint is handled properly and in a timely manner.
