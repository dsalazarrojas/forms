# Template Schedule CandyTea Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Template Schedule CandyTea Form is used to collect information about a template schedule for the "CandyTea" service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Service Name** in the first field. This is the name of the service being scheduled.
2. Select the **Schedule Date** using the date picker provided. This is the date when the service is scheduled to take place.
3. Choose the **Schedule Time** using the time picker provided. This is the time when the service is scheduled to start.
4. Select the **End Time** using the time picker provided. This is the time when the service is scheduled to end.
5. Enter the **Service Duration**. This is the length of time the service will run.
6. Enter the **Service Duration Unit**. This is the unit of time for the service duration (e.g., minutes, hours, etc.).
7. Enter the **Service Frequency**. This is how often the service will be performed (e.g., daily, weekly, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Name** (`service_name`, text, required/optional): The name of the service being scheduled.
* **Schedule Date** (`schedule_date`, date, required/optional): The date when the service is scheduled to take place.
* **Schedule Time** (`schedule_time`, time, required/optional): The time when the service is scheduled to start.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required/optional): The time when the service is scheduled to end.
* **Service Duration** (`service_duration`, number, required/optional): The length of time the service will run.
* **Service Duration Unit** (`service_duration_unit`, text, required/optional): The unit of time for the service duration (e.g., minutes, hours, etc.).
* **Service Frequency** (`service_frequency`, text, required/optional): The frequency of the service (e.g., daily, weekly, etc.).

## Tips
* Please ensure the service name is clear and descriptive.
* Use the correct format for the schedule date and time.
* Use the correct unit of time for the service duration.
* Select the correct frequency for the service.
* Review all fields carefully before submitting the form.
* Double-check the form for any errors before submitting it to your supervisor or administrator.
