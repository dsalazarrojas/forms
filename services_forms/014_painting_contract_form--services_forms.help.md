# Painting Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Painting Contract Form is used by property managers to document painting contracts with tenants or other stakeholders. It collects essential information for painting contracts, including contract details, signatures, and agreement terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the relevant information.
2. For each field, ensure you provide accurate and complete information.
3. Review and confirm all fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **painting_contract** (1, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the painting contract.
* **property_manager_info** (2, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information about the property management.
* **tenant_info** (3, `text`, optional): Enter any relevant information about the tenant or the property.
* **painters_info** (4, `text`, optional): Provide any relevant information about the painters or painting crew.
* **agreement_terms** (5, `text`, optional): Enter any terms or conditions related to the agreement.
* **agreement_terms_2** (12, `text`, optional): Enter any additional terms or conditions related to the agreement.
* **painting_contract_terms** (6, `text`, optional): Enter any terms or conditions specific to the painting contract.
* **painting_contract_terms_2** (13, `text`, optional): Enter any additional terms or conditions specific to the painting contract.
* **tenant_signature** (7, `text`, required): Sign the document to confirm your acceptance of the agreement terms.
* **property_manager_signature** (8, `text`, required): Sign the document as the property manager to confirm your approval.
* **date_signed** (9, `date`, optional): Enter the date the contract was signed.
* **painting_contract_number** (10, `number`, required): Enter a unique contract number.
* **painting_contract_version** (11, `select_one`, required): Select whether this is a new or updated contract.
* **property_manager_signature_2** (14, `text`, required): Sign the document as the property manager to confirm your approval for the second part of the contract.
* **tenant_signature_2** (15, `text`, optional): Sign the document as the tenant to confirm your acceptance of the second part of the agreement.
* **date_signed_2** (16, `date`, optional): Enter the date the second part of the contract was signed.
* **painting_contract_number_2** (17, `number`, required): Enter a unique contract number for the second part of the contract.
* **painting_contract_version_2** (18, `select_one`, required): Select whether this is a new or updated contract for the second part of the contract.

## Tips
* Double-check all fields for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* Ensure all required fields are filled in before proceeding.
* Review the terms and conditions carefully before signing the document.
