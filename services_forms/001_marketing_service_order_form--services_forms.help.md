# marketing_service_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The marketing service order form is used to collect information for marketing services orders from clients or customers. It is essential to complete this form accurately to ensure that the marketing team receives the necessary details to fulfill the order correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief service request in the "service_request" field.
2. Provide detailed project information in the "project_details" field.
3. Enter a project budget, if applicable, in the "project_budget" field.
4. Specify the project start and end dates in the "project_start_date" and "project_end_date" fields, respectively.
5. Enter your name in the "name" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
7. Select your preferred contact method in the "contact_method" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **service_request** (service_request, text, required): Enter a brief description of the marketing service you are requesting.
* **project_details** (project_details, text, required): Provide an accurate and detailed description of the project, including its objectives, scope, and any specific requirements.
* **project_budget** (project_budget, number, optional): Enter the budget for the project, if applicable.
* **project_start_date** (project_start_date, date, required): Select the date when the project is expected to start.
* **project_end_date** (project_end_date, date, optional): Select the date when the project is expected to end, if known.
* **name** (name, text, required): Enter your name.
* **email** (email, text, required): Enter your email address.
* **contact_method** (contact_method, select_one, required): Select your preferred contact method (phone, email, in person, or chat).
