# Emergency Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emergency Claim Form is a tool used to report incidents or emergencies that require immediate attention. This form collects relevant information to help our team respond promptly and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the options that apply to your situation by choosing from the dropdown menu on page 1.
2. Provide a brief description of what happened in the text field on page 2.
3. Enter any additional details about your report in the text field on page 3.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Emergency Response Required** (emergency_claim_form_page_1, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you require immediate emergency response. This will alert our team to prioritize your incident.
	+ Select 'No' if you do not require immediate emergency response.
* **Report Incident** (report_incident, text, required: false):
	+ Enter a brief description of what happened.
* **Provide Details** (claim_report_details, text, required: false):
	+ Enter any additional information about your report. This will help our team understand the situation better.
