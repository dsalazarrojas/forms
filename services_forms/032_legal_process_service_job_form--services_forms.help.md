# Legal Process Service Job Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request legal process service jobs for various types of documents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of service you need from the "Service Type" dropdown menu.
2. Choose the relevant client details from the "Client Info" dropdown menu.
3. Provide additional service details in the text field.
4. Select the documents required for the service in the "Documents" dropdown menu.
5. Enter the payment amount in the payment field.
6. Select the payment method (this field is currently empty, but will be populated in the future).
7. Enter the payment date for the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Type** (legal_process_service_job_form_service_type): Select the type of service you need (e.g., Court Filing, Document Retrieval, etc.).
* **Client Info** (legal_process_service_job_form_client_info): Choose the relevant client details for the service (e.g., Select One, Select Two, etc.).
* **Service Details** (legal_process_service_job_form_service_details): Provide any additional details about the service you need.
* **Documents** (legal_process_service_job_form_documents): Select the documents required for the service (e.g., Select One, Select Two, etc.).
* **Payment** (legal_process_service_job_form_payment): Enter the payment amount for the service.
* **Payment Date** (legal_process_service_job_form_payment_date): Enter the date when payment is due.
* **Payment Method** (legal_process_service_job_form_payment_method): Select the payment method (this field will be populated in the future).

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct service type and client details to ensure accurate processing of your request.
* Enter accurate payment information to avoid delays in processing your request.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact our support team for assistance.
