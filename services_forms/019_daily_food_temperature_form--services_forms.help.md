# Daily Food Temperature Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Daily Food Temperature Form" is a tool used for monitoring and recording the temperature of food items to ensure they are stored and handled properly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select all food items that need to be monitored from the "Food Item" dropdown menu.
2. Enter the initial temperature of the food item in the "Initial Temperature" field.
3. Enter the final temperature of the food item in the "Final Temperature" field.
4. Enter the start time of monitoring in the "Monitor Start" field in the format `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM`.
5. Enter the end time of monitoring in the "Monitor End" field in the format `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM`.
6. Choose the storage condition of the food item from the "Storage Condition" dropdown menu (Frozen, Refrigerated, or Ambient).
7. Choose who is responsible for the food item from the "Assigned To" dropdown menu.
8. Choose who submitted the food temperature data from the "Submitted By" dropdown menu.
9. Enter the time of submission in the "Submitted At" field in the format `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM`.
10. Provide any additional notes or comments about the food item in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Food Item**: Select all food items that need to be monitored.
* **Initial Temperature**: The temperature of the food item at the start of monitoring.
* **Final Temperature**: The temperature of the food item at the end of monitoring.
* **Monitor Start**: Enter the start time of monitoring in the format `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM`.
* **Monitor End**: Enter the end time of monitoring in the format `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM`.
* **Storage Condition**: Choose how the food item was stored (Frozen, Refrigerated, or Ambient).
* **Assigned To**: Choose who is responsible for the food item.
* **Submitted By**: Choose who submitted the food temperature data.
* **Submitted At**: Enter the time of submission in the format `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM`.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the food item.
