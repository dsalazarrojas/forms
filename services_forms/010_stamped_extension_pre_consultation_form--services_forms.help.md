# stamped_extension_pre_consultation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from customers who are interested in consulting services. Fill out this form to inquire about our services and schedule a consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
5. Select a date and time for the consultation in the Date and Time fields.
6. Choose the services you are interested in from the What services are you interested in? field.
7. If you have any other services in mind that are not listed, enter them in the Other field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (First Name, text, optional): Enter your first name here.
- **Last Name** (Last Name, text, optional): Enter your last name here.
- **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter your email address here. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
- **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number here.
- **Date** (Date, date, optional): Select a date for the consultation.
- **Time** (Time, time, optional): Select a time for the consultation.
- **What services are you interested in?** (select_one, optional): Choose one of the listed options: Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
- **Other** (text, optional): If you have any other services in mind that are not listed, enter them here.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the email and phone number you enter are accurate and up-to-date.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to our support team for assistance.
- You can choose one of the listed services or enter a different service in the Other field.
