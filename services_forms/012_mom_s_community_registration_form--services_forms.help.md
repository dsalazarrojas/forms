# Mom's Community Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mom's Community Registration Form is designed to collect personal and children's information for registration in a community or organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name and last name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your email address in the email field.
3. Enter your phone number in the phone field (optional).
4. Enter the number of children you have.
5. Enter your date of birth.
6. Enter the names of your children in the children names field.
7. Enter the ages of your children in the children ages field, separated by commas.
8. Select how you heard about the community.
9. Enter any additional comments in the additional comments field.
10. Review and agree to the terms of use.
11. Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms of use.
12. Confirm that you have read and agree to the privacy policy.
13. Enter your address.
14. Enter your zip code.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`registrant_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name, please.
* **Last Name** (`registrant_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name, please.
* **Email** (`registrant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`registrant_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **How Many Children** (`child_count`, number, required): Enter the number of children you have.
* **Date of Birth** (`birth_date`, date, required): Enter your date of birth.
* **Children Names** (`child_names`, note, required): Enter the names of your children, separated by commas.
* **Children Ages** (`child_ages`, note, required): Enter the ages of your children, separated by commas.
* **How Did You Hear** (`how_did_you_hear`, select_one, required): Select how you heard about the community.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments.
* **Agreement** (`registrant_agreement`, select_one, required): Select if you have read and agree to the terms of use.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`registrant_terms_accepted`, select_one, required): Confirm if you have read and agree to the terms of use.
* **Privacy Policy** (`privacy_policy_accepted`, select_one, required): Confirm if you have read and agree to the privacy policy.
* **Additional Comments 2** (`additional_comments_2`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments (optional).
* **Address** (`registrant_address`, text, required): Enter your address.
* **Zip Code** (`registrant_zip`, text, required): Enter your zip code.
