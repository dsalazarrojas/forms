# Service Activity Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Activity Log form is designed to record and track service-related activities. This form captures relevant information about each service activity, including job IDs, names, dates, times, service areas, job types, service teams, and any additional comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Job ID associated with the service activity.
2. Enter the Job Name (if applicable).
3. Enter the Activity Date (in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`).
4. Enter the Activity Time (in the format `HH:MM:SS`).
5. Select the Service Area related to the activity.
6. Select the Job Type associated with the activity.
7. If applicable, select the members of the service team who participated in the activity.
8. Enter any additional comments about the activity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job ID** (`job_id`):
	+ A unique identifier for the job.
* **Job Name** (`job_name`):
	+ A brief description of the job.
* **Activity Date** (`activity_date`):
	+ The date the service activity took place.
* **Activity Time** (`activity_time`):
	+ The time the service activity took place.
* **Service Area** (`service_area`):
	+ The area of service provided (e.g., "Service Area 1", "Service Area 2", etc.).
* **Job Type** (`job_type`):
	+ The type of job or service activity performed.
* **Service Team** (`service_team`):
	+ A list of team members who participated in the activity.
* **Activity Comments** (`activity_comments`):
	+ Any additional notes or comments about the service activity.
