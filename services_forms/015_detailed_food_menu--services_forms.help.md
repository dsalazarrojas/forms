# Detailed Food Menu - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Detailed Food Menu form is designed to create or update a detailed menu for a business, including information about food items, categories, prices, availability, and status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant pages and fields as required.
2. Fill in the necessary information for each field.
3. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Food Item
* **Food Item** (`food_item`, text, optional): Enter the name of the food item.
*   **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Select the category for the food item.
*   **Price Range** (`price_range`, select_multiple, optional): Select the price range for the food item.

### 2. Food Description
*   **Food Description** (`food_description`, text, optional): Enter a detailed description of the food item.
*   **Food Availability** (`food_availability`, select_multiple, optional): Select the availability of the food item.
*   **Food Image** (`food_image`, text, optional): Enter the image URL or upload the image for the food item.
*   **Food Availability Time** (`food_availability_time`, time, optional): Enter the availability time for the food item.

### 3. Additional Information
*   **Food Status** (`food_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the food item (Active or Inactive).
*   **Food Item Image** (`food_item_image`, text, optional): Enter the image URL or upload the image for the food item.
*   **Food Menu** (`food_menu`, select_multiple, optional): Select the menu type (Yes or No).
*   **Food Availability Time From** (`food_availability_time_from`, time, optional): Enter the availability time from.
*   **Food Availability Time To** (`food_availability_time_to`, time, optional): Enter the availability time to.
*   **Food Price From** (`food_price_from`, number, optional): Enter the price from.
*   **Food Price To** (`food_price_to`, number, optional): Enter the price to.
*   **Food Status 2** (`food_status_2`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the food item (Active or Inactive).
*   **Food Item 2** (`food_item_2`, text, optional): Enter the name of the food item 2.
*   **Food Description 2** (`food_description_2`, text, optional): Enter a detailed description of the food item 2.
*   **Food Image 2** (`food_image_2`, text, optional): Enter the image URL or upload the image for the food item 2.
*   **Availability** (`availability`, select_multiple, optional): Select the availability of the food item (Yes or No).
*   **Availability Time From** (`availability_time_from`, time, optional): Enter the availability time from.
*   **Availability Time To** (`availability_time_to`, time, optional): Enter the availability time to.
*   **Price** (`price`, number, optional): Enter the price of the food item.
*   **Availability 2** (`availability_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select the availability of the food item 2 (Yes or No).
