# General Maintenance Services Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about customer and service requirements for general maintenance services. It's intended for businesses or individuals offering maintenance services to gather essential details to fulfill customer requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your personal and contact details on the first page.
2.  Describe your maintenance order in the "Order Details" field.
3.  Specify your contact information, including name, email, and phone number.
4.  Select the order status: Active, Pending, or Completed.
5.  Choose the services you require (Air Conditioner Service, Fridge Service, or Pump Service).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Customer Information (Customer Info)** (`customer_info`, Text, Optional): 
    Enter your name and relevant contact details to ensure the customer service team can contact you about your order.

*   **Order Details (Order Details)** (`order_details`, Text, Optional): 
    Describe the maintenance service you need, including any specific requirements or requests.

*   **Contact Info (Contact Info)** (`contact_info`, Text, Optional): 
    Provide your name, email, and phone number to enable the customer service team to contact you for order updates or further information.

*   **Order Status (Order Status)** (`order_status`, Select One, Optional): 
    Choose the status of your order: Active, Pending, or Completed. This helps the customer service team track and manage your order.

*   **Service Requested (Service Requested)** (`service_requested`, Select Multiple, Optional): 
    Select the maintenance services you need: Air Conditioner Service, Fridge Service, or Pump Service. This will help the customer service team understand your needs accurately.
