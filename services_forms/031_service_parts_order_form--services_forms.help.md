# service_parts_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect customer and service information for ordering services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your customer information in the "Customer Info" section.
2. Enter the service details in the "Service Info" section.
3. Enter the parts required for the service in the "Parts Info" section.
4. Finally, provide any additional order details in the "Order Details" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, optional): Enter your personal details such as name, contact number, and address to help us understand who you are and how to contact you.
* **Service Info** (`service_info`, text, optional): Describe the service you require. This could include any specific tasks or procedures needed to be done.
* **Parts Info** (`parts_info`, text, optional): List the parts or materials needed for the service.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or information about the order such as deadlines, urgency, or specific requirements.
