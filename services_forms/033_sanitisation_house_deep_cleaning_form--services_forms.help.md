### Sanitisation House Deep Cleaning Form - Help Guide

### Purpose

The Sanitisation House Deep Cleaning Form is used to request deep cleaning services for a residential property. This form will guide you through the necessary information required to book a deep cleaning service for your home.

### How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the cleaning service you are requesting.
2. Provide a description of the service you need.
3. Select the type of cleaning service you require from the available options.
4. Enter the address of the property to be cleaned.
5. Specify the contact person's name and email.
6. Choose a start and end date for the cleaning service.
7. Select a start and end time for the service if required.
8. Select any additional services you may need.
9. If you have any additional comments or information, enter it in the "Additional Comments" fields.
10. Submit the form to complete your booking request.
