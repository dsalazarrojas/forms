# Service Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to submit service requests to the support team for assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out.
3. Review the form before submitting it to ensure that all information is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Request Form Fields**: This field is the main title of the form, and it is required.
* **Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on official documents or as you would like to be referred to. This field is required.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address associated with your service request. This field is required.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number where you can be reached. This field is required.
* **Service Type**: Select the type of service you are requesting from the dropdown options. This field is optional.
* **Service Request**: Describe the service you are requesting in as much detail as possible. This field is required.
* **Date**: Enter the date you would like to schedule the service. This field is required.
* **Time**: Enter the time you would like to schedule the service. This field is required.
* **Message**: If you have any additional information or comments, please include them here. This field is optional.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool you would like to be assigned to your service request from the dropdown options. This field is optional.
* **Submit**: Once you have filled out all required fields, click this button to submit your service request.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* Double-check your email address for accuracy to ensure that the support team can contact you.
* If you have any additional information or comments, please include them in the message field.
* If you have any special requests or preferences, such as a specific time or date, please include them in the service request field.
