# Tenant Reference Verification - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tenant Reference Verification form is used to gather information about a tenant from the user's perspective. This form helps to verify the tenant's reference by asking a series of questions that cover different aspects of their experience with the tenant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the form, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Use the options provided for questions with dropdown menus to select the best fit for your answer.
3. If a question is not applicable or not relevant, you can leave it blank.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure you have provided accurate and complete information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How did you meet this tenant?**: This question helps us understand the context of your relationship with the tenant. Please describe how you met the tenant, such as through a mutual friend, online, or at work.
* **How long have you known the tenant?**: This question helps us gauge the depth of your relationship with the tenant. Please select the duration of your relationship with the tenant.
* **How would you describe your relationship with the tenant?**: This question helps us understand your personal connection with the tenant. Please select the type of relationship you have with the tenant.
* **How would you describe the tenant's personality?**: This question helps us get an idea of the tenant's character. Please select all the personality traits that you think best describe the tenant.
* **How would you describe the tenant's behavior?**: This question helps us understand how the tenant operates. Please select the type of behavior you have observed in the tenant.
* **What is your experience with the tenant like?**: This question is a free-form text field where you can describe your experience with the tenant in as much detail as possible.
* **How would you rate your experience with the tenant?**: This question helps us gauge your satisfaction with the tenant. Please rate your experience on a scale of 1-5.
* **How would you rate the quality of service provided by the tenant?**: This question helps us understand how well the tenant performs their services. Please rate the quality of service on a scale of 1-5.
* **Would you recommend the tenant for future business?**: This question helps us determine if you would recommend the tenant to others. Please select 'True' if you would recommend the tenant or 'False' if you would not.
* **Would you like to contact the tenant again?**: This question helps us know if you would consider working with the tenant again. Please select 'True' if you would like to contact the tenant again or 'False' if you would not.
