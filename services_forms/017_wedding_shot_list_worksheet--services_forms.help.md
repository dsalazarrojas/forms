# Wedding Shot List Worksheet - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information for a wedding shot list, which is a list of essential tasks and activities to be performed on a wedding day.

## How To Complete This Form

- Follow these steps to complete this form:
	* Answer the questions on each page in order.
	* Make sure to provide accurate and relevant information for each field.
	* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Wedding Shot List Worksheet** (`wedding_shot_list_worksheet`): This is the main section where you can enter the list of tasks and activities to be performed on the wedding day.
- **Photographer Info** (`photographer_info`): Enter the name and contact information of the photographer responsible for the wedding.
- **Photographer Contact** (`photographer_contact`): Enter the email address of the photographer to ensure you can contact them with any questions or concerns.
- **Shot List Items** (`shot_list_items`): Select the essential shot list items to be captured on the wedding day (e.g., wedding party, family, friends, etc.).
- **Photographer Preferences** (`photographer_preferences`): Enter any specific preferences or requirements you have for the photographer.
- **Shot List Preferences** (`shot_list_preferences`): Select one or more preferences for the shot list, such as indoor vs. outdoor shots.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Enter any additional information you want to share with the photographer.
- **Photographer Comments** (`photographer_comments`): Enter any comments or notes for the photographer.
- **Date/Time** (`date_time`): Enter the wedding date and time.
- **Expected Attendance** (`expected_attendance`): Estimate the number of people expected to attend the wedding.
- **Budget** (`budget`): Enter the allocated budget for the photographer's services.
- **Services Form** (`services_form`): Select the type of services the photographer will provide (e.g., standard, premium, etc.).
