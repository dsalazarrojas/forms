# jumpstart_your_journey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users start their journey in a way that is both effective and efficient. The form is used to capture relevant information about the user's journey, including their fitness journey, well-being, and other relevant details. This information will be used to provide personalized recommendations and insights to help the user achieve their goals.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all the required fields, which are marked as "required".
2. Provide accurate and complete information about your journey in the text fields.
3. If you are unsure about any of the fields, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **jumpstart_your_journey** (`title`, text, false): Enter the title of your journey, such as "Starting a Fitness Journey".
* **fitness_journey** (`fitness_journey`, text, false): Describe your fitness journey, including your goals and aspirations.
* **well_being** (`well_being`, text, false): Discuss your current state of well-being, including any challenges or concerns you may be facing.
* **started** (`started`, text, false): Describe how you have started your journey so far, including any progress you have made.
* **services_forms_id** (`services_forms_id`, number, false): Enter a unique identifier for your journey, if required by your administrator.
* **assigned_tool_id** (`assigned_tool_id`, text, false): If you have been assigned a specific tool for your journey, enter its ID here.
* **output_file** (`output_file`, text, false): If you would like to receive the output of this form as a file, enter your preferred file format here.
* **chat_jimmy** (`chat_jimmy`, text, false): If you have any questions or concerns about your journey, enter them here.

## Tips

* Be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling in the text fields.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, refer to the field-by-field explanation above.
* If you have any feedback or suggestions for improving this form, please let us know!
