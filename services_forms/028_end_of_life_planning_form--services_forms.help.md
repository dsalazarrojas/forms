# end_of_life_planning_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about a deceased individual, including their name, relationship status, and date of birth. This information is used to facilitate end-of-life planning and ensure that the necessary steps are taken to respect the wishes of the deceased and their loved ones.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the deceased in the "name" field.
2. Select the relationship status of the deceased from the options provided.
3. Enter the date of birth of the deceased in the "date of birth" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name (Text)**: Enter the name of the deceased, as they were known.
* **relationship-status (Select One)**: Select the relationship status of the deceased, such as "Active" or "Inactive".
* **name of deceased (Text)**: Enter the name of the deceased, exactly as it was at the time of their passing.
* **date of birth (Date)**: Enter the date of birth of the deceased in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
