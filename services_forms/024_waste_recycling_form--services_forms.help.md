# Waste Recycling Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Waste Recycling Form is used to collect information about a waste recycling service. This form is used by customers or clients to provide details about their waste recycling needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of waste from the options provided.
2. Enter the amount of waste in the specified units (e.g., pounds, kilograms, etc.).
3. Provide your name and email address to ensure we can contact you.
4. Optionally, provide your phone number so we can reach out to you for further questions.
5. Add any additional notes about your waste recycling needs.
6. Choose a date and time for the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Waste Type** (`waste_type`, select_one): Select the type of waste you need assistance with (e.g., Landfill, Hazardous, Organics, etc.).
* **Amount** (`amount`, number): Enter the amount of waste you need assistance with in the specified units (e.g., pounds, kilograms, etc.).
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text): Enter your name so we can contact you.
* **Contact E-Mail** (`contact_email`, email): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your waste recycling service.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Optionally, enter your phone number so we can reach out to you for further questions.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note): Add any additional notes about your waste recycling needs.
* **Date of Service** (`date_of_service`, date): Choose a date for the waste recycling service.
* **Time of Service** (`time_of_service`, time): Choose a time for the service.
