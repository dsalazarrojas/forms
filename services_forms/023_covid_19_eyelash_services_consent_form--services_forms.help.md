# Covid 19 Eyelash Services Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from customers for the Covid-19 related eyelash services. It is crucial to understand the terms and conditions of the service before proceeding.

## How To Complete This Form
This form is divided into several sections. Please complete it in the following order:

1. Enter your full name in the "Covid 19 Eyelash Services Consent Form" field. This is the title of the form and is used for identification purposes.
2. In the "I consent to the terms and conditions" field, type "yes" or "I agree" to indicate your consent.
3. In the "I agree to obeying the precautions" field, type "yes" or "I agree" to acknowledge your understanding of the precautions.
4. In the "How will you be performing the service" field, describe how you plan to perform the service.
5. In the "What is your contact information" field, provide your name, address, phone number, and email address.
6. In the "Email" field, enter your email address.
7. In the "Phone" field, enter your phone number.
8. In the "Terms and conditions" field, read and agree to the terms and conditions of the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Covid 19 Eyelash Services Consent Form** (`title`, text, required): Enter your full name to help us identify you.
* **I consent to the terms and conditions** (`customer_consent`, text, required): Type "yes" or "I agree" to indicate your consent to the terms and conditions.
* **I agree to obeying the precautions** (`agree_precautions`, text, required): Type "yes" or "I agree" to acknowledge your understanding of the precautions.
* **How will you be performing the service** (`service_procedure`, text, required): Describe how you plan to perform the service. This includes any specific methods or precautions you will take.
* **What is your contact information** (`contact_information`, text, required): Provide your name, address, phone number, and email address for communication purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Terms and conditions** (`terms_conditions`, text, required): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the service.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you complete all the required fields accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* Once you have completed the form, click the submit button to send it to us.
* We will review and process your form accordingly.
