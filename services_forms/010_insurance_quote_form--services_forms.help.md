# Insurance Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The insurance quote form is used to collect information to provide you with an accurate insurance quote. This form collects information such as policy type, coverage area, coverage start date and end date, and other relevant details to give you a precise quote.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button when you have completed all required fields.
2. Fill out each field carefully and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Coverage Area** (`coverage_area`, `select_multiple`): Select one or more areas you would like to cover with your policy.
* **Rate** (`rate`, `number`): Enter your rate amount.
* **Coverage Start Date** (`coverage_start_date`, `date`): Enter the date when you would like your coverage to start.
* **Coverage End Date** (`coverage_end_date`, `date`): Enter the date when you would like your coverage to end.
* **Coverage Length** (`coverage_length`, `number`): Enter the length of your coverage.
* **Policy Type** (`policy_type`, `select_one`): Select the type of policy you are interested in.
* **Policy Status** (`policy_status`, `select_one`): Select your policy status.
* **Coverage Frequency** (`coverage_frequency`, `select_multiple`): Select how frequently you would like your coverage to be applied.
* **Policy Frequency** (`policy_frequency`, `select_one`): Select how frequently you would like to pay your policy.
