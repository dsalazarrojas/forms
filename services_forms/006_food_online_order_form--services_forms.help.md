# Food Online Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Online Order Form is a tool for users to order food online, specifying their preferences and requirements for the meal they wish to order. Please fill out this form to ensure that our team can prepare and deliver your order correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the meal you would like to order from the options provided in the **Select Your Meal** field.
2. Choose your dietary preference (Veggie or Non-Veggie) in the **Choose Your Diet** field.
3. Specify the texture of the food you prefer (Soft or Hard) in the **Choose Texture** field.
4. Select the desired temperature of the food (Hot or Cold) in the **Select Temperature** field.
5. If applicable, enter the quantity of food you would like to order in the **Select Quantity** field. Please note that this field is only available for select_one options.
6. Provide any additional delivery details, such as special instructions or instructions for the delivery address, in the **Delivery Details** field.
7. Enter your payment details in the **Payment Details** field. Please note that this field is intended for users to specify payment method or any special payment instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select Your Meal**: Please select one option from the list that best represents the food item you would like to order.
* **Choose Your Diet**: Indicate whether you have a dietary restriction of Veggie or Non-Veggie. Please choose one option that best fits your requirement.
* **Choose Texture**: Select the texture (Soft or Hard) that you prefer for your meal.
* **Select Temperature**: Choose the desired temperature (Hot or Cold) for your meal.
* **Select Quantity**: If you are ordering for more than one person, or if you have a specific quantity in mind, please enter the number of food items you would like to order. Please note that this field is only available for select_one options.
* **Delivery Details**: If you have any special delivery instructions or requirements, please provide them here.
* **Payment Details**: Specify your payment method or any special payment instructions for your order.
