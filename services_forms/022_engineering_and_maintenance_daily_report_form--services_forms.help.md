# Engineering And Maintenance Daily Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Engineering And Maintenance Daily Report Form is designed to gather information on the tasks and activities completed by engineers and technicians on a daily basis, including any issues encountered and solutions applied.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the tasks you've completed from the list of options provided.
2. Enter the task ID if required or applicable.
3. Describe the task, issue, or solution in the respective fields.
4. Choose the status of the task from the available options.
5. Indicate whether any maintenance tasks were performed.
6. Enter any issues or problems encountered.
7. Describe the solutions applied to address the issues.
8. List any solutions used.
9. Add any additional comments or notes.
10. Enter the date and time of task completion.
11. Select the person who completed the task.
12. Enter any comments or feedback.
13. Indicate whether attachments are provided.
14. Enter the task number (if required).
15. Report the date and time of reporting.
16. Select the person who submitted the report.
17. Enter the report ID (if required).
18. Choose the reporting frequency and period from the available options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Daily Tasks**: Select all tasks you've completed from the list of options.
* **Task ID**: Enter the ID of the task if required or applicable.
* **Task Description**: Describe the task, issue, or solution in this field.
* **Task Status**: Choose the status of the task from the available options.
* **Maintenance Tasks**: Indicate whether any maintenance tasks were performed.
* **Issues**: Enter any issues or problems encountered.
* **Issue Description**: Describe the issue.
* **Solutions**: Enter any solutions applied.
* **Solution Description**: Describe the solution.
* **Solutions Used**: List the solutions used.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Date Completed**: Enter the date of task completion.
* **Time Completed**: Enter the time of task completion.
* **Completed By**: Select the person who completed the task.
* **Completed Date**: Enter the date of task completion.
* **Completed Time**: Enter the time of task completion.
* **Completed By User**: Select the person who completed the task.
* **Comments**: Add any comments or feedback.
* **Attachments**: Indicate whether attachments are provided.
* **Task Number**: Enter the task number (if required).
* **Report Date**: Enter the date of reporting.
* **Report Time**: Enter the time of reporting.
* **Submitted By**: Select the person who submitted the report.
* **Report ID**: Enter the report ID (if required).
* **Reporting Frequency**: Choose the frequency of reporting from the available options.
* **Reporting Period**: Choose the reporting period from the available options.
