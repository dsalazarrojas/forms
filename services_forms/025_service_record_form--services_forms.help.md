# Service Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Record Form is used to collect and display service records for vehicles. This form is intended for use in a service department, such as a mechanic's shop, to record details about a vehicle's service history.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill in the Vehicle Number field with the vehicle's VIN (Vehicle Identification Number).
3. Enter the Make and Model of the vehicle.
4. Enter the Service Date, the date the service was performed.
5. Enter the current Odometer Reading of the vehicle.
6. Enter the current Mileage of the vehicle.
7. Enter the Service Cost, if applicable.
8. Add any relevant Notes, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Number** (Vehicle Number, text, required): Enter the vehicle's Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) to identify the vehicle accurately.
* **Make and Model** (Make and Model, text, optional): Enter the make and model of the vehicle being serviced.
* **Service Date** (Service Date, date, optional): Enter the date the service was performed.
* **Odometer Reading** (Odometer Reading, text, optional): Enter the current odometer reading of the vehicle at the time of service.
* **Mileage** (Mileage, number, optional): Enter the current mileage of the vehicle at the time of service.
* **Service Cost** (Service Cost, text, optional): Enter the cost of the service, if applicable.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, optional): Enter any relevant notes about the service performed.

## Tips
* Ensure that each field is filled in accurately and consistently.
* Review the form for completeness before finalizing.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the original purpose of the form and the provided instructions.
