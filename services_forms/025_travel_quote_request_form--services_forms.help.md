# Travel Quote Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help users request a quote for their travel plans. Please complete this form with as much detail as possible to ensure that we can provide you with an accurate quote.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Choose your destination from the list of available options.
2. Select your start and return dates, if applicable.
3. Enter the number of travelers and their names and email addresses.
4. Provide any additional information that you think would be helpful for us to know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Destination** (`destination`, `select_one`, required): Select the region of the world you are looking to visit. This could be Europe, Asia, or Americas.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, optional): If you have a specific start date for your trip, please enter it here. This will help us to provide you with a more accurate quote.
* **Return Date** (`return_date`, `date`, optional): Similarly, if you have a specific return date for your trip, please enter it here.
* **Number of Travelers** (`travelers`, `number`, optional): Please let us know how many people are traveling with you. This will help us to provide you with a more accurate quote.
* **Traveler Names** (`traveler_names`, `text`, optional): Please enter the names of all the people traveling with you.
* **Traveler Emails** (`traveler_emails`, `text`, optional): Please enter the email addresses of all the people traveling with you.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, `text`, optional): Do you have any special requests or requirements for your quote? Please let us know if you have any other details that you think would be helpful for us to know.

## Tips
* Please make sure to complete all required fields (marked as `required` in the form).
* If you have any questions or need help with your quote, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will review your submission and get back to you with a quote as soon as possible.
