# Cruise Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cruise Registration Form is used to collect information from customers for cruise travel bookings.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form completely, providing accurate information for the customer's cruise registration.
- Start by entering the customer's first name in the "First Name" field.
- Enter the customer's last name in the "Last Name" field.
- Add the customer's email address in the "EMail" field, following the standard email format (e.g., [name]@example.com).
- Enter the customer's contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
- Choose whether the customer is traveling on a cruise line by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Cruise Line" field.
- Enter the name of the ship the customer will be traveling on in the "Ship Name" field.
- Enter the departure and arrival dates of the customer's trip in the "Departure Date" and "Arrival Date" fields, respectively.
- If the customer is booking through an agency, enter the name of the agency in the "Travel Agency" field.
- If the customer has a travel agent, enter their name in the "Travel Agent" field.
- If the customer has any special requests or notes, add them in the "Special Requests" field.
- If the customer has a cabin number, enter it in the "Cabin Number" field.
- Indicate whether the customer will be traveling with roommates by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Roommates" field.
- Enter any travel documentation or relevant information in the "Travel Document" field.
- Finally, add any travel notes or information in the "Travel Notes" field.
