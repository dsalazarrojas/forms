# Snow Removal Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Snow Removal Request Form is designed to collect information from customers about their snow removal needs for a specific area.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer information in the Customer Info field.
2. Provide your email address in the Email field.
3. Enter your address in the Address field.
4. Select the type of service you require in the Service Type field.
5. Specify your priority level for the service in the Priority field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, required: false): Enter your name and contact information here.
* **Email** (`customer_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Address** (`customer_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address where you would like the snow removal service to take place.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose one or more of the following:
	+ Yes (select this to indicate you need snow removal service)
* **Priority** (`priority`, select_one, required: false): Choose one of the following:
	+ Yes (select this to indicate that you consider this request as high priority)
*Tips*
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information in each field.
* If you have any special requests, please include them in the customer info field or comment in the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the facilities management staff for help.
