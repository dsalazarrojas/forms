# Looking For A Lawyer - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users find a lawyer that suits their needs. It gathers information from the user about what they need from a lawyer and other relevant details to ensure the best possible matching with a suitable lawyer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the question "what_do_you_need" to describe what you are looking for in a lawyer (e.g., divorce, estate planning, etc.).
2. Provide your criminal history in the "what_is_your_criminal_history" field.
3. Enter your contact information in the "contact_information" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "email_address" field.
5. Select your lawyer firm of interest from the "lawyer_firm" field.
6. Choose how you heard about us from the "how_did_you_hear" field.
7. Select any additional lawyer firms that interest you from the "lawyer_firm" field.
8. Add any additional notes in the "notes" field.
9. Enter the date of your appointment in the "date" field.
10. Enter the time of your appointment in the "time" field.
11. Describe your question or concern in the "what_is_your_question" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **what_do_you_need** (`what_do_you_need`, text, required: false): Describe what you are looking for in a lawyer. This could be a specific type of legal service (e.g., divorce, estate planning, etc.) or a general description of your needs.
- **what_is_your_criminal_history** (`what_is_your_criminal_history`, text, required: false): If you have a criminal history, please describe it briefly.
- **contact_information** (`contact_information`, text, required: false): Provide your contact information to be reached by potential lawyers.
- **email_address** (`email_address`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so that lawyers can contact you.
- **lawyer_firm_info** (`lawyer_firm_info`, text, required: false): Enter any details about the lawyer firm you are interested in (e.g., firm name, location, etc.).
- **how_did_you_hear** (`how_did_you_hear`, select_one, required: false): Choose how you heard about us (e.g., Google search, social media, etc.).
- **lawyer_firm** (`lawyer_firm`, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or more lawyer firms that interest you.
- **notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments about your needs or concerns.
- **date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of your appointment or consultation.
- **time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of your appointment or consultation.
- **what_is_your_question** (`what_is_your_question`, text, required: false): Describe your question or concern that you would like to have addressed by a lawyer.
