# Service Repair Warranty Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request service and warranty for a service provided to a customer.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
2. Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required): Enter a brief title for your service request.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of service you require (Service, Repair, or Warranty).
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Describe the service or repair required in detail.
* **Service Date** (`service_date`, date, required): Choose a start date and time for the service.
* **Service Time** (`service_time`, time, required): Choose a time for the service.
* **Service Notes** (`service_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your service or repair.
* **Customer Message** (`customer_message`, text, required): Enter any additional information or comments.
* **Assigned Technician** (`assigned_technician`, select_one, optional): Select an available technician from the dropdown list.
* **Service Status** (`service_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of your service (Scheduled, In Progress, or Completed).
* **Service Priority** (`service_priority`, select_one, optional): Select a priority level (Low, Medium, or High) for your service.
* **Service Description** (`service_type_description`, text, optional): Enter a detailed description of the service or repair.
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, note, optional): Sign your name to confirm your request.
* **Service Signature** (`service_signature`, note, optional): Sign a technician's name to confirm the service.
* **Service Warranty** (`service_warranty`, select_one, optional): Select the warranty period (1, 2, or 3 Years).
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, required): Enter your full address.
* **Customer City** (`customer_city`, text, required): Enter the city where you reside.
* **Service Location** (`service_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the service.
* **Service Zip** (`service_zip`, text, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Service Comments** (`service_comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes.
* **Assigned Date** (`assigned_date`, date, required): Choose a date and time when a technician is assigned to you.
* **Additional Notes** (`service_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Customer Comments** (`customer_comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes from the customer.
