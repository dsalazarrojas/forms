# web_theme_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a web theme order. It is intended for customers to provide their details and theme selection preferences. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review the form instructions and ensure you have all necessary information before proceeding.
2. Fill out the form fields as follows:
	* Fill in the customer info field with your name and contact information.
	* For the theme selection field, select one or multiple theme preferences using the provided options.
	* In the order details field, provide any additional information about your theme order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info**: This field is used to input your name and contact information. Please provide the most up-to-date and accurate information to ensure timely communication.
* **Theme Selection**: This field is used to select one or multiple theme preferences. Use the options provided to choose your desired theme(s) and ensure they are accurate to your preferences.
	+ If you select multiple themes, please separate the selections with commas.
* **Order Details**: This field is used to provide any additional information about your theme order. Please be as detailed as possible to help us better understand your needs.
