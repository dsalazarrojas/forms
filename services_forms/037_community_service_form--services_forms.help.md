# Community Service Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Service Form is designed to gather information from individuals seeking assistance. It is used to collect personal and household details to provide tailored support services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form accurately and honestly.
2. Answer all required questions, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Select all applicable options for each field, if applicable.
4. Provide specific and detailed answers whenever possible.
5. Submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information**: This section is about the person seeking services. Please provide your name and contact information.
  * **First Name**: Your first name as it appears on your ID or documents.
  * **Last Name**: Your last name as it appears on your ID or documents.
  * **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
  * **Email Address**: Your email address for communication.
  * **Phone Number**: Your primary contact number.
  * **Street Address**: Your current address.
  * **City**: The city where you live.
  * **State**: The state where you live.
  * **ZIP Code**: Your postal code.
* **Household Information**: This section is about your household.
  * **Total Household Members**: The total number of people in your household.
  * **About Your Household**: Provide a brief description of your household.
* **Service Needs**: Select all services you need help with.
* **Type of Service Needed**: Select all applicable services from the list.
* **Describe Your Needs**: Briefly explain your needs and circumstances.
* **Urgency Level**: Indicate how soon you need help.
* **Income Information**: Provide your household income information for eligibility purposes.
* **Employment Status**: Select your current employment status.
* **Monthly Household Income**: Your monthly household income (if employed).
* **Income Sources**: Select all sources of income.
* **Have You Received Assistance Before**: Indicate if you've received assistance before.
* **Referral Source**: How you learned about this organization.
* **Do You Have Health Insurance**: Indicate your health insurance status.
* **Do You Have Reliable Transportation**: Indicate if you have reliable transportation.
* **Preferred Language**: Select your preferred language.
* **Do You Need an Interpreter**: Indicate if you need an interpreter.
* **Additional Information**: Share any additional information that might help us better understand your needs.
* **Consent to Services**: Indicate your consent to receive services.
