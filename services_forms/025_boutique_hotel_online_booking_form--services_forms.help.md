# Boutique Hotel Online Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Boutique Hotel Online Booking Form is designed to collect essential information from guests to book their stay at our hotel. This form will help us understand your preferences and requirements to provide you with the best possible experience during your stay.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Email Address**, **Phone Number**, and **Address** to identify yourself and contact information.
2. Select your **Check-in Date** and **Check-out Date** to determine your stay duration.
3. Enter the **Number of Guests** and **Number of Adults** and **Number of Children** to ensure we accommodate your group size.
4. Choose your **Room Type Preference** from the available options.
5. Select your **Bed Preference** from the available options.
6. Choose your **View Preference** if you have a specific view in mind.
7. Select your **Smoking Preference** to ensure we accommodate your comfort needs.
8. If you have any **Special Requests** or requirements, please specify them in the **Special Requests** field.
9. If you need any **Accessibility Needs**, please select the required accommodations.
10. If you wish to **Early Check-in** or **Late Check-out**, choose the available options.
11. If you need **Airport Transportation**, select the required services.
12. Select any **Package Preferences** that suit your stay.
13. Choose your preferred **Payment Method** to complete the booking process.
14. If you are celebrating a **Special Occasion**, select the relevant option.
15. If you are a member of our **Loyalty Program**, select the correct option.
16. Finally, confirm your understanding of our **Terms and Conditions** and **Cancellation Policy**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: A field to input your first name.
* **Last Name**: A field to input your last name.
* **Email Address**: A field to input your email address for booking confirmations and communications.
* **Phone Number**: A field to input your primary contact number.
* **Address**: A field to input your complete home address.
* **City**: A field to input the city of your residence.
* **State or Province**: A field to input the state or province of your residence.
* **Country**: A select field to choose your country of residence.
* **Check-in Date**: A field to input the date of arrival.
* **Check-out Date**: A field to input the date of departure.
* **Number of Guests**: A field to input the total number of guests.
* **Number of Adults**: A field to input the number of adults.
* **Number of Children**: A field to input the number of children.
* **Children Ages**: A field to input the ages of children.
* **Room Type Preference**: A select field to choose your preferred room type.
* **Bed Preference**: A select field to choose your preferred bed type.
* **View Preference**: A select field to choose your preferred room view.
* **Smoking Preference**: A select field to choose your smoking preference.
* **Special Requests**: A field to input any special requests or requirements.
* **Accessibility Needs**: A select field to choose any accessibility needs.
* **Early Check-in Request**: A select field to choose if you wish to early check-in.
* **Late Check-out Request**: A select field to choose if you wish to late check-out.
* **Transportation Needed**: A select field to choose if you need airport transportation.
* **Package Preference**: A select field to choose any package deals.
* **Payment Method**: A select field to choose your preferred payment method.
* **Special Occasion**: A select field to choose if you are celebrating a special occasion.
* **How Did You Hear About Us**: A select field to input how you found out about the hotel.
* **Loyalty Program Member**: A select field to choose if you are a member of our loyalty program.
* **Terms and Conditions**: A select field to confirm your understanding of our terms and conditions.
* **Cancellation Policy**: A select field to confirm your understanding of our cancellation policy.

## Tips

* Double-check your submission before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please contact us to ensure a smooth and enjoyable stay.
