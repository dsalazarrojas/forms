# Lift Truck Work Order Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information related to lift truck work orders, which are orders for maintenance or repair of lift trucks used in warehouse or material handling operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have a valid work order ID for the lift truck being maintained or repaired.
2. Select the lift truck's make from the list of available options.
3. Select the lift truck's model from the list of available options.
4. Enter the year of the lift truck.
5. Enter the lift truck's serial number.
6. Provide a detailed description of the work order.
7. Choose the status of the work order (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
8. Enter the customer's name.
9. Enter the customer's email address.
10. Enter the customer's phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Work Order ID (1)**: Enter a valid work order ID for the lift truck being maintained or repaired.
* **Lift Truck Make (2)**: Select the make of the lift truck from the list (e.g., Toyota, Caterpillar, Hyster, Komatsu).
* **Lift Truck Model (3)**: Select the model of the lift truck from the list (e.g., FT 8000, FT 9000, FT 10000, FT 12000, FT 14000).
* **Lift Truck Year (4)**: Choose the year of the lift truck from the list (e.g., 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022).
* **Lift Truck Serial Number (5)**: Enter the serial number of the lift truck.
* **Work Order Description (6)**: Provide a detailed description of the work order.
* **Work Order Status (7)**: Choose the status of the work order from the list (e.g., Active, Inactive, Pending).
* **Customer Name (8)**: Enter the name of the customer who owns the lift truck.
* **Email (9)**: Enter the email address of the customer.
* **Phone (10)**: Enter the phone number of the customer.

Note: All fields marked as "required" are mandatory and must be filled in order to submit the form.
