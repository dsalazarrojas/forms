# Wheelchair Repair Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from customers about their wheelchair repair needs. This information will be used to schedule a repair and ensure the correct parts and resources are available for the job.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter a brief description of your wheelchair issue in the **User Input** field.
2.  Select the type of chair you have (e.g., manual, power) from the **Chair Type** dropdown menu.
3.  If you have a manual or power chair with a seat, select the chair's manufacturer from the **Chair Manufacturer** dropdown menu.
4.  Enter the date you received your chair in the **Date Received** field. This will help us assess the chair's warranty status.
5.  If you'd like to include any additional comments or details about the issue, you can enter them in the **Notes** field.
6.  Enter your contact information (e.g., email, phone number) in the **Contact Info** field.
7.  If you're willing, provide your phone number in the **Phone Number** field. This will help us contact you with updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): A brief description of the issue you're experiencing with your wheelchair.
* **Chair Type** (`chair_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of chair you have (e.g., manual, power).
* **Chair Manufacturer** (`chair_manufacturer`, select_multiple, optional): If you have a manual or power chair with a seat, select the chair's manufacturer from the list.
* **Date Received** (`date_received`, date, optional): Enter the date you received your chair.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): If you'd like to include any additional comments or details about the issue, enter them here.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, email, optional): Enter your contact information (e.g., email, phone number).
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): If you'd like us to contact you by phone, enter your phone number here.
* **Wheelchair Repair Shop** (`wheelchair_repair_shop`, select_multiple, optional): If you have a preferred repair shop, select it from the list. 

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when filling out the **User Input** field.
* If you're unsure of the chair type or manufacturer, you can leave them blank.
* If you have any additional contact information (e.g., email), you can enter that in the **Contact Info** field.
* We will contact you with updates and follow up to schedule a repair if necessary.
