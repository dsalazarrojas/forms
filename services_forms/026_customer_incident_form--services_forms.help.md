# Customer Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Incident Form is used by customers to report incidents related to our services. This form allows you to provide detailed information about the issue you are experiencing, including its description, severity, status, and category.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter your customer email address in the "Customer Email" field.
3. Provide a detailed description of the incident in the "Incident Description" field.
4. Choose the category and status of the incident from the "Incident Category", "Incident Status", and "Incident Priority" fields.
5. Select the urgency and severity of the incident from the "Incident Urgency" and "Incident Severity" fields.
6. Choose who the incident has been assigned to from the "Assigned To" and "Assigned User" fields.
7. Select whether the incident has been submitted by you or someone else from the "Submitted By" field.
8. If applicable, upload an attachment related to the incident in the "Incident Attachment" field.
9. Finally, click "Submit" to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as the customer.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address associated with your account.
* **Incident Description**: Provide a detailed description of the incident, including its cause, symptoms, and resolution.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by our support team.
* **Customer Address**: Enter your address if you would like to provide additional information about your location.
* **Incident Category**: Choose the category of the incident from the options provided.
* **Incident Status**: Choose the status of the incident from the options provided.
* **Incident Priority**: Choose the priority of the incident from the options provided.
* **Incident Urgency**: Select the urgency of the incident from the options provided.
* **Incident Severity**: Select the severity of the incident from the options provided.
* **Incident Reopened**: Select whether the incident has been reopened.
* **Incident Closed**: Select whether the incident has been closed.
* **Incident Closed Date**: Enter the date the incident was closed.
* **Incident Comment**: Provide any additional comments about the incident.
* **Incident Attachment**: Upload an attachment related to the incident if applicable.
* **Assigned To**: Choose who the incident has been assigned to from the options provided.
* **Assigned User**: Choose who the incident has been assigned to from the options provided.
* **Submitted By**: Select whether you submitted the incident or someone else did.

## Tips
* Please provide as much detail as possible when filling out this form to ensure that our support team can effectively resolve the incident.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask our support team for clarification.
* If you have already reported a similar incident, please let us know so we can prevent duplication of efforts.
