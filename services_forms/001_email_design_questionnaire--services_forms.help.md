# Email Design Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Email Design Questionnaire is a form used to gather information from customers to design an email template that meets their specific needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly, as this information will be used to design your email template.
2.  Make sure to fill out all required fields. If you have any questions or concerns about a particular field, contact our support team.
3.  Review your answers carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and consistency.
4.  If you need assistance or have questions, don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*  **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, required):
    This field is used to capture the customer's name and other relevant details.
*  **Contact Preferences** (`contact_preferences`, select_multiple, required):
    Choose the contact preferences that the customer would like to have in their email template.
*  **Email Body** (`email_body`, text, required):
    Enter the text that you would like to have in the email body.
*  **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional):
    If you would like to have a contact person included in the email, enter their name and contact information here.
*  **Email Preferences** (`email_preferences`, select_one, optional):
    Choose the email preferences that you would like to have in your email template.
*  **Email Visuals** (`email_visuals`, select_multiple, optional):
    Choose the email visuals that you would like to have in your email template.
*  **Email Content** (`email_content`, text, optional):
    Enter any additional email content that you would like to have included in your email template.
*  **Business Preferences** (`business_preferences`, select_multiple, optional):
    Choose the business preferences that you would like to have in your email template.
*  **Email Footer** (`email_footer`, select_multiple, optional):
    Choose the email footer options that you would like to have in your email template.
*  **Follow-ups** (`follow_ups`, select_multiple, optional):
    Choose the follow-up options that you would like to have in your email template.
*  **Send Email** (`send_email`, select_one, required):
    Indicate whether you would like to send the email template immediately or not.
    - Select 'Yes' to send the email template immediately.
    - Select 'No' to schedule the email template for later.
    - This field is required to ensure that you are aware of the email template design and content.
 
# Tips
*  Please note that all required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*) and must be filled out in order to submit the form.
*  If you have any questions or concerns about your email template preferences, please don't hesitate to contact our support team.
*  We will use the information gathered from this form to design your email template according to your preferences.
