# Handyman Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Handyman Release Form is used to provide information about services performed by handymen for internal reporting and tracking purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
3. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
4. Select the date and time of service in the "Date of Service" and "Time of Service" fields, respectively.
5. Enter the type of service performed in the "Type of Service" field.
6. Enter a brief description of the service in the "Description of Service" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`handyman_release_form_2`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification or business card.
* **Contact Number** (`handyman_release_form_3`, text, required): Enter your contact number where you can be reached for further questions or follow-ups.
* **Address** (`handyman_release_form_4`, text, required): Enter the address where services were performed.
* **Date of Service** (`handyman_release_form_5`, date, required): Select the date on which the services were performed.
* **Time of Service** (`handyman_release_form_6`, time, required): Select the time at which the services were performed.
* **Type of Service** (`handyman_release_form_7`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the type of service performed (e.g., repair, maintenance, etc.).
* **Description of Service** (`handyman_release_form_8`, text, required): Enter a detailed description of the service, including any specific work performed or materials used.
