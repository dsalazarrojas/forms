# Hotel Booking Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for booking a hotel room, including guest information, travel details, and accommodation preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address.
2. Provide your Phone number.
3. Select your Check In and Check Out dates.
4. Choose the Room Type (e.g., single, double).
5. Enter the Number of Guests.
6. Select if you will be traveling (yes/no).
7. Choose your Arrival Time.
8. Choose if you will have Room Service (yes/no).
9. Enter the Hotel Address.
10. Provide the Room Number (if known).
11. Add any comments or special requests.
12. Select the Assigned Tool (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address (optional).
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
* **Check In** (date): Select the date you will check in to the hotel.
* **Check Out** (date): Select the date you will check out from the hotel.
* **Room Type** (select multiple): Choose the type of room you require (e.g., single, double).
* **Number of Guests** (number): Enter the number of guests in your group.
* **Travel Date** (select one): Select 'Yes' if you are traveling, 'No' otherwise.
* **Arrival Time** (time): Choose your arrival time at the hotel.
* **Room Service** (select one): Choose 'Yes' if you want room service, 'No' otherwise.
* **Hotel Address** (text): Enter the hotel address where you will be staying.
* **Room Number** (text): Enter the room number if known (optional).
* **Comments** (note): Add any comments or special requests.
* **Assigned Tool** (text): Enter the assigned tool (if applicable).

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your details before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the hotel management for clarification.
* This form is for booking purposes only; do not use it for any other reason.
