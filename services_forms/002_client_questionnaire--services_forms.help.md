# Client Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose

This questionnaire is designed to gather essential information about your business, helping us understand your needs and preferences. Please answer the questions to the best of your ability, and do not hesitate to ask if you need clarification on any of the fields.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read each question carefully and provide accurate information about your business.
2. Fill out each field with the required information.
3. If you need help with any question, do not hesitate to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name.
* **What type of business does your organization operate?** (`business_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the category that best describes your business (e.g., Retail, Technology, etc.).
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter your primary contact email.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Provide your best contact number.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter the legal name of your organization.
* **Number of Employees** (`employee_count`, `select_one`, required): Approximate the number of employees in your organization.
* **Approximate Annual Revenue** (`annual_revenue`, `select_one`, required): Choose the best approximation of your annual revenue.
* **What are your primary business goals?** (`primary_goals`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the goals that apply to your business (e.g., Increase Revenue, Improve Efficiency, etc.).
* **What are your biggest challenges currently?** (`main_challenges`, `text`, required): Describe your current challenges in detail.
* **How would you measure success with our services?** (`success_metrics`, `text`, required): Specify the key metrics you would use to measure success.
* **What is your preferred implementation timeline?** (`implementation_timeline`, `select_one`, required): Choose the timeline that best fits your project needs.
* **What is your budget range for this project?** (`budget_range`, `select_one`, required): Select the budget range that aligns with your project requirements.
* **Do you have any special requirements or constraints we should know about?** (`special_requirements`, `text`, required): Share any special requirements or constraints.
* **Who are the key decision makers in your organization?** (`decision_makers`, `text`, required): Provide the names and titles of the key decision makers.
* **Have you worked with service providers similar to us before?** (`previous_experience`, `select_one`, required): Answer whether you have experience with similar service providers.
* **How did you hear about us?** (`heard_about_us`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you heard about us (e.g., Referral, Search Engine, etc.).

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when answering the questions.
* If you need help with any question, do not hesitate to ask.
* We appreciate your honest and accurate responses, which will help us better understand your needs and preferences.
