# POS Setup Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The POS Setup Request Form is used for requesting setup of a point of sale system for a store. This form helps us understand your store's information and requirements for the setup process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and efficiently, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the contact information section with your store's current contact details.
2. Provide system requirements and payment information related to your store's operations.
3. Add any additional details that might aid us in setting up your POS system.
4. Ensure that the store's contact information is up-to-date and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Information** (`form_1_page_contact_info`, text, required: false):
This field is for your store's contact information such as name, address, and email.
* **Store Information** (`form_2_page`, number, required: false):
This field is for entering the store's information in a numerical format.
* **Store Name** (`form_2_page_store_name`, text, required: false):
Enter your store's name for easy reference.
* **Address** (`form_2_page_address`, text, required: false):
Provide your store's address for delivery and setup purposes.
* **Location** (`form_2_page_location`, text, required: false):
Describe the location of your store for accurate setup and configuration.
* **City** (`form_2_page_city`, text, required: false):
Enter your store's city for easy reference.
* **State/Province** (`form_2_page_state`, text, required: false):
Select your store's state or province for accurate tax calculations and regulations.
* **Country** (`form_2_page_country`, text, required: false):
Enter your store's country for international tax calculations and regulations.
* **Postal/Zip Code** (`form_2_page_zip`, text, required: false):
Enter your store's postal or zip code for accurate delivery and setup purposes.
* **Email** (`form_2_page_email`, email, required: false):
Enter your store's email address for easy communication and updates.
* **Phone** (`form_2_page_phone`, text, required: false):
Enter your store's phone number for easy contact and support.
* **Mobile** (`form_2_page_mobile`, text, required: false):
Enter your store's mobile number for easy contact and support.
* **System Requirements** (`form_1_page_system_requirements`, text, required: false):
Describe your store's system requirements for accurate setup and configuration.
* **Payment Information** (`form_1_page_payment_info`, text, required: false):
Provide payment information related to your store's operations.
* **Additional Information** (`form_1_page_additional_info`, text, required: false):
Add any additional details that might aid us in setting up your POS system.

Note: The form is split into two pages for better organization and clarity.
