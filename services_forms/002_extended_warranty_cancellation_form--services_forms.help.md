# extendedWarrantyCancellationForm - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from customers to initiate the cancellation of their extended warranty.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the warranty information in the `warrantyInformation` field, which is used to identify the warranty being cancelled.
2. Provide the reason for cancellation in the `reasonForCancellation` field, explaining why the customer wants to cancel their extended warranty.
3. Enter the date of cancellation in the `dateOfCancellation` field, which is the date when the customer wants to cancel their warranty.
4. Confirm that the cancellation request has been received by selecting either 'Yes' or 'No' in the `cancellationConfirmation` field.
5. Enter any additional contact information in the `contactInformation` field, which can be used to contact the customer with next steps.
6. Add any additional comments or information in the `additionalComments` field, which can be used to provide further context to the cancellation request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Warranty Information**: Enter the warranty information (e.g. warranty ID, policy number, etc.) to identify the warranty being cancelled.
* **Reason For Cancellation**: Explain the reason why the customer wants to cancel their extended warranty.
* **Date Of Cancellation**: Enter the date when the customer wants to cancel their warranty.
* **Cancellation Confirmation**: Confirm that the cancellation request has been received by selecting either 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Contact Information**: Enter any additional contact information that can be used to contact the customer with next steps.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or information that can be used to provide further context to the cancellation request.
