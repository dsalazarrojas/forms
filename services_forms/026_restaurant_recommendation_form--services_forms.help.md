# restaurant_recommendation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The restaurant recommendation form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from customers about their dining experiences at various restaurants. This form helps restaurants improve their services and offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the restaurant you visited in the field labeled "restaurant_name"
2. Provide your contact information in the fields labeled "customer_phone" and "customer_email" (optional)
3. Select the date you visited the restaurant in the field labeled "review_date" (date format is required)
4. Write a review of your experience in the field labeled "review_text" (optional)
5. Rate your experience by selecting a score in the field labeled "review_rating" (rating is required)
6. Click "submit" to send your feedback to the restaurant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **restaurant_name** (text): Enter the name of the restaurant you visited.
* **customer_phone** (text): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **customer_email** (email): Enter your email address (optional).
* **review_date** (date): Select the date you visited the restaurant. Please enter the date in [date format](https://www.w3schools.com/datetime/tryit.asp?cmd=calendar). 
* **review_text** (text): Write a brief review of your experience at the restaurant (optional).
* **review_rating** (text): Rate your experience by selecting a score out of [stars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rating_scale) (e.g., 1-5).
* **submit** (text): Click this button to send your feedback to the restaurant.
