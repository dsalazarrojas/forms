# Credit Consultation Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect relevant contact information and consultation preferences from users who are interested in credit-related services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact details in the "Contact Details" section.
2. Enter your FICO score in the "FICO Score" field. 
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Indicate your contact request in the "Contact Request" field.
6. Enter your name in the "Submitter Name" field.
7. Optionally, provide additional information about yourself in the "Submitter Comment" field.
8. Choose the representative you would like to contact in the "Submitter" field.
9. Select the type of credit score you are interested in for the "Credit Score" field.
10. Agree to the terms and conditions by selecting the correct option in the "Submitter Agreement" field.
11. Finally, indicate whether you would like to submit the form by selecting the correct option in the "Form Submit" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details_form`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **FICO Score** (`fico_score`, number, required/optional): Enter your FICO score. This field is required for credit-related services.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Request** (`contact_request`, text, required/optional): Indicate the reason you are contacting us.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name.
* **Submitter Comment** (`submitter_comment`, text, required/optional): Optionally, provide any additional information about yourself.
* **Submitter** (`contact_form_submitter`, select_one, required/true): Choose the representative you would like to contact.
* **Credit Score** (`credit_score`, select_multiple, required/true): Select the type of credit score you are interested in.
* **Submitter Agreement** (`submitter_agreement`, select_one, required/true): Agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Form Submit** (`form_submit`, select_multiple, required/false): Indicate whether you would like to submit the form.
