# Equipment Liability Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Equipment Liability Form is designed to record and document the details of equipment owned by individuals. This form helps to ensure that accurate and up-to-date information is collected about the equipment, including purchase details, warranty, and maintenance records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the equipment's owner in the "Owner Name" field.
2. Enter the address of the equipment in the "Address" field.
3. Enter the date the equipment was purchased in the "Date of Purchase" field.
4. Provide a detailed description of the equipment in the "Equipment Description" field.
5. Enter the model of the equipment in the "Equipment Model" field.
6. Enter the make of the equipment in the "Equipment Make" field.
7. Enter the year model of the equipment in the "Equipment Year Model" field.
8. Enter the purchase price of the equipment in the "Purchase Price" field.
9. Enter the date the equipment was purchased in the "Purchase Date" field.
10. Select the warranty currently in effect for the equipment from the list provided in the "Current Warranty" field.
11. Enter any maintenance records for the equipment in the "Maintenance Records" field.
12. Upload any relevant photos of the equipment in the "Photos" field.
13. Enter any certification or certification number for the equipment in the "Certification" field.
14. Enter the number of the certification in the "Certification Number" field.
15. Enter the name of the insurance provider for the equipment in the "Insurance Provider" field.
16. Enter the date the equipment was insured in the "Insurance Date" field.
17. Enter the insurance number for the equipment in the "Insurance Number" field.
18. Enter the name of the warranty provider for the equipment in the "Warranty Provider" field.
19. Enter the serial number of the equipment in the "Serial Number" field.
20. Enter the model number of the equipment in the "Model Number" field.
21. Enter the year the equipment was purchased in the "Year of Purchase" field.
22. Enter the currency of the purchase price of the equipment in the "Purchase Price Currency" field.
