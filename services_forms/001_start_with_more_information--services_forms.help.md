# Start With More Information - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the services you are interested in and the specific criteria that should be met.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Select your response to each question carefully, as it will help us understand your needs and preferences.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification or guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What do you want to do** (1): This field allows you to indicate what you want to do. Please enter a brief description of your needs or preferences.
* **Get the facts** (2): This field is where you provide additional details about the facts that are relevant to the service. Be as specific as possible to help us evaluate your request accurately.
* **Specific criteria** (3): Here, you can select the specific criteria that should be met for the service. You can choose one or multiple options from the list provided.
  - "Yes" indicates that the criteria should be met.
  - "No" indicates that the criteria should not be met.
* **Offer services** (4): In this field, you can select whether services should be offered or not.
  - "Yes" indicates that services should be offered.
  - "No" indicates that services should not be offered.

## Tips
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask for clarification or guidance.
- The more information you provide, the better we can understand your needs and preferences.
