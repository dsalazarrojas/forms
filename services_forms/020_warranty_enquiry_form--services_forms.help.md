# Warranty Enquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers or users who are inquiring about a warranty related issue with a product. It is used to gather details about the product in question, the customer's contact information, and the nature of the issue being experienced. This information will help our support team to better understand and resolve the customer's concern.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as the customer or user submitting the form.
2. Provide details about the product that is experiencing the issue (e.g., model number, purchase date).
3. Enter your contact number for us to get in touch with you.
4. Enter your email address so we can respond to your inquiry.
5. Enter your address for shipping and communication purposes.
6. Enter the product serial number (if applicable).
7. Choose the date of purchase for the product.
8. Choose the date the product was returned (if applicable).
9. Select the condition of the product (New, Used, or Refurbished).
10. Enter a description of the issue you are experiencing.
11. Enter the name of the tool that was assigned to resolve your issue.
12. Enter the category of the product (e.g., electronics, clothing, etc.).
13. Select from the following tags for your issue (Warranty, Claim, Service).
14. If applicable, provide additional information about your inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name (1)**: Enter your name as the customer or user submitting the form.
* **Product Details (2)**: Provide details about the product that is experiencing the issue (e.g., model number, purchase date).
* **Contact Number (3)**: Enter your contact number for us to get in touch with you.
* **Email (4)**: Enter your email address so we can respond to your inquiry.
* **Address (5)**: Enter your address for shipping and communication purposes.
* **Product Serial Number (6)**: Enter the product serial number (if applicable).
* **Product Purchase Date (7)**: Enter the date of purchase for the product.
* **Product Return Date (8)**: Enter the date the product was returned (if applicable).
* **Product Condition (9)**: Select the condition of the product (New, Used, or Refurbished).
* **Description (10)**: Enter a description of the issue you are experiencing.
* **Assigned Tool (11)**: Enter the name of the tool that was assigned to resolve your issue.
* **Category (12)**: Enter the category of the product (e.g., electronics, clothing, etc.).
* **Tags (13)**: Select from the following tags for your issue (Warranty, Claim, Service).
* **Submitted By (14)**: Enter your name or initials for identification purposes.
