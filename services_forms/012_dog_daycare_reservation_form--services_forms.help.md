# Dog Daycare Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for dog daycare reservations. It will help us understand your reservation details, payment method, and other relevant information to provide the best service for your furry friend.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your reservation details, including date and start and end times.
2. Add any additional notes or comments about your reservation.
3. Choose how you would like to make your payment (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Cash).
4. Review and submit your form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Reservation Details** (`Reservation Details`, `text`, **required**): 
    This field is where you'll provide your reservation date.
- **Start Time** (`Start Time`, `time`, **required**): 
    Enter the time you'd like to arrive for your dog's stay.
- **End Time** (`End Time`, `time`, **required**): 
    Enter the time you'd like to pick up your dog after their stay.
- **Reservation Notes** (`Reservation Notes`, `note`, **optional**): 
    If you have any special requests or information about your dog's stay, please add it here.
- **Services** (`Services`, `select_one`, **optional**): 
    If we offer additional services for your dog's stay, such as grooming or playtime, let us know if you'd like to take advantage of them.
- **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, `select_one`, **required**): 
    Please select how you'd like to make your payment: Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Cash.
- **Payment Terms** (`Payment Terms`, `text`, **optional**): 
    If you'd like to specify any terms or conditions for your payment, please add it here.
