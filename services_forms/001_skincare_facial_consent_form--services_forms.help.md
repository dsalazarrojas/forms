# Skincare Facial Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the upcoming visit, client expectations, medical conditions, skincare services, medical history, allergies, medical notes, and client signature. The form is intended to be used by skincare professionals to collect relevant data for providing optimal care and services to clients.

## How To Complete This Form
- To start filling out the form, click on the "Submit" button to open the first page.
- Fill in the required and optional fields as instructed in the "Field-by-Field Explanation" section.
- Make sure to review each page carefully and click the "Submit" button after completing all the pages.
- If you need to navigate through the pages, use the next and previous buttons at the top of the page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Upcoming Visit (<name>upcoming_visit</name>, <type>date</type>, optional)
* This field is where you can indicate the date of your upcoming visit. Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Expectations (<name>expectations</name>, <type>text</type>, optional)
* In this field, please describe your expectations from your skincare professional, including any specific concerns or goals you have for your visit.

### Medical Conditions (<name>medical_conditions</name>, <type>text</type>, optional)
* If you have any medical conditions that may affect your treatment, please list them here.

### Skincare Services (<name>skincare_services</name>, <type>select_multiple</type>, optional)
* Please select the type(s) of skincare service(s) you are interested in receiving during your visit, such as Face, Back, Body, Hands, or Feet.

### Medical History (<name>medical_history</name>, <type>text</type>, optional)
* Please provide a brief summary of your medical history, including any allergies, medical conditions, or previous treatments.

### Allergies (<name>allergies</name>, <type>text</type>, optional)
* If you have any allergies, please list them here.

### Medical Notes (<name>medical_notes</name>, <type>note</type>, optional)
* This is a text area where you can provide any additional information about your medical history or previous treatments.

### Client Signature (<name>client_signature</name>, <type>text</type>, optional)
* Please print and sign your name to confirm that you have reviewed and understood the contents of this form.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form carefully and accurately to provide your skincare professional with the best information to provide optimal care.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your skincare professional for guidance.
* Remember to review each page carefully before submitting the form.
* If you need to make changes to your submission, please contact your skincare professional directly.
