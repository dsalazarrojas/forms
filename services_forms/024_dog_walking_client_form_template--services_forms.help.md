# dog_walking_client_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The dog_walking_client_form_template is a form for clients to book a dog walking service. It helps us collect necessary information to provide a suitable walking service for your pet.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read through each field carefully and fill in the required information.
- Select the desired service type from the "Dog Walking Service" field.
- Enter your name in the "Client Name" field.
- Fill in your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
- Provide your dog's name in the "Dog Name" field.
- Choose a date and time for the appointment from the "Appointment Date" and "Appointment Time" fields.
- Select any additional services you'd like from the "Services" field.
- If you have any special requests or notes, you can add them in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Dog Walking Service** (`Dog Walking Service`, select_one, required): Choose the type of dog walking service you are interested in.
  * Morning Walk: A morning walk typically occurs between the hours of 8am-12pm.
  * Afternoon Walk: An afternoon walk typically occurs between the hours of 12pm-4pm.
  * Night Walk: A night walk typically occurs between the hours of 6pm-10pm.

- **Client Name** (`Client Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person submitting this form.

- **Contact Number** (`Contact Number`, text, required): Enter your contact number so we can reach out to you.

- **Dog Name** (`Dog Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the dog that needs walking.

- **Appointment Date** (`Appointment Date`, date, required): Select the date you'd like to schedule the appointment.

- **Appointment Time** (`Appointment Time`, time, required): Select the time you'd like to schedule the appointment.

- **Address** (`Address`, text, required): Enter the address where the dog is to be walked.

- **Services** (`Services`, select_multiple, required): Select any additional services you'd like to include in the walk.
  * Bathing: A bath for the dog.
  * Nail trimming: A nail trimming for the dog.
  * Fur brushing: A brushing of the dog's fur.

- **Notes** (`Notes`, note, optional): Add any additional information or notes regarding the walk or your dog's needs.
