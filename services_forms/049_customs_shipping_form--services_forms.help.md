# Customs Shipping Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about the sender and recipient of a shipment, including their contact details, the description of the shipment, its value and weight, and customs form selection. It is designed for international shipments and is meant to be completed by the sender.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in all the required fields.
2.  Select the sender and recipient countries from the respective drop-down menus.
3.  Enter the shipment description, value, and weight in the corresponding fields.
4.  Choose the customs form type from the drop-down menu.
5.  Add any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
6.  Enter the sender's and recipient's email and phone numbers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Sender Info
*   **Sender Info** (`sender_info`, text, required): Enter the sender's name and address. This field is required for customs clearance purposes.

### Sender Country
*   **Sender Country** (`sender_country`, select_one, optional): Select the country where the sender is located. This field is optional but recommended for international shipments.

### Recipient Info
*   **Recipient Info** (`recipient_info`, text, optional): Enter the recipient's name and address. This field is optional but recommended for customs clearance purposes.

### Recipient Country
*   **Recipient Country** (`recipient_country`, select_one, optional): Select the country where the recipient is located. This field is optional but recommended for international shipments.

### Shipment Description
*   **Shipment Description** (`shipment_desc`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the shipment. This field is optional but recommended for customs clearance purposes.

### Shipment Value
*   **Shipment Value** (`shipment_value`, number, optional): Enter the value of the shipment. This field is optional but required for customs clearance purposes.

### Shipment Weight
*   **Shipment Weight** (`shipment_weight`, number, optional): Enter the weight of the shipment. This field is optional but required for customs clearance purposes.

### Customs Form
*   **Customs Form** (`customs_form`, select_multiple, optional): Select the customs form type. This field is optional but recommended for customs clearance purposes.

### Note
*   **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments. This field is optional.

### Email
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the sender's email address. This field is required for customs clearance purposes.

### Phone
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the sender's phone number. This field is required for customs clearance purposes.

### Date
*   **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the shipment. This field is optional but recommended for customs clearance purposes.

### Time
*   **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the shipment. This field is optional but recommended for customs clearance purposes.

## Tips

*   Be sure to fill in all the required fields accurately and thoroughly to avoid any issues with customs clearance.
*   Select the correct customs form type for your shipment.
*   Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field if necessary.
*   Review the completed form carefully before submitting it for customs clearance.
