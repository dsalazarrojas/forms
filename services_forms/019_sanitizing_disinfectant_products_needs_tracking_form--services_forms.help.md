> # sanitizing_disinfectant_products_needs_tracking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sanitizing Disinfectant Products Needs Tracking Form is used to collect and track information about sanitizing and disinfectant products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of the product you are tracking.
2. Enter a description of the product.
3. Enter the product name.
4. Enter the product quantity.
5. Enter the name of the vendor providing the product.
6. Enter the contact information for the vendor.
7. Enter the date and time when you requested the product.
8. Add any notes about the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **category** (Services Forms, optional): Select the category of the product you are tracking. This will help the system understand the type of product.
* **description** (description, required): Enter a brief description of the product.
* **product_name** (product_name, required): Enter the name of the product as it is known to the user.
* **product_quantity** (product_quantity, required): Enter the quantity of the product requested.
* **vendor_name** (vendor_name, required): Enter the name of the vendor providing the product.
* **vendor_contact** (vendor_contact, required): Enter the contact information for the vendor, including any relevant phone number and email address.
* **contact_number** (contact_number, optional): Enter any contact number for the vendor if available.
* **email** (email, optional): Enter the email address for the vendor if available.
* **date_requested** (date_requested, required): Enter the date when the product was requested.
* **time_requested** (time_requested, optional): Enter the time when the product was requested.
* **notes** (notes, optional): Add any notes about the request that may be helpful for tracking.
* **submit** (submit, required): Press this button to complete and submit the form.

Note: The fields marked as required must be filled out before submitting the form.
