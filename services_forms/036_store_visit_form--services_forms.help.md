# store_visit_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The store_visit_form is used to collect information about store visits from customers. This data can be used by store management to monitor sales, improve customer service, and track customer behavior.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the store visit information, including date, time, and duration of the visit.
2. Add any additional comments or notes about the visit.
3. Enter any additional information about the store, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Store Visit Information (Store Visit Form Page 1)** (`title`, `text`, required: false): This is the first page of the form where you'll enter the main store visit details.
* **Store Information (Store Visit Form Page 1)** (`store_info`, `text`, required: false): Here, you can enter the store's name, address, and any other relevant details.
* **Visit Date (Store Visit Form Page 1)** (`visit_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the visit.
* **Visit Time (Store Visit Form Page 1)** (`visit_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the visit.
* **Visit Duration (Store Visit Form Page 1)** (`visit_duration`, `number`, required: false): Enter the duration of the visit.
* **Comments (Store Visit Form Page 1)** (`visit_comments`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about the visit.
* **Visit Notes (Store Visit Form Page 1)** (`visit_notes`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional notes about the visit.
* **Visit Date 2 (Store Visit Form Page 2)** (`visit_date2`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the second visit.
* **Visit Time 2 (Store Visit Form Page 2)** (`visit_time2`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the second visit.
* **Visit Duration 2 (Store Visit Form Page 2)** (`visit_duration2`, `number`, required: false): Enter the duration of the second visit.
* **More Information (Store Visit Form Page 2)** (`store_visit_form_page_2`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional information about the visit.
* **Store Visit Form Additional Information (Store Visit Form Page 3)** (`store_visit_form_page_3`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional store visit information, if necessary.

Tips:
* Please ensure that you enter accurate and complete information about the store visit.
* If you have any additional information about the store, feel free to add it in the "Store Visit Form Additional Information" field.
