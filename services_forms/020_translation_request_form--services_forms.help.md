# Translation Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to submit requests for translation services. Please fill it out if you need help with translating documents, such as PDFs, Word documents, or Excel spreadsheets.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of translation you need. Choose from English to Spanish, English to French, or English to German.
2. Identify the type of document you need translated. Select PDF Document, Word Document, or Excel Document.
3. Enter the URL of the document you need translated, if applicable.
4. Enter your email address so we can contact you about your translation request.
5. Enter any phone number where you can be reached.
6. If you have any additional comments or messages, enter them in the text area provided.
7. Confirm that you are submitting the form intentionally.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submit Translation Request** (submit_translation_request): This field is a unique identifier for the form submission. Don't worry about filling it out; it's automatically generated.
* **Translation Type** (translation_type): This field allows you to select the type of translation you need. Choose from English to Spanish, English to French, or English to German.
* **Document Type** (document_type): This field lets you choose the type of document you need translated. Select from PDF Document, Word Document, or Excel Document.
* **Document URL** (document_url): This field is for entering the URL of the document you need translated. If the document is not online, you can leave this field blank.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your translation request.
* **Phone Number** (phone_number): Enter your phone number where we can contact you about your translation request.
* **Additional Message** (message): This is a text area for any additional comments or messages you have regarding your translation request.
* **Confirm Submit** (confirm_submit): Confirm that you are intentionally submitting this form by selecting "True".
* **Error Message** (error_message): This field will display any error messages related to your form submission. Check this field after submitting the form to see if there were any issues.
