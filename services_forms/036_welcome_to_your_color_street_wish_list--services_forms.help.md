# welcome_to_your_color_street_wish_list_00 - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "welcome_to_your_color_street_wish_list_00" form is designed to collect information from users about their color preferences for street art or other street-related features. This form will help us better understand the community's interests and preferences for our street projects.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your preferred color from the options provided in the "color_street_wish_list_01" field.
2. If you have any special instructions or notes, please provide them in the "special_instructions_03" field.
3. You can add any additional comments or information in the text fields starting from "color_street_wish_list_04" and ending with "color_street_wish_list_25".
4. Enter any email address in the "color_street_wish_list_07" field, if you want to be contacted about your wish list.
5. Fill in the required information for all the fields. Note that the form is not required, but filling it in will help us better understand your preferences.
