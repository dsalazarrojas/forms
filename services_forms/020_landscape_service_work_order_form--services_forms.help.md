# Landscape Service Work Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Landscape Service Work Order Form is a tool used by businesses to gather information from customers about their landscaping service needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the start date for the service job. This is the date when the service will begin.
2. Enter the end date for the service job. This is the date when the service will end.
3. Enter the location where the service will be performed. This can be a house, office building, or any other location where the service will take place.
4. Enter the customer's name. This will help the business to keep track of who is requesting the service.
5. Enter any additional notes about the service required. This can include specific details about the job such as plants, equipment, or any other relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Start Date (1)
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the start date for the service job.

### End Date (2)
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the end date for the service job.

### Service Location (3)
* **Service Location** (`service_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location where the service will be performed. This can be a house, office building, or any other location where the service will take place.

### Customer Name (4)
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): Enter the customer's name. This will help the business to keep track of who is requesting the service.

### Service Notes (5)
* **Service Notes** (`service_notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes about the service required. This can include specific details about the job such as plants, equipment, or any other relevant information.
