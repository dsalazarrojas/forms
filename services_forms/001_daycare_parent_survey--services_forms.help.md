# Daycare Parent Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from parents or caregivers about their child's experiences with our daycare service. The survey aims to gather data on their satisfaction with our services, identify areas for improvement, and help us better understand their needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Select the correct pages (if multiple pages are available).
2. Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
3. Review and submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the child's first name.
- **Last Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the child's last name.
- **Address** (text, required/optional): Enter the child's address or the address where the child attends our daycare service.
- **Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter the child's phone number or the phone number of the child's caregiver (if different from the child's phone).
- **Email** (email, required/optional): Enter the child's email address or the email address of the child's caregiver (if different from the child's email).
- **Date of Birth** (date, required/optional): Enter the child's date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
- **Date of Last Visit** (date, required/optional): Enter the date of the child's last visit to our daycare service.
- **Rating** (number, required/optional): Rate your experience with our daycare service (e.g., 1-5).
- **Feedback** (text, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with our daycare service.
- **Comments** (text, required/optional): Share any further comments or suggestions about our daycare service.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Double-check the information you enter to ensure it is accurate.
- If you have any issues or need help, feel free to contact our support team.
- The information collected will be used to improve our services and better serve your needs.
