# YoniVaginal Steam Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The YoniVaginal Steam Intake Form is designed to gather information from patients before they receive steam services. This includes their contact details, medical history, and service requests. Completing this form ensures that patients are aware of their appointment schedule and any necessary preparations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the YoniVaginal Steam Intake Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Patient Information** (page 1) with your name, any medical conditions, and any other relevant details.
2. Provide a brief **Medical History** (page 2) if necessary, to inform our staff about any health concerns.
3. Select the **Steam Service(s) Requested** (page 11) for the treatment you require.
4. Enter your **Arrival Time** (page 4) to ensure you arrive 15 minutes early for your service.
5. Provide **Contact Information** (pages 5-7), including your phone number and email address.
6. Enter the contact details of an **Emergency Contact** (pages 8-10), including their phone number and name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Patient Information
* **Patient Information**: Please fill in your name, any relevant medical information, or other details.
* **Medical History**: Provide any relevant medical information to help our staff better understand your needs.
### Page 2: Medical History
* **Medical History**: A brief summary of your medical history, if necessary.
### Page 3: Medical Condition
* **Medical Condition**: Please describe any medical conditions you have that may affect your treatment.
### Page 4: Arrive Early
* **Arrive Early**: Enter your expected arrival time, 15 minutes prior to your scheduled appointment.
### Page 5: Contact Info
* **Contact Info**: Fill in your contact details, such as your name, phone number, and email address.
### Page 6: Phone
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for our staff to contact you.
### Page 7: Email
* **Email**: Enter your email address for us to contact you.
### Page 8: Emergency Contact
* **Emergency Contact**: Provide the name of the emergency contact person.
### Page 9: Name
* **Name**: Enter the name of your emergency contact.
### Page 10: Phone
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
### Page 11: Service Request
* **Service Request**: Select the steam service(s) you would like to receive.

## Tips
* Please ensure you complete the form accurately and thoroughly to avoid any delays or misunderstandings.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact our staff before or after submitting the form.
* If you have any medical conditions that may affect your treatment, please provide a detailed explanation in the **Medical History** section.
