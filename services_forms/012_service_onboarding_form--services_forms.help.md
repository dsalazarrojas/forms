# Service Onboarding Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to onboard a service, collecting necessary information for its configuration and management. It's essential to provide accurate and relevant data to ensure smooth service operation and minimize potential issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information, including company details (Company Name, Company Address, Phone, and Email).
2. Provide the Service Name and Type.
3. Enter Service Details, which will help us understand the service's requirements and specifications.
4. Select the Service Agreement status (Yes or No) and, if needed, describe the other agreement details.
5. Choose the Service Status (Active or Inactive).
6. If needed, provide Service Comments to explain any additional information.
7. For Service Terms, select the agreement status (Yes or No) and, if needed, describe the other agreement details. Enter the Service Terms Frequency and Duration.
8. Review the provided data and submit it for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company providing the service.
* **Company Address**: Enter the address of the company providing the service.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the company providing the service.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the company providing the service.
* **Service Name**: Enter a name for the service.
* **Service Type**: Enter the type of service being onboarded.
* **Service Details**: Provide a description of the service, including its requirements and specifications.
* **Service Agreement**: Choose 'Yes' if you agree to the service agreement, or 'No' if you do not. If you selected 'No', you can provide additional information about why you cannot agree to the service agreement.
* **Service Status**: Choose the status of the service (Active or Inactive).
* **Service Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information about the service.
* **Service Terms**: Choose 'Yes' if you agree to the service terms, or 'No' if you do not. If you selected 'No', you can provide additional information about why you cannot agree to the service terms.
* **Service Terms Frequency**: Enter the frequency of the service terms (e.g., weekly, monthly, etc.).
* **Service Terms Duration**: Enter the duration of the service terms (e.g., 1 week, 1 month, etc.).
* **Service Terms Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information about the service terms.
* **Service Terms Agreement**: Choose 'Yes' if you agree to the service terms, or 'No' if you do not. If you selected 'No', you can provide additional information about why you cannot agree to the service terms.
* **Service Terms Agreement Other**: If you selected 'No' for Service Terms Agreement, describe the other agreement details.
* **Service Terms Frequency Other**: If you selected 'Other' for Service Terms Frequency, describe the frequency details.
* **Service Terms Duration**: Enter the duration of the service terms.
* **Service Terms Agreement Other**: If you selected 'No' for Service Terms Agreement, describe the other agreement details.
* **Submit**: Click 'Submit' to submit the form and complete the service onboarding process.
* **Cancel**: Click 'Cancel' to cancel the onboarding process without saving any changes.
