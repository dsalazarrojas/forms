# mentoring_program_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants about their experience in the mentoring program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on "Submit" to begin.
2. On the first page, you will see the form title.
3. Fill in the form fields as prompted, one by one.
4. For each field, review the explanation below to understand what is required.
5. Click "Submit" to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **mentoring_program_survey** (`text`, required/optional): This field is the title of the form and is not editable.
* **program_length** (`number`, required/optional): Please select the length of the program you participated in (e.g., number of sessions, number of weeks).
* **program_frequency** (`number`, required/optional): Please select the frequency of the program (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, etc.).
* **program_outcome** (`text`, required/optional): Describe your experience with the program and what you achieved or learned.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* Provide specific examples or details about your experience.
* If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to contact us.
* Your feedback is greatly appreciated and will help us improve the program.
