# General Services Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The General Services Application Form is designed to collect essential details from users for general services requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) on the right.
2. Enter the service you are requesting in the `service_request` field.
3. Select the date you are available for services from the `service_date` field.
4. Choose the location you would like to have services provided from the `service_location` field. You can select multiple options if applicable.
5. Provide detailed information about the services you require in the `service_details` field.
6. Enter your name in the `name` field.
7. Enter your contact email address in the `contact_email` field.
8. Enter your contact phone number in the `contact_phone` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Request (*)**: Enter a brief description of the service you are requesting.
* **Service Date (*)**: Select the date you are available for services.
* **Service Location (*)**: Choose the location where you would like to have services provided. You can select multiple locations if applicable.
* **Service Details (*)**: Provide detailed information about the services you require.
* **Name**: Enter your name.
* **Contact Email (*)**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone (*)**: Enter your contact phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you have filled out all required fields to ensure that your service request is processed correctly.
