# patrol_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patrol Report Form is used to document and report patrol activities, providing details such as officer identity, dates, locations, and incident descriptions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct options for each field, ensuring you provide accurate information for each section.
2. Enter your information clearly and concisely, using the space provided for each field.
3. Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Officer ID** (`officer_id`, number, required: false): Enter your officer ID or badge number.
* **Date and Time** (`date_and_time`, date, required: false): Enter the date and time of your patrol activity.
* **Unit ID** (`unit_id`, number, required: false): Enter the ID of the unit you were patrolling with.
* **Officer Name** (`officer_name`, text, required: false): Enter your full name.
* **Report Start Time** (`report_start_time`, time, required: false): Enter the start time of your patrol report.
* **Report End Time** (`report_end_time`, time, required: false): Enter the end time of your patrol report.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of your patrol activity.
* **Event Description** (`event_description`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the patrol event.
* **Unit Type** (`unit_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of unit you were patrolling with (Police Patrol, K9 Unit, Traffic).
* **Unit Status** (`unit_status`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the status of the unit (Active, Inactive).
* **Report Type** (`report_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of report (Patrol, Arrest, Other).
* **Incident Number** (`incident_number`, text, required: false): Enter the incident number, if applicable.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the incident, if applicable.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the incident, if applicable.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of the incident, if applicable.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the incident, if applicable.

## Tips

* Ensure you provide accurate and relevant information for each field.
* Double-check your entries for completeness and consistency.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to your unit's standard operating procedures or consult with your supervisor.
