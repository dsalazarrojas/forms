# IT Service Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The IT Service Request Form is used to submit requests for IT services related to problems or issues you are experiencing with your devices, software, or network. By filling out this form, you will be able to provide detailed information about your problem and the services you require to resolve it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field according to the explanations provided below.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) next to the field label.
3. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **IT Service Request**
* **IT Service Request** (Field 1): Enter a brief description of your problem or issue.
* **Problem Start Date** (Field 2): Enter the date your problem started.
* **Problem Description** (Field 3): Provide a detailed description of your problem or issue.
* **Device Problem?** (Field 4): Check this box if your problem is related to a device.
* **Network Problem?** (Field 5): Check this box if your problem is related to a network issue.
* **Repair Service?** (Field 6): Check this box if you require repair service.
* **Service Type** (Field 7): Select the type of service you require (Hardware, Software, Network, or Network and Hardware).
* **Description** (Field 8): Provide a detailed description of the services you require.
* **Repair Service Details** (Field 9): Provide any additional information about the repair service you require.
* **Customer Phone** (Field 10): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted.
* **Customer Email** (Field 11): Enter your email address if you want to be contacted by email.
* **Technician** (Field 12): Select the technician you would like to work with (Jimmy, Sarah, or John).
* **Assigned Technician** (Field 13): Select a technician to be assigned to your request (Jimmy, Sarah, or John).
* **Status** (Field 14): Enter the current status of your request (e.g., New, In Progress, Resolved).
* **Due Date** (Field 15): Enter the due date for your request.
* **Priority** (Field 16): Select the priority level of your request (Low, Medium, High).
* **Notes** (Field 17): Provide any additional information or comments for the technician assigned to your request.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide detailed and accurate information in each field to help the technician assigned to your request resolve your issue efficiently.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact the support team.
* We will contact you as soon as possible after receiving your request to discuss further details.
