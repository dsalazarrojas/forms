# Client Consent - Waxing - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Client Consent - Waxing form is designed to gather crucial information from clients regarding their consent to undergo waxing services. This form helps to ensure clients are aware of their medical history, potential risks, and responsibilities after the procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Client Consent - Waxing form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Full Name** in the first field.
2. Provide your **Today's Date** in the second field.
3. Enter your **Phone Number** for contact purposes.
4. In the next section, provide your **Waxing Health History** as a note, detailing any health concerns you have regarding waxing.
5. Answer whether you have used **Accutane** in the last year. Select 'True' if you've used it, 'False' otherwise.
6. Let us know if you are using any **Topical Exfoliants** like Retin-A or Differin. Select 'True' if you're using any, 'False' otherwise.
7. List any known **Allergies** you have, e.g., latex or fragrance.
8. Answer whether you are **Pregnant or Nursing**. Select 'True' if you are, 'False' otherwise.
9. Select the areas you want to be **Waxed** from the options provided.
10. In the last section, provide your **Consent and Aftercare** information in the form of notes.
11. Confirm you **Understand the Risks of Waxing**, including redness and irritation. Select 'True' or 'I understand'.
12. Finally, confirm you will follow the **Aftercare Instructions** by selecting 'True' or 'I agree'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (1)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Today's Date (2)**: Provide the current date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Phone Number (3)**: Enter your contact phone number for any aftercare instructions or follow-up communications.
* **-- Waxing Health History -- (4)**: Briefly describe any health concerns or conditions you have that may impact your waxing experience.
* **Have you used Accutane in the last year? (5)**: Select 'True' if you've used Accutane recently, 'False' otherwise. Accutane can affect the skin's sensitivity during and after waxing.
* **Are you using Retin-A, Differin, or other topical exfoliants? (6)**: Select 'True' if you use any topical exfoliants, 'False' otherwise.
* **List any known allergies (7)**: Briefly list any known allergies you have, e.g., latex, wax, fragrance.
* **Are you currently pregnant or nursing? (8)**: Select 'True' if you are, 'False' otherwise.
* **Areas to be waxed (9)**: Select all the areas you wish to be waxed from the provided options (Eyebrows, Upper Lip, Chin, Underarms, Legs, Bikini, Back, Chest).
* **-- Consent and Aftercare -- (10)**: In this section, provide any notes regarding your consent to the procedure and your understanding of aftercare instructions.
* **I understand the risks of waxing including redness and irritation (11)**: Select 'True' to confirm you're aware of the potential risks.
* **I will follow the provided aftercare instructions (12)**: Select 'True' to confirm your commitment to following aftercare instructions.

By following these steps and completing this form accurately, you'll ensure your safety and comfort during the waxing procedure.
