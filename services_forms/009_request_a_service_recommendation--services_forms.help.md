# Request A Service Recommendation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Request A Service Recommendation form is a tool used to gather information from clients regarding their service requests. It helps internal teams provide efficient and personalized services to clients.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Service Request**: Enter a brief description of the service you are requesting. This is a required field.
2. **Priority**: Select the level of priority you assign to this service request (High, Medium, or Low). This is an optional field.
3. **Service Type**: Choose one or more types of service that applies to your request (Home Service, Medical Service, or Transportation Service). This is an optional field.
4. **Description**: Provide a detailed description of the service you are requesting. This is an optional field.
5. **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person you are requesting the service for. This is an optional field.
6. **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number for the person you are requesting the service for. This is an optional field.
7. **Contact Email**: Enter the email address for the person you are requesting the service for. This is an optional field and should be in a valid email format.
8. **Follow up Required**: Check if you require follow-up on this service request (True or False). This is an optional field.
9. **Follow up Date**: Enter the date you would like to be followed up on, in the format mm/dd/yyyy. This is an optional field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Request**: This field is used to enter a description of the service you are requesting. Enter a brief and clear description of the service you need.
* **Priority**: Select the level of priority you assign to this service request. This will help internal teams understand the level of urgency and attention required.
* **Service Type**: Choose one or more types of service that apply to your request. This will help internal teams tailor their response to your specific needs.
* **Description**: Provide a detailed description of the service you are requesting. This will help internal teams understand your requirements and provide accurate quotes or services.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person you are requesting the service for. This will ensure that internal teams know who to communicate with regarding your service request.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number for the person you are requesting the service for. This will help internal teams contact you for additional information or clarification.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address for the person you are requesting the service for. This should be in a valid email format and will help internal teams contact you regarding your service request.
* **Follow up Required**: Check if you require follow-up on this service request. This will inform internal teams if you need to be contacted for updates or progress on your service request.
* **Follow up Date**: Enter the date you would like to be followed up on, in the format mm/dd/yyyy. This will help internal teams schedule follow-up accordingly.
