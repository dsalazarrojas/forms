# Service Inquiry Form Green Theme - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from users to process a service inquiry request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your **First Name** in the text field.
2. Provide your **Last Name** in the text field.
3. Enter your **Email** in the email field, following the standard email format (e.g., `username@example.com`).
4. Write a brief description of your **Question** in the text field.
5. Enter your **Contact Number** in the text field.
6. Select the type of **Service** you are inquiring about from the dropdown menu.
7. If you have any **Account Information**, please provide it in the text field (optional).
8. If you have **Billing Information**, please provide it in the text field (optional).
9. Select any additional **Options** that apply to your inquiry from the dropdown menu (optional).
10. Enter the **Date** you would like to schedule the service in the date field.
11. Choose the **Time** you would like to schedule the service in the time field.
12. Add any additional **Note** regarding your inquiry in the text field (optional).
13. If you have a **Phone Number**, please enter it in the text field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Question** (`question`, `text`, required): Describe the service you are inquiring about.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter your contact number.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of service you need.
* **Account Info** (`account_info`, `text`, optional): Provide any relevant account information if applicable.
* **Billing Info** (`billing_info`, `text`, optional): Provide any relevant billing information if applicable.
* **Options** (`question`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any additional options that apply to your inquiry.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Select the date you would like to schedule the service.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Select the time you would like to schedule the service.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes regarding your inquiry.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, please feel free to contact us.
* We will respond to your inquiry as soon as possible.
