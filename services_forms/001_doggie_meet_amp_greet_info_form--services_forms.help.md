# Doggie Meet & Greet Info Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Doggie Meet & Greet Info Form is a collection of questions that will help us prepare for the meet-and-greet session with you and your pet. Please answer all the questions honestly and accurately to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for both you and your pet.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
2. Enter your first name, last name, address, city, state, zip, and phone number in the text fields.
3. Select the breed(s) of your pet from the multiple-choice select field.
4. Choose the size of your pet from the single-choice select field.
5. Select the type of pet you have (e.g., dog, cat, bird, etc.) from the single-choice select field.
6. Enter the time and date of your preferred appointment time in the time and date fields.
7. Enter your email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name.
* **Address**: Enter your home address.
* **City**: Enter the city where you reside.
* **State**: Enter the state where you reside.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **Pet breed**: Select the breed(s) of your pet from the options provided.
	+ Note: You can select multiple breeds if you have multiple pets.
* **Pet size**: Select the size of your pet from the options provided.
* **Pet type**: Select the type of pet you have (e.g., dog, cat, bird, etc.).
* **Appointment time**: Choose a time for your appointment.
* **Appointment date**: Choose a date for your appointment.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and honestly to avoid any issues during the meet-and-greet session.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.

This form is designed to be user-friendly and straightforward. If you have any issues or need further clarification, please do not hesitate to contact us.
