# Cable TV Registration Online Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Cable TV Registration Online Application Form is designed to register users for cable TV services. This form helps gather essential information for a smooth and efficient service activation process.

## How To Complete This Form

- Read each field's explanation to understand the expected input.
- Answer each question according to your personal and service preferences.
- Double-check your input before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Applicant name**: Your full name for registration and billing purposes.
- **Contact email**: A valid email address for service activation and billing notifications.
- **Service address**: The address where the cable TV service will be installed.
- **Package selected**: Choose your desired cable TV package (Basic, Sports, Premium, or International).
- **Installation preference**: Select how you would like to have your cable TV installed (Self install or Professional install).
- **Billing cycle**: Choose your billing cycle (Monthly or Annually).
- **Promotional code**: If you have a promotional code for discounts, enter it here (optional).
- **Special instructions for technician**: Provide any special access or parking instructions for the technician.

## Tips

- Ensure your contact email is valid and accessible for service activation and billing purposes.
- Double-check your package selection and installation preference to avoid delays in service activation.
- If you have a promotional code, do not forget to enter it for applicable discounts.
- If you have any special instructions for the technician, make sure to include them for a smooth installation process.
