# services_directory_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow users to submit their services to a shared directory.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Click on the "Submit Request" page to begin.
2. Fill out each field accordingly, making sure to provide as much detail as possible.
3. Review the form for any errors before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Submit Request** (1)
The first step is to click on the "Submit Request" page to start the submission process.

### **Service Provider** (2)
Enter the name of the service provider. This could be a person, organization, or business.

### **Service Title** (3)
Enter a descriptive title for the service. This should be a concise and clear title that describes the service.

### **Description** (4)
Provide a brief description of the service. This will help others understand the details of the service.

### **Service Type** (5)
Select the category and subcategory of the service from the dropdown menu. This will help to group services together and make them easier to search.

### **Service Tags** (6)
Select the relevant tags for the service from the dropdown menu. This will help to categorize the service and make it easier to search.

### **Submitter Email** (7)
Enter the email address of the person submitting the service. This is necessary for communication and follow-up.

### **Submitter Phone** (8)
Enter the phone number of the person submitting the service. This is optional and only necessary if you want to provide an additional contact method.

Tips:
* Make sure to check if the service already exists in the directory before submitting a new entry.
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form to ensure that others can get the most out of your service.
* If you're unsure about any field, you can always contact the administrator for help.
