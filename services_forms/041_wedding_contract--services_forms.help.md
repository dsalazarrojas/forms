# Wedding Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create and manage wedding contracts between a wedding planner and a client. It captures the necessary information for the wedding event, including contact details, date and time, payment terms, and contract terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and fill out the required information.
2. Make sure to include accurate and complete details.
3. Review each page carefully before proceeding to the next one.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (contact_information, email, required: false):
This field is used to capture the client's contact information.
* **Date and Time** (date_and_time, date, required: false):
This field is used to select the date and time of the wedding event.
* **Payment Terms** (payment_terms, text, required: false):
This field is used to outline the payment terms and conditions for the wedding service.
* **Contract Terms** (contract_terms, text, required: false):
This field is used to outline the contract terms and conditions for the wedding service.
* **Service Details** (service_details, text, required: false):
This field is used to provide detailed information about the wedding service.
* **Payment Options** (payment_options, select_one, required: false):
This field is used to select the payment options for the wedding service.
* **Signature Page** (signature_page, note, required: false):
This field is used for the client to sign and date the contract.
* **Review Page** (review_page, note, required: false):
This field is used for the client to review and agree to the contract terms.
* **Payment Terms Page** (payment_terms_page, note, required: false):
This field is used for the client to review and agree to the payment terms.
* **Contract Terms Page** (contract_terms_page, note, required: false):
This field is used for the client to review and agree to the contract terms.
* **Service Details Page** (service_details_page, note, required: false):
This field is used for the client to review and agree to the service details.

## Tips
* Please review each page carefully before proceeding to the next one.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your wedding planner for assistance.
