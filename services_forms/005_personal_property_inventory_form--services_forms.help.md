# personal_property_inventory_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The personal_property_inventory_form is used to record and track personal properties. This form is designed to collect information about the type, location, description, purchase date, and purchase price of a personal property. Please note that this form is not meant for commercial properties, so if you are filling out this form for a property you intend to rent out or sell, please use the appropriate form for commercial properties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Select the type of property you are recording, choosing from Automobile, Real Estate, or Other.
2. Enter the location of the property, including the address and any other relevant details.
3. Provide a detailed description of the property, including any features or notable characteristics.
4. If you have purchased the property, enter the date of purchase.
5. Enter the purchase price of the property.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Property Type (1)
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of property you are recording, such as an automobile, a house, or other types of property.

### Property Location (2)
* **Property Location** (`property_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of the property, including the address and any other relevant details.

### Property Description (3)
* **Property Description** (`property_description`, text, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the property, including any features or notable characteristics.

### Purchase Date (4)
* **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`, date, required: false): If you have purchased the property, enter the date of purchase.

### Purchase Price (5)
* **Purchase Price** (`purchase_price`, number, required: false): Enter the purchase price of the property.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out completely and accurately.
* For properties with complex or multiple components (e.g., a collection of properties), please submit each property as a separate form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields or have questions, please contact your administrator for assistance.
