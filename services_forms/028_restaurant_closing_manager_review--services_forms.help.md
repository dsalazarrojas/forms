# Restaurant Closing Manager Review - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Restaurant Closing Manager Review form is designed to gather information from restaurant closing managers about their experience and evaluation of a restaurant's closing process. This form will help restaurant owners and management teams assess the effectiveness of their closing practices and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the required fields:
	* **Restaurant Closing Manager Review** (Field 1): Enter a brief description of your experience with the closing process.
	* **Restaurant Name** (Field 2): Enter the name of the restaurant you are reviewing.
2. Optionally, select the closing date (Field 3) if it is relevant to your experience.
3. If you have any specific comments about the closing manager (Field 4), please enter their name.
4. You can add any additional comments about the closing manager (Field 5) if needed.
5. If you need to enter the manager's name who was present (Field 6), please do so.
6. Finally, add any comments about the manager's performance (Field 7).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Restaurant Closing Manager Review** (`Field 1`, text, required): A brief description of your experience with the closing process.
* **Restaurant Name** (`Field 2`, text, required): Enter the name of the restaurant you are reviewing.
* **Closing Date** (`Field 3`, date, optional): The date of the closing.
* **Restaurant Closing Manager** (`Field 4`, text, optional): Enter the name of the restaurant closing manager.
* **Closing Manager Comments** (`Field 5`, text, optional): Any comments or feedback you have about the closing manager.
* **Manager Name** (`Field 6`, text, optional): Enter the name of the manager present.
* **Manager Comments** (`Field 7`, text, optional): Add any comments or feedback about the manager's performance.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when entering your experience and comments.
* Use clear and concise language to ensure that your feedback is understood accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the form owner for assistance.
