# travel_questionnaire_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The travel questionnaire form is used to collect information for travel-related requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Choose the relevant answers for each field based on your travel request.
2. Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for all required fields.
3. Review your form to ensure all required fields are filled out before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Travel Agency** (`travel_agency`, `select_one`, required): Select the travel agency that booked your travel request.
* **Departure Date** (`departure_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are departing for your travel.
* **Arrival Date** (`arrival_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are arriving back from your travel.
* **Travel Duration** (`travel_duration`, `number`, required): Enter the duration of your travel, in days.
* **Travel Type** (`travel_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether your travel is for business or pleasure.
* **Travel Document** (`travel_document`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether you will need a travel document for your trip.
* **Travel Currency** (`travel_currency`, `select_one`, optional): Select the currency for your travel expenses.
* **Accommodation Type** (`accommodation_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the type of accommodation you will be using for your travel.
* **Special Request** (`special_request`, `text`, optional): Enter any special requests you may have for your travel.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter your contact number for the travel agency.
* **Travel Agent** (`travel_agent`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether you would like to work with a travel agent for your travel request.
* **Travel Notes** (`travel_notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes for the travel agency.
* **Travel Document Type** (`travel_document_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of travel document you will need for your trip.
* **Travel Currency Type** (`travel_currency_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of travel currency you will be using for your expenses.
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any special requirements you may have for your travel.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for the travel agency.
* **Travel Agent Note** (`travel_agent_note`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes for the travel agent.
* **Travel Notes 2** (`travel_notes_2`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes for the travel agency.
* **Travel Agency 2** (`travel_agency_2`, `select_one`, optional): Select the second travel agency that booked your travel request.
* **Travel Currency 2** (`travel_currency_2`, `select_one`, optional): Select the second currency for your travel expenses.
* **Accommodation Type 2** (`accommodation_type_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the second type of accommodation you will be using for your travel.
* **Travel Document 2** (`travel_document_2`, `select_one`, optional): Select the second travel document you will need for your trip.
* **Special Requirements 2** (`special_requirements_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any special requirements you may have for your second travel request.
* **Travel Currency 3** (`travel_currency_3`, `select_one`, optional): Select the third currency for your travel expenses.
* **Contact Number 2** (`contact_number_2`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact number for the second travel agency.
* **Travel Agent 2** (`travel_agent_2`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether you would like to work with a second travel agent for your second travel request.
* **Travel Notes 3** (`travel_notes_3`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes for the second travel agency.
