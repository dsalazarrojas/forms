# Workplace Housekeeping Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for tracking various aspects of workplace housekeeping, ensuring the workplace is kept clean, organized, and safe for employees.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully, and ensure you are completing it for the correct workplace.
2. Select the relevant options for each field based on your workplace's specific needs.
3. Fill in all required fields, and provide as much detail as possible for optional fields.
4. Submit the completed form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Morning Shift** (`morning_shift`, number, optional): Enter the frequency of morning shifts performed in the workplace.
* **Evening Shift** (`evening_shift`, number, optional): Enter the frequency of evening shifts performed in the workplace.
* **Cleaning Frequency** (`cleaning_frequency`, number, optional): Enter the frequency of cleaning performed in the workplace.
* **Waste Disposal** (`waste_disposal`, text, optional): Describe how waste is disposed of in the workplace (e.g., recycling, landfill, compost).
* **Personal Items** (`personal_items`, text, optional): List any personal items stored in the workplace, such as employee belongings.
* **Hand Hygiene** (`hand_hygiene`, text, optional): Describe the hand hygiene practices used in the workplace (e.g., handwashing, hand sanitizer).
* **First Aid Kit** (`first_aid_kit`, text, optional): Describe the first aid kit contents and location in the workplace.
* **Chemical Storages** (`chemical_storages`, text, optional): Describe the storage and management of chemicals in the workplace.
* **Pest Control** (`pest_control`, select_one, optional): Select from the options provided to indicate if pest control is performed (Pest Control, True, False).
* **Employee Access** (`employee_access`, select_multiple, optional): Select from the options provided to indicate which employees have access to areas with chemicals (False, True, Some).
* **Equipment Maintenance** (`equipment_maintenance`, date, optional): Enter the date of last equipment maintenance performed.
* **Record Keeper** (`record_keeper`, time, optional): Enter the time of last record keeping update.
* **Chemical Storage** (`chemical_storage`, select_multiple, optional): Select from the options provided to indicate if chemical storage is performed (False, True, Some).
* **Employee Training** (`employee_training`, select_multiple, optional): Select from the options provided to indicate if employee training is performed (False, True, Some).
* **Fire Safety Plan** (`fire_safety_plan`, select_one, optional): Select from the options provided to indicate if a fire safety plan is in place (Fire Safety, False, Other).
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Describe the emergency contact details for the workplace (e.g., name, phone number, location).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the contact number of the emergency contact.
