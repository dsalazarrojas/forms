# staff_interactive_museum_info - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information about museum staff, including their personal details and relevant notes. The form is used for internal purposes and does not require a login to access.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*), if applicable.
3. For fields with options, select the relevant information from the provided choices.
4. Enter your personal email address and phone number for contact purposes.
5. Add any additional notes in the "R2ISE Notes" field if necessary.
6. Once you have completed the form, review it carefully for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Museum Information**: Enter a brief description of the museum or organization.
* **Peer Information**: Select the relevant peers or colleagues who will be working with you on R2ISE projects.
* **R2ISE Date**: Enter the date of the R2ISE activity, following the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **R2ISE Time**: Enter the time of the R2ISE activity, following the standard time format (HH:MM:SS).
* **Email**: Enter your personal email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your personal phone number for contact purposes.
* **R2ISE Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the R2ISE activity.
* **RCO Staff**: Enter any relevant information about the RCO staff, if applicable.
