# Pest Control Estimate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Pest Control Estimate Form" is designed to collect information from clients seeking pest control services. This form aims to gather details about the client, the pest problem, and the services required for effective pest control solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in your client's name and contact information (email and phone number) in the "Client Name", "Email", and "Phone" fields.
2. Provide an estimated date for the service in the "Estimate Date" field.
3. Enter the location where the service is required in the "Location" field.
4. Select the type of service required from the list of options provided in the "Service Type" field.
5. Choose the frequency of the service required from the "Service Frequency" field.
6. Enter the estimated cost of the service in the "Estimate Cost" field.
7. Provide any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
8. If applicable, select the type(s) of pest(s) infestation from the "Pest Types" field.
9. Enter the name of the service provider in the "Service Provider" field.
10. Enter any additional information regarding the assigned tool or equipment in the "Assigned Tool" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name (Client Name, text, required):** Enter the client's name to whom the services are being provided.
* **Estimate Date (Estimate Date, date, required):** The estimated date for the service.
* **Location (Location, text, required):** The location where the service is required.
* **Service Type (Service Type, text, required):** The type of service required (e.g., bed bug, carpet beetle, etc.).
* **Pest Types (Pest Types, select multiple, optional):** Select all applicable pest types from the list of options provided.
* **Service Frequency (Service Frequency, select one, required):** The frequency of the service required (e.g., 1 time, 2 times, etc.).
* **Estimate Cost (Estimate Cost, number, required):** Enter the estimated cost of the service.
* **Notes (Notes, note, required):** Provide any additional notes or comments about the service.
* **Service Provider (Service Provider, text, required):** Enter the name of the service provider.
* **Phone (Phone, text, required):** Enter the client's phone number.
* **Email (Email, email, required):** Enter the client's email address.
* **Assigned Tool (Assigned Tool, text, required):** Enter any additional information regarding the assigned tool or equipment.
