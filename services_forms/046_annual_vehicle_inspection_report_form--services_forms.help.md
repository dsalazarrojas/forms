# Annual Vehicle Inspection Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Annual Vehicle Inspection Report Form is used to document the results of a vehicle inspection, including information about the vehicle's condition, maintenance, and defects.
## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in the inspector's name and ID.
2.  Enter the contact email and phone number of the inspector.
3.  Enter the name and address of the inspection facility.
4.  Enter the date and year of the vehicle inspection.
5.  Enter the vehicle owner's name and address.
6.  Enter the vehicle make, model, and year.
7.  Enter the vehicle's identification number (VIN).
8.  Enter the license plate number.
9.  Enter the state of registration.
10.  Enter the current odometer reading.
11.  Select the vehicle type.
12.  Select the type of fuel used by the vehicle.
13.  Evaluate the condition of the front and rear brakes.
14.  Evaluate the condition of the tires.
15.  Enter the minimum tread depth of the tires (if applicable).
16.  Evaluate the condition of the suspension and steering.
17.  Evaluate the condition of the exhaust system.
18.  Evaluate the condition of the lighting equipment.
19.  Evaluate the condition of the windshield and windows.
20.  Evaluate the condition of the mirrors.
21.  Evaluate the condition of the wipers.
22.  Evaluate the condition of the horn.
23.  Evaluate the condition of the seat belts.
24.  Evaluate the status of the airbags.
25.  Evaluate the condition of the fuel system.
26.  Evaluate the overall condition of the engine.
27.  Evaluate the condition of the transmission.
28.  Enter the result of the emissions test.
29.  Enter the result of the safety inspection.
30.  List any defects noted during the inspection.
31.  Suggest any repairs that are recommended.
32.  Enter the estimated cost of any repairs (if applicable).
33.  Enter the overall result of the inspection (Pass, Fail, or Conditional Pass).
34.  Enter the date and time the inspection sticker was issued (if applicable).
35.  Enter the date the next inspection is due.
36.  Enter your signature as the inspector.
37.  Enter any additional comments or information.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inspector Full Name** (`inspector_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the person conducting the inspection.
* **Inspector ID** (`inspector_id`, `text`, optional): Enter the ID or certification number of the inspector.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter the contact email address of the inspector.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the contact phone number of the inspector.
* **Shop Name** (`shop_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the inspection facility.
* **Shop Address** (`shop_address`, `text`, required): Enter the address of the inspection facility.
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the vehicle was inspected.
* **Inspection Year** (`inspection_year`, `select_one`, required): Select the year of the inspection from the options provided.
* **Vehicle Owner** (`vehicle_owner`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the vehicle owner.
* **Owner Address** (`owner_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the address of the vehicle owner.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, `text`, required): Enter the make of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, `text`, required): Enter the model of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, `text`, required): Enter the year of the vehicle manufacture.
* **VIN Number** (`vin_number`, `text`, required): Enter the vehicle identification number (VIN).
* **License Plate** (`license_plate`, `text`, required): Enter the license plate number.
* **License State** (`license_state`, `text`, required): Enter the state of registration.
* **Odometer Reading** (`odometer_reading`, `number`, required): Enter the current odometer reading.
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of vehicle from the options provided.
* **Fuel Type** (`fuel_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of fuel used by the vehicle from the options provided.
* **Front Brakes** (`brakes_front`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate the condition of the front brakes.
* **Rear Brakes** (`brakes_rear`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate the condition of the rear brakes.
* **Tires Condition** (`tires_condition`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate the condition of the tires.
* **Tires Tread Depth** (`tires_tread_depth`, `text`, optional): Enter the minimum tread depth of the tires (if applicable).
* **Suspension and Steering** (`suspension_steering`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate the condition of the suspension and steering.
* **Exhaust System** (`exhaust_system`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate the condition of the exhaust system.
* **Lighting Equipment** (`lighting_equipment`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate the condition of the lighting equipment.
* **Windshield and Windows** (`windshield_windows`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate the condition of the windshield and windows.
* **Mirrors** (`mirrors`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate the condition of the mirrors.
* **Wipers** (`wipers`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate the condition of the wipers.
* **Horn** (`horn`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate the condition of the horn.
* **Seat Belts** (`seat_belts`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate the condition of the seat belts.
* **Airbags** (`airbags`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate the status of the airbags.
* **Fuel System** (`fuel_system`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate the condition of the fuel system.
* **Engine Condition** (`engine_condition`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate the overall condition of the engine.
* **Transmission** (`transmission`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate the condition of the transmission.
* **Emissions Test** (`emissions_test`, `select_one`, required): Enter the result of the emissions test.
* **Safety Inspection** (`safety_inspection`, `select_one`, required): Enter the result of the safety inspection.
* **Defects Noted** (`defects_noted`, `text`, optional): List any defects noted during the inspection.
* **Repairs Recommended** (`repairs_recommended`, `text`, optional): Suggest any repairs that are recommended.
* **Estimated Repair Cost** (`estimated_repair_cost`, `number`, optional): Enter the estimated cost of any repairs (if applicable).
* **Overall Result** (`overall_result`, `select_one`, required): Enter the overall result of the inspection (Pass, Fail, or Conditional Pass).
* **Sticker Issued** (`sticker_issued`, `select_one`, required): Enter whether a sticker was issued during the inspection (True or False).
* **Sticker Expiration** (`sticker_expiration`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the inspection sticker expires (if applicable).
* **Next Inspection Due** (`next_inspection_due`, `date`, required): Enter the date the next inspection is due.
* **Inspector Signature** (`inspector_signature`, `text`, required): Type your name to sign the inspection report.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or information.
# Tips
*   Make sure to enter all required fields.
*   If a field is not applicable, enter N/A or leave blank.
*   Use the provided options for the condition of each component.
*   If a condition is not listed, use the most appropriate option.
*   Enter any additional comments if necessary.
