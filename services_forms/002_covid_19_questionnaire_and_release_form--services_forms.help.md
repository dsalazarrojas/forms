# covid-19-questionnaire-and-release-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and track patient information related to COVID-19 vaccination.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form accurately and completely, making sure to include all required fields.
2. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
3. Enter the patient's email address in the "Patient Email" field.
4. Enter the patient's phone number in the "Patient Phone" field.
5. Enter the name of the medical professional who administered the vaccination in the "Medical Professional" field.
6. Enter the date and time of the first vaccination dose in the "Vaccine Dose 1 Date" and "Vaccine Dose 1 Time" fields.
7. Enter the date and time of the second vaccination dose in the "Vaccine Dose 2 Date" and "Vaccine Dose 2 Time" fields.
8. Enter the location where the vaccination was administered in the "Vaccination Site" field.
9. Enter the patient's medical history in the "Medical History" field.
10. Enter any allergies the patient has in the "Allergies" field.
11. Enter any medical conditions the patient has in the "Medical Conditions" field.
12. Enter the patient's current medication in the "Medication" field.
13. Enter the date and time of release from the hospital in the "Release Date" field.
14. Select the reason for release from the "Reason For Release" field.
15. Enter any reason for non-release in the "Reason For Non-Release" field.
16. Sign and date the form in the "Patient Signature" field.
17. Select the physician's name from the "Physician" field.
18. Select the medical institution from the "Medical Institution" field.
19. Sign and date the release in the "Release From" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (Patient Name, text, required): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Patient Email** (Patient Email, email, required): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Patient Phone** (Patient Phone, text, required): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Medical Professional** (Medical Professional, text, required): Enter the name of the medical professional who administered the vaccination.
* **Vaccine Dose 1 Date** (Vaccine Dose 1 Date, date, required): Enter the date of the first vaccination dose.
* **Vaccine Dose 1 Time** (Vaccine Dose 1 Time, time, required): Enter the time of the first vaccination dose.
* **Vaccine Dose 2 Date** (Vaccine Dose 2 Date, date, required): Enter the date of the second vaccination dose.
* **Vaccine Dose 2 Time** (Vaccine Dose 2 Time, time, required): Enter the time of the second vaccination dose.
* **Vaccination Site** (Vaccination Site, text, required): Enter the location where the vaccination was administered.
* **Medical History** (Medical History, text, required): Enter the patient's medical history.
* **Allergies** (Allergies, text, required): Enter any allergies the patient has.
* **Medical Conditions** (Medical Conditions, text, required): Enter any medical conditions the patient has.
* **Medication** (Medication, text, required): Enter the patient's current medication.
* **Release From** (Release From, text, required): Enter the reason for release from the hospital.
* **Signature** (Signature, text, required): Sign and date this form to confirm release from the hospital.
* **Consent** (Consent, select_one, required): Select one of the provided options to confirm consent.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the patient's vaccination.
* **Patient Signature** (Patient Signature, text, required): Sign and date the release form to confirm release.
* **Date** (Date, date, required): Enter the date of release.
* **Time** (Time, time, required): Enter the time of release.
* **Physician** (Physician, select_one, required): Select the name of the physician who administered the vaccination.
* **Medical Institution** (Medical Institution, select_one, required): Select the name of the medical institution where the vaccination was administered.
* **Release Date** (Release Date, date, required): Enter the date of release.
* **Reason For Release** (Reason For Release, select_one, required): Select the reason for release from the provided options.
* **Reason For Non-Release** (Reason For Non-Release, text, required): Enter the reason for non-release.
* **Patient Signature** (Patient Signature, text, required): Sign and date the release form to confirm release.
* **Physician** (Physician, select_one, required): Select the name of the physician who administered the vaccination.
* **Medical Institution** (Medical Institution, select_one, required): Select the name of the medical institution where the vaccination was administered.
* **Release From** (Release From, text, required): Enter the reason for release from the hospital.
* **Date** (Date, date, required): Enter the date of release.
* **Time** (Time, time, required): Enter the time of release.

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If a field is optional, you may leave it blank if not applicable.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the relevant medical professional.
