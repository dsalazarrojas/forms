# Beauty Service Event Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect all the necessary information for a successful beauty service event, ensuring that all parties involved are aware of their responsibilities and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your client's name, email address, and phone number to ensure we can reach you and keep you updated.
2. Select the type of event you are hosting, such as a wedding, birthday party, or corporate event.
3. Provide the event date and start time, so we can plan accordingly.
4. Indicate when you would like the beauty services to start.
5. Specify the number of people receiving services.
6. Select the services needed for each individual, and if applicable, choose a bridal package.
7. Let us know if you would like to schedule a trial session.
8. Provide the distance from our salon to the event location.
9. Confirm parking availability at the venue.
10. Describe the setup space where services will be provided.
11. Specify electrical outlet access.
12. Estimate the total cost for all services.
13. Pay a non-refundable deposit to secure the booking.
14. Confirm that the deposit has been paid.
15. Choose a payment schedule.
16. Acknowledge the cancellation and refund policy, late fee policy, weather or emergency policy, and liability waiver.
17. Finally, review and sign the agreement to confirm your understanding of terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Full Name** (`1`: `client_name`): Enter your legal name as it appears on your ID.
* **Email Address** (`2`: `client_email`): Provide your primary contact email.
* **Phone Number** (`3`: `client_phone`): Enter the best number to reach you.
* **Event Address** (`4`: `client_address`): Enter the full address where the services will be provided.
* **Event Type** (`5`: `event_type`): Select the type of event, such as a wedding, birthday party, or corporate event.
* **Event Date** (`6`: `event_date`): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Start Time** (`7`: `event_start_time`): Indicate the time services should begin.
* **Desired Service Start Time** (`8`: `service_start_time`): Choose when you would like the services to start.
* **Number of People Receiving Services** (`9`: `number_of_people`): Indicate the total number of individuals receiving services.
* **Services Requested** (`10`: `services_requested`): Select all services needed for each individual.
* **Bridal Package Selection** (`11`: `bridal_package`): Select your bridal package if applicable.
* **Trial Session Requested** (`12`: `trial_session`): Indicate if you would like to schedule a trial session.
* **Travel Distance from Salon** (`13`: `travel_distance`): Approximate distance from our salon to the event location.
* **Parking Available at Venue** (`14`: `parking_available`): Confirm if parking is available and if it is free or paid.
* **Setup Space** (`15`: `setup_space`): Describe the space where services will be provided.
* **Electrical Outlets Access** (`16`: `electrical_access`): Specify if electrical outlets are accessible.
* **Total Estimated Cost** (`17`: `total_estimated_cost`): Enter the estimated total cost for all services.
* **Deposit Amount Required** (`18`: `deposit_amount`): Pay a non-refundable deposit to secure the booking.
* **Deposit Paid** (`19`: `deposit_paid`): Confirm that the deposit has been paid.
* **Payment Schedule** (`20`: `payment_schedule`): Choose the payment schedule.
* **Cancellation Policy Agreement** (`21`: `cancellation_policy`): Acknowledge the cancellation and refund policy.
* **Late Fee Policy** (`22`: `late_fee_policy`): Understand and agree to the late fee policy.
* **Weather or Emergency Policy** (`23`: `weather_policy`): Agree to the weather or emergency policy.
* **Liability Waiver** (`24`: `liability_waiver`): Release the service provider from liability.
* **Client Signature Agreement** (`25`: `client_signature`): Sign to confirm your understanding of terms.
* **Agreement Date** (`26`: `agreement_date`): Date of this agreement.

## Tips

* Double-check all fields to ensure accurate information.
* Review the form carefully before signing to understand all terms and agreements.
* If unsure about any field, contact us for clarification.
