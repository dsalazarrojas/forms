# Boat Repair Service Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from customers for boat repair services. Please fill out this form accurately to ensure that our team can provide the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read each field to understand what information is required.
2. Fill out each field accurately and thoroughly.
3. Ensure that you have permission from the boat's owner to submit this form, if required.
4. Double-check that you have not already submitted a similar request.
5. Once you have completed the form, a representative will review your submission and contact you to confirm.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the customer requesting the service.
* **Email Address** (`customer_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (`customer_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number where you can be reached.
* **Address** (`customer_address`, `text`, required): Enter the customer's address for service delivery.
* **City - State - Zip** (`customer_city_state_zip`, `text`, required): Enter the customer's city, state, and zip code.
* **Boat Name** (`boat_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the boat being serviced (if applicable).
* **Boat Manufacturer** (`boat_manufacturer`, `text`, required): Enter the manufacturer of the boat.
* **Boat Model** (`boat_model`, `text`, required): Enter the model number of the boat.
* **Year of Manufacture** (`boat_year`, `number`, required): Enter the year the boat was manufactured.
* **Hull Number** (`boat_hull_number`, `text`, required): Enter the hull number of the boat.
* **Type of Repair Needed** (`service_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the type of repair service needed (e.g., Engine Repair, Electrical System).
* **Detailed Description of Issues** (`detailed_issue_description`, `text`, required): Provide a detailed description of the problems with the boat.
* **Requested Service Date** (`service_date_needed`, `date`, required): Enter the date you would like to have the service completed by.
* **Service Urgency** (`service_urgency`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of urgency for the service (e.g., Emergency - ASAP, Standard - Within 2 weeks).
* **Estimated Service Duration** (`estimated_duration`, `select_one`, required): Select the estimated duration of the service (e.g., Less than 1 day, More than 2 weeks).
* **Maximum Authorization Amount** (`authorization_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the maximum amount you are authorizing for repairs.
* **How Should We Proceed with Estimates** (`approval_process`, `select_one`, required): Select how you would like to proceed with estimates (e.g., Proceed with repairs up to approved amount, Call me for estimates over 500 dollars).
* **Is this Repair Covered by Insurance?** (`insurance_coverage`, `select_one`, required): Select if the repair is covered by insurance (True, False, Not Sure).
* **Insurance Claim Number (if Applicable)** (`insurance_claim_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the insurance claim number if the repair is covered by insurance.
* **Do You Want a Warranty on Repairs?** (`warranty_option`, `select_one`, required): Select if you want a warranty on repairs (Yes - Warranty requested, No warranty needed, Unsure).
* **Preferred Warranty Period** (`warranty_period`, `select_one`, optional): Select the preferred warranty period if you want a warranty (e.g., 30 days, 1 year).
* **Parts Preference** (`parts_preference`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of parts you would like to use (OEM - Original Manufacturer, Aftermarket, Whatever is recommended).
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`contact_preference`, `select_one`, required): Select how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, Text Message).
* **I Accept the Service Agreement Terms** (`terms_acceptance`, `select_one`, required): Select to confirm that you accept the service agreement terms (I Accept, I Do Not Accept).
