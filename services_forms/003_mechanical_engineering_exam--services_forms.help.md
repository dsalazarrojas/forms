# Mechanical Engineering Exam - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mechanical Engineering Exam form is designed to collect information from users about their mechanical engineering exam preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal details, including your first name, last name, and email address.
2. Provide your date of birth and contact phone number (if applicable).
3. Enter your address (if applicable).
4. Choose your preferred mechanical engineering exam options, including questions 1-6.
5. Enter any additional notes or comments in the note field.
6. Click the Submit button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your email address that you check regularly.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number that you prefer to be contacted on.
* **Address**: Enter your physical address where you can be contacted or visited.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your secondary email address that you prefer to be contacted on (if different from your primary email).
* **Contact Phone (2)**: Enter your secondary phone number that you prefer to be contacted on (if different from your primary phone number).
* **Mechanical Engineer Question 1**: Select the exam option that best suits your preference.
* **Mechanical Engineer Question 2**: Select all the exam options that best suit your preference.
* **Mechanical Engineer Question 3**: Select all the exam options that best suit your preference.
* **Mechanical Engineer Question 4**: Select all the exam options that best suit your preference.
* **Mechanical Engineer Question 5**: Select all the exam options that best suit your preference.
* **Mechanical Engineer Question 6**: Select all the exam options that best suit your preference.
* **Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your preference.
* **Submit**: Click the submit button to complete the form and submit your preference.
