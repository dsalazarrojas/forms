# Equipment Pickup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Equipment Pickup Form is designed to collect information about equipment pickup requests from customers. This form helps customers provide necessary details about the equipment they want to pick up, which allows staff to process the request efficiently and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **customer name** in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Select the **storage unit ID** where your equipment is stored.
3. Choose the **date of transfer** when you would like to pick up your equipment.
4. Provide a brief description of the **equipment** you want to pick up.
5. Choose the condition of the equipment, which can be **Normal**, **Poor**, or **Damaged**.
6. Select the type of **transfer**, which can be **Internal Move**, **External Move**, or **Return**.
7. Choose the **staff member** who will handle your equipment pickup.
8. Enter the **equipment location** where the staff can find the equipment.
9. Sign your **customer signature** above the dotted line to confirm your information.
10. Sign your **staff signature** above the dotted line to confirm that you have received the information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (customer_name, text, required): Enter your name as the customer who is requesting the equipment pickup.
* **Storage Unit ID** (storage_unit_id, number, optional): If you have a storage unit ID, enter it here. This is not mandatory.
* **Date of Transfer** (date_of_transfer, date, optional): Choose the date when you would like to pick up your equipment.
* **Equipment Description** (equipment_description, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the equipment you want to pick up.
* **Equipment Condition** (equipment_condition, select_one, optional): Select the condition of the equipment, which can be **Normal**, **Poor**, or **Damaged**.
* **Transfer Type** (transfer_type, select_multiple, optional): Choose the type of transfer, which can be **Internal Move**, **External Move**, or **Return**.
* **Staff Member** (staff_member, select_one, optional): Choose the staff member who will handle your equipment pickup.
* **Equipment Location** (equipment_location, text, optional): Enter the location where the staff can find the equipment.
* **Customer Signature** (customer_signature, note, required): Sign above the dotted line to confirm your information.
* **Staff Signature** (staff_signature, note, required): Sign above the dotted line to confirm that you have received the information.

## Tips
* Ensure all mandatory fields are filled out.
* Double-check your information before signing.
* If you have any questions, contact the staff member handling your equipment pickup.
