# Pizza Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for various purposes, possibly for internal data processing or as a template form. However, it does not appear to be a traditional "Pizza Order Form" as expected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of pizza you wish to order (Field 1: pizza_type)
2. Choose any toppings you'd like to have on your pizza (Field 2: toppings)
3. Enter the quantity of the pizza you'd like to order (Field 3: quantity)
4. Enter your address (Field 4: address)
5. Enter your city (Field 5: city)
6. Enter your phone number (Field 6: phone)
7. Enter your email address (Field 7: email)
8. (Optional) Enter the total cost of the order (Field 8: total_cost)
9. Select the payment gateway for the order (Field 9: payment_gateway)
10. (Optional) Enter any additional comments or notes about your order (Field 10: comment)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Field 1: Pizza Type** (`pizza_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of pizza you would like to order.
* **Field 2: Toppings** (`toppings`, select_multiple, required): Choose any toppings you would like to have on your pizza.
* **Field 3: Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Enter the quantity of pizza you would like to order.

* **Field 6: Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Field 7: Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Field 8: Total Cost** (`total_cost`, number, optional): Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Field 9: Payment Gateway** (`payment_gateway`, select_one, optional): Select the payment gateway for the order.
* **Field 10: Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click this button to submit the form.
* **Field 11: Cancel** (`cancel`, text, required): Click this button to cancel the form submission.
* **Field 12: Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Field 13: Comment** (`comment`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about your order.
* **Field 14: Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any note about your order.
* **Field 15: Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, optional): Select the payment status of the order.
* **Field 16: Status** (`status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the status of the order.
* **Field 17: Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the order.
* **Field 18: Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the order.
* **Field 19: Form Type** (`form_type`, text, optional): Enter the form type.
