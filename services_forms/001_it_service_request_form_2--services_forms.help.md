# IT Service Request Form 2 - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and document IT service requests from customers or users. Please use this form to provide detailed information about the issues you are experiencing so the IT department can efficiently identify and address the problem.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your customer information in the **Customer Information** section.
2.  Describe the **Issue Details** you are experiencing.
3.  Specify the **Service Request** and any additional details about the service you are requesting.
4.  Choose the relevant **Category** for your issue from the provided options.
5.  Enter any **Contact Information** that the IT team may need to contact you.
6.  Optionally, provide an **Explanation** for your request.
7.  Leave any additional **Comments** if you need to add any further details.
8.  Finally, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Information**: This is the section to enter your name, email address, and any other contact details you want to share with the IT department. It's essential for us to know who to contact you with updates or additional questions.
- **Issue Details**: Please describe the issue you are experiencing in detail. Include any error messages, steps leading up to the problem, or relevant screenshots. This will help the IT team understand the problem quickly and efficiently.
- **Service Request**: If you are requesting a specific service, describe it here. If you have already decided on a service, you can enter it here, or if you are unsure, the IT team can help guide you through the process.
- **Category**: Choose the category that best describes the service request. This helps the IT team understand the nature of your request and prioritize it accordingly.
- **Contact Information**: If you want to be contacted via phone, email, or any other method, enter your contact details here.
- **Explanation**: If you have any additional information about your request, please provide it here. This could be context or background information that helps the IT team understand your needs better.
- **Comments**: If there is anything else you'd like to add or discuss regarding your service request, enter it here.

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible when describing your issue.
- Choose the most relevant category for your service request.
- If you have any relevant screenshots or error messages, attach them to the form or send them to the IT department separately.
- If you are unsure about any part of the process, do not hesitate to contact the IT team for guidance.
