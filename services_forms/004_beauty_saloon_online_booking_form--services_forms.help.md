# Beauty Saloon Online Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Beauty Saloon Online Booking Form is designed for customers to book appointments and services at the salon. This form captures essential information to ensure a smooth and personalized experience during the booking process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Submit" button once you've filled out all the required fields.
2. Enter your full name, email address, and phone number in the respective fields.
3. Select the desired service type from the options provided.
4. Add any additional services you'd like to include with the main service.
5. Choose your preferred stylist, if applicable.
6. Select a preferred date and time for the appointment.
7. Indicate whether this is your first visit to the salon.
8. Let us know how you found out about our salon.
9. Share any hair concerns or goals you'd like to address.
10. If you have any allergies or sensitivities, please inform us.
11. Add any special requests or accommodations.
12. Select your preferred reminder method for the appointment.
13. Confirm your understanding of the 24-hour cancellation policy.
14. Choose whether you'd like to receive promotional emails from us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Customer Name, Text, Required): This is your personal name as it appears on official records.
* **Email Address** (Customer Email, Email, Required): We will use this to communicate with you about your appointment and send any important notifications.
* **Phone Number** (Customer Phone, Text, Required): Please enter your contact phone number so we can reach you if needed.
* **Service Type** (Service Type, Select One, Required): Choose the service you'd like to book, such as Haircut and Style or Hair Color - Full.
* **Additional Services** (Additional Services, Select Multiple, Optional): Add any extra services you'd like to include with your main service.
* **Preferred Stylist** (Preferred Stylist, Select One, Required): Choose your preferred stylist or select from the available options.
* **Preferred Date** (Preferred Date, Date, Required): Select a date for your appointment.
* **Preferred Time** (Preferred Time, Time, Required): Choose a time slot for your appointment.
* **Is This Your First Visit** (Is This Your First Visit, Select One, Required): Confirm whether this is your first time visiting the salon.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (How Did You Hear About Us, Select One, Optional): Let us know how you found out about our salon.
* **Hair Concerns or Goals** (Hair Concerns or Goals, Text, Optional): Share any hair concerns or goals you'd like to address during your appointment.
* **Current Hair Color** (Current Hair Color, Select One, Optional): Inform us about your current hair color to help us prepare for your appointment.
* **Hair Length** (Hair Length, Select One, Optional): Tell us about your current hair length.
* **Allergies or Sensitivities** (Allergies or Sensitivities, Text, Optional): If you have any allergies or sensitivities to hair products, please inform us.
* **Special Requests** (Special Requests, Text, Optional): Add any special requests or accommodations you'd like to make during your appointment.
* **Reminder Preference** (Reminder Preference, Select One, Required): Choose how you'd like to receive reminders for your appointment.
* **Cancellation Policy Agreement** (Cancellation Policy Agreement, Select One, Required): Confirm your understanding of the 24-hour cancellation policy.
* **Receive Promotional Emails** (Receive Promotional Emails, Select One, Required): Choose whether you'd like to receive special offers and promotions from us.
