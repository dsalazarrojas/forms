# travel_agent_income_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The travel_agent_income_form is a tool used by travel agencies to collect and track income information related to travel agent activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the source of your income (income_source) from the available options.
2. Enter the amount of income earned (amount) in a numeric format.
3. Choose the payment method used for your income (payment_method) from the available options: Travel or Other.
4. Select the tax withholding rate applicable to your income (tax_withholding) from the options 0-15%, 15-20%, or Other.
5. Add any additional comments or notes about your income (comments) if necessary.
6. Enter your email address (email) for contact purposes.
7. Add your phone number (phone_number) if you'd like to be contacted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **income_source**: This field allows you to select the source of your income, such as commissions, bookings, or other sources of income.
* **amount**: Enter the exact amount of income earned in a numeric format (e.g., "5000.00").
* **payment_method**:
	+ Choose "Travel" if your payment was made through the travel agency for travel-related expenses.
	+ Choose "Other" for any other payment method not listed (e.g., bank transfers, credit card payments).
* **tax_withholding**: Select the tax withholding rate that applies to your income, or choose "Other" if it's not listed. If you choose "Other", please provide more information in the comments field.
* **comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about your income, such as payment details or specific expenses.
* **email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes, so we can get in touch with you if needed.
* **phone_number**: Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted regarding your income information.
