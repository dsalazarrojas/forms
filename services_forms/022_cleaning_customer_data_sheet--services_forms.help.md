# Cleaning Customer Data Sheet - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to collect and record customer information for cleaning services. The goal is to ensure accurate and consistent customer data for efficient service delivery and billing.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information about the customer.
2. Review each section carefully to ensure all required fields are completed.
3. If you need help or have questions, do not hesitate to reach out to our support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer ID** (Text, Required): Enter the customer's internal identifier or account number.
* **Customer Name** (Text, Required): Enter the customer's full name.
* **Company Name** (Text, Not Required): Enter the customer's business name if applicable.
* **Email Address** (Email, Required): Enter the customer's primary email contact.
* **Primary Phone** (Text, Required): Enter the customer's main contact number.
* **Secondary Phone** (Text, Not Required): Enter the customer's alternate contact number.
* **Service Address** (Text, Required): Enter the street address for the cleaning service.
* **City** (Text, Required): Enter the city where the service will be performed.
* **State/Province** (Text, Required): Enter the state or province where the service will be performed.
* **ZIP/Postal Code** (Text, Required): Enter the postal code where the service will be performed.
* **Service Frequency** (Select One, Required): Choose how often the customer needs service (e.g., Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, etc.).
* **Preferred Day of Week** (Select One, Not Required): Choose which day of the week works best for the customer.
* **Preferred Time of Day** (Select One, Not Required): Choose a preferred time of day for the service (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Evening, etc.).
* **Type of Cleaning Service** (Select One, Required): Choose the primary service being requested (e.g., General House Cleaning, Deep Cleaning, etc.).
* **Property Size** (Text, Not Required): Enter the approximate square footage of the property.
* **Number of Rooms** (Number, Not Required): Enter the total number of rooms to be cleaned.
* **Areas to Clean** (Select Multiple, Required): Select the areas of the property to be cleaned (e.g., Kitchen, Bathrooms, Bedrooms, etc.).
* **Cleaning Product Preferences** (Select One, Not Required): Choose the preferred cleaning product type (e.g., Green/Eco-Friendly, Hypoallergenic, etc.).
* **Fragrance Preference** (Select One, Not Required): Choose a preferred fragrance type (e.g., Unscented, Light Scent, etc.).
* **How We Will Access the Property** (Select One, Required): Choose how our team will access the property (e.g., Customer Home, Hidden Key, etc.).
* **Pets in Home** (Select One, Required): Choose if there are pets in the home (e.g., No Pets, Dogs, Cats, etc.).
* **Special Instructions** (Text, Not Required): Enter any special requests or concerns for the service.
* **Billing Contact Name** (Text, Not Required): Enter the person responsible for payment.
* **Billing Email** (Email, Not Required): Enter the email for billing and payment.
* **Customer Since Date** (Date, Not Required): Enter the date service began, if applicable.

## Tips

* Please review and fill out the form carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to our support team for assistance.
