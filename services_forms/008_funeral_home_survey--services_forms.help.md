# Funeral Home Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather your feedback and insights about your experience with our funeral home services. Your input will help us to better understand your needs and improve our services to serve you and other families better.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select all the services you used during your recent experience with our funeral home.
2. Rate your satisfaction with the provided services.
3. If you have any comments or suggestions, please share them with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction (id: 1)** (`title`, `text`, not required): Please provide a brief introduction to your experience with our funeral home. This could be a description of the services you used, the date and time of your visit, or any other relevant details.
* **Services Used (id: 2)** (`services-used`, `select_multiple`, not required): Please select all the services you used during your recent experience with our funeral home. You can choose more than one service, and you'll be able to see all the options below the field.
* **Satisfaction (id: 3)** (`satisfaction`, `select_one`, not required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the services provided by our funeral home. Please select one of the options below.
	+ Satisfied
	+ Dissatisfied
* **Comments (id: 4)** (`comments`, `text`, not required): If you have any comments or suggestions for us, please share them with us. This could be feedback about our services, staff, or any other aspect of your experience.
