# Financial Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect comprehensive financial information about an individual's situation for the purpose of providing personalized financial planning and advice.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully to ensure you understand what is being asked.
2. Answer each question honestly and accurately to the best of your ability.
3. Do not leave any fields blank unless explicitly instructed to do so.
4. Use numerical values for numerical questions.
5. Select the most relevant answer option for select questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Financial Consultation Form** (`financial_consultation_form`, text, not required): Enter your name or a unique identifier to be used for internal reference.
* **What is your current income** (`financial_consultation_form_2`, number, not required): Enter your current income level, or the amount you earn per year.
* **Do you own any property?** (`financial_consultation_form_3`, select_one, not required): Select 'Home', 'True', or 'False' to indicate if you own any property.
* **What is your age?** (`financial_consultation_form_4`, number, not required): Enter your current age.
* **What is your current job?** (`financial_consultation_form_5`, select_multiple, not required): Select one or more of the following: Bank, Financial Advisor, Consultant, Accountant.
* **When do you want to meet?** (`financial_consultation_form_6`, date, not required): Enter the date you would like to meet for the consultation.
* **How much do you earn?** (`financial_consultation_form_7`, number, not required): Enter the amount you earn per year or month.
* **What is your current status?** (`financial_consultation_form_8`, select_one, not required): Select one of the following: Student, Retiree, Single, Married, Engaged.
* **Do you have any children?** (`financial_consultation_form_9`, select_one, not required): Select 'True' or 'False' to indicate if you have any children.
* **How much do you have saved for retirement?** (`financial_consultation_form_10`, number, not required): Enter the amount you have saved for retirement.
* **What is the name of your spouse?** (`financial_consultation_form_11`, text, not required): Enter the name of your spouse or partner.
* **What is the number of dependents?** (`financial_consultation_form_12`, number, not required): Enter the number of dependents you have.
* **How much do you want to save for your children?** (`financial_consultation_form_13`, number, not required): Enter the amount you would like to save for your children.
* **How much do you spend on housing?** (`financial_consultation_form_14`, number, not required): Enter the amount you spend on housing per month or year.
* **How much do you spend on food and transportation?** (`financial_consultation_form_15`, number, not required): Enter the amount you spend on food and transportation per month or year.
* **How much do you spend on entertainment?** (`financial_consultation_form_16`, number, not required): Enter the amount you spend on entertainment per month or year.
* **What is your current credit score?** (`financial_consultation_form_17`, number, not required): Enter your current credit score.
* **Do you have any outstanding loans or debt?** (`financial_consultation_form_18`, select_one, not required): Select 'True' or 'False' to indicate if you have any outstanding loans or debt.
* **What is your current credit card debt?** (`financial_consultation_form_19`, number, not required): Enter the amount of your current credit card debt.
* **What is your current savings?** (`financial_consultation_form_20`, number, not required): Enter the amount you have saved.
* **What is your financial goal?** (`financial_consultation_form_21`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of your financial goal.
* **Do you have any other income sources?** (`financial_consultation_form_22`, select_one, not required): Select 'True' or 'False' to indicate if you have any other income sources.
* **What is the name of your other income source?** (`financial_consultation_form_23`, text, not required): Enter the name of your other income source.
* **How much do you receive from other income source?** (`financial_consultation_form_24`, number, not required): Enter the amount you receive from your other income source.
* **What is the name of your financial advisor?** (`financial_consultation_form_25`, text, not required): Enter the name of your financial advisor, if applicable.

## Tips

* Please answer each question honestly and accurately to provide the most accurate assessment of your financial situation.
* If you are unsure of any question, please enter '0' or select 'Unknown' to indicate that you are unsure.
* If you have any other questions or concerns, please feel free to provide them in the 'What is your financial goal?' field.
