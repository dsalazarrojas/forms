# Table Reservation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to reserve a table at your restaurant. To complete this form, you will need to provide the required information such as date, time, number of guests, special requirements, name, email, phone, and additional notes. The form is divided into sections for each of these fields, making it easy to fill out.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each field in the designated section.
2. Enter your information accurately and completely.
3. Review your form before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date** (`1`, date, required/optional): Please select the date you would like to make a reservation for. This is the date you will be visiting the restaurant.
* **Time** (`2`, time, required/optional): Please select the time you would like to make a reservation for. This is the time you will be visiting the restaurant.
* **Number of Guests** (`3`, number, required/optional): Please enter the number of guests that will be in your party.
* **Special Requirements** (`4`, text, required/optional): If there are any special requests or requirements for your reservation (e.g. "vegetarian", "no smoking area"), please enter them here.
* **Name** (`5`, text, required/optional): Please enter your name.
* **Email** (`6`, email, required/optional): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`7`, text, required/optional): Please enter your phone number.
* **Additional Notes** (`8`, text, required/optional): If there is anything else you would like to let us know about your reservation, please enter it here.
* **Submit** (`9`, text, required/optional): Once you have filled out all the necessary fields, press this button to submit your reservation. 

## Tips

* Make sure to review your form before submitting it to ensure all fields are filled out correctly.
* If you have any questions or need to make changes to your reservation, please contact us at your convenience.
