# Fire Extinguisher Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Fire Extinguisher Checklist is used to track and manage fire extinguisher-related information, including location, type, status, and maintenance history.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the location of the fire extinguisher from the options provided.
2. Choose the type of fire extinguisher from the dropdown menu.
3. Select the status of the fire extinguisher (Active, Inactive, or Unknown).
4. Enter the date of the last inspection.
5. Enter the date of the last service.
6. Select Yes or No for whether the fire extinguisher has been replaced.
7. If the fire extinguisher has been replaced, enter the phone number and email of the person who replaced it, as well as any additional notes.
8. If the fire extinguisher has not been replaced, select Yes or No for whether the fire extinguisher has been inspected recently.
9. If the fire extinguisher has been inspected recently, enter the phone number and email of the inspector, as well as any additional notes.
10. Enter the date of the last location inspection.
11. Select Yes or No for whether the location has been inspected recently.
12. If the location has been inspected recently, enter the phone number and email of the inspector, as well as any additional notes.
