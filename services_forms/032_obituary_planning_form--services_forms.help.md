# Obituary Planning Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a deceased individual for the purpose of creating an obituary.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the form one page at a time, starting with Page 1.
2. Enter the first name of the deceased in the "Deceased First Name" field.
3. Enter the deceased's middle name (if applicable) in the "Deceased Middle Name" field.
4. Enter the last name of the deceased in the "Deceased Last Name" field.
5. Enter the deceased's address in the "Deceased Address" field.
6. Enter the date of death in the "Date of Death" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
7. Enter the place of death in the "Place of Death" field.
8. Enter the city of death in the "City of Death" field.
9. Enter the state of death in the "State of Death" field.
10. Enter the zip code of death in the "Zip Code" field.
11. Enter the country of death in the "Country of Death" field.
12. Enter the cause of death in the "Cause of Death" field.
13. Enter the funeral home information in the "Funeral Home" field.
14. Enter the date and time of the service in the "Date of Service" and "Time of Service" fields.
15. Enter any contact information for the deceased's family or representative in the "Contact" field.
16. Enter the place of the service in the "Place of Service" field.
17. Enter the text for the obituary in the "Obituary Text" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Deceased First Name**: Enter the first name of the deceased person. 
* **Deceased Middle Name**: Enter the middle name of the deceased person (if applicable). 
* **Deceased Last Name**: Enter the last name of the deceased person. 
* **Deceased Address**: Enter the address of the deceased person's residence. 
* **Date of Death**: Enter the date of death in the format MM/DD/YYYY. 
* **Place of Death**: Enter the place where the deceased person passed away. 
* **City of Death**: Enter the city where the deceased person passed away. 
* **State of Death**: Enter the state where the deceased person passed away. 
* **Zip Code**: Enter the zip code of the place where the deceased person passed away. 
* **Country of Death**: Enter the country where the deceased person passed away. 
* **Cause of Death**: Enter a brief description of the circumstances surrounding the deceased person's passing. 
* **Funeral Home**: Enter the name and address of the funeral home handling the services. 
* **Date of Service**: Enter the date of the funeral service. 
* **Time of Service**: Enter the time of the funeral service. 
* **Contact**: Enter the contact information for the deceased's family or representative. 
* **Place of Service**: Enter the location of the funeral service. 
* **Obituary Text**: Enter the text for the obituary. 

## Tips
* Use the format MM/DD/YYYY for dates.
* Use the full name of the deceased person.
* Enter the address in the format "Street Address, City, State, Zip".
