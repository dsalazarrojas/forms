# Creative Services Job Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from businesses or organizations about their custom print or design service requests. It will help our team understand the specifics of the job and provide a clear and efficient way for us to fulfill your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of job you are requesting (print or design).
2. Choose the paper size that matches your requirements.
3. Select the envelope size needed for your job.
4. Choose the ink color for your print or design job.
5. Select the paper weight suitable for your job.
6. Enter a title for your job (e.g., "Brochure for Company X").
7. Provide any additional comments or details about your job.
8. Enter the name of the person or organization requesting the service.
9. Enter the email address of the person or organization responsible for the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Type** (Print, Design, Other): Select the type of service you are requesting. (Required)
* **Paper Size** (8.5 x 11, 8.5 x 14, Letter, Legal, Tabloid, Folio): Choose the size of paper needed for your print or design job.
* **Envelope Size** (A2, A3, A4, A5, A6): Select the size of the envelope needed for your job.
* **Ink Color** (Black, Blue, Red, Green): Choose the ink color for your print or design job.
* **Paper Weight** (Card, Text, Cover, V-Weight, Tabloid): Select the weight of the paper needed for your job.
* **Job Title** (text field): Enter a title for your job to help us identify it.
* **Comments** (text field): Provide any additional comments or details about your job.
* **Contact Name** (text field): Enter the name of the person or organization requesting the service.
* **Contact Email** (email field): Enter the email address of the person or organization responsible for the service.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out the form as accurately as possible to ensure we can provide the correct service.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
* You can contact us at form@gic.mx if you need help with anything else related to your service request.
