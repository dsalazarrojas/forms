# Auto Technician Service Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to capture information from clients for an auto technician service agreement, which is essential for ensuring that the work is done according to their requirements and expectations. It helps to provide a clear understanding of what services are to be performed on their vehicle, any specific instructions or details, and ensures that the client is aware of and agrees to the estimate and cost policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your legal name and contact information in the "Client Full Name" and "Billing Address" fields, respectively.
2. Enter your vehicle's 17-digit Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) in the "Vehicle VIN" field.
3. Provide a description of your vehicle, including its year, make, and model, in the "Vehicle Description" field.
4. In the "Services to be Performed" field, select all services that apply to your vehicle's issues or needs.
5. If you have any specific instructions or details for the technician, provide them in the "Specific Client Instructions" field.
6. Choose a scheduled service appointment date in the "Service Appointment Date" field.
7. Agree to authorize standard diagnostic testing in the "Authorize Standard Diagnostic Testing" field.
8. Acknowledge that additional repairs may require new estimates in the "Agree to Estimate and Cost Policies" field.
9. Consent to sign this agreement digitally in the "Electronic Signature Consent" field.
10. Type your printed name for signature in the "Signature (Printed Name)" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Full Name (2)**: The field is for the client's legal name.
* **Billing Address (3)**: Enter your billing address, which is usually your home or work address.
* **Vehicle VIN (4)**: The 17-digit Vehicle Identification Number is essential for identifying your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Description (5)**: Provide a brief description of your vehicle, including its year, make, and model.
* **Services to be Performed (7)**: Select all services that apply to your vehicle's issues or needs.
* **Specific Client Instructions (8)**: If you have any specific instructions or details for the technician, provide them here.
* **Service Appointment Date (9)**: Choose a scheduled service appointment date.
* **Authorize Standard Diagnostic Testing (11)**: Agree to authorize standard diagnostic testing for troubleshooting.
* **Agree to Estimate and Cost Policies (12)**: Acknowledge that additional repairs may require new estimates.
* **Electronic Signature Consent (13)**: Consent to sign this agreement digitally.
* **Signature (Printed Name) (14)**: Type your printed name for signature.
