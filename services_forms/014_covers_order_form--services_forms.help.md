# covers_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users for placing an order. Users are required to provide personal details, order details, payment method, and additional notes or contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the form to start filling it out.
2. Fill in the required and optional fields as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Information (personal_info)** (`personal_info`, `text`, not required): This field collects personal details from users, such as their name or address.
* **Order Information (order_info)** (`order_info`, `text`, not required): This field is for users to provide more information about their order, such as the ordered item or quantity.
* **Payment Method (payment_method)** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, not required): This field allows users to select a payment method from the provided options.
* **Notes (notes)** (`notes`, `note`, not required): This field is for users to add any additional comments or notes about their order.
* **Email (email)** (`email`, `email`, not required): This field collects the user's email address.
* **Phone (phone)** (`phone`, `text`, not required): This field collects the user's phone number.

## Tips
* Please fill in all required fields to ensure successful submission.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the field's label or the provided help guide for clarification.
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form to avoid errors.
