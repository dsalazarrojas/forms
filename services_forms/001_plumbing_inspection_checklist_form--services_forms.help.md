# plumbing_inspection_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for professionals to document and report findings from a plumbing-related inspection. It is used to gather information about a property's plumbing system, identify potential issues, and schedule repairs or maintenance tasks.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the address of the property being inspected.
2. Provide the customer's name.
3. Enter the contact number for the customer or property owner.
4. Enter the date of the inspection, using the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
5. Enter the time of the inspection, using the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
6. Select the type of inspection being performed (Basement, Foundation, or Roof).
7. Enter any relevant system information.
8. Add any comments or notes about the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Home address** (address_info): Enter the physical address of the property being inspected. This will help the inspector locate the property and provide accurate documentation.
* **Customer Name** (customer_name): Enter the name of the person being informed about the inspection findings.
* **Contact number** (contact_number): Enter the contact number of the customer or property owner for follow-up communication.
* **Date of inspection** (date_of_inspection): Enter the date of the inspection in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Time of inspection** (time_of_inspection): Enter the time of the inspection in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **Type of inspection** (type_of_inspection): Select the type of inspection being performed (Basement, Foundation, or Roof).
* **System information** (system_info): Enter any relevant details about the plumbing system, such as system type or installation date.
* **Comments** (comments): Enter any comments, notes, or observations about the inspection findings.
