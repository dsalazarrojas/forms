## Sample Receipt Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Sample Receipt Form is used to record information about a customer's receipt, including the receipt date, time, customer name, email, phone number, and a note about the receipt.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the receipt date in the "Receipt Date" field.
2. Enter the receipt time in the "Receipt Time" field.
3. Enter the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
4. Enter the customer's email address in the "Customer Email" field.
5. Enter the customer's phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
6. Enter any additional notes about the receipt in the "Receipt Note" field.

### Receipt Date (receipt_date)
* This field is for entering the date of the receipt.
* Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YY or DD/MM/YY, depending on your regional format.
* This field is optional, so you can leave it blank if not applicable.

### Receipt Time (receipt_time)
* This field is for entering the time of the receipt.
* Enter the time in 24-hour format (HH:MM or MM:MM).
* This field is optional, so you can leave it blank if not applicable.

### Customer Name (customer_name)
* This field is for entering the customer's name.

### Customer Phone (customer_phone)
