# vehicle_valuation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vehicle valuation form is a tool designed to collect information about a vehicle for valuation purposes. It is used in a business setting to gather specific details about a vehicle.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the type of vehicle (Sedan, SUV, Truck, or Van).
2. Enter the vehicle model.
3. Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN).
4. Enter the vehicle's mileage.
5. Enter the vehicle's year.
6. Select the vehicle's condition (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
7. Enter the vehicle's make.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **vehicle_type** (`Select One`, required: false): This field asks for the type of vehicle (Sedan, SUV, Truck, or Van). Select the correct type from the options provided.
* **vehicle_model** (`Text`, required: false): Enter the vehicle's model here. This will help identify the vehicle for valuation purposes.
* **vehicle_id** (`Text`, required: false): Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) here. This is a unique number assigned to each vehicle.
* **mileage** (`Number`, required: false): Enter the vehicle's mileage here. This will help determine the vehicle's condition and value.
* **year** (`Number`, required: false): Enter the vehicle's year here. This will help determine the vehicle's age and value.
* **vehicle_condition** (`Select One`, required: false): Select the vehicle's condition from the options provided (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **vehicle_make** (`Text`, required: false): Enter the vehicle's make here. This will help identify the vehicle for valuation purposes.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately to ensure accurate valuation results.
* If a field is not applicable (e.g., the vehicle is a new car), feel free to leave it blank or select "N/A" as necessary.
* If you're unsure about any field, please consult with the relevant expert or contact support for further guidance.
