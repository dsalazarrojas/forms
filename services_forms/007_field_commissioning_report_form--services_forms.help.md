# Field Commissioning Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Field Commissioning Report Form is a tool used to document the commissioning of equipment, ensuring that all necessary information is collected and recorded accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form for each piece of equipment that needs to be commissioned.
2. Enter the output of the equipment (e.g., quantity, unit, and description).
3. Enter the commission date of the equipment.
4. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Equipment Output (text)** (`equipment_output`, optional): Enter a brief description of the equipment's output, such as the quantity, unit, and type of output produced.
* **Process Output (text)** (`process_output`, optional): Enter a brief description of the process being used to commission the equipment, including any relevant details about the process.
* **Commission Date (date)** (`commission_date`, required): Enter the date the equipment was commissioned, in the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely for each piece of equipment.
- Review the form before submitting to ensure all required information is entered.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx) for assistance.
