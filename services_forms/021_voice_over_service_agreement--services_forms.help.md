# Voice Over Service Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to be used for voice over service agreements, capturing essential information about clients and projects. It's a general-purpose template for voice over agencies or individuals providing services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out all the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter client information in the Client Information section (Field 1).
3. Enter project information in the Project Information section (Field 2).
4. Read and agree to the Agreement Terms (Field 3) by typing your acceptance.
5. Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement.
6. Enter your client signature in the Client Signature section (Field 4).
7. Enter the project manager's signature in the Project Manager Signature section (Field 5).
8. Add any additional notes in the Additional Notes section (Field 8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information (Client Information)** (`text`, required: false): This field is for entering basic client information. Please fill in your name, company (if applicable), and other relevant details.
* **Project Information (Project Information)** (`text`, required: false): Enter details about the project, including project title, description, or any other relevant details.
* **Agreement Terms (Agreement Terms)** (`note`, required: false): Read and type your acceptance of the agreement terms. This is a non-mandatory section, but it's crucial that you understand the terms before proceeding.
* **Client Signature (Client Signature)** (`text`, required: false): Sign your name to confirm your agreement.
* **Project Manager Signature (Project Manager Signature)** (`text`, required: false): Sign and date this section to confirm your agreement as the project manager.
* **Date (Date)** (`date`, required: false): Enter the date of the agreement.
* **Time (Time)** (`time`, required: false): Enter the time of the agreement.
* **Additional Notes (Additional Notes)** (`note`, required: false): Add any additional notes about the project or agreement.

## Tips

* Make sure to read and understand the agreement terms before signing the form.
* Ensure that you're signing the form on the intended date and time.
* Review the client and project information for accuracy before proceeding.
* Save a copy of the signed form for reference or record-keeping purposes.
