# Taxi Help Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Taxi Help Form is designed to assist you in submitting service requests or reporting issues related to our taxi services. This form is intended to be a quick and easy way to interact with our team, ensuring we can provide you with the best possible experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form's fields and ensure you have the necessary information ready.
2.  Fill in the required fields, marked as `required` in the form.
3.  Select from the available options for the `Category` field.
4.  Add any additional details in the `Description` field as needed.
5.  Click submit to send your request or report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Request**: Enter a detailed description of your service request or issue you'd like us to address. (text)
* **Report Issue**: If you have a concern or problem with our services, describe it here. (text)
* **Category**: Choose one of the available options that best describes the nature of your request or issue. (Select One: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3)
* **Description**: Provide any additional information about your request or issue. (text)

## Tips
* Please ensure you provide clear and concise information to help us assist you quickly and efficiently.
* If you're unsure about a field or the form process, don't hesitate to reach out to our team for guidance.
* We'll do our best to respond to your request or report within a reasonable timeframe.
