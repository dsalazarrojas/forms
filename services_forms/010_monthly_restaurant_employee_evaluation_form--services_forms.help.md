# Monthly Restaurant Employee Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help restaurant managers evaluate the performance of their employees on a monthly basis. It assesses various aspects of an employee's work, providing an opportunity for managers to provide feedback and guidance to their team members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the employee you wish to evaluate from the drop-down menu or enter their name in the "employee_name" field.
2. Choose the "satisfaction" level for the employee, selecting "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor" from the drop-down menu.
3. Enter the employee's skills and comments in the "skills" field.
4. Enter the employee's attendance rate in the "attendance" field.
5. Select the employee's safety consciousness level, using the drop-down menu.
6. Enter the employee's courtesy and dedication levels, using the text fields.
7. Enter the employee's workload in the "workload" field.
8. Enter the employee's job environment, using the text field.
9. Enter the employee's email, phone number, and address in the corresponding fields.
10. Enter the employee's position.
11. Enter any comments about the employee in the "employee_comments" field (optional).
12. Enter any comments or suggestions from the restaurant manager in the "restaurant_manager_comments" field (optional).
13. Enter any recommendations or suggestions from the restaurant manager in the "restaurant_manager_recommendations" field (optional).
14. Enter the restaurant manager's signature (optional).
15. Select the status of the evaluation as "True" if it is completed or "False" if it is not.
16. Select the evaluation status as "In Progress" if the evaluation is not completed, "Completed" if it is completed, or "Not Started" if it is not started.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_performance**: This field assesses the employee's overall performance. Select the level of performance from the drop-down menu.
* **skills**: Enter the employee's skills and qualifications in this field.
* **attendance**: Enter the employee's attendance rate as a number (e.g., 95 for 95%).
* **customer_service**: This field assesses the employee's customer service level. Select the level from the drop-down menu.
* **safety_consciousness**: This field assesses the employee's safety consciousness. Enter the level as a number (e.g., 9 for 9 out of 10).
* **courtesy**: Enter the employee's courtesy level in this field.
* **dedication**: Enter the employee's dedication level in this field.
* **flexibility**: Enter the employee's flexibility level in this field.
* **workload**: Enter the employee's workload rate as a number (e.g., 8 for 8 out of 10).
* **environment**: Enter the employee's job environment in this field.
* **satisfaction**: This field assesses the employee's overall satisfaction. Select the level from the drop-down menu.
* **date_of_evaluation**: Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **time_of_evaluation**: Enter the time of the evaluation.
* **employee_name**: Enter the employee's full name.
* **employee_email**: Enter the employee's email address.
* **employee_phone**: Enter the employee's phone number.
* **employee_address**: Enter the employee's address.
* **employee_position**: Enter the employee's position.
* **employee_comments**: Enter any comments about the employee (optional).
* **restaurant_manager_comments**: Enter any comments or suggestions from the restaurant manager (optional).
* **restaurant_manager_suggestions**: Enter any recommendations or suggestions from the restaurant manager (optional).
* **restaurant_manager_signature**: Enter the restaurant manager's signature (optional).
* **evaluation_completed**: Select "True" if the evaluation is completed or "False" if it is not.
* **evaluation_status**: Select the evaluation status as "In Progress", "Completed", or "Not Started".
