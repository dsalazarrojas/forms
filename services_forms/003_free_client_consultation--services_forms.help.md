# free_client_consultation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for a free client consultation or meeting. It is intended to collect details from clients or customers for an initial meeting to discuss business, advice, or other services related needs.

## How To Complete This Form
- To begin, select the "Client Information" page.
- Fill in the client's personal details such as their name and contact information in the "Client Information" section.
- Select a suitable "Appointment Date" for the consultation from the calendar provided.
- Choose the "Appointment Time" that fits you best for the consultation.
- In the "Consultant Information" field, enter any necessary information regarding the consultant's availability, expertise, or other relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client Information** (Client Information, Text, Optional): This section collects personal details of the client such as their name, and contact information to be used in subsequent communications.
*   **Appointment Date** (Appointment Date, Date, Optional): Select a date for the consultation that suits you and your schedule.
*   **Appointment Time** (Appointment Time, Time, Optional): Choose the time of the consultation that best fits your schedule.
*   **Consultant Information** (Consultant Information, Text, Optional): This field allows you to note any specific information regarding the consultant’s expertise or availability for the consultation.
