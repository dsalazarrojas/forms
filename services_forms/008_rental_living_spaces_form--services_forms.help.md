# Rental Living Spaces Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect relevant information from individuals to provide tailored services or recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please enter your **First Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the second field.
3. Enter your **Email Address** in the third field. Make sure to use a valid email address that you can access.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the fourth field.
5. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the date field, following the format mm/dd/yyyy.
6. Select your **Preferred Language** from the dropdown list.
7. Choose your **Interests** from the list of available options.
8. If applicable, provide additional information or notes in the **Additional Info** field.
9. You may also provide notes in the **Option1-16** fields as per your preference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your ID or official documents.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your ID or official documents.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter a valid email address that you can access.
* **Phone** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number as it appears on your ID or official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Required): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Preferred Language** (Select One, Required): Choose the language you prefer to communicate in.
* **Interests** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose all the interests that apply to you.
* **Additional Info** (Note, Optional): Provide any additional information that might be helpful.
* **Option1-16** (Note, Optional): These fields are for additional notes or information, if applicable.
