# Client Intake 2 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Intake 2 form is designed to gather vital information from parents or guardians to help the behavioral consulting process. This form covers a range of questions covering the child's behavior, academic performance, medical conditions, and family dynamics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Client Intake 2 form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer all the questions accurately and honestly.
2. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*) symbol.
3. Ensure that all fields are properly filled before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Child First Name** (<name> `child_first_name`, <type> `text`, required/optional: *): Please enter the child's first name.
* **Child Last Name** (<name> `child_last_name`, <type> `text`, required/optional: *): Please enter the child's last name.
* **Child Date of Birth** (<name> `child_date_of_birth`, <type> `date`, required/optional: *): Please enter the child's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Child Gender** (<name> `child_gender`, <type> `select_one`, required/optional: *): Please select the child's gender. Options include:
	+ Male
	+ Female
	+ Non-Binary
	+ Prefer Not to Say
	+ Other
* **Parent or Guardian First Name** (<name> `parent_guardian_first_name`, <type> `text`, required/optional: *): Please enter your first name.
* **Parent or Guardian Last Name** (<name> `parent_guardian_last_name`, <type> `text`, required/optional: *): Please enter your last name.
* **Parent or Guardian Email** (<name> `parent_guardian_email`, <type> `email`, required/optional: *): Please enter your email address.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone** (<name> `parent_guardian_phone`, <type> `text`, required/optional: *): Please enter your phone number.
* **Home Address** (<name> `home_address`, <type> `text`, required/optional: *): Please enter the home address, including the street address, city, and state.
* **City** (<name> `home_city`, <type> `text`, required/optional: *): Please enter the city of the home address.
* **State** (<name> `home_state`, <type> `text`, required/optional: *): Please enter the state of the home address.
* **ZIP Code** (<name> `home_zip`, <type> `text`, required/optional: *): Please enter the ZIP code of the home address.
* **Who Lives in the Household?** (<name> `household_members`, <type> `text`, required/optional: *): Please list all members of the household.
* **Family Structure** (<name> `family_structure`, <type> `select_one`, required/optional: *): Please select the family structure. Options include:
	+ Two Parent Home
	+ Single Parent Home
	+ Blended Family
	+ Extended Family
	+ Foster Care
	+ Other
* **School Name** (<name> `school_name`, <type> `text`, required/optional: *): Please enter the current school name.
* **Grade Level** (<name> `grade_level`, <type> `text`, required/optional: *): Please enter the current grade level of the child.
* **Academic Performance** (<name> `academic_performance`, <type> `select_one`, required/optional: *): Please select the academic performance. Options include:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Below Average
	+ Struggling
* **Does Child Receive Special Education Services?** (<name> `special_education_services`, <type> `select_one`, required/optional: *): Please select if the child receives special education services. Options include:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Not Sure
* **Learning Disabilities or Challenges** (<name> `learning_disabilities`, <type> `text`, required/optional: *): Please enter any learning disabilities or challenges.
* **Medical Conditions** (<name> `medical_conditions`, <type> `text`, required/optional: *): Please list any diagnosed medical conditions.
* **Current Medications** (<name> `current_medications`, <type> `text`, required/optional: *): Please list any current medications, including dosages.
* **Allergies** (<name> `allergies`, <type> `text`, required/optional: *): Please list any allergies, including food, medication, or environmental.
* **Primary Healthcare Provider Name** (<name> `healthcare_provider_name`, <type> `text`, required/optional: *): Please enter the name of the primary healthcare provider.
* **Primary Healthcare Provider Phone** (<name> `healthcare_provider_phone`, <type> `text`, required/optional: *): Please enter the contact number of the primary healthcare provider.
* **Primary Behavior Problems** (<name> `behavior_problems`, <type> `text`, required/optional: *): Please describe the primary behavior problems.
* **How Often Do Behavior Problems Occur?** (<name> `behavior_frequency`, <type> `select_one`, required/optional: *): Please select the frequency of behavior problems. Options include:
	+ Daily
	+ Several Times Per Week
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Rarely
* **What Triggers the Behavior Problems?** (<name> `behavior_triggers`, <type> `text`, required/optional: *): Please describe what triggers the behavior problems, if known.
* **Intensity of Behavior Problems** (<name> `behavior_intensity`, <type> `select_one`, required/optional: *): Please select the intensity of behavior problems. Options include:
	+ Mild
	+ Moderate
	+ Severe
	+ Very Severe
* **Previous Behavioral Interventions** (<name> `previous_interventions`, <type> `text`, required/optional: *): Please describe any previous behavioral interventions.
* **Were Previous Interventions Effective?** (<name> `intervention_effectiveness`, <type> `select_one`, required/optional: *): Please select the effectiveness of previous interventions. Options include:
	+ Very Effective
	+ Somewhat Effective
	+ Not Effective
	+ Not Applicable
* **Child Strengths and Interests** (<name> `child_strengths`, <type> `text`, required/optional: *): Please describe the child's strengths and interests.
* **Primary Parent Concerns** (<name> `parent_concerns`, <type> `text`, required/optional: *): Please describe your primary concerns as a parent.
* **What Are Your Goals for Behavioral Consulting?** (<name> `family_goals`, <type> `text`, required/optional: *): Please describe your goals for the behavioral consulting process.
* **Family Support System** (<name> `support_system`, <type> `text`, required/optional: *): Please describe any support system available to you.
* **Insurance Provider** (<name> `insurance_provider`, <type> `text`, required/optional: *): Please enter the insurance provider name.
* **Insurance Policy Number** (<name> `insurance_policy_number`, <type> `text`, required/optional: *): Please enter the insurance policy number.
* **How Did You Hear About Us?** (<name> `referral_source`, <type> `select_one`, required/optional: *): Please select the referral source. Options include:
	+ Physician Referral
	+ School Referral
	+ Friend or Family
	+ Website
	+ Social Media
	+ Other
* **I Consent to Behavioral Consulting Services** (<name> `consent_to_services`, <type> `select_one`, required/optional: *): Please select if you consent to behavioral consulting services. Options include:
	+ I Consent
	+ I Do Not Consent
* **I Confirm All Information Is Accurate** (<name> `information_accuracy`, <type> `select_one`, required/optional: *): Please select if you confirm all information is accurate. Options include:
	+ I Confirm
	+ I Do Not Confirm
* **Intake Date** (<name> `intake_date`, <type> `date`, required/optional: *): Please enter the date for the intake form.
* **Parent or Guardian Electronic Signature** (<name> `parent_guardian_signature`, <type> `text`, required/optional: *): Please enter your electronic signature. Please type your full name.
