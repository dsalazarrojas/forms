## Flight Reservation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from passengers for flight reservations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the passenger's name in the Passenger Name field.
2. Enter the flight number in the Flight Number field.
3. Select the travel date in the Travel Date field.
4. Select the travel time in the Travel Time field.
5. Choose the flight class in the Flight Class field.
6. Enter the seat number in the Seat Number field.
7. Enter the contact phone number in the Contact Phone field.
8. Enter the contact email in the Contact Email field.
9. Add any additional notes in the Additional Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Passenger Name** (`passenger_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the passenger's name.
* **Flight Number** (`flight_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the flight number.
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, date, required/optional): Select the travel date.
* **Travel Time** (`travel_time`, time, required/optional): Select the travel time.
* **Flight Class** (`flight_class`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the flight class (Yes/No).
* **Seat Number** (`seat_number`, number, required/optional): Enter the seat number.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your contact email.
* **Additional Notes** (`note`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes.
