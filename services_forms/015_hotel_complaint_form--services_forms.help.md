# Hotel Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Complaint Form is designed to help hotel guests report their complaints and issues encountered during their stay. This form collects essential details to understand and address your concerns efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field with the required information as prompted.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3. Double-check your details for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hotel Name**: Enter the name of the hotel where you encountered the issue or concern.
* **Guest Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification document for hotel registration purposes.
* **Complaint Details**: A detailed description of your complaint or issue.
* **Date of Visit**: Enter the date of your visit to the hotel.
* **Time of Visit**: Enter the time you visited the hotel.
* **Room Number**: Enter the room number where you stayed.
* **Contact Number**: Enter a contact number (phone or email) where we can reach you for further information or to discuss your complaint.
* **Email Address**: Enter an email address where we can communicate with you.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information about your complaint.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out each field carefully to ensure your complaint is processed efficiently.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, contact the hotel directly.
- The hotel will review and address your complaint in a timely manner.
