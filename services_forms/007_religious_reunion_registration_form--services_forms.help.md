# Religious Reunion Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Religious Reunion Registration Form is designed to collect information from individuals participating in a religious reunion. This form helps the organizers keep track of attendees and their details.
## How To Complete This Form
1. To get started, click on the "Submit" button, and the form will be displayed with the fields you need to fill out.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information for each field.
3. Make sure to provide a valid email address and phone number if available.
4. Choose your RSVP response carefully, as it will help the organizers plan the reunion accordingly.
5. Select a date and time for the reunion if applicable.
6. Once you have completed the form, click "Submit" to submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Please fill out your first name in this field.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Fill out your last name in this field.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address to enable communication from the organizers.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): If you have a phone number, provide it to stay in touch with fellow attendees.
* **RSVP** (`rsvp`, select_one, required/optional): Choose "True" if you can attend, "False" if you cannot, or "Maybe" if unsure.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Select a date for the reunion, if it's not already specified.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Choose a time slot if you're able to attend during a specific time frame.

## Tips
* Be honest when selecting your RSVP response, as it will impact the organizers' planning.
* Make sure to double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask the organizers for clarification.
