# Service Agreement Template - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Service Agreement Template form is used to create and document service agreements for clients. This template captures essential details about the agreement, including client information, service terms, and payment conditions.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out the Service Agreement Template form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client's name in the **Client Name** field.
2. Enter the service contract details in the **Service Contract** field.
3. Provide the agreement terms in the **Agreement Terms** field.
4. Set the start and end dates for the service agreement in the **Service Agreement Start Date** and **Service Agreement End Date** fields, respectively.
5. Enter the service contract value in the **Service Contract Value** field.
6. Specify the payment terms, such as whether the payment is a **Holiday**, **Regular**, or **On Demand** arrangement.
7. Select the status of the service agreement, which can be **Active**, **Pending**, or **Cancelled**.
8. Add any additional notes about the agreement in the **Service Agreement Notes** field.
9. Enter the client's email address in the **Client Email** field.
10. Enter the client's phone number in the **Client Phone** field.
11. Fill in the client's address in the **Client Address** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Agreement Template Form**: This is the main section of the form where you can enter the service agreement details.
* **Client Name**: Enter the client's name, as required to identify the customer.
* **Service Contract**: Enter the service contract details, including any specific terms or conditions.
* **Agreement Terms**: Enter the agreement terms, which should be clear and concise.
* **Service Agreement Start Date**: Set the start date for the service agreement, which is the date when the service begins.
* **Service Agreement End Date**: Set the end date for the service agreement, which is the date when the service ends.
* **Service Contract Value**: Enter the value of the service contract, which is the total cost or value of the agreement.
* **Payment Terms**: Specify how payments are made, which can be a **Holiday**, **Regular**, or **On Demand** arrangement.
* **Service Agreement Status**: Select the status of the service agreement, which can be **Active**, **Pending**, or **Cancelled**.
* **Service Agreement Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the agreement.
* **Client Email**: Enter the client's email address.
* **Client Phone**: Enter the client's phone number.
* **Client Address**: Enter the client's address.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate and complete service agreement data.
* Review and update the agreement terms and payment conditions regularly to reflect any changes.
* Use this form to track and manage client service agreements efficiently.
