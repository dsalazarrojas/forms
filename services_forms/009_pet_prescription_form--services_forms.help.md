# Pet Prescription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Prescription Form is designed to collect essential information for pet prescriptions. This form is intended for pet owners and veterinarians to input data about pet medication. It will help them to create, manage, and track prescriptions for pets accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Pet Prescription Form, follow these steps:

1.  **Enter the Pet Name**: Type the name of the pet, and provide any additional details about the pet.
2.  **Enter the Owner Name**: Type the name of the pet owner, and provide any additional details about the owner.
3.  **Select Pet Species**: Choose the species of the pet from the dropdown menu.
4.  **Enter Prescription**: Provide the prescription details for the pet.
5.  **Enter Dosage Instructions**: Add any additional instructions for the dosage of the prescription.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Name** (pet\_name, text, required): Enter the name of the pet.
* **Owner Name** (owner\_name, text, required): Enter the name of the pet's owner.
* **Pet Species** (pet\_species, select\_one, required): Choose the species of the pet from the dropdown menu.
* **Prescription** (prescription, text, required): Enter the prescription details for the pet, including any specific medication and dosage information.
* **Dosage Instructions** (dosage\_instructions, text, optional): Add any additional instructions for the dosage of the prescription.

## Tips
- If you're unsure about any information, please consult your veterinarian.
- Be as specific as possible about the prescription details.
- If the pet species is not listed in the dropdown menu, select "Other" and type the species of the pet.
