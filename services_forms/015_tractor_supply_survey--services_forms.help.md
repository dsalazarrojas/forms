# Tractor Supply Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your tractor supply store experience. It helps us understand your perceptions of the store's name, location, merchandise quality, and services. Your feedback is valuable to us and will help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Fill out all the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*) symbol.
* Choose the correct options for each question.
* Provide any additional comments or suggestions in the text fields.

* **Store Name** (1, text, required): Enter the name of the store you visited.
* **Store Address** (2, text, required): Enter the address of the store you visited.
* **Merchandise Quality** (3, select_multiple, required): Choose the quality of the merchandise you purchased from the store:
	+ Good
	+ Poor

	+ Yes
	+ No

	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Store Services** (6, select_one, required): Did the store provide:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Store Facilities** (7, select_multiple, required): Did the store offer:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Store Atmosphere** (8, select_one, required): Did you find the store's atmosphere:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Store Staff** (9, select_one, required): Were the store staff:
	+ Yes

	* Format: HH:MM (e.g., 09:00)
