# Electrical Commissioning Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Electrical Commissioning Checklist" form is designed to gather information on the electrical commissioning process, including testing and instrumentation, acceptance criteria, and other relevant details. This form is intended for use by personnel or teams responsible for the electrical commissioning process to ensure that it is conducted correctly and consistently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to understand the purpose and requirements of each field.
2. Ensure that all fields are filled out correctly and consistently.
3. Enter relevant information for each field as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **page_1_label**: This is a header or title page with no specific information to enter. Please leave this page blank.
* **main_system_description**: Describe the main system or equipment being commissioned. This field is for a brief text description.
* **commissioning_scope**: Provide a brief description of the commissioning scope or objectives.
* **commissioning_process**: Describe the commissioning process or steps taken.
* **test_and_instrumentation**: Enter a brief description of the testing and instrumentation process or methods used.
* **testing_and_test_programs**: Enter information on the testing and test programs used during the commissioning process.
* **acceptance_testing**: Describe the acceptance testing process or criteria.
* **test_and_instrumentation_1**: Indicate whether testing and instrumentation were performed ("Yes" or "No").
* **test_and_instrumentation_2**: Select all relevant testing and instrumentation methods used during the commissioning process ("Yes", "No").
* **acceptance_testing_1**: Indicate whether the acceptance testing criteria were met ("Yes" or "No").
* **acceptance_testing_2**: Select all acceptance testing methods used during the commissioning process ("Yes", "No").
* **acceptance_testing_3**: Enter the date of acceptance testing completion.
* **acceptance_testing_4**: Enter the time of acceptance testing completion.
* **acceptance_testing_5**: Enter any additional notes or comments on the acceptance testing process.
* **electrical_equipment_testing**: Enter a brief description of the electrical equipment testing process.
* **electrical_equipment_testing_1**: Indicate whether electrical equipment testing was performed ("Yes" or "No").
* **electrical_equipment_testing_2**: Select all electrical equipment testing methods used during the commissioning process ("Yes", "No").
* **electrical_equipment_testing_3**: Enter the number of electrical equipment tested.
* **electrical_equipment_testing_4**: Indicate whether the electrical equipment testing criteria were met ("Yes" or "No").
* **electrical_equipment_testing_5**: Select all electrical equipment testing methods used during the commissioning process ("Yes", "No").
* **electrical_equipment_testing_6**: Enter the date of electrical equipment testing completion.
* **electrical_equipment_testing_7**: Enter the time of electrical equipment testing completion.
* **electrical_equipment_testing_8**: Enter any additional notes or comments on the electrical equipment testing process.
* **acceptance_criteria**: Enter any acceptance criteria for the electrical commissioning process.
