### Virtual Baby Shower_ Client Agreement Form (With Deposit) - Help Guide
### Purpose
The Virtual Baby Shower Client Agreement Form is used to collect necessary information from clients regarding their baby shower event. This includes their contact details, deposit information, and service preferences.

### How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information, making sure to provide a valid email address.
2. Select the services you would like to book for your baby shower event.
3. Provide your payment details, including deposit information.
4. Confirm your payment method.
5. Fill in the required payment information such as bank name, card number, card expiration, CVV, PIN, zip, CVC, and card holder name.

### Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, `required`): Please enter the client's first name.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, `required`): Please enter the client's last name.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, `optional`): Enter a valid email address for the client. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`4`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Address** (`5`, `text`, `required`): Enter the client's address.
* **Deposit Details** (`6`, `text`, `required`): Enter the deposit information.
* **Payment Terms** (`7`, `note`, `optional`): Add any payment terms or conditions for the booking.
* **Services** (`8`, `select_multiple`, `optional`): Select the services you would like to book for your baby shower event.
* **Date Of Service** (`9`, `date`, `required`): Select the date for the service.
* **Time Of Service** (`10`, `time`, `required`): Select the time for the service.
* **Time Of Payment** (`11`, `time`, `required`): Select the time for the payment.
* **Total Cost** (`12`, `number`, `required`): Enter the total cost of the service.
* **Payment Confirmation** (`13`, `select_one`, `optional`): Confirm your payment method.
* **Payment Method** (`14`, `select_one`, `optional`): Select the method of payment (Bank, Credit, PayPal).
* **Bank Name** (`15`, `text`, `required`): Enter the bank name.
* **Card Number** (`16`, `text`, `required`): Enter the card number.
* **Card Expiration** (`17`, `text`, `required`): Enter the card expiration date.
* **CVV** (`18`, `text`, `required`): Enter the CVV (Card Verification Value).
* **PIN** (`19`, `text`, `required`): Enter the PIN (Personal Identification Number).
* **Zip** (`20`, `text`, `required`): Enter the zip code.
* **CVC** (`21`, `text`, `required`): Enter the CVC (Card Verification Code).
* **CVC Expiration** (`22`, `text`, `required`): Enter the CVC expiration date.
* **Card Holder** (`23`, `text`, `required`): Enter the card holder's name.
* **Address 2** (`24`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the second address for the service.
* **Comments** (`25`, `note`, `optional`): Add any comments about the service.
