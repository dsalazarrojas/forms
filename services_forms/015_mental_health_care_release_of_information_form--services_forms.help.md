# mental_health_care_release_of_information_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The mental health care release of information form is used to obtain a patient's consent for the disclosure of their personal health information to a third party.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand the purpose and requirements.
2. Select the correct answer for each field, as indicated below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and other identifying information.
* **Release Of Information** (`release_of_information`, `select_one`, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you are authorized to receive the patient's information, 'No' if you are not.
* **Authorization** (`authorization`, `select_one`, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you are authorized to receive the patient's information on their behalf, 'No' if you are not.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select any additional contact information you have for the authorized person.
* **Release Method** (`release_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you will be sending the patient's information via email or mail, 'No' if you will be contacting the patient directly.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you signed this form.
* **Time Signed** (`time_signed`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you signed this form.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `note`, required: false): Sign your name as an authorized person.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of your signature.
* **Signature Time** (`signature_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of your signature.
* **Authorized Person** (`authorized_person`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name and title of the authorized person.
