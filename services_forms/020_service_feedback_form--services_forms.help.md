# Service Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from users about the services they have received. Please take a moment to share your thoughts, suggestions, and opinions about the quality of service you experienced.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide your feedback, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your comments in the **Comments** field. This is the main area where you can share your thoughts and suggestions about our service.
2. Rate our service by selecting a number between 1-5 in the **Rating** field. This will help us understand your overall satisfaction with our service.
3. If you'd like to add any additional feedback or comments, please use the **Feedback** field. This is an optional field where you can share more details about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter your thoughts, suggestions, or opinions about our service.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Select a number between 1-5 to rate our service. A higher number indicates higher satisfaction.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share any additional feedback or comments about your experience.
