# Cemetery Plot Transfer Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Cemetery Plot Transfer Form is designed to assist in transferring ownership of a cemetery plot from one person to another. This process requires accurate and complete information from both the current and new owners.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Current Owner Details
Enter the current owner's details in the following fields:
*   **Current Owner Full Name**: Enter the full name of the current owner.
*   **Current Owner Address**: Enter the full mailing address of the current owner.
*   **Current Owner Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the current owner.
*   **Current Owner Email**: Enter the email address of the current owner.

### Step 2: Plot Identification
Enter the plot's identification details:
*   **Cemetery Name**: Enter the name of the cemetery where the plot is located.
*   **Section - Lot - Plot Number**: Enter the exact location of the plot as per the deed.

### Step 3: New Owner Details
Enter the new owner's details:
*   **New Owner Full Name**: Enter the full name of the new owner.
*   **New Owner Address**: Enter the full mailing address of the new owner.
*   **New Owner Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the new owner.
*   **New Owner Email**: Enter the email address of the new owner.

### Step 4: Relationship Between Owners
Select the relationship between the current and new owners:
*   **Parent - Child**
*   **Siblings**
*   **Spousal**
*   **Heir**
*   **None - Private Sale**
*   **Other**

### Step 5: Reason for Transfer
Select the primary reason for the transfer:
*   **Gift**
*   **Inherited**
*   **Private Sale**
*   **Relocation**
*   **Other**

### Step 6: Authorization and Confirmation
Confirm the transfer:
*   **Do you authorize this transfer**: Select "Yes - I authorize this transfer" to confirm the transfer.
*   **No - I am just inquiring**: Select this if the transfer is not authorized.

### Step 7: Transfer Request Date
Enter the date of the transfer request:
*   Enter the date of the transfer request in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Step 8: Additional Information or Special Instructions
Provide any additional information or special instructions for the transfer:
*   Enter any other details that may be relevant for the transfer.

Note: Ensure that all fields with an asterisk (\*) are mandatory and must be completed for the form to be submitted successfully.
