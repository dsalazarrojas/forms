# rtt_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The rtt_intake_form is a patient intake form designed to collect essential information from patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Patient Information** page.
   - Enter your name, address, and any other relevant details as required.
   - Click "Next" to proceed to the next page.

2. On the **Contact Info** page, enter your contact information.
   - This includes your phone number and any other contact details as required.
   - Click "Next" to proceed to the next page.

3. On the **Insurance Info** page, enter your insurance information.
   - This includes your insurance provider and policy number as required.
   - Click "Next" to proceed to the next page.

4. On the **Medical History** page, describe your medical history.
   - This includes any relevant medical conditions, allergies, or previous medical procedures.
   - Click "Next" to proceed to the next page.

5. On the **Therapy Goals** page, specify your therapy goals.
   - Describe what you hope to achieve through therapy.
   - Click "Next" to proceed to the next page.

6. On the **Treatment Plan** page, create a treatment plan.
   - Outline your goals, strategies, and any specific requirements.
   - Click "Submit" to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information (text)**: Enter your patient information, including name, address, and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Info (text)**: Enter your contact information, including phone number and any other contact details.
* **Insurance Info (text)**: Enter your insurance information, including insurance provider and policy number.
* **Medical History (text)**: Describe your medical history, including any relevant medical conditions, allergies, or previous medical procedures.
* **Therapy Goals (text)**: Specify your therapy goals, including what you hope to achieve through therapy.
* **Treatment Plan (text)**: Create a treatment plan, outlining your goals, strategies, and any specific requirements.
