# receiving_temperature_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Receiving Temperature Form is used to record and report receiving temperatures from various sources, including multiple readings and their units. This form is typically used for temperature readings, but not for other types of data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form on the specified page: <insert page number> (e.g., <insert page number>).
2. Enter the temperature value in the "temperature" field. This field is a number field where you can input a numerical value.
3. Select the unit of measurement for the temperature in the "unit" field. This field is a select-one field with options for Celsius, Fahrenheit, and Kelvin.
4. Enter up to 10 temperature readings in the "reading 1" through "reading 10" fields. These fields are number fields where you can input numerical values.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **temperature** (temperature): Enter the temperature value.
* **unit** (unit): Select the unit of measurement for the temperature.
* **reading 1** (reading_1): Enter the first temperature reading.
* **reading 2** (reading_2): Enter the second temperature reading.
* **reading 3** (reading_3): Enter the third temperature reading.
* **reading 4** (reading_4): Enter the fourth temperature reading.
* **reading 5** (reading_5): Enter the fifth temperature reading.
* **reading 6** (reading_6): Enter the sixth temperature reading.
* **reading 7** (reading_7): Enter the seventh temperature reading.
* **reading 8** (reading_8): Enter the eighth temperature reading.
* **reading 9** (reading_9): Enter the ninth temperature reading.
* **reading 10** (reading_10): Enter the tenth temperature reading.

Note: This form is only for temperature readings and you should not use it for other types of data.
