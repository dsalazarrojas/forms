# Online Color Match Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Color Match Form is designed to help users match their personality to a specific color palette.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the "What is your first name?" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "What is your last name?" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "What is your email address?" field.
4. Select your favorite color from the options provided in the "What is your favorite color?" field.
5. Select your second favorite color from the options provided in the "What is your second favorite color?" field. (This field is optional.)
6. Select your third favorite color from the options provided in the "What is your third favorite color?" field. (This field is optional.)
7. Select your fourth favorite color from the options provided in the "What is your fourth favorite color?" field. (This field is optional.)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your first name?**: This field is a text input field where you can enter your first name.
* **What is your last name?**: This field is also a text input field where you can enter your last name.
* **What is your email address?**: This field is an email input field where you can enter your email address.
* **What is your favorite color?**: This field is a dropdown menu where you can select one color from the options: Red, Blue, Green, Yellow, Purple, or Other.
* **What is your second favorite color?** (Optional): This field is also a dropdown menu where you can select one color from the options: Red, Blue, Green, Yellow, Purple, or Other.
* **What is your third favorite color?** (Optional): This field is a dropdown menu where you can select one color from the options: Red, Blue, Green, Yellow, Purple, or Other.
* **What is your fourth favorite color?** (Optional): This field is a dropdown menu where you can select one color from the options: Red, Blue, Green, Yellow, Purple, or Other.

## Tips
* Please enter your actual email address to receive any further information related to the color palette match.
* If you have trouble selecting a color, please try to choose the one that best represents your personality.
* You can select multiple colors if you have multiple favorite colors.
* If you don't have a favorite color, you can select "Other" in the color dropdown menu.
