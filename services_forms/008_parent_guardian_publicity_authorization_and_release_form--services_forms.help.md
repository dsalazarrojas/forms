# Parent Guardian Publicity Authorization And Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to inform and obtain your consent for the use of your child's image, name, and likeness in various ways, including but not limited to, media, events, and projects. It's essential to review each field carefully to ensure you understand how your child's information will be used.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully to understand its purpose and how it relates to your child's publicity rights.
2. For fields with a "Other" option, select "Yes" to indicate that the information provided does not apply, and then enter a detailed description of the use or media type in the adjacent field.

* **Publicity Rights** (`publicity_rights`, Select Multiple, **Optional**): Select all the ways in which you are willing for your child's image, name, and likeness to be used (e.g., media, events, projects).
