# Snow Plow Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Snow Plow Inspection Checklist is a form used to gather information for snow plow inspections. It is essential to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure the equipment is functioning properly and is in good condition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review the form to understand the types of questions asked and what information is required.
2. Answer each question accurately and thoroughly.
3. Fill out the form section by section, making sure to complete all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Section 1 (main)
* **main** (`main`, `text`, required: false): This is the main section of the form where you can enter any additional information.
* **section_1** (`section_1`, `select_one`, required: true): Select one of the options provided (a, b, or c) to indicate the status of the snow plow.
* **section_2** (`section_2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select multiple options (d, e, or f) to indicate any issues with the snow plow.
* **section_3** (`section_3`, `number`, required: true): Enter a number value.
* **section_4** (`section_4`, `time`, required: true): Enter a time value.
* **notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes.

### Section 2 (phone number, email, comments)
* **phone_number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: true): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **email** (`email`, `email`, required: true): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes.

### Section 3 (assigned tool)
* **assigned_tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one of the options provided (chatjimmy or tool1) to indicate the assigned tool.

### Section 4 (section_5, section_6, section_7)
* **section_5** (`section_5`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional information in this text field.
* **section_6** (`section_6`, `date`, required: false): Enter a date value.
* **section_7** (`section_7`, `number`, required: false): Enter a number value.

### Section 5 (section_8, section_9, section_10)
* **section_8** (`section_8`, `text`, required: true): Enter any additional information in this text field.
* **section_9** (`section_9`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select multiple options (g, h, or i) to indicate any issues with the snow plow.
* **section_10** (`section_10`, `select_one`, required: true): Select one of the options provided (j, k, or l) to indicate the status of the snow plow.

### Section 6 (section_11, section_12, section_13, section_14, section_15, section_16, section_17, section_18, section_19)
* **section_11** (`section_11`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one of the options provided (m, n, or o) to indicate any issues with the snow plow.
* **section_12** (`section_12`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional information in this text field.
* **section_13** (`section_13`, `number`, required: false): Enter a number value.
* **section_14** (`section_14`, `time`, required: true): Enter a time value.
* **section_15** (`section_15`, `number`, required: true): Enter a number value.
* **section_16** (`section_16`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select multiple options (p, q, or r) to indicate any issues with the snow plow.
* **section_17** (`section_17`, `select_one`, required: true): Select one of the options provided (s, t, or u) to indicate the status of the snow plow.

## Tips
* Review the form carefully before starting to ensure that you have all the necessary information.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure of any of the questions or options, refer to your supervisor or the snow plow manual for guidance.
