# Vehicle Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Registration Form is used to collect information about vehicles for registration purposes. This form is designed to collect data required for insurance, licensing, or maintenance purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select the vehicle type**: Choose the type of vehicle from the list of options (Car, Truck, Motorcycle, Van, Bus).
2. **Enter vehicle details**: Provide the make and model of the vehicle.
3. **Enter registration details**: Enter the year of the vehicle and its Vehicle Identification Number (VIN).
4. **Enter color**: Select the color of the vehicle from the list of options (Red, Blue, Green, Yellow, Gray).
5. **Enter owner information**: Enter the owner's name and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Form Data
* **Form Data**: Enter any additional information about the vehicle, if needed. (Text, Not Required)

### Vehicle Type
* **Vehicle Type**: Choose the type of vehicle you are registering. (Select One, Required)

### Make
* **Make**: Enter the make of the vehicle. (Text, Not Required)

### Model
* **Model**: Enter the model of the vehicle. (Text, Not Required)

### Year
* **Year**: Enter the year of the vehicle. (Number, Not Required)

### VIN
* **VIN**: Enter the Vehicle Identification Number. (Text, Not Required)

### Color
* **Color**: Select the color of the vehicle. (Select One, Not Required)

### Plate Number
* **Plate Number**: Enter the plate number of the vehicle. (Text, Not Required)

### Owner Name
* **Owner Name**: Enter the name of the owner. (Text, Not Required)

### Owner Phone
* **Owner Phone**: Enter the phone number of the owner. (Text, Not Required)

## Tips
* Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure you select the correct vehicle type from the list.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the relevant authorities for assistance.
