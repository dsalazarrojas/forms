# Income Reduction Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Income Reduction Form is a tool designed to help users submit their application for financial assistance or services. This form will guide you through the necessary steps to complete your application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions on each page, and do not hesitate to ask for help if you need clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Details** (`page_1`, `note`, required: false): Enter a brief description of your application, including the reason for your submission.
* **Mortgage Details** (`page_2`, `text`, required: false): Enter your mortgage information, including relevant details such as address, loan amount, and loan type.
* **Credit Information** (`page_3`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of credit you are applying for, such as "Credit 1" or "Credit 2".
* **Income Information** (`page_4`, `date`, required: false): Enter your income information, including the date of your last pay stub or income verification.
* **Signature** (`page_5`, `text`, required: false): Enter your signature, confirming that the information you provided is accurate.
* **Contact Information** (`page_6`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
* **Additional Information** (`page_7`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select any additional information that may be relevant to your application, such as "Option 1" or "Option 2".
* **Submission** (`page_8`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the submission status, either "Submit" or "Cancel".
* **Back to Start** (`page_9`, `select_one`, required: false): Select "Back" to go back to the beginning of the form or "Continue" to proceed with submission.
* **Additional Information** (`page_10`, `text`, required: false): This field is likely for any additional comments or information that may be useful for the reviewer.

## Tips
* Please enter accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth review process.
* If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to ask.
* Review each field carefully before proceeding to ensure you have entered all necessary information.
