# Medical First Point Of Contact Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from patients for medical-related purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your name, email, and phone number accurately and in full.
2. Enter the date and time you would like to schedule your appointment.
3. Provide the duration of your condition, in minutes or hours.
4. Specify any medical conditions you are experiencing.
5. If applicable, list any medications you are currently taking.
6. Mention any allergies you have.
7. Write any additional comments about your visit.
8. Share your medical history.
9. Provide any additional medical records.
10. If applicable, enter the name and contact information of a second person you would like to include in your care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **name** (name, text, optional): Please enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
 
- **email** (email, email, required): Enter your primary email address where you can be contacted.
 
- **phone** (phone, text, optional): Enter your primary phone number where you can be reached.
 
- **date** (date, date, required): Choose the date you would like to schedule your appointment for.
 
- **time** (time, time, required): Choose the time you would like to schedule your appointment for.
 
- **duration** (duration, number, required): Enter the number of minutes or hours you would like your appointment to take.
 
- **medical-condition** (medical-condition, text, optional): Specify your current medical condition.
 
- **medication** (medication, text, optional): If applicable, list any medications you are currently taking.
 
- **allergies** (allergies, text, optional): If applicable, list any allergies you have.
 
- **comments** (comments, note, optional): Write any additional comments about your visit.
 
- **medical-history** (medical-history, text, optional): Share your medical history.
 
- **medical-records** (medical-records, text, optional): Provide any additional medical records.
 
- **Name 2** (name_2, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of a second person you would like to include in your care.
 
- **Email 2** (email_2, email, required): If applicable, enter the email address of a second person you would like to include in your care.
 
- **Phone 2** (phone_2, text, optional): If applicable, enter the phone number of a second person you would like to include in your care.
