# Lakeside Equipment Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about lakeside equipment, including its type, description, location, condition, date received, photo URLs, and any additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the equipment type in the first field. This is a text field where you can enter the name or description of the equipment.
2. Provide a brief description of the equipment in the second field. This can include its function, features, or any notable characteristics.
3. Enter the location of the equipment in the third field. This is where the equipment is currently situated.
4. Select the condition of the equipment from the options provided in the fourth field. This will help track its status.
5. If applicable, select the date the equipment was received from the available options in the fifth field. This can be a date when the equipment was acquired or started being used.
6. If you have any photo URLs related to the equipment, select them from the options provided in the sixth field. These can be URLs to images or videos that provide more information about the equipment.
7. Add any additional notes or comments about the equipment in the seventh and final field. This is a text area where you can provide more detailed information or observations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **equipment_type** (equipment_type, text, optional): Enter a brief description or name of the equipment. This will help identify the type of equipment being surveyed.
* **description** (description, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the equipment, including its function, features, or notable characteristics.
* **location** (location, text, optional): Enter the location where the equipment is currently situated. This can be a specific area, dock, or facility.
* **condition** (condition, select_one, optional): Select the condition of the equipment from the options provided. This will help track its status for maintenance or repair.
* **date_received** (date_received, date, optional): If applicable, select the date the equipment was received from the available options. This can be a date when the equipment was acquired or started being used.
* **photo_urls** (photo_urls, select_multiple, optional): Select any photo URLs related to the equipment from the options provided. These can be URLs to images or videos that provide more information about the equipment.
* **notes** (notes, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the equipment. This is a text area where you can provide more detailed information or observations.
