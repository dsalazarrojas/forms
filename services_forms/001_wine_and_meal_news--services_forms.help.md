# Wine And Meal News - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Wine And Meal News form is designed to collect information about wine and meal pairings. This form helps experts provide detailed recommendations for pairing wines with specific meals.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the meal(s) that you think pairs well with the wine. You can choose one or multiple options from the list.
2. Select the wine that you think pairs well with the meal. You can choose only one option.
3. If you have a specific date and time in mind for the wine and meal pairing, you can provide it.
4. Add any additional comments about the pairing.
5. Provide your rating or score for the pairing.
6. Add any final ratings or scores for the wine and meal combination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wine and Meal News (page)**: This is the title of the form.
* **Select Meal (meal_id)**: Choose one or multiple meals that you think pair well with the wine. You can select from the list of options provided.
* **Select Wine (wine_id)**: Choose the wine that you think pairs well with the meal. You can select only one option.
* **Date Paired (date_paired)**: If you have a specific date and time in mind for the wine and meal pairing, you can provide it. This field is not required.
* **Time Paired (date_paired_time)**: If you have a specific time in mind for the pairing, you can provide it. This field is optional.
* **Pairing Comment (pairing_comment)**: Add any additional comments about the wine and meal pairing.
* **Wine Notes (wine_notes)**: Add any notes about the wine.
* **Pairing Score (pairing_score)**: Provide your rating or score for the pairing.
* **Rating (rating)**: Add any final ratings or scores for the wine and meal combination.
