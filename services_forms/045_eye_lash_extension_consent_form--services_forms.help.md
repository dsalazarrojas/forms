# eye_lash_extension_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "eye_lash_extension_consent_form" is a form used to collect information from users related to eye lash extensions, likely for marketing or sales purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the correct options in "Form Data Section 1" and "Form Data Section 2" to provide relevant information.
2. In "Form Data Section 3", select one or multiple options from the list.
3. In "Signature", select one of the available options.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, if applicable.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field, if applicable.
6. Optionally, add any additional information or notes in the "Note" field.
7. Enter the name of the tool assigned to you in the "Assigned Tool Name" field, if applicable.
8. Finally, click submit to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Data** (`form_data`, text, optional): This is the main content area of the form where you can enter your details. 
* **Form Data Section 1** (`form_data_section_1`, select_one, required): Select one of the available options to provide more information about yourself.
* **Form Data Section 2** (`form_data_section_2`, select_multiple, required): Select multiple options from the list to provide more details.
* **Form Data Section 3** (`form_data_section_3`, text, required): Enter your answer to this question.
* **Signature** (`signature`, select_one, required): This indicates your consent or agreement with the selected data.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time in the format `HH:MM`.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional information or notes you'd like to share.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter the name of the tool assigned to you.
* **Assigned Tool Name** (`assigned_tool_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the tool assigned to you.
* **Created Date** (`created_date`, date, optional): This field displays the date the form was created.
* **Updated Date** (`updated_date`, date, optional): This field displays the date the form was last updated.

## Tips
- Please ensure you fill in all required fields.
- Double-check the information you enter before submitting the form.
- If you have any issues or need assistance, contact the form administrator.
