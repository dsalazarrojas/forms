# Air Conditioning Call Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Air Conditioning Call Out Form is used by customers to schedule a visit for air conditioning services. It collects essential information about the client, their service needs, and preferred appointment times. This information helps the service team to process the request efficiently and provide effective service.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. **Client Details**: Enter your name, phone number, address, and email address accurately. Your phone number and email will be used for contact purposes.
2. **Type of Property**: Choose the type of property you need service for. This could be Residential, Commercial, Industrial, or Rental Property.
3. **Urgency Level**: Indicate how soon you need the service. Options include Emergency (ASAP), Next Day, Within 3 Days, or Routine Maintenance. Choose the option that best describes your situation.
4. **Issue Description**: Select the specific issue you are experiencing with your air conditioning system. Choose from a list of common issues, including No Cooling, No Heating, Strange Noise, Water Leaking, Bad Smell, System Won't Turn On, or Routine Service.
5. **Approximate System Age**: If you know it, provide the age of your air conditioning system (e.g., 5 years). This can help the service team understand your system's performance more accurately.
6. **Preferred Visit Time**: Select a preferred time for the visit, if possible, or leave this field blank if you don't have a preference.
7. **Access Instructions**: Enter any access instructions, such as gate codes or key locations, that the service team may need to access your property.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Your full name as you want it to be addressed.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact number for any follow-up calls or confirmations.
* **Service Address**: Your address where the service will be conducted, including street, city, and zip code.
* **Email Address**: An additional contact method for confirmations and service updates.
* **Type of Property**: Indicate the type of property you own to tailor the service accordingly (Residential, Commercial, Industrial, or Rental Property).
* **Urgency Level**: Choose how quickly you need the service. Options include Emergency (ASAP), Next Day, Within 3 Days, or Routine Maintenance.
* **Issue Description**: Select the specific problem you are experiencing with your air conditioning.
* **Approximate System Age**: If you know the age of your system, please provide it for more accurate servicing.
* **Preferred Visit Time**: Choose a preferred time for the visit, if you have one.
* **Access Instructions**: Add any details about accessing your property that may aid the service team in their visit.

By following this guide, you should be able to fill out the Air Conditioning Call Out Form accurately and effectively, ensuring that your service request is processed correctly and efficiently by the service team.
