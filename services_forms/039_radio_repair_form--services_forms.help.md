# Radio Repair Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Radio Repair Form is used to collect information from customers who are seeking radio repair services. Please fill out this form to provide us with the necessary details to schedule your repair.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Customer Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
4. Select the type of service you require (radio, television, audio, or video) in the "Service Type" field.
5. Describe your radio repair request in the "Radio Repair Form Description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **radio_repair_form_name** (`text`, Optional):
  Enter a brief name for this repair service. This will help us identify your request.
* **customer_name** (`text`, Optional):
  Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **customer_email** (`email`, Optional):
  Enter your email address where we can contact you about your repair service.
* **customer_phone** (`text`, Optional):
  Enter your phone number where we can contact you about your repair service.
* **service_type** (`select_one`, Optional):
  Select the type of service you require:
  + Radio: Repair for a radio device.
  + Television: Repair for a television device.
  + Audio: Repair for an audio device.
  + Video: Repair for a video device.
* **service_type_selected** (`text`, Optional):
  This field is automatically populated based on your selected service type in the "Service Type" field. Please do not modify this field.
* **radio_repair_form_description** (`note`, Optional):
  Enter a detailed description of the repair service you require. This will help us understand your request better.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out the form correctly to avoid any delays or errors in processing your repair service.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
