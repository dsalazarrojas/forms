# Financial Counselling Agency Authorisation Form April 2020 - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used by financial institutions to grant authority to a financial counselling agency to provide services on their behalf.

## How To Complete This Form

Before completing this form, please ensure that you have the necessary authority to do so. This form should be completed accurately and thoroughly, and submitted correctly.

1. **First Name**: Enter your first name exactly as it appears on your official identification.
2. **Last Name**: Enter your last name exactly as it appears on your official identification.
3. **Email**: Enter a valid email address that can be used for communication purposes.
4. **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number, if you wish to be contacted regarding this authorisation.
5. **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD` (e.g., `1980-07-12`).
6. **Type of Service**: Select the type of service(s) that the financial counselling agency will be providing on behalf of the financial institution.
7. **Goals**: Provide a clear and concise description of the goals and objectives of this authorisation.
8. **Financial Institution**: Select the financial institution(s) that this authorisation is for.
9. **Date of Authorisation**: Enter the date on which this authorisation is being granted (if required).
10. **Time of Authorisation**: Enter the time on which this authorisation is being granted (if required).
11. **Notes**: Provide any additional information that may be helpful for the financial institution's records.
