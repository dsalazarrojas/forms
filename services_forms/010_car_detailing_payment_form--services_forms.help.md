# Car Detailing Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect payment information and vehicle details for our car detailing service. Please ensure you fill it out accurately to ensure a smooth process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Begin by providing your personal contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2.  Enter your vehicle's details such as the year, make, model, color, and size.
3.  Select the service package you wish to receive from our available options.
4.  Choose any additional services you would like us to perform on your vehicle.
5.  Indicate when you wish to receive the service and the preferred time.
6.  Select the location where you would like us to perform the service.
7.  Provide payment details such as the method of payment and card information (if applicable).
8.  If you wish to leave a tip for our technicians, select the amount.
9.  Enter any special instructions or concerns you have regarding the service.
10.  Agree to our terms and conditions.
11.  Finally, confirm your appointment by entering today's date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Customer Information
*   **Your Name** (id: 2, `customer_name`): Enter your full name for our records.
*   **Email Address** (id: 3, `customer_email`): Enter the email address you want us to use for receipt and communication.
*   **Phone Number** (id: 4, `customer_phone`): Provide your contact number.

### Vehicle Information
*   **Vehicle Year** (id: 6, `vehicle_year`): Enter the year of your vehicle.
*   **Vehicle Make** (id: 7, `vehicle_make`): Enter the manufacturer of your vehicle.
*   **Vehicle Model** (id: 8, `vehicle_model`): Enter the model name of your vehicle.
*   **Vehicle Color** (id: 9, `vehicle_color`): Enter the exterior color of your vehicle.
*   **Vehicle Size** (id: 10, `vehicle_size`): Select the size category of your vehicle from the available options.
*   **Vehicle Condition** (id: 11, `vehicle_condition`): Indicate the current condition of your vehicle from the available options.

### Service Details
*   **Service Selection** (id: 12, `service_selection`): Choose your desired service package from the options.
*   **Detailing Package** (id: 13, `detailing_package`): Select the specific package you wish to receive from the available options.
*   **Add-On Services** (id: 14, `add_on_services`): Select any additional services you would like us to perform.
*   **Service Date** (id: 15, `service_date`): Enter the date you wish to receive the service.
*   **Service Time** (id: 16, `service_time`): Indicate your preferred service time.
*   **Service Location** (id: 17, `service_location`): Select the location where you would like us to perform the service.

### Payment Information
*   **Payment Information** (id: 18, `payment_information`): This section is for notes only and is not required.
*   **Payment Method** (id: 19, `payment_method`): Choose your preferred payment method from the available options.
*   **Card Type** (id: 20, `card_type`): Select the type of card you have if paying by card.
*   **Card Number** (id: 21, `card_number`): If paying by card, enter the card number.
*   **CVV** (id: 23, `cvv`): If paying by card, enter the security code.
*   **Billing Address** (id: 24, `billing_address`): Enter the address on your card (if applicable).
*   **Tip Amount** (id: 25, `tip_amount`): If you wish to leave a tip, select the amount.
*   **Special Instructions** (id: 26, `special_instructions`): Enter any specific requests or concerns you have.

### Terms and Conditions
*   **Terms Agreement** (id: 27, `terms_agreement`): Confirm that you agree to our terms and conditions.
*   **Authorization Date** (id: 28, `authorization_date`): Enter today's date to confirm your appointment.

By following these steps and filling out this form accurately, you will be able to provide the necessary information for our car detailing service to proceed according to your wishes. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
