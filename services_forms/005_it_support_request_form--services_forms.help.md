# it_support_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed information about an IT support request. It consists of several sections that help the support team to identify and resolve the issue efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Please select the type of Internet connection you are experiencing issues with.
2.  Choose the operating systems affected by the issue (Windows, Linux, Mac, or Chrome OS).
3.  Describe the details of your problem in the "Problem Details" field.
4.  Provide your contact information so we can get in touch with you.
5.  Enter a description of the issue you are experiencing.
6.  You can optionally provide additional information about your support request if needed.
7.  Select the status of your support request (Open, In Progress, or Resolved).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Internet Connection** (`internet_connection`, required): Please select the type of Internet connection you are experiencing issues with (DSL or None).
* **Operating Systems** (`operating_system`, required): Choose the operating systems affected by the issue (Windows, Linux, Mac, or Chrome OS).
* **Problem Details** (`problem_details`, required): Describe the details of your problem, including any error messages or symptoms you are experiencing.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, required): Provide your contact information so we can get in touch with you.
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, required): Enter a description of the issue you are experiencing.
* **Support Request** (`support_request`, optional): You can optionally provide additional information about your support request if needed.
* **Support Status** (`support_status`, required): Select the status of your support request (Open, In Progress, or Resolved).
