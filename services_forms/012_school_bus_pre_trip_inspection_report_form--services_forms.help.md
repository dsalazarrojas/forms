# School Bus Pre Trip Inspection Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Bus Pre Trip Inspection Report Form is used to document the pre-trip inspection of a school bus, capturing the status of various critical systems and components to ensure the bus is safe and ready for the trip.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have read and understand the form's purpose.
2. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
3. Answer each question or selection based on your inspection results.

### Additional Information
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or observations about the inspection.
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter the name of the tool used for the inspection (if applicable).
* **Assigned To**: Enter the name of the person responsible for the inspection.
