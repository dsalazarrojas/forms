# Select Extras For Your Installation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to select extra services for your installation. It is designed to gather information about your installation needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. The form has multiple fields to input information about your installation.
2. Start by entering the date of your installation in the first field.
3. Select any extra services that you would like to request for your installation from the second field, "extras_requested".
4. Choose your preferred scheduling time for the installation in the third field, "scheduling_preferences".
5. Provide the address of your installation in the fourth field.
6. Enter the time of your installation in the fifth field.
7. Finally, add any additional notes about your installation in the last field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Installation Date** (`installation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your installation.
- **Extras Requested** (`extras_requested`, select multiple, optional): Select any extra services that you would like to request for your installation.
- **Scheduling Preferences** (`scheduling_preferences`, select one, optional): Choose your preferred scheduling time for the installation.
- **Installation Address** (`installation_address`, text, optional): Provide the address of your installation.
- **Installation Time** (`installation_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your installation.
- **Installation Notes** (`installation_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your installation.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and relevant information in each field.
* Select all extra services that you need for your installation.
* Be specific about your preferred scheduling time for the installation.
* Enter the correct address of your installation.
* Enter the correct time of your installation.
* Be concise and clear with your installation notes.
