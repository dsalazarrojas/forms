# vip_service_upgrade_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request an upgrade to a VIP service. Please fill out the form below to provide us with the necessary information to process your request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately, ensuring all required fields are filled out.
2. If you are unsure about any field, please contact us directly for clarification.
3. Once you have filled out the form, click the "Submit" button to send your request to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your name?**: This is the first field on the form. Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in our records.
* **What service are you requesting?**: This field is a dropdown menu with three options: Basic, Premium, and Advanced. Please select the service you are interested in upgrading to.
* **What is the reason for your service upgrade request?**: This field is where you can provide a brief explanation for your service upgrade request.
* **Do you have any additional requests for your service upgrade?**: This field is for any additional information you want to provide about your request.
* **How would you like to be contacted?**: This field is a dropdown menu with two options: Email and Phone. Please select your preferred contact method.
* **Email**: This field is for your email address.
* **What is your preferred contact method?**: This field is a dropdown menu with two options: Email and Phone. Please select your preferred contact method.
* **Phone Number**: This field is for your phone number.
* **Date of Birth**: This field is for your date of birth.
* **Time**: This field is for the time you are requesting the service upgrade.
* **What benefits do you expect from your service upgrade?**: This field is for any additional benefits you expect from the upgraded service.
* **When do you need the service upgrade?**: This field is a date field where you can select the date you need the service upgrade.
* **What is your budget for the service upgrade?**: This field is for your budget for the service upgrade.
* **Do you have any additional comments?**: This field is for any additional comments you want to provide about your request.
* **Have you read and agree to our service level agreement?**: This field is a checkbox where you can agree to our service level agreement.
* **What are your thoughts on our service level agreement?**: This field is for any additional comments about our service level agreement.
* **What services would you like to upgrade?**: This field is a dropdown menu with three options: Basic, Premium, and Advanced. Please select the services you want to upgrade.
* **Is this information correct?**: This field is a radio button where you can confirm that the information provided is correct.
