# Sfdc Campaign Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to request a campaign from the client. It is intended for clients to provide information about their campaign requirements, target audience, and expected return on investment (ROI). The form helps the client to clearly communicate their needs and preferences for the campaign, making it easier for the agency to understand and fulfill their requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the **Campaign Name** field with a brief title of your campaign.
2.  Provide a detailed description of your **Campaign Requirements** in the text field.
3.  Specify the **Media Used** by selecting from the options provided (if any).
4.  Identify your **Target Audience** by choosing a platform (e.g., Facebook, Google Adwords, Bing Ads).
5.  Enter the **Budget Allocated** for the campaign.
6.  Select the **Expected Return** by choosing from the options (e.g., ROI, Increased Sales, Brand Awareness, Conversions).
7.  Fill in the **Contact Person** field with your name and **Contact Email** and **Contact Phone** fields with your contact details.
8.  If you have any **Additional Comments**, you can use this field to provide further information.
9.  Confirm that you have read and agree to the **Terms and Conditions**.
10.  Select whether you **Agree** to the terms and conditions.
11.  Enter the **Date of Submission**.
12.  Specify whether you are submitting on behalf of **Client** or **Agency**.
13.  Confirm whether you **Agree** to the submission.
14.  Enter the **Submission Date**.
15.  (Optional) Choose the **Campaign Type**, **Campaign Objectives**, and **Campaign Duration** if applicable.
16.  Finally, fill in the **Form Submission Date** to confirm the completion of this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Campaign Name** (`campaign_name`, text, required): A brief title of your campaign.
*   **Campaign Requirements** (`campaign_requirements`, text, required): A detailed description of your campaign requirements.
*   **Media Used** (`media_used`, text, optional): A field to specify the media used (e.g., image, video, etc.).
*   **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, select one, required): Choose a platform for your target audience (e.g., Facebook, Google Adwords, Bing Ads).
*   **Target Audience Description** (`target_audience_description`, text, optional): A field to provide a detailed description of your target audience.
*   **Platform Used** (`platform_used`, select one, required): A field to specify the platform you will be using (e.g., Facebook, Google Adwords, Bing Ads).
*   **Budget Allocated** (`budget_allocated`, number, required): Enter the budget for your campaign.
*   **Expected Return** (`expected_return`, select multiple, required): Choose from the options the expected return on investment (e.g., ROI, Increased Sales, Brand Awareness, Conversions).
*   **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Fill in your name.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Fill in your email address.
*   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Fill in your phone number.
*   **Contact Message** (`contact_message`, text, optional): A field to provide further information.
*   **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, text, required): A field to read and agree to the terms and conditions.
*   **Terms and Conditions Agreed** (`terms_and_conditions_agreed`, select one, required): A field to confirm agreement to the terms and conditions.
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): A field to provide further information.
*   **Date of Submission** (`date_of_submission`, date, required): Enter the date of submission.
*   **Submission by** (`submission_by`, select one, required): Choose whether you are submitting on behalf of **Client** or **Agency**.
*   **Submission Agreed** (`submission_agreed`, select one, required): A field to confirm agreement to submission.
*   **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the submission date.
*   **Form Submission** (`form_submission`, text, optional): A field to provide further information.
*   **Campaign Type** (`campaign_type`, select multiple, optional): A field to choose the campaign type (e.g., Awareness, Conversions, Engagement, Sales).
*   **Campaign Objectives** (`campaign_objectives`, text, required): A field to specify your campaign objectives.
*   **Campaign Duration** (`campaign_duration`, number, optional): A field to specify the campaign duration.
*   **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, optional): A field to specify the submission time.
*   **Form Submission Date** (`form_submission_date`, date, required): A field to confirm the submission date.
