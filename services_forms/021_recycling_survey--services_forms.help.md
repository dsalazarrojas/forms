# Recycling Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from end users about their recycling habits, knowledge, and attitudes towards recycling. This information will help us understand the current state of recycling in your community and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and select the most accurate answer for each field.
2. Choose the options that best match your current recycling habits and knowledge.
3. For multiple-choice questions, select all that apply.
4. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Household Recycling** (Household_Recycling, select_multiple, required/optional): This question asks about your household's recycling practices. Select all the options that apply to your household's recycling habits.
* **Community Recycling** (Community_Recycling, select_one, required/optional): This question is asking about the type of recycling services you have access to in your community. Choose the option that best describes your current situation.
* **Education** (Education, select_one, required/optional): This question is about your awareness and understanding of recycling practices. Select the option that best matches your current level of knowledge.
* **Organization** (Organization, number, required/optional): This question is not currently applicable. Please ignore this field.
* **Participation** (Participation, select_multiple, required/optional): This question is asking about your participation in recycling activities. Select all the options that apply to you.
* **Knowledge** (Knowledge, select_one, required/optional): This question is about your current level of knowledge about recycling. Select the option that best matches your current understanding.
* **Concerns** (Concerns, select_one, required/optional): This question is asking about any concerns or issues you have about recycling. Select the option that best describes your current concerns.
* **Suggestions** (Suggestions, note, required/optional): This question is an open-ended field for you to provide any additional comments or suggestions about recycling in your community.
* **Demographic** (Demographic, text, required/optional): This question is asking about your age group. Please provide your current age group.
* **Comments** (Comments, text, required/optional): This question is an open-ended field for you to provide any additional comments about your recycling habits and knowledge.

## Tips

* Please answer honestly and accurately to the best of your ability.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* Your participation and feedback are greatly appreciated and will help us improve our services.
