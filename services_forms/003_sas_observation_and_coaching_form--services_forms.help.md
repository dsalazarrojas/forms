# SAS Observation And Coaching Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SAS Observation And Coaching Form is designed to gather feedback and insights from team members after a coaching or observation session. This form helps identify areas of improvement and progress made during the session.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions that apply to your experience.
2. Be as descriptive as possible for the "Observation", "Coaching", "Progress", "Review", and "Review 2" fields.
3. For the "Progress" field, select one of the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, text, optional): A brief summary of the context and background of the session.
* **What is your position/title in the organization?** (`intro_2`, text, optional): Please state your role or position within the organization.
* **Observation** (`observation`, text, optional): Describe the most significant observations that stood out to you during the session.
* **What were the most significant observations that stood out to you?** (`observation_2`, text, required): Expand on your previous observation, providing more details if needed.
* **Coaching** (`coaching`, text, optional): Summarize the guidance or advice provided during the session.
* **How did the client/individual respond to coaching?** (`coaching_2`, text, required): Describe how the client or individual reacted to the coaching or guidance provided.
* **Progress** (`progress`, select_one, required): Select one of the options that best describes the progress made during the session.
* **Describe the progress made** (`progress_2`, text, optional): Provide more details about the progress made.
* **Review** (`review`, text, required): Summarize your key takeaways from the session.
* **What did you learn from the experience?** (`review_2`, text, required): Reflect on what you learned from the session.
* **Additional comments** (`review_3`, note, optional): Add any additional thoughts or comments you'd like to share.
