# Cleaning Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to aid in documenting and tracking the progress of cleaning sessions, ensuring that all areas of a property are properly cleaned and maintained. It helps crew leaders or supervisors keep track of their tasks, crew members' presence, location information, and the status of each cleaning task. This form is meant to be a comprehensive guide for cleaning crews to follow and for clients to review.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information and equipment for the cleaning session.
2. Fill out the following fields:
   - **Cleaning Date**: Enter the date of the cleaning session.
   - **Crew Leader/Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of the person responsible for the cleaning session.
   - **Location/Property Name**: Enter the name or address of the property being cleaned.
   - **Location Type**: Select the type of property being cleaned (e.g., Residential Home, Office Building, etc.).
   - **Start Time** and **Completion Time**: Record the start and end times of the cleaning session.
3. For each section (Cleaning Schedule Information, Kitchen Cleaning, Bathroom Cleaning, Living Areas, General Tasks, and Completion Notes), review the tasks and select the status for each:
   - **Clean and Sanitize Countertops**, **Clean and Disinfect Toilet**, **Clean Shower/Bathtub**, **Clean Sink and Faucet** (Bathroom), **Clean Mirrors and Windows**, and **Clean Bathroom Floor** for the kitchen and bathroom areas respectively.
   - **Dust all Surfaces and Furniture**, **Vacuum Carpets and Rugs**, and **Clean Hard Floors** for the living areas.
   - **Empty Trash and Replace Liners**, **Restock Cleaning Supplies and Paper Products**, and **Conduct Final Walkthrough Inspection** for general tasks.
   - Record any issues or damages encountered and the time the cleaning session started and ended.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Cleaning Date**: Enter the date the cleaning session took place to keep a record of when the cleaning occurred.
	+ Required field to track when the cleaning session began.
* **Crew Leader/Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of the person leading or supervising the cleaning session to keep track of who was responsible.
	+ Required field to record who was in charge of the cleaning session.
* **Location/Property Name**: Enter the name or address of the property being cleaned for easy identification.
	+ Required field to identify the location of the cleaning session.
* **Location Type**: Select the type of property being cleaned to categorize and standardize the cleaning sessions.
	+ Required field to classify the location type.
* **Start Time**: Record the time the cleaning session began to track progress and completion time.
	+ Required field to track session duration.
* **Completion Time**: Record the time the cleaning session ended to track duration and completion status.
	+ Required field to track session duration.
* **Clean and Sanitize Countertops**: Check if the countertops were cleaned and sanitized in the kitchen area.
	+ Required field for kitchen cleaning status.
* **Clean and Disinfect Toilet**: Check if the toilet was cleaned and disinfected in the bathroom area.
	+ Required field for bathroom cleaning status.
* **Clean Shower/Bathtub**: Check if the shower/bathtub was cleaned in the bathroom area.
	+ Required field for bathroom cleaning status.
* **Clean Sink and Faucet** (Bathroom): Check if the sink and faucet were cleaned in the bathroom area.
	+ Required field for bathroom cleaning status.
* **Clean Mirrors and Windows**: Check if the mirrors and windows were cleaned in the bathroom area.
	+ Required field for bathroom cleaning status.
* **Clean Bathroom Floor**: Check if the bathroom floor was cleaned.
	+ Required field for bathroom cleaning status.
* **Dust all Surfaces and Furniture**: Check if all surfaces and furniture were dusted in the living areas.
	+ Required field for living area cleaning status.
* **Vacuum Carpets and Rugs**: Check if carpets and rugs were vacuumed in the living areas.
	+ Required field for living area cleaning status.
* **Clean Hard Floors**: Check if hard floors were cleaned in the living areas.
	+ Required field for living area cleaning status.
* **Empty Trash and Replace Liners**: Check if trash was emptied and liners replaced in general tasks.
	+ Required field for general task status.
* **Restock Cleaning Supplies and Paper Products**: Check if cleaning supplies and paper products were restocked in general tasks.
	+ Required field for general task status.
* **Conduct Final Walkthrough Inspection**: Check if a final inspection was conducted.
	+ Required field for general task status.
* **Any Issues or Damages Encountered**: Record any issues or damages found during the cleaning session.
	+ Optional field for recording issues or damages.
* **Time Started**: Record the time the cleaning session began.
	+ Required field to track session duration.
* **Time Completed**: Record the time the cleaning session ended.
	+ Required field to track session duration.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled out thoroughly and accurately to maintain a clear record of the cleaning session.
* Double-check the status of each task to ensure a complete and correct record.
* This form is meant to serve as a guide for cleaning crews to follow and for clients to review, please use it as a tool to improve your cleaning sessions.
