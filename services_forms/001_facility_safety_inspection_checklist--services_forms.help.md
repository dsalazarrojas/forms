# Facility Safety Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to conduct a thorough inspection of a facility's safety features and identify areas that require improvement or attention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Fill in your details**: Enter your name and email as the contact person responsible for the inspection.
2. **Provide facility information**: Enter the name, address, and location of the facility being inspected.
3. **Specify the facility type**: Confirm whether the facility has the type of safety features and equipment it should have.
4. **Record the inspection date**: Enter the date of the inspection to track progress and follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **facility_name**: Enter the name of the facility being inspected, e.g. "Warehouse 123".
* **contact_person**: Enter your name as the person responsible for the inspection, e.g. "John Doe".
* **contact_email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes, e.g. "[johndoe@example.com](mailto:johndoe@example.com)".
* **facility_location**: Enter the address of the facility, e.g. "123 Main St, Anytown, USA 12345".
* **facility_type**: Check the option that best describes the type of facility being inspected, e.g. "Industrial", "Residential", etc.
* **facility_address**: Enter the detailed address of the facility, e.g. "123 Main St, Anytown, USA 12345".
* **date_of_inspection**: Enter the date of the inspection in the format "YYYY-MM-DD", e.g. "2022-06-15".
