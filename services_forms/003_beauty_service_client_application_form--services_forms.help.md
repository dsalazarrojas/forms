# Beauty Service Client Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from clients for beauty services. It is a mandatory form that requires clients to fill in their personal details, medical history, and consent for services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill in the Personal Information Section**: This section requires your first name, last name, date of birth, email address, and phone number. Ensure that you enter your details accurately.
2. **Enter Your Health Information**: If you have any known allergies, skin conditions, or are currently taking medications that affect your skin, please list them in the respective fields.
3. **Provide Consent**: In the Consent Section, select 'I Agree' for all questions. This is to ensure that you understand and agree to the terms and conditions of our services.
4. **Date and Time**: Ensure that you fill in your application date accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Email Address**: Enter your valid email address to enable us to contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your valid phone number, including your area code.
* **Street Address**: Enter your street address where we can deliver or send services to.
* **City**: Enter the city where you reside.
* **State/Province**: Enter the state or province where you reside.
* **Postal Code**: Enter your postal code.
* **Allergies**: List any known allergies to beauty products.
* **Skin Conditions**: If you have any skin conditions like eczema, psoriasis, or acne, list them here.
* **Meditations**: If you are currently taking any medications that affect your skin, list them here.
* **Pregnancy Status**: Check if you are currently pregnant. If you prefer not to answer, select 'Prefer Not to Say'.
* **Medical Procedures**: If you have undergone any recent medical procedures or treatments, list them here.
* **Consent for Services**: Select 'I Agree' to confirm that you consent to receive beauty services.
* **Consent for Risks**: Select 'I Agree' to confirm that you understand the risks and potential side effects of the services.
* **Consent for Privacy Policy and Terms**: Select 'I Agree' to confirm that you understand our privacy policy and terms.
* **Consent for Contact**: Select 'True' to consent to being contacted about services. If you select 'False', we will not contact you.
* **Application Date**: Ensure that you fill in your application date accurately.
