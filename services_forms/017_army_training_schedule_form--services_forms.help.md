# Army Training Schedule Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about upcoming training schedules for army units, allowing unit commanders and training coordinators to plan and manage training events efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Unit Name" field and enter the name of the unit.
2. Enter the "Unit Commander Name and Rank" and "Training Coordinator Name" fields with the relevant details.
3. Select the "Schedule Period" from the options (Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Annual).
4. Enter the "Training Period Start Date" and "Training Period End Date".
5. For each course, enter the "Course Name", "Course Type", "Start Date", "Duration in Hours", "Number of Participants", "Primary Instructor Name", and "Training Location" fields.
6. Select the "Budget Approved for Training" option (True, False, or Pending).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Unit Name**: Enter the name of the unit participating in the training event.
* **Unit Commander Name and Rank**: Enter the name and rank of the unit commander responsible for the training event.
* **Training Coordinator Name**: Enter the name of the training coordinator responsible for the training event.
* **Schedule Period**: Select the period for which the training is scheduled (Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Annual).
* **Training Period Start Date**: Enter the start date of the training period.
* **Training Period End Date**: Enter the end date of the training period.
* **Course 1 Name**: Enter the name of the first course being offered.
* **Course 1 Type**: Select the type of the first course (Basic training, Advanced skills, Leadership development, Technical training, Physical fitness, or Other).
* **Start Date 1**: Enter the start date of the first course.
* **Duration 1 in Hours**: Enter the duration of the first course in hours.
* **Number of Participants 1**: Enter the number of participants for the first course.
* **Primary Instructor Name 1**: Enter the name of the primary instructor for the first course.
* **Training Location 1**: Enter the location of the first course.
* **Budget Approved for Training**: Select whether the training budget has been approved (True, False, or Pending).

## Tips
* Make sure to accurately enter all fields to ensure that the training event is well-planned and executed.
* If there are multiple courses, repeat the "Course 1" fields for each additional course.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the training coordinator or unit commander for clarification.
