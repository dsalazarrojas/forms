# vehicle_transfer_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vehicle transfer form is used to document the transfer of ownership of a vehicle between a buyer and a seller. This form captures essential details to ensure a smooth and secure transfer process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the seller's information:
	* Choose the seller's name
	* Enter the seller's phone number
	* Enter the seller's email
2. Enter the buyer's information:
	* This is a note field for the buyer's details, but it's not required.
3. Provide vehicle details:
	* Select the vehicle's license plate
	* Enter the vehicle's year
	* Choose the vehicle's make
	* Choose the vehicle's model
4. Select transfer details:
	* Choose the type of transfer (e.g., sold, gifted, etc.)
	* Select the reason for the transfer
5. Enter the transfer date (optional)
6. Add a witness statement (optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **seller_info** (seller name, select_one, required):
	+ Select the seller's name from the provided options.
* **buyer_info** (seller info, note, optional):
	+ This field is for the buyer to provide their information, but it's not required.
* **vehicle_details** (vehicle details, select_multiple, required):
	+ Select all required vehicle details, including license plate, year, make, and model.
* **transfer_details** (transfer type, select_one, required):
	+ Choose the type of transfer (e.g., sold, gifted, etc.)
* **transfer_date** (transfer date, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date of the transfer (optional).
* **witness_statement** (witness statement, note, optional):
	+ This field is for the witness to provide their statement, but it's not required.
