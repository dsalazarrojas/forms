# free_construction_contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
The free_construction_contract form is designed to collect information related to a construction project, including general details, construction specifics, contact information, and terms of agreement. This form is intended to be a comprehensive guide for project administrators, contractors, and other stakeholders involved in the construction process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the **General Info** page, which includes fields for project information.
2. Proceed to the **Construction Details** page, where you will find fields for specific construction details.
3. On the **Contact Info** page, enter your contact information.
4. Move on to the **Contract Terms** page, which includes fields for contract-specific information.
5. Review and confirm your information on the **Review Terms** page.
6. Finally, review and agree to the **Sign Terms** page, which includes the terms of agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Info (General Info)** (`id: 1`, text, not required):
  This page is where you can fill in general information about the project.
* **Construction Details (Construction Details)** (`id: 2`, text, not required):
  Enter specific details about the construction project, such as materials, location, and duration.
* **Contact Info (Contact Info)** (`id: 3`, text, not required):
  Enter your contact information for communication and follow-up purposes.
* **Contract Terms (Contract Terms)** (`id: 4`, text, not required):
  Enter the terms and conditions of the contract.
* **Review Terms (Review Terms)** (`id: 5`, text, not required):
  Double-check your information for accuracy and completeness before proceeding.
* **Sign Terms (Sign Terms)** (`id: 6`, text, not required):
  Review and agree to the terms of agreement before signing the contract.
* **Payment Terms (Payment Terms)** (`id: 7`, text, not required):
  Enter the payment terms and conditions of the contract.
* **Agreement Terms (Agreement Terms)** (`id: 8`, text, not required):
  Review and agree to the final agreement terms before signing the contract.

## Tips
- Double-check the form for completeness and accuracy before signing.
- Ensure you have filled in all required and relevant information.
- Review and agree to the terms before signing the contract.
