# Perfume Box - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Perfume Box form is used to collect information for a perfume subscription service. It helps us understand your preferences and needs to provide the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk \*.
2. Choose your options from the relevant dropdown menus.
3. Enter any additional information in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Each aspect of life** (`life_aspects`, `text`, required: false): Describe each aspect of your life that you would like to associate with perfume, e.g., relaxation, stress relief, or mood booster.
* **Monthly subscription** (`suscripcion_mensual`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose whether you would like to subscribe to our monthly perfume service.
* **Payment plan** (`payment_plan`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more payment plans that suits your needs, such as monthly or weekly subscription, or one-time payment.
* **Perfume combination** (`perume_combination`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the type of perfume combination that you prefer, such as classic, eau de toilette, or parfum.
* **Shipping method** (`shipping_method`, `select_one`, required: true): Select how you would like to receive your perfume, such as express delivery or standard delivery.
* **Shipping address** (`shipping_address`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the type of shipping address you would like to use, such as address, zip code, city, or state.
* **Payment method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose how you would like to pay for your perfume, such as credit card or PayPal.
* **Payment info** (`payment_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional payment information, e.g., card number or expiration date.
* **Shipping cost** (`shipping_cost`, `number`, required: false): Enter the total shipping cost, but this field is not required for calculation purposes.
* **Total cost** (`total_cost`, `number`, required: false): Display the total cost, but this field is not required for calculation purposes.
* **Shipping time** (`shipping_time`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you would like to receive your perfume.
* **Shipping status** (`shipping_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the shipping status, such as delivered, pending, or failed.
* **Shipping tracking number** (`shipping_tracking_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter the shipping tracking number, but this field is not required for tracking purposes.
