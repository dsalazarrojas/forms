# Wedding Hair & Makeup Service Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information regarding a wedding ceremony's hair and makeup services. It will be used to create an order for these services, which will be processed and fulfilled by our wedding service team.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form by following these steps:
	* Answer all relevant questions on each page.
	* Ensure you complete all pages as required.
2. Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **1. Wedding Details**: This page allows you to input any relevant details about the wedding ceremony, such as the bride's name, wedding date, or any specific requirements you may have.
* **2. Bride's Information**: Here you can provide information about the bride, including her height, weight, and any other relevant details.
* **3. Hair Services**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' next to each hair service option to indicate which services you would like to include in the order. Options include:
	+ Hair Style (e.g., updo, straight, curly)
	+ Hair Color
	+ Hair Accessories (e.g., flowers, veils)
* **4. Makeup Services**: Similar to hair services, select 'Yes' or 'No' next to each makeup service option to indicate which services you would like to include in the order. Options include:
	+ Basic (e.g., foundation, powder)
	+ Specialized (e.g., airbrush makeup, lashes)
	+ Makeup Trial
* **5. Further Data**: Here you can provide any additional information that will help our team complete your order. 

## Tips
- Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our team for assistance.
- Ensure to fill out all required fields to ensure efficient processing of your order.
