# Digital Pledge - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Digital Pledge form is a tool for users to create and track digital pledges, which represent a promise or commitment to complete a specific task or project. This form is used to collect information about the pledge, such as its title, description, category, assigned tool, and associated user. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Digital Pledge form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter a title for your digital pledge**: In the "digital_pledge" field, enter a concise and descriptive title for your pledge. This will help identify the purpose and scope of your commitment.
2. **Provide a description of your digital pledge**: In the "digital_pledge_description" field, write a brief description of the work or project you plan to undertake.
3. **Select a category for your digital pledge**: From the "category" field, choose a category that best describes the nature of your pledge, such as "Services" or "Other".
4. **Assign a tool for your digital pledge (optional)**: If applicable, select an assigned tool from the "assigned_tool" field for your pledge.
5. **Enter the associated user (optional)**: If necessary, type the ID of the user associated with your pledge in the "user" field.
6. **Enter the dollar amount (optional)**: If you're tracking a financial aspect, enter the dollar amount associated with your pledge in the "dollar_amount" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **digital_pledge (Form Title, text, required):** Enter a title for your digital pledge.
* **digital_pledge_description (Form Description, text, required):** Write a brief description of your digital pledge.
* **category (Form Category, select_one, required):** Choose a category that best describes the nature of your pledge, such as "Services" or "Other".
* **assigned_tool (Form Assigned Tool, text, optional):** If applicable, select an assigned tool for your pledge.
* **user (Form User, number, optional):** If necessary, type the ID of the user associated with your pledge.
* **dollar_amount (Form Dollar Amount, number, optional):** If you're tracking a financial aspect, enter the dollar amount associated with your pledge.

## Tips

* Be concise and accurate when filling out the form to ensure that your digital pledge is properly registered and easily identifiable.
* If you're unsure about which category to select, refer to the provided options for guidance.
* If you're not sure about the associated user or dollar amount, you can skip these fields if they're not applicable to your digital pledge.
* Make sure to save your progress after completing each field to avoid losing any entered data.
