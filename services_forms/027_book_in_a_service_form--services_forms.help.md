# Book In A Service Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Book In A Service Form is a tool for customers to book a service for their book-related needs. It helps us understand the customer's requirements and preferences, allowing us to provide efficient and effective service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Select the type of service you need in the "Type of Service" field.
5. Enter the title of the book needing service in the "Book Title" field.
6. Enter the author of the book in the "Book Author" field (optional).
7. Describe the book and its condition in the "Book Description" field.
8. Select the approximate age of the book in the "Approximate Age of Book" field.
9. Enter any estimated book value (if known) in the "Estimated Book Value" field.
10. Describe the damage or issue with the book in the "Description of Damage or Issue" field.
11. If possible, upload photos of the damage or issue in the "Damage Photos" field.
12. Select the urgency level of the service in the "How Urgent Is This Service" field.
13. Enter your preferred service date in the "Preferred Service Date" field (optional).
14. Select your preferred time for the service in the "Preferred Time" field (optional).
15. Select your preferred service location in the "Service Location Preference" field.
16. Enter your full address in the "Service Address" field (optional).
17. Select your expected budget range in the "Estimated Budget Range" field (optional).
18. If you have used our services before, select "True" in the "Have You Used Our Services Before" field (optional).
19. If you found us through a different source, select the corresponding option in the "How Did You Hear About Us" field (optional).
20. Enter any additional comments or information in the "Additional Comments" field (optional).
21. Select "Yes I agree" to confirm that you have read and agree to our service terms in the "I Agree to Service Terms" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name, which will be used as a reference for our communication and service.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Provide your email address, which will be used for communication and to send confirmations.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number, which will be used for communication and coordination.
* **Type of Service** (`service_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of service you need, such as book binding, restoration, or cleaning.
* **Book Title** (`book_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the book needing service.
* **Book Author** (`book_author`, `text`, optional): Enter the author of the book (if known).
* **Book Description** (`book_description`, `text`, required): Describe the book and its condition to help us understand the service needed.
* **Approximate Age of Book** (`book_age`, `select_one`, required): Select the approximate age of the book.
* **Estimated Book Value** (`book_value`, `select_one`, optional): Enter any estimated book value (if known).
* **Description of Damage or Issue** (`damage_description`, `text`, required): Describe the damage or issue with the book.
* **Damage Photos** (`damage_photos`, `text`, optional): If possible, upload photos of the damage or issue.
* **How Urgent Is This Service** (`urgency`, `select_one`, required): Select the urgency level of the service.
* **Preferred Service Date** (`preferred_date`, `date`, optional): Enter your preferred service date.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, `time`, optional): Select your preferred time for the service.
* **Service Location Preference** (`service_location`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred service location.
* **Service Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter your full address for service.
* **Estimated Budget Range** (`budget_range`, `select_one`, optional): Select your expected budget range.
* **Have You Used Our Services Before** (`previous_service`, `select_one`, optional): If you have used our services before, select "True".
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_did_you_hear`, `select_one`, optional): If you found us through a different source, select the corresponding option.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or information.
* **I Agree to Service Terms** (`terms_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes I agree" to confirm that you have read and agree to our service terms.
