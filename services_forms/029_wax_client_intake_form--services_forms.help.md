# Wax Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from clients about themselves, which will be used to provide personalized services for their specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields (indicated by an asterisk *).
2. Provide accurate information for each field.
3. If you have any medical conditions, allergies, or sensitivities, please list them in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (*First name*): Enter your given name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last name** (*Last name*): Enter your surname as it appears on your official documents.
* **Birth date** (*Birth date*): Enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Email** (*Email*): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone number** (*Phone number*): Enter a phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Service consent** (*Service consent*): Select `Yes` if you agree to receive services from this provider. You can select `No` if you do not consent.
* **Allergy** (*Allergy*): If you have any allergies, list them here.
* **Service details** (*Service details*): Describe any specific services you require or are interested in.
* **Medical conditions** (*Medical conditions*): If you have any medical conditions, list them here.
* **Skin type** (*Skin type*): Enter your skin type (e.g., normal, sensitive, dry, oily).
* **Service area** (*Service area*): Describe the area on your body you would like services to be applied to.
* **Wax sensitivity** (*Wax sensitivity*): If you have any wax sensitivity, list them here.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure you receive the right services.
* If you have any questions, feel free to ask your service provider.
* For urgent situations, please contact your provider directly.
