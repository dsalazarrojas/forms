# facilitator_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The facilitator evaluation form is designed to collect feedback about a facilitator's performance in presenting and facilitating a course. This form helps the facilitator understand their strengths and areas that need improvement to provide better services in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the form once you have attended a course facilitated by the person you are evaluating.
2. Review all the fields carefully before providing your input.
3. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, providing specific examples or anecdotes to support your feedback.
4. Use the available space to provide additional comments or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, optional)
* The name of the course you attended.

### 2. **Facilitator Name** (`facilitator_name`, text, optional)
* The name of the person who facilitated the course.

### 3. **Course Description** (`course_description`, text, optional)
* A brief description of the course content.

### 4. **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, optional)
* A number from 1 to 5 indicating your overall rating of the facilitator's performance.

### 5. **Facilitator Rating** (`facilitator_rating`, number, optional)
* A number from 1 to 5 indicating your rating of the facilitator's performance.

### 6. **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional)
* Any comments or suggestions you have about the facilitator's performance.

### 7. **Facilitator Support** (`facilitator_support`, text, optional)
* Comments about the facilitator's support, such as their availability, responsiveness, or patience.

### 8. **Clarity** (`facilitator_clarity`, number, optional)
* A number from 1 to 5 indicating how clear the facilitator was in explaining the course content.

### 9. **Effectiveness** (`facilitator_effectiveness`, number, optional)
* A number from 1 to 5 indicating how effective the facilitator was in conveying the course material.

### 10. **Caring** (`facilitator_caring`, number, optional)
* A number from 1 to 5 indicating how caring and supportive the facilitator was during the course.

### 11. **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional)
* Any additional comments or suggestions you have about the facilitator's performance.

Tips:
* Be specific when providing feedback, using concrete examples or anecdotes to support your comments.
* Avoid general statements or opinions.
* Keep your feedback focused on the facilitator's performance, rather than the course content.
