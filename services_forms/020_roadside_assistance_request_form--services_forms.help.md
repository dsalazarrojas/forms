# Roadside Assistance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers requesting roadside assistance services. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the category of assistance you require:
	* Service: Choose this option if you are requesting service assistance such as towing, fuel delivery, or repairs.
	* Other: Select this option if you have a different type of assistance request.
2. Provide a detailed description of your situation:
	* Describe the location where you are stranded.
	* Explain the nature of your issue (e.g., flat tire, dead battery, etc.).
	* Any additional information that can help us respond effectively.
3. Click submit to send us your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (Select One, Category): 
    Select the type of assistance you are requesting. Please choose "Service" for help such as towing or repairs, or "Other" for other types of assistance requests.
* **Description** (Text, Description):
    Provide a detailed description of your situation, including the location where you are stranded and any additional information that can help us respond effectively.
    Please be as detailed as possible, so we can dispatch assistance in a timely and efficient manner.
