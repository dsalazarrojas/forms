# Permission To Disclosure Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Permission To Disclosure Form" is a form used to gather necessary information for granting permissions for a specific purpose. It is essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure that we have all the required details to process your request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your User Information, including any relevant details about yourself.
2. Enter your Contact Information, including a valid phone number.
3. Specify the Sharing Information, if applicable.
4. Select the Permission Type from the provided options.
5. Enter the Permission Date (if applicable).
6. Enter the Permission Time (if applicable).
7. Enter the Permission Duration (if applicable).
8. Provide Organization Information (if applicable).
9. Select the Purpose of the permission request.
10. Read and agree to the Terms and Conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Information** (`user_info`, text, **required**): Fill in your name, email address, or any other relevant personal details.
- **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, **required**): Enter a valid phone number to be contacted for further information or clarification.
- **Sharing Information** (`sharing_info`, text, **optional**): If applicable, enter any details about how you plan to share the information.
- **Permission Type** (`permission_type`, select_one, **required**): Select the type of permission you are requesting.
- **Permission Date** (`permission_date`, date, **optional**): Enter the date for which you are requesting permission (if applicable).
- **Permission Time** (`permission_time`, time, **optional**): Enter the time for which you are requesting permission (if applicable).
- **Permission Duration** (`permission_duration`, number, **optional**): Enter the duration for which you are requesting permission (if applicable).
- **Organization Information** (`organization_info`, text, **optional**): If you are representing an organization, enter the relevant details.
- **Purpose** (`purpose`, select_multiple, **optional**): Select the purpose of your permission request.
- **Terms and Condition** (`terms_condition`, text, **optional**): Carefully read and agree to the terms and conditions before submitting the form.
