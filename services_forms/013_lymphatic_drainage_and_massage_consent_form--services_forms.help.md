# Lymphatic Drainage And Massage Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lymphatic Drainage And Massage Consent Form is a document that collects information from clients before undergoing lymphatic drainage and massage therapy. This form is intended to gather the client's consent and details necessary for the treatment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name and contact information in the **Client Information** field.
2. Provide your contact information, including phone number and email, in the **Contact Info** field.
3. Describe the massage and lymphatic drainage treatment you are interested in, including the location and any specific areas of focus, in the **Massage Info** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information** (`client_info`, text, required: false): This field is for you to provide your name and any other identifying information.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Please enter your contact information, including phone number and email, so we can stay in touch with you before and after your treatment.
* **Massage Info** (`massage_info`, text, required: false): Describe the massage and lymphatic drainage treatment you are interested in, including the location and any specific areas of focus, such as your back, neck, or legs.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask your practitioner.
* This form is a requirement for treatment and will be kept confidential.
