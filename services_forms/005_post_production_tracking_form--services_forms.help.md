# Post Production Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about post-production activities, allowing users to track progress, due dates, and other relevant details. It's a tool for agencies, production teams, or project managers to keep track of post-production tasks and ensure timely completion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the agency details in the **Agency Details** field.
2. Choose the **Acquisition Format** that best describes the project.
3. Select the **Due Date** for the completion of the post-production tasks.
4. Choose the **Deliverables** that are required for the project.
5. Enter the **Post Hours Budget** for the project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Agency Details (`agency_details`, text, optional)
* Enter the name of the agency handling the post-production tasks.

### Acquisition Format (`acquisition_format`, select_one, optional)
* Choose the format of the acquisition, such as Format 1 or Format 2.

### Due Date (`due_date`, date, optional)
* Enter the date by which the post-production tasks are expected to be completed.

### Deliverables (`deliverables`, select_multiple, optional)
* Select all the deliverables required for the project, such as Deliverable 1, 2, or 3.

### Post Hours Budget (`post_hours_budget`, number, optional)
* Enter the budget for the post-hours work, which is likely the time spent on post-production tasks after the initial hours have been covered.

**Tips:**

* Make sure to fill in all relevant fields to ensure accurate tracking and reporting.
* Use the correct format for the acquisition to ensure correct tracking.
* Double-check the due date and deliverables to avoid misunderstandings.
* Be accurate when entering the post hours budget to ensure proper cost tracking.
