# Winding Department Shift Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to report on the shifts for a winding department. It's a simple and concise form intended to gather essential information related to the department's work hours. The form helps track the start and end times of shifts, which is critical for administrative and operational purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the form section to begin entering your information.
2.  Enter the shift start time in the **shift_start_time** field. This is the time when the shift began.
3.  Enter the shift end time in the **shift_end_time** field. This is the time when the shift ended.
4.  In the **winding_department_shift_report** field, provide a brief description of your shift. This could include any notable events, issues, or accomplishments from your time on shift.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **shift_start_time** (time, required): Enter the time when your shift started. This is the starting point for calculating shift hours.
* **shift_end_time** (time, required): Enter the time when your shift ended. This is the ending point for calculating shift hours.
* **winding_department_shift_report** (text, required): Give a brief description of your shift, including any relevant information about what happened during your time on duty.

## Tips

* Be as accurate as possible when entering your shift start and end times to ensure correct calculations for your shift hours.
* Keep your **winding_department_shift_report** description concise and focused on the key events or issues that occurred during your shift.
* If you have any issues or concerns that require attention, make sure to include them in your shift report for further investigation.
