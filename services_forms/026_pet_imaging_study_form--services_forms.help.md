# Pet Imaging Study Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Pet Imaging Study Form is used to collect information about a pet's imaging study. This form is used to document the details of the imaging study, including the patient's name, study date and time, and imaging modality used. The information collected through this form will be used to track and manage the pet's imaging studies.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information, which includes:
	* Pet Imaging Study Form (unique identifier for the form)
	* Study Date and Time (date and time of the imaging study)
	* Owner's Name and Contact Information (owner's contact info)
2. Optionally, fill in any other information as required or relevant:
	* Patient Name and Age
	* Veterinary Clinician Name
	* Study Location
	* Patient Sex
	* Study Type
	* Pet Type
	* Notes

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Imaging Study Form** (`pet_imaging_study_form`, text, required): This field is a unique identifier for the form, used for internal tracking and management.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's name.
* **Owner Contact Info** (`owner_contact_info`, text, required): Enter the owner's contact information.
* **Veterinary Clinician Name** (`veterinary_clinician_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the veterinarian who performed the study.
* **Study Date** (`study_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the imaging study.
* **Study Time** (`study_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the imaging study.
* **Imaging Modality** (`imaging_modality`, text, required): Select the imaging modality used (e.g. MRI, CT, etc.).
* **Imaging Study Status** (`imaging_study_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple status options (Active, Inactive).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the study.
* **Pet ID** (`pet_id`, text, required): Enter the pet's ID number.
* **Study ID** (`study_id`, text, required): Enter the study ID number.
* **Patient Age** (`patient_age`, number, required): Enter the patient's age.
* **Study Location** (`study_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the study.
* **Patient Sex** (`patient_sex`, select_one, optional): Select the patient's sex (Yes, No).
* **Imaging Study Type** (`imaging_study_type`, select_one, optional): Select the study type (Yes, No).
* **Pet Type** (`pet_type`, select_one, optional): Select the pet type (Yes, No).
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, text, optional): Enter the owner's name.
* **Owner Phone** (`owner_phone`, text, optional): Enter the owner's phone number.
* **Fax** (`fax`, text, optional): Enter the fax number.
* **Imaging Study Date** (`imaging_study_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the imaging study.
* **Imaging Study Time** (`imaging_study_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the imaging study.
* **Imaging Study Status Note** (`imaging_study_status_note`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the imaging study status.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter any additional phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, text, optional): Enter any additional email address.

This form is designed to collect relevant information about the pet's imaging study, and the fields have been carefully chosen to ensure that all necessary details are captured. If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please don't hesitate to contact us.
