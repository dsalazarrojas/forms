# Electrical Services Price List - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Electrical Services Price List form is used to collect electrical services related information from customers. This form guides users to provide details on the requested service, preferred installation date, service type, schedule preference, and contact method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Service Request" field and enter the specific service you are requesting.
2. Choose your preferred "Preferred Install Date" in the calendar format.
3. Select the type of electrical service you require from the options provided in the "Service Type" field.
4. Provide your schedule preference in the "Schedule Preference" field.
5. If you have a price inquiry, provide it in the "Price Inquiry" field.
6. Select the method of contact you prefer in the "Contact Method" field.
7. If you select "Phone" or "Email" as your contact method, provide your corresponding contact number or email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Request** (`service_request`, text, required/optional: false): This field is used to specify the service you are requesting. Please enter a brief description of the service you need.
* **Preferred Install Date** (`preferred_install_date`, date, required/optional: false): This field is used to select your preferred installation date. Choose a date from the calendar format provided.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one, required/optional: false): This field is used to select the type of electrical service you require. Choose from the options provided, such as Electrical Service 1, Electrical Service 2, or Electrical Service 3.
* **Schedule Preference** (`schedule_preference`, text, required/optional: false): This field is used to provide your schedule preference. Enter your availability or any specific time slots you prefer.
* **Price Inquiry** (`price_inquiry`, text, required/optional: false): If you have a price inquiry, provide it in this field. Enter any specific price-related questions or concerns you may have.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_multiple, required: true): This field is used to select the method of contact you prefer. Choose from the options provided, such as Phone, Email, or Chat.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required/optional: false): If you select "Phone" as your contact method, provide your corresponding phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional: false): If you select "Email" as your contact method, provide your corresponding email address.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your contact method and provide the corresponding contact information.
* If you have any specific schedule preferences, provide them in the "Schedule Preference" field.
* If you have any price inquiries, provide them in the "Price Inquiry" field.
