# Food Defense Assessment Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help assess and evaluate a food defense program's strengths, weaknesses, and potential vulnerabilities. It captures information on intentional contamination vectors, assessment conducted, corrective actions, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the "Food defense program strengths" field and enter the program's current strengths.
2. Review the "Food defense program weaknesses" field and enter any areas for improvement.
3. Select the intentional contamination vectors (Food, Water, Air, or Soil) that pose a risk to the program.
4. Enter the assessment criteria used to evaluate the program.
5. If applicable, select corrective actions taken or planned.
6. Enter the details of next steps to be taken.
7. Assign a tool to the program and enter its ID.
8. Verify the assigned tool's ID and user ID.
9. Enter the form's version and name.
10. Review and update the form's version history.
