# site_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The site survey template is a form designed to collect feedback from users on their experience with our website. This information helps us to improve and refine our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Site Survey" field and enter your response.
2. Select your rating from the "Rate" field using the provided options (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
3. If you have any comments or feedback, type them in the "Comments" field.
4. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" (if you provide it, we'll be able to respond to your feedback).
6. Finally, enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (if you provide it, we'll be able to contact you if needed).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Site Survey** (`site_survey`, text, required/optional): Enter your thoughts on your experience with our website. This field is a free-text field where you can type in anything you want.
* **Rate** (`rating`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select one of the provided options (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor) to rate your overall experience with our website.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): If you have any feedback or suggestions, type them in this field. You can also use this field to ask questions or clarify any points you'd like.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name so we know who provided the feedback.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can respond to your feedback. This will allow us to contact you if we have any further questions or need more information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): If you have a phone number, you can enter it so we can contact you if needed.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible in your response to help us improve our services.
* If you have any attachments or files to support your feedback, you can upload them along with your response.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, you can contact us after submitting your feedback.
* We appreciate your input and will use it to improve our website and services.
* Please note that your feedback will be treated as anonymous unless you provide us with your name and contact information.
