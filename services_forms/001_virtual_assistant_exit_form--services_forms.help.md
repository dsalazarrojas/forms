# Virtual Assistant Exit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Assistant Exit Form is a tool used by service providers to gather information from users who are exiting a virtual assistant service. The form helps service providers understand the user's contact details, service usage, and feedback to improve their service and reach out to users who are exiting the service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Contact Details** section with your contact information, including your name and email address.
2. Provide your **Assistant Name** and **Service Details** in the relevant fields.
3. Choose your preferred **Contact Method** (Phone, Email, In-Person, or Online) to receive further communication from the service provider.
4. Enter your **Service Start Date**, **Service End Date**, and **Last Contact Date** if applicable.
5. Select your preferred **Next Contact Method** (Phone, Email, In-Person, or Online) for further communication.
6. Add any additional **Notes** regarding your experience with the virtual assistant service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Details**: This section is for you to provide your contact information, including your name and email address.
  - **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official documents or ID.
  - **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for further communication.
- **Assistant Name**: This is the name of the virtual assistant you interacted with.
- **Service Details**: Provide a brief description of the service you used.
- **Contact Method**: Select how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, In-Person, or Online).
- **Service Start Date**: Enter the date you started using the virtual assistant service.
- **Service End Date**: Enter the date you ended your usage of the virtual assistant service.
- **Last Contact Date**: Enter the date of your last contact with the virtual assistant.
- **Next Contact Method**: Select how you would like to be contacted for further communication.
- **Next Contact Date**: Enter the date you would like to be contacted again.
- **Notes**: Add any comments or feedback about your experience with the virtual assistant service.
- **Email**: This is your email address where you can be reached for further communication.
  - **Phone**: Not applicable in this case as it's a text field.
  - **In-Person**: Not applicable in this case as it's a text field.
  - **Online**: Not applicable in this case as it's a text field.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the service provider directly.
- If you have feedback or suggestions for improving the service, please include them in the **Notes** field.
