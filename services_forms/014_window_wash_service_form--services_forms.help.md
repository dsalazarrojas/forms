# Window Wash Service Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from customers for window washing services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "customer_name" field.
2. Provide your address in the "customer_address" field.
3. Select the date and time for the service in the "service_date" and "service_start_time" fields, respectively.
4. Choose the duration of the service in the "service_hours" field.
5. Specify the number of windows and stories to be cleaned.
6. Select the type of service you require ("Wash", "Clean", or "Wash and clean").
7. Enter any special payment terms or conditions in the "payment_terms" field.
8. Provide your signature to confirm your information.
9. If you have any contact information, include your phone number and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **customer_name** (`text`): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **customer_address** (`text`): Provide your address where the service will be provided.
* **service_date** (`date`): Choose the date for the service.
* **service_start_time** (`time`): Select the start time for the service.
* **service_hours** (`number`): Choose the duration of the service.
* **number_of_windows** (`number`): Specify the number of windows to be cleaned.
* **number_of_stories** (`number`): Specify the number of stories to be cleaned.
* **service_type** (`select_one`): Select the type of service required (Wash, Clean, or Wash and clean).
* **payment_terms** (`text`): Enter any special payment terms or conditions.
* **signature_of_customer** (`note`): Provide your signature to confirm your information.
* **signature_of_service_provider** (`note`): This field is intended for the service provider's signature.
* **customer_phone** (`text`): Enter your phone number if you want to be reached for any updates or questions.
* **customer_email** (`email`): Enter your email address if you want to be reached for any updates or questions.
* **payment_terms** (`text`): This field is intended for the service provider to note any special payment terms or conditions.

Note: The service provider will use the information collected in this form to provide the window washing service according to your requirements. Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and complete to avoid any delays or mistakes.

Tips: Make sure to double-check the form before submission to avoid errors. If you have any questions or need help, do not hesitate to contact the service provider.
