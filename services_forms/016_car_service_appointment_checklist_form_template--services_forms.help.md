# Car Service Appointment Checklist Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a checklist for scheduling a car service appointment.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, please follow the instructions above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer name**: Please enter the customer's name.
* **Phone number**: Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Email address**: Enter the customer's email address (if available).
* **Vehicle year**: Select the year of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle make**: Enter the vehicle's make.
* **Vehicle model**: Enter the vehicle's model.
* **License plate number**: Enter the vehicle's license plate number (if available).
* **Current mileage**: Enter the current mileage of the vehicle (if applicable).
* **Appointment date**: Select the appointment date.
* **Appointment time**: Select the appointment time.
* **Estimated duration**: Choose the estimated duration of the appointment.
* **Service type needed**: Select the type of service needed.
* **Service description or concerns**: Enter a brief description of the service or concern.
* **Appointment confirmed**: Confirm that the appointment is confirmed with the customer.
* **Customer notified**: Check if the customer has been notified of the appointment time.
* **Required parts available**: Check if the required parts are available.
* **Technician assigned**: Check if a technician has been assigned.
* **Customer authorization**: Check if customer authorization for work has been obtained.
* **Estimated cost**: Enter an estimated cost for the service (if available).
* **Special instructions or notes**: Enter any special instructions or notes.
* **Loaner vehicle needed**: Check if a loaner vehicle is needed.

Note: The form is designed to be filled out by staff members to gather necessary information for scheduling a car service appointment. It's essential to fill it out accurately to ensure that the appointment is handled correctly.
