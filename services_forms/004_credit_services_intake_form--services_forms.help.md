# Credit Services Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Credit Services Intake Form is designed to gather information from customers seeking credit services. This form aims to capture relevant details to ensure accurate and efficient processing of credit services requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Type** (`application_type`): Select the type of application you are making (Application for Loan, Credit Card, or Other).
* **Full Name** (`full_name`): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Address** (`address`): Provide your current address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Enter your phone number to ensure we can reach you.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address to facilitate communication.
* **Income Range** (`income_range`): Indicate your current income range (e.g., $0 - $100,000, etc.).
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`): Choose your current employment status (e.g., Employed, Unemployed, Self-Employed, or Retired).
* **Employment Length** (`employment_length`): Indicate the length of your employment history.
* **Employment Type** (`employment_type`): Select the type(s) of employment you have (e.g., Full-time, Part-time, Freelance, or Internship).
* **Investment Type** (`investment_type`): Identify the type(s) of investment you are interested in (e.g., Personal, Business).
* **Loan/ Credit Type** (`loan_type`): Select the type(s) of loan or credit you are applying for (e.g., Loan, Credit Card).
* **Collateral Type** (`collateral_type`): Indicate the type of collateral you are interested in (e.g., Real Estate, Vehicle).
* **Credit Score** (`credit_score`): Enter your credit score (a numerical value).
* **Credit History** (`credit_history`): Provide any relevant credit history details (e.g., past defaults, bankruptcies).
* **Employment Verification** (`employment_verification`): Confirm whether you have provided employment verification (Yes or No).
* **Income Verification** (`income_verification`): Confirm whether you have provided income verification (Yes or No).
* **Address Verification** (`address_verification`): Confirm whether you have provided address verification (Yes or No).
