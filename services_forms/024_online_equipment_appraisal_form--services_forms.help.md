# Online Equipment Appraisal Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Online Equipment Appraisal Form is a digital form designed for clients to provide information about their equipment needs. The form is used to gather relevant details from clients, including equipment descriptions, contact information, and other essential details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your equipment description in the "equipment description" field.
2. Provide your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number, in the corresponding fields.
3. Review and submit the form once you have completed all the required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Equipment Description** (`equipment description`, text, not required): This field is where you can enter a detailed description of the equipment you are looking to appraise. Please provide as much information as possible to help us better understand your equipment needs.
* **Client Name** (`client name`, text, not required): This field is where you can enter your name as the client or representative. This is an optional field, but we appreciate it for our records.
* **Client Email** (`client email`, email, not required): This field is where you can enter your email address. Please ensure it is a valid email address to enable us to contact you.
* **Client Phone** (`client phone`, text, not required): This field is where you can enter your phone number. Please format it according to your phone number format (e.g., XXX-XXX-XXXX).
* **Client Phone** (`client phone`, text, not required): This field is not required but can be useful for contact purposes.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure accurate processing of your information.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
* If you have any issues or questions while filling out the form, please don't hesitate to contact our support team.

Please note that this form is for gathering information only and does not store any sensitive or financial data. If you have any concerns or questions, please reach out to our support team for assistance.
