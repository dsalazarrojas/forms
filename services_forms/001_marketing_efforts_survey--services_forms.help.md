# marketing_efforts_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users reflect on their current marketing efforts and strategies. It will help us understand your marketing tactics, target audience, and communication channels, which will inform future marketing decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please review each field carefully and fill in the information as requested.
2. Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly, as this will help us create a more accurate picture of your marketing efforts.
3. If a field is not applicable or does not pertain to your situation, please mark it as "No" or leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. introduction
* **introduction** (`title`, `note`, not required): This field is a brief introduction to your current marketing efforts. Please provide a short description of what you've been doing in this area.

### 2. marketing_efforts
* **marketing_efforts** (`marketing_efforts`, `select_multiple`, not required): This field asks about the specific marketing efforts you're currently using. Please select the options that apply to your situation.
	+ "Yes" for efforts you're currently using
	+ "No" for efforts you're not currently using

### 3. efforts_description
* **efforts_description** (`efforts_description`, `text`, not required): This field is an open-ended question to provide more context about your marketing efforts. Please describe the specific marketing efforts you're undertaking, such as social media campaigns, email marketing, or events.

### 4. target_audience
* **target_audience** (`target_audience`, `text`, not required): This field is used to describe your target audience. Please provide more details about who you're marketing to, such as age, demographics, or interests.

### 5. marketing_channels
* **marketing_channels** (`marketing_channels`, `select_multiple`, not required): This field asks about the specific marketing channels you're using. Please select the options that apply to your situation.
	+ "Yes" for channels you're currently using
	+ "No" for channels you're not currently using

### 6. communication_channels
* **communication_channels** (`communication_channels`, `select_multiple`, not required): This field is similar to "marketing_channels", asking about the communication channels you're using. Please select the options that apply to your situation.
	+ "Yes" for channels you're currently using
	+ "No" for channels you're not currently using

## Tips

* Please be as accurate and thorough as possible when filling out this form.
* If you have any additional comments or information that might be helpful to us, please include them in the "efforts_description" field.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, feel free to reach out to us for clarification.
