# Lawn Care Service Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lawn Care Service Form is used to request and manage lawn care services. This form captures information about the service requested, the lawn condition, and the provider's details. It helps to efficiently gather the required information to provide the best service possible.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of service you need (Lawn Care, Lawn Maintenance, or Lawn Aeration).
2. Choose the lawn condition you are dealing with (Dry, Wet, or Normal).
3. Enter the date and time you would like the service to be performed.
4. Enter any additional comments or notes about the service.
5. Enter the provider's name and contact information, if applicable.
6. Enter your name and contact information.
7. Rate the service provider's performance.
8. Enter any additional notes about the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **what_service** (select one): Select the type of service you need, such as Lawn Care, Lawn Maintenance, or Lawn Aeration.
* **lawn_condition** (select multiple): Choose the condition of your lawn, such as Dry, Wet, or Normal.
* **work_date** (date): Enter the date you would like the service to be performed.
* **work_time** (time): Enter the time you would like the service to be performed.
* **notes** (text): Enter any additional comments or notes about the service.
* **service_provider_name** (text): Enter the name of the service provider.
* **service_provider_phone** (text): Enter the service provider's phone number.
* **service_provider_email** (email): Enter the service provider's email address.
* **customer_name** (text): Enter your name.
* **customer_email** (email): Enter your email address.
* **customer_phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
* **customer_address** (text): Enter your address.
* **comments** (text): Enter any additional comments or notes.
* **service_provider_rating** (number): Rate the service provider's performance (1-5).
* **service_provider_rating_notes** (note): Enter any additional notes about the service provider's performance.
* **service_provider_rating_date** (date): Enter the date of the service provider's performance.
* **service_provider_rating_time** (time): Enter the time of the service provider's performance.
* **service_provider_additional_notes** (text): Enter any additional notes about the service provider's performance.
* **service_provider_additional_fields_1** (text): Enter additional fields for the service provider.
* **service_provider_additional_fields_2** (text): Enter additional fields for the service provider.
* **service_provider_additional_fields_3** (text): Enter additional fields for the service provider.
* **service_provider_additional_fields_4** (text): Enter additional fields for the service provider.
* **service_provider_additional_fields_5** (text): Enter additional fields for the service provider.
