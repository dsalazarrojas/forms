# easter_basket_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers for an Easter basket registration. You will need to provide personal details, select basket preferences, and payment information to complete this form.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the **Introduction and Personal Details** section on page 1.
2. In the **Basket Preferences** section, select your preferred options from the dropdown menu.
3. In the **Additional Questions** section, type any additional information you'd like to share.
4. Enter your contact information in the **Contact Information** section.
5. Provide payment information in the **Payment Information** section.
6. If you have any special requests, select them in the **Special Requests** section.
7. Add any notes about your order in the **Notes** section (optional).
8. Finally, review and confirm your order in the **Confirm** section.
9. Select your preferred **Payment Method** from the dropdown menu.
10. Confirm your order again in the **Confirm 2** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Introduction and Personal Details (Page 1)
**Name (text)**: This is where you can enter your name.
**Email (text)**: This is where you can enter your email address.
**Phone (text)**: This is where you can enter your phone number.
**Message (text)**: This is where you can enter any additional information you'd like to share.

### Basket Preferences (Page 2)
- **What is your favorite color for the Easter basket?**: Choose a color that suits your style.
- **What shape of basket would you prefer?**: Select a shape that fits your personality.
- **How big would you like your basket to be?**: Choose the size that suits your needs.

### Additional Questions (Page 2)
**Additional Questions**: This is where you can type any extra information you'd like to share about your order.

### Contact Information (Page 3)
**Name (text)**: Enter your name as it should appear on the order.
**Email (text)**: Enter your email address.
**Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number.

### Payment Information (Page 4)
**Payment Amount (number)**: Enter the amount you'd like to pay.

### Special Requests (Page 4)
- **Option A**: Select this if you'd like to request something special.
- **Option B**: Select this if you'd like to request something else.

### Notes (Page 5)
**Notes**: Add any additional notes about your order here.

### Confirm (Page 5)
**Confirm**: Check this box to confirm your order.
### Special Requests (Page 5)
This is a checkbox field to select any special requests you might have. Select one or both of the options provided.
### Confirm 2 (Page 6)
**Confirm**: Check this box to confirm your order again.
### Payment Method (Page 6)
- **Bank Transfer**: Select this to pay via bank transfer.
- **Credit Card**: Select this to pay via credit card.
- **PayPal**: Select this to pay via PayPal.

### Confirm 2 (Page 6)
Confirm your order one last time before submitting.
### Confirm 2 (Page 6)
Select the payment method you'd like to use. This field is a dropdown menu with the payment options provided.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure your order is processed correctly.
- Review your order carefully before submitting it.
- If you have any issues or questions, please contact us for assistance.
