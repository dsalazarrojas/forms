# prospective_travel_client_vacation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The prospective travel client vacation form is a tool to gather information from clients about their travel plans. It is intended to be used by travel agents to provide personalized services and recommendations to the client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the travel dates start and end fields with the dates you are planning to travel.
2. Enter the travel destination in the travel destination field.
3. Enter the number of people traveling in the number of people field.
4. Enter the email of your travel agent contact in the travel agent field.
5. Enter the phone number of your travel agent contact in the travel agent phone field.
6. Choose the accommodation type that best suits your needs from the options provided.
7. If you have a specific accommodation type in mind that is not listed, enter it in the accommodation other field.
8. Any additional notes about your accommodation preferences can be entered in the accommodation note field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Travel Dates Start** (`travel_dates_start`, date, required/optional): Enter the start date of your trip.
- **Travel Dates End** (`travel_dates_end`, date, required/optional): Enter the end date of your trip.
- **Travel Destination** (`travel_destination`, text, required/optional): Enter the location you plan to travel to.
- **Number of People** (`number_of_people`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of people in your travel party.
- **Travel Agent** (`travel_agent`, email, required/optional): Enter the email of your travel agent contact.
- **Travel Agent Phone** (`travel_agent_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of your travel agent contact.
- **Accommodation Type** (`accommodation_type`, select one, required/optional): Choose the type of accommodation you are looking for (Hotel, Cottage, House, Other).
- **Accommodation Other** (`accommodation_other`, text, required/optional): Enter any other accommodation type not listed.
- **Accommodation Note** (`accommodation_note`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about your accommodation preferences.
