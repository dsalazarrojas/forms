# Remodel Furnace Relocation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a request to schedule a renovation for furnace relocation. This may include repositioning or relocating a residential or commercial furnace in a property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Upload any relevant documentation or files that support your renovation request in the "Upload File" field.
2. Select the date for your scheduled renovation in the "Schedule Renovation" field. Please use the date format "MM/DD/YYYY" (e.g., 01/15/2024).
3. Choose the type of renovation you want to schedule in the "What type of renovation would you like to schedule?" field. You can select "Residential", "Commercial", or "Both" depending on your property type.
4. In the "Select Renovation Type" field, choose all relevant options that apply to your renovation type. For example, if you selected "Residential" earlier, you may want to select "Residential" here as well.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, which will be used for communication about your renovation request.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field, which will be used for communication and scheduling purposes.

* **Schedule Renovation**: Select the date for your scheduled renovation using the date format "MM/DD/YYYY" (e.g., 01/15/2024). Please make sure the date is in the correct format to ensure accurate processing.
* **What type of renovation would you like to schedule?**: Choose the type of renovation you want to schedule. Select "Residential" for residential properties, "Commercial" for commercial properties, or "Both" if you're not sure or have a mixed-use property.

* **Email**: Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you about your renovation request.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number to facilitate communication and scheduling for your renovation request.
