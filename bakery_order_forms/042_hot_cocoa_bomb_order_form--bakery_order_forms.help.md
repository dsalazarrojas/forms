# Hot Cocoa Bomb Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hot Cocoa Bomb Order Form is a form designed for customers to place orders for hot cocoa bombs. This form is meant to collect shipping and billing information, as well as details about the order itself.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
2. Provide a valid shipping address.
3. Enter a valid billing address.
4. Select the quantity of hot cocoa bombs ordered.
5. Choose the type of hot cocoa bomb ordered.
6. Enter the item price.
7. Add any additional order notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Form** (text, required): Enter a brief description of your order, if necessary.
* **Shipping Address** (text): Enter your shipping address where the hot cocoa bomb will be delivered.
* **Shipping City** (text): Enter the city of your shipping address.
* **Shipping State** (text): Enter the state of your shipping address.
* **Shipping Zip** (text): Enter the zip code of your shipping address.
* **Billing Address** (text): Enter your billing address, which is where the payment will be processed.
* **Billing City** (text): Enter the city of your billing address.
* **Billing State** (text): Enter the state of your billing address.
* **Billing Zip** (text): Enter the zip code of your billing address.
* **Quantity** (number): Enter the quantity of hot cocoa bombs ordered.
* **Item Type** (select one):
	+ **Yes**: Select if you are ordering a hot cocoa bomb.
	+ **No**: Select if you are not ordering a hot cocoa bomb.
* **Item Price** (number): Enter the price of the hot cocoa bomb(s) ordered.
* **Order Notes** (note): Add any additional notes about your order, if necessary.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date shipping and billing information to ensure timely delivery and correct processing of payment.
* Double-check the quantity and item price before submitting the form.
* If you have any special requests or questions, include them in the Order Notes field.
