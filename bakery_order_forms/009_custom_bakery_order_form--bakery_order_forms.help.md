# Custom Bakery Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers regarding their bakery order. It is intended to be filled out by customers who wish to place an order for custom-made or specialty bakery products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your **Customer Name** in the first field.
2. Enter the **Order Date** of your order.
3. In the **Order Details** field, describe the specific items or services you would like to order.
4. If applicable, provide the **Delivery Date** for when you would like your order to be delivered.
5. Finally, provide your **Customer Email** and **Customer Phone** so we can contact you with any questions or updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required=false): This field is where you can enter your full name or the name of the person placing the order.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required=false): Enter the date you would like to receive your order. This helps us plan ahead and ensure timely delivery.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, note, required=false): Describe the specific items or services you would like to order, such as cake flavors, types of bread, or custom designs.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, time, required=false): If you have a specific time in mind for delivery, please enter it here. This helps us schedule delivery according to your needs.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required=false): Enter your email address so we can contact you with any questions or updates about your order.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required=false): Enter your phone number so we can reach out to you if we need more information about your order.

## Tips
* Please double-check your order details to ensure accuracy.
* Be as specific as possible when describing your order so we can meet your needs.
* If you have any allergies or dietary restrictions, please let us know in the **Order Details** field.
* We will contact you with any questions or updates about your order. If you don't hear from us, please call or email us to confirm your order status.
