# Donut Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donut Order Form is designed to help customers place orders for donuts. This form is a convenient way for customers to share their donut preferences and order details with the bakery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the **Customer Info** section: Please provide your contact information so we can get in touch with you to confirm your order.
2.  Select the donuts you'd like to order in the **Donut Selection** section. You can choose as many donut types as you like from the available options.
3.  In the **Date** section, select the date you'd like to pick up or have your donuts delivered.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name and contact information so we can get in touch with you to confirm your order.
*   **Donut Selection** (`donut_selection`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the donuts you'd like to order. You can choose as many donut types as you like from the available options:
    -   Sprinkles
    -   Glaze
    -   Old Fashioned
    -   Boston Creme
    -   Lemon
    -   Raspberry Filled
    -   Maple Bacon
*   **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Please select the date you'd like to pick up or have your donuts delivered.

## Tips
-   If you're unsure about any of the options or have special requests, please let us know in the comments section or contact us directly.
-   We'll be in touch with you to confirm your order details and answer any questions you may have.
-   If you have any dietary restrictions or allergies, please let us know when selecting your donuts.
