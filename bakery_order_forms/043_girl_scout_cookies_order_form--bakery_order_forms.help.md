# Girl Scout Cookies Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Girl Scout Cookies Order Form is designed for customers to order their favorite Girl Scout cookies. To complete this form, follow the instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out each field as instructed. Start by selecting your preferred Cookie Type, then enter the Quantity you would like to order. Choose a Payment Method and provide any additional Notes if necessary. Finally, confirm your order with the "Confirm" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Cookie Type** (`Cookie Type`, select_one): Select the type of Girl Scout cookie you would like to order. Choose from chocolate chip, oatmeal raisin, or peanut butter cookies.
* **Quantity** (`Quantity`, number): Enter the number of cookies you would like to order.
* **Order Date** (`Order Date`, date): Enter the date you would like to receive your cookies.
* **Cookie Price** (`Cookie Price`, number): This field is currently not visible as it's not required. If you need to know the price, please contact the relevant authority.
* **Scout Name** (`Scout Name`, text): Enter the name of the Girl Scout who will be delivering your cookies.
* **Cookie Price Total** (`Total`, number): This field is intended to show the total price of your order. This field will be calculated automatically based on the chosen options.
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, select_one): Choose how you would like to pay for your order. Select from cash, credit card, or bank transfer.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, note): Enter any additional notes about your order, such as special requests or instructions for delivery.
* **Email** (`Email`, email): Enter your email address to receive updates on your order.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text): Enter your phone number in case the delivery scout needs to contact you.
* **Confirm** (`Confirm`, select_multiple): Confirm your order by selecting 'True' to confirm or 'False' to cancel.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately to ensure smooth delivery.
* If you have any issues or questions, don't hesitate to contact the relevant authority.
