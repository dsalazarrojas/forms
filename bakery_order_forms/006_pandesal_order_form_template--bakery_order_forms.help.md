# Pandesal Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pandesal Order Form Template is designed to help customers place orders for pandesal, a sweet treat associated with Christmas. Fill in the form to provide your order details and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on each tab/page to fill in the corresponding section of the form.
2. Enter your order details in the "order_details" tab, including the type and quantity of pandesal you'd like to order.
3. Fill in your customer information in the "customer_info" tab, including your name, phone number, and other relevant details.
4. Enter your shipping address in the "shipping_address" tab.
5. Select your preferred payment method from the options in the "payment_method" tab.
6. Enter the total cost of your order in the "total_cost" tab.
7. If necessary, add any additional notes about your order in the "order_notes" tab.
8. Finally, enter your email address in the "customer_email" tab.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **order_details** (`order_details`, text, required/optional: false)
	+ This field is used to collect information about the specifics of your order, such as the type and quantity of pandesal you'd like to order.
* **customer_info** (`customer_info`, text, required/optional: false)
	+ This field is used to collect contact and demographic information about you, the customer.
* **shipping_address** (`shipping_address`, text, required/optional: false)
	+ Enter your delivery address in this field.
* **payment_method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, required/optional: false)
	+ Choose your preferred payment method from the options provided.
* **total_cost** (`total_cost`, number, required/optional: false)
	+ Enter the total cost of your order.
* **order_status** (`order_status`, text, required/optional: false)
	+ This field may be used to keep track of the order's progress or status.
* **order_notes** (`order_notes`, note, required/optional: false)
	+ Add any additional notes about your order if necessary.
* **customer_email** (`customer_email`, email, required/optional: false)
	+ Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you about your order.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you've selected the correct payment method and entered the correct total cost.
* If you have any specific requests or questions, use the "order_notes" field to provide additional information.
