# Cake Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cake Inquiry Form is used to collect information from customers who are interested in placing a custom cake order with our bakery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, contact email, and contact phone number (if provided) on the first page.
2. Enter the date for the event where the cake is needed.
3. Choose the design or theme for your custom cake.
4. Select the number of people you plan to serve.
5. Optionally, provide an approximate budget for the cake.
6. Add any additional details or reference images that can help us better understand your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inquirer name**: Enter your name as the person placing the order.
* **Contact email**: Enter your email address to enable us to follow up with you quickly.
* **Contact phone**: If you provide your phone number, we will be able to contact you faster.
* **Event date**: Enter the date when the cake is required.
* **Cake style**: Choose the design or theme for your custom cake.
* **Servings required**: Enter the number of people you plan to serve.
* **Budget**: Enter an approximate budget for the cake, if you have one.
* **Additional details**: Provide any reference images or notes that can help us better understand your order.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* Be as specific as possible when filling in the "Cake style" and "Additional details" fields to help us provide the best possible service.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on anything, feel free to contact us directly.
