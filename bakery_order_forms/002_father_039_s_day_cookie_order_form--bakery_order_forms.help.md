# Father's Day Cookie Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Father's Day Cookie Order Form is a tool used to place custom orders for Father's Day cookies. This form is designed to collect information about the order, including shape, size, category, and other details to ensure that your custom cookies are made correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the order details, including your name and contact information.
2. Select the cookie shape and size that you would like to order.
3. Enter any special messages or requests you may have.
4. Choose the category of your order.
5. If you have a specific birthday, please enter the date.
6. If you have any time constraints, please enter the desired time.
7. Fill out any additional comments about your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details** (`father_s_day_cookie_order_form_details`): Enter your name and contact information to help us process your order correctly.
* **Cookie Shape** (`cookie_order_form_cookie_shape`): Choose the shape of your cookie from the options:
	+ Ball
	+ Heart
	+ Circle
	+ Square
* **Cookie Size** (`cookie_order_form_size`): Choose the size of your cookie from the options:
	+ Large
	+ Medium
	+ Small
* **Cookie Message** (`cookie_order_form_message`): Enter any special messages or requests for your cookies.
* **Special Request** (`cookie_order_form_special_request`): If you have any special requests, enter them here.
* **Category** (`father_s_day_cookie_order_form_category`): Choose the category of your order from the options:
	+ Bakery
	+ Family
* **Birthday** (`father_s_day_cookie_order_form_birthday`): If you have a specific birthday, enter the date.
* **Time** (`father_s_day_cookie_order_form_time`): If you have any time constraints, enter the desired time.
* **Name** (`father_s_day_cookie_order_form_name`): Enter your name for the order details.
* **Email** (`father_s_day_cookie_order_form_email`): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`father_s_day_cookie_order_form_phone`): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Comments** (`father_s_day_cookie_order_form_comments`): Enter any additional comments about your order.
