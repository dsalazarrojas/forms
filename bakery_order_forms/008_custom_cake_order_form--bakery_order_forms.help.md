# Custom Cake Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Custom Cake Order Form is a form designed to gather information from customers for custom cake orders. Customers can provide their order details, date, time, and phone number to ensure efficient order fulfillment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "order_details" field with a brief description of your custom cake order.
2. Select the "order_date" field to enter the date when you would like to pick up or receive your order.
3. Choose the "order_time" field to select the time when you would like to pick up or receive your order.
4. Enter your "order_phone" in the corresponding field, so we can contact you if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Order Details**
`order_details` (required: false)
Enter a brief description of your custom cake order, including any specific designs, flavors, or decorations you would like to include.

### **Order Date**
`order_date` (required: false)
Select the date when you would like to pick up or receive your order.

### **Order Time**
`order_time` (required: false)
Choose the time when you would like to pick up or receive your order.

### **Order Phone**
`order_phone` (required: false)
Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter a valid date and time for your order.
- Use a clear and concise description for your order details.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, please do not hesitate to contact us.
