# Holiday Treat Pickup Invitation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Holiday Treat Pickup Invitation form is designed to gather essential information from customers to facilitate the pickup of holiday treats.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select your preferred "Holiday Treat Type" from the list.
5. Choose the date and time of your preferred pickup in the "Pickup Date" and "Pickup Time" fields.
6. Indicate whether or not you will RSVP by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "RSVP" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Please enter your name to help us identify you for pickup purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address so we can communicate with you about your holiday treat pickup.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you have a preferred phone number, please enter it so we can contact you about your pickup.
* **Holiday Treat Type** (`holiday_treat_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of holiday treat you would like to pick up. Please choose one of the options provided.
* **Pickup Date** (`pickup_date`, date, required): Choose the date you would like to pick up your holiday treat.
* **Pickup Time** (`pickup_time`, time, required): Select the time you would like to pick up your holiday treat.
* **RSVP** (`rsvp`, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether or not you will be able to attend the pickup event by selecting "Yes" or "No". If you are unable to attend, we will cancel your order.
