# Cake Order Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers who want to place a custom cake order, and it helps the bakery gather all the necessary information to create a cake that meets their expectations and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name, email address, and phone number as requested.
2. Choose a date for your event and the type of event.
3. Provide the approximate number of servings needed.
4. Select your cake style preference and favorite flavors.
5. If you have any dietary restrictions, let us know.
6. Choose how you would like to consult with the designer and when you are available for the consultation.
7. If you have any inspiration photos, let us know.
8. Decide if you need delivery and where you would like it delivered.
9. Let us know how you heard about us.
10. Choose your level of flexibility for the timeline.
11. If you have any additional questions or comments, please let us know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Please fill in your full name as you would like it to be displayed on the order.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address to receive updates and information about your order.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter a contact number in case we need to reach you.
* **Inquiry Date** (`inquiry_date`, `date`, required): Select the current date for your event or consultation.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required): Choose the date of your event or celebration.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of event you are having, such as a birthday or wedding.
* **Number of Servings Needed** (`number_of_servings`, `number`, not required): Enter the approximate number of people attending your event.
* **Cake Style Preference** (`cake_style_preference`, `select_one`, not required): Choose a style that interests you, such as traditional or modern.
* **Flavor Preferences** (`flavor_preferences`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select one or more flavors you like.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, `select_one`, not required): Choose an approximate budget for your order.
* **Design Inspiration** (`design_inspiration`, `text`, not required): Describe your vision or ideas for the cake design.
* **Color Preferences** (`color_preferences`, `text`, not required): Enter your preferred colors for the cake.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, `select_multiple`, not required): If you have any dietary restrictions, let us know.
* **Consultation Availability** (`consultation_availability`, `select_multiple`, not required): Choose when you are available for consultation.
* **Preferred Consultation Method** (`consultation_method`, `select_one`, not required): Select how you would like to consult with the designer.
* **Have Inspiration Photos** (`have_inspiration_photos`, `select_one`, not required): If you have inspiration photos, let us know.
* **Delivery Needed** (`delivery_needed`, `select_one`, not required): Decide if you need delivery and where you would like it delivered.
* **Delivery Location** (`delivery_location`, `text`, not required): Enter the address where you would like the cake delivered.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_did_you_hear`, `select_one`, not required): Let us know where you found us.
* **Timeline Flexibility** (`timeline_flexibility`, `select_one`, not required): Choose how flexible you are with your timeline.
* **Additional Questions** (`additional_questions`, `text`, not required): If you have any additional questions or comments, please let us know.
