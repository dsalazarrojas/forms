# Custom Cake Order Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Custom Cake Order Inquiry Form is designed to collect information from customers who are interested in placing a custom cake order. The form gathers details about the customer, their order preferences, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Make sure to provide detailed and accurate information about your preferences.
3. Review your information before submitting the form to ensure it is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information (1)**: This is where you will enter your name, phone number, and other relevant contact information. Please ensure that you provide a valid contact number so that we can reach out to you if needed.
* **Order Details (2)**: This is where you will enter the details of your custom cake order. Describe the design, size, and any specific preferences you have for your cake.
* **Delivery Information (3)**: This is where you will enter information about when and how you would like to receive your custom cake. Please ensure that you provide accurate delivery information so that we can ensure timely delivery.
* **Contact Information (4)**: This is where you will enter your address, phone number, and email address. This is the information we will use to contact you to confirm your order and provide updates on the status of your custom cake.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, feel free to contact us.
* Please allow up to 24 hours for us to contact you to confirm your order details.
* If you have any special requests or requirements that are not listed in the form, please let us know and we will do our best to accommodate your needs.
