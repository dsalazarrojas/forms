# homemade_cake_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The homemade_cake_order_form is a tool used to collect information for custom bakery orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the order details (page 1).
2. Select the order type (cake or cupcakes) and any special orders on page 2.
3. Provide your contact information, including address, phone number, and email on page 3.
4. Choose a delivery date and time on page 4.
5. Add any additional notes about your order on page 5.
6. Select your preferred payment method on page 6.
7. Confirm your order with the bakery details on page 7.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details (order_details)**: Enter a brief description of your order.
* **Orderer (orderer)**: Enter your name as it should appear on the order receipt.
* **Address (address)**: Enter your address for delivery purposes.
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter your phone number for contact information.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address for communication about your order.
* **Order Type (order_type)**: Select the type of order you are placing (cake or cupcakes).
* **Special Orders (special_orders)**: Check any special orders you would like to add to your cake (e.g. chocolate, fruit, etc.).
* **Delivery Date (delivery_date)**: Select the date you would like to pick up or have your order delivered.
* **Delivery Time (delivery_time)**: Select the time you would like to pick up or have your order delivered.
* **Notes (notes)**: Add any additional notes or instructions about your order.
* **Payment Method (payment_method)**: Select how you would like to pay for your order (cash, check, or bank transfer).
* **Bakery (bakery)**: Enter the name of the bakery making your order (not required).
* **Baker (baker)**: Enter the name of the baker creating your order (not required).
* **Order Status (order_status)**: Select the status of your order (new, pending, or completed). 

Note that some fields may not be relevant for a specific order type, such as the 'Bakery' and 'Baker' fields which appear to be for internal use only.
