# Bread and Pastry Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to place an order for bread and pastries from a bakery or bakery-like business. It collects information about the customer, their order details, and any special requests or requirements they may have.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the customer details: Enter your full name, email address, and phone number to ensure smooth communication and order confirmation.
2. Select the order type: Choose between pickup or delivery options to specify how you want to receive your order.
3. Choose the date and time for pickup or delivery: Enter the date and time you prefer for your order.
4. Select the bread and pastries: Choose the types and quantities of bread and pastries you want to order from the provided options.
5. Specify quantities for each bread and pastry: Enter the quantity for each bread and pastry type you selected.
6. Make special requests or modifications: If you have any custom requests or modifications, enter them here.
7. Select any dietary requirements: Choose any dietary requirements such as gluten-free, dairy-free, or vegan if applicable.
8. Enter delivery address and instructions (if applicable): If you selected delivery as your order type, enter your full delivery address and any special instructions.
9. Choose a payment method: Select how you want to pay for your order from the provided options.
10. Add any order notes: Enter any additional notes or comments about your order.
11. Accept terms and conditions: Confirm that you accept the bakery's terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Full Name**: Your full name as it appears on your identification.
  * Type: text
  * Required: yes

* **Email Address**: Your email address for order confirmation and communication.
  * Type: email
  * Required: yes

* **Phone Number**: Your contact number for order questions or confirmation.
  * Type: text
  * Required: yes

* **Order Type**: Choose between pickup or delivery options.
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: yes

* **Pickup or Delivery Date**: When do you need your order.
  * Type: date
  * Required: yes

* **Pickup or Delivery Time**: Preferred time.
  * Type: time
  * Required: yes

* **Bread Selection**: Select the bread types you want to order.
  * Type: select_multiple
  * Required: yes

* **Pastry Selection**: Select the pastry types you want to order.
  * Type: select_multiple
  * Required: yes

* **Bread Quantities**: Specify quantity for each bread type.
  * Type: text
  * Required: yes

* **Pastry Quantities**: Specify quantity for each pastry type.
  * Type: text
  * Required: yes

* **Special Requests**: Any custom requests or modifications.
  * Type: text
  * Required: no

* **Dietary Requirements**: Select any dietary requirements (e.g., gluten-free, dairy-free, vegan).
  * Type: select_multiple
  * Required: yes

* **Delivery Address**: Full delivery address (if applicable).
  * Type: text
  * Required: no

* **Delivery Instructions**: Any special delivery instructions (if applicable).
  * Type: text
  * Required: no

* **Payment Method**: How will you pay for your order.
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: yes

* **Order Notes**: Any additional notes or comments about your order.
  * Type: text
  * Required: no

* **Terms and Conditions**: Confirm that you accept the bakery's terms and conditions.
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: yes
