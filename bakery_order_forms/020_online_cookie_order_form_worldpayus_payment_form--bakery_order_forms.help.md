# Online Cookie Order Form WorldPayUS Payment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers for online orders of cookies from a bakery. Users can select the type of cookie they want, provide their contact details, and choose a payment method. The form also includes fields for bakery information and notes for special orders or payments.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the type of cookie you want to order by clicking on the "Cookie Order Form" option.
2. Choose your WorldPayUS payment method.
3. Enter your name, email address, phone number, and any special notes for your order.
4. Provide any special payment notes or describe your payment method.
5. Enter any special notes or instructions for your order.
6. Enter the description of your payment method.
7. Add a link to a picture of the cookie or bakery.
8. Enter the price of the cookie.
9. Enter the currency for the cookie price.
10. Enter a message for the customer.
11. Enter the name of the bakery.
12. Enter the address of the bakery.
13. Enter the phone number of the bakery.
14. Enter the email of the bakery.
15. Enter the time it takes to bake the cookie.
16. Enter the method used to produce the cookie.
17. Enter the number of calories in the cookie.
18. Enter the serving size of the cookie.
19. Add an image for the bakery order form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Cookie Order Form** (`cookie_order_form`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the type of cookie you want to order. You will be presented with multiple options; choose the one that best matches your preference.
* **WorldPayUS Payment Method** (`worldpay_payment_method`, select_one, required: false): Choose your WorldPayUS payment method. This is likely a payment gateway or service used by the bakery.
* **Baked Goods** (`baked_goods`, select_one, required: false): Select "Yes" if you are ordering baked goods, or "No" if you are not.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required: false): Enter the quantity of the cookie you want to order.
* **Submit** (`submit`, note, required: false): A placeholder field; you don't need to complete this field. It's just a note.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required: false): Select "Yes" if you have a payment method, or "No" if you don't.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your name for the order.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for the order.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for the order.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, note, required: false): Enter any special notes or instructions for your order.
* **Payment Notes** (`payment_notes`, note, required: false): Enter any special payment notes or instructions.
* **Payment Method Description** (`payment_method_description`, text, required: false): Enter a description of your payment method.
* **Cookie Picture** (`cookie_picture`, text, required: false): Enter a link to a picture of the cookie.
* **Cookie Price** (`cookie_price`, number, required: false): Enter the price of the cookie.
* **Cookie Price Currency** (`cookie_price_currency`, text, required: false): Enter the currency for the price of the cookie.
* **Customer Message** (`customer_message`, text, required: false): Enter a message for the customer.
* **Bakery Name** (`bakery_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the bakery.
* **Bakery Address** (`bakery_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of the bakery.
* **Bakery Phone** (`bakery_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the bakery.
* **Bakery Email** (`bakery_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email of the bakery.
* **Cookie Baking Time** (`cookie_baking_time`, text, required: false): Enter the time it takes to bake the cookie.
* **Cookie Production Method** (`cookie_production_method`, text, required: false): Enter the method used to produce the cookie.
* **Cookie Calories** (`cookie_calories`, text, required: false): Enter the number of calories in the cookie.
* **Cookie Serving Size** (`cookie_serving_size`, text, required: false): Enter the serving size of the cookie.
* **Bakery Order Form Image** (`bakery_order_form_image`, text, required: false): Enter an image for the bakery order form.
