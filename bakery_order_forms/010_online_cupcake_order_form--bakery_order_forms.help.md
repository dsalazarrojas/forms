# Online Cupcake Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Cupcake Order Form is designed to collect essential information for our customers' online orders. This form helps us process orders accurately and efficiently, ensuring timely delivery or pickup of our delicious cupcakes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your **Customer Name** and **Customer Email** fields with accurate and valid contact information.
2. Choose a **Pick-up Date** for when you'd like to collect your order.
3. Select a **Pick-up Time** that suits your schedule.
4. If you prefer your order to be delivered, enter the **Delivery Date** and **Delivery Time** accordingly.
5. Enter your **Address** where you'd like to receive your order.
6. Select your desired **Flavor** for your order.
7. Specify the number of cupcakes you'd like to purchase in the **Quantity** field.
8. Choose your preferred **Payment Method**: Cash, Credit Card, or PayPal.
9. Enter any additional **Payment Details** if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Enter the name of the person placing the order.
* **Customer Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted for order updates.
* **Pick-up Date**: Select a date when you'd like to collect your order.
* **Pick-up Time**: Choose a time that suits your schedule for order collection.
* **Delivery Date**: If you prefer delivery, enter the date when you'd like to receive your order.
* **Delivery Time**: Select a time that suits your schedule for order delivery.
* **Address**: Enter the address where you'd like to receive your order.
* **Flavor**: Choose the flavor of cupcakes you'd like to purchase.
* **Quantity**: Specify the number of cupcakes you'd like to order.
* **Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method: Cash, Credit Card, or PayPal.
* **Payment Details**: If using a payment method that requires additional information, such as a credit card number or PayPal reference number, enter it here.
* **Payment Method**: Note that payment method is required but optional fields for payment details will be prompted after selection.
Please fill in all relevant fields to ensure a smooth order processing experience.
