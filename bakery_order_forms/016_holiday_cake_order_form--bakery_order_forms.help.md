# Holiday Cake Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Holiday Cake Order Form is a tool designed to help customers place orders for cakes and other desserts during holidays and special occasions. Use this guide to learn how to complete the form accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To place an order, start by providing the necessary details about the cake.
2. Then, provide information about the delivery preferences.
3. Next, enter any additional order notes and payment details.
4. Finally, review your completed form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Cake Details** (`1`, text, optional): Enter the type of cake you are interested in ordering (e.g., fruit cake, chocolate cake, etc.).
* **delivery_details** (`2`, text, optional): Provide instructions for the delivery of your order, such as preferred delivery date and time, delivery location, or any special instructions.
* **order_details** (`3`, text, optional): Enter any special requests or instructions for your order, such as specific flavor combinations or special messages to be written on the cake.
* **billing_details** (`4`, text, optional): Enter your billing information, including name, address, and contact details.
* **Order Notes** (`5`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your order that the baker should know.
* **Payment Details** (`6`, text, optional): Enter your payment method and any relevant financial information (e.g., total cost, payment due date).
