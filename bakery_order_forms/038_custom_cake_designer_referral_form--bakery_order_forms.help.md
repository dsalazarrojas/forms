# Custom Cake Designer Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Custom Cake Designer Referral Form is designed for customers to refer a friend or a potential client to our custom cake design services. This form helps us understand how they found us and what made them interested in our services. It's a crucial tool for us to evaluate our marketing strategy and improve our customer acquisition process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, please make sure you have all the necessary information ready. This includes your name and contact details, as well as the details of your referral.
2. Fill in the required fields: 
   - **Custom Cake Designer Referral Form Title**: This is a text field for you to briefly describe the referral opportunity.
   - **Bakery Name**: Please fill in the name of the bakery that referred you to us.
   - **Designer Name**: If you are a customer of ours, please mention your designer's name, if known.
3. Fill in the optional fields:
   - **Referral Source**: Describe where you found out about our services.
   - **Portfolio URL**: If your referred friend/designer has a portfolio, please share the URL.
   - **Referral Details**: Share more information about your referral opportunity.
   - **Contact Email**: Enter the email of your referral's contact.
   - **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact information for us to follow up on the referral.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title (Custom Cake Designer Referral Form)** (`id: 1`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Briefly describe the referral opportunity.
* **Bakery Name** (`id: 2`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter the name of the bakery that referred you to us.
* **Designer Name** (`id: 3`, `type: text`, `required: false`): If you are a customer, mention the designer's name if known.
* **Referral Source** (`id: 4`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Describe where you found out about our services.
* **Portfolio URL** (`id: 5`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Share the URL of your referral's portfolio if they have one.
* **Referral Details** (`id: 6`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Provide more information about your referral opportunity.
* **Contact Email** (`id: 7`, `type: email`, `required: false`): Enter the referral's contact email.
* **Contact Phone** (`id: 8`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter your contact phone number for follow-up.
* **Designer Portfolio** (`id: 9`, `type: select_multiple`, `required: false`): Check if the referral has a designer portfolio.
* **More Designer Portfolios** (`id: 10`, `type: select_multiple`, `required: false`): Check if the referral has more designer portfolios.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all the required fields to help us process your referral efficiently.
* If the referral is a customer of ours, please mention the designer's name and any other relevant details.
* If you have any questions or need help filling out this form, please don't hesitate to contact us.
