# Snack Tray Kit Pre Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Snack Tray Kit Pre Order Form Template is used to collect information for a Snack Tray Kit Pre Order. This form is intended for customers to provide their contact information and order details to ensure a smooth and efficient order processing experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct category for your order from the provided options (Bakery, Produce, or Holiday).
2. Enter your name and email address in the corresponding fields.
3. If you have a phone number, provide it in the corresponding field.
4. Select the date and time for your order.
5. Choose the type of product you would like to order from the provided options (Fresh Produce, Holiday Kit, or Snack Tray Kit).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`Category`, select_one, required): Select the category of your order from the provided options (Bakery, Produce, or Holiday).
* **Customer Name** (`Customer Name`, text, optional): Enter your name to be used for order processing and communication.
* **Customer Email** (`Customer Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to receive updates and communication about your order.
* **Customer Phone** (`Customer Phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number to receive phone calls or updates about your order.
* **Order Date** (`Order Date`, date, optional): Select the date for your order.
* **Order Time** (`Order Time`, time, optional): Select the time for your order.
* **Product** (`Product`, select_one, required): Choose the type of product you would like to order from the provided options (Fresh Produce, Holiday Kit, or Snack Tray Kit).
