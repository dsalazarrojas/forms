# Online Cupcake Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Cupcake Order Form is designed to collect information from customers for their online cupcake orders. This form is meant to be completed by individuals who want to place a custom cupcake order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Enter your email address in the **Email** field. Please use the format `your_name@email.com`.
4. Provide your address in the **Address** field.
5. Select the date of your order in the **Order Date** field.
6. Choose the time of your order in the **Order Time** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional):
	+ This is the field where you enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional):
	+ This is the field where you enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional):
	+ This is the field where you enter your email address. Please use the format `your_name@email.com`.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional):
	+ This is the field where you enter your address.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional):
	+ This is the field where you select the date of your order.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, optional):
	+ This is the field where you select the time of your order.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
