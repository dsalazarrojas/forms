# easter_treats_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Easter Treats Order Form" is a form designed for customers or clients to order their preferred Easter treats.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the form title: Enter a brief title for your order, e.g., "Order for Easter Family Gathering".
2. Select your category: Choose the category that best suits your order, e.g., "Bakery Order Forms" or "Other".
3. Provide a description: Briefly describe your order, e.g., "Order for a large family gathering".
4. Review and submit: Review your information and click the submit button to place your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
## Page 1
* **Easter Treats Order Form** (`title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title for your order, e.g., "Order for Easter Family Gathering".
* **category** (`easter_treats_form_category`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the category that best suits your order, e.g., "Bakery Order Forms" or "Other".
* **description** (`easter_treats_description`, text, required): Briefly describe your order, e.g., "Order for a large family gathering".

## Tips
* If you are unsure about the category, choose "Other" to provide more details in the description field.
* If you need to place a large order, please contact the bakery directly to discuss your needs.
* The form will be reviewed and processed manually. Please allow up to [insert timeframe] for processing.
