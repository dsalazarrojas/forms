# Yummlies Cupcakes - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to take orders for "Yummlies Cupcakes" online. Customers can provide their details, select their order type, choose a date and time for pick-up, and add any additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your **Customer Name** (`Customer Name`, `text`, required) to identify you as the customer.
2. Enter your **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required) for contact purposes.
3. Enter your **Email** (`Email`, `email`, optional) for us to contact you, but this is not required.
4. Select the **Order Type** (`Order Type`, `select_multiple`, required) to indicate the type of order you want:
	* Cupcake
	* Cake
	* Cupcake Cake
5. Choose the **Date** (`Date`, `date`, required) and **Time** (`Time`, `time`, required) for pick-up.
6. Optionally, enter the **Quantity** (`Quantity`, `number`, optional) if you're ordering more than one item.
7. Enter the **Price** (`Price`, `number`, optional) if you want to specify a custom price for your order.
8. Enter the **Total Price** (`Total Price`, `number`, optional) if you want to calculate the total price for your order.
9. Add any **Note** (`Note`, `note`, optional) for the bakery to know about your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`Customer Name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the person placing the order.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for us to contact you, but this is not required.
* **Order Type** (`Order Type`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more of:
	+ Cupcake
	+ Cake
	+ Cupcake Cake
* **Date** (`Date`, `date`, required): Choose the date you want to pick up your order.
* **Time** (`Time`, `time`, required): Choose the time you want to pick up your order.
* **Quantity** (`Quantity`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of items you want to order.
* **Price** (`Price`, `number`, optional): Enter the custom price for your order.
* **Total Price** (`Total Price`, `number`, optional): Calculate the total price for your order.
* **Note** (`Note`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes for the bakery about your order.
