# dessert_bar_service_agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a dessert bar service. It's used to gather details about an event where a catering or event planning business will be providing their services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields and choose any applicable options. Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure the form is submitted correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **event_name** (1, text, optional): Enter the name of the event.
* **event_date** (2, date, required): Enter the date of the event in a standard date format (e.g., MM/DD/YY).
* **event_time** (3, time, optional): Enter the time of the event (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **service_terms** (4, text, required): Describe the specific services you will be providing, including the type of desserts and any other relevant details.
* **dessert_type** (5, select_one, optional): Choose the type of dessert that will be served (cakes, favors, or other).
* **bakery_name** (6, text, required): Enter the name of the bakery providing the service.
* **contact** (7, text, required): Enter the contact person's name and phone number.
* **email** (8, email, required): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **approval** (9, select_multiple, optional): Choose the approval status of the service (approved or pending).
* **notes** (10, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the event or service.
* **agreement** (11, select_one, required): Choose whether you accept or reject the service terms.
* **terms** (12, text, required): Review and agree to the service terms.
* **name** (13, text, required): Enter your name.
* **email_2** (14, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **submit** (15, text, required): Submit the form to complete the service agreement.

## Tips
Make sure to review and agree to the service terms before submitting the form. This form requires all required fields to be filled in order to be submitted correctly.
