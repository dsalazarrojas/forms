# paul_rhodes_bakery_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The paul_rhodes_bakery_order_form is a form used to collect customer information and order details for Paul Rhodes' bakery. It is intended for customers to place orders, provide their contact information, and include any additional notes about their order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer's name in the **customer_name** field.
2. Enter the date of the order in the **order_date** field.
3. Enter the time of the order in the **order_time** field.
4. Describe the bakery order in the **bakery_order** field.
5. Enter the customer's email address in the **customer_email** field.
6. Enter the customer's phone number in the **customer_phone** field.
7. If you have any additional information or notes about the order, enter them in the **order_notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **customer_name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the person placing the order.
- **order_date** (date, optional): Enter the date of the order.
- **order_time** (time, optional): Enter the time of the order.
- **bakery_order** (text, optional): Describe the order you are placing, including any specific products or items you would like to order.
- **customer_email** (email, optional): Enter the customer's email address.
- **customer_phone** (text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
- **order_notes** (note, optional): If you have any additional information or notes about the order, enter them here.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* If you have any specific requests or requests for special items, make sure to include them in the **bakery_order** field.
* If you have any questions or need assistance with your order, contact the bakery's support team.
