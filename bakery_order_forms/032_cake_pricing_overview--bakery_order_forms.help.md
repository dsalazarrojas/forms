# Cake Pricing Overview - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cake Pricing Overview form is designed to collect information from customers for the purpose of getting a cake price quote. It will help us understand their preferences, requirements, and expectations to provide an accurate pricing estimate.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal details, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the date of your event or inquiry.
3. Choose the type of event (e.g., birthday, wedding, graduation).
4. Specify the number of guests you plan to serve.
5. Indicate your preferred cake type, size, and design complexity.
6. Choose any special features or flavors you are considering.
7. If you have any dietary requirements or restrictions, please select accordingly.
8. If you need delivery, choose your preferred method and distance.
9. Select your budget range and timeline flexibility.
10. Indicate your preference for consultation and any additional requests or questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your contact information.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for pricing estimates or updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number where we can contact you for further details.
* **Inquiry Date** (`inquiry_date`, date, required): Select the date of your event or inquiry. This will help us understand your timeline.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Select the date of your event. If different from the inquiry date, please indicate.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of event (e.g., birthday, wedding, graduation).
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, number, required): Specify the number of guests you plan to serve.
* **Cake Type** (`cake_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of cake you are considering (e.g., layer cake, sheet cake).
* **Cake Size Preference** (`cake_size_preference`, select_one, false): Select your preferred cake size (if not sure, please choose "Not sure").
* **Flavor Preferences** (`flavor_preferences`, select_multiple, false): Choose the flavors you are considering (e.g., vanilla, chocolate).
* **Design Complexity** (`design_complexity`, select_one, required): Choose the level of design complexity you prefer (e.g., simple, elaborate).
* **Special Features** (`special_features`, select_multiple, false): Select any special features you would like to include (e.g., fresh flowers, edible images).
* **Dietary Requirements** (`dietary_requirements`, select_multiple, false): Specify any dietary requirements or restrictions (e.g., gluten-free, vegan).
* **Delivery Needed** (`delivery_needed`, select_one, required): If you need delivery, choose your preferred method (e.g., yes, no, maybe).
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, false): If you selected delivery, enter your delivery address.
* **Delivery Distance** (`delivery_distance`, select_one, false): If you need delivery, choose your preferred distance range.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, select_one, false): Select your approximate budget range.
* **Timeline Flexibility** (`timeline_flexibility`, select_one, false): Indicate how flexible you are with your timeline.
* **Consultation Preference** (`consultation_preference`, select_one, false): Choose how you would like to discuss details (e.g., in person, phone call, email).
* **Additional Requests** (`additional_requests`, text, false): If you have any other questions or notes, please include them here.
