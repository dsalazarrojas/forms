# Wedding Cake Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals interested in inquiring about wedding cake details. The form aims to collect essential details that will help us understand their wedding cake preferences and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for each field.
2. Provide accurate and relevant details for the wedding cake inquiry.
3. Ensure that all the fields are filled in correctly to avoid any confusion or errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Wedding Cake Inquiry**
* This field is where you can enter your name and any other relevant details about your wedding cake inquiry.
* Please enter a clear and concise description of your wedding cake requirements.

### 2. **Wedding Cake Inquiry Details**
* This field is where you can provide any additional details about your wedding cake preferences.
* You can include information such as cake flavors, sizes, designs, or any specific requirements you have.

### 3. **Confirmation**
* This field is for verification purposes, to confirm that you have filled out the form correctly.
* Please re-enter your information in the confirmation field to ensure accuracy and prevent any errors.

## Tips
* Please double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid any mistakes.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us directly.
