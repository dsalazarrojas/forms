# Bakery Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer information and order details for a bakery order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Full Name** in the "Full Name" field. This field is required.
2. Enter your **Email Address** in the "Email Address" field. This field is required.
3. Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Phone Number" field. This field is required.
4. Select a date for the **Order Date** in the "Order Date" field. This field is required.
5. Choose a date for the **Desired Pickup or Delivery Date** in the "Desired Pickup or Delivery Date" field. This field is required.
6. In the "Select Bakery Products" field, choose all the bakery products you want to order. You can select multiple options. This field is required.
7. If you have ordered any of the bakery products, fill in the **Bread Type and Quantity**, **Croissant Type and Quantity**, **Donut Type and Quantity**, **Cake Specifications**, **Cupcake Specifications** fields with the corresponding details.
8. If you have ordered **Custom Items**, fill in the **Custom Decorations or Special Requests** field with any additional requests.
9. Check the **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** field if applicable.
10. Choose a **Fulfillment Option** from the "Fulfillment Option" field. You can choose between "Pickup at Bakery", "Home Delivery", or "Will Arrange". This field is required.
11. If you have a preferred time for delivery, enter it in the "Preferred Time" field.
12. Add any **Special Notes or Comments** in the "Special Notes or Comments" field.
13. If you want to estimate the total cost, enter it in the "Estimated Total Cost" field.
14. Choose a **Payment Preference** from the "Payment Preference" field. You can choose between "Cash", "Card", "Check", or "PayPal". This field is not required.
15. Finally, confirm your order by selecting "I Confirm" in the "I Confirm This Order and Will Complete Payment" field. This field is required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
- **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
- **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
- **Order Date**: Select the date you would like to receive your order.
- **Desired Pickup or Delivery Date**: Select the date you would like to receive your order.
- **Select Bakery Products**: Choose all the bakery products you want to order.
- **Bread Type and Quantity**: If you ordered bread, enter the type and quantity.
- **Croissant Type and Quantity**: If you ordered croissants, enter the type and quantity.
- **Donut Type and Quantity**: If you ordered donuts, enter the type and quantity.
- **Cake Specifications**: If you ordered a cake, enter the specifications (size, flavor, decoration).
- **Cupcake Specifications**: If you ordered a cupcake, enter the specifications (flavor, decoration).
- **Custom Decorations or Special Requests**: If you have any special requests for custom items, enter them here.
- **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies**: Check if you have any dietary restrictions or allergies.
- **Fulfillment Option**: Choose how you want to receive your order (Pickup at Bakery, Home Delivery, or Will Arrange).
- **Preferred Time**: Enter your preferred time for delivery.
- **Special Notes or Comments**: Add any special notes or comments.
- **Estimated Total Cost**: Enter an estimate of the total cost.
- **Payment Preference**: Choose how you want to pay for your order (Cash, Card, Check, or PayPal).
- **I Confirm This Order and Will Complete Payment**: Confirm your order and complete payment.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Double-check your order details before submitting.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the bakery directly.
