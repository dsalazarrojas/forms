# Thanksgiving DIY Kits Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to place orders for custom-made or pre-made Thanksgiving DIY kits. By filling out this form, you will be able to provide us with your personal information and choose the kits you wish to purchase.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out this form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to enter a valid email address and phone number.
3. Provide your date of birth as it appears on your identification documents.
4. Once you have filled out the form, click the submit button to place your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for order updates and confirmations.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for order updates and confirmations.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth as it appears on your identification documents.
