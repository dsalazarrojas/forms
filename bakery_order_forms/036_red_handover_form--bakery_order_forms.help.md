## Purpose
The RED Handover Form is designed to capture essential information from customers for their orders. This form helps us understand their delivery preferences, address, and contact details. Please note that this form is not intended for internal use only and is a public-facing form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **customer name** in the text field at the top of the page.
2. Select your **order type** from the dropdown menu next to "order type". You can choose either "Delivery" or "Pickup".
3. Enter your **date** for the order in the date field.
4. Enter your preferred **time** for the order in the time field.
5. Enter your **customer email** in the text field.
6. Enter your **customer phone number** in the text field.
7. Enter your **delivery address** in the text field.
8. Add any **delivery note** in the text field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **customer name (customer_name)**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification card or document.
* **order type (order_type)**: Select "Delivery" if you would like to have your order delivered to your address. Select "Pickup" if you would like to pick up your order at our store or bakery.
* **date (date)**: Enter the date you would like to receive your order. Please use the format dd/mm/yyyy.
* **time (time)**: Enter the time you would like to receive your order. Please use 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00 for 2:00 PM).
* **customer email (customer_email)**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for confirmation and updates.
* **customer phone (customer_phone)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for confirmation and updates.
* **delivery address (delivery_address)**: Enter your address where you would like to have your order delivered. Please make sure to include house number and postal code.
* **delivery note (delivery_note)**: Add any special delivery instructions or notes for our staff.
