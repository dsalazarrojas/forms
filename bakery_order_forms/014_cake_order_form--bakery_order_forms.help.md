# Cake Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place a cake order for special occasions, events, or daily needs. It gathers essential information about the customer, cake requirements, and payment details to ensure a smooth and satisfying experience for both the customer and the bakery.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **Full Name**, **Email Address**, and **Phone Number** to ensure we can contact you with any questions or updates.
2. Choose a **Cake Flavor**, **Cake Size**, **Frosting Type**, **Cake Shape**, and any **Filling Options** that suit your taste.
3. Select your **Preferred Payment Type** and indicate if a **Deposit Amount** is required.
4. Provide the **Event Date**, **Event Time**, and select how you'd like to **Pickup or Delivery** your cake.
5. If applicable, specify any **Dietary Requirements** or **Allergies/Restrictions** you may have.
6. Add any **Special Message or Name** you'd like on the cake, if applicable.
7. Enter any **Special Requests or Notes** about your order.
8. Review your order carefully and submit it to complete your cake order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email Address**: The email address where you can be reached for updates and confirmations.
* **Phone Number**: The number we can use to contact you if needed.
* **Cake Flavor**: Select the type of cake that suits your taste: Chocolate, Vanilla, Strawberry, Red Velvet, Lemon, Carrot, Cheesecake, or Custom.
* **Cake Size**: Choose the size of your cake: 6 inch (4 servings), 8 inch (8 servings), 10 inch (12 servings), 12 inch (18 servings), or Sheet cake (30+ servings).
* **Cake Shape**: Pick the shape of your cake: Round, Square, Rectangular, Heart-shaped, or Custom.
* **Frosting Type**: Select the frosting style: Buttercream, Cream Cheese, Chocolate Ganache, Fondant, or Whipped Cream.
* **Filling Options**: Choose from fruit preserves, Chocolate mousse, Vanilla cream, Lemon curd, Caramel, or none.
* **Event Date**: The day of the event or your desired pickup/delivery date.
* **Event Time**: The time of the event or your desired pickup/delivery time.
* **Delivery or Pickup?**: Select whether you'd like to pick up your cake or have it delivered.
* **Delivery Address**: Enter the address where you'd like your cake delivered.
* **Preferred Payment Type**: Choose from Credit Card, Debit Card, Cash, Check, or Bank Transfer.
* **Deposit Amount**: If a deposit is required for your order, enter the amount here.
* **Special Message or Name**: Add a personal message or a name to be written on the cake.
* **Special Requests or Notes**: Enter any additional requests or comments about your order.
* **Dietary Requirements**: Select any dietary restrictions you have: Gluten-free, Dairy-free, Vegan, Nut-free, Sugar-free, or none.
* **Allergies or Restrictions**: Enter any allergies or restrictions you or the event attendees may have.
* **Decoration Preferences**: Enter any decoration preferences you may have.
* **Event Time**: The time of the event or your desired pickup/delivery time.
* **Delivery Method**: Select whether you'd like to pick up your cake or have it delivered.
