# Bakes by Nilo Custom Cake Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place a custom cake order with Bakes by Nilo. Fill out the form to provide necessary information for a seamless order process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the customer name, contact phone number, and email address fields with the required information.
2. Select the desired cake size from the dropdown menu.
3. Choose the primary flavor for the cake from the dropdown menu.
4. If you have any dietary requirements or allergies, provide them in the text field.
5. Describe any decoration details or text to include in the message field.
6. Enter the date the cake will be collected from the calendar.
7. Select the preferred pickup time from the time field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer name**: A required field to collect the customer's full name.
* **Contact phone**: A required field to receive order confirmations and updates.
* **Email address**: An optional field for receiving receipts and order updates.
* **Cake size**: Select the size of the cake you'd like to order (6 inch, 8 inch, 10 inch, or Sheet).
* **Cake flavor**: Choose one primary flavor for the cake (Vanilla, Chocolate, Red velvet, Lemon, or Carrot).
* **Dietary requirements**: If you have any allergies or special diets, provide them here.
* **Decoration details**: Describe any text to include in the message or design of the cake.
* **Pickup date**: Enter the date the cake will be collected.
* **Pickup time**: Select the preferred time for collection.
* **Additional instructions**: Provide any other order notes or special requests.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure a smooth order process.
* If you have any dietary requirements or allergies, please provide them in the text field.
* For decoration details, you can describe the design or text you'd like to include on the cake.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to contact us for clarification.
