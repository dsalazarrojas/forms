# Girl Scout Cookie Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Girl Scout Cookie Order Form is a tool to help customers place orders for cookies. Please fill out this form with the requested information to ensure your order is processed correctly.
## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type(s) of cookies you'd like to order by choosing from the available options.
2. Enter the number of cookies you'd like to purchase.
3. Enter your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
4. Provide any special requests or additional details about your order in the "Order Details" field.
5. Choose how you'd like to pay for your order.
6. Finally, select a date for the order to be fulfilled.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Cookie Type**: Select the type(s) of cookies you'd like to order. You can choose one or multiple options.
	+ Chocolate Chip
	+ Peanut Butter
	+ Oatmeal Raisin
	+ Snickerdoodle
* **Cookie Count**: Enter the number of cookies you'd like to purchase. Please enter a whole number.
* **Cookie Price**: This field is not currently used for pricing. Please do not fill it out.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Customer Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Order Details**: Provide any special requests or additional details about your order. This is a free text field for anything you'd like to add.
* **Order Date**: Select the date you'd like the order to be fulfilled.
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you'd like to pay for your order. Options include:
	+ Cash
	+ Credit Card
	+ PayPal
* **Special Request**: This field is optional and not currently used. Please do not fill it out.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields with accurate information.
* If you're unsure about any field, please reach out to a Girl Scout representative for clarification.
* Orders will be processed as soon as possible after submission. Thank you for your patience and understanding.
