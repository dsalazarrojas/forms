# Cake Jar Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Cake Jar Order Form is designed to gather essential information for creating customized cake orders, including personal details, order preferences, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your personal information: **Your Name**, **Email Address**, and **Phone Number**.
2.  Choose your **Jar Flavor**, **Jar Size**, and **Frosting Type** from the provided options.
3.  Select the **Quantity** of jars you'd like to order.
4.  Add any **Decorative Toppings** you'd like on your cakes.
5.  Choose your preferred **Delivery Method** and provide a **Delivery Address** if necessary.
6.  Select the **Occasion** for your order, if applicable.
7.  Choose your preferred **Payment Method**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Your Name**: A text field to enter your full name for order confirmation.
*   **Email Address**: A field to enter your email for order confirmation and any further communication.
*   **Phone Number**: A field for contact information, not required as it's already implied in "Customer Name".
*   **Pickup Date**: A date field where you can select when you'd like to pick up your order.
*   **Jar Flavor**: A dropdown menu to choose your preferred cake flavor.
*   **Jar Size**: A dropdown menu to select the size of the jar.
*   **Frosting Type**: A dropdown menu to choose your preferred frosting type.
*   **Quantity**: A field to specify how many jars you'd like to order.
*   **Decorative Toppings**: A multi-select menu to choose additional toppings for your cake.
*   **Delivery Method**: A dropdown menu to select how you'd like to receive your order.
*   **Delivery Address**: A text field to provide a delivery address, only required if delivery method is "Shipping".
*   **Occasion**: A dropdown menu to specify the occasion for your order, if applicable.
*   **Payment Method**: A dropdown menu to select your preferred payment method.
*   **Special Instructions**: A text field for any special requests or notes.
*   **Gift Packaging**: A dropdown menu to choose if you'd like a gift box.
*   **Custom Labels**: A dropdown menu to choose if you'd like custom labels.
*   **Custom Label Text**: A text field to specify the text for custom labels, only required if custom labels are chosen.
*   **Dietary Requirements**: A dropdown menu to specify any dietary restrictions.
*   **Special Instructions**: A text field for any special requests or notes.

## Tips

*   Please be as specific as possible when providing your order details and dietary requirements.
*   If you have any special requests or notes, use the **Special Instructions** field.
*   Review your order carefully before submission to ensure accuracy.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact our team.
