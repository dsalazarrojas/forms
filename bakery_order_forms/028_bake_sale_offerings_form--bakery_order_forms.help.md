# Bake Sale Offerings Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from bakers or contributors to a bake sale event. It will help organizers understand who is contributing, the event details, what baked goods will be available, pricing, and delivery details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form with the required and optional information as prompted.
2.  Enter your contact information as the baker or contributor.
3.  Provide event details such as event name, date, and location.
4.  List the categories of baked goods you will offer and describe them in detail.
5.  Estimate the total number of items you will bring.
6.  Provide information on any allergens or dietary accommodations.
7.  Indicate if all items are homemade or not.
8.  Suggest a price per item.
9.  Choose your delivery method and time.
10.  Confirm that the information provided is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Baker/Contributor Name**: Enter your name as the baker or contributor.
*   **Email Address**: Enter your email address where we can contact you.
*   **Email Address (again)**: Confirm your email address. (This is not a duplicate field, it is a confirmation field.)
*   **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where we can contact you.
*   **Organization or Group**: If you represent an organization or group, enter its name here.
*   **Event Name**: Enter the name of the bake sale event.
*   **Bake Sale Date**: Enter the date of the bake sale event.
*   **Event Location**: Enter the location of the bake sale event.
*   **Baked Item Categories You Will Provide**: Select all the categories of baked goods you will offer (e.g., Cookies, Brownies/Bars, Cupcakes, etc.).
*   **Detailed Description of Items**: Describe the baked goods you will offer in detail, including flavors and quantities.
*   **Estimated Total Number of Items**: Estimate the total number of items you will bring.
*   **Allergens Contained in Items**: List any allergens present in the baked goods, such as nuts, dairy, eggs, soy, gluten, etc.
*   **Dietary Accommodation Options**: Specify any dietary accommodation options such as vegan, gluten-free, sugar-free, etc.
*   **Are All Items Homemade?**: Indicate if all items are homemade or not.
*   **Suggested Price Per Item**: Suggest a price per item in dollars.
*   **Volume Discount or Bundle Pricing**: Specify if you offer volume discounts or bundle pricing.
*   **How Will You Deliver Items?**: Choose how you will deliver the items (e.g., Morning of Sale, Day Before, etc.).
*   **Delivery/Setup Time**: Specify your delivery or setup time.
*   **Special Requirements or Notes**: Specify any special requirements or notes for delivery, such as storage or refrigeration needs.
*   **I Confirm This Information Is Accurate and Complete**: Confirm that the information you provided is accurate and complete.

## Tips
1.  Be sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field.
2.  Review your form before submitting it to ensure you haven't missed any required fields.
3.  If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
