# holiday_cake_order_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for holiday cake orders, allowing you to gather all necessary details from customers in a single, structured format.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide delivery details, including the delivery method and any additional instructions.
2. Enter customer details, including their email address and phone number.
3. Enter order details, such as the order date and time, and any custom message.
4. Add billing details, including the billing address and email address.
5. Provide any delivery address and contact information for the delivery person.
6. Enter any additional notes or instructions for the order.
7. Add any custom messages or comments for the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
Here's an explanation of each field in the form:

* **Delivery details (ID 1)**: Enter the delivery details for the holiday cake, including the delivery method and any additional instructions.
* **Customer details (ID 2)**: Enter the customer's details, including their email address and phone number.
* **Order details (ID 3)**: Enter the order details, such as the order date and time, and any custom message.
* **Billing details (ID 4)**: Enter the billing address and email address for the order.
* **Delivery address (ID 7)**: Enter the delivery address for the holiday cake.
* **Delivery time (ID 8)**: Enter the time for the delivery.
* **Delivery method (ID 9)**: Select the delivery method, such as delivery to the customer's home or office.
* **Customer notes (ID 10)**: Enter any notes for the customer.
* **Order notes (ID 11)**: Enter any notes for the order.
* **Billing address (ID 12)**: Enter the billing address for the order.
* **Billing phone (ID 13)**: Enter the phone number for the billing contact.
* **Customer email (ID 14)**: Enter the customer's email address.
* **Billing email (ID 15)**: Enter the email address for the billing contact.
* **Delivery phone (ID 16)**: Enter the phone number for the delivery contact.
* **Delivery email (ID 17)**: Enter the email address for the delivery contact.
* **Delivery notes (ID 18)**: Enter any additional delivery notes.
* **Order instructions (ID 19)**: Enter any instructions for the order.
* **Billing notes (ID 20)**: Enter any notes for the billing contact.
* **Custom message (ID 21)**: Enter any custom message or comment for the order.
* **Order status (ID 22)**: Enter the status of the order.
* **Order date (ID 23)**: Enter the date of the order.
* **Order time (ID 24)**: Enter the time of the order.
* **Order total (ID 25)**: Enter the total cost of the order.

## Tips
* Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Make sure to include all required details for the order.
* Use the delivery notes field to provide any special instructions for the delivery person.
* Use the custom message field to include any personal message or comment for the customer.
* Verify the email and phone numbers are correct to ensure effective communication.
* Review the order status field to track the progress of the order.
