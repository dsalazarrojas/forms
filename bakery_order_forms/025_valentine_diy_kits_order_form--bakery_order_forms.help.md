# Valentine DIY Kits Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect order information for Valentine's DIY kits. Please provide your details accurately to ensure timely and efficient delivery of your order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field with the required information.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, required/optional): Provide your name and any other relevant customer information.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter your address to ensure accurate delivery of your order.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): Provide your contact information, such as phone number and email, so we can reach out to you if needed.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address to receive updates on your order status.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): If you're planning a special event, provide your date of birth to ensure the kit is customized accordingly.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Select the date you'd like to receive your order.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Choose the time slot you prefer for order delivery.

## Tips
* Please make sure to double-check your details before submitting the form.
* If you have any special requests or concerns, feel free to contact us directly.
* If you need help with any of the fields, we're here to assist you.
* Our team will contact you with any questions or updates regarding your order.
