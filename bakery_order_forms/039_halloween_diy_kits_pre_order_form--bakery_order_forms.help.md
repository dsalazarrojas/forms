# Halloween DIY Kits Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Halloween DIY Kits Pre Order Form is used to collect customer contact information and order details for pre-ordering Halloween-themed DIY kits. Customers can use this form to submit their order information, which will be reviewed and processed by our team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form correctly, follow these steps:
1. Enter your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Enter your Email (this will be used for communication and order updates).
4. Enter your Phone number (for customer support and order follow-up).
5. Enter your Address (including street address or PO box).
6. Enter your City.
7. Enter your State.
8. Enter your Zip Code (a five-digit number).
9. If you have an Order ID, please enter it (this is optional and only needed for specific orders).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Enter your first name, as it will be used to personalize communication and order updates.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Enter your last name for the same reasons as above.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that you regularly check, as this will be used for order updates, promotions, and customer support.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter a phone number that you can be reached at for order follow-up and support.
* **Address** (`Address`, text, required): Enter your full address, including street address or PO box number, for order shipping and delivery purposes.
* **City** (`City`, text, required): Enter the city where you live or your business is located.
* **State** (`State`, text, required): Enter the state where you live or your business is located.
* **Zip Code** (`Zip Code`, number, required): Enter a five-digit zip code that includes only numbers (e.g., 12345).
* **Order ID** (`Order ID`, text, optional): If you have an Order ID, please enter it to help our team track and process your order correctly.
