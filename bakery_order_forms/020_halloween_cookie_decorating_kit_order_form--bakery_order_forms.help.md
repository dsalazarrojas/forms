# Halloween Cookie Decorating Kit Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Halloween Cookie Decorating Kit Order Form is used to gather information from customers who are interested in purchasing a Halloween-themed cookie decorating kit. This form asks for details about the customer, their order preferences, and shipping information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your customer information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Select your preferred bakery type, cookie type, decoration type, and cookie color and shape.
3. Provide additional notes about your order if necessary.
4. Enter your shipping information, including name, street address, city, state, ZIP, and country.
5. Review and submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in the order confirmation.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address where you can be contacted about your order.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted about your order.
* **Customer Street Address** (`customer_street_address`, text, required: false): Enter your physical street address where you would like your order to be shipped.
* **Customer City** (`customer_city`, text, required: false): Enter your city where you would like your order to be shipped.
* **Customer State** (`customer_state`, text, required: false): Enter your state where you would like your order to be shipped.
* **ZIP** (`customer_zip`, text, required: false): Enter your 5-digit ZIP code where you would like your order to be shipped.
* **Customer Country** (`customer_country`, text, required: false): Enter your country where you would like your order to be shipped.
* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, select_one, required: false): Select "Select One" from the dropdown menu to indicate your preferred level of information to share about yourself.
* **Shipping Name** (`shipping_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person who will be receiving the order at the shipping address.
* **Shipping Street Address** (`shipping_street_address`, text, required: false): Enter the physical street address where the order will be delivered.
* **Shipping City** (`shipping_city`, text, required: false): Enter the city where the order will be delivered.
* **Shipping State** (`shipping_state`, text, required: false): Enter the state where the order will be delivered.
* **Shipping Cost** (`shipping_cost`, number, required: false): Enter the cost associated with shipping the order.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, required: false): Enter the total cost of the order before shipping.
* **Bakery Type** (`bakery_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Select "Yes" from the dropdown menu to indicate if you would like to include any of the following in your order: "Yes" or "No".
* **Cookie Type** (`cookie_type`, select_one, required: false): Select "Yes" from the dropdown menu to indicate if you would like to include any of the following in your order: "Yes" or "No".
* **Decoration Type** (`decoration_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Select "Yes" from the dropdown menu to indicate if you would like to include any of the following in your order: "Yes" or "No".
* **Cookie Color** (`cookie_color`, select_one, required: false): Select "Yes" from the dropdown menu to indicate if you would like to include any of the following in your order: "Yes" or "No".
* **Cookie Shape** (`cookie_shape`, select_one, required: false): Select "Yes" from the dropdown menu to indicate if you would like to include any of the following in your order: "Yes" or "No".
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes about your order.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, text, required: false): Enter any additional payment information about your order.
* **Shipping Cost** (`shipping_cost`, number, required: false): Enter the cost associated with shipping the order.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, required: false): Enter the total cost of the order before shipping.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields (if any) before submitting your order.
* If you have any additional notes or questions, please enter them in the "Additional Notes" field.
* If you have any concerns about your order or shipping, please contact us at your email or phone number.
