# Cupcake Box Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate customers in submitting their order for a custom cupcake box. Please ensure you have all the required information before completing the form to avoid any errors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your name in the "customer_name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "customer_email" field. Make sure to enter it in the correct format (e.g., example@domain.com).
3. Enter your phone number in the "customer_phone" field.
4. Decide on the size of the cupcake box by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "box_size" field.
5. Indicate the type of cupcake box you want to order by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "box_type" field.
6. Choose the color of the cupcake box by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "box_color" field.
7. Enter the quantity of the cupcake box you want to order in the "box_quantity" field.
8. Describe the contents of the box in the "box_contents" field.
9. Enter the price of the box in the "box_price" field.
10. Select the order date in the "order_date" field.
11. Enter the order time in the "order_time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Cupcake Box Order Form** (title): This field is the title of the form.
- **Customer Name** (customer_name, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
- **Customer Email** (customer_email, email, required): Provide a valid email address where we can contact you.
- **Customer Phone** (customer_phone, text, required): Enter your phone number for any further communication.
- **Box Size** (box_size, select_one, required): Indicate if you want a large or small cupcake box.
- **Box Type** (box_type, select_multiple, required): Choose what type of box you want to order (e.g., custom, standard, etc.).
- **Box Color** (box_color, select_multiple, required): Select the color of the cupcake box (e.g., red, blue, etc.).
- **Box Quantity** (box_quantity, number, required): Enter the number of cupcake boxes you want to order.
- **Box Contents** (box_contents, text, required): Describe what you want in the cupcake box (e.g., cupcakes, chocolates, etc.).
- **Box Price** (box_price, number, required): Enter the price for the cupcake box.
- **Order Date** (order_date, date, required): Select the date when you want to receive your order.
- **Order Time** (order_time, time, required): Choose the time when you want to receive your order.
