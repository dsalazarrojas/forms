# Tenant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tenant Application Form is used by potential tenants to apply to become tenants of a building or property. This form helps us to collect the necessary information about the applicant and their contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information required for the application.
* Fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Make sure to enter your real contact information, as we will be using this to contact you.
* Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
* If you have any issues or questions while filling out the form, please reach out to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your home address.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form carefully and accurately.
* If you have any issues or questions while filling out the form, please reach out to us.
* We will use the information provided to review and process your application.
