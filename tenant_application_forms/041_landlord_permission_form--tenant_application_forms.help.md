# Landlord Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Landlord Permission Form" is a form used to collect information from tenants to request permission from their landlord to keep a pet in a rental property. This form is intended to gather essential details that will facilitate a smooth and informed decision-making process between the tenant and landlord.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Please indicate whether you have a pet and provide a brief description of your pet (page 1, field 1: Pet Request).
2. Sign your name to confirm that you are the tenant (page 2, field 1: Tenant Signature).
3. Sign your name and date to confirm that you have obtained the necessary permission from your landlord (page 3, field 1: Landlord Signature).
4. Enter the date when the request was made (page 3, field 2: Date of Request).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Request** (page 1, Pet Request, optional): This field is for you to indicate whether you have a pet. Please type 'yes' or 'no' in this field.
* **Tenant Signature** (page 2, Tenant Signature, optional): This field is for you to sign your name as the tenant to confirm that you are the authorized party making this request.
* **Landlord Signature** (page 3, Landlord Signature, optional): This field is for you to sign your name and date to confirm that you have obtained the necessary permission from your landlord.
* **Date of Request** (page 3, Date of Request, optional): This field is for you to enter the date when the permission request was made.

## Tips
* Make sure to sign your name legibly and accurately in the Tenant Signature and Landlord Signature fields.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, you can contact your landlord or property manager for clarification.
