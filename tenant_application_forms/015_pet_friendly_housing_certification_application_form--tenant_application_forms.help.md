# Pet Friendly Housing Certification Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from tenants about their pet's details, health, and vaccination records, as well as their own contact and address information. This information will help landlords or property managers evaluate the pet-friendliness of a potential tenant's pet.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Tenant Application** page with your name and any other relevant information.
2. On the **Pet Details** page, provide your pet's information, such as type, name, and breed.
3. In the **Pet Friendly Policy** section, select whether your pet is friendly or not.
4. For **Pet Type**, select the type of pet you have (Cat, Dog, or Other).
5. Enter your **Pet Name** and any other relevant details about your pet.
6. Fill in the **Pet Breed** field with your pet's breed.
7. Enter your **Owner Name** and **Contact Number** so we can get in touch with you.
8. Provide your **Address**, **Unit Number**, and **Building Address**.
9. Enter your **City**, **State**, **Zip**, and **Country**.
10. In the **Pet Photo** field, upload a photo of your pet (optional).
11. Attach a **Proof of Residence** document to verify your address.
12. Upload a **Rental Agreement** that includes your pet's information (optional).
13. Provide **Pet Vax Records** and **Pet License** documents (if applicable).
14. Attach a **Pet Health Cert** document (if applicable).
15. Finally, sign the form with your **Tenant Signature** and **Owner Signature**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tenant Application**: This page is where you'll start by filling in your basic information as a tenant.
	+ **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Pet Details**: This page is where you'll provide information about your pet.
	+ **Pet Type**: Select the type of pet you have (Cat, Dog, or Other).
	+ **Pet Name**: Enter your pet's name.
	+ **Pet Breed**: Enter your pet's breed.
* **Pet Friendly Policy**: Check if your pet is friendly or not.
* **Pet Type (again)**: Select the type of pet you have (this is a repetition of the question above).
* **Pet Name (again)**: Enter your pet's name (this is a repetition of the question above).
* **Pet Breed (again)**: Enter your pet's breed (this is a repetition of the question above).
* **Owner Name**: Enter the name of the person responsible for your pet's well-being.
* **Contact Number**: Enter a contact number for you or the person responsible for your pet.
* **Address**: Enter your address.
* **Unit Number**: Enter your unit number (if applicable).
* **Building Address**: Enter the address of your building.
* **City**: Enter the city where you reside.
* **State**: Enter the state where you reside.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **Country**: Enter the country where you reside.
* **Pet Photo**: Upload a photo of your pet (optional).
* **Proof of Residence**: Attach a document to verify your address.
* **Rental Agreement**: Attach a document that includes your pet's information (optional).
* **Pet Vax Records**: Attach documents showing your pet's vaccination records (if applicable).
* **Pet License**: Attach documents showing your pet's license (if applicable).
* **Pet Health Cert**: Attach documents showing your pet's health certificate (if applicable).
* **Tenant Signature**: Sign the form to confirm the information provided.
* **Owner Signature**: Sign the form to confirm the information provided.
* **Date Submitted**: The date and time you submitted the form.
* **Time Submitted**: The time you submitted the form.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to avoid any delays or rejections.
- Attach all necessary documents to support your application.
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the landlord or property manager for assistance.
