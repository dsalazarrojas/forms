# Tenant Rules Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tenant Rules Acknowledgment Form is a mandatory form that requires tenants to acknowledge that they have read and agree to the rules of the property. This form is a crucial part of the tenant application process, ensuring that tenants are aware of their responsibilities and obligations as a tenant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the rules of the property carefully.
2. Fill out the "Tenant Information" field with your contact details.
3. Sign and date the "Signature" field to confirm that you have read and agree to the rules.
4. Select "Yes" in the "Rules Agreement" field to indicate that you have agreed to the rules.
5. Fill out the "Submission Date" and "Submission Time" fields with the date and time you submitted the form.
6. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
7. (Optional) If you have any additional comments or notes, you may enter them in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tenant Information (Required)**: This field is used to collect your contact details. Please fill out your name and any other relevant information.
* **Signature (Required)**: This is a date field where you will sign to confirm that you have read and agree to the rules.
* **Rules Agreement (Required)**: You must select "Yes" from the drop-down menu to confirm that you have agreed to the rules.
* **Submission Date (Required)**: This is a date field where you will enter the date you submitted this form.
* **Submission Time (Required)**: This is a time field where you will enter the time you submitted this form.
* **Email (Required)**: Please enter your email address.
* **Phone (Optional)**: This field is used to collect your phone number. If you don't have a phone, you may leave this field blank.
* **Notes (Optional)**: This field is used to provide any additional comments or notes about your submission. You may leave this field blank if you don't have any comments.

## Tips

* Please review the rules carefully before submitting this form.
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the landlord or property manager.
