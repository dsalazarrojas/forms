# Homeowner Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for homeowners to submit complaints or issues they are experiencing. It is used by property management or maintenance staff to gather information about these issues and address them accordingly.
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of the complaint from the options provided (HOA Forms, Tenant Application Forms, Complaint Forms)
2. Describe the issue in the "Description" field
3. Type the complaint in the "Complaint" field
4. Choose the date when the issue occurred or started
5. Choose the time when the issue occurred or was noticed
6. Enter your email address so we can contact you for more information
7. Optionally, provide your phone number for further contact
8. Click "Submit" to send the form

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Category** (`category`, select_one, required): Choose the category that best describes the type of complaint you are submitting. This will help us to direct your issue to the correct department.
* **Description** (description, text, optional): Please provide a clear and concise description of the issue.
* **Complaint** (complaint, text, optional): Type in the specific complaint or problem you are experiencing.
* **Date** (date, date, optional): Select the date when the issue occurred or started.
* **Time** (time, time, optional): Select the time when the issue occurred or was noticed.
* **Email** (email, email, optional): Please enter your email address so we can contact you for more information.
* **Phone** (phone, text, optional): Optionally, provide your phone number for further contact.
* **Submit** (submit, text, optional): Click this button to send your complaint form to us.
