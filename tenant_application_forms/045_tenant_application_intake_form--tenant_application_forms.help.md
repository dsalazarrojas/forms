# Tenant Application Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Tenant Application Intake Form is a crucial document that helps us gather essential information about you, the applicant, and your rental history. Please take a few minutes to complete this form carefully, as it will help us process your application efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

To ensure that we receive a complete and accurate application, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone in their respective fields. Make sure to double-check these fields for any typos or errors.
2. Provide your desired Move In Date and Lease Length. If you're unsure about the lease length, you can leave this field blank.
3. Choose your reason for renting the property from the provided options.
4. Answer the questions regarding your rental history, emergency contacts, and potential conflicts (if any).
5. Sign and date the form to confirm that you've read and understood the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Tenant_first_name, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (tenant_last_name, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (email, email, required): Enter your valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (phone, text, required): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Move In Date** (move_in_date, date, not required): Enter your desired move-in date, or leave blank if you're unsure.
* **Move Out Date** (move_out_date, date, not required): Enter your estimated move-out date, or leave blank if you're unsure.
* **Lease Length** (lease_length, number, required): Enter the length of your desired lease, in months or years.
* **Lease Start Date** (lease_start_date, date, required): Enter your desired lease start date.
* **Desired Move In Date** (desired_move_in_date, date, not required): Enter your potential move-in date, or leave blank if you're unsure.
* **Reason for Renting** (reason_for_renting, text, required): Select the reason you're renting the property from the options provided.
* **Rental History** (rental_history, select_multiple, not required): Indicate if you have rental history with previous landlords (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').
* **Landlord Reference** (landlord_reference, select_one, not required): If you have a landlord reference, select 'Yes', or 'No' if you don't.
* **Emergency Contacts** (emergency_contacts, select_multiple, not required): Indicate if you have emergency contacts (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (emergency_contact_relationship, select_multiple, not required): Specify your relationship with your emergency contacts (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (emergency_contact_phone, text, not required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (emergency_contact_email, email, not required): Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Tenant Signature** (tenant_signature, note, required): Sign and date this form to confirm that you've read and understood the information provided.
* **Landlord Signature** (landlord_signature, note, required): Sign and date this form to confirm that you've read and understood the information provided.
* **Property Info** (property_info, select_multiple, not required): Indicate if you have any specific property information (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').
* **Property Address** (property_address, text, required): Enter the address of the property you're applying for.
* **Property Type** (property_type, select_one, required): Select the type of property you're applying for (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').
* **Potential Move-In Conflicts** (move_in_date_conflict, select_multiple, not required): Indicate if there are any potential move-in conflicts (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').
* **Reason for Conflict** (move_in_date_conflict_reason, text, not required): Provide a reason for the potential move-in conflict, if applicable.
* **Potential Emergency Contact Conflicts** (emergency_contact_conflict, select_multiple, not required): Indicate if there are any potential emergency contact conflicts (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').
* **Reason for Conflict** (emergency_contact_conflict_reason, text, not required): Provide a reason for the potential emergency contact conflict, if applicable.
