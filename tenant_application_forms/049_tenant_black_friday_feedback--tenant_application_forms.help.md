# Tenant Black Friday Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from tenants who shopped during Black Friday, allowing us to improve future promotions and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your name and email address.
2. Answer the question "How was your Black Friday experience?" in the "Feedback" field.
3. Rate your overall experience using the "Overall Rating" field (e.g., 1-5 stars).
4. Provide more details about your rating in the "Rating Comment" field.
5. If you'd like to be contacted for further feedback or support, you can provide your phone number.
6. Share any additional comments or feedback about your experience in the "Additional Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: A field to collect your name, which will help us understand who submitted the feedback.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: False
* **Email**: A field to collect your email address, which will help us contact you for further feedback or support.
	+ Type: Email
	+ Required: False
* **Feedback**: A field to collect your comments about your Black Friday experience.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: False
* **Overall Rating**: A field to rate your experience, from 1-5 stars.
	+ Type: Number
	+ Required: False
* **Rating Comment**: A field to provide more details about your rating.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: False
* **Phone**: A field to provide your phone number, in case you'd like to be contacted for further feedback or support.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: False
* **Additional Feedback**: A field to collect any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: False
