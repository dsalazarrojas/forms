# Commercial Space Leasing Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to walk you through the Commercial Space Leasing Application form. Please follow this guide carefully to ensure you complete the form correctly and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the **Applicant and Business Entity** section. This includes providing your **Legal Name of Business Entity**, which is a required field. You can enter the name of your business as it is registered.
2. If your business operates under a different name, you can also provide your **DBA (If different)**. This is an optional field.
3. In the **Description of Business Use** field, describe exactly how you plan to use the commercial space. This is a required field.
4. Next, provide details about your **Years of Operation**. This will help us understand your business experience and financial stability.
5. In the **Space and Lease Requirements** section, enter the **Address/Suite** where you are interested in leasing.
6. Specify the **-Requested Lease Commencement Date**, which is the date you would like your lease to start.
7. Provide the **Requested Lease Term (Years)**, indicating how long you plan to lease the space.
8. If you have any specific requirements for build-out, describe them in the **Description of Tenant Improvement Needs** field.
9. In the **Financial Disclosures** section, provide your **Personal Guarantor Full Name**, which is a required field.
10. Enter the **Bank Reference (Name and Branch)** for your business or personal bank account.
11. You need to **Authorize** the landlord to verify your credit and background information. This is a required field with options to choose from.
12. The **Contact Information** section is next, where you provide your **Primary Point of Contact**.
13. Enter your **Contact Email**, which is a required field.
14. Provide your **Contact Phone Number**, another required field.
15. Finally, enter the **Date of Application**, which indicates when you submitted the application.
16. If you have any **Additional Information for Consideration**, use this field to provide further details about your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Legal Name of Business Entity**: Enter the name of your business as it is registered.
* **DBA (If different)**: Enter any other name your business operates under if it's different from the registered name.
* **Description of Business Use**: Describe exactly what you plan to use the commercial space for.
* **Years of Operation**: Enter the number of years your business has been in operation.
* **Address/Suite**: Enter the address and suite number of the commercial space you are interested in leasing.
* **-Requested Lease Commencement Date**: Provide the date you would like the lease to start.
* **Requested Lease Term (Years)**: Enter the number of years you plan to lease the space.
* **Description of Tenant Improvement Needs**: Describe any build-out requirements you may have for the space.
* **Personal Guarantor Full Name**: Enter the name of the personal guarantor for your business.
* **Bank Reference (Name and Branch)**: Provide the name and branch of your business or personal bank account.
* **I authorize the landlord to verify credit and background information**: Choose the option to authorize the landlord to verify your credit and background information.
* **Primary Point of Contact**: Enter your name as the primary point of contact.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Application**: Enter the date you submitted the application.
* **Additional Information for Consideration**: Use this field to provide any additional information that may be relevant to your application.
