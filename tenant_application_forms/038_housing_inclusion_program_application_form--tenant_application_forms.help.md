# Housing Inclusion Program Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Housing Inclusion Program Application Form is designed to collect essential information from individuals and families who are seeking support and assistance through the Housing Inclusion Program. This form helps the program administrators to assess the applicant's needs and determine the best course of action to provide aid.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Determine whether you are applying as an individual or family. Select "Yes" if you are applying for yourself or for your family members.
2.  Fill in your name and contact information (email and phone number).
3.  Provide your current residence address.
4.  Explain the reason for applying to the Housing Inclusion Program.
5.  Indicate your current housing status (Active or Inactive).
6.  Specify whether you have any medical information that might be relevant to your application.
7.  Add any additional comments or explanations regarding your application.
8.  Sign the application to confirm your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Individual or Family** (individual_or_family): Select "Yes" if you are applying for yourself or your family members.
- **Name** (name): Enter your full name.
- **Email** (email): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
- **Phone** (phone): Enter a working phone number where we can reach you.
- **Current Residence** (current_residence): Enter your current address where you reside.
- **Reason for Move** (reason_for_move): Explain why you are applying for the Housing Inclusion Program.
- **Housing Status** (housing_status): Indicate whether you are currently in active or inactive housing.
- **Program Type** (program_type): Specify whether you are eligible or not eligible for the program.
- **Program Location** (program_location): Indicate whether you are applying for a program at a specific location.
- **Program Length** (program_length): Specify the length of time you are applying for the program.
- **Emergency Name** (emergency_name): If you have an emergency contact, enter their name.
- **Emergency Relationship** (emergency_relationship): Explain your relationship with the emergency contact.
- **Emergency Phone** (emergency_phone): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
- **Medical Info** (medical_info): If relevant, provide any medical information that might impact your application.
- **Additional Comments** (additional_comments): Add any additional information that you feel is essential to your application.
- **Signature** (signature): Sign the application to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
