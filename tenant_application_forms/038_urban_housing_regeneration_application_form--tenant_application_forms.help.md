# Urban Housing Regeneration Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from tenants applying for the urban housing regeneration program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately.
2. Make sure to provide all required fields.
3. Double-check your information before submission.
