# Community Housing Alliance Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Housing Alliance Application Form is designed for individuals and families seeking housing assistance. The form helps the organization assess applicants' eligibility and suitability for its housing programs.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Provide Personal Information**: Start by filling out the "Applicant Full Name" field with your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
2.  **Enter Current Address**: Enter your current residential address in the format "Street, City, State, Zip" for the "Current Residential Address" field.
3.  **Enter Contact Information**: Provide your primary phone number and email address in the "Primary Phone Number" and "Email Address" fields, respectively.
4.  **Household Details**: If you're applying with others, indicate the total number of people who will live in the unit in the "Total Household Size" field.
5.  **Annual Income**: Report your total annual household income before taxes for the "Total Annual Household Income" field.
6.  **Housing Status**: Choose how you currently manage your housing from the options provided in the "Current Housing Status" field.
7.  **Application Reason**: Select the primary reason for your housing application from the options provided in the "Primary Reason for Housing Application" field.
8.  **Employment Status**: Indicate your current employment status by selecting the correct option in the "Current Employment Status" field.
9.  **Housing Preference**: Select all the types of housing that you are interested in or suitable for from the "Preferred Housing Type" field, as it is a multi-select option.
10. **Background Check Consent**: Confirm your consent to undergo a background and credit check by selecting "Yes, I agree."
11.  **Application Date**: Enter the date you are submitting this application in the "Date of Application" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Applicant Full Name** (Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Current Residential Address** (Required): Provide your current address in the format "Street, City, State, Zip."
*   **Primary Phone Number** (Required): Enter your primary phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Email Address** (Required): Enter the email address you check most frequently.
*   **Total Household Size** (Required): If you are applying with others, provide the total number of individuals who will reside in the unit.
*   **Total Annual Household Income** (Required): Report your total household income before taxes.
*   **Current Housing Status** (Required): Indicate how you currently manage your housing, choosing one of the given options.
*   **Primary Reason for Housing Application** (Required): Select the primary reason you are applying for housing.
*   **Current Employment Status** (Required): Choose your employment status from the options provided.
*   **Preferred Housing Type** (Required): Select all types of housing you are interested in or suitable for, taking into account your needs and preferences.
*   **I consent to a background and credit check** (Required): Confirm your consent to undergo a background and credit check by selecting "Yes, I agree."
*   **Date of Application** (Required): Enter the date you submit this application.

**Tips**

*  Ensure you fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
*  If you have any supporting documentation or additional information, attach them with your application.
*  Be honest and complete in your responses, as they will be used to assess your eligibility for housing programs.
*  If you have any questions or concerns, contact the Community Housing Alliance for assistance.
