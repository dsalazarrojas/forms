# Tenant Security Innovation Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your security innovations and register them for compliance or regulatory purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your tenant name in the "Tenants Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Fill in your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field using the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Enter your date of entry in the "Date of Entry" field using the format MM/DD/YYYY.
5. Check the "Security Program" field and select "Yes" if your security innovation is part of our security program.
6. In the "Location" field, enter the name of the city where you are located.
7. If your security innovation is not part of our standard programs, select "Yes" in the "Security Innovation" field and provide more details in the "Other Security Innovation" and "Other Security Innovation Details" fields.
8. If you have any comments, please add them in the "Comments" field.
9. Finally, confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions of your security innovation by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Other Security Innovation Agreement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tenant Name** (`tenant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can contact you with.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (not required but recommended).
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Fill in your date of birth using the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Date of Entry** (`date_of_entry`, date, required): Fill in your date of entry using the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Security Program** (`security_program`, select_one, optional): Check "Yes" if your security innovation is part of our security program.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the name of the city where you are located.
* **Security Innovation** (`security_innovation`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" if your security innovation is not part of our standard programs.
* **Other Security Innovation** (`security_innovation_other`, text, optional): If selected above, provide more details about your security innovation.
* **Other Security Innovation Details** (`security_innovation_other_details`, text, optional): Add more information about your security innovation (if selected above).
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any comments or remarks you'd like to share.
* **Other Security Innovation Agreement** (`security_innovation_other_agreement`, select_one, optional): Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions of your security innovation by selecting "Yes" or "No".
