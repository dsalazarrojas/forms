# Tenant Identity Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tenant Identity Verification Form is a tool used to collect and verify the identity of tenants. It gathers essential information to ensure accuracy and update in our records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Provide your date of birth in the "Date of birth" field in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.
4. Enter your social security number in the "Social security number" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone number" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
7. Provide your driver's license number in the "Driver's license number" field.
8. Choose your address type from the options in the "Address 1" field.
9. Select any additional addresses you'd like to provide in the "Address 2" field.
10. Enter the date and time of your arrival in the "Date of arrival" field in the format of hh:mm AM/PM.
