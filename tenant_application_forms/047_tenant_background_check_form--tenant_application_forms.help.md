# Tenant Background Check Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects background information on a potential tenant.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Background Check Form** (`background_check_form`, text): 
   Fill in a brief description of the applicant, including their background information.

2. **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text): 
   Enter the contact person's name and any relevant information.

3. **Property Details** (`property_details`, text): 
   Provide information about the property to be rented.

4. **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one): 
   Select the current employment status of the applicant. Choose from options: Active, Employed, Unemployed, Retired, Self Employed, Student, Disabled, Deceased.

5. **Income** (`income`, number): 
   Enter the applicant's income.

6. **Income Source** (`income_source`, text): 
   Describe the income source.

7. **Social Security Number** (`social_security`, text): 
   Enter the applicant's social security number.

8. **Housing Status** (`housing_status`, select_one): 
   Select the current housing status of the applicant. Choose from options: Owner, Renter, Both, None.

9. **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one): 
   Select the method of contact. Choose from options: Email, Phone, Mail, In Person.

10. **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text): 
    Enter the contact number.

11. **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text): 
    Describe the relationship of the emergency contact.

12. **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text): 
    Enter the emergency contact name.

13. **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, text): 
    Enter the emergency contact number.

14. **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text): 
    Enter the emergency contact address.

15. **Emergency Contact Relationship Type** (`emergency_contact_relationship_type`, select_one): 
    Select the type of relationship. Choose from options: Spouse, Partner, Parent, Child, Sibling, Other.

16. **Tenancy Dates** (`tenant_occupancy_dates`, date): 
    Enter the dates the tenant has occupied the property.

17. **Previous Tenancy** (`tenant_previous_occupancy`, text): 
    Describe any previous occupancy.

18. **Current Tenancy** (`tenant_current_occupancy`, select_one): 
    Select the current occupancy status. Choose from options: Currently Occupied, Previously Occupied, Not Occupied.

19. **Additional Tenant Information** (`tenant_additional_info`, text): 
    Enter any additional information about the tenant.

20. **Property Lease Term** (`property_lease_term`, text): 
    Enter the lease term of the property.

21. **Landlord Lease Term** (`landlord_lease_term`, text): 
    Enter the lease term of the landlord.

22. **Previous Property Details** (`tenant_previous_property_details`, text): 
    Describe any previous property details.

23. **Tenant Background Information** (`tenant_background_info`, text): 
    Enter any background information about the tenant.

24. **Additional Documents** (`tenant_additional_document`, text): 
    Upload any additional documents related to the tenant.

25. **Reference** (`tenant_reference`, text): 
    Enter a reference.

## Tips

- Be sure to answer all required questions.
- If unsure about a field, ask your property manager for clarification.
- Review your answers carefully before submitting.
