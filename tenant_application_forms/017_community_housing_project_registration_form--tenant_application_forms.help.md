# Community Housing Project Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect registration information for community housing projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact email address in the "Applicant name" and "Contact email" fields respectively.
2. Provide a brief description of your project in the "Project description" field.
3. Select your role in the project from the options in the "Role in project" field.
4. If applicable, enter the estimated start date and duration of your project in the "Proposed start date" and "Estimated duration in months" fields.
5. Indicate the main funding or sponsor sources for your project in the "Funding sources" field.
6. Specify the type of support needed from the community for your project in the "Support needed from community" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant name** (text, required): Enter your full name for registration purposes.
* **Contact email** (email, required): Enter your contact email address for project communications.
* **Phone number** (text, optional): Provide your direct contact number, if applicable.
* **Project description** (text, required): Briefly describe your community housing project.
* **Role in project** (select_one, required): Select your role in the project from the options:
	+ Leader
	+ Participant
	+ Volunteer
	+ Other
* **Proposed start date** (date, optional): Enter the estimated start date of your project.
* **Estimated duration in months** (number, optional): Provide an estimate of the project duration in months.
* **Funding sources** (text, optional): Indicate the main funding or sponsor sources for your project.
* **Support needed from community** (text, optional): Specify the type of support needed from the community, such as volunteers, materials, funding, or expertise.
