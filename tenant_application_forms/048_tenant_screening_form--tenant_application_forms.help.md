# Tenant Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tenant Screening Form is designed to collect essential information from potential tenants for rental property management purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out this form to provide detailed information about yourself as a potential tenant.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed accurately and truthfully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Name** (Required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Address** (Required): Enter your current and permanent address, including your street address, city, and state.
* **Contact Number** (Required): Enter your primary phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Move In Date** (Required): Enter your expected move-in date for the rental property.
* **Rental History** (Required): Provide a brief summary of your rental history, including previous addresses and reasons for moving.
* **Employment Status** (Required): Choose your employment status from the options provided, which includes Employed, Unemployed, Student, or Disabled.
* **Income Proof** (Optional): If applicable, choose the type of income proof you are able to provide, such as W2 or W4.
* **Income Frequency** (Optional): If applicable, choose how often you receive your income, such as Daily, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly, or Annual.
* **Rental Application Form Notes** (Required): Use this field to provide any additional comments or information about yourself as an applicant.
* **Applicant Signature** (Required): Indicate whether you are aware and agree with the lease terms by selecting 'True'.
* **Lease Terms** (Required): Provide any additional comments or agreements related to the lease terms.
* **Additional Comments** (Optional): Use this field for any additional comments or information you want to provide.
