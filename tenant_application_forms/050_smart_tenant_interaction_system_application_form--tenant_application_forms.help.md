# Smart Tenant Interaction System Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Tenant Interaction System Application Form is designed to collect information from tenants about their interaction with the system, including their contact details, communication preferences, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Tenant Details section with your name, address, and any other relevant details.
2. In the Communication Preferences section, select the communication methods you prefer to use for system interactions (e.g., email, phone, or other).
3. Enter your email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
5. Provide your consent by typing your name in the Consent field.
6. If necessary, enter any additional information in the Tenant Application Form field.
7. Specify your rent preferences in the Rent Preferences field.
8. Add any notes or comments in the Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tenant Details** (`tenant_details`, text, required/optional): This is the first section of the form where you can enter your name, address, and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Communication Preferences** (`communication_preferences`, select_multiple, required/optional): Here, you can select the communication methods you prefer to use for system interactions. Options include:
	+ Rent Due Date
	+ Lease End Date
	+ Other
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address to be contacted by the system or property manager.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number to be contacted by the system or property manager.
* **Consent** (`consent`, text, required/optional): Provide your consent by typing your name in this field.
* **Tenant Application Form** (`tenant_application_form`, text, required/optional): If necessary, enter any additional information about your application.
* **Rent Preferences** (`rent_preferences`, text, required/optional): Specify your rent preferences here.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Add any notes or comments about your interaction with the system.
