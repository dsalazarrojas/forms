# Tenant Income Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tenant Income Declaration Form is a tool for tenants to report their income sources, frequency, and tax status to the landlord/landlady. This information is used to determine rent rates and other applicable charges.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select all income sources you have.
2. Enter your annual and monthly net income.
3. Choose the frequency of your income.
4. Select all other income sources you have.
5. Choose the frequency of your other income sources.
6. Enter the amount earned from other income sources.
7. Enter the amount fully taxed from other income sources.
8. Enter the amount partially taxed from other income sources.
9. Enter the amount not taxed from other income sources.
10. Enter the total amount of other income sources.
11. Enter the amount fully taxed from taxes.
12. Enter the amount partially taxed from taxes.
13. Enter the amount not taxed from taxes.
14. Enter the total amount of taxes.
15. Select all other expenses you have.
16. Choose the frequency of your other expenses.
17. Enter the amount of other expenses.
18. Enter the amount fully taxed from other expenses.
19. Enter the amount partially taxed from other expenses.
20. Enter the amount not taxed from other expenses.
21. Enter the total amount of other expenses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **income_sources**: (Select multiple) Select all income sources you have. Examples: Employment, Business, Interest, etc.
* **income_earned**: (Optional) Enter your annual net income.
* **monthly_net_income**: (Optional) Enter your monthly net income.
* **income_frequency**: (Required) Choose the frequency of your income (e.g., monthly, quarterly, annually).
* **other_income_sources**: (Required) Select all other income sources you have. Examples: Rental income, Alimony, etc.
* **other_income_sources_frequency**: (Required) Choose the frequency of your other income sources.
* **other_income_sources_earned**: (Optional) Enter the amount earned from other income sources.
* **other_income_sources_fully_taxed**: (Optional) Enter the amount fully taxed from other income sources.
* **other_income_sources_partially_taxed**: (Optional) Enter the amount partially taxed from other income sources.
* **other_income_sources_not_taxed**: (Optional) Enter the amount not taxed from other income sources.
* **other_income_sources_total**: (Optional) Enter the total amount of other income sources.
* **tax_fully_taxed**: (Optional) Enter the amount fully taxed from taxes.
* **tax_partially_taxed**: (Optional) Enter the amount partially taxed from taxes.
* **tax_not_taxed**: (Optional) Enter the amount not taxed from taxes.
* **tax_total**: (Optional) Enter the total amount of taxes.
* **other_expenses**: (Optional) Enter all other expenses you have. Examples: Rent for a vacation home, etc.
* **other_expenses_frequency**: (Required) Choose the frequency of your other expenses.
* **other_expenses_earned**: (Optional) Enter the amount of other expenses.
* **other_expenses_fully_taxed**: (Optional) Enter the amount fully taxed from other expenses.
* **other_expenses_partially_taxed**: (Optional) Enter the amount partially taxed from other expenses.
* **other_expenses_not_taxed**: (Optional) Enter the amount not taxed from other expenses.
* **other_expenses_total**: (Optional) Enter the total amount of other expenses.
