# Pre Tenancy Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Tenancy Application Form is used to collect information from potential tenants applying for tenancy. This form helps landlords or property managers to assess the tenant's qualifications and suitability for tenancy.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address to ensure we can contact you.
3. Enter your Phone number for us to reach out to you if needed.
4. Provide your Date of Birth for verification purposes.
5. Enter your Address for us to know where to send the keys.
6. Briefly describe your Rental History.
7. Select your Employment Status.
8. Enter your Income for rent calculation purposes.
9. Provide your Move-in and Move-out Dates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that you check regularly for updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where we can reach out to you if needed.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the standard YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter the address where you would like to rent.
* **Rental History** (`rental_history`, text, required): Briefly describe your rental history, including previous landlords and reasons for moving out.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, optional): Select your employment status, whether Employed, Unemployed, or Self Employed.
* **Income** (`income`, number, required): Enter your annual income for rent calculation purposes.
* **Move-in Date** (`move_in_date`, date, optional): Provide your Move-in Date if you have a preferred date.
* **Move-out Date** (`move_out_date`, date, optional): Provide your Move-out Date if you have a preferred date.

## Tips

* Ensure the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, contact us.
* Please attach proof of income and employment documents if required. 
This is just a general help guide and should not be relied on as the sole source of truth for tenancy application process. The actual requirements and process may vary depending on the landlord or property manager.
