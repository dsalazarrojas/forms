# Online Rental Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from applicants who wish to rent a property. It is used for verifying the identity of the applicant and assessing their suitability for rental agreements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your address in the "Address" field.
2. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
3. Provide your contact email in the "Contact Email" field.
4. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
5. Select your preferred method of contact in the "Contact Method" field.
6. If you selected "Other" in the "Contact Method" field, please provide the method in the "Other" field.
7. Select your preferred language in the "Preferred Language" field.
8. Choose your move-in date in the "Move In Date" field.
9. Select the duration of your lease in the "Lease Duration" field.
10. Enter your starting rent in the "Rent" field.
11. Enter your security deposit in the "Security Deposit" field.
12. Select your preferred method of rental payment in the "Payment Method" field.
13. If you selected "Other" in the "Payment Method" field, please provide the method in the "Preferred Method Of Rental Payment Other" field.
14. Select your preferred payment frequency in the "Payment Frequency" field.
15. Enter your start date in the "Start Date" field.
16. Select your employment status in the "Employment Status" field.
17. Enter your employment start date in the "Employment Start Date" field.
18. Enter your employment end date in the "Employment End Date" field.
19. Enter your current employer in the "Current Employer" field.
20. Enter your previous occupation in the "Previous Occupation" field.
21. Select your rental status in the "Rent Status" field.
22. If you selected "Other" in the "Rent Status" field, please provide the status in the "Rental Status Other" field.
23. Select your income frequency in the "Income Frequency" field.
24. If you selected "Other" in the "Income Frequency" field, please provide the frequency in the "Income Frequency Other" field.
25. Select your income type in the "Income Type" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Address** (Address, text, required/optional): Enter your physical address where you currently reside or plan to reside.
* **Name** (Name, text, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact Email** (Contact Email, email, required/optional): Enter your valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone, text, required/optional): Enter your valid phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Contact Method** (Contact Method, select_one, required/optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted (Yes or No).
* **Other** (Other, text, required/optional): If you selected "Other", please specify the method of contact.
* **Preferred Language** (Preferred Language, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose your preferred language (Yes, No).
* **Move In Date** (Move In Date, date, required/optional): Choose the date you plan to move in.
* **Lease Duration** (Lease Duration, select_one, required): Choose the duration of the lease (Yes or No).
* **Rent** (Rent, number, required/optional): Enter the expected rent.
* **Security Deposit** (Security Deposit, number, required/optional): Enter the security deposit.
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method, select_one, required): Choose your preferred payment method (Yes or No).
* **Preferred Method Of Rental Payment Other** (Preferred Method Of Rental Payment Other, text, required/optional): If you selected "Other", please specify the method of payment.
* **Payment Frequency** (Payment Frequency, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose your preferred payment frequency (Yes, No).
* **Start Date** (Start Date, date, required/optional): Enter the start date of your lease.
* **Employment Status** (Employment Status, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose your employment status (Active or Inactive).
* **Employment Start Date** (Employment Start Date, date, required/optional): Enter the start date of your employment.
* **Employment End Date** (Employment End Date, date, required/optional): Enter the end date of your employment.
* **Current Employer** (Current Employer, text, required/optional): Enter your current employer.
* **Previous Occupation** (Previous Occupation, text, required/optional): Enter your previous occupation.
* **Rent Status** (Rent Status, select_one, required): Choose your rent status (Active or Inactive).
* **Rental Status Other** (Rental Status Other, text, required/optional): If you selected "Other", please specify the status.
* **Income Frequency** (Income Frequency, select_multiple, required): Choose your income frequency (Yes or No).
* **Income Frequency Other** (Income Frequency Other, text, required/optional): If you selected "Other", please specify the frequency.
* **Income Type** (Income Type, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose your income type (Yes or No).

## Tips
* Be accurate and honest when filling out the form.
* Ensure that you provide valid and up-to-date information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
* Please review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.
