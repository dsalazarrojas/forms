# prescreen_rental_questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "prescreen_rental_questionnaire" form is designed to gather essential information from tenants applying for a rental property. This form helps the landlord or property manager to better understand the tenant's background, employment status, and rental history to make an informed decision about the tenancy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand what information is required.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and honest information about yourself.
3. Ensure each applicant fills out the form individually.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General information (id: 1)**: This section is for providing your basic contact information, including your name, address, and any other relevant details about your identity.
* **Rental history (id: 2)**: Share your previous rental experiences, including the addresses and duration of tenancies.
* **Contact details (id: 3)**: Enter your current contact information, including phone numbers and email addresses.
* **Employment status (id: 4)**: Select your current employment status from the provided options (Active or Inactive) to help the landlord assess your financial stability.
* **Income verification (id: 5)**: Indicate whether you've been income-verified in the past or not. This helps the landlord understand your financial situation.
* **Rental history 2 (id: 6)**: Share any additional rental history information not covered in the previous section.
* **Employment verification (id: 7)**: Provide any employment verification information not covered in the previous section.
* **Rental history 3 (id: 8)**: Share any further rental history information that may be relevant to your tenancy.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
- If you're unsure about any information, please ask your landlord or property manager for guidance.
- Ensure each applicant fills out the form individually to maintain accuracy and fairness in the tenancy process.
