# Tenant Experience Enhancement Grant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tenant Experience Enhancement Grant Application Form is a tool for tenants to apply for financial support to improve their living conditions. The form is designed to collect relevant information about the tenant's situation, goals, and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To successfully complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review each page carefully and answer all required fields.
2.  Ensure you have attached all requested supporting documents.
3.  Proofread your application for accuracy and completeness.
4.  Submit the form when you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title (application_form, text, required):** Enter the title of your project or initiative that you are applying for.
* **Contact Details (contact_details, text, required):** Provide your full name, address, and contact information (email and phone number).
* **Tenant Experience (tenant_experience, text, required):** Describe your current living situation, including any challenges you face and goals you hope to achieve with the grant.
* **Financial Support (financial_support, text, required):** Outline your financial situation, including income, expenses, and any existing financial support you receive.
* **Community Engagement (community_engagement, text, required):** Explain how you plan to engage with your community and how the grant will support your goals.
* **Review and Verification (review_and_verification, text, required):** List any relevant documentation or evidence that supports your application, such as letters of recommendation or proof of income.
* **Submission (submission, text, required):** Confirm that you have reviewed and agree to the terms and conditions of the grant.
* **Submission Confirmation (submission_confirmation, text, required):** Enter your name and date to confirm that you have submitted the application correctly.
