# tenant_digital_portal_pilot_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The tenant_digital_portal_pilot_application_form is a form designed to collect information from tenants or individuals applying for a rental property or lease. This form is part of the digital tenant portal and is used to gather relevant details necessary for the application process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling in the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (*) in the label.
2. Enter your First Name and Last Name in their respective fields.
3. Provide your Email address.
4. Specify the Reported Area where you are applying for.
5. Enter your Contact Number.
6. Choose the Current Lease Status from the dropdown menu.
7. Select the Move In Date for the property you are applying for.
8. Choose the Property Type from the dropdown menu.
9. Enter the Reported Area Type.
10. Enter any additional reported areas in the Reported Area2, Reported Area3, and Reported Area4 fields.
11. Enter the Landlord Name.
12. Enter the Property Address.
13. Enter the Unit Number.
14. Enter the Rent Amount.
15. Select the Lease End Date and Lease Start Date.
16. Select the Property Size from the dropdown menu.
17. Enter any Parking Information.
18. Enter any Amenities provided with the property.
19. Enter the Move Out Date.
20. Choose the Lease Status from the dropdown menu.
21. Choose the Lease Type from the dropdown menu.
22. Enter any additional notes about the application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Reported Area** (text, optional): Specify the area you are applying for, e.g., "Building A".
* **Contact Number** (text, optional): Enter your contact number for communication purposes.
* **Current Lease Status** (select_one, optional): Choose from the dropdown menu:
	+ applied: You have applied for a lease in the past.
	+ leased: You are currently leased.
	+ not leased: You do not have a lease.
* **Move In Date** (date, optional): Select the date you plan to move in.
* **Property Type** (select_multiple, optional): Choose from the dropdown menu:
	+ house: A standalone house.
	+ apartment: A self-contained dwelling unit.
	+ condo: A condominium unit.
* **Reported Area Type** (text, optional): Enter the type of area you are applying for.
* **Reported Area2** (text, optional): Enter any additional reported areas.
* **Reported Area3** (text, optional): Enter any additional reported areas.
* **Landlord Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the landlord.
* **Property Address** (text, optional): Enter the property address.
* **Unit Number** (text, optional): Enter the unit number.
* **Rent Amount** (number, optional): Enter the rent amount.
* **Lease End Date** (date, optional): Select the lease end date.
* **Lease Start Date** (date, optional): Select the lease start date.
* **Property Size** (select_multiple, optional): Choose from the dropdown menu:
	+ Reported Area Size 1 Sq Ft: Square feet.
	+ Reported Area Size 2 Sq In: Square inches.
	+ Reported Area Size 3 Sq M: Square meters.
* **Parking Info** (text, optional): Enter any parking information.
* **Amenities** (text, optional): Enter any amenities provided with the property.
* **Move Out Date** (date, optional): Select the move out date.
* **Lease Status** (select_multiple, optional): Choose from the dropdown menu:
	+ lease: Currently leased.
	+ sublease: Subleased.
* **Lease Type** (select_one, optional): Choose from the dropdown menu:
	+ single: Single leaseholder.
	+ joint: Joint leaseholder.
* **Reported Area Size 4 Sq In** (text, optional): Enter any additional reported area size.
* **Notes** (note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the application.
