# Domain Leasing Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Domain Leasing Application Form is a simple, user-friendly form designed to collect necessary information from a lessee for their domain lease application. The form is used to streamline the domain leasing process and ensure that all required details are captured accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your domain name, lessee name, and lessee company name if applicable.
2.  Specify the start and end dates of the lease term.
3.  Choose the payment terms for the lease.
4.  List available domains for consideration.
5.  Specify any liability clauses or special conditions.
6.  Confirm your website maintenance needs.
7.  Enter your email address and phone number for communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Domain Name** (Page 1, Field 1): Enter your domain name as it is registered.
* **Lessee Name** (Page 1, Field 2): Enter your name.
* **Lessee Company** (Page 1, Field 3): If you are representing a company, enter the company name.
* **Start Date** (Page 1, Field 4): Select the start date of the lease term.
* **End Date** (Page 1, Field 5): Select the end date of the lease term.
* **Payment Terms** (Page 1, Field 6): Choose how you wish to pay (e.g., monthly, quarterly, annually).
* **Available Domains** (Page 1, Field 7): List any available domains for consideration.
* **Liability Clause** (Page 1, Field 8): Enter any special liability clauses or conditions.
* **Website Maintenance** (Page 1, Field 9): Choose whether your website maintenance will be included in the lease.
* **Lessee Email** (Page 1, Field 10): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Lessee Phone** (Page 1, Field 11): Enter your phone number for communication.

## Tips

* Double-check your domain name and lessee details for accuracy.
* Ensure you have selected the correct start and end dates for your lease term.
* Carefully consider your payment terms to avoid misunderstandings.
* List any available domains that you would like to consider.
* Clearly define any liability clauses or special conditions.
* Confirm your website maintenance needs accurately.

Note that this guide covers the essential fields and process, ensuring a smooth domain leasing application experience. Review the form carefully to ensure you have provided all required information before submitting.
