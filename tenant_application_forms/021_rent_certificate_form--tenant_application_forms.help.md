# Rent Certificate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rent Certificate Form is used to record and confirm rent payments between landlords or property owners and tenants. It helps in keeping accurate records of rent payments and can be used as a reference for future transactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Form ID**, which is a unique identifier for the form.
2. Enter the **Landlord or Property Owner**, who is the person or entity responsible for the property.
3. Select the **Period of Time** for which rent was paid, which can be One month, Two months, or Three months.
4. Enter the **Amount Received**, which is the total amount of rent paid.
5. Enter the **Acknowledgement Date**, which is the date the payment was acknowledged.
6. Sign the form with your **Tenant Signature** and date it with the **Date of Signature**.
7. Sign the form with the **Landlord Signature** and date it with the **Date of Signature**.
8. Enter the **Submitted By** name and date of submission.
9. Enter the **Submitted To**, which can be a person or department.
10. Enter the **Submitted To Email**, which is the contact email of the submitter.
11. Enter the **Submitted To Phone**, which is the contact phone number of the submitter.
12. Enter any additional **Remarks** or comments.
