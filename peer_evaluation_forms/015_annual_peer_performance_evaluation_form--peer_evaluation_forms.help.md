# Annual Peer Performance Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help peers evaluate the performance of their colleagues, providing feedback on their strengths and areas for improvement. It's meant to be completed by peers who have worked closely with the employee being evaluated.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the evaluator's name and email address.
2. Select the name of the employee being evaluated.
3. Choose how you would rate the employee's performance in the given areas.
4. Provide specific examples or suggestions for improvement.
5. Rate the overall performance of the employee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Evaluator Name**: Your name as the evaluator (required). Please enter your name as it appears on your ID badge or business card.
  - Your Email: Your contact email for follow-up questions or discussion (required).
- **Employee Name**: The name of the employee being evaluated (required). This should match the employee's name on our HR system.
  - **Employee ID**: The employee's ID number (optional). Please do not include this field if you're unsure what to enter.
- **Employee Position Title**: The employee's job title or role (required).
- **Department**: The department in which the employee works (required).
- **Evaluation Period**: The date range for this evaluation (required).
- **Relationship**: Your relationship to the employee (required). Choose the one that best describes your working relationship.
  - **Direct peer**: We work closely together on a regular basis.
  - **Team member**: We work together on a team but not closely enough for you to be considered a direct peer.
  - **Colleague in different department**: We work in the same company but different departments.
  - **Manager**: You are the employee's manager.
  - **Subordinate**: The employee is your subordinate.
  - **Cross-functional partner**: You work with the employee on projects or tasks that require collaboration across different departments or functions.

## Tips

* Please be specific and honest in your feedback.
* Keep the form concise and complete all required fields.
* Do not include personal or confidential information.
* If you're unsure what to enter in a field, please do not hesitate to ask your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
