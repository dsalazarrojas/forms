# Dental Assistant Peer Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help evaluate the performance of dental assistants in a peer-review process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the dental assistant being evaluated.
2. Choose the date and time of the evaluation (optional).
3. Select the evaluation period (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
4. Choose how often the assistant is evaluated (Frequent, Occasional, or Rarely).
5. Provide any feedback for the assistant (optional).
6. Rate the overall performance of the assistant (Good, Needs Improvement, or Excellent).
7. Select the preferred dental practice where the assistant works (North Star Dental, Central Dental, or West Side).
8. Select the address of the preferred dental practice (Main Street, Side Street, or Other).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dental Assistant Name** (`dental_assistant_name`, required): Please enter the name of the dental assistant being evaluated.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, optional): If desired, please enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, optional): If desired, please enter the time of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Period** (`evaluation_period`, optional): Select the time of day when the evaluation took place (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
* **Evaluation Frequency** (`evaluation_frequency`, optional): Select how often the assistant is evaluated (Frequent, Occasional, or Rarely).
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback for the assistant.
* **Overall Performance** (`overall_performance`, optional): Rate the overall performance of the assistant (Good, Needs Improvement, or Excellent).
* **Dental Practice Name** (`dental_practice_name`, optional): Select the preferred dental practice where the assistant works (North Star Dental, Central Dental, or West Side).
* **Dental Practice Address** (`dental_practice_address`, optional): Select the address of the preferred dental practice (Main Street, Side Street, or Other).
