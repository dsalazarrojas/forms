# Faculty Peer Resource Exchange Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register faculty members in the peer exchange program, providing a comprehensive directory of members with diverse expertise in various areas.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the basic contact information:
	* Enter your **First Name** (required).
	* Enter your **Last Name** (required).
	* Enter your **Email** (required).
2. Select your **Department** (required) from the available options:
	* Arts and Sciences
	* Business
	* Engineering
3. If applicable, select your secondary and tertiary departments:
	* Select your **Department 2** (optional) from the available options:
		+ Arts
		+ Engineering
		+ Business
	* Select your **Department 3** (optional) from the available options:
		+ Business
		+ Engineering
		+ Arts
4. Select your research areas of interest:
	* Choose all that apply from **Research Areas** (required) from the available options:
		+ Computer Science
		+ Physics
		+ Mathematics
5. Enter your administrative details:
	* Enter your **Role** (required) in the peer exchange program.
	* Enter your **Title** (required) in the peer exchange program.
6. Finally, verify your **Faculty ID** (required), which is likely a unique identifier for your faculty status.
7. Optionally, select your **Prophets** (optional) from the available options:
	* Moses
	* Elijah
	* Abraham
8. You can enter your **Phone** (optional) number if you prefer to be contacted via phone.
9. You can add any additional **Note** (optional) about yourself.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is known to you.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is known to you.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Department**: Select the department you belong to from the available options.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you prefer to be contacted via phone (optional).
* **Prophets**: Select all that apply from the available options (optional).
* **Note**: Add any additional information about yourself (optional).
* **Role**: Enter your role in the peer exchange program (required).
* **Title**: Enter your title in the peer exchange program (required).
* **Faculty ID**: Enter your faculty ID, which is a unique identifier for your faculty status (required).
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy (required).
* **Time**: Enter your time of birth, which is not required.
* **Research Areas**: Choose all that apply from the available options (required).
* **Department 2**: Select your secondary department from the available options (optional).
* **Department 3**: Select your tertiary department from the available options (optional).
* **Department 4**: Select your department (optional).
* **Goals**: Add any goals you have in the peer exchange program (optional).
* **Department 4**: Select your department (optional).
