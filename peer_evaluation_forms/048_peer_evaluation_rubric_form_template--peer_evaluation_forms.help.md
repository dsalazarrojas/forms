# Peer Evaluation Rubric Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Peer Evaluation Rubric Form Template is a tool used to collect feedback and suggestions from peers on individual work or projects. It is designed to promote growth, improvement, and learning within a team or organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the correct page (Peer Feedback) to begin your evaluation.
2. Complete the form by providing feedback on each section.
3. Ensure you are specific and objective in your feedback.
4. Use the provided space to explain the reasons behind your scores and comments.
5. Keep your feedback constructive and actionable.
6. Review your form for completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Peer Feedback** (page_1, text, required): This section is where you will provide feedback on the work or project. Please be specific and objective in your feedback, and use the provided space to explain the reasons behind your scores and comments.
* **Evaluation Date** (evaluation_date, date, required): Enter the date you are evaluating the work or project.
* **Evaluation Score 1** (evaluation_score_1, number, optional): Use this field to score specific aspects of the work or project. Please provide a specific reason for your score in the next field.
* **Reason for Score 1** (evaluation_comment_1, text, optional): Explain the reason for your score in this field.
* **Evaluation Score 2** (evaluation_score_2, number, optional): Use this field to score another aspect of the work or project.
* **Reason for Score 2** (evaluation_comment_2, text, optional): Explain the reason for your score in this field.
* **Overall Score** (overall_score, number, optional): Provide an overall score for the work or project.
* **Overall Comment** (overall_comment, text, optional): Use this field to provide a final comment on the work or project.

## Tips

* Be respectful and professional in your tone and language.
* Focus on specific aspects of the work, rather than making general statements.
* Use the provided space to explain the reasons behind your scores and comments.
* Keep your feedback constructive and actionable, providing suggestions for improvement.
* Review the form for completeness before submitting.
