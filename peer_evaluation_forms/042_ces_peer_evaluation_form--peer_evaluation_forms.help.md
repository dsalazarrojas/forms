# CES Peer Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate peer evaluations within teams, allowing members to assess their peers' performance, identify areas of improvement, and provide constructive feedback. This process promotes a culture of growth, accountability, and open communication.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please complete each section thoroughly and honestly, keeping in mind that your feedback will be shared anonymously.
2. Ensure that you're evaluating a specific peer, providing feedback on their performance in a project or team context.
3. Answer each question with a clear and concise response, using the options provided or writing in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Evaluator Name**: Your name as the evaluator.
* **Name of Peer Being Evaluated**: The name of the team member being evaluated.
* **Project or Team Name**: The name of the project or team with which your peer was involved.
* **Teamwork and Collaboration (Collaboration Score)**: Rate the peer's contribution to group efforts, considering their ability to work effectively with others.
	+ 1 - Needs Significant Improvement
	+ 2 - Occasionally Contributes
	+ 3 - Consistent Contributor
	+ 4 - Exceptional Team Player
* **Reliability and Accountability (Reliability Score)**: Rate the peer's reliability and accountability, considering their ability to meet deadlines and keep commitments.
	+ 1
	+ 2
	+ 3
	+ 4
* **Communication Skills (Communication Clarity)**: Rate the peer's communication skills, considering their clarity and frequency of updates and interaction.
	+ 1
	+ 2
	+ 3
	+ 4
* **Quality of Work and Technical Skill (Work Quality)**: Rate the peer's quality of work and technical skills, considering the effectiveness and accuracy of their output.
	+ 1
	+ 2
	+ 3
	+ 4
* **One Area to Improve**: Identify one area where the peer can improve, providing specific and constructive feedback.
* **Greatest Professional Strength**: Identify the peer's greatest professional strength, highlighting their positive attributes.
* **Conflict Handling**: Rate the peer's ability to handle conflict or stress, considering their reaction in high-pressure situations.
	+ Poorly
	+ Fairly
	+ Well
	+ Professionally
* **Willingness to Take Initiative**: Rate the peer's willingness to take initiative, considering their ability to go beyond assigned tasks.
	+ Rarely
	+ Sometimes
	+ Often
	+ Always
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional feedback or comments you think are relevant to the peer's performance.
* **Understanding of Evaluation Confidentiality**: Confirm that you understand the feedback will be shared anonymously.
* **Date of Evaluation**: The date you're completing this evaluation.
* **I Understand**: Confirm that you've read and understand the form's purpose and guidelines.
