# Consulting Peer Evaluation Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather peer feedback on employee performance, strengths, and areas for improvement. The evaluation is used to support employee development and growth within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form for the employee you are evaluating.
2. Be honest and thorough in your responses.
3. Ensure you have not already provided feedback on this employee in the past.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Employee name** (`Employee name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the colleague being evaluated.
- **Evaluator name** (`Evaluator name`, text, required): Enter your full name for contact purposes if follow-up is needed.
- **Evaluator role** (`Evaluator role`, text, optional): Describe your role in relation to the employee being evaluated.
- **Teamwork and collaboration** (`Teamwork and collaboration`, select_one, required): Rate from 1 to 5 the employee's ability to work collaboratively with others.
- **Communication skills** (`Communication skills`, select_one, required): Rate from 1 to 5 the employee's clarity and responsiveness in communication.
- **Problem solving and initiative** (`Problem solving and initiative`, select_one, required): Rate from 1 to 5 the employee's ability to resolve issues proactively.
- **Key strengths observed** (`Key strengths observed`, text, optional): Provide specific examples of strengths you have observed in the employee.
- **Development areas** (`Development areas`, text, optional): Offer constructive feedback for the employee's growth and development.
- **Overall recommendation** (`Overall recommendation`, select_one, required): Choose whether you would strongly recommend, recommend, neutral, do not recommend, or strongly not recommend the employee for promotion or increased responsibility.
- **Follow-up required** (`Follow-up required`, select_one, optional): Select if HR or management should follow up with the employee.
