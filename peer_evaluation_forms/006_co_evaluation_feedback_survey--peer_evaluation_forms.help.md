# Co Evaluation Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect feedback on a colleague's performance, focusing on their communication, teamwork, reliability, problem-solving, and overall effectiveness. Your input will help us assess their skills and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your name and email as a form of identification.
2. Provide your email so that we can contact you for follow-up.
3. Select the date when this evaluation was conducted.
4. Enter the name of the colleague being evaluated.
5. Choose how long you have worked with this colleague.
6. Rate the colleague's communication effectiveness.
7. Give specific feedback on their communication style.
8. Rate their ability to work in a team.
9. Give specific comments on their teamwork contributions.
10. Rate their dependability and follow-through.
11. Rate their problem-solving abilities.
12. Rate their adaptability and flexibility.
13. Identify their main strengths.
14. Suggest areas for improvement.
15. Describe specific contributions they've made.
16. Indicate if you would like to collaborate with them again.
17. Suggest how highly to recommend them for future projects.
18. Add any other comments or feedback.
19. Choose whether this feedback can be shared with the colleague.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (<name>, text, required): 
  Your name is required for identification purposes.
* **Your Email** (<email>, email, required): 
  Your email is required for follow-up purposes.
* **Evaluation Date** (<evaluation_date, date, required): 
  The date when this evaluation was conducted.
* **Colleague Being Evaluated** (<colleague_name, text, required): 
  The full name of the peer being evaluated.
* **How Long Have You Worked Together** (<collaboration_duration, select_one, required): 
  Select the duration of collaboration with this colleague.
* **Communication Skills Rating** (<communication_rating, select_one, required): 
  Rate their communication skills effectiveness.
* **Communication Feedback** (<communication_feedback, text, required): 
  Give specific comments on their communication style.
* **Teamwork Rating** (<teamwork_rating, select_one, required): 
  Rate their ability to work in a team.
* **Teamwork Feedback** (<teamwork_feedback, text, optional): 
  Give specific comments on their teamwork contributions.
* **Reliability Rating** (<reliability_rating, select_one, required): 
  Rate their dependability and follow-through.
* **Problem Solving Rating** (<problem_solving_rating, select_one, required): 
  Rate their problem-solving abilities.
* **Adaptability Rating** (<adaptability_rating, select_one, required): 
  Rate their flexibility and adaptability.
* **Key Strengths** (<strengths, text, required): 
  Identify their main strengths.
* **Areas for Improvement** (<areas_for_improvement, text, required): 
  Suggest areas where they could improve.
* **Specific Contribution Examples** (<contribution_examples, text, optional): 
  Describe specific contributions they made.
* **Would You Work With Them Again** (<would_work_again, select_one, required): 
  Indicate if you would choose to collaborate with them again.
* **How Would You Recommend Them** (<recommendation_level, select_one, required): 
  Suggest the level of recommendation for future projects.
* **Additional Comments** (<additional_comments, text, optional): 
  Add any other comments or feedback.
* **Can This Feedback Be Shared** (<feedback_sharing, select_one, required): 
  Choose whether this feedback can be shared with the colleague.
