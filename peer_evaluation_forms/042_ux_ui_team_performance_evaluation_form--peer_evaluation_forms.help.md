# ux_ui_team_performance_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The UX/UI team performance evaluation form is a tool for internal peer feedback and evaluation to assess individual team members' performance across various skills and principles. It's meant to be a supportive and constructive platform for team growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Select "Yes" or "No" for each field, depending on your observation of the team member's performance.
2.  You can also provide comments or feedback on the "project description" field if you feel that it's relevant and necessary.
3.  Make sure to answer all questions as accurately and honestly as possible.
4.  Save and submit your evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **team_lead** (team_lead, select_multiple, optional): This field assesses whether the team member has acted as a strong leader on the team, taking charge of projects, motivating others, and ensuring the team's success. Indicate "Yes" if you've observed them performing well in these areas.
* **team_members** (team_members, select_one, optional): Choose "Yes" if you believe the team member has demonstrated good collaboration with other team members, promoting a positive team dynamic.
* **project_manager** (project_manager, select_multiple, optional): Indicate "Yes" if you've observed the team member handling project management tasks effectively, such as setting deadlines, coordinating with team members, and adjusting to changes.
* **project_description** (project_description, text, optional): Use this field to provide any additional comments or feedback on the team member's project management skills.
* **overall_rating** (overall_rating, number, optional): Rate the team member's overall performance on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is below average and 5 is above average.
* **feedback_points** (feedback_points, number, optional): This field is for providing specific points of feedback you'd like to mention, separate from your general overall rating.
* **design_principles** (design_principles, select_multiple, optional): Assess the team member's adherence to design principles such as user experience, user-centered design, accessibility, and others.
* **design_skills** (design_skills, select_multiple, optional): Choose "Yes" for areas where you believe the team member demonstrates strong design skills like visual design, usability, and information architecture.
* **communication_skills** (communication_skills, select_multiple, optional): This field examines the team member's ability to articulate and present ideas, concepts, and designs to others.
* **collaboration** (collaboration, select_one, optional): Indicate "Yes" if you've observed the team member actively working towards collaboration within the team.
* **project_planning** (project_planning, select_multiple, optional): Choose "Yes" for the team member's effective project planning, organization, and prioritization skills.
* **time_management** (time_management, select_multiple, optional): Assess the team member's ability to manage time, prioritize tasks, and meet deadlines.
* **organization** (organization, select_multiple, optional): Indicate "Yes" for the team member's ability to maintain a well-organized workspace, keep team members focused, and adjust to changing priorities.
* **problem_solving** (problem_solving, select_multiple, optional): Choose "Yes" for the team member's creative and effective problem-solving skills.
* **adaptability** (adaptability, select_multiple, optional): This field looks at the team member's ability to adapt to new situations, learn from feedback, and grow in their role.
* **self_motivation** (self_motivation, select_multiple, optional): Assess the team member's intrinsic motivation, drive, and enthusiasm for the project and the team's goals.
 
## Tips
- Be honest and constructive with your feedback to help the team member grow.
- Consider the context and the team member's role in the team and the project.
- Focus on specific, actionable feedback rather than general opinions.
