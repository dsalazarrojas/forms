# Peer Evaluation Form RWA 3 - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect peer evaluations for RWA 3, a performance review process. It allows reviewers to assess and provide feedback on their peers' work, helping to identify strengths and areas for improvement. This form is used to gather information on the peer evaluator's first name, last name, role, evaluation date, time spent, comments, rating, evaluation comments, reviewer comments, email, phone, evaluation form submission status, and a unique submission ID.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have a valid email address and phone number filled in the corresponding fields.
2. Answer all required fields (none are currently marked as required).
3. Select the correct rating and evaluation form submission status.
4. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the comments fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Role** (`role`, `text`, optional): Enter your role or position.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Time Spent** (`time_spent`, `number`, optional): Enter the amount of time you spent evaluating.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Rating** (`rating`, `select_one`, optional): Select a rating from 1 to 5.
* **Evaluation Comments** (`evaluation_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter your evaluation comments.
* **Reviewer Comments** (`reviewer_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter your reviewer comments.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Evaluation Form Submitted** (`evaluation_form_submitted`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether the evaluation form has been submitted.
* **Submission ID** (`submission_id`, `number`, optional): Enter a unique submission ID.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have filled in all required fields before submitting the form.
* If a field is not required, it's up to you to decide whether to fill it in or leave it blank.
* If you have any doubts or concerns about this form, please reach out to the relevant authorities for assistance.
