# Faculty Promotion Peer Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Faculty Promotion Peer Assessment Form is a tool used to gather evaluations and recommendations from peer faculty members on a candidate's qualifications and fit for promotion. It is essential to complete this form accurately and thoroughly, as it plays a crucial role in the promotion process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you are familiar with the candidate's work and qualifications.
2. Complete the form carefully, answering all required questions thoroughly.
3. Use the space provided for each section to provide detailed and specific feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Peer Name** (`peer_name`, text, required): Provide the name of the peer faculty member completing this form.
* **Faculty Promotion** (`faculty_promotion`, number, required): This field is not applicable as it's a number type and doesn't seem to be relevant. It's likely a placeholder or a redundant field. Please ignore it.
* **Prominent Works and Publications** (`prophets`, select_multiple, required): Check all that apply for the candidate's prominent works and publications.
	+ 'Yes': The candidate has published works that are widely recognized and respected in their field.
	+ 'No': The candidate does not have published works that are widely recognized and respected in their field.
* **Peers Suggestions** (`peers_suggestions`, select_multiple, optional): Check all that apply if you have any suggestions for the candidate's qualifications and fit for promotion.
	+ 'Yes': You have suggestions for the candidate's qualifications and fit for promotion.
	+ 'No': You do not have any suggestions.
* **Chair Recommendation** (`chair_recommendation`, select_one, required): Provide your recommendation for the candidate's promotion.
	+ 'Yes': You fully support the candidate's promotion.
	+ 'No': You do not support the candidate's promotion.

	+ 'Yes': You fully support the candidate's promotion.
