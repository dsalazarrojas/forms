# 360 Degree Leadership Evaluation - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to provide a comprehensive evaluation of a leader's performance and potential for advancement. It assesses their leadership competencies, behavior, and areas for improvement, helping to identify key strengths and weaknesses. The evaluation is used to provide a fair and objective assessment of their performance, taking into account multiple perspectives from various stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required information in the "Employee Information" section:
	* Enter your full name as the evaluator.
	* Enter your work email address.
	* Specify your department.
	* Enter your current job title.
	* Select your relationship to the evaluated employee (e.g., direct report, peer, supervisor, or external stakeholder).
	* Choose the evaluation period (e.g., last quarter, last half-year, last year, or project duration).
2. Evaluate the leader's performance in the "Leadership Competencies" section:
	* Rate their communication effectiveness from 1 (poor) to 10 (excellent).
	* Assess their decision-making skills, choosing from exceptional, strong, adequate, needs improvement, or unsatisfactory.
	* Rate their team development and mentoring abilities from outstanding, exceeds expectations, meets expectations, below expectations, or significantly below expectations.
	* Assess their strategic thinking and vision from excellent, good, average, below average, or poor.
	* Rate their collaboration and teamwork abilities from 1 (poor) to 10 (excellent).
	* Assess their innovation and initiative from regularly proposes new ideas, implements process improvements, encourages creative thinking, takes calculated risks, or resists change.
	* Rate their conflict resolution effectiveness from highly effective, effective, moderately effective, somewhat effective, or ineffective.
	* Assess their emotional intelligence and empathy from 1 (poor) to 10 (excellent).
3. Provide constructive feedback in the "Constructive Feedback" section:
	* Describe specific examples of their strengths.
	* Suggest areas for development and recommended training or experiences.
	* Offer concrete examples of their leadership behaviors.
4. Complete the form by rating the leader's overall performance and providing a promotion recommendation:
	* Rate their overall leadership performance from 1 (unsatisfactory) to 10 (outstanding).
	* Indicate whether you would strongly recommend, recommend, be neutral, do not recommend, or strongly oppose their promotion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Employee Information

* **Full name of evaluator**: Enter your full name as the evaluator.
* **Email address**: Enter your work email address.
* **Department**: Specify your department.
* **Job title**: Enter your current job title.
* **Relationship to evaluated employee**: Select your relationship to the person being evaluated (e.g., direct report, peer, supervisor, or external stakeholder).
* **Evaluation period**: Choose the evaluation period (e.g., last quarter, last half-year, last year, or project duration).

### Leadership Competencies

* **Communication effectiveness**: Rate their communication effectiveness from 1 (poor) to 10 (excellent).
* **Decision-making skills**: Assess their decision-making skills, choosing from exceptional, strong, adequate, needs improvement, or unsatisfactory.
* **Team development and mentoring**: Rate their team development and mentoring abilities from outstanding, exceeds expectations, meets expectations, below expectations, or significantly below expectations.
* **Strategic thinking and vision**: Assess their strategic thinking and vision from excellent, good, average, below average, or poor.
* **Collaboration and teamwork abilities**: Rate their collaboration and teamwork abilities from 1 (poor) to 10 (excellent).
* **Innovation and initiative**: Assess their innovation and initiative from regularly proposes new ideas, implements process improvements, encourages creative thinking, takes calculated risks, or resists change.
* **Conflict resolution effectiveness**: Rate their conflict resolution effectiveness from highly effective, effective, moderately effective, somewhat effective, or ineffective.
* **Emotional intelligence and empathy**: Assess their emotional intelligence and empathy from 1 (poor) to 10 (excellent).
* **Accountability for results**: Assess their accountability for results from always accountable, usually accountable, sometimes accountable, rarely accountable, or not accountable.

### Constructive Feedback

* **Key leadership strengths observed**: Describe specific examples of their strengths.
* **Areas for development**: Suggest areas for development and recommended training or experiences.
* **Specific examples of leadership**: Provide concrete examples of their leadership behaviors.

### Overall Performance and Promotion Recommendation

* **Overall leadership performance**: Rate their overall leadership performance from 1 (unsatisfactory) to 10 (outstanding).
* **Promotion recommendation**: Indicate whether you would strongly recommend, recommend, be neutral, do not recommend, or strongly oppose their promotion.
* **Additional comments or observations**: Provide any other feedback or suggestions you may have.

## Tips

* Be specific and objective when assessing the leader's performance.
* Provide concrete examples and evidence to support your ratings and feedback.
* Consider multiple perspectives when evaluating the leader's strengths and weaknesses.
* Be honest and transparent in your assessment and feedback.
* Avoid biases and personal opinions.
