# Student Peer Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from students on their peers' performance, behavior, and overall contribution to class discussions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Student ID in the `Student ID` field.
2. Enter your name in the `Name` field.
3. Enter your peer's name in the `Peer Name` field.
4. Select a rating for `Overall Rating` on a scale from 1-5.
5. Provide your feedback on the content and technical skills of your peer in the `Feedback on Content` and `Feedback on Technical Skills` fields, respectively.
6. Add any additional comments about your peer's performance in the `Comments` field.
7. Evaluate your peer's reliability on a scale from 1-5 in the `Peer Reliability` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Student ID** (Student ID, number, required: false)
Enter your unique Student ID to help identify and track your feedback.

### **Name** (Name, text, required: false)
Enter your name to associate your feedback with your identity.

### **Peer Name** (Peer Name, text, required: false)
Enter your peer's name to identify the person you're evaluating.

### **Overall Rating** (Overall Rating, select_one, required: false)
Select a rating from 1 to 5 to indicate your overall assessment of your peer's performance.

### **Feedback on Content** (Feedback on Content, note, required: false)
Provide specific examples of your peer's strengths and areas for improvement regarding the content of their contributions.

### **Feedback on Technical Skills** (Feedback on Technical Skills, note, required: false)
Share your thoughts on your peer's technical skills, such as their ability to apply concepts or solve problems.

### **Comments** (Comments, note, required: false)
Add any additional comments or suggestions to help your peer grow and improve.

### **Peer Reliability** (Peer Reliability, select_one, required: false)
Evaluate your peer's reliability on a scale from 1-5, considering their commitment to their work and responsibilities.
