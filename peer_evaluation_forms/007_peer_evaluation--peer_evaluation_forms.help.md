# Peer Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Peer Evaluation form is designed to collect feedback, ratings, and comments from peers on a specific piece of work. This form helps identify areas of strength and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately, providing constructive feedback.
2. Rate the work based on the criteria provided.
3. Add any additional comments you have on the work.
4. Choose the correct Peer Evaluation Form from the dropdown menu.
5. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Questions** (`feedback_questions`): Provide your honest feedback on the work. Be specific and concrete in your comments.
* **Rating** (`rating`): Rate the work based on the provided criteria. This helps to quantify your feedback.
* **Peer Evaluation** (`peer_evaluation`): Provide any additional comments or observations about the work.
* **Feedback Comments** (`feedback_comments`): Add any additional comments or suggestions for improvement.
* **Date Given** (`date_given`): Enter the date you completed this evaluation.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`): Enter your name and title for reference.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Peer Evaluation Form** (`peer_evaluation_form`): Select the correct Peer Evaluation Form type (e.g., Peer Evaluation Form 1, Peer Evaluation Form 2, or Peer Evaluation Form 3). This helps to categorize and filter the feedback.

## Tips

* Be honest and constructive in your feedback.
* Provide specific examples or instances that support your comments.
* Proofread your answers before submitting the form.
* Make sure to choose the correct Peer Evaluation Form type.
* Keep the form for your records and reference later.
