# Community Health Program Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help healthcare providers assess your health needs and deliver personalized services. Please complete the form accurately to ensure a successful check-in process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your **Patient Full Name** in the provided field.
2. Select your **Date of Birth** from the calendar or enter it manually.
3. Describe your **Primary Health Concern for Today's Visit** in the provided text field.
4. List your **Current Medications** if applicable. This includes prescriptions and over-the-counter medications.
5. If you have any **Known Allergies**, list them in the provided field. These can include food, medication, or environmental allergies.
6. Choose your current **Pain Level** from the provided scale:
	* 0 - No Pain
	* 1-9 - Mild to Extreme Pain
7. If you have an **Insurance Provider Name**, please enter it for reference.
8. Choose your preferred **Contact Method** for follow-up:
	* Phone Call
	* Email
	* SMS Text
9. Enter the **Emergency Contact Full Name** who we should contact in case of an emergency.
10. Provide the **Emergency Contact Phone Number** for quick contact in case of an emergency.
11. Confirm your consent to receive **Health Services Today** by selecting "Yes, I consent" from the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Full Name**: This is the name of the person checking in for the Community Health Program.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth to verify your identity.
* **Primary Health Concern for Today's Visit**: Briefly describe why you are checking in for the Community Health Program.
* **Current Medications**: List all prescriptions and over-the-counter medications you are currently taking.
* **Known Allergies**: List any allergies you may have, including food, medication, or environmental allergies.
* **Current Pain Level**: Choose a number from 0 to 10 to indicate your current level of pain.
* **Insurance Provider Name**: If you have an insurance provider, enter their name for reference.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Choose how you prefer to be contacted for follow-up:
	+ Phone Call
	+ Email
	+ SMS Text
* **Emergency Contact Full Name**: Enter the name of the person we should contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Consent to Receive Health Services Today**: Confirm your consent to receive health services by selecting "Yes, I consent".
