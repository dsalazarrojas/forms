# Emergency Room Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emergency Room Check In Form is designed to gather necessary information from patients in emergency room situations. This information is used to quickly assess and treat any health issues that may be present.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure your medical information is up to date.
2. Start by filling in your personal details, such as your first name and last name.
3. Enter your date of birth to ensure we have a record of your medical history.
4. Provide the contact information for your emergency contact, including their name and phone number.
5. Describe the date and reason for your current visit.
6. If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please list them in the "Medical Condition" field.
7. If you have any medications, list the name and dosage in the "Medication Name" and "Medication Dosage" fields.
8. If you have a patient medical record, please provide it in the "Patient Medical Record" field.
9. Enter your insurance provider and group information, if applicable.
10. If you have any upcoming medical appointments, list the date and time in the "Date Of Next Visit" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Your first name, as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Your last name, as it appears on your identification.
* **Date Of Birth**: The date of your birth, in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Emergency Contact First Name**: The first name of the contact person you want us to notify in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name**: The last name of the contact person you want us to notify in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: The phone number of the contact person you want us to notify in case of an emergency.
* **Date Of Injury**: The date of the injury or condition that brought you to the emergency room.
* **Description Of Injury**: A brief description of your injury or condition.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: The relationship between you and your emergency contact (e.g. Mom, Dad, Brother, Sister, Husband, Wife).
* **Emergency Contact Address**: The address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact City**: The city of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact State**: The state of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Zip**: The ZIP code of your emergency contact.
* **Medical Condition**: A list of any medical conditions you have or are experiencing (e.g. Heart Failure, High Blood Pressure, Asthma, etc.).
* **Date Of Last Visit**: The date of your last medical visit.
* **Time Of Last Visit**: The time of your last medical visit.
* **Reason For Visit**: A brief description of the reason for your current visit.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (again): This field is optional and repeats "Emergency Contact Relationship" field, it's not actually used in this form.
* **Medical History**: A brief description of any medical history you have.
* **Medication Name**: The name of any medication you're currently taking.
* **Medication Dosage**: The dosage of any medication you're currently taking.
* **Patient Medical Record**: A copy of your patient medical record, if you have one.
* **Insurance Provider**: The name of your insurance provider.
* **Insurance Group**: The group number of your insurance provider.
* **Date Of Next Visit**: The date and time of your next scheduled medical appointment.

Note: Fields with asterisk (\*) are required to be filled in.
