# iv_therapy_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The iv_therapy_intake_form is a patient registration form for IV therapy. This form is designed to collect patient information for treatment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with your first name, last name, date of birth, medical condition, and treatment type. These are all required fields to ensure that we can provide the best care for you.
2. Enter your doctor's name and contact information, including their phone number and email address.
3. If necessary, fill out your address for our records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `text`, required): Please enter your current medical condition.
* **Treatment Type** (`treatment_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of treatment you are undergoing (IV Antibiotics, IV Infusion, IV Therapy, IV Support).
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, `text`, optional): Please enter the name of your treating doctor.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, optional): Please enter a contact number for your doctor.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of your doctor.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to provide your address for our records, please do so here.

## Tips

* Ensure that you fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, do not hesitate to ask a staff member for assistance.
* Please double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
