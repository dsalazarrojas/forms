# Chronic Disease Management Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from patients to facilitate effective chronic disease management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the **Full Name** field with your first and last names.
2. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
3. Select your **Gender** from the provided options.
4. Enter your **Email Address** for appointment reminders.
5. Provide your **Phone Number** as your primary contact number.
6. Choose your preferred contact method from the options provided.
7. Fill out your **Street Address** and **City**, **State or Province**, and **Postal Code** fields for your contact address.
8. Enter your **Insurance Provider** and **Insurance ID Number** for your insurance information.
9. Select your **Primary Care Physician** and enter their phone number.
10. List all your current **Medications**.
11. List any **Medication Allergies** you have.
12. Select your **Smoking Status**.
13. Indicate your **Alcohol Use** frequency.
14. Choose your level of **Physical Activity**.
15. Enter the **Emergency Contact Name**, **Phone Number**, and **Relationship**.
16. Enter your **Main Health Concern**, what brings you to this program.
17. Set your **Health Goals**.
18. Describe any **Barriers to Care** that you face.
19. Provide your **Consent to Treat**, indicating your willingness to receive treatment.
20. Check your **Consent to Privacy Acknowledgment**, indicating your understanding of your privacy rights.
21. Fill out any **Additional Comments**, if you have any further information to share with your healthcare provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your first and last name. This field is required for identification purposes.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format of YYYY-MM-DD. This field is required for understanding your age and medical history.
* **Gender**: Select your gender from the provided options (Male, Female, Non-binary, Prefer not to say, Prefer to self-describe). This field is required to better understand your healthcare needs.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for appointment reminders. This field is required for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your primary contact phone number. This field is required for communication purposes.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Choose how you would like to be contacted (Phone Call, Text Message, Email, Mail). This field is required to tailor communication to your preferences.
* **Street Address**: Enter your complete street address. This field is required for contact and appointment purposes.
* **City**: Enter the city of your contact address. This field is required for contact and appointment purposes.
* **State or Province**: Enter your state or province of your contact address. This field is required for contact and appointment purposes.
* **Postal Code**: Enter your postal code of your contact address. This field is required for contact and appointment purposes.
* **Insurance Provider**: Enter your insurance provider name. This field is required for billing and insurance purposes.
* **Insurance ID Number**: Enter your insurance member ID number. This field is required for billing and insurance purposes.
* **Group Number**: If applicable, enter your group number. This field is not required but may be necessary for billing and insurance purposes.
* **Primary Care Physician**: Enter the name of your primary care physician. This field is required for medical history and treatment purposes.
* **PCP Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of your primary care physician's office. This field is required for communication purposes.
* **Current Medications**: List all your current medications. This field is required for medical history and treatment purposes.
* **Medication Allergies**: List any medications that cause allergies or adverse reactions. This field is required for medical safety.
* **Other Allergies**: List any other allergies, such as food or environmental. This field is not required but recommended for medical safety.
* **Previous Hospitalizations**: Select the number of times you've been hospitalized in the past year. This field is required for medical history and risk assessment.
* **Smoking Status**: Select your current smoking status. This field is required for risk assessment and treatment purposes.
* **Alcohol Use**: Indicate how often you drink. This field is required for risk assessment and treatment purposes.
* **Physical Activity**: Select your level of physical activity. This field is required for treatment and risk assessment.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency. This field is required for emergency contact purposes.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact. This field is required for emergency contact purposes.
* **Relationship**: Choose the relationship of the emergency contact. This field is required for emergency contact purposes.
* **Main Health Concern**: Describe the main reason you're coming to this program. This field is required for treatment and care planning.
* **Health Goals**: Describe your health goals. This field is required for treatment and care planning.
* **Barriers to Care**: Describe any barriers to managing your health. This field is not required but recommended for treatment and care planning.
* **Consent to Treat**: Check if you consent to treatment. This field is required for consent and treatment purposes.
* **Consent to Privacy Acknowledgment**: Check if you understand your privacy rights. This field is required for patient rights and consent purposes.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional information you'd like to share with your healthcare provider. This field is not required but recommended for comprehensive care.

Tips:

* Be as accurate and complete as possible when filling out the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
* This form is designed to gather information for chronic disease management; if you have any questions or concerns, ask your healthcare provider.
* Please review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any additional comments or information, please share them with your healthcare provider.
