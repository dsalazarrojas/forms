# Mental Health Facility Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record a patient's discharge information from a mental health facility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the mental health facility the patient is discharging from.
* **Discharge Date** (`discharge_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of discharge. This should be in the format MM/DD/YY (month/day/year).
* **Discharge Time** (`discharge_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of discharge. This should be in the format 12:00 (hour 12:00 PM or 13:00).
* **Reason for Discharge** (`reason_for_discharge`, text, optional): Enter the reason the patient is being discharged. This could be due to improvement, transfer to another facility, or other reasons.
* **Next Care Provider** (`next_care_provider`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of the next care provider.
* **Follow up Instructions** (`follow_up_instructions`, note, optional): Enter any follow-up instructions or advice for the patient.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the contact number of the patient's family or next of kin.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the contact email of the patient's family or next of kin.
