# Neurotoxic Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Neurotoxic Questionnaire is designed to collect necessary patient information for accurate neurotoxicity assessment and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully review each field and ensure accuracy.
- Provide all required fields (marked as "required").
- Enter your first name, last name, and date of birth accurately.
- Select your gender from the provided options.
- Enter your phone number (if applicable) and email address.
- Enter your address for proper contact and treatment follow-up.
- If applicable, provide your current health status.
- If necessary, provide further details about your condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (patient_first_name, text, required): Enter your first name as it is spelled on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (last_name, text, required): Enter your last name as it is spelled on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (date_of_birth, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Gender** (patient_gender, select_one, required): Select your gender from the provided options (Male, Female, Other).
* **Phone Number** (contact_phone, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number if you have one.
* **Email** (email_address, email, required): Enter a valid email address for secure and efficient communication.
* **Address** (address, text, required): Enter your home or contact address for proper follow-up and treatment.
* **Health Status** (health_status, note, optional): Provide any relevant information about your current health status.
* **Further Details** (further_details, text, optional): If necessary, provide any additional information about your condition or concerns.
