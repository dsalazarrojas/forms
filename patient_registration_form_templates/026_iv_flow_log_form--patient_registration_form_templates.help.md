# IV Flow Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IV Flow Log Form is a tool used to record and track IV fluid administration events. It is designed to provide accurate and detailed information about the fluid administered, patient weight, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Patient Name** field with the name of the patient.
2. Enter the **Physician Name** field with the name of the physician who is administering the IV fluid.
3. Enter the total amount of **Total Fluid Administered** in milliliters (mL), liters (L), or gallons (G).
4. Enter the **Patient Weight** in kilograms (kg).
5. Enter the **Fluid Administered Rate** in milliliters per hour (mL/h), liters per hour (L/h), or gallons per hour (G/h).
6. Enter the **Flow Rate** in milliliters per minute (mL/min), liters per minute (L/min), or gallons per minute (G/min).
7. Enter the duration of the IV flow in **Duration In Hours**.
8. Enter any additional **Notes** about the IV fluid administration event.
9. Select the **Units** of the fluid administered from the dropdown menu (milliliters, liters, or gallons).
10. Select the **Value** of the fluid administered from the dropdown menu (250, 500, 750, 1000, 1250, 1500, 1750, 2000).
11. (Optional) If you need to enter a rate other than those listed in the **Units** dropdown menu, enter the **Fluid Administered Rate Hint** field to provide additional context.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: Enter the full name of the patient as it appears on their medical record.
* **Physician Name**: Enter the name of the physician responsible for administering the IV fluid.
* **Total Fluid Administered**: Enter the total amount of IV fluid administered, either in milliliters (mL), liters (L), or gallons (G).
* **Patient Weight**: Enter the patient's weight in kilograms (kg).
* **Fluid Administered Rate**: Enter the rate at which IV fluid is being administered, either in milliliters per hour (mL/h), liters per hour (L/h), or gallons per hour (G/h).
* **Flow Rate**: Enter the rate at which IV fluid is being administered, either in milliliters per minute (mL/min), liters per minute (L/min), or gallons per minute (G/min).
* **Duration In Hours**: Enter the duration of the IV fluid administration event in hours.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional information about the IV fluid administration event.
* **Fluid Administered Rate Units**: Select the units of the fluid administered from the dropdown menu (milliliters, liters, or gallons).
* **Value**: Select the value of the fluid administered from the dropdown menu (250, 500, 750, 1000, 1250, 1500, 1750, 2000).

## Tips

* Make sure to enter the patient's name correctly to ensure accurate identification.
* Ensure that the units and value selected match the actual amount of fluid administered.
* Enter the flow rate carefully to ensure accurate monitoring and recording.
* Use the **Notes** field to provide additional context or details about the IV fluid administration event.
* If unsure about any field, refer to the relevant medical guidelines or consult with a medical professional.
