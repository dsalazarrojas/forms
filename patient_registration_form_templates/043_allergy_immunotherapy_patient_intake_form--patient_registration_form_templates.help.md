# Allergy Immunotherapy Patient Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for patients to register for allergy immunotherapy, a treatment that involves administering small amounts of allergen extracts to build up tolerance to specific allergens.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1.  Start by filling out the patient's personal information, including their first name, last name, date of birth, and contact details.
2.  Select the patient's gender from the dropdown menu.
3.  Enter the patient's email address and phone number.
4.  Provide the patient's mailing address.
5.  Enter the emergency contact person's name and phone number.
6.  Specify the relationship between the patient and the emergency contact person.
7.  Select the patient's insurance provider and enter their policy number.
8.  Enter the patient's medical information, including any previous allergy treatments and current medications.
9.  Answer questions about the patient's allergies, including the duration and types of allergies, specific triggers, and symptom severity.
10.  Answer questions about the patient's medical history, including asthma diagnosis, heart conditions, lung conditions, and immune system disorders.
11.  Answer questions about the patient's treatment goals and availability for treatment.
12.  Sign and date the form to confirm the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your full first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your full last name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Gender**: Select your gender from the dropdown menu.
* **Email Address**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Mailing Address**: Enter your full mailing address.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Specify the relationship between you and the emergency contact person.
* **Insurance Provider**: Select your insurance provider from the dropdown menu.
* **Insurance ID**: Enter your insurance policy number.
* **Insurance Group Number**: Enter your insurance group number (optional).
* **Primary Care Physician**: Enter the name of your primary care physician.
* **PCP Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of your primary care physician.
* **Referring Physician**: If you have a referring physician, enter their name (optional).
* **Reason for Immunotherapy**: Answer the question "Why are you seeking treatment?".
* **Duration of Allergies**: Select how long you've had allergies.
* **Types of Allergies**: Select all the types of allergies you have (e.g., seasonal, perennial, pet allergies, etc.).
* **Specific Allergy Triggers**: List any specific triggers for your allergies.
* **Symptom Severity**: Rate the severity of your symptoms.
* **Current Symptoms**: Select all the current symptoms you are experiencing.
* **Previous Allergy Treatments**: Select any previous allergy treatments you have tried.
* **Current Medications**: List any current medications you are taking.
* **Medication Allergies**: If you have any allergies to medications, list them.
* **Asthma Diagnosis**: Answer whether you have an asthma diagnosis.
* **Asthma Medications**: If you have an asthma diagnosis, list any medications you are taking.
* **Heart Conditions**: Answer whether you have any heart conditions.
* **Lung Conditions**: Answer whether you have any lung conditions.
* **Immune System Disorders**: Answer whether you have any immune system disorders.
* **Cancer History**: Answer whether you have any cancer history.
* **Pregnancy Status**: Answer whether you are pregnant or nursing.
* **Previous Immunotherapy**: Answer whether you have had previous immunotherapy.
* **Reason for Stopping Immunotherapy**: If you have stopped immunotherapy, explain why.
* **Beta-Blocker Use**: Answer whether you take beta-blockers.
* **ACE Inhibitor Use**: Answer whether you take ACE inhibitors.
* **Ability to Communicate Symptoms**: Answer whether you can communicate your symptoms clearly.
* **Transportation Available**: Answer how you get to appointments.
* **Availability for Treatment**: Select when you are available for treatment.
* **Treatment Goals**: Specify your treatment goals.
* **Questions or Concerns**: If you have any questions or concerns, list them.
* **How Did You Hear About Us?**: Answer how you heard about our clinic.
* **Consent to Treatment**: Confirm that you consent to treatment.
* **HIPAA Acknowledgment**: Confirm that you have read the HIPAA notice.
* **Patient Signature**: Confirm that you have signed this form accurately.
* **Signature Date**: Date and sign this form.

Please review this form carefully and ensure that you provide accurate and thorough information. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to ask.
