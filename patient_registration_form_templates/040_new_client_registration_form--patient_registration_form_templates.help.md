# new_client_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect demographic information and satisfaction levels of new clients, likely for a medical or health-related context.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Make sure to use a valid email address.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select your overall satisfaction level with your experience in the "Overall Satisfaction" field.
6. Indicate your life status in the "Life Status" field.
7. Enter your expectations in the "Expectations" field.
8. Consent to the agreement terms by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Consent to Agreement Terms" field.
9. Select your overall satisfaction level with your life status in the "Overall Satisfaction Life Status" field.
10. Select your overall satisfaction level with your expectations in the "Overall Satisfaction Expectations" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`select_one`, required): Select your overall satisfaction level with your experience.
* **Life Status** (`select_multiple`, required): Indicate your life status.
* **Expectations** (`text`, required): Enter your expectations.
* **Consent to Agreement Terms** (`select_one`, required): Select "Yes" or "No" to consent to the agreement terms.
* **Overall Satisfaction Life Status** (`select_one`, required): Select your overall satisfaction level with your life status.
* **Overall Satisfaction Expectations** (`select_one`, required): Select your overall satisfaction level with your expectations.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and valid information in each field.
- Review the agreement terms before consenting to them.
