# hipaa_medical_history_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hipaa_medical_history_form is a medical history form designed to collect information from patients to better understand their medical background.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Fill out each field with the requested information.
3. Ensure all required fields are completed, indicated by an asterisk (\*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information**:
	+ (1, text, required: false): This section is for providing general information about the patient.
* **Contact Details**:
	+ (2, text, required: false): Please provide the patient's contact information, including name, address, phone number, and any other relevant details.
* **Symptoms**:
	+ (3, text, required: false): Describe the patient's symptoms, including any pain, discomfort, or health concerns.
* **Medications**:
	+ (4, text, required: false): List all medications currently being taken by the patient, including dosage and frequency.
* **Allergies**:
	+ (5, text, required: false): List any allergies or intolerances the patient has, including severity and treatment.
* **Drug Use**:
	+ (6, text, required: false): Describe any past or current substance use, including frequency and quantity.
* **Family Medical History**:
	+ (7, text, required: false): Provide information about the patient's family medical history, including any inherited conditions or health concerns.
