# dropbox_patient_intake_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect patient information for intake purposes at a medical facility or practice.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "User First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "User Last Name" field.
3. Provide your valid email address in the "User Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "User Phone" field.
5. Enter the date of your visit in the "Date" field.
6. Enter the time of your visit in the "Time" field.
7. Indicate whether you have a medical record by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Medical Record ID" field.
8. If you have a medical provider, select "Yes" or "No" in the "Medical Provider" field (optional).
9. Upload any relevant medical records in the "Upload Medical Record" field.
10. Check the box to confirm your consent in the "User Consent" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User First Name (User First Name, text, required)**: Enter the first name of the patient.
* **User Last Name (User Last Name, text, required)**: Enter the last name of the patient.
* **User Email (User Email, email, required)**: Enter a valid email address.
* **User Phone (User Phone, text, required)**: Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Date (Date, date, required)**: Enter the date of the patient's visit.
* **Time (Time, time, required)**: Enter the time of the patient's visit.
* **Medical Record ID (Medical Record ID, select_one, required)**: Check if the patient has a medical record.
* **Medical Provider (Medical Provider, select_multiple, optional)**: Check if the patient has a medical provider (if applicable).
* **Upload Medical Record (Upload Medical Record, text, required)**: Upload any relevant medical records.
* **User Consent (User Consent, note, required)**: Check the box to confirm consent.
