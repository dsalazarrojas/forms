# new-patient-registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The new-patient-registration form is designed to collect basic information from patients who do not have a pre-existing account with the healthcare provider. This form is intended to be used for new patients, and it ensures that the provider has accurate and up-to-date information about the patient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the text field provided.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the text field provided.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the correct format (e.g. `[patient_email@example.com](mailto:patient_email@example.com)`).
4. Provide your **Phone** number in the text field provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your first name as it is listed on your official identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your last name as it is listed on your official identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: `false`): Enter your email address where you would like to be contacted for appointment reminders and other communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted for appointment reminders and other communication.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have not already submitted this form before resubmitting it.
* If you have any issues with the form, please contact the healthcare provider's support team for assistance.
