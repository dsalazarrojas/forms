# plastic_surgery_patient_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "plastic_surgery_patient_intake_form" is designed for patients to provide accurate and complete information about themselves before undergoing a plastic surgery procedure. This form helps the medical staff to understand the patient's medical history, conditions, and contact information, making it essential for a successful surgery and post-operative care.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully and ensure you understand each question.
* Provide accurate and complete information for each field.
* Answer all questions truthfully, as this information will be used to assess your suitability for the surgery and plan your care.
* Double-check your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, text, optional): This section is where you can provide information about yourself, such as your name, date of birth, and contact information.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Please describe your medical history, including any previous surgeries, medical conditions, or allergies.
* **Procedure Understanding** (`procedure_understanding`, text, optional): In this section, explain your understanding of the planned surgery, including what you hope to achieve and any questions or concerns you may have.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): Provide the contact information of your emergency contact, including their name, phone number, and address.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Confirm the emergency contact information provided above.
* **Patient Conditions** (`patient_conditions`, text, optional): Describe any medical conditions that may affect your recovery or the success of the surgery, such as medication allergies or previous injuries.
