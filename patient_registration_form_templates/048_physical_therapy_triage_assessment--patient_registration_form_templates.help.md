# Physical Therapy Triage Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a physical therapy triage assessment, which is a preliminary evaluation of a patient's condition to determine the best course of action for treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the patient's information (Patient Information section)
2. Provide your contact information (Contact Information section)
3. Answer medical history questions (Medical History section)
4. Answer assessment questions (Assessment Questions section)
5. Select any symptoms you are experiencing (Symptoms section)
6. Choose a diagnosis (Diagnosis section)
7. Sign the form (Patient Signature section)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (`Patient 1`, `Patient 2`, `select_one`): Select the correct patient information from the list.
* **Contact Information** (`text`): Enter your contact information.
* **Medical History** (`select_multiple`):
	+ Question 1: Please answer this medical history question.
	+ Question 2: Please answer this medical history question.
* **Assessment Questions** (`date`): Answer the assessment questions, including date.
* **Symptoms** (`select_one`):
	+ Symptom 1: Please select any symptoms you are experiencing.
	+ Symptom 2: Please select any additional symptoms you are experiencing.
* **Diagnosis** (`select_multiple`):
	+ Diagnosis 1: Please choose a diagnosis.
	+ Diagnosis 2: Please choose another diagnosis.
* **Treatment Plan** (`text`): Enter any information about your treatment plan.
* **Patient Signature** (`note`): Sign here to acknowledge that you have completed the form.
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date you completed the form.
* **Healthcare Provider** (`select_one`):
	+ Provider 1: Please select a healthcare provider.
	+ Provider 2: Please select another healthcare provider.
* **Medical Staff** (`select_one`):
	+ Staff 1: Please select a medical staff member.
	+ Staff 2: Please select another medical staff member.
* **Other Information** (`text`): Enter any additional information.
* **Patient Comments** (`text`): Enter any comments or concerns you have.
