# Macro Coaching Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your macro-nutrient goals and dietary preferences. This data will help your coach provide you with personalized guidance and support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out each section completely and accurately.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand its purpose and relevance.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please ask your coach for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Client Information
* **Client Information** (`client_info`, text, required): This section is for your basic contact information. Please fill out your name and any other relevant details.
### Name
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your full name, as you would like it to be displayed in our system.
### Diet Type
* **Diet Type** (`diet_type`, select_one, required): Select your preferred diet type from the available options:
	+ Macro
	+ Keto
	+ Low Carb
	+ High Carb
	+ Vegetarian
	+ Vegan
### Workout Frequency
* **Workout Frequency** (`workout_frequency`, select_multiple, required): Choose your workout frequency from the available options:
	+ Daily
	+ 3-4 Times a Week
	+ 1-2 Times a Week
	+ Rarely
### Macro Goals
* **Macros Goal** (`macros_goal`, number, required): Enter your daily macro-nutrient goal, which represents the total amount of macros you aim to consume in a day.
### Macro Intake
* **Macro Intake** (`macros_intake`, number, required): Enter your daily macro-nutrient intake, which represents the total amount of macros you actually consume in a day.
### Workout Goal
* **Workout Goal** (`workout_goal`, number, required): Enter your workout goal, which represents the number of workouts you aim to perform in a week.
### Weight Goal
* **Weight Goal** (`weight_goal`, number, required): Enter your weight goal, which represents the amount of weight you aim to lose or gain in a week.
