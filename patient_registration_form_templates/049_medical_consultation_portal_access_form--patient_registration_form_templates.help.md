# Medical Consultation Portal Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Consultation Portal Access Form is used to facilitate patient access to the medical consultation portal. It is a one-time form that requires information about the patient to verify their identity and ensure accurate medical consultations. The portal is not mandatory, but it provides patients with easier access to consult with medical experts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's name in the **Patient Name** field.
2. Enter the patient's email in the **email** field.
3. Select the date and time of the appointment request in the **Appointment Request Date** and **Appointment Request Time** fields.
4. Choose the doctor(s) requested to consult with in the **Doctor Request** field.
5. Enter the patient's medical record number in the **Medical Record Number** field.
6. Enter the patient's phone number in the **Patient Phone** field.
7. Enter the patient's address in the **Address** field.
8. Select the medical specialty the patient is seeking in the **Medical Specialty** field.
9. Enter any additional message or comment in the **Patient Message** field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **email**: Enter the patient's email address for communication and updates.
* **Appointment Request Date**: Enter the date of the appointment request in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **Appointment Request Time**: Enter the time of the appointment request in the format "HH:MM AM/PM".
* **Doctor Request**: Choose the doctor(s) you are requesting to consult with.
* **Medical Record Number**: Enter the patient's medical record number.
* **Patient Phone**: Enter the patient's contact phone number.
* **Address**: Enter the patient's address.
* **Medical Specialty**: Select the medical specialty the patient is seeking consultation for.
* **Patient Message**: Enter any additional comments or messages about the consultation (optional).

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any field, please do not hesitate to ask for help.
* The information provided will be used to verify the patient's identity and ensure accurate medical consultations.
