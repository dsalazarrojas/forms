# Ayurvedic Case Diagnosis Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ayurvedic Case Diagnosis Form is a collection of questions and fields designed to gather information about a patient's physical characteristics and health concerns to help diagnose and provide treatment plans in the Ayurvedic system of medicine.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Patient Full Name" field with your full legal name.
2. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the standard format (DD/MM/YYYY).
3. Select your gender from the "Gender" field.
4. Describe your natural physical build in the "Body Frame and Build" field.
5. Choose your skin type from the "Skin Characteristics" field.
6. Select your appetite and digestion type from the "Appetite and Digestion" field.
7. Choose your sleep quality from the "Sleep Quality" field.
8. Select your bowel habits from the "Bowel Habits" field.
9. Select your mental nature from the "Mental and Emotional Nature" field (optional).
10. In the "Clinical Observations" section, describe your tongue appearance and coating, and any observations made during the consultation.
11. In the "Nadi Pariksha (Pulse Reading)" field, note any relevant pulse reading observations.
12. Finally, summarize your recommended treatment plan in the "Recommended Treatment Plan" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Full Name (text, required)**: Please enter your full legal name.
* **Date of Birth (date, required)**: Enter your date of birth in the standard format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Gender (select_one, required)**: Select your gender from the available options.
* **Body Frame and Build (select_one, required)**: Describe your natural physical build.
* **Skin Characteristics (select_one, required)**: Choose your skin type from the options.
* **Appetite and Digestion (select_one, required)**: Select your appetite and digestion type.
* **Sleep Quality (select_one, required)**: Choose your sleep quality from the options.
* **Bowel Habits (select_one, required)**: Select your bowel habits from the options.
* **Mental and Emotional Nature (select_multiple, optional)**: Choose your mental and emotional nature from the available options.
* **Tongue Appearance and Coating (text, optional)**: Describe your tongue appearance and coating during the consultation.
* **Nadi Pariksha (Pulse Reading) (text, optional)**: Note any relevant pulse reading observations.
* **Recommended Treatment Plan (text, optional)**: Summarize your recommended treatment plan.
