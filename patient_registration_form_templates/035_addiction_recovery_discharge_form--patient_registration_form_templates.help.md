# Addiction Recovery Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Addiction Recovery Discharge Form is used to document the discharge process of patients from an addiction recovery program. It ensures that patients have met the necessary goals and completed any required steps, and outlines the aftercare process to be followed after leaving the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the fields below, ensuring you answer each question accurately and fully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Full Name** (Patient Full Name, text, required): Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
- **Date of Admission** (admission_date, date, required): Enter the date the patient was admitted to the recovery program.
- **Date of Discharge** (discharge_date, date, required): Enter the date the patient is being discharged from the recovery program.
- **Discharge Status** (discharge_status, select_one, required): Select the reason for discharge from the options provided: Completed Program Successfully, Transferred to Higher Level of Care, Discharged Against Medical Advice (AMA), Administrative Discharge, or Other (please specify).
- **Summary of Clinical Goals Achieved** (goals_achieved, text, required): Describe the major milestones reached during treatment.
- **Relapse Prevention Plan Status** (relapse_prevention_plan, select_one, required): Select the status of the patient's relapse prevention plan: Completed and Reviewed, In Progress, or Not Completed.
- **Recommended Aftercare Level** (level_of_care_recommended, select_one, required): Select the level of aftercare recommended for the patient: Intensive Outpatient (IOP), Standard Outpatient Counseling, Sober Living Environment, Support Groups Only (AA/NA), or Specialized Medical Follow-up.
- **Designated Aftercare Provider/Facility** (aftercare_provider_name, text, required): Enter the name of the designated aftercare provider or facility.
- **First Follow-up Appointment Date** (follow_up_appointment_date, date, optional): Enter the date of the patient's first follow-up appointment, if applicable.
- **Medications at Discharge** (discharge_medications, text, optional): List the medications prescribed to the patient at the time of discharge, including dosages.
- **Primary Support Contacts** (support_network, text, optional): List family or peers involved in the patient's aftercare.
- **I certify that the discharge plan has been reviewed with the patient** (practitioner_signature_ack, select_one, required): Select the option "I Certify" to confirm the discharge plan has been reviewed with the patient, or specify "Other" if an alternative arrangement was made.
- **Patient Acknowledgment of Discharge Instructions** (patient_ack_signature, select_one, required): Select the option "I Understand and Accept Instructions" to confirm the patient understands and accepts the discharge instructions, or specify "Other" if an alternative arrangement was made.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
- Review and update the form according to the patient's progress and needs.
- Verify the patient's understanding and acceptance of the discharge instructions.
