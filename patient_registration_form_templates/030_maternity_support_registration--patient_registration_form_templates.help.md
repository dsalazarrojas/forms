**Maternity Support Registration - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The **Maternity Support Registration** form is designed to collect information from pregnant women who need support during and after their pregnancy. This form will help us to provide tailored support and resources to ensure they receive the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form in a quiet, distraction-free environment.
2. Ensure that you fill out all the required fields (if applicable).
3. Be as accurate and thorough as possible when filling out the form.
4. If you need help with any of the questions or fields, don't hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### User Details
**User Details** (`user_input`, `text`, required: false)

* This field is where you can enter any identifying information about yourself, such as your name and contact information.
* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.

### Contact Details
**Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, required: false)

* This field is where you can enter your contact information, such as your phone number and address.
* If you have a preferred method of contact, please let us know in the "Contact Preference" field.

### Maternity Status
**Maternity Status** (`maternity_status`, `select_multiple`, required: false)

* Please select all the options that apply to your current pregnancy status, such as "I am pregnant" and "I have a medical condition".
* This will help us provide you with relevant support and resources.

### Support Needs
**Support Needs** (`support_needs`, `select_multiple`, required: false)

* Please select all the areas where you need support during your pregnancy, such as "Emotional Support" and "Practical Support".
* This will help us tailor our support to your specific needs.

### Follow Up Information
**Follow Up Information** (`follow_up`, `note`, required: false)

* This field is where you can provide any additional information about how you would like to be contacted or followed up on.
* Please be as detailed as possible so we can best support you.

### Contact Preference
**Contact Preference** (`contact_preference`, `time`, required: false)

* Please select your preferred contact method, such as "Email" or "Phone".
* This will ensure that we contact you in a way that works best for you.

### Email
**Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false)

* If you have an email address, please enter it here.
* This will allow us to contact you via email if you have selected that as your contact preference.

### Phone
**Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false)

* If you have a phone number, please enter it here.
* This will allow us to contact you via phone if you have selected that as your contact preference.

### Additional Notes
**Additional Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false)

* This field is where you can provide any additional information that you think will help us best support you.
* Be as detailed as possible so we can tailor our support to your specific needs.

Note: Please review the form carefully and ensure that you have filled out all the required fields before submitting it. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
