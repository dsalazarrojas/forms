# vascular_health_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vascular health assessment form is used to collect patient information for the purpose of assessing their overall vascular health. This includes gathering details about the patient's medical history, risk factors, medications, and test results.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please ensure that you have the necessary patient information and consent before proceeding.
- Complete each field as thoroughly and accurately as possible.
- Review the form for any required fields before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Details** (`Patient Details`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to collect basic patient information such as name, age, and contact details.
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to record the patient's medical history, including any previous diagnoses, surgeries, or significant health issues.
* **Medical Conditions** (`Medical Conditions`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all relevant medical conditions that the patient has been diagnosed with, such as Hypertension, Diabetes, Heart disease, and Stroke.
* **Risk Factors** (`Risk Factors`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the primary risk factor that contributes to the patient's vascular health, such as High blood pressure, Smoking, or Physical inactivity.
* **Medications** (`Medications`, `text`, required/optional): List all current medications the patient is taking.
* **Vascular Problems** (`Vascular Problems`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all relevant vascular problems the patient has been diagnosed with, such as Carotid artery stenosis, Cerebrovascular accident, or Peripheral artery disease.
* **Test Results** (`Test Results`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the outcome of any relevant tests or exams performed on the patient, such as Normal, Abnormal, or Referred.
* **Next Steps** (`Next Steps`, `text`, required/optional): Outline any recommended next steps or follow-up actions for the patient's care.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, required/optional): Add any relevant notes or comments about the patient's case.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a contact method for patient follow-up and future communication.

Note: The form is not intended for patients to fill out. Only medical professionals should use this form.
