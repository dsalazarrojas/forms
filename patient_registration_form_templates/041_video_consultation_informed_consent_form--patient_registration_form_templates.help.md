# video_consulation_informed_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The video_consulation_informed_consent_form is a form designed to collect information and obtain informed consent from patients during video consultations. It serves as a crucial document for both the patient and the doctor to have a clear understanding of the consultation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and enter the required information.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out, as indicated by the "Required" label in the field description.
3. Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Info (1)
* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, `text`, required: false): This field is for entering the patient's basic information. Please type the patient's name, date of birth, contact details, or any other relevant personal information.

### Patient Consent (2)
* **Patient Consent** (`patient_consent`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose "True" to indicate you have understood and agreed to the consultation process, risks, and procedures. If you have any questions or concerns, please discuss them with your doctor before selecting this option.

### Doctor Info (3)
* **Doctor Info** (`doctor_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter the doctor's name, specialty, or any other relevant details about the doctor performing the consultation.

### Treatment Info (4)
* **Treatment Info** (`treatment_info`, `text`, required: false): Provide any relevant details about the treatment or procedure being performed during the consultation.

### Consent Date (5)
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date on which you provided your informed consent for the consultation.

## Tips
* Ensure you have read and understood the consultation process, risks, and procedures before providing your consent.
* If you have any doubts or questions, discuss them with your doctor before or during the consultation.
* Keep a copy of this form for your records, as it serves as proof of your informed consent.
