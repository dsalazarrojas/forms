# Cardiology Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cardiology Discharge Form is designed to document the details of a patient's discharge from a cardiology department. It collects information about the patient's medical status, medications, lifestyle restrictions, and follow-up care instructions.

## How To Complete This Form
### Complete the form in the following steps:
1. Ensure you have all necessary information about the patient.
2. Enter the patient's full name in the "Patient Full Name" field.
3. Select the date of discharge from the "Discharge Date" field.
4. Enter the primary cardiac diagnosis in the "Primary Cardiac Diagnosis" field.
5. List the prescribed medications and dosages in the "Prescribed Medications and Dosages" field.
6. Provide important medication instructions in the "Important Medication Instructions" field.
7. Select the physical activity restrictions applicable to the patient in the "Physical Activity & Lifestyle" section.
8. Choose the heart-healthy diet instructions that apply to the patient in the "Heart-Healthy Diet Instructions" section.
9. Enter the date of the follow-up appointment in the "Follow-up Appointment Date" field.
10. Provide the emergency contact number for the physician or clinic in the "Physician or Clinic Emergency Contact" field.
11. Enter the physician's initials to confirm discharge.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Full Name** (`Patient Full Name`, text, required): Enter the patient's full name, including first and last name.
* **Discharge Date** (`Discharge Date`, date, required): Select the date the patient is leaving the facility.
* **Primary Cardiac Diagnosis** (`Primary Cardiac Diagnosis`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the patient's primary cardiac diagnosis.
* **Prescribed Medications and Dosages** (`Prescribed Medications and Dosages`, text, required): List all prescribed medications with their dosages and include frequency and reason for each.
* **Important Medication Instructions** (`Important Medication Instructions`, text, optional): Provide any important medication instructions, including side effects, warnings, and when to call the doctor.
* **Physical Activity & Lifestyle** (`Physical Activity & Lifestyle`, note, required): This section contains restrictions on physical activities the patient should avoid. Select all that apply:
	+ No Heavy Lifting (>10 lbs)
	+ No Driving
	+ Strenuous Exercise
	+ Limited Stairs
* **Heart-Healthy Diet Instructions** (`Heart-Healthy Diet Instructions`, select_one, required): Choose the diet instructions that apply to the patient:
	+ Standard Cardiac Diet
	+ Low Sodium (<2000mg)
	+ Fluid Restricted
	+ Diabetic Friendly
* **Follow-up Appointment** (`Follow-up Appointment`, note, required): Ensure the patient has a follow-up appointment scheduled.
* **Follow-up Appointment Date** (`Follow-up Appointment Date`, date, required): Enter the date of the follow-up appointment.
* **Warning Signs - Seek Immediate Help** (`Warning Signs - Seek Immediate Help`, note, optional): List any warning signs that require immediate attention from the patient.
* **Physician or Clinic Emergency Contact** (`Physician or Clinic Emergency Contact`, text, required): Enter the contact information for the physician or clinic.
* **Physician Initials to Confirm Discharge** (`Physician Initials to Confirm Discharge`, text, required): Enter the physician's initials to confirm discharge.
