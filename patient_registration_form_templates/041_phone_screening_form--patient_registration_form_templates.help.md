# phone_screening_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for phone screenings with job applicants during the hiring process. It collects relevant information about the applicant's job information, contact details, and other essential data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" to answer the screening questions.
2. Enter the date and time the screening was completed.
3. Provide a note about the screening, if necessary.
4. Enter the applicant's email address.
5. Enter the phone number used for the screening.
6. Enter the screening's address.
7. Add any additional comments about the screening.
8. Click the "Submit" button to save the data.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Information Form** (`job_info_form`): Enter the job title, job description, and other relevant details about the job.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`): Enter the applicant's contact details, such as name, address, and phone number.
* **Job Applicant Information** (`job_applicant_info`): Enter any additional information about the applicant.
* **Screening Questions** (`screening_questions`): Select "Yes" or "No" to answer the screening questions.
* **Date Screening Completed** (`date_screening_completed`): Enter the date the screening was completed.
* **Time Screening Completed** (`time_screening_completed`): Enter the time the screening was completed.
* **Note Screening Completed** (`note_screening_completed`): Add any notes about the screening.
* **Email Screening Completed** (`email_screening_completed`): Enter the applicant's email address.
* **Phone Screening Completed** (`phone_screening_completed`): Enter the phone number used for the screening.
* **Address Screening Completed** (`address_screening_completed`): Enter the screening's address.
* **Comments Screening Completed** (`comments_screening_completed`): Add any additional comments about the screening.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`): Click this button to save the data.
* **Cancel** (`cancel_button`): Click this button to discard any changes and return to the previous page.
* **Save** (`save_button`): Save the data without submitting it.
* **Print** (`print_button`): Print the completed form.
* **Back** (`back_button`): Return to the previous page.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer the screening questions accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check the entered data for completeness and correctness.
* Use the "Submit" button to save the data after completing the form.
