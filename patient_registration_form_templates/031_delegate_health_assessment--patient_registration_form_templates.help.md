# delegate_health_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The delegate_health_assessment form is a medical form used by delegates to collect and record patient health information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First name in the **First name** field.
2. Enter your Last name in the **Last name** field.
3. Enter your age in the **Age** field.
4. Enter your Email address in the **Email** field.
5. Enter your Phone number in the **Phone** field.
6. Enter your Date of birth in the **Date of birth** field.
7. Enter the Date of last exam in the **Date of last exam** field.
8. Enter your Height in the **Height** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name.
* **Age**: Enter your age in years.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address associated with your account.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date of birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Date of last exam**: Enter the date of your last examination or visit.
* **Height**: Enter your height in centimeters.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Double-check the information you enter before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with a medical professional or seek additional information.
