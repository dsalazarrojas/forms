# Developmental Counseling Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Developmental Counseling Form is a document used for recording information about a patient's developmental stage and treatment plans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. On each page, enter the required information for the patient in the corresponding section.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out to the best of your abilities.
3. If you're unsure about any information, please ask your healthcare professional for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, text, required/optional):
	+ This section is for entering the patient's basic information, such as their name and identification number (if applicable).
	+ Enter the patient's name and any other identifying details as per their medical record.
- **Assessment Results** (`assessment_results`, text, required/optional):
	+ This section is for recording the results of the patient's developmental assessment.
	+ Write down the key findings and any notable observations from the assessment.
- **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`, text, required/optional):
	+ This section is for creating a treatment plan for the patient.
	+ Describe the treatment goals, interventions, and any other relevant information.
- **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, text, required/optional):
	+ This section is for scheduling follow-up appointments or sessions with the patient.
	+ Enter the date, time, and any other details for the follow-up appointment.

## Tips
- Make sure to review and update the form regularly to ensure it accurately reflects the patient's progress.
- If you need to add or remove pages, you can do so by contacting the form creator.
- If you have any questions or need further assistance, please reach out to your healthcare professional.
