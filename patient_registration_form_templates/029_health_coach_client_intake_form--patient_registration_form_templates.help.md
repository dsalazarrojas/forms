# Health Coach Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Coach Client Intake Form is designed to gather essential information from clients about their health goals, medical history, and health coach preferences. This form helps health coaches understand their clients' needs and develop effective coaching plans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and contact information in the "Client Info" section.
2. Share your health and wellness goals in the "Goals" section. Be specific about what you want to achieve, and why.
3. Enter your relevant health information in the "Health Info" section. This includes any medical conditions, allergies, or concerns that may impact your coaching sessions.
4. Identify your preferred health coach in the "Health Coach" section. This is the coach who will be working with you to achieve your goals.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Info
* **Client Info** (`client_info`, text, required: false): Enter your full name, email address, and phone number to enable your health coach to contact you.

### Goals
* **Goals** (`goals`, text, required: true): Describe your health and wellness goals, including any specific challenges you're facing and how you'd like to overcome them.

### Health Info
* **Health Info** (`health_info`, text, required: false): Share any relevant medical history, allergies, or concerns that may impact your coaching sessions. This could include conditions like diabetes, allergies, or previous surgeries.

### Health Coach
* **Health Coach** (`health_coach`, text, required: true): Choose your preferred health coach from our team. This will help us match you with the best fit for your needs and goals.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough when filling out this form. The more information you provide, the better your health coach can understand your needs and develop effective coaching plans.
* If you're unsure about any questions, ask your coach for guidance or clarification.
* This form is confidential and will be used solely for your coaching sessions.
