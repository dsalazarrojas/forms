# yoni_therapys_vaginal_steam_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from patients undergoing vaginal steam treatment at Yoni Therapies. The form will help gather necessary details for treatment and follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name.
2. Provide your medical history.
3. Describe the details of your steam treatment.
4. List any symptoms you are experiencing.
5. Select the expected treatment outcome.
6. Add any additional notes if needed.
7. (Optional) Fill in the follow-up date if scheduled.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **medical_history** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Describe your medical history, including any previous conditions or allergies.
* **steam_treatment_details** (`steam_treatment_details`, text, optional): Provide details about the steam treatment you are receiving, including any specific procedures or frequencies.
* **symptoms** (`symptoms`, text, optional): List any symptoms you are experiencing, such as discomfort, pain, or other issues.
* **treatment_outcome** (`treatment_outcome`, select one, optional): Select one of the following options:
	+ Positive
	+ Negative
* **patient_notes** (`patient_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments for the treatment provider.
* **follow_up** (`follow_up`, date, optional): (Optional) Fill in the follow-up date if scheduled.

## Tips
* Please provide as much detail as possible in the medical history and symptoms fields.
* Make sure to select the correct treatment outcome.
* Use the follow-up date field only if a follow-up appointment is scheduled.
