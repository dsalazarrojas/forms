# dental_health_record - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and store patient information for dental health records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field. This is the name you are known by.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field. This is the surname of the patient.
3. Enter the **Street Address 1** in the "Street 1" field. This is the street address where the patient lives.
4. Enter the **Street Address 2** in the "Street 2" field. This is the apartment or suite number of the patient's address.
5. Enter the **City** in the "City" field. This is the city where the patient resides.
6. Enter the **State** in the "State" field. This is the state where the patient lives.
7. Enter the **Zip** in the "Zip" field. This is the postal code of the patient's address.
8. Enter the **Phone** in the "Phone" field. This is the contact number of the patient.
9. Enter the **Email** in the "Email" field. This is the contact email of the patient.
10. Enter any **Medical History** in the "Medical History" field. This is any medical condition or allergy information that the patient has.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (text)**: Enter the first name of the patient.
* **Last Name (text)**: Enter the last name of the patient.
* **Street 1 (text)**: Enter the street address of the patient's residence.
* **Street 2 (text)**: Enter the apartment or suite number of the patient's address.
* **City (text)**: Enter the city where the patient resides.
* **State (text)**: Enter the state where the patient lives.
* **Zip (text)**: Enter the patient's postal code.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter the patient's contact phone number.
* **Email (text)**: Enter the patient's contact email.
* **Medical History (text)**: Enter any medical condition or allergy information that the patient has.
