# Healthcare Access Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form, "Healthcare Access Declaration Form," is designed to collect and document necessary information for patient registration, including access needs, insurance details, and contact information. It's an essential tool for healthcare providers to ensure accurate patient data and provide personalized care.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Registration Data**: In the "Registration Data" section, please provide your basic patient information, including your name and any other identifying details required for your care.
2.  **Access Needs**: In the "Access Needs" section, check the boxes that apply to your healthcare needs. If you have any other medical conditions or concerns not listed, please specify in the space provided.
3.  **Insurance Details**: If you have insurance, please provide your insurance provider's information in the "Insurance Details" section.
4.  **Contact Information**: In the "Contact Information" section, please supply the name, phone number, and email address (if available) of your emergency contact.
5.  **Medical Necessity and Relationship**: In the "Medical Necessity and Relationship" section, confirm your relationship with the contact person provided and the date this relationship began.
6.  **Signature and Dates**: In the "Signature and Dates" section, please sign and date the form to authenticate your consent and acknowledge the accuracy of the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Registration Data

*   **Registration Data** (id: 1): This is the primary patient registration information.
*   **Contact Name** (id: 14): This is the name of the person responsible for your care.
*   **Contact Phone** (id: 15): Please enter the contact's phone number for emergencies or updates.
*   **Contact E-mail** (id: 16): If you have an email address, please enter it for easy communication.
*   **Contact Relationship** (id: 17): Describe your relationship with the contact person (e.g., family, friend, or other).
*   **Contact Relationship Start Date** (id: 18): Date this relationship began.
*   **Contact Relationship End Date** (id: 19): If applicable, provide the date this relationship ended or is expected to end.

### Access Needs

*   **Access Needs** (id: 2): Check the boxes for any medical conditions or access needs you require. You can also specify any additional details in the provided space.

### Insurance Details

*   **Insurance Provider** (id: 4): Name of your insurance provider.
*   **Insurance Number** (id: 5): Your insurance provider's ID number.
*   **Policy Number** (id: 6): Policy number issued by your insurance provider.
*   **Policy Start Date** (id: 7): Date your policy began.
*   **Policy End Date** (id: 8): Date your policy ends or has ended.
*   **Insurance Address** (id: 9): Address of your insurance provider.
*   **Policy Effective Date** (id: 10): Date your policy became effective.
*   **Medical Necessity Date** (id: 11): Date the medical necessity began or was confirmed.
*   **Medical Condition** (id: 12): List any medical conditions you have that are relevant.
*   **Medical Provider** (id: 13): Name of your medical provider.

### Signature and Dates

*   **Patient Signature** (id: 20): Sign here to authenticate that the information provided is accurate.
*   **Provider Signature** (id: 21): Sign here to verify your healthcare provider's agreement with the information.
*   **Provider Date** (id: 22): Date of provider's signature.
*   **Patient Signature Date** (id: 23): Date the patient signed.
*   **Contact Signature Date** (id: 24): Date the contact person signed (if applicable).
