# Geriatric Care Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Geriatric Care Discharge Form is designed for healthcare providers to document the necessary information for the discharge of a patient from a care facility. This form helps to ensure a smooth and efficient discharge process by collecting relevant details about the patient's personal and medical information, contact details, and other critical discharge information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field. This is a required field that should be filled out with the patient's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
2.  Enter the date of discharge in the "Date Of Discharge" field. This field is optional, but it is recommended to be filled out for accuracy and clarity.
3.  Enter the name of the discharge facility in the "Discharge Facility" field. This field is optional, but it helps to identify the location and type of facility where the patient was discharged from.
4.  Enter the discharge date and time in the "Discharge Date" and "Discharge Time" fields, respectively. These fields are optional but should be filled out if available.
5.  Select the patient's status from the provided options in the "Patient Status" field. This field is optional but helps to understand the patient's current condition.
6.  Provide a brief description of the patient's medical history in the "Medical History" field. This is a required field that should be filled out with relevant details about the patient's medical background.
7.  Enter the patient's social security number in the "Social Security Number" field. This field is optional, but it is recommended for accurate billing and insurance purposes.
8.  Enter the patient's address in the "Address" field. This field is required and should be filled out with the patient's current address.
9.  Enter the city in the "City" field. This field is required and should be filled out with the city where the patient resides.
10. Enter the state in the "State" field. This field is required and should be filled out with the state where the patient resides.
11. Enter the zip code in the "Zip" field. This field is required and should be filled out with the patient's zip code.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name (Patient Name)**: Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Date Of Discharge (Date Of Discharge)**: Enter the date the patient was discharged from the care facility.
* **Discharge Facility (Discharge Facility)**: Enter the name of the facility where the patient was discharged from.
* **Discharge Date (Discharge Date)**: Enter the date the patient was discharged.
* **Discharge Time (Discharge Time)**: Enter the time the patient was discharged.
* **Patient Status (Patient Status)**: Select the patient's current status from the provided options.
* **Medical History (Medical History)**: Provide a brief description of the patient's medical history.
* **Social Security Number (Social Security Number)**: Enter the patient's social security number for accurate billing and insurance purposes.
* **Address (Address)**: Enter the patient's current address.
* **City (City)**: Enter the city where the patient resides.
* **State (State)**: Enter the state where the patient resides.
* **Zip (Zip)**: Enter the patient's zip code.
* **Patient Contact Information (Patient Contact Information)**: Enter any additional contact information for the patient that may be helpful for follow-up or communication.

Note: All fields marked as required should be filled out for the form to be considered complete.
