# Psychiatrist Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Psychiatrist Information Form is used to collect relevant information from patients, including their medical history, medications, and mental status. This form helps psychiatrists and other healthcare professionals assess patients' mental health conditions and provide proper care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's ID number in the "Patient Id" field.
2. Provide a brief description of the patient's medical history in the "Medical History" field.
3. Enter any relevant medical history 1 in the "Medical History 1" field.
4. Enter any relevant family history in the "Family History" field.
5. List any current medications in the "Medications" field.
6. Provide a description of any relevant medical history 2 in the "Medical History 2" field.
7. Enter any relevant medical history 3 in the "Medical History 3" field.
8. Select "Stable" or "Unstable" from the options for "Mental Status" and "Mental Status 1" and "Mental Status 2" fields.
9. Select "Stable" or "Unstable" from the options for "Mental Status 3" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Id** (`patient_id`, required: false): This field is used to enter the patient's ID number.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the patient's medical history.
* **Medical History 1** (`medical_history_1`, required: false): Enter any relevant medical history 1.
* **Family History** (`family_history`, required: false): Provide any relevant family history.
* **Medications** (`medications`, required: false): List any current medications.
* **Medical History 2** (`medical_history_2`, required: false): Provide any relevant medical history 2.
* **Medical History 3** (`medical_history_3`, required: false): Enter any relevant medical history 3.
* **Mental Status** (`mental_status`, required: false): Select "Stable" or "Unstable" to indicate the patient's mental status.
* **Mental Status 1** (`mental_status_1`, required: false): Select "Stable" or "Unstable" to indicate the patient's mental status.
* **Mental Status 2** (`mental_status_2`, required: false): Select "Stable" or "Unstable" to indicate the patient's mental status.
* **Mental Status 3** (`mental_status_3`, required: false): Select "Stable" or "Unstable" to indicate the patient's mental status.
