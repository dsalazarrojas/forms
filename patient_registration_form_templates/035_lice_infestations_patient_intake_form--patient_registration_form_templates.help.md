# Lice Infestations Patient Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from patients regarding their lice infestations. The form is designed to gather data on the patient's contact information, medical history, lice symptoms, and treatment history. This information will be used to track and manage patient treatment and progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your patient's name, phone number, and email address in the "patient_name", "contact_phone", and "patient_email" fields respectively.
2. Describe your medical history, including any previous illnesses or allergies, in the "medical_history" field.
3. List any current medications you are taking in the "current_meds" field (if applicable).
4. Report any symptoms of lice infestations you are experiencing in the "lice_symptoms" field.
5. Indicate the length of time you have been experiencing lice infestations in the "lice_infestations_length" field.
6. Enter the dates for the start, appointment, and end of treatment, in the "treatment_start_date", "appointment_date", and "treatment_end_date" fields respectively.
7. Describe the outcome of your lice treatment in the "lice_infestation_outcome" field.
8. Provide any additional comments or notes about your lice treatment in the "lice_infestation_outcome_comments" field.
9. Select the type of lice treatment you are undergoing from the "lice_type" field.
10. Report the number of lice infestations, locations, and families infested, in the "num_lice", "num_locations", and "num_families_infested" fields respectively.
11. List any lice control products you have used in the "lice_control_products_used" field.
12. Report the date you last checked in on the status of your lice treatment in the "lice_control_outcome_date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient_name**: Enter your name as it appears on your medical records.
* **contact_phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **patient_email**: Enter your email address for communication.
* **medical_history**: Describe any previous illnesses or allergies you may have.
* **current_meds**: List any medications you are currently taking (if applicable).
* **lice_symptoms**: Describe any symptoms of lice infestations you are experiencing.
* **lice_infestations_length**: Report the length of time you have been experiencing lice infestations.
* **treatment_start_date**: Enter the start date of your treatment.
* **appointment_date**: Enter the date of your appointment with the healthcare provider.
* **treatment_end_date**: Enter the end date of your treatment.
* **follow_up_date**: (Optional) Enter the date of your follow-up appointment with the healthcare provider.
* **follow_up_time**: (Optional) Enter the time of your follow-up appointment with the healthcare provider.
* **lice_infestation_description**: Describe the type of lice infestations you are experiencing.
* **patient_notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your treatment (if desired).
* **lice_type**: Select the type of lice treatment you are undergoing (head, body, or mixed).
* **num_lice**: Report the number of lice infestations you have.
* **num_locations**: Report the number of locations infested with lice.
* **num_families_infested**: (Optional) Report the number of families infested with lice.
* **num_lice_treatments**: (Optional) Report the number of lice treatments you have received.
* **lice_control_products_used**: Select any lice control products you have used.
* **lice_infestation_outcome**: Report the outcome of your lice treatment (resolved, not resolved, or pending).
* **lice_infestation_outcome_notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the outcome of your lice treatment (if desired).
* **lice_infestation_outcome_date**: (Optional) Report the date of the lice treatment outcome.
* **lice_control_outcome_date**: (Optional) Report the date of your last lice control check.
* **lice_infestation_outcome_comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about the outcome of your lice treatment (if desired).
