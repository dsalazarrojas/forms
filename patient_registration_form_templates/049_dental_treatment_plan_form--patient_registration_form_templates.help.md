# Dental Treatment Plan Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dental Treatment Plan Form is designed to collect information from patients to create a comprehensive treatment plan for their dental care. This form is used by dental professionals to understand the patient's needs and develop an effective treatment plan.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Personal Details**: Enter your name, address, phone number, and email address in this section.
2. **Patient Insurance**: Select your insurance type from the dropdown list. This information is required.
3. **Appointment Details**: Enter the date, time, and duration of your scheduled appointment.
4. **Dental Treatment Plan**: Enter the specific details of your dental treatment, including any necessary medical history and medications.
5. **Medical History**: Enter any relevant medical history or allergies that may affect your dental treatment.
6. **List of Medications**: List any medications you are currently taking.
7. **Emergency Contact**: Provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
8. **Contact Preferences**: Enter your preferred method of contact and any additional information you would like us to have.

* **Personal Details** (Personal Details, Text, Required/Optional): Enter your personal details, including your name, address, phone number, and email address.
