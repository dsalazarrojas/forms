# Telehealth Intake And Signature Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This Telehealth Intake And Signature Form is designed to collect essential patient information for virtual consultations. It's a crucial tool for healthcare providers to gather details about the patient's contact information, medical history, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Please fill out the form accurately and completely**. Incomplete or inaccurate information may lead to delays or errors in your care.
2. **Ensure you answer all required fields**. The form will guide you through the required fields.
3. **Double-check your contact method**. Select the method you would like to use for communication (phone, email, or video).
4. **Verify your date of birth**. Enter the correct date of birth as it appears on your identification documents.
5. **Sign and date the form**. Your signature will serve as acknowledgement of the information you've provided.
6. **Submit the form**. Once complete, click the submit button to send the form to your healthcare provider.

## Tips

1. **Be accurate and thorough**. Complete each field to ensure your healthcare provider has the necessary information.
2. **Double-check your contact information**. Ensure your provider has the correct contact details.
