# Nursing Home Accommodation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect vital information for the care and accommodation of patients at a nursing home. Please fill it out accurately and completely to ensure the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form in sections, starting from the top of the page.
2. For each section, answer the questions accurately and truthfully.
3. Use the provided options for selection-based fields.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`1`, `text`, required: `false`):
	+ Enter your general information, including your name, address, etc.
* **Contact Information** (`2`, `text`, required: `false`):
	+ Provide your contact information, such as phone number and email.
* **Medical Conditions** (`3`, `text`, required: `false`):
	+ List your medical conditions and any relevant health information.
* **Medications** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`):
	+ Select all the medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical Professionals** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`):
	+ List the names of your medical professionals, such as doctors and specialists.
* **Family Members** (`6`, `text`, required: `false`):
	+ Enter the names of family members, including their relationship to you.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`7`, `select_one`, required: `false`):
	+ Select whether you have an emergency contact person.
* **Payment Methods** (`8`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`):
	+ Select all payment methods used for your care.
* **Medical Records** (`9`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`):
	+ Select whether you have medical records on file.
* **Care Levels** (`10`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`):
	+ Select your care level, such as high, low, or other.
* **Care Period** (`11`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`):
	+ Select whether you need long-term or short-term care.
* **Move In Date** (`12`, `date`, required: `false`):
	+ Enter the date you plan to move in.
* **Move Out Date** (`13`, `date`, required: `false`):
	+ Enter the date you plan to move out.
* **Available Dates** (`14`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`):
	+ Select the dates you are available for care.
* **Availability Notice Period** (`15`, `date`, required: `false`):
	+ Enter the notice period for available dates.
* **Payment Status** (`16`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`):
	+ Select your payment status, such as active or inactive.

## Tips

* Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
* Ensure you have all necessary approvals and information before submitting the form.
* Contact the nursing home administration if you have any questions or concerns.
