# patient_identity_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The patient_identity_verification_form is used to verify the identity of a patient, collecting essential information for medical record creation and updating.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all the necessary patient information and permissions before proceeding.
2. Fill in the required fields as accurately and completely as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient_name**: Enter the patient's full name as recorded in their identification documents.
* **date_of_birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **social_security_number**: Enter the patient's social security number (if applicable).
* **email**: Enter the patient's email address (if applicable).
* **phone_number**: Enter the patient's phone number (if applicable).
* **medical_record_id**: Enter the patient's medical record ID (if applicable).

## Tips
1. Double-check the accuracy of the information before submitting the form.
2. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult the patient's identification documents or contact the relevant medical staff.
