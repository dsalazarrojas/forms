# Client Health Consultation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for patients to provide information about themselves for the purpose of a health consultation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form accurately and honestly.
2. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
3. Enter your Email Address, Phone Number, and Street Address in the respective fields.
4. Select the correct ZIP Code and State for your current address.
5. Enter your Date of Birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
6. Choose your Gender from the available options.
7. Enter your Height and Weight as prompted.
8. Select your Blood Type if you are aware of it.
9. Enter the reason for your consultation in the Reason for Consultation field.
10. Describe your primary symptoms in the Primary Symptoms field.
11. Select the duration of your symptoms from the available options.
12. Select the severity of your symptoms on a scale of 1-10.
13. List any current medications and dosages in the Current Medications field.
14. If you have any medication allergies, list them in the Medication Allergies field.
15. If you have any food allergies, list them in the Food Allergies field.
16. If you have any other allergies, list them in the Other Allergies field.
17. Enter your medical history, including past illnesses, surgeries, and conditions.
18. If you have a family medical history, describe it in the Family Medical History field.
19. If you have any lifestyle factors that may impact your health, such as diet or exercise habits, describe them in the Lifestyle Factors field.
20. Select your smoking status and alcohol consumption habits.
21. If you have previously tried any treatments for this condition, describe them in the Previous Treatments field.
22. If you have an insurance provider and policy number, please enter them in the respective fields.
23. Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
24. Select whether you consent to medical consultation and treatment.
25. Confirm that all information is accurate.
26. Finally, select the survey date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your legal first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your legal last name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your primary contact email.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your best contact number.
* **Street Address**: Enter your complete mailing address.
* **ZIP Code**: Select the correct ZIP code for your address.
* **City**: Enter the city where you reside.
* **State**: Select the correct state for your address.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Gender**: Choose your biological sex (Male, Female, Non-Binary, or Prefer Not to Say).
* **Height**: Enter your height in feet and inches or cm.
* **Weight**: Enter your weight in pounds or kg.
* **Blood Type**: If you are aware of your blood type, select it from the available options.
* **Reason for Consultation**: Describe why you are seeking care.
* **Primary Symptoms**: Describe your primary symptoms.
* **Symptom Duration**: Select how long you have had these symptoms.
* **Symptom Severity**: Select the severity of your symptoms on a scale of 1-10.
* **Current Medications**: List all your current medications and dosages.
* **Medication Allergies**: If you have any medication allergies, list them.
* **Food Allergies**: If you have any food allergies, list them.
* **Other Allergies**: If you have any other allergies, list them.
* **Medical History**: Describe your past illnesses, surgeries, and conditions.
* **Family Medical History**: Describe any relevant family medical history.
* **Lifestyle Factors**: Describe any lifestyle factors that may impact your health.
* **Smoking Status**: Select your smoking status.
* **Alcohol Consumption**: Select how often you consume alcohol.
* **Previous Treatments**: If you have previously tried any treatments, describe them.
* **Insurance Provider**: If you have an insurance provider, enter their name.
* **Insurance Policy Number**: If you have an insurance policy number, enter it.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Consent to Treatment**: Select whether you consent to medical consultation and treatment.
* **Truthfulness Confirmation**: Confirm that all information is accurate.
* **Survey Date**: Select the survey date.

## Tips

* Please complete this form carefully and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please describe your uncertainty.
* If you have any additional information that is not included in the form, please describe it in the provided fields.
* If you need help with any field, please ask your healthcare provider for assistance.
