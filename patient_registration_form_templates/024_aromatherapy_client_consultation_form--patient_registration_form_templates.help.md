# Aromatherapy Client Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather essential information from clients to create a tailored aromatherapy treatment plan.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with the following information:
	* **Full Name**: Enter your full name.
	* **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
	* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
	* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth (optional).
	* **Consultation Date**: Choose a date for the consultation.
2. Select the following options:
	* **Primary Wellness Goal**: Choose your primary wellness goal.
	* **Secondary Goals** (optional): Choose any secondary goals you have.
	* **Current Stress Level**: Choose your current stress level.
	* **Sleep Quality**: Choose your sleep quality.
	* **Exercise Frequency** (optional): Choose your exercise frequency.
3. List any:
	* **Current Health Conditions**: List any current health conditions you have.
	* **Current Medications**: List any current medications you are taking.
	* **Allergies or Sensitivities**: List any known allergies or sensitivities (including essential oil allergies).
	* **Scent Sensitivities or Preferences**: List any scent sensitivities or preferences.
4. Choose the following:
	* **Previous Aromatherapy Experience**: Select your previous aromatherapy experience.
	* **Essential Oils Used**: List any essential oils you have used before.
	* **Favorite Scents or Aromas**: List your favorite scents or aromas.
5. Choose your preferred:
	* **Treatment Application Method**: Choose your preferred treatment application method.
	* **Desired Treatment Frequency**: Choose your desired treatment frequency.
6. **Additional Comments or Questions**: Enter any additional comments or questions you may have.
