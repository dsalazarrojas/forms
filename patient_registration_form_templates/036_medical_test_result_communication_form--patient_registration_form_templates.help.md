## Medical Test Result Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Test Result Communication Form is a template used to share test results with patients. This form captures relevant patient and test information, allowing for clear communication of test results and any necessary follow-up instructions or comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "patient_name" field.
2. Select the date the test was performed in the "test_date" field.
3. Enter the test result in the "test_result" field.
4. Add any comments or additional information from the patient in the "patient_comments" field.
5. Enter any comments or instructions from the doctor in the "doctor_comments" field.
6. Select the test status (Active, Inactive, or Cancelled) in the "test_status" field.
7. Provide any follow-up instructions for the patient in the "follow_up_instructions" field.
8. If applicable, enter the patient's ID number in the "patient_id" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_name** (Text, required: false): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical record.
* **test_date** (Date, required: false): Enter the date the test was performed. This field can be left blank if the test date is not known.
* **test_result** (Text, required: false): Enter the test result, such as "Positive" or "Negative".
* **patient_comments** (Text, required: false): Add any additional comments or information from the patient.
* **doctor_comments** (Text, required: false): Add any comments or instructions from the doctor.
* **test_status** (Select Multiple, required: false): Select one or multiple test statuses (Active, Inactive, or Cancelled) to indicate the test's status.
* **follow_up_instructions** (Text, required: false): Provide any follow-up instructions for the patient.
* **patient_id** (Number, required: false): If applicable, enter the patient's ID number.
