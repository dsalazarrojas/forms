# health_services_availability_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the health services provided by a medical facility or provider. It is used to schedule and communicate availability of health services, making it easier for patients or clients to plan their appointments.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the **Services Category** to indicate the type of service you are looking for (e.g., General, Specialist).
2. Enter the **Services Name** of the service you are interested in.
3. Choose the days of the week you are available to receive the service by selecting the relevant options from the **Schedule** field.
4. If necessary, select a **Start Time** and **End Time** to schedule a specific time slot.
5. Optionally, provide a brief **Service Description** to help the provider understand your needs.
6. Add any additional **Notes** you have about your appointment or service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Services Category**: Choose the type of service you are looking for (e.g., General, Specialist).
* **Services Name**: Enter the name of the service you are interested in.
* **Schedule**: Select the days of the week you are available to receive the service.
* **Availability**: (Optional) Enter the date you are available to receive the service. This can help the provider schedule your appointment accordingly.
* **Start Time**: (Optional) Enter the start time of your appointment.
* **End Time**: (Optional) Enter the end time of your appointment.
* **Service Description**: (Optional) Provide a brief description of the service you are interested in.
* **Notes**: (Optional) Add any additional notes or comments about your appointment or service.
