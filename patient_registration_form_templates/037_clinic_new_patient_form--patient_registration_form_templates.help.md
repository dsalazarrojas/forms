# Clinic New Patient Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Clinic New Patient Form is a comprehensive form designed to capture essential information about new patients, ensuring a smooth registration process and accurate documentation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the Personal Information section.
2. Provide your full name, date of birth, and contact information.
3. Move on to the Address Information section.
4. Fill out your street address, city, state, and country details.
5. In the Emergency Contact section, provide the name, relationship, and phone number of your emergency contact.
6. If applicable, fill out the Medical History section.
7. Finally, enter your insurance provider information.
8. Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): This is your complete name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): The date you were born.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Your primary phone number.
* **Street Address** (`street_address`, `text`, required): Your house number and street name.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): The city where you reside.
* **State/Province** (`state`, `text`, required): The state or province you are from.
* **ZIP/Postal Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): The postal code of your residence.
* **Country** (`country`, `text`, required): The country where you reside.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): The name of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship to Patient** (`emergency_relationship`, `text`, required): Your relationship to the patient.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_phone`, `text`, required): The phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Insurance Provider Name** (`insurance_provider`, `text`, required): The name of your insurance provider.
* **Insurance ID Number** (`insurance_id`, `text`, required): Your insurance ID number.
* **Group Number** (`group_number`, `text`, optional): Optional insurance group number.

## Tips
* Ensure you fill out all required fields for accurate documentation.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
* If you need help or have questions, contact your clinic administrator.
* This form is designed to ensure a smooth and efficient registration process.
* Please be patient and complete the form carefully.
