### Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and update patient's information for the renewal of their health plan. It helps the healthcare provider to review and update the patient's information accurately.

### How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
2. Ensure you have all necessary information and documentation before submitting the form.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Demographics** (`patient_demographics`): This field collects the patient's basic information such as name, date of birth, and contact details.
	+ Please enter your name as it appears on your medical records.
	+ Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
	+ Enter your time of birth in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
	+ Enter your email address.
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Health Plan Information** (`health_plan_info`): This field collects information about your current health plan.
	+ Please provide the name of your current health plan provider.
* **Patient Preferences** (`patient_preferences`): This field collects information about your medical preferences.
	+ Please describe any medical preferences you may have.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`): This field collects information about your contact details.
	+ Please enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`): This field collects information about your emergency contact.
	+ Please enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
* **Review and Agreement** (`review_and_agreement`): This field asks you to review and agree to the terms and conditions of the health plan.
	+ Please review the terms and conditions of the health plan carefully and agree to them.
* **Healthcare Provider** (`healthcare_provider`): This field asks you to select your healthcare provider.
	+ Please select your healthcare provider from the options provided.
* **Medical Record Release** (`medical_record_release`): This field asks you to confirm if you want to release your medical records.
	+ Please select whether you want to release your medical records or not.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): This field collects your date of birth.
	+ Please enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`): This field collects your time of birth.
	+ Please enter your time of birth in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Email** (`email`): This field collects your email address.
	+ Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): This field collects your phone number.
	+ Please enter your phone number.
* **Medical Insurance Provider** (`medical_insurance_provider`): This field asks you to select your medical insurance provider.
	+ Please select your medical insurance provider from the options provided.
* **Medical History Information** (`medical_history_info`): This field collects information about your medical history.
	+ Please describe any medical history you have.
* **Allergy Information** (`allergy_info`): This field collects information about any allergies you may have.
	+ Please describe any allergies you may have.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`): This field collects information about any medical conditions you may have.
	+ Please describe any medical conditions you may have.
* **Medical Employment** (`medical_employment`): This field collects information about your medical employment.
	+ Please describe your medical employment.
* **Medical Employer** (`medical_employer`): This field collects information about your medical employer.
	+ Please enter your medical employer's name.
* **Medical Specialty** (`medical_specialty`): This field collects information about your medical specialty.
	+ Please describe your medical specialty.
* **Medical License** (`medical_license`): This field collects information about your medical license.
	+ Please enter your medical license number.
* **Medical Degree** (`medical_degree`): This field collects information about your medical degree.
	+ Please enter your medical degree information.
* **Medical License Number** (`medical_license_number`): This field collects information about your medical license number.
	+ Please enter your medical license number.
* **Submit** (`submit`): This field submits the form.

### Tips

* Please ensure you have all necessary information and documentation before submitting this form.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you are not sure about any field, please consult with your healthcare provider.
