# dental_health_history_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect important information about a patient's dental health history, including any medical conditions, allergies, and medical procedures they may have undergone. This information helps the dentist understand the patient's overall health and develop a treatment plan that takes into account their medical needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out all required fields (if any) and provide accurate and complete information about your medical history.
2. Review each field carefully and ensure you understand what is being asked.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your first name here. This is a required field.
### Medical History
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `note`, required/optional): Please provide any relevant medical history or information that may be helpful for the dentist to know. This can include any previous illnesses, surgeries, or medical conditions.
### Allergies
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Please select all allergies you have, including any medications or substances that cause an allergic reaction. You can select 'Yes' for each relevant allergy.
### Medical Conditions
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `select_one`, required/optional): Please select any medical conditions you may have, such as diabetes or asthma. You can select 'Yes' for each relevant condition.
### Dental Procedure
* **Dental Procedure** (`dental_procedure`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter any previous dental procedures you have undergone, such as dental implants or root canals.
### Medication
* **Medication** (`medication`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter any medications you are currently taking or have taken in the past. This can include prescription medications, over-the-counter medications, or supplements.
### Medical History Date
* **Medical History Date** (`medical_history_date`, `date`, required/optional): Please enter the date you first experienced your medical condition or procedure.
### Medical History Time
* **Medical History Time** (`medical_history_time`, `time`, required/optional): Please enter the time you first experienced your medical condition or procedure.
### 
Note: Please provide accurate and complete information to ensure the best possible care. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with the dentist before submitting the form.
