# patient-health-report-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The patient-health-report-form is designed to collect comprehensive information from patients for medical professionals to use in diagnosis, treatment, and care planning.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. Review and correct any errors before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Basic Info (1)**: Enter your name, address, and contact information.
* **Medical History (2)**: Report any medical conditions, allergies, or medications you are taking.
* **Medication (3)**: List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Symptoms (4)**: Describe any symptoms you are experiencing.
* **Vital Signs (5)**: Record your vital signs, such as temperature, blood pressure, and pulse.
* **Medical Conditions (6)**: List any medical conditions you have been diagnosed with.
* **Test Results (7)**: Collect test results, such as lab tests, imaging studies, and other diagnostic tests.
* **Medical Team Assessment (8)**: Describe your assessment of your medical team's care.
* **Allergies (9)**: List any allergies you have, including medications, foods, and latex.
* **Medical Observations (10)**: Report any notable medical observations, such as wounds or scars.
* **Medication Plan (11)**: Describe your medication plan, including dosages and frequencies.
* **Patient Feedback (12)**: Share any feedback or concerns you have about your care.
* **Patient Contact Info (13)**: Provide your contact information, including phone number and address.
* **Medical Records (14)**: Collect medical records, including lab results and imaging studies.
* **Test Results 2 (15)**: Collect additional test results.
* **Patient Education (16)**: Receive education on your condition, treatment, and care.
* **Medical Notes (17)**: Record medical notes, including diagnosis and treatment plan.
* **Medical Reports (18)**: Collect medical reports, including lab results and imaging studies.
* **Medical Certifications (19)**: List any medical certifications you have, such as licensure and specialty.
* **Patient Consent (20)**: Give consent for treatment, procedures, and care.
* **Medical Photos (21)**: Provide medical photos, including photos of wounds or scars.
* **Test Results 3 (22)**: Collect additional test results.
* **Medication History (23)**: Record your medication history, including dosages and frequencies.
* **Patient Address (24)**: Provide your address.
* **Patient Contact Info 2 (25)**: Provide additional contact information, such as emergency contact details.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when completing the form.
* Review and correct any errors before submission.
* Ask for help if you are unsure about any question or field.
* Consult with your healthcare professional if you have any concerns or questions about your care.
