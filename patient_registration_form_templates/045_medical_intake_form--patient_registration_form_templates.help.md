# medical_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The medical_intake_form is a patient registration form designed to gather relevant medical information for healthcare professionals to evaluate and diagnose patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page, providing as much detail as possible.
2. For the `past_surgeries` field, select all relevant surgeries that have been performed.
3. If you have a medical history or symptoms that are not listed, you may add them in the `medical_history` and `symptoms` fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Medical History
* **Medical History (Page 1)** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Provide a brief overview of your medical history, including any previous illnesses, allergies, or other health conditions.

### Past Surgeries
* **Past Surgeries (Page 1)** (`past_surgeries`, select multiple, optional): Select all past surgeries that have been performed, such as knee replacement, heart surgery, or other relevant procedures.

### Medical Genetics
* **Medical Genetics (Page 1)** (`medical_genetics`, text, optional): If you have any known genetic disorders or conditions, please list them here.

### Symptoms
* **Symptoms (Page 1)** (`symptoms`, text, optional): Describe any symptoms you are currently experiencing, such as pain, nausea, or difficulty breathing.

## Tips
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, consult with a medical professional for guidance.
* Please fill out the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible, as this information will be used to inform your evaluation and diagnosis.
* If you have any additional information that is not listed, please provide it in the `medical_history` or `symptoms` fields.
