# Adult ADHD Investigator Symptom Rating Scale (AISRS) - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adult ADHD Investigator Symptom Rating Scale (AISRS) is a comprehensive rating scale designed to assess the presence and severity of symptoms associated with Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD) in adult patients.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure you have the necessary information about the patient, including their full name and medical record number.
2. Enter the patient's full name in the "Patient Name" field.
3. If applicable, enter the patient's medical record number in the "Patient ID" field.
4. Record the date this rating scale is being completed in the "Assessment Date" field.
5. Enter your name and professional title in the "Rater Name" and "Rater Professional Title" fields.
6. For each symptom rating scale, select the corresponding level of severity from 0 (Not Present) to 3 (Severe) based on your clinical assessment of the patient's symptoms.
7. Enter any additional observations or notes in the "Clinician Additional Notes" field, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, Text, **Required**): Enter the full name of the patient.
* **Patient ID** (`patient_id`, Text, **Optional**): Enter the patient's medical record number, if available.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, Date, **Required**): Record the date this rating scale is being completed.
* **Rater Name** (`rater_name`, Text, **Required**): Enter your name as the professional completing the form.
* **Rater Professional Title** (`rater_title`, Text, **Required**): Enter your clinical title or position.
* **Difficulty maintaining attention in tasks** (`symptom_1_attention`, Select One, **Required**): Rate from 0 (Not Present) to 3 (Severe) based on the patient's ability to maintain attention in tasks.
* **Carelessness with details** (`symptom_2_carelessness`, Select One, **Required**): Rate from 0 (Not Present) to 3 (Severe) based on the patient's carelessness with details.
* **Difficulty with listening** (`symptom_3_listening`, Select One, **Required**): Rate from 0 (Not Present) to 3 (Severe) based on the patient's difficulty with listening.
* **Difficulty following instructions** (`symptom_4_instructions`, Select One, **Required**): Rate from 0 (Not Present) to 3 (Severe) based on the patient's ability to follow instructions.
* **Poor organizational skills** (`symptom_5_organization`, Select One, **Required**): Rate from 0 (Not Present) to 3 (Severe) based on the patient's poor organizational skills.
* **Avoidance of tasks requiring sustained effort** (`symptom_6_avoidance`, Select One, **Required**): Rate from 0 (Not Present) to 3 (Severe) based on the patient's avoidance of tasks requiring sustained effort.
* **Frequently misplacing things** (`symptom_7_misplacing`, Select One, **Required**): Rate from 0 (Not Present) to 3 (Severe) based on the patient's frequent misplacing of things.
* **Easily distracted by external stimuli** (`symptom_8_distraction`, Select One, **Required**): Rate from 0 (Not Present) to 3 (Severe) based on the patient's ease of distraction by external stimuli.
* **Forgetfulness in daily activities** (`symptom_9_forgetfulness`, Select One, **Required**): Rate from 0 (Not Present) to 3 (Severe) based on the patient's forgetfulness in daily activities.
* **Fidgetiness or restlessness** (`symptom_10_hyperactivity`, Select One, **Required**): Rate from 0 (Not Present) to 3 (Severe) based on the patient's fidgetiness or restlessness.
* **Difficulty remaining seated** (`symptom_11_sitting`, Select One, **Required**): Rate from 0 (Not Present) to 3 (Severe) based on the patient's difficulty remaining seated.
* **Excessive talking** (`symptom_12_talkative`, Select One, **Required**): Rate from 0 (Not Present) to 3 (Severe) based on the patient's excessive talking.
* **Interrupting or intruding on others** (`symptom_13_interrupting`, Select One, **Required**): Rate from 0 (Not Present) to 3 (Severe) based on the patient's interrupting or intruding on others.
* **Difficulty waiting turns** (`symptom_14_waiting`, Select One, **Required**): Rate from 0 (Not Present) to 3 (Severe) based on the patient's difficulty waiting turns.
* **Acting without thinking** (`symptom_15_impulsive`, Select One, **Required**): Rate from 0 (Not Present) to 3 (Severe) based on the patient's tendency to act without thinking.
* **Clinician Additional Notes** (`clinician_notes`, Text, **Optional**): Enter any additional observations or notes regarding the patient's condition.
