# Aromatherapy Client Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about you, the client, to help our aromatherapy team provide personalized and safe treatment. Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure the best possible outcome for your sessions.

## How To Complete This Form

### 1. Client Information
*   **Full Name**: This is your complete name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
*   **Email Address**: This is the email address we will use to contact you with any questions or updates about your sessions.
*   **Phone Number**: This is your current phone number where we can reach you if needed.

### 2. Medical History
*   **Current Medications**: List all medications you are currently taking.
*   **Current Medical Conditions**: Please select all medical conditions that apply to you (e.g., High blood pressure, Heart condition, Asthma, etc.). We will take these conditions into account when deciding on essential oil blends for your treatment.
*   **Details About Your Conditions**: Provide any additional information about your medical conditions.
*   **Recent Surgery or Injuries**: Please select the time frame of your recent surgery or injury:
    *   **No recent surgery**: If you have not had any surgery or injury in the past year.
    *   **Within 6 months**: If you have had surgery or injury within the past 6 months.
    *   **Within 1 year**: If you have had surgery or injury within the past year.
    *   **Yes - describe**: If you have had surgery or injury and want to provide additional details.
*   **Details of Surgery or Injury**: Provide any additional information about your recent surgery or injury.

### 3. Allergies and Sensitivities
*   **Known Allergies**: List all allergies you have, including food, environmental, medication, etc.
*   **Allergies to Essential Oils**: List any essential oils you are allergic to.
*   **Scent Sensitivities or Triggers**: List any smells that cause you discomfort.

### 4. Skin and Dermatological Health
*   **Skin Type**: Select the type of skin you have:
    *   **Dry**
    *   **Oily**
    *   **Combination**
    *   **Sensitive**
    *   **Normal**
*   **Current Skin Conditions**: List any skin conditions you are experiencing (e.g., eczema, psoriasis, acne, rashes, etc.).
*   **Sensitive to Any Skin Care Products**: Select if you are sensitive to any skin care products:
    *   **No sensitivity**: If you are not sensitive to any skin care products.
    *   **Yes - list below**: If you are sensitive to certain skin care products.

### 5. Treatment Preferences
*   **Scent Application Preference**: Select the method you prefer for scent application:
    *   **Topical application**: Applying scents directly to the skin.
    *   **Diffusion only**: Smelling oils through a diffuser.
    *   **Inhalation**: Smelling the oils through the nose.
    *   **No preference**: No preference for scent application.
*   **Preferred Scent Strength**: Select the strength of scents you prefer:
    *   **Light and subtle**
    *   **Medium strength**
    *   **Strong**
    *   **Very strong**

### 6. Consent and Additional Information
*   **I understand the treatment and give my consent**: Select if you understand and consent to the treatment.
*   **Additional Health Information**: Provide any additional health information that is not listed above.
