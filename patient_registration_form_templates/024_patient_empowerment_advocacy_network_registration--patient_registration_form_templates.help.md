# Patient Empowerment Advocacy Network Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the steps to complete the Patient Empowerment Advocacy Network registration form. The form is designed to gather information about you, the individual joining the network.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out your Member Details on Page 1.
2. On Page 2, select your interests from the list provided.
3. On Page 3, provide your contact preferences.
4. On Page 4, add any additional comments you'd like to share.
5. On Page 5, review the submission information and ensure it's accurate.
6. Finally, submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Member Details** (`member_details`, text, required): This is where you will enter your basic contact information. Please fill in your full name, email address, and phone number.
* **Interests** (`interests`, select_multiple, false): Select all the interests that apply to you, such as Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, or Data Science.
* **Contact Preferences** (`contact_preferences`, text, false): Indicate how you would like to be contacted, such as email, phone, or messaging apps.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, false): If you have any additional information you'd like to share with the network, please add it here.
* **Submission Info** (`submission_info`, text, false): Double-check your contact information and submission details before submitting. This is the last step before completing your registration.
* **JotForm Builder** (`jfm_builder`, note, false): This is a note field for internal use by the form creator. You can disregard this field.
* **JotForm Version** (`jfm_version`, text, false): This field is automatically populated by the form creator and indicates the version of the form you are using.
* **JotForm Status** (`jfm_status`, text, false): This field is also automatically populated and indicates the status of your submission.
