# psychiatric_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect patient information, medical history, and psychiatric history for a comprehensive assessment and treatment planning in a psychiatric setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's personal information in the **Patient Information** page (page 1).
2. Provide a brief account of the patient's medical history in the **Medical History** page (page 2).
3. Describe the patient's mental status and any related issues in the **Mental Status** page (page 3).
4. Document the patient's psychiatric history, including any previous treatments or diagnoses, in the **Psychiatric History** page (page 4).
5. List any current medications and their side effects in the **Medications** page (page 5).
6. Note any allergies or medical conditions that may impact treatment in the **Allergies** and **Medical Conditions** pages (pages 6 and 7).
7. Provide the name, phone number, and relationship of the emergency contact in the **Emergency Contact** page (page 8).
8. Add any additional medical information in the **Additional Medical Information** page (page 9).
9. Describe the patient's mental health problems and treatment goals in the **Mental Health Problems** and **Treatment Goals** pages (pages 10 and 11).
10. Document the patient's family history and any relevant information in the **Family History** page (page 12).
11. Add any additional mental status information in the **Mental Status Additional Information** page (page 13).
12. Specify any psychiatric diagnosis, if applicable, in the **Psychiatric Diagnosis** page (page 14).
13. Document any medication side effects in the **Medication Side Effects** page (page 15).
14. Record the patient's social history, including any relevant details, in the **Social History** page (page 16).
15. Add any additional medical history information in the **Medical History Additional Information** page (page 17).
16. Outline the therapy plan, if applicable, in the **Therapy Plan** page (page 18).
17. Document any additional family history information in the **Family History Additional Information** page (page 19).
18. Record the psychiatric assessment and any relevant details in the **Psychiatric Assessment** page (page 20).
19. Specify the medication treatment plan, if applicable, in the **Medication Treatment Plan** page (page 21).
20. Schedule any follow-up appointments or check-ins in the **Follow Up** page (page 22).
21. Establish an emergency plan, if necessary, in the **Emergency Plan** page (page 23).
22. Evaluate the patient's mental status using the provided checklist in the **Mental Status Checklist** page (page 24).
23. Outline the discharge plan, if applicable, in the **Discharge Plan** page (page 25).
