# Coronavirus Suspected Patient Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about patients suspected of having COVID-19 for the purpose of assessment, management, and treatment of their condition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Select the correct options from dropdown menus where applicable.
3. Enter any necessary notes or comments in the text fields.
4. Sign the form to confirm you have completed it accurately and truthfully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (1, Text, Required): Enter the patient's first name as per their identity documents (e.g., passport, driver's license).
* **Last name** (2, Text, Required): Enter the patient's last name as per their identity documents (e.g., passport, driver's license).
* **Email** (3, Email, Optional): Enter the patient's email address, which can be used for communication.
* **Contact phone** (4, Text, Optional): Enter the patient's contact phone number, which can be used for communication.
* **Symptoms** (5, Select One, Required): Select the type of symptoms the patient is experiencing (e.g., COVID-19 symptoms, Common cold, Other).
* **Medical group** (6, Select Multiple, Required): Select the type of medical group the patient is associated with (e.g., Primary care physician, Specialist, Other).
* **Other medical group** (7, Text, Optional): If you selected "Other" in the medical group field, enter the specific medical group in this field.
* **Date last seen doctor** (8, Date, Required): Enter the date the patient last visited a doctor.
* **Time last seen doctor** (9, Time, Optional): Enter the time the patient last visited a doctor.
* **Last seen doctor name** (10, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the doctor the patient last visited.
* **Date of birth** (11, Date, Required): Enter the patient's date of birth as per their identity documents (e.g., passport, driver's license).
* **Gender** (12, Select One, Required): Select the patient's gender (e.g., Male, Female, Other).
* **Medical history** (13, Note, Optional): Enter any relevant medical history the patient has.
* **Insurance provider** (14, Select Multiple, Required): Select the patient's insurance provider (e.g., Private, Medicare, Medicaid, Other).
* **Emergency contact name** (15, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency contact relation** (16, Text, Optional): Enter the relation between the patient and the emergency contact person (e.g., Spouse, Child, Parent).
* **Emergency contact phone** (17, Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency contact email** (18, Email, Optional): Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency contact address** (19, Text, Optional): Enter the address of the emergency contact person.
* **Medical note** (20, Note, Optional): Enter any notes about the patient's medical condition.
* **Consent to share** (21, Select One, Required): Indicate whether the patient consents to share their data with healthcare professionals.
* **Note** (22, Text, Optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the patient's condition.
* **Note 2** (23, Text, Optional): Enter any further notes or comments about the patient's condition.
* **Patient signature** (24, Note, Required): The patient signs this form to confirm they have completed it accurately and truthfully.
* **Doctor signature** (25, Note, Required): The doctor signs this form to confirm they have received and reviewed the patient's information.
