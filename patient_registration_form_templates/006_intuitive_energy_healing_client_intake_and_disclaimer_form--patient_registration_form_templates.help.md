# Intuitive Energy Healing Client Intake And Disclaimer Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from clients for an Intuitive Energy Healing session. It includes a range of fields to gather details about the client, their medical history, and their consent to participate in the session. The purpose of this form is to ensure that the client provides necessary information for a safe and effective healing experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Enter your client's name in the "Client Name" field.
3. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Information" field, if applicable.
4. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
5. Indicate any medical conditions you have in the "Medical Conditions" field by selecting "Yes" or "No".
6. List any allergies you have in the "Allergies" field by selecting "Yes" or "No".
7. List your emergency contacts in the "Emergency Contacts" field by selecting "Yes" or "No".
8. Briefly describe any medical procedures you have undergone in the "Medical Procedure" field.
9. Provide a brief medical history in the "Medical History" field.
10. Indicate if you have medical insurance and provide details in the "Medical Insurance" field, if applicable.
11. Confirm your consent to participate in the session by selecting "Yes" in the "Client Consent" field.
12. Read and agree to the disclaimers by filling in the "Disclaimers" field.
13. Sign your name in the "Signature" field.
14. Enter the current date in the "Date" field in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
15. Enter your name in the "Client" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (Client Name, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, text, optional): Provide your contact information, including your phone number and email address.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
* **Medical Conditions** (Medical Conditions, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if you have any medical conditions, such as diabetes or heart disease.
* **Allergies** (Allergies, select_multiple, optional): List any allergies you have, such as peanuts or shellfish.
* **Emergency Contacts** (Emergency Contacts, select_multiple, optional): Identify any emergency contacts, such as family members or friends.
* **Medical Procedure** (Medical Procedure, text, optional): Describe any medical procedures you have undergone, such as surgery or hospitalizations.
* **Medical History** (Medical History, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your medical history, including any previous illnesses or conditions.
* **Medical Insurance** (Medical Insurance, text, optional): Indicate if you have medical insurance and provide details, if applicable.
* **Client Consent** (Client Consent, select_one, required): Confirm your consent to participate in the session by selecting "Yes".
* **Disclaimers** (Disclaimers, text, optional): Read and agree to the disclaimers, which are provided below.
* **Signature** (Signature, text, required): Sign your name in this field.
* **Date** (Date, time, required): Enter the current date in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
* **Client** (Client, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.

Please note that it is essential to fill in all the required fields (*) to complete this form correctly.
