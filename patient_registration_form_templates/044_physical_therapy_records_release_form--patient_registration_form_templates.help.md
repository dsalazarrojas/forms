The form "Physical Therapy Records Release Form" is used to collect information from patients about their medical history, contact details, and consent for physical therapy treatment. This form should only be completed for new patients, and any existing patients already registered with the physical therapy clinic should not be asked to complete it again. Before proceeding, please check if a similar form has already been completed for this patient.

# Physical Therapy Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Physical Therapy Records Release Form is used to collect essential patient information for treatment purposes. It helps the physical therapist to have a clear understanding of the patient's medical history, contact details, and consent for treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select one of the options provided in the "Physical Therapy Records Release Form" field.
2. Enter the patient's name in the "patient_name" field.
3. Enter the patient's date of birth in the "date_of_birth" field in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
4. Enter the patient's contact number in the "contact_number" field.
5. (Optional) If you have a medical record number, enter it in the "medical_record_number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Physical Therapy Records Release Form** (physical_therapy_records_release_form_page, select_one, required/optional):
	+ This is the main section of the form where you select one of the provided options. Please choose the most relevant option that best describes your treatment.
* **patient_name** (patient_name, text, required):
	+ Please enter the patient's full name.
* **date_of_birth** (date_of_birth, date, required):
	+ Enter the patient's date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **contact_number** (contact_number, text, required):
	+ Enter the patient's contact number (mobile or home phone number).
* **medical_record_number** (medical_record_number, text, optional):
	+ (Optional) If you have a medical record number, enter it here.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check the patient's name and date of birth for accuracy.
- If the patient has multiple contact numbers, enter the primary one.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact the physical therapist or clinic staff.
