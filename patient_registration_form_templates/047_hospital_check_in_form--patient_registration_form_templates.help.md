# hospital_check_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hospital check-in form is used to record the details of a patient's visit, including their identifying information, medical history, and contact details. This form is essential for providing timely and effective care to the patient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the patient's identifying information, such as their medical record number, date of birth, and contact phone number.
2. Provide the patient's medical record number, which is a unique identifier for their medical history.
3. Enter the date and time of admission, which is the date and time the patient is admitted to the hospital.
4. Enter the patient's contact email address and emergency contact information, including relation, name, phone number, and email.
5. Select the patient's medical record status, which indicates their current status in the hospital system.
6. Enter the patient's condition, if applicable.
7. Select the patient's language and race, which are essential for communication and record keeping.
8. Finally, select the patient's gender.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Identifier** (required): Enter the patient's medical record number to ensure accurate identification and linking to their medical history.
* **Medical Record Number** (required): Enter the patient's medical record number, which is a unique identifier for their medical history.
* **Date Of Birth** (required): Enter the patient's date of birth to ensure accurate record keeping and age calculation.
* **Date Of Admission** (required): Enter the date of admission, which is the date the patient is admitted to the hospital.
* **Time Of Admission** (optional): Enter the time of admission, which is the time the patient is admitted to the hospital.
* **Contact Phone Number** (optional): Enter the patient's contact phone number for easy communication.
* **Contact E-mail** (optional): Enter the patient's contact email address for easy communication.
* **Emergency Contact Relation** (optional): Select the relation of the emergency contact, such as family member or friend.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (optional): Enter the emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (optional): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact E-mail** (optional): Enter the emergency contact's email address.
* **Medical Record Status** (optional): Select the patient's medical record status, which indicates their current status in the hospital system.
* **Patient Condition** (optional): Enter the patient's current condition, if applicable.
* **Patient Language** (optional): Select the patient's language, which is essential for communication.
* **Patient Race** (optional): Select the patient's race, which is essential for record keeping.
* **Patient Gender** (optional): Select the patient's gender.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have the patient's consent before collecting their personal and medical information.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult the patient or the hospital's medical staff.
* If the patient's language is not listed, please select "Other" and specify the language in the "Other" field.
