# Life Coach Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Life Coach Intake Form is designed to collect essential information about you, our patient. This information will help our life coaches understand your needs and provide personalized support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out each section carefully and accurately.
2. Provide as much detail as possible for each field.
3. Review your answers for completeness and accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Personal Info
* **Personal Info (1)**: Enter your full name.
* **Coaching Goals (2)**: Describe your reasons for seeking life coaching services.
* **Contact Info (3)**: Enter your address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Emergency Contact (4)**: Enter the name of the person you would like us to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Info (5)**: Enter the contact information for the emergency contact person, including their phone number and address.
* **Medical Info (6)**: Share any medical information that may be relevant to your coaching experience.
* **Insurance Info (7)**: If applicable, provide your insurance information for any medical conditions or treatments.
* **Emergency Medical Info (8)**: Share any emergency medical information that may be relevant.
* **Medical History (9)**: Describe any medical history or conditions that may affect your coaching experience.
* **Allergies (10)**: List any allergies or sensitivities you have.
* **Medical Conditions (11)**: Share any medical conditions you may have.
* **Medications (12)**: List any medications or supplements you are currently taking.
* **Emergency Info (13)**: Share any additional emergency contact information or instructions.
* **Medical History Details (14)**: Provide more information about your medical history.
* **Medical History 2 (15)**: Describe any other medical history or conditions that may affect your coaching experience.
* **Emergency History (16)**: Share any emergency history or concerns.
* **Emergency Medical History (17)**: Share any emergency medical history or concerns.
* **Medication Info (18)**: List any medication or supplement information.
* **Emergency Contact Number (19)**: Enter the contact number for the emergency contact person.
* **Insurance Number (20)**: If applicable, provide your insurance number.
* **Emergency Info 2 (21)**: Share any additional emergency information.
* **Medical History 3 (22)**: Describe any additional medical history or conditions.
* **Contact Name (23)**: Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Contact Phone (24)**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email (25)**: Enter the email address of the contact person.

**Tips**

* Please answer each field as accurately as possible.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask.
* Completing this form will help us provide you with the best possible experience as a patient.
