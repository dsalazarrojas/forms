# Pediatric Patient Demographic Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect demographic information for pediatric patients. It's essential to fill it out accurately to ensure the best possible care for the patient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your basic demographic information, including your age, gender, and date of birth.
2. Provide your medical record number and primary care provider information.
3. Enter your emergency contact's name, phone number, and relationship with you.
4. Enter your address and zip code.
5. Enter your city, state, and country.
6. Add your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Age**: Enter your age in years, if applicable. This field is optional, but it's helpful for healthcare professionals to understand your stage of development.
* **Patient Gender**: Select your gender (Male, Female, or Other).
* **Patient DOB**: Enter your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY` to help the healthcare team understand your age.
* **Patient Weight**: Enter your weight in pounds and ounces. This field is optional but can be helpful for monitoring your growth.
* **Patient Height**: Enter your height in inches and feet. This field is optional but can be helpful for monitoring your growth.
* **Medical Record Number**: Enter your medical record number, which is used to identify your patient records.
* **Primary Care Provider**: Enter the name and contact information of your primary care provider.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of your emergency contact, such as a parent or guardian.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Describe your relationship with your emergency contact, such as a parent or sibling.
* **Patient Address**: Enter your home address, including street number and name.
* **Patient Zip**: Enter your zip code to help the healthcare team locate you.
* **Patient City**: Enter the name of your city or town.
* **Patient State**: Select your state from the dropdown list.
* **Patient Country**: Select your country from the dropdown list.
* **Patient Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Patient Email**: Enter your email address (not available in the original YAML, but added here for completeness)
