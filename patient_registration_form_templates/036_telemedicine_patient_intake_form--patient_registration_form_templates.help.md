# telemedicine_patient_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect patient information for telemedicine services, ensuring that healthcare providers have a comprehensive understanding of the patient's health status and needs.

## How To Complete This Form

### Completing the form is a straightforward process:

* Fill out the form by entering the required information in each field.
* Ensure that you fill out all fields marked as required.
* If a field is unclear, please refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section for clarification.
* Once completed, submit the form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`id: 1`): Enter your basic personal information, such as name and contact details.
* **Medical History** (`id: 2`): Describe your medical history, including any previous illnesses, operations, or hospitalizations.
* **Medical Conditions** (`id: 3`): List any current or past medical conditions that you may have.
* **Meditations** (`id: 4`): Report any medications you are currently taking.
* **Allergies** (`id: 5`): List any allergies or sensitivities you may have.
* **Medications List** (`id: 6`): List the medications you are currently taking, including dosages and frequencies.
* **Patient Notes** (`id: 7`): Share any additional information about your health or medical concerns.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`id: 8`): Enter the contact information for your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Person** (`id: 9`): Enter the name and contact information for your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Phone** (`id: 10`): Enter your emergency contact person's phone number.
* **Patient Signature** (`id: 11`): Sign and date this form to confirm that you have completed it truthfully.
* **Healthcare Provider** (`id: 12`): Enter the name and contact information for your healthcare provider.
* **Insurance** (`id: 13`): Enter your insurance information, if applicable.
* **Billing Address** (`id: 14`): Enter your billing address.
* **Home Address** (`id: 15`): Enter your home address.
* **Work Address** (`id: 16`): Enter your work address.
* **Insurance Provider** (`id: 17`): Enter the name and contact information for your insurance provider.
* **Phone Number** (`id: 18`): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`id: 19`): Enter your date of birth.
* **Social Security** (`id: 20`): Enter your social security number or other government-issued ID number.
* **Medical Record Number** (`id: 21`): Enter your medical record number.
* **Insurance Plan** (`id: 22`): Enter the name of your insurance plan.
* **Medical Record Number Copy** (`id: 23`): Enter your medical record number.
* **Insurance Plan Copy** (`id: 24`): Enter the name of your insurance plan.
* **Patient Interview** (`id: 25`): This field is intended for additional comments or information, but is optional and not required for processing.

### Tips

* Please answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
* If unsure about a field or question, refer to the relevant medical professional for guidance.
* Ensure that you complete all required fields before submitting the form.
