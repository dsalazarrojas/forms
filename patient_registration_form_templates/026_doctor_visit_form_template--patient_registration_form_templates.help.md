# Doctor Visit Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Doctor Visit Form Template is a form designed to collect information from patients during a doctor visit. The form gathers essential details that help healthcare providers understand the patient's background, medical history, and any relevant information that may impact their treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter the patient's name**: In the "Patient Name" field, type the patient's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
2. **Enter the patient's contact phone number**: In the "Contact Phone" field, type the patient's contact phone number.
3. **Enter the patient's date of birth**: In the "Date of Birth" field, select the patient's date of birth.
4. **Enter the patient's email**: In the "Patient Email" field, type the patient's email address.
5. **Enter the visit date**: In the "Visit Date" field, select the date of the visit.
6. **Enter the visit time**: In the "Visit Time" field, select the time of the visit.
7. **Add doctor comments**: In the "Doctor Comments" field, type any relevant comments or observations made by the doctor during the visit.
8. **Add medical notes**: In the "Medical Notes" field, type any medical history, allergies, or other relevant information about the patient.
9. **Enter the patient's address**: In the "Patient Address" field, type the patient's current address.
10. **Enter the patient's medical history**: In the "Medical History" field, type any relevant medical history or conditions that may impact the patient's treatment.
11. **List the patient's current medications**: In the "Patient Medication" field, list any current medications or treatments the patient is taking.
