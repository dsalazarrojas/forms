# Patient Data Sharing Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about who to share patient data with and how to share it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand its purpose and implications.
3. Double-check your answers to prevent errors or duplication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical record.
* **Email**: Enter the patient's email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter the patient's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Sharing Consent**: Select 'Yes' to consent to sharing your patient data with others. If 'No', please provide a reason in the 'Sharing Reason' field.
* **Sharing Reason**: If you selected 'No' for Sharing Consent, describe the reason for not sharing the data.
* **Sharing Agreement**: Select the agreements you are willing to adhere to for data sharing. You can select multiple options.
* **Data Sharing**: Select the types of data you are willing to share. You can select multiple options.
* **Sharing Method**: Select the method(s) through which you are willing to share the data. You can select multiple options.
* **Sharing Frequency**: Select the frequency at which you are willing to share the data. You can select multiple options.
* **Sharing Status**: Select the current status of the data sharing arrangement.
