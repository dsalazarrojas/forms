# Holistic Health Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Holistic Health Intake Form is designed to gather essential information from patients or their authorized representatives for the purpose of providing comprehensive and personalized care. It is a critical tool for healthcare professionals to understand a patient's medical history, contact details, and personal preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and date of birth as accurately as possible.
2. Provide your address if you have one.
3. Enter your email address and phone number for effective communication.
4. Select your medical specialty from the list provided.
5. Provide any medical notes or details that may be relevant to your condition.
6. If necessary, add any additional comments or notes in the "Note" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Home Address** (`address`, text, optional): Provide your home address for billing or contact purposes.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for easy communication with healthcare professionals.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
- **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Provide any relevant medical history or conditions that may affect your treatment.
- **Medical Specialties** (`medical_specialties`, select_one, required): Choose one or more medical specialties that apply to your condition from the provided options.
- **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes that may be helpful for your healthcare professionals to understand your condition better.

## Tips
- Ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask your healthcare professional for clarification.
- This form is designed to be completed in a private setting, so please be respectful of patient confidentiality.
