# Health History Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect comprehensive health information from patients, including their personal details, medical history, and family history. The information collected will help healthcare professionals assess and address patient needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by filling out the patient's personal details (Page 1).
2. Move on to the contact details section (Page 2).
3. Provide information about the patient's medical history (Page 3).
4. Select the patient's medical conditions (Page 4).
5. List the patient's medications (Page 5).
6. Provide information about the patient's surgical history (Page 6).
7. Record the patient's medical tests and results (Page 7).
8. List the patient's allergies (Page 8).
9. Provide information about the patient's family history (Page 9).
10. Select the patient's personal history (Page 10).
11. Enter the patient's goals and objectives (Page 11).
12. Record the patient's current status (Page 12).
13. Provide information about the patient's status date and location (Pages 13-14).
14. Enter any other status details or locations (Pages 15-16).
15. Select the patient's healthcare provider and other relevant information (Pages 17-18).
16. Provide contact information for a family member or emergency contact (Pages 19-20).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Personal**: Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and other identifying information.
* **Contact Details**: Enter the patient's contact information, including phone number and address.
* **Medical History**: Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous medical conditions or events.
* **Medical Conditions**: Select all the medical conditions that apply to the patient (e.g., diabetes, hypertension, etc.).
* **Medications**: List all the patient's current medications, including the name and dosage.
* **Surgical History**: Select all the patient's previous surgeries or medical procedures.
* **Medical Tests**: Record the date and results of all the patient's recent medical tests.
* **Medical Test Results**: Select all the results of the patient's recent medical tests.
* **Allergies**: List all the patient's known allergies.
* **Family History**: Select all the medical conditions or events that affect the patient's family members.
* **Personal History**: Select all the patient's personal history events or conditions (e.g., education, employment, etc.).
* **Patient Goals**: Enter the patient's goals and objectives for treatment or recovery.
* **Patient Status**: Select the patient's current status (e.g., healthy, sick, etc.).
* **Patient Status Date**: Enter the date of the patient's current status.
* **Patient Status Time**: Enter the time of the patient's current status.
* **Patient Status Location**: Select the location where the patient's current status was recorded.
* **Other Status Details**: Enter any additional details about the patient's status.
* **Other Status Location**: Enter any other location where the patient's status was recorded.
* **Other Status**: Enter any other relevant information about the patient's status.
* **Patient Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm completion.
* **Healthcare Provider**: Select the patient's healthcare provider or doctor.
* **Healthcare Worker**: Select the healthcare worker who will be providing care to the patient.
* **Family Members**: Select all the family members who are relevant to the patient's care.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and contact information of the patient's emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone Number**: Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Patient Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm completion.
