# IVF Patient Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IVF Patient Information Form is designed to collect essential details about patients undergoing IVF treatment. This form is used to gather contact information, medical history, and other relevant details that are critical for their treatment and care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form accurately and completely, using your real name, address, phone number, and email.
2. Be honest when answering the medical history section, including any pre-existing medical conditions or allergies.
3. If you are unsure about any question, do not hesitate to reach out to your healthcare provider.
4. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (<contact_info>, text, optional): This section asks for your contact details, including your name, address, phone number, and email. Please ensure that you provide your accurate and up-to-date information to facilitate effective communication.
* **Patient Details** (<patient_details, text, optional): This field is for you to describe yourself, including any relevant medical history or allergies.
* **Consent** (<consent, select_one, optional): Please read and select "I understand the purpose of this form" and "I have read and agree to the terms of this form" to acknowledge your consent.
* **Medical History** (<medical_history, text, optional): Describe any pre-existing medical conditions that may affect your treatment.
* **Appointment Information** (<appointment_info, text, optional): This field is for you to provide details about your appointment schedule and timing.
* **Contact Number** (<contact_number, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number to ensure that you can be reached during your treatment.
* **Address** (<address, text, optional): Provide your home address to facilitate any necessary medical correspondence.
* **Email** (<email, email, optional): Enter your email address for secure communication and updates about your treatment.
* **Medical Conditions** (<medical_conditions, text, optional): List any pre-existing medical conditions that may affect your treatment.
* **Appointment Date** (<appointment_date, date, optional): Enter the date and time of your appointment for scheduling purposes.

## Tips
- Be clear and concise in your answers.
- Review your responses before submission.
- If you are unsure about any question, do not hesitate to reach out to your healthcare provider.
- Ensure that you answer all required fields accurately.
