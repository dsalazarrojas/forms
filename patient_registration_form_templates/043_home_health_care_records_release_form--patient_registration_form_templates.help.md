# Home Health Care Records Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Home Health Care Records Release Form is a document used to collect and share the patient's medical information with healthcare providers. It ensures that the patient has given their consent for their medical records to be accessed and used by their providers.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information and emergency contact information.
2. Share your medical information, medical history, procedures, and conditions.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (`id: 1`, `name: patient_info`, `type: text`, `required: false`):
    * This field is where you enter your name, contact information, and other relevant details.
* **Contact Info** (`id: 2`, `name: contact_info`, `type: text`, `required: false`):
    * This field is for you to provide your contact information, such as phone number and address.
* **Medical Info** (`id: 3`, `name: medical_info`, `type: text`, `required: false`):
    * Here, you can provide any relevant medical information or history that your healthcare provider should know.
* **Release Info** (`id: 4`, `name: release_info`, `type: text`, `required: false`):
    * This field is for you to specify what medical information you are releasing to your healthcare provider.
* **Signature** (`id: 5`, `name: signature_info`, `type: text`, `required: false`):
    * Sign and date this field to confirm your consent for the healthcare provider to access and use your medical records.
* **Medical Record** (`id: 6`, `name: medical_record`, `type: text`, `required: false`):
    * This is where you provide any specific medical records or information.
* **Provider Info** (`id: 7`, `name: provider_info`, `type: text`, `required: false`):
    * This field is for you to provide any relevant information about your healthcare provider.
* **Signature (2)** (`id: 8`, `name: signature_2`, `type: text`, `required: false`):
    * This is a second signature field for you to confirm your consent.
* **Date Signed** (`id: 9`, `name: date_signed`, `type: date`, `required: false`):
    * Enter the date you signed this form.
* **Time Signed** (`id: 10`, `name: time_signed`, `type: time`, `required: false`):
    * Enter the time you signed this form.
* **Date Signed 2** (`id: 11`, `name: date_signed_2`, `type: date`, `required: false`):
    * This is an additional date field for confirmation.
* **Medical History** (`id: 12`, `name: medical_history`, `type: text`, `required: false`):
    * This field is for you to provide any relevant medical history.
* **Medical Procedure** (`id: 13`, `name: medical_procedure`, `type: text`, `required: false`):
    * Here, you can specify any medical procedures you are undergoing or have undergone.
* **Medical Condition** (`id: 14`, `name: medical_condition`, `type: text`, `required: false`):
    * This field is for you to report any current or past medical conditions.
* **Medication** (`id: 15`, `name: medication`, `type: text`, `required: false`):
    * This field is for you to list any medications you are taking.
* **Medical Supplies** (`id: 16`, `name: medical_supplies`, `type: text`, `required: false`):
    * This field is for you to specify any medical supplies you are using.
* **Medical Equipment** (`id: 17`, `name: medical_equipment`, `type: text`, `required: false`):
    * This field is for you to list any medical equipment you are using.
* **Emergency Contact** (`id: 18`, `name: emergency_contact`, `type: text`, `required: false`):
    * Provide the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`id: 19`, `name: emergency_contact_phone`, `type: text`, `required: false`):
    * Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`id: 20`, `name: emergency_contact_relationship`, `type: text`, `required: false`):
    * Specify the relationship between you and your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`id: 21`, `name: emergency_contact_info`, `type: text`, `required: false`):
    * This field is for you to provide additional information about your emergency contact.
* **Insurance Info** (`id: 22`, `name: insurance_info`, `type: text`, `required: false`):
    * This is where you can provide any relevant insurance information.
* **Medical Provider Info** (`id: 23`, `name: medical_provider_info`, `type: text`, `required: false`):
    * This field is for you to provide any information about your healthcare provider.
* **Signature Info** (`id: 24`, `name: signature_info_2`, `type: text`, `required: false`):
    * This field is for you to provide any additional information related to your signature.
* **Signature (3)** (`id: 25`, `name: signature_2`, `type: text`, `required: false`):
    * This is the third signature field for you to confirm your consent.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that your healthcare provider has all necessary information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact your healthcare provider.
* Keep in mind that this form is only valid for your consent to be treated by your current healthcare provider.
