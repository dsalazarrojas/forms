# Ultrasound Examination Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect patient registration information for an ultrasound examination.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information in each of the text fields.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you have provided accurate details.
3. Submit the form once you have filled in all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Registration Form**: Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and other identifying information.
* **Patient Details**: Provide any additional details about the patient, such as their medical history or allergies.
* **Contact Info**: Enter the patient's contact information, including phone number and email.
* **Appointment Requests**: Enter any specific requests or requirements for the appointment, such as time of day or day of the week.
* **Consent**: Confirm that you have obtained consent from the patient to proceed with the examination.
* **Medical History**: Enter any relevant medical history that may be helpful for the examination.
* **Ultrasound Examination Details**: Provide details about the examination, such as the location and type of ultrasound.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information that may be helpful for the examiners.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check your entries for spelling and grammar errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
