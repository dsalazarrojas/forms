# vaccination_check_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vaccination check-in form is used to collect information from patients during a vaccination process. It gathers data on vaccination status, contact information, patient details, and potential medical history to aid healthcare providers in delivering care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the "First Page" option, which is a general overview of the form.
2. Fill in the "Contact Information" section with the patient's contact details.
3. Move on to the "Patient Information" section and provide any necessary details about the patient.
4. In the "Vaccination Details" section, select whether the patient has received the vaccination or not.
5. If necessary, provide medical history and medical notes for the patient.
6. Finally, choose a contact method and provide the contact phone number.
