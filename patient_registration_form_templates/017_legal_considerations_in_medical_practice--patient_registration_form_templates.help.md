# Legal Considerations In Medical Practice - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legal Considerations In Medical Practice form is a tool used to collect and document important legal considerations that patients need to be aware of in medical practice. This form is designed to be completed by patients or their representatives and will be used to maintain accurate and up-to-date medical records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure that you understand the purpose and fields involved.
2. Complete each field accurately and to the best of your ability.
3. If a field is required, please ensure that it is completed before moving on to the next field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Patient Registration Form
* **Patient Registration Form** (`patient_registration_form`, text, optional): This field allows you to enter a brief description of the patient or their representative.
* **Consent Type** (`consent_type`, select one, optional): Please select the type of consent that applies to your situation.
	+ Informed Consent: You have been informed and understand the risks and benefits of the medical treatment or procedure.
	+ Express Consent: You have given consent for a specific medical treatment or procedure without being fully informed.
	+ Implied Consent: You have implied consent for a medical treatment or procedure, typically because you are an emergency patient.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): Please enter the patient's name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Please enter the patient's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Medical License Number** (`medical_license_number`, text, optional): Please enter your medical license number if applicable.
* **Address 1** (`address_1`, text, optional): Please enter the address of the patient or their representative.
* **Address 2** (`address_2`, text, optional): Please enter any additional address information if needed.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Please enter the patient's phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter the patient's email address.
* **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, text, optional): Please enter the patient's medical record number if applicable.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field or the form as a whole, please consult with a medical professional before completing it.
* This form is a tool to help maintain accurate and up-to-date medical records, and any errors or inaccuracies may have legal consequences.
