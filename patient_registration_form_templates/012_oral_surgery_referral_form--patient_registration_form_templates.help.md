# Oral Surgery Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Oral Surgery Referral Form is a tool used by oral surgeons to refer patients to other surgeons for further treatment. This guide will walk you through each section of the form, helping you to complete it accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Review the form carefully**: Before completing the form, ensure you have all the necessary information about the patient, including their contact details, medical history, and referral information.
2. **Fill in patient information**: Enter the patient's details, including their name, address, phone number, and date of birth.
3. **Select patient status**: Choose the patient's status as "Active", "Inactive", or "Unknown".
4. **Select doctor of visit and date**: Choose the doctor who will be seeing the patient next and the date of the visit.
5. **Enter referral details**: Provide any relevant information about the referral, including the reason for referral and any additional comments.
6. **Enter contact details**: Enter the patient's and surgeon's contact details, including phone numbers and email addresses.
7. **Add any additional notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the patient's condition or treatment.
8. **Review and submit**: Review the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting it to the receiving surgeon.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's details, including their name, address, phone number, and date of birth.
* **Referral Information**: Enter any relevant information about the referral, including the reason for referral and any additional comments.
* **Contact Information**: Enter the patient's and surgeon's contact details, including phone numbers and email addresses.
* **Surgeon Signature**: Enter your signature as the referring surgeon.
* **Patient Status**: Select the patient's status as "Active", "Inactive", or "Unknown".
* **Doctor of Visit**: Choose the doctor who will be seeing the patient next.
* **Date of Visit**: Enter the date of the visit.
* **Reason for Referral**: Enter the reason for referral.
* **Referral Number**: Enter the referral number (if applicable).
* **Surgeon Phone**: Enter your phone number as the referring surgeon.
* **Patient Address**: Enter the patient's address.
* **Patient Email**: Enter the patient's email address.
* **Referring Doctor**: Enter the name of the doctor referring the patient.
* **Patient Note**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the patient's condition or treatment.
* **Surgeon Note**: Enter any additional notes or comments from the referring surgeon.
* **Date of Follow-up**: Enter the date of the follow-up appointment.
* **Follow-up Status**: Select the follow-up status as "None" or "Other".
* **Patient Phone**: Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Patient DOB**: Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Surgeon Specialty**: Enter your specialty as the referring surgeon.
* **Patient Insurance**: Enter the patient's insurance information.
* **Surgeon Department**: Enter your department as the referring surgeon.

Note that some fields may not be required for patients who are already established patients of your practice.
