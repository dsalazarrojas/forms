# Medication And Neuropathy Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your personal details, medications, neuropathy symptoms, medical history, and contact information to help us better understand your condition and provide you with the most effective treatment options.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your personal details in the "Personal Details" section.
3. Select the medications you are currently taking in the "Medications" section.
4. Choose all the neuropathy symptoms you are experiencing in the "Neuropathy Symptoms" section.
5. Provide a detailed description of your medical history in the "Medical History" section.
6. Enter the date and time when you first started experiencing neuropathy symptoms in the "Date of Symptom Onset" and "Time of Symptom Onset" sections.
7. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Information" section.
8. Enter your email address in the "Email" section.
9. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Personal Details** (`personal_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, date of birth, and any other relevant personal details.
  - **Medsications** (`medications`, select_one, required/optional): Select all the medications you are currently taking, such as pain medications, antibiotics, or other prescription medications.
  - **Neuropathy Symptoms** (`neuropathy_symptoms`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose all the neuropathy symptoms you are experiencing, such as numbness, tingling, or pain.
  - **Medical History** (`medical_history`, note, required/optional): Provide detailed information about your medical history, including any previous treatments, surgeries, or hospitalizations.
  - **Date of Symptom Onset** (`date_of_symptom_onset`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when you first started experiencing neuropathy symptoms.
  - **Time of Symptom Onset** (`time_of_symptom_onset`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of day when you first started experiencing neuropathy symptoms.
  - **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact information, including your address and any other relevant details.
  - **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
  - **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
