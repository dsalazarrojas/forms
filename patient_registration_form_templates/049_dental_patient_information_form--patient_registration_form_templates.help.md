# Dental Patient Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect essential information about dental patients, including their name, date of birth, contact details, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the patient's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field, using the YYYY-MM-DD format.
3. Enter the patient's address in the "Address" field.
4. Enter the patient's phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, Text, Required): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, Date, Optional): Enter the patient's date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Address** (`address`, Text, Required): Enter the patient's home or work address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Required): Enter the patient's phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter all required fields (Patient Name, Date of Birth, Address, and Phone).
* Use the correct format for the Date of Birth field (YYYY-MM-DD).
* Double-check your entries for accuracy.
