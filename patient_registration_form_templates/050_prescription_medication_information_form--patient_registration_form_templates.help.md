# Prescription Medication Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a patient's prescription medication to help healthcare providers plan and monitor treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the patient's information, including their name, address, and any other relevant details.
2. Enter the name of the medication the patient is taking.
3. Specify the dosage instructions for the medication.
4. Enter the start and end dates for the medication regimen.
5. Indicate how often the patient takes the medication.
6. Enter the name of the physician prescribing the medication.
7. If available, provide any relevant medical record information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, text, optional): Enter the patient's address, including street, city, state, and zip code.
* **Medication Name** (`medication_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the medication the patient is taking.
* **Medication Dosage** (`medication_dosage`, text, optional): Specify the dosage instructions for the medication, including frequency and quantity.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the medication regimen began.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the medication regimen will end.
* **Medication Frequency** (`medication_frequency`, text, optional): Indicate how often the patient takes the medication (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly).
* **Physician Name** (`physician_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the physician prescribing the medication.
* **Medical Record** (`medical_record`, text, optional): If available, provide any relevant medical record information that may aid in treatment planning.

## Tips
* Ensure accurate and complete information is provided to help ensure effective patient care.
* If unsure about any of the fields, consult with the patient or their healthcare provider.
* Review and update the form regularly as medication regimens may change over time.
