# Hospital Accommodation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from patients or their representatives to ensure their comfort and safety during their stay at the hospital.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's name, contact information, and patient ID (if applicable).
2.  **Accommodation Needs**: Describe the type of accommodation the patient requires (e.g., single, double, triple, family, or suite).
3.  **Preferred Room Type**: Select the preferred room type for the patient (e.g., single, double, triple, family, or suite).
4.  **Special Requests**: Enter any special requests or requirements for the patient's stay (e.g., wheelchair, medical equipment).
5.  **Emergency Contact**: Enter the contact information for the patient's emergency contact.
6.  **Patient Status**: Select the patient's status (e.g., inpatient, outpatient, or observation).
7.  **Doctor Information**: Enter the doctor's name and contact information (if applicable).
8.  **Patient ID**: Enter the patient's ID number (if applicable).
9.  **Medical History**: Enter the patient's medical history, including any relevant health information.
10. **Allergies & Intolerances**: Enter any allergies or intolerances that the patient has.
11. **Patient Photo**: Upload a photo of the patient (if applicable).
12. **Accommodation Arrival**: Enter the date and time of the patient's arrival.
13. **Accommodation Departure**: Enter the date and time of the patient's expected departure.
14. **Patient Contact**: Enter the contact information for the patient's representative (if applicable).

### Patient Information
* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name.
* **Contact Information**: Enter the patient's contact information, including address, phone number, and email.
* **Patient ID**: If applicable, enter the patient's ID number.
