# Pet Care Reminder Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Care Reminder Form is designed to collect information for patients' upcoming procedures. This form is used by medical staff to create reminders for patients' appointments, procedures, and other important dates and times.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the patient's **Patient ID** to ensure that the correct patient information is associated with the reminder.
2. Select the **Reminder Date** for the procedure or appointment.
3. Choose the **Reminder Time** for the procedure or appointment.
4. Enter the **Procedure ID**, which is the unique identifier for the procedure.
5. Enter the **Client Name** for the patient.
6. Select **Yes/No** to indicate whether the procedure is for **Yes** (a pet) or **No** (not a pet).
7. Enter the **Procedure Date** for the procedure or appointment.
8. Enter the **Procedure Time** for the procedure or appointment.
9. Provide any additional **Notes** about the procedure or appointment.
10. Enter the patient's **Email** address for contact purposes.
11. Enter the patient's **Phone Number** for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Care Reminder** (`pet_care_reminder_form`): This is the main text field for entering the reminder details.
* **Patient ID** (`patient_id`): Enter the unique identifier for the patient, usually an ID number.
* **Reminder Date** (`reminder_date`): Select the date for the reminder.
* **Reminder Time** (`reminder_time`): Select the time for the reminder.
* **Procedure ID** (`procedure_id`): Enter the unique identifier for the procedure.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`): Enter the name of the client (patient).
* **Pet Type** (`pet_type`): Select **Yes** if the procedure is for a pet, or **No** if it's not.
* **Procedure Date** (`procedure_date`): Select the date for the procedure.
* **Procedure Time** (`procedure_time`): Select the time for the procedure.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Provide any additional notes or comments about the procedure.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter the patient's email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter the patient's phone number for contact purposes.
