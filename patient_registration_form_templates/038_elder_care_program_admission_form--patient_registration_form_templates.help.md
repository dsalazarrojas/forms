# Elder Care Program Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect patient information for the Elder Care Program, which provides medical care services for older adults.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Provide accurate and relevant information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text): Please enter your name as it appears on your official government-issued ID or passport.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text): Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, email): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, note): Provide any relevant medical history, including past injuries, illnesses, or surgeries.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text): Enter the name of the person you would like to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email): Enter the email address of the emergency contact.
* **Address** (`patient_address`, text): Enter your address for billing and other purposes.
* **Relation** (`emergency_contact_relation`, select_one): Choose the relation between you and the emergency contact (e.g., Mother, Father, Brother, Sister, Friend, or Other).
* **Care Facility Name** (`care_facility_name`, text): Enter the name of the care facility you are being admitted to.
* **Care Facility Contact** (`care_facility_contact`, text): Enter the contact information for the care facility.
* **Physician Name** (`physician_name`, text): Enter the name of your primary care physician.
* **Physician License Number** (`physician_license`, number): Enter your primary care physician's license number (if applicable).
* **Physician Phone** (`physician_phone`, text): Enter your primary care physician's phone number.
* **Physician Email** (`physician_email`, email): Enter your primary care physician's email address (if applicable).
* **Medication List** (`medication_list`, note): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Allergy List** (`allergy_list`, note): List any allergies you have.
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, note): Provide any additional medical notes or information.
* **Social Worker Name** (`social_worker_name`, text): Enter the name of your social worker (if applicable).
* **Social Worker Phone** (`social_worker_phone`, text): Enter the phone number of your social worker (if applicable).
* **Social Worker Email** (`social_worker_email`, email): Enter the email address of your social worker (if applicable).
* **Patient Language** (`patient_language`, select_one): Choose your primary language (e.g., English, Spanish, Other).
* **Other Language** (`patient_language_other`, text): If you chose "Other" above, enter the language you speak.
* **Signature** (`signature`, date): Date and sign this form to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
