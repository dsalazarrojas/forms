# Child Consultation Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from a caregiver or parent about a child who requires consultation services. The information gathered will help healthcare professionals provide the best possible care and guidance for the child.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the preferred date and time for the consultation.
2. Provide the child's legal first and last name.
3. Enter the child's date of birth.
4. Enter the child's current age.
5. Provide the caregiver's legal first and last name.
6. Select the relationship between the caregiver and the child.
7. Enter the caregiver's email address.
8. Enter the caregiver's phone number.
9. Choose the type of consultation needed.
10. Briefly describe the reason for seeking consultation.
11. Indicate if the child has had previous consultations.
12. List any current medications the child is taking (if applicable).
13. Enter the name of the insurance provider (if applicable).
14. Enter the insurance policy or member number (if applicable).
15. Select the preferred contact method.
16. Add any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consultation Date Requested**: Select the preferred date for the consultation.
* **Preferred Time**: Select the preferred time for the consultation.
* **Child First Name**: Enter the legal first name of the child.
* **Child Last Name**: Enter the legal last name of the child.
* **Child Date of Birth**: Enter the child's birth date.
* **Child Age**: Enter the current age of the child in years.
* **Caregiver First Name**: Enter the legal first name of the caregiver.
* **Caregiver Last Name**: Enter the legal last name of the caregiver.
* **Relationship to Child**: Select the relationship between the caregiver and the child (e.g., Parent, Guardian, Grandparent, etc.).
* **Caregiver Email**: Enter the primary email address for contact.
* **Caregiver Phone**: Enter the primary phone number for contact.
* **Type of Consultation Needed**: Select the type of consultation needed (e.g., Initial assessment, Behavioral consultation, etc.).
* **Reason for Consultation**: Briefly describe the reason for seeking consultation.
* **Has the Child Had Previous Consultations**: Indicate if the child has had previous consultations.
* **Current Medications**: List any current medications the child is taking (if applicable).
* **Insurance Provider**: Enter the name of the insurance provider (if applicable).
* **Insurance Policy Number**: Enter the insurance policy or member number (if applicable).
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Select the preferred contact method (e.g., Email, Phone call, etc.).
* **Additional Notes**: Add any additional information you would like to share.
