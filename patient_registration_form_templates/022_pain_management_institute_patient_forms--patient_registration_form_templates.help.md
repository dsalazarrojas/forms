# Pain Management Institute Patient Forms - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pain Management Institute Patient Forms are designed to collect relevant information from patients to aid in their treatment and care. The forms are intended to be a starting point for further assessment and consultation with medical professionals.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Patient Information** page with your personal details.
2. On the **Pain Level** page, select the level that best describes your current pain experience.
3. On the **Medication History** page, choose the medication you are currently taking.
4. On the **Medical Conditions** page, provide any relevant medical conditions that may be affecting your treatment.
5. On the **Treatment Plan** page, note down your treatment plan or any specific concerns you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (text)**: This section is for providing your personal details, such as your name, contact information, and any other relevant information you'd like to share with medical professionals.
* **Pain Level (select_multiple)**: Choose the level that best describes your current pain experience:
	+ Low: You are experiencing minimal pain or discomfort.
	+ Moderate: Your pain is noticeable but manageable with treatment.
	+ Severe: Your pain is intense and significantly impacts your daily activities.
* **Medication History (select_one)**: Choose the medication you are currently taking:
	+ Ibuprofen: A pain reliever commonly used for headaches and minor aches.
	+ Acetaminophen: A pain reliever and fever reducer.
	+ Aspirin: A pain reliever and anti-inflammatory.
* **Medical Conditions (text)**: Please list any medical conditions that may be affecting your treatment. This can include pre-existing conditions, allergies, or previous surgeries.
* **Treatment Plan (note)**: Provide a brief note about your treatment plan or any specific concerns you have. This can include your treatment goals, medications, or exercises you are following.

## Tips

* Be as accurate and honest as possible when answering the questions.
* If you are unsure about any of the options, feel free to ask a medical professional for guidance.
* Remember to review your form carefully before submitting it to ensure all relevant information is included.
