# Patient Ownership Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a patient's ownership status, decision-making ability, and medical records management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. If applicable, describe the patient's medical condition in the "Medical Condition" field.
3. Select all applicable options for the "Owns" field to indicate the patient's ownership status.
4. Choose the correct option for "Decision Making" to indicate the patient's decision-making ability.
5. Enter any relevant information about the patient's medical records in the "Medical Record" field.
6. Select all applicable options for "Records" to indicate the patient's medical records management status.
7. If necessary, provide a note about the patient's consent status in the "Consent" field.
8. Provide any additional details about the patient's consent in the "Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name.
* **Medical Condition**: Describe the patient's medical condition, if applicable.
* **Owns**: Select all applicable options to indicate the patient's ownership status:
	+ Option 1: Patient fully owns their records.
	+ Option 2: Patient partially owns their records.
	+ Option 3: Patient does not own their records.
* **Decision Making**: Choose an option to indicate the patient's decision-making ability:
	+ Option 1: Patient makes decisions with full capacity.
	+ Option 2: Patient makes decisions with partial capacity.
	+ Option 3: Patient does not make decisions.
	+ Option 4: Not applicable/unknown.
* **Medical Record**: Enter any relevant information about the patient's medical records.
* **Records**: Select all applicable options to indicate the patient's medical records management status:
	+ Option 1: Patient manages their records themselves.
	+ Option 2: Patient delegates record management to someone else.
	+ Option 3: Not applicable/unknown.
	+ Option 4: Medical records are not kept.
* **Consent**: If necessary, provide a note about the patient's consent status.
* **Details**: Provide any additional details about the patient's consent.
* **Consent**: This field is not required.
* **Details**: This field is not required.
* **Medical Record**: This field is not required.
* **Medical Records**: This field is required to understand the patient's records management status. 

Note: This form is a template and should not be used without the professional guidance of a healthcare professional.
