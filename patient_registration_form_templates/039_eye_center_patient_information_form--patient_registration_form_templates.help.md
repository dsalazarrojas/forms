# eye_center_patient_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and record a patient's medical history, contact information, and other relevant details for the eye center's registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and contact information on page 1 (patient_info).
2. Share your contact information on page 2 (contact_info).
3. Enter your medical history, including any allergies, medications, and medical procedures on page 3-5 (medical_history, medical_history_other, medical_history_illness).
4. Specify any medical conditions or illnesses you may have on page 6 (medical_history_disease).
5. Mention any injuries, hospitalizations, or travel history on page 7-9 (medical_history_injury, medical_history_travel, medical_history_hospitalization).
6. Enter any work-related medical history on page 10 (medical_history_work).
7. Provide additional medical history information on page 11 (medical_history_other).
8. Specify your relationship with the emergency contact on page 12 (emergency_contact_relationship).
9. Add any notes or comments about your medical history on page 13 (patient_notes).
10. Enter any family or social history on page 14-15 (medical_history_family_history, medical_history_social_history).
11. Finally, confirm your medical history information on page 16-17 (medical_history_surgery, medical_history_illness).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info** (page 1): Enter your name, address, phone number, and other contact details.
* **Contact Info** (page 2): Share your contact information, including phone number and email address.
* **Medical History** (page 3): Describe any medical conditions, allergies, or previous illnesses.
* **Medical Conditions** (page 4): List any medical conditions you are currently experiencing.
* **Emergency Contact** (page 5): Provide the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
* **Next of Kin** (page 6): Enter the name and contact information of your next of kin.
* **Medical Insurance** (page 7): Specify your medical insurance details.
* **Allergies** (page 8): List any allergies or sensitivities you may have.
* **Medical History Other** (page 9): Provide any additional medical history information not covered in previous fields.
* **Medications** (page 10): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Medications Other** (page 11): Provide any additional medication information not listed above.
* **Medical Procedure** (page 12): Describe any recent medical procedures you have undergone.
* **Surgical Procedure** (page 13): List any surgical procedures you have undergone.
* **Medical History Disease** (page 14): Specify any chronic diseases or conditions you have.
* **Medical History Injury** (page 15): Describe any recent injuries or accidents.
* **Medical History Hospitalization** (page 16): List any previous hospitalizations.
* **Medical History Travel** (page 17): Specify any travel history.
* **Medical History Work** (page 18): Describe any work-related medical history.
* **Medical History Other** (page 19): Provide any additional medical history information not listed above.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (page 20): Specify your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Patient Notes** (page 21): Add any comments or notes about your medical history.
* **Medical History Family History** (page 22): Describe any family medical history.
* **Medical History Social History** (page 23): Specify any social history.
* **Medical History Illness** (page 24): List any recent illnesses or conditions.
 

## Tips
* Please answer each question accurately and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any information, please consult your doctor or healthcare provider.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your eye center representative.
