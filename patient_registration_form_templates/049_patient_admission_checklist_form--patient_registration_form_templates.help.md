# patient admission checklist form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The patient admission checklist form is designed to collect essential information about a patient's demographic, medical history, and treatment details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately, ensuring that all required fields are completed.
2. If you are unsure about any field, please ask the medical staff for guidance.
3. Please note that some sections may require additional documentation or clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Date of Birth** (`patient_date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Email** (`patient_email`, email, optional): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Phone** (`patient_phone`, text, optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Address** (`patient_address`, text, optional): Enter the patient's address.
* **Medical History** (`patient_medical_history`, note, optional): Please note down the patient's medical history.
* **Allergies** (`patient_allergies`, select_multiple, optional): Check all the allergies that apply to the patient.
* **Medication** (`patient_medication`, select_one, optional): Select the medication the patient is currently taking.
* **Medical Conditions** (`patient_medical_conditions`, select_multiple, optional): Check all the medical conditions that apply to the patient.
* **Vaccination Record** (`patient_vaccination_record`, select_one, optional): Select the vaccination record that applies to the patient.
* **Surgical History** (`patient_surgical_history`, text, optional): Note down the patient's surgical history.
* **Family History** (`patient_family_history`, note, optional): Note down the patient's family history.
* **Family History Additional Info** (`patient_family_history_additional_info`, note, optional): Please add any additional information about the patient's family history.
* **Social History** (`patient_social_history`, text, optional): Note down the patient's social history.
* **Caregiver Name** (`patient_caregiver_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the patient's caregiver.
* **Caregiver Relationship** (`patient_caregiver_relationship`, select_one, optional): Select the relationship between the patient and their caregiver.
* **Language** (`patient_language`, select_one, optional): Select the language the patient is most comfortable with.
* **Next of Kin** (`patient_next_of_kin`, text, optional): Enter the name of the patient's next of kin.
* **Domicile** (`patient_domicile`, text, optional): Enter the patient's address.
* **Discharge Date** (`patient_discharge_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of discharge (if applicable).
* **Discharge Status** (`patient_discharge_status`, select_one, optional): Select the discharge status.
* **Payment Method** (`patient_payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select the payment method for the patient's treatment.
* **Additional Info** (`patient_additional_info`, text, optional): Please add any additional information about the patient.
* **Referral Source** (`patient_referral_source`, select_multiple, optional): Check all the referral sources that apply to the patient.
* **Referral Info** (`patient_referral_info`, text, optional): Note down the referral information.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask the medical staff for guidance.
* Some sections may require additional documentation or clarification.
* Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure the patient's information is thoroughly documented.
