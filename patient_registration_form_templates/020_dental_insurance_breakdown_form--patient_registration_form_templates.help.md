# dental_insurance_breakdown_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather detailed information about a patient's dental insurance coverage, procedures, and billing status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's information in the "patient_info" field.
2. Select the insurance coverage details in the "insurance_coverage" field.
3. Enter the procedure details in the "patient_procedure" field.
4. Enter the procedure cost in the "procedure_cost" field.
5. Select the billing provider in the "billing_provider" field.
6. Enter the billing date in the "billing_date" field.
7. Select the payment method in the "payment_method" field.
8. Enter the payment status in the "payment_status" field.
9. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`):
	+ Enter the patient's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Insurance Coverage** (`insurance_coverage`):
	+ Select the insurance company and policy number that the patient has.
* **Procedure** (`patient_procedure`):
	+ Enter the details of the dental procedure performed, including any relevant dates and times.
* **Procedure Cost** (`procedure_cost`):
	+ Enter the total cost of the procedure.
* **Billing Provider** (`billing_provider`):
	+ Select the healthcare provider who performed the billing services.
* **Billing Date** (`billing_date`):
	+ Enter the date when the billing was completed.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`):
	+ Select the method of payment used for the billing (e.g. Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover).
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`):
	+ Select the current status of the payment (e.g. Paid, Not paid, Pending).
* **Notes** (`notes`):
	+ Enter any additional comments or information about the billing process.
* **Comments** (`comments`):
	+ Enter any additional information or comments about the patient's insurance and billing details.
* **Insurance ID** (`insurance_id`):
	+ Enter the insurance company's ID number.
* **Policy Number** (`policy_number`):
	+ Enter the patient's insurance policy number.
* **Policy Holder** (`policy_holder`):
	+ Enter the name of the policy holder.
* **Policy Provider** (`policy_provider`):
	+ Enter the name of the insurance provider.
* **Policy Effective Date** (`policy_effective_date`):
	+ Enter the effective date of the policy.
* **Policy Expires Date** (`policy_expires_date`):
	+ Enter the expiration date of the policy.
* **Insurance Company** (`insurance_company`):
	+ Enter the name of the insurance company.
* **Insurance Plan** (`insurance_plan`):
	+ Enter the name of the insurance plan.
* **Insurance Deductible** (`insurance_deductible`):
	+ Enter the deductible amount.
* **Insurance Copay** (`insurance_copay`):
	+ Enter the copay amount.
* **Total Cost** (`total_cost`):
	+ Enter the total cost of the billing.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`):
	+ Enter the date of the payment.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`):
	+ Select the method of payment used for the billing (e.g. Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover).
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`):
	+ Select the current status of the payment (e.g. Paid, Not paid, Pending).
* **Comments** (`comments`):
	+ Enter any additional comments or information about the billing process.

# Tips
* Make sure to accurately fill out each field according to the patient's information and insurance coverage.
* Double-check the form for any errors or missing information before submitting.
* Use this form as a template to create and customize for your specific needs.
