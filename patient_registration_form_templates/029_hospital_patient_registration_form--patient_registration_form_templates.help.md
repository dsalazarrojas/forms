# Hospital Patient Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect patient information for registration and medical history at the hospital. The form is used to gather essential details about the patient, their contact and medical history, emergency contact, and any allergies they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please enter the patient's personal details in the **Patient Information** page.
2. Provide contact information for the patient in the **Contact Information** page.
3. Describe the patient's medical history in the **Medical History** page.
4. List the emergency contact person's information in the **Emergency Contact** page.
5. Enter the patient's medical details in the **Patient Details** page.
6. Provide any relevant medical information in the **Medical Information** page.
7. List any allergies the patient has in the **Allergies** page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (Page 1)**:
	+ **Patient Name** (text): Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
	+ **Date of Birth** (text): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format DD/MM/YY.
	+ **ID Number** (text): Enter the patient's identification number (e.g., ID card number).
* **Contact Information (Page 2)**:
	+ **Address** (text): Enter the patient's home address.
	+ **Phone Number** (text): Enter the patient's phone number.
	+ **Email** (text): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Medical History (Page 3)**:
	+ **Medical Condition/History** (text): Describe the patient's current medical condition or history.
* **Emergency Contact (Page 4)**:
	+ **Name** (text): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
	+ **Phone Number** (text): Enter the emergency contact person's phone number.
* **Patient Details (Page 5)**:
	+ **Height** (text): Enter the patient's height in centimeters.
	+ **Weight** (text): Enter the patient's weight in kilograms.
* **Medical Information (Page 6)**:
	+ **Previous Medical Conditions** (text): Describe any previous medical conditions or surgeries.
	+ **Current Medications** (text): List any current medications the patient is taking.
* **Allergies (Page 7)**:
	+ **Allergies** (text): List any allergies the patient has.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
- If you are unsure about any information, please consult with the patient's medical team.
- If you need assistance, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
