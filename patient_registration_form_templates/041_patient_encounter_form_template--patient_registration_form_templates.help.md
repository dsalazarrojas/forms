# Patient Encounter Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Encounter Form Template is used to gather information about a patient's medical encounter, including medical history, medications, allergies, medical conditions, and other relevant details. This form ensures that all necessary information is captured accurately and consistently for medical records.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form for each patient encounter.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed accurately.
3. Use the provided options or select "Yes" or "No" for medical history, allergies, and medical conditions.
4. Enter dates and times using the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY for dates and HH:MM for times).
5. Verify that the patient's contact information (phone, email, address) is up to date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Medical History**: Enter a brief description of the patient's medical history.
- **Meds**: Select all medications the patient is currently taking. You can choose from the provided options or type in a custom value.
- **Allergies**: List any allergies the patient has. You can choose from the provided options or type in a custom value.
- **Medical Condition**: Select the current medical condition from the provided options or type in a custom value.
- **Medical Notes**: Select "Yes" if the patient has any medical notes, or "No" otherwise.
- **Date**: Enter the date of the patient's encounter.
- **Time**: Enter the time of the patient's encounter.
- **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth.
- **Date of Visit**: Enter the date of the visit.
- **Patient Phone**: Enter the patient's contact phone number.
- **Patient Email**: Enter the patient's email address.
- **Patient Address**: Enter the patient's address. 

# Tips
- Be accurate and complete when filling out the form.
- Use the provided options whenever possible to avoid errors.
- Ensure that all fields are filled out before submitting the form.
- Review and update the form as necessary to reflect changes in the patient's condition or status.
