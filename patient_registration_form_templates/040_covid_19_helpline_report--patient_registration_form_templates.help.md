# COVID 19 Helpline Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about a patient's call to a helpline for COVID-19 concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "COVID 19 Helpline Report" form.
2. Review each section of the form carefully.
3. Answer each question accurately and thoroughly.
4. Submit the form once complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Call Date** (ID: 2, Required): Enter the date of the call in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Call Time** (ID: 3, Required): Enter the time of the call in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Helpline Operator Name** (ID: 4, Required): Enter your name as a helpline operator.
* **Call Duration (minutes)** (ID: 5, Required): Enter the duration of the call in minutes.
* **Caller Name** (ID: 7, Required): Enter the caller's name accurately.
* **Caller Age** (ID: 8, Required): Enter the caller's age accurately.
* **Caller Phone Number** (ID: 9, Required): Enter the caller's phone number accurately.
* **Caller Email Address** (ID: 10, Optional): Enter the caller's email address if available.
* **COVID-19 Diagnosis Status** (ID: 12, Required): Select the current status of the COVID-19 diagnosis.
* **Current Symptoms** (ID: 13, Required): Select all symptoms the caller is experiencing.
* **Duration of Symptoms** (ID: 14, Optional): Enter the duration of the symptoms.
* **Primary Health Concerns** (ID: 15, Required): Enter any primary health concerns the caller has.
* **Chronic Conditions** (ID: 17, Optional): Enter any chronic conditions the caller has.
* **Current Medications** (ID: 18, Optional): Enter any current medications the caller is taking.
* **Treatment or Advice Provided** (ID: 20, Required): Enter any treatment or advice provided to the caller.
* **Referral to Medical Facility** (ID: 21, Required): Select whether a referral to a medical facility is needed.
* **Referral Details** (ID: 22, Optional): Enter any referral details if necessary.
* **Follow-up Required** (ID: 23, Required): Select whether follow-up is required.
* **Follow-up Plan** (ID: 24, Optional): Enter any follow-up plan if necessary.
* **Additional Notes** (ID: 25, Optional): Enter any additional notes about the call.

Note: All fields with a "Required" label must be completed accurately to ensure proper documentation of the patient's call.
