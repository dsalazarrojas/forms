# Functional Analysis Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Functional Analysis Screening Form is used to gather information about a patient's functional status, including their ability to perform daily activities, manage their medication, and their overall health status. This form is designed to help healthcare professionals assess the patient's condition and make informed decisions about their care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide patient information, including their ID and any relevant medical history.
2. Answer each question carefully, selecting the best response for each field.
3. Enter any additional comments or concerns in the `Comments` field.
4. Enter the date and time of the last screening or assessment.
5. Indicate the screening status, whether the patient is active, discontinued, or another option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and any other identifying information.
* **Functional Status** (`Functional Status`): Select the patient's current functional status, whether they are active, inactive, or another option.
* **Bathing Ability** (`Bathing Ability`): Indicate the patient's ability to bathe, with options for minimal, moderate, or maximal assistance, or unable to bathe.
* **Eating Ability** (`Eating Ability`): Select the patient's ability to eat, with options for minimal, moderate, or maximal assistance, or unable to eat.
* **Dressing Ability** (`Dressing Ability`): Indicate the patient's ability to dress, with options for minimal, moderate, or maximal assistance, or unable to dress.
* **Personal Care** (`Personal Care`): Select the patient's ability to perform personal care tasks, including bathing, dressing, and grooming, with options for limited or unable to perform.
* **Mobility** (`Mobility`): Indicate the patient's mobility, with options for limited in bed, limited outside of bed, normal, or unable to walk.
* **Transferability** (`Transferability`): Select the patient's ability to transfer, with options for needs to be transferred, able to transfer, or unable to transfer.
* **Continence** (`Continence`): Indicate the patient's continence, with options for normal, limited, frequent, or incontinent.
* **Communication** (`Communication`): Select the patient's ability to communicate, with options for normal, limited, or no response.
* **Vision** (`Vision`): Indicate the patient's vision, with options for normal, limited, or blind.
* **Hearing** (`Hearing`): Select the patient's hearing, with options for normal, limited, or deaf.
* **Cognition** (`Cognition`): Indicate the patient's cognitive status, with options for normal, limited, confused, or unable to recognize people.
* **Emotional Status** (`Emotional Status`): Select the patient's emotional status, with options for normal, limited, depressed, or unable to cope.
* **Safety** (`Safety`): Indicate the patient's safety status, with options for safe or not safe.
* **Medication Management** (`Medication Management`): Enter the patient's current medication regimen, including dosage and frequency.
* **Medication Side Effects** (`Medication Side Effects`): Enter any adverse effects of the patient's medications, if applicable.
* **Pain Level** (`Pain Level`): Enter the patient's current pain level, on a scale of 1-10.
* **Overall Status** (`Overall Status`): Enter a brief summary of the patient's overall health status.
* **Comments** (`Comments`): Enter any additional comments or concerns about the patient.
* **Date Last Screening** (`Date Last Screening`): Enter the date of the last screening or assessment.
* **Time Last Screening** (`Time Last Screening`): Enter the time of the last screening or assessment.
* **Total Days in Care** (`Total Days in Care`): Enter the total number of days the patient has been in care.
* **Screening Status** (`Screening Status`): Indicate the patient's current screening status, whether active, discontinued, or another option.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If a patient's condition changes, update their screening status accordingly.
* Consult with the patient and their healthcare team to ensure that all fields are accurately completed.
