# Social Medical History Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a comprehensive survey that collects information about a patient's medical history, including their name, address, phone number, medical history, family history, and other relevant details. The purpose of this form is to help healthcare providers understand the patient's medical background and provide appropriate care.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: First Name and Last Name

* First Name: This field is for your first name, typically the name your parents gave you at birth.
* Last Name: This field is for your surname, the last name you were given at birth.

### Step 2: Birth Date

* Birth Date: This field is for your date of birth. Please format it as MM/DD/YYYY.

### Step 3: Address

* Address: This field is for your current address. Please include your street address, apartment number, and postal code.

### Step 4: Phone Number

* Phone Number: This field is for your primary phone number. Please format it as 10 digits.

### Step 5: Medical History

* Medical History: This field is for any previous medical conditions, surgeries, or hospitalizations you have experienced.

### Step 6: Family History

* Family History: This field is for any known medical conditions or history in your family.
* Options: Yes/No

### Step 7: Social History

* Social History: This field is for any social or behavioral issues that may impact your health, such as smoking or substance abuse.
* Options: Yes/No

### Step 8: Medical Conditions

* Medical Conditions: This field is for any medical conditions you have or have had in the past.
* Options: Yes/No

### Step 9: Medications

* Medications: This field is for any medications you are currently taking or have taken in the past.
* Options: Yes/No

### Step 10: Medical Procedures

* Medical Procedures: This field is for any medical procedures or surgeries you have undergone.
* Options: Yes/No

### Step 11: Allergies

* Allergies: This field is for any allergies or sensitivities you have.
* Options: Yes/No

### Step 12: Emergency Contact

* Emergency Contact: This field is for the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
* First Name: This is for the name of the emergency contact.
* Last Name: This is for the surname of the emergency contact.
* Phone Number: This is for the phone number of the emergency contact.

### Step 13: Emergency Phone Number

* Emergency Phone Number: This field is for your emergency contact's phone number.

### Step 14: Insurance Provider

* Insurance Provider: This field is for your current insurance provider.
* Insurance ID Number: This field is for your insurance ID number.
* Insurance Effective Date: This field is for your insurance effective date.
* Insurance Expiration Date: This field is for your insurance expiration date.

### Step 15: Emergency Contact Relation

* Emergency Contact Relation: This field is for your relation to the emergency contact.

### Step 16: Insurance Provider Relation

* Insurance Provider Relation: This field is for your relation to the insurance provider.

### Step 17: Medical History Notes

* Medical History Notes: This field is for any additional notes about your medical history.

### Step 18: Family History Notes

* Family History Notes: This field is for any additional notes about your family history.

### Step 19: Medical Conditions Notes

* Medical Conditions Notes: This field is for any additional notes about your medical conditions.

### Step 20: Medical Procedures Notes

* Medical Procedures Notes: This field is for any additional notes about your medical procedures.

### Step 21: Medications Notes

* Medications Notes: This field is for any additional notes about your medications.

## Tips

* Please answer all fields accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
* This form is designed to be completed once per patient, so please make sure to update your information only when necessary.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, please reach out to your healthcare provider.
