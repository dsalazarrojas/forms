# Returning Patient Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
<thinking>
This form is used for returning patients to provide updated information and history. It helps us to keep track of patient's medical history, allergies, medications, and other relevant details. Please ensure that you only submit this form if you are a returning patient. If you are a new patient, please use the new patient form.
</thinking>

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of birth" field. Please use the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. If you have medical insurance, select "True" or "False" in the "Medical insurance" field.
8. If you have medical insurance, enter your insurance provider in the "Medical insurance provider" field.
9. If you have medical insurance, enter your insurance ID number in the "Medical insurance ID" field.
10. Enter any emergency contact information, such as their name and phone number, in the "Emergency contact info" field.
11. Specify your relationship with the emergency contact in the "Emergency contact relationship" field.
12. Enter the phone number of your emergency contact in the "Emergency contact phone" field.
13. Enter any allergies you have in the "Allergies" field.
14. Enter your medical history in the "Medical history" field.
15. Enter any medications you are currently taking in the "Medication" field.
16. If you have medical history from a provider, enter their name in the "Medical history provider" field.
17. Enter any medical history you have, including any surgeries, in the "Medical history" field.
18. Enter the date of your medical history in the "Medical history date" field.
19. Enter any medical notes you have in the "Medical notes" field.
20. If you have medical history from a doctor, enter their name in the "Medical history doctor" field.
21. Enter the dosage of your medication in the "Medication dosage" field.
22. Enter any side effects of your medication in the "Medication side effects" field.
23. Enter any medical diagnosis you have received in the "Medical history diagnosis" field.
24. Specify your current patient status (Active, Inactive, or Suspended) in the "Patient status" field.
25. Enter any medical history you have related to surgery in the "Medical history surgery" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (Text, Required: False): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (Text, Required: False): Enter your last name.
* **Date of birth** (Date, Required: False): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address** (Text, Required: False): Enter your address.
* **Email** (Email, Required: False): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Text, Required: False): Enter your phone number.
* **Medical insurance** (Select one, Required: False): If you have medical insurance, select "True". Otherwise, select "False".
* **Medical insurance provider** (Text, Required: False): If you have medical insurance, enter your insurance provider name or company name.
* **Medical insurance ID** (Text, Required: False): If you have medical insurance, enter your insurance ID number.
* **Emergency contact info** (Text, Required: False): Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency contact relationship** (Text, Required: False): Specify your relationship with the emergency contact (e.g., parent, family member, etc.).
* **Emergency contact phone** (Text, Required: False): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Allergies** (Text, Required: False): Enter any allergies you have.
* **Medical history** (Text, Required: False): Enter any medical history you have, including any surgeries.
* **Medication** (Text, Required: False): Enter any medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical history provider** (Text, Required: False): If you have medical history from a provider, enter their name.
* **Medication history** (Text, Required: False): Enter any medical history you have related to medication.
* **Medical history date** (Date, Required: False): Enter the date of your medical history.
* **Medical notes** (Text, Required: False): Enter any medical notes or comments.
* **Medical history doctor** (Text, Required: False): If you have medical history from a doctor, enter their name.
* **Medication dosage** (Text, Required: False): Enter the dosage of your medication.
* **Medication side effects** (Text, Required: False): Enter any side effects of your medication.
* **Medical history diagnosis** (Text, Required: False): Enter any medical diagnosis you have received.
* **Patient status** (Select multiple, Required: False): Specify your current patient status (Active, Inactive, or Suspended).
* **Medical history surgery** (Text, Required: False): Enter any medical history you have related to surgery.

## Tips

Please ensure that you only submit this form if you are a returning patient. If you are a new patient, please use the new patient form.
