# Body Contouring Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients who are interested in body contouring treatments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, and gender.
2. Select your preferred appointment time.
3. Answer questions about your medical history, including any medical conditions, medications, allergies, and surgeries.
4. List any previous body contouring treatments you have had.
5. Select your goals for treatment, including what you hope to achieve and the areas of your body you are interested in treating.
6. Answer questions about your lifestyle, including your diet and exercise habits, smoking status, and alcohol consumption.
7. Finally, confirm that you understand your HIPAA rights and sign the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (1)**: Enter your first name in the field provided.
* **Last Name (2)**: Enter your last name in the field provided.
* **Email Address (3)**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number (4)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth (5)**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Gender (6)**: Select your gender from the options provided.
* **Height (7)**: Enter your height in feet and inches.
* **Current Weight (8)**: Enter your current weight in pounds.
* **Goal Weight (9)**: Enter your target weight if you have one. (This field is not required.)
* **Occupation (10)**: Enter a brief description of your occupation.
* **How Did You Hear About Us? (11)**: Select how you heard about us from the options provided.
* **Primary Goal (12)**: Select your primary goal from the options provided.
* **Treatment Areas of Interest (13)**: Select all areas of your body you are interested in treating.
* **Previous Body Contouring (14)**: Select if you have had previous body contouring treatments.
* **Previous Treatments List (15)**: List any previous body contouring treatments you have had.
* **Medical Conditions (16)**: List any medical conditions you have.
* **Current Medications (17)**: List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Allergies (18)**: List any allergies you have.
* **Surgical History (19)**: List any relevant surgeries you have had.
* **Pregnancy History (20)**: List the number of pregnancies you have had, if applicable.
* **Lifestyle Factors (21)**: Describe your diet and exercise habits.
* **Smoking Status (22)**: Select your smoking status from the options provided.
* **Alcohol Consumption (23)**: Select how often you consume alcohol from the options provided.
* **Preferred Appointment Time (24)**: Select your preferred appointment time from the options provided.
* **Special Requests (25)**: Enter any specific needs or requests you have.
* **HIPAA Acknowledgment (26)**: Confirm that you understand your HIPAA rights.
* **Client Signature (27)**: Sign your full name as confirmation of your identity.
* **Signature Date (28)**: Enter the date of intake in MM/DD/YYYY format.
