# Microdermabrasion Client Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients before undergoing microdermabrasion treatment. It includes general contact information, medical history, treatment preferences, and details about the expected treatment session. Completing this form will help our medical practitioners gather the necessary information for a safe and effective treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the form link to access the online version of the Microdermabrasion Client Intake Form.
2. Follow the instructions below for each field to complete the form accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: This field is for your legal name as listed on your identification documents. 
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for any follow-up questions or emergencies.
* **Address**: Enter your current address for contact and appointment purposes.
* **Medical History**: Select any known medical conditions you have, such as Allergies, Asthma, or High Blood Pressure.
* **Medications**: Select the type of medications you are currently taking, including Prescription, Non-Prescription, or Over the Counter.
* **Medical Practitioner**: Enter the name of your current medical practitioner, if applicable.
* **Medical Condition**: Select the medical condition you are currently treating, such as Skin Cancer or Skin Conditions.
* **Treatment Area**: Enter the specific area on your body where you would like to undergo microdermabrasion treatment.
* **Expected Treatment Date**: Enter the date you are expecting to receive microdermabrasion treatment.
* **Microdermabrasion Frequency**: Enter the number of microdermabrasion sessions you would like to have per week/month.
* **Microdermabrasion Downtime**: Enter the amount of time you need to take off work/school after microdermabrasion treatment.
* **Note**: Enter any additional information or concerns you would like to share with your medical practitioner.
