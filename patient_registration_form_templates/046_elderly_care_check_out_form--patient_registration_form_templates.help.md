# Elderly Care Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Elderly Care Check Out Form is used to document the details of a patient's care during their stay in a facility. This information will be reviewed by healthcare professionals to ensure continuity of care and to provide accurate information to the patient's next care provider.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the patient's name.
2. Enter the date of admission and discharge.
3. Provide the patient's contact information and emergency contact details.
4. Select the patient's status (e.g., stable, recovering, or unstable).
5. List any medication allergies or medical conditions the patient has.
6. Write down the discharge instructions for the patient's care.
7. Sign the form to confirm completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification.
* **Date Of Admission** (`date_of_admission`, date, required): Enter the date the patient was admitted to the facility.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Provide the patient's contact information, such as phone numbers and addresses.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of the patient's emergency contact.
* **Date Of Discharge** (`date_of_discharge`, date, required): Enter the date the patient is being discharged from the facility.
* **Patient Status** (`patient_status`, select_one, optional): Select the patient's status from the provided options (e.g., stable, recovering, or unstable).
* **Medication Allergies** (`medication_allergies`, text, optional): List any medication allergies or sensitivities the patient has.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): List any medical conditions the patient has that require attention.
* **Discharge Instructions** (`discharge_instructions`, text, optional): Write down the instructions for the patient's care after discharge.
* **Caregiver Name** (`caregiver_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the caregiver or responsible party.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Sign the form to confirm completion and verification.

## Tips
* Double-check the accuracy of all information entered before submitting the form.
* Ensure you have the necessary permissions and approvals from the patient's family or guardians if applicable.
* Review and update the form as needed based on the patient's changing condition or care requirements.
