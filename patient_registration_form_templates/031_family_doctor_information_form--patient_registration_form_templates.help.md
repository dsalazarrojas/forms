# Family Doctor Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Doctor Information Form is used to collect and update information about a patient's family doctor, including their contact information, medical specialty, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the patient's name.
2. Enter the patient's date of birth.
3. Provide the patient's address.
4. Enter the patient's contact number.
5. Enter the patient's email address.
6. Enter the family doctor's name.
7. Select the doctor's specialty.
8. Enter the hospital name (if applicable).
9. Add any additional notes (if necessary).
10. Select if the doctor is assigned to the patient.
11. Enter the patient's ID.
12. Enter the family doctor's ID.
13. Select the medical specialty of the doctor.
14. Enter the doctor's ID (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (text, required): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Date of Birth** (date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth in the correct format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Patient Address** (text, optional): Enter the patient's home or mailing address.
* **Contact Number** (text, optional): Enter the patient's contact phone number.
* **Email Address** (email, optional): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Family Doctor Name** (text, optional): Enter the family doctor's name.
* **Doctor Specialty** (select one, optional): Select the doctor's specialty (e.g., primary care, specialist, etc.).
* **Hospital Name** (text, optional): Enter the hospital name (if applicable).
* **Note** (note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the patient's medical history.
* **Assigned Doctor** (select one, optional): Select if the doctor is assigned to the patient.
* **Patient ID** (number, optional): Enter the patient's ID number.
* **Family Doctor ID** (number, optional): Enter the family doctor's ID number.
* **Medical Specialty** (select multiple, optional): Select the medical specialty of the doctor (e.g., primary care, specialist, etc.).
* **Doctor ID** (number, optional): Enter the doctor's ID number.
