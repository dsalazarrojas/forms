# COVID 19 Booster Shot Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for patients to register for the COVID-19 booster shot. The form will collect relevant information to ensure the patient receives the correct vaccine and medical guidance. Please ensure you have reviewed the form carefully to provide accurate and up-to-date information.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Patient Information

*   **Patient Name**: Fill in your full legal name.
*   **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
*   **Email Address**: Enter your email address for confirmation purposes.
*   **Phone Number**: Provide your best contact number.

### Step 2: Contact Information

*   **Street Address**: Enter your complete address.
*   **City**: Enter the city of your residence.
*   **State**: Enter the state or province of your residence.
*   **ZIP Code**: Enter your postal code.

### Step 3: Insurance and Medical History

*   **Insurance Provider**: If you have insurance, enter the name of your insurance company.
*   **Insurance ID Number**: If applicable, enter your member ID number.
*   **Primary Care Physician**: If applicable, enter your primary care physician's name.
*   **Physician Phone**: If applicable, enter your doctor's contact number.

### Step 4: Vaccination History

*   **Initial Vaccine Brand**: If you received the vaccine before, select the brand that matches what you received.
*   **First Dose Date**: Enter the date of your first vaccination.
*   **Second Dose Date**: Enter the date of your second vaccination, if applicable.
*   **Previous Vaccination Site**: If applicable, enter where you received the previous vaccination.
*   **Booster Preference**: Select the vaccine brand you would prefer for your booster shot.

### Step 5: Eligibility and Medical Conditions

*   **Eligibility Category**: Select your eligibility category for the booster shot.
*   **Underlying Medical Conditions**: If you have any underlying medical conditions, list them here.
*   **Current Symptoms**: Select whether you have any current COVID-19 symptoms.
*   **Recent COVID-19 Infection**: Select whether you have had a recent COVID-19 infection within the past 3 months.
*   **Severe Allergies**: Select whether you have any severe allergies, especially to vaccines or other medications.
*   **Allergy Details**: If you have severe allergies, describe them in detail.
*   **Previous Vaccine Reaction**: Select whether you experienced any previous severe reaction to a vaccine.

### Step 6: Medical Status

*   **Pregnancy Status**: Select your current pregnancy status.
*   **Immunocompromised Status**: Select whether you have a weakened immune system.
*   **Medical Conditions**: If you have a weakened immune system, list the conditions causing it.
*   **Booster Preference**: If you have a weakened immune system, select your preferred vaccine brand.

### Step 7: Appointment and Language Preference

*   **Preferred Appointment Date**: Select the date you would like to schedule your appointment.
*   **Preferred Appointment Time**: Select your preferred appointment time.
*   **Transportation Assistance**: Select whether you need assistance with transportation.
*   **Language Preference**: Select the language you would prefer for communication.

### Step 8: Consent and Signature

*   **Consent to Vaccination**: Select whether you consent to receiving the booster shot.
*   **Patient Signature**: Sign your full name.
*   **Parent or Guardian Signature**: If you are under 18, sign your full name with a parent or guardian's signature.
*   **Date Signed**: Enter the date you signed the form.

Note: This form is for registration purposes only. The information you provide will be used to ensure the correct vaccine is administered and to provide medical guidance. Your consent is required to receive the booster shot.
