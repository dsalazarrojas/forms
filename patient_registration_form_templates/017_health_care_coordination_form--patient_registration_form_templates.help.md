# health_care_coordination_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assist healthcare professionals in coordinating patient care by gathering essential patient information and medical history. It ensures that all relevant details are captured to provide comprehensive care.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review each section carefully and ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
* Select the correct options for each field from the provided dropdown menus.
* Enter any additional information that is not listed in the provided dropdown menus.
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name (`basic_information`, select_one, required: false):** Please select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the patient's name is provided or not.
* **Date of Birth (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false):** Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Contact Information (`contact_info`, text, required: false):** Enter any relevant contact information for the patient.
* **Address (`address`, select_multiple, required: false):** Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the patient has a valid address.
* **Medical Conditions (`medical_conditions`, select_multiple, required: false):** Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the patient has any medical conditions.
* **Medications (`medications`, select_multiple, required: false):** Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the patient is taking any medications.
* **Allergies (`allergies`, select_multiple, required: false):** Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the patient has any allergies.
* **Medical History (`medical_history`, text, required: false):** Enter a brief medical history of the patient.
* **Insurance Info (`insurance_info`, select_multiple, required: false):** Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the patient has insurance information provided.
* **Emergency Contact (`emergency_contact`, select_multiple, required: false):** Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the patient has an emergency contact.
* **Next of Contact (`next_of_contact`, text, required: false):** Enter any information about the patient's next of contact.
* **Medical Provider (`medical_provider`, select_one, required: false):** Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether a medical provider is assigned.
* **Medical Specialty (`medical_specialty`, select_one, required: false):** Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the medical specialty of the provider.
* **Medical Provider Address (`medical_provider_address`, text, required: false):** Enter the address of the medical provider.
* **Medical Provider Phone (`medical_provider_phone`, text, required: false):** Enter the phone number of the medical provider.
* **Medical Provider Email (`medical_provider_email`, email, required: false):** Enter the email address of the medical provider.
* **Medical Provider Notes (`medical_provider_notes`, text, required: false):** Enter any additional notes about the medical provider.
* **Patient Signature (`patient_signature`, note, required: false):** The patient is asked to sign their medical history.
* **Medical Signature (`medical_signature`, note, required: false):** The medical provider is asked to sign the medical history.
* **Medical Notes (`medical_notes`, text, required: false):** Enter any additional medical notes for the patient.
* **Next of Contact Note (`next_of_contact_note`, text, required: false):** Enter any additional next of contact notes.
* **Medical Provider Signature (`medical_provider_signature`, note, required: false):** The medical provider is asked to sign their part of the medical history.
* **Next of Contact Signature (`next_of_contact_signature`, note, required: false):** The next of contact is asked to sign their part of the medical history.
* **Medical Provider Address Signature (`medical_provider_address_signature`, note, required: false):** The medical provider is asked to sign their address.
* **Medical Provider Phone Signature (`medical_provider_phone_signature`, note, required: false):** The medical provider is asked to sign their phone number.
