# first_steps_ed_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The First Steps ED Referral Form is a comprehensive referral form used to gather essential patient information and details about their medical referral. This form helps healthcare providers to efficiently and accurately collect patient data, ensuring that referrals are made with the necessary details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **First Steps ED Referral Form** (Page 1): Enter the basic information about the referral.
2. **Patient Information** (Page 2): Enter the patient's details, including their email and contact number.
3. **Referral Information** (Page 3-5): Provide the referral details, including the referral date, time, reason, contact information, and additional notes.
4. **Referral Additional Info** (Page 6): Enter any additional information about the referral.
5. **Referral Signature** (Page 7): Sign and date the referral form to confirm it has been reviewed and approved.
