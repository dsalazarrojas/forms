# Nutrition Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nutrition Consultation Form is designed for patients to provide information about their nutritional needs and goals. This form will be used by healthcare professionals to assess and address these needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form accurately and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. If you have any pre-existing medical conditions or are currently undergoing treatment, please indicate this in the "medical_conditions" field.
3. Select all applicable goals for your nutrition consultation, such as "healthy eating", "weight loss", or "muscle gains", in the "nutrition_goal" field. You may select multiple options.
4. Indicate your preferred medical specialty for the consultation, such as "dietician", "nutritionist", or "doctor".
5. Provide the date and time of your appointment for the consultation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information (patient_info)**: This field is a text box where you can provide any relevant information about yourself, such as your name, age, and contact details.
* **Patient Weight (patient_weight)**: Please enter your current weight in kg.
* **Medical Conditions (medical_conditions)**: Select any medical conditions you are currently experiencing, such as "diabetes", "high cholesterol", or "obesity".
* **Nutrition Goal (nutrition_goal)**: Select all applicable goals for your nutrition consultation, such as "healthy eating", "weight loss", or "muscle gains".
* **Medical Specialty (medical_specialty)**: Indicate your preferred medical specialty for the consultation, such as "dietician", "nutritionist", or "doctor".
* **Appointment Date (appointment_date)**: Provide the date of your appointment.
* **Appointment Time (time_of_day)**: Provide the time of your appointment.
* **Ongoing Needs (ongoing_needs)**: This field is for any additional comments or information you would like to provide about your ongoing needs or concerns.
