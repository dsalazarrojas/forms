# Tissue Bank Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tissue Bank Registration Form is used to onboard new patients into the tissue bank and obtain their consent for collecting biological samples. This form helps us understand your demographic information, medical history, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "first_name" and "last_name" fields with your name.
2. Enter your valid email address in the "email" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "phone" field.
4. Briefly describe your medical history in the "Medical History" field.
5. Expand on your medical history in the "Medical History Text Area" field.
6. Indicate your consent for collecting biological samples by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "consent" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **first_name** (first_name, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it is written on your official documents.
- **last_name** (last_name, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it is written on your official documents.
- **email** (email, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
- **phone** (phone, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
- **Medical History** (Medical History, note, required/optional): Briefly describe your medical history, including any relevant health conditions or allergies.
- **Medical History Text Area** (Medical History Text Area, note, required/optional): Expand on your medical history, including any details you feel are important for us to know.
- **consent** (consent, select multiple, required/optional): Indicate your consent for collecting biological samples by selecting "Yes" or "No".
