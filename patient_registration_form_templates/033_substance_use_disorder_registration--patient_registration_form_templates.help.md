# Substance Use Disorder Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from patients about their substance use disorder status, medical history, and treatment goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Please answer all the questions honestly to the best of your ability.
2.  Enter the required information in the corresponding fields.
3.  Use the available help text to understand each field's purpose.
4.  If you need help with any section, do not hesitate to ask your healthcare professional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Individual Details
• **Individual Details**: Please provide your basic contact information and other relevant details about yourself.
    (Required: false)

### Substance Use Status
• **Substance Use Status**: Please select your current substance use status from the options provided.
    (Options: Active, Inactive, Required: false)

### Intake Questions
• **Intake Questions**: This is a text field where you can provide any additional information you think is important for your treatment.
    (Required: false)

### Next Steps
• **Next Steps**: Please select the next date for your next appointment or treatment.
    (Options: , Required: false)

### Additional Info
• **Additional Info**: Please provide any further information you think is relevant to your treatment.
    (Required: false)

### Medical History
• **Medical History**: Please provide your medical history, including any medical conditions, allergies, or previous medical interventions.
    (Required: false)

### Mental Health History
• **Mental Health History**: Please provide your mental health history, including any mental health conditions, therapies, or interventions.
    (Required: false)

### Treatment Goals
• **Treatment Goals**: Please select all the goals you are currently pursuing or plan to pursue during treatment.
    (Options: Yes, No, Required: false)

## Tips
- Please answer the questions honestly to the best of your ability.
- If you need help with any section, do not hesitate to ask your healthcare professional.
- This form is intended to support your treatment plan, not to diagnose or treat you.
- Please note that your information will be kept confidential and used only for your treatment.
