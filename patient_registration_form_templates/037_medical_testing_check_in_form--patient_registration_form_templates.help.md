# Medical Testing Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Testing Check In Form is designed to collect information from patients before they undergo medical testing. This form is used to gather essential data about the patient's medical history, test information, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's information, including their name, date of birth, and ID number (if applicable).
2. Provide a brief medical history, including any relevant health conditions or allergies.
3. Enter the test information, including the type of test and any relevant details.
4. Add the patient's contact information, including their address, phone number, and emergency contact (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info**: Enter the patient's full name, date of birth, and ID number (if applicable).
* **Medical History**: Describe the patient's medical history, including any health conditions, allergies, or previous surgeries.
* **Test Info**: Enter the type of test and any relevant details, such as the date and time of the test.
* **Patient Contact**: Enter the patient's address, phone number, and emergency contact (if applicable).
* **Submit**: Click the submit button to complete and review the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate and complete patient information.
* If you are unsure about any information, ask a medical professional for guidance.
* Double-check the form for errors before submitting it.
