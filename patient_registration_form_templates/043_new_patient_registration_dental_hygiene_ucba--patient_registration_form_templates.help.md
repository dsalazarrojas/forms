# new_patient_registration_dental_hygiene_ucba - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from patients seeking dental hygiene services. It helps dental professionals understand the patient's medical and dental history, allergies, medications, and contact details, ensuring a comprehensive and safe registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Full Name** in the "Full Name" field.
2. Describe your **Medical History** in the "Medical History" field.
3. Provide your **Contact Details** in the "Contact Details" field.
4. List your **Dental History** in the "Dental History" field.
5. List your **Allergies** in the "Allergies" field.
6. List your **Medications** in the "Medications" field.
7. Provide additional **Patient Information** in the "Patient Info" field.
8. Describe your **Medical History 2** in the "Medical History 2" field.
9. Provide your **Dental History 2** in the "Dental History 2" field.
10. List your **Medications 2** in the "Medications 2" field.
11. Provide additional **Medical Information** in the "Medical Info" field.
12. List your **Allergies 2** in the "Allergies 2" field.
13. Submit your registration by selecting "Submit".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (`general_info`, text, required): Please provide your full name.
- **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): Describe your medical history.
- **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required): Provide your contact details.
- **Dental History** (`dental_history`, text, required): List your dental history.
- **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, required): List your allergies.
- **Medications** (`medications`, text, required): List your medications.
- **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, required): Provide additional patient information.
- **Medical History 2** (`medical_history_2`, text, required): Describe your medical history.
- **Dental History 2** (`dental_history_2`, text, required): Provide your dental history.
- **Medications 2** (`medications_2`, text, required): List your medications.
- **Medical Info** (`medical_info`, text, required): Provide additional medical information.
- **Allergies 2** (`allergies_2`, text, required): List your allergies.
- **Submit** (`submit`, radio, required): Please submit your registration.

## Tips
- Please fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the dental practice.
- The submitted form will be reviewed by dental professionals to ensure a safe and effective registration process.
