# VestibularRehabilitationRegistration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect relevant patient information for vestibular rehabilitation treatment planning and coordination.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for each field.
2. If a field is optional, fill it in if you have relevant information.
3. Be accurate and truthful when providing your medical history and information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Registration** (patient_registration): This field is the first page of the form and asks for the patient's registration information.
* **Medical History** (medical_history): Describe your medical history, including any previous injuries, illnesses, or conditions that may be relevant to your treatment.
* **Symptoms** (symptoms): List your current symptoms related to vestibular rehabilitation.
* **Medical Profession** (medical_profession): Select your medical profession or specialist.
* **Date of Birth** (date_of_birth): Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Time of Admission** (time_of_admission): Enter the time you were admitted for treatment.

## Tips
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your healthcare professional for guidance.
* If you have any medical documentation or records, please bring them with you to the appointment.
* Be as detailed as possible when providing your medical history and symptoms.
* If you need assistance, do not hesitate to contact your healthcare provider.
