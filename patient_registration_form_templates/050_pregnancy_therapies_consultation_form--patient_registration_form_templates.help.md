# Pregnancy Therapies Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pregnancy Therapies Consultation Form is designed to gather personal, contact, and pregnancy information from pregnant women for medical consultations. This information will help healthcare providers better understand the patient's needs and provide accurate advice.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the personal information page (page 1) with your details, including any relevant medical history or allergies.
2. On the contact information page (page 2), provide your contact details, including your name, phone number, and email.
3. On the pregnancy information page (page 3), fill out the form with information about your pregnancy, including any relevant medical conditions or concerns.
4. Finally, on the consent page (page 4), indicate your agreement to share the provided information with healthcare providers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Personal Info** (Page 1, text, required/optional): This page is for you to provide your personal details, including any relevant medical history or allergies. Please be as accurate as possible.
- **Contact Info** (Page 2, text, required/optional): This page is for you to provide your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email. This will help healthcare providers contact you if needed.
- **Pregnancy Info** (Page 3, text, required/optional): This page is for you to provide information about your pregnancy, including any relevant medical conditions or concerns. Be as specific as possible to help healthcare providers understand your needs.
- **Consent** (Page 4, text, required/optional): This page is for you to indicate your agreement to share the provided information with healthcare providers. Please review the terms of sharing carefully before signing.

## Tips
* Please be as accurate as possible when filling out the form.
* If you're unsure about any information or have questions, please contact the healthcare provider directly.
* This form is intended for medical consultations only. Please do not use it for other purposes.
