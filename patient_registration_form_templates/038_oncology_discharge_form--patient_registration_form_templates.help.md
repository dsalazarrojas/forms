# Oncology Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Oncology Discharge Form is a tool for healthcare professionals to gather necessary information about patients undergoing oncology treatment or care. This form helps to identify and document key details regarding the patient's condition, treatment, and care plan.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the general information section with the patient's demographics and contact information.
2.  In the patient info section, provide details about the patient's medical condition, treatment plan, and any relevant medical history.
3.  List any allergies or medical concerns the patient may have in the allergies section.
4.  In the medication list, document any medications the patient is currently taking or has taken in the past.
5.  Add any relevant lab results or test information in the lab results section.
6.  Record any additional medical notes or comments in the medical notes section.
