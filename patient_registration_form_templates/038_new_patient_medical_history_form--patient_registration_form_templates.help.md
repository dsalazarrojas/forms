# New Patient Medical History Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects medical history and contact information of new patients.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill up the form accurately and completely with your personal details.
- Select "Other" as emergency contact if you do not have anyone listed in the emergency contact list.
- You can add or remove emergency contacts as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your given name (e.g., John) (<name>, <type>: text, required: false)
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname (e.g., Doe) (<name>, <type>: text, required: false)
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth (mm/dd/yyyy) (<name>, <type>: date, required: false)
* **Email**: Enter your contact email (<name>, <type>: email, required: false)
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number (<name>, <type>: text, required: false)
* **Medical History**: Describe your past medical history (e.g., allergies, medical conditions, previous operations) (<name>, <type>: note, required: false)
* **Consent and Terms and Conditions**: Agree to our terms and conditions, we will use your information for medical care (<name>, <type>: note, required: false)
* **Emergency Contact**: Select at least one emergency contact from the list (<name>, <type>: select_multiple, options: John Doe, Jane Doe, Other, required: false)
* **Relationship**: Describe your relationship with your emergency contact (e.g., mother, father, brother) (<name>, <type>: text, required: false)
* **Phone**: Enter your emergency contact phone number (<name>, <type>: text, required: false)

Please note that all fields are optional unless otherwise stated.
