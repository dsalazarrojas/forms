# Healthcare Access Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Healthcare Access Assessment Form is a tool used by healthcare providers to collect information about patients' access to medical services and any potential barriers to accessing those services. This form helps healthcare providers understand patients' needs and experiences, enabling them to provide more effective care and support.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please ensure that you have the patient's consent before completing this form.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability based on the patient's information.
3. Be sure to review the patient's chart for previous assessments before completing this form.
4. Complete this form accurately and thoroughly, as the information collected will be used to inform patient care and services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter the patient's contact number, if available.
* **Medical Services** (`medical_services`, select_multiple, optional): Check all applicable medical services that the patient has accessed or would like to access. This field will help healthcare providers understand the patient's needs and preferences.
* **Access Barriers** (`access_barriers`, select_one, optional): Check "Yes" if the patient experiences any barriers to accessing medical services. This field will help healthcare providers understand the patient's challenges and provide targeted support.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional information or comments about the patient's access to medical services. This field is an opportunity for you to note any special circumstances or concerns that may be relevant to the patient's care.
