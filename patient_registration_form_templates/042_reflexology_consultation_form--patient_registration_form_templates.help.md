# Reflexology Consultation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Reflexology Consultation Form is designed to collect patient information and medical history for reflexology consultations. This form helps ensure that you have a clear understanding of a patient's health status, medical background, and treatment preferences before providing reflexology services. 

## How To Complete This Form

### Please answer the questions thoroughly and accurately, and choose one or multiple relevant options for each field as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Patient Registration Form** (`patient_registration_form`, `text`, required: false)
* Please provide your name and contact information, including your email address and phone number.
* This will help us to contact you for further information or to schedule follow-up appointments.
### **Health Problems** (`health_problems`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
* Select all the health problems that you are currently experiencing, from the list of options provided.
* This will help us understand your medical history and tailor the reflexology treatment plan to your specific needs.
### **Treat Tips** (`treat_tips`, `text`, required: false)
* Provide any additional information about your medical background or allergies that may be relevant to your treatment.
* This will help us to take necessary precautions and provide safe and effective reflexology services.
### **Medical Background** (`medical_background`, `date`, required: false)
* Please provide your date of birth, medical history, or any other relevant medical information.
* This will help us to understand your medical history and provide informed consent for treatment.
### **Reflexology Treatment** (`reflexology_treatment`, `select_one`, required: false)
* Select the type of reflexology treatment that you would like to receive, from the options provided.
* This will help us to determine the most effective treatment plan for your needs.

### Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any question or option, please ask your reflexology practitioner for clarification.
* This form is designed to be a starting point for discussion and treatment planning, not a replacement for professional medical advice.
