# Dialysis Patient Discharge Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to capture essential patient information for discharge from dialysis treatment. It ensures that the patient's vital signs, medical history, and treatment information are documented accurately and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the patient's Medical Record Number to ensure accurate tracking and identification.
2. Provide the patient's Last Name, Given Name, and Date of Birth as recorded in their medical record.
3. Enter the patient's Blood Pressure, Pulse, and Oxygen Saturation readings from the latest clinical assessment.
4. List any known allergies or allergic reactions the patient may experience.
5. Indicate the patient's current medications, including Blood Thinners, Insulin, or Other as per medical history.
6. Specify the patient's medical history as per their condition (Cardiac, Pulmonary, Renal, or Other).
7. Include a note for the patient's signature to acknowledge the form completion.
8. Enter the name of the healthcare provider responsible for the patient's treatment.
9. (Optional) Enter the dialyser model number if relevant.
10. (Optional) Record the patient's weight if necessary for further treatment.
11. Document the date and time of discharge.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical Record Number (1)**: Enter the patient's unique identification number for accurate tracking and identification.
* **Patient Last Name (2)**: Enter the patient's last name as recorded in their medical history.
* **Patient Given Name (3)**: Enter the patient's first name or given name as recorded in their medical history.
* **Blood Pressure (4)**: Record the patient's latest blood pressure reading in mmHg.
* **Pulse (5)**: Record the patient's latest pulse reading.
* **Oxygen Saturation (6)**: Record the patient's latest oxygen saturation reading (%).
* **Medication Allergies (7)**: List any known allergies or allergic reactions the patient may experience, selecting Allergie or Allergic Reaction.
* **Current Medications (8)**: Indicate the patient's current medications, selecting Blood Thinners, Insulin, or Other as per medical history.
* **Medical History (9)**: Specify the patient's medical history, selecting Cardiac, Pulmonary, Renal, or Other.
* **Patient Signature (10)**: Include a note for the patient's signature to acknowledge form completion.
* **Date of Birth (11)**: Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Healthcare Provider (12)**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider responsible for the patient's treatment.
* **Dialyser Model Number (13)**: (Optional) Enter the dialyser model number if relevant.
* **Patient Weight (14)**: (Optional) Record the patient's weight if necessary for further treatment.
* **Discharge Date (15)**: Document the date of discharge.
* **Discharge Time (16)**: (Optional) Record the discharge time if necessary for further documentation.

**Tips:**

* Ensure accuracy and up-to-date information in each field to maintain accurate patient records.
* Verify the patient's medical history and allergies with the patient or their healthcare provider.
* Document any necessary instructions or follow-up actions for the patient post-discharge.
