# Speech/Language Pathology Progress Note - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Speech/Language Pathology Progress Note form is used to record and track the progress of patients undergoing speech-language therapy. This form helps healthcare professionals to monitor patient data, set goals, and adjust treatment plans as needed.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form for each therapy session, using the following fields:
	* Therapy Date: The date of the current therapy session.
	* Patient Name: The name of the patient receiving therapy.
	* Diagnoses: Check the diagnoses that apply to the patient, such as idiopathic stuttering or fluency disorder.
	* Therapy Goals: Briefly describe the goals of the current therapy session.
	* Therapy Frequency: Record the frequency of therapy sessions in a number.
	* Assessment Frequency: Record how often the patient is assessed in a number.
	* Treatment Frequency: Record the frequency of treatment sessions in a number.
	* Next Session: Schedule the next therapy session date.
	* Patient Notes: Record any notes or comments about the patient's progress or concerns.
	* Provider Notes: Record any notes or comments from the healthcare provider.
	* Assessment Notes: Record any notes or comments about the assessment process.
	* Treatment Notes: Record any notes or comments about the treatment process.
	* Speech Level: Record the level of speech proficiency in a number.
	* Language Level: Record the level of language proficiency in a number.
	* Fluency Level: Record the level of fluency in a number.
	* Rate Level: Record the rate of speech in a number.
	* Voice Quality: Record the quality of the patient's voice.
	* Speech Articulation: Record the level of speech articulation.
	* Prosody: Record the level of prosody.
	* Fluency: Record the level of fluency.
	* Language Production: Record any notes or comments about language production.
	* Speech Clarity: Record the clarity of the patient's speech.
	* Voice Clarity: Record the clarity of the patient's voice.
	* Prosody Clarity: Record the clarity of the prosody.
	* Speech Intensity: Record the intensity of the patient's speech.

## Tips

* Be consistent in your notation and scoring.
* Keep the notes concise and clear.
* Review and update the form regularly to track progress over time.
* Use the provided fields to tailor treatment plans to the individual needs of each patient.
