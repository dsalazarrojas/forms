# dietitian_patient_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The dietitian_patient_questionnaire form is used to collect information from patients about their medical history, contact details, and nutrition habits. This information will help dietitians understand their patients' needs and provide better care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the fields on page 1, "Patient Info", providing your patient's basic information.
2. Proceed to page 2, "Contact Info", and fill out the fields with your patient's contact details.
3. Continue to page 3, "Medical History", and describe your patient's medical history.
4. Finally, go to page 4, "Nutrition Habits", and provide information about your patient's diet and eating habits.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (Patient Info, text, optional): This section is for providing basic information about your patient, such as their name, age, and contact information.
* **Contact Info** (Contact Info, text, optional): This section is for providing your patient's contact details, such as their address, phone number, and email.
* **Medical History** (Medical History, text, optional): Describe your patient's medical history, including any previous illnesses or conditions that may be relevant to their dietitian consultation.
* **Nutrition Habits** (Nutrition Habits, text, optional): Describe your patient's nutrition habits, including their diet, eating patterns, and any relevant dietary restrictions.
