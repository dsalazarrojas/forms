# telehealth_patient_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect basic patient registration information for telehealth services. It will be used to create or update patient records and facilitate communication with the patient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter your date of birth.
3. Provide your phone number.
4. Enter your email address.
5. If applicable, describe your medical condition or reason for visiting the telehealth service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name (text)**: Enter your full name as you would like it to appear in your patient record.
* **Date of Birth (date)**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Phone Number (text)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Medical Condition (text)**: If you have a specific medical condition or reason for visiting the telehealth service, enter a brief description here. This information will help the service provider better understand your needs.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the information entered is accurate and up-to-date.
- If any of the fields are required, an asterisk (*) will be displayed next to the label.
- If you have any issues or questions while completing this form, contact the telehealth service provider for assistance.
