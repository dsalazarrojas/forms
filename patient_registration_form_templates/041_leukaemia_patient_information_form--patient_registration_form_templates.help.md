# Leukaemia Patient Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Leukaemia Patient Information Form is designed to collect information about patients with leukaemia, a type of cancer that affects the blood and bone marrow. This form is used to gather essential details about the patient's personal and medical history, contact information, and ongoing care requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Answer the questions in each section, using the options provided.
* Select "Yes" or "No" for questions that require a binary response.
* Enter your contact information in the "Contact Details" field.
* Use the "Leukaemia Details" field to select the specific type of leukaemia you are experiencing.
* Use the "Ongoing Care" field to report the frequency of your ongoing care.
* Answer the questions about your medical history, including the presence or absence of certain diseases.
* Report your blood test result, if applicable.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Details**: Choose "Yes" if you have leukaemia. If you do not have leukaemia, select "No".
* **Contact Details**: Enter your name, email address, and phone number. Please use a format that is easy to read (e.g., "John Smith, 123 Main St, 555-1234").
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Leukaemia Details**: Select the type of leukaemia you are experiencing from the list.
* **Ongoing Care**: Choose the frequency of your ongoing care:
	+ Less than 1 month
	+ 1 month or more
	+ Every 2-3 months
	+ Less than 6 months
	+ More than 6 months
* **Medical History**: Answer the question about whether you have a history of disease "Yes" or "No".
* **Blood Test Result**: Report the result of your blood test, if applicable:
	+ Positive
	+ Negative
	+ Inconclusive
* **Medical History - Disease**: Answer the question about whether you have a history of disease "Yes" or "No".

## Tips
* Please answer all questions accurately and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any question, you can ask for clarification.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
