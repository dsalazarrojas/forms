# patient_extensive_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect patient information for medical records, including demographics, medical history, allergies, medications, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the patient's demographics, such as gender and address.
2. Indicate any medical history, including any allergies.
3. List any medications the patient is currently taking.
4. Provide information about the patient's emergency contact.
5. Add any additional insurance information.
6. Enter details about the patient's next of kin.
7. Add any medical notes.
8. Choose the type of medication administration (if applicable).
9. Provide a signature from the patient and the doctor.
10. Enter the date and time of the consultation.
11. Enter the name and details of the medical provider.
12. Provide a second patient signature (if required).
13. Enter the name and details of the second medical provider.
14. Enter the date and time of the second consultation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main page** (`1`, `text`, optional): Start by entering the main page information.
* **Demographics** (`2`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the patient's gender.
* **Address** (`3`, `text`, optional): Enter the patient’s address.
* **Phone number** (`4`, `text`, optional): Enter the patient’s phone number.
* **Medical history** (`5`, `text`, optional): Describe the patient’s medical history.
* **Meds** (`6`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any medications the patient is taking.
* **Allergies** (`7`, `text`, optional): List any allergies the patient has.
* **Medical conditions** (`8`, `text`, optional): Describe any medical conditions the patient has.
* **Medical procedures** (`9`, `text`, optional): Describe any medical procedures the patient has undergone.
* **Emergency contact** (`10`, `text`, optional): Provide information about the patient’s emergency contact.
* **Insurance** (`11`, `text`, optional): Enter any insurance information for the patient.
* **Emergency contact relationship** (`12`, `text`, optional): Describe the relationship between the patient and their emergency contact.
* **Next of Kin contact** (`13`, `text`, optional): Enter details about the patient’s next of kin.
* **Next of Kin address** (`14`, `text`, optional): Enter the address of the patient’s next of kin.
* **Medical notes** (`15`, `text`, optional): Add any additional medical notes.
* **Medication administration** (`16`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the type of medication administration (if applicable).
* **Patient signature** (`17`, `note`, optional): Provide a signature from the patient.
* **Doctor signature** (`18`, `note`, optional): Provide a signature from the doctor.
* **Date** (`19`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the consultation.
* **Time** (`20`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the consultation.
* **Medical provider** (`21`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the medical provider.
* **Patient signature 2** (`22`, `text`, optional): Provide a second patient signature (if required).
* **Medical provider 2** (`23`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the second medical provider.
* **Date 2** (`24`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the second consultation.
* **Time 2** (`25`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the second consultation.
