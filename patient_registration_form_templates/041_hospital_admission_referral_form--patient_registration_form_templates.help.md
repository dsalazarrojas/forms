# hospital admission referral form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather essential information from patients for their hospital admission. Please complete this form carefully to provide accurate and up-to-date information.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form carefully to ensure all required fields are completed.
2.  Fill out each section according to your information, making sure to provide accurate details.
3.  Submit the form once you have filled out all required sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Demographics**: Enter the patient's personal details, including their name, date of birth, and other relevant information.
    *   **Contact Information**: Provide the patient's contact details, including their phone number and email address.
*   **Medical History**: Describe any pre-existing medical conditions or health issues that the patient has. This is a required field, as it helps medical staff understand the patient's health status.
*   **Medical Allergies**: Select any known allergies the patient has from the list provided.
*   **Medications**: If applicable, list any medications the patient is currently taking.
*   **Insurance Information**: Provide insurance details, such as policy number and provider name.
*   **Emergency Contact**: Indicate if an emergency contact is available and provide their name and phone number.
*   **Patient Consent**: Confirm the patient's consent for treatment and procedures.
*   **Patient Consent Details**: If necessary, provide any additional details about the patient's consent.
*   **Medical Condition**: Describe the patient's current medical condition or reason for hospital admission.
*   **Medical Treatment**: Indicate any specific medical treatments or procedures the patient requires.
*   **Physician Information**: Provide the name and contact details of the patient's attending physician.
*   **Hospital Admission Date**: Enter the date and time of hospital admission.
*   **Patient Notes**: Add any additional notes about the patient's condition or treatment.

## Tips

*   Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
*   Ensure all required fields are completed to avoid any delays in treatment.
*   If unsure about any field, consult the hospital staff for guidance.
