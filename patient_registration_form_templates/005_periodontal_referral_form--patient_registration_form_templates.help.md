# Periodontal Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Periodontal Referral Form is a patient registration form used to collect information from patients who are being referred to a dental specialist for periodontal treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Make sure to enter a valid email address.
6. If you have a medical condition, enter a brief description of it in the "Medical Condition" field.
7. Enter the reason for your referral to a dental specialist in the "Referral Reason" field.
8. If applicable, enter the name of your insurance provider in the "Insurance Provider" field.
9. Enter your patient ID number in the "Patient ID" field.
10. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
11. Enter the date and time of your visit in the "Visit Date" and "Visit Time" fields, respectively.
12. Enter your doctor's name in the "Doctor's Name" field.
13. Enter your doctor's phone number in the "Doctor's Phone" field.
14. Enter your doctor's email address in the "Doctor's Email" field.
15. If applicable, enter the name of your current dentist in the "Patient's Dentist" field.
16. If applicable, enter your dentist's phone number in the "Patient's Dentist Phone" field.
17. If applicable, enter your dentist's email address in the "Patient's Dentist Email" field.
18. Enter the periodontal disease or condition that requires treatment in the "Periodontal Disease" field.
19. Enter the periodontal procedure recommended by your doctor in the "Periodontal Procedure" field.
20. Enter your doctor's signature in the "Doctor's Signature" field.
21. Enter your doctor's title in the "Doctor's Title" field.
22. Enter your patient signature in the "Patient's Signature" field.
23. Enter any additional notes from your doctor in the "Doctor's Note" field.
24. Enter any additional notes from you as the patient in the "Patient Note" field.
