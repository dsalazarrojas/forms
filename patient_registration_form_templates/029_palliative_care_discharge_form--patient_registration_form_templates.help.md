# palliative_care_discharge_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "palliative_care_discharge_form" is used to document the discharge process of patients receiving palliative care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "patient_name" field.
2. Select the date and time of the patient's discharge in the "date_of_discharge" and "time_of_discharge" fields.
3. Document the patient's discharge summary in the "discharge_summary" field.
4. Enter the patient's ID in the "patient_id" field.
5. Select the patient's status in the "patient_status" field.
6. Document the patient's medical history in the "medical_history" field.
7. Document the patient's vital signs in the "vital_signs" field (optional).
8. Select the medications administered to the patient in the "medications" field (optional).
9. Select any allergies the patient has in the "allergies" field (optional).
10. Document the patient's medical history in the "medical_history_2", "medical_history_3", "medical_history_4", "medical_history_5", "medical_history_6", "medical_history_7", "medical_history_8", "medical_history_9", "medical_history_10", "medical_history_11", and "medical_history_12" fields (optional).
11. Document any discharge care plans for the patient in the "discharge_care_plan", "discharge_care_plan_2", and "discharge_care_plan_3" fields (optional).
