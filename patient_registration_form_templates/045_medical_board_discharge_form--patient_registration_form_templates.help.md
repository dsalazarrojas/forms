# Medical Board Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to inform patients about their discharge from a medical board and provide information on follow-up care.

## How To Complete This Form

### Medical Board Discharge Form

Please fill out the Medical Board Discharge Form to ensure a smooth transition after leaving the medical board. To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the relevant details about your discharge on Page 1 (patient_discharge_details).
2. Provide the contact information for follow-up care on Page 2 (follow_up_care).
3. Read and follow the discharge instructions on Page 3 (discharge_instructions).
4. Select the follow-up contact person from the options on Page 4 (follow_up_contact).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: patient_discharge_details
* **Patient Discharge Details (patient_discharge_details)** (`text`, required/optional): This page is for providing information about your discharge from the medical board. Please fill in your name, date of birth, and any other relevant details about your discharge.

### Page 2: follow_up_care
* **Follow-up Care (follow_up_care)** (`text`, required/optional): This page is for providing information on the follow-up care you should follow after leaving the medical board. Please fill in the name of your follow-up contact person and any other relevant details.

### Page 3: discharge_instructions
* **Discharge Instructions (discharge_instructions)** (`text`, required/optional): This page is for reading and following the instructions for your discharge from the medical board. Please carefully read and follow the instructions to ensure a smooth transition.

### Page 4: follow_up_contact
* **Follow-up Contact Person (follow_up_contact)** (`select_one`, required/optional): This page is for selecting the contact person for follow-up care. Please choose one of the options provided: Primary Care Physician, Nurse Practitioner, Social Worker, Therapist, or Other.

## Tips

* Make sure to carefully read and follow the discharge instructions to ensure your safety and well-being.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
* This form is intended for one-time use and should not be used for patients discharged multiple times.
