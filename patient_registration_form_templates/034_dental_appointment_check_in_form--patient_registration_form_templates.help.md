# Dental Appointment Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help patients confirm their appointments at the dental clinic. It collects essential information, such as patient details, appointment date and time, and confirmation status. This form ensures that patients can accurately confirm their appointments, enabling efficient scheduling and reducing no-shows.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the form carefully**: Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
2. **Enter patient information**: In the "patient-info" field, enter your name, date of birth, and contact details as per the patient's records.
3. **Select appointment date**: Choose the date of your scheduled appointment from the calendar.
4. **Select start time**: Choose the start time of your appointment from the provided time options.
5. **Confirm with doctor**: Choose "Yes" if you have confirmed your appointment with the doctor, or "No" if you have not.
6. **Add any additional notes**: If you have any additional information or comments related to your appointment, you can add them in the "notes" field (optional).
7. **Confirm appointment status**: Select "Confirm" if you have checked in for your appointment or "Other" if you need to cancel or reschedule.
