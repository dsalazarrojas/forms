# Pet Imaging Study Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect essential information from patients before undergoing imaging studies. The goal is to ensure accurate processing of their cases and provide a smooth experience for both patients and medical professionals.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you have provided all required and optional information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Details** (`patient_details`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide your basic patient information, such as your name, date of birth, or contact number.
* **Imaging Details** (`imaging_details`, text, optional): Here, you can mention the details about the imaging study you are undergoing. For example, you can specify the type of study (e.g., X-ray, MRI), date, time, or any other relevant information.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): If you want to provide additional contact information, such as an alternate phone number or email, you can use this field.

## Tips

- Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask a healthcare professional for assistance.
- Ensure you have filled out all required and optional fields before submitting the form.
