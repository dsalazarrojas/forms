# new_patient_paperwork - Help Guide
## Purpose
The new patient paperwork form is used to gather essential contact and demographic details from new patients.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
3. Provide your address in the "Address" field.
4. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
5. Enter your email in the "Email" field.
6. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
7. Check "Yes" or "No" for "Medical Insurance" if applicable.
8. Provide any relevant medical history in the "Medical History" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Address** (`Address`, `text`, required): Enter your physical address.
* **Contact Number** (`Contact Number`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email** (`Email`, `text`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Medical Insurance** (`Medical Insurance`, `select_multiple`, required): Check "Yes" if you have medical insurance.
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, `text`, required): Briefly describe any medical conditions you are currently experiencing.
