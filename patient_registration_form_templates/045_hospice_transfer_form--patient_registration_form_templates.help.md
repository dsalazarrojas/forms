# hospice_transfer_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the transfer of a patient's care from one hospice provider to another.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form fields with the necessary information about the patient.
2. Review the form carefully to ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
3. Make any necessary adjustments or updates before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name (1)** (`text`, required/optional): This field is used to capture the patient's name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Date of Birth (2)** (`date`, required/optional): This field is used to record the patient's date of birth, which is essential for tracking the patient's medical history.
* **Current Hospice Provider (3)** (`text`, required/optional): This field is used to identify the current hospice provider that the patient is currently receiving care from.
* **New Hospice Provider (4)** (`text`, required/optional): This field is used to identify the new hospice provider that the patient will be transferred to.
* **Transfer Date (5)** (`date`, required/optional): This field is used to record the date of the transfer, which is crucial for tracking care transitions.
* **Reason for Transfer (6)** (`text`, required/optional): This field is used to describe the reason for the transfer, which can include changes in the patient's condition, financial considerations, or other factors that necessitate a care transfer.
* **Patient Condition (7)** (`text`, required/optional): This field is used to report the patient's current medical condition.
* **Patient Status (8)** (`text`, required/optional): This field is used to indicate the patient's status, which can include indicators such as "admitted," "discharged," or other relevant updates.

## Tips
Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and completely. If you are unsure about any field, you can review the form carefully or seek clarification from relevant stakeholders before submitting the form.
