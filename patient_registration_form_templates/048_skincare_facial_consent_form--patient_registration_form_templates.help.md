# Skincare Facial Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form collects information from clients to provide personalized skincare services. It includes details such as contact information, preferred services, and medical history.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, ensuring all required fields are filled.
2. Enter your name as it appears on your ID.
3. Enter your contact number for communication purposes.
4. Enter your email address for updates and reminders.
5. If applicable, specify your expected visit date and time for the therapist.
6. Select the type of service you want to receive (if applicable).
7. If necessary, describe any medical history or allergies.
8. Sign the consent date and time to confirm your visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID.
* **Contact Number** (Optional): Enter your phone number for the therapist to contact you.
* **Email Address** (Required): Enter your email address for updates and reminders.
* **Expected Visit Date** (Optional): Specify the date of your expected visit.
* **Visit Time** (Optional): Specify the time of your expected visit.
* **Therapist Name** (Optional): If you have a preferred therapist, enter their name.
* **Service Type** (Optional): Select the type of service you are interested in (if applicable).
* **Service Area** (Optional): Describe the area of your face or skin requiring treatment (if applicable).
* **Medical History** (Optional): Share any relevant medical history or allergies with the therapist.
* **Consent Date** (Required): Enter the date you sign this form to confirm your visit.
* **Consent Time** (Required): Enter the time you sign this form to confirm your visit.
* **Signature** (Required): Sign your name to confirm you understand the information on this form and agree to the services provided.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
- If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please inform the therapist.
- Review the form carefully to ensure all information is accurate.
