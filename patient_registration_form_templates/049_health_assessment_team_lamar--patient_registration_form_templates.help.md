# Health Assesment Team Lamar - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Assesment Team Lamar form is used to collect patient information for medical assessment purposes. This form is designed to gather basic demographic information, medical history, and contact details to facilitate accurate and efficient medical assessments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Fill in your First Name in the First Name field.
* Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
* Provide your Email address in the Email field.
* Enter your Phone Number for communication purposes.
* Select your Birthday in the Date field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents, such as your passport or driver's license.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted for further communication.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number for easy communication.
* **Birthday** (`birthday`, date, required/optional): Select your date of birth in the date format MM/DD/YYYY.
