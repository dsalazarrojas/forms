# Optometry Patient Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect vital information about patients visiting the optometry clinic. The data collected will help the clinic to provide personalized care and keep accurate medical records.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your Date of Birth in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
6. Fill in your Medical Record Number and the Date of your last medical visit in the "Medical Record Number" and "Medical Record Date" fields respectively.
7. Enter the Optometrist's First and Last Name.
8. Provide your Patient Address in the "Patient Address" field.
9. Fill in your Medical History in the "Medical History" field.
10. List any allergies you may have in the "Allergies" field.
11. List any medications you are currently taking in the "Medications" field.
12. Enter any medical notes or comments in the "Medical Notes" field.
13. Schedule your next visit by selecting a date in the "Next Visit" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your given name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Medical Record Number**: Enter your unique patient ID number.
* **Medical Record Date**: Enter the date of your last medical visit.
* **Optometrist First Name**: Enter the optometrist's first name (this field is not typically required for patients).
* **Optometrist Last Name**: Enter the optometrist's last name (this field is not typically required for patients).
* **Patient Address**: Enter your address where you can be reached.
* **Medical History**: Describe any medical conditions, surgeries, or treatments you have had in the past.
* **Allergies**: List any substances or materials you are allergic to.
* **Medications**: List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical Notes**: Enter any relevant medical notes or comments.
* **Next Visit**: Schedule your next visit date by selecting a date in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
