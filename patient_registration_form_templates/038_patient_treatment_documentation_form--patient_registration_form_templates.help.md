# Patient Treatment Documentation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Patient Treatment Documentation Form is designed to collect patient data for treatment purposes. This form serves as a tool for healthcare professionals to gather essential information about patients and their medical conditions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form completely and honestly** as much as possible.
2. **Start with the Patient Registration Form**, where you'll provide your basic contact and demographic information.
3. **Proceed to the Treatment Details section**, where you will provide information about the treatment you are undergoing.
4. **In the Medical History section**, describe any medical conditions, allergies, or previous treatments that are relevant to your treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Registration Form** (Text Field, Required: Optional)
	+ This section collects your basic contact and demographic information, such as your name, contact number, and other details.
* **Treatment Details** (Text Field, Required: Optional)
	+ Describe the treatment you are undergoing, including the reason for the treatment and any relevant details.
* **Medical History** (Text Field, Required: Optional)
	+ Provide any relevant medical information, such as allergies, previous treatments, or existing medical conditions that may affect your treatment.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible in the Medical History section to ensure the healthcare professionals can provide you with the best care.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please don't hesitate to ask your healthcare professional for guidance.
* If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to us at form@gic.mx.
