# Patient History Declaration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Patient History Declaration Form is used to collect information about a patient's personal details, medical history, and allergies. This form is essential for our medical professionals to have a comprehensive understanding of a patient's condition, medical requirements, and treatment needs. It is typically filled out by the patient or their authorized representative before or during a medical consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information about the patient.
2. Fill out the fields as accurately as possible.
3. Review the form carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`patient_information`, text, required): Please enter the patient's first name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`patient_last_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's last name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, note, optional): Describe any significant medical conditions, surgeries, or allergies that may affect their treatment.
* **Medical History 2** (`medical_history_2`, note, optional): Describe any additional medical conditions, surgeries, or allergies that may affect their treatment.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, note, optional): List any allergies or sensitivities the patient has, including medications and foods.
* **Medications** (`medications`, note, optional): List any prescribed medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): If applicable, describe any medical condition the patient is currently experiencing.
* **Medical Condition 2** (`medical_condition_2`, text, optional): If applicable, describe any additional medical conditions the patient is currently experiencing.

## Tips

* Be as accurate and detailed as possible when providing information.
* If unsure about any field, consult with the medical professional conducting the consultation.
* This form is for the benefit of the patient's treatment and care; please provide all relevant information.
