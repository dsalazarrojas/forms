# Hospital Admission Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospital Admission Verification Form is used to verify patient information before admittance to the hospital. This form collects essential details about the patient to ensure accurate and efficient processing of their medical records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's **Patient Identifier**, which is a unique number assigned to the patient for identification purposes.
2. Provide the patient's **Name**.
3. Enter the **Medical Record Number**, which is the hospital's internal identifier for the patient's medical records.
4. Enter the patient's **Address**.
5. Enter the **Date of Birth**, which is the patient's birthdate.
6. Enter the **Contact Number**, which is the patient's or next of kin's contact number for communication purposes.
7. Indicate whether there is an **Emergency Contact**, which is a person the patient designates as their emergency contact.
8. Provide the name of the **Next of Kin**, who may be a relative or other responsible adult for the patient.
9. Enter the contact details of the **Next of Kin**.
10. Finally, provide the **Emergency Contact Relationship**, which is a brief description of the emergency contact's relationship to the patient.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Identifier**: Enter a unique identifier assigned to the patient for identification and tracking purposes.
* **Patient Name**: Enter the full name of the patient, including first name, last name, and any middle names.
* **Medical Record Number**: Enter the hospital's internal number for the patient's medical records.
* **Patient Address**: Enter the patient's address, including street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's birthdate in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the patient's or next of kin's contact number for communication purposes.
* **Emergency Contact**: Indicate whether there is an emergency contact, and if so, select 'Yes'.
* **Next of Kin Name**: Enter the name of the next of kin.
* **Next of Kin Contact**: Enter the contact details of the next of kin.
