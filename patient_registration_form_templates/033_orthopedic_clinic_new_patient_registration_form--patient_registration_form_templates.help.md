# Orthopedic Clinic New Patient Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register new patients at an orthopedic clinic, gathering essential information for their care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Patient Registration Form** (`new-patient-registration-form`, text, required): This is the form's title and a required field.
* **Contact Info** (`contact-info`, text, required): Enter your name, email, and phone number to allow our clinic to contact you.
* **Medical History** (`medical-history`, text, required): Please provide a brief overview of your medical history, including any previous surgeries, allergies, or medical conditions.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency-contact`, text, required): Provide the name, phone number, and relationship of an emergency contact person.
* **Contact Details** (`contact-details`, text, required): Enter your address and any additional contact information.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, required): List any allergies, intolerances, or sensitivities you have.
* **Medications** (`medications`, text, required): List any medications, supplements, or vitamins you are currently taking.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical-conditions`, text, required): Briefly describe any medical conditions you have, such as diabetes, high blood pressure, etc.
* **Medical Procedure** (`medical-procedure`, text, required): Describe any upcoming medical procedures or surgeries you have scheduled.
* **Patient Info** (`patient-info`, text, required): Enter your date of birth and any other relevant patient information.
* **Doctor Signature** (`doctor-signature`, text, required): A doctor will sign this field to confirm they have reviewed your information.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient-signature`, text, required): You must sign this field to confirm that the information provided is accurate and truthful.

## Tips
* Please review this form carefully before submitting it to ensure all information is accurate and complete.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask a clinic staff member for assistance.
* This form is confidential and your information will be kept secure.
