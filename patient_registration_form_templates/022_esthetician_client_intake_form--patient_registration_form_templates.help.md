# Esthetician Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Esthetician Client Intake Form is designed to collect essential information from clients visiting an esthetician or skincare professional for initial consultations, follow-up appointments, or routine check-ins. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and efficiently, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your First Name, Last Name, and Email fields with your personal and contact information. 
2. Provide your Medical History, including any relevant health details. 
3. List any Allergies or sensitivities you may have. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Fill out your first name as it appears on your official documents. 
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Fill out your last name as it appears on your official documents. 
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your email address associated with your contact information. 
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, text, required): Provide any relevant health information that may be important for the esthetician or skincare professional to know. 
* **Allergies** (`Allergies`, text, optional): List any allergies or sensitivities you may have. 

## Tips
- Be as accurate and detailed as possible when filling out the Medical History field. 
- If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to ask your esthetician or skincare professional before or after filling out this form. 
- Remember to provide a suitable placeholder (e.g., "N/A" or "Not Applicable") for any field that is not applicable or not relevant to your situation.
