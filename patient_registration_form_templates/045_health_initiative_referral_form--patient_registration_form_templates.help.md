# health_initiative_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to allow patients to refer themselves to healthcare providers or health initiatives in an efficient and organized manner. By filling out this form, patients can provide the necessary information for healthcare providers to contact them and initiate care.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are the correct person completing this form. Only patients should complete this form, not healthcare providers or anyone else on their behalf.
2. Fill out the form accurately and legibly, using the fields provided below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your full name, as it appears on your identification documents (e.g., driver's license, passport).
* **Patient DOB** (`patient_dob`, `date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD (e.g., 1990-02-15).
* **Initiative Name** (`initiative_name`, `text`, required: false): If you are referring yourself to a specific health initiative or program, please enter its name as provided by the program administrators.
* **Provider Name** (`provider_name`, `text`, required: false): If you are referring yourself to a specific healthcare provider, please enter their name as provided by the provider.
* **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number, including any area code and extension (e.g., 555-123-4567).
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your valid email address.
* **Provider Email** (`provider_email`, `email`, required: false): If you are referring yourself to a specific healthcare provider, enter their email address.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your physical address, including street address, apartment number, and city, state, and zip code (e.g., 123 Main St, Anytown, ST 12345).

## Tips

* Be thorough and accurate when filling out this form.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your healthcare provider or the health initiative administrator for guidance.
* This form is a referral form and does not replace clinical documentation. Your healthcare provider will need to verify your identity and other information before providing care.
