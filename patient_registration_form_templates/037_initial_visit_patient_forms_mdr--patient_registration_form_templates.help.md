# Initial Visit Patient Forms (MDR) - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Initial Visit Patient Forms (MDR) is a comprehensive form designed to collect essential information about patients visiting a medical facility. This form ensures accurate and efficient data collection, streamlining the registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the **Patient Information** section with the patient's personal details.
2. In the **Medical History** section, provide the patient's medical background, including any relevant health conditions or previous medical procedures.
3. For **Medications**, select all medications the patient is currently taking, if any.
4. In the **Allergies** section, select any allergies the patient has, if applicable.
5. Provide the **Visit Details** by selecting the date of the visit.
6. In the **Patient ID** field, enter the patient's unique identifier.
7. Enter the **Medical Record** time for the visit.
8. For **Email**, enter the patient's contact information.
9. In the **Phone** field, enter the patient's contact number.
10. Complete the **Address** field with the patient's current address.
11. In the **Medical Notes** field, add any relevant medical comments or notes about the patient.
12. Select the **Visit Date** for the appointment.
13. Enter your **Patient Signature** below to confirm you're aware of the provided information.
14. Finally, enter your **Practitioner Signature** to confirm you've reviewed the patient's information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Information
• **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and other personal details.

### Medical History
• **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required/optional): Describe the patient's medical background, including any health conditions, previous medical procedures, or allergies.

### Medications
• **Medications** (`medications`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all medications the patient is currently taking, if applicable.

### Allergies
• **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_one, required/optional): Select any allergies the patient has, if applicable.

### Visit Details
• **Visit Details** (`visit_details`, date, required/optional): Select the date of the visit.

### Patient ID
• **Patient ID** (`patient_id`, number, required/optional): Enter the patient's unique identifier.

### Medical Record
• **Medical Record** (`medical_record`, time, required/optional): Enter the medical record time for the visit.

### Email
• **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the patient's email address.

### Phone
• **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's contact number.

### Address
• **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's current address.

### Medical Notes
• **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, text, required/optional): Add any relevant medical comments or notes about the patient.

### Visit Date
• **Visit Date** (`visit_date`, date, required/optional): Select the date of the visit.

### Patient Signature
• **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, text, required/optional): Enter your patient signature to confirm you're aware of the provided information.

### Practitioner Signature
• **Practitioner Signature** (`practitioner_signature`, text, required/optional): Enter your practitioner signature to confirm you've reviewed the patient's information.

### Medical Practitioner
• **Medical Practitioner** (`medical_practitioner`, select_one, required/optional): Select the medical practitioner attending to the patient.

### Medical Department
• **Medical Department** (`medical_department`, select_one, required/optional): Select the medical department treating the patient.

### Medical Facility
• **Medical Facility** (`medical_facility`, select_one, required/optional): Select the medical facility where the patient is visiting.

### Medical Specialty
• **Medical Specialty** (`medical_specialty`, select_one, required/optional): Select the medical specialty the patient requires.

### Medical License
• **Medical License** (`medical_license`, select_one, required/optional): Select the medical license of the practitioner.

### Medical Insurance
• **Medical Insurance** (`medical_insurance`, select_one, required/optional): Select the medical insurance coverage the patient has.

### Medical Referral
• **Medical Referral** (`medical_referral`, select_one, required/optional): Select the reason for the medical referral.

### Medical Diagnosis
• **Medical Diagnosis** (`medical_diagnosis`, select_one, required/optional): Select the medical diagnosis of the patient.

### Medical Notes
• **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, text, required/optional): Add any relevant medical comments or notes about the patient.

### Medical Procedure
• **Medical Procedure** (`medical_procedure`, select_one, required/optional): Select the medical procedure the patient is undergoing.

### Medical Prescription
• **Medical Prescription** (`medical_prescription`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all medical prescriptions the patient requires.

### Tips
• Be accurate when filling out the form to ensure data quality.
• Review the form carefully before submitting.
• Make sure to enter all required fields.
• Ensure the patient's information is up-to-date and accurate.
