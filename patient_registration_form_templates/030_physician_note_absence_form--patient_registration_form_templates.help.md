# Physician Note Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document a patient's absence from the healthcare system, ensuring that their absence is properly recorded and communicated.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter the patient's last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter the date the patient started their absence in the "Date Absence Started" field.
4. Provide a brief explanation for the reason of the patient's absence in the "Reason for Absence" field.
5. Enter the name of the patient's employer in the "Employer Name" field.
6. Enter the address of the patient's employer in the "Employer Address" field.
7. Enter your name as the treating physician in the "Physician Name" field.
8. Sign the form with your signature in the "Physician Signature" field.
9. Enter the patient's ID number in the "Patient ID" field.
10. Enter the date the patient ended their absence in the "Date Absence Ended" field.
