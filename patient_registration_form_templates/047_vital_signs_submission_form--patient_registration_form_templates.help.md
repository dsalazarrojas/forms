# Vital Signs Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect vital signs and medical history information from patients. Please fill out this form to provide the necessary details for our medical professionals to better understand your health status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start.
2. Fill out the form by following the field-by-field instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`): Please enter your name and any other identifying information as instructed by the healthcare provider.
* **Vital Signs** (`vital_signs`): Enter your vital signs, such as blood pressure, temperature, and other relevant health metrics as instructed.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`): Describe any relevant medical history you'd like to share with the healthcare provider.
* **Medical Certifications** (`medical_certifications`): Select any medical certifications you hold, such as 'Yes' or 'No' for Medical Doctor, Specialist, or Surgeon.
* **Select Medical Certifications** (`medical_certifications_select`): Choose one of the options provided.
* **Blood Pressure** (`blood_pressure`): Enter your blood pressure reading.
* **Temperature** (`temperature`): Enter your body temperature reading.
* **Blood Glucose Level** (`blood_glucose_level`): Enter your blood glucose level reading.
* **Heart Rate** (`heart_rate`): Enter your heart rate reading.
* **Oxygen Saturation** (`oxygen_saturation`): Enter your oxygen saturation reading.
* **Respiratory Rate** (`respiratory_rate`): Enter your respiratory rate reading.
* **Pain Level** (`pain_level`): Choose the pain level you are experiencing.
* **Medical History Description** (`medical_history_description`): Briefly describe any medical history you'd like to provide.
* **Medical History Text Field** (`medical_history_text_field`): Enter any additional medical history information in this text field.
* **Medical History Note** (`medical_history_note`): Enter any additional comments or notes about your medical history.
* **Medical History Date** (`medical_history_date`): Enter the date of your medical history.
* **Medical History Time** (`medical_history_time`): Enter the time of your medical history.
* **Medical History Email** (`medical_history_email`): Enter your email address for follow-up contact.
* **Medical History Phone** (`medical_history_phone`): Enter your phone number for follow-up contact.
* **Medical History Checkbox** (`medical_history_checkbox`): Select one or more options from the provided list.
* **Medical History Radio** (`medical_history_radio`): Choose one of the options provided.
* **Medical History Select** (`medical_history_select`): Select one or more options from the provided list.
* **Medical History Date Range** (`medical_history_date_range`): Enter the range of dates related to your medical history.
* **Medical History Time Range** (`medical_history_time_range`): Enter the range of times related to your medical history.
* **Medical History Text Area** (`medical_history_text_area`): Enter any additional medical history information in this text area.
