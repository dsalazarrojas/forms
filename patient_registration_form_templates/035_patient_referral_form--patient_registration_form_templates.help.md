# Patient Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Referral Form is used to collect and document patient information for referrals to healthcare providers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's name, date of birth, and address.
2. Enter the referral reason and the name of the doctor the patient is being referred to.
3. Provide the patient's phone number and email address.
4. Select the patient's medical condition from the dropdown list.
5. Enter the name of the referring doctor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the patient.
* **Date Of Birth** (`patient_date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the patient's date of birth (MM/DD/YYYY format).
* **Referral Reason** (`referral_reason`, text, optional): Describe the reason for the referral.
* **Referred To Physician Name** (`referred_to_physician_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the doctor the patient is being referred to.
* **Referred To Department** (`referred_to_department`, text, optional): Enter the department the patient is being referred to.
* **Address** (`patient_address`, text, optional): Enter the patient's address.
* **City** (`patient_city`, text, optional): Enter the patient's city.
* **State** (`patient_state`, text, optional): Enter the patient's state.
* **Zip** (`patient_zip`, text, optional): Enter the patient's zip code.
* **Phone** (`patient_phone`, text, optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Email** (`patient_email`, text, optional): Enter the patient's email address. Make sure to enter a valid email address.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, select_one, optional): Select the patient's medical condition.
* **Referring Doctor Name** (`referring_doctor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the doctor referring the patient.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields, if applicable.
* Double-check the patient's contact information for accuracy.
* If you've already submitted a referral for the same patient, please do not resubmit the form.
