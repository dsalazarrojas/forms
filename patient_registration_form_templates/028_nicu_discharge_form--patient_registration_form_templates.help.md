# NICU Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The NICU Discharge Form is designed to collect relevant information about a patient's discharge from the hospital's NICU unit. This form helps to gather essential details for smooth discharge processes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Patient Name**, which should be as it appears in medical records.
2. Provide the **Date of Discharge**, in the format of mm/dd/yyyy or mm/dd/yy (e.g., 06/25/2022).
3. Enter the **Infant Name**, which is required for accurate documentation.
4. Enter the **Contact Number**, a valid phone number for follow-up purposes.
5. If applicable, enter the **Birth Weight** of the patient.
6. Add any **Feeding Instructions** for the patient, if necessary.
7. Provide any **Follow-up Instructions** for the patient.
8. Indicate if the patient has **Medication Allergies** (Yes/No).
9. Check if the patient has a **Medical History** (Yes/No).
10. Confirm the **Discharge Caregiver** (Yes/No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter the name as it appears in medical records.
* **Date of Discharge**: Enter the date in the format mm/dd/yyyy or mm/dd/yy.
* **Infant Name**: Required field for accurate documentation.
* **Contact Number**: A valid phone number for follow-up purposes.
* **Birth Weight**: If known, enter the birth weight of the patient.
* **Feeding Instructions**: Add any specific feeding instructions for the patient.
* **Follow-up Instructions**: Any additional instructions for the patient's follow-up care.
* **Medication Allergies**: Check if the patient has medication allergies.
* **Medical History**: Check if the patient has a medical history.
* **Discharge Caregiver**: Confirm the person responsible for the patient's care post-discharge.
