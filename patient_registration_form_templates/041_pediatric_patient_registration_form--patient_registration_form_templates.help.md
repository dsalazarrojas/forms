# Pediatric Patient Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Pediatric Patient Registration Form is designed to collect necessary patient information for pediatric care. It's essential for healthcare providers to have this information to ensure the patient receives the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's name, birth date, and any other relevant contact information.
2. Enter any medical history, including conditions and medications.
3. Provide information about the patient's medical background.
4. Select the contact relationship (mother, father, or other).
5. Select the pediatrician (Dr. A, Dr. B, etc.).
6. Add any medical images or images.
7. Enter any medical notes.
8. Enter the family medicine information.
9. Enter any additional medical information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info**: This field is for the patient's name, birth date, and contact information.
* **Contact Info**: This field is for additional contact information, such as phone numbers and email.
* **Medical History**: This field is for any medical conditions or allergies the patient may have.
* **Medical Background**: This field is for any additional medical background information.
* **Allergies**: This field is for any allergies the patient may have.
* **Medical Conditions**: This field is for any medical conditions the patient may have.
* **Medications**: This field is for any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Family Info**: This field is for any family medical information.
* **Pediatric Info**: This field is for any additional pediatric information.
* **Contact Relationship**: This field is for specifying who to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Pediatrician**: This field is for selecting a pediatrician for the patient.
* **Medical Image**: This field is for uploading any medical images.
* **Medical Notes**: This field is for any medical notes about the patient.
* **Family Medicine**: This field is for any family medicine information.
* **Birth Date**: This field is for the patient's birth date.
* **Weight**: This field is for the patient's weight.
* **Height**: This field is for the patient's height.
* **Medical Notes**: This field is for any additional medical notes about the patient.
* **Contact Number**: This field is for the contact number.
* **Email**: This field is for the patient's email address.
* **Medical Record Number**: This field is for the patient's medical record number.
* **Address**: This field is for the patient's address.
* **Medical History Date**: This field is for the date of the medical history.
* **Medical History Date/Time**: This field is for the time of the medical history.
* **Pediatrician Other**: This field is for selecting a different pediatrician not listed.
* **Medical Image**: This field is for uploading any medical images.

Please fill in the form carefully and accurately to provide the best possible care for the patient. If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your healthcare provider for assistance.
