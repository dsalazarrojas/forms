# Patient Preparation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect relevant and detailed information about a patient's medical history, medical conditions, allergies, medications, and emergency contact information. It is intended to help healthcare professionals gather essential data for patient care and treatment.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your medical history, including any previous illnesses, surgeries, or medical conditions you have experienced.
2. List any allergies you have, including medications you are allergic to.
3. Describe any medications you are currently taking, including dosages and frequencies.
4. Add any additional medical history information that may be relevant to your treatment.
5. Provide details about any medical conditions you have, including their severity and impact on your daily life.
6. List any allergies you have, including medications you are allergic to.
7. Describe any emergency contacts, including their name, relationship, and contact information.
8. Provide details about any medical conditions you have that may interact with medications.
9. Add any additional medical notes or comments.
10. Complete the contact information, including the emergency contact's name, relationship, and contact information.
11. Specify the relationship between the patient and the emergency contact.
12. Provide the start and end dates (if applicable) for the emergency contact relationship.
13. Add any additional contact notes or comments.
14. Specify any medical notes or comments for the patient's medical history.
15. Add any additional medical notes or comments for the patient's contact information.
16. Specify any medical notes or comments for any allergies.
17. Provide any medication interactions or conflicts with medical conditions.
18. Specify any medical notes or comments for the patient's medications.
19. Complete the form by reviewing and submitting it for review by a healthcare professional.

* **Medical History**: A text field where you can provide any previous illnesses, surgeries, or medical conditions you have experienced.
* **Contact Info**: A text field for providing emergency contact information, including name and contact details.
* **Emergency Contact**: A text field for listing any emergency contacts, including name and contact information.
* **Medical Conditions**: A text field for describing any medical conditions you have, including their severity and impact on your daily life.
* **Allergies**: A text field for listing any allergies you have, including medications you are allergic to.
* **Medications**: A text field for describing any medications you are currently taking, including dosages and frequencies.
* **Medical History Additional**: A text field for adding any additional medical history information that may be relevant to your treatment.
* **Medical Conditions Details**: A text field for providing further details about any medical conditions you have.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: A text field for specifying the relationship between you and the emergency contact.
* **Contact Info Relationship**: A text field for specifying the relationship between you and the emergency contact.
* **Allergies Details**: A text field for providing further details about any allergies you have.
* **Medications Details**: A text field for providing further details about any medications you are taking.
