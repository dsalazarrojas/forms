# Medical Assistance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is intended for patients to request medical assistance. It is a simple, one-page form with four fields to collect relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Provide as much detail as possible for each field.
3. Review your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information**
  * **User Information** (`user_info`, text, optional): Enter your name, date of birth, or any other relevant identification information.
  * **User Information** (`user_info`, text, optional): Please provide a clear and concise statement describing your medical condition or concern.
* **Contact Information**
  * **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): Enter your contact information, including your phone number and email address, so we can reach out to you for any further questions or assistance.
* **Medical History**
  * **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Share any relevant medical history or allergies that may be useful for your medical assistance request.
* **Medical Assistance Request**
  * **Medical Assistance Request** (`medical_assistance_request`, text, optional): Clearly and concisely describe the specific medical assistance you are requesting.

## Tips

* Be as accurate and detailed as possible when filling out each field.
* Use clear and concise language to describe your medical condition or concern.
* If you have any supporting documents or files, you can attach them to your form submission.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, you can contact us at form@gic.mx.
