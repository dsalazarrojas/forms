# Patient Care Program Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Care Program Approval Form is a document used to approve and track patient care programs. These programs are essential for ensuring continuity of care and monitoring of patient conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information about the patient and their program.
2.  Ensure that all fields are completed correctly to ensure a seamless approval process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input**: This field is used to enter any additional information about the patient or program that is not included in the other fields.
* **Program Name**: Enter the name of the patient care program you are seeking approval for. This could be a medication management program, disease management program, or wound care program.
* **Program Type**: Choose the type of program that the patient is enrolled in. This could be Medication Management, Disease Management, or Wound Care.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date the program started.
* **End Date**: Enter the date the program is expected to end.
* **Status**: Choose the status of the program. This could be Active or Inactive.
* **Patient Name**: Enter the name of the patient enrolled in the program.
* **Patient Phone**: Enter the phone number of the patient.
* **Provider Name**: Enter the name of the provider or clinician overseeing the patient care program.
* **Provider Phone**: Enter the phone number of the provider or clinician.

## Tips
*   Ensure that all fields are completed accurately to avoid any discrepancies or delays in the approval process.
*   Double-check the patient's name and phone number for correctness.
*   Review the form thoroughly before submitting to ensure that all necessary fields are completed.
*   If you are resubmitting a form that was previously completed, please ensure that it is because of new or updated information.
