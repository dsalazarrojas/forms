# Counseling Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Counseling Intake Form is used to collect necessary demographic and medical information from patients for the purpose of facilitating the intake process for counseling services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the "Date of Birth" field in the MM/DD format (e.g., 01/01/2020).
4. Enter your **Contact Email** in the "Contact Email" field.
5. Enter your **Contact Phone** number in the "Contact Phone" field.
6. Enter your **Address** in the "Address" field.
7. Enter the **Name** of your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Name" field.
8. Select the **Relationship** to the patient (e.g., parent, guardian, or other) from the "Relationship to Patient" field.
9. Enter the **Phone number** of your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field.
10. Select your **Primary Language** from the list provided in the "Primary Language" field.
11. Enter your **Preferred Name** (if different from your first name) in the "Preferred Name" field (optional).
12. If applicable, enter your **Insurance Provider** and **Insurance Policy** numbers in the "Insurance Provider" and "Insurance Policy" fields respectively.
13. Enter your **Medical Record Number** and **Medical Record Date** (in MM/DD format) in the "Medical Record Number" and "Medical Record Date" fields respectively.
14. Select your **Primary Care Provider** from the list provided in the "Primary Care Provider" field.
15. Select your medications from the "Medication" field (e.g., Aspirin, Ibuprofen, Acetaminophen).
16. Enter any **Allergies** in the "Allergies" field.
17. Enter any **Additional Notes** about your medical history or concerns in the "Additional Notes" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Required, Text): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Required, Text): Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (Required, Date): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD format (e.g., 01/01/2020).
* **Contact Email** (Required, Email): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (Required, Text): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Address** (Required, Text): Enter your home address.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Required, Text): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship to Patient** (Required, Text): Select the relationship to the patient (e.g., parent, guardian, or other).
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Required, Text): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Primary Language** (Required, Select): Select your primary language from the list.
* **Preferred Name** (Optional, Text): Enter your preferred name (if different from your first name).
* **Insurance Provider** (Optional, Text): Enter your insurance provider's name or number.
* **Insurance Policy** (Optional, Text): Enter your insurance policy number.
* **Medical Record Number** (Optional, Text): Enter your medical record number.
* **Medical Record Date** (Optional, Date): Enter the date of your medical record in MM/DD format.
* **Primary Care Provider** (Required, Select): Select your primary care provider from the list.
* **Medication** (Required, Select Multiple): Select your medications from the list.
* **Allergies** (Required, Text): Enter any allergies you have.
* **Additional Notes** (Optional, Note): Enter any additional notes about your medical history or concerns.

## Tips
* Be accurate and complete when filling out the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please ask a staff member for assistance.
* If you have previously completed this form, do not submit it again.
* If you need to make changes to your submitted form, please contact the staff.
