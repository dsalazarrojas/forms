# Lactation Consultation Pre Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lactation Consultation Pre Assessment form is a tool used to assess the patient's lactation experience and identify potential challenges or issues.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps above.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **First Page** (`id: 1`): This page is used to capture general information about the patient's lactation experience.
*   **Patient Details** (`id: 2`): Enter the patient's details, such as their name, contact information, and date of birth.
*   **Medical History** (`id: 3`): Record the patient's medical history, including any relevant medical conditions or allergies.
*   **Breastfeeding Information** (`id: 4`): Enter information about the patient's current breastfeeding experience, including the frequency and duration of feedings.
*   **Feeding Issues** (`id: 5`): Report any issues or challenges the patient is experiencing with breastfeeding.
*   **Breastfeeding Challenges** (`id: 6`): Enter any challenges the patient is facing with breastfeeding.
*   **Feeding Plan** (`id: 7`): Enter a plan for the patient's lactation care.
*   **Maternal and Newborn** (`id: 8`): Enter information about the patient's maternal and newborn health.
*   **Maternal and Newborn Information** (`id: 9`): Enter any relevant information about the patient's maternal and newborn health.
*   **Medical History Important** (`id: 10`): Enter any important medical history information.
*   **Lactation Support** (`id: 11`): Enter information about the patient's lactation support, including any support received or needed.
*   **Lactation Resources** (`id: 12`): Enter information about any lactation resources the patient has access to.
*   **Lactation Consultation Date** (`id: 13`): Enter the date of the lactation consultation.
*   **Lactation Consultation Time** (`id: 14`): Enter the time of the lactation consultation.
*   **Lactation Notes** (`id: 15`): Enter any notes or comments from the lactation consultation.
*   **Next Steps** (`id: 16`): Enter any next steps or recommendations for the patient's lactation care.
*   **Lactation Resources 2** (`id: 17`): Enter information about any additional lactation resources the patient has access to.
*   **Lactation Support 2** (`id: 18`): Enter information about any additional lactation support the patient has received or needs.
*   **Lactation Follow-up** (`id: 19`): Enter information about any follow-up lactation consultations or appointments.
*   **Lactation Follow-up Date** (`id: 20`): Enter the date of the lactation follow-up.
*   **Lactation Follow-up Time** (`id: 21`): Enter the time of the lactation follow-up.
*   **Lactation Notes 2** (`id: 22`): Enter any additional notes or comments from the lactation follow-up.
*   **Lactation Support 3** (`id: 23`): Enter information about any additional lactation support the patient has received or needs.
*   **Lactation Referral Information** (`id: 24`): Enter any information about lactation referrals.
*   **Lactation Referral** (`id: 25`): Enter any lactation referrals the patient has received or needs.

### Tips

*   Be sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please ask your healthcare professional for guidance.
*   This form is intended to be used by healthcare professionals to assess the patient's lactation experience and provide guidance on next steps.
*   Please note that some fields may have additional instructions or hints to help guide you through the process.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your healthcare professional for assistance.
