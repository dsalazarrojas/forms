# medication_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The medication consent form is a patient registration form designed to collect personal and medication-related information from patients before treatment or medication administration. It is intended to provide healthcare professionals with a comprehensive understanding of the patient's medical history and current condition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. Provide your current address for contact and billing purposes.
3. Describe your current medication regimen, including dosage and frequency.
4. Read and sign the terms and conditions section, which outlines the patient's rights and responsibilities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, text, required): Provide your current address for contact and billing purposes.
* **Medication Details** (`medication_details`, text, required): Describe your current medication regimen, including dosage and frequency.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, text, required): Read and sign this section to acknowledge your understanding of your rights and responsibilities as a patient.
