# Client History Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client History Form is a comprehensive medical history form designed to gather essential information about a patient's health status, medical conditions, and other relevant details. This form is used to inform medical decisions and ensure that the patient receives proper care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **First Name** (`patient_first_name`, text, required): Enter your legal first name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. **Last Name** (`patient_last_name`, text, required): Enter your legal last name as it appears on your identification documents.
3. **Email Address** (`patient_email`, email, required): Enter your primary contact email address.
4. **Phone Number** (`patient_phone`, text, required): Enter your best contact phone number.
5. **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
6. **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required): Select your gender from the options provided.
7. **Blood Type** (`blood_type`, select_one, optional): If known, select your blood type from the options provided. If not known, please leave blank.
8. **Primary Care Physician Name** (`primary_care_physician`, text, optional): Enter the name of your regular doctor.
9. **Physician Phone** (`physician_phone`, text, optional): Enter your doctor's contact phone number.
10. **Past Medical Conditions** (`past_medical_conditions`, text, required): List all diagnosed medical conditions.
11. **Chronic Conditions** (`chronic_conditions`, select_multiple, required): Select any long-term health issues from the options provided.
12. **History of Infectious Diseases** (`infectious_diseases`, text, optional): List any history of infectious diseases, such as Hepatitis, TB, or HIV.
13. **Hospitalizations** (`hospitalizations`, text, optional): List any hospitalizations with dates and reasons.
14. **Surgeries** (`surgeries`, text, optional): List any surgeries with dates and types.
15. **Significant Injuries or Accidents** (`injuries_accidents`, text, optional): List any major trauma, fractures, or other significant injuries.
16. **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, text, required): List all current medications, including dosages and frequency.
17. **Past Medications of Note** (`past_medications`, text, optional): List previously taken medications.
18. **Drug Allergies** (`drug_allergies`, text, optional): List medication allergies and reactions.
19. **Food Allergies** (`food_allergies`, text, optional): List food allergies and reactions.
20. **Environmental Allergies** (`environmental_allergies`, text, optional): List environmental allergies, such as pollen or pet allergies.
21. **Family Medical History** (`family_history`, text, required): List medical conditions of parents, siblings, or children.
22. **Genetic Conditions** (`genetic_conditions`, text, optional): List hereditary conditions.
23. **Immunization History** (`immunizations`, text, optional): List vaccines received.
24. **Smoking History** (`smoking_history`, text, optional): List current and past smoking history, including pack years if applicable.
25. **Alcohol Use History** (`alcohol_history`, text, optional): List current and past alcohol use.
26. **Recreational Drug Use History** (`drug_use_history`, text, optional): List current and past recreational drug use.
27. **Occupational History** (`occupational_history`, text, optional): List jobs with potential health exposures.
28. **Recent Travel History** (`travel_history`, text, optional): List international travel within the past year.
29. **Reproductive History** (`reproductive_history`, text, optional): List any pregnancies, births, or other relevant reproductive history.
30. **Mental Health History** (`mental_health_history`, text, optional): List any mental health conditions, such as depression or anxiety.
31. **Confirm Truthfulness** (`information_accuracy`, select_one, required): Select "I Confirm" to confirm that all information provided is accurate.
32. **Form Date** (`form_date`, date, required): Enter the date the form was completed.

## Tips

* Please be as thorough and accurate as possible when completing this form.
* If you are unsure about any information, please ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
* This form is designed to be completed by the patient, but healthcare providers may need to review and verify the information provided.
* Some fields may require special instructions or formatting, so please refer to the specific instructions provided for each field.
* Please confirm truthfulness before submitting the form to ensure that the information provided is accurate.
