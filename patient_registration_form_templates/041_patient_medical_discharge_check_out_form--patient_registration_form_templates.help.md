# Patient Medical Discharge Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Medical Discharge Check Out Form is designed to gather essential information from patients when they are being discharged from a medical facility. This form helps ensure that all necessary details are collected and documented before the patient is discharged.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the patient's medical chart and ensure that all relevant information is up-to-date.
2. Answer each question carefully and accurately, providing as much detail as possible.
3. If you are unsure about any information, consult the attending physician or a medical professional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (`patient_medical_discharge_check_out_form_data`, text, optional): This field is for capturing the patient's basic information, such as name, date of birth, and medical record number.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_multiple, optional): Please list any allergies or sensitivities the patient has. Choose from the available options (Ibuprofen, Amoxicillin, Cephalexin) or add any other relevant information not listed.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): Describe any medical conditions or illnesses the patient has, including any relevant medical history or previous treatments.
* **Medication History** (`medication_history`, text, optional): Record any medications the patient is currently taking, including dosage and frequency.
* **Test Results** (`test_results`, select_one, optional): Indicate the status of any relevant tests or examinations (Normal, Abnormal).
* **Vital Signs** (`vital_signs`, text, optional): Report any vital signs taken during the patient's stay, such as blood pressure, temperature, or pulse.
* **Discharge Info** (`discharge_info`, text, optional): Provide any relevant information for the patient's discharge, including any instructions or advice given to the patient.

## Tips

* Always consult the patient's medical chart and review their medical history before completing this form.
* Be thorough and accurate in your responses to ensure that all necessary information is collected.
* If you are unsure about any information, please consult the attending physician or a medical professional.
* This form is a template and should be reviewed and updated according to your institution's specific policies and procedures.
