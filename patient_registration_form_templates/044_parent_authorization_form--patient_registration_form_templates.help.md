# Parent Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Parent Authorization Form is used to collect information from a parent or guardian to verify the identity and relationship of a patient. This form is essential for official purposes and requires accurate and up-to-date information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your first name in the "First name" field.
* Fill in your last name in the "Last name" field.
* If applicable, fill in your date of birth in the "Date of birth" field.
* Enter your address in the "Address" field.
* Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
* Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
* If applicable, describe your relationship to the patient in the "Relationship" field.
* Sign and date the "Parent signature" field to confirm your identity and authorization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it is written on your government-issued ID.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it is written on your government-issued ID.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): If you are the parent, please enter your date of birth for verification purposes.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your current address as it is written on your government-issued ID or utility bills.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Relationship** (`relationship`, `text`, optional): If you are not the patient's parent, please describe your relationship to the patient (e.g., guardian, emergency contact, etc.).
* **Parent Signature** (`signature`, `note`, required): Sign and date this field to confirm your identity and authorization as the parent or guardian.
