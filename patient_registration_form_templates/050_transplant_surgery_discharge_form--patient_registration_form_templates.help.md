# Transplant Surgery Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information on the patient's status after a transplant surgery, including contact information, medications, and follow-up appointments.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Select the date of the surgery in the "Date Of Surgery" field.
3. Enter the date of the transplant in the "Transplant Date" field.
4. For "Follow Up Appointments," select "Yes" or "No" accordingly.
5. List any medications the patient is currently taking in the "Medications" field.
6. Provide contact information, including name, address, phone number, and email.
7. Enter any emergency contact information, including name, phone number, and email.
8. Specify the date for the follow-up appointment in the "Follow Up Date" field.
9. Enter the time for the follow-up appointment in the "Follow Up Time" field.
10. Provide the follow-up location.
11. Enter any comments for the follow-up appointment in the "Follow Up Comment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name (Text)**: Enter the name of the patient.
* **Date Of Surgery (Date)**: Select the date of the transplant surgery.
* **Transplant Date (Date)**: Enter the date of the transplant.
* **Follow Up Appointments (Select Multiple)**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if follow-up appointments are necessary.
* **Medications (Text)**: List any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Contact Information (Text)**: Provide contact information, including name, address, phone number, and email.
* **Emergency Contact (Text)**: Enter emergency contact information, including name, phone number, and email.
* **Discharge Date (Date)**: Select the discharge date of the patient.
* **Follow Up Contact (Text)**: Enter contact information for the follow-up appointment.
* **Next Steps (Text)**: Enter any additional information or next steps for the patient's care.
* **Discharge Instructions (Text)**: Enter any specific instructions for the patient's discharge.
* **Post Transplant Care (Select Multiple)**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if post-transplant care is necessary.
* **All Medications (Select Multiple)**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the patient is taking all prescribed medications.
* **New Medications (Select Multiple)**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the patient is taking new medications.
* **Contact Relationship (Text)**: Enter the relationship with the contact person (e.g., family member, friend, etc.).
* **Contact Address (Text)**: Enter the address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone (Text)**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email (Text)**: Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Name (Text)**: Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (Text)**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email (Text)**: Enter the email of the emergency contact.
* **Follow Up Date (Date)**: Select the date for the follow-up appointment.
* **Follow Up Time (Time)**: Enter the time for the follow-up appointment.
* **Follow Up Location (Text)**: Enter the location for the follow-up appointment.
* **Follow Up Comment (Text)**: Enter any comments or notes for the follow-up appointment.
* **Post Transplant Care (Select Multiple)**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if post-transplant care is necessary.
* **All Medications (Select Multiple)**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the patient is taking all prescribed medications.
* **New Medications (Select Multiple)**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the patient is taking new medications.
