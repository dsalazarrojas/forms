# Patient Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Patient Intake Form is used to collect basic patient information for medical purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the First Name field with your patient's first name.
2. Fill in the Last Name field with your patient's last name.
3. Enter your patient's Date of Birth in the Date of Birth field.
4. Enter your patient's Email address in the Email field.
5. If applicable, fill in the Medical Record ID field with your patient's existing medical record ID.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your patient's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your patient's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your patient's date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your patient's email address.
* **Medical Record ID** (`medical_record_id`, text, required/optional): If your patient has an existing medical record ID, enter it here.

## Tips
* Please make sure to double-check the information you enter to ensure accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us for assistance.
* You can save your progress and come back to this form later, if needed.
