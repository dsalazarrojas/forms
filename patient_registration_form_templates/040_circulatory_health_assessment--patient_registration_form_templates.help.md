# Circulatory Health Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Circulatory Health Assessment form is used to collect information from patients about their circulatory health and wellness habits, including demographic details, medical history, and lifestyle factors. This form will help healthcare professionals gather essential data to provide personalized advice and guidance for maintaining healthy cardiovascular function.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **Patient Full Name** in the text field. This is the first and last name of the person being assessed.
2. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
3. Select your **Gender** from the provided options (Male, Female, Other, Prefer not to say).
4. Enter your **Email Address** in a valid email format (e.g., `example@example.com`).
5. Enter your **Phone Number** in the phone number format.
6. For your **Height**, enter your height in inches or centimeters.
7. Enter your **Weight** in pounds or kilograms.
8. If known, provide your **Recent Blood Pressure Reading** in the text field (e.g., 120/80 mmHg).
9. If known, enter your **Resting Heart Rate** in beats per minute.
10. Answer **Do You Know Your Cholesterol Levels** (True or False).
11. If known, provide your **Total Cholesterol** level.
12. Select your **Family History of Heart Disease**:
	* Yes, immediate family
	* Yes, extended family
	* False
	* Not sure
13. Select your **Smoking Status**:
	* Never smoked
	* Former smoker
	* Current smoker
	* Occasional smoker
14. If a current smoker, enter your **How Many Cigarettes Per Day**.
15. Select how often you **Exercise**:
	* Daily
	* 3-5 times per week
	* 1-2 times per week
	* Rarely
	* Never
16. Select all the types of **Exercise** you regularly perform:
	* Walking
	* Running
	* Swimming
	* Cycling
	* Weight training
	* Yoga
	* Sports
	* None
17. Rate your **Diet Quality**:
	* Excellent
	* Good
	* Fair
	* Poor
18. Enter your average **Daily Servings of Fruits and Vegetables**.
19. Select all your **Existing Medical Conditions**:
	* High blood pressure
	* High cholesterol
	* Diabetes
	* Heart disease
	* Stroke
	* Peripheral artery disease
	* None
20. Enter your **Current Medications** (especially for heart or blood pressure medications).
21. Select all your **Current Symptoms**:
	* Chest pain
	* Shortness of breath
	* Palpitations
	* Dizziness
	* Leg pain when walking
	* Swelling in legs
	* Fatigue
	* None
22. Answer how you rate your **Average Stress Level** (Scale of 1-10).
23. Select your **Sleep Quality**:
	* Excellent
	* Good
	* Fair
	* Poor
24. Select your **Alcohol Consumption**:
	* None
	* 1-3 per week
	* 4-7 per week
	* 8-14 per week
	* More than 14 per week
25. Enter your **Health Goals** for cardiovascular health.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Full Name**: Your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth**: The date you were born (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Gender**: Select your biological sex (Male, Female, Other, Prefer not to say).
* **Email Address**: A valid email address for communication.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number.
* **Height**: Your height in inches or centimeters.
* **Weight**: Your weight in pounds or kilograms.
* **Recent Blood Pressure Reading**: If you know your blood pressure, enter it (e.g., 120/80 mmHg).
* **Resting Heart Rate**: If you know your resting heart rate, enter it in beats per minute.
* **Do You Know Your Cholesterol Levels**: Answer whether you know your cholesterol levels (True or False).
* **Total Cholesterol**: If you know your total cholesterol level, enter it.
* **Family History of Heart Disease**: Select whether you have a family history of heart disease.
* **Smoking Status**: Select your smoking status.
* **How Many Cigarettes Per Day**: If a current smoker, enter how many cigarettes you smoke per day.
* **Exercise**: Select how often you exercise.
* **Exercise Type**: Select all the types of exercise you regularly perform.
* **Diet Quality**: Rate your diet quality (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Daily Servings of Fruits and Vegetables**: Enter your average daily servings of fruits and vegetables.
* **Existing Medical Conditions**: Select all your existing medical conditions.
* **Current Medications**: Enter any medications you're currently taking, especially for heart or blood pressure medications.
* **Current Symptoms**: Select all your current symptoms.
* **Average Stress Level**: Rate your average stress level (Scale of 1-10).
* **Sleep Quality**: Select your sleep quality.
* **Alcohol Consumption**: Select your alcohol consumption.
* **Health Goals**: Enter your goals for maintaining healthy cardiovascular function.
