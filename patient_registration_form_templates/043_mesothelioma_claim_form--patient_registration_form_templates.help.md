# Mesothelioma Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mesothelioma Claim Form is designed to gather essential information from patients or their representatives to support the claims process for mesothelioma-related compensation or benefits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Provide your Date of Birth in the **Date of Birth** field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Enter the Date of Diagnosis in the **Date of Diagnosis** field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
5. Enter the Date of Diagnosis for the diagnosis date (if different) in the **Diagnosis Date of Diagnosis** field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
6. Enter the name of your doctor in the **Doctor Name** field.
7. Enter your contact number in the **Contact Number** field.
8. Enter your address in the **Address** field.
9. (Optional) If you have an insurance provider, enter their name in the **Insurance Name** field.
10. Enter your insurance ID in the **Insurance ID** field.
11. Select the type of claim you are submitting (e.g., Asbestos, Other, or Mesothelioma) in the **Claim Type** field.
12. Enter the claim amount in the **Claim Amount** field.
13. Provide your medical history in the **Medical History** field.
14. Enter your treatment plan in the **Treatment Plan** field.
15. Select the current claim status (Pending, Approved, Denied, Reconsider, or other) in the **Claim Status** field.
16. (Optional) Enter any additional notes in the **Notes** field.
17. Enter the date of the claim in the **Claim Date** field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
18. (Optional) Enter the last updated date in the **Last Updated** field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
19. (Optional) Enter the date the form was submitted in the **Submitted** field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
20. (Optional) Enter the date the form was reviewed in the **Reviewed** field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
21. (Optional) Enter the date the form was completed in the **Completed** field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
22. (Optional) Enter the date the claim was closed in the **Claim Closed** field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
23. (Optional) Select the type of mesothelioma (e.g., Pleural Mesothelioma, Peritoneal Mesothelioma, etc.) in the **Mesothelioma Type** field.
24. (Optional) Select the type of asbestos exposure (e.g., Asbestos in the work-place, Asbestos in the home, etc.) in the **Asbestos Exposure** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
  - **Last Name** (Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
  - **Date of Birth** (Required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
  - **Date of Diagnosis** (Required): Enter the date of mesothelioma diagnosis in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
  - **Diagnosis Date of Diagnosis** (Required): Enter the date of the diagnosis (if different from Date of Diagnosis) in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
  - **Doctor Name** (Required): Enter the name of your doctor.
  - **Contact Number** (Required): Enter your contact number for communication purposes.
  - **Address** (Required): Enter your address for contact purposes.
  - **Insurance Name** (Optional): If you have an insurance provider, enter their name.
  - **Insurance ID** (Required): Enter your insurance ID.
  - **Claim Type** (Required): Select the type of claim you are submitting (e.g., Asbestos, Other, or Mesothelioma).
  - **Claim Amount** (Required): Enter the amount of the claim.
  - **Medical History** (Required): Provide a detailed description of your medical history.
  - **Treatment Plan** (Required): Enter your treatment plan details.
  - **Claim Status** (Required): Select the current status of your claim (e.g., Pending, Approved, Denied, Reconsider, or other).
  - **Notes** (Optional): Enter any additional notes or information regarding your claim.
  - **Claim Date** (Required): Enter the date you submitted the claim in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
  - **Last Updated** (Optional): Enter the last updated date of your claim in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
  - **Submitted** (Optional): Enter the date the form was submitted in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
  - **Reviewed** (Optional): Enter the date the form was reviewed in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
  - **Completed** (Optional): Enter the date the form was completed in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
  - **Claim Closed** (Optional): Enter the date the claim was closed in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
  - **Mesothelioma Type** (Optional): Select the type of mesothelioma (e.g., Pleural Mesothelioma, Peritoneal Mesothelioma, etc.).
  - **Asbestos Exposure** (Optional): Select the type of asbestos exposure (e.g., Asbestos in the work-place, Asbestos in the home, etc.).
