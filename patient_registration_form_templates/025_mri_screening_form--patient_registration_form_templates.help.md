# MRI Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from patients for the purpose of screening their medical history and contact information before undergoing an MRI procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the General Information page.
2. Proceed to the Date of Birth page and enter your date of birth.
3. Move on to the Contact Information page and select one or more of the provided options for contact information.
4. Answer the Medical History question by selecting one or more of the provided medical conditions.
5. If you have any medical conditions, please select the relevant options from the Medical Conditions page.
6. On the Medication Information page, select any medications you are currently taking.
7. If you have any medical conditions, please select the relevant options from the Medical Conditions page.
8. On the MRI Information page, enter the date and time of your scheduled MRI procedure.
9. Finally, list your emergency contacts on the Emergency Contacts page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information**: This page provides space for you to enter any general information that the healthcare provider may need to know about you.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Contact Information**: Choose one or more of the provided options for your contact information. This will help us ensure that we can reach you or your emergency contact in case of any issues during the MRI procedure.
* **Medical History**: Select one or more of the provided medical conditions that you have been diagnosed with.
* **Medical Conditions**: If you have any medical conditions, please select the relevant options from this list.
* **Medication Information**: Choose any medications you are currently taking.
* **MRI Information**: Enter the date and time of your scheduled MRI procedure.
* **Emergency Contacts**: List your emergency contacts, choosing one or more of the provided options.
* **Contact Information 2**: This page is similar to the first Contact Information page, but please note that it's optional.
* **Medical Condition Info**: This is a text field where you can enter any additional information about your medical conditions.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions thoroughly and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
* If you have any concerns or questions about the form, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
