# Psychotherapy Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Psychotherapy Intake Form is designed to collect essential information from clients who are seeking psychotherapy services. This form helps therapists gather relevant details about the client's personal and medical history, which will aid in creating a safe and effective treatment plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer all required fields marked as "required".
2. Enter your birthdate in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Describe any medical conditions you are currently experiencing.
4. List any medications you are taking.
5. Mention any allergies you have.
6. Indicate if you have any emergency contacts that the therapist should reach out to in case of an emergency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information (Client Information)** (`client_info`, text, required): This is where you should provide your name and any other identification information that may be relevant to your treatment.
* **Birthdate (Birthdate)** (`birthdate`, date, required): Please enter your birthdate in the MM/DD/YYYY format. This will help the therapist understand your age and plan your treatment accordingly.
* **Medical Conditions (Medical Conditions)** (`medical_conditions`, text, required): Describe any medical conditions you are currently experiencing, including any chronic or acute conditions, injuries, or illnesses.
* **Medications (Medications)** (`medications`, text, required): List all medications you are currently taking, including prescription and non-prescription medications, dosages, and frequencies.
* **Allergies (Allergies)** (`allergies`, text, required): Mention any allergies you have, including any severe allergies or sensitivities.
* **Emergency Contacts (Emergency Contacts)** (`emergency_contacts`, select_multiple, required): Check all emergency contacts that you would like the therapist to reach out to in case of an emergency, such as a family member, friend, or emergency contact service.
