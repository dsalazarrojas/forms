# Peripheral Vascular Emergency Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Peripheral Vascular Emergency Assessment form is designed to collect information about a patient's medical history, contact information, and current symptoms and conditions. This form is intended for emergency situations where a quick and accurate assessment of the patient's situation is crucial.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the patient's contact information, including their contact name and number.
*   Provide any relevant medical history or previous medical conditions.
*   List any medications or allergies the patient is currently taking or has taken.
*   Note any symptoms or medical conditions the patient is currently experiencing.
*   Record any relevant vital signs, such as blood pressure, pulse, or oxygen levels.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Patient Info**:
    *   Enter the patient's name, age, and any other relevant demographic information.
*   **Contact Number**:
    *   Enter the patient's contact number, in case we need to reach them or their emergency contact.
*   **Contact Name**:
    *   Enter the name of the person we can contact for emergency purposes.
*   **Emergency Contact**:
    *   Enter the name and contact number of a person we can contact in case of an emergency.
*   **Medical History**:
    *   Describe any previous medical conditions, surgeries, or allergies the patient has.
*   **Symptoms**:
    *   List any symptoms the patient is currently experiencing, such as chest pain or shortness of breath.
*   **Medications**:
    *   List any medications the patient is currently taking, including their dosage and frequency.
*   **Allergies**:
    *   List any allergies the patient has, including any reactions or sensitivities.
*   **Medical Conditions**:
    *   Describe any current medical conditions the patient is experiencing, such as hypertension or diabetes.
*   **Vital Signs**:
    *   Record any vital signs, such as blood pressure, pulse, or oxygen levels.

# Tips
*   Make sure to provide as much information as possible about the patient's medical history and current symptoms.
*   Double-check any medication lists and allergies for accuracy.
*   If you have any doubts or concerns, contact emergency services or medical professionals for assistance.
