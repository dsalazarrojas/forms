# Infection Control Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Infection Control Discharge Form is designed to document the necessary information for the discharge of a patient from care or treatment. This form helps healthcare providers ensure that patients are properly discharged and that their discharge is compliant with infection control measures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Patient Discharge Date** (Field 1) using the date field provided. This is the date when the patient will be discharged from care or treatment.
2. Enter the **Patient Name** (Field 2) in the text field provided. Please enter the full name of the patient.
3. Select the **Infection Control Measures** (Field 3) that were taken for the patient's care. This field allows you to document the measures taken to prevent or control the spread of infection.
   - Antimicrobial stewardship: This refers to the practice of promoting the appropriate use of antimicrobial agents and preventing their misuse.
   - Infection control measures: This refers to the actions taken to prevent or control the spread of infection.
   - Infection prevention and control: This refers to the practices and protocols implemented to prevent the spread of infection.
   - Infection surveillance: This refers to monitoring patients for signs of infection.
   - Patient education: This refers to educating patients on how to prevent the spread of infection.
4. Finally, provide a **Patient Signature** (Field 4) by signing in the designated area. This section is required for patient consent and confirmation of their discharge.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Discharge Date** (Field 1, Date, required/optional): Enter the date of the patient's discharge using the provided date field.
- **Patient Name** (Field 2, Text, required/optional): Enter the full name of the patient.
- **Infection Control Measures** (Field 3, Select Multiple, required/optional): Select all relevant measures that were taken to control or prevent the spread of infection.
- **Patient Signature** (Field 4, Note, required/optional): Provide your signature as the patient, confirming their understanding of their discharge and the infection control measures taken. 

## Tips
- Make sure to review all fields carefully before submitting the form to ensure accurate information is provided.
- If you're unsure about any of the fields, please ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
- This form is designed to help ensure a smooth and safe discharge process.
