# Diabetes Patient Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect comprehensive patient information for diabetes management and care. Please use this form to gather essential details about your patients, including contact and medical history information. This form can be adapted or modified according to your specific needs, as stated in the form's license.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the required information and documentation at hand before proceeding.
2. Review each section carefully to provide accurate and detailed answers.
3. Fill out the form for each patient separately, using a new submission for each individual.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`Patient Information`, Text, Required): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and any other relevant identifying information.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, Text, Required): Provide the patient's address, phone number, and email (if applicable).
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, Text, Optional): Describe the patient's medical history, including any chronic conditions, surgeries, or relevant medical events.
* **Medications and Allergies** (`Medications and Allergies`, Text, Optional): List any prescribed medications the patient is taking, including dosages and frequencies. Also, note any known allergies or sensitivities.
* **Medical Conditions** (`Medical Conditions`, Text, Optional): Record any chronic conditions the patient may have, including diabetes-related conditions like kidney disease or heart disease.
* **Insurance Coverage** (`Insurance Coverage`, Text, Optional): Verify the patient's insurance coverage and coverage type, if applicable.
* **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, Text, Optional): Provide the name, phone number, and relationship with the emergency contact person (e.g., spouse, family member, friend).
* **Relationship with Emergency Contact** (`Relationship with Emergency Contact`, Text, Optional): Specify the nature of the relationship with the emergency contact (e.g., spouse, family member, friend).
* **Medical Team** (`Medical Team`, Text, Optional): List any healthcare professionals currently treating or monitoring the patient, including physicians, endocrinologists, or dietitians.
