# Foot And Ankle Care Center Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register patients at the Foot and Ankle Care Center for medical treatment and follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, providing as much information as you need.
2. Select the contact method that best suits your needs.
3. Enter your contact number and method for other contact methods, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`First name`, `text`, optional): Please enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`Last name`, `text`, optional): Please enter your last name.
* **Date of birth** (`Date of birth`, `date`, optional): Please enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, optional): Please enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, optional): Please enter your email address.
* **Contact method** (`Contact method`, `select_one`, optional): Select your preferred contact method: Mobile, Email, or In-person.
* **Contact number** (`Contact number`, `number`, optional): If you selected In-person as your contact method, enter your contact number.
* **Contact method other** (`Contact method other`, `text`, optional): If you have a different contact method, please enter it here.
* **Contact method other other** (`Contact method other other`, `text`, optional): If you have another contact method, please enter it here.

## Tips

- Please ensure that your information is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the form, feel free to ask a staff member for assistance.
- This form is designed to be completed by patients and should not be used for business or commercial purposes.
