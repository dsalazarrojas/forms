# Specialist Doctor Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Specialist Doctor Access Form is a template used to request an appointment with a specialist doctor. This form allows patients to enter their details and schedule a meeting with a doctor who specializes in their required field.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Request Appointment" button to begin the form.
2. Select a date and time to schedule your appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Appointment** (`patient_request_appointment`, date, required): Enter the date and time you would like to schedule your appointment. Please be as specific as possible, including the year, month, and day of the appointment.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief summary of your medical history, including any relevant information about your health conditions or previous treatments. This will help the specialist doctor understand your background and prepare for your appointment.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact details, including your name, phone number, and email address. This will allow the specialist doctor to contact you with any necessary information or updates about your appointment.

## Tips
* Be specific when entering the date and time for your appointment, as this will help the specialist doctor ensure that they are available to see you.
* Make sure to include any relevant medical information in the "Medical History" field, as this will help the specialist doctor prepare for your appointment.
* Enter accurate contact details to ensure that the specialist doctor can contact you with any necessary information or updates.
