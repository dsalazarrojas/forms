# urgent_care_patient_information - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect critical information from patients or their representatives during urgent care visits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and provide accurate information.
2. If you are unsure about any section, please refer to the explanation provided for that field.
3. Fill out the form completely and accurately, taking your time to ensure you provide the best information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info (1)**: Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and any other relevant information.
* **Contact Info (2)**: Provide the patient's contact information, including their phone number and address.
* **Symptoms (3)**: Describe the patient's symptoms or medical issues that require urgent care.
* **Emergency Contacts (4)**: List the names and contact information of emergency contacts for the patient.
* **Allergies (5)**: Mention any known allergies or medical conditions that could affect the patient's treatment.
* **Consent (6)**: Provide a brief description of the patient's medical condition and the treatment plan.
* **Other Contact Info (7)**: Enter any other relevant contact information for the patient.
* **Visit Details (8)**: Describe the purpose of the visit and any relevant medical history.
* **Medical History (9)**: Describe the patient's medical history, including any allergies or conditions.
* **Insurance Info (10)**: Provide the patient's insurance information, including card number and type.
* **Medical Condition (11)**: Specify if the patient has any known medical condition, e.g. 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Medical Procedure (12)**: Specify if the patient is undergoing any medical procedure, e.g. 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Medication (13)**: Describe any current or recent medication taken by the patient.
* **Dosages (14)**: Provide any dosages or frequencies of medication taken.
* **Medication Allergies (15)**: Specify if the patient has any medication allergies or reactions.
* **Medical History Other (16)**: Describe any other medical history that is not listed elsewhere.
* **Medical Condition Other (17)**: Specify any other known medical condition not listed elsewhere.
* **Medical Procedure Other (18)**: Describe any other medical procedures not listed elsewhere.
* **Medical Procedure Reason (19)**: Describe the reason for any medical procedures.
* **Medical History Reason (20)**: Describe the reason for any medical history not listed elsewhere.
* **Contact Person (21)**: Identify a contact person for the patient.
* **Contact Email (22)**: Enter the patient's email address.
* **Contact Phone (23)**: Enter the patient's contact phone number.
* **Report (24)**: Describe any additional information about the patient's condition.
* **Urgent Care (25)**: Specify the reason for the urgent care visit.

Note that all fields may not be required but are still essential for providing proper care and treatment.
