# new_patient_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "new_patient_questionnaire" form is a crucial tool for collecting and organizing patient information. It helps ensure that new patients can be efficiently registered, and their medical history and contact details are accurately captured.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's **Patient Information** in the "Patient Information" field.
2. Fill out the **Name** field with the patient's full name.
3. Provide the patient's **Date of Birth** in the "Date of Birth" field.
4. Enter the patient's **Phone** number in the "Phone" field.
5. If applicable, fill out the **Email** field with the patient's email address.
6. Share the patient's **Medical History** in the "Medical History" field.
7. List any **Medsications and Allergies** the patient may have.
8. Provide the patient's **Next of Contact** in the "Next of Contact" field.
9. Enter the patient's **Address** in the "Address" field.
10. Provide information on the patient's **Medical Insurance** coverage.
11. Enter details on the patient's **Emergency Contact**, including their name and relationship.
12. Enter the patient's **Emergency Phone** number.
13. If available, fill out the **Emergency Email** field.
14. If applicable, share the patient's **Medical Conditions** and any relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (1)**: This field is for entering the patient's basic information, such as address, phone, and other relevant details.
* **Name (2)**: Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Date of Birth (3)**: Provide the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Phone (4)**: Enter the patient's primary phone number.
* **Email (5)**: Enter the patient's email address if available.
* **Medical History (6)**: Share any relevant medical history, including previous illnesses or treatments.
* **Medsications and Allergies (7)**: List any medications the patient is currently taking, as well as any known allergies.
* **Next of Contact (8)**: Enter the name and relationship of the patient's next of contact, such as a family member or caregiver.
* **Address (9)**: Enter the patient's address.
* **Medical Insurance (10)**: Provide details on the patient's medical insurance coverage, including provider names and numbers.
* **Emergency Contact (11)**: Enter the name and relationship of the patient's emergency contact, such as a family member or caregiver.
* **Emergency Phone (12)**: Enter the patient's emergency contact phone number.
* **Emergency Email (13)**: Enter the emergency contact's email address if available.
* **Medical Conditions (14)**: If applicable, list any medical conditions or concerns the patient may have.
