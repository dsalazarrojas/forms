# medical_history_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect important information about a patient's medical history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (contact_info)**: Enter the patient's contact information, including their name, address, and any other relevant details.
* **Medical History (medical_history)**: Provide a brief description of the patient's medical history.
* **Allergies (allergies)**: Indicate whether the patient has any allergies or not. Select one of the options: "Yes" or "No".
* **Meditations (medications)**: Indicate whether the patient is taking any medications or not. Select one of the options: "Yes" or "No".
* **Medical Conditions (medical_conditions)**: Choose the medical condition(s) that applies to the patient. Select one of the options: "Yes" or "No".
* **Medical Procedures (medical_procedures)**: Indicate whether the patient has undergone any medical procedures or not. Select one of the options: "Yes" or "No" (this field is optional).
* **Medical History Date (medical_history_date)**: Enter the date of the patient's medical history (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Medical History Time (medical_history_time)**: Enter the time of the patient's medical history (HH:MM AM/PM).

## Tips
* Please ensure that the patient's information is accurate and up-to-date.
* If the patient has any medical conditions, be sure to ask for additional information about the condition.
* If the patient has undergone any medical procedures, ask for details about the procedure.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all fields are completed correctly.
