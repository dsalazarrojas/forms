# Mental Health Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Health Intake Form is used to collect patient information for mental health services. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the corresponding field. You can type directly into the text boxes or select from the options provided.
2. Provide as much detail as possible to ensure accurate and comprehensive patient records.
3. Please review the form before submitting to ensure all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient medical history 1**: This is a text field for you to enter any relevant medical information about yourself.
* **Patient medical history 2**: This is another text field for additional medical information about yourself.
* **Family medical history 1**: Please enter any relevant medical history about your family.
* **Supporting documents**: Select one or more options that apply to you from the provided list.
* **Fee payments 1**: This is a numeric field for you to enter any fee information related to your care.
* **Patient data 1**: This is a note field for any additional information about yourself.
* **Patient data 2**: Another note field for any further information about yourself.

## Tips
- If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to our staff for assistance.
- Please do not submit incomplete or duplicate records.
- Ensure you have reviewed all fields before submitting the form.
