# Medical Review Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Review Submission Form is designed to collect information from patients or healthcare providers for medical reviews.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the form fields as instructed.
2. Enter your personal information in the "patient_info" field.
3. Provide your medical history in the "medical_history" field.
4. Specify your diagnosis in the "diagnosis" field.
5. Outline your treatment plan in the "treatment_plan" field.
6. Enter your doctor's name in the "doctor_name" field.
7. Indicate whether you have a medical specialty in the "medical_specialty" field.
8. Choose the date of your medical history in the "medical_history_date" field.
9. Enter the date of your review submission in the "review_submission_date" field.
10. If applicable, provide your medical license number in the "medical_license" field.
