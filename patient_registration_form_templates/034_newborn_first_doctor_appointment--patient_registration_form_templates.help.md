# Newborn First Doctor Appointment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Newborn First Doctor Appointment form is used to record the first doctor visit of a newborn baby, capturing essential information about their health and development at birth or during the initial visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the baby's first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter the baby's last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter the baby's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
4. Enter the baby's weight in the "Weight" field.
5. Enter the baby's height in the "Height" field.
6. Enter the baby's head circumference measurement in the "Head Circumference" field.
7. Enter the baby's body length in the "Body Length" field.
8. Describe the baby's physical exam results in the "Physical Exam Results" field.
9. Select the baby's motor function status as "Normal", "Abnormal", or indicate if "Not tested" in the "Motor Function" field.
10. Select the baby's reflexes status as "Normal", "Abnormal", or "Not tested" in the "Reflexes" field, and indicate multiple options as necessary.
11. If applicable, enter any other conditions or health issues in the "Other conditions" field.
12. Indicate if the baby has any allergies in the "Allergies" field.
13. Enter any relevant medical history in the "Medical History" field.
14. If necessary, provide information about any medication the baby is taking in the "Medication" field.
15. Enter any blood work or lab results in the "Blood work" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter the baby's first name, which is required for identification purposes.
* **Last Name**: Enter the baby's last name, which is also required for identification purposes.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the date the baby was born.
* **Weight**: Enter the baby's weight in grams (e.g., 3.5 kg).
* **Height**: Enter the baby's height in centimeters (e.g., 50 cm).
* **Head Circumference**: Enter the measurement of the baby's head circumference in centimeters (e.g., 34 cm).
* **Body Length**: Enter the baby's body length in centimeters (e.g., 50 cm).
* **Physical Exam Results**: Describe the results of the baby's physical examination, including any notable findings or concerns.
* **Motor Function**: Select "Normal" if the baby's motor functions are functioning normally, "Abnormal" if they are not, or indicate "Not tested" if not performed.
* **Reflexes**: Select "Normal", "Abnormal", or "Not tested" for each of the baby's reflexes, and indicate multiple options as necessary.
* **Other conditions**: If the baby has any other conditions or health issues, enter them here.
* **Allergies**: Indicate if the baby has any allergies, selecting "True" or "False".
* **Medical History**: Enter any relevant medical history for the baby, including previous illnesses or conditions.
* **Medication**: If the baby is taking any medication, enter the details here.
* **Blood work**: Enter any blood work or lab results for the baby, including test dates and results.
