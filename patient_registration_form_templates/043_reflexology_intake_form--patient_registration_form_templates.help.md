# Reflexology Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from patients before their reflexology session. It includes fields for patient registration, appointment scheduling, medical history, and health information.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth and effective reflexology experience.
- Make sure to answer each question to the best of your abilities, and don't hesitate to ask if you're unsure about any field.
- If you need to provide additional information or clarification, you can contact us at form@gic.mx.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Patient Registration Form**
  **Name**, **Phone Number**, **Email** (`text`, required)
  Enter your name, phone number, and email address to allow us to contact you and schedule your session efficiently.

### 2. **Schedule Appointment**
  **Appointment Date**, **Time** (`date`, required)
  Please select a date and time for your reflexology appointment. Be sure to pick a date and time that works best for you, and we'll do our best to accommodate your schedule.

### 3. **Medical History**
  **Medical History** (`text`, optional)
  If you have any existing medical conditions, injuries, or allergies, please provide a brief description so we can take necessary precautions.

### 4. **Health Information**
  **Health Information** (`text`, optional)
  Share any relevant health information, such as medications, recent illnesses, or previous medical procedures, to help us tailor your reflexology experience.

## Tips
- Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us at form@gic.mx.
- This form is for reflexology services only; if you're seeking other healthcare services, please contact the relevant service provider directly.
