# Eye Clinic Patient Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Eye Clinic Patient Registration Form is designed to gather essential information from patients for their registration and appointment scheduling purposes. This form serves as a tool for the clinic to collect patient details, medical history, and appointment requests, making it easier to manage patient care and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your full name in the "Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. In the "Emergency Name" field, provide the name of the person you would like us to contact in case of an emergency.
5. Enter the phone number of the emergency contact in the "Phone" field.
6. In the "Relationship" field, describe your relationship with the emergency contact.
7. Select the type(s) of appointment you are requesting from the "Appointment Request" dropdown menu. Options include:
	* Appointment Type
	* Consultation
	* Exam
	* Procedure
	* Surgery
	* Other
8. Write a brief summary of your medical history in the "Medical History" field. This will help our staff better understand your medical background and provide more effective care.
9. In the "Next of Kin" field, provide the name of the person you would like us to contact for support or updates about your care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`full_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your full name.
* **Email** (`patient_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address, which will be used for communication and appointment notifications.
* **Phone** (`patient_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number, which will be used for communication and emergency contact.
* **Name** (`emergency_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person you would like us to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_relationship`, text, required/optional): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact, e.g., spouse, parent, or guardian.
* **Appointment Request** (`appointment_request`, select_multiple, required): Select the type(s) of appointment you are requesting:
	+ Appointment Type
	+ Consultation
	+ Exam
	+ Procedure
	+ Surgery
	+ Other
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): Write a brief summary of your medical history, including any previous medical conditions, allergies, or surgeries.
* **Next of Kin** (`next_of_kin`, text, required/optional): Provide the name of the person you would like us to contact for support or updates about your care.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please describe them in the "Medical History" field.
* If you would like to request a specific appointment type, please select all relevant options in the "Appointment Request" dropdown menu.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask our staff for assistance.
