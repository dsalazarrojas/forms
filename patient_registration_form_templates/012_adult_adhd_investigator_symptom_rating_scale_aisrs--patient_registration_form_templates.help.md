# Adult ADHD Investigator Symptom Rating Scale - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adult ADHD Investigator Symptom Rating Scale is a tool used to assess the severity of symptoms associated with Adult Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD). This form helps clinicians evaluate and rate various symptoms, providing a total score to aid in diagnosis and treatment planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's full name, which is required for identification purposes.
2. Enter the patient's ID or medical record number, if available.
3. Select the assessment date, which is the date the symptom evaluation is being conducted.
4. Enter the patient's date of birth, if available.
5. Enter your name as the clinician conducting the assessment.
6. Read and understand the rating instructions provided under the "Rating Instructions" field.
7. For each symptom rating field, select the severity of the symptom experienced by the patient on a scale from 0 (Not Present) to 3 (Severe).
8. For symptoms with an approximate onset age, estimate the age when the symptoms began.
9. If applicable, report the duration of symptoms in months.
10. Indicate if symptoms cause functional impairment at home, work, or social situations.
11. Calculate the total score by summing the ratings for all 11 symptom fields.
