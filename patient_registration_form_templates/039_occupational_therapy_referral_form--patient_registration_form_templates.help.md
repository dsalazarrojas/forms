# Occupational Therapy Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to refer patients to an occupational therapist for therapy services. The form collects essential information about the patient, including demographics, referral source, therapy goals, medical history, insurance provider, treatment duration, therapist name, referral date, referral status, and provider note.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have the patient's consent to complete this form.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely, using the provided guidelines.
3. Select the relevant options for each field.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm its completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the patient's date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Referral Source** (`referral_source`, select_one, optional): Select the source of the referral (Doctor, Nurse, or Family Member).
* **Therapy Goals** (`therapy_goals`, text, optional): Describe the reasons for the referral in this field.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Describe any medical history or conditions that may impact treatment.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, text, optional): Enter the patient's insurance provider.
* **Treatment Duration** (`treatment_duration`, number, optional): Enter the expected duration of treatment.
* **Therapist Name** (`therapist_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the therapist.
* **Referral Date** (`referral_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the referral in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Referral Status** (`referral_status`, select_one, optional): Select the current status of the referral (New, Pending, or Completed).
* **Provider Note** (`provider_note`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about the patient.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, note, optional): Obtain the patient's signature to confirm their understanding and agreement with the referral.
