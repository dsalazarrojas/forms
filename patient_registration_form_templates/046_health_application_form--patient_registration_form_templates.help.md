# health_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The health_application_form is used to gather essential details about patients, including their name, contact information, and medical history, to provide proper medical care services.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, please carefully fill in all the required fields.
2.  Ensure that all information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
3.  For fields that are not required, you may leave them blank if not applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your active email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your current phone number for communication and follow-up.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your current address for scheduling appointments and sending information.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): If applicable, enter your date of birth for age-related medical care.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): If you have any pre-existing medical conditions, provide a brief description here.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): If applicable, provide the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
* **Tips:** Please ensure that all information provided is accurate and up-to-date to ensure proper care. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask your healthcare provider.
