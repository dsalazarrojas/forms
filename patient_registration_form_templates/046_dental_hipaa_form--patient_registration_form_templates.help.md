# dental_hipaa_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The dental_hipaa_form is a patient registration form used for HIPAA compliance in dental care settings. It is essential to fill out this form to register a patient and ensure that their information is collected, stored, and handled according to the law.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Start by entering the patient's name in the **patient_name** field.
2.  Next, fill in the patient's address in the **address** field.
3.  Enter the patient's birth date using the date picker in the **birth_date** field.
4.  Finally, enter the patient's contact number in the **contact_number** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_name**: Enter the patient's full name (first name, last name, and any middle name).
* **address**: Enter the patient's address, including the street number and name, city, state, and zip code.
* **birth_date**: Enter the patient's birth date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **contact_number**: Enter the patient's contact phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
* If the patient has multiple contact numbers, separate each number with a comma.
* Double-check all fields before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
