# Patient Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Assessment Form is designed to collect data from patients and aid healthcare professionals in making informed decisions about their care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's information, including their name and ID (if available).
2. Select the appropriate assessment level for the patient's condition (Severe, Moderate, or Mild).
3. Note any relevant medical history that may be contributing to the patient's condition.
4. Select the symptoms the patient is experiencing (Fatigue, Headache, Cough, etc.).
5. Enter any test results or lab values that are relevant to the patient's condition.
6. Enter any diagnosis or suspected diagnosis for the patient.
7. Select any treatment options that have been prescribed or recommended for the patient (Physical Therapy, Medication, Surgery, etc.).
8. Enter the follow-up date for the patient's assessment.
9. Confirm whether follow-up is required ('Yes' or 'No').
10. Enter the patient's ID for easy reference.
11. Enter the doctor's name and contact number (if available).
12. Enter the assessment date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (1)** (optional): Enter the patient's name and ID.
* **Assessment (2)** (optional): Select the severity of the patient's condition (Severe, Moderate, or Mild).
* **Medical History (3)** (optional): Enter any relevant medical history that may be contributing to the patient's condition.
* **Symptoms (4)** (optional): Select any symptoms the patient is experiencing (e.g., Fatigue, Headache, Cough, etc.).
* **Test Results (5)** (optional): Enter any test results or lab values that are relevant to the patient's condition.
* **Diagnosis (6)** (optional): Enter any diagnosis or suspected diagnosis for the patient.
* **Treatment (7)** (optional): Select any treatment options that have been prescribed or recommended for the patient (Physical Therapy, Medication, Surgery, etc.).
* **Follow-up (8)** (optional): Enter the follow-up date for the patient's assessment.
* **Notes (9)** (optional): Confirm whether follow-up is required ('Yes' or 'No').
* **Patient ID (10)** (optional): Enter the patient's ID for easy reference.
* **Doctor Name (11)** (optional): Enter the doctor's name and contact number (if available).
* **Assessment Date (12)** (optional): Enter the date of the patient's assessment.
* **Contact No (13)** (optional): Enter the contact number of the doctor or healthcare provider.
