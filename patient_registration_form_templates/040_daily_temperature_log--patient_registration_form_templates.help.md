# Daily Temperature Log - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Daily Temperature Log form is used to collect and record patient temperature readings for early identification of potential health issues.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the patient's name in the `patient_name` field.
2. Enter the patient's age in the `patient_age` field (optional).
3. Record the patient's temperature reading in the `temperature` field.
4. Enter the name of the healthcare staff member who took the reading in the `recorded_by` field (optional).
5. Select the date the reading was taken in the `recorded_on` field.
6. Enter the name of the healthcare staff member who completed the form in the `form_completed_by` field (optional).
7. Enter the start time of the healthcare staff member's shift in the `shift_start_time` field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_name**: Enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical records.
* **patient_age**: Enter the patient's age (optional).
* **temperature**: Record the patient's current body temperature in degrees Celsius (°C).
* **recorded_by**: Enter the name of the healthcare staff member who took the reading.
* **recorded_on**: Select the date the reading was taken.
* **staff_name**: Enter the name of the healthcare staff member who completed the form.
* **shift_start_time**: Enter the start time of the healthcare staff member's shift (optional).
* **form_completed_by**: Enter the name of the healthcare staff member who completed the form.
 
## Tips
- Ensure accurate and reliable temperature readings are recorded for each patient.
- Complete the form for each patient daily.
- Review and act upon any abnormal temperature readings promptly.
