# hospital_admission_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The hospital admission form is a crucial document used to collect essential patient information for medical staff to provide proper care and treatment. This form helps to gather personal details, medical history, and contact information to ensure the patient's treatment is tailored to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to understand what information is required and what sections are optional.
2. Enter the patient's personal details, including name, date of birth, and contact information.
3. Provide medical history, including any allergies, medical conditions, and previous medical treatments.
4. Specify the patient's relationship with the hospital and any emergency contact information.
5. Complete the medical specialty and hospital department sections to ensure accurate referral to the right specialist.
6. Provide the doctor's contact information, including their name, phone number, and specialty.
7. Enter the admission and discharge dates, if applicable.
8. Sign and date the form to confirm the patient's consent and agreement to the terms of treatment.

* Review the form with a medical professional if unsure about any section.

* Keep the form up-to-date and current to reflect any changes in the patient's condition or treatment.
