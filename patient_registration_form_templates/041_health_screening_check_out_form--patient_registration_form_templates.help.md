# Health Screening Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Screening Check Out Form is a medical form used to gather comprehensive information about a patient's health status, medical history, and discharge instructions for follow-up care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and answer all questions truthfully.
2. Enter the patient's information in the corresponding fields.
3. For each field, ensure the correct information is provided, such as dates, times, and medical conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Patient Information (<patient_info>)
* **Patient Information** (optional): This field is for the patient's general details, such as name, address, and contact information.

### 2. Medical History (<medical_history>)
* **Medical History** (optional): Select all relevant medical conditions, such as Heart and Lung conditions.

### 3. Health Issues (<health_issues>)
* **Health Issues** (optional): Select the patient's current health issues, such as Diabetes and Heart Conditions.

### 4. Medication List (<medication_list>)
* **Medication List** (optional): Enter the patient's current medications.

### 5. Medical Notes (<medical_notes>)
* **Medical Notes** (optional): Enter any additional medical notes about the patient's health.

### 6. Discharge Information (<discharge_info>)
* **Discharge Information** (optional): Select the discharge location, such as Home or Hospital.

### 7. Follow-up Care (<follow_up_care>)
* **Follow-up Care** (optional): Select the follow-up care frequency, such as 0 Days or 1 Week.

### 8. Contact Information (<contact_info>)
* **Contact Information** (optional): Select the contact method, such as Phone or Email.

### 9. Additional Information (<additional_info>)
* **Additional Information** (optional): Select all relevant information, such as Family History and Social History.

### 10. Allergies (<allergies>)
* **Allergies** (optional): Select all allergies, such as Penicillin.

### 11. Additional Medical History (<medical_history_additional>)
* **Additional Medical History** (optional): Select the additional medical history status, such as Yes or No.

### 12. Other Medical History (<medical_history_other>)
* **Other Medical History** (optional): Enter any other medical history information.

### 13. Other Medical History Notes (<medical_history_other_notes>)
* **Other Medical History Notes** (optional): Enter any additional medical history notes.

### 14. Other Patient Information (<patient_info_other>)
* **Other Patient Information** (optional): Enter any other patient information.

### 15. Other Patient Information Notes (<patient_info_other_notes>)
* **Other Patient Information Notes** (optional): Enter any additional patient information notes.

### 16. Discharge Date (<discharge_date>)
* **Discharge Date** (optional): Enter the discharge date.

### 17. Discharge Time (<discharge_time>)
* **Discharge Time** (optional): Enter the discharge time.

### 18. Follow-up Date (<follow_up_date>)
* **Follow-up Date** (optional): Enter the follow-up date.

### 19. Follow-up Time (<follow_up_time>)
* **Follow-up Time** (optional): Enter the follow-up time.

### 20. Additional Medical History Date (<medical_history_additional_date>)

### 21. Additional Medical History Time (<medical_history_additional_time>)

### 22. Additional Medical History Notes (<medical_history_additional_notes>)
* **Additional Medical History Notes** (optional): Enter any additional medical history notes.

## Tips
* Please review the form carefully before completing it.
* Answer all questions truthfully.
