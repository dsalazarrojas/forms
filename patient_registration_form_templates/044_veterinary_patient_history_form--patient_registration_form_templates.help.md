# Veterinary Patient History Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Patient History Form is a document used to collect and record essential information about a patient's identity, medical history, and current condition. This form helps veterinary professionals to gather detailed insights into a patient's breed, age, weight, and primary diagnoses, enabling accurate and effective treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Start by providing the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
*   Enter the owner's name and contact information in the "Owner Name" and "Owner Contact Info" fields respectively.
*   Enter the patient's breed in the "Patient Breed" field.
*   Provide the patient's age and weight in the "Patient Age" and "Patient Weight" fields, respectively.
*   Enter the patient's date of birth using the "Date of Birth" field.
*   Describe the primary medical diagnoses in the "Primary Diagnoses" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Name (Text):** Enter the patient's name in this field. This is a required field for patient identification.
*   **Owner Name (Text):** Enter the name of the patient's owner or caregiver.
*   **Owner Contact Info (Text):** Enter the owner's contact information for follow-up and communication purposes.
*   **Patient Breed (Text):** Describe the patient's breed.
*   **Patient Age (Number):** Enter the patient's age in years.
*   **Patient Weight (Number):** Enter the patient's weight in kilograms.
*   **Date of Birth (Date):** Enter the patient's date of birth.
*   **Primary Diagnoses (Text):** Describe the patient's primary medical condition or diagnosis.

## Tips

*   Make sure to complete the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure proper patient care and treatment.
*   If you are unsure about any information, please consult with the veterinarian or patient care team.
*   This form is for one-time use, please ensure that you have not already filled out this form for the same patient before.
