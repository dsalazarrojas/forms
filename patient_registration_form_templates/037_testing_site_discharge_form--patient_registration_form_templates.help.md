# Testing Site Discharge Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Testing Site Discharge Form is a template used for documenting a patient's visit and providing discharge instructions after an on-site medical testing procedure. This form helps ensure that patients receive proper care and guidance upon leaving the testing site.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide patient information in the "Patient Information" section.
2. Enter the discharge instructions in the "Discharge Instructions" section.
3. Select the date of the site visit in the "Site Visit Date" section.
4. Specify the time of the site visit in the "Site Visit Time" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`Patient Information`, text, **required: false**): This section is used to collect basic patient information, such as name, contact details, or any other relevant data that may be required for follow-up or future reference.
* **Discharge Instructions** (`Discharge Instructions`, text, **required: false**): Enter any specific instructions given to the patient upon discharge, such as medication regimens, follow-up appointment schedules, or any other important information to ensure a smooth recovery or further care.
* **Site Visit Date** (`Site Visit Date`, date, **required: false**): Choose the date of the site visit from the calendar, and select the correct date to record the visit's occurrence.
* **Site Visit Time** (`Site Visit Time`, time, **required: false**): Choose the time of the site visit to specify the exact time the testing procedure took place. 

## Tips
- Be sure to double-check all input fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
- In case of any discrepancy or issue, contact your healthcare provider or testing site staff for guidance.
- This form is meant to be used for general purpose only; consult your local medical regulations for specific guidance on patient discharge procedures.
- Review and update form fields as necessary to fit your testing site's specific needs.
