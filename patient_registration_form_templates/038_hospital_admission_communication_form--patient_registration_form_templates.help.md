# Hospital Admission Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospital Admission Communication Form is designed to collect information from patients about their medical history, contact details, and other relevant medical information in a hospital admission setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your patient's **Patient Information** on page 1, including any relevant details about their medical history.
2. Answer the **Medical History** question on page 1 to indicate whether they have a medical history.
3. Enter your contact details, including any emergency contact information, on page 3.
4. Provide any **Contact Details** for your patient on page 4.
5. List any **Emergency Contacts** for your patient on page 5.
6. Enter any **Medical Information** relevant to your patient's care on page 6.
7. Provide any **Allergy Information** on page 7.
8. List any **Medication Information** relevant to your patient's care on page 8.
9. Provide any **Family History** information on page 9.
10. Enter any **Insurance Information** relevant to your patient's care on page 10.
11. Answer the **Medical Conditions** question on page 11 to indicate whether your patient has certain medical conditions.
12. Enter any **Medical Test Results** on page 12.
13. List any **Medical Procedures** relevant to your patient's care on page 13.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (page 1)** (`patient_info`, text, required: false): Enter your patient's name, date of birth, and any other relevant information.
* **Medical History (page 1)** (`medical_history`, select, required: false): Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you have a medical history.
* **Contact Details (page 3)** (`contact_details`, text, required: false): Enter your name, phone number, and email address.
* **Emergency Contacts (page 4)** (`emergency_contacts`, text, required: false): List any emergency contacts with their name, relationship, phone number, and email address.
* **Medical Information (page 5)** (`medical_info`, text, required: false): Enter any relevant medical information about your patient.
* **Allergy Information (page 6)** (`allergy_info`, text, required: false): List any allergies your patient has, including the medication or substance.
* **Medication Information (page 7)** (`medication_info`, text, required: false): List any medications your patient is currently taking.
* **Family History (page 8)** (`family_history`, text, required: false): Provide any relevant family medical history information.
* **Insurance Information (page 9)** (`insurance_info`, text, required: false): Enter any insurance information relevant to your patient's care.
* **Medical Conditions (page 10)** (`medical_conditions`, select, required: false): Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether your patient has certain medical conditions.
* **Medical Test Results (page 11)** (`medical_test_results`, text, required: false): Enter any relevant medical test results.
* **Medical Procedures (page 12)** (`medical_procedures`, text, required: false): List any relevant medical procedures your patient has undergone.

## Tips

* Ensure you fill out all required fields.
* If a field is not required, fill it out if it's relevant to your patient's care.
* Double-check the accuracy of all information entered.
* Save and print the completed form for your patient's file.
