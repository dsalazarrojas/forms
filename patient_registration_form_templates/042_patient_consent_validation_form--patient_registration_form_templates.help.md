# Patient Consent Validation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by healthcare providers to obtain patient consent for medical procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate medical procedure that the patient is consenting to.
2. Enter the date of consent.
3. Indicate whether the patient has consented to treatment by a healthcare provider.
4. Indicate whether the patient has consented to treatment at a medical facility.
5. Enter the patient's name.
6. Enter the patient's age.
7. Enter the patient's address.
8. Enter the patient's phone number.
9. Enter the patient's email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Consent Validation Form** (Select Multiple):
	+ Select all applicable medical procedures that you are asking the patient to consent to.
* **Medical Procedure** (Select One):
	+ Select the specific medical procedure that the patient is consenting to.
* **Consent Date** (Date):
	+ Enter the date of the patient's consent.
* **Healthcare Provider** (Select One):
	+ Indicate whether the patient has consented to treatment by a healthcare provider.
* **Medical Facility** (Select One):
	+ Indicate whether the patient has consented to treatment at a medical facility.
* **Patient Name** (Text):
	+ Enter the patient's name.
* **Patient Age** (Number):
	+ Enter the patient's age.
* **Patient Address** (Text):
	+ Enter the patient's address.
* **Patient Phone** (Text):
	+ Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Patient Email** (Email):
	+ Enter the patient's email address.

## Tips

* Make sure to select all applicable medical procedures that the patient is consenting to.
* Enter the date of consent correctly.
* If the patient has not consented to treatment by a healthcare provider, indicate "No".
* If the patient has not consented to treatment at a medical facility, indicate "No".
