# Medical Report Clarification Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Report Clarification Request Form is designed to gather information from patients or their representatives regarding medical reports that require clarification. The form is used to request additional details or corrections to a specific medical report.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form with the requested information.
2. Make sure all required fields are filled in, denoted by \*.
3. Review the form before submitting it to ensure that you have provided accurate information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Form** (request_form, text, optional): A short description of the requested clarification.
	+ This field is optional, but it helps to provide context for the medical report.
* **Patient Name** (patient_name, text, optional): The name of the patient who is requesting the clarification.
	+ This field is used to identify the patient and ensure that the clarification request is related to their specific medical history.
* **Date of Birth** (patient_dob, date, optional): The date of birth of the patient.
	+ This field is optional but helps in identifying the patient's age and medical history.
* **Medical Report** (medical_report, text, optional): A detailed description of the medical report that requires clarification.
	+ This field is optional, but it helps to provide context for the clarification request.
* **Medical Report Date** (medical_report_date, date, optional): The date the medical report was issued.
	+ This field is used to track when the medical report was created.
* **Medical Report Provider** (medical_report_provider, text, optional): The name of the healthcare provider who issued the medical report.
	+ This field is optional, but it helps to identify who created the medical report.
* **Request for Clarification** (medical_report_clarification_request, text, optional): A detailed description of what clarification is needed.
	+ This field is optional, but it helps to provide context for the clarification request.
* **Patient Signature** (patient_signature, note, required): A signature from the patient or their representative, confirming that the information is accurate and complete.
	+ This field is required to confirm that the patient or their representative has reviewed and agreed to the information provided.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure that your clarification request is processed correctly.
* Double-check the information provided to ensure that it is accurate and complete.
* If you need to make a request for clarification, please contact your healthcare provider directly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
