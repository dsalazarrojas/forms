# healthcareDigitalTwinResearchRegistration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for patient registration, specifically for research purposes. It gathers general information, contact details, medical history, and other relevant data to facilitate research registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **General Information** page with your personal details.
2. Select your **Contact Information** options (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
3. Provide your **Medical History** in the note field.
4. Indicate your **Research Consent** (Yes or No).
5. Enter your **Participant Details** (a numerical value).
6. Select the **Contact Person** (if applicable).
7. Enter your **Email** address.
8. Choose your **Submission** options (Yes or No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (Required: False, Type: text): This is the first page of the form where you will be asked to provide your personal details.
* **Contact Information** (Required: False, Type: select_multiple): Select one or more options for how you would like to be contacted (e.g., email, phone, or both).
* **Medical History** (Required: False, Type: note): Describe your medical history in this field.
* **Research Consent** (Required: False, Type: select_one): Indicate whether you consent to participate in research (Yes or No).
* **Participant Details** (Required: False, Type: number): Enter a numerical value to provide more details about your participation.
* **Contact Person** (Required: False, Type: text): If applicable, provide the name and contact information of the person who can contact you.
* **Email** (Required: False, Type: email): Enter your email address to enable communication.
* **Submission** (Required: False, Type: select_multiple): Choose whether you would like to submit your registration (Yes or No).
