# Patient Medical History Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Medical History Form is a document used to record a patient's medical history, including personal details and medical conditions. This form helps healthcare providers gather information about the patient's health status to assess and provide the best course of treatment.
## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in the patient's contact information, including phone and email (if applicable) in fields 12 and 13.
- Fill in the patient's personal details in field 1, including the title (e.g., "Mr.", "Ms.", etc.), name, and address.
- Select the type of medical provider (e.g., Dr., Prof., Pharmacist, or Other) in field 11.
- Provide information about any medical conditions, operations, or healthy and unhealthy habits in fields 3, 4, 6, and 7.
- Fill in the date and time of the patient's last medical checkup in fields 8 and 9, if applicable.
- Add any additional notes about the patient's medical history in field 10.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Personal Information (1)** (Text, Optional): Enter the patient's personal details, including title (e.g., "Mr.", "Ms.", etc.), name, and address.
- **Weight (2)** (Number, Optional): Record the patient's weight.
- **Allergies (3)** (Text, Optional): List any allergies the patient has, such as medication or substance allergies.
- **Illnesses (4)** (Text, Optional): Describe any medical conditions or illnesses the patient has.
- **Operations (5)** (Text, Optional): Record any surgeries or medical procedures the patient has undergone.
- **Healthy Habits (6)** (Text, Optional): Describe any healthy habits or activities the patient engages in regularly.
- **Unhealthy Habits (7)** (Text, Optional): Describe any unhealthy habits or activities the patient engages in, such as smoking or excessive drinking.
- **Date of Last Medical Checkup (8)** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of the patient's last medical checkup, if applicable.
- **Time of Last Medical Checkup (9)** (Time, Optional): Enter the time of the last medical checkup, if applicable.
- **Note (10)** (Note, Optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the patient's medical history.
- **Medical Provider (11)** (Select One, Required): Select the type of medical provider (e.g., Dr., Prof., Pharmacist, or Other).
- **Phone (12)** (Text, Optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
- **Email (13)** (Email, Optional): Enter the patient's email address.
