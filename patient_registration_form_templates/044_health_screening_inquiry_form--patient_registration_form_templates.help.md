# health-screening-inquiry-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect general information and specific medical details from patients. It is intended for health care professionals to gather essential data for patient screening and assessment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your general information, including your name and contact details, in the "General Info" section.
2. Provide information about your medical history, including any screenings you have undergone, in the "Medical History" section.
3. Answer "Yes" or "No" to the question about screenings in the "Screenings" section.
4. List any medical conditions you have in the "Medical Conditions" section.
5. Mention any allergies you have in the "Allergies" section.
6. List any medications you are currently taking in the "Medications" section.
7. Provide information about your medical professionals in the "Medical Professionals" section.
8. List any family members with medical conditions in the "Family Members" section.
9. Provide any relevant medical records in the "Medical Records" section.
10. Enter your patient history in the "Patient History" section.
11. Enter your Social Security number (if applicable) in the "Social Security" section.
12. If you have medical records from alternative sources, provide them in the sections "Medical Professionals Alt", "Medical Record Alt 1" to "Medical Record Alt 11".
