# tb_screening_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for conducting a thorough screening and assessment of patients. It is designed to gather relevant information about a patient's medical history, symptoms, and other relevant details to aid in their diagnosis and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your patient's information in the "Patient Info" field.
2. Provide details about your patient's exposure history, including any potential exposure to contagious diseases, in the "Exposure History" field.
3. Select any symptoms that your patient is experiencing from the dropdown list in the "Symptoms" field.
4. Enter any relevant medical history for your patient in the "Medical History" field.
5. List any medication allergies your patient has in the "Medication Allergies" field.
6. Provide any relevant test results for your patient in the "Test Results" field.
7. Enter any medical treatment your patient is currently undergoing in the "Medical Treatment" field.
8. Provide any other relevant medical history for your patient in the "Other Medical History" field.
9. Enter any relevant medical family history for your patient in the "Medical Family History" field.
10. Provide any relevant medical social history for your patient in the "Medical Social History" field.
11. Enter any relevant medical travel history for your patient in the "Medical Travel History" field.
12. Provide any other relevant information not covered in the previous fields in the "Other Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Info
* **Patient Info (text):** Enter your patient's name, date of birth, and contact information here.

### Exposure History
* **Exposure History (text):** Describe any potential exposure to contagious diseases or other health risks that your patient may have experienced. This could include travel history, exposure to sick individuals, or other relevant factors.

### Symptoms
* **Symptoms (select multiple):** Check any of the following symptoms that your patient is experiencing:
	+ Fever
	+ Coughing
	+ Shortness of breath
	+ Fatigue
	+ Night sweats
	+ Weight loss
	+ Loss of appetite

### Medical History
* **Medical History (text):** Enter any relevant medical history for your patient, including past illnesses, surgeries, or other health concerns.

### Medication Allergies
* **Medication Allergies (text):** List any medications that your patient is allergic to or cannot take.

### Test Results
* **Test Results (text):** Enter any relevant test results or lab reports for your patient.

### Medical Treatment
* **Medical Treatment (text):** Describe any medical treatment your patient is currently undergoing.

### Other Medical History
* **Other Medical History (text):** Enter any other relevant medical history for your patient that doesn't fit in the previous fields.

### Medical Family History
* **Medical Family History (text):** Describe any relevant medical history in your patient's family, including any genetic predispositions or family member's medical conditions.

### Medical Social History
* **Medical Social History (text):** Enter any relevant social history for your patient, including living situation, occupation, or other relevant information.

### Medical Travel History
* **Medical Travel History (text):** Describe any recent travel history for your patient, including destinations and dates.

### Other Info
* **Other Info (text):** Provide any other relevant information about your patient that doesn't fit in the previous fields.
