# New Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Client Intake Form is designed to collect essential information from new clients in a healthcare setting. This form helps gather personal details, medical history, and emergency contact information to provide the best possible care for the client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first and last name in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide your email address and phone number.
3. Enter your address.
4. Fill in your birth date.
5. If applicable, provide a brief description of your medical history.
6. Enter your emergency contact's name and phone number.
7. Describe your emergency contact's address (if applicable).
8. If you have medical insurance, provide the necessary information.
9. If you have a medical condition, provide a brief description.
10. If required, provide your doctor's recommendation and name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
- **E-mail** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
- **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your address.
- **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, required): Enter your birth date (mm/dd/yyyy).
- **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): If you have a medical condition, describe it here.
- **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
- **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
- **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of your emergency contact.
- **Insurance** (`medical_insurance`, text, optional): If you have medical insurance, provide the necessary information.
- **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): If you have a medical condition, provide a brief description.
- **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
- **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
- **Next of Kin** (`next_of_kin`, text, optional): Enter the name of your next of kin.
- **Signature** (`signature`, note, optional): This is a text field for your digital signature.
- **Medical History Description** (`medical_history_description`, text, optional): If you have a medical history, provide a brief description.
- **Medical Condition Description** (`medical_condition_description`, text, optional): If you have a medical condition, provide a brief description.
- **Doctor Recommendation** (`doctor_recommendation`, text, optional): If your doctor has recommended any treatment, provide the details.
- **Doctor Recommendation Name** (`doctor_recommendation_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your doctor who made the recommendation.
- **Doctor Recommendation** (`doctor_recommendation`, text, optional): This field is not used in this form, please use the "Doctor Recommendation Name" field to enter your doctor's name.

## Tips
- Please double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you're unsure about any field, do not hesitate to ask for help.
- This form is designed to be filled out by the client themselves, but please ensure that a healthcare professional reviews and verifies the information before treatment begins.
