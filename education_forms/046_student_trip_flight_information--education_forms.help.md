# Student Trip Flight Information - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Trip Flight Information form is used to gather essential details about student trips, including flight arrangements, travel dates, and contact information. This information helps travel coordinators and administrators manage and process student travel requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the required fields for flight information, including:
    *   **Flight Number**: Enter the specific flight number for the trip.
    *   **Flight Date**: Enter the date of the flight.
    *   **Flight Time**: Enter the time of the flight.
    *   **Departure Airport**: Enter the airport of departure.
    *   **Arrival Airport**: Enter the airport of arrival.
    *   **Travel From**: Enter the departure location or city.
    *   **Travel To**: Enter the destination city or location.
    *   **Travel Method**: Enter the mode of travel (e.g., airplane, bus, etc.).
    *   **Student Count**: Enter the number of students traveling.
2.  Enter the **Student Flight Details** section with any additional information or notes about the trip.
3.  Enter the **Student Information** section with any relevant details about the students traveling.
4.  Enter the **Emergency Contact** section with any emergency contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student Flight Details**: Enter any additional details about the trip that may be useful for organizers and travel coordinators.
*   **Student Information**: Enter any relevant details about the students traveling, such as their names, ages, or special requirements.
*   **Emergency Contact**: Enter the contact information for the emergency contact person.
*   **Flight Arrangement**: Select the flight arrangement type (e.g., School or Group).
*   **Flight Number**: Enter the specific flight number for the trip.
*   **Flight Date**: Enter the date of the flight.
*   **Flight Time**: Enter the time of the flight.
*   **Departure Airport**: Enter the airport of departure.
*   **Arrival Airport**: Enter the airport of arrival.
*   **Travel From**: Enter the departure location or city.
*   **Travel To**: Enter the destination city or location.
*   **Travel Method**: Enter the mode of travel (e.g., airplane, bus, etc.).
*   **Student Count**: Enter the number of students traveling.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately to avoid any issues with processing the trip information.
*   If you are unsure about any field, refer to your travel coordinator or the trip organizer for clarification.
*   Double-check the entered information for accuracy before submitting the form.
