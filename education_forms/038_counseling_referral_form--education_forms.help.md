# counseling_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The counseling referral form is used to refer a student to a counseling program or service. It provides the necessary information to facilitate the referral process and ensure the student receives the support they need.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's name, grade level, education level, and referral reason.
2. Select the referral program and counselor responsible for the referral.
3. Enter the student's contact information, including their school or college address.
4. Add any additional comments or notes as necessary.
5. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Please enter the student's name as it appears on their records.
* **Student Grade Level**: Select the student's current grade level from the options provided.
* **Student Education Level**: Select the student's current education level from the options provided.
* **Referral Reason**: Please select the reason for the referral from the provided options or enter a brief description in the text field.
* **Referral Program**: Select the program or service that the student is being referred to.
* **Counselor Name**: Enter the name of the counselor responsible for the referral.
* **Counselor Email**: Enter the email address of the counselor.
* **Referral Date**: Enter the date of the referral.
* **Student Phone**: Enter the student's phone number.
* **Student Address**: Enter the student's address.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about the referral.
* **Student Notes**: Add any notes about the student's needs or progress.
* **Referral Source**: Select who is referring the student (Student, Counselor, or Parent).
* **Referral By Whom**: Enter the name of the person referring the student.
* **Referral Source 2**: Select an additional referral source if necessary.
* **Counselor Notes**: Add any notes from the counselor about the referral.
* **Follow-up Date**: Enter the date for the next follow-up.
* **Next Step**: Select what the next step should be in the referral process.
* **Counselor Phone**: Enter the counselor's phone number.
* **Additional Phone Number**: Enter any additional phone numbers related to the referral.
* **Student Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments from the student.
* **Referral Program 1**: Select any additional referral programs that apply.
* **School or College Address**: Enter the address of the school or college.
* **Student Additional Comments 2**: Add any further comments from the student.
