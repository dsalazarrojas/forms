# Student Leadership Teacher Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to evaluate a student's performance in a leadership role, providing them with feedback on their strengths and weaknesses, and helping them become better leaders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the student you are evaluating from the school's leadership roster or by referring to the student's ID number.
2. Answer the questions on the form as accurately as possible, based on your observation of the student's performance.
3. Fill out the form for each student you need to evaluate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **General Information** (`General Information`, `text`, required: false): This field is for general comments about the student's performance. You can include any relevant background information or context that might be helpful for the student to understand their performance.
- **Student Name** (`Student Name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the name of the student you are evaluating.
- **Student ID** (`Student ID`, `number`, required: false): Enter the student's ID number for reference.
- **Teacher Name** (`Teacher Name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name and title for reference.
- **Evaluation Date** (`Evaluation Date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you are evaluating the student.
- **Overall Rating** (`Overall Rating`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate the student's overall performance, on a scale of 5-10.
- **Specific Skills** (`Specific Skills`, `note`, required: false): Describe any specific skills you observed the student using during their leadership role.
- **Specific Behaviours** (`Specific Behaviours`, `note`, required: false): Describe any behaviors you observed the student exhibiting during their leadership role.
- **Overall Behaviours** (`Overall Behaviours`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate whether or not the student has demonstrated overall positive behaviours.
- **Leadership Quality 1** (`Leadership Quality 1`, `text`, required: false): Describe a leadership quality you observed in the student.
- **Leadership Quality 2** (`Leadership Quality 2`, `text`, required: false): Describe another leadership quality you observed in the student.
- **Leadership Quality 3** (`Leadership Quality 3`, `text`, required: false): Describe a third leadership quality you observed in the student.
- **Other Behaviours** (`Other Behaviours`, `text`, required: false): Describe any other behaviors you observed the student exhibiting during their leadership role.
- **Evaluation Comments** (`Evaluation Comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments you have about the student's performance.
- **Overall Comments** (`Overall Comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any overall comments you have about the student's performance.
- **Student Leadership Score** (`Student Leadership Score`, `number`, required: false): Enter a score representing the student's overall leadership performance.
- **Average Score** (`Average Score`, `number`, required: false): This field is automatically populated based on the student's overall performance.
- **Comments** (`Comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or feedback for the student.
- **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for reference.
- **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for reference.
- **Date** (`Date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the current date for reference.
- **Time** (`Time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the current time for reference.
- **Specific Skills 1** (`Specific Skills 1`, `text`, required: false): Describe a specific skill you observed the student using during their leadership role.
- **Specific Skills 2** (`Specific Skills 2`, `text`, required: false): Describe another specific skill you observed the student using during their leadership role.)

This guide should help you understand the form and fill it out accurately and efficiently. If you have any further questions or concerns, please reach out to your school's administration for guidance.
