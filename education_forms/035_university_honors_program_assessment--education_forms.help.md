# university-honors-program-assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and experiences from students about the university's honors program. This information will help administrators better understand how the program is meeting its goals and how it can be improved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Please provide your honest feedback about your experiences in the university's honors program.
2. Describe your honors experiences, including any academic, personal, or social benefits you've encountered.
3. Share your thoughts on how the program has impacted your education and personal growth.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Feedback** (`student_feedback`, text, required: false): This field is where you can provide any general feedback about your experiences in the university's honors program. Please keep your response concise and clear.

* **Honors Experiences** (`honors_experiences`, text, required: false): Describe any experiences you've had as part of the honors program, including academic, personal, or social benefits.

* **Program Impact** (`program_impact`, text, required: false): Share your thoughts on how the honors program has impacted your education and personal growth. This could include any positive or negative effects you've noticed.

* **Other Experiences** (`other_experiences`, text, required: false): If you've had any experiences outside of the honors program that you think are relevant to your education or personal growth, please describe them here.

## Tips
- Please be honest and sincere in your feedback.
- Keep your responses concise and clear.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to the form administrators.
- Thank you for taking the time to participate in this assessment!
