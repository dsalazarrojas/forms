# Cryptography Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cryptography Knowledge Assessment form is designed to evaluate an individual's knowledge of cryptography, a crucial aspect of computer security.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Familiarize yourself with the form's sections and questions.
2. Read each question carefully and understand what is being asked.
3. Choose the correct answers for each question based on your knowledge of cryptography.
