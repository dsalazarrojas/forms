# Indexing Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Indexing Knowledge Quiz form is designed to gather feedback or opinions from users on their indexing knowledge. It is intended to be used in an educational or academic setting to gauge understanding of a subject or topic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

* Please answer each question based on your actual knowledge or experience.
* If you are unsure or do not have information on a particular topic, it is okay to leave the field blank.
* For questions that have multiple options, select all that apply.
* For questions that have a time component, please enter the correct time in the format `HH:MM:SS` or `HH:MM` if only hours and minutes are required.
