# Character Study Reflection - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Character Study Reflection form is designed to gather information on a literary character's traits, motivations, and relationships with the environment. This form helps students and educators to analyze and understand the character's development throughout the story.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Fill out each section of the form** carefully, providing detailed information about the character and their analysis.
2.  **Enter your first and last name** in the "Student Full Name" field.
3.  **Enter your student ID number** in the "Student ID Number" field.
4.  **Select the course or class title** from the given options or enter it manually.
5.  **Choose the character being studied** from the given options or enter it manually.
6.  **Enter the title of the literary work** where the character appears.
7.  **Reflect on the character's primary motivation** and how it drives their actions.
8.  **Analyze the character's significant flaw** and how it affects their development.
9.  **Rate your empathy for the character** using the given scale.
10. **Describe a moment that caught you off guard** about the character's actions.
11. **Write down a lesson you learned from the character study**.
12.  **Rate the character's development arc** using the given scale.
13.  **Describe how the setting influences the character**.
14.  **Confirm that this reflection is your original work** by selecting the correct option.
15.  **Enter the date of completion** to record the date of submission.
16.  **Add any additional comments or thoughts** about the character or the story.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student Full Name**: Enter your first and last name to identify yourself as the student submitting this reflection.
*   **Student ID Number**: Enter your student ID number to track your progress and completion.
*   **Course or Class Title**: Select the course or class title from the given options or enter it manually.
*   **Character Being Studied**: Choose the character you are reflecting on from the given options or enter it manually.
*   **Literary Work Title**: Enter the title of the book, play, or story where the character appears.
*   **Character Primary Motivation Reflection**: Reflect on what drives the character's actions and decisions.
*   **Significant Character Flaw Analysis**: Analyze the character's significant flaw and how it affects their development.
*   **Empathy for Character Rating**: Rate your level of empathy for the character using the given scale.
*   **Most Surprising Character Action**: Describe a moment that surprised you about the character's actions.
*   **Lesson Learned from Character Study**: Write down a lesson you learned from analyzing this character.
*   **Character Development Arc Rating**: Rate the character's development arc using the given scale.
*   **Impact of Setting on Character**: Describe how the character's environment shapes them.
*   **Confirmation of Reflection Details**: Confirm that this reflection is your original work and analysis.
*   **Date of Completion**: Enter the date of completion to record the submission date.
*   **Additional Reflection Comments**: Add any additional comments or thoughts about the character or the story.

## Tips

*   Take your time to fill out each section carefully, ensuring that you provide detailed and accurate information about the character and their analysis.
*   Use the given options or enter your answers manually, depending on the field requirements.
*   Proofread your work before submitting to ensure that everything is accurate and complete.
