# digital_ethics_for_educators_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registration for the "digital_ethics_for_educators" program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) in the field label.
* Fill out any additional fields as requested.
* Review your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. User Info
**User Info** (`user_info`, text, optional): This field is for providing any additional information about yourself. You can type in any relevant details, such as your background or experience.

### 2. Email
**Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you.

### 3. Confirm Email
**Confirm Email** (`email_confirmation`, select_one, optional): Confirm your email address. Select "Email" to confirm that your email is the same as the one entered above.

### 4. Phone
**Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.

### 5. Name
**Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like to be listed.

### 6. Role
**Role** (`role`, select_multiple, optional): Select the roles you hold or are interested in: "Educator", "Program Organizer", or "Both".

### 7. Workshop Info
**Workshop Info** (`workshop_info`, note, optional): Enter any additional workshop information, such as the workshop title, date, or location.

### 8. Preferred Contact
**Preferred Contact** (`preferred_contact`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred contact method: "Email", "Phone", or "Other (Specify)".

### 9. Other Contact
**Other Contact** (`other_contact`, text, optional): If you selected "Other (Specify)" above, enter your preferred contact method here.

### 10. Program Organizer
**Program Organizer** (`program_organizer`, select_one, optional): Confirm if you are a program organizer. Select "True" to confirm.

### 11. Follow Up
**Follow Up** (`follow_up`, number, optional): Enter a number to allow us to follow up with you, if necessary.
 
### 11. Other fields (hidden from display in this guide):
* "Program Organizer" is used to check if you are a program organizer or not.
* "Other Contact" is for specifying your preferred contact method if "Other (Specify)" is selected in "Preferred Contact".
