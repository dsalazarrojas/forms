# School Counseling Progress Note - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Counseling Progress Note is a document used to track student progress and provide a clear picture of their academic and personal development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Student Name** field with the student's name as it appears on their official records.
2. Enter the name of the **Counselor** who provided the counseling session in the **Counselor** field.
3. Select the **Grade** of the student in the **Grade** field.
4. Enter the date of the counseling session in the **Date** field.
5. Add any **Notes** regarding the student's progress or concerns in the **Notes** field.
6. If available, enter the student's **Email** address in the **Email** field.
7. If available, enter the student's **Phone** number in the **Phone** field.
8. Enter the name of the **Assigned Counselor** who is responsible for the student's case.
9. Enter the name of the **School** where the student attends.
10. Finally, enter the name of the **Department** the student is part of, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required: false): Enter the student's name as it appears on their official records.
* **Counselor** (`teacher_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the counselor who provided the session.
* **Grade** (`grade`, number, required: false): Select the grade level of the student.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the counseling session.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any notes regarding the student's progress or concerns.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): If available, enter the student's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): If available, enter the student's phone number.
* **Assigned Counselor** (`assigned_counselor`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the assigned counselor.
* **School** (`school_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the school the student attends.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the department the student is part of, if applicable.

## Tips
* Always ensure that the student's data is accurate and up-to-date.
* Review the form carefully to ensure all fields are filled out correctly.
* This information will be used to support student development and progress.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the assigned counselor or school administration.
