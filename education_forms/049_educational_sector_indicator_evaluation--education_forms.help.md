# Educational Sector Indicator Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the current status of the educational sector. It will help in identifying areas that need improvement and provide valuable insights for informed decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select one or multiple options from the dropdown menus where applicable.
2. Enter relevant information in the text fields as instructed.
3. Fill in the required fields marked as "required" in the form.
4. Ensure that all required fields are filled before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sector**: This option is a dropdown menu that asks you to confirm or deny the presence of the educational sector in the specified time frame.
	+ Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the sector is present or not.
* **Indicator**: This field is for entering a relevant indicator about the educational sector.
	+ Enter a brief but meaningful description of the indicator, e.g., literacy rate, graduation rate, or any other relevant measure.
* **Level**: This field is for specifying the level of the indicator.
	+ Enter a numerical value representing the level of the indicator, e.g., 80, 90, or 100.
* **Form**: This option is a dropdown menu asking to confirm or deny the existence of the form for the educational sector.
	+ Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the form is present or not.
* **Evaluation**: This field is for entering your evaluation or comments about the educational sector.
	+ Provide a brief but thoughtful description of your evaluation, e.g., positive, negative, or neutral.
* **Date**: This field is for entering the date when the evaluation was conducted.
	+ Use the date format as specified (mm/dd/yyyy) for accuracy.
* **Time**: This field is for specifying the time of day when the evaluation was conducted.
	+ Enter the time in 24-hour format (hh:mm AM/PM) for accuracy.
* **Indicator**: Similar to field 2, this field is for entering another relevant indicator about the educational sector.
* **Sector**: Another dropdown menu asking to confirm or deny the presence of the educational sector in the specified time frame.
