# Student Writing Competition Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Writing Competition Registration Form is used to collect information from students participating in the writing competition. This form is used to register students for the competition, collecting details about their personal and entry information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the field labeled "First Name".
2. Enter your Last Name in the field labeled "Last Name".
3. Enter your email address in the field labeled "Email".
4. Select the type of entry you are submitting from the field labeled "Entry Type".
5. Enter the title of your entry in the field labeled "Entry Title".
6. Enter a description of your entry in the field labeled "Entry Description".
7. Upload or enter any media related to your entry in the field labeled "Entry Media".
8. Enter the date of your entry in the field labeled "Entry Date".
9. Enter the time of your entry in the field labeled "Entry Time".
10. Enter the name of your school in the field labeled "School Name".
11. Enter the address of your school in the field labeled "School Address".
12. Enter the name of the contact person responsible for your entry in the field labeled "Contact Name".
13. Enter the phone number of the contact person in the field labeled "Phone".
14. Select the category of your entry from the field labeled "Entry Category".
15. Select the status of your entry from the field labeled "Entry Status".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is written on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is written on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that can be used to contact you.
* **Entry Type**: Select the type of entry you are submitting, such as fiction or non-fiction writing.
* **Entry Title**: Enter a brief title for your entry.
* **Entry Description**: Enter a description of your entry, including relevant details and context.
* **Entry Media**: Upload any relevant media, such as images or videos, that are associated with your entry.
* **Entry Date**: Enter the date you completed your entry, in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Entry Time**: Enter the time you completed your entry, in the format HH:MM:SS.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of your school or educational institution.
* **School Address**: Enter the address of your school or educational institution.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person responsible for your entry.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Entry Category**: Select the category of your entry, such as poetry or fiction.
* **Entry Status**: Select the status of your entry, such as submitted or pending.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields, marked as required, before submitting the form.
* Use a valid email address and phone number for contact purposes.
* Ensure that your entry is accurate and complete before submitting it for review.
