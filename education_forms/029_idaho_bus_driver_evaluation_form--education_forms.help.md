# idaho_bus_driver_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Idaho Bus Driver Evaluation Form is used to collect information about bus drivers and their vehicles. This form helps evaluate the legitimacy and safety of buses and their drivers on Idaho roads.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the "Bus Driver" field with the name of the driver.
2. Enter the "License Plate Number" of the bus in question.
3. Provide the "Date Last Inspection" when the bus underwent its last inspection.
4. Specify the "Vehicle Make" and "Vehicle Model" of the bus.
5. Enter the "Vehicle Year" of the bus.
6. Choose the "Vehicle Type" of the bus from the options available (Bus, Car, or Truck).
7. Enter the "License Number" of the bus.
8. Specify the "Vehicle Color".
9. Provide the "Vehicle Registration Last Inspection" date.
10. Select the "Vehicle Registration Status" (Registered, Unregistered, or Pending Registration).
11. Choose the "Vehicle Weight Certified" status (Certified, Non-Certified, or Unknown).
12. Select the "Vehicle Weight Certification Status" (Certified, Non-Certified, or Unknown).
13. Choose the "Vehicle Inspection Type" (Inspections Performed, No Inspections Performed, or Unknown).
14. Enter the "Inspection Date" when the bus underwent its last inspection.
15. Select the "Vehicle Inspection Status" (Passed, Failed, or Not Tested).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Bus Driver** (Text): Enter the name of the bus driver.
* **License Plate Number** (Text): Enter the license plate number of the bus.
* **Date Last Inspection** (Date): Provide the date of the last inspection of the bus.
* **Vehicle Make** (Text): Enter the make of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model** (Text): Enter the model of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Year** (Number): Enter the year of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Year Last Inspection** (Number): Enter the year of the last inspection of the vehicle. (Note: This field is likely intended to capture the year of the last inspection, but the name could be improved for clarity.)
* **Vehicle Type** (Select One): Choose the type of vehicle from the options (Bus, Car, or Truck).
* **Vehicle License Number** (Text): Enter the license number of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Color** (Text): Enter the color of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Registration Last Inspection** (Date): Provide the date of the last vehicle registration inspection.
* **Vehicle Registration Status** (Select Multiple): Select the status of the vehicle registration from the options (Registered, Unregistered, or Pending Registration).
* **Vehicle Weight Certified** (Select Multiple): Choose the weight certification status of the vehicle from the options (Certified, Non-Certified, or Unknown).
* **Vehicle Weight Certification Status** (Select Multiple): Select the weight certification status of the vehicle from the options (Certified, Non-Certified, or Unknown).
* **Vehicle Inspection Type** (Select Multiple): Choose the inspection type from the options (Inspections Performed, No Inspections Performed, or Unknown).
* **Inspection Date** (Date): Enter the date of the last vehicle inspection.
* **Vehicle Inspection Status** (Select Multiple): Select the inspection status of the vehicle from the options (Passed, Failed, or Not Tested).

## Tips

- Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
