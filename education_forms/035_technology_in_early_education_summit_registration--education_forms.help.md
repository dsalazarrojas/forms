# Technology In Early Education Summit Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Technology In Early Education Summit Registration form is designed to collect information from attendees, speakers, and organizers of the event. This form helps the event organizers to manage registrations, track attendees' interests, and process payments efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Choose your role as a Speaker, Attendee, or Organizer.
3. Select your session interests or accessibility needs.
4. Enter your name and email address.
5. Provide your company and phone number (if applicable).
6. Choose your payment method (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
7. Enter payment method details (if choosing Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Interests**: Select all interests that you wish to attend or present sessions on. This helps us understand your areas of expertise and interest. You can select multiple interests.
	+ True: You have interest in this area.
	+ False: You do not have interest in this area.
* **Accessibility Needs**: If you require any specific accessibility arrangements, please select the options that apply to you.
	+ True: You require this accommodation.
	+ False: You do not require this accommodation.
* **Name**: Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Company**: If you are representing a company, enter its name.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Address**: Enter your address (if applicable).
* **Payment Method**: Select how you intend to pay for your registration.
* **Payment Method Credit Card Details**: If you choose Credit Card, please enter your credit card details in the text field below.
* **Payment Method PayPal Details**: If you choose PayPal, please enter your PayPal email address or account details in the text field below.
* **Payment Method Bank Transfer Details**: If you choose Bank Transfer, please enter your bank transfer details in the text field below.
* **Role**: Select your role in the summit.
	+ Speaker: You are presenting a session.
	+ Attendee: You are attending the summit.
	+ Organizer: You are helping to organize the event.

## Tips

* Double-check your details before submitting the form.
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
