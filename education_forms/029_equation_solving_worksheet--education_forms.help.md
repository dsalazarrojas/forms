# Equation Solving Worksheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students about their approach to solving mathematical equations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each page according to the prompts. Most fields are not required, but feel free to provide as much information as you like. You can type in your answers, select from the options provided, or attach any relevant work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name** (Page 1, text, optional): Please enter your name so that we can get to know you better.
* **How many correct answers do you have?** (Page 2, number, optional): If you've solved any equations recently, how many did you get right?
* **How do you get the answer?** (Page 3, text, optional): Describe your thought process or method for solving the equation.
* **Do you have any notes?** (Page 4, text, optional): Do you have any notes or reminders that help you remember the equation or its solution?
* **What is your teacher's name** (Page 5, text, optional): If applicable, please enter the name of your teacher who assigned the equation.
* **What do you use to solve it?** (Page 6, select one, optional): Choose one of the following options to describe how you typically solve equations:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Other
* **What is your grade?** (Page 7, number, optional): If you've been graded on your work, please enter your grade.
* **Do you have any work to attach?** (Page 8, text, optional): If you have any relevant work or examples of your solutions, you can attach them here.
* **What is your school?** (Page 9, text, optional): If applicable, please enter the name of your school.
* **Equation Solving Worksheet Page 10** (Page 10, select multiple, optional): If you've solved multiple equations, choose all that apply from the following options:
	+ A
	+ B
	+ C
	+ D
* **What is the date?** (Page 11, date, optional): If you've solved an equation recently, please enter the date.
* **What time of day?** (Page 12, time, optional): If you've solved an equation at a specific time, please enter the time.
* **What is your contact number** (Page 13, text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted, please enter your contact number.
* **What is your email** (Page 14, email, optional): If you'd like to be contacted, please enter your email address.
* **What is the answer?** (Page 15, text, optional): If you've solved an equation, please enter your answer.
* **Do you have any other comments?** (Page 16, text, optional): Do you have any other feedback or comments about equation solving?
* **What is the correct answer?** (Page 17, text, optional): If you know the correct answer, please enter it.
* **What is your grade level?** (Page 18, number, optional): If you've been graded on your work, please enter your grade level.
* **Equation Solving Worksheet Page 19** (Page 19, select multiple, optional): If you've solved multiple equations, choose all that apply:
	+ A
	+ B
	+ C
	+ D
* **Equation Solving Worksheet Page 20** (Page 20, text, optional): Any additional comments or feedback about equation solving.
* **What is your school name?** (Page 21, text, optional): If applicable, please enter the name of your school.
* **Do you use a calculator?** (Page 22, text, optional): Do you use a calculator when solving equations?
* **How long does it take to solve?** (Page 23, time, optional): How long it typically takes you to solve an equation.
* **Why is it difficult to solve?** (Page 24, text, optional): If you find it challenging to solve equations, explain why.
* **Is there anything else you'd like to add?** (Page 25, text, optional): Do you have any other feedback or comments about equation solving?
