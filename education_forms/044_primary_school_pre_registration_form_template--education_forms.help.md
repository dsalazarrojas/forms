# Primary School Pre Registration Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect pre-registration information from students or their guardians before the student starts attending primary school.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name and address information, including your first and last name.
2. Enter your date of birth.
3. Provide your address, city, and postal code.
4. Add your phone number, email, and emergency contact information.
5. Provide the necessary medical conditions and special needs information.
6. Enter your student ID number and select the type of ID you have.
7. Indicate whether or not you have a school reference number.
8. Add any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, required): Enter your first name, e.g., John.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, required): Enter your last name, e.g., Smith.
- **Date of Birth** (`birth_date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY, e.g., 07/12/1995.
- **Address Line 1** (`address_line_1`, required: false): Enter your address line 1, e.g., 123 Main St.
- **Address Line 2** (`address_line_2`, required: false): Enter your address line 2, e.g., Apt 101.
- **City** (`city`, required): Enter the city of your residence, e.g., New York.
- **State** (`state`, required): Enter the state of your residence, e.g., NY.
- **Postal Code** (`postal_code`, required): Enter your postal code, e.g., 10001.
- **Phone** (`phone`, required: false): Enter your phone number, e.g., 555-1234.
- **Email** (`email`, required: false): Enter your email address, e.g., [john.smith@example.com](mailto:john.smith@example.com).
- **Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, required): Enter the name of your guardian or parent, e.g., Jane Doe.
- **Guardian Phone** (`guardian_phone`, required): Enter the phone number of your guardian or parent, e.g., 555-5678.
- **Guardian Email** (`guardian_email`, required): Enter the email of your guardian or parent, e.g., [jane.doe@example.com](mailto:jane.doe@example.com).
- **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact, e.g., Bob Johnson.
- **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, required): Enter your relationship to the emergency contact, e.g., Parent.
- **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact, e.g., 555-9012.
- **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, required: false): Enter the email of the emergency contact, e.g., [bob.johnson@example.com](mailto:bob.johnson@example.com).
- **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, required: false): Enter any medical conditions you have, e.g., allergy to peanuts.
- **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, required: false): Enter any additional medical notes, e.g., "has a pacemaker."
- **Special Needs** (`special_needs`, required: false): Enter any special needs you have, e.g., wheelchair access required.
- **Special Needs Details** (`special_needs_details`, required: false): Enter any additional details about your special needs, e.g., requires extra time to complete tasks.
- **Student ID Number** (`student_id_number`, required): Enter your student ID number, e.g., #1234.
- **ID Type** (`student_id_type`, required): Select the type of ID you have, e.g., Student ID Number.
- **Have school reference number** (`school_reference_number`, required): Indicate whether or not you have a school reference number.
- **Notes** (`notes`, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments, e.g., "I'm a good student."
