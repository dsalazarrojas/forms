# Fish Nutrition Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Fish Nutrition Knowledge Quiz is a form designed to test your knowledge of fish nutrition in various areas such as aquaculture training and correct answers related to fish nutrition. It is a self-contained form with 5 fields that you will need to fill in to complete the quiz.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the correct answer for each field, making sure to check the options provided for each question.
2. Make sure to submit your answers before moving on to the next field.
3. Double-check your answers to ensure they are accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fish Nutrition Knowledge Quiz** (`fish_nutrition_knowledge_quiz`, text, required):
	+ This is the title of the form, which will not be edited by the user.
* **Correct Answer** (`correct_answer`, select_one, required):
	+ Select one of the options A, B, C, or D as the correct answer to the question.
* **Aquaculture Training** (`aquaculture_training`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ If applicable, select all the relevant training methods or courses you have received in aquaculture.
	+ You can select multiple options if you have received training in multiple areas.
* **Fish Nutrition Knowledge** (`fish_nutrition_knowledge`, text, required):
	+ Answer the question about fish nutrition, providing as much detail as you can.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required):
	+ Provide the date you are submitting your answers.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer each question carefully and accurately.
* Double-check your answers before submitting.
* If you have any doubts or questions, don't hesitate to ask for help.
* You can ask for feedback or clarification on your answers if you are unsure of them.
* Remember, this quiz is designed to test your knowledge, not to trip you up!
