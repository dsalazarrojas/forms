# Teacher Contributions 2 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Teacher Contributions 2" form is designed to collect information from teachers about their contributions in a specific subject area. This form helps to track and document the activities, goals, and progress of students, as well as the teacher's involvement and ratings. The form is used to provide a comprehensive overview of the teacher's contributions and to facilitate collaboration and planning between teachers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the relevant options for each field, ensuring that all required fields are filled out.
2.  Enter relevant details for each field, using the provided hints as a guide.
3.  Review your entries carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Activity** (`activity`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the activity you have completed.
*   **Goal** (`goal`, `text`, required): Enter the goal of the activity you have completed.
*   **Fulfilled Expectation** (`fulfilled_expectation`, `text`, required): Describe the expectation that was fulfilled as a result of this activity.
*   **Progress Level** (`progress_level`, `number`, required): Select the progress level you have achieved for this activity (e.g., 0-100%).
*   **Activities Completed** (`activities_completed`, `number`, required): Enter the number of activities you have completed for this activity.
*   **Target Activities** (`target_activities`, `number`, required): Enter the number of target activities for this activity.
*   **Activities Completed Percent** (`activities_completed_percent`, `number`, required): Calculate the percentage of activities completed based on the number of activities completed and target activities.
*   **Progress Percent** (`progress_percent`, `number`, required): Calculate the percentage of progress made on this activity.
*   **Fulfilled Expectation Date** (`fulfilled_expectation_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date on which the expectation was fulfilled.
*   **Last Update** (`last_update`, `date`, required): Enter the date on which this form was last updated.
*   **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as a teacher.
*   **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address as a teacher.
*   **Teacher Phone** (`teacher_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number as a teacher.
*   **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the school where you teach.
*   **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, `text`, required): Enter the grade level you teach.
*   **Subject** (`subject`, `text`, required): Enter the subject area you teach.
*   **Teacher ID** (`teacher_id`, `text`, required): Enter your teacher ID number.
*   **Student ID** (`student_id`, `text`, required): Enter the ID number of the student(s) you are working with.
*   **Classroom Name** (`classroom_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the classroom where you teach.

*   **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments you would like to make about this activity.

*   **Target Activities Range** (`target_activities_range`, `text`, optional): Enter a range of target activities (e.g., 1-10).
*   **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback about this activity.
