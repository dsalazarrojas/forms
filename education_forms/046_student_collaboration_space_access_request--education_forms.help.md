# Student Collaboration Space Access Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Collaboration Space Access Request form is used to request access to a collaboration space for educational purposes. This form is intended for students who need to access a space for research, study groups, or other academic-related activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the start and end dates for your requested access.
2. Provide a brief description of your purpose for accessing the collaboration space.
3. Check the box indicating whether you agree to the space rules.
4. Fill out your name, role, contact info, school, library, and university information.
5. Provide your school's and university's email addresses and phone numbers.
6. If applicable, enter the space ID, name, and type.
7. Enter the start and end dates for your requested access.
8. Enter the start and end times for your requested access.
9. Enter the duration of your requested access.
10. Provide any additional notes about your request.
11. Check the box indicating whether you agree to the space agreement.
12. Briefly describe the reason for your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Timing**: Enter the start and end dates for your requested access. This will help the librarian determine the availability of the space.
* **Request Purpose**: Provide a brief description of your purpose for accessing the collaboration space. This should be a clear and concise statement of your academic intentions.
* **Agree to Space Rules**: Check the box indicating whether you agree to the space rules. This is essential to ensure that you understand and will adhere to the rules of the space.
* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your student ID or university records.
* **Role**: Enter your role in the university or school (e.g., undergraduate, graduate student, teaching assistant).
* **Contact Info**: Enter your contact information, including email and phone number.
* **School**: Enter the name of your school or department.
* **Library**: Enter the name of your library or departmental library.
* **University**: Enter the name of your university.
* **Space ID**: Enter the ID number of the collaboration space you are requesting access to, if applicable.
* **Space Name**: Enter the name of the collaboration space you are requesting access to, if applicable.
* **Space Type**: Enter the type of collaboration space you are requesting access to (e.g., study room, meeting room).
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date for your requested access.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date for your requested access.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time for your requested access.
* **End Time**: Enter the end time for your requested access.
* **Duration**: Enter the duration of your requested access in hours or minutes.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional information about your request, including any specific needs or requirements.
* **Space Agreement**: Check the box indicating whether you agree to the space agreement.
* **Reason for Requesting Access**: Briefly describe the reason for your request.
* **School Email**: Enter your school's email address.
* **School Phone**: Enter your school's phone number.
* **University Email**: Enter your university's email address.
* **University Phone**: Enter your university's phone number.
* **Librarian**: Enter the name of your librarian or departmental librarian.
* **Space Agreement**: Check the box indicating whether you agree to the space agreement.
* **Reason for Requesting Access**: Briefly describe the reason for your request.
