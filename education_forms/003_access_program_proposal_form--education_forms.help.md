# ACCESS Program Proposal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you submit a proposal for a new program to support the education sector. It collects essential information about your program, including its title, description, target audience, and budget. Your proposal will be reviewed to determine its alignment with our institution's mission and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and detailed information about your proposed program.
2. Be sure to review each section carefully and provide clear explanations for your answers.
3. Submit your completed form for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Proposer Name** (id: 2, name: proposer_name, label: Proposer Name, required: true)
	+ This is the name of the person submitting the proposal.
* **Proposer Email** (id: 3, name: proposer_email, label: Proposer Email, required: true)
	+ This is the email address of the person submitting the proposal.
* **Department or School Name** (id: 4, name: department_school, label: Department or School Name, required: true)
	+ This is the name of the department or school associated with the proposed program.
* **Date of Submission** (id: 5, name: proposal_date, label: Date of Submission, required: true)
	+ The date the proposal is submitted.
* **Proposed Program Title** (id: 6, name: program_title, label: Proposed Program Title, required: true)
	+ A concise and descriptive title for your proposed program.
* **Program Type** (id: 7, name: program_type, label: Program Type, required: true)
	+ Select the type of program you are proposing (e.g., Academic Support, Financial Assistance, etc.)
* **Target Student Population** (id: 8, name: target_audience, label: Target Student Population, required: true)
	+ Choose the student population that will benefit from your program (e.g., K-5 Students, Middle School Students, etc.)
* **Program Description** (id: 9, name: program_description, label: Program Description, required: true)
	+ Provide a detailed overview of your proposed program and its core activities.
* **Core Objectives** (id: 10, name: program_objectives, label: Core Objectives, required: true)
	+ Describe the specific goals your program aims to achieve.
* **Alignment with Institutional Goals** (id: 11, name: alignment_with_mission, label: Alignment with Institutional Goals, required: true)
	+ Explain how your proposal supports our broader mission and goals.
* **Proposed Implementation Timeline** (id: 12, name: implementation_timeline, label: Proposed Implementation Timeline, required: true)
	+ Provide start dates, key milestones, and end dates for your program.
* **Total Estimated Budget ($)** (id: 13, name: budget_total_estimate, label: Total Estimated Budget ($), required: true)
	+ Enter the total estimated budget for your program.
* **Budget Breakdown and Justification** (id: 14, name: budget_breakdown, label: Budget Breakdown and Justification, required: false)
	+ Briefly explain how the funds will be used.
* **Additional Resource Requirements** (id: 15, name: resource_requirements, label: Additional Resource Requirements, required: false)
	+ List any additional resources required for your program (e.g., staffing, space, technology).
* **Expected Outcomes and Evaluation** (id: 16, name: expected_outcomes, label: Expected Outcomes and Evaluation, required: true)
	+ Describe how success will be measured and evaluated.
* **Potential Challenges or Risks** (id: 17, name: risk_assessment, label: Potential Challenges or Risks, required: false)
	+ Identify any potential challenges or risks associated with your program.
* **Has this been discussed with your direct supervisor?** (id: 18, name: supervisor_approval_needed, label: Has this been discussed with your direct supervisor?, required: true)
