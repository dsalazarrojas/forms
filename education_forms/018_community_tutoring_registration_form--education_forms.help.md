# Community Tutoring Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register as a tutor or student in the Community Tutoring program. It's designed to collect relevant information about your role, qualifications, availability, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each field carefully and answer the questions truthfully.
2. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
3. Provide your primary contact email address in the "Email Address" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Enter your mailing address in the "Mailing Address" field.
6. Select your role as a tutor or student in the "Are you registering as a tutor or student?" field.
7. If you're a parent or guardian registering for a student, enter the student's name in the "If parent, student's name" field.
8. Select your student's grade level in the "Student Grade Level" field.
9. Choose your areas of expertise in the "Which subjects do you need/want to tutor in?" field.
10. Describe your areas of expertise in detail in the "Specific subjects in detail" field.
11. Select your highest level of education completed in the "Highest Education Level Completed" field.
12. Enter your current institution or school in the "Current School or Institution" field.
13. Enter your major or field of study in the "Major or Field of Study" field.
14. Select your level of tutoring experience in the "Do you have tutoring experience?" field.
15. Describe your tutoring experience in the "Please describe your tutoring experience" field.
16. Choose your available days and times in the "Available Days" and "Available Times" fields.
17. Select your preferred format for tutoring in the "Preferred Tutoring Format" field.
18. Choose your preferred location for in-person tutoring in the "Preferred Location for In-Person Tutoring" field.
19. Select your expected hourly rate for tutoring in the "Expected Hourly Rate" field.
20. Consent to a background check in the "I consent to a background check" field.
21. Confirm if you can provide references in the "Can you provide references?" field.
22. Select your experience working with special needs students in the "Do you have experience working with special needs students?" field.
23. Select the languages you speak in the "What languages do you speak?" field.
24. Enter your motivations for wanting to tutor or be tutored in the "Why do you want to tutor/be tutored?" field.
25. Enter any additional comments or questions in the "Any additional comments or questions?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name, including first and last name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Mailing Address**: Enter your street, city, state, and zip code.
* **Are you registering as a tutor or student?**: Select your role in the program.
* **If parent, student's name**: Enter the student's name if you're a parent or guardian registering for a student.
* **Student Grade Level**: Select your student's grade level.
* **Which subjects do you need/want to tutor in?**: Choose all the subjects you're interested in tutoring.
* **Specific subjects in detail**: Describe your areas of expertise in detail.
* **Highest Education Level Completed**: Select your highest level of education completed.
* **Current School or Institution**: Enter your current institution or school.
* **Major or Field of Study**: Enter your major or field of study.
* **Do you have tutoring experience?**: Select your level of tutoring experience.
* **Please describe your tutoring experience**: Describe your tutoring experience in detail.
* **Available Days**: Select all the days you're available for tutoring.
* **Available Times**: Select all the times you're available for tutoring.
* **Preferred Tutoring Format**: Select your preferred format for tutoring.
* **Preferred Location for In-Person Tutoring**: Choose your preferred location for in-person tutoring.
* **Expected Hourly Rate**: Select your expected hourly rate for tutoring.
* **I consent to a background check**: Select to agree to a background check.
* **Can you provide references?**: Confirm if you can provide references.
* **Do you have experience working with special needs students?**: Select your level of experience working with special needs students.
* **What languages do you speak?**: Choose all the languages you speak.
* **Why do you want to tutor/be tutored?**: Enter your motivations for wanting to tutor or be tutored.
* **Any additional comments or questions?**: Enter any additional comments or questions.
* **Registration Date**: This field is automatically populated with the current date.
