# Online Student Major Change Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Student Major Change Form is a tool used to facilitate students' requests for major changes in their academic program. This form is designed to collect essential information to process and validate major changes efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the form and start filling it out.
2. Fill out the form with the required information.
3. Review the form for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Major Change Request** (<student_major_change_form>, text, required): Enter a brief description of the major change request. This field should include any necessary details to support your change request.
* **Current Major** (<student_major_current_major>, text, required): Indicate your current major, which is the major you are currently pursuing.
* **Requested Major** (<student_major_requested_major>, text, required): Specify the major you are requesting to change to.
* **Date Requested** (<student_major_date_major_request>, date, optional): Provide the date you are requesting the major change, if applicable.
* **Reason for Major Request** (<student_major_reason_major_request>, text, optional): Explain the reason for your major change request. If you have any supporting documentation or evidence, attach it to the form.

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when filling out the form.
- Attach any supporting documentation or evidence to the form if required.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact your academic advisor or support team.
