# Free Life Coaching with Makayla Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from applicants for free life coaching with Makayla, a personalized coaching service. This form is used to determine the applicant's eligibility and tailor the coaching experience to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Review each field for any additional guidance or hints.
3. Submit the form once all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title-And-Category (Text, Optional)**: Enter a brief title that describes your current situation, such as "Seeking life coaching to improve relationships" or "Wish to enhance my career growth".
* **Personal Details (Text, Optional)**: If you have a personal story or background that you'd like to share, you can enter it here.
* **Category (Select One, Required)**: Choose "Student" if you're seeking life coaching as a student or "Working Professional" if you're seeking coaching as a working professional.
* **Job Role (Select One, Required)**: Select "Student" if you're a student or "Working Professional" if you're a working professional.
* **Allergies (Text, Optional)**: If you have any allergies or health conditions that may impact our coaching sessions, please list them here.
* **Hobbies (Text, Optional)**: Share your hobbies or interests that you'd like to incorporate into our coaching sessions.
* **Education Level (Select One, Required)**: Choose your current education level: Primary School, High School, or College/University.
* **Income Level (Select One, Optional)**: Choose your income level: Less than $20,000, $20,000-$30,000, $30,000-$40,000, or Greater than $40,000.
* **Description (Text, Optional)**: Enter a brief description of your goals or expectations from the coaching experience.
* **Category Selection2 (Select One, Required)**: Choose "Student" or "Working Professional" to confirm your category selection.
* **Job Role2 (Select One, Required)**: Select "Student" or "Working Professional" to confirm your job role selection.

## Tips
* Please be as accurate and honest as possible when filling out this form.
* If you're unsure about any field, you can always ask for clarification.
* Once you've completed the form, review your answers carefully before submitting it for review.
