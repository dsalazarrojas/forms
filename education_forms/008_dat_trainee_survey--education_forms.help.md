# DAT Trainee Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The DAT Trainee Survey is a collection of questions designed to gather information from trainees. Please answer each question to the best of your ability.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read each field label to understand what information is being requested.
2. Enter your response in the corresponding field.
3. If a field is marked as required, ensure you complete it accurately.
