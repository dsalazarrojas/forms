# Lifelong Learning Initiative Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lifelong Learning Initiative Application Form is used to collect information from applicants who are interested in pursuing further education or skill development through our program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information, including your name, contact details, and educational background.
2. Choose your current education level and select all applicable education levels for which you are currently enrolled or have completed.
3. Enter your enrollment start and end dates for each selected education level.
4. Provide any additional information about your education provider (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Education Programs
- **Education Programs** (`education_programs`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe the education or training programs you are interested in or have previously pursued.

### applicant_name
- **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification document.

### Phone
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

### Email
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.

### Date of Birth
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Time
- **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): Enter your available time for education or training.

### Education Level
- **Education Level** (`education_level`, `select_one`, optional): Select your highest completed or current education level:
	* High School
	* Bachelor's
	* Master's
	* Doctoral

### Selected Education Level
- **Selected Education Level** (`selected_education_level`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all applicable education levels for which you are currently enrolled or have completed:
	* High School
	* Bachelor's
	* Master's
	* Doctoral

### Enrollment Start Date
- **Enrollment Start Date** (`enrollment_start_date`, `date`, optional): Enter your start date of enrollment for the selected education level.

### Enrollment End Date
- **Enrollment End Date** (`enrollment_end_date`, `date`, optional): Enter your end date of enrollment for the selected education level.

### Education Provider
- **Education Provider** (`education_provider`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information about your education provider (if applicable).

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have not previously submitted an application for this program to avoid duplicate applications.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please do not hesitate to reach out to us for clarification.
* This form will take approximately 10-15 minutes to complete, depending on your level of detail.
