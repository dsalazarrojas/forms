# dorothy_m_whitmire_global_memorial_scholarship_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dorothy M. Whitmire Global Memorial Scholarship is an opportunity for students to apply for financial support to further their education. This form collects necessary information from applicants to ensure fair and accurate assessment of their qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the application form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully to ensure accurate and thorough responses.
2. Enter your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
3. Provide your birthdate and marital status.
4. List the number of dependents and their relationship to you.
5. Select your current education level and highest education level achieved.
6. Choose your field of study and current scholarship committee status.
7. Enter your current location and permanent address details.
8. Select your primary language and current employment status.
9. Choose your employment type and provide your employer's name and address.
10. Describe your work experience and education pursued.
11. Double-check your responses for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as you would like to be referred to in our communication.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number so we can reach you.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, optional): Enter your birth date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Marital Status** (`marital_status`, select_one, optional): Choose your current marital status from the options: Single, Married, Divorced, or Widowed.
* **Number of Dependents** (`number_of_dependants`, number, optional): If applicable, list the number of dependents and their relationship (e.g., spouse, children, parent).
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your current education level from the options: High School, College, University, Post-Graduate, or Doctoral.
* **Current Education Level** (`current_education_level`, select_multiple, optional): Select your current education level from the options: High School, College, University, Post-Graduate, or Doctoral.
* **Field of Study** (`field_of_study`, text, optional): Describe the field of study you are interested in.
* **Scholarship Committee** (`scholarship_committee`, select_one, optional): If applicable, indicate your role on the scholarship committee.
* **Current Location** (`current_location`, text, optional): Enter your current location (city, state, country).
* **Permanent Address** (`permanent_address`, text, optional): Provide your permanent address for future communication.
* **Postal Code** (`postal_code`, text, optional): Enter your postal code (zip code) of your permanent address.
* **Country** (`country`, text, optional): Select your country of residence.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter your state of residence.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter your city of residence.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter your zip code of your current address.
* **Primary Language** (`primary_language`, select_multiple, optional): Select your primary language from the options: English, Spanish, French, Arabic, Chinese, Russian, Japanese, Portuguese, Hindi, Korean, or Thai.
* **Current Employment Status** (`current_employment_status`, select_one, optional): Choose your current employment status from the options: Employed, Self-Employed, Unemployed, Retired, or Student.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your employment status from the options: Employed, Self-Employed, Unemployed, Retired, or Student.
* **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, text, optional): Describe your employment type.
* **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, text, optional): Enter your employer's name.
* **Employer Address** (`employer_address`, text, optional): Provide your employer's address.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, optional): Describe your relevant work experience.
* **Education Pursued** (`education_pursued`, text, optional): Describe the education you have pursued.

Note: The optional fields are not required for application submission, but we encourage you to provide as much information as possible to help us assess your qualifications. Double-check your responses for accuracy before submitting the form.
