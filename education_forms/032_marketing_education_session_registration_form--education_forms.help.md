# Marketing Education Session Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for a marketing education session. Please ensure that you are registering for the correct session and that the information provided is accurate and complete.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct session to register for.
2. Enter the contact person's name and select from the available options.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Marketing Education Session** (Page 1, Text, Required: False)
	+ Please enter the name of the marketing education session you are registering for.
* **Contact Person** (Page 2, Select Multiple, Required: True)
	+ Select the name of the contact person from the available options.
* **Email** (Page 3, Email, Required: False)
	+ Enter a valid email address to be contacted for further information.
* **Phone** (Page 4, Text, Required: False)
	+ Enter a valid phone number to be contacted for further information.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct session to avoid duplication.
* Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled.
