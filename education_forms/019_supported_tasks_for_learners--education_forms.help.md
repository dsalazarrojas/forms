# Supported Tasks For Learners - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from learners about their supported tasks, satisfaction levels, and preferred help methods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of support you need from the list of options under "Learner Support Needs".
2. Choose your preferred methods of support from the list of options under "Preferred Help Methods".
3. If you have any other needs or preferences not listed, provide a brief description in the "Other Needs" field.
4. If you have any other preferred help methods not listed, provide a brief description in the "Preferred Help Methods Other (please specify)" field.
5. Enter your preferred phone number and email address if you would like to be contacted.
6. Enter the date and time that you would like to be contacted.
7. Provide any additional notes in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Learner Support Needs** (`select_one`, `required: false`): This field allows you to select the type of support you need, such as help with coursework, projects, or other needs.
* **Support Methods** (`select_multiple`, `required: false`): This field allows you to choose multiple methods of support, such as online video tutorials, one-on-one tutoring, group work, or phone call.
* **Satisfaction Level** (`select_one`, `required: false`): This field allows you to select your satisfaction level with the current support, such as very satisfied, somewhat satisfied, neutral, somewhat dissatisfied, or very dissatisfied.
* **Other Needs** (`text`, `required: false`): This field is for any other needs or preferences that you do not see listed in the previous field.
* **Preferred Help Methods** (`select_multiple`, `required: false`): This field allows you to select your preferred methods of support, such as online video tutorials, one-on-one tutoring, group work, or phone call.
* **Preferred Help Methods Other** (`text`, `required: false`): This field is for any other preferred help methods that you do not see listed in the previous field.
* **Phone Number** (`text`, `required: false`): This field is for entering your phone number if you would like to be contacted.
* **Email** (`email`, `required: false`): This field is for entering your email address if you would like to be contacted.
* **Date** (`date`, `required: false`): This field is for entering the date you would like to be contacted.
* **Time** (`time`, `required: false`): This field is for entering the time you would like to be contacted.
* **Note** (`note`, `required: false`): This field is for any additional comments or notes.
