# Beginning Of The Year Student Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help teachers get to know their students better at the beginning of the school year. It will help teachers tailor their teaching approach to the students' needs and create a positive and inclusive classroom environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and answer all the questions honestly.
2. For questions that require a response, please type your answer in the provided text fields.
3. For questions with select options, choose one or multiple options that apply to you.
4. Ensure you answer all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) in the label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Preferred Name (text)**: This is the name you like to be called, e.g., Jane Doe or JD.
* **What are your preferred pronouns? (text)**: Please type the pronouns you use, e.g., he/him, she/her, they/them, or any other.
* **Three words that describe you (text)**: Think of three words that best describe you, e.g., curious, creative, or determined.
* **Favorite subject (select_one)**: Choose the subject you enjoy the most, e.g., Math, Science, English, or Art.
* **Least favorite subject (select_one)**: Choose the subject you enjoy the least, e.g., Math, Science, or English.
* **How do you learn best (select_multiple)**: Select all the ways you learn best, e.g., Watching/Visuals, Listening/ Discussions, or Working alone.
* **Where do you feel most focused in the classroom (select_one)**: Choose where you feel most focused, e.g., Near the front, Near the back, or No preference.
* **Homework comfort level (select_one)**: Choose how you feel about homework, e.g., I usually get it done quickly, I try but sometimes need help, or It is often very difficult for me.
* **Recent favorite book, movie, or game (text)**: Share a favorite book, movie, or game you enjoyed recently.
* **Goal for this year (text)**: Set one goal you have for yourself this school year.
* **What can I do as your teacher to help you succeed (text)**: Share one thing you think I can do to help you succeed.
* **Something unique about you (text)**: Share one unique thing about yourself that I should know.
* **Date (date)**: This field is not editable and is used for tracking purposes only.

## Tips
* Be honest and authentic in your answers.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your teacher.
* Remember, this form is for the teacher's eyes only, and your answers will not be shared with anyone else.
