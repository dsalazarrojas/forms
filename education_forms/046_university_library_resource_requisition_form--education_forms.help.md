# university-library-resource-requisition-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Library Resource Requisition Form is designed to gather information for the library's resource management process. This form is used to request resources from the library and to track the status of these requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the "Resource Requester" field to provide your name and email address, which will be used for communication purposes.
2. Enter the "Resource Title" field with the name of the resource you are requesting.
3. Choose the "Resource Type" from the dropdown menu to specify the type of resource you need.
4. Enter the "Resource Due Date" field with the deadline for the resource request.
5. Select the "Resource Status" from the dropdown menu to indicate the current status of your request (e.g., "Pending", "In Progress", or "Completed").
6. Optionally, provide additional "Resource Comments" to clarify any further information about your request.
7. Attach relevant documents or files as "Resource Attachments" to support your request.
8. Enter your "Contact Email" and "Contact Phone" for communication purposes.
9. Select the "Resource Owner" field to specify the person responsible for the resource.
10. Enter the "Resource Urgency" field with a number indicating the level of urgency for the resource request.
11. Choose the "Resource Priority" from the dropdown menu to specify the priority level of the request (e.g., "High", "Medium", or "Low").
12. Enter the "Resource Due Date 2" field with the revised deadline for the resource request.
13. Finally, click the "Submit" button to send the form to the library's resource management team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Resource Requester** (`resource_requester`, text, required): Enter your name and email address to be contacted for further information.
* **Resource Title** (`resource_title`, text, required): Provide the name of the resource you are requesting.
* **Resource Type** (`resource_type`, select_one, required): Choose from the dropdown menu to specify the type of resource you need.
* **Resource Due Date** (`resource_due_date`, date, required): Enter the deadline for the resource request.
* **Resource Status** (`resource_status`, select_one, required): Indicate the current status of your request (e.g., "Pending", "In Progress", or "Completed").
* **Resource Notes** (`resource_notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about your request.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Resource Owner** (`resource_owner`, text, required): Specify the person responsible for the resource.
* **Resource Urgency** (`resource_urgency`, number, required): Enter a number indicating the level of urgency for the resource request.
