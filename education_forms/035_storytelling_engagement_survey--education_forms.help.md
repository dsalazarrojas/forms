# Storytelling Engagement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Storytelling Engagement Survey is designed to gather information about your experiences and interactions with the storytelling format. This data will help us better understand how to improve the format and make it more engaging for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions as they appear. Some fields may have multiple options, please select only one that best suits your answer.
2. Enter your contact information (email address) in the "Contact" field.
3. Enter any additional comments or questions in the "Comments" field.
4. Scroll to the bottom and click the "Submission" button to submit your survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Storytelling Engagement Survey** (Text): This is the title of the survey.
* **Storytelling Engagement Survey** (Text): This is the introduction to the survey.
* **Story type** (Select One): Check which type of stories you have listened to or participated in. You can choose only one option.
	+ "Yes" if you have listened to or participated in a story.
	+ "No" if you have not listened to or participated in a story.
* **Audience type** (Select Multiple): Check which types of audiences you have listened to or participated in a story with. You can select multiple options.
	+ "Yes" if you have listened to or participated in a story with this type of audience.
	+ "No" if you have not listened to or participated in a story with this type of audience.
* **Frequency** (Select One): Check how often you listen to or participate in storytelling.
	+ "Yes" if you listen to or participate in storytelling frequently.
	+ "No" if you do not listen to or participate in storytelling frequently.
* **Engagement** (Select One): Check if you are engaged in storytelling.
	+ "Yes" if you are engaged in storytelling.
	+ "No" if you are not engaged in storytelling.
* **Demographics** (Note): Please add any additional demographic information about yourself.
* **Contact** (Email): Enter your contact email address.
* **Submission** (Text): This is a text field for any additional comments or questions.
* **Consent** (Select One): Check that you have given your consent for your submission.
	+ "Yes" if you have given your consent.
	+ "No" if you have not given your consent.
* **Additional comments** (Text): Please add any additional comments or questions you have.
* **Submitter** (Text): Please enter any additional submitter information.
* **Submitter contact** (Text): Please enter any contact information about the submitter.
* **Submitter email** (Email): Enter the submitter's email address.
* **Submitter phone** (Text): Please enter the submitter's phone number.
* **Submitter location** (Text): Please enter the submitter's location.
* **Submitter department** (Text): Please enter the submitter's department.
* **Submitter job** (Text): Please enter the submitter's job title.
* **Submitter organization** (Text): Please enter the submitter's organization.
* **Submitter notes** (Text): Please add any additional notes about the submitter.
* **Submitter agreement** (Select One): Check that you have given your agreement for the submitter's information.
	+ "Yes" if you have given your agreement.
	+ "No" if you have not given your agreement.
* **Additional submitter information** (Select Multiple): Check which additional information you would like to provide about the submitter.
	+ "Yes" if you would like to provide additional information.
	+ "No" if you would not like to provide additional information.
* **Additional submitter survey notes** (Text): Please add any additional notes about the submitter survey.
* **Submission** (Text): This is a text field for any additional comments or questions.
* **Consent** (Select One): Check that you have given your consent for the submission.
	+ "Yes" if you have given your consent.
	+ "No" if you have not given your consent.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form completely and honestly.
* You can add any additional comments or questions in the "Comments" field.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please reach out to us at [support email].
* Your responses will help us improve the storytelling format and make it more engaging for everyone.
