# Student Loan Assistance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Loan Assistance Request Form is intended for students who need assistance with their student loans. This form will be used by the financial aid office to understand your needs and provide guidance on available resources and options.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select your **Current Employment Status**. This will help us understand your current situation and provide relevant assistance.
2.  Choose the type of **Student Loan** you are seeking assistance with. This will help us understand the specific challenges you are facing.
3.  Enter your **Annual Income**. This will help us understand your financial situation.
4.  Enter the **Loan Amount** you are struggling with.
5.  Select the **Loan Interest Rate** you are being charged.
6.  Enter the **Student Loan Balance** you are currently paying off.
7.  Select your **Loan Payment Status**. This will help us understand whether you are making payments, behind on payments, or in default.
8.  Select whether you have completed **Loan Counseling**. This will help us understand your level of awareness and understanding of your loan obligations.
9.  Choose your **Annual Income Hint**. This will provide additional context for your employment situation.
10.  Select your **Loan Payment Plan**. This will help us understand how you plan to pay off your loan.
11.  Choose your **Loan Forgiveness Option**. This will help us understand any available options for forgiveness.
12.  Enter a brief description of your **Student Loan Assistance Request 2**.
13.  Enter your **Contact Number**, **Email**, and **Notes** for our staff to contact you.
14.  Enter any additional **Notes** about your request.
15.  Select the **Student Loan Assistance Request Form Category** you think this request falls under.
16.  Enter the **Form ID**, **Assigned Tool**, **Output File**, **Date Completed**, **Form Assigned Tool**, and **Assigned Tool 2**. These are technical fields that will be used for internal record-keeping purposes only.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student Loan Assistance Request**: This is the main field where you can describe your loan assistance request. Please provide a clear and concise explanation of your needs.
*   **Current Employment Status**: Select this field to indicate your current employment status. This will help us understand your situation and provide relevant assistance.
*   **Student Loan Type**: Choose the type of student loan you are seeking assistance with.
*   **Annual Income**: Enter your annual income to help us understand your financial situation.
*   **Loan Amount**: Enter the loan amount you are struggling with.
*   **Loan Interest Rate**: Select the loan interest rate you are being charged.
*   **Student Loan Balance**: Enter the student loan balance you are currently paying off.
*   **Loan Payment Status**: Select your loan payment status to help us understand whether you are making payments, behind on payments, or in default.
*   **Loan Counseling Status**: Select whether you have completed loan counseling to help us understand your level of awareness and understanding of your loan obligations.
*   **Annual Income Hint**: This field is for additional context about your employment situation.
*   **Loan Payment Plan**: Select your loan payment plan to help us understand how you plan to pay off your loan.
*   **Loan Forgiveness Option**: Choose your loan forgiveness option to help us understand any available options for forgiveness.
*   **Student Loan Assistance Request 2**: Enter a brief description of your request.
*   **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number so our staff can reach out to you.
*   **Email**: Enter your email address so our staff can contact you.
*   **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about your request.
*   **Student Loan Assistance Request Form Category**: Select the category you think your request falls under.
*   **Form ID**: Enter the Form ID for internal record-keeping purposes.
*   **Assigned Tool**: Enter the Assigned Tool for internal record-keeping purposes.
*   **Output File**: Enter the Output File for internal record-keeping purposes.
*   **Date Completed**: Enter the Date Completed for internal record-keeping purposes.
*   **Form Assigned Tool**: Enter the Form Assigned Tool for internal record-keeping purposes.
*   **Assigned Tool 2**: Enter the Assigned Tool 2 for internal record-keeping purposes.
*   **Output File 2**: Enter the Output File 2 for internal record-keeping purposes.

## Tips

*   Please make sure to fill out this form completely and accurately to ensure that we can assist you properly.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask our staff for guidance.
*   This form will be kept confidential and will only be used for internal purposes.
