# Student Life Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect feedback from students about their overall experience at the campus. It aims to understand what makes their life as a student better or worse, and to identify areas where the campus can improve.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Select the relevant options for each question by clicking on the corresponding checkboxes.
2. For select_one, select_one_multiple, and select_multiple questions, choose the option that best describes your experience or satisfaction.
3. For true/false questions, select the option that accurately reflects your experience or satisfaction.
4. Make sure to fill out all required fields, marked with a * symbol.
