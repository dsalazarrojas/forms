# Classroom Supplies Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from donors who wish to contribute classroom supplies to support the school. This form helps the school administration to track and acknowledge donations, and to communicate with the donors about the impact of their contributions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your donor information, including your full name and email address.
2. Select your association with the school (Parent, Alumni, Community Member, Local Business, or Other).
3. Describe the type of supplies you are donating and their quantity.
4. Indicate the condition of the items (New, Like New, or Gently Used).
5. Estimate the total value of your donation for tax purposes.
6. Choose how you would like the items to be delivered (Dropped off at Front Office, Sent with Student, or Requires Pickup).
7. If you would like to receive a donation receipt for tax purposes, select 'True'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Full Name** (`Donor Full Name`, **Required**): Please enter your full name as you would like to be listed on the donation receipt.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, **Required**): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your donation and any further details.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, **Optional**): If you would like to be contacted about your donation, please provide your phone number.
* **Association with School** (`Association with School`, **Required**): Choose how you are associated with the school (Parent, Alumni, Community Member, Local Business, or Other).
* **Type of Supplies Donated** (`Type of Supplies Donated`, **Required**): Select all that apply from the list of available options (Writing Tools, Paper Products, Art Supplies, Cleaning Supplies, Technology, Classroom Furniture, or Books/Reading Material).
* **Description of Items and Quantity** (`Description of Items and Quantity`, **Required**): Describe the items you are donating and their quantity (e.g., 5 boxes of pencils, 2 packs of paper).
* **Condition of Items** (`Condition of Items`, **Required**): Indicate the condition of the items you are donating (New, Like New, or Gently Used).
* **Estimated Total Value** (`Estimated Total Value $`, **Optional**): Estimate the total value of your donation for tax purposes.
* **How will the items be delivered?** (`How will the items be delivered?`, **Required**): Choose how you would like the items to be delivered (Dropped off at Front Office, Sent with Student, or Requires Pickup).
* **Would you like a donation receipt for tax purposes?** (`Would you like a donation receipt for tax purposes?`, **Required**): Select 'True' if you would like to receive a donation receipt.
* **Additional Comments or Specific Instructions** (`Additional Comments or Specific Instructions`, **Optional**): If you have any additional comments or specific instructions about your donation, please provide them here.
