# Exam Schedule Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Exam Schedule Communication Form is a communication tool used by staff members to share exam schedules with students. It's a crucial document that informs students about exam dates, times, locations, and other essential details. This form ensures timely and accurate communication between staff and students, enhancing their understanding and preparation for exams.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the exam schedule details in the designated fields.
2. Fill in the student's name, email, and phone number to ensure they can be contacted for any updates or changes.
3. Select the assigned tool for the exam (e.g., "chatjimmy" or "other").
4. Add any additional comments or notes about the exam schedule.
5. Review and submit the form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Exam Schedule** (`exam_schedule`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of the exam schedule, including the date, start time, and duration.
- **Exam Location** (`exam_location`, text, not required): Specify the location where the exam will take place.
- **Exam Instructions** (`exam_instructions`, text, not required): Provide any important instructions or guidelines for the exam.
- **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, not required): Enter the student's full name.
- **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, not required): Enter the student's email address for communication purposes.
- **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, not required): Enter the student's phone number for contact.
- **Exam Date** (`exam_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of the exam.
- **Exam Start Time** (`exam_start_time`, time, not required): Enter the start time of the exam.
- **Exam Duration** (`exam_duration`, number, not required): Specify the duration of the exam.
- **Exam Room** (`exam_room`, text, not required): Enter the room number or location where the exam will take place.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, not required): Add any additional comments or notes about the exam schedule.
- **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select one, not required): Select the tool assigned for the exam (e.g., "chatjimmy" or "other").
