# Music Class Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for checking out students from a music class. It collects information about students' attendance, feedback, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined in the explanation above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Enter the name of the student.
* **Student ID**: Enter a unique student ID number or other identifying information.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the music class.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the music class.
* **Class Title**: Enter the title of the music class.
* **Student Attendance**: Select whether the student was present or not.
* **Instructor Feedback**: Select whether you as the instructor have any feedback on the student.
* **Student Feedback**: Optional field to provide feedback from the student.
* **Music Type**: Select whether the student is enrolled in a specific type of music class.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments about the student.
* **Student Signature**: The student signs this form to confirm they have been checked out.
* **Teacher Signature**: The teacher signs this form to confirm they have checked out the student.
* **Student Email**: Enter the student's email address.
* **Student Phone**: Enter the student's phone number.
* **Student Grade**: Enter the student's grade (if applicable).
* **Student Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the student.
* **Teacher Email**: Enter the teacher's email address.
* **Teacher Phone**: Enter the teacher's phone number.
* **Teacher Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the teacher.
* **Parent Signature**: The parent or guardian signs this form to confirm they have been informed about the student's checkout status.
* **Teacher Attendance**: Select whether the teacher was present or not.
* **Class Duration**: Enter the duration of the music class.
* **Total Students**: Enter the total number of students present in the class.
