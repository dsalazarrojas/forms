# Learning Preferences Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Learning Preferences Survey is a form designed to help educational institutions understand students' learning preferences and styles. It's essential to provide accurate information to help tailor teaching methods and improve the learning experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all the questions truthfully, based on your current preferences.
2. Select the answers that best fit your learning style, preferences, and availability.
3. Make sure to review your answers before submitting the form.
