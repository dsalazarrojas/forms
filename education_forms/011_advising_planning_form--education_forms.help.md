# Advising Planning Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from students about their academic goals, course planning, and advising needs. It is intended to be completed by students before or after meeting with their academic advisors.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your personal and academic information, including your student name, ID number, current major/program, and academic goals.
2. List the courses you plan to take in the fall and spring semesters.
3. Identify any anticipated challenges you may face, such as course difficulty, time management, financial issues, or personal/health issues.
4. Select any support resources you would like to discuss with your advisor, such as tutoring, writing center, or financial aid.
5. Choose the next scheduled meeting date with your advisor.
6. Provide any additional notes or feedback for your advisor.
7. Finally, indicate your agreement with the plan outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your student record.
* **Student ID Number**: Enter your Student ID Number as printed on your student ID card or available on your student account.
* **Current Major/Program**: List your current major or academic program.
* **Advisor Name**: Enter the name of your academic advisor.
* **Advising Session Date**: Enter the date of your advising session.
* **Short-term Academic Goals**: Describe your goals for the current academic semester.
* **Long-term Career/Academic Goals**: Describe your plans after graduation.
* **Planned Courses (Fall Semester)**: List the courses you plan to take in the fall semester.
* **Planned Courses (Spring Semester)**: List the courses you plan to take in the spring semester.
* **Anticipated Challenges**: Select any challenges you may face, such as course difficulty, time management, financial issues, or personal/health issues.
* **Recommended Support Resources**: Select any resources you would like to discuss with your advisor, such as tutoring, writing center, or financial aid.
* **Next Scheduled Meeting**: Enter the tentative date for your next meeting with your advisor.
* **Advisor Notes**: Summarize any key points discussed with your advisor and any action items to be addressed.
* **Student Acknowledgment**: Select "I Agree" to indicate you understand and agree with the plan outlined above, or choose "Other" if you have other comments or concerns.

## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate when providing information to help your advisor better understand your needs.
* Use clear and concise language when describing your goals and challenges.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure you have provided all necessary information.
* If you have any concerns or questions, don't hesitate to discuss them with your advisor.
