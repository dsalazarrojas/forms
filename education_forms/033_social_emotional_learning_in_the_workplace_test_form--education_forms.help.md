# Social Emotional Learning In The Workplace Test Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you assess your social emotional learning in the workplace. It is not a test in the classical sense, but rather a series of questions to help you reflect on your skills and experiences in a work-related setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer the questions to the best of your ability. For most questions, you will be provided with options or text fields where you can respond.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **workplace-experience** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Please share a brief description of your work experience, including any relevant job titles or positions held.
* **team-management** (`2`, `number`, required/optional): Think of a specific situation where you effectively managed a team, and rate your level of experience from 1 (low) to 10 (high).
* **empathy-understanding** (`3`, `number`, required/optional): Consider a time when you had to understand someone else's perspective. Rate your level of understanding from 1 (low) to 10 (high).
* **self-awareness** (`4`, `select_one`, required/optional): How often do you reflect on your own strengths and weaknesses?
	+ a) Rarely
	+ b) Occasionally
	+ c) Often
* **self-motivation** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): What motivates you to achieve your goals?
	+ a) Intrinsic motivations (e.g., personal interest)
	+ b) Extrinsic motivations (e.g., rewards, recognition)
	+ c) Both
* **relationships** (`6`, `note`, required/optional): Think of a time when you had a positive relationship with a colleague or team member. Share a brief description of the experience.
* **social-skills** (`7`, `text`, required/optional): What skills do you consider essential for effective communication in the workplace?
* **communication-skills** (`8`, `email`, required/optional): Please provide an example of effective communication in a professional setting.
* **conflict-resolution** (`9`, `time`, required/optional): Consider a time when you resolved a conflict in the workplace. Rate the time it took to resolve the conflict from 1 (short) to 10 (long).
* **stress-management** (`10`, `text`, required/optional): How do you manage stress in the workplace?
* **emotional-regulation** (`11`, `number`, required/optional): Rate your ability to regulate your emotions in a high-pressure situation from 1 (low) to 10 (high).
* **personal-responsibility** (`12`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Which of the following best describes your approach to personal responsibility?
	+ a) I always take responsibility
	+ b) I sometimes take responsibility
	+ c) I rarely take responsibility
* **self-directed** (`13`, `date`, required/optional): Provide a date for when you last set a personal goal and achieved it.
* **self-acceptance** (`14`, `select_one`, required/optional): How do you rate your self-acceptance?
	+ a) Uncomfortable with self-acceptance
	+ b) Somewhat uncomfortable with self-acceptance
	+ c) Comfortable with self-acceptance
* **self-initiative** (`15`, `text`, required/optional): Please describe a situation where you took the initiative to improve a process or workflow.
* **emotional-expression** (`16`, `note`, required/optional): Think of a time when you expressed your emotions in a positive way in the workplace. Share a brief description of the experience.
* **conflict-resolvence** (`17`, `time`, required/optional): Consider a time when you resolved a conflict in the workplace. Rate the time it took to resolve the conflict from 1 (short) to 10 (long).
* **stress-management-2** (`18`, `text`, required/optional): How do you manage stress in the workplace?
* **self-compassion** (`19`, `select_one`, required/optional): Which of the following best describes your level of self-compassion?
	+ a) Low
	+ b) Moderate
	+ c) High
* **positive-communication** (`20`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): What are some examples of positive communication that you practice in the workplace?
	+ a) Active listening
	+ b) Open-ended questions
	+ c) Both
* **self-awareness-2** (`21`, `text`, required/optional): Think of a time when you reflected on your own strengths and weaknesses. Share a brief description of the experience.
* **self-acceptance-2** (`22`, `time`, required/optional): Consider a time when you accepted yourself with all your strengths and weaknesses. Rate the time it took to accept yourself from 1 (short) to 10 (long).
* **self-assertiveness** (`23`, `number`, required/optional): Rate your level of self-assertiveness from 1 (low) to 10 (high).
* **positive-assertiveness** (`24`, `select_one`, required/optional): What motivates you to assert yourself in the workplace?
	+ a) Internal factors (e.g., personal values)
	+ b) External factors (e.g., external validation)
	+ c) Both
* **social-emotional-learning-in-the-workplace-test-form** (`25`, `date`, required/optional): Please provide a date for this form.
