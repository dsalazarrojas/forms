# Academic Recognition Award Nomination Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Recognition Award is a platform for recognizing and rewarding outstanding individuals for their academic achievements. This form helps you to submit a nomination for an Academic Recognition Award by providing detailed information about the nominee and the reasons why they deserve recognition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the Nominee Details section with the full name of the person being nominated.
2. Provide the nominee's affiliation, such as their institution or department.
3. In the Nominator Details section, enter your own name and contact email address.
4. Select the relevant award category that best fits the nominee's achievements.
5. Write a clear and concise justification for why the nominee deserves recognition.
6. (Optional) Attach any supporting documents, such as a CV or publications, to help strengthen the nomination.
7. (Optional) Provide additional endorsements from others who can vouch for the nominee's work.
8. Set the submission date for the nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nominee name**: Enter the full name of the person being nominated.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: true
* **Nominee affiliation**: Enter the institution or department the nominee is affiliated with.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
* **Nominator name**: Enter your own name as the person submitting the nomination.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: true
* **Nominator email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
	+ Type: email
	+ Required: true
* **Award category**: Select the relevant award category that best fits the nominee's achievements.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: true
	+ Options: Teaching excellence, Research impact, Service to community, Early career achievement, Lifetime achievement
* **Nomination justification**: Explain in detail why the nominee deserves recognition.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: true
* **Supporting documents**: Attach any relevant documents, such as a CV or publications, to support the nomination.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
* **Additional endorsements**: Provide names and contact information of individuals who can vouch for the nominee's work.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
* **Submission date**: Set the date of submission for the nomination.
	+ Type: date
	+ Required: true

## Tips
* Make sure to proofread your form for accuracy and completeness before submitting it.
* Use a clear and concise writing style to explain why the nominee deserves recognition.
* Attach relevant supporting documents to strengthen the nomination.
* If you have additional information that may be relevant to the nomination, consider adding it in the Additional endorsements field.
