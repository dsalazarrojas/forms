# Academic Recommendation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from academic recommenders for a student's academic or professional goals, such as graduate school applications, undergraduate programs, scholarships, research opportunities, or job applications.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the student's basic information: **Student Name**, **Student Email**, and **Student ID**. These fields help the recommender understand who the student is and how to contact them.
2.  Enter the **Recommender Name**, **Recommender Email**, and **Recommender Title** fields. This information allows the recommender to contact the student and understand their professional context.
3.  Describe your **Relationship to Student** in a few words. This helps the recommender understand your personal connection to the student.
4.  Select the **Purpose of Recommendation** (e.g., Graduate school application, Undergraduate program, etc.).
5.  Enter the **Target Institution or Program** name, which is the specific academic or professional goal the student is aiming for.
6.  Provide the **Deadline Date**, when the recommendation is due.
7.  If applicable, provide additional details that the recommender should focus on in their recommendation, such as specific skills or achievements.
8.  Decide if you will **Provide a Resume/CV** to support the student's application or if you will send it separately.
9.  If you have any **Special Circumstances** that the recommender should be aware of, enter them here.
10.  Grant permission for the recommender to contact you for further information, or specify when they can contact you.
11.  Finally, add any **Additional Comments** you'd like the recommender to know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Academic Recommendation Request** (Page 1): Brief introduction to the form and its purpose.
*   **Student Name** (Required, Text): Full name of the student requesting the recommendation.
*   **Student Email** (Required, Email): Contact email for the student.
*   **Student ID** (Required, Text): Official student identification number.
*   **Recommender Name** (Required, Text): Full name of the person being asked for a recommendation.
*   **Recommender Email** (Required, Email): Professional email for the recommender.
*   **Recommender Title** (Required, Text): Professional title or position of the recommender.
*   **Relationship to Student** (Required, Text): How does the recommender know the student?
*   **Purpose of Recommendation** (Required, Select): What is this recommendation for? (e.g., Graduate school application, Undergraduate program, etc.)
*   **Target Institution or Program** (Required, Text): Name of the institution or program for which the recommendation is needed.
*   **Deadline Date** (Required, Date): When is the recommendation due?
*   **Details Needed in Recommendation** (Optional, Text): What specific aspects should the recommender highlight?
*   **Student Resume/CV Attached** (Required, Select): Will you provide a resume or CV to support the student's application or if already provided?
*   **Summary of Previous Interactions** (Optional, Text): Briefly describe your academic or professional interactions with the student.
*   **Special Circumstances** (Optional, Text): Are there any special circumstances the recommender should be aware of?
*   **Follow-Up Permission** (Required, Select): May the recommender contact you if they need more information?
*   **Additional Comments** (Optional, Text): Any other information for the recommender?
