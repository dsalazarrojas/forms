# FE Exam Study Plan Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help students create a personalized study plan for the FE exam. It gathers information about the student's preferences, needs, and goals to provide a tailored plan that suits their individual requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Choose the correct options for each field according to your needs and preferences.
3. Make sure to proofread your submission before submitting it to prevent any errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Date** (`request_date`): This field is for the student to specify the date when they would like to start their study plan.
* **Exam Date** (`exam_date`): This field is for the student to indicate when they plan to take the FE exam.
* **Study Duration** (`study_duration`): Choose the duration of the study period, which can be:
	+ Short Term (less than 3 months)
	+ Medium Term (3-6 months)
	+ Long Term (more than 6 months)
* **Study Plan** (`study_plan`): Briefly describe the areas you would like to focus on during your study plan.
* **Communication Channel** (`communication_channel`): Select the communication method(s) you would prefer to use for updates, discussions, and progress tracking:
	+ Email
	+ Phone
	+ WhatsApp
	+ Skype
* **Study Location** (`study_location`): Specify the location(s) where you would like to study.
* **Tutor** (`tutor`): If you have a preferred tutor or coach, provide their name or ID.
* **Tutor Note** (`tutor_note`): If applicable, provide any additional notes or comments for your tutor.
* **Submission** (`submission`): Briefly describe your current progress or goals.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`): Select the type of submitter:
	+ Student
	+ Parent
	+ Tutor
* **Submitter Note** (`submitter_note`): If applicable, provide any additional notes or comments for the submitter.
* **Submitter ID** (`submitter_id`): If you have a preferred ID (e.g., email, name, phone number), specify it here.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`): If you have a valid email address, provide it here.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`): Specify your phone number (if applicable).
* **Submitter Address** (`submitter_address`): Specify your address (if applicable).
* **Submitter City** (`submitter_city`): Specify your city (if applicable).
* **Submitter State** (`submitter_state`): Specify your state (if applicable).
* **Submitter Country** (`submitter_country`): Specify your country (if applicable).
* **Submitter Notes** (`submitter_notes`): If applicable, provide any additional notes or comments for the submitter.
* **Submitter Signature** (`submitter_signature`): Provide your signature to confirm your submission.
* **Submitter Agreement** (`submitter_agreement`): Select whether you agree to the terms and conditions:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Submitter Agreement Date** (`submitter_agreement_date`): If you agree to the terms and conditions, specify the date you made this agreement.
* **Submitter Agreement Time** (`submitter_agreement_time`): If applicable, specify the time you made this agreement.
* **Submitter Agreement Notes** (`submitter_agreement_notes`): If applicable, provide any additional notes or comments for the submitter agreement.
