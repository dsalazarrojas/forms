# Grade Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request changes to student grades. This includes challenging or disputing grades received by a student.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the required student and teacher IDs in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide a valid grade that you wish to request a change for.
3. Enter a clear and concise reason for the grade change request.
4. Optionally, add any additional comments regarding the grade.
5. Sign and submit the form to initiate the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **grade_request_form** (Grade Request Form, Text, Required/Optional: false)
This is the main form title and description.
* **student_id** (Student ID, Number, Required)
Enter the student's ID number as assigned by the school or institution.
* **teacher_id** (Teacher ID, Number, Required)
Enter the teacher's ID number as assigned by the school or institution.
* **grade** (Grade, Number, Required)
Enter the grade that you wish to request a change for.
* **reason_for_change** (Reason for Change, Text, Required)
Enter a clear and concise reason for requesting a grade change.
* **comments** (Comments, Text, Optional)
Add any additional comments or information regarding the grade request.
* **signature** (Signature, Text, Required)
Enter your signature to confirm that you are the student making the request.

## Tips
- Be clear and concise when entering the reason for the grade change.
- Ensure that you have reviewed and understand the school's policies on grade requests before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any part of the process, please contact your teacher or school administrator for guidance.
