# University Administrative Operations Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Administrative Operations Audit Form is a tool used by university administrators to evaluate and improve their internal processes. The form is designed to gather information about administrative operations, and its completion is mandatory for all administrative staff.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Navigate to the form's first page, "university_administrative_operations_audit_form".
2.  On this page, you will be asked to provide a brief description of the administrative operation being audited.
3.  Move to the next page, "contact_information", to provide your contact details.
4.  On the next page, "administrative_processes", you will be asked to describe the administrative processes used in your department.
5.  Next, move to "administrative_operations" to describe the administrative operations performed in your department.
6.  On the final page, "review_findings", you will be asked to review and provide any findings or observations related to the audit.
7.  Finally, on the "shared_outcomes" page, you will be asked to share any outcomes or recommendations resulting from the audit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **university_administrative_operations_audit_form** (Page 1: Text Field)
	+ This field is a description of the administrative operation being audited.
* **contact_information** (Page 2: Text Field)
	+ This field is used to provide your contact details, such as your name and title.
* **administrative_processes** (Page 3: Text Field)
	+ This field is used to describe the administrative processes used in your department.
* **administrative_operations** (Page 4: Text Field)
	+ This field is used to describe the administrative operations performed in your department.
* **review_findings** (Page 5: Text Field)
	+ This field is used to review and provide any findings or observations related to the audit.
* **shared_outcomes** (Page 6: Text Field)
	+ This field is used to share any outcomes or recommendations resulting from the audit.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor for guidance.
- The data collected through this form will be used to improve the university's administrative operations.
