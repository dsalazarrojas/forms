# Learning Preference Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Learning Preference Survey is designed to understand your learning style and preferences. This information will help us create resources and content tailored to your individual needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form:

1. For each question, please select all options that apply to you.
2. Be honest with your answers; there are no right or wrong answers.
3. Take your time to think about each question carefully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Participant Info (`survey_participant_info`, text, required: false)**: This field is for collecting general information about you.
* **Dominant Learning Style (`dominant_learning_style`, select_one, required: false)**: Please select your dominant learning style:
	+ Visual
	+ Auditory
	+ Kinesthetic (hands-on learning)
	+ Tactile (touch-based learning)
	+ Vocal (speaking and listening)
* **Age Range (`age_range`, select_multiple, required: false)**: Please select all the age ranges that apply to you:
	+ 18-20 years
	+ 21-25 years
	+ 26-30 years
	+ 31-35 years
	+ 36-40 years
	+ 41-45 years
	+ 46-50 years
	+ 51-55 years
	+ 56-60 years
	+ 61-65 years
	+ 66-70 years
* **Educational History (`educational_history`, text, required: false)**: Describe your educational history.
* **Learning Environment (`learning_environment`, select_one, required: false)**: Please select where you usually learn:
	+ Classroom
	+ Lab
	+ Library
	+ Online
	+ Other
* **Learning Frequency (`learning_frequency`, select_multiple, required: false)**: Please select how often you learn:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Rarely
	+ Never
* **Time Allocation (`time_allocation`, number, required: false)**: Please enter the average amount of time you allocate for learning each day.
* **Preferred Education Medium (`preferred_education_medium`, select_one, required: false)**: Please select your preferred medium of education:
	+ Video
	+ Image
	+ Text
	+ Audios
	+ PPT
* **Preferred Education Method (`preferred_education_method`, select_multiple, required: false)**: Please select your preferred methods of learning:
	+ Online Lecture
	+ Offline Lecture
	+ Group Discussion
	+ Self Study
	+ Project Work
* **Preferred Education Platform (`preferred_education_platform`, select_multiple, required: false)**: Please select your preferred platforms for learning:
	+ YouTube
	+ Udemy
	+ Coursera
	+ edX
	+ Other

**Tips**

* Take your time to answer each question carefully.
* If you are unsure, please ask a colleague or instructor for guidance.
* The survey data will be kept confidential and anonymous.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete the survey!
