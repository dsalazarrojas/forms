# Educational Seminar Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from attendees about their experience at an educational seminar.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Provide your event organizer's details on the first page.
2. Enter the number of attendees on the second page.
3. Provide details about the seminar you attended.
4. Register your details on the fourth page, if necessary.
5. Add any additional information, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event Organizer
* **Event Organizer** (`event_organizer`, text, optional): Enter the name of the event organizer. This will help us identify the person behind the seminar.
### Attendees
* **Attendees** (`attendee_info`, text, optional): Enter the number of attendees. This will help us understand the scale of the event.
### Seminar Details
* **Seminar Details** (`seminar_details`, text, optional): Describe the seminar you attended. Be as detailed as possible about what you learned, liked, or disliked.
### Registration
* **Registration** (`registration_info`, text, optional): If you need to register for any additional event or follow-up, enter the details here.
### Additional Information
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, text, optional): If there's anything else you'd like to share about your experience, this is the place to do it.

## Tips
* Be honest and as detailed as possible in your answers.
* Use clear and concise language to help us understand your feedback.
* You can add any supporting documents or images to your answers, if needed.
