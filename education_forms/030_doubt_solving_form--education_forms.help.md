# Doubt Solving Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Doubt Solving Form is a tool used to collect information about a user's doubts, concerns, or questions in the Education and Customer Service categories. This form aims to gather relevant details to provide accurate support and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information, which includes:
	* **Enter Category** (Select One): Choose a category for your doubt (Education or Customer Service).
	* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number.
	* **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
	* **Time**: Enter the time of your doubt.
	* **Enter your doubt (2)**: Type a clear and concise description of your doubt.
	* **Select Category (4)**: Choose a category for your doubt (Education or Customer Service).
	* **Doubt**: Enter a brief description of your doubt (this field is a duplicate of Enter your doubt, please ignore it).
