# Conversions Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your knowledge on various types of conversions. It's divided into several sections, each focusing on a different type of conversion, such as length, weight, volume, time, and temperature conversions. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your Full Name and Email Address. Please ensure your email address is valid as we will be sending you the results of the quiz.
2. Select the correct answer for each conversion question.
3. Once you've completed the quiz, assess your confidence level in conversions.
4. Identify the topics that you need to review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (participant_name, text, required): Fill in your name as it will be displayed in the results.
- **Email Address** (email, email, required): Enter your valid email address to receive the results of the quiz.
- **School or Organization** (institution, text, optional): Enter the name of your school or organization, if applicable.
- **Grade or Education Level** (grade_level, select_one, optional): Select your current education level.
- **-- Length Conversions --**: This section includes questions on length conversions.
    - **How many centimeters are in 1 meter** (q1, select_one, required): Select the number of centimeters in 1 meter.
    - **How many feet are in 1 yard** (q2, select_one, required): Select the number of feet in 1 yard.
    - **How many inches are in 1 foot** (q3, select_one, required): Select the number of inches in 1 foot.
    - **How many millimeters are in 1 centimeter** (q4, select_one, required): Select the number of millimeters in 1 centimeter.
- **-- Weight Conversions --**: This section includes questions on weight conversions.
    - **How many grams are in 1 kilogram** (q5, select_one, required): Select the number of grams in 1 kilogram.
    - **How many ounces are in 1 pound** (q6, select_one, required): Select the number of ounces in 1 pound.
    - **How many pounds are in 1 ton US** (q7, select_one, required): Select the number of pounds in 1 ton US.
- **-- Volume Conversions --**: This section includes questions on volume conversions.
    - **How many milliliters are in 1 liter** (q8, select_one, required): Select the number of milliliters in 1 liter.
    - **How many cups are in 1 pint** (q9, select_one, required): Select the number of cups in 1 pint.
    - **How many quarts are in 1 gallon** (q10, select_one, required): Select the number of quarts in 1 gallon.
    - **How many fluid ounces are in 1 cup** (q11, select_one, required): Select the number of fluid ounces in 1 cup.
- **-- Time Conversions --**: This section includes questions on time conversions.
    - **How many seconds are in 1 minute** (q12, select_one, required): Select the number of seconds in 1 minute.
    - **How many minutes are in 1 hour** (q13, select_one, required): Select the number of minutes in 1 hour.
    - **How many hours are in 1 day** (q14, select_one, required): Select the number of hours in 1 day.
    - **How many days are in a leap year** (q15, select_one, required): Select the number of days in a leap year.
- **-- Temperature Conversions --**: This section includes questions on temperature conversions.
    - **What is the freezing point of water in Celsius** (q16, select_one, required): Select the freezing point of water in Celsius.
    - **What is the freezing point of water in Fahrenheit** (q17, select_one, required): Select the freezing point of water in Fahrenheit.
    - **What is the boiling point of water in Celsius** (q18, select_one, required): Select the boiling point of water in Celsius.
    - **What is the boiling point of water in Fahrenheit** (q19, select_one, required): Select the boiling point of water in Fahrenheit.
- **-- Practical Applications --**: This section includes questions on practical applications of conversions.
    - **If a recipe calls for 500ml of milk how many liters is this** (q20, select_one, required): Select the number of liters in 500ml of milk.
    - **If you weigh 70kg approximately how many pounds is this** (q21, select_one, required): Select the number of pounds in 70kg.
    - **A 5 kilometer race is approximately how many miles** (q22, select_one, required): Select the number of miles in a 5 kilometer race.
- **-- Self Assessment --**: This section is to assess your confidence level in conversions.
    - **How confident are you with conversions** (confidence_level, select_one, required): Select your confidence level.
    - **Topics you need to review** (topics_for_review, select_multiple, optional): Select the topics you need to review.
