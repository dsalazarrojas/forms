# E Portfolio Public Link Display Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The E Portfolio Public Link Display Consent Form is a form that collects consent from participants to display their e-portfolio links publicly. This form helps to establish a clear understanding between the participant and the institution or organization managing the e-portfolios.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the options for "Public Link Display Consent" and choose "Yes" to display your e-portfolio link publicly.
2. Enter your participant information in the "Participant Info" field.
3. Provide your student ID or participant ID in the "Student ID" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
5. Select your contact method in the "Contact Method" field.
6. Enter your contact method value in the "Contact Method Value" field.
7. Write a statement of consent in the "Consent Statement" field.
8. Sign the "Signature" field.
9. Select a date and time for the form completion in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
10. Answer "True" or "False" to indicate if you have read and understood the terms and conditions in the "Have You Read Terms and Conditions" field.
11. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you have agreed to the terms and conditions in the "Agreed to Terms and Conditions" field.
12. Enter any additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field.
13. Select an option in the "Option" field.
14. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you have agreed to the terms and conditions again in the "Agreed to Terms and Conditions" field (this field is identical to field 11).
15. Enter any additional information in the "Additional Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Public Link Display Consent** (`public_link_display_consent`, select_multiple, required): This field allows you to select if you consent to display your e-portfolio link publicly. Select "Yes" to display your e-portfolio link publicly.
* **Participant Info** (`participant_info`, text, optional): Enter any information about your participant role or affiliation.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as a participant.
* **Student ID/Participant ID** (`student_id`, select_one, optional): Select whether your ID is a Student ID or a Participant ID. If you select "Other", you can enter a custom value in the "Student ID/Participant ID" field.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, optional): Select how you would like to be contacted. Options are Email, Phone, or Other.
* **Contact Method Value** (`contact_method_value`, text, optional): If you selected "Other" in the Contact Method field, enter the value for your contact method.
* **Consent Statement** (`consent_statement`, text, optional): Write a statement of consent for displaying your e-portfolio link publicly.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, optional): Sign the form to confirm your consent.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select a date for the form completion.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select a time for the form completion.
* **Have You Read Terms and Conditions** (`form_12`, select_one, required): Answer "True" or "False" if you have read and understood the terms and conditions.
* **Agreed to Terms and Conditions** (`form_13`, select_multiple, required): Select "Yes" to indicate you have agreed to the terms and conditions.
* **Additional Comments** (`form_20`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments.
* **Option** (`form_18`, select_one, optional): Select an option.
* **Agreed to Terms and Conditions (Again)** (`form_19`, select_multiple, required): Select "Yes" to indicate you have agreed to the terms and conditions.
* **Additional Information** (`form_25`, text, optional): Enter any additional information. 

Note: Some fields seem to be redundant or duplicates in the provided YAML, these have been included here to maintain consistency with the original form structure.
