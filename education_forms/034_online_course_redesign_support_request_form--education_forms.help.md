# online_course_redesign_support_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture information from users who need help with redesigning an online course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields on the first page:
   - **Course Name**: The title of the course you need help with.
   - **Course ID**: A unique identifier for the course.
   - **Description**: A brief description of the issue you are experiencing with the course.
   - **Category**: The category of the course.
   - **Email**: Your contact email address.
   - **Phone**: Your contact phone number.
   - **Date Submitted**: The date you submitted the request.
   - **Time Submitted**: The time you submitted the request.
   - **Submitted By**: The person submitting the request.
2. If necessary, add an attachment to support your request on the second page.
3. Provide additional comments if you have any further information about your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Name**: The title of the course you are seeking help with. (<code>text</code>)
* **Course ID**: A unique identifier for the course. (<code>number</code>)
* **Description**: A brief description of the issue you are experiencing with the course. (<code>text</code>)
* **Category**: The category of the course. (<code>select_one</code>)
* **Email**: Your contact email address. (<code>email</code>)
* **Phone**: Your contact phone number. (<code>text</code>)
* **Attachment**: An optional file to support your request. (<code>note</code>)
* **Date Submitted**: The date you submitted the request. (<code>date</code>)
* **Time Submitted**: The time you submitted the request. (<code>time</code>)
* **Submitted By**: The person submitting the request. (<code>select_multiple</code>)
* **Comments**: Additional information about your request. (<code>text</code>)
* **Attachment Name**: The name of the attachment file. (<code>text</code>)
* **Attachment Description**: A description of the attachment. (<code>text</code>)
* **Attachment File**: The URL of the attachment file. (<code>text</code>)
* **Attachment Title**: The title of the attachment. (<code>text</code>)
* **Attachment Date**: The date of the attachment. (<code>date</code>)
* **Attachment Time**: The time of the attachment. (<code>time</code>)
* **Attachment User**: The user who uploaded the attachment. (<code>text</code>)
* **Attachment Tags**: Tags for the attachment. (<code>text</code>)
* **Attachment Notes**: Additional notes about the attachment. (<code>text</code>)
* **Attachment Comments**: Comments about the attachment. (<code>text</code>)

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure that your request is processed efficiently.
- If you have an attachment, make sure it is relevant to your request.
- Provide additional comments if you have any further information about your request.
