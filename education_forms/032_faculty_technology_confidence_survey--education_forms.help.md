# Faculty Technology Confidence Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Faculty Technology Confidence Survey is a questionnaire designed to assess the technology confidence of faculty members in our institution.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the survey questions carefully before starting.
2. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. Use the provided options for select and multiple-choice questions.
4. Enter dates and numbers exactly as they appear on your calendar or device.
5. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* Use specific examples or situations to support your answers.
