# Campus Recruiting Visit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Campus Recruiting Visit Form is designed to gather essential information from employers who want to conduct on-campus recruiting activities. This form helps the campus administration plan and prepare for a successful visit, ensuring a smooth experience for both the employer and the students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the employer information section, including company name, website, industry/field, and company size.
2. Provide contact information for the person visiting the campus, including their first and last name, job title, email address, and phone number.
3. Specify the visit details, such as visit purpose, preferred date, visit duration, number of recruiters, and space and technology requirements.
4. Confirm your agreement to the campus visit policies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, Text, Required): This is the name of your company.
* **Industry or Field** (`company_industry`, Text, Required): Please specify the industry or field related to your company.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, Select One, Required): Select the size of your company from the options: Startup (1-50), Small (51-250), Medium (251-1000), Large (1000+).
* **First Name** (`contact_first_name`, Text, Required): This is the first name of the contact person visiting the campus.
* **Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, Text, Required): This is the last name of the contact person visiting the campus.
* **Job Title** (`contact_title`, Text, Required): Please specify the job title of the contact person visiting the campus.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, Email, Required): Provide the email address of the contact person visiting the campus.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, Text, Required): Enter the phone number of the contact person visiting the campus.
* **Purpose of Campus Visit** (`visit_purpose`, Select One, Required): Specify the reason for the campus visit from the options: Job Fair, On-campus Interviews, Information Session, Internship Recruitment, Networking Event, Other.
* **Preferred Visit Date** (`preferred_visit_date`, Date, Required): Choose a preferred date for the visit.
* **Duration of Visit** (`visit_duration`, Select One, Required): Select the duration of the visit from the options: Half Day, Full Day, Multiple Days, Flexible.
* **Number of Recruiters** (`number_of_recruiters`, Number, Required): Enter the number of recruiters attending the visit.
* **Positions Recruiting For** (`positions_recruiting`, Text, Required): List the positions or job openings you are recruiting for.
* **Target Majors or Fields of Study** (`target_majors`, Text, Required): Specify the target majors or fields of study for the job openings.
* **Minimum GPA Requirement** (`minimum_gpa`, Number, Optional): If applicable, specify the minimum GPA required for the job openings.
* **Space Requirements** (`space_requirements`, Select One, Required): Select the space requirements from the options: Single Booth, Multiple Booths, Meeting Room, Auditorium, Custom Setup.
* **Technology or Equipment Needs** (`technology_needs`, Text, Optional): Provide any technology or equipment needs you have for the visit.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, Text, Optional): If you have any special requests, please list them here.
* **Agreement Confirmation** (`agreement_confirmation`, Select One, Required): Confirm your agreement to campus visit policies by selecting "Yes, I agree".

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* If you have any special requirements or requests, please provide detailed information to help us prepare for your visit.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact us at [your contact email or phone number].
