## Preschool Admissions Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for parents or guardians to provide information about their child's admission to our preschool program.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please go through each section carefully and provide the requested information in the corresponding fields. If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent's Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as the child's parent or guardian.
* **Child's Name** (`child_name`, text, optional): Enter your child's name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`birth_date`, date, optional): Enter your child's date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Parent's Phone Number** (`parent_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the contact number of the person who will be responsible for picking up the child from school.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the person who will be picking up the child from school.
* **Child's Age** (`child_age`, number, optional): Enter your child's age.
* **Preferred Schedule** (`preferred_schedule`, select multiple, optional): Select the days and times you prefer for your child's schedule.
* **Special Needs** (`special_needs`, select one, optional): If your child has any special needs, please select the relevant option.
* **School Years** (`school_years`, text, optional): Enter the number of years your child will be attending our preschool program.
* **Tuition Fees** (`tuition_fees`, number, optional): Enter the expected tuition fees you are willing to pay.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any doubts or questions, do not hesitate to contact us.
* We will be in touch with you shortly after receiving your completed form to discuss further details.

Note: The fields marked as "optional" are not mandatory, but providing accurate and complete information will ensure a smoother and faster admission process.
