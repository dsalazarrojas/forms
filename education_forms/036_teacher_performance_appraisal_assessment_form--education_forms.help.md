# Teacher Performance Appraisal Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback and evaluations from students about a teacher's performance in the classroom, which can help with professional development and other internal HR purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read each question carefully and answer it honestly.
* Answer all the questions that are relevant to your experience with the teacher.
* Use the select_one or select_multiple options to provide more detailed feedback.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, you can include them in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. `teacher-performance-appraisal-form` (Text field)
*   **Teacher Performance Appraisal Form**
    This field is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or feedback about the teacher's performance.

### 2. `student-feedback` (Text field)
*   **Student Feedback**
    This field is another text field where you can provide any comments or feedback from students about the teacher's performance.

### 3. `teacher-performance-appraisal-form-page-3` (Select_one field)
*   **Has the teacher consistently demonstrated a positive attitude?**
    This field allows you to select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the teacher consistently demonstrated a positive attitude in the classroom.

### 4. `teacher-performance-appraisal-form-page-4` (Select_multiple field)
*   **Which areas of the teacher's performance have they excelled in?**
    This field allows you to select multiple options to indicate areas where the teacher has excelled, such as "Yes" for each of the options.

### 5. `student-feedback-2` (Text field)
