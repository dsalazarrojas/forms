# Teacher Effectiveness Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to evaluate a teacher's performance in the classroom, focusing on their strengths and weaknesses. This assessment will help in identifying areas of improvement, providing valuable feedback for personal and professional growth.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Review the form carefully before starting to fill it out.
2. Answer each question truthfully and accurately.
3. Ensure that you provide specific examples or reasons to support your answers.
4. If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (General Information, text, optional): This section is a brief introduction to the evaluation form. Please provide any relevant background information about the teacher you are evaluating.
* **Teacher Performance** (Teacher Performance, number, optional): This field is used to assess the overall performance of the teacher. Please rate the teacher's performance on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
* **Teacher Strengths** (Teacher Strengths, select multiple, required): In this section, please select the strengths you have observed in the teacher. You can choose from the following options: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair.
* **Teacher Weaknesses** (Teacher Weaknesses, select one, required): This field is used to identify one area where the teacher needs improvement. Please select from the following options: 1, 2, 3, 4.
* **Parent Feedback** (Parent Feedback, text, optional): This section is for any additional feedback you may have as a parent or guardian. Please use this space to provide any comments or suggestions you have for the teacher.
* **Teacher Comments** (Teacher Comments, text, optional): This field is for any comments or suggestions you may have for the teacher. Please use this space to provide specific feedback or recommendations for improvement.

## Tips
* Be honest and objective in your assessment.
* Provide specific examples or reasons to support your answers.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification before proceeding.
* This form is intended to help the teacher grow and improve, so please be constructive in your feedback.
