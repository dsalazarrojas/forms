# learning_verification_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Learning Verification Survey" form is used to collect feedback and data from participants after a training session. It is designed to gather information about the effectiveness of the training, the participant's experience, and any comments they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
2. For each question, select the most accurate response from the options provided.
3. If a question does not apply to you, select "No" or "Not applicable" as the answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Feedback** (`participant_feedback`, text, required): This field is a text area where you can provide feedback about your experience in the training session. Please be as detailed as possible and try to cover all aspects of your experience.
* **How effective were the training sessions?** (`training_effectiveness`, select_one, required): This question is about how effective the training sessions were for you. Please select "Yes" if you found the training sessions helpful and informative, and "No" if you did not.
* **Were the training objectives met?** (`training_objectives`, select_multiple, required): This question is about whether the training objectives were met. Please select all the objectives that apply to your experience.
* **Participant Experience** (`participant_experience`, note, required): This field is a place to provide any additional information about your experience. It can be used to describe any positive or negative aspects of your experience that are not covered by the other questions.
* **Email** (`email_input`, email, required): Please enter your email address so that we can contact you for any follow-up questions or to send you any additional information.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Please enter your phone number so that we can contact you for any follow-up questions or to send you any additional information.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): This field is used to record your date of birth. Please enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of Completion** (`time_of_completion`, time, required): This field is used to record the time it took you to complete the training session. Please enter the time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Please provide any additional comments or feedback you may have about the training session.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Please enter your name so that we can associate your answers with your identity.
* **Participant Title** (`participant_title`, text, required): Please enter your title or position so that we can better understand your role in the training session.
