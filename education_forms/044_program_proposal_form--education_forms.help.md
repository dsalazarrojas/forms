# Program Proposal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from project proposers about their program proposals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill in the project title in the "project_title" field.
3. Enter a brief description of your project in the "description" field.
4. Select the type of project (Event, Project, or Initiative) in the "project_type" field.
5. Enter the project's budget in the "project_budget" field.
6. Enter the project's start and end dates in the "project_start_date" and "project_end_date" fields.
7. Describe the expected outcomes of your project in the "expected_outcomes" field.
8. Outline the expected impact of your project in the "expected_impact" field.
9. Specify the target audience for your project in the "target_audience" field.
10. Define the evaluation criteria for your project in the "evaluation_criteria" field.
11. Enter the submission date in the "submission_date" field.
12. Provide your contact email in the "contact_email" field.
13. Enter your contact phone number in the "contact_phone" field.
14. Select the status of your form in the "form_review" field (Not Started, In Progress, or Completed).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Title** (`project_title`, text, required): A brief title of your project.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): A brief description of your project.
* **Project Type** (`project_type`, select_one, required): The type of project (Event, Project, or Initiative).
* **Project Budget** (`project_budget`, number, required): The budget of your project.
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, date, required): The start date of your project.
* **Project End Date** (`project_end_date`, date, required): The end date of your project.
* **Expected Outcomes** (`expected_outcomes`, text, required): A description of the expected outcomes of your project.
* **Expected Impact** (`expected_impact`, text, required): A description of the expected impact of your project.
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, text, required): The target audience for your project.
* **Evaluation Criteria** (`evaluation_criteria`, text, required): The evaluation criteria for your project.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): The date of submission of your form.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Your contact email.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Your contact phone number.
* **Form Review** (`form_review`, select_one, required): The status of your form (Not Started, In Progress, or Completed).
