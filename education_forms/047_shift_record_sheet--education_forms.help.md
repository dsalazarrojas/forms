# Shift Record Sheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shift Record Sheet is a form used to collect information about shifts worked by staff members. It's essential to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure proper tracking and reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information before starting to fill out the form.
2. Select the relevant subject from the list provided in the "subject" field.
3. If relevant, choose the tutors assigned to the shift in the "tutor" field.
4. Enter the number of hours worked in the "hours" field.
5. Select the date of the shift from the calendar in the "shift_date" field.
6. Enter the start time of the shift in the "start_time" field.
7. Enter the end time of the shift in the "end_time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shift Record Sheet (shift_record_sheet)** (`shift_record_sheet`, text, required/optional): This is the title of the form, which is already filled in.
* **Subject (subject)** (`subject`, select_one, false): Select the subject of the shift from the list provided. This could be Math, Science, or English.
* **Tutor (tutor)** (`tutor`, select_multiple, false): Choose the tutors assigned to the shift. You can select one or multiple tutors, depending on the shift's requirements.
* **Hours (hours)** (`hours`, number, false): Enter the total number of hours worked for the shift.
* **Shift Date (shift_date)** (`shift_date`, date, false): Select the date of the shift from the calendar.
* **Start Time (start_time)** (`start_time`, time, false): Enter the start time of the shift.
* **End Time (end_time)** (`end_time`, time, false): Enter the end time of the shift.

## Tips
* Be accurate when entering information to ensure correct tracking and reporting.
* Review and update the form regularly to ensure it reflects the current shifts and information.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, consult with the relevant authorities or review the form's purpose and use case.
