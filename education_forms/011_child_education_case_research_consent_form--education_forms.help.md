# Child Education Case Research Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect consent from parents or guardians for a child's participation in educational research. It's essential to ensure that the researcher understands the child's identity and age eligibility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your research title and a brief description of the study.
2. Enter the child's full name and date of birth.
3. Select whether you consent to the child's participation.
4. Choose the specific research activities for which you consent.
5. Confirm that you understand the confidentiality protocols.
6. Provide your electronic signature with your full name.
7. Select the date of consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Research Study Title** (Text, required): Enter the title of the study provided by the lead investigator.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (Text, required): Enter your full legal name.
* **Child Full Name** (Text, required): Enter the child's full name.
* **Child Date of Birth** (Date, required): Enter the child's date of birth to verify age eligibility.
* **Do you consent to the child participation?** (Select One, required): Choose 'Yes - I Consent' if you agree to the child's participation.
* **Consent for Specific Research Activities** (Select Multiple, required): Select all the specific research activities for which you consent (e.g., One-on-one Interviews, Classroom Observation, etc.).
* **Consent for audio or video recording?** (Select One, required): Choose 'Yes - Audio and Video' if you agree to audio or video recordings.
* **Do you understand the confidentiality protocols?** (Select One, required): Confirm that you understand the confidentiality protocols.
* **Electronic Signature - Full Name** (Text, required): Type your full name for electronic signature.
* **Today Date** (Date, required): Select the date of this consent.
