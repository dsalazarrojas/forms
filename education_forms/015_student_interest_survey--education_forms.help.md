# Student Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about students' interests, school activities, lessons, and preferences to help schools or institutions understand their needs and tailor their programs or services accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the school year you are currently in.
2. Choose the activities you enjoy at school (e.g., Sports, Arts, Music, etc.).
3. Select the lessons you are taking (e.g., Math, Science, History, etc.).
4. Choose your expectations from the list (e.g., Improve skills, Have fun, Learn new things, etc.).
5. Indicate whether you enjoy school (True or False).
6. For optional fields, provide your school activities and lessons in both activities and lessons, select the time of day you are most active, select your phone number, email address, note any additional information, and provide your name, age, school name, grade, and parent name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Year** (ID 1, text, required: false): Enter the current school year you are in.
* **School Activities** (ID 2, select_multiple, required: true): Choose the activities you enjoy at school (e.g., Sports, Arts, Music, etc.).
* **School Lessons** (ID 3, select_one, required: false): Select the lessons you are taking (e.g., Math, Science, History, etc.).
* **Expectations** (ID 4, select_multiple, required: true): Choose your expectations from the list (e.g., Improve skills, Have fun, Learn new things, etc.).
* **Enjoy School** (ID 5, select_one, required: true): Indicate whether you enjoy school (True or False).
* **School Activities & Lessons** (ID 6, select_multiple, required: false): For optional fields, choose the activities and lessons you enjoy.
* **School Year 2** (ID 7, date, required: false): Enter the date of the second school year.
* **School Year 2 2** (ID 8, select_one, required: false): Select the season of the second school year (e.g., Summer, Fall, Winter, etc.).
* **School Time** (ID 9, time, required: false): Choose the time of day you are most active.
* **School Time 2** (ID 10, select_one, required: false): Select the time of day you are most active.
* **Phone** (ID 11, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (ID 12, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Note** (ID 13, note, required: false): Provide any additional information.
* **Student Name** (ID 14, text, required: false): Enter your name.
* **Age** (ID 15, number, required: false): Enter your age.
* **School Name** (ID 16, text, required: false): Enter the name of your school.
* **Grade** (ID 17, text, required: false): Enter your grade level.
* **Parent Name** (ID 18, text, required: false): Enter your parent's name.
