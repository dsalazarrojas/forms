# Campus Mental Wellness Ambassador Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from students who are interested in becoming a Mental Wellness Ambassador on campus. The ambassadors will be responsible for promoting mental wellness and providing support to their peers. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately. Make sure to provide all the necessary information.
2. If you have any questions or need help, feel free to contact the relevant authorities.
3. Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Student ID Number**: Please enter your student ID number, which can be found on your student ID card or in your student portal.
* **Email Address**: Please enter your email address that is affiliated with your university account.
* **Phone Number**: Please enter your phone number that can be reached at.
* **Class Year**: Please select your current class year (Freshman, Sophomore, Junior, Senior).
* **Major or Field of Study**: Please enter your major or field of study.
* **Why are you interested in being a Mental Wellness Ambassador?**: Please explain why you are interested in becoming a Mental Wellness Ambassador, and what qualities you think you can bring to the role.
* **How would you describe your own mental health?**: Please select one of the options that best describes your current mental health status (Excellent, Good, Fair, Struggling, Prefer not to say).
* **Experience with mental health or wellness initiatives**: If you have experience with mental health or wellness initiatives, please describe them briefly.
* **Key Strengths**: Please select the strengths you think you can bring to the role (Leadership, Communication, Empathy, Creativity, Organization, Problem-solving, Other).
* **Experience with peer support or mentoring**: If you have experience with peer support or mentoring, please describe them briefly.
* **Will you complete mental health training?**: Please select "True" if you are willing to complete the mental health training required for the ambassador role.
* **Hours per week available for the role**: Please select the number of hours you can commit to the role per week.
* **Semester Commitment**: Please select the semester in which you can commit to the role (Fall semester, Spring semester, Full year, As needed).
* **Activities interested in**: Please select the activities you are interested in as a Mental Wellness Ambassador (Event Planning, Peer Outreach, Workshop Facilitation, Social Media, One-on-one Support, Campus Advocacy).
* **Comfort discussing mental health topics**: Please select how comfortable you are in discussing mental health topics (Very Comfortable, Comfortable, Somewhat Comfortable, Not Comfortable).
* **Additional Information**: Please provide any additional information that may be relevant to your application.
* **Two References (names and phone numbers)**: Please provide the names and phone numbers of two references.
* **Additional Comments**: Please provide any additional comments about your application.
