## Fee Payment Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Fee Payment Checklist form is designed to record the payment of fees from students. It helps staff or administrators to track and verify the payment details of students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Student Name** field with the full name of the student.
2. Select the payment method from the options provided (Credit Card, Cash, Check, or Online Banking). If the payment method is not listed, you can type in an alternative method in the **Other Payment Method** field.
3. Enter the **Amount Paid** field with the exact amount paid.
4. Select the **Payment Date** field to enter the date of payment.
5. If applicable, choose the **Payment Time** field to record the exact time of payment. Note that this field is optional.
6. Enter the **Student ID** field with the student's unique identification number.
7. Select the **Fee Type** field to specify the type of fee paid (Regular, Late, or Discounted).
8. You can add any additional notes or comments in the **Note** field if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (text, required): Enter the full name of the student.
* **Payment Method** (select_one, required): Select the method of payment used (Credit Card, Cash, Check, or Online Banking). If the payment method is not listed, you can type in an alternative method in **Other Payment Method** field.
* **Amount Paid** (number, required): Enter the exact amount paid.
* **Payment Date** (date, required): Select the date of payment using the date picker.
* **Payment Time** (time, optional): Choose the exact time of payment. Note that this field is optional.
* **Student ID** (text, required): Enter the student's unique identification number.
* **Fee Type** (select_one, required): Select the type of fee paid (Regular, Late, or Discounted).
* **Other Payment Method** (text, optional): If the payment method is not listed, enter the alternative method used.
* **Note** (note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the payment.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately to avoid any discrepancies or errors in the payment record.
* If the payment method is not listed, use the **Other Payment Method** field to record the alternative method used.
* You can use the **Note** field to add additional information about the payment.
* This form is meant to be used by staff or administrators to record and verify the payment details of students.
