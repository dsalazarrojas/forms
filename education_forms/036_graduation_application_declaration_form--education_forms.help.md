# graduation_application_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Graduation Application Declaration Form is a document used to declare a student's graduation status. It is a required form for students who have completed their academic program and wish to update their status in the university's records.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the following required fields:
	* **Student Information** (page 1): Enter your name, student ID number, and other relevant information.
	* **Graduation Date** (page 2): Enter the date you expect to graduate.
	* **Major** (page 2): Select your major from the options provided.
	* **Student Status** (page 3): Select your current status as a student (Active or Inactive).
2. Fill in the optional fields:
	* **University Name** (page 2): If you are currently enrolled in a university, enter the name of your university.
	* **Expected Graduation Date** (page 2): If you expect to graduate at a different date than your graduation date, enter that date here.
	* **GPA** (page 3): Select your current GPA from the provided options.
	* **Academic Program** (page 4): Select your academic program from the options provided.
	* **Graduation Ceremony Date** (page 4): Enter the date of your graduation ceremony.
	* **Faculty Review** (page 4): Select the status of your faculty review (Approved, Pending, or Rejected).
3. Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information**: Enter your name, student ID number, and other relevant information.
* **Graduation Date**: Enter the date you expect to graduate.
* **Major**: Select your major from the options provided. This will help us track your degree requirements.
* **University Name**: If you are currently enrolled in a university, enter the name of your university.
* **Expected Graduation Date**: If you expect to graduate at a different date than your graduation date, enter that date here.
* **GPA**: Select your current GPA from the provided options.
* **Student Status**: Select your current status as a student (Active or Inactive).
* **Graduation Date Confirmation**: Enter the exact date of your graduation (if known).
* **Student Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm your information.
* **Academic Program**: Select your academic program from the options provided.
* **Graduation Ceremony Date**: Enter the date of your graduation ceremony.
* **Faculty Review**: Select the status of your faculty review (Approved, Pending, or Rejected).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and honestly.
* Double-check your information before submitting.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities.
