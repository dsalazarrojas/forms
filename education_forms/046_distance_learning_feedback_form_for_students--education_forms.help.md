# Distance Learning Feedback Form For Students - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from students about their distance learning experience. Your opinions and suggestions will help us improve our programs and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Student ID, if you have one.
2. Share your honest opinion about the distance learning class.
3. Provide any comments or suggestions you have for improvement.
4. Enter your email address and phone number, if you want to be contacted for follow-up.
5. Click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Id** (`student_id`, number, optional): Enter your Student ID if you have one.
* **What do you think about this distance learning class?** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share your honest opinion about the class. Be as specific as possible.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have for improvement.
* **Suggestions for improvement** (`suggestions`, text, optional): Suggest changes or improvements for the distance learning class.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for follow-up purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for follow-up purposes.
* **Please enter your email for follow-up** (`email_hint`, text, optional): This is a duplicate field for your email address, for your convenience.
* **Please enter your phone number for follow up** (`phone_hint`, text, optional): This is a duplicate field for your phone number, for your convenience.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click this button to send your feedback to us.

## Tips
* Please be honest and specific in your feedback.
* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
