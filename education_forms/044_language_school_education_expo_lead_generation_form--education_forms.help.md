# language-school-education-expo-lead-generation-form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to gather information from potential students or language learners about their experience, interests, and demographics in order to generate leads for language school events.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Determine if you have attended a language school event in the past.
2. Select the first language you attended.
3. Choose your experience level with the language (if applicable).
4. Indicate the location of the event (if applicable).
5. List your interests (max 3).
6. Confirm if the first language you are interested in is your preferred language.
7. Choose your level of knowledge for the selected language (if applicable).
8. Provide any additional details about your knowledge level (if different from the options).
9. Enter your contact information and name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Attended** (`event_attended`, select multiple, required: false): Check "Yes" if you have attended a language school event in the past.
* **First Language Attended** (`first_language_attended`, text, required: false): If you have attended a language school event, specify the first language you were exposed to.
* **Language Experience** (`language_experience`, select one, required: false): Choose your level of experience with the first language you attended.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required: false): If you attended an event, enter the location.
* **Student Interests** (`student_interests`, text, required: false): List up to three interests with commas separated values.
* **First Language Preferred** (`first_language_preferred`, select one, required: false): Confirm if the first language you are interested in is your preferred language.
* **Student Level** (`student_level`, select multiple, required: false): Choose your level of knowledge for the selected language (High/Low).
* **Student Level Other** (`student_level_other`, text, required: false): If your level of knowledge is different, provide a description.
* **Student Contact** (`student_contact`, text, required: false): Enter your preferred contact method.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name.
