# Cheder Reading Log - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Cheder Reading Log is used to track and record student reading progress, including weekly reading minutes and assignments.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the student's full name in the "Student Full Name" field.
2. Select the correct grade or class from the "Grade / Class" field.
3. Enter the week's ending date in the "Week Ending Date" field.
4. Record the reading minutes for each day of the week in the corresponding fields (Sunday, Monday, etc.).
5. Enter the book or assignment title in the "Book or Sefer Title" field.
6. If necessary, provide comments or observations about the reading experience in the "Parent Comments / Observations" field.
7. Sign the form with your parent or guardian name in the "Parent / Guardian Name" field.
8. Confirm that the reading times are accurate by selecting "Certified by Parent" in the "Log Certification Statement" field.
9. If you are a staff member, enter your initials in the "Teacher Initials (Staff Use)" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Full Name**: Enter the student's full name as it appears on their official documents.
* **Grade / Class**: Select the student's grade or class from the available options.
* **Week Ending Date**: Select the Sunday ending date of the week being logged.
* **Sunday**: Record the number of minutes the student read on Sunday.
* **Monday**: Record the number of minutes the student read on Monday.
* **Tuesday**: Record the number of minutes the student read on Tuesday.
* **Wednesday**: Record the number of minutes the student read on Wednesday.
* **Thursday**: Record the number of minutes the student read on Thursday.
* **Friday**: Record the number of minutes the student read on Friday.
* **Shabbos**: Record the number of minutes the student read on Shabbos (likely a reference to Shabbat, a day of rest).
* **Book or Sefer Title**: Enter the title of the book or assignment being read.
* **Current Page or Chapter Reached**: If necessary, enter the current page or chapter number reached in the book.
* **How was the reading difficulty**: Select the level of difficulty from the options (Too Easy, Just Right, Challenging, Too Difficult).
* **Parent Comments / Observations**: If you have any comments or observations about the reading experience, enter them here.
* **Parent / Guardian Name**: Enter your name as the parent or guardian.
* **Log Certification Statement**: Confirm that the reading times are accurate by selecting "Certified by Parent".
* **Teacher Initials (Staff Use)**: If you are a staff member, enter your initials here.
