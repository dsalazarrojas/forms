# Student Statement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Statement Form is designed for students to provide their personal and academic information. It collects demographic data to help create a comprehensive student profile.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the First name field.
2. Enter your last name in the Last name field.
3. Enter your date of birth in the Date of birth field.
4. Enter your student number in the Student number field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name (First name, text, required/optional)**: Enter your first name.
* **Last name (Last name, text, required/optional)**: Enter your last name.
* **Date of birth (Date of birth, date, required/optional)**: Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format, e.g., 1990-01-01.
* **Student number (Student number, text, required/optional)**: Enter your student identification number.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your date of birth in the correct format.
* Enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure your profile is complete and accurate.
