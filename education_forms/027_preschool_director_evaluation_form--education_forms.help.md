# Preschool Director Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the director's performance in various aspects of their job.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read each section and ensure you understand what each field is asking.
2. Rate each field based on your assessment, using the available rating options (typically 1-5 or 1-10).
3. Be as objective as possible, avoiding any personal biases or emotions.
4. Complete the form thoroughly, ensuring all fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Rating (1)**: Rate the director's overall performance as an educator and leader.
* **Director's Communication (2)**: Assess how well the director communicates with students and parents.
* **Director's Friendliness (3)**: Evaluate how approachable and friendly the director is.
* **Teacher Support (4)**: Rate how well teachers support each other and the director.
* **Teacher Expertise (5)**: Assess the level of expertise among teachers.
* **Facilities Availability (6)**: Rate the maintenance and availability of facilities.
* **Director's Conflict Handling (7)**: Evaluate how well the director handles conflicts.
* **Director's Availability (8)**: Assess how available the director is to answer questions.
* **Director's Patience (9)**: Rate the director's patience with students.
* **Director's Time Management (10)**: Evaluate how effectively the director manages time.
* **Director's Leadership (11)**: Assess the director's leadership skills.
* **Director's Attitude (12)**: Rate the director's overall attitude.
* **Director's Emergency Handling (13)**: Evaluate how well the director handles emergency situations.
* **Director's Commitment to Diversity and Inclusion (14)**: Assess the director's commitment to diversity and inclusion.
* **Director's Classroom Environment (15)**: Rate the safety and engagement of the classroom environment.
* **Director's Student Engagement (16)**: Evaluate how well the director engages students in activities.
* **Director's Behavior Management (17)**: Assess how well the director manages classroom behavior.
* **Director's Overall Effectiveness (18)**: Rate the director's overall effectiveness.
