# Student Photo Contest Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Photo Contest Entry Form is designed to collect entries from students who would like to participate in a photography contest. The form is meant to gather relevant information about each submission, such as name, email, photo title, description, categories, and more.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the **student_name** field.
2. Enter your email address in the **student_email** field.
3. Provide a title for your photo in the **photo_title** field.
4. Describe your photo in the **photo_description** field.
5. Select the categories that best fit your photo's genre from the **categories** field. You can choose more than one category.
6. Select the submitter of your photo from the **submitter** field.
7. Enter the URL of your photo in the **photo_url** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **student_name** (text): Enter your name as a student.
* **student_email** (email, optional): Enter your email address. Please note that this field is optional, but providing your email address will allow us to contact you with updates about your entry.
* **photo_title** (text, optional): Enter a title for your photo.
* **photo_description** (text, optional): Describe your photo in a few words.
* **categories** (select_multiple, optional): Select the categories that best fit your photo's genre. You can choose more than one category.
* **submitter** (select_one, optional): Select who took the photo: yourself, a friend, or a family member.
* **photo_url** (text, optional): Enter the URL of your photo.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately.
* If you are not sure about any field, please ask the contest organizers for clarification.
* Please note that providing an email address is optional, but it's recommended to facilitate communication.
