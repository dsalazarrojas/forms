# Teaching Session Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students after a teaching session. It allows students to share their experiences, thoughts, and opinions about the session. This feedback will help trainers improve their teaching methods, materials, and overall session quality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form completely and accurately.
2. Answer all required and optional questions.
3. Review and submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header (form_header)**: Enter the title of the session that you are providing feedback for.
* **Feedback Question 1 (feedback_question_1)**: Share your thoughts and opinions about the session.
* **Feedback Question 2 (feedback_question_2)**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about the session.
* **Evaluation (evaluation_question)**: Indicate whether you found the session helpful or not.
* **Trainer Name (trainer_name)**: Enter the name of the trainer who conducted the session.
* **Trainer Email (trainer_email)**: Enter the email address of the trainer.
* **Trainer Phone (trainer_phone)**: Enter the phone number of the trainer.
* **Trainer Notes (trainer_notes)**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the trainer.
* **Student Name (student_name)**: Enter your name.
* **Student Email (student_email)**: Enter your email address.
* **Student Phone (student_phone)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Student Notes (student_notes)**: Provide any additional comments or notes about yourself.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when answering all questions.
* Use clear and concise language.
* Proofread your responses before submitting the form.
