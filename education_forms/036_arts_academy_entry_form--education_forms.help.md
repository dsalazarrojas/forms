# Arts Academy Entry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Arts Academy Entry Form is designed to help us get to know you better. Please take a few minutes to complete this form and we'll be in touch soon.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first and last name in the respective fields.
2. Provide your date of birth.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Provide your current address.
6. Enter your city and state or province.
7. Enter your zip or postal code.
8. If applicable, provide the name of your parent or guardian.
9. Enter their contact number.
10. If you have attended any previous school or education, please provide details.
11. Select the program of interest to you (Painting and Drawing, Sculpture and Ceramics, Music Performance, Dance, Theater, Digital Arts, Mixed Media, or Foundation Year).
12. Choose your experience level in the chosen art form (Beginner, Some Experience, Intermediate, or Advanced).
13. If you have had any prior training or instruction, please provide details.
14. Indicate if you have attached your portfolio or work samples.
15. If you have not attached your portfolio, select the option to submit it later.
16. Write a brief artist statement or essay explaining why you want to attend this academy.
17. Indicate if you need financial aid or a scholarship.
18. Select your requested enrollment start date.
19. Choose how you heard about the academy (Website, Social Media, Word of mouth, School Counselor, Arts Organization, or Other).
20. Provide any additional information you would like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`student_first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`student_last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Email Address** (`student_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Current Address** (`current_address`, `text`, required): Enter your current address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the city of your current address.
* **State or Province** (`state_province`, `text`, required): Enter the state or province of your current address.
* **Zip or Postal Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter the zip or postal code of your current address.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent Contact Number** (`parent_contact`, `text`, required): Enter the contact number of your parent or guardian.
* **Previous School or Education** (`previous_education`, `text`, optional): Provide details about your previous school or education.
* **Program of Interest** (`program_of_interest`, `select_one`, required): Select the program that interests you the most.
* **Experience Level** (`experience_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose your experience level in the chosen art form.
* **Prior Training or Instruction** (`prior_training`, `text`, optional): Provide details about any prior training or instruction you have received.
* **Portfolio or Work Samples Attached** (`portfolio_attached`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you have attached your portfolio or work samples.
* **Artist Statement or Essay** (`artist_statement`, `text`, required): Write a brief statement or essay explaining why you want to attend this academy.
* **Financial Aid or Scholarship Needed** (`financial_aid_needed`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if you need financial aid or a scholarship.
* **Requested Enrollment Start Date** (`enrollment_start_date`, `date`, required): Enter your requested enrollment start date.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`referral_source`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how you heard about the academy.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information you would like to share.

## Tips
* Please make sure to double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
* If you are unable to attach your portfolio, select the option to submit it later.
* Write a clear and concise artist statement that explains why you want to attend this academy.
* Be specific about your experience level in the chosen art form.
* If you have any additional information, please provide it in the additional information field.
