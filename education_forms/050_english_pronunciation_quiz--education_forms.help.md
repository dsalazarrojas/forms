# English Pronunciation Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your understanding of English pronunciation and gather information about your practice habits and language proficiency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer each question according to your understanding of English pronunciation. You will be asked to choose between options for select_one questions, and provide a single answer for text questions. If you're unsure about the correct pronunciation, don't worry! This form is meant to be a helpful tool for you to gauge your own knowledge and identify areas for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Correct Pronunciation of "hello"** (Text, Optional): This question asks you to type out the correct pronunciation of the word "hello".
* **Correct Pronunciation of "cat"** (Select One, Optional): Choose the correct option that best represents the pronunciation of the word "cat".
* **Pronunciation of "dog"** (Text, Optional): This question asks you to type out the correct pronunciation of the word "dog".
* **Correct Pronunciation of "mouse"** (Select One, Optional): Choose the correct option that best represents the pronunciation of the word "mouse".
* **Pronunciation of "apple"** (Text, Optional): This question asks you to type out the correct pronunciation of the word "apple".
* **Pronunciation of "bank"** (Select One, Optional): Choose the correct option that best represents the pronunciation of the word "bank" (note: this question is intentionally labeled incorrectly; the correct pronunciation of "bank" is /ˈbæŋk/, but we'll use /bæŋk/ as the option for this example).
* **Pronunciation of "phone"** (Text, Optional): This question asks you to type out the correct pronunciation of the word "phone".
* **How often do you practice English pronunciation?** (Number, Optional): Choose a number that best describes how often you practice English pronunciation.
* **When do you usually practice English pronunciation?** (Date, Optional): Enter a date and time when you usually practice English pronunciation.
* **Are you a native English speaker?** (Checkbox, Optional): Check if you are a native English speaker.
* **Which languages do you speak fluently besides English?** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose all the languages you speak fluently besides English.
* **Additional languages you speak fluently** (Text, Optional): This question allows you to provide any other languages you speak fluently that are not listed.
