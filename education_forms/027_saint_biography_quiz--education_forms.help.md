# Saint Biography Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Saint Biography Quiz is a form used to collect information about a saint's biography, including their date of birth and death, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct responses to each question.
2. Fill in the required fields with the correct information.
3. Leave optional fields blank unless you have information to fill them in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Quiz Responses** (Select Multiple, required):
  This field asks you to select all the correct responses to the saint's biography questions.
  Please choose the correct options from the dropdown list.
* **Participant Name** (Text, required):
  Please enter the name of the participant who is completing this form.
  This will help us identify who completed the form.
* **Participant Email** (Email, optional):
  If you have a valid email address, enter it here.
  This will help us contact you if we need more information.
* **Participant Phone** (Text, optional):
  If you have a phone number, enter it here.
  This will help us contact you if we need more information.
* **Saint Birthday** (Date, required):
  Enter the date of the saint's birthday in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Saint Birthday Year** (Number, required):
  Enter the year of the saint's birthday.
* **Saint Birthday Month** (Number, required):
  Enter the month of the saint's birthday.
* **Saint Birthday Time** (Time, required):
  Enter the time of the saint's birthday in the format 12:00 PM.
* **Saint Died** (Date, required):
  Enter the date of the saint's death in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Saint Died Year** (Number, required):
  Enter the year of the saint's death.
* **Saint Died Month** (Number, required):
  Enter the month of the saint's death.
* **Saint Died Day** (Number, required):
  Enter the day of the saint's death.
* **Saint Died Time** (Time, required):
  Enter the time of the saint's death in the format 12:00 PM.
* **Saint Name** (Text, required):
  Enter the name of the saint.
* **Saint Title** (Text, required):
  Enter the title of the saint.
* **Saint Order** (Number, required):
  Enter the order of the saint in your collection.
* **Saint Description** (Text, optional):
  If you have a brief description of the saint, enter it here.
* **Saint Biography** (Note, required):
  Enter a brief biography of the saint.
* **Created By** (Text, required):
  Enter the name of the person who created this form.
* **Created Date** (Date, optional):
  If you have the date the form was created, enter it here.
* **Updated By** (Text, required):
  Enter the name of the person who updated this form.
* **Updated Date** (Date, optional):
  If you have the date the form was updated, enter it here.
* **Created By Date** (Date, optional):
  If you have the date this form was created, enter it here.
* **Updated By Date** (Date, optional):
  If you have the date this form was updated, enter it here.
