# Lifesaving Skills Video Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Lifesaving Skills Video Feedback" form is used to collect feedback from participants on their experience with a training program, specifically on the lifesaving skills they learned.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for this feedback submission.
2. Provide your personal feedback on your experience with the training program.
3. Share your feedback on the trainer's performance and support.
4. Rate the overall quality of the training program (1-5).
5. Rate the quality of the training materials provided (1-5).
6. Rate your achievement of the learning outcomes (1-5).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, false): Enter a brief title for this feedback submission (e.g., "Lifesaving Skills Feedback").
* **Participant Feedback** (`participant_feedback`, text, false): Share your thoughts on your experience with the training program. What did you like or dislike about it?
* **Trainer Feedback** (`trainer_feedback`, text, false): Share your feedback on the trainer's performance and support. How did they engage you in the training?
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, false): Rate the overall quality of the training program (1-5). How would you rate your experience?
* **Training Materials** (`training_materials_rating`, text, false): Rate the quality of the training materials provided (1-5). How clear and comprehensive were the materials?
* **Learning Outcomes** (`learning_outcomes_rating`, text, false): Rate your achievement of the learning outcomes (1-5). How relevant and applicable were the skills you learned?

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback, so we can better understand your experience.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas on how to improve the training program, please share them with us.
* If you have already completed this form in the past, please do not submit duplicate feedback.
