# Knowledge Assessment On Religious Figures - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gauge users' knowledge on religious figures, helping us understand their understanding and application of religious principles. Please note that you can only submit one assessment per religious leader at a time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reading the introduction, which provides background information on the assessment.
2. In the "Religious Leaders" section, select all the religious figures you are familiar with. You can choose multiple options.
3. The "Assessment" section is where you will provide your numerical evaluation of the religious figure's impact on their respective faith or world events.
4. In the "Additional Comments" section, you can provide any further explanations or insights related to your assessment.
5. Please provide your contact information, including your Email and Phone number.
6. Finally, enter the date and time of your assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, text, required/optional): This is where you can read about the background information on the assessment and understand what the form is asking you to do.
* **Religious Leaders** (`religious_leaders`, select_multiple, required): Choose all the religious figures you are familiar with. This is where you can demonstrate your understanding of various religious leaders and their roles in history.
* **Assessment** (`assessment`, number, required/optional): Provide a numerical evaluation of the religious figure's impact on their respective faith or world events. This is where you can apply your knowledge and demonstrate critical thinking.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, required/optional): If you need to provide further explanation or insights, you can do so here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Please provide your contact information for future reference.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you completed the assessment.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time you completed the assessment.
* **Score** (`score`, number, required/optional): Not applicable in this version of the form.

Tips: If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
