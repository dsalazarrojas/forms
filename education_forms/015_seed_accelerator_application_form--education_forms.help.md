# Seed Accelerator Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This Seed Accelerator Application Form is a comprehensive guide for entrepreneurs to submit an application for our seed accelerator program. The purpose of this form is to gather all necessary information for evaluation and review.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Introduction**: Briefly introduce yourself and your company, and explain why you are interested in joining our seed accelerator program.
2. **Contact Details**: Select your contact details accurately, this will help us get in touch with you for further discussion.
3. **Company Details**: Select your company details accurately, this will help us understand your business model.
4. **Idea Description**: Describe your business idea and its potential impact.
5. **Mentorship Goals**: If you are interested in mentorship, describe your mentorship goals.
6. **Start and End Dates**: Enter the start and end dates of your program.
7. **Program Length**: Enter the length of your program.
8. **Program Start**: Confirm whether you are ready to start the program.
9. **Application Type**: Select the type of application that suits your business.
10. **Application Deadline**: Enter the deadline for your application.
11. **Program Focus Areas**: Select the focus areas of your program.
12. **Startup Equipment**: Select the equipment you have or need for your startup.
13. **Team Experience**: If you have an existing team, describe their experience.
14. **Team Goals**: If you have an existing team, describe their goals.
15. **Business Plans**: Confirm whether you have a business plan.
16. **Financial Support**: Select the financial support you require.
17. **Mentorship Goals**: If you are interested in mentorship, describe your mentorship goals.
18. **Mentorship Support**: Select whether you require mentorship support.
19. **Program Commitment**: If you are accepted into the program, describe your commitment level.
20. **Communication Channels**: Select the communication channels you are comfortable with.
21. **Communication Frequencies**: Select the communication frequencies you prefer.
22. **Program Commitment Level**: Select your expected level of commitment to the program.
23. **Startup Equipment Details**: Describe your startup equipment details (if any).
24. **Submission Date**: Enter the date of your submission.
