# Educational Technology Pilot Filming Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a consent form for the educational technology pilot filming. Please provide your consent by completing the form and submitting it before the filming begins.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please review the form carefully and ensure that you understand the information required.
2. Enter your details and submit the form to provide your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Filming Consent** (`Filming Consent`, number, optional): Please select your level of consent for the filming (e.g., 1, 2, 3).
* **Filming Purpose** (`Filming Purpose`, text, optional): Describe the purpose of the filming, e.g., "To document a lecture" or "To record a tutorial".
* **Filming Location** (`Filming Location`, text, optional): Specify the location where the filming will take place, e.g., "Lecture hall" or "Classroom".
* **Filming Duration** (`Filming Duration`, date, optional): Enter the duration of the filming in "MM/DD/YY" format, e.g., "10/12/2024 12:00-14:00".
* **Filming Date** (`Filming Date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the filming in "MM/DD/YY" format, e.g., "10/12/2024".
* **Filming Start Time** (`Filming Start Time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the filming in "HH:MM" format, e.g., "12:00".
* **Filming End Time** (`Filming End Time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of the filming in "HH:MM" format, e.g., "14:00".
* **Filming Location Details** (`Filming Location Details`, text, optional): Provide more details about the filming location, e.g., "Room 101, Main Building, University Campus".

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have reviewed and understand the information required in this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the project team.
* Please submit the form once you have reviewed and understood the information required.
