# Scholarship Applicant Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from applicants to the scholarship program. Please fill out the form thoroughly to ensure we receive all necessary details about you.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the "Scholarship Applicant Information Form" in its entirety, ensuring all required fields are filled in.
2. Answer all questions truthfully, without any omissions.
3. Upload any necessary documents, if applicable.
4. Review your answers before submitting, ensuring accuracy and completeness.
5. Click the "Submit" button to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name** (text, required): Enter your full name, including your first and last names.
* **date_of_birth** (date, required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **gender** (select_one, optional): Choose your gender (Yes or No).
* **country** (select_one, optional): Select your country of origin (Yes or No).
* **address** (text, optional): Enter your current address.
* **contact_email** (email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **phone_2** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **nationality** (select_one, optional): Choose your nationality (Yes or No).
* **school_name** (text, optional): Enter the name of your school.
* **school_level** (select_one, optional): Choose your school level (High or Low).
* **grade_level** (number, optional): Enter your current grade level.
* **major_subject** (select_one, optional): Choose your major subject (Yes or No).
* **family_size** (select_one, optional): Choose your family size (Yes or No).
* **scholarship_type** (select_one, optional): Choose the type of scholarship you are applying for (Yes or No).
* **basic_info** (text, optional): Enter any additional information about yourself.
* **personal_info** (text, optional): Enter any additional information about your personal background.
* **educational_info** (text, optional): Enter any additional information about your educational background.
* **family_info** (text, optional): Enter any additional information about your family.
* **scholarship_info** (text, optional): Enter any additional information about your scholarship goals.
* **uploaded_documents** (select_multiple, optional): Choose which documents you have uploaded.
* **email_verification** (email, optional): Verify your email address.
* **phone_verification** (text, optional): Verify your phone number.
* **submit** (text, optional): Click this button to submit your form.

Note: All fields with a checkbox/ radio button will have "Yes" or "No" options. These correspond to "Yes" or "No" for the respective field. Please answer truthfully.
