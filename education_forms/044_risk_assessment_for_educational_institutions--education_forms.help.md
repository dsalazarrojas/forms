# risk_assessment_for_educational_institutions - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "risk_assessment_for_educational_institutions" form is designed to gather information from educational institutions to assess their risks and vulnerabilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of your educational institution in the "Educational Institution" field.
2. Provide the name and contact information of the person responsible for completing this form.
3. Select the risk areas that are relevant to your institution from the list provided.
4. Describe the risk factors that you have identified.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Educational Institution
**Educational Institution (`institution_name`, text, optional):**
Enter the name of your educational institution.

### 2. Contact Person
**Contact Person (`contact_person`, text, optional):**
Provide the name and contact information of the person responsible for completing this form.

### 3. Risk Areas
**Risk Areas (`risk_areas`, select_multiple, optional):**
Select all the risk areas that are relevant to your institution from the list provided.

### 4. Risk Factors
**Risk Factors (`risk_factors`, text, optional):**
Describe the risk factors that you have identified.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Only select risk areas that are relevant to your institution.
* Describe risk factors in detail, providing as much information as possible.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, do not hesitate to contact the relevant authorities for guidance.
