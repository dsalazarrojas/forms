# Student Leadership Development Impact Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Leadership Development Impact Survey is designed to collect data on the impact of leadership development programs in schools.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your school's name in the "School Name" field.
2. Enter your school's location in the "School Location" field.
3. Enter your school's email address in the "School Email" field.
4. Enter your school's phone number in the "School Phone" field.
5. Select the programs your school offers (if applicable) in the "School Programs" field.
6. Enter the date you took the survey in the "Survey Date" field.
7. Select the year you are reporting on in the "School Year" field.
8. Enter your name in the "Student Name" field.
9. Enter your email address in the "Student Email" field.
10. Enter your phone number in the "Student Phone" field.
11. Select your program (if applicable) in the "Student Program" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name**: Enter the name of your school.
* **School Location**: Enter the location of your school.
* **School Email**: Enter your school's email address.
* **School Phone**: Enter your school's phone number.
* **School Programs**: Select the programs your school offers, if applicable.
* **Survey Date**: Enter the date you took the survey.
* **School Year**: Select the year you are reporting on.
* **Student Name**: Enter your name.
* **Student Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Student Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Student Program**: Select your program (if applicable).

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for all fields.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact your school's administration or the form administrator for assistance.
* The survey will only take a few minutes to complete, and your input is invaluable to help improve leadership development programs in schools.
