# social_justice_in_education_network_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in or actively working towards social justice in the education sector. It aims to connect like-minded individuals and organizations to facilitate collaboration and networking opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Personal Details** in the field provided. This section requires your first and last name.
2. Select one or multiple **Interests** from the dropdown list that align with your current or future work.
3. Read and agree to the **Consent** statement, which outlines the intended use of your data. This is a non-editable note field.
4. Enter your **Contact Details** in the field provided. This will be used for further communication and connection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Details (1)**: Please enter your first and last name in the text field.
* **Interests (2)**: Select one or multiple interests from the dropdown list that align with your current or future work. This will help us connect you with others who share similar goals.
* **Consent (3)**: This is a non-editable note field that provides information on how your data will be used. Please review it carefully and agree to it.
* **Contact Details (4)**: Enter your contact information, which will be used for further communication and connection.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have not previously registered with this form to avoid duplication of records.
* If you have any issues or questions while filling out this form, feel free to contact us for assistance.
