# Percent Change Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Percent Change Knowledge Quiz is designed to evaluate the user's knowledge on percent change.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Select "Yes" or "No" for percent_change_knowledge_quiz_question**: This field asks whether you are knowledgeable about percent change. Please choose the correct answer according to your understanding.
2. **Provide the correct answer for correct_answer**: This field allows you to select the correct answer related to percent change.
3. **Enter your answer for percent_change_knowledge_quiz_answer**: This field is for entering your thoughts or understanding related to percent change.
4. **Enter your result for percent_change_knowledge_quiz_result**: This field allows you to provide the outcome or result related to your understanding of percent change.
5. **Add additional hint for percent_change_knowledge_quiz_hint**: This field is for providing additional information or hint related to percent change.
6. **Enter your email address for percent_change_knowledge_quiz_email**: This field is for providing your email address, which is an optional field.
7. **Enter your phone number for percent_change_knowledge_quiz_phone**: This field is for providing your phone number, which is an optional field.
