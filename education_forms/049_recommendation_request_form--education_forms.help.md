# Recommendation Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Recommendation Request Form" is designed to collect information from students who are seeking recommendations for various purposes. This form helps students gather essential details about themselves, their contact information, and any supporting documents that may be required.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Student Information" section with your personal details.
2. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Information" section.
3. Enter the details of the recommender you are requesting a recommendation from in the "Recommender Information" section.
4. Specify the type of letter you are requesting in the "Letter Requests" section.
5. Share your academic background and education history in the "Academic Background" and "Education History" sections.
6. Add any additional personal information you would like to provide in the "Personal Information" section.
7. Upload any required documents in the "Upload Documents" section.
8. Confirm your request in the "Request Confirmation" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information (id: 1)** (`title: Recommendation Request Form`, `type: text`, required: false): Please provide a brief description of yourself, including your name, major, and any relevant academic or professional background.
* **Contact Information (id: 2)** (`type: text`, required: false): Enter your email address and phone number so that your recommender can contact you.
* **Recommender Information (id: 3)** (`type: text`, required: false): Provide the name and title/position of the person you are requesting a recommendation from.
* **Letter Requests (id: 4)** (`type: text`, required: false): Specify the type of letter you are requesting (e.g., internship, research, or professional letter).
* **Academic Background (id: 5)** (`type: text`, required: false): Share your academic background, including relevant courses, projects, or research experiences.
* **Education History (id: 6)** (`type: text`, required: false): List your education history, including institutions attended and degrees earned.
* **Personal Information (id: 7)** (`type: text`, required: false): Add any additional personal information you would like to share with your recommender.
* **Upload Documents (id: 9)** (`type: text`, required: false): Upload any required documents or supporting materials that may be necessary for your recommender to make a recommendation.
* **Uploaded Documents (id: 10)** (`type: text`, required: false): List the uploaded documents, including their titles and descriptions.
* **Signature (id: 11)** (`type: text`, required: false): Sign your name as a confirmation of the information provided.
* **Review Request (id: 12)** (`type: text`, required: false): Specify whether you would like your recommender to review your submitted information before sending the letter.
* **Request Confirmation (id: 13)** (`type: text`, required: false): Confirm that you have reviewed and agree to the terms of the request.

Note: The fields marked as "required: false" are optional and can be completed or left blank as needed.
