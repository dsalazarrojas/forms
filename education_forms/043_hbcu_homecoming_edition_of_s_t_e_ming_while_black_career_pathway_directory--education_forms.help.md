# HBCU Homecoming Edition Of S.T.E.Ming While Black Career Pathway Directory - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you explore career pathways related to STEM fields as a Black individual. It will collect information about your educational background, career goals, and interests to provide you with relevant resources and tools to aid in your career development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the following sections:

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Enter User Information** (`user_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your name and any other identifying information you are comfortable sharing.
- **Select Tool** (`tool_info`, text, required/optional): Select a tool or resource that you are interested in or currently using for your career development.
- **Upload File** (`file_info`, text, required/optional): You may upload any relevant file or document that you would like to share with us.
- **Education Background** (`education_background`, text, required/optional): Describe your educational background, including any degrees, certifications, or relevant courses you have completed.
- **Career Pathway** (`career_pathway`, text, required/optional): Share your career goals and aspirations, including any specific roles or industries you are interested in.
- **Form Submission** (`form_submission`, text, required/optional): You may provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the form or the directory.

## Tips
Please be as specific as possible when filling out the form, and do not hesitate to reach out if you have any questions or need further clarification. The information collected will be used to improve the relevance and functionality of the HBCU Homecoming Edition Of S.T.E.Ming While Black Career Pathway Directory.
