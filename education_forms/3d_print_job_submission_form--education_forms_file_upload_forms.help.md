# 3D Print Job Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 3D Print Job Submission Form is a form used by users to submit information about a 3D print job. This form is used to gather necessary details to ensure that the job meets the required guidelines and is properly processed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the personal information section with your full name, email address, and department.
2. Enter the job title and job description.
3. Select the submission date and estimated print time.
4. Choose the level of support needed and print priority.
5. Select the material type and post-processing steps required.
6. Enter the shipping address and method.
7. Add any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your legal name.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter a valid institutional email address.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required): Enter the department you are affiliated with.
* **Job title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter a short descriptive title for the job.
* **Job description** (`job_description`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe the purpose of the print.
* **Submission date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are submitting the job.
* **Estimated print time** (`estimated_print_time`, `decimal`, required): Enter the estimated print time in hours.
* **Support needed** (`support_needed`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of support required for the job.
* **Print priority** (`print_priority`, `select_one`, required): Choose the urgency of the job.
* **Material type** (`material_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the primary material for the print.
* **Post-processing steps** (`post_processing`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all post-processing steps required.
* **Shipping address** (`shipping_address`, `text`, required): Enter the full address where the print should be delivered.
* **Shipping method** (`shipping_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose the method of shipping.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Add any extra information you want to provide.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the email address you enter is a valid institutional email address.
* Make sure to choose the correct shipping method and address.
* If you have any additional comments or notes, use the comments field to provide more information.
* Confirm that the file is not copyrighted and meets the guidelines by checking the "I confirm" option.
