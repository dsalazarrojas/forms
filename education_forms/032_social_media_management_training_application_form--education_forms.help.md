# social_media_management_training_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The social_media_management_training_application_form is a tool used by educators and administrators to collect information about social media management training applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill out the form in sections, using the tabs provided.
- Each tab contains specific questions and options related to social media management training applications.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Social Media Expectations
• **Social Media Expectations** (`social_media_expectations`, text, optional): This field is for writing a description of your social media management training expectations.
### Training Areas to Optimize
• **Training Areas to Optimize** (`training_areas_to_optimize`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the areas you want to optimize for your social media management training application.
	+ None
	+ Other (If you select "Other", please provide your own text in the **Social Media Expectations** field.)
### Specific Areas to Optimize
• **Specific Areas to Optimize** (`specific_areas_to_optimize`, select_multiple, optional): Choose specific areas you want to optimize for your social media management training application.
	+ None
	+ Other (If you select "Other", please provide your own text in the **Social Media Expectations** field.)
### Account Type
• **Account Type** (`account_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of account you are managing.
	+ None
	+ Other (If you select "Other", please provide your own text in the **Social Media Expectations** field.)
### Training Expectations
• **Training Expectations** (`training_expectations`, text, optional): This field is for writing a description of your training expectations.
### Number of Hours
• **Number of Hours** (`number_of_hours`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours you are looking for in your training application.
### Start Date
• **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date for your training application.
### End Date
• **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date for your training application.
### Contact Email
• **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email address.
### Contact Phone
• **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
### Comments
• **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): This field is for additional comments or explanations.
### Application Form
• **Application Form** (`application_form`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of application form you want to submit.
	+ None
	+ Other (If you select "Other", please provide your own text in the **Social Media Expectations** field.)
### Submit
• **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): This is a submit button to complete your application form.
