# Play Script Educational Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Play Script Educational Release Form is designed to collect information about a play script's educational performance. It is used by educators, schools, or institutions to release a play script for educational purposes. This form helps to gather details about the script's category, description, date, time, location, and performance-related information.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2.  Enter the category in which the play script belongs to.
3.  Provide a detailed description of the play script.
4.  Enter your name if you are a teacher or educator.
5.  If you are affiliated with a school, enter your school's name.
6.  Enter the date when the play script was performed or will be performed.
7.  Enter the time when the play script was performed or will be performed.
8.  Provide the location where the play script was performed or will be performed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Category** (`Category`, `Select One`, required/optional): This field is where you will select the category in which the play script belongs to. You can choose one of the available options: Education Forms, Drama Programs, Educational Organizations, or Other.
*   **Description** (`Description`, `Text`, required/optional): Here, you will provide a detailed description of the play script.
*   **Teacher Name** (`Teacher Name`, `Text`, required/optional): If you are a teacher or educator, enter your name here.
*   **School Name** (`School Name`, `Text`, required/optional): If you are affiliated with a school, enter the name of the school here.
*   **Date of Performance** (`Date of Performance`, `Date`, required/optional): Enter the date when the play script was performed or will be performed.
*   **Time of Performance** (`Time of Performance`, `Time`, required/optional): Enter the time when the play script was performed or will be performed.
*   **Location of Performance** (`Location of Performance`, `Text`, required/optional): Provide the location where the play script was performed or will be performed.

## Tips
*   Ensure that you fill out all required fields accurately.
*   Use a valid format for the date and time fields (e.g., DD/MM/YY HH:MM AM/PM).
*   If you are unsure about any field, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx) for assistance.
