# Graduate Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Graduate Program Application Form is designed to collect information about your academic background, education, and experiences to support your application to a graduate program. This form is divided into various sections to help you provide detailed and accurate information about your application.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Application Information**: Provide a brief introduction about yourself, including your name, contact information, and a short bio.
2. **Academic Details**: Share your academic background, including your previous degrees, institutions attended, and relevant courses taken.
3. **References**: List your professional references, including their name, title, and contact information.
4. **Resume**: Upload your resume or CV to showcase your work experience and skills.
5. **Personal Statement**: Write a brief statement explaining why you are applying to the graduate program and how you align with the program's goals.
6. **Transcripts**: Upload your academic transcripts from previous institutions.
7. **Letters of Recommendation**: Request letters from your professional references, and upload them as attachments.
8. **Resume Attachment** (if needed): If you have multiple resumes or CVs, you can upload them here.
9. **Transcripts Attachment** (if needed): If you have multiple transcripts, you can upload them here.
10. **Letters of Recommendation Attachment** (if needed): If you have multiple letters of recommendation, you can upload them here.
11. **Personal Statement Attachment** (if needed): If you have an extended personal statement, you can upload it here.
12. **Resume 2, Transcripts 2, Personal Statement 2, Letters Of Recommendation 2, etc.**: If you have additional information for the program, you can provide it here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Information (text)**: Please provide your name, contact information, and a brief introduction about yourself.
* **applicant_contact (text)**: Enter your email address and phone number so we can contact you.
* **academic_details (text)**: Share your academic background, including previous degrees, institutions attended, and relevant courses taken.
* **references (text)**: List your professional references, including their name, title, and contact information.
* **resume (text)**: Upload your resume or CV to showcase your work experience and skills.
* **personal_statement (text)**: Write a brief statement explaining why you are applying to the graduate program and how you align with the program's goals.
* **education_history (text)**: Provide information about your previous institutions attended and the academic programs you pursued.
* **transcripts (text)**: Upload your academic transcripts from previous institutions.
* **letters_of_recommendation (text)**: Request letters from your professional references, and upload them as attachments.
* **certified_by (text)**: Specify the name and credentials of the certifying body for your transcripts.
* **transcripts_translation (text)**: Indicate if your transcripts require translation.
* **personal_statement_attachment (text)**: Upload your personal statement as an attachment.
* **resume_attachment (text)**: Upload your resume or CV as an attachment.
* **transcripts_attachment (text)**: Upload your academic transcripts as an attachment.
* **letters_of_recommendation_attachment (text)**: Upload your letters of recommendation as an attachment.
* **resume_2, transcripts_2, personal_statement_2, letters_of_recommendation_2, etc. (text)**: If you have additional information for the program, you can provide it here.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have reviewed the required documents and have all the necessary information ready to be uploaded.
* You can upload multiple attachments for each section, but please make sure they are relevant to your application.
* You can write multiple personal statements, but please make sure they are concise and related to your application.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
