# teacher_complaint_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Teacher Complaint Form" is a tool for teachers to report any incidents or complaints they have experienced within the educational institution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the complainant name in the "complainant name" field. This is the name of the person submitting the complaint.
2. Enter the ID number of the teacher involved in the "teacher id" field.
3. Fill in the name of the teacher who is the subject of the complaint in the "teacher name" field.
4. Select the date of the incident in the "complaint date" field.
5. Provide a detailed description of the incident in the "complaint description" field.
6. If applicable, select the date of resolution in the "resolution date" field.
7. Choose the status of the resolution in the "resolution status" field.
8. Attach any supporting evidence or documentation in the "evidence" field.
9. Provide any additional information or attachments related to the complaint in the "attachment" field.
10. Finally, enter your name as the person submitting the form in the "submitter name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **complainant name** (complainant_name, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person submitting the complaint.
* **teacher id** (teacher_id, number, required: false): Enter the ID number of the teacher who is the subject of the complaint.
* **teacher name** (teacher_name, text, required: false): Enter the name of the teacher who is the subject of the complaint.
* **complaint date** (complaint_date, date, required: false): Select the date of the incident.
* **complaint description** (complaint_description, text, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the incident.
* **resolution date** (resolution_date, date, required: false): If applicable, select the date of resolution.
* **resolution status** (resolution_status, select_one, required: false): Choose the status of the resolution (pending, resolved, ongoing).
* **evidence** (evidence, text, required: false): Attach supporting evidence or documentation related to the complaint.
* **attachment** (attachment, text, required: false): Provide any additional information or attachments related to the complaint.
* **submitter name** (submitter_name, text, required: false): Enter your name as the person submitting the form.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate documentation of the complaint.
* If you are unsure about any field or option, feel free to ask for clarification from the relevant authorities.
* This form is meant for internal use only, please do not submit any sensitive or confidential information.
