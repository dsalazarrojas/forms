# Daycare Child Admission Packet Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register a child for daycare services by collecting essential information about the child and their parent/guardian.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the child's First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Fill in the child's Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter the child's email address in the "Email" field.
4. Provide the child's phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter the child's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
6. Fill in the child's home address in the "Address Line 1", "Address Line 2", "City", "State", and "Country" fields.
7. Enter the parent's name in the "Parent Name" field.
8. Enter the parent's email address in the "Parent Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the child's first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the child's last name.
* **Email** (email, required/optional): Enter the child's email address.
* **Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter the child's phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (date, required/optional): Enter the child's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address Line 1** (text, required/optional): Fill in the first line of the child's home address.
* **Address Line 2** (text, required/optional): Fill in the second line of the child's home address.
* **City** (text, required/optional): Enter the city where the child resides.
* **State** (text, required/optional): Enter the state where the child resides.
* **Country** (text, required/optional): Enter the country where the child resides.
* **Parent Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the parent's name.
* **Parent Email** (email, required/optional): Enter the parent's email address.
