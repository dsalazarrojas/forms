# Classical Ballet Education Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students and their parents/guardians for the classical ballet education program. It includes demographic data, medical information, and scheduling details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the student's demographic information, including their full name, date of birth, age, and gender.
2. Provide information about the student's previous ballet experience and the level of class they are interested in.
3. Enter the parent/guardian's contact information, including their name and phone number.
4. Provide any relevant medical information, such as any medical conditions or allergies.
5. Select the term of enrollment and preferred class schedule.
6. Confirm that you agree to the studio's policies and conduct standards.
7. Finally, select the date of registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Full Name** (`Student Full Name`, text, required): Enter the student's full name, including first and last name.
- **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, required): Enter the student's birth date.
- **Age** (`Age`, number, required): Enter the student's current age in years.
- **Gender** (`Gender`, select_one, optional): Select the student's gender.
- **Previous Ballet Experience** (`Previous Ballet Experience`, select_one, required): Select the student's previous ballet experience.
- **Requested Class Level** (`Requested Class Level`, select_one, required): Select the level of class the student is interested in.
- **Parent/Guardian Name** (`Parent/Guardian Name`, text, required): Enter the parent/guardian's name.
- **Parent/Guardian Phone** (`Parent/Guardian Phone`, text, required): Enter the parent/guardian's phone number.
- **Parent/Guardian Email** (`Parent/Guardian Email`, email, required): Enter the parent/guardian's email address.
- **Emergency Contact Name** (`Emergency Contact Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person to contact in emergency.
- **Emergency Contact Phone** (`Emergency Contact Phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the person to contact in emergency.
- **Any Medical Conditions or Allergies** (`Any Medical Conditions or Allergies`, text, optional): Provide any relevant medical conditions or allergies.
- **Current Medications** (`Current Medications`, text, optional): List any medications the student is taking.
- **Physical Restrictions or Injuries** (`Physical Restrictions or Injuries`, text, optional): Describe any physical limitations or injuries the student has.
- **Has a Physician Approved Participation in Ballet?** (`Has a Physician Approved Participation in Ballet?`, select_one, optional): Select if a physician has approved participation in ballet.
- **Enrollment Term** (`Enrollment Term`, select_one, required): Select the term of enrollment.
- **Preferred Class Time** (`Preferred Class Time`, select_one, required): Select the preferred class schedule.
- **Consent Agreement** (`Consent Agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree to the studio's policies and conduct standards.
- **Registration Date** (`Registration Date`, date, required): Enter the date of registration.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form carefully and accurately.
- Ensure that you are not duplicating a previously submitted form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the studio administration.
