# Completion Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to verify student completion of their academic program. It ensures that students have met all the necessary requirements and standards set by the institution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, please follow the step-by-step guide below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your full legal name as it appears on your student ID or documents.
* **Student ID Number** (`student_id`, text, required): Enter your student ID number as listed on your student portal.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required): Use your institution-provided email address.
* **Academic Department** (`academic_department`, select_one, required): Select the department in which you are completing your program (e.g., Humanities, Social Sciences, Engineering, etc.).
* **Program Type** (`program_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of program you are completing (e.g., Certificate, Associate Degree, Bachelor Degree, etc.).
* **Program or Course Title** (`course_or_program_title`, text, required): Enter the full title of the program or course you are completing.
* **Date of Completion** (`completion_date`, date, required): Enter the expected or actual completion date of your program.
* **Total Credits Earned** (`total_credits_earned`, number, required): Enter the total number of credits earned towards completion.
* **Overall GPA** (`gpa_achieved`, number, optional): If applicable, enter your overall GPA on a 4.0 scale.
* **Core Competencies Fulfilled** (`core_competencies_met`, select_multiple, required): Check all the core competencies you have fulfilled (e.g., Critical Thinking, Communication, Quantitative Literacy, etc.).
* **Faculty Advisor Name** (`faculty_advisor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your primary advisor during your studies.
* **-- Final Requirement Checklist --** (`section_final_checklist`, note, optional): Review the final checklist to ensure all requirements are met.
* **All Program Requirements Met** (`requirements_check`, select_one, required): Confirm that all program requirements are met.
* **Financial Clearance Obtained** (`financial_clearance`, select_one, required): Confirm that all tuition and fees have been settled.
* **Student Comments** (`student_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments for the registrar.
* **Submission Timestamp** (`submission_timestamp`, text, optional): Enter the timestamp of your submission.

Note: Please fill out this form carefully, as it will be reviewed and verified by the registrar. If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact your academic advisor or the registrar's office.
