# Peer Evaluation By Dr. Baloran - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Peer Evaluation By Dr. Baloran form is used to collect feedback from students about Dr. Baloran's teaching style, communication skills, organization, and preparation. This form helps Dr. Baloran to understand how well they are meeting the expectations of their students, identify areas of improvement, and make necessary adjustments to their teaching methods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name on the first page.
2. Enter your Email address on the second page.
3. Enter your Phone Number on the third page.
4. Rate Dr. Baloran's Teaching Style, Communication Skills, Organization, and Preparation on the subsequent pages.
	* Select one of the two options (Good or Fair) for each rating.
5. If you have any additional comments, provide them on the last page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name to be identified in the evaluation.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so Dr. Baloran can contact you for any follow-up questions or discussions.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number in case Dr. Baloran needs to discuss any issues or concerns over the phone.
* **Rating of Dr. Baloran's Teaching Style** (`rating`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you think Dr. Baloran's teaching style is Good or Fair.
* **Date of Evaluation** (`date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you evaluated Dr. Baloran's teaching.
* **Time of Evaluation** (`time`, `time`, required): Enter the time you evaluated Dr. Baloran's teaching.
* **Rating of Dr. Baloran's Communication Skills** (`rating`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you think Dr. Baloran's communication skills are Good or Fair.
* **Rating of Dr. Baloran's Organization and Preparation** (`rating`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you think Dr. Baloran's organization and preparation are Good or Fair.
* **Additional Comments** (`note`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional comments about Dr. Baloran's performance if you have something specific to mention.
* **Additional Comments (2)** (`note`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional comments about Dr. Baloran's performance if you have something specific to mention.

## Tips

* Please be honest and accurate in your evaluation to help Dr. Baloran improve their teaching methods.
* Provide specific examples or incidents that support your ratings and comments.
* Keep your comments concise and clear, but feel free to express your thoughts and opinions.
