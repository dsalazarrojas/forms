# Culturally Inclusive Learning Project Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Culturally Inclusive Learning Project Application Form is designed to gather information from interested parties about their project or initiative that promotes culturally inclusive learning practices. This form helps our team assess applications and support initiatives that foster diversity, equity, and inclusion in education.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have a clear and concise project description and have reviewed our project guidelines.
2. Fill in the contact information section with your name, school or organization name, and contact details.
3. Provide your address in the address section.
4. Enter your email address in the email section.
5. Enter your phone number in the phone number section.
6. Describe your project or initiative in the project details section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, optional): This section is where you can describe your project or initiative in detail. Please provide a clear and concise overview of your project, including its goals, objectives, and how it promotes culturally inclusive learning practices.

* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): This is your address where you can be contacted or where your project is located. Please ensure that it is accurate and up-to-date.

* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This is your email address where we can contact you for further information or clarification about your project.

* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): This is your phone number where we can contact you for further information or clarification about your project.

* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): This section is where you can provide your name, school or organization name, and any other relevant contact details. Please ensure that it is accurate and up-to-date.
