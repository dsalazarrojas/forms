# Active Supervision Policy Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to confirm that a staff member has received and read the active supervision policy, ensuring they understand their commitments and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Complete the form by entering the requested information for each field, as described below.
2.  Ensure all required fields are filled out completely and accurately.
3.  If you have questions or concerns about the policy, please refer to the "Questions or Concerns About Policy" field and select "Yes, I have questions or concerns".
4.  Once you have finished, sign the form with your current signature date and have your supervisor witness the acknowledgment by writing their name in the "Supervisor Witness Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Staff Member Name** (`staff_member_name`): Enter your full name as a staff member.
*   **Staff ID** (`staff_id`): Enter your employee identification number.
*   **Position Title** (`position_title`): Enter your job title.
*   **Department or Program** (`department_program`): Enter the department or program you work in.
*   **Policy Version** (`policy_version`): Enter the version of the policy you are acknowledging.
*   **Date Received Policy** (`policy_receipt_date`): Enter the date you received the policy document.
*   **Acknowledgment of Policy Receipt** (`policy_acknowledgment`): Select "Yes, I acknowledge receipt and understanding" to confirm you have received and read the policy.
*   **Understanding of Policy Requirements** (`understanding_confirmation`): Select "Yes, I fully understand" if you have a clear understanding of the policy's requirements.
*   **Commitment to Policy Compliance** (`commitment_to_compliance`): Select "Yes, I commit to full compliance" to indicate you will follow the policy's requirements.
*   **Active Supervision Training Completion** (`training_completion`): Select the status of your training completion (e.g., "Yes, training completed", "In progress", "Scheduled", "Not yet completed").
*   **Questions or Concerns About Policy** (`questions_or_concerns`): If you have questions or concerns, select "Yes, I have questions or concerns".
*   **Signature Date** (`signature_date`): Enter the date of your acknowledgment and signature.
*   **Supervisor Witness Name** (`supervisor_witness`): Enter your supervisor's name as a witness to the acknowledgment (optional, but recommended).

## Tips

*   If you are unsure about any field or policy requirement, refer to your HR department or supervisor for clarification.
*   Ensure your supervisor signs the "Supervisor Witness Name" field as a witness to your acknowledgment.
*   Keep a copy of the completed form for your records.
