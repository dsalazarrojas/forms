# Master Program Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Master Program Evaluation Form is a tool for students to provide feedback on their experience with a Master's program. It's essential to understand the student's perspective to help improve the program and provide better support in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and honestly. Please answer all questions that apply to you.
2. Start on the first page, which is titled "Introduction". Provide a brief introduction to your experience with the program.
3. Move on to the subsequent pages, where you will be asked about your satisfaction with the program and its various aspects.
4. Use the options provided to select your level of satisfaction. If you have additional comments, you can add them in the "Comments" section.
5. Once you have completed all pages, review your answers to ensure they are accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (Page 1):
  - This is a text field where you can provide a brief introduction to your experience with the program.
* **Program Title** (Page 2):
  - This is the title of the program you have been through. Please enter the exact title as it appears on your transcript or diploma.
* **Program Duration** (Page 3):
  - This field is for you to enter the length of time you spent in the program. Please enter a numeric value.
* **Satisfaction with Program** (Page 4):
  - This is a dropdown menu where you select how satisfied you are with the program. You have six options to choose from.
* **Accessibility of Program Resources** (Page 5):
  - This is a dropdown menu where you select how satisfied you are with the accessibility of program resources. You have six options to choose from.
* **Comments** (Page 6):
  - This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments about your experience with the program.
* **Additional Comments** (Page 7):
  - This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments about your experience with the program.
* **Program Satisfaction** (Page 8):
  - This is a dropdown menu where you select how satisfied you are with the program. You have six options to choose from.
* **Faculty Satisfaction** (Page 9):
  - This is a dropdown menu where you select how satisfied you are with the faculty. You have six options to choose from.
* **Academic Support Satisfaction** (Page 10):
  - This is a dropdown menu where you select how satisfied you are with academic support. You have six options to choose from.
* **Accessibility Of Program Resources Satisfaction** (Page 11):
  - This is a dropdown menu where you select how satisfied you are with the accessibility of program resources. You have six options to choose from.
* **Total Satisfaction** (Page 12):
  - This is a dropdown menu where you select how satisfied you are with your overall experience with the program. You have six options to choose from.

## Tips
* Please be honest in your answers, and do not hesitate to provide specific examples to support your answers.
* If you have any additional comments, use the "Comments" and "Additional Comments" fields to provide more detailed feedback.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.
* Once you have completed the form, review it carefully before submitting.
