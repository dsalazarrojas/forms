## School Teacher Supply Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The School Teacher Supply Request Form is designed to assist school teachers in requesting supplies for their classrooms. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your school's name in the **school_name** field.
2. Enter your name and contact email address in the **teacher_name** and **teacher_email** fields, respectively.
3. Enter your contact email address for your school in the **school_email** field.
4. Enter the name of your class in the **class_name** field.
5. Enter the grade level of your class (if applicable) in the **class_grade** field.
6. Describe the specific supplies you need in the **supply_needed** field.
7. Enter the date and time you need the supplies by in the **supply_request_date** and **supply_request_time** fields, respectively.
8. Add any additional comments about your request in the **additional_comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **school_name** (text, required): Enter the name of your school.
* **teacher_name** (text, required): Enter your name.
* **teacher_email** (email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **school_email** (text, required): Enter your school's contact email address.
* **class_name** (text, required): Enter the name of your class.
* **class_grade** (text, optional): Enter the grade level of your class, if applicable.
* **supply_needed** (text, required): Describe the specific supplies you need.
* **supply_request_date** (date, required): Enter the date you need the supplies by.
* **supply_request_time** (time, required): Enter the time you need the supplies by.
* **teacher_phone** (text, optional): Enter your contact phone number, if applicable.
* **school_phone** (text, optional): Enter your school's contact phone number, if applicable.
* **classroom_location** (text, optional): Enter the location of your classroom, if applicable.
* **teacher_signature** (note, optional): Add your signature to confirm your request.
* **school_signature** (note, optional): Add your school's signature to confirm receipt of the request.
* **additional_comments** (note, optional): Add any additional comments about your request.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when describing the supplies you need.
* Make sure to enter the correct dates and times for when you need the supplies.
* If you have any additional comments or questions, use the **additional_comments** field to include them.
* Make sure to sign both your teacher and school signatures to confirm receipt of the request.
