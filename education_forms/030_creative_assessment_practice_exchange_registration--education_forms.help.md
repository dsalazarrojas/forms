# creative_assessment_practice_exchange_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The creative assessment practice exchange is a platform for users to share their creative assessment practices. This form is used to register your interest in participating in the exchange and provide necessary details for your participation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your interests in a creative assessment practice exchange.
2. Select your experience level.
3. Provide your participant details.
4. Select your role in the exchange.
5. Indicate how you will implement your interests.
6. Select your session preferences.
7. Choose your preferred session date and time.
8. Select your preferred session language.
9. Agree to the participant agreement.
10. Confirm your participant agreement terms.
