# Isolationism Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Isolationism Knowledge Quiz is designed to assess your understanding of the concept of isolationism, a complex economic policy that emphasizes self-sufficiency and minimal external dependence. This form aims to measure your knowledge on the topic.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the "User Information" page, which includes your basic demographic details.
2. Move on to the "Isolationism Knowledge" field, where you will be asked to provide a response demonstrating your understanding of the concept.
3. If you have any additional comments or thoughts, you can share them in the "Additional Comments" field.
4. Fill out the "Student Name" field with your actual name.
5. If you have a valid email address, please enter it in the "Email Address" field.
6. You can also provide your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
7. For the "Quiz Score" field, please enter a numerical value indicating your assessment of your knowledge on the topic.
8. In the "Quiz Score Out of" field, enter the total number of points or maximum score you were aiming for.
9. Finally, provide the date and time of submission in the "Submission Date" and "Submission Time" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information**: This field collects your basic demographic details, such as your name, email address, and phone number. Please ensure that you fill out all required fields accurately.
* **Isolationism Knowledge**: This field requires you to demonstrate your understanding of the concept of isolationism. Please enter your response in the text format, and try to be as clear and concise as possible.
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any additional thoughts or comments about the form or the quiz, please share them in this field.
* **Student Name**: This field requires your actual name, which will be used for identification purposes.
* **Email Address**: If you have a valid email address, please enter it in this field. This will help us contact you if needed.
* **Phone Number**: You can provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Quiz Score**: Enter a numerical value indicating your assessment of your knowledge on the topic.
* **Quiz Score Out of**: Enter the total number of points or maximum score you were aiming for.
* **Submission Date**: Provide the date of submission, which will help us track the timeline of your response.
* **Submission Time**: Enter the time of submission, which will help us identify any potential time-sensitive issues.
