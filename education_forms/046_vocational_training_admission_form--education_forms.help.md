# vocational_training_admission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from applicants for vocational training programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Program Name and Name fields.
2. Enter your email address in the Email field.
3. Provide your phone number in the Phone Number field.
4. Enter your Date of Birth in the Date of Birth field in YYYY-MM-DD format.
5. Select your program type from the dropdown list.
6. Enter the Start and End dates for your training program.
7. Enter the location for the program.
8. Choose your language from the dropdown list.
9. Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
10. Describe your relationship with your emergency contact person.
11. Enter the contact person's phone number.
12. Enter the contact person's email address.
13. If applicable, describe any medical conditions you have.
14. Provide a brief medical history.
15. If applicable, describe any disabilities you have.
16. Enter any additional comments about your application.
17. Enter your program ID (if applicable).
18. Choose your student type from the dropdown list.
19. Choose your gender from the dropdown list.
20. Choose your ethnicity from the dropdown list.
21. Choose your highest education level from the dropdown list.
22. Choose your highest degree from the dropdown list.
23. Choose your student status from the dropdown list.
24. Choose your program status from the dropdown list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Name** (1, text, required): Enter the name of the vocational training program you are applying for.
* **Name** (2, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email** (3, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (4, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (5, date, required): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Program Type** (6, select_one, required): Choose the type of program you are applying for.
* **Training Start Date** (7, date, required): Enter the start date of your training program.
* **Training End Date** (8, date, required): Enter the end date of your training program.
* **Program Location** (9, text, required): Enter the location of your training program.
* **Language** (10, select_multiple, required): Choose the languages you are proficient in.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (11, text, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (12, text, required): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (13, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (14, email, required): Enter the email address of your emergency contact person.
* **Medical Condition** (15, note, required): If you have any medical conditions, describe them briefly.
* **Medical History** (16, text, required): Provide a brief medical history.
* **Disability** (17, text, required): If you have any disabilities, describe them briefly.
* **Additional Comments** (18, text, required): Enter any additional comments about your application.
* **Program ID** (19, text, required): Enter your program ID (if applicable).
* **Student Type** (20, select_multiple, required): Choose your student type (adult/minor).
* **Gender** (21, select_multiple, required): Choose your gender.
* **Ethnicity** (22, select_multiple, required): Choose your ethnicity.
* **Highest Education Level** (23, select_multiple, required): Choose your highest education level.
* **Highest Degree** (24, select_multiple, required): Choose your highest degree.
* **Student Status** (25, select_multiple, required): Choose your student status (active/inactive/pending).
* **Program Status** (26, select_multiple, required): Choose your program status (active/inactive/pending).
