# School Withdrawal Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on a student's personal details and reasons for withdrawing from school.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and efficiently, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the personal information section with your name, school ID, or any other relevant student identification number.
2. In the "Who Filled Out" field, type the name of the person filling out the form on behalf of the student (e.g., teacher, parent, or student).
3. Enter the student's current grade (numeric value) in the "Grade" field.
4. For the "Grade Level" field, choose the correct grade level that the student is currently at from the dropdown options.
5. In the "Grade Range" field, select the grade range that the student is currently in.
6. Provide any additional thoughts or comments about the student's situation in the "Thoughts" field.
7. If applicable, list any disappointments or concerns that led to the student's withdrawal in the "Disappointments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Info** (text, required/optional): This field is for entering your name, school ID, or any other relevant student identification number.
* **Who Filled Out** (text, required/optional): Type the name of the person filling out the form on behalf of the student.
* **Grade** (number, required/optional): Enter the student's current grade (numeric value).
* **Grade Level** (select_one, required/optional): Choose the student's current grade level from the dropdown options.
* **Grade Range** (select_multiple, required/optional): Select the grade range that the student is currently in.
* **Thoughts** (text, required/optional): Add any additional thoughts or comments about the student's situation.
* **Disappointments** (text, required/optional): List any disappointments or concerns that led to the student's withdrawal.

## Tips
- Be accurate when entering the student's grade and grade level to ensure correct categorization.
- Use the "Thoughts" field to provide context or additional information that might be helpful for administrators or teachers.
- If you're listing disappointments, please provide specific details to help the school address potential issues.
- Double-check your entries for spelling and grammar accuracy.
