# Online Course Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Online Course Feedback Questionnaire" form is designed to collect feedback from students or instructors about online courses. This information helps us understand what works well and what can be improved in our course content, instruction, and overall learning experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Click on the form and select your response to each question.
2. For select multiple-choice questions, choose one or more of the options provided.
3. For text fields, type in your answer or feedback.
4. You can save your progress as you go, and you can also submit the entire form at once.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Online Course Feedback

* **Online Course Feedback**: Select one or both of the following options to indicate your overall feedback for the course:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'

### Page 2: Instructor Name

* **Instructor Name**: Please type in the name of the instructor who taught the course.

### Page 3: Course Name

* **Course Name**: Select the name of the course you are providing feedback about:
	+ Introduction to Python
	+ Python for Data Science
	+ Python for Beginners

### Page 4: Email

* **Email**: If you'd like to be contacted for further discussion or follow-up questions, please provide your email address.

### Page 5: Phone

* **Phone**: If you'd like to be contacted via phone, please provide your phone number.

### Page 6: Feedback

* **Feedback**: Share your thoughts, comments, and suggestions about the course. This is your chance to tell us what worked well and what didn't.

### Page 7: Suggestions

* **Suggestions**: If you have any suggestions for improvement, please share them here.

### Page 8: Additional Comments

* **Additional Comments**: Any additional thoughts or comments about the course that you'd like to share.
