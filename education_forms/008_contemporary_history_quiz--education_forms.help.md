# Contemporary History Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your understanding of contemporary history, covering a range of historical events, organizations, and movements. Please answer the multiple choice and essay questions to the best of your ability, and provide any additional comments or feedback you may have.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Student Name**: Enter your full name, as recorded in your school records.
2. **Student ID**: Enter your student identification number, as issued by your school.
3. **Course Name**: Enter the name of the history course you are taking.
4. **Quiz Date**: Enter the date the quiz was administered.
5. **Instructor Name**: Enter the name of your history teacher or instructor.
6. **Quiz Section**: Select the section of the course this quiz covers (e.g., Post-World War II Era, Civil Rights Movement).
7. For **Questions 1-8**, select the correct answer from the provided options for each question.
8. **Essay Question**: Answer the essay question in the space provided, discussing the impact of globalization on contemporary world politics.
9. **Time Spent on Quiz**: If you wish, estimate the number of minutes you spent on this quiz.
10. **Confidence Level**: Select your confidence level in your answers (Very Confident, Confident, Somewhat Confident, Not Confident).
11. **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional feedback or comments about the quiz.
