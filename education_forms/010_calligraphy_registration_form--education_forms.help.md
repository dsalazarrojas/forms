# Calligraphy Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from students who want to register for a calligraphy workshop or course. The gathered data will help the instructors tailor the material according to the students' needs and provide a smoother registration experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Student Full Name" field with your full name as you want to be addressed.
2. Enter your "Student Email Address" to receive course materials and updates.
3. Provide your "Phone Number" for contact purposes.
4. Select the workshop you wish to attend from the "Workshop - Class Selection" dropdown menu.
5. Choose your "Experience Level" to help the instructor tailor the course material.
6. If you need a starter supply kit, select "Yes - I need a kit ($45 extra)" or "No - I will bring my own supplies".
7. If you have any dietary restrictions, please specify in the "Dietary Restrictions" field.
8. Choose how you heard about the workshop in the "Referral Source" field (e.g., "Social Media", "Friend or Family", "Search Engine", etc.).
9. Select your "Payment Method" (e.g., "Credit Card", "PayPal", "Bank Transfer", or "Cash at Workshop").
10. Review and agree to the "Terms and Conditions Agreement" by selecting "I Agree".
11. Finally, provide your "Digital Signature - Full Name" for verification purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Full Name** (`student_full_name`, text, required): Fill in your full name as you want to be addressed.
* **Student Email Address** (`student_email`, email, required): Enter your email address to receive course materials and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`student_phone`, text, required): Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Workshop - Class Selection** (`workshop_selection`, select_one, required): Choose the workshop you wish to attend from the dropdown menu.
* **Experience Level** (`experience_level`, select_one, required): Select your experience level to help the instructor tailor the course material.
* **Do you need a starter supply kit?** (`supplies_needed`, select_one, required): If you need a starter supply kit, select "Yes - I need a kit ($45 extra)" or "No - I will bring my own supplies".
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, text, optional): If you have any dietary restrictions, please specify.
* **How did you hear about us?** (`referral_source`, select_one, optional): Choose how you heard about the workshop (e.g., "Social Media", "Friend or Family", "Search Engine", etc.).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select your payment method (e.g., "Credit Card", "PayPal", "Bank Transfer", or "Cash at Workshop").
* **Terms and Conditions Agreement** (`terms_and_conditions`, select_one, required): Review and agree to the terms and conditions by selecting "I Agree".
* **Digital Signature - Full Name** (`student_signature`, text, required): Fill in your full name as a digital signature for verification purposes.
