# Home Language Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Language Survey form is a simple tool used to collect information about an individual's language skills, including their native language and current language proficiency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  The form is divided into multiple pages. Each page will ask you to provide information about your language skills.
2.  For each field, please fill in the required information. You can leave a field blank if it does not apply to you.
3.  Once you have completed all the pages, review your answers to ensure they are accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Survey Language** (`survey_language`, text, required: false)
	* This field is optional, but it will help us understand the language in which you are most comfortable communicating. Please enter the language you typically use for personal or professional purposes.

### **Native Language** (`native_language`, text, required: false)
	* This field is optional, but it will help us understand the language you were born in or are most familiar with. If you have multiple native languages, you can enter all of them separated by commas.

### **Native Language Proficiency** (`native_language_proficiency`, text, required: false)
	* This field is optional, but it will help us understand how proficient you are in your native language. Please describe your level of proficiency, such as "fluent" or "basic".

### **Language Proficiency Other** (`language_proficiency_other`, text, required: false)
	* This field is optional, but it will help us understand any other languages you are proficient in that are not included in the provided options. Please describe your level of proficiency and the language in question.

## Tips
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask for clarification or leave a note to describe your response.
* If you have any other languages you would like to add that are not included in the options, please use the "Language Proficiency Other" field.
* Please be as accurate as possible when filling out the form.
