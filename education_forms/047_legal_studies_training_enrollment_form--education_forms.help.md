# Legal Studies Training Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to record student enrollments in the Legal Studies program. It collects relevant information to track student activities, such as their student ID, name, grade level, and selected courses.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select "Yes" or "No" for "enrollment_information" to confirm or decline student enrollment.
2.  Enter student ID in the "student_id" field to verify student identity.
3.  Enter student name, grade level, and other relevant details in the "student_name" field.
4.  Choose the appropriate course for the student from the available options in the "course_name" field.
5.  Select the "enrollment_date" as the date the student was enrolled.
6.  Enter the contact number of the student in the "contact_number" field.
7.  Enter the student's email address in the "email" field.
8.  Provide any additional comments about the student's enrollment in the "additional_comments" field (if applicable).
9.  Select "Yes" or "No" for "submitted_by" to confirm if the form was submitted by the student.
10.  Select the "submission_date" as the date the form was submitted.
11.  Select the "enrollment_status" as the status of the student's enrollment, such as Active or Inactive.
12.  Select "Yes" or "No" for "enrollment_agreement" to confirm if the student has agreed to the enrollment terms.
13.  Select the "submission_status" as the status of the form submission, such as Active or Inactive.
14.  Enter the institution name that the student is enrolled in.
15.  Choose the program name and description from the provided options.
16.  Select "Yes" or "No" for "submitted_by_institution" to confirm if the form was submitted by the institution.
17.  Enter the URL of the institution.
18.  Select the "submitted_at" as the date the form was submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Enrollment Information** (`enrollment_information`): Select "Yes" to confirm student enrollment or "No" to decline.
*   **Student ID** (`student_id`): Enter the student's ID to verify their identity.
*   **Student Name** (`student_name`): Enter the student's name.
*   **Student Grade Level** (`student_grade`): Enter the student's grade level.
*   **Course Name** (`course_name`): Choose the selected course for the student.
*   **Enrollment Date** (`enrollment_date`): Select the date the student was enrolled.
*   **Contact Number** (`contact_number`): Enter the student's contact number.
*   **Email** (`email`): Enter the student's email address.
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Provide any additional comments about the student's enrollment.
*   **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`): Select "Yes" if the form was submitted by the student.
*   **Submission Date** (`submission_date`): Select the date the form was submitted.
*   **Enrollment Status** (`enrollment_status`): Select the status of the student's enrollment (Active or Inactive).
*   **Enrollment Agreement** (`enrollment_agreement`): Select "Yes" if the student has agreed to the enrollment terms.
*   **Submission Status** (`submission_status`): Select the status of the form submission (Active or Inactive).
*   **Institution Name** (`institution_name`): Enter the institution name.
*   **Program Name** (`program_name`): Choose the selected program name and description.
*   **Enrollment By Institution** (`submitted_by_institution`): Select "Yes" if the form was submitted by the institution.
*   **Institution URL** (`institution_url`): Enter the institution's URL.
*   **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`): Select the date the form was submitted.
