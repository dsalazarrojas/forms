# Curriculum Development Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting a leave of absence for curriculum development purposes. It is used to notify the team and management of your planned absence, ensuring a smooth transition of tasks and responsibilities during your leave.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the start date of your leave from the calendar.
2. Provide your end date of leave.
3. Check the Team Lead box if you need to inform your team lead about your leave.
4. If you select Team Lead, provide their email address and phone number.
5. Describe the reason for your leave, if necessary.
6. Choose the type of leave you are requesting (Leave of Absence or Other).
7. Provide the start date of your leave.
8. Select the end date of your leave.
9. Enter the duration of your leave in days.
10. Describe your coverage plan, if applicable.
11. Add any comments or comments on your return date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): The date when you will start your leave.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): The date when your leave will end.
* **Team Lead** (`team_lead`, select_one, required): If you need to inform your team lead about your leave, select "Yes" here.
* **Team Lead Email** (`team_lead_email`, email, optional): If you select Team Lead, provide their email address.
* **Team Lead Phone** (`team_lead_phone`, text, optional): If you select Team Lead, provide their phone number.
* **Reason for leave** (`reason_for_leave`, note, optional): Provide a brief description of your reason for leave, if necessary.
* **Leave type** (`leave_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of leave you are requesting (Leave of Absence or Other).
* **Leave start** (`leave_start`, date, required): The date when your leave will start.
* **Leave end** (`leave_end`, date, required): The date when your leave will end.
* **Leave duration** (`leave_duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of your leave in days.
* **Coverage plan** (`coverage_plan`, note, optional): Describe your coverage plan, if applicable.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any comments or comments on your return date.
* **Return date** (`return_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when you will return to work.
* **Comments return** (`comments_return`, note, optional): Add any comments or comments on your return date.
