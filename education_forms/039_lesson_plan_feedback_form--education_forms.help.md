# Lesson Plan Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form was created to collect feedback from students about a lesson provided by an educator or instructor. The feedback is meant to help the teacher and the organization improve the teaching process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **What did you think of the lesson?** Please provide your honest feedback about what you learned from the lesson and what you thought of it. This will help the teacher understand your perspective and make improvements for future lessons.
2. **Who was the teacher?** Please enter the name of the teacher who provided the lesson.
3. **What did the teacher do well?** Provide feedback about what the teacher did well during the lesson, such as their presentation, explanation, or interaction with the class.
4. **What was the lesson plan about?** Please provide a brief description of what the lesson was about, so the teacher can improve their planning in the future.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What did you think of the lesson?** (`lesson_plan_feedback_form`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide your overall feedback about the lesson. Please be honest and provide specific examples or details about what you liked or disliked about the lesson.
* **Who was the teacher?** (`teacher_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of the teacher who provided the lesson.
* **What did the teacher do well?** (`teacher_comments`, text, optional): Provide feedback about what the teacher did well during the lesson. For example, their presentation, explanation, or interaction with the class.
* **What was the lesson plan about?** (`lesson_plan`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of what the lesson was about.

## Tips
* Be honest and provide specific feedback.
* Keep your feedback respectful and professional.
* The teacher will use this feedback to improve their teaching and the lesson.
* Your feedback is valuable, and the teacher will learn from it.
