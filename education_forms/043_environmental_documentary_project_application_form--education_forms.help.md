# environmental_documentary_project_application_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Environmental Documentary Project Application Form is used to collect information about environmental projects submitted to the Grant Agency. This form is essential in the evaluation and management of these projects, providing a systematic way to gather and store relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information such as project title, summary, and description.
2. Provide details about the project completion date and time.
3. Enter the email address of the project applicant.
4. Select the Grant Agency responsible for the project.
5. Identify the Grant Organizers and School involved in the project.
6. Select the project topics and budget.
7. Choose the project location and grant amount.
8. Provide the grant start and end dates.
9. Specify the grant funding source and status.
10. Identify the reviewers and their contact information.
11. Enter any additional notes about the project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`): A brief description about the project applicant.
* **Title** (`title`): A concise title of the environmental project.
* **Summary** (`summary`): A brief summary of the environmental project.
* **Description** (`description`): A detailed description of the environmental project.
* **Date of Completion** (`date_of_completion`): The date when the environmental project is expected to be completed.
* **Time of Completion** (`time_of_completion`): The time when the environmental project is expected to be completed.
* **Email** (`email`): The email address of the project applicant.
* **Phone** (`phone`): The phone number of the project applicant.
* **Grant Agency** (`grant_agency`): The Grant Agency responsible for the project.
* **Grant Organizers** (`grant_organizers`): The Grant Organizers involved in the project.
* **School** (`school`): The schools involved in the project.
* **Project Topics** (`project_topics`): The topics covered in the environmental project.
* **Project Budget** (`project_budget`): The budget allocated for the environmental project.
* **Project Location** (`project_location`): The location where the environmental project will take place.
* **Grant Amount** (`grant_amount`): The amount of grant allocated for the environmental project.
* **Grant Start Date** (`grant_start_date`): The start date of the grant period.
* **Grant End Date** (`grant_end_date`): The end date of the grant period.
* **Grant Funding Source** (`grant_funding_source`): The source of funding for the environmental project.
* **Grant Funding Justification** (`grant_funding_justification`): An optional field for additional information about the grant funding.
* **Grant Funding Status** (`grant_funding_status`): The status of the grant funding.
* **Reviewers** (`reviewers`): The reviewers who will be evaluating the environmental project.
* **Reviewers' Emails** (`reviewers_emails`): The email addresses of the reviewers.
* **Reviewers' Phones** (`reviewers_phones`): The phone numbers of the reviewers.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Any additional notes about the environmental project.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`): The date of submission of the environmental project.
* **Reviewers' Comments** (`reviewers_comments`): The comments from the reviewers about the environmental project.
