# Clone Of C19 Screening Questions - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to screen students or participants for potential COVID-19 risks and ensure their safety and well-being within the educational institution.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out this form carefully and accurately.
2.  Make sure to select the correct options for each field.
3.  Answer all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (full_name): Enter your full name as it is written on your official documents.
* **Date of screening** (date_of_screening): Enter the date of this screening.
* **Do you have any symptoms** (have_symptoms): Select 'True' if you are experiencing any symptoms of COVID-19. Select 'False' if you are not experiencing any symptoms.
* **Symptoms experienced** (symptoms_list): If you selected 'True' for the previous question, list any symptoms you are experiencing.
* **Recently tested positive for COVID** (recent_positive_test): Select 'True' if you have recently tested positive for COVID-19 within the last 14 days.
* **Close contact with confirmed case** (close_contact): Select 'True' if you have been in close contact with someone who has tested positive for COVID-19 within the last 14 days.
* **Temperature reading in degrees C** (temperature_reading): Enter a numeric value representing your current body temperature if you are experiencing symptoms or have tested positive.
* **Additional comments** (additional_comments): Enter any additional information that may be relevant for the screening process.
* **Consent to follow school guidance** (consent_statement): Select 'I agree' to follow public health guidance and protocols established by the school.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have provided accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please reach out to the relevant authorities.
* This form is a one-time submission and does not require repeated completion.
