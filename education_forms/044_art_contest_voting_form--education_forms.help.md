# Art Contest Voting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect ratings and comments from art contest voters on various aspects of the artworks submitted for judging. The form helps to gather information on the importance of technical skill, creativity, and artistic vision in the selected artworks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your information as a voter, including your name, email address, and voting date.
2. Select the contest name and category from the provided options.
3. Select your top three choices for the top three prizes.
4. Provide the artist and artwork title for each of your top three selections.
5. Rate the importance of technical skill, creativity, and artistic vision for each of your top three selections.
6. Provide comments on your selections, including why you chose each artwork for their respective prize.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Voter Name** (`voter_name`, required): Enter your name as a voter.
* **Voter Email Address** (`voter_email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Voting Date** (`voting_date`, required): Enter the date on which you are voting.
* **Contest Name** (`contest_name`, required): Select the name of the contest you are voting on.
* **Contest Category** (`contest_category`, required): Select the category of the contest.
* **First Place Entry Number** (`first_place`, required): Enter the number of your first place selection.
* **First Place Artist Name** (`first_place_artist`, required): Enter the name of the artist for your first place selection.
* **First Place Artwork Title** (`first_place_title`, required): Enter the title of the first place artwork.
* **Second Place Entry Number** (`second_place`, required): Enter the number of your second place selection.
* **Second Place Artist Name** (`second_place_artist`, required): Enter the name of the artist for your second place selection.
* **Second Place Artwork Title** (`second_place_title`, not available): Not available in the given YAML.
* **Third Place Entry Number** (`third_place`, not required): This field is not required.
* **Third Place Artist Name** (`third_place_artist`, not required): This field is not required.
* **Importance of Technical Skill** (`technical_skill_rating`, required): Rate the importance of technical skill for your first, second, and third place selections from the provided options.
* **Importance of Creativity and Originality** (`creativity_rating`, required): Rate the importance of creativity and originality for your first, second, and third place selections from the provided options.
* **Importance of Artistic Vision** (`artistic_vision_rating`, required): Rate the importance of artistic vision for your first, second, and third place selections from the provided options.
* **Why did you choose first place** (`first_place_comment`, required): Provide a comment on why you chose your first place selection.
* **Why did you choose second place** (`second_place_comment`, not available): Not available in the given YAML.
* **Why did you choose third place** (`third_place_comment`, not available): Not available in the given YAML.
* **Overall Impressions of the Contest** (`overall_impressions`, not required): This field is not required.
* **Additional Comments or Feedback** (`additional_comments`, not required): This field is not required.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Select your top three choices for the top three prizes.
* Rate the importance of technical skill, creativity, and artistic vision for each of your selections.
* Provide comments on your selections to explain your choices.
* Double-check your selections and ratings for accuracy.
