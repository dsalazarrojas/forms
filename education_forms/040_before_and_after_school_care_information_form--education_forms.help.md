# Before And After School Care Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Before And After School Care Information Form is used to collect information about your child's needs, medical conditions, and emergency contact information. This information is used to ensure the well-being and safety of your child.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each section carefully, and fill in the required fields with accurate and detailed information.
2. Make sure to complete all the required fields, as indicated by the "required" label next to each field.
3. If a field has an "options" label, select the relevant option that best matches your child's situation.
4. Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Child Name** (`child_full_name`, required/required): Please enter your child's full name.
* **Date of Birth** (`child_date_of_birth`, required/required): Enter your child's date of birth in the format `mm/dd/yyyy` (month/day/year).
* **Grade Level** (`child_grade_level`, required/required): Select the current or upcoming grade level of your child.
* **Name of School Attending** (`school_attending`, required/required): Enter the name of the school your child is currently attending.
* **Primary Guardian Name** (`guardian_1_name`, required/required): Enter the name of the primary guardian or caregiver.
* **Primary Phone Number** (`guardian_1_phone`, required/required): Enter the primary guardian or caregiver's phone number.
* **Primary Email Address** (`guardian_1_email`, required/required): Enter the primary guardian or caregiver's email address.
* **Alternate Emergency Contact (Secondary)** (`emergency_contact_alt`, required/required): Enter the name and phone number of an alternate emergency contact.
* **Type of Care Needed** (`care_type_needed`, required/required): Select the type of care your child requires, such as "Before School Only", "After School Only", or "Both Before and After School".
* **Days of the week care is required** (`weekly_care_days`, required/required): Select all the days of the week when care is required.
* **Estimated Drop-off Time** (`estimated_drop_off_time`, optional/optional): Enter the estimated drop-off time for your child.
* **Estimated Pick-up Time** (`estimated_pick_up_time`, optional/optional): Enter the estimated pick-up time for your child.
* **Does the child have any food allergies?** (`food_allergies_child`, required/required): If your child has any food allergies, list them clearly in this field.
* **Any chronic medical conditions or regular medications?** (`chronic_medical_conditions`, optional/optional): If your child has any chronic medical conditions or regular medications, list them in this field.
* **List of people authorized to pick up child** (`authorized_pick_up_list`, required/required): Enter the names of people authorized to pick up your child, besides the primary guardian.
* **I consent to emergency medical treatment for my child if parents cannot be reached** (`consent_to_emergency_medical`, required/required): Select "I Consent" if you agree to emergency medical treatment for your child if you cannot be reached.
* **Form Completion Date** (`form_completion_date`, required/required): Enter the date of form completion.

Please ensure you have filled out all required fields accurately and completely before submitting the form.
