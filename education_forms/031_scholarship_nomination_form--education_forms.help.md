# Scholarship Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The scholarship nomination form is a tool for nominating a student for a scholarship. It collects information about the nominee, their education institution, and the status of the submission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Nomination Form section with the required information about the nominee.
2. Provide details about the education institution associated with the nominee.
3. Submit the nomination and review the status of the submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nomination Form** (`nomination_form`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the nomination information in this text field.
* **Nominee Information** (`nominee_info`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the nominee and their background.
* **Education Institution Info** (`education_institution_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name and address of the education institution associated with the nominee.
* **Scholarship Information** (`scholarship_info`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the scholarship and its benefits.
* **Nomination Form Participants** (`nomination_form_participants`, `text`, required/optional): List any participants involved in the nomination process.
* **Nominee Education Info** (`nominee_education_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the nominee's education history and qualifications.
* **Nominee Contact Info** (`nominee_contact_info`, `text`, required/optional): Provide the contact information for the nominee.
* **Nominator Information** (`nominator_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your information as the nominator.
* **Submission Information** (`submission_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional information about the submission.
* **Scholarship Details** (`scholarship_details`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the details of the scholarship.
* **Review Form** (`review_form`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any review comments or feedback.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, `select_one`, required/true): Select the status of the submission: Waiting For Review, Accepted, Declined, or Rejected.
* **Review Comment** (`review_comment`, `text`, required/true): Enter any comments or feedback from the reviewer.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required/true): Enter the date of submission.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, `date`, required/true): Enter the date of review.
* **Review Time** (`review_time`, `time`, required/true): Enter the time of review.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, `time`, required/true): Enter the time of submission.
* **Review Status** (`review_status`, `select_one`, required/true): Select the status of the review: Not Started, In Progress, Completed.
* **Nomination Status** (`nomination_status`, `select_one`, required/true): Select the status of the nomination: Pending, Confirmed, Declined.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `select_one`, required/true): Select if you submitted the nomination: Yes, No.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, `select_one`, required/true): Select if the nomination was submitted: Yes, No.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, `select_one`, required/true): Select if the nomination was updated: Yes, No.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, `select_one`, required/true): Select if the nomination was updated: Yes, No.
* **Deleted By** (`deleted_by`, `select_one`, required/true): Select if the nomination was deleted: Yes, No.
* **Deleted At** (`deleted_at`, `select_one`, required/true): Select if the nomination was deleted: Yes, No.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Double-check your information before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
