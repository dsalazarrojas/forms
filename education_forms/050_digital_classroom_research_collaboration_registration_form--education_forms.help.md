# Digital Classroom Research Collaboration Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for researchers to register and connect with each other for collaboration purposes in a digital classroom setting. It collects information about the researcher's department, email, phone number, role, collaboration type, research area, research focus, institution, and supervisor details.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Click on "Submit" to start the registration process.
2.  Fill in the required fields (indicated by an asterisk\*).
3.  Select your department and collaboration type from the provided options.
4.  Enter your email, phone number, research area, research focus, institution, and supervisor details in the corresponding text fields.
5.  Click on "Submit" again to complete the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Researcher**: *This field is for the researcher's name.*
*   **Department**: *Select your department from the provided options.*
*   **Email**: *Enter your email address.*
*   **Phone**: *Enter your phone number.*
*   **Role**: *Select your role from the provided options.*
*   **Collaboration Type**: *Select the type of collaboration you are interested in.*
*   **Research Area**: *Enter your research area.*
*   **Research Focus**: *Enter your research focus.*
*   **Institution**: *Enter your institution name.*
*   **Supervisor**: *Enter your supervisor's name.*
*   **Co-Authors**: *Enter the names of your co-authors.*
*   **Submit**: *Click to submit your registration details.*

## Tips

*   Please fill in accurate and complete information to ensure efficient collaboration.
*   Select only relevant options for collaboration type and department.
*   Use a professional format for entering names and institution details.
*   Review your filled-in details before submission to avoid errors.
