# Career Cluster Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from respondents about their career interests and goals. It is intended to help respondents identify potential career clusters that align with their interests and skills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
2. Select the clusters of interest from the list provided.
3. Choose your preferred learning modes for each selected cluster.
4. Indicate when you expect to enter the field of study.
5. If you face any barriers to entry, describe them in the text field.
6. Finally, add any additional comments you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent name** (`respondent_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like to be addressed in this form.
* **School or College** (`school_or_college`, text, required): Enter the name of the institution you are affiliated with or the school you are interested in.
* **Interested career clusters** (`interested_clusters`, select_multiple, required): Select the clusters of interest from the list provided. These clusters are:
	+ Health science
	+ Information technology
	+ Business management
	+ Arts design
	+ Engineering
* **Interest level in chosen cluster** (`interest_level`, select_one, optional): Choose how high or low you are interested in each selected cluster on a scale of High, Moderate, or Low.
* **Preferred learning modes** (`preferred_learning_modes`, select_multiple, optional): Select how you prefer to learn for each cluster:
	+ Classroom
	+ Hands-on labs
	+ Internships
	+ Online courses
* **Career goal timeline** (`career_goal_timeline`, select_one, optional): Indicate when you expect to enter the field of study. You can choose:
	+ Immediately after graduation
	+ Within two years
	+ Longer term
* **Barriers to entry** (`barriers_to_entry`, text, optional): Describe any obstacles you face in pursuing your career cluster.
* **Support needed** (`support_needed`, text, optional): Specify what support from the school or institution would help you pursue your career cluster.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any other notes or comments about this form or your career goals.
