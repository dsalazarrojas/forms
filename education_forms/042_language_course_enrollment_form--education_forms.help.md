# Language Course Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users who want to enroll in a language course. It's a straightforward, one-page form that requires minimal information from you. Please take a few minutes to review the fields and instructions to ensure you provide the necessary details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On the first page, "Enrollment Info", enter your name, and any additional enrollment information you'd like to provide. This will help us get a better understanding of your background and preferences.
2. On the second page, "Course Info", select the language course you're interested in enrolling in. Be sure to choose the course that best aligns with your interests and goals.
3. If you'd like to provide payment information, you can do so on the third page, "Payment Info". Please note that this page is optional and is not required for course enrollment.
4. Once you've completed all the pages, click "Submit" on the fourth page to finalize your enrollment application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Enrollment Info
• **Enrollment Info** (text, required): Enter your name and any other enrollment details you'd like to share with us.

### Course Info
• **Course Info** (text, required): Choose the language course you're interested in enrolling in.

### Payment Info
• **Payment Info** (text, optional): If you'd like to provide payment information, enter it here. Please note that this page is not required for course enrollment.

### Submit
• **Submit** (text, required): Click this button to finalize your enrollment application.
