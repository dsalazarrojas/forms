# Pedagogical Storytelling Resource Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pedagogical Storytelling Resource Request Form is designed for educators to request resources that support their pedagogical practices. This form will help us understand your resource needs and provide you with the relevant resources.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form accurately and honestly.
2. Ensure that the form is submitted only once for the same purpose.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pedagogical Storytelling Resource Request** (`pedagogical_storytelling_resource_request`, `text`, required: false): 
    Please enter a brief description of the resource you are requesting.
* **Educators Name** (`educator_name`, `text`, required: false): 
    Enter your name as an educator, this will help us track your requests and provide you with the relevant resources.
* **Resource Type** (`resource_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
    Choose the type of resource you are requesting (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Resource URL** (`resource_url`, `text`, required: false): 
    Provide the URL of the resource you are requesting, if applicable.
* **Request Reason** (`request_reason`, `text`, required: false): 
    Briefly explain the reason for your resource request.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): 
    Enter your email address where we can contact you for further communication.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required: false): 
    Optional: provide your contact number for us to reach out to you.
