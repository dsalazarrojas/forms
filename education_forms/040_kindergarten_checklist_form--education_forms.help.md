# Kindergarten Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Kindergarten Checklist Form is designed to collect important information from parents and teachers for kindergarten registration purposes. This form helps us verify the accuracy of information, ensure a smooth transition for the child, and facilitate communication between home and school.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields as follows:

1. Enter the parent's name in the "Parent's Name" field.
2. Provide the child's birthdate in the "Child's Birthdate" field.
3. Enter the teacher's name in the "Teacher's Name" field.
4. Enter the classroom name in the "Classroom Name" field.
5. Enter the school name in the "School Name" field.
6. Enter the school phone number in the "School Phone" field.
7. Enter the school email in the "School Email" field.
8. Enter the teacher's phone number in the "Teacher's Phone" field.
9. Enter the teacher's email in the "Teacher's Email" field.
10. Sign the "Teacher's Signature" field to confirm.
11. Enter the parent's phone number in the "Parent's Phone" field.
12. Enter the classroom address in the "Classroom Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent's Name** (text, required): Enter the parent's full name.
* **Child's Birthdate** (date, required): Enter the child's birthdate in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Teacher's Name** (text, required): Enter the teacher's full name.
* **Classroom Name** (text, required): Enter the classroom name where the child will be enrolled.
* **School Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the school the child attends.
* **School Phone** (text, required): Enter the school's phone number.
* **School Email** (email, required): Enter the school's email address.
* **Teacher's Phone** (text, required): Enter the teacher's phone number.
* **Teacher's Email** (email, required): Enter the teacher's email address.
* **Teacher's Signature** (note, required): Sign with your official signature to confirm.
* **Parent's Phone** (text, required): Enter the parent's phone number.
* **Classroom Address** (text, required): Enter the address of the classroom where the child will be enrolled.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Use the correct format for dates (MM/DD/YYYY).
* Use the email format for the "School Email" and "Teacher's Email" fields.
* Sign the "Teacher's Signature" field carefully, as it will be used as confirmation.
