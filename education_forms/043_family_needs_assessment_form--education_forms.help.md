# Family Needs Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a family's needs, particularly in relation to counseling services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Enter the Parent Name**: Enter the name of the parent or guardian who will be completing this form.
2. **Enter the Child Name**: Enter the name of the child or children for whom the assessment is being conducted.
3. **Indicate In Counseling Service**: Check the box to indicate if the family is currently in counseling service or not.
4. **Requests From Service Provider**: Enter any specific requests the family has from the service provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Parent Name (`parent_name`, text, required: false)
Enter the name of the parent or guardian who will be completing this form.
### 2. Child Name (`child_name`, text, required: false)
Enter the name of the child or children for whom the assessment is being conducted.
### 3. In Counseling Service (`in_counseling_service`, select_one, required: false)
Indicate whether the family is currently in counseling service or not.
### 4. Requests From Service Provider (`requests_from_service_provider`, text, required: false)
Enter any specific requests the family has from the service provider.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the form is completed accurately and thoroughly to provide the best possible outcome for the family.
* If you're unsure about any information, please ask a social worker or counselor for guidance.
* This form should only be completed once for each family to prevent duplication.
