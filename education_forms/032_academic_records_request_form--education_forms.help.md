# Academic Records Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals seeking access to their academic records from the institution. You can use this form to request transcripts, degree verification, or other academic records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address so we can contact you about your request.
2. If you are a former student, please provide your student ID number.
3. Indicate your relationship to the institution (e.g., current student, former student, parent/guardian, etc.).
4. Specify the type of academic record you are requesting (e.g., transcript, degree verification, etc.).
5. Provide the dates of the academic period you need records for.
6. Choose how you would like to receive the records (e.g., mail, electronic delivery, etc.).
7. Specify the urgency level of your request (e.g., standard, expedited, etc.).
8. Explain the purpose of your request.
9. If applicable, indicate if you have any special instructions for processing your request.
10. Choose how you will pay any applicable fees.
11. If applicable, list any additional recipients you would like to receive copies of the records.
12. Provide the reference number of any previous requests, if applicable.
13. Finally, confirm your consent to the processing of your request according to institutional policies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester Name** (`requester_name`, required): Your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Requester Email** (`requester_email`, required): A valid email address for communication about this request.
* **Student ID (if applicable)** (`requester_student_id`, optional): If you are a former student, enter your student ID number.
* **Relationship to Institution** (`requester_relationship`, required): Choose your relationship to the institution:
	+ Current student
	+ Former student
	+ Parent/guardian
	+ Employer
	+ Government agency
	+ Other educational institution
	+ Other
* **Type of Record Requested** (`record_type_requested`, required): Select the type of academic record you are requesting:
	+ Official transcript
	+ Unofficial transcript
	+ Degree verification
	+ Enrollment verification
	+ Course completion certificate
	+ Test scores
	+ Other
* **Academic Period From** (`academic_period_from`, optional): Enter the start date of the academic period you need records for.
* **Academic Period To** (`academic_period_to`, optional): Enter the end date of the academic period you need records for.
* **Destination Address** (`destination_address`, required): Enter where the records should be sent.
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, required): Choose how you would like to receive the records:
	+ Mail to address provided
	+ Pick up in person
	+ Electronic delivery
	+ Secure portal
	+ Other
* **Urgency Level** (`urgency_level`, required): Choose how urgent this request is:
	+ Standard processing
	+ Expedited processing
	+ Rush processing
	+ Emergency
* **Purpose of Request** (`purpose_of_request`, required): Explain why you need these academic records.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, optional): Enter any special instructions for processing this request.
* **Fee Payment Method** (`fee_payment_method`, required): Choose how you will pay any applicable fees:
	+ Credit card
	+ Check
	+ Money order
	+ Wire transfer
	+ Payment plan
	+ Fee waiver requested
* **Additional Recipients** (`additional_recipients`, optional): Enter any additional recipients you would like to receive copies of the records.
* **Previous Request Number** (`previous_request_number`, optional): If this is a follow-up to a previous request, enter the reference number.
* **Consent to Processing** (`consent_to_processing`, required): Confirm your consent to the processing of your request according to institutional policies:
	+ Yes, I consent
	+ Yes, with questions
	+ Need clarification before proceeding

Please fill out this form carefully and accurately to ensure we can process your request efficiently and effectively. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
