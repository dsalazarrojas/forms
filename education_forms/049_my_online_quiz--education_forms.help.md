# My Online Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to create a personal online quiz with multiple-choice questions and a text field for answers. It is intended for users to input their preferences and receive personalized results.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each page carefully to ensure you understand the questions and options presented.
2. On the "questions" page, select all the options that apply to your preferences by checking the corresponding boxes.
3. On the "answers" page, type in your answers to the questions presented in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, required: false): This page provides a brief description of the quiz and its purpose.
* **questions** (`2`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ This page presents multiple-choice questions. Check the boxes of all the options that apply to your preferences.
* **answers** (`3`, `text`, required: false):
	+ This page is where you will type in your answers to the questions presented.

## Tips
* Be sure to answer all the questions truthfully and accurately to receive the most accurate results.
* If you are unsure about any question, you can always refer back to the previous page for clarification.
* Remember to submit your quiz results after completing all the questions and answering all the answers.
