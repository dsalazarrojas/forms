# Adjunct Teaching Preference Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect teaching preferences from adjunct instructors for use in academic semester planning and scheduling.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you are providing accurate and relevant information in all fields.
2. Enter your full name as requested in the "Instructor Full Name" field.
3. Enter your faculty/employee ID number in the "Faculty / Employee ID Number" field.
4. Select the academic term in which you would like to teach from the options provided.
5. List any courses you are most interested in teaching in the "Courses you are most interested in teaching" field.
6. Choose your desired course load from the options provided.
7. Select your preferred instruction modality (in-person, online, or hybrid).
8. Choose your preferred campus location.
9. Select the time blocks in which you are available to teach (weekday mornings, weekday afternoons, evenings, or weekends).
10. If you have any specific scheduling conflicts or constraints, enter them in the "Specific Scheduling Conflicts or Constraints" field.
11. Enter your confirmation email address.
12. Select the date you are submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Instructor Full Name** (`instructor_name`, Text, Required): Please enter your full name as you would like to appear on course listings and other materials.
* **Faculty / Employee ID Number** (`instructor_id`, Text, Required): Enter your faculty/employee ID number, which is used for administrative purposes.
* **Academic Term for Planning** (`planning_term`, Select One, Required): Select the semester in which you would like to teach (e.g., Fall 2026, Spring 2027, etc.).
* **Courses you are most interested in teaching** (`preferred_courses`, Text, Required): List any courses you are most interested in teaching. Please use course codes if known.
* **Desired Course Load** (`load_preference`, Select One, Required): Choose the number of course sections you are interested in teaching.
* **Preferred Instruction Modality** (`modality_preference`, Select One, Required): Select your preferred instruction modality (in-person, online, or hybrid).
* **Preferred Campus Location** (`campus_preference`, Select One, Required): Choose your preferred campus location.
* **Weekday Mornings** (`avail_weekday_mornings`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the days and times of week when you are available to teach in the morning.
* **Weekday Afternoons** (`avail_weekday_afternoons`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the days and times of week when you are available to teach in the afternoon.
* **Evening / Night Classes** (`avail_evenings`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the days and times of week when you are available to teach in the evening.
* **Weekend Availability** (`avail_weekends`, Select One, Optional): Select the day and time of week when you are available to teach on the weekend.
* **Specific Scheduling Conflicts or Constraints** (`additional_scheduling_notes`, Text, Optional): If you have any specific scheduling conflicts or constraints, enter them here.
* **Confirmation Email** (`contact_email`, Email, Required): Enter your email address to which confirmation will be sent upon successful submission of this form.
* **Date of Submission** (`submission_date`, Date, Required): Select the date you are submitting this form.
