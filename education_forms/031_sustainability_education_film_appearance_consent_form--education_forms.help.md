# Sustainability Education Film Appearance Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Sustainability Education Film Appearance Consent Form is used to obtain consent from participants in a sustainability education film project. The form is used to collect participant contact information, demographic details, and consent for the use of their media content.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant's information in the "Participant Information" section, including their contact email and phone number.
2. Provide a participant email address in the "Participant Email" field.
3. Fill in the participant's phone number in the "Participant Phone" field.
4. Provide a participant address in the "Participant Address" field.
5. Check the "Participant Photo Release" field to indicate if you are consenting to the use of your photo in the film.
6. Check the "Participant Audio Release" field to indicate if you are consenting to the use of your audio recordings in the film.
7. Check the "Participant Video Release" field to indicate if you are consenting to the use of your video recordings in the film.
8. For the "Participant Other Media Release" field, select "Other" if you are consenting to the use of other media that is not listed.
9. In the "Participant Other Media" field, describe the other media you are consenting to.
10. Sign and date the "Participant Signature" field to indicate your consent.
11. Provide any additional notes about the participant's involvement in the project in the "Participant Notes" field.
12. Fill in the school or organization's information in the "School or Organization" section, including their contact email and phone number.
13. Provide a school or organization address in the "School or Organization Address" field.
14. Fill in the school or organization contact information in the "School or Organization Contact" field.
15. Provide a school or organization email address in the "School or Organization Email" field.
16. Fill in the school or organization phone number in the "School or Organization Phone" field.
17. For the "School or Organization Other Media" field, select "Other" if you are providing other media that is not listed.
18. In the "School or Organization Other Media Details" field, describe the other media you are providing.
