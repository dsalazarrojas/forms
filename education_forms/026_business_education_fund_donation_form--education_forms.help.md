# Business Education Fund Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from individuals and organizations making donations to the Business Education Fund. It's crucial to fill out the form accurately to ensure that your donation is processed correctly and you receive recognition and updates about your donation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through each section carefully and fill in the required fields. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Donor Information
* **Full Name** (`donor_name`, text, required): This is your name as you would like to be recognized on our platform.
* **Email Address** (`donor_email`, email, required): This is your email address where we will send updates and follow-up information about your donation.
* **Phone Number** (`donor_phone`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted by phone, please provide your number.
* **Company Name (Optional)** (`company_name`, text, optional): If you're making a donation on behalf of your company, please provide the name of your organization.

### Donation Details
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, required): This is the amount you're donating to the Business Education Fund.
* **Donation Type** (`donation_type`, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to donate: One-Time, Monthly Recurring, Quarterly Recurring, or Annual Recurring.
* **Fund or Program Focus** (`fund_focus`, select_one, required): Select where you would like your donation to be directed: General Education Fund, Scholarships, Business Skills Training, Entrepreneurship Programs, Technology in Education, Leadership Development, or No Preference.
* **Restricted Use** (`restricted_use`, text, optional): If you have any specific use or condition for your donation, please describe it here.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, required): Repeat the amount you're donating to double-check.

### Payment Method
* **Payment Method** (`payment_type`, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to pay: Credit Card, Debit Card, Bank Transfer, Check, or Payroll Deduction.
* **Tax Deductible** (`tax_deductible`, select_one, required): Check if your donation is tax deductible.
* **Recognition Preference** (`recognition_preference`, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to be recognized: Public, Private, or Anonymous.
* **Non-Profit or Business Name** (`nonprofit_name`, text, optional): If you're making a donation on behalf of an organization, please provide the name of your organization.
* **Receive Updates** (`communication_opt_in`, select_one, optional): Check if you would like to receive updates from us.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any special requests or instructions, please describe them here.
