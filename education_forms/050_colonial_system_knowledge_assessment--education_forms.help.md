# Colonial System Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Colonial System Knowledge Assessment form is designed to evaluate a user's understanding of the colonial system, its history, effects, and legacy.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To begin, fill out the required fields, including your Student Name, Student ID Number, Class or Course Name, and Assessment Date.
2. Answer the following questions about the colonial system:
	* What is the colonial system (select one of the provided options)?
	* Which nations were major colonial powers (select all that apply)?
	* What was the Scramble for Africa (select one of the provided options)?
	* What were the effects of colonialism on colonized regions (select all that apply)?
	* When did most African colonies gain independence (select one of the provided options)?
	* What forms of resistance were there to colonialism (select all that apply)?
	* What is the legacy of colonialism today (describe in your own words)?
3. For each question, indicate your level of confidence in your answer, choosing from Very Confident, Confident, Somewhat Confident, or Not Confident.
4. Finally, rate your overall understanding of the colonial system as Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Needs Improvement, or Poor.
5. You may also provide any additional questions or comments you have.
