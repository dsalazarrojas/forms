# Reading Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess users' reading habits, preferences, and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Answer each question carefully and honestly**. You will be asked about your favorite book, reading frequency, preferred book type, reading mode, reading time, reading speed, and any difficulties you face while reading due to eye sight.
2. **Use the provided options**. For questions with multiple options, select the one that best answers your response.
3. **Enter your answer in the required format**. If a question asks for a numerical value, such as reading time, enter it in the format requested (e.g., "X hours").

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your favorite book**: This question asks about your favorite book. Please type the title of the book you enjoy reading the most.
* **How often do you read**: This question helps us understand how frequently you read. Please select the option that best describes your reading frequency:
	+ Every day
	+ Every week
	+ Never
* **What type of books do you prefer**: This question inquires about your preferred book type. Select all that apply:
	+ Fiction
	+ Non-Fiction
	+ Comic
	+ Graphic Novel
* **Do you have a preferred reading mode**: This question asks about your preferred reading medium. Please select the option that best suits your reading habits:
	+ Print
	+ Digital
	+ Audio
* **How long does it take you to read a book**: If you can estimate the time it takes you to finish a book, please enter that value. Be as accurate as possible.
* **What is your reading speed**: This question helps us understand how quickly you read. Please select the option that best describes your reading speed:
	+ Fast
	+ Medium
	+ Slow
* **Do you have difficulty reading due to eye sight**: If you experience any issues with reading due to eye sight, please answer this question truthfully:
	+ True
	+ False
