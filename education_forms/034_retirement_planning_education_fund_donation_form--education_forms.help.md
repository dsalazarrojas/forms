# Retirement Planning Education Fund Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retirement Planning Education Fund Donation Form is used to collect donations for educational programs and initiatives related to retirement planning. The information collected will be used to better understand donor preferences and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the available fields and determine which information you want to provide.
2. Fill in the required fields (if any).
3. Choose the options for the Donor Organization and Purpose of Donation fields.
4. Provide any additional comments or notes in the Additional Comments field.
5. Review the Form Submission field for any additional information or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Donor Organization
**Organization:** (`Yes', 'No') 
- Choose whether the donation is from an organization or an individual.

### First Name
**First Name:** 
- Enter your first name.

### Email Address
**Email Address:** 
- Enter your email address.

### Phone Number
**Phone Number:** 
- Enter your phone number.

### Donation Amount
**Donation Amount:** 
- Enter the amount you would like to donate.

### Purpose of Donation
**Purpose of Donation:** 
- Select the purpose of your donation from the provided options (Education and Research, General Donation, or Specific Initiative).

### Additional Comments
**Additional Comments:** 
- Provide any additional comments or notes about your donation.

### Form Submission
**Form Submission:** 
- Review and submit the form. If you need to add or modify any information, use this field for any additional comments.

# Tips
- Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, contact the relevant personnel.
- Review the form carefully before submission to ensure all required fields are filled.
