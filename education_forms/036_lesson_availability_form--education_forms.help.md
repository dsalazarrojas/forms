# Lesson Availability Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lesson Availability Form is a structured way to submit details about a lesson, including its schedule and requirements. This information helps educators and administrators plan and organize lessons effectively.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Teacher Name** in the corresponding field.
2. Enter the **Lesson Name** in the field provided.
3. Select a valid **Date** for the lesson.
4. Enter the **Start Time** and **End Time** for the lesson.
5. Enter the **Maximum Students** and **Minimum Students** numbers for the lesson.
6. (Optional) Enter a **Description** of the lesson.
7. (Optional) Select up to three **Tags** that describe the lesson.
8. Click the "Submit" button to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Teacher Name**: Enter the name of the teacher who will be teaching the lesson.
* **Lesson Name**: Enter the name of the lesson being submitted.
* **Date**: Select a valid date for the lesson.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time for the lesson in 24-hour format.
* **End Time**: Enter the end time for the lesson in 24-hour format.
* **Duration**: (Optional) Enter the duration of the lesson in minutes.
* **Description**: (Optional) Enter a brief description of the lesson.
* **Max Students**: Enter the maximum number of students allowed for the lesson.
* **Min Students**: Enter the minimum number of students required for the lesson.
* **Location**: (Optional) Enter the location where the lesson will take place.
* **Tags**: (Optional) Select up to three tags that describe the lesson, such as its subject or topic.

Note: Some fields are marked as required, which means you must fill them out. Optional fields can be left blank.
