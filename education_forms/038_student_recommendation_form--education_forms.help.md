# Student Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Recommendation Form is designed to collect information about students and teachers for recommendation purposes. This form helps to gather essential details about students and their teachers, which can be used to support their growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter the teacher's name in the "Teacher Name" field.
3. Provide a recommendation for the student in the "Recommendation" field.
4. Select the student's achievements from the list in the "Student Achievements" field.
5. Select the teacher's achievements from the list in the "Teacher Achievements" field (if applicable).
6. Enter the student's essay in the "Student Essay" field.
7. Enter the teacher's essay in the "Teacher Essay" field (if applicable).
8. Enter the student's email address in the "Student Email" field.
9. Enter the teacher's email address in the "Teacher Email" field.
10. Enter the student's phone number in the "Student Phone" field.
11. Enter the teacher's phone number in the "Teacher Phone" field (if applicable).
12. Enter the student's date of birth in the "Student Date of Birth" field.
13. Enter the teacher's date of birth in the "Teacher Date of Birth" field.
14. Enter the student's time of birth in the "Student Time of Birth" field (if applicable).
15. Enter the teacher's time of birth in the "Teacher Time of Birth" field (if applicable).
16. Enter the student's address in the "Student Address" field.
17. Enter the teacher's address in the "Teacher Address" field (if applicable).
18. Select the student's religion from the list in the "Student Religion" field.
19. Select the teacher's religion from the list in the "Teacher Religion" field.
20. Select the student's language from the list in the "Student Language" field.
21. Select the teacher's language from the list in the "Teacher Language" field.
