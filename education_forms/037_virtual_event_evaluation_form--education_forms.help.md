# Virtual Event Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from end users about their experience at a virtual event. The feedback collected will be used to evaluate and improve future virtual events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully to ensure you understand the questions and answer honestly.
2. Select the correct answer for each field, taking into account your experience at the virtual event.
3. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "What was your biggest takeaway from the virtual event?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (<label type: text>): This is the first section of the form where you can briefly introduce yourself if you wish.
* **How would you rate the virtual event?** (<label type: select_one>): This is a scale to rate the virtual event. Please choose one of the two options provided: "Good" or "Fair".
* **What was your biggest takeaway from the virtual event?** (<label type: text>): This field is for you to share any additional comments or feedback about the virtual event.
