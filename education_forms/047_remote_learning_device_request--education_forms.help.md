# Remote Learning Device Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is for students to request access to remote learning devices, such as laptops or tablets, for educational purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your student's name in the **Student Name** field.
2. Enter the name of the school you attend in the **School Name** field.
3. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
4. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
5. Select the type of device needed from the **Device Needed** dropdown list.
6. If necessary, select multiple devices from the **Devices** dropdown list.
7. Choose a date and time to request the device in the **Request Date** field.
8. Select a time to request the device in the **Request Time** field.
9. Leave any additional comments or requests in the **Notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your school record.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of the school you attend, such as "ABC High School".
* **Email**: Enter your school email address, which will be used for communication about your device request.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Device Needed**: Choose the type of device you need for your educational activity, such as a laptop or tablet.
* **Devices**: If you need multiple devices, select them from this list. For example, if you need a laptop and a tablet, select both.
* **Request Date**: Enter the date you need the device, in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2024-02-15).
* **Request Time**: Select the time you need the device, in 24-hour format (e.g., 10:00 AM).
* **Notes**: Leave any additional comments or requests you have for the device, such as a specific reason for needing it.

Note: The school's device management policies and availability will determine the outcome of the request. If your request is approved, you will be notified by your school contact person or email.
