# Student Attendance Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Attendance Tracking Form is used to track student attendance, including their names, attendance status, and any comments or remarks. This form helps teachers and school administrators keep a record of student engagement and ensures accurate tracking of student attendance.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the student you wish to track attendance for.
2. Check the attendance status of the student by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Is Absent" field.
3. Indicate who checked the student in by selecting "Student", "Teacher", or "Both" in the "Checked By" field.
4. Enter the date of attendance in the "Date" field.
5. Enter the time of attendance in the "Time" field.
6. Enter any comments or remarks about the student's attendance in the "Comments" field.
7. Enter the student's email address in the "Student Email" field.
8. Enter the student's phone number in the "Student Phone" field.
9. Indicate if the student has been checked in by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Checked In" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (student_name): Enter the name of the student you are tracking attendance for.
* **Is Absent** (is_absent): Select "Yes" if the student is absent, "No" otherwise.
* **Checked By** (checked_by): Select who checked the student in, such as "Student", "Teacher", or "Both".
* **Date** (date): Enter the date of attendance in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time** (time): Enter the time of attendance in the format of 12:00 PM.
* **Comments** (comments): Enter any comments or remarks about the student's attendance.
* **Student Email** (student_email): Enter the student's email address, if applicable.
* **Student Phone** (student_phone): Enter the student's phone number, if applicable.
* **Checked In** (checked_in): Select "Yes" if the student has been checked in, "No" otherwise.
