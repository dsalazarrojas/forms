# Dissertation Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a leave of absence for a student's dissertation period.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your student's details, including their name, email, and phone number.
2. Specify the start and end dates of your absence.
3. Provide a reason for your absence.
4. Enter your expected return date.
5. Select your academic department.
6. Provide contact information for your academic officer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **student_name** (text, required: false): Enter your name as a student.
* **date_of_absence_start** (date, required: false): Enter the start date of your absence.
* **date_of_absence_end** (date, required: false): Enter the end date of your absence.
* **reason_for_absence** (text, required: false): Provide a brief explanation for your absence.
* **expected_return_date** (date, required: false): Enter your expected return date.
* **academic_department** (text, required: false): Enter your academic department.
* **student_email** (email, required: false): Enter your student email address.
* **student_phone** (text, required: false): Enter your student phone number.
* **academic_officer** (text, required: false): Enter the name of your academic officer.
* **academic_officer_email** (email, required: false): Enter your academic officer's email address.
* **academic_officer_phone** (text, required: false): Enter your academic officer's phone number.
* **academic_officer_department** (text, required: false): Enter your academic officer's department.
* **status** (select_one, required: false): Select the status of your request: Pending, Approved, or Denied.
* **notes** (note, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments about your request.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check the accuracy of your information before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, please consult with your academic department or relevant authorities.
- This form is for requesting a leave of absence for your dissertation period, so please ensure that you have a valid reason for your absence.
