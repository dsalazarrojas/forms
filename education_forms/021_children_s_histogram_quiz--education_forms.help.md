# Children's Histogram Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This quiz is designed to test your understanding of histograms, their basics, and their application in various contexts. It is a self-assessment tool for children to gauge their knowledge and identify areas where they need more guidance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question carefully, selecting the most appropriate response from the given options.
2. Read each question's hint carefully to understand what is being asked.
3. Submit the quiz once you have completed all questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of the student taking the quiz.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_one, required): Choose the grade level of the student.
* **Quiz Date** (`quiz_date`, date, required): Enter the date the quiz was taken.
* **What is a histogram** (`question_1`, select_one, required): A histogram is a type of graph that shows data in intervals. Choose the correct definition from the options.
* **What does the height of a bar in a histogram represent** (`question_2`, select_one, required): The height of a bar in a histogram represents the frequency or count of data in that interval.
* **What is the difference between a bar graph and a histogram** (`question_3`, select_one, required): Histograms use intervals and show continuous data, while bar graphs show categorical data.
* **Looking at a histogram, how do you find the most common interval** (`question_4`, select_one, required): Find the tallest bar, which represents the most common interval.
* **If a histogram shows student test scores, what does each interval represent** (`question_5`, select_one, required): Each interval represents a range of scores, like 70-79 or 80-89.
* **How many students scored between 80-89 if the bar height is 7** (`question_6`, number, required): The number of students who scored within the 80-89 interval is 7.
* **Which of these would be best shown in a histogram** (`question_7`, select_one, required): The heights of students in a class.
* **A histogram shows daily temperature readings. What information does it best show** (`question_8`, select_one, required): The frequency or occurrence of certain temperature ranges.
* **If a histogram has two bars of equal height, what does that tell you** (`question_9`, select_one, required): Those two intervals have the same frequency.
* **How would a histogram change if more data from one interval was added** (`question_10`, select_one, required): The bar for that interval would be taller.
* **How confident are you in understanding histograms** (`confidence`, number, required): Rate your confidence level from 1 (not confident) to 5 (very confident).
* **What parts of histograms do you find most difficult** (`difficult_parts`, text, optional): Describe the parts of histograms you find challenging to understand.
* **Do you need more help understanding histograms** (`need_more_help`, select_one, optional): Choose your answer, and we will provide additional guidance if needed.
* **I have completed this quiz** (`quiz_submission`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have finished the quiz.

## Tips

* Take your time to carefully read each question and its hint before answering.
* Use the given options to choose the most accurate response.
* If you are unsure about any question, try to use your best judgment or refer to additional resources for help.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the quiz.
