# Lecture Evaluation Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users evaluate lectures, providing feedback on overall quality, content, and personal experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Review the lecture and gather your thoughts before starting.
* Answer each field carefully, providing honest and constructive feedback.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Quality of the Lecture** (`introduction`, text): Please provide a brief overview of your experience with the lecture.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, select_one): Rate the overall quality of the lecture using the scale:
	+ Poor
	+ Fair
	+ Good
	+ Excellent
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note): Please provide any comments or suggestions about the lecture.
* **Lecturer's Name** (`lecturer_name`, text): Please provide the lecturer's name.
* **Date of the Lecture** (`date_of_lecture`, date): Please provide the date of the lecture in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Time of the Lecture** (`time_of_lecture`, time): Please provide the time of the lecture in HH:MM AM/PM format.
* **How Helpful?** (`helpfulness`, number): Rate how helpful the content was to you.
* **Relevance** (`relevance`, number): Rate how relevant the content was to you.
* **Enjoyment** (`enjoyment`, number): Rate how enjoyable the lecture was.
* **Pace** (`pace`, number): Rate how well-paced the lecture was.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number): Rate your overall satisfaction with the lecture.
* **Would Recommend** (`would_recommend`, text): Please provide a statement indicating whether you would recommend the lecture to others.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note): Please provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Would Recommend to Others** (`would_recommend_lecture_to`, text): Please provide a statement indicating whether you would recommend the lecture to others.
