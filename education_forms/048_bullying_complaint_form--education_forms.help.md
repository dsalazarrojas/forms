# Bullying Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help students, parents, and staff report incidents of bullying that occur within the school or educational institution.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the correct reporter type.
2. Provide your name and contact information if applicable.
3. Select the date you are submitting this report.
4. Choose the location where the incident occurred.
5. Provide the name and details of the student being bullied.
6. List the names of the students or individuals bullying the victim.
7. Indicate if there were witnesses and list their names if applicable.
8. Describe the type of bullying that occurred.
9. Provide a detailed description of the incident.
10. If necessary, explain the reason for the bullying.
11. Indicate the impact of the bullying on the victim.
12. If applicable, indicate if there have been previous incidents of bullying.
13. Confirm if the incident has been reported before.
14. Specify the resolution or action you would like to take.
15. Attach any supporting evidence or documentation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Date** (`report_date`, date, required): Select the date you are submitting this report.
* **Who Is Making This Report** (`reporter_type`, select_one, required): Choose the correct reporter type from the options provided.
* **Your Name** (`reporter_name`, text, required): Enter your full name if you are not submitting an anonymous report.
* **Contact Email** (`reporter_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address if you are reporting this incident.
* **Contact Number** (`reporter_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact number if you are reporting this incident.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Choose the school or institution where the incident occurred.
* **Grade or Department** (`grade_or_department`, text, required): Enter the grade or department where the incident occurred.
* **Date Incident Occurred** (`incident_date`, date, required): Select the date the incident occurred.
* **Time of Incident** (`incident_time`, time, required): Provide the approximate time of the incident.
* **Location of Incident** (`incident_location`, select_one, required): Select where the incident happened from the provided options.
* **Name of Student Being Bullied** (`bullied_student_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the victim.
* **Name of Student(s) Bullying** (`bullying_student_name`, text, required): Enter the full name(s) of the students or individuals bullying the victim.
* **Were There Witnesses** (`witnesses_present`, select_one, required): Indicate if there were witnesses.
* **Names of Witnesses** (`witness_names`, text, required): If applicable, list the names of witnesses.
* **Type of Bullying** (`bullying_type`, select_multiple, required): Choose the type of bullying that occurred from the provided options.
* **Detailed Description of Incident** (`incident_description`, text, required): Provide a detailed account of what happened.
* **Reason for Bullying** (`bullying_motivation`, text, required): If applicable, explain why the bullying may have occurred.
* **Impact on Bullied Student** (`impact_on_victim`, select_multiple, required): Indicate the impact on the victim from the provided options.
* **Previous Bullying Incidents** (`previous_incidents`, select_one, required): Indicate if this is not the first time the victim has been bullied.
* **Has This Been Reported Before** (`reported_before`, select_one, required): Confirm if the incident has been reported before.
* **Action Requested** (`action_requested`, text, required): Specify the resolution or action you would like to take.
* **Supporting Evidence or Documentation** (`supporting_evidence`, select_one, required): Indicate if you are attaching any supporting evidence or documentation.
* **Keep This Report Confidential** (`confidentiality`, select_one, required): Choose who you would like to limit access to the report.
