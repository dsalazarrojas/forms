# Digital Teaching Practices Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Teaching Practices Assessment is a tool for teachers to evaluate their own digital teaching practices. This assessment helps teachers reflect on their use of various digital tools and methods to enhance their teaching effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields marked as 'required' in the form. These fields are crucial for the assessment and should be filled out thoroughly.
2. Select the correct options for each question, and make sure to choose one option for select_one type fields and multiple options for select_multiple type fields.
3. Leave out any optional fields that do not apply to your teaching practices.
4. Review the form carefully to ensure all required fields are completed before submitting.

Tips:

* Be honest and thorough in your responses, as this assessment is for your own reflection and growth.
* If you are unsure about any question, refer to the provided options or your own records for guidance.
* This assessment is confidential and will not be shared with anyone without your explicit consent.
