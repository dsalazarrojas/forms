# ferpa_notice_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ferpa_notice_form is used to collect information from students about their consent to grant or deny access to their educational records to specific entities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the entities you grant or deny access to, and select the corresponding options from the ferpa_consent field.
2. Select the purpose for which you want to grant or deny access from the different_purposes field.
3. Indicate whether you want to grant or deny access on your laptop, smartphone, or tablet from the opened_on field.
4. Add any additional comments or notes about your consent in the note field.
5. Finally, confirm your consent or denial in the ferpa_consent_2 field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **ferpa_consent** (select_multiple, required): This field allows you to grant or deny access to your educational records to specific entities. Select all the entities you grant access to.
* **records_and_data** (select_one, required): This field lets you select the purpose for which you grant or deny access. Choose the purpose that best suits your needs.
* **different_purposes** (text, optional): Provide a brief description of the purpose for which you want to grant or deny access.
* **opened_on** (select_one, required): Choose the device on which you want to grant or deny access.
* **note** (note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your consent.
* **ferpa_consent_2** (select_multiple, required): Confirm your consent or denial to grant or deny access to your educational records.
