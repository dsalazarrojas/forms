# Veterinary Faculty Presentation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Faculty Presentation Request Form is designed for veterinary faculty members to request to present at events or share their expertise with others. This form helps the event organizers to understand the presenter's information, schedule, and presentation details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your user information in the "User Information" page.
2. Provide details about the event you are interested in presenting at in the "Event Details" page.
3. Select the event dates you are available to present at in the "Event Dates" page.
4. Fill out the "Presenter Information" page with your expertise and experience.
5. Describe your presentation content in the "Presentation Content" page.
6. Choose the format of your presentation in the "Presentation Format" page.
7. Select the language of your presentation in the "Presentation Language" page.
8. Specify the length of your presentation in the "Presentation Length" page.
9. Choose a topic for your presentation in the "Presentation Topic" page.
10. If you are providing a presentation file, upload it in the "Presentation File" page.
11. Finally, provide your availability information in the "Presenter Availability" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (page 1)
	+ Enter your name and contact information to help us contact you regarding your request.
* **Event Details** (page 2)
	+ Enter the event details where you would like to present at.
* **Event Dates** (page 3)
	+ Select the event dates you are available to present at, by choosing 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Presentation Information** (page 4)
	+ Provide your expertise and experience as a presenter.
* **Schedule Request** (page 5)
	+ Select whether you have a specific schedule request for your presentation.
* **Presenter Information** (page 6)
	+ Enter your relevant information as a presenter.
* **Presenter Experience** (page 7)
	+ Describe your experience as a presenter.
* **Presentation Content** (page 8)
	+ Describe the content of your presentation.
* **Presentation Format** (page 9)
	+ Select the format of your presentation, such as video, text, or image.
* **Presentation Language** (page 10)
	+ Select the language of your presentation.
* **Presentation Length** (page 11)
	+ Select the length of your presentation.
* **Presentation Topic** (page 12)
	+ Choose a topic for your presentation.
* **Presentation File** (page 13)
	+ If applicable, upload the presentation file.
* **Presenter Availability** (page 14)
	+ Provide your availability information to help us schedule your presentation.
