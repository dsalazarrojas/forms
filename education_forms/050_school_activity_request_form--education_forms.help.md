# School Activity Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed for school staff to submit requests for activities, events, or programs that they want to organize or participate in. This form helps them to provide necessary information about the activity, including its title, date, number of participants, and other details.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Activity Title
Enter the title of the activity, event, or program you want to submit.

### Step 2: Activity Date
Select the date when the activity will take place.

### Step 3: Number of Participants
Enter the number of participants expected for the activity.

### Step 4: Program
Choose the program or category under which the activity falls (e.g., School Club, School Organization, or Student Event).

### Step 5: Payment Details
Add any additional payment details, if necessary.

### Step 6: Category
Select the category of the activity (e.g., School Club, School Organization, or Student Event).

### Step 7: Location
Enter the location where the activity will take place.

### Step 8: Description
Provide a detailed description of the activity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Activity Title** (activity_title, text, required: false): Enter a short title of the activity, event, or program.
* **Activity Date** (activity_date, date, required: false): Select the date when the activity will take place.
* **Number of Participants** (num_participants, number, required: false): Enter the number of participants expected for the activity.
* **Program** (program, text, required: false): Choose the program or category under which the activity falls (e.g., School Club, School Organization, or Student Event).
* **Payment Details** (payment_details, note, required: false): Add any additional payment details, if necessary.
* **Category** (category, select_one, required: false): Select the category of the activity (e.g., School Club, School Organization, or Student Event).
* **Location** (location, text, required: false): Enter the location where the activity will take place.
* **Description** (description, text, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the activity.

## Tips
* Ensure that you provide accurate and complete information to help the school staff review and approve your request.
* Please note that some fields are optional, but it's recommended to fill in all required fields for better review and approval.
