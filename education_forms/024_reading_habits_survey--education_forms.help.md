# Reading Habits Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about your reading habits, including how often you read, your favorite book genres, authors, and formats. Your responses will help us better understand your reading behavior.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer all questions thoroughly and accurately.
2. Select only one option for questions that have multiple options.
3. For questions without options, provide a text description of your answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Do you read frequently?** (`reading_habits_survey`, select_multiple, required): Answer with "Yes" or "No" to indicate how often you read.
* **2. How often do you read?** (`reading_frequency`, number, required): Choose a number to indicate how many times you read in a week.
* **3. Favorite book genres?** (`favorite_book_genres`, select_one, required): Select one of the options that best represents your favorite book genre.
* **4. Most liked book** (`most_liked_book`, text, required): Describe the book that you enjoy the most.
* **5. Do you read e-books or physical books?** (`book_format`, select_multiple, required): Answer with "Yes" or "No" for both e-books and physical books.
* **6. Other (please describe)** (`reading_frequency_other`, text, optional): If you don't see a specific option for your reading frequency, describe your reading frequency here.
* **7. Additional information about other (optional)** (`reading_frequency_other_details`, note, optional): If you'd like to provide more information about your reading frequency, do so here.
* **8. Do you have a favorite author?** (`favorite_author`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you have a favorite author.
* **9. Other authors (please list)** (`reading_frequency_other_other`, select_multiple, optional): If you don't see an author on the list, you can choose "Yes" and list the author(s).
* **10. Additional information about other authors (optional)** (`reading_frequency_other_other_details`, note, optional): If you'd like to provide more information about your favorite author(s), do so here.
* **11. Do you prefer a specific book format?** (`favorite_book_format`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your preference.
* **12. Other formats (please list)** (`reading_frequency_other_other_other`, select_multiple, optional): If you don't see a format on the list, you can choose "Yes" and list the format(s).
* **13. Additional information about other formats (optional)** (`reading_frequency_other_other_other_details`, note, optional): If you'd like to provide more information about your preferred format(s), do so here.

* **15. Additional information about other things (optional)** (`reading_frequency_other_other_other_details`, note, optional): If you'd like to provide more information about what you read, do so here.
* **16. Other other things (please list)** (`reading_frequency_other_other_other_other`, select_multiple, optional): If you don't see an option for what you read, you can choose "Yes" and list what you read.
* **17. Additional information about other other things (optional)** (`reading_frequency_other_other_other_other_details`, note, optional): If you'd like to provide more information about what you read, do so here.
* **18. Other other other things (please list)** (`reading_frequency_other_other_other_other_other`, select_multiple, optional): If you don't see an option for what you read, you can choose "Yes" and list what you read.
* **19. Additional information about other other other things (optional)** (`reading_frequency_other_other_other_other_other_details`, note, optional): If you'd like to provide more information about what you read, do so here.
* **20. Other other other other other things (please list)** (`reading_frequency_other_other_other_other_other_other`, select_multiple, optional): If you don't see an option for what you read, you can choose "Yes" and list what you read.
* **21. Additional information about other other other other other things (optional)** (`reading_frequency_other_other_other_other_other_other_details`, note, optional): If you'd like to provide more information about what you read, do so here.
