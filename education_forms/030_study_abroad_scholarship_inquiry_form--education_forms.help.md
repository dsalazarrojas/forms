# Study Abroad Scholarship Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students about their study abroad plans, preferences, and requirements for a scholarship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the options that apply to you and fill in your contact information. Make sure to answer all fields that are marked as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Study Abroad Country** (study_abroad_country): Select yes or no to indicate if you are interested in studying abroad in any country.
  - If you select yes, you will be considered for study abroad scholarship opportunities.
  - If you select no, you will not be considered for study abroad scholarship opportunities.
- **Study Abroad Program** (study_abroad_program): Check all that apply to indicate which study abroad programs interest you.
  - If you are interested in language programs, select "Yes" under "Language Programs".
  - If you are interested in academic programs, select "Yes" under "Academic Programs".
  - You can select multiple programs if you are interested in more than one.
- **Study Abroad Duration** (study_abroad_duration): Select yes or no to indicate if you are interested in short-term or long-term study abroad opportunities.
- **Study Abroad Language** (study_abroad_language): Check all that apply to indicate which languages you are proficient in or would like to learn abroad.
  - If you select "Yes" for English, you are proficient in English.
  - If you select "Yes" for Other, you have other languages that you are proficient in or would like to learn.
- **Study Abroad Budget** (study_abroad_budget): Enter your expected budget for the study abroad program.
  - This field is optional, but it will help us to better understand your financial requirements.
- **Study Abroad Email** (study_abroad_email): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
  - This field is required for us to communicate with you about your scholarship application.
- **Study Abroad Phone** (study_abroad_phone): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
  - This field is optional, but it will help us to contact you in case we need more information.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer all required fields to ensure your application is complete.
- If you have any questions or need help, feel free to contact us.
- We will review your application and contact you with more information about your study abroad scholarship opportunities.
