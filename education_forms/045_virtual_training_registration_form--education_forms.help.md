# Virtual Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for virtual training sessions. Please complete the form with your details to sign up for the training you're interested in.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill in the form fields with your details as prompted.
- Enter your first and last names in the respective fields.
- Enter your email address and phone number in their respective fields.
- Enter the date and time you wish to attend the training in the 'Date' and 'Time' fields.
- Finally, select the course title you're interested in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone**: Enter your primary contact phone number.
* **Date**: Enter the date you wish to attend the training in the 'yyyy-mm-dd' format.
* **Time**: Enter the time you wish to start the training in the 'hh:mm AM/PM' format.
* **Course Title**: Select the title of the course you're interested in.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your email address correctly to ensure you receive training updates and reminders.
* If you have any issues while filling the form, contact your training administrator.
* Review your entries before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
