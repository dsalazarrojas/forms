# Parental Consent And Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from parents or guardians for a child's participation in a particular activity or event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Parent Name" field with the name of the parent or guardian.
2. Enter the contact information in the "Contact Info" field, including a phone number and/or an email address.
3. Provide the child's name in the "Child Name" field.
4. Enter the date of birth of the child in the "Date of Birth" field.
5. Select "True" or "False" for "Consent to Participate" to indicate the parent's or guardian's consent for the child to participate in the activity or event.
6. Select "True" or "False" for "Release of Liability" to indicate the parent's or guardian's understanding of the potential risks and hazards associated with the activity or event.
7. If necessary, use the "Minimalist" field to provide any additional comments or information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Parent Name (text)**: The name of the parent or guardian.
*   **Contact Info (text)**: The contact information of the parent or guardian, including a phone number and/or an email address.
*   **Child Name (text)**: The name of the child participating in the activity or event.
*   **Date of Birth (date)**: The date of birth of the child.
*   **Consent to Participate (select_one)**: Select "True" to indicate the parent's or guardian's consent for the child to participate in the activity or event.
*   **Release of Liability (select_one)**: Select "True" to indicate the parent's or guardian's understanding of the potential risks and hazards associated with the activity or event.
*   **Minimalist (note)**: A note field for any additional comments or information.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
*   Be sure to select the correct options for "Consent to Participate" and "Release of Liability".
