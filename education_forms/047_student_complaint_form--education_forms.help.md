> **Student Complaint Form - Help Guide**
>
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and track student complaints, allowing administrators to efficiently manage and resolve issues related to student experiences, concerns, or issues within the educational institution. This form helps to provide a structured format for documenting and addressing student complaints, ensuring that concerns are addressed in a fair and timely manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter Complainant Info**: Provide information about the student who is submitting the complaint. This includes any relevant details that may help in addressing the concern.
2. **Select Complaint Type**: Choose the category that best describes the nature of the complaint. This will help in assigning the issue to the relevant authority.
3. **Provide Complaint Description**: Enter a clear and concise description of the issue or concern.
4. **Select Resolution Status**: Indicate the current status of the complaint resolution.
5. **Enter Complaint Date**: Provide the date when the issue was first raised.
6. **Enter Follow Up Actions**: Provide any actions taken to follow up on the complaint.
7. **Enter Resolution Outcome**: Describe the outcome of the resolution.
8. **Enter Resolution Comment**: Add any additional comments or notes related to the resolution.
9. **Assign to**: Select the person or department responsible for addressing the complaint.
10. **Create By**: Identify the person who initially created the complaint.
11. **Update By**: Record the person who last updated the complaint.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Complainant Info** (Required): Enter the name and contact information of the student submitting the complaint.
* **Complaint Type (Select One)**: Choose one of the three options to categorize the nature of the complaint. This will help in assigning the issue to the relevant authority.
* **Complaint Description (Text)**: Provide a clear and concise description of the issue or concern.
* **Resolution Status (Select One)**: Indicate the current status of the resolution. Choose one of the two options.
* **Complaint Date (Date)**: Enter the date when the issue was first raised.
* **Follow Up Actions (Text)**: Provide any actions taken to follow up on the complaint.
* **Resolution Outcome (Text)**: Describe the outcome of the resolution.
* **Resolution Comment (Text)**: Add any additional comments or notes related to the resolution.
* **Assigned To (Select One)**: Select the person or department responsible for addressing the complaint.
* **Created By (Select One)**: Identify the person who initially created the complaint.
* **Updated By (Select One)**: Record the person who last updated the complaint.
