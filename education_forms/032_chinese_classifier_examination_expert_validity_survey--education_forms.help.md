# Chinese Classifier Examination Expert Validity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the validity of a Chinese classifier examination, which is a test used to evaluate the proficiency of language learners in a specific language. The expert who completes this form will rate and provide feedback on the exam's items, instructions, and overall validity, ensuring that the exam is effective and unbiased. The results will help to improve the exam's validity and reliability.

## How To Complete This Form

This form consists of several sections, each with its own set of questions and instructions. Please answer all fields carefully and honestly, as your input is crucial in helping us improve the exam.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Expert Name** (text, required): Please enter your name and affiliation as the expert who is administering or has experience with the language assessment.
* **Affiliation** (text, optional): If you are affiliated with an institution or organization, please enter it here. This is not required, but it will help us understand your background and experience.
* **Years of Relevant Experience** (number, optional): If you have experience working with language assessments, please enter the number of years you have been doing so. This will help us understand your expertise level.
* **Relevance of Test Items** (select_one, required): Please rate how relevant each test item is to the competencies it is supposed to measure. Choose from the following options:
	+ Highly Relevant
	+ Relevant
	+ Somewhat Relevant
	+ Not Relevant
* **Clarity of Instructions** (select_one, required): Please rate how clear the instructions are for test administrators and takers. Choose from the following options:
	+ Very Clear
	+ Clear
	+ Somewhat Clear
	+ Unclear
* **Item Difficulty Alignment** (select_one, required): Please rate whether the item difficulties are appropriate for the target level. Choose from the following options:
	+ True
	+ Somewhat
	+ False
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (text, optional): If you have any suggestions or recommendations for improving the exam, please enter them here. This is not required, but it will help us make the exam better.
* **Overall Validity Rating** (select_one, required): Please provide your expert judgment on the overall validity of the exam. Choose from the following options:
	+ High
	+ Moderate
	+ Low

## Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly and carefully, as your input is crucial in helping us improve the exam.
* If you are unsure about any question, please choose the option that best reflects your expert opinion.
* You can save your progress and come back to the form later if needed.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any question, please don't hesitate to contact us.
