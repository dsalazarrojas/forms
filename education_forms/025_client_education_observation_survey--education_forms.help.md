# Client Education Observation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback and observations about client education sessions to improve future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each section carefully and accurately.
2. Make sure you're filling out the correct information about the client and the session.
3. Review each section carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Observer First Name** (`observer_first_name`, text, required): Enter your legal first name.
* **Observer Last Name** (`observer_last_name`, text, required): Enter your legal last name.
* **Observer Email** (`observer_email`, email, required): Enter your primary contact email.
* **Observer Role** (`observer_role`, select_one, required): Choose your position (e.g., Instructor, Program Coordinator, Supervisor, Evaluator, Administrator, or Other).
* **Client First Name** (`client_first_name`, text, required): Enter the client's first name.
* **Client Last Name** (`client_last_name`, text, required): Enter the client's last name.
* **Client ID Number** (`client_id_number`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the client's ID number.
* **Education Session Type** (`education_session_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of session (e.g., Individual Instruction, Group Class, Workshop, Seminar, Online Session, Hands-On Training, or Other).
* **Session Topic** (`session_topic`, text, required): Enter the subject matter covered.
* **Observation Date** (`observation_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the observation.
* **Observation Time** (`observation_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of the observation.
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, select_one, required): Choose the session duration (e.g., Under 30 Minutes, 30-60 Minutes, 1-2 Hours, 2-3 Hours, or Over 3 Hours).
* **Client Engagement Level** (`client_engagement_level`, select_one, required): Choose the client's engagement level (e.g., Very Engaged, Engaged, Somewhat Engaged, Minimally Engaged, or Not Engaged).
* **Client Participation** (`client_participation`, select_one, required): Choose the client's participation level (e.g., Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Client Comprehension Level** (`comprehension_level`, select_one, required): Choose the client's understanding of the material (e.g., Excellent Understanding, Good Understanding, Adequate Understanding, Limited Understanding, or Poor Understanding).
* **Communication Effectiveness** (`communication_effectiveness`, select_one, required): Choose the client's communication effectiveness (e.g., Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Did Client Ask Questions?** (`questions_asked`, select_one, required): Choose the frequency of questions asked (e.g., Frequently, Occasionally, Rarely, or Never).
* **Quality of Questions Asked** (`questions_quality`, select_one, optional): Choose the quality of the questions asked (e.g., Excellent, Good, Adequate, Poor, or Not Applicable).
* **Observed Learning Barriers** (`learning_barriers`, select_multiple, required): Choose all the learning barriers observed (e.g., Language Barrier, Learning Disability, Lack of Prior Knowledge, Distractions, Fatigue, Anxiety or Stress, Technology Issues, or Other).
* **Client Strengths Observed** (`strengths_observed`, text, required): Enter the client's strengths observed.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, text, required): Enter areas where the client needs improvement.
* **Effectiveness of Educational Materials** (`materials_effectiveness`, select_one, required): Choose the effectiveness of the educational materials (e.g., Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Instructor Effectiveness** (`instructor_effectiveness`, select_one, optional): Choose the effectiveness of the instructor (e.g., Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor, or Not Applicable).
* **Recommendations for Future Sessions** (`recommendations`, text, required): Enter suggestions for improvement for future sessions.
* **Is Follow-Up Needed?** (`follow_up_needed`, select_one, required): Choose if additional support is needed (e.g., True or False).
* **Type of Follow-Up Needed** (`follow_up_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of follow-up needed (if applicable).
* **Overall Session Rating** (`overall_rating`, select_one, required): Choose the overall rating of the session (e.g., Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or observations.
* **Survey Completion Date** (`survey_completion_date`, date, required): Enter the date of completion.

## Tips

* Make sure to review each section carefully before submitting the form.
* Enter accurate and relevant information.
* If you're unsure about any part of the form, please refer to the provided guidance.
* This form is designed to improve future education sessions; please fill it out thoughtfully.
