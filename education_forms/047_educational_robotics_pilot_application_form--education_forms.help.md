# Educational Robotics Pilot Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Robotics Pilot Application Form is a crucial document that helps us assess your school's eligibility and feasibility for participating in the Educational Robotics Pilot program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your school's name and address in the `School Name` and `School Address` fields.
2. Provide the contact information of the person responsible for communicating with us, including their name, email, and phone number.
3. Indicate the type of robot(s) your school is interested in piloting or is already using.
4. Specify the number of robots you plan to use or are currently using.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your school or educational institution. 
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the person in charge of communication with our team. 
* **Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email of the person in charge of communication with our team. 
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the person in charge of communication with our team. 
* **School Address** (`school_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the address of your school or educational institution. 
* **District Name** (`district_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your school district. 
* **Robot Type** (`robot_type`, `text`, required/optional): Specify the type of robot you plan to use or are already using. 
* **Number of Robots** (`robot_number`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the number of robots you plan to use or are currently using.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are filled out to avoid any delays in processing your application.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us directly.
* Please ensure that the contact person's email and phone number are correct to facilitate smooth communication.
