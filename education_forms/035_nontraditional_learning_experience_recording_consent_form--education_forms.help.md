# Nontraditional Learning Experience Recording Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect participant information and recording details for non-traditional learning experiences. It will be used to ensure that participants are aware of how their data will be used and that it is accurately recorded.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name as the participant.
2. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
3. Enter your date of birth for demographic purposes.
4. Describe your learning experience, including the program name, type, location, and date.
5. Specify the duration of the program.
6. Provide any additional recording information.
7. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your permission for your data to be recorded.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Your name as the participant.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Your email address for contact purposes.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): Your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`participant_date_of_birth`, date, optional): Your date of birth for demographic purposes.
* **Learning Experience** (`participant_learning_experience`, text, optional): A description of your non-traditional learning experience, including program name, type, location, and date.
* **Program Info** (`program_info`, text, optional): Additional information about the program you participated in.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, optional): The name of the program you participated in.
* **Program Type** (`program_type`, select_one, optional):
	+ Internship
	+ Training
	+ Education Program
	+ Other
* **Program Location** (`program_location`, text, optional): The location of the program you participated in.
* **Program Date** (`program_date`, date, optional): The date of the program you participated in.
* **Duration** (`duration`, text, optional): The duration of the program you participated in.
* **Recording Information** (`recording_info`, text, optional): Any additional information about the recording.
* **Permission** (`participant_permission`, select_one, required):
	+ Yes
	+ No

Note: The "Permission" field is required and must be selected to indicate your consent for your data to be recorded.
