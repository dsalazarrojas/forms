# Weathering And Erosion Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the weathering and erosion processes on a specific site. The information gathered will help users understand and document site conditions, observations, and potential actions to mitigate erosion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer the questions about site conditions.
2. Record your weathering and erosion observations.
3. Plan actions to mitigate erosion.
4. Select relevant educational materials and objectives.
5. Assess the environmental impact of the site.
6. Choose a recommended action plan (if applicable).
7. Evaluate the educational outcome of the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Site Conditions** (Page 1): Describe the site's current conditions, including any notable features or concerns related to weathering and erosion.
  - **Weathering Observation** (Page 2): Note any notable weathering or erosion observations you've made on the site.
  - **Erosion Observation** (Page 3): Describe any erosion features you've observed on the site.
  - **Action Plan** (Page 4): Outline the steps you plan to take to mitigate weathering and erosion on the site.
  - **Educational Material** (Page 5): Select the educational materials that apply to the site, such as materials used and methods used.
  - **Educational Objectives** (Page 6): Choose the primary objectives of the educational materials, such as documenting site conditions or recording observations.
  - **Environmental Impact** (Page 7): (Not required) Rate the potential environmental impact of weathering and erosion on the site (e.g. on a scale of 1-10).
  - **Recommended Action Plan** (Page 8): (Not required) Select a recommended action plan to mitigate weathering and erosion on the site.
  - **Educational Outcome** (Page 9): Assess the educational outcome of the assessment, rating it as "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
  - **Other Notes** (Page 10): Record any additional information or observations not covered by the other fields.
