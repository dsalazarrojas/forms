# Educational Equity Resource Access Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about the educational equity resource access of students, teachers, and school administrators.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Select the relevant options for each field from the dropdown menus or enter the required information in the text fields.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) in the field label.
3. If you need help with any of the fields, refer to the field-by-field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Location**: Select the location of your school from the provided options.
	+ This field is used to gather information about the school's location.
* **Demographic Info**: Enter your demographic information, such as age, gender, or ethnicity.
	+ This field is used to collect demographic data about the respondent.
* **Educational Experience**: Enter your educational experience, such as years of experience or level of education completed.
	+ This field is used to gather information about the respondent's educational background.
* **Access Barriers**: Select any access barriers you are experiencing, such as language access or technological barriers.
	+ This field is used to identify potential barriers to resource access.
* **Support Services**: Enter any support services you currently receive or have access to, such as counseling or mentoring.
	+ This field is used to gather information about available support services.
* **Language Access**: Select your primary language of access, such as English or Spanish.
	+ This field is used to identify the language of communication.
* **Survey Participation**: Enter any additional comments or information about your survey participation.
	+ This field is used to collect feedback or comments about the survey process.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your contact information, such as email or phone number.
	+ This field is used to gather contact information for follow-up purposes.
* **Resource Access**: Select the resources you currently have access to, such as textbooks or technology.
	+ This field is used to identify available resources.
* **Parent Info**: Select any relevant parent information, such as parent-teacher association involvement.
	+ This field is used to gather information about parent involvement.
* **Staff Info**: Select any relevant staff information, such as school staff training.
	+ This field is used to gather information about staff involvement.
* **Student Info**: Select any relevant student information, such as student clubs or organizations.
	+ This field is used to gather information about student involvement.
* **School Admin Info**: Select any relevant school administration information, such as school policies.
	+ This field is used to gather information about school administration.
* **School Teacher Info**: Select any relevant teacher information, such as teacher training or experience.
	+ This field is used to gather information about teacher involvement.
* **Teacher Info**: Select any relevant teacher information, such as teacher certification.
	+ This field is used to gather information about teacher qualifications.
* **Student Teacher Info**: Select any relevant student-teacher information, such as student-teacher organizations.
	+ This field is used to gather information about student-teacher relationships.
* **School Info**: Select any relevant school information, such as school events or activities.
	+ This field is used to gather information about school events.
* **Teacher School Info**: Select any relevant teacher-school information, such as teacher-school collaboration.
	+ This field is used to gather information about teacher-school relationships.
* **Resource Provider**: Select any relevant resource providers, such as textbook vendors.
	+ This field is used to gather information about resource providers.
* **Resource Provider Info**: Enter any additional information about resource providers.
	+ This field is used to gather more detailed information about resource providers.
* **Resource Info**: Enter any information about resources not listed in other fields.
	+ This field is used to collect more detailed information about resources.
* **School Year**: Enter the school year of the current academic year.
	+ This field is used to gather information about the academic year.
* **Survey Response**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about the survey.
	+ This field is used to collect feedback or comments about the survey process.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for contact.
	+ This field is used to gather contact information for follow-up purposes.

## Tips

* Please be as accurate and honest as possible when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the administrator for clarification.
* If you have any suggestions or recommendations for improving the form, please let us know.
