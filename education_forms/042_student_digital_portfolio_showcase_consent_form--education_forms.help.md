# Student Digital Portfolio Showcase Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Digital Portfolio Showcase Consent Form is a document that requires parents or guardians to provide consent for their child to participate in a digital portfolio showcase. This form is used to ensure that students' work is shared with their parents or guardians and that they are aware of their child's participation in the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields. Fields marked as "required" are necessary to complete the form.
2. For fields that allow multiple selections, select all applicable options.
3. Enter your contact information, including your email and phone number, so that we can reach out to you for any questions or updates.
4. Provide any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field if necessary.
5. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the parent or guardian who is signing this form.
* **Guardian Permission** (`guardian_permission`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all applicable options that apply to your child's participation in the program. Options are:
	+ True
	+ False
* **School Program** (`school_program`, select_one, required: false): Select the school program that your child is participating in from the options provided.
	+ Program 1
	+ Program 2
	+ Program 3
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the student who is participating in the program.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so that we can reach out to you for any questions or updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so that we can reach out to you for any questions or updates.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of birth or start date of the program.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time (not applicable in this form, but left as is to match the original YAML structure).
* **Notes** (`note`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments about your child's participation in the program.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so that we can reach out to you for any questions or updates.
