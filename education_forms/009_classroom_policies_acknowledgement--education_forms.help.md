# Classroom Policies Acknowledgement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from parents or guardians to acknowledge their understanding of the classroom policies for a particular class or school.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please review the provided pages and follow these steps:

1. Review the classroom policies and understand the expectations outlined in the form.
2. Fill out the required fields with your relevant information.
3. Sign the electronic signature field by typing your full name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Please enter your child's name as it appears on their official identification documents.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, text, required): Please select the grade level of your child from the provided list.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_guardian_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as the parent or guardian of the student.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Please enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **I understand that attendance is mandatory and I must report absences by 8-30 AM** (`policy_1_attendance`, select_one, required): Please select "Yes, I agree" to indicate your understanding of the attendance policy.
* **I agree to support my child in completing homework assignments and checking the folder daily** (`policy_2_homework`, select_one, required): Please select "Yes, I agree" to indicate your understanding of the homework policy.
* **I have read the Classroom Behavior and Discipline policy** (`policy_3_behavior`, select_one, required): Please select "Yes, I have read it" to indicate your understanding of the behavior policy.
* **I understand the acceptable use policy for classroom technology and devices** (`policy_4_technology`, select_one, required): Please select "Yes, I understand" to indicate your understanding of the technology policy.
* **I am aware that this is a nut-free classroom** (`policy_5_snacks`, select_one, required): Please select "Yes, I am aware" to indicate your understanding of the health and safety policy.
* **Please type your full name as an electronic signature** (`electronic_signature`, text, required): Please type your full name to serve as an electronic signature.
* **Date** (`date_signed`, date, required): Please select the date you are signing this acknowledgment.
* **Any questions or comments for the teacher?** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Please use this field to provide any questions or comments you may have for the teacher.
