# Engineering Diploma Image Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Engineering Diploma Image Submission form is designed to collect and verify information about engineering diplomas. This form is intended for students, institutions, or authorities to submit and verify diploma images.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Upload supporting documents related to the diploma in the "Upload Supporting Documents" field.
3. Enter the institution name where the diploma was issued in the "Institution Name" field.
4. Enter the date the diploma was issued in the "Issuing Date" field.
5. Enter the issuing authority that granted the diploma in the "Issuing Authority" field.
6. Enter any comments or remarks in the "Comments" field.
7. Upload the diploma image in the "Upload Supporting Documents" field.
8. Enter the date the diploma was issued in the "Date of Issue" field.
9. Enter the date the diploma was issued in the "Date of Issuance" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Engineering Diploma Image**: This is the main field where you can upload the image of the engineering diploma.
* **Supporting Documents**: Upload any supporting documents related to the diploma, such as transcripts or certificates.
* **Student Name**: Enter the name of the student who received the diploma.
* **Student ID**: Enter the student ID number or any other unique identifier for the student.
* **Date of Issue**: Enter the date the diploma was issued.
* **Date of Issuance**: Enter the date the diploma was issued.
* **Institution Name**: Enter the name of the institution where the diploma was issued.
* **Institution Address**: Enter the address of the institution where the diploma was issued.
* **Issuing Authority**: Enter the name of the authority that issued the diploma.
* **Issuing Date**: Enter the date the diploma was issued.
* **Signature**: This field is likely for the signature of the person submitting the form, but it is not a required field.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or remarks about the diploma.
* **Upload Supporting Documents**: Upload any supporting documents related to the diploma, such as transcripts or certificates.
* **Uploaded Files**: This field displays the uploaded supporting documents.
* **Review Comments**: Enter any review comments or remarks about the submitted form.
