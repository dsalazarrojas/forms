# Online Teaching Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Online Teaching Application Form is a tool for individuals to apply for teaching positions online.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the available course types in the "Choose course type" dropdown and select the one that best describes your qualifications.
2. If required, upload any relevant certification files by clicking on the "Browse" button next to the "Certification" field.
3. Upload your CV by clicking on the "Browse" button next to the "CV" field.
4. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. Repeat the above steps for any additional "Name" fields as they appear.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Choose course type** (`application_form`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of course you are qualified to teach from the dropdown menu.
* **Certification file** (`certifications`, text, required: false): If you have any relevant certification files, upload them here.
* **CV upload** (`uploaded_cv`, text, required: false): Upload your CV to provide more information about your experience and qualifications.
* **Choose course** (`application_form_2`, select_multiple, required: false): Select multiple courses you are qualified to teach, this field is likely meant to be used in combination with the "application_form" field.
* **CV** (`uploaded_cv_2`, text, required: false): Similar to the "CV upload" field, this appears to be a redundant field and can be ignored.
* **Certification upload** (`uploaded_certification`, text, required: false): Upload any certifications you have.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your name for our records.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Name 2** (`name_2`, text, required: false): This field seems redundant and can be ignored.
* **Name 3** (`name_3`, text, required: false): This field seems redundant and can be ignored.
* **Name 4** (`name_4`, text, required: false): This field seems redundant and can be ignored.
* **Name 5** (`name_5`, text, required: false): This field seems redundant and can be ignored.
