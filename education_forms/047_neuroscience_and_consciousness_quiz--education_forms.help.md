# Neuroscience And Consciousness Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Neuroscience And Consciousness Quiz form is designed to gather data from learners about their thoughts, feelings, and experiences related to neuroscience and consciousness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the "Quiz Questions" page to begin.
2. Answer the questions to the best of your ability, taking your time to ensure accuracy.
3. Move on to the "Learner Confidence" page to provide your confidence level regarding your responses.
4. On the "Follow Up" page, share any additional information or thoughts you'd like.
5. On the "Participant Email" page, enter your email address so that we can follow up with you.
6. In the "Participant Phone" page, provide your contact phone number.
7. On the "Additional Comments" page, add any further details or comments.
8. On the "Form 7" page, select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your agreement or disagreement with the statements.
9. On the "Form 8" page, select "Yes" or "No" for each of the options provided.
10. On the "Form 9" page, select "Yes" or "No" for each of the options provided.
