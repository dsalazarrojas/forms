# School Withdrawal Offboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to document the withdrawal of a student from a school. It captures essential details about the student, the reason for withdrawal, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the school from which the student is withdrawing (school_id).
2. Enter the student's name (school_name).
3. Enter the student's first name (student_first_name).
4. Enter the student's last name (student_last_name).
5. Enter the student's birthdate (student_birthdate).
6. Enter the date of withdrawal (withdrawal_date).
7. Provide the reason for withdrawal (reason_for_withdrawal).
8. Enter the contact person's name (contact_person).
9. Enter the contact person's phone number (phone_number).
10. Enter the contact person's email address (email_address).
11. Enter the school's phone number (school_phone_number).
12. Enter the school's email address (school_email_address).
13. Enter any additional notes about the student (notes).
14. Enter any additional notes from the school (school_notes).
15. Confirm parent consent for the student's withdrawal (parent_consent).
16. Confirm staff consent for the student's withdrawal (staff_consent).
17. Obtain the student's signature (student_signature).
18. Obtain the staff signature (staff_signature).
19. Obtain the school signature (school_signature).
20. Obtain the parent signature (parent_signature).
21. Enter the date the student was offboarded (date_offboarded).
22. Enter the date of the student's last attendance (date_of_last_attendance).
23. Provide the reason for transfer (reason_for_transfer).
24. Enter any other reasons for withdrawal (other_reasons).
25. Enter any other comments or information (other).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **school_id** (Number): Select the school from which the student is withdrawing.
* **school_name** (Text): Enter the name of the school.
* **student_first_name** (Text): Enter the student's first name.
* **student_last_name** (Text): Enter the student's last name.
* **student_birthdate** (Date): Enter the student's birthdate.
* **withdrawal_date** (Date): Enter the date of withdrawal.
* **reason_for_withdrawal** (Text): Provide a reason for the student's withdrawal.
* **contact_person** (Text): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **phone_number** (Text): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **email_address** (Email): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **school_phone_number** (Text): Enter the school's phone number.
* **school_email_address** (Email): Enter the school's email address.
* **notes** (Note): Enter any additional notes about the student.
* **school_notes** (Note): Enter any additional notes from the school.
* **parent_consent** (Select One): Confirm parent consent for the student's withdrawal.
* **staff_consent** (Select One): Confirm staff consent for the student's withdrawal.
* **student_signature** (Text): Obtain the student's signature.
* **staff_signature** (Text): Obtain the staff signature.
* **school_signature** (Text): Obtain the school signature.
* **parent_signature** (Text): Obtain the parent signature.
* **date_offboarded** (Date): Enter the date the student was offboarded.
* **date_of_last_attendance** (Date): Enter the date of the student's last attendance.
* **reason_for_transfer** (Text): Provide the reason for transfer.
* **other_reasons** (Text): Enter any other reasons for withdrawal.
* **other** (Text): Enter any other comments or information.

Note: The required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*).
