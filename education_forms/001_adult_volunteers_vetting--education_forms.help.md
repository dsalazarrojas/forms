# Adult Volunteers Vetting - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from adult volunteers who wish to help with school activities. The purpose of this form is to ensure that we have accurate and up-to-date information about you, which will enable us to effectively manage our volunteers and match them with suitable opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name, ensuring it is spelled correctly and as it appears on official documents.
2. Provide your date of birth to confirm your age.
3. Enter your contact information, including email and phone number, to ensure we can reach you.
4. Provide your current home address to help us with logistical arrangements.
5. Select your areas of interest for volunteering.
6. Choose your general availability for volunteering.
7. Confirm your consent for a Disclosure and Barring Service (DBS) check, if applicable.
8. Briefly describe any previous volunteering experience.
9. Enter the name and contact phone number of your emergency contact.
10. Sign the declaration, confirming that the information provided is accurate and you agree to the school's volunteer code of conduct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, Text, **Required**): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, Date, **Required**): Ensure the date is entered in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, Email, **Required**): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, Text, **Required**): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Current Home Address** (`current_address`, Text, **Required**): Provide your full address including postcode to help with logistical arrangements.
* **Connection to School** (`connection_to_school`, Text, **Required**): Describe how you connect to the school, e.g., parent, former student, or community member.
* **Areas of Interest** (`areas_of_interest`, Select Multiple, **Optional**): Choose all that apply from the options provided:
	+ Classroom Assistance
	+ Field Trips
	+ Sports Coaching
	+ Arts and Crafts
	+ Library Support
	+ Fundraising Events
* **General Availability** (`availability`, Select Multiple, **Required**): Choose your general availability for volunteering:
	+ Weekday Mornings
	+ Weekday Afternoons
	+ Evenings
	+ Weekends
* **DBS Check Consent** (`dbs_check_consent`, Select One, **Required**): Confirm your consent for a Disclosure and Barring Service (DBS) check.
* **Previous Volunteering Experience** (`previous_experience`, Text, **Optional**): Briefly describe any relevant experience working with children.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, Text, **Required**): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, Text, **Required**): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Declaration** (`declaration`, Select One, **Required**): Confirm that the information provided is accurate and you agree to the school's volunteer code of conduct.
