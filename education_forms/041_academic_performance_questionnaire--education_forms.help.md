# Academic Performance Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Performance Questionnaire is a survey designed to gather feedback on various aspects of a course, including the instructor's performance, learning outcomes, and resources. This guide will walk you through each section of the form to help you provide accurate and helpful feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing the course code or title and instructor name.
2. Rate the clarity of learning outcomes, teaching effectiveness, fairness of assessment and grading, manageability of workload, availability of learning resources, and provide suggestions for improvement and additional comments.
3. Select the options that best fit your experience with the course.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Code or Title (1)**: Enter the unique identifier of the course or its title.
* **Instructor Name (2)**: Enter the name of the instructor or teaching team.
* **Semester or Term (4)**: Enter the academic term for which you are providing feedback (e.g., Fall, Spring, Summer).
* **Clarity of Learning Outcomes (5)**: Rate how clear the course learning outcomes were. Choose from:
	+ Very clear
	+ Clear
	+ Somewhat clear
	+ Unclear
* **Teaching Effectiveness (6)**: Rate the instructor's teaching performance. Choose from:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Fairness of Assessment and Grading (7)**: Rate how fair and transparent the assessment and grading were. Choose from:
	+ Very fair
	+ Fair
	+ Somewhat fair
	+ Unfair
* **Manageability of Workload (8)**: Rate the workload relative to the number of credits. Choose from:
	+ Very manageable
	+ Manageable
	+ Challenging
	+ Overwhelming
* **Availability of Learning Resources (9)**: Rate the accessibility of learning resources, such as readings and materials. Choose from:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Suggestions for Improvement (10)**: Provide specific suggestions for improving the course or instructor.
* **Additional Comments (11)**: Share any further feedback or comments about the course.

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough in your feedback to help instructors and administrators improve the course.
- Select the option that best fits your experience with the course.
- If you have already completed a similar form, try to provide new and additional insights rather than duplicating your previous feedback.
