# School Model Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about educational models. It helps to gather details about the respondents' backgrounds and preferences related to learning and educational settings.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the text fields with your actual school model information.
2. In the "learning_method" field, choose one method that best describes how you learned or model your learning style.
3. You may add a note if you want to provide additional context.
4. In the "email_address" and "phone_number" fields, provide your actual email address and phone number, respectively.
5. Fill in your date of birth in the "date" field in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
6. In the "time" field, select your actual time of day.
7. In the "school_model_survey_category" field, choose a category that best describes your school model.
8. In the "school_model_survey_subcategory" field, choose multiple subcategories that best describe your school model. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **school_model** (`school_model`, `text`, required: false): This field is for entering information about your school model.
* **parent_guadian** (`parent_guadian`, `text`, required: false): This is for entering a contact person's name.
* **learning_method** (`learning_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one method that describes your learning method.
* **note** (`note`, `note`, required: false): Add additional context or information you want to share.
* **email_address** (`email_address`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **phone_number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Select your time of day.
* **school_model_survey_category** (`school_model_survey_category`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose a category that best describes your school model.
* **school_model_survey_subcategory** (`school_model_survey_subcategory`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select multiple subcategories that describe your school model.

Note: If any field is marked as required, please fill in that field as it's essential for processing the information.
