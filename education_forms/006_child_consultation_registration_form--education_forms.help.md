# Child Consultation Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for caregivers to provide information about their child's needs and circumstances to support an educational consultation. Please ensure that you provide accurate and complete information to help the consultants understand the child's situation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by providing the child's full name as registered in school records.
2. Enter the child's date of birth and current school grade.
3. Identify the primary areas of concern for the child, selecting all that apply.
4. If applicable, provide a brief history of when you first noticed these concerns.
5. Confirm whether the child has received any prior services (e.g., tutoring, counseling).
6. Provide your contact information (phone number and email address) for follow-up consultations.
7. Choose a relationship to the child (Parent, Legal Guardian, Grandparent, or Other).
8. Select a preferred consultation date, subject to availability.
9. Provide any other information that may be helpful for the consultation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Child Full Name** (`child_full_name`, text, required): Enter the child's full name as registered in school records.
* **Date of Birth** (`child_dob`, date, required): Select the child's date of birth.
* **Current School and Grade** (`current_school_grade`, text, required): Enter the child's current school and grade level (e.g., Sunset Elementary - 2nd Grade).
* **Primary Caregiver Name** (`caregiver_name`, text, required): Enter your full legal name.
* **Relationship to Child** (`relationship_to_child`, select_one, required): Select one of the options provided (Parent, Legal Guardian, Grandparent, or Other).
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Provide your primary mobile or home phone number for follow-up consultations.
* **Contact Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication and follow-up consultations.
* **Preferred Consultation Date** (`preferred_date`, date, required): Select a preferred consultation date, subject to availability.
* **Primary Areas of Concern** (`areas_of_concern`, select_multiple, required): Select all areas of concern that apply to the child (Academic Progress, Emotional Well-being, Social Interactions, or Speech and Language).
* **Brief History of Concerns** (`concern_history`, text, optional): Provide a brief history of when you first noticed these concerns.
* **Has the Child Received Prior Services?** (`prior_services`, select_one, required): Confirm whether the child has received any prior services (e.g., tutoring, counseling).
* **Areas of Concern History** (`brief_history_of_concerns`, text, optional): This is an optional field to provide more context on the concerns.
* **Prior Services History** (`prior_services_history`, text, optional): This is an optional field to provide more context on prior services.
* **Other Information** (`other`, text, optional): You can use this field to provide any additional information that may be helpful for the consultation.

## Tips
