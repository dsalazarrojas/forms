# Learning Equity Accelerator Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for individuals and organizations to register their interest in learning from our services and provide their contact information for communication.

## How To Complete This Form
- Click on the "Fill out this form" button to begin.
- Fill out each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
- Use the provided drop-down menus to select your state, country, and communication preferences.
- Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your agreement to our terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name? (contact_name)**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **What is your organization name? (organization_name)**: Enter the name of the organization you are registering for.
* **What is your role in the organization? (role_in_organization)**: Describe your job title or position within the organization.
* **What is your address? (contact_address)**: Enter your complete address, including street, apartment number, and postal code.
* **What is your state? (contact_state)**: Select your state from the provided drop-down menu.
* **What is your country? (contact_country)**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you are from the United States or not.
* **What type of information do you want to be contacted with (email, phone, mail)? (contact_preferences)**: Select one or more communication methods to reach you.
* **What are your comments? (contact_comments)**: Enter any additional comments or questions you may have.
* **What is your city? (contact_city)**: Enter the city where you live or work.
* **What is your zip code? (contact_zip)**: Enter your zip code for postal purposes.
* **What is your title in the form? (contact_title)**: Enter a title for yourself, e.g., Mr., Mrs., Dr., etc.
* **Have you read and agreed to the terms and conditions? (contact_terms_conditions)**: Select "Yes" to confirm you have read and agreed to our terms and conditions.
* **What are your further comments? (contact_further_comments)**: Enter any additional comments or questions you may have.
* **Do you have any comments? (contact_privacy)**: Enter any comments or requests regarding your contact information.
* **What is your agreement status with the terms and conditions? (contact_agreed_terms)**: Select "Agreed" to confirm your agreement to our terms and conditions, or "Not Agreed" if you disagree.
