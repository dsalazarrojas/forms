# A2P 10DLC Business Registration Support Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for businesses to request support with their A2P 10DLC business registration. It helps the support team understand your business's specific needs and provides the necessary information to assist with your registration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Select all applicable message types for your business.
3. Describe any compliance or regulatory concerns you may have.
4. Choose your desired response speed for support requests.
5. If necessary, specify any platform access or configuration requirements.
6. Add any additional information or reference numbers for the support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business legal name** (`Business legal name`, Text, *): Enter your business's official name as it is registered. This should match the name on your official documents.
* **EIN / Tax ID** (`EIN / Tax ID`, Text, *): Enter your business's EIN (Employer Identification Number) or Tax ID. This is a unique numeric identifier.
* **Primary contact person** (`Primary contact person`, Text, *): Provide the name of the person handling your registration and support requests.
* **Contact email** (`Contact email`, Email, *): Enter your business's contact email address. This is used for registration updates and support requests.
* **Contact phone** (`Contact phone`, Phone, ): Enter your business's contact phone number. This can be a mobile or office number.
* **Company website or domain** (`Company website or domain`, Text, ): If you have a website, include the URL (e.g., https://example.com).
* **Messaging use case** (`Messaging use case`, Select One, *): Choose the primary use case for A2P traffic for your business.
	+ Marketing promotions
	+ Transactional alerts
	+ Two-factor authentication (OTP)
	+ Customer care / support
	+ Appointment reminders
* **Estimated monthly message volume** (`Estimated monthly message volume`, Integer, *): Approximate the number of messages your business sends per month.
* **Message types** (`Message types`, Select Multiple, ): Choose all applicable message types for your business.
	+ SMS
	+ MMS
	+ Short codes
	+ Toll-free messaging
	+ Rich messaging (RCS)
* **Known compliance or regulatory concerns** (`Known compliance or regulatory concerns`, Text, ): Describe any opt-in/opt-out or consent issues you have.
* **Support priority** (`Support priority`, Select One, ): Select your desired response speed for support requests.
	+ Standard (3-5 business days)
	+ Expedited (1-2 business days)
	+ Urgent (same day)
* **Platform access or configuration required** (`Platform access or configuration required`, Select Multiple, ): If necessary, specify any platform access or configuration requirements.
	+ API integration
	+ Sender registration
	+ Compliance documentation
	+ Monitoring & alerts
	+ Message routing
* **Additional information for support team** (`Additional information for support team`, Text, ): Include any attachment references or internal ticket IDs that may be relevant for the support team.
