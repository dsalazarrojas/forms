# COVID 19 On Site Health Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID 19 On Site Health Screening Form is designed to help assess the health and safety of individuals attending on-site programs or courses. This form helps us understand each participant's COVID-19-related health status, exposure, and vaccination history to ensure a safe and healthy environment for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal information:
	* Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
	* Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
	* Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field (MM-DD-YYYY format).
2. Confirm your contact information:
	* Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
	* Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
3. Provide program details:
	* Enter the name of the program or course you're attending in the "Program or Course Name" field.
	* Enter the start date of the program in the "Program Start Date" field.
	* Enter the location where the program is being held in the "Program Location" field.
4. Answer health screening questions:
	* Indicate whether you have a fever or chills in the "Do You Currently Have a Fever or Chills" field.
	* Confirm if you have a new or persistent cough in the "Do You Have a Cough" field.
	* Answer if you have shortness of breath or difficulty breathing in the "Do You Have Shortness of Breath" field.
	* Report whether you have experienced loss of taste or smell in the "Loss of Taste or Smell" field.
	* Describe any other COVID-19 symptoms you're experiencing in the "Other COVID-19 Symptoms" field.
5. Report your COVID-19 exposure:
	* Indicate if you've had close contact with someone who tested positive for COVID-19 in the "Close Contact with COVID-19 Positive Person" field.
	* Confirm if you've tested positive for COVID-19 in the "Tested Positive for COVID-19" field.
	* If you've tested positive, enter the date of the positive test in the "Date of Positive Test" field.
6. Confirm your vaccination status:
	* Select your COVID-19 vaccination status in the "COVID-19 Vaccination Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it is commonly known.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it is commonly known.
* **Date of Birth** (date, required): Enter your date of birth in the MM-DD-YYYY format (e.g., 01-01-1990).
* **Email Address** (email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (phone, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Program or Course Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the program or course you're attending.
* **Program Start Date** (date, required): Enter the start date of the program or course.
* **Program Location** (text, required): Enter the location where the program is being held.
* **Do You Currently Have a Fever or Chills** (select_one, required):
	+ Select "False" if you do not have a fever or chills.
	+ Select "True" if you currently have a fever or chills.
	+ Select "Unsure" if you're unsure or have not experienced symptoms recently.
* **Do You Have a Cough** (select_one, required):
	+ Select "False" if you do not have a cough.
	+ Select "True" if you have a new or persistent cough.
	+ Select "Unsure" if you're unsure or have not experienced symptoms recently.
* **Do You Have Shortness of Breath** (select_one, required):
	+ Select "False" if you do not have shortness of breath.
	+ Select "True" if you have difficulty breathing.
	+ Select "Unsure" if you're unsure or have not experienced symptoms recently.
* **Loss of Taste or Smell** (select_one, required):
	+ Select "False" if you do not have lost your taste or smell.
	+ Select "True" if you have experienced loss of taste or smell.
	+ Select "Unsure" if you're unsure or have not experienced symptoms recently.
* **Other COVID-19 Symptoms** (select_one, required):
	+ Select "No other symptoms" if you do not have any other symptoms.
	+ Select "Yes, have other symptoms" if you have experienced other COVID-19 symptoms.
	+ Select "Unsure" if you're unsure or have not experienced symptoms recently.
* **Close Contact with COVID-19 Positive Person** (select_one, required):
	+ Select "No close contact" if you have not had close contact with someone who tested positive for COVID-19.
	+ Select "Yes, close contact" if you have had close contact with someone who tested positive for COVID-19.
	+ Select "Unsure" if you're unsure or have not had close contact.
* **Tested Positive for COVID-19** (select_one, required):
	+ Select "No positive test" if you have not tested positive for COVID-19.
	+ Select "Yes, tested positive" if you tested positive for COVID-19.
	+ Select "Unsure" if you're unsure or have not been tested.
* **Date of Positive Test** (date, optional): If you tested positive, enter the date of the positive test.
* **COVID-19 Vaccination Status** (select_one, required):
	+ Select "Fully vaccinated and boosted" if you are fully vaccinated and boosted.
	+ Select "Fully vaccinated, not boosted" if you are fully vaccinated but not boosted.
	+ Select "Partially vaccinated" if you are partially vaccinated.
	+ Select "Not vaccinated" if you are not vaccinated.
	+ Select "Prefer not to disclose" if you do not want to disclose your vaccination status.
* **Are You Approved for In-Person Attendance** (select_one, required):
	+ Select "Yes, approved to attend" if you are approved to attend in-person.
	+ Select "No, not approved to attend" if you are not approved to attend in-person.
	+ Select "Conditional approval pending review" if your approval is pending review.
* **Date of Screening** (date, required): Enter the date of this screening was completed.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly to ensure your safety and the safety of others.
* If you select "Unsure" for any question, it will be reviewed further.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your program administrator.
* This form is subject to change or revision as necessary.
