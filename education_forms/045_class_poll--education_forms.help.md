# Class Poll - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather opinions from students on various aspects of their class experience. Your responses will help instructors understand your preferences and adjust their teaching methods accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling in your name and class name.
2. Choose the poll topic you're voting on (e.g., review session, assignment extension, etc.).
3. Select your preference for each question.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to include them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your name to help us get to know you better.
* **Class Name** (`class_name`, text, required): Indicate the class for which you're taking this poll.
* **Poll Topic** (`poll_topic`, select_one, required): Choose the topic you're voting on.
* **Option 1 - Review Session** (`option_1`, select_one, required): Select if you prefer a review session before an exam.
	+ **Option 1 - Review Session Day** (`option_2`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred day for a review session.
	+ **Option 1 - Review Session Time** (`option_3`, select_one, required): Select your preferred time for a review session.
* **Option 2 - Study Format** (`option_4`, select_one, required): Indicate how you'd like to study.
* **Option 3 - Assignment Extension** (`option_5`, select_one, required): Select if you'd benefit from an assignment extension.
* **Option 4 - Group Work Preference** (`option_6`, select_one, required): Choose your group work preference for the next project.
* **Option 5 - Class Participation** (`option_7`, select_one, required): Indicate how comfortable you are with class participation.
* **Option 6 - Feedback Method** (`option_8`, select_one, required): Select your preferred method for receiving feedback.
* **Will You Attend the Next Class** (`option_9`, select_one, required): Confirm your attendance for the next class.
* **Reason for Absence** (`absence_reason`, text, optional): If you won't be attending the next class, provide a reason.
* **Understanding Level** (`understanding_level`, select_one, required): Self-assess your understanding of current material.
* **Do You Need Additional Help** (`help_needed`, select_one, required): Indicate if you need additional help with current material.
* **What Type of Help Would You Prefer** (`preferred_help`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of help you'd like if you need it.
* **Additional Comments or Suggestions** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Share any extra thoughts or suggestions you have.

## Tips

* Answer honestly and think about what would work best for you and your fellow students.
* Feel free to provide as much detail as possible, especially in the "Additional Comments or Suggestions" field.
* If you're unsure about any option, choose the one that best represents your preference.

This form is designed to be user-friendly and help instructors get a better understanding of your needs and preferences. Your input is invaluable, and we appreciate your participation!
