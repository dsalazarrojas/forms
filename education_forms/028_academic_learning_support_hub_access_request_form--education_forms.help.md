# Academic Learning Support Hub Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for students and staff to request access to the Academic Learning Support Hub, a platform that provides various resources and services to improve academic performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling out the basic information section with your name and email address.
2.  Then, select your department or program affiliation (if applicable).
3.  Clearly state the reason for accessing the support hub.
4.  Choose the support services you wish to access.
5.  Indicate your urgency level for accessing the support services.
6.  If needed, specify any accessibility needs.
7.  Select your preferred method of contact.
8.  Finally, provide any additional information that may be helpful for staff to better understand your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester name**: Enter your full name as a student or staff member.
* **Email address**: Enter your valid email address where staff can contact you for access confirmation.
* **Affiliation or program**: If you're affiliated with a department or study program, select it from the list. This is optional.
* **Reason for access**: Clearly state the purpose for accessing the support hub, such as seeking tutoring or study skills workshops.
* **Preferred support services**: Select the services you wish to access, such as tutoring, study skills workshops, or peer mentoring.
* **Urgency level**: Indicate how soon you need support, whether immediate, within a week, within a month, or not urgent.
* **Accessibility needs**: If you require any accommodations, describe them here.
* **Preferred contact method**: Choose how you'd like to be contacted by staff, either via email, phone, or text.
* **Additional information**: Provide any other relevant context that may help staff understand your needs.

## Tips
* Be accurate and clear when filling out the form to ensure timely processing of your request.
* If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to us at form@gic.mx.
