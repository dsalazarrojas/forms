# Creative Educators Network Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Creative Educators Network Registration Form" is a tool designed to help creative educators connect with each other, access resources, and find opportunities for collaborations, mentorship, and networking. This form helps us understand your education background, teaching experience, and preferences to provide you with tailored support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact details, including your name, email, and phone number, in the "Enter your contact details" field.
2. Select your education background (Bachelor's, Master's, or Ph.D.) in the "Education background" field.
3. Choose the subjects you teach (Math, Science, or Art) in the "Teaching subjects" field. You can select multiple subjects.
4. Enter your teaching experience in the "Teaching experience" field.
5. Select the resources you would like to access (Articles, Videos, or Lesson plans) in the "Resources you'd like to access" field. You can select multiple resources.
6. Choose how you would like to connect with others (Collaborations, Mentorship, or Networking) in the "How would you like to connect with others?" field. You can select multiple options.
7. Select any special interest groups you'd like to join (Math, Art, or Science) in the "Would you like to join any special interest groups?" field. You can select multiple groups.
8. Provide any feedback or suggestions you may have in the "Any feedback or suggestions?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enter your contact details (Required)**: This field is for you to enter your name, email, and phone number so we can get in touch with you.
* **Education background (Required)**: Please select your level of education (Bachelor's, Master's, or Ph.D.) to help us understand your qualifications.
* **Teaching subjects (Required)**: Choose the subjects you teach (Math, Science, or Art) to help us tailor our support to your needs.
* **Teaching experience (Required)**: Enter your teaching experience to help us understand your level of expertise.
* **Resources you'd like to access (Required)**: Select the resources you would like to access (Articles, Videos, or Lesson plans) to help us provide you with relevant materials.
* **How would you like to connect with others (Required)**: Choose how you'd like to connect with others (Collaborations, Mentorship, or Networking) to help us facilitate meaningful connections.
* **Would you like to join any special interest groups (Required)**: Select any special interest groups you'd like to join (Math, Art, or Science) to help us tailor our support to your interests.
* **How would you like to be contacted (Required)**: Please select how you'd like to be contacted (Email, Phone, or Social media) so we can reach out to you effectively.
* **Any feedback or suggestions (Optional)**: Provide any feedback or suggestions you may have to help us improve our services.

## Tips

* Make sure to select all relevant fields and provide accurate information to help us serve you better.
* You can select multiple options in fields that allow it.
* If you have any questions or need help, please don't hesitate to contact us.
