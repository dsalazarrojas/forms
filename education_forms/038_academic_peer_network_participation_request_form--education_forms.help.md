# Academic Peer Network Participation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Peer Network Participation Request Form is designed to gather essential details from individuals interested in joining the Academic Peer Network. This helps organizers understand participants' preferences, availability, and experiences, making the integration process smoother and more efficient.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, start by providing your Participant Details.
- Fill in your Full name and Email address. These are mandatory fields.
- If you have an affiliation with an institution, you can specify it in the Institution field, though this is optional.
- Choose your Area of interest by selecting one or multiple topics you are interested in engaging with.
- Select your Preferred activity type, which determines how you wish to participate in the network.
- Provide information about your Availability for participation, describing times or days you are typically available.
- If applicable, describe your Prior network experience, sharing details about previous related participation.
- Indicate your preferred method of Communication, choosing from Email, Newsletter, or Slack or messaging.
- Finally, add any Additional comments you may have for the organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details**: This section is an introduction to the form.
* **Full name**: Please enter your full name to ensure accurate identification within the network.
* **Email address**: Provide a contact email address for organizers and network activities. This will be used for updates and notifications.
* **Institution**: This is where you specify your affiliated organization, if any.
* **Area of interest**: Select one or multiple topics related to the Academic Peer Network, such as Research collaboration, Peer mentoring, Teaching practice, or Policy engagement.
* **Preferred activity type**: Choose how you prefer to participate in the network: Attend events, Host sessions, Contribute resources, or Volunteer.
* **Availability for participation**: Describe your usual availability, including days and times you can participate in activities.
* **Prior network experience**: If you have prior experience in similar networks or activities, please describe them for organizers.
* **Communication preference**: Indicate your preferred method of communication for updates and notifications. You can choose Email, Newsletter, or Slack or messaging.
* **Additional comments**: Use this field to provide any other relevant information you think the organizers should know.

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when filling in the "Availability for participation" and "Prior network experience" fields.
- Ensure the "Communication preference" aligns with your actual ability to receive email, access to newsletters, or be on a shared messaging platform.
- Keep "Additional comments" for any other relevant information that does not fit into other fields.
- Fill in the form accurately and honestly, as this will aid in a smoother integration process for everyone involved in the network.
