# NGO Research Fellowship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The NGO Research Fellowship Application Form is designed to collect information from applicants to support their research projects. This guide will help you understand each section of the form and ensure you provide accurate and complete information for your application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details in the "Applicant Details" section.
2. Write a clear and concise proposal title in the "Proposal Title" section.
3. Provide a detailed description of your research proposal in the "Proposal Description" section.
4. Share any relevant experience and information about your research in the "Research Experience" section.
5. Specify the goals of your research project in the "Research Goals" section.
6. Choose the relevant NGO connection for your research project.
7. Affirm your connection with the selected NGO.
8. Upload your CV and proposal document if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Details** (`applicant_details`, text, required: false)
	+ This section is for providing your personal details, such as your name, contact information, and other relevant personal information.
* **Proposal Title** (`proposal_title`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter a clear and concise title of your research proposal.
* **Proposal Description** (`proposal_description`, text, required: false)
	+ Write a detailed description of your research proposal, including the background, objectives, and expected outcomes.
* **Proposal Details** (`proposal_details`, text, required: false)
	+ Provide additional information about your research proposal, including any relevant documentation or supporting materials.
* **Curriculum Vitae (CV)** (`cv`, text, required: false)
	+ Upload a URL to your CV or provide a brief description of your relevant experience and education.
* **Research Program Proposal** (`research_program_proposal`, text, required: false)
	+ Write a brief overview of your research program, including its objectives and expected outcomes.
* **Research Experience** (`research_experience`, text, required: false)
	+ Share your experience and qualifications in conducting research.
* **Research Goals** (`research_goals`, text, required: false)
	+ Specify the goals of your research project.
* **Nonprofit Connection** (`nonprofit_connection`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Select the NGOs with which you have a connection. This can be an important factor in evaluating your proposal.
* **Nonprofit Affiliated** (`nonprofit_affiliated`, select_one, required: false)
	+ Choose how you are affiliated with the selected NGOs.
* **CV Upload URL** (`cv_upload_url`, text, required: false)
	+ Upload a URL to your CV or provide a brief description of your relevant experience and education.
* **Proposal Upload URL** (`proposal_upload_url`, text, required: false)
	+ Upload a URL to your research proposal or provide a brief description of your research proposal.
* **Research Fellowship Terms** (`research_fellowship_terms`, text, required: false)
	+ Review and agree to the terms of the research fellowship. 

Tips:

* Please provide clear and concise answers to each section.
* Ensure that your answers are well-structured and easy to read.
* Proofread your form before submitting it for any errors or inaccuracies.
