# Staff Development Day Sign In - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to capture attendance and feedback from staff members who attend the Staff Development Day sessions. This information is used by HR or training department to monitor attendance and gather feedback for future improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your session ID (page 1) if you have one.
2. Provide your personal information (page 2) such as your name and any other relevant details.
3. Choose the start time of the session you attended (page 3).
4. Select the session you attended (page 4).
5. Add any additional notes or comments about the session (page 5).
6. Fill in the session topic and description if you have the information (pages 7 and 8).
7. Enter the session ID of the second session (page 9).
8. Enter the date you submitted this form (page 10).
9. Choose the start time of the session you submitted (page 11).
10. Enter the time you submitted this form (page 12).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **session_id**: Enter your session ID if you have one. This is a unique identifier for the session you attended.
* **personal_info**: Provide your name, job title, or any other relevant details to help us know who you are.
* **start_time**: Choose the start time of the session you attended. Please select the correct time from the provided options.
* **attended_session**: Select the session you attended from the options provided. This will help us track which sessions were popular.
* **notes**: Add any additional comments or feedback about the session you attended. This is a free-text field for any thoughts or suggestions you have.
* **session_topic**: Enter the topic of the session you attended if you can remember it.
* **session_description**: Provide a brief description of the session you attended if you can remember it.
* **session_id_2**: Enter the session ID of the second session you attended.
* **submitted_date**: Enter the date you submitted this form.
* **session_start_time**: Choose the start time of the session you submitted.
* **submitted_time**: Enter the time you submitted this form.
* **session_start_time**: This field seems to be a redundant field and not used in this form.

Note: Please ensure to submit this form only once per session attended.
