# Education Support Leave Time Off Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Education Support Leave Time Off Form.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Begin by filling out the form completely. All fields are required, so ensure you have all necessary information before submitting.
2. Select the relevant reason for your time off from the "Time Off Reason" field. This will help us better understand your situation.
3. Enter your preferred dates for your time off in the "Time Off Dates" field. Ensure these dates are accurate and up-to-date.
4. Indicate the duration of your time off in the "Time Off Duration" field.
5. Enter the number of days you will be taking off in the "Number of Days" field.
6. If you have supporting faculty, select "Yes" from the "Supporting Faculty" field. This will help us identify if you need additional support during your time off.
7. Enter the name and email of the faculty member supporting you in the "Supporting Faculty Name", "Supporting Faculty Email" fields respectively.
8. If the faculty member is willing to be contacted, select "Yes" from the "Supporting Faculty" field. This will help us coordinate with them for any necessary arrangements.
9. Select "Yes" from the "Supporting Faculty Location" field if you will be taking your leave from a specific location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Education Support Leave Time Off Form Page1**: This is the form title and a placeholder page for the form.
* **Time Off Reason** (`label`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose the reason for your time off from the provided options.
* **Time Off Dates** (`label`, `date`, **required**): Enter the start and end dates of your time off. Please use the format mm/dd/yyyy for the dates.
* **Time Off Duration** (`label`, `number`, **required**): Enter the number of days you will be taking off.
* **Number of Days** (`label`, `number`, **required**): This field is used to track the number of days for the leave.
* **Supporting Faculty** (`label`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Select "Yes" if you have a supporting faculty member who will be assisting you during your time off.
* **Supporting Faculty Comments** (`label`, `text`, **optional**): Enter any comments related to your supporting faculty member.
* **Supporting Faculty Name** (`label`, `select_one`, **required**): Select "Yes" to indicate if you have a supporting faculty member.
* **Supporting Faculty Title** (`label`, `text`, **required**): Enter the title or position of your supporting faculty member.
* **Supporting Faculty Email** (`label`, `email`, **required**): Enter the email address of your supporting faculty member.
* **Supporting Faculty Phone** (`label`, `text`, **required**): Enter the phone number of your supporting faculty member.
* **Supporting Faculty Location** (`label`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Select "Yes" if you will be taking your leave from a specific location.
* **[Various additional pages]**: These pages are placeholders and have no specific meaning or requirement.

## Tips
Please ensure that you complete all fields before submitting the form. If you have any doubts or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
