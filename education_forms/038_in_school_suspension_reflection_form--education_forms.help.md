# In School Suspension Reflection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The In School Suspension Reflection Form is designed to gather information from students regarding their experiences and actions taken during a suspension. The form helps to document incidents and reflections, which can aid in the student's growth and support. It is typically filled out by students who have been suspended from school.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your Student ID number in the "Student ID" field.
2.  Enter your name in the "Student Name" field.
3.  Enter your teacher's name in the "Teacher Name" field.
4.  Describe the reason for your suspension in the "Reason for Suspension" field.
5.  Provide a reflection of your actions and thoughts in the "Reflection" field.
6.  List any actions taken to address the issue in the "Actions Taken" field.
7.  If applicable, select which design elements you would like to see on the form in the "Minimalist Design" field.
8.  Finally, if desired, sign your name in the "Student Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student ID (1)** (`number`, `required`): Enter your unique Student ID number.
* **Student Name (2)** (`text`, `required`): Enter your name as it appears on your school records.
* **Teacher Name (3)** (`text`, `required`): Enter the name of the teacher who is handling your suspension.
* **Reason for Suspension (4)** (`text`, `required`): Describe the reason for your suspension.
* **Reflection (5)** (`note`, `required`): Write a reflection on what happened, what you learned, and how you plan to improve in the future.
* **Actions Taken (6)** (`text`, `required`): List any actions you took to address the issue or improve your behavior.
* **Student Signature (7)** (`text`, `required`): If desired, sign your name to confirm that you have filled out this form.
* **Minimalist Design (8)** (`select_multiple`, `optional`): Choose from the options of Student ID, Teacher Name, Reason for Suspension, or Reflection to customize the design of your form.
* **Student ID 2 (9)** (`number`, `optional`): This field is redundant and not required. You can ignore it.
