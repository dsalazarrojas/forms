# Education Technology Product Demo Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Education Technology Product Demo Consent Form" is used to collect information from participants and school administrators about the demo details, participant information, and demo preferences. This form is a crucial tool for facilitating effective product demos and ensuring that both the school administrators and Edtech providers are informed and aligned with the demo's requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
*   Review each page carefully and enter your information according to the guidelines.
*   If you have questions or need clarification on any field, please do not hesitate to contact your assigned user or school administrator.
*   Once you have completed all the necessary information, please review your submission carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Details (Page 1)**
    *   **Participant Name (Participant Name)** (`Participant Name`, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID or driver's license.
*   **Participant Email (Participant Email)** (`Participant Email`, email, required): Enter your email address that can be used to communicate with you.
*   **Participant Phone (Participant Phone)** (`Participant Phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number that can be used for communication.
*   **Participant School (Participant School)** (`Participant School`, text, required): Select your school from the list. If not found in the list, please enter your school name in "Other School (if applicable)" field.
*   **Other School (if applicable) (Participant School Other)** (`Participant School Other`, text, required): Enter your school name if not found in the list.
*   **Product Demo Details (Page 2)**
    *   **Product Name (Product Name)** (`Product Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the product you are interested in or are currently using.
    *   **Product Version (Product Version)** (`Product Version`, text, required): Enter the version of the product you are using or interested in.
*   **Demo Details (Page 3)**
    *   **Demo Date (Demo Date)** (`Demo Date`, date, required): Select the date for the demo.
    *   **Demo Time (Demo Time)** (`Demo Time`, time, required): Select the time for the demo.
    *   **Demo Location (Demo Location)** (`Demo Location`, text, required): Enter the location of the demo.
*   **Participant Info (Page 4)**
    *   **Form Approved (Form Approved)** (`Form Approved`, select_one, required): Select 'Approved' to indicate that you have reviewed and understand the demo details.
*   **Contact Information (Page 5)**
    *   **School Administrator Name (School Administrator Name)** (`School Administrator Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the school administrator who will be attending the demo.
    *   **School Administrator Email (School Administrator Email)** (`School Administrator Email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the school administrator.
    *   **School Administrator Phone (School Administrator Phone)** (`School Administrator Phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the school administrator.
    *   **School Administrator Notes (School Administrator Notes)** (`School Administrator Notes`, text, required): Enter any additional notes about the school administrator.
*   **Edtech Provider Details (Page 6)**
    *   **Edtech Provider (Edtech Provider)** (`Edtech Provider`, select_multiple, required): Select 'Yes' if you are an Edtech provider.
    *   **Other Edtech Provider (if applicable) (Edtech Provider Other)** (`Edtech Provider Other`, select_multiple, required): Select 'Yes' if you are an Edtech provider. If not applicable, select 'No'.
    *   **Edtech Provider Notes (Edtech Provider Notes)** (`Edtech Provider Notes`, text, required): Enter any notes about the Edtech provider.
*   **Form Approval (Page 7)**
    *   **Assigned User (Assigned User)** (`Assigned User`, text, required): Enter the name of the user assigned to you.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all the required fields are filled out carefully.
*   Review your submission before submitting to ensure accuracy.
*   If you need any assistance, do not hesitate to contact your assigned user or school administrator.
