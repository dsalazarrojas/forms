# Student Progress Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for educators to report on the progress of students. Please fill out this form to provide updates on a student's current state, include any comments or issues that need to be addressed, and specify next steps for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Student Progress Form**: Provide a brief description of the student's current progress, achievements, or concerns.
2. **Student Name**: Enter the name of the student you are reporting on.
3. **Email**: Enter the student's email address (if available).
4. **Educator Name**: Enter your name and title/role (e.g., teacher, instructor, advisor).
5. **Student Progress**: Describe the student's progress, including any notable achievements or concerns.
6. **Comments**: Add any comments or notes about the student's progress.
7. **Next Steps**: Specify the next steps the student should take to continue improving.
8. **Student Progress Rating**: Optional - provide a numerical rating of the student's progress (e.g., 1-5).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Progress Form**: A brief description of the student's current state.
* **Student Name**: Enter the student's name to identify who this report is about.
* **Email**: Enter the student's email address to facilitate direct communication.
* **Educator Name**: Your name and title/role to provide context for the report.
* **Student Progress**: Describe the student's current progress, focusing on achievements or areas of improvement.
* **Comments**: Add any relevant comments or notes about the student's progress.
* **Next Steps**: Specify the next steps the student should take to progress further.
* **Student Progress Rating**: Optional - provide a numerical rating of the student's progress (if applicable).
