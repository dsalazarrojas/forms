# Application For License As A Journeyman Electrician - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from applicants for a journeyman electrician license. It helps us evaluate the applicant's qualifications and experience to determine eligibility for licensure.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
- Make sure to enter accurate and legible information.
- If the applicant is a former apprentice, provide the supervising electrician's name and contact information.
- If applicable, upload any supporting qualification documents.
- Confirm payment of the application fee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant name (required)**: Full legal name of the applicant.
* **Date of birth (required)**: Date of birth of the applicant, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Apprenticeship completion date (required)**: Date when the apprenticeship was completed.
* **Journeyman exam date (optional)**: Date of examination if applicable.
* **Qualification documents filename (optional)**: Indicate the uploaded certificates.
* **Years of practical experience (required)**: Whole number of years of the applicant's experience.
* **Supervising electrician (optional)**: Name and contact information of the supervisor during the apprenticeship.
* **Contact phone (required)**: Best phone number to reach the applicant.
* **Application fee paid (required)**: Confirmation of payment of the application fee.
* **Additional notes (optional)**: Any other relevant details about the application.

## Tips

- Ensure you provide accurate and legible information for efficient processing.
- Make sure to attach all necessary documents.
- If the applicant is a former apprentice, ensure the supervising electrician is informed about this application.
- Confirm payment of the application fee before submitting the form.
