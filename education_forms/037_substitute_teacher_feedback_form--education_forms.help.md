# Substitute Teacher Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from substitute teachers about their experiences in the class. It's used to gather information about their likes, learning, and any feedback for the regular teacher.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
* Fill in the required sections as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* You may add any additional thoughts or comments in the provided text fields.
* You may skip questions that are not applicable to your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What did you like about the class**: This field is for you to share what you enjoyed or appreciated about the class. This could be the lesson material, the class atmosphere, or any other positive aspect of the class.
* **What did you learn today**: This field is for you to share any new insights, concepts, or ideas you gained from the class. You can write as much or as little as you want, but try to be as specific as possible.
* **Teacher feedback**: This field is for any feedback or comments you may have for the regular teacher. This could be constructive criticism, suggestions for improvement, or simply a thank you note.
* **Classroom environment**: This field is for you to share your impressions of the classroom environment, such as the condition of the classroom, the availability of resources, or any other relevant information.
