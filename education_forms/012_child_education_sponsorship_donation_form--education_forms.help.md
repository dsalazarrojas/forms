# Child Education Sponsorship Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Child Education Sponsorship Donation Form is used to collect information from donors who wish to support educational initiatives for children. The form helps our organization to understand the donor's preferences and process their donation efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out your personal information**:
   - Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
   - Enter your email address for receipt and acknowledgement purposes.
   - If you wish, add your phone number as an optional contact point.
   - Choose your preferred donation frequency from the options below.

2. **Choose your donation details**:
   - Select the sponsorship type that best suits your giving preference.
   - Choose a suggested donation amount or enter a custom amount.
   - Indicate where you would like your funds to go towards, choosing from the options provided.

3. **Select your payment method**:
   - Indicate your preferred payment method from the available options.
   - Ensure that secure processing is initiated after your selection.

4. **Consider anonymity**:
   - If you wish to keep your donation private, select "Yes" for anonymous donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Full Name**: Enter your personal or corporate name as it appears on your identification.
* **Donor Email Address**: This is required for receipt and acknowledgement purposes.
* **Donor Phone Number**: This is an optional field for additional contact information.
* **Donation Type**: Choose from one-time, monthly, or annual donation options.
* **Suggested Donation Amount**: Select a pre-defined amount or choose custom to enter a different value.
* **Custom Amount**: If you select custom amount, enter a numeric value only.
* **Primary Initiative to Support**: Indicate where you want your donation to be directed (e.g., student scholarships, school supplies, teacher training, or facility improvements).
* **Preferred Payment Method**: Select a method for secure processing (e.g., credit or debit card, PayPal, or bank transfer).
* **Anonymous Donation?**: Indicate if you prefer for your donation to remain anonymous.
