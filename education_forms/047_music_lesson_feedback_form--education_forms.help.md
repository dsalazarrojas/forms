# Music Lesson Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Music Lesson Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback from students after a music lesson, helping instructors improve their teaching methods, identify areas of improvement, and provide better services in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question or provide information as prompted.
2. Be as concise as possible while providing clear and detailed feedback.
3. If you have additional comments, feel free to include them in the "Additional comments" field.
4. Fill in any relevant information, such as your phone number, instructor details, and student instrument.
5. Submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First name** (`music_lesson_feedback_form_page1`, `text`, optional): Please enter your first name.
- **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Please enter your last name.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address if you want to be contacted for follow-up or additional information.
- **Was the lesson engaging?** (`feedback_question_1`, `select_one`, optional): Choose if the lesson was engaging or not. Options: 
    • True
    • False
    • Unsure
- **Was the instructor knowledgeable about the subject?** (`feedback_question_2`, `select_one`, optional): Choose if the instructor was knowledgeable about the subject or not. Options: 
    • True
    • False
    • Unsure
- **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, optional): Rate your experience (1-5).
- **Did you feel supported?** (`feedback_question_3`, `select_one`, optional): Choose if you felt supported or not. Options: 
    • True
    • False
    • Unsure
- **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`, optional): If you have any additional comments or feedback, please include them here.
- **Phone number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted.
- **What instrument do you play?** (`music_instrument`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the instrument(s) you play. Options:
    • Piano
    • Guitar
    • Violin
    • Drums
    • Other
- **Instructor name** (`instructor_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your instructor's name.
- **Instructor email** (`instructor_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your instructor's email address.
- **Instructor phone number** (`instructor_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your instructor's phone number.
- **Instructor instrument** (`instructor_instrument`, `text`, optional): Enter your instructor's instrument.
- **Instructor experience** (`instructor_experience`, `number`, optional): Rate your instructor's experience (1-5).
- **Student instrument** (`student_instrument`, `text`, optional): Enter the instrument you play.
- **Student level** (`student_level`, `text`, optional): Enter your student level.
- **Student experience** (`student_experience`, `number`, optional): Rate your experience as a student (1-5).
- **Lesson type** (`lesson_type`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the type of lesson you attended. Options: 
    • Private
    • Group
    • Online
- **Lesson date** (`lesson_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the lesson.
- **Lesson time** (`lesson_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the lesson.
- **Lesson duration** (`lesson_duration`, `text`, optional): Enter the duration of the lesson.
- **Lesson notes** (`lesson_notes`, `note`, optional): If you have any notes from the lesson, please include them here.
- **Next lesson** (`next_lesson`, `text`, optional): Enter details about your next lesson.
- **Submitted** (`submitted_at`, `date`, optional): This field is automatically populated and should not be edited by users.
