# Yearbook Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students about their personal details and educational background.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have reviewed the form carefully and understand its purpose.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
3. Select the correct options for non-text fields like School Year and Day of Birth.
4. Enter your email and phone number, if applicable.
5. Leave any optional fields (Email and Phone) blank if you do not have this information.
6. Ensure that your Date of Birth is in the correct date format.
7. Enter your Time of Birth in 24-hour format (e.g., 12:00, 13:00, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name (First Name)
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Last Name (Last Name)
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Grade (Grade)
* **Grade** (`grade`, `number`, required): Select your current grade level (e.g., 1, 2, 3, etc.).

### School (School)
* **School** (`school`, `text`, required): Enter your school name.

### School Year (School Year)
* **School Year** (`school_year`, `select_one`, required): Select your current school year (e.g., First, Second, Third, etc.).

### Email (Email)
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address, if you have one.

### Phone (Phone)
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number, if you have one.

### Note (Note)
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional information or comments about yourself.

### Date of Birth (Date of Birth)
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY` (e.g., 02/15/2000).

### Day of Birth (Day)
* **Day** (`date_of_birth_day`, `select_one`, required): Select the day of your birth (1-31).

### Time of Birth (Time of Birth)
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, `time`, required): Enter your time of birth in 24-hour format (e.g., 12:00, 13:00, etc.).

## Tips

* Please ensure that your responses are accurate and complete.
* If you need help or clarification about any field, contact your school administration or support staff.
* Review your responses carefully before submitting the form.
