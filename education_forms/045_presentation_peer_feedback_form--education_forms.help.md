# Presentation Peer Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from peers on presentations, helping the presenter improve their skills and style.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the presentation and gather your thoughts on what you liked and disliked about it.
2. Select a rating (High, Medium, or Low) to indicate your overall impression of the presentation.
3. Provide a comment to offer specific, constructive feedback on the presentation.
4. Decide whether to submit or cancel your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Give Feedback** (`presentation_peer_feedback_form`, text, required): Use this field to type in your thoughts and impressions on the presentation.
* **Rate** (`rating`, select_one, required): Choose a rating (High, Medium, or Low) to indicate your overall impression of the presentation.
* **Comment** (`comment`, text, required): Provide specific, constructive feedback on the presentation.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_multiple, required): Choose "Save" to submit your feedback, or "Cancel" to discard it.
* **Email** (`email_address`, email, optional): If you have a email address, you may want to enter it so we can contact you about your feedback.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): A duplicate field, please do not fill this one out.
* **Presentation** (`presentation_id`, number, optional): Enter the ID of the presentation you are providing feedback on.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name (optional, but recommended so we can know who provided the feedback).

## Tips
* Please be specific and objective in your feedback.
* Avoid generic comments like "I didn't like it" and provide actionable suggestions for improvement.
* Use the "Rate" field to offer a general impression of the presentation, and the "Comment" field to provide detailed feedback.
