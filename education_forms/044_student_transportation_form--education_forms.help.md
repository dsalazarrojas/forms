# Student Transportation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Transportation Form is used to gather student data for efficient and effective transportation management and communication with parents or guardians.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's ID number.
2. Provide the student's name.
3. Enter the student's school ID.
4. Select the student's grade level.
5. Enter the bus number (if applicable).
6. Add the student's contact information (phone number, etc.).
7. Enter the teacher's contact information (phone number, etc.).
8. Enter the student's email address.
9. Enter the school email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, required: false): Enter the student's unique ID number.
- **Student Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Provide the student's full name.
- **Student School ID** (`student_school_id`, number, required: false): Enter the student's school ID number.
- **Student Grade Level** (`student_grade_level`, select_one, required: false): Choose the student's grade level from the dropdown options:
  * 1
  * 2
  * 3
  * 4
  * 5
  * 6
- **Bus Number** (`bus_number`, text, required: false): Enter the bus number (if applicable).
- **Student Contact** (`student_contact`, text, required: false): Add the student's contact information (phone number, etc.).
- **Teacher Contact** (`teacher_contact`, text, required: false): Enter the teacher's contact information (phone number, etc.).
- **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required: false): Enter the student's email address.
- **School Email** (`school_email`, email, required: false): Enter the school email address.
