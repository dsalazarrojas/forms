# Bar Exam Study Plan Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help law students track their progress and prepare for the bar exam.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by entering the date you last updated your study plan.
2. Fill in your name and email address, as required.
3. Enter the date of your scheduled bar exam.
4. Indicate how many weeks you have left until the exam.
5. Select your primary method of studying (e.g., bar review course, private tutor, self-study, etc.).
6. If using a bar review course, enter the name of the course.
7. Specify the number of hours you plan to study per week.
8. Choose the subjects you are focusing on.
9. Identify areas where you need the most help (weakest subjects).
10. Select where you feel most confident (strongest subjects).
11. Choose how you are organizing your study time (e.g., morning sessions, afternoons, etc.).
12. Select how often you are taking practice tests.
13. Enter your recent practice test score (if applicable).
14. Specify the score you need to pass the bar exam.
15. Choose all resources you are using for studying (e.g., textbooks, online materials, etc.).
16. Select your current level of confidence.
17. Describe the main challenges you are facing while studying.
18. If applicable, list members of your study group.
19. Indicate whether you are seeking mental health or wellness support.
20. Enter how many hours of sleep you get per night.
21. Finally, specify what you plan to focus on this week.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tracking Date** (`tracker_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you last updated this form.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`student_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Bar Exam Date** (`bar_exam_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your scheduled bar exam.
* **Weeks Until Exam** (`weeks_until_exam`, `number`, optional): Indicate how many weeks you have left until the exam.
* **Primary Study Method** (`study_method`, `select_one`, required): Select your primary way of studying (e.g., bar review course, private tutor, self-study, etc.).
* **Bar Review Course Name** (`review_course_name`, `text`, optional): If using a bar review course, enter its name.
* **Planned Weekly Study Hours** (`weekly_study_hours`, `number`, required): Enter the number of hours you plan to study per week.
* **Subjects to Focus On** (`subjects_to_study`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all subjects you are focusing on.
* **Weakest Subject Areas** (`weakest_subject`, `text`, optional): Identify areas where you need the most help.
* **Strongest Subject Areas** (`strongest_subject`, `text`, optional): Select areas where you feel most confident.
* **Study Schedule** (`study_schedule`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you are organizing your study time (e.g., morning sessions, afternoons, etc.).
* **Practice Test Frequency** (`practice_test_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select how often you are taking practice tests.
* **Recent Practice Test Score** (`recent_practice_score`, `number`, optional): Enter your recent practice test score.
* **Target Score** (`target_score`, `number`, optional): Specify the score you need to pass the exam.
* **Study Resources Using** (`study_resources`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all resources you are using for studying (e.g., textbooks, online materials, etc.).
* **Current Confidence Level** (`current_confidence`, `select_one`, required): Select your current level of confidence.
* **Main Challenges** (`main_challenges`, `text`, optional): Describe the main challenges you are facing while studying.
* **Study Group Members** (`study_group_members`, `text`, optional): If you are part of a study group, list its members.
* **Seeking Mental Health or Wellness Support** (`mental_health_support`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if you are seeking mental health or wellness support.
* **Average Sleep Hours** (`sleep_hours`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of hours of sleep you get per night.
* **Next Action Items for This Week** (`next_action_items`, `text`, optional): Describe what you plan to focus on this week.
