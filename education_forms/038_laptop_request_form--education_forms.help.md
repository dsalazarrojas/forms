# Laptop Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Laptop Request Form is used to collect information from students who are requesting a laptop for educational purposes. This form helps to identify students who are eligible for laptop allocation and to ensure that the required information is accurately captured.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please ensure that you have the necessary permission from the student to access and collect their information.
2.  Fill out the student's name in the **Student Name** field. This is the name of the student who is requesting a laptop.
3.  Enter the student's ID number in the **Student ID** field.
4.  Provide the student's email address in the **Student Email** field.
5.  Enter the student's phone number in the **Student Phone** field.
6.  Enter the name of the school the student attends in the **School Name** field.
7.  Select the student's grade level from the options in the **Student Grade** field.
8.  Select the year and month the student is requesting the laptop for in the **Laptop Request Year** and **Laptop Request Month** fields.
9.  Enter the location of the school the student attends in the **School Location** field.
10. Select the school division from the options in the **School Division** field.
11. Enter the tool assigned to the student in the **Assigned Tool** field.
12. Enter any additional information in the **Form ID** field if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student Name** (Student Name, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the student who is requesting a laptop.
*   **Student ID** (Student ID, number, required/optional): Enter the student's ID number.
*   **Student Email** (Student Email, email, required/optional): Enter the student's email address.
*   **Student Phone** (Student Phone, text, required/optional): Enter the student's phone number.
*   **School Name** (School Name, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the school the student attends.
*   **Student Grade** (Student Grade, select_one, required/optional): Select the student's grade level.
*   **Laptop Request Year** (Laptop Request Year, date, required/optional): Select the year the student is requesting the laptop for.
*   **Laptop Request Month** (Laptop Request Month, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the month the student is requesting the laptop for.
*   **School Location** (School Location, text, required/optional): Enter the location of the school the student attends.
*   **School Division** (School Division, select_one, required/optional): Select the school division.
*   **Assigned Tool** (Assigned Tool, text, required/optional): Enter the tool assigned to the student.
*   **Form ID** (Form ID, text, required/optional): Enter any additional information in this field if required.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that you have the necessary permission from the student to access and collect their information.
*   Please double-check the information entered in each field to ensure accuracy.
*   If you have any questions or issues while filling out this form, please contact the relevant authority for assistance.
