# AHP Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The AHP Questionnaire is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with our application. Your input will help us improve and refine the questionnaire to better serve your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal information: Enter your full name and a valid email address.
2. Choose your role: Select the option that best describes your position.
3. Provide decision context: Select the type of decision made using our application.
4. Rate your experience: Rate the priority criteria and your satisfaction with the application.
5. Share your overall experience: Rate your overall satisfaction and provide feedback.
6. Optional: Share any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter your legal name.
* **Email address** (`3`, `email`, required): A valid email address is required.
* **Phone number** (`4`, `phone`, optional): Include your country code and phone number (e.g., +1 555-123-4567).
* **Institution or organization** (`5`, `text`, required): Name of the university, school, or company.
* **Your role** (`6`, `select_one`, required): Select the option that best describes your position (e.g., Student, Teacher, Administrator, Researcher, or Other).
* **Course or program name** (`7`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the course or program.
* **Decision type** (`9`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of decision made using our application (e.g., Project selection, Resource allocation, Curriculum design, Staff hiring, or Other).
* **Decision date** (`10`, `date`, optional): Date when the decision was made.
* **Priority criteria** (`11`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the priority criteria that applied to your experience (e.g., Time efficiency, Cost effectiveness, User friendliness, Scalability, Customizability, and Data accuracy).
* **Time efficiency rating** (`12`, `integer`, required): Rate from 1 (worst) to 10 (best).
* **Cost effectiveness rating** (`13`, `integer`, required): Rate from 1 (worst) to 10 (best).
* **User friendliness rating** (`14`, `integer`, required): Rate from 1 (worst) to 10 (best).
* **Scalability rating** (`15`, `integer`, required): Rate from 1 (worst) to 10 (best).
* **Customizability rating** (`16`, `integer`, required): Rate from 1 (worst) to 10 (best).
* **Data accuracy rating** (`17`, `integer`, required): Rate from 1 (worst) to 10 (best).
* **Satisfaction** (`18`, `select_one`, required): Choose the most appropriate option (e.g., Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very dissatisfied).
* **Overall rating** (`19`, `integer`, required): Rate from 1 (worst) to 10 (best).
* **Aspects applied to your experience** (`20`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the aspects that applied to your experience (e.g., Easy to use, Fast, Reliable, Missing feature, Confusing, Well designed).
* **Open feedback** (`21`, `text`, optional): Provide any suggestions or concerns about the application.
* **Additional comments** (`22`, `text`, optional): Any additional comments or notes.
* **Completion date** (`23`, `date`, optional): Date when you finished the questionnaire.
* **Completion time** (`24`, `time`, optional): Time of day when you finished.
