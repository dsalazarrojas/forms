# Microcredential Pathway Development Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Microcredential Pathway Development Form is a tool to help you create a personalized learning pathway to achieve specific learning outcomes or skills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and understand what information is being requested.
2. Answer each question or prompt in the space provided.
3. Save your progress and return to the form at any time to continue or complete it.
4. Make sure to submit your completed form when you are finished.
