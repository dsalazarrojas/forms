1.  Review the form carefully to understand the different sections and their requirements.

*   **Leaders of the Crusades** (`historical_quiz_leaders`, select_one, optional): This field allows you to select the key leaders of the Crusades from the given options. Choose one or multiple leaders based on your knowledge.
*   **Events of the Crusades** (`historical_events`, select_multiple, optional): This field is for selecting the significant events of the Crusades from the provided options.
*   **Outcome of the Crusades** (`historical_outcome`, text, optional): This field is for describing the outcome or result of the Crusades.
*   **Key Figures of the Crusades** (`historical_figures`, select_multiple, optional): This field is for selecting the key figures associated with the Crusades from the given options.
*   **Start Time of the Crusades** (`historical_start_time`, date, optional): This field is for entering the start date of the Crusades.
*   **Location of the Crusades** (`historical_location`, select_multiple, optional): This field is for selecting the locations associated with the Crusades from the given options.
*   **Motivation of the Crusades** (`historical_motivation`, text, optional): This field is for providing a brief explanation of the motivation behind the Crusades.
*   **Impact on the Byzantine Empire** (`historical_byzantine_impact`, text, optional): This field is for describing the impact of the Crusades on the Byzantine Empire.
*   **Role of the Church in the Crusades** (`historical_church_role`, text, optional): This field is for explaining the role of the Church during the Crusades.
*   **Additional Notes** (`historical_note`, note, optional): This field is for entering any additional notes or information about the Crusades.
