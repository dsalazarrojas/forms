# Class Proposal Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Class Proposal Submission Form is a tool used by instructors to submit proposals for new classes to be listed on the education platform. This form helps gather essential information about the class, making it easier for the platform administrators to review and approve the proposal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields (indicated by *) as they are essential to the class proposal.
2. Answer each field carefully, providing detailed and accurate information about your proposed class.
3. Review your form for completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Proposer Full Name** (`proposer_name`, text, *required*):
This field is used to identify the instructor proposing the class.

*   Label: Proposer Full Name
*   Hint: Your first and last name

### 2. **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, *required*):
This field is used for correspondence and communication purposes.

*   Label: Email Address
