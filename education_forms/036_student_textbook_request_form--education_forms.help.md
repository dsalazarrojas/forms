# Student Textbook Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students and teachers for textbook requests. It includes fields for student and teacher details, textbook information, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Student Name.
2. Enter your Teacher Name (if applicable).
3. Provide Textbook Information.
4. Enter Course Information.
5. Specify the Textbook Quantity.
6. Enter your Student Email (if applicable).
7. Enter your Student Phone (if applicable).
8. Enter your Teacher Email (if applicable).
9. Enter your Teacher Phone (if applicable).
10. Specify your School Information (School, Address, District, State, and Zip).
11. Select your Teacher Department (if applicable).
12. Select your Student Grade Level.
13. Choose the Course Section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Enter your full name as a student. (Required)
* **Teacher Name**: Enter your teacher's name (if applicable). (Optional)
* **Textbook Info**: Provide information about the textbook(s) you are requesting. (Optional)
* **Course Info**: Enter information about the course you are enrolled in. (Optional)
* **Textbook Quantity**: Specify how many textbooks you are requesting. (Required)
* **Student Email**: Enter your email address (if applicable). (Optional)
* **Student Phone**: Enter your phone number (if applicable). (Optional)
* **Teacher Email**: Enter your teacher's email (if applicable). (Optional)
* **Teacher Phone**: Enter your teacher's phone number (if applicable). (Optional)
* **School**: Enter your school name. (Optional)
* **School Address**: Enter your school address. (Optional)
* **District**: Select your school district. (Optional)
* **School State**: Select your state. (Optional)
* **School Zip**: Select your school zip code. (Required)
* **School Phone**: Enter your school phone number. (Optional)
* **Teacher Subject**: Select your teacher's subject area. (Optional)
* **Student Grade Level**: Select your grade level. (Required)
* **Course Section**: Choose the course section you are in. (Required)

**Tips:** Make sure to fill in all required fields (Student Name, Textbook Quantity, School Zip, Course Section, Teacher ID, Student Grade Level) to ensure accurate and complete submission of your request.
