# Strengths And Difficulties Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Strengths and Difficulties Questionnaire is a tool used to evaluate an individual's strengths and difficulties in various categories, providing valuable insights into personal growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the category that best describes your strengths and difficulties.
2. Answer each question in the corresponding category with your thoughts and reflections.
3. Ensure that all questions are completed as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
4. Review your answers for completeness and clarity before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Strengths And Difficulties Questionnaire** (`text`, required/optional): A description of the form's purpose and instructions.
* **Category** (`select_one`, required/optional): Choose the category that best describes your strengths and difficulties. For example: "Category A", "Category B", etc.
* **Questions 1-22** (`text`, required/optional): Answer each question in the corresponding category with your thoughts and reflections. For example: "Question 1: Describe your greatest strength...", "Question 2: Describe your most significant difficulty...", etc.

Note: Questions 1-22 are text-based questions that require detailed answers.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate in your answers.
* Take your time to provide thoughtful and reflective responses.
* If you're unsure about a question, consult your administrator or refer to the user guide for guidance.
* Review and edit your answers before submitting the form.
