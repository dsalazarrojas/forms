# Parental Complaint Resolution Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parental Complaint Resolution Acknowledgement Form is used to document and track complaints filed by parents regarding their children's experiences at school, ensuring both the parent and school acknowledge and resolve the complaint in a timely and satisfactory manner.
## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, start by filling out the required fields. Each field is labeled with its name and a description of its purpose.
2. Enter your complaint in the **Student Complaint** field. Describe the issue you are experiencing with your child's school experience.
3. In the **Complaint Date** field, select the date when the issue occurred.
4. Indicate the school's action taken to address the complaint in the **School Action** field. Choose whether the school has taken action to address the issue.
5. Sign your name in the **Parent Signature** field, acknowledging that you have submitted the complaint.
6. The school staff will also sign in the **School Signature** field once they have reviewed and addressed the complaint.
7. Finally, update the **Complaint Status** field to indicate whether the complaint is **Active**, **Resolved**, or **Closed**.
8. Provide any additional details about the resolution in the **Resolution Details** field, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Complaint**: Enter a brief description of the issue you are experiencing with your child's school experience.
* **Parent Name**: Enter your name, the parent submitting the complaint.
* **Complaint Date**: Enter the date when the issue occurred. Use the date format mm/dd/yy.
* **School Action**: Choose whether the school has taken action to address the issue. Select 'Yes' if the school has taken action, 'No' otherwise.
* **Parent Signature**: This field is for your signature, acknowledging that you have submitted the complaint.
* **School Signature**: This field is for the school staff to sign once they have reviewed and addressed the complaint.
* **Complaint Status**: Select the status of your complaint from the options **Active**, **Resolved**, or **Closed**.
* **Resolution Details**: Provide any additional details about the resolution, if necessary.

## Tips
* Please be specific and clear when describing your complaint to ensure the school can effectively address the issue.
* Ensure that the school has reviewed and addressed the complaint before selecting **Resolved** or **Closed**.
* If the complaint requires further action from the school, please be patient and allow the school sufficient time to resolve the issue.
