# Education Accommodation Plan Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Education Accommodation Plan Form is used to gather information about a student's support needs and accommodations required to support their education.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the student's individual circumstances and needs.
2. Select all the student's support needs from the list.
3. List any requested accommodations.
4. If applicable, provide the parent or guardian's consent and their name.
5. Enter the captured date and time.
6. Enter the student's identifier number (if applicable).
7. Enter the contact number for the student (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Support Needs** (select multiple):
	+ Select all the support needs that apply to the student from the dropdown menu.
* **Requested Accommodations** (select multiple):
	+ List all the accommodations the student requires to support their education.
* **Parent or Guardian Consent** (text):
	+ If applicable, enter the parent or guardian's consent for the accommodations.
* **Captured Date** (date):
	+ Enter the date the form was filled out.
* **Captured Time** (time):
	+ Enter the time the form was filled out.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (text):
	+ Enter the parent or guardian's name (if applicable).
* **Student Identifier** (number):
	+ Enter the student's identifier number (if applicable).
* **Contact Number** (text):
	+ Enter the contact number for the student (if applicable).
