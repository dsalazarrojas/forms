# Application For Ethical Review Renewal Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for renewing ethical review approvals for research projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter the project lead name in the `Project lead name` field.
2. Enter the title of the research project in the `Project title` field.
3. Enter the original ethical approval date in the `Original approval date` field.
4. Explain the reason for renewal in the `Reason for renewal` field.
5. If necessary, describe any changes or amendments to the protocol in the `Changes to protocol` field.
6. If applicable, list any new or increased risks to participants in the `Participant risks` field.
7. If applicable, describe the steps taken to mitigate identified risks in the `Mitigation measures` field.
8. List any funding or sponsors supporting the project in the `Funding sources` field.
9. If applicable, upload or reference supporting documents, such as consent forms, in the `Supporting documents filename` field.
10. Enter the email address for committee correspondence in the `Contact email` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project lead name (Project lead name, text, required)**: Enter the name of the student or researcher who is leading the project.
* **Project title (Project title, text, required)**: Enter the title of the research project.
* **Original approval date (Original approval date, date, required)**: Enter the date of the original ethical approval.
* **Reason for renewal (Reason for renewal, text, required)**: Explain why renewal is requested.
* **Changes to protocol (Changes to protocol, text, optional)**: Describe any changes or amendments to the protocol.
* **Participant risks (Participant risks, text, optional)**: If new or increased risks to participants have been identified, list them here.
* **Mitigation measures (Mitigation measures, text, optional)**: Describe the steps taken to reduce identified risks.
* **Funding sources (Funding sources, text, optional)**: List any funding or sponsors supporting the project.
* **Supporting documents filename (Supporting documents filename, text, optional)**: If applicable, upload or reference supporting documents, such as consent forms.
* **Contact email (Contact email, email, required)**: Enter the email address for committee correspondence.
