# school-registration-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "school-registration-form" is designed to collect information from students and parents for school registration purposes. This form aims to gather essential details such as student name, contact information, and class choices to streamline the registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in the required fields and select the necessary options.
2. Make sure to enter accurate information for the student.
3. Review and sign the School Terms & Conditions section to acknowledge understanding and acceptance of the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter the student's full name as it appears on their identification.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the student's date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format (Year-Month-Day).
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the parent or guardian who will be the primary contact point for the student.
* **Class Choice 1**: Select one class choice option from the dropdown list.
* **Class Choice 2**: Select multiple class choice options from the dropdown list (multiple selection allowed).
* **School Terms & Conditions**: Review and sign the terms and conditions to acknowledge understanding and acceptance of the terms.
* **Student Identifier**: Enter a unique student identifier (if applicable).
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the parent or guardian.

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the school administration for clarification.
* Review your submission carefully before finalizing the form.
* Make sure to save or print a copy of the completed form for your records.
