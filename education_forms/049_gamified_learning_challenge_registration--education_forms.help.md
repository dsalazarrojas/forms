# Gamified Learning Challenge Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants and teams involved in a gamified learning challenge. The form is not affiliated with any form platform and is an original adaptation of the form design.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure you provide all required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant** (`participant_form`, text, required: false): This field is for the participant's name or username.
	+ Please enter your name or username as you are known to the challenge organizers.
* **Team** (`team_form`, text, required: false): This field is for the team name or username.
	+ If you are part of a team, enter the team name or username.
* **Challenge Info** (`challenge_info`, text, required: false): This field is for any additional information about the challenge.
	+ You can add any relevant details about the challenge, such as the challenge type or any specific rules.
* **Individual Participant Info** (`individual_participant_info`, text, required: false): This field is for any additional information about the individual participant.
	+ You can add any relevant details about the participant, such as their experience level or any special abilities.
* **Team** (`team_info`, text, required: false): This field is for more detailed information about the team.
	+ If you are part of a team, this is the place to add more details about your team, such as team members or team goals.

## Tips
- Be accurate and complete in your responses.
- Ensure you provide all required information.
- If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to the challenge organizers for clarification.
- You can modify the form as needed to fit your specific use case, but be sure to give credit to the original form designer.
