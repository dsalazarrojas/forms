# Intermediate English Exam - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an individual's background, including their name, contact details, and languages spoken, for the Intermediate English Exam.

## How To Complete This Form
- **Start by carefully reading this guide** to understand each field's purpose and requirement.
- **Answer each question carefully and accurately**, providing as much detail as possible.
- **Submit the form when you have completed all required fields**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, Optional): Enter your name in the format you would like to be addressed as.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, Optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, Optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, Optional): Enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, text, Optional): Enter your current address for contact purposes.
* **Student Identification** (`student_identification`, text, Optional): Enter any government-issued ID number for verification purposes.
* **Student Nationality** (`student_nationality`, text, Optional): Enter your nationality or country of origin.
* **Student Language 1** (`student_language_1`, text, Optional): Enter the first language you speak or are proficient in.
* **Student Language 2** (`student_language_2`, text, Optional): Enter the second language you speak or are proficient in.
* **Student Language 3** (`student_language_3`, text, Optional): Enter the third language you speak or are proficient in.
* **Student Language 4** (`student_language_4`, text, Optional): Enter the fourth language you speak or are proficient in.
* **Student Language 5** (`student_language_5`, text, Optional): Enter the fifth language you speak or are proficient in.
* **Student Language 6** (`student_language_6`, text, Optional): Enter the sixth language you speak or are proficient in.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submission.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer back to this guide for clarification.
* If you need help, contact your supervisor or support team.
* This guide will be updated periodically to reflect any changes to the form or its purpose.
