# Student Placement Request Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for student placement requests, allowing administrators or teachers to track student progress, assignments, or other educational data.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the Student Information section, providing the student's details such as name, school level, grade level, and any other relevant information.
2. Select the correct school level (High School, College, or University) that corresponds to the student's current educational level.
3. If applicable, fill in the student's grade level and current grade.
4. Enter the name of the course the student is currently enrolled in or will be placed in.
5. Enter the start and end dates of the placement, if applicable.
6. Provide the reason why the student is leaving or being placed out of the institution.
7. If assigned to a specific teacher, enter their name.
8. Add any comments or notes about the student's placement.
9. Check the student's status (Active, Inactive, Absent, or Withdrawn) as per their current situation.
10. If necessary, enter any notes about the student.
11. Finally, confirm that the form has been completed by signing off.
12. Enter the date the form was submitted, as well as the name of the person submitting the form and the date they submitted it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Information (text)**: Enter the student's name, a unique identifier or any other relevant details.
* **Student Name (text)**: Enter the student's full name as per their official records.
* **School Level (select_one)**: Select the correct level of schooling (High School, College, or University) corresponding to the student's current educational level.
* **Grade Level (number)**: If applicable, enter the student's current grade level.
* **Student Grade (number)**: If applicable, enter the student's current grade.
* **Course Name (text)**: Enter the name of the course the student is currently enrolled in or will be placed in.
* **Start Date (date)**: Enter the date the student started the placement, if applicable.
* **End Date (date)**: Enter the date the student ended the placement, if applicable.
* **Reason for Leaving (text)**: Provide a brief explanation of why the student is leaving or being placed out of the institution.
* **Assigned Teacher (text)**: If the student has been assigned to a specific teacher, enter their name.
* **Comments (text)**: Add any additional comments or notes about the student's placement.
* **Student Status (select_multiple)**: Select the student's current status (Active, Inactive, Absent, or Withdrawn) based on their current situation.
* **Student Notes (note)**: If necessary, enter any additional information or notes about the student.
* **Form Completed (text)**: Confirm that the form has been completed and signed off by the person submitting it.
* **Submitted By (text)**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted On (date)**: Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Assigned Tool (text)**: If the student has been assigned to a specific tool or resource, enter its name.
* **Assigned User (text)**: If the student has been assigned to a specific user or staff member, enter their name.
* **Created By (text)**: Enter the name of the person who created the form.
* **Created On (date)**: Enter the date the form was created.
* **Updated By (text)**: Enter the name of the person who updated the form last.
* **Updated On (date)**: Enter the date the form was last updated.
