# Course Choice Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Course Choice Registration Form is designed to collect information from students about their course choices, including course selection, grade level, language, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the course you are interested in from the "Select Course" dropdown menu.
2. Enter your student information, including first name, last name, email, and phone number.
3. Select your grade level and language preference.
4. Choose the course type and any additional details as required.
5. Enter the start and end dates of the course.
6. Select the status and instructor of the course.
7. Choose the room and level of the course.
8. Select the time and days of the course.
9. Add any comments or additional notes about the course.
10. Enter the course choice number and ID (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Course**: Select the course you are interested in from the dropdown menu. You can choose from the available courses listed.
* **Student First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Student Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Student E-mail**: Enter your email address as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Student Phone**: Enter your phone number as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Select Grade Level**: Select your grade level from the dropdown menu.
* **Select Language**: Select your preferred language from the dropdown menu.
* **Select Course Type**: Choose the type of course you are interested in from the dropdown menu.
* **Course Name**: Enter the name of the course you are interested in.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the course in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of the course in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Status**: Select the status of the course from the dropdown menu.
* **Select Instructor**: Select the instructor of the course from the dropdown menu.
* **Select Room**: Choose the room where the course will be held from the dropdown menu.
* **Select Level**: Select the level of the course from the dropdown menu.
* **Select Time**: Choose the time of the course from the dropdown menu.
* **Select Days**: Choose the days the course will be held from the dropdown menu.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about the course.
* **Course Choice Number**: Enter the course choice number as given to you by the course administration.
* **Course Choice ID**: Enter the unique course choice ID given to you by the course administration.
* **User Input**: Add any additional information about yourself or your course preferences.
* **Course Registration Date**: The date the course was registered.
* **Select User**: Select the user who registered the course from the dropdown menu.
