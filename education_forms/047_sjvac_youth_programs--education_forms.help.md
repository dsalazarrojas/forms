**SJVAC Youth Programs - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The **SJVAC Youth Programs** form is designed to collect information about programs available for youth. This form helps to create new programs, manage existing ones, and track their details. It is essential to have accurate and up-to-date information about youth programs, including their titles, categories, descriptions, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter Program Title**: Type the title of your youth program in the "Program Title" field.
2. **Select Category**: Choose the category of your program from the "Program Category" dropdown menu (e.g., Education, Recreation, Health & Wellness).
3. **Enter Description**: Write a brief description of your program in the "Program Description" field.
4. **Enter Contact Information**: Provide the following contact details:
   * **Mailing Address**: Enter the mailing address of your program.
   * **Meeting Location**: Enter the location where your program takes place.
   * **Contact Number**: Enter the phone number of your program contact.
   * **Contact Email**: Enter the email of your program contact.
5. **Enter Additional Details**:
   * **Zip Code**: Enter the zip code of your program location.
   * **Program Start Date**: Enter the start date of your program.
   * **Program End Date**: Enter the end date of your program.
   * **Program Hours**: Enter the hours your program operates.
   * **Days of Operation**: Select the days your program operates (e.g., Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday).
   * **Program Time**: Enter the time of your program operations.
6. **Enter Evaluation Details**:
   * **Program Rating Scale**: Choose the rating scale for your program (e.g., Very Poor, Poor, Average, Good, Excellent).
7. **Enter Notes**:
   * **Program Notes**: Add any additional notes about your program.
   * **Contact Notes**: Add any additional notes about your program contact.

## Tips

* Double-check the accuracy of your input data before submitting the form.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out to avoid submission errors.
* For fields with dropdown menus or multiple selection options, choose the most relevant or accurate option for your program.
* If you encounter any issues or have questions, don't hesitate to contact your program administrator or support team.
