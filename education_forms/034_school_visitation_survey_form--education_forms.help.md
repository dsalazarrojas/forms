# School Visitation Survey Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from visitors to help the school administration understand their experience and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the space provided, being as detailed as possible in your feedback.
2. For rating fields (e.g., "Rating Facilities", "Rating Staff", etc.), please select a number between 1 (very dissatisfied) and 5 (very satisfied).
3. For text fields (e.g., "School Visitation Suggestions", "Other Comments"), please provide a brief description or suggestion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Visitation Suggestions (13)**: Please provide any suggestions or comments about how the school can improve its facilities or services.
* **Rating Facilities (2)**: Rate the overall facilities of the school, such as cleanliness, condition, and amenities, on a scale of 1 (very dissatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).
* **Rating Services (3)**: Rate the quality of services provided by the school, such as support staff, resources, and amenities, on a scale of 1 (very dissatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).
* **Rating Experience (4)**: Rate your overall experience at the school, on a scale of 1 (very dissatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).
* **Rating Staff (5)**: Rate the friendliness and helpfulness of the school staff, on a scale of 1 (very dissatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).
* **Rating Facilities and Services (6)**: Rate the overall quality of facilities and services provided by the school, on a scale of 1 (very dissatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).
* **Rating Value of Service (7)**: Rate the overall value of the services provided by the school, on a scale of 1 (very dissatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).
* **Rating Environment (9)**: Rate the overall environment of the school, on a scale of 1 (very dissatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).
* **Rating Value of Visit (10)**: Rate the overall value of your visit to the school, on a scale of 1 (very dissatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).
* **Rating Value of Visit (11)**: This field is a duplicate of the previous "Rating Value of Visit" field. Please skip this field.
* **Other Comments (12)**: Please provide any additional comments or suggestions that are not related to the above fields.
* **Email Address (School) (13)**: If you would like to provide your email address, please enter it here so we can follow up on your suggestions or feedback.

Note: Fields with the same label are intentionally duplicated in the form schema, but only one instance is used in the explanation.
