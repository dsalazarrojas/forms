# National History Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The National History Quiz form is designed to collect data from students and teachers on the completion status, submission method, and other details related to the national history quiz.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the option that best answers the question "National History Quiz" at the top of the form.
2. Enter the date of submission (mm/dd/yyyy) in the "Date of Submission" field.
3. Enter your name in the "Student Name" field.
4. (Optional) Enter your age in the "Age of Student" field.
5. Enter the name of your school in the "Teacher School Location" field, if applicable.
6. Select the option that best answers the question "Form Submitted by" from the dropdown menu.
7. Select one or more rating options for "Teacher Rating" and "Student Rating" from the dropdown menu.
8. Enter the time of submission in the "Submission Time" field, if applicable.
9. Select the option that best answers the question "National History Formation" from the dropdown menu.
10. Select the option that best answers the question "Submission Method" from the dropdown menu.
11. Select the option that best answers the question "Quiz Type" from the dropdown menu.
12. Select the option that best answers the question "Submission Status" from the dropdown menu.
13. Select the option that best answers the question "Quiz Completed" from the dropdown menu.
14. Select one or more rating options for "Teacher Rating Value" and "Student Rating Value" from the dropdown menu.
15. Enter the time when the quiz started and ended in the "Quiz Started At" and "Quiz Ended At" fields, if applicable.
16. Enter the time taken to complete the quiz in minutes in the "Submission Time in Minutes" field, if applicable.
