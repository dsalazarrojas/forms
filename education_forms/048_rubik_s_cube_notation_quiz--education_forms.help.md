# Rubiks_Cube_Notation_Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users understand and learn the notation system used for the Rubik's Cube. The form will guide you through a series of questions and ask for personal details to help us assess your understanding of the cube notation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as instructed. The form is divided into several pages, each with its own set of questions. You will be asked to provide information about your first, second, third, and fourth moves, as well as the side of the cube you are looking at, the correct answer, and your personal details such as email, name, age, phone number, and any other information you'd like to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Move** (`first_move`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the first move of the rubik's cube.
* **Second Move** (`second_move`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the second move of the rubik's cube.
* **Third Move** (`third_move`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the third move of the rubik's cube.
* **Fourth Move** (`fourth_move`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the fourth move of the rubik's cube.
* **First Cube Side** (`first_cube_side`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the side of the cube you are looking at.
* **Cube Notation** (`cube_notation`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your understanding of the cube notation.
* **Correct Answer** (`correct_answer`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select the correct answer from the options provided.
* **Other Correct Answer** (`correct_answer_other`, `text`, required: false): Please enter any other correct answer you are aware of.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required: false): Please confirm your answers.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Please enter a valid email address.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name.
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, required: false): Please enter your age.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your phone number.
* **Other Info** (`other_info`, `text`, required: false): Please enter any other information you'd like to share.
