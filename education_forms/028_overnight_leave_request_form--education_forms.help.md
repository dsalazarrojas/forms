# Overnight Leave Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Overnight Leave Request Form is a document used to submit a request for overnight leave to management. It is designed to provide essential information for processing and approving leave requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact details, including your first name, last name, phone number, and email address.
2. Provide your leave details, including the start and end dates of your leave, a description of your reason for leave, and the number of nights you will be away.
3. Choose the reason for your leave from the provided options.
4. Add any additional information that may be helpful in processing your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`contact_details_first_name`, text, optional): Enter your given name.
* **Last Name** (`contact_details_last_name`, text, optional): Enter your family name.
* **Phone** (`contact_details_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number for communication.
* **Email** (`contact_details_email`, email, optional): Enter your work email address.
* **Leave Details** (`leave_details`, text, optional): Briefly describe your leave request.
* **Start Date** (`leave_details_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when you plan to start your leave.
* **End Date** (`leave_details_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when you plan to end your leave.
* **Description** (`leave_details_description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of your leave request.
* **Reason** (`leave_details_reason`, select one, optional): Select the reason for your leave:
	+ Sick leave
	+ Vacation
	+ Other
* **Number of Nights** (`leave_details_number_of_nights`, number, optional): Enter the number of nights you will be away.
* **Additional Information** (`leave_details_additional_info`, note, optional): Add any other relevant details about your leave request.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, optional): Include your digital signature, if required for approval.

## Tips
* Make sure to proofread your form before submitting to ensure all information is accurate and complete.
* Double-check your reason for leave to ensure it matches the company's leave policy.
* Attach any supporting documents as needed.
