# Graduate Employment Rate Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on graduate employment rates, program outcomes, and other relevant details to help academic institutions monitor graduate employment trends and track program effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your current employment status from the options provided.
2. Select the type of graduate outcome data you want us to report.
3. Enter the employment rate.
4. Select the graduate employment rate.
5. Enter your email address and phone number.
6. Choose the date you graduated.
7. Enter the name of the program you attended.
8. Enter the name of the institution you attended.
9. Enter any additional notes you'd like to provide.
10. Repeat steps 2-8 for any additional graduate outcomes (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What is your current employment status**: Select your current employment status from the options provided.
  • Employed: Currently working full-time or part-time.
  • Not Employed: Not currently employed.
- **Program Outcome Data**: Select the type of graduate outcome data you want us to report (e.g., internships, job placements, or research projects).
- **What type of graduate outcome data do you want us to report**: Select all applicable options for the type of graduate outcome data you want us to report (e.g., Full-time prophets of the company, Part-time prophets of the company, Other).
- **Employment Rate**: Enter the employment rate (e.g., Less than 5%, 5-20%, 21-50%, 51-80%, More than 80%).
- **Graduate Employment Rate**: Select the graduate employment rate from the options provided.
  • Less than 5%
  • 5-20%
  • 21-50%
  • 51-80%
  • More than 80%
- **Email**: Enter your email address.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
- **Date Graduated**: Enter the date you graduated.
- **Program Name**: Enter the name of the program you attended.
- **Institution**: Enter the name of the institution you attended.
- **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional notes you'd like to provide.
- **Date Graduated 3/4/5**: Enter the date you graduated for any additional graduate outcomes (if applicable).
- **Program Name 3/4/5**: Enter the name of the program you attended for any additional graduate outcomes (if applicable).
- **Institution 3/4/5**: Enter the name of the institution you attended for any additional graduate outcomes (if applicable).
- **Additional Notes 3/4/5**: Enter any additional notes for any additional graduate outcomes (if applicable).
