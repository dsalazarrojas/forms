# Teacher Student Interaction Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to help students evaluate their interaction with their teacher. It's meant to be a constructive feedback mechanism to help teachers understand how to better support their students and improve their teaching practices.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question based on your honest and genuine experience with your teacher.
2. Select the option that best fits your experience.
3. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Any additional comments" fields.
4. You can skip any question if it's not applicable or if you don't have an opinion on it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How would you rate your interaction with the teacher?** (`teacher_rating`, `number`, optional): This question is meant to gauge your overall experience with the teacher.
* **How often did the teacher provide support when you needed it?** (`teacher_support`, `select_one`, optional): This question is asking about the teacher's availability and willingness to help when you need it.
* **How often did the teacher offer help when you needed it?** (`teacher_help`, `select_one`, optional): This question is similar to the previous one, but with a focus on the teacher's initiative to help.
* **Classwork completion and submission rate** (`classwork_completion`, `number`, optional): This question is about the amount of coursework and assignments you completed and submitted.
* **Homework completion rate** (`homework_completion`, `number`, optional): This question is similar to the previous one, but about homework.
* **How often did the teacher offer feedback on your work?** (`teacher_feedback`, `select_one`, optional): This question is asking about the teacher's ability to provide constructive feedback.
* **Class attendance** (`class_attendance`, `number`, optional): This question is about your attendance in the class.
* **Teacher communication** (`communication`, `number`, optional): This question is about how well the teacher communicates with you.
* **How would you rate the teacher's time management?** (`time_management`, `select_one`, optional): This question is about the teacher's ability to manage time effectively.
* **Overall experience with the teacher** (`overall_experience`, `number`, optional): This question is meant to summarize your overall experience with the teacher.
* **How clear were the teacher's instructions?** (`teacher_clarity`, `select_one`, optional): This question is about the teacher's ability to explain things clearly.
* **How helpful was the teacher in class?** (`teacher_helpfulness`, `select_one`, optional): This question is similar to the previous one, but with a focus on help.
* **How fair was the teacher's grading system?** (`teacher_fairness`, `select_one`, optional): This question is about the teacher's fairness in grading.
* **How well did the teacher help you set and achieve your learning goals?** (`student_learning_goals`, `select_one`, optional): This question is about the teacher's ability to help you set and achieve your learning goals.
* **How much freedom did the teacher give you to make choices and decisions?** (`student_autonomy`, `select_one`, optional): This question is about the teacher's ability to give you autonomy in your learning process.
* **How often did the teacher encourage self-directed learning?** (`student_self_directed_learning`, `select_one`, optional): This question is about the teacher's encouragement of self-directed learning.
* **How often did the teacher show support for students' well-being?** (`teacher_support_of_students`, `select_one`, optional): This question is about the teacher's ability to show support for students' well-being.
* **How much did the teacher show respect towards students?** (`teacher_respect`, `select_one`, optional): This question is about the teacher's level of respect.
* **How well did the teacher understand and manage emotions in the class?** (`teacher_emotional_intelligence`, `select_one`, optional): This question is about the teacher's emotional intelligence.
* **Any additional comments about your interaction with the teacher** (`comments`, `note`, optional): This question is for any additional comments or feedback you have.
* **How much did the teacher help you develop your teaching skills?** (`teacher_proficiency`, `select_one`, optional): This question is about the teacher's ability to help you develop your teaching skills.
* **How open-minded and receptive is the teacher towards new ideas?** (`teacher_openness`, `select_one`, optional): This question is about the teacher's openness to new ideas.
* **How well was the teacher able to incorporate cultural relevance?** (`teacher_culturally_responsive`, `select_one`, optional): This question is about the teacher's ability to incorporate cultural relevance.
* **Overall rating of this course teacher** (`overall_rating`, `number`, optional): This question is meant to be a summary of your overall experience with the teacher.
* **Any additional comments** (`comments_2`, `note`, optional): This question is for any additional comments or feedback you have.
