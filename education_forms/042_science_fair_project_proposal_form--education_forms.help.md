# Science Fair Project Proposal Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Science Fair Project Proposal Form is a document that helps us gather information about your science fair project. Please fill in the required and optional fields accurately to ensure your project is considered for the science fair.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Form Title** (`form_title`, Text, Optional): Enter the title of your science project. This will give us an idea of what your project is about. 
2. **Project Name** (`project_name`, Text, Optional): If your project has a specific name, please enter it here.
3. **Category** (`project_category`, Select One, Optional, Select from: Category, Other): Choose a category for your project. This will help us evaluate its relevance to the science fair theme.
4. **Project Description** (`project_description`, Text, Optional): Briefly describe your science project. This should include your research question, hypothesis, and expected outcomes.
5. **Teacher's Name** (`teacher_name`, Text, Optional): If you have a teacher's name, please enter it here.
6. **Student's Name** (`student_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your name as the student who submitted the project.
7. **Student's Email** (`student_email`, Email, Optional, e.g., johndoe@example.com): Please enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
8. **Student's Phone** (`student_phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number if you want us to contact you.

## Tips

- Ensure that your project is not already submitted or under evaluation.
- Fill in all fields accurately and clearly.
- If you have any questions, contact our support team.
- Make sure to proofread before submitting your form.
