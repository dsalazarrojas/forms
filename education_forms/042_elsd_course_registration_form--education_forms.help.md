# elsd_course_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The elsd_course_registration_form is used to register for courses in our system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the required text fields.
2. Select your Gender from the drop-down menu.
3. Provide your E-Mail address in the format required for the field.
4. Enter your Phone Number, which may be used for contacting you regarding your course.
5. Enter the Course ID, which must be a numeric value.
6. Enter the name of the course you wish to register for.
7. Choose the date you would like to start the course (if applicable).
8. Choose the date you would like the course to end (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as you would like it to appear in your records.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as you would like it to appear in your records.
* **Date Of Birth** (date, not required): If you wish to provide this information, select a date of birth for our records.
* **E-Mail** (email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (text, not required): Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted by us.
* **Gender** (select_one, required): Select your gender from the provided options.
* **Course ID** (number, required): Enter the numeric code for the course you wish to register for.
* **Course Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the course you wish to register for.
* **Course Start Date** (date, not required): If you have selected a start date, enter the date you would like the course to begin.
* **Course End Date** (date, not required): If you have selected a start date, enter the date you would like the course to end.

## Tips
Please make sure to double-check your entered information for accuracy before submitting the form. Ensure that you have the correct Course ID for the course you wish to register for.
