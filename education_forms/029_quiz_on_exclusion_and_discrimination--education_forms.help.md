# Quiz On Exclusion And Discrimination - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users identify and document instances of exclusion and discrimination in educational settings. It aims to provide a comprehensive assessment of the impact of these behaviors on students' experiences and outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the questions carefully and provide detailed answers to each one.
2. Be as specific as possible when describing your experiences or observations.
3. Use clear and concise language to ensure accurate and efficient processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Question** (`first_question`, text, required): Briefly describe a situation where you felt excluded or discriminated against in an educational setting.
* **Second Question** (`second_question`, text, optional): If applicable, describe any subsequent events or reactions that followed the initial exclusion or discrimination.
* **Third Question** (`third_question`, select one, required): How did you feel about the situation?
	+ option 1: Frustrated
	+ option 2: Angry
	+ option 3: Helpless
* **Fourth Question** (`fourth_question`, number, required): On a scale of 1-10, how severe was the exclusion or discrimination?
* **Fifth Question** (`fifth_question`, select multiple, optional): If applicable, which of the following actions were taken by the institution or individuals involved?
	+ option 4: Acknowledged and apologized
	+ option 5: Took no action
* **Sixth Question** (`sixth_question`, date, required): When did the exclusion or discrimination occur?
* **Seventh Question** (`seventh_question`, time, optional): If applicable, what time of day or night was the exclusion or discrimination?
* **Eight Question** (`eight_question`, note, optional): If applicable, provide any additional notes or comments about the situation.
* **Ninth Question** (`ninth_question`, email, optional): If applicable, provide contact information for follow-up or support.
* **Tenth Question** (`tenth_question`, text, optional): If applicable, describe any further actions taken by the institution or individuals involved.
* **Eleventh Question** (`eleventh_question`, text, required): Describe the outcome or resolution of the situation.
* **Twelfth Question** (`twelfth_question`, text, optional): If applicable, describe any long-term effects or consequences of the exclusion or discrimination.
* **Thirteenth Question** (`thirteenth_question`, text, optional): If applicable, describe any subsequent interactions or conversations with the institution or individuals involved.
* **Fourteenth Question** (`fourteenth_question`, text, optional): If applicable, describe any changes made to prevent similar situations in the future.
* **Fifteenth Question** (`fifteenth_question`, text, optional): If applicable, describe any support or resources provided to you as a result of the situation.
* **Sixteenth Question** (`sixteenth_question`, text, required): Describe your experience in the past 30 days.
* **Seventeenth Question** (`seventeenth_question`, text, optional): If applicable, describe any previous experiences similar to this one.
* **Eighteenth Question** (`eighteenth_question`, text, optional): If applicable, describe any ongoing or future experiences related to exclusion and discrimination.
* **Nineteenth Question** (`nineteenth_question`, text, required): Describe how you would like to be treated in a future exclusion or discrimination situation.
* **Twentieth Question** (`twentieth_question`, text, optional): If applicable, describe any specific actions or changes the institution could take to improve their policies or procedures.
* **Twenty-First Question** (`twentyfirst_question`, text, optional): If applicable, describe any further comments or suggestions for improvement.
* **Twenty-Second Question** (`twentysecond_question`, text, required): Summarize your overall experience and any remaining concerns.
* **Twenty-Third Question** (`twentythird_question`, text, optional): If applicable, describe any additional support or resources needed.
* **Twenty-Fourth Question** (`twentyfourth_question`, text, optional): If applicable, describe any follow-up or follow-through actions taken by the institution.
* **Twenty-Fifth Question** (`twentyfifth_question`, text, optional): If applicable, describe any final thoughts or comments about the experience.
