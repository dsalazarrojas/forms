# Geography And Environment Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your knowledge and understanding of geography and environment-related topics. It will guide you through several questions, each covering a different aspect of the topic.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting your area of focus in the environmental science section (Page 2).
2. Read each page carefully and answer the questions based on your understanding of the topic.
3. Select one or multiple options from the provided lists, depending on the page type.
4. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as you are comfortable with.
5. Enter numerical values for numerical questions.
6. Answer all questions truthfully and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Environmental Science** (`page_2`, `select_one`, required): Choose the area of environmental science that best describes your focus.
* **Geography Science Facts** (`page_3`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply from the list of geographical science facts.
* **Environmental Awareness** (`page_4`, `text`, required): Describe your thoughts on environmental awareness.
* **Environmental Policy** (`page_7`, `select_one`, required): Choose the environmental policy that best aligns with your views.
* **Geography Quiz Results** (`page_5`, `number`, required): Enter the score or numerical value that represents your results from the previous quiz.
* **Environmental Impact** (`page_6`, `number`, required): Enter a numerical value that represents your understanding of the environmental impact of a particular action or event.
* **Geography Data** (`page_8`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the geographical data was collected or when you last checked your location.
* **Geography Location** (`page_9`, `time`, required): Enter the time when you last checked your location.
* **Geography Question** (`page_10`, `text`, required): Answer the geographical question in as much detail as you are comfortable with.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate in your answers.
* Take your time to read each page carefully before proceeding.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
