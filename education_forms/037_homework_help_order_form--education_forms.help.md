# Homework Help Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Homework Help Order Form is a tool for students to request help on homework-related matters. It collects information about the student's identity, the subject they need help with, and details about their problem statement.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out the form with your name (first and last).
- Enter your student ID.
- Specify the subject you need help with.
- Describe your problem statement in detail.
- If you have any attachments (e.g., files or images), upload them with this form.
- Provide your contact information (email or phone number).
- Choose your preferred contact method (call or chat).
- Select who submitted this request (student, teacher, or parent).
- Check the "Agree" checkbox to confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
- Sign and date the form by filling out the "Submitter Signature" field and "Submitter Date" field.
- Add any additional comments if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Enter your first name.
  * Type: text
  * Required: yes
- **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
  * Type: text
  * Required: yes
- **Student ID**: Enter your student ID number.
  * Type: text
  * Required: yes
- **Subject**: Specify the subject you need help with (e.g., math, science, or language arts).
  * Type: text
  * Required: yes
- **Problem Statement**: Describe your problem or question in detail.
  * Type: text
  * Required: yes
- **Attachment**: If you have any attachments (e.g., files or images), upload them here.
  * Type: text
  * Required: no
- **Email**: Enter your email address.
  * Type: email
  * Required: no
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
  * Type: text
  * Required: no
- **Preferred Contact**: Choose how you would like to be contacted (e.g., call or chat).
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: yes
- **Who Submitted**: Select who submitted this request (e.g., student, teacher, or parent).
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: yes
- **Submitter Email**: Enter the email of the person submitting this request.
  * Type: email
  * Required: no
- **Submitter Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person submitting this request.
  * Type: text
  * Required: no
- **Submitter Comments**: Add any additional comments or information about this request.
  * Type: text
  * Required: no
- **Agree**: Check the box to confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: yes
- **Terms**: Enter any terms or conditions related to this request.
  * Type: text
  * Required: no
  * Hint: This might be for terms and conditions of the help request.
- **Terms Agreement**: Check the box to confirm you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions.
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: yes
- **Submitter Signature**: Sign this form by filling out the signature field.
  * Type: text
  * Required: yes
- **Submitter Date**: Date of submission.
  * Type: date
  * Required: no
- **Submitter Time**: Time of submission.
  * Type: time
  * Required: no
- **Submitter Notes**: Additional comments or information about this request.
  * Type: text
  * Required: no
- **Agreement**: Check the box to confirm you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions.
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: yes
- **Other**: Add any additional information not covered in other fields.
  * Type: text
  * Required: no

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as "Required: yes") accurately and thoroughly.
- Attach any relevant files or images to support your request.
- Be clear and concise when describing your problem statement.
- Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
- Ensure that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions before submitting this form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask the form administrators or teachers for help.
