# Typing Speed Exam Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Typing Speed Exam Form is used to collect information about your typing speed and habits. This form will ask you for your name, email address, phone number, and other details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your name in the first field.
3. Enter your email address in the second field.
4. Enter your phone number in the third field.
5. Enter your score and time taken in the fourth and fifth fields respectively.
6. Submit the form once you have filled out all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Score** (`score`, `number`, required: false): Enter your typing score, if you have it.
* **Time Taken** (`time_taken`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time taken for the typing test, if you have it.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can skip it or contact us for clarification.
* The information collected from this form will be used for internal purposes only.
