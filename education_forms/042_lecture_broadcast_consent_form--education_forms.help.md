# Lecture Broadcast Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lecture Broadcast Consent Form is used to collect consent from participants for the recording and sharing of their lectures. It ensures that participants understand the terms and conditions of their participation and consent to the recording and sharing of their lectures. This form is an essential part of the educational process, allowing participants to make informed decisions about their participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Select your participant type (Yes or No) in the "Participant Type" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select your participant status (Active or Inactive) in the "Participant Status" field.
6. Select the recording type (Yes or No) in the "Recording Type" field.
7. Choose whether to share participant information (Yes or No) in the "Share Participant Info" field.
8. Select whether to record and share your lectures (Yes or No) in the "Record and Share?" field.
9. Choose which lectures to share with others (Yes or No) in the "Share Lectures with" field.
10. Choose which lectures to share on other platforms (Yes or No) in the "Share Lectures on" field.
11. Confirm your consent to record participant consent (Yes or No) in the "Record Participant Consent" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Lecture Broadcast Consent Form (form_title)**: Enter a brief title for your form submission.
* **Name (name)**: Enter your name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., driver's license, passport).
* **Participant Type (participant_type)**: Indicate whether you are a participant in the lecture (Yes) or not (No).
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Participant Status (participant_status)**: Indicate your current status as a participant (Active or Inactive).
* **Recording Type (recording_type)**: Indicate whether you consent to being recorded during the lecture (Yes or No).
* **Share Participant Info (share_participant_info)**: Indicate whether you consent to sharing your participant information with others (Yes or No).
* **Record and Share? (record_and_share)**: Indicate whether you consent to recording and sharing your lectures with others (Yes or No).
* **Share Lectures with (share_lectures_with)**: Indicate which lectures you consent to share with others (Yes or No).
* **Share Lectures on (share_lectures_on)**: Indicate which platforms you consent to share your lectures on (Yes or No).
* **Record Participant Consent (record_participant_consent)**: Confirm your consent to being recorded as a participant (Yes or No).
