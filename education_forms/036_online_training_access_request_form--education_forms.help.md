# Online Training Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request access to online training programs. Fill out this form completely and accurately to ensure a timely and successful access request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Program Name**, which is the title of the training program you are requesting access to.
2. Select your **Access Level**, which determines the type of access you are requesting. Select from one of the following options:
	* Internal Access: Access to internal training programs only.
	* External Access: Access to external training programs.
	* Admin Access: Access to both internal and external training programs.
3. Choose the **Training Type** that best describes the type of training you are requesting. Select from the following options:
	* Online: Online training programs.
	* In Person: In Person training programs.
	* Phone: Phone-based training programs.
4. Provide your **User Name**, which is the name of the person requesting access.
5. Select your **User Role**, which is the title or position of the person requesting access.
6. Choose your **Department**, which is the department that will be accessing the training program.
7. Provide the **Training Location**, where the training program will take place.
8. Select the **Training Provider**, which is the organization or individual offering the training program.
9. Choose the **Training Topic**, which describes the subject matter of the training program.
10. Provide the **Request Date** and **Request Time** for when you would like to access the training program.
11. Optionally, provide **Notes** regarding any additional information you would like to include in your request.
12. Finally, provide your **Email** address and **Phone**, which will be used to communicate with you regarding your access request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Name**: The title of the training program you are requesting access to.
* **Access Level**: Determines the type of access you are requesting. Select from:
	+ Internal Access: Access to internal training programs only.
	+ External Access: Access to external training programs.
	+ Admin Access: Access to both internal and external training programs.
* **Training Type**: The type of training you are requesting. Select from:
	+ Online: Online training programs.
	+ In Person: In Person training programs.
	+ Phone: Phone-based training programs.
* **User Name**: The name of the person requesting access.
* **User Role**: The title or position of the person requesting access.
* **Department**: The department that will be accessing the training program.
* **Training Location**: Where the training program will take place.
* **Training Provider**: The organization or individual offering the training program.
* **Training Topic**: The subject matter of the training program.
* **Request Date**: When you would like to access the training program.
* **Request Time**: When you would like to access the training program.
* **Notes**: Any additional information you would like to include in your request.
* **Email**: The email address used to communicate with you regarding your access request.
* **Phone**: The phone number used to communicate with you regarding your access request.
* **Assigned Tool**: Not currently applicable, but may be used in the future for internal tools assigned to the training program.
