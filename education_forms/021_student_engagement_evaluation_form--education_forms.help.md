# student-engagement-evaluation-form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The "student-engagement-evaluation-form" is designed to collect feedback from students to evaluate their learning experience. It is meant to be completed by students to help instructors and educational administrators understand their needs and challenges.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start with the "Introduction" page, which provides a brief overview of the form and its purpose.
2. On the "What Have You Learned" page, share a brief description of what you have learned from the program or course.
3. On the "How Fully Engaged" page, select all the levels that apply to your current engagement with the program:
	* Low
	* Medium
	* High
4. If you have any feedback or challenges, please share your thoughts on the "What Did You Find Most Challenging" page.
5. If you have any suggestions for improvement, share them on the "What Suggestions For Improvement" page.
6. If you have any additional comments, please share them on the "Any Comments" page.
7. On the "What Is Your Username" page, please enter your username or email address so that we can contact you to follow up on your feedback.
8. Finally, select "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Text, Optional): A brief description of the form and its purpose.
* **What Have You Learned** (Text, Required): Share a brief description of what you have learned from the program or course.
* **How Fully Engaged** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select all the levels that apply to your current engagement with the program:
	+ Low
	+ Medium
	+ High
* **What Did You Find Most Challenging** (Text, Optional): Share any feedback or challenges you faced during the program or course.
* **What Suggestions For Improvement** (Text, Optional): Share any suggestions you have for improving the program or course.
* **Any Comments** (Text, Optional): Share any additional comments or thoughts you have.
* **What Is Your Username** (Email, Optional): Enter your username or email address so that we can contact you to follow up on your feedback.
* **Submit** (Select One, Required): Select this option to complete the form and submit your feedback.

## Tips

* Please complete this form honestly and to the best of your abilities.
* You can submit your feedback without providing a username, but your email address will be used to contact you for follow-up.
* If you have any difficulties or concerns, please contact the course administrators.

Note: This help guide is provided to assist users in completing the form correctly.
