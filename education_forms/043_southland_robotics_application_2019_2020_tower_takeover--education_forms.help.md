# southland_robotics_application_2019_2020_tower_takeover - Help Guide
## Purpose
The southland_robotics_application_2019_2020_tower_takeover form is designed to collect job preferences and details for Southland Robotics' internal use. This form aims to gather information about your job preferences, contact details, job status, and other relevant job attributes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the job preferences fields with your desired job attributes (e.g., job title, job type, job status).
2. Select the relevant contact details (e.g., Phone, Email, Address).
3. Enter any additional job details in the job details fields.
4. Review and submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### job_preferences (1)
* **Job Preferences** (`job_preferences`, `text`, optional): Enter your preferred job attributes, such as job title, job type, or job status.

### contact_details (3)
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your preferred contact details, such as Phone, Email, or Address.

### job_details (4)
* **Job Details** (`job_details`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional job details as desired.

### job_title (6)
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `select_one`, optional): Select your preferred job title from the options provided.

### job_type (11)
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your preferred job type, such as Full Time, Part Time, or Contract.

### job_status (18)
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your preferred job status, such as Active, On Hold, or Retired.

In this form, most fields are optional, so you can fill in as much or as little information as you like. If you need help or have questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
