# Parent Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parent Information Form is designed to collect information about a parent or guardian's contact details and occupation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form on the designated page.
2. Enter the parent's name in the "Parent Name" field.
3. Provide the parent's phone number in the "Parent Phone" field.
4. Enter the parent's email address in the "Parent E-mail" field.
5. Enter the parent's occupation in the "Parent Occupation" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required: false): Enter the parent's name.
- **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the parent's phone number.
- **Parent E-mail** (`parent_email`, email, required: false): Enter the parent's email address.
- **Parent Occupation** (`parent_occupation`, text, required: false): Enter the parent's occupation.
- **Parent Info** (`parent_info`, note, required: false): This field is for any additional information you'd like to share about the parent.
