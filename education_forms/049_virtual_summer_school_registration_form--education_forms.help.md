# Virtual Summer School Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for the registration of students in a virtual summer school program. Please ensure you are eligible to register before proceeding. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each field carefully and provide accurate information for each field. 
2. Enter your information in the required fields marked as "Required". 
3. If you are unsure about any field, contact the program administrators for assistance. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, Required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification. 
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, Required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification. 
* **Email** (`email`, email, Required): Enter your email address. 
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, Required): Enter your contact phone number. 
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, Required): Enter your student ID number. 
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_one, Required): Please select your current grade level. 
* **Course** (`course`, select_multiple, Required): Select the course(s) you wish to enroll in. 
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, Required): Enter your date of birth. 
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, Required): Enter the start date of the virtual summer school program. 
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, Required): Enter the end date of the virtual summer school program. 
* **Time** (`time`, time, Required): Enter the time you are available for the virtual summer school program. 
* **Note** (`note`, note, Optional): Please add any additional notes or comments. 
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, Optional): If you have been assigned a tool, please enter it here. 
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, Optional): This is a generated ID for your registration. 
* **Output File** (`output_file`, text, Optional): You will receive a generated file with your information and registration details. 

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form. 
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the program administrators. 
* Please complete the form in one session, as it may not be saved if you navigate away. 

This form has been designed to collect necessary information for your virtual summer school program registration. Please fill it out carefully and accurately. If you have any questions or need assistance, contact the program administrators. 

Note: This help guide is intended to assist end-users in completing the form. The form's fields and their labels are based on the provided YAML fields. If any field label is unclear, an effort has been made to explain the field's best-effort intent.
