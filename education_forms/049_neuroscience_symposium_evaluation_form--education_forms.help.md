# Neuroscience Symposium Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Neuroscience Symposium Evaluation Form is used to collect feedback from attendees about their experience at the symposium. This form aims to gauge attendee satisfaction, gather feedback on speaker performance, topics, and organization, and collect contact information for follow-up purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the best way you can, providing as much detail as possible.
2. For questions with dropdown options, select the option that best matches your opinion.
3. If you have any additional comments or feedback, use the additional comments field to provide more information.
4. Finally, fill in your email and phone number to allow us to follow up with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Symposium Questions
* **Symposium questions** (Text): This field allows you to provide any questions or concerns you may have about the symposium. Please be as specific as possible in your response.
### Session Attendance
* **Session Attendance** (Number): Please indicate how many sessions you attended during the symposium.
### Speaker Performance
* **Speaker Performance** (Text): This field is to gather feedback on the speaker's performance during the symposium.
### Speaker Topics
* **Speaker Topics** (Select One): Please select the speaker topic that resonated with you the most.
### Symposium Organization
* **Symposium Organization** (Text): This field is for you to provide any feedback on the symposium organization, such as venue, logistics, etc.
### Overall Satisfaction
* **Overall Satisfaction** (Select Multiple): Please select all that apply to indicate your level of satisfaction with the symposium.
### Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments** (Note): This field is for you to provide any additional comments or feedback about the symposium.
### Email
* **Email** (Email): Please enter your email address so that we can follow up with you about your feedback.
### Phone
* **Phone** (Text): Please enter your phone number so that we can follow up with you about your feedback.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible in your responses, but keep them concise enough to fit within the form fields.
* If you have any issues with the form or your feedback, please contact us at [insert contact info].
* We appreciate your feedback and value your input to make future symposia better!
