# Student Support Team Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Support Team Referral Form is a tool used to refer students to the support team for assistance. This form gathers information about the student, the reason for referral, and other relevant details that help the support team provide effective support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form instructions carefully to understand its purpose and the required information.
2. Enter the student's name in the **Student Name** field.
3. Enter the student's ID number in the **Student ID** field.
4. Provide a brief explanation of why the student is being referred in the **Why are you referring the student?** field.
5. Select the referral date in the **Referral Date** field.
6. Review your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Why are you referring the student?** (text, required): This field requires a brief explanation of why the student is being referred to the support team. Please be as specific as possible to help the support team understand the reason for the referral.
- **Student Name** (text, required): Enter the student's name in this field.
- **Student ID** (number, required): Enter the student's ID number in this field.
- **Referral Date** (date, required): Select the date of the referral by clicking on the calendar icon or entering the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure that the support team can provide the best possible assistance to the student.
- If you are unsure about any information, contact the support team for guidance.
- Double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid mistakes.
