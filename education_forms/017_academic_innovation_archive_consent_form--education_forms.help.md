# Academic Innovation Archive Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain consent from contributors to store and archive their materials in the Academic Innovation Archive.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your name in the "Contributor name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Contributor email" field.
3. Provide a brief description of the materials you are submitting in the "Description of material" field.
4. Indicate your rights and permissions held for the submitted materials in the "Rights and permissions held" field.
5. Confirm your consent to store the materials in the "Consent to store materials indefinitely" field.
6. If necessary, specify any access restrictions for the materials in the "Access restrictions requested" field.
7. State your preference for citation in the "Citation preference" field.
8. Sign your name in the "Signature name" field to confirm your consent.
9. Enter the date of your consent in the "Signature date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contributor name** (`contributor_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Contributor email** (`contributor_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address for archive correspondence.
* **Description of material** (`material_description`, text, required): Briefly describe the materials you are submitting.
* **Rights and permissions held** (`rights_and_permissions`, select_one, required): Select the correct option that describes your rights and permissions held for the submitted materials.
* **Consent to store materials indefinitely** (`consent_to_store`, select_one, required): Confirm your consent to store the materials in the archive.
* **Access restrictions requested** (`access_restrictions`, text, optional): Specify any access restrictions or embargoes you require for the submitted materials.
* **Citation preference** (`citation_preference`, text, optional): State your preference for citation.
* **Signature name** (`signature_name`, text, required): Type your full name as it appears on official documents to confirm your consent.
* **Signature date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your consent to store materials in the archive.
