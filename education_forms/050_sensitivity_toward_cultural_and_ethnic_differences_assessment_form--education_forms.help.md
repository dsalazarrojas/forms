# Sensitivity Toward Cultural And Ethnic Differences Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users assess their sensitivity and awareness of cultural and ethnic differences. It aims to gauge their understanding and behavior in various situations related to cultural and ethnic differences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer all questions honestly and thoroughly, taking into account your current attitudes and behaviors.
2. Choose the correct responses based on your personal experience and understanding.
3. Ensure all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title (`form_title`, text, required):** Enter a title for your assessment, which will be used to identify your response.
* **Introduction Paragraph (`intro_paragraph`, text, optional):** Provide a brief overview of your current thoughts and experiences related to cultural and ethnic differences. This will help us understand your perspective.
* **Scale 1 (`scale_1`, number, required):** Rate your sensitivity to cultural and ethnic differences on a scale of 1-5, where 1 represents a lack of sensitivity and 5 represents high sensitivity.
* **Scale 2 (`scale_2`, text, required):** Describe a situation where you felt sensitive or insensitive to cultural and ethnic differences.
* **Scale 3 (`scale_3`, number, required):** Rate your willingness to learn about and engage with people from different cultural backgrounds on a scale of 1-5.
* **Scale 4 (`scale_4`, text, required):** Describe a situation where you felt comfortable or uncomfortable interacting with someone from a different cultural background.
* **Scale 5 (`scale_5`, select_one, required):** Have you ever felt embarrassed or ashamed about cultural or ethnic differences? Yes/No.
* **Scale 6 (`scale_6`, number, required):** Rate your ability to adapt to different cultural norms and customs on a scale of 1-5.
* **Scale 7 (`scale_7`, select_multiple, required):** Which of the following behaviors demonstrate your sensitivity to cultural and ethnic differences? (Select all that apply)
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Scale 8 (`scale_8`, date, required):** Date of your first cultural exchange or interaction with someone from a different background.
* **Scale 9 (`scale_9`, time, optional):** Time of day when you felt sensitive or insensitive to cultural and ethnic differences.
* **Scale 10 (`scale_10`, select_one, required):** Do you believe cultural and ethnic differences are a barrier to communication? Yes/No.
* **Scale 11 (`scale_11`, email, required):** Provide your email address for follow-up questions or further discussion.
* **Scale 12 (`scale_12`, text, optional):** Additional comments or thoughts about your sensitivity to cultural and ethnic differences.
* **Scale 13 (`scale_13`, select_multiple, required):** Which of the following statements describe your behavior in a culturally diverse environment? (Select all that apply)
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Scale 14 (`scale_14`, date, required):** Date of your most significant cultural exchange or interaction.
* **Scale 15 (`scale_15`, time, optional):** Time of day when you felt sensitive or insensitive to cultural and ethnic differences.
* **Scale 16 (`scale_16`, number, required):** Rate your willingness to engage in discussions about cultural and ethnic differences on a scale of 1-5.
* **Scale 17 (`scale_17`, select_one, required):** Do you believe cultural and ethnic differences are a natural aspect of human experience? Yes/No.
* **Scale 18 (`scale_18`, email, required):** Provide your email address for follow-up questions or further discussion.
* **Scale 19 (`scale_19`, text, required):** Describe a situation where you overcame a cultural or ethnic difference.
* **Scale 20 (`scale_20`, number, optional):** Rate your ability to handle conflicts related to cultural and ethnic differences on a scale of 1-5.

Note: Please ensure you complete all required fields to ensure accurate assessment.
