# School Admission Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The School Admission Checklist Form is designed to collect essential information from students and their parents during the admission process in schools. This form serves as a comprehensive guide to ensure that all necessary details are captured accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your student's name in the "student_name" field.
2. Provide your student's date of birth in the "student_dob" field. Please use the date format YYYY-MM-DD.
3. Fill in your contact details in the "parent_contact_details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **student_name** (text, required): This is the student's full name. Please enter your student's full name as it appears on their official documents.
* **student_dob** (date, required): This field is for your student's date of birth. Please use the format YYYY-MM-DD (e.g., 1999-01-01).
* **parent_contact_details** (text, required): This is an optional field for parents or guardians to provide their contact information, such as email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully to ensure a smooth and efficient admission process.
* If you are unable to access the form online, please contact the school's administration for assistance.
* You can save and revisit your form responses at any time to make edits before submission.
