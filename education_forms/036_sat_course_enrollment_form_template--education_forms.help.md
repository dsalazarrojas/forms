# sat-course-enrollment-form-template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sat-course-enrollment-form-template form is used to collect data from users for course enrollment purposes, specifically for the Specialized Aptitude Test (SAT) course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your course information, including the course name, description, or any other relevant details.
2. Enter your contact information, including your name and email address.
3. Enter your payment information, including the payment method and any other relevant details.
4. Select the type of enrollment you wish to make (e.g. individual, group, etc.).
5. Add any additional notes or comments you'd like to share with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Information (page 1)**:
	+ Enter your course name or description.
* **Contact Information (page 2)**:
	+ Enter your name and a valid email address to ensure we can contact you for further details.
* **Payment Information (page 3)**:
	+ Enter the payment method and any other relevant details.
* **Enrollment Type (page 4)**:
	+ Select the type of enrollment you wish to make (e.g. individual, group, etc.).
* **Notes (page 5)**:
	+ Add any additional comments or notes you'd like to share with us.
* **Email Suffix (page 6)**:
	+ Enter any email suffix or tag if you'd like to be contacted with a specific email account (e.g. @example.com).

## Tips
* Please ensure you've already expressed interest in taking the course before using this form.
* Use accurate and up-to-date information when filling out the form to avoid delays or issues with your enrollment.
