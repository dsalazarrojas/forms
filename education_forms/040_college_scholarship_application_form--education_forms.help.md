# College Scholarship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students applying for a college scholarship. It's essential to fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure that the applicant's eligibility for the scholarship is assessed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by carefully reading through the form and its requirements.
2. Fill out each field with the requested information, following the guidelines provided for each section.
3. Ensure that you've completed all required fields before submitting the form.
4. Review your submission for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Scholarship Name** (`Scholarship Name`, text, required)
The name of the scholarship you are applying for.

### **First Name** (`applicant_first_name`, text, required)
Your first name as it appears on your identification documents.

### **Last Name** (`applicant_last_name`, text, required)
Your last name as it appears on your identification documents.

### **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required)
Your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### **Student ID Number** (`student_id`, text, required)
Your student ID number, which can be found on your student ID card or student record.

### **Email Address** (`applicant_email`, email, required)
Your email address where the scholarship committee can contact you.

### **Phone Number** (`applicant_phone`, text, required)
Your phone number where the scholarship committee can contact you.

### **School or College Name** (`school_name`, text, required)
The name of the school or college you are currently attending or have attended.

### **Year in School** (`year_in_school`, select_one, required)
Your current year in school (Freshman, Sophomore, Junior, Senior, Graduate Student).

### **Major or Field of Study** (`major_field`, text, required)
The major or field of study you are currently pursuing or have been pursuing.

### **Current GPA** (`gpa`, number, required)
Your current GPA on a 4.0 scale.

### **Meet Minimum GPA Requirement** (`minimum_gpa_met`, select_one, required)
Do you meet the minimum GPA requirement for the scholarship? (Yes/No)

### **Level of Financial Need** (`financial_need`, select_one, required)
The level of financial need you have (Exceptional, Significant, Moderate, Limited).

### **Family Income Range** (`family_income_range`, select_one, optional)
The income range of your family (Under 25k, 25k-50k, 50k-75k, 75k-100k, 100k+).

### **Extracurricular Activities and Leadership** (`extracurricular_activities`, text, required)
A list of your extracurricular activities, sports, volunteer work, or leadership positions.

### **Community Service Hours** (`community_service`, number, optional)
The number of hours you've spent on community service.

### **Academic Honors and Awards** (`academic_honors`, text, optional)
Any academic honors or awards you've received.

### **Essay Question 1** (`essay_prompt_1`, text, required)
Write a 500-word essay on why you deserve this scholarship.

### **Essay Question 2** (`essay_prompt_2`, text, required)
Describe how this scholarship will impact your education in 500 words.

### **Personal Statement or Story** (`personal_statement`, text, optional)
Tell us about yourself in 750 words or less.

### **Reference 1 Name** (`reference_1_name`, text, required)
Name of your Reference 1 (teacher, counselor, or mentor).

### **Reference 1 Email** (`reference_1_email`, email, required)
Email of your Reference 1.

### **Reference 2 Name** (`reference_2_name`, text, required)
Name of your Reference 2 (teacher, counselor, or mentor).

### **Reference 2 Email** (`reference_2_email`, email, required)
Email of your Reference 2.

### **I Certify That All Information Is True and Accurate** (`application_agreement`, select_one, required)
Do you certify that all information is true and accurate? (Yes/No)

## Tips
- Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Ensure that you've completed all required fields.
- Proofread your essays for grammar, spelling, and clarity.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the scholarship committee for assistance.
- This form is intended for new submissions only, so please do not reuse or duplicate your application.
