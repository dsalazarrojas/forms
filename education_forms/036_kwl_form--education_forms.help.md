# KWL Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The KWL Form is a questionnaire designed to help educators and staff in the education field understand learners' preferences and goals. It's essential to complete this form to provide better support and services to learners.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the KWL Form, follow these steps:

1. **Introduction**: Provide a brief introduction about yourself and your learning goals. This field is optional.
2. **What do you want to learn about?**: Choose all the topics you're interested in learning more about. You can select multiple options.
3. **What do you already know?**: Select the topics you already have knowledge about. You can only select one option.
4. **What have you learned?**: Choose all the topics you've learned about. You can select multiple options.
5. **What is important to you?**: Choose how important each topic is to you, with options being "Very Important" or "Not Important".
6. **Why do you want to learn more?**: Provide a brief explanation about your motivation for learning.
7. **Additional comments**: If you have any additional information or comments, please provide them in this field.
8. **Email address**: If you'd like to be contacted, please provide your email address.
9. **Phone number**: If you'd like to be contacted, please provide your phone number.
10. **Name**: Provide your name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Text): A brief introduction about yourself and your learning goals.
* **What do you want to learn about?** (Select Multiple): Choose all the topics you're interested in learning more about.
	+ **Yes**: You're interested in learning more about this topic.
	+ **No**: You're not interested in learning more about this topic.
* **What do you already know?** (Select One): Select the topics you already have knowledge about.
	+ **Yes**: You have knowledge about this topic.
	+ **No**: You don't have knowledge about this topic.
* **What have you learned?** (Select Multiple): Choose all the topics you've learned about.
	+ **Yes**: You've learned about this topic.
	+ **No**: You haven't learned about this topic.
* **What is important to you?** (Select One): Choose how important each topic is to you.
	+ **Very Important**: This topic is very important to you.
	+ **Not Important**: This topic is not important to you.
* **Why do you want to learn more?** (Text): Provide a brief explanation about your motivation for learning.
* **Additional comments** (Note): If you have any additional information or comments, please provide them in this field.
* **Email address** (Email): If you'd like to be contacted, please provide your email address.
* **Phone number** (Text): If you'd like to be contacted, please provide your phone number.
* **Name** (Text): Provide your name.
