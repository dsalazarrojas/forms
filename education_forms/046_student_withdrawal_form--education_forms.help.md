# Student Withdrawal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Withdrawal Form is used to collect information from students who are withdrawing from a course. It's essential to ensure the form is completed accurately and thoroughly, as this information will be used to process the withdrawal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the Student ID: Enter the student's ID number as it appears on their student ID card or in our student information system.
2. Enter the Course ID: Enter the course ID number as it appears on the course schedule or in our course information system.
3. Select the Reason For Withdrawal: Choose the reason for withdrawal from the provided options:
	* Financial reasons
	* Academic reasons
	* Other (please specify in the comments section)
4. Enter the Date Of Withdrawal: Enter the date the student is withdrawing from the course, in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
5. Add Comments (optional): If you'd like to provide further context or explanations, please use this section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, required): Enter the student's ID number as it appears on their student ID card or in our student information system.
* **Course ID** (`course_id`, number, required): Enter the course ID number as it appears on the course schedule or in our course information system.
* **Reason For Withdrawal** (`reason_for_withdrawal`, select_one, optional): This field allows you to select the reason for withdrawal from the provided options. If you select "Other," please provide further explanation in the comments section.
* **Date Of Withdrawal** (`date_of_withdrawal`, date, optional): Enter the date the student is withdrawing from the course, in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): This field is for additional comments or explanations that provide further context for the withdrawal.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please ask for assistance from your department or supervisor.
* If you need to make changes to the form, please contact the form administrator.
