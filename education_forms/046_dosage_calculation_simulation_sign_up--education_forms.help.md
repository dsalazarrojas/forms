# Dosage Calculation Simulation Sign Up - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to sign up participants for a dosage calculation simulation exercise. It gathers information about the participant's demographic details, simulation preferences, and learning objectives.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your first name in the "participant_first_name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "participant_last_name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "participant_email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "participant_phone" field.
5. (Optional) Enter your learning goals and objectives in the "learning_goals" field.
6. Select your simulation type preference in the "simulation_type" field.
7. Select your preferred simulation dates in the "simulation_date_1" and "simulation_date_2" fields.
8. Select your preferred simulation time in the "simulation_time" field.
9. Select your preferred locations in the "location" field.
10. Enter your Participant ID in the "participant_did" field. (Optional)
11. Enter your age in the "participant_age" field.
12. Select your gender in the "participant_gender" field. (Optional)
13. Enter your job title in the "participant_job_title" field. (Optional)
14. Enter your department or affiliation in the "participant_department" field. (Optional)
15. Enter your job role in the "participant_job_role" field.
16. Select your preferred simulation duration in the "simulation_duration" field. (Optional)
17. Select your preferred facilitator in the "simulation_facilitator" field. (Optional)
18. Enter any additional notes about your participation in the "notes" field. (Optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Simulation Date**: This field is not present in the form, but it is actually the "simulation_date" field which is located at id: 1.
	+ Enter the date you prefer to participate in the simulation exercise.
* **Participant First Name**: Enter your given name in this field. (Required)
* **Participant Last Name**: Enter your surname in this field. (Required)
* **Participant Email**: Enter your email address in this field. (Required)
* **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number in this field.
* **Learning Goals**: (Optional) Enter any learning objectives or goals you have for participating in the simulation exercise.
* **Simulation Type**: Select the type of simulation you prefer to participate in.
* **Simulation Date 1**: Select the first date you prefer to participate in the simulation exercise.
* **Simulation Date 2**: Select the second date you prefer to participate in the simulation exercise.
* **Simulation Time**: Select the time you prefer to participate in the simulation exercise.
* **Location**: Select the location you prefer to participate in the simulation exercise.
* **Participant ID**: (Optional) Enter a unique identifier for yourself.
* **Participant Age**: Enter your age in this field. (Required)
* **Participant Gender**: Select your gender.
* **Participant Job Title**: (Optional) Enter your job title or position.
* **Participant Department**: (Optional) Enter your department or affiliation.
* **Participant Job Role**: Enter your job role or function.
* **Simulation Duration**: Select the duration of the simulation you prefer.
* **Simulation Facilitator**: Select the facilitator you prefer to work with.
* **Learning Objectives**: (Optional) Enter any learning objectives or goals you have for participating in the simulation exercise.
* **Notes**: (Optional) Enter any additional notes about your participation.

Note: The optional fields are those that are not marked as "required" in the form schema.
