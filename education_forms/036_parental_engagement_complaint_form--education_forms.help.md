# Parental Engagement Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parental Engagement Complaint Form is a tool for students to report incidents of poor parental engagement or concerns about their educational institution. This form will help the institution's administration to address and resolve these issues effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to include as much detail as possible in the complaint description.
3. Choose the relevant educational institution from the options provided.
4. Provide your contact information so that we can reach out to you for further clarification or resolution.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parental Engagement Complaint Form**: The title of the form that you're filling out.
* **Student Name** (student name): Please enter your name as a student of the institution.
* **Complaint Description** (complaint description): Describe the issue you're experiencing with your parent or guardian. Try to be as specific as possible to help us address the issue effectively.
* **Educational Institution** (educational institution): Choose the institution that you're reporting the complaint about from the list of options. This will help us identify which institution to address your complaint to.
* **Parent Contact Information** (parent contact info): Please provide your contact information so that we can reach out to you for further clarification or resolution.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* Avoid using profanity or derogatory language.
* Make sure to sign the form digitally or by hand, as proof of submission.
* If you're still unsure about any of the fields, please refer back to this guide for help.
