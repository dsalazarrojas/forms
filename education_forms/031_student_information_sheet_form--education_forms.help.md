# Student Information Sheet Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Information Sheet Form is designed to gather essential details about students, which are used to ensure accurate student data representation and to prevent duplication.
## How To Complete This Form
Before starting, please ensure you have the necessary information and documents required to fill out the form accurately. The form is divided into different sections that need to be filled out in the order of appearance. 

### Filling out the Basic Student Information Section
Please select the relevant option for the following questions:

* `Student Information Sheet` (Select "Yes" or "No" depending on your response to this question)
* `Grade Level` (Select either "High" or "Low" depending on the student's grade level)
* `School` (Select "Yes" or "No" depending on the student's school affiliation)

### Filling out the Contact Information Section
Please provide accurate and up-to-date contact information for the student, including:

* `First Name` and `Last Name` of the student
* `Email` of the student
* `Student Number`
* `Date of Birth`
* `Address`
* `Contact Number`

### Filling out the Emergency Contact Information Section
Please provide information about the student's emergency contact, including:

* `Emergency Contact`
* `Emergency Contact Address`
* `Emergency Contact Number`

### Filling out the Educational and Conduct History Section
Please provide any relevant educational and conduct history of the student, including:

* `Student Education History`
* `Student Attendance History`
* `Student Conduct History`

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information Sheet**: A multiple-choice question asking whether the student information sheet is relevant to this student.
* **First Name**: The student's first name.
* **Last Name**: The student's last name.
* **Email**: The student's email address.
* **Student Number**: A student number assigned to the student for identification purposes.
* **Date of Birth**: The student's date of birth.
* **School**: A multiple-choice question asking whether the student is affiliated with a school.
* **Grade Level**: A multiple-choice question asking about the student's grade level.
* **Parent Information**: A free-text field for any parent or guardian information.
* **Emergency Contact**: The name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Address**: The address of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Number**: The contact number of the emergency contact person.
* **Medical Information**: A free-text field for any medical information.
* **Address**: The student's home address.
* **Emergency Contact Information**: A free-text field for any additional emergency contact information.
* **Emergency Contact Number**: The contact number of the emergency contact person.
* **Student Education History**: A free-text field for any information about the student's education history.
* **Student Attendance History**: A free-text field for any information about the student's attendance history.
* **Student Conduct History**: A free-text field for any information about the student's conduct history.
* **Guardian Information**: A free-text field for any guardian information.
* **Guardian Relationship**: A multiple-choice question asking about the guardian's relationship to the student.
* **Guardian Contact**: The contact information of the guardian.
* **Guardian Address**: The address of the guardian.
* **Emergency Contact Information**: A free-text field for any additional emergency contact information.
* **Emergency Contact Number**: The contact number of the emergency contact person.
* **Student Notes**: A free-text field for any additional student notes.
* **Guardian Notes**: A free-text field for any guardian notes.

## Tips
- Always ensure that you have the necessary information and documents before filling out the form.
- Double-check your answers for accuracy.
- If you are unsure about any information, please reach out to the relevant authority for clarification.
