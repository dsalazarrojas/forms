# film_directing_admission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The film directing program admission form is used to collect application information from applicants. This form helps us to evaluate your suitability for the program and ensures that you provide all necessary details for review.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the respective text fields.
2. Provide your Email address in the Email field.
3. Enter your Phone Number in the Phone Number field.
4. Select your Date of Birth using the Date of Birth field.
5. Upload your Portfolio URL in the Portfolio URL field.
6. Attach your Resume in the Resume Upload field.
7. Write a Cover Letter explaining why you are a good fit for the film directing program in the Cover Letter field.
8. Attach your Transcript File, if applicable.
9. Attach Portfolio Files, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Film Directing Program Application** (form_group_1, text, required): This is the main application form where you will be asked to provide a brief description of why you are interested in the film directing program.
* **First Name** (first_name, text, optional): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (last_name, text, optional): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (email, email, optional): Enter your email address here, this will be used for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (phone_number, text, optional): Enter your phone number here.
* **Date of Birth** (date_of_birth, date, optional): Select your date of birth using the date field.
* **Portfolio URL** (portfolio_url, text, optional): Upload your portfolio URL here, if you have a website with your work samples.
* **Resume Upload** (resume_upload, text, optional): Attach your resume here, this will help us get to know you better.
* **Cover Letter** (cover_letter, text, optional): Write a brief cover letter explaining why you are a good fit for the film directing program.
* **Transcript File** (transcript_file, text, optional): Attach your transcript file here, if you have completed any relevant coursework.
* **Portfolio Files** (portfolio_files, text, optional): Attach your portfolio files here, if you have any work samples that demonstrate your skills. 

## Tips
* Ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you have any supporting materials, such as a resume, cover letter, and transcript, be sure to attach them to the form.
* Do not submit duplicate applications, as this will not expedite the review process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
