# History Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "History Quiz" form is designed to collect information about historical events, questions, and answers related to a specific historical period. This form is meant to be a general-purpose tool for capturing historical data, and it does not cover any specific historical period or topic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for the quiz in the "History Quiz" field. This field is optional and is used to provide a descriptive title for the quiz.
2. Answer each question in the following pages, providing your responses in the relevant text or select fields.
3. When you've completed all the questions, submit the form to review and score your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **History Quiz** (`history_quiz`, text, optional): Enter a descriptive title for the quiz.
* **First Quiz Question** (`question_1`, text, optional): Answer the first historical question.
* **Answer 1** (`answer_1`, select_one, optional): Select either "Yes" or "No" to answer the first question.
* **Second Quiz Question** (`question_2`, text, optional): Answer the second historical question.
* **Answer 2** (`answer_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the options ("Yes" or "No") to answer the second question.
* **Third Quiz Question** (`question_3`, date, optional): Enter the date of the historical event.
* **Fourth Quiz Question** (`question_4`, time, optional): Enter the time of the historical event.
* **Quiz Time** (`quiz_time`, number, optional): Enter the time taken to complete the quiz.
* **Score** (`score`, number, optional): Enter your score out of the total number of questions.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address (if you'd like to receive feedback on your quiz).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if you'd like to receive feedback on your quiz).
* **Submit** (`submit`, note, optional): Click to submit your answers for review and scoring.

## Tips

* This form is meant to be a general-purpose tool for capturing historical data, so feel free to ask any historical questions you like.
* You can enter any historical date or time in the format you prefer, but make sure to enter it correctly.
* Remember to enter your email and phone number if you'd like to receive feedback on your quiz.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your administrator for assistance.
