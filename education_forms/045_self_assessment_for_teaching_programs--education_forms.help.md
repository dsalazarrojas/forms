# Self Assessment For Teaching Programs - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help educators evaluate their teaching practices, provide feedback on their own teaching, and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, you will be presented with a series of text fields for you to answer. Please fill out each field, providing as much detail as possible in the designated text boxes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Teaching Practices** (`teaching_practices`): Please describe your current teaching practices, including methods, techniques, and strategies you use in your classes.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`): Share any feedback or insights you have about your own teaching, such as what works well and what doesn't.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`): Identify areas where you feel you need to improve, whether it's related to your teaching style, lesson plans, or interactions with students.
* **Teaching Practices 2** (`teaching_practices_2`): Describe any additional teaching practices, methods, or techniques you use.
* **Feedback 2** (`feedback_2`): Provide feedback or insights on your teaching, including any successes or challenges you've faced.
* **Areas for Improvement 2** (`areas_for_improvement_2`): Reflect on areas where you think you need to improve.
* **Teaching Practices 3** (`teaching_practices_3`): Share your approaches to teaching, including any new ideas or methods you've tried.
* **Feedback 3** (`feedback_3`): Share feedback or insights on your teaching, including any positive or negative experiences.
* **Areas for Improvement 3** (`areas_for_improvement_3`): Identify areas where you can develop further.
* **Teaching Practices 4** (`teaching_practices_4`): Describe any new or innovative teaching practices you've adopted.
* **Feedback 4** (`feedback_4`): Reflect on your feedback, including any areas of improvement you've identified.
* **Areas for Improvement 4** (`areas_for_improvement_4`): Summarize areas where you feel you can improve.

## Tips
* Please be honest in your self-assessment and feedback. This will help ensure that your self-assessment is accurate and helpful for you.
* Take your time to fill out each field, providing as much detail as possible.
* If you're unsure about what to write in a field, try to recall specific events, conversations, or experiences related to your teaching practices.
