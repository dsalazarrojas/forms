# Educational Leave Time Off Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Educational Leave Time Off Form is used by employees to request time off from their work for educational purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your unique Employee ID in the `Employee ID` field.
2. Select the reason(s) for your leave from the `Prophets` field.
3. Enter a brief description of the reason for your leave in the `Reason` field.
4. Enter the start and end dates of your leave in the `Start Date` and `End Date` fields, respectively.
5. Enter the number of days you will be absent in the `Number of Days` field.
6. Select the status of your leave request in the `Status` field.
7. Enter any additional comments about your leave request in the `Comments` field.
8. Enter your email address in the `Submitted By` field.
9. Enter the email address of the person who created the request in the `Created By` field.
10. Enter the email address of the person who last updated the request in the `Updated By` field.
11. Enter the date when the request was submitted in the `Submitted Date` field.
12. Enter the email address of the person who approved the request in the `Approved By` field.
13. Enter the date when the request was last updated in the `Updated Date` field.
14. Enter the date when the request was approved in the `Approved Date` field.
15. (Optional) Select a reason for change in the `Reason for Change` field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `number`, required): Enter your unique employee ID.
* **Prophets** (`prophets`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or more reasons for leave from the available options.
* **Reason** (`reason`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the reason for your leave.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date when your leave starts.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date when your leave ends.
* **Number of Days** (`num_days`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of days you will be absent.
* **Status** (`status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the status of your leave request.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments about your leave request.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the person who created the request.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the person who last updated the request.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date when the request was submitted.
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the person who approved the request.
* **Updated Date** (`updated_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date when the request was last updated.
* **Approved Date** (`approved_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date when the request was approved.
* **Reason for Change** (`reason_for_change`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or more reasons for change from the available options.
