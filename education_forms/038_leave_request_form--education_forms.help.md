# Leave Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Leave Request Form is used to collect information about an employee's leave request, which will be reviewed and processed by the management team.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the start date of your leave in the **Start Date** field. This is the date you would like to start your leave.
2. Enter the end date of your leave in the **End Date** field. This is the date you would like to end your leave.
3. Select the **Leave Type** from the dropdown menu. Please choose 'Yes' if you are requesting a leave, 'No' if you are not.
4. Provide a brief **Reason** for your leave request in the text field. Please be specific about why you need to take a leave.
5. Enter your **Employee ID** number.
6. Enter your **Name** as it appears on your employee record.
7. Enter your **Department** as it appears on your employee record.
8. Enter your manager's **Email Address** so that they can be contacted regarding your leave request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required/optional: no): Enter the date you would like to start your leave.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required/optional: no): Enter the date you would like to end your leave.
* **Leave Type** (`leave_type`, select_multiple, required/optional: no): Select 'Yes' if you are requesting a leave, 'No' if you are not.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, required/optional: no): Provide a brief reason for your leave request.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required/optional: no): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required/optional: no): Enter your name as it appears on your employee record.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required/optional: no): Enter your department as it appears on your employee record.
* **Manager** (`manager`, text, required/optional: no): Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, required/optional: no): Enter your manager's email address.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have the necessary approval from your manager before submitting your leave request.
* Be specific about your reason for requesting a leave so that it can be reviewed and processed efficiently.
* If you have any supporting documents or attachments, you can attach them to your leave request.
