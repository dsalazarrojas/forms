# Power Skills Course Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for a Power Skills Course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your registration information in the **Registration Information** field.
2. Provide your participant details in the **Participant Details** field.
3. Specify your learning needs in the **Learning Needs** field.
4. Select the course you want to register for in the **Course** field.
5. Select any additional learning needs that apply from the **Learning Needs 1** and **Learning Needs 2** fields.
6. Add any additional participant notes in the **Participant Notes** field.
7. Submit your registration by clicking the **Submit** button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Registration Information** (`Registration Information`, `text`, required: false)
Provide a brief description of your registration purpose.

### **Participant Details** (`Participant Details`, `text`, required: false)
Enter your participant details, including your name and any additional information you'd like to share.

### **Participant Name** (`Participant Name`, `text`, required: false)
Enter your name.

### **Participant Email** (`Participant Email`, `email`, required: false)
Enter your email address.

### **Participant Phone** (`Participant Phone`, `text`, required: false)
Enter your phone number.

### **Participant Address** (`Participant Address`, `text`, required: false)
Enter your address.

### **Participant City** (`Participant City`, `text`, required: false)
Enter your city.

### **Participant State** (`Participant State`, `select_one`, required: false)
Select your state of residence from the dropdown list.

### **Course** (`Course`, `select_one`, required: false)
Select the Power Skills Course you wish to register for.

### **Learning Needs 1** (`Learning Needs 1`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
Select any additional learning needs that apply from the dropdown list.

### **Learning Needs 2** (`Learning Needs 2`, `text`, required: false)
Enter any additional learning needs that don't appear in the dropdown list.

### **Participant Notes** (`Participant Notes`, `text`, required: false)
Add any additional notes about your registration.

### **Submit** (`Submit`, `text`, required: false)
Click the submit button to complete your registration.
