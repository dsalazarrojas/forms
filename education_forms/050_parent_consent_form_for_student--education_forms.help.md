# Parent Consent Form For Student - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from a parent or guardian for their child to participate in extracurricular activities within a school. The form ensures that both the parent and the school are in agreement about the child's involvement in activities such as sports teams, clubs, or other programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and your child's name in the corresponding fields.
2. Select a date for the consent to be valid from the calendar.
3. Choose the school where your child is enrolled from the options provided.
4. Indicate whether your child is participating in extracurricular activities by answering 'Yes' or 'No'.
5. If your child is participating, fill in the name of the activity they are taking part in.
6. If applicable, fill in the duration and hours per day your child will be involved in the activity.
7. Add any additional notes about your child's participation.
8. Finally, fill in your email address and phone number so that we can contact you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): This field is where you fill in your name as a parent or guardian.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): This field is where you fill in your child's name.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): This field is where you select the date for the consent to be valid.
* **School** (`school`, text, required): This field is where you choose the school where your child is enrolled.
* **Grade** (`grade`, select_one, required): This field is where you indicate whether your child is participating in extracurricular activities by answering 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Activity Name** (`activity_name`, text, required): This field is where you fill in the name of the activity your child is participating in.
* **Duration** (`duration`, number, required): This field is where you fill in the duration of the activity your child is participating in.
* **Hours Per Day** (`hours_per_day`, number, required): This field is where you fill in the hours per day your child will be involved in the activity.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): This field is where you add any additional notes about your child's participation.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): This field is where you fill in your email address so that we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): This field is where you fill in your phone number so that we can contact you.

## Tips

* Be accurate when filling in your child's name and grade level.
* Make sure to select the correct date for the consent to be valid.
* Double-check the school your child is enrolled in.
* If your child is participating in extracurricular activities, ensure you fill in all required fields accurately.
