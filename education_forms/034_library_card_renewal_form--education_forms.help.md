# Library Card Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Library Card Renewal Form is used to renew an existing library card for a library patron. This form helps to ensure that the card is up-to-date and active for future use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name and email address to help us verify your identity.
2. Provide your phone number so we can contact you with any questions or updates.
3. Enter the expiration date of your current library card.
4. Enter your library card number for reference.
5. Select a new expiration date for your library card.
6. If you wish to pay for the renewal, select your preferred payment method and enter the amount to be paid.
7. Add any comments or notes about your library card renewal.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Library Card Renewal Form**: This is the main title of the form and is used to identify the purpose of the form.
* **User Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your library card.
* **User Email**: Enter your email address as it appears on your library card or the one you use to contact you.
* **User Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you with any updates or questions.
* **Expiration Date**: Enter the expiration date of your current library card.
* **Library Card Number**: Enter the number on your library card for reference.
* **Renewal Date**: Select a new expiration date for your library card. This will be the new expiration date for your library card.
* **Payment Method**: If you wish to pay for the renewal, select one of the available payment methods (payment option 1, 2, or 3). This field is optional.
* **Payment Amount**: If you selected a payment method, enter the amount to be paid. This field is optional.
* **Comments**: Add any comments or notes about your library card renewal. This field is optional.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately to avoid any issues with your library card renewal.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your local library for assistance.
* You can only use this form once per library card renewal.
