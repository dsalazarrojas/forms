# Gamified Learning Experiment Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect participant demographics and motivations for a gamified learning experiment. It is intended to be completed by participants, not researchers.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, fill out the Participant Details section with your relevant information.
2.  Move on to the Motivations section, where you will select the reasons why you want to participate in this experiment.
3.  In the Consent section, choose whether you consent to participate in the study. If you choose to participate, please provide your contact information.
4.  In the Researcher Information section, choose the researcher that you are interested in working with.
5.  Finally, review the other sections to ensure you have filled out all the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details**: This section is where you will provide your basic demographic information, including age.
* **Motivations**: This section is where you will indicate your reasons for participating in the experiment.
* **Consent**: Choose whether you agree to participate in the experiment. If you do not wish to participate, you can select "No" and skip the other sections that follow.
* **Participant Age**: This field is where you will enter your age in years. Please enter a whole number.
* **Research Interests**: Here, you will select the areas of research that interest you the most.
* **Participant Incentives**: This field is for you to select what motivates you to participate in the experiment.
* **Participant Consent Text**: This field is for you to provide more details about your consent.
* **Researcher Information**: This section is where you will select the researcher that you would like to work with.
* **Researcher Name**: This field is for you to enter your name.
* **Researcher Email**: Enter your email address here.
* **Researcher Phone**: Enter your phone number here.
* **Research Group**: Select the research group that you would like to work with.
* **Experiment Name**: Enter a name for your research experiment.
* **Study Duration**: Select the duration of your study.
* **Participant Gender**: Select your gender.
* **Participant Ethnicity**: Select your ethnicity.
* **Participant Disability**: Select any disability that you have.
* **Participant Language**: Select your native language.
