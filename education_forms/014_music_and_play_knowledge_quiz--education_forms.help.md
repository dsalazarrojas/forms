# Music And Play Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form, "Music And Play Knowledge Quiz", is designed to collect information about users' musical preferences and habits, including how often they listen to music, their favorite musicians, instruments, music genres, and more. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose how often you listen to music from the options on "How often do you listen to music?" (Q1).
2. Select your favorite musician from the options on "Who is your favorite musician?" (Q2).
3. Choose the instruments you play from the options on "Which instrument do you usually play?" (Q3).
4. If you play music, indicate how long you have been playing from the options on "How long have you been playing music?" (Q4). 
5. Select your favorite music genre from the options on "What is your favorite music genre?" (Q5).
6. Choose how often you go to concerts from the options on "How often do you go to concerts?" (Q6).
7. Select your favorite music style from the options on "Which music style do you like best?" (Q7).
8. Indicate how you usually purchase music from the options on "How do you usually purchase music?" (Q8).
9. If you have a favorite music artist, select them from the options on "What is your favorite music artist?" (Q9). 
10. Choose how often you go to music festivals from the options on "How often do you go to music festivals?" (Q10).
11. If you usually listen to music, indicate how long you listen for from the options on "How long do you usually listen to music?" (Q11).
12. Select your favorite music festival from the options on "What is your favorite music festival?" (Q12).
