# Student Information Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for school administrators to collect necessary information from students.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field with the corresponding information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out, unless otherwise specified.
3. Use the options provided to select the correct information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter the student's full name.
* **Student Class** (`student_class`, select_one, optional): Select the student's class year from the provided options.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, select_one, optional): Select the student's grade level from the provided options.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, optional): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, optional): Enter the student's email address.
* **Additional Information** (`student_note`, note, optional): Enter any additional information about the student.
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, text, optional): Enter the student's address.
* **School Administrative Contact** (`school_administrative_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name of the school's administrative contact.
* **School** (`student_school`, text, optional): Enter the name of the student's school.
* **School Principal** (`school_principal`, text, optional): Enter the name of the school's principal.
* **School Department** (`school_department`, text, optional): Enter the name of the school's department.
* **School Position** (`school_position`, text, optional): Enter the school administrative contact's position.
* **School Administrative Contact Title** (`school_administrative_contact_title`, text, optional): Enter the administrative contact's title.
* **School Administrative Contact Phone** (`school_administrative_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the administrative contact's phone number.
* **School Administrative Contact Email** (`school_administrative_contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the administrative contact's email address.

## Tips
* Please fill out all fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact the school's administrative contact.
* Please review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all information is complete and accurate.
