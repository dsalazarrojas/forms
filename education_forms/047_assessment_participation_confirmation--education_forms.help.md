# Assessment Participation Confirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to confirm your participation in the assessment and gather necessary information for the assessment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Participant Full Name** in the first field. This is the name of the participant who is scheduled to take the assessment.
2. Enter the **Participant ID or Email Address** in the second field. This is the participant's ID or email address that will be used for communication and proctoring purposes.
3. Select the **Assessment Title** from the provided options in the third field. This ensures that you're confirming participation for the correct assessment.
4. Enter the **Scheduled Date and Time** in the fourth field. This is the date and time you have scheduled for the assessment.
5. Choose your **Attendance Confirmation** from the options: "Yes, I Confirm" or "No, I need to reschedule". If you choose "Yes, I Confirm", you're committing to attend the assessment at the scheduled time. If you select "No, I need to reschedule", you'll be able to provide a reason for rescheduling.
6. Select **I consent to the assessment terms and conditions** from the options: "I Consent" or "Other". This is your agreement to abide by the assessment's rules and regulations.
7. If the assessment will be conducted online, confirm if you have access to a **stable internet connection**. Select "True" if you have a stable internet connection or "False" otherwise.
8. Choose the device you'll be using for the assessment from the options: **Laptop/Desktop Computer**, **Tablet Device**, **Smartphone**, or **Provided Lab Computer**.
9. Provide the **Emergency Contact Name and Phone** in case of any technical or personal issues during the session.
10. If you're comfortable with the possibility of your assessment session being recorded for proctoring purposes, select **I Acknowledge**. If not, choose "Other".
11. Finally, enter the **Date of Confirmation**, which is the date you're confirming your participation for the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name** (required): Enter the full name of the participant who is scheduled to take the assessment.
* **Participant ID or Email Address** (required): Enter the participant's ID or email address for communication and proctoring purposes.
* **Assessment Title** (required): Select the correct assessment title to confirm participation.
* **Scheduled Date and Time** (required): Enter the date and time of the scheduled assessment.
* **Attendance Confirmation** (required): Confirm or express your need to reschedule the assessment.
* **I consent to the assessment terms and conditions** (required): Agree or disagree with the assessment's terms and conditions.
* **I have access to a stable internet connection** (not required): If conducting an online assessment, confirm or deny having a stable internet connection.
* **Which device will you be using for the assessment** (required): Select the device you'll be using for the assessment.
* **Emergency Contact Name and Phone** (required): Provide the emergency contact name and phone number in case of any issues during the session.
* **I acknowledge that this session may be recorded for proctoring purposes** (not required): Confirm or disagree with the possibility of your session being recorded.
* **Date of Confirmation** (required): Enter the date you're confirming your participation for the assessment.
