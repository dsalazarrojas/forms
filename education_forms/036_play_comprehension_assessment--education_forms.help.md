# Play Comprehension Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to assess the audience's understanding of a play. It aims to gather information about their overall impression, recommended level, and other specific details regarding the play's characters, plot, setting, and performance. The form is used to evaluate the audience's comprehension and provide feedback to the play's creators and organizers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in the form with your honest and accurate opinions about the play you have watched.
2. Select the correct answers for each question, based on your understanding and impression of the play.
3. Provide additional comments if you have any specific thoughts or feelings that you did not mention in the previous questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Audience Reaction**: Briefly describe your overall reaction to the play. What did you think of it?
* **Characters Understood**: Select the characters that you understood well during the play. You can choose multiple characters.
* **Plot Summary**: Provide a brief summary of the plot, including the main events and key moments.
* **Character Relationships**: Describe the relationships between the characters in the play.
* **Setting**: Describe the setting where the play took place.
* **Plot Climax**: Describe the most intense or critical moment in the plot.
* **Characters Ending**: Describe the ending of the characters in the play.
* **Recommended**: Select if you would recommend this play to others.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or thoughts about the play.
* **Overall Impression**: Share your overall impression of the play.
* **Would Recommend**: Briefly explain why you would or would not recommend the play to others.
* **Rating**: Select the rating that best describes your impression of the play.
* **Theater Group**: Describe the theater group that produced the play.
* **Teacher Group**: Describe the teacher or group that organized the play.
* **Production Details**: Provide any additional details about the production, such as the director, actors, etc.
* **Performance Date**: Select the date of the performance you attended.
* **Performance Time**: Select the time of the performance you attended.
* **Performance Location**: Describe the location where the play was performed.
* **Audience Size**: Select the approximate size of the audience that attended the performance.
* **Audience Age**: Select the age group of the audience that attended the performance.
* **Audience Gender**: Select the gender breakdown of the audience that attended the performance.
* **Audience Educational**: Select the educational level of the audience that attended the performance.
* **Production Length**: Select the length of the play in minutes.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or thoughts about the play that you did not mention in the previous questions.

**Tips**

* Be honest and accurate in your answers.
* Answer each question based on your genuine understanding and impression of the play.
* Provide specific examples or details to support your answers whenever possible.
* If you are unsure about any question, please select "None" or "Other" and provide a brief explanation.

Please fill out the form carefully, and thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
