# Stt Supervisor Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stt Supervisor Confirmation Form is a form used to confirm the completion of tasks or projects assigned to a volunteer. This form is intended to be completed by the supervisor who has worked with the volunteer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the volunteer you are confirming the completion of tasks for.
2. Fill out the required fields, which include the supervisor's name, email, and phone number.
3. Provide a brief description of your work with the volunteer, including any relevant notes or comments.
4. Confirm that your work with the volunteer is complete.
5. Select a submit date and time.
6. Add any additional notes or comments if necessary.
