# Cotillion Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cotillion Application Form is a crucial document that collects essential information from applicants to process their application for a specific event. Please review each field carefully to ensure accurate completion.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with all required fields marked as "required".
2. Review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors.
3. Ensure you have attached all necessary documents and information as required by the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Form** (`application_form`, text, required): Please provide a brief description of your application.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address for correspondence.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Event** (`dateOfEvent`, date, required): Select the date of the event you are applying for.
* **Time of Event** (`timeOfEvent`, time, required): Select the time of the event you are applying for.
* **Event Description** (`eventDescription`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the event.
* **Number of Guests** (`numberOfGuests`, number, required): Enter the number of guests you plan to bring.
* **Age of Applicant** (`ageOfApplicant`, number, required): Enter your age.
* **Payment Method** (`paymentMethod`, text, required): Select your preferred method of payment.
* **Payment Status** (`paymentStatus`, select_one, required): Indicate your payment status.
* **Additional Comments** (`additionalComments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or information.
* **Confirm Payment** (`confirmPayment`, select_one, required): Confirm your payment method.
* **Confirm Payment Comment** (`confirmPaymentComment`, text, optional): Provide a comment if you need to clarify your payment method.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, select_one, required): Confirm your application submission.
* **Confirm Submit** (`confirmSubmit`, select_one, required): Confirm your application submission.
* **Confirm Submit Comment** (`confirmSubmitComment`, text, optional): Provide a comment if you need to clarify your application submission.
* **Signature** (`Signature`, note, required): Please sign below to confirm your application.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`termsAndConditions`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Terms and Conditions Comment** (`termsAndConditionsComment`, text, optional): Provide a comment if you need to clarify your understanding of the terms and conditions.
* **Submitter** (`Submitter`, text, required): Enter your name as the submitter.
* **Submitter Email** (`SubmitterEmail`, email, required): Enter your email address as the submitter.
* **Submitter Phone** (`SubmitterPhone`, text, required): Enter your phone number as the submitter.
* **Submitter Organization** (`SubmitterOrganization`, text, optional): Provide the name of your organization (if applicable).
* **Submitter Note** (`SubmitterNote`, note, optional): Enter any additional information about your submission.
