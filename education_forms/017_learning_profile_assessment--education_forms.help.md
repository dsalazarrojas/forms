# learning_profile_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The learning profile assessment form is used to create a comprehensive assessment of a learner's profile, providing educators with valuable information to tailor their instruction to meet the individual needs of each learner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the form and ensure you have a clear understanding of each field before starting.
* For each field, provide accurate and relevant information about the learner's characteristics.
* Be sure to complete all required fields (indicated by a * next to the field label).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`1`, text, optional): This is a brief text field where you can provide any additional information about the learner that is not covered by the other fields. This is an optional field and can be left blank if not applicable.
* **Learning Style** (`2`, select_one, required): Select one of the following learning styles that best describes the learner's learning style:
	+ Self-Directed: The learner is independent and takes charge of their learning process.
	+ Structured: The learner benefits from a structured and organized approach to learning.
	+ Project-Based: The learner learns best through hands-on projects and applications.
* **Preferences** (`3`, text, optional): This field allows you to capture any additional preferences or requirements of the learner, such as language or cultural background.
