# scholar_program_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from scholars on their experience with the program. It aims to gather information on their enjoyment level, areas for improvement, overall satisfaction, future expectations, and recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your participant information.
2. Rate your enjoyment level with the program on a scale of 1-10.
3. Select areas of the program you'd like to see improved.
4. Choose your level of overall satisfaction with the program.
5. State how you expect the program to change in the next 6 months.
6. Indicate whether you recommend the program to others.
7. Add any additional comments.
8. Choose your preferred method of contact for feedback.
9. Provide any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information**: Provide your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **How much do you enjoy the scholar program?**: Rate your enjoyment level with the program on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is "I do not enjoy it at all" and 10 is "I enjoy it very much".
* **Which areas of the program would you like to see improved?**: Choose all areas that apply, such as Academics, Logistics, or Communication.
* **Overall satisfaction with the scholar program**: Choose one of the five options: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neither Satisfied Nor Dissatisfied, Somewhat Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.
* **How do you expect the scholar program to change in the next 6 months?**: Describe your expectations for the program's development in the next 6 months.
* **Do you recommend this scholar program to others?**: Choose "True" if you would recommend the program, and "False" otherwise.
* **Additional comments**: Add any additional comments or feedback about the program.
* **How would you like to be contacted for feedback?**: Choose your preferred method of contact, such as Phone, Email, or Neither.
* **Additional comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **How urgent is this feedback for improvement?**: Choose one of the three options: High, Medium, or Low.
