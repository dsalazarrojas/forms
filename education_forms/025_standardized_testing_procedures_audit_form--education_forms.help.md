# standardized_testing_procedures_audit_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The standardized_testing_procedures_audit_form is used to document and track the audit process and findings related to standardized testing procedures. It is essential to provide a clear and consistent record of compliance status, actions taken, and observations. This form helps in monitoring and improving the adherence to testing procedures in your educational settings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant pages (sections) from the form, each corresponding to a specific part of the audit process.
2. Fill in the information for each page according to the specific instructions provided for each field below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Testing Form** (`test_form`, text, optional):
	+ This field is used to provide any additional testing-related information.
* **Date of Audit** (`date_of_audit`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date of the audit.
* **School or Testing Center** (`school_or_testing_center`, select multiple, optional):
	+ Select all the schools or testing centers that are part of the audit. Options: Yes, No.
* **Compliance Status** (`compliance_status`, select one, optional):
	+ Indicate the compliance status for the testing procedures. Options: Compliant, Not Compliant.
* **Follow-up Actions** (`follow_up_actions`, text, optional):
	+ Describe any follow-up actions that are required or taken.
* **Compliance Comments** (`compliance_comments`, text, optional):
	+ Provide additional comments on the compliance status.
* **Observations** (`observations`, text, optional):
	+ Record any observations or comments on the testing procedures.
* **Next Action Date** (`next_action_date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date for the next action or follow-up.
* **Follow-up Status** (`follow_up_status`, select one, optional):
	+ Indicate the status of the follow-up actions. Options: Active, Inactive.
* **Follow-up Comments** (`follow_up_comments`, text, optional):
	+ Provide comments on the follow-up status.
* **Follow-up Actions Taken** (`follow_up_actions_taken`, text, optional):
	+ Describe the actions taken as a result of the follow-up.
