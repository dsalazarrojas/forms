# Education Market Research Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for researchers to register their education market research projects. It collects essential information about each project, including researcher details, research dates, locations, and category. This guide will walk you through the process of completing this form accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information about your research project before starting to fill out the form.
2. Fill out the form in the following order:
	* School and researcher details (fields 1-5)
	* Research project details (fields 6-11)
	* Additional researcher details (fields 12-15)
	* Duplicate project details (fields 16-25)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School ID (#1, school_id)**: Enter the unique identification number of your school.
* **Researcher ID (#2, researcher_id)**: Enter your research project's ID or unique identification number.
* **Research Name (#3, research_name)**: Provide a concise and descriptive name for your research project.
* **Email (#4, email)**: Enter your contact email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone (#5, phone)**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Research Start Date (#6, research_start_date)**: Enter the date when your research project begins.
* **Research End Date (#7, research_end_date)**: Enter the date when your research project ends.
* **Research Description (#8, research_description)**: Provide a brief description of your research project.
* **Research Category (#9, research_category)**: Select one or more categories that best describe your research project (e.g., Research Category 1, Research Category 2, or Research Category 3).
* **Research Location (#10, research_location)**: Enter the location where your research project will take place.
* **School Admin (#11, school_admin)**: Enter your school's administrator's contact information.
* **Researcher Contact (#12, researcher_contact)**: Enter your contact information for further communication.
* **Follow-up (#13, follow_up)**: Indicate whether or not you would like to receive follow-up information after submitting this form.
* **School ID 2 (#14, school_id_2)**: (Duplicate) Enter the unique identification number of your school (if applicable).
* **Researcher ID 2 (#15, researcher_id_2)**: (Duplicate) Enter your research project's ID or unique identification number (if applicable).
* **Research Name 2 (#16, research_name_2)**: (Duplicate) Provide a concise and descriptive name for your research project (if applicable).
* **Email 2 (#17, email_2)**: (Duplicate) Enter your contact email address (if applicable).
* **Phone 2 (#18, phone_2)**: (Duplicate) Enter your contact phone number (if applicable).
* **Research Start Date 2 (#19, research_start_date_2)**: (Duplicate) Enter the date when your research project begins (if applicable).
* **Research End Date 2 (#20, research_end_date_2)**: (Duplicate) Enter the date when your research project ends (if applicable).
* **Research Description 2 (#21, research_description_2)**: (Duplicate) Provide a brief description of your research project (if applicable).
* **Research Category 2 (#22, research_category_2)**: (Duplicate) Select one or more categories that best describe your research project (if applicable).
* **Research Location 2 (#23, research_location_2)**: (Duplicate) Enter the location where your research project will take place (if applicable).
* **School Admin 2 (#24, school_admin_2)**: (Duplicate) Enter your school's administrator's contact information (if applicable).
* **Researcher Contact 2 (#25, researcher_contact_2)**: (Duplicate) Enter your contact information for further communication (if applicable).

## Tips
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact your school's administrator for support.
* You can submit multiple projects with duplicate entries, but be aware that each entry will require additional verification and processing time.
