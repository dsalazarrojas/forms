# reading_log_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect reading logs from students. It is used to track the student's reading habits, including the date, time spent reading, comprehension, total pages read, average speed, reading frequency, comfort level, and comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter the date of reading in the "Date Read" field in the format MM/DD/YY.
3. Enter the time spent reading in the "Time Spent Reading" field.
4. Select the student's comprehension level in the "Comprehension" field.
5. Enter the total number of pages read in the "Total Pages Read" field.
6. Enter the average speed of reading in the "Average Speed" field.
7. Enter the reading frequency in the "Reading Frequency" field.
8. Select the student's comfort level while reading in the "Reading Comport" field.
9. Enter any additional comments in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Enter the student's name in this field.
* **Date Read**: Enter the date of reading in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Time Spent Reading**: Enter the total time spent reading in hours.
* **Comprehension**: Select one of the following options: Very High, High, Medium, or Low.
* **Total Pages Read**: Enter the total number of pages read.
* **Average Speed**: Enter the average speed of reading in pages per hour.
* **Reading Frequency**: Enter the frequency of reading in hours per week.
* **Reading Comport**: Select one of the following options: Very Comfortable, Neutral, or Uncomfortable.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the reading experience.
