# Cicerone Discourse Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess an individual's knowledge and self-assessed confidence in answering questions related to beer-related topics. It's a series of multiple-choice questions meant to evaluate an individual's understanding of beer styles, brewing processes, serving techniques, and more.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Brewer", "Bartender", "Server", "Manager", "Owner", or "Beer Educator" option for "Your Position" (Question 5).
2. Choose an option for each question by selecting the best answer for the specific question.
3. Indicate your level of confidence for each question by selecting one of the four options provided.
4. Select all topics that you feel less confident about by checking the boxes for "Beer styles", "Brewing process", "Serving techniques", "Beer storage", "Off-flavors", "History" (Question 9).
5. Indicate your interest in further training by selecting "Yes, definitely", "Maybe", or "No, thank you" (Question 20).
6. Provide any additional comments or feedback on the quiz by typing in the text field (Question 21).
