# Global Health Education Exchange Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect demographic and academic background information from individuals or organizations interested in participating in the Global Health Education Exchange. The information collected will be used to facilitate connections, collaborations, and information exchange within the platform.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your first name.
2. Enter your last name.
3. Provide your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Provide your date of birth in the format of "month/day/year".
6. Enter your address.
7. Indicate your degree program (e.g., Bachelor, Master, PhD).
8. Specify your degree field of interest (e.g., Biology, Medicine, Engineering).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your full first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your full last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address that you use and check regularly.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format of "month/day/year".
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your mailing address.
* **Degree Program** (`degree_program`, text, optional): Indicate your degree program (e.g., Bachelor, Master, PhD).
* **Degree Field** (`degree_field`, text, optional): Specify your degree field of interest (e.g., Biology, Medicine, Engineering).
