# University Course Curriculum Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and track approval status and comments from faculty members for a specific university course. It helps track changes made to the course curriculum, records the approval status, and allows for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, follow these steps:

1. **General Information**: Enter the general information about the course, such as the course title and description.
2. **Course Details**: Provide detailed information about the course, including its syllabus and any relevant updates.
3. **Faculty Details**: Enter your faculty information, including your name and position.
4. **Curriculum Changes**: Describe the changes you are proposing to make to the course curriculum.
5. **Approval Status**: Select your approval status for the course curriculum from the options provided.
6. **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or suggestions you have for the course curriculum.
7. **Faculty Approvals**: Select the faculty members who have approved or disapproved the course curriculum.
8. **Date Approved**: Enter the date you approved or disapproved the course curriculum.
9. **Time Approved**: Enter the time you approved or disapproved the course curriculum.
10. **Comments**: Add any additional comments or suggestions for the course curriculum.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`general_information`, text, required: false): Enter the general information about the course.
* **Course Details** (`course_details`, text, required: false): Provide detailed information about the course, including its syllabus and any relevant updates.
* **Faculty Details** (`faculty_details`, text, required: false): Enter your faculty information, including your name and position.
* **Curriculum Changes** (`curriculum_changes`, text, required: false): Describe the changes you are proposing to make to the course curriculum.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, text, required: false): Select your approval status for the course curriculum from the options provided.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments or suggestions you have for the course curriculum.
* **Faculty Approvals** (`faculty_approvals`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the faculty members who have approved or disapproved the course curriculum. Options: Approve, Do Not Approve, No Opinion.
* **Date Approved** (`date_approved`, date, required: false): Enter the date you approved or disapproved the course curriculum.
* **Time Approved** (`time_approved`, time, required: false): Enter the time you approved or disapproved the course curriculum.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments or suggestions for the course curriculum.
