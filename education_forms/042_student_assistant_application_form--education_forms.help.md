# Student Assistant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for students who want to apply to be a Student Assistant. It will help us understand their personal and work-related information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your information. Please ensure that all fields are accurate and up-to-date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name (First name)** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name, as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last name (Last name)** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name, as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of birth (Date of birth)** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your current address, including your street, city, and state.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address that you regularly check.
* **Contact number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter a phone number where you can be reached.
* **Student number (Student number)** (`student_number`, `text`, required): Enter your student ID number, if applicable.
* **Department (Department)** (`department`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' if you are applying for a position within your department.
* **Position (Position)** (`position`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' if you are available to take on a position.
* **Availability (Availability)** (`availability`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the days and times you are available to work. 
  *Please note that you can select multiple options*
* **Availability time (Availability time)** (`availability_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time you are available to work in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Salary (Salary)** (`salary`, `number`, required): Enter your expected salary in the format $X,XXX.XX.
* **Availability date (Availability date)** (`availability_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are available to start working.
* **Start date (Start date)** (`availability_start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the start date of your availability period.
* **End date (End date)** (`availability_end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the end date of your availability period.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask a faculty member for guidance.
* The information collected from this form will be used to determine your eligibility to be a Student Assistant.
