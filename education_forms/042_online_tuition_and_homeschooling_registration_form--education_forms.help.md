# Online Tuition And Homeschooling Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect demographic and contact information from users interested in online tuition and homeschooling services. By completing this form, the user will help the service provider understand their needs and preferences, allowing for a more personalized experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the relevant information. Make sure to select the correct category (Homeschooling or Online Classes) and enter a brief description of your needs. If you are a parent or guardian, enter your name and contact information. If you are a student, enter your student ID number if applicable. Finally, enter your contact email and contact phone number to facilitate communication with the service provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (`Category`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of educational service you are interested in, whether it is Homeschooling or Online Classes.
* **Description** (`Description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your needs and preferences for the educational service.
* **User Name** (`User Name`, text, optional): Enter your name if you are a parent or guardian, or your name if you are a student.
* **Student ID** (`Student ID`, number, optional): Enter your student ID number if you are a student.
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email address so the service provider can reach out to you.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number so the service provider can communicate with you.
* **Tuition Amount** (`Tuition Amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of tuition you are willing to pay for the educational service.
