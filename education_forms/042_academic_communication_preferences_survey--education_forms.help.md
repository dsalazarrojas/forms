# Academic Communication Preferences Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information about your preferences for academic communication, which will help us better understand your needs and improve our communication channels and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill in your full name, email address, and primary academic role.
2. Choose your preferred way to receive updates from instructors and administrative announcements.
3. Indicate how often you would like to receive institutional updates.
4. Specify your expected response time for academic emails.
5. Select all the digital tools you currently use for academic communication.
6. Choose your preferred format for group discussions or meetings.
7. Indicate whether you feel overwhelmed by academic notifications.
8. If you have any communication accessibility needs, provide a brief description.
9. Add any additional comments about academic communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your institution's records.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your institutional email address where you can be reached for academic purposes.
* **Your primary role** (`academic_role`, select_one, required): Select your primary role at the institution (Student, Faculty, Staff, Teaching Assistant, or Administrator).
* **Program or department** (`program_or_department`, text, required): Enter your academic program or department name.
* **Preferred way to receive updates from instructors** (`preferred_channel_instructor`, select_one, required): Select how you would like to receive updates from instructors (Email, Learning Management System (LMS), In-class announcements, Text or SMS, or Messaging app).
* **Preferred way to receive administrative announcements** (`preferred_channel_admin`, select_one, required): Select how you would like to receive administrative announcements (Email, Student portal, Mobile app notifications, Social media, or Physical bulletin board).
* **How often would you like to receive institutional updates** (`frequency_preference`, select_one, required): Choose how often you would like to receive institutional updates (Daily, A few times per week, Weekly, Bi-weekly, or Monthly).
* **Expected response time for academic emails** (`response_time_expectation`, select_one, required): Select your expected response time for academic emails (Within a few hours, Within 24 hours, Within 48 hours, Within one week, or No specific expectation).
* **Digital tools used** (`tools_used`, select_multiple, required): Select all the digital tools you currently use for academic communication (Email, Microsoft Teams, Slack, Google Chat, WhatsApp, Zoom, or Discussion forums).
* **Preferred format for group discussions or meetings** (`preferred_meeting_format`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred format for group discussions or meetings (In-person, Video conference, Hybrid, or Asynchronous messaging).
* **Feeling about academic notifications** (`notification_overload`, select_one, required): Select how you feel about receiving academic notifications (Yes, significantly, Somewhat, Not really, or Not at all).
* **Accessibility needs** (`accessibility_needs`, text, optional): If you have any communication accessibility needs, provide a brief description.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_feedback`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about academic communication.

Note: This help guide is a general guide and may not cover every possible scenario. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
