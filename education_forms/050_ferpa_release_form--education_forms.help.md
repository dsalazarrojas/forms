# Ferpa Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Ferpa Release Form is used to collect necessary information to share student data with third parties. This form is designed to be completed by students or their authorized representatives.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with your name, date of birth, address, and contact information.
2. Select your Ferpa consent status: "Consent" to share your data or "Decline" to restrict sharing.
3. Enter the date and time you wish to release your information (optional).
4. Provide a brief reason for releasing your information (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it is listed on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it is listed on your official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 12/25/1990).
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter your current address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number (e.g., (123) 456-7890).
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Ferpa Consent** (`ferpa_consent`, select_one, required: false): Select "Consent" to share your data or "Decline" to restrict sharing.
* **Ferpa Date** (`ferpa_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you wish to release your information (MM/DD/YYYY format, e.g., 12/25/1990).
* **Ferpa Time** (`ferpa_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time you wish to release your information (HH:MM AM/PM format, e.g., 02:00 PM).
* **Ferpa Reason** (`ferpa_reason`, text, required: false): Enter a brief reason for releasing your information (e.g., for academic purposes).
