# stem_educator_recommendation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The stem_educator_recommendation_form is used to collect information from educators to recommend students for educational programs or opportunities. This form is meant to be completed by educators, such as teachers, counselors, or administrators, who have first-hand knowledge of a student's abilities and potential. Please note that you should only fill out this form if you have a genuine recommendation to make, and ensure that the student's information is up-to-date and accurate.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the required fields (indicated by the red asterisk \*).
* Provide a valid student ID if required.
* Select the correct recommendation type.
* Choose the student's grade level.
* Type out the recommendation letter and list student accomplishments if required.
* Sign your name as the educator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the student you are recommending.
* **Educator Name** (`educator_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the educator making the recommendation.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the student's school.
* **Recommendation Type** (`recommendation_type`, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose from: Teacher, Counselor, Administrator
* **Recommendation Date** (`recommendation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are making this recommendation.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, optional): Enter the student's ID number if required.
* **Grade Level** (`student_grade_level`, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose from: 9th, 10th, 11th, 12th
* **Student Accomplishments** (`student_accomplishments`, text, optional): Describe the student's notable accomplishments.
* **Recommendation Letter** (`recommendation_letter`, text, optional): Type out a brief letter of recommendation, explaining why you think this student is suitable for the educational opportunity.
* **Educator Signature** (`educator_signature`, text, optional): Sign your name as the educator.
* **School Name Confirmation** (`school_name_confirmation`, text, optional): Confirm the name of the student's school.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the student's information is up-to-date and accurate.
* This form is meant to be used for genuine recommendations only. Do not use this form to make false or misleading recommendations.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, please contact your supervisor or school administration for guidance.
