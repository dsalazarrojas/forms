# AI Ethics In Education Research Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for educators and researchers to submit applications for AI ethics review. It ensures that you, as the applicant, understand and comply with the necessary guidelines and regulations for conducting AI-related research in educational settings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal and project details (Fields 2-4).
2. Identify the primary ethical domains relevant to your research (Field 6).
3. Describe how you plan to involve AI in your project, including its role and impact (Field 7).
4. Assess the potential risk level of your research (Field 8).
5. Outline your plan to mitigate identified risks (Field 9).
6. Explain the participant consent process, especially for minors (Field 10).
7. Describe how you will store and secure the research data (Field 11).
8. If applicable, provide information about third-party AI service providers used (Field 12).
9. Outline your algorithmic transparency plan (Field 13).
10. Describe the oversight mechanism for the AI during the study (Field 14).
11. Consider the potential long-term impact of your project (Field 15).
12. Finally, agree to abide by established AI ethics guidelines and confirm that your supervisor has reviewed and approved your application (Fields 17 and 18).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name (Required)** (Field 2): Enter your name to identify yourself as the applicant.
* **Institutional Affiliation (Required)** (Field 3): Specify your institutional affiliation to ensure transparency about your research background.
* **Research Project Title (Required)** (Field 4): Briefly describe your research project title for clarity and identification.
* **Primary Ethical Domains (Required)** (Field 6): Choose the primary ethical domains relevant to your research from the provided options:
	+ Data Privacy and Protection
	+ Algorithmic Bias and Fairness
	+ Human Agency and Oversight
	+ Transparency and Explainability
	+ Safety and Robustness
	+ Accountability
* **Description of AI Involvement (Required)** (Field 7): Describe how you plan to use AI models or algorithms in your research, including their role and expected impact.
* **Initial Risk Assessment (Required)** (Field 8): Assess the risk level of your research based on institutional guidelines, choosing from Low (Minimal risk to participants), Medium (Some potential for bias or data exposure), or High (Involves sensitive data or significant impact on learners).
* **Risk Mitigation Strategies (Required)** (Field 9): Outline the plans you have to minimize the risks identified in Field 8.
* **Participant Consent Process (Required)** (Field 10): Describe how you will obtain informed consent from participants, especially if minors are involved.
* **Data Storage and Security Measures (Required)** (Field 11): Explain where and how you will store the research data.
* **Third-party AI Service Providers (Optional)** (Field 12): If applicable, list third-party AI service providers used in your research.
* **Algorithmic Transparency Plan (Optional)** (Field 13): Outline how you plan to explain the AI's role or decisions to participants.
* **Oversight Mechanism (Required)** (Field 14): Describe who will monitor the AI during the study and how participants can report issues.
* **Potential Long-term Impact Statement (Optional)** (Field 15): Consider the potential long-term implications of your AI application.
* **Declarations and Sign-off (Required)** (Field 17): Confirm that you agree to conduct this research in accordance with established AI ethics guidelines.
* **Supervisor/PI Approval (Required)** (Field 18): Confirm that your supervisor has reviewed and approved your application.
* **Date of Application (Required)** (Field 19): Enter the date of your application submission.

Remember, this form helps you ensure that your research meets the necessary standards for conducting AI-related research in educational settings.
