# physics_equations_reference_sheet_submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is intended to collect and organize physics-related information, including equations, descriptions, and submission details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields as needed.
2. Ensure you select the correct submission type (e.g., Equation, Description, Source).
3. Choose relevant tags (e.g., Physics, Math, Education, Reference) to categorize your submission.
4. Select the correct submission status (e.g., Approved, Rejected, Pending).
5. Fill in any additional notes or comments as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Data** (`form_data`, text, optional): Briefly describe the purpose of your submission.
* **Equation** (`equation`, text, optional): Enter a relevant equation related to physics.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a description of the submission.
* **Source** (`source`, text, optional): Specify the source or origin of the submission.
* **Tags** (`tags`, select_multiple, optional): Select relevant tags (e.g., Physics, Math, Education, Reference) to categorize your submission.
* **Submission Type** (`submission_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of submission (e.g., Equation, Description, Source).
* **Physics Category** (`physics_category`, select_one, optional): Select a relevant physics category (e.g., Kinematics, Thermodynamics, Electromagnetism, Mechanics).
* **Submission Date/Time** (`submission_date_time`, date, optional): Enter the date and time of submission.
* **Submission Date/Time Note** (`submission_date_time_note`, text, optional): Add any additional notes about the submission date and time.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter your name or identifier.
* **Submitted For** (`submitted_for`, select_one, optional): Choose who the submission is for (e.g., Student, Educator).
* **Submitted To** (`submitted_to`, select_one, optional): Choose who the submission is submitted to (e.g., Student, Educator).
* **Submitted By Email** (`submitted_by_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Submitted By Phone** (`submitted_by_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Submitted By Address** (`submitted_by_address`, text, optional): Enter your address.
* **Submission Note** (`submission_note`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the submission.
* **Tags For Submission** (`tags_for_submission`, select_multiple, optional): Select additional relevant tags (e.g., Physics, Math, Education, Reference).
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the submission status (e.g., Approved, Rejected, Pending).
* **Submission ID** (`submission_id`, text, optional): Enter a unique identifier for the submission.
* **Submission Revision** (`submission_revision`, text, optional): Enter the revision number or version of the submission.
* **Submitted By Note** (`submitted_by_note`, text, optional): Add any additional notes about who submitted the entry.
* **Submitted To Note** (`submitted_to_note`, text, optional): Add any additional notes about who the submission is submitted to.
* **Submitted For Note** (`submitted_for_note`, text, optional): Add any additional notes about who the submission is for.
* **Physics Equations** (`physics_equations`, text, optional): Enter any relevant physics equations.
* **Submitted To** (`submitted_to`, text, optional): Enter the recipient's name or identifier.

Note: Some fields may have additional guidance or instructions specific to their type or purpose. Always review each field carefully to ensure accurate and complete information.</thinking>
