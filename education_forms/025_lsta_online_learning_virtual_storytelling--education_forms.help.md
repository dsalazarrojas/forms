# lsta_online_learning_virtual_storytelling - Help Guide
## Purpose
The LSTA Online Learning Virtual Storytelling form is designed to collect demographic information from users, enabling us to better understand and cater to their needs in our online learning environment. This form will help us gather data on your personal details such as name, contact information, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the form's submission button to begin.
2. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
3. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
4. Select whether you prefer Minimalist settings or not in the Minimalist field.
5. Enter your Email address in the Email field (format: `example@example.com`).
6. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
7. Select your birthdate in the Birthdate field (YYYY-MM-DD format).
8. Choose your preferred rating (one, two, three, four, five) in the Rating field.
9. Type any additional comment in the Comment field.
10. Select your gender (male, female, other) in the Gender field.
11. Enter your Age in years in the Age field.
12. Enter your height in centimeters in the Height field.
13. Enter your weight in kilograms in the Weight field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): This field requires your first name. Please enter your full first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): This field requires your last name. Please enter your full last name.
* **Minimalist** (`minimalist`, select_one, optional): Select whether you prefer to keep your settings minimal or not. This will help us tailor the learning experience to your liking.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address (e.g., `example@example.com`).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Birthdate** (`birthdate`, date, optional): Select your birthdate in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Hour** (`hour`, time, optional): Select your preferred hour of the day in the time format.
* **Rating** (`rating`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one, two, three, four, or five stars to rate your preferences.
* **Comment** (`comment`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share with us.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, optional): Select your gender from the options male, female, or other.
* **Age** (`age`, number, optional): Enter your age in years.
* **Height** (`height`, number, optional): Enter your height in centimeters.
* **Weight** (`weight`, number, optional): Enter your weight in kilograms.
