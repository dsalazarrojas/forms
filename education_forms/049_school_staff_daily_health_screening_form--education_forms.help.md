# School Staff Daily Health Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Staff Daily Health Screening Form is a tool used to quickly assess school staff members' health status, helping the school to take necessary measures to prevent the spread of illness.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of your screening in the "Date" field.
2. Enter your staff member number in the "Staff Member Number" field.
3. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Select your current status (e.g., Normal, High, Low) in the "Current Status" field.
7. Select any symptoms you are experiencing from the list (e.g., Fever, Headache, Fatigue) in the "Symptoms" field.
8. Indicate whether you have any travel history in the "Travel History" field.
9. Enter the date of your last contact with someone who may be sick in the "Last Contact" field (if applicable).
10. Add any relevant health notes or comments in the "Health Notes" field.
11. Select the action you have taken regarding your health status (e.g., Self-isolate, Self-report) in the "Action Taken" field.
12. Sign and date the form if applicable.
13. Add any additional comments or concerns in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date**: Enter the date of your screening.
* **Staff Member Number**: Enter your staff member number.
* **Name**: Enter your name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Current Status**: Select Normal, High, or Low to indicate your current health status.
* **Symptoms**: Select any symptoms you are experiencing, such as Fever, Headache, or Fatigue.
* **Travel History**: Indicate if you have any recent travel history.
* **Last Contact**: Enter the date of your last contact with someone who may be sick.
* **Health Notes**: Add any relevant health notes or comments.
* **Action Taken**: Select the action you have taken regarding your health status (e.g., Self-isolate, Self-report).
* **Signature**: Sign and date the form if you are indicating a health status change.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or concerns.
