# Emergency Contact Sheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect emergency contact information for students, staff, and faculty in case of an emergency situations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the name of the School Administrator.
2. Select the staff member you would like to contact in case of an emergency.
3. Provide the contact information (email or phone number) for the School Administrator.
4. Select the contact method (phone, email, or in-person) for the School Administrator.
5. Provide the contact information (email or phone number) for the staff.
6. Select the contact method (phone, email, or in-person) for the staff.
7. Provide the contact information (email or phone number) for the Faculty.
8. Select the contact method (phone, email, or in-person) for the Faculty.
9. Indicate your relationship with the person (parent/guardian, spouse/elder, sibling, or friend).
10. Select the contact method (phone, email, or in-person) for the relationship.
11. If applicable, provide additional contact information.
12. Select the contact method (phone, email, or in-person) for the additional contact information.
13. Provide the emergency contact information (email or phone number) if you have someone other than the listed staff/faculty member.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Administrator** (`school_admin`, text, not required): Enter the name of the School Administrator.
* **Staff** (`staff`, text, not required): Select the staff member you would like to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Contact Info for School Administrator** (`contact_info_school_admin`, email, not required): Enter the email or phone number of the School Administrator.
* **Contact Method for School Administrator** (`contact_method_school_admin`, select_one, not required): Select how you would like to contact the School Administrator (phone, email, or in-person).
* **Contact Info for Staff** (`contact_info_staff`, email, not required): Enter the email or phone number of the staff member.
* **Contact Method for Staff** (`contact_method_staff`, select_one, not required): Select how you would like to contact the staff member (phone, email, or in-person).
* **Contact Info for Faculty** (`contact_info_faculty`, email, not required): Enter the email or phone number of the Faculty member.
* **Contact Method for Faculty** (`contact_method_faculty`, select_one, not required): Select how you would like to contact the Faculty member (phone, email, or in-person).
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_one, not required): Indicate your relationship with the person (parent/guardian, spouse/elder, sibling, or friend).
* **Contact Method for Relationship** (`contact_method_relationship`, select_one, not required): Select how you would like to contact the relationship (phone, email, or in-person).
* **Other** (`emergency_contact_other`, text, not required): If you have someone other than the listed staff/faculty member to contact, enter their name.
* **Contact Method for Other** (`contact_method_other`, select_one, not required): Select how you would like to contact the other person (phone, email, or in-person).
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_info`, email, not required): Enter the email or phone number of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
