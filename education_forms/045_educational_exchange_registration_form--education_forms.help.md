# Educational Exchange Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The educational exchange registration form is used to collect information from students participating in an educational exchange program. The form helps to gather necessary details to facilitate the exchange process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Student Name.
2. Enter your Student Email.
3. Provide information about your Home Institution.
4. Enter details about the Exchange Institution where you will be participating in.
5. Choose your Semester of exchange.
6. Select the Academic Program you are enrolled in.
7. Enter your Start Date and End Date of the exchange.
8. Enter your Start Time and End Time of the activities during the exchange.
9. Enter your Phone Number.
10. Enter your Student ID.
11. Enter details about the Academic Program you are participating in.
12. Enter details about the Host Department where you will be participating in.
13. Add any additional Program Details.
14. Add any Notes about your participation in the exchange program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on official documents.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required): Enter your official email address.
* **Home Institution** (`home_institution`, text, optional): Enter your current institution's name.
* **Exchange Institution** (`exchange_institution`, text, optional): Enter the name of the institution where you will be participating in the exchange.
* **Semester** (`semester`, select_one, optional): Choose the semester you plan to participate in the exchange (Spring, Fall, Summer).
* **Exchange Program** (`exchange_program`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more academic programs you are enrolled in during the exchange.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to start participating in the exchange.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to end participating in the exchange.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of your activities during the exchange.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of your activities during the exchange.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, optional): Enter your student ID number.
* **Academic Program** (`academic_program`, text, optional): Enter details about the academic program you are participating in.
* **Host Department** (`host_department`, text, optional): Enter the name of the department where you will be participating in.
* **Program Details** (`program_details`, text, optional): Add any additional details about your participation in the exchange program.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your participation in the exchange program.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click to submit your registration form.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to receive confirmation about your registration.
* **Confirm Email To** (`confirm_email_to`, email, optional): Enter the email address where you want to receive confirmation.
* **Exchange Institution Address** (`exchange_institution_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the institution where you will be participating in.
* **Exchange Institution City** (`exchange_institution_city`, text, optional): Enter the city of the institution where you will be participating in.
* **Exchange Institution Country** (`exchange_institution_country`, text, optional): Enter the country of the institution where you will be participating in.
* **Exchange Institution State** (`exchange_institution_state`, text, optional): Enter the state of the institution where you will be participating in.
* **Exchange Institution Postal Code** (`exchange_institution_postal_code`, text, optional): Enter the postal code of the institution where you will be participating in.
* **Host Institution Address** (`host_institution_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the host institution where you will be participating in.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field.
* Double-check your contact information before submitting the form.
* If you need to add any additional details or notes, use the Program Details and Notes fields.
* Review your form carefully before submitting it to ensure you have entered all required and optional fields accurately.
