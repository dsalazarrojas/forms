# Become A Tutor Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Become A Tutor Application form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in becoming tutors for your organization. This form will guide you through the process of providing your contact details, academic background, and preferences for tutoring sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and fill out the required fields.
2. Ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Select the correct options for each field, if applicable.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Text, Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (Email, Required): Enter your valid email address that we can use to communicate with you.
* **Phone Number** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Mailing Address** (Text, Required): Enter your current mailing address.
* **Highest Level of Education Completed** (Select One, Required): Select the highest level of education you have completed. This can be High School Diploma, Associate Degree, Bachelor Degree, Master Degree, or Doctoral Degree.
* **Current Major or Profession** (Text, Required): Describe your current major or profession.
* **Subjects You Are Qualified to Tutor** (Select Multiple, Required): Select all subjects you are qualified to tutor, such as Mathematics (Basic to Algebra), Advanced Mathematics (Calculus, Statistics), English Literature and Composition, Science (Biology, Chemistry, Physics), History and Social Studies, Foreign Languages, or Test Prep (SAT, ACT, GRE).
* **Previous Tutoring Experience (Years)** (Number, Required): Enter the number of years you have been tutoring. If none, enter 0.
* **Preferred Student Level** (Select Multiple, Required): Select the grade level of students you prefer to tutor, such as Elementary (K-5), Middle School (6-8), High School (9-12), or College / Adult.
* **Preferred Tutoring Format** (Select One, Required): Select your preferred tutoring format, such as In-Person, Online / Remote, or Both In-Person and Online.
* **Estimated Hours Available Per Week** (Number, Required): Enter the estimated number of hours you are available to tutor per week.
* **Background Check Consent** (Select One, Required): Select whether you consent to a background check as part of the application process.
* **Application Date** (Date, Required): Select the date you are submitting your application.
* **Why do you want to become a tutor?** (Text, Optional): Briefly describe your teaching philosophy.
