# Doctoral Program Monthly Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to track the progress of doctoral students in their monthly check-in meetings with their advisors. The form is a crucial tool for monitoring and evaluating the students' development and progress.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your first name, last name, and email address.
2. Provide your current phone number.
3. Share your progress since the last check-in, if any.
4. Mention any challenges you are facing or have faced since the last check-in.
5. Outline your goals for the upcoming month.
6. Enter your advisor's name and contact information (if applicable).
7. Choose whether you have made any progress or not for the current month.
8. Enter the year and date of the current month.
9. Enter the time of the current month.
10. Specify whether you will be working from a different location for the current month.
11. Add any additional notes or comments.
12. Sign and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your active email address where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your active phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Progress** (`progress`, text, optional): Describe your progress since the last check-in, if any.
* **Challenges** (`challenges`, text, optional): Mention any challenges you are facing or have faced since the last check-in.
* **Goals** (`goals`, text, optional): Outline your goals for the upcoming month.
* **Advisor Name** (`advisor_name`, text, required): Enter your advisor's name (if applicable).
* **Advisor Email** (`advisor_email`, email, optional): Enter your advisor's email address (if applicable).
* **Advisor Phone** (`advisor_phone`, text, optional): Enter your advisor's phone number (if applicable).
* **Month** (`month`, select_multiple, required): Choose whether you have made any progress or not for the current month.
* **Year** (`year`, number, required): Enter the year of the current month.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of the current month.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time of the current month.
* **Location** (`location`, select_multiple, required): Specify whether you will be working from a different location for the current month.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your progress.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or feedback from your advisor or others.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required): Sign and submit the form to confirm your progress.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, optional): Enter a unique identifier for this form (if required).
* **Form Version** (`form_version`, text, optional): Enter the version of this form (if required).
* **User ID** (`user_id`, text, optional): Enter your unique ID (if required).
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Submit the form to complete it.

Note to self: This guide assumes that some fields are required for the purpose of tracking and monitoring progress, while others are optional for additional information or feedback.
