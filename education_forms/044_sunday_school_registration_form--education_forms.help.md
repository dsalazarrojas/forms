# Sunday School Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from individuals who wish to register for Sunday school activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields with your information.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
3. Review your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### User Info
* **User Info** (`user_info`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide your basic information, such as your name and any other identifying details you'd like to share.

### Contact Info
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide your contact information, such as your address and phone number.

### Emergency Contact
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide the name of an emergency contact person.

### Contact Info (Emergency Contact)
* **Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_info`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide the contact information of the emergency contact person you listed above.

### Name of Emergency Contact
* **Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide the name of the emergency contact person.

### Relation to Emergency Contact
* **Relation** (`emergency_contact_relation`, text, optional): This field is for you to specify your relation to the emergency contact person.

### Phone of Emergency Contact
* **Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide the phone number of the emergency contact person.

### Address of Emergency Contact
* **Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide the address of the emergency contact person.

## Tips
* Please make sure to review your submission before submitting it to ensure all information is accurate and complete.
* If you have any difficulties or questions, please do not hesitate to contact us.
