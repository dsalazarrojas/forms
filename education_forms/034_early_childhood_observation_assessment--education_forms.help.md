# Early Childhood Observation Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Early Childhood Observation Assessment form is used to collect data on observations made during early childhood assessments. This data is used to track and analyze children's development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have a valid user ID.
2. Choose the correct assessment date.
3. Select the frequency of the assessment (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Other).
4. Choose one or multiple observation types (e.g., Child Developmental Skills, Social and Emotional Development, Language and Literacy, Fine and Gross Motor Skills).
5. Enter the duration of the observation (a numeric value).
6. Provide the location of the observation (free text).
7. Add any relevant observation notes (free text).
8. Choose the assessment tool used (e.g., ChatJIMMY, Other).
9. Enter your user name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Assessment Date** (assessment_date, date, required/optional): The date on which the assessment was made.
* **Observation Frequency** (observation_frequency, select_one, optional): Select the frequency at which the assessment is administered (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Other).
* **Observation Type** (observation_type, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or multiple types of observations made during the assessment (e.g., Child Developmental Skills, Social and Emotional Development, Language and Literacy, Fine and Gross Motor Skills).
* **Observation Duration** (observation_duration, number, optional): Enter the duration of the observation as a numeric value.
* **Observation Location** (observation_location, text, optional): Enter the location where the observation took place.
* **Observation Notes** (observation_notes, note, optional): Add any relevant notes about the observation.
* **Assessment Tool** (assessment_tool, select_one, optional): Choose the assessment tool used (e.g., ChatJIMMY, Other).
* **User ID** (user_id, number, optional): Enter your user ID.
* **User Name** (user_name, text, optional): Enter your name.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Enter accurate and clear information in all fields.
