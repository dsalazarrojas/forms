# Academic Data Visualization Project Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide explains how to complete the Academic Data Visualization Project Application form.

## How To Complete This Form

### Completing the Form
To submit your application, provide the following information in the form below. Please be concise and accurate in your responses.

### What To Expect
After submitting the form, the project lead will review your application and follow up with you to discuss further details about your project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant name** (`applicant_name`, `text`, **required**: true): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Applicant email** (`applicant_email`, `email`, **required**: true): Enter your preferred contact email address. This will be used by the project lead for further communication.
* **Institutional affiliation** (`affiliation`, `text`, **required**: true): Provide your department or organization name.
* **Project title** (`project_title`, `text`, **required**: true): Enter a concise title for your project.
* **Project summary** (`project_summary`, `text`, **required**: true): Describe the main goals and expected outcomes of your project.
* **Dataset sources** (`dataset_sources`, `text`, **required**: true): Describe the datasets you plan to use and their access status.
* **Tools and methods** (`tools_and_methods`, `select_multiple`, **optional**: false): Select all the tools and software you plan to use for your project, separated by commas.
* **Estimated project timeline** (`timeline`, `text`, **optional**: false): Provide estimated start and end dates or duration for your project.
* **Expected outputs** (`expected_outputs`, `text`, **optional**: false): Specify the type of visualizations, dashboards, or publications you plan to create.
* **Additional needs** (`additional_needs`, `text`, **optional**: false): List any hardware, software, or data access needs you require for the project.

## Tips
Please ensure you have reviewed the form carefully before submission. If you have any questions or concerns, contact the project lead at [insert contact email].
