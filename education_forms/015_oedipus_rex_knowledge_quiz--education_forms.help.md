This form is designed to assess users' knowledge about the classic play "Oedipus Rex" by Sophocles. It gauges their understanding of the play's key elements, including its main plot, characters, theme, and resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Read each question carefully and answer with a simple "Yes" or "No" or a brief description of the element being asked about.
* For multiple-choice questions, select all that apply.
* Answer each question to the best of your knowledge, even if you're unsure or lack confidence in your answer.
* Submit the form when you've completed all questions.
