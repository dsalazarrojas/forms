# Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Registration Form is designed to collect user registration information, including their name and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (First Name, text, required: false)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name (Last Name, text, required: false)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email (Email, email, required: false)**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Date of Birth (Date of Birth, date, required: false)**: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yy (e.g., 06/25/1990).

## Tips

* Ensure that your email address is valid and can receive emails from the form owner.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please contact the form owner at form@gic.mx.
