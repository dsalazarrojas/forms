# Course Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Course Request Form is designed to allow instructors, staff, and students to request a new course from the institution. This form gathers information about the course they want to request, including its name, department, level, and language, as well as the contact email of the person submitting the request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Make sure to research and double-check the course ID, name, department, level, and language to ensure that the request is unique and well-researched.
3. Enter your contact email address to allow the institutional administration to contact you for further discussions and updates.

	+ Please enter the name of the course you are interested in.
* **Institution Name** (`institution_name`):

	+ Please enter the name of the institution where the course will be offered.

* **Interested in Course Name** (`interested_in_course_name`):

	+ Please enter the name of the course you are interested in.
* **Interested in Course Description** (`interested_in_course_description`):

* **Interested in Course Language** (`interested_in_course_language`):

## Tips
* Make sure to research and double-check the course information before submitting the request.
* Ensure that all fields are accurately filled in to avoid errors and duplicate requests.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, please contact the institutional administration.
