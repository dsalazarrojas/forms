# Trial Version: Sta..Maria National High School Online Enrolment S.Y.2020 2021 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for the online enrollment of students to Sta..Maria National High School for the School Year 2020-2021. It collects basic information about the student, including personal details and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the `First Name` field. This is the first name of the student enrolling.
2. Enter the **Last Name** of the student in the `Last Name` field. This is the last name of the student.
3. Provide your **Email** in the `Email` field. This will be used for further communication.
4. Enter the **Date of Birth** of the student in the `Date of Birth` field. This is the date when the student was born.
5. Fill in your **Phone** number in the `Phone` field. This is the contact number of the student's guardian/parent.
6. Enter the **Student Number** of the student in the `Student Number` field. This is a unique identifier for the student.
7. Select the **Year Level** of the student from the dropdown menu. This is the year level of the student (e.g., 1, 2, 3, or 4).
8. Provide the **Parent Name** in the `Parent Name` field. This is the name of the student's guardian/parent.
9. Enter the **Address** of the student in the `Address` field. This is the address of the student's guardian/parent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter the first name of the student.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter the last name of the student.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter the email address of the student or guardian/parent.
* **Date of Birth** (date, required): Enter the date of birth of the student.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter the phone number of the student's guardian/parent.
* **Student Number** (text, required): Enter a unique identifier for the student.
* **Year Level** (select_one, required): Select the year level of the student.
* **Parent Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the student's guardian/parent.
* **Address** (text, required): Enter the address of the student's guardian/parent.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check the information provided for accuracy.
* If you're having trouble with any field, try to ask for help from the school's administration.
