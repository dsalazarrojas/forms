# teacher_input_for_iep - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for teachers to input information about students' Individualized Education Programs (IEP). The purpose of this form is to collect and report student data, likely for tracking and reporting purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in all the required fields marked as "required". The "Student Id" field is not required, but it's good to have it for reference.
2. Enter the student's name in the "Teacher Name" field, as it's used for tracking and reporting purposes.
3. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
4. Fill in the "Date of Birth" field, which is the student's birthdate.
5. Fill in the "Date of Last Attendance" field, which is the date the student last attended school.
6. Select any physical needs the student may have from the dropdown options.
7. If the student has other physical needs that are not listed, enter them in the "Other Physical Needs" field.
8. If the student has any other health insurance besides the main insurance listed, enter it in the "Other Health Insurance" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Id** (number, not required): Enter the student's ID number for reference.
* **Teacher Name** (text, required): Enter your name as the teacher.
* **Student Name** (text, required): Enter the student's name.
* **Date of Birth** (date, not required): Enter the student's birthdate.
* **Date of Last Attendance** (date, not required): Enter the date the student last attended school.
* **Physical Needs** (select multiple, not required): Select any of the physical needs that apply to the student:
	+ Vision Impairment
	+ Hearing Impairment
	+ Mild Intellectual Disability
	+ Moderate Intellectual Disability
	+ Severe Intellectual Disability
	+ Speech or Language Impairment
	+ Speech or Language Disorder
	+ Physical Disability
	+ Emotional Disability
	+ Other
* **Other Physical Needs** (text, not required): Enter any other physical needs that are not listed.
* **Health Insurance** (text, not required): Enter the student's main health insurance provider.
* **Other Health Insurance** (text, not required): Enter any other health insurance providers besides the main one listed.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate reporting.
* If unsure about any field, please consult the relevant authorities or seek guidance from superiors.
* Double-check the form for accuracy before submitting it.
