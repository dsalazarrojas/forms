# Tutoring Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from both clients and tutors to create a comprehensive tutoring contract that outlines the terms and conditions of the tutoring sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read this guide carefully before filling out the form.
- Ensure you fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
- If you need help, don't hesitate to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information (client_info)** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the client's name, address, and other relevant contact information to identify and connect with them.
* **Tutor Information (tutor_info)** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): Fill out your name, contact details, and any other relevant information to provide a personal touch to the tutoring sessions.
* **Agreement (agreement)** (`3`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for you to outline the terms and conditions of the tutoring sessions, such as payment details and session schedules.
* **Terms and Conditions (terms_and_conditions)** (`4`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional terms and conditions that are specific to your tutoring service, such as cancellation policies or confidentiality agreements.
* **Signature (signature)** (`5`, `text`, required/optional): Sign and date the contract to confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions.

## Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
- Ensure you keep a copy of the contract for your records.
- If you need further clarification on any of the fields, don't hesitate to contact the form's creators.
