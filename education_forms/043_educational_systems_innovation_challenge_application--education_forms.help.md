# educational_systems_innovation_challenge_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The educational systems innovation challenge application form is designed to collect information from users about their educational systems innovation projects. This form will be used to support the evaluation process of the projects.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the project idea and impact description fields with your project's details.
2. If applicable, add supporting documents to your project.
3. Select yes or no for team members and contact details.
4. Choose the status of your project.
5. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Project Idea** (`project_idea`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of your project, including its name and a short summary.
- **Impact Description** (`impact_description`, `text`, required): Describe the potential impact of your project on the educational system.
- **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, `text`, optional): Attach any relevant documents or files that support your project, such as images, videos, or spreadsheets.
- **Team Members** (`team_members`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check yes if you have a team behind your project. If yes, list the team members' names and roles.
- **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check yes if you want to share your contact information with the evaluation committee. If yes, provide your name, email, and phone number.
- **Project Status** (`project_status`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the status of your project, whether it's active or inactive.
 
## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting your form.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, please contact the evaluation committee.
