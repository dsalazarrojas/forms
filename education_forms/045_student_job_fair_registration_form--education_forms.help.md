# Student Job Fair Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Job Fair Registration Form is designed to collect information from students who want to participate in a job fair. It's intended to help students showcase their interests, skills, and availability for potential job opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Student Information section with your details.
2. Provide information about the company you're interested in working for.
3. Specify the job details you're looking for.
4. Enter your contact information so we can get in touch with you.
5. Review and submit your form to confirm your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Information (Page 1)** (`Student Information`, `text`, optional): This field is where you can enter your student ID, name, and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Company Information (Page 1)** (`Company Information`, `text`, optional): In this field, you can provide details about the company you're interested in working for, such as its name and website.
* **Job Details (Page 1)** (`Job Details`, `text`, optional): Describe the job you're looking for, including the job title, department, and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Information (Page 1)** (`Contact Information`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number, so we can get in touch with you.
* **Notes (Page 1)** (`notes`, `note`, optional): If you have any additional comments or questions, you can add them here.
* **Submit (Page 2)** (`Submit`, `text`, optional): This is the final step to confirm your registration. Double-check your information before submitting.

## Tips
Please make sure to fill in all the required fields carefully to avoid any issues with your registration. If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to reach out to the form administrators.
