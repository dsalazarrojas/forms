# Library Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users for library reservations. Please fill out the form with your details and preferences, and library staff will process your request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your User Details, including your name, phone number, and email.
2. Indicate whether you want to reserve a book or resource from the library.
3. Select a date for your reservation.
4. Choose a start and end time for your reservation.
5. Enter the duration of your reservation.
6. Provide the name of the library you are visiting.
7. Enter the name of the librarian you will be interacting with.
8. Enter any additional comments or questions you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details** (1, text, required/optional): Enter your name, phone number, and email to help us contact you about your reservation.
* **Book Reservation** (2, select_one, required/optional): Check 'Yes' if you want to reserve a book or resource. This will help us understand your needs.
* **Date** (3, date, required/optional): Select a date for your reservation. Please choose a date that works for you and our library staff.
* **Start Time** (4, time, required/optional): Choose a start time for your reservation. Be as specific as possible so we can schedule it properly.
* **End Time** (5, time, required/optional): Select an end time for your reservation. This will help us ensure we're not scheduling conflicts.
* **Reservation Duration** (6, text, required/optional): Enter the duration of your reservation in text format (e.g., 2 hours, 1 day, etc.). This will help us understand how long you need.
* **Library Name** (7, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the library you will be visiting. This ensures we can direct you to the correct location.
* **Librarian Name** (8, text, required/optional): If you know the name of the librarian you will be working with, please enter it here. This helps us prepare them for your visit.
* **Phone Number** (9, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you with updates or questions.
* **Email** (10, email, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can send you notifications or updates about your reservation.
* **Comment** (11, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or questions you have about your reservation. This is your chance to provide more context.
* **Submit** (12, text, required/optional): Click this button to send your reservation request to us. We will review and process it as soon as possible.

Please fill out the form carefully and submit it. Our library staff will review and process your request to ensure a smooth reservation experience.
