# Institution Verification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Institution Verification Form is used to verify a student's institutional information, including their name, ID, email, phone number, and any additional notes. This form is often used in educational contexts, such as when a student is applying to a program or institution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, entering all required fields.
2. Ensure that you only fill out this form if you are authorized to do so by the institution or the student.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all information is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **student_name**: Enter the full name of the student.
* **student_id**: Enter the student's ID number.
* **student_email**: Enter the student's email address.
* **student_phone**: Enter the student's phone number.
* **student_note**: Enter any additional notes about the student.
* **institution_name**: Enter the name of the institution the student is associated with.
* **institution_id**: Enter the institution's ID number.
* **institution_phone**: Enter the institution's phone number.
* **institution_note**: Enter any additional notes about the institution.

## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any information, it's best to double-check with the student or institution before submitting the form.
* Remember to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all information is correct.
