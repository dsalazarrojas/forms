# Educator Climate Change Literacy Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from educators interested in learning more about climate change and its effects. Please fill out this form carefully to ensure that your submission is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields marked as `required` in the form.
2. Enter your email address and agree to the Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy.
3. Double-check your information before clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your official email address associated with your institution.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your department or institution where you work.
* **Institution** (`institution`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your institution.
* **Position** (`position`, `text`, required): Enter your position or title within your institution.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the city where your institution is located.
* **State** (`state`, `select_one`, required): Select your state from the dropdown list.
* **Country** (`country`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your country.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **I agree to the Terms and Conditions** (`agree_terms`, `select_one`, required): Select "I agree to the Terms and Conditions".
* **I agree to the Privacy Policy** (`agree_privacy`, `select_one`, required): Select "I agree to the Privacy Policy".
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes you'd like to provide.

## Tips
* Make sure you're filling out the form for the correct purpose and department.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact us at [support email or phone number].
