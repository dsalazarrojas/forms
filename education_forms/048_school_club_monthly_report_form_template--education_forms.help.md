# School Club Monthly Report Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Club Monthly Report Form Template is designed to help school clubs report their activities, events, and budget allocations on a monthly basis. This form provides a standardized way for clubs to report their progress and achievements to administrators and advisors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the form sections carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
* Complete all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) in the field label.
* Use the "Event History" section to report all events that occurred during the month.
* Use the "Budgets" section to report on the club's budget allocations.
* Use the "Notes" section to add any additional comments or notes about the club's activities.
* For each event, provide a description, date, and time in the corresponding sections.
* If applicable, provide budget amounts for each event.
* Make sure to review and submit the form accurately and thoroughly.
