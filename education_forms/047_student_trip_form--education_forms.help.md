# Student Trip Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Trip Form is designed to gather essential information about student trips, allowing our team to efficiently plan and coordinate logistics. Please use this form for all student trips to ensure accurate and up-to-date information.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out the form from top to bottom.
- Ensure that you fill out all required fields, but remember that some fields are optional.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Order Tickets** (`order_tickets`, text, optional): Enter the number of tickets you've ordered for the trip. This helps our team keep accurate headcounts.
- **Select Check Out Time** (`select_check_out_time`, select_one, optional): Choose the time of day you'll be checking out. This helps us schedule pickups and dropoffs efficiently.
- **Select Meal Preferences** (`select_meal_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Choose which meals you'd like to have during the trip. This informs our catering team about your preferences.
- **Enter Contact Information** (`enter_contact_information`, select_multiple, optional): Select how you'd like to be contacted during the trip. This helps us reach out to you effectively.

## Tips
- If you're planning multiple trips, it's best to submit this form separately for each trip.
- If you've made an error in filling this form, don't worry – our team is available to help you correct it.
