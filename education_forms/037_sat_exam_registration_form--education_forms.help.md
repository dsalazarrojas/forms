# SAT Exam Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SAT Exam Registration Form is used to register students for the SAT exam. This form is intended for authorized personnel within the institution to collect and record student information for the exam.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate payment method for the student's exam registration.
2. Provide the student's information, including any relevant details.
3. Review and submit the form to complete the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Information (Student Info)**: This field is for entering the student's details, such as name, date of birth, and contact information.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: Not Required
- **Exam Information (Exam Info)**: Enter any relevant exam information, such as exam dates, times, or locations.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: Not Required
- **Select a Payment Method**: Choose the payment method for the student's exam registration. Options include 'Yes' and 'No', indicating whether the student has chosen to pay or not pay for the exam.
  * Type: Select One
  * Required: Not Required
- **Payment Method Selected (Payment Method Selected)**: This field is a note field displaying the selected payment method.
  * Type: Note
  * Required: Not Required
- **Submit**: Click this button to submit the form and complete the registration process.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: Not Required
