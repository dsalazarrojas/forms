# Food Safety And Hygiene Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Safety And Hygiene Training Form is designed to record the completion of food safety and hygiene training sessions. This form helps track training completion data for compliance and record-keeping purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct training completion option for the session.
2. Enter the date of the training session.
3. Enter the duration of the training session.
4. Indicate whether a food safety certificate was issued.
5. Provide the location where the training session took place.
6. (Optional) Enter the name of the facilitator who conducted the training.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Training Completion** (`food_safety_and_hygiene_training_form_1`, Number, Required): This field is used to select the level of completion of the training session. Choose the correct option to indicate whether the training was completed or not.
* **Training Date** (`training_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date on which the training session took place.
* **Training Time** (`training_time`, Time, Required): Enter the duration of the training session. Please format this field as HH:MM.
* **Food Safety Certificate** (`food_safety_certificate`, Select One, Required): Indicate whether a food safety certificate was issued to the trainee.
* **Training Location** (`training_location`, Text, Required): Provide the location where the training session took place.
* **Training Facilitator** (`training_facilitator`, Text, Optional): If you wish to provide additional information about the person who conducted the training, enter their name here (optional).
