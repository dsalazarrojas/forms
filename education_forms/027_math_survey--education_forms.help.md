# Math Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Math Survey is a form designed to collect information from students about their math abilities and interests. This form helps us better understand how students perceive their math abilities and interests, aiding in the creation of relevant and effective math educational resources.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your student name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter your teacher's name in the "Teacher Name" field.
3. Rate your math ability in the "Math Ability" field using a whole number (e.g., 1, 2, 3, etc.).
4. Rate your math interest in the "Math Interest" field using a text description (e.g., "high", "medium", "low").
5. Rate your math ability level 1 using a whole number (e.g., 1, 2, 3, etc.).
6. Rate your math ability level 2 using a whole number (e.g., 1, 2, 3, etc.).
7. Rate your math interest level 1 using a text description (e.g., "high", "medium", "low").
8. Rate your math interest level 2 using a text description (e.g., "high", "medium", "low").

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (Student Name, text, optional): 
    Enter your student name as it appears on your school records.
* **Teacher Name** (Teacher Name, text, optional): 
    Enter your teacher's name.
* **Math Ability** (Math Ability, text, optional): 
    Rate your math ability on a scale from 1 to a high number, where 1 is low and high is high.
* **Math Interest** (Math Interest, text, optional): 
    Rate your math interest on a scale from "low" to "high".
* **Math Ability Level 1** (Math Ability Level 1, number, optional): 
    Rate your math ability level 1 on a scale from 1 to a high number, where 1 is low and high is high.
* **Math Ability Level 2** (Math Ability Level 2, number, optional): 
    Rate your math ability level 2 on a scale from 1 to a high number, where 1 is low and high is high.
* **Math Interest Level 1** (Math Interest Level 1, text, optional): 
    Rate your math interest level 1 on a scale from "low" to "high".
* **Math Interest Level 2** (Math Interest Level 2, text, optional): 
    Rate your math interest level 2 on a scale from "low" to "high".

## Tips

- Please answer the fields truthfully and accurately.
- If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact the relevant authority.
- If you've filled out this form before, please do not fill it out again.
- Ensure that you're a genuine student and not a teacher or other staff member attempting to access the form for administrative purposes.
