# Triple Attendance Sheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Triple Attendance Sheet form is used to record the attendance of students and instructors in a course, providing valuable data for instructors and staff to monitor attendance patterns, provide feedback, and take necessary actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Student Name**, **Email**, and **Phone** fields with the student's information.
2. Enter the **Student Attendance** field with the number of times the student has attended the course.
3. For **Instructor Name**, **Email**, and **Phone**, provide the instructor's contact information (if applicable).
4. Enter the **Instructor Attendance** field with the number of times the instructor has attended the course.
5. In the **Attendance Note** field, add any additional comments or feedback you have about the student's or instructor's attendance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Triple Attendance Sheet** (page 1): This is the title of the form.
* **Student Name** (page 1): Enter the student's name.
* **Signature** (page 2): This is for instructors to sign off on their attendance.
* **Email** (page 2): Enter the student's email address.
* **Phone** (page 3): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Student Attendance** (page 4): Record the number of times the student has attended the course.
* **Instructor Name** (page 4): Enter the instructor's name (if applicable).
* **Email** (page 5): Enter the instructor's email address (if applicable).
* **Phone** (page 5): Enter the instructor's phone number (if applicable).
* **Instructor Attendance** (page 6): Record the number of times the instructor has attended the course.
* **Attendance Note** (page 7): Add any additional comments or feedback about the student's or instructor's attendance.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled out completely.
- Double-check the accuracy of the information you enter before submitting the form.
- If you have any issues or concerns, contact your instructor or staff member for assistance.
