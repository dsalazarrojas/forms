# railway_industry_training_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The railway_industry_training_survey form is designed to collect feedback about the railway industry training experience. It is intended for individuals who have received training related to the railway industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the guidelines provided above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your first name.
### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your last name.
### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Please enter your email address.
### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your phone number.
### What training experience did you have?
* **What training experience did you have?** (`training_experience`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of training experience you had.
	+ Training on a train
	+ Training in a classroom
	+ Other (please specify in the comments)
### Who provided your training?
* **Who provided your training?** (`training_provider`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the name of the training provider you received from.
	+ Rail Safety Academy
	+ Rail Industry Training
	+ Other (please specify in the comments)
### Date
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Please enter the date of the training.
### Time
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Please enter the time of the training.
### Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required: false): Please provide any additional comments or feedback about the training.
### How satisfied were you with the training?
* **How satisfied were you with the training?** (`satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false): Select how satisfied you were with the training.
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Somewhat satisfied
	+ Not satisfied
### Organization
* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the name of the organization you represent.
### Program
* **Program** (`program`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the name of the program you participated in.

Note: This form is for feedback purposes only, and the information collected will be used to improve training programs.
