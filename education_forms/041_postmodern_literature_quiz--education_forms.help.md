# Postmodern Literature Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gauge users' understanding and appreciation of postmodern literature. It is used to collect information about their favorite authors, books, and other related preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Select the correct author of 'The Crying of the Sun' from the provided options.
3. Choose your favorite postmodern author from the list.
4. If your favorite author is not listed, you can provide the name of your favorite book in the 'If not listed, what is your favorite book?' field.
5. Fill in the 'Other book -' field with the name of your favorite book, if applicable.
6. Provide your email address and phone number (if applicable).
7. Confirm your email address and phone number to ensure accuracy.
8. Click the 'Submit' button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is Postmodern literature?** (Text field): This is the first question to introduce the concept of postmodern literature. Please provide a brief description of your understanding of this literary movement.
* **Who is the author of 'The Crying of the Sun'?** (Select One field): Select the correct author from the provided options. This is a multiple-choice question.
* **Who is your favorite postmodern author?** (Select Multiple field): Choose up to 3 postmodern authors from the list.
* **If not listed, what is your favorite book?** (Text field): If your favorite author is not listed, provide the name of your favorite book in this field.
* **Other book -** (Text field): This is a secondary field to provide more information about your favorite book, if applicable.
* **Date of completion** (Date field): This field is not required but can be used to provide the date of completion of the quiz.
* **Time of completion** (Time field): This field is not required and is used to provide the time of completion of the quiz.
* **Additional thoughts** (Note field): This field can be used to provide any additional thoughts or comments about postmodern literature.
* **Email address** (Email field): This is a required field to receive any further communication about the quiz.
* **Phone number** (Text field): This field is optional and can be used to contact you.
* **Confirm email address** (Email field): This field is used to confirm the accuracy of your email address.
* **Confirm phone number** (Text field): This field is used to confirm the accuracy of your phone number.
* **Submit** (Submit button): Click this button to complete and submit the quiz.
