# Academic Innovation Fellowship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Innovation Fellowship Application Form is a comprehensive application form designed to collect information from applicants who wish to be considered for the Academic Innovation Fellowship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal details, including your name and contact email address.
2. Describe your proposed project, including its title, a brief summary of its objectives and methods, and expected outcomes.
3. Provide a high-level budget justification and any relevant references or collaborators.
4. Upload any necessary supporting documents, such as your CV or publications.
5. Ensure you complete all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (*) symbol.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant name**: Your full name as it appears on your official documents. Please enter your name exactly as it is on your passport or government-issued ID.
* **Email address**: Your contact email address where you can be reached for further questions or communication.
* **Institutional affiliation**: The department or research unit you are affiliated with.
* **Project title**: A concise title for your proposed project.
* **Project summary**: A brief description of your project's objectives and methods.
* **Expected outcomes**: Describe the anticipated outputs and impact of your project.
* **Duration and timeline**: If applicable, provide the project start and end dates or duration.
* **Budget summary**: A high-level overview of your project budget and justification.
* **References or collaborators**: List names and contacts for any relevant references or collaborators.
* **Supporting documents**: Upload any necessary documents, such as your CV or publications, to support your application.

## Tips
* Ensure you provide accurate and complete information in all required fields.
* Please proofread your application carefully before submitting to ensure it is error-free and easy to read.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, do not hesitate to contact us at [contact email].
