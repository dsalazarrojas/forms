# Research Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed information about a research project, including the project's objective, methodology, and other relevant details. The form helps researchers provide accurate and comprehensive information to facilitate effective research and collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the "Research Project" field, which should include a brief description of the project's name and details.
2. In the "Research Question" field, provide a clear and concise statement of the research question or problem being investigated.
3. For "Research Objective", describe the main goal or outcome of the research project.
4. If necessary, provide additional information in the "Research Form First Page Additional Information" field.
5. If additional pages are needed, click on the "Next Page" button to access the "Second Page" section.
6. In the "Research Form Second Page" field (if applicable), provide any additional comments or information relevant to the research project.
7. Finally, enter any additional comments or information in the "Research Form Additional Information" field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Research Project** (`research_project`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the research project, including its name and details.
* **Research Question** (`research_question`, text, required): Provide a clear and concise statement of the research question or problem being investigated.
* **Research Objective** (`research_objective`, text, required): Describe the main goal or outcome of the research project.
* **Research Methodology** (`research_methodology`, text, optional): Describe the research methodology or approach used in the project.
* **Research Form First Page Additional Information** (`research_form_first_page_additional_information`, note, optional): Provide any additional information relevant to the research project, such as context or background.
* **Research Form Second Page** (`research_form_second_page`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information relevant to the research project.
* **Research Form Additional Information** (`research_form_additional_information`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about the form or project.
