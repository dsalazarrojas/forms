# Special Education Teacher Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from special education teachers during the hiring process, allowing us to assess their qualifications and experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
- Fill out the New Application field with a brief description of your new application.
- In the Resume field, upload or paste your resume to provide more information about your education and experience.
- In the Experience field, describe your teaching experience and any relevant qualifications.
- In the Certifications field, select all relevant certifications you have obtained.
- In the Qualifications field, select all qualifications that apply to you.
- In the References field, provide references if you have any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Application** (`new_application`, text, optional): A field to provide a brief description of your new application.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, optional): A field to upload or paste your resume, which should include information about your education and experience.
* **Experience** (`experience`, text, optional): A field to describe your teaching experience and any relevant qualifications.
* **Certifications** (`certifications`, multiple-select, optional): A field to choose whether you have obtained specific certifications.
* **Qualifications** (`qualifications`, multiple-select, optional): A field to choose your qualifications.
* **References** (`references`, text, optional): A field to provide references if you have any.

## Tips
- Please proofread your answers carefully before submission to ensure there are no errors or typos.
- If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, please don't hesitate to contact us.
- We appreciate your honest and concise answers to help us assess your qualifications accurately.
