# Oral Examination Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from examiners on the performance of a candidate during an oral examination. This feedback will be used to help improve the assessment process and the candidate's development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required and optional fields as instructed.
2. Make sure you have the relevant information and context about the candidate and the examination before submitting your feedback.
3. Avoid duplication of effort by not submitting multiple versions of the same form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Name** (`candidate_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the candidate who underwent the oral examination.
* **Examiner Name** (`examiner_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the examiner who conducted the oral examination.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the oral examination.
* **Score** (`score`, number, optional): Enter the score you awarded to the candidate during the oral examination.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any comments or feedback you have about the candidate's performance.

## Tips
* Be accurate and thorough when filling in your feedback.
* Avoid using jargon or technical terms that may be unfamiliar to others.
* Focus on providing constructive feedback that will help the candidate improve.
