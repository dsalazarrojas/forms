# Student Representative Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Representative Nomination Form is designed to collect information about students who are being nominated for a representative role within the organization. This form helps to ensure that the nomination process is fair and well-documented.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter the student's ID number in the "Student ID" field.
3. Select the date of nomination in the "Nomination Date" field.
4. Enter the student's email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter the student's phone number in the "Phone" field (optional).
6. Enter the reason for nomination in the "Reason for Nomination" field.
7. Enter your name in the "Nominator Name" field.
8. Enter your email address in the "Nominator Email" field.
9. Enter your phone number in the "Nominator Phone" field (optional).
10. Enter your message to the student in the "Nominator Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nomination Form**: This field is not visible to the user, it's just a page label.
* **Student Name**: Enter the student's name you are nominating.
* **Student ID**: Enter the student's ID number.
* **Nomination Date**: Select the date of nomination.
* **Email**: Enter the student's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the student's phone number (optional).
* **Reason for Nomination**: Enter the reason why you are nominating this student.
* **Nominator Name**: Enter your name.
* **Nominator Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Nominator Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Nominator Message**: Enter your message to the student.
