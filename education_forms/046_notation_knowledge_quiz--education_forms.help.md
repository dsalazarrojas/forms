# Notation Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Notation Knowledge Quiz is a tool to assess a student's knowledge on musical notes, their relationships, and their ability to recognize which notes sound an octave higher than others.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the Student Name field with the student's name.
2. Answer the two musical notation questions by selecting the correct note that sounds an octave higher than the others.
3. Fill in the Score fields for each question with your assessment of the student's answer.
4. Enter the student's email, phone number, and school name for communication and record-keeping purposes.
5. If desired, add a comment from the teacher and the student.
6. Finally, click the Submit button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's name for identification and record-keeping purposes.
* **Which notation is correct for this note?** (`quiz_question_one`, select_one, required): Select the correct note that sounds higher than the others.
	+ Select one of the options A, B, C, D, or E.
* **Score for question 1** (`student_score_one`, number, required): Assess the student's answer as a score out of a possible maximum.
* **Which of these notes sound an octave higher than the others?** (`quiz_question_two`, select_multiple, required): Select all notes that sound higher than the others.
	+ Select multiple notes from A, C, or E.
* **Score for question 2** (`student_score_two`, number, required): Assess the student's answer as a score out of a possible maximum.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, optional): Enter the student's email address for communication and record-keeping purposes.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, optional): Enter the student's phone number for communication and record-keeping purposes.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the teacher for identification and record-keeping purposes.
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication and record-keeping purposes.
* **Teacher Phone** (`teacher_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication and record-keeping purposes.
* **Score for 1st question** (`teacher_score_one`, number, required): Assess your question 1 answer as a score out of a possible maximum.
* **Score for 2nd question** (`teacher_score_two`, number, required): Assess your question 2 answer as a score out of a possible maximum.
* **Teacher Comment** (`teacher_comment`, text, optional): Add any comments about the student's performance.
* **Student Comment** (`student_comment`, text, optional): Add any comments from the student.
* **Subject** (`student_subject`, text, optional): Enter the subject being taught.
* **Quiz Date** (`quiz_date`, date, optional): Record the date of the quiz.
* **Quiz Time** (`quiz_time`, time, optional): Record the time of the quiz.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter the school name.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, text, optional): Enter the school address.
* **Answer 1** (`student_answer_one`, select_one, optional): Select the correct answer for the first question.
* **Answer 2** (`student_answer_two`, select_one, optional): Select the correct answer for the second question.
* **Answer 3** (`student_answer_three`, select_one, optional): Select the correct answer for the third question.
* **Answer 4** (`student_answer_four`, select_one, optional): Select the correct answer for the fourth question.
* **Answer** (`student_answer_five`, select_one, optional): Select the correct answer for the fifth question.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, button, required): Click to submit the form after completing all fields.
