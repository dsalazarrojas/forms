# Word Validation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Word Validation Survey is designed to collect information about an individual's reading and writing habits.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Answer all the questions in each section to the best of your ability.
* Be honest and accurate with your responses.
* Make sure to complete all questions in each section before proceeding to the next one.
* Review and submit your answers once you have completed all sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**:
	+ This section serves as an introduction to the survey and explains its purpose.
* **Section A - Comprehension**:
	+ **What type of texts do you usually read?**:
		- This question asks about your reading preferences, and you can select all that apply.
		- For example, if you read both newspapers and novels, select both options.
	+ **Which type of texts do you usually write?**:
		- Similar to the previous question, this one asks about your writing preferences.
		- Choose all applicable options for your writing habits.
	+ **What is your favorite reading material?**:
		- Select one option for your favorite type of reading material.
		- This will help us understand what you enjoy reading the most.
* **Section B - Usage**:
	+ This section looks into your actual usage of texts in daily activities.
	+ **How often do you read?** and **How often do you write?**:
		- These two questions ask about your frequency of reading and writing activities.
		- Please answer with a numerical value representing how often you perform these activities.
