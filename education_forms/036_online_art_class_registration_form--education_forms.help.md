# online_art_class_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The online art class registration form is used for registering for an online art class. To complete the form, follow the steps below.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the necessary information for each field. You will be asked for the following:

- First Name: This is your name.
- Last Name: This is your last name.
- Email: This is your email address. Please make sure it is valid and up to date.
- Phone Number: This is your phone number. 
- Art Class: Select the type of art class you are interested in taking. You can choose one of the options provided.
- Start Date: Select the start date for the art class.
- End Date: Select the end date for the art class.
- Time: Select the time for the art class.
- Art Level: Select the level of art you are interested in taking.
- Payment Method: Check if you have a payment method. 
- Payment Amount: If you have a payment method, enter the amount you are willing to pay.
- Note: If you have any additional comments, please provide them here.
- Submit: Click the submit button to send in your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your first name.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your last name.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address. Please make sure it is valid and up to date.

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number. 

### Art Class
* **Art Class** (`art_class`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of art class you are interested in taking. You can choose one of the options provided.

### Start Date
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, optional): Select the start date for the art class.

### End Date
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, optional): Select the end date for the art class.

### Time
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): Select the time for the art class.

### Art Level
* **Art Level** (`art_level`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the level of art you are interested in taking.

### Payment Method
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, optional): Check if you have a payment method. 

### Payment Amount
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, `number`, optional): If you have a payment method, enter the amount you are willing to pay.

### Note
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): If you have any additional comments, please provide them here.

### Submit
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, optional): Click the submit button to send in your registration.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter valid information for each field.
- Select the correct payment method and amount if you have a payment method.
- Enter your email address correctly. 
- Select the correct start and end date for the art class. 
- Enter your phone number correctly. 
- Select the correct art level and class you are interested in taking. 
- Click the submit button when you are finished.
