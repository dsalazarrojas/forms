# Practice Questions Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather your feedback and input on practice questions in an educational setting. We value your honesty and suggestions in helping us improve the quality and relevance of our practice questions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Read each question carefully and respond to the ones that apply to you.
2. Answer all questions that you feel are relevant to your experience with practice questions.
3. You can leave blank any questions that do not apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, text, optional): This is where you can provide any background information about your experience with practice questions.
* **Questions** (`questions`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the questions that you found helpful or unhelpful during your practice question experience. You can select multiple answers if they apply.
* **Retention** (`retention`, text, optional): If you'd like to provide any comments on how well you retained information from the practice questions.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, optional): Let us know if you'd like to submit your feedback now or if you're not ready to submit yet. You can select "Yes" to submit or "No" to save and come back later.
* **[Question one]** (`question one`, text, optional): This is a question about your experience with a specific question.
* **[Question two]** (`question two`, text, optional): This is another question about your experience with a specific question.
* **[Question three]** (`question three`, text, optional): This is yet another question about your experience with a specific question.
* ... (repeat for questions 4-20)
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, optional): This is the final step to submit your feedback and suggestions. Select "Yes" to submit or "No" to save and come back later.

Note: You can skip any questions that do not apply to you, and your feedback is greatly appreciated regardless of how many questions you answer.
