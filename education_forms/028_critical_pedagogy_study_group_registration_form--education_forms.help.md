# Critical Pedagogy Study Group Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Critical Pedagogy Study Group Registration Form is a tool for users to register their participation in a study group focused on critical pedagogy. This form collects information about the user's identity, study group preferences, and meeting schedule.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User Name.
2. Enter your valid Email address.
3. Enter your Password.
4. Confirm your Password.
5. Select your role in the study group (Member, Leader, Observer, or Teacher).
6. Choose the Department you are affiliated with (Department of Education, Department of Fine Arts, Department of Humanities, or Department of Science).
7. Enter the name of your study group.
8. Select the type of study group (Online or Offline).
9. Enter the start and end dates of your study group.
10. Provide a brief Description of your study group (optional).
11. Choose the Meeting Time (optional).
12. Select the Meeting Frequency (optional).
13. Choose the Meeting Days (optional).
14. Enter the Meeting Duration (optional).
15. Enter the Location of your meetings (optional).
16. Enter your Phone number (optional).
17. Enter the Join Code (optional).
18. Enter the name of the user who submitted the form (optional).
19. Enter the date the form was submitted (optional).
20. Enter the Status of your study group (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (1, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email** (2, email, required): Enter a valid email address associated with your university account.
* **Password** (3, text, required): Enter the password for your university account.
* **Confirm Password** (4, text, required): Re-enter your password to confirm it.
* **Role** (5, select_one, required): Select your role in the study group (Member, Leader, Observer, or Teacher).
* **Department** (6, select_multiple, required): Choose the department you are affiliated with (Department of Education, Department of Fine Arts, Department of Humanities, or Department of Science).
* **Study Group** (7, text, required): Enter the name of your study group.
* **Study Group Type** (8, select_one, required): Select the type of study group (Online or Offline).
* **Start Date** (9, date, required): Enter the start date of your study group.
* **End Date** (10, date, required): Enter the end date of your study group.
* **Description** (11, note, optional): Provide a brief description of your study group (max 255 characters).
* **Meeting Time** (12, time, optional): Choose your preferred meeting time (e.g., 2 PM).
* **Meeting Frequency** (13, select_multiple, optional): Select the frequency of your meetings (Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
* **Meeting Days** (14, select_multiple, optional): Choose the days you meet (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday).
* **Meeting Duration** (15, text, optional): Enter the duration of your meetings (e.g., 1 hour).
* **Location** (16, text, optional): Enter the location of your meetings (e.g., Room 101).
* **Phone** (17, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Join Code** (18, text, optional): Enter the join code for your study group (if applicable).
* **Submitted By** (19, text, optional): Enter the name of the user who submitted the form.
* **Submitted On** (20, date, optional): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Status** (21, text, optional): Enter the status of your study group (e.g., Active, Inactive).
