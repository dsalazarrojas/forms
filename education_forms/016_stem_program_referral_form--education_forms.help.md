# stem_program_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "stem_program_referral_form" is a comprehensive form used to collect information about a student for referrals or admissions purposes within the education sector.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the General section with the student's information.
2. Provide information about the student's education background, including the date of Education Background 1 and selecting the relevant options for Education Background 2 and Education Background 3.
3. Choose the student's program preferences, selecting the relevant options for Program Preferences 1 and 2.
4. Select the student's academic support needs for Academic Support 1.
5. Answer the family-related questions, selecting the relevant options for Family 1 and 2.
6. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the student's general information, including their name, contact details, etc.
* **Student Information** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the student's personal details, such as their name, email, phone number, etc.
* **Education Background** (`3`, `text`, required/optional): Provide information about the student's education background, including any relevant courses or qualifications.
* **Program Preferences** (`4`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the student's preferences for programs, such as their interests or goals.
* **Academic Support** (`5`, `text`, required/optional): Specify the student's academic support needs, such as tutoring or language classes.
* **Family** (`6`, `text`, required/optional): Enter information about the student's family, including any relevant details.
* **Emergency Contact** (`7`, `text`, required/optional): Provide the contact information for the student's emergency contact.
* **Student Programs** (`8`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the student's programs or courses of interest.
* **Other Programs** (`9`, `text`, required/optional): Specify any other programs the student is interested in or currently enrolled in.
* **Submit** (`10`, `text`, required/optional): Confirm your submission.
* **Submit Review** (`11`, `text`, required/optional): Review and confirm your submission before final submission.
* **Review** (`12`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback about the form submission.
* **Submit Confirmation** (`13`, `text`, required/optional): Confirm the submission confirmation.
* **Student Programs 1** (`14`, `select_one`, required): Select the relevant option for the student's program(s) of interest.
* **Student Programs 2** (`15`, `select_multiple`, required): Select multiple relevant options for the student's program(s) of interest.
* **Education Background 1** (`16`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the student's education background.
* **Education Background 2** (`17`, `select_one`, required): Select the relevant option for the student's education background.
* **Education Background 3** (`18`, `select_multiple`, required): Select multiple relevant options for the student's education background.
* **Education Background 4** (`19`, `select_one`, required): Select the relevant option for the student's education background.
* **Program Preferences 1** (`20`, `select_one`, required): Select the relevant option for the student's program preferences.
* **Program Preferences 2** (`21`, `select_multiple`, required): Select multiple relevant options for the student's program preferences.
* **Academic Support 1** (`22`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the student's academic support.
* **Academic Support 2** (`23`, `select_one`, required): Select the relevant option for the student's academic support needs.
* **Family 1** (`24`, `select_multiple`, required): Select multiple relevant options for the student's family.
* **Family 2** (`25`, `select_one`, required): Select the relevant option for the student's family.

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Review and confirm each section carefully before final submission.
* If you need help or clarification about any field, contact the relevant authority or support team.
