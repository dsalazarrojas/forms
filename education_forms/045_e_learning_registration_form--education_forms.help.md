# E Learning Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The E Learning Registration Form is a document used for registering students to an online course. It is intended to be completed by students to provide necessary information to the course administrators for course enrollment and other related activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your **Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Email** in the second field.
3. Provide your **Contact Number** in the third field.
4. Select the **Course Name** you are registered for in the fourth field.
5. Enter your **Student Number** in the last field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Name (1) (text, required: false)
* Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Email (2) (email, required: false)
* Enter a valid email address associated with your student account.

### Contact Number (3) (text, required: false)
* Enter your contact number where you can be reached for course-related activities.

### Course Name (4) (text, required: false)
* Select the name of the course you are enrolling in from the provided list.

### Student Number (5) (text, required: false)
* Enter your student identification number for course registration.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* If you encounter any issues with the form, contact your course administrator for assistance.
