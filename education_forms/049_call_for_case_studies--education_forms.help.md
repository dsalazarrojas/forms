# Call For Case Studies - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Call For Case Studies form is a submission tool for researchers, authors, and experts to share their case studies with the public. This form collects essential information about the case study, including author details, study characteristics, and submission requirements. It is used to evaluate and publish case studies on various topics, focusing on areas like Business Strategy, Operations Management, Marketing, Finance, Technology, Human Resources, Sustainability, and Innovation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your **Author Information**:
    *   **Author Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your passport or official ID.
    *   **Email Address**: Enter your official email address associated with your institution or organization.
    *   **Institution or Organization**: Enter the name of the institution or organization you represent.
    *   **Position or Title**: Enter your position or title within your institution or organization (optional).
2.  Provide **Case Study Details**:
    *   **Case Study Title**: Enter a concise title for your case study.
    *   **Subject Organization or Entity**: Enter the name of the organization or entity related to the case study.
    *   **Case Study Description**: Write a brief overview of the case study.
3.  Choose your **Primary Focus Area**: Select the primary area of focus for your case study from the provided options.
4.  Enter **Keywords**: Comma-separated list of up to 5 keywords related to your case study.
5.  Describe your **Research Methodology**: Explain how the case study was conducted.
6.  List your **Data Sources**: Select all the data sources used in your case study from the provided options.
7.  Specify the **Time Period Covered**: Enter the time period covered in your case study.
8.  **Key Findings or Outcomes**: Enter the main findings or outcomes of your case study.
9.  **Lessons Learned**: Enter any lessons or insights obtained from your case study (optional).
10. Enter **Recommendations or Implications**: Suggest actions or implications arising from your case study (optional).
11. Provide **Document Information**:
    *   **Estimated Word Count**: Enter the estimated word count of your submission.
    *   **Submission Format**: Select the format in which you would like to submit your case study (e.g., PDF, Word Document, Google Docs).
12. **Verify Originality**: Confirm that your work is original and approved for publication.
