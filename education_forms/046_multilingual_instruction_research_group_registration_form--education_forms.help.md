# multilingual_instruction_research_group_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in joining a research group, focusing on their personal details, research interests, and contact information. It is intended to facilitate the formation and registration of research groups.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Ensure you have all the necessary information ready, as some fields are required.
- Fill in your First Name, Last name, Email, and Date of registration, as they are required fields.
- Choose your preferred Language from the dropdown menu.
- Select the type of Research group you are interested in (Academic, Research, or Education).
- If you have time, fill in your Phone and Address fields for contact purposes.
- You can specify your Research group size and interests, but these fields are not required.
- If you have a project, provide details like Project start date, Project budget, and Research group funding.
- Finally, list your Research group members and their roles (if you're a group leader).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **First Name (text, required: false)**
First Name: Enter your first name. This is not a required field, but it's useful for us to know.

### 2. **Last Name (text, required: true)**
Last Name: Enter your last name. This field is required.

### 3. **Email (email, required: true)**
Email: Enter a valid email address. This field is required for communication.

### 4. **Phone (text, required: false)**
Phone: Enter your phone number for contact purposes. This is not required but helpful.

### 5. **Address (text, required: false)**
Address: Enter your address for contact purposes. This is not required but helpful.

### 6. **Research Group (text, required: false)**
Research Group: Enter the name of the research group you are interested in. This field is not required but useful.

### 7. **Country (text, required: false)**
Country: Select your country of origin. This field is not required but helpful for geographical purposes.

### 8. **Language (select_one, required: true)**
Language: Choose the language you prefer to communicate in. This field is required.

### 9. **Time Zone (time, required: false)**
Time Zone: Select your timezone for scheduling purposes. This field is not required but helpful.

### 10. **Date of Birth (date, required: false)**
Date of Birth: Enter your date of birth for demographic purposes. This field is not required but helpful.

### 11. **Date of Registration (date, required: true)**
Date of Registration: Enter the date you are registering for the research group. This field is required.

### 12. **Research Group Type (select_multiple, required: true)**
Research Group Type: Choose the type of research group you are interested in (Academic, Research, or Education). This field is required.

### 13. **Research Group Size (number, required: false)**
Research Group Size: Enter the number of people in your research group. This is not required but useful.

### 14. **Research Area (text, required: false)**
Research Area: Describe the area of research your group is focused on. This field is not required but helpful.

### 15. **Research Interests (text, required: false)**
Research Interests: Enter your research interests. This field is not required but helpful.

### 16. **Contact Name (text, required: false)**
Contact Name: Enter the contact name for your research group. This field is not required but helpful.

### 17. **Contact Info (text, required: false)**
Contact Info: Enter any additional contact information for your group. This field is not required but helpful.

### 18. **Research Group Description (text, required: false)**
Research Group Description: Provide a brief description of your research group. This field is not required but helpful.

### 19. **Research Group Leader (text, required: false)**
Research Group Leader: Enter the leader's name of your research group. This field is not required but helpful.

### 20. **Research Group Members (text, required: false)**
Research Group Members: List the members of your research group. This field is not required but helpful.

### 21. **Research Group Members Count (number, required: false)**

Project End Date: Enter the end date of your project. This field is not required but helpful.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that you have filled in all required fields.
*   Double-check your details for accuracy before submitting.
*   If you need more information, contact the administrators of the research group.
