# online_course_completion_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from students who have completed an online course. Your input helps us evaluate the course and identify areas for improvement, allowing us to provide better educational experiences in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly. The more feedback we receive, the better we can understand your experience.
2. Make sure to complete all required questions (marked as "Required" in the form).
3. For questions with multiple options, select the one that best describes your experience.
4. You can provide additional feedback in the "Any additional feedback about the course?" text box if you have anything else you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
* **Introduction** (Page 1): This section is a brief overview of the form. Please don't worry about writing an essay here; just a short sentence or two to get us started.

### How would you rate the effectiveness of this course?
* **How would you rate the effectiveness of this course?** (Page 2): Choose how you feel the course helped you achieve its learning objectives. 
  - **Highly effective**: The course helped you achieve its goals completely.
  - **Somewhat effective**: The course helped you somewhat achieve its goals.
  - **Not very effective**: The course partially helped you achieve its goals.
  - **Not at all effective**: The course didn't help you at all with its goals.

### How would you rate the support received from the instructor?
* **How would you rate the support received from the instructor?** (Page 3): This question helps us understand how well the instructor provided guidance and support throughout the course.
  - **Highly supportive**: The instructor was very helpful and provided excellent support.
  - **Somewhat supportive**: The instructor was somewhat helpful, but could improve.
  - **Not very supportive**: The instructor was not very helpful.
  - **Not at all supportive**: The instructor was not helpful at all.

### Overall, how would you rate your experience with this course?
* **Overall, how would you rate your experience with this course?** (Page 4): This question helps us understand your overall satisfaction with the course.
  - **Highly satisfied**: You had a very good experience with the course.
  - **Somewhat satisfied**: You had a somewhat good experience with the course.
  - **Neutral**: You had a neutral experience with the course.
  - **Somewhat dissatisfied**: You had a somewhat negative experience with the course.
  - **Highly dissatisfied**: You had a very negative experience with the course.

### Would you recommend this course to a friend or colleague?
* **Would you recommend this course to a friend or colleague?** (Page 5): This question helps us understand if you think others would benefit from the course.
  - **True**: Yes, you would recommend the course.
  - **False**: No, you would not recommend the course.

### Any additional feedback about the course?
* **Any additional feedback about the course?** (Page 6): This is an open text field where you can provide any comments, suggestions, or concerns you have about the course. Please keep in mind that this feedback will help us improve the course for future students.
