# Educational Comfort Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Comfort Assessment form is designed for educators to evaluate the comfort level of their students in a learning environment. This form helps teachers understand the effectiveness of their teaching practices and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the learning environment where you taught the class.
2. Choose the learning style used in the class (e.g., lecture, discussion, group work).
3. Describe the teaching method used (e.g., lecturing, hands-on, project-based).
4. Rate your interaction with the students during the class (e.g., frequent, infrequent).
5. Rate the interaction between students during the class (e.g., frequent, infrequent).
6. Describe the presentation used by the teacher (e.g., slides, videos, textbooks).
7. List the outcomes or results achieved by the students (e.g., learning objectives met, skills developed).
8. Rate the comfort level of the students in the class (e.g., comfortable, uncomfortable).
9. Provide any suggestions for improvement or notes about the class.
10. Add any additional comments or feedback about the class.
11. If applicable, select the tool or resource assigned to the class (e.g., textbook, online platform).
11. Enter the date and time when you submitted this assessment.
12. Enter the ID of this form for tracking purposes.
13. Optional: If you have any additional information or notes, please enter them below.
