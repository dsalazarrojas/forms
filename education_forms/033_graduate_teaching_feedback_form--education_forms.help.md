# Graduate Teaching Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Graduate Teaching Feedback Form is used to gather feedback from educators and administrators on graduate students' teaching performance. It will help evaluate the quality of teaching, identify areas for improvement, and inform professional development opportunities for graduate students.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the "Teaching" section with a brief description of the graduate's teaching approach, methods, and effectiveness.
2. Provide feedback on the graduate's teaching in the "Feedback" section. Please be specific and objective in your comments.
3. In the "Evaluation" section, select "Yes" if the graduate's teaching meets or exceeds expectations, or "No" if it falls short of expectations. This will help us track progress and identify areas that need improvement.
4. Share any additional comments or suggestions in the "Comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teaching** (`graduate_teaching_feedback_form_1`, text, required): Describe the graduate's teaching approach, methods, and effectiveness. Please include specific examples and examples from your interactions with the graduate.
* **Feedback** (`graduate_teaching_feedback_form_2`, text, required): Provide feedback on the graduate's teaching, focusing on specific strengths and areas for improvement. Be concrete and objective in your comments.
* **Evaluation** (`graduate_teaching_feedback_form_3`, select_one, optional): Evaluate the graduate's teaching by selecting "Yes" if it meets or exceeds expectations, or "No" if it falls short. This will help us track progress and identify areas that need improvement.
* **Comments** (`graduate_teaching_feedback_form_4`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or suggestions for the graduate's professional development.

## Tips

* Please ensure that each field is completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Avoid duplicating comments, as this may lead to confusion and affect the form's utility.
* Be specific and objective in your feedback, focusing on facts and data rather than personal opinions or biases.
* If you are unsure about any aspect of the form, please reach out to your administrator or supervisor for guidance.
