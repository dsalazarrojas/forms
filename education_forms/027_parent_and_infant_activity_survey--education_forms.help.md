# Parent And Infant Activity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your activities related to your infant. Please answer the questions honestly to help us understand how you engage with your child.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below for each page:

1. Read the introduction to understand the purpose of this form.
2. On page 2, select the frequency with which you talk to your infant.
3. On page 3, select all the activities you often do with your infant.
4. On page 4, select the frequency with which you play with your infant.
5. On page 5, select the activities you often do with your infant (other than those listed).
6. On page 6, select the frequency with which you learn with your infant.
7. On page 7, describe any other activities you do with your infant (if any).
8. On page 8, enter your email address (if you'd like to be contacted about this form).
9. On page 9, enter your phone number (if you'd like to be contacted about this form).
10. On page 10, confirm your submission and date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Page 1): This page provides background information about the form and its purpose.
* **How Often Parents Talk** (Page 2): Select the frequency with which you talk to your infant. The options are:
	+ Parent very frequently
	+ Parent occasionally
	+ Parent seldom
* **How Often Parents Read** (Page 3): Select all the activities you often do with your infant, such as reading, watching TV/videos, or listening to music/podcasts.
* **How Often Parents Play** (Page 4): Select the frequency with which you play with your infant. The options are:
	+ Parent very frequently
	+ Parent occasionally
	+ Parent seldom
* **How Often Parents Tend** (Page 5): Select all the activities you often do with your infant, such as tending to their garden or playing sports.
* **Other Activities** (Page 7): Describe any other activities you do with your infant that are not listed above.
* **Email** (Page 8): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted about this form.
* **Phone** (Page 9): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted about this form.
* **Submission** (Page 10): Confirm your submission and the date.

## Tips
Please answer all questions honestly and accurately. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
