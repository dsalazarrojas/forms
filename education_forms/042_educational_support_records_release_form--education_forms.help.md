# Educational Support Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Support Records Release Form is used to collect and document the educational history and contact information of students, ensuring that institutions have the necessary records to support the student's educational journey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the `Student Name` field.
2. Provide the student's ID number in the `Student ID` field.
3. Enter the parent/guardian's name and contact email in the `Parent Guardian Name` and `Parent Guardian Email` fields respectively.
4. Record the date and time of consent in the `Consent Date` and `Consent Time` fields.
5. Enter the name of the person who completed the form in the `Form Completed By` field.
6. Add your signature in the `Signature` field.
7. Provide the parent/guardian's phone number in the `Parent Guardian Phone` field.
8. Enter a consent message in the `Consent Message` field.
9. Enter the institution's name in the `Institution` field.
10. Enter the department or division in the `Department` field.
11. Enter your role or position in the `Role` field.
12. Enter the contact person's name in the `Contact Person` field.
13. Enter the contact person's email in the `Contact Email` field.
14. Enter the contact person's phone number in the `Contact Phone` field.
15. Enter the school year in the `School Year` field.
16. Enter any additional comments about the student in the `Form Comments` field.
17. Enter any other comments in the `Form Comments 2` field.
18. Enter the parent/guardian's address in the `Parent Guardian Address` field.
19. Enter the student's date of birth in the `Student DOB` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Information**: This field is not required but is a placeholder for any additional information about the form.
* **Title**: Enter a brief title describing the purpose of the form.
* **Student Name**: Enter the student's name.
* **Student ID**: Enter the student's ID number.
* **Parent Guardian Name**: Enter the name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent Guardian Email**: Enter the email address of the parent or guardian.
* **Consent Date**: Enter the date on which the consent was granted.
* **Consent Time**: Enter the time on which the consent was granted.
* **Form Completed By**: Enter the name of the person who completed the form.
* **Signature**: Add a handwritten signature as proof of consent.
* **Parent Guardian Phone**: Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian.
* **Consent Message**: Enter a message explaining the purpose and scope of the consent.
* **Institution**: Enter the name of the educational institution.
* **Department**: Enter the department or division.
* **Role**: Enter your role or position.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **School Year**: Enter the school year in which the student is enrolled.
* **Form Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the student.
* **Form Comments 2**: Enter any other comments about the student.
* **Parent Guardian Address**: Enter the address of the parent or guardian.
* **Student DOB**: Enter the student's date of birth.
