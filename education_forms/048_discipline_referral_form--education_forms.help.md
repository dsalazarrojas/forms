# Discipline Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document incidents involving students that require referral for disciplinary action. It helps to provide a structured way of gathering information and ensuring consistency in the referral process. Before completing this form, please review the school's policies and procedures for referrals to ensure the incident is not a duplicate or covered by existing rules.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information as the referral officer or teacher.
2. Enter the student's name and other relevant details.
3. Provide the date and location of the incident.
4. Give a brief description of what happened.
5. Choose the reason for referral from the options provided.
6. Suggest potential consequences or disciplinary actions for the student.
7. Choose the actions that will be taken in response to the incident.
8. Set the follow-up date and time if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the teacher who is referring the student.
  - This field is required to identify the referring teacher.
- **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the student involved in the incident.
  - This field is optional, but it's recommended to fill it in for accurate tracking.
- **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the incident.
  - This field is optional, but it's best to provide the exact date for accurate record-keeping.
- **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the location of the incident.
  - This field is optional, but it's useful to provide the location for context.
- **Details** (`details`, text, required/optional): Give a brief description of what happened during the incident.
  - This field is required to provide context for the referral.
- **Reason for Referral** (`referral_reason`, text, required/optional): Choose the reason for referral from the provided options.
  - Select the reason that best describes the incident.
- **Referral Suggestions** (`referral_suggestions`, text, required/optional): Suggest potential consequences or disciplinary actions for the student.
  - This field is optional, but it's useful for providing context.
- **Action Taken** (`action_taken`, select_one, required): Choose the actions that will be taken in response to the incident.
  - Select one of the provided options to ensure consistency in disciplinary actions.
- **Disciplinary Action** (`disciplinary_action`, select_multiple, required): Choose multiple actions that will be taken in response to the incident.
  - Select multiple actions from the provided options to ensure a comprehensive response.
- **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, required/optional): Set the date for follow-up on the incident.
  - This field is optional, but it's recommended to set a follow-up date for tracking.
- **Follow-up Time** (`follow_up_time`, time, required/optional): Set the time for follow-up on the incident.
  - This field is optional, but it's useful for scheduling follow-up meetings or interventions.
