# Exam Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help students or test-takers sign up for an exam. It captures necessary contact information and exam details to ensure a smooth exam process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your email address in the "user_email" field.
2. Enter your phone number in the "user_phone" field.
3. Select your exam date and time in the "exam_date" and "exam_time" fields.
4. Provide a note regarding your exam in the "exam_note" field.
5. Enter the title of the exam in the "exam_title" field.
6. Choose an exam category in the "exam_category" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **user_email** (Email, required: false): Enter your email address to stay updated on exam details and schedules.
* **user_phone** (Phone Number, required: false): Provide your phone number so we can contact you about exam arrangements.
* **exam_date** (Date, required: false): Choose the date for your exam.
* **exam_time** (Time, required: false): Select the time for your exam.
* **exam_note** (Note, required: false): Enter any additional information or comments about your exam.
* **exam_title** (Exam Title, required: false): Enter the title of the exam you're signing up for.
* **exam_category** (Exam Category, required: false): Select the category of the exam you're signing up for.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your exam date and time before submitting the form.
* Ensure your email address and phone number are up-to-date and accurate.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, include them in the "exam_note" field.
