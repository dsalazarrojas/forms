# Academic Writing Collaboration Network Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Writing Collaboration Network Registration form is designed for academics and researchers to connect with peers who share their research interests and goals. By registering, you can join a network that provides a supportive environment for collaboration, accountability, and mutual growth throughout the writing process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your First name
2.  Enter your Last name
3.  Enter your Email address
4.  Enter your Phone number (optional)
5.  Choose your Institution or university
6.  Select your Department
7.  Select your Academic role
8.  Choose your Primary research areas or disciplines
9.  Select the current stage of your primary writing project
10.  Choose the type of collaboration you are looking for
11.  Select your Preferred meeting format
12.  Choose your Preferred meeting times
13.  Enter your main goals for joining the network
14.  Add any additional information you would like the network coordinators to know

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Last name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Email address**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for communication related to the network.
*   **Phone number**: This is optional, but it can be useful for coordinators to contact you for more information or meetings.
*   **Institution or university**: Select your current institution or university from which you are affiliated.
*   **Department**: Choose your department within the institution.
*   **Academic role**: Select the role you hold within your department (e.g., Undergraduate Student, Graduate Student - Masters, Faculty, etc.).
*   **Primary research areas or disciplines**: Select all the areas or disciplines that best match your research focus.
*   **Current stage of your primary writing project**: Choose the current stage your primary writing project is in.
*   **What type of collaboration are you looking for**: Choose all the types of collaboration you are interested in (e.g., Writing accountability partner, Peer reviewer or feedback partner, etc.).
*   **Preferred meeting format**: Select your preferred way to meet or interact with others in the network.
*   **Preferred meeting times**: Select all the time slots that are most convenient for you.
*   **What are your main goals for joining the network**: Describe the primary goals you have for joining the network.
*   **Anything else you would like the network coordinators to know**: Provide any additional information that you think will help the network coordinators understand your needs or concerns.

Note: Ensure that all required fields are completed to ensure a successful registration.
