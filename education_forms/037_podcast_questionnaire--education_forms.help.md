# Podcast Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help podcasters create engaging and relevant content for their podcast episodes. It will be used by internal teams to review and approve content before it is published.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the relevant options for each field as it applies to your podcast episode. You can choose multiple options for the "Topics To Cover" field and can provide a brief description for each topic. You do not need to fill out fields that do not apply to your episode.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Topics To Cover** (`topics_to_cover`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the topics that you want to cover in your podcast episode.
* **Topic Name** (`topic_name`, text, optional): Provide a brief name for the topic you are covering.
* **Topic Description** (`topic_description`, text, optional): Give a brief description of the topic you are covering.
* **Topic Length** (`topic_length`, select_one, optional): Indicate if this topic will be covered in a short or long format (e.g. 5-10 minutes or 30-60 minutes).
* **Time For Session** (`time_for_session`, time, optional): Select the time of day you plan to record this podcast episode.
* **Session Length** (`session_length`, text, optional): Provide the expected length of the session in minutes.
* **Session Type** (`session_type`, select_one, optional): Indicate if this is a live or pre-recorded session.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, text, optional): Provide the time of the day you plan to publish this podcast episode.
* **Topic Frequency** (`topic_frequency`, select_one, optional): Indicate how frequently this topic will be covered in your podcast.
