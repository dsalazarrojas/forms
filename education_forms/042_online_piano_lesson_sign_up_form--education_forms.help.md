# Online Piano Lesson Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for students who want to sign up for online piano lessons. It helps instructors plan and schedule lessons that fit your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your student information, including your name and contact details.
2. Choose your lesson schedule (e.g., Monday, Wednesday, Friday or other options).
3. Select your preferred start and end dates for the lessons.
4. Choose your lesson duration and frequency (e.g., Once a week or other options).
5. Choose the type of instrument you want to play (e.g., Piano, Guitar, Violin, Cello).
6. Provide any additional contact details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information** (`student_info`, text, required/optional):
	+ This field is for your student name, contact number, and other relevant details.
* **Lesson Schedule** (`lesson_schedule`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select the days you are available for lessons (Monday, Wednesday, Friday or other options).
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date you would like to start the lessons.
* **Lesson Duration** (`lesson_duration`, number, optional):
	+ Select the number of minutes you want each lesson to last.
* **Lesson Frequency** (`lesson_frequency`, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose how often you would like to have lessons (Once a week, Twice a week, Three times a week or other options).
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date you would like to end the lessons.
* **Lesson Time** (`lesson_time`, time, optional):
	+ Enter the time you are available for lessons (format: 08:00 or 14:30).
* **Instrument** (`instrument`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select the type of instrument you want to play (Piano, Guitar, Violin, Cello).
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional):
	+ Provide any additional contact details, such as email or address.

## Tips

- Please fill in the form accurately and thoroughly to avoid delays or missed lessons.
- If you have any special requests or requirements, let us know in the contact details field.
- Our instructors will review your form submission and contact you to schedule lessons that fit your needs and preferences.
