# Delegate Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals or organizations to apply for a delegate position at a convention. It is used to gather necessary information to evaluate and consider applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please ensure that you have all the required information ready before starting. Follow the instructions below for each field to ensure accurate and correct input.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Conventional Details** (`convention_details`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the convention you are applying for, including the name, location, and any other relevant details.
* **Delegate Name** (`delegate_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address that can be used for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number that can be reached at for communication.
* **Reason for Applying** (`reason_for_applying`, text, required): Enter a brief statement explaining why you are interested in the delegate position.
* **Available Convention Dates** (`available_convention_dates`, date, required): Enter the dates when you are available to attend the convention.
* **Brief Bio** (`bio`, note, optional): Enter a brief description of yourself, including your background and experience.
* **Education Background** (`education_background`, text, optional): Enter any relevant education background that may be beneficial for the delegate position.
* **Experience** (`experience`, text, optional): Enter any relevant experience that may be beneficial for the delegate position.
* **Availability** (`availability`, text, optional): Enter your availability for the convention, including any constraints or limitations.
* **Convention Location** (`convention_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the convention you are applying for.
* **Convention Length** (`convention_length`, number, required): Enter the number of days the convention will last.
* **Convention Type** (`convention_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of convention you are applying for (Conference or Convention).
* **Convention Frequency** (`convention_frequency`, select_multiple, required): Select the frequency of the convention (Monthly, Quarterly, Bi-Annual, Annual).
* **Convention Start Date** (`convention_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the convention.
* **Convention End Date** (`convention_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the convention.
* **Convention Days** (`convention_days`, text, required): Enter the number of days you are available to attend the convention.
* **Convention Hours** (`convention_hours`, text, required): Enter the number of hours you are available to attend the convention.
* **Convention Timezones** (`convention_timezones`, text, required): Enter the timezones you are available to attend the convention.
* **Convention Language** (`convention_language`, text, optional): Enter the language of the convention (if different from your native language).
* **Convention Currency** (`convention_currency`, text, optional): Enter the currency of the convention (if different from your country's standard currency).
* **Convention Currency Symbol** (`convention_currency_symbol`, text, optional): Enter the symbol of the convention currency (if different from your country's standard currency symbol).
* **Convention Rate** (`convention_rate`, text, optional): Enter the rate of the convention (if different from your country's standard rate).
* **Convention Terms** (`convention_terms`, text, optional): Enter any special terms or conditions related to the convention.
* **Convention Terms 2** (`convention_terms_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional special terms or conditions related to the convention.
