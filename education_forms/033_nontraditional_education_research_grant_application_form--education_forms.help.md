# Nontraditional Education Research Grant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to support nontraditional education research grant applications. It gathers essential information from applicants to help them create a comprehensive grant application.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out each section carefully**: Each field is an essential part of the application. Ensure that you provide accurate and detailed information to increase your chances of a successful grant application.
2. **Review each field carefully**: Before submitting, review the form to ensure that you have filled out all the required fields and that the information is accurate and clear.
3. **Submit your application**: Once you have filled out the form, click the "Submit" button to send your application to the relevant authorities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Applicant Details
* **Applicant Details**: This field is required (true) and should include your name, email address, and other relevant personal details. This information will be used to contact you regarding your application.
* **Contact Details**: This field is also required and should include your phone number and any other contact information that you are willing to share. This information will be used to reach out to you regarding your application.
### Project Details
* **Project Details**: This field is required and should include a detailed description of your project, including its goals, objectives, and timelines. 
* **Research Proposal**: This field is also required and should include a detailed proposal outlining your research objectives, methods, and expected outcomes.
### Institutional Support
* **Institutional Support**: This field is optional and should include any information on institutional support, such as internal or external funding, or any other supporting documentation. If you do not have any institutional support, you can leave this field blank.
* **Budget Breakdown**: This field is optional and should include an estimated breakdown of your budget, including any expenses related to your project.
* **Reviewers**: This field is optional and should include a list of reviewers who can vouch for your project and provide an independent assessment of its quality and potential impact.
### Submit
* **Submit**: This field is required and should be clicked once all other fields have been filled out to submit your application.
