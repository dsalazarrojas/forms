# Kindergarten Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kindergarten Enrollment Form is a collection of information required for kindergarten enrollment. It is used to gather details from parents and students to process their enrollment requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill in the sections as accurately and thoroughly as possible**. This will ensure that your information is processed correctly.
2. **Review each section carefully** to avoid missing any important details.
3. **Submit the form once you have completed all the sections**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Enrollment Form** (`enrollment_form`, text, optional): This section is a general introduction to the form. Please provide any additional information or comments that you would like to share with us.
- **Student Details** (`student_details`, text, optional): In this section, please provide the student's name, age, and any other relevant details.
- **Parent Details** (`parent_details`, text, optional): Here, you will find space to provide your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself as a parent.
- **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Please provide your email address and any other contact information that you would like to share with us.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your details for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
