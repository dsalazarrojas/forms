# Literature Topic Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Literature Topic Assessment form is designed to collect user input on various literature topics. This form is likely used for educational or research purposes, such as assessing user understanding or opinions on specific topics.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  To complete this form, simply type out your answer or input for each of the 25 literature topics listed.
2.  Each field is a blank text entry field, so type in your response and press enter or submit when finished.
