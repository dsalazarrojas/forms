# Compliance Reminders Bi Weekly - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Compliance Reminders Bi Weekly form is a tool to aid in tracking and monitoring compliance reminders within an educational institution. This form is designed for school staff to document and manage bi-weekly compliance reminders, helping to ensure that all necessary tasks and actions are completed on time.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Introduction
Before starting to fill out the form, make sure you have a clear understanding of the compliance items that need to be addressed and the relevant dates. 

### Step 2: Fill Out the Form
1. In the **Submitter name** field, enter your full name as the staff member submitting this form. 
2. In the **Department** field, select your school department or team.
3. Provide the **Reminder date** for the compliance item in the specified format (DD/MM/YY).
4. Describe the **Compliance item** and due date in this field.
5. Select the **Status** of the compliance item, which could be Open, In progress, or Closed.
6. In the **Next steps** field, enter the actions required to complete the compliance item (this field is optional).
7. Identify the **Responsible person** for the actions.
8. In the **Notes** field, add any additional context that could be helpful for the supervisor(s).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submitter name** (Submitter name, text, required): Enter your full name as the staff member submitting this form.
* **Department** (Department, text, required): Identify the school department or team you belong to.
* **Reminder date** (Reminder date, date, required): Enter the date the reminder applies to.
* **Compliance item** (Compliance item, text, required): Provide a brief description of the item and its due date.
* **Status** (Status, select_one, required): Select the current status of the compliance item (Open, In progress, Closed).
* **Next steps** (Next steps, text, optional): Enter the actions required to complete the compliance item (this field is optional).
* **Responsible person** (Responsible person, text, required): Identify the person responsible for taking action.
* **Notes** (Notes, text, optional): Add any additional context that could be helpful for supervisors.

## Tips
* Ensure that you submit the form in a format consistent with the specified date format (DD/MM/YY).
* Be as specific as possible in your descriptions for **Compliance item** and **Next steps**, to ensure that the form can be easily understood by others.
* Make sure the **Responsible person** is clear and specific.
* Be mindful that the **Notes** field is optional, but adding relevant information can help supervisors better understand the context.
