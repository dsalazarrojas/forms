# Scholarship Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scholarship Interview Form is a tool to collect information for scholarship purposes. It is used to gather data about the scholarship interview process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all the necessary information ready before starting the form.
* Fill out each field as instructed:
	* `First name`: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
	* `Last name`: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
	* `Email`: Enter your active email address for communication purposes.
	* `Phone`: Enter your phone number for further contact.
	* `Address`: Enter your permanent address.
	* `Country`: Select 'Yes' if your country is one of the countries for which the scholarship is available.
	* `State`: Select 'Yes' if your state is eligible for the scholarship.
	* `City`: Enter your city name.
	* `Date`: Enter the date of the scholarship interview.
	* `Time`: Enter the time of the scholarship interview.
	* `Notes`: Add any additional comments or observations about the interview.
	* `Category`: Select 'Yes' if the scholarship falls under this category.
	* `Subcategory`: Select all relevant subcategories for the scholarship.
	* `Scholarship type`: Select 'Yes' if this scholarship is for a specific type.
	* `Scholarship amount`: Enter the amount of the scholarship.
	* `Interview date`: Enter the date of the scholarship interview.
	* `Interview time`: Enter the time of the scholarship interview.
	* `Interview notes`: Add any additional comments or observations about the interview.
	* `Scholarship status`: Select the current status of the scholarship.
	* `Interviewer name`: Enter the name of the interviewer.
	* `Interviewer designation`: Enter the designation of the interviewer.
	* `Interviewer department`: Enter the department of the interviewer.
	* `Designation designation`: Enter the designation of the interviewer.
	* `Reference`: Enter any additional information about the scholarship.
	* `Interviewer notes`: Add any additional comments or observations about the interviewer.
