# Online Screen Addiction Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your screen usage habits and how they affect your daily life. Your responses will help us better comprehend the impact of screen addiction on individuals and provide valuable insights for our research. Please answer these questions honestly, and remember that all answers are confidential and anonymous.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Select the relevant options for each question, taking into account your actual behavior or experiences.
3. If a question doesn't apply to you, choose "Not applicable" or select "Never" as an option.
4. For the "Any comments" field, feel free to add any additional information or insights you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Screen time** (`screen_time`, number, optional): Please estimate the average hours you spend on screens per day.
* **How often do you check your phone?** (`screen_frequency`, select_one, optional):
	+ **Always**: You check your phone constantly throughout the day.
	+ **Most of the time**: You check your phone frequently, but not constantly.
	+ **Rarely**: You only check your phone occasionally.
	+ **Never**: You don't use your phone at all.
* **Screen usage at bedtime** (`screen_sleep`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ **Before**: You check your phone before sleep.
	+ **After**: You check your phone after sleep.
	+ **Never**: You don't check your phone before or after sleep.
* **Do you ever feel anxious or stressed when offline?** (`anxiety`, select_one, optional):
	+ **True**: You experience anxiety or stress when offline.
	+ **False**: You don't experience anxiety or stress when offline.
	+ **Not sure**: You're unsure about your feelings when offline.
* **How satisfied are you when you're not on screens?** (`satisfaction`, select_one, optional):
	+ **Highly satisfied**: You feel very content without screens.
	+ **Somewhat satisfied**: You feel somewhat content without screens.
	+ **Not satisfied**: You feel somewhat unsatisfied without screens.
* **How do you usually use your phone?** (`screen_use`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ **Calls**: You primarily use your phone for calls.
	+ **Messages**: You mostly use your phone for messages.
	+ **Email**: You use your phone for email.
	+ **Social media**: You use your phone for social media.
	+ **Browsing**: You use your phone for browsing.
	+ **Games**: You use your phone for games.
	+ **Music**: You use your phone for music.
	+ **Apps**: You use your phone for apps.
	+ **Videos**: You use your phone for videos.
	+ **Other**: You use your phone for other purposes.
* **Do you have difficulty turning off your phone?** (`apps`, select_one, optional):
	+ **True**: You struggle to turn off your phone.
	+ **False**: You can easily turn off your phone.
	+ **Sometimes**: You occasionally struggle to turn off your phone.
* **How satisfied are you with your current relationship with screens?** (`satisfaction_score`, number, optional): Please enter a number to represent your level of satisfaction.
* **Any comments** (`notes`, text, optional): Feel free to add any additional thoughts or insights about your screen usage habits.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address for follow-up purposes (if needed).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number for follow-up purposes (if needed).
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Please enter the date to complete this form.
* **How satisfied are you with your screen time?** (`screen_satisfaction`, select_one, optional):
	+ **Highly satisfied**: You're very satisfied with your screen time.
	+ **Somewhat satisfied**: You're somewhat satisfied with your screen time.
	+ **Not satisfied**: You're not satisfied with your screen time.

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough in your responses.
- If you're unsure about a question, choose the option that best represents your behavior or feelings.
- If you have any comments or suggestions, please use the "Any comments" field.
- Your responses will help us better understand screen addiction and its impact on individuals.
