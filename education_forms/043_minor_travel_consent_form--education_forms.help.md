# Minor Travel Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is for obtaining consent for a minor to travel. It requires the parent or guardian to provide information about the minor's travel plans, including the purpose of the trip, the duration of the trip, the destination, and other relevant details. The form is designed to ensure that parents or guardians are informed about their child's travel plans and to obtain their consent for the trip.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and ensure that you have all the necessary information required.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" to the "Request for Travel" question. This will help determine what type of information you will need to provide.
3. If you selected "Yes", complete the following fields:
   - **Parent/Guardian Name**: Enter the name of the parent or guardian.
   - **Parent/Guardian Email**: Enter the email address of the parent or guardian.
   - **Child Name**: Enter the name of the child traveling.
   - **Child Birthdate**: Enter the child's birthdate.
   - **Travel Date**: Enter the date of the trip.
   - **Travel Duration**: Enter the length of the trip in days.
   - **Travel Destination**: Enter the location of the trip.
   - **Reason for Travel**: Provide a brief explanation of the purpose of the trip.
   - **Parent/Guardian Phone**: Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian.
   - **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
   - **Emergency Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
   - **Travel Accomodation**: Enter information about the accommodations for the trip.
   - **Travel Documentation**: Provide any necessary travel documents required for the trip.
   - **Travel Insurance**: Enter information about travel insurance coverage.
   - **Travel Adults**: Enter the number of adults accompanying the child on the trip.
   - **Travel Children**: Enter the number of children accompanying the child on the trip.
   - **Travel Group**: Provide information about any group the child is traveling with.
   - **Travel Itineraary**: Enter a brief itinerary of the trip.
   - **Returning**: Enter the date and time of the child's return.

* **Emergency Phone**: This field is for the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Travel Accomodation**: This field is for information about the accommodations for the trip.

* **Travel Insurance**: This field is for information about travel insurance coverage.
