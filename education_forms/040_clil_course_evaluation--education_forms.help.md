# Clil Course Evaluation - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather student feedback on their experience with a CLIL (Content and Language Integrated Learning) course. The evaluation will help instructors and administrators assess the quality of the course and make improvements for future classes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields and answer the questions to the best of your ability. Please note that all fields are required unless otherwise specified.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the student completing the evaluation.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, optional): Enter the student's ID number if applicable.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the course being evaluated.
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the instructor who taught the course.
* **Semester or Term** (`semester`, text, optional): Enter the semester or term the course was taken.
* **Date of Evaluation** (`date_of_evaluation`, date, required): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Overall Course Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, required): Rate the course overall, where 1 is poor and 10 is excellent.
* **Overall Satisfaction with the Course** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Select your overall satisfaction with the course, choosing from Very Unsatisfied, Unsatisfied, Neutral, Satisfied, or Very Satisfied.
* **Quality of Course Content** (`content_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of the course content, choosing from Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Excellent.
* **Content Was Relevant and Up-to-Date** (`content_relevance`, select_one, required): Select whether or not the course content was relevant and up-to-date, choosing from Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, or Strongly Agree.
* **Course Content Was Well-Organized** (`content_organization`, select_one, required): Select whether or not the course content was well-organized, choosing from Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, or Strongly Agree.
* **Learning Objectives Were Clear and Met** (`learning_objectives_met`, select_one, required): Select whether or not the learning objectives were clear and met, choosing from Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, or Strongly Agree.
* **Instructor's Knowledge and Expertise** (`instructor_knowledge`, select_one, required): Rate the instructor's knowledge and expertise, choosing from Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Excellent.
* **Teaching Methods Were Effective** (`teaching_effectiveness`, select_one, required): Select whether or not the teaching methods were effective, choosing from Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, or Strongly Agree.
* **Instructor Explained Concepts Clearly** (`instructor_clarity`, select_one, required): Select whether or not the instructor explained concepts clearly, choosing from Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, or Strongly Agree.
* **Instructor Encouraged Student Engagement** (`student_engagement`, select_one, required): Select whether or not the instructor encouraged student engagement, choosing from Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, or Strongly Agree.
* **Instructor Provided Timely Feedback** (`feedback_on_assignments`, select_one, required): Select whether or not the instructor provided timely feedback, choosing from Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, or Strongly Agree.
* **Quality of Course Materials** (`materials_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of the course materials, choosing from Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Excellent.
* **Materials Were Useful for Learning** (`materials_usefulness`, select_one, required): Select whether or not the course materials were useful for learning, choosing from Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, or Strongly Agree.
* **Resources Were Easily Accessible** (`resources_accessibility`, select_one, required): Select whether or not the resources were easily accessible, choosing from Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, or Strongly Agree.
* **Course Workload Was Appropriate** (`workload_appropriateness`, select_one, required): Select whether or not the course workload was appropriate, choosing from Far Too Heavy, Too Heavy, Appropriate, Too Light, or Far Too Light.
* **Course Pace Was Appropriate** (`pace_appropriateness`, select_one, required): Select whether or not the course pace was appropriate, choosing from Too Slow, Slow, Just Right, Fast, or Too Fast.
* **You Developed New Skills from This Course** (`skill_development`, select_one, required): Select whether or not you developed new skills from this course, choosing from Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, or Strongly Agree.
* **You Gained New Knowledge from This Course** (`knowledge_gained`, select_one, required): Select whether or not you gained new knowledge from this course, choosing from Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, or Strongly Agree.
* **Course Will Be Useful in Your Future Career** (`application_value`, select_one, required): Select whether or not the course will be useful in your future career, choosing from Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, or Strongly Agree.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* You can only submit one form per evaluation.
* If you have any comments or suggestions, please include them in the comments section.
