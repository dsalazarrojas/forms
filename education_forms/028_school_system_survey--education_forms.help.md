# School System Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School System Survey form is designed to collect information about a school system for administrative or data collection purposes. It gathers details about the school's address, name, grade level, starting year, type, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **School Address** field with the address of the school.
2. Enter the **School Name** in the designated field.
3. Select the **School Grade Level** from the available options (Elementary, Junior High, High School).
4. Enter the **School Starting Year** when the school began.
5. Select the **School Type** from the options (Elementary, Junior High, High School).
6. Enter the **Teacher Name** if applicable.
7. If applicable, provide the **Teacher Phone** number.
8. Fill in the **Student Count** with the total number of students attending the school.
9. Choose the **School Funding** source (Government Funded, Private Funded, Public Funded).
10. Enter the **Prophet Name** ( likely a reference to the school's founder or founder).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **School Address** (text, required: false): A text field to enter the school's address.
- **School Name** (text, required: false): Enter the school's name.
- **School Grade Level** (number, required: false): Select the grade level of the school (e.g., Elementary, Junior High, High School).
- **School Starting Year** (number, required: false): Enter the year the school started.
- **School Type** (select_multiple, required: false): Select the type of school (Elementary, Junior High, High School).
- **Teacher Name** (text, required: false): If applicable, enter the teacher's name.
- **Teacher Phone** (text, required: false): If applicable, enter the teacher's phone number.
- **Student Count** (number, required: false): Enter the total number of students attending the school.
- **School Funding** (select_one, required: false): Choose the source of school funding (Government Funded, Private Funded, Public Funded).
- **Prophet Name** (text, required: false): Enter the name of the school's founder or prophet.

## Tips
- Fill in all fields carefully and thoroughly to ensure accurate data collection.
- If a field is not applicable, leave it blank or mark it as 'Not Applicable' if allowed.
- Review your submission before submitting to ensure it is complete and accurate.
