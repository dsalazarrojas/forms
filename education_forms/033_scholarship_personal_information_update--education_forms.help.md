# Scholarship Personal Information Update - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to update personal information of students. It's essential to fill out this form accurately and truthfully to ensure that your records are up-to-date and reflect your current status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with your current personal information, making sure to enter accurate and complete details.
2. Ensure that you have a valid reason for updating your information.
3. Check your email address and contact number to ensure they are correct.
4. Enter your correct date of birth and address.
5. Choose the correct program and institution where you are enrolled.
6. Select the correct level and status of your studies.
7. Choose your correct gender, ethnicity, and language.
8. Select your correct campus of enrollment.
9. Enter your major and minor details, if applicable.
10. Verify your date of birth, email, and phone number (if applicable).
11. Double-check all information entered for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`student_first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`student_last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date Of Birth** (`student_date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email** (`student_email`, email, required: false): Enter your active email address.
* **Phone** (`student_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your active phone number.
* **Address** (`student_address`, text, required: false): Enter your home address.
* **Country** (`student_country`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the country where you reside.
* **State** (`student_state`, select_one, required: false): Select your current state of residence.
* **City** (`student_city`, text, required: false): Enter your city of residence.
* **Zip** (`student_zip`, text, required: false): Enter your zip code of residence.
* **Program** (`student_program`, text, required: false): Enter your current program of study.
* **Institution** (`student_institution`, text, required: false): Enter your institution of study.
* **Grade Level** (`student_grade`, select_one, required: false): Select your grade level (High or Low).
* **Level** (`student_level`, select_one, required: false): Select your level of study (High or Low).
* **Status** (`student_status`, select_one, required: false): Select your current status (Active or Inactive).
* **Gender** (`student_gender`, select_one, required: false): Select your gender.
* **Ethnicity** (`student_ethnicity`, select_multiple, required: false): Select your ethnicity.
* **Language** (`student_language`, select_multiple, required: false): Select your language preference.
* **Campus** (`student_campus`, select_one, required: false): Select your current campus of enrollment.
* **Major** (`student_major`, text, required: false): Enter your major field of study.
* **Minor** (`student_minor`, text, required: false): Enter your minor field of study.
* **Date of Birth Verification** (`student_dob_verification`, date, required: false): Verify your date of birth.
* **Email Verification** (`student_email_verification`, email, required: false): Verify your email address.
* **Phone Verification** (`student_phone_verification`, text, required: false): Verify your phone number.
* **Address Verification** (`student_address_verification`, text, required: false): Verify your address.

## Tips
* Double-check all entered information for accuracy and completeness.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out, if applicable.
* Use a valid reason for updating your information.
