# Antigone Literature Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Antigone Literature Quiz is a tool to gauge user knowledge of the Antigone play by Sophocles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Antigone Literature Quiz, follow these steps:

1. Read the quiz instructions to understand the questions and answer requirements.
2. Provide your full name for grading purposes.
3. Select the correct answers to the two multiple-choice questions (question one and question two).
4. Write a short analysis (2-4 sentences) of the themes and characters in the play.
5. (Optional) Add any additional comments or references you would like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student name** (student_name): Required. Please enter your full name for grading purposes.
* **Who is Antigone related to in the play** (question_one): Select the correct answer from the options:
	+ Creon
	+ Ismene
	+ Oedipus
	+ Polyneices
* **What central theme is explored** (question_two): Select the most appropriate theme from the options:
	+ Fate versus free will
	+ Economic policy
	+ Scientific method
	+ Modern politics
* **Provide a short analysis of Antigone motives** (short_answer): Answer in 2-4 sentences, providing your understanding of the characters and themes in the play.
* **Additional comments** (additional_notes): Optional. Use this field to add any additional comments or references you would like to share.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions based on your reading of the Antigone play.
* Use your best understanding of the characters and themes in the play to answer the questions.
* Keep your short analysis concise (2-4 sentences).
* The additional comments field is optional, but may be helpful if you have any relevant references or comments to share.
