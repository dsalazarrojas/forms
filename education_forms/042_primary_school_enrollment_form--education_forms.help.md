# Primary School Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps us enroll a student into our primary school. To provide the best possible experience for the student, kindly complete this form carefully and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out the form in sections. Each section is designed to collect specific information about the student and their parent/guardian.
2.  Start by entering the student's first name in the "Student First Name" field.
3.  Next, enter the student's last name in the "Student Last Name" field.
4.  Then, enter the parent/guardian's first name in the "Parent First Name" field.
5.  Finally, enter the parent/guardian's last name in the "Parent Last Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Student First Name (text)**: Enter the student's first name as it is written on their government-issued ID or birth certificate.
*  **Student Last Name (text)**: Enter the student's last name as it is written on their government-issued ID or birth certificate.
*  **Parent First Name (text)**: Enter the parent/guardian's first name as it is written on their government-issued ID or driver's license.
*  **Parent Last Name (text)**: Enter the parent/guardian's last name as it is written on their government-issued ID or driver's license.

## Tips

1.  Please ensure that the information entered is accurate and up-to-date.
2.  If you're unsure about any field, contact us for clarification.
3.  This form does not collect sensitive information, so you can feel free to share it with us.
