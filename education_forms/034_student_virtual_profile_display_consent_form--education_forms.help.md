# student_virtual_profile_display_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The student_virtual_profile_display_consent_form is used to collect information about students' virtual profile display preferences. This form is required to be filled out by students who want to have their virtual profile displayed on the platform.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your name, student ID, and email address in the respective fields.
2. Decide whether you want to keep your email address confidential.
3. Choose whether you consent to display your virtual profile.
4. Select your preferred option for displaying your student profile.
5. If you have any additional information you'd like to add, you can do so in the "Is there anything else you would like to add?" field.
6. If you have a date of birth, you can fill it out.
7. If you have a preferred time of contact, you can fill it out.
8. If you want to add your phone number.
9. (Optional) If you want to keep your email address confidential again, you can select this option.
10. (Optional) If you want to change your profile display preference again, you can select this option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name?** (`student_name`, text, required): Fill in your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **What is your student ID?** (`student_id`, number, required): Fill in your student ID number.
* **What is your email address?** (`email`, text, required): Fill in your email address.
* **Do you want to keep your email confidential?** (`email_confidentiality`, select_one, required): Check 'True' if you want to keep your email address confidential.
* **Do you consent to display your virtual profile?** (`student_virtual_profile_display_consent`, select_multiple, required): Check 'Yes' if you consent to display your virtual profile.
* **Do you want your student profile to be displayed?** (`student_profile_display_preference`, select_one, required): Check 'Yes' if you want your student profile to be displayed.
* **Is there anything else you would like to add?** (`other`, text, optional): If you have any additional information you'd like to add, you can do so in this field.
* **What is your date of birth?** (`date`, date, optional): If you have a date of birth, fill it out.
* **What time do you prefer to be contacted?** (`time`, time, optional): If you have a preferred time of contact, fill it out.
* **What is your phone number?** (`phone`, text, optional): If you have a phone number, fill it out.
* **Email Confidentiality Duplicate** (`email_confidentiality_duplicate`, select_one, optional): Check 'True' if you want to keep your email address confidential.
* **Profile Display Preference Duplicate** (`profile_display_preference_duplicate`, select_one, optional): Check 'Yes' if you want to change your profile display preference.
