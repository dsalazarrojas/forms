# Cumulative Record Collection Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on students' cumulative records for educational or administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form:
1. Fill in the student's name, grade, and school year accurately.
2. Indicate the student's attendance status.
3. Select the student's behavior and performance levels.
4. Provide a signature for electronic submission.
5. Enter the student's email address (if provided).
6. Enter the student's phone number.
7. Add any comments about the student's record.
8. Fill in the school's name.
9. Indicate who submitted the form.
10. Enter the date and time of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required): Enter the student's full name accurately.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, `number`, required): Enter the student's grade (e.g., 9, 10, etc.).
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, `text`, required): Enter the teacher's name responsible for the student.
* **School Year** (`school_year`, `text`, required): Enter the academic year (e.g., 2020-2021, 2021-2022, etc.).
* **Attendance** (`attendance`, `select_one`, required): Select the student's attendance status (yes/no).
* **Behavior** (`behavior`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the student's behavior levels (yes/no for each level).
* **Student Performance** (`student_performance`, `select_one`, required): Select the student's performance level (yes/no).
* **E-Signature** (`e_signature`, `note`, required): Provide an electronic signature.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter the student's email address (if provided).
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any comments about the student's record.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, required): Enter the school's name.
* **Form Submitted By** (`form_submitted_by`, `select_one`, required): Indicate who submitted the form (yes/no).
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, `date`, required): Enter the date of submission.
* **Time Submitted** (`time_submitted`, `time`, required): Enter the time of submission.
