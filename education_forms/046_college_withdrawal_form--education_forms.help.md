# College Withdrawal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The College Withdrawal Form is designed to collect information from students who are withdrawing from the institution. This form helps us understand your current status and reasons for withdrawal, which enables us to improve our support systems and services for future students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each field carefully and thoroughly. You can save your progress and come back to the form later if needed. Once you've finished, review your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official student records.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official student records.
* **Student ID Number**: Enter your student ID number issued by the college.
* **Email Address**: Enter your current email address on record with the college.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your current phone number on record with the college.
* **Current Academic Status**: Select your current academic status:
	+ Full-Time Student
	+ Part-Time Student
	+ On Leave of Absence
	+ Other
* **Year in School**: Select the current academic year you are in:
	+ Freshman
	+ Sophomore
	+ Junior
	+ Senior
	+ Graduate
* **Current Major or Program**: Enter your current major or program as it appears on your student records.
* **Type of Withdrawal**: Select the type of withdrawal you are requesting:
	+ Temporary Leave of Absence
	+ Permanent Withdrawal
	+ Medical Withdrawal
	+ Other
* **Effective Withdrawal Date**: Enter the date you plan to withdraw from the college.
* **Reason for Withdrawal**: Select the reason for your withdrawal:
	+ Financial Hardship
	+ Medical or Health Reasons
	+ Family or Personal Issues
	+ Poor Academic Performance
	+ Career Opportunity
	+ Military Service
	+ Transfer to Another School
	+ Other
* **Detailed Explanation**: Provide a brief explanation for your decision to withdraw.
* **Semester Completion Status**: Select your completion status for the current semester:
	+ Completing Current Semester
	+ Leaving Immediately
	+ End of Academic Year
	+ Other
* **Current Course Load**: Enter the number of credit hours you are currently enrolled in.
* **Outstanding Financial Obligations**: Select whether you have any outstanding financial obligations:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Unsure
* **Financial Aid Plans**: If applicable, indicate what will happen to your financial aid (e.g., scholarships, loans, grants).
* **Future Plans**: Describe your plans after withdrawal from the college.
* **Intent to Return**: Select whether you plan to return to the college:
	+ Yes, I Plan to Return
	+ Possibly
	+ No, This is Permanent
	+ Unsure
* **Planned Return Timeline**: If you plan to return, enter your anticipated return date or semester.
* **Required Documents Submitted**: Select whether you have submitted all required documents (e.g., transcript requests, administrative documents):
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Willingness to Participate in Exit Interview**: Indicate your willingness to participate in an exit interview:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Interest in Alumni Services or Stay Connected**: Select whether you are interested in staying connected with the college:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Select your preferred method of contact:
	+ Email
	+ Phone
	+ Mail
	+ No Contact
* **Additional Comments or Feedback**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the college.
* **Confirmation**: Confirm that your withdrawal request is complete and accurate:
	+ Yes
	+ No
