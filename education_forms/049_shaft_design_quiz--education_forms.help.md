# Shaft Design Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Shaft Design Quiz is a self-assessment tool designed to evaluate your knowledge of shaft design concepts and calculations. It's essential to provide accurate and thoughtful responses to each question to get an accurate evaluation of your understanding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Shaft Design Quiz, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct answers for each question by choosing from the provided options for questions 2 (Shafts Design Concepts) and 3 (Calculations).
2. Enter your email address and phone number for identification purposes (questions 5 and 6).
3. Enter the submission date and time (question 7) if applicable.
4. Review your answers and provide any additional comments or feedback in question 4 (Review and Follow-up).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shafts Design Concepts** (`shaft_design_concepts`, select_multiple, **required**): Choose all the correct options that apply to your understanding of shaft design concepts.
* **Calculations** (`calculations`, number, **required**): Enter the result of the calculation related to shaft design.
* **User Email** (`user_email`, email, **required**): Enter your email address.
* **User Phone** (`user_phone`, text, **optional**): Enter your phone number for identification purposes.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, **optional**): Enter the date you submitted the form.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, **optional**): Enter the time you submitted the form.
* **Review and Follow-up** (`review_follow_up`, note, **optional**): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the quiz.
