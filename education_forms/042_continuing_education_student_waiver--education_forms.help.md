# Continuing Education Student Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Continuing Education Student Waiver form is a mandatory document required for adult learners taking courses or training programs. It collects essential information for the student, including personal details, emergency contacts, and medical conditions, to ensure a safe and successful learning experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by providing your student identification information on the first page.
2.  Proceed to the second page to enter your full name and email address.
3.  Move on to the third page to input your course or program title.
4.  On the fourth page, enter the emergency contact information, including the emergency contact's name and phone number.
5.  The fifth page is for medical conditions or allergies that may impact your learning experience.
6.  Review the waiver and release of liability section, and if you agree to its terms, select the appropriate option.
7.  Decide whether you grant permission for media usage.
8.  Sign and date the form to complete it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID or other official documents. (Required)
*   **Email Address**: Enter your personal email address that the institution can use to contact you. (Required)
*   **Course or Program Title**: Enter the title of the course or program you are enrolled in. (Required)
*   **Emergency Contact Full Name**: Provide the full name of the emergency contact person who can be reached during course hours. (Required)
*   **Emergency Contact Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person. (Required)
*   **Relevant Medical Conditions or Allergies**: List any medical conditions or allergies you have that may impact your learning experience. (Required)
*   **I release the institution from liability for injury or loss**: Agree to the release of liability terms. (Required)
*   **I grant permission for media usage**: Decide whether you agree to be photographed or videoed for educational promotion. (Required)
*   **Student Digital Signature**: Type your full name as your digital signature. (Required)

## Tips

*   Please review the waiver and release of liability carefully before agreeing to it.
*   Ensure you understand the purpose and implications of granting permission for media usage.
*   Enter your name correctly in the student digital signature field.
