# Academic Records Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain authorization from students to share their academic records with third parties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student name, ID, and email address.
2. Enter the name of the party authorized to receive your records.
3. Enter the institution or organization name of the authorized party.
4. Provide the complete address of the authorized party.
5. Select the type of records you want to share (e.g., transcripts, course syllabi, grades).
6. Specify the academic periods you want to be covered.
7. State the purpose of the authorization.
8. Choose the authorization validity period.
9. Acknowledge that fees may apply for record processing.
10. Confirm your understanding of your privacy and revocation rights.
11. Sign the form as your electronic signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Student ID**: Your official student identification number.
* **Student Email**: Your current email address.
* **Authorized Party Name**: Full name of the person or organization authorized to receive your records.
* **Authorized Party Institution/Organization**: Name of the institution or organization of the authorized party.
* **Authorized Party Address**: Complete mailing address of the authorized party.
* **Type of Records Requested**: Select the type of records you want to share (e.g., transcripts, course syllabi, grades).
* **Academic Periods Covered**: Specify the academic periods you want to be covered.
* **Purpose of Authorization**: State the reason for requesting these records.
* **Authorization Validity Period**: Choose the duration of the authorization.
* **Fee Payment Acknowledgment**: Acknowledge that fees may apply for record processing.
* **Privacy Rights Acknowledgment**: Confirm your understanding of your right to inspect these records before release.
* **Revocation Rights Acknowledgment**: Confirm your understanding that you can revoke this authorization at any time.
* **Electronic Signature Consent**: Confirm your consent to using an electronic signature.
* **Student Signature**: Type your full name as your electronic signature.
* **Date of Signature**: Date when this authorization is signed.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you are authorized to share your records before completing this form.
- Review the form carefully to ensure you understand what records are being shared and with whom.
- Be aware of your rights and responsibilities regarding the sharing of your academic records.
- Complete this form with accuracy and honesty.
