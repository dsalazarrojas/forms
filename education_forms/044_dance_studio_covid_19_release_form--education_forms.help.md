# Dance Studio COVID 19 Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is required for participation in dance studio activities. It gathers information from students and parents to ensure the studio's policies and procedures are understood and agreed upon.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review each section carefully and answer all required questions.
* Select the correct options for each field, and make sure to sign and date the form.
* Complete all fields accurately and truthfully to ensure your child's safety and well-being.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Info**: Choose whether you are a student or not. This information is used to track student participation.
* **Parent Info**: Provide your name and contact information as a parent or guardian.
* **Medical Conditions**: Check if the student has any medical conditions that may affect their participation. If yes, please provide more details in the field below.
* **Allergies**: List any allergies the student may have.
* **Emergency Contact**: Provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
* **Waiver**: Sign and date this section to agree to the studio's policies and procedures.
* **Medical Release**: Sign and date this section to grant permission for medical treatment in case of an emergency.
* **Signature**: Sign and date this section to confirm you have read and understood the form.
* **Date**: Enter the date of participation.
* **Time**: Enter the time of participation.
* **Faculty Release**: Choose whether you would like to give permission for faculty members to have your student's emergency contact information.
* **Student Conducted**: Choose whether the student conducted themselves or not during the activity.
* **Faculty Conducted**: Choose whether faculty members were involved in the student's activity.
* **Studio Info**: Provide any additional comments or information about your student's participation.
* **Faculty Info**: Provide any additional comments or information about your student's faculty.
* **Faculty**: Choose whether the faculty member is a parent.
* **Adult**: Choose whether the student is an adult or not.
* **Age**: Enter the age of the student.
* **Classes**: Choose which classes the student is participating in.
* **Genre**: Choose which dance genres the student is interested in.
* **Adult & Kids**: Choose which adult and kids programs the student is participating in.
* **Studio**: Provide any additional comments or information about your student's participation in the studio.
* **Waiver 2**: Sign and date this section to re-agree to the studio's policies and procedures.
* **Medical Condition**: Provide more details about any medical conditions listed above.
* **Emergency Contact Info**: Provide any additional comments or information about your emergency contact person.
* **Waiver 2**: Sign and date this section to re-agree to the studio's policies and procedures.
* **Medical Condition**: Not applicable, this field is for information only. 

Note: This form is a sample and may need to be reviewed and modified according to your studio's policies and procedures.
