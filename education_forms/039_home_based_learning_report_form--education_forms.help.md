This form is designed to gather information for a home-based learning report, which is a report that documents a student's progress and performance at home. This form is intended to be completed by parents or educators who have direct contact with the student. The form is meant to be used in conjunction with other documentation and assessment tools to provide a comprehensive picture of the student's learning experience at home. Please ensure that you have reviewed the relevant policies and procedures before completing this form, and that you have not previously submitted a report for this student using this form.

# Home Based Learning Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Based Learning Report Form is designed to gather information on a student's progress and performance at home.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the student's ID number in the "Student ID" field.
2. Fill in the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
3. Enter the parent's name and phone number in the "Parent Name" and "Parent Phone" fields, respectively.
4. Fill in the student's date of birth in the "Date Of Birth" field.
5. Describe the student's progress in the "Student Progress" field.
6. Enter the name of the teacher who has been working with the student in the "Teacher Name" field.
7. Record any feedback or comments from the parent in the "Feedback From Parent" field.
8. Include the teacher's signature in the "Teacher Signature" field.
9. Include the parent's signature in the "Parent Signature" field.
10. Set the date of the report in the "Date" field.
11. Fill in the time spent on the report in the "Time" field.
12. Select the student's grade level from the dropdown menu in the "Student Grade Level" field.
13. Select the student's subject area from the dropdown menu in the "Student Subject" field.
14. Record any comments or feedback from the teacher in the "Teacher Comments" field.
15. Describe any student achievements in the "Student Achievements" field.
16. Describe the student's behavior in the "Student Behavior" field.
17. Identify any counseling or medical needs the student may have in the "Student Counseling Needs" and "Student Medical Needs" fields, respectively.
18. Include any teacher notes in the "Teacher Notes" field.
19. Record any concerns or worries from the parent in the "Parent Concerns" field.
20. Record any suggestions or advice from the teacher in the "Teacher Suggestions" field.
21. Record the number of days the student has been absent in the "Student Absence Days" field.
22. Record any comments about the student's behavior in the "Student Behavior Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, optional): Enter the student's ID number.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Fill in the student's name.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): Enter the parent's name.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, optional): Enter the parent's phone number.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Fill in the student's date of birth.
* **Student Progress** (`student_progress`, text, optional): Describe the student's progress.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the teacher who has been working with the student.
* **Feedback From Parent** (`feedback_from_parent`, text, optional): Record any feedback or comments from the parent.
* **Teacher Signature** (`teacher_signature`, text, optional): Include the teacher's signature.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, text, optional): Include the parent's signature.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Set the date of the report.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Fill in the time spent on the report.
* **Student Grade Level** (`student_grade_level`, select_one, optional): Select the student's grade level.
* **Student Subject** (`student_subject`, select_multiple, optional): Select the student's subject area.
* **Teacher Comments** (`teacher_comments`, text, optional): Record any comments or feedback from the teacher.
* **Student Achievements** (`student_achievements`, select_multiple, optional): Describe any student achievements.
* **Student Behavior** (`student_behavior`, select_multiple, optional): Describe the student's behavior.
* **Student Counseling Needs** (`student_counseling_needs`, select_multiple, optional): Identify any counseling needs the student may have.
* **Student Medical Needs** (`student_medical_needs`, select_multiple, optional): Identify any medical needs the student may have.
* **Teacher Notes** (`teacher_notes`, text, optional): Include any teacher notes.
* **Parent Concerns** (`parent_concerns`, text, optional): Record any concerns or worries from the parent.
* **Teacher Suggestions** (`teacher_suggestions`, text, optional): Record any suggestions or advice from the teacher.
* **Student Absence Days** (`student_absence_days`, number, optional): Record the number of days the student has been absent.
* **Student Behavior Comments** (`student_behavior_comments`, text, optional): Record any comments about the student's behavior.
* **Student Achieves A's**, **Student Achieves B's**, etc. are not used in this context.
* **Other** field is used to select any additional achievements, behavior and needs that are not listed
