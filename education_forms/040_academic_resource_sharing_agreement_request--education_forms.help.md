# Academic Resource Sharing Agreement Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Resource Sharing Agreement Request form is used to request access to academic resources such as database access, software licenses, lab equipment, and other specialized instruments. This form ensures that both the resource owner and the requester agree on the terms of use, including the duration of access, access level, and any potential liabilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name, email, and institutional affiliation as the requester.
2. Provide a detailed description of the resources you are requesting access to.
3. Specify how you intend to use the resources.
4. Choose the duration of access you need.
5. Select the level of access you require.
6. Indicate how many users need access to the resources.
7. Acknowledge that you will comply with all usage terms and conditions.
8. Acknowledge the intellectual property rights associated with the resources.
9. Accept responsibility for any damages caused by misuse of the resources.
10. Sign the agreement by typing your full name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester Name** (1): Enter your full name as the person requesting access to the resources.
* **Requester Email** (2): Enter your professional email address for communication about this request.
* **Requester Institution** (3): Enter the university or organization affiliation of the requester.
* **Requester Department** (4): Enter the academic department or research unit of the requester.
* **Resource Owner Name** (5): Enter the full name of the person who owns/ manages the resources.
* **Resource Owner Institution** (6): Enter the university or organization affiliation of the resource owner.
* **Type of Resource Requested** (7): Select the type of resource being requested from the dropdown menu.
*   *Examples:* Database access, Software license, Lab equipment, Specialized instruments, Research materials, Computational resources, Library materials, Other
* **Resource Description** (8): Provide a detailed description of the specific resources being requested.
* **Intended Use** (9): Describe how the resources will be used.
* **Sharing Duration** (10): Select the duration of access required, such as One-time use, One week, One month, Three months, Six months, One year, or Ongoing.
*   *Examples:*
* **Access Level Required** (11): Choose the level of access needed, such as Read-only, Read and write, Full administrative, Specific functions only, Other.
*   *Examples:*
* **Number of Users** (12): Specify the number of people who need access to the resources.
* **Compliance Acknowledgment** (13): Confirm that you will comply with all usage terms and conditions.
*   *Options: Yes, I acknowledge, Yes, with questions, Need clarification, No, not applicable*
* **Intellectual Property Acknowledgment** (14): Acknowledge the intellectual property rights associated with the resources.
*   *Options: Yes, I acknowledge, Yes, with questions, Need clarification, No, not applicable*
* **Liability Acceptance** (15): Confirm that you accept responsibility for any damages caused by misuse of the resources.
*   *Options: Yes, I accept, Yes, with limitations, Need clarification, No, not applicable*
* **Agreement Signature** (16): Sign the agreement by typing your full name as an electronic signature.

Please submit this form to complete your Academic Resource Sharing Agreement Request.
