# Parent Teacher Conference Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parent Teacher Conference Form is used to document and schedule parent-teacher conferences, enabling effective communication and collaboration between teachers and parents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and fill in all required fields.
2. Enter the Parent Conference Date in the designated field.
3. Select the Meeting Time for the scheduled conference.
4. Enter the Parent Name, Teacher Name, and Meeting Location fields as applicable.
5. Provide any additional Notes if necessary.
6. Confirm the Meeting Status and Meeting Status Notes fields according to the conference's outcome.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Conference Date** (`parent_conference_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the scheduled parent-teacher conference.
* **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, text, optional): Select the time of the scheduled meeting.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter the parent's name.
* **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the meeting.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required): Enter the teacher's name.
* **Conference Subject** (`conference_subject`, text, optional): Enter a brief subject or topic to be discussed.
* **Note** (`conference_note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the conference.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, optional): Enter the parent's email address (if applicable).
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, email, optional): Enter the teacher's email address (if applicable).
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, optional): Enter the parent's phone number (if applicable).
* **Teacher Phone** (`teacher_phone`, text, optional): Enter the teacher's phone number (if applicable).
* **Conference Duration** (`conference_duration`, number, optional): Enter the length of the conference in minutes (if applicable).
* **Parent Notes** (`parent_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments from the parent.
* **Meeting Place** (`meeting_place`, text, optional): Enter the location of the meeting (if applicable).
* **Subject** (`subject`, select_multiple, required): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the parent or teacher has a concern or topic to discuss.
* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the meeting.
* **Meeting Time 2** (`meeting_time_2`, time, required): Select the time of the meeting.
* **Parent Address** (`parent_address`, text, optional): Enter the parent's address (if applicable).
* **Teacher Address** (`teacher_address`, text, optional): Enter the teacher's address (if applicable).
* **Subject 2** (`subject_2`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the parent or teacher has a concern or topic to discuss.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required): Enter any additional notes or comments about the conference.
* **Meeting Status** (`meeting_status`, select_one, required): Select "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate the status of the meeting.
* **Meeting Status Notes** (`meeting_status_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the meeting status.
* **Meeting Location 2** (`meeting_location_2`, text, required): Enter the location of the meeting.
* **Teacher Conference Note** (`teacher_conference_note`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments from the teacher.
