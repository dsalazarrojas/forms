# New Agency Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to provide a structured way to collect and store essential information about a new agency. The information collected will be used for various purposes within the system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with the relevant information. Please make sure to fill in all the fields marked as "required" in the explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Agency Name** (Agency Name, text, false): Enter the name of the agency. This is a basic field to identify the agency.
- **Agency Address** (Agency Address, text, false): Enter the physical address of the agency.
- **Agency Phone** (Agency Phone, text, false): Enter the agency's phone number for contact purposes.
- **Agency Email** (Agency Email, email, false): Enter the agency's email address. Please use a valid email address format (e.g., `user@example.com`).
- **Agency Website** (Agency Website, text, false): Enter the agency's website URL, if available.
- **Agency Facebook** (Agency Facebook, text, false): Enter the agency's Facebook handle or URL, if available.
- **Agency Twitter** (Agency Twitter, text, false): Enter the agency's Twitter handle or URL, if available.
- **Agency Instagram** (Agency Instagram, text, false): Enter the agency's Instagram handle or URL, if available.
- **Agency Description** (Agency Description, note, false): Enter a brief description of the agency.
- **Agency Contact** (Agency Contact, text, false): Enter the name of the person responsible for the agency's contact.
- **Agency Category** (Agency Category, text, false): Enter the category or type of the agency.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure successful submission.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact your system administrator for guidance.
- If you are filling this form on behalf of someone else, ensure they have authorized you to do so.
