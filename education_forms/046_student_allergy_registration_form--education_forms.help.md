# Student Allergy Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about a student's allergies, which is essential for the student's safety and well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your student's name.
2. Select the type of allergy from the options provided.
3. Indicate the severity of the allergy (Mild, Moderate, Severe).
4. If necessary, provide details about the treatment plan for the allergy.
5. Record the date and time the allergy was first diagnosed or suspected.
6. Record the date and time any reactions occurred.
7. Provide the name of your school.
8. Enter your school's email contact information.
9. Enter your school's phone contact information.
10. Provide the name of the student's emergency contact person.
11. Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
12. Provide the email address of the emergency contact person.
13. Provide the name of the student's doctor or healthcare provider.
14. Enter the doctor's email address.
15. Enter the doctor's phone number.
16. Provide the name and address of the medical facility where the student receives treatment.
17. Enter the phone number and email address of the medical facility.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter the student's name as it appears on their identification card or record.
* **Allergy Type**: Select one of the following options: Peanut, Tree Nut, Fish, Shellfish.
* **Severity**: Select one of the following options: Mild, Moderate, Severe.
* **Treatment Plan**: If necessary, provide details about any medication or emergency procedures taken to manage the allergy.
* **Allergy Date**: Record the date and time the allergy was first diagnosed or suspected.
* **Allergy Since Date**: Record the date and time any reactions occurred.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of the school or educational institution.
* **School Email**: Enter the email address of your school or institution.
* **School Phone**: Enter the phone number of your school or institution.
* **Teacher Name**: Provide the name of the teacher or staff member responsible for the student.
* **Teacher Email**: Enter the email address of the teacher or staff member.
* **Teacher Phone**: Enter the phone number of the teacher or staff member.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Provide the name of the person authorized to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Specify the relationship between the student and the emergency contact (parent, guardian, guardian's name).
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Doctor's Name**: Provide the name of the student's healthcare provider.
* **Doctor's Email**: Enter the email address of the healthcare provider.
* **Doctor's Phone**: Enter the phone number of the healthcare provider.
* **Medical Facility**: Provide the name and address of the medical facility where the student receives treatment.
* **Medical Facility Phone**: Enter the phone number of the medical facility.
* **Medical Facility Email**: Enter the email address of the medical facility.
* **Emergency Contact Address**: Provide the address of the emergency contact person.
* **Allergy Information**: Provide any additional allergy-related information or notes.
