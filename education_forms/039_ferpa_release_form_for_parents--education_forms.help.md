# FERPA Release Form For Parents - Help Guide
## Purpose

The FERPA Release Form For Parents is a document that allows parents or guardians to provide consent for the release of their child's educational records. This form is an essential tool for schools to ensure that they have the necessary permissions to access and share student information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information about your child.
2. Ensure you provide the correct school affiliation (school name) associated with your child.
3. Select the correct academic level of your child from the provided options.
4. Sign the form to confirm your consent for the release of your child's records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Name** (1): Enter your name as the parent or guardian of the student.
* **Student Name** (2): Enter the name of the student you are providing consent for.
* **Parent Email** (3): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Parent Phone** (4): Enter a contact phone number for you or your family.
* **School Name** (5): Enter the name of the school associated with your child.
* **Grade Level** (6): Select the academic grade level of your child from the provided options (9th, 10th, 11th, 12th).
* **Student DOB** (7): Enter the date of birth of your child.
* **School Address** (8): Enter the address of the school associated with your child (optional).
* **Teacher Name** (9): Enter the name of your child's teacher (optional).
* **Student ID Number** (10): Enter the student ID number associated with your child (optional).
* **Parent Signature** (11): Provide your signature to confirm your consent for the release of your child's records.
* **Date** (12): Enter the date you are providing this form (optional).
* **School Signature** (13): This field is not applicable for parents.
* **School Principal Signature** (14): This field is for school administrators to sign.
* **School Administrative Signature** (15): This field is for school administrators to sign.
* **School Officer Signature** (16): This field is for school officers to sign.
* **School Representative Signature** (17): This field is for school representatives to sign.
* **School Principal Name** (18): Enter the name of the school principal (optional).
* **School Department** (19): Enter the name of the school department (optional).
* **9th Level** (20): Select the level of your child's 9th-grade performance (High or Low).
* **10th Level** (21): Select the level of your child's 10th-grade performance (High or Low).
* **11th Level** (22): Select the level of your child's 11th-grade performance (High or Low).
* **12th Level** (23): Select the level of your child's 12th-grade performance (High or Low).
* **Teacher** (24): Enter the name of your child's teacher (optional).
* **Other** (25): Enter any additional comments or information about your child (optional).

Note: The fields with an asterisk (*) are required fields. Please ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information in these fields to ensure a smooth and secure release of your child's records.
