# Father Son Learning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Father Son Learning Survey is a questionnaire designed to help fathers and sons assess their relationship and identify areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the survey in the "Title" field. This will help identify the purpose and objective of the survey.
2. Choose the category that best describes the survey from the provided options.
3. Provide a brief description of the survey's purpose and objective in the "Description" field.
4. Select all the hobbies you enjoy doing with your son from the "Hobbies" field. You can choose multiple options.
5. List all the conversation topics you usually discuss with your son in the "Conversation Topics" field. You can choose multiple options.
6. Indicate how often you reflect on your relationship with your son in the "Reflections" field.
7. Specify how often you take time to reflect on your relationship with your son in the "Reflections Frequency" field.
8. Provide any suggestions for improving the father-son relationship in the "Suggestions" field.
9. Indicate how often you take an active role in your son's education in the "Active Role" field.
10. Specify your son's current level of education in the "Education" field.
11. List any other hobbies you enjoy with your son in the "Other Hobbies" field. (Optional)
12. List any other topics you discuss with your son in the "Other Topics" field. (Optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title**: (`father_son_learning_survey_title`, text, required) Enter the title of the survey.
* **Category**: (`father_son_learning_survey_category`, select_one, required) Choose the category that best describes the survey from the provided options.
* **Description**: (`father_son_learning_survey_description`, text, required) Briefly describe the purpose and objective of the survey.
* **Hobbies**: (`father_son_learning_survey_hobbies`, select_multiple, required) Select all the hobbies you enjoy doing with your son.
* **Conversation Topics**: (`father_son_learning_survey_conversation_topics`, select_multiple, required) List all the conversation topics you usually discuss with your son.
* **Reflections**: (`father_son_learning_survey_reflections`, select_one, required) Indicate how often you reflect on your relationship with your son.
* **Reflections Frequency**: (`father_son_learning_survey_reflections_frequency`, select_one, required) Specify how often you take time to reflect on your relationship with your son.
* **Suggestions**: (`father_son_learning_survey_suggestions`, text, required) Provide any suggestions for improving the father-son relationship.
* **Active Role**: (`father_son_learning_survey_active_role`, select_one, required) Indicate how often you take an active role in your son's education.
* **Education**: (`father_son_learning_survey_education`, select_one, required) Specify your son's current level of education.
* **Other Hobbies**: (`father_son_learning_survey_other_hobbies`, text, optional) List any other hobbies you enjoy with your son.
* **Other Topics**: (`father_son_learning_survey_other_topics`, text, optional) List any other subjects you discuss with your son.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer these questions honestly and to the best of your knowledge.
* This form is designed to help you better understand and improve your relationship with your son.
* Do not fill out this form if you have already done so in the past to avoid duplication.
* This form is part of an internal system and should not be used outside of this specific context.
