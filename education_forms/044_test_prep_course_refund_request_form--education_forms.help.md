# Test Prep Course Refund Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for students to request a refund for a Test Prep course they have taken. The form should be completed by the student to provide necessary details for a refund processing.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure the refund request is processed correctly.
- Review each field carefully to make sure it matches your information accurately.
- Submit the form once you have filled out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Course Details**: 
    - This field is used to describe the Test Prep course you are requesting a refund for.
    - Please provide a brief summary of the course name, location, and any other relevant details.
    - This information will help the refund processing staff understand your request.
- **Course ID**: 
    - This field is used to identify the specific course you are requesting a refund for.
    - Enter the unique ID associated with your course.
- **Student ID**: 
    - This field is used to identify you, the student, for refund purposes.
    - Enter your unique student ID.
- **Reason for Refund**: 
    - This field is used to explain why you are requesting a refund.
    - Please provide a clear and concise explanation of the reason for the refund.
    - Examples include: course material not received, poor instruction, or other relevant reasons.
- **Refund Amount**: 
    - This field is used to specify the amount you are requesting as a refund.
    - Enter a numerical value for the refund amount.
- **Refund Date**: 
    - This field is used to specify the date you are requesting the refund from.
    - Enter a date in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
- **Student E-mail**: 
    - This field is used to contact you, the student, for further information or refund processing.
    - Enter your student email address.
- **Student Phone**: 
    - This field is used to contact you, the student, for further information or refund processing.
    - Enter your student phone number.
- **Course Instructor**: 
    - This field is used to provide information about the instructor of the course you took.
    - Enter the name of the course instructor.
- **Course Location**: 
    - This field is used to provide information about the location of the course you took.
    - Enter the location of the course.
- **Student Name**: 
    - This field is used to identify you, the student, for refund purposes.
    - Enter your full name.
- **Student Address**: 
    - This field is used to provide your address.
    - Enter your address. 

## Tips
- Make sure to proofread your submission for spelling and grammar errors.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact the refund processing staff for assistance.
- If you have any additional documentation or evidence to support your refund request, please attach it to the form.
