# Chapter Progress Tracker - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Chapter Progress Tracker form is designed to provide a snapshot of a participant's progress in a course or book. It helps the instructor or course coordinator understand the participant's current status and provide better support.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the **Full Name** field with your complete name.
2. Enter your **Email Address** for tracking and communication purposes.
3. Enter the **Course or Book Title** you are tracking progress for.
4. Fill out the **Total Number of Chapters or Sections** to indicate the total number of chapters or sections in the course.
5. Enter the **Number of Chapters or Sections Completed** you have finished so far.
6. Enter the **Completion Percentage** you have achieved in the course.
7. Fill out the **Course Start Date** when you began the course.
8. Enter the **Expected Completion Date** when you plan to finish the course (if applicable).
9. Enter the **Current Chapter Number** you are currently on.
10. Select your **Comprehension Level** to indicate how well you understand the material.
11. Select the **Pace** of the course to indicate if it's too fast, good, or too slow for you.
12. Enter any **Learning Challenges or Obstacles** you have encountered.
13. Enter your **Favorite Topics or Sections** that you found most interesting.
14. Select your **Class Participation Level** to indicate how actively you have participated.
15. Finally, enter the **Last Update Date** when you last updated this progress.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your complete name as you would like to be referred to.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: yes
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for tracking and communication purposes.
	+ Type: email
	+ Required: yes
* **Course or Book Title**: Enter the title of the course or book you are tracking progress for.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: yes
* **Total Number of Chapters or Sections**: Enter the total number of chapters or sections in the course.
	+ Type: number
	+ Required: yes
* **Chapters or Sections Completed**: Enter the number of chapters or sections you have finished so far.
	+ Type: number
	+ Required: yes
* **Completion Percentage**: Enter the percentage of the course you have completed.
	+ Type: number
	+ Required: no
* **Course Start Date**: Enter the date when you began the course.
	+ Type: date
	+ Required: yes
* **Expected Completion Date**: Enter the date when you plan to finish the course (if applicable).
	+ Type: date
	+ Required: no
* **Current Chapter Number**: Enter the chapter number you are currently on.
	+ Type: number
	+ Required: yes
* **Comprehension Level**: Select the level of understanding you have achieved in the course.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: yes
* **Pace**: Select the pace of the course as it suits you.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: no
* **Learning Challenges or Obstacles**: Enter any difficulties you have faced in the course.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
* **Favorite Topics or Sections**: Enter the topics or sections you found most interesting.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
* **Class Participation Level**: Select your level of participation in the course.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: no
* **Last Update Date**: Enter the date when you last updated this progress.
	+ Type: date
	+ Required: yes
