# Educational Grant Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Grant Declaration Form is a document that students must complete to apply for educational grants from the institution. The form collects relevant information about the student, their purpose for applying, and the grant amount they are seeking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Ensure that you are eligible to apply for the educational grant.
*   Fill in your student name and email address in the respective fields.
*   Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by the institution.
*   Specify the name of the educational institution you are affiliated with.
*   Choose the date you are making this declaration.
*   State the purpose of the grant you are applying for.
*   Specify the grant amount you are seeking.
*   Indicate the status of your grant declaration (Applied, Declined, or Approved).
*   Add any additional comments you would like to provide.
*   Click the "Submit" button to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   *Student Name* (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
*   *Student Email* (`student_email`, email, optional): Enter your active and official email address associated with your student account.
*   *Student Phone* (`student_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further clarification or discussion.
*   *Educational Institution* (`educational_institution`, text, optional): Enter the name of the educational institution you are affiliated with.
*   *Declaration Date* (`declaration_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are making this declaration.
*   *Purpose Statement* (`purpose_statement`, text, optional): Describe the purpose of the grant you are applying for.
*   *Grant Amount* (`grant_amount`, number, optional): Enter the grant amount you are seeking.
*   *Educational Grant Declaration Status* (`educational_grant_declaration_status`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you have Applied, Declined, or had your grant Approved.
*   *Comments* (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments you would like to provide.
*   *Submit* (`submit`, text, optional): Click the "Submit" button to submit your form.
