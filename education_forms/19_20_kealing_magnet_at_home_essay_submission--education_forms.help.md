# 19 20 Kealing Magnet At Home Essay Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 19 20 Kealing Magnet At Home Essay Submission form is used to collect information from students who have submitted their essays for evaluation. This form gathers personal details, essay submission information, and feedback from the students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Personal Information**: Enter your full name and email address. This information will be used to contact you regarding your essay submission.
2. **Essay Submission**: Provide a title for your essay, the date you completed it, and the word count.
3. **Essay Type**: Choose the genre of your essay (e.g., Narrative, Analytical, Research, Creative, or Reflective).
4. **Evaluation Criteria**: Evaluate your essay on originality, organization, clarity, grammar, citation style, and overall quality.
5. **Feedback**: Provide feedback on your experience with the form. Select all that apply from the list of options, and add any additional comments or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Personal Information
• **Full name** (`full_name`): Enter your full legal name as it appears on official documents.
• **Email address** (`email_address`): Enter the email address where you would like to receive confirmation and updates.
### Essay Submission
• **Essay title** (`essay_title`): Provide a concise title for your essay.
• **Submission date** (`submission_date`): Enter the date you completed the essay.
• **Essay type** (`essay_type`): Choose the genre of your essay from the list provided.
• **Word count** (`essay_word_count`): Enter the total number of words in your essay.
### Evaluation Criteria
• **Originality** (`originality`): Evaluate your essay's originality on a scale of 1-5 (Fully Original, Prolly Original, Slightly Original, Not Very Original, Prolly Plagiarized).
• **Organization** (`organization`): Evaluate the organization and structure of your essay.
• **Clarity** (`clarity`): Evaluate the clarity and readability of your essay.
• **Grammar** (`grammar`): Evaluate the grammatical accuracy of your essay.
• **Citation style** (`citation_style`): Choose the citation style used in your essay.
### Feedback
• **How satisfied** (`satisfaction`): Select the level of satisfaction with your experience (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
• **Overall rating** (`overall_rating`): Rate your experience with the form on a scale of 1-10.
• **Aspects applied** (`experience_aspects`): Select all that apply from the list of options (Easy to use, Fast, Reliable, Missing feature, Confusing, Well-designed).
• **Open feedback** (`open_feedback`): Share any suggestions or concerns you may have.
• **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`): Add any extra information you think is relevant.

**Tips**

* Ensure that your email address is accurate, as it will be used for confirmation and updates.
* Be honest and thorough when evaluating your essay on originality, organization, clarity, grammar, and citation style.
* Select all that apply from the list of options when giving feedback on your experience.
* Share any suggestions or concerns you may have to help us improve the form and your experience.
