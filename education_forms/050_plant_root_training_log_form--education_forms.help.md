# Plant Root Training Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to record and log training sessions for plant roots. The information you provide will help track progress and evaluate the effectiveness of the training.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Plant Root ID** (required): Enter the ID of the plant root undergoing training.
2. **Plant Name** (optional): Enter the name of the plant root (if known).
3. **Training Session Start Time** (optional): Enter the start time of the training session.
4. **Training Session End Time** (optional): Enter the end time of the training session.
5. **Training Duration** (optional): Enter the duration of the training session in hours.
6. **Training Session Notes** (optional): Enter any notes or comments about the training session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Plant Root ID**: A unique identifier for the plant root undergoing training. This helps identify the specific plant root being trained.
* **Plant Name**: The name of the plant root, if known. This can be helpful for reference or to identify the plant root.
* **Training Session Start Time**: The start time of the training session. This is the time when the training began.
* **Training Session End Time**: The end time of the training session. This is the time when the training concluded.
* **Training Duration**: The length of time the training session lasted in hours.
* **Training Session Notes**: Any additional comments or notes about the training session that may be helpful for reference.

## Tips

* Be sure to enter accurate information to ensure accurate tracking and evaluation.
* If you've already completed the form for this plant root, do not repeat the entry.
* If you're unsure about a field, try to provide the best estimate or leave it blank if it's not applicable.
