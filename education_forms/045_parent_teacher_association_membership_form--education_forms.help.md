# Parent Teacher Association Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parent Teacher Association Membership Form is used to collect information from parents and teachers who want to join the Parent-Teacher Association (PTA) and to track their membership status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Member Type**: Select the type of member you are (PTA Member, PTA Member - Student, or Non Member).
* **First Name**: Enter your first name in the field provided.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name in the field provided.
* **Email**: Enter your email address in the format "example@example.com".
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number in the format "XXX-XXX-XXXX" or "XXX XXX XXXX".
* **Member Since**: Indicate when you joined the PTA (e.g., January 2020).
* **Role**: Specify your role within the PTA.
* **School Year**: Enter the school year you are associated with.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments about your membership.
