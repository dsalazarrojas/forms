# Data Ethics Awareness Workshop Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request a Data Ethics Awareness Workshop. Please fill out the form with your information and any relevant details to help the workshop organizers plan and prepare for the event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
2. **Email**: Enter your official email address.
3. **Role**: Describe your role in the organization or institution requesting the workshop.
4. **Event Date**: Enter the date of the workshop in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
5. **Event Time**: Enter the start time of the workshop in the format 12:00 AM/PM.
6. **Location**: Enter the physical location of the workshop.
7. **Number of Attendees**: Estimate the number of people who will be attending the workshop.
8. **Additional Info**: Provide any additional information that might be relevant to the workshop's organization.
9. **Contact Info**: Enter any other contact information for yourself or your organization, such as a phone number or department name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your official email address for communication about the workshop.
* **Role** (`role`, text, required): Describe your role in the organization or institution requesting the workshop.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the workshop in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of the workshop in the format 12:00 AM/PM.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter the physical location of the workshop.
* **Number of Attendees** (`attendees`, text, required): Estimate the number of people who will be attending the workshop.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional information that might be relevant to the workshop's organization.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter any other contact information for yourself or your organization, such as a phone number or department name.
