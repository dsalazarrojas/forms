# Open Day Appointment Scheduling - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help students schedule appointments for "Open Day" events. It captures essential details about students, their preferences, and appointment specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Student Details" section with the student's information.
2. In the "Student Preferences" section, provide any additional details or notes about the student's preferences.
3. Enter the "Appointment Details" in the corresponding section.
4. Review and confirm the information provided in the "Confirm and Submit" section before submitting the form.
5. Finally, review the "Email Preferences" section to adjust email settings and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Details** (`student_details_1`, `text`, required): Enter the student's name, grade level, and other relevant details.
* **Student Preferences** (`student_preferences_1`, `text`, required): Add any additional details or notes about the student's preferences.
* **Appointment Details** (`appointment_details_1`, `text`, required): Provide the appointment details, such as date, time, and duration.
* **Student Preferences 2** (`student_preferences_2`, `text`, required): This field seems to be a duplicate of "Student Preferences", which is unusual. Please use the "Student Preferences 1" field instead.
* **Additional Details** (`additional_details_1`, `text`, required): Enter any additional details or information not covered in previous sections.
* **Submit and Share** (`submit_and_share_1`, `text`, required): Review and confirm the information before submitting the form. This field appears to be redundant, as it's similar to the "Confirm and Submit" fields.
* **Email Preferences** (`email_preferences_1`, `text`, required): Adjust email settings as needed.
* **Confirm and Submit** (`confirm_and_submit_1`, `text`, required): Review the information provided and confirm before submitting the form.
* **Confirm and Submit 2** (`confirm_and_submit_2`, `text`, required): Another "Confirm and Submit" field, which seems to be redundant.
* **Confirm and Submit 3-16** (`confirm_and_submit_2-16`, `text`, not required): These fields appear to be unnecessary and can be removed or merged with the previous "Confirm and Submit" field.

## Tips
* Double-check all fields before submitting the form to ensure accurate information is provided.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
* Review and confirm each section carefully to avoid errors.
* Use the "Email Preferences" section to adjust email settings as needed.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
