# Kindergarten Afternoon Arrangement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kindergarten Afternoon Arrangement Form is used to communicate with parents and staff about a student's pickup and drop-off during the school day. Please complete this form to ensure accurate and timely arrangements for your child's pickup.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each field carefully and answer all the necessary questions.
2. Ensure you select the correct options from the dropdown menus.
3. Enter all relevant information in the text fields.
4. Double-check your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pickup Permission**: Select 'Yes' if you are authorized to pick up your child, or 'No' if you are not.
* **Schedule**: Select the date for your child's pickup.
* **Special Instructions**: Provide any special instructions or requests you have for your child's pickup.
* **Parent Name**: Enter your name as the parent or guardian.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number in case we need to reach you.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter an emergency contact's name and number.
* **Pickup Contact**: Select 'Yes' if you will be picking up your child, or 'No' if someone else will.
* **Pickup Time**: Select the time for your child's pickup.
* **Pickup Location**: Enter the location where your child will be picked up.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional information about your child's pickup.
* **Email**: Enter a contact email in case we need to reach you.
* **Notes for Staff**: Any additional information for the staff to know about your child's pickup.
* **Pickup Details**: Select 'Yes' if you will be picking up your child, or 'No' if someone else will.
* **Special**: Select 'Yes' for any special arrangements or conditions.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method used for pickup (e.g., money, credit card, etc.).
* **Pickup Method**: Select the method used for pickup (e.g., car, walking, etc.).
* **Pickup Person**: Select 'Yes' if you will be the one picking up your child, or 'No' if someone else will.
* **Pickup Date**: Select the date for your child's pickup.
* **Notes 2**: Provide any additional information about your child's pickup.
* **Pickup Time 2**: Select the time for your child's pickup.
* **Pickup Person 2**: Select 'Yes' if you will be the one picking up your child, or 'No' if someone else will.
* **Pickup Location 2**: Enter the location where your child will be picked up.
* **Pickup Time 3**: Select the time for your child's pickup.
* **Pickup Date 2**: Select the date for your child's pickup.
* **Payment Method 2**: Select the payment method used for pickup (e.g., money, credit card, etc.).
