# Summer Class Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Class Registration Form is designed to collect information from students who are interested in registering for summer classes or programs. This form will help the institution or organization running the classes or programs to understand who is interested and prepare accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your student status (Yes or No) on the "Student Information" page.
2. Enter your Student ID number (if applicable) on the "Student ID" page.
3. Choose whether you are interested in "Course Information" (Yes or No).
4. Select your interest in "Summer Programs" (Yes or No).
5. Choose your answer to "Program Information" (Yes or No).
6. Enter your date of birth and time zone on the "Date and Time" page.
7. Select your payment method (Yes or No).
8. Enter the total cost of the program on the "Total Cost" page.
9. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information** (Student Info, Select Multiple, Not Required):
	+ This field is used to ask if you are a student or not. Please select "Yes" if you are a student.
* **Student ID** (Student ID, Number, Not Required):
	+ Please enter your student ID number if you have one.
* **Course Information** (Course Info, Select One, Not Required):
	+ This field is used to ask if you are interested in course information. Please select "Yes" if you are interested.
* **Summer Programs** (Summer Prog, Select Multiple, Not Required):
	+ This field is used to ask if you are interested in summer programs. Please select "Yes" if you are interested.
* **Program Information** (Prog Info, Select One, Not Required):
	+ This field is used to ask if you are interested in program information. Please select "Yes" if you are interested.
* **Date and Time** (Date & Time, Date, Not Required):
	+ Please enter your date of birth and select your time zone.
* **Payment Method** (Pay Meth, Select One, Not Required):
	+ This field is used to ask how you plan to pay for the program. Please select "Yes" if you plan to pay.
* **Total Cost** (Tot Cost, Number, Not Required):
	+ If you have already paid for the program, please enter the total cost here.
* **Submit** (Sub, Note, Not Required):
	+ Please review your information and submit the form to complete the registration process.
* **First Name** (FName, Text, Not Required):
	+ Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (LName, Text, Not Required):
	+ Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, Email, Not Required):
	+ Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Phone, Text, Not Required):
	+ Please enter your phone number.
* **Address** (Addr, Text, Not Required):
	+ Please enter your address.
* **City** (City, Text, Not Required):
	+ Please enter your city.
* **State** (State, Text, Not Required):
	+ Please enter your state.
* **Zip Code** (Zip, Text, Not Required):
	+ Please enter your zip code.

Note: The fields marked as "Not Required" can be optional and are not necessary for the registration process. However, providing this information will help the institution or organization running the classes or programs to better understand their target audience.
