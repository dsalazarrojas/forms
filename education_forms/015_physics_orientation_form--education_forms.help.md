# Physics Orientation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from students in the physics department. It's an administrative form used to help students get familiar with departmental procedures or for data collection and analysis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form's purpose and instructions.
2. Fill out the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
3. Enter the student's contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
4. Provide the student's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
5. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, Text, Required/Optional: Required): Enter your full name as it appears on official records.
* **Contact Number** (`student_contact_number`, Text, Required/Optional: Required): Enter your contact information for the department or university.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, Date, Required/Optional: Required): Enter the date of your birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
 
## Tips
* Ensure you fill out the form with accurate information.
* Double-check the fields for completeness.
* If you have any issues, contact the physics department.
