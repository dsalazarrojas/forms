# small_group_chapel_sign_up_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register student groups for the chapel service. It is designed for students and group leaders/leaders to sign up for the chapel service. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the first page and fill out the required fields: First Name, Last Name, and Email.
2. On the second page, you will be asked about the student's Student ID and Group status.
3. On the third page, you will be asked about the Class Year.
4. On the fourth page, you will be asked about the parental and teacher involvement in the group, and if any teachers are leading the group.
5. If the group has leaders other than teachers, please indicate the number of group leaders in the Class Size field, and provide their names in the Notes field.
6. Finally, on the last page, you will be asked for any additional notes and confirm the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, `text`, required): Enter your student ID number.
* **Student Group** (`student_group`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' if you are part of a student group, 'No' otherwise.
* **Class Year** (`class_year`, `number`, required): Enter the class year you are in.
* **Parent Given** (`parent_given`, `select_multiple`, required): Select 'Yes' if your parents are involved in the group, 'No' otherwise.
* **Teacher Given** (`teacher_given`, `select_multiple`, required): Select 'Yes' if your teacher is involved in the group, 'No' otherwise.
* **Group Leaders** (`group_leaders`, `select_multiple`, required): If there are group leaders other than teachers, select 'Yes' and provide their names in the Notes field.
* **Teachers** (`teacher_leaders`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select 'Yes' if any teachers are leading the group, 'No' otherwise.
* **Teachers 2** (`teacher_leaders_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select 'Yes' if any additional teachers are leading the group, 'No' otherwise.
* **Teachers 3** (`teacher_leaders_3`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select 'Yes' if any additional teachers are leading the group, 'No' otherwise.
* **Group Church** (`group_church`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select 'Yes' if the group is related to a church, 'No' otherwise.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes about the group.
* **Student Count** (`student_count`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of students in the group.
* **Class Size** (`class_size`, `number`, optional): Enter the size of the class.
* **Teacher Count** (`teacher_count`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of teachers in the group.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the teacher's name.
* **Teacher Name 2** (`teacher_name_2`, `text`, optional): Enter the teacher's name.
* **Teacher Name 3** (`teacher_name_3`, `text`, optional): Enter the teacher's name.
* **Teacher Name 4** (`teacher_name_4`, `text`, optional): Enter the teacher's name.
* **Teacher Name 5** (`teacher_name_5`, `text`, optional): Enter the teacher's name.
* **Teacher Name 6** (`teacher_name_6`, `text`, optional): Enter the teacher's name.
* **Teacher Name 7** (`teacher_name_7`, `text`, optional): Enter the teacher's name.
* **Teacher Name 8** (`teacher_name_8`, `text`, optional): Enter the teacher's name.

## Tips
- Please make sure to check all the required fields before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the relevant authority or group leader for clarification.
- If you are signing up as a group leader, please fill out all the required fields for your group.
- If you are signing up as a student, please only fill out the required fields for yourself.
