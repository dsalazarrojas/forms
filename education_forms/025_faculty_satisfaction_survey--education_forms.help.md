# Faculty Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Faculty Satisfaction Survey is designed to collect feedback from students about their experience with a specific course. This form helps instructors and course administrators understand the quality of their teaching, materials, and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction carefully and ensure you're taking the survey for the right course.
2. Choose the course you're completing the survey for from the "Which course are you taking?" dropdown menu.
3. Answer each question honestly, and provide specific examples or explanations when necessary.
4. Use the "Select One" or "Select Multiple" options for questions that require multiple choices.
5. Enter your name and contact information (email and phone number) for communication purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome to our Faculty Satisfaction Survey**: This is a brief introduction to the survey, explaining its purpose and importance. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **Which course are you taking?** (`course_id`, `select_one`): Choose the course you're taking from the dropdown menu. This helps us understand which course your feedback is related to.
* **Teaching Quality** (`teaching_quality`, `number`): Rate the quality of your teacher's teaching on a scale, typically from 1-5 or 1-10. Be honest and provide specific reasons for your rating.
* **Class Size** (`class_size`, `number`): Provide your ideal class size, which can be a number or a range (e.g., 10-20 students). This helps us understand your preferences for class size.
* **Teacher Support** (`teacher_support`, `select_one`): Choose how you feel about the support your teacher provides, from "Much more than enough" to "A little less than enough".
* **Materials Provided** (`materials_provided`, `select_multiple`): Select all the materials that you feel have been provided, such as "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
* **Communication** (`communication`, `note`): Provide your thoughts on how well your teacher communicated with you. Be specific about what worked well and what didn't.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`): Rate your overall satisfaction with the course on a scale from "Very satisfied" to "Very dissatisfied".
* **Do you have any comments or suggestions?** (`comments`, `text`): Share your thoughts, suggestions, or concerns about the course.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further questions or discussions.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`): Enter your name for identification purposes.

Please take a few minutes to complete this survey, and thank you for your time and feedback!
