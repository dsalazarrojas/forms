# Graduation Ceremony Invitation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a graduation ceremony invitation. Please fill it out carefully to avoid errors.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in the necessary fields as prompted
- Enter your graduation ceremony information accurately and completely
- If you are unsure about any field, leave it as is or ask for clarification

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Graduation Ceremony Invitation** (`graduation_ceremony_invitation_form`): This is the main heading of the invitation. Enter the name of the graduation ceremony.
- **Date** (`date`): Enter the date of the graduation ceremony in a standard date format (e.g., 2024-02-15).
- **Time** (`time`): Enter the time of the graduation ceremony in a 24-hour format (e.g., 14:30).
- **Graduation Name** (`graduation_name`): Enter the name of the person graduating.
- **Graduation Title** (`graduation_title`): Enter a title or nickname (e.g., "The Proud Alumni" for the graduate's name).
- **Ceremony Name** (`ceremony_name`): Enter the name of the graduation ceremony (e.g., "Graduation Ceremony").
- **Graduation Date** (`graduation_date`): This field should match the Date field.
- **Graduation Time** (`graduation_time`): This field should match the Time field.
- **Invitation Message** (`invitation_message`): Enter a brief message to include with the invitation.
- **Graduation Year** (`graduation_year`): Enter the year of graduation.
- **Email** (`email`): Enter the email address to send the invitation to.
- **Phone** (`phone`): Enter the phone number to send the invitation to.
- **Send Reminders** (`send_reminders`): Choose whether to send reminders for this graduation ceremony.
- **Send Reminders Before** (`send_reminders_before`): Enter the time before the graduation ceremony when you want to send reminders.
- **Send Reminders After** (`send_reminders_after`): Enter the time after the graduation ceremony when you want to send reminders.
- **Save and Share** (`save_and_share`): Choose what to do with the completed form - save or share it.

Note: If you're unsure about any field, please contact an administrator for assistance.
