# olli_south_class_proposal_and_information_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Olli South Class Proposal and Information Form is used to create and submit proposals for new classes, workshops, or seminars. This form allows you to provide detailed information about your class, such as title, description, facilitator contact information, and class schedule.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Class Title field with a concise and descriptive title for your class.
2. Enter a brief description of your class in the Class Description field.
3. Provide your Facilitator Name and Facilitator Email in the respective fields.
4. Enter the Location for your class.
5. Choose a date and time for your class from the start and end date fields.
6. Select the desired length of your class from the Meeting Length options.
7. Choose the target audience for your class from the Target Audience options.
8. Select the level of your class from the Class Level options.
9. Choose the type of class from the Class Type options.
10. Select the meeting length and days from the Meeting Length and Meeting Days options.
11. Enter any additional notes or comments in the Notes field.
12. Write up to 5 proposals for your class in the Proposal 1 to Proposal 5 fields.
13. Enter any contact information in the Contact Information field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Class Title** (1, text, required: false): Enter a concise and descriptive title for your class.
* **Class Description** (2, text, required: false): Briefly describe your class in this field.
* **Facilitator Name** (3, text, required: false): Enter the name of the facilitator of your class.
* **Facilitator Email** (4, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the facilitator.
* **Facilitator Phone** (5, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the facilitator.
* **Location** (6, text, required: false): Enter the location for your class.
* **Dates** (7, date, required: false): Choose a start date for your class.
* **Start Time** (8, time, required: false): Choose a start time for your class.
* **End Time** (9, time, required: false): Choose an end time for your class.
* **Length** (10, number, required: false): Select the length of your class in hours.
* **Target Audience** (11, select_one, required: false): Choose the target audience for your class.
* **Class Level** (12, select_one, required: false): Select the level of your class.
* **Class Type** (13, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of class.
* **Meeting Length** (14, select_one, required: false): Select the meeting length.
* **Meeting Days** (15, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the meeting days.
* **Start Date** (16, date, required: false): Enter the start date of your class.
* **End Date** (17, date, required: false): Enter the end date of your class.
* **Notes** (18, text, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Class Proposal** (19, text, required: false): Enter your class proposal.
* **Contact Information** (20, text, required: false): Enter any contact information.
* **Proposal 1** (21, text, required: false): Enter a proposal for your class.
* **Proposal 2** (22, text, required: false): Enter a proposal for your class.
* **Proposal 3** (23, text, required: false): Enter a proposal for your class.
* **Proposal 4** (24, text, required: false): Enter a proposal for your class.
* **Proposal 5** (25, text, required: false): Enter a proposal for your class.

## Tips
* Please fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Make sure to proofread your form submission before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your administrator for assistance.
