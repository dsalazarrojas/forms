# Tablet Feedback Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tablet Feedback Form is a tool to gather information from tablet users about their learning experience. It helps us to identify areas of improvement and provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out each page of the form as instructed below.
2. Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Make sure to provide clear and concise answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Feedback (page_one)
* **General Feedback** (`General Feedback`, `note`, required: false): Please provide any general comments or suggestions about your learning experience.

### Specific Feedback (page_two)
* **Specific Feedback** (`Specific Feedback`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all that apply about your specific feedback on the learning experience.
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3

### Satisfaction (page_three)
* **How satisfied are you with your learning experience?** (`Satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your satisfaction level on a scale from Very Satisfied to Very Dissatisfied.
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied

### Additional Comments (page_four)
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or suggestions about your learning experience.

### Rating (page_five)
* **Rating** (`Rating`, `number`, required: false): Rate your overall experience on a scale from 1 to 5.

### Recommendation (page_six)
* **Recommend this learning experience to others?** (`Recommendation`, `select_one`, required: false): Please indicate if you would recommend this learning experience to others.
	+ Yes
	+ No

### Contact Information (page_seven)
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, required: false): Provide your email address so we can follow up with you about your feedback.

### Email (page_eight)
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you about your feedback.
