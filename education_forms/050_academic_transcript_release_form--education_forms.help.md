# Academic Transcript Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Academic Transcript Release Form is a document that allows students to authorize the release of their academic transcript to a third party. This form is used to ensure that students are aware of their rights and responsibilities related to their educational records and to maintain data privacy and security.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student name in the `Student Name` field.
2. Enter your student ID number in the `Student ID` field.
3. Provide your current email address in the `Student Email` field.
4. Enter the name of the person or organization requesting the transcript in the `Recipient Name` field.
5. Specify the name of the institution or organization receiving the transcript in the `Recipient Institution/Organization` field.
6. Enter the address of the recipient in the `Recipient Address` field.
7. Select the type of transcript being requested from the `Type of Transcript` dropdown menu.
8. Specify the academic periods to be covered in the `Academic Periods Covered` field.
9. State the purpose of the release in the `Purpose of Release` field.
10. Choose the authorization validity period from the `Authorization Validity Period` dropdown menu.
11. Acknowledge that fees may apply for transcript processing.
12. Confirm that you understand your privacy rights related to your educational records.
13. Acknowledge that you can revoke this authorization at any time.
14. Consent to using an electronic signature for this authorization.
15. Sign your name as an electronic signature in the `Student Signature` field.
16. Enter the date when this authorization was signed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter your full name as a student.
* **Student ID**: Enter your official student identification number.
* **Student Email**: Enter your current email address.
* **Recipient Name**: Enter the full name of the person or organization receiving the transcript.
* **Recipient Institution/Organization**: Enter the name of the institution or organization receiving the transcript.
* **Recipient Address**: Enter the complete mailing address of the recipient.
* **Type of Transcript**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Official transcript: Official transcript with all grades and courses.
	+ Unofficial transcript: Unofficial transcript without grades.
	+ Partial transcript: Partial transcript with selected courses.
	+ Degree verification: Verification of degree completion.
	+ Course verification: Verification of course completion.
	+ Other: Specify other type of transcript.
* **Academic Periods Covered**: Specify the academic periods to be included in the transcript.
* **Purpose of Release**: Explain the reason for the transcript release.
* **Authorization Validity Period**: Choose one of the following options:
	+ One-time request: The authorization is valid only for this single request.
	+ 30 days: The authorization is valid for 30 days.
	+ 60 days: The authorization is valid for 60 days.
	+ 90 days: The authorization is valid for 90 days.
	+ 6 months: The authorization is valid for 6 months.
	+ 1 year: The authorization is valid for 1 year.
	+ Indefinite: The authorization is valid indefinitely.
* **Fee Acknowledgment**: Acknowledge that fees may apply for transcript processing.
* **Privacy Rights Acknowledgment**: Confirm that you understand your privacy rights related to your educational records.
* **Revocation Rights Acknowledgment**: Confirm that you understand you can revoke this authorization at any time.
* **Electronic Signature Consent**: Consent to using an electronic signature for this authorization.
* **Student Signature**: Enter your full name as an electronic signature.
* **Date of Signature**: Enter the date when this authorization was signed.
