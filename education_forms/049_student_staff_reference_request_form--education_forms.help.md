# Student Staff Reference Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Staff Reference Request Form is a document used to request a staff member to provide a character reference for a student. This form ensures that the request is properly documented and submitted to the relevant staff member in the correct department.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure you are requesting a reference from a staff member you have a relationship with.
3. Be mindful of the required fields to avoid unnecessary delays.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Student Name (`student_name`, text, required)
Enter the name of the student requesting the reference.

### Email (`email`, email, required)
Enter the student's email address.

### Job Title (`job_title`, text, optional)
If applicable, enter the staff member's job title.

### Department (`department`, text, optional)
If applicable, enter the staff member's department.

### Academic Institution (`academic_institution`, text, required)
Enter the name of the academic institution affiliated with the staff member.

### Reference Relationship (`reference_relationship`, select_one, required)
Choose how you know the staff member (Supervisor, Colleague, Mentor).

### Contact Method (`contact_method`, select_one, required)
Choose the method to contact the staff member (Phone, Email, In Person).

### Academic Year (`academic_year`, text, optional)
If applicable, enter the academic year the staff member was involved with the student.

### Staff Member Since (`staff_member_since`, date, optional)
If applicable, enter the date the staff member joined the institution.

### Reference Request Date (`reference_request_date`, date, required)
Enter the date the staff reference request was made.
