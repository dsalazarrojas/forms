# Laboratory Experiment Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from students who have participated in a laboratory experiment. This feedback will help instructors and laboratory staff to evaluate and improve the quality of their experiments and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer each question to the best of your ability. Make sure to provide your honest opinion and feedback. If a question does not apply to you, you can skip it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction (Page 1)
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, optional): This section is a space for you to briefly introduce yourself and mention any relevant background information about your experience in the laboratory or with the experiment.

### Experiment Information (Page 2)
* **Experiment Information** (`experiment_info`, `text`, optional): Describe the laboratory experiment you participated in, including any relevant details such as the experiment's objective, procedures, and outcomes.

### Rate Your Experiment Experience (Page 3)
* **Rate Your Experiment Experience** (`rating_scale`, `select_one`, optional): On a scale from 1 to 3, rate your overall experience in the laboratory. This will help us understand how we can improve your experience in the future.
	+ Option 1: Poor
	+ Option 2: Average
	+ Option 3: Good

### Comments (Page 4)
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions about your experience, please feel free to share them here.

### Rate Your Instructor (Page 5)
* **Rate Your Instructor** (`rating_scale_2`, `select_one`, optional): On a scale from 1 to 3, rate your overall experience with your instructor. This will help us evaluate the quality of our instructors.
	+ Option 1: Poor
	+ Option 2: Average
	+ Option 3: Good

### Instructor Information (Page 6)
* **Instructor Information** (`instructor_info`, `text`, optional): If you have any comments or suggestions about your instructor, please share them here.

### Rate Your Equipment (Page 7)
* **Rate Your Equipment** (`rating_scale_3`, `select_one`, optional): On a scale from 1 to 3, rate the quality of the equipment used during the experiment. This will help us evaluate the quality of our equipment and make improvements as needed.
	+ Option 1: Poor
	+ Option 2: Average
	+ Option 3: Good

### Equipment Information (Page 8)
* **Equipment Information** (`equipment_info`, `text`, optional): If you have any comments or suggestions about the equipment used, please share them here.

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough in your feedback. Your input is valuable to us.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to us.
