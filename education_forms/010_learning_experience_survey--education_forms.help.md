# Learning Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and information about the learning experience from both students and teachers. It is used to gather insights into how the learning process is going, what students have achieved, and what areas for improvement exist.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form in its entirety, providing accurate and honest information about your learning experience.
2. If you have participated in a learning experience as both a student and a teacher, please fill out the form twice, once for each role.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Student Info (Page 1)**
* **Student Info (student_info)**: Enter your name, student ID, or any other unique identifier that helps us identify you.
* **Student Info (student_info)**: This field is optional and allows you to provide any additional information about yourself that might be relevant to the learning experience.

### **Teacher Info (Page 2)**
* **Teacher Info (teacher_info)**: Enter the name of your teacher or instructor who led the learning experience.
* **Teacher Info (teacher_info)**: This field is optional and allows you to provide any additional information about your teacher that might be relevant to the learning experience.

### **Learning Outcomes (Page 3)**
* **Learning Outcomes (learning_outcomes)**: Choose all the learning outcomes you achieved during the learning experience.
* **Learning Outcomes (learning_outcomes)**: This field is a multiple-choice question that asks you to select all the outcomes you achieved during the learning experience.

### **Student Feedback (Page 4)**
* **Student Feedback (student_feedback)**: Provide your honest feedback about the learning experience.
* **Student Feedback (student_feedback)**: This field is a text input where you can express your thoughts and opinions about the learning experience.

### **Teacher Feedback (Page 5)**
* **Teacher Feedback (teacher_feedback)**: Provide your feedback about the learning experience as a teacher.
* **Teacher Feedback (teacher_feedback)**: This field is a text input where you can express your thoughts and opinions about the learning experience.

### **Learning Process (Page 6)**
* **Learning Process (learning_process)**: Choose the learning process that best describes your experience.
* **Learning Process (learning_process)**: This field is a single-choice question that asks you to select one of the given options.

### **Assessment (Page 7)**
* **Assessment (assessment)**: Choose all the assessments you used during the learning experience.
* **Assessment (assessment)**: This field is a multiple-choice question that asks you to select all the assessments you used during the learning experience.

### **Student Goals (Page 8)**
* **Student Goals (student_goals)**: Enter your goals for participating in the learning experience.
* **Student Goals (student_goals)**: This field is a text input where you can express your goals and objectives for participating in the learning experience.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate in your responses.
* If you have any additional feedback or comments, please use the student_feedback or teacher_feedback fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [insert contact information].
