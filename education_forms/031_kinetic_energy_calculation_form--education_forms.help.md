# Kinetic Energy Calculation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to calculate the kinetic energy of an object given its mass and velocity. The form is intended to be used in a physics or engineering context to quickly calculate the kinetic energy of an object in motion.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the mass of the object in the "mass" field.
2. Enter the velocity of the object in the "velocity" field.
3. Press the "submit" button to calculate the kinetic energy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **mass**: Enter the mass of the object in kilograms (kg). This is the amount of matter in the object.
* **velocity**: Enter the velocity of the object in meters per second (m/s). This is the speed at which the object is moving.
* **result**: This is the calculated kinetic energy of the object, displayed in a note field after submitting the form. The kinetic energy is calculated using the formula: Kinetic Energy = (1/2) \* mass \* velocity^2.
* **Note**: The form assumes that the mass and velocity are in standard international units (kilograms and meters per second).
