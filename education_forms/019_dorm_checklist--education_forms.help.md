# Dorm Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to verify the items a student has for their stay in the dorm. Please fill in the form to ensure that you have all the required items.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Student ID number to identify which student's items are being verified.
2. Fill in the student's Name.
3. Enter the student's email address.
4. Enter the room number where the student is staying.
5. List any additional items the student may need that are not listed below.
6. Answer yes or no for each item below, depending on whether the student has the item or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student ID (`student_id`, number, required)**: Enter your student ID number to identify you as the student being checked.
* **Name (`name`, text, required)**: Enter your name as it appears in your student database.

* **Room Number (`room_number`, number, required)**: Enter the number of the room you are staying in.
