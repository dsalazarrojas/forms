# Faculty Sabbatical Leave Of Absence Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Faculty Sabbatical Leave Of Absence Application form is used to request a sabbatical leave of absence from your duties as a faculty member. A sabbatical is a period of temporary leave taken by a faculty member to focus on research, writing, or other professional development activities.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill out all required fields, marked as required in the field description.
* Enter your faculty name in the "Name" field.
* Enter your university email address in the "Email" field.
* Enter your department in the "Department" field, choosing one of the available options.
* If your department is not listed, please contact your department chairperson for assistance.
* Select the appropriate date range for your sabbatical leave of absence in the "Dates Of Absence" field.
* Choose the reason for your sabbatical leave from the "Reason For Leave" field.
* In the "Approval Status" field, select the relevant approval status for your sabbatical leave. Note that this field is required.
* In the "Notes" field, include any additional information or context that may be useful for your department to process your request.
* If you are requesting a specific date, please enter it in the "Date Of Request" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (Name, text, required): Enter your faculty name.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your university email address.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Department** (Department, select one, optional): Choose the department in which you work.
* **Dates Of Absence** (Dates Of Absence, date, required): Select the date range for your sabbatical leave of absence.
* **Reason For Leave** (Reason For Leave, select multiple, optional): Choose the reason for your sabbatical leave.
* **Approval Status** (Approval Status, select one, required): Select the relevant approval status for your sabbatical leave.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, optional): Include any additional information or context that may be useful for your department to process your request.
* **Date Of Request** (Date Of Request, date, optional): Enter a specific date for your sabbatical leave request, if applicable.
