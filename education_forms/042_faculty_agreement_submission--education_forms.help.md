# Faculty Agreement Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from faculty members about their agreements with the institution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "User Name" field.
2. Enter the start and end dates of your contract in the "Contract Start Date" and "Contract End Date" fields, respectively.
3. Select your department from the "Department" dropdown menu.
4. Choose the type of contract you have with the institution from the "Contract Type" dropdown menu.
5. Enter the length of your contract in the "Contract Length" field.
6. Read and agree to the acknowledgements by typing "I agree" in the corresponding fields.
7. Enter any additional details about your contract in the "Details" field.
8. Indicate the status of your contract as "Active", "Inactive", "Pending", or "Expired" in the "Contract Status" field.
9. Enter the amount of your contract in the "Contract Amount" field.
10. Enter your email address in the "User Email" field.
11. Enter your phone number in the "User Phone" field.
12. Enter the name of your department in the "Department Name" field.
13. Enter the duration of your contract in the "Contract Duration" field.
14. Indicate whether you agree with the agreement by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Agreement Status" field.
15. Enter the date and time of your contract in the "Contract Date" and "Contract Time" fields, respectively.
16. Enter the duration of your contract in the "Contract Duration 2" field.
17. Enter any additional notes about your contract in the "Contract Notes" field.
18. Enter the date of submission in the "Submission Date" field.
19. Enter the name of the person submitting the form in the "Submitted By" field.
