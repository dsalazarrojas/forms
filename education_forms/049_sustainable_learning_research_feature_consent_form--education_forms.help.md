# sustainable_learning_research_feature_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect participant information and consent for research projects related to sustainable learning.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction section and provide any information you want to share about yourself.
2. Fill out the participant details section with your basic contact information.
3. In the research feature section, select all the research feature(s) you are interested in participating in.
4. In the consent section, write your consent for the research project.
5. Fill out the contact details section if you have any questions or need further information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, text, optional): This is a short text field where you can provide any information you want to share about yourself.
* **Participant Details** (`2`, text, optional): This field is for providing your basic contact details.
* **Research Feature** (`3`, select multiple, optional): This field is a multi-select option where you can choose which research feature(s) you are interested in participating in. You can select all that apply.
	+ **Sustainable Development Goals**: This feature is related to sustainable development goals.
	+ **Climate Change**: This feature is related to climate change.
	+ **Education**: This feature is related to education.
* **Consent** (`4`, note, optional): This field is a note field for providing your consent for the research project.
* **Contact Details** (`5`, text, optional): This field is for providing your contact details if you have any questions or need further information.

## Tips

* Please take a moment to read the introduction section carefully before proceeding.
* If you are interested in more than one research feature, select all that apply in the research feature section.
* If you have any questions or need further information, fill out the contact details section.
