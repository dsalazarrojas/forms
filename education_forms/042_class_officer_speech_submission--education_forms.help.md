# Class Officer Speech Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Class Officer Speech Submission form is a critical component of the class election process. It allows class officers to submit their speech, platform, and other essential information to the school administration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the candidate information section with your personal details.
2. Provide a catchy campaign slogan in the campaign slogan field.
3. Write a compelling speech title and content in the speech title and speech text fields.
4. Outline your key platform points and goals if elected.
5. Answer questions about your previous leadership experience and goals for the election.
6. Upload a campaign photo and provide a description of it.
7. If applicable, provide your social media handle and campaign materials.
8. Confirm your candidacy and provide any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Full Name (Candidate Full Name, text, required)**: Fill in your first and last name as it appears on official documents.
* **Student ID Number (Student ID Number, text, required)**: Enter your student ID number as assigned by the school.
* **Email Address (Email Address, email, required)**: Use your school email address for official communication.
* **Phone Number (Phone Number, text, optional)**: Provide a contact number for the candidate or campaign team.
* **Grade Level (Grade Level, select_one, required)**: Select your current grade level as a student.
* **Position You Are Running For (Position You Are Running For, select_one, required)**: Choose the office you are running for from the list.
* **Campaign Slogan (Campaign Slogan, text, optional)**: Enter a catchy phrase for your campaign.
* **Speech Title (Speech Title, text, required)**: Name the title of your speech.
* **Speech Text (Speech Text, text, required)**: Write your full speech content.
* **Estimated Speech Duration (Estimated Speech Duration, select_one, required)**: Choose the estimated time it will take to deliver your speech.
* **Key Platform Points (Key Platform Points, text, required)**: Outline your main objectives and promises to the class.
* **Do You Have Previous Leadership Experience (Do You Have Previous Leadership Experience, select_one, required)**: Answer whether you have prior leadership experience in student government or clubs.
* **Please Describe Your Experience (Please Describe Your Experience, text, optional)**: If yes, provide details about your experience.
* **What Are Your Goals If Elected (What Are Your Goals If Elected, text, required)**: Outline your objectives for the position.
* **Why Should Students Vote for You (Why Should Students Vote for You, text, required)**: Explain why students should vote for you.
* **Campaign Photo (Campaign Photo, note, optional)**: Upload a photo to use in campaign promotion.
* **Photo Description (Photo Description, text, optional)**: Describe the photo you uploaded.
* **Social Media Handle (Social Media Handle, text, optional)**: Provide your social media handle for campaign promotion.
* **Will You Submit Campaign Materials (Will You Submit Campaign Materials, select_one, optional)**: Answer whether you have or will submit campaign materials.
* **Are You Available for Candidate Debate (Are You Available for Candidate Debate, select_one, required)**: Confirm your availability for debate.
* **Faculty Advisor Name (Faculty Advisor Name, text, optional)**: If applicable, enter the name of your faculty advisor.
* **Do You Confirm Your Candidacy (Do You Confirm Your Candidacy, select_one, required)**: Select yes to confirm your candidacy.
* **Additional Comments (Additional Comments, text, optional)**: Provide any other relevant information.
