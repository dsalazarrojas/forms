# Science Communicator Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help evaluate and recommend science communicators for various purposes. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the recommender's expertise and communication skills are effectively represented.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form fields carefully before starting.
2.  Select the education level of the science communicator (9th-12th, Baccalaureate, Master, Doctoral).
3.  Describe the science background of the science communicator in the text field.
4.  Indicate whether the science communicator possesses the required communication skills (True/False).
5.  Enter the name of the recommender in the 'Recommender Name' field.
6.  Enter the position of the recommender in the 'Recommender Position' field (optional).
7.  Enter the institution of the recommender in the 'Recommender Institution' field (optional).
8.  Enter the recommender's email address.
9.  Enter the date and time of the recommendation (Date and Time fields).
10.  Indicate whether you agree with the recommendation (True/False).
11.  Enter any additional comments regarding the science communicator in the 'Recommender Comments' field (optional).
12.  Enter any additional comments regarding the recommendation in the 'Additional Comments' field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Education Level**: Select the level of education completed by the science communicator (9th-12th, Baccalaureate, Master, Doctoral).
*   **Science Background**: Describe the science background of the science communicator, including their areas of expertise and notable achievements.
*   **Communication Skills**: Indicate whether the science communicator possesses the required communication skills (True or False).
*   **Recommender Name**: Enter the name of the person making the recommendation.
*   **Recommender Position**: Enter the position of the person making the recommendation (optional).
*   **Recommender Institution**: Enter the institution of the person making the recommendation (optional).
*   **Recommender Email**: Enter the email address of the person making the recommendation.
*   **Recommender Date**: Enter the date of the recommendation.
*   **Recommender Time**: Enter the time of the recommendation.
*   **Recommender Agreed**: Indicate whether you agree with the recommendation (True or False).
*   **Recommender Comments**: Enter any comments or observations regarding the science communicator.
*   **Recommender Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or explanations regarding the recommendation.
*   **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or explanations regarding the science communicator or recommendation.
