# learning_accessibility_audit_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The learning_accessibility_audit_request_form is used to request and track learning accessibility audits. This form ensures that accessibility needs are being met in the education sector by providing a structured way to submit and track requests for audits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Form ID**: This is a unique identifier for your request. You can leave this field blank if you're unsure what to enter.
2. **Title**: Enter a brief title for your request, describing the purpose of the audit.
3. **Request Type**: Select the type of audit you are requesting (accessibility audit, accessibility report, or accessibility recommendation).
4. **Resources**: Select the resources that need to be audited (courses, platforms, or learning materials).
5. **Start Date**: Enter the date when you would like the audit to start.
6. **Form ID 2** and **Form ID 3**: These fields are optional and can be left blank.
7. **Categories**: Select the category or categories related to your request (accessibility, education, or training).
8. **Teams**: Select the team that should handle your request (schools, universities, or training teams).
9. **Requestee**: Enter the person or team that the audit is intended for.
10. **Description**: Provide a detailed description of the audit request.
11. **Contact**: Enter your contact information so that we can reach out to you regarding your request.
12. **Status**: Select the status of your request (pending, in progress, or completed).
13. **Urgency**: Indicate the level of urgency for your request (high, medium, or low).
14. **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about your request.
15. **Comments**: Add any comments or feedback about your request.
16. **Assignee**: Select the person or team that should handle your request.
17. **Assigned To**: Select the person or team that the audit is assigned to.
18. **Due Date**: Enter the date by which you would like the audit to be completed.
19. **Goals**: Select the goals of the audit (prophets or other).
20. **Form ID 4** and **Form ID 5**: These fields are optional and can be left blank.
21. **Notes 2**: Add any additional notes or comments about your request.
22. **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can reach out to you regarding your request.
23. **Tags**: Select any relevant tags or keywords for your request.
24. **Created At**: This field is automatically populated and cannot be edited.
25. **Updated At**: This field is automatically populated and cannot be edited.
