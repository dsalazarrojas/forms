# Firm Crawl Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from firms that will be participating in events, conferences, or other types of gatherings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the event name in the `Firm Crawl` field.
2. Select the date of the event from the calendar.
3. Enter the time of the event.
4. Enter the firm's name.
5. Enter the firm's address.
6. Enter the firm contact's name.
7. Enter the firm contact's title.
8. Enter the firm contact's email address.
9. Enter the firm contact's phone number.
10. Enter any details about the firm's participation in the event.
11. Enter your own name.
12. Enter your email address.
13. Enter your phone number.
14. Enter your job title.
15. Enter your company name.
16. Enter your email address.
17. Enter your phone number.
18. Select the type of firm participation (Publicly Funded, Private Company Event, Company Sponsored, or Other).
19. Enter any additional details about the firm's participation.
20. Select how often the firm participates (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Rarely).
21. Enter the start date of the firm's participation.
22. Enter the end date of the firm's participation.
23. Enter the location of the firm's participation.
24. Enter any additional notes about the firm's participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Firm Crawl** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Select the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the event.
* **Firm Name** (`firm_name`, text, required): Enter the firm's name.
* **Firm Address** (`firm_address`, text, required): Enter the firm's address.
* **Firm Contact Name** (`firm_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the firm contact's name.
* **Firm Contact Title** (`firm_contact_title`, text, required): Enter the firm contact's title.
* **Firm Contact Email** (`firm_contact_email`, email, required): Enter the firm contact's email address.
* **Firm Contact Phone** (`firm_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the firm contact's phone number.
* **Firm Crawl Details** (`firm_crawl_details`, text, required): Enter any details about the firm's participation.
* **Your Name** (`user_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`user_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`user_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Job Title** (`user_job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title.
* **Job Company** (`user_job_company`, text, required): Enter your company name.
* **Email** (`user_job_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`user_job_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Firm Crawl Participants** (`firm_crawl_participants`, text, required): Enter any details about firm participants.
* **Firm Participation Type** (`firm_participation_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of firm participation (Publicly Funded, Private Company Event, Company Sponsored, or Other).
* **Firm Participation Details** (`firm_participation_details`, text, required): Enter any additional details about firm participation.
* **Firm Participation Frequency** (`firm_participation_frequency`, select_one, required): Select how often the firm participates (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Rarely).
* **Firm Participation Start Date** (`firm_participation_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of firm participation.
* **Firm Participation End Date** (`firm_participation_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of firm participation.
* **Firm Participation Location** (`firm_participation_location`, text, required): Enter the location of firm participation.
* **Firm Participation Notes** (`firm_participation_notes`, text, required): Enter any additional notes about firm participation.
