# gamma_test - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "gamma_test" form is designed to collect information about users' educational backgrounds, interests, or preferences in an educational setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for each field, marked as "required".
2. Review and ensure that all the fields are filled correctly.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the necessary fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Form ID (1)
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, required/optional: false): Enter a unique identifier for this form, if required.

### Category (2)
* **category** (`category`, text, required/optional: false): Select or enter the category related to your educational background or interest.

### Description (3)
* **description** (`description`, text, required/optional: false): Provide a detailed and comprehensive description of your educational background or interest.

### Title (4)
* **Title** (`title`, text, required/optional: false): Enter a title that reflects your educational background or interest.

### Category Title (5)
* **Category Title** (`category_title`, text, required/optional: false): Enter a title for the category you selected.

### Description Title (6)
* **Description Title** (`description_title`, text, required/optional: false): Enter a title for the description you provided.

### Form IDs (7)
* **Form IDs** (`form_ids`, text, required/optional: false): Enter any relevant form IDs related to your educational background or interest.

## Tips
* Be as concise and accurate as possible when filling in the form.
* Ensure that all the required fields are filled correctly before submission.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the support team for guidance.
