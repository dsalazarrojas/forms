# Architecture Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Architecture Knowledge Quiz is designed to assess your knowledge in various architecture-related subjects, including architectural orders, building features, architectural movements, and other related topics. This quiz aims to gauge your understanding of fundamental concepts in architecture.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form by selecting the correct answers to each question.
2. For questions that require you to choose from multiple options, select the option that best aligns with your understanding.
3. For questions that ask about your confidence level and areas of strength and improvement, provide a brief text response.
