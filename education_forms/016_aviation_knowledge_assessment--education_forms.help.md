# Aviation Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess the aviation knowledge and confidence levels of a learner, providing an evaluation of their understanding of core concepts and aerodynamics, as well as their confidence levels in various areas.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your full name in the Learner Full Name field.
2. Enter your email address for feedback purposes in the Learner Email Address field.
3. Enter the name of your flight school or club in the Flight School or Club Name field.
4. Select your current pilot experience level from the options provided in the Current Pilot Experience Level field.
5. Answer each question in the Aviation Knowledge Check section, selecting the correct answer for each question.
6. Rate your confidence levels for navigation and meteorology in the Navigation Confidence and Meteorology Confidence fields.
7. Describe the most challenging topic for you so far in the Most Difficult Topic field.
8. Specify your training goal for the next training block in the Goal for the Next Training Block field.
9. Select your preferred learning style from the options provided in the Preferred Learning Style field.
10. If an instructor has reviewed the material with you, select 'True' in the Has an Instructor Reviewed this with You field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Learner Full Name** (`learner_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name for identification purposes.
* **Learner Email Address** (`learner_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for feedback purposes.
* **Flight School or Club Name** (`flight_school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your flight school or club.
* **Current Pilot Experience Level** (`experience_level`, select_one, required): Choose your current pilot experience level from the options provided.
* **What is the primary cause of induced drag?** (`q1_induced_drag`, select_one, required): Select the correct answer for this question.
* **What is the meaning of the V-speed V_so?** (`q2_vso_meaning`, select_one, required): Select the correct answer for this question.
* **The angle of attack is defined as the angle between?** (`q3_aoa_definition`, select_one, required): Select the correct answer for this question.
* **Navigation Confidence** (`confidence_nav`, select_one, required): Rate your confidence level in navigation, cross-country planning and pilotage.
* **Meteorology Confidence** (`confidence_met`, select_one, required): Rate your confidence level in meteorology, weather theory and briefing.
* **Most Difficult Topic** (`most_difficult_topic`, text, required): Describe the most challenging topic for you so far.
* **Goal for the Next Training Block** (`training_goal`, text, required): Specify your training goal for the next training block.
* **Preferred Learning Style** (`learning_style`, select_one, required): Select your preferred learning style from the options provided.
* **Has an Instructor Reviewed this with You?** (`instructor_review`, select_one, required): If an instructor has reviewed the material with you, select 'True'.
