# Online Language Course Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Language Course Application Form is designed to gather information from applicants for the online language course. This form will help the course administrators to assess the applicant's language proficiency, course level, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

* Please select your preferred language from the list provided in the "Language preferred" field.
* Enter your full name in the "Applicant name" field.
* Provide your contact email in the "Contact email" field.
* Enter your contact telephone number in the "Contact telephone" field.
* Select your country of residence from the list provided in the "Applicant country" field.
* Choose the course level that suits your current language proficiency from the list in the "Course level" field.
* If you have any additional comments or questions, please provide them in the "Additional comments" field.
* Finally, enter a unique Application ID in the "Application ID" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Language preferred**: Select your preferred language from the list provided:
	+ English
	+ French
	+ Spanish
	+ Chinese
	+ Arabic
* **Applicant name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Contact email**: Enter your valid email address that you use for communication.
* **Contact telephone**: Enter your contact telephone number where we can reach you.
* **Applicant country**: Select your country of residence from the list provided:
	+ USA
	+ Canada
	+ Mexico
	+ India
	+ France
* **Course level**: Choose the course level that suits your current language proficiency:
	+ Beginner
	+ Intermediate
	+ Advanced
* **Additional comments**: If you have any additional comments or questions, please provide them here.
* **Application ID**: Enter a unique ID to identify your application.

Note: The fields marked as "required" are obligatory and must be filled. The "Additional comments" field is optional and not required, but highly recommended for any additional information.
