# College Service Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a service request to the college or university administration. It collects information about the requestor, the type of service requested, and the details of the service needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the request date when the service is needed.
2. Enter your full name and title (e.g., student, staff, faculty, etc.).
3. Provide your email address for communication purposes.
4. Enter your phone number for contact.
5. Specify your department or organization affiliation.
6. Choose the type of service requested (e.g., maintenance, facilities, technology, etc.).
7. Describe the service needed in detail.
8. Specify the location where the service is needed.
9. Choose the date when the service is needed.
10. Choose the preferred service time (optional).
11. Estimate the duration of the service.
12. Choose the urgency level of the service (e.g., emergency, urgent, standard, etc.).
13. Provide any specific requirements or special instructions.
14. Enter an estimated budget or cost (if applicable).
15. Enter a budget code or account number (if applicable).
16. Specify any special access or key requirements.
17. Enter a contact name during the service (if applicable).
18. Enter a contact phone number (if applicable).
19. Indicate if you have obtained the necessary approvals.
20. Enter the name of the supervisor or manager authorizing this request.
21. Enter a request priority number (if applicable).
22. Add any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Date (1)**: The date when you need the service.
* **Requestor Name (2)**: Your full name.
* **Requestor Role or Title (3)**: Your title or position in the college or university (e.g., student, staff, faculty, etc.).
* **Email Address (4)**: Your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number (5)**: Your phone number for contact.
* **Department or Organization (6)**: Your department or organization affiliation.
* **Type of Service Requested (7)**: The type of service you need (e.g., maintenance, facilities, technology, etc.).
* **Description of Service Needed (8)**: A detailed description of the service you need.
* **Location (9)**: The location where the service is needed (e.g., building, room number, area).
* **Date Service is Needed (10)**: The date when you need the service.
* **Preferred Service Time (11)**: Your preferred time for the service (optional).
* **Estimated Duration (12)**: The estimated duration of the service.
* **Urgency Level (13)**: The level of urgency for the service (e.g., emergency, urgent, standard, etc.).
* **Specific Requirements or Special Instructions (14)**: Any special considerations or instructions for the service.
* **Estimated Budget or Cost (15)**: The estimated budget or cost for the service (if applicable).
* **Budget Code or Account Number (16)**: A code or account number for billing purposes (if applicable).
* **Special Access or Key Requirements (17)**: Any special access or key requirements for the service.
* **Contact Name During Service (18)**: The name of the person to contact during the service (if applicable).
* **Contact Phone Number (19)**: The phone number of the person to contact during the service (if applicable).
* **Appropriate Approvals Obtained (20)**: Indicate if you have obtained the necessary approvals.
* **Supervisor or Manager Name (21)**: The name of the supervisor or manager authorizing this request.
* **Request Priority Number (22)**: A number indicating the priority level of the request (if applicable).
* **Additional Notes or Comments (23)**: Any additional information or comments about the service.
