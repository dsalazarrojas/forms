# staff-training-feedback-form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for staff to provide feedback on training sessions. It is used to collect feedback on the effectiveness of the training, areas for improvement, and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Click on the "Submit" button to access the form.
2.  Select the relevant fields to provide feedback for each question.
3.  Fill in the required fields marked with \*.
4.  Enter your answers or responses in the text fields.
5.  Select the options for the select_one field (feedback_question_9).
6.  Enter the date and time of the training session using the date and time fields.
7.  Click on the "Submit" button to submit your feedback.
