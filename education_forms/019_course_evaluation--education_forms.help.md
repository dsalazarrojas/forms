# Course Evaluation - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students about their experience with a course. Your responses will help instructors and course administrators evaluate the course's quality, effectiveness, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each page carefully and fill out the form in its entirety.
2. Select the relevant options for each field to reflect your experience with the course.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional comments" field.
