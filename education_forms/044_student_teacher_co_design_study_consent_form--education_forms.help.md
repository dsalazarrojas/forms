# student_teacher_co_design_study_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a study or project that a teacher is conducting with a student. It captures details such as student and teacher names, study duration, contact information, institution, location, start and end dates, description, and co-designer information. This form helps to track and record study projects for administrative and regulatory purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's name in the "student name" field.
2. Enter the teacher's name in the "teacher name" field.
3. Enter the study duration (if applicable) in the "study duration" field.
4. Provide your contact information in the "contact number", "email address" (if applicable), and "study institution" fields.
5. Enter the study location in the "study location" field.
6. Enter the study start and end dates in the "study start date" and "study end date" fields.
7. Briefly describe the study or project in the "study description" field.
8. Provide information about the co-designer in the "study co-designer", "study co-designer position", "study co-designer institution", "study co-designer institution title", "study co-designer email", "study co-designer phone", "study co-designer website", and "study co-designer social media" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Study Consent** (`1`, `text`, required/required): This field is a brief statement of the student's consent to participate in the study or project.
* **Student Name** (`2`, `text`, required/required): Enter the name of the student participating in the study or project.
* **Teacher Name** (`3`, `text`, required/required): Enter the name of the teacher conducting the study or project.
* **Study Duration** (`4`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the duration of the study or project. If not applicable, leave this field blank.
* **Contact Number** (`5`, `text`, required/required): Enter your contact information, including your phone number.
* **Email Address** (`6`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address. If not applicable, leave this field blank.
* **Study Institution** (`7`, `text`, required/required): Enter the name of the institution or organization associated with the study or project.
* **Study Location** (`8`, `text`, required/required): Enter the location where the study or project will take place.
* **Study Start Date** (`9`, `date`, required/required): Enter the start date of the study or project.
* **Study End Date** (`10`, `date`, required/required): Enter the end date of the study or project.
* **Study Description** (`11`, `text`, required/required): Briefly describe the study or project.
* **Study Co-Designer** (`12`, `text`, required/required): Enter information about the co-designer of the study or project.
* **Study Co-Designer Position** (`13`, `text`, required/required): Enter the position of the co-designer in the study or project.
* **Study Co-Designer Institution** (`14`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the institution or organization associated with the co-designer of the study or project. If not applicable, leave this field blank.
* **Study Co-Designer Institution Title** (`15`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the title of the institution or organization associated with the co-designer of the study or project. If not applicable, leave this field blank.
* **Study Co-Designer Email** (`16`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the co-designer. If not applicable, leave this field blank.
* **Study Co-Designer Phone** (`17`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the co-designer. If not applicable, leave this field blank.
* **Study Co-Designer Website** (`18`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the website of the co-designer. If not applicable, leave this field blank.
* **Study Co-Designer Social Media** (`19`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the social media of the co-designer. If not applicable, leave this field blank.

Note: Fields marked as "required" must be filled in for the form to be considered complete, while fields marked as "optional" can be left blank.
