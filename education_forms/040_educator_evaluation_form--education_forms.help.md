# Educator Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and ratings on educators' performance in the classroom and educational institution.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To get started, please provide the educator's name, their title or position, and any other relevant information about them.
2. Evaluate the educator's knowledge and application of teaching methods by providing a rating between 1 and 10.
3. Assess the educator's classroom management skills by giving a rating between 1 and 10.
4. Provide any additional comments or feedback about the educator in the "Comments" field, if applicable.
5. Rate the overall performance of the educator on a scale of 1 to 10.
6. Indicate whether or not you would recommend this educator to others, with a rating between 1 and 10.
7. For the "Rating" fields, provide a rating between 1 and 10 for each of the listed criteria.
