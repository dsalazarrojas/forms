# Membership Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Membership Nomination Form is used to collect information about graduating students and their nominators. This form helps to identify and recognize students who are graduating and have made significant contributions to a group or organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Make sure you are a valid nominator for the graduating student.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Answer the questions as they apply to the graduating student's circumstances.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Graduating student** (`graduating_student`, text, required): Enter the name of the graduating student.
* **Group or Organization** (`group_or_organization`, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether the graduating student is a member of a group or organization. Select all that apply.
* **Nomination Date** (`nomination_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the nomination.
* **Nomination Time** (`nomination_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the nomination.
* **Nominator** (`nominator`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if you are nominating yourself. If not, select 'No'.
* **Nominator Phone** (`nominator_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the nominator.
* **Nominator Email** (`nominator_email`, email, required): Enter the email of the nominator.
* **Nominee** (`nominee`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if the graduating student is a nominee.
* **Nominee Email** (`nominee_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the nominee.
* **Nomination Reason** (`nomination_reason`, text, required): Enter a brief reason for the nomination.
