# parent_and_teacher_meeting_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The parent_and_teacher_meeting_booking_form is used to book a meeting between a parent/guardian and a teacher for their child. This meeting can be used to discuss the child's progress, set goals, or address any concerns.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your name and contact information.
2. Provide guardian details, if applicable.
3. Enter your student's details, including their name and any relevant information.
4. Select an available time slot that suits you and your child's schedule.
5. Choose one or more teachers you would like to meet with.
6. Add any additional meeting details, such as the purpose of the meeting.
7. Submit the form to book the meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Details** (`Personal Details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name and contact information to allow us to get in touch with you regarding the meeting.
* **Guardian Details** (`Guardian Details`, `text`, required/optional): If you are not the primary contact person, enter the guardian's name and contact information.
* **Contact Details** (`Contact Details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your contact information, such as phone number and email, so we can reach you regarding the meeting.
* **Student Details** (`Student Details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your child's name and any relevant information to help us prepare for the meeting.
* **Available Slots** (`Available Slots`, `select_one`, required): Select a time slot that suits you and your child's schedule.
* **Select Teacher** (`Select Teacher`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose one or more teachers you would like to meet with.
* **Meeting Details** (`Meeting Details`, `text`, required/optional): Add any additional details about the meeting, such as its purpose.
* **Additional Details** (`Additional Details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any other information you think would be helpful for us to know before the meeting.
