# School Curriculum Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Curriculum Feedback Survey is designed to collect feedback from students, parents, and teachers on the effectiveness of a school's curriculum. This feedback is used to help the school administration and faculty refine and improve the curriculum to better meet the needs of all students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on each page to navigate to the next section.
2. Read each field explanation carefully to understand what is being asked.
3. Fill in the required fields with accurate and honest information.
4. Use the submission buttons to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (2): Please enter your full name as it appears on your student ID or identification.
* **Grade Level** (3): Select the level you are currently in (9, 10, 11, or 12).
* **Parent Name** (4): Please enter your parent/guardian's full name.
* **Teacher Name** (5): Please enter your teacher's full name.
* **Curriculum Effectiveness** (6): On a scale of 1 to 5, rate how effective you think the curriculum is in meeting your needs. A rating of 1 means you are not satisfied, while a rating of 5 means you are very satisfied.
* **What are your suggestions for improvement?** (7): Provide any specific suggestions you have for improving the curriculum. This is an optional field, but it will help the school administration and faculty make informed decisions.
* **Submit** (8): Select "Submit" to submit your feedback, or "Other" if you need to provide additional feedback that is not captured in the above fields.

## Tips

* Please be honest and thorough in your feedback, as it will help the school administration and faculty make informed decisions.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please contact the school administration or faculty member in charge of the curriculum.
* You can submit your feedback as many times as you want, but please avoid repetitive submissions. The system will automatically check for and prevent duplicate submissions.
