# University Sports Event Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Sports Event Incident Report Form is designed to help the university's sports department track and respond to incidents that occur during events. This form will help you provide necessary information for incident management and follow-up actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Event Details section with the date and location of the event where the incident occurred.
2. Provide a brief description of the incident in the Incident Description field.
3. Specify the location where the incident happened in the Incident Location field.
4. Choose the type of incident that occurred from the list of options.
5. Select the people involved in the incident, including students, staff, or spectators.
6. Indicate the severity of the incident and its outcome.
7. Describe the actions taken to address the incident in the Action Taken field.
8. Outline any follow-up actions planned or completed in the Follow Up Action field.
9. Identify who reported the incident and how they can be contacted for further information.
10. Add any additional follow-up contact information and notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details**: This section should be filled with the date and location of the sports event where the incident occurred.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event in the form `YYYY-MM-DD` format.
* **Event Location**: Provide the name of the event venue or location.
* **Incident Description**: Give a brief description of what happened during the event.
* **Incident Location**: Specify the exact location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Type**: Choose the type of incident from the options: Football, Soccer, Basketball, Volleyball, or Other.
* **Incident Involvement**: Select all the individuals involved in the incident, such as Student, Staff, Faculty, or Spectator.
* **Incident Severity**: Indicate the level of severity of the incident, such as Not Serious, Serious, or Critical.
* **Incident Outcome**: Choose the outcome of the incident, such as Not Investigated, Investigated, Closed, or Open.
* **Action Taken**: Describe the steps taken to address the incident.
* **Follow Up Action**: Outline any follow-up actions planned or completed.
* **Incident Reporter**: Identify who reported the incident and their contact information.
* **Follow Up Contact**: Add any additional contact information for follow-up purposes.
* **Follow Up Notes**: Include any additional notes or information that may be helpful for follow-up activities.

Tips

* Make sure to provide as much detail as possible in each field to help the university's sports department respond effectively to the incident.
* If an incident is serious or critical, please prioritize reporting it immediately to ensure prompt response and resolution.
* If you need to report multiple incidents, create a new form for each one.
