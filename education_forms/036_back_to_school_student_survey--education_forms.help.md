# Back To School Student Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Back To School Student Survey, simply follow these steps:

1.  Read each field's label and description carefully to understand what is required.
2.  Fill in the required fields: Your Full Name, Favorite Subject, What is most exciting about this year?, and Where do you learn best in the classroom?
3.  Choose from multiple options as needed, such as your favorite subjects, how you learn best, and where you feel comfortable in the classroom.
4.  Provide as much detail as you'd like in the text fields, such as hobbies, interests, and what you're excited or nervous about this year.
5.  Submit the form to share your thoughts with your teacher.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Your Full Name** (`student_full_name_survey`, text, required): This field is where you'll enter your full name, which is a mandatory field for us to know who's sharing their thoughts.
*   **Do you have a nickname you prefer?** (`student_nickname`, text, optional): Feel free to share a nickname you'd like to use in class or online.
*   **Favorite subject in school** (`favorite_subject_student`, select_one, required): Choose one subject that you particularly enjoy learning about or enjoy doing the most.
*   **Most challenging subject** (`least_favorite_subject`, select_one, optional): If there's one subject that's giving you a hard time, share it with us.
*   **How do you learn best?** (`learning_style_preference`, select_multiple, required): Select the top two ways you prefer to learn, such as watching a demonstration or working alone.
*   **Hobbies and interests outside of school** (`student_hobbies_interests`, text, required): Share with us what you enjoy doing when you're not in class.
*   **Most exciting thing about this year** (`excited_about_this_year`, text, required): Share what's got you most excited about this academic year.
*   **Anything you're nervous about?** (`nervous_about_this_year`, text, optional): If there's something that's got you nervous or worried, we're here to listen.
*   **Where do you feel you learn best in the classroom?** (`classroom_seating_preference`, select_one, required): Choose where you're most comfortable learning, such as at the front or near a window.
*   **One social goal for this year** (`student_social_goal`, text, optional): If you've got a goal to make friends or be more helpful, share it with us.
*   **How can your teacher help you be successful this year?** (`teacher_support_need`, text, optional): Share how you think your teacher could best support you in the coming year.

## Tips

*   Be honest and open in your answers, as your teacher values your feedback to create a positive and productive learning experience.
*   Don't worry if you're not sure about something – just do your best to share your thoughts and feelings.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to your teacher for clarification or support.
