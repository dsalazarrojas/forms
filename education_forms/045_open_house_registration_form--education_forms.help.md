# open_house_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The open house registration form is designed to collect information from visitors or attendees for an open house event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter your email address in the **Email** field. Please make sure to enter a valid email address.
3. Enter your contact number in the **Contact Number** field.
4. Enter the **Event Date** in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.
5. Enter the **Event Time** in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00).
6. Enter the **Event Location**.
7. Enter a brief description of the event in the **Event Description** field.
8. If applicable, enter the date and time you would like to be followed up in the **Follow Up** field.
9. If you have any additional notes or comments, please enter them in the **Additional Notes** field.
10. If you were referred to this event by someone, please enter their name in the **Referred By** field.
11. If you are attending the event for a specific purpose, please select your **Visitor Type** from the available options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address. This will be used to contact you regarding the event.
* **Contact Number** (text, required): Enter your contact number.
* **Event Date** (date, required): Enter the date of the event in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Event Time** (time, required): Enter the time of the event in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00).
* **Event Location** (text, required): Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Description** (text, required): Enter a brief description of the event.
* **Follow Up** (text, required): If you would like to be followed up, enter the date and time.
* **Visitor Interest** (text, optional): Enter any specific interests or reasons for attending the event.
* **Additional Notes** (text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Referred By** (text, optional): If you were referred to this event by someone, enter their name.
* **Visitor Type** (text, optional): Select your visitor type from the available options.
