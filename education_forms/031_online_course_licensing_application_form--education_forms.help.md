# Online Course Licensing Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users who wish to license their online courses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **User Information** (`user_info`, text, optional): This section is for you to provide your personal details.
    Fill in the requested information accurately.
2.  **License Application** (`license_application`, text, optional): Here, you can describe your license application.
    Provide as much detail as possible about your license request.
3.  **Course Information** (`course_info`, text, optional): In this section, enter information about the course you wish to license.
    Ensure that this section accurately reflects the details of your course.
4.  **Course License** (`course_license`, text, optional): This field is related to the license you are seeking.
    Enter any relevant information related to the license you are requesting.
5.  **Review Information** (`review_info`, text, optional): If applicable, fill out this section with any relevant review information about your course.
    This might include details about peer review processes, feedback, or any other relevant review activity.
6.  **Submit Information** (`submit_info`, text, optional): Here, you'll find space to enter any additional information that might be needed to process your license application.
    Use this space to clarify any part of your application that hasn't been covered in other sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Information**: Enter your contact details and any other identifying information that you're willing to share. 
*   **License Application**: Describe the type of license you are seeking. Be as specific as possible about what you want to achieve through this license.
*   **Course Information**: Provide details about your course, including the name of the course, the course content, the duration and format of the course, the intended audience, any prerequisites required for the course, and any other relevant details that you wish to share. Also, mention the level of difficulty of the course if applicable.
*   **Course License**: Enter details about any existing licenses you have for this course, any agreements that you're willing to accept with your potential license holders, and any other relevant licensing information. 
*   **Review Information**: If any peer review process was conducted for your course, describe it here. Also, mention the results of this review process. If this is not applicable to your course, simply mention so in this section.
*   **Submit Information**: In case we need any additional information to process your application for license, enter it here. This could be about any other relevant information, any other documentation that you have or anything else that you think might be useful in the evaluation of your application.

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately.
- Use clear and concise language when filling out this form.
- Be as specific as possible with your answers.
- If you're unsure about any part of this form, please contact us at form@gic.mx and we'll be happy to help.
