# math_student_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from math students who are seeking tutoring services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Math Student Details page, providing your name and any other relevant information.
2. On the Select A Math Course page, choose one or more math courses you are interested in.
3. On the Student Tutor Request page, you can add any additional notes about your tutoring needs.
4. On the Email page, enter your email address.
5. On the Phone page, enter your phone number.
6. On the Date of Birth page, select your date of birth.
7. On the Math Tutor Name page, enter your name, but note that this field is not required.
8. On the Math Tutor Availability page, select your preferred availability.
9. On the Math Tutor Qualifications page, you can add any additional information about your qualifications.
10. On the Math Tutor Rate page, enter your preferred rate for tutoring services.
11. On the Student Agreement page, read and agree to the terms of service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Math Student Details** (`math_student_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name and any other relevant information.
* **Select A Math Course** (`student_course_selection`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose one or more math courses you are interested in.
* **Student Tutor Request** (`student_tutor_request`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional notes about your tutoring needs.
* **Email** (`student_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`student_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`student_dob`, `date`, required/optional): Select your date of birth.
* **Math Tutor Name** (`math_tutor_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name, but note that this field is not required.
* **Math Tutor Availability** (`math_tutor_availability`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your preferred availability.
* **Math Tutor Qualifications** (`math_tutor_qualifications`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional information about your qualifications.
* **Math Tutor Rate** (`math_tutor_rate`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your preferred rate for tutoring services.
* **Student Agreement** (`student_agreement`, `note`, required/optional): Read and agree to the terms of service.
