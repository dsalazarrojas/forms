# teaching Demonstration Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from educators on the teaching demonstration. This form is used to evaluate the demonstration's effectiveness and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the instructor's name in the "teacher" field.
2. Provide a brief description of the performance in the "performance" field.
3. Evaluate the demonstration by selecting "Yes" or "No" for the "evaluation" field.
4. Enter the date of the demonstration in the "date" field (optional).
5. Enter the time of the demonstration in the "time" field (optional).
6. Enter the email of the instructor in the "email" field (optional).
7. Enter the phone number of the instructor in the "phone" field (optional).
8. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "comments" field (optional).
9. Select the assigned tool used during the demonstration in the "assigned Tool" field (optional).
10. Select the category of the form in the "category" field (required).
11. Enter any additional form IDs in the "form ids" field (required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher** (teacher, text, required):
	+ Enter the name of the instructor who conducted the demonstration.
* **Performance** (performance, text, required):
	+ Provide a brief description of the performance demonstrated during the teaching demonstration.
* **Evaluation** (evaluation, select multiple, required):
	+ Select "Yes" if the demonstration was effective, "No" if it was not.
* **Date** (date, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date the demonstration took place (if applicable).
* **Time** (time, time, optional):
	+ Enter the time the demonstration took place (if applicable).
* **Email** (email, email, optional):
	+ Enter the email address of the instructor (if applicable).
* **Phone** (phone, text, optional):
	+ Enter the phone number of the instructor (if applicable).
* **Comments** (comments, text, optional):
	+ Provide any additional comments or feedback on the demonstration.
* **Assigned Tool** (assigned Tool, select one, optional):
	+ Select the tool assigned to the instructor during the demonstration (if applicable).
* **Category** (category, select multiple, required):
	+ Select the category of the form, such as "Education Forms" or "Other".
* **Form IDs** (form ids, number, required):
	+ Enter any additional form IDs related to the demonstration.
