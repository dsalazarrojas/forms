> **Online Learning Accessibility Support Request Form - Help Guide**
=====================================================

**Purpose**
----------

This form is designed to help students and instructors request support for accessibility issues related to online courses. It gathers information to help our team understand and address the accessibility needs for each course.

**How To Complete This Form**
---------------------------

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields with the requested information.
2. Be as detailed as possible with your answers, especially in the "Description" field.
3. If you are submitting on behalf of another student, please make sure to include their Student ID or name.

**Field-by-Field Explanation**
---------------------------

* **Student Information** (Student ID, Name): Enter your student ID or name to help us track your request.
* **Student ID** (Student ID): Enter your student ID number to help us quickly identify you in our system.
* **Name**: Enter your name to help us address you personally.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to enable us to contact you back.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number to allow us to contact you via phone.
* **Course Name**: Enter the name of the course in which you are experiencing the accessibility issue.
* **Section**: Enter the name of the section of the course in which you are experiencing the accessibility issue.
* **Accessibility Issue**: Describe the accessibility issue you are experiencing in the course.
* **Instructor Name**: Enter the name of the instructor of your course.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date you submitted this form (not required, but helps us track the request).
* **Description**: Provide a detailed description of the accessibility issue you are experiencing.
* **Course Info** (Course Name, Instructor Name, Start Date, End Date, Location, Time Zone, Time Zone Abbreviation, Instructor Info, Learners Info, Additional Info, Support Request Info, Accommodations, Comments): Enter additional course information that might be relevant to your request, such as course name, instructor name, start date, end date, location, time zone, time zone abbreviation, instructor information, learners information, additional comments, etc.
* **Course Name** (Course Name): Enter the name of the course.
* **Instructor Name** (Instructor Name): Enter the name of the instructor.
* **Start Date** (Start Date): Enter the start date of the course.
* **End Date** (End Date): Enter the end date of the course.
* **Location** (Location): Enter the location of the course.
* **Time Zone** (Time Zone): Select the time zone of the course (Pacific, Mountain, Central, Eastern).
* **Time Zone Abbreviation** (Time Zone Abbreviation): Enter the time zone abbreviation.
* **Instructor Info** (Instructor Info): Enter additional instructor information.
* **Learners Info** (Learners Info): Enter information about other learners in the course.
* **Additional Info** (Additional Info): Enter additional information that might be relevant to your request.
* **Support Request Info** (Support Request Info): Enter any additional information about your support request.
* **Accommodations** (Accommodations): Enter any accommodations you require for the course.
* **Comments** (Comments): Enter any comments about your course or accessibility issue.
