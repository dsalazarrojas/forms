# Sunday School Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Sunday School Evaluation Form is used to gather feedback and comments from students, parents, and teachers about a student's performance in Sunday school.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields as needed. Each field is explained in detail below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Evaluation Form** (`evaluation_form`, text, optional): This field allows you to provide any additional comments or feedback about the student's performance.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter the student's name who is being evaluated.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): Enter the parent's name who has provided comments or feedback.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, optional): Enter the teacher's name who has provided comments or feedback.
* **Comments from Teacher** (`teacher_comments`, text, optional): This field is for comments or feedback from the teacher.
* **Comments from Parent** (`parent_comments`, text, optional): This field is for comments or feedback from the parent.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Form** (`evaluation_form`, text, optional): This field allows you to provide any additional comments or feedback about the student's performance.

## Tips
* Please complete the form as thoroughly as possible to provide a clear and accurate evaluation.
* If a field is not applicable, you can leave it blank.
* You can use this form for both regular and special events, such as holidays or theme days.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
