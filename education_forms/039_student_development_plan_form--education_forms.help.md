# Student Development Plan Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to track and support a student's progress in specific areas, particularly in their academic development. This form helps in setting and monitoring goals, providing feedback, and analyzing progress.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Enter Student ID**: This is a unique number assigned to each student. Please enter the correct `student_id` number.
2. **Enter Name**: Provide the student's name accurately.
3. **Select Grade Level**: Choose the student's current grade level from the options provided.
4. **Choose Subject Area**: Select the specific subject or area where the student is being monitored for development.
5. **Set School Year**: Select the academic year the student is currently in.
6. **Write Goals**: Clearly state the student's goals or objectives in the selected subject area.
7. **Add Comments**: If necessary, provide any additional comments or feedback about the student's progress.
8. **Choose Who Submitted**: Indicate who submitted the form - whether it's the student, a teacher, or a parent.
9. **Submit Date and Time**: Record the date and time the form was submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student ID (student_id)**: A unique number assigned to each student to track progress.
* **Name (name)**: Provide the student's accurate name.
* **Grade Level (grade_level)**: Choose the student's current grade level.
* **Subject Area (subject_area)**: Select the area of focus for the student's development.
* **School Year (school_year)**: Choose the student's current academic year.
* **Goals (goals)**: Write a clear and concise statement of the student's objectives.
* **Comments (comments)**: Provide any additional feedback or comments about the student's progress.
* **Submitted By (submitted_by)**: Choose who submitted the form - the student, teacher, or parent.
* **Submitted Date and Time (submitted_at)**: Record the date and time the form was submitted.

## Tips

- Ensure you fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
- Be specific and clear in writing the student's goals and progress.
- Double-check the grade level and subject area to ensure accuracy.
- Submit the form with the correct date and time for a clear record of progress.
