# Field Trip Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a school-sponsored Field Trip Consent Form used to inform and obtain consent from students and parents before taking students on field trips.

## How To Complete This Form

Before starting, please ensure that you have all required information and documents ready. You will need to complete the form for each student attending the field trip.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Student Name**
(Student Name) (`student_name`, text, required)
Enter the student's name.

### 2. **Parent Name**
(Parent Name) (`parent_name`, text, required)
Enter the parent's name or guardian's name.

### 3. **Parent Emergency Contact Info**
(Parent Emergency Contact Info) (`emergency_contact_info`, text, required)
Enter the parent's or guardian's emergency contact information, including phone number and address.

### 4. **Parent Email**
(Parent Email) (`parent_email`, email, optional)
Enter the parent's or guardian's email address. This field is not required but recommended for communication purposes.

### 5. **Parent Phone**
(Parent Phone) (`parent_phone`, text, optional)
Enter the parent's or guardian's phone number. This field is not required but recommended for communication purposes.

### 6. **Student Grade Level**
(Student Grade Level) (`student_grade_level`, select_one, required)
Select the student's grade level using the dropdown options provided.

### 7. **Student Date of Birth**
(Student Date of Birth) (`student_date_of_birth`, date, required)
Enter the student's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### 8. **Student Attendance**
(Student Attendance) (`student_attendance`, select_multiple, required)
Select the student's expected attendance status using the provided options.

### 9. **Student Medical Condition**
(Student Medical Condition) (`student_medical_condition`, text, optional)
Enter any relevant medical conditions or allergies that the school should be aware of.

### 10. **Student Medication**
(Student Medication) (`student_medication`, text, optional)
Enter any relevant student medication information.

### 11. **Trip Start Date**
(Trip Start Date) (`trip_start_date`, date, required)
Enter the date of the trip in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### 12. **Trip End Date**
(Trip End Date) (`trip_end_date`, date, required)
Enter the end date of the trip in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### 13. **Trip Start Time**
(Trip Start Time) (`trip_start_time`, time, required)
Enter the trip start time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.

### 14. **Trip End Time**
(Trip End Time) (`trip_end_time`, time, required)
Enter the trip end time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.

### 15. **School Name**
(School Name) (`school_name`, text, required)
Enter the name of the school.

### 16. **Trip Address**
(Trip Address) (`trip_address`, text, required)
Enter the address of the trip location.

### 17. **Trip Notes**
(Trip Notes) (`trip_notes`, note, optional)
Enter any additional trip notes or details.

### 18. **Student Agreement**
(Student Agreement) (`student_agreement`, select_multiple, required)
Select all the statements of understanding for the student. This includes the terms and conditions of the trip.

### 19. **Parent Agreement**
(Parent Agreement) (`parent_agreement`, select_multiple, required)
Select all the statements of understanding for the parent or guardian. This includes the terms and conditions of the trip.

### 20. **Student Signature**
(Student Signature) (`student_signature`, text, required)
Enter the student's signature.

### 21. **Parent Signature**
(Parent Signature) (`parent_signature`, text, required)
Enter the parent's or guardian's signature.

### 22. **School Signature**
(School Signature) (`school_signature`, text, required)
Enter the school's signature.

### 23. **School Signature Date**
(School Signature Date) (`school_signature_date`, date, required)
Enter the school's signature date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### 24. **Student DOB Verification**
(Student DOB Verification) (`student_dob_verification`, text, required)
Verify the student's date of birth to ensure accuracy.

### 25. **Trip Notes School Admin**
(Trip Notes School Admin) (`trip_notes_school_admin`, note, optional)
Enter any additional trip notes or details for school administrators.
