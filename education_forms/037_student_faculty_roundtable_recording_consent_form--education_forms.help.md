# Student Faculty Roundtable Recording Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a Student Faculty Roundtable Recording Consent Form, designed for students to provide their consent for recording during a faculty roundtable discussion. It helps students and faculty members to ensure that they are aware of and agree to the recording of their participation, promoting a safe and respectful environment for all participants. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields as accurately as possible. You will need to provide:
* Your student name
* Your email address (which will be used for follow-up communication)
* Your date of birth
* Your consent status (yes/no, with the option to be undecided)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your student ID or official documents.
* **Faculty Name** (`faculty_name`, text, optional): If you are participating in the roundtable, enter the name of your faculty advisor or department head.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): If you are under 18, please fill in your date of birth.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): This is your official student email address. Please ensure it's up-to-date and accurate.
* **Student Audio Recordings Consent** (`student_audio_recordings_consent`, note, optional): Briefly explain your consent status for recording your voice during the roundtable discussion. Please keep it concise and respectful.
* **Faculty Audio Recordings Consent** (`faculty_audio_recordings_consent`, note, optional): Similarly, brief your consent status for recording your voice during the roundtable discussion. Please keep it concise and respectful.
* **Recording Consent** (`recording_consent`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the three options: "Consent", "No Consent", or "Undecided". Please be clear and honest in your response.
* **Recording Purpose** (`recording_purpose`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the options: "Research", "Development", "Marketing", or "Other". Please be specific about why you're agreeing to be recorded.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): If you have any further thoughts or concerns, please share them here. This is your space to add any additional information.

## Tips
* Please fill out this form accurately and honestly.
* Be respectful of your peers and colleagues during the roundtable discussion.
* If you're unsure or uncomfortable with the recording, please let your faculty advisor or department head know.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to the contact (form@gic.mx).
