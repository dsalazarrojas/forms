# Game Dev Club Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Game Dev Club Registration Form is designed to gather information from users interested in joining the club. This information will help the club tailor its events and activities to cater to the users' needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, users should carefully fill out each field according to their personal information and preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Skills**: Select the skills you have from the provided list.
* **Select One Skill**: Choose one skill from the provided list.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Time**: Select your time zone.
* **Game Development Interests**: Indicate your interest in game development.
* **Comments**: Write any additional comments or information.
* **Game Development Experience**: Provide your game development experience.
* **References**: Mention any references or recommendations.
* **School Name**: Enter your school name.
* **Grade Level**: Enter your grade level.
* **How did you hear about us?**: Indicate how you heard about the club.
* **Additional Comments**: Write any additional comments or information.
* **Do you have any allergies or health issues that we should be aware of?**: Indicate if you have any allergies or health issues.
* **Is there anything else we should know?**: Provide any further information that the club should be aware of.
* **Do you have any special equipment or software you would like to bring to our club?**: Indicate if you have any special equipment or software you'd like to bring to the club.
* **Do you have any questions or concerns?**: Write any questions or concerns you may have.
* **What is your favorite game?**: Mention your favorite game.
* **What is your favorite game series?**: Indicate your favorite game series.
* **Favorite Game Genre**: Select your favorite game genre.
* **Do you have a favorite character?**: Mention your favorite character.
* **Do you like pizza?**: Indicate if you like pizza.
* **Do you have any siblings?**: Indicate if you have any siblings.
