# Molecules And Compounds Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information about your preferences and interests related to molecules and compounds. It's likely intended for educational or research purposes and should help us understand what you find engaging or interesting about these topics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply provide text responses to each field. There are no required fields, so feel free to answer at your own pace.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **What is your favorite molecule to learn about?** (`page_1`, `text`, optional): This field asks you to share a specific molecule that you find interesting or engaging. Feel free to type in the name of a molecule that piques your curiosity.
*   **Favorite compound** (`page_2`, `text`, optional): This field asks you to identify a compound that you find fascinating. Again, feel free to type in the name of a compound that interests you.
*   **What interests you most about compounds?** (`page_3`, `text`, optional): This field seeks to understand what aspect of compounds captivates you. Share your thoughts on what specifically draws you to compounds.
*   **What would you like to do next?** (`page_4`, `text`, optional): This field is an open-ended opportunity for you to share any additional thoughts or suggestions you might have.
