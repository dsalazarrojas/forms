# inclusive_curriculum_design_studio_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a registration form for the Inclusive Curriculum Design Studio, used to collect information from individuals interested in participating in this educational program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Fill in your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select any potential conflicts with emails in the "Email Conflict" field.
5. Fill in the date you are available in the "Date" field.
6. Fill in the time you are available in the "Time" field.
7. Enter any additional notes in the "Note" field.
8. Select your education level in the "Education Level" field.
9. Select your area of interest in the "Education Area" field.
10. Select any potential conflicts with education in the "Education Conflict" field.
11. Select multiple education programs you are interested in, if applicable, in the "Education 5" field.
12. Fill in your number of years of experience for each education field, from "Education 10" to "Education 20".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (Text)**: Fill in your full name.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (Text)**: Fill in your phone number.
* **Email Conflict (Select One)**: Select any potential conflicts with emails.
* **Date (Date)**: Fill in the date you are available.
* **Time (Time)**: Fill in the time you are available.
* **Note (Note)**: Enter any additional notes.
* **Education Level (Select One)**: Select your education level (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Education Area (Select One)**: Select your area of interest (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Education Conflict (Select One)**: Select any potential conflicts with education.
* **Education 5 (Select Multiple)**: Select multiple education programs you are interested in, if applicable.
* **Education 10-20 (Number)**: Fill in your number of years of experience for each education field.
