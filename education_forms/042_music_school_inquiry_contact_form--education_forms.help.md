# Music School Inquiry Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool to gather information from students interested in music schools. Please use it to provide your details and the music school's information you are interested in learning more about.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your personal information, including your name and phone number.
2. Select the music school you are interested in from the dropdown list.
3. Choose the type of lesson you would like to take.
4. Specify the frequency of lessons you would like to have (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, monthly).
5. Provide a schedule and time for the lesson.
6. Enter any additional message you would like to include in your inquiry.
7. Enter the Music School ID (a unique identifier for the music school).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): This is your name, which we will use to address you in our communication.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number, so we can contact you with updates and questions.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where we can reach you.
* **Music School** (`music_school`, select_one, required): Select the music school you are interested in. Please choose one of the options provided.
* **Instrument** (`instrument`, select_one, required): Select the instrument you would like to learn.
* **Lesson Type** (`lesson_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of lesson you are interested in taking (e.g., lessons, music theory, music performance).
* **Lesson Frequency** (`lesson_frequency`, select_multiple, required): Select the frequency of lessons you would like to have (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, monthly).
* **Schedule** (`schedule`, date, required): Enter a specific date and time for your lesson.
* **Lesson Time** (`lesson_time`, time, required): Enter the time you would like to have your lesson.
* **Message** (`message`, note, required): Write any additional message you would like to include in your inquiry.
* **School ID** (`music_school_id`, number, required): Enter the Music School ID provided to you by the music school.
* **School Name** (`music_school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the music school you are interested in.
* **School Address** (`music_school_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the music school.
* **School Phone** (`music_school_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the music school.
* **School E-mail** (`music_school_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the music school.
* **School Hours** (`music_school_hours`, select_multiple, required): Select the days the music school is open (e.g., Monday to Sunday).
* **School Website** (`music_school_website`, text, required): Enter the website of the music school.
* **School Latitude** (`music_school_latitude`, number, required): Enter the latitude of the music school location.
* **School Longitude** (`music_school_longitude`, number, required): Enter the longitude of the music school location.
* **School Rating** (`school_rating`, text, required): Enter a rating or review of the music school.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Make sure to select the correct music school and instrument you are interested in.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [insert contact information].
