# Secondary School Teaching Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Secondary School Teaching Job Application Form is a streamlined way for potential teachers to submit their applications for a teaching position at our school. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth application process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your name, date of birth, and contact information.
2. Provide information about your teaching experience, education background, employment history, and qualifications.
3. Upload any required documents, such as references or certifications.
4. Verify your email address and phone number to ensure we can contact you.
5. Select a date and time for your application submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Details**: This section requires your name, date of birth, and contact information. Please fill in your accurate and up-to-date information.
* **Contact Information**: Provide your email address and phone number to ensure we can contact you regarding your application.
* **Teaching Experience**: Describe your teaching experience, including the school you worked at, your role, and the subjects you taught.
* **Education Background**: List your educational qualifications, including degrees earned and institutions attended.
* **Employment History**: Provide information about your previous work experience, including job titles and dates of employment.
* **References**: Upload references or certifications that support your application.
* **Qualifications**: List any relevant qualifications or certifications you hold.
* **Uploaded Documents**: Upload any required documents, such as transcripts or diplomas.
* **Email**: Select your email address from the dropdown list.
* **Phone**: Select your phone number from the dropdown list.
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Date Of Application**: Enter the date you submit your application in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Time**: Enter the time of your application submission.
* **Note**: This section is for any additional information you want to provide about your application.
* **Submitted By**: This field is automatically populated with the HR staff member who received your application.

## Tips

* Please ensure all fields are filled out completely and accurately.
* Upload any required documents, such as references or certifications.
* Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
* Enter your date of birth and application submission date in the correct format.
