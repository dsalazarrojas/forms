# Faculty Research Collaboration Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Faculty Research Collaboration Application Form is designed to gather information about research collaborations between faculty members and other researchers. The form is used to facilitate collaboration and resource sharing between faculty members and to track research activities and progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all the necessary information ready before filling out the form.
* Start by filling out your university and researcher information (fields 1-4).
* Provide a brief description of your research title, area, and start and end dates (fields 5-8).
* Specify whether you are collaborating with others and who they are (fields 9-10).
* Enter your budget and research status (fields 11-12).
* Add any additional notes or comments about your research (field 13).
* Provide your contact information (fields 14-15).
* If applicable, enter your research supervisor's information (fields 16-18).
* Finally, review your form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **University** (1): Enter your university name.
- **Researcher** (2): Enter your name as the researcher.
- **Research Area** (3): Briefly describe the area of your research.
- **Research Title** (4): Enter a concise title for your research project.
- **Research Description** (5): Provide a brief description of your research project.
- **Collaboration Type** (6): Indicate whether you are collaborating with others (Yes/No).
- **Collaborators** (7): List the names of your collaborators (Yes/No).
- **Budget** (8): Enter your research budget.
- **Research Start Date** (9): Enter the start date of your research project.
- **Research End Date** (10): Enter the end date of your research project.
- **Research Status** (11): Indicate the status of your research project (Active/Inactive).
- **Research Notes** (12): Add any additional notes or comments about your research.
- **Email** (13): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (14): Enter your phone number.
- **Researcher Email** (15): Enter your research email address.
- **Researcher Phone** (16): Enter your research phone number.
- **University Website** (17): Enter your university's website URL.
- **Research Collaboration Website** (18): Enter the website of your research collaboration (if applicable).
- **Research Institution** (19): Enter the name of your research institution.
- **Department** (20): Enter the name of the department where your research is conducted.
- **Faculty** (21): Indicate whether you are a faculty member (Yes/No).
- **Research Area Department** (22): Enter the name of the department of your research area.
- **Research Supervisor** (23): Enter the name of your research supervisor.
- **Research Supervisor Phone** (24): Enter your research supervisor's phone number.
- **Research Supervisor Email** (25): Enter your research supervisor's email address.
