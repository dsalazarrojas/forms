# online_exam_proctoring_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from proctors who have conducted online exams. Your input will help us refine the proctoring process and ensure that exams are conducted fairly and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the "What was the title of the exam?" field and enter the title of the exam you proctored.
2. In the "What was your proctoring experience like?" field, share your honest feedback about your experience proctoring the exam.
3. If you had a proctor for the exam, enter their name in the "Proctor name" field.
4. For "Did you feel proctors were adequately instrumentality?", select one of the options ("Yes", "No", or "Maybe") that best reflects your experience.
5. For "Did you feel the proctoring was fair?", select one of the options ("Yes", "No", or "Maybe") that best reflects your experience.
6. In the "How effective was the proctoring?" field, select all options that apply to your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was the title of the exam? (exam_title, text, required: false)**: Enter the title of the exam you proctored. This helps us keep track of the exam's title and ensures we can match your feedback with the correct exam.
* **What was your proctoring experience like? (exam_proctoring_experience, text, required: false)**: Share your thoughts about your experience proctoring the exam. Your feedback is invaluable in helping us refine the proctoring process.
* **Proctor name (exam_proctoring_prophet, text, required: false)**: If you had a proctor for the exam, please enter their name. This will help us follow up with them and gather more detailed feedback.
* **Did you feel proctors were adequately instrumentality? (exam_proctoring_instrumentality, select_multiple, required: false)**: Select "Yes" if you felt the proctors were adequately instrumentality, "No" if you did not, or "Maybe" if you're unsure.
* **Did you feel the proctoring was fair? (exam_proctoring_fairness, select_one, required: false)**: Select "Yes" if you felt the proctoring was fair, "No" if you did not, or "Maybe" if you're unsure.
* **How effective was the proctoring? (exam_proctoring_effectiveness, select_multiple, required: false)**: Select all options that apply to your experience, such as "Yes" if the proctoring was effective, or "No" if it was not.
