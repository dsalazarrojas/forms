# Tutorial Student Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register students in a tutorial setting, collecting relevant information for administrative or educational purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's **Student Name** in the first field.
2. Provide the student's **Student Email** in the correct format (e.g., student@example.com).
3. Enter the student's **Student Phone** number.
4. Select one of the options in the **Course ID** field, indicating whether the student is registered for the course.
5. Select multiple options in the **Class ID** field, indicating which classes the student is registered for.
6. Select multiple options in the **Teacher ID** field, indicating which teachers the student is registered with.
7. Enter the **Study Date** in the required format (yyyy-mm-dd).
8. Select the **Study Time** for the student's tutorial sessions.
9. Provide any additional **Note** about the student's tutorial registration (optional).
10. Confirm the student's registration by selecting **Yes** or **No**.
11. Enter the student's unique **Student ID** number.
12. Enter the **Course Name** the student is registered for.
13. Enter the **Class Name** the student is registered in.
14. Enter the **Teacher Name** the student is registered with.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter the student's full name.
* **Student Email**: Enter the student's email address in the correct format.
* **Student Phone**: Enter the student's phone number.
* **Course ID**: Select whether the student is registered for the course.
* **Class ID**: Select multiple classes the student is registered for.
* **Teacher ID**: Select multiple teachers the student is registered with.
* **Study Date**: Enter the date the student will study.
* **Study Time**: Enter the time the student will study.
* **Note**: Enter any additional information about the student's registration.
* **Confirm**: Confirm the student's registration status.
* **Student ID**: Enter the student's unique ID number.
* **Course Name**: Enter the course the student is registered for.
* **Class Name**: Enter the class the student is registered in.
* **Teacher Name**: Enter the teacher the student is registered with.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the student's contact details are accurate.
* Make sure to register the student for the correct course, class, and teacher.
* Review the student's registration information before confirming.
* Double-check the student's study date and time to ensure they match with the schedule.
