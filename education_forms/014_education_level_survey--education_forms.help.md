# education_level_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an individual's educational background and experiences. It's a survey that aims to gather data on various aspects of education, including educational levels, institutions, languages, and training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in your educational background information, including your current level of education and student age group.
2. Select your current level of education from the list provided (Elementary, Secondary, Tertiary).
3. If you don't see your current level listed, you can provide a custom answer in the "current_level_other" field.
4. Choose your student age group from the available options (18-20, 21-24, 25-29, 30+).
5. If you don't see your student age group listed, you can provide a custom answer in the "student_age_group_other" field.
6. Select your highest level of education attained from the list provided (High school diploma, Some college, College degree, Associate degree, Bachelor degree, Master degree, Doctorate degree).
7. You can select multiple highest degree levels if applicable.
8. Choose the institution where you received your highest level of education from the list provided (Public School, Private School, Online School).
9. If you didn't receive your highest level of education from one of the listed institutions, you can provide a custom answer in the "educational_institution_other" field.
10. Select your level of teacher training from the list provided (Basic training, Intermediate training, Advanced training, Other training).
11. You can select multiple training levels if applicable.
12. Choose the language of education you are most familiar with from the list provided (English, Spanish, Chinese, Japanese, Other language).
13. Finally, review your answers and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Educational Background**: This field is for providing a brief description of your educational background.
	+ (no options)
- **Current Level**: This field is for selecting your current level of education.
	+ Label: Current Level, Type: select_one, Required: false
	+ Options: Elementary, Secondary, Tertiary
	+ Example: If you're currently in Elementary school, select "Elementary".
