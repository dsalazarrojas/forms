# Student Performance Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to assess the performance of students. It is designed to collect relevant information about students, including their first name, last name, student ID, date of birth, current grade, performance level, subject area, email, phone number, notes, assessment comments, and submitted by. The form's purpose is to provide an evaluation system for educators and administrators to evaluate students' progress and provide necessary feedback.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter the student's last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter the student's ID number in the "Student ID" field.
4. Enter the student's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
5. Enter the student's current grade in the "Current Grade" field.
6. Enter the assessment date in the "Assessment Date" field.
7. Select the student's performance level from the drop-down menu in the "Performance Level" field.
8. Select all relevant subject areas from the drop-down menu in the "Subject Area" field.
9. Enter the student's email address in the "Email" field.
10. Enter the student's phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
11. Provide any additional comments or notes about the student in the "Notes" field.
12. Enter any assessment comments in the "Assessment Comments" field.
13. Enter the name of the person submitting the assessment in the "Submitted By" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter the student's first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter the student's last name.
* **Student ID**: Enter the student's unique identifier.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the student's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Current Grade**: Enter the student's current grade.
* **Assessment Date**: Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Performance Level**: Select the student's performance level, which can be 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5.
* **Subject Area**: Select all relevant subject areas, which can be Math, Science, English, Social Studies, or Physical Education.
* **Email**: Enter the student's email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the student's phone number.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the student.
* **Assessment Comments**: Enter any assessment comments about the student.
* **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person submitting the assessment.
