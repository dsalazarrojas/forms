# Academic Learning Design Accelerator Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Learning Design Accelerator Application Form is a crucial step in the application process for the Academic Learning Design Accelerator program. It allows applicants to provide essential information about themselves and their project goals, enabling the program team to review and match them with suitable projects and resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your full name in the "Full name" field (id: 2). This field is required and should be filled out with your correct name.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email address" field (id: 3). This field is also required and should be a valid email address that you check regularly.
3. If applicable, provide your institution or department in the "Institution or department" field (id: 4).
4. In the "Project focus area" field (id: 5), describe the topic or course area you are interested in designing for.
5. Outline your objectives for the accelerator program in the "Objectives for accelerator" field (id: 6). Be specific about what you aim to achieve through the program.
6. If you have any available time commitment during the accelerator, indicate it in the "Time commitment available" field (id: 7).
7. If you have prior instructional design experience, describe it in the "Prior instructional design experience" field (id: 8).
8. If you are working with a team, provide their names and roles in the "Team members involved" field (id: 9).
9. If you require any additional support, such as mentoring or resources, specify it in the "Additional support requested" field (id: 10).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name (id: 2)**: This field is required to collect your full name.
* **Email address (id: 3)**: This field is required and should be a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Institution or department (id: 4)**: If you are affiliated with an institution or department, provide the name here.
* **Project focus area (id: 5)**: Describe the topic or course area you are interested in designing for.
* **Objectives for accelerator (id: 6)**: Be specific about what you aim to achieve through the program.
* **Time commitment available (id: 7)**: If you have any available time commitment during the accelerator, indicate it here.
* **Prior instructional design experience (id: 8)**: Describe any relevant experience you have in instructional design.
* **Team members involved (id: 9)**: Provide the names and roles of your collaborators if applicable.
* **Additional support requested (id: 10)**: Specify any additional support you need, such as mentoring or resources.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form to ensure a successful match with the program.
* Proofread your responses carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the program team for assistance.
* Make sure to submit the form by the designated deadline.
