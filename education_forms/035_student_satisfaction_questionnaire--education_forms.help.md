# Student Satisfaction Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Satisfaction Questionnaire is designed to collect feedback from students about their experience with their instructor and the overall satisfaction with their program. This form helps instructors and administrators identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions to enhance the learning experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully to understand its purpose and context.
2. Answer each question accurately and honestly.
3. If you have any comments or suggestions, use the text fields to provide detailed feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Question 1: how_would_you_rate_your_overall_satisfaction (id: 2)
* **How would you rate your overall satisfaction?**: This question asks for your general satisfaction rating with your experience, with options ranging from 1 (very dissatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).

### Question 2: what_are_the_areas_where_your_instructor_made_a_positive_impact (id: 3)
* **What are the areas where your instructor made a positive impact?**: Choose one or more options that reflect the ways your instructor had a positive influence on you, such as "instructor quality of teaching," "feedback," or "enthusiasm."

### Question 3: how_would_you_rate_your_experience (id: 4)
* **How would you rate your experience?**: This question is similar to the first question, asking for your general satisfaction rating with your experience, with options ranging from 1 (very dissatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).

### Question 4: comments (id: 5)
* **Comments**: Use this text field to provide any additional comments or suggestions about your experience.

### Question 5: do_you_have_any_comments_or_suggestions (id: 6)
* **Do you have any comments or suggestions?**: If you have any thoughts or feedback you'd like to share, use this text field to provide them.

## Tips
* Be accurate and honest in your responses.
* Take your time to carefully consider each question before answering.
* Don't forget to use the text fields to provide additional comments and suggestions!
