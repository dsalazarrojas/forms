# Worms Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Worms Knowledge Quiz" form is a tool used to gauge users' knowledge on a specific subject matter, likely related to worms. It is designed to be a multiple-choice quiz with sections that cover different topics. The form is meant to be completed by users who wish to assess their understanding of the subject.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Start" button to begin the quiz.
2. Read each question carefully and select the most accurate response from the options provided.
3. For questions requiring multiple selections, choose all relevant answers.
4. Once you have completed all questions in a section, click on the "Next" button to proceed to the next section.
5. After completing all sections, click on the "Submit" button to finalize your answers.
