# Student Of Concern Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture information about a student who has concerns or needs, providing teachers and educators with the necessary details to support the student in their care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's details on page 1, including any relevant information about the student.
2. Provide the parent's contact information on page 2.
3. Add any comments or concerns from the teacher on page 3.
4. Select any support services that the student may need on page 4.
5. Sign and date the form on page 5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Student Details
* **Student Details** (student_of_concern_form_student_details, text, required): This field is for the student's name, date of birth, and any other identifying information.
### Page 2: Parent Contact
* **Parent Contact** (parent_contact_info, text, required): This field is for the parent's name, phone number, and email address.
### Page 3: Teacher Comments
* **Teacher Comments** (teacher_comments, text, optional): This field is for the teacher to add any comments or concerns about the student.
### Page 4: Teacher Concerns
* **Teacher Concerns** (teacher_concerns, text, optional): This field is for the teacher to identify any specific concerns or issues with the student.
### Page 5: Support Services
* **Support Services** (student_of_concern_form_support_services, select_multiple, optional): This field allows you to select any support services that the student may need, such as counseling or academic support.
### Page 5: Teacher Signature
* **Teacher Signature** (teacher_signature, text, required): This field is for the teacher to sign and date the form, indicating that they have reviewed and understood the information provided.
