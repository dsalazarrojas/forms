# Geometry Surface Area Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Geometry Surface Area Quiz is a tool to help users estimate the surface area of various three-dimensional objects using mathematical formulas. This form is designed to guide students through calculations for a cube, square, sphere, and cylinder.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the instructions for each page carefully and fill in the required fields.
2. On **Surface Area Formula**, enter the formula relevant to your selected object.
3. If you chose to estimate the surface area of a cube or square, fill in the **Cube** or **Square** fields with the corresponding dimensions.
4. For a sphere, enter the radius in **Radius**, which will be used to calculate the surface area.
5. If you chose the cylinder option, fill in **Radius** and **Height** fields.
6. Finally, press "Submit" to see your calculated surface area in the **Surface Area** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Surface Area Formula**: This field is where you'll enter the formula for the selected object. For example, if you choose to calculate the surface area of a cube, you'll need to enter the formula for the surface area of a cube.
* **Cube**: If you've selected the cube option, fill in the length of the cube's side.
* **Square**: If you've selected the square option, fill in the length of the square's side.
* **Cylinder**: This field is not used directly in the calculation. It's likely intended for selecting the cylinder option from the previous field.
* **Radius**: This field is used to calculate the surface area of a sphere and a cylinder. Enter the radius of the sphere or the cylinder's radius for the calculation.
* **Height**: This field is used to calculate the surface area of a cylinder. Enter the height of the cylinder for the calculation.
* **Surface Area**: This field displays the calculated surface area of your selected object.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in the correct formulas and dimensions for each object.
* Use the correct units for your measurements (e.g., meters, feet, etc.).
* Double-check your calculations before submitting.
