# pbe_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pbe_feedback_survey form is designed to collect feedback from students and teachers about a course, providing valuable insights to improve teaching methods, student engagement, and overall course performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each section carefully and answer questions based on your experiences with the course.
2. Answer all questions to the best of your ability, using the options provided in each section.
3. If an option does not apply to your experiences, select "None" or "Not Applicable".
4. Double-check your answers to ensure you've completed all required fields (marked with an asterisk \*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **introduction** (1) (text, optional): This section is an introduction to the form, where you can provide any general comments or feedback about the course.
* **teacher_performance** (2) (number, optional): Rate the teacher's performance, with options ranging from "High" to "Low".
* **student_attendance** (3) (number, optional): Rate the student's attendance, with options ranging from "High" to "Low".
* **teacher_feedback** (4) (text, optional): Provide specific feedback about the teacher's approach, teaching style, or communication.
* **student_performance** (5) (number, optional): Rate the student's performance, with options ranging from "High" to "Low".
* **teacher_support** (6) (select multiple, optional): Select all ways in which the teacher provided support, with options ranging from "High" to "Low".
* **teacher_directions** (7) (select one, optional): Rate how well the teacher provided clear instructions and directions, with options ranging from "None" to "Good" to "Fair" to "Poor".
* **student_motivation** (8) (select multiple, optional): Select all ways in which students were motivated, with options ranging from "High" to "Low".
* **teacher_classwork** (9) (select one, optional): Rate how well the teacher managed classroom work, with options ranging from "None" to "Good" to "Fair" to "Poor".
* **teacher_clarity** (10) (select multiple, optional): Select all ways in which the teacher provided clear communication, with options ranging from "High" to "Low".
* **student_clarity** (11) (select multiple, optional): Select all ways in which students understood the material, with options ranging from "High" to "Low".
* **teacher_effectiveness** (12) (select multiple, optional): Select all ways in which the teacher was effective, with options ranging from "High" to "Low".
* **teacher_presentation** (13) (select one, optional): Rate how well the teacher presented the material, with options ranging from "None" to "Good" to "Fair" to "Poor".
* **teacher_feedback_frequency** (14) (select multiple, optional): Select how often the teacher provided feedback, with options ranging from "High" to "Low".
* **student_feedback_frequency** (15) (select multiple, optional): Select how often students provided feedback, with options ranging from "High" to "Low".
* **teacher_time_management** (16) (select one, optional): Rate how well the teacher managed time, with options ranging from "None" to "Good" to "Fair" to "Poor".
* **student_time_management** (17) (select one, optional): Rate how well students managed time, with options ranging from "None" to "Good" to "Fair" to "Poor".

By following this guide, you'll be able to complete the pbe_feedback_survey form effectively and provide valuable insights to instructors and course administrators.
