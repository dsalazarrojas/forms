# Student Ministry Event Medical Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a part of the student ministry process and is required for student participation in events. It ensures that students' health and safety are taken into consideration while they engage with their peers and staff in ministry events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Fill in the Event Details**: Provide a brief description of the event you are participating in, including the event name, date, time, location, and duration.
2. **Provide Student Information**: Enter your name, age, and any other relevant student details as requested.
3. **Share Medical Details**: List any medical conditions or allergies you have that the event organizers should be aware of.
4. **Confirm Medical Release**: Confirm if you are willing to participate in the event and grant permission for the staff to take medical actions on your behalf.
5. **List Medications**: If you take any medications, list them in the designated field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Event Details**: This field is where you need to provide a brief description of the event you will be attending. Please include the event name, date, time, location, and duration to help ensure your safety and the safety of others.
  • **Student**: This field is where you need to enter your name, age, and any other relevant student information as requested. This helps us get a better understanding of your needs and ensure your participation in the event.
  • **Medical Details**: This field is where you need to list any medical conditions or allergies you have that the event staff should be aware of. This will help us take necessary precautions and ensure your safety during the event.
  • **Medical Release**: This field is where you need to confirm whether you are willing to participate in the event and grant permission for the staff to take medical actions on your behalf. If you choose 'Yes', you grant permission for medical staff to take care of you in case of any emergency. If you choose 'No', please provide an explanation.
  • **Allergies**: This field is where you need to list any allergies you may have. This will help us ensure that you stay safe during the event and avoid any potential allergies complications.
  • **Medical Conditions**: This field is similar to **Allergies**, where you need to list any medical conditions you may have that could affect your participation in the event.
  • **Medications**: If you take any medications, list them here so that we can be aware of your health needs during the event.
 
## Tips
- Please ensure that you accurately fill out this form to avoid any potential medical risks.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask your event organizers or staff for clarification.
- This form is for your safety and well-being, so please take it seriously and complete it thoroughly.
