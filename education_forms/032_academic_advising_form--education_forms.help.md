# Academic Advising Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Advising Form is used to collect information from students for academic advising purposes. This form helps advisors understand student progress and provide guidance on their academic path.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the student's information, including name, ID number, and email address.
2. Select the student's major or program of study.
3. Indicate the current academic year.
4. Enter the academic advisor's name.
5. Choose the date of the advising meeting.
6. Enter the student's current GPA and total credits completed.
7. Provide the estimated number of credits remaining to graduate (if applicable).
8. Enter the expected graduation date (if applicable).
9. Select all topics discussed during the meeting from the list of options.
10. List courses planned for the next semester (if applicable).
11. Enter any action items or next steps for the student.
12. Indicate if a follow-up meeting is needed and choose a suggested follow-up date (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student full name**: Enter the student's full name as it appears on their student ID card or transcript.
* **Student ID number**: Enter the student's unique ID number.
* **Student email**: Enter the student's university email address.
* **Major or program of study**: Select the student's major or program of study from the list of options.
* **Current academic year**: Select the student's current academic year from the list of options.
* **Academic advisor name**: Enter the name of the student's academic advisor.
* **Date of advising meeting**: Enter the date of the advising meeting.
* **Current cumulative GPA**: Enter the student's current cumulative GPA.
* **Total credits completed**: Enter the total number of credits the student has completed.
* **Estimated credits remaining to graduate**: Enter the estimated number of credits the student has remaining to graduate (if applicable).
* **Expected graduation date**: Enter the date the student expects to graduate (if applicable).
* **Topics discussed**: Select all topics that were discussed during the meeting from the list of options.
* **Courses planned for next semester**: List the courses the student plans to take for the next semester.
* **Action items or next steps**: Enter any action items or next steps for the student.
* **Is a follow-up meeting needed**: Select yes if a follow-up meeting is needed and choose a suggested follow-up date (if applicable).
