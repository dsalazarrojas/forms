# Biblical Studies Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess a participant's knowledge, engagement, and growth in their biblical studies. It provides valuable feedback for educators, instructors, or facilitators to understand the participant's strengths and weaknesses.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields in each section, including name, email, program/class name, assessment date, and future goals.
2. Answer the questions in the "Scripture Knowledge" section, providing the correct numbers of books in the New Testament and gospels.
3. Indicate your level of understanding and application of scriptural concepts in the "Comprehension and Application" section.
4. Answer questions about your study habits, engagement, and growth in the "Engagement and Study Habits" and "Growth and Development" sections.
5. In the "Future Learning Goals" section, type in your future goals and plans for your biblical studies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, `required/True`): Fill in your full name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, `required/True`): Enter your email address for results delivery.
* **Program or Class Name** (`program_name`, `text`, `required/True`): Type in the name of the program or class you are currently in.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, `date`, `required/True`): Fill in the date you are taking this assessment.
* **How Many Books Are in the New Testament** (`testament_books`, `number`, `required/True`): Answer with the number of books in the New Testament.
* **How Many Gospels Are in the Bible** (`gospels_count`, `number`, `required/True`): Answer with the number of gospels in the Bible.
* **How Many Letters Did Apostle Paul Write** (`paul_letters`, `number`, `required/True`): Answer with the approximate number of letters written by Apostle Paul.
* **How Many Main Disciples Did Jesus Call** (`jesus_disciples`, `number`, `required/True`): Answer with the number of main disciples Jesus called.
* **Your Scripture Comprehension Level** (`comprehension_level`, `select_one`, `required/True`): Choose the level of your understanding of scripture from the options provided.
* **Ability to Apply Scripture to Life** (`application_ability`, `select_one`, `required/True`): Select the level of your ability to apply scripture to life from the options provided.
* **Your Overall Engagement Level** (`engagement_level`, `select_one`, `required/True`): Choose the level of your overall engagement in studies from the options provided.
* **How Often Do You Study** (`study_frequency`, `select_one`, `required/True`): Answer with the frequency of your scripture study from the options provided.
* **Average Time Spent in Study per Week** (`study_time`, `number`, `required/True`): Fill in the average time spent in scripture study per week.
* **Primary Scripture Study Method** (`study_method`, `select_one`, `required/True`): Choose the primary method of your scripture study from the options provided.
* **Overall Engagement Level** (`engagement_level`, `select_one`, `required/True`): Select the level of your overall engagement in studies from the options provided.
* **Perceived Spiritual Growth from Studies** (`spiritual_growth`, `select_one`, `required/True`): Choose the level of your perceived spiritual growth from studies from the options provided.
* **Has Your Faith Been Strengthened Through Learning** (`faith_strengthening`, `select_one`, `required/True`): Select the level of faith strengthening through learning from the options provided.
* **Major Things You've Learned** (`key_learnings`, `text`, `required/True`): Type in the major things you've learned from your studies.
* **Challenges in Scripture Study** (`study_challenges`, `text`, `required/False`): Describe any challenges you face in scripture study.
* **Support or Resources Needed** (`support_needed`, `text`, `required/False`): Type in any support or resources you need for better learning.
* **Most Difficult Topics or Passages** (`difficult_topics`, `text`, `required/False`): Identify the most challenging topics or passages in your studies.
* **Future Learning Goals** (`future_goals`, `text`, `required/True`): Type in your future learning goals and plans for your biblical studies.
* **Additional Comments or Feedback** (`additional_comments`, `text`, `required/False`): Provide any additional comments or feedback.
