# Student Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from students on their satisfaction with a particular program, course, or service. This will help us understand their experiences, identify areas of improvement, and make informed decisions for future development and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please take a few minutes to answer the questions honestly, and make sure to provide as much detail as possible.
2. The form is divided into several sections, each with a brief explanation. Please read each section carefully before providing your feedback.
3. If you have any suggestions or comments, please use the "Suggestion-Answer" field to provide them.
4. Make sure to fill in your email address if you wish to receive a copy of your feedback.
5. Click the "Submit" button to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Page 1): This is a brief introduction to the form. Please read it carefully and let us know if you have any questions or need clarification.
* **Rating Question** (Page 2): This is a question where you can rate your overall satisfaction with the program, course, or service on a scale of 1-5.
* **Suggestion Question** (Page 3): This is a question where you can suggest any improvements or changes that you would like to see in the program, course, or service.
	+ **Suggestion-Answer** (Page 4): This is where you provide the answer to the suggestion question. Please choose one of the suggested answers or provide your own.
* **Comments** (Page 5): This is a free-text field where you can provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Email** (Page 6): If you would like to receive a copy of your feedback, please fill in your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (Page 7): This is a field where you can provide your contact phone number.
* **Submit** (Page 8): This is the final step to submit your feedback.

Please note that the form is not required, but it is highly encouraged that you fill it out to help us improve.
