# Student Ambassador Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from students who are interested in becoming ambassadors for the organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please provide your contact details in the first field.
2.  If you have any education history, please provide it in the second field.
3.  If you have any qualifications, please provide them in the third field.
4.  If you have any additional information, please provide it in the fourth field.
5.  If you attended an educational institution, please provide the name of the institution in the fifth field.
6.  If you have earned a degree, please provide it in the sixth field.
7.  If you are studying a specific field, please provide it in the seventh field.
8.  If you have a student ID, please provide it in the eighth field.
9.  If you are willing to share your date of birth, please provide it in the ninth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Contact Details**: This field is used to collect the applicant's name, email address, and phone number.
*   **Education History**: This field is used to provide information about the applicant's education history.
*   **Qualifications**: This field is used to provide information about the applicant's qualifications, including their skills, certifications, or experience.
*   **Additional Information**: This field is used to collect any additional information the applicant may feel is relevant to their application.
*   **Education Institution**: This field is used to provide information about the applicant's education institution.
*   **Degree**: This field is used to provide information about the applicant's degree.
*   **Field of Study**: This field is used to provide information about the applicant's field of study.
*   **Student ID**: This field is used to provide the applicant's student ID.
*   **Date of Birth**: This field is used to collect the applicant's date of birth.

## Tips

*   Please make sure to fill in all the required fields.
*   You can leave fields that are not required blank.
*   If you are unsure about any field, feel free to contact the organization for clarification.
*   Please make sure to provide accurate information to avoid any issues with your application.
