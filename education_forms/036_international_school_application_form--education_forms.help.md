# International School Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from students who are interested in applying to our international school. It will help us understand your background, academic qualifications, and reasons for applying.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your student name, address, email, and phone number.
2. Provide your guardian's name and email.
3. Enter your birthdate and select your country of origin.
4. Explain why you are interested in attending our school.
5. Specify your language proficiency and any other languages you speak.
6. Select the program you are interested in and any other programs that apply to you.
7. Describe your English and math proficiency levels.
8. If you have siblings applying, please specify the number and provide details.
9. List your references.
10. Submit the form and wait for our response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (Student Name, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your passport or ID.
* **Student Address** (Student Address, text, required): Enter your current address.
* **Student Email** (Student Email, email, required): Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Guardian Name** (Guardian Name, text, required): Enter your guardian's name.
* **Guardian Email** (Guardian Email, email, required): Enter your guardian's email address.
* **Student Birthdate** (Student Birthdate, date, required): Enter your birthdate.
* **Student Country** (Student Country, select_one, required): Select your country of origin from the list.
* **Reason For Attending** (Reason For Attending, text, required): Please provide a brief explanation for applying to our school.
* **Student Language** (Student Language, select_multiple, required): Select the languages you speak or are proficient in.
* **Other Language** (Other Language, text, optional): If you have any other languages, please specify.
* **Other Language Details** (Other Language Details, text, optional): Provide details about your other language.
* **Student Program** (Student Program, select_multiple, required): Select the program you are interested in.
* **Other Program** (Other Program, text, optional): If you have any other programs that apply to you, please specify.
* **Other Program Details** (Other Program Details, text, optional): Provide details about your other program.
* **Student English Proficiency** (Student English Proficiency, select_multiple, required): Select your English proficiency level.
* **Other English Proficiency** (Other English Proficiency, text, optional): If you have any other English proficiency, please specify.
* **Other English Proficiency Details** (Other English Proficiency Details, text, optional): Provide details about your other English proficiency.
* **Student Math Proficiency** (Student Math Proficiency, select_multiple, required): Select your math proficiency level.
* **Other Math Proficiency** (Other Math Proficiency, text, optional): If you have any other math proficiency, please specify.
* **Other Math Proficiency Details** (Other Math Proficiency Details, text, optional): Provide details about your other math proficiency.
* **Siblings** (Siblings, text, required): If you have siblings applying, please specify the number and provide details.
* **References** (References, text, required): List your references.
* **References Details** (References Details, text, optional): Provide details about your references.

Note: If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us and we will be happy to help.
