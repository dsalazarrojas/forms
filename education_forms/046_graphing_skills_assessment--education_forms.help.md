# Graphing Skills Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Graphing Skills Assessment form is used to evaluate a student's graphing skills and tool proficiency. It is designed to assess their ability to create graphs and use various graphing tools to understand and visualize data. The form is typically completed by students, teachers, or instructors to track progress and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter Student ID**: Fill in the student's ID number in the field provided.
2. **Select Tools Used**: Choose all the tools the student has used for graphing, such as a graph paper and ruler.
3. **Describe Challenges Faced**: Write a brief description of any challenges the student faced during the graphing activity.
4. **Score Tools**: Assign a score out of 5 for each tool used, indicating their proficiency level.
5. **Assess Graphing Skills**: Select the student's graphing skill level (Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced).
6. **Score Tools and Graphing Skills**: Enter a score for the total tools and graphing skills.
7. **Overall Score**: Enter an overall score out of 5 for the student's graphing skills.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **student_id**: Enter the student's ID number.
* **tools_used**: Select all the tools the student has used for graphing, such as graph paper and a ruler.
* **challenges_faced**: Write a brief description of any challenges the student faced during the graphing activity.
* **tools_score**: Score the student's proficiency level for each tool used, out of 5.
* **graphing_skills**: Select the student's graphing skill level (Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced).
* **graphing_tool_score**: Enter a score for the student's proficiency level with graphing tools, out of 5.
* **graphing_skills_score**: Enter a score for the student's overall graphing skills, out of 5.
* **graphing_score**: Enter a score for the student's graphing skills, out of 5.
* **overall_graphing_score**: Enter an overall score out of 5 for the student's graphing skills.
* **overall_graphing_tool_score**: Enter a score for the student's overall proficiency level with graphing tools, out of 5.
* **overall_score**: Enter an overall score out of 5 for the student's graphing skills.
* **comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about the student's graphing skills.
* **graphing_tool_score**: Note: This field was previously mentioned as tools_score. Enter a score for the student's proficiency level with graphing tools, out of 5.
* **graphing_score**: Note: This field was previously mentioned as overall_graphing_score. Enter an overall score out of 5 for the student's graphing skills.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate scores and comments to provide a fair assessment of the student's graphing skills.
* If a student faces any challenges, provide a brief description to help improve their understanding.
* Review the overall scores carefully to ensure they reflect the student's true graphing skills.
