# Course Enrollment Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect student and course information for course enrollment declarations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Student Information" page with the relevant details.
2. Proceed to the "Course Information" page and provide the necessary course details.
3. Move on to the "Contact Details" page and supply the required contact information.
4. Finally, enter the "Course Enrollment Details" in the last page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information (Student Information)** (`text`, Not required): Enter the student's name, ID, date of birth, or any other relevant information.
* **Course Information (Course Information)** (`text`, Not required): Provide the course name, course code, or any other relevant course details.
* **Contact Details (Contact Details)** (`text`, Not required): Enter the contact person's name, email, or phone number.
* **Course Enrollment Details (Course Enrollment Details)** (`text`, Not required): Fill out this field with the specific course enrollment details, such as the date of enrollment or the enrolled course duration.
