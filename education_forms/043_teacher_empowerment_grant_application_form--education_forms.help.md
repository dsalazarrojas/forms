# Teacher Empowerment Grant Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help teachers apply for a grant to support their professional development and education initiatives. The grant is intended to empower teachers with resources and funding to enhance their teaching practices and contribute to the growth and success of their students. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the required fields, indicated by a red asterisk (*).
2. Provide accurate and complete information for each field.
3. Review your application form before submission to ensure all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher Information** (`teacher_information`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide a brief introduction to yourself, including your name, title, and a short description of your background as a teacher.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Please enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
* **Student Information** (`student_info`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide details about your students, such as their names, ages, and grade levels.
* **Teacher Information** (`teacher_info`, text, optional): This field is similar to the first "Teacher Information" field, where you can provide more details about yourself as a teacher.
* **Grant Details** (`grant_details`, text, optional): Please describe the specific grant you are applying for, including its purpose and expected outcome.
* **School Information** (`school_info`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide information about your school, such as its name and location.
* **Grant Amount** (`grant_amount`, number, optional): If the grant is approved, you may be eligible for a certain amount of funding. Enter the amount you are eligible for here.
* **Grant Duration** (`grant_duration`, number, optional): If the grant is approved, enter the expected duration of the grant in months.
* **Grant Status** (`grant_status`, select_one, optional): Select the current status of your grant application from the following options:
	+ Approved
	+ Pending
	+ Rejected

Note: If you have any questions or need further clarification on any field, please contact us at your institution or organization.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information in each field.
* Review your form carefully before submission to ensure all required fields are filled out.
* If you have any supporting documents or attachments, please attach them separately.
* Please keep in mind that the grant review process may take several weeks to complete.
