# Thesis Defense Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback and comments from committee members after a thesis defense. The form is used to assess the quality of the defense presentation, the department head's presence, and the overall performance of the student. The feedback collected through this form will help to improve the thesis defense process and provide valuable insights for future improvements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Read through the form carefully and understand what each field represents.
*   Answer each field according to your evaluation of the defense presentation.
*   Fill out all required fields.
*   Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Committee Member**: Enter the number of the committee member attending the defense, if applicable.
*   **Name**: Enter the name of the committee member.
*   **Department**: Enter the name of the department where the defense took place.
*   **Score 1**, **Score 2**, and **Score 3**: Evaluate the defense presentation and select a score from 1 to 12 to indicate its quality.
*   **Date**: Enter the date of the defense.
*   **Comments**: Provide any comments or feedback you have about the defense presentation.
*   **Email**: Enter the email address of the committee member (not necessarily the one they used during the defense).
*   **Phone**: Enter a phone number for the committee member (if applicable).
*   **Department Name**: Enter the name of the department (if different from the one mentioned earlier).
*   **Department**: Enter the name of the department (if different from the one mentioned earlier).
*   **Defense Date**: Enter the date of the defense (if different from the one mentioned earlier).
*   **Defense Time**: Enter the time of the defense (if applicable).
*   **Committee Size**: Enter the number of committee members attending the defense.
*   **Committee Type**: Enter the type of committee (if applicable).
*   **Defense Location**: Enter the location where the defense took place.
*   **Thesis Title**: Enter the title of the thesis defended.
*   **Student Name**: Enter the name of the student who defended the thesis.
*   **Student Id**: Enter the student ID (if applicable).
*   **Department Head**: Select whether the department head was present (Present, Absent, or Unable to Attend).
*   **Department Head Note**: Enter any comments or feedback about the department head's presence.
*   **Defense Presentation**: Evaluate the defense presentation and select a score from Good, Fair, or Poor.
*   **Defense Presentation Note**: Enter any comments or feedback about the defense presentation.
*   **Overall Score**: Evaluate the overall performance of the student and select a score from 1 to 12.

By filling out this form, you will be providing valuable feedback to improve the thesis defense process and help students grow and develop their academic performance.
