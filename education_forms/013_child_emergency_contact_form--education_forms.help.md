# Child Emergency Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Child Emergency Contact Form is used to collect contact information for a child's primary and secondary emergency contacts in case of an unexpected event or emergency. This information will be used to ensure that the child receives proper care and attention when needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the child's full name as registered in our system.
2. Enter the child's date of birth.
3. Select the relationship between the child and the primary contact.
4. Enter the primary contact's full name and primary phone number.
5. (Optional) Enter the secondary contact's full name and primary phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Child Full Name** (`child_name`, `text`, required): Enter the child's full name as registered in our system.
* **Child Date of Birth** (`child_dob`, `date`, required): Select the child's date of birth from the calendar.
* **Contact 1 Full Name** (`contact_1_name`, `text`, required): Enter the primary contact's full name, who should be the first person called in an emergency.
* **Contact 1 Relationship to Child** (`contact_1_relationship`, `select_one`, required): Select the relationship between the child and the primary contact (e.g., Mother, Father, Guardian).
* **Contact 1 Primary Phone** (`contact_1_phone_primary`, `text`, required): Enter the primary contact's primary phone number, ideally a number that can be reached during the day.
* **Contact 2 Full Name** (`contact_2_name`, `text`, required): Enter the secondary contact's full name, who should be called if the primary contact is unavailable.
* **Contact 2 Relationship to Child** (`contact_2_relationship`, `select_one`, required): Select the relationship between the child and the secondary contact (e.g., Aunt, Grandparent, Friend).
* **Contact 2 Primary Phone** (`contact_2_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the secondary contact's primary phone number, which should be a backup emergency number.
* **Critical Medical Information or Allergies** (`medical_info`, `text`, optional): List any critical medical information or allergies that the child may have.
* **Name of Primary Care Physician** (`physician_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the child's primary care physician for medical reference.

Note: The form is designed to be completed by a parent or guardian, and the information collected will only be used in emergency situations. The child's well-being and safety are the top priority.
