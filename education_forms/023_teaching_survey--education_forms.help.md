# Teaching Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from students about their recent experience with a teaching course or program. It helps instructors and administrators to identify areas of improvement and provide better services to students.

## How To Complete This Form

* Please answer each question to the best of your knowledge and honesty.
* For questions with multiple options, select only one that best represents your opinion.
* If a question is not applicable to you, you can leave it blank.
* Take as much time as you need to complete the form, and don't hesitate to ask if you have any questions or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher Id**: This is a unique identifier for the teacher who taught you.
* **What did you learn in the last week?**: Please describe the most significant things you learned in the last week. Be as detailed as possible.
* **What was the most challenging part of the experience?**: This can be a difficult question, but try to be as specific as you can. What part of the experience was most challenging for you?
* **How would you rate your experience?**: Rate your experience on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "very poor" and 5 is "excellent".
* **What could be improved?**: If there's anything that you think could be improved, please let us know. This will help us to make the course better for future students.
* **How likely are you to recommend the course?**: Please select one option that best represents your willingness to recommend the course.
* **Do you need help or resources?**: If you need help or resources, please select all that apply.
* **How do you rate the teacher?**: Rate the teacher's performance on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "very poor" and 5 is "excellent".
* **How do you rate the teaching assistant?**: Rate the teaching assistant's performance on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "very poor" and 5 is "excellent".
* **What other comments do you have?**: If there's anything else you'd like to share, please write it here.

## Tips

* Please be honest and thorough in your answers.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask.
* Your feedback is valuable to us and will help us to improve the course. Thank you for taking the time to complete this form!
