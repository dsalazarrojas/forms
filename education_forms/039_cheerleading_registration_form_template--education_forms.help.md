# Cheerleading Registration Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential participant information for cheerleading activities, including personal details, guardian contact information, and medical consent. Completing this form accurately ensures that each participant's profile is properly represented in the system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the participant's personal details, including their full name, current age, grade level, and date of birth.
2. Enter the primary guardian's name, email address, and phone number.
3. Add any secondary or emergency contact information, including their name and relationship to the participant.
4. In the "Medical and Consent" section, list any medical allergies or conditions the participant may have and provide a summary of their previous cheerleading experience.
5. Finally, confirm your consent for the participant's liability and indicate their registration fee status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name**: Enter the participant's full name, including any middle initials or suffixes if applicable.
* **Current Age**: Enter the participant's age in years.
* **Grade Level**: Select the participant's current grade level.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the participant's date of birth (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Primary Guardian Name**: Enter the primary guardian's name.
* **Primary Email Address**: Enter the primary guardian's email address.
* **Primary Phone Number**: Enter the primary guardian's phone number.
* **Secondary / Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and relationship of the secondary or emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone Number**: Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Medical Allergies or Conditions**: If the participant has any medical allergies or conditions, list them here. Leave blank if none apply.
* **Previous Cheerleading Experience Summary**: Briefly describe the participant's previous cheerleading experience, if applicable.
* **Liability Waiver and Consent**: Select "I Agree" to confirm liability waiver and consent.
* **Registration Fee Status**: Choose how you plan to pay the registration fee: Paid Online, Will Bring Check, or Financial Aid Requested.
* **Submission Date**: This field will automatically be populated upon form submission and is for your reference only.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
* If the participant has any medical allergies or conditions, list them clearly to ensure their safety and proper care.
* Confirm liability waiver and consent for each participant before proceeding.
* Review the registration fee status carefully to ensure you understand the payment arrangements.
