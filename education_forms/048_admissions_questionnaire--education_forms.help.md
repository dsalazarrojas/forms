# Admissions Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect personal and educational information from students applying to various programs or courses at the institution.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by reading the instructions and hints carefully for each field.
- Answer all required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
- Enter accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
- Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`student_full_name`, text, required): Enter your legal name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email Address** (`student_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that you regularly use for communication.
* **Phone Number** (`student_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Address** (`student_address`, text, required): Enter your current residential address.
* **City** (`student_city`, text, required): Enter the city of your residence.
* **State/Province** (`student_state`, text, required): Enter the state or province of your residence.
* **ZIP/Postal Code** (`student_zip`, text, required): Enter the postal code of your residence.
* **Country** (`student_country`, text, required): Enter the country of your residence.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your birth date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Program Applying For** (`program_applying_for`, text, required): Select the program or course you are applying for.
* **Degree Level** (`degree_level`, select_one, required): Select the level of degree you are pursuing (Certificate, Diploma, Associate, Bachelor's, Master's, Doctorate, Other).
* **Start Term** (`start_term`, select_one, required): Select the term you wish to start (Fall, Spring, Summer, Winter, Other).
* **Start Year** (`start_year`, number, required): Select the year you wish to start.
* **Education Background** (`education_background`, text, required): Describe your educational background, including any relevant courses or degrees.
* **Previous Institution** (`previous_institution`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of your previous educational institution.
* **GPA** (`gpa`, number, optional): Enter your cumulative GPA (if applicable).
* **Standardized Test Scores** (`standardized_test_scores`, text, optional): Enter scores from standardized tests such as SAT, ACT, GRE, GMAT, or other relevant tests.
* **Reason for Applying** (`reason_for_applying`, text, required): Enter why you are applying to this institution.
* **Career Goals** (`career_goals`, text, required): Enter your career goals after completing this program.
* **Academic Interests** (`academic_interests`, text, required): Enter the academic areas that interest you most.
* **Extracurricular Activities** (`extracurricular_activities`, text, optional): If applicable, describe any extracurricular activities you have participated in.
* **Leadership Experience** (`leadership_experience`, text, optional): If applicable, describe any leadership experience you have.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, optional): If applicable, describe any relevant work experience.
* **Challenges Overcome** (`challenges_overcome`, text, optional): If applicable, describe any significant challenges you have overcome.
* **Special Circumstances** (`special_circumstances`, text, optional): If applicable, enter any special circumstances that may be relevant to your application.
* **Contribution to Community** (`contribution_to_community`, text, optional): If applicable, describe how you plan to contribute to the campus community.
* **References** (`references`, text, optional): If applicable, provide contact information for references.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required): Enter the relationship to your emergency contact.
* **Questionnaire Date** (`questionnaire_date`, date, required): Enter the date of completing this questionnaire.
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, text, required): Electronic signature to confirm completion of this questionnaire.

## Tips
- Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
- Ensure that you have answered all required fields.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact the institution's admissions department for clarification.
- Please note that the institution's admissions department will review your application carefully.
