# Architecture Program Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Architecture Program Admission Form is used to gather information from students applying to the Architecture Program. This form helps the admission committee to evaluate your qualifications and motivation for joining the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information in the "Personal Information" section, including your First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth, Email Address, and Phone Number.
2. Provide your education background in the "Education Background" section, including your High School Name and Graduation Year.
3. If you have prior college or university experience, provide details about your experience and GPA in the "Prior College Experience" section.
4. If you have taken standardized tests like SAT or ACT, please provide your scores in the "Standardized Test Scores" section.
5. In the "Personal Statement" section, briefly explain why you want to study architecture and what qualities you bring to the program.
6. Finally, provide any additional information about your design or drawing experience, or relevant experiences or skills you have acquired in the "Prior Design or Drawing Experience" and "Relevant Experience or Skills" sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (Required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email Address** (Required): Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (Required): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Citizenship or Residency Status** (Required): Select your citizenship or residency status from the provided options (Domestic, International, or Permanent resident).
* **High School Name** (Required): Enter the name of the school you attended.
* **High School Graduation Year** (Required): Enter the year you graduated from high school.
* **High School GPA** (Optional): If you have a high school GPA, enter it here.
* **Prior College or University Experience** (Required): Select your prior college or university experience from the provided options (False, Some college, Associate degree, Bachelor degree, or Other).
* **Prior College Name (if applicable)** (Optional): Enter the name of the college or university you attended.
* **Prior College GPA** (Optional): If you have a prior college GPA, enter it here.
* **Standardized Test Scores** (Optional): If you have taken standardized tests like SAT or ACT, provide your scores here.
* **SAT Score (if available)** (Optional): Enter your SAT score.
* **ACT Score (if available)** (Optional): Enter your ACT score.
* **Why do you want to study architecture?** (Required): Briefly explain why you want to study architecture and what qualities you bring to the program.
* **Prior Design or Drawing Experience** (Optional): Provide any additional information about your design or drawing experience.
* **Relevant Experience or Skills** (Optional): List any relevant experiences or skills you have acquired that you believe are valuable for the program.

Note: All fields marked as "Required" must be completed before submitting the form.
