# Advanced Education Division Selection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Advanced Education Division Selection Form is designed to collect essential information from applicants who are interested in enrolling in the division. The form is divided into sections that cover the applicant's profile, division selection, program specifics, rationale for selection, and enrollment details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out each field carefully and accurately.**
2. **Use the available options in the select fields (e.g., Primary Division Choice, Study Format Preference).**
3. **For text fields (e.g., Applicant Full Name, Specific Major or Concentration Interest), enter your relevant information.**
4. **Check the mandatory fields (marked with *) as they are required for the application process.**

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Full Name (*)**: Enter your full name as it should be listed in official records.
* **Student ID if Enrolled**: If you are currently enrolled in any educational institution, enter your student ID number.
* **Contact Email (*)**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Primary Division Choice (*)**: Select your primary area of interest from the provided options (e.g., Humanities and Social Sciences, STEM and Applied Technology).
* **Secondary Division Choice**: Select your secondary area of interest from the provided options (e.g., Humanities and Social Sciences, STEM and Applied Technology). If none of the options apply, select "None".
* **Specific Major or Concentration Interest (*)**: Enter your specific major or concentration interest (e.g., Data Analytics, Public Health).
* **Study Format Preference (*)**: Select how you intend to study (e.g., Full-time On-campus, Part-time On-campus, Fully Online, Hybrid).
* **Career Aspirations Related to Division (*)**: Describe where you see yourself in 5 years in relation to the division you selected.
* **Relevant Previous Coursework Summary**: Provide a brief summary of relevant coursework already completed.
* **Intended Start Date (*)**: Enter your intended start date.
* **Attendance at Orientation Confirmed (*)**: Confirm if you have attended or plan to attend the orientation session.

Note: The (*) symbol indicates mandatory fields.
