# Honor Roll Graduation Recognition Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Honor Roll Graduation Recognition Form is used to formally recognize and celebrate students' academic achievements and milestones in their educational journey.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2.  Select the student's grade level from the dropdown menu in the "Student Grade Level" field.
3.  Enter the student's grade point average (GPA) in the "Student Grade Point Average" field.
4.  Choose the academic program the student is part of from the dropdown menu in the "Academic Program" field.
5.  Enter the date of the student's achievement recognition in the "Student Achievement Recognition Date" field.
6.  Enter the name of the teacher who is recognizing the student in the "Teacher Name" field.
7.  Enter the teacher's title in the "Teacher Title" field.
8.  Enter the name of the school the student attends in the "School Name" field.
9.  Enter the school's address in the "School Address" field.
10.  Enter the name of the award the student is receiving in the "Award Name" field.
11.  Enter a brief description of the award in the "Award Description" field.
12.  Enter the date the award was received in the "Award Recognition Date" field.
13.  Enter the name of the student who received the award in the "Award Recipient Name" field.
14.  Enter the name of the school administrator responsible for the award in the "School Administrator" field.
15.  Enter the name of the organization or entity sponsoring the award in the "Award Sponsor" field.
16.  Enter the method used to recognize the award in the "Award Recognition Method" field.
17.  Enter the name of the school the award was received from in the "Award Recipient School" field.
18.  Enter the name of the contact person at the school in the "School Contact Name" field.
19.  Enter the contact email of the school in the "School Email" field.
20.  Enter the school's phone number in the "School Phone" field.
21.  Enter the school's fax number in the "School Fax" field.
22.  Enter the school's address in the "School Address" field.
23.  If the award is in digital format, enter the image URL in the "Award Image" field.
24.  If the award is in PDF format, enter the PDF URL in the "Award PDF" field.
