The "Director Recommendation Form" is a document used to formalize and document director recommendations made by the organization to students or professionals for future career opportunities. This form is typically completed by the organization's personnel to verify and provide information about the recommended individual's qualifications and abilities. 

**Director Recommendation Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose

This form is used to provide a director's official endorsement of an individual's abilities and qualifications, which can be used to support future career opportunities such as job applications, internships, or further education. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. To begin, please select a blank form.
2. Enter your name and last name.
3. Identify your department.
4. Enter the title of your position.
5. If applicable, select whether you have made a recommendation or not.
6. Provide information on your relationship with the individual you are making a recommendation for.
7. Leave any additional comments that may be helpful to the recipient.
8. Enter the email address of the individual being recommended.
9. Enter the date of recommendation, using the date format of your choice.
10. Finally, select "Submit" to complete your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **first_name** (Text, Optional)
Enter your first name.

### 2. **last_name** (Text, Optional)
Enter your last name.

### 3. **department** (Text, Optional)
Identify the department you belong to.

### 4. **title** (Text, Optional)
Enter your job title.

### 5. **recommendation_date** (Date, Optional)
Select the date of recommendation using the standard date format of your choice.

### 6. **recommendation_period** (Text, Optional)
Enter the period of recommendation, if applicable.

### 7. **recommended_by** (Select One, Optional)
Select 'True' if you have made a recommendation and 'False' if you have not.

### 8. **relationship** (Text, Optional)
Describe your relationship with the individual you are making a recommendation for.

### 9. **comment** (Text, Optional)
Leave any additional comments you feel are necessary for further context.

### 10. **email** (Email, Optional)
Enter the email address of the individual being recommended.
