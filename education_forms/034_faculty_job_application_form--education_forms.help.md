# Faculty Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Faculty Job Application Form is designed to collect information from candidates applying for a job at our institution. Please fill out the form with your details to apply for a job. This form is for internal use only, so please do not submit it if you are not a candidate for a job opening.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Select the page with your candidate information.
2. Fill out all the required fields.
3. Select the job type and job posting that best match your qualifications.
4. Attach your resume and cover letter if required.
5. Fill out the contact information fields.
6. Answer the questions regarding your employment status, employment type, job location, work status, and job category.
7. Answer the questions regarding your job title, job classification, and job description.
8. Fill out the job requirements and job posting description fields.
9. Select the application process.
10. Review and submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate Information** (`candidate_info`, text, required: false): Provide your name, email, and any other relevant details.
* **Application Form** (`application_form`, text, required: false): Briefly describe your qualifications and experience.
* **Job Posting** (`job_posting`, select_one, required: false): Select the job posting that best matches your qualifications.
* **Application Documents** (`application_documents`, text, required: false): Attach any relevant documents, such as a CV or letter of recommendation.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, required: false): Attach your resume.
* **Cover Letter** (`cover_letter`, text, required: false): Attach your cover letter.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Provide your contact information, such as phone number and mailing address.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or more job titles that match your qualifications.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of job you are applying for.
* **Job Classification** (`job_classification`, select_one, required: false): Select the job classification that matches your qualifications.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required: false): Select the department you are applying for.
* **Job Category** (`job_category`, select_one, required: false): Select the job category you are applying for.
* **Salary Range** (`salary_range`, number, required: false): Provide the salary range you are expecting.
* **Benefits** (`benefits`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the benefits you are expecting.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required: false): Provide the start date you are expecting.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required: false): Provide the end date you are expecting.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, required: false): Select your employment status.
* **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, select_one, required: false): Select your employment type.
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the job locations you are applying for.
* **Work Status** (`work_status`, select_multiple, required: false): Select your work status.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, required: false): Provide a description of the job you are applying for.
* **Job Requirements** (`job_requirements`, text, required: false): Provide the requirements for the job you are applying for.
* **Application Process** (`application_process`, text, required: false): Select the application process you prefer.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required: false): Review and submit your application.
