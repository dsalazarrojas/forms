# Faculty Contact Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Faculty Contact Request Form is used by students or staff to request contact information of a faculty member.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the department of the faculty member you are inquiring about.
2. Select the specific faculty member you would like to contact.
3. Provide your email address so that we can contact you with the faculty member's contact information.
4. Enter your name (if you would like to be contacted directly).
5. Specify the reason for your inquiry.
6. Enter any additional message or question you have for the faculty member.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Department** (Select One, required): Choose the department of the faculty member you are inquiring about.
* **Faculty Inquire** (Select Multiple, required): Select the specific faculty member you would like to contact.
* **Email** (Email, required): Enter your email address so that we can contact you with the faculty member's contact information.
* **Faculty Name** (Text, required): Enter your name (if you would like to be contacted directly).
* **Reason for Inquire** (Text, required): Specify the reason for your inquiry.
* **Message** (Text, required): Enter any additional message or question you have for the faculty member.
* **Faculty Contact** (Text, required): Enter the faculty member's contact information (this will be populated by us based on your selection).
