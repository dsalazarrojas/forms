# School Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from applicants who are interested in enrolling in a school. Please provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field (this should be a valid email address).
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field (in the format MM/DD/YYYY).
6. Enter the name of the school you are applying to in the "School Name" field.
7. Enter the name of the program you are applying for in the "Program Name" field.
8. Enter the date you submitted your application in the "Application Date" field (in the format MM/DD/YYYY).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address that can be used to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the school you are applying to.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the program you are applying for.
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you submitted your application in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact the school's admissions office.
* Please be aware that incomplete forms may result in delayed or denied applications.
