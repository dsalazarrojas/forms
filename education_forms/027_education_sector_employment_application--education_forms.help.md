# Education Sector Employment Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals seeking employment in the education sector. It gathers details about the applicant, their personal and professional background, and relevant supporting documents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and other identifying details in the **applicant_info** field.
2. Enter your personal contact information in the **contact_info** field.
3. Share your educational background in the **education_info** field.
4. Describe your previous work experience relevant to the education sector in the **work_experience** field.
5. List any employment-related references in the **references** field.
6. Upload any relevant documents in the **upload_documents** field.
7. Review your submission in the **review** field to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **applicant_info** (text, optional): Enter your name and other identifying details as the applicant.
* **personal_info** (text, optional): Enter your personal contact information (e.g., email, phone number).
* **contact_info** (text, optional): Share your work contact information (e.g., email, phone number).
* **education_info** (text, optional): Describe your educational background, including degrees and institutions attended.
* **work_experience** (text, optional): Describe your previous work experience in the education sector, including job titles, dates, and relevant skills.
* **employment_info** (text, optional): Provide details about your current or previous employment in the education sector.
* **references** (text, optional): List any professional references with their contact information.
* **upload_documents** (text, optional): Upload any relevant supporting documents, such as certificates or diplomas.
* **submit** (text, optional): Click this button to submit your application.
* **review** (text, optional): Review your submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.
