# Digital Classroom Feature Testing Consent - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain consent from users to test digital classroom features. It will be used to gather contact information and other details from users for the purpose of feature testing, and not for any other purpose.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the User Information section with your name, email address, and any other requested details.
2. Choose your role in the testing process by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' in the Role field.
3. Enter your phone number and any additional contact details if required.
4. Provide your email address and any other contact details if required.
5. Choose whether or not you consent to take part in the feature testing by selecting 'I consent to take part' or 'Do not consent'.
6. Fill out any other contact details as required and provide any additional notes if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Information
* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, optional): Fill in your name.
### Name
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
### Role
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you are taking part in the testing process by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
### Contact Details
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional contact details.
### Contact Details Email
* **Contact Details Email** (`contact_details_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
### Contact Details Phone
* **Contact Details Phone** (`contact_details_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
### Consent
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you consent to take part in the feature testing by selecting 'I consent to take part' or 'Do not consent'.
### Contact Details Address
* **Contact Details Address** (`contact_details_address`, text, optional): Provide your address.
### Contact Details Company
* **Contact Details Company** (`contact_details_company`, text, optional): Enter your company name.
### Contact Details Department
* **Contact Details Department** (`contact_details_department`, text, optional): Enter your department name.
### Contact Details Job Title
* **Contact Details Job Title** (`contact_details_job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title.
### Contact Details Extension
* **Contact Details Extension** (`contact_details_extension`, text, optional): Enter your extension number.
### Contact Details Notes
* **Contact Details Notes** (`contact_details_notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional notes.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the testing administrators.
* This form is used for testing purposes only, and your participation is greatly appreciated.
