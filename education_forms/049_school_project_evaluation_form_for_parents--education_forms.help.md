# School Project Evaluation Form For Parents - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Project Evaluation Form For Parents is a tool for teachers, administrators, or staff members to evaluate student projects submitted by students. The form helps to assess the project's quality, relevance, and other important aspects, providing constructive feedback and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the project submission and familiarize yourself with the project's title, description, and category.
2.  Evaluate the project's quality by selecting one of the options under "Evaluation".
3.  Provide any additional comments or feedback on the project in the "Comments" field.
4.  Enter the student's email address and phone number in the "Email" and "Phone" fields, respectively.
5.  Select the project category from the list provided under "Category".
6.  Optionally, record the date and time when the project was completed in the "Date Completed" and "Time Completed" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Project Title**: Enter a brief title of the project.
    This field should contain a short title of the project. For example, "My Science Project".
*   **Project Description**: Enter a detailed description of the project.
    In this field, provide a detailed description of the project, including the project's main objectives, methods, and results.
*   **Evaluation**: Select the evaluation of the project.
    This field is used to assess the project's quality, with options such as "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", and "Poor".
*   **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback on the project.
    Use this field to provide specific, constructive feedback on the project, such as suggestions for improvement or points of praise.
*   **Email**: Enter the student's email address.
    This field is used to contact the student, so make sure to enter their actual email address.
*   **Phone**: Enter the student's phone number.
    This field is used to contact the student, so make sure to enter their actual phone number.
*   **Category**: Select the category of the project.
    This field is used to classify the project into one of the provided categories, such as "Math", "Science", or "English".
*   **Date Completed**: Record the date when the project was completed.
    This field is used to track the date when the project was finished.
*   **Time Completed**: Record the time when the project was completed.
    This field is used to track the time when the project was finished.
*   **Assigned By**: Enter the staff member who assigned the project.
    This field is used to keep track of who assigned the project, in case of any further questions or issues.
*   **Category**: Select the category of the project that the staff member assigned. 

## Tips

*   Be specific and constructive in your evaluation and comments.
*   Use the provided options under "Evaluation" to help guide your assessment.
*   Make sure to provide feedback that is actionable and helpful for the student.
*   Proofread your form carefully before submitting it.
