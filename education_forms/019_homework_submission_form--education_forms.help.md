This form is used to facilitate the submission of homework assignments by students. It captures essential details such as student and homework information, submission date and time, and any attached files. The form is designed to be completed by the student to provide a clear and concise submission record that can be easily reviewed by educators.

To use this form, follow these steps:

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2.  Enter the student's ID in the "Student ID" field. This is usually a unique identifier assigned to each student.
3.  Provide the name of the homework assignment in the "Homework Name" field.
4.  Enter the date of submission in the "Submission Date" field.
5.  Enter the time of submission in the "Submission Time" field.
6.  Upload any relevant files related to the homework in the "Submitted Files" field. This can include documents, images, or videos.
7.  Enter the grade awarded for the homework in the "Grade" field.
8.  Provide any remarks or feedback for the student in the "Remarks" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student Name** (student_name, text, required): Please enter the student's name as it appears on their student ID or record.
*   **Student ID** (student_id, number, required): Enter the student's unique ID assigned to them by the educational institution.
*   **Homework Name** (homework_name, text, required): Enter the name of the homework assignment being submitted.
*   **Submission Date** (submission_date, date, required): Enter the date the homework was submitted.
*   **Submission Time** (submission_time, time, required): Enter the time the homework was submitted.
*   **Submitted Files** (submitted_files, note, required): Upload any relevant files related to the homework, such as documents, images, or videos.
*   **Grade** (grade, number, required): Enter the grade awarded for the homework assignment.
*   **Remarks** (remarks, text, required): Provide any feedback or remarks for the student.

## Tips

*   Be sure to double-check the information you enter is accurate.
*   Use the correct format for entering dates (YYYY-MM-DD) and times (HH:MM:SS).
*   Ensure any uploaded files are relevant to the homework assignment.
*   Proofread your input before submitting to avoid errors.
*   If there are any issues with submitting or accessing the form, contact the support team.

This form is designed to be user-friendly and intuitive, allowing students to easily submit their homework assignments with all necessary details. Follow these steps and guidelines to ensure accurate and complete submission records.
