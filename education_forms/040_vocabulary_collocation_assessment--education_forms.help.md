# Vocabulary Collocation Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to test your understanding of vocabulary collocations, which are pairs of words that often go together in language. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer the questions on each page and submit your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is an English collocation?** (`vocabulary_collocation_assessment_1`, text, required): This is the first question where you can provide your answer in text format.
* **Vocabulary collocation example** (`vocabulary_collocation_assessment_2`, text, optional): Provide an example of a vocabulary collocation you are familiar with.
* **Which part of speech** (`vocabulary_collocation_assessment_3`, select_multiple, optional): This question allows you to choose which part of speech you think the words in a collocation should be. Choose as many options as apply.
* **How do you know** (`vocabulary_collocation_assessment_4`, select_one, optional): This question is asking about your approach to learning vocabulary collocations.
* **How much do you agree** (`vocabulary_collocation_assessment_5`, number, optional): This question is about your level of agreement with the provided collocation.
* **How much do you agree with other** (`vocabulary_collocation_assessment_6`, text, optional): Provide your thoughts on how much you agree with other people's opinions on the collocation.
* **Vocabulary collocation assessment 8** (`vocabulary_collocation_assessment_7`, text, optional): Another question where you can provide your answer in text format.
* **Vocabulary collocation assessment 10** (`vocabulary_collocation_assessment_8`, text, optional): Another question where you can provide your answer in text format.
* **Vocabulary collocation grammar** (`vocabulary_collocation_assessment_9`, select_multiple, optional): Choose which of the provided options you think is correct about the grammar of vocabulary collocations.
* **Vocabulary collocation example other** (`vocabulary_collocation_example_other`, text, optional): If none of the provided collocation examples are correct, provide your own.
* **Vocabulary collocation grammar 2** (`vocabulary_collocation_grammar_2`, select_one, optional): Choose the correct option about the grammar of vocabulary collocations.

## Tips
- Please answer the questions to the best of your ability.
- Make sure to use specific examples to support your answers.
- If you're unsure about a question, try to provide a reason why you're unsure.
- This form should take approximately 5-10 minutes to complete.
- If you need help or have questions, please contact us.
