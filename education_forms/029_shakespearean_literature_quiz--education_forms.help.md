# Shakespearean Literature Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information and feedback from users about Shakespearean literature. You can fill out as many or as few questions as you like, depending on your interest and knowledge.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below.

1. Review each page of questions carefully, and select the answers that best match your knowledge or opinion.
2. For select_multiple and select_one questions, choose one or more options that apply.
3. For text and number questions, enter your answer in the corresponding field.
4. Make sure to fill out all required fields, indicated by the "required" label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **What is the Bard?** (`question_one`, `select_multiple`, not required): This question is asking for the name of the author of the plays you will be answering questions about. You can select one or more options: 
  - Romeo
  - Juliet
  - Hamlet

- **Who is the main theme of this play?** (`question_two`, `select_one`, not required): This question is asking for the central idea or message of the play you are thinking of. Choose one of the following options: 
  - Love
  - Revenge
  - Loyalty

- **What is the name of the location where the play is set?** (`question_three`, `text`, not required): This is asking for the place where the play takes place. Enter the name of the location, such as "Verona" or "Elsinore".

- **In what century was Shakespeare born?** (`question_four`, `number`, not required): This question is asking for the century in which the bard was born. Enter the number of the century, such as "16th".

- **Select the time period of the play** (`question_five`, `select_one`, not required): This question is asking for the historical time period in which the play is set. Choose one of the following options: 
  - Renaissance
  - Elizabethan
  - Shakespeare

- **Select the time of day** (`question_six`, `time`, not required): This question is asking for the time of day when the play takes place. Enter a time of day, such as "morning", "noon", "evening".

- **Select the name of the play** (`question_seven`, `text`, not required): This is asking for the name of the play you are thinking of. Enter the name of the play, such as "Romeo and Juliet" or "Hamlet".

- **What is your name?** (`question_eight`, `text`, not required): This question is asking for your name, the person completing the form. Enter your name, but this information is not used for anything else in the system.

- **What is the name of the author?** (`question_nine`, `text`, not required): This question is asking for the name of the author of the play. Enter the name of the author, such as "William Shakespeare".

- **What is the name of the main character?** (`question_ten`, `text`, not required): This question is asking for the name of the main character in the play. Enter the name of the character, such as "Romeo" or "Hamlet".

- **What is the location where the play is set?** (`question_eleven`, `text`, not required): This is asking for the place where the play takes place. Enter the name of the location.

- **What is the year of the play?** (`question_fourteen`, `number`, not required): This question is asking for the year the play was written. Enter the year, such as "1595".
