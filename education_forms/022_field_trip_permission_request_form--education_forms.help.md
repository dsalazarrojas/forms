# Field Trip Permission Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request permission for field trips that involve students. It helps staff and teachers manage and coordinate student activities outside of regular classroom hours.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow the steps below:

1. **Enter your name (User)**: Please fill in your name as the staff member or teacher requesting permission.
2. Fill in the trip details:
   1. **Trip Details (Date)**: Enter the date of the field trip.
   2. **Start Time**: Enter the start time of the trip.
   3. **End Time**: Enter the end time of the trip.
   4. **Destination**: Enter the location or destination of the trip.
3. Select the teacher in charge:
   1. **Teacher**: Choose the teacher who will be supervising or accompanying the students during the trip.
4. Add additional details:
   1. **Student Count**: Enter the number of students attending the trip.
5. Grant permission:
   1. **Permission**: Indicate if you are granting permission for the trip ('Yes') or not ('No').
6. (Optional) Add comments:
   1. **Comments**: Provide any additional notes or comments about the trip.
7. Provide contact details:
   1. **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
   2. **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
8. Assign a contact person:
   1. **Assigned User**: Choose who will be the primary contact person for the trip.
