# Student Peer Collaboration Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Peer Collaboration Effectiveness Survey aims to gather feedback from students on their experiences and perceptions of peer collaboration in academic settings. This feedback will help the institution evaluate the effectiveness of their collaboration strategies and make informed decisions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions according to your experiences and opinions. The form is divided into sections, each focusing on different aspects of peer collaboration. Select the relevant options that best describe your experiences for each section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Group work** (1): This question assesses whether you participate in group work activities during class or group projects.
	+ Select "Yes" if you often participate in group work activities, or "No" if you rarely or never participate.
* **Communication** (2): This question evaluates the quality of communication within your groups.
	+ Select "Yes" if you experience good communication among group members, or "No" if you experience poor communication.
* **Collaboration challenges** (3): This question identifies common challenges you face during collaboration.
	+ Select "Yes" if you experience challenges, or "No" if you do not experience challenges.
* **Collaboration skills** (4): This question assesses your collaboration skills, such as teamwork and time management.
	+ Select "Yes" for skills you possess, or "No" for those you lack.
* **Group dynamics** (5): This question evaluates the dynamics within your groups.
	+ Select "Yes" for good group dynamics, or "No" for poor dynamics.
* **Collaboration outcomes** (6): This question assesses the outcomes of your collaboration efforts.
	+ Select "Yes" for positive outcomes or "No" for negative outcomes.

- Be honest and accurate in your responses to provide valuable feedback.
- Select the options that best describe your experiences and opinions.
- If you are unsure about any question, please select the option that best reflects your situation.
