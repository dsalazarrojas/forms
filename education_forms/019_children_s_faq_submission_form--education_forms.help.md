# Children's FAQ Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Children's FAQ Submission Form is designed to help students, parents, and teachers submit questions related to education, categorize and prioritize them, and track their status and follow-up instructions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Choose the correct category for your question from the provided options.
3. Write a clear and concise question that describes your query.
4. Select the priority level of your question as high, medium, or low.
5. Specify how you will use the answer for school, personal interest, or other purposes.
6. Optionally, indicate if you have already researched the question, the sources you used, and what you already know about the topic.
7. Check if you want a detailed or simple answer.
8. Choose your preferred method of receiving the answer (email, in-person discussion, class presentation, or online discussion).
9. Select if you want to submit the question anonymously.
10. Confirm your submission by agreeing to the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (Student Name, text, required): Enter your name or your child's name to help identify you as the question submitter.
* **Grade Level** (Grade Level, select_one, required): Choose your grade level from the provided options (e.g., K, 1st, 2nd, etc.).
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, required): Enter your email or your child's email to help us contact you about your question.
* **School Name** (School Name, text, required): Provide the name of your child's school or your own school name.
* **Teacher or Class Name** (Teacher or Class Name, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of your child's teacher or class.
* **Question Category** (Question Category, select_one, required): Choose the category related to your question from the provided options (e.g., Science, Math, History, etc.).
* **Specific Topic** (Specific Topic, text, required): Describe the specific topic related to your question.
* **Your Question** (Your Question, text, required): Write a clear and concise question that describes your query.
* **Question Priority** (Question Priority, select_one, optional): Select the priority level of your question as high, medium, or low.
* **Related Assignment** (Is this related to a school assignment, select_one, optional): Indicate if your question is related to a school assignment.
* **Assignment Name** (Assignment Name, text, optional): If your question is related to a school assignment, provide the name of the assignment.
* **Already Researched** (Have you already researched this question, select_one, optional): Indicate if you have already researched the question.
* **Research Sources** (What sources have you checked, text, optional): Provide the sources you used to research the question.
* **What You Already Know** (What do you already know about this topic, text, optional): Describe what you already know about the topic.
* **Why It Matters** (Why this question is important to you, text, optional): Explain why this question is important to you.
* **How You Will Use** (How will you use the answer, text, optional): Specify how you will use the answer for school, personal interest, or other purposes.
* **Detailed Answer** (Would you like a detailed answer, select_one, optional): Select if you want a detailed or simple answer.
* **Preferred Method** (Preferred way to receive answer, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred method of receiving the answer (email, in-person discussion, class presentation, or online discussion).
* **Anonymous Submission** (Submit this question anonymously, select_one, optional): Select if you want to submit the question anonymously.
* **Submission Confirmation** (I agree to submit this question, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree to submit the question.
