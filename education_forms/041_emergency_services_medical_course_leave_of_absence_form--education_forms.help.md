# Emergency Services Medical Course Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students seeking a leave of absence due to medical emergencies or services such as the Emergency Services Medical Course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Provide your personal details, including your first name, last name, and email address.
2. Select the service that best describes your medical course (Emergency Services Medical Course or Other).
3. Enter a brief description of your medical condition or service.
4. Enter the code of the course you are taking.
5. Provide the name of the course you are currently taking.
6. Enter the start and end dates of your leave of absence.
7. Enter the name and email of your instructor.
8. Click submit to send the form to your institution's support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email**: Enter your institutional email address.
* **Service**: Select the service that best describes your medical course (Emergency Services Medical Course or Other).
	+ Emergency Services Medical Course: Select this option if you are taking the Emergency Services Medical Course.
	+ Other: Select this option if you have a different medical course.
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of your medical condition or service.
* **Code**: Enter the code of the course you are taking.
* **Name**: Enter the name of the course you are currently taking.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date when your leave of absence begins.
* **End Date**: Enter the date when your leave of absence ends.
* **Instructor's Name**: Enter the name of your instructor.
* **Instructor's Email**: Enter the email address of your instructor.

## Tips
* Please provide accurate and honest information to ensure the institution's support is tailored to your needs.
* If you have already submitted a leave of absence form, please do not submit again.
* Ensure you have the necessary permissions from your instructor or department head before filling out this form.
