# Graduation Recognition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Graduation Recognition Form is used to collect information from graduates to acknowledge their academic achievement. This form helps the institution to recognize and celebrate the graduate's accomplishment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form with your name, graduation year, and academic institution details accurately.
2. Select your academic level from the options provided.
3. If your academic level is not listed, provide the correct information in the "Other (if not listed)" field.
4. Make sure to fill in all mandatory fields (marked as required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Graduation Year** (`graduation_year`, `number`, required): Enter the year you graduated.
* **Graduation Date** (`graduation_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you graduated in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, `text`, required): Enter your student ID if it's available.
* **Major** (`major`, `text`, required): Enter your major or field of study.
* **Academic Level** (`academic_level`, `select_one`, required): Select your academic level from the options provided. If not listed, choose "Other (if not listed)" and provide the correct information in the next field.
* **Academic Level (if not listed)** (`academic_level_other`, `text`, optional): This field is optional and should only be filled if the "Academic Level" field does not include the correct option.
* **Academic Institution** (`academic_institution`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your academic institution.
* **Academic Institution (if not listed)** (`academic_institution_other`, `text`, optional): This field is optional and should only be filled if the "Academic Institution" field does not include the correct option.

## Tips
* Please ensure all mandatory fields are filled accurately and correctly.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the explanations above.
* Double-check your submission before submitting the form.
