# Tier 1 Classroom Observation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tier 1 Classroom Observation form is used to document a classroom observation event. This form is intended to capture essential information about the observation, including the teacher's name, school, grade level, date and time, and student demographics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the school and grade level of the observed classroom.
2. Enter the teacher's name and the date and time of the observation.
3. Record the total number of students present and their attendance.
4. Provide additional information as needed, such as start and end times, which are not required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the teacher being observed.
* **School** (`school`, text, optional): Enter the name of the school where the observation took place.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_one, optional): Select the grade level of the observed classroom from 1, 6, 9, or 12.
* **Classroom Observation Date** (`classroom_observation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the observation.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the observation (format: 12:00 AM/PM).
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of the observation (format: 12:00 AM/PM).
* **Student Population** (`student_population`, number, optional): Enter the total number of students present during the observation.
* **Total Students** (`total_students`, number, optional): Enter the total number of students present during the observation.
* **Student Attendance** (`student_attendance`, number, optional): Enter the attendance percentage of students present during the observation (calculated from Student Population and Total Students).

Note: The form is designed to be completed by educators or administrators to report on a classroom observation, focusing on the essential information to be captured.
