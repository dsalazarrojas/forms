# School Resource Upgrade Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Resource Upgrade Request Form is used by school administrators to request upgrades for school resources, ensuring that the required information is collected in a standardized and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your school's name in the "School Name" field.
2. Select the "Category" of the resource being upgraded.
3. Choose the "Resource Type" of the resource being upgraded.
4. Enter the location of the resource that needs to be upgraded.
5. Provide a brief description of the upgrade being requested.
6. Enter the priority level of the upgrade.
7. Enter the budget allocated for the upgrade.
8. Enter your school's email address.
9. Enter your school's phone number.
10. If applicable, select the "Assignee" for the upgrade.
11. Add any additional comments about the upgrade.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Name**: Enter the name of your school in this field.
* **Category**: Select the category of the resource being upgraded (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Resource Type**: Choose the type of resource being upgraded (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Location**: Enter the location of the resource that needs to be upgraded.
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of the upgrade being requested.
* **Priority**: Enter the priority level of the upgrade, which will help determine the order of upgrades.
* **Budget**: Enter the budget allocated for the upgrade.
* **Email**: Enter your school's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your school's phone number.
* **Assignee**: If the upgrade requires specific staff or personnel, select "Yes" and enter their name.
* **Comment**: Add any additional comments or details about the upgrade.
