# Speech And Language Service Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather background information from individuals seeking speech and language services. It helps our team understand your needs and provide personalized support. Please provide as much detail as possible in each field.

## How To Complete This Form

- Start by answering each question to the best of your ability.
- You can type your response in the text boxes provided.
- If you need to add additional information or clarification, you can also upload supporting documents or attachments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Background Information (#1)**
	+ This field is where you can describe your current situation and any relevant medical history.
	+ Please provide a brief overview of your current background information.
* **Medical History (#2)**
	+ This field is for any relevant medical conditions or health concerns you may have.
	+ Provide information about your medical history, but be as brief and concise as possible.
* **Communication Concerns (#3)**
	+ This field is for any concerns or difficulties you have with communication.
	+ Describe any challenges you face with communication, such as speaking, hearing, or understanding.
* **Patient Background (#4)**
	+ This field is for more detailed information about your current situation.
	+ Please provide additional context about yourself and your goals.
* **Therapy Goals (#5)**
	+ This field is for specifying what you want to achieve through speech and language services.
	+ Be as specific as possible about your goals and what you hope to accomplish.
* **Patient History (#6)**
	+ This field is for any relevant history or context about your current situation.
	+ Please provide any additional information that might be helpful for our team to understand your needs.
* **Language Profile (#7)**
	+ This field is for describing your language abilities and strengths.
	+ Please include any information about your language skills, such as accent, dialect, or language proficiency.
* **Additional Comments (#8)**
	+ This field is for any additional information you want to provide or discuss.
	+ Feel free to share anything you think might be helpful for our team to know.
* **Patient Goals (#9)**
	+ This field is for specifying what you want to achieve through speech and language services.
	+ Be as specific as possible about your goals and what you hope to accomplish.
* **Medical Specialties (#10)**
	+ This field is for any relevant medical specialties or conditions you may have.
	+ Please provide information about any medical specialties or conditions that might impact your needs.
* **Family History (#11)**
	+ This field is for any relevant family medical history or genetic conditions.
	+ Please provide information about any relevant family history that might impact your needs.
* **Patient Education (#12)**
	+ This field is for your educational background and achievements.
	+ Please provide information about your educational history and any relevant credentials.
* **Speech/Language Pathologist (#13)**
	+ This field is for designating the speech-language pathologist who will be working with you.
	+ Please ensure this information is accurate and up-to-date.

	+ This field is for describing your current emotional state.

	+ This field is for any medical updates or changes that may impact your needs.

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough in your responses.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask our team for clarification.
- You can upload supporting documents or attachments if needed.
- Please provide as much detail as possible to help our team better understand your needs.
