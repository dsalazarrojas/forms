# Student Winter Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Winter Assessment Survey is designed to gather feedback from students about their experiences during the winter term. Your participation helps the educational institution to understand what they can improve in their winter programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the first question, "What was your favorite winter experience?", in the text field below.
2. Rate your overall experience with the winter term by selecting a number from 1 to 5.
3. Select the experience you found most valuable from the options below.
4. Choose up to 3 of your favorite courses from the list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What was your favorite winter experience?**
	+ This question is optional and allows you to share your favorite experience from the winter term.
	+ Type in a few words to describe your favorite experience.
* **How would you rate your overall experience with the winter term?**
	+ Rate your experience on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is the least satisfied and 5 is the most satisfied.
* **Which of the following experiences did you find most valuable?**
	+ Select one experience from the list that you found most valuable during the winter term.
	+ This question is optional and helps the institution to understand what students value most.
* **What was your favorite course this winter term?**
	+ Select up to 3 of your favorite courses from the list.
	+ This question is optional and allows you to share your favorite courses from the winter term.

## Tips
* Take a few minutes to think about your experiences and responses carefully before submitting this form.
* If you need help or have any questions, contact the educational institution for assistance.
* Your feedback is greatly appreciated and helps to improve the winter programs.
