# Parenting Style Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This guide explains how to complete the Parenting Style Survey, a self-assessment form designed to gauge parents' perceptions of their own parenting style.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Review the form**: Take a few moments to review the form and its questions before starting.
2. **Answer each question honestly**: Answer each question as accurately and honestly as possible, based on your current situation and attitudes.
3. **Select all that apply**: For questions with "select multiple" options, choose all the answers that best fit your situation.
4. **Submit your responses**: Once you've completed all questions, submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parenting Style** (1. Parent Response)
	+ This is the first question, asking how you perceive your parenting style. It's meant for you to briefly describe your approach to parenting, without selecting a pre-defined option.
* **How do you Discipline your child?** (2. Parent Response 2)
	+ Select all methods or strategies you currently use to discipline your child (e.g., rewards, time-outs, positive reinforcement).
* **Parental Involvement** (3. Parent Response 3)
	+ Choose all ways in which you are involved in your child's life (e.g., attending school events, helping with homework).
* **Parental Support** (4. Parent Response 4)
	+ Select all ways in which you support your child's emotional and educational development (e.g., reading together, encouraging physical activity).
* **Parental Responsiveness** (5. Parent Response 5)
	+ Choose all ways in which you respond to your child's needs (e.g., being available for questions, showing empathy).

## Tips

* Be honest with your responses. This form is designed for your reflection and growth, not for judgment.
* If you're unsure about an option, choose "no" and consider discussing with a professional or support network.
* It's okay to select multiple answers, as many parents blend different approaches.
