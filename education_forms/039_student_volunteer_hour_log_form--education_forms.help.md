# student_volunteer_hour_log_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to log volunteer hours for students who have participated in volunteer activities. It provides a way to track and record the hours spent volunteering and serves as a record of student's volunteer work.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form with the required and optional information below.
2.  Ensure that you are recording hours for events or programs that are student-related.
3.  Check the form for completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student Volunteer Organization** (`student_volunteer_organization`, text, optional): Enter the name of the organization where you volunteered.
*   **Volunteer Date** (`volunteer_date`, date, optional): Choose the date of the event or program you participated in.
*   **Volunteer Time** (`volunteer_time`, time, optional): Select the time you spent volunteering, in 24-hour format (e.g., 09:00).
*   **Volunteer Hour** (`volunteer_hour`, number, optional): Enter the total number of hours you spent volunteering.
*   **Volunteer Location** (`volunteer_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of where you volunteered.

## Tips

*   If you have any doubts or questions about the form, please consult with a designated authority.
*   Double-check for duplicate or non-relevant entries before submitting.
*   Complete all required fields for accurate logging.
*   This form is intended for recording student-related volunteer hours.
