# Tutoring Services Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on tutoring services within the education sector. It helps administrators or educators gather valuable insights to improve the quality and effectiveness of their services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Form Title**: Provide a brief and descriptive title for the form.
2. Select a **Category**: Choose between "Education Forms" and "Other" to categorize the feedback.
3. Enter a **Description**: Provide a detailed description of the feedback, including your suggestions or comments.
4. Provide **Feedback**: Share your thoughts, ideas, or concerns about the tutoring services.
5. Optional: Add a **Category List**: If relevant, select additional categories that apply to your feedback.
6. Enter the **Date** and **Time** of when you experienced the tutoring service (optional).
7. Enter your **Email** address (if you want to be contacted for follow-up or further discussion).
8. Provide your **Phone** number (if you want to be contacted for further discussion).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Title (1)**: A brief title that describes the purpose and content of the form.
- **Category (2)**: Select one category that best applies to your feedback (Education Forms or Other).
  * This field is optional, but it helps administrators or educators to filter or categorize feedback.
- **Description (3)**: Provide a detailed description of your feedback, including suggestions or comments.
- **Feedback (4)**: Share your thoughts, ideas, or concerns about the tutoring services.
- **Form Description (5)**: This field is for internal use, providing a brief description of the form.
- **Category List (6)**: Select additional categories that apply to your feedback (if relevant).
  * This field is optional and helps administrators or educators to better understand your feedback.
- **Date (7)**: Enter the date when you experienced the tutoring service (optional).
- **Time (8)**: Enter the time when you experienced the tutoring service (optional).
- **Email (9)**: Enter your email address if you want to be contacted for follow-up or further discussion.
- **Phone (10)**: Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted for further discussion.

## Tips
- Please be specific and detailed in your feedback to help us improve our services.
- If you have suggestions or ideas, feel free to share them with us.
- We appreciate your input and will use it to enhance our tutoring services.
