# College Application Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "College Application Intake Form" is a required form for students who are applying to college. This form is designed to gather information about the applicant's background, preferences, and application status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information, including your first and last name, date of birth, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide your mailing address and city.
3. Choose your country of residence.
4. Enter your high school name, city, and state.
5. Select your graduation year and GPA (unweighted and weighted).
6. Choose your intended major and select your degree type (e.g., Bachelor of Arts).
7. Indicate your test scores (SAT or ACT) or select "No Test Optional".
8. Select the term you plan to start and whether you are interested in on-campus housing.
9. Choose your method of application (e.g., Early Decision, Early Action).
10. Indicate your financial aid requirements.
11. Select your FAFSA status, CSS Profile status, and whether you are first-generation college student.
12. Confirm your veteran or active duty status.
13. Choose your application fee waiver status.
14. Confirm that all information is accurate.
15. Finally, select your preferred major and application type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (id: 1) (`text`, required): Your given name.
* **Last Name** (id: 2) (`text`, required): Your family name.
* **Date of Birth** (id: 3) (`date`, required): Your birthdate in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Email Address** (id: 4) (`email`, required): Your primary email address.
* **Phone Number** (id: 5) (`text`, required): Your contact phone number.
* **Mailing Address** (id: 6) (`text`, required): Your complete address.
* **City** (id: 7) (`text`, required): The city of your current location.
* **State** (id: 8) (`text`, required): Your state or province of residence.
* **ZIP Code** (id: 9) (`text`, required): Your postal code.
* **Country** (id: 10) (`select_one`, required): Your country of residence.
* **High School Name** (id: 11) (`text`, required): The name of your high school.
* **High School City** (id: 12) (`text`, required): The city of your high school.
* **High School State** (id: 13) (`text`, required): The state of your high school.
* **Graduation Year** (id: 14) (`number`, required): The year you graduated from high school.
* **Unweighted GPA** (id: 15) (`number`, required): Your unweighted GPA on a 4.0 scale.
* **Weighted GPA** (id: 16) (`number`, optional): Your weighted GPA on a 4.0 scale, if applicable.
* **Class Rank** (id: 17) (`text`, optional): Your class rank, if available.
* **Submitting Test Scores** (id: 18) (`select_one`, required): Whether you are submitting SAT or ACT scores.
* **SAT Total Score** (id: 19) (`number`, optional): Your SAT total score, if submitting.
* **ACT Composite Score** (id: 20) (`number`, optional): Your ACT composite score, if submitting.
* **Intended Major** (id: 21) (`text`, required): Your first-choice major.
* **Second Choice Major** (id: 22) (`text`, optional): Your alternative major interest.
* **Degree Type** (id: 23) (`select_one`, required): The type of degree you are pursuing (e.g., Bachelor of Arts).
* **Starting Term** (id: 24) (`select_one`, required): When you plan to start college.
* **Interested in On-Campus Housing** (id: 25) (`select_one`, required): Whether you are interested in on-campus housing.
* **Will You Apply for Financial Aid** (id: 26) (`select_one`, required): Whether you need-based aid.
* **FAFSA Status** (id: 27) (`select_one`, optional): Your FAFSA status.
* **CSS Profile Status** (id: 28) (`select_one`, optional): Your CSS Profile status.
* **First-Generation College Student** (id: 29) (`select_one`, required): Whether you are a first-generation college student.
* **Veteran or Active Duty** (id: 30) (`select_one`, optional): Your military service status.
* **Are You a Transfer Student** (id: 31) (`select_one`, required): Whether you are a transfer student.
* **Previous College Name** (id: 32) (`text`, optional): The name of your previous college, if a transfer student.
* **College Credits Earned** (id: 33) (`number`, optional): The number of credits you have earned, if a transfer student.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (id: 34) (`select_one`, required): How you heard about the college.
* **Have You Visited Campus** (id: 35) (`select_one`, optional): Whether you have visited campus.
* **Application Type** (id: 36) (`select_one`, required): The type of application you are submitting (e.g., Early Decision).
* **Requesting Fee Waiver** (id: 37) (`select_one`, required): Whether you are requesting a fee waiver.
* **Information Certification** (id: 38) (`select_one`, required): Confirmation that all information is accurate.
