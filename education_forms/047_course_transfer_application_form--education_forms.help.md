# Course Transfer Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to facilitate the process of a student requesting to transfer to a different course in the university. The form requires the student to provide necessary details such as their application details, student information, course details, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your application details, including your name and any other relevant information.
2. Enter your student ID or last name and first name to identify yourself as a student.
3. Provide details about the course you are currently enrolled in and the course you wish to transfer to.
4. Choose the reason for your request to transfer.
5. Add any additional notes about your application.
6. Confirm that the information you provided is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Details** (`application_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter a short description of your application.
* **Student** (`student`, `text`, required: false): Enter your student ID or last name and first name to verify your identity as a student.
* **Course Details** (`course_details`, `text`, required: false): Describe the course you are currently enrolled in and the course you wish to transfer to.
* **Current Course** (`current_course`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the course you are currently enrolled in.
* **Transfer Course** (`transfer_course`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the course you wish to transfer to.
* **Reason** (`reason`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief explanation for your request to transfer.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, required: false): Sign your name to verify your identity.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you are submitting this form.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional notes about your application.
* **Confirm** (`confirm`, `select_one`, required: true): Confirm that the information you provided is accurate.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form carefully and accurately.
* If you have any questions or need help, please reach out to your academic advisor.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you are not sure about any of the fields, please do not submit the form.
