# e_learning_poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The e_learning_poll form is designed to gather information about your eLearning platform experiences and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all that apply from the eLearning Platform field regarding your eLearning experiences.
2. Enter your email address in the email field.
3. Choose your preferred time of day for learning in the preferred time of day field.
4. Add any additional comments or information in the note field.
5. Enter your phone number (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### eLearning Platform (<name>, select_multiple, required: false)
- Select all that apply from the options 'Yes' and 'No' regarding your eLearning platform experiences.

### email (<name>, email, required: false)
- Enter your email address.

### preferred time of day (<name>, time, required: false)
- Choose your preferred time of day for learning (e.g., 9:00 AM, 10:00 AM, etc.).

### note (<name>, note, required: false)
- Add any additional comments or information about your eLearning platform experiences.

### phone (<name>, text, required: false)
- Enter your phone number (optional).

Tips:
- Please ensure that you have filled out all applicable fields accurately.
- If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
- This form is designed to be user-friendly and efficient.
