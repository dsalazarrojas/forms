# Postgraduate Program Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from students who are interested in or are already enrolled in postgraduate programs. The form collects data on various aspects of these programs, including fields of study, study modes, interests, report formats, and contact information. This information will be used to tailor programs to the needs and interests of students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form effectively, follow these steps:

1. Select all fields of study that you are interested in.
2. Indicate whether you are interested in each study mode (e.g., full-time, part-time, online).
3. Specify your interests, including any relevant skills or areas of expertise.
4. Choose the report formats you are interested in receiving (e.g., PDF, Excel).
5. Enter your contact email address.
6. Provide your contact phone number.
7. Add any additional comments or notes you'd like to share with us.
