# Assignment Topic Voting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect student feedback on available assignment topics, allowing instructors to gauge interest levels, assess prior knowledge, and determine research capabilities for each topic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Student Name**, **Student ID**, and **Email Address** to identify yourself as a participant.
2. Choose the **Class Section** you belong to for accurate topic selection.
3. Select the **Course Name** and **Instructor Name** for context.
4. Enter the **Voting Date** for timestamping.
5. Describe the **Assignment Description** and any **Instructions** for topic selection.
6. Choose your interest levels for each of the four available **Topic Options** (one, two, three, and four) using the provided scale (Very Interested, Interested, Neutral, Less Interested, or Not Interested).
7. If you're interested in a particular topic, provide a brief explanation in the **Why This Topic?** field.
8. Finally, enter your **Top Preference** for the topic you find most appealing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`): Enter your full name as recorded in the course records.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`): Enter your student identification number.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`): Enter a valid email address for future reference.
* **Class Section** (`class_section`): Select the class or section you're enrolled in for accurate topic selection.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`): Enter the name of the course for context.
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`): Enter the name of your instructor for context.
* **Voting Date** (`voting_date`): Choose the date of this vote for timestamping.
* **Assignment Description** (`assignment_description`): Describe the assignment topic in a few words.
* **Instructions** (`instructions`): If any special instructions or guidelines apply, enter them here.
* **Topic Option One** (`topic_one_choice`): Select your interest level for the first available topic using the provided scale.
* **Topic One - Interest Level** (`topic_one_interest`): Explain why you're interested in the first topic.
* **Topic Option Two** (`topic_two_choice`): Select your interest level for the second available topic using the provided scale.
* **Topic Two - Interest Level** (`topic_two_interest`): Explain why you're interested in the second topic.
* **Topic Option Three** (`topic_three_choice`): Select your interest level for the third available topic using the provided scale.
* **Topic Three - Interest Level** (`topic_three_interest`): Explain why you're interested in the third topic.
* **Topic Option Four** (`topic_four_choice`): Select your interest level for the fourth available topic using the provided scale (if applicable).
* **Topic Four - Interest Level** (`topic_four_interest`): Explain why you're interested in the fourth topic (if applicable).
* **Your Top Preference** (`top_preference`): Enter the topic you find most appealing.
* **Prior Knowledge of Top Topic** (`topic_knowledge`): Describe what you already know about your top-preferred topic.
* **Can You Research This Topic?** (`research_capability`): Select "Yes" if you have access to resources for research, "No" if not, or "Somewhat" if you're unsure.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Enter any additional information or comments about your choices.
* **Form Submission Time** (`submission_timestamp`): The system will automatically timestamp the form submission for your record.
