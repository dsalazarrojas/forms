# seminar_rsvp - Help Guide
## Purpose
The `seminar_rsvp` form is designed for users to confirm their attendance and provide contact information to seminar organizers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Seminar Details" section with any additional information about the seminar you're attending.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Is Attending" field to indicate your attendance status.
3. Enter the number of attendees in the "Number of Attendance" field, if applicable.
4. Choose whether you'd like to provide your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
5. If you have any further comments or requests, type them in the "Further Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Seminar Details** (Seminar Details, note, required: false): Use this field to provide any additional information about the seminar, such as the seminar title, date, time, or location.
* **Is Attending** (Is Attending, select_one, required: false): Select "Yes" if you are planning to attend the seminar, or "No" if you are not attending.
* **Number of Attendance** (Number of Attendance, number, required: false): Enter the number of attendees if you are attending.
* **Contact Info** (Contact Info, select_multiple, required: false): Check "Yes" if you'd like to provide your contact information, which will be shared with seminar organizers.
* **Further Comments** (Further Comments, text, required: false): Type any additional comments or requests you have regarding the seminar.
