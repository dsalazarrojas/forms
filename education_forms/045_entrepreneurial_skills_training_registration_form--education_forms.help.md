# Entrepreneurial Skills Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for participants to register for Entrepreneurial Skills Training, which is a program designed to equip individuals with essential skills and knowledge to succeed in their entrepreneurial ventures. It is recommended that you only register once for the current program to avoid duplication of efforts.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill out your basic participant information in the "Participant Name" field. This is the name that will be associated with your training records.
2. Choose "Yes" or "No" for the training sessions you would like to attend from the "Sessions" field.
3. Select the training topics that you are interested in from the "Topics" field. You can select multiple topics.
4. Enter your desired training dates in the "Dates" field. Please follow the date format (dd/mm/yyyy).
5. Provide your contact email and a phone number in the "Participant Email" and "Phone" fields respectively.
6. Enter your address in the "Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information (<type:text>, required:false)**: This is your name that will be associated with your training records. Please fill out your name in the format as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Sessions (<type:select_one, required:false)**: Choose "Yes" or "No" for the training sessions you would like to attend.
* **Topics (<type:select_multiple, required:false)**: Select the training topics that you are interested in. You can select multiple topics.
* **Dates (<type:date, required:false)**: Enter your desired training dates in the format dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Participant Name (<type:text, required:false)**: This is your name that will be associated with your training records. Please fill it out as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Email (<type:email, required:false)**: Please enter your contact email address.
* **Participant Email (<type:text, required:false)**: This is to confirm your email address as a verification step.
* **Phone (<type:text, required:false)**: Enter your phone number for further contact purposes.
* **Address (<type:text, required:false)**: Enter your address where the training will be conducted, if applicable.
