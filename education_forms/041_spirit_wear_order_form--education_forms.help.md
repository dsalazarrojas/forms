# Spirit Wear Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spirit Wear Order Form is designed to collect order details from customers who want to purchase spirit wear items. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents. 
2. **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents. 
3. **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address. 
4. **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted. 
5. **Item Type** (select_one, required): Select the type of spirit wear item you'd like to order. 
6. **Quantity** (number, required): Enter the quantity of the item you'd like to order. 
7. **Size** (select_multiple, optional): Select the size of the spirit wear item you'd like to order. 
8. **Address 1** (text, required): Enter your shipping address. 
9. **City** (text, required): Enter the city of your shipping address. 
10. **State** (text, required): Enter the state of your shipping address. 
11. **Zip** (text, required): Enter the zip code of your shipping address. 
12. **Country** (text, optional): Select the country of your shipping address. 
13. **Special Instructions** (text, optional): Enter any special instructions for your order. 
14. **Payment Method** (select_one, required): Select the payment method you'd like to use. 
15. **Card Number** (text, required): Enter your credit card number. 
16. **Expiration Date** (date, required): Enter the expiration date of your credit card. 
17. **CVV** (text, optional): Enter the CVV of your credit card. 
18. **Confirmation** (note, optional): Enter any additional confirmation details. 
19. **Confirmation 2** (select_one, optional): Select any additional confirmation option. 
20. **Confirmation 3** (date, optional): Enter any additional date-based confirmation. 
21. **Confirmation 4** (time, optional): Enter any additional time-based confirmation.
