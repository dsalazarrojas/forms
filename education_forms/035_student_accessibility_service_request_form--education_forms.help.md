# Student Accessibility Service Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Accessibility Service Request Form is designed to collect information from students about their accessibility needs or requests. This form is intended to help the institution ensure that students' requirements are considered and met.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Provide your personal information**:
	* Fill out the `student_name` field with your full name.
	* Enter your `school_name` in the `school_name` field.
2. **Enter your contact details**:
	* Type your email address in the `student_email` field.
	* Enter your phone number in the `student_phone` field.
3. **Disclose your disability information**:
	* Select whether you have a disability by answering "Yes" or "No" in the `disability_type` field.
	* If you answered "Yes", provide any relevant details in the `disability_details` field.
4. **Specify your accommodation requests**:
	* Choose the accommodations you need from the options provided in the `accommodation_request` field.
5. **Submit your request**:
	* Enter the name, email, department, and phone number of the person submitting the request in the `submitter_name`, `submitter_email`, `submitter_department`, and `submitter_phone` fields.
	* Optionally, provide a note with any additional information for the submitters in the `submitter_note` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your school.
* **Contact Information** (`student_contact_information`, text, optional): Provide any other relevant contact information about yourself.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, optional): Type your email address.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Disability Type** (`disability_type`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you have a disability, or "No" if you do not.
* **Disability Details** (`disability_details`, note, optional): Provide any other relevant information about your disability or accommodation request.
* **Accommodation Request** (`accommodation_request`, select_multiple, required): Choose the accommodations you need from the provided options.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person submitting the request.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, optional): Type the email address of the person submitting the request.
* **Submitter Department** (`submitter_department`, text, optional): Enter the department of the person submitting the request.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person submitting the request.
* **Submitter Position** (`submitter_position`, text, optional): Enter the position of the person submitting the request.
* **Submitter Contact Method** (`submitter_contact_method`, text, optional): Provide any other relevant contact information about the person submitting the request.
* **Submitter Note** (`submitter_note`, note, optional): Provide any additional information for the submitters.

Note: This guide is intended to be a general guide, and the fields may have specific requirements or rules based on the institution's policies and procedures. Always check with the institution for specific guidelines on submitting requests.
