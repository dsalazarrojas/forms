# Historical Document Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Historical Document Review Form is designed to collect feedback or ratings on historical documents. This form helps educators or institutions gather valuable insights on the quality, accuracy, and overall value of the documents.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form title field with a brief description of the document.
2. Enter the document contents in the document field.
3. Choose a rating for the document in the Ratings field, selecting either Good or Fair.
4. Provide any additional comments about the document in the Comments field.
5. If applicable, upload relevant files related to the document in the File Uploads field.
6. Evaluate your overall form experience in the Review Form Rating field, also selecting either Good or Fair.
7. Enter your contact information in the Reviewer field, including your name and email address.
8. Optional: Enter your phone number in the Phone field, and the time you took to complete the form in the Time field.
9. Select a date when you completed the form in the Date field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Historical Document Review Form** (`historical_document_review_form`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the document you are reviewing.
* **title** (`title`, text, required): Enter a brief title or description of the document.
* **document** (`document`, text, required): Enter the contents of the historical document being reviewed.
* **Ratings** (`Ratings`, select_one, required): Choose a rating for the document, either Good or Fair.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, text, required): Enter any additional comments or feedback you have about the document.
* **File Uploads** (`File Uploads`, text, required): Upload any relevant files related to the document.
* **Review Form Rating** (`Review Form Rating`, select_one, required): Evaluate your overall experience with the form, selecting either Good or Fair.
* **Reviewer** (`Reviewer`, text, required): Enter your name and contact information.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, if applicable.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, optional): Enter the time it took you to complete the form.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, required): Select the date you completed the form.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, if applicable.
* **time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time it took you to complete the form.
* **review_form_rating** (`review_form_rating`, select_one, required): Evaluate your overall experience with the form, selecting either Good or Fair.
