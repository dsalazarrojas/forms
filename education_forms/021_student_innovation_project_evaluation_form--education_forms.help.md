# Student Innovation Project Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate student projects, providing a comprehensive assessment of their progress, feasibility, and effectiveness. It helps identify strengths, weaknesses, and areas for improvement, guiding project refinements and future endeavors.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the student project. This will help the evaluator identify the project being assessed.
2. **Project Status** (`project_status`, select_one, optional): Select the current status of the project:
	* `Active`: The project is currently ongoing or active.
	* `Inactive`: The project is no longer active or has been completed.
3. **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the student associated with the project.
4. **Evaluation Comments** (`evaluation_comments`, text, optional): Provide detailed comments about the project's strengths, weaknesses, and areas for improvement.
5. **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, optional): Describe the project's objectives, methodology, and any notable achievements or challenges.
6. **Evaluation Scale** (`evaluation_scale`, number, optional): Enter a score or rating for the project's evaluation (e.g., 1-10).
7. **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, optional): The date when the evaluation was conducted.
8. **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, time, optional): The time when the evaluation was conducted.
9. **Evaluation Score** (`evaluation_score`, number, optional): Enter the final evaluation score or grade for the project.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when completing the form to ensure that the evaluation is fair and accurate.
* Use clear and concise language when providing comments and project descriptions.
* If you're unsure about any aspect of the form, please contact the project lead or instructor for guidance.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
