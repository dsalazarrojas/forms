# Day 1 We Transform You - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from users about their educational background and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select Multiple** (`education`, required): 
Select all that apply to your educational background:
	* Yes, I have a degree in a related field.
	* Yes, I have some college education.
	* No, I don't have any formal education.
2. **Phone** (`phone`, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted by us.
3. **Email** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to receive updates about our services.
4. **Time** (`time`, optional): Choose a specific time that works for you to be contacted or receive updates.
5. **Note** (`note`, optional): Add any additional information or comments about your educational background or interests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **education**: This field is asking about your educational background. Please select all that apply to your situation:
	+ If you have a degree in a related field, select "Yes, I have a degree in a related field."
	+ If you have some college education, select "Yes, I have some college education."
	+ If you don't have any formal education, select "No, I don't have any formal education."
* **phone**: This field is for your phone number. Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted by us.
* **email**: This field is for your email address. Enter your email address if you'd like to receive updates about our services.
* **time**: This field is for choosing a specific time that works for you to be contacted or receive updates.
* **note**: This field is for adding any additional information or comments about your educational background or interests.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all the required fields (education).
* If you have any questions or need help, contact us.
* You can always update your information later if needed.
