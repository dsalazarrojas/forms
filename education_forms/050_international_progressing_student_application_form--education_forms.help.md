# International Progressing Student Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The International Progressing Student Application Form is designed to collect personal and demographic information from students who are progressing to a new stage in their education. This form helps to ensure that all necessary details are captured accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your First name, Last name, Date of birth, Email, Student ID, Nationality, Address, Country, City, and State/Province.
2. Make sure to enter your details accurately and correctly.
3. If you need to select a country, choose from the list of available options.
4. If you need to select a state or province, choose multiple options that apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Enter your first name, which is a required field.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name, which is a required field.
* **Date of birth**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YY or MM/DD/YY).
* **Email**: Enter your email address that is not already in use by someone else. This field is for communication purposes only.
* **Student ID**: Enter your unique student ID number, which is required for identification purposes.
* **Nationality**: Select your nationality from the provided options.
* **Address**: Enter your current residential address, which is a required field.
* **Country**: Select your country of origin from the list of available options.
* **City**: Enter the city where you currently reside.
* **State/Province**: Select all the states or provinces that apply to you from the provided options.
