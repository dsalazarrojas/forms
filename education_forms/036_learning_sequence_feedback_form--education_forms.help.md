# learning_sequence_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The learning_sequence_feedback_form is designed to collect feedback from participants or trainees about a specific learning sequence. This feedback helps educators and course administrators evaluate the effectiveness of their programs and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide helpful feedback, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page to understand what information is requested.
2. Answer all questions and fields that apply to your experience.
3. Be as detailed as possible in your feedback, especially in the "Suggest something" and "Comments" fields.
4. If unsure about a field, try to provide your best-effort interpretation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select whether you are a participant or not. This will help us understand your relationship with the learning sequence.
* **Learning Goals** (`learning_goals`, select_one, required: false): Indicate whether you have achieved the learning goals set for the sequence.
* **Suggest something** (`suggestions`, text, required: false): Share any suggestions or ideas you have about the learning sequence. This is a free text field for you to express your thoughts.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or insights about your experience with the learning sequence.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for further feedback or discussions.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Provide your phone number if you would like to be contacted via phone.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Record the date you completed the learning sequence.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Record the time you completed the learning sequence.
* **Feedback Score** (`feedback_score`, select_one, required: false): Indicate whether you found the learning sequence helpful or not.

Note: Please be honest and thorough in your feedback, as it will help us improve our learning sequences for future participants.
