# royal-english-medium-school-registration-form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Royal English Medium School Registration Form is designed to collect student and teacher information for the registration process of the royal-English medium school. This form helps the school administration collect relevant data from students and teachers, enabling them to efficiently manage the school's records and provide better services to its community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the student section with the student's details, such as their name, grade, email, phone number, and date of birth.
2. Enter the parent's contact information, including their name and email address.
3. Provide the student's availability (morning, afternoon, or evening) and availability (if applicable).
4. Fill out the teacher section with the teacher's name, email, phone number, and availability.
5. Enter the school name and address.
6. Select the school ID number (choose one or more).
7. Add any additional notes if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **student_name** (Text, Required): Enter the student's name.
* **grade** (Number, Optional): Enter the student's grade level, if applicable.
* **parent_name** (Text, Optional): Enter the parent's name.
* **student_email** (Email, Optional): Enter the student's email address.
* **student_phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the student's phone number.
* **student_dob** (Date, Optional): Enter the student's date of birth.
* **student_address** (Text, Optional): Enter the student's address.
* **student_grade** (Number, Optional): Enter the student's grade level, if applicable.
* **school_name** (Text, Optional): Enter the school name.
* **school_email** (Email, Optional): Enter the school email address.
* **school_address** (Text, Optional): Enter the school address.
* **school_phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the school phone number.
* **student_availability** (Select One, Optional): Select the student's availability (morning, afternoon, or evening).
* **prophets** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional information (Note: This field is not clearly labeled; we will assume it's meant to capture additional comments or notes).
* **teacher_name** (Text, Optional): Enter the teacher's name.
* **teacher_email** (Email, Optional): Enter the teacher's email address.
* **teacher_phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the teacher's phone number.
* **teacher_address** (Text, Optional): Enter the teacher's address.
* **teacher_grade** (Number, Optional): Enter the teacher's grade level, if applicable.
* **teacher_availability** (Select One, Optional): Select the teacher's availability (morning, afternoon, or evening).
* **teacher_specialty** (Text, Optional): Enter the teacher's specialty or subject area of expertise.
* **school_id** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose one or more school IDs.
* **notes** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional notes.
* **submit** (Text, Optional): Submit the form to complete the registration process.
