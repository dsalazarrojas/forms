# Farmer Degree Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Farmer Degree Application Form is designed to help applicants provide accurate and complete information about themselves and their farming experience. This form will be used to evaluate their application for a degree in a farming-related field.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your applicant information in the "Applicant Information" section, including any necessary details such as name, address, and contact numbers.
2. Provide information about your farm in the "Farm Info" section.
3. Enter your contact details in the "Contact Info" section.
4. If you are applying for a degree, provide information about your academic background in the "Academic Info" section.
5. If you are uploading any supporting documents, select "Yes" or "No" as appropriate in the "Upload Documents" section.
6. Finally, submit the form by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Information (Applicant Information)**: Enter your name, address, and other personal details.
* **Farm Info (Farm Info)**: Provide details about your farm, including its location and size.
* **Contact Info (Contact Info)**: Enter your contact information, including email and phone number.
* **Application Section (Application Section)**: This section is for providing details about your application. Please enter any relevant information that supports your application.
* **Academic Info (Academic Info)**: If you are applying for a degree, provide information about your academic background, such as your level of education and relevant courses.
* **Upload Documents (Upload Documents)**: If required, select "Yes" to indicate that you will be uploading supporting documents as part of your application.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address, which will be used for communication purposes.
* **Phone (Phone)**: Enter your phone number, which will be used for communication purposes.
* **Date (Date)**: Enter the date of your farm work experience, if applicable.
* **Time (Time)**: Enter the time of your farm work experience, if applicable.
* **Select One (Select One)**: Select "Yes" or "No" as appropriate to indicate your eligibility for the degree program.
* **Note (Note)**: Enter any additional information that may support your application.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to avoid any issues with your application.
* Ensure that the information you provide is accurate and truthful.
* You may need to upload supporting documents as part of the application process, so be prepared to do so.
