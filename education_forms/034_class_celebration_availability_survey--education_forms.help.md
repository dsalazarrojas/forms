# Class Celebration Availability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Class Celebration Availability Survey is designed to gather information from parents or guardians about their child's preferences and availability to help with a class celebration. This information will be used to plan and organize the celebration to ensure that all children's needs are met.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the parent/guardian name, student name, and teacher name in the respective fields.
2. Select the celebration type that best fits your child's needs.
3. Choose the dates that work best for your family.
4. Indicate if you are available to help with the celebration and what tasks you can assist with.
5. Indicate what you can contribute to the celebration, such as food, decorations, or activities.
6. If your child has dietary restrictions, select the relevant option and provide additional details in the "Dietary Details" field.
7. Indicate what activities your child enjoys and can participate in.
8. Choose your budget contribution for the class fund.
9. Provide any special requests or suggestions for the celebration.
10. Add any additional comments you would like to share with the celebration organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Parent or Guardian Name
**Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): This field is used to collect the name of the parent or guardian who is completing this form.

### Student Name
**Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): This field is used to collect the name of the student who will be attending the celebration.

### Teacher Name
**Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required): This field is used to collect the name of the teacher who will be leading the class.

### Email Address
**Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): This field is used to collect the email address of the parent or guardian for updates and communication.

### Phone Number
**Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): This field is used to collect the phone number of the parent or guardian for emergency contact.

### Celebration Type
**What Celebration Is This For** (`celebration_type`, select_one, required): This field is used to select the type of celebration that best fits your child's needs.

### Proposed Dates
**Which Dates Work for Your Family** (`proposed_dates`, select_multiple, required): This field is used to select the dates that work best for your family.

### Volunteer Availability
**Are You Available to Help** (`parent_availability`, select_one, required): This field is used to indicate if you are available to help with the celebration.

### Tasks to Help With
**What Tasks Can You Help With** (`volunteer_tasks`, select_multiple, optional): This field is used to select tasks you can help with during the celebration.

### Item Contribution
**What Can You Contribute** (`item_contribution`, select_multiple, optional): This field is used to select items you can contribute to the celebration.

### Dietary Restrictions
**Does Your Child Have Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, select_one, required): This field is used to select if your child has any dietary restrictions.

### Dietary Details
**Please Describe Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_details`, text, optional): This field is used to provide additional details about your child's dietary restrictions.

### Food Preferences
**What Foods Does Your Child Enjoy** (`food_preferences`, text, optional): This field is used to collect information about your child's food preferences.

### Activity Preferences
**What Activities Would Your Child Enjoy** (`activity_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): This field is used to select activities your child enjoys and can participate in.

### Budget Contribution
**Can You Contribute to Class Fund** (`budget_contribution`, select_one, optional): This field is used to select the amount you can contribute to the class fund.

### Special Requests
**Any Special Requests or Suggestions** (`special_requests`, text, optional): This field is used to collect any special requests or suggestions for the celebration.

### Additional Comments
**Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): This field is used to add any additional comments or information about your child.

## Tips

* Please answer the questions as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the teacher or celebration organizer.
* Make sure to complete all required fields before submitting the form.
