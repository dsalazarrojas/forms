# Sonship Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sonship Registration Form is designed for registering information about participants in a program or community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details:
	* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is written on your official documents.
	* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is written on your official documents.
	* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
	* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
2. Provide emergency contact information:
	* **First Name (Emergency Contact)**: Enter the first name of the person you'd like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
	* **Last Name (Emergency Contact)**: Enter the last name of the person you'd like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
	* **Relationship to Participant**: Select the relationship you have with the participant (e.g., Spouse, Parent, Guardian, Other).
	* **Phone Number (Emergency Contact)**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
3. Enter participant information:
	* **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the participant.
	* **Participant Age**: Enter the age of the participant.
	* **Date of Birth**: Enter the date of birth of the participant.
	* **Date of Participation**: Enter the date the participant started participating in the program or community.
4. Select participant status and group affiliation:
	* **Participant Status**: Select the current status of the participant (Active, Inactive, Unknown).
	* **Group**: Select if the participant is part of a group (Yes, No).
	* **Group Other**: If the participant is not part of the listed groups, select "Other" and enter the name of the group.
	* **Church/Tribe**: Select if the participant is affiliated with a church or tribe (Yes, No).
	* **Church/Tribe Other**: If the participant is not part of the listed churches or tribes, select "Other" and enter the name of the church or tribe.
	* **Participant Status Other**: If the participant's status is not listed, select "Other" and enter a brief description.
	* **Group Status**: Select the current status of the participant's group (Active, Inactive).
	* **Group Status Other**: If the participant's group status is not listed, select "Other" and enter a brief description.
5. Add any additional comments or notes about the participant.
