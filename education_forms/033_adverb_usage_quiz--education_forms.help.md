# Adverb Usage Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to test your knowledge of adverbs, their definitions, classification, and usage in sentences. It will help assess your understanding of adverbs and their role in language.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the student information section at the beginning of the form. This includes your name, ID, class, and date of taking the quiz.
2. Move on to the Adverb Definition Questions section, where you will be asked to define an adverb and identify the word class it typically modifies.
3. The Adverb Identification section contains two questions asking you to identify adverbs in sentences.
4. In the Adverb Classification section, you will be asked to classify adverbs as adverbs of manner, time, place, or frequency.
5. The Adverb Comparison section contains questions about the comparative and superlative forms of adverbs.
6. Finally, the Sentence Construction section requires you to create two sentences using adverbs of place and frequency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information -- Student Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Student Information -- Student ID**: Enter your student identification number assigned to you by your school or institution.
* **Class Section**: Type the name of your class or section.
* **Quiz Date**: Enter the date you are taking the quiz.
* **Adverb Definition Questions -- What is an adverb?**: Define what an adverb is in a few words.
* **Adverb Definition Questions -- Which word class do adverbs typically modify?**: Choose all the word classes that adverbs usually modify. You can select multiple options.
* **Adverb Identification -- Identify the adverb in this sentence - 'She sings beautifully.'**: Identify the adverb in the given sentence.
* **Adverb Identification -- Identify the adverb in this sentence - 'He ran quickly to catch the bus.'**: Identify the adverb in the given sentence.
* **Adverb Classification -- What type of adverb is 'slowly' in the sentence 'He walked slowly.'?**: Choose the correct category of adverb for the given word.
* **Adverb Classification -- What type of adverb is 'yesterday' in the sentence 'We went hiking yesterday.'?**: Choose the correct category of adverb for the given word.
* **Adverb Comparison -- What is the comparative form of the adverb 'well'?**: Provide the comparative form of the given adverb.
* **Adverb Comparison -- What is the superlative form of the adverb 'carefully'?**: Provide the superlative form of the given adverb.
* **Sentence Construction -- Construct a sentence using an adverb of place**: Create a sentence that uses an adverb indicating place.
* **Sentence Construction -- Construct a sentence using an adverb of frequency**: Create a sentence that uses an adverb indicating frequency.
