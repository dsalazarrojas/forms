# Free Math Tutoring - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Free Math Tutoring" form is designed to collect information from math tutors or educators who want to offer their services online. This form is used to gather details about their qualifications, availability, and services offered.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all the required fields, which are marked as required in the legend below.
2. Fill in the student grade and math subject fields to specify your qualifications and specialty.
3. Select the days you are available to tutor.
4. Choose how you would like to be contacted (phone, email, or in-person).
5. Provide a description of yourself and your tutoring experience.
6. Select a price range for your services (free or paid).
7. Fill in your contact phone and email (if applicable).
8. Choose a tool for online meetings (Google Meet, Zoom, or Skype).
9. Provide additional information (if needed).
10. Review all the fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
