# Dog Behavior Training Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your dog's behavior and training history to help our consultants better understand your dog's needs and provide effective behavior training consultations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each section with as much detail as possible. You may skip fields that are not relevant to your situation, but please make sure to answer all required fields (indicated by an asterisk *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Home Environment** (`home_environment`, text, optional): Please describe your dog's home environment, including living space, furniture, and any other relevant details.
* **Previous Bites** (`previous_bites`, text, required): List any previous bites or incidents of aggression.
* **Social Status** (`social_status`, text, optional): Describe your dog's social status, including socialization with other dogs and humans.
* **Separation Anxiety** (`separation_anxiety`, text, optional): Describe any separation anxiety your dog may experience.
* **House Trained** (`house_trained`, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether your dog is house trained or not.
* **Training Methods** (`training_methods`, text, optional): Describe any previous training methods used on your dog.
* **Previous Treats** (`previous_treats`, text, optional): List any previous treats used for training.
* **Separation Stress** (`separation_stress`, number, optional): Estimate how much stress your dog experiences when left alone.
* **Escape Attempts** (`escape_attempts`, select_one, required): Indicate whether your dog has attempted to escape or has a history of escape attempts.
* **Previous Escapes** (`previous_escapes`, text, optional): Describe any previous escape attempts.
* **Socialization** (`socialization`, text, optional): Describe your dog's socialization, including interactions with other dogs and humans.
* **Previous Training** (`previous_training`, text, optional): Describe any previous training methods or experiences.
* **Previous Treats** (`previous_treats`, text, optional): Describe any previous treats used for training.
* **Training Level** (`training_level`, select_one, required): Select your dog's current training level (Basic, Intermediate, Advanced).
* **Training Method** (`training_method`, text, optional): Describe your dog's current training method.
* **Training Frequency** (`training_frequency`, number, optional): Estimate how often your dog receives training.
* **Escape Frequency** (`escape_frequency`, number, optional): Estimate how often your dog attempts to escape.
* **Social Frequency** (`social_frequency`, number, optional): Estimate how often your dog interacts with other dogs or humans.
* **Previous Behavioral Issues** (`previous_behavioral_issues`, text, optional): Describe any previous behavioral issues with your dog.
* **Previous Medication** (`previous_medication`, text, optional): Describe any previous medication used on your dog.
* **Training Hours** (`training_hours`, number, optional): Estimate how many hours your dog receives training per session.
* **Social Hours** (`social_hours`, number, optional): Estimate how many hours your dog spends interacting with other dogs or humans per day.
* **Escape Hours** (`escape_hours`, number, optional): Estimate how many hours your dog attempts to escape per day.
* **Training Days** (`training_days`, number, optional): Estimate how many days your dog receives training per week.
* **Social Days** (`social_days`, number, optional): Estimate how many days your dog spends interacting with other dogs or humans per week.
