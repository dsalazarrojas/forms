# Sex Education Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sex Education Survey is a form designed to collect information on users' knowledge and attitudes towards sex education.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question on the survey pages, making sure to fill in all required fields.
2. Select the correct options for select one and select multiple questions.
3. Enter your answer in the text fields.
4. Fill in the number field if required.
5. Note any additional information in the note field if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **sex_education_survey** (Text, required): This field is a free-form text area where you can provide any information related to sex education.

- **sex_survey_page_2** (Select multiple, required): Choose all that apply from the options provided. This question is likely asking about your current understanding or knowledge of sex education.

- **sex_education** (Text, required): Describe your thoughts or experiences related to sex education.

- **sex_education_survey_page_3** (Select one, required): Choose the option that best applies to your current situation or understanding. This question is likely asking about your awareness or knowledge of certain aspects of sex education.

- **sex_education_survey_page_4** (Select multiple, required): Choose all that apply from the options provided. This question is likely asking about your current understanding or knowledge of various aspects of sex education.

- **sex_education_survey_page_5** (Text, required): Describe your thoughts or experiences related to sex education.

- **sex_education_survey_page_6** (Number, required): Enter a numerical value or answer as requested. This question is likely asking for a specific numerical answer related to sex education.

- **sex_education_survey_page_7** (Note, required): Provide additional information or context as requested. This field is for any additional comments or explanations.

- **sex_education_survey_page_8** (Text, required): Provide any final thoughts or summaries related to sex education.
