# Email And Narrative Writing Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your understanding of email and narrative writing best practices.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer the questions honestly, and try to complete each section accurately.
- For the email body section, provide a brief description of the email's purpose and context.
- For the narrative writing section, tell a short story that illustrates your writing skills.
- For other sections, select the correct options or answer with a brief description.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Formal Email Writing** (`email_etiquette`, select_multiple, required: false): Answer 'Yes' if you know it's essential to write formal emails in a professional setting.
* **Email Body** (`email_body`, text, required: false): Briefly describe the email's purpose and context (e.g., request, announcement, inquiry).
* **Narrative Writing** (`narrative_writing`, text, required: false): Tell a short story that shows your writing skills, such as a personal anecdote, a fictional tale, or a descriptive paragraph.
* **CCs** (`email_cc`, select_one, required: false): Answer 'Yes' if you usually CC recipients in your emails.
* **BCCs** (`email_bcc`, select_one, required: false): Answer 'Yes' if you usually BCC recipients in your emails.
* **Subject** (`email_subject`, text, required: false): Briefly describe the email's subject (e.g., meeting request, project update).
* **To** (`email_to`, text, required: false): Enter the recipient's name, email, or department (e.g., John Smith, john.smith@example.com, Sales Department).
* **From** (`email_from`, text, required: false): Enter your name, email, or department (e.g., Jane Doe, janie.doe@example.com, Marketing Department).
* **Attachments** (`attachment`, select_multiple, required: false): Answer 'Yes' if you usually attach files to your emails.
* **Email Signature** (`email_signature`, text, required: false): Enter a short description of your email signature (e.g., company logo, contact information).

## Tips
- Use clear and concise language in your answers.
- Be creative and showcase your writing skills in the narrative writing section.
- Proofread your answers carefully to ensure accuracy.
- If unsure about any section, refer to email and narrative writing best practices guidelines.
