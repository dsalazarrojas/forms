# Open Curriculum Design Fellowship Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Open Curriculum Design Fellowship Registration form is a crucial part of the application process for the Open Curriculum Design Fellowship program. This form is used to collect essential information from applicants to evaluate their eligibility and assess their suitability for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form successfully, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information ready before starting the form.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Be truthful in your responses, as the information provided will be reviewed carefully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details** (`User Details`, `text`, required): Please provide your name and other personal details as per your identity.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, required): Enter your contact information, including your address, phone number, and email address, to ensure we can reach you for further communication.
* **Program Information** (`Program Information`, `text`, required): Share your relevant work experience, education, or other background information that supports your application.
* **Agreement & Terms** (`Agreement & Terms`, `text`, required): Carefully read and agree to the terms and conditions of the fellowship program.
* **Select One** (`Select One`, `select_one`, required): Choose 'Yes' if you have the necessary skills or experience for the fellowship, or 'No' if you don't. If 'Other', please explain in the notes section.
* **Select Multiple** (`Select Multiple`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all relevant options for the fellowship that apply to you.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to receive important updates and notifications about the fellowship.
* **Date** (`Date`, `date`, required): Provide the date you are available to start the fellowship program.
* **Time** (`Time`, `time`, required): Enter the time you are available to start the fellowship program.
* **Note** (`Note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or information that you feel is relevant to your application.
* **Review and Submit** (`Review and Submit`, `text`, required): Double-check your information and submit the form to complete the registration process.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Application Attachments** (`Application Attachments`, `text`, optional): Attach relevant documents or files that support your application.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, `email`, optional): Enter an alternate email address for further communication.
* **Date 2** (`Date 2`, `date`, optional): Provide an alternate date you are available to start the fellowship program.
* **Time 2** (`Time 2`, `time`, optional): Enter an alternate time you are available to start the fellowship program.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, optional): Enter an alternate phone number for further communication.

Please note that all required fields must be filled out before submitting the form. If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact us for further assistance.
