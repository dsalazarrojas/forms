# Syllabus Sign Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Syllabus Sign Off Form is a document used for recording course attendance and obtaining signatures from students and instructors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and date in the "Student Signature" field to confirm that you have attended the course.
2. Provide any comments or feedback in the "Student/Instructor Comments" field.
3. Enter the course details in the "Course Details" field.
4. Enter the course instructor's name in the "Course Instructor" field.
5. Select the date of the course in the "Course Date" field.
6. Enter the start and end times of the course in the "Start Time" and "End Time" fields.
7. Enter any comments or feedback about the course in the "Course Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Signature** (`Student Signature`, `text`, required): Sign your name to confirm that you have attended the course.
* **Course Details** (`course_details`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the course, instructor's name, and any other details about the course you wish to record.
* **Course Instructor** (`course_instructor`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the instructor who taught the course.
* **Course Date** (`course_date`, `date`, required): Select the date of the course you attended.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, `time`, required): Enter the start time of the course.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, `time`, required): Enter the end time of the course.
* **Student/Instructor Comments** (`student_instructor_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments or feedback about the course.
* **Course Comments** (`course_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments or feedback about the course you wish to record.
* **Signature Request** (`signature_request`, `text`, optional): Enter your signature request.
* **Course Comments** (`course_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback about the course you wish to record.
* **Student/Instructor Comments** (`student_instructor_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments or feedback from student or instructor.

## Tips
* Make sure to sign your name in the "Student Signature" field to confirm that you have attended the course.
* Enter accurate and detailed information about the course to ensure that the record is complete.
* If you have any comments or feedback about the course, enter them in the "Student/Instructor Comments" field.
* Make sure to enter your signature request in the "Signature Request" field if you require a signature from the student or instructor.
