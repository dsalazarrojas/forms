# Student Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Form is a collection of fields used to gather information from students. This form is designed to provide the necessary details to identify and contact students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields and provide any additional details you feel are relevant for the staff or institution.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **name** (text, required): Please fill in the student's name as it appears on their official documents.
- **surname** (text, required): Enter the student's surname.
- **date_of_birth** (date, required): Enter the student's date of birth in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
- **email** (email, required): Enter the student's official email address.
- **phone** (text, optional): Enter the student's phone number if you wish to contact them.
- **address** (text, optional): Enter the student's address for correspondence or other purposes.
- **student_id** (number, optional): Enter a unique student identification number if applicable.
- **student_form** (text, optional): This field is currently empty and is not required. If you have any additional comments or information to share, you may use this field at your discretion.
