# Social Emotional Learning Curriculum Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from Social Emotional Learning (SEL) providers for the curriculum application process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely, providing your name, experience, email, phone number, and consent to share data.
2. Select your provider type from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Provider Name** (`provider_name`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter your name or name of your organization.
* **Years of Experience** (`experience_years`, `number`, optional):
	+ If you have experience working with students, please enter the number of years you have been working with them.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional):
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Consent to Share Data** (`consent_to_share_data`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Confirm that you are okay with sharing data related to your application.
* **Provider Type** (`provider_type`, `select_multiple`, optional):
	+ Select your provider type from the options provided: school, nonprofit, or provider.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you have any issues or questions, please reach out to the relevant authorities for assistance.
