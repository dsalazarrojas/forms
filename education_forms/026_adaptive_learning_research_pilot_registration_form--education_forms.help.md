# Adaptive Learning Research Pilot Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adaptive Learning Research Pilot Registration Form is designed for researchers to register participants for an Adaptive Learning Research Pilot. The form will gather essential information about participants, including their demographics, educational experience, and technology familiarity. This information will help us understand how adaptive learning technologies can be effectively implemented in educational settings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the instructions carefully and ensure you understand the purpose and requirements of the form.
2. Fill in your **Full Name** in the corresponding field.
3. Enter your **Primary Email Address** to ensure we can contact you for any updates or further instructions.
4. Select your **Institutional / School Affiliation** from the dropdown menu.
5. Choose your **Current Role in Education** from the options provided:
	* K-12 Teacher
	* University Professor / Instructor
	* Educational Administrator
	* Instructional Designer
	* Research Student
	* Other
6. Enter your **Years of Teaching / Educational Experience** in the number field.
7. Rate your **Adaptive Technology Familiarity** from 1 (No experience) to 5 (Expert user).
8. Select your **Primary LMS currently used** from the dropdown menu:
	* Canvas
	* Moodle
	* Blackboard
	* Google Classroom
	* Other
	* None
9. Write a brief description of why you are interested in this research pilot in the **Pilot Motivation** field.
10. Review and agree to the **Informed Consent** and **Data Usage Consent** terms.
11. Finally, confirm your participation in the **Participation Agreement** and **Minor Protection Acknowledgement** sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: This is your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Primary Email Address**: Enter your official email address that we can use to contact you for updates or further instructions.
* **Institutional / School Affiliation**: This is the name of the school or institution you are affiliated with.
* **Current Role in Education**: Select the role that best describes your position in the education sector.
* **Years of Teaching / Educational Experience**: Enter the number of years you have been teaching or working in the educational sector.
* **Adaptive Technology Familiarity**: Rate your level of expertise with adaptive learning technologies.
* **Primary LMS currently used**: Select the Learning Management System you are currently using.
* **Pilot Motivation**: Briefly describe why you are interested in this research pilot.
* **Informed Consent**: Agree to the terms and conditions of the research pilot, including the use of your anonymized data.
* **Data Usage Consent**: Agree to the use of your anonymized data for research purposes.
* **Participation Agreement**: Confirm your participation in the weekly feedback sessions for the 8-week pilot.
* **Minor Protection Acknowledgement**: Certify that you will not share sensitive student data during this pilot.
* **Date of Registration**: This field will be auto-filled with the current date.

Note: Please fill in the form accurately and honestly. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for clarification.
