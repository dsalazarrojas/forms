# Course Selection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from the user about their course selection preferences. It is an essential part of the educational system, and filling out this form will help us get to know you better and provide you with the most suitable course options.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select a subject from the options provided in the "Select Subject" field.
2. Provide your preferred start date for the course in the "Start Date" field.
3. If applicable, provide your preferred end date for the course in the "End Date" field.
4. Answer the question about having a side project in the "Have Side Project" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Subject**: This is a dropdown menu to choose the subject you are interested in.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date you prefer to start the course. This can be in the format of MM/DD/YYYY or YYYY/MM/DD.
* **End Date**: If you have a specific end date in mind, enter it here. Leave this field blank if you don't have a preferred end date.
* **Have Side Project**: Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you have a side project that you will be working on during the course.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for us to get in touch with you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for us to get in touch with you.

## Tips
* Please make sure to double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need help with any field, feel free to ask.
* We will review your form submission and get back to you shortly.
