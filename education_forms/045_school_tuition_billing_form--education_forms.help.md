# School Tuition Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Tuition Billing Form is a document used to record and track student payment information. This form is essential for school administrators to keep accurate records of student tuition payments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter the student's ID number in the "Student ID" field.
3. Select the payment date using the "Payment Date" field.
4. Select the payment method used from the options provided in the "Payment Method" field.
5. Enter the tuition amount paid in the "Tuition Amount" field.
6. Enter the fee amount paid in the "Fee Amount" field.
7. Choose the payment status from the options in the "Payment Status" field.
8. Add any relevant notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
9. Enter the payment reference number in the "Payment Reference" field.
10. Enter the student's email address in the "Student Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter the name of the student making the payment.
* **Student ID**: Enter the unique ID number assigned to the student.
* **Payment Date**: Enter the date of the payment. This can be in any date format accepted by the system.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method used to make the payment (Visa, MasterCard, American Express).
* **Tuition Amount**: Enter the amount of the tuition paid.
* **Fee Amount**: Enter the amount of the fee paid.
* **Payment Status**: Choose the status of the payment (Paid, Pending, Overdue).
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes related to the payment.
* **Payment Reference**: Enter any reference number associated with the payment.
* **Student Email**: Enter the email address of the student making the payment.
