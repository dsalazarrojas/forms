# social_innovation_in_learning_cohort_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registering applicants to a social innovation in learning cohort. It is intended to collect necessary personal and contact information from applicants.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email** in the required fields. Please use a valid email address that you can be contacted on.
2. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Select the type of application from the **Application Type** dropdown menu.
4. If applicable, attach a file for upload in the **File Upload** field.
5. Enter your **Address**, **City**, and **State** and **Postal Code** for your contact information.
6. Enter your **Phone** number for communication.
7. If you are submitting an application on behalf of someone else, select the corresponding option in the **Submitter** field.
8. Enter your **Submitter Information** and attach any supporting files (if necessary).
9. Finally, add a digital **Signature** and the **Date Signed** and **Time Signed**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant First Name** (`applicant_first_name`, text, required): This is your first name.
* **Applicant Last Name** (`applicant_last_name`, text, required): This is your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): This is your contact email address.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Application Type** (`application_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of application from the dropdown menu.
* **Application Form** (`application_form`, note, optional): This field is not required, but you can use it to provide additional information.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter your city.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter your state.
* **Postal Code** (`postal_code`, text, required): Enter your postal code.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, note, optional): This field is not required, but you can use it to provide additional information.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, select_multiple, required): If you are submitting on behalf of someone else, select the corresponding option.
* **Submitter Info** (`submitter_info`, text, required): Enter information about the person you are submitting on behalf of.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): This is a digital signature.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, optional): Enter the date you signed this form.
* **Time Signed** (`time_signed`, time, optional): Enter the time you signed this form.
* **Timestamp** (`timestamp`, text, optional): This field is not required, but you can use it to track when the form was submitted.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This is an additional email address.
* **File Upload** (`file_upload`, text, required): Attach a file for upload.
* **File** (`file`, text, required): Attach a file.
* **File 1** (`file1`, text, optional): This field is not required, but you can use it to attach another file.
* **File 2** (`file2`, text, optional): This field is not required, but you can use it to attach another file.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter a date.
