# Graduation Eligibility Validation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to validate a student's graduation eligibility status and track their progress through the academic program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
*   Enter the student's personal details, such as First Name, Last Name, and Date of Birth.
*   Enter the student's graduation details, such as Graduation Date, Graduation Status, and Graduation Requirements.
*   Indicate the student's Graduation Requirements Met status.
*   List any outstanding requirements, if applicable.
*   Enter the status and details of any outstanding requirements.
*   Indicate whether the student has met all outstanding requirements.
*   Enter the Program Requirements of the student's current academic program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false)
    Enter the student's first name.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false)
    Enter the student's last name.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false)
    Enter the student's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
*   **Graduation Date** (`graduation_date`, `date`, required: false)
    Enter the date when the student graduated or expects to graduate.
*   **Graduation Status** (`graduation_status`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
    Select the student's graduation status (Pending, Graduated, Incomplete).
*   **Program Name** (`program_name`, `text`, required: false)
    Enter the name of the student's current academic program.
*   **GPA** (`gpa`, `number`, required: false)
    Enter the student's current GPA.
*   **Student ID** (`student_id`, `text`, required: false)
    Enter the student's ID number.
*   **Graduation Requirements** (`graduation_requirements`, `text`, required: false)
    Enter a brief description of the graduation requirements.
*   **Graduation Eligibility Date** (`graduation_eligibility_date`, `date`, required: false)
    Enter the date when the student became eligible for graduation.
*   **Graduation Requirements Met** (`graduation_requirements_met`, `select_one`, required: false)
    Indicate whether the student has met all graduation requirements.
*   **Outstanding Requirement** (`outstanding_requirement`, `text`, required: false)
    Enter a brief description of any outstanding requirement.
*   **Outstanding Requirement Status** (`outstanding_requirement_status`, `text`, required: false)
    Enter the status of the outstanding requirement.
*   **Outstanding Requirement Details** (`outstanding_requirement_details`, `text`, required: false)
    Enter a brief description of the outstanding requirement.
*   **Outstanding Requirements** (`outstanding_requirements`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
    Indicate whether the student has any outstanding requirements.
*   **Program Requirements** (`program_requirements`, `text`, required: false)
    Enter a brief description of the graduation requirements of the student's current academic program.
