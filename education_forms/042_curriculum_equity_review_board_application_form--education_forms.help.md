# Curriculum Equity Review Board Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from applicants applying to the Curriculum Equity Review Board for review and decision-making purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your personal details, including your name, phone number, and email address.
2. Enter your school name, address, and the years you attended.
3. If you are applying for someone else, fill in their name, phone number, and relationship to you.
4. Make sure to select the correct relationship type from the options provided.
5. Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Applicant Details
* **Applicant Details**: 
	+ Enter your name and any other personal details you want to provide about yourself.
	+ This field is not mandatory.

### Address
* **Address**: 
	+ Enter your school address.
	+ This field is not mandatory.

### Email
* **Email**: 
	+ Enter your email address.
	+ This field is not mandatory.

### Phone
* **Phone**: 
	+ Enter your phone number.
	+ This field is not mandatory.

### School Name
* **School Name**: 
	+ Enter your school name.
	+ This field is not mandatory.

### School Address
* **School Address**: 
	+ Enter your school address.
	+ This field is not mandatory.

### Years Attended
* **Years Attended**: 
	+ Enter the years you attended your school.
	+ This field is not mandatory.

### Referee Details
* **Name**: 
	+ Enter the name of the referee who is recommending you.
	+ This field is not mandatory.

* **Position**: 
	+ Enter the position or title of the referee.
	+ This field is not mandatory.

* **Phone**: 
	+ Enter the phone number of the referee.
	+ This field is not mandatory.

* **Relationship**: 
	+ Select the type of relationship between you and the referee.
	+ This field is mandatory.

### Relationship Type
* **Relationship**: 
	+ Choose from the options: Family, Friend, Colleague.
	+ This field is mandatory and is used to determine the relationship between you and the referee.
