# Engineering Calculus Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Engineering Calculus Quiz is designed to test students' understanding of calculus concepts, including limits, derivatives, and integrals. The quiz is intended to be a comprehensive assessment of their knowledge in this area.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the quiz, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Limit Questions" field by providing your answers in the text box.
2. Select the correct answer for each of the derivative questions in the "Derivative Questions" field.
3. Answer the integral questions in the "Integral Questions" field.
4. Choose the assessment strategy from the options provided in the "Assessment Strategy" field.
5. Select the correct answer for each of the answer questions (Answer 1, Answer 2, Answer 3, Answer 4, and Answer 5).
6. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "Notes" field.
7. Fill out the "Submit Date" field with the date of submission.
8. Enter the "Submit Time" field with the time of submission.
9. Provide your email address in the "Submit Email" field.
10. Enter your phone number in the "Submit Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Limit Questions** (`limit_questions`, text, required: false): Enter your answers to the limit questions in this text box. This field is intended for you to provide your thoughts or solutions to the limit questions.
* **Derivative Questions** (`derivative_questions`, text, required: false): Provide your answers to the derivative questions in this text box.
* **Integral Questions** (`integral_questions`, text, required: false): Answer the integral questions in this text box.
* **Assessment Strategy** (`assessment_strategy`, select_one, required: false): Select the assessment strategy that you used to complete this quiz. Options include:
	+ Limits
	+ Derivatives
	+ Integrals
	+ Other
* **Answer 1** (`answer_1`, select_one, required: false): Select the correct answer for the first question.
* **Answer 2** (`answer_2`, select_one, required: false): Choose the correct answer for the second question.
* **Answer 3** (`answer_3`, select_one, required: false): Select the correct answer for the third question.
* **Answer 4** (`answer_4`, select_one, required: false): Choose the correct answer for the fourth question.
* **Answer 5** (`answer_5`, select_one, required: false): Select the correct answer for the fifth question.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes about your submission.
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of submission.
* **Submit Time** (`submit_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of submission.
* **Submit Email** (`submit_email`, email, required: false): Provide your email address.
* **Submit Phone** (`submit_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
