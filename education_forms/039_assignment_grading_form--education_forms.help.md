# Assignment Grading Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Assignment Grading Form is a comprehensive tool used for evaluating student assignments. Its primary function is to collect detailed feedback and scores from graders, which helps in calculating overall grades and providing actionable feedback to students. 

## How To Complete This Form
### Page 1: Grader and Assignment Details
- **Grader Name** (`Grader Name`, text, required): Enter your name, which is used to identify you as the grader.
- **Grader ID** (`Grader ID`, text, required): Enter your employee ID.
- **Grading Date** (`Grading Date`, date, required): Select the date you are grading this assignment.
- **Course Name** (`Course Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the course this assignment belongs to.
- **Course Code** (`Course Code`, text, required): Enter the ID of the course.
- **Assignment Title** (`Assignment Title`, text, required): Enter the name of the assignment.
- **Assignment Type** (`Assignment Type`, select_one, required): Select the category of the assignment (e.g., Homework, Quiz, Exam, etc.).
- **Total Points Possible** (`Total Points Possible`, number, required): Enter the maximum score for this assignment.
- **Points Earned** (`Points Earned`, number, required): Enter the score the student earned.
- **Percentage Score** (`Percentage Score`, number, required): Calculate the percentage score based on earned points.
- **Letter Grade** (`Letter Grade`, select_one, required): Select the letter grade (A, A-, B+, B, etc.) corresponding to the percentage score.
- **Grader Signature** (`Grader Signature`, text, required): Enter your name as a signature.

### Page 2: Quality Assessments
- **Content Quality Rating** (`Content Quality Rating`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of the assignment content.
- **Organization Rating** (`Organization Rating`, select_one, required): Rate how well the assignment is organized.
- **Grammar and Mechanics Rating** (`Grammar and Mechanics Rating`, select_one, required): Rate the writing quality.
- **Creativity and Originality Rating** (`Creativity and Originality Rating`, select_one, required): Rate how creative the assignment is.
- **Effort Demonstrated Rating** (`Effort Demonstrated Rating`, select_one, required): Rate the level of effort shown.
- **Content Quality Rating** (`Content Quality Rating`, text, optional): Enter a specific rating for Content Quality if you want to.
- **Organization Rating** (`Organization Rating`, text, optional): Enter a specific rating for Organization if you want to.
- **Grammar and Mechanics Rating** (`Grammar and Mechanics Rating`, text, optional): Enter a specific rating for Grammar and Mechanics if you want to.
- **Creativity and Originality Rating** (`Creativity and Originality Rating`, text, optional): Enter a specific rating for Creativity and Originality if you want to.
- **Effort Demonstrated Rating** (`Effort Demonstrated Rating`, text, optional): Enter a specific rating for Effort Demonstrated if you want to.

### Page 3: Additional Feedback
- **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional feedback or comments about the assignment.
- **Resubmission Allowed** (`Resubmission Allowed`, select_one, required): Select if resubmission is allowed or not.
- **Resubmission Deadline** (`Resubmission Deadline`, date, optional): Enter the deadline for resubmission if allowed.
- **Strengths of This Assignment** (`Strengths of This Assignment`, text, required): Enter what went well with this assignment.
- **Areas for Improvement** (`Areas for Improvement`, text, required): Enter what could be improved upon.
- **Student ID** (`Student ID`, text, required): Enter the student's ID.
- **Student Name** (`Student Name`, text, required): Enter the student's full name.
- **Student ID** (`Student ID`, text, optional): Enter the student's ID again (if necessary).

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out each field accurately and completely, as it will aid in the overall assessment and feedback process.
- If you are unsure about a field, use the default options or 'No Grade' if necessary.
- Ensure that you sign this form with your name as a grader to confirm your evaluation.
- Review the form carefully to ensure accuracy before submission.
