# scholarship_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from students for a scholarship application.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out all required fields marked as "required: true"
- Ensure that you have uploaded all necessary files, such as pictures and signature/document, if applicable.
- Review your form for accuracy and completeness before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Personal Details
* **Personal Details (Personal Details)** (Required): This field is used to gather your personal details such as your name, address, etc. Please fill out this field completely and accurately.

### 2. Upload Pictures
* **Upload Pictures (Upload Pictures)** (Optional): This field is used to upload pictures or images that are relevant to your application. Please ensure that the uploaded files are in the correct format and are not too large.

### 3. References
* **References (Select Multiple)** (Required): List your references or recommenders who can vouch for your academic abilities. Please ensure that you select all relevant references.

### 4. What's An Interesting Idea About You
* **What's An Interesting Idea About You (What's An Interesting Idea About You)** (Required): Share an interesting or unique idea about yourself. Please be creative and concise in your answer.

### 5. List Of Books You've Read
* **List Of Books You've Read (Select Multiple)** (Required): Indicate which books you have read and enjoyed. Please select all relevant books.

### 6. Essay Question
* **Essay Question (Essay Question)** (Required): This field is used for you to answer a question related to the scholarship you are applying for. Please answer the question accurately and thoroughly.

### 7. Upload Your Signature
* **Upload Your Signature (Upload Your Signature)** (Optional): If required, please upload your signature as per the specified format.

### 8. Upload Document
* **Upload Document (Upload Document)** (Optional): If required, please upload a document as per the specified format.

Please note that the review process may take some time, so please allow for a few days for the review process to complete.
