# Educational Leadership Think Tank Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals interested in participating in the educational leadership program. It collects information about your background, motivation, and application. Please provide accurate and detailed information to help us understand your eligibility and suitability for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully read the instructions before starting.
- Fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Ensure you have a valid email address for communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first-name** (`first_name`, text, required):
  First name.
* **last-name** (`last_name`, text, required):
  Last name.
* **role** (`role`, select, required):
  Select one or more of the available roles (Teacher, Principal/Dean, Superintendent/Dean, Other).
* **School Name** (`school-name`, text, required):
  Name of the school you are associated with or the school you would like to represent.
* **email** (`email`, email, required):
  A valid email address for communication and follow-up.
* **Motivation Statement** (`motivation_statement`, text, required):
  Briefly explain why you are interested in the program and how you plan to contribute to it.
* **Motivation Justification** (`motivation_statement_justification`, text, required):
  Provide additional details about your motivation and how you will benefit from the program.
* **CV Upload** (`cv`, text, required):
  Upload your curriculum vitae (CV) as a supporting document.
* **Application Statement** (`application_statement`, text, required):
  Write a statement explaining why you are a strong candidate and what you can bring to the program.
* **Other Role** (`other-role`, select multiple, optional):
  Select additional roles you identify with that are not listed (e.g., Other).
* **Other School Name** (`other-school-name`, text, optional):
  Name of the school you associate with or represent if different from the school in the "School Name" field.

## Tips
- Please proofread your application before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
- Attach your CV as a supporting document to your application.
