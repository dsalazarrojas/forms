# Aquarium Educational Program Lead Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Aquarium Educational Program Lead Interest Form is a necessary step for schools, groups, or other educational institutions to express their interest in hosting an aquarium educational program. By filling out this form, you will provide essential information to help us tailor the program to meet your specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the contact information section with your institution's details.
2. Select your institution type (e.g., K-12 school, university, community center, etc.).
3. Provide the number of students you plan to bring to the program.
4. Choose the type of program you are interested in (e.g., school field trip, virtual program, etc.).
5. Optionally, you can provide additional information about the program, such as the focus or subject area.
6. If necessary, let us know about any special needs or accommodations we should be aware of.
7. Finally, select the preferred visit dates for the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information**: <code>Provide your contact name, email address, and phone number so we can get in touch with you to confirm the program details.</code>
* **Contact Name**: <code>The name of the person filling out this form, typically the lead contact person for the institution.</code>
* **Email Address**: <code>Your institution's email address, where we will send program information and updates.</code>
* **Phone Number**: <code>Your institution's phone number, where we will reach out to confirm the program details.</code>
* **School or Group Information**: <code>Optional information about the school or group, such as its address or description.</code>
* **Institution or Organization Name**: <code>Provide the name of your institution or organization, such as a school, university, or community center.</code>
* **Institution Type**: <code>Select the type of institution or organization you represent, such as K-12 school, university, etc.</code>
* **Number of Students**: <code>The number of students you plan to bring to the program.</code>
* **Grade Levels**: <code>Optional information about the grade levels served by your institution, such as elementary, middle school, high school, etc.</code>
* **Program Interest**: <code>Optional information about the program's focus or subject area, such as marine biology, conservation, etc.</code>
* **Type of Program Interested In**: <code>Select one or more program types you are interested in, such as school field trip, virtual program, etc.</code>
* **Learning Focus or Subject Area**: <code>Optional information about the subject area or focus of the program.</code>
* **Preferred Visit Dates**: <code>The dates you prefer to visit the aquarium, considering our availability and program schedule.</code>
* **Any Special Needs or Accommodations**: <code>If you have any special needs or accommodations, such as wheelchair accessibility, please let us know so we can prepare accordingly.</code>
