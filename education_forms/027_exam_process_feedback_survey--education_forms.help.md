# exam_process_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback on a student's experience with an examination process, aiming to improve future exam processes and provide valuable insights to exam administrators.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Please take a few minutes to answer the questions below. Your feedback is crucial in helping us understand and improve the examination process.
*   If you are unsure or have no opinion, please select "Don't know" or leave it blank.
*   You can provide as much detail as you feel is necessary for each question.
*   When providing feedback, try to be as specific as possible, so we can understand your point.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **How was your examination experience?** (`introduction`, `note`, required: false) This field is a note section where you can describe your overall experience with the examination. Please be as concise as possible, but provide enough detail to give us an understanding of your opinion.
*   **Was the examination process clear and well-organized?** (`exam_process`, `select_one`, required: false) Select one of the options provided: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor. If you find the process confusing or unclear, please provide more details in the "Do you have any suggestions or comments?" field.
*   **Were the questions clear and relevant?** (`exam_questions`, `select_multiple`, required: false) Select all that apply: Very clear, Somewhat clear, Not clear at all, or Don't know. If you found some questions unclear or not relevant, please specify which ones and why.
*   **Was the exam duration reasonable?** (`exam_duration`, `select_one`, required: false) Select 'True' if you felt the exam duration was reasonable, or 'False' if it was too long or too short. If you have suggestions, please provide them in the "Do you have any suggestions or comments?" field.
*   **Do you have any suggestions or comments?** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false) Please share any feedback, suggestions, or comments you have about the examination process. This is an opportunity to provide specific details that will help us improve.
*   **What is your name?** (`student_name`, `text`, required: false) Please provide your name so we can identify your feedback.
*   **What course were you taking during the exam?** (`course`, `text`, required: false) Please let us know which course you were taking during the exam.
*   **Where did the exam take place?** (`exam_location`, `text`, required: false) Tell us about the location of the exam.
*   **Date of examination** (`date`, `date`, required: false) Please provide the date of the examination.
