# Faculty Collaboration Accelerator Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to register faculty members for the Faculty Collaboration Accelerator program, collecting necessary information for registration and program management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each field's purpose and requirement.
2. Fill out the **General Information** page with your basic details, if applicable.
3. Proceed to the **Contact Details** page, where you will be asked to provide your contact information.
4. On the **Project Details** page, provide a detailed description of your project or the specific area of interest you would like to collaborate on.
5. Click the **Submit** button to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_information`, `text`, optional): This page is intended to gather basic information about you. If you choose to fill it, provide your name, department, or any other relevant details.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, required): Please enter your name, email address, and any other contact information you're willing to share.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, `text`, required): Describe your project, research area, or specific collaboration interest. Be as detailed as possible to help us understand your goals and objectives.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `note`, required): This is the final step to submit your registration. Once you click this button, your form will be sent to the administrators for review and follow-up.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields (*required) to avoid any delays in processing your registration.
- Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
- Provide clear and detailed information about your project or collaboration interest. This will help us get you connected with the right resources and partners.
- If you have any questions or need help, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
