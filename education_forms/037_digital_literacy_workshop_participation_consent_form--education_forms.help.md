# Digital Literacy Workshop Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information about workshop participants, ensuring a comfortable and inclusive experience for all.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out all the required fields (marked as 'required') and provide accurate information.
2. For optional fields, provide as much detail as possible to help us tailor the workshop experience.
3. Review each field carefully before submitting the form to avoid errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details** (Required)
	+ This field collects your basic contact information, which will help us keep in touch with you about the workshop.
* **Emergency Info** (Optional)
	+ In case of an emergency during the workshop, please provide us with the name and contact information of a person we can reach out to for assistance.
* **Accessibility Needs** (Optional)
	+ If you have any mobility or other impairments that may affect your participation, please select the option that best describes your needs. This will help us arrange necessary accommodations.
* **Participation Consent**
	+ Please indicate your agreement to participate in the workshop by selecting 'True'. If you have any concerns or reservations, select 'False' and we will discuss this further.
* **Contact Person** (Optional)
	+ If there's a specific person we should contact in case of an emergency or for further information, please provide their name and contact information.
* **Contact Phone** (Optional)
	+ Enter your phone number so we can reach out to you for any important workshop updates or concerns.
* **Contact Email** (Optional)
	+ Provide your email address so we can send you updates and information about the workshop.
* **Other Contact Info** (Optional)
	+ If there's any other relevant contact information you'd like to share with us, please specify.
 
## Tips
* Please double-check the information you provide to ensure it's accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us before or after submitting the form.
* We will review each submission carefully to provide an inclusive and comfortable experience for all participants.
