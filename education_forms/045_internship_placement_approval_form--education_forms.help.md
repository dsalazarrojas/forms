# Internship Placement Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Internship Placement Approval Form is used to collect and validate information about students' internship placements. This form is a critical part of managing and tracking internship placements, ensuring all necessary details are captured correctly for future reference and reporting.
 
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. **Enter Student Information**: Fill in the student's details, which include student ID number, email, and phone number. These are essential identifiers for the student and are required for processing.
2. **Capture Job Details**: Enter the job title, start and end dates of the internship, and any other necessary job information. Ensure that the dates are in a standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY) for accurate tracking and reporting.
3. **Review and Submit**: After filling in all required fields, review your submission for accuracy and completeness. Ensure that all fields are filled in correctly and that there are no errors or warnings.
 
## Field-by-Field Explanation
• **Student Information (Student ID Number)**: Required. A unique identifier for the student.
• **Student Name**: Enter the student's name.
• **Student Email**: Required. The student's email address for contact.
• **Phone**: Optional. The student's contact phone number.
• **Job Information (Job Title)**: Enter the internship job title to identify the work placement.
• **Start Date**: Enter the date the internship started, in a standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
• **End Date**: Enter the date the internship ended, in a standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
 
## Tips
- Ensure all fields are filled in accurately to avoid errors or warnings.
- Use standard date formats for job start and end dates to facilitate easy tracking and reporting.
