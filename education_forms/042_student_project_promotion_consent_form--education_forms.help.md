# Student Project Promotion Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from students and their guardians for the promotion and potential use of a student's project in a specific context. This form ensures that both the student and their guardian are aware of and agree to the terms of the promotion, including how the project will be used and shared.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Project Details**: Enter a brief description of the project, including any relevant details that you want to share with the student and their guardian.
2. **Student Info**: Provide your name, email address, and any other relevant information to contact you.
3. **Guardian Info**: If you are under 18, please provide the contact information for your guardian or parent.
4. **Contact Details**: Enter your contact details, so we can reach out to you with any questions or concerns.
5. **Student Consent**: Indicate if you give your consent to participate in the student project promotion by selecting "Yes" from the drop-down menu.
6. **Guardian Consent**: If you are under 18, indicate if your guardian gives their consent by selecting "Yes" from the drop-down menu.
7. **Project Usage**: Describe the intended use of the project, including any plans for sharing or publishing.
8. **Educational Use**: Describe if and how the project will be used for educational purposes.
9. **Other Use**: If there's any other use for the project, please describe it here.
10. **Student Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your student identification.
