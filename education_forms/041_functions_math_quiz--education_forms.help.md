# Functions Math Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a quiz to assess your knowledge of different types of functions and their properties.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of function you are familiar with from the list provided.
2. For each question, select all the attributes that apply to the function.
3. Enter your First Name, Last Name, and Email (if you want to be notified of your results).
4. You can add any additional comments you'd like to provide context or clarify your answers.
5. Click the "Submit" button to submit your quiz.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your official ID.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your surname.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your valid email address to receive your results.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number to allow us to contact you regarding your results.
* **Function Type** (`function_type`, select_one, required): Select the function type you are familiar with from the list provided:
	+ Quadratic
	+ Cubic
	+ Linear
	+ Exponential
	+ Logarithmic
	+ Polynomial
	+ Trigonometric
	+ Rational
	+ Absolute Value
* **Question 1** (`question_1`, select_multiple, required): Select all the attributes that apply to the function regarding:
	+ What is the domain and range of the function?
	+ What is the inverse of the function?
	+ What is the vertex of the function?
	+ What is the value of a function?
	+ What is the derivative of the function?
* **Question 2** (`question_2`, select_multiple, required): Select all the attributes that apply to the function regarding:
	+ What is the equation of the function?
	+ What is the graph of the function?
	+ What is the value of a function?
	+ What is the inverse of the function?
	+ What is the derivative of the function?
	+ What is the domain of the function?
	+ What is the range of the function?
* **Question 3** (`question_3`, select_multiple, required): Select all the attributes that apply to the function regarding:
	+ What is the graph of a function?
	+ What is the equation of the function?
	+ What is the value of a function?
	+ What is the inverse of the function?
	+ What is the domain of the function?
* **Submit** (`submit`, note, required/optional): Click to submit your answers and receive your results.
* **Additional comments** (`comment`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or context about your answers.
