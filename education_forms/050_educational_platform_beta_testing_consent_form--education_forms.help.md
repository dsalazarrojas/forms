# Educational Platform Beta Testing Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from beta testers who want to provide feedback on our new educational platform. By completing this form, you'll be able to participate in our beta testing process and help us improve the platform.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please make sure you have the necessary information and consent to fill out this form.
2. Fill out each field according to the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`participant_details`, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like it to appear on our records.
* **Email** (`participant_details`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you for feedback and updates.
* **Age** (`participant_details`, number, required): Enter your age to help us understand our audience demographics.
* **Beta Testing Agreement** (`beta_testing_agreement`, text, required): Read and agree to our beta testing agreement terms, which include the understanding that your feedback will be kept confidential.
* **Beta Testing Expectations** (`beta_testing_expectations`, text, required): Review our beta testing expectations, which explain how we'll use your feedback and what you can expect from the beta testing process.
* **Contact Information** (`participant_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional contact information you'd like to share with us.
* **Phone Number** (`participant_details`, text, required): Enter a phone number we can use to contact you if needed.
* **Address** (`participant_details`, text, required): Enter a valid address we can use to ship any materials related to the beta testing process.
 
## Tips

* Please make sure to answer all required fields to ensure your participation in the beta testing process.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions or have any questions, feel free to contact us at [insert contact information].
