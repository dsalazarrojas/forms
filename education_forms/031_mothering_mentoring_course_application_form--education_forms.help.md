# Mothering Mentoring Course Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from applicants who are interested in enrolling in the Mothering Mentoring Course. Completing this form will help the course administrators review your application and ensure that you are a good fit for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Decide whether you are interested in enrolling in the Mothering Mentoring Course.
2. Enter your name for the course (select one of the options: "Yes" or "No").
3. If you select "Yes", enter the name of the course you are interested in (text field).
4. Enter your applicant email address.
5. Enter your phone number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Mothering Mentoring Course** (`mothering_mentoring_course_form`, select_one, **required: false**): This is a multiple choice question where you need to select either "Yes" or "No" to indicate your interest in enrolling in the Mothering Mentoring Course.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, **required: false**): If you selected "Yes" in the previous field, enter the name of the course you are interested in. This field is optional, but please ensure that the course name is correct and up-to-date.
* **Applicant Email** (`applicant_email`, email, **required: false**): Enter your email address to allow course administrators to contact you about your application.
* **Applicant Phone** (`applicant_phone`, text, **required: false**): If you have a phone number, please enter it so that course administrators can contact you if needed.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your answers before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
- If you have any questions or issues while filling out the form, please contact the course administrators.
- Ensure that you are providing accurate information to help the course administrators review your application efficiently.
