# multiple_intelligence_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect multiple types of survey responses from a single user.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page of the form carefully and fill out the relevant questions.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Make sure to save your progress frequently, as you will be unable to undo actions.
4. When finished, click the "Submit" button to complete the form.
