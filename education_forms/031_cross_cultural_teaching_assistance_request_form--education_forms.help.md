# Cross Cultural Teaching Assistance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help teaching staff provide cross-cultural teaching assistance to students who require it. It aims to collect necessary information from instructors and students to provide targeted support.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your user information on the "User Info" page.
2.  Enter student details on the "Student Info" page.
3.  On the "Contact Info" page, enter your contact details so that the support team can reach out to you.
4.  Provide course details on the "Course Info" page.
5.  Describe the teaching context on the "Teaching Context" page.
6.  Share student background information, if relevant.
7.  Specify if the student requires language support.
8.  Add any additional information that will help the support team assist the student.
9.  Finally, upload any relevant files, if necessary.
10. Click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **User Info** (`User Info`, text, required: false):
    - This page is where you, as a teaching staff, will enter your personal details.
    - Please fill in your name and any other relevant information about yourself.
    - This will help the support team contact you regarding the student's request.

- **Student Info** (`Student Info`, text, required: false):
    - On this page, enter details about the student in need of cross-cultural assistance.
    - This might include their name, grade level, or any other relevant student information.
    - Be as accurate as possible with the student's details.

- **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, text, required: false):
    - Provide your contact information so that the support team can reach out to you for further assistance or clarification.
    - Include your name, email, phone number, or any other way the support team can contact you.

- **Course Info** (`Course Info`, text, required: false):
    - Describe the course in which the student is enrolled and requires assistance.
    - Be specific about the course title, level, and any other relevant details.

- **Teaching Context** (`Teaching Context`, text, required: false):
    - Describe the teaching context, including the class size, teaching style, or any other relevant information.
    - This will help the support team understand the specific needs of the student.

- **Student Background** (`Student Background`, text, required: false):
    - If relevant, provide background information about the student's background or experience.
    - This might include their cultural or language background, for instance.

- **Language Support** (`Language Support`, text, required: false):
    - Specify if the student requires language support, and describe the specific language support needed.
    - This will help the support team tailor their assistance accordingly.

- **Additional Info** (`Additional Info`, text, required: false):
    - Add any additional information that will help the support team assist the student effectively.
    - Be as specific as possible with this information.

- **Upload Files** (`Upload Files`, text, required: false):
    - If necessary, upload any relevant files, such as supporting documents or resources.
    - Make sure to only upload files relevant to the student's request.

- **Submit** (`Submit`, text, required: false):
    - Once you have filled out all the relevant fields, click "Submit" to complete the form and send it to the support team.
