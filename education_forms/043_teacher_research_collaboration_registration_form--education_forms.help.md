# Teacher Research Collaboration Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teacher Research Collaboration Registration Form is a tool to help teachers register their research interests and projects with the institution. This form collects necessary information for internal record-keeping and potential future collaborations among teachers and staff.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address.
2. Select your department(s) of interest.
3. Describe your research interests and projects.
4. Provide a title and brief description of your research project.
5. Indicate whether you are interested in collaborating with others.
6. If yes, provide more details about the collaboration.
7. Choose the tools you are interested in using for the project.
8. Date and time the form was submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter your email address as it appears in your email signature or on your institutional profile.
* **Institution**: Enter your department(s) of interest (e.g., biology, math, history).
* **Department**: Select all departments where you would like to collaborate with (e.g., 'Yes' for biology, 'Yes' for math).
* **Research Interests**: Describe your current or future research interests and projects.
* **Project Title**: Enter a brief title of your research project.
* **Research Description**: Provide a brief description of your research project.
* **Collaboration Type**: Indicate whether you are interested in collaborating with others (yes or no).
* **Collaboration Details**: If yes, provide more details about the collaboration (e.g., specific areas of interest).
* **Assigned Tool**: If assigned a tool, enter the name of the tool.
* **Date Submitted**: Leave this field blank, it is automatically filled in by the system.

## Tips
* Please fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Ensure your department(s) of interest are correctly selected.
* Be specific about your collaboration details for best results.
* If assigned a tool, make sure to enter the correct name. 
* If you have any questions or need help, please contact your internal support team.
