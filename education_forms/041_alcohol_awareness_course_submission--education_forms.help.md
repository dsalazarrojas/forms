# Alcohol Awareness Course Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to submit an alcohol awareness course registration. It collects participant information and course preferences for registration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal details, including full name, date of birth, email address, phone number, and organization or school name if applicable.
2. Select the level of the course you wish to register for.
3. Choose your preferred course date and time slot.
4. Select the course delivery format you prefer.
5. State your reason for enrolling in the course.
6. Confirm your consent to participate in the course.
7. Declare the accuracy of the information you provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official government-issued ID or documents.
* **Date of Birth** (required): Enter your date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email Address** (required): Enter a valid email address where you can receive course materials and updates.
* **Phone Number** (required): Enter your phone number in the format of XXX-XXX-XXXX or 123-456-7890.
* **Organization or School Name** (optional): If you're representing an organization or school, enter the name here.
* **Course Level Requested** (required): Select the level of course you wish to register for:
	+ Basic Awareness (1 Hour)
	+ Intermediate (Half-Day)
	+ Advanced/Intensive (Full Day)
	+ Corporate/Workplace Safety
* **Preferred Course Date** (required): Enter a date for the course you prefer to attend in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Preferred Time Slot** (required): Choose a time slot you prefer for the course:
	+ Morning (9am - 12pm)
	+ Afternoon (1pm - 4pm)
	+ Evening (5pm - 8pm)
* **Course Delivery Format** (required): Select the delivery format you prefer for the course:
	+ Online (Live Webinar)
	+ In-person (Classroom)
	+ Self-paced (E-learning)
* **Reason for Enrollment** (required): Select the reason you're enrolling in the course:
	+ Professional Development
	+ School Requirement
	+ Legal/Court Requirement
	+ Personal Interest
* **I consent to participate** (required): Select "I consent" to confirm you're willing to participate in the course.
* **I declare that all information provided is accurate** (required): Select "I agree" to confirm the accuracy of the information you provided.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately to ensure successful course registration.
