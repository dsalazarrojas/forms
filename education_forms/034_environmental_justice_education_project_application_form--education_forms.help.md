## Environmental Justice Education Project Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Environmental Justice Education Project Application Form is a tool for individuals and organizations to apply for educational resources and support from the Environmental Justice Education Project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your user information, including any relevant details about yourself or your organization.
2. Provide school information, if applicable.
3. Enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
4. Describe your educational background and how it relates to your project.
5. Outline your project details, including the objectives and timeline.
6. List your project partners and their roles.
7. Review and approve your application to ensure accuracy.
8. Submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info** (`user_info`, text, required): Enter your name, title, or affiliation, as well as any other relevant details about yourself or your organization.
* **School Info** (`school_info`, text, required): If you are affiliated with a school, please provide the school's name and location.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter your email address and phone number where we can contact you.
* **Education Background** (`education_background`, text, required): Describe your educational background, including relevant degrees, courses, or certifications.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, required): Outline your project's objectives, timeline, and any other relevant details.
* **Project Partners** (`project_partners`, text, required): List your project partners and their roles.
* **Review and Approvals** (`review_and_approvals`, text, required): Review and approve your application to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where we can contact you.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the date you plan to start and complete your project.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): If applicable, enter the time you plan to dedicate to your project.
* **Education Background Info** (`education_background_info`, text, required): Provide any additional information about your educational background that is not covered in the previous field.

## Tips
* Ensure accuracy and completeness in all fields.
* Review and approve your application before submission.
* Use a valid email address and phone number.
* Be specific and detailed in your project description.
* Proofread your application carefully before submission.
