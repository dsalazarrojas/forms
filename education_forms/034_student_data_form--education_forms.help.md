# Student Data Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Student Data Form" is used to collect and maintain accurate and up-to-date information about students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and fill it out completely.
2. Ensure that you are using the most up-to-date information.
3. Check the student's profile first to see if the information is already recorded.
4. Fill out each field with accurate and complete information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name (`student_name`):** A field for entering the student's name.
* **Mother's Name (`mother_name`):** A field for entering the mother's name.
* **Father's Name (`father_name`):** A field for entering the father's name.
* **Home Phone (`home_phone`):** A field for entering the student's home phone number.
* **Cell Phone (`cell_phone`):** A field for entering the student's cell phone number.
* **Work Phone (`work_phone`):** A field for entering the mother's or father's work phone number.
* **Address (`address`):** A field for entering the student's address.

## Tips
* Double-check the information you enter for accuracy and completeness.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the student or their guardian/parent before proceeding.
* Review the form carefully to avoid duplication.
* Use the student's profile as a reference point to ensure you are not overwriting existing information.
