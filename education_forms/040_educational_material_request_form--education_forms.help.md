# Educational Material Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Material Request Form is designed to help you request educational materials such as books, resources, or other materials from the system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a clear and concise title for your request in the **Form Title** field.
2. Provide your name and email address in the **Name** and **Email** fields, respectively.
3. Select the type of material you are requesting in the **Material Type** field. You can choose from **Books**, **Resources**, or **Other**.
4. Enter the quantity of material you need in the **Quantity** field.
5. Select the department related to your request in the **Department** field. You can choose from **Science**, **Arts**, or **Other**.
6. Provide any additional comments or information about your request in the **Comment** field (optional).
7. If you want to track the progress of your request, select the current **Form Status** in the **Form Status** field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required): Enter a clear and concise title for your request that accurately describes the material you are seeking.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Provide your name so that we can contact you about your request.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so that we can contact you about your request.
* **Material Type** (`material_type`, select multiple, required): Select all the types of material you are requesting (e.g., Books, Resources, Other).
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Enter the quantity of material you need.
* **Department** (`department`, select multiple, required): Select the department related to your request (e.g., Science, Arts, Other).
* **Comment** (`comment`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or information about your request.
* **Form Status** (`form_status`, select one, optional): Select the current status of your form (e.g., Draft, Published, Archived). 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure your request is processed correctly.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please ask your supervisor or the relevant department for guidance.
* Please note that this form is only for requesting educational materials. If you need to request non-educational materials, please use a different form.
