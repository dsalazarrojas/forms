# listening-comprehension-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The listening-comprehension-form is designed to measure the listening comprehension of users by asking a series of questions or prompts that require the user to respond accurately and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the comprehension question carefully and respond accurately.
2. Choose the most appropriate option from the response options provided.
3. Enter your email address and other required contact information.
4. Review your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **intro-page**: A brief introduction to the form and its purpose.
* **comprehension-question**: Answer the following question carefully and accurately:
	+ **Please answer the following question:** (Text field for user response)
* **listening-task**: This field is for the user to respond to the given prompt.
	+ This is where you should demonstrate your listening skills by providing a clear and accurate response.
* **response-options**: Choose the most appropriate option from the options provided:
	+ Option 1, Option 2, Option 3 (Select multiple options)
* **note**: This field is for any additional information or comments you'd like to provide.
	+ You can use this field to add any additional details or clarifications.
* **email**: Enter your email:
	+ This is the contact information we'll use to get in touch with you.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number:
	+ This is optional, but it will help us contact you if needed.
* **date**: Enter your date:
	+ This is optional, but it will help us keep track of your responses.
* **time**: Enter your time:
	+ This is optional, but it will help us keep track of your responses.
* **time-zone**: Enter your time zone:
	+ This is optional, but it will help us adjust the time to your time zone.
* **phone**: This field is currently not necessary but might be used in other contexts, so just put your phone number or "N/A" here, so it's clear the field is not needed.
	+ Note that this field is currently redundant with the "email" field and might be removed or modified in the future.

**Tips**

* Make sure to answer each question accurately and thoroughly.
* If you need help or have questions, contact us.
* The form is designed to be completed within a reasonable time frame, so please take your time and respond carefully.
