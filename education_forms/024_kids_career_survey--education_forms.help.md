# Kids Career Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kids Career Survey form is a tool designed to collect information from students, teachers, and parents about career aspirations, interests, and goals. This form will help the institution or organization better understand student needs and develop effective career guidance programs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Read each page carefully**: The form is divided into sections, each addressing a specific area of interest. Take your time to review each section and answer accordingly.
2. **Provide accurate and up-to-date information**: Make sure to enter correct and current details about the child's interests, goals, and experiences.
3. **Select relevant options**: For questions with multiple-choice options, choose the ones that best apply to the child's situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1 - First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter the child's first name as it appears on their official documents.

### Page 1 - Birthdate
* **Birthdate** (`birth_date`, date, optional): Please enter the child's birthdate in YYYY-mm-dd format (e.g., 1999-03-12).

### Page 1 - Grade Completed
* **Completed Grade Level** (`grade_completed`, number, optional): Enter the highest grade level the child has completed (e.g., 9th grade).

### Page 1 - Highest Grade
* **Highest Grade Completed** (`highest_grade`, number, optional): Enter the highest grade level the child has completed (e.g., 9th grade).

### Page 1 - Career Interests
* **Career Interests** (`career_interests`, select_multiple, optional): Select all career options that interest the child:
	+ Software engineer
	+ Teacher
	+ Doctor
	+ Scientist
	+ Artist

### Page 1 - Email Address
* **Email Address** (`email`, optional): Enter the child's email address (if applicable).

### Page 1 - Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, optional): Enter the child's phone number (if applicable).

### Page 1 - School Name
* **School Name** (`school_name`, optional): Enter the name of the school the child attends.

### Page 1 - Parent First Name
* **Parent First Name** (`parent_first_name`, optional): Enter the parent's first name.

### Page 1 - Parent Email
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, optional): Enter the parent's email address.

### Page 1 - Parent Phone Number
* **Parent Phone Number** (`parent_phone_number`, optional): Enter the parent's phone number.

### Page 1 - Teacher Education
* **Teacher's Education** (`teacher_education`, optional): Enter information about the teacher's educational background.

### Page 1 - Teacher Experience
* **Teacher Experience** (`teacher_experience`, number, optional): Enter the teacher's years of experience.

### Page 1 - Career Goals
* **What career goals do you have for your child?** (`career_goals`, text, optional): Describe your career goals for the child.

### Page 1 - Student Survey Response
* **Student survey response** (`student_survey_response`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback from the student.

### Page 1 - Teacher Survey Response
* **Teacher survey response** (`teacher_survey_response`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback from the teacher.

### Page 1 - Parent Survey Response
* **Parent survey response** (`parent_survey_response`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback from the parent.

### Page 1 - School Survey Response
* **School survey response** (`school_survey_response`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback from the school.

### Page 1 - Career Survey Response
* **Career survey response** (`career_survey_response`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the career guidance process.
