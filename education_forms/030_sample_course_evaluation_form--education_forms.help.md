# Sample Course Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sample Course Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback from students on various aspects of a course, helping instructors and course designers improve the curriculum and provide better learning experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields first, which are marked as "required" in the form.
2. For fields with dropdown menus or select_one options, choose the most accurate answer from the provided options.
3. Be honest and thorough in your responses, as your feedback will help shape the course for future students.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Course ID** (`course_id`, `text`, required): 
Please provide the unique identifier for the course you're evaluating.
### 2. **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, `text`, optional): 
Enter the name of the instructor who taught the course.
### 3. **Course Name** (`course_name`, `text`, optional): 
Provide the name of the course being evaluated.
### 4. **Course Instructor** (`course_instructor`, `text`, optional): 
Enter the name of the instructor who taught the course.
### 5. **Course Description** (`course_description`, `text`, optional): 
Briefly describe the course you took.
### 6. **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, optional): 
Enter your name as a student who took the course.
### 7. **Course Satisfaction** (`course_satisfaction`, `text`, optional): 
Rate your satisfaction with the course.
### 8. **Course Relevance** (`course_relevance`, `number`, optional): 
On a scale of 1-5, rate how relevant the course material was to your learning.
### 9. **Course Difficulty** (`course_difficulty`, `number`, optional): 
Rate the difficulty level of the course on a scale of 1-5.
### 10. **Course Implementation** (`course_implementation`, `number`, optional): 
Rate how well the course was implemented on a scale of 1-5.
### 11. **Student Learning** (`student_learning`, `text`, optional): 
Describe any improvements you made as a student or areas where you needed more support.
### 12. **Would Recommend** (`course_recommendation`, `select_one`, optional): 
Choose how likely you are to recommend this course to others.
### 13. **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): 
Add any additional comments or suggestions you have for the course.
### 14. **Course Evaluation** (`course_evaluation`, `note`, optional): 
Write your overall evaluation of the course, including any positive or negative aspects.
### 15. **Form Category** (`form_category`, `text`, optional): 
Choose a category for the form, if required.
### 16. **Category 2** (`form_category2`, `text`, optional):
