# My Goals By - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "My Goals By" form is designed to help students set and reflect on their academic goals for the semester. It's a tool for students to establish a clear understanding of their objectives and provide a framework for planning and achieving their goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your First Name, Last Name, and Email address.
2. Answer the questions about your major goals, including one for the semester and two more for long-term goals.
3. Consider potential obstacles to achieving your goals and think about how you will overcome them.
4. Plan for contingency scenarios where you may become unsure of your major, minor, or long-term goals.
5. Provide any additional comments or information in the Additional Notes field.
6. Confirm your email address and provide a phone number (primary or alternate).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (text, optional): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (text, optional): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address as it appears on your profile.
- **What is one major goal you have for this semester?** (text, required): Describe one major goal you have for the semester.
- **What is one minor goal you have for this semester?** (text, required): Describe one minor goal you have for the semester.
- **What is one long-term goal you have for this academic year?** (text, required): Describe one long-term goal you have for the academic year.
- **Is there anything else you are hoping to achieve this year?** (text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your goals.
- **What are your expectations about learning and growth this year?** (text, optional): Describe your expectations about what you want to learn and grow from this experience.
- **What are some things that will help you achieve your major goals?** (text, optional): Describe any strategies or resources that will help you achieve your major goals.
- **What will be some potential obstacles to your achieving your goals?** (text, optional): Consider any challenges that might hinder your progress towards your goals.
- **How will you plan to overcome these obstacles?** (text, optional): Describe how you will address potential challenges.
- **What will you do if you get to the point where you are unsure of your major goal?** (text, optional): Plan for scenarios where you may become unsure of your major goal.
- **What will you do if you get to the point where you are unsure of your minor goal?** (text, optional): Plan for scenarios where you may become unsure of your minor goal.
- **What will you do if you get to the point where you are unsure of your long-term goal?** (text, optional): Plan for scenarios where you may become unsure of your long-term goal.
- **What will you do if you get to the point where you are unsure of your other goals?** (text, optional): Plan for scenarios where you may become unsure of your other goals.
- **Additional Notes** (text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your goals.
- **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
- **Confirm Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address as it appears on your profile.
- **Confirm Phone** (text, optional): Confirm your phone number.
- **Is this your primary phone number?** (text, optional): Indicate whether the phone number you entered is your primary phone number.
- **Do you have an alternate phone number?** (text, optional): Enter an alternate phone number.
- **Confirm Additional Notes** (text, optional): Confirm any additional comments or information about your goals.
- **Confirm Primary Phone Number** (text, optional): Confirm that the phone number you entered is your primary phone number.
- **Confirm Alternate Phone Number** (text, optional): Confirm your alternate phone number.
