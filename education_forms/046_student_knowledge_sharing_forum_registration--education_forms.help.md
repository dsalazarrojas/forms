# Student Knowledge Sharing Forum Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Knowledge Sharing Forum Registration form is a collection of fields that gather information about students' school, club, and program involvement. Completing this form helps to share knowledge and experiences with peers and contributes to the overall student knowledge sharing community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your school name in the "School Name" field.
2. Provide a brief description of yourself in the "Student Profile" field.
3. Specify the name of your club or organization.
4. Enter the name of your program or academic field.
5. Enter your enrollment date (if applicable).
6. Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you.
7. Enter your phone number (if you wish to be contacted).
8. Upload a profile image to make your profile more engaging.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your school.
* **Student Profile** (`student_profile`, text, required: false): Describe yourself in a few sentences.
* **Club Name** (`club_name`, text, required: false): Specify the name of your club or organization.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your program or academic field.
* **Enrollment Date** (`enrollment_date`, date, required: false): Enter your enrollment date (if you wish to do so).
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number (if you wish to be contacted).
* **Profile Image** (`profile_image`, note, required: false): Upload a profile image to make your profile more engaging.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* Keep your profile image in line with our community guidelines.
* Review your submission before hitting submit to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any issues, contact our support team for assistance.
