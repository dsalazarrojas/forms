# Stem Teacher Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stem Teacher Assessment Form is designed to gather data on student and teacher performance, progress, and other relevant factors to inform evaluation, reporting, and decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully and provide accurate information.
2. Enter your responses in the corresponding fields.
3. Save or submit the form when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Subject Area**: (`subject_area`, Text, Optional)
 Enter the area of study or subject that the student is being assessed in.
* **Question**: (`question`, Text, Optional)
 Describe the question or topic that the student was assessed on.
* **Student Assessment**: (`student_assessment`, Number, Optional)
 Rate the student's performance on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Teacher Assessment**: (`teacher_assessment`, Number, Optional)
 Rate the teacher's performance on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Student Performance**: (`student_performance`, Select One, Optional)
 Indicate whether the student has demonstrated satisfactory performance ('Yes') or not ('No').
* **Teacher Performance**: (`teacher_performance`, Select One, Optional)
 Indicate whether the teacher has demonstrated satisfactory performance ('Yes') or not ('No').
* **Date Assessment**: (`date_assessment`, Date, Optional)
 Record the date of the assessment.
* **Time Assessment**: (`time_assessment`, Time, Optional)
 Record the time of the assessment.
* **Note Assessment**: (`note_assessment`, Note, Optional)
 Enter any comments or notes about the assessment.
* **Email Assessment**: (`email_assessment`, Email, Optional)
 Enter the email address associated with the assessment.
* **Phone Assessment**: (`phone_assessment`, Text, Optional)
 Enter the phone number associated with the assessment.
