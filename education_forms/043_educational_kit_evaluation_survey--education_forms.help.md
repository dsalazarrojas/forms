# Educational Kit Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a brief survey designed to gather feedback and ratings from users about their experience with educational kits. Your feedback will help us improve the quality and usability of our educational materials.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out each field with your thoughts and ratings about the educational kit you are evaluating.
2. For each field, please provide as much detail as possible.
3. If a field is not required, you can skip it if you don't have any information to provide.
4. When asked about the rating scale, choose the option that best fits your assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): This is the name of your school or institution where you are evaluating the educational kit.
	+ Please enter the name of your school or institution.
* **Kit Type** (`kit_type`, select_one, optional): This is the type of educational kit you are evaluating.
	+ Please choose the type of kit that best fits what you are evaluating (e.g., Kit A, Kit B, Kit C).
* **Kit Usefulness** (`kit_usefulness`, number, optional): This is a rating of how useful the kit is in its intended purpose.
	+ Please rate the usefulness of the kit on a scale of 0 to 10, where 0 means not useful at all and 10 means very useful.
* **Kit Content Quality** (`kit_content_quality`, select_multiple, optional): This is a list of aspects of the kit's content quality.
	+ Please choose all the options that best describe your assessment of the kit's content quality (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Kit Usability** (`kit_usability`, select_one, optional): This is a rating of how easy it is to use the kit.
	+ Please choose the option that best describes your assessment of the kit's usability (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Kit Ease of Use** (`kit_ease_of_use`, number, optional): This is a rating of how easy it is to use the kit.
	+ Please rate the ease of use on a scale of 0 to 10, where 0 means not easy at all and 10 means very easy.
* **Kit Feedback** (`kit_feedback`, text, optional): This is an open-ended field for providing any feedback you have about the kit.
	+ Please enter any thoughts or comments you have about the kit.
* **Kit Additional Comments** (`kit_additional_comments`, text, optional): This is an open-ended field for providing any additional comments about the kit.
	+ Please enter any additional thoughts or comments you have about the kit.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): This is a rating of the kit overall.
	+ Please rate the kit on a scale of 0 to 10, where 0 means very poor and 10 means very good.
* **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`, select_one, optional): This is a scale for the rating.
	+ Please choose the option that best fits your rating (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Rating Reasons** (`rating_reasons`, select_multiple, optional): This is a list of reasons why you rated the kit as you did.
	+ Please choose all the options that best describe your reasons for the rating (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
