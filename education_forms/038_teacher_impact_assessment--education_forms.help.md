# teacher_impact_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about a teacher's impact on their students, helping school administrators assess and improve teaching performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your general information in the first field, providing an overview of your teaching experience and experience in the specific teaching area you are being assessed for.
2. Enter your name in the correct format, with a title (first and last) in the following two fields.
3. Enter your official school email address in the "email" field. This will help the school keep track of communication.
4. Type the name of your school in the "school_name" field.
5. Choose your teaching area (yes or no) in the "teaching_area" field.
6. Select an overall rating out of a scale (to be determined by the school) in the "overall_rating" field.
7. If desired, provide any additional comments about your teaching performance or areas of improvement in the "comments" field.
8. Once you have completed all the fields, click "submit" to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **general_info** (text, required): This field is for providing a general overview of your teaching experience and experience in the specific teaching area you are being assessed for.
* **first_name** (text, required): This field is for entering your first name.
* **last_name** (text, required): This field is for entering your last name.
* **email** (email, required): This field is for entering your official school email address.
* **school_name** (text, required): This field is for entering the name of your school.
* **teaching_area** (select one, required): This field asks you to confirm if you are a teacher in this specific teaching area.
* **overall_rating** (number, required): This field requires you to select an overall rating of your teaching performance out of a scale determined by the school.
* **comments** (note, optional): This field is for providing any additional comments or feedback about your teaching performance or areas for improvement.
* **submit** (text, not required): This field is for submitting the form once you have completed all the required fields.
