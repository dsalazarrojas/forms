# Academic Integrity Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about incidents of academic integrity, such as cheating, plagiarism, or other forms of academic misconduct. The purpose of this form is to document the incident, gather evidence, and determine the desired outcome for resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Incident Report" page and read the instructions carefully.
2. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. For non-required fields, you can leave them blank or fill them in if relevant.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Incident Report -- (Section 1)** (`section_incident_report`, `note`, required: false): This is the introduction section of the form. Please read the instructions carefully to understand the purpose of the form.
* **Reporter name** (`reporter_name`, `text`, required: false): If you want to remain anonymous, you can fill in your name or leave this field blank.
* **Reporter contact email** (`reporter_contact`, `email`, required: false): This field is for follow-up contact information if you want to be reached after reporting the incident.
* **Date of incident** (`incident_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date when the incident occurred.
* **Accused person or parties** (`accused_person`, `text`, required: false): If you know the names of the individuals involved, enter them here.
* **Detailed account of events** (`description_of_incident`, `text`, required: true): Describe the incident in as much detail as possible. This is a critical field that helps us understand what happened.
* **Evidence provided** (`evidence_provided`, `text`, required: false): If you have evidence related to the incident, describe it here or attach relevant attachments.
* **Desired outcome from report** (`desired_outcome`, `text`, required: false): This field is for specifying the desired outcome or resolution for the incident.
* **Confidentiality requested** (`confidentiality_requested`, `select_one`, required: false): If you request confidentiality, please select "True".
* **Signature name** (`signature`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your full name to confirm that you submitted the report.

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible in the "Detailed account of events" field.
- If you have attachments or additional evidence, please attach them or describe them in the "Evidence provided" field.
- If you request confidentiality, please understand that our organization will respect your request, but we cannot guarantee complete confidentiality.
