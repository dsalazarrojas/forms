# Math Practice Worksheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students about their math practice work, typically in educational settings. It helps teachers or administrators monitor student progress and understanding of specific math topics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Select the student's grade level from the options provided in the "Grade" field.
3. Enter the specific math topic being practiced in the "Math Topic" field.
4. Enter the student's written work in the "Written Work" field.
5. Upload any relevant math files, if necessary, in the "Math File" field.
6. Rate the student's confidence level about their work in the "Confidence Rating" field.
7. Upload any additional files, if necessary, in the "File 1" and "File 2" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required: false): Enter the student's name as it appears on their identification.
* **Grade** (`grade_level`, text, required: true): Select the student's grade level from the options provided.
* **Math Topic** (`math_topic`, text, required: false): Enter the math topic being practiced or worked on.
* **Written Work** (`written_work`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description or the student's written work in this field.
* **Math File** (`upload_file`, text, required: false): Upload any relevant math files or work, if necessary.
* **Confidence Rating** (`confidence_rating`, select_one, required: true): Rate the student's confidence level in their work, where 1 is least confident and 5 is most confident.
* **File 1** (`file_upload_1`, text, required: false): Upload any additional files, if necessary.
* **File 2** (`file_upload_2`, text, required: false): Upload any further additional files, if necessary.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask your teacher or administrator for guidance.
* Double-check your work before submitting the form to avoid errors.
