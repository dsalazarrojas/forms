# EdTech Evaluation Research Panel Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The EdTech Evaluation Research Panel Registration form is designed for researchers and experts in the field of Education Technology who want to join the panel to contribute to the evaluation and improvement of educational research. This form requires users to provide their expertise, education background, contact information, and other relevant details to be considered for panel membership.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting "Registration" to begin the process.
2. In the "Education Background" section, provide a brief description of your educational background, including your education level (e.g., Master's, Doctoral) and institution(s) you were educated at.
3. Select your "Research Interests" from the available options (Ed Tech, Research, Evaluation, Panel).
4. Choose your "Expertise Areas" by selecting multiple options from the list (Instructional Design, Learning Technology, Curriculum Development, Education Policy, Higher Education).
5. In the "Contact Info" section, select a contact method (Email or Phone) and provide your work email and/or work phone number.
6. Select your "Panel Interests" by choosing one or more options from the list (Research, Development, Evaluation, Panel).
7. If applicable, provide your "Job Title", "Department", "Organization", and "Job Description".
8. Finally, review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration**: Select this option to start the registration process.
* **Education Background**: Provide a brief description of your educational background, including your education level (e.g., Master's, Doctoral) and institution(s) you were educated at.
* **Research Interests**: Select one or more research interests from the available options (Ed Tech, Research, Evaluation, Panel).
* **Expertise Areas**: Select multiple options from the list that represent your areas of expertise (Instructional Design, Learning Technology, Curriculum Development, Education Policy, Higher Education).
* **Contact Info**: Select a contact method (Email or Phone) and provide your work email and/or work phone number.
* **Contact Number**: If you chose "Phone" as your contact method, enter your work phone number.
* **Contact Email**: If you chose "Email" as your contact method, enter your work email.
* **Panel Interests**: Select one or more panel interests from the available options (Research, Development, Evaluation, Panel).
* **Research Experience**: If applicable, provide the number of years of research experience you have.
* **Education Level**: Select the level of education you have attained (Doctoral, Master's, Bachelor's, Other).
* **Education Institution**: Select one or more institutions where you were educated.
* **Job Title**: If you have a job title, enter it here.
* **Department**: If you have a department, enter it here.
* **Organization**: If you have an organization, enter it here.
* **Other Job Title**: If your job title is not in the list, enter it here.
* **Job Description**: Provide a brief description of your job responsibilities.
* **Panel Experience**: If applicable, provide the number of years of panel experience you have.
* **Years of Experience**: If applicable, provide the number of years of research experience you have.
* **Available Time**: If applicable, provide the number of hours available for panel work.
* **Available Days**: If applicable, provide the number of days available for panel work.
* **Available Hours**: If applicable, provide the number of hours available for panel work.
* **Work Location**: If you have a work location, enter it here.
* **Work Email**: If you have a work email, enter it here.
* **Work Phone**: If you have a work phone number, enter it here.
* **CV Attachment**: If you have a CV, attach it here. 

## Tips
* Make sure to select only your relevant expertise areas and education institutions.
* Choose a contact method that you are comfortable with (e.g., email or phone).
* Review all the fields carefully before submitting the form to ensure you have provided all required information.
* Attach your CV if you have one, it will be helpful for the panel to review your qualifications.
* Do not submit duplicate applications.
