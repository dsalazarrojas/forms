# physics_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for students to register for a physics course. It collects necessary information from students, including their name, address, parent information, class ID, teacher name, grade level, school information, and the level of physics course they wish to register for.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name as the student in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter your address in the "Student Address" field.
3. If applicable, provide your parent's or guardian's name, contact information, and any relevant details in the "Parent Info" field.
4. Enter your class ID in the "Class ID" field.
5. Fill in your teacher's name in the "Teacher Name" field.
6. Select your grade level in the "Student Grade" field.
7. Check "Yes" or "No" for "School Info" if you have any school-related information to provide.
8. Choose the level of physics course you wish to register for in the "Physics Course Level" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (Text, Required): This is the field where you enter your name as the student.
* **Student Address** (Text, Required): This is the field where you enter your address.
* **Parent Info** (Text, Optional): If you are under 18 or require your parent's or guardian's information, use this field to provide it.
* **Class ID** (Number, Required): This is the field where you enter your class ID.
* **Teacher Name** (Text, Required): This is the field where you enter your teacher's name.
* **Student Grade** (Select One, Required): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you have school information or not.
* **School Info** (Select Multiple, Optional): Check "Yes" if you have school information to provide, "No" otherwise.
* **Physics Course Level** (Select One, Optional): Choose the level of physics course you wish to register for, either "High" or "Low".
