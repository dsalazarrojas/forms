# Youth Literacy Program Guardian Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain permission from guardians or parents for their child to participate in the Youth Literacy Program. It is essential for the program administrators to have this information to ensure the child's safety and well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Indicate your permission for your child to participate in the program by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Guardian Consent" field.
2. Fill in your name and any additional names associated with you or your child (if applicable) in the corresponding fields.
3. Provide your contact information, including your phone number and email address.
4. Specify your relationship with the child, and if it's not listed, provide an explanation in the "relationship_other" field.
5. Identify the emergency contact person(s) and provide their contact information.
6. Indicate whether you have granted permission for emergency contacts to be contacted in case of an emergency, and provide a reason if "No" is selected.
7. If you have any medical conditions or requirements, provide details in the "medical_conditions" and "medical_details" fields.
8. Confirm whether you have granted permission for medical treatment, and provide a reason if "No" is selected.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guardian Consent** (`guardian_consent`): Please select "Yes" to indicate your permission for your child to participate in the program.
* **Name** (`name`): Enter your name, or any additional names associated with you or your child.
* **Name 2**, **Name 3**, **Name 4** (`name2`, `name3`, `name4`): Enter any additional names that may be relevant to your child's participation in the program.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address for further contact and updates.
* **Address** (`address`): Enter your address for mailing or other correspondence.
* **Relationship** (`relationship`): Choose from the list or enter "Other" to specify your relationship with the child.
* **Relationship Other** (`relationship_other`): Enter a brief explanation of your relationship with the child.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`): Identify the person(s) who can contact you in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`): Provide the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`): Choose from the list or enter "Other" to specify the relationship of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Other** (`emergency_contact_other`): Enter a brief explanation of the emergency contact person's relationship with the child.
* **Emergency Contact Permission** (`emergency_contact_permission`): Select "Yes" to grant permission for emergency contacts to be contacted.
* **Emergency Contact Permission Reason** (`emergency_contact_permission_reason`): If "No" is selected, enter a reason for not granting permission.
* **Emergency Contact Permission Other** (`emergency_contact_permission_other`): Enter a reason for not granting permission for emergency contacts to be contacted.
* **Emergency Contact Permission Reason 2** (`emergency_contact_permission_reason2`): Enter a reason for not granting permission for emergency contacts to be contacted.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`): If you have any medical conditions or requirements, provide details here.
* **Medical Details** (`medical_details`): Provide any additional medical information that may be relevant.
* **Medical Permission** (`medical_permission`): Select "Yes" to grant permission for medical treatment.
* **Medical Permission Reason** (`medical_permission_reason`): If "No" is selected, enter a reason for not granting permission.
* **Medical Permission Reason Other** (`medical_permission_reason_other`): Enter a reason for not granting permission for medical treatment.
* **Medical Permission Reason 2** (`medical_permission_reason2`): Enter a reason for not granting permission for medical treatment.
