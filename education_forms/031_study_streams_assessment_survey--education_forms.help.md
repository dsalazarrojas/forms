# Study Streams Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect your feedback and opinions on your study experience with different study streams and teaching methods. Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer the questions as they apply to you.
2. Select the answers that best fit your experience.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Please provide any additional opinions or comments" field.
4. Submit the form when you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Which of the following study streams are available for you** (`study_streams`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question asks you to select all the study streams that are available for you to choose from.
* **How do teachers provide instruction?** (`teaching_methods`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select the way you think teachers provide instruction in the study stream(s) you are enrolled in.
* **Which teaching formats are available for you?** (`teaching_formats`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question asks you to select all the teaching formats that are available for you to choose from in the study stream(s) you are enrolled in.
* **What do you think about the study streams?** (`student_feedback`, `note`, required: false): Please provide any comments or feedback you have about the study streams.
* **Please provide any additional opinions or comments** (`student_opinions`, `note`, required: false): This is an open-ended field where you can provide any additional opinions or comments about the study streams or teaching methods.
* **Do you have any recommendations?** (`student_recommendations`, `note`, required: false): Please provide any recommendations you have for the study streams or teaching methods.
* **Do you have any suggestions?** (`student_suggestions`, `note`, required: false): This question asks you to provide any suggestions you have for the study streams or teaching methods.
* **How would you rate the quality of teaching?** (`teaching_quality`, `number`, required: false): Please rate the quality of teaching on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **How would you rate the support of teachers?** (`teaching_support`, `number`, required: false): Please rate the support of teachers on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **How would you rate the teaching environment?** (`teaching_environment`, `number`, required: false): Please rate the teaching environment on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **How satisfied are you overall?** (`overall_satisfaction`, `number`, required: false): Please rate your overall satisfaction on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Which study stream do you like best?** (`study_streams_liking`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks you to select the study stream that you like best.
* **Which teaching format do you like best?** (`teaching_formats_liking`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks you to select the teaching format that you like best.
