# Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Interest Survey form is a tool for collecting information about users and their interests. It is designed to gather specific data about each user, including their interests, education, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form correctly, follow these steps:

1. Select your category for "User Category" (id: 8).
2. Choose your interests from the available options for "User Interests 1" (id: 11), "More Interests" (id: 10), and "Additional Interests" (id: 12).
3. Enter your education background in the text field "User Education" (id: 2).
4. Enter your phone number in the "User Phone" (id: 3) text field.
5. Enter your email address in the "User Email" (id: 4) field.
6. If you have any additional information about yourself, you can add it in the "User Note" (id: 5) field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Interests** (`User Interests`): A text field where you can enter your primary interests.
* **User Education** (`User Education`): Enter your educational background.
* **User Phone** (`User Phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **User Email** (`User Email`): Enter your email address.
* **User Note** (`User Note`): A text field for any additional information about yourself.
* **User Date** (`User Date`): Enter your date of birth or the date you are interested in.
* **User Time** (`User Time`): Enter your time of interest (e.g., morning, afternoon, evening).
* **User Category** (`User Category`): Select your category from the available options.
* **User Subcategory** (`User Subcategory`): Choose your subcategory from the available options.
* **More Interests** (`More Interests`): Select any additional interests you have.
* **Interests** (`Interests`): Select your additional interests.
* **Additional Interests** (`Interests`): Select any additional interests you may have.

## Tips
* Please fill out each field as accurately and truthfully as possible.
* If you're unsure about any field, please refer to the available options or seek clarification from the relevant authorities.
* This form is for the purpose of collecting information about you, so please do not submit it for someone else.
