# Music And Theater Club Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students and their parents/guardians for the Music And Theater Club. It is essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
2. Make sure to select the correct role(s) for the student.
3. Enter the student's date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
4. Select the student's grade level from the provided options.
5. Enter the school name as it appears on the school's official documents.
6. Provide accurate contact information for the student and their parents/guardians.
7. Ensure that both the student and parent/guardian have signed the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the student's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the student's last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the student's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Role** (`role`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select one or more roles (Director, Actor, Singer, Dancer, or Member).
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the student's date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the student's grade level (9th, 10th, 11th, or 12th).
* **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the school name as it appears on the school's official documents.
* **Parent Contact Name** (`parent_contact_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the contact parent.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email of the contact parent.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the contact parent.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email of the emergency contact.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the signature of the parent/guardian.
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the signature of the student.
* **Additional Role Information** (`role_additional_info`, `text`, required/optional): Provide more information about the student's role.
* **Student Agreement** (`student_agreement`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the student's agreement.
* **Parent Agreement** (`parent_agreement`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the parent/guardian's agreement.
* **School Signature** (`school_signature`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the signature of the school representative.
