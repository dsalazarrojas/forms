# E Learning Program Evaluation Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from E-learning program participants to help us improve the program's quality and effectiveness.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name as a participant of the E-learning program.
2. Provide the title of the course you took part in.
3. Choose the format in which you took the course.
4. Rate your satisfaction with the program on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
5. Select the options that best describe the relevance of the course material to your needs.
6. Describe your overall opinion about the course content.
7. Rate the quality of the instructors in the course.
8. Choose the options that best describe the availability of support.
9. Describe your experience with the course organization and navigation.
10. Provide any additional feedback about the program.
11. Share any feedback you have about your experience as a participant.
12. Rate your satisfaction with the program on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
13. Decide whether you would recommend the program.
14. Rate the quality of the instructors.
15. Provide an overall rating for the program.
16. Describe any areas of improvement for the program.
17. Share any comments or thoughts about your experience as a participant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as a participant of the E-learning program.
* **Course Title** (`course_title`, text, required): Please enter the title of the course you took part in.
* **Course Format** (`course_format`, text, required): Please choose the format in which you took the course (e.g., online, offline, hybrid).
* **Program Satisfaction** (`program_satisfaction`, number, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the program on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **Course Relevance** (`course_relevance`, select_multiple, required): Select the options that best describe the relevance of the course material to your needs (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Course Content** (`course_content`, text, required): Describe your overall opinion about the course content.
* **Instructor Quality** (`instructor_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of the instructors in the course (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Support Availability** (`support_availability`, select_multiple, required): Choose the options that best describe the availability of support (e.g., Always Available, Sometimes Available, Never Available).
* **Course Organization** (`course_organization`, text, required): Describe your experience with the course organization and navigation.
* **Additional Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required): Provide any additional feedback about the program.
* **Participant Feedback** (`participant_feedback`, text, required): Share any feedback you have about your experience as a participant.
* **Participant Satisfaction** (`participant_satisfaction`, number, required): Rate your satisfaction with the program on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **Participant Recommend** (`participant_recommend`, select_multiple, required): Decide whether you would recommend the program (e.g., True, False, Maybe).
* **Instructor Rating** (`instructor_rating`, number, required): Rate the quality of the instructors.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, required): Provide an overall rating for the program.
* **Program Improvement** (`program_improvement`, text, required): Describe any areas of improvement for the program.
* **Participant Comments** (`participant_comments`, text, required): Share any comments or thoughts about your experience as a participant.
