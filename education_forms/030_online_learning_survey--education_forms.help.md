# online_learning_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and opinions from users who have taken an online course. We want to hear about your experiences and suggestions to improve future iterations of the course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the fields provided.
2. Enter your Email address in the Email field. This will help us ensure that the response is linked to your account.
3. Select your Date of Birth from the calendar.
4. Choose a time that represents your Time of Birth.
5. Enter any additional Notes about your experience, if desired.
6. Answer the question about how you rated your e-learning experience.
7. Select all that apply for how likely you are to recommend the course.
8. Enter your preferred decimal number in the Decimal field.
9. Enter your Integer number in the Integer field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Email** (`email`, required/optional): Enter your email address that we can use to contact you or verify your response.
* **Phone** (`phone`, required/optional): Enter your phone number for any additional contact or follow-up.
* **select one** (`select_one`, required/optional): Choose one option that best describes your e-learning experience.
* **select multiple** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all that apply for how likely you are to recommend the course.
* **Date of Birth** (`date`, required/optional): Choose your date of birth from the calendar.
* **Time of Birth** (`time`, required/optional): Select your time of birth.
* **Notes** (`note`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your experience.
* **Decimal** (`decimal`, required/optional): Enter your preferred decimal number.
* **Integer** (`integer`, required/optional): Enter your preferred integer number.
* **How do you rate your e-learning experience?** (`survey_type`, required/optional): Select one of the three options provided.
* **How likely are you to recommend the course?** (`rating`, required/optional): Select all that apply for how likely you are to recommend the course.

## Tips
* Please respond based on your genuine experience and feedback.
* Be as detailed and specific as possible in your answers.
* If you have any further questions or need clarification, please feel free to contact us.
* Your response will help us improve future iterations of the online course.
