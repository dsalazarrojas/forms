# global_education_exchange_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a global education exchange program, including participant details, contact information, and other relevant data.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form to ensure you are registering a new participant or updating an existing one.
2. Select the participant category (Student, Teacher, or Other).
3. Enter the participant's details in the text field.
4. Choose the participant's preferred contact methods (Phone, Email, Address, or Fax).
5. Select the participant's education level (High School, Bachelor, Master, or Doctoral).
6. Choose the participant's field of study (Arts, Engineering, Science, or Languages).
7. Select the participant's language proficiency (English, Spanish, French, or Chinese).
8. Choose the participant's preferred location (USA, Europe, Asia, or Africa).
9. Select the type of exchange program (Short-Term, Long-Term, or Summer).
10. Choose the participant's status (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
11. Enter the start date of the exchange (if applicable).
12. Enter the end date of the exchange (if applicable).
13. Enter the termination date of the participant (if applicable).
14. Select the reason for termination (if applicable).
15. Enter the renewal date of the exchange (if applicable).
