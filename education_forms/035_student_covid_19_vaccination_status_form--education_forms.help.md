# Student COVID 19 Vaccination Status Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of the Student COVID-19 Vaccination Status Form is to collect and verify the vaccination status of students. This form ensures that students, teachers, and school administrators have accurate and up-to-date information on vaccination status, which is crucial for maintaining a healthy and safe school environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Student COVID-19 Vaccination Status Form" link or button.
2. Review and select the student's vaccination status from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <i class="fa fa-square"></i>
#### 1. **Student Vaccination Status**
**Student Vaccination Status** (`student_vaccination_status`, `select_one`, required: false):
	* This field asks about the student's overall vaccination status, which can be:
		- Fully Vaccinated
		- Partially Vaccinated
		- Not Vaccinated

### <i class="fa fa-square"></i>
#### 2. **Vaccine Type**
**Vaccine Type** (`vaccine_type`, `select_one`, required: false):
	* This field is for selecting the type of vaccine the student received, which can be:
		- Pfizer
		- Moderna
		- J&J (Janssen)
		- AstraZeneca

### <i class="fa fa-square"></i>
#### 3. **Vaccination Date**
**Vaccination Date** (`vaccination_date`, `date`, required: false):
	* This date field is for recording the date the student received the vaccine.

### <i class="fa fa-square"></i>
#### 4. **Vaccination Location**
**Vaccination Location** (`vaccination_location`, `text`, required: false):
	* This field is for recording the location where the student received the vaccine.

### <i class="fa fa-square"></i>
#### 5. **Dose Number**
**Dose Number** (`dose_number`, `number`, required: false):
	* This field is for recording the number of vaccine doses the student received.

### <i class="fa fa-square"></i>
#### 6. **Dose Date**
**Dose Date** (`dose_date`, `date`, required: false):
	* This date field is for recording the date the student received each vaccine dose.

### <i class="fa fa-square"></i>
#### 7. **Student Vaccination Status Note**
**Student Vaccination Status Note** (`student_vaccination_status_note`, `note`, required: false):
	* This field is for any additional notes or comments about the student's vaccination status.

### <i class="fa fa-square"></i>
#### 8. **Teacher Confirmation**
**Teacher Confirmation** (`teacher_confirmation`, `select_one`, required: false):
	* This field is for teachers to confirm or not confirm the student's vaccination status.

### <i class="fa fa-square"></i>
#### 9. **School Admin Confirmation**
**School Admin Confirmation** (`school_admin_confirmation`, `select_one`, required: false):
	* This field is for school administrators to confirm or not confirm the student's vaccination status.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* If unsure about a particular field, contact the school administration or student's healthcare provider for guidance.
* Review and confirm the form before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.
