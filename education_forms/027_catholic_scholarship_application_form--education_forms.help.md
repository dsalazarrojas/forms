# Catholic Scholarship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about applicants for a Catholic scholarship. It gathers personal, academic, and financial data to assess eligibility and award scholarships to deserving students.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out each section, starting with the Applicant Information section.
2.  Complete all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
3.  Answer questions accurately and honestly.
4.  Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Required): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (Required): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Current Grade Level** (Required): Select your current grade level from the dropdown list.
* **Email Address** (Required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (Required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (Required): Enter the name of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent Contact Phone** (Required): Enter your parent's or guardian's phone number.
* **Number of Siblings** (Optional): If you have siblings, enter the number of siblings.
* **Annual Family Income Range** (Required): Select the range of your family's annual income from the dropdown list.
* **Current School Name** (Required): Enter the name of your current school.
* **Current GPA** (Required): Enter your current GPA on a 4.0 scale.
* **Standardized Test Scores** (Optional): Enter any relevant standardized test scores, such as SAT or ACT.
* **Extracurricular Activities** (Optional): Describe any extracurricular activities you participate in.
* **Volunteer Service and Community Involvement** (Optional): Describe any volunteer service or community involvement.
* **Awards and Honors** (Optional): List any academic or other achievements.
* **Parish or Church Participation** (Required): Select how actively involved you are in your parish or church from the dropdown list.
* **Statement of Your Catholic Faith** (Required): Describe how your faith guides your life.
* **Scholarship Amount Needed** (Required): Enter the total annual scholarship amount you need.
* **Essay - Why You Deserve This Scholarship** (Required): Write a descriptive essay about your financial need and service commitment.

Note: This help guide is based on the provided YAML fields and aims to assist users in understanding the form's purpose and completing it accurately.
