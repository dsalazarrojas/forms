# profile_builder_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The profile_builder_form is used to create a professional profile.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the profile, fill in the required information for each field. The form is divided into multiple pages, each with its own set of fields. Make sure to fill in all the required fields (marked as `required`) before proceeding further.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **education_background** (Required, text): Enter a brief description of your educational background.
* **education_level** (Optional, text): Enter your highest level of education.
* **job_title** (Optional, text): Enter your current or desired job title.
* **job_status** (Required, select_one): Select your current job status.
* **job_role** (Optional, text): Enter your job role or responsibilities.
* **job_level** (Optional, select_multiple): Select your job level (e.g., Level 1, Level 2, Level 3).
* **education_institution** (Optional, text): Enter your educational institution (e.g., university, college, high school).
* **graduation_date** (Optional, date): Enter your graduation date.
* **job_type** (Optional, text): Enter your job type (e.g., full-time, part-time, freelance).
* **years_of_experience** (Optional, number): Enter the number of years of experience.
* **education_degree** (Required, select_multiple): Select your highest education degree (e.g., Bachelor, Master, Doctorate).
* **email** (Required, email): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (Optional, text): Enter your phone number.
* **profile_picture** (Optional, note): Upload your profile picture.
* **address** (Optional, text): Enter your address.
* **country** (Optional, select_one): Select your country.
* **state** (Optional, text): Enter your state or region.
* **city** (Optional, text): Enter your city.
* **zip_code** (Optional, text): Enter your zip code.
* **profile_summary** (Optional, text): Enter a brief summary of your profile.
* **linkedin** (Optional, text): Link to your LinkedIn profile.
* **profile_picture_url** (Optional, text): Enter the URL of your profile picture.
* **profile_background** (Optional, text): Enter a brief background information about yourself.
