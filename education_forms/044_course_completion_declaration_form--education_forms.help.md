1. **Provide User Input**: On the first page, enter your user details, if required.
2. **Enter Course Completion Date**: On the second page, select the date you completed the course.

4. **Enter Student ID**: On the fourth page, enter your student ID, if required.
5. **Make Program Completion Declaration**: On the fifth page, describe your completion of the program in a few sentences.
