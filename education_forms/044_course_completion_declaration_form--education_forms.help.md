# Course Completion Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Course Completion Declaration Form" is a tool used to collect information from students or learners about their course completion status. It can be used for administrative purposes or internal tracking of student progress in educational programs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide User Input**: On the first page, enter your user details, if required.
2. **Enter Course Completion Date**: On the second page, select the date you completed the course.
3. **Write Course Completion Declaration**: On the third page, describe your completion of the course in a few sentences.
4. **Enter Student ID**: On the fourth page, enter your student ID, if required.
5. **Make Program Completion Declaration**: On the fifth page, describe your completion of the program in a few sentences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Enter your user details, such as name and contact information.
* **Course Completion Date** (`course_completion_date`, date, optional): Select the date you completed the course.
* **Course Completion Declaration** (`course_completion_declaration`, text, optional): Describe your completion of the course in a few sentences. This will help us understand your experience and progress.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, optional): Enter your student ID, if required for your program.
* **Program Completion Declaration** (`program_completion_declaration`, text, optional): Describe your completion of the program in a few sentences. This will help us understand your overall progress and achievements.
