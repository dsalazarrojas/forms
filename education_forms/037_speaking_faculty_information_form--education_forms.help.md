# Speaking Faculty Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about speaking faculty engagements, allowing administrators to keep track of events, speakers, and presentations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Select "Page 1" to view the first page with general information.
2. On "Speaker Information" (Page 2), enter your name, job title, and other relevant details about yourself.
3. On "Presentation Information" (Page 3), describe your presentation or talk, including the topic, format, and duration.
4. On "Event Information" (Page 4), provide details about the event, such as date, time, location, and sponsor.
5. On "Speaker Bio" (Page 5), write a brief bio about yourself, highlighting your expertise and experience.
6. Finally, review "Event Title" (Page 6) for any last-minute details about the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Speaker Information** (Page 2, text): Enter your name, job title, and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Presentation Information** (Page 3, text): Describe your presentation or talk, including the topic, format, and duration.
* **Event Information** (Page 4, text): Provide details about the event, such as date, time, location, and sponsor.
* **Speaker Bio** (Page 5, text): Write a brief bio about yourself, highlighting your expertise and experience.
* **Event Title** (Page 6, text): Enter a brief title of the event.
 
## Tips

* Be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor or event coordinator for clarification.
* Make sure to proofread your submission carefully before submitting it.
