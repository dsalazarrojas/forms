# Prospective Student Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prospective Student Inquiry Form is a tool used to collect information from individuals interested in enrolling in a program or course. The form helps us understand your needs and preferences, ensuring that we can provide you with the most suitable options.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "What is your name?" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "What is your email address?" field.
3. Select one or more programs you are interested in from the "Which program or course are you interested in?" field.
4. Choose how you discovered about the program in the "How did you find out about this program?" field.
5. If you have any financial aid or scholarship questions, select the corresponding option in the "Do you have any financial aid or scholarship questions?" field.
6. Indicate your preferred contact method in the "How would you like to be contacted?" field.
7. Fill in your phone number in the "What is your phone number?" field (if you'd like to be contacted by phone).
8. Enter your address in the "What is your address?" field.
9. If you have any additional comments or questions, please type them in the "Do you have any additional comments or questions?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your name?** (Name, text, required): Please fill in your full name.
* **What is your email address?** (Email, text, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Which program or course are you interested in?** (Program, select multiple, required): Select one or more programs that interest you.
* **How did you find out about this program?** (Method, select one, required): Choose how you discovered about this program.
* **Do you have any financial aid or scholarship questions?** (Financial aid, checkbox, optional): If you have any financial aid or scholarship questions, select this option.
* **How would you like to be contacted?** (Contact, select one, optional): Choose how you'd like to be contacted.
* **What is your phone number?** (Phone number, number, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted by phone.
* **What is your address?** (Address, text, optional): Fill in your address.
* **Do you have any additional comments or questions?** (Additional comments, note, optional): Type any additional comments or questions you have.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure that we can provide you with the best possible support.
* If you have any concerns or questions, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We will review your submission to ensure that we can provide you with the most suitable options.
