# School Media Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The School Media Release Form is used to obtain media release from parents or guardians for a student. It informs parents or guardians that their child's image, audio, or video may be used for educational purposes and requires their consent for this use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's name and the parent's name, email, phone number, and consent date.
2. Select the type of media (photo, image, or video) that will be used for educational purposes.
3. Indicate whether the student's photo, audio, or video will be used for educational purposes.
4. If other types of media will be used, provide a detailed description.
5. Sign the form to provide your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your child's name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Parent Consent** (`parent_consent`, `text`, required): Check if you give your consent for your child's image, audio, or video to be used in educational materials.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required): Enter your child's name.
* **School Media Release** (`school_media_release`, `text`, required): Check if you give your consent for your child's image, audio, or video to be used in educational materials.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `note`, required): Sign this form to provide your consent.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you signed this form.
* **School Media Release Form Date** (`school_media_release_form_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you signed this form.
* **Student Photo** (`student_photo`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the type of media that will be used for your child (photo, image, or video).
* **School Media Release Form Image** (`school_media_release_form_image`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the type of media that will be used for your child (photo, image, or video).
* **School Media Release Form Audio** (`school_media_release_form_audio`, `select_multiple`, required): Select if your child's audio will be used for educational purposes.
* **School Media Release Form Video** (`school_media_release_form_video`, `select_multiple`, required): Select if your child's video will be used for educational purposes.
* **Other Media** (`school_media_release_form_other`, `text`, optional): If other types of media will be used, provide a detailed description.
* **Other Image** (`school_media_release_form_image_other`, `text`, optional): If other types of images will be used, provide a detailed description.
* **Other Audio** (`school_media_release_form_audio_other`, `text`, optional): If other types of audio will be used, provide a detailed description.
* **Other Video** (`school_media_release_form_video_other`, `text`, optional): If other types of video will be used, provide a detailed description.
