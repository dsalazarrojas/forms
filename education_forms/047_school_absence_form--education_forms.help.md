# School Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Absence Form is designed to report a student's absence from school due to an illness, family emergency, or other valid reasons. This form is meant to be completed by parents or guardians, providing essential information to the school administration for accurate tracking and record-keeping.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the student's details:
    *   First name: Enter the first name of the student.
    *   Surname: Enter the surname of the student.
    *   Address: Enter the student's address.
2.  Enter the parent's details:
    *   Parent's First Name: Enter the first name of the parent or guardian.
    *   Parent's Surname: Enter the surname of the parent or guardian.
    *   Parent's Address: Enter the parent's or guardian's address.
3.  Provide the absence dates:
    *   Start Date: Enter the date the student will be absent from school.
    *   End Date: Enter the date the student will return to school.
4.  Reason for Absence:
    *   Enter a brief explanation for the student's absence.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter the student's first name.
*   **Surname** (`surname`, text, required): Enter the student's surname.
*   **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter the student's address.
*   **Parent's First Name** (`parent_first_name`, text, required): Enter the parent or guardian's first name.
*   **Parent's Surname** (`parent_surname`, text, required): Enter the parent or guardian's surname.
*   **Parent's Address** (`parent_address`, text, required): Enter the parent or guardian's address.
*   **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date the student will be absent from school.
*   **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the date the student will return to school.
*   **Reason for Absence** (`reason_for_absence`, text, required): Enter a brief explanation for the student's absence.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
*   If you are reporting a prolonged absence, please provide an estimated return date.
*   If you need assistance or have questions, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
