# Scholarship Fund Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scholarship Fund Application Form is used by students to apply for scholarships. It is a form that helps students submit their applications for financial aid to fund their education expenses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Select the correct scholarship type that best fits your needs.
3. Enter your student details, such as name and student ID.
4. Provide your contact information, including email and phone number.
5. Choose the status of your application.
6. Select the user who submitted your application.
7. Select the department that submitted your application.
8. Choose the group that submitted your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Scholarship Type** (`scholarship_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of scholarship you are applying for. Choose from Full-Ride, Partial-Scholarship, or Both.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, required): Enter your student ID number.
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, date, required): Enter the date you submitted your application.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required): Enter your student email address.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, optional): Enter your student phone number.
* **Application Status** (`application_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the status of your application. Choose from Pending, Approved, or Denied.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the user who submitted your application.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, date, optional): Enter the date your application was submitted.
* **Updated On** (`updated_on`, date, optional): Enter the date your application was updated.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your application.
* **Submitted By User** (`submitted_by_user`, select_one, optional): Select the user who submitted your application. Choose from John, Jane, or Jim.
* **Submitted To User** (`submitted_to_user`, select_one, optional): Select the user who received your application. Choose from Jane, John, or Jim.
* **Submitted To Department** (`submitted_to_department`, select_one, optional): Select the department that received your application. Choose from Education, Finance, or Marketing.
* **Submitted To Group** (`submitted_to_group`, select_one, optional): Select the group that received your application. Choose from Department 1, Department 2, or Department 3.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your application is complete.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant department or user.
