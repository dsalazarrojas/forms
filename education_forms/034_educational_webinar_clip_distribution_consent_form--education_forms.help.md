# Educational Webinar Clip Distribution Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to obtain your consent for the distribution of your webinar clip through various channels. The information you provide will help us share your content with relevant audiences, while ensuring your preferences and boundaries are respected.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the User Info section with your name, email address, and phone number.
2. In the Webinar Details section, provide a brief description of the webinar you are participating in.
3. In the Distribution Channels section, select the channels you are comfortable with your webinar clip being distributed through. You can choose multiple channels.
4. In the Consent section, select whether you consent to the distribution of your webinar clip or not.
5. In the Additional Info section, you can provide any additional information you would like to be included with your clip.
6. Finally, click the submit button to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### User Info

* **User Info (text)**: Fill in your full name, email address, and phone number to identify yourself.

### Webinar Details

* **Webinar Details (text)**: Briefly describe the webinar you are participating in, including the title and a short description.

### Distribution Channels

* **Distribution Channels (select multiple)**: Choose the channels you are comfortable with your webinar clip being distributed through. You can select multiple channels.
	+ Channel 1
	+ Channel 2
	+ Channel 3

### Consent

* **Consent (select one)**: Select whether you consent to the distribution of your webinar clip or not.
	+ I consent to the distribution of my webinar clip
	+ I do not consent to the distribution of my webinar clip

### Additional Info

* **Additional Info (note)**: Use this field to provide any additional information you would like to be included with your clip.

### Email

* **Email (email)**: This field should be your email address where you can be contacted.
  (optional)

### Phone Number

* **Phone Number (text)**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted by phone (optional)

## Tips

* Make sure to complete the form accurately and truthfully.
* Only select distribution channels you are comfortable with.
* If you change your mind about your consent, please contact us and we will update your information accordingly.
