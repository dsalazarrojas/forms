# College Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The College Event Registration Form is a crucial document that helps us manage event attendance, accommodations, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details such as your First Name, Last Name, Email Address, Phone Number, and Student ID Number (if applicable).
2. Select your status as an attendee: Current Student, Prospective Student, Alumni, Faculty, Staff, Community Member, or Guest.
3. Enter your school name and department (if applicable).
4. Choose the activities you plan to attend, such as Keynote Address, Panel Discussion, or Workshop Session A/B.
5. If applicable, select your workshop preference: Morning, Afternoon, or Both.
6. Indicate your role in the event: Attendee, Presenter, Panelist, Volunteer, Sponsor, or Organizer.
7. If presenting, enter the title and abstract of your presentation.
8. Choose your T-Shirt size and meal preference (if meals are provided).
9. Indicate if you need any accessibility accommodations or transportation assistance.
10. Select your payment method: Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, Check, Invoice, or Waiver.
11. Specify how you heard about the event: Email, Website, Social Media, Professor, Friend, or Other.
12. Indicate your interest in networking opportunities: Very Interested, Somewhat Interested, or Not Interested.
13. Select the topics you are most interested in.
14. Describe what you hope to gain from this event.
15. Indicate if you have attended this event before.
16. If applicable, mention who referred you to the event.
17. Provide any additional comments or questions you may have.
18. Agree to the terms and conditions of the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`attendee_first_name`, Text, Required): Enter your given name.
* **Last Name** (`attendee_last_name`, Text, Required): Enter your family name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, Email, Required): Enter your primary email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, Text, Required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Student ID Number** (`student_id`, Text, Optional): If you are a student, enter your student ID number.
* **Your Status** (`attendee_type`, Select One, Required): Choose your status as an attendee: Current Student, Prospective Student, Alumni, Faculty, Staff, Community Member, or Guest.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of your institution.
* **Department** (`department`, Text, Optional): Enter your department (if applicable).
* **Major or Field of Study** (`major`, Text, Optional): If you are a student, enter your major or field of study.
* **Class Year** (`class_year`, Number, Optional): If you are a student, enter your class year.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the event you wish to attend.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date of your registration.
* **Activities You Plan to Attend** (`activities_selection`, Select Multiple, Required): Select all activities you plan to attend.
* **Workshop Preference** (`workshop_preference`, Select One, Optional): If attending a workshop, choose your preference: Morning, Afternoon, or Both.
* **Your Role in Event** (`role_in_event`, Select One, Required): Choose your role in the event: Attendee, Presenter, Panelist, Volunteer, Sponsor, or Organizer.
* **Presentation Title** (`presentation_title`, Text, Optional): If presenting, enter the title of your presentation.
* **Presentation Abstract** (`presentation_abstract`, Text, Optional): If presenting, enter a brief description of your presentation.
* **T-Shirt Size** (`tshirt_size`, Select One, Optional): Choose your T-Shirt size: XS, S, M, L, XL, XXL, or XXXL.
* **Meal Preference** (`meal_preference`, Select One, Optional): If meals are provided, choose your meal preference: No Preference, Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, Kosher, Halal, or Other.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, Text, Optional): Enter any dietary restrictions.
* **Accessibility Accommodations Needed** (`accommodation_needed`, Select One, Required): Indicate if you need any accessibility accommodations: Yes or No.
* **Accommodation Details** (`accommodation_details`, Text, Optional): Enter any accommodation details.
* **Transportation Assistance Needed** (`transportation_needed`, Select One, Optional): Indicate if you need transportation assistance: Yes or No.
* **Housing Assistance Needed** (`housing_needed`, Select One, Optional): Indicate if you need housing assistance: Yes or No.
* **Registration Fee** (`registration_fee`, Text, Required): Enter the registration fee amount.
* **Fee Waiver Request** (`fee_waiver`, Select One, Optional): If necessary, indicate if you need a fee waiver: Yes or No.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, Select One, Required): Choose your payment method: Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, Check, Invoice, or Waived.
* **How Did You Hear About This Event** (`how_heard`, Select One, Required): Choose how you heard about the event: Email, Website, Social Media, Professor, Friend, or Other.
* **Interested in Networking Opportunities** (`networking_interest`, Select One, Optional): Indicate your interest in networking opportunities: Very Interested, Somewhat Interested, or Not Interested.
* **Topics You Are Most Interested In** (`topics_of_interest`, Text, Optional): Select the topics you are most interested in.
* **What Do You Hope to Gain from This Event** (`expectations`, Text, Optional): Describe what you hope to gain from this event.
* **Have You Attended This Event Before** (`previous_attendance`, Select One, Optional): Indicate if you have attended this event before: Yes or No.
* **Who Referred You** (`referral`, Text, Optional): If applicable, mention who referred you to the event.
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (`additional_comments`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments or questions.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_accepted`, Select One, Required): Agree to the terms and conditions of the event.

## Tips
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting.
* Ensure you have filled in all required fields.
* If you need any assistance or have any questions, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
