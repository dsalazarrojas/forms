# Digital Study Club Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Digital Study Club Registration form. Follow these steps to complete the form and register for the study club.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please make sure to enter a valid email address that you can be reached at.
3. Confirm your email address in the "Confirm email" field by re-entering the same email address.
4. Enter a password for your account in the "Password" field. Make sure to choose a strong and unique password.
5. Confirm your password in the "Confirm password" field by re-entering the same password.
6. List your interests, separated by commas, in the "Interests" field. This will help us get a better understanding of what topics you're interested in studying.
7. Select your preferred format of study from the "Preferred format" field. You can choose between "Online", "Offline", or "Both".
8. Choose your timezone in the "Timezone" field. This will help us schedule sessions according to your availability.
9. Select your availability in the "Availability" field. You can choose between "Morning", "Afternoon", "Evening", or "Other".
10. Enter your availability time in the "Availability time" field. This will help us schedule sessions accordingly.
11. Choose how often you can join a session in the "Session frequency" field. You can select "Daily", "Weekly", "Bi-weekly", or "Monthly".
12. Select the length of time you can commit to a session in the "Session length" field. You can choose between "30 minutes", "1 hour", "2 hours", "3 hours", "4 hours", or "5 hours" or "Other".
13. Choose your preferred language in the "Preferred language" field. You can select "English", "Spanish", "French", or "Other".
14. If you prefer a specific topic, select it in the "Preferred topic" field. You can choose between "Yes" and "No".
15. If you have any other preferred topic, enter it in the "Other preferred topic" field.
16. Finally, add any additional information about yourself in the "Additional info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, Text, Required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, Text, Required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, Text, Required): Enter a valid email address that you can be reached at.
* **Confirm email** (`email_confirmation`, Text, Required): Re-enter your email address to confirm.
* **Password** (`password`, Text, Required): Enter a strong and unique password.
* **Confirm password** (`password_confirmation`, Text, Required): Re-enter your password to confirm.
* **Interests** (`interests`, Text, Optional): List your interests, separated by commas.
* **Preferred format** (`format_preferences`, Select one, Optional): Select your preferred format (Online, Offline, Both).
* **Timezone** (`timezone`, Time, Optional): Choose your timezone.
* **Availability** (`availability`, Select multiple, Optional): Choose your availability (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Other).
* **Availability time** (`availability_time`, Time, Optional): Enter your availability time.
* **Session frequency** (`session_frequency`, Select multiple, Optional): Choose how often you can join a session (Daily, Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly).
* **Session length** (`session_length`, Select one, Optional): Choose the length of time you can commit to a session (30 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours, 3 hours, 4 hours, 5 hours, Other).
* **Preferred language** (`preferred_language`, Select one, Optional): Choose your preferred language (English, Spanish, French, Other).
* **Preferred topic** (`preferred_topic`, Select one, Optional): If you prefer a specific topic, choose it (Yes, No).
* **Other preferred topic** (`preferred_topic_other`, Text, Optional): If you have any other preferred topic, enter it here.
* **Additional info** (`additional_info`, Text, Optional): Add any additional information about yourself.
