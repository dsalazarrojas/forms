# Feudalism And Empire Analysis - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about the analysis of Feudalism and Empires. This form is designed to gather insights on how empires affected the political, economic, and social structures of the time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant empire you would like to analyze (Ancient Rome, Ottoman Empire, Sui Dynasty, or Han Dynasty).
2. Describe the relationship between Feudalism and the selected empire.
3. Explain the impact of Feudalism on the selected empire and its society.
4. If applicable, provide any additional notes or comments about your analysis.
5. Enter your email address so we can contact you for further clarification or discussion.
6. Select any additional options that are relevant to your analysis (Select Multiple).
7. If you would like to provide a specific time frame for your analysis, enter it in the "Time" field.
8. Select a single option that best describes your conclusion (Select One).
9. If you have any additional notes or comments about your analysis, enter them in the "Note" field.

* **Emperies** (`empires`, `select_one`, required): Select the empire you would like to analyze (Ancient Rome, Ottoman Empire, Sui Dynasty, or Han Dynasty).

* **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): If you would like to provide a specific time frame for your analysis, enter it here.
