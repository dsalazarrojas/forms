# youth_resilience_program_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your personal details in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the correct options for each field.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, **required**): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, **optional**): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, **required**): Enter a valid email address that you regularly check.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, **optional**): Enter a phone number where you can be reached.
* **Grade Completed** (`grade_completed`, select_one, **optional**): Select the grade you are currently in or have completed. Options: 6th, 7th, 8th, 9th, 10th.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_multiple, **optional**): Select all applicable genders. Options: Male, Female, Other.
* **Ethnicity** (`ethnicity`, select_one, **optional**): Select your ethnicity. Options: Asian, African American, Hispanic or Latino, Caucasian, Other.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, **optional**): Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **School Attendance** (`school_attendance`, select_multiple, **optional**): Select whether you are currently in school. Options: True, False.
* **Parent/Guardian** (`parent_guardian`, text, **optional**): Enter your parent or guardian's name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, **optional**): Enter the email address of the parent or guardian who can be contacted.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, **optional**): Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian who can be contacted.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, select_one, **optional**): Select who you are submitting this form on behalf of. Options: Student, Parent, Guardian, School Official.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, **optional**): Enter the email address of the submitter.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, **optional**): Enter the phone number of the submitter.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, **optional**): Enter your student ID number, if applicable.
* **Student ID Type** (`student_id_type`, select_one, **optional**): Select the type of student ID you have. Options: SSN, Student ID Number, Other.
* **Student ID Number** (`student_id_number`, text, **optional**): Enter your student ID number, if applicable.
* **Student ID Type Other** (`student_id_type_other`, text, **optional**): Enter a description of your student ID type, if it's not listed.
* **Student ID Type Other Description** (`student_id_type_other_description`, text, **optional**): Enter a description of your student ID type, if it's not listed.
* **School Year** (`school_year`, select_one, **optional**): Select the school year you are currently in or have completed. Options: 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th, 5th, 6th, 7th, 8th, 9th, 10th, 11th, 12th.
* **School Start Date** (`school_start_date`, date, **optional**): Enter the date you started school in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **School End Date** (`school_end_date`, date, **optional**): Enter the date you ended school in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
