# student_private_tuition_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The student_private_tuition_registration_form is a private tuition registration form used to collect information from students to schedule and conduct private tuition sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Student Name** in the first field.
2. Provide your **Email** address to enable easy communication.
3. Enter your **Contact Number** so we can reach you for session reminders or any updates.
4. Enter your **Address** to enable smooth communication and session arrangements.
5. Select the date you are **Available** for a private tuition session.
6. Choose a specific **Time** for the session that suits you best.
7. Specify the **Subject** you'd like to focus on during the session.
8. Select your **Student Grade** level to ensure we provide the right level of assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents. This is a crucial piece of information that helps us confirm your identity and tailor the tuition session to your needs.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address that we can use to send session reminders and updates.
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number so we can reach you for any updates, reminders, or session changes.
- **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Provide your current address to enable smooth communication and session arrangements.
- **Date Available** (`date_available`, date, required/optional): Select a date that you are available to have a private tuition session.
- **Time Available** (`time_available`, time, required/optional): Choose a time that suits you best for the session.
- **Subject** (`subject`, text, required/optional): Specify the subject area you would like to focus on during the private tuition session.
- **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, number, required/optional): Select your current grade level to ensure we provide the right level of assistance and tailored guidance.

## Tips
- Double-check your contact number and email address for accuracy.
- Be specific when selecting your date and time for the session.
- Clearly specify your subject area of focus to ensure we provide relevant assistance.
- Choose a grade level that accurately represents your current academic standing.
- If you have any special requirements or requests, please let us know in the comments section or by contacting us directly.
