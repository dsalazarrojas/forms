# School Supply Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The School Supply Order Form is used to collect information about student orders for school supplies. This form is intended for students, parents, or school administrators to submit requests for school supplies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter the date you would like to place the order in the "Order Date" field.
3. Enter your school's name in the "School Name" field.
4. Select your grade level from the "Grade Level" dropdown menu.
5. Indicate whether you need school supplies by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "School Supply" field.
6. Enter your parent's name in the "Parent Name" field.
7. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
8. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required: false): Enter your student's name as it appears on their records.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you would like to place the order.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required: false): Enter your school's name as it appears on the school's records.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_one, required: false): Select your current grade level from the dropdown menu.
* **School Supply** (`school_supply`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate whether you need school supplies by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required: false): Enter your parent's name if you are submitting this form on behalf of your student.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter your contact information so we can reach out to you about your order.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your order.
