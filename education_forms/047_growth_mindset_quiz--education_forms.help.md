# Growth Mindset Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your growth mindset, a set of beliefs about the ability to develop your abilities through effort and learning from failures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer the questions honestly and thoroughly. You can select one of the options in the "Goal Setting" field, and type your response in the other fields. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Intro** (`intro`, text, required: false): This is the introduction to the form. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **Self Theory** (`self_theory`, text, required: false): Describe your current understanding of how you approach challenges and failures. Think about times when you've learned from experiences and how you've grown as a result.
* **Challenge Theory** (`challenge_theory`, text, required: false): Describe a recent time when you faced a challenge. How did you approach it? What did you learn from the experience?
* **Self Reflection** (`self_reflection`, text, required: false): Reflect on a time when you had a setback or failure. How did you respond to it? What did you do to recover?
* **Goal Setting** (`goal_setting`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the goals that resonates with you the most. This will help you understand how you approach goal setting.
* **Self Motivation** (`self_motivation`, text, required: false): Describe your current motivations and drives. What gets you excited and motivated to learn and grow?
* **Self Discipline** (`self_discipline`, text, required: false): Describe your self-discipline habits, such as setting goals and sticking to them, or practicing self-care and self-compassion.
* **Self Regulation** (`self_regulation`, text, required: false): Describe your self-regulation habits, such as managing stress, emotions, and impulses.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your answers, as this will help you get the most out of the form.
* Take your time to reflect on your answers, and think about how they relate to your personal growth and development.
* This form is not a scientifically validated instrument, but it is a helpful tool for personal growth and self-awareness.
