# Geometry Exercise Response Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect student responses to geometry exercises. It is intended for use by teachers or educators to collect student work and provide feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student name in the "Student Name" field.
2. If you have an email address, enter it in the "student_email" field.
3. Answer each of the 22 questions in the corresponding "answer_1" to "answer_22" fields.
4. If you are a teacher, fill in your name and email (optional) in the "Teacher Name" and "teacher_email" fields.
5. Record the submission time in the "Submission Time" field.
6. Select the category of the geometry exercise from the available options (not shown in this form).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter your name as a student.
* **student_email**: Enter your email address (optional).
* **answer_1**: Answer to the first geometry exercise question.
* **answer_2**: Answer to the second geometry exercise question.
* ...
* **answer_20**: Answer to the 20th geometry exercise question.
* **answer_21**: Answer to the 21st geometry exercise question.
* **answer_22**: Answer to the 22nd geometry exercise question.
* **Teacher Name**: Enter your name if you are a teacher.
* **teacher_email**: Enter your email address if you are a teacher (optional).
* **Submission Time**: Record the time of submission.
* **Category**: Select the category of the geometry exercise (not shown in this form).

## Tips
* Make sure to answer each question carefully and accurately.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
* If you are a teacher, ensure that you have permission to collect student data and that you handle it according to your institution's data protection policies.
