**Homework Assistance Registration - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from students and schools for homework assistance purposes. It helps us understand the student's needs and provides schools with the necessary details to offer support.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Ensure you have all the necessary information and permissions before submitting the form.
2.  Complete the form accurately and honestly.
3.  Make sure to fill out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **School Name**: Enter the name of your school.
*   **Student Name**: Enter your full name.
*   **Student Email**: Enter your email address.
*   **Subjects**: Select all the subjects you require help with.
*   **Schedule Start Time**: Enter the time you are available to start your homework sessions.
*   **Schedule End Time**: Enter the time you would like your homework sessions to end.
*   **Student Notes**: Enter any additional notes about your requirements.
*   **School Phone**: Enter your school's phone number.
*   **Student Address**: Enter your student address.
*   **Schedule Days of Week**: Select the days of the week you are available for your homework sessions.
*   **Schedule Frequency**: Select all the frequencies you are available for your homework sessions.
*   **Student Schedule Status**: Select whether you are active or inactive in the homework sessions.
*   **Additional Info**: Enter any additional information about yourself.
*   **School Additional Info**: Enter any additional information about your school.
*   **School Additional Info 1**: Enter any additional information about your school.
*   **School Additional Info 2**: Enter any additional information about your school.

**Tips**

*   Please make sure to double-check all your information before submitting the form.
*   Ensure your school's information is accurate and up-to-date.
*   If you have any doubts or questions, please contact us for assistance.
