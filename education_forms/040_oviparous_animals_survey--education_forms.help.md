# oviparous_animals_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on oviparous animals, such as reptiles, birds, and fish, to inform research and conservation efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill out the form in a logical order, starting with the introduction section.
3. Select the correct species from the options provided.
4. Enter the number of eggs laid, egg size, and incubation time as required.
5. Choose the correct egg shape and lay frequency from the options provided.
6. Select multiple egg colors if applicable.
7. Enter the incubation period as required.
8. Provide comments on your observations and any additional information.
9. Enter your contact information, including name, institution, email, and phone number.
10. Proofread your submission for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`1`, `Introduction`, `note`, `[]`): A brief overview of the form and its purpose.
* **Species** (`2`, `species`, `select_one`, `Bird, Reptile, Fish`): Choose the species being studied.
* **Eggs Laid** (`3`, `eggs_laid`, `number`, `[]`): Enter the number of eggs laid by the species.
* **Egg Size** (`4`, `egg_size`, `number`, `[]`): Enter the size of the eggs (in mm or inches).
* **Incubation Time** (`5`, `incubation_time`, `select_multiple`, `1-7 days, 8-14 days, 15-30 days, 31-60 days, 61-90 days, 91 days or more`): Select the incubation time for the species.
* **Egg Shape** (`6`, `egg_shape`, `select_one`, `Round, Oval, Elliptical, Irregular`): Choose the shape of the eggs.
* **Lay Frequency** (`7`, `lay_frequency`, `number`, `[]`): Enter the lay frequency (how often the species lays eggs).
* **Egg Color** (`8`, `egg_color`, `select_multiple`, `Brown, Green, Blue, Red, Yellow, Other`): Select multiple egg colors if applicable.
* **Incubation Period** (`9`, `incubation_period`, `time`, `[]`): Enter the incubation period as required.
* **Comments** (`10`, `comments`, `select_multiple`, `Agree, Disagree, Neutral`): Provide comments on your observations and any additional information.
* **Species Additional Info** (`11`, `species_additional_info`, `select_multiple`, `True, False, N/A`): Check if additional information is available for the species.
* **Additional Comments** (`12`, `additional_comments`, `text`, `[]`): Enter any additional comments or information.
* **Researcher Name** (`13`, `researcher_name`, `text`, `[]`): Enter your name as the researcher.
* **Researcher Institution** (`14`, `researcher_institution`, `text`, `[]`): Enter your institution as the researcher.
* **Researcher Email** (`15`, `researcher_email`, `email`, `[]`): Enter your email as the researcher.
* **Researcher Phone** (`16`, `researcher_phone`, `text`, `[]`): Enter your phone number as the researcher.
* **Researcher Title** (`17`, `researcher_title`, `text`, `[]`): Enter your title as the researcher.
