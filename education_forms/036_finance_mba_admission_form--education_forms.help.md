# Finance MBA Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Finance MBA Admission Form is a critical part of the application process for the Finance MBA program at our institution. This form is used to collect information about prospective students' background, interests, goals, and professional experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2. Select the correct options from the dropdown menus (e.g., "Education" field).
3. Be as detailed as possible in the "Personal Details", "Academic Background", "Professional Experience", and "Comments" fields.
4. Upload your resume in the "Resume" field.
5. Review your submission carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Prospective Student**: Provide a brief introduction about yourself.
* **Personal Details**: Share any relevant personal details that you think would be helpful for the admission committee.
* **Academic Background**: Describe your academic background, including your educational institution and degree earned.
* **Professional Experience**: Share your professional experience, including job titles and duration of employment.
* **Education**: Select the highest level of education you have achieved (Master's or Bachelor's).
* **Education Institution**: Enter the name of your educational institution.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Resume**: Upload your resume in PDF format.
* **References**: Provide references for your professional experience.
* **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person who submitted the application.
* **Submitted Date**: The date the application was submitted.
* **Submitted Time**: The time the application was submitted.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or information about your application.
* **Assigned Tool**: This field is not currently used.
* **Assigned Form IDs**: This field is not currently used.
* **Output File**: This field is not currently used.
* **Output File Path**: This field is not currently used.
* **Assigned Tool Comment**: This field is not currently used.
* **Comments 2**: This field is not currently used.
* **Assigned Tool 2**: This field is not currently used.
* **Submitted By 2**: This field is not currently used.
* **Submitted Date 2**: This field is not currently used.
* **Submitted Time 2**: This field is not currently used.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or information about your application.
