# Winter Camp Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for parents to enroll their children in a winter camp program. Please fill in the required information to register your child.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in all the required fields.
2. Select the correct option for "Camp Type" (if you are unsure, please ask for help).
3. Make sure to double-check all dates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Please enter your name.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Please enter your child's name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Please enter your child's date of birth.
* **Camp Type** (`camp_type`, select_one, required): Please select the type of camp your child will be attending.
* **Camp Start Date** (`camp_start_date`, date, required): Please enter the date when the camp will start.
* **Camp End Date** (`camp_end_date`, date, required): Please enter the date when the camp will end.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, required): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Please enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check all dates to avoid any confusion.
* If you have any issues or questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
