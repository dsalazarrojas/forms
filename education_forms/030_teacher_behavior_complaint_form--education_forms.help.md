# Teacher Behavior Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teacher Behavior Complaint Form is a tool used to report concerns or incidents involving teacher behavior. This form allows users to provide detailed information about incidents, which helps school administrators to address and resolve issues promptly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the category of concern from the list provided.
2. Provide a brief description of the incident or concern.
3. Enter the date and time of the incident, including the time of day.
4. Include any relevant details about the teacher, such as their name and contact information.
5. Enter any details about the student(s) involved, including their name and contact information if necessary.
6. Enter any relevant school details that may help in resolving the incident.
7. Once you've completed all the necessary information, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Report Concerns
* **Report Concerns** (teacher_behaviour_complaint, text): This field is used to enter a brief description of the incident or concern. Please provide a clear and concise summary of what happened.

### Category
* **Category** (category, select_one): Please select the category that best describes the type of concern or incident you are reporting. Choose from the options provided, and select the one that best fits the situation.

### Description
* **Description** (description, text): Enter a more detailed description of the incident or concern. Be as specific as possible, providing any relevant details that can help in resolving the issue.

### Date of Incident
* **Date of Incident** (issue_date, date): Enter the date of the incident in the format dd/mm/yy.

### Time of Incident
* **Time of Incident** (issue_time, time): Enter the time of the incident, including AM/PM. Please use 12-hour clock format (e.g., 02:30 PM).

### Time of Day
* **Time of Day** (issue_time_of_day, time): This field is not directly related to the time of the incident but rather to the time of day when the incident occurred. Please enter the time of day in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:30).

### Teacher Details
* **Teacher Details** (teacher_details, text): Provide any relevant information about the teacher involved, such as their name and contact information.

### Student Details
* **Student Details** (student_details, text): Enter any relevant details about the student(s) involved, including their name and contact information if necessary.

### School Details
* **School Details** (school_details, text): Enter any relevant information about the school, such as the location or department involved.

## Tips

* Please be as detailed and specific as possible when filling out the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please don't hesitate to ask for clarification.
* Remember to submit the form only once to avoid duplication of information.
* If you have further concerns or questions, please contact the relevant authorities directly.

By following this guide, users can understand how to complete the form and provide detailed, helpful information for resolving incidents involving teacher behavior.
