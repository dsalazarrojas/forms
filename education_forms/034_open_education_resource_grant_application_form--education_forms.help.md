# Open Education Resource Grant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for educators or institutions seeking funding for open education resources. However, without more information, I'm not sure what specific purpose it's intended to serve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill out the form with the required information, which includes:
	* Whether you represent any education institutions (Page 2)
	* The name of the institution you represent (Page 3)
	* A description of your OER grant proposal (Page 4)
	* A detailed description of your OER grant proposal (Page 5)
	* A budget request (Page 6)
3. Ensure you answer all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*) and provide accurate information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **List of Education Institutions** (Page 2, <code>type: select_multiple</code>): This field allows you to select whether you represent one or multiple education institutions. Please choose the relevant options from the list:
	* Yes
	* No

- **Name of Institution** (Page 3, <code>type: text</code>): If you selected "Yes" on the previous field, provide the name of the institution you represent. If not, please leave this field blank.

- **OER Grant Proposal** (Page 4, <code>type: text</code>): This field is for a brief description of your OER grant proposal. Please provide a concise summary of your project.

- **OER Grant Proposal Details** (Page 5, <code>type: text</code>): In this field, please provide a detailed description of your OER grant proposal, including your goals, objectives, and expected outcomes.

- **Budget Request** (Page 6, <code>type: number</code>): If you're requesting funding, please enter the amount of money you need to support your project. Ensure it's a numerical value.
