## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Test Taker Information** (`test_taker_info`, text, optional): This field is for providing general information about yourself as a test taker. Please include your name and any other relevant details you'd like to share.
* **Exam Information** (`exam_info`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the exam you took, including the exam's name or identifier.
* **Test Score** (`test_score`, number, optional): Report the test score you received.
* **Result** (`test_result`, select_one, optional): Select one of the provided options to indicate the outcome of your test, i.e., Pass, Fail, Incomplete, or No Result.
* **Date of Exam** (`date_of_exam`, date, optional): Enter the date on which you took the exam, following the 'YYYY-MM-DD' format.
* **Time of Exam** (`time_of_exam`, time, optional): Report the time you started taking the exam.
* **Exam Center** (`exam_center`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable exam centers where you took the exam.
* **Additional Comments** (not explicitly defined in the YAML but implied): You can use the space below for any additional remarks or comments related to your exam experience.
