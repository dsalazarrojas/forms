# Petition For Study Resource - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from students or supporters who want to request or provide resources for academic purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with your relevant information.
2. Ensure that you provide enough details for the form's purpose.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Study Request** (`study_request`, text, optional): This field allows you to write a brief description of your study resource request. Please provide a clear and concise statement of what resource you need.
* **Supporter Details** (`supporter_details`, text, optional): This field is for additional information about the supporter who is providing the resource. You can add any supporting details or comments here.
* **Email** (`supporter_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the supporter who is providing the resource. This will help us contact them if needed.
* **Phone** (`supporter_phone`, text, optional): This field is for the supporter's phone number. You can enter it if you prefer to communicate with them over the phone.
* **Supporter Message** (`supporter_message`, text, optional): This field is for any additional comments or messages for the supporter.
* **Supporter Signature** (`supporter_signature`, date, optional): This field is for the supporter to sign and date their agreement to provide the resource. Please ensure that you fill out this field if you are a supporter providing a resource.
* **Type of Academic Resource** (`academic_resourcetype`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of resource you are requesting or providing. Select one of the options: "Books", "Software", or "Equipment".
* **Resource Description** (`resource_description`, text, optional): This field is for providing a detailed description of the resource you are requesting or providing.
* **Supporter Date** (`supporter_signature_date`, date, optional): This field is for the supporter to date their signature.
* **Supporter Time** (`supporter_signature_time`, time, optional): This field is for the supporter to record the time of their signature.

Please ensure to complete each field according to your needs and the form's purpose. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for further assistance.
