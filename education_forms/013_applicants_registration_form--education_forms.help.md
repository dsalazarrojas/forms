# Applicants Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Applicants Registration Form is designed to collect demographic and academic information from individuals interested in enrolling in a training program at the center. This form helps the center to verify the authenticity of the applicants and to process their applications in an orderly manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the following steps:

- Fill in your personal and contact information (Fields 2-7)
- Select your highest level of education completed (Field 10)
- Select your preferred course of interest (Field 14)
- Select your preferred learning mode (Field 15)
- Select your preferred intake period (Field 16)
- If applicable, provide your parent/guardian consent (Field 19)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Field 2, Text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (Field 3, Text, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (Field 4, Date, Required): Enter your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Gender** (Field 5, Select one, Required): Select your gender.
* **Email Address** (Field 6, Email, Required): Enter your official email address.
* **Phone Number** (Field 7, Text, Required): Enter your phone number.
* **Full Residential Address** (Field 8, Text, Required): Enter your residential address as it appears on your official documents.
* **Highest Education Level Completed** (Field 10, Select one, Required): Select the highest level of education you have completed.
* **Name of Last Institution Attended** (Field 11, Text, Required): Enter the name of the institution you last attended.
* **Year of Graduation** (Field 12, Number, Required): Enter the year you graduated.
* **Select Course of Interest** (Field 14, Select one, Required): Select the course you are interested in.
* **Preferred Learning Mode** (Field 15, Select one, Required): Select the mode of learning you prefer.
* **Preferred Intake Period** (Field 16, Select one, Required): Select the intake period you prefer.
* **How did you hear about our training center?** (Field 17, Select one, Optional): Select how you heard about the center.
* **Any questions or specific requirements?** (Field 18, Text, Optional): If you have any questions or specific requirements, please mention them here.
* **Parent/Guardian Consent (If applicant is under 18)** (Field 19, Text, Optional): If you are under 18, please provide your parent/guardian's name and contact information.
