# regional_academic_decathlon_tournament_entry_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for teams to register for the regional academic decathlon tournament. It's a one-time entry form for capturing team and school information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Team Details section with your team's information.
2. Enter your school's details in the School Details section.
3. List your team members in the Team Members section. You can select as many members as your team has.
4. Enter your school's name and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Team Details** (Team Details, text, required: false): Enter your team's name, description, and any other relevant details.
- **School Details** (School Details, text, required: false): Enter your school's name and any other relevant details.
- **Team Members** (Team Members, select_multiple, required: false): Select all team members from the list. Please ensure that each member is listed only once.
- **School Name** (School Name, text, required: false): Enter your school's name.
- **School Email** (School Email, email, required: false): Enter your school's email address.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your team and school information for accuracy.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the tournament organizers.
- You can submit this form only once per team. If you've already submitted, you won't be able to resubmit the same team.
