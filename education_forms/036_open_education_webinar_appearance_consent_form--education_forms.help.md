# Open Education Webinar Appearance Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect user consent and demographic information for an open education webinar. It is essential to complete this form before attending the webinar to ensure that you understand and agree to the terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

* Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
* Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
* Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
* Select your preferred presentation time in the "What best describes your preferred presentation time" field.
* Enter any additional comments or notes in the "Note" field.
* Read and agree to the terms and conditions in the "Agree to terms and conditions" field.
* Click the "Submit" button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your full name as it should be displayed in the webinar.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No (optional)
* **Email**: Enter your email address that you use to receive communications from us.
	+ Type: email
	+ Required: No (optional)
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No (optional)
* **Time**: Select your preferred presentation time for the webinar.
	+ Type: time
	+ Required: No (optional)
* **What best describes your preferred presentation time**: Select one or more of the options that best describe your preferred presentation time.
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Required: Yes
* **Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes that you would like to share with us.
	+ Type: note
	+ Required: No (optional)
* **Agree to terms and conditions**: Select "I agree to the terms and conditions" to confirm that you have read and understand the terms and conditions.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: Yes
* **Agree to terms and conditions 2**: Select "I agree to the terms and conditions 2" to confirm that you have read and understand the terms and conditions 2.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: Yes
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit the form.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No (optional)
* **Name 2**: Enter your name (if you have multiple names to enter).
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No (optional)
* **Agree to terms and conditions 2**: Select "I agree to the terms and conditions 2" to confirm that you have read and understand the terms and conditions 2.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: Yes
* **Submit 2**: Click this button to submit the second part of the form.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No (optional)

Note: The fields "Name 2", "Agree to terms and conditions 2", and "Submit 2" seem to be redundant and should be removed or clarified to avoid confusion.
