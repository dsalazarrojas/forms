# internship_program_funding_approval_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The internship program funding approval form is used to request funding for internship programs. It is a crucial document that helps the university or organization to review and approve funding requests for internship programs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the program name and start and end dates.
2. Enter the organization name where the internship will take place.
3. Enter the student's name and email address.
4. Select the program duration and funding amount.
5. Choose the funding terms and program status.
6. Add any additional comments or notes.
7. Select the program type and location.
8. Enter the department and job title of the contact person.
9. Enter the supervisor's name.
10. Enter the contact person's name.
11. Select the funding source and approval level.
12. Add any comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Name**: (`program_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the internship program.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the start date of the internship program.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the end date of the internship program.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the organization where the internship will take place.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the student participating in the internship program.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the student participating in the internship program.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the student participating in the internship program.
* **Program Duration** (`program_duration`, number, required/optional): Enter the duration of the internship program.
* **Funding Amount** (`funding_amount`, number, required/optional): Enter the funding amount requested for the internship program.
* **Funding Terms** (`funding_terms`, select_one, required/optional): Select the funding terms for the internship program (Full, Partial, None).
* **Program Status** (`program_status`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the program status (Approved, Not Approved, Under Review).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the internship program.
* **Email Confirmation** (`email_confirmation`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address to receive email confirmation of the funding approval.
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of approval for the internship program.
* **Approval Time** (`approval_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of approval for the internship program.
* **Program Type** (`program_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of the internship program (Full-Time, Part-Time, Internship).
* **Program Location** (`program_location`, select_one, required/optional): Select the location of the internship program (On Campus, Remote, Hybrid).
* **Department** (`department`, text, required/optional): Enter the department name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required/optional): Enter the job title of the contact person.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the supervisor.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Funding Source** (`funding_source`, select_one, required/optional): Select the funding source (University, Company, Other).
* **Approval Level** (`approval_level`, select_one, required/optional): Select the approval level (Director, Manager, Assistant).
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required/optional): Add any comments or notes.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of submission for the internship program.
