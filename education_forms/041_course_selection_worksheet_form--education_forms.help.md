# Course Selection Worksheet Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Course Selection Worksheet Form is a flexible template designed to help you select courses based on your preferences. This form guides you through the process of choosing courses that suit your needs and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the desired course from the text field.
2. Provide your preferred institution(s) in the text field.
3. Enter your preferred priority level (High, Medium, or Low) in the dropdown menu.
4. Enter any additional preferences or details in the text field.
5. Select your preferred instructor in the text field.
6. Enter your preferred program in the text field.
7. Provide the location where you'd like to take the course in the text field.
8. Enter the desired dates for the course in the date field.
9. Enter the desired times for the course in the time field.
10. Enter your email address in the email field.
11. Enter your phone number in the text field.
12. Finally, select your desired action: "Save and Continue Later" or "Submit and Finish".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course** (`course_selection`, text): Enter the title of the course you're interested in.
* **Institution** (`institutions`, text): Provide the name of the institution offering the course.
* **Priority** (`priority`, select_one): Select your preferred priority level for the course (High, Medium, Low).
* **Priority Hint** (`priority_hint`, text): Enter any additional information about your priority level.
* **Preferences** (`preferences`, text): Enter any additional preferences or details about the course.
* **Instructor** (`instructor`, text): Enter the name of your preferred instructor.
* **Instructor Hint** (`instructor_hint`, text): Enter any additional information about your preferred instructor.
* **Program** (`program`, text): Enter the program or department offering the course.
* **Program Hint** (`program_hint`, text): Enter any additional information about the program offering the course.
* **Location** (`location`, text): Provide the location where you'd like to take the course.
* **Location Hint** (`location_hint`, text): Enter any additional information about the location.
* **Dates** (`dates`, date): Enter the desired dates for the course.
* **Dates Hint** (`dates_hint`, text): Enter any additional information about the dates.
* **Times** (`times`, time): Enter the desired times for the course.
* **Times Hint** (`times_hint`, text): Enter any additional information about the times.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address.
* **Email Hint** (`email_hint`, text): Enter any additional information about your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter your phone number.
* **Phone Hint** (`phone_hint`, text): Enter any additional information about your phone number.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_multiple): Select "Save and Continue Later" or "Submit and Finish" to complete the form.
*   Save and Continue Later: Save your progress and continue later.
*   Submit and Finish: Submit the form and complete the course selection process.
 
This form is designed to be flexible and easy to use. If you have any questions or need help, feel free to ask.
