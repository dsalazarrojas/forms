# Student Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Admission Form is designed to collect relevant information from students who are seeking admission into the institution. This information will be used to facilitate the admission process and ensure that the institution has accurate records about potential students.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and ID in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your date of birth in the "date_of_birth" field in the format of dd/mm/yyyy.
3. Provide your contact number in the "contact_number" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
5. Fill in your address in the "address" field.
6. Select your student type from the options provided in the "student_type" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Student ID**: Enter your student ID or any other unique identifier.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format of dd/mm/yyyy (e.g., 12/12/1990).
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where the institution can send you important updates.
* **Address**: Enter your full address where you can be contacted at.
* **Student Type**: Select your student type from the options provided:
	+ **Student**: Regular student.
	+ **Part-time**: Part-time student.
	+ **Full-time**: Full-time student.
* **Program Name**: Enter the program or course you are applying for (optional).
