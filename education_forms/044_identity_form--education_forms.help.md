## Identity Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Identity Form is a document used to collect and verify the identity of students. It is intended to be completed by students to provide accurate and up-to-date information about themselves.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Identity Form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" to answer the question regarding uploading a Selfie.
3. Enter your Photo in the provided text field.
4. Type your ID Number in the provided text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Upload Selfie** (`user_input`, select_multiple, optional): This field is used to select whether you want to upload a Selfie. You can choose between "Yes" and "No" to indicate your preference.
* **Photo** (`photo`, text, optional): Enter your photo details in this field. Please provide the photo details, such as the photo's description, location, and any other relevant information.
* **ID Number** (`id_number`, text, optional): Please enter your student ID number in this field. This is an important field to ensure accurate identity verification.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure the form is complete and accurate.
- If you're unsure about any field, please consult with your instructor or contact the form administrator for assistance.
- Please note that the fields marked as "optional" can be filled out at your discretion.
