# Peer Tutoring Leadership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Peer Tutoring Leadership Application Form is designed to collect information from peer tutors who are interested in taking on leadership roles within the peer tutoring program. This form helps us to better understand the structure and composition of our peer tutoring team and to identify potential candidates for leadership positions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your general information, including any relevant details about yourself.
2. Provide your contact information, such as your email and phone number.
3. Enter your student ID and contact ID (if applicable).
4. Select your role in peers and role in tutoring to indicate your leadership aspirations.
5. Enter the average hours per week you spend in tutoring and leading.
6. Submit the form once you have filled out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (Page 1)** (`general_info`, text, optional): This section is where you can provide any relevant information about yourself, such as your name, interests, or experience in the program.
* **Contact Information (Page 1)** (`contact_info`, text, optional): This section is where you can provide your email address and phone number so that we can contact you about your application.
* **Student ID (Page 2)** (`student_id`, number, optional): This field is used to identify you as a student in the program. If you don't have a student ID, please leave this field blank.
* **Contact ID (Page 2)** (`contact_id`, number, optional): This field is used to identify you as a contact person. If you don't have a contact ID, please leave this field blank.
* **Phone Number (Page 2)** (`phone_number`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter your phone number so that we can contact you about your application.
* **Email (Page 2)** (`email`, email, optional): This field is where you can enter your email address so that we can contact you about your application.
* **Role in Peers (Page 3)** (`role_in_peers`, select one, optional): This field allows you to select your role in the peer tutoring program:
	+ Peer Mentor: A peer mentor is a student who has taken on a leadership role in supporting their peers.
	+ Team Member: A team member is a student who is part of a peer tutoring team but does not have a leadership role.
	+ Team Lead: A team lead is a student who has taken on a leadership role in leading their peer tutoring team.
	+ Other: If none of the above options apply to you, please choose "Other" and describe your role in the notes field.
* **Role in Tutoring (Page 3)** (`role_in_tutoring`, select one, optional): This field allows you to select your role in the tutoring program:
	+ Peer Mentor: A peer mentor is a student who has taken on a leadership role in supporting their peers in tutoring.
	+ Tutor: A tutor is a student who provides one-on-one support to their peers.
	+ Assistant Director: An assistant director is a student who has taken on a leadership role in assisting the director of the peer tutoring program.
	+ Other: If none of the above options apply to you, please choose "Other" and describe your role in the notes field.
* **Average hours per week in tutoring (Page 4)** (`hours_per_week`, number, optional): This field is where you can enter the average number of hours per week you spend in tutoring.
* **Average hours per week leading (Page 4)** (`average_hours_per_week`, number, optional): This field is where you can enter the average number of hours per week you spend leading in the peer tutoring program.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, please contact us.
* We appreciate your commitment to the peer tutoring program and look forward to hearing from you.
