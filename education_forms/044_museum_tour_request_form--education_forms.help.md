# Museum Tour Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help schools schedule a guided tour at the museum. Please fill out this form to request a tour date, time, and other details for your visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for your school:
	* **School Name**: Enter the name of your school.
	* **Tour Date**: Choose a date for your tour.
	* **Start Time**: Select the time you would like your tour to begin.
	* **End Time**: Select the time you would like your tour to end.
	* **Duration**: Choose the length of your tour (in minutes).
2. Provide additional details about your tour:
	* **Tour Guide**: Enter the name of your tour guide (if you have one).
	* **Number of Students**: Enter the number of students in your group.
	* **Student Age Range**: Choose which age ranges will be participating in the tour.
3. Add your contact information:
	* **School Email**: Enter the email address of your school.
	* **School Phone**: Enter the phone number of your school.
4. Specify the focus of your tour:
	* **Tour Topic**: Enter a brief description of what you would like your tour to focus on.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name**: Enter the official name of your school.
* **Tour Date**: Select a date for your tour from the calendar.
* **Start Time**: Choose the time you would like your tour to begin.
* **End Time**: Choose the time you would like your tour to end.
* **Duration**: Enter the number of minutes you would like your tour to last.
* **Tour Guide**: If you have a specific tour guide, enter their name here.
* **Number of Students**: Enter the number of students in your group.
* **Student Age Range**: Choose which age ranges will be participating in the tour (Yes for the selected age groups, No otherwise).
* **School Email**: Enter the email address of your school for communication purposes.
* **School Phone**: Enter the phone number of your school in case we need to contact you.
* **Tour Topic**: Enter a brief description of what you would like your tour to focus on.
* **Comments**: Leave any additional comments or requests for your tour.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when selecting your tour date, start time, and end time to ensure we can accommodate your request.
* Please note that we will need at least 30 days' notice for large groups or special requests.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at your school's email or phone number.
