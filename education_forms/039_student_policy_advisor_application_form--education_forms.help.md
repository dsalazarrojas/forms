# Student Policy Advisor Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Policy Advisor Application Form is designed to help students apply for the Policy Advisor position. This role is crucial in ensuring that students receive guidance on university policies and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form accurately and honestly.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed.
3. Review and update the form before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Please provide your name, email address, and phone number so that we can contact you regarding your application.
* **Student Details** (`student_details`, text, optional): Share your academic background, including your student ID number, and any relevant academic achievements.
* **Education Details** (`education_details`, text, optional): Provide information about your educational background, including your degree, institution, and dates of study.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, optional): Share any relevant work experience that you have, including job titles, dates, and a brief description of your job responsibilities.
* **References** (`references`, text, optional): Provide the names and contact information of at least two professional references, including their job titles and relationship with you.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and honestly.
* You can ask for help if you are unsure about any field.
* Submit your application once you have completed all the required fields.
