# Quiz On Inclusion Policies - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help staff understand and provide feedback on the company's inclusion policies. The form is intended to be a self-assessment tool for staff to evaluate their understanding of the policies and provide suggestions for improvement. It is also used to collect information about staff members, such as their job title, department, and contact details. The goal of this form is to ensure that staff is aware of and adheres to the company's inclusion policies, and to identify areas for policy revision.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by reading through each page of the form, and answer the questions and provide feedback as needed.
2. For the "staff_understanding" page, provide a short paragraph explaining your understanding of the company's inclusion policies.
3. On the "feedback_on_policies" page, provide any feedback or suggestions you have about the policies.
4. On the "current_policies" page, select the policies that you currently adhere to from the list of options.
5. On the "improving_culture" page, select the policies that you think should be revised or improved.
6. For the remaining pages, provide any additional comments or information as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **staff_understanding** (Required/Optional): A short paragraph explaining your understanding of the company's inclusion policies.
* **feedback_on_policies** (Required/Optional): A short paragraph providing feedback or suggestions about the company's inclusion policies.
* **current_policies** (Select One): Select the policies that you currently adhere to from the list of options.
* **improving_culture** (Select Multiple): Select the policies that you think should be revised or improved from the list of options.
