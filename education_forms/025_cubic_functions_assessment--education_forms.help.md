# Cubic Functions Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cubic Functions Assessment is a form designed to evaluate a student's understanding of cubic functions. This form is meant to gather data for educational assessment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Answer the mixed questions in the dropdown menu.
* Provide your answers to each question in the text field below.
* Upload your solution file as a file attachment.
* Rate your confidence in each answer from High, Medium, Low, or Unknown.
* Explain your reason for rating your confidence in the text field.
* Add any comments or additional information in the text field.
* Fill in your name and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Mixed Questions** (`select_multiple`, required: false): This is a dropdown menu where you choose your answer to the mixed question.
	+ Choose Yes or No for each question.
* **What is your answer for question 1?** (`text`, required: false): This is where you provide your answer to question 1.
* **What is your answer for question 2?** (`text`, required: false): This is where you provide your answer to question 2.
* **What is your answer for question 3?** (`text`, required: false): This is where you provide your answer to question 3.
* **Upload Solution** (`text`, required: false): This is where you upload your solution file as an attachment.
* **Confidence Rating** (`select_one`, required: false): This is a dropdown menu where you rate your confidence in your answers.
	+ Choose High, Medium, Low, or Unknown.
* **Reason for Confidence Rating** (`text`, required: false): This is where you explain your reason for rating your confidence in your answers.
* **Comments** (`note`, required: false): This is where you add any comments or additional information.
* **Student Name** (`text`, required: false): This is where you fill in your name.
* **Student Email** (`email`, required: false): This is where you fill in your email address.
