# Research Study Group Availability - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Research Study Group Availability" form is designed to gather information from research group members about their availability and preferences for meetings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Go through each page and fill out the fields as needed.
*   The form is divided into several pages, each covering different aspects of the research group's availability and preferences.
*   Fill out the fields as accurately and concisely as possible, and make sure to answer all required fields.
*   When you're done, review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Member Schedules** (<member_schedules>): This field is not directly used in this form, as it seems to be a placeholder for a different type of field. You can ignore it.
*   **Meeting Formats** (<meeting_formats>): Select the formats in which you are available to meet. You can choose from "Yes" or "No".
*   **Contact Details** (<contact_details>): Please enter your contact details, including your email and phone number. This will help the project coordinator get in touch with you.
*   **Preferred Meeting Time** (<preferred_meeting_time>): Enter your preferred meeting time. This is a date field, so make sure to use the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
*   **Start Date** (<start_date>): Enter the start date of your availability. This is also a date field, so use the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
*   **End Date** (<end_date>): Enter the end date of your availability. Again, use the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
*   **Preferred Meeting Days** (<preferred_meeting_days>): Select the days you are available to meet. You can choose from "Yes" or "No".
*   **Research Session Details** (<research_session_details>): Enter any additional details about your research sessions.
*   **Research Session Time** (<research_session_time>): Enter the time of your research sessions.
*   **Preferred Meeting Format** (<preferred_meeting_format>): Select the format you prefer for your meetings. You can choose from "Yes" or "No".
*   **Meeting Location** (<meeting_location>): Enter the location where you would like to meet.
*   **Research Session Length** (<research_session_length>): Enter the length of your research sessions.
*   **Preferred Meeting Time** (<preferred_meeting_time>): Enter your preferred meeting time. This is a time field, so make sure to use the correct format (HH:MM).
*   **Member Email** (<member_email>): Please enter your email address.
*   **Member Phone** (<member_phone>): Enter your phone number.
*   **Member Notes** (<member_notes>): Enter any additional notes about yourself.
*   **Meeting Notes** (<meeting_notes>): Enter any notes about your meetings.
*   **Lab Meeting Schedule** (<lab_meeting_schedule>): Enter your lab meeting schedule.
*   **Lab Meeting Details** (<lab_meeting_details>): Enter any additional details about your lab meetings.
*   **Lab Meeting Format** (<lab_meeting_format>): Select the format of your lab meetings. You can choose from "Yes" or "No".
*   **Research Session Frequency** (<research_session_frequency>): Enter the frequency of your research sessions.
*   **Research Session Frequency Details** (<research_session_frequency_details>): Enter any additional details about your research session frequency.
*   **Meeting Day** (<meeting_day>): Select the days you are available to meet. You can choose from "Yes" or "No".
*   **Meeting Time** (<meeting_time>): Enter the time of your meetings. This is a time field, so make sure to use the correct format (HH:MM).
*   **Coordinator Email** (<coordinator_email>): Please enter the email address of the project coordinator.

**Tips**

*   Make sure to answer all required fields.
*   Be as accurate and concise as possible when filling out the form.
*   Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
