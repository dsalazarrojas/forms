## Purpose
The Transpiration Process Quiz is a tool used to evaluate a user's knowledge of transpiration in plants. It is essential to understand plant transpiration to comprehend how plants absorb and process water, which is crucial for their survival.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Transpiration Process Quiz, follow these steps:

* Carefully read and understand each question carefully.
* Fill in your answer to each question according to your knowledge.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting.
