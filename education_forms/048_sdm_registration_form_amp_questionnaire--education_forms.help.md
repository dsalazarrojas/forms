## sdms_registration_form_questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users for SDM (Software Development Manager) registration purposes. It captures essential details such as name, contact information, SDM level, experience, and other relevant details for registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email and Confirm Email to ensure accurate and secure contact information.
3. Create a Username and Confirm Password for secure access.
4. Provide your Phone number for contact purposes.
5. Enter your Date of Birth and Time of Birth for identification purposes.
6. Select your SDM Level from the options provided.
7. Choose your SDM Experience from the available options.
8. Select your SDM Specialty from the options provided.
9. Choose your SDM Status from the options provided.
10. Add any additional Notes if necessary.
11. Select your Category of registration (Education, Personal, or Work).
12. Enter your Form ID for tracking purposes.
13. Select your SDM Education level from the options provided.
14. Select your SDM Certifications from the options provided.
15. Choose your SDM License from the options provided.
16. Select your SDM Registration Type (Online or Offline).
17. Enter the SDM Registration Date and Time.
18. Provide the Assigned Tool for your SDM.
19. Fill in who submitted the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Confirm Email** (`email_confirmation`, email, required): Confirm your email address.
* **Username** (`username`, text, required): Create a unique username for secure access.
* **Password** (`password`, text, required): Enter a secure password for access.
* **Confirm Password** (`password_confirmation`, text, required): Confirm your password.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth for identification purposes.
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, required): Enter your time of birth for identification purposes.
* **SDM Level** (`sdm_level`, select_one, required): Select your SDM level from the options provided.
* **SDM Experience** (`sdm_experience`, select_multiple, required): Choose your SDM experience from the options provided.
* **SDM Specialty** (`sdm_specialty`, select_multiple, required): Select your SDM specialty from the options provided.
* **SDM Status** (`sdm_status`, select_one, required): Choose your SDM status from the options provided.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Add any additional notes if necessary.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required): Select the category of your registration (Education, Personal, or Work).
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, required): Enter your Form ID for tracking purposes.
* **SDM Education** (`sdm_education`, select_multiple, required): Select your SDM education level from the options provided.
* **SDM Certifications** (`sdm_certification`, select_multiple, required): Select your SDM certifications from the options provided.
* **SDM License** (`sdm_license`, select_multiple, required): Choose your SDM license from the options provided.
* **SDM Registration Type** (`sdm_registration_type`, select_one, required): Select your SDM registration type (Online or Offline).
* **SDM Registration Date** (`sdm_registration_date`, date, required): Enter the SDM registration date.
* **SDM Registration Time** (`sdm_registration_time`, time, required): Enter the SDM registration time.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required): Provide the assigned tool for your SDM.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required): Fill in who submitted the form.

Please make sure to fill in all required fields accurately for successful registration.
