# teacher_favorite_things_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your favorite things as a teacher, such as your favorite food, color, book, and activity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Answer each question honestly and accurately to the best of your abilities.
2. Fill out the form in its entirety, making sure to provide your favorite food, color, book, and activity.
3. Make sure to provide a valid response for each field, as indicated by the "required" label next to some fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Favorite Food** (text, Optional): Please input your favorite type of cuisine or food you enjoy.
* **Favorite Color** (text, Optional): Enter the color you like best or associate with.
* **Favorite Book** (text, Optional): Input the title of your favorite book or series.
* **Favorite Activity** (text, Optional): Specify your favorite hobby or pastime.

## Tips
- Be creative and have fun with your responses.
- Please ensure that your responses are your own and not someone else's.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact form@gic.mx.
