# Research To Practice Bridge Program Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Research to Practice Bridge Program Application form is designed to collect information from individuals applying for the Research to Practice Bridge Program. The purpose of this program is to connect research and practice in a specific field or industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your researcher information, including any relevant background details.
2. Provide information about your practice area, such as relevant work experience or projects.
3. Submit your application, ensuring you have reviewed and agreed to the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Researcher Information (1)** (`researcher_info`, Optional):
	+ This section is for you to share your background and qualifications related to the Research to Practice Bridge Program.
* **Practice Information (2)** (`practice_info`, Optional):
	+ Share your experience and projects related to the practice area you're interested in.
* **Application Information (3)** (`application_info`, Optional):
	+ Provide any additional information about your application, such as motivation or goals.
* **Review and Submission (4)** (`review`, Optional):
	+ This section is for you to review and submit your application. Please carefully review your submission before finalizing it.
* **Submit (5)** (`submit`, Optional):
	+ Once you have reviewed and confirmed your submission, click the "Submit" button to complete your application.
