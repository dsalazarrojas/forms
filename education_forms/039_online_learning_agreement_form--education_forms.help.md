# Online Learning Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to establish an agreement between the online learning platform and the instructor teaching a course. It collects information about the course, such as the course name, start and end dates and times, instructor contact information, and the duration of the agreement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the course.
2. Select the start date and time of the course.
3. Select the end date and time of the course.
4. Enter the email address of the instructor.
5. Enter the phone number of the instructor.
6. Select the instructor(s) teaching the course.
7. Enter the duration of the agreement.
8. Select the start date of the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Name** (`online_learning_agreement_form`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the course.
* **Start Date** (`online_learning_agreement_form_start_date`, date, required: false): Select the start date of the course.
* **Start Time** (`online_learning_agreement_form_start_time`, time, required: false): Select the start time of the course.
* **End Date** (`online_learning_agreement_form_end_date`, date, required: false): Select the end date of the course.
* **End Time** (`online_learning_agreement_form_end_time`, time, required: false): Select the end time of the course.
* **Instructor Email** (`online_learning_agreement_form_instructor_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the instructor.
* **Instructor Phone** (`online_learning_agreement_form_instructor_telephone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the instructor.
* **Course Instructors** (`online_learning_agreement_form_course_instructors`, select multiple, required: false): Select the instructor(s) teaching the course.
* **Agreement Duration** (`online_learning_agreement_form_agreement_duration`, number, required: false): Enter the duration of the agreement in weeks.
* **Agreement Start Date** (`online_learning_agreement_form_agreement_start_date`, date, required: false): Select the start date of the agreement.

## Tips

* Ensure you have reviewed the terms and conditions of the agreement carefully before filling out this form.
* Enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure the agreement is valid and enforceable. 
* If you are unsure about any field, ask your administrator or contact support for assistance.
