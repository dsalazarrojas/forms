# Healthcare Conference Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect attendee details for a healthcare conference. It is a one-time registration form and is not intended for repeat usage.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Provide your contact information and details about your attendance.
2.  Choose the conferences you will be attending.
3.  Select your accommodation and transportation preferences.
4.  Choose your contact method and dietary requirements.
5.  Provide any medical requirements and special requests.
6.  Add any comments you would like to share with the conference organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Details**: Enter your name, email address, and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be reached during the conference.
* **Conferences Attending**: Select all conferences you will be attending.
* **Interested Conferences**: Select which conferences you are interested in attending.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Date of Arrival**: Enter your expected date of arrival at the conference location.
* **Time of Arrival**: Enter your expected time of arrival at the conference location.
* **Time of Departure**: Enter your expected time of departure from the conference location.
* **Accommodation Preferences**: Choose where you would like to stay during the conference.
* **Transportation Preferences**: Choose how you would like to travel to and from the conference location.
* **Contact Method**: Select your preferred contact method during the conference.
* **Dietary Requirement**: Choose your dietary requirement.
* **Medical Requirements**: Check the boxes if you have any medical requirements.
* **Special Requests**: Add any special requests or information you would like to share with the conference organizers.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or information about your attendance.

Note: All fields marked as `required` are mandatory and must be filled out.
