# Student Government Purchase Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Government Purchase Request Form is used to submit a formal request for purchase to the student government. This form helps to track and verify purchases, ensuring that all necessary information is captured accurately and consistently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields, marked with <font face="Arial Black">Required</font>. This includes Form Id and Amount.
2. Be brief but clear in your Description.
3. Choose the correct Department Name from the options provided.
4. Select the correct status for Approved By.
5. Fill out the Purchase Date in the correct format.
6. Enter the Total Cost.
7. Add any additional Remarks if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Government Purchase Request** (`student_government_purchase_request_form`, text, false): Provide a brief description of the purchase request.
* **Form Id** (`form_id`, number, true): A unique identifier for this form.
* **Description** (`description`, text, false): A brief description of the purchase.
* **Amount** (`amount`, number, true): The total cost of the purchase.
* **Purpose** (`purpose`, text, false): A brief statement of the purpose of the purchase.
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`, select_one, false): The status of the purchase, either Approved, Denied, or Pending.
* **Department Name** (`department_name`, select_multiple, false): Choose the department that the purchase is for.
* **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`, date, false): The date of the purchase.
* **Total Cost** (`total_cost`, number, false): The total cost of the purchase.
* **Remarks** (`remarks`, text, false): Additional comments or notes about the purchase.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
* Double-check that your Department Name selection is correct.
* Use a valid date format for Purchase Date.
* If unsure about any information, contact the responsible authority for clarification.
* Only submit one request per form.
