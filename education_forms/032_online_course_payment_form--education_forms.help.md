# Online Course Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The online course payment form is designed to collect payment information from students to enable instructors to receive payment for their courses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the course title, price, and description of the course you wish to pay for.
2. Enter your student email address.
3. Enter your instructor's email address (if applicable).
4. Select the payment method you wish to use (e.g., Card Number, Card Expiration Date, Card Security Code).
5. Choose the type of payment method you want to use (e.g., Credit, Debit, Other).
6. If you choose "Other" as the payment method, enter the details in the "Card Details" field.
7. Optionally, enter your date of birth.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Title**: Enter the title of the course you are paying for.
* **Price**: Enter the price of the course.
* **Description**: Enter a brief description of the course.
* **Student Email**: Enter your email address as a student.
* **Instructor Email**: Enter the instructor's email address if applicable.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method you wish to use (e.g., Card Number, Card Expiration Date, Card Security Code).
* **Type**: Choose the type of payment method you want to use (e.g., Credit, Debit, Other).
* **Card Details**: If you choose "Other" as the payment method, enter the details in this field.
* **Other Type**: If you choose "Other" as the payment method, enter the type of payment method you are using.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth (if required by the instructor).
