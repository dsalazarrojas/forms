# Moodle Training Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users who wish to request Moodle training. It is used to track and manage training requests within an organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out your First name, Last name, and Email fields with accurate information.
2. Select the course you wish to be trained on (Yes/No).
3. Choose the start date for the training (in date format).
4. Optionally, select the duration of the training (Yes/No).
5. Select the training mode you wish to be trained on (Yes/No, multiple selections allowed).
6. Enter the level of training you wish to achieve (optional).
7. If applicable, select whether a tool will be assigned to you (Yes/No).
8. If applicable, select the user that will be assigned to train you (Yes/No).
9. Enter any comments or notes you have about your request.
10. Confirm that you have submitted this form by selecting (Yes) for "Submitted" and providing accurate "Submit date" and "Submit city", "Submit country", "Submit region" fields.
11. Optionally, you may select additional details about your submission (Submit time, submitted IP address, submitted state, submitted postal).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Training course** (`course`, select_one, required): Select the course you wish to be trained on.
* **Prophets** (`start_date`, date, required): Choose the start date for your training.
* **Training duration** (`end_date`, select_one, optional): Optionally, select the duration of your training.
* **Training mode** (`training_mode`, select_multiple, optional): Select the mode of training you wish to be trained on.
* **Training level** (`training_level`, text, optional): Enter the level of training you wish to achieve.
* **Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): If applicable, select whether a tool will be assigned to you.
* **User** (`assigned_user`, select_one, optional): If applicable, select the user that will be assigned to train you.
* **Submission** (`submitted`, text, optional): Enter any comments or notes about your submission.
* **Submit date** (`submitted_at`, date, required): Confirm that you have submitted this form by selecting a date.
* **Submit time** (`submitted_time`, time, optional): Optionally, select a submit time.
* **Submit ip** (`submitted_ip`, text, required): Enter your IP address at the time of submission.
* **Submit city** (`submitted_city`, text, required): Enter the city at the time of submission.
* **Submit country** (`submitted_country`, text, required): Enter the country at the time of submission.
* **Submit region** (`submitted_region`, text, optional): Optionally, enter the region at the time of submission.
* **Submit postal** (`submitted_postal`, text, optional): Optionally, enter the postal code at the time of submission.
* **Submit state** (`submitted_state`, text, optional): Optionally, enter the state at the time of submission.
* **Submit language** (`submitted_language`, text, optional): Optionally, enter the language at the time of submission.
* **Submitter** (`submitted_by`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have submitted this form by selecting (Yes).
* **Submit time** (`submitted_time`, time, optional): Optionally, select the time of submission.
