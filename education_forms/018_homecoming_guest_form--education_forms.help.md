# homecoming_guest_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The homecoming_guest_form is used to capture information about guests who are returning home after a period of absence. This form is designed to be filled out by the guests themselves, their representatives, or other authorized individuals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your **Email** in the "Email" field. Please use a valid email address.
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it is commonly known or used.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it is commonly known or used.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address that you have access to.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips
* Please double-check that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to the relevant authority for clarification.
* Make sure to save or print your completed form for your records.
