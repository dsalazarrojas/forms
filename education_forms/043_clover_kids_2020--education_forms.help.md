# Clover Kids 2020 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Clover Kids 2020** form is a comprehensive tool used to collect essential information about your child for participation in our program. This form helps us understand your child's background, interests, and needs to provide a safe and enjoyable experience for them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out.
3. Double-check your answers before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Child's Name** (Required)
* Child's Name (`child_name`, `text`, required)
	+ Enter your child's first and last name.

### 2. **Child's Age** (Required)
* Child's Age (`child_age`, `number`, required)
	+ Enter your child's age in years.

### 3. **School Grade** (Required)
* School Grade (`child_grade`, `select_one`, required)
	+ Select your child's current grade level.

### 4. **Parent or Guardian Information** (Optional)
* Parent or Guardian Information (`section_contact`, `note`, required=false)
	+ This section is for any additional contact details you'd like to provide.

### 5. **Parent or Guardian Name** (Required)
* Parent or Guardian Name (`parent_name`, `text`, required)
	+ Enter your full name.

### 6. **Phone Number** (Required)
* Phone Number (`parent_phone`, `text`, required)
	+ Enter your contact phone number.

### 7. **Email Address** (Required)
* Email Address (`parent_email`, `email`, required)
	+ Enter your contact email.

### 8. **Address Information** (Optional)
* Address Information (`section_address`, `note`, required=false)
	+ This section is for any additional address details you'd like to provide.

### 9. **Street Address** (Required)
* Street Address (`street_address`, `text`, required)
	+ Enter your full street address.

### 10. **City** (Required)
* City (`city`, `text`, required)
	+ Enter the city or town where you live.

### 11. **State or Province** (Required)
* State or Province (`state`, `text`, required)
	+ Enter the state abbreviation.

### 12. **Zip or Postal Code** (Required)
* Zip or Postal Code (`zip_code`, `text`, required)
	+ Enter your postal code.

### 13. **Program Information** (Optional)
* Program Information (`section_program`, `note`, required=false)
	+ This section is for any additional program details you'd like to provide.

### 14. **Participation Level** (Required)
* Participation Level (`participation_level`, `select_one`, required)
	+ Select how your child will participate in the program.

### 15. **Primary Club Focus Area** (Required)
* Primary Club Focus Area (`club_focus`, `select_one`, required)
	+ Select your child's primary area of interest.

### 16. **Child's Interests** (Optional)
* Child's Interests (`section_interests`, `note`, required=false)
	+ This section is for any additional interests or hobbies your child enjoys.

### 17. **Interests or Hobbies** (Required)
* Interests or Hobbies (`child_interests`, `select_multiple`, required)
	+ Select your child's interests or hobbies.

### 18. **Learning Goals for This Year** (Optional)
* Learning Goals for This Year (`learning_goals`, `text`, required=false)
	+ Enter any goals your child hopes to achieve this year.

### 19. **Health and Safety Information** (Optional)
* Health and Safety Information (`section_health`, `note`, required=false)
	+ This section is for any important medical information or allergies.

### 20. **Allergies** (Optional)
* Allergies (`allergies`, `text`, required=false)
	+ List any food, environmental, or other allergies your child may have.

### 21. **Significant Medical Conditions** (Optional)
* Significant Medical Conditions (`medical_conditions`, `text`, required=false)
	+ List any significant medical conditions your child may have.

### 22. **Medications** (Optional)
* Medications (`medications`, `text`, required=false)
	+ List any medications your child may be taking.

### 23. **Emergency Contact Name** (Required)
* Emergency Contact Name (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required)
	+ Enter another adult's name that we can contact in case of an emergency.

### 24. **Emergency Contact Phone** (Required)
* Emergency Contact Phone (`emergency_phone`, `text`, required)
	+ Enter the emergency contact's phone number.

### 25. **Consent** (Required)
* I consent to my child's participation in Clover Kids (`parental_consent`, `select_one`, required)
	+ Confirm your consent for your child's participation.
