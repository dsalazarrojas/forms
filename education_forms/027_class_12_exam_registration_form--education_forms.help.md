# Class 12 Exam Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Class 12 Exam Registration Form is designed to collect necessary information from students to facilitate a smooth exam registration process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
3. Review your information carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Full Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on official records.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required): Select your gender from the provided options.
* **Student ID Number** (`student_id`, text, required): Enter your school identification number.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your current school.
* **School Code** (`school_code`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your school code.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Email** (`parent_email`, email, required): Enter the email of your parent or guardian for correspondence.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, required): Enter the best contact number of your parent or guardian.
* **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`, text, required): Enter your mailing address in the required format (street, city, state, ZIP).
* **Exam Year** (`exam_year`, select_one, required): Select the year you will take the exam.
* **Preferred Exam Session** (`exam_session`, select_one, required): Select your preferred exam session.
* **Academic Stream** (`stream_selection`, select_one, required): Select your academic stream.
* **Core Subject 1** (`subject_1`, select_one, required): Select the first core subject.
* **Core Subject 2** (`subject_2`, select_one, required): Select the second core subject.
* **Core Subject 3** (`subject_3`, select_one, required): Select the third core subject.
* **Core Subject 4** (`subject_4`, select_one, required): Select the fourth core subject.
* **Optional Subject 5** (`subject_5`, select_one, optional): Select the optional subject if applicable.
* **Is This Your First Attempt** (`previous_exam_attempt`, select_one, required): Indicate if this is your first attempt or if you have taken the exams before.
* **Do You Need Special Accommodations** (`special_requirements`, select_one, required): Indicate if you need special accommodations for any reason.
* **Describe Required Accommodations** (`accommodations_details`, text, optional): If yes, describe the required accommodations.
* **Preferred Exam Center Location** (`exam_center_preference`, text, optional): Enter the preferred exam center location.
* **Registration Fee Payment Status** (`registration_fee`, select_one, required): Indicate your payment status for the registration fee.
* **Do You Declare the Information Is Correct** (`declaration`, select_one, required): Indicate that you declare the provided information is correct.
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or questions you have.
