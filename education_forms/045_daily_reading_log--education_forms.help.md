# Daily Reading Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Reading Log form is a tool to collect and track reading activity, providing a structured format to record and reflect on reading experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the "Reading Log" section with a brief description of your reading activity.
2. Provide a reflection on the reading in the "Reflection" section, including your thoughts, feelings, or any notable points.
3. Set goals for the next reading session in the "Goals" section.
4. Enter the date and time of the reading activity in the "Date" and "Time" sections.
5. Choose one or more categories related to the reading (e.g., fiction, non-fiction, self-help, etc.) in the "Category" section.
6. Select the progress you made on the reading (e.g., high, medium, low) in the "Progress" section.
7. Optionally, select who submitted the log (e.g., a user or a team member) in the "Submitted By" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reading Log**: A text field to describe the content of the reading activity.
* **Reflection**: A text field to reflect on the reading, including thoughts, feelings, or notable points.
* **Goals**: A text field to set goals for the next reading session.
* **Date**: A date field to record the date of the reading activity.
* **Time**: A time field to record the time of the reading activity.
* **Category**: A multiple-select field to choose one or more categories related to the reading (e.g., fiction, non-fiction, self-help, etc.).
* **Progress**: A single-select field to select the progress made on the reading (e.g., high, medium, low).
* **Submitted By**: A single-select field to select who submitted the log (e.g., a user or a team member).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields.
* Be as detailed as possible in the "Reflection" section.
* Use the provided category options or add your own if necessary.
* Choose the correct progress level based on your reading experience.
* Select the correct submitter based on who actually completed the reading log.
