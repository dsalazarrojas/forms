# robotics_training_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The robotics training evaluation form is designed to assess the effectiveness of robotics training sessions. This form gathers information on the trainee's experience, training session details, and overall outcome.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and trainee ID (if applicable) in the respective fields.
2. Select the date of the training session.
3. Choose the robotics systems used during the session.
4. Enter the duration of the session.
5. Evaluate the trainee's performance using the provided score.
6. Choose the outcome of the training (e.g., successful, not successful).
7. Enter the location of the training session.
8. Enter the name of the session instructor.
9. Provide any additional comments about the session.
10. Enter the date of evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user name** (Text, Optional): Enter your name, which will be associated with this evaluation.
* **trainee id** (Number, Optional): If applicable, enter the trainee's ID to ensure accurate tracking and evaluation.
* **training session date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of the training session you are evaluating.
* **robotics systems used** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select all the robotics systems used during the session, separated by commas.
* **session duration** (Number, Optional): Enter the duration of the training session in minutes.
* **evaluation score** (Number, Optional): Evaluate the trainee's performance using the provided score scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **training outcome** (Select One, Optional): Choose the outcome of the training session (e.g., successful, not successful).
* **session location** (Text, Optional): Enter the location of the training session.
* **session instructor** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the session instructor.
* **comments** (Note, Optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the session.
* **evaluation date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of evaluation.
