# Faculty Administrative Support Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Faculty Administrative Support Inquiry Form is a tool to facilitate faculty members in submitting requests for administrative support related to their work. The form is meant to make it easier for faculty to communicate their needs and ensure timely assistance from their departments.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "form name" field, which will be displayed as your name in the submission.
2. Select the department relevant to your support request from the dropdown list.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field. This will help us contact you with updates and information.
4. Optionally, enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. In the "message" field, please provide a detailed description of your support request, including any relevant dates or deadlines.
6. Choose the date you would like to schedule your support request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **form name** (text, optional): Enter your name, which will be displayed as the sender of the request.
* **department** (select one):
	+ Select the department that aligns with your support request.
* **email** (email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates and information.
* **phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number if you prefer to be reached via phone.
* **message** (text, optional): Provide a detailed description of your support request, including any relevant dates or deadlines.
* **date** (date, optional): Choose a date to schedule your support request.

## Tips

* Please ensure that your support request is not already in progress with your department before submitting this form.
* If you are submitting on behalf of someone else, please ensure the faculty member is aware their request has been made.
* You can submit multiple support requests if needed, but this may lead to delays in processing and responding to each individual request.
