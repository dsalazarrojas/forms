# Grade Change Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document and track grade changes for students. It is used to update a student's grade in a particular course.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the correct information.
2. Ensure you have permission to make a grade change before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`): Enter the student's name as it appears in the course records.
* **Student Identifier** (`student_identifier`): Enter the student's ID number or equivalent.
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`): Enter the name of the instructor who is making the grade change.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`): Enter the name of the course that the grade change is for.
* **Grade Change Date** (`grade_change_date`): Enter the date of the grade change.
* **Change Request** (`change_request`): Briefly explain the reason for the grade change.
* **New Grade** (`new_grade`): Enter the new grade to be assigned to the student.
* **Reason for Grade Change** (`reason_for_grade_change`): Provide a detailed explanation of the grade change.
* **Student Signature** (`signature`): The student must sign this form to confirm their understanding of the grade change.
* **Date Signature** (`date_signature`): Date and time of when the student signed the form.
* **Instructor Signature** (`instructor_signature`): The instructor must sign this form to confirm their approval of the grade change.
* **Date Instructor Signature** (`date_instructor_signature`): Date and time of when the instructor signed the form.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Additional comments about the grade change.
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`): Date of when the approver approved the grade change.
* **Grade Change Status** (`grade_change_status`): Select the status of the grade change: pending, approved, or denied.
* **Comments for Approver** (`comments_approver`): Additional comments from the approver.
* **Approver Name** (`approver_name`): Name of the person who approved the grade change.
* **Approver Role** (`approver_role`): Role of the person who approved the grade change.
* **Approver Signature** (`approver_signature`): The approver must sign this form to confirm their approval of the grade change.
* **Date Approver Signature** (`date_approver_signature`): Date and time of when the approver signed the form.
* **Approver ID** (`approver_id`): ID of the person who approved the grade change.
* **Approver Email** (`approver_email`): Email of the person who approved the grade change.
* **Approver Phone** (`approver_phone`): Phone number of the person who approved the grade change.
* **Approver Comments** (`approver_comments`): Additional comments from the approver.
* **Approver Name 2** (`approver_name_2`): Name of a secondary approver (if applicable).

Note that fields with a hint will have an explanation in the hint itself, and fields with a required/optional status will be marked as required or optional in this explanation.
