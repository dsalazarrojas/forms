# Application For Emergency Aid - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help students in requesting emergency assistance from the university or college. This form is used to collect information about the student's emergency situation, allowing the university or college to review and provide aid as needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
2.  Enter your university or college ID number.
3.  Enter your contact email address.
4.  Select the type of emergency you are experiencing.
5.  Provide a detailed description of your emergency.
6.  Specify the amount of financial assistance you are requesting.
7.  If applicable, provide a filename and description of any supporting documents you are uploading.
8.  Select your preferred contact method.
9.  Choose your current study status.
10.  Add any additional notes that can help the university or college review your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Student ID**: Enter your university or college ID number.
*   **Email address**: Enter your contact email address.
*   **Type of emergency**: Select the category of your emergency from the provided options (Medical, Housing, Financial, Family, or Other).
*   **Description of emergency**: Explain the circumstances and immediate needs surrounding your emergency in a detailed and clear manner.
*   **Amount requested**: Specify the financial amount you are requesting for assistance.
*   **Supporting documents**: If you are uploading supporting documents, indicate the filename and provide a brief description of the file.
*   **Preferred contact method**: Choose your preferred method of contact for future communication (Email or Phone).
*   **Study status**: Select your current enrollment status from the provided options (Full-time, Part-time, or Not enrolled).
*   **Additional notes**: Add any other information that can help the university or college review your application.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure a prompt review of your application.
*   Use clear and concise language when describing your emergency and additional notes.
*   If you have any supporting documents, ensure they are attached and described correctly.
*   Check your preferred contact method to ensure you can be easily reached for further assistance.
