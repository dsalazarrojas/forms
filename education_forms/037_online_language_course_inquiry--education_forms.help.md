# Online Language Course Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for users to provide information about language course inquiries. The data collected will help in understanding prospect's qualifications, interests, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required and optional fields as necessary. Each field's label and explanation are provided below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Inquiries** (`user_inquiries`, note, required: false): This field is not applicable as it's a note type field and not used in the form, it's likely a placeholder.
* **Prospect Qualification** (`prospect_qualification`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the prospect is qualified or not.
* **Course Details** (`course_details`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the course you're interested in or inquire about.
* **Prospect Details** (`prospect_details`, number, required: false): This field seems to be intended for entering a numerical value but is labeled as 'Prospect Details', which might be misleading. It's best-effort to enter a number as the label is unclear. 
* **School Name** (`school_name`, select_one, required: false): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the school or institution associated with the course.
* **Prospect Interests** (`prospect_interests`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the interests that apply to the prospect. 
* **Prospect Qualifications** (`prospect_qualifications`, text, required: false): Enter the prospect's qualifications or skills related to the course.
* **Follow-up** (`prospect_followup`, select_one, required: false): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if follow-up is desired or not.
* **Course Level** (`course_level`, select_one, required: false): Choose High or Low to select the course level.
* **Language** (`language`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the language you are interested in.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all the fields that apply to your inquiry.
* Double-check the 'Prospect Qualification' and 'School Name' fields for accurate 'Yes' or 'No' responses.
* The form is designed for language course inquiries, so be specific about the course name and language of interest.
* Enter numbers accurately in the 'Prospect Details' field.
* Ensure the 'Prospect Interests' and 'Prospect Qualifications' fields are filled with relevant information.
