# Acoustics Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a quiz designed to evaluate students' knowledge of acoustics concepts. It consists of multiple-choice and short-answer questions that cover various topics in acoustics, such as wave properties, sound speed, decibel scale, room acoustics, and noise control.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your student information, including your full name and optional student ID.
2. Answer each question carefully, selecting the correct option for multiple-choice questions.
3. Provide a short answer for the question on practical application of acoustics knowledge.
4. Ensure that you submit the form with the correct submission date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student name** (`Student name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Student ID** (`Student ID`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your course or school ID number.
* **Which property describes frequency** (`Number of cycles per second`, select_one, required): Select the correct property that describes frequency.
*   • Number of cycles per second is the correct answer as it describes the number of waves or cycles per unit of time.
* **Which factor increases sound speed** (`Higher temperature`, select_one, required): Choose the correct factor that affects sound speed.
*   • Higher temperature is generally the correct answer as it affects the speed of sound waves.
* **Decibel scale measures** (`Sound pressure level`, select_one, required): Select the correct measurement that the decibel scale represents.
*   • Sound pressure level is the correct answer as it measures the intensity of sound.
* **What reduces reverberation** (`Absorptive materials`, select_one, required): Choose the correct method to reduce reverberation.
*   • Absorptive materials are the correct answer as they absorb sound energy and reduce reverberation.
* **Best approach to control noise at source** (text, optional): This question requires a short answer explaining your approach to controlling noise at its source.
* **Practical application of acoustics knowledge** (text, optional): Provide an example of how acoustics knowledge is applied in your life or field of study.
* **Submission date** (`Submission date`, date, required): Enter the date you submit this form.

## Tips
• Be honest and accurate in your answers.
• Read each question carefully before selecting an option.
• Submit your answers before the submission deadline.
