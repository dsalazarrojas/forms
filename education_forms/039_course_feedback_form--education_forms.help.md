# Course Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Course Feedback Form is a tool used to gather comments and suggestions from students, instructors, or staff about a specific course. The form is designed to be completed by anyone who wishes to provide feedback about a course, such as students, instructors, or staff members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

*   Enter a valid course title in the **Course Title** field to specify which course you're providing feedback on.
*   Provide a clear and concise answer to the **Feedback question** field to share your thoughts about the course.
*   Enter a detailed response to the **Feedback** field to offer suggestions or comments about the course.
*   Optionally, provide your **Email** address to allow us to follow up with you about your feedback.
*   If you have a **Phone** number, feel free to enter it to allow us to contact you directly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Course Title**: Enter a valid course title to let us know which course you're providing feedback on.
*   **Feedback Question**: Respond clearly and concisely to this field to share your thoughts about the course.
*   **Feedback**: Provide a detailed response to this field to offer suggestions or comments about the course.
*   **Suggestions**: Enter any additional comments or suggestions you might have about the course.
*   **Email**: Enter a valid email address to allow us to follow up with you about your feedback. Please note that your email address will not be shared or used for any other purpose other than follow-up.
*   **Phone**: Enter a valid phone number to allow us to contact you directly.
