# Case Study Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information and permissions from participants in a case study, typically for academic or research purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the required fields and review the permissions and conditions carefully. Make sure you understand what information is being shared and how it will be used. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
• **Release Date** (`release_date`): This is the date on which you are granting permission to publish and distribute the case study.
• **Organization Information** (`organization_info`): Please provide a brief description of the publishing entity.
• **Organization Name** (`organization_name`): Enter the name of the organization responsible for publishing the case study.
• **Contact Person Name** (`contact_person`): Enter your name as the contact person for this case study.
• **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
• **Participant Full Name** (`participant_name`): Enter your full name as the participant in the case study.
• **Participant Role or Title** (`participant_role`): Describe your role or title in the case study.
• **Case Study Title** (`case_study_title`): Enter the title of the case study.
• **Case Study Time Period** (`case_study_period`): Enter the duration of the case study.
• **Scope of Release** (`release_scope`): Specify the scope of what is being released, such as the types of materials.
• **Materials Being Released** (`materials_released`): Select the types of materials being released, such as written case studies, interview transcripts, photographs, or videos.
• **Publication Rights Granted** (`publication_rights`): Select the types of publications for which you are granting permission, such as print or online publication.
• **Distribution Scope** (`distribution_scope`): Select where the case study can be shared, such as worldwide, academic institutions only, or internal use only.
• **Identity Usage** (`identity_usage`): Enter any relevant information about using your name and likeness in the case study.
• **May Your Name Be Used** (`name_usage`): Select whether your full name, first name only, or initials only can be used.
• **May Your Likeness Be Used** (`likeness_usage`): Select whether photographs or videos of you can be used.
• **May Your Title or Position Be Used** (`title_usage`): Select whether your title or position can be used.
• **Review Rights** (`review_rights`): Select the level of review you require before publication.
• **Will You Review Before Publication** (`review_opportunity`): Select whether you will review the case study before publication.
• **Review Timeframe** (`review_timeframe`): Select how much time you need to review the case study before publication.
• **Are Edits or Revisions Allowed** (`edits_allowed`): Select whether you can request edits or revisions to the case study.
• **Compensation** (`compensation`): Enter any compensation or consideration provided for your participation.
• **Payment or Consideration** (`compensation_provided`): Select whether you received any payment or consideration for your participation.
• **What and When** (`compensation_details`): Describe what compensation you received and when it was provided.
• **Confidentiality** (`confidentiality`): Select whether any information is confidential or restricted.
• **Is Any Information Confidential** (`confidential_info`): Select whether any information is confidential or restricted.
• **Describe Confidential Information** (`confidential_details`): If confidential, describe the type of information.
• **Term and Duration** (`term_duration`): Enter the term and duration of the release.
• **How Long Is This Release Valid** (`release_duration`): Select how long the release is valid, such as perpetual or for 5 years.
• **Can This Release Be Revoked** (`revocation`): Select whether the release can be revoked under certain conditions.
• **Legal Understanding** (`legal_understanding`): Acknowledge that you have read and understood the terms of release.
• **I Have Read and Understood This Release** (`read_understood`): Select whether you have fully read and understood the terms.
• **I Agree Voluntarily** (`voluntary_agreement`): Select whether you agree to the terms voluntarily.
• **I Have Legal Capacity to Sign** (`legal_capacity`): Select whether you have the authority to sign this release.
• **Authorization** (`authorization`): Enter any additional comments or authorization.
• **I Agree to the Terms of This Release** (`agreement`): Select whether you agree to the terms of the release.
• **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`): Sign and date this form to confirm your agreement.
• **Witness Name** (`witness_name`): Enter the name of the witness, if applicable.
• **Witness Signature Date** (`witness_signature_date`): Sign and date the witness signature, if applicable.
• **Organization Representative Name** (`organization_representative`): Enter the name of the organization representative, if applicable.
• **Organization Signature Date** (`organization_signature_date`): Sign and date the organization signature, if applicable.
