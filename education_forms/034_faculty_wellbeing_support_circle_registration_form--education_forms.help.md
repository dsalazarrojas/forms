## Faculty Wellbeing Support Circle Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help faculty members register for the Wellbeing Support Circle, a resource provided to support mental health, physical health, and academic performance of staff members. The goal is to create a community where staff can share their experiences, challenges, and support needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1.  Fill in your Faculty/Staff Member's Name on page 1.
2.  Enter your Email on page 1.
3.  Select how you heard about the initiative on page 1.
4.  Select your motivations for joining the circle on page 1.
5.  Select the specific challenges you are facing on page 1.
6.  Choose how many sessions you are available to attend per week on page 1.
7.  Select your preferred time for circle meetings on page 1.
8.  Add any other information you'd like to share about yourself in the provided note field on page 1.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Faculty/Staff Member's Name** (`faculty_wellbeing_support_circle_registration_form_page_1_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as a faculty or staff member.
*   **Email** (`faculty_wellbeing_support_circle_registration_form_page_1_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you about the Wellbeing Support Circle.
*   **How did you hear about this initiative?** (`faculty_wellbeing_support_circle_registration_form_page_1_source`, select_multiple, required): Please select how you heard about the Wellbeing Support Circle. This will help us understand which channels are most effective for promoting the initiative.
*   **What motivates you to join this circle?** (`faculty_wellbeing_support_circle_registration_form_page_1_motivation`, select_multiple, required): Select your reasons for joining the Wellbeing Support Circle. This will help us tailor the support provided to your needs.
*   **What specific challenges are you facing?** (`faculty_wellbeing_support_circle_registration_form_page_1_challenges`, select_multiple, required): Select the specific challenges you are currently facing. This will help us provide targeted support and resources.
*   **How many sessions of the wellbeing support circle you are available to attend per week?** (`faculty_wellbeing_support_circle_registration_form_page_1_frequency`, select_one, required): Please choose how many sessions you can attend per week. This will help us schedule meetings effectively.
*   **Preferred time for circle meetings** (`faculty_wellbeing_support_circle_registration_form_page_1_time`, select_one, required): Select your preferred time for circle meetings. This will help us accommodate your schedule.
*   **Any other information you'd like to share about you?** (`faculty_wellbeing_support_circle_registration_form_page_1_note`, note, required): Please use this field to share any additional information about yourself that might be helpful for the Wellbeing Support Circle.

## Tips

*   Be honest about your motivations and challenges to get the best out of the support circle.
*   Choose a time slot that suits your schedule to ensure consistent attendance.
*   Don't hesitate to share any other information that might help us tailor support to your needs.
