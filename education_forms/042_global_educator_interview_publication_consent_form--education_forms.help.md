# Global Educator Interview Publication Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from a global educator for the publication of their interview. It is essential to complete this form accurately and truthfully to ensure the educator's privacy and security.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and understand the terms and conditions of publication.
2. Fill in the required fields (marked as `required`):
	* `Interview Start Date`: Enter the start date of the interview.
	* `Interview End Date`: Enter the end date of the interview.
	* `Educator Email`: Enter the educator's email address.
	* `Educator Phone`: Enter the educator's phone number.
	* `Publish Interview?`: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether to publish the interview.
3. Fill in the optional fields:
	* `Interview Details`: Provide any additional information about the interview.
	* `Signature`: Sign the form to indicate consent.
	* `Date`: Enter the date of signing.
	* `Time`: Enter the time of signing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Educator Consent Form** (`educator_consent_page_1`):
	+ This is the first page of the form where you will provide your name and other details.
* **Interview Details** (`educator_consent_page_2`):
	+ Provide any additional information about the interview, such as the topic or purpose.
* **Submit** (`educator_consent_form_submit`):
	+ Click to submit the form after filling in all required fields.
* **Interview Start Date** (`interview_start_date`):
	+ Enter the start date of the interview.
* **Interview End Date** (`interview_end_date`):
	+ Enter the end date of the interview.
* **Signature** (`educator_consent_form_signature`):
	+ Sign the form to indicate consent.
* **Date** (`educator_consent_form_date`):
	+ Enter the date of signing.
* **Time** (`educator_consent_form_time`):
	+ Enter the time of signing.
* **Email** (`educator_email`):
	+ Enter the educator's email address.
* **Phone** (`educator_phone`):
	+ Enter the educator's phone number.
* **Publish Interview?** (`interview_pub_perm`):
	+ Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether to publish the interview.
