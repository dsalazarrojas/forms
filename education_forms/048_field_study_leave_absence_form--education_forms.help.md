# Field Study Leave Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Field Study Leave Absence Form is used by students to request absence during field studies. This form helps to record the student's details, field studies information, and absence dates, duration, and reason for absence. It also requires submission by a specified person, who can add comments for approval or rejection.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide student details in the "Student Details" field.
2. Select the field studies relevant to the absence request.
3. Specify the dates of absence.
4. Enter the duration of absence.
5. Provide the reason for absence.
6. Select the person to be submitted to (required).
7. Add comments for approval or rejection (required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Details**: Enter the student's name and any other relevant details.
* **Field Studies**: Select all the field studies relevant to the absence request.
* **Dates of Absence**: Enter the start and end dates of the absence, in the format MM/DD/YY or DD/MM/YY.
* **Duration of Absence**: Enter the number of days the student will be absent.
* **Reason for Absence**: Provide a clear and concise reason for the absence.
* **Submitter**: Select the person who will submit the absence request.
* **Submitted By**: The name of the person who submitted the request.
* **Submitter Comments**: Add any comments or information for the approver to consider during approval or rejection.
