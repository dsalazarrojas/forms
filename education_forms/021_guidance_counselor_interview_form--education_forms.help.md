# Guidance Counselor Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from guidance counselors about students, which can help in making informed decisions about students' progress, needs, and plans. 

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Start with the first page.
2. Enter the student's name in the "Student name" field.
3. Select the student's grade level.
4. Enter the school year the student is in.
5. Describe the next steps for the student in the "Next steps" field.
6. Add any additional notes about the student in the "Notes" field.
7. Review the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First page** (`1`, `text`, required: false): This page is the starting point for the form. It is not a required field, so you can skip it if you don't need to provide any information.
* **Student name** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Enter the student's name as it is spelled and formatted in your records.
* **Grade level** (`3`, `text`, required: false): Choose the grade level that the student is currently in (e.g., 9th, 10th, 11th, or 12th).
* **School year** (`4`, `text`, required: false): Enter the school year that the student is in (e.g., 2022-2023, 2023-2024, etc.).
* **Next steps** (`5`, `text`, required: false): Describe the next steps or actions you plan to take regarding the student. Be as specific as possible.
* **Notes** (`6`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about the student. This field can be useful for keeping track of important details.
