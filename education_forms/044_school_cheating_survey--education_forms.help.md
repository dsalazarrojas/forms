# School Cheating Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about students' attitudes and behaviors regarding cheating in school. By completing this survey, you will help us understand the factors that influence students' attitudes towards cheating and the school environment's effects on cheating habits.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Choose only one option for questions with multiple options (e.g., select one or multiple answers for questions 4, 5, 6, 10, 11, 12).
3. Use numerical values for questions 9 and 13.
4. Make sure to fill out all required questions, as indicated by the required label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Do you cheat in school** (1): This question asks about your personal attitude towards cheating in school.
- **What is your attitude towards cheating** (2): Choose one of the three options: Always cheat, Never cheat, or Sometimes cheat.
- **Do teachers support cheating** (3): Share your thoughts about whether teachers in your school support or discourage cheating.
- **How does the school environment influence cheating** (4): Select all that apply: Positive, Negative, or Neutral.
- **Do your peers influence your cheating behavior** (5): Choose one or more of the three options: Strong, Weak, or No influence.
- **Do your parents influence you to cheat** (6): Select one or more of the three options: Strong, Weak, or No influence.
- **Are there clear rules against cheating in school** (7): Choose one of the two options: True or False.
- **What are the consequences for cheating in school** (8): Share your thoughts about the consequences of cheating in school.
- **How would you rate your teachers' support** (9): Use a numerical value between 1 and 10 to rate your teachers' support.
- **How involved are your parents in your school life** (10): Select one of the three options: Very involved, Somewhat involved, or Not involved.
- **Are there enough resources available in the school** (11): Choose one of the two options: True or False.
- **Do you get support from your parents** (12): Select one or more of the three options: Strong, Weak, or No support.
- **How would you rate the school climate** (13): Use a numerical value between 1 and 10 to rate the school climate.
