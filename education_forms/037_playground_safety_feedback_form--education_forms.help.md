# Playground Safety Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from parents regarding playground safety concerns they have experienced while visiting a school or park facility. The goal is to gather accurate and helpful information to improve the safety and quality of facilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your comments as a parent in the Parent Comments field.
2. Assess the condition of the facility and select all that apply in the Facility Condition field.
3. If you have any concerns, please enter them in the Parent Concerns field (optional).
4. Rate the facility in the Facility Rating field.
5. Add any additional comments or concerns about the park in the Park Comments field (optional).
6. Enter any additional comments or concerns about the school in the School Comments field (optional).
7. Enter your name in the Parent Name field.
8. Enter your email address in the Contact Email field.
9. Enter your phone number in the Contact Phone field.
10. Enter your address in the Parent Address field.
11. Select the type of facility you visited (School, Park, Recreation Center, or Other) in the Facility Type field.
12. Select who submitted the form (Parent, Guardian, Teacher, or Other) in the Submitted By field.
13. Enter the date and time you submitted the form in the Submitted Date and Submitted Time fields.
14. Enter your IP address in the Submitted IP field.
15. Enter your device type in the Submitted Device field (optional).
16. Enter your user agent in the Submitted User Agent field (optional).
17. Enter your operating system in the Submitted OS field (optional).
18. Enter your browser type in the Submitted Browser field (optional).
19. Enter your platform in the Submitted Platform field (optional).
20. Enter your city in the Submitted City field (optional).
21. Enter your country in the Submitted Country field (optional).
22. Enter your region in the Submitted Region field (optional).
23. Enter your language in the Submitted Language field (optional).
24. Enter your second IP address in the Submitted IP2 field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Comments** (Parent Comments, text, required): Enter any comments or concerns you have as a parent about the facility.
* **Facility Condition** (Facility Condition, select multiple, required): Assess the condition of the facility and select all that apply.
* **Parent Concerns** (Parent Concerns, note, optional): If you have any concerns, please enter them here.
* **Facility Rating** (Facility Rating, number, required): Rate the facility from 1 to 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Park Comments** (Park Comments, text, optional): Add any additional comments or concerns about the park.
* **School Comments** (School Comments, text, optional): Add any additional comments or concerns about the school.
* **Parent Name** (Parent Name, text, required): Enter your name as a parent.
* **Contact Email** (Contact Email, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Parent Address** (Parent Address, text, required): Enter your address.
* **Facility Type** (Facility Type, select one, required): Select the type of facility you visited (School, Park, Recreation Center, or Other).
* **Submitted By** (Submitted By, select one, required): Select who submitted the form (Parent, Guardian, Teacher, or Other).
* **Submitted Date** (Submitted Date, date, required): Enter the date you submitted the form.
* **Submitted Time** (Submitted Time, time, required): Enter the time you submitted the form.
* **Submitted IP** (Submitted IP, number, required): Enter your IP address.
* **Submitted Device** (Submitted Device, text, optional): Enter your device type.
* **Submitted User Agent** (Submitted User Agent, text, optional): Enter your user agent.
* **Submitted OS** (Submitted OS, text, optional): Enter your operating system.
* **Submitted Browser** (Submitted Browser, text, optional): Enter your browser type.
* **Submitted Platform** (Submitted Platform, text, optional): Enter your platform.
* **Submitted City** (Submitted City, text, optional): Enter your city.
* **Submitted Country** (Submitted Country, text, optional): Enter your country.
* **Submitted Region** (Submitted Region, text, optional): Enter your region.
* **Submitted Language** (Submitted Language, text, optional): Enter your language.
* **Submitted IP2** (Submitted IP2, number, required): Enter your second IP address.

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough in your feedback to help us improve the safety and quality of facilities.
* If you have any concerns or comments, please enter them in the designated fields.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask for clarification.
* This form is a one-time submission form, so please fill it out carefully and accurately.
