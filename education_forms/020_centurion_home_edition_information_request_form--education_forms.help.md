# Centurion Home Edition Information Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from potential customers or students interested in learning more about Centurion Home Edition. It helps us understand your contact details, student information, topics of interest, and preferred method of contact. We use this information to provide you with relevant details about our programs and services, and to ensure you receive the information you need in a way that suits your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out your contact details, including first name, last name, and email address. Make sure to enter a valid phone number, as we may contact you to discuss further.
2. If you are a student, provide your student name (if applicable) and grade level (if applicable) to help us personalize the information package.
3. Select all the topics you are interested in learning more about.
4. Choose your preferred method of contact (email, phone call, or video consultation).
5. If you have any specific questions or requests, type them in the text box provided.
6. Finally, indicate how you heard about us (e.g., search engine, friend recommendation, print advertisement).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`contact_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address to receive the requested information.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the student you are interested in.
* **Student Grade - Level** (`student_grade_level`, number, optional): If applicable, select the current academic level of the student.
* **Topics of Interest** (`topics_of_interest`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply from the options listed:
	+ Curriculum Details
	+ Pricing and Packages
	+ Support and Tutoring
	+ Technical Requirements
	+ Enrollment Process
* **How would you like us to contact you** (`contact_method`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred communication channel:
	+ Email
	+ Phone Call
	+ Video Consultation
* **Specific Questions or Requests** (`specific_questions`, text, optional): Type any other information you would like to share with us.
* **How did you hear about us** (`discovery_source`, select_one, optional): Select the source that led you to our website:
	+ Search Engine
	+ Friend Recommendation
	+ Social Media
	+ Print Advertisement
* **Date of Request** (`information_request_date`, date, required): This is the date you submitted this form.
* **Additional Comments** (`specific_questions`, text, optional): Use this field to share any further information or questions you may have.
