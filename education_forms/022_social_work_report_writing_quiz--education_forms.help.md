# Social Work Report Writing Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your skills and experience in writing reports on various social work scenarios. It will help evaluate your confidence and proficiency in writing reports on specific topics, such as emotional distress, substance abuse, homelessness, domestic violence, and other multiple scenarios.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer the fields as accurately as possible.
2. For checkbox and select questions, choose one of the provided options (Yes, No, or Other).
3. For select multiple questions, choose all that apply.
4. For select one questions, choose only one option that best describes your situation.
5. For time fields, enter the actual time spent on the specific task or activity.
6. For date fields, enter the actual date of the last report written.
7. For text fields, enter a brief description of your thoughts or experiences.
