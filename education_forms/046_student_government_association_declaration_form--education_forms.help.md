# Student Government Association Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from students declaring their association or involvement with student government bodies or committees within the university. It requires essential details for processing and verification of student declarations.

## How To Complete This Form

- Ensure you have all the required details before filling out the form.
- Provide accurate and complete information for each field.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant student government authority.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Declaration Form** (`student_declaration_form`): Enter a brief description or statement declaring your association or involvement with a student government body or committee.
* **Student Declaration Details** (`student_declaration_details`): Provide any additional details related to your declaration.
* **Declaration Date** (`declaration_date`): Enter the date you wish to declare your association.
* **Declaration Time** (`declaration_time`): Enter the time at which you wish to declare.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`): Enter your contact number where you can be reached for further information or clarification.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`): Enter your official university email address.
* **Student Identification** (`student_identification`): Please provide your student identification number or any other unique identifier as requested.
* **Student Government Declaration** (`student_government_declaration`): Choose the type of student government association or committee you wish to join or be part of. You can select from the provided options or type 'Other (Specify)' if not listed.
* **Other Declaration** (`other_declaration`): If your declaration does not fit into any of the provided categories, use this field to provide a detailed description of your intention.
