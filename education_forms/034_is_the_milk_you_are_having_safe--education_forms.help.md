# Is The Milk You Are Having Safe!!! - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you identify whether the milk you are having is safe to consume. It will guide you through a series of checks to help you determine if your milk has gone bad or not.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Answer each question based on your observation of the milk.
* Select the option that best matches your findings.
* Fill in any additional information requested in the `Note` field if you have any additional comments.
* Enter your contact information (email, phone number, and address) if you would like to be contacted for further assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Check for Water** (`milk_water`, select_one): Check the milk for cloudiness or wateriness. If it looks like water, select the "Water" option.
* **Check for Starch** (`milk_starch`, select_one): Check the milk for the presence of starch. If you find starch, select the "Starch" option.
* **Check for Detergent** (`milk_detergent`, select_one): Check the milk for any signs of detergent presence. If you detect detergent, select the "Detergent" option.
* **Check Color** (`milk_color`, select_one): Check the color of the milk. If it looks different from normal milk color, select the "Changed Color" option.
* **Check Smell** (`milk_smell`, select_one): Check the smell of the milk. If it smells different from normal milk smell, select the "Changed Smell" option.
* **Check Taste** (`milk_taste`, select_one): Check the taste of the milk. If it tastes different from normal milk taste, select the "Changed Taste" option.
* **Check Feel** (`milk_feel`, select_one): Check the texture and feel of the milk. If it feels different from normal milk feel, select the "Changed Feel" option.
* **Check Visual** (`milk_visual`, select_one): Check the overall appearance of the milk. If it looks different from normal milk appearance, select the "Changed Visual" option.
* **Note** (`milk_note`, note): If you have any additional comments or observations about the milk, enter them here.
* **Email** (`milk_email`, email): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for further assistance.
* **Phone** (`milk_phone`, text): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted for further assistance.
* **Date** (`milk_date`, date): Enter the date when you observed the milk.
* **Time** (`milk_time`, time): Enter the time when you observed the milk.
* **Address** (`milk_address`, text): Enter your address if you would like to be contacted for further assistance.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when answering each question.
* Take your time to carefully observe the milk before submitting your answers.
* If you are unsure about anything, feel free to ask for further guidance.
