## Principal For A Day Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for the "Principal For A Day" program at their school.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your school's name.
2. Enter your name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Select a preferred date for the principal for a day event.
5. Confirm your consent to participate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **school name** (`school_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your school.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as a participant.
* **email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address so that we can contact you.
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, date, required/optional): Select a date for the principal for a day event. Please choose a date that works best for you.
* **Consent** (`consent`, text, required/optional): Confirm that you consent to participate in the principal for a day program.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in accurate and complete information for each field.
* Select a date that you are available and able to participate.
* Confirm your consent to participate in the principal for a day program.
