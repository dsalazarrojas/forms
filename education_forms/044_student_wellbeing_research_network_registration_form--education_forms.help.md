# Student Wellbeing Research Network Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Wellbeing Research Network Registration Form is designed for students to provide information about their wellbeing, which will be used for research purposes. Please note that this form is a one-time entry and is only meant for students who are interested in participating in the wellbeing research study.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form on the page.
2. Select all relevant options for the wellbeing questions that apply to you.
3. Enter your contact information in the "Contact info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### User info (Text Field)
• **User info** (`text`, optional): Enter your name, student ID, or any other identifying information you wish to share with us. This will help us to better understand your background and identify you in our records.

### Wellbeing questions (Multiple Select Question)
• **Wellbeing questions** (`select_multiple`, optional): Choose all questions that apply to you:
	+ Q1: How do you feel about the past year?
	+ Q2: How do you feel about the past 5 years?
	+ Q3: How do you feel about the past 10 years?

### Contact info (Text Field)
• **Contact info** (`text`, optional): Enter your email address, phone number, or any other contact information you wish to share with us.
