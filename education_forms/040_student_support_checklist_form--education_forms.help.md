# Student Support Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Support Checklist Form is a tool used to gather essential information about a student's support level and needs to plan the next steps for their support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's ID number in the "student_id" field.
2. Enter the student's name in the "student_name" field.
3. Enter the student's email address in the "student_email" field.
4. Enter the student's phone number in the "student_phone" field.
5. Enter the student's birthdate in the "date_of_birth" field.
6. Select the student's support level in the "student_support_level" field.
7. Enter any relevant student comments or notes in the "student_notes" field.
8. Select the staff member's support level for the student in the "staff_support_level" field.
9. Enter any staff comments or notes in the "staff_notes" field.
10. Select whether to follow up with the student ("Yes" or "No") in the "follow_reported_to" field.
11. Select which follow-up options are available for the student ("Yes" or "No") in the "student_followups" field.
12. Select whether to follow up with staff ("Yes" or "No") in the "staff_followups" field.
13. Enter the date the support was given in the "date_followed" field.
14. Select the follow-up frequency (yes or no) in the "follow_frequency" field.
15. Select the follow-up location (yes or no) in the "follow_location" field.
16. Select which follow-up modes are available for the student ("Yes" or "No") in the "follow_mode" field.
17. Select which staff follow-up modes are available for the student ("Yes" or "No") in the "staff_follow_mode" field.
18. Enter the next follow-up steps in the "next_follow_steps" field.
19. Enter any follow-up notes in the "follow_notes" field.
20. Enter any staff comments in the "staff_comments" field.
21. Select which follow-up types are available for the student ("Yes" or "No") in the "follow_type" field.
22. Select which next actions are available for the student ("Yes" or "No") in the "next_actions" field.

* **staff_support_level (Select One)**: Select the staff member's support level for the student (High or Low).
* **staff_notes (Note)**: Enter any comments or notes from the staff member.
* **follow_reported_to (Select One)**: Select whether to follow up with the student ("Yes" or "No").
* **student_followups (Select Multiple)**: Select which follow-up options are available for the student ("Yes" or "No").
* **staff_followups (Select Multiple)**: Select which follow-up options are available for the staff member ("Yes" or "No").
* **date_followed (Date)**: Enter the date the support was given.
* **follow_frequency (Select One)**: Select the follow-up frequency (yes or no).
* **follow_location (Select One)**: Select the follow-up location (yes or no).
* **follow_mode (Select Multiple)**: Select which follow-up modes are available for the student ("Yes" or "No").
* **staff_follow_mode (Select Multiple)**: Select which staff follow-up modes are available for the student ("Yes" or "No").
* **next_follow_steps (Text)**: Enter the next follow-up steps.
* **follow_notes (Note)**: Enter any follow-up notes.
* **staff_comments (Text)**: Enter any comments from the staff member.
