# Lab Student Safety Contract CCSD - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lab Student Safety Contract CCSD is a form designed to document a student's understanding and agreement to adhere to safety protocols while participating in a lab or experimental environment. This form is intended to ensure students are aware of their responsibilities and the potential risks associated with lab activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Lab Student Label" field with the name of the lab or experiment you are participating in.
2. In the "Goals" field, describe your objectives and expectations for the lab or experiment.
3. In the "Student Contract" field, provide a brief statement of your understanding and agreement to follow lab safety protocols.
4. Select the "Contact Person" responsible for your safety and well-being during the lab or experiment. You can choose between Parent, Guardian, or Teacher.
5. If necessary, provide the "Contact Phone" number and "Contact Email" of the contact person selected above.
6. Date and sign the "Student Signature Date" field with the current date to signify your acknowledgment and agreement to the terms outlined in this contract.
7. Date and sign the "Teacher Signature Date" field with the current date to confirm their acknowledgment and agreement to the terms outlined in this contract.
8. Select your "Student" gender from the dropdown menu.
9. Review and ensure all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Lab Student Label** (text, required): Enter the name of the lab or experiment you are participating in.
* **Goals** (text, optional): Describe your objectives and expectations for the lab or experiment.
* **Student Contract** (note, optional): Provide a brief statement of your understanding and agreement to follow lab safety protocols.
* **Contact Person** (select_one, optional): Select the person responsible for your safety and well-being during the lab or experiment. Options: Parent, Guardian, Teacher.
* **Contact Phone** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person selected above.
* **Contact Email** (email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person selected above.
* **Student Signature Date** (date, optional): Date and sign this field with the current date to signify your acknowledgment and agreement to the terms outlined in this contract.
* **Teacher Signature Date** (date, optional): Date and sign this field with the current date to confirm their acknowledgment and agreement to the terms outlined in this contract.
* **Student** (select_multiple, optional): Select your gender from the dropdown menu.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly before submission.
* If you have any questions or concerns regarding the safety protocols or contract terms, please discuss with the contact person selected above.
* Review and sign the contract only when you are fully aware and in agreement with the terms outlined.
