# E Seminar Student Registration Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The E Seminar Student Registration Form Template is used to gather information from students attending a seminar.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with your information, making sure to provide accurate details about yourself. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Your first name as it appears on your official identification or as you would like it to be displayed on your seminar materials.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Your last name as it appears on your official identification or as you would like it to be displayed on your seminar materials.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Grade** (`grade`, select_one, optional): If you are registering as a student, select 'Yes'. If you are not a student, select 'No'.
* **1st Grade** (`grade_1`, select_multiple, optional): If you are registering for 1st grade, select 'Yes'. If not, select 'No'.
* **2nd Grade** (`grade_2`, select_multiple, optional): If you are registering for 2nd grade, select 'Yes'. If not, select 'No'.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): The name of the school you are currently attending or associated with.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, optional): Your student ID number, if applicable.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling out your information.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact the seminar organizers for guidance.
- This form should only be filled out by students actually attending the seminar.
