# University Research Ethics Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Research Ethics Audit Form is a crucial tool used by the research ethics committee to track and evaluate ongoing research projects within the university. Its purpose is to ensure that all studies adhere to the established guidelines and standards for ethics and safety.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Ensure you have access to the correct information and data required to complete the form accurately.
2.  Review each field carefully and provide the necessary details.
3.  Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Study Name** (`study_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the research study, which should be a brief and descriptive title that identifies the project's purpose and scope.
* **Study ID** (`study_id`, number, optional): Enter the unique identifier for the research study, if applicable.
* **Study Lead Person** (`study_lead_person`, text, optional): Provide the name and contact information of the lead researcher or principal investigator of the study.
* **Study Co-Investigators** (`study_co_investigators`, text, optional): List the names and contact information of all co-investigators involved in the study.
* **Study Co-Investigators Emails** (`study_co_investigators_emails`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether study co-investigators have emails or not.
* **Study Co-Investigators Emails List** (`study_co_investigators_emails_list`, text, optional): List the emails of study co-investigators if they have them.
* **Department Approving** (`department_approving`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether the department is approving or not.
* **Department Approving List** (`department_approving_list`, text, optional): List the names of departments approving the study.
* **Ethics Review Committee** (`ethics_review_committee`, select_one, optional): Check "Yes" if the study requires review by the Ethics Review Committee.
* **Ethics Review Committee List** (`ethics_review_committee_list`, text, optional): List the members of the Ethics Review Committee, if required.
* **Risk Level** (`risk_level`, select_one, optional): Select the risk level of the study, which can be High or Low.
* **Risk Level List** (`risk_level_list`, text, optional): Provide a brief explanation of the risk level selected.
* **Study Start Date** (`study_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the study, if applicable.
* **Study End Date** (`study_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the study, if applicable.
* **Study Status** (`study_status`, select_one, optional): Select the current status of the study, which can be Active or Inactive.
* **Study Status List** (`study_status_list`, text, optional): Provide a brief explanation of the study status selected.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the last review, if applicable.
* **Review Time** (`review_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the last review, if applicable.
* **Reviewer** (`reviewer`, text, optional): Provide the name and contact information of the reviewer.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the department name responsible for the study.
* **Research Area** (`research_area`, text, optional): Enter the area of research for the study.
* **Study Budget** (`study_budget`, number, optional): Enter the budget allocated for the study, if applicable.
* **Study Notes** (`study_notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional notes or comments related to the study.
* **Study Files** (`study_files`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether study files are uploaded or not.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, text, optional): Provide the name and contact information of the person submitting the form.
