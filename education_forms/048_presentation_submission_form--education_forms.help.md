# Presentation Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from presenters for presentations to be submitted to a platform or event. It gathers basic details about the presenter and their presentation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the title of your presentation in the "Presentation Submission" field.
2. Enter your information as the presenter in the "presenter_info" field.
3. Provide more details about your presentation in the "presentation_info" field.
4. Enter your name in the "presenter_name" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "presenter_email" field. Please use the correct email format (e.g., example@example.com).
6. Enter your phone number in the "presenter_phone" field, formatted as E.123.456.7890 (e.g., 1.234.567.890).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Presentation Submission**: The title of your presentation.
* **presenter_info**: A brief description or details about yourself as the presenter.
* **presentation_info**: Additional information about your presentation, such as the topic, abstract, or keywords.
* **presenter_name**: Your full name as the presenter.
* **presenter_email**: Your email address, used to contact you for further information or updates.
* **presenter_phone**: Your phone number, formatted as E.123.456.7890, with a maximum of 10 digits.
