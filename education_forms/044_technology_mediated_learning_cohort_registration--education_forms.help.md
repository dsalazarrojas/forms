# Technology Mediated Learning Cohort Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from users interested in participating in the Technology Mediated Learning Cohort program. This program aims to provide a comprehensive learning experience for individuals who want to improve their skills in technology mediated learning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by entering your cohort details, including your name and email address.
2. Select your preferred role in the cohort program.
3. Indicate your experience level, which will help us tailor the learning experience to your needs.
4. Describe your training goals, areas of interest, and any other relevant details about your participation in the cohort.
5. Provide your phone number and other contact information, if applicable.
6. Specify your focus areas and topics for the training, if desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Cohort Details** (cohort_details, text, optional): This field is for entering your general cohort details.
* **Name** (name, text, optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (email, email, optional): Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Role** (role, select_one, optional): Select your role in the cohort program: Administrator, Educator, or Participant.
* **Experience Level** (experience_level, number, optional): Rate your experience level in the technology mediated learning domain.
* **Training Goals** (training_goals, text, optional): Describe your goals for the training, and how you want to apply it in your work.
* **Technology Focused Areas of Interest** (technology_focused_areas_of_interest, text, optional): Identify your technology-focused areas of interest in the cohort.
* **Participant Details** (participant_details, text, optional): Enter any additional details about your participation in the cohort.
* **First Name** (first_name, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (last_name, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Phone Number** (phone_number, text, optional): Enter your primary contact phone number.
* **Training Teams** (training_teams, text, optional): Identify the teams you would like to train.
* **Training Focus Areas** (training_focus_areas, text, optional): Describe the areas you would like to focus on during the training.
* **Training Topics** (training_topics, text, optional): Specify the topics you would like to cover during the training.
* **Training Methods** (training_methods, text, optional): Indicate your preferred training methods.

**Tips**

* Ensure your email address is accurate and up-to-date.
* Be specific about your goals and areas of interest to help us tailor the learning experience to your needs.
* Fill in any additional details that will help us understand your participation in the cohort.
