# mentor_mentee_matching_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The mentor-mentee matching form is designed to help match mentees with suitable mentors based on their preferences and interests. This form will help in creating a personalized match between a mentee and their potential mentor, ensuring that both parties are well-suited for a productive and rewarding mentoring relationship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill in the required fields and select from the provided options. Please make sure to answer accurately and honestly, as the form will be used to create a match that meets your needs and preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: (`1`, `text`, required): This field is used to input your first name.
* **Email**: (`2`, `email`, required): This field is used to input your email address.
* **Phone Number**: (`3`, `text`, required): This field is used to input your phone number.
* **What do you want to achieve?**: (`4`, `text`, optional): This field is used to input your goals and objectives for the mentoring relationship.
* **What's your current level of experience in this field?**: (`5`, `select_one`, optional): This field is used to select your current level of experience in the field you are interested in. Please select from the provided options: High, Low.
* **How much time do you have available per week?**: (`6`, `number`, optional): This field is used to input the amount of time you have available per week for the mentoring relationship.
* **What type of mentor would you like?**: (`7`, `select_multiple`, required): This field is used to select the type of mentor you would like to be matched with. Please select from the provided options.
* **What skills do you want to learn from your mentor?**: (`8`, `select_multiple`, required): This field is used to select the skills you want to learn from your mentor. Please select from the provided options.
* **What type of support do you need?**: (`9`, `select_multiple`, required): This field is used to select the type of support you need from your mentor. Please select from the provided options.
* **What type of support do you need?**: (`10`, `select_multiple`, required): This field is used to select the type of support you need from your mentor again. Please select from the provided options.
* **What do you want to get out of mentoring?**: (`11`, `select_multiple`, required): This field is used to select what you want to get out of the mentoring relationship. Please select from the provided options.
* **Do you want to meet in-person or virtually?**: (`12`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to select whether you prefer to meet your mentor in-person or virtually.
* **How often would you like to meet with your mentor?**: (`13`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field is used to select the frequency of meetings with your mentor. Please select from the provided options.
* **What kind of relationship would you prefer with your mentor?**: (`14`, `select_one`, optional): This field is used to select the type of relationship you prefer with your mentor. Please select from the provided options.
* **What time of the day would you like to meet with your mentor?**: (`15`, `select_one`, optional): This field is used to select the time of day you prefer to meet with your mentor. Please select from the provided options.
* **Are you available to meet at different times of the day?**: (`16`, `select_one`, optional): This field is used to select whether you are available to meet at different times of the day.
* **Are you available to meet at different times of the day?**: (`17`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to select whether you are available to meet at different times of the day for the mentor-mentee matching process.
* **What day would you like to meet with your mentor?**: (`18`, `select_one`, optional): This field is used to select the day you prefer to meet with your mentor.
* **Do you want to be matched with a mentor from the same company?**: (`19`, `select_one`, optional): This field is used to select whether you want to be matched with a mentor from the same company.
* **Do you want to be matched with a mentor from a different company?**: (`20`, `select_one`, optional): This field is used to select whether you want to be matched with a mentor from a different company.
* **What industry do you want to be matched with?**: (`21`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field is used to select the industry you are interested in being matched with.
* **What is your preferred language?**: (`22`, `select_one`, optional): This field is used to select your preferred language.
* **Do you prefer to be matched with a mentor who is an introvert or extrovert?**: (`23`, `select_one`, optional): This field is used to select whether you prefer to be matched with a mentor who is an introvert or extrovert.
* **Do you prefer to be matched with a mentor who is a male or female?**: (`24`, `select_one`, optional): This field is used to select whether you prefer to be matched with a mentor who is male or female.
