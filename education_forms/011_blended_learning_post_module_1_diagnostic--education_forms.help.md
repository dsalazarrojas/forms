## Blended Learning Post Module 1 Diagnostic - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students after completing Module 1 of the blended learning program. By providing honest and insightful information, students can help instructors understand their strengths and weaknesses, identify knowledge gaps, and make necessary adjustments for future modules.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your name, student ID, and email address.
2. Rate your understanding of Module 1 using the provided scale.
3. Select all the topics you mastered in Module 1.
4. Identify the most challenging concept you faced in Module 1.
5. Indicate whether you utilized all provided online resources for Module 1.
6. Provide feedback for the instructor regarding Module 1.
7. Enter the approximate number of hours you spent on this module.
8. Choose a date of completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your university records.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, required): Enter your student ID as assigned by the university.
* **Email Address** (`student_email`, email, required): Enter your university-provided email address.
* **How would you rate your understanding of Module 1?** (`understanding_level`, select_one, required): Rate your understanding of Module 1 using the provided scale (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Which topics did you master in Module 1?** (`mastered_topics`, select_multiple, required): Select all the topics you found easy to understand and master in Module 1.
* **Which concept from Module 1 was most challenging for you?** (`challenging_concepts`, text, required): Describe the concept that gave you the most difficulty.
* **Did you utilize all provided online resources for Module 1?** (`resource_usage`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you used all resources, most of them, only a few, or none.
* **Any feedback for the instructor regarding Module 1?** (`instructor_feedback`, text, optional): Provide constructive feedback or suggestions for future improvements.
* **Approximately how many hours did you spend on this module?** (`module_study_hours`, number, required): Enter the approximate number of hours you spent on Module 1.
* **Date of Completion** (`completion_date`, date, required): Enter the date when you completed Module 1.
