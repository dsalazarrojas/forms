# Format Attendance Sheet (Public Program) - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals about their public program attendance.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To fill out this form, simply follow the instructions below.
2.  Start by filling out the personal details section with your First Name, Last Name, and Date of Birth.
3.  Next, enter your Email Address and Phone Number.
4.  Then, add any additional notes about yourself in the Note section.
5.  If you have attended any educational programs, select the level of education you have completed and provide the details about the program in the respective sections.
6.  Finally, fill out your Graduation Date and Year, select the Month and Day of graduation, and provide the details about the attended School, College, and University.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your First Name here.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your Last Name here.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your Date of Birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
*   **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required/optional): Enter your Email Address here.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your Phone Number here.
*   **Note** (`note`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes or details about yourself here.
*   **Education Level** (`education_level`, select_one, required/optional): Select the level of education you have completed from the options.
*   **Graduation Date** (`graduation_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the Graduation Date in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
*   **Graduation Time** (`graduation_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the Graduation Time here.
*   **Graduation Place** (`graduation_place`, text, required/optional): Enter the location where you graduated.
*   **Attended School** (`attended_school`, text, required/optional): If applicable, enter the name of the school you attended.
*   **Attended College** (`attended_college`, text, required/optional): If applicable, enter the name of the college you attended.
*   **Attended University** (`attended_university`, text, required/optional): If applicable, enter the name of the university you attended.
*   **Graduation Year** (`graduation_year`, number, required/optional): Enter the year you graduated.
*   **Graduation Month** (`graduation_month`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the month you graduated from the options.
*   **Graduation Day** (`graduation_day`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the day of the month you graduated from the options.
*   **Graduation Day of the Week** (`graduation_day_of_week`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the day of the week you graduated.
