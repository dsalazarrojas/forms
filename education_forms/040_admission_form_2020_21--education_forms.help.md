# Admission Form 2020 21 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Admission Form 2020 21 is a crucial document required for the admission process in our school. This form collects information about the child, parent/guardian, and other relevant details to ensure a smooth and effective admission process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully read through each section of the form
- Answer all questions honestly and accurately
- Use the hints provided for guidance on each field

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent/Guardian Full Name** (`parent_guardian_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as the parent or guardian of the child.
* **Parent/Guardian Email** (`parent_guardian_email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Parent/Guardian Phone** (`parent_guardian_phone`, `text`, required): Provide your contact phone number.
* **Address** (`parent_address`, `text`, required): Enter the current residential address of the parent/guardian.
* **City** (`parent_city`, `text`, required): Enter the city of residence.
* **State/Province** (`parent_state`, `text`, required): Enter the state or province of residence.
* **ZIP/Postal Code** (`parent_zip`, `text`, required): Enter the postal code of residence.
* **Country** (`parent_country`, `text`, required): Enter the country of residence.
* **Student Full Name** (`student_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the child.
* **Student Date of Birth** (`student_date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter the date of birth of the child.
* **Student Gender** (`student_gender`, `select_one`, required): Select the gender of the child (Male, Female, Other, or Prefer not to say).
* **Grade Applying For** (`grade_applying_for`, `select_one`, required): Select the grade level the child is applying for.
* **Previous School** (`previous_school`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the child's previous school.
* **Previous Grade Completed** (`previous_grade_completed`, `select_one`, required): Select the grade the child completed last year.
* **Special Educational Needs** (`special_needs`, `select_one`, required): Select whether the child has any special educational needs (Yes, No).
* **Special Needs Details** (`special_needs_details`, `text`, optional): If yes, provide a description of the special needs.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `select_one`, required): Select whether the child has any medical conditions (Yes, No).
* **Medical Conditions Details** (`medical_conditions_details`, `text`, optional): If yes, provide a description of the medical conditions.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Provide the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, required): Describe the relationship to the child.
* **Transportation Needs** (`transportation_needs`, `select_one`, required): Select the transportation arrangement (School bus, Private transportation, Walking, Other).
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of submitting this application.
* **Parent/Guardian Signature** (`parent_signature`, `text`, required): Provide an electronic signature to confirm the application.

## Tips
- Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting
- Ensure all required fields are completed
- Attach any supporting documents as necessary
