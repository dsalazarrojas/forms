# Teaching Experience Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teaching Experience Evaluation Form is used to evaluate the teaching experience of an instructor. This form is for authorized personnel to provide feedback on the quality of instruction and course content.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and title as required to identify yourself.
2. Provide the title of the course being evaluated.
3. Check whether the course is part of a section that you have taught in the past. If it is, select 'Yes' from the select_one field. Otherwise, select 'No'.
4. Enter the start and end dates of the evaluation period, which is the time when the course was taught. If the start date is not filled in, it will default to the current date. 
5. Enter the duration of the evaluation period in years.
6. Enter your comments about the teaching experience. These comments should be specific and concrete.
7. Rate the instructor's quality of teaching, section's quality, and course's quality using the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **instructor_name**: Enter your name as the evaluator of the teaching experience.
- **course_name**: Enter the title of the course being evaluated.
- **course_section**: Check whether the course is part of a section that you have taught in the past.
  - Option 'Yes' means you are evaluating a course within a section you have taught before.
  - Option 'No' means you are evaluating a course that is not part of a section you have taught before.
- **evaluation_period**: Enter the date when the evaluation period starts. If not filled in, it will default to the current date.
- **evaluation_period_end**: Enter the date when the evaluation period ends. If not filled in, it will default to one year after the start date.
- **evaluation_period_duration**: Enter the duration of the evaluation period in years. This field is not required but can provide additional context for the evaluation period.
- **evaluation_comments**: Enter your comments about the teaching experience.
- **instructor_quality**: Rate the instructor's quality of teaching using one of the provided options.
  - Option 'Good' means the instructor provided a good quality of teaching.
  - Option 'Poor' means the instructor did not provide a good quality of teaching.
- **section_quality**: Rate the section's quality using one of the provided options.
  - Option 'Good' means the section provided a good quality of content.
  - Option 'Poor' means the section did not provide a good quality of content.
- **course_quality**: Rate the course's quality using one of the provided options.
  - Option 'Good' means the course provided a good quality of content.
  - Option 'Poor' means the course did not provide a good quality of content.
- **student_comments**: Enter any comments from students about the teaching experience. This field is not required but can provide additional context.
- **form_comments**: Enter any additional comments about the form itself or the evaluation process. This field is not required but can provide additional context.
