# online_course_development_appointment_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "online_course_development_appointment_form" is a form used to collect information from students who are interested in developing online courses. This form is used to gather details about the students' user information, course preferences, contact preferences, and scheduling preferences. The form helps our team determine which courses to develop next and ensure they meet the needs of our students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your user information by filling out the fields on page 1.
2. Share your course preferences on page 2.
3. Choose your contact preferences on page 3.
4. Select your preferred time and date for the course development appointment on page 4.
5. Enter your email address and phone number for communication purposes on page 5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information (page 1)** (`User Information`, `text`, required: false):
	+ This section is used to collect your basic user information.
* **Course Information (page 2)** (`Course Information`, `text`, required: false):
	+ In this section, you can provide details about the course you are interested in.
* **Contact Preferences (page 3)** (`Contact Preferences`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Here, you can specify how you would like to be contacted for further information about course development.
* **Preferred Time (page 4)** (`Preferred Time`, `time`, required: false):
	+ Choose a time that suits you best for the course development appointment.
* **Preferred Date (page 4)** (`Preferred Date`, `date`, required: false):
	+ Select a date that you are available to meet for the course development appointment.
* **Email (page 5)** (`Email`, `email`, required: false):
	+ Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number (page 5)** (`Phone Number`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Provide your phone number so we can reach you for further discussions.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (if any) to ensure we can process your request accurately.
* Be as specific as possible with your course preferences to help us understand your needs better.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to contact us.
