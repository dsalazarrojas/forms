# weekly assessment form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The weekly assessment form is designed to gather information on team members' progress, status, and goals for a specific week.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your team member's name in the "Team Member" field.
2. Select the week you are currently assessing from the "Week" field (if required).
3. Enter the start date of the assessment period in the "Start Date" field (if required).
4. Enter the end date of the assessment period in the "End Date" field (if required).
5. Select the status of your weekly progress from the "Weekly Progress Status" field. This field requires you to choose a status for your current progress, which can be Not Started, In Progress, or Completed.
6. Add any additional comments or notes about your progress in the "Weekly Progress Notes" field.
7. List any issues encountered during the week in the "Weekly Progress Issues" field.
8. Enter your weekly goals in the "Weekly Goals" field.
9. List your weekly objectives in the "Weekly Objectives" field.
10. Enter actions taken for the week in the "Weekly Actions" field.
11. Select the status of your weekly actions from the "Weekly Actions Status" field. This field requires you to choose a status for your actions, which can be Not Started, In Progress, or Completed.
12. Click on the "Submit" button to save your assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Member**: Enter the name of the team member being assessed.
* **Week**: If required, select the week being assessed. Enter a numerical value representing the week.
* **Start Date**: If required, enter the start date of the assessment period. Format is YYYY-MM-DD.
* **End Date**: If required, enter the end date of the assessment period. Format is YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Weekly Progress Status**: Select a status for your progress, which can be Not Started, In Progress, or Completed.
* **Weekly Progress Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your progress.
* **Weekly Progress Issues**: List any issues encountered during the week.
* **Weekly Goals**: Enter your weekly goals.
* **Weekly Objectives**: List your weekly objectives.
* **Weekly Actions**: Enter actions taken for the week.
* **Weekly Actions Status**: Select the status of your actions, which can be Not Started, In Progress, or Completed.

Note: Fields marked as "required" are necessary to fill out the form.
