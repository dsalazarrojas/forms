# Islamic Academy Interview Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal and contact information from applicants and their parents or guardians. It will be reviewed and processed by our admissions team to prepare for interviews and tailor the educational experience to each applicant's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your applicant's personal details.
2. Provide your contact information.
3. Share your academic background and previous school details.
4. Answer questions about any special needs and medical conditions.
5. Indicate your language preference.
6. Confirm your religion.
7. Enter your date of birth.
8. Select a contact date and time.
9. Choose a contact phone number.
10. Enter a valid contact email address.
11. Provide your address.
12. Add any additional notes or comments.
13. Specify whether you will have an interviewer for the interview.
14. Choose an interview date and time.
15. Select an interview location.
16. Leave comments for the interviewer.
17. Sign the form with your applicant signature.
18. Sign the form with the interviewer signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Data**: Enter the name and other personal details of the applicant.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your contact details such as phone number, email address, and address.
* **Academic Background**: Describe your educational background and previous school details.
* **Previous School Details**: Share details about your previous school, including the name, address, and contact information.
* **Parent Details**: Provide details about your parent or guardian's contact information and relationship to you.
* **Medical Information**: If the applicant has any medical conditions or special needs, please describe.
* **Language**: Select the primary language you wish to communicate with us.
* **Religion**: Confirm your religion for our records.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Contact Date**: Choose a date for the contact or interview.
* **Contact Time**: Choose a time for the contact or interview.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter a valid phone number for us to contact you.
* **Contact Email**: Enter a valid email address for us to contact you.
* **Address**: Enter your physical address.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or information for the interviewers.
* **Interviewers**: Specify whether you will bring an interviewer for the applicant.
* **Interview Date**: Choose a date for the interview.
* **Interview Time**: Choose a time for the interview.
* **Interview Location**: Select a location for the interview.
* **Interviewers Comment**: Leave comments for the interviewers.
* **Applicant Signature**: Sign the form with your applicant signature.
* **Interviewer Signature**: Sign the form with the interviewer's signature.
