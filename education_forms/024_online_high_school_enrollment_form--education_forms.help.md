# Online High School Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects demographic information and preferences from students and their guardians to create a personalized learning plan and to ensure that the student receives relevant resources and support during the online high school program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. For required fields, ensure that you provide the requested information.
3. Select the appropriate options for grade level and school type from the dropdown menus.
4. Enter your email address and contact number for communication and follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name (Required)
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name in the field provided.

### Last Name (Required)
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name in the field provided.

### Date of Birth (Required)
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Contact Number (Required)
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter your contact number for communication and follow-up.

### Email (Required)
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication and follow-up.

### School Name (Required)
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the school you are enrolling from.

### Grade Level (Required)
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_one, required): Select your current grade level from the options provided:
	+ 9th
	+ 10th
	+ 11th
	+ 12th
	+ Senior
	+ Pre-Senior

### School Type (Required)
* **School Type** (`school_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of school you are enrolling from:
	+ High School
	+ Middle School
	+ Elementary School

### Parent Name (Optional)
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the parent or guardian accompanying the student.

### Student ID (Required)
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, required): Enter a unique student ID number.

## Tips

* Double-check that all fields are filled out accurately and honestly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
* Please note that this form is not a comprehensive application for enrollment, but rather a supporting form that will be reviewed and verified by our staff.
