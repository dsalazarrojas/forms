# Online Course Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather user feedback on their experience with an online course. It is intended to provide valuable insights to the course creators and administrators to improve the course content, structure, and overall quality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the designated fields.
2. Provide the title of the course you have taken.
3. Give a brief description of your experience with the course.
4. Rate your overall satisfaction with the course (on a scale of 1-5).
5. Provide any additional comments or instructions you received with the course.
6. Select the date you submitted your evaluation.
7. Specify the time you spent on the course.

# Field-by-Field Explanation
## Name (`name`, `text`, required: false)
Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.

## Email (`email`, `text`, required: false)
Enter your email address, which we may use to contact you for follow-up questions.

## Form Title (`form_title`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the title of the online course you are evaluating.

## Form Description (`form_description`, `text`, required: false)
Provide a brief description of your experience with the course.

## Rating (`rating`, `number`, required: false)
Rate your overall satisfaction with the course on a scale of 1-5.

## Form Instructions (`form_instructions`, `text`, required: false)
If you received any instructions or guidance with the course, please describe them here.

## Submission Date (`submission`, `date`, required: false)
Enter the date you submitted your evaluation.

## Submission Time (`submission_time`, `time`, required: false)
Enter the approximate time you spent on the course.

## Tips
- Please be as concise and honest as possible in your responses.
- If you have any additional comments or suggestions, you can add them below.
- Your feedback is invaluable in helping us improve the course, and we appreciate your input!
