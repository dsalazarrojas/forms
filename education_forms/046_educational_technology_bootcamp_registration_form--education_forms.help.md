# Educational Technology Bootcamp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants who are interested in attending an educational technology bootcamp. The form will help us understand your preferences and requirements for the bootcamp, ensuring that we can tailor the experience to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for the form (Field 1)
2. Select a category (Field 2)
3. Provide a description of your interest in the bootcamp (Field 3)
4. If you have a Form ID (a unique identifier for the bootcamp), enter it (Field 4)
5. If a file is generated as a result of your participation in the bootcamp, specify the Output File name (Field 5)
6. Identify the tool you have been assigned to work with during the bootcamp (Field 6)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Title** (Field 1, text, optional): This field is for you to enter a title that best describes your interest in the bootcamp.
- **Category** (Field 2, text, optional): Please select a category that best represents your area of interest or expertise.
- **Description** (Field 3, text, optional): Describe your interest in the bootcamp, what you hope to achieve, or what you expect from the experience.
- **Form ID** (Field 4, number, optional): If you have a unique identifier for the bootcamp, enter it here.
- **Output File** (Field 5, text, optional): If a file is generated as a result of your participation, please specify the name of the file.
- **Assigned Tool** (Field 6, text, optional): Identify the tool you have been assigned to work with during the bootcamp.
