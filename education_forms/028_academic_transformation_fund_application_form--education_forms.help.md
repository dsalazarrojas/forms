# Academic Transformation Fund Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to guide applicants through the submission of their academic transformation project proposal, ensuring that all necessary details are provided.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Provide the required personal contact information (Applicant Name, Email Address, Phone Number, and Title or Position).
2. Specify the department or college, and provide the name of your institution.
3. Describe the project details, including the project title, summary, and proposed start and end dates.
4. Select the area of academic transformation that your project falls under.
5. Indicate who will benefit from your project (e.g., undergraduate students, graduate students, faculty, etc.).
6. Provide a breakdown of your budget, including the total funding amount requested.
7. If you are receiving funding from other sources, indicate this and describe the other funding sources.
8. List any co-investigators or collaborators.
9. Certify that the information provided is accurate and that you agree to comply with all fund reporting requirements.
10. Specify the date of application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name** (required): Please provide your full name.
* **Email Address** (required): Enter a valid email address that can be used for communication.
* **Phone Number** (optional): If you prefer to provide a contact phone number.
* **Title or Position** (required): Enter your academic title or position.
* **Department** (required): Specify the department or college where you are working.
* **Institution Name** (required): Enter the name of your institution.
* **Project Title** (required): Enter a concise title of your project.
* **Project Summary** (required): Provide a 300-word summary of your project goals, methods, and expected outcomes.
* **Area of Academic Transformation** (required): Select the category that best describes your project (Curriculum Redesign, Technology Integration, Pedagogical Innovation, etc.).
* **Who will Benefit** (required): Indicate who will benefit from your project (e.g., undergraduate students, graduate students, faculty, etc.).
* **Proposed Project Start Date** (required): Enter the proposed start date of your project.
* **Proposed Project End Date** (required): Enter the proposed end date of your project.
* **Total Funding Requested** (required): Provide the total funding amount requested.
* **Budget Breakdown** (required): List major expense categories and estimated amounts.
* **Other Funding Sources** (required): Indicate if you are receiving funding from other sources.
* **Other Funding Sources** (optional): If yes, provide a description of the other funding sources.
* **Co-Investigators** (optional): List co-investigators or collaborators (name, title, department).
* **Agreement** (required): Certify that the information provided is accurate and that you agree to comply with all fund reporting requirements.
* **Application Date** (required): Specify the date of application.
