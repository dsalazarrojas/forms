# Coursework Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request a leave of absence for coursework due to various reasons such as health issues, family matters, or other personal reasons. The request will be reviewed by relevant authorities, and the outcome will be communicated to the student.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your course information in the designated fields, including course identifier, course name, and reason for the request.
2. Choose the start and end dates of the leave of absence.
3. Select the duration of the leave.
4. Choose the type of leave (Leave of Absence or Other).
5. If you have a supporting document, attach it to the form.
6. Provide any additional comments or information about your request.
7. Review your form for accuracy and completeness before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Course Identifier** (`course_identifier`, `text`, required)
Enter your course identifier for which you are requesting the leave of absence.

### 2. **Course Name** (`course_name`, `text`, required)
Enter the name of the course you are requesting the leave of absence for.

### 3. **Reason For Request** (`reason_for_request`, `text`, required)
Explain the reason for your leave of absence request. This can be due to health issues, family matters, or other personal reasons.

### 4. **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required)
Enter the start date of your leave of absence.

### 5. **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required)
Enter the end date of your leave of absence.

### 6. **Duration** (`duration`, `number`, required)
Enter the duration of your leave of absence.

### 7. **Leave Type** (`leave_type`, `select_one`, required)
Choose the type of leave you are requesting (Leave of Absence or Other).

### 8. **Reason For Absence** (`reason_for_absence`, `text`, optional)
If your leave of absence is not due to a Leave of Absence, provide a reason for your absence.

### 9. **Supporting Document** (`supporting_document`, `text`, optional)
If you have any supporting documents (e.g., medical certificate, etc.), attach them to this form.

### 10. **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional)
Provide any additional comments or information about your request.

### 11. **Status** (`status`, `text`, optional)
This field will be populated by the relevant authorities after reviewing your request.

Note: The status field will not be filled out by the user but will be used to track the progress of the request.
