# Classroom Book Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Classroom Book Order Form is used by teachers to order books for their students. This form helps us to accurately process your book orders and ensure timely delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your information.
2. Select the correct book titles and quantities for each book ordered.
3. Provide your preferred book condition, format, and any special requests.
4. Enter your delivery address and preferred delivery date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as a teacher.
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for order confirmation.
* **Teacher Phone** (`teacher_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your school or institution.
* **School District** (`school_district`, text, optional): Enter the district or region where your school is located.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_one, required): Select the grade level of the students who will use these books.
* **Book 1 - Title** (`book_1_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the first book ordered.
* **Book 1 - Author** (`book_1_author`, text, required): Enter the author name of the first book.
* **Book 1 - ISBN** (`book_1_isbn`, text, optional): Enter the ISBN number of the first book.
* **Book 1 - Quantity** (`book_1_quantity`, number, required): Enter the number of copies of the first book needed.
* **Book 1 - Unit Price** (`book_1_unit_price`, number, optional): Enter the price per book for the first book.
* **Book 2 - Title** (`book_2_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the second book (if applicable).
* **Book 2 - Author** (`book_2_author`, text, optional): Enter the author name of the second book (if applicable).
* **Book 2 - Quantity** (`book_2_quantity`, number, optional): Enter the number of copies of the second book needed (if applicable).
* **Book 3 - Title** (`book_3_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the third book (if applicable).
* **Book 3 - Quantity** (`book_3_quantity`, number, optional): Enter the number of copies of the third book needed (if applicable).
* **Additional Books** (`additional_books`, text, optional): Enter information about any additional books ordered.
* **Preferred Book Condition** (`book_condition`, select_one, optional): Select the preferred book condition (New, Like New, Good Condition, Used, or No Preference).
* **Format Preference** (`hardcover_paperback`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred format (Hardcover, Paperback, or No Preference).
* **Special Requests or Notes** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Enter any special instructions or requests for this order.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, required): Enter the delivery address where the books should be delivered.
* **Date Books Needed By** (`delivery_date_needed`, date, required): Enter the date when the books are needed.
