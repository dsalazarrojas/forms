# global_exchange_fellowship_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals to apply for a global exchange fellowship program. It gathers information about the candidate's contact details, motivation, references, and educational background.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form sections below to provide accurate and complete information.
2. Review each section carefully to ensure all required information is provided.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: A field to provide your full name.
	+ Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., passport, ID card).
* **Email**: A field to provide your email address.
	+ Enter your personal or work email address that you frequently use.
* **Phone**: A field to provide your phone number.
	+ Enter your phone number that you use for communication.
* **Address**: A field to provide your address.
	+ Enter your current address for correspondence and delivery purposes.
* **City**: A field to provide your city.
	+ Enter the city where you currently reside or work.
* **State**: A field to provide your state/province.
	+ Enter the state or province where you currently reside or work.
* **Zip**: A field to provide your zip code.
	+ Enter your postal code for delivery purposes.
* **Country**: A field to provide your country of residence.
	+ Enter your country of current residence.
* **University**: A field to provide your university name.
	+ Enter the name of your current or past university.
* **Program**: A field to provide your university program.
	+ Enter the name of your current or past university program.
* **CV/Resume**: A field to upload your CV/Resume.
	+ Upload your up-to-date CV or Resume document.
* **Motivation Statement 1**: A field to provide your motivation for applying.
	+ Describe why you want to join this global exchange fellowship program.
* **Motivation Statement 2**: A field to provide additional motivation.
	+ Explain your motivation for applying to this program further.
* **References**: A field to provide references.
	+ List any relevant references you have (e.g., professors, supervisors).
* **Additional Info**: A field to provide any additional information.
	+ Add any relevant details not mentioned elsewhere on the form.
* **NGOs**: A field to provide information about NGOs.
	+ If you have any affiliation with NGOs, provide details.
* **Motivation Statement 3**: A field to provide further motivation.
	+ Explain your motivation for applying to this program further.
* **Submit**: A field to submit your application.
	+ Once you have completed all the required fields, click the submit button to send your application.
