# Woodworking Class Proposal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Woodworking Class Proposal Form is designed for instructors to propose, create, or list classes related to woodworking or carpentry. It gathers information about the class, such as its title, description, schedule, location, and other details that will help in its promotion, management, and student enrollment.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Woodworking Class Proposal Form**: This is the main text field where you will write a detailed proposal of your class, including its objectives, content, and any other relevant information.
2.  **Instructor Name**: Please enter your name as the instructor leading the class.
3.  **Class Name**: Enter the name of your class as it will be advertised or listed.
4.  **Scheduling (Start Date and Start Time)**: Fill in the start date and time of your class.
5.  **Scheduling (End Date and End Time)**: Fill in the end date and time of your class.
6.  **Duration**: Select how long your class will last from the options provided (1, 2, 3, or 4 hours).
7.  **Location**: Specify where your class will take place.
8.  **Capacity and Maximum Capacity**: Indicate how many students your class can accommodate and the maximum capacity.
9.  **Price Range (Min Price and Max Price)**: Set the price range for your class.
10.  **Description**: Write a detailed description of your class, including its objectives, content, and what students can expect to learn.
11.  **Equipment Needed**: If any specific equipment or tools are required for your class, list them here.
12.  **Expected Skills**: Specify the skills that students are expected to have or acquire from your class.
13.  **Teaching Method**: Choose how your class will be taught: through lecture, demo, hands-on, or other methods.
14.  **Class Category**: Categorize your class under woodworking, carpentry, woodcarving, or other categories.
15.  **Level of Difficulty**: Rate the difficulty level of your class as basic, intermediate, or advanced.
16.  **Course Level**: Specify whether your class is for beginners, intermediate, or advanced learners.
17.  **Course Language**: Choose the language of your class.
18.  **Course Topic**: Specify a detailed topic or theme of your class.
19.  **Course Format**: Describe the format of your class, if it’s a course.
20.  **Instructor Experience**: Specify your level of experience in teaching or the specific skill required for the class.
21.  **Instructor Education**: Specify your educational background.
22.  **Institution of Education**: If relevant, specify where you graduated from.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Propose Form**: This is the primary field where you will write a detailed proposal of your class.
- **Instructor Name:** This is to enter your name as the instructor.
- **Class Name:** The name of your class should be clear and descriptive so students and administrators can easily recognize it.
- **Scheduling (Start Date and Start Time):** These two fields are for setting up the schedule of your class.
- **Duration:** Choose how many hours your class will run.
- **Location:** This should be a specific location where your class will take place.
- **Capacity and Maximum Capacity:** Define how many students your class can accommodate and the maximum capacity.
- **Price Range (Min Price and Max Price):** Set a price range for your class, which can vary based on the content, duration, and other factors.
- **Description:** Write a comprehensive description of your class, including its objectives and what students can expect.
- **Equipment Needed:** List any specific tools or equipment that students need to have or provide before attending the class.
- **Expected Skills:** Specify the skills students are expected to have or acquire from your class.
- **Teaching Method:** This is for specifying how your class will be taught.
- **Class Category:** Categorize your class under a specific category.
- **Level of Difficulty:** This is for rating how difficult or easy your class is.
- **Course Level:** Specify whether your class is for beginners, intermediate, or advanced learners.
- **Course Language:** Choose the language of your class.
- **Course Topic:** This is for specifying a detailed topic or theme of your class.
- **Course Format:** Describe the format of your class, if it’s a course.
- **Instructor Experience:** This is for specifying your level of experience in teaching or the specific skill required for the class.
- **Instructor Education:** This is for specifying your educational background.
- **Institution of Education:** If relevant, specify where you graduated from.

## Tips
- Always make sure to fill out all fields relevant to your class.
- Use clear and concise language in writing your class proposal and description.
- Be accurate in specifying your class schedule, location, and other details.
- Ensure that your class proposal and description align with the specified fields in the form.
- If you are not sure about any field, use the options or select the one that best fits your class information.
- Proofread your proposal for any errors before submitting it.
