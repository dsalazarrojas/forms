# Intercultural Learning Fellowship Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Intercultural Learning Fellowship Application is a form designed for applicants to provide information about themselves for an intercultural learning fellowship. This application will be reviewed to select candidates for the fellowship.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2. Attach relevant documents as required (e.g., a CV, motivation attachment).
3. Provide accurate and thorough information in each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Please enter your name.
* **Applicant Email** (`applicant_email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **CV Upload** (`cv_upload`, text, required): Upload a PDF or document file containing your CV.
* **Motivation** (`motivation`, text, optional): If required, describe your motivation for the fellowship. This field is optional but recommended.
* **University Name** (`university_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your current or intended university.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the program you are interested in.
* **Motivation Attachment** (`motivation_attachment`, text, optional): If required, attach a document describing your motivation.

## Tips
- Please make sure you have filled out all required fields.
- Double-check the information you provide, especially your email address and university name.
- Use clear and concise language in your motivation statement.
