# Educational Facility Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to record and report incidents that occurred at the educational facility. This includes but is not limited to incidents involving students, staff, or faculty.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of incident that occurred from the options provided in the "What type of incident" field.
2. Enter the date of the incident in the "Date of incident" field using the date format (yyyy-mm-dd).
3. Describe the location of the incident in the "Location of incident" field.
4. Provide a detailed description of what happened in the "Description of incident" field.
5. Select the current investigation status from the options provided in the "Investigation status" field.
6. Provide the name of the person who reported the incident in the "Reported by" field.
7. List any safety measures taken after the incident in the "Safety measures taken" field.
8. Enter the name and contact information of the person who reported the incident in the "Incident reporter" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What type of incident** (`incident_type`, `select_one`): Select the type of incident that occurred, such as "Student injury" or "Equipment malfunction".
* **Date of incident** (`incident_date`, `date`): Enter the date of the incident in the format (yyyy-mm-dd).
* **Location of incident** (`incident_location`, `text`): Describe the location where the incident occurred, including the building and room number if applicable.
* **Description of incident** (`incident_description`, `text`): Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
* **Investigation status** (`incident_investigation_status`, `select_one`): Select the current status of the investigation, such as "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Reported by** (`incident_reporter`, `text`): Enter the name and contact information of the person who reported the incident.
* **Safety measures taken** (`incident_safety_measures`, `text`): List any safety measures taken after the incident, such as new procedures or equipment installed.
* **Incident reporter** (`incident_incident_reporter`, `text`): Enter the name and contact information of the person who reported the incident.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that the incident is properly documented.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the facility management for guidance.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the facility administration.
