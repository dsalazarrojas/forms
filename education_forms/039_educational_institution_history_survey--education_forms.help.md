# Educational Institution History Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about educational institutions, likely for research, reporting, or other internal purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your student information in the first section, "Student Information".
2. Share staff information in the second section, "Staff Information".
3. Include any relevant details about the institution's alumni in the third section, "Alumni Information".
4. If you have any historical insights to share, you can do so in the fourth section, "Historical Insights".
5. Finally, click the "Submit" button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Information** (Student Information, text, optional): This section is for providing your student profile, including your name, student ID, and any other relevant details.
* **Staff Information** (Staff Information, text, optional): Share information about the staff members, including their names, positions, and contact details.
* **Alumni Information** (Alumni Information, text, optional): This section is for sharing details about the institution's alumni, including their names, years of graduation, and any notable achievements.
* **Historical Insights** (Historical Insights, text, optional): If you have any historical insights, such as notable events or milestones, you can share them here.
* **Submit** (Submit, text, required): Clicking this button will send the form and allow you to review and correct any errors before finalizing your submission.
