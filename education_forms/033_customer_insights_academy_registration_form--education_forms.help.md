# Customer Insights Academy Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Insights Academy Registration Form is designed to collect participant information for the Customer Insights Academy. This form is used to gather details about participants, including contact information, program details, and additional information. Please note that this form is not affiliated with any specific form platform and is an original adaptation created using AI.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your participant information in the "Participant Information" section.
2. Enter your contact details in the "Contact Details" section.
3. Fill in the "Program Information" section with relevant details.
4. If necessary, provide additional information in the "Additional Information" section.
5. If you have files to upload, please do so in the "Upload Files" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information (Participant Information)** (`text`, required: false): This is the first section of the form where you can enter your participant information. Please provide your name, title, or any other relevant details.
* **Contact Details (Contact Details)** (`text`, required: false): In this section, you can enter your contact details such as your email address, phone number, or any other contact information.
* **Program Information (Program Information)** (`text`, required: false): Here, you can fill in relevant details about the program you are registering for, such as the program name, date, or any other relevant information.
* **Additional Information (Additional Information)** (`text`, required: false): If you have any additional information you'd like to provide, please enter it here.
* **Upload Files (Upload Files)** (`text`, required: false): If you have files to upload, please do so here. This could include resumes, certificates, or any other relevant documents.

## Tips
