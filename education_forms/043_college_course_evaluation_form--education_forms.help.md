# College Course Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help instructors and administrators evaluate the effectiveness of a college course. It gathers feedback from students on various aspects of the course, including course content, instructor performance, and overall experience. The data collected will be used to improve the course and provide insights for future course development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Read the form carefully to understand what each question is asking.
2.  Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, providing your opinions and experiences.
3.  For questions with specific rating options (e.g., "Overall Course Rating" and "Overall Instructor Rating"), choose the best answer that reflects your opinion.
4.  For open-ended questions (e.g., "Course Strengths," "Course Weaknesses," "Instructor Strengths," and "Instructor Improvements"), provide detailed and specific feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Your Name** (`student_name`): This is the name of the student who is filling out the form. This field is optional and only required if you want to be anonymous.
*   **Email Address** (`student_email`): This is the email address of the student. This field is optional and only required if you want to receive a follow-up email with your feedback.
*   **Student ID** (`student_id`): This is the student ID number. This field is optional and only required if you want to be identified as a specific student in the evaluation.
*   **Course Name** (`course_name`): This is the title of the course you are evaluating.
*   **Course Number** (`course_number`): This is the code or number of the course.
*   **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`): This is the name of the instructor of the course.
*   **Semester** (`semester`): Choose the semester in which you took the course.
*   **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`): This is the date on which you completed this evaluation.
*   **Overall Course Rating** (`overall_course_rating`): Rate the course overall, using the options: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
*   **Overall Instructor Rating** (`overall_instructor_rating`): Rate the instructor overall, using the options: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
*   **Course Objectives Were Clear** (`course_objectives_clarity`): Rate how clear the course objectives were, using the options: Strongly Agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, or Strongly Disagree.
*   **Course Content Was Relevant** (`course_content_relevance`): Rate how relevant the course content was, using the options: Strongly Agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, or Strongly Disagree.
*   **Workload Was Appropriate** (`workload_appropriate`): Rate how appropriate the workload was, using the options: Strongly Agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, or Strongly Disagree.
*   **Assignments Helped Me Learn** (`assignments_helpful`): Rate how helpful the assignments were in learning, using the options: Strongly Agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, or Strongly Disagree.
*   **Grading Was Fair** (`grading_fair`): Rate how fair the grading was, using the options: Strongly Agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, or Strongly Disagree.
*   **Instructor Was Knowledgeable** (`instructor_knowledge`): Rate how knowledgeable the instructor was, using the options: Strongly Agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, or Strongly Disagree.
*   **Instructor Was Organized** (`instructor_organization`): Rate how organized the instructor was, using the options: Strongly Agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, or Strongly Disagree.
*   **Instructor Was Available for Help** (`instructor_availability`): Rate how available the instructor was for help, using the options: Strongly Agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, or Strongly Disagree.
*   **Instructor Communicated Clearly** (`instructor_communication`): Rate how clearly the instructor communicated, using the options: Strongly Agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, or Strongly Disagree.
*   **Instructor Treated Students With Respect** (`instructor_respect`): Rate how the instructor treated students, using the options: Strongly Agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, or Strongly Disagree.
*   **Class Discussions Were Valuable** (`class_discussions`): Rate how valuable the class discussions were, using the options: Strongly Agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, or Strongly Disagree.
*   **Course Materials Were Helpful** (`materials_helpful`): Rate how helpful the course materials were, using the options: Strongly Agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, or Strongly Disagree.
*   **My Skills Improved in This Course** (`skills_improved`): Rate how much your skills improved in the course, using the options: Strongly Agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, or Strongly Disagree.
*   **Would Recommend This Course** (`would_recommend`): Rate whether you would recommend the course to other students, using the options: Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Not Sure, Probably No, or Definitely No.
*   **Course Strengths** (`course_strengths`): Provide detailed feedback on what strengths the course had.
*   **Course Weaknesses** (`course_weaknesses`): Provide detailed feedback on what weaknesses the course had.
*   **Instructor Strengths** (`instructor_strengths`): Provide detailed feedback on what strengths the instructor had.
*   **Suggestions for Instructor Improvement** (`instructor_improvements`): Provide detailed feedback on what the instructor could improve on.
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Provide any other feedback or comments you have about the course or instructor.

## Tips
*   Be honest and thorough in your feedback.
*   Provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your answers.
*   If you have questions or concerns, contact your instructor or course administrator.

Note: This guide is meant to be a general help guide. The form structure and fields may be subject to change, so make sure to review the form carefully before filling it out.
