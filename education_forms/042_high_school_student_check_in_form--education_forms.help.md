# high_school_student_check_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The high school student check-in form is used to collect student information for high school students, typically for attendance or academic purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter the student's ID number in the "Student Id" field if required.
* Enter the student's name in the "student_name" field.
* Select the student's grade level from the "grade_level" dropdown menu.
* Choose the student's room number(s) from the available options.
* Enter the name of the student's teacher in the "teacher_name" field.
* Select the student's grade (e.g., A, B, C, D, or F) from the "grade" dropdown menu.
* Indicate the student's status as "Present", "Absent", or "Late" in the "status" field.
* Enter any additional comments about the student's attendance in the "comments" field.
* Select the date of the student's attendance (if applicable).
* Choose the time of the student's attendance (if applicable).
* Enter the student's email address in the "email" field.
* Enter the student's phone number in the "phone" field.
* Enter any other relevant information about the student in the "other_info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Id** (`student_id`, Number, Optional): Enter the student's ID number if required.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the student's full name.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, Select One, Optional): Select the student's grade level (9th, 10th, 11th, or 12th).
* **Room Number** (`student_room_number`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the student's room number(s).
* **Teacher Name** (`student_teacher_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the student's teacher.
* **Grade** (`student_grade`, Select One, Optional): Select the student's grade (A, B, C, D, or F).
* **Status** (`student_status`, Select One, Optional): Indicate the student's status as "Present", "Absent", or "Late".
* **Comments** (`student_comments`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments about the student's attendance.
* **Date** (`student_date`, Date, Optional): Select the date of the student's attendance (if applicable).
* **Time** (`student_time`, Time, Optional): Choose the time of the student's attendance (if applicable).
* **Email** (`student_email`, Email, Optional): Enter the student's email address.
* **Phone** (`student_phone`, Text, Optional): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Other Info** (`student_other_info`, Text, Optional): Enter any other relevant information about the student.
