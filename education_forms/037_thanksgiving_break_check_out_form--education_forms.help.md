# Thanksgiving Break Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for students to provide essential information and confirm their status for Thanksgiving break. It helps the school or university ensure that students are properly accounted for and their absence is documented.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your student information, including your name and other relevant details.
2. Provide your contact information, which may include your phone number, email, or address.
3. Enter your emergency contact's information, including their name and relationship to you.
4. Give parent permission for your absence, if applicable.
5. Confirm your student absence, if you are leaving for the break.
6. Sign the form to confirm your identity and commitment to follow school rules.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Information** (Student Information, text, required): Enter your name and any other relevant student details.
* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, text, required): Provide your contact details, such as your phone number, email, or address.
* **Emergency Contact** (Emergency Contact, text, required): Enter the name and relationship of your emergency contact, such as a parent or guardian.
* **Parent Permission** (Parent Permission, text, required): If you are a minor, give permission for your parent or guardian to confirm your absence.
* **Student Absence** (Student Absence, text, required): Confirm that you will be absent during the break.
* **Student Confirmation** (Student Confirmation, text, required): Confirm your identity and your status during the break.
* **Student Signature** (Student Signature, text, required): Sign the form to confirm your identity and commitment to follow school rules.

## Tips
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Make sure to include your up-to-date contact details.
* If you have any special circumstances, such as a medical condition or family emergency, notify your school or university administrators.
* Review the school or university rules and regulations before signing the form.
