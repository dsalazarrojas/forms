# Disability Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Disability Assessment Form is a document used to gather information about a student's disability status and circumstances. This form is essential in helping educational institutions provide necessary support and accommodations to ensure equal access to education for students with disabilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Review each question carefully and answer truthfully.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed.
* For the "Student ID" field, enter your student ID number.
* For the "Student Name" field, enter your full name as it appears on your student record.
* For the "Date of Birth" field, enter your date of birth using the standard date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* For the "Disability Type" field, select "Yes" or "No" depending on whether you have a disability.
* For the "Disability Description" field, provide a brief description of your disability, if applicable.
* For the "Student Contact Info" field, indicate whether you would like to provide additional contact information for the institution to follow up with you about your disability assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Disability Assessment** (`disability_assessment`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any additional information about your disability, if applicable.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, required): Enter your student ID number to ensure that your information is tied to your student record.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your student record.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the standard date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Disability Type** (`disability_type`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you have a disability ("Yes" or "No").
* **Disability Description** (`disability_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your disability, if applicable.
* **Student Contact Info** (`student_contact_info`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether you would like to provide additional contact information for the institution to follow up with you about your disability assessment.
