# Interdisciplinary Learning Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Interdisciplinary Learning Satisfaction Survey is a form designed to gather feedback from students on their learning experience in a course. This feedback is essential for instructors and administrators to assess the effectiveness of the course material and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the course you are providing feedback on.
2. Provide a brief description of your experience in the course.
3. Rate your overall satisfaction with the course (if applicable).
4. Share any comments or thoughts about the course.
5. Enter your email address (if you want to be contacted about your feedback).
6. Enter your phone number (if you want to be contacted about your feedback).
7. Select the dates you participated in the course.
8. Select the time you spent on the course.
9. Choose your preferred options for the questions below:
	* Select one option from the list for "select one" questions.
	* Select multiple options from the list for "select multiple" questions.
