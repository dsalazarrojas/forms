# Teacher Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teacher Recommendation Form is used to gather information from teachers about their students for purposes such as academic recommendations, character references, or scholarship applications. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter the teacher's name in the "Teacher Name" field.
3. Enter the school name in the "School Name" field.
4. Select the student's grade level from the "Grade Level" dropdown menu.
5. Enter the teacher's recommendation or endorsement in the "Teacher Recommendation" field.
6. Enter the teacher's signature in the "Teacher Signature" field.
7. Enter the school's email address in the "School Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter the name of the student you are recommending.
* **Teacher Name**: Enter your name as the teacher recommending the student.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of the school where you teach.
* **Grade Level**: Select the grade level of the student you are recommending (Elementary, Middle School, or High School).
* **Teacher Recommendation**: Enter a detailed recommendation or endorsement of the student's abilities, qualities, or achievements.
* **Teacher Signature**: Sign your name as the teacher recommending the student.
* **School Email**: Enter your school's email address.

## Tips
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer back to this help guide for clarification.
* If you are unable to fill out the form, contact support at form@gic.mx for assistance.
