# Youth Ministries Reference Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Ministries Reference Form is a tool for youth ministries to gather information about a youth's involvement in their programs. This form helps to provide a comprehensive view of a youth's engagement, skills, and interests, and is typically completed by a reference, such as a supervisor, pastor, or director.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the youth's name in the "Name" field.
2. Indicate if the youth is seeking a position in the "Position Sought" field.
3. Select all the programs in which the youth has been involved in the "Program Activity" field.
4. Enter the date of the youth's last program in the "Date of Last Program" field.
5. Enter the youth's contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
6. Provide the name of the reference in the "Name of Reference" field.
7. Enter the title of the reference in the "Title of Reference" field.
8. Describe the relationship between the reference and the youth in the "Relationship to Reference" field.
9. Enter the reference's email address in the "Email" field.
10. Specify how long the reference has known the youth in the "How Long" field.
11. Enter any additional comments about the youth in the "Comments" field.
12. Specify if the reference has access to the youth's assigned tool in the "Assigned Tool" field.
13. Enter any form IDs for the youth in the "Form IDs" field.
14. Indicate if the youth has any category-specific involvement in the "Category" field.
15. Enter a brief description of the youth's strengths and weaknesses in the "Description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (Text, Required): Enter the youth's name.
* **Position Sought** (Select One, Optional): Indicate if the youth is seeking a position.
* **Program Activity** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select all the programs in which the youth has been involved.
* **Date of Last Program** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of the youth's last program.
* **Contact Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the youth's contact phone number.
* **Name of Reference** (Text, Optional): Provide the name of the reference.
* **Title of Reference** (Text, Optional): Enter the title of the reference.
* **Relationship to Reference** (Text, Optional): Describe the relationship between the reference and the youth.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter the reference's email address.
* **How Long** (Text, Optional): Specify how long the reference has known the youth.
* **Comments** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments about the youth.
* **Assigned Tool** (Text, Optional): Specify if the reference has access to the youth's assigned tool.
* **Form IDs** (Text, Optional): Enter any form IDs for the youth.
* **Category** (Select One, Optional): Indicate if the youth has any category-specific involvement.
* **Description** (Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of the youth's strengths and weaknesses.
