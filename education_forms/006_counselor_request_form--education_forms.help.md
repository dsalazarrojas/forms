# Counselor Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from a client to support a counselor in providing the best possible service. It gathers essential details for a meeting, allowing the counselor to understand the client's needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on each field to start typing.
2. Enter your full name, email, and phone number in the *Client Information* section.
3. Provide the desired date and time for the meeting in the *Appointment Details* section.
4. Indicate your desired counselor preferences in the *Counselor Preferences* section.
5. Add any additional information that might be helpful for the counselor to know in the *Additional Information* section.
6. Sign the form by typing your name in the *Client Signature* and *Counselor Signature* sections.
7. Click the *Submit* button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information (1)** (Required): Enter your full name, email, and phone number for the counselor to contact you.
* **Appointment Details (2)** (Required): Enter the desired date and time for the meeting with the counselor.
* **Counselor Preferences (3)** (Optional): Indicate any specific preferences you have for the counselor, such as language or location.
* **Additional Information (4)** (Optional): Provide any additional information that might be helpful for the counselor to know, such as medical history or personal details.
* **Client Signature (6)** (Required): Type your name to sign the form, acknowledging that you are the client and agree to the meeting details.
* **Counselor Signature (7)** (Required): Type the counselor's name to sign the form, indicating agreement with the meeting details.
* **Submit (5)** (Required): Click to send the form to the counselor.
