# course_enrollment_contract_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The course_enrollment_contract_form is a form used to collect necessary information from students and instructors for course enrollment. It captures consent, course details, and signature confirmation to ensure a smooth and organized course administration process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Consent**: Please indicate your consent to enroll in the course by selecting "Yes" or "No". This will help us understand your willingness to participate.
2. **Course Details**: Please provide the course title, code, or other relevant information.
3. **Contract Acceptance**: Confirm your acceptance of the course contract by selecting "True" or "False".
4. **Student Signature**: Indicate whether you are the student signing the contract by selecting "Yes" or "No".
5. **Instructor Signature**: Confirm whether the instructor is signing the contract by selecting "Yes" or "No".
6. **Date Signed**: Please enter the date when the contract was signed.
7. **Time Signed**: Please enter the time when the contract was signed.
8. **Save and Submit**: Once you have completed all the fields, click "Save and Submit" to finalize your enrollment contract.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Consent**: A note field where you can provide a statement of consent.
  
- **Course Details**: A text field to input course information, such as title or code.
  
- **Contract Acceptance**: A select_one field to confirm acceptance of the course contract.
  
- **Student Signature**: A select_one field to determine if you are the student signing the contract.
  
- **Instructor Signature**: A select_one field to determine if the instructor is signing the contract.
  
- **Date Signed**: A date field to record the date the contract was signed.
  
- **Time Signed**: A time field to record the time the contract was signed.
  
- **Student Signature**: A field to capture the student's signature. This field should be used only if the "Student Signature" select_one field indicates "Yes".
  
- **Instructor Signature**: A field to capture the instructor's signature. This field should be used only if the "Instructor Signature" select_one field indicates "Yes".
