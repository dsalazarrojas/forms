# data_model_program_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from candidates about their application for a data model program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Candidate Details section with the candidate's personal details.
2. Select whether or not the candidate has access to necessary resources.
3. Provide the candidate's contact information, including their email and phone number.
4. Enter a project proposal, which should outline the candidate's plan and approach.
5. Add any additional details about the project proposal.
6. Enter the candidate's address, which should be a valid email address.
7. Enter the candidate's phone number, which will be used for communication.
8. Enter the candidate's email address, which will be used for correspondence.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate Details** (`candidate_details`, `text`, required: false): Fill in the candidate's name, job title, or any other relevant details about the candidate.
* **Resources** (`resources`, `select_multiple`, required: false): If the candidate has access to necessary resources, please select "Yes" from the dropdown menu.
* **Candidate Contacts** (`candidate_contacts`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional contact information for the candidate, such as their department or supervisor.
* **Project Proposal** (`project_proposal`, `note`, required: false): Enter a brief summary of the candidate's project proposal, including their approach and goals.
* **Project Proposal Details** (`project_proposal_details`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional details about the project proposal, such as timelines or milestones.
* **Candidate Address** (`candidate_address`, `email`, required: false): Enter the candidate's email address, which will be used for correspondence.
* **Candidate Phone** (`candidate_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the candidate's phone number, which will be used for communication.
* **Candidate Email** (`candidate_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address for the candidate to receive important notifications.

## Tips

* Please make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information about the candidate.
* If the candidate has access to necessary resources, please select "Yes" from the dropdown menu in the Resources field.
* Enter the candidate's email address carefully, as it will be used for critical communication.
