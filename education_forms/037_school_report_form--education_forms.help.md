# School Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The School Report Form is designed to collect and document student progress, attendance, and other relevant information. This form is typically completed by educators, administrators, or other stakeholders to ensure students' needs are met and progress is tracked.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form accurately and effectively, follow these steps:

4. Record the date of the last contact with the student in the **Date Last Contacted** field (id: 4) or select the date and time of the meeting/event.
5. Check the student's attendance status in the **Student Attendance** field (id: 5) and select all relevant options (e.g., 'Yes' for attending).
6. Note any behavior issues the student is experiencing in the **Behavior Issues** field (id: 6).
7. Document the student's academic performance in the **Academic Performance** field (id: 7).
8. Add any parent comments or feedback in the **Parent Comments** field (id: 8).

13. Identify the student's support needs in the **Student Support Needs** field (id: 13).

15. Sign and confirm the form by selecting the appropriate option in the **Parent Signature** field (id: 15).
