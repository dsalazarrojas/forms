# student_insurance_claim_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for an insurance claim related to your education. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth processing process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the first page of the form (`student_insurance_claim_form_1`) with your claim details.
2. On the next page (`claimant_details`), enter information about the claimant, such as their name and email address.
3. On the next page (`claimant_name`, `claimant_email`, `claimant_phone`, `claimant_address`), enter additional claimant details.
4. On the next page (`insurance_details`), enter information about the insurance coverage.
5. On the next page (`insurance_provider`), select the insurance provider.
6. On the next page (`claim_date`), select the date of the claim.
7. On the next page (`claim_amount`), enter the claim amount.
8. On the next page (`claim_description`), describe the reason for the claim.
9. On the next page (`supporting_documents`), select the supporting documents required for the claim.
10. On the last page (`upload_document`), upload any supporting documents selected in the previous page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Claim Details** (`student_insurance_claim_form_1`):
	+ Please enter a brief description of your insurance claim.
* **Claimant Details** (`claimant_details`):
	+ Enter details about the claimant, such as their name and email address.
* **Name** (`claimant_name`):
	+ Enter the name of the claimant.
* **Email** (`claimant_email`):
	+ Enter the email address of the claimant.
* **Phone** (`claimant_phone`):
	+ Enter the phone number of the claimant.
* **Address** (`claimant_address`):
	+ Enter the address of the claimant.
* **Insurance Details** (`insurance_details`):
	+ Enter information about the insurance coverage.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`):
	+ Select the insurance provider.
* **Claim Date** (`claim_date`):
	+ Select the date of the claim.
* **Claim Amount** (`claim_amount`):
	+ Enter the claim amount.
* **Claim Description** (`claim_description`):
	+ Describe the reason for the claim.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`):
	+ Select the supporting documents required for the claim. Options are:
		- Proof of Attendance
		- Proof of Expenses
		- Other
* **Upload Document** (`upload_document`):
	+ Select whether to upload supporting documents.
