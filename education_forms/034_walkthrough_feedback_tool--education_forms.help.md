# Walkthrough Feedback Tool - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Walkthrough Feedback Tool is designed to gather feedback from users who have completed a walkthrough process or activity. This feedback will help the organization understand the effectiveness and usability of the walkthrough process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand the questions and options.
2. For **Walkthrough Feedback Tool Form 1**, type your comments and feedback in the text field.
3. For **Select One**, choose one of the provided options from the dropdown menu.
4. If the field is not required, you can skip it.
5. Once you have filled out the entire form, submit it for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Walkthrough Feedback Tool Form 1**: This field is a text input where you can provide detailed comments and feedback about your walkthrough experience. It is not required, so you can submit the form without filling it out if you don't have any comments.
* **Select One**: This field is a dropdown menu where you choose one of the provided options. The options are: 1, 2, and 3. You must select one of these options.
* **Select Multiple**: This field is not currently available in the form, as it is not present in the inputSchema. However, this field would typically be a dropdown menu where you choose multiple options from a list.
* **Tips**: If you have any general comments or questions, please contact form@gic.mx for assistance.

## Tips
* Please provide detailed and specific feedback to help the organization improve the walkthrough process.
* Be honest and objective in your feedback, avoiding personal opinions or biases.
* You can modify the form or use it as a base to create your own custom form, and share your adaptation with others under CC BY 4.0.
