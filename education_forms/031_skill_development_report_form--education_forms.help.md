# Skill Development Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skill Development Report Form is a template used to gather feedback and progress information from educators and learners on a learner's skill development. It is designed to track and evaluate a learner's progress and provide guidance for next steps.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the "Educator Comments" field to provide an update on the learner's progress and feedback.
2. Answer the "Learner Progress" question with either "Yes" or "No" based on the learner's current level of progress.
3. Optionally, provide additional feedback or comments in the "Feedback" field.
4. Select the "Training Programs" that the learner is currently enrolled in or has completed.
5. Enter the evaluation dates for the skill development report.
6. If applicable, provide notes on the "Next Steps" field for the learner's next steps.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Learner Progress** (Yes/No): Please select "Yes" if the learner has shown significant progress in the skill development process, and "No" otherwise.
* **Educator Comments**: Please provide a detailed update on the learner's progress and feedback. This is a required field.
* **Feedback** (Text): Provide any additional feedback or comments on the learner's progress.
* **Training Programs** (Select Multiple): Select the training programs that the learner is currently enrolled in or has completed.
* **Evaluation Dates** (Date): Enter the evaluation dates for the skill development report.
* **Next Steps** (Note): If applicable, provide notes on the learner's next steps for further guidance.
* **Evaluation Dates** (Date): Enter the evaluation dates for the skill development report.
