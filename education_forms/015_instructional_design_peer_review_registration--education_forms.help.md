# instructional_design_peer_review_registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The instructional design peer review registration form is intended to gather information from potential peer reviewers with expertise in instructional design and related fields. This form will help identify suitable reviewers for peer review processes, ensuring that they have the necessary qualifications and experience to provide valuable feedback on educational materials.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Fill out the required fields
Enter the following information:

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number for any additional contact or follow-up purposes.
* **What type of expertise do you have?**: Select all relevant areas of expertise, such as Instructional Design, Adult Learning, Educational Technology, and Assessment.
* **What days are you available to review?**: Select all the days you are available to participate in peer reviews, such as Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, and Sunday.
* **What kind of reviews do you want to participate in?**: Select all the types of reviews you wish to participate in, such as Module, Lesson, and Full Course.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments about your experience and qualifications.
* **Association Membership**: Select any relevant association membership that aligns with your expertise and experience.
* **Learning Management System (LMS) experience**: Select all the LMS platforms you have experience with, such as Canvas, Brightspace, and Blackboard.

### Step 2: Optionally fill out the remaining fields
You may also choose to enter additional information about your experience and qualifications, such as:

* **Other relevant information**: Enter any other relevant details about your experience, skills, or qualifications.

### Tips

* Please ensure that you have selected all the relevant fields for your expertise and availability.
* Review your form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us for assistance.
