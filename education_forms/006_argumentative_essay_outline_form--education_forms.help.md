# Argumentative Essay Outline Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Argumentative Essay Outline Form is a tool for students to plan and organize their argumentative essays. It guides them through the key elements of a well-structured essay, helping them to develop a clear and arguable thesis statement and supporting their arguments with evidence and reasoning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your Student Name**: Type your name as it appears on your school records.
2. **Enter Class Name or Course Number**: Type the name of your class or course number to help the instructor identify your class assignment.
3. **Select Submission Date**: Choose the date by which you need to submit your essay.
4. **Enter Essay Topic or Prompt**: Type the main topic or prompt of your essay.
5. **Develop a Thesis Statement**: Write a brief statement (1-2 sentences) that presents your main argument.
6. **Assess Thesis Clarity**: Select "Yes, very clear" if your thesis is strong and arguable, "Somewhat clear" if it needs improvement, or "Needs improvement" if it's not clear.
7. **First Main Argument**: Identify the topic or claim of your first main argument.
8. **Evidence for Argument 1**: List sources or examples you will use to support your first main argument.
9. **Second Main Argument**: Identify the topic or claim of your second main argument.
10. **Evidence for Argument 2**: List sources or examples you will use to support your second main argument.
11. **Optional Third Main Argument**: If you have more than two main arguments, enter the topic or claim of your third main argument.
12. **Optional Evidence for Argument 3**: If you have more than two main arguments, list sources or examples you will use to support your third main argument.
13. **Opposing or Counter Argument**: Identify an opposing viewpoint or counterargument to your main argument.
14. **Refute Counterargument**: Explain how you will respond to the counterargument.
15. **Conclusion Approach**: Describe your approach to summarizing and reinforcing your thesis statement.
16. **Number of Sources Identified**: Count and enter the total number of sources you have identified so far.
17. **Research Status**: Select one of the options to indicate the status of your research: "Yes, fully complete", "Mostly complete", "About half done", or "Just starting".
18. **Optional: Outline Strengths**: If you'd like to, list the strengths of your outline.
19. **Optional: Areas of Concern**: If you'd like to, list areas that need improvement in your outline.
20. **Instructor Feedback**: This field is for instructor use only, and you can leave it blank.
