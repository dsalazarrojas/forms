# Spelling Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Spelling Quiz form is designed to help students practice their spelling skills by providing a list of words for different grade levels. This form is intended to help students improve their spelling accuracy and speed.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select your grade level from the options provided in the "Word List" field.
2. Choose the words that you can spell correctly from the provided options in the "Quiz Words" field.
3. Enter your name in the "Students Name" field.
4. Enter your score in the "Score" field.
5. Click the "Submit" button to submit your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Students Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your name here. This field is required.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, text, required): Select your grade level from the list provided. This field is required.
* **Word List** (`word_list`, select_multiple, required): Select the grade level that corresponds to your current grade. This field is required.
* **Quiz Words** (`quiz_words`, text, optional): Choose the words you can spell correctly from the provided options. This field is optional.
* **Score** (`score`, number, required): Enter your score after completing the quiz. This field is required.
* **Word** (`word`, select_one, optional): Select the correct word from the provided options. This field is optional.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click this button to submit your answers. This field is required.

## Tips

* Make sure to select your grade level correctly, as it will determine the difficulty level of the words you will be asked to spell.
* Choose words that you are confident you can spell correctly.
* Enter your score accurately, as it will affect how your performance is evaluated.
