# Faculty Live Demo Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from faculty members on live demo events, helping organizers evaluate the presenter's performance and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide valuable feedback, follow these steps:

1. Review the questions carefully and answer each one accordingly.
2. Rate the presenter's performance using the provided scales (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
3. Provide comments or notes where applicable.
4. Submit your feedback by selecting the "Submit" option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How would you rate this live demo**: Rate your overall impression of the live demo on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Presenter effectiveness**: Rate how well the presenter communicated the ideas and concepts.
* **Comments about the live demo**: Add any specific comments or suggestions about the live demo.
* **Presenter's knowledge**: Rate how well the presenter demonstrated their knowledge on the topic.
* **Presenter's materials**: Rate the quality and usefulness of the presentation materials provided.
* **Presenter care and concern for the audience**: Rate how well the presenter interacted with the audience and addressed their questions.
* **Overall rating**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the live demo.
* **Presenter participation**: Rate how engaging the presenter was during the live demo.
* **Presenter suggestions for improvement**: Add any specific suggestions for the presenter to improve.
* **Event planning and organization**: Rate how well the event was planned and organized.
* **Presenter availability**: Rate the presenter's availability to answer questions and interact with the audience.
* **Presenter time management**: Rate how well the presenter managed their time during the live demo.
* **Presenter clarification**: Rate how well the presenter clarified any doubts or questions.
* **Presenter Q and A**: Rate how well the presenter responded to questions from the audience.
* **Presenter presentation**: Rate the overall quality of the presentation.
* **Presenter presentation materials**: Rate the quality and usefulness of the presentation materials provided.
* **Presenter Q and A**: Rate how well the presenter responded to questions from the audience.
* **Presenter handouts**: Rate the quality and usefulness of any handouts provided.
* **Presenter presentation skills**: Rate the overall quality of the presenter's presentation skills.
* **Faculty feedback**: Add any specific comments or suggestions about the presenter's feedback.
* **Other notes**: Add any other notes or comments about the live demo.
* **Faculty suggestions**: Add any specific suggestions for improvement.
* **Overall satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the live demo.
* **Submit**: Select this option to submit your feedback.
