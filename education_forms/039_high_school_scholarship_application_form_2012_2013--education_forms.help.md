# High School Scholarship Application Form 2012 2013 - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a application for students to provide personal, school, academic, and contact details for the High School Scholarship 2012 2013.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read the purpose and instructions carefully.
* Fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal details** (`personal_details`, text, optional): This field is for providing your personal details such as name, address, and any other relevant information you would like to share.
* **School details** (`school_details`, text, required): This field is for providing your school's name, address, and any other relevant details.
* **Academic details** (`academic_details`, text, required): This field is for listing your academic achievements, grades, or any other relevant academic information.
* **Contact details** (`contact_details`, text, required): This field is for providing your parent's/guardian's contact information.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): This is a final submit button to complete the application.
