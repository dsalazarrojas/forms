# Student Progress Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Student Progress Evaluation form is designed to help teachers assess and document student progress in various subjects. This form is used to track student grades, comments, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Student ID**: Enter the student's ID number to identify the student being evaluated.
2. **Name**: Enter the student's name as it appears on their records.
3. **Grade Level**: Select the student's current grade level from the dropdown menu.
4. **Subject**: Enter the subject being evaluated.
5. **Grade**: Enter the student's current grade in the subject.
6. **Comments**: Enter any comments or observations about the student's progress.
7. **Teacher Name**: Enter your name as the teacher evaluating the student.
8. **Report Type**: Select the type of report being submitted (e.g., Report Card, Progress Report).
9. **Student Progress**: Select the student's overall progress from the dropdown menu.
10. **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or observations about the student.
11. **Student Signature**: If necessary, sign and date the form.
12. **Teacher Signature**: Sign and date the form as the teacher evaluating the student.
13. **Report Date**: Enter the date the report is being submitted.
14. **School Name**: Enter the name of the school.
15. **School Location**: Enter the location of the school.
16. **School Phone**: Enter the school phone number.
17. **School Email**: Enter the school email address.
18. **School Address**: Enter the school address.
19. **Teacher Comments**: Enter any additional comments from the teacher.
20. **Submit**: Click the submit button to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student ID**: A unique ID number assigned to the student.
* **Name**: The student's full name.
* **Grade Level**: The student's current grade level (e.g., First, Second, Third, Fourth, Fifth).
* **Subject**: The subject being evaluated (e.g., Math, English, Science).
* **Grade**: The student's current grade in the subject.
* **Comments**: Any comments or observations about the student's progress.
* **Teacher Name**: The name of the teacher evaluating the student.
* **Report Type**: The type of report being submitted (e.g., Report Card, Progress Report).
* **Student Progress**: The student's overall progress (e.g., Very Good, Needs Improvement, Unsatisfactory).
* **Additional Comments**: Any additional comments or observations about the student.
* **Student Signature**: An optional field for the student to sign and date the form.
* **Teacher Signature**: The teacher's signature and date.
* **Report Date**: The date the report is being submitted.
* **School Name**: The name of the school.
* **School Location**: The location of the school.
* **School Phone**: The school phone number.
* **School Email**: The school email address.
* **School Address**: The school address.
* **Teacher Comments**: Any additional comments from the teacher.
* **Submit**: Click to complete and submit the form.

Note: Fields marked as "required" are mandatory and must be filled out. Fields with "optional" status can be left blank if not applicable.
