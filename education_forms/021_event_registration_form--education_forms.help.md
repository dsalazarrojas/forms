# event_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The event registration form is designed to gather relevant details about an event, including the event name, date, time, and registration date. It also requires participant information such as name, email, and phone number. The form is completed to register participants for events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name in the "Name" field. This is a required field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field. This is a required field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field. This is a required field.
4. Type in the "Event Name" field with the name of the event you are registering for.
5. Choose the date of the event from the calendar in the "Event Date" field. This is a required field.
6. Select the time of the event from the time picker in the "Event Time" field. This is a required field.
7. Enter the date you are registering for in the "Registration Date" field. This is a required field.
8. If you have any notes or comments, you can type them in the "Notes" field. This is not a required field.
9. If you have payment information, you can type it in the "Payment Information" field. This is not a required field.
10. Confirm that you are registering for the event by selecting "Yes" in the "Confirm" field. This is a required field.
11. Agree to the terms and conditions of the event by selecting "Yes" in the "Agree" field. This is a required field.
12. Select "Yes" in the "Terms" field to confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions of the event. This is a required field.
13. Select "Yes" in the "Privacy" field to confirm that you have read and agree to the privacy policy of the event. This is not a required field.
14. Click "Submit" to complete and submit the form.
15. You can also click "Cancel" to cancel the form and return to the previous page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (1)** (`text`, required): Your name as it should appear on the event registration.
* **Email (2)** (`email`, required): Your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone (3)** (`text`, required): Your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Event Name (4)** (`text`, required): The name of the event you are registering for.
* **Event Date (5)** (`date`, required): The date of the event in the format of a calendar.
* **Event Time (6)** (`time`, required): The time of the event in the format of a time picker.
* **Registration Date (7)** (`date`, required): The date you are registering for the event.
* **Notes (8)** (`note`, optional): Any additional notes or comments about your registration.
* **Payment Information (9)** (`text`, optional): Any payment or financial information about your registration.
* **Confirm (10)** (`select_one`, required): Confirm that you are registering for the event.
* **Agree (11)** (`select_one`, required): Agree to the terms and conditions of the event.
* **Terms (12)** (`select_one`, required): Agree to the terms and conditions of the event.
* **Privacy (13)** (`select_one`, optional): Agree to the privacy policy of the event.
* **Submit (14)** (`text`, optional): Complete and submit the form.
* **Cancel (15)** (`text`, optional): Cancel the form and return to the previous page.
* **Back (16)** (`text`, optional): Return to the previous page.
* **Next (17)** (`text`, optional): Move on to the next page of the form.
* **Save and Submit (18)** (`text`, optional): Save the form and submit it.
