This help guide is intended to provide clear instructions for end-users on how to complete the Lab Experiment Consent Form. This form is designed for participants to provide their information, informed consent, and sign off on the experiment. It's crucial to ensure that users understand each field's purpose and requirements.

To prevent duplication and maintain consistency, please review this guide carefully before filling out the form.

**Purpose**
-----------

The Lab Experiment Consent Form is a document that collects necessary information from participants before they engage in a lab experiment. It's essential to provide accurate and honest answers to help researchers understand the experiment's requirements and ensure a smooth experience for all parties involved.

**How To Complete This Form**
---------------------------

1. Fill out the **Participant Information** section with your personal details, such as your name, age, and any other relevant information.
2. In the **Experiment Details** section, provide a brief description of the experiment, including its purpose, duration, and any other relevant information.
3. Select your **Informed Consent** by choosing one of the three options: "I am 18 years or older," "I am 17 years or younger," or "Decline to answer."
4. Sign your name in the **Participant Signature** field to confirm your understanding and agreement with the experiment's terms.
5. Sign your name in the **Researcher Signature** field to confirm that the researcher has explained the experiment's details and terms to you.
6. Enter the **Date Completed** to mark the date and time you completed the form.
7. In the **Date Informed**, select the date you received informed consent from the researcher.
8. Enter the **Expiration Date** (if applicable) to mark the date the informed consent expires.

**Field-by-Field Explanation**
---------------------------

### Participant Information

* **Participant Information** (required: **no**): Enter your personal details, such as your name, age, and any other relevant information.

### Experiment Details

* **Experiment Details** (required: **no**): Briefly describe the experiment, including its purpose, duration, and any other relevant information.

### Informed Consent

* **Informed Consent** (required: **no**): Select one of the three options:
	+ I am 18 years or older
	+ I am 17 years or younger
	+ Decline to answer

### Participant Signature

* **Participant Signature** (required: **no**): Sign your name to confirm your understanding and agreement with the experiment's terms.

### Researcher Signature

* **Researcher Signature** (required: **no**): Sign your name to confirm that the researcher has explained the experiment's details and terms to you.

### Date Completed

* **Date Completed** (required: **no**): Enter the date and time you completed the form.

### Date Informed

* **Date Informed** (required: **no**): Select the date you received informed consent from the researcher.

### Expiration Date

* **Expiration Date** (required: **no**): Enter the date the informed consent expires.

**Tips**

* If you have any questions or concerns about the experiment, don't hesitate to ask the researcher.
* Make sure to read and understand each field carefully before proceeding.
* If you decline to answer any question, please provide a brief explanation.
