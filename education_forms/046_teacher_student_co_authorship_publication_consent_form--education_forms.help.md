# Teacher Student Co Authorship Publication Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teacher Student Co Authorship Publication Consent Form is used to collect consent from teachers and students for their work being published in a publication. This form ensures that both teachers and students have given their consent before their work is published, maintaining the academic integrity and transparency of the publication.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Teacher Name**, if applicable.
2. Select the **Student Names** of all students who have contributed to the publication.
3. Choose the **Co-author Status** of each student (Teacher, Student, or Both).
4. If a student is not listed as a Teacher, select **Co-author Status Other** to provide more details.
5. Select the **School College Research Program** type if applicable.
6. Enter the **Consent Date** of the publication.
7. Choose the **Publication Type** of the published work.
8. Mark whether the **Teacher Consent** and **Student Consent** is given.
9. Add any additional **Notes** about the consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher Name**: Enter the name of the teacher who has contributed to the publication.
* **Student Names**: Select all the students who have contributed to the publication. This could be multiple students.
* **Co-author Status**: Select whether the student is a Teacher, Student, or Both.
* **Co-author Status Other**: This field is for providing more details about the co-author status if it is not listed as Teacher, Student, or Both. This could be used to describe a non-traditional co-authorship arrangement.
* **School College Research Program**: Select the type of program if it is applicable (School, College, or Research Program).
* **Consent Date**: The date when both teacher and student consent was given.
* **Publication Type**: Select the type of publication (Book, Paper, or Research Report).
* **Teacher Consent**: Mark whether the teacher has given their consent.
* **Student Consent**: Mark whether the student has given their consent.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or details about the consent given.

## Tips

* Ensure that you fill out the form accurately and completely before submitting.
* Review each field carefully before proceeding.
* Ask for help if you are unsure about filling out any part of the form.
