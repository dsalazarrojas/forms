# Teacher Work Expectation Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from teachers on their work expectations and responsibilities. It is meant to be completed by teachers themselves, and the information gathered will help school administrators understand their roles and tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
1. The form is divided into sections, each with a specific purpose. Please read each section carefully to ensure you understand what is being asked.
2. You will need to complete all the fields on every page. If a field is not required, you can still fill it out if you feel it's relevant to your role.
3. The form can be completed on multiple pages, with each page having a specific set of questions.
4. Once you have filled out all the fields, review your responses carefully to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher Expectations**: This field is used to describe your work expectations as a teacher. Please provide a clear and concise summary of your daily tasks and responsibilities.
* **School Administrators**: List the names and email addresses of all school administrators you work with. This may include department heads, principals, or other teachers.
* **Educational Institution**: Please provide the name of your educational institution where you are employed.
* **Clarity Alignment**: Select all the options that best describe your work expectations. This will help administrators understand how you like to communicate and work.
* **Teacher Responsibilities**: This field is used to outline your specific responsibilities as a teacher. Please provide a clear and concise summary of your tasks and duties.
* **School Administrators 1, 2, and 3**: These fields are used to list the email addresses of specific school administrators you work with. Please ensure you enter the correct email addresses for each administrator.
* **Tips**: If you're unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor or HR representative for clarification. If you have any additional comments or questions, please add them to the "Teacher Comments" section (not included in this form).
