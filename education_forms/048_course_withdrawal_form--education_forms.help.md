# Course Withdrawal Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Course Withdrawal Form is used to document a student's request to withdraw from a course. This form helps in formally recording the student's decision to leave the course, which is useful for various administrative and academic purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, ensure you have access to the system and are authorized to fill out this form.
2. Fill out each field as required. Note that not all fields are mandatory.
3. Review and submit the form once all fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Id** (`1`, `number`, not required): Enter the ID of the course you are requesting to withdraw from.
* **Student Name** (`2`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the student requesting to withdraw from the course.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, not required): Enter the student's email address for correspondence.
* **Reason For Withdrawal** (`4`, `text`, not required): Enter a brief explanation for the withdrawal request.
* **Course Name** (`5`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the course the student is withdrawing from.
* **Instructor Name** (`6`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the instructor associated with the course.
* **Student Id** (`7`, `number`, not required): Enter the ID of the student requesting to withdraw from the course.
* **Date Of Withdrawal** (`8`, `date`, not required): Enter the date when the student officially withdraws from the course.
* **Time Of Withdrawal** (`9`, `time`, not required): Enter the time when the student officially withdraws from the course.
* **Comments** (`10`, `note`, not required): Add any additional comments or notes regarding the withdrawal.
* **Form Submission** (`11`, `text`, not required): Enter any additional information or notes regarding the form submission.
