# Decomposing Whole Numbers Addition - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assist users in breaking down whole numbers into their base 10 parts for addition exercises. The form will guide you through the process, ensuring accuracy and efficiency in your calculations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each field with the required information.
2. Select your numbers for "Number 1" and "Number 2" fields. You can enter any whole numbers you'd like to work with.
3. Enter the sum of the numbers you selected in the "Sum" field.
4. Type the broken-down parts of the numbers in the "answer" field, using the base 10 notation (e.g., 10 for 10, 5 for 5).
5. Optionally, fill out the "Part 1" field with the first part of the broken-down number for further calculation or reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Number 1** (`number1`, `number`, required): Enter a whole number you'd like to break down. For example, if you select 14, you're breaking down 10 + 4.
* **Number 2** (`number2`, `number`, required): Enter another whole number you'd like to break down. For example, if you select 5, you're breaking down 0 + 5.
* **Sum** (`sum`, `number`, required): Enter the sum of the two numbers you selected. This should be the same as the sum of their base 10 parts.
* **answer** (`answer`, `text`, required): Type the base 10 parts of the numbers, separated by + or - signs. For example, 10+4 for 14 and 0+5 for 5.
* **Part 1** (`number1_part1`, `number`, optional): Optional field for further calculation or reference. Enter the first part of the broken-down number.

## Tips

* Be sure to fill out both numbers you'd like to add, as both are required fields.
* When entering the sum, double-check it against your calculations to ensure accuracy.
* For more complex numbers, use the base 10 notation for accurate representation.
* This form is designed for educational purposes, so feel free to experiment with various numbers to understand the concept better!
