# Scholarship Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scholarship Request Form is a document used to collect relevant information from applicants for scholarships. This form helps us understand your background, goals, and requirements to evaluate your eligibility for a scholarship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow the steps below:

1.  Fill out the form accurately and completely.
2.  Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3.  Attach any required attachments (Resume and Transcript).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
*   First Name: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., passport, ID, or driver's license).

### Email Address
*   Email Address: Enter your active and valid email address that you check regularly.

### Phone Number
*   Phone Number: Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.

### Street Address
*   Street Address: Enter your current or permanent street address where you can be reached.

### City
*   City: Enter the city where you live or attend school.

### State
*   State: Select your state of residence from the list provided.

### Zip Code
*   Zip Code: Enter your zip code as it appears on your address.

### Career Goals
*   Career Goals: Describe your short-term and long-term career goals.

### Resume Attachment
*   Resume Attachment: Attach your resume or upload a document that lists your experience and qualifications.

### Transcript Attachment
*   Transcript Attachment: Attach a scan or photo of your transcript or upload a document that lists your academic records.
