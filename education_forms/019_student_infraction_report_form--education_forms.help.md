## Student Infraction Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Infraction Report Form is used to report any incidents or infractions that occur within the educational institution. This form is intended to be completed by students to document and report any incidents, providing valuable information for administrative actions and follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your student's name in the "student_name" field.
2.  Enter your student's email address in the "student_email" field.
3.  Confirm your preferred contact method in the "student_contact_method" field. Select "Yes" if you're available for contact, and "No" if not.
4.  Describe the infraction details in the "infraction_details" field.
5.  Specify the actions taken to address the infraction in the "actions_taken" field.
6.  Add any additional comments or remarks from administration in the "comments_administration" field.
7.  Enter the date of the infraction in the "date_infraction" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name (student_name)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your name to identify yourself as the student reporting the infraction.
* **Student Email (student_email)** (`email`, required/optional): Enter your email address for administrative follow-up and communication.
* **Contact Method (student_contact_method)** (`select_one`, required/optional): Confirm your availability for contact. Select "Yes" if you're available, and "No" if not.
* **Infraction Details (infraction_details)** (`text`, required/optional): Describe the incident or infraction that occurred.
* **Actions Taken (actions_taken)** (`text`, required/optional): Specify the actions you've taken or plan to take to address the infraction.
* **Comments (comments_administration)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter any comments or remarks from administrative staff.
* **Date of Infraction (date_infraction)** (`date`, required/optional): Enter the date when the infraction occurred.

## Tips

- If you're unsure about any information, please ask for assistance from administrative staff.
- Ensure that the form is completed accurately and thoroughly to provide a clear understanding of the incident.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact administrative staff for support.
