# Curriculum Review Committee Observation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Curriculum Review Committee Observation Consent Form is a document used to gather information from teachers regarding their consent to observe a student in a classroom setting. This form is used to ensure that all parties involved in the observation process are aware of and agree to the terms and conditions of the observation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User Name.
2. Enter your Teacher Name (if applicable).
3. Provide the Observation Date.
4. Choose the Start Date.
5. Choose the End Date.
6. Select the Observation Time.
7. Enter the School Name.
8. Enter the Teacher ID Number.
9. Select your relationship with the teacher (Student, Teacher, or Staff).
10. Enter the Observation Location.
11. Enter the Observer Name.
12. Sign and date the Observer Signature.
13. Select your relationship with the student (Student, Teacher, or Staff).
14. Enter the Observation Duration.
15. Choose your Contact Method (Phone, Email, or In Person).
16. Choose the School Contact Method (Phone, Email, or In Person).
17. Enter the Contact Person.
18. Enter the Contact Phone Number.
19. Enter the Contact E-mail Address.
20. Enter the Observer E-mail.
21. Enter the Observer Phone.
22. Enter the School E-mail Address (if applicable).
23. Enter the School Phone Number (if applicable).
24. Enter any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required): Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, optional): Enter your teacher's name (if you have one).
* **Observation Date** (`observation_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the observation.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the observation.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the observation.
* **Observation Time** (`observation_time`, time, required): Select the time of the observation.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the school.
* **Teacher ID Number** (`teacher_id_number`, number, required): Enter your teacher ID number.
* **Teacher Relationship** (`teacher_relationship`, select_one, required): Select your relationship with the teacher (Student, Teacher, or Staff).
* **Observation Location** (`observation_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the observation.
* **Observer Name** (`observer_name`, text, required): Enter the observer's name.
* **Observer Signature** (`observer_signature`, note, required): Sign and date the observer signature.
* **Student Teacher Relationship** (`student_teacher_relationship`, select_one, required): Select your relationship with the student (Student, Teacher, or Staff).
* **Observation Duration** (`observation_duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of the observation.
* **Contact Method** (`teacher_contact_method`, select_multiple, required): Choose your preferred contact method (Phone, Email, or In Person).
* **School Contact Method** (`school_contact_method`, select_multiple, required): Choose the school's preferred contact method (Phone, Email, or In Person).
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone_number`, text, required): Enter the contact phone number.
* **Contact E-mail Address** (`contact_email_address`, email, required): Enter the contact email address.
* **Observer E-mail** (`observer_email`, email, required): Enter the observer's email address.
* **Observer Phone** (`observer_phone`, text, required): Enter the observer's phone number.
* **School E-mail Address** (`school_email`, email, optional): Enter the school's email address (if applicable).
* **School Phone Number** (`school_phone`, text, optional): Enter the school's phone number (if applicable).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments you may have.

Remember to review the form carefully before submitting it. Make sure to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth observation process.
