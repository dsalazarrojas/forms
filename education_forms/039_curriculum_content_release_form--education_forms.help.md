# Curriculum Content Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Curriculum Content Release Form is used to declare the intention to share curriculum content, specifying details about the content, its distribution method, and the permissions granted.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your User Name to identify yourself.
2. Provide your Email Address for contact purposes.
3. Specify the Institution and Location of the content origin.
4. Choose your Role in the institution.
5. Select the Document Type of the content you're sharing.
6. Name the File (if applicable).
7. Specify the Purpose and Distribution Method of the shared content.
8. Provide your Contact Information for further inquiries.
9. Sign and date the form as proof of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as an institutional user.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your institutional email address for contact purposes.
* **Institution** (`institution_abbreviation`, text, optional): Type the abbreviated name of your institution.
* **Location** (`institution_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of your institution.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, optional): Choose your role within the institution (e.g., Teacher, Admin, etc.).
* **Permission Date** (`permission_date`, date, optional): Date when permission was granted (if applicable).
* **Document Type** (`document_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of document being shared (e.g., textbook, research paper, etc.).
* **File Name** (`file_name`, text, optional): Name of the file being shared (if applicable).
* **File Version** (`file_version`, text, optional): Version number of the file (if applicable).
* **Purpose** (`purpose`, text, optional): Briefly describe the purpose of sharing the content.
* **Distribution Method** (`distribution_method`, text, optional): Describe how the content will be distributed (e.g., online, in-person, etc.).
* **Distribution Medium** (`distribution_medium`, text, optional): Specify the medium used for distribution (e.g., email, print, etc.).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Leave any additional comments or notes.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Provide a contact name for further inquiries.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email for further inquiries.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number for further inquiries.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, optional): Enter your contact address for further inquiries.
* **Permission** (`permission`, note, optional): This field is not used in the current implementation.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): This field is not used in the current implementation.
* **Signature Name** (`signature_name`, text, optional): This field is not used in the current implementation.
* **Signature Title** (`signature_title`, text, optional): This field is not used in the current implementation.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, optional): Date of submission (if applicable).
* **Attach File** (`file`, text, optional): Upload the file being shared (if applicable).
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click to submit the form.
