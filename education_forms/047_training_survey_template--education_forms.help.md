# Training Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Training Survey Template is a form designed to collect feedback and ratings from employees on their training experiences. It is used to evaluate the effectiveness of training programs and identify areas for improvement. This form is intended for employees to rate their overall satisfaction with the training program and provide comments and feedback on various aspects of the training.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the ratings and provide comments for each section as requested.
2. For the "overall_satisfaction" field, choose a rating from 1 to 5, with 1 being very dissatisfied and 5 being very satisfied.
3. For the "interaction_with_instructor" field, select all that apply from 1 to 5, where 1 is very poor and 5 is very good.
4. For the "feedback" field, provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
5. For the "overall_satisfaction" field, provide any additional comments or feedback on the overall satisfaction of the training program.
6. Finally, click the submit button to complete the form.
