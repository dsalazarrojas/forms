# graduate_research_presentation_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a graduate research presentation. It collects information about the presentation, presenter, and supporting documents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the title of your presentation.
2. Select the research area that best describes your presentation.
3. Provide a brief description of your presentation.
4. Enter the date and time of your presentation.
5. Enter your name and contact information (email and phone number).
6. If applicable, enter your title and affiliation.
7. If applicable, list your co-authors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **title (Required)**: Enter the title of your presentation. This will be the title of your presentation that appears on the schedule and any promotional materials.
* **research_area (Required)**: Choose the research area that best describes your presentation from the list of available options. This will help categorize your presentation and make it easier for the audience to find.
* **description (Optional)**: Provide a brief description of your presentation. This can help the audience understand what your presentation is about and make it easier for them to decide whether to attend.
* **research_presentation_date (Required)**: Enter the date of your presentation.
* **research_presentation_time (Required)**: Enter the time of your presentation.
* **presenter_name (Required)**: Enter your name as it appears on the presentation materials.
* **institution_name (Required)**: Enter the name of your institution where you are affiliated with.
* **email (Required)**: Enter your email address for contact and communication purposes.
* **phone_number (Optional)**: Enter your phone number for contact and communication purposes.
* **presenter_title (Optional)**: Enter your title or position for presentation and promotional materials.
* **presenter_affiliation (Optional)**: Enter your affiliation for presentation and promotional materials.
* **presenter_department (Optional)**: Enter your department for presentation and promotional materials.
* **coauthors (Optional)**: Select any co-authors who are presenting with you.
* **file (Optional)**: Attach any supporting files or documents related to your presentation.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the conference organizers or your department for assistance.
* Ensure that your presentation materials are in the required format and ready for upload or print.
