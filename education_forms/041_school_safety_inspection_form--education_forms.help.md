# School Safety Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information on school safety measures and procedures, enabling administrators to assess and evaluate the safety protocols in place.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each section carefully before providing responses.
2. Answer each question based on your actual knowledge of the school's safety measures and procedures.
3. Fill out all required fields, which are indicated by the "Required" label.
4. Use the available options for select fields.
5. Use the "Notes" field for any additional comments or observations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Safety Inspection Form** (`school_safety_inspection_form`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the form purpose and scope.
* **General Safety Measures** (`general_safety_measures`, text, optional): Describe the general safety measures in place at the school.
* **Emergency Plan** (`emergency_plan`, select_one, optional): Select the emergency plan that best describes your school's response to emergencies.
* **Fire Safety Equipment** (`fire_safety_equipment`, select_multiple, optional): Select all fire safety equipment available at the school.
* **Exit Routes** (`exit_routes`, number, optional): Enter the number of exit routes from each building/room at the school.
* **Fire Drills** (`fire_drills`, select_one, optional): Select the type of fire drills conducted at the school.
* **Fire Safety Equipment List** (`fire_safety_equipment_list`, text, optional): List any additional fire safety equipment not included in the previous field.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_info`, text, optional): Enter the primary contact person and their phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Info 2** (`emergency_contact_info_2`, email, optional): Enter a secondary contact person's email address.
* **Emergency Contact Info 3** (`emergency_contact_info_3`, date, optional): Enter a date for the last emergency contact update.
* **Emergency Contact Info 4** (`emergency_contact_info_4`, time, optional): Enter a time for the last emergency contact update.
* **Safety Measures Adopted** (`safety_measures_adopted`, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional safety measures adopted at the school.
* **Safety Measures Implemented** (`safety_measures_implemented`, text, optional): Describe any safety measures implemented at the school.
* **Safety Measures Effective** (`safety_measures_effective`, select_one, optional): Select the effectiveness of the safety measures in place.
* **Safety Measures Suggestions** (`safety_measures_suggestions`, text, optional): Suggest any additional safety measures not currently in place.
* **Fire Safety Equipment 1** (`fire_safety_equipment_1`, number, optional): Enter the number of fire safety equipment available at the school.
* **Fire Safety Equipment 2** (`fire_safety_equipment_2`, number, optional): Enter the number of fire safety equipment available at the school.
* **Fire Safety Equipment 3** (`fire_safety_equipment_3`, number, optional): Enter the number of fire safety equipment available at the school.
* **Exit Routes 1** (`exit_routes_1`, select_one, optional): Select the type of exit route available from each building/room at the school.
* **Exit Routes 2** (`exit_routes_2`, select_one, optional): Select the type of exit route available from each building/room at the school.
* **Exit Routes 3** (`exit_routes_3`, select_one, optional): Select the type of exit route available from each building/room at the school.
* **Exit Routes 4** (`exit_routes_4`, select_one, optional): Select the type of exit route available from each building/room at the school.
* **Exit Routes 5** (`exit_routes_5`, select_one, optional): Select the type of exit route available from each building/room at the school.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or observations related to the safety measures and procedures.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter your name and title.

Note: The form fields are self-explanatory, with clear labels and options to guide your responses. If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the specific field label or options available.
