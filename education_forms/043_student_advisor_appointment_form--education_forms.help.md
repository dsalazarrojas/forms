# Student Advisor Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Advisor Appointment Form is designed to help students schedule appointments with their advisors for meetings, discussions, or other advising events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Student Schedule Meeting" to indicate whether you would like to schedule a meeting with your advisor.
2. If you've selected "Yes", choose the preferred meeting time from the available options:
	* 9.0
	* 10.0
	* 11.0
	* 12.0
	* 13.0
3. Choose the meeting date for your scheduled meeting. Note that this field is not required, so you can leave it blank if you don't have a specific date in mind.
4. If you want to specify a preferred meeting location, choose "Yes" or "No" from the options. However, please note that this field is not required, so you can leave it blank if you don't have a specific location preference.
5. If you have a preferred phone number, please enter it in the "Student Phone Number" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "Student Email" field, which will be used for communication with your advisor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Schedule Meeting** (`student_schedule_meeting`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select "Yes" to schedule a meeting with your advisor, or "No" if you don't have a meeting scheduled.
* **Advisor Name** (`advisor_name`, text, required/optional): This field is not required, but if you do have a specific advisor in mind, you can enter their name here.
* **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the time of day for your meeting from the available options: 9.0, 10.0, 11.0, 12.0, 13.0.
* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, date, required/optional): Select the date for your meeting. If you don't have a specific date, you can leave this field blank.
* **Meeting Location** (`preferred_meeting_location`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose "Yes" or "No" if you have a preferred meeting location.
* **Student Phone Number** (`student_phone_number`, text, required/optional): If you have a phone number, enter it here for communication purposes.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for your advisor to communicate with you.
