# Faculty Promotion Peer Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect peer assessments and ratings for faculty promotion within departments. It helps evaluate a faculty member's performance and determine if they should be promoted. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the department from the options provided.
2. Choose the peer you are assessing from the list of faculty members.
3. Provide a rating for the peer you are assessing (optional).
4. Enter any additional comments or suggestions about the peer's performance.
5. Make a promotion recommendation for the peer (optional).
6. Provide any additional comments about the promotion recommendation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Department** (1): Select the department of the faculty member being assessed.
* **Peer Name** (2): Choose the name of the peer you are assessing. Note that this field is optional.
* **Peer Rank** (3): If applicable, choose the rank of the peer you are assessing (e.g., Senior, Associate, Adjunct). This field is optional.
* **Peer Suggestions** (4): Provide any additional comments or suggestions about the peer's performance.
* **Peer Comments** (5): Enter any additional comments about the peer's performance.
* **Faculty Name** (6): Select the name of the faculty member being assessed.
* **Faculty Title** (7): Choose the title of the faculty member being assessed (e.g., Associate Professor, Assistant Professor). This field is optional.
* **Peer Rating** (8): Provide a rating for the peer's performance. This field is optional and has three options: Exceeds Expectations, Meets Expectations, Below Expectations.
* **Peer Comments 2** (9): Enter any additional comments about the peer's performance. This field is optional.
* **Faculty Promotion Recommendation** (10): Make a promotion recommendation for the peer. This field is optional and has three options: Promote, Not Promote, Maybe.
* **Faculty Promotion Comment** (11): Enter any additional comments about the promotion recommendation. This field is optional.

## Tips
- Make sure to complete the form accurately and honestly.
- Provide specific examples or reasons for your ratings and comments.
- Use the optional fields only if you have relevant information to provide.
- Review the form carefully before submitting your assessment.
