# Weekly College Bound Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Weekly College Bound Check In Form is a tool for mentors, advisors, and students to stay organized and on track with college-bound activities. It is meant to be used for regular check-ins to monitor progress and provide support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please fill out the required fields as accurately as possible.
* Enter the student's name and check-in date in the corresponding fields.
* Provide any relevant comments or concerns in the "Mentor Comments" and "Student Comments" fields.
* Choose the check-in status, check-in frequency, and select any other options as applicable.
* Enter any additional notes as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's name as it appears in your records.
* **Check In Date** (`check_in_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the current check-in.
* **Mentor Comments** (`mentor_comments`, text, optional): Provide any comments or concerns you have regarding the student's progress.
* **Mentor Name** (`mentor_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the mentor or advisor.
* **Check In Status** (`check_in_status`, select_one, required): Select the current check-in status, either "Not Started" or "Completed".
* **Student Comments** (`student_comments`, text, optional): Allow the student to provide any comments or concerns regarding the check-in process.
* **Advisor Name** (`advisor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the student's advisor.
* **Check In Frequency** (`check_in_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Select the frequency of check-ins, options include "Weekly", "Bi-Weekly", "Monthly".
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional notes as needed.
* **Check In Date and Time** (`check_in_date_time`, time, optional): Enter the date and time of the current check-in, if applicable.

## Tips
* Please make sure to keep the form up-to-date and complete for each check-in.
* Review the student's progress and provide feedback as needed.
* Use the "Mentor Comments" field to note any concerns or areas for improvement.
* Use the "Student Comments" field to allow the student to provide their perspective.
* Keep the "Notes" field for any additional information that does not fit into other fields.
* Regularly review the form to ensure the student is on track with their college-bound activities.
