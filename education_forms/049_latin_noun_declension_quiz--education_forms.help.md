# Latin Noun Declension Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This quiz is designed to test a user's knowledge of Latin noun declensions. It is meant to be a helpful and educational tool for those learning Latin.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the questions and answers carefully.
2. Enter your answers in the provided fields.
3. Make sure to enter the correct answers, as they will be checked for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Latin Noun Declension** (noun_form): This is the main field of the quiz where you will be presented with questions about Latin nouns.
- **Noun Form** (case1): Enter the correct form of the Latin noun.
- **Noun Form Plural** (case1_plural): Enter the correct plural form of the Latin noun.
- **Case2** (case2): Select the correct case for the Latin noun.
- **Case2 Plural** (case2_plural): Select the correct plural form of the case for the Latin noun.
- **Noun Ending** (ending): Enter the correct ending for the Latin noun.
- **Noun Ending Plural** (ending_plural): Enter the correct ending for the Latin plural form of the noun.
- **Noun Meaning** (meaning): Enter the correct meaning of the Latin noun.
- **Noun Meaning Plural** (meaning_plural): Enter the correct meaning of the Latin noun in the plural form.
- **Submit Quiz** (submit): Enter the date and time you would like to submit the quiz.
- **Submit Plural** (submit_plural): Enter your comment about the quiz.
- **Submit Date** (submit_date): Enter the date you would like to submit the quiz.
- **Submit Time** (submit_time): Enter the time you would like to submit the quiz.
