# impact_assessment_of_academic_events - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Impact Assessment of Academic Events" form is designed to collect feedback from various stakeholders to evaluate the effectiveness of academic events, such as workshops, seminars, and conferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Select the relevant page**: The form is divided into different sections, each representing a page. Please choose the page that best corresponds to your role or feedback category.
2. **Fill in each field**: Answer the questions and provide your feedback according to the explanations below.
3. **Submit your feedback**: Once you have completed all the pages and fields, click the submit button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Event Feedback (Page 1)
* **Event Feedback** (`event_feedback`, `text`, **optional**):
	+ This section is for any general comments or observations you have about the event itself. Please provide your honest feedback on the event's organization, content, and overall experience.
* 
### Attendee Feedback (Page 2)
* **Attendee Feedback** (`attendee_feedback`, `text`, **optional**):
	+ In this section, share your thoughts on what you learned or found valuable during the event. Your feedback can help improve the content and relevance of future events.
* 
### Stakeholder Feedback (Page 3)
* **Stakeholder Feedback** (`stakeholder_feedback`, `text`, **optional**):
	+ If you are an organizer or part of the event planning process, use this section to provide feedback on the event's overall impact on your role or organization. Your input is valuable to help refine event management strategies.
* 
### Event Evaluation (Page 4)
* **Event Evaluation** (`event_evaluation`, `note`, **optional**):
	+ This last section is for more in-depth thoughts or observations about the event. You can use free text to discuss anything from the event that stood out to you or areas for improvement.

## Tips
* Be honest and specific with your feedback to help create valuable insights.
* If you have any additional comments or requests, use the event evaluation section or include them in your submission.
* Your feedback is greatly appreciated and will help us improve future events.
