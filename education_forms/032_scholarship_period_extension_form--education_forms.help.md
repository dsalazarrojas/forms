# Scholarship Period Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request an extension to the scholarship period for students. It is used to provide additional information and supporting documents to support the extension request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student name and ID number.
2. Provide a clear reason for the extension request.
3. Upload supporting documents (if applicable).
4. Select the extension date you would like to request.
5. Fill in your contact information (email and phone number).
6. Select the submitted by options to indicate who submitted the form on your behalf.
7. Enter the submitted date and time.
8. If applicable, select additional submitted by options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, **Required**): Enter your name as it appears on your student ID.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, **Required**): Enter your student ID number.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, **Required**): Provide a clear explanation for the extension request.
* **Supporting Document** (`supporting_document`, text, **Optional**): Upload any supporting documents relevant to the extension request.
* **Email** (`email`, email, **Required**): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, **Optional**): Enter your phone number.
* **Extension Date** (`extension_date`, date, **Required**): Select the new date you would like to request.
* **Supporting Document 2** (`supporting_document_2`, text, **Optional**): Upload any additional supporting documents.
* **Supporting Document 3** (`supporting_document_3`, text, **Optional**): Upload any further supporting documents.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, **Required**): Select who submitted the form on your behalf.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, date, **Required**): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Submitted Time** (`submitted_time`, time, **Required**): Enter the time the form was submitted.
* **Submitted By 2** (`submitted_by_2`, select_multiple, **Optional**): Select who else submitted the form on your behalf.
* **Submitted Time 2** (`submitted_time_2`, time, **Optional**): Enter the time the form was submitted by others.
* **Reason 2** (`reason_2`, text, **Optional**): Provide additional explanation for the extension request.
* **Submitted Date 2** (`submitted_date_2`, date, **Optional**): Enter the date of submission by others.
* **Submitted Date 3** (`submitted_date_3`, date, **Optional**): Enter the date of submission by others.
* **Submitted By 3** (`submitted_by_3`, select_multiple, **Optional**): Select who else submitted the form on your behalf.
* **Submitted Time 2** (`submitted_time_2`, time, **Optional**): Enter the time of submission by others.
* **Submitted Date 4** (`submitted_date_4`, date, **Optional**): Enter the date of submission by others.
* **Submitted By 4** (`submitted_by_4`, select_one, **Optional**): Select who else submitted the form on your behalf.
* **Submitted Time 3** (`submitted_time_3`, time, **Optional**): Enter the time of submission by others.
* **Submitted Date 5** (`submitted_date_5`, date, **Optional**): Enter the date of submission by others.
* **Submitted By 6** (`submitted_by_6`, select_multiple, **Optional**): Select who else submitted the form on your behalf.
* **Submitted Date 6** (`submitted_date_6`, date, **Optional**): Enter the date of submission by others.
