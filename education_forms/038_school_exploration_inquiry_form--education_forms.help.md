# School Exploration Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for prospective families to express their interest in exploring a school for their child.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields, marked by an asterisk (*).
2. Choose your preferred contact method and schedule for a visit or call.
3. Provide any additional follow-up information if necessary.
4. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Prospective Family** (`prospective_family`, text, optional): Enter your name and your family's name to help us understand who is interested in exploring the school.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more contact methods (Phone, Email, Mail) that you would like to use to communicate with us.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred contact method (Mail, Phone, Email) for us to reach out to you.
* **Schedule Visit** (`schedule_visit`, date, optional): Select a date for the visit.
* **Schedule Visit Time** (`schedule_visit_time`, time, optional): Select a time for the visit.
* **Schedule Visit Date** (`schedule_visit_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the visit in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Schedule Call** (`schedule_call`, date, optional): Select a date for the call.
* **Schedule Call Time** (`schedule_call_time`, time, optional): Select a time for the call.
* **Schedule Call Date** (`schedule_call_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the call in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Schedule Call Type** (`schedule_call_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of call (Phone, Mail, In-person).
* **Follow-up Information** (`follow_up_info`, note, optional): Provide any additional follow-up information.
* **Follow-up Method** (`follow_up_method`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more follow-up methods (Phone, Mail).
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of follow-up in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Follow-up Time** (`follow_up_time`, time, optional): Select a time for follow-up.
* **Follow-up Method Type** (`follow_up_method_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the follow-up method (Phone, Mail).
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, note, optional): Enter any next steps or information.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the school you are interested in.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Mail** (`mail`, text, optional): Enter your mailing address.
* **Visit Type** (`visit_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of visit (In-person, Virtual).
* **Visit Date** (`visit_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the visit.
* **Visit Time** (`visit_time`, time, optional): Select a time for the visit.
* **Call Type** (`call_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of call (Virtual, In-person).
* **Next Steps 2** (`next_steps2`, note, optional): Enter any additional next steps or information.
