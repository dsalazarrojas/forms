# Language App Module Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Language App Module Inquiry Form is designed to gather information from users who have inquiries, comments, or suggestions about the language app modules. This form helps to collect contact information, language level, and specific module interests to ensure that our team can respond appropriately to each user's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the inquiry form with the required and optional information requested in the fields below.
2. Make sure to provide accurate contact information (email, phone, or school) for our team to follow up with you.
3. If you have any specific requests or concerns about the language app modules, please include them in the "Module Inquiry" field.
4. Choose your preferred language level and module(s) of interest from the respective dropdown menus.
5. Set a follow-up date and time if you would like us to get back to you at a specific date and time.
6. Add any additional notes or comments you would like to share with our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Inquiry Form
* **Inquiry Form** (text, not required): A field for you to describe your inquiry, comment, or suggestion about the language app modules.

### Contact Information
* **Name** (text, not required): Your name (e.g., your name, school name, or institution name).
* **Email** (email, not required): Your email address for our team to contact you back.
* **Phone** (text, not required): Your phone number for our team to call you back.
* **School Name** (text, not required): Your school name or institution name (if applicable).
* **School Location** (text, not required): Your school location or address (if applicable).
* **School Email** (email, not required): Your school email address (if applicable).
* **School Phone** (text, not required): Your school phone number (if applicable).
* **School Address** (text, not required): Your school address (if applicable).

### Language Level
* **Language Level** (select_one, not required): Choose your language level: Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.

### Module Inquiry
* **Module Inquiry** (text, not required): Describe any specific requests or concerns you have about the language app modules.

### Module Match
* **Module Match** (select_one, not required): Choose the module(s) that interests you: module1, module2, module3, module4, or module5.

### Follow Up
* **Follow Up** (date, not required): Set a follow-up date and time if you want our team to get back to you at a specific date and time.

### Time Inquiry
* **Time Inquiry** (time, not required): Describe any specific time inquiries you have.

### Note
* **Note** (note, not required): Add any additional notes or comments about your inquiry.

By following this guide, you should be able to fill out the "Language App Module Inquiry Form" easily and effectively, ensuring that our team receives the necessary information to respond to your inquiry promptly and accurately.
