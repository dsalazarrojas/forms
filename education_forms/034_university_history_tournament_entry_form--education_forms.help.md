# University History Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University History Tournament Entry Form is a data collection tool used to gather information from users about their university history tournament entry. This form is designed to record participant data for a historical tournament at a university.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your university information in the "University" field.
2. Select your registration type (Individual or Team) in the "Registration Type" field.
3. If you are a team, enter your team name in the "Team Name" field.
4. If you are a team, select your team members in the "Team Members" field.
5. Choose your student category (Undergraduate or Graduate) in the "Student Category" field.
6. Choose your entry category (Individual or Team) in the "Entry Category" field.
7. Select your registration date in the "Registration Date" field.
8. Optionally, upload your university logo in the "University Logo" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **University Information** (Page 1, University Information):
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
	+ Description: Enter your university's name and other relevant information.
* **Registration Type** (Page 1, University Information):
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: No
	+ Description: Select "Individual" if you are a single participant or "Team" if you are part of a team.
* **Name** (Page 2, Individual Information):
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
	+ Description: Enter your name if you are an individual or your team name if you are part of a team.
* **Team Name** (Page 2, Team Information):
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
	+ Description: Enter your team's name if you are part of a team.
* **University** (Page 2, Team Information):
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
	+ Description: Enter your university's name.
* **Student Category** (Page 3, Entry Information):
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: No
	+ Description: Select "Undergraduate" or "Graduate" to indicate your academic level.
* **Entry Category** (Page 3, Entry Information):
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: No
	+ Description: Select "Individual" if you are a single participant or "Team" if you are part of a team.
* **Registration Date** (Page 4, Entry Information):
	+ Type: date
	+ Required: No
	+ Description: Enter the date of your registration.
* **Team Members** (Page 4, Team Information):
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Required: No
	+ Description: Select whether you have team members or not.
* **University Logo** (Page 4, Team Information):
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
	+ Description: Optionally, upload your university's logo.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and truthfully.
* If you are part of a team, ensure that all team members are also filling out their own forms.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with your university's history department or tournament organizers.
