# Homeschool High School Report Card Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Homeschool High School Report Card Form is designed to help homeschooling parents and teachers document a student's progress and achievements throughout the school year. This form is used to provide an overview of a student's grades, academic performance, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name and other identifying information, including their first name, last name, student ID, and grade level.
2. Select the relevant subjects for which you are reporting on the student's progress.
3. Enter the grade for each subject and provide any additional comments from the teacher.
4. Enter the date on which you completed the form.
5. Sign and date the form with your teacher signature, parent signature, and student signature.
6. Enter any contact information, such as email and phone number, so we can reach out to you with any questions or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the student's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: true): Enter the student's last name.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, `text`, required: true): Enter the student's ID number, as provided by the homeschooling program.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the student's current grade level, such as 9, 10, 11, or 12.
* **Subject 1** (`subject_1`, `text`, required: true): Enter the student's grade and any comments for the first subject.
* **Subject 2** (`subject_2`, `text`, required: true): Enter the student's grade and any comments for the second subject.
* **Subject 3** (`subject_3`, `text`, required: true): Enter the student's grade and any comments for the third subject.
* **Subject 4** (`subject_4`, `text`, required: true): Enter the student’s grade and any comments for the fourth subject.
* **Subject 5** (`subject_5`, `text`, required: false): Enter the student’s grade and any comments for the fifth subject (if applicable).
* **Subject 6** (`subject_6`, `text`, required: false): Enter the student’s grade and any comments for the sixth subject (if applicable).
* **Subject 7** (`subject_7`, `text`, required: false): Enter the student’s grade and any comments for the seventh subject (if applicable).
* **Subject 8** (`subject_8`, `text`, required: false): Enter the student’s grade and any comments for the eighth subject (if applicable).
* **Subject 9** (`subject_9`, `text`, required: false): Enter the student’s grade and any comments for the ninth subject (if applicable).
* **Subject 10** (`subject_10`, `text`, required: false): Enter the student’s grade and any comments for the tenth subject (if applicable).
* **Grade** (`grade`, `number`, required: true): Enter the student’s overall grade for the semester.
* **Teacher Comments** (`teacher_comments`, `text`, required: false): Enter any comments from the teacher.
* **Student Comments** (`student_comments`, `text`, required: false): Enter any comments from the student.
* **Parent Comments** (`parent_comments`, `text`, required: false): Enter any comments from the parent.
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, `date`, required: true): Enter the date on which you completed the form.
* **Teacher Signature** (`teacher_signature`, `text`, required: true): Sign and date the form with your teacher signature.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, `text`, required: true): Sign and date the form with your parent signature.
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, `text`, required: true): Sign and date the form with your student signature.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: true): Enter your email address so we can reach out to you with any questions or concerns.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: true): Enter your phone number so we can reach out to you with any questions or concerns.
* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the category for this report card, such as Education Forms or Report Forms.

Note: The homeschool high school report card form is designed for use by homeschooling parents and teachers to document and report on a student's progress. The fields provided are intended to help capture relevant information about the student's grades, comments, and other achievements. By completing this form, you will be able to keep track of the student's progress and provide an overview of their performance at the end of the school year.
