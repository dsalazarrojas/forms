# Horse Riding Instructor Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about horse riding instructors from students or parents.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Instructor Name**: Enter the name of the instructor you are recommending.
2. **Instructor Employment Status**: Select the employment status of the instructor from the provided options.
3. **Student Name**: Enter the name of the student or parent who is recommending the instructor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Instructor Name
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the horse riding instructor you are recommending. This field is optional, but it's helpful to have a specific name for reference.

### Is Instructor Employed
* **Is Instructor Employed** (`instructor_employment_status`, select_one, optional): Select the employment status of the instructor from the provided options:
	+ **Full Time**: The instructor is employed full-time.
	+ **Part Time**: The instructor is employed part-time.
	+ **Retired**: The instructor is retired from their job.
	+ **Self Employed**: The instructor is self-employed.

### Student Name
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the student or parent recommending the instructor. This field is optional, but it helps to keep track of who made the recommendation.
