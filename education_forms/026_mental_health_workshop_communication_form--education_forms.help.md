This form is designed to collect information from workshop participants to improve mental health workshops provided by the organization. The purpose of this form is to collect participant preferences, workshop topics, and other relevant information to tailor the workshop to the needs of the participants.

The form is part of the education forms category.

The form consists of 8 fields:

1. **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional)
    This field is where the participant should enter their name. This will help us know who is attending the workshop.

2. **Email** (`email`, email, optional)
    Please enter your email address. This will help us reach out to you after the workshop.

3. **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional)
    Enter your phone number. This will help us communicate with you regarding the workshop.

4. **Participant Preferences** (`participant_preferences`, text, optional)
    Please let us know any specific preferences you have, such as any health conditions that may affect your participation.

5. **Workshop Topics** (`workshop_topics`, select multiple, optional)
    Choose all that apply from the following workshop topics:
    - Cognitive Behavioural Therapy
    - Mindfulness
    - Self-Care
    - Other (please specify)

6. **Workshop Schedule** (`workshop_schedule`, date, optional)
    Choose the date of the workshop you would like to attend.

7. **Workshop Facilitator** (`workshop_facilitator`, select one, optional)
    Choose a facilitator from:
    - Jim
    - Jane
    - Other (please specify)

8. **Workshop Location** (`workshop_location`, text, optional)
    Let us know if you have any specific requirements for the workshop location.

# mental_health_workshop_communication_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mental health workshop communication form is designed to collect participant preferences and information to improve the workshop experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Participant Preferences**: Share any specific preferences you have.
* **Workshop Topics**: Select all that apply from the list of workshop topics.
* **Workshop Schedule**: Choose the date you would like to attend.
* **Workshop Facilitator**: Choose a facilitator from the list.
* **Workshop Location**: Enter any specific location requirements.
