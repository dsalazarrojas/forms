# Conference Leadership Team Member Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect applications from individuals who want to join the conference leadership team. The information provided will be used to assess their suitability for the role and ensure that they are a good fit for the team.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the form in its entirety.
2. Be accurate and honest in your responses.
3. Use standard business email addresses and contact numbers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Date** (`application_date`, `date`, required):
	+ Date of application
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required):
	+ Your full name as you would like it to be displayed on conference materials
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required):
	+ Your contact email address
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required):
	+ Your contact number
* **Current Address** (`current_address`, `text`, required):
	+ Your current full address
* **Professional Information** (`professional_info`, `note`, required):
	+ This field is for additional information about your professional background
* **Current Position** (`current_position`, `text`, required):
	+ Your current job title
* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, required):
	+ The company or institution you work for
* **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, `number`, required):
	+ In the relevant field
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Select your highest degree level (High School, Associate, Bachelor, Master, Doctorate, Other)
* **Field of Expertise** (`field_of_expertise`, `text`, required):
	+ The area of specialization
* **Leadership Experience** (`leadership_experience`, `note`, required):
	+ This field is for additional information about your leadership experience
* **Previous Leadership Roles** (`previous_leadership_roles`, `text`, required):
	+ Describe your previous leadership experience
* **Conference Involvement** (`conference_involvement`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Select the level of your conference involvement (First Time Applicant, Previous Attendee, Previous Speaker, Previous Volunteer, Previous Organizer)
* **Motivation** (`motivation`, `note`, required):
	+ This field is for additional information about your motivation for joining the leadership team
* **Why Do You Want to Join the Leadership Team?** (`why_apply`, `text`, required):
	+ Your motivation for joining the conference leadership team
* **What Do You Bring to This Role?** (`what_bring_to_role`, `text`, required):
	+ Your strengths and how you can contribute to the team
* **Leadership Style** (`leadership_style`, `text`, required):
	+ How you lead and manage teams
* **Role Preferences** (`role_preferences`, `note`, required):
	+ Areas of interest for your role on the leadership team
* **Preferred Roles** (`preferred_roles`, `select_multiple`, required):
	+ Select all roles that you are interested in (Program Committee, Marketing and Communications, Sponsorship and Partnerships, Logistics and Operations, Volunteer Coordination, Speaker Liaison, Attendee Experience)
* **Time Commitment** (`time_commitment`, `select_one`, required):
	+ The hours per week you are available to commit to the role
* **Availability** (`availability`, `select_multiple`, required):
	+ Select all the times when you are available to contribute to the team (Weekday Mornings, Weekday Afternoons, Weekday Evenings, Weekends, Flexible Schedule)
* **Relevant Skills** (`skills`, `text`, required):
	+ A list of your relevant skills
* **References** (`references`, `text`, required):
	+ Professional references
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required):
	+ Any other information you want to provide
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Please accept the terms of the conference leadership team by selecting "I Agree to Terms"

Please note that you should only select "I Agree to Terms" if you have read and agree to the terms of the conference leadership team. 

Please answer all questions thoroughly and accurately. If you have any questions or need help, feel free to reach out to the conference leadership team.
