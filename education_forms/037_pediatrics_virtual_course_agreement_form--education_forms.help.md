# Pediatrics Virtual Course Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pediatrics Virtual Course Agreement Form is designed to collect information and agreements from students and instructors for a virtual pediatrics course. This form helps to ensure accurate and comprehensive information is collected for course enrollment, progress, and evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:
1. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "User Information" questions.
2. Fill out your name, email address, and phone number (if provided).
3. Enter your date of birth.
4. If applicable, provide a medical history and list any allergies you have.
5. Indicate if you have any medical conditions.
6. List any medications you are currently taking.
7. Provide emergency contact information, including name and relationship to you.
8. Indicate if the emergency contact has given their consent.
9. Sign an agreement to complete the course and provide course completion details.
10. Choose a course start date and end date.
11. Select your current student status (Active or Inactive).
12. Agree to the enrollment terms.
13. Indicate if you have an emergency contact who has agreed to receive notifications.
14. Select your current course progress (Yes or No).
15. Provide details about your course progress.
16. Indicate if you have an instructor contact.
17. List the relationship you have with the instructor.
18. Provide a course evaluation completion date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Information** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select "Yes" or "No" for this field. This indicates whether the form is for yourself or not.
- **Name** (`text`, required): Fill out your name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Email** (`email`, required): Enter your official email address.
- **Phone** (`text`, optional): If you have a phone number, please enter it.
- **Date Of Birth** (`date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Medical History** (`note`, optional): If you have a medical history, please provide a brief description.
- **Allergies** (`select_one`, optional): If you have any allergies, please select "Yes".
- **Medical Conditions** (`select_multiple`, optional): If you have any medical conditions, please select "Yes" for each applicable condition.
- **Medication** (`text`, required): List any medications you are currently taking.
- **Emergency Contact Information** (`text`, required): Provide the name and relationship of your emergency contact.
- **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`select_one`, optional): Indicate the relationship you have with your emergency contact.
- **Consent** (`select_one`, optional): Indicate if your emergency contact has given their consent.
- **Agreement** (`select_one`, optional): Sign an agreement to complete the course.
- **Emergency Contact** (`select_one`, optional): Indicate if you have an emergency contact who has agreed to receive notifications.
- **Course Completion** (`date`, optional): Choose a date for course completion.
- **Course Start Date** (`date`, required): Choose a start date for the course.
- **Course End Date** (`date`, required): Choose an end date for the course.
- **Student Status** (`select_one`, optional): Indicate your current student status (Active or Inactive).
- **Enrollment Agreement** (`select_one`, optional): Agree to the enrollment terms.
- **Emergency Contact Agreement** (`select_one`, optional): Indicate if your emergency contact has agreed to receive notifications.
- **Course Progress** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select "Yes" or "No" for this field. This indicates your current course progress.
- **Course Progress Details** (`text`, required): Provide details about your course progress.
- **Instructor Information** (`select_multiple`, optional): If you have an instructor contact, please select "Yes".
- **Instructor Relationship** (`select_one`, optional): Indicate the relationship you have with your instructor.
- **Course Evaluation** (`date`, required): Choose a completion date for the course evaluation.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- If you have any doubts or questions, please contact the course administration.
- This form is for the Pediatrics Virtual Course Agreement Form only. Do not use for any other purpose.
