# Lab Equipment Use Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to inform the laboratory management about the use of specific equipment in the lab. It is used to document the start and end dates of equipment use, as well as the status and purpose of the equipment. This form helps the management to keep track of all equipment usage and ensure that equipment is not misused or left unaccounted for.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user information (name and department).
2. Enter the name of the laboratory where the equipment is being used.
3. Enter the name of the equipment you are using.
4. Select the start and end dates of your equipment use.
5. Describe the purpose of your equipment use (optional).
6. Enter your name as the user of the equipment.
7. Enter the name of your department.
8. Enter the name of your supervisor.
9. Select the status of the equipment (available, in use, reserved, or broken).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information (1)**: This field is for your personal details. Please enter your name and any other relevant information.
* **Laboratory Name (2)**: Enter the name of the laboratory where you are using the equipment.
* **Equipment Name (3)**: Enter the name of the equipment you are using.
* **Start Date (4)**: Enter the date you started using the equipment.
* **End Date (5)**: Enter the date you finished using the equipment.
* **Purpose (6)**: This field is optional. If you want to describe why you are using the equipment, enter your purpose here.
* **User Name (7)**: Enter your name as the user of the equipment.
* **Department Name (8)**: Enter the name of your department.
* **Supervisor Name (9)**: Enter the name of your supervisor.
* **Equipment Status (10)**: Select the status of the equipment from the options: available, in use, reserved, or broken.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate dates for both start and end dates.
* If the equipment is reserved, please do not use it without permission from the supervisor.
* If the equipment is broken, report it to the maintenance team immediately.
