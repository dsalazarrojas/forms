# parent_teacher_meeting_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The parent-teacher meeting form is designed to enable parents to report their child's progress, provide feedback, or schedule meetings with the teacher. This form helps teachers keep track of meeting requests and progress updates from parents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: 
	* **Meeting Date**: Enter the date of the meeting.
	* **Teacher Name**: Enter the name of the teacher who met with the parent.
	* **Student Name**: Enter the name of the student.
2. Optional fields can be used for additional context:
	* **Meeting Time**: Enter the time of the meeting.
	* **Parent Name**: Enter the name of the parent who attended the meeting.
	* **Email**: Enter a contact email for the parent or teacher.
	* **Student Grade**: Enter the student's current grade level.
	* **Meeting Subject**: Enter a brief description of the meeting topic.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Date**: 
	* The date of the meeting, in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Meeting Time**: 
	* The time of the meeting, in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **Teacher Name**: 
	* The name of the teacher who met with the parent.
* **Parent Name**: 
	* The name of the parent who attended the meeting.
* **Email**: 
	* A contact email for the parent or teacher.
* **Student Name**: 
	* The name of the student.
* **Student Grade**: 
	* The student's current grade level.
* **Meeting Subject**: 
	* A brief description of the meeting topic.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Use the optional fields to provide additional context or information that may be useful for the teacher or parent.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the teacher or school administration.
