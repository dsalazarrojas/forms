# Academic Library Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Academic Library Survey" is a form designed to gather information from academic library users about their experiences, satisfaction, and expectations. The data collected will help the library understand its users' needs and improve services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and email (if you wish to receive a response).
2. Select your primary role as a library user (student, faculty, staff, or visitor).
3. Choose how often you use the library services.
4. Rate your satisfaction with the current resources and services.
5. Rate the accessibility of library services and space.
6. Select up to three services you would like the library to offer.
7. Provide any open comments or suggestions for improvement.
8. Optionally, provide your email address if you want a response to your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent Information** (`respondent_info`, note, required: false)
  This field is for providing your name and email address. If you wish to receive a response, please provide a valid email address.
* **Patron Type** (`patron_type`, select_one, required: true)
  This field asks about your primary role as a library user (student, faculty, staff, or visitor).
* **Frequency of Use** (`frequency_of_use`, select_one, required: true)
  Please select how often you use library services (daily, weekly, monthly, or rarely).
* **Satisfaction with Resources** (`satisfaction_resources`, select_one, required: true)
  Rate your satisfaction with the current resources and services available at the library.
* **Accessibility** (`accessibility`, select_one, required: true)
  This field assesses how accessible the library's services and space are.
* **Services you would like the library to offer** (`desired_services`, select_multiple, required: false)
  Select up to three services that you would like the library to offer or improve. Examples include extended hours, research consultations, technology lending, and workshops and training.
* **Open comments and suggestions** (`open_comments`, text, required: false)
  This field is for any open comments or suggestions you may have for library improvement.
* **Optional contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false)
  If you would like to receive a response to your feedback, please provide your email address.

## Tips
- Be honest and specific when answering the questions to help the library understand your needs better.
- Please select only one option for each field where required.
- If you have any comments or suggestions, please provide them in the "Open comments and suggestions" field.
- If you want to receive a response to your feedback, please provide your email address.
