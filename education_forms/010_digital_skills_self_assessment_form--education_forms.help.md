# digital_skills_self_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals or organizations assess their digital skills, identify areas for improvement, and create plans for development. It's a tool for evaluating and improving digital skills in various contexts, including professional, educational, or training settings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions on each page to the best of your abilities. You can refer to the field explanations below for help.
2. Complete all the required fields, if any.
3. Review your answers to ensure they are accurate and complete.
4. Use the form to identify areas for improvement and create development plans.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is a digital skills self-assessment form?** (`1`, `text`, optional): This field asks you to describe the form's purpose and what you expect from it. Keep it brief and to the point.
* **What is it used for?** (`2`, `text`, optional): Explain how you plan to use the results of this form, for example, to identify skills gaps, create development plans, or improve performance.
* **Who can use it?** (`3`, `select_one`, optional): This field is about who can benefit from this form, such as employees, students, or trainers.
* **When to use it?** (`4`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose when you plan to use this form, for example, quarterly, annually, or as needed.
* **What skills to assess?** (`5`, `select_multiple`, optional): List the digital skills you want to assess, such as coding, data analysis, or project management.
* **What are the skills levels?** (`6`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the skill level you're evaluating, for example, beginner, intermediate, or advanced.
* **Assessment frequency?** (`7`, `select_one`, optional): Select how often you plan to assess digital skills, for example, monthly, quarterly, or annually.
* **Skills development?** (`8`, `text`, optional): Describe your plans for skills development, such as training, mentorship, or workshops.
* **Performance?** (`9`, `text`, optional): Share any performance-related thoughts or comments.
* **Self-assessment process?** (`10`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the self-assessment process you'll use, for example, peer review, self-reflection, or coaching.
* **Feedback?** (`11`, `select_one`, optional): Select how you plan to gather feedback, for example, peer feedback, supervisor feedback, or self-reflection.
* **Next steps?** (`12`, `text`, optional): Outline your next steps after completing this form.
* **Skills gap analysis?** (`13`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how you'll perform skills gap analysis, for example, identify gaps, prioritize skills, or develop plans.
* **Training and development?** (`14`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select types of training or development opportunities you'll provide, for example, courses, mentorship, or workshops.
* **Performance improvement?** (`15`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how you'll improve performance, for example, through coaching, mentoring, or recognition.
* **Skills identification?** (`16`, `select_one`, optional): Select how you'll identify skills, for example, self-assessment, peer review, or skills tests.
* **Performance monitoring?** (`17`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how you'll monitor performance, for example, regular check-ins, progress reports, or performance reviews.
* **Job description?** (`18`, `text`, optional): Describe the job or role you're focusing on, if any.
* **Job requirements?** (`19`, `text`, optional): Outline the job requirements or expectations, if any.
* **Skills identification frequency?** (`20`, `select_one`, optional): Select how often you'll identify skills, for example, daily, weekly, or monthly.
* **Skills development plans?** (`21`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how you'll create development plans, for example, through mentorship, workshops, or courses.
* **Performance improvement plans?** (`22`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select types of performance improvement plans, for example, coaching, mentoring, or recognition.
* **Feedback frequency?** (`23`, `select_one`, optional): Select how often you'll gather feedback, for example, regularly, quarterly, or annually.
* **Evaluation frequency?** (`24`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how often you'll evaluate progress, for example, weekly, monthly, or quarterly.
* **Next steps frequency?** (`25`, `select_one`, optional): Select how often you'll take next steps, for example, daily, weekly, or monthly.

**Tips** 
- Be honest and thorough when completing this form.
- Use the form to identify areas for improvement and create plans for development.
- Review and update the form regularly to track progress.
