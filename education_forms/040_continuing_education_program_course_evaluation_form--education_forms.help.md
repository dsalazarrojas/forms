# Continuing Education Program Course Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from learners who have taken our Continuing Education (CE) courses. Your responses will help us evaluate the quality and effectiveness of our programs and make informed decisions about course development and offerings.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and fill out all required fields.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Use the hints provided for guidance on each field.
4. Submit the form when you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Program/Certificate Name** (`parent_program_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the program or certificate you are evaluating, e.g., Executive Leadership Certificate.
* **Course Code/Title** (`specific_course_code`, text, required): Enter the course code or title you are evaluating, e.g., LEAD-402 Strategic Planning.
* **Were the stated learning objectives met** (`objectives_met_status`, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Yes - All objectives met
	+ Yes - Most objectives met
	+ No - Objectives were not clear or met
* **If objectives were not met, please explain why** (`objectives_commentary`, text, optional): If you chose "No" for the previous question, please provide more details on why the objectives were not met.
* **Rate the pace of the course** (`course_pace_rating`, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Too Fast - Difficult to keep up
	+ About Right - Challenging but manageable
	+ Too Slow - Could have covered more material
* **Level of 'Real-World' applicability** (`real_world_application_score`, number, required): Rate the likelihood that you will use what you learned in this course in your job tomorrow, from 1 (not likely at all) to 10 (very likely).
* **Rating of Administrative Support** (`administrative_support_rating`, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Satisfactory
	+ Needs Improvement
	+ N/A
* **Do you feel this course aids in career advancement** (`career_advancement_potential`, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Yes, significantly
	+ Yes, moderately
	+ Not really
* **What other courses would you like to see in this program** (`future_course_interest`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions for other courses that you would like to see offered in this program, please list them here.
* **Overall Satisfaction with this Program** (`overall_program_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ 1 - Not Satisfied
	+ 2 - Slightly Satisfied
	+ 3 - Neutral
	+ 4 - Satisfied
	+ 5 - Extremely Satisfied
* **Date of Submission** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are submitting this form.

Note: All fields marked as required must be filled out for the form to be submitted.
