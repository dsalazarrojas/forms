# Scholarship Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scholarship Inquiry Form is a tool for students to provide information about their background, contact details, and additional information required for scholarship applications. This form helps us to understand your eligibility and gather all the necessary information in a single submission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your **Student Information** (Page 1):
	* Enter your name, student ID, or other identifying details.
2. Share your **Contact Information** (Page 2):
	* Enter your email address, phone number, or other contact details.
3. Share your **Academic Background** (Page 3):
	* Enter any relevant academic details, such as your degree level, institution, or grades.
4. Add any **Additional Information** (Page 4):
	* Share any other relevant details about yourself or your application.
5. Click **Submit** (Page 5):
	* Review and submit your form to complete your scholarship inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Information** (Page 1):
	* Enter your name and any other identifying details.
* **Contact Information** (Page 2):
	* Enter your email address, phone number, or other contact information so we can get back to you.
* **Academic Background** (Page 3):
	* Share any relevant academic details, such as degree level, institution, or grades.
* **Additional Information** (Page 4):
	* If there's anything else you'd like to share or add to your application, use this field to do so.
* **Submit** (Page 5):
	* Review and submit your form to complete your scholarship inquiry.
