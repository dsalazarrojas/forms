# ICT In Education Awareness Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ICT In Education Awareness Quiz form is designed to assess users' knowledge and awareness about the benefits and challenges of integrating Information and Communication Technology (ICT) in educational settings. This quiz aims to evaluate understanding of ICT's role in enhancing learning outcomes, improving access to information, and identifying potential barriers to adoption.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form successfully, follow these steps:

1. **Enter a title**: In the "ICT Awareness Quiz" field, provide a title that reflects your understanding of the quiz's purpose.
2. Fill in your name in the "Your Name" field, as it is a required field.
3. If you have an email address associated with your account, enter it in the "Email Address" field for verification purposes (though this field is not required).
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field, if applicable.
5. Select the type of quiz you'd like to take by choosing from the "Quiz Type" dropdown menu: ICT Awareness, Digital Literacy, or Technology Integration.
6. Answer the questions by selecting the correct options for each multiple-choice question:
	* For "What is the primary purpose of ICT in education?", choose one of the four available options.
	* For "Which of the following are the main benefits of ICT in education?", select all applicable options from the multiple-choice list.
	* For "What are some common barriers to adopting ICT in education?", choose two or more from the multiple-choice list.
	* For "Which of the following best describe the role of teachers in the ICT-rich classroom?", select all applicable options from the multiple-choice list.
7. Optionally, provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name (Your Name)**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address (Email Address)**: Provide your email address if you have one associated with your account. This field is optional.
* **Phone Number (Phone Number)**: Enter your phone number if you have one. This field is not required.
* **Quiz Type (Quiz Type)**: Select the type of quiz that best suits your needs: ICT Awareness, Digital Literacy, or Technology Integration.
* **What is the primary purpose of ICT in education? (questions_1)**: Choose the option that best answers the question.
* **Which of the following are the main benefits of ICT in education? (questions_2)**: Select all applicable options from the multiple-choice list.
* **What are some common barriers to adopting ICT in education? (questions_3)**: Choose two or more from the multiple-choice list.
* **Which of the following best describe the role of teachers in the ICT-rich classroom? (questions_4)**: Select all applicable options from the multiple-choice list.
* **Comments (comments)**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about the quiz or your experience. This field is optional.
