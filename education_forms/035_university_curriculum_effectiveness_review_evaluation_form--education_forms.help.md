# University Curriculum Effectiveness Review Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Curriculum Effectiveness Review Evaluation Form is a tool used to assess the effectiveness of a university's curriculum. This form helps administrators, educators, and curriculum developers evaluate the strengths and weaknesses of a particular program and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Choose the Category and Subcategory
* **Category** (`category`): Select the category that best describes the curriculum being evaluated. This will help narrow down the evaluation process and ensure that the form is relevant to the specific curriculum.
* **Subcategory** (`subcategory`): Choose the subcategory that best fits the curriculum's focus or area of study.

### Step 2: Provide General Information
* **Title** (`title`): Enter a brief title that describes the curriculum being evaluated.
* **Description** (`description`): Provide a short description of the curriculum, including its purpose, goals, and objectives.
* **Category Name** (`category_name`): Enter the name of the category selected in Step 1.
* **Subcategory Name** (`subcategory_name`): Enter the name of the subcategory selected in Step 1.

### Step 3: Evaluate the Curriculum
* **Date** (`date`): Record the date when the evaluation was conducted.
* **Time** (`time`): Enter the time of day when the evaluation was completed.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`): Enter the date when the evaluation was completed.
* **Review** (`review`): Enter a brief review or summary of the curriculum.
* **Rating** (`rating`): Choose one of the available options to rate the curriculum (e.g., Option A, Option B, Option C).
* **Score** (`score`): Enter a numerical score (0-10) to further evaluate the curriculum.

### Step 4: Additional Information
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`): Record the date and time when the form was submitted.
* **Submitted By (Email)** (`submitter_email`): Enter the email address of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted By (Phone)** (`submitter_phone`): Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted Location** (`submitter_location`): Enter the location where the form was submitted.
* **Submitted Department** (`submitter_department`): Enter the department of the person submitting the form.

### Step 5: Reviewer Information
* **Reviewer Comments** (`reviewer_comments`): Enter any comments or feedback from the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Rating** (`reviewer_rating`): Choose one of the available options to rate the curriculum (e.g., Option A, Option B, Option C).
* **Comments to Reviewer** (`comments_to_reviewer`): Enter any comments or feedback for the reviewer.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Enter any additional comments or feedback.

### Tips
* Be thorough and accurate when completing this form to ensure that the evaluation is fair and useful.
* Use the available options for choices and ratings as a guide, but feel free to provide additional information or comments as needed.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant department or administrator.
