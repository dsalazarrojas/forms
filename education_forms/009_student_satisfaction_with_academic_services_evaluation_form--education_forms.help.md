# Student Satisfaction With Academic Services Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather feedback from students about their experience with academic services. By completing this form, you will be helping us understand your perceptions and opinions about the quality and availability of resources and support provided.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page carefully and answer the questions according to your experience.
2. Choose the answer that best reflects your feelings about each question.
3. Fill in any additional feedback or suggestions you may have at the end.
4. Submit your feedback by clicking the submit button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Experience (Page 1)**: This is a brief introduction to the form. Please review this page and answer the questions that follow.
	+ **Satisfaction with Quality of Instruction (Question 2)**: How satisfied are you with the quality of instruction provided?
		- Select one of the options: 
			- Very Dissatisfied
			- Somewhat Dissatisfied
			- Neither
			- Somewhat Satisfied
			- Very Satisfied
	+ **Satisfaction with Availability of Resources (Question 3)**: How satisfied are you with the availability of resources such as quality of instruction, facilities, library, etc.?
		- Select one of the options: 
			- Very Dissatisfied
			- Somewhat Dissatisfied
			- Neither
			- Somewhat Satisfied
			- Very Satisfied
* **Quality of Academic Advice and Guidance (Question 7)**: How satisfied are you with the quality of academic advice and guidance provided?
	+ **Satisfaction with Support from Instructors (Question 4)**: How satisfied are you with the support from instructors?
	+ **Satisfaction with Support from Peers (Question 5)**: How satisfied are you with the support from peers?
	+ **Overall Satisfaction (Question 6)**: How satisfied are you with your overall experience?
	+ **Suggestions and Comments (Question 11)**: Do you have any additional feedback or suggestions?
	+ **Additional Feedback (Question 12)**: Do you have any other feedback or comments?

**Tips**

* Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about any question, you can also leave it blank or select "Neither".
* Your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your participation.
