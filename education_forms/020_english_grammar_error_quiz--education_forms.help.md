# English Grammar Error Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The English Grammar Error Quiz form is a self-assessment tool designed to evaluate users' knowledge of English grammar rules. It is a simple and user-friendly form that helps you identify and correct English grammar errors in your writing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the User Info section with your name, email address, and any other relevant information.
2. In the Error Info section, describe an English grammar error you are aware of or have encountered in your writing.
3. In the Confirm section, confirm whether the provided error is correct or incorrect.
4. Finally, add a note (if needed) to elaborate on your answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info (1)**: This section is for providing your name, email address, and any other relevant information.
* **Error Info (2)**: Describe an English grammar error you are aware of or have encountered in your writing.
* **Submit (3)**: This is the submit button. Please click on it to save your progress.
* **Confirm (4)**: Confirm whether the provided error is correct or incorrect.
* **Note (5)**: Add a note (if needed) to elaborate on your answer.

## Tips
* Make sure to describe the error accurately to get accurate feedback.
* If you are unsure about a particular error, try to identify it first.
* Remember that this form is for self-assessment and improvement of your English grammar knowledge.
