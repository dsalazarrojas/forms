# Research Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Research Log Form is used to track and monitor research activities, including dates, locations, and personnel involved.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Start by selecting the Research Log page and entering the relevant details in the text fields provided. 
*   Select the date fields as required for each entry. 
*   Enter the Researcher's name, title, institution, department, project name, project description, research question, research area, and research location as required. 
*   Enter the first entry date, last entry date, research start date, research end date, research duration, research hours, researchers count, participants count, participants location, and participants description as required. 
*   Select the status as required from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Research Log** (`research_log_form_first_page`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the research activity.
*   **Last Research Log** (`research_log_form_last_page`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the last research activity.
*   **First Entry** (`research_log_form_first_entry_page`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the first entry in the research log.
*   **Date** (`research_log_form_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the research activity.
*   **Researcher Name** (`research_log_form_researcher_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the researcher.
*   **Researcher Title** (`research_log_form_researcher_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the researcher.
*   **Researcher Institution** (`research_log_form_researcher_institution`, text, required): Enter the institution of the researcher.
*   **Researcher Department** (`research_log_form_researcher_department`, text, required): Enter the department of the researcher.
*   **Project Name** (`research_log_form_project_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the project.
*   **Project Description** (`research_log_form_project_description`, text, required): Enter a description of the project.
*   **Research Question** (`research_log_form_research_question`, text, required): Enter the research question.
*   **Research Area** (`research_log_form_research_area`, text, required): Enter the research area.
*   **Research Location** (`research_log_form_research_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the research.
*   **First Entry Date** (`research_log_form_first_entry_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the first entry.
*   **Last Entry Date** (`research_log_form_last_entry_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the last entry.
*   **Research Start Date** (`research_log_form_research_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the research.
*   **Research End Date** (`research_log_form_research_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the research.
*   **Research Duration** (`research_log_form_research_duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of the research.
*   **Research Hours** (`research_log_form_research_hours`, number, required): Enter the number of research hours.
*   **Researchers Count** (`research_log_form_researchers_count`, number, required): Enter the number of researchers.
*   **Participants Count** (`research_log_form_participants_count`, number, required): Enter the number of participants.
*   **Participants Location** (`research_log_form_participants_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the participants.
*   **Participants Description** (`research_log_form_participants_description`, text, required): Enter a description of the participants.
*   **Notes** (`research_log_form_notes`, text, required): Enter any additional notes about the research activity.
*   **Status** (`research_log_form_status`, select_multiple, required): Select the status of the research activity from the dropdown menu.

## Tips

*   Be as specific as possible when entering dates and times.
*   Use the provided fields to ensure accurate and comprehensive documentation of the research activity.
*   Make sure to complete all required fields.
