# Recognition Of Prior Learning (RPL) Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) Assessment Form is used to evaluate an individual's prior learning experiences and determine their relevance to a particular qualification or certification. It helps assessors make an informed decision on whether to grant recognition for the skills and knowledge gained through these experiences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand each section before proceeding.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and detailed information about your prior learning experiences.
3. Provide relevant evidence to support your claims, such as certificates, transcripts, or documentation from the relevant providers.
4. Answer all questions truthfully and thoroughly, as incomplete or inaccurate information may affect the assessment outcome.
5. If you are unsure about any part of the form, do not hesitate to ask for guidance from the assessor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Details** (`Application Details`, `text`, optional): Please provide a brief introduction or summary of your prior learning experiences and the reasons why you believe they should be recognized.
* **Personal Details** (`Personal Details`, `text`, optional): Enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant personal details that may be helpful for the assessor.
* **Education and Work Experience** (`Education and Work Experience`, `text`, optional): Describe your education and work history, including any relevant institutions, degrees, or certifications earned.
* **Skills and Abilities** (`Skills and Abilities`, `text`, optional): List any skills or abilities you possess that are relevant to the qualification or certification you are applying for.
* **RPL Evidence** (`RPL Evidence`, `text`, optional): Attach or provide copies of any relevant documentation, such as certificates, transcripts, or letters from the relevant providers.
* **Training Provider Details** (`Training Provider Details`, `text`, optional): If applicable, provide information about the training providers that delivered your prior learning experiences.
* **Assessor Comments** (`Assessor Comments`, `text`, optional): The assessor may use this section to provide comments or feedback on your application.
* **RPL Outcome** (`RPL Outcome`, `select_one`, optional): Select "Yes" if your prior learning experiences meet the requirements for recognition, or "No" if they do not.
* **Date Completed** (`Date Completed`, `date`, optional): If applicable, enter the date you completed your prior learning experiences.

# Tips

* Be as detailed and thorough as possible when filling out the form.
* Provide all relevant supporting evidence, such as certificates, transcripts, or letters from the relevant providers.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, do not hesitate to ask for guidance from the assessor.
* Ensure that your prior learning experiences are not already recognized or assessed through other means.
