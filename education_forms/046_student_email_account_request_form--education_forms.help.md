# Student Email Account Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for students, faculty, or staff members to request a new email account or update existing account information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form completely**: Each field is required to ensure we have all the necessary information to process your request.
2. **Choose the correct request type**: Select the type of email account you need (New Student, Change Password, or Update Existing).
3. **Provide a valid reason for your request**: Explain why you need an email account or why you need to update your existing account information.
4. **Submit the form**: Once you've filled out the form, click the submit button to send your request to our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student ID**: Enter your unique student ID as issued by the school.
* **Student Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your student ID.
* **Course Number**: Enter your course number, if applicable.
* **Department**: If you're affiliated with a specific department, enter it here.
* **Email Address**: Enter your existing email address (if you're requesting an update) or your new email address (if you're requesting a new account).
* **New Email**: Enter your new email address (if you're requesting a new account).
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number, if applicable.
* **Student Phone**: Enter your student phone number, if applicable.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Year of Birth**: Enter your year of birth.
* **Graduation Date**: Enter your expected graduation date, if applicable.
* **Department Email**: Enter your department's email address, if applicable.
* **Department Phone**: Enter your department's phone number, if applicable.
* **Department Address**: Enter your department's address, if applicable.
* **Student Note**: Add any relevant notes or comments about your account request.
* **Department Note**: Add any relevant notes or comments from your department about your account request.
* **Course Note**: Add any relevant notes or comments about your course, if applicable.
* **Email Status**: Select the status of your email account (Pending, Active, or Inactive).
* **Email Request Type**: Select the type of email account you need (New Student, Change Password, or Update Existing).
* **Request Reason**: Explain why you need an email account or why you need to update your existing account information.
* **Request Date**: Enter the date you are requesting the email account.
* **Request Time**: Enter the time you are requesting the email account (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Request IP**: Enter the IP address from which you're submitting this form.
* **Department IP**: Enter the IP address of your department's network, if applicable.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool(s) you've been assigned to use (ChatJImmy, Other).
* **Form Category**: Select the category of this form (Education Forms, General Forms, Custom Forms).

Please note that all fields marked as required must be filled out to process your request. If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact our support team.
