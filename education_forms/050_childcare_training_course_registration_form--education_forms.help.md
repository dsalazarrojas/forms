# Childcare Training Course Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for a childcare training course. It is designed to collect your contact and participant information, understand your experience and training needs, and process payment for the course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your registration date, which is the date you are registering for the course.
2. Enter your legal first name and last name as they appear on your identification.
3. Provide your primary email address for contact purposes.
4. Enter your primary phone number for contact purposes.
5. Fill in your full street address.
6. Enter the city where you reside.
7. Enter your state or province.
8. Fill in your zip code.
9. Select the participant role that best describes you, such as "Parent or guardian", "Childcare provider", or "Teacher".
10. If applicable, enter the name of your childcare organization.
11. Enter the number of years of childcare experience you have.
12. Select the training course(s) you are interested in registering for.
13. Choose how you would prefer to take the course.
14. Choose a preferred start date for the course.
15. Select the schedule that works best for you.
16. If you need continuing education units (CEUs), select why you need them.
17. If you need CEUs, enter the organization that requires them.
18. If you have previously completed any childcare training, select the number of courses you have completed.
19. If you have completed previous training, list the details of the courses.
20. Enter your goals for taking this course.
21. If you need special accommodations, describe the accommodations you require.
22. If you need childcare during the training, select "Yes" and describe the number of children.
23. List the ages of the children that require childcare.
24. Enter the fee amount for the course.
25. Select the method of payment for the course.
26. If your employer is sponsoring your training, select the level of sponsorship.
27. If you are applying for a scholarship, select the type of scholarship you need.
28. Select how you learned about the course.
29. If you have any additional comments or questions, add them here.
30. Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the course.
31. Add your electronic signature and type your full name.
32. Add the date of your electronic signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Date (1)**: Enter the date you are registering for the course.
* **Participant First Name (2)**: Enter your legal first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Participant Last Name (3)**: Enter your legal last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Participant Email (4)**: Enter your primary email address for contact purposes.
* **Participant Phone (5)**: Enter your primary phone number for contact purposes.
* **Participant Address (6)**: Enter your full street address.
* **City (7)**: Enter the city where you reside.
* **State (8)**: Enter your state or province.
* **Zip Code (9)**: Enter your zip or postal code.
* **Participant Role (10)**: Select the participant role that best describes you.
* **Organization Name (11)**: If applicable, enter the name of your childcare organization.
* **Years of Experience (12)**: Enter the number of years of childcare experience you have.
* **Course Selection (13)**: Select the training course(s) you are interested in registering for.
* **Course Format Preference (14)**: Choose how you would prefer to take the course.
* **Preferred Start Date (15)**: Enter the date you would like to start the course.
* **Schedule Preference (16)**: Select the schedule that works best for you.
* **Continuing Education Units (17)**: If you need CEUs, select why you need them.
* **Certification Body (18)**: If you need CEUs, enter the organization that requires them.
* **Previous Training Completed (19)**: If you have previously completed any childcare training, select the number of courses you have completed.
* **Previous Training Details (20)**: If you have completed previous training, list the details of the courses.
* **Learning Goals (21)**: Enter your goals for taking this course.
* **Special Accommodations (22)**: If you need any special accommodations, describe them.
* **Childcare During Training (23)**: If you need childcare during the training, select "Yes" and describe the number of children.
* **Children Ages for Childcare (24)**: List the ages of the children that require childcare.
* **Course Fee (25)**: Enter the fee amount for the course.
* **Payment Method (26)**: Select the method of payment for the course.
* **Employer Sponsorship (27)**: If your employer is sponsoring your training, select the level of sponsorship.
* **Scholarship Application (28)**: If you are applying for a scholarship, select the type of scholarship you need.
* **How Did You Hear About This Course (29)**: Select how you learned about the course.
* **Additional Comments (30)**: If you have any additional comments or questions, add them here.
