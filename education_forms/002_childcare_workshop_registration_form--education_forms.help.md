# Childcare Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Childcare Workshop Registration Form is a tool used to collect information from participants attending the workshop on Effective Communication in Childcare Environments. This form is used by workshop organizers to better understand the needs and expectations of the attendees and tailor the content to suit their needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Workshop Details (Page 1)**
	* The Workshop Details section is for general information about the workshop you are registering for.
	* Fill in the **Workshop Title** field with the name of the workshop, **Effective Communication in Childcare Environments**.
2. **Contact Information (Page 2)**
	* **First Name** and **Last Name**: Fill in your full name.
	* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where workshop materials will be sent to.
	* **Phone Number**: Provide your contact number for any communication related to the workshop.
3. **Workshop Details (Page 3)**
	* **Workshop Date**: This field will be auto-filled based on the specified date. If not, fill it in according to the workshop schedule.
	* **Workshop Time**: Enter the time of the workshop, e.g., "9:00 AM - 4:00 PM daily".
4. **Childcare Background (Page 4-5)**
	* **Childcare Experience Level**: Choose the level of your experience in childcare, which will help in planning the content of the workshop.
	* **Organisation/ School Name**: If you're currently in a position in childcare, provide the name of your organization or school.
	* **Job Title**: Enter your job title in your current position.
5. **Workshop Preferences (Page 6-7)**
	* **Primary Interest**: Select the main reason you're interested in this workshop, such as **Improve Communication Skills** or **Develop Conflict Resolution Strategies**.
	* **Specific Topics**: Choose all the specific topics you are interested in, such as **Effective Listening**, **Difficult Conversations**, **Parent Communication**, **Team Collaboration**, or **Crisis Management**.
6. **Additional Information (Page 8)**
	* **Expectations**: Describe what you want to learn from this workshop in the **What do you expect to learn from this workshop?** field.
7. **Accessibility and Special Requests (Page 9-10)**
	* **Do you require any accessibility accommodations?**: If yes, select **Yes** and describe your requirements in the **Please describe your accommodation needs** field.
8. **Payment Information (Page 11)**
	* **Workshop Registration Fee**: Fill in the registration fee according to the provided information.
	* **Preferred Payment Method**: Choose your preferred payment method, such as **Credit Card**, **Bank Transfer**, **Check**, or **Purchase Order**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Title**: Enter the name of the workshop you are registering for.
* **Workshop Date**: Enter the date of the workshop according to the provided schedule.
* **Workshop Time**: Enter the time of the workshop according to the provided schedule.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where workshop materials will be sent to.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact number for any communication related to the workshop.
* **Childcare Experience Level**: Choose your experience in childcare, from **Less than 1 year** to **10+ years**.
* **Organisation/School Name**: If you're in a position in childcare, enter the name of your organization or school.
* **Job Title**: Enter your current job title in childcare.
* **Primary Interest**: Select the main reason you're interested in this workshop, from **Improve Communication Skills** to **Professional Development**.
* **Specific Topics**: Choose all the topics you are interested in, from **Effective Listening** to **Crisis Management**.
* **Expectations**: Describe what you want to learn from this workshop.
* **Accessibility Needs**: Select **Yes** if you require any accessibility accommodations, and describe your needs in the provided field.
* **Accommodation Needs**: If you selected **Yes**, describe your accommodation requirements.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: Select the type of diet you follow, from **None** to **Gluten-free**.
* **Workshop Registration Fee**: Fill in the registration fee according to the provided information.
* **Preferred Payment Method**: Choose your preferred payment method, from **Credit Card** to **Check**.
