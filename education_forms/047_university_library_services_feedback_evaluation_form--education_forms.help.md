# university_library_services_feedback_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Library services feedback evaluation form is designed to help us understand how effective our services are in meeting your needs. Your responses will help us improve our services and make them better for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form and make sure you have understood all the questions.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
3. Don't worry if you have any difficulty or need clarification on a particular question.
4. Submit the form to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`id: 1`, `label: Introduction`, `type: note`): This is a brief introduction to the form, explaining its purpose and importance.
* **Library Services** (`id: 2`, `label: Library Services`, `type: select_multiple`): Please select all the services you have used from the list provided. For example, "Yes" for services you have used and "No" for services you haven't used.
* **Satisfaction** (`id: 3`, `label: Satisfaction`, `type: select_one`): Select your level of satisfaction with the library services. The options are: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, and Very Dissatisfied.
* **Suggestions** (`id: 4`, `label: Suggestions`, `type: text`): If you have any suggestions or improvements for the library services, please share them with us. This is a good place to provide feedback on how we can improve our services.
* **Comments** (`id: 5`, `label: Comments`, `type: text`): If you have any additional comments or thoughts about the library services, please share them with us.
* **Email** (`id: 6`, `label: Email`, `type: email`): Please provide your contact email address so we can follow up with you about your feedback.
* **Phone Number** (`id: 7`, `label: Phone Number`, `type: text`): If you would like to be contacted by phone about your feedback, please provide your phone number.
* **Submit** (`id: 8`, `label: Submit`, `type: text`): Click this button to send your feedback to us.

## Tips

* Please be honest and sincere in your feedback.
* Try to be specific about your suggestions and comments.
* If you have any difficulty or need clarification on a particular question, don't hesitate to ask.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback! It is greatly appreciated.
