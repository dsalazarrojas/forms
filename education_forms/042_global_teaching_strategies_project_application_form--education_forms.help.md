# Global Teaching Strategies Project Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your project to ensure that it meets the criteria of the Global Teaching Strategies Project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each section as instructed. You can navigate between sections using the "Next" and "Back" buttons.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your project.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your project.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the person responsible for your project.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for your project.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the person responsible for your project.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the school where your project is located.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the school where your project is located.
* **School City** (`school_city`, text, required): Enter the city where your school is located.
* **School State** (`school_state`, text, required): Enter the state where your school is located.
* **School ZIP** (`school_zip`, number, required): Enter the zip code of your school.
* **School Country** (`school_country`, text, required): Enter the country where your school is located.
* **School Region** (`school_region`, text, required): Enter the region of your school.
* **School Type** (`school_type`, text, required): Enter the type of school (e.g., Elementary, High School, University).
* **School Size** (`school_size`, number, required): Enter the number of students at your school.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the teacher responsible for your project.
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the teacher.
* **Teacher Phone** (`teacher_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the teacher.
* **Teacher Degree** (`teacher_degree`, text, required): Enter the degree of the teacher.
* **Teacher Experience** (`teacher_experience`, number, required): Enter the number of years of teaching experience.
* **Teacher Specialization** (`teacher_specialization`, text, required): Enter the specialization of the teacher (e.g., subject area).
* **Number of Students** (`student_number`, number, required): Enter the number of students participating in your project.
* **Program Length** (`program_length`, number, required): Enter the length of the program (in days or weeks).
* **Start Date** (`program_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of your program.
* **End Date** (`program_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of your program.
* **Submission** (`submission`, select_one, required): Select the submission option that best describes your project.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional information about your project.
* **Submission Type** (`submission_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the types of submissions you are providing (e.g., document, video, image).

Please note that some fields are marked as required, so be sure to fill out all required fields.
