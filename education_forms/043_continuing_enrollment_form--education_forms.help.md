# Continuing Enrollment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to help you declare your intent to continue your studies and maintain your active status in your academic program. It ensures that your progress and any relevant information are up-to-date, which is essential for your studies and administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Maintain Your Active Status**: Start by reviewing your current status in the first field, "Current Student Status".
2. **Provide Identification Information**: Enter your "Student Identification Number", "Student Full Name", and "Degree Program or Department" to ensure that your information is accurate and up-to-date.
3. **Continuation Details**: Enter the "Semester and Year" you plan to continue your studies.
4. **Intent to Continue**: Choose whether you intend to remain active in your program ("Yes - Full-time Status" or "Yes - Part-time Status") or if you will not be continuing ("No - Requesting Leave of Absence" or "No - Graduating/Withdrawing").
5. **Expected Graduation Date**: Enter the expected date of graduation, following the format of Month/Year.
6. **Contact Details Updates**: If you have any changes to your address or phone number, select "Yes - Provide updates below".
7. **Update Contact Information**: If you chose to update your contact details, enter the new information in the "Updated Contact Information (If any)" field.
8. **Financial Status**: Ensure you have made the required "Continuation Fee Transaction ID" payment.
9. **Authorization and Date**: Sign the form by indicating your intent to continue your studies, and select "Today's Date" using the provided date field.
10. **Administrative Routing**: Enter the name of your academic advisor for administrative purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Current Student Status**: A note field to remind you to maintain your active status.
* **Student Identification Number**: A text field to enter your student ID number.
* **Student Full Name**: A text field to enter your full name.
* **Degree Program or Department**: A text field to indicate your degree program or department.
* **Semester and Year for Continuation**: A text field to enter the semester and year you plan to continue your studies.
* **Do you intend to remain active in your current program**: A select_one field with four options to indicate your intent to continue your studies.
* **Expected Graduation Date**: A text field to enter the expected date of graduation, following the format of Month/Year.
* **Any changes to Address or Phone**: A select_one field with two options to indicate if you have any changes to your contact details.
* **Updated Contact Information (If any)**: A text field to enter the updated contact information if you chose to update it.
* **Continuation Fee Transaction ID**: A text field to provide proof of payment for maintaining active status.
* **I certify my intent to continue my studies**: A text field for you to declare your intent to continue your studies.
* **Today's Date**: A date field to select today's date.
* **Name of Academic Advisor**: A text field for your academic advisor's name.
* **Any other comments or questions**: This section is currently missing but could be added for future fields.
*   **Continuation Fee Reference**: Ensure you have made the required "Continuation Fee" payment.
*   **Signature**: Sign this section to confirm your intent to continue your studies. 
*   **Academic Advisor Name**: Enter your academic advisor's name for administrative purposes.
*   **Today's Date**: Select today's date as a reference point. 

Note: This help guide assumes that the form fields and their types are correct and complete. If any field is removed or modified, the guide should be adjusted accordingly.
