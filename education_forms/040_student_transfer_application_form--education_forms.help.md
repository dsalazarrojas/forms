# Student Transfer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is intended to assist students, staff, and administrators in completing the Student Transfer Application Form. The form is used to capture information about a student's transfer between institutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the `Student Name` field.
2. Enter the student's ID number in the `Student ID` field.
3. Select the current institution the student is attending from the `Current Institution` dropdown menu.
4. Select the institution the student wishes to transfer to from the `New Institution` dropdown menu.
5. Provide a brief explanation for the reason of the transfer in the `Student Transfer Reason` field.
6. Select the academic program the student is currently enrolled in from the `Academic Program` dropdown menu.
7. Select the academic level the student is currently at from the `Academic Level` dropdown menu.
8. Enter the student's contact email in the `Student Contact Email` field.
9. Enter the student's contact phone number in the `Student Contact Phone` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's full name as it appears in their records.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, required): Enter the student's ID number as recorded in their institution's database.
* **Current Institution** (`current_institution`, select_one, required): Select the institution the student is currently enrolled in from the dropdown menu.
* **New Institution** (`new_institution`, select_one, required): Select the institution the student wishes to transfer to from the dropdown menu.
* **Student Transfer Reason** (`student_transfer_reason`, note, optional): Provide a brief explanation for the reason of the transfer. This can include academic, administrative, or personal reasons.
* **Academic Program** (`academic_program`, select_one, required): Select the academic program the student is currently enrolled in from the dropdown menu.
* **Academic Level** (`academic_level`, select_one, required): Select the academic level the student is currently at from the dropdown menu.
* **Student Contact Email** (`student_contact_email`, email, required): Enter the student's contact email address.
* **Student Contact Phone** (`student_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the student's contact phone number.

## Tips
Make sure to double-check the student's information for accuracy before submitting the form. Ensure the student has given their consent for the transfer.
