# Invention Contest Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Invention Contest Entry Form is used by individuals who want to participate in the competition by submitting their inventions. It helps gather essential contact and project details for review.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your personal contact information (First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone, and Address).
2. Describe your invention in the text field provided.
3. Select the type(s) of invention you are submitting (if applicable).
4. Choose your preferred contact method for communication (Email, Phone, or Mail).
5. Once you have filled out all the required fields, review your entry before submitting it for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your personal first name to help us identify you.
* **Last Name**: Enter your personal last name to help us identify you.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address we can use to contact you about your invention.
* **Phone**: Enter your primary phone number we can use to contact you about your invention.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Invention Description**: Describe your invention in detail, including its purpose, design, and features.
* **Idea or Invention Type**: Select all types that your invention fits into (if applicable). You can choose multiple types.
* **Contact Method**: Choose how you want to be contacted about your invention (Email, Phone, or Mail).
* **Address**: Enter a valid address we can use for any necessary mail-outs or other contact.

# Tips

* Double-check all fields for accuracy to ensure your entry is valid and can be properly reviewed.
* Use a descriptive text for your invention description to increase its chances of being showcased.
* Choose the right contact method to ensure timely and efficient communication.
