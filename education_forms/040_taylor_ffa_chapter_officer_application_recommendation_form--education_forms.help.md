# taylor_ffa_chapter_officer_application_recommendation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to recommend a student for a chapter officer position in their school.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the teacher's name in the "Teacher Name" field.
2. Enter the teacher's email in the "Teacher Email" field.
3. Enter the supervisor's name in the "Supervisor Name" field.
4. Enter the supervisor's email in the "Supervisor Email" field.
5. Enter the student's ID in the "Student ID" field.
6. Select the chapter officer position the student is applying for using the "Chapter Officer Position" dropdown menu.
7. Enter the start date of the student's chapter officer position in the "Chapter Officer Start Date" field.
8. Enter the end date of the student's chapter officer position in the "Chapter Officer End Date" field.
9. If applicable, enter the student's committee position in the "Committee Position" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Teacher Name (1)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your name as the teacher recommending the student.
* **Teacher Email (2)** (`email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Supervisor Name (3)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the person who will be supervising the student in their chapter officer position.
* **Supervisor Email (4)** (`email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the supervisor.
* **Student ID (5)** (`number`, required/optional): Enter the student's ID number.
* **Chapter Officer Position (6)** (`select_one`, required/true): Select the chapter officer position the student is applying for.
* **Chapter Officer Start Date (7)** (`date`, required/optional): Enter the start date of the student's chapter officer position.
* **Chapter Officer End Date (8)** (`date`, required/optional): Enter the end date of the student's chapter officer position.
* **Committee Position (9)** (`text`, required/optional): If applicable, enter the student's committee position.
