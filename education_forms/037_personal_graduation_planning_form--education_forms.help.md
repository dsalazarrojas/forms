# Personal Graduation Planning Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Graduation Planning Form is a tool for students to outline their academic and professional goals, plans, and objectives for their graduation. It helps students and staff track and achieve these goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Choose your expected graduation year from the "Graduation Year" dropdown menu.
3. Enter your expected graduation date in the "Expected Graduation Date" field.
4. Select your academic program from the provided options.
5. Describe your academic plan in the "Academic Plan" field.
6. Specify your major in the "Major" field.
7. Provide additional information about your academic program in the "Academic Program Details" field.
8. Outline your academic objectives in the "Academic Objectives" field.
9. Create an action plan in the "Action Plan" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (Text): Enter your full name as a student.
* **Graduation Year** (Select One): Choose the year you expect to graduate.
* **Expected Graduation Date** (Date): Enter the date you expect to graduate.
* **Academic Program** (Text): Select the academic program you are pursuing.
* **Academic Plan** (Text): Describe your academic plan, including courses and requirements.
* **Major** (Text): Specify your major or field of study.
* **Academic Program Details** (Text): Provide additional information about your academic program.
* **Academic Objectives** (Text): Outline your academic objectives, such as research, internships, or other goals.
* **Action Plan** (Text): Create a plan to achieve your academic and professional objectives, including steps and deadlines.
