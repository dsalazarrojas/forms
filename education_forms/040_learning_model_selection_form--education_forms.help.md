# Learning Model Selection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the steps to complete the "Learning Model Selection Form" to ensure a successful learning experience for your students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review each field carefully and ensure you have all necessary information before proceeding.
2. Select the appropriate option or enter the required information for each field.
3. Review your selections to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Next Term Planning** (`next_term_planning`, text, required: false): This field allows you to describe your plans for the next term. Please provide a brief overview of your plans and goals for the upcoming term.
* **Learning Model** (`learning_model`, select_one, required: false): Choose the learning model that best suits your needs. The options provided are:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Student Attendance** (`student_attendance`, text, required: false): This field is intended for you to report on student attendance. If you have data, please provide it.
* **Student Attendance Frequency** (`student_attendance_frequency`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the frequency of student attendance that best suits your needs. The options provided are:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
* **Next Term Classes** (`next_term_classes`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the next term classes, if applicable.
* **Class Start Date** (`class_start_date`, time, required: false): Enter the start time of the class, if applicable.
* **Class Duration** (`class_duration`, number, required: false): Enter the duration of the class, if applicable.

## Tips

* Be concise and clear in your responses to ensure accurate selections.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the administrator for guidance.
* Please review your selections carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
