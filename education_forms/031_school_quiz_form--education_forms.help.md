# School Quiz Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Quiz Form is designed to collect information from students about their academic performance and progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Student Name** field, which is the name of the student.
2. Enter the **Class ID** of the student.
3. Enter the student's **Score** achieved in the quiz.
4. Select the **Subject** the student studied for.
5. Choose the **Section** the student belongs to.
6. Enter the **Date** of the quiz.
7. Enter the **Time** of the quiz.
8. Fill in the **School Name** of the student.
9. Provide the student's **Email** address.
10. Enter the student's **Phone** number.
11. Add any additional **Note** about the student.
12. Select the **Assigned Tool** used in the quiz.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the student.
* **Class ID** (`class_id`, number, required: false): Enter the student's class ID.
* **Score** (`score`, number, required: false): Enter the student's score achieved in the quiz.
* **Subject** (`subject_ids`, select_one, required: false): Select the subject the student studied for.
* **Section** (`section_id`, select_one, required: false): Choose the section the student belongs to.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the quiz.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the quiz.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required: false): Fill in the school name of the student.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Provide the student's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes about the student.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required: false): Select the assigned tool used in the quiz.

Please note that all fields are optional except for **Student Name**. The School Quiz Form is designed to collect data on a variety of aspects of student performance and progress, but you can fill in only the fields that are relevant to your specific use case.
