# College Facility & Support Service Satisfaction Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from students on their satisfaction with various facilities and services offered by the college. This feedback will help the college identify areas of improvement and strengths.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Student Name**: If you would like to provide your name, please enter it in this field. This is optional.
2. **Student ID Number**: If you would like to provide your ID number, please enter it in this field. This is optional.
3. **Survey Date**: Please select the date you completed this survey.
4. **Year in School**: Please select your current year in school.
5. **Facilities and Infrastructure**: Rate the overall quality of campus facilities.
   * **Overall Building Condition**: Rate the cleanliness, maintenance, and repairs of buildings.
   * **Classroom Facilities and Equipment**: Rate the technology, seating, and lighting in classrooms.
   * **Library Facilities and Resources**: Rate the collections, computers, and study space available.
   * **Laboratory and Research Facilities**: Rate the equipment quality and availability.
   * **Recreational Facilities**: Rate the gym, sports fields, and fitness centers.
   * **Dining Facilities and Food Quality**: Rate the cafeteria, food options, and cleanliness.
   * **Parking and Transportation**: Rate the availability and convenience of parking and transportation services.
6. **Support Services Quality**:
   * **Academic Advising Services**: Rate the accessibility and helpfulness of counselors.
   * **Mental Health and Counseling Services**: Rate the availability and quality of care.
   * **Tutoring and Academic Support**: Rate the effectiveness and availability of tutoring services.
   * **Career Services and Job Placement**: Rate the resume help, job fairs, and internships.
   * **Student Activities and Clubs**: Rate the variety and engagement opportunities.
   * **Administrative Services**: Rate the registrar, financial aid, and billing services.
   * **Health and Wellness Services**: Rate the clinic, preventive care, and vaccines available.
   * **Campus Security and Safety**: Rate the police presence and emergency response.
7. **Biggest Strength and Weakness**:
   * **Biggest Strength of College**: What does the college do best?
   * **Biggest Area for Improvement**: What needs the most attention?
8. **Additional Feedback or Suggestions**: Please provide any additional feedback or suggestions you may have.
