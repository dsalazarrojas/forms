# CFS Professional Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CFS Professional Application Form is designed for Certified Financial Specialists (CFS) applicants to initiate their certification journey. This form collects necessary information to evaluate and verify the applicant's eligibility for certification.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by entering your **Full Legal Name** in the **Full Legal Name** field. This is a required field.
2.  Enter your **Professional Email** in the **Professional Email** field. This is a required field and will be used for communication purposes.
3.  Provide your **Current Job Title** in the **Current Job Title** field. This is a required field to verify your current position.
4.  Enter your **Current Employer** in the **Current Employer** field. This is a required field to validate your current employment.
5.  Enter the **Years of Professional Experience** in finance/accounting you've had in the **Years of Professional Experience in Finance/Accounting** field. This is a required field to assess your relevant experience.
6.  Select your **Highest Degree Earned** from the options provided in the **Highest Degree Earned** field. This is a required field to ensure you meet the educational requirements.
7.  If applicable, select all certifications you've obtained from the options in the **Current Certifications Held** field. This is an optional field and will be used to evaluate your existing certifications.
8.  Select your desired **Examination Path** from the options provided in the **Desired Examination Path** field. This is a required field to tailor the examination process to your needs.
9.  Choose your **Preferred Examination Window** from the options provided in the **Preferred Examination Window** field. This is a required field to plan your examination schedule.
10. Select **I Agree** to the **Code of Professional Ethics** in the **Code of Professional Ethics** field. This is a required field to demonstrate your commitment to professional ethics.
11. Select **I Consent** to a **Professional Background Verification** in the **Professional Background Verification** field. This is a required field to ensure a background check can be conducted.
12. Acknowledge the **Non-Refundable Application Fee** in the **Non-Refundable Application Fee** field. This is a required field to confirm understanding of the application fee.
13. Enter your supervisor's **Professional Reference (Supervisor) Email** in the **Professional Reference (Supervisor) Email** field. This is a required field to verify your professional references.
14. Sign the form with your **Applicant Digital Signature** in the **Applicant Digital Signature** field. This is a required field to confirm your identity.
15. Select the **Date of Application** in the **Date of Application** field. This is a required field to keep a record of your application submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Legal Name**: Enter your full legal name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Professional Email**: Enter your professional email address for communication purposes.
* **Current Job Title**: Provide your current job title to verify your professional position.
* **Current Employer**: Enter your current employer to validate your employment.
* **Years of Professional Experience in Finance/Accounting**: Enter the number of years you've had experience in finance or accounting.
* **Highest Degree Earned**: Select your highest educational achievement from the provided options.
* **Current Certifications Held**: Select all certifications you've obtained, if any.
* **Desired Examination Path**: Choose the examination path that suits your career goals.
* **Preferred Examination Window**: Select your preferred examination period.
* **Code of Professional Ethics**: Confirm your agreement to the code of professional ethics.
* **Professional Background Verification**: Confirm your consent to a professional background verification.
* **Non-Refundable Application Fee**: Acknowledge the non-refundable application fee.
* **Professional Reference (Supervisor) Email**: Enter your supervisor's email for verification purposes.
* **Applicant Digital Signature**: Sign the form with your full name.
* **Date of Application**: Select the date of your application submission.
