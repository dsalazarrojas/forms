# Dance Rehearsal Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used for tracking and documenting dance rehearsals. This includes scheduling, attendance, notes, and other essential details.

## How To Complete This Form
- To start, ensure you have all the required information and permissions before proceeding.
- Fill out each field with accurate and relevant information.
- Complete as many fields as you need, depending on your requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Title** (1, text, required):
  This field should contain a brief title summarizing the rehearsal or meeting.
- **Category** (2, text, optional):
  Choose the category this rehearsal falls under (e.g., class, workshop, event).
- **Rehearsal Schedule** (3, date, optional):
  Enter the scheduled date and time of the rehearsal.
- **Start Date** (4, date, optional):
  If applicable, enter the start date of the rehearsal.
- **End Date** (5, date, optional):
  If applicable, enter the end date of the rehearsal.
- **Notes** (6, note, optional):
  Add any additional notes or comments about the rehearsal.
- **Attendance** (7, select_one, optional):
  Select the number of attendees or participants.
  - 1: Fewer than 10
  - 2: 10-20
  - 3: 20-30
  - 4: More than 30
- **Important Updates** (8, note, optional):
  Add any important updates or reminders about the rehearsal.
- **Email** (9, email, optional):
  Enter the email address for communication or follow-up.
- **Phone** (10, text, optional):
  Enter the phone number for contact or coordination.
- **Assigned Tool** (11, select_one, optional):
  Choose the tool assigned to this rehearsal.
  - chatjimmy
  - chatjenny
- **Form ID** (12, text, optional):
  This is a unique identifier for the form. You can fill it out or leave it blank.
