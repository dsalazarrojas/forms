# Postdoctoral Fellowship Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from a recommender about a candidate for a postdoctoral fellowship. This form helps the reviewer to evaluate the candidate's potential for the fellowship, considering their academic background, research interests, and other relevant details. **Please note that this form should only be completed by authorized recommenders who know the candidate well.**
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Ensure you are an authorized recommender for the candidate.
2. Fill in the candidate's information on the "Candidate's Information" page.
3. Select the candidate's research interests on the "Research Interests" page.
4. Provide any additional information about the candidate's affiliation and dates in the respective pages.
5. Write a brief evaluation of the candidate on the "Evaluation" page.
6. Submit the form to complete the recommendation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate's Information** (text, required/optional):
	+ This page is for providing general information about the candidate, such as name, title, department, and institution.
* **Affiliation** (text, optional):
	+ This field is for describing the candidate's affiliation with the institution or organization they are currently working with.
* **Research Interests** (select multiple, required):
	+ Select all the research interests that best describe the candidate's expertise and potential research areas.
* **Dates** (text, optional):
	+ This field is for providing any additional dates or timeframes related to the candidate's research experience or projects.
* **Evaluation** (text, required):
	+ Write a brief evaluation of the candidate's potential for the fellowship, including their academic and research abilities.
* **Submission Information** (text, optional):
	+ This field is for providing any additional submission information, such as the submission date or the number of submissions.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your evaluation of the candidate.
* Ensure you are familiar with the candidate's research background and interests before completing this form.
* If you have any doubts or concerns about the candidate, it is best to reach out to the candidate or other recommenders for clarification.
* Please note that your evaluation will be kept confidential and will only be used by the review committee to evaluate the candidate's application.
