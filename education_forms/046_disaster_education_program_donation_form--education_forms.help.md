## Purpose
The "disaster_education_program_donation_form" is designed to collect information from donors who are interested in supporting the organization's disaster education programs. This form is intended for donors to provide their details, including their name, organization, email, phone number, and payment method, in order to process a donation to the disaster education program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the donor's name in the "Donor Name" field (required).
2. Provide the donor's organization name (if applicable) in the "Donor Organization" field (optional).
3. Enter the donor's email address in the "Email" field (required).
4. Enter the donor's phone number in the "Phone" field (optional).
5. Select the desired donation amount in the "Donation Amount" field (required).
6. Choose the payment method from the options provided in the "Payment Method" field (required).
