# Open Access Education Partnership Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register your organization as a partner with our Open Access initiative. By filling out this form, you will be able to access exclusive resources and support to help you achieve your educational goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your information:
	* **First Name**: Enter your personal name as it appears on your official documents.
	* **Last Name**: Enter your surname as it appears on your official documents.
	* **Email**: Enter your institutional email address or your personal email address.
	* **Organization**: Enter the full name of your organization.
	* **Partner Type**: Select the type of organization you represent (Community Initiative, Non-Profit, School, University, or Other).
	* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person submitting this form (this is usually someone from your organization).
	* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the person submitting this form.
	* **Contact Phone**: Enter your direct phone number or the phone number of the person submitting this form.
2. Optionally, provide additional information about your organization and role:
	* **Role**: Enter a brief description of your role within your organization.
	* **Department**: Enter the department or team you belong to within your organization.
	* **Location**: Enter the physical location of your organization.
	* **Website**: Enter the URL of your organization's website.
	* **Notes**: Provide any additional information about your partnership goals or expectations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Your personal name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Your surname as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): The email address you want us to use to contact you.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required): The full name of your organization.
* **Role** (`role`, text, optional): A brief description of your role within your organization.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): The department or team you belong to within your organization.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): The physical location of your organization.
* **Website** (`website`, text, optional): The URL of your organization's website.
* **Partner Type** (`partner_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of organization you represent.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): The name of the person submitting this form.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): The email address of the person submitting this form.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Your direct phone number or the phone number of the person submitting this form.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Any additional information about your partnership goals or expectations.
