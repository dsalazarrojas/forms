# Feedback For Introduction To Dermatology Module Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about the Introduction to Dermatology module. The feedback will be used to refine and enhance the module to better serve the needs of its users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand each question and ensure you're providing accurate and relevant feedback.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability, providing detailed and constructive comments or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Module Feedback** (`module_feedback`, `note`, optional): This field is for any comments or observations you have about the module. Provide specific details about what you liked or disliked about the module.
* **Comment** (`comment`, `text`, optional): Please share any additional comments or suggestions you have about the module. This is an optional field, but your input will help refine the module.
* **Suggest Improvement** (`suggest_improvement`, `text`, optional): Here, you can suggest changes or improvements to the module. Be specific about what you think would make the module better.
* **Suggest Improvement Other** (`suggest_improvement_other`, `text`, optional): If you have a specific improvement suggestion that doesn't fit in the previous field, use this space to share it.
* **Suggest Improvement 2** (`suggest_improvement_2`, `text`, optional): This field is similar to the previous one, providing an opportunity to share additional suggestions or improvements.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional): If applicable, please provide the date you interacted with the module or when you completed this form.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): If you've provided a date, you can also specify the time you interacted with the module. This will help us understand the context of your feedback. 

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when providing feedback or suggestions.
* Focus on the module's functionality, content, or design.
* If you're unsure about anything, please ask for clarification.
* This form is an optional way to provide feedback, so don't worry if you don't have any comments or suggestions to share.
