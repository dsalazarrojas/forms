# Martial Arts Class Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students wishing to enroll in a martial arts class at a local martial arts school.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your information.
2. Choose your preferred class type, level, time, and frequency from the provided options.
3. Select your preferred martial arts school and style.
4. Indicate your experience and certification level in martial arts.
5. If you have any medical conditions, please list them and provide any necessary details.
6. Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms of the form.
7. If you have any special requests or notes for the instructor, please include them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Class Type** (`class_type`, select_multiple, required): Choose the type of martial arts class you wish to take (e.g. martial arts, taekwondo, karate, kickboxing, self-defense).
* **Class Level** (`class_level`, select_one, required): Choose your skill level in martial arts (e.g. beginner, intermediate, advanced).
* **Class Time** (`class_time`, time, required): Choose the time of day you prefer to take the class (e.g. morning, afternoon, evening).
* **Class Frequency** (`class_frequency`, select_one, required): Choose how often you would like to take the class (e.g. once a week, twice a week, thrice a week).
* **Martial Arts School** (`martial_arts_school`, select_one, required): Choose the martial arts school you would like to attend (e.g. school1, school2, school3).
* **Martial Arts Style** (`martial_arts_style`, select_one, required): Choose the style of martial arts you would like to learn (e.g. style1, style2, style3).
* **Martial Arts Level** (`martial_arts_level`, select_one, required): Choose your current level of experience in martial arts (e.g. level1, level2, level3).
* **Martial Arts Experience** (`martial_arts_experience`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you have experience in martial arts (True/False).
* **Martial Arts Certification** (`martial_arts_certification`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you have martial arts certification (True/False).
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter the name of an emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Email** (`emergency_email`, text, required): Enter the email of the emergency contact person.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required): List any medical conditions you have.
* **Medical Condition Details** (`medical_condition_details`, text, required): Provide any necessary details about your medical condition.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms of the form (True/False).
* **Special Request** (`special_request`, text, required): Make any special requests or notes for the instructor.
* **Special Request Details** (`special_request_details`, text, required): Provide any necessary details about your special request.
* **Student Notes** (`student_notes`, text, required): Make any notes or comments for the instructor.
* **Instructor Notes** (`instructor_notes`, text, required): Provide any notes or comments for the instructor.
