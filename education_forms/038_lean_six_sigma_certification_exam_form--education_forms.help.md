# Lean Six Sigma Certification Exam Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users who are undergoing the Lean Six Sigma certification process. It gathers relevant details such as exam date, submission type, and submission status among other fields.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the exam form fields as required.
2. Enter the exam date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Select the submission type and submission status accordingly.
4. Add any contact information you feel is necessary.
5. Upload any submission files required for your certification process.
6. If none of the above fields apply to your situation, you can choose the 'Other' option and provide any additional details in the 'Other' field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Exam Form
* **Exam Form** (`exam_form`, text, optional): This field is a text box where you can enter any relevant information related to your exam form.

### Exam Date
* **Exam Date** (`exam_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your exam in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Exam Time
* **Exam Time** (`exam_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your exam.

### Contact Info
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter any relevant contact information related to your exam.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.

### Submission Type
* **Submission Type** (`submission_type`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you are submitting your exam form.

### Submission Details
* **Submission Details** (`submission_details`, select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you are submitting details related to your exam.

### Submission Status
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_one, optional): Select 'Active' or 'Inactive' to indicate the status of your submission.

### Exam Location
* **Exam Location** (`exam_location`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you took your exam at a location.

### Other
* **Other** (`exam_location_other`, text, optional): If you did not take your exam at a location, provide the details of where you took your exam.

### Submission Status Other
* **Submission Status Other** (`submission_status_other`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the status of your submission.

### Comments
* **Comments** (`submission_comments`, note, optional): Add any comments related to your submission.

### Notes
* **Notes** (`submission_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes related to your submission.

### Submission Files
* **Submission Files** (`submission_files`, text, optional): Upload any files related to your submission.

### File 1
* **File 1** (`submission_file1`, text, optional): Upload the first file related to your submission.

### File 2
* **File 2** (`submission_file2`, text, optional): Upload the second file related to your submission.

### File 3
* **File 3** (`submission_file3`, text, optional): Upload the third file related to your submission.

### Exam Form Other
* **Exam Form Other** (`exam_form_other`, text, optional): If none of the above fields apply, provide any additional details related to your exam form.

### Submission Status Other 2
* **Submission Status Other 2** (`submission_status_other_2`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the status of your submission.

### Other Notes
* **Other Notes** (`submission_notes_other`, note, optional): Add any additional notes related to your submission.

### Other Comments
* **Other Comments** (`submission_comments_other`, note, optional): Add any additional comments related to your submission.

### Other Files
* **Other Files** (`submission_files_other`, text, optional): Upload any additional files related to your submission.

### Other File 1
* **Other File 1** (`submission_file1_other`, text, optional): Upload the first additional file related to your submission.

### Other File 2
* **Other File 2** (`submission_file2_other`, text, optional): Upload the second additional file related to your submission.
