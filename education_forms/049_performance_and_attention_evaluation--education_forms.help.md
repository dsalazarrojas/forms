# Performance And Attention Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from teachers about their students' performance and attention during a teaching session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, the teacher will need to answer a series of questions about the student's performance and behavior in the classroom. This includes questions about the student's abilities, work habits, and any other relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Performance** (1): This field is to be filled in by the teacher with a description of the student's performance during the teaching session.
* **Teacher** (2): This field is to be filled in by the teacher with their name, so that the student's file can be easily identified.
* **Student** (3): This field is to be filled in by the teacher with the student's name, to ensure that the correct student's file is being updated.
* **How many years of teaching experience do you have?** (4): This field is to be filled in by the teacher with their number of years of teaching experience, to provide context for their evaluation.
* **Student Performance** (5): This field is to be filled in by the teacher with a description of the student's performance during the teaching session.
* **Student Attention** (6): This field is to be filled in by the teacher with a description of the student's attention during the teaching session.
* **Teacher Notes** (7): This field is to be filled in by the teacher with any additional comments or notes they may have about the student.
* **Student Notes** (8): This field is to be filled in by the teacher with any additional comments or notes they may have about the student.
* **Other Comments** (9): This field is to be filled in by the teacher with any other comments or notes they may have about the student.
* **Form Submit** (10): This field is a select-one option to indicate whether the teacher has submitted the form or not.
