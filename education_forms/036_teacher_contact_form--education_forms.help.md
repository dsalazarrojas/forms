# Teacher Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a communication tool for parents to contact teachers about their children's education.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Please fill in your name and contact information (Parent Name, Email, and Phone).
2.  Describe the reason for your contact in the text field "Message".
3.  If you are contacting your child's teacher, please fill in their name (Teacher Name) and the name of their school (School Name).
4.  Optionally, select the grade level and class name of your child (Grade Level, Class Name).
5.  Fill in your child's name (Student Name) if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Parent Name** (Parent Name, Text, Optional):
    *   Please enter your name to help the teacher know who is contacting them.
*   **Email** (Email, Email, Optional):
    *   Enter your email address so that the teacher can contact you easily.
*   **Phone** (Phone, Text, Optional):
    *   Enter your phone number to facilitate communication.
*   **Message** (Message, Text, Optional):
    *   Describe the reason for your contact, and the teacher will respond accordingly.
*   **Teacher Name** (Teacher Name, Text, Optional):
    *   If you are contacting your child's teacher, please fill in their name.
*   **School Name** (School Name, Text, Optional):
    *   If you are contacting your child's teacher, please fill in the name of their school.
*   **Grade Level** (Grade Level, Select, Optional):
    *   If applicable, select the grade level of your child.
*   **Class Name** (Class Name, Text, Optional):
    *   If applicable, fill in the name of your child's class.
*   **Student Name** (Student Name, Text, Optional):
    *   If applicable, fill in the name of your child.

## Tips
*   Please be as specific as possible when filling in the "Message" field, so the teacher can respond accordingly.
*   The more information you provide, the better the teacher can assist you.
