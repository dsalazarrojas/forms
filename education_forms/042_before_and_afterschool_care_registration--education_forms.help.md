## Purpose
The Before and Afterschool Care Registration form is designed for parents and guardians to provide information about their child for before- and afterschool care programs. This form is required for enrollment and helps us understand your child's profile, medical history, and emergency contact information. It also includes an enrollment agreement, liability waiver, and consent for photos.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the student's profile information (Student Full Name, Grade Entering, Date of Birth, Gender).
2. Provide the primary guardian's contact information (Primary Guardian Name, Primary Phone Number).
3. Enter emergency contact information (Emergency Contact (Person NOT living with child), Name and Phone).
4. Share your child's medical history and doctor's information (Family Physician Name, Physician Phone Number, List any allergies or chronic medical conditions).
5. Indicate if your child has any dietary restrictions.
6. Agree to the enrollment agreement, liability waiver, and consent for photos.
7. Sign and date the form electronically.
