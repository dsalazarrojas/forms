# Student Led Learning Reform Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for students to register their interest in participating in a student-led learning reform initiative. The collected information will be used by the educational organization to better understand the students' needs, interests, and motivations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your School Name if you have one associated with your participation.
2. Indicate your Role(s) in the initiative (if any).
3. Enter your Grade Level (if applicable).
4. Type in your Program Name (if you have one).
5. Describe your Interests in participating in the initiative.
6. Provide your Motivation for participating.
7. Enter your Participant Name (your name).
8. Enter your School Email (if you have one).
9. Enter your Contact Person's Name (if applicable).
10. Enter your Contact Person's Email (if applicable).
11. Enter your Contact Person's Phone Number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of your school if you have one associated with your participation.
* **Role** (`role`, select_multiple, optional): Check the roles that apply to you, if any.
* **Grade Level** (`grade`, number, optional): Enter your grade level, if applicable.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, optional): If you have a specific program in mind, enter its name here.
* **Interests** (`interests`, text, optional): Describe your interests and why you want to participate in the initiative.
* **Motivation** (`motivation`, text, optional): Explain why you want to participate in the initiative.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, email, optional): Enter your school email, if you have one.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person, if applicable.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the contact person's email, if applicable.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact person's phone number, if applicable.
