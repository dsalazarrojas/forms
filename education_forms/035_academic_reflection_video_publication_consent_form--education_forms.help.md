# Academic Reflection Video Publication Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect your consent for the academic reflection video publication. Please review and complete this form carefully to ensure you understand how your video will be recorded, distributed, and retained.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your name, email address, and institution affiliation.
2. Provide the title and topic of the video.
3. Confirm the date of the video recording.
4. Indicate your consent to video, audio, and image recording.
5. Specify the purpose of the video.
6. Choose your preferred access restriction and video retention period.
7. Add any special requests or conditions.
8. Acknowledge your understanding of withdrawal rights.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name (2)**: Your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address (3)**: Your contact email for communication and future reference.
* **Institution (4)**: Your current or affiliated institution's name.
* **Video Title (5)**: A brief title describing the video's main topic.
* **Video Topic (6)**: A detailed description of the video's main theme or topic.
* **Recording Date (7)**: The date when the video recording will take place.
* **Consent to Video Recording (8)**: Choose "Yes, I consent" to allow video recording.
* **Consent to Audio Recording (9)**: Choose "Yes, I consent" to allow audio recording.
* **Consent to Image Use (10)**: Choose "Yes, I consent" to use your image in the video.
* **Consent to Publication (11)**: Choose "Yes, I consent" or "Yes, with editorial review" for online publication.
* **Consent to Distribution (12)**: Specify how you want the video distributed ("Yes, for educational purposes", "Yes, with restrictions", etc.).
* **Purpose of Video (13)**: Briefly describe the intended purpose of the video.
* **Access Restriction Preferences (14)**: Choose how you want access to the video restricted ("Open to public", "Restricted to institution", etc.).
* **Video Retention Period (15)**: Specify the video retention period ("One semester", "One academic year", etc.).
* **Special Requests or Conditions (16)**: Add any specific requests or conditions for the video.
* **Withdrawal Rights Acknowledged (17)**: Confirm that you understand you can withdraw consent at any time.
