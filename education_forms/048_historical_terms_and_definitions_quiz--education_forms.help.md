# Historical Terms And Definitions Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Historical Terms And Definitions Quiz is designed to test users' knowledge of historical terms and definitions. It is a self-assessment tool that helps users evaluate their understanding of historical events, figures, and concepts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the available questions on historical terms and definitions.
2. Type your answers to each question in the corresponding field.
3. Ensure you answer all required fields (none are marked as required in this form).
4. Submit your completed form to generate your score.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Historical Quiz Form** (history_quiz_form)
This field is where you will enter your answers to the historical quiz questions. Type your answers to each question in this field.

### 2. **Historical Terms**
Enter the historical terms or events you are familiar with.

### 3. **Definitions**
Enter the definitions of the historical terms you entered in the previous field.

### 4. **Historical Events**
Enter any historical events related to the terms you entered.

### 5. **Correct Answers**
Indicate the correct number of correct answers you have given.

### 6. **Incorrect Answers**
Indicate the number of incorrect answers you have given.

### 7. **Total Score**
The total score will be automatically calculated based on your answers.

### 8. **Time Spent**
Indicate the amount of time you spent on the quiz.

### 9. **Date Submitted**
The date and time when you submitted your quiz.

### 10. **Submitted By**
A unique identifier for the user who submitted the quiz.

### Tips
* Take your time and answer each question carefully.
* Review your answers before submitting to ensure accuracy.
* Use your score as a reference to identify areas where you need improvement.
* You can retake the quiz if you feel you need to practice more.
