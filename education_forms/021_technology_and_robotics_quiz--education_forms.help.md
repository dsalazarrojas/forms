# technology_and_robos_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data from participants in a technology and robotics quiz. It is intended for educational purposes, such as gathering feedback or assessing user engagement in a robotics-related course or program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **participant name** in the `participant_name` field.
2. Provide your **email address** in the `participant_email` field.
3. Enter your **phone number** in the `participant_phone` field.
4. Answer the **technology and robotics questions** in the `tech_and_robotics_questions` field.
5. Enter your **correct answers** in the `correct_answers` field.
6. List your **incorrect answers** in the `incorrect_answers` field.
7. Add any additional **comments** about your experience in the `comments` field.
8. If applicable, enter your **participant score** in the `participant_score` field.
9. If applicable, report the time you spent on the quiz in the `time_taken` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name (participant_name)**: Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Participant Email (participant_email)**: Enter your email address for future communication.
* **Participant Phone (participant_phone)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Technology and Robotics Questions (tech_and_robotics_questions)**: Answer the questions as instructed, and provide any necessary explanations or supporting evidence.
* **Correct Answers (correct_answers)**: List the correct answers to the questions, one per line.
* **Incorrect Answers (incorrect_answers)**: List any incorrect answers you entered, one per line.
* **Comments (comments)**: Add any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the quiz.
* **Participant Score (participant_score)**: If applicable, enter your final score from the quiz.
* **Time Taken (time_taken)**: If applicable, report the time you spent on the quiz.

## Tips
* Ensure that your answers are accurate and clear in the **Technology and Robotics Questions** and **Correct Answers** fields.
* Use separate lines for each answer in the **Correct Answers** and **Incorrect Answers** fields.
