# Learning Center Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Learning Center Volunteer Application Form is designed to collect information from potential volunteers to determine if they are a good fit for the Learning Center's programs.

## How To Complete This Form
- To begin, fill in your first name, surname, and email address in the provided fields.
- Select your availability and interests from the respective dropdown menus.
- Provide a brief user note if you have any additional information you'd like to share.
- If you have any previous experience, education, or skills that you think would be beneficial for the Learning Center, select them from the respective dropdown menus.
- Finally, agree to the terms and conditions and confirm your start and end dates for your volunteer commitment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User First Name** (`user_first_name`, `text`, Optional): This field is used to enter your first name. It's used as a reference point for the Learning Center to contact you.
* **User Last Name** (`user_last_name`, `text`, Optional): This field is used to enter your surname. It's used as a reference point for the Learning Center to contact you.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, Optional): Enter your email address to ensure that we can contact you regarding your volunteer opportunities.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, Optional): If you have a phone number, enter it here so that we can contact you in case of emergencies.
* **Availability** (`availability`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select one or more of the following options to indicate your availability:
	+ Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday
	+ Weekends
	+ Other (please specify)
* **User Note** (`user_note`, `note`, Optional): Provide any additional information you'd like to share with us, such as any relevant experience, education, or skills.
* **Volunteer Interests** (`volunteer_interests`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select one or more of the following options to indicate your interests:
	+ Teaching
	+ Learning
	+ Community service
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Volunteer Education** (`volunteer_education`, `select_one`, Optional): Select one of the following options to indicate your highest level of education:
	+ High school
	+ Bachelor's degree
	+ Master's degree
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Volunteer Experience** (`volunteer_experience`, `select_one`, Optional): Select one of the following options to indicate your volunteer experience:
	+ 0-1 year
	+ 1-3 years
	+ 3-5 years
	+ More than 5 years
* **Volunteer Skills** (`volunteer_skills`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select one or more of the following options to indicate your skills:
	+ Leadership
	+ Communication
	+ Teamwork
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Volunteer Reference** (`volunteer_reference`, `select_one`, Optional): Select one of the following options to indicate if you have a volunteer reference:
	+ Available
	+ Not available
* **Agreed to Volunteer** (`agreed_to_volunteer`, `select_one`, Required): Select "Agreed to Volunteer" to confirm your commitment to volunteering with the Learning Center.
* **Agreed to Terms** (`agreed_to_terms`, `select_one`, Required): Select "Agreed to Terms" to confirm that you understand and agree to the terms and conditions of volunteering with the Learning Center.
* **Volunteer Start Date** (`volunteer_start_date`, `date`, Optional): If you have a preferred start date for your volunteer commitment, enter it here.
* **Volunteer End Date** (`volunteer_end_date`, `date`, Optional): If you have a preferred end date for your volunteer commitment, enter it here.
* **Volunteer Commitment** (`volunteer_commitment`, `note`, Optional): Provide a brief description of your commitment to volunteering with the Learning Center.
* **Agreed to Sure** (`agreed_to_sure`, `select_one`, Optional): Select "Agreed to Sure" to confirm that you understand that volunteering with the Learning Center is a commitment to regularly participate and be available for scheduled shifts.
* **Agreed to Terms 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9** (`agreed_to_terms_2`, `agreed_to_terms_3`, `agreed_to_terms_4`, `agreed_to_terms_5`, `agreed_to_terms_6`, `agreed_to_terms_7`, `agreed_to_terms_8`, `agreed_to_terms_9`, `select_one`, Optional): Select "Agreed to Terms" for each of the respective terms to confirm that you understand and agree to the terms and conditions of volunteering with the Learning Center.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and honestly.
- If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, contact us for support.
- Review the terms and conditions carefully before agreeing to them.
- Make sure to provide your start and end dates clearly to avoid any confusion.
