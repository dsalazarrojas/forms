# work_experience_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your work experience, including job title, employer, dates of employment, job hours, industry, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select all relevant work experiences by choosing the corresponding checkboxes next to each job.
2. Enter your job title in the job_title field.
3. Enter your employer's information in the job_employer field.
4. Provide the dates you started and ended working at the job in the job_start_date and job_end_date fields, using the date format MM/DD/YYYY.
5. Enter the number of hours you worked at the job in the job_hours field.
6. Check the job_industry field to indicate whether your job is in a certain industry (yes/no).
7. Enter your employer's phone number in the job_employer_phone field.
8. Provide a reference or contact person in the job_reference field.
9. Describe your job duties and responsibilities in the job_duties field.
10. Enter your job location in the job_location field.
11. Check the job_education_relevant_to_occupation field to indicate whether your education is relevant to your job (yes/no).
12. If your education is not relevant, provide a reason in the job_education_relevant_to_occupation_other field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **work_experience** (`select_multiple`): Select all relevant work experiences by choosing the corresponding checkboxes next to each job.
* **job_title** (`text`): Enter your job title, e.g., software developer, data analyst, manager.
* **work_employer** (`text`): Enter your employer's name, e.g., company name or organization name.
* **job_start_date** (`date`): Provide the start date of your employment in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **job_end_date** (`date`): Provide the end date of your employment in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **job_hours** (`number`): Enter the number of hours you worked at the job per week.
* **job_industry** (`select_one`): Check whether your job is in a certain industry (yes/no).
* **job_employer_phone** (`text`): Enter your employer's phone number.
* **job_reference** (`note`): Provide a reference or contact person.
* **job_duties** (`text`): Describe your job duties and responsibilities.
* **job_location** (`text`): Enter your job location, e.g., city, state, country.
* **job_education_relevant_to_occupation** (`select_one`): Check whether your education is relevant to your job (yes/no).
* **job_education_relevant_to_occupation_other** (`text`): If your education is not relevant, provide a reason in this field.
