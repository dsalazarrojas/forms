# Interdisciplinary Pedagy Incubator Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants interested in joining an interdisciplinary pedagogy incubator program. The data collected will help the program administrators to understand the participants' interests, goals, and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully read the instructions and ensure you have all the required information before starting the form.
- Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
- Use the provided examples or instructions as a guide for formatting and inputting data.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Details
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required): Enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself that you feel are essential to share with the program administrators.

### Interests
* **Interests** (`interests`, select_multiple, required): Select all the interests that align with your goals and the program's focus areas. You can choose multiple options.

### Consent
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required): Indicate your consent to participate in the incubator program by selecting "Agree".

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address that you regularly check and use for communication.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Provide your phone number that you can be reached at.

### University
* **University** (`university`, text, required): Enter the name of the university where you are affiliated or studying.

### Teaching Center
* **Teaching Center** (`teaching_center`, text, required): Identify the teaching center or department you are associated with.

### Department
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Specify the department you are part of within the university.

### Contact Person
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Provide the name and title of the contact person who can be reached for more information.

### Contact Phone
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.

### Contact Email
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.

### Participant Notes
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, text, optional): Share any additional information about yourself that you feel is relevant to your participation in the program.

### University Address
* **University Address** (`university_address`, text, optional): Provide the physical address of the university.

### Participant Address
* **Participant Address** (`participant_address`, text, optional): Enter your personal address for communication or logistics purposes.

### University Contact Info
* **University Contact Info** (`university_contact_info`, text, optional): Provide additional contact information for the university.

### Participant Agreement
* **Participant Agreement** (`participant_agreement`, text, optional): Write a short statement outlining your understanding and commitment to the program's terms and conditions.

### Incubator Name
* **Incubator Name** (`incubator_name`, text, required): Choose the name of the incubator program you wish to join from the provided list.

### Incubator Location
* **Incubator Location** (`incubator_location`, text, required): Select the location of the incubator.

### Incubator Duration
* **Incubator Duration** (`incubator_duration`, date, required): Choose the duration of the incubator program (e.g., one week, two months, etc.).

### Incubator Start Date
* **Incubator Start Date** (`incubator_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the incubator program.

### Incubator End Date
* **Incubator End Date** (`incubator_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the incubator program.

### Incubator Description
* **Incubator Description** (`incubator_description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the incubator's goals and activities.

### Incubator Participants
* **Incubator Participants** (`incubator_participants`, text, required): List the number and names of participants in the incubator program.

### Participant Goals
* **Participant Goals** (`participant_goals`, text, required): Outline your personal goals and objectives for participating in the incubator.

### Incubator Goals
* **Incubator Goals** (`incubator_goals`, text, required): Outline the incubator's goals and objectives.

Please note that all fields marked as "required" are mandatory and must be filled out accurately. Ensure you have all the necessary information before submitting the form.
