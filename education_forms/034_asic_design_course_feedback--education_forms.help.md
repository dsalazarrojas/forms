# ASIC Design Course Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from students who have taken the ASIC Design course. Please take a few minutes to complete this form to help us improve the course.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in your name and email (if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up).
2. Select the course you took and provide its code.
3. Rate the overall quality of the course, its content relevance, and materials quality.
4. Provide feedback on the instructor's knowledge and teaching effectiveness.
5. Share your experience with practical application opportunities and assignment difficulty.
6. Choose a pace that best describes your experience with the course.
7. Indicate how likely you are to recommend this course to others.
8. If you have any suggestions for improving the course, please type them in below.
9. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please feel free to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your official record or ID card.
* **Student Email**: Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up or further information. This is optional.
* **Course Name**: Select the name of the course you took from the list below.
* **Course Code**: Enter the course code or identifier provided by the course administrator.
* **Instructor Name**: Enter the name of the instructor who taught the course.
* **Semester/Term**: Choose the semester or term during which you took the course.
* **Overall Course Rating**: Choose how you rate the overall quality of the course (Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, Very Poor).
* **Course Content Relevance**: Rate the relevance of the course content to ASIC design (Very Relevant, Relevant, Somewhat Relevant, Not Very Relevant, Not Relevant).
* **Course Materials Quality**: Rate the quality of the course materials provided (Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, Very Poor).
* **Instructor Knowledge**: Rate the instructor's knowledge about ASIC design (Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, Very Poor).
* **Instructor Teaching Effectiveness**: Rate how effectively the instructor presented the material (Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, Very Poor).
* **Practical Application Opportunities**: Choose how many opportunities you had to apply concepts practically (Abundant, Sufficient, Adequate, Insufficient, None).
* **Assignment Difficulty**: Rate the difficulty level of the assignments (Too Easy, Appropriate, Challenging, Too Difficult, Extremely Difficult).
* **Course Pacing**: Choose how you experienced the course pace (Too Fast, Appropriate, Too Slow).
* **Recommendation Likelihood**: Choose how likely you are to recommend this course to others (Very Likely, Likely, Neutral, Unlikely, Very Unlikely).
* **Course Improvement Suggestions**: Share any ideas you have to improve the course.
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any other feedback or suggestions, please feel free to share them.
