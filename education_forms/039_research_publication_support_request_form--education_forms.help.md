# Research Publication Support Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Research Publication Support Request Form is designed to support researchers in managing their research publications. The form helps to track and update information about research publications, including their status, due dates, and assigned personnel.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the category of the research publication from the list.
2. Provide a brief description of the research publication.
3. Select the research group associated with the publication.
4. Select the type of research publication (research article, research report, or other).
5. Specify the manuscript status (active or inactive).
6. If the manuscript is active, provide more details about its status.
7. Specify the manuscript format (true or false).
8. Provide the length of the manuscript.
9. Specify the language of the manuscript.
10. Provide more details about the manuscript format.
11. Determine if attachments are required for the manuscript.
12. Add any comments about the attachments.
13. Specify if the attachments are required (true or false).
14. Select the person assigned to review or update the manuscript.
15. Select a due date and time for the manuscript.
16. Provide the current status of the publication.
17. Add any status updates for the publication.
18. Specify the due date and time for the publication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category**: Select the category of the research publication. This can be "Research Article", "Research Report", or "Other".
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of the research publication.
* **Research Group**: Select the research group associated with the publication. This can be "Research Group A", "Research Group B", or "Other".
* **Research Publication Type**: Select the type of research publication. This can be "Research Article", "Research Report", or "Other".
* **Manuscript Status**: Specify the status of the manuscript. This can be "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Manuscript Status Details**: If the manuscript is active, provide more details about its status.
* **Manuscript Format**: Specify if the manuscript format is true or false.
* **Manuscript Length**: Provide the length of the manuscript.
* **Manuscript Language**: Specify the language of the manuscript. This can be "English" or "Other".
* **Manuscript Format Details**: Provide more details about the manuscript format.
* **Attachments Required**: Determine if attachments are required for the manuscript.
* **Attachments Comments**: Add any comments about the attachments.
* **Attachments**: Specify if attachments are required (true or false).
* **Assigned To**: Select the person assigned to review or update the manuscript. This can be "User A", "User B", or "Other".
* **Assigned Date**: Select a due date for the manuscript.
* **Assigned Time**: Select a due time for the manuscript.
* **Status**: Specify the current status of the publication. This can be "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Status Update**: Add any status updates for the publication.
* **Due Date**: Specify the due date for the publication.
* **Due Time**: Specify the due time for the publication.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about the publication.
