# CPKING Presentation Proposal Entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CPKING Presentation Proposal Entry form is designed to collect information from potential speakers for the CPKING conference. This form helps us assess your presentation proposal and determine its suitability for the conference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide your professional title or position and your organization or institution affiliation.
3. Write a brief professional biography, including a summary of your credentials.
4. Describe your presentation, including its title, abstract or summary, and the expected duration of the presentation.
5. Specify the learning outcomes and the target audience for your presentation.
6. Choose the presentation track or category that best fits your presentation.
7. Mention any special equipment or room requirements.
8. Indicate if handout or resource materials will be available.
9. Confirm if this is your first time presenting at CPKING.
10. Mention any previous conference presentations you have done.
11. Share your motivation for presenting at CPKING.
12. Confirm your agreement to present at the conference if selected.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Presenter Full Name** (`presenter_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as the primary contact person for this proposal.
* **Email Address** (`presenter_email`, email, required): This is your primary email address for communication.
* **Phone Number** (`presenter_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for follow-up communication.
* **Organization or Institution** (`presenter_organization`, text, required): Specify your affiliation with an educational institution or organization.
* **Professional Title or Position** (`presenter_title`, text, optional): Provide your professional title or position, if applicable.
* **Professional Biography** (`presenter_bio`, text, required): Write a brief summary of your credentials and expertise.
* **Co-Presenters** (`co_presenters`, text, optional): If you are proposing with other presenters, list their names and email addresses.
* **Presentation Title** (`presentation_title`, text, required): Enter a title for your presentation that reflects its content.
* **Presentation Description** (`presentation_description`, text, required): Provide an abstract or summary of your presentation content.
* **Requested Presentation Duration** (`presentation_duration`, select_one, required): Choose the duration of your presentation, which should be 30, 45, 60, 90 minutes, or 2 hours.
* **Learning Outcomes** (`learning_outcomes`, text, required): Specify what the audience will learn from your presentation.
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, select_multiple, required): Choose the target audience for your presentation, which could be educators, administrators, healthcare professionals, business professionals, technology professionals, or the general public.
* **Presentation Track or Category** (`presentation_track`, select_one, required): Select the track or category that best fits your presentation.
* **Special Equipment or Room Requirements** (`special_requirements`, text, optional): If your presentation has any special requirements, list them here.
* **Handout or Resource Materials Available** (`handout_available`, select_one, optional): Specify if you will be providing handouts or resource materials.
* **First Time Presenter at CPKING** (`first_time_presenter`, select_one, optional): Confirm if this is your first time presenting at CPKING.
* **Previous Conference Presentations** (`previous_conferences`, text, optional): If you have previously presented at a conference, mention it here.
* **Why Do You Want to Present** (`motivation`, text, required): Share your motivation or perspective for presenting at CPKING.
* **I Agree to Present** (`consent_agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree to present at the conference if selected.
* **Proposal Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your proposal submission.
