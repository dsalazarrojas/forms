# Evening School Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from students applying to an evening school program. The purpose of this form is to gather details about the student's school and education information, contact details, and application status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the **Student Info** section:
	* **Student Info (student_info)**: Enter your name and any other relevant student information.
	* **School Info (school_info)**: Enter your school's name and any other relevant school information.
	* **Contact Info (contact_info)**: Enter your contact details so we can reach you.
2. Confirm your **Program Options (school_programs)**: Select the programs you are interested in.
3. Enter your **Education Info (education_info)**: Provide details about your education background.
4. Confirm your **Student Programs (student_programs)**: Select the programs you are interested in.
5. Provide your **Application Date (application_date)**: Enter the date of your application.
6. Confirm your **Application Time (application_time)**: Enter the time of your application.
7. Enter any additional **Application Notes (application_note)**: Provide any other information that you think is relevant to your application.
8. Confirm the **Application Status (application_status)**: Select your current application status.
9. Review and confirm all information before submitting your application.
