# University Workshop Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about university workshops, including check-in details, event records, follow-up activities, and attendance statistics. It helps workshop organizers and administrators to efficiently track and manage workshop-related events.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:
1. Select the relevant pages to fill out, depending on the specific workshop event.
2. Complete each field according to the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Check In**: Provide a brief description of the workshop check in.
  * (Text field): Enter a short text describing the workshop check in.
  * Required: No

- **Follow-ups**: Check if there are any follow-up activities or meetings scheduled for this workshop.
  * (Select One: Yes/No): Select 'Yes' if follow-ups are scheduled, 'No' otherwise.
  * Required: No

- **Event Records**: Add any additional event-related comments or records.
  * (Note field): Enter any relevant information about the event.
  * Required: No

- **University Name**: Indicate if this workshop is associated with a university.
  * (Select Multiple: Yes/No): Select 'Yes' if the workshop is associated with a university, 'No' otherwise.
  * Required: No

- **Date and Time**: Enter the date and time of the workshop check in.
  * (Date field): Enter the date of the workshop check in (DD/MM/YYYY).
  * (Time field): Enter the time of the workshop check in (HH:MM).
  * Required: No

- **Contact Number**: Enter the contact number of the workshop attendees.
  * (Text field): Enter the phone number of the workshop attendees.
  * Required: No

- **Email**: Enter the email of the workshop attendees.
  * (Email field): Enter the email address of the workshop attendees.
  * Required: No

- **Workshop Attendance**: Indicate the number of attendees at the workshop.
  * (Number field): Enter the number of people who attended the workshop.
  * Required: No

- **Comments**: Add any comments or feedback from workshop attendees.
  * (Note field): Enter any comments or feedback from workshop participants.
  * Required: No

- **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or information about the workshop.
  * (Note field): Enter any additional comments or information about the workshop.
  * Required: No

- **Follow-up Date**: Enter the date of the next follow-up activity or meeting.
  * (Date field): Enter the date of the follow-up activity (DD/MM/YYYY).
  * Required: No

- **Follow-up Time**: Enter the time of the next follow-up activity or meeting.
  * (Time field): Enter the time of the follow-up activity (HH:MM).
  * Required: No

- **Follow-up Frequency**: Indicate the frequency of the follow-up activities.
  * (Number field): Enter the number of follow-up activities scheduled.
  * Required: No

- **University Workshop Name**: Enter the name of the university workshop.
  * (Text field): Enter the name of the workshop.
  * Required: No

- **Workshop Type**: Indicate the type of workshop.
  * (Select One: Yes/No): Select 'Yes' if the workshop is of a specific type, 'No' otherwise.
  * Required: No

- **Workshop Location**: Enter the location of the workshop.
  * (Text field): Enter the location of the workshop.
  * Required: No

- **Attendees**: Indicate if attendees are available to receive follow-up activities.
  * (Select Multiple: Yes/No): Select 'Yes' if attendees are available, 'No' otherwise.
  * Required: No

- **Workshop Topic**: Enter the topic of the workshop.
  * (Text field): Enter the topic of the workshop.
  * Required: No

- **Workshop Organizer**: Enter the name of the workshop organizer.
  * (Text field): Enter the name of the workshop organizer.
  * Required: No

- **University Organizer**: Enter the name of the university organizer.
  * (Text field): Enter the name of the university organizer.
  * Required: No

- **Notes**: Add any additional notes about the workshop.
  * (Note field): Enter any additional notes about the workshop.
  * Required: No

- **Follow-up Status**: Indicate the status of the follow-up activities.
  * (Select One: Active/Inactive): Select 'Active' if follow-up activities are ongoing, 'Inactive' otherwise.
  * Required: No

- **University Follow-up Status**: Indicate the status of the university follow-up activities.
  * (Select One: Active/Inactive): Select 'Active' if university follow-up activities are ongoing, 'Inactive' otherwise.
  * Required: No

- **Follow-up Next**: Enter the date and time of the next follow-up activity.
  * (Text field): Enter the date and time of the next follow-up activity.
  * Required: No

- **Follow-up Last**: Enter the date and time of the last follow-up activity.
  * (Text field): Enter the date and time of the last follow-up activity.
  * Required: No
