# new_vendor_setup_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **new_vendor_setup_form** is a form designed to collect information about new vendors. This form is used to register a new vendor and set up their account details. Please ensure that the form is filled out accurately and completely to avoid any issues with the vendor's account creation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct event information from the provided options.
2. Choose the type of booth you represent (Vendor Booth or Vendor Table).
3. Enter the name of your booth.
4. Enter the size of your booth (optional).
5. Enter your goals for participating in the event (optional).
6. Enter your name and title (Vendor Representative) (optional).
7. Enter your contact number (optional).
8. Enter your email address (optional).
9. Enter your address (optional).
10. Enter your city (optional).
11. Enter your zip code (optional).
12. Select your state (optional).
13. Enter a brief description of your booth (optional).
14. Enter your payment terms (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Info**: Select the event you are representing your vendor for (e.g., Event A, Event B, or Event C).
* **booth_type**: Choose whether you have a Vendor Booth or a Vendor Table.
* **booth_size**: Enter the size of your booth (only if you have a booth).
* **Goals**: Enter your goals for participating in the event (e.g., selling products, networking).
* **Name**: Enter your name (if you are the main contact).
* **Vendor Representative**: Enter the title of the person representing your vendor (e.g., John Doe, Sales Manager).
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number for communication.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact and communication.
* **Address**: Enter your business address for delivery and shipping.
* **City**: Enter your city for billing and delivery purposes.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code for billing and delivery purposes.
* **State**: Select your state for tax purposes.
* **Description**: Enter a brief description of your booth and products/services.
* **payment_terms**: Enter any special payment terms or requirements (e.g., COD, payment on delivery).

Note: The form is optional for most fields, but please fill out as much information as possible to ensure accurate vendor account creation.
