# Teacher Input For Educational Plan - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help teachers gather information and provide feedback on a student's learning style, IEP goals, and recommended interventions or recommendations. The purpose of this form is to ensure that teachers have a clear understanding of the student's needs and can provide targeted support to help the student succeed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and title as the teacher completing this form.
2. Provide your student's name and grade level.
3. Select the student's learning style (hands-on, visual, auditory, or kinesthetic).
4. Enter any IEP goals or objectives for the student.
5. Provide any recommended interventions or recommendations for the student.
6. Enter any comments or feedback you may have for the student.
7. Date and time the form was completed.
8. Enter your contact information (email, phone number, and fax number).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, not required): Please enter your name as the teacher completing this form.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, not required): Please enter the student's name.
* **Student Grade Level** (`student_grade_level`, select_one, not required): Please select the student's grade level (1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th, 5th, 6th, 7th, 8th, 9th, 10th, 11th, 12th, postgraduate, or other).
* **Student Grade Level Other** (`student_grade_level_other`, text, not required): Please enter any other grade level if the student is not in a traditional grade level.
* **Learning Style** (`learning_style`, select_multiple, not required): Please select the student's learning style (hands-on, visual, auditory, or kinesthetic).
* **IEP Goal** (`iep_goal`, text, not required): Please enter any IEP goals or objectives for the student.
* **IEP Interventions** (`iep_interventions`, text, not required): Please describe any recommended interventions or recommendations for the student.
* **IEP Recommendations** (`iep_recommendations`, text, not required): Please enter any IEP recommendations for the student.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, not required): Please provide any comments or feedback you may have for the student.
* **Date Started** (`date_started`, date, not required): Please enter the date you started working with the student.
* **Time Started** (`time_started`, time, not required): Please enter the time you started working with the student.
* **Date Ended** (`date_ended`, date, not required): Please enter the date you ended working with the student.
* **Time Ended** (`time_ended`, time, not required): Please enter the time you ended working with the student.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Please enter your phone number.
* **Fax** (`fax`, text, not required): Please enter your fax number.
* **Contact Preferred** (`contact_preferred`, text, not required): Please enter your preferred contact method (e.g., phone, email, or fax).
* **Email Preferred** (`email_preferred`, text, not required): Please enter your preferred email address.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, not required): Please enter any additional comments or feedback for the student.
* **Teacher Signature** (`teacher_signature`, note, not required): Please sign your name as the teacher completing this form.
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, note, not required): Please sign your name as the student.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, not required): Please enter your name as the teacher.
* **Teacher Title** (`teacher_title`, text, not required): Please enter your title (e.g., teacher, instructor, etc.).
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, not required): Please enter the name of your school.
