# Preschool Student Skills Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help educators assess a child's skills in various areas, including cognitive, social, and emotional development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and select the answer that best describes the child's behavior or ability.
2. Select only one option for select_one questions, and multiple options for select_multiple questions.
3. Answer all questions, even if you are unsure or have limited information.
4. Do not leave any questions blank, as this may affect the accuracy of the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the student's first name?** (`general_knowledge`, `text`, required)
  This question is to gather basic information about the student. Please enter the student's first name.
  * Hint: Try to spell the name correctly

  This question assesses the student's basic math concepts, such as understanding numbers and quantity.

* **Does the student understand the sounds of letters** (`language_comprehension`, `select_one`, required)

* **Does the student display self-awareness** (`emotional_intelligence`, `select_one`, required)

## Tips

* Always answer the questions based on observations and evidence.
* Be honest and accurate in your assessment.
* Use the assessment results to inform instruction and tailor interventions to the student's needs.
* Review the assessment form regularly to track progress and adjust the assessment as needed.
