# Parent Consultation Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parent Consultation Appointment Form is designed to collect necessary information from parents to schedule and conduct consultations for their children's education. Please use this form to provide accurate and up-to-date details about your child and the consultation appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure everything is correct.
3. Make sure to fill in all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Information** (`parent_consultation_appointment_form_parent_info`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your child and any relevant details about their education.
* **Scheduling** (`parent_consultation_appointment_form_scheduling`, text, optional): Share any specific scheduling requests or preferences.
* **Notes** (`parent_consultation_appointment_form_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or concerns you have.
* **Parent Consultation Date** (`parent_consultation_appointment_form_parent_consultation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to schedule the consultation.
* **Parent Consultation Time** (`parent_consultation_appointment_form_parent_consultation_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you would like to schedule the consultation.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_consultation_appointment_form_parent_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as a parent/guardian.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_consultation_appointment_form_parent_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_consultation_appointment_form_parent_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Parent Notes** (`parent_consultation_appointment_form_parent_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or concerns.
* **Teacher Information** (`parent_consultation_appointment_form_teacher_info`, text, optional): Provide any relevant information about your child's teacher or class.
* **Scheduling Information** (`parent_consultation_appointment_form_scheduling_info`, text, optional): Share any additional scheduling details or requests.

## Tips
- Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Ensure that you provide a valid email address and contact phone number.
- If you have any concerns or questions, contact your child's teacher or school administrator for assistance.
