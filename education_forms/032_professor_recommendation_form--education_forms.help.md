# professor_recommendation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from professors who wish to recommend students for academic or professional opportunities. It is a crucial step in the evaluation process, providing valuable insights and recommendations from educators who have worked with the student.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Ensure you have all necessary information and supporting documentation before starting.
*   Fill in the student's name as it appears on their university ID or passport.
*   Enter the student's ID number as it appears on their university ID or other official documents.
*   Describe your relationship to the student (e.g., professor, advisor, mentor).
*   Enter the date of your evaluation, if applicable. This is optional.
*   Write a detailed recommendation for the student, highlighting their strengths and accomplishments.
*   If necessary, upload supporting documentation for further reference.
*   Enter your own name and position for verification purposes.
*   Enter your institution or organization name and department for verification purposes.
*   Enter your title and office number for contact information purposes.
*   Optionally, add any further comments about your evaluation or the student.
*   Finally, acknowledge that you are submitting this form and are aware of the institution's policies regarding academic integrity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **student name** (Student Name, Text, Required): Enter the name of the student you are recommending.
*   **student id** (Student ID, Number, Required): Enter the ID number as it appears on their university ID or other official documents.
*   **professor name** (Professor's Name, Text, Required): Enter your name or title as it appears on your university ID or other official documents.
*   **relationship to student** (Relationship to Student, Text, Required): Describe your relationship to the student (e.g., professor, advisor, mentor).
*   **evaluation date** (Evaluation Date, Date, Optional): Enter the date of your evaluation, if applicable.
*   **recommendation** (Recommendation, Text, Required): Write a detailed recommendation for the student, highlighting their strengths and accomplishments.
*   **supporting document** (Supporting Document, Note, Optional): If necessary, upload supporting documentation for further reference.
*   **submitters name** (Submitter's Name, Text, Required): Enter your name for verification purposes.
*   **submitters email** (Submitter's Email, Email, Required): Enter your email address for verification purposes.
*   **submitters phone** (Submitter's Phone, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for contact information purposes.
*   **submitters institution** (Submitter's Institution, Text, Required): Enter your institution or organization name for verification purposes.
*   **submitters department** (Submitter's Department, Text, Optional): Enter your department for verification purposes.
*   **submitters position** (Submitter's Position, Text, Optional): Enter your job title for contact information purposes.
*   **submits title** (Submitter's Title, Text, Optional): Enter your title or position for verification purposes.
*   **submits office number** (Submitter's Office Number, Text, Optional): Enter your office phone number for contact information purposes.
*   **submits signature** (Submitter's Signature, Text, Optional): Enter your signature for verification purposes.
*   **submits comment** (Submitter's Comment, Text, Optional): Optionally, add any further comments about your evaluation or the student.
*   **submits acknowledgement** (Submitter's Acknowledgement, Text, Optional): Optionally, acknowledge that you are submitting this form and are aware of the institution's policies.
*   **submitter 1 name** (Submitter 1 Name, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the first co-submitter or verifier.
*   **submitter 1 phone** (Submitter 1 Phone, Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the first co-submitter or verifier.
*   **submitter 2 name** (Submitter 2 Name, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the second co-submitter or verifier.
*   **submitter 2 phone** (Submitter 2 Phone, Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the second co-submitter or verifier.
*   **submitter 3 name** (Submitter 3 Name, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the third co-submitter or verifier.
*   **submitter 3 phone** (Submitter 3 Phone, Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the third co-submitter or verifier.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and thoroughly, as this information will be used to support the student's application.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authorities or department for clarification.
*   Remember to save your draft regularly to avoid losing any progress.
*   If you are experiencing technical difficulties, please contact our support team for assistance.
