# online_course_idea_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect online course ideas from users, helping the course creation team understand what types of courses are of interest to learners and what topics they would like to learn.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your information as a submitter in the "submitter_info" field.
2.  Provide a detailed description of the course you would like to see online in the "course_info" field.
3.  Specify the date and time when you would like to take the course in the "submit_date" and "submit_time" fields respectively.
4.  If you have a preferred name for the course, enter it in the "course_name" field.
5.  Enter a brief description of the course content in the "course_description" field.
6.  Enter your email address in the "submitter_email" field.
7.  Enter your phone number in the "submitter_phone" field.
8.  Select one or more categories for your course idea in the "category" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **submitter_info**: Enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself as the submitter.
*  **course_info**: Describe the course you would like to see online in detail.
*  **submit_date**: Enter the date when you would like to start or take the course.
*  **submit_time**: Enter the time of day when you would like to take the course.
*  **course_name**: Enter a name for the course, if you have one in mind.
*  **course_description**: Enter a brief description of what you want to learn or achieve through the course.
*  **submitter_email**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you.
*  **submitter_phone**: Enter your phone number so that we can contact you.
*  **category**: Select one or more categories for your course idea, such as "Online Courses" or "Other".
