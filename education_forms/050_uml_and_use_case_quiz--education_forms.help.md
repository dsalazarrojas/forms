# UML and Use Case Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The UML and Use Case Quiz form is designed to test your knowledge on Unified Modeling Language (UML) and use cases. This form is a series of multiple-choice questions and answer fields that aim to assess your understanding of UML and use cases.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the username field with your name.
* Enter your email address in the email field.
* Read each question carefully and select the most accurate answer from the options provided.
* For questions with no options, type your answer in the corresponding answer field.
* Click the "submit" button to submit your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **username** (text, required): This field is where you enter your name.
* **email** (email, required): This field is where you enter your email address.
* **question1**, **question2**, **question3**, ..., **question16** (text, optional): These fields are multiple-choice questions related to UML and use cases. Read each question carefully and select the most accurate answer from the options provided.
* **answer1**, **answer2**, **answer3**, ..., **answer6** (text, optional): These fields are where you enter your answers to the corresponding questions.
* **submit** (text, required): This button is used to submit your answers to the form.
