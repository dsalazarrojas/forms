# Student Clubs Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Clubs Survey is a form designed to collect information about student clubs at the school. This form helps administrators and teachers gather data on each club's purpose, activities, and other relevant details to make informed decisions about club management and resource allocation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting a club from the list of student clubs at your school.
2. For each field, provide accurate and detailed information about the club.
3. Make sure to fill out all fields with accurate information, and do not leave any section blank.
4. If you are unsure about a field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is the purpose of the club** (1): Briefly describe the main goal or objective of the club. This will help administrators understand the club's focus.
* **What activities does the club offer** (2): List the types of activities or programs offered by the club. This will help identify the club's scope and impact.
* **How many students are currently part of the club** (3): Enter the current number of students participating in the club. This will help administrators assess the club's popularity.
* **What is the current condition of the club** (4): Select from the options: Active, Inactive, or Disbanded. This will indicate the club's status.
* **Contact teacher name** (5): Enter the name of the teacher in charge of the club. This is the main point of contact for club-related queries.
* **Contact teacher email** (6): Enter the email address of the teacher in charge of the club. This will help administrators and teachers communicate with the club.
* **Contact teacher phone number** (7): Enter the phone number of the teacher in charge of the club. This will help in emergency situations or urgent queries.
* **When are you available to meet with the students** (8): Enter the date and time when the teacher is available to meet with the students.
* **School year this club started** (9): Enter the school year the club began. This will help track the club's longevity.
* **How long does the club meet** (10): Enter the number of hours the club meets per session. This will help in scheduling and resource allocation.
* **How many days a week does the club meet** (11): Enter the frequency of club meetings per week. This will help in scheduling and resource allocation.
* **Where does the club meet** (12): Select from the options: School Library, School Auditorium, or Other. This will help identify the club's location.
* **Who is the club sponsor** (13): Select from the options: Teacher, School Administration, School Counselor, or Other. This will help understand the club's support structure.
* **What is the student club interest** (14): Select from the options: Academic, Social, Sports, Arts, or Clubs. This will help administrators and teachers understand the club's focus.
* **How often does the club meet** (15): Select from the options: Daily, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually. This will help in scheduling and resource allocation.
* **Club status** (16): Select from the options: Active, Inactive, or Disbanded. This will help understand the club's status.
* **What school department is the club affiliated with** (17): Select from the options: English, Mathematics, Science, History, or Other. This will help identify the club's subject area.
* **Club description** (18): Provide a brief description of the club. This will help administrators and teachers understand the club's scope.
* **Teacher supervisor** (19): Select from the options: Teacher, School Administration, School Counselor, or Other. This will help understand the club's leadership structure.
* **Student club leadership** (20): Select from the options: Student President, Faculty Advisor, or None. This will help identify the club's leadership structure.
* **Club meeting time** (21): Enter the time of the club's meetings. This will help in scheduling and resource allocation.
* **School year this club ended** (22): Enter the school year the club ended. This will help track the club's history.
* **What is the source of the club's funding** (23): Select from the options: School Administration, Student Government, Parent Volunteers, or Other. This will help understand the club's financial backing.
* **What percentage of students participate in this club** (24): Enter the percentage of students participating in the club. This will help in assessing the club's impact.
* **Additional comments** (25): Provide any additional information or comments about the club. This will help administrators and teachers understand the club's unique aspects.
