# Digital Classrom Innovation Grant Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for the Digital Classroom Innovation Grant application process. It will help us understand your project's details and assess its eligibility for funding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your project's title in the "Project Title" field.
2. Provide a brief description of your project in the "Project Description" field.
3. If applicable, enter your project's budget in the "Project Budget" field.
4. Specify whether your project has a budget frequency (e.g., yearly, monthly) in the "Budget Frequency" field.
5. If your budget is not in USD or EUR, please specify the budget amount in the "Budget Amount" field and choose "Other" as the budget currency in the "Budget Currency" field. If you select "Other" as the budget currency, please enter the name of the currency in the "Budget Currency Other" field.
6. If your project has supporting documents, please attach them to the "Supporting Documents" field.
7. Enter the title of your supporting documents in the "Supporting Documents Title" field.
8. Describe your supporting documents in the "Supporting Documents Description" field.
9. Enter your project's start and end dates in the "Project Start Date" and "Project End Date" fields respectively.
10. Specify your project's start and end times in the "Project Start Time" and "Project End Time" fields respectively.
11. Enter your project's location in the "Project Location" field.
12. If your project location is not one of the standard options, please enter the name of your location in the "Project Location Other" field.
13. Enter your project contact information in the "Project Contact" field.
14. Enter your project contact's email in the "Project Contact Email" field.
15. Enter your project contact's phone number in the "Project Contact Phone" field.
16. Specify your project's status (e.g., active, inactive) in the "Project Status" field.
17. If your project status is not one of the standard options, please enter the name of your status in the "Project Status Other" field.
18. Enter any comments or notes about your project in the "Project Comments" and "Project Notes" fields.
19. Finally, describe your project's implementation in the "Project Implementation" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Title (project_title)**: Enter the title of your project.
* **Project Description (project_description)**: Enter a brief description of your project.
* **Project Budget (project_budget)**: If your project has a budget, enter the amount here.
* **Budget Frequency (project_budget_frequency)**: Specify whether your project has a budget frequency (e.g., yearly, monthly).
* **Budget Amount (project_budget_amount)**: Enter the budget amount if it's different from the standard currencies (USD, EUR).
* **Budget Currency (project_budget_currency)**: Specify the budget currency (e.g., USD, EUR, Other).
* If you select "Other" as the budget currency, please enter the name of the currency in the **Budget Currency Other (project_budget_currency_other)** field.
* **Budget Frequency Other (project_budget_frequency_other)**: Enter the name of your project's budget frequency if it's not one of the standard options.
* **Supporting Documents (supporting_documents)**: Attach any supporting documents for your project.
* **Supporting Documents Title (supporting_documents_title)**: Enter the title of your supporting documents.
* **Supporting Documents Description (supporting_documents_description)**: Describe your supporting documents.
* **Project Start Date (project_start_date)**: Enter your project's start date.
* **Project End Date (project_end_date)**: Enter your project's end date.
* **Project Start Time (project_start_time)**: Specify your project's start time.
* **Project End Time (project_end_time)**: Specify your project's end time.
* **Project Location (project_location)**: Enter your project's location.
* If your project location is not one of the standard options, please enter the name of your location in the **Project Location Other (project_location_other)** field.
* **Project Contact (project_contact)**: Enter your project contact's name.
* **Project Contact Email (project_contact_email)**: Enter your project contact's email.
* **Project Contact Phone (project_contact_phone)**: Enter your project contact's phone number.
* **Project Status (project_status)**: Specify your project's status (e.g., active, inactive).
* If your project status is not one of the standard options, please enter the name of your status in the **Project Status Other (project_status_other)** field.
* **Project Comments (project_comments)**: Enter any comments or notes about your project.
* **Project Notes (project_notes)**: Enter any additional notes about your project.
* **Project Implementation (project_implementation)**: Describe your project's implementation.
