# Release Of Academic Records Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Release Of Academic Records Form" is designed to allow users to release their academic records for educational purposes. This includes applications to schools, universities, or other institutions for academic verification or certification.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are filling out this form for the correct purpose, such as an application to a school or university.
2. Select the correct category for your request from the dropdown menu.
3. Provide a brief description of the reason you are requesting to release your academic records.
4. Confirm that the release of your academic records is authorized.
5. Add any additional notes or comments, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Release Of Academic Records Form**: Enter the title of the form to be completed. This field is a text input and is optional.
* **category**: Select the correct category for your request from the following options:
	+ education forms
	+ Other
* **description**: Provide a brief description of the reason you are requesting to release your academic records. This field is a text input and is optional.
* **Authorized Release Of Academic Records Form**: Confirm that the release of your academic records is authorized. Select 'True' to confirm authorization.
* **notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your request. This field is a text area and is optional.

## Tips

- Be careful when selecting the category to ensure you choose the correct option for your request.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please feel free to ask for clarification.
- Remember to carefully read through the entire form before submitting to ensure accuracy. 

Note: The fields marked as "required" should be filled out in order to submit the form correctly.
