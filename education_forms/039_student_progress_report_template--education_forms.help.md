# Student Progress Report Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help instructors and educators track student progress in a structured and standardized way. It's a template that can be filled out for each student, providing a comprehensive view of their performance, progress, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have all the necessary information and data before starting to fill out the form.
2.  Complete each field with the required information, starting from the top and working your way down.
3.  Use the provided hints and label explanations to help clarify any unclear fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the student's full name.
* **Assignment** (`assignment_id`, number, optional): Enter the ID of the assignment or project the student is working on. 
*   **Assignment Date** (`assignment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the assignment was started or due.
*   **Teacher** (`teacher_name`, text, optional): Enter the teacher's name who is handling the student's progress.
*   **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, text, optional): Enter the student's current grade level or status.
*   **Progress Notes** (`progress_notes`, note, optional): Add any relevant notes or comments about the student's progress.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the student's email address if available.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the student's phone number if available.
*   **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, optional): Select the relevant category or categories that best describe the student's progress or area of study.
*   **Subcategory** (`subcategory`, text, optional): Enter a subcategory or specific area of study within the selected category.
*   **Progress Status** (`progress_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the current status of the student's progress (In Progress, Completed, Not Started).
*   **Due Date** (`due_date`, date, optional): Enter the due date for the assignment or project.
*   **Created At** (`created_at`, time, optional): Record the time the progress report was created.
*   **Modified At** (`modified_at`, time, optional): Record the time the progress report was last modified.
*   **Assigned At** (`assigned_at`, time, optional): Record the time the progress report was assigned or created.
*   **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, optional): Enter the student's unique ID number.
*   **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, optional): Select the category or categories that best describe the student's progress.

**Tips**

*   Make sure to review and update the form regularly to ensure the student's progress is accurately reflected.
*   Use clear and concise language when filling out the form to avoid confusion.
*   Ensure that the form is completed in a consistent format to maintain accuracy and fairness among students.
