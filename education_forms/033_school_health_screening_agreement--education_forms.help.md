# School Health Screening Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Health Screening Agreement form is designed to collect personal health information from school staff members. This form helps schools to identify and manage potential health concerns, ensuring a safe and healthy work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your **Staff Name** in the provided text field.
2. Select your **Staff Title** from the available options (if any are provided).
3. Enter your **School Name**, which is likely to be the name of the school where you work.
4. Choose the **Screening Date**, which is the date of the screening process.
5. Select the **Screening Time**, which is the time of the screening.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Staff Name** (staff_name): Enter your full name as it is known by your employer or colleagues.
* **Staff Title** (staff_title): Enter your job title or position within the school.
* **School Name** (school_name): Enter the name of the school where you are employed.
* **Screening Date** (screening_date): Enter the date of the screening process, in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Screening Time** (screening_time): Select the time of the screening process, using the 24-hour clock format (e.g., 08:00 for 8am).
