# Student Exchange Program Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a leave of absence from the Student Exchange Program. Fill out the form to provide necessary information about your leave, which will help the administration process your request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Go to the first page of the form.
2. Fill out the "Student Information" section with your student details.
3. Move to the second page of the form.
4. Provide your contact details in the "Contact Details" section.
5. In the "Leave Application" section, describe your reason for taking a leave of absence.
6. Select the type of leave you are applying for (Academic, Administrative, or Other).
7. Enter the start and end dates of your leave in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields, respectively.
8. In the "Student ID" field, provide your student ID number.
9. Enter your student's name in the "Student Name" field.
10. Specify the department you are associated with in the "Department" field.
11. Review your form before submission to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Information (exchange_program_details)**: Provide your student details, such as any relevant background information.
* **Contact Details (contact_details)**: Fill out your contact information, including your name and email.
* **Leave Application (leave_application)**: Describe your reason for taking a leave of absence.
* **Leave Type (leave_type)**: Select the type of leave you are applying for (Academic, Administrative, or Other).
* **Start Date (start_date)**: Enter the date you wish to start your leave.
* **End Date (end_date)**: Enter the date you wish to end your leave.
* **Student ID (student_id)**: Enter your student ID number.
* **Student Name (student_name)**: Enter your student's name.
* **Department (department)**: Specify the department you are associated with.

## Tips
* Ensure you have filled out all required fields before submission.
* Review your form before submitting it to avoid errors.
* Attach any necessary supporting documentation, if required.
