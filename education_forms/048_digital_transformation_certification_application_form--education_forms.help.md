# Digital Transformation Certification Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Digital Transformation Certification Application Form is designed to collect information about individuals seeking digital transformation certification. This form helps ensure that all relevant data is collected accurately and efficiently for review and approval purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the First Name and Last Name fields.
2. Provide a valid Email address in the Email field.
3. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
4. Describe your Education and any relevant experience in the Education and Experience fields.
5. Share your Motivation for seeking digital transformation certification in the Motivation field.
6. Select one of the available options for Select One.
7. Select multiple relevant options for Select Multiple.
8. If applicable, enter a date for the Date field.
9. Choose your preferred Time for the Time field.
10. Review your entries and click Submit to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted for further communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted for further communication.
* **Education** (`education`, `text`, required: false): Describe your educational background and any relevant qualifications.
* **Experience** (`experience`, `text`, required: false): Describe any relevant experience you have in the field of digital transformation.
* **Motivation** (`motivation`, `text`, required: false): Share your motivation for seeking digital transformation certification.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose one of the available options for this field.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select multiple relevant options for this field.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): If applicable, enter a date.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Choose your preferred time.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required: false): Review your entries and click Submit to submit the form.

## Tips

* Be as accurate and specific as possible when filling out the form.
* Use clear and concise language in your entries.
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form to avoid errors.
