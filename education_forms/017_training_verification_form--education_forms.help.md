# training_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to verify training information for team members. It helps to collect data on their training status, performance, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information before starting the form.
2. Select the relevant options for each field based on the team member's training status.
3. Fill in the required fields accurately and thoroughly.
4. Provide any additional comments or information in the "Comments" field if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Member** (id: 1, text, required: false): Enter the team member's name.
* **Has Received Training** (id: 2, select_one, required: false): Select 'True' if the team member has received the training, 'False' otherwise.
* **Training Date** (id: 3, date, required: false): Enter the date the team member received the training.
* **Training Time** (id: 4, time, required: false): Enter the duration of the training.
* **Job Performance** (id: 5, select_multiple, required: false): Select how the team member's job performance improved/same/worse after the training.
	+ improved: The team member's job performance improved after the training.
	+ same: The team member's job performance remained the same after the training.
	+ worse: The team member's job performance worsened after the training.
* **Training Location** (id: 6, text, required: false): Enter the location where the team member received the training.
* **Training Instructor** (id: 7, text, required: false): Enter the name of the training instructor.
* **Comments** (id: 8, note, required: false): Provide any additional comments or information about the training.
* **E-mail** (id: 9, email, required: false): Enter the team member's email address.
* **Phone** (id: 10, text, required: false): Enter the team member's phone number.

## Tips
- Ensure you have the team member's consent to complete this form.
- If the team member has not received the training, please select 'False' in the "Has Received Training" field.
- If the team member's job performance improved, select "improved" in the "Job Performance" field.
