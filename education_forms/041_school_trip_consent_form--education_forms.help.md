# School Trip Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The School Trip Consent Form is designed to collect necessary information and signatures from students, guardians, and school authorities for school trips. This form aims to ensure that all parties involved are aware of and agree to the trip's details, including travel dates, destination, and other relevant information. By completing this form, students and guardians provide their consent and assurance that they are aware of the trip's terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. For the fields marked as "required," ensure that you complete them before proceeding.
3. Review the form carefully to ensure that all information is correct and complete.
4. Sign and date the fields marked for signatures (Student Signature, Guardian Signature, and School Signature).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
1. **Student Name** (`student_name`, Text, Required): Enter the student's full name who will be participating in the trip.
2. **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, Number, Required): Enter the student's contact number for communication purposes.
3. **Student DOB** (`student_dob`, Date, Required): Enter the student's date of birth.
4. **School Name** (`school_name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the school or institution.
5. **Trip Dates** (`trip_dates`, Date, Required): Enter the dates of the trip.
6. **Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, Text, Required): Enter the guardian's full name.
7. **Guardian Contact Number** (`guardian_number`, Number, Required): Enter the guardian's contact number.
8. **Trip Destination** (`trip_destination`, Text, Optional): Enter the destination of the trip.
9. **Mode Of Transport** (`mode_of_transport`, Text, Optional): Enter the mode of transport used for the trip.
10. **Number Of Students** (`number_of_students`, Number, Required): Enter the number of students participating in the trip.
11. **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
12. **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, Number, Optional): Enter the emergency contact number.
13. **School Email** (`school_email`, Email, Optional): Enter the school's email address.
14. **Student Attendance** (`student_attendance`, Note, Optional): Enter any relevant notes about the student's attendance.
15. **Guardian Relationship** (`guardian_relationship`, Select One, Optional): Choose the guardian's relationship to the student (Mother, Father, Brother, Sister, or Other).
16. **Has Guardian Agreed** (`guardian_agreed`, Text, Optional): Check if the guardian has agreed to the trip.
17. **Trip Duration** (`trip_duration`, Number, Optional): Enter the duration of the trip.
18. **Travel Details** (`travel_details`, Text, Optional): Enter any other relevant travel details.
19. **Accommodation** (`accommodation`, Text, Optional): Enter the type of accommodation used during the trip.
20. **School Authority** (`school_authority`, Text, Optional): Enter the school authority responsible for the trip.
21. **Student Authority** (`student_authority`, Text, Optional): Enter the student's authority or permission given to participate in the trip.
22. **Has Student Agreed** (`trip_agreed`, Text, Optional): Check if the student has agreed to the trip.
23. **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, Date, Optional): Enter the student's signature date.
24. **Guardian Signature** (`guardian_signature`, Date, Optional): Enter the guardian's signature date.
25. **School Signature** (`school_signature`, Date, Optional): Enter the school's signature date.

## Tips
* Double-check all fields for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in.
