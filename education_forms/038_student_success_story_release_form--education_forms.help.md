# Student Success Story Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Success Story Release Form is designed to collect information about students' achievements and stories of success, allowing schools to share and showcase their students' accomplishments. This form is intended for students to share their personal stories, and by filling it out, you will be granting permission for your story to be published and shared.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Student Name** in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter the name of your **School** in the "School Name" field.
3. Provide your **School Email** address to ensure that we can contact you about your story.
4. Briefly describe your **Story** in the "Story" field, telling us about your accomplishments and achievements.
5. Choose which types of **Publication Permissions** you are comfortable with (yes/no).
6. If applicable, add your **Parent Signature** in the "Parent Signature" field.
7. Finally, select the **Date** you are submitting this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_success_story_release_form_1`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as a student.
* **School Name** (`student_success_story_release_form_2`, text, required/optional): Enter your school name.
* **School Email** (`student_success_story_release_form_3`, email, required/optional): Enter your school email address.
* **School** (`student_success_story_release_form_4`, text, required/optional): Enter your school name.
* **Story** (`student_success_story_release_form_5`, text, required/optional): Share your personal story of success.
* **Publication Permissions** (`student_success_story_release_form_6`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose which types of publications you are comfortable with (yes/no).
* **Parent Signature** (`student_success_story_release_form_7`, text, required/optional): If applicable, add your parent's signature.
* **Date** (`student_success_story_release_form_8`, date, required/optional): Select the date you are submitting this form.

## Tips
Make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure that your story can be properly shared and published. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
