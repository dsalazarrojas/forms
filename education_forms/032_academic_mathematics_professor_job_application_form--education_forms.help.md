# Academic Mathematics Professor Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help academic institutions or departments gather information from candidates applying for a mathematics professor position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your full legal name.
2. Enter your contact email address for correspondence.
3. Describe your current institution or affiliation.
4. Enumerate your qualifications and degrees obtained.
5. State your areas of mathematical research interest.
6. Summarize your teaching experience, including courses taught and levels.
7. List your selected publications and major grants.
8. Provide contact details of referees (academic references).
9. Paste a short summary or copy of your cover letter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Candidate name**: Enter your full legal name as it appears on your official documents.
  (Type: text)
- **Contact email**: Enter your email address that is not publicly available on the internet or your personal social media profiles.
  (Type: email)
  (Required: Yes)
- **Current institution**: Describe your current institution or affiliation (e.g., university, research center).
  (Type: text)
  (Required: No)
- **Qualifications and degrees**: List your highest qualification and degree obtained (e.g., PhD, MSc, Bachelor's).
  (Type: text)
  (Required: Yes)
- **Research interests**: Describe your areas of mathematical research interest.
  (Type: text)
  (Required: Yes)
- **Teaching experience**: Summarize your teaching experience, including courses taught and levels.
  (Type: text)
  (Required: Yes)
- **Publications and grants**: List your selected publications and major grants.
  (Type: text)
  (Required: No)
- **References**: Provide contact details of your referees (academic references).
  (Type: text)
  (Required: No)
- **Cover letter summary**: Paste a short summary or copy of your cover letter.
  (Type: text)
  (Required: No)

## Tips
- Please ensure that all information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
- Use proper grammar and spelling in your responses.
- Proofread your form responses before submission.
- You can use this form as a template for your own job applications.
