# Graduate Student Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from graduate students about their demographic information, satisfaction levels, and feedback on services provided.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your introduction in the text field on the first page.
2. Fill in your demographic information, such as your demographic_info (a numerical value, e.g., 1-10).
3. Answer the question about the quality of service provided (Service Quality).
4. Choose one or more options to describe your level of satisfaction (satisfaction).
5. Provide your recommendation in the text field (recommendation).
6. (Optional) Add any comments about your experience (comments).
7. Answer the question about your level of satisfaction with the program (program_satisfaction).
8. Enter your email address (email).
9. (Optional) Provide your phone number (phone).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `text`, required): Please provide a brief introduction about yourself.
* **Demographic information** (`demographic_info`, `number`, required): Please enter a numerical value between 1-10.
* **Service Quality** (`Service Quality`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose one or more of the following options:
	+ Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Not Satisfied
	+ Very Satisfied
* **Recommendation** (`recommendation`, `text`, required): Please provide your recommendation about our services.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): This is an optional field for you to provide any additional comments about your experience.
* **Program Satisfaction** (`program_satisfaction`, `number`, required): Please answer by rating your satisfaction level (1-10).
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Please enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): This is an optional field for you to provide your phone number.
