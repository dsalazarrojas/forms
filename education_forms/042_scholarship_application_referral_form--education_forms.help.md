# scholarship-application-referral-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The scholarship application referral form is used to collect information for a scholarship application, to be reviewed and considered by the scholarship committee.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand the required and optional fields.
2. Complete the "Applicant Details" section with the applicant's information.
3. Select the referral information for the applicant from the options provided.
4. Provide contact information for the referee.
5. Write a short message to the referee to ask for their recommendation.
6. Enter the scholarship award amount, if applicable.
7. Enter the applicant's school graduation date.
8. Write a message to the scholarship committee explaining why the applicant is deserving of the award.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Details** (`applicant_details`, text, required): Enter the applicant's name, email address, and any other relevant details.
* **Referral Information** (`referral_info`, select_one, optional): Select the referral information from the options provided to describe the applicant's relationship to the referee.
* **Referee Contact** (`referee_contact`, text, optional): Enter the contact information for the referee, including their name and email address.
* **Referee Information** (`referee_info`, text, optional): Write a short description of the referee's position and qualifications.
* **Scholarship Award** (`scholarship_award`, number, optional): Enter the scholarship award amount, if applicable.
* **School Graduation Date** (`school_graduation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the applicant graduated from school.
* **Referee Recommendation** (`referee_recommendation`, text, optional): Write a brief message to the referee asking for their recommendation and providing more information about the applicant.
* **Scholarship Committee Review** (`scholarship_committee_review`, time, optional): You may enter a brief message to explain why you believe the applicant is deserving of the award, but this is not required.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
* If a field is marked as required, please ensure it is completed.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the scholarship committee for clarification.
* Double-check the form before submitting to ensure all fields are filled out correctly.
* You can save and come back to the form later, if needed.
