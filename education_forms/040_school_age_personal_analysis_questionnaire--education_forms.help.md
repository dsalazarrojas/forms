# school_age_personal_analysis_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a student's academic performance, interests, and demographics to provide an accurate and comprehensive analysis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Start by reading the Introduction section, which provides an overview of the form's purpose and instructions.
2. Fill in the student's name in the "student_name" field.
3. Select the student's grade level from the "school_year" field.
4. Choose the student's favorite subjects in the "student_subjects" field. Select all applicable subjects from the list.
5. If the student has other interests not listed, enter them in the "student_subjects_other" field.
6. Select the student's interests from the "student_interest" field. Select all applicable interests from the list.
7. Enter the teacher's name in the "teacher_name" field.
8. Enter the teacher's email in the "teacher_email" field.
9. Enter the teacher's phone number in the "teacher_phone" field.
10. Enter any additional notes in the "teacher_notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: A brief overview of the form's purpose and instructions.
* **student_name**: Enter your name to identify who is completing the form.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
* **school_year**: Select your current grade level.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: yes
* **student_grade**: Not applicable as it seems to be a duplicate field.
* **student_subjects**: Select all your favorite subjects. 
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: yes
* **student_subjects_other**: If you have other interests not listed, enter them here.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
* **student_interest**: Select all your interests.
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Required: no
* **school_subjects**: Select all your favorite subjects. This field seems to be a duplicate of "student_subjects".
* **school_subjects_other**: Not applicable as it seems to be a duplicate field of "student_subjects_other".
* **school_year**: Select your current grade level.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: yes
* **teacher_name**: Enter your name to identify who is completing the form.
* **teacher_email**: Enter your email address.
* **teacher_phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **teacher_notes**: Enter any additional notes.
