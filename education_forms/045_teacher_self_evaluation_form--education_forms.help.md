# <string> - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to evaluate and rate a teacher's performance, self-reflection on goals, areas of improvement, and other relevant attributes. This form is designed to be completed by teachers to provide a self-evaluation of their performance and identify areas that need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, fill in the form completely and honestly. Make sure to review each section carefully before submitting the form.
2.  Answer each question thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3.  Ensure that you have filled in all required fields (indicated by the asterisk \*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher Performance Rating** (`teacher_performance_rating`, number, required):
    This field asks you to rate your performance on a scale of 1-10.
* **Self Reflection on Goals** (`self_reflection_on_goals`, text, required):
    This field is for you to write down your reflections on your goals and how you've met them.
* **Areas of Improvement** (`areas_of_improvement`, select_multiple, required):
    Select all areas where you need improvement and provide a brief explanation.
* **Comments** (`teacher_comments`, note, required):
    Write down any additional comments or observations about your performance.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required):
    Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required):
    Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Teaching Methods** (`teaching_methods`, select_one, optional):
    Select the teaching methods you use (e.g., Online Classes, In-person Classes).
* **Teaching Hours** (`teaching_hours`, number, required):
    Report the number of teaching hours you've worked.
* **Self Evaluation Form Completion Date** (`self_evaluation_form_completion_date`, date, required):
    Fill in the date and time you completed this form.
* **Evaluation Frequency** (`evaluation_frequency`, select_one, optional):
    Select how often you are evaluated (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
* **Self Evaluation Form Completion Time** (`self_evaluation_form_completion_time`, time, required):
    Report the time you spent completing this form.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, optional):
    Select who submitted this form (e.g., Admin, Peer, Self).
* **Review Status** (`review_status`, select_one, optional):
    Select the status of this review (e.g., Pending, Approved, Rejected, In Progress).

Note: This help guide is designed to be comprehensive and clear, but if you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to reach out to your administrator or support team.
