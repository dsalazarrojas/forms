# University Research Program Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to refer potential applicants to the university's research programs, allowing them to apply to a variety of programs based on their interests and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **University ID**. This is a unique identifier for your university.
2. Enter your **Department Name**. This is the department you are interested in applying to.
3. Enter your **Contact First Name**. This is the first name of the person you would like to be contacted by.
4. Enter your **Contact Last Name**. This is the last name of the person you would like to be contacted by.
5. Enter your **Contact Email**. This is the email address of the person you would like to be contacted by.
6. Choose a **Departmental Inquiries Time** for your department. This will determine when your inquiry will be reviewed by the department.
7. Choose a **Departmental Inquiries** date for your department. This will determine when your inquiry will be reviewed by the department.
8. Enter the **Research Program** you are interested in applying to. This will help us determine the best fit for you.
9. Enter the **Research Area** you are interested in applying to. This will help us determine the best fit for you.
10. If you have **Faculty Recommendations** for your program, please enter them here. These can be any relevant notes or information about your faculty contacts.
11. If you have **Advisor Recommendations** for your program, please enter them here. These can be any relevant notes or information about your advisor contacts.
12. Choose additional **Submission Options** for your program. This will help us determine the best fit for you.
13. If you have additional **Submission Options 2**, please choose them here. This will help us determine the best fit for you.
14. Review and submit your form for review by the university's research committee.

* **University ID**: This is a unique identifier for your university. It is not required but helps us determine your affiliation.

* **Departmental Inquiries**: This is the date when your department will be reviewed. Choose a date that works best for you.

* **Research Program**: This is the research program you are interested in applying to. Choose the one that best fits your needs.
* **Research Area**: This is the research area you are interested in applying to. Choose the one that best fits your needs.
