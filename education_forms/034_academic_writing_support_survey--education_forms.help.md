# Academic Writing Support Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from students, faculty, and staff about their experience with the institution's writing support services.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Fill out the form completely and truthfully to provide valuable feedback.
*   Please answer each question carefully and provide specific examples when possible.
*   You can select all options that apply to each question.
*   You can also provide additional comments at the end of the form if you have more to say.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Your role**: Select your current role at the institution.
    + Undergraduate Student
    + Graduate Student
    + Faculty
    + Staff
*   **Department or major**: Type your current department or major.
*   **Have you used the institutions writing support services?**: Select how often you have used the services.
    + Yes - regularly
    + Yes - a few times
    + Yes - once
    + No - but I am interested
    + No - I was not aware of them
*   **Which writing support services have you used (select all that apply)?**: Choose the services you have used.
    + Writing center consultations
    + Online writing tutoring
    + Writing workshops or seminars
    + Citation and reference help
    + ESL writing support
    + Thesis or dissertation writing group
    + None
*   **How satisfied are you with the writing support services you have used?**: Select your level of satisfaction.
    + Very dissatisfied
    + Dissatisfied
    + Neutral
    + Satisfied
    + Very satisfied
    + Have not used any
*   **How helpful was the feedback you received from writing support staff?**: Select how helpful you found the feedback.
    + Not helpful at all
    + Slightly helpful
    + Moderately helpful
    + Very helpful
    + Extremely helpful
    + Not applicable
*   **What are your biggest writing challenges (select all that apply)?**: Choose the challenges you face.
    + Grammar and mechanics
    + Organizing ideas
    + Developing arguments
    + Academic tone and style
    + Citation formatting
    + Time management for writing
    + Writer block or motivation
*   **What format do you prefer for writing support?**: Select your preferred format.
    + One-on-one in-person consultation
    + Online one-on-one session
    + Group workshop
    + Self-paced online resources
    + Email feedback on drafts
*   **How easy is it to schedule a writing support appointment?**: Select how easy or difficult it is to schedule.
    + Very difficult
    + Difficult
    + Neutral
    + Easy
    + Very easy
    + Never tried to schedule
*   **What additional writing support services would you find valuable?**: Provide a brief text describing the services you think would be helpful.
*   **How much has writing support improved your academic writing?**: Select the impact of writing support.
    + No impact
    + Slight improvement
    + Moderate improvement
    + Significant improvement
    + Transformative improvement
    + Have not used services
*   **Would you recommend writing support services to other students?**: Select whether you would recommend.
    + Yes - definitely
    + Yes - with some reservations
    + Neutral
    + False
*   **Any additional feedback or suggestions for improving writing support?**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
    + Please keep them as brief and specific as possible.
