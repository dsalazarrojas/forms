# After School Permission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain permission for your child to participate in the after-school program. It provides a way for you to give your consent for your child's participation in the program and to provide important information about your child and their emergency contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student information section with your child's details.
2. Provide your name and contact information in the parent/guardian section.
3. Answer the medical information and permission questions.
4. Choose the emergency contact person and their details.
5. Sign the form to confirm your permission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Student Information
* **Student Full Name** (`student_full_name`): Enter your child's complete legal name.
* **Student Date of Birth** (`student_date_of_birth`): Enter your child's date of birth.
* **Current Grade Level** (`student_grade`): Select your child's current grade level.
* **School Attended** (`student_school`): Enter the name of your child's school.
* **Student ID Number** (`student_id_number`): If applicable, enter your child's student identification number.

### 2. Program Information
* **After-School Program Name** (`program_name`): Enter the name of the after-school program.
* **Program Dates** (`program_dates`): Enter the start and end dates of the program.
* **Program Schedule** (`program_schedule`): Enter the days and times of the program.

### 3. Parent/Guardian Information
* **Parent/Guardian Full Name** (`parent_guardian_name`): Enter your complete legal name.
* **Relationship to Student** (`parent_guardian_relationship`): Select your relationship to the student.
* **Email Address** (`parent_guardian_email`): Enter your email address for program updates and communication.
* **Phone Number** (`parent_guardian_phone`): Enter your primary phone number for contact.

### 4. Emergency Contact Information
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`): Select the relationship of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`): Enter the phone number for the emergency contact person.

### 5. Permission and Authorizations
* **Program Participation Permission** (`program_participation_permission`): Give permission for your child to participate in the after-school program.
* **Field Trip Permission** (`field_trip_permission`): Give permission for your child to participate in field trips related to the program.
* **Photo and Video Permission** (`photo_video_permission`): Give permission for your child to be photographed or filmed during program activities.
* **Discipline Authorization** (`discipline_authorization`): Authorize program staff to use appropriate discipline measures as outlined in the program guidelines.
* **Transportation Method** (`transportation_method`): Select the method your child will use to get home from the program.
* **Authorized Adults** (`authorized_adults`): If applicable, list other authorized adults to pick up your child.
* **Pickup Instructions** (`pickup_instructions`): Enter any special instructions for picking up your child.

### 6. Signature and Date
* **Parent/Guardian Signature** (`parent_guardian_signature`): Sign to confirm your permission.
* **Date of Signature** (`signature_date`): Enter the date when the permission is granted.

Please note that some fields are optional but are included for completeness and potential future use. Ensure that all required fields are filled accurately and completely before submitting the form.
