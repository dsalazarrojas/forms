# summer_school_enrollment_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for enrolling a student in a summer school program. The form collects information from the student and their parent or guardian.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Select the relevant options for the student's parent information (name, contact details).
2. Enter the student's name.
3. Enter the student's date of birth.
4. Choose the program details (program type, start and end dates, payment method and amount).
5. Indicate if the student is enrolling in multiple programs.
6. Enter any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Information**: Enter the student's parent or guardian's name, contact details.
* **Student Name**: Enter the student's name.
* **Student DOB**: Enter the student's date of birth.
* **Program Details**: Choose the type of program the student is enrolling in, and select the start and end dates of the program.
* **Payment Method**: Choose how the student's program fees will be paid.
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the total amount to be paid.
* **Enrollment Status**: Indicate if the student is enrolling in multiple programs.
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the student's enrollment.
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter any relevant tools or resources assigned to the student.
* **Email**: Enter the student's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the student's phone number.
* **Created At**: This field is automatically generated by the system and not editable.
* **Updated At**: This field is automatically generated by the system and not editable.
* **Deleted At**: This field is automatically generated by the system and not editable.
* **User ID**: This field is automatically generated by the system and not editable.
* **User Name**: Enter the name of the user who submitted the form.
* **User Email**: Enter the email address of the user who submitted the form.
* **User Phone**: Enter the phone number of the user who submitted the form.
* **User Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the user who submitted the form.
* **User Programs**: Indicate if the user is enrolling in multiple programs.
