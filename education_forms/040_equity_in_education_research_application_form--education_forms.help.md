# Equity In Education Research Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from researchers and institutions about their education-related research projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the project details section with a clear and concise description of your research project.
2. Provide accurate and up-to-date information about yourself as the researcher.
3. Fill out the institutional information section with details about your institution.
4. Describe the study details, including study location, study budget, and research questions.
5. Outline your methodology, timeline, and study equipment needed.
6. Specify the study supervision, study security, and study institution.
7. Provide information about your study collaborators, including their names, institutions, and contact information.
8. Fill out the study outcomes, study funding, research areas, project duration, and study collaborators' email and phone numbers (if applicable).
9. Finally, review and submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Details (1)**: Describe your research project in a clear and concise manner.
* **Researcher Information (2)**: Provide up-to-date and accurate information about yourself as the researcher.
* **Institutional Information (3)**: Share details about your institution.
* **Study Details (4)**: Describe the study details, including study location, budget, and research questions.
* **Study Location (5)**: Specify the location(s) where the study will take place.
* **Contact Information (6)**: Fill out your personal contact information.
* **Study Budget (7)**: Provide a detailed breakdown of your study budget.
* **Research Questions (8)**: Clearly define the research questions or objectives of your study.
* **Methodology (9)**: Outline the methodology you will use for your study.
* **Timeline (10)**: Specify the timeline for your study, including start and end dates.
* **Study Equipment (11)**: Describe the equipment needed for your study.
* **Study Supervision (12)**: Specify how your study will be supervised.
* **Study Security (13)**: Explain the security measures you will take for your study.
* **Study Institution (14)**: Provide information about your study institution.
* **Institutional Information (15)**: Share details about your institution.
* **Project Outcomes (16)**: Outline the expected outcomes of your project.
* **Study Funding (17)**: Specify how your study will be funded.
* **Research Areas (18)**: Identify the research areas related to your project.
* **Project Duration (19)**: Specify the duration of your project.
* **Study Collaborators (20)**: List your study collaborators.
* **Study Collaborator Names (21)**: Provide the names of your study collaborators.
* **Study Collaborator Institutions (22)**: Share the institutions of your study collaborators.
* **Study Collaborator Email (23)**: Provide the email addresses of your study collaborators.
* **Study Collaborator Phone (24)**: Provide the phone numbers of your study collaborators.
* **Submit (25)**: Review and submit your application.

Note: Required fields are marked as such and must be filled out for the form to be successfully submitted. Please ensure you fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
