# Practical Exam Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Practical Exam Form is used to record information about a practical exam taken by students. This information includes the exam question, student answer, examiner's notes, examiner's score, student's score, and examiner's comment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's answer to the exam question in the "Student Answer" field.
2. Record any notes you may have during the exam in the "Examiner Notes" field.
3. Enter your score for the student's performance in the "Examiner Score" field.
4. Enter the student's score for their performance in the "Student Score" field.
5. Enter any comments you may have for the student's performance in the "Examiner Comment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`general_info`, text, optional): Enter any general information about the exam, such as the date and time it was taken.
* **What was the exam question?** (`exam_question`, text, optional): Enter the exam question as it was presented to the student.
* **Student Answer** (`student_answer`, text, optional): Enter the student's answer to the exam question.
* **Examiner Notes** (`examiner_notes`, text, optional): Enter any notes you may have during the exam.
* **Examiner Score** (`examiner_score`, number, optional): Enter your score for the student's performance. Use a numerical value to indicate your assessment.
* **Student Score** (`student_score`, number, optional): Enter the student's score for their performance. Use a numerical value to indicate their grade.
* **Examiner Comment** (`examiner_comment`, text, optional): Enter any comments you may have for the student's performance.
 
## Tips

Note: The fields marked "optional" may not necessarily be left blank. Enter relevant information to provide as much context as possible.
 
Please keep in mind that this form is only for recording information about student performance during practical exams, and not for any other purpose.
