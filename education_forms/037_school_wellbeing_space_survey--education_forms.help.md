# School Wellbeing Space Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Wellbeing Space Survey is a form designed to collect feedback from students on their experience with the wellbeing space or area in their school. The purpose of this form is to gather information on student satisfaction, participation, and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the space provided, ensuring that you provide detailed responses to help us understand your thoughts and opinions.
2. Select your responses for each question using the provided options or typing in the text fields.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to include them in the text fields provided at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wellbeing Space Survey School** (`id: 1`, `type: text`, required: false): Please provide a brief description of your school's wellbeing space or area.
* **Wellbeing Space Satisfaction Level** (`id: 2`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Select your level of satisfaction with the wellbeing space, using the options:
	+ Very Low
	+ Low
	+ Neutral
	+ High
	+ Strongly High
* **Wellbeing Space Satisfaction Reasons** (`id: 3`, `type: text`, required: false): If you chose not to select "Neutral" in the previous question, provide reasons for your satisfaction level.
* **Wellbeing Space Improvement Ideas** (`id: 4`, `type: text`, required: false): Share your ideas for improving the wellbeing space or area.
* **Student Participation** (`id: 5`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Select your level of participation in wellbeing space activities, using the options:
	+ Strongly Agree
	+ Somewhat Agree
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Disagree
	+ Strongly Disagree
* **Staff Participation** (`id: 6`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Select your level of staff participation in wellbeing space activities, using the options:
	+ Strongly Agree
	+ Somewhat Agree
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Disagree
	+ Strongly Disagree
* **Feedback** (`id: 7`, `type: text`, required: false): Share any feedback or suggestions you may have for the wellbeing space or area.
* **Assigned Tool** (`id: 8`, `type: select_multiple`, required: false): If a tool has been assigned to you, select which tool you have been assigned, using the options:
	+ chatjimmy
	+ Other
* **Assigned Tool Other** (`id: 9`, `type: text`, required: false): If you selected "Other" in the previous question, please provide the name of the assigned tool.
* **Wellbeing Space Satisfaction Frequency** (`id: 10`, `type: select_multiple`, required: false): Select how frequently you visit the wellbeing space or area, using the options:
	+ Almost Daily
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Rarely
* **Wellbeing Space Satisfaction Time** (`id: 11`, `type: time`, required: false): Provide the time of day you visit the wellbeing space or area.
* **Wellbeing Space Satisfaction Duration** (`id: 12`, `type: number`, required: false): Enter the duration of your visit to the wellbeing space or area.
* **Wellbeing Space Satisfaction Amplification** (`id: 13`, `type: number`, required: false): Enter the level of satisfaction amplification you experience when visiting the wellbeing space or area.
* **Wellbeing Space Survey Support** (`id: 14`, `type: select_multiple`, required: false): Select your level of support from the wellbeing space or area, using the options:
	+ Strongly Agree
	+ Somewhat Agree
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Disagree
	+ Strongly Disagree
* **Wellbeing Space Survey Support Reasons** (`id: 15`, `type: text`, required: false): If you chose not to select "Neutral" in the previous question, provide reasons for your level of support.
* **Wellbeing Space Survey Suggestions** (`id: 16`, `type: text`, required: false): Share your suggestions for improving the wellbeing space or area.
* **Wellbeing Space Survey Ideas** (`id: 17`, `type: text`, required: false): Share any additional comments or ideas for the wellbeing space or area.
* **Wellbeing Space Survey Comments** (`id: 18`, `type: text`, required: false): Share any additional comments or concerns you have about the wellbeing space or area.
* **Wellbeing Space Survey Support Frequency** (`id: 19`, `type: select_multiple`, required: false): Select how frequently you receive support from the wellbeing space or area, using the options:
	+ Almost Daily
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Rarely
* **Assigned Person** (`id: 20`, `type: text`, required: false): If you have been assigned a person to work with, please enter their name.
* **Feedback** (`id: 21`, `type: text`, required: false): Share any additional feedback or comments you have about the wellbeing space or area.
* **Assigned Tool Others** (`id: 22`, `type: text`, required: false): If you have been assigned a tool, enter the name of the tool.
* **Wellbeing Space Survey Other** (`id: 23`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or suggestions you have about the wellbeing space or area.
* **Wellbeing Space Survey Other Ideas** (`id: 24`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or ideas you have about the wellbeing space or area.
* **Wellbeing Space Survey Other Comments** (`id: 25`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or concerns you have about the wellbeing space or area.

## Tips
* Please answer each question truthfully and concisely.
* If you have any difficulty or concerns completing this form, feel free to reach out to your school administrators or faculty members.
* The completion of this form is entirely optional, but your input and feedback are greatly appreciated and will help us create a more engaging and supportive wellbeing space or area for everyone.
