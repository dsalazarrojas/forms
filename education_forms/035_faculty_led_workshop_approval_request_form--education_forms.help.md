# Faculty Led Workshop Approval Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended for faculty members to request approval for workshops they wish to lead or organize. It provides a structured way to communicate all relevant details about the workshop, including its title, description, date, location, and other necessary information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
2. Provide a clear and concise description of the workshop.
3. Enter the correct workshop date.
4. Specify the workshop location.
5. Enter the number of participants expected.
6. Enter your contact email and phone number.
7. Sign and date the form as the requesting faculty member.
8. Sign and date the form as the approver (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `text`, optional): This field should contain a brief introduction or overview of the workshop, including the purpose and scope of the event.
* **Faculty Name** (`2`, `text`, optional): Enter your faculty member's name.
* **Workshop Title** (`3`, `text`, optional): Enter a descriptive title for the workshop.
* **Workshop Description** (`4`, `text`, optional): Enter a detailed description of the workshop, including its objectives, outcomes, and any other relevant information.
* **Workshop Date** (`5`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the workshop, in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Faculty Department** (`6`, `text`, optional): Specify the department of the faculty member who will lead the workshop.
* **Workshop Location** (`7`, `text`, optional): Enter the location of the workshop, which could be a building or a room number.
* **Workshop Time** (`8`, `time`, optional): Enter the start and end time of the workshop, in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **Number of Participants** (`9`, `number`, optional): Estimate the number of participants expected to attend the workshop.
* **Contact E-mail** (`10`, `email`, optional): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`11`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Faculty Signature** (`12`, `text`, optional): Sign and date the form as the requesting faculty member.
* **Submitter Signature** (`13`, `text`, optional): Sign and date the form as the person submitting the request.
* **Approval** (`14`, `text`, optional): This field is for the approver's comments or approval status.
* **Comments** (`15`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or information related to the workshop.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled in accurately and completely to avoid any issues with your request.
* Use a valid email address for the contact email field.
* If you need to make any changes to the workshop details after submitting the form, please contact the relevant authorities.
* This form is designed for workshops that are not already approved or proposed; if you have a workshop that fits into this category, use the existing approval process instead.
