# intermediate_course_delegate_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants in an intermediate course. It helps us understand their experiences and suggestions for improvement, which we'll use to refine the course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Rate your experience as a participant (page 1).
2. Choose the level of the course (page 2).
3. Provide any additional feedback or comments about the course (page 3).
4. Suggest any changes or improvements for future courses (page 4).
5. Rate the course overall (page 5).
6. Indicate whether you would take the course again (page 6).
7. Share any suggestions or recommendations for the course (page 7).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Rating** (`participant_rating`): Rate your experience as a participant on a scale of 1 to 10.
* **Course Level** (`course_level`): Choose the level of the course that best describes your experience. Select one of the following options: Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3.
* **Feedback Comment** (`feedback_comment`): If you have any additional feedback or comments about the course, please share them here.
* **Recommended Course Changes** (`recommended_course_changes`): Suggest any changes or improvements for future courses. Select one or more of the following options: New course topic, New course material.
* **Rating** (`rating_scale`): Rate the course overall on a scale of 1 to 10.
* **Would Take Again** (`would_take_again`): Indicate whether you would take the course again. Select 'True' if you would, 'False' if you wouldn't.
* **Participant Suggestions** (`participant_suggestions`): Share any additional suggestions or recommendations for the course.
