# Training Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the effectiveness of training programs. Your input will help us understand how well the training programs are meeting their objectives and identify areas for improvement. Please provide your honest feedback to help us better serve you and your team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required and optional fields as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program title** (`program_title`, Text, Optional): Enter the title of the training program you are evaluating.
* **Program duration** (`program_duration`, Number, Optional): Enter the length of the training program in minutes or hours.
* **Program type** (`program_type`, Select One, Optional):
	+ Choose the type of training program:
		- Instructor Led
		- Online
		- In-House
* **Rating** (`rating`, Number, Optional): Enter a rating from 1 to 10 to indicate how effective you found the training program to be.
* **Comments** (`comments`, Text, Optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the training program.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.
* **Category** (`category`, Select One, Optional):
	+ Choose a category for your feedback:
		- Education
		- Training
		- Development
* **Submission date** (`submission_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date you submitted your feedback.
* **Submission time** (`submission_time`, Time, Optional): Enter the time you submitted your feedback.
* **Session time** (`session_time`, Time, Optional): Enter the duration of the training session.
* **Training duration** (`training_duration`, Number, Optional): Enter the length of the training program in minutes or hours.
* **Session duration** (`session_duration`, Number, Optional): Enter the length of the training session.
* **Attendees** (`attendees`, Number, Optional): Enter the number of attendees in the training session.
* **Attendees attire** (`attendees_attire`, Select Multiple, Optional):
	+ Choose the attire of the attendees:
		- Formal
		- Casual
		- Business Casual
* **Session type** (`session_type`, Select One, Optional):
	+ Choose the type of session:
		- Online
		- In-House
		- In-Person
* **Location** (`location`, Select Multiple, Optional):
	+ Choose the location:
		- Remote
		- On-site
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, Select One, Optional):
	+ Choose whether you'd like to be contacted for follow-up:
		- True
		- False
		- Maybe
* **Follow-up date** (`follow_up_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.
* **Follow-up time** (`follow_up_time`, Time, Optional): Enter the time you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.
* **Session attendees** (`session_attendees`, Text, Optional): Enter any comments or feedback about the attendees in the training session.
* **Comments attendees** (`comments_attendees`, Text, Optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the attendees in the training session.
* **Follow-up session duration** (`follow_up_session_duration`, Number, Optional): Enter the duration of the follow-up session.
* **Comments** (`comments`, Text, Optional): Provide any final comments or feedback about the training program.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as required.
