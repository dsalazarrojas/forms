# Tycic Business Plan And Budget - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a business plan and budget for a user, likely in an academic setting. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
- Fill in your User Name, which should match the name on your ID or student ID.
- Enter your Business Name, which should be the name of your business or project.
- Describe your business Idea, which should be a brief overview of what your business is about.
- If applicable, add your Budget, which should be a numerical value (e.g., dollars).
- Enter the Date your project is due or relevant to.
- Provide Time, which should be in the format of hours and minutes (e.g., 02:15).
- Add any additional Note about your project (e.g., any specific requirements or considerations).
- Fill in your Email and Phone if you have one or if they are required by your instructor.
- Select your Category, which is likely to be Junior or Senior depending on your academic level.
- For School Year, choose the year(s) your project applies to.
- Write a detailed Project Description of your business plan.
- Lastly, rate the usefulness of your project, which is subjective and depends on your opinion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, Text, **Required**): Please enter your name as it appears on your ID or student ID.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, Text, **Required**): Enter the name of your business or project.
* **Idea** (`idea`, Text, **Required**): Briefly describe your business idea.
* **Budget** (`budget`, Number, **Optional**): If applicable, add your budget (e.g., dollars).
* **Date** (`date`, Date, **Required**): Enter the date your project is due or relevant to.
* **Time** (`time`, Time, **Required**): Enter the time in the format of hours and minutes (e.g., 02:15).
* **Note** (`note`, Note, **Optional**): Add any additional notes about your project.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, **Optional**): Fill in your email address if required by your instructor or if you have one.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, **Optional**): Enter your phone number if required by your instructor or if you have one.
* **Category** (`category`, Select One, **Required**): Choose Junior or Senior depending on your academic level.
* **School Year** (`school_year`, Select Multiple, **Required**): Choose the year(s) your project applies to.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, Text, **Required**): Write a detailed description of your business plan.
* **Useful** (`useful`, Select One, **Optional**): Rate the usefulness of your project, which is subjective.
* **Make Up** (`make_up`, Text, **Optional**): Add any additional information about your project.
