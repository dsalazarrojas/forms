# Time Slot Booking Form USM - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a time slot for a student to meet with a staff member. It collects information about the student and staff member involved in the booking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's first name in the "Student First Name" field.
2. Enter the student's last name in the "Student Last Name" field.
3. Enter the student's email in the "Student Email" field.
4. Enter the student's phone number in the "Student Phone" field.
5. Choose the student's date of availability in the "Student Date" field.
6. Enter the staff member's last name in the "Staff Last Name" field.
7. Enter the staff member's first name in the "Staff First Name" field.
8. Enter the staff member's title in the "Staff Title" field.
9. Enter the staff member's email in the "Staff Email" field.
10. Choose the date for the booking in the "Time Slot Booking Form USM Date" field.
11. Choose the time for the booking in the "Time Slot Booking Form USM Time" field.
12. Select the time slot for the booking in the "Time Slot Booking Form USM Time Slot" field.
13. Add any comments about the booking in the "Time Slot Booking Form USM Comments" field.
