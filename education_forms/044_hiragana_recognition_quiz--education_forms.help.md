# Hiragana Recognition Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hiragana Recognition Quiz is a tool to assess learners' recognition skills in the Hiragana writing system used in Japanese language.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, fill in the required information on each page as prompted.
- Start by completing the Hiragana recognition quiz itself (page 1).
- Proceed to the next page to indicate your confidence in answering each question (page 2).
- On subsequent pages, provide your personal contact information, including student name, email, and phone number (pages 3-5).
- Lastly, report the time and date the quiz was completed, and your final score (pages 6-8).
- Provide any additional comments or feedback in the final field (page 9).
