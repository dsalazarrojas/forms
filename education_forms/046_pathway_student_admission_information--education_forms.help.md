# pathway_student_admission_information - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "pathway_student_admission_information" form is designed to collect student admission information for efficient processing and management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Review and ensure that all fields are filled in correctly before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Provide the student's contact number for communication purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the student's email address for contact and communication.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the student's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, text, optional): Enter the student's permanent address.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): Enter the parent's or guardian's name.
* **Parent Contact Number** (`parent_contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the parent's or guardian's contact number.
* **Student ID Number** (`student_id_number`, number, optional): Enter the student's ID number (if applicable).
* **Student Start Date** (`student_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the student started their program.
* **Student Grade Level** (`student_grade_level`, select_one, optional): Select the student's current grade level.
* **Student Program** (`student_program`, select_multiple, optional): Select the programs the student is interested in or already enrolled in.
