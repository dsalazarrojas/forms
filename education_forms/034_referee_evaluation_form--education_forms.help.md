# Referee Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Referee Evaluation Form is used by referees to assess their own performance after a game or match. The form helps referees reflect on their strengths and weaknesses and identify areas for improvement.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the Evaluator's Name and Evaluator's Email fields, respectively.
2. Enter the name of the referee being evaluated in the Referee's Name field.
3. Choose the date of the game or match being evaluated from the Date field.
4. Select the competition level of the game or match.
5. Evaluate each aspect of the referee's performance using the corresponding radio buttons. For example, you can select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the referee showed up on time or not.
6. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the Additional Comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Evaluator's Name** (Text, Required): Enter your name as the evaluator.
* **Evaluator's Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address.
* **Referee's Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the referee being evaluated.
* **Date** (Date, Required): Choose the date of the game or match being evaluated.
* **Competition Level** (Select One, Required): Select the competition level of the game or match (High, Low).
* **Dress & Appearance** (Select Multiple, Optional): Check if the referee's dress and appearance were satisfactory. 
* **Pre-Game** (Select One, Optional): Check if the referee was prepared for the game.
* **Fitness** (Select One, Optional): Check if the referee's fitness level was satisfactory.
* **Attitude** (Select One, Optional): Check if the referee's attitude was satisfactory.
* **Courage** (Select One, Optional): Check if the referee showed courage during the game.
* **Character** (Select One, Optional): Check if the referee showed good character during the game.
* **Consistency** (Select One, Optional): Check if the referee's consistency was satisfactory.
* **Position** (Select One, Optional): Check if the referee's position was correct.
* **Mechanics** (Select One, Optional): Check if the referee's mechanics were correct.
* **Signals** (Select One, Optional): Check if the referee's signals were clear and effective.
* **Accuracy of Decisions** (Select One, Optional): Check if the referee's decisions were accurate.
* **Game Control** (Select One, Optional): Check if the referee's game control was effective.
* **Additional Comments** (Text, Optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Referee's Comments** (Text, Optional): Provide any comments or feedback from the referee being evaluated.
