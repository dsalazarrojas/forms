# Digital Wellness Education Series Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants on their experience with the Digital Wellness Education Series. Your input will help us understand what worked well and what can be improved in future workshops.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer all questions that apply to you.
2. Select one of the options for each question that best describes your experience or preference.
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Text Field)
  * A brief introduction or personal statement about your experience with the series.
* **Workshop Attendance** (Select Multiple Field)
  * When you attended the workshop: 1 - 3 days ago, 4 - 6 days ago, or 7 or more days ago.
* **Workshop Length** (Select One Field)
  * How long was the workshop: 30 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours, or 3 hours or more.
* **Content Quality** (Number Field)
  * Rate the quality of the content on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Relevance** (Number Field)
  * Rate how relevant you found the content to your interests on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Workshop Friendly** (Select Multiple Field)
  * Was the workshop friendly and inviting: True or False.
* **Future Workshops** (Select One Field)
  * Do you think there should be future workshops on this topic: True or False.
* **Workshop Improvement** (Select One Field)
  * What could be improved in future workshops: Content, Schedule, Location, or None.
* **Digital Resources** (Select One Field)
  * How satisfied are you with the digital resources provided: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (Select One Field)
  * How satisfied are you with the overall series: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.
* **Comments** (Text Field)
  * Any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Participant Information** (Text Field)
  * Any additional information about yourself that you feel is relevant to your experience.
* **Participant Email** (Email Field)
  * Your email address for follow-up communication.
* **Participant Phone** (Text Field)
  * Your phone number for follow-up communication.
* **Participant Notes** (Text Field)
  * Any additional notes about your experience.
* **Staff Notes** (Text Field)
  * Any additional notes from the staff about your experience.
* **Staff Comments** (Text Field)
  * Any additional comments from the staff about your experience.
* **Participant Did Attend** (Select One Field)
  * Did you attend the workshop: True or False.
* **Staff Did Attend** (Select One Field)
  * Did the staff attend the workshop: True or False.
* **Staff Did Arrive** (Select One Field)
  * Did the staff arrive on time: True or False.
* **Staff Did Participate** (Select One Field)
  * Did the staff participate actively in the workshop: True or False.
* **Form Completion** (Note Field)
  * A field for any additional information or comments from the form designer.

Tips:

* Please be honest and constructive in your feedback.
* Your input will help us improve future workshops.
* If you have any questions or need help completing the form, feel free to contact us.
