# Data Literacy Training Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Data Literacy Training Request Form is designed to collect information from users interested in attending data literacy training sessions. This form helps us understand session preferences, training needs, and contact information to provide a suitable and effective training experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select your session preferences by typing in the Session Preferences field.
2. Describe your training details in the Training Details field.
3. Enter your email address in the Contact Information field.
4. Enter your phone number in the Phone Number field.
5. Select the number of participants you'll be bringing along in the Participant Details field.
6. (Optional) Select a suitable session date and time in the Session Date and Session Time fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Preferences** (session_preferences, text, optional): Please describe your ideal session format, such as duration, frequency, or any other specific requirements.
* **Training Details** (training_details, text, required): Describe your training needs, including any specific topics or skills you'd like to focus on during the training session.
* **Contact Information** (contact_information, email, optional): Enter your email address to ensure we can get in touch with you.
* **Phone Number** (phone_number, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about the training session.
* **Participant Details** (participant_details, select_one, optional): Select the number of participants you'll be bringing along.
* **Session Date** (session_date, date, optional): If you have a specific date in mind for the training session, please select it.
* **Session Time** (session_time, time, optional): If you have a preferred time for the training session, please select it.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any specific requests or requirements, please include them in the Training Details field.
* We'll be in touch with you via the email address provided to confirm the training details.
* If you're experiencing any difficulties or have questions, feel free to reach out to us at form@gic.mx.
* Please note that this form is for data literacy training requests only; if you have other inquiries, kindly use the corresponding form.
