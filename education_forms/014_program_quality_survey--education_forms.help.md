# Program Quality Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Program Quality Survey is designed to collect feedback and information about your program's performance. It's a tool used by management and stakeholders to assess your program's quality and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question carefully and honestly, providing as much detail as needed.
2. You can skip questions if they are not applicable or not relevant to your program.
3. If you have any concerns or comments that don't fit into the provided answer options, feel free to include them in the "Feedback" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Title** (`program_quality_survey_first_page`): Provide a brief title of your program.
- **Program Information** (`program_quality_survey_second_page`): Please select "Yes" or "No" based on whether you think your program meets your expectations regarding quality standards.
  * Yes: The program meets or exceeds quality expectations.
  * No: The program does not meet quality expectations.
- **Staff** (`program_quality_survey_third_page`): Select "Yes" or "No" based on whether you feel the staff meets quality standards.
  * Yes: The staff provides high-quality support and service.
  * No: The staff does not meet quality expectations.
- **Services** (`program_quality_survey_fourth_page`): If applicable, provide a number that best reflects the quality of services provided.
- **Facilities** (`program_quality_survey_fifth_page`): Please provide any comments or observations about the facilities used for your program.
- **Feedback** (`program_quality_survey_fifth_page`): Provide any additional feedback or comments you have about your program's quality.
