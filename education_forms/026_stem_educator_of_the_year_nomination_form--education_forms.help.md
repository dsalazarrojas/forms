# STEM Educator Of The Year Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to nominate a STEM educator for recognition and awards.
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the "Nominate Educator" page carefully, making sure to select "Yes" if you want to nominate an educator.
2. On the "Educator Information" page, fill in all required fields, including the name of the educator, their email, and your own contact information.
3. On the "Nomination Details" page, provide a detailed reason for the nomination and fill in any additional information that you think is relevant.
4. On the "Submission Details" page, confirm that you have the necessary permissions to submit the nomination and provide any additional information if required.
5. Review all the information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nominate Educator**: Select "Yes" to indicate that you want to nominate an educator.
* **Name of Educator**: Enter the name of the educator you are nominating.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of the school the educator is from (if applicable).
* **Nominee Email**: Enter the email address of the educator being nominated.
* **Nominee Phone**: Enter the phone number of the educator being nominated (if applicable).
* **Reason for Nomination**: Provide a detailed reason why you are nominating this educator.
* **Nomination Organization**: Enter your own organization name.
* **Position**: Enter your own position in your organization (if applicable).
* **Department**: Enter your department in your organization (if applicable).
* **Office**: Enter your office in your organization (if applicable).
* **Work Phone**: Enter your work phone number (if applicable).
* **Work Email**: Enter your work email (if applicable).
* **Work Extension**: Enter your work extension (if applicable).
* **Work Title**: Enter your work title (if applicable).
* **Work URL**: Enter your work URL (if applicable).
* **Work**: Enter any additional information about your work (if applicable).
* **Notes**: Enter any additional information about the nomination (if applicable).
* **Agree**: Confirm that you have the necessary permissions to submit this nomination.
* **Agree Other (if applicable)**: Provide any additional information about your nomination (if applicable).
* **Submit**: Review all the information carefully before submitting the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields carefully.
* Provide a detailed reason for the nomination.
* Review all the information carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
