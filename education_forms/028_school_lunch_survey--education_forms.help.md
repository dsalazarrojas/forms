# School Lunch Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Lunch Survey is a form designed to collect feedback from students about their school lunch experiences. The survey aims to gather information about students' favorite foods, drinks, and activities, which can help school administrators and nutritionists make informed decisions about meal planning and improve the school lunch program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your First Name.
2. Enter your Student Id.
3. Select your Grade Level (High School, Middle School, or Elementary).
4. Enter your Parent Name (optional).
5. Provide Comments about your lunch experience (optional).
6. Enter your Email (optional).
7. Select your School (Elementary, High School, or Middle School).
8. Choose your Favorite Lunch Type (Pizza, Salad, Sandwich, Soup, or Fruit).
9. Rate your favorite food (on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is the least favorite and 10 is the most favorite).
10. Select up to 3 of your Favorite Foods at School (Pizza, Salad, Sandwich, Fruit, or Soup).
11. Select up to 3 of your Favorite Drinks at School (Water, Juice, or Soda).
12. Choose up to 3 of your Favorite Activities at School (Playing outside, Reading, Art, or Music).
13. Select up to 3 of your Favorite Times at School (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
14. Select up to 3 of your Favorite Foods at School (Pizza, Salad, Sandwich, Fruit, or Soup).
15. Select up to 3 of your Favorite Drinks at School (Water, Juice, or Soda).
16. Choose up to 3 of your Favorite Activities at School (Playing outside, Reading, Art, or Music).
17. Select up to 3 of your Favorite Times at School (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
18. Enter a rating for your school (on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is the least favorite and 10 is the most favorite).
19. Provide Comments about your school (optional).
20. Add Comments about your lunch experience (optional).
