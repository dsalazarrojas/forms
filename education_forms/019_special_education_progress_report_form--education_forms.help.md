# Special Education Progress Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Special Education Progress Report Form is used to report on the progress of students in special education programs, providing updates on their performance and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the progress status for the student from the "Progress Report Form" dropdown menu.
2. Enter the student's unique ID in the "Student ID" field.
3. Enter the date of the progress report in the "Special Education Progress Report Date" field.
4. Enter the start date of the reporting period in the "Start Date" field.
5. Enter the end date of the reporting period in the "End Date" field.
6. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
7. Enter the teacher's name who is working with the student in the "Special Education Teacher" field.
8. Enter the name of the special education class the student is participating in in the "Special Education Class" field.
9. Enter any progress comments in the "Progress Comments" field.
10. Enter any special education progress comments in the "Special Education Progress Comments" field.
11. Enter the student's goals in the "Student Goals" field.
12. Enter any special education services the student is receiving in the "Special Education Services" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Progress Report Form** (`1`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select "Yes" if the student has made progress, or "No" if they have not.
* **Student ID** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Enter the student's unique identifier to track their progress.
* **Special Education Progress Report Date** (`3`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the progress report.
* **Start Date** (`4`, `date`, required: false): Enter the start date of the reporting period.
* **End Date** (`5`, `date`, required: false): Enter the end date of the reporting period.
* **Student Name** (`6`, `text`, required: false): Enter the student's name.
* **Special Education Teacher** (`7`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the teacher working with the student.
* **Special Education Class** (`8`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the special education class the student is participating in.
* **Progress Comments** (`9`, `text`, required: false): Enter any progress comments for the student.
* **Special Education Progress Comments** (`10`, `text`, required: false): Enter any special education progress comments.
* **Student Goals** (`11`, `text`, required: false): Enter the student's goals.
* **Special Education Services** (`12`, `text`, required: false): Enter any special education services the student is receiving.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that student progress is properly documented.
* Enter any relevant progress comments or special education progress comments to provide context for the student's performance.
* Review and update the student's goals regularly to ensure they are on track to meet their objectives.
