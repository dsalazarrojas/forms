# Educational Program Accreditation Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Program Accreditation Evaluation Form is designed to collect data for the evaluation and accreditation of educational programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the educational program in the "Program Name" field.
2. Enter the name of the institution in the "Institution Name" field.
3. Select the date of the evaluation in the "Evaluation Date" field.
4. Enter the name of the evaluator in the "Evaluator Name" field.
5. Enter the length of the program in the "Program Length" field.
6. Describe the evaluation criteria in the "Evaluation Criteria" field.
7. Select the accreditation status of the program in the "Accreditation Status" field.
8. Enter any comments or evaluation results in the "Evaluation Results" field.
9. Enter any additional comments about the program or institution in the "Evaluation Comments" field.
10. Enter any comments from the evaluator in the "Evaluator Comments" field.
11. Enter any comments from the institution in the "Institution Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Name** (`Program Name`): Enter the name of the educational program being evaluated.
* **Institution Name** (`Institution Name`): Enter the name of the institution offering the program.
* **Evaluation Date** (`Evaluation Date`): Select the date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluator Name** (`Evaluator Name`): Enter the name of the person evaluating the program.
* **Program Length** (`Program Length`): Enter the length of the program in the format of a number.
* **Evaluation Criteria** (`Evaluation Criteria`): Describe the criteria used to evaluate the program.
* **Accreditation Status** (`Accreditation Status`): Select the status of the program's accreditation (Accredited, Accredited with conditions, Not Accredited, or Pending).
* **Evaluation Results** (`Evaluation Results`): Enter any comments or results from the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Comments** (`Evaluator Comments`): Enter any comments from the evaluator.
* **Institution Comments** (`Institution Comments`): Enter any comments from the institution.
* **Program Fax** (`Program Fax`): Enter the fax number of the program.
* **Institution Fax** (`Institution Fax`): Enter the fax number of the institution.
* **Program Contact** (`Program Contact`): Enter the contact information for the program.
* **Institution Contact** (`Institution Contact`): Enter the contact information for the institution.
* **Program Website** (`Program Website`): Enter the website of the program.
* **Institution Website** (`Institution Website`): Enter the website of the institution.
* **Program Address** (`Program Address`): Enter the address of the program.
* **Institution Address** (`Institution Address`): Enter the address of the institution.
* **Program Email** (`Institution Email`): Enter the email of the program.
* **Institution Email** (`Institution Email`): Enter the email of the institution.
* **Program Phone** (`Institution Phone`): Enter the phone number of the program.
* **Evaluator Email** (`Evaluator Email`): Enter the email of the evaluator.
* **Evaluator Phone** (`Evaluator Phone`): Enter the phone number of the evaluator.
* **Evaluation Form Submitted By** (`Evaluation Form Submitted By`): Select who submitted the form (System Admin or User).
* **Institution Phone** (`Institution Phone`): Enter the phone number of the institution.
* **Evaluator Comments** (`Evaluator Comments`): Enter any comments from the evaluator.
* **Institutional Comments** (`Institutional Comments`): Enter any comments from the institution.

Please note that some fields are not required if you select "System Admin" as the option for "Evaluation Form Submitted By". 

This guide is designed to help you complete the form correctly and efficiently. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to contact us.
