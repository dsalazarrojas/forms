# Weekdays Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Weekdays Knowledge Quiz form is designed to collect information from students about their knowledge of weekdays, including their answers, comments, and status updates. This form will help you track student progress and monitor their status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your answer to the first question, "Weekday Questions", from the provided options.
2. Enter your student name in the "Student Name" field.
3. Enter your student email address in the "Student Email" field.
4. Provide your answer to the question in the "Weekday Answer" field.
5. Add any additional comments about your answer in the "Weekday Answer Comment" field.
6. Select your status from the "Weekday Answer Status" field.
7. Add any comments or notes about your status in the "Weekday Answer Status Comment" field.
8. Optionally, select who submitted the form, "Submitted By", and when it was submitted.
9. Enter your student phone number in the "Student Phone" field (optional).
10. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the form was submitted to anyone.
11. Provide the date and time of your status update in the "Weekday Answer Status At" and "Weekday Answer Status Date" fields (optional).
12. Finally, review and submit the form to track your progress and status.
