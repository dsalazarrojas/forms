# Educational Meeting Minutes Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Meeting Minutes Form is a tool used to record and document the details of educational meetings, including meeting details, attendees, and actions taken. This form helps to improve communication and reduce errors by standardizing the collection and sharing of information across teams and stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Meeting Details** (Required: false)
	* Enter the date of the meeting.
2. **Date** (Required: false)
	* Enter the date of the meeting in a format that follows the standard date format.
3. **Time** (Required: false)
	* Enter the time of the meeting.
4. **Location** (Required: false)
	* Enter the location of the meeting.
5. **Meeting Leader** (Required: false)
	* Enter the name of the person who led the meeting.
6. **Meeting Attendees** (Required: false)
	* Select 'Yes' for each person who attended the meeting.
7. **Decisions** (Required: false)
	* Record any decisions made during the meeting.
8. **Next Steps** (Required: false)
	* Outline any next steps or actions to be taken.
9. **Follow-up Actions** (Required: false)
	* Indicate if any follow-up actions are required.
10. **Meeting Notes** (Required: false)
	* Add any meeting notes or attachments.
11. **Attachment** (Required: false)
	* Upload any relevant attachments related to the meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Details (meeting_details)**: This field captures the basic details of the meeting.
* **Date (date)**: Enter the date of the meeting in a format that follows the standard date format.
* **Time (time)**: Enter the time of the meeting.
* **Location (location)**: Enter the location of the meeting.
* **Meeting Leader (meeting_leader)**: Enter the name of the person who led the meeting.
* **Meeting Attendees (meeting_attendees)**: Select 'Yes' for each person who attended the meeting.
* **Decisions (decisions)**: Record any decisions made during the meeting.
* **Next Steps (next_steps)**: Outline any next steps or actions to be taken.
* **Follow-up Actions (follow_up_actions)**: Indicate if any follow-up actions are required.
* **Meeting Notes (meeting_notes)**: Add any meeting notes or attachments.
* **Attachment (attachment)**: Upload any relevant attachments related to the meeting.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required information is accurate and complete before submitting the form.
* Use clear and concise language when entering information in the form.
* Review the form before submitting it to ensure that all necessary fields are filled out correctly.
