# School Bus Driver Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the performance and safety concerns of school bus drivers. It helps supervisors and school administrators assess the quality of service provided by the driver during the evaluation period.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form thoroughly and accurately.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed.
3. If a field does not apply, please use "N/A" (Not Applicable) to indicate so.
4. Please sign and date the form at the bottom.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Driver Information** (`driver_info`, Text, Optional): Provide a brief description of the driver, including their name, job title, and any other relevant details.
* **Performance** (`performance`, Select Multiple, Required): Evaluate the driver's performance based on their service quality, using the following options:
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Comments** (`comments`, Text, Required): Describe any specific incidents or events that occurred during the evaluation period.
* **Safety Concerns** (`safety_concerns`, Select One, Required): Indicate if there were any safety concerns during the evaluation period:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, Text, Optional): Provide any additional information that may be relevant to the evaluation.
* **Supervisor Comments** (`supervisor_comments`, Text, Required): Enter any observations or feedback from the supervisor or other relevant personnel.
* **Date of Evaluation** (`date_of_evaluation`, Date, Required): Enter the date the evaluation was conducted.
* **Time of Evaluation** (`time_of_evaluation`, Time, Required): Enter the time the evaluation was conducted.

Note: The form will only be completed by authorized personnel and is intended for internal use only. The information provided will be kept confidential and used to evaluate and improve the driver's performance.
