# transition_words_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose

The `transition_words_quiz` form is designed to collect information from students and teachers about transition words used in a school setting.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Navigate to the form and click on the "Submit" button.
2.  Fill out the form with the required information.
3.  Ensure that all fields are filled out completely and accurately.
4.  Review the form for any errors or inconsistencies.
