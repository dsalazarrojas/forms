# Nigeria School Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students seeking admission to a school in Nigeria. It is essential to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a successful and seamless admission process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information readily available before starting to fill out the form.
2. Begin by providing your Student Name, Date Of Birth, and Contact Email.
3. Next, enter your Parent Contact Number and Student Address.
4. Fill in your Parent Name and select your preferred class (S1, S2, S3, S4, or S5) for your student.
5. Choose the schools you are interested in (Loris, Nuru, Saka, Akin, or Oloye).
6. Select the subjects you are interested in (Maths, Science, English, History, or Geography).
7. Finally, add any relevant student notes or comments in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YY).
* **Contact Email**: Enter your or your parent's email address where you can be contacted.
* **Parent Contact Number**: Enter your parent's contact number for easy communication.
* **Student Address**: Enter your current address where you live with your parent or guardian.
* **Parent Name**: Enter your parent's name for verification purposes.
* **Student Class**: Select the class level you wish to be admitted to (S1, S2, S3, S4, or S5).
* **Student School**: Select the school(s) you are interested in attending (Loris, Nuru, Saka, Akin, or Oloye).
* **Student Subject**: Select the subject(s) you are interested in studying (Maths, Science, English, History, or Geography).
* **Student Note**: Add any additional comments or information about yourself that may be relevant to your admission.
