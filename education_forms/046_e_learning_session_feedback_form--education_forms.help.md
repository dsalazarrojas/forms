# e_learning_session_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from learners on their experience with an educational session. The goal is to help instructors improve their teaching and material presentation.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide feedback, simply fill out the form with the following information:

### Session Name
*   The name of the session you attended.

### Session Start Date
*   The date when the session started.

### Overall Feedback
*   A brief description of your overall impression of the session.

### Instructor Performance
*   Rate your instructor's performance (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).

### Material Coverage
*   Choose the level of material coverage that best describes your experience (Enough, Too little, Too much).

### Feedback
*   Enter any additional feedback or comments about the session.

### Suggestions
*   Suggest any changes or improvements for future sessions.

### Comments
*   Enter any comments or observations you'd like to share about the session.

## Tips
*   Please be honest and provide constructive feedback to help improve the session.
*   If you have any questions, you can contact us at form@gic.mx.
*   This form is designed to be completed by learners who have attended the session.
