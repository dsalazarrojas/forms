## Purpose
The Educational Library Access Request Form is designed to collect information from users seeking access to the educational library. This form helps the library management team to assess and fulfill the request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal user information in the "User Information" section.
2. Select the appropriate "Access Type" from the provided options.
3. Enter the library details in the "Library Details" section.
4. Enter your school's contact information, including school name, location, and address.
5. Provide your contact information, including name, email, and phone number.
6. Enter your library contact information, including name, email, and phone number.
7. Enter the submission deadline and time, if applicable.
8. Add any additional library notes or comments, if necessary.
