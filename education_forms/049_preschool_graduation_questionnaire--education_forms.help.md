# Preschool Graduation Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Preschool Graduation Questionnaire is a form used to collect data on a student's progress and assessment at the end of a preschool program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the required fields and provide accurate information for each section. If you are unsure about any field, please ask the administrator or teacher for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student ID** (`1`, `number`, required): A unique identifier for the student, which helps track their progress and assessment.
- **Date of Graduation Assessment** (`2`, `date`, required): The date when the student's graduation assessment was conducted.
- **Teacher Assessment** (`3`, `note`, optional): A written evaluation of the student's skills and abilities by their teacher.
- **Student Feedback** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required): Check the boxes to indicate whether the student has shown progress in specific areas (e.g., social skills, language skills, emotional intelligence).
- **Teacher Feedback** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required): Check the boxes to indicate whether the student has shown progress in specific areas (e.g., social skills, language skills, emotional intelligence).
- **Parent Feedback** (`6`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check the boxes to indicate whether the student's parents have provided feedback on their child's progress.
- **Graduation Date** (`7`, `date`, required): The date when the student will graduate from the preschool program.
- **Teacher Suggestion** (`8`, `text`, optional): Any suggestions or comments from the teacher regarding the student's progress and development.
- **Student Rating** (`9`, `number`, required): A numerical rating of the student's overall progress and development.
- **Teacher Rating** (`10`, `number`, required): A numerical rating of the student's overall progress and development.
- **Parent Rating** (`11`, `number`, optional): A numerical rating of the student's overall progress and development.
- **Teacher Recommendation** (`12`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check the boxes to indicate whether the teacher recommends further education and support for the student.
