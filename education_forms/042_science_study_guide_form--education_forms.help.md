# Science Study Guide Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students for a science study guide. It helps to create a personalized study plan or schedule based on the student's preferences and interests.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's information (name, email, phone number).
2. Select the topic of interest and subject for the study guide.
3. Choose the science level (High, Low) and select any additional subjects or topics related to the study guide.
4. Enter any other notes or comments you'd like to add.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information** (`science_study_guide_form_1`): Enter the student's name, email, and phone number.
* **Student Name** (`science_study_guide_form_2`): Enter the student's name.
* **Student Email** (`science_study_guide_form_3`): Enter the student's email address.
* **Student Phone** (`science_study_guide_form_4`): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Topic of Interest** (`science_study_guide_form_5`): Select the topic of interest for the study guide. You can choose from "Yes" or "No".
* **Subject** (`science_study_guide_form_6`): Select the subject for the study guide. You can choose from "Yes" or "No".
* **Section** (`science_study_guide_form_7`): Select any additional sections or topics related to the study guide. You can choose multiple options.
* **Date** (`science_study_guide_form_8`): Enter the date for the study guide in mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Time** (`science_study_guide_form_9`): Enter the time for the study guide (e.g., 08:00).
* **Notes** (`science_study_guide_form_10`): Enter any additional notes or comments about the study guide.
* **Comments** (`science_study_guide_form_11`): Enter any other comments or thoughts about the study guide.
* **Science Subject** (`science_study_guide_form_12`): Select the science subject for the study guide. You can choose from "Yes" or "No".
* **Science Topic** (`science_study_guide_form_13`): Select the science topic for the study guide. You can choose from "Yes" or "No".
* **Science Level** (`science_study_guide_form_14`): Select the science level (High, Low) for the study guide. 

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure the study guide is relevant and effective.
* Double-check your selections and entries before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
