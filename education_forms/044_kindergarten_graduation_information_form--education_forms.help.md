# Kindergarten Graduation Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a kindergarten graduation ceremony.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please provide the following information for each student.
- Start by filling in the student's first name and last name.
- Enter the student's date of birth.
- Select the grade the student has completed.
- Enter the student's email address.
- Enter the student's phone number.
- Enter the student's address.
- Enter the student's city.
- Enter the parent's name.
- Enter the parent's phone number.
- Select the school year the student completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Kindergarten Graduation Information Form** (`form_title`, text, required: false): This is the title of the form. Please enter a meaningful title for the student's graduation ceremony.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, required: false): Enter the student's ID number or any other unique identifier.
* **Student First Name** (`student_first_name`, text, required: false): Enter the student's first name.
* **Student Last Name** (`student_last_name`, text, required: false): Enter the student's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`student_dob`, date, required: false): Enter the student's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Grade Completed** (`student_grade_completed`, select_one, required: false): Select the grade the student has completed from the provided options.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required: false): Enter the student's email address.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, text, required: false): Enter the student's address.
* **Student City** (`student_city`, text, required: false): Enter the student's city.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required: false): Enter the parent's name.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the parent's phone number.
* **School Year** (`school_year`, select_one, required: false): Select the school year the student completed from the provided options.

## Tips
* Please ensure accurate and complete information is provided for each field.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask the teacher for clarification.
* This form is not to be filled out for students who have previously completed a graduation form.
