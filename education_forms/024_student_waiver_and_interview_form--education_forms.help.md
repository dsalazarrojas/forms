# Student Waiver And Interview Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Waiver and Interview Form is a tool used to collect personal and contact information from students as part of the onboarding process. It helps in verifying the identity of the student and ensuring that all necessary details are captured accurately. This form is used to create a comprehensive profile of the student, which can be used for further reference.

## How To Complete This Form

- **Please navigate to the Main Page**, where you will find a brief introduction to the form.
- Fill out the form by providing your personal information, such as your contact details and emergency contact information.
- Answer questions related to your physical and mental characteristics.
- Provide details about your skills and experience.
- Sign the form to confirm your identity.
- Complete the student's signature section, which is a required field for student onboarding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title**: This is the title of the form, which is currently "Student Waiver And Interview Form".
* **Student ID**: This field is used to capture the student ID number. Please enter your valid student ID number as per the institution's guidelines.
* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date Of Birth**: Provide your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Student Type**: Select your student type from the options:
	+ Full-time
	+ Part-time
	+ Student
	+ Other
* **Student Status**: Select your student status from the options:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive
	+ Pending
	+ Other
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relation**: Select the relation of the emergency contact person from the options:
	+ Parent
	+ Guardian
	+ Spouse
	+ Other
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the contact email of your emergency contact person.
* **Student Height**: Enter your height in feet and inches, for example, 5'8" (for 5 feet 8 inches).
* **Student Weight**: Enter your weight in pounds.
* **Student Address**: Enter your home address.
* **Student City**: Enter the city where you currently reside.
* **Student State**: Enter the state where you currently reside.
* **Student Postal Code**: Enter the postal code of your residence.
* **Student Country**: Enter the country where you currently reside.
* **Student Signature Date**: Enter the date you sign this form.
* **Signature**: Sign the form to confirm your identity.
* **Student Signature**: This is the student's signature, which is a required field for student onboarding.
