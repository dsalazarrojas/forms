# Camp Ministries Reference Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Camp Ministries Reference Form" is a tool used by references to assess a candidate's spiritual maturity, leadership skills, and suitability for a ministry role. This form helps to provide an honest and detailed evaluation of the candidate's strengths and areas for growth, ensuring they are well-suited for a position of influence and responsibility.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form completely**: Each section is designed to gather specific information about the candidate. Please answer each question as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. **Rate the candidate's traits**: For select-one fields, choose the best rating that reflects your honest assessment of the candidate's spiritual maturity, leadership ability, and other traits.
3. **Provide specific examples**: When answering open-ended questions (like "Applicant Primary Strengths"), provide concrete examples that demonstrate the candidate's strengths and areas for growth.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name of Applicant** (`name: applicant_name_ref_ministry`): This field asks for the name of the individual seeking the position in the camp ministry.
* **Your Full Name (Reference)** (`name: reference_name_ministry`): As the reference, please enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Church Affiliation** (`name: reference_church_affiliation`): Enter the name of the church or organization you are affiliated with.
* **Your Phone or Email Address** (`name: reference_contact_info_ministry`): Please provide your phone number or email address for any follow-up questions or correspondence.
* **How long have you known the applicant?** (`name: length_relationship_ministry`): Estimate the length of time you have known the applicant, whether it's in a professional or personal capacity.
* **Evaluation of Spiritual Maturity and Character** (`name: character_ministry_eval_header`): This section assesses the candidate's spiritual maturity and character. Please rate their:
	+ **Spiritual Maturity and Commitment** (`name: spiritual_maturity_rating`): Choose how well the candidate exhibits spiritual maturity and commitment.
	+ **Leadership and Initiative** (`name: leadership_ability_rating`): Rate the candidate's leadership skills and initiative.
	+ **Suitability for Working with Youth** (`name: work_with_youth_suitability_ministry`): Assess the candidate's ability to work effectively with youth.
	+ **Emotional Resilience and Stability** (`name: emotional_resilience_rating`): Evaluate the candidate's emotional resilience and stability, especially under stress and long hours.
	+ **Integrity and Honesty** (`name: integrity_honesty_ministry_rating`): Rate the candidate's integrity and honesty in their work and personal life.
* **Applicant Primary Strengths** (`name: applicant_primary_strengths_ministry`): Describe how the candidate's strengths will contribute to their success in this role.
* **Areas for Further Development** (`name: applicant_areas_for_growth_ministry`): Identify any areas where the candidate may need growth or improvement.
* **Recommend for Ministry Role** (`name: recommend_for_ministry_role`): Choose how strongly you recommend the candidate for the position.
* **Electronic Signature (Typed Name)** (`name: reference_ministry_signature`): Enter your name as it appears on official documents, certifying that your reference is true and given in good faith.
* **Date of Reference** (`name: reference_ministry_date`): Enter the date you're providing this reference.
