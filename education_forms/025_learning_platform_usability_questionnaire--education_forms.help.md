### Form: learning_platform_usability_questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user feedback and usability data to improve the learning platform's usability and user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by filling in your user feedback**: In the "User Feedback" field, please provide any comments or suggestions you have about your experience on the learning platform. This will help us understand how we can better serve you.
2. **Report your platform usage**: In the "Platform Usage" field, please indicate how often you use the platform, using the number scale (e.g., 1-5 times a week, 1-2 times a month, etc.).
3. **Set your learning goals**: Choose all the learning goals that apply to you from the "Learning Goals" field. This will help us understand what you want to achieve on the platform.
4. **Select a platform feature**: In the "Platform Features" field, select the feature that is most important to you.
5. **Report the time you spent on the platform**: In the "Time Spent" field, report the total time you spent on the platform.
6. **Leave comments**: In the "Comments" field, provide any additional comments or feedback you have.
7. **Share your contact information**: In the "Contact Info" field, provide your email address or phone number so we can get in touch with you.
8. **Share your phone number**: In the "Phone Number" field, provide your phone number for further communication.
