# LAUSD Media Release Form (English) - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain media release permission from parents or guardians for various educational media productions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the required information about yourself and your child.
2. Select the type of media release you are comfortable with your child being featured in.
3. Choose the purpose of the media release.
4. Enter the date and time of the media production.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, Optional): Please enter the first name of the parent or guardian.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, Optional): Please enter the last name of the parent or guardian.
* **School ID** (`school_id`, select multiple, Optional): Select the LAUSD school your child attends.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, Optional): Enter the date of birth of the child in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Address** (`address`, text, Optional): Enter the address of the parent or guardian.
* **City** (`city`, text, Optional): Enter the city of the parent or guardian.
* **State** (`state`, text, Optional): Enter the state of the parent or guardian.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, Optional): Enter the zip code of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent name** (`parent_name`, text, Optional): Please enter your name.
* **Parent phone** (`parent_phone`, text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Parent email** (`parent_email`, email, Optional): Enter your email address.
* **School district** (`school_district`, text, Optional): Enter the name of the LAUSD school district your child attends.
* **School grade** (`school_grade`, text, Optional): Enter the grade of your child.
* **Media release type** (`media_release_type`, select one, Optional): Choose the type of media release. 
  *  **photographer**: A single photo opportunity.
  *  **videographer**: A video opportunity.
  *  **photographer-videographer**: Both a photo and video opportunity.
* **Media release purpose** (`media_release_purpose`, select multiple, Optional): Choose the purpose of the media release.
  *  **photo**: To be featured in a photo.
  *  **interview**: To be interviewed.
  *  **video**: To be featured in a video.
  *  **audio**: To be recorded with audio.
  *  **broadcast**: To be featured in a live broadcast.
  *  **live**: To be featured in a live event.
  *  **social media**: To be featured on social media.
* **Media release date** (`media_release_date`, date, Optional): Enter the date of the media production.
* **Media release time** (`media_release_time`, time, Optional): Enter the time of the media production.
* **Parent note** (`parent_note`, note, Optional): Any additional comments or information from the parent or guardian.
* **Student name** (`student_name`, text, Optional): Please enter the name of your child.
* **Student grade** (`student_grade`, text, Optional): Enter your child's grade level.
* **Student date of birth** (`student_date_of_birth`, date, Optional): Enter the date of birth of your child in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Student address** (`student_address`, text, Optional): Enter the address of your child.
* **Student city** (`student_city`, text, Optional): Enter the city of your child.
* **Student state** (`student_state`, text, Optional): Enter the state of your child.
* **Student zip** (`student_zip`, text, Optional): Enter the zip code of your child.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure that the media release is valid.
* If you are unsure about any information, please do not hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or HR department.
* This form is not intended for official LAUSD business. It's only for external media productions.
