# Engineering Final Exam Review Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students about their experience with the engineering final exam review sessions. The feedback will help instructors and the institution improve the effectiveness of these sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Review Session" field with the ID number of the session you attended.
2. Rate the effectiveness of the session by selecting a number from 1-5.
3. Provide any suggestions you have for improving future review sessions in the "Suggestions" field.
4. Choose the type of session you attended from the "Session Type" dropdown menu.
5. Select the instructors who taught the session from the "Instructor" field.
6. Choose the department associated with the session from the "Department" field.
7. Provide feedback about your experience in the "Student Feedback" field.
8. Provide feedback about the instructor's performance in the "Instructor Feedback" field.
9. Enter the review session ID and session ID numbers.
10. Enter the total number of students who attended the session.
11. Select the length of the session.
12. Provide feedback about your experience in the "Student Feedback 2" field.
13. Provide feedback about the instructor's performance in the "Instructor Feedback 2" field.
14. Choose the department associated with the session from the "Departmental" field.
15. Enter the date and time of the session.
16. Enter the duration of the session.
17. Provide feedback about your experience in the "Student Feedback 3" field.
18. Provide feedback about the instructor's performance in the "Instructor Feedback 3" field.
19. Choose the department associated with the session from the "Departmental 2" field.
20. Select the type of feedback you have about the session from the "Feedback" field.
21. Provide feedback about your experience in the "Review Session 2" field.
22. Enter any additional notes about the session in the "Session Notes" field.
