# Course Registration Form CCC - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Course Registration Form CCC" is intended to collect information about course registrations. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill in the required information for each field.
- Ensure that you provide correct and accurate information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Course Code** (`course_code`, text, required: false): Enter the course code for the course you wish to register for.
- **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the course.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
- **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the start date of the course.
- **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the end date of the course.
- **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required: false): Enter the start time of the course.
- **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required: false): Enter the end time of the course.
- **Registration Status** (`registration_status`, select_one, required: false): Choose your registration status from Registered, Waiting List, or Cancelled.
- **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false): Enter any additional comments about the course.
- **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name.
- **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (`student_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submission.
* Use correct formatting for date and time inputs.
* Ensure that you provide valid email addresses.
* If a field is not required, you may leave it blank if it's not applicable to your situation.
