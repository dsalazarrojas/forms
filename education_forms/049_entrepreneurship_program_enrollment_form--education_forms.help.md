# entrepreneurship_program_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The entrepreneurship program enrollment form is designed to gather information from new participants, allowing us to tailor the program's services and ensure a smooth experience for each individual. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, denoted by an asterisk \*.
2. Enter your name and contact details (name, email, phone, and date of birth).
3. If applicable, select the options for "select_one" and "select_multiple" fields.
4. If you need to add any additional information, use the "note" field.
5. Finally, provide your availability date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, text, not required): Please enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address to stay updated on program news and updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, not required): Enter your date of birth for age verification purposes.
* **Time** (`time`, time, not required): Enter your availability time for scheduling purposes.
* **Note** (`note`, note, not required): Add any additional information or special requirements you may have.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, not required): Choose one option from the dropdown list. 
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, not required): Select multiple options from the dropdown list. 
* **Date** (`date`, date, not required): Enter your availability date for scheduling purposes.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are already enrolled in the program, please do not fill out this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [insert contact information].
