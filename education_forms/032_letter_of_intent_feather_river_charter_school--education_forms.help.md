# Letter Of Intent   Feather River Charter School - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Letter of Intent form is used to gather information about the student, their parents, and emergency contacts to ensure that everyone involved is on the same page.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields with the relevant information. You can navigate between the different pages using the navigation buttons.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of the student.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): If known, please enter the student's date of birth.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of the student's parent or guardian.
* **Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of the student's guardian if different from the parent.
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, text, optional): Please enter the student's home address.
* **Parent Address** (`parent_address`, text, optional): If different from the student's address, please enter the parent's address.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of the student's emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Please enter the phone number of the student's emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): Please enter the relationship between the student and their emergency contact person (e.g., mother, father, aunt, etc.).

## Tips
* Be sure to fill in all fields accurately and completely to ensure that your Letter of Intent is comprehensive and effective.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with school staff for guidance.
* This form is for administrative purposes only and will not be used to influence decisions about your education or progress at Feather River Charter School.
