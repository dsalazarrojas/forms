# Education Content Distribution Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request educational content for distribution to schools and educational institutions.

## How To Complete This Form

### Before Submitting
Before proceeding, please ensure you have all the required information available. This includes:

* School name
* School email
* School phone number
* School address
* Content name
* Content type (book, article, video, document)
* Content format (pdf, docx, pptx, xls, jpg, png, gif, mp3, zip, mp4, txt)
* Content version (latest, older, other)
* Quantity
* Language
* Content request date
* Content request time
* Name, job title, and department of the person submitting the request
* Email and phone number of the person submitting the request
* Any additional comments

### Step 1: Fill Out School Information
* School Name: Enter the name of your school.
* School Email: Enter the email address of your school.
* School Phone: Enter the phone number of your school.
* School Address: Enter the address of your school.

### Step 2: Specify Content Details
* Content Name: Enter the name of the content you are requesting.
* Content Type: Select the type of content you are requesting (book, article, video, document).
* Content Format: Select the format of the content you are requesting (pdf, docx, pptx, xls, jpg, png, gif, mp3, zip, mp4, txt).
* Content Version: Select the version of the content you are requesting (latest, older, other).
* Quantity: Enter the quantity of the content you are requesting.

### Step 3: Specify Submission Information
* Language: Select the language of the content you are requesting.
* Content Request Date: Enter the date when you need the content.
* Content Request Time: Enter the time of day when you need the content.
* Assigned To: Enter the name of the person or team assigned to handle your request.
* Submitter Information:
	+ Submitter: Enter your name.
	+ Submitter Email: Enter your email address.
	+ Submitter Phone: Enter your phone number.
	+ Submitter Name: Enter your name.
	+ Submitter Job Title: Enter your job title.
	+ Submitter Department: Enter your department.
	+ Submitter Position: Enter your position.
	+ Submitter Job Title Ext: Enter your external job title.
	+ Submitter Email Ext: Enter your external email address.
	+ Submitter Phone Ext: Enter your external phone number.
	+ Submitter Dept Ext: Enter your external department.
	+ Submitter Position Ext: Enter your external position.
