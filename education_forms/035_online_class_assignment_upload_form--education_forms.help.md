# Online Class Assignment Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect online class assignments from students. Fill out this form to submit your assignments, making it easy for instructors to track and assess your work.

## How To Complete This Form
- Ensure you have all necessary documents and information before starting the form.
- Select the relevant file upload section (File Uploads).
- Enter your Student ID and File Name in the designated fields.
- Describe the file you are uploading in the File Description field.
- Choose the correct date and time for the Assignment Date and Submission Date (if applicable).
- Finally, click the submit button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **File Uploads**: Upload relevant files for your assignment, such as Word documents or images.
* **Student ID**: Enter your unique student ID assigned by your institution to identify yourself.
* **File Name**: Enter a descriptive name for the file you are uploading.
* **File Description**: Briefly describe the file you are uploading, so instructors know what to expect.
* **Assignment Date**: Enter the date the assignment was assigned to you.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date you are submitting the assignment.
* **Submission Time**: Enter the time you are submitting the assignment (if applicable). 

## Tips
- Use clear and concise file names and descriptions for better understanding.
- Double-check the dates and times you enter to ensure accuracy.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact your instructor or the course support team.
