# Opt Validation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and validate student information for educational settings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Ensure you have the necessary permissions or authority to submit this form for the student.
3. Double-check the options for select fields to ensure you're selecting the correct options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, **required**): Enter the name of the student.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, **optional**): Enter the ID number assigned to the student. If none is known, leave blank.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, **optional**): Enter the student's date of birth.
* **Country of Origin** (`country_of_origin`, text, **optional**): Enter the country where the student was born or originates from.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, **optional**): Enter the student's email address, if available.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, **optional**): Enter the student's contact phone number, if available.
* **Institution** (`institution`, text, **optional**): Enter the institution or school the student is affiliated with.
* **Program** (`program`, text, **optional**): Enter the program or course the student is enrolled in.
* **Student Status** (`student_status`, select_one, **required**): Select whether the student is a Student or Non-Student.
* **Graduation Date** (`graduation_date`, date, **optional**): Enter the student's graduation date, if applicable.
* **Education Level** (`student_education_level`, select_multiple, **required**): Select the student's education level (High, Low).
* **Student Employment Status** (`student_employment_status`, select_multiple, **optional**): Select the student's employment status (Employed, Unemployed, Self-Employed).
* **Ogp Status** (`student_ogp_status`, select_one, **required**): Select whether the student's OGP status is True or False.
* **OAI Status** (`student_oai_status`, select_one, **required**): Select whether the student’s OAI status is True or False.
