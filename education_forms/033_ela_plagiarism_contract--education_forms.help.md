# ELA Plagiarism Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document the student's understanding and agreement regarding plagiarism and other academic honesty issues. It's essential to use this form when a student's work is suspected of being plagiarized or when there is a need to establish a record of the student's understanding and agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the introduction page (page 1) to understand the purpose and scope of the form.
2. On the "Student Name" page (page 2), enter the student's name as it appears on their official records.
3. On the "Class Name" page (page 3), enter the name of the class or course the student is enrolled in.
4. On the "Parent Permission" page (page 4), select whether the parent has given permission for the student to participate in the class or activity.
5. On the "Signature Date" page (page 5), enter the date when the parent's signature is required.
6. On the "Teacher Signature" page (page 6), select whether the teacher has given permission for the student to participate in the class or activity.
7. On the "Student Signature" page (page 7), select whether the student has agreed to the terms of the form.
8. On the "Academic Honesty" page (page 8), select whether the student has agreed to uphold academic honesty and integrity.
9. On the "Teacher Name" page (page 9), enter the name of the teacher signing the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: A brief overview of the purpose and scope of the form.
* **Student Name**: Enter the student's name as it appears on their official records. (Type: Text, Required: False)
* **Class Name**: Enter the name of the class or course the student is enrolled in. (Type: Text, Required: False)
* **Parent Permission**: Select whether the parent has given permission for the student to participate in the class or activity. (Type: Select Multiple, Required: False)
	+ "Yes" means the parent has given permission.
	+ "No" means the parent has not given permission.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date when the parent's signature is required. (Type: Date, Required: False)
* **Teacher Signature**: Select whether the teacher has given permission for the student to participate in the class or activity. (Type: Select One, Required: False)
	+ "Yes" means the teacher has given permission.
	+ "No" means the teacher has not given permission.
* **Student Signature**: Select whether the student has agreed to the terms of the form. (Type: Select One, Required: False)
	+ "Yes" means the student has agreed to the terms.
	+ "No" means the student has not agreed to the terms.
* **Academic Honesty**: Select whether the student has agreed to uphold academic honesty and integrity. (Type: Select Multiple, Required: False)
	+ "Yes" means the student has agreed to uphold academic honesty and integrity.
	+ "No" means the student has not agreed to uphold academic honesty and integrity.
* **Teacher Name**: Enter the name of the teacher signing the form. (Type: Text, Required: False)
* **Signature**: The student and teacher signatures are not displayed on this page, as they are meant to be filled out on a separate sheet of paper or electronically.

## Tips
* Ensure the student and teacher signatures are collected on a separate sheet of paper or electronically.
* Use this form when there is a suspected case of plagiarism or a need to establish a record of the student's understanding and agreement.
* Review the introduction page before starting the form to ensure understanding of the purpose and scope.
* Be clear and concise when explaining the purpose and expectations to the student.
