# Student Technology Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Technology Survey is designed to gather information about your personal technology habits, preferences, and behaviors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question according to your personal experience and habits.
2. Be honest and accurate when reporting your technology usage.
3. If you have a primary device, select it from the list.
4. If you have other devices, you can specify them in the text fields.
5. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Technology Use** (`General Technology Use`, note, optional): This field is a general question to gauge your overall technology use. Please provide a brief description of your technology use.
* **Devices Used At Home** (`Devices Used At Home`, select_multiple, optional): Select all devices you use at home, such as Laptops, Smartphones, Tablets, Smartwatches, Gaming Consoles, etc.
* **Primary Device Used** (`Primary Device Used`, select_one, optional): Select the primary device you use most frequently, such as Laptop, Smartphone, Tablet, etc.
* **How Long Do You Leave Your Device On** (`How Long Do You Leave Your Device On`, time, optional): Please report the average amount of time you leave your device on per session.
* **What Time Do You Start Your Day** (`What Time Do You Start Your Day`, time, optional): Report the average time you start your day by using your device.
* **Average Screen Time** (`Average Screen Time`, number, optional): Please report the average screen time per week.
* **Average Screen Time Per Day** (`Average Screen Time Per Day`, number, optional): Report the average screen time per day.
* **Favorite Applications** (`Favorite Applications`, text, optional): List your favorite applications used on your primary device.
* **Favorite Applications Ever** (`Favorite Applications Ever`, text, optional): List any other favorite applications used.
* **How Do You Get Internet** (`How Do You Get Internet`, select_one, optional): Select how you get internet, such as Wi-Fi, Mobile, Other.
* **Other Internet Source** (`Other Internet Source`, text, optional): Please specify other internet sources.
* **How Many Days In A Week Do You Use Your Device** (`How Many Days In A Week Do You Use Your Device`, select_multiple, optional): Select how many days in a week you use your device.
* **What Time Do You Start Your Device** (`What Time Do You Start Your Device`, time, optional): Report the average time you start using your device.
* **Average Battery Life** (`Average Battery Life`, number, optional): Please report the average battery life of your device.
* **Favorite Social Media** (`Favorite Social Media`, select_multiple, optional): Select all your favorite social media platforms, such as Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, etc.
* **Other Social Media** (`Other Social Media`, text, optional): Please specify other social media platforms.
* **What Time Do You Stop Your Device** (`What Time Do You Stop Your Device`, time, optional): Report the average time you stop using your device.
* **Favorite Games** (`Favorite Games`, text, optional): List your favorite games played on your primary device.
* **Other Games** (`Other Games`, text, optional): Please specify other games played.
* **Average Sleep Time Before Bed** (`Average Sleep Time Before Bed`, time, optional): Report the average time you start sleeping before using your device.
* **Average Screen Time Before Bed** (`Average Screen Time Before Bed`, number, optional): Report the average screen time before bed.
* **Favorite Music** (`Favorite Music`, text, optional): List your favorite music platforms or apps.
* **Other Music** (`Other Music`, text, optional): Please specify other music platforms or apps.
* **Average Screen Time Before Bed Ever** (`Average Screen Time Before Bed Ever`, number, optional): Report the average screen time before bed.
* **How Do You Charge Your Device** (`How Do You Charge Your Device`, select_multiple, optional): Select how you charge your device, such as Plug, Wireless, Other.
* **Other Charge Methods** (`Other Charge Methods`, text, optional): Please specify other charge methods.

Note: If you are unsure about any question, please answer according to your best judgment.
