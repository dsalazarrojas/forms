# Social Innovation In Education Fellowship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from applicants to assess their eligibility and suitability for the Social Innovation In Education Fellowship. The information provided will be used to evaluate your background, experiences, and goals as an education innovator.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Provide your name and email address** (fields 2 and 4): Enter your name and a valid email address that you have access to.
2. **Enter your school name and other details** (field 3): If applicable, enter the name of your current or previous school, or any other relevant details about your educational background.
3. **Enter your contact information** (field 5): Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
4. **Select your highest education level attained** (field 7): Choose the highest level of education you have completed, such as High School, Bachelor's, Master's, or Doctorate.
5. **Choose your current occupation** (field 8): Select your current occupation or role, such as Student, Teacher, or Other.
6. **Describe your fellowship goals** (field 9): Briefly explain your goals for the fellowship, including what you hope to achieve or learn through this opportunity.
7. **Enter your motivation statement** (field 10): Write a short statement explaining why you are interested in and suited for the fellowship.
8. **Attach a resume or CV** (field 11): Upload a copy of your resume or CV, showcasing your education, work experience, and achievements.
9. **Describe your project (if applicable)** (fields 12-16): If you are applying for a project or initiative related to the fellowship, provide a brief description, project link, and other relevant details (fields 12-16).
10. **Submit your application** (field 24): Once you have completed all the required fields, submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Form** (field 1): This is the title of the form, used for identification purposes.
* **Name** (field 2): Enter your full name as it should appear on your application.
* **School Name** (field 3): If you have attended or currently attend a school, enter the name here. (Optional)
* **Email** (field 4): Enter a valid email address that you have access to. (Required)
* **Phone** (field 5): Enter your contact phone number. (Required)
* **Highest Education Level Attained** (field 7): Choose the highest level of education you have completed.
* **Current Occupation** (field 8): Select your current occupation or role.
* **Fellowship Goals** (field 9): Describe your goals for the fellowship, including what you hope to achieve or learn.
* **Motivation Statement** (field 10): Write a short statement explaining why you are interested in and suited for the fellowship.
* **Resume** (field 11): Upload a copy of your resume or CV, showcasing your education, work experience, and achievements.
* **Portfolio** (field 12): If applicable, attach a link or description of your portfolio, showcasing your work and experiences. (Optional)
* **Project Description** (field 13): If applicable, provide a brief description of your project or initiative. (Optional)
* **Team Members** (field 14): If applicable, list the names and roles of team members involved in your project or initiative. (Optional)
* **Project Link** (field 15): If applicable, provide a link to your project or initiative. (Optional)
* **Project Status** (field 16): If applicable, describe the current status of your project or initiative. (Optional)
* **Project Duration** (field 17): If applicable, enter the duration of your project or initiative. (Optional)
* **Start Date** (field 18): If applicable, enter the start date of your project or initiative. (Optional)
* **End Date** (field 19): If applicable, enter the end date of your project or initiative. (Optional)
* **Budget** (field 20): If applicable, enter the budget for your project or initiative. (Optional)
* **Location** (field 21): If applicable, enter the location of your project or initiative. (Optional)
* **Project Tags** (field 22): If applicable, enter relevant tags for your project or initiative. (Optional)
* **Project Notes** (field 23): If applicable, enter any additional notes about your project or initiative. (Optional)
* **Submit** (field 24): Once you have completed all the required fields, click this button to submit your application.

Please note that the fellowship program is looking for education innovators and professionals, so ensure that your background and interests align with this focus area before proceeding.
