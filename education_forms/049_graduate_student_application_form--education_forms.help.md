# Graduate Student Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Graduate Student Application Form is designed to collect information from graduate students who are applying for admission to a program. This form will be reviewed and processed by the admissions committee to determine eligibility for admission.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that your application is processed correctly.
2. Enter your personal details, including your contact information.
3. Provide your academic history, education background, and other relevant information.
4. Select the program(s) you are applying for.
5. Choose your current student status.
6. Select the degree you are pursuing (if applicable).
7. Choose your current application status.
8. Choose what to do with your application (submit, save for later, or cancel).
9. Enter your date of birth and application dates (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Details**: Enter your full name, address, and other relevant contact information.
* **Academic History**: Provide information about your academic background, including any relevant coursework, degrees, and research experience.
* **Education Background**: Enter any relevant education history, including schools attended and degrees earned.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your full name, address, phone number, and email address.
* **Application Form**: This field is not currently used and can be ignored.
* **Student Status**: Select your current student status, which should reflect your current enrollment status (e.g. In Progress, Completed, Deferred, Denied).
* **Program Selection**: Select all programs you are applying for. For example, if you are applying for both the Master's and PhD programs, select both options.
* **Degree Selection**: Select the degree you are pursuing (if applicable). For example, Bachelor, Master, or Doctoral.
* **Application Status**: Select your current application status, which should reflect where you are in the application process (e.g. In Progress, Completed, Withdrawn).
* **Submit Application**: Choose what to do with your application, which should be either Submit, Save for Later, or Cancel.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Date of Application**: Enter the date you submitted your application.
* **Date of Entry**: Enter the date you expect to enter the program (if applicable).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your application is processed correctly.
* Use the format specified for date fields (e.g. MM/DD/YYYY).
* Only select programs and degree options that apply to you.
* Review your application carefully before submitting it.
