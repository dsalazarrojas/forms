# Educational Outreach Film Recording Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for participants in educational outreach programs to provide their consent for filming and recording.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Provide your name and contact information on Page 1: Participant Information.
3. Answer the question about filming details on Page 2: Filming Details.
4. Answer the question about filming location on Page 3: Filming Location.
5. Select your consent on Page 4: Consent.
6. Add any additional information on Page 5: Additional Information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information (Page 1)**: Provide your name and contact information to identify yourself as a participant.
* **Filming Details (Page 2)**: Describe the details of the filming, such as the date, time, and location.
* **Filming Location (Page 3)**: Enter the location where the filming will take place.
* **Consent (Page 4)**: Select "Yes" if you give your consent for the filming and recording, or "No" if you do not.
* **Additional Information (Page 5)**: Provide any additional comments or context relevant to your participation.

## Tips
* Be accurate and truthful when providing your information.
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the form administrator at form@gic.mx.
