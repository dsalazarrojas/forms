# mba_program_giveaway_entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users interested in entering the M.B.A. program giveaway. It helps us gather information about you and your program preferences to determine eligibility for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out your user information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide details about the M.B.A. program you are interested in, including the program name and link.
3. Write down your motivation for applying to the M.B.A. program.
4. Repeat the motivation step for up to 10 iterations, as you have multiple reasons why you want to be part of the program.
5. Submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. User Info
* **User Info** (`user_info`, `text`, required: false): This is the first section of the form where you will provide basic contact information about yourself. Enter your name, email, and phone number to help us get in touch with you if you are selected for the program.

### 2. Program Info
* **Program Info** (`program_info`, `text`, required: false): This section is where you can provide more details about the M.B.A. program you are interested in. Please enter the name of the program and its corresponding link if available.

### 3. Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): This is your email address where we will contact you if you are selected for the program. Please ensure it is a valid email.

### 4. Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): This is your phone number where we can reach you for further information or updates about the program.

### 5. Program Name
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the M.B.A. program you are interested in.

### 6. Program Link
* **Program Link** (`program_link`, `text`, required: false): If available, enter the link to the M.B.A. program's website.

### 7. Additional Link
* **Additional Link** (`program_link2`, `text`, required: false): This is an additional link related to the program, if available.

### 8-10. Motivation
* **Motivation** (`motivation`, `text`, required: false): You will have the opportunity to provide up to 10 reasons why you want to be part of the M.B.A. program. This will help us understand your interest and motivation for applying.
