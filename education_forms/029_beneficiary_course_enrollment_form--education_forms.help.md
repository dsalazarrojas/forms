# Beneficiary Course Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from beneficiaries for the course enrollment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

* Ensure you have all the necessary information and details to fill out the form accurately.
* Fill out the form in a clear and readable format.
* Review your answers carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, optional): Select your gender from the options provided.
* **Street Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your street address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter your city or town name.
* **State/Province** (`state_province`, text, required): Enter your state or province name.
* **Zip/Postal Code** (`zip_postal`, text, required): Enter your zip or postal code.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the course you are enrolling in.
* **Course Category** (`course_category`, select_one, required): Select the category of the course from the options provided.
* **Course Start Date** (`course_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the course.
* **Course Duration** (`course_duration`, select_one, required): Select the duration of the course from the options provided.
* **Training Format** (`training_format`, select_one, required): Select the training format from the options provided.
* **Motivation for Taking This Course** (`motivation`, text, required): Enter your motivation for taking the course.
* **Learning Goals** (`learning_goals`, text, optional): Enter your learning goals for the course.
* **Prior Experience** (`prior_experience`, select_one, optional): Select your prior experience in the field from the options provided.
* **Special Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, text, optional): If you require any special accommodations, enter them here.
* **Financial Assistance Needed** (`financial_assistance`, select_one, optional): Select if you need financial assistance for the course.
* **Agree to Course Terms and Conditions** (`agreement_terms`, select_one, required): Select if you agree to the course terms and conditions.
* **Enrollment Date** (`enrollment_date`, date, required): The system will automatically populate this field with the current date.
