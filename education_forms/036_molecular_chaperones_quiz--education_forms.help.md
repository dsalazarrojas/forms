# Molecular Chaperones Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Molecular Chaperones Quiz is a form that aims to assess the knowledge of educators on the topic of molecular chaperones, specifically their role in protein folding and the differences between protein structure and complex.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the correct options for each question and provide any additional comments if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Quiz questions (Select multiple)**: Choose the correct answers for the quiz questions by selecting the options in the dropdown list.
* **What is the process of protein folding?** (`answer_1`): This is a free-text field where you can provide your answer to the question. Please type your response in the text area.
* **What is the role of chaperones in protein folding?** (`answer_2`): This is a select-one field where you can choose the correct option from the list.
* **What is the relationship between protein structure and function?** (`answer_3`): This is a free-text field where you can provide your answer to the question. Please type your response in the text area.
* **What is the difference between a protein structure and a protein complex?** (`answer_4`): This is a free-text field where you can provide your answer to the question. Please type your response in the text area.
* **Submission comments** (`submission_comments`): This is a note field where you can add any additional comments or feedback on the quiz questions. Please type your response in the text area.
* **Educator email** (`submission_email`): This is an email field where you can provide your email address for contact purposes. Please type your email address in the format `example@example.com`.
* **Educator phone** (`submission_phone`): This is a text field where you can provide your phone number. Please type your phone number in the format `XXX-XXX-XXX`.
* **Educator name** (`submission_name`): This is a text field where you can provide your name. Please type your name in the format `John Doe`.
* **School name** (`submission_school`): This is a text field where you can provide your school name. Please type your school name in the format `School Name`.
