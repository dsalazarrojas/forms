# Jotform Reading Challenge Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Jotform Reading Challenge Log is designed to help teams track and maintain their reading habits, providing a platform to record progress, goals, and reflections.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form with your team's details.
2.  Select the relevant category for your team.
3.  Enter the number of team members.
4.  Choose the number of days per week you read.
5.  Set the start and finish dates for the challenge.
6.  Provide the location where you usually read.
7.  Add any notes or comments about your reading experience.
8.  Enter the start and finish dates for the challenge.
9.  Enter your email address.
10.  Enter your phone number.
11.  List the team members involved.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Reading Challenge Log** (`challenge_log`, text, optional): A text field where you can enter a brief description of your team's reading challenge.
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the category that best describes your team's reading challenge (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, number, optional): Enter the number of team members participating in the challenge.
* **Days per week** (`days_per_week`, select_one, optional): Select the number of days per week you usually read (e.g., 0-1 day, 1-2 days, etc.).
* **Start Date** (`reading_challenge_start`, date, optional): Enter the start date of your reading challenge.
* **Finish Date** (`finish_date`, date, optional): Enter the finish date of your reading challenge.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location where you usually read (e.g., school, library, etc.).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or reflections about your reading experience.
* **Challenge Start Date** (`challenge_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the challenge.
* **Challenge Finish Date** (`challenge_finish_date`, date, optional): Enter the finish date of the challenge.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, text, optional): List the team members involved in the challenge.

## Tips

* Ensure you have filled out all required fields accurately.
* Double-check your category selection for accuracy.
* Enter correct team member names for accurate tracking.
* Enter the correct start and finish dates for the challenge and reading log.
* Enter your email address and phone number correctly.
