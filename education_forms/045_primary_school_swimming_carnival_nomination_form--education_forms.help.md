# Primary School Swimming Carnival Nomination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a primary school swimming carnival. It's essential to fill out this form accurately to help the school track and manage student participation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name and grade level in the respective fields.
2. Provide the parent's name and contact phone number.
3. Enter the event date and time, and select the event location.
4. Briefly describe the event details.
5. Enter the teacher's email address and phone number.
6. Select the teacher's name and grade level.
7. Specify the teacher's department.
8. Choose the student's participation status (Registered or Not Registered).
9. Add any additional comments regarding the student's participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the student participating in the event.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, select_one, required): Select the grade level of the student participating in the event.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the parent or guardian of the student.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact phone number of the parent or guardian.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the event in 12-hour format (e.g., 09:00 AM).
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, optional): Enter the venue or location of the event.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, note, optional): Briefly describe the event in this field.
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the teacher associated with the student.
* **Teacher Phone** (`teacher_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the teacher.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, optional): Enter the full name of the teacher.
* **Teacher Grade** (`teacher_grade`, text, optional): Enter the grade level of the teacher.
* **Teacher Department** (`teacher_department`, text, optional): Enter the department of the teacher.
* **Student Carnival Status** (`student_carnival_status`, select_multiple, required): Choose the student's participation status in the event (Registered or Not Registered).
* **Comments** (`event_comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments regarding the student's participation.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Use the correct format for date and time fields.
* Proofread the form before submission to catch any errors.
