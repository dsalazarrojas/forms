# Student Housing Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and record student housing agreement information, including student personal data, provider information, and agreement terms. It is essential for both students and providers to accurately and truthfully complete the form to establish a valid agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the student information section with your personal details.
2. Provide your provider information, including their name and contact information.
3. Specify the start and end dates of the agreement.
4. Choose the room details, including number and type.
5. Sign and date the agreement to acknowledge your acceptance of the terms.
6. Review and agree to the terms by selecting "Yes" for each relevant question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. First Name 
* <strong>first-name</strong> (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name.

### 2. Last Name 
* <strong>last-name</strong> (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name.

### 3. Date of Birth 
* <strong>date-of-birth</strong> (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).

### 4. Student ID 
* <strong>student-id</strong> (`student_id`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your student ID number.

### 5. Guardian Name 
* <strong>guardian-name</strong> (`guardian_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your guardian or parent.

### 6. Guardian Contact 
* <strong>guardian-contact</strong> (`guardian_contact`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the contact information of your guardian or parent.

### 7. Provider Name 
* <strong>provider-name</strong> (`provider_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the provider or host family.

### 8. Provider Address 
* <strong>provider-address</strong> (`provider_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the address of the provider or host family.

### 9. Start Date 
* <strong>start-date</strong> (`start_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you will start living with the provider.

### 10. End Date 
* <strong>end-date</strong> (`end_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you will end living with the provider.

### 11. Room Number 
* <strong>room-number</strong> (`room_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the room number or apartment number you will be staying in.

### 12. Room Type 
* <strong>room-type</strong> (`room_type`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the type of room (e.g. single room, shared room, etc.).

### 13. Room Description 
* <strong>room-description</strong> (`room_description`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional details about the room.

### 14. Student Signature 
* <strong>student-signature</strong> (`student_signature`, `text`, required/optional): Sign your name to acknowledge you have read and agree to the terms.

### 15. Provider Signature 
* <strong>provider-signature</strong> (`provider_signature`, `text`, required/optional): Sign your name to acknowledge you have read and agree to the terms.

### 16. Date Signed 
* <strong>date-singed</strong> (`date_signed`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you signed the agreement.

### 17. Student Agreed 
* <strong>student-agreed</strong> (`student_agreed`, `select_one`, required/true): Select "Yes" to agree to the terms.

### 18. Provider Agreed 
* <strong>provider-agreed</strong> (`provider_agreed`, `select_one`, required/true): Select "Yes" to agree to the terms.

### 19. Terms Accepted 
* <strong>terms-accepted</strong> (`terms_accepted`, `select_one`, required/true): Select "Yes" to acknowledge you have read and agree to the terms.

### 20. Date Signed 1 
* <strong>date-signed-1</strong> (`date_signed1`, `date`, required/false): Enter the date you signed the agreement.

### 21. Provider Notes 
* <strong>provider-notes</strong> (`provider_notes`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes from the provider.

### 22. Student Notes 
* <strong>student-notes</strong> (`student_notes`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes from the student.

### 23. Student Agreement 
* <strong>student-agreement</strong> (`student_agreement`, `text`, required/false): Enter any additional agreement terms from the student.

### 24. Provider Agreement 
* <strong>provider-agreement</strong> (`provider_agreement`, `text`, required/false): Enter any additional agreement terms from the provider.

### 25. Form Version 
* <strong>form-version</strong> (`form_version`, `text`, required/false): Enter the current version of the form.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are filled in accurately and truthfully.
- Double-check dates in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
- Sign and date all agreements to create a record of your acceptance of the terms.
- Review the terms carefully before selecting "Yes" to agree.
