# data_driven_instruction_workshop_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information for the data-driven instruction workshop. Please fill out the form accurately to help us efficiently manage the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, please go through each page (participant_details, workshop_schedule, registration, participant_email, and participant_phone).
2. Fill out each field as instructed and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, optional): Enter your name, title, or any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Workshop Schedule** (`workshop_schedule`, text, optional): If you have specific schedule requirements or requests, please mention them here.
* **Registration** (`registration`, text, optional): If you have any special registration requests or needs, please provide them here.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Enter your work email address for communication purposes.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number for any follow-up communication.

## Tips
* Fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure we have the necessary information for the workshop.
* If you have any doubts or concerns about the form, please contact us at the provided email address.
* Submission of this form confirms that you are aware of and agree with our [terms and conditions].
