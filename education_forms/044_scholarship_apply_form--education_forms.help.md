# Scholarship Apply Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for students to submit their application for a scholarship. Please fill out this form carefully and honestly to ensure your chances of receiving the scholarship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details:
	* Provide your student name as it appears on your official records.
	* Enter your email address for communication purposes.
2. Select your academic program:
	* Choose the relevant program for your current academic level.
3. Upload your scholarship essay:
	* Explain why you deserve the scholarship and how it will benefit you.
4. Provide your contact information:
	* Enter your student address for communication and any additional contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your official records.
* **Email** (`student_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **GPA** (`gpa`, number, optional): Enter your current GPA.
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, text, optional): Enter your student address for communication purposes.
* **Student** (`student`, note, optional): This field is currently not applicable.
* **Academic Program** (`academic_program`, select_one, optional): Choose the relevant program for your current academic level.
* **Scholarship Essay** (`scholarship_essay`, text, optional): Write a short essay explaining why you deserve the scholarship and how it will benefit you.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, optional): Enter your student phone number for communication purposes.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have reviewed and understood the terms and conditions of the scholarship before submitting your application.
* Check your email for any notifications or updates about your application status.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
* Please be honest and accurate in filling out this form to ensure a smooth evaluation process.
