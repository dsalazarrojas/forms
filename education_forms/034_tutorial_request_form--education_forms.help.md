# tutorial_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about tutorials or guides you'd like to request. This includes the subject, topic, and any additional details you'd like to provide. The form also allows you to specify your preferred method and time of contact for further communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the "request_tutorial" page and enter the title of your tutorial request.
2.  In the "subject_area" field, enter the subject area you'd like to focus on.
3.  In the "topic_area" field, specify the topic within that subject area.
4.  In the "description" field, provide a brief description of your request.
5.  If you have a preferred method of contact, select it in the "preferred_contact_method" field.
6.  Specify your preferred contact time in the "preferred_contact_time" field.
7.  In the "assigned_tool" field, select the tool you'd like to be assigned for your tutorial.
8.  If you have a specific email address for communication, enter it in the "email" field.
9.  If you'd like to provide a phone number for contact, enter it in the "phone_number" field.
10. Finally, select the tool you'd like to be assigned to you from the "assigned_tool" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### request_tutorial (Page 1)
*   **Request Tutorial**: This is the title of your tutorial request. Enter a brief title that captures the essence of your request.
*   **Subject Area**: This field is where you enter the main subject area you'd like to focus on for your tutorial.
*   **Topic Area**: This field is for specifying the topic within that subject area.
*   **Description**: Here, you provide a detailed description of your tutorial request.
### subject (Page 2)
*   **Subject Area**: This is where you specify the main subject area of your tutorial request.
### topic (Page 2)
*   **Topic Area**: This field is for specifying the topic within that subject area.
### preferred_contact_method (Page 3)
*   **Preferred Contact Method**: This field allows you to select how you'd like to be contacted for further communication. You can choose between Contact me via email, Contact me via phone, or Contact me via chat.
### preferred_contact_time (Page 4)
*   **Preferred Contact Time**: Here, you specify when you'd like to be contacted. Choose from Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Weekend.
### assigned_tool (Page 5)
*   **Assigned Tool**: This field allows you to select the tool you'd like to be assigned for your tutorial.
### email (Page 6)
*   **Email**: If you have a specific email address where you can be contacted, enter it here.
### phone_number (Page 7)
*   **Phone Number**: If you'd like to provide a phone number for contact, enter it here.
### assigned_tool (Page 8)
*   **Assigned Tool**: This field is for selecting the tool you'd like to be assigned to you for your tutorial.
