# School Counselor Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The School Counselor Application Form is a crucial document that helps us evaluate your application for a school counselor position. Please provide as much detail as possible to increase your chances of being considered for this role.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read each field explanation below to understand what information is required.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and relevant information about yourself.
3. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Counselor Form Data** (`school_counselor_form_data`, text, optional): This field is for any additional comments or information about your application that does not fit into other fields. Please use this space to provide any relevant background or context that you think we should know.
* **Applicant Info** (`applicant_info`, text, optional): Provide a brief summary of your background and experience.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Include your contact information, such as phone number and email address.
* **Education Background** (`education_background`, text, optional): Share your educational background and qualifications.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, optional): Describe your work experience and relevant skills.
* **Education History** (`education_history`, text, optional): List your educational background, including degrees earned and institutions attended.
* **Personal Details** (`personal_details`, text, optional): Provide any relevant personal details that may impact your application.
* **References** (`references`, text, optional): List any relevant professional references.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select one, optional): Select 'Active' if you are currently employed or 'Inactive' if you are not.
* **Job Preferences** (`job_preferences`, select multiple, optional): Indicate your willingness to work in a variety of settings, such as 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Salary Range** (`salary_range`, number, optional): Provide your desired salary range.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Indicate the title you would prefer for the position.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Provide your date of birth.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Include any other contact information you wish to share.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Provide your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Include your phone number.
* **Upload Resume** (`upload_resume`, text, optional): Upload your resume for easy reference.
* **Upload Transcript** (`upload_transcript`, text, optional): Upload your transcript for further evaluation.
* **Upload Certifications** (`upload_certifications`, text, optional): Upload any relevant certifications.
* **Upload Other** (`upload_other`, text, optional): Upload any other supporting documents.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Use this field to add any further comments or information.
* **Application Type** (`application_type`, select one, optional): Indicate whether you are a 'Yes' or 'No' applicant.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Provide the name of the school you are applying to.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Indicate the department you are interested in applying to.
* **School Location** (`school_location`, text, optional): Provide the location of the school you are applying to.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as required.
