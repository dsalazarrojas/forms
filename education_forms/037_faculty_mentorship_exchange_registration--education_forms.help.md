# Faculty Mentorship Exchange Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Faculty Mentorship Exchange Registration form is used to register faculty members for the mentorship program. It is intended for faculty members to express their interest in participating in the mentorship program and provide information about their areas of expertise and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your Faculty Title from the dropdown menu.
2. Choose your Institution Type.
3. Select your Areas of Interest from the list.
4. Enter your Institution Name, Department, and Contact Information (Email, Phone, and Address).
5. Select your Program Type.
6. Enter your Program Name and Description.
7. Set your Program Start and End Dates.
8. Choose your Mentorship Status.
9. Enter your Mentorship Notes.
10. Select your Program Type again.
11. Enter your Program Name again.
12. Enter your Program Description again.
13. Set your Program Start and End Dates again.
14. Choose your Mentorship Program Type.
15. Enter your First and Last Name.
16. Enter your Job Title and Department.
17. Enter your Institution.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Faculty Title** (`faculty_title`, select_one, required): Select your current faculty title from the dropdown menu.
* **Institution Type** (`institution_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of institution you are affiliated with (University, College, Research Center, Department, Other).
* **Areas of Interest** (`mentorship_interests`, select_one, required): Select one or more areas of interest for the mentorship program (Academic and Research, Administrative, Community Involvement, Diversity and Inclusion, Mentoring, Research, Teaching and Learning).
* **Institution Name** (`institution_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your institution.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the name of your department.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, optional): Enter your contact address.
* **Program Participation** (`program_participation`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more program participation options (Mentoring, Research, Teaching, Service).
* **Program Start Date** (`program_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of your program.
* **Program End Date** (`program_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of your program.
* **Mentorship Status** (`mentorship_status`, select_one, optional): Choose your current mentorship status (Active, Inactive, Pending).
* **Mentorship Notes** (`mentorship_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about your mentorship experience.
* **Program Type** (`program_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more program types (Academic, Research, Administrative).
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your program.
* **Program Description** (`program_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your program.
* **Program Start Date/Time** (`program_start_date_time`, time, optional): Enter the start date and time of your program.
* **Program End Date/Time** (`program_end_date_time`, time, optional): Enter the end date and time of your program.
* **Mentorship Program** (`mentorship_program`, select_one, optional): Choose your mentorship program type (Both, Only Mentoring, Only Mentee).
* **First Name** (`contact_first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Job Title** (`contact_job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Department** (`contact_department`, text, optional): Enter your department.
* **Institution** (`contact_institution`, text, optional): Enter the name of your institution.

Note: Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely to ensure that you are effectively matched with a mentee and have a successful experience in the program.
