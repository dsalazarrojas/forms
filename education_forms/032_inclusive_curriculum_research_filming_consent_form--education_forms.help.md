# Inclusive Curriculum Research Filming Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to obtain informed consent from research participants before filming takes place. It ensures that participants understand the purpose, scope, and implications of the filming, and agree to participate.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question truthfully and carefully.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select the most accurate option.
3. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as possible.
4. Make sure to read the form carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (text):
	+ This is a brief overview of the filming project and its purpose.
* **Participant Information** (select multiple):
	+ Choose all participants who are involved in the filming.
* **Filming Details** (select one):
	+ Indicate whether the filming is taking place in a real-world setting or a simulated environment.
* **Filming Location** (text):
	+ Provide the exact location where the filming will take place.
* **Camera Angle** (number):
	+ Choose the angle at which the camera will be positioned (e.g., 90 degrees).
* **Camera Distance** (number):
	+ Choose the distance between the camera and the participant (e.g., 5 meters).
* **Consent Date** (date):
	+ Enter the date when the consent is given.
* **Consent Time** (time):
	+ Enter the time when the consent is given.
* **Notes** (note):
	+ Provide any additional comments or questions you may have about the filming.
* **Researcher Contact** (text):
	+ Provide your contact information for any further questions or concerns.
* **Emergency Contact** (text):
	+ Provide the contact information for an emergency contact person.
* **Contact Number** (text):
	+ Enter your phone number for any further questions or concerns.
* **Email** (email):
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Signature** (select multiple):
	+ Indicate whether you are willing to sign the consent form.
* **Consent Form Completed** (select multiple):
	+ Indicate whether the form is completed and signed.

## Tips

* If you have any concerns or questions, contact the research team.
* This form is not a legal document, and its completion is not a guarantee of consent.
* Make sure to read the form carefully before submitting.
