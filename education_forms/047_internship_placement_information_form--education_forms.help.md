# internship_placement_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The internship_placement_information_form is a tool used to gather relevant details about an internship opportunity. It helps in understanding your availability, education level, and preferences for an internship placement. This form is used to streamline the internship application process and ensure that all necessary information is captured accurately.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your **User Name**, which is a required field.
2. Enter your **Email** address in the format of [email@example.com](mailto:example.com).
3. Enter your **Phone** number for easy contact.
4. Select your **Date Available** for an internship. Please use the date format MM/DD/YYYY.
5. Choose your **Time Available** for an internship. Please use the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
6. Add any **Note** about your availability or preferences.
7. Choose the **Select One** option from the available options. Please select only one option.
8. Select multiple **Select Multiple** options from the available options.
9. Enter your **Education Level**, e.g., High School, Bachelor's, etc.
10. Enter your **School Name** or the name of the institution where you studied.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name (text, required)**: Enter your full name to help us identify you.
* **Email (email, required)**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone (text, required)**: Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Date Available (date, required)**: Select the date when you are available for an internship.
* **Time Available (time, required)**: Choose the time when you are available for an internship.
* **Note (note, optional)**: Add any additional information you'd like to share about your availability or preferences.
* **Select One (select_one, required)**: Choose one option from the available options.
* **Select Multiple (select_multiple, required)**: Select multiple options from the available options.
* **Education Level (text, required)**: Enter your education level, e.g., High School, Bachelor's, etc.
* **School Name (text, required)**: Enter the name of the institution where you studied.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate processing of your internship application.
* If you are unsure about the format of any field, please refer to the specific field's explanation above.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, please don't hesitate to contact us.
