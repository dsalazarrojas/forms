## Educational Growth Plan Consultation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about a student's educational growth plan. It's a tool to help students, parents, and teachers identify areas where the student needs improvement and determine the best course of action to support the student's educational growth.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  For each section, read the instructions carefully and answer the questions truthfully.
2.  Make sure to fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) or indicated as "Required" in the field-by-field explanation.
3.  If you are unsure about any field, don't hesitate to ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Goals** (`student_goals`, `text`, Required: False):
    This field is where you will write your short-term and long-term educational goals. Please list 2-3 specific goals that you want to achieve in your educational journey. Examples of goals might include:
    *   Improve your reading comprehension
    *   Increase your math grades
    *   Learn a new language

* **Academic Challenges** (`academic_challenges`, `select_multiple`, Required: False):
    This field asks about the challenges you face in your education. Please select all the challenges that apply:
    *   Yes, I am facing difficulties
    *   No, I am not facing difficulties

* **Educational Support** (`educational_support`, `select_one`, Required: False):
    This field asks about who provides you with support in your education. Please select one of the following:
    *   Student Advisor
    *   Parent
    *   Teacher

* **Student Performance Data** (`student_performance_data`, `date`, Required: False):
    This field is for you to record your performance data. Please fill out the date and add any relevant data about your performance, such as scores or grades.

* **Teacher Feedback** (`teacher_feedback`, `text`, Required: False):
    This field is for your teacher to provide feedback about your performance. Please ask your teacher to fill out this section, and they will provide guidance on how you can improve.

## Tips

*   Be honest and thorough when completing this form.
*   If you are unsure about any field, don't hesitate to ask for clarification.
*   Remember, this form is a tool to help you grow and improve your education. It's a collaborative effort between you, your teacher, and your support system to ensure you achieve your goals.
