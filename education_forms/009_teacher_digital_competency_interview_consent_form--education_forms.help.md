# Teacher Digital Competency Interview Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teacher Digital Competency Interview Consent Form is used to gather information about the teacher's digital competency interview schedule and consent status. This form helps ensure that the teacher is aware of and consents to the terms of service and schedule for the interview.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the teacher's details, including name, email, and phone number.
2. Schedule the interview by selecting a date and time that works for you.
3. Choose your consent status for the terms of service.
4. Select whether you consent to the schedule of the interview.
5. Sign the form with your signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Teacher Details (1)**: This field is for providing your name, email, and phone number. Please fill out this section with accurate and up-to-date information.
  * **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
  * **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for communication.
  * **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for communication.
- **Schedule Interview (2)**: This field is for selecting a date and time for the interview. Choose a date and time that works best for you.
- **Selected Date (3)**: This field is for confirming the selected date of the interview.
- **Teacher Notes (4)**: This field is for providing any additional notes or comments about the interview.
- **Consent Status (5)**: This field is for selecting whether you have read and understood the terms of service.
  * **Consent Status Options**: Choose 'True' if you have read and understood the terms of service, 'False' if you haven't, or 'Pending' if you need to review them further.
- **Teacher Consent (6)**: This field is for selecting whether you have read and agreed to the terms of service.
  * **Teacher Consent Options**: Choose 'I have read and understood the terms of service' if you have, 'I have read and agreed to the terms of service' if you've agreed, or 'I have read but not understood the terms of service' if you need to review them further.
- **Schedule Consent (7)**: This field is for selecting whether you consent to the schedule of the interview.
  * **Schedule Consent Options**: Choose 'True' if you consent, 'False' if you don't.
- **Email (8)**: This field is for entering your email address where you can be reached for communication.
- **Phone (9)**: This field is for entering your phone number where you can be reached for communication.
- **Teacher Signature (10)**: This field is for signing the form to confirm your consent and information.
