# open_educational_resource_contributor_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register contributors of open educational resources with the organization. It is meant to collect information about the contributor, their expertise, contribution, and submission details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Provide accurate and valid information for each field.
3. Ensure that the submission status and review status are unique for each contributor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Email** (required: true): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (required: true): Enter your phone number.
* **Expertise** (required: true): Describe your area of expertise.
* **Contribution** (required: true): Describe your contribution.
* **Institution** (required: true): Enter the name of your institution.
* **Onboarding Date** (required: true): Enter the date you were onboarded.
* **Review Date** (required: true): Enter the date of the review.
* **Review Status** (required: true, select one): Select the review status (pending, approved, or rejected).
* **Submission Status** (required: true, select one): Select the submission status (pending or submitted).
* **Submission Date** (required: true): Enter the date of the submission.
* **Submission Type** (required: true, select one): Select the type of submission (article, book, or presentation).
* **Submission Link** (required: true): Enter the link to your submission.
* **Onboarding Status** (required: true, select one): Select the onboarding status (pending or completed).
* **Comments** (optional, text): Provide any additional comments about your submission.
