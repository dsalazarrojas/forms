# Global Educators Book Club Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Educators Book Club Registration form is a simple online form used to collect information from interested educators. It's a non-mandatory registration process that allows you to express your interest in joining the club and provide details about your preferences and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your School Information in the "School Information" field. This field is optional.
2.  In the "Member Details" field, provide your details as required.
3.  Select the subjects you teach or are interested in from the "Interests" field.
4.  Specify your availability in the "Availability" field. Please note that this field is used for internal purposes and will not affect your registration status.
5.  Choose a preferred meeting time from the "Meeting Time" field. This field is optional.
6.  In the "Book Club Interests" field, select "Yes" if you're interested in joining the book club.
7.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This will be used for internal communication purposes.
8.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field. This is optional.
9.  If assigned to an individual or group, select "Yes" in the "Assigned to" field. If not assigned, you can select "Other" and provide the assigned individual or group in the "Other assigned to" field.
10.  Add any additional comments or details in the "Notes" field, which is optional.
11.  Confirm your email address in the "Confirm Email" field. This is optional.
12.  You can also enter any additional information about your phone number in the "Confirm Phone" field, which is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **School Information** (Text): Enter the name of your school or educational institution.
- **Member Details** (Text): Enter your name, title, and any other relevant details you'd like to share.
- **Interests** (Select Multiple): Select the subjects you teach or are interested in. You can choose multiple options.
- **Availability** (Date): Specify your available dates and times for meetings or discussions.
- **Meeting Time** (Time): Choose your preferred time for meetings or discussions. This is optional.
- **Book Club Interests** (Select One): Select "Yes" if you're interested in joining the book club.
- **Email** (Email): Enter your email address for internal communication purposes.
- **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number for internal communication purposes. This is optional.
- **Assigned to** (Select One): Select "Yes" if you're assigned to an individual or group. If not assigned, choose "Other" and enter the assigned individual or group in the "Other assigned to" field.
- **Other assigned to** (Text): Enter the name of the individual or group assigned to you. This field is optional.
- **Notes** (Note): Enter any additional comments or details you'd like to share.
- **Confirm Email** (Email): Enter your email address to confirm your registration. This field is optional.
- **Confirm Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number to confirm your registration. This field is optional.
