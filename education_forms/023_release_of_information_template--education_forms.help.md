# release_of_information_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect standardized release information from users, which can be used in various settings such as research, academic, or business.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user name in the "user name" field.
2. Select or enter your release date in the "release_date" field.
3. Choose "Yes" or "No" for the "contact_method" field if you have a contact method. If you don't have a contact method, you can leave this field blank.
4. If you have an other contact method that is not listed, enter it in the "other_contact_method" field.
5. Enter your contact phone number in the "contact_method_phone" field.
6. Enter your contact email address in the "contact_method_email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **user name** (user name, text, optional): Enter your name to be identified as the user releasing the information.
- **release_date** (release_date, date, optional): Select or enter the date you are releasing the information.
  - This field can be either selected from a calendar or entered manually as a date in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
- **contact_method** (contact_method, select_one, optional): Indicate if you have a contact method.
  - Choose "Yes" if you have a contact method, "No" otherwise.
- **other_contact_method** (other_contact_method, text, optional): Enter an other contact method if not listed.
  - This field is for entering any contact method not listed in the contact_method field.
- **contact_method_phone** (contact_method_phone, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
  - This field is for entering your contact phone number if you have one.
- **contact_method_email** (contact_method_email, email, optional): Enter your contact email address.
  - This field is for entering your contact email address if you have one.
