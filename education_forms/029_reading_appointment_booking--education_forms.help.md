# reading_appointment_booking - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request reading appointments between students and designated readers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief description of your reading request in the **Session Request** field.
2. Fill in your **Student Name**, **Student Age**, and **Student Grade** (if applicable).
3. Indicate whether you have your **Parent Permission** to participate in the reading appointment.
4. Enter your **Student Phone** and **Student Email** (if applicable).
5. Select your preferred **Preferred Time** for the reading appointment.
6. Enter the **Reader Name** and their **Reader Phone** (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Request**: Briefly describe your reading request. (Required)
* **Student Name**: Enter your name. (Optional, but recommended)
* **Student Age**: Enter your age. (Optional, but recommended)
* **Student Grade**: Select your grade level (e.g., 1-2, 3-4, 5-6, 7-8, 9-12, High School). (Optional)
* **Parent Permission**: Check if you have your parent's permission to participate. (Optional)
* **Student Phone**: Enter your phone number. (Optional, but recommended)
* **Student Email**: Enter your email address. (Optional, but recommended)
* **Preferred Time**: Select your preferred time for the reading appointment (09:00 AM-06:00 PM). (Optional)
* **Reader Name**: Enter the reader's name. (Optional)
* **Reader Phone**: Enter the reader's phone number. (Optional, but recommended)

Please note that some fields are marked as optional, but it's recommended to fill them out for better communication and to ensure a smooth reading appointment process.
