# Student Peer Review Research Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Student Peer Review Research Consent Form is used to gather information from students participating in a peer review research study. This form helps researchers understand students' perspectives and experiences on a particular research subject.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your category (e.g., category 1, category 2, or category 3).
2. Enter your name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Enter your address.
6. Enter the date of the research study participation.
7. Enter the time of the research study participation.
8. Enter any additional notes or comments.
9. Enter the form ID.
10. Enter the name of the form.
11. Enter the ID of the user who assigned this form.
12. Enter the tool assigned to you for this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Peer Review Research Consent Form** (`form_title`): This field is for entering a brief title of the form, which is already pre-populated with the form's title.
* **Category** (`category`): Select the category that best describes your participation in the research study.
* **Category Value** (`category_value`): This field is for entering a more detailed description of your category selection.
* **Name** (`name`): Enter your name as it appears on your official records.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Address** (`address`): Enter your address where you can be reached.
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date of your research study participation.
* **Time** (`time`): Enter the time of your research study participation.
* **Note** (`note`): Enter any additional notes or comments about your participation.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`): This is the unique identifier of the form you are completing.
* **Form Name** (`form_name`): Enter the name of the form you are completing.
* **User ID** (`user_id`): Enter the ID of the user who assigned this form to you.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): Enter the tool assigned to you for this form.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate information in all fields.
* Ensure you have the necessary permissions and approvals to participate in the research study.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the researcher or administrator responsible for the study.
