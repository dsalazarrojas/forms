# Peer Educator Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Peer Educator Application Form is designed for individuals interested in becoming peer educators at our institution. This form collects information about your availability, role, and other relevant details to help us assess your application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Full Name** in the first field.
2. Provide your **email** address in the second field.
3. Select either **Yes** or **No** for the **Role** field, indicating your willingness to take on a peer educator role.
4. If applicable, enter your **Availability** (date) for when you are available to serve as a peer educator.
5. Select your available **Time** for a peer educator session.
6. Sign your name with your **E-Signature** in the last field. This is a required field to ensure the form is submitted correctly.
7. You can add any additional comments or information in the **Comments** field, but it's optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional: required):
    Fill in your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional: required):
    Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Role** (`role`, `select_one`, required/optional: false):
    Select "Yes" if you are willing to take on a peer educator role, or "No" if not.
* **Availability** (`availability`, `date`, required/optional: false):
    If you are available to serve as a peer educator, enter the date when you are free.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required/optional: false):
    Select your available time for a peer educator session.
* **E-Signature** (`e_signature`, `text`, required/optional: required):
    Sign your name as a digital signature to confirm your application.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required/optional: false):
    Add any additional comments or information about your application.
