# Teacher Nomination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect and document information about a teacher's performance and service. The goal is to recognize and reward exceptional teachers who have made significant contributions to their school and students.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have the permission of the teacher to fill out this form.
2. Provide accurate and respectful information about the teacher.
3. Fill out the form completely and thoroughly.
4. Attach any supporting documentation or evidence that may support your nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, Text, Required: False): Enter the teacher's first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, Text, Required: False): Enter the teacher's last name.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required: False): Enter the teacher's email address.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, Text, Required: False): Enter the teacher's phone number.
* **Teacher name** (`teacher_name`, Text, Required: False): Enter the teacher's name.
* **Title** (`teacher_title`, Select One, Required: False): Check if the teacher holds a title such as "Assistant Professor" or "Department Head".
* **Reason for Nomination** (`reason_for_nomination`, Text, Required: False): Provide a brief explanation for why you are nominating this teacher.
* **Years of service** (`years_of_service`, Number, Required: False): Enter the number of years the teacher has been serving the institution.
* **Years employed current school** (`years_employed_current`, Number, Required: False): Enter the number of years the teacher has been employed at the current school.
* **Teacher ID** (`teacher_id`, Select Multiple, Required: False): Check if the teacher has a unique ID number.
* **School** (`school`, Text, Required: False): Enter the school name where the teacher works.
* **Department** (`department`, Select One, Required: False): Check if the teacher's department is relevant to the nomination.
* **Subject area** (`subject_area`, Select One, Required: False): Check if the teacher's subject area is relevant to the nomination.
* **Award/Recognition type** (`award_recognition_type`, Select One, Required: False): Check the type of award or recognition you think the teacher deserves.
* **Submitter name** (`submitter_name`, Text, Required: False): Enter your name and title.
* **Submitter phone** (`submitter_phone`, Text, Required: False): Enter your phone number.
* **Submitter email** (`submitter_email`, Email, Required: False): Enter your email address.
* **Submitter department** (`submitter_department`, Text, Required: False): Enter your department name.
* **Submitter school** (`submitter_school`, Text, Required: False): Enter your school name.
* **Comments** (`comments`, Note, Required: False): Provide any additional comments or supporting information.
* **Notes** (`submitter_notes`, Note, Required: False): Provide any additional notes or comments.
* **Title** (`submitter_title`, Text, Required: False): Enter your title.
* **Years of service** (`submitter_years_of_service`, Number, Required: False): Enter the number of years you have been serving the institution.
* **Years employed** (`submitter_years_employed`, Number, Required: False): Enter the number of years you have been employed at the current school.
* **Currently employed** (`submitter_years_employed`, Select Multiple, Required: False): Check if you are currently employed at the current school.

Note: Some fields may not be displayed if they are not applicable to your situation.
