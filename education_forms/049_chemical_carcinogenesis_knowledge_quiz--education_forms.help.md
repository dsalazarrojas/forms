# Chemical Carcinogenesis Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess students' knowledge on chemical carcinogenesis. It consists of multiple fields that test their understanding of various concepts related to carcinogenesis.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Student Name" field to enter your name as requested in the format "Last Name, First Name"
2. Click on the "Date" field and select the date on which you took the quiz
3. Select the "Evaluation Type" to indicate whether this is a pre-test (before module), post-test (after module), or practice quiz
4. Complete the knowledge assessment questions by selecting the correct answers from the provided options
5. Rate your confidence in your answers and provide feedback on the clarity of the questions

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter your full name as requested in the format "Last Name, First Name"
* **Date**: Select the date on which you took the quiz
* **Evaluation Type**: Select the type of quiz you are taking: Pre-test (Before Module), Post-test (After Module), or Practice Quiz
* **Question 1 - Which best defines a carcinogen**: Select the definition of a carcinogen from the provided options
* **Question 2 - What is the primary type of DNA damage caused by genotoxic carcinogens**: Select the type of DNA damage caused by genotoxic carcinogens
* **Question 3 - Select known chemical carcinogens**: Select all applicable chemical carcinogens from the provided options
* **Question 4 - What are the three stages of carcinogenesis**: Select the correct stages of carcinogenesis
* **Question 5 - Metabolism of procarcinogens usually occurs in which organ**: Select the organ where metabolism of procarcinogens usually occurs
* **How confident are you in your answers**: Rate your confidence in your answers from Very Confident, Somewhat Confident, or Not Confident
* **Rating of question clarity**: Rate the clarity of the questions from Very Clear, Clear, or Confusing
* **Final Score (Percentage)**: This field is for staff use only and will display your final score

Note: Please ensure that the fields and their requirements are clearly understood before proceeding. The internal score field is not visible to the user and is only for staff use. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask for clarification.
