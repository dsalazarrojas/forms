# Adjunct Faculty Employment Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide information about your qualifications and experience for potential employment as an adjunct faculty member. Please ensure that all sections of the form are completed accurately and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting the form, please review the instructions below.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by confirming that you are applying for an adjunct faculty position by carefully reading the introductory note.
2. Fill out your full name, email address, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
3. Select your primary academic department of interest from the list provided.
4. Provide information about your academic credentials, including your highest degree earned, the institution and major that awarded your degree, and the year you graduated.
5. If applicable, list the courses you have previously taught and your experience with online or hybrid instruction.
6. Provide information about your professional references, including their name, title, and email address.
7. Confirm your agreement to a background check and certify that you are eligible to work in the country.
8. Date and sign the application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 2. **Full Name (Text)** 
*Required* This field is where you enter your full name.

### 3. **Email Address (Email)**
*Required* This field is where you enter your email address, which will be used for communication with the institution and any follow-up inquiries.

### 4. **Phone Number (Text)**
*Required* This field is where you enter your phone number, which will be used for communication and potential phone interviews.

### 5. **Primary Academic Department of Interest (Select One)**
*Required* Select the department in which you are interested in teaching.

### 6. **-- Academic Credentials -- (Note)**
*Optional* This section provides a heading for your academic credentials.

### 7. **Highest Degree Earned (Select One)**
*Required* Select your highest degree earned from the options provided.

### 8. **Awarding Institution and Major (Text)**
*Required* Enter the name of the institution that awarded your degree and your major.

### 9. **Year Degree Awarded (Number)**
*Required* Enter the year you earned your degree.

### 10. **-- Professional Experience -- (Note)**
*Optional* This section provides a heading for your professional experience.

### 11. **Years of Teaching Experience in Higher Education (Number)**
*Required* Enter the number of years you have taught in higher education.

### 12. **List of Courses Previously Taught (Text)**
*Required* List the courses you have previously taught.

### 13. **Do you have experience with Online / Hybrid instruction? (Select One)**
*Required* Select your level of experience with online or hybrid instruction.

### 14. **Professional References (Text)**
*Required* Enter the names, titles, and email addresses of your professional references.

### 15. **I consent to a background check as a condition of employment (Select One)**
*Required* Confirm your agreement to a background check.

### 16. **I certify that I am legally authorized to work in this country (Select One)**
*Required* Confirm your eligibility to work in the country.

### 17. **Date of Application (Date)**
*Required* Date and sign the application.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the institution's HR department.
* Applications submitted without the required information will not be considered complete.
* Review and submit the application carefully before signing and dating it.
