# Student Well Being Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students about their well-being and support systems in school, home, and other social settings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select all the options that apply to you for each question.
3. Rate your experiences using the provided scoring system.
