# educational_background_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The educational background information form is designed to collect details about your educational history. This information will help us understand your educational qualifications and background.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Scroll down each page to fill out each field.
2. Each field is a text input field where you can enter your educational information.
3. Enter your educational information for each field as prompted.
4. Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Educational Background Information**: This is the title of the form.
- **Estudios**: Enter your relevant educational qualifications here.
- **Estudios 2**: Enter your relevant educational qualifications here. (This field may be related to Estudios, but its specific context is unclear.)
- **Estudios 3**, **Estudios 4**, ..., **Estudios 24**: Continue entering your educational qualifications in each field.

## Tips
- Be as detailed and specific as possible when entering your educational qualifications.
- If unsure about a field, enter "N/A" and explain the reason for not having information in the corresponding field.

Note that the field explanations are general and based on the field labels. If more context or specific details about the form fields are required, please provide further instructions or adjust this help guide accordingly.
