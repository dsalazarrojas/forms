# Animal Care Contract - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Animal Care Contract is a form used to collect information from a student and their guardian to ensure that both parties understand their responsibilities and liabilities when it comes to caring for an animal. This guide will walk you through each field of the form, making it easier to complete.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's full name in the "Student name" field.
2. If the student is under 18, provide the guardian's or parent's full name in the "Guardian or parent name" field.
3. Enter a phone number for emergencies in the "Contact phone" field.
4. Choose the species or description of the animal being cared for in the "Animal type" field.
5. In the "Care responsibilities summary" field, outline the daily tasks the student will perform for the animal's care.
6. Confirm that both the student and guardian have reviewed the health and safety rules by selecting "Acknowledged" in the "Health and safety rules acknowledgement" field.
7. Select "Agree" in the "Liability and insurance agreement" field to confirm understanding of liability terms.
8. Provide the name and phone number of an emergency contact person in the "Emergency contact name and phone" field.
9. If necessary, provide any special care instructions or dietary restrictions in the "Special care instructions" field.
10. Enter the start date of the care arrangement in the "Contract start date" field.
11. Type your full name as an electronic signature in the "Guardian signature name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student name** (`student_name`, required/optional): Enter the full name of the student responsible for the animal's care.
* **Guardian or parent name** (`guardian_name`, optional): If the student is under 18, enter the guardian's or parent's full name.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, required): Enter a phone number where the student can be reached in case of emergencies.
* **Animal type** (`animal_type`, required): Choose the species or common description of the animal being cared for.
* **Care responsibilities summary** (`care_responsibilities`, required): Outline the daily tasks the student will perform for the animal's care.
* **Health and safety rules acknowledgement** (`health_and_safety_rules_acknowledgement`, required): Select "Acknowledged" to confirm that the student and guardian have reviewed the rules.
* **Liability and insurance agreement** (`liability_and_insurance`, required): Select "Agree" to confirm understanding of liability terms.
* **Emergency contact name and phone** (`emergency_contact`, required): Provide the name and phone number of an emergency contact person.
* **Special care instructions** (`special_instructions`, optional): Provide any special care instructions or dietary restrictions for the animal.
* **Contract start date** (`start_date`, required): Enter the date when the care responsibilities begin.
* **Guardian signature name** (`signature_guardian`, required): Type your full name as an electronic signature.
