# Cognitive Education Enhancement Grant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cognitive Education Enhancement Grant Application Form is a tool to help you submit a grant application for your cognitive education enhancement project. This form is designed to gather detailed information about your project, including its goals, budget, timeline, and evaluation plan.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out all required fields**: Make sure to fill out all required fields marked with an asterisk (*) before submitting the form.
2. **Use a detailed and clear format**: Provide thorough and clear information in each field to help the evaluators understand your project.
3. **Attach supporting documents**: If necessary, attach relevant documents to support your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Full Name (*):** Provide your full name as it appears on your official institutional ID or passport.
* **Email Address (*):** Enter your official institutional email address.
* **Phone Number (*):** Enter your contact phone number.
* **Institution Name (*):** Enter the name of your institution where you are affiliated with.
* **Position Title (*):** Enter your official position title within the institution.
* **Project Title (*):** Enter a brief title of your project.
* **Project Description (*):** Provide a detailed explanation of your project, including its objectives, goals, and expected outcomes.
* **Target Population (*):** Specify who will benefit from this project (e.g., students, teachers, researchers, etc.).
* **Number of Students to Benefit (*):** Enter an approximate number of students that will benefit from this project.
* **Primary Objective (*):** Enter the main goal of your project.
* **Expected Outcomes (*):** Specify what results you expect to achieve from this project.
* **Success Metrics (*):** Describe how you will measure the success of your project.
* **Requested Grant Amount (*):** Enter the exact dollar amount you are requesting.
* **Budget Breakdown (*):** Provide a detailed breakdown of how you plan to allocate funds for this project.
* **Other Funding Sources (*):** If you have already secured or are seeking additional funding sources, provide details here.
* **Project Start Date (*):** Enter the date when you plan to start your project.
* **Project End Date (*):** Enter the date when you plan to finish your project.
* **Major Project Milestones (*):** Enter key dates and deliverables for your project.
* **Evaluaton and Assessment Plan (*):** Describe how you will evaluate and assess the impact of your project.
* **Institutional Support (*):** Provide details on the resources or support you are expecting from your institution.
* **Director or Administrator Name (*):** Enter the name of the official supporting your application.
* **Director Email (*):** Enter the email address of the supporting official.
 
Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly to ensure your application is considered for funding.
