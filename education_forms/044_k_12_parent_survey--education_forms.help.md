# K 12 Parent Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The K 12 Parent Survey is a form designed to gather information and feedback from K 12 parents about their child's classroom experience. It aims to help improve and better support the educational environment and services provided to students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for the survey in the "K 12 Parent Survey" field.
2. Select a category from the drop-down menu.
3. Provide a brief description of your experience.
4. Select the options that best describe your child's classroom experience.
5. Share your feedback about your child's classroom experience.
6. Choose the tool assigned to your child from the drop-down menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **K 12 Parent Survey** (`form_title`, text, required/optional): Enter a title for the survey in this field. Please provide a brief and accurate title that describes the form's purpose.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Select a category that best describes your child's classroom experience.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your experience. This will help us understand your perspective.
* **Classroom Experience** (`classroom_experience`, select_multiple, optional): Select the options that best describe your child's classroom experience.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share your feedback about your child's classroom experience. Be as specific as possible.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Choose the tool assigned to your child from the drop-down menu.

## Tips
* Take a few minutes to carefully review each field before submitting the form.
* Ensure the form is completed accurately and truthfully to help us improve our services. 
* If you have any further questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
