# high_school_community_service_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The High School Community Service Form is designed to track and report community service hours completed by high school students. It helps to record hours earned and pledged, as well as details about the service activities undertaken. This form ensures accurate and consistent reporting of community service hours, which can be used for various purposes such as awarding credits, verifying hours for scholarships, or for reporting to external organizations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and materials before starting.
2. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form. These include:
	* Service hours earned
	* Service hours pledged
	* Organization
3. Select the type of service activity you participated in from the options provided.
4. Provide details about the service activity, including the date(s) it took place, a description of the service, and any notes.
5. If you were referred to this service activity by someone, provide their name, relationship to you, email, phone number, and details about their organization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service hours earned (Page 1)**: Enter the total number of hours you have earned through your community service activities. This is a required field.
* **Service hours pledged (Page 1)**: Enter the total number of hours you have pledged to dedicate to community service activities. This is a required field.
* **Organization (Page 1)**: Select the organization you participated in your service activity. You can choose from the provided options or select "Other" if your organization is not listed.
* **Service Type (Page 2)**: Select the type of service activity you participated in, such as volunteer, fundraising, tutoring, event planning, environmental, animal shelter, food drive, arts and crafts, sports, or disaster relief.
* **Date (Page 3)**: Enter the date(s) when the service activity took place. You can enter multiple dates if you participated in multiple activities.
* **Service Description (Page 3)**: Provide a brief description of the service activity, including what you did and what you achieved.
* **Notes (Page 3)**: Add any additional notes or comments about the service activity.
* **Referral Name (Page 4)**: If you were referred to this service activity by someone, enter their name.
* **Referral Relationship (Page 4)**: Enter the relationship between you and the person who referred you to this service activity (e.g. teacher, mentor, family member).
* **Referral Email (Page 4)**: Enter the email address of the person who referred you to this service activity.
* **Referral Phone (Page 4)**: Enter the phone number of the person who referred you to this service activity.
* **Referral Organization (Page 4)**: Select the organization associated with the person who referred you to this service activity. You can choose from the provided options or select "Other" if not listed.
* **Referral Address (Page 4)**: Enter the address of the person who referred you to this service activity.
* **Referral City (Page 4)**: Enter the city of the person who referred you to this service activity.
* **Referral State (Page 4)**: Enter the state of the person who referred you to this service activity.

**Tips**

* Be as accurate and thorough as possible when filling out the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to your service activity documentation or ask your organization's representative.
* If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to reach out to the form administrator.
