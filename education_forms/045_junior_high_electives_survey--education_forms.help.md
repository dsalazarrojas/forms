# Junior High Electives Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Junior High Electives Survey is a form designed to collect feedback and preferences from junior high students about their electives experience. This information will help educators and administrators make informed decisions about electives offerings and improve the electives program. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. The form is divided into four sections.
2. For each section, select the answer that best corresponds to your response.
3. If a section has a "select multiple" or "select one" type field, you can select one or more option(s) that apply to you.
4. In the "Special Requests" section, you can provide any additional comments or requests you have about electives.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Electives**: Please select all electives that you have taken or are interested in.
	+ This field allows you to choose from a list of electives that you have taken or are interested in.
* **Past Courses**: Please select the courses you have taken in the past.
	+ This field allows you to choose from a list of past courses that you have taken.
* **Special Requests**: Please provide any additional comments or requests you have about electives.
	+ This field is for any extra information or requests you have about electives that are not listed in the other fields.
* **Electives Preferences**: Please select all electives that you prefer.
	+ This field allows you to choose from a list of electives that you prefer. 

## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any difficulty completing the form, you can ask for assistance.
* Please submit the completed form to complete the survey.
