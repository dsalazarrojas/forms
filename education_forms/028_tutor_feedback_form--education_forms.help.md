# Tutor Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Tutor Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from tutors on their overall experience with our educational programs. This information will help us improve the quality of our services and tailor them to better suit your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Introduction**: Briefly introduce yourself and share a bit about your experience with our program.
2.  **Overall Experience (Rating)**: Rate your overall experience with our program. Choose a number that best represents how you feel about your experience.
3.  **Course**: Provide the name of the course(s) you participated in.
4.  **Feedback**: Share your specific thoughts and feelings about your experience. What did we do well? What could we do better?
5.  **Additional Feedback**: Is there anything you'd like to share that's not covered above?
6.  **Date**: If applicable, provide the date of your experience. This can be helpful for context.
7.  **Time**: If applicable, provide the time you experienced our services. Again, this can be helpful for context and scheduling purposes.
8.  **Email**: Please provide your email address for us to follow up with you or to send you updates on our services.
9.  **Phone**: We won't use this field, but it's here in case you need to reach us quickly.
10. **Submitted By**: Please choose who submitted this feedback (you, or an assistant).
