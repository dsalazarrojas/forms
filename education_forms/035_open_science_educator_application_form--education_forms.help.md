# Open Science Educator Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Open Science Educator Application Form is designed to collect information about individuals interested in becoming Open Science Educators. This form is intended for those with a background in science education who are looking to join our community of educators. The application form is used to assess the suitability of potential applicants for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary documents and information required for the application. This may include your resume, CV, and relevant publications and teaching experience.
2. Read each section carefully and answer all fields as accurately as possible.
3. Use the options dropdowns to select relevant research focus areas.
4. Ensure you have included all relevant information in the contact section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Background Info (Page 1)** 
- **Background Info**: Provide a brief introduction about your educational background, research experience, and any relevant accomplishments.
- **Education**: Share your educational background, including degrees and institutions.
- **Experience**: Describe your relevant research experience and any notable accomplishments.
- **Qualifications**: List any relevant qualifications or certifications that align with the Open Science Educator role.
- **Research Experience**: Describe your research experience, including any notable projects or achievements.
- **Publication List**: List any relevant publications or presentations you have made.
- **Awards and Honors**: List any awards or honors you have received.
- **Teaching and Teaching Experience**: Describe your teaching experience, including any notable achievements.
- **Research Program Participation**: List any research programs you have participated in.
- **Research Collaboration**: Describe any research collaborations you have had.
- **Research Funding**: Describe any research funding you have received.
- **Research Focus Areas**: Select all relevant areas of research focus, such as Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, and Option 4.
- **Other Research Focus Areas**: Provide any other relevant research focus areas not listed above.
- **Education Background**: Describe your educational background, including any gaps or breaks in education.
- **Employment Background**: Describe your employment history, including any relevant jobs or positions.
- **Other Employment Background**: Provide any other relevant employment background information not listed above.
- **Contact Information**: Provide your contact information, including name, email, phone, and address.
- **Name**: Your full name.
- **Email**: Your professional email.
- **Phone**: Your phone number.
- **Address**: Your postal address.
- **Upload Resume**: Upload your resume.
- **Upload CV**: Upload your CV.

## Tips
- Ensure you have answered all relevant fields and provided all required information.
- Proofread your application carefully before submitting.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us.
