## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to gather information about the respondent's perception and experiences of interdisciplinary teaching, including benefits, challenges, and effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply navigate through the pages and fill in the required and optional fields as instructed. You can start by reading the introduction, then answer the questions on the following pages about benefits, challenges, and effectiveness. Be as detailed and honest as possible to provide valuable insights.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction (page 1)**: This is a brief welcome message introducing the survey. It's a note field, which means you can type in any information you'd like to understand the context of the survey.
* **Benefits (page 2)**: This text field asks about the benefits you've experienced or observed in interdisciplinary teaching. Be specific about what you've gained or seen as a result of this approach.
* **Challenges (page 3)**: In this text field, describe any challenges or difficulties you've faced while implementing interdisciplinary teaching.
* **Effectiveness (page 4)**: This text field is for your evaluation of how effective you think interdisciplinary teaching has been or can be. Share your thoughts on its success and areas for improvement.

## Tips

* Please be honest and detailed in your answers, as your input will help create a comprehensive understanding of interdisciplinary teaching.
* If you have any thoughts or experiences you'd like to add, but don't see a field for it, feel free to include them in the introduction or any other field.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
