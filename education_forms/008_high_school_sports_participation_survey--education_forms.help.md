# high_school_sports_participation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on high school student participation in sports programs to identify potential barriers and inform strategies to promote student engagement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Review each section carefully to ensure accuracy.
2. Fill out all required fields (marked as "required").
3. For each section, provide as much detail as possible in the answer fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Student Participation (Page 1)
* **Student Participation** (`Student Participation`, `number`, required): This field asks for the number of sports or activities that the student is currently participating in.

### Barriers to Participation (Page 2)
* **Barriers to Participation** (`Barriers to Participation`, `text`, required): This field is for the student to describe any obstacles or challenges they face in participating in sports.

### Interest in Future Teams (Page 3)
* **Interest in Future Teams** (`Interest in Future Teams`, `select_multiple`, required): This field asks if the student is interested in joining future sports teams. Please select all that apply from the given options.

### Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately.
* For text fields, please provide detailed answers to help us understand the student's perspective.
* If you have any questions or are unsure about any of the questions, contact our support team for assistance.
