# School Delegation Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to register the school's delegation, which is a critical part of the school's administration process. It collects essential information from the school's staff or representatives to facilitate proper delegation and communication.

## How To Complete This Form

* Read the form carefully and make sure to fill out all required fields accurately.
* Select the appropriate role from the list provided in the "Role" field.
* Enter your first name and last name in their respective fields.
* Enter your school's name in the "School Name" field.
* If you have a personal email address, enter it in the "Email" field. This is not required, but it can be helpful for communication.
* Enter your phone number for easier contact in the "Phone Number" field. This is not required.
* Select a date for the delegation confirmation in the "Confirm Date" field.
* Select a time for the delegation confirmation in the "Confirm Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text): Enter your last name.
* **School Name** (Text): Enter your school's name.
* **Role** (Select One): Select your role as a delegate, which can be Delegate, Principal, or Teacher.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your personal email address if you wish to be contacted. (Optional)
* **Phone Number** (Text): Enter your phone number for easier contact. (Optional)
* **Date** (Date): Enter the date for the delegation. (Optional)
* **Time** (Time): Enter the time for the delegation. (Optional)
* **Note** (Note): Enter any additional comments or notes about the delegation. (Optional)
* **Confirm Date** (Date): Select a date to confirm the delegation. (Optional)
* **Confirm Time** (Time): Select a time to confirm the delegation. (Optional)

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact our support team.
* If you have already submitted this form, please do not resubmit it.
* The information collected from this form will be used for official purposes, so please ensure it is accurate and complete.
