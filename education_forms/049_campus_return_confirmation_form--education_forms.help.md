# Campus Return Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Campus Return Confirmation Form is a crucial document that assists the campus management in managing the re-entry process of individuals on campus. This form helps to ensure a smooth and safe re-entry process by gathering essential information about the individual's presence, housing, and health status.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have a valid reason for your return to campus, and that you have been cleared or cleared to be on campus.
2. Provide your individual details, including your name, ID number, and contact information.
3. Specify your role on campus (Student, Faculty, Staff, Visitor, or Other).
4. Choose your department or school if applicable.
5. Confirm your return to campus on the specified date and estimated time.
6. Specify your frequency of presence on campus (Full-time, Part-time, Occasional, or Rare).
7. Indicate if you will be living on campus (True, False, or Undecided).
8. Select your housing location or dorm (if applicable).
9. Confirm your completion of required health screening (True, False, or In Progress).
10. Specify your vaccination status (Fully Vaccinated, Partially Vaccinated, Not Vaccinated, or Prefer not to say).
11. Confirm your willingness to participate in health testing (True, False).
12. Specify any medical or accessibility accommodations you may need.
13. Mention any dietary restrictions or accommodations you may have.
14. Finally, confirm your return to campus on the specified date (Yes, I confirm, False, or Tentative).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Student or Employee ID Number**: Enter your unique ID number issued by the institution.
* **Email Address**: Enter your valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number where you can be reached.
* **Role on Campus**: Choose the category that best describes your role on campus (Student, Faculty, Staff, Visitor, or Other).
* **Department or School**: If applicable, select your department or school.
* **Date of Return to Campus**: Choose the date of your return to campus.
* **Estimated Time of Return**: Choose the estimated time of your return to campus.
* **Primary Location on Campus**: Specify the location where you will be on campus.
* **Frequency of Campus Presence**: Choose how frequently you will be on campus (Full-time, Part-time, Occasional, or Rare).
* **Will you live on campus**: Indicate if you will be living on campus (True, False, or Undecided).
* **Housing Location or Dorm**: If you choose to live on campus, specify your housing location or dorm.
* **I have completed required health screening**: Confirm if you have completed the required health screening (True, False, or In Progress).
* **Vaccination Status**: Specify your vaccination status (Fully Vaccinated, Partially Vaccinated, Not Vaccinated, or Prefer not to say).
* **Will I participate in health testing**: Confirm if you are willing to participate in health testing (True, False).
* **Medical or Accessibility Accommodations**: If you need any medical or accessibility accommodations, specify them.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Accommodations**: If you have any dietary restrictions or accommodations, specify them.
* **I confirm my return to campus**: Confirm your return to campus on the specified date (Yes, I confirm, False, or Tentative).
