# PhD Thesis Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PhD Thesis Evaluation Form is a document used to assess the quality and completeness of a PhD thesis. It helps the evaluator to evaluate the thesis and provide feedback to the author. This form is used to collect the evaluator's rating and comments for the thesis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `Thesis Title` field with the title of the thesis being evaluated.
2. Enter your name and position in the `Evaluator Name` field.
3. Select the department that the thesis belongs to from the `Department` field.
4. Rate the thesis using the `Reviewer Rating` field, with a value between 1 and 5.
5. Provide feedback and comments in the `Feedback` and `Evaluator Comments` fields.
6. Date and time of the evaluation should be entered in the `Date Evaluated` and `Time Evaluated` fields, respectively.
7. Select the type of thesis (Ph.D., M.Sc., or M.A.) from the `Thesis Type` field.
8. Enter any additional comments or evaluation in the `Evaluation Comments` field.
9. Finally, select the final grade for the thesis from the `Final Grade` field, using the grade scale provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <label> Theses Title
• **Thesis Title (1)**: Enter the title of the thesis being evaluated.

### <label> Evaluator Name
• **Evaluator Name (2)**: Enter your name and position.

### <label> Department
• **Department (3)**: Select the department that the thesis belongs to from the options provided.

### <label> Reviewer Rating
• **Reviewer Rating (4)**: Rate the thesis using a value between 1 and 5.

### <label> Feedback
• **Feedback (5)**: Enter any feedback or comments for the thesis.

### <label> Date Evaluated
• **Date Evaluated (6)**: Enter the date when the evaluation was conducted.

### <label> Time Evaluated
• **Time Evaluated (7)**: Enter the time when the evaluation was conducted.

### <label> Thesis Type
• **Thesis Type (8)**: Select the type of thesis (Ph.D., M.Sc., or M.A.).

### <label> Evaluation Comments
• **Evaluation Comments (9)**: Enter any additional comments or evaluation.

### <label> Evaluator Comments
• **Evaluator Comments (10)**: Enter any additional comments or evaluation.

### <label> Final Grade
• **Final Grade (11)**: Enter the final grade for the thesis using the grade scale provided.

### <label> Grade Scale
• **Grade Scale (12)**: Select the grade for the thesis using the options provided.

### <label> Evaluation Form Type
• **Evaluation Form Type (13)**: Select the type of evaluation form used (Thesis or Report).

Note: This form is intended to be used for PhD thesis evaluation and should not be used for other types of forms.

Tips:

* Please fill in all the fields carefully and accurately.
* Ensure that the date and time entered are correct.
* Use the grade scale provided to ensure consistency.
* Enter any additional comments or evaluation in the feedback and comments fields. 

(Note: This form is for evaluation purposes only, and any grades or comments entered may be used in assessment and feedback to the author.)
