# Student Parent Handbook Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to have students and parents acknowledge that they have received and reviewed the student parent handbook, which outlines the rules, regulations, and policies of the institution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Select the student's grade level from the "Student Grade" dropdown menu.
3. Enter the parent's name in the "Parent Name" field.
4. Enter the parent's email address in the "Parent Email" field (if required).
5. Fill in the student handbook title in the "Student Handbook" field.
6. Add any attachments related to the handbook in the "Attachments" field (if required).
7. Select the date completed from the "Date Completed" field.
8. Sign the "Signature" field with your name.
9. If necessary, add any additional notes in the "Notes" field.
10. Select the date in the "Date" field.
11. Select the time in the "Time" field.
12. Select "Student" or "Parent" from the "Student/Parent" dropdown menu.
13. Fill in your agreement with the policies and procedures in the "Agreement" field.
14. Select the date of your signature in the "Signature Date" field.
15. Sign with your name in the "Student Signature" field.
16. Sign with your name in the "Parent Signature" fields.
17. Fill in your name label in the "Student Name Label" field.
18. Fill in your grade label in the "Student Grade Label" field.
19. Fill in your name label in the "Parent Name Label" field.
20. Fill in your email label in the "Parent Email Label" field (if required).
21. Fill in the handbook label in the "Student Handbook Label" field.
22. Fill in the attachment label in the "Attachment Label" field (if required).
23. Fill in the date label in the "Date Completed Label" field.
24. Fill in your signature label in the "Signature Label" field.
25. Fill in your notes in the "Notes Label" field (if required).
