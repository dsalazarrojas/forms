# Research Theory Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Research Theory Submission Form is a basic tool for submitting theories or ideas to be reviewed by experts or peers. It provides a straightforward way to collect metadata about the theory submission, such as title, subject area, abstract, and manuscript.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the title of your theory submission.
2. Select the relevant subject area from the provided options.
3. Write a brief abstract of your theory submission.
4. Upload your manuscript or provide a link to the online version.
5. List the names of reviewers you would like to be assigned to review your submission (comma-separated).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### research_theory_submission_form_title
* **Title**: The title of your theory submission.
	+ A brief and descriptive title of your theory.
* **select_subject_area**: Select the relevant subject area of your theory.
	+ Choose the area of study or discipline your theory belongs to.
* **research_theory_submission_form_abstract**: 
	+ A brief summary of your theory submission.
* **research_theory_submission_form_manuscript**: 
	+ Upload your manuscript or provide a link to the online version.
* **research_theory_submission_form_reviewers**: 
	+ List the names of reviewers you would like to be assigned to review your submission (comma-separated).

## Tips
* Ensure that the title accurately reflects the content of your theory submission.
* Choose the most relevant subject area to help reviewers understand the context of your theory.
* Keep the abstract concise and clear to effectively communicate your theory submission.
* Make sure to upload or link your manuscript to provide supporting material.
* Be specific and thorough when listing reviewers to ensure accurate assignment.
