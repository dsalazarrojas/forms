# Library Patron Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Library Patron Check In Form is used to collect information from library patrons when they visit a library branch. This form helps library staff to quickly and accurately check in patrons, gather feedback, and improve library services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the text box provided.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the text box provided.
3. Enter your **Email** (optional) in the text box provided.
4. Select the **Contact Method** you prefer to reach you back, if you'd like us to follow up.
5. Enter your **Phone Number** (optional) in the text box provided.
6. You can choose to mark **Email** (optional) as another contact method.
7. Choose the **Visit Length** of your visit, if applicable.
8. Select the **Visit Type** of your visit (e.g., Study, Research, Other).
9. Choose the **Library Branch** you visited from the list provided.
10. Select the **Visit Frequency** of your visit (e.g., First time, Regular, Other).
11. Mark if you'd like to be contacted by library staff.
12. If you have a **Staff Contact**, enter their name, email, and phone number.
13. Enter any additional **Feedback** you'd like to share.
14. Provide your **Visit Date** (optional).
15. Enter your **Visit Time** (optional).
16. Select the **Visitor Type** (e.g., Adult, Child, Group).
17. Enter the **Group Size** of your visit, if applicable.
18. Share any **Comments** about your visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter your email address, if you'd like library staff to contact you.
* **Date Visited**: Enter the date you visited the library, if you'd like to be accurate.
* **Time Visited**: Enter the time you visited the library, if you'd like to be accurate.
* **Purpose Visited**: Briefly describe the reason you visited the library (e.g., study, research, attend an event).
* **Feedback**: Share any feedback or comments about your visit.
* **Contact Method**: Choose how you'd like library staff to contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Visit Length**: Enter the length of your visit, if applicable.
* **Visit Type**: Select the type of visit (e.g., Study, Research, Other).
* **Library Branch**: Choose the library branch you visited.
* **Visit Frequency**: Select the frequency of your visit.
* **Staff Contact**: If you have a staff contact, enter their name, email, and phone number.
* **Staff Name**: Enter the name of the staff member you interacted with.
* **Staff Email**: Enter the email of the staff member you interacted with.
* **Staff Phone**: Enter the phone number of the staff member you interacted with.
* **Library Staff Member**: Indicate if you'd like to be contacted by library staff.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your visit.
* **Visit Date**: Enter the date of your visit.
* **Visit Time**: Enter the time of your visit.
* **Visitor Type**: Select the type of visitor you are (e.g., Adult, Child, Group).
* **Group Size**: Enter the number of people in your group, if applicable.
* **Comments**: Share any comments or feedback about your visit.
