# Yearbook Application Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Yearbook Application Form Template is designed to collect user information and yearbook application details for processing. This form is intended for users who want to apply for the yearbook. Please fill out this form if you meet the required conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field accurately and completely.
2. Ensure you fill out all required fields (marked as **Required**).
3. Review your form carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documentation. This field is **Required**.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documentation. This field is **Required**.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address you have access to. This field is **Required**.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact phone number. This field is **Required**.
* **Application Date**: Enter the date you submitted your yearbook application. This field is **Required**.
* **Year**: Enter the year you submitted your yearbook application. This field is **Required**.
* **Month**: Enter the month you submitted your yearbook application. This field is **Required**.
* **Role**: Select one or more roles that apply to you from the list provided. This field is **Optional**.
* **Application Status**: Select the current status of your application. This field is **Optional**.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information you have about your application. This field is **Optional**.
* **Yearbook Information**: Enter any specific yearbook information. This field is **Optional**.
* **Application Status**: Select the current status of your application. This field is **Optional**.

## Tips
* Ensure you have filled out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Be accurate and honest when filling out your information.
* If you have any issues with the form, contact your yearbook administrator for assistance.
