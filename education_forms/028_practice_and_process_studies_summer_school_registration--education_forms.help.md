# Practice And Process Studies Summer School Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users register for the Practice And Process Studies Summer School. It collects user information and preferences for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete each page one by one, starting from the "User Input" page.
2. Enter your name and any other relevant user details on the "User Input" page.
3. Move on to the "Contact" page, enter your contact information.
4. Proceed to the "Email" page and enter a valid email address.
5. On the "Phone" page, enter a real 10-digit phone number.
6. Select the correct date on the "Date" page.
7. Choose the correct time on the "Time" page.
8. On the "Practice" page, select one or more practice sessions that interest you.
9. Enter any additional notes on the "Notes" page.
10. On the "Email Confirmation" page, select "True" to receive a confirmation email.
10. Finally, review and submit the form on the "Submitted" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (User Input): A page to enter your user details.
* **Contact** (Contact): Enter your contact information.
* **Email** (Email): Enter a valid email address for contact and confirmation purposes.
* **Phone** (Phone): Enter a real 10-digit phone number.
* **Date** (Date): Enter a date in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Time** (Time): Enter a time in the correct format (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Practice** (Practice): Select one or more practice sessions that interest you.
* **Notes** (Notes): Enter any additional notes.
* **Email Confirmation** (Email Confirmation): Select "True" to receive a confirmation email.
* **Submitted** (Submitted): Review and submit your form.
