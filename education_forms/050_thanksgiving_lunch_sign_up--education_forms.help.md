# Thanksgiving Lunch Sign Up - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to sign up for Thanksgiving lunch at the office. It will help us gauge the number of guests attending and assign the necessary tools and resources for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Phone Number in the "Phone Number" field.
3. Enter your Email in the "Email" field.
4. Select your meal choice(s) from the "Meal Choice" dropdown menu.
5. Fill in the number of guests attending from the "Number of Guests" field.
6. Add any comments or additional information in the "Comments" field (optional).
7. Choose the "Assigned Tool" and "Category" for the event.
8. Fill in the "Form ID" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact and communication purposes.
* **Meal Choice** (`Select a meal`, select multiple, required): Choose the meal(s) you would like to have for Thanksgiving lunch. Options include "Yes" and "No" to indicate if you'll be attending.
* **Turkey** (`Turkey`, text, required): If you selected "Yes" for meal choice, fill in this field with any additional comments.
* **Ham** (`Ham`, text, optional): If you selected "Yes" for meal choice and would like to have ham, fill in this field with any additional comments.
* **Roast Beef** (`Roast Beef`, text, optional): If you selected "Yes" for meal choice and would like to have roast beef, fill in this field with any additional comments.
* **Veggie Burger** (`Veggie Burger`, text, optional): If you selected "Yes" for meal choice and would like to have a veggie burger, fill in this field with any additional comments.
* **Number of Guests** (`Number of Guests`, number, required): Enter the number of guests attending with you.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about the event or special requests.
* **Event Details** (`Event Details`, text, optional): Add any additional information about the event.
* **Assigned Tool** (`Assigned Tool`, text, required): Choose the tool assigned to you for the event.
* **Category** (`Category`, text, required): Choose the category for the event.
* **Form ID** (`Form ID`, text, optional): Enter the form ID (if provided).
* **Description** (`Description`, text, optional): Add a description of the event (if desired).
