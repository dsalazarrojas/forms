# Student Device Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Device Check In Form is used to record student's device check in status during events or activities. This form helps school staff or administrators track student's device check in status.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Determine if the student is checking in their device

*   For "Device Check In Form", select "Yes" if the student is checking in their device, and "No" otherwise.

### Step 2: Enter the device ID and student ID

*   Enter the student's ID in the "Student ID" field.
*   Enter the device ID in the "Device ID" field.

### Step 3: Choose the device type

*   For "Device Type", select the correct type of device being checked in (e.g., laptop, tablet, etc.).

### Step 4: Enter the configuration and any notes

*   In the "Configuration" field, enter any additional information about the device's configuration.
*   Enter any notes about the check in process in the "Notes" field.

### Step 5: Enter the date and time

*   Enter the date of the device check in in the "Date" field.
*   Enter the time of the device check in in the "Time" field.

### Step 6: Enter the student name and device model

*   Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
*   Enter the device model in the "Device Model" field.
