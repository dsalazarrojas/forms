# Band Scholarship Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Band Scholarship Application Form is a crucial tool for students to showcase their musical talents, academic achievements, and personal qualities to receive support for their educational pursuits.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start, ensure that you have all necessary documents and statements prepared, such as your personal statement and any required recommendation letters. These can be uploaded through the application process.
2.  Begin by filling in your personal information in the **Applicant Personal Information** section.
3.  Enter your **Student Full Name**, which should match the name on your student ID or passport.
4.  Provide your **Student Email Address** and **Student Phone Number**, which we will use to contact you for any further information or updates.
5.  Enter your **Permanent Mailing Address**, which should be where we can send any necessary documents or communication.
6.  In the **Academic and School Information** section, list the **High School Name** where you attended or are currently attending.
7.  Enter your **Cumulative GPA** from high school.
8.  Choose the college or university you plan to attend in the **Intended College or University** field.
9.  In the **Musical Background** section, enter your **Primary Instrument**, which you play.
10.  Describe your **Number of Years in Band**, including any extracurricular activities or performances you have been involved in.
11.  If applicable, list your **Leadership Roles** you have held within bands or music groups.
12.  If applicable, list any **Music Honors and Awards** you have received.
13.  In the **Application Documents and Statements** section, write a **Music Impact Personal Statement** that describes how music has influenced your high school career.
14.  Provide the name of your **Band Director Providing Recommendation**.
15.  Choose how you plan to **Use Scholarship Funds** from the options provided.
16.  Finally, review and confirm your **Applicant Certification** by selecting all the relevant options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your student ID or passport.
*   **Student Email Address**: Enter your official student email address used for communication.
*   **Student Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
*   **Permanent Mailing Address**: Enter your current or permanent mailing address.
*   **High School Name**: Provide the name of the high school you attended or currently attend.
*   **Cumulative GPA**: Enter your cumulative GPA from high school.
*   **Intended College or University**: Choose the college or university you plan to attend.
*   **Primary Instrument**: List your primary instrument you play.
*   **Number of Years in Band**: Describe your experience in band, including marching band, concert band, jazz band, etc.
*   **Leadership Roles**: List any leadership positions you have held in bands or music groups, if applicable.
*   **Music Honors and Awards**: List any music-related honors or awards you have received, if applicable.
*   **Music Impact Personal Statement**: In 500 words or less, describe how music has influenced your high school career.
*   **Band Director Providing Recommendation**: Provide the name of your band director who will be providing a recommendation.
*   **Planned Use of Scholarship Funds**: Choose how you plan to use the scholarship funds from the given options.
*   **Applicant Certification**: Confirm that you have reviewed and understand the application process and that all information provided is accurate.
