# Remote Learning Daily Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Remote Learning Daily Log Form is used to document a student's daily progress in remote learning activities. This form helps teachers and administrators track and monitor students' performance, providing a snapshot of their progress and challenges faced.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Student Id** - a unique identifier for the student.
2. Fill in the **Student Name** - the name of the student.
3. Select the **Date** of the day's remote learning activity.
4. Choose the **Progress Status** - select "Completed" if the student has finished the activity, "Partially Completed" for partial completion, or "Not Completed" if they haven't started or finished.
5. Add any **Notes** about the day's progress or challenges.
6. Select who **Submitted** the form - choose "Teacher" if the teacher submitted it or "Student" if the student submitted it themselves.
7. Select who the form was **Submitted To** - choose "Teacher", "Parent", or "Admin" to indicate who received the form.
8. Optionally, add a **Notes** section for additional comments or feedback.
9. Fill in the **Form Title**, **Form Description**, **Form Category**, and any other relevant fields, which are not explicitly described but seem to be metadata for form administration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Id**: A unique identifier for the student, required for accurate tracking.
* **Student Name**: Enter the name of the student.
* **Date**: Select the date of the remote learning activity.
* **Progress Status**: Choose the correct status from "Completed", "Partially Completed", or "Not Completed".
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or feedback about the day's progress or challenges.
* **Submitter**: Select who submitted the form - choose "Teacher" or "Student".
* **Submitted To**: Select who received the form - choose "Teacher", "Parent", or "Admin".
* **Form Title**, **Form Description**, **Form Category**, **Form Updated At**, **Form Created At**, **Form Assigned To**, **Form Issued To**, **Form Submitter**, **Form Submitter**, **Form Submitted At**, and **Form Submitter By User**: These fields appear to be metadata for form administration and are not explicitly described, but are likely used to track form-related details such as title, description, category, and submission history.
