# Scholarship Application 2018 - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from students applying for the 2018 scholarship. It will help us understand your background, academic performance, and other relevant details for consideration.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields and provide as much detail as possible.
2. Be accurate and honest with your responses.
3. Ensure that your contact information is up to date and correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Scholarship Application**: This field is for your general application information. Please provide a brief summary of why you are applying for the scholarship.
* **Student Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., driver's license, passport).
* **School Name**: Enter the name of your current or intended school.
* **Gpa**: Enter your current GPA (if you are a current student) or your expected GPA (if you are a prospective student). Please note that a GPA of 3.0 or above is typically required for consideration.
* **Gpa Scale**: Select the GPA scale used by your school. This will help us understand your academic performance.
* **Major**: Select your primary major(s) or field of study. If you have multiple majors, select all that apply.
* **Minor**: Enter any additional fields of study or interests you have.
* **Email**: Enter your current email address. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your current phone number. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **Address**: Enter your current address. This will be used for mailing purposes.
* **City**: Enter the city where you currently reside.
* **State**: Enter the state where you currently reside.
* **Country**: Enter the country where you currently reside.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Date of Birth (date)**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Date of Birth (time)**: Enter your time of birth in the format 12:00 AM/PM.
* **Date of Birth (range)**: Enter your date range of birth (e.g., from 1999-01-01 to 1999-06-01).
* **Date of Graduation**: Enter your expected or actual date of graduation. Note: If you are a deceased student, please only fill in the date of death.
* **Date of Graduation (date)**: Enter your expected or actual date of graduation in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Date of Graduation (time)**: Enter your expected or actual time of graduation in the format 12:00 AM/PM.
* **Date of Graduation (range)**: Enter your expected or actual date range of graduation (e.g., from 2022-01-01 to 2022-06-01).
* **Date of Death**: Enter the date of your passing, if applicable.
* **Date of Death (date)**: Enter the date of your passing in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Date of Death (time)**: Enter the time of your passing in the format 12:00 AM/PM.
* **Date of Death (range)**: Enter the date range of your passing (e.g., from 2022-01-01 to 2022-06-01).
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional information you would like to share with us.

Note: If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us. We are here to help.
