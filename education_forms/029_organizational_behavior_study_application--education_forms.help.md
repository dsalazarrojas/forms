# organizational_behavior_study_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant details for research or study purposes related to organizational behavior.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Name**, **Email**, and **Phone** fields, as these are required for contact purposes.
2. Choose your **Role** from the available options (Student, Employee, or Faculty).
3. Enter your **Availability** in the format MM/DD/YY (e.g., 01/01/22).
4. If you have any additional information to provide, you can enter it in the **Additional Information** field.
5. Review and submit the form when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details** (Participant Details): 
  * A space to enter your name.
* **Availability** (Availability): 
  * Enter your availability in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Consent** (Consent): 
  * This field is currently empty, but please note that it's a placeholder for any necessary consent or disclaimer statements.
* **Name** (Name): 
  * Enter your name.
* **Email** (Email): 
  * Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Phone): 
  * Enter your phone number.
* **Role** (Role): 
  * Choose your role within the organization (Student, Employee, or Faculty).
* **Department** (Department): 
  * Enter your department or area of affiliation.
* **Additional Information** (Additional Information): 
  * Enter any relevant additional information you'd like to provide.

## Tips
* Ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you have any issues or questions while completing the form, please contact the form administrators.
* Please review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
