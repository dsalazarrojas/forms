# Student ID Card Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a student ID card, which is a unique identifier card for students. It captures information such as student's ID type, number, name, email, phone number, and date of birth. The purpose of this form is to ensure that students can easily access their ID card information and perform necessary actions related to their student status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your ID card type by choosing from the provided options.
2. Enter your unique student ID number.
3. Fill in your student's name.
4. Enter a valid email address for your student.
5. Enter a phone number for your student.
6. Enter your student's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
7. Upload a student photo (this field is optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student ID Card Type** (`student_id_card_type`, select_one, required/optional): This field allows you to select the type of student ID card you are registering. Choose from the provided options to ensure accurate classification.
* **Student ID Number** (`student_id_number`, number, required): Enter a unique ID number for your student.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Provide the name of the student.
* **Email** (`student_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for the student.
* **Phone** (`student_phone`, text, required): Enter a phone number for the student.
* **Date of Birth** (`student_birthdate`, date, required): Enter the student's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Student Photo** (`student_photo`, text, optional): Upload a photo of the student (optional).

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately.
- Enter valid email and phone numbers.
- Use a valid date format for date of birth.
- Ensure that the student ID card type and number are correct and unique.
- The student photo is optional and not required to be uploaded.
