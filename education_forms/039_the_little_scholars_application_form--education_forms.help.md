# The Little Scholars Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide necessary information for the enrollment process of new students into The Little Scholars program. 
## How To Complete This Form
### Step 1: Fill out each field accurately and completely.
### Step 2: Ensure all required fields are filled in.
### Step 3: Review and submit your form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Name (student_name, text, required)**: Please enter your full legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
  * This field is used to record your name for identification purposes.
- **Contact Person (contact_person, select_one, optional)**: Please select the name of the person you would like to be our point of contact.
  * You can choose between John, Jane, or Other.
- **Date of Birth (date_of_birth, date, required)**: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
  * This field is used for age verification and to confirm your eligibility for The Little Scholars program.
- **School Name (school_name, select_multiple, required)**: Select all schools you are interested in applying to.
  * Choose from ABC School, DEF School, or GHI School.
- **Address (address, text, required)**: Enter your home address or the address of your school.
  * This field is used to send important notifications and reminders.
- **Phone Number (phone_number, text, optional)**: Enter your phone number for further contact.
  * Please use the format of (+country code)xxxxxxxxx.
- **Email (email, email, optional)**: Enter your email for further contact and important updates.
  * Please enter a valid email address.
- **Parent Signature (parent_signature, text, required)**: Sign your name as a parent or guardian.
  * Please print your name clearly for verification purposes.
- **School Signature (school_signature, text, optional)**: If applicable, enter your school signature.
  * This field is not required but may be necessary for school verification.
