# Database Design Concepts Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide will walk you through the Database Design Concepts Assessment form. The form is designed to assess your understanding of database design principles and their application in real-world scenarios.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form carefully, providing your answers to the theory questions and selecting the most appropriate responses to the scenarios.
2. Make sure to answer all required fields, as indicated by the red asterisk (\*) mark.
3. If a field is not required, you can skip it or leave it blank.
4. For the date field, select the date you are currently completing the form.
5. For the time field, select the time you are currently completing the form, if applicable.
6. For the email and phone fields, you can provide your contact information if you wish.
7. In the note field, you can add any additional comments or explanations you'd like to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Database Design Concepts Assessment Form** (`database_design_concepts_assessment_form_title`, text, required): This is the title of the form, where you are required to enter your name or a description of the assessment.
* **Theory Questions** (`theory_questions`, text, required): Please provide your answers to the theory questions in this field.
* **Database Design Scenarios** (`database_design_scenarios`, text, required): Select your answers to the database design scenarios in this field.
* **Questions** (`questions`, select_multiple, required): Select all the questions that apply to your understanding of database design concepts.
* **Scenario** (`scenario`, select_one, optional): Select the scenario that best describes your situation.
* **Theory Questions Score** (`theory_questions_score`, select_one, required): Select your score for the theory questions, indicating your level of agreement.
* **Database Design Scenarios Score** (`database_design_scenarios_score`, select_one, required): Select your score for the database design scenarios, indicating your level of agreement.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Select the date you are currently completing the form.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select the time you are currently completing the form, if applicable.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Provide your email address, if you wish to be contacted about your assessment.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number, if you wish to be contacted about your assessment.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or explanations you'd like to provide about your assessment.
