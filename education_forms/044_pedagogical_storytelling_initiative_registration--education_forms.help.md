# Pedagogical Storytelling Initiative Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information for the Pedagogical Storytelling Initiative, a project aimed at gathering data on storytelling approaches and methods used in educational settings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field as prompted, providing as much detail as possible for each section. You can submit the form when all required fields (if any) are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First-page Label**: This is an optional section for you to describe this form or introduce yourself if you'd like.
*   **Storyteller Info**: In this text field, please provide basic information about yourself, including your name, role within the storytelling project (if applicable), or any other relevant details.
*   **Role and Motivation**: Here, you can explain your role within the project and what motivates you to participate. This can be as simple or as detailed as you'd like.
*   **Program Info**: Share any background or details about the programs you are associated with, or how your program might benefit from the Pedagogical Storytelling Initiative.
*   **Workshop Info**: Use this field for any other details you'd like to share about workshops or events associated with your project or programs.

## Tips
- Please provide as much detail as you can for each section, as it will help the initiative gather more comprehensive data.
- If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
