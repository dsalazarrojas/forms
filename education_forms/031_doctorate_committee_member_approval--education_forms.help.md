# Doctorate Committee Member Approval - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Doctorate Committee Member Approval" form is used to collect information about a committee member's approval status for a doctorate candidate. It is typically used in academic settings to streamline the approval process and reduce paperwork.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Committee Member Name in the first field.
2. Select the Role of the committee member from the options provided.
3. If applicable, enter the Evaluation Date and Evaluation details.
4. Make a Decision about the approval status of the committee member (e.g., Approve, Not Approve, Abstain).
5. Enter the Signature Date and Signature of the committee member (if applicable).
6. Add any Comments about the approval process.
7. Enter the Committee Member Email and Phone (if applicable).
8. Enter the Committee Member Institution (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Committee Member Name** (`committee_member_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the committee member.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, required: false): Select the role of the committee member from the options provided.
* **Evaluation** (`evaluation`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the evaluation (if applicable).
* **Decision** (`decision`, select_multiple, required: false): Make a decision about the approval status of the committee member (e.g., Approve, Not Approve, Abstain).
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the signature (if applicable).
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required: false): Enter the signature of the committee member (if applicable).
* **Approved** (`approved`, select_one, required: false): Select the approval status of the committee member.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the evaluation (if applicable).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Add any comments about the approval process.
* **Committee Member Email** (`committee_member_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email of the committee member (if applicable).
* **Committee Member Phone** (`committee_member_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the committee member (if applicable).
* **Committee Member Institution** (`committee_member_institution`, text, required: false): Enter the institution of the committee member (if applicable).
