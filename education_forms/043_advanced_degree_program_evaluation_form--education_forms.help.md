# Advanced Degree Program Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Advanced Degree Program Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback from students about the quality and effectiveness of their advanced degree program. The form is used to evaluate various aspects of the program, including academic rigor, faculty support, resources and facilities, and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer each question truthfully.
2. Select the most accurate option for each question.
3. Provide specific examples or details to support your answers where possible.
4. Ensure you answer all required questions before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: This is a text field where you can enter your name.
	+ Please enter your name as it appears on your identification document.
* **Degree Program**: This is a select-one field where you can choose the type of degree program you are enrolled in (e.g., Master of Science, Master of Arts, MBA, PhD, etc.).
	+ Select the degree program that aligns with your current or intended program of study.
* **Area of Specialization**: This is a text field where you can enter your area of specialization within your degree program.
	+ Please describe your area of specialization briefly (e.g., Computer Science, Business Administration, etc.).
* **Current Year of Study**: This is a number field where you can enter the current year of your study.
	+ Enter the current year of your study (e.g., 1, 2, 3, etc.).
* **Academic Rigor Rating**: This is a select-one field where you can rate the academic rigor of your coursework.
	+ Select the level of academic rigor that best describes your experience (e.g., Very High, High, Moderate, Low).
* **Faculty Support and Mentorship**: This is a select-one field where you can rate the support and mentorship you receive from your professors and advisors.
	+ Select the level of support you receive from your faculty (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Relevance to Career Goals**: This is a select-one field where you can rate how well your program prepares you for your career goals.
	+ Select the level of relevance you perceive to your career goals (e.g., Very Relevant, Relevant, Somewhat Relevant, Not Relevant).
* **Library and Research Database Access**: This is a select-one field where you can rate the quality of library and research database access.
	+ Select the level of access you have to library and research databases (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Lab or Studio Facility Quality**: This is a select-one field where you can rate the quality of lab or studio facilities if applicable.
	+ Select the level of quality you perceive in lab or studio facilities (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Not Applicable).
* **Would Recommend This Program**: This is a select-one field where you can indicate whether you would recommend this program to prospective students.
	+ Select one of the options to indicate your stance (e.g., Definitely, Probably, Maybe, False).
* **Key Strengths of the Program**: This is a text field where you can describe the strengths of your program.
	+ Provide a brief description of the program's strengths (e.g., excellent faculty, comprehensive curriculum, etc.).
* **Suggestions for Program Improvement**: This is a text field where you can suggest areas for program improvement.
	+ Offer specific suggestions for improvement (e.g., more internship opportunities, better library resources, etc.).
