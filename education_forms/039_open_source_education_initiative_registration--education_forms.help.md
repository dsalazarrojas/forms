# Open Source Education Initiative Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Open Source Education Initiative Registration form is designed to collect information about education programs that participate in the Open Source Education Initiative. This information is used to categorize and promote education programs on our platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of education program you represent:
    - Open Source Education Program
    - Non Profit
    - Community Group
2.  Enter your name and email address to register your interest.
3.  Select one or more interests that your program aligns with.
4.  Confirm your consent to join the initiative.
5.  Enter your program's name, URL, and description.
6.  Provide contact information for your program, including the name, email, phone number, address, and contact person's title.
7.  Enter the organization's name and URL, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Education Program Type**: Select the type of education program you represent:
	+ Open Source Education Program
	+ Non Profit
	+ Community Group
* **Name**: Enter the name of the participant.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the participant.
* **Interests**: Select one or more interests that your program aligns with:
	+ Programming
	+ Data Science
	+ Machine Learning
	+ Artificial Intelligence
* **Consent to Join**: Confirm your agreement to join the Open Source Education Initiative.
* **Program Name**: Enter the name of your education program.
* **Program URL**: Enter the URL of your education program's website.
* **Program Description**: Provide a brief description of your education program.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Address**: Enter the address of the contact person.
* **City**: Enter the city of the contact person.
* **State**: Enter the state of the contact person.
* **Zip**: Enter the zip code of the contact person.
* **Country**: Enter the country of the contact person.
* **Program URL 2**: Enter the URL of your education program's website (if different from Program URL).
* **Program Url 3**: Enter any additional URL related to your education program.

## Tips
Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date. If you need to update any of the fields, please contact us at [your contact email].
