# Written Production Evaluation Rubric - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Written Production Evaluation Rubric form is designed to help reviewers evaluate written works based on specific criteria.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Introduction**: This is a text field where you can provide any necessary context or background information about the submitted work.
2. **Criteria 1-3**: These three fields are used to score specific aspects of the written work. Each score should be entered as a number between 1 and 10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
3. **Overall Comment**: This field is for providing a brief overview of your evaluation.
4. **Submission Information**:
	* **Submission Date**: Enter the date when the work was submitted.
	* **Submission Time**: Enter the time when the work was submitted (if applicable).
	* **Submission Time Zone**: Enter the time zone where the work was submitted (if applicable).
5. **Submission Details**:
	* **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person who submitted the work.
	* **Submitted To**: Enter the name or title of the work's recipient or reviewer.
6. **Scores and Feedback**:
	* **Score**: Enter your overall score for the written work, between 1 and 10.
	* **Rubric Scores**: Enter scores for each of the specific rubrics (1-4).
	* **Rubric 1-4 Scores**: Enter individual scores for each rubric.
