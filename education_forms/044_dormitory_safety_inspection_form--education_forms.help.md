# Dormitory Safety Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document the safety inspection of a dormitory, ensuring that all necessary safety measures are taken and hazards are addressed. This form helps facility management and staff to maintain a safe living environment for residents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the date of your inspection, which is required for record-keeping and tracking purposes.
2. Enter your name and title as the inspector, if you are not already logged in.
3. Input your facility ID, which is usually a unique code assigned to your facility.
4. Provide the name of the facility being inspected.
5. Enter the address of the facility, including any relevant contact information.
6. Take note of any safety concerns you have found during your inspection, such as electrical or water issues, and list them in the "Safety concerns" field.
7. Indicate the action items you will take to address the safety concerns, such as fixing electrical issues or repairing water issues.
8. Provide a brief description of your inspection results, including any notable findings or observations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <inspection date> (Required)
The inspection date is the date you conducted the safety inspection of the dormitory.

*   <dormitory_inspector> (Optional)
    Your name and title as the inspector, which can be left blank for anonymous submissions.

*   <dormitory_facility_id> (Optional)
    A unique code assigned to your facility for identification and tracking purposes.

*   <dormitory_facility_name> (Optional)
    The name of the dormitory being inspected.

*   <dormitory_facility_address> (Optional)
    The address of the facility, including any relevant contact information.

*   <dormitory_facility_contact_info> (Optional)
    Any additional contact information for the facility, such as phone numbers or emails.

*   <dormitory_notes> (Optional)
    A text field for any additional notes or comments about the inspection.

*   <dormitory_safety_concerns> (Required)
    List any safety concerns you have found during your inspection, such as electrical or water issues.

*   <dormitory_action_items> (Required)
    Choose one or more actions to be taken to address the safety concerns.

*   <dormitory_inspection_results> (Optional)
    Briefly describe your inspection results, including any notable findings or observations.

### Tips
*   Be thorough in listing safety concerns and taking action items.
*   Clearly document your inspection results to ensure a smooth handover to future inspectors.
*   Ensure your submissions are accurate and up-to-date for tracking and record-keeping purposes.
