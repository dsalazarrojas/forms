# Student Experiential Learning Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to report on student experiential learning experiences. Complete this form to provide information about your learning activities and projects.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully to understand what information is required.
2. Answer the questions truthfully and accurately, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Use the available fields to provide your thoughts and feedback about the learning experience.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Introduction
*   **Introduction** (note, not required)
    Provide a brief introduction to the learning experience, including the context, objectives, and any other relevant information.

### 2. Student Information
*   **Student Information** (select one: Yes/No)
    Please indicate if this form is being completed by a student or instructor. If you are a student, select "Yes" to indicate that this form is your report. If you are an instructor, select "No" and provide the student's name in the `activity_info` field.

### 3. Teacher Information
*   **Teacher Information** (select multiple: Yes/No)
    Please indicate if you are the teacher or instructor who supervised the learning experience. Select "Yes" to indicate that you are the teacher, or "No" if you are not. 

### 4. Activity Info
*   **Activity Info** (text)
    Provide details about the activity or project, including any relevant dates, times, or locations.

### 5. Feedback
*   **Feedback** (date)
    Share your thoughts and feedback about the learning experience, including what you liked or disliked about it.

### 6. Evaluation
*   **Evaluation** (select one: Yes/No)
    Please evaluate the effectiveness of the learning experience, selecting "Yes" if it was beneficial and "No" if not.

### 7. Recommendations
*   **Recommendations** (text)
    Share any suggestions or recommendations for future learning experiences, including what could be improved.

### 8. Next Steps
*   **Next Steps** (text)
    Outline any next steps or follow-up activities that could be taken to further improve the learning experience.

### 9. Comments
*   **Comments** (text)
    Provide any additional comments or observations about the learning experience.

### 10. Additional Comments
*   **Additional Comments** (text)
    Share any additional thoughts or insights about the learning experience.

### 11. Signature
*   **Signature** (time)
    Please sign the form, indicating that you have reviewed and submitted it accurately.

### 12. Submission Date
*   **Submission Date** (date)
    Please submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.
