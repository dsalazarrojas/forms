# Cohort Retake Dates Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cohort Retake Dates Survey form is designed to help you provide your retake dates and preferences for a course or program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill in your participant information, including your name, email, student ID, and cohort name.
- Indicate if you need to retake the course and provide a reason for your retake request.
- Choose your preferred dates for the retake (up to three options).
- Select your preferred course format (in-person, online, hybrid, or no preference).
- Indicate if you have any location preferences.
- Specify if you need additional support and what type of support you require.
- Provide your phone number and preferred contact method.
- Add any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Fill in your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your official email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, required): Enter your student ID number for identification purposes.
* **Cohort Name** (`cohort_name`, text, required): Select the cohort you are currently enrolled in.
* **Program or Course Name** (`program_name`, text, required): Select the program or course you wish to retake.
* **Do you need to retake the course?** (`needs_retake`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" if you need to retake the course.
* **Reason for Retake Request** (`reason_for_retake`, text, optional): Provide a brief explanation for your retake request (if applicable).
* **First Choice Date** (`first_choice_date`, date, required): Choose a date for the retake that suits you best.
* **Second Choice Date** (`second_choice_date`, date, optional): Provide an alternative date for the retake (if the first choice is not available).
* **Third Choice Date** (`third_choice_date`, date, optional): Another date option for the retake (if the first two choices are not available).
* **Dates You Cannot Attend** (`unavailable_dates`, text, optional): List any dates you are unable to attend.
* **Preferred Session Time** (`preferred_time`, select_one, required): Select your preferred time slot for the retake (morning, afternoon, evening, or flexible).
* **Preferred Course Format** (`preferred_format`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred course format (in-person, online, hybrid, or no preference).
* **Location Preference** (`location_preference`, text, optional): Specify any location preferences you may have (if in-person).
* **Do you need additional support?** (`additional_support`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you require additional support.
* **Type of Support Needed** (`support_type`, text, optional): Specify the type of support you need (tutoring, accommodations, etc.).
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact`, select_one, required): Select your preferred contact method (email, phone, text message).
* **Additional Notes or Comments** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional information or comments about your retake request.
