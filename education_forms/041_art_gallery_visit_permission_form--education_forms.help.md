# Art Gallery Visit Permission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect essential information and permission from parents for their children to attend an art gallery visit as part of a school trip. It includes details about the school trip, student information, and parent - guardian consent and contact.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the School Trip Details section with the required information:
	* Enter the school name.
	* Specify the grade class of the students attending.
	* Identify the teacher in charge of the trip.
	* Enter the name of the gallery exhibition to be visited.
2. Provide Student Information:
	* Enter the student's full name.
	* List any relevant medical conditions or allergies.
3. Provide Parent - Guardian Information:
	* Enter your name (parent - guardian).
	* Provide your contact phone number.
4. Grant Permission:
	* Select "Yes - I Grant Permission" if you agree to your child attending the trip.
5. Grant Photography Consent:
	* Select "True" if you agree to allow gallery staff to photograph your child for educational purposes.
6. Sign the Form:
	* Enter your digital signature.
	* Enter the date of your digital signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name** (`school_name`): Enter the name of the school your child is attending.
* **Grade - Class** (`grade_class`): Enter the grade level of the students attending the trip.
* **Teacher in Charge** (`teacher_in_charge`): Enter the name of the teacher leading the trip.
* **Gallery Exhibition to be Visited** (`exhibition_name`): Enter the name of the art gallery exhibition to be visited.
* **Date of Visit** (`trip_date`): Enter the date of the trip.
* **Estimated Arrival and Departure Times** (`arrival_departure_times`): Enter the estimated arrival and departure times of the trip.
* **Student Full Name** (`student_name`): Enter your child's full name.
* **Relevant Medical Conditions or Allergies** (`medical_conditions_allergies`): List any medical conditions or allergies your child may have.
* **Parent - Guardian Name** (`parent_guardian_name`): Enter your name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`parent_guardian_phone`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **I grant permission for my child to attend the art gallery field trip** (`permission_granted`): Select "Yes - I Grant Permission" if you agree to your child attending the trip.
* **Permission for gallery staff to photograph students for educational - school purposes** (`photography_consent_educational`): Select "True" if you allow gallery staff to photograph your child for educational purposes.
* **Digital Signature of Parent - Guardian** (`electronic_signature_parent`): Enter your digital signature.
* **Date of Signature** (`signature_date`): Enter the date of your digital signature.
