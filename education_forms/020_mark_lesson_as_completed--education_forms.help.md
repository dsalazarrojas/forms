# Mark Lesson As Completed - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to mark a lesson as completed by a student. It's essential to fill out this form only for lessons that are not currently active or scheduled. This ensures that the lesson's completion status is up-to-date and accurate.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the lesson you want to mark as completed from the "Lesson Completion" page.
2. Choose the status of the lesson you've completed:
	* Select "Active" if the lesson was completed successfully.
	* Select "Inactive" if the lesson was not completed or had issues.
3. Provide any additional comments about the lesson, if necessary.
4. Enter the student's email (if available).
5. Enter the student's phone number (if available).
6. Enter your name as the trainer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lesson Completion** (`lesson_page`): Select the lesson you want to mark as completed. This field is a dropdown menu that lists all the lessons available for completion.
* **Status** (`lesson_completion_status`): Choose the status of the lesson you've completed. This field has two options: "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Comment** (`trainer_comment`): Provide any additional comments about the lesson, if necessary.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`): Enter the student's email address, if available.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`): Enter the student's phone number, if available.
* **Trainer Name** (`trainer_name`): Enter your name as the trainer who marked the lesson as completed.

## Tips

* Make sure to select the correct lesson and status to ensure the completion status is accurate.
* Enter any additional comments to provide context about the lesson's completion.
* Fill out the student's email and phone number only if they are available.
