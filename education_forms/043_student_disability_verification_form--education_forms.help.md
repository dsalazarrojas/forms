# Student Disability Verification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from students to verify their disability status. This information may be used to provide accommodations and support to students who require it.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully and fill out the required fields (if any).
2. Select the relevant options for "Disability Type" based on your disability.
3. Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Enter your student identification number (if applicable).
5. Provide a brief statement explaining your disability.
6. Enter the date of your disability onset.
7. Enter the name, title, and contact information of your medical provider.
8. Sign the "Disability Statement" and date it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header**: This section provides a summary of the form and its purpose.
* **Student Name**: Please enter your student ID or name as it appears on your student record.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Student Identification**: Enter your student ID number if applicable.
* **Disability Type**: Select all applicable options from the list.
	+ Physical: This includes physical disabilities such as mobility issues or chronic illnesses.
	+ Mental: This includes mental health conditions such as anxiety or depression.
	+ Emotional: This includes emotional disabilities such as post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD) or other emotional challenges.
* **Disability Severity**: Indicate the level of severity of your disability using the scale provided.
* **Date of Disability Onset**: Enter the date when your disability first became apparent.
* **Disability Implications**: Provide a brief statement explaining how your disability affects your daily activities or academic performance.
* **Medical Documentation**: Enter any relevant medical documentation or documentation from your medical provider.
* **Medical Provider**: Enter the name and title of your medical provider.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the phone number of your medical provider.
* **Disability Statement**: Provide a brief statement explaining your disability.
* **Disability Statement Date**: Enter the date you signed the disability statement.
* **Disability Statement Signed By**: Enter your name as it appears on your disability statement.
* **Disability Statement Signed**: Sign and date the disability statement.
* **Disability Statement Signed By**: Sign and date the disability statement.
