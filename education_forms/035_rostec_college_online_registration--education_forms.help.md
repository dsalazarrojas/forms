# Rostec College Online Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register as a student or applicant for Rostec College's online courses. Fill out this form to provide us with necessary information for processing your registration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read this guide carefully to understand the required fields and the format of the form.
2. Fill out the form with the requested information. Make sure to double-check your entries for accuracy.
3. Click submit once you have filled out the entire form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., ID, passport).
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., ID, passport).
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you have access to. This will be used as your contact information and for any subsequent communication from us.
* **Phone**: Enter a valid phone number that you can be reached at.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address**: Enter your current address for our records.
* **City**: Enter the city where you currently reside.
* **Country**: Select your country of residence from the dropdown menu.
* **State**: Select all states you are currently registered to vote in or plan to reside in.
* **Zip Code**: Enter your valid zip code.
* **None**: This field is currently unused and can be left blank.
* **State**: Select the state where you currently reside or plan to reside (this field is different from the state field above, which is for voting registration).
* **Zip Code**: Enter your valid zip code for the selected state.
