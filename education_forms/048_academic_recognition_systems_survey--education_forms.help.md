# Academic Recognition Systems Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This help guide is designed to provide detailed information about the "Academic Recognition Systems Survey" form, including how to complete it and what each field represents.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Review the purpose and instructions above
*   Select the relevant options for each question
*   Fill in all fields with relevant and accurate information
*   Review your answers before submitting the form

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Institutional role** (`role`, `select_one`, required): Choose the option that best describes your position within the institution. This could be Student, Faculty, Staff, Administrator, or Other.
*   **Department or unit** (`department`, `text`, optional): Enter your faculty department or administrative unit. This is an optional field and will not impact your ability to submit the form.
*   **Satisfaction with current recognition practices** (`current_recognition_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the current recognition practices using the provided options: Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very dissatisfied.
*   **Types of recognition most valued** (`types_of_recognition_valued`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply from the provided options: Public awards, Teaching awards, Research grants, Career advancement, or Certificates. You can select more than one option.
*   **Main barriers to fair recognition** (`barriers_to_recognition`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe any perceived barriers to fair recognition practices within your institution.
*   **Suggestions to improve recognition systems** (`improvement_suggestions`, `text`, optional): Provide specific and actionable recommendations for improving the recognition system.
*   **Willingness to participate in reform efforts** (`willingness_to_participate`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your willingness to participate in recognition system reform efforts using the provided options: True, Maybe, or False.
*   **Contact information for follow-up** (`contact_for_followup`, `email`, optional): If you agree to be contacted for follow-up communication, leave your email address. This field is optional but allows the researchers to contact you.

## Tips

*   Be thorough but concise in your answers
*   Review the questions and fields carefully to ensure accurate completion
*   If you have any questions or concerns, refer to this help guide or contact the researchers for further assistance.
