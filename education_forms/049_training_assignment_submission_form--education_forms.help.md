# Training Assignment Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Training Assignment Submission Form is designed to collect data on training assignments from users. This form helps us track and manage training assignments accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully before starting.
2. Select the correct options for each field as needed.
3. Fill in all required fields (marked with \*).
4. Submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submission Status** (11): Select the current status of the submission: *Pending*, *Completed*, or *Rejected*.
* **User Input** (1): Choose whether you have provided user input for the training assignment.
* **File Upload 1** (2): Upload a file related to the training assignment.
* **Training Assignment** (3): Enter a brief description of the training assignment.
* **File Upload 2** (4): Upload another file related to the training assignment.
* **Instructor Name** (5): Enter the name of the instructor leading the training assignment.
* **Student Name** (6): Enter the name of the student participating in the training assignment.
* **Student Grade** (7): Enter the student's grade for the training assignment (if applicable).
* **Submission Date** (8): Enter the date you submitted this form.
* **Submission Time** (9): Enter the time you submitted this form.
* **Notes** (10): Add any additional notes regarding the submission.
* **Email Address** (12): Enter your email address (if you want to be contacted about the submission).
* **Phone** (13): Enter your phone number (if you want to be contacted about the submission).
* **Training Assignment Id** (14): Enter the ID of the training assignment (if applicable).
* **Form Submission Date** (15): This date is automatically populated upon form submission and is not required to be filled out.

Note: The fields marked with \* are required to be filled out in order to submit the form.
