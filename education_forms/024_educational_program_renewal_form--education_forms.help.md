# Educational Program Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Program Renewal Form is a tool used by educators and institutions to renew and update their program information. This form helps gather important details about the program, contact information, and any comments or notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review your current program details and information before starting.
* Fill in the relevant fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
* Make sure to provide all required information, marked as "Required" in each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Information** (`student_info`, text, optional):
	+ Please enter your name and any other relevant student information.
* **Program Information** (`program_info`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your current program details, including any relevant information about the program.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your contact information, including your name, email, phone number, and any other relevant details.
* **Program Renewal Information** (`program_renewal_info`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any relevant information about the program renewal, including any updates or changes.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional):
	+ Add any additional comments or notes about the program renewal, such as future plans or concerns.

## Tips
* Please be accurate and thorough when filling in the form to ensure that your information is up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx) for assistance.
* This form is a supporting document for educational institutions, and your input is greatly appreciated!
