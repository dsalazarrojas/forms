# mba_qualifying_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect background information and qualifications from individuals interested in pursuing an MBA degree.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Read each section carefully to ensure you understand the type of information being requested.
2. Answer all required fields to provide the necessary information.
3. Double-check your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Background information
* **Background information** (Text): This field is for providing a brief background about yourself. Please include any relevant information that may be helpful in evaluating your qualifications.

### Contact details
* **Contact details** (Text): In this field, provide your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number. 

### Have you completed a bachelor's degree from a recognized university?
* **Have you completed a bachelors degree from a recognized university?** (Select One):
	+ Option 1: Yes
	+ Option 2: No
	+ Option 4: Undecided

### Have you taken the GMAT or GRE?
* **Have you taken the GMAT or GRE?** (Select One):
	+ Option 1: Yes
	+ Option 2: No
	+ Option 3: Undecided

### Have you taken the TOEFL or IELTS test?
* **Have you taken the TOEFL or IELTS test?** (Select One):
	+ Option 1: Yes
	+ Option 2: No
	+ Option 3: Undecided

### Language proficiency
* **Language proficiency** (Select Multiple):
	+ Option 1: English
	+ Option 2: French
	+ Option 3: Spanish
	+ Option 4: Other

### Work experience
* **Work experience** (Text): In this field, describe any work experience you have, including job titles, companies, and durations.

### Personal details
* **Personal details** (Text): This field is for providing any additional personal information that you feel may be relevant to your application.

### Submit
* **Submit** (Text): Once you have completed all the fields, click this button to submit your application.
