# course_session_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "course_session_report_form" is used to collect information about course sessions from students. This form is designed to gather details on student participation, course performance, and session specifics to provide valuable insights for course administrators and instructors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the required and optional fields.
2. Enter or select relevant information in each field, making sure to fill out all required fields if applicable.
3. For fields with options, select the correct response from the available choices.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`introduction_page`, text, optional): Provide a brief overview of the course session, explaining its purpose and objectives.
* **Student Information** (`student_info`, number, optional): Enter relevant student information, such as student ID or enrollment date.
* **Course Details** (`course_details`, select_one, optional): Indicate if the student has completed or not completed the course session.
* **Session Information** (`session_info`, select_multiple, optional): Select the relevant session information, such as session status or attendance.
* **Performance Data** (`performance_data`, number, optional): Enter any relevant performance data, such as grade or score.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional notes or comments about the student's participation.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the student's email address if applicable.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the student's phone number if applicable.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the course session.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the course session.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, select_one, optional): Confirm that you have reviewed and submitted the form accurately.

## Tips
* Be accurate and thorough in your responses to ensure the form's purpose is met.
* If unsure about any field, refer to the course session documentation or consult with the course administrator or instructor.
* Double-check your form before submitting it to avoid errors or omissions.
