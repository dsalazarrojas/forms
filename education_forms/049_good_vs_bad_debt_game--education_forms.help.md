# Good Vs Bad Debt Game - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Good Vs Bad Debt Game form is designed to help users evaluate and identify their debt, whether it's good or bad, by guiding them through a series of questions and prompts.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please complete each page of the form in order.
- Start with the Introduction page to set the context for the game.
- On the Scenario page, describe a situation related to debt, e.g., credit card debt or a mortgage.
- On the Reflection page, reflect on your feelings and thoughts about the scenario you described.
- On the Confidence page, rate your confidence levels on a scale.
- On the Review pages, assess your debt as Good or Bad and select multiple aspects that contribute to your evaluation.
