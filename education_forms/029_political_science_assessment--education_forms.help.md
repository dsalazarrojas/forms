# Political Science Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your knowledge and understanding of key concepts in Political Science. It is a self-administered evaluation tool for personal or academic purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question on the four pages to the best of your ability.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by a * below).
3. Use the available space to provide as much detail as possible in each field.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Political Science Assessment 1: What is Politics
* **What is Politics** (`What is Politics`, text, required/optional): *A brief definition of politics, its nature, and its significance.*


### Political Science Assessment 2: Political System
* **Political System** (`Political System`, text, required/optional): *An explanation of how politics is structured and governed within a society or state.*


### Political Science Assessment 3: Political Ideology
* **Political Ideology** (`Political Ideology`, text, required/optional): *A description of the dominant ideology that shapes the political landscape, including its underlying principles and values.*


### Political Science Assessment 4: Political Participation
* **Political Participation** (`Political Participation`, text, required/optional): *An account of how individuals participate in politics, including voting, activism, and other forms of engagement.*


## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when answering each question.
* Use clear and concise language to provide your response.
* Make sure to answer all required fields (indicated by *).
* If you are unsure about any question, refer to the provided explanations above or consult with a relevant expert.
