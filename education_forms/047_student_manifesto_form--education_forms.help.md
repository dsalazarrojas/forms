# Student Manifesto Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow students to express themselves and showcase their interests, goals, and academic history in a comprehensive way.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the fields with accurate and honest information about yourself. You can add as much or as little as you feel comfortable sharing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information (Page 1)** (`general_info`, text, optional): This field is a brief description of yourself, your interests, and your goals.
* **Contact Details (Page 1)** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Please provide your contact information so that others can reach out to you or collaborate with you.
* **Personal Goals (Page 1)** (`personal_goals`, text, optional): Share your short-term and long-term goals, including any specific skills you want to develop or achievements you want to attain.
* **Academic History (Page 1)** (`academic_history`, text, optional): Write a brief account of your past academic experiences, including any notable achievements or challenges you've faced.
* **Manifesto (Page 1)** (`manifesto`, text, optional): Craft a short manifesto that expresses your thoughts, passions, and values - your personal vision and mission for the future.
* **Signature (Page 1)** (`signature`, text, optional): Sign your name and add a personal touch to your manifesto.
* **Contact Information (Page 2)** (`contact_details_1`, text, optional): This field is for additional contact information to help others connect with you.
* **Contact Details (Page 2)** (`contact_details_2`, text, optional): Provide any extra contact details that you feel would be helpful for others to know.

## Tips
* Be honest and authentic in your responses.
* Don't worry too much about grammar or spelling - just focus on being clear and concise.
* You can add images or links to support your answers, if you feel it's necessary.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for help or clarification.
* Remember, this form is a personal and public expression of yourself, so be proud of who you are and what you have to offer!
