# University Senate Petition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The University Senate Petition Form is a tool used to submit formal petitions to the university senate for consideration. The form is designed to gather information from individuals who wish to propose new ideas, policies, or initiatives to be presented to the senate for discussion and potential implementation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2.  Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3.  Enter your contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.
4.  Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
5.  Provide a message describing your petition in the "Message" field.
6.  Select the department that is most relevant to your petition from the "Department" dropdown menu.
7.  If you are a student, enter your student ID number in the "Student ID" field (optional).
8.  If you want to track the status of your petition, enter the date you wish to submit it in the "Date" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
*   **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, optional): If you are a student, enter your university-issued student ID number.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address that the university staff can use to contact you for further information.
*   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where the university staff can contact you for further information.
*   **Message** (`message`, note, required): Provide a clear and concise description of your petition. This field should clearly explain the reason, benefits, and proposed implementation details.
*   **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Select the department that is most related to your petition. This will help the senate understand the context and relevance of your proposal.
*   **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you wish to submit your petition for consideration. This will help the senate track the status of your petition.

## Tips

*   Make sure to provide enough information in the "Message" field to clearly explain your petition.
*   Select the "Department" that is most relevant to your proposal to ensure that your petition reaches the right people.
*   Use the "Date" field to track the status of your petition and ensure that it is reviewed in a timely manner.
