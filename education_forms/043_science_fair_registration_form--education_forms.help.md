# Science Fair Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Science Fair Registration Form is used to collect information from students participating in the science fair. This includes personal details and project specifics to help the organizers review and prepare for the event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **First Name**: Enter your first name in this field. This is the name that will be displayed on the science fair program and other materials.
2. **Last Name**: Enter your last name in this field. This is also the name that will be displayed on the science fair program and other materials.
3. **Email**: Enter your email address in this field. This will be used to contact you with updates and notifications about the science fair.
4. **Project Title**: Enter a brief title of your science project. This should describe the main theme or topic of your project.
5. **Category**: Select the category that best fits your project from the options provided (Science Projects, Art and Design, Robotics, or Other).
6. **School Name**: Enter the name of your school.
7. **Grade Level**: Select the grade level you are currently in from the options provided (9-12, 6-8, or K-5).
8. **Project Description**: Briefly describe your science project in this field. This should give a clear idea of what your project is about.
9. **Teacher Name**: Enter the name of your teacher who supervised your project.
10. **Contact Phone**: Enter a phone number where you can be reached during the science fair.
11. **Parent Email**: Enter the email address of a parent or guardian who can be contacted during the science fair.
12. **Student Address**: Enter your address to help with any necessary contact or delivery during the science fair.
13. **Project Type**: Select the type of project you are submitting (Science Experiment, Science Experiment - Other, Science Experiment - Robotics, or Science Experiment - Engineering).
14. **Submission Date**: Enter the date you are planning to submit your project for review (this should be a date in the format MM/dd/yyyy).
15. **Project Status**: Select the current status of your project from the options provided (Pending Review, Accepted, or Rejected).
16. **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes from the science fair team in this field.
