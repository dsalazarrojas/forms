# Bible College Camp Internship Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the performance and progress of an intern who participated in a Bible College Camp internship. It provides an objective assessment of the intern's work quality, reliability, initiative, teamwork, communication skills, spiritual growth, and problem-solving abilities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the required fields with the necessary information:
	* **Intern Name**: Enter the full name of the intern.
	* **Email Address**: Enter the intern's email address.
	* **Student ID or Matriculation Number**: Enter the intern's student ID or matriculation number (if applicable).
	* **College or University**: Enter the name of the college or university the intern is associated with.
	* **Internship Position or Role**: Enter the position or role the intern held during the internship.
	* **Camp or Program Name**: Enter the name of the Bible camp or program the intern participated in.
	* **Internship Duration**: Select the duration of the internship from the options provided.
	* **Internship Start Date**: Enter the start date of the internship.
	* **Internship End Date**: Enter the end date of the internship.
	* **Supervisor or Mentor Name**: Enter the name of the person overseeing the intern.
2. Evaluate the intern's performance based on the criteria:
	* **Quality of Work**: Select how well the intern performed their duties.
	* **Reliability and Dependability**: Select how consistent and reliable the intern was.
	* **Initiative and Self-Direction**: Select how well the intern took action without prompting.
	* **Teamwork and Collaboration**: Select how well the intern worked with others.
	* **Communication Skills**: Select how effectively the intern communicated.
	* **Spiritual Growth and Development**: Select the level of personal spiritual progress during the internship.
	* **Problem Solving Ability**: Select how well the intern handled challenges.
3. Identify the intern's strengths and areas for growth:
	* **Key Strengths Demonstrated**: Describe the intern's key strengths.
	* **Areas for Growth and Development**: Describe areas where the intern needs improvement.
	* **Unique Contributions Made**: Describe any unique contributions the intern made to the program.
4. Summarize the overall performance and provide a rehire recommendation:
	* **Overall Performance**: Select a summary of the intern's overall performance.
	* **Would You Rehire or Re-Involve This Intern?**: Select whether or not to rehire the intern.

## Tips

* Be specific and objective when evaluating the intern's performance.
* Provide examples or anecdotes to support your evaluation.
* Keep the evaluation concise and focused on the main points.
* Use the space provided for each field to elaborate on your assessment.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed.
