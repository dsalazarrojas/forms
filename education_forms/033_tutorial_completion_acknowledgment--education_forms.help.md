# Tutorial Completion Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tutorial Completion Acknowledgment form is a way for individuals to acknowledge completing a tutorial, providing their name, contact information, and feedback to help us improve our tutorials.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Fill in your Last Name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Provide your phone number.
5. Select the date you completed the tutorial from the calendar.
6. Select the category you fall under from the dropdown menu.
7. Optionally, provide any feedback about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., `example@domain.com`).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Completed on** (`date_completed`, date, required): Select the date you completed the tutorial from the calendar.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required): Choose the category you fall under from the dropdown menu.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, optional): Provide any comments or suggestions about your experience with the tutorial.
