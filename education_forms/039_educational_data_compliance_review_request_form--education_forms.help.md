**Educational Data Compliance Review Request Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose

The **Educational Data Compliance Review Request Form** is designed to collect information and feedback on educational projects to ensure they meet certain standards and guidelines. This form is used to document compliance reviews, which helps maintain quality control and consistency across all educational projects. Please note that this form does not collect any sensitive or sensitive personal data. All information collected will be used to review and update relevant project documentation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in all applicable fields with relevant information.
2. Select options that best describe your project lead, compliance review request, review date, review by, and status.
3. Enter any additional comments or notes if needed.
4. Review and confirm your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Education Project Details** (`education_project_details`): A short description of the educational project you are submitting for review.
* **Project Lead** (`project_lead`): Select the person in charge of the project. This will help us associate the project with the relevant contact person.
* **Prophets** (`education_data`): Choose all applicable prophets that are relevant to your project.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`): Date when the project was reviewed. This is when compliance reviewers assessed your project.
* **Compliance Review Request** (`educational_data_compliance_review_request`): This field is automatically filled in based on your project lead selection.
* **Review By** (`review_by`): Select the person or entity that reviewed your project.
* **Status** (`status`): Current status of the project, e.g., "Approved", "Not Approved", "Pending Review".
* **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional comments or notes about your project.
* **Review Comments** (`review_comments`): Add any comments or feedback provided by the reviewer.
* **Internal Review Prophets** (`internal_review_prophets`): Choose all internal reviewers (employees) involved in the review process.
* **External Review Prophets** (`external_review_prophets`): Select all external reviewers (external entities) involved in the review process.

## Tips

* Ensure all fields are filled in as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* If unsure about any field, consult your project lead or relevant documentation.
* This form is designed to be user-friendly and easy to fill out. If you have any issues or problems, contact our support team for assistance.
