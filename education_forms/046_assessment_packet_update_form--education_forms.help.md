# Assessment Packet Update Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to update assessment packets in a structured and systematic way, ensuring accurate record-keeping and version control.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name as the submittter.
2. Select the department or subject area related to the assessment packet.
3. Provide the Assessment Packet ID or Title.
4. Choose the primary nature of the change (Type of Update Required).
5. Describe the detailed changes made to the assessment packet.
6. Explain the rationale for the update.
7. If necessary, attach or paste new content related to the update.
8. Specify the date of the update request.
9. Select the implementation priority level of the update.
10. If necessary, enter the name of the reviewer assigned to review the update.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submitter Full Name (1)**: Enter your full name as the submittter.
* **Department or Subject Area (2)**: Select the department or subject area related to the assessment packet.
* **Assessment Packet ID or Title (3)**: Provide the Assessment Packet ID or Title.
* **Type of Update Required (4)**: Choose the primary nature of the change (e.g., Content Correction, New Question Addition, etc.).
* **Detailed Description of Changes (5)**: Specify the exact sections or questions affected by the update.
* **Rationale for Update (6)**: Explain the reason for the update.
* **New Content Placeholder (7)**: If necessary, attach or paste new content related to the update.
* **Date of Update Request (8)**: Specify the date of the update request.
* **Implementation Priority Level (9)**: Select the implementation priority level of the update (Low, Medium, High).
* **Assigned Reviewer Name (10)**: Enter the name of the reviewer assigned to review the update.

Note: The form includes a date picker for the Date of Update Request field, making it easy to enter the date accurately.
