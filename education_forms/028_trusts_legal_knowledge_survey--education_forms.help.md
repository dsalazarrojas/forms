# Trusts Legal Knowledge Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your knowledge and experience in the trust sector. Your responses will help us understand your level of understanding, involvement, and preferences, which will be used for educational or research purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Provide Your Basic Contact Information

* **User Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your business card or identification.
* **Title**: Enter your title or professional designation.

### Step 2: Answer Questions About Your Trust Experience

* **What is your role within the trust sector?**: Please select one of the following options: Trustee, Beneficiary, Advisor, or Other.
* **Which of the following best describes your level of understanding of trusts?**: Select all that apply: Basic, Intermediate, Advanced.
* **How long have you been involved in the trust sector?**: Select one of the following options: 1-5 years, 5-10 years, 10-20 years, More than 20 years.
* **How often do you engage in trust-related activities?**: Select all that apply: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely.
* **How do you currently use the information from the trust sector?**: Select all that apply: Directly, Indirectly, Rarely.
* **Have you ever had any formal training related to trusts?**: Select True or False.
* **What motivated you to take this survey?**: Please enter a brief description of your reason for taking this survey.
* **Additional comments or feedback**: If you have any additional thoughts or suggestions, please enter them here.

### Step 3: Provide Your Contact Information and Preferences

* **Contact Information**: Enter your preferred email address or phone number.
* **How do you prefer to contact me?**: Select one of the following options: Email, Phone, Both Email and Phone.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **How do you currently use the information from the trust sector?**: Enter a brief description of how you use the information from the trust sector.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions to the best of your ability.
* If you need help or clarification on any question, feel free to ask.
* Your responses will be kept confidential and used for educational or research purposes only.
