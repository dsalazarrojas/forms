# College Interview Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about you for the college interview process. It will help us understand your preferences and requirements for the interview, ensuring a smooth and productive experience for you and our admissions team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details such as your first and last name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide your Application ID Number for reference.
3. Select your preferred interview type (in-person, virtual, or phone interview).
4. Choose your preferred interview format (one-on-one or group interview).
5. Select your preferred interview dates and times.
6. Indicate your time zone.
7. Choose your preferred language for the interview (English, Spanish, Mandarin, or French).
8. If necessary, indicate if you need an interpreter and specify the language required.
9. Confirm any accessibility or accommodation needs.
10. Describe your accommodation needs in detail.
11. Indicate if you're interested in a campus tour before or after the interview.
12. Provide any questions or concerns you'd like to discuss during the interview.
13. If having an in-person interview, specify the location preference.
14. Finally, confirm that the information provided is accurate and agree to the interview terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1) (`text`, Required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (2) (`text`, Required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (3) (`email`, Required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (4) (`text`, Required): Enter your phone number.
* **Application ID Number** (5) (`text`, Required): Enter your application reference number.
* **Intended Major or Program** (6) (`text`, Required): Indicate the major or program you're interested in.
* **Interview Type Preference** (7) (`select_one`, Required): Choose between In-Person Campus Visit, Virtual Video Interview, Phone Interview, or No Preference.
* **Interview Format Preference** (8) (`select_one`, Required): Select One-on-One or Group Interview or No Preference.
* **First Preferred Interview Date** (9) (`date`, Required): Enter your first preferred interview date.
* **First Preferred Time** (10) (`time`, Required): Enter your first preferred interview time.
* **Second Preferred Interview Date** (11) (`date`, Optional): Enter your second preferred interview date (optional).
* **Second Preferred Time** (12) (`time`, Optional): Enter your second preferred interview time (optional).
* **Third Preferred Interview Date** (13) (`date`, Optional): Enter your third preferred interview date (optional).
* **Third Preferred Time** (14) (`time`, Optional): Enter your third preferred interview time (optional).
* **Your Time Zone** (15) (`text`, Required): Enter your time zone.
* **Preferred Interview Language** (16) (`select_one`, Required): Choose between English, Spanish, Mandarin, or French.
* **Do You Need an Interpreter** (17) (`select_one`, Required): Select Yes or No if you need an interpreter.
* **Interpreter Language Needed** (18) (`text`, Optional): If Yes, specify the language required.
* **Accessibility or Accommodation Needs** (19) (`select_one`, Required): Select Yes or No if you need any accessibility or accommodation.
* **Accommodation Details** (20) (`text`, Optional): Describe your accommodation needs in detail.
* **Interested in Campus Tour** (21) (`select_one`, Required): Select Yes, No, or Maybe if you're interested in a campus tour.
* **Questions or Concerns** (22) (`text`, Optional): Provide any questions or concerns you'd like to discuss during the interview.
* **Interview Location** (23) (`text`, Optional): If having an in-person interview, specify the location preference.
* **I confirm accuracy of information and agree to interview terms** (24) (`select_one`, Required): Confirm that the information provided is accurate and agree to the interview terms.
