# Academic Partnership Success Factors Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Partnership Success Factors Survey is designed to gather information about your institution's partnerships, focusing on key success factors, challenges, and potential areas for improvement. This survey aims to understand the dynamics of your partnerships and identify best practices for future collaborations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Respondent Information: This section is a summary of the form's purpose and instructions.
2. Organization type: Select your institution's type (university, industry partner, non-profit, government, or other).
3. Partnership duration: Describe how long your partnership has existed.
4. Key success factors: Select up to three factors that contribute to the success of your partnership.
5. Main challenges: Describe any persistent obstacles that have hindered your partnership.
6. Impact measurement: Explain how you measure the impact of your partnership.
7. Improvement recommendations: Provide practical steps that can be taken to improve your partnership.
8. Willingness to collaborate: Indicate your interest in engaging in future collaborations.
9. Contact email (optional): Provide an email address for follow-up discussions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Respondent Information** (Respondent Info, Note, Optional)

This section is a summary of the form's purpose and instructions. Please read this section carefully before proceeding.

### 2. **Organization type** (Select One, Required)

Select your institution's type from the provided options. This will help us understand your organization's context and tailor the survey questions accordingly.

### 3. **Partnership duration** (Text, Optional)

Describe the length of time your partnership has existed. This will help us understand the dynamics of your partnership over time.

### 4. **Key success factors** (Select Multiple, Required)

Select up to three factors that contribute to the success of your partnership. Examples of success factors include:

* Clear objectives
* Mutual benefits
* Dedicated resources
* Strong communication
* Leadership support

### 5. **Main challenges** (Text, Optional)

Describe any persistent obstacles that have hindered your partnership. Be as specific as possible, so we can better understand the challenges you're facing.

### 6. **Impact measurement** (Text, Optional)

Explain how you measure the impact of your partnership. This could include metrics, evaluation approaches, or methods used to assess progress.

### 7. **Improvement recommendations** (Text, Optional)

Provide practical steps that can be taken to improve your partnership. This will help us identify areas for improvement and suggest potential solutions.

### 8. **Will you consider future collaborations?** (Select One, Required)

Indicate your interest in engaging in future collaborations. Please select one of the provided options:

* True
* Maybe
* False

### 9. **Contact email (optional)** (Email, Optional)

Provide an email address if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up discussions or have questions about the survey.
