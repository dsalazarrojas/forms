# University Advisor Meeting Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record meeting details between university advisors and students, ensuring efficient communication and collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form:

1. Enter the student's name in the **Student Name** field.
2. If applicable, enter the student's ID number in the **Student ID** field.
3. Select the type of meeting from the **Meeting Type** dropdown menu. If it's not a standard academic advising meeting, select **Other**.
4. Enter the date of the meeting in the **Meeting Date** field.
5. Enter the time of the meeting in the **Meeting Time** field.
6. Enter the advisor's name in the **Advisor Name** field.
7. Enter any relevant student contact information in the **Student Contact Info** field.
8. Add any notes from the meeting in the **Notes** field.
9. Enter the follow-up action planned for the student in the **Follow-up Action** field.
10. Select the status of the follow-up action from the **Follow-up Status** dropdown menu.
11. Enter the next step in the **Next Step** field.
12. Enter the length of the meeting in the **Meeting Length** field (not used in this version, remove it?).
13. Enter the next follow-up step in the **Follow-up Next Step** field.
14. Enter the advisor's email in the **Advisor Email** field.
15. Enter the student's email in the **Student Email** fields.
16. Enter any relevant phone numbers in the **Advisor Phone** field and the **Student Phone** fields.
17. Add any notes for the advisor in the **Advisor Notes** field and the student's notes in the **Student Notes** fields.
18. Enter the next follow-up step date and time in the **Follow-up Next Step Date** and **Follow-up Next Step Time** fields, if applicable.
19. Assign the form to someone by entering their name in the **Assigned To** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (text, optional): Enter the student's name.
* **Student ID** (number, optional): Enter the student's ID number, if applicable.
* **Meeting Type** (select_one, optional): Select the type of meeting from the options provided. If it's not a standard academic advising meeting, select **Other**.
* **Meeting Date** (date, optional): Enter the date of the meeting.
* **Meeting Time** (time, optional): Enter the time of the meeting.
* **Advisor Name** (text, optional): Enter the advisor's name.
* **Student Contact Info** (text, optional): Enter any relevant student contact information.
* **Notes** (note, optional): Add any notes from the meeting.
* **Follow-up Action** (text, optional): Enter the follow-up action planned for the student.
* **Follow-up Status** (select_one, optional): Select the status of the follow-up action from the options provided.
* **Next Step** (text, optional): Enter the next step in the process.
* **Meeting Length** (text, optional): Enter the length of the meeting (not used in this version, remove it?).
* **Follow-up Next Step** (text, optional): Enter the next follow-up step.
* **Advisor Email** (email, optional): Enter the advisor's email address.
* **Student Email** (email, optional): Enter the student's email address.
* **Advisor Phone** (text, optional): Enter the advisor's phone number.
* **Student Phone** (text, optional): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Student Notes** (note, optional): Add any notes for the student.
* **Advisor Notes** (note, optional): Add any notes for the advisor.
* **Follow-up Next Step Date** (date, optional): Enter the next follow-up step date.
* **Follow-up Next Step Time** (time, optional): Enter the next follow-up step time.
* **Assigned To** (text, optional): Enter the name of the person assigned to follow up on this meeting.
