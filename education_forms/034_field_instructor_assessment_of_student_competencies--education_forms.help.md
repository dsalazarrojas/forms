# Field Instructor Assessment Of Student Competencies - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to help instructors assess the competencies of their students in a field setting. It allows instructors to evaluate the student's performance and provide feedback on their strengths and areas for improvement. The form is intended to be completed by instructors who have had the opportunity to work with students in a field environment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2.  Select the grade level of the student (if applicable) from the "Grade" dropdown menu.
3.  Enter your name in the "instructor_name" field.
4.  Assess the student's competencies in the three fields under "social_work_competency_1", "social_work_competency_2", and "social_work_competency_3". Select all the competencies that apply to the student's performance.
5.  Optionally, provide any additional comments about the student's performance in the "Comments" field.
6.  Finally, sign your name in the "instructor_signature" field to confirm your assessment.

* **Student Name**: Enter the student's name as it appears in their identification documents or academic records.

## Tips

*   Make sure to review the student's performance carefully before completing this form.
*   Select all the competencies that apply to the student's performance in each field.
*   Provide clear and specific feedback in the "Comments" field, if applicable.
*   Sign and date your assessment in the "instructor_signature" field, if applicable.
