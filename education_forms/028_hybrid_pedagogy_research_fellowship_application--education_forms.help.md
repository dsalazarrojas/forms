# Hybrid Pedagogy Research Fellowship Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hybrid Pedagogy Research Fellowship Application form is designed to collect information from applicants for the Hybrid Pedagogy Research Fellowship program. This form will guide you through the required and optional fields to provide a complete application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "proposal" field with a brief statement describing your background, interests, and how you plan to contribute to the research fellowship.
2. Upload a copy of your current CV (curriculum vitae) in the "cv" field.
3. Provide a clear indication of whether you have references (yes or no) in the "references" field.
4. If applicable, list your current academic affiliation in the "academic-affiliation" field.
5. Choose one relevant "research-focus" option that best describes your area of focus.
6. Select multiple "research-interests" options that best describe your areas of interest.
7. Upload a file containing your CV if you have one to be uploaded in the "cv-uploaded-file" field.
8. If you have references, upload their CVs in the "references_uploaded_files" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **proposal** (`proposal`, text, required): A brief statement describing your background, interests, and how you plan to contribute to the research fellowship.
* **cv** (`cv`, text, required): A copy of your current CV, showcasing your education and professional experience.
* **references** (`references`, select_multiple, optional): Provide a clear indication of whether you have references (yes or no).
* **academic-affiliation** (`academic-affiliation`, text, optional): Your current academic affiliation, if applicable.
* **research-focus** (`research-focus`, select_one, optional): Select one relevant "research-focus" option that best describes your area of focus.
* **research-interests** (`research-interests`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple "research-interests" options that best describe your areas of interest.
* **cv-uploaded-file** (`cv-uploaded-file`, text, required): Upload a file containing your CV.
* **references_uploaded_files** (`references_uploaded_files`, text, optional): Upload a file containing your references' CVs.
## Tips
* Please ensure that your proposal is clear and concise.
* Make sure to proofread your CV and uploaded CV files for any errors.
* If you have any issues uploading files, contact our support team.
