# School Counseling Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect detailed information about you and your needs as a student. This information will help us provide you with the best possible support during your time in school.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out each section carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Answer each question honestly and accurately.
3. If you're unsure about any field, don't hesitate to reach out to the school's administration for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **student_background** (required): This section is for your personal background, including any relevant family, social, or educational history.
* **family_info** (required): This section is for information about your family, including any relevant details about your parents or caregivers.
* **medical_info** (required): This section is for any health-related information that might impact your school experience.
* **academic_info** (required): This section is for any details about your academic history or goals.
* **behavioral_info** (required): This section is for any relevant information about your behavior, including any mental health or counseling history.
* **emotional_info** (required): This section is for any concerns or feelings you might have about your emotional well-being.
* **social_info** (required): This section is for any relevant social information, including any friendships or social relationships.
* **student_id** (required): This section is for a unique identifier assigned to you by the school.
* **student_preferences** (required): Please select one of the following languages as your preference:
	+ English
	+ Spanish
	+ French
	+ Other (please specify)
* **goals** (required): This section is for any goals or objectives you might have for your school experience.
* **needs** (required): This section is for any specific needs or concerns you might have.
* **contact_info** (required): This section is for any contact information you'd like to share with us, such as phone numbers or email addresses.

Please note that all fields marked as "required" are necessary for us to understand your needs and provide you with the best possible support. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to the school's administration.
