# ET Parent Teacher Meeting Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate booking and scheduling meetings between teachers and parents for their children. It gathers essential details such as meeting date, time, and room, as well as student and parent information for the meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the teacher you wish to meet with from the "Teacher" dropdown list.
2. Choose the date and time for the meeting using the provided date and time input fields.
3. Select the duration of the meeting from the available options.
4. Choose the room where the meeting will take place from the provided list.
5. Enter the student's email address.
6. Enter the parent's email address (optional but recommended for follow-up).
7. Add a message or note for the teacher's reference.
8. Add any additional comments or notes for the student's reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Booking Details
* **Booking Details**: This section is where you provide the date and time you wish to book the meeting.
### Teacher
* **Teacher**: Select the teacher you wish to meet with from the dropdown list.
### Date
* **Date**: Choose the date for the meeting using the provided date input field.
### Time
* **Time**: Choose the time for the meeting using the provided time input field.
### Duration
* **Duration**: Select the duration of the meeting from the available options.
### Room
* **Room**: Choose the room where the meeting will take place from the provided list.
### Student
* **Student**: Enter the student's email address.
### Parent
* **Parent**: Enter the parent's email address (optional but recommended for follow-up).
### Teacher's Email
* **Teacher's Email**: This field is not currently used in the form.
### Student's Email
* **Student's Email**: This field is not currently used in the form.
### Message
* **Message**: Add a message or note for the teacher's reference.
### Teacher's Note
* **Teacher's Note**: Add any additional comments or notes for the teacher's reference.
### Student's Note
* **Student's Note**: Add any additional comments or notes for the student's reference.
### Teacher's Comment
* **Teacher's Comment**: This field is not currently used in the form.

## Tips
* Please make sure to select the correct teacher and date/time for the meeting.
* Enter the student's and parent's email addresses correctly to ensure follow-up communication.
* Use the "Message" field to provide any important information for the teacher's reference.
* Use the "Teacher's Note" and "Student's Note" fields for any additional comments or notes.
