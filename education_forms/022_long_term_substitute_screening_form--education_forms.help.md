# Long Term Substitute Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Long Term Substitute Screening Form is a crucial tool used by our district to gather information from substitute teachers for long-term substitute positions. This form helps us understand your availability, education level, role, and other essential details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and fill in the required information.
2. Ensure that all the fields are filled with accurate and relevant details.
3. If you select "Other" in any of the fields, provide a clear explanation in the designated text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Page
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for future communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number for communication and verification.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Substitute Date** (`substitute_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are available to start substitute work.
* **School District** (`school_district`, text, optional): Enter the school district you are interested in working with.
* **Teacher Supervisor** (`teacher_supervisor`, text, optional): Enter the name of your teacher supervisor.
* **Principal** (`principal`, text, optional): Enter the name of your principal.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the school you are interested in working with.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the school you are interested in working with.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the school you are interested in working with.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the school you are interested in working with.
* **Role Description** (`role_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your role in the school.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, select_one, optional): Select the highest level of education you have attained. Options: High School, Middle School, College/University, Other, None.
* **Educational Level Other (Specify)** (`education_level_other`, text, optional): If you selected "Other" in the previous field, provide a brief explanation.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, optional): Select the type of substitute role you are looking for. Options: Long-term Substitute, Short-term Substitute, Emergency Substitute, None.
* **Role Other (Specify)** (`role_other`, text, optional): If you selected "Other" in the previous field, provide a brief explanation.
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_multiple, optional): Select the days you are available to work as a substitute. Options: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday, All, None.
* **Availability Other (Specify)** (`availability_other`, text, optional): If you selected "Other" in the previous field, provide a brief explanation.
* **Work Schedule** (`work_schedule`, time, optional): Enter your regular work schedule (start time and end time).
* **Review Process** (`review_process`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback for the review process.

Note: Fields marked as "optional" are not required but can be completed if you have the necessary information.
