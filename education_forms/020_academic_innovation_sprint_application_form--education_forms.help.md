# Academic Innovation Sprint Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from participants about their interests, skills, and availability for the Academic Innovation Sprint. This will help the organizers to select the right people for the project and ensure a productive and enjoyable experience for everyone involved.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out the participant information section, providing your full name and email address.
- In the project idea section, briefly describe your proposed project idea in one paragraph.
- If you have any specific skills or roles that you can bring to the project, select them from the provided options.
- Indicate your availability during the sprint, including any specific dates or times you are available.
- If you have any accessibility needs, please specify them in the text box.
- If you have any prior related experience, briefly describe it.
- Finally, if you have any additional comments or information that you want to share with the organizers, use the additional comments section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`name`, `text`, required): Please fill out your full name as it will be used for event logistics.
* **Email address** (`email`, `email`, required): Provide a contact email address for event organizers to reach you.
* **Institution or organization** (`affiliation`, `text`, optional): If you are affiliated with an institution or organization, please provide it. This will help the organizers to understand your background.
* **Brief project idea** (`project_idea`, `text`, required): Write a one-paragraph description of your proposed project idea.
* **Skills and roles you bring** (`skills_and_roles`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the skills and roles you have experience with. The options are: 
    + Data analysis
    + Visualization
    + UX design
    + Project management
    + Teaching and facilitation
* **Availability during sprint** (`availability`, `text`, required): Indicate when you are available during the sprint, including specific dates or times.
* **Accessibility needs** (`accessibility_needs`, `text`, optional): If you have any special accommodations or requirements, please specify them.
* **Prior related experience** (`prior_experience`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe any relevant experience you have.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Share any additional information you want to with the organizers.
