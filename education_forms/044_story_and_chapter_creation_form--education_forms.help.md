# Story And Chapter Creation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about stories and chapters for publishing or educational purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out all the required fields**: The fields with a asterik (*) symbol are required.
2. **Provide accurate and relevant information**: Please ensure that the data you provide is accurate and relevant to your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Story Details
* **Story Details**: This field is where you can enter a brief description of your story. 

### Chapter Information
* **Chapter Information**: This field is for providing details about the chapter, such as title, number, or any other relevant information. 

### Chapter Content
* **Chapter Content**: This field is for writing the actual content of the chapter.

### Author Details
* **Author Details**: Here, you can enter your name, credentials, and any other relevant information about yourself as the author.

### Submission Details
* **Submission Details**: This field is not clearly defined in the provided YAML. However, based on the label, it might be for providing additional context about the submission, such as the story's genre or category.

### Submission Date
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date you are submitting the story and chapter.

### Submission Time
* **Submission Time**: Enter the time you are submitting the story and chapter.

### Submission Email
* **Submission Email**: Provide your email address for contact purposes.

### Submission Phone
* **Submission Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

Note that some fields may not be required, so you can skip them if not applicable.
