# Algebra And Statistics Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Algebra And Statistics Quiz" form is a comprehensive assessment tool designed to evaluate students' algebra and statistics knowledge. It covers a wide range of topics, from basic algebraic expressions to statistical concepts such as probability, correlation, and standard deviation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with the provided information, starting from "Student Name", "Student ID", "Email Address", "Course Name", and "Quiz Date".
2. Answer each question according to the specific format and requirements outlined in each question. For questions with numerical answers, use decimal or whole numbers as required.
3. Select the correct option for questions with select-one options.
4. Provide any additional comments or feedback if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, required): Enter your student ID number.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the math course you are enrolled in.
* **Quiz Date** (`quiz_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the quiz.
* **Question 1: Solve for x: 3x + 7 = 22** (`question_1`, number, required): Enter your answer to this algebra question.
* **Question 2: What is the mean of: 5, 8, 12, 15, 20?** (`question_2`, number, required): Enter your answer to this statistics question.
* **Question 3: Simplify: 2(x + 4) - 3(x - 1)** (`question_3`, text, required): Enter the simplified algebraic expression.
* **Question 4: What is the median of: 3, 7, 9, 12, 15, 18, 21?** (`question_4`, number, required): Enter your answer to this statistics question.
* **Question 5: Factor: x^2 - 9** (`question_5`, text, required): Enter the factored form of this algebraic expression.
* **Question 6: What is the mode of: 2, 3, 3, 5, 7, 7, 9?** (`question_6`, number, required): Enter the mode of this set of numbers.
* **Question 7: Solve: 2x - 5 = x + 10** (`question_7`, number, required): Enter the value of x.
* **Question 8: Calculate the range of: 4, 9, 15, 22, 28** (`question_8`, number, required): Enter the range of this set of numbers.
* **Question 9: Expand: (x + 3)(x - 5)** (`question_9`, text, required): Enter the expanded algebraic expression.
* **Question 10: What is the probability of rolling a 4 on a standard die?** (`question_10`, text, required): Enter the probability as a fraction.
* **Question 11: Solve for y: 4y + 8 = 32** (`question_11`, number, required): Enter the value of y.
* **Question 12: Find the standard deviation of: 2, 4, 4, 5, 5, 7, 9** (`question_12`, number, required): Round to 2 decimal places.
* **Question 13: Simplify: (3x^2)(4x^3)** (`question_13`, text, required): Enter the simplified algebraic expression.
* **Question 14: In a normal distribution, what percentage falls within 1 standard deviation?** (`question_14`, text, required): Enter the percentage.
* **Question 15: Factor completely: 2x^2 + 7x + 3** (`question_15`, text, required): Enter the factored form.
* **Question 16: What is the correlation coefficient range?** (`question_16`, select_one, required): Select the correct range for the correlation coefficient.
* **Question 17: Solve the system: x + y = 10, x - y = 4** (`question_17`, text, required): Enter x and y values that solve the system.
* **Question 18: What type of correlation exists when one variable increases as the other decreases?** (`question_18`, select_one, required): Select the correct type of correlation.
* **Question 19: Simplify: (x^5)/(x^2)** (`question_19`, text, required): Enter the simplified algebraic expression.
* **Question 20: If P(A) = 0.3 and P(B) = 0.5, and events are independent, what is P(A and B)?** (`question_20`, number, required): Enter the probability as a decimal.
* **Time Spent on Quiz** (`time_spent`, number, optional): Enter the time spent on the quiz.
* **Difficulty Rating** (`difficulty_rating`, select_one, optional): Select the difficulty level of this quiz.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback.

## Tips
* Double-check your answers for accuracy and clarity before submitting the form.
* If you need help with any question, don't hesitate to ask your instructor or a classmate.
* Make sure to follow the instructions and formatting requirements for each question.
