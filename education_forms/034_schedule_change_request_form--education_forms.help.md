# Schedule Change Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a change to a student's schedule. Please use this form to provide clear and concise information about the schedule change you are requesting.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure that the reason for the schedule change is clearly stated.
3. If the schedule change is due to a teacher not adjusting to the schedule change, select the corresponding option.
4. If you have any additional comments, provide them in the comments field.
5. Review your form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Teacher** (`teacher`, text, required): Enter the name of the teacher requesting the schedule change.
* **Student name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the student whose schedule is being changed.
* **Teacher name** (`teacher_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the teacher affected by the schedule change.
* **Reason for change** (`reason_for_change`, text, required): Provide a clear and concise reason for the schedule change.
* **New start date** (`new_start_date`, date, required): Enter the new start date for the schedule change.
* **New end date** (`new_end_date`, date, required): Enter the new end date for the schedule change.
* **Reason for return** (`reason_for_return`, select_one, required): Select one of the provided options for the reason for the return.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or information about the schedule change.

Note: All fields marked as "required" must be filled in completely. If an option is selected, ensure that it accurately reflects the reason for the schedule change.
