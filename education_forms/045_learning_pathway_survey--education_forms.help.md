# Learning Pathway Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your learning pathway, including the category of the program, description of the program, date and time completed, feedback, contact information, and assigned tool used. The form is intended to help us improve our learning programs and better serve our students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Choose the category of the program that you completed (Education or Other).
2. Provide a brief description of the program (required).
3. Enter the date and time you completed the program (optional).
4. Give us feedback on your experience with the program (required).
5. Enter your email address (required).
6. Enter your phone number (required).
7. If applicable, select the assigned tool used during the program (optional).
8. Add any additional notes or comments (optional).
9. Finally, fill out any other required fields as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Form Title (`form_title`, required, true)
Enter a brief title for your program, such as "Introduction to Programming."

### Category (`category`, optional, false)
Choose the category of the program that you completed. This will help us understand the type of program you participated in.

### Description (`description`, required, true)
Please provide a brief description of the program. This could include the title, objectives, and outcomes.

### Date Completed (`date_completed`, optional, false)
If you remember the date and time you completed the program, please enter it in the format MM/DD/YY.

### Time Completed (`time_completed`, optional, false)
If you remember the time you completed the program, please enter it in the format HH:MM AM/PM.

### Feedback (`feedback`, required, true)
Please provide feedback on your experience with the program. This will help us improve our programs and better serve our students.

### Email (`email`, required, true)
Enter your email address where you can be reached for further questions or follow-up.

### Phone (`phone`, required, true)
Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further questions or follow-up.

### Assigned Tool (`assigned_tool`, optional, false)
If you were assigned a tool during the program, please select it from the list.

### Notes (`notes`, optional, false)
Add any additional comments or notes about your experience.

### Form ID (`form_id`, optional, false)
This field is not intended for public use. You can ignore it.

### Output File (`output_file`, optional, false)
This field is not intended for public use. You can ignore it.

By following these steps and providing all the required fields, you will be able to effectively complete the Learning Pathway Survey form and contribute to the improvement of our learning programs.
