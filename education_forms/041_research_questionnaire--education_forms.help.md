# research_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about research questionnaires, which are tools used to collect data from a specific population. The goal is to understand the characteristics and tools used in a research study.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the questions and options.
2. Answer each question truthfully and accurately to the best of your ability.
3. Select the relevant options that apply to your research study.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, required: false): This is a brief overview of the research questionnaire.
* **Research Questionnaire Problems** (`research_questionnaire_problems`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks whether you have encountered any problems while using the research questionnaire. Answer 'Yes' if you have encountered any issues, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Research Questionnaire Objectives** (`research_questionnaire_objectives`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question asks about the objectives of your research study. Select all the objectives that apply to your study.
* **Research Questionnaire Population** (`research_questionnaire_population`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks about the population you are targeting with your research study. Answer 'Yes' if the population is your research target, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Research Questionnaire Instrument** (`research_questionnaire_instrument`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question asks about the instruments or tools used in your research study. Select all the instruments that apply to your study.

Answer 'Yes' if the population is your research target, and 'No' otherwise.
