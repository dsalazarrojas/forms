# Online Class Quick Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Class Quick Registration Form is a tool for students to register for online classes. This form aims to collect necessary information from students to facilitate their enrollment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name.
2. Enter your Email address.
3. Provide your Contact Number.
4. Select the Student Class you wish to register for.
5. Choose your preferred Class Schedule.
6. Enter your Course Code (if applicable).
7. Select your preferred Payment Method.
8. Specify your Payment Terms.
9. Choose your Payment Status.
10. Select your Class Type.
11. If necessary, enter your Other Payment Method.
12. Enter your Student ID (if required).
13. Enter the Course Name (if applicable).
14. Select your Class Level.
15. Choose your Student Type.
16. Select your Student Gender.
17. Enter your Student Age.
18. Enter your Student Country (if applicable).
19. Enter your Student Language (if applicable).
20. Choose your Student Status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your valid email address to facilitate communication.
* **Contact Number** (Text, Optional): Enter your contact number for any follow-up communication.
* **Student Class** (Text, Optional): Select the student class you wish to register for.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Optional): Enter your date of birth for age verification purposes.
* **Class Schedule** (Select One, Required): Choose your preferred class schedule.
* **Course Code** (Text, Optional): Enter your course code if required.
* **Payment Method** (Select Multiple, Required): Select your preferred payment method.
* **Payment Terms** (Text, Optional): Specify any payment terms or conditions.
* **Payment Status** (Select One, Required): Choose your payment status.
* **Payment Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the payment date if required.
* **Tutor Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of your tutor (if applicable).
* **Class Type** (Select One, Required): Choose your class type.
* **Other Payment Method** (Text, Optional): Enter any other payment method you prefer (if applicable).
* **Student ID** (Text, Optional): Enter your student ID (if required).
* **Course Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the course name (if applicable).
* **Class Level** (Select Multiple, Required): Select your class level.
* **Payment Amount** (Number, Optional): Enter the payment amount (if required).
* **Student Type** (Select One, Required): Choose your student type.
* **Student Gender** (Select One, Required): Choose your student gender.
* **Student Age** (Number, Optional): Enter your age for verification purposes.
* **Student Country** (Text, Optional): Enter your country of residence (if applicable).
* **Student Language** (Text, Optional): Enter your preferred language (if applicable).
* **Student Status** (Select One, Required): Choose your student status.
