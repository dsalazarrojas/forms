# Medical Association Membership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Association Membership Application Form is designed for medical professionals to apply for membership in the association. It collects necessary information to process and verify the application.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **First page**: This is the introductory page of the form.
2. **Medical Degree**: Provide your medical degree information.
3. **Personal Information**: Fill in your personal details.
4. **Medical Practice**: Describe your medical practice.
5. **Date of Graduation**: Enter the date you graduated from your medical degree program.
6. **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
7. **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
8. **Medical Association Membership Application**: Choose your membership type.
9. **Medical Association Membership Application Verification**: If you have existing membership with us, select "Verified".
10. **Date of Application**: Enter the date you submitted this application.
11. **Medical Association Membership Application Fee**: Enter the application fee amount.
12. **Medical Association Membership Application Status**: Describe your application status.
13. **Medical Association Membership Application Comments**: Add any comments or remarks about your application.
14. **Medical Association Membership Application Verified**: Indicate if you have verified your membership application.
15. **Medical Association Membership Application Review Date**: Enter the date your application was reviewed.
16. **Medical Association Membership Application Reviewer**: Enter the name of the reviewer who reviewed your application.
17. **Medical Association Membership Application Reviewer Verified**: Indicate if the reviewer verified your application.
18. **Medical Association Membership Application Comments Reviewer**: Add any comments or remarks from the reviewer.
19. **Medical Association Membership Application Reviewer Comments**: Add any additional comments from the reviewer.
20. **Medical Association Membership Application Administrative Fee**: Enter the administrative fee amount.
21. **Medical Association Membership Application Fee Amount**: Enter the fee amount for this application.
22. **Medical Association Membership Application Fee Paid**: Indicate if you have paid the application fee.
23. **Medical Association Membership Application Fee Paid Date**: Enter the date you paid the application fee.
24. **Medical Association Membership Application Status Updated by**: Enter the name of the person who updated your application status.
25. **Medical Association Membership Application Reviewer Review Date**: Enter the date the reviewer reviewed your application.
