# education_quality_complaint_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The education quality complaint form is designed to collect information about your experience with our educational institution. Please use this form to report any issues or concerns you have about your education. Your feedback is valuable in helping us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in your user name in the first field.
2. Select your contact method from the options in the second field. Please choose how you would like to be contacted (if at all) about your complaint.
3. Enter your email address in the third field.
4. Provide your phone number in the fourth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **user_name**: Please enter your name as you would like to be known.
* **contact_information**: This field is a multiple-choice option to indicate how you would like to be contacted about your complaint. Choose 'Yes' if you would like to be contacted via email, phone, or mail, or 'No' if you do not want to be contacted at all.
* **email_address**: Please enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **telephone_number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* If you have any supporting documents or evidence for your complaint, be sure to attach them separately.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask your administrator for guidance.
