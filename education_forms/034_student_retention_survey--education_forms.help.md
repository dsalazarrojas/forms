# Student Retention Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Student Retention Survey is designed to gather feedback from students about their interactions with their teachers. The survey will help teachers to understand how their teaching methods are being received and make adjustments for future semesters.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your student ID number in the "What is your student id" field.
2. Answer questions about why you left school and what reasons you would like your teacher to work on.
3. Rate your teacher's performance on a scale of 1-5 for each of the following categories: how well they explain things, how well they listen, how well they care about you, how often they are approachable, how often they give feedback, how well they are clear, how well they are organized, how well they are supportive, and how well they are enthusiastic.
4. Rate how often you attend school, how often you would rate your attendance, and the average number of days you have been absent in a term.
5. Rate how often you come to school on time.
6. Provide any comments about your overall experience with your teacher.
7. Suggest any areas your teacher could improve on.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your student id** (1, required): Fill in your student ID number as requested.
* **Why did you leave school** (2, optional): Choose one or more reasons why you left school.
* **Reasons for leaving school** (3, optional): Choose one or more reasons why you left school.
* **How well do you know your teacher** (4, required): Rate your teacher on a scale of 1-5 for how well you know them.
* **How well does your teacher understand you** (5, required): Rate your teacher on a scale of 1-5 for how well they understand you.
* **How well does your teacher listen to you** (6, required): Rate your teacher on a scale of 1-5 for how well they listen to you.
* **How well does your teacher care about you** (7, required): Rate your teacher on a scale of 1-5 for how well they care about you.
* **How approachable is your teacher** (8, required): Rate your teacher on a scale of 1-5 for how approachable they are.
* **How often does your teacher give feedback** (9, required): Rate your teacher on a scale of 1-5 for how often they give feedback.
* **How clear is your teacher** (10, required): Rate your teacher on a scale of 1-5 for how clear they are.
* **How well organized is your teacher** (11, required): Rate your teacher on a scale of 1-5 for how well organized they are.
* **How supportive is your teacher** (12, required): Rate your teacher on a scale of 1-5 for how supportive they are.
* **How enthusiastic is your teacher** (13, required): Rate your teacher on a scale of 1-5 for how enthusiastic they are.
* **How often do you attend school** (14, required): Rate how often you attend school.
* **How would you rate your attendance** (15, required): Rate how often you would rate your attendance.
* **Average number of days you have been absent in a term** (16, required): Rate the average number of days you have been absent in a term.
* **Average number of times you have been late in a term** (17, required): Rate the average number of times you have been late in a term.
* **How often do you come to school on time** (18, required): Rate how often you come to school on time.
* **Comments** (19, optional): Provide any comments about your overall experience with your teacher.
* **Suggestion** (20, optional): Suggest any areas your teacher could improve on.
* **Suggestion 2** (21, optional): Suggest any additional areas your teacher could improve on.
* **Suggestion 3** (22, optional): Suggest any further areas your teacher could improve on.
* **Suggestion 4** (23, optional): Suggest any additional areas your teacher could improve on.
* **Suggestion 5** (24, optional): Suggest any further areas your teacher could improve on.

## Tips

* Please be honest in your feedback, as it will help your teacher to improve their teaching methods.
* If you have any comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Comments" and "Suggestion" fields.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, you can reach out to your teacher or educational institution for assistance.
