# Educational Resilience Project Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Educational Resilience Project Application Form is used to gather information from individuals interested in applying for the project. This guide will walk you through each section of the form to ensure you complete it accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification documents.
2. **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification documents.
3. **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
4. **Birth Date**: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
5. **Birth Time**: Enter your birth time in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00 for 2:00 PM).
6. **Home Address**: Enter your current home address.
7. **Proposal**: Enter a brief proposal for why you are interested in the Educational Resilience Project.
8. **Resume**: Upload your resume or paste a link to a publicly accessible online resume.
9. **Home Phone**: Enter your current home phone number.
10. **Education**: Check if you have any formal education background. 
11. **School Name**: Enter the name of your educational institution.
12. **Graduate Year**: Enter the year you graduated from your educational institution.
13. **School Level**: Select the level of your education (High or Low).
14. **Major**: Check if you have a major in your educational background.
15. **Course**: Check if you have completed any courses.
16. **Degree**: Check if you have earned a degree.
17. **Field of Study**: Check if you have studied in a specific field.
18. **Current Status**: Select your current employment status (Active or Inactive).
19. **Current Education Level**: Select your current level of education (High or Low).
20. **Current Employer**: Enter the name of your current employer.
21. **Employer Phone**: Enter your current employer's phone number.
22. **Job Title**: Enter your current job title.
23. **Job Start Date**: Enter the date you started your current job.
24. **Job End Date**: Enter the date you ended your current job (if applicable).
25. **Employment Status**: Select your employment status (Active or Inactive).

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are filled with accurate and up-to-date information.
- Double-check your employment and education status for accuracy.
- If you need help with a specific field, contact project administrators using the provided contact information.
