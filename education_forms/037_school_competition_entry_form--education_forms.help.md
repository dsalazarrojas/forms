# school_competition_entry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from students and school representatives for a competition or event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through the pages and fill in the required information. Each page is dedicated to a specific section of the form. Please ensure that you provide accurate and complete information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. first_name
* **first_name** (Text, Optional): Enter your first name.
### 2. last_name
* **last_name** (Text, Optional): Enter your last name.
### 3. school_name
* **school_name** (Text, Optional): Enter your school name.
### 4. grade_level
* **grade_level** (Number, Optional): Enter your current grade level (e.g., 1st, 2nd, 3rd, etc.).
### 5. date_of_birth
* **date_of_birth** (Date, Optional): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
### 6. contact_number
* **contact_number** (Text, Optional): Enter a contact phone number.
### 7. email_address
* **email_address** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address.
### 8. school_address
* **school_address** (Text, Optional): Enter your school address.
### 9. event_type
* **event_type** (Select One, Optional): Choose the type of event you are participating in (Event Type A, Event Type B, Event Type C, Event Type D).
### 10. minimalist_description
* **minimalist_description** (Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of the event or competition.
### 11. competition_type
* **competition_type** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the type of competition (Competition Type A, Competition Type B, Competition Type C, Competition Type D).
### 12. event_date
* **event_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of the event in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
### 13. event_time
* **event_time** (Time, Optional): Enter the time of the event in the format 12:00 PM.
### 14. location
* **location** (Text, Optional): Enter the location of the event.
### 15. event_duration
* **event_duration** (Number, Optional): Enter the duration of the event.
### 16. start_date
* **start_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the start date of the event in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
### 17. end_date
* **end_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the end date of the event in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
### 18. event_details
* **event_details** (Text, Optional): Enter a detailed description of the event.
### 19. event_organizer
* **event_organizer** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the event organizer.
### 20. competition_organizer
* **competition_organizer** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the competition organizer.
### 21. entry_requirements
* **entry_requirements** (Text, Optional): Enter any specific entry requirements for the competition.
### 22. school_competition_committee
* **school_competition_committee** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the school's competition committee.
### 23. event_location
* **event_location** (Text, Optional): Enter the location of the event.
### 24. school
* **school** (Text, Optional): Enter your school name.
### 25. school_principal
* **school_principal** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of your school principal.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate and complete information for each field.
* If a field is marked as Optional, it is not required to be filled, but it is recommended to provide as much detail as possible.
* If you are unsure about any field or option, please ask for clarification.
* This form is designed to facilitate the competition process, so please provide all necessary information to ensure a smooth process.
