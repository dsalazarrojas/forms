# School Admission Enquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from prospective parents or guardians about their child's admission to the school. It is to be filled out when an individual is seeking admission into one of the school's programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Choose the correct options for fields where multiple options are available.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School ID** (`School ID`, `number`, required): This is the school's unique identifier.
* **Student Name** (`Student Name`, `text`, required): Enter the student's name.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, `date`, required): Enter the student's date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Contact Number** (`Contact Number`, `text`, required): Enter a valid contact number for the parent/guardian.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address for the parent/guardian.
* **Course** (`Course`, `select_one`, required): Select the course the student is interested in, from the list:
	+ 10th
	+ 11th
	+ 12th
	+ Diploma
	+ Other (please specify in the "School Name" field)
* **School Type** (`School Type`, `select_one`, not required): Select the type of school the student is interested in, from the list:
	+ Public
	+ Private
	+ Government
	+ Other
* **School Name** (`School Name`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the school the student is interested in, if not listed in the "Course" field.
* **Type of Enquiry** (`Type of Enquiry`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the types of enquiry you are interested in, from the list:
	+ General Enquiry
	+ Admission Enquiry
	+ Scholarship Enquiry
	+ Other (please specify in the "Enquiry Type Description" field)

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask the school administration for guidance.
* This form will be reviewed and responded to by the school administration within a reasonable timeframe.
