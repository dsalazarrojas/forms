# Instructional Technology Needs Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on the instructional technology needs of educators in educational institutions. By completing this form, you will help us understand your technology requirements for effective teaching and learning, ensuring that we can provide you with the necessary resources to support your instructional goals.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please enter your name and title (if applicable) in the "Instructor Name" and "Instructor Title" fields, respectively.
2. Select the department you belong to from the "Department" options.
3. Enter the school building and classroom where you teach.
4. Provide the date of your assessment.
5. Enter the time you prefer to have access to technology resources.
6. Check any technology needs you have from the "Technology Needs" options.
7. If you have any additional technology needs, please provide a brief description in the "Technology Needs Comments" field.
8. If you have any other needs that are not listed, please describe them in the "Other Needs" field.
9. Provide any additional comments about your technology needs in the "Other Needs Comments" field.
10. Once you have completed all the fields, click the "Submit" button to send your assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`title`, required): Please enter a title for your assessment, such as "Technology Needs Assessment for [Department/Building]".
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official school records.
* **Instructor Title** (`instructor_title`, optional): If you have a title or role that may influence your technology needs, please enter it.
* **Department** (`department`, required): Select the department you belong to from the options provided.
* **School Building** (`school_building`, required): Enter the school building where you teach.
* **Classroom** (`classroom`, required): Enter the classroom where you teach.
* **Date** (`date`, required): Enter the date of your assessment.
* **Time** (`time`, required): Enter the time you prefer to have access to technology resources.
* **Technology Needs** (`technology_needs`, required): Check any technology needs you have from the options provided.
* **Technology Needs Comments** (`technology_needs_comments`, optional): If you have any additional comments about your technology needs, please provide them.
* **Other Needs** (`other_needs`, optional): If you have any other needs that are not listed, please describe them.
* **Other Needs Comments** (`other_needs_comments`, optional): If you have any additional comments about your other needs, please provide them.
* **Submit** (`submit`, optional): Click this button to send your assessment.

## Tips

* Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly, as the information collected will be used to make informed decisions about instructional technology resources.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to us.
* Please note that this form is for internal use only, and the information collected will not be shared externally.
* If you need to submit the form for multiple departments or buildings, you can create a new form and repeat the process.
