# Case-Based Discussion Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Case-Based Discussion Assessment Form is designed to evaluate a trainee's performance during a case-based discussion. This form assesses the trainee's clinical skills, decision-making, and communication in a specific clinical case. The form helps assessors and trainees understand areas of strength and development, identify knowledge gaps, and create an action plan for further learning.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the case-based discussion.
2. Provide the trainee's name and select their training level.
3. Enter the date of the discussion.
4. Select the clinical setting where the case took place.
5. Rate the complexity of the case.
6. Rate the trainee's performance in each competency area.
7. Identify areas for development or further learning.
8. Create an action plan for the trainee.
9. Enter the total time spent on the discussion.
10. Provide your digital signature as the assessor.
11. Enter the trainee's digital acknowledgment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Assessment Information --** (Required: False)
  • This section provides a general introduction and context for the case-based discussion.
* **Trainee Name** (Required: True)
  • Enter the name of the trainee who participated in the case-based discussion.
* **Year of Training** (Required: True)
  • Select the year the trainee is currently in their training program.
* **Assessor Name** (Required: True)
  • Enter your name as the assessor for this case-based discussion.
* **Date of Discussion** (Required: True)
  • Enter the date of the case-based discussion.
* **Clinical Case Details --** (Required: False)
  • Provide a brief description of the case discussed.
* **Case Summary ID or Clinical Topic** (Required: True)
  • Enter the case summary ID or the clinical topic discussed.
* **Complexity of Case** (Required: True)
  • Select the level of complexity of the case based on patient factors and diagnosis.
* **Clinical Setting** (Required: True)
  • Select where the case-based discussion took place (e.g., inpatient, outpatient, emergency, surgical, critical care).
* **Competency Ratings --** (Required: False)
  • Rate the trainee's performance in each competency area (1-5).
* **Clinical Record Keeping** (Required: True)
  • Rate the trainee's clinical record keeping (1-5).
* **Diagnostic Reasoning and Clinical Logic** (Required: True)
  • Rate the trainee's diagnostic reasoning and clinical logic (1-5).
* **Selection and Planning of Management or Treatment** (Required: True)
  • Rate the trainee's selection and planning of management or treatment (1-5).
* **Professional Judgment and Decision Making** (Required: True)
  • Rate the trainee's professional judgment and decision-making (1-5).
* **Consideration of Patient Values and Holistic Care** (Required: True)
  • Rate the trainee's consideration of patient values and holistic care (1-5).
* **Feedback and Action Plan --** (Required: False)
  • Enter areas for development or further learning.
* **What were the trainee's primary strengths in this case?** (Required: True)
  • Enter the trainee's primary strengths in the case-based discussion.
* **Identified areas for development or further learning** (Required: True)
  • Enter areas the trainee needs to improve or further learn.
* **Agreed Action Plan for the trainee** (Required: False)
  • Enter the action plan for the trainee to improve or further learn.
* **Global rating of performance in this Case-Based Discussion** (Required: True)
  • Rate the trainee's overall performance in the case-based discussion (Below Expectations, At Expectations, Above Expectations).
* **Total time spent on discussion (minutes)** (Required: False)
  • Enter the total time spent on the case-based discussion.
* **Authentication --** (Required: False)
  • A section for authentication purposes.
* **Assessor Digital Signature** (Required: True)
  • Enter your digital signature as the assessor.
* **Trainee Digital Acknowledgment** (Required: True)
  • Enter the trainee's digital acknowledgment.
