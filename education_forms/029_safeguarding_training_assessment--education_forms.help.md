# Safeguarding Training Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Safeguarding Training Assessment form is a tool used to evaluate the knowledge and training of individuals on safeguarding practices and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

* Carefully read each section and provide accurate and relevant information.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
* For sections with multiple options, select the relevant response that best describes your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Section 1: Section 1** (`section_1`, text, required): This section is a text field that asks you to provide any additional information about yourself. Please provide any relevant details that you think are necessary to answer the assessment questions.
* **Section 2: Section 2** (`section_2`, select_one, required): This section asks you to select your response to a question about safeguarding practices. Please choose the option that best describes your understanding of safeguarding practices.
* **Section 3: Section 3** (`section_3`, select_multiple, required): This section also asks you to select your response to another question about safeguarding practices. You can choose multiple options if applicable.
* **Section 4: Section 4** (`section_4`, date, required): This section is a date field that asks you to provide a specific date. Please enter the date in the correct format (dd/mm/yyyy).
* **Section 5: Section 5** (`section_5`, time, required): This section is a time field that asks you to provide a specific time. Please enter the time in the correct format (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Section 6: email 1** (`email_1`, email, optional): This section asks you to provide your email address. This is an optional field, but please provide your email address if you have one.
* **Section 7: phone 1** (`phone_1`, text, optional): This section asks you to provide your phone number. This is an optional field, but please provide your phone number if you have one.
* **Section 8: email 2** (`email_2`, email, optional): This section is another email field for you to provide an email address. This is an optional field, and you can leave it blank if you don't have an email address.
* **Section 9: introduction 2** (`introduction_2`, note, optional): This section is a note field that provides additional information about the form. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
