# Kids Cooking Class Allergy Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your child's food allergies, medical conditions, and emergency contact details to ensure their safety and well-being in our cooking classes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form carefully and thoroughly.
2. For each allergy, please specify the name and type (e.g., peanuts, dairy, etc.).
3. Select the severity level of each allergy (Mild, Moderate, or Severe).
4. If your child has more than one allergy, please fill out each field separately.
5. Provide emergency contact information, including the name, relationship, and contact details (phone, email).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Food Allergies** (`1. Food Allergies`): Please list your child's food allergies, including the name and type of each allergy (e.g., peanuts, dairy, etc.).
* **Food Restrictions** (`2. Food Restrictions`): If your child has any food restrictions or preferences (e.g., vegetarian, gluten-free, etc.), please specify.
* **Contact Information** (`3. Contact Information`): Enter your name and relationship with your child.
* **Emergency Contact** (`4. Emergency Contact`): Enter your emergency contact's name and contact details (phone and email).
* **Medical Conditions** (`5. Medical Conditions`): If your child has any medical conditions that may affect their participation in the cooking classes, please specify.
* **Allergy 1 Name** (`6. Allergy 1 Name`): Enter the name of the first allergy.
* **Allergy 1 Type** (`7. Allergy 1 Type`): Select the type of the first allergy from the options provided.
* **Allergy 1 Severity** (`8. Allergy 1 Severity`): Select the severity level of the first allergy (Mild, Moderate, or Severe).
* **Allergy 2 Name** (`9. Allergy 2 Name`): Enter the name of the second allergy (optional).
* **Allergy 2 Type** (`10. Allergy 2 Type`): Select the type of the second allergy from the options provided (optional).
* **Allergy 2 Severity** (`11. Allergy 2 Severity`): Select the severity level of the second allergy (optional).
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`12. Dietary Restrictions`): If your child has any dietary restrictions, please specify.
* **Name** (`13. Name`): Enter your name.
* **Relationship** (`14. Relationship`): Enter your relationship with your child.
* **Phone** (`15. Phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`16. Email`): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Name** (`17. Contact Name`): Enter the contact's name.
* **Contact Relationship** (`18. Contact Relationship`): Enter the contact's relationship with your child.
* **Contact Phone** (`19. Contact Phone`): Enter the contact's phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`20. Contact Email`): Enter the contact's email address.
* **Doctor's Name** (`21. Doctor's Name`): If your child has a doctor, enter their name (optional).
* **Doctor Phone** (`22. Doctor Phone`): If your child has a doctor, enter their phone number (optional).
* **Doctor Email** (`23. Doctor Email`): If your child has a doctor, enter their email address (optional).
* **Phone2** (`24. Phone2`): Enter an additional phone number (optional).
* **Phone3** (`25. Phone3`): Enter an additional phone number (optional).
