# Art Class Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Art Class Intake Form is a required form for all students enrolling in the art class. It is designed to collect essential information about your background, art experience, and expectations from the class.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, providing all required information.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability, and be as specific as possible.
3. If you're unsure about any question, please feel free to contact the instructor for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (Student Name)**:
	+ This is the name you would like to be referred to in class.
* **Email Address (Student Email)**:
	+ This is your primary email address that we will use to communicate with you.
* **Phone Number (Student Phone)**:
	+ This is the phone number we can use to contact you.
* **Date of Birth (Date of Birth)**:
	+ This is your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 07/12/1990).
* **Intake Date (Intake Date)**:
	+ This is the date you are enrolling in the art class.
* **-- Educational Background --**:
	+ This is a note section to provide more information about your educational background.
* **Highest Education Level**:
	+ Please select your highest level of education completed.
* **Field of Study or Major**:
	+ If you're currently studying or have studied in a specific field, please provide it.
* **-- Art Background --**:
	+ This is a note section to provide more information about your art background.
* **Art Experience Level**:
	+ Please select your level of art experience.
	+ Complete beginner: You have little to no art experience.
	+ Some basic experience: You have some basic knowledge or experience with art.
	+ Moderate experience: You have a good understanding of art principles and techniques.
	+ Advanced experience: You have extensive experience with art.
	+ Professional: You have a professional background in art.
* **Art Forms You Have Experience With**:
	+ Please select the art forms you have experience with.
* **What are your art learning goals**:
	+ Please describe your goals and expectations from the class.
* **-- Learning Style and Preferences --**:
	+ This is a note section to provide more information about your learning style and preferences.
* **Preferred Learning Style**:
	+ Please select your preferred learning style.
	+ Visual: You learn best through visual instruction and demonstrations.
	+ Hands-on practice: You learn best through hands-on practice and experimentation.
	+ Verbal instruction: You learn best through spoken instructions.
	+ Demonstration: You learn best through demonstrations.
	+ Mix of styles: You learn best through a mix of styles.
* **Preferred Work Pace**:
	+ Please select your preferred work pace.
	+ Slow and methodical: You prefer to work at a slow and methodical pace.
	+ Moderate: You prefer to work at a moderate pace.
	+ Fast-paced: You prefer to work at a fast-paced environment.
	+ Flexible: You prefer to work at a flexible pace.
* **Comfortable Working with Others**:
	+ Please select how you prefer to work.
	+ Individual work: You prefer to work individually.
	+ Mix of individual and group work: You prefer a mix of individual and group work.
	+ Group work: You prefer to work in a group setting.
* **-- Accessibility and Special Needs --**:
	+ This is a note section to provide more information about your accessibility and special needs.
* **Do you have accessibility needs**:
	+ Please select whether you have any accessibility needs.
	+ False: You do not have any accessibility needs.
	+ True: You have accessibility needs.
* **Please describe your accessibility needs**:
	+ If you selected that you have accessibility needs, please describe them.
* **Any physical limitations affecting art making**:
	+ If you have any physical limitations, please describe them.
* **-- Class Goals and Expectations --**:
	+ This is a note section to provide more information about your class goals and expectations.
* **Specific Goals for This Class**:
	+ Please describe your specific goals for the class.
* **What do you expect from this class**:
	+ Please describe what you expect from the class.
* **Any Additional Information We Should Know**:
	+ If there's anything else you'd like to share with us, please provide it.
