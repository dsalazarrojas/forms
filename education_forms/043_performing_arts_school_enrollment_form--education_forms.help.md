# Performing Arts School Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information for a student's enrollment in the Performing Arts School. It is essential to provide accurate and up-to-date information for the student's registration.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all the required fields to ensure accurate and complete information.
2. Review each field carefully before proceeding to the next step.
3. Make sure to sign the parent and student signatures at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, false): This is the name of the school the student is enrolling in.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, false): This is the name of the student enrolling in the school.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_one, false): Select the grade level of the student, from 9th to 12th grade.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, select_one, false): Choose the program the student is enrolling in, such as Art, Music, or Dance.
* **Email** (`email`, email, false): Enter the student's email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, false): Provide the student's contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`student_dob`, date, false): Enter the student's date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, false): This is the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, false): Provide the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, text, false): Enter the student's address.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_address`, text, false): Enter the emergency contact's address.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, note, false): Sign as a parent or guardian, indicating your agreement with the enrollment terms.
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, note, false): Sign as a student, indicating your agreement with the enrollment terms.
* **School ID** (`school_id`, text, false): This is the school ID number for the student.
