# Expression Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Expression Quiz" form is designed to assess your interpretation of simple expressions. It consists of two questions, and you will be presented with a choice of answers for each question. The form is meant to evaluate your ability to understand the meaning behind a given expression.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

* Answer each question to the best of your ability.
* For question 2, select one of the available options that you believe best answers the expression.
* Click the "Submit" button to submit your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Question 1: <span class="label">What is the meaning of the expression? </span>
* This question requires you to provide a written answer to the expression "The cat is looking."
* Think about what you would typically associate with the word "looking" in this context.

### Question 2: <span class="label">The cat is looking</span>
* This question requires you to select one of the available options:
  * <span class="option">It is happy</span>
  * <span class="option">It is sad</span>
  * <span class="option">It is tired</span>
  * Choose the option that you believe best matches your interpretation of the expression.

### Question 3: <span class="label">What does it mean</span>
* This question requires you to provide a written answer to the expression's meaning.
* Think about what the expression "The cat is looking" typically means in everyday language. 

## Tips
* Take your time to read each question carefully before answering.
* If you need help, feel free to ask.
* Remember that the form is designed to be a fun assessment of your understanding, so don't worry about making mistakes!
* Click the "Submit" button to submit your answers and get your score!
