# Student COVID 19 Vaccine Attestation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student COVID 19 Vaccine Attestation Form is designed to collect information from students about their interest in being vaccinated against COVID-19, as well as demographic details and any comments or reasons they may have for not wanting to be vaccinated.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your student name in the Student Name field.
2. Enter your parent's name in the Parent Name field.
3. Enter your email address in the Student Email field.
4. Enter your phone number in the Student Phone field.
5. Select "Yes" or "No" from the Interested In Vaccination field.
6. If you have a reason for not wanting to be vaccinated, enter it in the Why No Vaccination field.
7. Enter any comments you may have in the Comments field.
8. Enter your school ID in the School ID field.
9. Enter the date you completed the form in the Form Completion Date field.
10. Enter your name in the Completed By field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Interest**: Enter your interest in being vaccinated as a text.
- **Student Name**: Enter your name.
- **Parent Name**: Enter your parent's name.
- **Student Email**: Enter your email address.
- **Student Phone**: Enter your phone number.
- **Interested In Vaccination**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your interest in vaccination.
- **Why No Vaccination**: Enter a reason for not wanting to be vaccinated if applicable.
- **Comments**: Enter any comments you may have.
- **School ID**: Enter your school ID.
- **Form Completion Date**: Enter the date you completed the form.
- **Completed By**: Enter your name.

## Tips
- Please answer all the required fields accurately and truthfully.
- If you have any comments or questions, please enter them in the Comments field.
- Make sure to enter your school ID and form completion date accurately.
- If you have any issues or concerns, contact your school administration or the person who distributed the form.
