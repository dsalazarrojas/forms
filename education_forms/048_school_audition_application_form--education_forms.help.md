# School Audition Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Audition Application Form is designed to collect information from individuals who are applying for an audition at a school. It helps the school's admissions team evaluate applicants' qualifications and schedules.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow the steps below:
1. Fill out the Applicant Information section with your basic contact and demographic details.
2. Provide your contact information in the Contact Information section.
3. Describe your audition details in the Audition Details section.
4. If applicable, indicate the audition fee you are willing to pay.
5. Choose your preferred schedule for the audition.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Applicant Information
* **Applicant Information** (`applicant_info`, Text, Optional):
	+ This section collects your basic contact and demographic details, such as your name, address, phone number, and email.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, Text, Optional):
	+ This section asks for your contact information, including your email and phone number, so we can reach out to you for further instructions and audition details.
* **Audition Details** (`audition_details`, Text, Optional):
	+ Please describe the specific audition you are interested in and any relevant background information about yourself.
* **Performance Information** (`performance_info`, Text, Optional):
	+ If you are a performer, please provide any relevant performance experience and information about your skills and experience.
* **Audition Fee** (`audition_fee`, Number, Optional):
	+ If you are asked to pay a fee for the audition, please enter the amount you are willing to pay.
* **Preferred Schedule** (`preferred_schedule`, Time, Optional):
	+ Choose your preferred schedule for the audition. Please note that we will contact you to schedule a specific time and date.
## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out.
* We will contact you to confirm your audition schedule and provide further instructions.
