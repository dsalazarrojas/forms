# Substitute Teacher Reporting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Substitute Teacher Reporting Form is a tool for teachers to record notes and observations from their lessons, as well as provide comments and ratings for their students. This form helps teachers to provide feedback and guidance to students and administrators, and ensures that the reporting process is thorough and accurate.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by selecting the relevant fields for the lesson you are reporting on. You can choose to report on one or multiple fields depending on the specific circumstances of the lesson.
2. Enter your thoughts and observations in the "Lesson Notes" field, describing what was covered in the lesson.
3. In the "Student Behavior" field, record any notable behaviors or actions from the students during the lesson.
4. In the "Student Comments" field, include any comments or feedback you have for individual students.
5. For the "Lesson Planned" field, describe the lesson as it was originally intended, including any changes or adjustments made during the lesson.
6. In the "Lesson Actual" field, describe how the lesson actually went, including any changes or deviations from the original plan.
7. Enter any comments or feedback from the teacher in the "Teacher Comments" field.
8. In the "Student Participation" field, record the level of student participation and engagement during the lesson.
9. If applicable, rate the student's performance using the "Teacher Rating" field.
10. Finally, enter the "Teacher Name" field with your name for reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lesson Notes** (Text, Required: False): Enter a brief summary of what was covered in the lesson.
* **Student Behavior** (Text, Required: False): Record any notable behaviors or actions from the students during the lesson.
* **Student Comments** (Text, Required: False): Include any comments or feedback you have for individual students.
* **Lesson Planned** (Text, Required: False): Describe the lesson as it was originally intended, including any changes or adjustments made during the lesson.
* **Lesson Actual** (Text, Required: False): Describe how the lesson actually went, including any changes or deviations from the original plan.
* **Teacher Comments** (Text, Required: False): Enter any comments or feedback from the teacher.
* **Student Participation** (Text, Required: False): Record the level of student participation and engagement during the lesson.
* **Teacher Rating** (Number, Required: False): If applicable, rate the student's performance using the rating scale.
* **Teacher Name** (Text, Required: False): Enter your name for reference.
