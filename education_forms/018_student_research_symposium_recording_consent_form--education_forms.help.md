# Student Research Symposium Recording Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to obtain consent from students participating in the Research Symposium. It captures information about the student, their research institution, and the specific details of the recording. This form is part of the consent process to ensure that the students are aware of what they are agreeing to, and to provide a clear understanding of the terms and conditions of the recording. Please ensure you have not completed this form previously.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information in the corresponding fields and sign the form at the end. Make sure to review each field carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`): Enter a brief title for the form, indicating the purpose and scope of the form. This should be a concise and descriptive title that summarizes the content of the form.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`): Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in the recording or publication of the presentation.
* **Research Institution** (`research_institution`): Select the institution where you are affiliated with, if applicable. If your institution is not listed, select "Other" and provide the name of your institution in the following field.
* **Other Institution** (`research_institution_other`): If you selected "Other" in the previous field, enter the name of your institution here.
* **Permission Date** (`permission_date`): Indicate the date on which you are granting permission for the recording.
* **Recording Media** (`recording_media`): Select the type of recording medium you are permitting, e.g., audio, video, or photo.
* **Recording Location** (`recording_location`): Specify the location of the recording, e.g., university campus or online meeting.
* **Participant Permission** (`participant_permission`): Select the level of permission you are granting, e.g., for use in presentations or publications.
* **Form Signature** (`form_signature`): Sign the form to indicate your agreement with the terms and conditions of the recording.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`): Sign your name above as a participant to confirm your agreement.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you understand what you are agreeing to before submitting the form.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask the research coordinator or presenter for clarification.
