# Vocabulary Definition Lookup Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vocabulary Definition Lookup Request form is a tool to collect information from users for a vocabulary definition lookup request. It's designed to gather details about a word or phrase that needs to be looked up for its definition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the title of the word or phrase in the "Form Title" field.
2. Provide the context in which the word or phrase is used in the "Context" field.
3. Specify your preferences for the definition lookup in the "Preferences" field.
4. Enter the date of your request in the "Request Date" field.
5. Enter the time of your request in the "Request Time" field.
6. Select the context of your request from the options provided in the "Select Context" field.
7. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (optional).
8. Enter any additional notes about your request in the "Note" field (optional).
9. Select a note about your request from the options provided in the "Select Note" field (optional).
10. Enter your phone number in the "Phone 1" field (required).
11. Enter your email address in the "Email 1" field (required).
12. Select a note about your request from the options provided in the "Select Note 1" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, string, required): Enter a brief title of the word or phrase you are looking up.
* **Context** (`context`, string, required): Provide the context in which the word or phrase is used. This will help in understanding the definition better.
* **Preferences** (`preferences`, select_multiple, required): Select your preferences for the definition lookup. You can choose 'Yes', 'No', or 'Other' for your preferences.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your request. This will help in tracking and scheduling the lookup process.
* **Request Time** (`request_time`, time, required): Enter the time of your request. This will help in scheduling the lookup process.
* **Select Context** (`select_context`, select_multiple, required): Select the context of your request from the provided options.
* **Phone** (`phone`, string, optional): Enter your phone number if you have one. This will help in contacting you for further information.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about your request. This will help in understanding your requirements better.
* **Select Note** (`select_note`, select_multiple, optional): Select a note about your request from the provided options.
* **Phone 1** (`phone_1`, string, required): Enter your phone number to contact you.
* **Email 1** (`email`, string, required): Enter your email address to contact you.
* **Select Note 1** (`select_note_1`, select_multiple, optional): Select a note about your request from the provided options.
