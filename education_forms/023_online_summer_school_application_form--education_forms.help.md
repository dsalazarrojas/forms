# online_summer_school_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The online summer school application form is designed to collect information from schools or institutions applying to participate in the online summer school program. It is essential to fill out this form to ensure that your application is complete and considered for participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and ensure you understand the purpose and requirements of the online summer school program.
2. Select whether you are interested in participating in the program ("Online Summer School Application Form" section).
3. Enter the number of students you expect to enroll in the program.
4. Provide your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
5. Enter your school's name and contact information, including email and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Online Summer School Application Form** (Select Multiple, Required): Please select "Yes" if you are interested in participating in the online summer school program.
- **Students Count** (Number, Required): Enter the number of students you expect to enroll in the program. This will help us estimate the demand and plan the program accordingly.
- **Name** (Text, Required): Enter your name and title as the representative of the institution applying.
- **E-mail** (Email, Required): Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your application.
- **Phone** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number to receive updates and notifications about the program.
- **School Name** (Text, Required): Enter your school's name.
- **School Email** (Email, Required): Enter your school's email address.
- **School Phone** (Text, Required): Enter your school's phone number.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you select "Yes" in the first field to indicate your interest in participating in the online summer school program.
- Double-check your entries for accuracy and consistency throughout the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to contact us.
