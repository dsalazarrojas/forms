# Student Information Sheet Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Information Sheet Template is a comprehensive form used to collect and document student information for administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully to understand its purpose and required information.
2. Complete the form by providing accurate and complete details for each field.
3. Ensure all required fields are filled in to avoid delays or issues with processing the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **High School**: Enter the student's high school name or institution.
* **Grade Point Average (GPA)**: Enter the student's current or expected grade point average.
* **Academic Interests**: Select all relevant academic areas the student is interested in (e.g., Computer Science, Art, Business, Engineering).
* **Grade Level**: Select the student's current grade level (9th, 10th, 11th, 12th, Post-Grad, Pre-Grad, or Other).
* **Student ID**: Enter a unique identifier for the student (not always required but can be useful for tracking).
* **Name**: Enter the student's full name.
* **Email**: Enter the student's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the student's contact phone number (not always required but can be useful for communication).
* **Birth Date**: Enter the student's date of birth (only if applicable).
* **Gender**: Select the student's gender (Male, Female, or Other).
* **Ethnicity**: Select the student's ethnicity (Asian, African American, White, Hispanic, Native American, or Other).
* **Student Type**: Select the student's type (Full-Time Student, Part-Time Student, Transfer Student, or Other).
* **Program Type**: Select the student's program type (Undergraduate, Graduate, or Other).
* **Campus Location**: Select the campus location (Main Campus, Off Campus, Online).
* **Student Status**: Select the student's status (New Student, Returning Student, Non-Student).
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and contact details of the student's emergency contact person.
* **Contact Relationship**: Select the relationship between the student and the emergency contact (Parent, Guardian, Other).
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the contact email of the emergency contact person.
* **Student's Signature**: Include the student's signature as proof of acknowledgment.
* **Emergency Contact's Signature**: Include the emergency contact's signature as proof of acknowledgment.
* **Student Agreement**: Confirm that the student understands and agrees to the terms and conditions outlined in this document.
* **Parent Agreement**: Confirm that the parent or guardian understands and agrees to the terms and conditions outlined in this document.
* **Emergency Contact Agreement**: Confirm that the emergency contact understands and agrees to the terms and conditions outlined in this document.

Note: Some fields may not be required for every student, so ensure you fill in all relevant fields as applicable.
