# Private School Scholarship Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for students or guardians to apply for a private school scholarship.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out your personal information as an applicant or guardian.
2. Provide contact information for easy communication with the scholarship committee.
3. Share information about your education background, including any relevant academic achievements.
4. Describe your financial situation to help the committee understand your needs.
5. Add any additional information that you think is relevant to your application.
6. Upload any supporting documents that you think will help with your application.
7. Review and submit your application for review.
8. Wait for the scholarship committee to review and make a decision.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Info** (`applicant_info`, required): Enter your name and any other information you'd like to share with the scholarship committee.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, required): Provide your contact information so the committee can easily reach out to you.
* **Guardian Info** (`guardian_info`, optional): If you're a guardian applying on behalf of a student, enter your information here.
* **Student Info** (`student_info`, optional): If you're a student applying for yourself, enter your information here.
* **Education Background** (`education_background`, optional): Share your education history, including any relevant academic achievements.
* **Scholarship Info** (`scholarship_info`, optional): Describe the scholarship you're applying for and why you're eligible.
* **Financial Info** (`financial_info`, optional): Explain your financial situation and how it affects your ability to fund your education.
* **Submission Info** (`submission_info`, optional): Provide any additional information about your submission, such as any supporting documents.
* **Upload Documents** (`upload_documents`, optional): Attach any necessary documents to support your application.
* **Review and Submit** (`review_and_submit`, required): This is the final step to submit your application.
* **Review Status** (`review_status`, optional): The scholarship committee will update this field with their review status.
* **Review Notes** (`review_notes`, optional): Any notes the committee may have for your application.
* **Review Comments** (`review_comments`, optional): Comments from the committee about your application.
* **Decision Status** (`decision_status`, optional): The final decision on your application.
* **Scholarship Status** (`scholarship_status`, optional): The status of your scholarship.
* **Scholarship Details** (`scholarship_details`, optional): Any details about the scholarship you've been awarded.
* **User Info** (`user_info`, optional): Additional information about the user who is submitting the application.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, optional): Any extra information that may be relevant to your application.
* **Review Info** (`review_info`, optional): Information about the review process.
* **Review Comments 2** (`review_comments_2`, optional): Comments from the review team.
* **Review Status 2** (`review_status_2`, optional): The review status.
* **Review Notes 2** (`review_notes_2`, optional): Notes from the review team.
* **Review Status 3** (`review_status_3`, optional): Another review status.
* **Review Notes 3** (`review_notes_3`, optional): Another set of notes from the review team.
* **Review Comments 3** (`review_comments_3`, optional): Another set of comments from the review team.
