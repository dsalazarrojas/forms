# Teaching Method Effectiveness Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to evaluate the effectiveness of a teaching method in a classroom setting.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form for each unique classroom session or teaching method you want to evaluate.
2. Provide accurate and concise answers to each question.
3. Make sure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, required): Enter your name as the instructor who taught the class.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, required): Enter the name of the course or subject being taught.
* **Classroom Context** (`classroom_context`, optional): Provide a brief description of the classroom environment and any relevant details about the class.
* **Teaching Method** (`teaching_method`, optional): Describe the specific teaching method or approach used during the class.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, optional): Provide any general feedback or comments about the class or teaching method.
* **Student Attendance** (`student_attendance`, required): Enter the number of students in the class.
* **Method Effectiveness** (`method_effectiveness`, required): Choose how effective you found the teaching method to be:
	+ Highly effective
	+ Effective
	+ Somewhat effective
	+ Not very effective
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, required): Choose multiple ratings for the overall effectiveness of the class:
	+ Highly effective
	+ Somewhat effective
	+ Effective
	+ Somewhat ineffective
	+ Not very effective
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, optional): Provide any recommendations or suggestions for improvement for the teaching method.

Tips: Make sure to provide honest and constructive feedback to help inform future teaching methods and improve student outcomes.
