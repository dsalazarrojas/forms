# TZS Foundation Scholarship 2020 Information Seeking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from colleges that are interested in partnering with the T.Z.S. Foundation for the 2020 scholarship.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please ensure that you are an authorized representative of your college.
2. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **College Name** (College Name, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your college.
* **Contact Person** (Contact Person, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person to contact for more information.
* **Contact Email** (Contact Email, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the contact person. This email address will be used for communication.
* **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Are you interested in partnering with us** (Select one or more, select_multiple, required/optional): Select "Partner with us" if you are interested in partnering with the T.Z.S. Foundation for the 2020 scholarship.
* **College Details** (College Details, note, required/optional): Enter any additional information about your college that is relevant to our partnership.
* **Other Information** (Other Information, text, required/optional): Enter any other information that you think is relevant to our partnership.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to contact us.
