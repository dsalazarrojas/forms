# Student Information Form Dark And Responsive - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Information Form Dark And Responsive is designed to collect comprehensive information about students in a school or educational setting. This form helps educators and administrators to record and manage student data effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Select the student's grade level from the "Select Background" dropdown menu.
3. Enter the student's contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
4. Enter the student's email address in the "Email" field.
5. Select multiple background options from the "Select Multiple Background" dropdown menu.
6. Enter the student's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
7. Enter the student's time of birth in the "Time of Birth" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter the student's name.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, number, optional): Select the student's grade level using numbers (e.g., 1 for grade one, 2 for grade two, and so on).
* **Student Age** (`student_age`, number, optional): Enter the student's age.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the student's email address.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the student's contact number.
* **Student Background** (`student_background`, note, optional): Enter a note about the student's background information.
* **Select Background** (`select_background`, select_one, optional): Select one option from the list.
* **Select Multiple Background** (`select_multiple_background`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple options from the list.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the student's date of birth.
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, optional): Enter the student's time of birth.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
* Double-check the entered information for accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to your administrator or educator for assistance.
