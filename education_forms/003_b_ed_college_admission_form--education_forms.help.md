# BEd College Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals who wish to apply for admission to BEd College. It is a mandatory form that requires specific information from the applicant to evaluate their suitability for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full legal name in the "Applicant name" field.
2. Provide your date of birth in the "Date of birth" field, in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
3. Enter your official email address for correspondence in the "Email address" field.
4. If you have a mobile number, enter it in the "Contact phone" field.
5. Provide your permanent address in the "Permanent address" field, including street, city, state, and postal code.
6. Describe your previous educational institutions, degrees, and years of completion in the "Previous education details" field.
7. If you have any teaching experience, enter the number of years in the "Years of teaching experience" field.
8. Select your preferred area of specialization from the "Preferred specialization" dropdown menu.
9. If you have a professional reference, provide their name and phone number in the "Reference contact information" field.
10. Attach any relevant supporting documents, such as your transcript and ID, as per the "Supporting documents uploaded" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Enter your full legal name as per your ID.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Use the YYYY-MM-DD format to enter your date of birth.
* **Email address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your official email address for correspondence.
* **Contact phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your mobile number or any other contact number.
* **Permanent address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your street, city, state, and postal code.
* **Previous education details** (`previous_education`, text, required): Describe your previous educational institutions, degrees, and years of completion.
* **Years of teaching experience** (`teaching_experience_years`, number, optional): Enter the number of years you have teaching experience. If you have no experience, enter 0.
* **Preferred specialization** (`preferred_specialization`, select_one, required): Select your preferred area of specialization from the dropdown menu.
* **Reference contact information** (`reference_contact`, text, optional): If you have a professional reference, provide their name and phone number.
* **Supporting documents uploaded** (`supporting_documents`, note, optional): Attach any relevant documents, such as your transcript and ID, as required.
