# Electric Vehicle Maintenance Program Admission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users who are interested in joining the Electric Vehicle Maintenance Program. The program is designed to provide support and resources to help users maintain their electric vehicles properly, ensuring a safe and efficient driving experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your information, including your contact method and contact number.
2. Select your vehicle type from the available options.
3. Review your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User information** (Required: No): This field is used to collect general information about yourself.
* **Vehicle type** (Required: No, Type: Select One): Select your type of electric vehicle from the options provided to help us better understand your specific needs.
* **Contact method** (Required: No, Type: Select Multiple): Choose the method(s) through which you would like to be contacted, such as "Yes" for email, phone, or both.
* **Contact number** (Required: No, Type: Text): Enter your contact number for us to reach you.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all fields accurately to ensure we can better assist you with your electric vehicle maintenance needs.
* If you're unsure about any of the options, feel free to ask for clarification.
* We will be in touch with you shortly after submitting this form to provide further information about the program.
