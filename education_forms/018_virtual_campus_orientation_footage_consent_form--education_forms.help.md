# Virtual Campus Orientation Footage Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information and consent for recording footage during the campus orientation. It is intended for individuals who have been informed that they will be participating in the campus orientation program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you are a participant in the campus orientation program.
2. Carefully read each field in the form and answer all the questions as instructed.
3. Fill out each field completely and accurately.
4. Submit the form to confirm your participation and consent for recording footage.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Campus Orientation Form
* **Campus Orientation Form** (`campus_orientation_form`, text, optional): This section is intended to gather any relevant information about your campus orientation experience. If you have any specific needs or concerns, please feel free to mention them here.

### Participant Information
* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, text, optional): This field is to gather any additional information about you as a participant. Please provide your name, role, or any other relevant details about your participation.

### Video Consent
* **Video Consent** (`video_consent`, select_multiple, optional): Please select which options you are consenting to for recording footage:
	+ Option 1: You are okay with being recorded.
	+ Option 2: You do not want to be recorded.
	+ Option 3: You have specific concerns or restrictions for recording, please describe below.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you with follow-up information or any other relevant materials.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can reach out to you if needed.

### Signature
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, optional): Please print your name to confirm your participation.

### Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or concerns, please mention them here.

## Tips
* Please make sure to provide accurate and honest information in each field.
* Each participant should submit their own form.
* The form is not intended for individuals who are not participating in the campus orientation program.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the organizers of the campus orientation program.
