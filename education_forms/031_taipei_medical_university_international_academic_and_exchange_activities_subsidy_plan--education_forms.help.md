# taipei_medical_university_international_academic_and_exchange_activities_subsidy_plan - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from international students or academic staff at Taipe Medical University regarding their exchange activities, academic and exchange subsidies, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, please carefully fill out each section with accurate and relevant information.
*   Make sure to answer all required fields, which are marked as such.
*   If a field has options, select the most suitable option that applies to your situation.
*   Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Exchange Activities Subsidy Plan** (`exchange_activities_subsidy_plan`, text, not required): This field is for you to describe your proposed exchange activities subsidy plan. Please provide a brief but comprehensive explanation of your plan.
*   **Exchange Period** (`exchange_period`, text, not required): In this field, specify the period you plan to participate in the exchange activities. Be as specific as possible (e.g., semester, year, etc.).
*   **Exchange Duration** (`exchange_duration`, text, not required): This field is for you to mention the duration of your planned exchange activities.
*   **Exchange Objective** (`exchange_objective`, text, not required): Describe the main objective of your exchange activities. What do you hope to achieve or gain from this experience?
*   **Exchange Scholarship** (`exchange_scholarship`, text, not required): If you have received or plan to receive a scholarship for your exchange activities, provide details about it in this field.
*   **Exchange Expenses** (`exchange_expenses`, text, not required): Estimate and provide details about the expenses you expect to incur during your exchange activities.
*   **Exchange Travel Document** (`exchange_travel_document`, text, not required): Upload or provide details about travel documents required for your exchange activities, such as visa, passport, etc.
