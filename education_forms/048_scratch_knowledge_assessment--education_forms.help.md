# Scratch Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scratch Knowledge Assessment form is a tool used to evaluate the knowledge and skills of users in different areas related to the Scratch project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form and ensure you have a clear understanding of each question.
2.  Select the options that best describe your experience, skills, goals, and confidence in the areas asked.
3.  Enter any additional comments or feedback in the "Feedback" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Introduction** (`introduction`, `text`, optional): This is a text field where you can briefly describe your background and experience with the Scratch project.

- **Project Experience** (`project_experience`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the project experiences that apply to you. This will help the administrator evaluate your understanding of the project's concepts and your ability to apply them.

  - Option 1: I have some experience with Scratch projects
  - Option 2: I have extensive experience with Scratch projects

- **Project Skills** (`project_skills`, `select_one`, required): Select the skills you are most proficient in. This will help the administrator evaluate your ability to apply project skills.

  - Option 1: I am proficient in Scratch basics
  - Option 2: I am proficient in Scratch programming
  - Option 3: I am proficient in Scratch game development

- **Project Goals** (`project_goals`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the project goals that apply to you. This will help the administrator evaluate your understanding of the project's objectives and your ability to set achievable goals.

  - Option 1: Create a fun game
  - Option 2: Create a interactive story
  - Option 3: Create a animation

- **Project Confidence** (`project_confidence`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of confidence you have in your project skills. This will help the administrator evaluate your self-assessment of your abilities.

  - Option 1: I am not confident
  - Option 2: I am somewhat confident
  - Option 3: I am confident

- **Coding Skills** (`coding_skills`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the programming languages and tools you are proficient in. This is an optional field and will help the administrator evaluate your ability to apply programming concepts.

  - Option 1: I am proficient in Scratch basics
  - Option 2: I am proficient in Python programming
  - Option 3: I am proficient in JavaScript

- **Problem Solving** (`problem_solving`, `text`, optional): This is a text field where you can describe your approach to problem-solving in the context of Scratch projects.

- **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, optional): This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or feedback about the form, the Scratch project, or your experience.
