## Global Learning Data Collection Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Learning Data Collection Request Form is designed to collect contact information and data from users to better understand their learning needs and preferences. This form is typically used by educational institutions to gather data from students, staff, or faculty members. By completing this form, you will be able to provide valuable insights to support the development of educational programs and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide your name and email address in the "Name" and "Email" fields, respectively.
3. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone" field.
4. If you want to specify a date and time for the learning activity or event, enter it in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
5. Add any additional comments or details in the "Text Area" field.
6. For the "Radio Buttons" field, select one of the provided options.
7. For the "Dropdown" field, select all applicable options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, text, required): This field is used to collect your contact information, including your name and email address.
* **Name** (Name, text, optional): This field is used to collect your name.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): This field is used to collect your email address.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): This field is used to collect your phone number.
* **Date** (Date, date, optional): This field is used to specify a date for the learning activity or event.
* **Time** (Time, time, optional): This field is used to specify a time for the learning activity or event.
* **Text Area** (Text Area, note, optional): This field is used to add any additional comments or details.
* **Radio Buttons** (Radio Buttons, select_one, required): This field is used to select one of the provided options. 
  Options:
  • option-1
  • option-2
  • option-3
* **Dropdown** (Dropdown, select_multiple, required): This field is used to select all applicable options. 
  Options:
  • option-1
  • option-2
