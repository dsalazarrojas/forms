# Extracurricular Activities Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain parental consent for a student to participate in extracurricular activities at school.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter the student's name in the **student_name** field.
2. Enter the parent's name in the **parent_name** field.
3. Enter the parent's email address in the **parent_email** field.
4. Enter the parent's phone number in the **parent_phone** field.
5. Enter the student's grade level in the **student_grade_level** field.
6. List the extracurricular activities the student wishes to participate in in the **student_activities** field.
7. Provide any additional comments in the **student_additional_comments** field.
8. Select "Yes" or "No" in the **parent_consent** field to indicate whether the parent consents to the student's participation.
9. Select "Yes" or "No" in the **school_consent** field to indicate whether the school approves of the student's participation.
10. Select "Yes" or "No" in the **teacher_consent** field to indicate whether the teacher approves of the student's participation.
11. Enter the date and time of signing in the **date_signed** and **time_signed** fields, respectively.
12. Enter the school's name in the **school_name** field.
13. Enter the school's phone number in the **school_phone** field.
14. Enter the school's email address in the **school_email** field.
15. Provide any additional comments in the **school_comments** field.
16. Enter the teacher's name in the **teacher_name** field.
17. Enter the teacher's email address in the **teacher_email** field.
18. Enter the teacher's phone number in the **teacher_phone** field.
19. Provide any additional comments in the **teacher_comments** field.
20. Sign and date the **student_signature** field to verify the student's identity.
21. Sign and date the **parent_signature** field to verify the parent's identity.
22. Sign and date the **school_signature** field to verify the school's identity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **student_name** (Text, Optional): Enter the student's name.
* **parent_name** (Text, Optional): Enter the parent or legal guardian's name.
* **parent_email** (Email, Optional): Enter the parent or legal guardian's email address.
* **parent_phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the parent or legal guardian's phone number.
* **student_grade_level** (Text, Optional): Enter the student's grade level.
* **student_activities** (Text, Optional): List the extracurricular activities the student wishes to participate in.
* **student_additional_comments** (Text, Optional): Provide any additional comments or requests from the student.
* **parent_consent** (Select One, Optional): Select "Yes" to indicate that the parent or legal guardian consents to the student's participation.
* **school_consent** (Select One, Optional): Select "Yes" to indicate that the school approves of the student's participation.
* **teacher_consent** (Select One, Optional): Select "Yes" to indicate that the teacher approves of the student's participation.
* **student_signature** (Text, Optional): Sign and date to verify the student's identity.
* **parent_signature** (Text, Optional): Sign and date to verify the parent or legal guardian's identity.
* **school_signature** (Text, Optional): Sign and date to verify the school's identity.
* **date_signed** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of signing.
* **time_signed** (Time, Optional): Enter the time of signing.
* **school_name** (Text, Optional): Enter the school's name.
* **school_phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the school's phone number.
* **school_email** (Email, Optional): Enter the school's email address.
* **school_comments** (Text, Optional): Provide any additional comments or requests from the school.
* **teacher_name** (Text, Optional): Enter the teacher's name.
* **teacher_email** (Email, Optional): Enter the teacher's email address.
* **teacher_phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the teacher's phone number.
* **teacher_comments** (Text, Optional): Provide any additional comments or requests from the teacher.
