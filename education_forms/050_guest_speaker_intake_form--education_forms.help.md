# Guest Speaker Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Guest Speaker Intake Form is designed to collect information from guest speakers who will be presenting at events. This form helps us ensure that we have all the necessary details to communicate with the speaker and coordinate their presentation effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Double-check that you have filled in all required fields.
3. Review your form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Speaker Name (Speaker Name, text, not required)
Enter the name of the guest speaker as they would like to be referred to during the presentation.

### 2. Contact E-mail (Contact E-mail, email, not required)
Enter the email address of the guest speaker or their representative.

### 3. Contact Phone (Contact Phone, text, not required)
Enter the phone number of the guest speaker or their representative.

### 4. Speaker Contract Type (Speaker Contract Type, select_one, not required)
Select the type of contract agreed upon with the guest speaker.

### 5. Contract Date (Contract Date, date, not required)
Enter the date on which the contract was signed.

### 6. Contract Time (Contract Time, time, not required)
Enter the time at which the contract was signed.

### 7. Contract Duration (Contract Duration, number, not required)
Enter the duration of the contract.

### 8. Signature (Signature, note, not required)
A field for adding any additional comments or notes from the guest speaker.

## Tips
Please make sure to double-check all fields before submitting the form to avoid any errors. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
