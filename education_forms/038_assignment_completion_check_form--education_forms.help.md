# Assignment Completion Check Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track the completion status of student assignments. It helps teachers to monitor student progress and ensure that all requirements are met.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter the student's ID number in the "Student ID" field.
3. Enter the class name in the "Class Name" field.
4. Enter the teacher's name in the "Teacher Name" field.
5. Enter the date of the check in the "Check Date" field.
6. Enter the title of the assignment in the "Assignment Title" field.
7. Enter the reference number of the assignment in the "Assignment ID" field (if applicable).
8. Enter the date when the assignment was given in the "Date Assigned" field.
9. Enter the due date of the assignment in the "Due Date" field.
10. Select the current completion status of the assignment in the "Completion Status" field.
11. Enter the percentage complete in the "Percentage Complete" field.
12. Enter the date when the student submitted the assignment in the "Date Submitted" field (if applicable).
13. Select the method through which the student submitted the assignment in the "Submission Method" field (if applicable).
14. Select whether all parts of the assignment are complete in the "Are All Parts Completed" field.
15. If applicable, describe the missing parts in the "Missing Parts Description" field.
16. Select whether the assignment meets all requirements in the "Does It Meet Requirements" field.
17. Select the quality check status of the assignment in the "Quality Check Status" field.
18. Select whether the formatting is correct in the "Is Formatting Correct" field.
19. Select whether the word count is met in the "Is Word Count Met" field.
20. Select whether the citations are complete in the "Are Citations Complete" field.
21. If applicable, enter the teacher's comments in the "Teacher Comments" field.
22. Select whether revision is needed in the "Is Revision Needed" field.
23. If applicable, enter the date when the revision is due in the "Revision Due Date" field.
24. If graded, enter the grade assigned in the "Grade Assigned" field.
25. Enter your name in the "Teacher Signature" field.
26. Select whether the student acknowledges the submission in the "Student Acknowledges Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter the full name of the student.
* **Student ID**: Enter the student's ID number.
* **Class Name**: Enter the title of the course or class.
* **Teacher Name**: Enter the name of the instructor or teacher.
* **Check Date**: Enter the date of the check.
* **Assignment Title**: Enter the title of the assignment.
* **Assignment ID** (optional): Enter the reference number of the assignment.
* **Date Assigned**: Enter the date when the assignment was given.
* **Due Date**: Enter the date when the assignment is due.
* **Completion Status**: Select the current status of the assignment.
* **Percentage Complete**: Enter the percentage complete of the assignment.
* **Date Submitted** (optional): Enter the date when the student submitted the assignment.
* **Submission Method** (optional): Select the method through which the student submitted the assignment.
* **Are All Parts Completed**: Select whether all parts of the assignment are complete.
* **Missing Parts Description** (optional): Describe any missing parts of the assignment.
* **Does It Meet Requirements**: Select whether the assignment meets all requirements.
* **Quality Check Status**: Select the quality check status of the assignment.
* **Is Formatting Correct**: Select whether the formatting is correct.
* **Is Word Count Met**: Select whether the word count is met.
* **Are Citations Complete**: Select whether citations are complete.
* **Teacher Comments** (optional): Enter any comments from the teacher.
* **Is Revision Needed**: Select whether revision is needed.
* **Revision Due Date** (optional): Enter the date when the revision is due.
* **Grade Assigned** (optional): Enter the grade assigned by the teacher.
* **Teacher Signature**: Enter your name as the teacher.
* **Student Acknowledges Status**: Select whether the student acknowledges the submission.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Review each field carefully to ensure that it is correct.
* If a field is not applicable, select "Not Applicable" or leave it blank.
* If you are unsure about any field, seek clarification from your supervisor or relevant authority.
