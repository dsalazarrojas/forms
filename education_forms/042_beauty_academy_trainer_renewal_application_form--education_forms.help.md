# Beauty Academy Trainer Renewal Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for trainers seeking to renew their certification at the Beauty Academy. Trainers must complete this form to provide updated information and requirements for their renewal. Please ensure you have all necessary documents and information before starting the form.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Select the correct options for your certification and teaching experience.
3. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **First Name**: (`trainer_first_name`, `text`, required/true): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Last Name**: (`trainer_last_name`, `text`, required/true): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Email Address**: (`email_address`, `email`, required/true): Enter your contact email address.
- **Phone Number**: (`phone_number`, `text`, required/true): Enter your contact phone number.
- **Trainer ID Number**: (`trainer_id`, `text`, required/true): Enter your trainer ID number as assigned by the Beauty Academy.
- **Certification Number**: (`certification_number`, `text`, required/true): Enter your current certification number or license number.
- **Current Certification Expiry**: (`certification_expiry`, `date`, required/true): Enter the date when your current certification is set to expire.
- **Subjects Taught**: (`subjects_taught`, `select_multiple`, required/true): Select all subjects you have taught at the Beauty Academy.
- **Years Teaching at Academy**: (`years_teaching`, `select_one`, required/true): Select the number of years you have been teaching at the Beauty Academy.
- **Number of Courses Completed**: (`courses_completed`, `number`, required/true): Enter the number of courses you completed in the past year.
- **Student Success Rate**: (`student_success_rate`, `number`, required/true): Enter your student success rate as a percentage.
- **Continuing Education Hours**: (`continuing_education`, `number`, required/true): Enter the number of continuing education hours completed.
- **CE Certificates Uploaded**: (`ce_certificates`, `select_one`, required/true): Confirm if you have uploaded your CE certificates.
- **New Qualifications Obtained**: (`new_qualifications`, `text`, required/false): Enter any new qualifications or certifications obtained.
- **Teaching Evaluation Score**: (`teaching_evaluations`, `select_one`, required/true): Select your teaching evaluation score.
- **Renewal Type**: (`renewal_type`, `select_one`, required/true): Choose the type of renewal you are applying for.
- **Renewal Fee Paid**: (`renewal_fee`, `select_one`, required/true): Confirm if you have paid the renewal fee.
- **Background Check Current**: (`background_check`, `select_one`, required/true): Confirm if your background check is current.
- **Academy Comments**: (`academy_comments`, `text`, required/false): Enter any comments or feedback from the academy.
- **Trainer Signature**: (`trainer_signature`, `text`, required/true): Type your full name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Signature Date**: (`signature_date`, `date`, required/true): Enter the date you signed this form.

Please ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form. If you have any questions or need assistance, contact the Beauty Academy administration.
