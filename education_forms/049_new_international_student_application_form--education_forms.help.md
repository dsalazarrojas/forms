# New International Student Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New International Student Application Form is a form used to collect information from international students applying to the program for the first time. It is used in conjunction with other forms and documents to ensure that students meet all the necessary requirements for admission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, including `first_name`, `last_name`, `email`, `phone`, `address`, `housing_information`, `confirm_acknowledge`, `confirm_acknowledge_all`, and `confirm_file_1` through `confirm_file_3`.
2. Select the correct options for `acknowledge`, `file_1` through `file_3`, `confirm_acknowledge`, `confirm_acknowledge_all`, and `confirm_file_1` through `confirm_file_3`.
3. Enter any additional information in the `notes` field if necessary.
4. Confirm your identity by filling in the `confirm_email`, `confirm_phone`, `confirm_date`, `confirm_time`, `confirm_notes`, `confirm_housing`, `confirm_address`, `confirm_first_name`, `confirm_last_name`, and `confirm_confirm_acknowledge`.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **last_name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your address.
* **housing_information** (`housing_information`, text, required): Enter any relevant housing information.
* **acknowledge** (`acknowledge`, select_multiple, required): Please select "I have read and understand this document." to confirm that you have read and understand the document. If you have attached all required documents, select "I have attached all required documents.".
* **file_1** (`file_1`, select_one, required): Select the required file 1 (Document 1).
* **file_2** (`file_2`, select_one, required): Select the required file 2 (Document 2).
* **file_3** (`file_3`, select_one, required): Select the required file 3 (Document 3).
* **confirm_email** (`confirm_email`, email, required): Confirm your email address.
* **confirm_phone** (`confirm_phone`, text, required): Confirm your phone number.
* **confirm_date** (`confirm_date`, date, required): Confirm the date.
* **confirm_time** (`confirm_time`, time, required): Confirm the time.
* **confirm_notes** (`confirm_notes`, note, required): Confirm any additional notes.
* **confirm_housing** (`confirm_housing`, text, required): Confirm any housing information.
* **confirm_address** (`confirm_address`, text, required): Confirm your address.
* **confirm_first_name** (`confirm_first_name`, text, required): Confirm your first name.
* **confirm_last_name** (`confirm_last_name`, text, required): Confirm your last name.
* **confirm_acknowledge** (`confirm_acknowledge`, select_multiple, required): Select "Confirmed" to confirm that you have read and understand the document.
* **confirm_acknowledge_all** (`confirm_acknowledge_all`, select_multiple, required): Select "Confirmed" to confirm that you have attached all required documents.
* **confirm_file_1** (`confirm_file_1`, select_multiple, required): Select "Confirmed" to confirm that you have attached the required file 1 (Document 1).
* **confirm_file_2** (`confirm_file_2`, select_multiple, required): Select "Confirmed" to confirm that you have attached the required file 2 (Document 2).
* **confirm_file_3** (`confirm_file_3`, select_multiple, required): Select "Confirmed" to confirm that you have attached the required file 3 (Document 3).

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Please attach the required documents to support your application.
* Please note that this form is subject to change, and the fields and options may change over time.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities for assistance.
