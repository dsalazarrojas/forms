# Law School Application Deadline Tracker - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Law School Application Deadline Tracker form helps track and monitor the status of law school applications.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form for each individual law school application**.
2. **Enter the relevant details for each application**, including the application status, deadline date and time, number of materials submitted, and submitted materials list.
3. **Save the completed form**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Status**: Select the current status of the application from the provided options: Application Received, Application Under Review, Interview Scheduled, Interviewed, Accepted, Rejected, Withdrawn.
* **Law School Name**: Enter the name of the law school being applied to.
* **Application Deadline**: Enter the deadline date and time for the application.
* **Application Completed**: Enter the number of applications completed.
* **Interview Scheduled**: Enter the date and time of the interview (if scheduled).
* **Interview Completed**: Enter the date and time of the interview (if completed).
* **Application Received**: Enter the date received by the law school.
* **Communication Status**: Select all relevant communication statuses with the law school (e.g., Accepted, Rejected, Under Review, Withdrawn).
* **Next Steps**: Select the next steps to be taken for the application (e.g., Send follow-up email, Review and update status, Send reminder email, etc.).
* **Submitted Materials**: Select the types of materials submitted to the law school (e.g., Resume, Transcripts, Letter of recommendation, Application essay, etc.).
* **Submitted Documents**: Select all relevant documents submitted for the application (e.g., Resume, Transcripts, Letter of recommendation, etc.).
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes on the application status.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the applicant.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the applicant.
* **Follow Up Email**: Select whether to send a follow-up email.
* **Interview Location**: Enter the location of the interview.
* **Interview Time**: Enter the time of the interview.
* **Interview Faculty**: Enter the faculty member's name conducting the interview.
* **Interview Faculty Response**: Select whether the faculty member has responded to the interview invitation.
* **Application Status Updated**: Enter the date and time the application status was updated.
* **User Name**: Enter the name of the user filling out the form.
* **User Email**: Enter the email address of the user filling out the form.
* **User Phone**: Enter the phone number of the user filling out the form.
* **Submitted Materials Description**: Enter a brief description of the materials submitted.
* **Notes Description**: Enter a brief description of the notes.

## Tips

* **Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly** to ensure accurate tracking of law school applications.
* **Save the completed form** for future reference.
