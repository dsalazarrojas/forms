# Beauty School Financial Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and document the student's financial agreement with the beauty school. It is essential to complete this form carefully, as it contains important information about the student's enrollment and payment plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the student's program of enrollment from the options listed.
2. Enter the student's full name.
3. Enter the student's ID number.
4. Select the preferred payment plan for the program.
5. Enter the agreed-upon monthly payment amount (if on an installment plan).
6. Enter the day of the month when the payment is due (if on an installment plan).
7. Sign the agreement, acknowledging that you understand the late payment fee and refund policy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Full Name**: Enter the full name of the student as it appears on their identification.
* **Student ID Number**: Enter the student's ID number as issued by the school.
* **Program of Enrollment**: Select the program the student is enrolling in (Cosmetology, Esthetics, Nail Technology, Hair Design, or Instructor Training).
* **Total Program Tuition**: Enter the total program tuition amount.
* **Non-Refundable Enrollment Fee**: Enter the non-refundable enrollment fee.
* **Preferred Payment Plan**: Select the preferred payment plan (Full Payment (Upfront), Monthly Installments, Bi-Weekly Payments, or Financial Aid / Loan).
* **Agreed Monthly Payment**: Enter the agreed-upon monthly payment amount (if on an installment plan).
* **Day of Month Payment is Due**: Enter the day of the month when the payment is due (if on an installment plan).
* **I understand that late payments will incur a 5% fee**: Acknowledge that you understand that late payments will incur a 5% fee.
* **I have read and agree to the refund policy**: Acknowledge that you have read and agree to the refund policy.
* **Name of Person Responsible for Payment**: Enter the name of the person responsible for payment (if different from the student).
* **Date signed**: Enter the date the agreement was signed.

## Tips

* Make sure to review the refund policy and understand the consequences of late payments before signing the agreement.
* Ensure that the student's name, ID number, and program of enrollment are accurate and up-to-date.
* If the student has a different person responsible for payment, ensure that this person is aware of the agreement terms.
* Review the payment plan carefully to avoid any misunderstandings or disputes.
