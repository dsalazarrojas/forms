# Awards Nomination 2 Red Theme And Responsive - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for an award nomination in the education sector.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the full name of the voter in the "Full Name of Voter" field.
2. Enter the email address of the voter in the "Email Address of Voter" field.
3. Select the school or affiliation of the voter in the "Voter School or Affiliation" field.
4. Choose the primary category for the nomination in the "Category to Vote In" field.
5. Enter the full name of the nominee in the "Nominee Full Name" field.
6. Indicate the grade or position of the nominee in the "Nominee Grade or Position" field.
7. Describe the reason for nominating the nominee in the "Reason for Voting" field.
8. Choose how long you have known the nominee in the "How long have you known the nominee?" field.
9. Rate the impact of the nominee on students from 1 to 5 in the "Impact on Students" field.
10. Rate the nominee's contribution to the school community from 1 to 5 in the "Impact on School Community" field.
11. Select the primary strength of the nominee in the "Primary Nominee Strength" field.
12. If desired, provide additional feedback or comments about the nominee in the "Additional Feedback or Comments" field.
13. Enter the date of the vote in the "Date of Vote" field.
14. Confirm your agreement to the official voting rules and regulations in the "I agree to the Official Voting Rules and Regulations" field.
15. Type your full name in the "Voting Confirmation" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name of Voter** (`Voter Name`, Text, Required): Enter your full name to identify yourself as the voter.
- **Email Address of Voter** (`Voter Email`, Email, Required): Enter your email address to contact you with any questions or follow-up information.
- **Voter School or Affiliation** (`School Affiliation`, Text, Required): Choose the school or institution you are affiliated with.
- **Category to Vote In** (`Category`, Select One, Required): Select one of the available categories for the nomination.
- **Nominee Full Name** (`Nominee Name`, Text, Required): Enter the full name of the person you are nominating.
- **Nominee Grade or Position** (`Nominee Grade`, Text, Required): Indicate the grade or position of the nominee.
- **Reason for Voting** (`Reason for Nomination`, Text, Required): Describe why you are nominating the individual.
- **How long have you known the nominee?** (`Duration`, Select One, Required): Choose the duration of your relationship with the nominee.
- **Impact on Students** (`Student Impact`, Select One, Required): Rate the impact of the nominee on students from 1 to 5.
- **Impact on School Community** (`School Community`, Select One, Required): Rate the contribution of the nominee to the school community from 1 to 5.
- **Primary Nominee Strength** (`Nominee Strength`, Select One, Required): Select the primary strength of the nominee.
- **Additional Feedback or Comments** (`Additional Feedback`, Text, Optional): Provide any other comments or feedback about the nominee.
- **Date of Vote** (`Vote Date`, Date, Required): Enter the date of the vote.
- **I agree to the Official Voting Rules and Regulations** (`Agreement`, Select One, Required): Confirm your agreement to the official rules and regulations.
- **Voting Confirmation** (`Confirmation`, Text, Required): Type your full name to confirm your vote.
