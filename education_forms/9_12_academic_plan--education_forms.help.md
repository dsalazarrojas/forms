# 9 12 Academic Plan - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a comprehensive 9-12 academic plan for a student. The plan outlines the student's academic goals, progress, and schedule for the upcoming year, including subject-specific credits, extracurricular activities, and accommodations required. This form is intended for students, parents, and educators to collaborate and ensure a smooth academic experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's full legal name in the "Student name" field.
2. Select the student's grade level (9-12) for the plan.
3. Choose the academic plan start and end dates (semester or school year).
4. Set the target total credits for the year, using decimals for half credits.
5. List the student's subjects, including the name and credits for each subject.
6. Select extracurricular activities the student plans to participate in.
7. Provide a list of core curriculum resources or textbooks used.
8. Mention any requested accommodations or modifications.
9. Enter a parent/guardian contact phone or email for plan questions.
10. Add any additional notes about the academic plan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Student & Plan Information
* **Student name** (`student_name`, `text`, required): Enter the student's full legal name used in records.
* **Grade level for this plan** (`student_grade_level`, `select_one`, required): Select the student's current grade level (9-12).
* **Academic plan start date** (`plan_start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the start date of the academic plan (semester or school year).
* **Academic plan end date** (`plan_end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the end date of the academic plan (semester or school year).
* **Target total credits for the year** (`total_credits_target`, `decimal`, required): Set the target total credits for the year, using decimals for half credits.
* **Subject 1** (`subject_1_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the first subject (e.g., Math).
* **Credits for Subject 1** (`subject_1_credits`, `decimal`, required): Set the credits for the first subject.
* **Subject 2** (`subject_2_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the second subject (e.g., Science).
* **Credits for Subject 2** (`subject_2_credits`, `decimal`, required): Set the credits for the second subject.
* **Additional subjects and credits** (`additional_subjects_summary`, `text`, optional): Provide a list of additional subjects and their credits separated by semicolons.
* **Extracurricular activities planned** (`extracurricular_activities`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all extracurricular activities the student plans to participate in.
* **Core curriculum resources or textbooks** (`learning_resources`, `text`, optional): List the core curriculum resources or textbooks used.
* **Requested accommodations or modifications** (`accommodations_requested`, `text`, optional): Mention any requested accommodations or modifications.
* **Parent/guardian contact** (`parent_contact`, `text`, required): Enter a parent/guardian contact phone or email for plan questions.
* **Additional notes** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional notes about the academic plan.

## Tips
* Use decimals for half credits.
* Provide semicolons to separate subjects and credits in the "Additional subjects and credits" field.
* Enter a parent/guardian contact phone or email.
* Use this form to collaborate with students, parents, and educators.
* Review and update the plan as needed throughout the year.
