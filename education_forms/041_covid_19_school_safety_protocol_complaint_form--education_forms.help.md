# COVID 19 School Safety Protocol Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help identify and document instances of potential COVID-19 safety protocol violations within schools.

## How To Complete This Form
Please provide the following information to help us investigate your concern:

- Fill out all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*).
- For "Relationship to School", select the option that best fits your affiliation with the school.
- Use the "Date of Incident or Observation" field to enter the date you witnessed or experienced the violation.
- If you are reporting on behalf of someone else, enter their full name(s) in the "Witness Names" field.
- For "Location of Violation", provide the specific area or room where the incident occurred.
- Enter a detailed description of the incident in the "Detailed Description of Violation" field.
- If applicable, select the type of evidence you have available in the "Evidence Available" field.
- Specify the resolution you would like to see for the matter in the "Desired Resolution" field.
- Enter your contact email address in the "Contact Email for Follow-up" field so we can reach out to you for additional information or clarification.
- Finally, confirm that the information you provided is true to the best of your knowledge by selecting "True" in the "I certify that the information in this complaint is true to the best of my knowledge" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Complainant Full Name**: Your name as the individual filing this complaint.
* **Relationship to School**: The category that best describes your affiliation with the school (Parent/Guardian, Student, Staff Member, Faculty, or Community Member).
* **School Name**: The name of the school in question.
* **Date of Incident or Observation**: The date on which you witnessed or experienced the violation.
* **Protocol Being Violated**: The specific safety protocol that was violated (Mask Wearing, Social Distancing, Hand Hygiene, Entry Screening, Sanitation/Cleaning, or Capacity Limits).
* **Location of Violation**: The specific area or room where the incident occurred (e.g. Classroom 101, Cafeteria, School Bus).
* **Detailed Description of Violation**: Provide a detailed account of what you witnessed or experienced.
* **Witness Names**: If you are reporting on behalf of someone else, list their full name(s) here.
* **Evidence Available**: Indicate if you have photos, videos, or a written statement to support your claim (None, Photos, Video, Written Statement).
* **Desired Resolution**: Specify the resolution you would like to see for the matter (e.g. increased enforcement, additional resources).
* **Contact Email for Follow-up**: Enter your email address so we can reach out to you for follow-up or clarification.
* **I certify that the information in this complaint is true to the best of my knowledge**: Confirm that the information provided is true to the best of your knowledge by selecting "True".
