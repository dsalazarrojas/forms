# COI Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COI form is used to collect information about your institutional responsibilities, interests, and potential conflicts of interest. It's essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure compliance with the institution's policies and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each field carefully and fill out the form step by step.
2. Provide accurate and truthful information for each field.
3. Review the form thoroughly before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (1, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
- **Email Address** (2, email, required): Enter your institutional email address.
- **Phone Number** (3, text, optional): Enter your best contact number.
- **Position Title** (4, text, required): Enter your position title within the institution.
- **Department** (5, text, required): Enter your department or division within the institution.
- **Institution** (6, text, required): Enter the name of your institution.
- **Reporting Period** (7, select_one, required): Select the time period this form covers.
  - Annual Disclosure 2026: For the current fiscal year.
  - New Hire Disclosure: For a new hire within the institution.
  - Event-Driven Disclosure: For a specific event or occurrence within the institution.
  - Update to Previous Disclosure: To update an existing disclosure.
- **Purpose of This Form** (8, note, optional): Enter the reason for submitting this form.
- **Do You Have Financial Interests** (9, select_one, required): Answer whether you have financial interests related to your institutional responsibilities.
  - True: Yes, I have financial interests.
  - False: No, I do not have financial interests.
- **Describe Financial Interests** (10, text, optional): If you have financial interests, provide details.
- **Do You Hold Outside Positions** (11, select_one, required): Answer whether you hold outside positions.
  - True: Yes, I hold outside positions.
  - False: No, I do not hold outside positions.
- **Describe Outside Positions** (12, text, optional): If you hold outside positions, provide details.
- **Do You Have External Research Funding** (13, select_one, required): Answer whether you receive external research funding.
  - True: Yes, I receive external research funding.
  - False: No, I do not receive external research funding.
  - Not Applicable: This option is for situations where you do not receive research funding.
- **Describe Research Funding** (14, text, optional): If you receive research funding, provide details.
- **Do You Have Intellectual Property** (15, select_one, required): Answer whether you have intellectual property with financial value.
  - True: Yes, I have intellectual property.
  - False: No, I do not have intellectual property.
- **Describe Intellectual Property** (16, text, optional): If you have intellectual property, provide details.
- **Potential Family Conflicts** (17, select_one, required): Answer whether you have potential family conflicts.
  - True: Yes, I have potential family conflicts.
  - False: No, I do not have potential family conflicts.
- **Describe Family Conflicts** (18, text, optional): If you have family conflicts, provide details.
- **Did You Receive Travel Reimbursements** (19, select_one, required): Answer whether you received travel reimbursements.
  - True: Yes, I received travel reimbursements.
  - False: No, I did not receive travel reimbursements.
- **Describe Travel Reimbursements** (20, text, optional): If you received travel reimbursements, provide details.
- **Are You Aware of Any Conflicts** (21, select_one, required): Answer whether you are aware of any conflicts.
  - True: Yes, I am aware of conflicts.
  - False: No, I am not aware of conflicts.
- **Describe Potential Conflicts** (22, text, optional): If you are aware of conflicts, provide details.
- **Do You Need a Management Plan** (23, select_one, required): Answer whether you need a management plan.
  - True: Yes, I need a management plan.
  - False: No, I do not need a management plan.
  - Unsure: I am unsure.
- **Certification Statement** (24, select_one, required): Certify that this disclosure is complete and accurate.
  - I Certify: This is my certification.
  - I Do Not Certify: This is not my certification.
- **Signature** (25, text, required): Type your full name as a signature.
- **Date Signed** (26, date, required): Enter the date you signed the form.
