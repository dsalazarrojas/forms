# Graduation Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from students applying for graduation. Please fill out this form if you are a student who is planning to graduate at the end of the current academic year.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the student name field with your full name.
2. Enter your graduation date in the format MM/DD/YYYY (month/day/year).
3. Select your current grade level from the options provided.
4. Choose the type of graduation you are applying for from the provided options.
5. Enter your address, school name, school phone number, and school email address.
6. Enter your email address and contact number.
7. If applicable, provide your parent contact information, guardian's name, email address, contact number, and emergency contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required): Your full name as a student.
* **Graduation Date** (`graduation_date`, `date`, required): The date you plan to graduate.
* **Graduation Level** (`grade_level`, `select_one`, required): The grade you are in currently.
* **Graduation Type** (`graduation_type`, `select_multiple`, required): The type of graduation you are applying for.
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, `text`, required): Your home address.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, required): The name of your school.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, `text`, required): The address of your school.
* **School Phone Number** (`school_phone_number`, `text`, required): The phone number of your school.
* **School Email Address** (`school_email_address`, `text`, required): The email address of your school.
* **Student Email Address** (`student_email_address`, `text`, required): Your personal email address.
* **Student Contact Number** (`student_contact_number`, `text`, required): Your personal contact number.
* **Parent Contact Information** (`parent_contact_information`, `text`, optional): If you have a parent or guardian with whom you would like to share your contact information.
* **Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, `text`, optional): If you have a guardian, their name.
* **Guardian Email Address** (`guardian_email_address`, `text`, optional): If you have a guardian, their email address.
* **Guardian Contact Number** (`guardian_contact_number`, `text`, optional): If you have a guardian, their contact number.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, optional): The contact information for an emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, optional): The phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, `text`, optional): The email address of your emergency contact.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check all entered information for accuracy.
* If you have any questions, please contact your school's administration office.
* If you are unsure about any field, try to use your best judgment to fill it out as accurately as possible.
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* If you need help with this form, contact [support email or phone number].
* Please review the submitted form for any errors before submitting it to your school.
