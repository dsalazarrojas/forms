# AI Technique Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess and record an assessor's opinion on various AI techniques, providing feedback on understanding, application, and complexity.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the instructions on the first page to ensure you understand the purpose of the form and the guidelines for completing it.
2. On each subsequent page, rate your understanding and application of the technique, providing a brief description of your experience.
3. If you have additional comments or suggestions, use the "Additional Assessment Notes" field to provide further feedback.
4. Finally, review your answers and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Assessment Instructions -- (1)**: Read the instructions on this page to understand the guidelines and purpose of the form.
* **Assessor Name (2)**: This is the name of the person completing the form. If not required, leave this field blank.
* **Technique Name (3)**: This is where you'll enter the name of the AI technique you're assessing, e.g., "Transformer models" or "Clustering".
* **Understanding Level (4)**: Rate your understanding of the technique, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest. This scale measures how well you think you understand the technique, based on your experience and knowledge.
* **Experience Applying the Technique (5)**: Select one of the options to indicate your level of experience applying this technique, from "None" to "Led implementations".
* **Perceived Complexity for Learners (6)**: Rate how difficult you think this technique is for others to learn, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Recommended Learning Resources (7)**: Suggest books, courses, or tutorials that you think would be helpful for others learning this technique.
* **Additional Assessment Notes (8)**: Leave any additional comments or suggestions you have about the technique, or provide further clarification if needed.

Remember, this form is designed to provide valuable feedback and insights for others who will be learning these techniques. Please answer honestly and accurately to help others understand and apply these techniques more effectively.
