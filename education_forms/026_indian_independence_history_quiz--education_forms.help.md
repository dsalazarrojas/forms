# Indian Independence History Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Indian Independence History Quiz is a form designed to gather information from participants about their knowledge of Indian history. This form is intended to collect data about the participant's answers to a series of historical questions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the questions carefully and answer them as accurately as possible.
2. Fill in the required fields (Event Date, Event Location, question_1, question_4, question_2, question_3).
3. If you are unsure about any question, you may choose not to answer it or skip it.
4. Once you have answered all the questions, submit the form.

* **Event Date** (`2`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the historical event.
* **Organizer Name** (`3`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide your name if you are the organizer of the event.
* **Participant Name** (`4`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide your name if you are participating in the event.

	+ Option 1

	+ Option 3
* **Participant Email** (`6`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address if you want to be contacted for any updates.
* **Event Location** (`7`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the options to answer this question. Please select:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **What is the name of the Indian national animal?** (`8`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose one or multiple options to answer this question. Please select:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Participant Phone** (`9`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted for any updates.
* **When was Indian Independence celebrated?** (`10`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the historical event.
* **Participant Notes** (`11`, `note`, required/optional): If you have any additional comments or notes, please provide them in this field.
