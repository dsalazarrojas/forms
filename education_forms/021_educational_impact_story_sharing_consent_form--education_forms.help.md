# Educational Impact Story Sharing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather consent for sharing educational impact stories. It ensures that individuals control how and when their stories are shared, and allows them to provide information for attribution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Please fill out the "story_info" field with a brief description of your story.
2. Select the ways in which you would like your story to be shared (e.g., Publicly, Privately, With educators, With the school community).
3. Choose the method of follow-up you would like to receive (e.g., Email, Call, In-person, None).
4. If you selected a follow-up method, please provide your email address in the "follow_up_email" field.
5. If you selected "In-person" follow-up, please enter your phone number in the "follow_up_phone" field.
6. Optionally, provide additional notes on how you would like to be recognized when sharing your story in the "sharing_consent_notes" field.
7. Confirm whether you would like to be attributed (named) in the "sharing_consent_attribution" field.
8. Confirm whether you would like your story to be shared publicly or not in the "sharing_consent_publicly" field.
9. Confirm whether you would like to receive follow-up in-person in the "follow_up_in_person" field.
10. If you chose to be attributed, provide your name, title, and institution in the "attribution_name", "attribution_title", and "attribution_institution" fields, respectively.
11. If you chose to be attributed, you can provide a URL where you can be found online in the "attribution_url" field.
12. If you chose to be attributed, you can provide an image or identifier that represents you in the "attribution_image" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **story_info** (`story_info`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide a brief description of your story.
* **attribution_preferences** (`attribution_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): This field allows you to select how you would like to be attributed when sharing your story. You can choose to be attributed, remain anonymous, or select other options.
* **sharing_consent** (`sharing_consent`, select_multiple, optional): This field allows you to select the ways in which you would like to share your story. You can choose to share it publicly, privately, with educators, or with the school community.
* **follow_up** (`follow_up`, select_one, optional): This field allows you to select the method of follow-up you would like to receive (e.g., Email, Call, In-person, None).
* **follow_up_email** (`follow_up_email`, email, optional): If you selected "Email" follow-up, please provide your email address here.
* **follow_up_phone** (`follow_up_phone`, text, optional): If you selected "In-person" follow-up, please enter your phone number here.
* **sharing_consent_notes** (`sharing_consent_notes`, text, optional): This field allows you to provide additional information on how you would like to be recognized when sharing your story.
* **sharing_consent_attribution** (`sharing_consent_attribution`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide your attribution information (e.g., name, title, institution).
* **sharing_consent_publicly** (`sharing_consent_publicly`, select_one, optional): This field allows you to confirm whether you would like your story to be shared publicly or not.
* **follow_up_in_person** (`follow_up_in_person`, select_one, optional): This field allows you to confirm whether you would like to receive follow-up in-person.
* **attribution_name** (`attribution_name`, text, optional): If you chose to be attributed, please provide your name here.
* **attribution_title** (`attribution_title`, text, optional): If you chose to be attributed, please provide your title here.
* **attribution_institution** (`attribution_institution`, text, optional): If you chose to be attributed, please provide your institution here.
* **attribution_url** (`attribution_url`, text, optional): If you chose to be attributed, please provide a URL where you can be found online here.
* **attribution_image** (`attribution_image`, text, optional): If you chose to be attributed, you can provide an image or identifier that represents you here.

## Tips

* Please fill out the fields accurately and truthfully.
* Be sure to check your email address and phone number for correct spelling and format.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us for assistance.
