# Education Course Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Education Course Discharge Form is used to formally close an educational course, capturing relevant information for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the course's title and basic information on the "Course Information" page.
2. Provide details about the student, including their status, on the "Student Information" page.
3. Select the reason for course discharge on the "Withdrawal Reason" page.
4. Update the student's status and course status on the corresponding pages.
5. Record the date and time of submission on the "Submission Date" and "Submission Time" pages.
6. Leave any additional feedback for the student on the "Student Feedback" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Information** (`course_info`, text, required): Enter the title and basic details of the course being discharged.
* **Student Information** (`student_info`, text, required): Enter the student's information, including their status.
* **Withdrawal Reason** (`withdrawal_reason`, text, optional): Provide a reason for discharging the course.
* **Student Status** (`student_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or both of the student's statuses: Active or Inactive.
* **Course Status** (`course_status`, select_one, optional): Select one of the course's statuses: Active or Inactive.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Record the date of the submission.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, optional): Record the time of submission.
* **Student Feedback** (`student_feedback`, note, optional): Leave any additional comments or feedback for the student.
