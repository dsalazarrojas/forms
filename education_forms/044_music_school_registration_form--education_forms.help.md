# Music School Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Music School Registration Form is a tool used to collect information from students registering for classes at the music school. This form is essential in creating accurate student records and facilitating communication between the school and students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Select your preferred class days by choosing one or more days from the "Preferred Class Days" dropdown menu.
3. Enter your start time for classes in the "Start Time" field.
4. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
6. If you have any additional information to provide, enter it in the "Additional Info" field.
7. Enter your date of birth in the "Student DOB" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Preferred Class Days** (`preferred_class_days`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more days you are available to attend classes (e.g., Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, etc.).
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you plan to start your classes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, note, optional): If you have any additional information to provide, enter it here.
* **Student DOB** (`student_dob`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or need clarification about any field, don't hesitate to ask the school staff.
* Review your information before submitting the form to ensure it is correct.
