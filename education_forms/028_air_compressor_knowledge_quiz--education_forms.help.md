# Air Compressor Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a knowledge quiz for employees working with air compressors, designed to test their understanding of compressor-related concepts and best practices.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by entering your employee information in the "Employee Information" section.
2. Proceed to the "Knowledge Quiz" section, where you will find questions about air compressors.
3. For each question, select the correct answer from the provided options.
4. Enter your response in the text field for question 8.
5. Once you have completed all questions, submit the form.
