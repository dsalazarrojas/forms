# Late Withdrawal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Late Withdrawal Form is used to facilitate the withdrawal process for students who are unable to complete their course requirements within the given timeframe. This form will help students and staff members to effectively manage the withdrawal process and ensure that all necessary information is captured.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's ID number in the "Student ID" field.
2. Provide the student's name in the "Name of Student" field.
3. Select the date of withdrawal in the "Date" field.
4. Enter the reason for withdrawal in the "Reason for Withdrawal" field.
5. Add any additional contact information in the "Contact Number" and "Email" fields.
6. Specify the department handling the withdrawal in the "Department" field.
7. Provide any additional details about the reason for withdrawal in the "Reason for Withdrawal Details" field.
8. Review and add any notes in the "Notes" field.
9. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Late Withdrawal Form** (`late_withdrawal_form`, object, Required): This is the main form header.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, Required): Enter the student's ID number.
* **Name of Student** (`name_of_student`, text, Required): Enter the student's name.
* **Date** (`date`, date, Required): Select the date of withdrawal.
* **Reason for Withdrawal** (`reason_for_withdrawal`, text, Required): Enter the reason for withdrawal.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, Required): Enter the student's contact number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, Required): Enter the student's email address.
* **Department** (`department`, text, Required): Specify the department handling the withdrawal.
* **Reason for Withdrawal Details** (`reason_for_withdrawal_details`, text, Required): Provide additional details about the reason for withdrawal.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, Required): Add any notes related to the withdrawal process.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, Required): Click this button to complete the form.
