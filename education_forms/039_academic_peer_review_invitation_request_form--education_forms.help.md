# Academic Peer Review Invitation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Peer Review Invitation Request Form is designed to collect information for inviting peer reviewers to review manuscripts for publication. This form is used to gather essential details from the requester, such as the manuscript title, subject area, and preferred review deadline.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form successfully, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your **Requester Name**: Provide your name as the author or representative submitting the request.
2.  Enter your **Contact Email**: Enter the email address you want the editor or reviewer to contact you for further details on the manuscript.
3.  Enter your **Manuscript Title**: Provide the title of the manuscript needing review, including any relevant versions (e.g., 'manuscript title v2').
4.  Specify the **Subject Area**: Choose the discipline or topic relevant to the manuscript's content.
5.  Select **Review Deadline**: Choose the date by which you need the review to be completed.
6.  Optionally, provide any **Confidentiality Requirements**: If there are specific confidentiality or embargo needs, include them here.
7.  Add **Additional Notes**: If there are any further instructions or details you'd like to pass on to the editor or reviewers, include them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Requester Name** (`requester_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person submitting this review request.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email where you can be contacted for further details.
*   **Manuscript Title** (`manuscript_title`, text, required): Provide the title of the manuscript to be reviewed.
*   **Subject Area** (`subject_area`, text, required): Select the discipline or topic relevant to the manuscript's content.
*   **Preferred Review Deadline** (`review_deadline`, date, optional): Choose the date by which you need the review completed.
*   **Confidentiality Requirements** (`confidentiality_requirements`, text, optional): Specify any special confidentiality or embargo needs.
*   **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Include any further instructions or details you'd like to pass on to the editor or reviewers.

## Tips
- Ensure the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
- Double-check that you're not duplicating a request for the same manuscript.
- If you have any doubts or concerns, reach out to the form's maintainers for assistance.
