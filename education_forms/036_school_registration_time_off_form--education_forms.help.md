# School Registration Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Registration Time Off Form is designed to collect student information for time off requests from school. This form is intended to be completed by students or their representatives to provide details about their absence from school due to time off requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Provide your student's date of birth in the "Student Date of Birth" field.
3. Enter your name in the "Parent Name" field, if applicable.
4. Describe the reason for your student's time off request in the "Reason for Request" field.
5. Enter the start and end dates of the time off in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields, respectively.
6. Enter the name of the school your student is registered in the "School Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Request Time Off** (`request_time_off`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the time off request.
- **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your student's name.
- **Student Date of Birth** (`student_date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your student's date of birth.
- **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): Enter your name, if you are the parent or guardian of the student.
- **Reason for Request** (`reason_for_request`, text, optional): Describe the reason for your student's time off request.
- **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the time off.
- **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the time off.
- **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the school your student is registered in. 

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are providing information on behalf of the student, ensure that you have the necessary permissions to do so.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
