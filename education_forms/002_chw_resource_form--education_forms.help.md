# CHW Resource Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CHW Resource Form is used to create and manage resources for Community Health Workers.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review and agree that the resource you are adding is not already listed in the system.
2. Fill in the required fields: Resource name, Resource category, and Service area.
3. Provide a brief description of the services offered, if desired.
4. Enter the name and contact information (phone number and email) of the primary contact person, if desired.
5. Enter the general contact email, if desired.
6. Optionally, provide information on regular hours of operation and referral instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Resource name**: Enter the name of the organization or service (e.g., "Local Food Bank").
* **Resource category**: Select the most relevant category for the resource (e.g., Food assistance).
* **Service area**: Enter the city or counties served by this resource (e.g., "City of New York").
* **Resource description**: Provide a brief description of the services offered (e.g., "Food assistance, grocery distribution, and nutrition counseling").
* **Contact person**: Enter the name of the primary contact person (e.g., "Jane Doe").
* **Resource phone**: Enter the main phone number of the resource (e.g., "555-123-4567").
* **Resource email**: Enter the general contact email (e.g., "foodbank@example.com").
* **Hours of operation**: Provide regular hours of operation or any scheduling notes (e.g., "Monday to Friday, 9am-5pm").
* **Referral instructions**: Enter steps to refer a client to this resource (e.g., "Call the phone number above to schedule an appointment").
