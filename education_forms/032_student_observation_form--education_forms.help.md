# Student Observation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Observation Form is designed to collect information about student performance, behavior, and progress. It helps teachers and educators to assess and document student performance, identify areas of strength and weakness, and provide insights for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the **Student Name** field.
2. Select the student's class from the **Class** dropdown menu.
3. Enter the student's grade level in the **Student Grade** field.
4. Describe the student's behavior in the **Student Behavior** field.
5. Enter the teacher's name in the **Teacher Name** field.
6. Choose the class size from the **Class Size** field.
7. Enter the observation date and time in the **Observation Date** and **Observation Time** fields.
8. Assess the student's progress in the **Student Progress** field.
9. Enter any comments or feedback in the **Comments** field.
10. Enter any recommendations for improvement in the **Recommendations** field.
11. Select the observation status as **Completed** or **Not Completed** in the **Observation Status** field.
12. Enter the evaluation date and time in the **Evaluation Date** and **Evaluation Time** fields.
13. Enter the name of the user assigned to complete the form in the **Form Assigned User** field.
14. Enter any additional class level information in the **Class Level** field.
15. If necessary, provide further details about the class level in the **Class Level Other** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter the student's name as it appears on their report card or ID card.
* **Class**: Select the student's class from the dropdown menu.
* **Student Grade**: Enter the student's current grade level (e.g., 3rd grade, 9th grade, etc.).
* **Student Behavior**: Describe the student's behavior during the observation, including any notable strengths or weaknesses.
* **Teacher Name**: Enter the name of the teacher who conducted the observation.
* **Class Size**: Choose the class size from the dropdown menu (e.g., Small, Medium, Large).
* **Observation Date**: Enter the date of the observation in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Observation Time**: Enter the time of the observation in the format HH:MM.
* **Student Progress**: Assess the student's progress as Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback about the student's performance.
* **Recommendations**: Enter any recommendations for improvement.
* **Observation Status**: Select Completed or Not Completed.
* **Evaluation Date**: Enter the date of the evaluation in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Evaluation Time**: Enter the time of the evaluation in the format HH:MM.
* **Form Assigned User**: Enter the name of the user assigned to complete the form.
* **Class Level**: Enter any additional class level information.
* **Class Level Other**: Provide further details about the class level if necessary.

By following this guide, you should be able to complete the Student Observation Form effectively and accurately. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to your team or supervisor for support.
