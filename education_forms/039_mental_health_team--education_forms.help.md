# Mental Health Team - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect demographic information from individuals seeking mental health services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with your accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed.
3. Review your information for any errors before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. What is your name
* **What is your name** (text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.

### 2. What is your email
* **What is your email** (email, required): Enter your valid email address for contact and communication purposes.

### 3. What is your phone number
* **What is your phone number** (text, required): Enter your correct phone number for us to contact you.

### 4. When is your appointment date
* **When is your appointment date** (date, required): Please select your appointment date with your mental health provider.

### 5. What are your symptoms
* **What are your symptoms** (text, required): Provide a detailed description of your symptoms for the mental health provider to understand.

### 6. What is your medical history
* **What is your medical history** (text, required): Please disclose your medical history, including any past mental health issues, medical conditions, or surgeries.

### 7. What are your medications
* **What are your medications** (text, required): List all your current medications, including dosage and frequency.

### 8. What is your mental health provider
* **What is your mental health provider** (text, required): Enter the name and contact information of your current mental health provider.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any information, please discuss it with your mental health provider.
* Remember to review your information carefully before submitting the form.
