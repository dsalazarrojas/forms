# Teacher Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teacher Feedback Form is a tool used to collect feedback from teachers, parents, and students about a teacher's performance. It helps administrators and school staff gather quantitative and qualitative data to evaluate the teacher's strengths and weaknesses and suggest areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Rate the teacher's performance on a scale (e.g., 1-5 or 1-10).
2.  Provide a brief description of your experience with the teacher (Student Feedback).
3.  Share your thoughts about the teacher's performance (Parent Feedback).
4.  Enter the date when the feedback was provided (Date Given).
5.  Add any additional comments or suggestions about the teacher's performance (Comments).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Teacher Performance Rating** (`teacher_performance_rating`, number, required: false):
    A numerical rating of the teacher's performance.
*   **Student Feedback** (`student_feedback`, text, required: false):
    A free-text comment from a student about their experience with the teacher.
*   **Parent Feedback** (`parent_feedback`, text, required: false):
    A free-text comment from a parent about the teacher's performance.
*   **Date Given** (`date_given`, date, required: false):
    The date when the feedback was provided.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false):
    Additional comments or suggestions about the teacher's performance.
