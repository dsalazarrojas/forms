# Writing Skills Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to assess the writing skills of students. The form consists of a series of questions and fields that ask students to submit their writing work, provide feedback, and enter contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by carefully reading the instructions and questions provided on each page of the form.
2. Answer each question and complete each field according to the instructions provided.
3. Make sure to provide detailed feedback on each writing task.
4. Enter your email address and phone number so that we can contact you with the results.
5. Enter the date and time you completed the form.
6. Add any additional comments or observations you have about your writing skills.
7. Finally, submit the form by selecting 'Yes' for each of the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header** (`Form Header`, note, required: false): This page provides a brief introduction to the form and its purpose.
* **Introduction Question** (`Introduction Question`, text, required: false): This field asks you to write a short introduction that explains the purpose and importance of the assessment.
* **Writing Task** (`Writing Task`, text, required: false): This field is where you will submit your writing work.
* **Writing Task 2** (`Writing Task 2`, text, required: false): This field is another chance for you to submit your writing work.
* **Introduction Question 2** (`Introduction Question 2`, text, required: false): This field asks you to write a short introduction that explains the purpose and importance of the second writing task.
* **Feedback 1** (`Feedback 1`, note, required: false): This field is where you provide detailed feedback on each writing task.
* **Feedback 2** (`Feedback 2`, note, required: false): This field is another chance for you to provide feedback on each writing task.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' to submit your assessment.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so that we can contact you with the results.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you with the results.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you completed the form.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, required: false): Enter the time you completed the form.
* **Form Footer** (`Form Footer`, note, required: false): This field is where you can enter any additional comments or observations.
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, text, required: false): This field is where you can enter any additional comments or observations.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' to submit your assessment.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so that we can contact you with the results.

Please note that some fields are required or not, as indicated by the required field flag in the form schema.
