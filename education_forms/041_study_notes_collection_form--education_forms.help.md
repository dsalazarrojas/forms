# study_notes_collection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The study notes collection form is used to capture study-related information from students or educators.
## How To Complete This Form
1. **Study Group**: Enter the group or group ID where the study session took place.
2. **Study Date**: Choose the date of the study session.
3. **Study Time**: Select the time of the study session.
4. **Study Note**: Enter any notes or comments about the study session.
5. **Study Tags (Multiple)**: Select all relevant tags related to the study (e.g., 'Yes', 'No', subject, level, lesson).
6. **Study Tags (Category)**: Select all relevant tags related to the category of the study (e.g., 'Yes', 'No', subject, level).
7. **Study Category**: Select whether the study is a category or not ('Yes', 'No', or Other).
8. **Study Educator**: Select whether the study is led by an educator ('Yes', 'No', or Other).
9. **Study Student**: Select which students were involved in the study ('Yes', 'No', or Other).
10. **Study Description**: Enter a brief description of the study session.
