# Information Mind Map Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Information Mind Map Form is designed to help you create mind maps for planning, brainstorming, and presenting complex ideas and projects. It guides you through a series of questions to help you structure your thoughts and visualize your concepts in a visually appealing way.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by entering your User Input, providing a brief description of your idea or project.
2. Answer each of the questions in the format provided, starting with "What is your goal?", "Describe your process?", etc.
3. Select "True" or "False" answers for the select_one fields as relevant to your mind map.
4. Choose multiple options or enter a date, time, or number in their respective fields.
5. Use the "User Input" fields to provide additional context or details.
6. Review and refine your mind map as you go through the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`user_input`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of your idea or project.
* **What is your goal?** (`question_1`, `text`, optional): Answer this question to clarify your objective.
* **Describe your process?** (`question_2`, `text`, optional): Outline the steps or methods you plan to use.
* **What are the key elements?** (`question_3`, `text`, optional): Identify the essential components of your project.
* **Mind Map** (`mind_map`, `note`, optional): A space to take notes and brainstorm ideas.
* **Is this correct?** (`mind_map_1`, `select_one`, optional): Determine the accuracy or relevance of your approach.
* **Select multiple options** (`mind_map_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose multiple alternatives or solutions.
* **Mind Map Date** (`mind_map_3`, `date`, optional): Record the date of your mind map.
* **Mind Map Time** (`mind_map_4`, `time`, optional): Record the time of your mind map.
* **Mind Map Number** (`mind_map_4_2`, `number`, optional): Enter a numerical value or code.
* **User Input 2** (`user_input_2`, `text`, optional): Add more context or details.
* **How do you feel?** (`question_4`, `text`, optional): Reflect on your emotions or sentiment.
* **What do you need?** (`question_5`, `text`, optional): Identify any resources or requirements.
* **How do you execute?** (`question_6`, `text`, optional): Describe the actions or steps you take.
* **Is this effective?** (`question_7`, `select_one`, optional): Evaluate the success or impact of your project.
* **User Input 3** (`user_input_3`, `text`, optional): Provide additional context or information.
* **How do you evaluate?** (`question_8`, `text`, optional): Describe how you assess progress or performance.
* **Question 9** (`question_9`, `text`, optional): Add more details or questions.
* **How do you perceive?** (`question_10`, `text`, optional): Consider your perspective or viewpoint.
* **What do you think?** (`question_11`, `text`, optional): Share your thoughts or insights.
* **What do you do?** (`question_12`, `text`, optional): Describe your approach or strategy.
* **How do you adapt?** (`question_13`, `text`, optional): Discuss your ability to adjust or innovate.
* **Is it a priority?** (`question_14`, `select_one`, optional): Determine its importance or relevance.
* **What is the outcome?** (`question_15`, `text`, optional): Outline the results or consequences of your project.
* **User Input 4** (`user_input_4`, `text`, optional): Provide more context or information.
