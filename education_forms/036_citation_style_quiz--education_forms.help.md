# Citation Style Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide helps you complete the Citation Style Quiz, a self-assessment form that tests your knowledge of different citation formats used in academic and professional writing.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form carefully and honestly to the best of your ability.
2. Make sure to choose the correct answer for each question.
3. If you need help or clarification, use the provided hints.
4. Review your answers before submitting to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or official documents.
* **Email Address** (`email`, required/optional): Enter your institutional email address or a valid contact email.
* **Grade Level or Class** (`grade_level`, required/optional): Select your level of education or current class:
	+ High school
	+ Undergraduate
	+ Graduate
	+ Professional
* **Commonly Used Citation Style in Humanities and Literature** (`question_1`, required/optional): Select the citation style commonly used in humanities and literature:
	+ APA
	+ MLA
	+ Chicago
	+ IEEE
* **APA Publication Date Position** (`question_2`, required/optional): Select where the publication date appears in an APA style citation:
	+ After the author name
	+ At the end of the citation
	+ At the beginning
	+ In parentheses after the title
* **What MLA Stands For** (`question_3`, required/optional): Select the correct abbreviation for the Modern Language Association:
	+ Modern Language Association
	+ Modern Literature Analysis
	+ Multilingual Learning Association
	+ Modern Legal Authority
* **Chicago Field Separator** (`question_4`, required/optional): Select what appears between the author name and publication year in Chicago style:
	+ A colon
	+ A comma
	+ A period
	+ An ampersand

	+ MLA
