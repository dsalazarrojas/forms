# Child Care Center Teacher Sign In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to record your daily activities, attendance, and other important details for administrative and operational purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Select Your Date of Attendance

*   In the "Date" field, select the date you are signing in.

### Step 2: Enter Your Sign In Time

*   In the "Sign in time" field, enter the time you started your shift.

### Step 3: Complete Your Personal Details

*   In the "Teacher name" field, enter your name as a teacher.
*   In the "Employee ID" field, enter your employee ID (if required).

### Step 4: Choose Your Classroom and Shift

*   Select your assigned classroom from the options provided in the "Classroom assigned" dropdown menu.
*   Choose your scheduled shift from the options in the "Scheduled shift" dropdown menu.

### Step 5: Provide Health and Status Information

*   Select your current health status from the options in the "Current health status" dropdown menu.
*   If you are currently ill or not feeling well, enter your symptoms or concerns in the "If ill, describe symptoms or concerns" field.

### Step 6: Confirm Your Expected Sign Out Time and Role

*   Enter the expected time you will sign out in the "Expected checkout time" field.
*   Choose your role for this shift from the options in the "Role for this shift" dropdown menu.

### Step 7: Record Any Schedule Changes or Issues

*   If there are any schedule changes or modifications, enter them in the "Any schedule changes or modifications today" field.

### Step 8: Provide Additional Details

*   If you have any special assignments or responsibilities, describe them in the "Special assignments or responsibilities" field.
*   If you are leading a special activity or project, select "True" in the "Leading special activity or project" field.
*   If you have any additional comments or activities, describe them in the "Activity description" field.
*   If there are any other teachers assisting you, enter their name in the "Co-teacher name (if applicable)" field.
*   If you have an approximate number of children present, enter it in the "Approximate number of children present" field.
*   If you have any supply needs or requests, enter them in the "Supply needs or requests" field.
*   If there are any maintenance or facility issues, describe them in the "Any maintenance or facility issues" field.

### Step 9: Finalize Your Sign-Out

*   Enter the time you actually signed out in the "Sign out time" field.
*   Add any additional attendance notes in the "Additional attendance notes" field.

## Tips

*   Ensure you complete all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
*   If you are unsure about any field, review the options or seek clarification from your supervisor.
*   Keep your entries clear and concise for easy understanding by others.
