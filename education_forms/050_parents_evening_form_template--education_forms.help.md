# Parents Evening Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form template is designed to help parents and teachers document and organize information about parent-teacher meetings, making it easier to keep track of details and outcomes. It's a flexible tool that allows users to input various aspects of the meeting, from basic information like meeting date and time to more important details like meeting outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the meeting details: Provide the date and time of the meeting, where it took place, and any notes about the discussion.
2. Enter parent and student information: Type in the parent's name, email, and student's name.
3. Complete the meeting outcomes section: Write down any important points or agreements made during the meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Details** (`meeting_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the meeting, including the date, time, and location.
* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, `date`, required: false): Input the date of the meeting in the format dd/mm/yy or dd-mm-yy.
* **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, `time`, required: false): Fill in the time of the meeting in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Parent's Name** (`parents_name`, `text`, required: false): Type in the name of the parent who attended the meeting.
* **Parent's Email** (`parent_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the parent.
* **Student's Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required: false): Type in the name of the student who was part of the meeting.
* **Teacher's Name** (`teacher_name`, `text`, required: false): Fill in the name of the teacher who conducted the meeting.
* **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`, `text`, required: false): Provide the location where the meeting took place.
* **Meeting Notes** (`meeting_notes`, `note`, required: false): Write down any notes or important details from the meeting.
* **Meeting Outcomes** (`meeting_outcomes`, `note`, required: false): Document any agreements, decisions, or outcomes from the meeting.

## Tips

* Make sure to input accurate and clear information for each field to ensure easy reference and follow-up.
* Use the notes section to record any important points or action items to be addressed.
* Review and update the form as needed to reflect any changes or progress made after the meeting.
