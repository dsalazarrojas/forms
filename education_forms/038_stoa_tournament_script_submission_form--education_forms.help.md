# Stoa Tournament Script Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants about their script submissions for the Stoa tournament. It helps the organizers to review and evaluate the submissions efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your participant information on page 1, including any necessary details about yourself.
2.  Choose the type of submission you are making (Script, Project, Presentation, or Other).
3.  Upload your submission file (if applicable).
4.  Attach your script file if it's required for your submission.
5.  Write a brief summary of your submission in the note field.
6.  Provide any additional details about your script in the note field.
7.  Select the submission date and time if you know it.
8.  Choose the submission time if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **participant information** (text): This field is for you to provide your participant information. Please fill it in with your name, email address, and any other relevant details.
* **submission type** (select_one): This field is to choose the type of submission you are making. Select from the options: Script, Project, Presentation, or Other.
* **submission file** (text): If you are uploading a file as part of your submission, please attach it here.
* **script file** (text): If your submission requires a script file, please attach it here.
* **submission** (note): This field is for you to briefly describe your submission. Please keep it concise and to the point.
* **script details** (note): If you have any additional details about your script, please write them here.
* **submission date** (date): If you know the submission date, please select it from the calendar.
* **submission time** (time): If you know the submission time, please select it from the time options.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields to avoid any errors in the review process.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, do not hesitate to reach out to the form creators at form@gic.mx.
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form, as it will be reviewed by the tournament organizers.
* If you have any concerns about the form or the submission process, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
