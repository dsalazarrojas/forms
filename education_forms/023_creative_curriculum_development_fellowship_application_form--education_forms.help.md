# creative_curriculum_development_fellowship_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Creative Curriculum Development Fellowship Application Form is for prospective fellows to apply for the program. Please note that each field is optional, unless otherwise stated, and you may choose to complete them all or partially.

## How To Complete This Form
To submit your application, please fill out the following form completely. Make sure to provide accurate and concise answers to each question, and use the following guidance for reference:

1. **First Name**:
	* This field is for your first name only.
	* Please enter your first name in plain text.
2. **Last Name**:
	* This field is for your last name only.
	* Please enter your last name in plain text.
3. **Email**:
	* This field is for your email address.
	* Please enter a valid email address in the format `username@example.com`.
4. **Phone**:
	* This field is for your phone number.
	* Please enter your phone number in plain text, including the country code.
5. **Educational Background**:
	* This field is for your educational history or qualifications relevant to the fellowship.
	* Please provide a brief description of your educational background.
6. **Project Title**:
	* This field is for the title of your project.
	* Please enter a title that accurately describes your project.
7. **Project Description**:
	* This field is for a detailed description of your project.
	* Please provide a clear and concise description of your project, including its goals, objectives, and expected outcomes.
8. **Project Link**:
	* This field is for a link to your project (e.g., website, GitHub repository).
	* Please enter the URL of your project if applicable.
9. **Resume**:
	* This field is for an optional attachment of your resume.
	* Please ensure the file is in a supported format (PDF or docx).
10. **Project Image**:
	* This field is for an optional image of your project.
	* Please ensure the image is relevant to your project.
11. **Project Video**:
	* This field is for an optional video showcasing your project.
	* Please ensure the video is relevant to your project.
12. **Project Audio**:
	* This field is for an optional audio file related to your project.
	* Please ensure the audio file is relevant to your project.
13. **Other**:
	* This field is for any additional information not covered by the previous fields.
	* Please provide a brief description of the information you are providing.
14. **Other 2**:
	* This field is for any additional information not covered by the previous fields.
	* Please provide a brief description of the information you are providing.
15. **Date of Birth**:
	* This field is for your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
	* Please enter your date of birth accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Enter your first name only.
- **Last Name**: Enter your last name only.
- **Email**: Enter a valid email address in the format `username@example.com`.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number in plain text, including the country code.
- **Educational Background**: Provide a brief description of your educational history or qualifications.
- **Project Title**: Enter the title of your project that accurately describes it.
- **Project Description**: Provide a clear and concise description of your project, including its goals, objectives, and expected outcomes.
- **Project Link**: Enter the URL of your project if applicable.
- **Resume**: Attach your resume in a supported format (PDF or docx).
- **Project Image**: Attach an image relevant to your project.
- **Project Video**: Attach a video showcasing your project.
- **Project Audio**: Attach an audio file related to your project.
- **Other**: Provide a brief description of any additional information not covered by the previous fields.
- **Other 2**: Provide a brief description of any additional information not covered by the previous fields.
- **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

## Tips
- Please ensure all fields are accurate and complete before submission.
- Use the correct format for dates (YYYY-MM-DD).
- Attach supported file formats for Resume, Project Image, Project Video, and Project Audio.
- Proofread your submission for grammar and spelling errors.
