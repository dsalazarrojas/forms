# Architectural Course Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for students to enroll in an architectural course. It collects information to ensure the student's eligibility for the course and to provide relevant information to the course instructors and staff.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information in the Student Information section, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide your date of birth.
3. If you have a student ID, enter it. If not, please answer "None" or leave it blank.
4. Select your current education level from the dropdown menu.
5. Enter your degree program (if applicable).
6. Enter your current GPA (if applicable).
7. In the Course Selection section, choose your desired course title, course level, semester, and enrollment year.
8. In the Prerequisites section, answer whether all prerequisites have been met or waived, and list any prerequisite courses you have completed (if applicable).
9. In the Learning Goals section, describe what you hope to learn from the course.
10. In the Financial Aid section, answer whether you need financial aid information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Student ID (if returning)**: If you are a returning student, enter your student ID number. If not, please answer "None" or leave it blank.
* **Current Education Level**: Select your current education level from the dropdown menu.
* **Degree Program**: Enter your degree program (if applicable).
* **Current GPA**: Enter your current GPA (if applicable).
* **Course Title**: Enter the title of the architectural course you want to enroll in.
* **Course Level**: Select the course level you want to enroll in.
* **Semester**: Select the semester you want to enroll in.
* **Enrollment Year**: Enter the enrollment year you are enrolling in.
* **All Prerequisites Met or Waived**: Answer whether all prerequisites have been met or waived.
* **Prerequisite Courses Completed**: List any prerequisite courses you have completed (if applicable).
* **Why Enrolling in this Course?**: Describe why you are enrolling in the course (academic requirement, career development, etc.).
* **What to Learn?**: Describe what you hope to learn from the course.
* **Financial Aid Needed**: Answer whether you need financial aid information.

Note: Please answer all required fields. If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask the course instructor or staff for assistance.
