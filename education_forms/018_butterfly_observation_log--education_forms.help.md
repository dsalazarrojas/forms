# Butterfly Observation Log - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Butterfly Observation Log form is a tool for reporting and recording butterfly sightings, observations, and behaviors. It is designed for use by individuals in educational, research, or conservation settings to collect relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form in its entirety. Each section is designed to gather specific information, so please ensure that you answer all required fields.
2. Review each section carefully before submitting the form to ensure that your information is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Observer Name** (`observer_name`):
Your full name

*This field is used to identify the person reporting the sighting.*

### 2. **Email Address** (`observer_email`):
Your email address

*This field is used for communication and contact purposes.*

### 3. **Organization or School** (`organization`):
Name of the affiliated organization or school

*This field is optional and used to attribute your sighting to a specific institution.*

### 4. **Observation Date** (`observation_date`):
Date of the butterfly sighting

*Use the format `MM/DD/YYYY` for the date.*

### 5. **Observation Time** (`observation_time`):
Time of the sighting

*Use the 24-hour format `HH:MM` for the time.*

### 6. **Location Name** (`location_name`):
Name of the observation site

*Be as specific as possible, including the general area or site.*

### 7. **GPS Coordinates** (`location_coordinates`):
Latitude and Longitude if available

*Use the format `latitude: 00.0000, longitude: 00.0000`.*

### 8. **Weather Conditions** (`weather_conditions`):
Describe the weather conditions during the sighting

*Be as specific as possible, including any notable weather events.*

### 9. **Temperature** (`temperature`):
Temperature in degrees Fahrenheit or Celsius

*Be as accurate as possible.*

### 10. **Butterfly Species Observed** (`butterfly_species`):
Identify the species if known

*If unsure, you can also report the genus or family.*

### 11. **Number of Butterflies** (`butterfly_count`):
How many butterflies were observed

*Count the total number of individuals.*

### 12. **Butterfly Behavior** (`butterfly_behavior`):
Describe the behavior of the butterflies during the sighting

*Select all that apply.*

*   Feeding
*   Flying
*   Mating
*   Resting
*   Basking
*   Other

### 13. **Wing Condition** (`wing_condition`):
General state of the wings

*Select the most accurate option.*

*   Perfect
*   Good
*   Fair
*   Damaged
*   Unknown

### 14. **Habitat Description** (`habitat_description`):
Describe the surrounding environment

*Be as specific as possible.*

### 15. **Host Plants** (`host_plants`):
List any plants that the butterflies use

*Be as specific as possible.*

### 16. **Additional Notes** (`observations_notes`):
Any other relevant observations or comments

*Be as detailed as possible.*

### 17. **Photo Attached** (`photo_attached`):
Whether you took a photo during the sighting

*Be honest, but only report a photo if you actually took one.*
