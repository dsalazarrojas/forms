# Online Instructors Mini Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate an instructor's performance and provide feedback for improvement. Please use this form to assess an instructor's ability to meet course objectives, provide clear and concise instructions, and demonstrate effective teaching methods.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the instructor being evaluated.
2. Select the date of the evaluation.
3. Choose a rating for the instructor's performance (optional).
4. Provide feedback on the instructor's teaching methods and methods of communication.
5. Add any additional comments or suggestions for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Instructor Name**: Enter the name of the instructor being evaluated.
* **Evaluation Date**: Select the date of the evaluation.
* **Instructor Rating**: Choose a rating for the instructor's performance. The rating options are:
	+ Not Satisfactory: The instructor did not meet expectations.
	+ Satisfactory: The instructor met expectations.
	+ Exceeds Expectations: The instructor exceeded expectations.
* **Feedback**: Provide feedback on the instructor's teaching methods and methods of communication.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or suggestions for improvement.
* **Rating Scale**: Enter a numerical rating for the instructor's performance (optional).

## Tips

* Be specific and concrete when providing feedback and comments.
* Use the rating scale only if you have a clear numerical assessment of the instructor's performance.
* If you have any concerns or issues with the instructor, be sure to include them in the feedback and comments sections.
