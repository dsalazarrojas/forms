# College Supplemental Recommendation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The College Supplemental Recommendation Request Form is a document that requests a recommender to provide a letter of recommendation for a student applying to college.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Make sure to provide all necessary details to help the recommender understand the request.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it to the recommender.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (Student Name, text, required): Fill in the name of the student who is requesting the recommendation.
* **Student Email Address** (Student Email Address, email, required): Enter the student's email address where the recommender can send the recommendation.
* **Student Phone Number** (Student Phone Number, text, required): Enter the student's phone number for easy contact.
* **Current High School or Institution** (Current High School or Institution, text, required): Enter the name of the current high school or institution the student is attending.
* **Year in School or Class** (Year in School or Class, select_one, required): Select the year the student is in (Junior, Senior, Undergraduate, Graduate).
* **Recommender Name** (Recommender Name, text, required): Fill in the name of the person providing the recommendation.
* **Recommender Title or Position** (Recommender Title or Position, text, required): Enter the title or position of the recommender.
* **Recommender Email Address** (Recommender Email Address, email, required): Enter the recommender's email address where the recommendation will be sent.
* **Recommender Phone Number** (Recommender Phone Number, text, required): Enter the recommender's phone number for easy contact.
* **School or Institution** (School or Institution, text, required): Enter the name of the school or institution where the recommender works.
* **Describe Your Relationship with Recommender** (Describe Your Relationship with Recommender, text, required): Explain how you know the recommender and in what context.
* **College or University Applying To** (College or University Applying To, text, required): Enter the name of the college or university the student is applying to.
* **Recommendation Submission Deadline** (Recommendation Submission Deadline, date, required): Enter the deadline for submitting the recommendation.
* **Type of Supplemental Recommendation** (Type of Supplemental Recommendation, select_one, required): Select the type of recommendation (Academic, Extracurricular Activity, Athletic, etc.).
* **Focus or Topic for Recommendation** (Focus or Topic for Recommendation, text, required): Explain what the recommender should focus on when writing the recommendation.
* **Key Strengths to Highlight** (Key Strengths to Highlight, text, optional): If applicable, enter the key strengths the recommender should highlight.
* **Context or Background Information for Recommender** (Context or Background Information for Recommender, text, optional): If applicable, provide additional context or background information for the recommender.
* **Submission Method Requested** (Submission Method Requested, select_one, required): Select how the recommender should submit the recommendation (Email, Online Portal, Sealed Envelope, etc.).
* **Submission Instructions** (Submission Instructions, text, optional): If applicable, provide specific instructions for submitting the recommendation.
* **Student Signed FERPA Waiver** (Student Signed FERPA Waiver, select_one, required): Select whether the student has signed the FERPA waiver.
* **Additional Context or Special Circumstances** (Additional Context or Special Circumstances, text, optional): If applicable, provide any additional context or special circumstances.
* **Request Follow-Up Reminder** (Request Follow-Up Reminder, select_one, required): Select whether a reminder should be sent to the recommender.

Please review and submit the form carefully before sending it to the recommender. They will provide their recommendation based on the information provided.
