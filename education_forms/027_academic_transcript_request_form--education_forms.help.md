# Academic Transcript Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assist students in requesting their academic transcript. It gathers necessary information for processing and delivery of the transcript. Please ensure you provide accurate and complete information to avoid delays or issues with your transcript request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your full name, as it appears on your records.
2. Provide your date of birth and student ID number for identification purposes.
3. Enter your email address and phone number for contact information.
4. Select your current status as a student (current, alumni, or former student).
5. Specify the dates of attendance at the institution.
6. Choose the degree program or major you are enrolled in.
7. Select the type of transcript you require (official or unofficial).
8. Indicate the number of copies you need.
9. Choose your preferred delivery method (mail, email, pick-up in person, or secure credential service).
10. Select the purpose of your transcript request (e.g., graduate school application, employment, etc.).
11. Choose the processing speed you require (standard or rush).
12. Confirm your consent to release your transcript information.
13. Finally, select the date you submit this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full name (as it appears on records)** (`requester_full_name`, required): Your full name as recorded by the institution.
- **Previous name (if records are under a different name)** (`previous_name`, optional): If your name has changed since your enrollment, provide your previous name.
- **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, required): Your date of birth for identification purposes.
- **Student ID number** (`student_id`, required): Your student ID number for identification purposes.
- **Email address** (`requester_email`, required): Your email address for contact information.
- **Phone number** (`requester_phone`, required): Your phone number for contact information.
- **Current status** (`current_status`, required): Select your current status as a student (current, alumni, or former student).
- **Dates of attendance** (`dates_of_attendance`, required): Specify the dates of your attendance at the institution (e.g., August 2019 - May 2023).
- **Degree program or major** (`degree_program`, required): Choose the degree program or major you are enrolled in.
- **Type of transcript** (`transcript_type`, required): Select the type of transcript you require (official or unofficial).
- **Number of copies requested** (`number_of_copies`, required): Indicate the number of copies you need.
- **Delivery method** (`delivery_method`, required): Choose your preferred delivery method (mail, email, pick-up in person, or secure credential service).
- **Delivery address or recipient email** (`delivery_address`, optional): This field is required for mail or email delivery.
- **Purpose of transcript request** (`purpose`, required): Select the purpose of your transcript request (e.g., graduate school application, employment).
- **Processing speed** (`processing_speed`, required): Choose the processing speed you require (standard or rush).
- **Consent** (`consent`, required): Confirm your consent to release your transcript information.
- **Date of request** (`request_date`, required): Select the date you submit this form.
