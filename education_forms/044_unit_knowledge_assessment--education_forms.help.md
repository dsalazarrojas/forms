# Unit Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help students or trainees assess their own knowledge and understanding of a unit of study after completion.

## How To Complete This Form
- Begin by reading the questions carefully and providing thoughtful, thorough answers.
- For each question, provide a concise and clear response that demonstrates your understanding of the unit material.
- If you're unsure about the answer to a question, try to explain why or how you would approach it.
- Take your time, and don't worry about running out of space or time.
- You can add or remove pages as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a text input field where you can briefly describe the purpose and context of the unit.
* **What is the purpose of this unit?**: This question is asking you to summarize the main objective of the unit. 
* **What are the key concepts?**: Describe the most important ideas and concepts that are covered in the unit.
* **How confident are you in your answers?** (Confidence level 1): Choose how confident you are in your answers, from "Very confident" to "Not at all confident."
* **Confidence level** (Confidence level 2): This field is similar to the previous one, but with another question asking about confidence.
* **Reflective comments**: This is an open-ended text field where you can reflect on your answers and provide any additional thoughts or comments.
* **What are the most important topics in this unit?**: This question is asking you to identify the most significant topics covered in the unit.
* **What are the key concepts covered?**: Another question asking you to describe the most important ideas in the unit.
* **How do you apply the concepts in this unit to real life?**: This question is asking you to think about how you would use the concepts from the unit in real-world scenarios.
* **What do you think the teacher/trainer should know about this unit?**: Suggest any information or insights that you think the instructor should be aware of about the unit.
* **What should students know about this unit?**: Identify any essential information that students should be aware of about the unit.
* **What should students do with this unit?**: Consider any actions or steps students should take when engaging with the unit.
