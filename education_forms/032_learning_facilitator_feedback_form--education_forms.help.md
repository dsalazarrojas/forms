# Learning Facilitator Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from learning facilitators, such as educators or instructors, to improve teaching methods, materials, and overall learning outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Start a new submission**: Begin by starting a new submission.
2.  **Fill in fields as needed**: Fill in the required and optional fields as they apply to your experience.
3.  **Submit your feedback**: Once you've filled in all applicable fields, submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **learning_facilitator_feedback_form**: This is the main field where you will provide your feedback about your experience as a learning facilitator. Please be as detailed and specific as possible.
- **teacher_name**: This field is for your name as a learning facilitator.
- **teacher_email**: This field is for your email, which will be used to contact you for any further information or clarification.
- **course_name**: This field is for the name of the course you are providing feedback about.

## Tips
- Be as honest and detailed as possible when filling in the learning facilitator feedback form.
- Only complete this form if you are a learning facilitator providing feedback about your own experiences.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
