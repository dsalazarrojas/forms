# Kindergarten Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from teachers on the progress of kindergarten students, helping to identify areas of strength and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the Kindergarten Evaluation Form field on page 1.
2. Move on to the Progress Evaluation field on page 2, where you can add your observations and comments on the student's progress.
3. On page 3, fill out the Student General Progress field with any relevant information about the student's overall development.
4. In the Teacher Comments field on page 4, you can provide any additional feedback or comments about the student.
5. Finally, add any additional comments or feedback about the form itself in the Kindergarten Evaluation Form Comments field on page 5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Kindergarten Evaluation Form** (`kindergarten_evaluation_form`, text, optional):
	+ This is the main field where you can provide your overall evaluation of the student's performance in the kindergarten setting.
* **Progress Evaluation** (`progress_evaluation`, text, optional):
	+ This field allows you to provide specific details about the student's progress in different areas, such as social, emotional, or cognitive development.
* **Student General Progress** (`student_general_progress`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for providing an overview of the student's overall progress and development in the kindergarten setting.
* **Teacher Comments** (`teacher_comments`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for providing additional feedback or comments about the student's performance and areas for improvement.
* **Kindergarten Evaluation Form Comments** (`kindergarten_evaluation_form_comments`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for any additional comments or feedback about the form itself, such as suggestions for improvement or any issues you've encountered while using it.

## Tips
* Please be as specific and detailed as possible when filling out the form to provide valuable feedback.
