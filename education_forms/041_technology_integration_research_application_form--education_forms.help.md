# Technology Integration Research Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users interested in conducting research with our institution. Please complete the form accurately and thoroughly to help facilitate a smooth review and approval process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

* Select one or more institutions to apply to.
* Select a review committee (if applicable).
* Enter the title of your research proposal.
* Choose the type of technology research proposal.
* Describe your research proposal in the field provided.
* Describe the technology use in your proposal.
* Enter your institution's email address.
* Enter your institution's phone number (if applicable).
* Enter your review committee's email address (if applicable).
* Enter your review committee's phone number (if applicable).
* Attach relevant research proposal files to the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select an Institution**: Select one or more institutions to apply to.
* **Select a Review Committee**: Select a review committee (if applicable) to ensure your research proposal is reviewed by the relevant committee.
* **Research Title**: Enter the title of your research proposal in this field.
* **Research Proposal Type**: Choose the type of technology research proposal you are submitting.
* **Research Description**: Describe your research proposal in this field.
* **Technology Use**: Describe the use of technology in your proposal.
* **Institution Email**: Enter your institution's email address.
* **Institution Phone**: Enter your institution's phone number (if applicable).
* **Review Committee Email**: Enter your review committee's email address (if applicable).
* **Review Committee Phone**: Enter your review committee's phone number (if applicable).
* **Proposal Files**: Attach relevant research proposal files to the form.
* **Additional Proposal Files**: Attach any additional files related to your research proposal.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Attach only relevant and required proposal files.
* Duplicate applications will not be processed.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for further assistance.
