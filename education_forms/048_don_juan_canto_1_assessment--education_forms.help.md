# Don Juan Canto 1 Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Don Juan Canto 1 Assessment form is used to collect information and feedback from students and teachers regarding the assessment activity. The form is designed to gather data on student performance, understanding, and feedback, as well as teacher assessment and grading systems.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your student name.
2. Provide your reading comprehension score.
3. Indicate your understanding of major characters.
4. Select all the major themes you understand.
5. Write a reflection on your understanding.
6. Rate the difficulty of the assessment.
7. Provide feedback on your experience.
8. Enter your teacher's feedback (if applicable).
9. Choose the grading system used.
10. Enter the submission date and time.
11. Enter your student email.
12. Enter your teacher's email.
13. Enter your phone number.
14. Indicate the time spent on the assessment.
15. Indicate the time spent in minutes.
16. Select all locations where you completed the assessment.
17. Enter the teacher's name.
18. Enter the teacher's location.
19. Select the time zone.
20. Enter any justification for the grading system used (if applicable).
21. Enter your student IP address.
22. Enter your teacher IP address.
23. Indicate the time of submission.
24. Enter the time of completion (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`Student Name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Reading Comprehension** (`reading comprehension`, number, required): Provide your score out of a scale of 1-100.
* **Understanding of Major Characters** (`understanding of major characters`, select_one, required): Rate your understanding of major characters as Excellent, Average, Below Average, or Very Low.
* **Understanding of Major Themes** (`understanding of major themes`, select_multiple, required): Select all major themes you understand.
* **Reflection** (`reflection`, note, required): Write a brief reflection on your understanding.
* **Difficulty Rating** (`difficulty rating`, number, required): Rate the difficulty of the assessment out of 100.
* **Student Feedback** (`student feedback`, text, optional): Provide any feedback on your experience.
* **Teacher Feedback** (`teacher feedback`, text, optional): Provide any feedback on your experience if applicable.
* **Grading System** (`grading system`, select_one, optional): Select the grading system used.
* **Submission Date** (`submission date`, date, required): Enter the date and time you submitted the assessment.
* **Student Email** (`student email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher email`, email, required): Enter your teacher's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Time Spent** (`time spent`, time, required): Indicate the time you spent on the assessment.
* **Time Spent in Minutes** (`time spent in minutes`, number, required): Indicate the time spent in minutes on the assessment.
* **Student Location** (`student location`, select_multiple, required): Select all locations where you completed the assessment.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher name`, text, required): Enter your teacher's name.
* **Teacher Location** (`teacher location`, text, required): Enter your teacher's location.
* **Time Zone** (`time zone`, select_one, optional): Select the time zone.
* **Grading System Justification** (`grading system justification`, note, optional): Enter any justification for the grading system used if applicable.
* **Student IP Address** (`student ip address`, text, required): Enter your student IP address.
* **Teacher IP Address** (`teacher ip address`, text, optional): Enter your teacher's IP address if applicable.
* **Time of Submission** (`time of submission`, time, required): Indicate the time of submission.
* **Time of Completion** (`time of completion`, time, optional): Indicate the time of completion if applicable.
* **Submission Date and Time** (`submission date and time`, date, required): Enter the submission date and time.
