# Student Essay Upload Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Essay Upload Form is a tool for students to submit their essays in a structured and organized manner. It's used to collect information about the essay title, file upload, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the essay title in the "Essay Title" field.
2. Upload the essay file by clicking on the "Essay File" field and selecting the file from your computer.
3. Enter your student name in the "Student Name" field.
4. Enter your student ID in the "Student ID" field.
5. Select the upload date and time in the "Upload Date" and "Upload Time" fields.
6. Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you have read and understood the instructions.
7. Select the relevant section from the list provided in the "Section" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Essay Title** (`essay_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title of your essay. This will help administrators identify your essay and ensure that it's properly labeled.
- **Essay File** (`essay_file`, text, optional): Upload your essay file by clicking on this field and selecting the file from your computer. Make sure to select a file with the correct format and size.
- **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your university ID or student record.
- **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, optional): Enter your university ID or student record number.
- **Upload Date** (`upload_date`, date, optional): Select the date when you uploaded your essay.
- **Upload Time** (`upload_time`, time, optional): Select the time when you uploaded your essay.
- **Grade** (`student_grade`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you have read and understood the instructions. Select "Yes" if you have, and "No" if you haven't.
- **Section** (`student_section`, select_one, optional): Select the relevant section from the list provided. This will help administrators categorize and prioritize your essay.
- **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, optional): Click this button to submit your essay and complete the form.
