# Curriculum Developer Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from new curriculum developers to aid in their onboarding process. It helps in streamlining the onboarding experience by collecting all necessary details in one place.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form accurately, follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal details such as your first name, last name, and email address.
2. Select your role within the organization, which is either Curriculum Developer, Education Coordinator, or Team Lead.
3. If you are a Team Lead, fill out the team details such as team name, title, email, and phone number.
4. List your relevant skills and certifications.
5. Select the onboarding status and dates.
6. Write any additional notes or comments if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your given name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
* **Role**: Select your role within the organization.
* **Team Name**: If you are a Team Lead, enter your team name.
* **Department**: Enter your department name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Time of Birth**: Enter your time of birth.
* **Contact Number**: If you are a Team Lead, enter your contact number.
* **Onboarding Expectations**: Write any special onboarding expectations you have.
* **Education Background**: Write your education background.
* **Work Experience**: Write your work experience.
* **Skills**: Select all relevant skills you possess.
* **Certifications**: Select all relevant certifications you hold.
* **Onboarding Dates**: Enter your start and end dates of onboarding.
* **Onboarding Status**: Select your current onboarding status.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Team Lead Name**: If you are a Team Lead, enter your name.
* **Team Lead Title**: If you are a Team Lead, enter your title.
* **Team Lead Email**: If you are a Team Lead, enter your email.
* **Team Lead Phone**: If you are a Team Lead, enter your phone number.
* **Education Team**: If you are a Team Lead, enter your team name.
* **Team Lead Address**: If you are a Team Lead, enter your address.
* **Education Manager**: Enter your education manager's name.
* **Education Manager Email**: Enter your education manager's email.

Note: The fields marked as required are essential to complete for the onboarding process.
