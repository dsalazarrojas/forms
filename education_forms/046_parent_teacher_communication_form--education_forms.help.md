# Parent Teacher Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by parents to communicate with teachers and express their needs and concerns regarding their child's education. It is designed to facilitate collaboration and mutual understanding between parents and teachers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each section as instructed, providing your feedback, comments, and preferences for communication and involvement in your child's education.
2. Choose the frequency of communication with the teacher that suits you best.
3. Specify how you would like to get involved in your child's education.
4. Provide your contact information so the teacher can reach out to you.
5. Provide the teacher's contact information so you can stay in touch with them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Feedback on Student Progress** (`student_progress`, `text`, optional): This field is for you to give feedback on your child's progress and performance in school.
* **Parent Comments and Concerns** (`concerns`, `text`, optional): This field is for you to express any concerns or comments you have about your child's education.
* **How Often Would You Like to Communicate with the Teacher** (`communication_frequency`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose how often you would like to communicate with the teacher, such as Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly, or As Needed.
* **How Often Would the Teacher Communicate with You** (`teacher_communication`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose how often you would like the teacher to communicate with you.
* **How Would You Like to Get Involved in Your Child's Education** (`parent_involvement`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose how you would like to get involved in your child's education, such as volunteering in the classroom or participating in school events.
* **How Would You Like the Teacher to Get Involved in Your Child's Education** (`teacher_involvement`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose how you would like the teacher to get involved in your child's education.
* **Parent Contact Information** (`parent_information`, `text`, optional): Provide your email, phone number, or any other contact information so the teacher can reach out to you.
* **Teacher Contact Information** (`teacher_information`, `text`, optional): Provide the teacher's email, phone number, or any other contact information so you can stay in touch with them.
