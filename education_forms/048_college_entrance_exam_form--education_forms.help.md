# College Entrance Exam Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The College Entrance Exam Form is a collection of questions designed to gather essential information from potential college students to ensure a smooth and fair testing experience. This form is intended for students who wish to take a college entrance exam.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately with your personal details.
2. Make sure to enter the required information for each field, marked as "required".
3. Select the correct options for select-type questions.
4. Ensure all questions are answered before submitting the form.
5. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`applicant_first_name`, text, required): Enter your given name.
* **Last Name** (`applicant_last_name`, text, required): Enter your family name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required): Select your gender from the provided options.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your primary email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Street Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your complete street address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the name of your city.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter the state or province you reside in.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip`, text, required): Enter your postal code.
* **Country** (`country`, select_one, required): Select your country of residence from the provided options.
* **High School Name** (`high_school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your current or recent high school.
* **High School Address** (`high_school_address`, text, required): Enter the address of your high school.
* **Expected Graduation Date** (`graduation_date`, date, required): Enter the date you expect to graduate from high school.
* **Exam Type** (`exam_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of exam you wish to take.
* **Preferred Exam Date** (`exam_date`, date, required): Select a date to take the exam.
* **Preferred Exam Location** (`exam_location`, select_one, required): Select a testing center or location where you wish to take the exam.
* **Preferred Exam Time** (`exam_time`, select_one, required): Select a time of day to take the exam.
* **Testing Accommodations Needed** (`accommodations_needed`, select_one, required): If you require accommodations, select "True".
* **Type of Accommodations** (`accommodations_type`, select_multiple, optional): If you selected "True", select the type of accommodations you need.
* **Accommodations Documentation** (`accommodations_documentation`, text, optional): Upload supporting documentation for your accommodations.
* **Current GPA** (`gpa`, number, optional): Enter your current unweighted GPA.
* **Class Rank** (`class_rank`, text, optional): If available, enter your class rank.
* **SAT Score** (`sat_score`, number, optional): If you've already taken the SAT, enter your score.
* **ACT Score** (`act_score`, number, optional): If you've already taken the ACT, enter your score.
* **Intended Major** (`intended_major`, text, optional): Enter the field of interest you plan to pursue.
* **Exam Fee Payment** (`exam_fee`, text, required): Enter the exam fee amount.
* **Fee Waiver Request** (`fee_waiver`, select_one, required): Select "True" if you require a fee waiver.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select the payment method you will use.
* **Upload Photo** (`photo_upload`, text, required): Upload a photo for exam identification purposes.
* **ID Type** (`id_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of ID you will bring.
* **ID Number** (`id_number`, text, required): Enter your ID number.
* **Will You Use a Calculator** (`calculator_use`, select_one, optional): If allowed, select "True".
* **Have You Taken Exam Prep Courses** (`prep_courses`, select_one, optional): If applicable, select "True" or "Planning To".
* **Number of Previous Exam Attempts** (`previous_attempts`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the number of attempts.
* **How Did You Hear About This Exam** (`how_heard`, select_one, required): Select how you heard about the exam.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_accepted`, select_one, required): Select "Yes I Accept" to agree to exam policies.

## Tips

* Ensure accuracy and completeness of information to avoid delays or issues.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
* Contact the exam administrators if you have any questions or concerns.
* This form is designed to be completed by the exam applicant themselves.
