# exam_results_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The exam results release form is used to provide exam results to students. It is a crucial process for students to access their results after taking exams.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have the necessary permissions to access this form.
2. Confirm that you are not duplicating this process for a student who has already been released their results.
3. Start by entering the student's name in the first field, "Student Name".
4. Enter the student's ID in the "Student ID" field if required.
5. Enter the exam results in the "Exam Results" field.
6. If applicable, provide the exam date and time in the "Exam Date" and "Exam Time" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter the student's full name as it appears in the student database.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, optional): Enter the student's ID number if required by your institution.
* **Exam Results** (`exam_results`, text, optional): Enter the exam results as a string (e.g., "Pass", "Fail", "Distinction", etc.).
* **Exam Date** (`exam_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the exam in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **Exam Time** (`exam_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the exam in the format "HH:MM:SS".
