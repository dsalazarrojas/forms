# Title I, Part C Intent To Participate And Affirmation Of Timely And Meaningful Consultation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track the intent to participate and affirmation of timely and meaningful consultation for Title I, Part C programs. It is intended to be completed by participants to ensure that they understand their commitment to the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill in the `title` field with a brief title of the consultation or project.
- Enter your `participant name` in the `participant name` field.
- Select your `intent to participate` by choosing either `True` or `False`.
- Provide an `affirmation of timely and meaningful consultation` in the `affirmation of timely and meaningful consultation` field.
- If you have participated in the project, provide your `participant signature` in the `participant signature` field.
- Optionally, you can provide the `date participation` and `time participation` fields if your participation is not immediately available.
- You can also provide your `email address` and `phone number` for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **title** (`title`, text, required): The title of the consultation or project.
* **participant name** (`participant name`, text, required): Your name as a participant in the project.
* **intent to participate** (`intent to participatie`, select_one, optional): Select `True` if you intend to participate, `False` if you do not.
* **affirmation of timely and meaningful consultation** (`affirmation of timely and meaningful consultation`, text, optional): A statement affirming your understanding of the consultation.
* **participant signature** (`participant signature`, text, optional): Your signature as a participant in the project, if you have participated.
* **date participation** (`date participation`, date, optional): The date you participated in the project.
* **time participation** (`time participation`, time, optional): The time you participated in the project.
* **email address** (`email address`, email, optional): Your email address for contact purposes.
* **phone number** (`phone number`, text, optional): Your phone number for contact purposes.
