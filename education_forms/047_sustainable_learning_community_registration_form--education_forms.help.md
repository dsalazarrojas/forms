# Sustainable Learning Community Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for schools and their members to register and provide necessary contact and demographic information to become part of the Sustainable Learning Community. The information gathered will be used for community management and communication purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill in your school's name in the "School Name" field.
2. Enter your school's email address in the "School Email" field.
3. Provide your school's website address in the "School Website" field.
4. Fill in your name in the "Member Name" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Member Email" field.
6. Select all the interests that apply to you from the "Member interests" field.
7. Indicate your preferred way(s) of participation in the "Member participation preferences" field.
8. Choose your education level from the "Member education level" field.
9. Select your occupation from the "Member occupation" field.
10. Fill in your address in the "Member Address" field.
11. Enter your phone number in the "Member Phone" field.
12. Provide your school's address in the "School Address" field.
13. Enter your school's phone number in the "School Phone" field.
14. Add any additional notes about yourself or your school in the "Additional notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your school.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, email, optional): Enter your school's email address.
* **School Website** (`school_website`, text, optional): Provide your school's website address.
* **Member Name** (`member_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as a representative of your school.
* **Member Email** (`member_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address as a representative of your school.
* **Member interests** (`interests`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the interests that apply to you or your school, such as Environmental Education, Community Engagement, or Sustainable Living.
* **Member participation preferences** (`participation_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Select your preferred way(s) of participation, such as In-person, Online, or Both.
* **Member education level** (`member_education_level`, select_one, optional): Choose your education level, such as High School, College, or University.
* **Member occupation** (`member_occupation`, select_one, optional): Select your occupation, such as Student, Teacher, or Staff.
* **Member Address** (`member_address`, text, optional): Enter your address as a representative of your school.
* **Member Phone** (`member_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number as a representative of your school.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, text, optional): Enter your school's address.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, text, optional): Enter your school's phone number.
* **Additional notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes about yourself or your school.
