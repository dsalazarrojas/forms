# Faculty Peer Resource Exchange Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Faculty Peer Resource Exchange Registration Form is a tool to facilitate connections between faculty members with similar research interests and expertise.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form with your registration information, including your Faculty Member ID, Faculty Position, and email.
2.  Select your research interests and expertise to help other faculty members find you for potential collaborations.
3.  Provide details about your current and past projects, including duration and funding information.
4.  Specify your collaboration preferences, including any peer review processes you'd like to engage in.
5.  Choose whether you've had any peer reviewers on your projects.
6.  Select the status of your current projects (active or inactive).
7.  If applicable, provide a completion date for your projects.
8.  Add any additional comments or information about your projects.
9.  Review and submit your registration details carefully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Details**: Please fill in your Faculty Member ID, Faculty Position, and email address. This information will help us verify your identity and contact you about potential collaborations.
* **Faculty Member ID**: Enter your unique Faculty Member ID. This will help us track your registrations and prevent duplicate submissions.
* **Faculty Position**: Select your current faculty position (e.g., professor, associate professor, etc.).
* **Email**: Enter your institutional email address. This will allow us to contact you about potential collaborations.
* **Research Interests**: List your primary and secondary research interests to help other faculty members find you for potential collaborations.
* **Research Expertise**: Describe your areas of research expertise to help other faculty members understand your capabilities.
* **Research Areas**: Specify your research areas of focus to help others with similar interests find you.
* **Research Projects**: Provide details about your current and past research projects, including duration and funding information.
* **Collaborators**: List any collaborators you've worked with in the past or are currently working with.
* **Project Details**: Add any additional information about your research projects, such as methodologies and outcomes.
* **Project Duration**: If applicable, provide the duration of your research projects.
* **Project Funding**: Specify the funding source(s) for your research projects.
* **Project Budget**: Enter the budget for your research projects, if applicable.
* **Project Start Date**: Enter the start date for your research projects, if applicable.
* **Project End Date**: Enter the completion date for your research projects, if applicable.
* **Faculty Peer Exchange Goals**: Specify your goals for engaging in faculty peer exchange activities, such as networking, collaboration, etc.
* **Collaboration Details**: Add any information about your collaboration preferences, including peer review processes you'd like to engage in.
* **Project Evaluation**: Provide an evaluation of your research projects, if applicable.
* **Peer Review Process**: Describe any peer review processes you've engaged in or would like to engage in.
* **Peer Reviewers**: Choose whether you've had peer reviewers on your projects (yes or no).
* **Project Status**: Select the status of your current projects (active or inactive).
* **Project Completion Date**: If applicable, provide the completion date for your research projects.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or information about your research projects.

By following these instructions and providing accurate information, you'll be able to effectively register for the Faculty Peer Resource Exchange and connect with other faculty members with similar research interests and expertise.
