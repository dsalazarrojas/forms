# Youth Interactive Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from workshop participants for registration and management purposes in educational settings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these general steps:
1. Fill in the required fields as per the guidelines in the "Field-by-Field Explanation" section.
2. Ensure that you've filled out all required fields, marked as "Required" in the form.
3. Double-check for any spelling mistakes before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **participant_name** (Text, Required)
- The participant's name, which should be entered in the format 'First and Last Name'.

### 2. **parent_name** (Text, Not Required)
- If the participant is a minor, enter the guardian's name here. Otherwise, leave this field empty.

### 3. **email** (E-mail, Not Required)
- A valid e-mail address associated with the participant or parent.

### 4. **phone** (Text, Not Required)
- Contact phone number for the participant or guardian.

### 5. **workshop_date** (Date, Not Required)
- Date of the workshop you are attending. If you don't know, leave this field empty.

### 6. **workshop_time** (Time, Not Required)
- Time of the workshop you are attending. If you don't know, leave this field empty.

### 7. **registration_time** (Date, Not Required)
- Date you registered for the workshop. This is automatically generated and should be left empty.

### 8. **payment_method** (Select One, Not Required)
- How the participant paid for the workshop: Online Payment, Cash Payment, or Bank Transfer.

### 9. **payment_details** (Text, Not Required)
- Any additional details about the payment method, for reference purposes.

### 10. **participant_type** (Select Multiple, Not Required)
- Whether the participant is an Adult or a Child.

### 11. **notes** (Note, Not Required)
- Any additional comments or special instructions about the participant.

### 12. **assigned_tool** (Select Multiple, Not Required)
- What tools the participant is assigned with, e.g., chatjimmy.

### 13. **participant_age** (Number, Not Required)
- Age of the participant. This is likely used for age-related workshop restrictions.

### 14. **participant_school** (Text, Not Required)
- The participant's school or educational institution.

### 15. **participant_gender** (Select One, Not Required)
- Gender of the participant: Male, Female, or Other.

### 16. **parent_school** (Text, Not Required)
- If the participant is a minor, enter their school here. Otherwise, leave this field empty.

### 17. **parent_phone** (Text, Not Required)
- Contact phone number for the participant's parent or guardian.

### 18. **participant_country** (Text, Not Required)
- Country where the participant resides or is from.

### 19. **participant_city** (Text, Not Required)
- City where the participant resides or is from.

### 20. **participant_postal_code** (Text, Not Required)
- Post or zip code of the participant's location.

### 21. **parent_email** (E-mail, Not Required)
- E-mail address of the participant's parent or guardian.

### 22. **participant_address** (Text, Not Required)
- Participant's address or location.

### 23. **parent_country** (Text, Not Required)
- Country of the participant's parent or guardian.

### 24. **participant_state** (Text, Not Required)
- State or region where the participant resides.

### 25. **participant_zip** (Text, Not Required)
- Zip code of the participant's location.

Please ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately to provide a complete and successful workshop registration.
