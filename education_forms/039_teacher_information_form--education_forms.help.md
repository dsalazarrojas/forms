# Teacher Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teacher Information Form is designed to collect essential details about a teacher's identity, department, and position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as instructed:

1.  Enter your first name in the `first_name` field.
2.  Enter your last name in the `last_name` field.
3.  Type in your work email address in the `email` field.
4.  Enter your phone number in the `phone` field.
5.  Select your department(s) of expertise from the `department` field (you can choose multiple departments).
6.  Select your position within the school from the `position` field.
7.  Enter the number of years you have worked as a teacher in the `years_of_experience` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name** (text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **last_name** (text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **email** (email, required/optional): Type in your work email address.
* **phone** (text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **department** (select multiple, required/optional): Select one or more departments you're associated with (Math, English, Science, History, Computer Science, Music).
* **position** (select one, required/optional): Choose your position at the school (Teacher, Headteacher, Assistant, Substitute).
* **years_of_experience** (number, required/optional): Enter the number of years you've worked as a teacher.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check the accuracy of your information before submitting.
- If you're unsure about any field, ask your administrator or supervisor for clarification.
- This form can be completed online or offline, depending on your preference.
