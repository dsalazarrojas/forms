# Guest Lecturer Claim Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Guest Lecturer Claim Form" is a form used by guest lecturers to claim reimbursement for their work. This form allows them to declare their intention to claim, request reimbursement for their expenses, and confirm their submission details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and select the appropriate option for each question.
2. For the select multiple and select one fields, choose the options that apply to your situation.
3. Enter the relevant dates and numbers for the date and number fields, respectively.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Claim Form** (claimer_form_1, select_multiple, required/optional): This field allows you to declare your intention to claim reimbursement. Select "Yes" if you are making a claim, and "No" otherwise.
* **Reimbursement Form** (claim_form_2, select_one, required/optional): This field asks if you are also submitting a reimbursement claim. Select "Yes" if you are making a reimbursement claim, and "No" otherwise.
* **Date of Claim** (date_form_3, date, required/optional): Enter the date you are claiming for.
* **Claim Amount** (amount_form_4, number, required/optional): Enter the total amount you are claiming.
* **Total Amount Claimed** (claimer_form_5, number, required/optional): Enter the total amount claimed.
* **Submit Claim** (submitter_form_6, date, required/optional): Enter the date and time you intend to submit your claim. Please format the date and time according to your preferred format.
* **Submit Claim** (submitter_form_7, select_one, required/optional): This field asks if you are confirming your submission. Select "Yes" to confirm your submission, and "No" otherwise.
