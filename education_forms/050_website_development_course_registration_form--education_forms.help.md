# Website Development Course Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from users who want to participate in a website development course. The form will help in planning and organization of the course, ensuring that everyone who joins is aware of the course's format, pace, and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these simple steps:

* Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email in the respective fields.
* If you have a website, please provide it.
* Mention your Company (if applicable).
* Specify your Role in the course (if applicable).
* List your skills and tools you are proficient in.
* Add any comments or notes you want to share with the course organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: 
    * **First Name** (`text`, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your passport or official identification.
* **Last Name**: 
    * **Last Name** (`text`, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your passport or official identification.
* **Email**: 
    * **Email** (`email`, Required): Enter a valid email address that can be used for communication during the course.
* **Date**: 
    * **Date** (`date`, Required): Enter the date you expect to start taking the course.
* **Website**: 
    * **Website** (`text`, Optional): If you have a website, provide the URL.
* **Company**: 
    * **Company** (`text`, Optional): If you are representing a company, enter its name.
* **Role**: 
    * **Role** (`text`, Optional): Specify your role in the course (e.g., developer, designer, or student).
* **Skills**: 
    * **Skills** (`text`, Optional): List your relevant skills (e.g., HTML, CSS, JavaScript).
* **Tools**: 
    * **Tools** (`text`, Optional): List any tools you are proficient with (e.g., IDEs, software tools, or frameworks).
* **Comments**: 
    * **Comments** (`text`, Optional): Share any additional comments or notes about your participation in the course.

## Tips
* Please fill in the form accurately and honestly.
* If you have any questions or need help, do not hesitate to contact us.
