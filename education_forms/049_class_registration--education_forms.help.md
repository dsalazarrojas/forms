# Class Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect student information for registration into a class. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that your registration is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting your first choice class from the "First Choice Class" dropdown menu.
2. Fill out your student's personal details such as name, date of birth, email address, phone number, and mailing address.
3. Select your emergency contact's name, phone number, and relationship to you.
4. Choose your academic year, semester or term, and enrollment status.
5. Indicate if you have prior college credits or need special accommodations.
6. Select a payment method for your registration fees.
7. Specify how you would like to travel to class and if you need accommodation assistance.
8. Indicate how you heard about us.
9. Confirm that you accept the terms and conditions of the registration.
10. Finally, add any additional comments or questions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Full Name (Student Name)** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your first and last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date of Birth (Date of Birth)** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Gender (Gender)** (`gender`, select_one, required): Select your gender from the options provided.
* **Student ID Number (Student ID Number)** (`student_id`, text, optional): If you have a student ID number, enter it here.
* **Email Address (Email Address)** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter the email address you want to use for class communications.
* **Phone Number (Phone Number)** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your best contact phone number.
* **Mailing Address (Mailing Address)** (`mailing_address`, text, required): Enter your street address, city, state, and ZIP code.
* **Emergency Contact Name (Emergency Contact Name)** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter the full name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (Emergency Contact Phone)** (`emergency_phone`, text, required): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship (Emergency Contact Relationship)** (`emergency_relationship`, text, optional): Describe your relationship to your emergency contact.
* **First Choice Class (First Choice Class)** (`class_selection_1`, select_one, required): Select your primary class selection from the options provided.
* **Second Choice Class (Second Choice Class)** (`class_selection_2`, select_one, optional): Select your backup class option from the options provided.
* **Third Choice Class (Third Choice Class)** (`class_selection_3`, select_one, optional): Select your third class option from the options provided.
* **Academic Year (Academic Year)** (`academic_year`, select_one, required): Select the academic year you are registering for.
* **Semester or Term (Semester or Term)** (`semester_term`, select_one, required): Choose when you want to start your class.
* **Enrollment Status (Enrollment Status)** (`enrollment_status`, select_one, required): Select your student status.
* **Prior Credits (Do You Have Prior College Credits)** (`prior_credits`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you have prior college credits.
* **Special Accommodations (Do You Need Special Accommodations)** (`special_accommodations`, select_one, required): Indicate if you need special accommodations.
* **Accommodation Needs (Please Describe Accommodation Needs)** (`accommodations_details`, text, optional): If you need special accommodations, describe your needs here.
* **Payment Method (Payment Method)** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select the method you will use to pay for your registration fees.
* **Housing Information (Do You Need Housing Information)** (`housing_needed`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you need accommodation assistance.
* **Transportation Method (How Will You Travel to Class)** (`transportation_method`, select_one, optional): Choose how you will travel to class.
* **How Did You Hear About Us (How Did You Hear About Us)** (`how_heard`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the ways you heard about us.
* **Terms and Conditions (Do You Accept Terms and Conditions)** (`terms_acceptance`, select_one, required): Confirm that you accept the terms and conditions.
* **Additional Comments or Questions (Additional Comments or Questions)** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any other comments or questions you have.
