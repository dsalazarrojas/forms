# Linear Function Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Linear Function Evaluation Form is used to assess a student's understanding of linear functions, their ability to apply mathematical operations, and their problem-solving skills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name and subject.
2. Choose the type of function being evaluated (Linear Function, Quadratic Function, or Cubic Function).
3. Enter the x and y values of the function.
4. Enter the x squared, y squared, x cubed, y cubed, x to the fourth, and y to the fourth values of the function.
5. Enter the coefficients a, b, and c of the function.
6. Choose the type of evaluation (Teacher, Student, or Parent).
7. Enter the evaluation date and time.
8. Enter any evaluation notes.
9. Enter the student's note.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required): Enter the teacher's name who is evaluating the student.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's name being evaluated.
* **Subject** (`subject`, select_one, required): Choose the subject being evaluated (Linear Algebra, Calculus, or Geometry).
* **Function** (`function`, text, required): Enter the function being evaluated.
* **x Value** (`x_value`, number, required): Enter the x value of the function.
* **y Value** (`y_value`, number, required): Enter the y value of the function.
* **x²** (`x_squared`, number, required): Enter the x squared value of the function.
* **y²** (`y_squared`, number, required): Enter the y squared value of the function.
* **x³** (`x_cube`, number, required): Enter the x cubed value of the function.
* **y³** (`y_cube`, number, required): Enter the y cubed value of the function.
* **x⁴** (`x_fourth`, number, required): Enter the x to the fourth value of the function.
* **y⁴** (`y_fourth`, number, required): Enter the y to the fourth value of the function.
* **Coefficient** (`coefficient_a`, number, required): Enter the coefficient a of the function.
* **Constant** (`coefficient_b`, number, required): Enter the constant b of the function.
* **Coefficient** (`coefficient_c`, number, required): Enter the coefficient c of the function.
* **Constant** (`coefficient_d`, number, required): Enter the constant d of the function.
* **Function Type** (`function_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of function being evaluated (Linear Function, Quadratic Function, or Cubic Function).
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Note** (`evaluation_note`, note, required): Enter any evaluation notes.
* **Student Note** (`student_note`, note, required): Enter any student notes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the student's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_multiple, required): Choose who submitted the evaluation (Teacher, Student, or Parent).
* **Submitted From** (`submitted_from`, select_multiple, required): Choose where the evaluation was submitted from (School, Home, or Online).

## Tips
* Please ensure that the student has a basic understanding of linear functions before completing this form.
* Please enter all values accurately and clearly.
* Please choose the correct function type and coefficients.
* Please enter the correct evaluation date and time.
* Please enter any evaluation notes clearly and concisely.
