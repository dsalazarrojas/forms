# Online Teaching Performance Questionnaire For Teachers - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect data on the performance and evaluation of teachers in an educational setting. This form is designed to gather information about the teacher's performance level, challenges faced, and evaluation periods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you understand the questions and fields.
2. Answer each question carefully and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Complete all required fields (if any) and use the options provided for select fields.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Teaching Performance Level** (2, Select One, Required: False):
	+ This field asks you to evaluate your overall teaching performance level. Select the option that best describes your current performance:
		- High: Excellent performance, above average.
		- Low: Below-average performance, struggling to meet expectations.
		- Medium: Average performance, meeting expectations but with some room for improvement.
- **Challenges Faced** (3, Note, Required: False):
	+ List specific issues or challenges you faced during the teaching performance period. Be as detailed as possible to help us understand your situation.
- **Teaching Performance Evaluation** (4, Select Multiple, Required: False):
	+ Evaluate your teaching performance in the following aspects:
		- Excellent: Outstanding performance, far exceeding expectations.
		- Good: Good performance, meeting or slightly exceeding expectations.
		- Satisfactory: Average performance, meeting expectations but with some room for improvement.
		- Poor: Below-average performance, struggling to meet expectations.
- **Teaching Evaluation Period** (5, Select One, Required: False):
	+ Indicate the period over which you are evaluating your performance:
		- 1 month
		- 2 months
		- 3 months
		- 4 months
		- 5 months
		- 6 months
		- 7 months
		- 8 months
		- 9 months
		- 10 months
		- 11 months
		- 12 months
