# Fls Daily Health Screening - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Fls Daily Health Screening form is designed to collect information on students' health and well-being. It is meant to be completed by students on a daily basis, typically at the beginning of the day, to help schools or institutions monitor their health and provide necessary support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
2. Enter your student's name in the "Student Name" field.
3. Enter your parent/guardian's name in the "Parent Name" field.
4. Enter your student's date of birth (mm/dd/yy) in the "Student DOB" field.
5. If applicable, enter any allergies or medical conditions in the "Allergies" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Drop Off Screening** (`school_screening_form`, text, required: false): This field is a free-text field where you can enter any relevant information about your student's health status.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required: true): Enter your student's name.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required: false): Enter your parent/guardian's name.
* **Student DOB** (`student_dob`, date, required: true): Enter your student's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yy.
* **Student Temperature** (`student_temperature`, text, required: false): Enter your student's current temperature (e.g. 98.6°F).
* **Student Symptoms** (`student_symptoms`, text, required: false): Enter any symptoms your student is currently experiencing.
* **Allergy 1 - 1** (`allergy_1_1`, text, required: false): Enter any allergies or medical conditions your student has (e.g. peanut allergy).
* **Allergy 1 - 2** (`allergy_1_2`, text, required: false): Enter any additional information about the first allergy.
* **Allergy 1 - 3** (`allergy_1_3`, text, required: false): Enter any additional information about the first allergy.
* **Allergy 1 - 4** (`allergy_1_4`, text, required: false): Enter any additional information about the first allergy.
* **Allergy 1 - 5** (`allergy_1_5`, text, required: false): Enter any additional information about the first allergy.
* **Allergy 2 - 1** (`allergy_2_1`, text, required: false): Enter any allergies or medical conditions your student has (e.g. shellfish allergy).
* **Allergy 2 - 2** (`allergy_2_2`, text, required: false): Enter any additional information about the second allergy.
* **Allergy 2 - 3** (`allergy_2_3`, text, required: false): Enter any additional information about the second allergy.
* **Allergy 2 - 4** (`allergy_2_4`, text, required: false): Enter any additional information about the second allergy.
* **Allergy 2 - 5** (`allergy_2_5`, text, required: false): Enter any additional information about the second allergy.

## Tips

* Please answer all the required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any information, please contact your school's health administration for guidance.
* Make sure to review and update your student's information regularly to ensure accuracy.
