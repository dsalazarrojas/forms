# IEP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IEP Form is a tool used to document and track a student's Individualized Education Program (IEP). It gathers essential information about the student's performance, progress, and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the IEP Form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the student's information: 
    *   Parent name
    *   Student name
    *   Student birthdate
    *   Student age
2.  Set the goals:
    *   Enter the goals 1-8
3.  Provide teacher and parent contact information:
    *   Teacher name
    *   Teacher email
    *   Parent name
    *   Parent email
4.  Schedule reviews:
    *   Date of last review
    *   Date of next review
    *   Frequency of reviews
    *   Review by (Teacher, Parent, or Both)
5.  Add any additional comments or notes

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Parent Input**: Enter the parent's name and email.
*   **Student Name**: Enter the student's name.
*   **Student Birthdate**: Enter the student's birthdate in the format (MM/DD/YYYY).
*   **Student Age**: Enter the student's age.
*   **Parent Name**: Enter the parent's name.
*   **Parent Email**: Enter the parent's email address.
*   **Teacher Name**: Enter the teacher's name.
*   **Teacher Email**: Enter the teacher's email address.
*   **Goal 1**: Enter the first goal for the student.
*   **Goal 2**: Enter the second goal for the student.
*   **Goal 3**: Enter the third goal for the student.
*   **Goal 4**: Enter the fourth goal for the student.
*   **Goal 5**: Enter the fifth goal for the student.
*   **Goal 6**: Enter the sixth goal for the student.
*   **Goal 7**: Enter the seventh goal for the student.
*   **Goal 8**: Enter the eighth goal for the student.
*   **Teacher Signature**: Enter the teacher's signature.
*   **Parent Signature**: Enter the parent's signature.
*   **Date of Last Review**: Enter the date of the last review in the format (MM/DD/YYYY).
*   **Date of Next Review**: Enter the date of the next review in the format (MM/DD/YYYY).
*   **Frequency of Reviews**: Select the frequency of reviews from the options provided.
*   **Review By**: Select who will be doing the reviews from the options provided.
*   **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the student.
*   **Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the student.
*   **Submit**: Click submit to save the changes.

**Tips**

*   Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information about the student.
*   Clearly define and document the student's goals.
*   Schedule reviews regularly to ensure consistent progress tracking.
*   Ensure that both parents and teachers are aware of and agree on the review schedule and frequency.
