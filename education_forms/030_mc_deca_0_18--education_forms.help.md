# mc_deca_0_18 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect demographic and contact information from students or their parents/guardians.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out your **First Name** and **Last Name** fields in the first section.
2. Select your **Grade Level** from the available options (High or Low).
3. Indicate whether you are interested in a specific **Subject**.
4. Provide your **Class Name**, **School Name**, and **Address** details.
5. Enter your **Student Number**, **Parent Name**, and **Contact Number**.
6. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
7. Provide your **Email** address.
8. Enter your **Address** details including **Address Line 1**, **Address Line 2**, **City**, **State**, and **Zip**.
9. Add any additional **Note** if necessary.
10. Enter your **Parent Email** address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Grade Level**: Select your grade level from the available options. This field is optional.
* **Subject**: Indicate whether you are interested in a specific subject.
* **Class Name**: The name of the class you are enrolled in or interested in.
* **School Name**: The name of the school you are attending or associated with.
* **Student Number**: Your student number or ID.
* **Parent Name**: The name of your parent or guardian.
* **Contact Number**: Your phone number for communication.
* **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Email**: Your email address.
* **Address Line 1**: The first line of your address.
* **Address Line 2**: The second line of your address.
* **City**: The city you live in.
* **State**: The state you live in.
* **Zip**: Your zip code.
* **Note**: Any additional comments or remarks.
* **Parent Email**: The email address of your parent or guardian.
