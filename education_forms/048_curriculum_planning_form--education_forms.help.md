# Curriculum Planning Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Curriculum Planning Form is a tool designed to help educators plan and organize their curriculum for a class. This form will help you outline the key details of your class, including class details, subject areas, teaching methods, and class size, among other things.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the following steps:

1. Ensure you have reviewed the form carefully and understand what information it requires.
2. Fill out the required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
3. For each section, provide accurate and detailed information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Class Details** (`class_details`, `text`, required/optional: false): This field is for providing general details about the class, such as the class title, description, or any other relevant information.
- **Subject Areas** (`subject_areas`, `select_multiple`, required/optional: false): Select one or more subject areas that you will be teaching. This will help in organizing the curriculum and planning.
- **Subject Areas Hint** (`subject_areas_hint`, `text`, required/optional: false): Provide a brief explanation of the subject areas you will be teaching.
- **Teaching Methods** (`teaching_methods`, `select_one`, required/optional: false): Choose the method or approach you will be using to teach the subject area(s) selected.
- **Teaching Methods Hint** (`teaching_methods_hint`, `text`, required/optional: false): Provide a brief description of the teaching methods you will be using.
- **Curriculum Plan** (`curriculum_plan`, `note`, required/optional: false): This field is for providing a detailed note about the curriculum plan, such as the learning objectives, assessment strategies, and evaluation methods.
- **Class Size** (`class_size`, `number`, required/optional: false): Enter the expected class size for this curriculum.
- **Class Start Date** (`class_start_date`, `date`, required/optional: false): Enter the date when the class is set to start.

## Tips
* Ensure you review and update this form regularly to keep your curriculum plans up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, don't hesitate to reach out to your department or administration for support.
