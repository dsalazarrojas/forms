# Optional Support Class Selection - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather optional information from users for support class selection purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your school name in the "School Name" field.
2. Select your grade level from the "Grade Level" dropdown menu.
3. Enter your interest level in the "Interest Level" field.
4. Choose your preferred start time from the available options.
5. Select your preferred days of the week for support class.
6. Enter your end time for the support class.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### School Name
* **School Name** (`school_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of your school. This information will help us understand your school context and provide relevant support.

### Grade Level
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, Select One, Optional): Select your current grade level to help us tailor our support to your specific needs.

### Interest Level
* **Interest Level** (`interest_level`, Text, Optional): Describe your interest level for the support class. This will help us understand your motivation and provide more focused support.

### Start Time
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, Time, Optional): Select your preferred start time for the support class. This will help us determine the most suitable time slot for you.

### Preferred Days
* **Preferred Days** (`preferred_days`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose your preferred days for the support class. This will help us schedule your sessions according to your availability.

### End Time
* **End Time** (`end_time`, Time, Optional): Enter your preferred end time for the support class. This will help us ensure that we provide support within your time constraints.

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when filling in the fields to get the most relevant support.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to reach out to us.
- You can also ask your teachers or classmates for help if you need further assistance.
