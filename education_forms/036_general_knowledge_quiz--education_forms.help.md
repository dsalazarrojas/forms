# General Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to take a general knowledge quiz and provide some basic information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "First Name" field with your first name, as requested.
2. Fill in the "Last Name" field with your last name, as requested.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Input your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select a date in the "Date" field, following the format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (first_name, text, optional): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (last_name, text, optional): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (date, date, optional): Select a date in MM/DD/YYYY format.
