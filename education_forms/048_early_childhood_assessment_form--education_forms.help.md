# Early Childhood Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Early Childhood Assessment Form is designed to collect information about children's developmental progress and family contact details. This form is intended for use in assessing children's early childhood development and gathering information from families for further evaluation or research purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the first section, "Name and Contact," with the child's name and the contact information of the person submitting the form.
2. Proceed to the next sections: "Child Information," "Family Information," "Developmental Progress," "Assessment Tools," "Submission Details," "Family Contact Information," and "Family Relationship."
3. In each section, enter the relevant information as prompted.
4. Once you have filled in all the required fields, review the "Submission Confirmation" section to ensure all required fields have been completed.
5. In the "Additional Comments" section, provide any additional information that may be relevant to the assessment.
6. Finally, select the submission status in the "Submission Status 2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name and Contact** (`Name and Contact`, text, required: false): Enter the child's name and your contact information, including your name and contact number, so that we can get in touch with you for further clarification or discussion.
* **Child Information** (`Child Information`, text, required: false): Provide any relevant information about the child, such as age, date of birth, or medical conditions.
* **Family Information** (`Family Information`, text, required: false): Enter any information about the child's family members, such as parents or caregivers.
* **Developmental Progress** (`Developmental Progress`, text, required: false): Describe the child's developmental progress, including any notable achievements or challenges.
* **Assessment Tools** (`Assessment Tools`, text, required: false): List any assessment tools or resources used for the child's development.
* **Submission Details** (`Submission Details`, text, required: false): Provide any details about the submission, such as the purpose or context of the assessment.
* **Family Contact Information** (`Family Contact Information`, text, required: false): Enter any contact information for the child's family members, such as phone numbers or email addresses.
* **Family Relationship** (`Family Relationship`, text, required: false): Describe your relationship to the child's family, such as your role or position.
* **Additional Information** (`Additional Information`, text, required: false): Enter any additional information about the child or family that may be relevant to the assessment.
* **Submission Confirmation** (`Submission Confirmation`, text, required: false): Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submission.
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, text, required: true): Enter any additional comments or observations that may be relevant to the assessment.
* **Submission Status 2** (`Submission Status 2`, select_one, required: true): Select the current submission status from the options provided.
