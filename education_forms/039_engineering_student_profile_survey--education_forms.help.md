# Engineering Student Profile Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Engineering Student Profile Survey is a tool used by the educational institution to gather essential information about engineering students. This information will help us create a comprehensive profile of each student, enabling us to better understand their needs and provide targeted support for their growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
3. **Email**: Enter your valid email address that you use for your university-related communications.
4. **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number, including the country code (if applicable).
5. **Student Status**: Check if you are currently an active or inactive student. This will help us understand your current status.
6. **Major**: Check the relevant box if you have declared a major or are interested in a specific field.
7. **Graduation Year**: If applicable, enter the year you plan to graduate or have graduated.
8. **Skills**: Enter any relevant skills you possess that you would like to highlight.
9. **Career Goals**: Describe your career aspirations and interests.
10. **Departmental Interest**: Check if you are interested in any of our departments.
11. **Faculty Interest**: Check if you are interested in any of our faculty members or research areas.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you use for your university-related communications.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number, including the country code (if applicable). This will enable us to contact you for any university-related matters.
* **Student Status**: Check if you are currently an active or inactive student. This will help us understand your current status and provide appropriate support.
* **Major**: Check the relevant box if you have declared a major or are interested in a specific field. This will help us understand your academic focus.
* **Graduation Year**: If applicable, enter the year you plan to graduate or have graduated. This will help us track your progress and provide timely support.
* **Skills**: Enter any relevant skills you possess that you would like to highlight. These skills can be related to your academic or professional pursuits.
* **Career Goals**: Describe your career aspirations and interests. This will help us provide guidance and support tailored to your needs.
* **Departmental Interest**: Check if you are interested in any of our departments. This will help us understand your academic focus and provide relevant resources.
* **Faculty Interest**: Check if you are interested in any of our faculty members or research areas. This will enable us to provide personalized support and guidance.

## Tips
* Ensure you fill in all the required fields accurately and completely.
* Be specific when describing your skills and career goals.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us for assistance.
* We appreciate your cooperation in providing us with accurate and timely information about yourself.
