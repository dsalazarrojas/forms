# School Safety Plan Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the safety protocols and procedures in a school setting. It is designed to ensure that schools maintain a safe environment for students and staff, and that safety measures are in place in case of emergencies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the safety protocols and procedures that are currently in place at your school.
	* For each section, choose the options that best describe your school's safety measures.
2. Choose the review frequency of the safety plan.
	* Select the frequency with which the safety plan is reviewed.
3. Select the review protocol used for the safety plan.
	* Choose the protocol used to review the safety plan.
4. Identify the contact information of the person responsible for the safety plan.
	* Select the person or department responsible for the safety plan.
5. Enter the review status of the safety plan.
	* Indicate the current status of the review.
6. Enter the review comments (if any).
	* Provide any additional comments or feedback about the safety plan.
7. Choose the person responsible for the review.
	* Select the person or department responsible for the review.
8. Enter the review due date.
	* Enter the date by which the safety plan is to be reviewed.
9. Indicate if the safety plan has been completed.
	* Confirm if the safety plan has been reviewed and completed.
10. Enter the contact information of the person responsible for the review.
	* Provide the contact information of the person who reviewed the safety plan.
11. Enter the contact email and phone number of the person responsible for the review.
	* Provide the contact email and phone number of the person who reviewed the safety plan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **School Safety Plan** (Select multiple): This section allows you to select the safety protocols and procedures that are currently in place at your school.
*   Choose all the options that best describe your school's safety measures.
*   For example, you can select "Locking doors" and "Conducting drills" if these are part of your school's safety protocols.

- **Review Frequency** (Select one): This section asks you to choose the frequency with which the safety plan is reviewed.
*   Select the frequency that best describes how often the safety plan is reviewed.
*   For example, you can select "Frequent" if the safety plan is reviewed regularly.

- **Review Protocol** (Select multiple): This section allows you to select the protocol used for reviewing the safety plan.
*   Choose the protocol that best describes the process used for reviewing the safety plan.
*   For example, you can select "Review protocols" and "Conducting drills" if the review process involves both reviewing protocols and conducting drills.

- **Contact Information** (Select multiple): This section allows you to identify the contact information of the person or department responsible for the safety plan.
*   Select the person or department that is responsible for the safety plan.
*   For example, you can select "Principal" and "Safety officer" if both are responsible for the safety plan.

- **Review Status** (Select one): This section asks you to enter the current status of the review.
*   Select the status that best describes the current state of the review.
*   For example, you can select "Completed" if the review has been completed.

- **Review Comments** (Text): This section allows you to enter any additional comments or feedback about the safety plan.
*   Provide any relevant comments or feedback about the safety plan.
*   For example, you can enter "The safety plan is up-to-date and effective."

- **Review Responsible** (Select one): This section allows you to select the person or department responsible for the review.
*   Select the person or department that is responsible for the review.
*   For example, you can select "School administration" and "Safety officer" if both are responsible for the review.

- **Review Due Date** (Date): This section asks you to enter the date by which the safety plan is to be reviewed.
*   Enter the date by which the safety plan is to be reviewed.
*   For example, you can enter a date 6 months from the current date.

- **Review Completed** (Select one): This section asks you to confirm if the safety plan has been completed.
*   Select the option that best describes the current state of the review.
*   For example, you can select "Complete" if the safety plan has been reviewed and completed.

- **Contact Person** (Text): This section allows you to enter the name of the person responsible for the review.
*   Enter the name of the person who reviewed the safety plan.
*   For example, you can enter the name of the school's safety officer.

- **Contact Email** (Email): This section asks you to enter the contact email of the person responsible for the review.
*   Enter the contact email of the person who reviewed the safety plan.
*   For example, you can enter the email of the school's safety officer.

- **Contact Phone** (Text): This section asks you to enter the contact phone number of the person responsible for the review.
*   Enter the contact phone number of the person who reviewed the safety plan.
*   For example, you can enter the phone number of the school's safety officer.

## Tips
- Make sure to accurately fill out each section to ensure that the safety plan is up-to-date and effective.
- Ensure that all necessary safety measures are in place at your school.
- Review the safety plan regularly to identify any areas for improvement.
- Communicate any issues or concerns to the relevant authorities.
- Update the safety plan as needed to reflect any changes or additions.
- Verify the contact information of the person responsible for the review.
- Confirm that the review has been completed and documented properly.
