# education_sector_consulting_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The education_sector_consulting_contact_form is designed to collect information from education sector consultants. This form is intended for consultants who wish to inquire about, discuss, or propose services related to education consulting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your User Input, which is your name and any relevant identifier you'd like to associate with your submission.
2. Enter your Contact Details, including a valid Email address.
3. Select your preferred Contact Method (phone, email, or mail).
4. If other contact methods are preferred, provide an explanation in the Other Contact Method field.
5. Describe your Consultation Inquiry in the Consultation Inquiry field.
6. Provide the name of your School, University, and Department.
7. Specify the Contact Person you would like to be contacted by.
8. Enter your Email address for correspondence.
9. Provide your Phone number for phone calls.
10. If mail is a preferred contact method, enter your mail address.
11. Enter a Message to our organization.
12. Optionally, select a Preferred Contact Time for follow-up conversations.
13. Enter a Follow-up Date and time for follow-up conversations.
14. If you want us to follow up with you via phone, select Phone, otherwise select another contact method.
15. If you'd like, provide a Follow-up Contact Person for follow-up conversations.
16. Add any relevant notes about your submission.
17. Select a tool you'd like us to use for follow-up (if any).
18. Finally, if you'd like to be assigned a specific representative for follow-up, select 'Yes', otherwise select 'No'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (User Input, text, optional): Enter a brief description of yourself or your organization.
* **Contact Details** (Contact Details, text, required): Provide your email address for us to contact you back.
* **Contact Method** (Contact Method, select_one, optional): Choose how you'd like to be contacted (phone, email, or mail).
* **Other Contact Method** (Other Contact Method, text, optional): If other contact methods are preferred, describe them here.
* **Consultation Inquiry** (Consultation Inquiry, text, required): Describe the purpose of your consultation.
* **School Name** (School Name, text, required): Enter the name of your School.
* **University Name** (University Name, text, required): Enter the name of your University.
* **Department** (Department, text, required): Enter the name of your Department.
* **Contact Person** (Contact Person, text, required): Specify the name and contact information of the person who will be contacted.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter a valid Email address.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your Phone number.
* **Mail** (Mail, text, optional): If you prefer mail contact, enter your mail address here.
* **Message** (Message, text, required): Enter a Message to our organization.
* **Preferred Contact Time** (Preferred Contact Time, time, optional): Select a time for follow-up conversations.
* **Follow-up Date** (Follow-up Date, date, optional): Select a date for follow-up conversations.
* **Follow-up Method** (Follow-up Method, select_one, optional): Choose a follow-up method (phone, email, or mail).
* **Follow-up Contact Person** (Follow-up Contact Person, text, optional): If you'd like, provide a Follow-up Contact Person for follow-up conversations.
* **Note** (Note, text, optional): Add any relevant notes about your submission.
* **Assigned Agent** (Assigned Agent, select_one, optional): Select a representative to handle your case (chatjimmy or Other).
* **Assigned Agent Note** (Assigned Agent Note, text, optional): If you'd like, provide a note for the assigned representative.
* **Assigned Tool** (Assigned Tool, select_one, optional): Choose if you'd like to be assigned a tool for follow-up (Yes or No).
* **Preferred Language** (Preferred Language, select_one, optional): Select your preferred language for communication (en, es, fr, or ar).
