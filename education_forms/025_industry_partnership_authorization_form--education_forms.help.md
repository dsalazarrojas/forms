# industry_partnership_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The industry_partnership_authorization_form is used to collect information from industry partners for partnership authorization purposes. This form is designed to gather necessary data for partnership collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide general information about the partnership by filling in the "partnership_information" field.
2. Enter the name of the partner company or entity in the "partner_name" field.
3. Enter contact information for the partner company or entity in the "contact_information" field.
4. Select the type of partnership from the "role_information" dropdown menu.
5. Describe the agreement terms between the partner company or entity and the submitting organization in the "agreement_details" field.
6. Specify any partnership terms or conditions in the "partnership_terms" field.
7. If necessary, provide a review or note about the agreement in the "agreement_review" field.
8. Finally, select the signature type from the "partner_signature" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **partnership_information** (text, required: false): This field is for entering general information about the partnership.
* **partner_name** (text, required: false): Enter the name of the partner company or entity.
* **contact_information** (text, required: false): Enter contact information for the partner company or entity.
* **role_information** (select_one, required: false): Select the type of partnership from the dropdown menu.
* **agreement_details** (text, required: false): Describe the agreement terms between the partner company or entity and the submitting organization.
* **partnership_terms** (text, required: false): Specify any partnership terms or conditions.
* **agreement_review** (note, required: false): If necessary, provide a review or note about the agreement.
* **partner_signature** (select_one, required: false): Select the signature type from the dropdown menu.
