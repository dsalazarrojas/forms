# Science Fair Participation Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to enroll participants in a science fair.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, you will be asked to provide your personal and project details. Please follow the steps below:

1. Enter your first name.
2. Enter your last name.
3. Enter your project's title.
4. Enter a brief description of your project.
5. Choose the category of your project.
6. Choose the level of your project (beginner, intermediate, advanced).
7. Choose your grade level (6th, 7th, 8th, 9th, 10th).
8. If you have any images to include with your project, click the "project_image" field.
9. Enter the name of your school.
10. Enter the city of your school.
11. If you are not the project participant, enter your name and contact information.
12. Check the box to confirm you have read and agree to the terms of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your birth certificate or ID.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your birth certificate or ID.
* **Project Title** (`project_title`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief title of your project.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your project.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or birth certificate.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address for yourself or your guardian.
* **Project Category** (`project_category`, select_one, required): Choose the category of your project from the list.
* **Project Level** (`project_level`, select_one, required): Choose the level of your project (beginner, intermediate, advanced).
* **Student Age** (`student_age`, number, required/optional): Enter your age.
* **Project Image** (`project_image`, note, required/optional): If you have an image to include with your project, click here.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your school.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_multiple, required): Choose the grade level you are in (6th, 7th, 8th, 9th, 10th).
* **Project Image Count** (`project_image_count`, text, required/optional): Enter the number of images you will submit.
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date your project began.
* **Project End Date** (`project_end_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date your project ended.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address for yourself or the person submitting on your behalf.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **School** (`school`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your school.
* **School City** (`school_city`, text, required/optional): Enter the city of your school.
* **Project Image Count** (`project_image_count`, text, required/optional): Enter the number of images you will submit.
* **Submitter Address** (`submitter_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your address.
* **Submitter Note** (`submitter_note`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes for the project submission.
* **Submitter Agreement** (`submitter_agreement`, note, required/optional): Check the box to confirm you have read and agree to the terms of submission.
* **Submitter Organisation** (`submitter_organisation`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your organisation.
