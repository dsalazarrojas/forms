To ensure that this form accurately captures the necessary information for a zoo field trip registration, we will cover the purpose of this form and the process of completing it step-by-step.

As we design this form for the "zoo field trip registration", we will focus on collecting essential details about students, teachers, and their contact information to make the trip a success.

## Purpose
This form is designed to register students and their accompanying individuals (teachers, parents, or guardians) for a zoo field trip. The form captures essential information about the students, such as their names, grade level, medical conditions, and emergency contact details. It also includes fields for the teacher or parent/guardian to provide their name, contact phone number, and relationship with the student.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the school name in the "School Name" field.
2. Enter the teacher's name in the "Teacher Name" field.
3. Enter each student's name in the respective "Student Name", "Student Name 2", "Student Name 3", and "Student Name 4" fields.
4. For each student, select their grade level from the "Student Grade" dropdown menu.
5. Enter the parent/guardian's name in the "Parent Name", "Emergency Contact Name", "Emergency Contact Name 2", and "Emergency Contact Name 3" fields.
6. For each parent/guardian, select their relationship with the student from the "Relationship" dropdown menu.
7. Enter the contact phone number for each parent/guardian in the "Contact Phone", "Contact Phone 2", and "Contact Phone 3" fields.
8. Select whether the student has any medical conditions or allergies that the school should be aware of in the "Medical Conditions" field.
9. For each student, select whether they have given permission to participate in the trip in the "Student Permission" field.
10. For each student, enter their student count, if any.
11. Enter your school email address in the "School Email" field (optional).
12. Enter any special instructions or notes in the "Special Instructions" field (optional).
13. Enter any additional contact phone numbers for the teacher or parent/guardian in the "Contact Phone 2" and "Emergency Contact Phone 2" fields (optional).
14. Select whether the teacher or parent/guardian has given permission to participate in the trip for each student in the "Student Permission 2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Name** (`School Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the school.
* **Teacher Name** (`Teacher Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the teacher accompanying the students.
* **Student Name** (`Student Name`, text, required): Enter the name of each student.
* **Student Grade** (`Student Grade`, number, required): Select the grade level of each student.
* **Parent Name** (`Parent Name`, text, required): Enter the parent/guardian's name.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, text, required): Enter the contact phone number for the parent/guardian.
* **Medical Conditions** (`Medical Conditions`, select_one, required): Select any medical conditions or allergies that the parent/guardian would like the school to be aware of.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`Emergency Contact Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship** (`Relationship`, select_one, required): Select the relationship of the emergency contact person with the student.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`Emergency Contact Phone`, text, required): Enter the contact phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Student Permission** (`Student Permission`, select_one, required): Select whether the student has given permission to participate in the trip.
* **Student Count** (`Student Count`, number, required): Enter the number of students, if any.
* **School Email** (`School Email`, email, optional): Enter your school email address (optional).
* **Special Instructions** (`Special Instructions`, note, optional): Enter any special instructions or notes (optional).
* **Contact Phone 2** (`Contact Phone 2`, text, optional): Enter an additional contact phone number (optional).
* **Emergency Contact Name 2** (`Emergency Contact Name 2`, text, optional): Enter the name of an additional emergency contact person (optional).
* **Relationship 2** (`Relationship 2`, select_one, optional): Select the relationship of the additional emergency contact person with the student (optional).
* **Emergency Contact Phone 2** (`Emergency Contact Phone 2`, text, optional): Enter an additional contact phone number (optional).
* **Student Permission 2** (`Student Permission 2`, select_one, required): Select whether the teacher or parent/guardian has given permission to participate in the trip for each student.
* **Student Name 2** (`Student Name 2`, text, required): Enter the name of each additional student.
* **Student Name 3** (`Student Name 3`, text, required): Enter the name of each additional student.
* **Contact Phone 3** (`Contact Phone 3`, text, optional): Enter an additional contact phone number (optional).
* **Emergency Contact Name 3** (`Emergency Contact Name 3`, text, optional): Enter the name of an additional emergency contact person (optional).

## Tips
* Please fill out all required fields to ensure accurate and complete information.
* Double-check the information entered for each student to avoid errors.
* If you have any special instructions or requests, please include them in the "Special Instructions" field.
* If you need to enter multiple contact phone numbers, please use the "Contact Phone 2", "Contact Phone 3", etc. fields.
