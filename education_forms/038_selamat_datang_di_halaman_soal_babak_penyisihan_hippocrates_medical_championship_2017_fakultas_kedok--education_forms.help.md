# Selamat Datang Di Halaman Soal Babak Penyisihan Hippocrates Medical Championship 2017 Fakultas Kedok - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants, judges, or officials involved in the "Hippocrates Medical Championship 2017" event hosted by "Fakultas Kedok".

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your information.
2. Provide details about the "Halaman Soal" (Page/Section) of the event.
3. Answer questions about the "Penyisihan" (Assessment/Competition) held during the event.
4. Enter your details about the "Hippocrates Medical Championship" event.
5. Provide your association with "Fakultas Kedok" (Faculty/Department) if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Halaman Soal (Halaman Soal)** (`halaman_soal`, text, optional): This field is for entering information about the "Halaman Soal" section of the event. You can type in any relevant details you want to share.
* **Penyisihan (Penyisihan)** (`penyisihan`, text, optional): This field is for answering questions about the "Penyisihan" assessment or competition held during the event. Please provide any relevant information you want to share.
* **Hippocrates Medical Championship (Hippocrates Medical Championship)** (`hippocrates_medical_championship`, text, optional): This field is for entering details about the "Hippocrates Medical Championship" event. Please provide any relevant information you want to share.
* **Fakultas Kedok (Fakultas Kedok)** (`fakultas_kedok`, text, optional): This field is for providing your association with "Fakultas Kedok", if applicable. Please type in your affiliation details if you have any.
