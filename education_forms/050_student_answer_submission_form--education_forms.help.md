# Student Answer Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for students to submit their answers to assignments and exams. It ensures that students can provide clear and organized responses to questions and tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `Assignment Title` field with the title of the assignment you are submitting.
2. Provide a brief description of your answer in the `Assignment Description` field.
3. Enter your name as it appears on your student ID in the `Student Name` field.
4. Type your answer to the assignment question in the `Answer` field.
5. Select the correct `Submission Status` from the dropdown menu.
6. Enter the `Assignment ID` number (if provided by your instructor).
7. Select the type of assignment from the `Assignment Type` dropdown menu (e.g., Assignment, Exam).
8. Optionally, enter your `Student ID` number (if provided by your instructor).
9. Enter the date and time of submission for each assignment (if applicable).
10. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the `Submission Comment` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Assignment Title** (`assignment_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the assignment you are submitting.
* **Assignment Description** (`assignment_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your answer.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your student ID.
* **Answer** (`answer`, text, optional): Type your answer to the assignment question.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of submission.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of submission.
* **Submission Comment** (`submission_comment`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_one, required): Select the correct submission status from the dropdown menu (Approved, Rejected, Pending, Draft).
* **Assignment ID** (`assignment_id`, number, optional): Enter the number of the assignment (if provided by your instructor).
* **Assignment Type** (`assignment_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of assignment from the dropdown menu (Assignment, Exam).
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, optional): Enter your student ID number (if provided by your instructor).
* **Submission Date 2** (`submission_date_2`, date, optional): Enter the date of submission for the second assignment.
* **Submission Time 2** (`submission_time_2`, time, optional): Enter the time of submission for the second assignment.
* **Submission Comment 2** (`submission_comment_2`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback for the second assignment.

**Tips**

* Make sure to fill in all required fields (marked as `required`).
* Use the `Assignment Type` dropdown menu to select the correct type of assignment.
* If you need help or have questions, contact your instructor or support team for assistance.
