# Program Enrollment Communication Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Program Enrollment Communication Form is a critical tool used to update and manage participant information in our program's database. This form helps ensure accurate and up-to-date data, allowing us to efficiently communicate with participants and manage their enrollment status.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Gather Information

*   Collect the relevant participant's details, such as their first name, last name, email address, and program participant ID.
*   Verify the participant's program start and end dates to ensure accuracy.
*   Determine the participant's type (Student, Parent, or Guardian) and status (Pending, Active, or Inactive).

### How to Fill Out This Form

1.  **Program Title** (<name>)
    *   Enter the title of the program the participant is enrolled in.
2.  **First Name** (<label>)
    *   Enter the participant's first name.
3.  **Last Name** (<label>)
    *   Enter the participant's last name.
4.  **Email** (<label>)
    *   Enter the participant's email address.
5.  **Program Start Date** (<label>)
    *   Enter the date the participant started the program.
6.  **Program End Date** (<label>)
    *   Enter the date the participant ended the program.
7.  **Participant Type** (<label>)
    *   Choose the participant type (Student, Parent, or Guardian).
8.  **Enrollment Status** (<label>)
    *   Select the participant's enrollment status (Pending, Active, or Inactive).
9.  **Participant ID** (<label>)
    *   Enter the participant's unique ID number.
10. **Participant Group** (<label>)
    *   Enter the participant's group or category.
11.  **Communication Frequency** (<label>)
    *   Choose how often to communicate with the participant (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
12.  **Contact Method** (<label>)
    *   Select the preferred method of contact (Phone, Email, or Text).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Program Title**:
    *   A short description of the program the participant is enrolled in.
*   **First Name**:
    *   The participant's first name.
*   **Last Name**:
    *   The participant's last name.
*   **Email**:
    *   The participant's email address.
*   **Program Start Date**:
    *   The date the participant started the program.
*   **Program End Date**:
    *   The date the participant ended the program.
*   **Participant Type**:
    *   The category of participant (Student, Parent, or Guardian).
*   **Enrollment Status**:
    *   The participant's status in the program (Pending, Active, or Inactive).
*   **Participant ID**:
    *   A unique ID number assigned to the participant.
*   **Participant Group**:
    *   The participant's group or category.
*   **Communication Frequency**:
    *   How often to communicate with the participant (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
*   **Contact Method**:
    *   The preferred method of contact (Phone, Email, or Text).

## Tips

*   Always ensure that you have the most up-to-date participant information before filling out this form.
*   Review the participant's current enrollment status and other details to avoid duplication of effort.
*   Double-check your entries for accuracy to prevent errors in the database.
*   For any discrepancies or issues with participant information, contact our support team for assistance.
