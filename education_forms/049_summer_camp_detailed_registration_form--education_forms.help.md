# Summer Camp Detailed Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Camp Detailed Registration Form is used to collect information from participants and their parents/guardians regarding their attendance at our summer camp. The form aims to ensure that we have accurate and up-to-date information about each participant, which is essential for providing a safe and enjoyable experience. **Please ensure you fill in the form accurately and completely, and note that submission of false or incomplete information can be grounds for exclusion from the camp.**

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the participant's information.
2. If you are unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to contact us.
3. Review and submit the form for approval before sending it to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Summer Camp Name**: Enter the name of the summer camp you are registering for (e.g., Camp Sunshine, Summer Adventure Camp).
* **Camp Dates**: Select all the dates the participant will be attending the summer camp.
* **Participant Name**: Enter the participant's full name as it appears on their official documents (e.g., passport, ID, or birth certificate).
* **Participant DOB (Date of Birth)**: Enter the participant's date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 1990-02-12).
* **Participant Age**: Enter the participant's age as of the start date of the summer camp (e.g., 12).
* **Participant Contact**: Enter the participant's contact information (e.g., phone number, email, or physical address).
* **Camp Director Email**: Enter the email address of the summer camp director or administration contact.
* **Participant Special Needs**: Select "Participant has special needs" if the participant requires any special accommodations or has a medical condition that we should be aware of.
* **Participant Medical Condition**: If you selected "Participant has special needs," provide a detailed explanation of the participant's medical condition or special need (e.g., allergy, disability, or medical equipment required).
* **Note**: Please ensure you fill in all required fields accurately and completely. If you are unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to contact us.
