# student_research_publication_consent_waiver_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The student research publication consent and waiver form is designed to obtain informed consent from students who are participating in research activities and to document their agreement to publish their research output. This form is typically used to ensure that students understand their rights and responsibilities when publishing research, and to obtain their permission to use their work in academic or commercial contexts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your basic information, such as your first name, last name, date of birth, and date of publication.
2. Provide details about your research, including the title of your research, the research area, and the type of publication (e.g., academic paper, book, etc.).
3. Enter the URL of the publication, if applicable.
4. Indicate whether you have given your consent for the publication of your research.
5. Enter the date you gave your consent.
6. Sign the form with your signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Please enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Date of Publication** (`date_of_publication`, date, optional): Please enter the date of publication in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Publication Name** (`publication_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of the publication.
* **Institution Name** (`institution_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of the institution or organization associated with the publication.
* **Research Title** (`research_title`, text, optional): Please enter a brief title of your research.
* **Research Area** (`research_area`, text, optional): Please enter a brief description of the research area.
* **Publication Type** (`publication_type`, select multiple, optional): Please select all that apply from the following options: Yes, No.
* **Publication URL** (`publication_url`, text, optional): Please enter the URL of the publication, if applicable.
* **Consent Given** (`consent_given`, select one, optional): Please select True or False to indicate whether you have given your consent for the publication.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, optional): Please enter the date you gave your consent in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, text, optional): Please sign this form with your signature.
* **Guardian Signature** (`guardian_signature`, text, optional): Please sign this form with your guardian's signature, if applicable.
* **Institution Signature** (`institution_signature`, text, optional): Please sign this form with your institution's signature, if applicable.
