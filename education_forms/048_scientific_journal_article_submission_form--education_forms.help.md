# scientific_journal_article_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The scientific journal article submission form is designed to collect information from authors submitting articles to a scientific journal. This form is used to gather essential details about the article, such as category, title, abstract, keywords, and submission details. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Category** field with the relevant category of your article.
2. Enter the **Article Title** in the text field provided.
3. Provide a concise **Article Abstract** that summarizes your article's main points.
4. Add relevant **Keywords** that describe your article's subject matter.
5. Select the **Journal Name** where your article will be submitted.
6. Confirm whether you have a **Referee 1**, **Referee 2**, and **Referee 3** for your article.
7. Enter the file name and size for your **Article Files**.
8. Provide the **Submission Date** and **Submission Time** for your article.
9. Choose the **Submission Status** of your article (Active or Inactive).
10. Select whether you have any **Comments** to include with your submission.
11. Choose the **Article Type**, **Article Language**, and **Article Format** of your article.
12. Enter the **Article Length** in words.
13. Provide the file name for your **Article File Name**.
14. Enter the size of your **Article File Size** in MB.
15. Enter your **Submitter** name and department.
16. Enter your **Submitter Institution**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category**: Please select the relevant category of your article from the given options.
* **Article Title**: Enter a descriptive title for your article.
* **Author**: Enter your name as the author of the article.
* **Article Abstract**: A concise summary of your article's main points.
* **Keywords**: Enter relevant keywords that describe your article's subject matter.
* **Journal Name**: Select the journal where you are submitting your article.
* **Referee 1**, **Referee 2**, and **Referee 3**: Confirm whether you have a referee for each of these positions.
* **Article Files**: Upload any relevant files for your article.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date you are submitting your article.
* **Submission Time**: Enter the time you are submitting your article.
* **Submission Status**: Choose whether your article is active or inactive.
* **Comments**: Select whether you have any comments to include with your submission.
* **Article Type**: Choose the type of article you are submitting.
* **Article Language**: Select the language of your article.
* **Article Format**: Choose the format of your article.
* **Article Length**: Enter the length of your article.
* **Article File Name**: Enter the name of your article file.
* **Article File Size**: Enter the size of your article file in MB.
* **Submitter**: Enter your name as the submitter.
* **Submitter Department**: Enter your department.
* **Submitter Institution**: Enter your institution.
