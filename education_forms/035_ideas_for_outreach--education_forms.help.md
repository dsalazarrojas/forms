# Ideas For Outreach - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Ideas For Outreach" form is used to collect information about potential outreach events and contacts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required") to provide the necessary information about the event.
2. Enter the event title, date, location, and details in the respective fields.
3. Provide the contact's name, email, and phone number (if applicable).
4. If submitting on behalf of an institution, enter the institution's name and department.
5. Click "Submit" to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Title** (`event_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the event.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required): Enter any additional details about the event.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact person's phone number (if applicable).
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the submitter.
* **Submitter Institution** (`submitter_institution`, text, optional): Enter the name of the institution or organization you are submitting from.
* **Submitter Department** (`submitter_department`, text, optional): Enter the department or unit within the institution or organization you are submitting from.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate and relevant submissions.
* Check the form for any duplicate submissions before submitting.
* If you are submitting on behalf of an institution, ensure that you have the necessary permissions and authorities to do so.
