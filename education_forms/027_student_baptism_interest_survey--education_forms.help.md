# Student Baptism Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Baptism Interest Survey is designed to collect information from students interested in the baptism process. This form helps us understand your background, interests, and availability for baptism.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Provide your valid Email address to help us contact you.
3. Enter your contact Phone number to ensure we can reach you.
4. Describe your current faith background in the Faith Background field.
5. Mention the name of the Church you are currently attending or a brief description if it is not listed.
6. If you are not currently attending a Church, please mention the name of the Church in the "Church (Other)" field.
7. Check the "Consent" box to indicate your understanding of our data collection and usage policy.
8. Select one or more options from the "Schedule Baptism" dropdown menu to indicate your interest in scheduling a baptism service or class, or study.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Enter your given name.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Enter your surname.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter a valid email address to help us contact you.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Faith Background** (Faith Background, text, required): Describe your current faith background, including your denomination or denomination-free description.
* **Church** (Church, text, required): Mention the name of the Church you are currently attending. If it is not listed, provide a brief description.
* **Church (Other)** (Church Other, text, optional): Mention the name of the Church you are attending if it is not listed. This field is optional but recommended.
* **Consent** (Consent, select_one, required): Check the box to indicate your understanding of our data collection and usage policy.
* **Schedule Baptism** (Schedule Baptism, select_multiple, required): Select one or more options to schedule a baptism service, class, or study. Options include:
	+ Baptism Service: Schedule a baptism service.
	+ Baptism Class: Schedule a baptism class.
	+ Baptism Study: Schedule a baptism study. 

Note: This form is only intended for students who are at least 18 years old. Please make sure to review our data collection and usage policy before checking the "Consent" box.
