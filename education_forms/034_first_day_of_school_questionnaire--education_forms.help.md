# First Day Of School Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The First Day Of School Questionnaire is a form designed to collect information about students' experiences and backgrounds on the first day of school. This information helps educators or administrators create a supportive and inclusive learning environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

* Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your abilities.
* You can use the provided fields to describe your experiences and backgrounds.
* You do not need to complete all the questions if they are not relevant to you.
* If you need help, feel free to ask your teacher or administrator for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### What was your previous school like?
* **What was your previous school like?** (`previous_schooling`, text, optional): This field is a place for you to describe your previous school experience. Please provide as much detail as you can about your time at your previous school.

### What is your family like?
* **What is your family like?** (`family_background`, text, optional): This field is a place for you to describe your family dynamics. Please provide as much detail as you can about your family.

### What are your interests?
* **What are your interests?** (`student_interests`, text, optional): This field is a place for you to describe your interests and hobbies. Please provide as much detail as you can about what you enjoy doing in your free time.

### What is your school history?
* **What is your school history?** (`school_history`, text, optional): This field is a place for you to describe your educational history. Please provide as much detail as you can about your educational journey so far.

## Tips
* Please be honest and open when filling out this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask your teacher or administrator for help.
* Completing this form will help create a more supportive and inclusive learning environment for you and your peers.
