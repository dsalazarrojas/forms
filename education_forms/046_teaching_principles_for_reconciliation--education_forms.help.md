# Teaching Principles For Reconciliation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on your experience with various teaching principles or approaches in a reconciliation context. The form aims to gather nuanced ratings on how well different teaching methods are received or experienced.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer according to your experience with the teaching principles listed.
2. For each rating field, select the rating that best reflects your experience.
3. Provide additional comments on your reflections or insights, if any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **EducaPrinciples** (`EducaPrinciples`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the options to indicate whether you have experience with the EducaPrinciples teaching approach.
- **Years of Experience** (`Years of Experience`, number, required: false): Enter your years of experience with the teaching principles or approaches, if any.
- **Teaching Approach** (`Teaching Approach`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the approaches that you have experience with.
- **Reflection on Reconciliation** (`Reflection on Reconciliation`, text, required: false): Provide any reflections or insights on your experience with the teaching principles or approaches.
- **Rating 5-point scale** (`Rating 5-point scale`, number, required: false): Select a rating between 1 and 5 to indicate your experience with the principle or approach.
- **Rating 9-point scale** (`Rating 9-point scale`, number, required: true): Rate your experience with the principle or approach on a scale from 1 to 9.
- **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments or insights on your experience with the teaching principles or approaches.
- **Rating 3-point scale** (`Rating 3-point scale`, number, required: false): Rate your experience with the principle or approach on a 3-point scale.
- **Rating 4-point scale** (`Rating 4-point scale`, number, required: false): Select a rating between 1 and 4 to indicate your experience with the principle or approach.
- **Rating 6-point scale** (`Rating 6-point scale`, number, required: false): Rate your experience with the principle or approach on a 6-point scale.
- **Rating 7-point scale** (`Rating 7-point scale`, number, required: false): Select a rating between 1 and 7 to indicate your experience with the principle or approach.
- **Rating 8-point scale** (`Rating 8-point scale`, number, required: false): Rate your experience with the principle or approach on an 8-point scale.
- **Rating 10-point scale** (`Rating 10-point scale`, number, required: false): Select a rating between 1 and 10 to indicate your experience with the principle or approach.
- **Rating 11-point scale** (`Rating 11-point scale`, number, required: false): Rate your experience with the principle or approach on an 11-point scale.
- **Rating 12-point scale** (`Rating 12-point scale`, number, required: false): Rate your experience with the principle or approach on a 12-point scale.
- **Rating 0-point scale** (`Rating 0-point scale`, number, required: false): Rate your experience with the principle or approach on a 0-point scale.
- **Rating 2-point scale** (`Rating 2-point scale`, number, required: false): Select a rating between 1 and 2 to indicate your experience with the principle or approach.
