## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on your experience with various teaching principles or approaches in a reconciliation context. The form aims to gather nuanced ratings on how well different teaching methods are received or experienced.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer according to your experience with the teaching principles listed.
2. For each rating field, select the rating that best reflects your experience.
3. Provide additional comments on your reflections or insights, if any.
