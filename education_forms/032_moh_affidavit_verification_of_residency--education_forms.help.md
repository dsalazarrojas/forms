# Moh Affidavit Verification Of Residency - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to verify the residency status of a student in higher education.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your information:
  * **Moh Affidavit**: Enter a brief description of your educational background.
  * **Education**: Enter your level of education completed (e.g. High School Diploma, GED, Associate, etc.).
  * **School Name**: Enter the name of the school where you attended.
  * **1st Year Attended**: Enter the year you first attended the school.
  * **2nd Year Attended**: Enter the year you last attended the school.
  * **Degree Completed**: Select the degree you completed from the list.
  * **Program of Study**: Enter the name of the program you studied.
  * **Location of Study**: Select where you attended school (In-State or Out-of-State).
  * **Length of Attendance**: Enter the number of years you attended school.
  * **Date of Attendance Start**: Enter the start date of your attendance.
  * **Date of Attendance End**: Enter the end date of your attendance.
  * **Current Status**: Select your current status (e.g. Currently Enrolled, Transferred, Graduated, etc.).
  * **Contact Address**: Enter your contact address.
  * **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
  * **Authorized Representative Name**: Enter the name of your authorized representative.
  * **Authorized Representative Phone**: Enter the phone number of your authorized representative.
  * **Authorized Representative Email**: Enter the email address of your authorized representative.
  * **Authorized Representative Title**: Enter the title of your authorized representative.
  * **Relationship to Student**: Select your relationship to the student (e.g. Self, Parent, Spouse, etc.).
  * **Authorized Representative During Attendance**: Select if your authorized representative was present during your attendance.
  * **Authorized Representative Address**: Enter the address of your authorized representative.
  * **Authorized Representative City**: Enter the city of your authorized representative.
  * **Authorized Representative State**: Enter the state of your authorized representative.
  * **Authorized Representative Zip**: Enter the zip code of your authorized representative.
  * **Authorized Representative Country**: Enter the country of your authorized representative.
