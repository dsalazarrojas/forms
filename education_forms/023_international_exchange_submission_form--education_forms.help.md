# international_exchange_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit international exchange applications or documents. It gathers personal and program-specific information and allows users to upload relevant documents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details:
	* Enter your name.
	* Provide your email address.
	* Enter your phone number (optional).
2. Select your program and semester:
	* Choose the program you are applying for.
	* Select the semester you are applying for (if applicable).
3. Upload documents:
	* Upload the document(s) required for your application.
4. Submit your application:
	* Set the submission date and time.
5. Review and confirm your submission status:
	* Select the status of your submission (Draft, Submitted, Completed, Rejected).
6. Enter additional comments (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your passport or ID.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
- **Program** (`program`, select_one, optional): Select the program you are applying for.
- **Semester** (`semester`, select_multiple, optional): Select the semesters you are applying for (if applicable).
- **Upload Document** (`upload_document`, text, optional): Upload any required documents for your application.
- **Upload Additional Document** (`upload_additional_document`, text, optional): Upload any additional documents (if applicable).
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your submission.
- **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, date, required): Set the date you are submitting your application.
- **Submit Time** (`submit_time`, time, required): Set the time you are submitting your application.
- **Submit Date Time** (`submit_date_time`, date, required): Set the date and time you are submitting your application.
- **Submit Date Time 2** (`submit_date_time2`, date, required): Set the date and time you are submitting your application (if applicable).
- **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of your submission (Draft, Submitted, Completed, Rejected).
- **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required): Enter the name of the person submitting the document.
- **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, date, required): Set the date you submitted the document.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate submission.
* Upload all required documents to avoid submission rejection.
* Review your submission status regularly to ensure accuracy.
* Use the additional comments field for any further information about your submission.
