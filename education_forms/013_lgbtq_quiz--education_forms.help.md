# LGBTQ Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The LGBTQ Quiz is a form designed to assess your knowledge, understanding, and beliefs related to the LGBTQ community. This form is meant to be a helpful tool for education, awareness, and advocacy purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer each question in the text field provided. You can type as much or as little as you like, and all fields are optional. You can navigate through the form by scrolling or by clicking on the "Next" button.
