# Class Year Overview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Class Year Overview Form is a crucial tool for capturing the highlights and challenges of a class's academic year. It is used by class advisors or school administrators to provide an objective overview of the class's performance and to identify areas that require improvement or attention for the next academic year.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Academic Year** in the text field provided. This should be in the format of "YYYY-YYYY" (e.g., 2023-2024).
2. Provide the **Class or Cohort Name** of the class in the text field. This should be a brief name or designation for the class (e.g., Class of 2024, Cohort A).
3. In the **Major Achievements** field, describe the notable achievements of the class, such as academic, athletic, or social accomplishments.
4. In the **Notable Events** field, list any special events, competitions, performances, or milestones that occurred during the academic year.
5. In the **Challenges and Areas for Improvement** section, describe the obstacles the class faced and areas that require improvement for next year.
6. Enter the **Total student enrollment** number, which should be the number of students in the class.
7. Enter the **Average Attendance Rate** as a percentage or decimal (e.g., 95.5%).
8. In the **Special Programs or Initiatives** section, describe any new programs, clubs, or initiatives launched during the academic year.
9. In the **Goals for Next Year** field, describe the goals the class aims to accomplish for the next academic year.
10. Select the **Primary Focus Area** for next year from the provided options (Academic Excellence, Community Engagement, Leadership Development, Wellness and Wellbeing, or Diversity and Inclusion).
11. Enter the **Class Advisor Name** in the text field.
12. Enter the **Report Submission Date**, which should be the date this form is being submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Academic Year**: Enter the academic year (e.g., 2023-2024).
* **Class or Cohort Name**: Enter the name or designation of the class (e.g., Class of 2024, Cohort A).
* **Major Achievements**: Describe academic, athletic, or social accomplishments of the class.
* **Notable Events**: List special events, competitions, performances, or milestones that occurred during the academic year.
* **Challenges and Areas for Improvement**: Describe obstacles encountered academically or socially and areas that require improvement for next year.
* **Total student enrollment**: Enter the number of students in the class.
* **Average Attendance Rate**: Enter the attendance rate as a percentage or decimal (e.g., 95.5%).
* **Special Programs or Initiatives**: Describe new programs, clubs, or initiatives launched during the academic year.
* **Goals for Next Year**: Describe the goals the class aims to accomplish for the next academic year.
* **Primary Focus Area**: Select the primary focus area for next year (Academic Excellence, Community Engagement, Leadership Development, Wellness and Wellbeing, or Diversity and Inclusion).
* **Class Advisor Name**: Enter the name of the primary advisor for the class.
* **Report Submission Date**: Enter the date this form is being submitted.

Note: All fields are mandatory unless otherwise specified.
