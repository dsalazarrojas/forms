# registration_form_for_training_children - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register children for training programs. It collects essential information to ensure accurate and up-to-date records of participant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your child's first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your child's last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your child's email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your child's birth date in the "Birth Date" field (format: dd/mm/yyyy).
5. Enter your child's birth time in the "Birth Time" field (format: hh:mm am/pm).
6. Enter your child's address in the "Address" field.
7. Enter the name of your child's school in the "School Name" field.
8. Provide the name and contact details of your child's emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your child's first name as per their official documents. (Required)
* **Last Name**: Enter your child's last name as per their official documents. (Required)
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that can be used to contact you and your child. (Optional)
* **Birth Date**: Enter your child's birth date in the format dd/mm/yyyy. (Required)
* **Birth Time**: Enter your child's birth time in the format hh:mm am/pm. (Optional)
* **Address**: Enter your child's current address. (Optional)
* **School Name**: Enter your child's school name as per their official documents. (Optional)
* **Emergency Contact**: Provide the name, phone number, and relationship of your child's emergency contact person. (Optional)

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are filled accurately and honestly.
- Double-check for any spelling mistakes or missing information.
- If you're unsure about any information, please consult your child's guardian or relevant documents before proceeding.

Please fill out the form carefully to avoid delays in processing and to maintain accurate records. If you need any further assistance, do not hesitate to contact us.
