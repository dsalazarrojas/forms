# Flamenco Basics Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Flamenco Basics Quiz is a tool designed to gather information about users' knowledge and experience with Flamenco. This form helps us understand what users know and what they need more help with, allowing us to refine our offerings and make Flamenco more effective for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First name
2. Enter your Last name
3. Enter your Contact First Name (optional)
4. Enter your Email (optional)
5. Enter your Phone (optional)
6. Choose your Flamenco knowledge (select all that apply)
7. Enter your Contact Email (optional)
8. Choose your Results (select only one)
9. Enter your Instructor (optional)
10. Enter your School/Program (optional)
11. Enter your Location (optional)
12. Add any comments about your experience with Flamenco
13. Choose who submitted this form (select one)
14. If "Submitted by" is not "Option 1", enter their name (optional)
15. If "Submitted by" is not "Option 1", enter their position (optional)
16. Enter the date this form was submitted (optional)
17. Enter your submitted phone number (optional)
18. Enter the date and time this form was submitted (optional)
19. Enter your submitted email address (optional)
20. Enter the name of the program you are in (optional)
21. Enter the name of your school (optional)
22. Enter the name of your instructor (optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Contact First Name**: This is an optional field for users who want to provide additional contact information.
* **Email**: Enter your email address, if you wish to be contacted for further information.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number, if you wish to be contacted via phone.
* **Flamenco knowledge**: Choose all the options that apply to your experience with Flamenco.
* **Contact Email**: This is an optional field for users who want to provide additional contact information.
* **Results**: Choose the option that best describes your results from using Flamenco.
* **Instructor**: If you had an instructor while using Flamenco, enter their name.
* **School/Program**: If you are in a school or program, enter the name of your school or program.
* **Location**: If you'd like to provide additional context about where you used Flamenco, enter your location.
* **Comments**: Add any comments or feedback you have about your experience with Flamenco.
* **Submitted by**: Choose who submitted this form.
* **Submitted by name**: If "Submitted by" is not "Option 1", enter the name of the person who submitted this form.
* **Submitted by position**: If "Submitted by" is not "Option 1", enter their position or job title.
* **Submitted**: Enter the date and time this form was submitted.
* **Submitted by Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person who submitted this form.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date this form was submitted.
* **Submitted by Email**: Enter the email address of the person who submitted this form.
