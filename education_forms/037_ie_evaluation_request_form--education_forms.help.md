# IE Evaluation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from schools about their evaluation requests for the Individual Evaluation (IE) process. This process helps to identify areas of improvement in schools and ensures that they are meeting the necessary standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of your school team in the `school_team_name` field.
2. Enter your name as the student or parent submitting the evaluation request in the `student_name` field.
3. Enter the name of the parent or guardian associated with the student in the `parent_name` field.
4. Choose a date for the evaluation in the `evaluation_date` field.
5. Enter the location where the evaluation will take place in the `evaluation_location` field.
6. Provide a brief description of the concern that led to the evaluation request in the `concern_description` field.
7. (Optional) If you have any further concerns or additional information that you would like to provide, enter them in the `follow_up_concerns` field.
8. (Optional) If you have any additional comments or information that you would like to provide, enter them in the `additional_comments` field.
9. Finally, enter the name of the staff member assigned to the evaluation in the `assigned_staff_member` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **school_team** (`school_team_name`, required): Enter the name of your school team, which is the team responsible for the student's care and education.
* **student_name** (`student_name`, required): Enter your name as the student or parent submitting the evaluation request.
* **parent_name** (`parent_name`, required): Enter the name of the parent or guardian associated with the student.
* **evaluation_date** (`evaluation_date`, required): Choose a date for the evaluation. This is the date when the evaluation will take place.
* **evaluation_location** (`evaluation_location`, required): Enter the location where the evaluation will take place.
* **concern_description** (`concern_description`, required): Provide a brief description of the concern that led to the evaluation request.
* **follow_up_concerns** (`follow_up_concerns`, optional): If you have any further concerns or additional information that you would like to provide, enter them here.
* **additional_comments** (`additional_comments`, optional): If you have any additional comments or information that you would like to provide, enter them here.
* **assigned_staff_member** (`assigned_staff_member`, required): Enter the name of the staff member assigned to the evaluation.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, please contact us.
* This form is for internal use only and any personal data collected will be kept confidential and used for internal purposes only.
