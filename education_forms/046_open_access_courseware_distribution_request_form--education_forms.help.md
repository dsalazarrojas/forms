# Open Access Courseware Distribution Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Open Access Courseware Distribution Request Form is designed to facilitate the process of requesting open access to courseware for students. This form ensures that the correct information is collected to provide access to courseware, while also avoiding duplication of effort and misallocated resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your first name, last name, and email address to identify yourself as the requester.
2. Specify the course title and institution you are affiliated with.
3. Select the type of access you are requesting for the course.
4. Choose the department or office you belong to.
5. Provide the date of birth in day, month, and year format.
6. Enter the date of request in day, month, and year format.
7. Choose the request type and purpose.
8. If applicable, provide the start and end dates and times for the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your official university email address.
* **Course Title** (`course_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the course for which you are requesting open access.
* **Institution** (`institution`, text, optional): Enter the name of the institution you are affiliated with.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the department you belong to.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in day, month, year format (e.g., 01, 12, 1990).
* **Date of Request** (`date_of_request`, date, required): Enter the date you are submitting this request in day, month, year format (e.g., 01, 12, 2023).
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter a contact phone number if you want to be reached for further questions or clarification.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, note, optional): Enter any additional details or context about your request.
* **What are you?** (`select_one_what_career`, select_one, required): Select one of the options to specify what you are (e.g., student, staff).
* **What department or office do you belong to?** (`select_multiple_what_career`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the departments or offices you are associated with.
* **Day** (`date_of_birth_day`, number, required): Enter the day of your birth.
* **Month** (`date_of_birth_month`, number, required): Enter the month of your birth.
* **Year** (`date_of_birth_year`, number, required): Enter the year of your birth.
* **Date Of Request Day** (`date_of_request_day`, number, required): Enter the day you are submitting this request.
* **Date Of Request Month** (`date_of_request_month`, number, required): Enter the month you are submitting this request.
* **Date Of Request Year** (`date_of_request_year`, number, required): Enter the year you are submitting this request.
* **Time** (`time_of_request`, time, optional): Enter the time you are submitting this request (e.g., 12:00 PM).
* **Request Type** (`select_one_request_type`, select_one, required): Select one of the options to specify the type of request (e.g., course evaluation, course adoption).
* **What are you requesting for?** (`select_multiple_request_what_for`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the reasons why you are requesting open access.
* **Start Date** (`date_range_start`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the request period.
* **End Date** (`date_range_end`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the request period.
* **Start Time** (`date_range_start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the request period.
* **End Time** (`date_range_end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of the request period.

## Tips
* Double-check your selections and entries for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact your local support team for assistance.
