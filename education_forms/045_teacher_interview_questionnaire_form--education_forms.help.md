# Teacher Interview Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The **Teacher Interview Questionnaire Form** is designed to collect information about your contact details, teaching experience, and availability for teaching positions. This will help us get to know you better and match your qualifications with potential teaching opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your **Contact Information** (fields 1-4) with your name, email address, phone number, and any other contact method you prefer.
2. Select your **Years of Experience** (field 5) and list any teaching certifications you have earned (e.g., Bachelor's degree, Master's degree, Teaching Certification).
3. Choose your **Teaching Areas** (field 6) that you are proficient in (e.g., Math, Science, English, History).
4. List any **Languages Spoken** (field 7) you are proficient in (e.g., English, Spanish, Mandarin).
5. Provide an overview of your **Education Background** (field 8).
6. Specify your **Availability** (fields 9-10) for teaching, including the time of day and day of the week.
7. Fill in any additional **Contact Information** (fields 11-14) as needed.
8. If you have an **Online Application** (field 14), please let us know.
9. Attach a copy of your **Resume** (field 15).
10. If you have **References** (fields 17-20), please list their names and provide any necessary contact information.
11. Finally, provide any additional **Other Information** (field 21) you would like to share.
12. Choose your **Submission** option (field 25) to either **Submit** your application, **Save for Later**, or **Cancel**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (1)** (`name`, `text`, required): Fill in your name.
* **Contact Information (2)** (`email`, `email`, required): Fill in your email address.
* **Years of Experience (3)** (`years_of_experience`, `number`, required): Select your years of experience.
* **Teaching Certifications (4)** (`teaching_certifications`, `select_multiple`, required): List all teaching certifications you have earned.
* **Teaching Areas (5)** (`teaching_areas`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all subject areas you are proficient in.
* **Languages Spoken (6)** (`languages_spoken`, `select_multiple`, required): List all languages spoken.
* **Education Background (7)** (`education_background`, `note`, required): Provide an overview of your education background.
* **Availability (8)** (`availability`, `date`, required): Specify your availability for teaching.
* **Availability (9)** (`availability_2`, `time`, required): Specify the day of the week you are available to teach.
* **Email (10)** (`email`, `email`, required): Fill in your email address.
* **Contact Method (11)** (`contact_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred contact method (e.g., Phone, Email, Online Application).
* **Phone (12)** (`phone`, `text`, required): Fill in your phone number.
* **Address (13)** (`address`, `text`, required): Fill in your address.
* **Online Application (14)** (`online_application`, `select_one`, required): Let us know if you have an online application available.
* **Resume (15)** (`resume`, `text`, required): Attach a copy of your resume.
* **References (17)** (`references`, `select_one`, required): Let us know if you have professional references available.
* **Reference Names (18)** (`reference_names`, `text`, required): List all professional references.
* **Reference Relationship (19)** (`reference_relationship`, `select_multiple`, required): Specify your relationship with each reference.
* **Reference Phone (20)** (`reference_phone`, `text`, required): Fill in the phone number of each reference.
* **Reference Email (21)** (`reference_email`, `email`, required): Fill in the email address of each reference.
* **Reference Address (22)** (`reference_address`, `text`, required): Fill in the address of each reference.
* **Other Reference Information (23)** (`other_reference_info`, `text`, required): Provide any additional information about each reference.
* **Submission (24)** (`submission`, `select_one`, required): Choose your submission option (e.g., Submit, Save for Later, Cancel).
