# digital_learning_experience_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather data on the digital learning experience of students. The main goal is to collect information on their preferences, behaviors, and perceptions regarding online courses.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select all applicable options that apply to you.
3. For text boxes, provide as much detail as you can.
4. If you have any difficulties or concerns, please let us know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Please provide your full name as it appears on your student ID or in your university records.
* **Teacher ID** (`teacher_id`, number, optional): This field is not used in this form. Please leave it blank.
* **Class Name** (`class_name`, text, optional): Please provide the name of your class or the course you are taking.
* **Survey Start Date** (`survey_start_date`, date, optional): This field is not used in this form. Please leave it blank.
* **Survey End Date** (`survey_end_date`, date, optional): This field is not used in this form. Please leave it blank.
* **Have you ever taken an online course?** (`question1`, select_one, optional): Please answer with either 'True' or 'False'. 
* **How often do you take online courses?** (`question2`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all applicable options that apply to you (e.g., Rarely, Occasionally, Frequently).
* **What type of device do you use most frequently for taking online courses?** (`question3`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all applicable options that apply to you (e.g., Laptop, Tablet, Phone, Other).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Please provide any additional information or comments about your learning experience.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please provide your university email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Please provide your personal phone number.
* **How do you prefer to learn?** (`question4`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all applicable options that apply to you (e.g., Video, Audio, Reading, Other).
* **What type of content do you prefer to learn?** (`question5`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all applicable options that apply to you (e.g., Text, Images, Videos, Audio).
* **How do you prefer to learn in a group?** (`question6`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all applicable options that apply to you (e.g., With others, Alone, Both).
* **What time of day do you prefer to learn?** (`question7`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all applicable options that apply to you (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Any time).
* **What is your level of experience with the subject matter?** (`question8`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all applicable options that apply to you (e.g., Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
* **On a scale of 1-5, how engaged do you feel in online learning?** (`question9`, number, optional): Please rate your level of engagement on a scale of 1-5.
* **On a scale of 1-5, how satisfied are you with online learning?** (`question10`, number, optional): Please rate your level of satisfaction on a scale of 1-5.
* **On a scale of 1-5, what is the level of support you received from teachers while taking online courses?** (`question11`, number, optional): Please rate the level of support you received from teachers on a scale of 1-5.
* **On a scale of 1-5, what is the level of support you received from fellow students while taking online courses?** (`question12`, number, optional): Please rate the level of support you received from fellow students on a scale of 1-5.
* **How do you usually access online courses?** (`question13`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all applicable options that apply to you (e.g., Computer, Phone, Tablet, Other).
* **What do you usually do after an online course?** (`question14`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all applicable options that apply to you (e.g., Apply what I learned, Discuss with others, Do nothing, Other).
* **What do you usually do after an online course?** (`question15`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all applicable options that apply to you (e.g., Apply what I learned, Discuss with others, Do nothing, Other).
* **Do you usually have any difficulties with taking online courses?** (`question16`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all applicable options that apply to you (e.g., 'True', 'False', Other).
* **Do you usually take breaks while taking online courses?** (`question17`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all applicable options that apply to you (e.g., 'True', 'False', Other).

## Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure or have any questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to your teacher or administrator.
* Your responses will be kept confidential and anonymous.
* This form is not a test, so there are no right or wrong answers.
* Take your time and be as detailed as you can.
* If you have any feedback or suggestions, please let us know!
