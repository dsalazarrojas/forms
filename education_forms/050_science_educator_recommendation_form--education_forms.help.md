# Science Educator Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Science Educator Recommendation Form is a tool for science educators to provide recommendations for students who are applying for various programs or scholarships.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **User Input**: Enter a brief description of the student you are recommending.
2. **Science Educator Info**: Provide your name, title, and school affiliation.
3. **Recommendation**: Write a detailed recommendation for the student, including their strengths and areas for improvement.
4. **School Name**: Enter the name of the school where you had an interaction with the student.
5. **Science Educator Position**: Select the position you hold at your school (e.g., professor, instructor, teacher).
6. **Educational Background**: Briefly describe your own educational background and qualifications.
7. **Teaching Experience**: Provide a brief description of your teaching experience.
8. **Recommendation Date**: Enter the date of your recommendation.
9. **Signature**: Provide your signature as a science educator.
10. **User Comments**: Add any additional comments or thoughts about the student.

Please note that all fields marked as "required: false" are optional, and you should only provide information if necessary. Also, ensure that you provide a genuine and detailed recommendation, and that you are familiar with the student's work.
