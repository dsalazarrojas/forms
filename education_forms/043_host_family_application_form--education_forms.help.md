# Host Family Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Host Family Application Form is used by host families to provide information about themselves and their families to host international students or exchange visitors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide detailed information about your family, including your address, contact details, and experience as a host family.
2. Share your education background and any relevant qualifications or skills that may be beneficial for hosting.
3. Describe your family structure, including the number and ages of family members.
4. List your language proficiency, including the languages you speak at home and any languages you are proficient in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Host Family Application Form** (`host_family_application_form`, text, optional): This is the main section where you can introduce your family and tell us about your experience as a host family.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Please provide a detailed address for your home, including your city, state, and country.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Share your contact information, including phone numbers and email addresses.
* **Experience As A Host Family** (`experience_host_family`, text, optional): Describe your past experience as a host family, including any relevant events or experiences you've had.
* **Education Background** (`education_background`, text, optional): Share your educational background, including any relevant qualifications or skills you have.
* **Family Structure** (`family_structure`, text, optional): Describe your family structure, including the number and ages of family members.
* **Language Proficiency** (`language_proficiency`, text, optional): List the languages you speak at home and any languages you are proficient in.

## Tips
* Please provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth hosting process.
* If you are unsure about any section, please contact us for assistance.
* This form is an initial step in the hosting process, and we will review your application and follow up with further questions or requests for more information.
