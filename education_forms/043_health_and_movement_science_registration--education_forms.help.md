# health and movement science registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect user information for Health and Movement Science registration. It is not affiliated with any specific platform and is designed to gather essential details from users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields with the requested information. The form is divided into three sections: User Information, Contact Details, and Emergency Contact. Each section contains a single field where you can enter your details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Information
* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter your name, title, or any other relevant information about yourself.

### Contact Details
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Here, you can enter your contact information, such as your phone number, email, or any other contact method you'd like to provide.

### Emergency Contact
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): If you'd like to provide an emergency contact person's information, you can enter their name, relationship to you, and contact details in this field.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date information in each field.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask for clarification.
* You can always save your progress and come back to complete the form later, if needed.
* If you're having trouble accessing the form or have any issues during completion, contact our support team for assistance.
