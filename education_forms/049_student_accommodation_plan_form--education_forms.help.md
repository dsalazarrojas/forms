# Student Accommodation Plan Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Accommodation Plan Form is designed to collect information from students and their guardians to plan and manage student accommodation. This form helps to gather necessary details such as student and guardian contact information, accommodation type, and move-in dates and times.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions according to your situation.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for the questions that ask for binary information.
3. Enter relevant details in the text fields such as student and guardian names, emails, phone numbers, and addresses.
4. Choose the correct accommodation type and status for your stay.
5. If you have any additional information to provide, use the "Additional Info" field.
