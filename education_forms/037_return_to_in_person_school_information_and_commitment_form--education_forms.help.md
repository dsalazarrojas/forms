# Return To In Person School Information And Commitment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about students returning to in-person school after a period of online or remote learning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the parent's information on **Page 1: Parent Information**.
2. Enter the student's information on **Page 2: Student Information**.
3. Confirm the parent's and teacher's availability to return to in-person school on **Page 3: Parent Confirmation** and **Page 4: Teacher Confirmation**.
4. Indicate the meeting preferences for the student on **Page 5: Meeting Preferences**.
5. List the school preferences for the student on **Page 6: School Preferences**.
6. Enter the student's commitment date on **Page 7: Student Commitment**.
7. Provide the guardian's information on **Page 8: Guardian Information**.
8. Enter the emergency contact information on **Page 9: Emergency Contact**.
9. Confirm the school's commitment to return to in-person school on **Page 10: School Commitment**.
10. Indicate if any other commitments need to be made on **Page 11: Other Commitment**.
11. Add any additional notes or comments on **Page 12: Notes**.
12. Finally, submit the form by selecting **Yes** on **Page 13: Submit**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Information**: Enter the parent's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Student Information**: Enter the student's name, date of birth, and any other relevant details.
* **Parent Confirmation**: Confirm that the parent is available to return to in-person school. Select **Yes** if this is the case.
* **Teacher Confirmation**: Confirm that the teacher is available to return to in-person school. Select **Yes** if this is the case.
* **Meeting Preferences**: Indicate the meeting preferences for the student, such as the meeting time and frequency.
* **School Preferences**: List the school preferences for the student, such as the school name and grade level.
* **Student Commitment**: Enter the date the student is committed to return to in-person school.
* **Guardian Information**: Provide the guardian's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the emergency contact person's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **School Commitment**: Confirm that the school is committed to return to in-person school. Select **Yes** if this is the case.
* **Other Commitment**: Indicate if any other commitments need to be made for the student's return. Select **Yes** if this is the case.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the student's return.
* **Submit**: Select **Yes** to submit the form.
