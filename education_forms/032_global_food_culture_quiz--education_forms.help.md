# Global Food Culture Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your global food culture. Please answer the questions to the best of your abilities, and don't worry about thinking too much; just be honest and natural in your responses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and choose the option that best represents your preference or behavior.
2. Select one or multiple answers depending on the question type (e.g., select one or select multiple).
3. Be honest and accurate in your responses.
4. If you're unsure about a question, try to choose the option that best fits your typical behavior or preference.
5. Take as much time as you need to complete the form; there's no rush.
