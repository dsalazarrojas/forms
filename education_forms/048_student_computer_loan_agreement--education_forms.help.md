# Student Computer Loan Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Computer Loan Agreement form is used to collect information from students borrowing a computer from the institution for academic purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information, including your Student ID, Loan Amount, Loan Date, and Return Date.
2. If you have borrowed a computer from the institution, please ensure that you have the correct make and model of the computer.
3. You can optionally provide your Student Name and Contact Number for further contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, required): A unique identifier used by the institution to track your student records.
* **Computer Make** (`computer_make`, text, optional): The make of the computer you are borrowing.
* **Computer Model** (`computer_model`, text, optional): The model of the computer you are borrowing.
* **Computer Year** (`computer_year`, text, optional): The year of the computer model (if known).
* **Loan Amount** (`loan_amount`, number, required): The amount borrowed for the loan.
* **Loan Date** (`loan_date`, date, required): The date the loan began.
* **Return Date** (`return_date`, date, required): The date the loan is due to be returned.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Your name for contact purposes.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Your contact number for further communication.
