# Final Study Guide Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Final Study Guide Quiz is a comprehensive form designed to help create a study guide quiz that assesses knowledge or understanding of a subject matter.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the category for your quiz from the Category field.
2. Fill in the Section 1 - Review field with a brief description of the topic or subject matter.
3. In the Section 2 - Questions field, enter 2-5 questions with their corresponding correct answers.
4. In the Section 2 - Answers field, select the correct answer for each question.
5. Repeat steps 3-4 for each subsequent section (Section 3, Section 4, etc.).
6. In the Confidence level field, enter your confidence level in the subject matter.
7. Once you've completed all sections, click the Submit button to submit your quiz.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Section 1 - Review
* **Section 1 - Review** (`section_1_review`, `text`, required: false)
  This field is for a brief description of the topic or subject matter. It will help users set the context for the quiz.

### Section 2 - Questions
* **Section 2 - Questions** (`section_2_questions`, `text`, required: false)
  This field allows users to enter 2-5 questions with their corresponding correct answers.

### Section 2 - Answers
* **Section 2 - Answers** (`section_2_answers`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
  In this field, select the correct answer for each question entered in Section 2 - Questions.

### Section 3 - Review
* **Section 3 - Review** (`section_3_review`, `text`, required: false)
  This field is for another brief description of the topic or subject matter.

### Section 4 - Questions
* **Section 4 - Questions** (`section_4_questions`, `text`, required: false)
  Similar to Section 2 - Questions, this field allows users to enter 2-5 questions with their corresponding correct answers.

### Section 4 - Answers
* **Section 4 - Answers** (`section_4_answers`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
  In this field, select the correct answer for each question entered in Section 4 - Questions.

### Confidence level
* **Confidence level** (`confidence_1`, `number`, required: false)
  Enter your confidence level in the subject matter on a scale.

### Section 5 - Review
* **Section 5 - Review** (`section_5_review`, `text`, required: false)
  This field is for a final description of the topic or subject matter.

### Submit

### Final Feedback
