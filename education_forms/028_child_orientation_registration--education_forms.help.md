# Child Orientation Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Child Orientation Registration form is designed to gather details from parents or guardians about their child's orientation preferences, personal details, and contact information. This information will help us tailor the orientation session to their needs and ensure a smooth communication process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select your preferred orientation session**: Please choose the time that works best for you among "Morning - 9AM", "Afternoon - 2PM", or "Evening - 6PM".
2. **Enter your child's full name**: Provide the full name of the student as it appears on their official documents.
3. **Enter your child's date of birth**: This information is crucial for grade placement and is usually found on the child's official documents.
4. **Provide your contact information**: Enter your primary phone number and email address for any important updates or notifications.
5. **Specify childcare needs**: If your child will require supervision during the session, please let us know so we can make necessary arrangements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Preferred Orientation Session**: Select the time that works best for you, which will help us schedule the session accordingly.
* **Child Full Name**: Enter the full legal name of the child as it appears on their official documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your child's date of birth to ensure accurate grade placement.
* **Primary Guardian Name**: Enter your full legal name as the primary contact person.
* **Primary Email Address**: Enter your email address for any important updates or notifications.
* **Primary Phone Number**: Enter your primary phone number for session reminders and updates.
* **Will you require childcare during the session?**: If your child will need supervision during the session, please select "Yes - Need Childcare" so we can make necessary arrangements.
* **Interests and Hobbies**: Select all the interests and hobbies that apply to your child from the provided options.
* **Any questions for orientation?**: If you have any questions or topics you'd like to discuss during the session, please provide them here.
* **Additional Information**: If you have any other relevant information about your child, please provide it here.
