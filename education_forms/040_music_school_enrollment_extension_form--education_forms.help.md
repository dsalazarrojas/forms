# music_school_enrollment_extension_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The music_school_enrollment_extension_form is designed to collect information from students and parents for enrolling students in a music school. This form helps to gather necessary details about the student and their school to facilitate the enrollment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter the parent's name in the "Parent Name" field.
3. Enter the student's email address in the "Email" field. Please use a valid email format (e.g., `john.doe@example.com`).
4. Provide the student's phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter the school year in the "School Year" field. This can be a numeric value (e.g., `9` for ninth grade).
6. Select the instrument the student is interested in from the list of available options.
7. Enter the date of the student's potential enrollment in the "Enrollment Date" field. Please use the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
8. Add any additional comments from the teacher in the "Teacher Comments" field.
9. Select the student's current grade level from the dropdown menu:
	* Kindergarten
	* Elementary
	* Middle School
	* High School
	* College

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (Student Name, text, required): Enter the student's name as it appears on official documents.
* **Parent Name** (Parent Name, text, optional): Enter the parent's name if applicable.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): A valid email address for the student or parent.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): A phone number for the student or parent.
* **School Year** (School Year, number, optional): The student's current grade level (e.g., 9 for 9th grade).
* **Instrument** (Instrument, text, optional): Select the instrument the student is interested in learning.
* **Enrollment Date** (Enrollment Date, date, optional): Enter the date of the potential enrollment.
* **Teacher Comments** (Teacher Comments, text, optional): Additional comments from the teacher.
* **Student Grade** (Student Grade, number, optional): The student's current grade level.
* **School Level** (School Level, select_one, required): Select the student's current level of education (Kindergarten, Elementary, Middle School, High School, College).
* **Music School Name** (Music School Name, text, optional): Name of the music school or organization.

## Tips
* Make sure to use a valid email address in the "Email" field.
* Use the correct format for the "Enrollment Date" field (YYYY-MM-DD).
* Choose the correct school level from the dropdown menu for "School Level".
* If the student is not currently enrolled in school, select "Kindergarten" for "School Level" if the student is not yet in school, otherwise select the appropriate level.
