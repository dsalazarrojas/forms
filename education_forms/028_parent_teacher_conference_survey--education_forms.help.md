# parent-teacher-conference-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from parents or guardians about their experience at a parent-teacher conference. Completing this form will help us understand your thoughts, opinions, and suggestions for future conferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply click on each field and type your answer. For questions with multiple options, select one or more of the available choices that best fit your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Conference Experience (page 1, id: 1)
* **What was your experience at the conference?** (`conference_experience`, text, optional): Share your thoughts about your experience at the conference. Please type in your response in the text box below.
* **How would you rate the communication between your child's teacher and you?** (`communication`, select_one, optional): Rate the communication between your child's teacher and you using the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Do you have any comments about the conference?** (`comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions about the conference, type them in the text box below.
* **How would you rate the communication between your child's teacher and you?** (`teacher_communication`, select_multiple, optional): Rate the communication between your child's teacher and you using the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **How would you rate the communication between you and your child's teacher?** (`communication_between_parents`, select_one, optional): Rate the communication between you and your child's teacher using the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **How likely are you to attend future parent-teacher conferences?** (`future_conferences`, select_multiple, optional): Select how likely you are to attend future parent-teacher conferences using the options: Definitely, Somewhat, Not very, Not at all.
* **Do you have any suggestions for improving future parent-teacher conferences?** (`suggestions`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions or ideas for improving future conferences, type them in the text box below.
* **Additional feedback about the conference** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share any additional feedback or thoughts about the conference.
* **How would you rate the communication between you and your child's teacher?** (`communication_between_parents`, select_one, optional): Rate the communication between you and your child's teacher using the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **How likely are you to attend future parent-teacher conferences?** (`future_conferences`, select_multiple, optional): Select how likely you are to attend future parent-teacher conferences using the options: Definitely, Somewhat, Not very, Not at all.

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough in your responses. Your feedback is valuable to us.
* If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We appreciate your time and effort in completing this form.
