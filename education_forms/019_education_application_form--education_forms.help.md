# education-application-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from prospective students applying for educational programs or services. It includes fields for basic details, contact information, education level, previous education, and other relevant information.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your basic details, including your prospective-student field.
2. Enter your contact information in the contact-information field.
3. Choose your education level from the options provided in the education-level field.
4. Briefly describe your previous education in the previous-education field.
5. If applicable, mention any academic achievements in the academic-achievements field.
6. List any personal qualifications that may be relevant to your application.
7. Provide references, if any.
8. Check the consent-to-share box if you consent to share your information.
9. Upload any supporting documents, if required.
10. Finally, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **prospective-student** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your name or ID number.
* **contact-information** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your contact information, including email, phone number, and address.
* **education-level** (`select_one`, required/optional): Choose your current education level. For example, High or Low.
* **previous-education** (`text`, required/optional): Briefly describe your previous education, including any relevant courses, degrees, or certifications.
* **student-availability** (`date`, required/optional): Enter your availability, if any.
* **school-history** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Check all that apply for your school history, for example, "Yes" for attended a school with a specific focus.
* **academic-achievements** (`text`, required/optional): List any relevant academic achievements, such as awards, honors, or publications.
* **personal-qualifications** (`text`, required/optional): Enter any personal qualifications, such as language proficiency or certifications.
* **references** (`text`, required/optional): Provide names and contact information for any references.
* **consent-to-share** (`select_one`, required/optional): Check "Yes" if you consent to share your information.
* **uploaded-document** (`text`, required/optional): Upload any supporting documents, if required.
* **prophets** (`text`, required/optional): (Note: This field is not well-defined in the form, so no explanation is provided)
* **submitted-on** (`date`, required/optional): Enter the date you submitted the form.
* **uploaded-document** (`text`, required/optional): Upload any supporting documents, if required.

Please note that the "prophets" field is not clearly defined and is likely a placeholder or redundant field.
