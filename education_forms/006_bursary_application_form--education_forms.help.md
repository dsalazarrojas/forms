# Bursary Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Bursary Application Form is designed to assist students in applying for bursary support. The form collects personal and academic details, financial information, and supporting documentation from applicants to determine their eligibility for a bursary. 

To ensure a successful application, please ensure that you have all necessary supporting documents and that your answers accurately reflect your current situation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your personal details, including your full name, student ID number, and contact email address. Please ensure that your email address is accurate as you will receive important updates and instructions via this email.
2. Specify your current programme of study and the year you are currently studying.
3. Estimate your household annual income and select the corresponding category.
4. Indicate the number of dependents in your household, including yourself.
5. In the statement of financial hardship, describe your reasons for seeking bursary support in 500 words or less. Please be clear and concise in your explanation.
6. If you receive other sources of funding, list them in this field.
7. Read and agree to the conditions for data sharing.
8. Finally, select your date of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name of Applicant** (`applicat_full_name`, text, required): Enter your complete legal name as it appears on your identification.
* **Student Identification Number** (`student_id_number`, text, required): Enter your student ID number as issued by your institution.
* **Contact Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your university email address or any other email that is best for us to contact you.
* **Current Programme of Study** (`current_programme_of_study`, text, required): Enter the programme of study that you are currently enrolled in.
* **Year of Study** (`year_of_study`, select_one, required): Select the year you are currently studying (e.g., Year 1, Year 2, Year 3, Year 4, or Postgraduate).
* **Estimated Household Annual Income** (`household_annual_income`, select_one, required): Select the estimated household income category (Below 20,000 USD, 20,001 - 40,000 USD, 40,001 - 60,000 USD, 60,001 - 80,000 USD, Above 80,000 USD).
* **Number of Dependents in Household** (`dependents_count`, number, required): Enter the total number of dependents in your household, including yourself.
* **Statement of Financial Hardship** (`financial_hardship_statement`, text, required): Provide a clear and concise statement explaining why you are applying for bursary support.
* **Other Current Funding Sources** (`current_funding_sources`, text, optional): If you receive other sources of funding, list them here.
* **Supporting Documentation** (`supporting_docs`, note, optional): Please note that you will be required to upload proof of income later.
* **I consent to the Verification of my Financial Status** (`data_sharing_consent`, select_one, required): Select yes if you agree to the verification of your financial status. Select no if you do not.
* **Date of Submission** (`application_date`, date, required): Select the date you are submitting this application.

Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately, and do not hesitate to contact us if you need further assistance.
