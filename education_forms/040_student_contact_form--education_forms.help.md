# Student Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Contact Form is designed to collect contact information and demographic details of a student from their parent/guardian, which will help in categorizing and understanding the students' backgrounds for better communication and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Parent Name** field with the name of the student's parent or guardian.
2. Enter the **Contact Number** of the parent or guardian in the format of a phone number.
3. Provide the **Email** address of the parent or guardian.
4. Indicate whether the student is a **Student Grade** (yes or no).
5. Enter the **Student Name** field with the name of the student.
6. Select the **Parent Occupation** field with the occupation of the student's parent or guardian.
7. Select all that apply in the **Relationship** field with the relationship between the parent or guardian and the student (Single, Married, Partnered).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Name** (Parent Name, text, Required/Optional): Enter the name of the student's parent or guardian.
* **Contact Number** (Contact Number, number, Required/Optional): Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian.
* **Email** (Email, email, Required/Optional): Enter the email address of the parent or guardian.
* **Student Grade** (Student Grade, select_one, Required/Optional): Indicate whether the student is a yes or no.
* **Student Name** (Student Name, text, Required/Optional): Enter the name of the student.
* **Parent Occupation** (Parent Occupation, text, Required/Optional): Select the occupation of the student's parent or guardian.
* **Relationship** (Relationship, select_multiple, Required/Optional): Select all that apply for the relationship between the parent or guardian and the student (Single, Married, Partnered).
