# Tech Tutoring Session Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tech Tutoring Session Signup Form is for students to sign up for one-on-one tech sessions with certified tutors. This form helps in maintaining a record of the sessions, ensuring that both parties involved are aware of the session details.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out each field with accurate and complete information.
- Ensure all required fields are filled before submission.
- Double-check session details for any errors after submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Session Details**: 
  * **Session Details** (`session_details`, text, optional)
    Enter a brief description of the session you are seeking. This could be about the topic you wish to cover during the session.
    
- **Session Date**:
  * **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, optional)
    Enter the date you are available for the session. The format should be in MM/DD/YY (month/day/year).

- **Session Time**:
  * **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, optional)
    Enter the time you are available for the session. The format should be in 12-hour clock format (e.g., 2:00 PM).

- **Student Name**:
  * **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional)
    Enter your name for the session. This helps identify you as the student.

- **Student Email**:
  * **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, optional)
    Enter your email address for contact purposes. This is where session reminders or updates will be sent.

- **Student Phone**:
  * **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, optional)
    Enter your phone number for any emergency or last-minute updates.

- **Tutor Name**:
  * **Tutor Name** (`tutor_name`, text, optional)
    Enter the name of your tech tutor. This ensures they receive the correct information for the session.

- **Tutor Email**:
  * **Tutor Email** (`tutor_email`, email, optional)
    Enter your tech tutor's email address for contact purposes. Updates or reminders will be sent to this address.

- **Tutor Phone**:
  * **Tutor Phone** (`tutor_phone`, text, optional)
    Enter your tech tutor's phone number for any emergency or last-minute updates.

- **Session Topic**:
  * **Session Topic** (`session_topic`, text, optional)
    Enter the topic you wish to cover during the session. This helps the tutor prepare accordingly.

- **Session Duration**:
  * **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, number, optional)
    Enter the length of the session in minutes.

- **Session Status**:
  * **Session Status** (`session_status`, select_one, optional)
    Select the status of the session: Available, Not Available, or In Progress.

- **Session Notes**:
  * **Session Notes** (`session_note`, note, optional)
    Enter any additional information about the session.

- **Event Date**:
  * **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional)
    Enter the date for the event or session. 

Note: This form does not currently support saving of sessions, and students can sign up for multiple sessions. Please review this form carefully to ensure accurate session planning. 

**Tips:**
- Double-check session details for accuracy.
- Ensure all required fields are filled in.
- Review session status for up-to-date availability.
