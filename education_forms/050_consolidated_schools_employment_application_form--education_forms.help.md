# Consolidated Schools Employment Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for employment applications at Consolidated Schools. It helps the school's HR team assess your qualifications, experience, and availability for potential employment opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your contact information (Application Date, Position Applied For, and Department).
2. Provide your educational background and qualifications (University or College, Degree Earned, Major, and Graduation Year).
3. List your work experience, including your previous employer, position, and employment dates.
4. Answer questions related to your availability and desired salary.
5. Sign and date the form to confirm your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Date (1)**: Enter the date you're submitting your application.
* **Position Applied For (2)**: List the job title you're applying for.
* **Department (3)**: Choose the department or subject area you're interested in.
* **Full Name (4)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Preferred Name (5)**: Enter your preferred name, if different from your full name.
* **Address (6)**: Enter your mailing address.
* **City (7)**: Enter your city of residence.
* **State (8)**: Enter your state or province of residence.
* **Postal Code (9)**: Enter your postal or ZIP code.
* **Phone Number (10)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Email (11)**: Enter your email address.
* **Date of Birth (12)**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Last Four of SSN (13)**: Enter the last four digits of your Social Security Number for background check purposes.
* **Are You Authorized to Work (14)**: Select your immigration status (US Citizen, Permanent Resident, or Work Visa).
* **Highest Education Level (15)**: Select your highest educational level (High school diploma, Associate degree, etc.).
* **University or College (16)**: Enter the name of your university or college.
* **Degree Earned (17)**: Enter the type of degree you've earned.
* **Major (18)**: Enter the field of study.
* **Graduation Year (19)**: Enter the year you graduated.
* **Do You Have Teaching Certification (20)**: Select if you have teaching certification.
* **Certification Type (21)**: If yes, enter the type of teaching certification.
* **State of Certification (22)**: If yes, enter the state or province where you're certified.
* **Certification Expiry Date (23)**: If yes, enter the date your certification expires.
* **Years of Teaching Experience (24)**: Select the total years you've been teaching.
* **Grade Levels You Can Teach (25)**: Select all grade levels you're qualified to teach.
* **Subject Areas (26)**: Enter the subjects you can teach.
* **Current or Previous Employer (27)**: Enter your current or previous employer.
* **Previous Position (28)**: Enter your previous job title.
* **Employment Dates (29)**: Enter your employment start and end dates.
* **Reason for Leaving (30)**: Explain why you left your previous job.
* **Supervisor Name (31)**: Enter the name of your supervisor or reference contact.
* **Supervisor Phone (32)**: Enter your supervisor's phone number.
* **Supervisor Email (33)**: Enter your supervisor's email address.
* **Additional References (34)**: List other professional references.
* **Special Skills or Qualifications (35)**: Enter your special skills or qualifications.
* **Languages Spoken (36)**: Enter the languages you speak.
* **Technology Skills (37)**: Enter your technology skills or experience.
* **Availability (38)**: Enter when you can start working.
* **Desired Salary (39)**: Enter your desired salary.
* **Do You Have Criminal History (40)**: Select if you have criminal history.
* **Criminal History Details (41)**: If yes, explain the details.
* **Consent to Background Check (42)**: Select if you consent to a background check.
* **Consent to Drug Test (43)**: Select if you consent to a drug test.
* **Consent to Fingerprinting (44)**: Select if you consent to fingerprinting.
* **Equal Opportunity Statement (45)**: Select if you understand the school's equal opportunity statement.
* **Truthfulness Statement (46)**: Select if you certify the accuracy of your application.
* **Applicant Signature (47)**: Sign your full name.
* **Date of Signature (48)**: Date of signing.
* **Application Status (49)**: Leave blank for administrative use.

Note that the required fields are marked with a "*". Ensure you fill out all required fields accurately to ensure a successful application.
