# Change Of Class Schedule Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Change of Class Schedule Form is a document used by students to request updates to their class schedules. This form helps students and administrators to track and process such requests in an organized and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out the form with your information in the following order:
	* Request Date: Please enter the date you are making the request.
	* Student Name: Enter your full name as it appears on your school records.
	* Student ID Number: Enter your student ID number assigned to you by the school.
	* School Email: Enter your school email address for communication purposes.
	* Phone Number: Enter your contact phone number (optional but recommended).
2.  Select the current grade level or year you are in from the provided options.
3.  Enter the name of the institution where you are currently enrolled.
4.  Choose the current term or semester from the provided options.
5.  Enter the name of the class you want to drop, including the course name and number.
6.  Enter the meeting time of your current class (if applicable).
7.  Enter the name of your current class instructor (if applicable).
8.  Enter the name of the class you want to add, including the course name and number.
9.  Enter the meeting time of your new class (if applicable).
10.  Enter the name of your new class instructor (if applicable).
11.  If there are any additional class changes you want to make, describe them in this section.
12.  Explain the reason for requesting a schedule change.
13.  Confirm if you are aware of the change deadline.
14.  Check if your academic advisor has approved the change.
15.  Enter the name of your academic advisor (if applicable).
16.  Confirm that you understand the change deadline.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Date (1)**: Date of request.
* **Student Name (2)**: Full name as it appears on your school records.
* **Student ID Number (3)**: Student identification number assigned by the school.
* **Student Email (4)**: School email address for communication.
* **Phone Number (5)**: Contact phone number (optional but recommended).
* **Grade Level or Year (6)**: Current grade level or year you are in.
* **School or University Name (7)**: Name of the institution where you are enrolled.
* **Academic Term (8)**: Current term or semester.
* **Class to Drop - Course Name and Number (9)**: Class you want to remove from your schedule.
* **Current Class - Meeting Time (10)**: Meeting time of the class you want to drop (if applicable).
* **Current Class - Instructor Name (11)**: Name of the current class instructor (if applicable).
* **Class to Add - Course Name and Number (12)**: Class you wish to include in your schedule.
* **New Class - Meeting Time (13)**: Meeting time of the class you want to add (if applicable).
* **New Class - Instructor Name (14)**: Instructor name of the new class (if applicable).
* **Additional Class Changes (15)**: Describe other class changes you wish to make.
* **Reason for Schedule Change (16)**: State the reason for your request.
* **Aware of Change Deadline (17)**: Confirm if you are aware of the change deadline.
* **Academic Advisor Approval (18)**: Check if your academic advisor has approved the change.
* **Academic Advisor Name (19)**: Enter the name of your academic advisor (if applicable).
* **I Understand the Change Deadline (20)**: Confirm that you understand the deadline for making changes.

## Tips
Please ensure that you fill out all required fields before submitting the form. Additionally, be as specific as possible when providing information to help us process your request efficiently. If you need assistance or have questions, do not hesitate to contact your academic advisor.
