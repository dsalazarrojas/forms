# mcq_practice_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mcq_practice_submission_form is designed to collect student-practice-answer details for multiple choice questions in a course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the required fields with your practice answer, course, section, student, and submission date details.
2. You do not need to enter all fields, but it is mandatory to fill out the fields marked as required.
3. Enter your answer in the format of the field type (e.g., date for submission_date).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Practice Answer** (`student_practice_answer`, text, optional): This field is where you enter your practice answer to the multiple choice question.
* **Course ID** (`course_id`, text, optional): Enter the Course ID of the course you are taking.
* **Section ID** (`section_id`, text, optional): Enter the Section ID of the course section you are practicing in.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, optional): Enter your Student ID.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your submission in the format of "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **Practice Question ID** (`practice_question_id`, number, optional): Enter the Practice Question ID of the question you are submitting practice answer to.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter the correct format for the Submission Date field.
* Fill out only the required fields.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact your course instructor or support team.
