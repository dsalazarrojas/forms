# International Student Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from international students applying for a program. It gathers bio data, contact information, passport details, medical data, and other relevant qualifications. It also asks for motivation to join the program and if they have any supporting documents. The form will be reviewed to ensure that it meets the requirements and eligibility for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your bio data, including any relevant background information.
2. Provide your contact information, including your address, phone number, and email.
3. Enter your passport details, including your passport number and expiry date.
4. Share your medical data, including any medical conditions or allergies.
5. List your relevant qualifications, including degrees and skills.
6. Write a motivation letter explaining why you want to join the program.
7. Attach supporting documents, such as certificates, letters of recommendation, etc.
8. Select whether you have any course prerequisites or not.
9. Finally, add any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Bio Data** (`bio_data`, Text, Optional): Provide a brief description of yourself and your background.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, Text, Optional): Enter your address, phone number, and email to ensure we can get in touch with you.
* **Passport Details** (`passport_details`, Text, Optional): Provide your passport number and expiry date for identification purposes.
* **Medical Data** (`medical_data`, Text, Optional): Share any medical conditions or allergies that we should be aware of.
* **Qualifications** (`qualifications`, Text, Optional): List your degrees, diplomas, or skills relevant to the program.
* **Motivation for Appliance** (`motivation_for_appliance`, Text, Optional): Write a letter explaining why you want to join the program.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, Text, Optional): Attach relevant documents that support your application, such as certificates, letters of recommendation, etc.
* **Select Course** (`select_course`, Select, Optional): Choose whether you have any course prerequisites.
* **Comments** (`comments`, Text, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your application.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields (if any).
- Be honest and accurate when providing information.
- Attach all necessary supporting documents.
- Clearly explain your motivation for joining the program.
