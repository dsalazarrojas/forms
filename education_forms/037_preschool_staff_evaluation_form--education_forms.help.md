# Preschool Staff Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from preschool staff members about their experiences and satisfaction with their work environment, colleagues, and overall job performance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. The form is divided into several sections. You will start with a brief overview of the form header, followed by rating and comments sections.
2. In the **Rating** section, please select a number from 1 to 5 to indicate your level of satisfaction with your work environment, colleagues, and overall job performance.
3. In the **Rating Note** section, please provide a brief explanation of your rating choice.
4. In the **Comments** section, please write any additional comments or thoughts about your experiences or suggestions for improvement.
5. In the **Additional Comments** section, please provide any further comments or suggestions that you may have.
6. Finally, click the **Submit** button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Rating**: This section allows you to rate your level of satisfaction with your work environment, colleagues, and overall job performance. Please select a number from 1 to 5 to indicate your level of satisfaction.
* **Rating Note**: This section is for you to provide a brief explanation of your rating choice. Please use this section to elaborate on your rating and provide specific examples or details.
* **Comments**: This section is for you to write any additional comments or thoughts about your experiences or suggestions for improvement.
* **Additional Comments**: This section is for you to provide any further comments or suggestions that you may have.
* **Submit**: This is the final step to complete the form. Please review your answers before submitting.

## Tips
* Please be honest and open in your feedback.
* Try to be specific and provide concrete examples.
* If you have any suggestions for improvement, feel free to share them.
* This form is intended to be a constructive tool for self-reflection and growth.
