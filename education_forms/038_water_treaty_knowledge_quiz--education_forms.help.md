# water_treaty_knowledge_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate users' knowledge on water treaties, covering various aspects such as types, main goal, responsible entities, benefits, and submission details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the name of the water treaty that you are familiar with.
2. Choose how many types of water treaties you know.
3. Describe the main goal of a water treaty.
4. Identify the primary responsible entity for enforcing water treaties.
5. List the main benefits of having a water treaty.
6. If applicable, select whether the water treaty was submitted or not.
7. Enter the submission date and time (if applicable).
8. Enter the form description (if applicable).
9. Enter any additional form ID or submission details (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the name of the water treaty that you are familiar with?** (`form_name`, `select_one`, required: false)
This field allows you to select the name of the water treaty you are familiar with. Choose from the options: Agreement, Treaty, Protocol, Convention, Declaration, Accord, Pact, Agreement on, Treaty on, Protocol on, Convention on.
* **How many types of water treaties are there?** (`num_types`, `number`, required: false)
This field is used to input the number of types of water treaties you are aware of.
* **What is the main goal of a water treaty?** (`main_goal`, `text`, required: false)
Describe the primary purpose of a water treaty.
* **Who is the primary responsible for enforcing water treaties?** (`main_responsible`, `select_one`, required: false)
Choose from the options: United Nations, Country, International Community, Global Community, National Government.
* **What are the main benefits of having a water treaty?** (`main_benefits`, `text`, required: false)
List the main advantages of having a water treaty.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
