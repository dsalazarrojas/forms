# Educational Workshop Partner Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about educational workshop partners and gather feedback on their performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the partner's profile before filling out the form to avoid duplication.
2. Fill out the form with the required information (indicated by a star (*) in the field label).
3. Ensure that the contact details (name, email, phone number) are accurate.
4. Provide a brief explanation for the rating and recommendation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **School Type** (`School`, `Nonprofit`, `Training Team`): Select the type of educational workshop partner.
  - School: Partner is a school.
  - Nonprofit: Partner is a non-profit organization.
  - Training Team: Partner is a training team.
- **Contact Name** (`text`): Enter the name of the contact person.
- **Contact Email** (`email`): Enter the email address of the contact person.
- **Contact Phone** (`text`): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
- **School Name** (`text`): Enter the name of the school or organization.
- **Partner Since** (`date`): Enter the date the partnership started.
- **Partner Status** (`Active`, `Inactive`, `Pending`): Select the current status of the partnership.
  - Active: Partner is actively collaborating with the platform.
  - Inactive: Partner is not actively collaborating with the platform.
  - Pending: Partner's status is pending review.
- **Comments** (`note`): Provide any additional comments or information about the partner.
- **Notes** (`note`): Enter any additional notes about the partner.
- **Recommendation** (`text`): Enter a brief recommendation for the partner.
- **Rating** (`number`): Enter a rating (1-5) for the partner.
- **Rating Comment** (`text`): Enter a brief explanation for the rating.
- **Next Steps** (`date`): Enter the date for the next steps.
