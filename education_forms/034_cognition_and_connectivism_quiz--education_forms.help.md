# Cognition And Connectivism Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your understanding of Cognition And Connectivism, two important concepts in education. It will help you identify your knowledge in these areas and provide you with self-assessment questions to reflect on your understanding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant information section with your name, email, and institution/organization.
2. Answer each question in the "Quiz Questions" section, selecting the best option for each question.
3. Rate your confidence level for each question.
4. Finally, provide any additional comments or feedback you have on the quiz.

* **Full Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name to identify yourself as a participant in this quiz.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Please enter your email address to allow us to contact you for any follow-up questions or feedback.
* **Institution or Organization** (`institution`, text, required): Type in the name of your school or organization to help us understand your context.
* **Course or Program Code** (`course_code`, text, optional): If you are taking this quiz as part of a course or program, please enter the relevant code.
* **Question 1 - What is cognition primarily concerned with** (`q1_cognition`, select_one, required): Choose the best answer from the options provided. Cognition is primarily concerned with mental processes, such as thinking, problem-solving, and decision-making.
* **Question 2 - Piaget's theory focuses on** (`q2_piaget`, select_one, required): Select the best answer from the options provided. Piaget's theory focuses on social learning and cognitive development stages.
* **Question 3 - Connectivism emphasizes** (`q3_connectivism`, select_one, required): Choose the best answer from the options provided. Connectivism emphasizes learning through network connections and relationships.
* **Question 4 - Who is a key theorist in connectivism** (`q4_siemens`, select_one, required): Select the best answer from the options provided. George Siemens is a key theorist in connectivism.
* **Question 5 - In connectivism, learning happens through** (`q5_learning_network`, select_one, required): Choose the best answer from the options provided. In connectivism, learning happens through network relationships and diversity.
* **Question 6 - Metacognition refers to** (`q6_metacognition`, select_one, required): Select the best answer from the options provided. Metacognition refers to thinking about your own thinking.
* **Question 7 - A schema in cognitive theory is** (`q7_schema`, select_one, required): Choose the best answer from the options provided. A schema in cognitive theory is a mental framework for understanding.
* **Question 8 - Distributed cognition theory suggests** (`q8_distributed`, select_one, required): Select the best answer from the options provided. Distributed cognition theory suggests that cognition is spread across people and tools.
* **Question 9 - Situated learning theory emphasizes** (`q9_situated_learning`, select_one, required): Choose the best answer from the options provided. Situated learning theory emphasizes learning within specific contexts.
* **Question 10 - In connectivism, nodes represent** (`q10_nodes`, select_one, required): Select the best answer from the options provided. In connectivism, nodes represent individuals, groups, systems, and information sources.
* **How confident are you in your answers** (`confidence_level`, select_one, required): Rate your confidence level from 1 to 5, indicating how confident you are in your answers.
* **Which area did you find strongest** (`strongest_area`, select_one, optional): Choose the area you found strongest, either cognition concepts or connectivism concepts.
* **Areas needing more study** (`improvement_areas`, text, optional): Type in the topics that you found unclear or need more study on.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments you have on the quiz.
