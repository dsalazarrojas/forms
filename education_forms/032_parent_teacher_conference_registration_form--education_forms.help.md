# Parent Teacher Conference Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a parent-teacher conference, which is a meeting between a parent and their child's teacher to discuss the child's progress and address any concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your child's details on the **Parent Teacher Conference Details** page.
2. Enter your contact information on the **Parent Contact Info** page.
3. Enter your email address and phone number on the **Email** and **Parent Phone** pages.
4. Enter your child's contact information on the **Student Contact Info** page.
5. Enter your child's name on the **Student Name** page.
6. Choose a date for the conference on the **Conference Date** page.
7. Enter the teacher's name on the **Teacher Name** page.
8. Once you have filled out all the required fields, click the "Submit" button to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Teacher Conference Details** (`parent_teacher_conference_details`, note, required): This is the main page where you will provide details about the conference you are requesting.
* **Parent Contact Info** (`parent_contact_info`, text, required): Please enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact information you would like to provide.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you about the conference.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about the conference.
* **Student Contact Info** (`student_contact_info`, text, required): Please enter your child's name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your child's name exactly as it appears on their report card or student database.
* **Conference Date** (`conference_date`, date, required): Choose a date and time for the conference that works best for you and your child's teacher.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your child's teacher, who will be attending the conference with you.
