# Science Concepts Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Science Concepts Quiz form is a simple survey that gathers information about users' preferences and demographics related to science education.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Choose your favorite science subject from the options provided.
2. Enter your age in years.
3. Select your preferred method of learning science (e.g., reading, hands-on experimentation, video tutorials, or lectures).
4. Enter your email address.
5. Enter your contact phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your favorite science subject?** (`science_concepts_quiz`, select_one, required/optional): Choose your favorite science subject from the options provided.
* **What is your age in years?** (`science_concepts_2`, number, required/optional): Enter your age in years, if required.
* **How do you prefer to learn science?** (`science_concepts_3`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all methods you prefer to learn science (e.g., reading, hands-on experimentation, video tutorials, or lectures).
* **What is your email address?** (`science_concepts_4`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **What is your contact phone number?** (`science_concepts_5`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields to submit the form.
* If a field is not required, you can skip it.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact us for assistance.
