# Au Pair Demipair Nanny Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Au Pair Demipair Nanny Application form is designed to collect information from potential applicants for the au pair or nanny position. This form helps us evaluate your qualifications, experience, and preferences for the position. Please complete this form carefully to provide us with accurate and detailed information about yourself.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields and provide as much relevant information as possible. If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, please don't hesitate to contact us. Here's what you need to do:

1.  Fill out the personal information section (fields 1-6) with your correct details.
2.  In the education section (fields 8-9), select your highest education level and list all languages you're proficient in.
3.  In the experience section (fields 11-12), indicate your years of childcare experience and select all age groups you've worked with.
4.  In the availability and preferences section (fields 15-18), select your preferred start date and indicate how long you plan to stay, if you smoke or not, and if you have a valid driver's license.
5.  Finally, write a brief motivation statement in the last field (field 14) explaining why you're interested in the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (1, text, required): Your full name as it appears on your government ID or passport.
*   **Date of Birth** (3, date, required): Your date of birth in the format dd/mm/yyyy.
*   **Nationality** (4, text, required): Your country of origin.
*   **Email Address** (5, email, required): Your valid email address.
*   **Phone Number** (6, text, required): Your phone number including the country code.
*   **Highest Education Level** (8, select_one, required): Select your highest level of education completed (High School, Vocational Training, Associate Degree, Bachelor Degree, Master Degree).
*   **Languages Spoken** (9, text, required): List all languages you're proficient in and your proficiency level.
*   **First Aid Certified** (10, select_one, required): Indicate if you have a first aid certification.
*   **Years of Childcare Experience** (12, number, required): Number of years you've worked with children.
*   **Age Groups Experienced** (13, select_multiple, required): Select all age groups you've worked with (Newborns, Toddlers, Preschool, School Age, Teens).
*   **Why Do You Want to Be an Au Pair or Nanny?** (14, text, required): Describe your interest in the program.
*   **Preferred Start Date** (16, date, required): Your preferred start date for the position.
*   **Intended Duration of Stay (Months)** (17, number, required): Number of months you plan to stay.
*   **Do You Smoke?** (18, select_one, required): Indicate if you smoke or not (Non-smoker, Occasional smoker, Smoker).
*   **Do You Have a Valid Driver's License?** (19, select_one, required): Indicate if you have a valid driver's license.
*   **Relevant Health Conditions** (20, text, optional): List any allergies or chronic conditions you have.

Note: Please provide accurate and truthful information in all fields.
