# Subject Representative Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from subject representatives for academic purposes. It is intended to gather information on concerns and suggestions related to subjects, which will help in making improvements to the subject's performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the category of the feedback you wish to provide (Education Forms or Other).
2. Provide a brief title for the feedback.
3. Write a detailed description of your concerns.
4. Offer suggestions for improvement.
5. Indicate the type of response you are providing (For Academic Improvement or Other).
6. Select the terms that apply to the feedback (First term, Second term, Third term, etc.).
7. Enter your email address so we can contact you.
8. Optionally, you can provide your phone number.
9. Enter any additional comments.
10. If the feedback is no longer relevant, you can select the status as 'Draft', 'In Progress', or 'Completed'.
11. If you want to make the feedback private, you can select 'Yes' for 'Archived'.
12. Select the date and time you wish to receive feedback.
13. Enter the date and time the feedback was created.
14. Enter the date and time the feedback was last updated.
15. Enter the date and time the feedback was deleted.
16. Enter the date and time the feedback was last deleted.
17. Enter the date and time the feedback was archived.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Subject Representative Feedback Form Title** (`form_title`, `text`, required: false): This is the title of the feedback form. Please enter a brief and descriptive title to identify the purpose of the feedback.
* **Category** (`form_category`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select the category of the feedback you wish to provide (Education Forms or Other).
* **Description** (`form_description`, `text`, required: false): Enter a detailed description of your concerns and feedback.
* **Concerns** (`form_concerns`, `text`, required: false): Write a brief description of your concerns or issues with the subject.
* **Suggestions** (`form_suggestions`, `text`, required: false): Offer suggestions for improvement or changes to the subject's performance.
* **Response Type** (`form_organize_responses`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate the type of response you are providing (For Academic Improvement or Other).
* **Academic Terms** (`form_terms`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the academic terms related to the feedback (First term, Second term, Third term, etc.).
* **Email** (`form_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you with further instructions or feedback.
* **Phone Number** (`form_phone`, `text`, required: false): If you have a phone number where you can be reached, enter it.
* **Comments** (`form_comments`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional comments related to the feedback.
* **Status** (`form_status`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate the status of the feedback (Draft, In Progress, Completed).
* **Archived** (`form_archived`, `select_one`, required: false): If you want to make the feedback private, select 'Yes' for 'Archived'.
* **Archived Date** (`form_archived_at`, `date`, required: false): Select the date and time the feedback was archived.
* **Archived By** (`form_archived_by`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the person archiving the feedback.
* **Deleted Date** (`form_deleted_at`, `date`, required: false): Select the date and time the feedback was deleted.
* **Deleted By** (`form_deleted_by`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the person deleting the feedback.
* **Created By** (`form_created_by`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the person creating the feedback.
* **Updated By** (`form_updated_by`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the person last updating the feedback.
* **Created At** (`form_created_at`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date and time the feedback was created.
* **Updated At** (`form_updated_at`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date and time the feedback was last updated.
