# Youth Club Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Youth Club Registration Form is used to collect demographic and contact information from youth club members, as well as medical and emergency contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow the steps outlined above to complete this form accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, Text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, Text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, Email, optional): Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Birth Date** (Birth Date, Date, optional): Enter your birth date to help us understand your age.
* **Age** (Age, Number, optional): Enter your age to help us tailor activities and support to your needs.
* **Contact Number** (Contact Number, Text, optional): Enter your contact number for emergency purposes.
* **Address 1 Line** (Address 1 Line, Text, optional): Enter your current address for contact and communication purposes.
* **Address 2 Line** (Address 2 Line, Text, optional): Enter your address's second line for contact and communication purposes.
* **City** (City, Text, optional): Enter your city for contact and communication purposes.
* **State** (State, Text, optional): Enter your state for contact and communication purposes.
* **ZIP Code** (ZIP Code, Text, optional): Enter your ZIP code for contact and communication purposes.
* **Skills** (Skills, Text, optional): List any relevant skills you have.
* **Educational Background** (Educational Background, Text, optional): Briefly describe your educational background.
* **Experience** (Experience, Text, optional): Share your experience to help us understand your strengths and areas of interest.
* **Goals** (Goals, Text, optional): List any specific goals you would like to achieve through the youth club.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Emergency Contact Name, Text, optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (Emergency Contact Number, Text, optional): Enter the emergency contact person's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Relation** (Emergency Contact Relation, Text, optional): Indicate the relationship of the emergency contact person.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (Parent or Guardian Name, Text, optional): Enter the name of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Number** (Parent or Guardian Number, Text, optional): Enter your parent or guardian's phone number.
* **Parent or Guardian Email** (Parent or Guardian Email, Email, optional): Enter your parent or guardian's email address.
* **Medical Condition** (Medical Condition, Text, optional): List any medical conditions you have.
* **Disability** (Disability, Text, optional): Indicate any disabilities you have.
* **Medical Notes** (Medical Notes, Text, optional): Provide any relevant medical notes or documentation.
* **Medical Document** (Medical Document, Text, optional): Attach a copy of relevant medical documents.

## Tips
Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that your information is up-to-date and can be used to communicate with you effectively. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
