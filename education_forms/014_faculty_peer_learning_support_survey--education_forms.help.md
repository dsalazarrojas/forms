# Faculty Peer Learning Support Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from faculty members on the effectiveness of peer learning support. Your input will help us understand your experiences and provide insights to improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the fields that best describe your experiences with peer learning support. Be as brief or detailed as you see fit, but please answer all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Feedback**: A text field where you can provide any general comments or thoughts on your experience with peer learning support.
  • Please share any thoughts or comments you have about your experience with peer learning support.
- **Peer Learning Activities**: Select all the activities you have participated in or observed that fall under peer learning support.
  • Examples include peer mentoring, peer review, and peer-led workshops or discussions.
- **Effectiveness**: Choose one of the three options to describe your experience with peer learning support:
  • Very Effective: This means that you found peer learning support to be extremely beneficial and effective in your work.
  • Somewhat Effective: This indicates that peer learning support has been somewhat helpful, but not entirely so.
  • Not Very Effective: This option suggests that peer learning support has not been particularly impactful in your work.
- **Challenges**: If there were challenges or difficulties you faced while using peer learning support, please describe them here.
  • Be as specific as possible about any difficulties you encountered.
- **Improvement Ideas**: If you have any suggestions on how we could improve our peer learning support, share them here.
  • Your input will help us tailor our services to better meet your needs.
- **General Comments**: This is another text field where you can provide any additional thoughts or comments on peer learning support.
  • Similar to the General Feedback field, please share any other comments or ideas you have.
- **Faculty Email**: Enter your faculty email address so we can get in touch with you.
  • Make sure to enter a valid email address.
- **Faculty Phone**: If you have a preferred phone number where you can be reached, please enter it here.
  • Please ensure the number is in a format that allows us to contact you successfully.

## Tips
* Be honest and as detailed as possible in your answers.
* If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* Your feedback is crucial to us, and we value your input.
