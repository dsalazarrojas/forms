# Skin Care EGuide - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skin Care EGuide is a form designed to collect information from users regarding their skin care preferences and needs. It helps in providing personalized guidance and advice on skin care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the User Information page.
2. Choose your skin type and concerns from the respective dropdown menus.
3. Select your skin condition, product, and frequency from the respective dropdown menus.
4. Specify the method and goal of your skin care routine.
5. Indicate any precautions you should take while using skin care products.
6. Choose your preferred delivery method and time for skin care products.
7. Enter your email address to receive further guidance and advice.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information**: This is the first page of the form where you can provide your basic information. Please fill out this page completely.
* **Skin Type**: Select your skin type from the dropdown menu. If your skin type is not listed, choose "Other" and provide a brief description in the text field.
* **Skin Concerns**: Choose all the skin concerns that apply to you. If none of the options are relevant, select "Other" and provide a brief description.
* **Skin Condition**: Select your skin condition from the dropdown menu. If your skin condition is not listed, choose "Other" and provide a brief description.
* **Skin Care Product**: Select the skin care product you are currently using. If you are using a different product, choose "Other" and provide a brief description.
* **Skin Care Product Details**: If you have selected "Other" for skin care product, please provide a brief description of the product you are using.
* **Skin Care Frequency**: Choose the frequency of your skin care routine. If none of the options apply, select "Other" and provide a brief description.
* **Skin Care Method**: Choose the method you use for skin care. If none of the options apply, select "Other" and provide a brief description.
* **Skin Care Goal**: Choose the goal of your skin care routine. If none of the options apply, select "Other" and provide a brief description.
* **Skin Care Precautions**: Choose any precautions you should take while using skin care products. If none of the options apply, select "Other" and provide a brief description.
* **Delivery Method**: This field is not currently used in the form implementation.
* **Delivery Time**: This field is not currently used in the form implementation.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address to receive further guidance and advice on skin care.

**Tips**:

* Please ensure that you fill out all the relevant fields accurately to ensure that you receive personalized guidance and advice.
* If you have any unique skin concerns or conditions, please select "Other" and provide a brief description.
* If you are using a skin care product not listed in the options, please select "Other" and provide a brief description.
* If you have any questions or concerns regarding skin care, please contact us at the email address provided.
