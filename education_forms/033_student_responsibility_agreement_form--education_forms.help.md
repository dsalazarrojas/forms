To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your name as the student.
2. Fill in the 'Category' field, choosing from either 'Education Forms' or 'Other', depending on the type of agreement you are entering.
3. Provide a brief description of the agreement if necessary.
4. Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to each of the signature fields, 'Signature 1', 'Signature 2', 'Teacher Signature', and 'Student Signature'.

6. Add any additional notes about the agreement.

8. Enter your email address.
