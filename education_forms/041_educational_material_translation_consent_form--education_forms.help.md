# Educational Material Translation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Educational Material Translation Consent Form" is a tool used by school administrators or educators to gather user information, student and parent details, and obtain consent for translating educational materials.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your user information in the "User Information" field.
2. Provide the "Student Name" of the student whose educational materials are being translated.
3. Enter the "Parent Name" of the student.
4. Specify the "School" where the student attends.
5. Select the "Grade Level" of the student.
6. Choose the language from the options for the translation.
7. Describe the "Educational Materials Description" being translated.
8. Select the "Translation Permissions" for the translation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### User Information
* **User Information**: Please enter your name and any additional user information relevant to your role as an administrator or educator.
*   **Student Name**: Enter the name of the student whose educational materials are being translated.
*   **Parent Name**: Enter the name of the parent or guardian of the student.
*   **School**: Enter the name of the school where the student attends.
*   **Grade Level**: Enter the grade level of the student.
*   **Language**: Select the language for which you are obtaining translation consent.
*   **Educational Materials Description**: Describe the educational materials being translated.
*   **Translation Permissions**: Select the permissions for the translation, such as who can view or share the materials.

### Tips
*   Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form to ensure that the correct students and parents are informed and provide consent.
*   Review the "Translation Permissions" carefully to ensure that you are granting the correct permissions for the translation.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact your school administrator or educator for guidance.
