# Class Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Class Enrollment Form is designed to gather information from students to ensure proper enrollment in a class. This form will help the institution understand the student's background, academic needs, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Class Enrollment Form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your **Student Full Name** in the first field.
2. Provide your **Date of Birth** in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
3. Select your **Gender** from the provided options.
4. Enter your **Email Address** for notifications.
5. Fill in your **Phone Number** for contact purposes.
6. Provide your **Mailing Address** in the format: Street, City, State, ZIP.
7. If applicable, enter the **Parent or Guardian Name** and their **Email** and **Phone Number**.
8. Select the **Class or Course Name** you wish to enroll in from the provided options.
9. If necessary, select your **Preferred Section** for the class.
10. Choose your **Academic Year** from the provided options.
11. Select the **Semester or Term** when you wish to start the class.
12. Select your **Enrollment Type** from the provided options.
13. If applicable, select the number of **Credit Hours** you will take.
14. Select your **Highest Level of Education Completed** from the provided options.
15. Confirm that you have met the **Prerequisites** for the class.
16. If necessary, indicate if you require **Special Accommodations** for disabilities.
17. If you selected "True" for special accommodations, provide details in the **Special Needs Details** field.
18. Select your **Payment Method** from the provided options.
19. Choose how you will **Travel to Class** from the provided options.
20. If you selected "Public transportation", "Carpool" or "Bicycle or walking", select all sources how you **Heard About This Class**.
21. Finally, confirm that you **Accept the Terms and Conditions** of enrollment.
22. You may add any additional **Comments or Questions** about your enrollment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Full Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Gender**: Select your gender from the provided options.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for notifications.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Mailing Address**: Enter your address in the format: Street, City, State, ZIP.
* **Parent or Guardian Name**: Enter the full name of your parent or guardian (if applicable).
* **Parent or Guardian Email**: Enter the email address of your parent or guardian (if applicable).
* **Parent or Guardian Phone**: Enter the phone number of your parent or guardian (if applicable).
* **Class or Course Name**: Select the class or course you wish to enroll in from the provided options.
* **Preferred Section**: Select your preferred class schedule (if applicable).
* **Academic Year**: Select the academic year you are in.
* **Semester or Term**: Select the semester or term when you wish to start the class.
* **Enrollment Type**: Select your enrollment status.
* **Credit Hours**: Select the number of credits you will take (if applicable).
* **Highest Level of Education Completed**: Select your highest level of education completed.
* **Prerequisites Met**: Confirm that you have met the prerequisites for the class.
* **Special Accommodations**: If you have disabilities, select "True" and provide details in the **Special Needs Details** field.
* **Special Needs Details**: Provide details about your special needs (if applicable).
* **Payment Method**: Select your payment method.
* **Travel to Class**: Select how you will travel to class.
* **How Did You Hear About This Class**: Select all sources where you heard about the class (if applicable).
* **Do You Accept the Terms and Conditions**: Confirm that you accept the terms and conditions of enrollment.
* **Additional Comments or Questions**: Add any additional comments or questions about your enrollment.
