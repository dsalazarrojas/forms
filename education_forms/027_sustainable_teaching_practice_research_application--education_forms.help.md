# Sustainable Teaching Practice Research Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a research application for educators to share their sustainable teaching practices and goals. It is used to gather information from educators about their research applications, interests, and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your educator information in the first field.
2. Provide your contact information in the second field.
3. Describe your research question or area of interest in the third field.
4. Specify your teaching focus or area of expertise in the fourth field.
5. Enter participant information, if applicable, in the fifth field.
6. Provide your consent to participate in the sixth field.
7. Indicate your research experience in the seventh field.
8. Provide more details about your study or research in the eighth field.
9. Describe your research goals or objectives in the ninth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Educator Information** (`educator_info`, text, optional): Enter your name and title/position.
- **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Provide your contact details (email, phone number, etc.).
- **Research Question** (`research_question`, text, optional): Describe the area you're interested in researching or exploring.
- **Teaching Focus** (`teaching_focus`, text, optional): Specify your area of teaching expertise or specialty.
- **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, text, optional): Enter any relevant information about the participants in your research or study (e.g., student demographics).
- **Consent** (`consent`, text, optional): Check if you're willing to participate in this research application.
- **Research Experience** (`research_experience`, select multiple, optional): Indicate if you have research experience in "Yes" or "No".
- **Study Details** (`study_details`, text, optional): Provide more details about your study or research, if applicable.
- **Research Goals** (`research_goals`, text, optional): Describe your research goals or objectives.

## Tips
* Please answer each field as accurately and concisely as possible.
* If you have any additional information or attachments to support your application, please upload them separately.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* You can save your progress and come back to complete the form later, as needed.
