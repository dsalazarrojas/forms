# School Curriculum Compliance Audit Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The School Curriculum Compliance Audit Form is designed to help schools evaluate their curriculum for compliance with relevant standards and guidelines.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the school that you wish to audit (school_id).
2. Enter the date of the audit (audit_date).
3. Enter the name of the school being audited (school_name).
4. Enter your auditor ID or name (auditor_id).
5. Select the compliance status of the school's curriculum (compliance_status).
6. Provide any additional comments or notes about the audit (comments).
7. Enter the name of the person submitting the form (submitter_name).
8. Enter the email address of the person submitting the form (submitter_email).
9. Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form (submitter_phone).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **school_id**: Enter the school's ID number.
* **audit_date**: Enter the date of the audit in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **school_name**: Enter the name of the school being audited.
* **auditor_id**: Enter your auditor ID or name.
* **compliance_status**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Compliant
	+ Non-Compliant
	+ Under Review
* **notes**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the audit. This field supports multiple lines of text.
* **comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about the audit.
* **submitter_name**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **submitter_email**: Enter the email address of the person submitting the form.
* **submitter_phone**: Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and authorizations to complete this form.
* Only provide new or updated information that is not already documented elsewhere.
* If you are unsure about any field, please seek clarification before proceeding.
* Please note that all fields are optional, but providing accurate and complete information is essential for a thorough audit.
