# Mechanical Engineering Exam - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from mechanical engineering exam participants.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select "Yes" or "No" for "first_page" to indicate whether you are a mechanical engineering exam participant.
2. Enter your first name in the "first_name" field.
3. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
4. Provide your date of birth in the "date_of_birth" field (YYYY-MM-DD format).
5. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "phone_number" field.
7. Provide a brief response to "mechanical_engineering_questions" for any additional notes or comments.
8. Select your favorite subjects(s) from the list in the "favorite_subject" field.
9. Select your least favorite subject from the list in the "least_favorite_subject" field.
10. Choose a time of day when you are most likely to be available in the "time_of_day" field.
11. Select your favorite color from the list in the "favorite_color" field.
12. Select your favorite food from the list in the "favorite_food" field.
13. Select your least favorite subject from the list in the "least_favorite_subject" field.
14. Select your favorite note or comment in the "random_note2" field.
15. Select your favorite subject from the list in the "random_field2" field.
16. Select your least favorite subject from the list in the "random_field6" field.
17. Select your favorite subject from the list in the "random_field7" field.
18. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
19. Enter a brief response to "random_note3" for any additional notes or comments.
20. Select your favorite subject from the list in the "random_field5" field.
