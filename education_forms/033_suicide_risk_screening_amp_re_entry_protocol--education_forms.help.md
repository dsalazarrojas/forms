# Suicide Risk Screening & Re Entry Protocol - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about an individual's suicide risk and their re-entry protocol following a potential suicide risk incident.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Complete this form to provide a detailed account of the individual's experience and situation.
2.  Please answer all questions truthfully and in as much detail as possible.
3.  The form is divided into several pages with different sections. Please complete each page carefully and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Suicide Risk Screening** (`1`): This section is for providing an overview of the individual's situation.
    *   Please describe the circumstances surrounding the suicide risk incident.
*   **Re Entry Protocol** (`2`): This section is for providing details of the re-entry protocol.
    *   Please provide as much information as possible about the individual's re-entry protocol.
*   **Re Entry Date** (`3`): This section is for selecting the re-entry date.
    *   Please select the date on which the individual plans to re-enter the program or service.
*   **Re Entry Time** (`4`): This section is for selecting the re-entry time.
    *   Please select the time on which the individual plans to re-enter the program or service.
*   **Counselor Name** (`5`): This section is for entering the name of the counselor.
    *   Please enter the name of the counselor who provided support during the re-entry protocol.
*   **Counselor Title** (`6`): This section is for entering the counselor's title.
    *   Please enter the title of the counselor who provided support during the re-entry protocol.
*   **Client Name** (`7`): This section is for entering the client's name.
    *   Please enter the name of the individual who underwent the re-entry protocol.
*   **Client Phone** (`8`): This section is for entering the client's phone number.
    *   Please enter the phone number of the individual who underwent the re-entry protocol.
*   **Client Email** (`9`): This section is for entering the client's email.
    *   Please enter the email of the individual who underwent the re-entry protocol.
*   **Have you had suicidal thoughts?** (`10`): This section is for selecting whether the individual had suicidal thoughts.
    *   Please answer 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the individual had suicidal thoughts.
*   **Previous suicide attempts** (`11`): This section is for selecting whether the individual had previous suicide attempts.
    *   Please answer 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the individual had previous suicide attempts.
*   **Feelings of suicidalness (scale 1-10)** (`12`): This section is for selecting the individual's feelings of suicidalness on a scale of 1-10.
    *   Please select a number on the scale to indicate the individual's feelings of suicidalness.
*   **Have you had suicidal thoughts in the past 24 hours?** (`13`): This section is for selecting whether the individual had suicidal thoughts in the past 24 hours.
    *   Please answer 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the individual had suicidal thoughts in the past 24 hours.

**Tips**

*   Please answer all questions carefully and truthfully.
*   If you're unsure about any question, please contact the relevant authorities or support services for guidance.
*   This form is intended to ensure the individual receives proper support and care.
