# Case Study Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Case Study Evaluation Form is designed to help evaluators assess and provide feedback on a presentation or case study. This form is intended for use by presenters, instructors, and other stakeholders who want to evaluate the quality and effectiveness of a presentation or case study.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Start by filling out your contact information, including your name, role, and date of evaluation.
*   Select the evaluation date to reflect when you evaluated the presentation or case study.
*   Choose the presentation setting where you evaluated the presentation or case study.
*   Evaluate the presentation's overall quality, including its organization, structure, and communication skills.
*   Provide specific feedback on various aspects of the presentation, such as the use of time, accuracy, and clarity of information, and engagement with the audience.
*   Identify the presenter's strong points and areas for improvement.
*   Provide suggestions for improvement and additional comments.
*   Finally, sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date): This field is used to record the date when you evaluated the presentation or case study.
*   **Evaluator Information** (`evaluator_info`, note): Please provide a brief description of who you are, as an evaluator.
*   **Evaluator Name** (`evaluator_name`, text): Enter your full name.
*   **Evaluator Role** (`evaluator_role`, select_one): Select your role from the following options: Instructor, Teaching assistant, Peer Evaluator, External reviewer, or Other.
*   **Presenter Information** (`presenter_info`, note): Please provide a brief description of who presented.
*   **Presenter Name** (`presenter_name`, text): Enter the presenter's full name.
*   **Case Study Title** (`presentation_title`, text): Enter the title of the presentation.
*   **Presentation Date** (`presentation_date`, date): Enter the date the presentation was given.
*   **Presentation Setting** (`presentation_setting`, select_one): Select where the presentation was given from the following options: Classroom, Conference, Seminar, Workshop, Online webinar, or Other.
*   **Time Management** (`time_management`, note): Describe how well the presenter used their allotted time.
*   **Time Allocation** (`time_allocation`, select_one): Evaluate how well the time was used, from Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
*   **Did Presenter Stay Within Time Limit** (`stayed_within_time`, select_one): Evaluate whether the presenter stayed within their time limit.
*   **Subject Mastery** (`subject_mastery`, note): Provide feedback on the presenter's knowledge demonstration.
*   **Depth of Knowledge** (`knowledge_depth`, select_one): Evaluate the level of knowledge demonstrated, from Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
*   **Accuracy** (`accuracy`, select_one): Evaluate the accuracy of the information presented.
*   **Handling of Questions** (`question_handling`, select_one): Evaluate the quality of the presenter's responses.
*   **Organization** (`organization`, note): Provide feedback on the presentation's structure.
*   **Presentation Structure** (`structure`, select_one): Evaluate the logical flow of the presentation.
*   **Introduction Quality** (`introduction`, select_one): Evaluate the effectiveness of the introduction.
*   **Conclusion Quality** (`conclusion`, select_one): Evaluate the effectiveness of the conclusion.
*   **Transitions Between Sections** (`transitions`, select_one): Evaluate the quality of transitions between sections.
*   **Visual Aids** (`visuals`, note): Describe any visual aids used during the presentation.
*   **Visual Aid Quality** (`visual_quality`, select_one): Evaluate the quality of the visual aids.
*   **Visual Clarity** (`visual_clarity`, select_one): Evaluate the readability of the visual aids.
*   **Visual Relevance** (`visual_relevance`, select_one): Evaluate the relevance of the visual aids to the content.
*   **Communication Skills** (`communication`, note): Provide feedback on the presenter's delivery quality.
*   **Verbal Clarity** (`verbal_clarity`, select_one): Evaluate the clarity of the presenter's speech.
*   **Volume and Pace** (`volume_pace`, select_one): Evaluate the presenter's volume and pace.
*   **Body Language** (`body_language`, select_one): Evaluate the presenter's non-verbal communication.
*   **Audience Engagement** (`audience_engagement`, select_one): Evaluate the quality of engagement with the audience.
*   **Overall Impression** (`overall_impression`, note): Provide a general assessment of the presentation.
*   **Overall Presentation Rating** (`overall_rating`, select_one): Provide an overall rating of the presentation.
*   **Did Presentation Meet Objectives** (`met_objectives`, select_one): Evaluate whether the presentation met its objectives.
*   **Would You Recommend This Presenter** (`would_recommend`, select_one): Indicate whether you would recommend the presenter.
*   **Strong Points** (`strong_points`, text): Identify the presenter's strong points.
*   **Weak Points** (`weak_points`, text): Identify areas for improvement.
*   **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions`, text): Provide suggestions for improvement.
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text): Add any additional comments or feedback.
*   **Evaluator Signature Date** (`evaluator_signature`, date): Sign and date the form.
