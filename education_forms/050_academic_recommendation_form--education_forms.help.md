# Academic Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about a candidate from an academic recommendor, providing a professional evaluation of the candidate's abilities and potential.

## How To Complete This Form

*   **Recommender Details**: This is the first page of the form, where you'll provide your contact information as the recommendor. Make sure to fill in your full name and email address, as these will be used for verification purposes.
*   **Recommender Name**, **Recommender Email**, **Relationship to Candidate**: On the second page, you'll need to provide your relationship to the candidate, such as how you know them and in what capacity.
*   **Candidate Information**: In this section, fill in the candidate's full name and the program or position they are applying for.
*   **Candidate Strengths and Development Areas**: Describe the candidate's key strengths and contributions, as well as any areas where they could improve or develop further.
*   **Overall Recommendation Rating**: Choose one of the four rating options: Strongly recommend, Recommend, Recommend with reservations, or Do not recommend. This will give the candidate a clear understanding of your overall assessment.
*   **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional information or observations that support your recommendation.
*   **Signature**: Please type your full name to sign the recommendation.
*   **Date**: Fill in the date of the recommendation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Recommender Name** (`Recommender name`, text, required): This field collects your full name as the recommendor.
*   **Recommender Email** (`Recommender email`, email, required): This field is for your contact email address, which will be used for verification purposes.
*   **Relationship to Candidate** (`Relationship to candidate`, text, required): Describe how you know the candidate and in what capacity.
*   **Candidate Name** (`Candidate name`, text, required): Fill in the candidate's full name.
*   **Candidate Program** (`Candidate program or position`, text, optional): If applicable, fill in the program or position the candidate is applying for.
*   **Candidate Strengths** (`Candidate strengths`, text, required): Describe the candidate's key strengths and contributions.
*   **Areas for Development** (`Areas for development`, text, optional): Provide constructive feedback on areas where the candidate could improve or develop further.
*   **Overall Recommendation Rating** (`Overall recommendation rating`, select_one, required): Choose one of the four rating options: Strongly recommend, Recommend, Recommend with reservations, or Do not recommend.
*   **Additional Comments** (`Additional comments`, text, optional): Provide any other observations or information that support the recommendation.
*   **Signature** (`Signature name`, text, required): Type your full name to sign the recommendation.
*   **Date** (`Date`, date, required): Fill in the date of the recommendation.
