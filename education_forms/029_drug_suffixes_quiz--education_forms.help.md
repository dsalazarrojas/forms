# Drug Suffixes Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Drug Suffixes Quiz is an educational tool designed to assess and test your knowledge of medication suffixes. This form will guide you through a series of questions and provide you with a chance to demonstrate your understanding of medication naming patterns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all the correct medication suffixes from the list on page 1. 
2. Enter your answer to the question on page 2. 
3. Once you are ready, click the "Submit" button on page 3 to submit your answers. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Medication Naming Patterns** (`medication_naming_patterns`, select_multiple, required): This field asks you to select all the correct medication suffixes from a list of options. Examples of medication suffixes include acetate, hydrochloride, and others.
- **Quiz Questions** (`quiz_questions`, text, optional): This field is where you will enter your answer to the question presented on this page.
- **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required): This is the final step to submit your answers after completing the form. You can choose between "Submit" and "Reset" to either submit or clear your answers.
- **Medication Naming Patterns 2** (`medication_naming_patterns_2`, text, optional): This field is not used in this specific form but could be used for additional information or feedback. 

## Tips
- Be careful when selecting the correct medication suffixes, as some may be similar in spelling or pronunciation.
- Make sure to enter your answer to the question clearly and accurately.
- If you need to reset your answers, click the "Reset" button on page 3.
