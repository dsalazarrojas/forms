# Virtual Teaching Observation Recording Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Virtual Teaching Observation Recording Consent Form is used to collect consents from teachers and participants in virtual classes to record their observations. This form is required for all virtual classes to ensure that all parties involved are aware of and agree to the recording of observations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, you will need to answer the following questions for yourself and the participant in the virtual class. Please select the correct answers for each field and provide any additional information as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher Consent** (`teacher_consent`, `select_one`, required): Select the correct option for your teaching status:
	+ I am a licensed teacher
	+ I am a non-licensed teacher
	+ I am a non-teaching staff
* **Participant Consent** (`participant_consent`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you are a participant in this virtual class:
	+ I am a participant in this virtual class
	+ I am not a participant in this virtual class
* **Observation Recording Consent** (`observation_recording_consent`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you agree to the recording of observations:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Participant Informed Consent** (`participant_informed_consent`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you have been informed about the recording of observations:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Recording Consent Date** (`consent_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date on which you are giving this consent.
* **Recording Consent Time** (`consent_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time on which you are giving this consent.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the participant.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the participant (if applicable).
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the teacher.
* **Teacher Phone** (`teacher_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the teacher.
* **Recording Consent Message** (`recording_consent_message`, `note`, required): Enter any additional comments or messages regarding recording consent.
* **Participant Consent Note** (`participant_consent_note`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes for participant consent.
* **Teacher Consent Note** (`teacher_consent_note`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes for teacher consent.
* **Participant Agreed** (`participant_agree`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the options that apply to you as a participant:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Teacher Agreed** (`teacher_agree`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you agree to the recording of observations:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Recording Consent** (`recording_consent`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you allow recording:
	+ Allow
	+ Not Allow
* **Teacher Informed Consent** (`teacher_informed_consent`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you have been informed about the recording of observations:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Participant Informed Consent** (`participant_informed_consent`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you have been informed about the recording of observations:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Participant Consent Form** (`participant_consent_form`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you agree to the participant consent form:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Teacher Consent Form** (`teacher_consent_form`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you agree to the teacher consent form:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Recording Permission** (`recording_permission`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you grant recording permission:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Participant Permission** (`participant_permission`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you grant participant permission:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Recording Consent Note 2** (`recording_consent_note_2`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes for recording consent.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, `note`, required): Enter your signature to confirm participant consent.
* **Teacher Signature** (`teacher_signature`, `note`, required): Enter your signature to confirm teacher consent.

## Tips

* Please make sure to select the correct options for all fields that apply to you.
* Enter any additional comments or notes as required.
* Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.
* Keep a copy of this form for future reference.

Please note that the purpose of this form is to ensure that all parties involved are aware of and agree to the recording of observations. Completing this form will help to maintain a clear and transparent process.
