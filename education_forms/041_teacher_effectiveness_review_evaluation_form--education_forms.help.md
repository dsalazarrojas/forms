# Teacher Effectiveness Review Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from reviewers on a teacher's performance, highlighting their strengths and areas for improvement. This feedback is essential for teachers to grow professionally and provide excellent education to students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the review cycle (e.g., Fall, Spring, Summer, or Academic year).
2. Select the date of the review.
3. Enter the teacher's name.
4. Rate the teacher's overall performance (High, Good, Fair, or Low).
5. List areas where the teacher excels (Lesson planning, Classroom environment, Assessment, or Communication).
6. List areas where the teacher needs improvement (Classroom management, Parent-teacher communication, Time management, or Technology integration).
7. Provide any additional comments about the teacher's performance.
8. Enter the evaluator's name and position.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Review Cycle**: Select the review cycle that corresponds to the academic term being evaluated.
* **Review Date**: Enter the date of the review.
* **Teacher's Name**: Enter the teacher's name being evaluated.
* **Teacher's Rating**: Rate the teacher's overall performance using one of the following options:
	+ High: Indicates the teacher's performance is exceptional.
	+ Good: Indicates the teacher's performance is above average.
	+ Fair: Indicates the teacher's performance is average.
	+ Low: Indicates the teacher's performance is below average.
* **Areas of Strength**: List specific areas where the teacher excels.
* **Areas for Improvement**: List specific areas where the teacher needs improvement.
* **Evaluator's Comments**: Provide any additional comments about the teacher's performance.
* **Evaluator's Name**: Enter the evaluator's name completing the review.
* **Evaluator's Position**: Enter the evaluator's position or title.
* **Reviewers**: Select all the reviewers participating in the evaluation.
* **Reviewers Position**: Enter the position or title of the reviewers.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when listing areas of strength and improvement.
* Provide constructive feedback that is actionable and helpful for the teacher.
* Ensure the reviewer's name and position are accurate.
* Review the form before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled in.
