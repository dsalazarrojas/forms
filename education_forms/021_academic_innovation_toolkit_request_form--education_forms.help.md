# Academic Innovation Toolkit Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Innovation Toolkit Request Form is a form designed to collect requests for the Academic Innovation Toolkit. This form will help us understand your needs and preferences for the toolkit, ensuring that it is delivered to you efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name and email address in the "Requester name" and "Email address" fields, respectively.
2. Select the department or unit where you will be using the toolkit.
3. Choose the type of toolkit you are requesting (e.g., Teaching innovation toolkit, Data literacy toolkit, etc.).
4. Describe how you intend to use the toolkit in practice.
5. If necessary, indicate the quantity of toolkit copies or licenses you require.
6. Choose your preferred delivery method (digital, physical, or both).
7. Provide a date by which you need the toolkit.
8. Finally, provide any additional information or context that might be helpful for us to know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Requester Details
* **-- Requester Details --** (`section_requester`, `note`, required: false): This section is a heading for the form.
* **Requester name** (`requester_name`, `text`, required: true): Please enter your full name as the person requesting the toolkit.
* **Email address** (`email`, `email`, required: true): Enter your email address to ensure that we can contact you regarding the toolkit.
* **Department or unit** (`department`, `text`, required: false): If you are requesting the toolkit for your department or unit, please enter the name here.
* **Quantity needed** (`quantity_needed`, `number`, required: false): If you require more than one copy or license, please indicate the number here.
* **Delivery preference** (`delivery_preference`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how you would like to receive the toolkit (digital, physical, or both).
* **Required timeline** (`timeline`, `date`, required: false): If you need the toolkit by a specific date, please enter the date here.
* **Additional information** (`additional_information`, `text`, required: false): If you have any special requests or context, please provide it here.
* **Toolkit requested** (`toolkit_requested`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the type of toolkit you are requesting (e.g., Teaching innovation toolkit, Data literacy toolkit, etc.). 
* **Intended use of toolkit** (`intended_use`, `text`, required: true): Please describe how you plan to use the toolkit in practice.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are not duplicating a previous request.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the relevant department or team for guidance.
* We will review your request and get back to you with further instructions.
* Please allow sufficient time for the delivery of the toolkit, according to your chosen timeline.
