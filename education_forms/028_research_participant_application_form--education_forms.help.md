This form is a participant application form for research studies. The form's purpose is to collect demographic and research-related information from potential participants to determine their eligibility and suitability for the study. 

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email Address in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Phone Number and Date Of Birth.
3. Provide your Address information (Address Line 1, Address Line 2, City, and State).
4. Enter your Zip Code and Country.
5. Select your Education Level (Bachelor's, Master's, or Doctoral).
6. Choose your research experience (1-2 years, 2-5 years, or More than 5 years).
7. Enter any medical conditions or allergies you may have.
8. Provide information about your emergency contact (Name, Relationship, Phone, and Email).
9. If necessary, list any other medical conditions or allergies.
10. Finally, click the submit button to submit your application.

## research_participant_application_form - Help Guide

### Purpose
This form is a participant application form for research studies.

### How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps listed above.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Your valid email address where you can be reached for updates and communications.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Your phone number where you can be contacted for updates and communications.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address Line 1** (`address_1`, text, required): Your address line 1.
* **Address Line 2** (`address_2`, text, required): Your address line 2.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): The city where you reside.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): The state where you reside.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, number, required): Your zip code.
* **Country** (`country`, select_one, required): Your country of residence (United States, Canada, Mexico).
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, select_multiple, required): Select your highest level of education completed (Bachelor's, Master's, or Doctoral).
* **Research Experience** (`research_experience`, select_multiple, required): Select your research experience level (1-2 years, 2-5 years, or More than 5 years).
* **Research Interest** (`research_interest`, text, optional): If applicable, describe your research interests.
* **Research Goals** (`research_goals`, text, optional): If applicable, describe your research goals.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): The name of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): Your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): The phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, text, optional): The email address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, optional): The address of your emergency contact.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): Any medical conditions you may have.
* **Medical Information** (`medical_information`, text, optional): Any relevant medical information.
* **Allergy or Condition** (`allergy_or_condition`, text, optional): Any allergies or conditions you may have.
* **Health Insurance** (`health_insurance`, text, optional): Your health insurance information.
* **Other Medical Condition** (`other_medical_condition`, text, optional): Any other medical conditions you may have.
