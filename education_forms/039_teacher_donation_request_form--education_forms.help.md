# Teacher Donation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teacher Donation Request Form is a tool used to collect information from teachers to request donations for their school or department. It is a simple and straightforward form designed to gather necessary details for the donation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as it appears on your official teacher documentation or ID card.
2. Enter your teacher ID, if applicable.
3. Specify the amount of money you are requesting as a donation.
4. Provide a brief explanation for why you are requesting a donation (e.g., for a specific project or event).
5. If you have any contact information, such as an email or phone number, please provide it to ensure that we can reach out to you if needed.
6. Add any additional notes about your request, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Teacher Name** (teacher_name, text, optional)
Enter your name as it appears on your official teacher documentation or ID card.

### 2. **Teacher ID** (teacher_id, number, optional)
Enter your teacher ID, if you have one. This is typically a unique identifier used to track your account.

### 3. **Amount Requested** (amount_requested, number, optional)
Specify the amount of money you are requesting as a donation. Please enter a valid number without symbols or punctuation.

### 4. **Reason for Request** (reason_for_request, text, optional)
Provide a brief explanation for why you are requesting a donation (e.g., for a specific project or event).

### 5. **Contact Information** (contact_info, text, optional)
If you have any contact information, such as an email or phone number, please provide it to ensure that we can reach out to you if needed.

### 6. **Notes** (notes, note, optional)
Add any additional notes about your request if necessary.
