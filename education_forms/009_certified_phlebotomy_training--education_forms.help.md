# Certified Phlebotomy Training - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the steps to complete the Certified Phlebotomy Training registration form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Scroll through each section of the form carefully.
2. Fill in each field according to the instructions provided.
3. Answer all questions accurately and honestly.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Full Legal Name** (`student_full_legal_name`, text, **Required**):
	+ Enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Student Contact Email** (`student_contact_email`, email, **Required**):
	+ Enter your valid email address for course materials and communication.
* **Student Contact Phone** (`student_contact_phone`, text, **Required**):
	+ Enter your phone number for contacting you.
* **Student Date of Birth** (`student_date_of_birth`, date, **Required**):
	+ Enter your date of birth to verify age requirements for certification.
* **Phlebotomy Program Session** (`phlebotomy_program_session`, select_one, **Required**):
	+ Choose the 4-week program cycle you wish to join (Spring, Summer, Fall, or Winter).
* **Training Modality** (`training_modality_selected`, select_one, **Required**):
	+ Select your preferred learning format (Full-Time, Part-Time, or Hybrid).
* **Highest Level of Education Attained** (`highest_level_education_attained`, select_one, **Required**):
	+ Choose your completed level of education (High School Diploma or GED, Associate, Bachelor, Master, or Current Healthcare Professional).
* **Previous Healthcare Experience** (`medical_experience_healthcare`, select_one, **Required**):
	+ Select if you have prior experience in a clinical or administrative role.
* **Vaccinations Up to Date?** (`vaccinations_up_to_date_confirmed`, select_one, **Required**):
	+ Confirm if you have up-to-date vaccinations required for the program.
* **TB Screening Completed?** (`tuberculosis_screening_complete`, select_one, **Required**):
	+ Confirm if your TB screening is current and negative or pending.
* **Consent for Criminal Background Check** (`criminal_background_check_consent`, select_one, **Required**):
	+ Agree or decline to undergo a criminal background check for clinical placement.
* **Attendance Requirement Agreement** (`attendance_requirement_agreement`, select_one, **Required**):
	+ Confirm you understand the 100% attendance requirement for program completion.
* **NHA Exam Policy Acknowledgement** (`nha_exam_policy_acknowledgement`, select_one, **Required**):
	+ Understand that the program covers only the first attempt at the National Exam.
* **Student Registration Date** (`phlebotomy_student_signature_date`, date, **Required**):
	+ Select today's date for registration.
* **Additional Information or Comments** (`additional_student_info_comments`, text, **Optional**):
	+ Add any additional information or comments about your registration.
