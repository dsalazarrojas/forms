# course_audit_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students about their courses, including student and course details, and signatures from students and instructors for verification and approval purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's details in the "Student Details" field.
2. Provide information about the course in the "Course Information" field.
3. Sign and date the "Student Signature" field to confirm the accuracy of the information provided.
4. Sign and date the "Instructor Signature" field to approve or endorse the student's course information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Student Details
* **Student Details** (`student_details`, text, required): Enter your name, student ID, or any other identifying information that you want to include in the form.

### Course Information
* **Course Information** (`course_info`, text, required): Enter the course title, code, or any other relevant details about the course you are auditing.

### Student Signature
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, text, required): Sign and date your signature to confirm that the information you provided is accurate and truthful.

### Instructor Signature
* **Instructor Signature** (`instructor_signature`, text, required): Sign and date your signature to approve or endorse the student's course information and ensure its accuracy.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you need help or have any questions, contact our support team at form@gic.mx.
