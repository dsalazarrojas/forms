# Educator Testimonial Collection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Educator Testimonial Collection" form is used to gather feedback from educators about their experiences with programs and initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Review the form and determine what sections you would like to complete. Some sections are optional, so choose only what is relevant to your experience.
2. Enter your testimonial about the program(s) you have taught or participated in. This section allows you to share your thoughts and opinions about the program.
3. List the programs you have taught or participated in. Select all that apply from the list provided.
4. List the initiatives you have participated in. Select all that apply from the list provided.
5. If you have participants in your program, please provide their names and any relevant details.
6. If you have parents who have participated in your program, please list their names and any relevant details.
7. Answer the questions about the participants, including whether you have received feedback from them and their age.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your testimonial about**: Enter a brief testimonial about the program you are reviewing. This is your chance to share your thoughts and opinions about the program.
* **List programs**: This section allows you to list all the programs you have taught or participated in. Select all that apply from the provided list.
* **Initiatives**: List the initiatives you have participated in. Select all that apply from the provided list.
* **List participants**: If you have participants, please select "Yes" if you have received feedback from them, or "No" if you haven't.
* **Participant name**: If you have selected "Yes" in the previous question, enter the name(s) of your participant(s).
* **Participant age**: Enter the age of your participant(s).
* **List parents**: If you have parents who have participated in your program, please select all that apply from the provided list.
* **Participant name**: If you have selected "Yes" in the previous question, enter the name(s) of your parent(s).
