# Scholarship Recommendation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Scholarship Recommendation Form is used to gather information from a recommender about an applicant for a scholarship award. The form is designed to collect relevant details about the recommender's relationship with the applicant, their qualifications and education, and their recommendation statement. This form helps to assess the applicant's strengths and qualities that make them suitable for the scholarship award.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your relationship with the applicant (Recommender Relationship).
2. Provide your institutional affiliation (Recommender Institution).
3. Describe your relationship with the applicant and how long you have known them (Recommender Years Known).
4. Share your education details and qualifications (Recommender Education).
5. Write a brief recommendation statement about the applicant (Recommendation).
6. Sign and date this form with your signature and email address (Recommender Signature, Date).
7. Add any additional comments about the applicant (Comments).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Information**: This is the main contact information for the applicant. Please make sure to enter accurate details.
* **Recommender Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Recommender Title**: Your official title or position that you are using to recommend the applicant.
* **Recommender Institution**: The institution where you work or study, e.g., university or college.
* **Recommender Relationship**: Describe your relationship with the applicant, e.g., academic advisor, teacher, or colleague.
* **Recommender Years Known**: The number of years you have known the applicant.
* **Recommender Education**: Your educational background and qualifications.
* **Recommendation**: Write a brief statement about why you recommend the applicant for the scholarship award.
* **Recommender Signature**: Your handwritten or typed signature, confirming that this statement is true.
* **Date**: The date when you sign this form.
* **Email**: Your official email address associated with your institution or organization.
* **Phone**: Your contact phone number.
* **Comments**: Any additional comments or observations about the applicant.

Note: Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely to provide a strong recommendation for the applicant.
