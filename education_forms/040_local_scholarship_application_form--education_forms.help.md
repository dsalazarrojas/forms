# local_scholarship_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for applicants to provide information to be considered for a local scholarship. It's a comprehensive form that gathers details about the applicant's contact, education history, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read through the form carefully and fill out each required field.
- Make sure to provide accurate and truthful information.
- Use clear and concise language when filling out the fields.
- Attach any necessary documents, such as your resume, transcript, or letters of recommendation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact** (Contact, Text, Required): Please fill out your name, email address, and phone number. This information will be used to contact you for further communication.
* **Address** (Address, Text, Required): Provide your home address or the address you'd like to use for communication.
* **Email** (Email, Email, Required): Enter your email address. This will be used for communication and to send notifications.
* **Phone** (Phone, Text, Required): Enter your phone number. This will be used for communication and to receive notifications.
* **Education History** (Education History, Text, Required): Briefly describe your education history, including any relevant courses, degrees, or certifications.
* **Education 1** (Education 1, Text, Required): Provide details about your first education institution.
* **Education 2** (Education 2, Text, Required): Provide details about your second education institution.
* **Education 3** (Education 3, Text, Required): Provide details about your third education institution.
* **Education 4** (Education 4, Text, Required): Provide details about your fourth education institution.
* **Education 5** (Education 5, Text, Required): Provide details about your fifth education institution.
* **Education 6** (Education 6, Text, Required): Provide details about your sixth education institution.
* **Education 7** (Education 7, Text, Optional): If you have more than six education institutions, use this field to provide details about the seventh one.
* **Education 8** (Education 8, Text, Optional): If you have more than six education institutions, use this field to provide details about the eighth one.
* **Education 9** (Education 9, Text, Optional): If you have more than six education institutions, use this field to provide details about the ninth one.
* **Education 10** (Education 10, Text, Optional): If you have more than six education institutions, use this field to provide details about the tenth one.
* **Transcript** (Transcript, Text, Optional): If applicable, attach a copy of your transcript or provide a link to it.
* **Resume** (Resume, Text, Optional): If applicable, attach a copy of your resume or provide a link to it.
* **Letters of Recommendation** (Letters of Recommendation, Text, Optional): If applicable, attach copies of your letters of recommendation or provide a link to them.
* **Personal Statement** (Personal Statement, Text, Required): Write a brief statement about yourself, your goals, and why you're applying for this scholarship.
* **Scholarship** (Scholarship, Text, Optional): If you're applying for a specific scholarship, provide details about it.
* **Scholarship Amount** (Scholarship Amount, Text, Optional): If you're applying for a specific scholarship, provide the amount you're requesting.
* **Award Name** (Award Name, Text, Optional): If you're applying for a specific award, provide the name of it.
* **Award Start Date** (Award Start Date, Date, Optional): If you're applying for a specific award, provide the start date of it.
* **Award End Date** (Award End Date, Date, Optional): If you're applying for a specific award, provide the end date of it.
* **Award Amount** (Award Amount, Number, Required): Fill out the amount of the award you're applying for.

## Tips
- Double-check the form for any required fields that you haven't filled out yet.
- Make sure to attach any necessary documents and provide links to them.
- Proofread your application before submitting it to ensure there are no errors.
- Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
- If you're unsure about any field, contact the scholarship committee or the form's administrator for assistance.
