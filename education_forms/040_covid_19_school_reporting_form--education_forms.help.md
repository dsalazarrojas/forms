# COVID 19 School Reporting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report and track COVID-19 cases and vaccination metrics within a school setting. It is intended to be completed by a reporting officer and is used to provide accurate and up-to-date information on student and staff health and vaccination status.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, please start by entering the school's name and district name in the respective fields.
2.  Next, enter the date of the report and the total number of students and staff within the school.
3.  Report the number of fully and partially vaccinated students and staff, as well as the total number of new positive COVID-19 cases this week.
4.  Indicate the current number of individuals in quarantine and whether the local health department has been notified of new cases.
5.  Finally, certify that the data is accurate and reflects the school's current records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name (1)**: Enter the name of your school. This field is required.
* **School District (2)**: Enter the name of the school district. This field is required.
* **Reporting Officer Name (3)**: Enter your name as the reporting officer. This field is required.
* **Date of Report (4)**: Enter the date of the report. This field is required.
* **Total Student Population (5)**: Enter the total number of students within your school. This field is required.
* **Number of Fully Vaccinated Students (6)**: Enter the number of students who have received all required doses of the COVID-19 vaccination. This field is required.
* **Number of Partially Vaccinated Students (7)**: Enter the number of students who have received at least one dose of the COVID-19 vaccination. This field is required.
* **Total Staff Population (8)**: Enter the total number of staff (teachers, admin, and support staff) within your school. This field is required.
* **Number of Fully Vaccinated Staff (9)**: Enter the number of staff members who have received all required doses of the COVID-19 vaccination. This field is required.
* **-- Vaccination Metrics -- (10)**: This field is an optional note field for any additional vaccination metrics or notes.
* **New Positive COVID-19 Cases this Week (11)**: Enter the total number of new positive COVID-19 cases among students and staff this week. This field is required.
* **Current Number of Individuals in Quarantine (12)**: Enter the current number of students and staff in quarantine. This field is required.
* **Local Health Department has been Notified of all New Cases (13)**: Select 'True' if the local health department has been notified of all new cases. This field is required.
* **I certify the above data is accurate and reflects current school records (14)**: Select 'True' to certify that the data is accurate. This field is required.
