# Course AddDrop Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Course AddDrop Request Form is used by students to inform the administration of their intention to add or drop courses in their program. This form helps students make changes to their course list and allows administrators to review and process these requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your student ID number in the "Student ID" field.
4. Select your program from the "Program" dropdown menu.
5. If you want to add courses, select "Yes" in the "Add Course List" field, and list the courses you wish to add in the "Add Course" field.
6. If you want to drop courses, select "Yes" in the "Drop Course List" field, and list the courses you wish to drop in the "Drop Course" field.
7. Click the "Submit Request" button to submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`Name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your university email address.
* **Student ID** (`Student ID`, number, required): Enter your student ID number.
* **Program** (`Program`, select one, required): Select your current program from the list of available programs.
* **Add Course List** (`Add Course List`, select multiple, required): Select "Yes" if you wish to add courses.
* **Add Course** (`Add Course`, text, required): List the courses you wish to add, one per line.
* **Drop Course List** (`Drop Course List`, select multiple, required): Select "Yes" if you wish to drop courses.
* **Drop Course** (`Drop Course`, text, required): List the courses you wish to drop, one per line.
* **Submit Request** (`Submit Request`, note, required): Click this button to submit your completed form.
