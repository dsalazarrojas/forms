# Academic Accessibility Research Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide is designed to assist applicants in completing the Academic Accessibility Research Application form, which is used to submit proposals for accessibility-related research projects. The form collects information about the applicant, research proposal, accessibility needs, and ethics considerations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by reviewing the form's sections: Applicant Information, Research Proposal, Accessibility Needs, and Ethics and Consent.
2. Ensure you have all necessary information and documents ready before starting the application.
3. Fill in the required fields, starting from the top of each section.
4. Use clear and concise language when providing answers, especially in fields with character limits.
5. Review and edit your application before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (1): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email address** (3): Enter your institutional email address or any other contact email you'd like to be associated with.
* **Phone number** (4): Enter your phone number for potential contact purposes.
* **University or research institution** (5): Enter the name of your university or research institution.
* **Department or program** (6): Enter the name of your department or academic program.
* **Academic role** (7): Select one of the following:
	+ Faculty member
	+ Post-doctoral researcher
	+ Graduate student
	+ Undergraduate student
	+ Research assistant
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Title of proposed research** (9): Enter a concise title of your research proposal.
* **Primary research area** (10): Select one of the following:
	+ Physical accessibility
	+ Digital accessibility
	+ Learning disabilities
	+ Sensory impairments
	+ Cognitive accessibility
	+ Universal design
	+ Assistive technology
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Summary of proposed research** (11): Provide a 200-word overview of your research objectives, methodology, and expected outcomes.
* **Target population for the study** (12): Describe the group you plan to study or investigate.
* **Expected duration of the research** (13): Select one of the following:
	+ Less than 3 months
	+ 3 to 6 months
	+ 6 to 12 months
	+ More than 12 months
* **Accessibility needs as a researcher** (15): Select all that apply:
	+ Screen reader compatible materials
	+ Sign language interpretation
	+ Physical access accommodations
	+ Extended time for submissions
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Do you have any accessibility needs as a researcher?** (16): Provide details about your accessibility needs, if any.
* **Ethics or IRB approval status** (18): Select one of the following:
	+ Approved
	+ Pending
	+ Not yet submitted
	+ Not required
* **Describe how informed consent will be obtained from participants** (19): Explain how you plan to obtain informed consent from your study participants.
* **Describe how participant data will be stored and protected** (20): Explain your plan for storing and protecting participant data.
* **I confirm that the information provided is accurate and that this research will be conducted ethically** (21): Select "Yes, I confirm" to confirm the accuracy and ethics of your research proposal.
