# Game Development Workshop Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Game Development Workshop Attendance Form is designed for attendees to register for the Game Development Workshop. It helps the organizers gather essential information about attendees, which is used to tailor the workshop experience and ensure accurate participant management.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter your first name in the field labeled "first name".
*   Enter your last name in the field labeled "last name".
*   Enter your email address in the field labeled "Email".
*   Enter your phone number in the field labeled "phone".
*   Enter your preferred date for the workshop in the field labeled "Date".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Workshop Registration** (`workshop_registration`, text, optional): Enter a brief description or any additional information about your attendance.
*   **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
*   **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter your preferred date for the workshop in the required format (DD/MM/YYYY).
