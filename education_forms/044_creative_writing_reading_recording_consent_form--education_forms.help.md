# Creative Writing Reading Recording Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect consent from individuals for recording and storing their responses for creative writing projects. It is used for internal purposes only and does not involve public disclosure of personal information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Input 1 (Text)**: A text input field where you can type any relevant information about your response.
* **Usage Permissions**: A multiple-choice field where you can select how you want your response to be used. Please choose one or more of the following options:
	+ Option 1: Only for research purposes
	+ Option 2: For educational purposes
	+ Option 3: For marketing purposes
* **Date of Event**: A date field where you can input the date of the event or experience you are recording.
* **Time of Event**: A time field where you can input the time of the event or experience you are recording.
* **Recording Location**: A text input field where you can input any relevant information about the location where the recording took place.
* **Response Storage**: A single-choice field where you can select how you want your response stored. Please choose one of the following options:
	+ Option 1: On our internal servers
	+ Option 2: On a secure external server
* **Email**: A field where you can input your email address for contact purposes.
* **Note**: A free text input field where you can input any additional information or context about your response.
* **Form Input 10 (Text)**: Another text input field where you can type any additional information about your response.
