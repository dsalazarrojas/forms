# Student Voice In Learning Forum Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Voice In Learning Forum Registration form is designed to gather information from students about their interests and expectations for participating in a learning event or forum.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your information.
2. Select the options that apply to your interests and expectations.
3. Answer the questions in the text fields.
4. Submit the form to register your participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter your school name to help us identify your institution.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address to contact you about your participation.
* **What is your question?** (`question1`, text, required): Share your question or topic of interest with us.
* **Do you have any questions or concerns?** (`question2`, text, required): Share any questions or concerns you have about the event.
* **Interest** (`interest`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply to indicate your interests:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Sign-up Interest** (`signup_interest`, text, required): Briefly describe your interests.
* **Are you available to participate?** (`question3`, select_one, required): Select:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **How often can you participate?** (`question4`, select_one, required): Select how often you can participate:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **How do you want to be contacted?** (`question5`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **What is your preferred contact method?** (`question6`, select_one, required): Select:
	+ Yes
	+ No
