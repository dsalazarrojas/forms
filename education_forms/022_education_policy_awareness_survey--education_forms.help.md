# Education Policy Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from educators, administrators, and other stakeholders in the education sector to better understand their awareness and understanding of educational policies. The data collected will help in creating a comprehensive report that will aid in policy development and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions carefully and accurately. You can choose one or multiple options for each question. If you're unsure about any question, please refer to the "Field-by-Field Explanation" section for clarification. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Student Role 
* **Student**: Please select this option if you are a student.
* **Teacher**: Please select this option if you are a teacher or instructor.
* **Parent**: Please select this option if you are a parent or guardian.
* **Other**: Please select this option if you don't fit into the above categories.

### Years of Service 
* This field is for you to indicate the number of years you've been working in education.

### Policy Understanding 
* **Strongly Agree**: You have a good understanding of educational policies.
* **Somewhat Agree**: You have some understanding of educational policies.
* **Somewhat Disagree**: You have some understanding but don't entirely agree with educational policies.
* **Strongly Disagree**: You have no understanding of educational policies.

### Importance of Policy 
* **Very Important**: Educational policies are very important in your decision-making.
* **Somewhat Important**: Educational policies are somewhat important in your decision-making.
* **Not Very Important**: Educational policies are not very important in your decision-making.
* **Not At All Important**: Educational policies are not important in your decision-making.

### Additional Comments 
* Please use this field to provide any additional comments or suggestions about educational policies.

### Contact Method 
* **Email**: Please select this option if you prefer to be contacted via email.
* **Phone**: Please select this option if you prefer to be contacted via phone.
* **Text Message**: Please select this option if you prefer to be contacted via text message.

### Contact Information 
* This field is for you to provide your email address.

### Phone Number 
* This field is for you to provide your phone number.

### Name 
* This field is for you to provide your name.

### School Department 
* This field is for you to specify the department you work for.

### School District 
* This field is for you to specify the school district you work for.

### School Name 
* This field is for you to specify the name of the school you work for.

### School Level 
* This field is for you to specify the level of school you work for (e.g., Elementary, Secondary, High School).

### School Email 
* This field is for you to provide the school's email address.

### School Phone Number 
* This field is for you to provide the school's phone number.

### School Address 1 
* This field is for you to provide the school's address line 1.

### School Address 2 
* This field is for you to provide the school's address line 2.

### School City

### School State 
* This field is for you to provide the state of the school.

### School Zip Code

### School Fax Number

## Tips
* Please fill out the form carefully and accurately.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, refer to the "Field-by-Field Explanation" section.
* If you're unsure about any field, please use your best judgment and provide the most relevant information.
