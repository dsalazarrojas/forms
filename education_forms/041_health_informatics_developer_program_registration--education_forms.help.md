# Health Informatics Developer Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from applicants interested in joining the Health Informatics Developer Program. The purpose of this form is to gather details about the applicant's education background, work experience, and contact information. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
2. Enter your education background, work experience, and program details.
3. Upload your resume.
4. Provide your contact details and emergency contact information.
5. Review your submission for accuracy before clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Details** (`program_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the program you are registering for.
* **Program Duration** (`program_duration`, text, required): Enter the duration of the program you are registering for.
* **Program Location** (`program_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the program you are registering for.
* **Program Start Date** (`program_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the program you are registering for.
* **Program End Date** (`program_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the program you are registering for.
* **Education Background** (`education_background`, text, required): Provide a brief description of your education background.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, required): Describe your relevant work experience in the field of health informatics.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, text, required): Indicate your level of education (e.g., bachelor's, master's, or Ph.D.).
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, required): Upload your resume to demonstrate your qualifications.
* **Resume Upload** (`upload_resume`, text, required): Upload your resume file.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required): Enter your contact information, including name, address, and other relevant details.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, text, required): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Details** (`emergency_contact_details`, text, required): Provide the contact information of an emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, text, required): Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Title** (`emergency_contact_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Institution** (`emergency_contact_institution`, text, required): Enter the institution of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Note** (`emergency_contact_note`, text, required): Enter any additional note about the emergency contact person.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click this button to submit your registration.
* **Cancel** (`cancel`, text, required): Click this button to cancel your submission and start over.
* **Program Start Date** (`program_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the program you are registering for, if applicable.
* **Program End Date** (`program_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the program you are registering for, if applicable.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the program you are registering for, if applicable.

Note: The optional fields can be filled out if you have additional information to provide.
