# Handwriting Program Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants in a handwriting program. The form helps us understand your goals, schedule, and contact details, which will allow us to tailor the program to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the **Participant Details** section with your name and any other relevant information about yourself.
2.  In the **Program Goals** section, describe your objectives for the handwriting program. Please be specific and brief.
3.  In the **Contact Information** section, provide your contact details, such as phone number and email.
4.  Finally, in the **Schedule A Session** section, tell us about your preferred schedule for the session. Be as detailed as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Details**: This is the first section where you can enter your name and any other details you think are relevant to us.
- **Program Goals**: Describe your specific objectives for the handwriting program. What do you hope to achieve through this program?
- **Contact Information**: Please provide your contact details so we can get in touch with you.
- **Schedule A Session**: Let us know about your preferred schedule for the session. Be as detailed as possible.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to help us tailor the program to your needs.
- Please double-check your contact details for accuracy.
- If you have any questions or need further clarification, feel free to ask.
