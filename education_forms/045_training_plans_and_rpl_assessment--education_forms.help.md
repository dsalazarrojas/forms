# Training Plans And RPL Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create and track training plans for employees, assess RPL status, and monitor training progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Provide the title of the training in the "Training Title" field.
3. If applicable, enter the date of the training in the "Training Date" field.
4. Enter the duration of the training in the "Training Time" field.
5. Select the training location from the available options in the "Training Venue" field.
6. If the training location is not listed, enter the alternative location in the "Other Training Venue" field.
7. Select the current status of the training from the available options in the "Training Status" field.
8. If the training status is not listed, enter the alternative status in the "Other Training Status" field.
9. Provide evidence of the training in the "Training Evidence" field.
10. Select the current RPL status from the available options in the "RPL Status" field.
11. If the RPL status is not listed, enter the alternative status in the "Other RPL Status" field.
12. Provide evidence of the RPL status in the "RPL Evidence" field.
13. Enter any comments or feedback from the employee in the "Employee Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the employee receiving the training.
* **Training Title** (`training_title`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the training being completed.
* **Training Date** (`training_date`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the date of the training.
* **Training Time** (`training_time`, time, optional): Enter the duration of the training.
* **Training Location** (`training_location`, text, optional): Select the training location from the available options.
* **Other Training Venue** (`training_venue_other`, text, optional): Enter an alternative location if not listed.
* **Training Status** (`training_status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of the training from the available options.
* **Other Training Status** (`training_status_other`, text, optional): Enter an alternative status if not listed.
* **Training Evidence** (`training_evidence`, note, optional): Provide evidence of the training completion.
* **RPL Status** (`rpl_status`, select_one, required): Select the current RPL status from the available options.
* **Other RPL Status** (`rpl_status_other`, text, optional): Enter an alternative RPL status if not listed.
* **RPL Evidence** (`rpl_evidence`, note, optional): Provide evidence of the RPL status.
* **Employee Comments** (`employee_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or feedback from the employee.
