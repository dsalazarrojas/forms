# informatics_institute_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to collect information from potential applicants for admission to the Informatics Institute. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your Email in the Email field, using the correct email format (e.g., [username@example.com](mailto:username@example.com).
4. Enter your Phone Number in the Phone Number field.
5. Select your preferred Academic Program from the Academic Program field.
6. Enter your Date of Birth in the Date of Birth field, using the correct date format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
7. Enter your Time of Birth in the Time of Birth field, if applicable.
8. Enter your Address in the Address field, providing your current address.
9. (Optional) If relevant, provide your Education Background in the Education Background field.
10. (Optional) If relevant, provide your Work Experience in the Work Experience field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, Text, Required/Optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text, Required/Optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required/Optional): Enter your email address, using the correct email format (e.g., [username@example.com](mailto:username@example.com)).
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, Text, Required/Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Academic Program** (`academic_program`, Select One, Required): Select the academic program you are interested in, such as Bachelor of Science in Computer Science or Master of Science in Computer Science.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, Date, Required/Optional): Enter your date of birth, using the correct date format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, Time, Required/Optional): Enter your time of birth, if applicable.
* **Address** (`address`, Note, Required/Optional): Enter your current address.
* **Education Background** (`education_background`, Note, Required/Optional): Provide your education background, if relevant.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, Note, Required/Optional): Provide your work experience, if relevant.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate and complete information in each field.
* If you are having trouble with any field, please contact us for assistance.
* We will review and verify the submitted information before making any decisions regarding admission to the Institute.
