# World Religions Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The World Religions Knowledge Quiz is designed to collect data on users' knowledge and preferences related to various religions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:
- Read through each question carefully and answer accordingly.
- Fill in your answers in the provided text boxes.
- Make sure to answer all required questions (marked as "required: true").
- You can skip questions that are not required.
- Review your answers before submission to ensure accuracy.
