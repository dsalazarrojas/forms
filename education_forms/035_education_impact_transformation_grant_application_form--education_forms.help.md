# Education Impact Transformation Grant Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Education Impact Transformation Grant Application Form is a form designed for applicants to provide their information and application details for an education grant. This form is used to collect relevant data from applicants to assess their eligibility for the grant.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name, email address, phone number, and address in the respective fields.
2. Select the category you fall under (Education Forms, Scholarship Application Forms, or Other Forms).
3. Choose the correct options for the required fields (required_1, required_2, required_3, required_4, required_5, required_6, required_7, required_8, required_9, required_10).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name (id: 1, <name>, text, required)**: Enter your name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Email (id: 2, <email>, email, required)**: Enter your valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone (id: 3, <phone>, text, required)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Category (id: 4, <category, select_one, required)**: Select the category you fall under (Education Forms, Scholarship Application Forms, or Other Forms).
* **Address (id: 5, <address>, text, required)**: Enter your address where you can be reached.
* **Date (id: 6, <date>, date, required)**: Enter the date of your application.
* **Time (id: 7, <time, time, required)**: Enter the time of your application.
* **Notes (id: 8, <notes, note, required)**: Enter any additional comments or notes you would like to include with your application.
* **Required 1 (id: 9, <required_1, text, required)**: Enter any required fields that need to be filled (e.g., ID number).
* **Required 2 (id: 10, <required_2, select_multiple, required)**: Select multiple email options that are relevant to your application.
* **Required 3 (id: 11, <required_3, select_multiple, required)**: Select multiple phone numbers that are relevant to your application.
* **Required 4 (id: 12, <category, select_multiple, required)**: Select multiple category options that are relevant to your application.
* **Required 5 (id: 13, <address, select_multiple, required)**: Select multiple address options that are relevant to your application.
* **Required 6 (id: 14, <required_6, select_multiple, required)**: Select multiple options for your name that are relevant to your application.
* **Required 7 (id: 15, <required_7, select_multiple, required)**: Select multiple email options that are relevant to your application.
* **Required 8 (id: 16, <required_8, select_multiple, required)**: Select multiple phone options that are relevant to your application.
* **Required 9 (id: 17, <required_7, select_multiple, required)**: Select multiple category options that are relevant to your application.
* **Required 10 (id: 18, <required_10, select_multiple, required)**: Select multiple address options that are relevant to your application.

## Tips

- Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and honestly.
- Double-check that you have selected the correct options for each required field.
- If you have any doubts or questions, please contact the grant administrators for assistance.
