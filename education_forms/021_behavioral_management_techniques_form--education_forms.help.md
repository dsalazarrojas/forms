# Behavioral Management Techniques Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by educators to document behavioral management techniques applied to students with specific behavioral issues. It helps track progress towards behavior change and identify next steps for follow-up actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Select the grade level of the student.
3. Enter the date of the observation.
4. Enter the teacher's name.
5. Describe the behavioral issue in the "Describe the Behavioral Issue" field.
6. Indicate the frequency of the behavior in the "Frequency of Behavior" field.
7. Indicate the severity of the behavior in the "Severity of Behavior" field.
8. Choose the management techniques applied in the "Management Technique 1 Applied" and "Management Technique 2 Applied" fields.
9. Evaluate the effectiveness of each technique in the "Effectiveness of Technique 1" and "Effectiveness of Technique 2" fields.
10. Record the student's response to interventions in the "Student Response to Interventions" field.
11. Indicate progress towards behavior change in the "Progress Toward Behavior Change" field.
12. Confirm or deny the need for follow-up actions in the "Follow-up Actions Needed" field.
13. Provide a description of any follow-up actions needed in the "Description of Follow-up Actions" field.
14. Enter the next review date (if applicable).
15. Add any additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's full name.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_one, required): Select the grade level of the student.
* **Date of Observation** (`observation_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the observation.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required): Enter the teacher's name.
* **Describe the Behavioral Issue** (`behavioral_issue`, text, required): Describe the behavioral issue in detail.
* **Frequency of Behavior** (`issue_frequency`, select_one, required): Indicate the frequency of the behavior (Rarely, Sometimes, Often, Very Often).
* **Severity of Behavior** (`issue_severity`, select_one, required): Indicate the severity of the behavior (Mild, Moderate, Severe).
* **Identify Behavioral Triggers** (`trigger_identification`, text, optional): Identify the triggers for the behavioral issue (if applicable).
* **Management Technique 1 Applied** (`technique_1`, select_one, required): Choose the management technique applied (Positive Reinforcement, Redirecting Attention, Timeout, Loss of Privileges, Class Discussion, Parent Communication, Other).
* **Effectiveness of Technique 1** (`technique_1_effectiveness`, select_one, required): Evaluate the effectiveness of the technique applied.
* **Management Technique 2 Applied** (`technique_2`, select_one, optional): Choose the second management technique applied (if applicable).
* **Effectiveness of Technique 2** (`technique_2_effectiveness`, select_one, optional): Evaluate the effectiveness of the second technique applied (if applicable).
* **Student Response to Interventions** (`student_response`, text, optional): Record the student's response to interventions.
* **Progress Toward Behavior Change** (`progress_made`, select_one, required): Indicate progress towards behavior change (Significant Progress, Some Progress, No Progress, Regression).
* **Follow-up Actions Needed** (`followup_needed`, select_one, required): Confirm or deny the need for follow-up actions.
* **Description of Follow-up Actions** (`followup_description`, text, optional): Provide a description of any follow-up actions needed.
* **Next Review Date** (`next_review_date`, date, optional): Enter the next review date (if applicable).
* **Additional Comments** (`teacher_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments.
