# Nursing Student Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nursing Student Evaluation Form is designed to collect feedback and evaluations from nursing students regarding their experience with their instructors and the overall course content. This form will help instructors gain insights and improve the quality of their teaching and the overall learning experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please answer all questions based on your honest opinion.
* Review each question carefully and select the option that best represents your opinion.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Classroom Experience** (`classroom_experience`, text, optional): This field is for you to share your thoughts and experiences about your time in the classroom. Please write any notable events or incidents that happened during class.
* **Teacher Support** (`teacher_support`, select_one, optional): This field asks about your opinion on how well your instructor supported you during the course. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
	+ Option 1: Strongly Agree
	+ Option 2: Somewhat Agree
	+ Option 3: Neither Agree nor Disagree
* **Teacher Support Hints** (`teacher_support_hints`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or thoughts about the teacher's support, please share them here.
* **Course Materials** (`course_materials`, select_multiple, optional): This field asks about the quality and usefulness of the course materials. Choose the options that best describe your experience.
	+ Option 1: Strongly Agree
	+ Option 2: Somewhat Agree
	+ Option 3: Neither Agree nor Disagree
* **Communication with Teacher** (`communication_with_teacher`, text, optional): This field is for you to share your thoughts about how well you felt supported or communicated with by your instructor.
* **Instructor Clarity** (`instructor_clarity`, number, optional): Please rate how clear and easy to understand your instructor's instructions were.
* **Instructor Friendliness** (`instructor_friendliness`, select_one, optional): This field asks about how well you felt you connected with your instructor. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
	+ Option 1: Strongly Agree
	+ Option 2: Somewhat Agree
	+ Option 3: Neither Agree nor Disagree
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): This field asks about your overall satisfaction with the course. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
	+ Option 1: Strongly Satisfied
	+ Option 2: Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Option 3: Neither Satisfied nor Dissatisfied
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): This field is for you to share any additional comments or thoughts about your experience with the course.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions for improvements, please share them here.
* **Classroom Environment** (`classroom_environment`, text, optional): This field is for you to share your thoughts about the classroom environment and any notable events or incidents that happened.
* **Teacher Availability** (`teacher_availability`, select_one, optional): This field asks about how easily you could get in touch with your instructor. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
	+ Option 1: Strongly Agree
	+ Option 2: Somewhat Agree
	+ Option 3: Neither Agree nor Disagree
* **Instructor Quality** (`instructor_quality`, select_one, optional): This field asks about how well you felt your instructor's teaching quality. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
	+ Option 1: Strongly Agree
	+ Option 2: Somewhat Agree
	+ Option 3: Neither Agree nor Disagree
* **Course Relevance** (`course_relevance`, select_one, optional): This field asks about how relevant you felt the course content was to your learning. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
	+ Option 1: Strongly Agree
	+ Option 2: Somewhat Agree
	+ Option 3: Neither Agree nor Disagree
* **Course Effectiveness** (`course_effectiveness`, select_one, optional): This field asks about how effective you felt the course was in achieving its goals. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
	+ Option 1: Strongly Agree
	+ Option 2: Somewhat Agree
	+ Option 3: Neither Agree nor Disagree
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): This field is for you to share any additional feedback or suggestions.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): This field is for you to share any additional comments or thoughts about your experience.
* **Instructor Supportiveness** (`instructor_supportiveness`, select_one, optional): This field asks about how well you felt supported by your instructor. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
	+ Option 1: Strongly Agree
	+ Option 2: Somewhat Agree
	+ Option 3: Neither Agree nor Disagree
* **Communication with Instructors** (`communication_with_instructors`, select_multiple, optional): This field asks about how well you felt you connected with your instructors. Choose the options that best describe your experience.
	+ Option 1: Strongly Agree
	+ Option 2: Somewhat Agree
	+ Option 3: Neither Agree nor Disagree

## Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
* Review each question carefully and select the option that best describes your experience.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Additional Comments" field.
