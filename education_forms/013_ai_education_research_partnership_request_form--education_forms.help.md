# AI Education Research Partnership Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The AI Education Research Partnership Request Form is a submission form used by researchers to request partnership for their research projects. This form is used to collect necessary information about the researcher, their institution, and the project details to facilitate partnership requests.

## How To Complete This Form

Please follow these steps to complete the form:

1. Fill in the required information for the principal investigator and their institution.
2. Select the partnership type and provide a brief summary of the research project and its goals.
3. Choose the relevant AI focus areas and educational levels for the project.
4. Provide project logistics and outcomes, including proposed duration, current funding status, resources requested, expected outcomes, and institutional review board approval status.
5. Complete any additional fields as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Principal Investigator (PI) Name**: The name of the principal investigator or researcher submitting the partnership request.
* **Contact Email**: The email address of the principal investigator or researcher.
* **Institutional Affiliation**: The name of the university, research lab, or company where the researcher is affiliated with.
* **Department / Research Center**: The department or research center where the researcher is working on the project.
* **Partnership Type**: The type of partnership being requested (Academic-Industry Collaboration, Inter-University Partnership, Non-Profit / Public Sector Collaboration, or Technology Provider Partnership).
* **Research Project Title**: A brief title of the research project.
* **Research Abstract / Executive Summary**: A brief summary of the proposed research project and its goals.
* **AI in Education Focus Areas**: Select all relevant areas of AI in education that the research project focuses on (Generative AI / LLMs in the Classroom, Personalized / Adaptive Learning Systems, AI Ethics and Policy in Education, Intelligent Tutoring Systems, Automated Assessment & Feedback, Educational Data Mining / Analytics).
* **Target Educational Level**: Select all relevant educational levels for the project (Early Childhood (Pre-K), K-12 Education, Higher Education (Undergraduate/Graduate), Vocational / Professional Training, Informal / Lifelong Learning).
* **Proposed Project Duration (Months)**: The proposed duration of the project in months.
* **Current Funding Status**: The current funding status of the project (Fully Funded, Partially Funded, Seeking Funding (Grant Proposal Phase), Not Funded (In-kind partnership only)).
* **Resources Requested from Partner**: The resources requested from the partner (e.g., data access, computing power, subject access, funding).
* **Expected Outcomes and Impact**: The expected outcomes and impact of the project (publications, software tools, policy recommendations, etc.).
* **Institutional Review Board (IRB) / Ethics Approval Status**: The status of the institutional review board approval (Approved, Submitted / Pending, Not Yet Submitted, Not Applicable).
* **Additional Comments or Specific Requirements**: Any additional comments or specific requirements for the project.
* **Date of Submission**: The date when the submission was made.

Please note that all required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*) in the form. Please make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
