# Educational Technology Expo Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Technology Expo Registration Form is a tool for attendees to register for the event. The form collects personal and organization-related information to provide attendees with a tailored experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Provide your Email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your Organization name in the Organization field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, optional): Enter your given name, as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, optional): Enter your surname as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address that you use to communicate with your organization or institution.
* **Organization** (`Organization`, text, optional): Enter the name of the school, university, or institution you represent.
