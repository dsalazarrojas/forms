# Child Biographical Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect biographical information about a child, including their personal details, medical history, and contact information for parents or guardians.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have the necessary permissions and follow any applicable laws and regulations regarding data collection and usage.
2. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*) in the form.
3. Provide accurate and complete information in each field, taking into account the hints and labels.
4. Review and verify the form before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Child's First Name** (`child_first_name`, text, required): Enter the child's first name.
* **Child's Last Name** (`child_last_name`, text, required): Enter the child's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the child's date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required): Select the child's gender from the provided options.
* **Place of Birth (city, state, country)** (`place_of_birth`, text, optional): Enter the city, state, and country where the child was born.
* **Citizenship Status** (`citizenship_status`, select_one, optional): Select the child's citizenship status from the provided options.
* **Primary Language Spoken at Home** (`primary_language`, text, required): Enter the primary language spoken at home.
* **Parent or Guardian 1 Name** (`parent_1_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the first parent or guardian.
* **Relationship to Child** (`parent_1_relationship`, select_one, required): Select the relationship between the child and the parent or guardian (e.g., Mother, Father, Guardian).
* **Parent or Guardian 1 Phone** (`parent_1_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the first parent or guardian.
* **Email Address** (`parent_1_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the first parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian 2 Name (if applicable)** (`parent_2_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the second parent or guardian (if applicable).
* **Parent 2 Relationship (if applicable)** (`parent_2_relationship`, select_one, optional): Select the relationship between the child and the second parent or guardian (if applicable).
* **Parent 2 Phone (if applicable)** (`parent_2_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the second parent or guardian (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required): Select the relationship between the child and the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Health Insurance Provider** (`health_insurance_provider`, text, optional): Enter the name of the health insurance provider.
* **Policy Number** (`policy_number`, text, optional): Enter the policy number of the health insurance provider.
* **Allergies (food, medication, environmental)** (`allergies`, text, optional): Enter any allergies or sensitivities the child may have.
* **Current Medications** (`medications`, text, optional): Enter any current medications the child is taking.
* **Chronic Medical Conditions** (`chronic_conditions`, text, optional): Enter any chronic medical conditions the child may have.
* **Special Needs or Accommodations** (`special_needs_accommodations`, text, optional): Enter any special needs or accommodations the child may require.
* **Immunization Status** (`immunization_status`, select_one, required): Select the child's immunization status from the provided options.
* **Recent Illness or Exposure to Illness** (`recent_illness_exposure`, text, optional): Enter any recent illnesses or exposures to illnesses the child may have experienced.

## Tips

* Be accurate and complete in your responses.
* Double-check the form before submission.
* If you are unsure about any field or requirement, ask for clarification before proceeding.
* This form is designed to be completed by individuals with permission to access it, so please ensure you have the necessary permissions before filling it out.
