# Campus Tour Scheduling Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from visitors to the campus who are interested in scheduling a tour.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including First Name, Last Name, and Email Address.
2. Select your visitor category (Prospective Student, Parent/Guardian, Transfer Student, etc.).
3. Choose a primary reason for visiting the campus.
4. Select your preferred tour date and time.
5. Specify the number of people in your group and their composition.
6. Choose any specific areas of interest on campus.
7. If necessary, indicate any accessibility needs or requirements.
8. Provide any additional information or comments you would like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`2`, `text`, **required**): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`3`, `text`, **required**): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`4`, `email`, **required**): Enter your email address, which we will use to send tour confirmations.
* **Phone Number** (`5`, `text`, **required**): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Are you a prospective student, parent, or other?** (`6`, `select_one`, **required**): Select one of the options to indicate your visitor category.
* **Primary reason for visiting campus** (`7`, `select_one`, **required**): Select one of the options to indicate the primary reason for your visit.
* **Preferred Tour Date** (`9`, `date`, **required**): Choose a date for your tour.
* **Preferred Tour Time** (`10`, `select_one`, **required**): Select a time slot for your tour.
* **Number of people in your group** (`11`, `number`, **required**): Enter the number of people in your group.
* **Who else will be joining you?** (`12`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Select all members of your group from the options.
* **Areas of interest on campus** (`13`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Select all areas of the campus that interest you.
* **Do you require accessibility accommodations?** (`15`, `select_one`, **required**): Indicate if you have any accessibility needs.
* **Please describe your accessibility needs** (`16`, `text`, **optional**): Provide any additional details about your accessibility needs.
* **Intended Major or Field of Study** (`18`, `text`, **optional**): If known, enter your intended major or field of study.
* **Current or Expected Year of Entry** (`19`, `select_one`, **optional**): Select your current or expected year of entry.
* **Additional Questions or Comments** (`20`, `text`, **optional**): Leave any additional comments or questions you have.
