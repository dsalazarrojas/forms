# Vocabulary Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vocabulary Quiz form is designed to help educators or administrators collect and track student's vocabulary knowledge.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your student's first and last name in the **First Name** and **Last Name** fields, respectively.
2. Select your student's **Grade Level** from the provided options (if applicable).
3. Enter the **Subject** that the vocabulary word relates to.
4. Enter the **Vocabulary Word** that you want to test.
5. Enter the **Definition** of the vocabulary word.
6. Select whether the **Correct Answer** is yes or no.
7. Enter your student's answer to the vocabulary word.
8. Enter a score for the student's answer (if applicable).
9. Enter any additional **Result** information, if needed.
10. Enter the date and time you submitted the form in the **Date Submitted** and **Time Submitted** fields, respectively.
11. Enter your contact information in the **Submitter** field, including your email address and phone number.
12. Enter any additional comments or notes about the submission in the **Submitter Comments** field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Enter the student's first name.
- **Last Name**: Enter the student's last name.
- **Grade Level**: Select the student's grade level (if applicable).
- **Subject**: Enter the subject that the vocabulary word relates to.
- **Vocabulary Word**: Enter the word you want to test.
- **Definition**: Enter the definition of the vocabulary word.
- **Correct Answer**: Select whether the answer is yes or no.
- **Student Answer**: Enter your student's answer to the vocabulary word.
- **Score**: Enter a score for the student's answer (if applicable).
- **Result**: Enter any additional result information, if needed.
- **Date Submitted**: Enter the date you submitted the form.
- **Time Submitted**: Enter the time you submitted the form.
- **Submitter**: Enter your contact information, including email address and phone number.
- **Submitter Email**: Enter your email address.
- **Submitter Phone**: Enter your phone number.
- **Submitter Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the submission.
- **Submitter Location**: Enter your location.
- **Submitter Department**: Enter your department.
- **Submitter Job Title**: Enter your job title.
- **Submitter Organization**: Enter your organization.
- **Submitter Job Code**: Enter your job code.
- **Submitter Job Description**: Enter your job description.
- **Submitter Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the submission.
