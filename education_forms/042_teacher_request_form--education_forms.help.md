# teacher_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The teacher_request_form is a form designed to capture information from a teacher about a student's request. This form will help the teacher to provide the necessary details about the student's needs and preferences for support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form with the student's information, including their name, email, phone number, request date and time, and any additional message or comment.
2. Select the tool that the teacher wants to assign to the student from the list provided.
3. If the teacher wants to assign a custom tool that is not on the list, enter the tool in the "assigned_tool_other" field.
4. Review your entries before submitting the form to ensure that all required information is complete and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the student who is requesting support.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the student who is requesting support.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the student who is requesting support.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the student is requesting support on.
* **Request Time** (`request_time`, time, optional): Enter the time the student is requesting support on.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): Enter any additional message or comment about the student's request.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, optional): Enter your own name as the teacher.
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Teacher Phone** (`teacher_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Select the tool that you want to assign to the student from the list.
* **Assigned Tool Other** (`assigned_tool_other`, text, optional): If you selected "Other" for the tool, enter the custom tool name here.
