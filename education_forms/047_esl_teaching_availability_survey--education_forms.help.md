# ESL Teaching Availability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect user information for teaching purposes to schedule sessions with potential students.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields, including Form ID, Category, Form ID, Schedule Start, Schedule End, Schedule Frequency, Schedule Days, Schedule Time, Availability, Contact Method, and Assigned User.
2. If you have a preferred contact method, select it from the list.
3. Enter your schedule and availability information in the text fields.
4. Select the assigned user who will be working with you.
5. Confirm your schedule information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, `text`, optional): Enter a title for the form, which will help us to identify it.
* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, required): Select the category of the form, such as Education Forms.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, optional): Enter a short description of the form.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, `text`, required): Enter a unique Form ID to identify and track your submissions.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, required): Select the tool assigned to you.
* **Schedule Start** (`schedule_start`, `date`, required): Enter the start date of your schedule.
* **Schedule End** (`schedule_end`, `date`, required): Enter the end date of your schedule.
* **Schedule Frequency** (`schedule_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select how often you would like to schedule, such as Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
* **Schedule Days** (`schedule_days`, `select_multiple`, required): Select which days of the week you would like to schedule, such as Monday to Friday.
* **Schedule Time** (`schedule_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of day you would like to schedule.
* **Availability** (`availability`, `text`, required): Enter your schedule availability.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, `select_one`, required): Select how you would like to be contacted, such as Phone, Email, or Chat/Message.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone_number`, `text`, optional): If selected as contact method, enter your phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`email_address`, `email`, optional): If selected as contact method, enter your email address.
* **Contact Chat Message** (`contact_chat_message`, `text`, optional): If selected as contact method, enter your chat message.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, `select_one`, required): Select the assigned user who will be working with you.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Confirmation** (`confirmation`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your schedule information.
* **Contact Method (Duplicate)** (`contact_method`, `select_one`, optional): Select how you would like to be contacted (this field seems to be a duplicate of the previous field).
* **Form ID Duplicate** (`form_id_duplicate`, `text`, optional): This field seems to be a duplicate of the Form ID field.
* **Form ID 1 Duplicate** (`form_id_1_duplicate`, `text`, optional): This field seems to be a duplicate of the Form ID field.
* **Form ID 2 Duplicate** (`form_id_2_duplicate`, `text`, optional): This field seems to be a duplicate of the Form ID field.
* **Form ID 3 Duplicate** (`form_id_3_duplicate`, `text`, optional): This field seems to be a duplicate of the Form ID field.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your schedule information.
* Ensure that your contact method and contact information are accurate.
