# Summer League Team Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Summer League Team Registration Form is designed to gather information from teams participating in the summer program. Teams can use this form to provide their details, such as league participation, team name, contact information, and fundraising goals.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the leagues you'd like your team to participate in by choosing one or more leagues from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter your team's name and age in the corresponding fields.
3. Provide a contact email address for your team in the "Team Email" field.
4. Fill in the "Team Contact" field with the name of the person responsible for contacting you.
5. Enter a phone number for the team contact person in the "Team Phone" field.
6. Provide the team's address in the "Team Address" field.
7. Add any additional notes about your team in the "Team Notes" field.
8. List the names of your team's coaches in the "Team Coaches" field.
9. List the names of your team players in the "Team Player" field.
10. Select whether your team is participating or not participating in the summer program in the "Team Participation" dropdown menu.
11. Enter any additional coach information in the "Team Coach Info" field.
12. Enter the coach's contact phone number in the "Team Coach Phone" field.
13. Provide the coach's contact email address in the "Team Coach Email" field.
14. Enter the coach's address in the "Team Coach Address" field.
15. Add any additional player information in the "Team Player Info" field.
16. Enter the player's contact phone number in the "Team Player Phone" field.
17. Provide the player's contact email address in the "Team Player Email" field.
18. Enter the player's address in the "Team Player Address" field.
19. Set your team's fundraising goal and amount in the "Team Fundraising Goal" and "Team Fundraising Amount" fields, respectively.
20. Add any additional fundraising information in the "Team Fundraising Notes" field.
21. Enter the date and time of the fundraising event in the "Team Fundraising Date" and "Team Fundraising Time" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Details**: Enter your team's name and age.
* **Team League Selection**: Select the leagues your team would like to participate in.
* **Team Name**: Enter your team's name.
* **Team Age**: Enter your team's age.
* **Team Email**: A contact email address for your team.
* **Team Contact**: The name of the person responsible for contacting you.
* **Team Phone**: A team contact phone number.
* **Team Address**: A team address.
* **Team Notes**: Additional notes about your team.
* **Team Coaches**: List the names of your team's coaches.
* **Team Player**: List the names of your team players.
* **Team Participation**: Select whether your team is participating or not participating in the summer program.
* **Team Coach Info**: Additional coach information.
* **Team Coach Phone**: A coach contact phone number.
* **Team Coach Email**: A coach contact email address.
* **Team Coach Address**: A coach address.
* **Team Player Info**: Additional player information.
* **Team Player Phone**: A player contact phone number.
* **Team Player Email**: A player contact email address.
* **Team Player Address**: A player address.
* **Team Fundraising Goal**: The team's fundraising goal.
* **Team Fundraising Amount**: The team's fundraising amount.
* **Team Fundraising Notes**: Additional fundraising information.
* **Team Fundraising Date**: The date of the fundraising event.
* **Team Fundraising Time**: The time of the fundraising event.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your team's information for accuracy.
* Ensure that you have selected the correct leagues for your team.
* Be clear and concise in providing your team's contact information and notes.
* Review your team's fundraising information for accuracy and completeness.
