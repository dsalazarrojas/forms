# University Research Proposal Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Research Proposal Voting Form is used to evaluate and vote on research proposals submitted by university faculty members. This form is intended to gather evaluations and scores from research committees for each proposal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the research committee member(s) who will evaluate your proposal in the "Research Committee" field.
2. Enter a title for your proposal in the "Proposal Title" field.
3. Write a brief summary of your proposal in the "Proposal Summary" field.
4. Define the evaluation criteria for your proposal in the "Evaluation Criteria" field.
5. Enter a score for your proposal on the scale defined in the "Evaluation Scale" field.
6. Enter any evaluation comments in the "Evaluation Comments" field.
7. Cast a vote on your proposal by selecting 'True' or 'False' in the "Vote" field.
8. Enter the date of your evaluation in the "Evaluation Date" field.
9. Enter the time of your evaluation in the "Evaluation Time" field.
10. Enter the name of the university in the "University" field.
11. Identify the research committee member who will be handling your proposal in the "Research Committee Member" field.
12. Enter the title of your research project in the "Research Project Title" field.
13. Enter a description of your research project in the "Research Project Description" field.
14. Enter the amount of funding for your research project in the "Research Funding Amount" field.
15. Enter the start and end dates for your research project in the "Research Project Start Date" and "Research Project End Date" fields.
16. Enter the status of your research project in the "Research Project Status" field.
17. Enter the category of your research project in the "Research Project Category" field.
18. Finally, enter any comments on your proposal in the "Comments" field.
