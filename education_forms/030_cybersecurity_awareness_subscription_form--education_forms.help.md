# Cybersecurity Awareness Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect demographic information for cybersecurity awareness purposes. It allows the submission of personal and professional data to receive targeted and relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Fill in your Last Name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. If applicable, fill in your organization and role.
5. If applicable, fill in your job title, department, job function, and company.
6. If applicable, fill in your address, city, state, and zip code for multiple locations (Job Title 1, 2, and 3).
7. Fill in your phone number.
8. If applicable, fill in job title 2 and 3 with additional job details for multiple job roles.

* **First Name**: A person's first name.

* **Email**: A person's email address for subscription.
* **Organization**: Your work organization.

* **City 1**: A person’s city for the first location.

* **State 1**: A person’s state for the first location.

* **Zip 1**: A person’s zip code for the first location.

* **Department 1**: A person’s department for the first location.
