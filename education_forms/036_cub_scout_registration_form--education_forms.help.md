# cub_scout_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "cub_scout_registration_form" is used to register a new scout for a Cub Scout program. This form collects contact information, scout information, and event details to register a scout with the program.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled in (indicated by a red asterisk \*).
3. Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, \*): Enter the first name of the scout to be registered.
* **Last Name** (2, text, \*): Enter the last name of the scout to be registered.
* **Email** (3, email, \*): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Date of Birth** (4, date, \*): Enter the date of birth of the scout.
* **Contact Number** (5, text, \*): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Address** (6, text, ): Enter the address of the contact person (optional).
* **Scout Level** (7, select_one, \*): Select the level of the scout (Den, Pack, Unit, or Troop).
* **Unit ID** (8, number, \*): Enter the ID number of the unit the scout is part of.
* **Scout Name** (9, text, \*): Enter a name for the scout (e.g., nickname).
* **Scout ID** (10, text, \*): Enter a unique ID for the scout.
* **Event Date** (11, date, ): Enter the date of an event the scout will attend (optional).
* **Event Time** (12, time, ): Enter the time of an event the scout will attend (optional).
