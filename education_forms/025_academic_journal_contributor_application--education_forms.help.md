# Academic Journal Contributor Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Journal Contributor Application form is a tool for academic authors to submit their work to a specific academic journal. The form collects essential information from contributors about their submission, including contributor details, manuscript information, and supporting documents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your **Contributor Name** in the "Contributor name" field. This is your full name as it should appear in the journal.
2.  Enter your **Contact Email** in the "Contact email" field. This is the email address where you will receive editorial communication.
3.  Select your **Submission Type** from the dropdown menu. You can choose from "Research article", "Review", "Commentary", "Case study", or "Other".
4.  Enter a brief **Manuscript Title** in the "Manuscript title" field. This should be a concise title for your submission.
5.  Write a short **Abstract** of your submission in the "Abstract" field. This should provide a brief summary of your work.
6.  List any **Keywords** that describe your submission in the "Keywords" field. This field accepts comma-separated keyword list.
7.  If applicable, list **Coauthor Names** and their affiliations in the "Coauthor names" field.
8.  List any **Supporting Documents** that you have provided in the "Supporting documents" field.
9.  If you have a **Conflict of Interest** statement, enter it in the "Conflict of interest statement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contributor name**: Enter your full name as it should appear in the journal.
- **Contact email**: Enter the email address where you will receive editorial communication.
- **Submission type**: Select the type of contribution you are making.
  - **Research article**: A research-based submission.
  - **Review**: A submission for review.
  - **Commentary**: A commentary or short opinion piece.
  - **Case study**: A case study submission.
  - **Other**: Select this if your submission type is not listed above.
- **Manuscript title**: Enter a brief title for your submission.
- **Abstract**: Provide a brief summary of your submission.
- **Keywords**: Enter a comma-separated list of keywords describing your submission.
- **Coauthor names**: List the names and affiliations of coauthors.
- **Supporting documents**: List any documents you are providing to support your submission.
- **Conflict of interest statement**: If you have a conflict of interest, enter it here.
