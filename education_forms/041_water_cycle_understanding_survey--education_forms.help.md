# Water Cycle Understanding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Water Cycle Understanding Survey is a self-assessment tool designed to evaluate the user's knowledge and confidence level regarding the water cycle.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your quiz score in the "Quiz Score" field.
5. Select your confidence level regarding the water cycle in the "Confidence Level" field.
6. Select the water cycle concepts you understand in the "Water Cycle Concepts" field.
7. Enter any additional comments or explanations in the "Additional Comments" field.
8. Review the form for any errors in the "Error" fields, which will prompt you to correct any incorrect information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Quiz Score** (`quiz_score`, number, required): Enter your quiz score, which should be a number between 0 and 100.
* **Confidence Level** (`confidence_level`, select_one, required): Select your confidence level regarding the water cycle from the following options: Very confident, Somewhat confident, Not very confident, Not at all confident.
* **Water Cycle** (`water_cycle`, text, required): Enter a description of your understanding of the water cycle.
* **Water Cycle Concepts** (`water_cycle_concepts`, select_multiple, required): Select the water cycle concepts you understand, which are: Transpiration, Evaporation, Condensation, Clouds, Precipitation, Runoff.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required): Enter any additional comments or explanations about your understanding of the water cycle.
* **First Name Error** (`first_name_error`, text, required): This field will display an error message if your first name is invalid.
* **Last Name Error** (`last_name_error`, text, required): This field will display an error message if your last name is invalid.
* **Email Error** (`email_error`, email, required): This field will display an error message if your email address is invalid.
* **Water Cycle Error** (`water_cycle_error`, text, required): This field will display an error message if your understanding of the water cycle is invalid.
* **Quiz Score Error** (`quiz_score_error`, number, required): This field will display an error message if your quiz score is not a number or not between 0 and 100.
* **Confidence Level Error** (`confidence_level_error`, select_one, required): This field will display an error message if your confidence level is not one of the options: Very confident, Somewhat confident, Not very confident, Not at all confident.
* **Explanation Error** (`explanation_error`, note, required): This field will display an error message if your explanation is invalid.
* **Water Cycle Concepts Error** (`water_cycle_concepts_error`, select_multiple, required): This field will display an error message if you do not select any of the water cycle concepts.
* **Additional Comments Error** (`additional_comments_error`, text, required): This field will display an error message if your additional comments or explanations are invalid.
* **First Name Corrected** (`first_name_corrected`, text, required): This field will display your corrected first name if it was previously entered incorrectly.
* **Last Name Corrected** (`last_name_corrected`, text, required): This field will display your corrected last name if it was previously entered incorrectly.
* **Email Corrected** (`email_corrected`, email, required): This field will display your corrected email address if it was previously entered incorrectly.
* **Water Cycle Corrected** (`water_cycle_corrected`, text, required): This field will display your corrected understanding of the water cycle if it was previously entered incorrectly.
* **Quiz Score Corrected** (`quiz_score_corrected`, number, required): This field will display your corrected quiz score if it was previously entered incorrectly.
* **Confidence Level Corrected** (`confidence_level_corrected`, select_one, required): This field will display your corrected confidence level regarding the water cycle if it was previously entered incorrectly.
* **Explanation Corrected** (`explanation_corrected`, note, required): This field will display your corrected explanation if it was previously entered incorrectly.
