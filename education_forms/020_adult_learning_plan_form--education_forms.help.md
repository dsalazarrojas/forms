# Adult Learning Plan Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help learners create an individualized learning plan that aligns with their specific needs and objectives.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please provide your **Learner Full Name**, **Date of Enrollment**, and **Learner Email** as they are required fields.
2.  Enter your **Date of Enrollment**, which is the date you enrolled in the learning program.
3.  Provide your **Learner Email**, which will be used as a primary contact point.
4.  In the **Education Background** section, describe your highest **Highest Level of Education Completed** (e.g., High School Diploma, Bachelor's Degree).
5.  If applicable, list any **Relevant Certifications or Experience** you have.
6.  In the **Learning Goals** section, specify your:
    *   **Short Term Goal - 3 Months**: Describe a specific, measurable goal you want to achieve in the next three months.
    *   **Long Term Goal - 1 Year**: Describe a specific, measurable goal you want to achieve in the next year.
7.  Select the **Skills to be Developed**, which are **Technical**, **Communication**, **Leadership**, **Analytical**, and **Creative** skills.
8.  In the **Learning Preferences** section, select your preferred **Preferred Learning Style**: **Visual**, **Auditory**, **Kinesthetic**, or **Reading and Writing**.
9.  Indicate your **Time Commitment per Week**, which is the number of hours you can dedicate to learning each week (1-5 hours, 6-10 hours, 11-20 hours, or 20+ hours).
10. If necessary, provide **Specific Learning Accommodations Needed** (e.g., Screen readers, extended time).
11. In the **Milestone Tracking** section, set the **Next Review Date**, which is the date to check progress on your goals.
12. Finally, provide your **Instructor or Mentor Name**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Learner Full Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Date of Enrollment**: The date you enrolled in the learning program.
*   **Learner Email**: The email we will use to communicate with you.
*   **Highest Level of Education Completed**: Describe the level of education you have completed (e.g., High School Diploma, Bachelor's Degree).
*   **Relevant Certifications or Experience**: List any relevant certifications, licenses, or experience you have.
*   **Short Term Goal - 3 Months**: A specific, achievable goal for the next three months.
*   **Long Term Goal - 1 Year**: A specific, achievable goal for the next year.
*   **Skills to be Developed**: Select the skills you want to develop (Technical, Communication, Leadership, Analytical, Creative).
*   **Preferred Learning Style**: Choose your preferred learning style: Visual, Auditory, Kinesthetic, or Reading and Writing.
*   **Time Commitment per Week**: Indicate the number of hours you can dedicate to learning each week.
*   **Specific Learning Accommodations Needed**: If needed, list any specific accommodations you require for learning.
*   **Next Review Date**: Set the date to check progress on your goals.
*   **Instructor or Mentor Name**: Provide the name of your assigned advisor.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
*   Be specific and realistic when setting your **Short Term Goal - 3 Months** and **Long Term Goal - 1 Year**.
*   Choose skills that align with your learning objectives and interests.
*   Consider any specific learning accommodations you may need to complete your goals effectively.
