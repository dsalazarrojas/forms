# Class Relinquishment Claim Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Class Relinquishment Claim Form is designed to help students formally relinquish their enrollment in a class. This form collects information about the student, their academic year, semester, and course details. By filling out this form, students can provide a valid reason for relinquishing their class and help the institution manage their enrollment records accurately.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Please fill out each section carefully, making sure to answer all required questions.
2.  Provide accurate and truthful information, as it may affect your financial aid, graduation timeline, and other important aspects.
3.  Ensure you answer all required questions before submitting the form.
4.  If you are unsure about any section or question, it is best to consult with your academic advisor or institutional representative for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Student Full Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Fill out your full name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Student ID Number** (`student_id`, text, required): Enter your student ID number as it is recorded in the institution's records.
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Provide your school email address so that the institution can communicate with you regarding your class relinquishment.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your best contact number in case the institution needs to contact you regarding your class relinquishment.
- **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`, text, required): Enter your current mailing address, including street, city, and ZIP for official correspondence.
- **Academic Year** (`academic_year`, select_one, required): Select the current academic year you are in (e.g., 2025-2026).
- **Semester or Term** (`semester_term`, select_one, required): Select the current semester or term you are in (e.g., Fall Semester).
- **Class Name to Relinquish** (`class_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the class you wish to relinquish.
- **Class Code or Number** (`class_code`, text, optional): If you know the class code or number, enter it here (e.g., MATH101).
- **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your current instructor.
- **Reason for Relinquishment** (`relinquishment_reason`, select_one, required): Select the primary reason you are relinquishing the class (e.g., Schedule conflict).
- **Please Explain Your Reason** (`reason_details`, text, required): Provide a brief explanation of your reason for relinquishing the class.
- **Current Grade in Class** (`current_grade`, select_one, optional): If known, select your current grade in the class (e.g., A).
- **How Many Weeks Completed** (`weeks_completed`, select_one, required): Select how many weeks of the semester you have completed.
- **Do You Plan to Take a Replacement Class** (`replacement_class`, select_one, required): Select whether you plan to take a replacement class in the same semester.
- **Replacement Class Name** (`replacement_class_name`, text, optional): If you have selected a replacement class, enter its name here.
- **Will This Affect Your Graduation Timeline** (`graduation_impact`, select_one, required): Select if relinquishing this class will delay your graduation.
- **Have You Consulted With Your Advisor** (`advisor_consultation`, select_one, required): Select whether you have consulted with your advisor about this decision.
- **Academic Advisor Name** (`advisor_name`, text, optional): If you have consulted with your advisor, enter their name here.
- **Have You Checked Financial Aid Impact** (`financial_aid_impact`, select_one, required): Select whether relinquishing this class will have any financial aid implications.
- **Are You Requesting a Refund** (`refund_requested`, select_one, required): Select whether you are requesting a full or partial refund for tuition or fees.
- **Do You Plan to Retake This Class** (`future_enrollment`, select_one, required): Select whether you plan to retake this class in the future.
- **Additional Comments or Information** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional information or comments regarding your class relinquishment.

Note: Ensure you answer all required questions before submitting the form. If you are unsure about any section or question, it is best to consult with your academic advisor or institutional representative for guidance.
