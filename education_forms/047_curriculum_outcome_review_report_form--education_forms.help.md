# Curriculum Outcome Review Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Curriculum Outcome Review Report Form is designed to collect information from reviewers on the status of a specific curriculum outcome in a school setting. This form helps reviewers assess the outcome's achievement, provide action plans for improvement, and offer recommendations for further development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **School Name** (Required) - This field is for the name of the school where the outcome is being reviewed.
2. Enter the **Reviewer Name** (Required) - This field is for the name of the person reviewing the outcome.
3. Enter the **Department Name** (Required) - This field is for the department responsible for the outcome.
4. Enter the **School Type** (Optional) - This field is for the type of school (e.g., elementary, middle, high school, etc.).
5. Enter the **Outcome Name** (Required) - This field is for the name of the specific curriculum outcome being reviewed.
6. Enter the **Term Name** (Required) - This field is for the term in which the outcome was reviewed.
7. Enter the **Reviewer Email** (Required) - This field is for the email address of the reviewer.
8. Select the **Outcome Achieved** (Required) - This field has three options: Achieved, Not Achieved, and Partially Achieved.
9. Provide any **Outcome Comments** (Optional) - This field is for additional comments on the outcome's achievement.
10. Enter the **Action Plan** (Optional) - This field is for any plans or strategies for improving the outcome.
11. Enter any **Recommendations** (Optional) - This field is for suggestions for further development or improvement.
12. Enter the **Date Completed** (Required) - This field is for the date the review was completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name**: The name of the school where the outcome was reviewed.
* **Reviewer Name**: The name of the person reviewing the outcome.
* **Department Name**: The department responsible for the outcome.
* **School Type**: The type of school (e.g., elementary, middle, high school).
* **Outcome Name**: The name of the specific curriculum outcome being reviewed.
* **Term Name**: The term in which the outcome was reviewed.
* **Reviewer Email**: The email address of the reviewer.
* **Outcome Achieved**: A select-one field with options: Achieved, Not Achieved, and Partially Achieved.
* **Outcome Comments**: Additional comments on the outcome's achievement.
* **Action Plan**: Strategies or plans for improving the outcome.
* **Recommendations**: Suggestions for further development or improvement.
* **Date Completed**: The date the review was completed.
