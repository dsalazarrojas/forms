# Summer Camp Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Camp Interest Form is a tool to gauge interest in our summer camp program. It helps us understand how many people are anticipating or have already registered for our summer camp program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the number of people anticipating our summer camp.
2.  Fill in the number of people who would register for our summer camp if it were available.
3.  Provide your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How many people are anticipating our summer camp?** (`Anticipated`, number, not required): This field helps us estimate the number of people interested in our summer camp before it opens for registration. If you know the exact number, please fill it in. If not, you can leave it blank.
* **How many people would register for this summer camp?** (`Register Interest`, number, not required): This field gives us an idea of how many people would register for our summer camp if it were available. If you know the exact number, please fill it in. If not, you can leave it blank.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, note, not required): In this field, please provide your name, email address, and phone number so we can contact you with updates and more information about our summer camp program.
