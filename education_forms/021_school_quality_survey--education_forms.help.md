# School Quality Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about the quality of a school. It assesses various aspects of a school's performance, including teaching quality, school environment, facilities, teacher quality, and more.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by providing your honest and constructive feedback about the school. This will help the school administration and staff understand your experiences and opinions.
2. Review each page of the form carefully and answer every question accurately.
3. Use the available options for each question to choose the best description that fits your experience with the school.
4. If you have any specific concerns or suggestions for improvement, provide detailed explanations in the relevant fields.
5. Double-check your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the school you are providing feedback about.
* **Overall Quality** (`overall_quality`, number, required): Rate the overall quality of the school, using a scale of 1 (Poor) to 4 (Excellent).
* **Teaching Quality 1** (`teaching_quality_1`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of teaching at the school, using options of Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **What could be improved** (`teaching_quality_what_improvement`, text, optional): If you have any specific suggestions for improvement for the teaching quality, provide them here.
* **School Environment** (`school_environment`, select_one, required): Rate the school environment, using options of Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **School Facilities** (`school_facilities`, select_multiple, required): Select all facilities that apply to the school, using options of Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Teacher Quality** (`teacher_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of teachers at the school, using options of Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Teacher Helpfulness** (`teacher_helpfulness`, select_multiple, optional): Select all aspects of teacher helpfulness that apply to the school, using options of Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Student Quality** (`student_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of students at the school, using options of Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **School Leadership** (`school_leadership`, select_one, required): Rate the leadership of the school, using options of Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Communication** (`communication`, select_multiple, optional): Select all aspects of communication that apply to the school, using options of Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Student Satisfaction** (`student_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate the satisfaction of students at the school, using options of Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Parent Satisfaction** (`parent_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Rate the satisfaction of parents at the school, using options of Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Teacher Satisfaction** (`teacher_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Rate the satisfaction of teachers at the school, using options of Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Overall Quality What Improvement** (`overall_quality_what_improvement`, text, optional): If you have any specific suggestions for overall quality improvement, provide them here.
* **Overall Quality What Improvement What** (`overall_quality_what_improvement_what`, text, optional): If you have any specific suggestions for overall quality improvement, provide them here.

Note: Please make sure to complete each field accurately and honestly. Your feedback is invaluable to the school, and your input will help shape their improvement and growth.
