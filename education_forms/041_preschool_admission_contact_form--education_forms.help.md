# Preschool Admission Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Preschool Admission Contact Form is a tool used to gather contact information and preferences from parents interested in enrolling their child in our preschool program. This information will be used to facilitate communication and scheduling of meetings with the parents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in your name and any other required fields.
* Enter your child's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* Select one or more of the interest levels that apply to you.
* Choose your preferred contact method for future communication.
* Indicate if you would like to schedule a meeting with a school staff member.
* Enter your email address for school staff to contact you at.
* Provide any additional notes or comments you would like to share.
* Indicate your preferences for follow-up actions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as the parent/guardian.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address so we can contact you with further information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This is your contact phone number for school staff to reach you at.
* **Child Name** (`child_name`, text, optional): Please enter your child's name.
* **Child's Date of Birth** (`child_dob`, date, optional): Enter your child's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Interest Level** (`interest_level`, select_multiple, optional): Please select one or more of the following interest levels that apply to you:
	+ High
	+ Low
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, optional): Please choose how you would like to be contacted:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **School Staff Member** (`school_staff_member`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you would like to schedule a meeting with a school staff member:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **School Staff Email** (`school_staff_email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address so school staff can contact you directly.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): Provide any additional notes or comments you would like to share.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Any additional comments you would like to share about your child's enrollment.
* **Follow-up Action** (`follow_up_action`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you would like the school to follow-up with you:
	+ Yes
	+ No
