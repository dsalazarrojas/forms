# ielts_prediction_test_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IELTS Prediction Test Form is a tool designed to help you predict your IELTS test scores. Fill out the form with your actual test date, center, and scores to get an accurate prediction of your IELTS scores.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your student's information:
	* Enter your student's name in the `Student Name` field.
	* Enter your student's ID number in the `Student ID` field.
2. Select the relevant fields:
	* Choose the date your student took the IELTS test from the `Test Date` field.
	* Enter the name of the test center where the test was taken from the `Test Center` field.
3. Enter your scores:
	* Enter your student's `Band Score` in the `Band Score` field.
	* Enter your student's `Speaking Score` in the `Speaking Score` field.
	* Enter your student's `Listening Score` in the `Listening Score` field.
	* Enter your student's `Reading Score` in the `Reading Score` field.
	* Enter your student's `Writing Score` in the `Writing Score` field.
4. Calculate the overall score:
	* The `Overall Score` field is automatically calculated based on the scores above.
5. Choose the prediction method:
	* Select whether the score prediction was made manually or automatically from the `Prediction Method` field.
6. Add any comments or attachments (optional):
	* You can add any comments related to the test or upload an attachment in the `Comments` field.
	* You can also attach any relevant documents or files in the `Attachment` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (required): Enter your student's name as it appears on their identification.
* **Student ID** (required): Enter your student's ID number as it appears on their ID card.
* **Test Date** (required): Enter the date your student took the IELTS test.
* **Test Center** (required): Enter the name of the test center where the test was taken.
* **Band Score** (required): Enter your student's Band Score from the IELTS test.
* **Speaking Score** (required): Enter your student's Speaking Score from the IELTS test.
* **Listening Score** (required): Enter your student's Listening Score from the IELTS test.
* **Reading Score** (required): Enter your student's Reading Score from the IELTS test.
* **Writing Score** (required): Enter your student's Writing Score from the IELTS test.
* **Overall Score** (required): This field is automatically calculated based on the scores above.
* **Predicted Score** (required): This field is automatically generated based on the scores above.
* **Prediction Method** (required): Select whether the score prediction was made manually or automatically.
* **Comments** (optional): Add any comments related to the test.
* **Attachment** (optional): Attach any relevant documents or files.

Please note that this form requires accurate information for accurate predictions. Make sure to fill out the form with correct information.
