# Principal-Interview-Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Principal-Interview-Form is designed to gather information from school administrators or educators for research, assessment, or other purposes. It is an adaptation of a public survey topic and is intended to be used as a template for collecting information about principals or head teachers in educational settings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
2. Enter your answers in the text fields provided on each page.
3. Make sure to fill out all the required fields, which are indicated by a red asterisk (\*) on the label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Title** (`title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief title or label for the form. This will help identify the form and its purpose.
- **Page 1** (`principal_interview_form_page_1`, `text`, required/optional): This is the first page of the form. Please enter any relevant information about the principal or head teacher.
- **Page 2** (`principal_interview_form_page_2`, `text`, required/optional): This is the second page of the form. Please enter any additional information about the principal or head teacher.
- **Page 3** (`principal_interview_form_page_3`, `text`, required/optional): This is the third page of the form. Please enter any more details about the principal or head teacher.
- **Page 4** (`principal_interview_form_page_4`, `text`, required/optional): This is the fourth page of the form. Please enter any further information about the principal or head teacher.
- **Page 5** (`principal_interview_form_page_5`, `text`, required/optional): This is the fifth page of the form. Please enter any final details about the principal or head teacher.

## Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Make sure to ask for clarification if you're unsure about any question or field.
