# Online Class Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about online classes, including their titles, descriptions, categories, schedules, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields for your online class.
2. Select relevant options for each field as needed.
3. Review your entries carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Online Class Title** (1): Enter the title of your online class.
* **Description** (2): Enter a brief description of your online class.
* **Category** (3): Select one or more categories for your online class (e.g. Art, Science, Math, History, Language).
* **Start Time** (4): Enter the start time of your online class.
* **End Time** (5): Enter the end time of your online class.
* **Duration** (6): Select the duration of your online class (e.g. 1 hour, 2 hours, 3 hours, 4 hours, 5 hours).
* **Instructor Name** (7): Enter the name of your online class instructor.
* **Price** (8): Enter the price of your online class.
* **Schedule** (9): Select a schedule for your online class (e.g. Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday).
* **Level** (10): Select the level of your online class (e.g. Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
* **Format** (11): Select the format of your online class (e.g. Online, Offline, Hybrid).
* **Capacity** (12): Enter the maximum number of students your online class can accommodate.
* **Language** (13): Enter the language of your online class.
* **Level Requirement** (14): Select the level requirement for your online class (e.g. High School Diploma, Bachelor's Degree, Master's Degree, Associate's Degree, PhD).
* **Start Date** (15): Enter the start date of your online class.
* **End Date** (16): Enter the end date of your online class.
* **Status** (17): Select the status of your online class (e.g. Open, Closed, Pending).
* **Tags** (18): Select one or more tags for your online class.
* **URL** (19): Enter a URL for your online class.
* **Image** (20): Enter an image URL for your online class.
* **Video** (21): Enter a video URL for your online class.
* **Description** (22): Enter a detailed description of your online class.
* **Instructor** (23): Enter the name of your online class instructor.
* **Enrollment** (24): Select the enrollment method for your online class (e.g. Self-Enrollment, Instructed).
* **Enrollment Status** (25): Select the enrollment status of your online class (e.g. Active, Inactive).

# Tips

* Ensure that you fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* Use relevant categories and tags to help users find your online class.
* Be specific and detailed in your descriptions and titles to attract users.
* Use clear and concise language in your field entries.
* Review your entries carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
