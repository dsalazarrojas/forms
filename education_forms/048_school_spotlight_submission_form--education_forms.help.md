# School Spotlight Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Spotlight Submission Form is used to collect information from schools about themselves. This form is intended to provide a platform for schools to showcase their unique features, achievements, and interests.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of your school in the "School Name" field (page 1).
2. Provide the website URL of your school in the "School Website" field (page 2).
3. Enter your school's address in the "School Address" field (page 3).
4. Select the type of school you represent (e.g., "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3") from the "School Type" field (page 4).
5. Share a story about your school in the "School Story" field (page 5).
6. Describe your school's achievements, if any, in the "School Achievement" field (page 6).
7. Share a link to a video or photo of your school in the "School Media" field (page 7).
8. Enter your school's contact email in the "School Email" field (page 8).
9. Enter your school's contact phone number in the "School Phone" field (page 9).
10. Select one or more categories that best describe your school from the "School Category" field (page 10).
11. Finally, enter any additional information in the "School Other" field (page 11).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name** (page 1): Enter the name of your school.
* **School Website** (page 2): Provide the URL of your school's website.
* **School Address** (page 3): Enter your school's physical address.
* **School Type** (page 4): Select the type of school you represent (e.g., "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3").
* **School Story** (page 5): Share a story about your school that highlights its unique features.
* **School Achievement** (page 6): Describe any notable achievements or accomplishments of your school.
* **School Media** (page 7): Share a link to a video or photo of your school.
* **School Email** (page 8): Enter your school's contact email address.
* **School Phone** (page 9): Enter your school's contact phone number.
* **School Category** (page 10): Select one or more categories that best describe your school.
* **School Other** (page 11): Enter any additional information about your school.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled accurately and consistently to avoid errors.
* If you encounter any issues or have questions, contact us for assistance.
* You can submit your School Spotlight at any time, and we will review it for approval.
* Note that we may contact you for additional information or clarification before publishing your School Spotlight.
* You can also check our public-facing platform to see how your school's information will be displayed.
