# Exam Difficulty Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Exam Difficulty Feedback Form" is used to collect information about the difficulty level of an exam. This form is designed to help instructors understand how challenging their exams are for students and identify areas for improvement. It's essential to provide honest and detailed feedback to help shape future exam questions and improve student outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the difficulty level of the exam from the options provided.
2. Choose the type of question(s) that you found most challenging.
3. Enter any additional comments or feedback about the exam.
4. Provide your contact information so we can reach out to you for further discussion.
5. Submit your feedback by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How difficult was the exam** (`exam_difficulty`): 
  This field asks about the overall difficulty level of the exam. Select from the options: Very easy, Somewhat easy, Neutral, Somewhat difficult, Very difficult.
* **What type of question were you given during the exam?** (`question_type`): 
  This field asks about the type of questions you encountered during the exam. Select from the options: Multiple Choice, Short Answer, Essay Question, True/False, Fill in the Blank.
* **How long did the exam last?** (`exam_duration`): 
  This field asks for the duration of the exam in minutes.
* **How many questions were on the exam?** (`question_count`): 
  This field asks for the number of questions on the exam.
* **How often do you usually take exams like this one?** (`exam_difficulty_2`): 
  This field asks about your usual exam frequency.
* **What type of question did you have the most trouble with?** (`question_type_2`): 
  This field asks about the most challenging question type.
* **How long did you usually have to prepare for this type of exam?** (`exam_duration_2`): 
  This field asks for the usual preparation time in days.
* **Do you have any other comments about the exam?** (`exam_comments`): 
  This field is for any additional feedback or comments about the exam.
* **How can we contact you?** (`contact_info`): 
  This field is for your contact information, in case we need to reach out for further discussion.
* **Would you like to be contacted to review your answers?** (`review`): 
  This field asks whether you'd like to be contacted for review.
* **What is your name?** (`name`): 
  This field is for your name.
* **What is the name of the course this exam was for?** (`course_name`): 
  This field is for the course name.
* **What is the name of your school?** (`school_name`): 
  This field is for the school name.
* **What is the name of the exam?** (`exam_name`): 
  This field is for the exam name.
* **What date did you take the exam?** (`exam_date`): 
  This field asks for the date of the exam.
* **What time did you start the exam?** (`exam_start_time`): 
  This field asks for the start time of the exam.
* **What time did you finish the exam?** (`exam_end_time`): 
  This field asks for the finish time of the exam.
* **Where did you take the exam?** (`exam_location`): 
  This field is for the exam location.
* **What is your review message?** (`review_message`): 
  This field is for any additional feedback or comments about the exam.
* **What date is the exam in the future?** (`exam_date_2`): 
  This field asks for the future exam date.
* **What is your difficulty scale for the exam?** (`exam_difficulty_scale`): 
  This field asks for the difficulty level of the exam on a scale of 1-10.
* **What is your difficulty scale for the course?** (`course_difficulty_scale`): 
  This field asks for the difficulty level of the course on a scale of 1-10.

Note: This form is designed to be filled out by students after completing an exam. If you have any questions or need help, feel free to ask!
