# Scholarship Renewal Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The **Scholarship Renewal Declaration Form** is designed for students or applicants to declare their intention to renew an existing scholarship. This form helps the financial aid office understand the reasons and requirements for renewal, ensuring a smooth and efficient process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the form completely and accurately with the requested information.
2. Make sure all required fields are completed.
3. Review your submission before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **scholarship_renewal_declaration_form** (1, note, required: false)
	+ Please briefly describe the reasons for your scholarship renewal request. This field is not required but is helpful for the financial aid office to understand your reasons.
* **education_institution** (2, text, required: false)
	+ Enter the name of the education institution you are currently attending or have attended. This could be a university, college, or school name.
* **financial_aid_office** (3, text, required: false)
	+ Mention the name and contact information of the financial aid office you are using for your scholarship. This could be a department name and email address.
* **student_declaration** (4, text, required: false)
	+ Write a declaration stating your intention to renew your scholarship. Please make this clear, concise, and accurate.

## Tips

- Make sure to proofread your submission before sending it to the financial aid office.
- If you are unsure about any part of the form, contact the financial aid office for assistance.
- If you have any supporting documents or attachments, you can include them with your form submission.
