# Permission To Walk Home Unattended Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain permission for students to walk home unattended from school.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter the event or activity that the student needs to go home for, in the "Event" field.
3. Choose the correct answer for "Permission" to indicate if the student is allowed to walk home unattended.
4. Enter the date and time you expect the student to go home.
5. Provide your contact information, including your phone number and email address.
6. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
7. Optionally, select the tool or equipment that will be assigned to the student, if necessary.
8. Enter a category for the form, if applicable.
9. Enter a form ID, if required.
10. Finally, select the output file format, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Name**: Enter the parent/guardian's name who is granting permission.
    (Text field)
* **Student Name**: Enter the student's name who needs to walk home unattended.
    (Text field)
* **Event**: Choose the reason for the student to walk home, such as School Event or Field Trip.
    (Select one from the options)
* **Permission**: Indicate if the student is allowed to walk home unattended. Choose 'Yes' or 'No'.
    (Select multiple options)
* **Date**: Enter the date when the student expects to go home.
    (Date field)
* **Time**: Enter the time when the student expects to go home.
    (Time field)
* **Contact Phone**: Provide your contact phone number for emergencies.
    (Text field)
* **Contact Email**: Provide your contact email address.
    (Email field)
* **Note**: Add any additional comments or notes about the form.
    (Note field)
* **Tool**: If applicable, select the tool or equipment assigned to the student.
    (Text field)
* **Category**: Enter a category for the form, if required.
    (Text field)
* **Form ID**: Enter a unique ID for the form, if necessary.
    (Number field)
* **Output File**: Select the output file format, if needed.
    (Text field)
