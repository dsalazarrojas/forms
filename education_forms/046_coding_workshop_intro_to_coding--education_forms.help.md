# Coding Workshop Intro To Coding - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for students or parents/guardians to provide information about the student and their interest in participating in the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the student information section, including the student's first name, last name, date of birth, grade level, and parent/guardian name and contact information.
2. Select the student's preferred workshop session.
3. Choose the duration of the workshop.
4. Indicate the student's prior programming experience and the programming languages they are familiar with.
5. State the student's learning goals and interests.
6. Confirm the student's computer and internet access.
7. Provide consent for the workshop and permission to use photos or recordings.
8. Choose the start date of the workshop.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student First Name** (`student_first_name`, `text`, required): Enter the student's first name.
* **Student Last Name** (`student_last_name`, `text`, required): Enter the student's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter the student's date of birth.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose the student's current grade level (Elementary, Middle School, High School, College, or Adult).
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, required): Enter the parent/guardian's name.
* **Parent or Guardian Email** (`parent_email`, `email`, required): Enter the parent/guardian's email.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone Number** (`parent_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the parent/guardian's phone number.
* **Workshop Name** (`workshop_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the workshop (Intro to Coding).
* **Preferred Workshop Session** (`workshop_session`, `select_one`, required): Choose the student's preferred workshop session (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Weekend).
* **Preferred Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required): Choose the preferred start date of the workshop.
* **Workshop Duration** (`duration`, `select_one`, required): Choose the duration of the workshop (4 weeks, 8 weeks, 12 weeks, or 16 weeks).
* **Prior Programming Experience** (`prior_experience`, `select_one`, required): Indicate the student's prior programming experience (No experience, Some experience, Moderate experience, or Advanced experience).
* **Programming Languages Familiar With** (`programming_languages`, `text`, optional): List the programming languages the student is familiar with.
* **Learning Goals and Interests** (`learning_goals`, `text`, required): State the student's learning goals and interests.
* **Computer Access** (`computer_access`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether the student has access to a computer.
* **Internet Access** (`internet_access`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether the student has access to the internet.
* **I Consent** (`parent_consent`, `select_one`, required): Provide consent for the student's participation in the workshop.
* **Permission to Use Photos or Recordings** (`photo_permission`, `select_one`, required): Provide permission to use photos or recordings of the student.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of registration.

## Tips
Please ensure you fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
