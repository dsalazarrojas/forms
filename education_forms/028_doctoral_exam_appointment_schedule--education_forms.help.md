# Doctoral Exam Appointment Schedule - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about doctoral exam appointments, allowing students, faculty, and staff to schedule and manage exam sessions. The form is used to create and manage exam schedules, ensuring that exam dates, times, and types are accurately recorded and easily accessible.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date for the exam appointment.
2. Enter the name of the student taking the exam.
3. Choose the selected time slots for the exam appointment.
4. Enter the name of the faculty member conducting the exam.
5. Specify the duration of the exam.
6. Choose the type of exam being held.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Exam Appointment Date (1)
* **Exam Appointment Date** (`exam_appointment_date`, `date`, required/optional: not required): Enter the date of the exam appointment in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

### Student Name (2)
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required/optional: not required): Enter the name of the student taking the exam.

### Selected Time Slots (3)
* **Selected Time Slots** (`selected_time_slots`, `select_multiple`, required/optional: not required): Choose up to three time slots for the exam appointment from the available options: Option 1, Option 2, and Option 3.

### Faculty Name (4)
* **Faculty Name** (`faculty_name`, `text`, required/optional: not required): Enter the name of the faculty member conducting the exam.

### Exam Durations (5)
* **Exam Durations** (`exam_durations`, `number`, required/optional: not required): Specify the duration of the exam, which can be a number representing the exam's length in hours or minutes.

### Exam Type (6)
* **Exam Type** (`exam_type`, `select_one`, required/optional: not required): Select the type of exam being held from the available options: Type A, Type B, and Type C.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information entered to avoid errors.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with the relevant authorities or administrators for clarification.
