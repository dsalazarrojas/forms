# letter_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for students to request a letter, likely from a teacher or professor. It collects information about the student, the nature of the request, and signature details to facilitate the letter's creation and delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your student information in the "Student Info" field.
2. Describe the nature of your letter request in the "What is the nature of your letter request?" field.
3. Specify the target date for the letter's delivery in the "Target Date" field.
4. Enter the date of signing in the "Signature Date" field.
5. Optionally, schedule a specific time for the letter's signing in the "Signature Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Info** (`student_info`, text, optional): Enter your student information.
* **What is the nature of your letter request?** (`question`, text, optional): Describe the reason for your letter request.
* **Target Date** (`target_date`, date, optional): Specify the target date for the letter's delivery.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of signing.
* **Signature Time** (`signature_time`, time, optional): Optionally, schedule a specific time for the letter's signing.

## Tips
Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure efficient processing of your letter request. If you're unsure about any field, refer to the form's description or consult with the form creator.
