# Church's Children Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registration purposes, likely for a church or educational program. It collects demographic information about children, including their name, date of birth, age, and gender. It also collects contact information for the parent or guardian, as well as medical and health information. The form is used to provide a safe and comfortable environment for the child and to set expectations for who can pick up the child.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To begin, fill out the required fields with the child's information, including:
	* Child's Full Name
	* Date of Birth
	* Age
	* Gender
	* Which program(s) the child is registering for
2. Then, fill out the parent/guardian's contact information, including:
	* Parent/Guardian Full Name
	* Parent/Guardian Email
	* Parent/Guardian Phone Number
	* Home Address
3. Next, answer the medical and health questions, including:
	* Does the child have any allergies?
	* Does the child have any medical conditions?
	* Is the child taking any medications?
4. Provide any behavioral considerations or special instructions for caregivers.
5. Grant permission for photo release and acknowledge the pickup policy.
6. Sign and date the form with the parent/guardian's signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Child's Full Name** (`Child's Full Name`, text, required): Fill in the child's full name, including first and last name.
* **Child's Preferred Name** (`Child's Preferred Name`, text, optional): Provide the child's preferred name, if different from their full name.
* **Child's Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, required): Enter the child's date of birth.
* **Current Age** (`Current Age`, number, required): Enter the child's current age.
* **Gender** (`Gender`, select_one, required): Select the child's gender.
* **Current Grade Level** (`Current Grade Level`, select_one, required): Select the child's current grade level.
* **Parent/Guardian Full Name** (`Parent/Guardian Full Name`, text, required): Enter the parent/guardian's full name.
* **Parent/Guardian Email** (`Parent/Guardian Email`, email, required): Enter the parent/guardian's email address.
* **Parent/Guardian Phone Number** (`Parent/Guardian Phone Number`, text, required): Enter the parent/guardian's phone number.
* **Home Address** (`Home Address`, text, required): Enter the child's home address, including street, city, and zip.
* **Emergency Contact Name and Phone** (`Emergency Contact Name and Phone`, text, required): Enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact.
* **Who Is Authorized to Pick Up Your Child** (`Who Is Authorized to Pick Up Your Child`, text, required): List all individuals authorized to pick up the child.
* **Which Program Are You Registering For** (`Which Program Are You Registering For`, select_multiple, required): Select all programs the child is registering for.
* **Does Your Child Have Any Allergies** (`Does Your Child Have Any Allergies`, select_one, required): Answer if the child has any allergies.
* **Please Describe Allergies** (`Please Describe Allergies`, text, optional): Describe the child's allergies if they have any.
* **Does Your Child Have Any Medical Conditions** (`Does Your Child Have Any Medical Conditions`, select_one, required): Answer if the child has any medical conditions.
* **Please Describe Medical Conditions** (`Please Describe Medical Conditions`, text, optional): Describe the child's medical conditions if they have any.
* **Is Your Child Taking Any Medications** (`Is Your Child Taking Any Medications`, select_one, required): Answer if the child is taking any medications.
* **Please List Medications** (`Please List Medications`, text, optional): List the child's medications if they have any.
* **Any Behavioral Considerations** (`Any Behavioral Considerations`, text, optional): Provide any behavioral considerations for the child's caregiver.
* **Special Instructions for Caregivers** (`Special Instructions for Caregivers`, text, optional): Provide any special instructions for the child's caregiver.
* **Photo Release Permission** (`Photo Release Permission`, select_one, required): Grant permission for photos to be taken of the child.
* **Pickup Policy Acknowledgment** (`Pickup Policy Acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Acknowledge the pickup policy for the child.
* **Parent/Guardian Signature** (`Parent/Guardian Signature`, text, required): Sign and date the form with the parent/guardian's signature.
* **Date of Signature** (`Date of Signature`, date, required): Enter the date of signature.
