# Medical Procedure Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Procedure Knowledge Quiz is a form designed to help assess your knowledge of a medical procedure's steps.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the form, answering each question about a medical procedure's steps.
2. Fill out fields 1, 2, 6, 7, 8, and 9 as they are required fields.
3. For field 8, you will be asked for the "Date of Procedure" using a date format.
4. For field 9, you will be asked for the "Time of Procedure" using a time format.
5. Field 7 is not required but can be filled if you have information about the 7th step of the medical procedure.
6. Field 10 is a select multiple field where you need to select all the correct medical procedures that have been performed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What is the first step in a medical procedure?** (`procedure_1`, text, required): The first step of the medical procedure. Please enter your answer in this field.
- **What is the second step in a medical procedure?** (`procedure_2`, text, required): The second step of the medical procedure. Please enter your answer in this field.
- **Date of procedure** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date the procedure was performed in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
- **Time of procedure** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time the procedure was performed in the format `HH:MM:SS`.
- **Medical procedure ID** (`medical_procedure_id`, text, optional): This field is not required but if you have information about the medical procedure ID, please enter it here.
- **What is the 7th step in a medical procedure?** (`procedure_7`, text, optional): This field is optional, if you know the 7th step of the medical procedure, please enter it here.
- **What is the 8th step in a medical procedure?** (`procedure_8`, text, optional): This field is optional, if you know the 8th step of the medical procedure, please enter it here.
- **Date of Procedure** (`date_of_procedure`, date, required): Enter the date the medical procedure was performed in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
- **Time of Procedure** (`time_of_procedure`, time, required): Enter the time the medical procedure was performed in the format `HH:MM:SS`.
- **Medical Procedure IDs** (`medical_procedure_ids`, select_multiple, required): Select all the medical procedures that have been performed.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer each question accurately and truthfully.
- If you are unsure about any step, please enter "Unknown" in the corresponding field.
- If you have any information about the medical procedure that is not asked, feel free to add it in the "Medical Procedure ID" field.
- Please make sure to answer all required fields as they are essential for the assessment.
