# music_reading_proficiency_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The music_reading_proficiency_assessment form is designed to evaluate the music reading proficiency of a student or musician. It assesses their skills in reading musical notation from 3-12 and gauges their confidence level in reading different notation levels. Additionally, it asks about their preferred method of lesson planning, which can help inform instruction. This form helps music educators identify areas of strength and weakness for individual students, and inform their instructional approaches accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the form completely**: Ensure that you answer all required fields.
2. **Select the correct notation skills**: Choose the notation levels you feel most comfortable with from the list.
3. **Choose a lesson planning method**: Select one of the three methods: Student Placement, Lesson Planning, or Progress Tracking.
4. **Enter a note (optional)**: If you have any comments or progress notes about the student, you can provide them in the note field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **notation_skills**: Select up to 3 notation levels you feel most comfortable reading. The options range from Bar 3-4 to 12.
* **confidence**: Choose your level of confidence reading the selected notation levels. Enter a number from 1 (not confident at all) to 10 (extremely confident).
* **lesson_planning**: Choose how you prefer to plan your lessons: do you focus on placing students, planning the lesson content, or tracking their progress?
* **student_progress**: Note any comments or progress notes about the student, if desired.

## Tips
* Be honest about your notation skills and confidence level to provide an accurate assessment.
* Select up to 3 notation levels to reflect your current skill level.
* Choose one lesson planning method that best suits your teaching style.
* Use the note field to provide any relevant comments or progress notes about the student, if desired.
