# Parents Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect permission from parents or guardians for their child to participate in a school trip or activity.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the correct permission status for your child:
    *   Choose "Yes" if you give your permission for your child to participate.
    *   Choose "No" if you do not give your permission.
2.  Fill in your child's details:
    *   Enter your child's name.
3.  Provide your contact information:
    *   Enter your phone number so that the school can contact you.
    *   Enter your email address so that the school can contact you.
4.  Provide any additional comments or notes:
    *   Enter any relevant information that may be important for the school.
5.  Confirm the trip details:
    *   Enter the date you are giving permission for.
    *   Provide a reason for the trip or activity (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Parents Permission Form Label**: This label indicates that this is the permission field where you select whether or not you grant permission for your child to participate.
    *   **Yes/No**: Choose "Yes" to grant permission or "No" if you do not grant permission.
*   **Name of Child**: Enter the name of your child.
*   **Parents Name**: Enter your name as the parent or guardian.
*   **Date of Birth**: Enter your child's date of birth.
*   **Parents Phone**: Enter your phone number so that the school can contact you.
*   **Parents Email**: Enter your email address so that the school can contact you.
*   **Permission Date**: Enter the date you are giving permission for.
*   **Reason for Trip**: Provide a reason for the trip or activity.
*   **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person.
*   **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
*   **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
*   **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes that may be relevant for the school.
