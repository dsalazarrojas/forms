# Program Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Program Satisfaction Survey is a form designed to collect feedback from individuals who have participated in a program offered by our institution.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To get started, review the form carefully and make sure you have all the required information before proceeding.
2. Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.
3. For the select_one question, choose one of the options provided.
4. For the text field, type your answer in the space provided.
5. Make sure to include your contact information if you would like to be contacted for follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Satisfaction**:
	+ A number rating from 1 (low) to 5 (high) to gauge your overall satisfaction with the program.
* **How satisfied are you with our program?**:
	+ A number rating from 1 (low) to 5 (high) to gauge your satisfaction with the program.
* **How satisfied are you with the courses?**:
	+ A number rating from 1 (low) to 5 (high) to gauge your satisfaction with the courses.
* **Communication**:
	+ How would you rate the communication with our team? Choose from the provided options:
		- Excellent
		- Good
		- Neutral
		- Bad
		- Very Bad
* **What would you like to tell us about your experience?**:
	+ A text field for any comments or feedback you would like to share.
* **Email**:
	+ Your email address for follow-up contact.
* **Phone**:
	+ Your phone number for follow-up contact.
* **When were you in the program?**:
	+ Date of participation in the program.
