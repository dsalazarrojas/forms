# Global Education Partnership Endorsement Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request endorsement for a global education partnership. The purpose of this form is to gather information about your organization, its partnership goals, and project specifics. This data will be used to review and potentially endorse your partnership.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out each field carefully and accurately. Each field is labeled below with a description of what information to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name**: Please enter the name of your organization. This will be used to identify your partner in our system.
* **Organization Type**: Please select one of the following options for your organization's type:
	+ Non-Profit: A non-profit organization is a type of organization that operates for a social or charitable purpose rather than to make a profit.
	+ Private: A private organization is a business or individual that is not publicly owned or controlled.
	+ Public: A public organization is a government or government-related entity.
	+ Government: A government agency or department.
* **Global Education Impact**: Please enter a brief description of the impact you hope to achieve with your global education partnership.
* **Organization Address**: This is the physical address of your organization. If you are a virtual organization without a physical address, please enter a mailing address where we can send correspondence.
* **Organization Phone**: This is the main contact phone number for your organization. If you have a different phone number that you prefer to be used, please let us know.
* **Organization Email**: This is the main contact email for your organization. Please use the format [email@domain.com](mailto:email@domain.com).
* **Global Education Focus Areas**: Please select all areas of education your organization is interested in:
	+ Early Childhood Education
	+ Primary Education
	+ Secondary Education
	+ Tertiary Education
	+ Special Education
* **Organization Structure**: Please select one of the following options for your organization's structure:
	+ Clear: Your organization has a clear and defined structure.
	+ Unclear: Your organization's structure is unclear or undefined.
* **Number of Partners**: Please enter the number of partners you are looking to collaborate with.
* **Project Duration**: Please enter the number of years you project the partnership will last.
* **Number of Beneficiaries**: Please enter the number of people you expect to benefit from your partnership.
* **Start Date**: Please enter the date you hope to begin your partnership. Use the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Communication Plan**: Please indicate if you have a plan for regular communication with your partners:
	+ Yes: You have a plan in place.
	+ No: You do not have a plan.
* **Budget**: Please enter the total budget for your partnership.
* **Skills/Expertise**: Please select all areas of expertise your organization has:
	+ Research
	+ Consulting
	+ Teaching
	+ Training
	+ Other
* **Monitoring & Evaluation**: Please indicate if you will be monitoring and evaluating your partnership:
	+ Yes: You will be monitoring and evaluating your partnership.
	+ No: You will not be monitoring and evaluating your partnership.
* **Start Date 2** and **End Date**: These fields are duplicates of the start and end dates entered in **Start Date** and **End Date**, and should not be filled out unless you have multiple start and end dates for your partnership.
* **End Date 2**: This field is a duplicate of the **End Date** field and should not be filled out.

## Tips

* Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly. Incomplete or inaccurate information may delay our review process.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* Please ensure that all fields are accurate and up-to-date before submitting the form.
