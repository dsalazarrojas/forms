# NVC Exercise Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information on Non-Violent Communication (NVC) exercises, a set of tools and practices for effective communication. It is used for educational purposes to gather data and gain insights on the effectiveness of NVC exercises in real-world scenarios.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Answer each question thoroughly, and do not skip any questions.
3. Select the correct category for your response (Education Forms or Other Forms).
4. Choose the assigned tool that best fits your NVC exercise (chatjimmy, chatjim, or Other Tool).
5. Provide a brief description of your NVC exercise.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category**: Select the category that best describes the purpose of your NVC exercise. This can be either Education Forms or Other Forms.
* **Assigned Tool**: Choose the tool that you used or plan to use for your NVC exercise. This can be chatjimmy, chatjim, or Other Tool.
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of your NVC exercise, including any relevant details or context.
* **Question 1**: Answer Question 1, which asks for a brief description of your NVC exercise.
* **Question 2**: Answer Question 2, which asks for the benefits or goals of your NVC exercise.
* **Question 3**: Answer Question 3, which asks for the challenges or obstacles you faced during your NVC exercise.
* **Question 4**: Answer Question 4, which asks for any lessons or takeaways from your NVC exercise.
* **Question 5**: Answer Question 5, which asks for any recommendations or suggestions for future NVC exercises.
* **Submit**: Once you have completed all the questions, click the Submit button to finalize your response.

## Tips

* Please answer each question thoroughly and honestly.
* If you have any difficulty or need help with the form, contact form@gic.mx.
* If you have any suggestions or feedback for improving the form, please provide it in the form of an NVC exercise description in the description field.
* The form is designed to be used for educational purposes, so please use it responsibly and follow any applicable laws and regulations.
