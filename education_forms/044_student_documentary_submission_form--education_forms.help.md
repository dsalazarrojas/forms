# Student Documentary Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Documentary Submission Form is a tool used by students to submit documentation related to their student status, experiences, or other relevant details. It is designed to gather information and provide an easy-to-use interface for students to input their information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out each field with relevant information as prompted. You can add or remove fields as necessary.
2. Review each field and ensure that you have provided accurate information.
3. Submit your form once you have completed all required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submission Form** (`submission_form`, text, required: false): A free-form text entry for students to submit their thoughts or ideas.
* **Student Info** (`student_info`, text, required: false): A field for students to input their personal details.
* **Documentary Details** (`documentary_details`, text, required: false): A field for students to provide a brief description of their documentation.
* **Review** (`review`, text, required: false): A field for students to review and confirm their submissions.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the options provided to confirm the submission.
* **Multiple Submit** (`submit_multiple`, select_multiple, required: false): Select multiple options to confirm the submission.
* **Document Date** (`document_date`, date, required: false): A field for students to input the date of their documentation.
* **Document Time** (`document_time`, time, required: false): A field for students to input the time of their documentation.
* **Document Email** (`document_email`, email, required: false): A field for students to input their email address.
* **Document Phone** (`document_phone`, text, required: false): A field for students to input their phone number.
* **Document File** (`document_file`, text, required: false): A field for students to attach a file related to their documentation.
* **Document Note** (`document_note`, note, required: false): A field for students to add any additional notes about their documentation.
* **Document Comments** (`document_comments`, text, required: false): A field for students to add any additional comments about their documentation.

## Tips
* Make sure to review each field for accuracy before submitting.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask.
* You can submit multiple attachments or files if necessary.
* Please ensure that your submissions are accurate and complete.
