# enroll_for_an_online_seminar_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about the online seminar you wish to enroll in. To confirm that this is the correct form, please ensure that you are enrolling in an online seminar.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide basic details about the seminar you are enrolling in. This includes the seminar title, date, and time.
2. Enter your name and email address for communication purposes.
3. Select the type of seminar you are enrolling in (Online or In-Person).
4. If your seminar type is not listed, you can provide more information in the "Other" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Seminar Details** (`seminar_details`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of the seminar you are enrolling in.
* **Name** (`name`, text, not required): Please enter your name for enrollment purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address so we can contact you about seminar updates and information.
* **Date** (`date`, date, not required): Enter the date of the seminar you are enrolling in.
* **Time** (`time`, time, not required): Enter the time of the seminar you are enrolling in.
* **Seminar Type** (`seminar_type`, select_one, not required): Choose between Online and In-Person seminars.
* **Other** (`seminar_type_other`, text, not required): If the seminar type is not listed above, provide more information about the seminar in this field.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, not required): Leave any additional comments or information about the seminar in this field.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all the required fields (if any).
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact us for assistance.
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- This form should only be used for online seminars.
