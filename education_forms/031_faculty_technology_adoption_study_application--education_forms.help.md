# Faculty Technology Adoption Study Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Faculty Technology Adoption Study Application is a form designed to gather information from faculty members about their interests, projects, and involvement in various technology initiatives. This form helps to better understand faculty adoption and support needs for future initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your University ID number.
2. Provide your contact email address.
3. Enter your first and last name.
4. Specify your faculty title.
5. Identify your department or affiliation.
6. Enter your institution's name.
7. Provide your contact phone number.
8. Enter your university's website.
9. Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate your interest in the following:
	* Faculty Interest (general)
	* Technologies Used (e.g., Yes for "Yes" or multiple options for "Multiple selections")
	* Teaching Interest (e.g., describe your teaching interests)
	* Research Interest (e.g., describe your research interests)
	* Innovation Interest (e.g., describe your innovation interests)
10. Describe any Other Projects or initiatives you are involved in.
11. For any projects mentioned, provide a brief description.
12. Enter start and end dates for each project.
13. Identify the location of each project.
14. Select the current status of each project ("Active" or "Inactive").

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **University ID (1)** (`number`, required): Enter your university ID number.
* **Contact Email (2)** (`email`, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **First Name (3)** (`text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (4)** (`text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Faculty Title (5)** (`text`, required): Enter your faculty title.
* **Department (6)** (`text`, required): Identify your department or affiliation.
* **Institution (7)** (`text`, required): Enter your institution's name.
* **Contact Phone (8)** (`text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **University Website (9)** (`text`, required): Enter your university's website address.
* **Faculty Interest (10)** (`select_one`, required): Answer "Yes" to indicate interest.
* **Technologies Used (11)** (`select_multiple`, required): Select multiple "Yes" options for technologies you are using.
* **Teaching Interest (12)** (`text`, required): Describe your teaching interests.
* **Research Interest (13)** (`text`, required): Describe your research interests.
* **Innovation Interest (14)** (`text`, required): Describe your innovation interests.
* **Other Projects (15)** (`text`, required): Describe any other projects or initiatives you are involved in.
* **Project Description (16)** (`text`, required): Provide a brief description of each project mentioned.
* **Project Start Date (17)** (`date`, required): Enter the start date of each project.
* **Project End Date (18)** (`date`, required): Enter the end date of each project.
* **Project Location (19)** (`text`, required): Identify the location of each project.
* **Project Status (20)** (`select_one`, required): Select "Active" or "Inactive" for each project status.
