# Internship Experience Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Internship Experience Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback from participants about their internship experience. It will help organizations evaluate the quality of their internship programs and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please read each question carefully and answer according to your experience. You may refer to your memory or your notes from the internship. If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Experience** (`first_experience`, text, required): Please describe your first experience in the internship, including any notable events, achievements, or challenges you faced.
* **Second Experience** (`second_experience`, text, required): Describe your second experience in the internship, including any notable events, achievements, or challenges you faced.
* **Improvements** (`improvements`, text, required): Provide specific ways the internship program could be improved. For example, suggest changes to the program structure, content, or logistics.
* **Recommendation** (`recommendation`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you would recommend this internship experience to a friend. Choose from the options: "Yes, I recommend it" or "No, I do not recommend it".
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required): Rate your overall experience in the internship on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **Internship Type** (`internship_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of internship you participated in, such as "Summer Internship", "Co-op Program", "Part-time Internship", or "Full-time Internship".
* **Company** (`company`, text, required): Provide the name of the company where you did the internship.
* **Supervisor** (`supervisor`, text, required): Identify your supervisor or the person you reported to during the internship.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Specify the location where the internship took place.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Indicate the job title you held during the internship.
* **Duration** (`duration`, time, required): Describe the duration of the internship, including the start and end dates, and any notable time off or interruptions.
* **Hours Worked** (`hours`, number, required): Provide the total number of hours you worked in the internship.
* **Rating 2** (`rating_2`, number, required): Rate your overall experience in the internship again on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being "Poor" and 5 being "Excellent". 

Note: This form is designed to be completed by participants who have had an internship experience and is not intended to be used by participants who have not had such an experience. Please do not complete this form if you have not had an internship experience. 

Please refer back to your notes or memories for details about your experiences, and answer the questions as accurately as possible. If you are unsure about any question, please contact the administrator for clarification.
