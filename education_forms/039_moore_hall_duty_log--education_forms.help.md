# moore_hall_duty_log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect data from individuals who interact with students in Moore Hall. This data is likely used for administrative or research purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the student's name in the **student_name** field.
2. Answer the **prophets and sages** questions, selecting all that apply.
3. Select the **goals and outcomes** that align with the student's progress.
4. Enter the date of the **prophet** interaction in the **date_prophets** field.
5. Enter the time of the **prophet** interaction in the **time_prophets** field.
6. Provide any additional **comments** about the **prophet** interaction.
7. Enter the contact information of the **photographer** or **scribe** who interacted with the student, including their email and phone number.
8. Enter any comments or notes about the **scribe** interaction.
9. If applicable, enter any additional comments or notes for the **prophets and sages** interaction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **student_name** (text, required): The student's name.
* **prophets and sages** (select multiple, optional): Check all that apply for prophets and sages who interacted with the student.
* **goals and outcomes** (select one, optional): Select the goals and outcomes that align with the student's progress.
* **date_prophets** (date, optional): Enter the date of the prophet interaction.
* **time_prophets** (time, optional): Enter the time of the prophet interaction.
* **comments_prophet** (note, optional): Provide additional comments about the prophet interaction.
* **email_prophet** (email, optional): Enter the email of the individual who interacted with the student as a prophet or scribe.
* **phone_prophet** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the individual who interacted with the student as a prophet or scribe.
* **prophets and sages comments** (text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the prophets and sages interaction.
* **email_photographer** (email, optional): Enter the email of the photographer who interacted with the student.
* **phone_photographer** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the photographer who interacted with the student.
* **email_scribe** (email, optional): Enter the email of the scribe who interacted with the student.
* **phone_scribe** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the scribe who interacted with the student.
* **date_scribe** (date, optional): Enter the date of the scribe interaction.
* **time_scribe** (time, optional): Enter the time of the scribe interaction.
* **comments_scribe** (note, optional): Provide additional comments or notes about the scribe interaction.
* **email_prophets and sages comments** (email, optional): Enter the email for any comments or notes about the prophets and sages interaction.
* **phone_prophets and sages comments** (text, optional): Enter the phone number for any comments or notes about the prophets and sages interaction.
* **date_prophets and scribes** (date, optional): Enter the date of the prophets and sages interaction.
* **prophets and scribes comments** (text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes for the prophets and sages interaction.
