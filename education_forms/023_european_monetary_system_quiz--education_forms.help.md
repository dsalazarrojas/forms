# European Monetary System Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The European Monetary System Quiz is designed to assess your understanding of the European Monetary System. The quiz will present you with a series of questions, and you will be asked to provide your details and answers. This will help us evaluate your knowledge and provide you with a score based on your performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Read the introduction to understand the context of the quiz.
2. Provide your participant details in the "Participant Details" field.
3. Answer the quiz questions in the "Quiz Questions" field.
4. Review your answers in the "Review" field.
5. Submit your form to get your score.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction (Note)**: This is a brief introduction to the quiz. Please read it carefully to understand the context.
* **Participant Details (Text)**: Please provide your details, including your name and any other relevant information.
* **Quiz Questions (Select Multiple)**: Answer the quiz questions by selecting the options that best fit your knowledge.
* **Quiz Score (Number)**: This is a calculated field that will display your score based on your answers.
* **Participant Answers (Select One)**: This field is to record your final answer to the last question.
* **Review (Select Multiple)**: Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure they are accurate.
 
## Tips
- Make sure to answer the questions based on your understanding of the European Monetary System.
- Review your answers carefully to avoid any mistakes.
- If you are unsure about any question, you can select "Other" in the "Participant Answers" field.
