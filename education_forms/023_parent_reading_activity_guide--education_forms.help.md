# Parent Reading Activity Guide - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for parents to record and track their reading activities with their children, including frequency, duration, and favorite books/themes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information for each section. You can save and submit the form when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, Optional): Enter the name of the parent completing the form.
* **Parent Reading Level** (`parent_reading_level`, select_one, Optional): Select the reading level that best describes your reading ability.
* **Caregiver Name** (`caregiver_name`, text, Optional): Enter the name of the person caring for your child.
* **Caregiver Reading Level** (`caregiver_reading_level`, select_one, Optional): Select the reading level that best describes their reading ability.
* **Reading Frequency** (`reading_frequency`, text, Optional): Describe how often you read with your child (e.g., once a week, daily, sporadically).
* **Reading Duration** (`reading_duration`, text, Optional): Enter the typical duration of your reading sessions.
* **Favorite Book** (`favorite_book`, text, Optional): List a favorite book that you've read with your child.
* **Favorite Character** (`favorite_character`, text, Optional): Identify a favorite character from the books you've read together.
* **Favorite Theme** (`favorite_theme`, select_multiple, Optional): Select the themes that you and your child enjoy most (e.g., Fantasy, Science, Adventure, Mystery, Romance).
* **Favorite Series** (`favorite_series`, text, Optional): Mention a favorite book series that you've read together.
* **Caregiver Feedback** (`caregiver_feedback`, text, Optional): Share any thoughts or comments from the caregiver.
* **Parent Feedback** (`parent_feedback`, text, Optional): Provide feedback or thoughts about the form or the reading sessions.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, Optional): Enter the date you submitted the form.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, Optional): Record the time you submitted the form.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, Optional): Identify the person submitting the form.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, Optional): Add any additional comments or thoughts about the form or the reading sessions.

Tips:
- Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
- Keep the form for future reference and use it as a tool to track your reading habits and preferences.
- Use the form to identify themes and books that you and your child enjoy most.
- Share the form with your caregiver for their feedback and insights.
