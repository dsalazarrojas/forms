# Accreditation Completion Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Accreditation Completion Form is used to collect information about an institution's accreditation status. This form helps the institution provide accurate and up-to-date information about their accreditation body, type, and period, as well as the standards met during the review process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of your institution in the **Institution Name** field.
2. Select the **Accreditation Body** that is responsible for accrediting your institution.
3. Choose the **Accreditation Type** that applies to your institution (Initial, Renewal, or Special Review).
4. Enter the start and end dates of the **Accreditation Period**.
5. In the **Standards Met** field, select all the standards that were verified during this review.
6. In the **Summary of Compliance Evidence** field, provide a brief description of how the requirements were met.
7. In the **Status Recommendation** field, select the final status of your institution's accreditation (Full Accreditation, Provisional Accreditation, or Denied).
8. Enter the name of the authorized official certifying this submission.
9. Finally, select the **Date of Submission**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Institution Name** (Required): Enter the name of your institution. This will help us identify your institution correctly.
* **Accreditation Body** (Required): Enter the name of the body that accredits your institution. For example, "Regional Accreditation Agency".
* **Accreditation Type** (Required): Select the type of accreditation your institution has (Initial, Renewal, or Special Review).
* **Start of Accreditation Period** (Required): Enter the start date of your institution's accreditation period.
* **End of Accreditation Period** (Required): Enter the end date of your institution's accreditation period.
* **-- Requirement Verification --** (Note): This section is a divider to separate the requirements from the standards met.
* **Standards Met** (Required): Select all the standards that were verified during this review.
* **Summary of Compliance Evidence** (Required): Provide a brief description of how the requirements were met.
* **Status Recommendation** (Required): Select the final status of your institution's accreditation (Full Accreditation, Provisional Accreditation, or Denied).
* **Authorized Official Name** (Required): Enter the name of the authorized official certifying this submission.
* **Date of Submission** (Required): Select the date when this submission was made.
