# Virtual School Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in joining a virtual school or online educational institution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student details in the "Student Details" section.
2. Specify your enrollment preferences in the "Enrollment Preferences" section.
3. Answer any additional questions you may have in the "Additional Questions" section.
4. Review and submit your application in the "Review and Submit" section.
5. Read and agree to the "Terms and Conditions" section.
6. Finally, submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Details** (Page 1): This is the first page of the form, where you will fill in your name, email, and other relevant details.
* **Student Details** (Field): This section is required and should be filled in with accurate and up-to-date information about yourself.
* **Enrollment Preferences** (Page 2): Here, you can specify your preferred course, study mode, and any other relevant details about your enrollment.
* **Enrollment Preferences** (Field): This section is optional and should be filled in with details that are accurate and relevant to your interests.
* **Additional Questions** (Page 3): This section allows you to ask any additional questions you may have about the virtual school or online educational institution.
* **Additional Questions** (Field): This section is optional and should be filled in with questions that are relevant and clear.
* **Review and Submit** (Page 4): Here, you will review your application and submit it once you are satisfied with the information you have provided.
* **Review and Submit** (Field): This section is optional and should be used to review your application before submitting it.
* **Terms and Conditions** (Page 5): This section outlines the terms and conditions of your application and enrollment in the virtual school or online educational institution.
* **Terms and Conditions** (Field): This section is required and should be read and agreed upon before submitting your application.
* **Contact Info** (Page 6): This is the final page of the form, where you can provide your contact information so that the virtual school or online educational institution can reach out to you.
* **Contact Info** (Field): This section is optional and should be filled in with your current contact information.
* **Submit** (Page 7): This is the final step in the application process, where you will submit your application once you have completed all the previous sections.
* **Submit** (Field): This section is optional and should be used to submit your application once you have reviewed and are satisfied with the information you have provided.
