# virtual_exchange_student_story_consent_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "virtual_exchange_student_story_consent_form" is a form used to collect information and obtain consent from students, parents, and teachers for a virtual exchange program. This form is designed to gather necessary details, including student and teacher information, permissions, and signatures, to facilitate a smooth and secure exchange experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the student information (page 1).
* Provide story information (page 2).
* Answer the parent and teacher permission questions (page 3).
* Share your story and teacher's story (page 4).
* Sign and date the form with your parent's signature (page 5).
* Sign and date the form with your teacher's signature (page 6).
* Record the date and time of submission (page 7).
* Enter your email address and phone number (page 8).
* Add any additional notes (page 9).
* Enter your education information (page 10).
* Sign and date the form with your student signature (page 11).
* Sign and date the form with your teacher's signature (page 12).
* Enter your student's name and grade level (page 13).
* Enter your school name and school year (page 14).
* Enter your teacher's name, grade level, and school name (page 15).
* Enter your teacher's school year (page 16).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Info** (`1`, `text`, required: false): This section is for the student to provide basic information about themselves.
* **Story Info** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Enter your story or experience related to the virtual exchange program.
* **Parent Permission** (`3`, `select_one`, required: false): Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you have parental permission for the student to participate in the virtual exchange program.
* **Teacher Permission** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you, as a teacher, have permission for the student to participate in the virtual exchange program.
* **Student Story** (`5`, `text`, required: false): Share your story or experience related to the virtual exchange program.
* **Teacher Story** (`6`, `text`, required: false): Share your story or experience as a teacher related to the virtual exchange program.
* **Parent Signature** (`7`, `date`, required: false): Date and sign the form to confirm parental consent.
* **Teacher Signature** (`8`, `date`, required: false): Date and sign the form to confirm teacher consent.
* **Date** (`9`, `date`, required: false): Record the date you submitted the form.
* **Time** (`10`, `time`, required: false): Record the time you submitted the form.
* **Email** (`11`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`12`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Note** (`13`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about the student or the exchange.
* **Education Info** (`14`, `text`, required: false): Enter information about your education background.
* **Student Signature** (`15`, `date`, required: false): Date and sign the form to confirm student consent.
* **Teacher Signature 2** (`16`, `date`, required: false): Date and sign the form to confirm teacher consent.
* **Student Name** (`17`, `text`, required: false): Enter your student's name.
* **Student Grade** (`18`, `text`, required: false): Enter your student's grade level.
* **School** (`19`, `text`, required: false): Enter your school name.
* **School Year** (`20`, `select_one`, required: false): Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you are currently in school.
* **Teacher Name** (`21`, `text`, required: false): Enter your teacher's name.
* **Teacher Grade** (`22`, `text`, required: false): Enter your teacher's grade level.
* **Teacher School** (`23`, `text`, required: false): Enter your school name.
* **Teacher School Year** (`24`, `text`, required: false): Enter your school year.
* **Teacher Position** (`25`, `text`, required: false): Enter your teacher position.

Note that while some fields are marked as required, they may not actually be required due to the form's design and intended use case.
