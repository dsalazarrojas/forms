# Student Award Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a student's award nomination, including their name, school, contact information, and achievements. It is used to provide detailed recommendations for students seeking recognition for their accomplishments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter the student's school in the "Student School" field.
3. Enter the student's email and phone number in their respective fields.
4. Select the nominator's name title (e.g. Mr., Mrs., Dr., Prof.) in the "Nominator Title" field.
5. Enter the nominator's organization and relationship with the student in their respective fields.
6. Choose the award category and type in the respective fields.
7. Enter the nomination reason and justification for the award.
8. Choose the student's grade level (e.g. Elementary, High School, College, University).
9. Enter any additional comments from the nominator in the "Nominator Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Enter the student's name as it appears on their identification card or diploma.
* **Student School**: Enter the student's current or former school.
* **Student Email**: Enter the student's email address.
* **Student Phone**: Enter the student's phone number.
* **Nominator Name**: Enter your name, as the person nominating the student.
* **Nominator School**: Enter your school or organization.
* **Nominator Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Nominator Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Nominator Title**: Select your title (e.g. Mr., Mrs., Dr., Prof.).
* **Nominator Organization**: Check if you are affiliated with an organization.
* **Nominator Relationship**: Describe your relationship with the student (e.g. teacher, mentor, colleague).
* **Student Award**: Enter the award or recognition for which the student is being nominated.
* **Award Category**: Enter the category of the award or recognition.
* **Award Type**: Select the type of award (e.g. Scholarship, Award, Grant, Other).
* **Nomination Reason**: Provide a brief explanation for why the student is being nominated.
* **Award Justification**: Provide additional information to support the nomination.
* **Student Grade Level**: Select the student's grade level (e.g. Elementary, High School, College, University).
* **Nominator Comments**: Enter any additional comments or observations from the nominator.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are only nominating students for awards for which they are eligible.
* Please fill in as much information as possible for each field.
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the above explanations for guidance.
