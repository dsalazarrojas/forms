# Student Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Student Incident Report Form is used to gather information about incidents that occurred during school hours or on school grounds. This form is for students to report incidents such as bullying, accidents, fights, or other incidents that may have occurred while in school.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately to the best of your ability.
2. Ensure that you have reviewed the incident and are reporting it correctly.
3. Answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your full name.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, required/optional): Describe the location where the incident took place.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, required/optional): Provide a detailed description of the incident.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select_one, required/optional): Check 'Yes' if the incident involved bullying, fighting, or other similar incidents.
* **Incident Severity** (`incident_severity`, select_multiple, required/optional): Check 'Yes' if the incident was severe or involved severe injuries.
* **Incident Outcomes** (`incident_outcomes`, text, required/optional): Describe any outcomes or results of the incident.
* **Incident Reported By** (`incident_reported_by`, text, required/optional): Enter the person who reported the incident.
* **Incident Witnesses** (`incident_witnesses`, text, required/optional): List any witnesses who saw or heard the incident.
* **Was student suspended?** (`incident_suspended`, select_one, required/optional): Check 'Yes' if the student involved was suspended due to the incident.
* **Touched during incident** (`incident_touched`, select_one, required/optional): Check 'Yes' if the student involved touched or made physical contact with someone during the incident.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form.
* If you're unsure about any part of the form, please consult with the school administration or student incident report guidelines.
* Remember to review the incident carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and relevance.
