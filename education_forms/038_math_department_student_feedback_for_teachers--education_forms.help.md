# math_department_student_feedback_for_teachers - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow students to provide feedback about the math department and its offerings to teachers and department staff.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Review the form's pages and questions.
2. Answer each question based on your experience in the math department.
3. Use the optional fields to provide supplementary information.
4. Submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Experience** (`Overall Experience`, `select_multiple`, required):
	+ Choose 'Yes' if you had a positive overall experience in the math department.
	+ Choose 'No' if you did not have a positive overall experience in the math department.
* **Course Content** (`Course Content`, `number`, required):
	+ Rate the course content on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Teacher Performance** (`Teacher Performance`, `number`, required):
	+ Rate the teacher's performance on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Feedback** (`Feedback`, `text`, required):
	+ Write your thoughts and comments about the math department and its offerings.
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, `text`, optional):
	+ Provide any additional information you think is relevant to your feedback.
* **Teacher Performance (Again)** (`Teacher Performance (Again)`, `number`, optional):
	+ Rate the teacher's performance on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent. (This is an optional field for students who wish to re-evaluate their initial assessment.)
* **Course Content (Again)** (`Course Content (Again)`, `number`, optional):
	+ Rate the course content on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent. (This is an optional field for students who wish to re-evaluate their initial assessment.)
* **Overall Experience (Again)** (`Overall Experience (Again)`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Choose 'Yes' if you re-evaluate your overall experience in the math department as positive.
	+ Choose 'No' if you re-evaluate your overall experience in the math department as not positive.
