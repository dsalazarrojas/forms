# Student Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Interest Form is a crucial tool for teachers and school administrators to understand each student's unique needs and interests. It helps tailor the educational approach to individual students' needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Student Name**: Enter the student's name as they prefer to be addressed.
2. **Grade Level**: Select the student's current grade level (e.g., 9th, 10th, etc.).
3. **Student Interests**: Select all interests that apply to the student (e.g., sports, music, art, etc.).
4. **Academic Preferences**: Indicate whether the student has any academic preferences or concerns.
5. **Career Goals**: Select all career goals that apply to the student (e.g., engineer, artist, etc.). If no career goals apply, please select "None" or "Unknown".
6. **Hobbies**: Enter any hobbies or activities the student enjoys outside of school.
7. **Extracurricular Activities**: Indicate if the student participates in any extracurricular activities.
8. **Parent Involvement**: Indicate whether parents or guardians are involved in the student's educational decisions.
9. **Additional Comments**: Leave any additional comments or notes about the student's situation or concerns.
10. **Email**: Enter the student's email address (if applicable).
11. **Phone Number**: Enter the student's phone number (if applicable).
12. **School**: Indicate if the student is currently attending school (this is not applicable for students who are not currently enrolled).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter the student's full name as they prefer to be addressed.
* **Grade Level**: Select the student's current grade level, which helps teachers and administrators track academic progress.
* **Student Interests**: Select all interests that apply to the student, such as sports, music, art, or reading.
* **Academic Preferences**: Indicate if the student has any academic preferences or concerns, such as needing extra help or a particular learning style.
* **Career Goals**: Select all career goals that apply to the student, such as engineer, artist, or scientist. If no career goals apply, select "None" or "Unknown".
* **Hobbies**: Enter any hobbies or activities the student enjoys outside of school, such as dancing, playing an instrument, or volunteering.
* **Extracurricular Activities**: Indicate if the student participates in any extracurricular activities, such as sports teams, clubs, or competitions.
* **Parent Involvement**: Indicate if parents or guardians are involved in the student's educational decisions, such as attending meetings or helping with homework.
* **Additional Comments**: Leave any additional comments or notes about the student's situation or concerns, such as medical conditions or learning challenges.
* **Email**: Enter the student's email address (if applicable) for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the student's phone number (if applicable) for communication purposes.
* **School**: This field is not applicable for students who are not currently enrolled; if the student is enrolled, indicate whether they are currently attending school.
