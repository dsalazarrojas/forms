# Institutional Learning Policy Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Institutional Learning Policy Awareness Survey is designed to collect feedback and insights from students and staff on institutional learning policies. This form helps to raise awareness and improve understanding of these policies, which are essential for creating a positive and supportive learning environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. The form is divided into two sections: "Student Answers" and "Staff Survey Form". Choose the section that applies to you (students or staff).
2. Read the instructions for each section carefully.
3. Complete each question accurately and truthfully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Answers** (Student Answers, Text, Optional):
	+ This field is for students to share their thoughts, experiences, and opinions about institutional learning policies. Please provide any relevant information you think will help us understand your perspective.
* **Student Answers** (Student Answers, Text, Optional):
	+ This field is for students to provide any additional comments or feedback on institutional learning policies.
* **Staff Survey Form** (Staff Survey Form, Text, Optional):
	+ This field is for staff members to provide feedback and insights on the effectiveness of institutional learning policies.
	+ Please answer the questions accurately and truthfully to help us improve these policies.
* **Staff Survey Form** (Staff Survey Form, Text, Optional):
	+ This field is for staff members to provide suggestions for future improvements to institutional learning policies.
	+ Please keep your answers concise and relevant.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when completing this form.
* Keep your answers concise and clear.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
