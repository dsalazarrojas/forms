# Student Artwork Submission Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow students to submit their artwork for evaluation or display. It is intended for individual submissions, and it is best to submit each artwork separately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field accurately and completely.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out, even if they are not marked as required.
3. Review your submission carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Artwork** (`student_artwork`, text, not required): Please describe your artwork in this field, including the name of the artwork and any relevant details.
* **Artwork Title** (`artwork_title`, text, not required): Enter a brief title for your artwork.
* **Artwork Description** (`artwork_description`, text, not required): Describe your artwork in detail, including materials used and techniques applied.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of your teacher or art instructor.
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, email, not required): Enter the email address of your teacher or art instructor.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of your school.
* **School URL** (`school_url`, text, not required): Enter the URL of your school website.

## Tips

* Please make sure to submit each artwork separately to avoid confusion in the review process.
* Double-check your submission for accuracy before submitting.
* If you have any questions or issues with the submission process, contact us at form@gic.mx.
