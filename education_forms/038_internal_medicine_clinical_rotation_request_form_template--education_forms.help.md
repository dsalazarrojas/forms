# Internal Medicine Clinical Rotation Request Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to request internal medicine clinical rotations, which are a type of clinical rotation program for medical students or residents to gain hands-on experience in internal medicine. The purpose of this form is to provide a standardized template for requesting and managing internal medicine clinical rotations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Rotation Request Details" field with a brief description of your rotation request.
2. Enter the name of the physician who will be overseeing your rotation in the "Physician Name" field.
3. Select the length of your rotation in the "Rotation Length" field.
4. Enter the start and end dates of your rotation in the "Rotation Start Date" and "Rotation End Date" fields, respectively.
5. Choose the type of rotation you are requesting (Academic, Clinical, or Elective) in the "Rotation Type" field.
6. Enter the name and email of the rotation preceptor (the physician who will be your supervisor) in the "Rotation Preceptor Name" and "Rotation Preceptor Email" fields.
7. Enter the rotation location in the "Rotation Location" field.
8. Enter the name and contact information of the rotation supervisor (the physician who will be overseeing your rotation) in the "Rotation Supervisor Name", "Rotation Supervisor Email", and "Rotation Supervisor Phone" fields.
9. Enter your name and contact information in the "Rotation Requester Name", "Rotation Requester Email", and "Rotation Requester Phone" fields.
10. Select the status of your rotation (Active, Completed, or Cancelled) in the "Rotation Status" field.
11. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Rotation Comment" and "Rotation Additional Comments" fields.
12. Indicate whether you will receive educational credit for the rotation in the "Rotation Educational Credit" field.
13. Select the status of your rotation evaluation (Complete, In Progress, or Not Evaluated) in the "Rotation Evaluation Status" field.

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check the accuracy of your information before submitting the form.
