# Remote Learning Support Volunteer Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Remote Learning Support Volunteer Registration form is designed to collect information from potential volunteers about their availability, willingness to confirm their volunteer information, and terms and conditions of volunteering. This form helps the volunteer registration process by ensuring that volunteers understand their roles and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the User Info section with your name and contact information.
2. Provide your volunteer information, including your experience and skills.
3. Indicate your availability for volunteering.
4. Confirm your volunteer information.
5. Confirm that you have read and accepted the terms and conditions.
6. Submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info** (`User Info`, `text`, required/optional): This section collects your name and contact information.
* **Volunteer Info** (`Volunteer Info`, `text`, required/optional): This section collects information about your volunteering experience and skills.
* **Availability** (`Availability`, `time`, required/optional): This section asks you to select your available time slots for volunteering.
* **Confirm Volunteer Info** (`Confirm Volunteer Info`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This section allows you to confirm your volunteer information, which should match the information you provided in the Volunteer Info section.
* **Additional Info** (`Additional Info`, `note`, required/optional): This section is for any additional information you'd like to provide about yourself.
* **Error Message** (`Error Message`, `text`, required): This section is for any error messages or comments about your submission.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`Terms and Conditions`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This section allows you to confirm that you have read and accepted the terms and conditions of volunteering.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, `select_one`, required/optional): This section allows you to submit your registration.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`Terms and Conditions`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This is the same as above.
* **Submit Button** (`Submit Button`, `select_one`, required/optional): This section is not applicable as it's not a field but a button.
* **Confirm Submission** (`Confirm Submission`, `select_one`, required/optional): This section is for confirming your submission.
* **Submit Confirmation** (`Submit Confirmation`, `select_one`, required/optional): This section is for confirming your submission status.
* **Confirm Submission Status** (`Confirm Submission Status`, `select_one`, required/optional): This section is for confirming your submission status.
* **Submit Status** (`Submit Status`, `select_one`, required/optional): This section is for confirming your submission status.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and correctly.
* If you have any errors or issues with your submission, check the error message section for details.
* Confirm your volunteer information and terms and conditions before submitting your registration.
* Once you submit your registration, you will receive a confirmation of your submission status.
