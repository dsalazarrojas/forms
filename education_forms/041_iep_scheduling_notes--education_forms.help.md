# ie_p_scheduling_notes - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "ie_p_scheduling_notes" form is designed to record and track information about meetings, including meeting titles, attendees, dates, and times. Meeting organizers and administrative staff can use this form to note down essential details about meetings and plan follow-up actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the meeting title in the "meeting_title" field.
2. Choose who will attend or not in the "attendees" field.
3. Record the names and roles of attendees in "attendee_1_name" and "attendee_1_role" fields (repeat for other attendees as needed).
4. Fill in the meeting date and time in the "meeting_date" and "meeting_time" fields.
5. Write any notes about the meeting in the "meeting_notes" field.
6. Record any follow-up actions and their due dates and times in the "follow_up_actions" field (repeat for other actions as needed).
7. Submit the form when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **meeting_title** (Text): Enter the title of the meeting, e.g., "Weekly Team Meeting".
* **attendees** (Select Multiple): Choose who will or will not attend the meeting.
* **attendee_1_name** (Text): Enter the name of the first attendee, e.g., "John Smith".
* **attendee_1_role** (Select One): Choose the role of the first attendee, e.g., "Team Lead".
* **meeting_date** (Date): Enter the date of the meeting, e.g., "2024-03-12".
* **meeting_time** (Time): Enter the time of the meeting, e.g., "14:00".
* **meeting_notes** (Text): Enter any notes about the meeting, e.g., "Discuss project progress".
* **follow_up_actions** (Text): Enter any follow-up actions, e.g., "Send reminders to attendees".
* **follow_up_action_1** (Text): Enter the first follow-up action, e.g., "Send reminders to attendees".
* **follow_up_action_1_due_date** (Date): Enter the due date for the first follow-up action, e.g., "2024-03-15".
* **follow_up_action_1_due_time** (Time): Enter the due time for the first follow-up action, e.g., "16:00".
* **follow_up_action_1_status** (Select One): Choose the status of the first follow-up action, e.g., "Active".
* **submit** (Text): Submit the form when complete.
* **scheduling_notes_id** (Number): This field is not currently used; leave blank.
* **submitted_by** (Text): This field is not currently used; leave blank.
* **submitted_date** (Date): This field is not currently used; leave blank.
* **attendee_0_name** (Text): Enter the name of the first attendee, e.g., "Jane Doe".
* **attendee_0_role** (Select One): Choose the role of the first attendee, e.g., "Project Manager".
* **attendee_0_notes** (Text): Enter any notes about the first attendee, e.g., "Needs to review reports".
* **attendee_0_name_1** (Text): Enter the name of the second attendee, e.g., "Bob Johnson".
* **attendee_0_role_1** (Select One): Choose the role of the second attendee, e.g., "Software Developer".
* **attendee_0_notes_1** (Text): Enter any notes about the second attendee, e.g., "Needs to review code".
