# Clinical Clerkship Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Clinical Clerkship Evaluation Form is a tool used to assess the performance of students during their clinical clerkship. The form is designed to evaluate the student's clinical skills, professionalism, and overall performance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
2. For select_one fields, choose the option that best describes the student's performance.
3. Be as specific and detailed as possible when filling in the text fields (e.g., "Student's key strengths" and "Areas for improvement").

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Preceptor/Supervisor Name**: This field is required and should be filled in with the name of the person evaluating the student.
* **Clinical Specialty**: This field is required and should be filled in with the specialty of the rotation (e.g., Medicine, Surgery, Pediatrics).
* **Evaluation Date**: This field is required and should be filled in with the date of the evaluation.
* **Student Name**: This field is required and should be filled in with the name of the student.
* **Student ID**: This field is required and should be filled in with the student's ID number.
* **Clinical Rotation**: This field is required and should be filled in with the name and duration of the rotation (e.g., "Internal Medicine, 8 weeks").
* **Duration of Rotation**: This field is required and should be filled in with the number of weeks the student spent in the rotation.
* **History and Physical Examination Skills**: This field is required and should be filled in with how the student performed in history and physical examination skills, choose from "Below expectations", "Meets expectations", or "Exceeds expectations".
* **Diagnostic Reasoning and Analysis**: This field is required and should be filled in with how the student performed in diagnostic reasoning and analysis, choose from "Below expectations", "Meets expectations", or "Exceeds expectations".
* **Clinical Judgment**: This field is required and should be filled in with how the student performed in clinical judgment, choose from "Below expectations", "Meets expectations", or "Exceeds expectations".
* **Technical/Procedural Skills**: This field is optional and should be filled in with how the student performed in technical/procedural skills, choose from "Below expectations", "Meets expectations", or "Exceeds expectations".
* **Reliability and Responsibility**: This field is required and should be filled in with how the student performed in reliability and responsibility, choose from "Below expectations", "Meets expectations", "Exceeds expectations".
* **Communication Skills**: This field is required and should be filled in with how the student performed in communication skills, choose from "Below expectations", "Meets expectations", or "Exceeds expectations".
* **Professionalism and Etiquette**: This field is required and should be filled in with how the student performed in professionalism and etiquette, choose from "Below expectations", "Meets expectations", or "Exceeds expectations".
* **Teamwork and Collaboration**: This field is required and should be filled in with how the student performed in teamwork and collaboration, choose from "Below expectations", "Meets expectations", or "Exceeds expectations".
* **Initiative and Self-Directed Learning**: This field is required and should be filled in with how the student performed in initiative and self-directed learning, choose from "Below expectations", "Meets expectations", or "Exceeds expectations".
* **Response to Feedback**: This field is required and should be filled in with how the student responded to feedback, choose from "Below expectations", "Meets expectations", or "Exceeds expectations".
* **Student's Key Strengths**: This field is optional and should be filled in with the student's key strengths.
* **Areas for Improvement**: This field is optional and should be filled in with areas where the student needs improvement.
* **Overall Evaluation**: This field is required and should be filled in with an overall evaluation of the student's performance, choose from "Does not meet expectations", "Meets expectations", or "Exceeds expectations".
