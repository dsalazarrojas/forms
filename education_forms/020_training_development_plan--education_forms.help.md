# Training Development Plan - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will help you complete the Training Development Plan form correctly and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Training Plan Name** (`training-plan-name`, text, optional): Enter a brief title or name for the training plan.
2. **Training Plan Goal** (`Training Plan Goal`, text, optional): Describe the purpose and objectives of the training plan.
3. **Training Plan Duration** (`Training Plan Duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of the training plan in days or hours.
4. **Target Audience** (`Target Audience`, select multiple, optional): Select the internal or external target audience for the training plan.
5. **Start Date** (`Start Date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the training plan.
6. **End Date** (`End Date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the training plan.
7. **Location** (`Location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the training will take place.
8. **Training Plan Status** (`Training Plan Status`, select one, optional): Set the status of the training plan to Active, Inactive, or Draft.
9. **Comments** (`Comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes related to the training plan.
10. **Plan Owners** (`Plan Owners`, select multiple, optional): List the users who own or are responsible for the training plan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Plan Name**: Enter a brief title or name for the training plan.
* **Training Plan Goal**: Describe the purpose and objectives of the training plan.

* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the training plan.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of the training plan.
* **Location**: Enter the location where the training will take place.
* **Training Plan Status**: Set the status of the training plan to Active, Inactive, or Draft.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes related to the training plan.
* **Plan Owners**: List the users who own or are responsible for the training plan.
