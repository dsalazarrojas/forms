# Student Enrollment Approval Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect the necessary information from students who are applying for enrollment in a school or educational program. It is intended to be filled out by the student themselves, and should be completed in a clear and accurate manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Application Information** page, which includes details about yourself as a student.
2. Move on to the **Student Information** page and provide your personal details.
3. If you have any special accommodations, please provide them on the **Student Accommodations** page.
4. If you have any additional information about your parents, please provide it on the **Parent Information** page.
5. Describe your enrollment request on the **Student Enrollment Request** page.
6. Finally, provide any relevant information about the school administration on the **School Administrator Information** page.
7. Once you have filled out all the pages, submit the form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Application Information (page 1)
* **Application Information (application_info)**: This is the first page of the form where you can provide a brief description of your application.
* **Application Information (type: text)**: This is a free-text field where you can enter any relevant information about your application.
* **Application Information (required: false)**: This field is optional, but providing a brief description of your application can help the school administrators to better understand your needs.

### Student Information (page 2)
* **Student Information (student_info)**: This page is where you can provide your personal details, such as your name, contact information, and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Student Information (type: text)**: This is another free-text field where you can enter any information you feel is relevant to your application.
* **Student Information (required: false)**: While not required, providing accurate and up-to-date information is essential for processing your application.

### Student Accommodations (page 3)
* **Student Accommodations (student_accommodations)**: If you need any special accommodations, such as a wheelchair ramp or a quiet room, please provide the details here.
* **Student Accommodations (type: text)**: Describe any special accommodations you may require in this free-text field.
* **Student Accommodations (required: false)**: This field is optional, but providing the information can help the school to better accommodate your needs.

### Parent Information (page 4)
* **Parent Information (parent_info)**: If you have any additional information about your parents or guardians, please provide it here.
* **Parent Information (type: text)**: Enter any relevant information about your parents or guardians in this field.
* **Parent Information (required: false)**: While not required, providing this information can help the school administrators to contact them if needed.

### Student Enrollment Request (page 5)
* **Student Enrollment Request (student_enrollment_request)**: Describe your enrollment request, including the reason why you are applying and what you hope to achieve in the program.
* **Student Enrollment Request (type: text)**: Enter a detailed description of your enrollment request in this field.
* **Student Enrollment Request (required: false)**: Providing this information can help the school administrators to better understand your motivations and goals.

### School Administrator Information (page 6)
* **School Administrator Information (school_admin_info)**: If you have any information about the school administration, such as contact details or relevant policies, please provide it here.
* **School Administrator Information (type: text)**: Enter any relevant information about the school administration in this field.
* **School Administrator Information (required: false)**: While not required, providing this information can help the school administrators to process your application more efficiently.

### Student Enrollment Approval (page 7)
* **Student Enrollment Approval (student_enrollment_approval)**: This is where the school administrator will enter their approval or denial status for your application.
* **Student Enrollment Approval (type: text)**: This field will be filled out by the school administrator once they have reviewed your application.
* **Student Enrollment Approval (required: false)**: This field is not required to be filled out by the user, but rather by the school administrator.
