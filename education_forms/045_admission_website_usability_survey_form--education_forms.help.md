# Admission Website Usability Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the usability of the admission website from users. It aims to understand how well the website meets the needs of its visitors and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely, but do not spend more than 10-15 minutes to complete it.
2. Answer the questions honestly, and provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
3. If you have technical issues or other comments, please describe them in detail.
