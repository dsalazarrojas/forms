# College Survey For High School Students - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help college admissions officers better understand high school students' preferences and priorities when considering colleges. By filling out this form, you will be able to express your thoughts on what matters most to you in a potential college.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Choose your current grade level and expected graduation year from the provided options.
3. Select whether you have visited the college and your experience of it (if applicable).
4. Choose your preferences for the college size, location, and housing options.
5. Indicate the importance of different college attributes, such as academic reputation, program offerings, and affordability.
6. Select your intended major or field of study and whether it is offered at the college.
7. Assess the overall fit of the college with your goals.
8. Express your likelihood of applying to the college.
9. Describe what appeals to you most about the college.
10. If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to share them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **High School Name**: Enter the name of your current high school.
* **Current Grade Level**: Please choose your current grade level from the provided options (e.g., 9th Grade, 10th Grade, etc.).
* **Expected Graduation Year**: Please enter the year you expect to graduate from high school.
* **Have you visited this college**: If you have visited the college, please select the correct option.
* **College Visit Experience**: Select how your experience has been if you've visited the college.
* **College Size Preference**: Choose the size of the college that suits you best.
* **Location Preference**: Select the location of the college that suits you best.
* **Residential vs Commute Preference**: Choose how you'd prefer to live, whether residentially or through commutes.
* **Academic Reputation**: Indicate how important the college's academic reputation is to you.
* **Program Offerings**: Select how important the variety of academic programs offered is to you.
* **Financial Aid Availability**: Choose how important financial aid is in your decision-making process.
* **Student Life and Social Activities**: Select how important student life and social activities are to you.
* **Athletics Importance**: Choose how important the college's athletic programs are to you.
* **Intended Major or Field of Study**: Enter the major or field of study you're interested in.
* **Is your intended major offered at this college?**: Select whether the college offers the major you're interested in.
* **Overall College Fit Assessment**: Assess how well the college fits your goals and aspirations.
* **Likelihood of Applying**: Choose how likely you are to apply to this college.
* **College Strengths**: Describe what appeals to you most about the college.
* **Any Concerns or Questions**: If you have any concerns or questions, share them here.
* **Parent or Guardian Input**: If you'd like to add any additional feedback from your family, please enter it here.
