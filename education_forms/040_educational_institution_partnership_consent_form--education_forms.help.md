# Educational Institution Partnership Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from educational institutions to collaborate on educational programs, share resources, and establish a partnership between our organization and the institution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Representative Details** section with your name and contact information.
2. Enter the name and title of the authorized signatory for the institution in the **Authorized Signatory** section.
3. Provide the institution's details in the **Institution Details** section.
4. Check the **Agreement** box to indicate your acceptance of our terms and conditions.
5. Read and agree to the **Terms and Conditions** in the space provided.
6. Enter your submission date and time in the **Submission** section.
7. Sign the form by entering your date of signature in the **Signature** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Representative Details
* **Representative Details** (text): Enter your name and contact information, including your email address and phone number.

### Authorized Signatory
* **Authorized Signatory** (text): Enter the name and title of the authorized signatory for the institution, as designated by the institution's administration.

### Institution Details
* **Institution Details** (text): Enter the institution's name, address, and other relevant details.

### Agreement
* **Agreement** (number): Check this box to indicate your acceptance of our terms and conditions.

### Terms and Conditions
* **Terms and Conditions** (text): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the partnership. Please note that these terms are non-negotiable and must be accepted in order to establish the partnership.

### Submission
* **Submission** (text): Enter the date and time of submission of this form.

### Signature
* **Signature** (date): Sign the form by entering your date of signature, indicating your acceptance of the terms and conditions.

## Tips
Please ensure that the person filling out this form is an authorized representative of the institution. Also, check that a similar request has not been made or is not in process before submitting this form. This will help us to avoid duplicating effort and ensure that our partnership efforts are organized.
