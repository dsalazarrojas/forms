# Geography Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Geography Quiz form is designed to assess a user's knowledge on geography-related questions. It consists of multiple-choice questions that test their understanding of geography.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the options that you think are correct for each question.
2. Make sure to review each question carefully before submitting your answers.
3. If you're unsure of an answer, you can always ask your teacher or instructor for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Quiz students on geography** (`geography_quiz`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This section asks you to select multiple options that you think are correct answers to a geography-related question.
* **Country** (`country`, `select_one`, required: false): This field asks you to select one option that you think is the correct country.
* **Continent** (`continent`, `text`, required: false): This field asks you to type in the name of a continent. 

## Tips
* Make sure to read each question carefully before selecting your answers.
* If you're unsure of an answer, ask your teacher or instructor for guidance.
* This form is for educational purposes only, so don't worry too much about making mistakes - it's all about learning and improving your geography knowledge!
