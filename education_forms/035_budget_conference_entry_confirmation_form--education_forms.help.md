# Budget Conference Entry Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to confirm and register participants for the Budget Conference. It gathers relevant information such as participant details, conference specifics, and additional requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **Participant Full Name** and **Email Address** accurately as these are mandatory fields.
2. Provide your **Phone Number**, **Organization or Institution**, **Job Title or Position**, and **Conference Name** correctly to help us contact and organize your participation.
3. Choose the correct **Conference Date** and **Conference Location** to ensure we know your conference schedule.
4. Select the **Session or Track of Interest** that you'd like to attend, if applicable.
5. Choose your **Registration Type** (Full Conference, Single Day, Half Day, or Workshop Only) and **Registration Fee Amount** accurately.
6. Select your **Payment Method** from the options provided.
7. Fill in your **Expected Arrival Date** and **Expected Departure Date** to help us plan for your stay.
8. If you have any **Additional Information** or special requests, please include them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Participant Full Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, **Required**)
The **Participant Full Name** field is for your full name as recorded on your registration documents. Please ensure it matches the name on your ID or passport.
### 2. **Email Address** (`participant_email`, `email`, **Required**)
Enter your active and valid email address that we can use for correspondence and notifications.
### 3. **Phone Number** (`participant_phone`, `text`, **Required**)
Provide your active phone number that we can use to reach out in case of emergencies or clarifications.
### 4. **Organization or Institution** (`organization`, `text`, **Required**)
State your organization or institution for our records and to help us organize your participation.
### 5. **Job Title or Position** (`job_title`, `text`, **Required**)
Specify your job title or position to help us understand your role and requirements.
### 6. **Conference Name** (`conference_name`, `text`, **Required**)
Enter the name of the conference you are registering for.
### 7. **Conference Date** (`conference_date`, `date`, **Required**)
Provide the date of the conference for our records and planning.
### 8. **Conference Location** (`conference_location`, `text`, **Required**)
State the city and state of the conference location for our planning purposes.
### 9. **Session or Track of Interest** (`session_or_track`, `text`, Optional)
If you're interested in a specific session or track, please indicate it here.
### 10. **Registration Type** (`registration_type`, `select_one`, **Required**)
Choose your registration type from the provided options (Full Conference, Single Day, Half Day, or Workshop Only).
### 11. **Accommodation Needed** (`accommodation_needed`, `select_one`, **Required**)
Select if you require accommodation (Yes, hotel accommodation) or plan to be a local resident (No, local resident).
### 12. **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, `text`, Optional)
If you have dietary restrictions, please mention them here for conference meal planning.
### 13. **Special Accommodations** (`special_accommodations`, `text`, Optional)
If you need any special accommodations, let us know here.
### 14. **Transportation Assistance Needed** (`transportation`, `select_one`, **Required**)
Choose if you require transportation assistance (Yes, parking needed, Yes, shuttle needed, or No assistance needed).
### 15. **Registration Fee Amount** (`registration_fee`, `number`, **Required**)
Enter the amount you're paying for registration.
### 16. **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, **Required**)
Select your payment method from the options provided.
### 17. **Confirmation Number** (`confirmation_number`, `text`, Optional)
If you've already registered, provide your confirmation number for reference.
### 18. **Expected Arrival Date** (`arrival_date`, `date`, **Required**)
Provide your expected arrival date for accommodation and planning.
### 19. **Expected Departure Date** (`departure_date`, `date`, **Required**)
State your expected departure date for our records and planning.
### 20. **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, `text`, Optional)
If you have any special requests or notes, feel free to include them here.
