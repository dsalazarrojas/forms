# School Registration Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Registration Information Form is designed to collect essential information about students for administrative and registration purposes. This form is used to gather details such as student name, contact information, and demographic data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information for each field.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out, although most fields are not mandatory.
3. Double-check your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name (Student Name)** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Student Email (Student Email)** (`student_contact_email`, email, optional): Provide your valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Parent Contact Name (Parent Contact Name)** (`parent_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person to contact in case of emergencies or important announcements.
* **Parent Email (Parent Email)** (`parent_contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person mentioned above.
* **Address 1 (Address 1)** (`address_1`, text, optional): Enter your current address for registration purposes.
* **Address 2 (Address 2)** (`address_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional address information if necessary.
* **Date Of Birth (Date Of Birth)** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the correct date format (dd/mm/yyyy).

## Tips
* Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Make sure to use a valid date in the correct format for the Date of Birth field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to form@gic.mx for assistance.
