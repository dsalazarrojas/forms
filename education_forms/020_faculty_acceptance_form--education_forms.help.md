# Faculty Acceptance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect faculty member's information for HR purposes, such as tracking job titles, departments, salaries, and job types.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the correct job title from the list of options.
2. Select one or more departments that the faculty member is affiliated with.
3. Enter the start date of the faculty member's job.
4. Enter the end date of the faculty member's job (if applicable).
5. Enter the faculty member's salary.
6. Select the job classification of the faculty member.
7. Select the type of job the faculty member holds (e.g. Tenure-Track, Non-Tenure, Visiting).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information (Text Field)**: Enter the faculty member's name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Job Title (Select One)**: Select the correct job title from the list of options (e.g. Associate Professor, Assistant Professor, etc.).
* **Department (Select Multiple)**: Select one or more departments that the faculty member is affiliated with.
* **Job Start Date (Date Field)**: Enter the date the faculty member started their job.
* **Job End Date (Date Field)**: Enter the date the faculty member's job ended (if applicable).
* **Salary (Number Field)**: Enter the faculty member's salary.
* **Job Classification (Select One)**: Select the job classification of the faculty member (e.g. Faculty, Staff, etc.).
* **Job Type (Select One)**: Select the type of job the faculty member holds (e.g. Tenure-Track, Non-Tenure, etc.).

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled accurately.
* Review the form carefully before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact HR for clarification.
* Note: This form is not meant for submission of new job openings, but rather for updating existing faculty member information.
