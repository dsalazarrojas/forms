# Prefix Mastery Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prefix Mastery Assessment is designed to evaluate the mastery of students in a specific subject area. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please make sure you have a strong understanding of the subject area that this form is intended to assess. 
* Ensure that you haven't already completed this form in the past.
* Start by providing information about yourself, which includes:
	+ **prefix_name**: Enter the prefix that is being assessed. This field is meant to capture the prefix that you are being tested on.
	+ **student_name**: Enter your name in this field. 
	+ **student_email**: Enter your email address. 
	+ **student_school**: Enter the name of your school. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is this prefix?** (`prefix_name`, text, required/optional): This field is meant to capture the prefix that you are being tested on. Please enter the prefix that you are being assessed for.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to enter your name.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address to help us contact you with any further instructions or results.
* **Student School** (`student_school`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your school to associate your assessment results with your institution.

## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your abilities.
* Ensure that all fields are accurate and complete before submitting the form.
* If you need help or have questions, contact us at form@gic.mx.
