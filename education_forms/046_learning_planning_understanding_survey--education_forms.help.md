# learning_planning_understanding_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and insights from students and educators to understand their experiences and perceptions about learning planning and understanding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Student Feedback" section to provide your feedback as a student.
2. In the "Educators" section, provide feedback from educators who have interacted with you.
3. Enter the date you completed the form in the "Date Completed" field.
4. Enter the time you completed the form in the "Time Completed" field.
5. Add any additional comments or insights in the "Comments" field.
6. If you have an email address, enter it in the "Email" field for further communication.
7. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone" field for further contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Feedback** (`student_survey`, text, optional): This section is for students to provide feedback and insights about their experiences with learning planning and understanding.
* **Educators** (`educator_survey`, text, optional): This section is for educators to provide feedback and insights about students' learning experiences.
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, date, optional): Enter the date you completed this form.
* **Time Completed** (`time_completed`, time, optional): Enter the time you completed this form.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or insights about your experiences.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for further communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for further contact.

## Tips
* Please provide as much detail as possible in each field to help us better understand your experiences.
* If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to reach out to us.
* This form is designed to be completed individually, but you can also fill it out on behalf of others if necessary.
* If you have any feedback or suggestions for improvement, please let us know!
