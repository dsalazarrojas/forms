# School Laboratory Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for laboratory supply orders from educational institutions. Please fill out the form with your school's and department's details to place a successful order.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name and contact details (first name, last name, email, school name, department, phone number).
2. Specify the school and department where the order is being placed.
3. Enter the quantity of supplies needed.
4. Enter the order date and time (if desired).
5. Add any additional notes regarding the order.
6. Provide the school's and your own phone number (for contact purposes).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, **optional**): Please enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, **optional**): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, **optional**): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, **optional**): Enter your school's name as it appears on official documents.
* **Department** (`department`, text, **optional**): Please specify the department where the order is being placed (e.g., Biology, Chemistry, etc.).
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, **optional**): Enter the quantity of supplies needed, if applicable.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, **optional**): Enter the date when the order is expected to be delivered or used (if desired).
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, **optional**): Enter the time when the order is expected to be delivered or used (if desired).
* **Order Note** (`order_note`, note, **optional**): Add any additional notes regarding the order (e.g., special instructions or delivery requirements).
* **Order Phone** (`order_phone`, text, **optional**): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, text, **optional**): Enter your school's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Order Date 2** (`order_date2`, date, **optional**): Enter the desired delivery date of the order (if different from Order Date).

Please fill out the form carefully, ensuring all required fields are completed to the best of your ability. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities for assistance.
