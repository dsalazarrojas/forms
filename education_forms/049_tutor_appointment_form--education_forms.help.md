# Tutor Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tutor Appointment Form is designed to collect information from students and tutors for scheduling appointments. This form helps to streamline the process of booking a meeting between students and tutors, ensuring that all necessary details are provided in a clear and concise manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the necessary information. You can find the fields below with corresponding labels and instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the student who is seeking tutoring assistance.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, optional): Provide the email address associated with the student's account.
* **Tutor Name** (`tutor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the tutor who will be providing assistance.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, date, optional): Select the date and time for the scheduled appointment.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, optional): Choose the date for the scheduled appointment. Please note that this field is independent of the appointment time, allowing for flexibility in scheduling.
* **Subject** (`subject`, text, optional): Describe the subject or topic of the tutoring session.
* **Session Length** (`session_length`, number, optional): Specify the duration of the tutoring session. Please use the format "X" to denote the length in hours or minutes.
* **Session Notes** (`session_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the session.
* **Student Tutoring Experience** (`student_tutoring_experience`, select multiple, optional): Choose the level of the student's previous experience with the subject.
* **Tutoring Language** (`tutoring_language`, select one, optional): Select the language in which the tutoring session will be held. Please choose from English, Spanish, French, or Mandarin.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill in all required fields, marked as required.
* Be as detailed as possible when filling in optional fields to ensure accurate scheduling and preparation.
* If you need assistance or clarification, please contact the support team for further guidance.
