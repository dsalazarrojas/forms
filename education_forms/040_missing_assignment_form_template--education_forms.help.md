# Missing Assignment Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Missing Assignment Form Template is designed for students or parents to report a missing assignment or item in an educational setting. This form is intended to gather necessary information to help teachers or school administrators track and resolve the issue efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the question on what is missing, providing a clear and concise description of the assignment or item.
2. Confirm that the parent or guardian is aware of this, by answering the "Parent Confirmation" question, which serves as an acknowledgment.
3. Reconfirm that the parent or guardian is aware of this in the second "Parent Confirmation 2" question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is missing?** (`missing_assignment_form`, Text, Required): Please describe the specific assignment or item that is missing. Be as detailed as possible.
* **Parent Confirmation** (`parent_confirmation`, Text, Required): Confirm that your parent or guardian is aware of this missing assignment or item.
* **Parent Confirmation 2** (`parent_confirmation_2`, Text, Required): Reconfirm that your parent or guardian is aware of this missing assignment or item.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and clear information to ensure that the issue is resolved efficiently.
* If you are unsure about any information, feel free to ask your teacher or school administration for clarification.
* This form is meant to be used for specific instances of missing assignments or items, so please use it accordingly and do not duplicate the effort for the same issue.
