# Open Education Resource Contribution Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form helps contributors provide their consent and details about their Open Education Resources to be shared with the academic community.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question carefully, providing as much information as possible.
2. If you're unsure about any field, you can always contact the form administrators for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **contributor-info** (Text): This is the main section where you can provide any relevant details about yourself as a contributor.
* **contribution-type** (Select One): Choose whether your contribution is a "Contributed Resource" or an "Original Work".
* **contributor-role** (Select Multiple): Select the role(s) you hold related to your contribution, such as "Instructor", "Author", or "Creator". You can choose one or more options.
* **more-info1** (Text): This field is for additional information about your contribution.
* **more-info2** (Text): Another field for more information about your contribution.
* **more-info3** (Text): Yet another field for more information about your contribution.
* **more-info4** to **more-info22** (Text): These additional fields are for any other information you'd like to provide about your contribution.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form carefully and accurately.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx) for help.
* Your contribution will help create a richer and more diverse repository of Open Education Resources. Thank you for your participation!
