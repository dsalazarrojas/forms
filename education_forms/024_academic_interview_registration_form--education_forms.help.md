# Academic Interview Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from candidates for academic interviews, enabling efficient scheduling and logistical management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name for scheduling purposes.
2. Specify the job title or role you are applying for.
3. Provide your contact email for interview details.
4. List your preferred interview dates and times.
5. Choose the preferred format for the interview (in-person, video conference, or phone call).
6. If applicable, provide the names of the interviewers (panel members).
7. If you have any accessibility needs or requirements, describe them in the "Accommodations needed" field.
8. Attach any necessary CVs and supporting documents.
9. Add any additional notes or information for the organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate name** (text, required): Enter your full name for scheduling purposes.
* **Position applied** (text, required): Specify the job title or role you are applying for.
* **Contact email** (email, required): Enter your contact email for interview details.
* **Preferred interview dates** (text, required): List your available dates and times for the interview.
* **Interview mode** (select_one, required): Choose the format you prefer for the interview.
* **Panel members** (text, optional): If you know the names of the interviewers, enter them here.
* **Accommodations needed** (text, optional): Describe any accessibility needs or requirements.
* **CV and documents** (text, optional): Attach necessary CVs and supporting documents.
* **Additional notes** (text, optional): Add any other relevant information for the organizers.

## Tips

* Use your full name for the candidate name field.
* Ensure your contact email is accurate and active.
* Be specific with your preferred interview dates to increase scheduling efficiency.
* If you have a preference for the interview format, select the relevant option.
* For panel members, if you don't know their names, you can leave this field blank.
* In the "Accommodations needed" field, describe any specific needs, such as wheelchair accessibility or language support.
* Attach all relevant CVs and supporting documents related to your application.
