# Pronunciation Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and score on pronunciation. It is intended for users to fill out for themselves or others, potentially for educational purposes such as language learning or teacher evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each field as instructed.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out (none are marked as required in this form).
3. If you're unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General info** (Text, Optional): Enter any relevant information about yourself or the student being evaluated, such as name, language proficiency level, or background.
* **Pronunciation** (Number, Optional): Rate the pronunciation on a scale (though no scale is provided, so you may need to decide on a suitable one for your evaluation).
* **Audio upload** (Text, Optional): If applicable, upload an audio file with the pronunciation to be evaluated.
* **Audio transcription** (Text, Optional): Transcribe or describe the audio file if it cannot be uploaded.
* **Score** (Number, Optional): Assign a score to the pronunciation, likely on a scale.
* **Follow up** (Date, Optional): Schedule a follow-up evaluation or meeting to discuss the feedback.
* **Feedback** (Text, Optional): Provide any general feedback or comments on the pronunciation.
