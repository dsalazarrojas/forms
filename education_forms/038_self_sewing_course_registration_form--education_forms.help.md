# Self Sewing Course Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Self Sewing Course Registration Form is used to register students for the Self Sewing Course. This form is designed to collect the necessary information to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the required information before starting.
2. Enter your name and contact information in the "Student Information" field.
3. Select the course dates you are available to start the course in the "Course Dates" field.
4. Enter the name and contact information of the instructor in the "Instructor" field.
5. Optionally, you can add any additional notes about yourself in the "Student Notes" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "Student Email" field.
7. Enter your phone number in the "Student Phone" field.
8. Review your entire form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Details**: Enter a brief description of the course you are registering for.
* **Student Information (Student Name)**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Student Information (Student Email)**: Enter your valid email address.
* **Student Information (Student Phone)**: Enter your valid phone number.
* **Course Dates**: Select the date you are available to start the course.
* **Instructor**: Enter the name and contact information of the instructor who will be teaching the course.
* **Student Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about yourself.
* **Submit**: Review and submit your completed form.

## Tips

* Ensure you have all the required information before submitting the form.
* Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
* Be honest and accurate when entering your course dates.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the course administrator for assistance.
