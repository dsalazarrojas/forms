## Eureka Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Eureka Admission Form is used to gather information from a student and their parent/guardian during the admission process. This form helps us to understand the student's background and contact details for further communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, you will need to fill out each field carefully and accurately. Please note that some fields are optional, but we encourage you to fill out as much information as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required): Please enter the student's name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, `select_one`, required): Select the student's current grade level from the available options (9, 10, 11).
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter the student's date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YY).
* **Date of Application** (`application_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date on which the application is being submitted.
* **Contact Person (Parent)** (`contact_person`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the parent or guardian who is submitting this application.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the parent or guardian's contact phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter the parent or guardian's email address.
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, `text`, required): Enter the student's permanent address.
* **Parent/Guardian Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, required): Enter the parent or guardian's name.
* **Parent/Guardian Phone** (`parent_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the parent or guardian's phone number.
* **Parent/Guardian Email** (`parent_email`, `email`, required): Enter the parent or guardian's email address.
* **Emergency Contact Person** (`emergency_contact_person`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter the emergency contact person's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter the emergency contact person's email address.
* **Guardian Contact Person** (`guardian_contact_person`, `text`, required): Enter the guardian's name.
* **Guardian Phone** (`guardian_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the guardian's phone number.
* **Guardian Email** (`guardian_email`, `email`, required): Enter the guardian's email address.

Note: Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely. If you are unsure about any field, please ask your contact person for assistance.
