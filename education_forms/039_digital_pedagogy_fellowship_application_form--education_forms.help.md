# Digital Pedagogy Fellowship Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Digital Pedagogy Fellowship Application Form is designed to collect information from applicants to evaluate their qualifications and suitability for the fellowship program. Please read this guide carefully to ensure you complete the form accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each section carefully and fill out the form in the corresponding fields.
2. Ensure that you provide accurate and complete information in each field.
3. If a field is marked as required, make sure to complete it.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, contact form@gic.mx.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Info** (`applicant_info`, text, optional): Provide a brief introduction of yourself, including your name, contact information, and any other relevant details you'd like to share.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your contact information, including your email address, phone number, and any other way we can reach out to you.
* **Education** (`education`, text, optional): List your academic background, including your degrees earned, institutions attended, and any relevant coursework or specializations.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, optional): Describe your professional experience, including your job titles, job responsibilities, and any relevant job achievements.
* **Teaching Experience** (`teaching_experience`, text, optional): Share your teaching experiences, including your teaching positions, courses taught, and any relevant teaching achievements.
* **Professional References** (`professional_references`, text, optional): Provide the names and contact information of your professional references.
* **Upload CV** (`upload_cv`, text, optional): Upload a copy of your CV or resume, which should include your education, work experience, and any other relevant information that showcases your qualifications.
* **Submit** (`submit_form`, text, optional): Review and submit your application form.

## Tips
* Make sure to proofread your application form carefully before submitting it.
* Ensure that you attach a complete and up-to-date CV or resume when uploading it.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact form@gic.mx for assistance.
