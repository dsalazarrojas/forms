# Hand Washing Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information for hand washing training purposes, including hand washing practices, training history, and application for hand washing forms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions according to your hand washing practices and goals.
2. Select the correct options that apply to your hand washing history and training.
3. Specify the form of application, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Education Forms (Required)
* **Page 1 Label** (`education_forms_page_1`, `note`, `required`): This page is a note type field, providing a brief introduction to the form.
### Page 2: First Training Question (Optional)
* **First Training Question** (`training_forms_page_2`, `number`, `required`: false): This question will be shown as a prompt for the submitter.
