# Coating Techniques Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the user's knowledge and experience in coating techniques, specifically in the fields of surface preparation, coating methods, and quality control.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name in the "Participant Name" field (Field 2).
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field (Field 3).
3. Provide your organization name in the "Organization" field (Field 4).
4. Select your experience level in the "Coating Industry Experience" field (Field 6).
5. Choose your current role in the "Current Role" field (Field 7).
6. Answer the following questions:
	* Question 1: What is the primary purpose of surface preparation? (Field 9)
	* Question 2: Which coating method provides the best coverage for complex surfaces? (Field 10)
	* Question 3: What is the ideal humidity level for most coating applications? (Field 11)
	* Question 4: How does temperature affect coating viscosity? (Field 12)
	* Question 5: What is the purpose of primer in coating systems? (Field 13)
	* Question 6: Which defect is caused by inadequate mixing? (Field 14)
	* Question 7: What is dry film thickness (DFT) measured in? (Field 15)
7. Select the difficulty level of the quiz (Field 17).
8. Choose topics you would like to learn more about (Field 18).
9. Add any additional comments or suggestions you have for the training (Field 19).
