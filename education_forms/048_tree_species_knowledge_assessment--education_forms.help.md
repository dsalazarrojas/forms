# Tree Species Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about a user's knowledge regarding tree species. However, the exact purpose and scope of this assessment are unclear.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your [User Name] if applicable.
2. Provide your [Participant Name] if applicable.
3. Enter your [Participant Email] if applicable.
4. Enter your [Participant Phone Number] if applicable.
5. Select the [Type of Forest] relevant to your assessment.
6. Indicate your [Tree Species] knowledge by selecting all applicable options.
7. Enter any additional [Tree Species ID] information if necessary (though this field is not strictly required).
8. Record your [Tree Knowledge] and [Tree Knowledge 2] dates, though these fields are not strictly required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (Text, Optional)
	+ Enter your name if you are a representative of an organization or have a specific affiliation related to this assessment.
* **Participant Name** (Text, Optional)
	+ Enter the name of the participant or individual this assessment is for.
* **Participant Email** (Email, Optional)
	+ Enter the email address of the participant or individual this assessment is for.
* **Participant Phone Number** (Text, Optional)
	+ Enter the phone number of the participant or individual this assessment is for.
* **Type of Forest** (Select One, Required)
	+ Choose the type of forest that this assessment is related to.
* **Tree Species** (Select Multiple, Optional)
	+ Indicate your knowledge about different tree species by selecting all applicable options.
* **Tree Species ID** (Text, Optional)
	+ Provide any additional tree species ID information if necessary.
* **Tree Species ID 2** (Text, Optional)
	+ Provide any additional tree species ID 2 information if necessary.
* **Tree Knowledge** (Date, Optional)
	+ Record the date you obtained knowledge about tree species.
* **Tree Knowledge 2** (Date, Optional)
	+ Record the date you obtained additional knowledge about tree species.

## Tips
This form is meant to collect information and assessments about your knowledge regarding tree species. Please fill it out honestly and accurately to the best of your abilities.
