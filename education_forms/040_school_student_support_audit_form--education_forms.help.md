# School Student Support Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Student Support Audit Form is designed to gather information about students' support needs and services provided by schools. This form helps school staff identify areas of need and allocate resources effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with the following information:
	* Student ID: Enter the student's unique identifier.
	* Student Name: Enter the student's name.
	* School ID: Enter the student's school ID.
	* Support Level: Select the level of support the student requires (High, Medium, or Low).
	* Feedback: Provide any additional feedback about the student's support needs.
2. For the second set of questions, please select the relevant options from the dropdown menus:
	* School: Choose the student's school from the list.
	* Student: Choose the student from the list.
	* Support Level 2: Select the level of support required for the student (High, Medium, or Low).
	* Teacher: Choose the teacher who provides support to the student.
	* School Id 2: Choose the school ID for the student's school.
3. Fill in the additional information:
	* Date: Enter the date of the support audit (optional).
	* Time: Enter the time of the support audit (optional).
	* School: Choose the school where the student attends (optional).
	* Teacher Name: Enter the teacher's name (optional).
	* Teacher Email: Enter the teacher's email (optional).
	* Teacher Phone: Enter the teacher's phone number (optional).
	* Teacher Feedback: Provide any additional feedback from the teacher (optional).
	* School Address: Enter the school address (optional).
	* Support Services: Select the support services provided to the student (e.g., Guidance and Counseling, Academic Support, Mental Health, Special Needs).
	* Other Support Services: Enter any additional support services provided to the student (e.g., tutoring, mentoring, etc.).
	* Additional Feedback: Provide any additional feedback about the student's support needs.
