# Student Council Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to nominate students for positions in the student council. It requires information about the nominee, their position, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Start by filling out all the required fields. The form has 7 fields in total.
*   Fill in the student's name in the "Name" field.
*   Provide the student's email address in the "Email" field.
*   Select the position you are nominating the student for from the list of options in the "Position" field.
*   Enter the reason why you are nominating the student in the "Reason" field.
*   Provide the nominee's name in the "Nominee Name" field.
*   Fill in the School ID of the student in the "School ID" field.
*   Make sure to review your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Name** (`student_council_form_fields`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the student you are nominating.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter the student's email address for contact purposes.
*   **Position** (`role`, `select_one`, required): Select the position you are nominating the student for from the options provided: Chairman, Vice-Chairman, Secretary, Treasurer, President.
*   **Reason** (`reason`, `text`, required): Briefly explain why you are nominating the student. This can include their qualifications, skills, or past achievements.
*   **Nominee Name** (`nominee_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the student you are nominating.
*   **School ID** (`school_id`, `text`, required): Enter the School ID of the student you are nominating.
*   **Nominee Name** (`position`, `select_one`, required): This field is actually the same as the "Position" field, but it seems there are two fields with the same label and name. In this case, please choose the position you want from the list: Chairman, Vice-Chairman, Secretary, Treasurer, President.

Note: There is a duplicate field with the label "Nominee Name" and name "nominee_name", which seems to be a mistake in the original YAML. To avoid confusion, we recommend skipping it as it doesn't seem to add any extra information.
 
## Tips
*   Make sure to enter accurate and truthful information to ensure the nomination process proceeds smoothly.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask for help or clarification.
*   Review the information carefully before submitting the form to avoid mistakes.
*   Nomination forms are an excellent way to recognize and support students' contributions to their school community.
