# Pre Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to assess an individual's language skills and proficiency levels in listening, reading, speaking, and writing. It helps to identify areas where an individual may need improvement and provides a baseline for language learning goals.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Provide your age in the "Age" field.
3. Enter your contact email address in the "Contact Email" field (optional).
4. Select your language skills and proficiency levels in the "English Language Proficiency" section.
5. Answer the questions in the "Language Skills" section by selecting "Yes" or "No" for each skill.
6. Enter your proficiency levels in the "Listening Comprehension", "Reading Comprehension", "Speaking", "Writing Comprehension", "Writing Production", "Reading Production", "Listening Production", and "Speaking Production" fields.
7. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Additional Information" and "Other languages Spoken" fields.
8. Enter your native language and language learned in the "Native Language" and "Language Learned" fields (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### English Language Proficiency (section_1)
**English Language Proficiency** (`section_1`, `number`, required/optional): This field assesses your English language proficiency level.

### Name
**Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name.

### Age
**Age** (`age`, `number`, required): Enter your age.

### Contact Email
**Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your contact email address.

### Language Skills (section_2)
**Language Skills** (`section_2`, `select_multiple`, required): Select "Yes" or "No" for each language skill.

### Listening Comprehension
**Listening Comprehension** (`listening_comprehension`, `number`, required): Enter your listening comprehension level.

### Reading Comprehension
**Reading Comprehension** (`reading_comprehension`, `number`, required): Enter your reading comprehension level.

### Speaking Skill (section_3)
**Speaking Skill** (`speaking`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select "Yes" or "No" for speaking skill.

### Writing Comprehension
**Writing Comprehension** (`writing_comprehension`, `number`, required): Enter your writing comprehension level.

### Writing Production
**Writing Production** (`writing_production`, `number`, required): Enter your writing production level.

### Reading Production
**Reading Production** (`reading_production`, `number`, required): Enter your reading production level.

### Listening Production
**Listening Production** (`listening_production`, `number`, required): Enter your listening production level.

### Speaking Production
**Speaking Production** (`speaking_production`, `number`, required): Enter your speaking production level.

### Additional Information (section_4)
**Additional Information** (`section_3`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select "Yes" or "No" for additional information.

### Other languages Spoken
**Other languages Spoken** (`other_languages_spoken`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select "Yes" or "No" for other languages spoken.

### Native Language
**Native Language** (`native_language`, `text`, optional): Enter your native language.

### Language Learned
**Language Learned** (`language_learned`, `text`, optional): Enter the language you have learned.

### Field 18
**Field 18** (`field_18`, `text`, optional): This field is not currently in use.

## Tips

- Please answer all required fields.
- You can enter your native language and language learned in the "Native Language" and "Language Learned" fields if applicable.
- Select "Yes" or "No" for language skills and additional information in the corresponding fields.
