# School Age Enrollment Packet Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about students and parents for school enrollment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out all the required fields marked as required.
2. Enter accurate and up-to-date information about the student and parent.
3. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please refer to the field-by-field explanation section for clarification.
