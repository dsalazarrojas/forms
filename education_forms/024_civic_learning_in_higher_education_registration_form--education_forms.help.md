# Civic Learning In Higher Education Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register and collect information from students interested in participating in civic learning programs in higher education. It's essential to provide accurate information to ensure correct guidance and support from faculty members.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, please provide your personal and institutional details on Page 1 of the form.
2.  Proceed to Page 2 and enter your name, university student ID number, university name, and major area of study.
3.  On Page 3, select your current academic year and confirm that you are interested in participating in a civic learning program.
4.  Enter the name of the civic learning course/module you're interested in on Page 4.
5.  If applicable, indicate whether you want to earn academic credit for this course on Page 5.
6.  On Page 6, enter the name of your supervising faculty/advisor.
7.  In Page 7, provide a brief explanation of why you're interested in this civic learning program.
8.  On Page 8, let us know if you're interested in a community service-learning placement.
9.  Briefly describe any prior civic or volunteer experience you have on Page 9.
10. On Page 10, confirm your commitment to completing the program requirements and hours.
11. Finally, select a date for your registration on Page 11.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`student_name`): This field asks for your full name as per university records.
* **University Student ID Number** (`student_id`): Enter your unique university-issued student ID number to ensure accurate tracking and registration.
* **University or College Name** (`university_name`): Please enter the name of your institution to confirm your affiliation.
* **Major/Area of Study** (`major_study_area`): Indicate your current major or area of study to help us tailor the program to your needs.
* **Current Academic Year** (`academic_year`): Select your current academic year from the options provided.
* **Major/Area of Study** (`academic_year`): Note that this field has the same label as another field in the form, but it seems to be a typo. Please ignore this field.
* **Name of the Civic Learning Course/Module** (`course_module_title`): Enter the name of the specific civic learning course/module you're interested in participating in.
* **Is this course for academic credit?** (`credit_requirement`): If you want to earn academic credit for this course, select "True".
* **Supervising Faculty/Advisor Name** (`advisor_faculty_name`): If you have a supervising faculty member or advisor, enter their name here.
* **Why are you interested in this civic learning program?** (`primary_motivation`): Provide a brief explanation of why you're interested in this program.
* **Are you interested in a community service-learning placement?** (`service_learning_interest`): If you're interested in a community service-learning placement, select "Yes, definitely".
* **Briefly describe any prior civic or volunteer experience** (`prior_civic_engagement`): If you have any prior civic or volunteer experience, please describe it briefly.
* **I commit to completing the program requirements and hours** (`registration_acknowledgment`): Confirm your commitment to completing the program requirements and hours by selecting "I Agree".
* **Date of Registration** (`submission_date`): Select the date you're registering for the program.
