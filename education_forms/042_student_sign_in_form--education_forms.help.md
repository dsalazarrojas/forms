# Student Sign In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Sign In Form is a basic data collection tool used to gather student information for academic and administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First name**, which is your given name.
2. Enter your **Last name**, which is the name your parents gave you.
3. Enter your **Email**, which is your contact email address.
4. Enter your **Student ID**, which is a unique identifier assigned to you by your school.
5. Select your **Grade**, which is your current academic grade.
6. Choose whether you are a **Student** or not.
7. Optionally, provide a **Note** for any additional information.
8. Click **Submit** to complete and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your given name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last name**: Enter your surname as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your personal email address where you can be contacted.
* **Student ID**: Enter your unique student identifier number assigned to you by your school.
* **Grade**: Select your current grade level (e.g., 9th, 10th, etc.).
* **Student Type**: Choose whether you are a registered student or not.
* **Student Type Options**: This is a note field for additional information about your student status.
* **Submit**: Click to complete and submit your form.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Time of Attendance**: Enter your expected time of attendance in the format 24-hour clock (HH:MM).
* **Note**: Enter any additional information or notes about your attendance status.
