# College Information Packet Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for prospective students who are interested in learning more about our college and its offerings. By filling out this form, you will help us understand your needs and provide you with the most relevant information and support.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form carefully and completely, using the following fields:
  - **First Name**: Enter your full first name.
  - **Last Name**: Enter your full last name.
  - **Email Address**: Enter your email address, which will be used to contact you with information about your inquiry.
  - **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number, which will be used to contact you if needed.
  - **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth, if you wish to receive information about specific programs or services.
  - **Current High School or Institution**: Enter the name of your current high school or institution.
  - **Graduation Year**: Enter the year you graduated from high school or your current institution.
  - **Type of Inquiry**: Select the type of information you are interested in learning more about (e.g., undergraduate programs, graduate programs, etc.).
  - **Academic Interests or Intended Major**: Enter the academic subject or field you are interested in or planning to major in.
  - **Program Type**: Select the type of program you are interested in (e.g., bachelor's degree, associate degree, etc.).
  - **Planned Enrollment Timeline**: Select the timeline for your planned enrollment (e.g., next fall, next spring, etc.).
  - **Citizenship Status**: Select your citizenship status.
  - **Country of Origin** (if applicable): Enter your country of origin if you are an international student.
  - **State or Region** (if applicable): Enter the state or region where you are from if you are a U.S. student.
  - **Interested in Scheduling Campus Visit**: Indicate if you are interested in scheduling a campus visit.
  - **Preferred Campus Visit Date** (if applicable): Enter the date you would prefer to visit the campus.
  - **Interested in Financial Aid Information**: Indicate if you are interested in financial aid information.
  - **Interested in Scholarship Information**: Indicate if you are interested in scholarship information.
  - **How Did You Hear About Our College**: Select how you heard about our college (e.g., website, social media, etc.).
  - **Mailing Address for Information Packet**: Enter your mailing address where you would like to receive the information packet.
  - **City**: Enter the city of your mailing address.
  - **State or Province**: Enter the state or province of your mailing address.
  - **Postal Code**: Enter the postal code of your mailing address.
  - **Additional Information or Questions**: Provide any additional information or questions you would like to have answered.
