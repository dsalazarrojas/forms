# nature_program_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nature Program Enrollment Form is a tool for users to enroll in nature and conservation programs. This form helps gather essential information about the program, making it easier for program administrators to manage and coordinate the program efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Select the relevant program type from the "Program type" dropdown list.
4. Enter the start date of the program in the "Start date" field (mm/dd/yyyy format).
5. Enter the end date of the program in the "End date" field (mm/dd/yyyy format).
6. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
7. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
8. Provide information about the organization you represent in the "Organization" field.
9. Enter the department you belong to in the "Department" field.
10. Provide information about the contact person in the "Contact person" field.
11. Enter the contact person's phone number in the "Contact number" field.
12. Enter the number of participants in the "Participant count" field.
13. Select the program location from the "Program location" dropdown list.
14. Choose the relevant details about the program from the "Program details" dropdown list.
15. Enter the name of the program organizer in the "Program organizer" field.
16. Enter the name of the program coordinator in the "Program coordinator" field.
17. Select the relevant participant agreement from the "Participant agreement" dropdown list.
18. You can add any additional notes for the program in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Program type** (select_one, required): Select the type of program you are enrolling in (e.g., Nature Walk, Nature Hike, Conservation Day).
* **Start date** (date, required): Enter the start date of the program in mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **End date** (date, required): Enter the end date of the program in mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Organization** (text, optional): Provide information about the organization you represent.
* **Department** (text, optional): Enter the department you belong to.
* **Contact person** (text, optional): Provide information about the contact person.
* **Contact number** (text, optional): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Participant count** (number, optional): Enter the number of participants.
* **Program location** (select_one, required): Select the location of the program.
* **Program details** (select_multiple, optional): Choose the relevant details about the program.
* **Program organizer** (text, optional): Enter the name of the program organizer.
* **Program coordinator** (text, optional): Enter the name of the program coordinator.
* **Participant agreement** (select_one, required): Select the relevant participant agreement.
* **Participant agreement date** (date, optional): Enter the date of the participant agreement.
* **Participant agreement signed** (select_one, optional): Indicate whether the participant agreement has been signed.
* **Participant agreement signed date** (date, optional): Enter the date the participant agreement was signed.
* **Organization notes** (note, optional): Add any additional notes about the organization.
* **Participant notes** (note, optional): Add any additional notes about the participant.
* **Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional notes about the program.
