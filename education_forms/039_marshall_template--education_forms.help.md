# marshall_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information in a structured and organized manner, ideal for use in educational settings where data needs to be tracked and recorded.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Form Title** field with the title of the event, project, or activity being recorded.
2. Select the relevant **Category** from the dropdown list.
3. Enter a brief **Description** of the event, project, or activity in the **Description** field.
4. Fill in the **Date** in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
5. Enter the time of the event in the **Time** field.
6. If applicable, provide your **Email** address.
7. Enter your **Phone** number if relevant.
8. Add any additional information in the **Note** field.
9. Select whether to **Submit** or not, as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the form, such as a project or event name.
* **Category** (`form_category`, select_one, required): Choose the relevant category from the dropdown list to associate the form with the correct category.
* **Description** (`form_description`, note, optional): A brief description of the event, project, or activity.
* **Date** (`form_date`, date, required): Enter the date in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Time** (`form_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the event.
* **Email** (`form_email`, email, optional): If applicable, provide your email address.
* **Phone** (`form_phone`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your phone number.
* **Note** (`form_note`, note, optional): Add any additional information that is relevant.
* **Submit** (`form_submit`, select_one, required): Select whether to submit or not, as required.
