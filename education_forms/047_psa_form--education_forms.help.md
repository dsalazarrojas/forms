# Psa Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Psa Form is designed to collect information about household media usage and children's details from users. This form is used to:

* Gather information about the number of children in your household
* Collect additional information about your children
* Collect agreement on terms and conditions

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Start Form" button to begin.
2. Answer the questions on each page, providing as much detail as requested.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
