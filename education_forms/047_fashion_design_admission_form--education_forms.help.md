# Fashion Design Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form guides you through the admission process for the Fashion Design course. To submit your application, please complete the form below.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each page carefully and provide accurate information.
- Fill in all required fields (indicated by <required>true</required>).
- Select one of the provided options for Second Page.
- Leave comments and additional information in the Third Page text field (if desired).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Page** (text, optional, no hints): Please leave this field blank for now. It is intended as a placeholder page.
* **Second Page** (select_one, true): Select one option from the list to confirm your application status.
  - **Option 1**: I am eligible to apply for the Fashion Design course.
  - **Option 2**: I am not eligible to apply for the Fashion Design course.
  - **Option 3**: I need more information about the course requirements before I can confirm my eligibility.
* **Third Page** (text, false): Provide any additional comments or information regarding your application here.
