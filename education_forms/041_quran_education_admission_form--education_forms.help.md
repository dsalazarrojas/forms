# quranEducationAdmissionForm - Help Guide
## Purpose
The quranEducationAdmissionForm is used to gather information from parents or guardians for enrollment of their wards in educational programs related to the study of the Quran.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields with the correct information.
2. Make sure all fields are accurate and up to date.
3. Review the form before submission for any mistakes or errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (id: 1, <name> first_name, <type> text, required: false): Enter the student's first name.
* **Last Name** (id: 2, <name> last_name, <type> text, required: false): Enter the student's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (id: 3, <name> date_of_birth, <type> date, required: false): Enter the student's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email Address** (id: 4, <name> email_address, <type> email, required: false): Enter the student's email address.
* **Phone Number** (id: 5, <name> phone_number, <type> text, required: false): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Course of Interest** (id: 6, <name> course_of_interest, <type> text, required: false): Select the course or program of interest for the student.
* **Parent Name** (id: 7, <name> parent_name, <type> text, required: false): Enter the parent's name.
* **Parent Email** (id: 8, <name> parent_email, <type> email, required: false): Enter the parent's email address.
* **Parent Phone** (id: 9, <name> parent_phone, <type> text, required: false): Enter the parent's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (id: 10, <name> emergency_contact_name, <type> text, required: false): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (id: 11, <name> emergency_contact_relationship, <type> text, required: false): Specify the relationship of the emergency contact person with the student.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (id: 12, <name> emergency_contact_phone, <type> text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check all fields before submission.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the educational program administrator for assistance.
* If you're enrolling multiple students, fill in separate forms for each student.
