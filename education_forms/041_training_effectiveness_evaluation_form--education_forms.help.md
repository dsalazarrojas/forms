# Training Effectiveness Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the effectiveness of training programs, allowing users to rate and comment on different aspects of the course, including content, interaction design, and instructor performance. The form aims to provide valuable feedback for trainers to improve their programs and enhance the learning experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the training course that you participated in.
2. Select the type of course (in-person, online, or other).
3. Choose the location where the training took place.
4. Enter the name of the instructor leading the course.
5. Select the date you attended the training.
6. Enter the start and end times of the training session.
7. Rate the overall effectiveness of the training on a scale of 1-5.
8. Rate different aspects of the training, such as content relevance, interaction design, and instructor performance.
9. Provide comments on the training's content, interaction design, and instructor performance.
10. Suggest any recommendations for future improvements.
11. Enter your participant ID (optional).
12. Enter your name and email (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Course Name**: Enter the name of the training course you participated in. This will help trainers understand which course you're evaluating.
* **Course Type**: Select the type of course you attended. This can be in-person, online, or another type.
* **Training Location**: Enter the location where the training took place. This can be a physical location or an online platform.
* **Instructor**: Choose the name of the instructor leading the course.
* **Date Attended**: Enter the date you attended the training.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of the training session.
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of the training session.
* **Overall Rating**: Rate the overall effectiveness of the training on a scale of 1-5.
* **Content Relevance**: Rate how relevant the training content was to your needs.
* **Interaction Design**: Rate the quality of the interaction design, such as slides, handouts, and activities.
* **Presentation Delivery**: Rate how well the instructor delivered the presentation.
* **Instructor Performance**: Rate the instructor's performance, including their ability to engage and explain the material.
* **Material Use**: Rate the use of materials provided, such as handouts, slides, and other resources.
* **Follow-up Support**: Rate the support you received after the training.
* **Evaluation Comments**: Provide comments on the training's content, interaction design, and instructor performance.
* **Recommendations**: Suggest any improvements or changes you think could be made to the training.
* **Training Effectiveness Score**: Rate the overall effectiveness of the training on a scale of 1-5.
* **Training Recommendation**: Choose whether you would recommend this training to others.
* **Participant ID**: Enter your participant ID (optional).
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name (optional).
* **Participant Email**: Enter your email (optional).
