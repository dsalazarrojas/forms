# role_play_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The role play evaluation form is designed to gather feedback and evaluations of scenarios or role plays. This form is used to assess the effectiveness and performance of a role play, providing an opportunity for individuals to reflect on their experiences and offer constructive feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the scenario description and understand the context of the role play.
2. Answer the questions about whether the role was played as and with whom.
3. Provide your observations about the scenario.
4. Rate the scenario's effectiveness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Scenario Description** (`scenario_description`, text, optional): This is a text field where you can provide a brief description of the scenario or role play. Please provide a clear and concise summary of the experience.
- **Role Played As** (`role_played_as`, select_one, optional): This question asks if the role was played as expected or intended. Select 'Yes' if you think the role was played correctly, 'No' if you don't think it was.
- **Observation** (`observation`, text, optional): Here, you can share any observations or thoughts you had during the role play. This can include anything that came up during the exercise, such as challenges or areas for improvement.
- **Role Played With** (`role_played_with`, select_multiple, optional): This question asks which other roles or individuals you interacted with during the scenario. Select all the roles that applied to your experience.
- **Scenario Rating** (`scenario_rating`, number, optional): Rate the scenario on a scale from 0 to 10, where 0 is the lowest and 10 is the highest. This rating will help us understand the scenario's effectiveness.
