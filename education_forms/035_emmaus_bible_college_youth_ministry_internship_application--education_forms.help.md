# emmaus_bible_college_youth_ministry_internship_application - Help Guide

## Purpose
The emmaus_bible_college_youth_ministry_internship_application form is designed to collect information from students who wish to apply for a youth ministry internship at Emmaus Bible College. This form is used to gather demographic and submission information from applicants, as well as to track their submission status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field. This should be a valid email address that we can contact you with.
3. Provide your phone number in the "phone" field.
4. Select your desired department from the "department" field.
5. Enter the dates you are available for the internship in the "dates" field.
6. Choose your preferred time of day for the internship in the "time" field.
7. Select the location where you would like to complete the internship in the "location" field.
8. In the "description" field, briefly describe your background and experience related to youth ministry.
9. Select the department that submitted this application in the "submitted_by" field.
10. Enter the name of the person who submitted this application in the "submitted_by_name" field.
11. Enter the department that submitted this application in the "submitted_by_department" field.
12. Enter the email address of the person who submitted this application in the "submitted_by_email" field.
13. Enter the phone number of the person who submitted this application in the "submitted_by_phone" field.
14. Enter the time the application was submitted in the "submitted_by_time" field.
15. Select the location of the person who submitted this application in the "submitted_by_location" field.
16. Provide any additional information about the submission in the "submitted_by_additional_info" field.
17. Enter the name of the person who approved this application in the "approved_by" field.
18. Enter the name of the person who approved this application in the "approved_by_name" field.
19. Enter the department of the person who approved this application in the "approved_by_department" field.
20. Enter the email address of the person who approved this application in the "approved_by_email" field.
21. Enter the phone number of the person who approved this application in the "approved_by_phone" field.
22. Enter the date the application was approved in the "approved_by_date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Form**: This is the main form to submit your application.
* **name**: Please enter your name as you would like to be known.
* **email**: Enter a valid email address that we can contact you with.
* **phone**: Provide your phone number so we can contact you.
* **department**: Select the department that you are interested in.
* **dates**: Enter the dates you are available for the internship.
* **time**: Choose your preferred time for the internship.
* **location**: Select the location where you would like to complete the internship.
* **description**: Briefly describe your background and experience in youth ministry.
* **submitted_by**: Select the department that submitted this application.
* **submitted_by_name**: Enter the name of the person who submitted this application.
* **submitted_by_department**: Enter the department that submitted this application.
* **submitted_by_email**: Enter the email address of the person who submitted this application.
* **submitted_by_phone**: Enter the phone number of the person who submitted this application.
* **submitted_by_time**: Enter the time the application was submitted.
* **submitted_by_location**: Select the location of the person who submitted this application.
* **submitted_by_additional_info**: Provide any additional information about the submission.
* **submitted_by_form**: Enter the form used to submit this application.
* **approved_by**: Enter the name of the person who approved this application.
* **approved_by_name**: Enter the name of the person who approved this application.
* **approved_by_department**: Enter the department of the person who approved this application.
* **approved_by_email**: Enter the email address of the person who approved this application.
* **approved_by_phone**: Enter the phone number of the person who approved this application.
* **approved_by_date**: Enter the date the application was approved.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as required in the form definition.
