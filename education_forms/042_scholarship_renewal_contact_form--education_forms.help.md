This form is used by students to request a renewal of their scholarship, allowing them to update their contact information and provide context for the renewal request. This form is typically filled out by students who are nearing the end of their scholarship period and need to extend it. The form helps the scholarship administration track the renewal requests and update the status of the scholarship accordingly. To avoid submitting duplicate requests, please ensure you have not already submitted a renewal request for this scholarship. 

# Scholarship Renewal Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scholarship Renewal Contact Form is a document that allows students to request a renewal of their scholarship. This form is used to update the contact information and provide context for the renewal request.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the Scholarship Renewal Contact Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Student Id** number, which is a unique identifier assigned to you as a student.
2. Enter your **Scholarship Id**, which is a unique identifier assigned to the scholarship you are renewing.
3. Select the **Contact Method** through which you prefer to be contacted, e.g., Phone, Email, or In-person.
4. Enter the **Contact Number** associated with your selected contact method.
5. Enter your **Contact Email** (if selected as the contact method).
6. Enter the **Contact Name** associated with your selected contact method (e.g., the office or department contact name).
7. Enter the **Contact Office Address** and **Contact Office Email** associated with your selected contact method.
8. Enter a **Message** explaining the reason for the renewal request.
9. Select the **Scholarship Status** that best describes the current status of your scholarship.
10. Select the **Financial Status** that best describes your current financial situation.
11. Select the **Academic Status** that best describes your current academic situation.
12. Enter the **Renewal Date**, which is the date you would like to renew your scholarship.
13. Enter the **Renewal Time**, which is the time you would like to renew your scholarship.
14. Enter the **Renewal Reason**, which explains why you are requesting a renewal.
15. Select whether this is a **Contact Reason**, which can be for renewal request, general inquiry, or other.
16. Enter your **Renewal Request**, which is a brief description of your renewal requirements.
17. Enter any additional **Comments** or notes about your renewal request.
18. Finally, select whether to **Save and Continue** or **Save and Finish**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Id** (`Student Id`, `number`, required): Enter your unique student ID number.
* **Scholarship Id** (`Scholarship Id`, `text`, required): Enter the unique scholarship ID associated with your renewal request.
* **Contact Method** (`Contact Method`, `select_one`, required): Select the method through which you prefer to be contacted.
	+ **Phone**: Select this option to be contacted through phone calls.
	+ **Email**: Select this option to be contacted through email.
	+ **In-person**: Select this option to be contacted in-person.
* **Contact Number** (`Contact Number`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number associated with your selected contact method.
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address associated with your selected contact method.
* **Contact Name** (`Contact Name`, `text`, required): Enter the name associated with your selected contact method.
* **Contact Office Number** (`Contact Office Number`, `text`, required): Enter the office number associated with your selected contact method.
* **Contact Office Address** (`Contact Office Address`, `text`, required): Enter the office address associated with your selected contact method.
* **Contact Office Email** (`Contact Office Email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address associated with your selected contact method.
* **Message** (`Message`, `note`, required): Enter a brief message explaining the reason for your renewal request.
* **Scholarship Status** (`Scholarship Status`, `select_one`, required): Select the current status of your scholarship.
	+ **active**: Select this option if your scholarship is active.
	+ **inactive**: Select this option if your scholarship is inactive.
	+ **on hold**: Select this option if your scholarship is on hold.
* **Financial Status** (`Financial Status`, `select_one`, required): Select the current financial status of your scholarship.
	+ **active**: Select this option if your scholarship is active.
	+ **inactive**: Select this option if your scholarship is inactive.
	+ **on hold**: Select this option if your scholarship is on hold.
* **Academic Status** (`Academic Status`, `select_one`, required): Select the current academic status of your scholarship.
	+ **active**: Select this option if your scholarship is active.
	+ **inactive**: Select this option if your scholarship is inactive.
	+ **on hold**: Select this option if your scholarship is on hold.
* **Renewal Date** (`Renewal Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you would like to renew your scholarship.
* **Renewal Time** (`Renewal Time`, `time`, required): Enter the time you would like to renew your scholarship.
* **Renewal Reason** (`Renewal Reason`, `text`, required): Enter a brief explanation for the renewal request.
* **Contact Reason** (`Contact Reason`, `text`, required): Enter a brief explanation for the contact reason.
* **Renewal Request** (`Renewal Request`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of your renewal requirements.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, `text`, required): Enter any additional comments or notes about your renewal request.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, `select_multiple`, required): Select whether to **Save and Continue** or **Save and Finish**.
