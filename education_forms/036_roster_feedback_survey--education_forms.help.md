# roster_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from team members on a specific team's performance. It is meant to be a quick and easy way to gather opinions and suggestions from individuals who have interacted with the team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Team Name**: Type in the name of the team you are providing feedback on.
2. **Participant Name**: Enter your name as a participant who has worked with or interacted with the team.
3. **Feedback**: Share your thoughts and opinions on the team's performance. Be as specific as possible.
4. **Category**: Choose the category that best describes your feedback (Category A, Category B, Category C).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the team you are providing feedback on. This will help the team know which team you are referring to.
- **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as a participant who has worked with or interacted with the team.
- **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share your thoughts and opinions on the team's performance. Be as specific as possible.
- **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Choose the category that best describes your feedback. This will help categorize and filter your feedback for the team.

## Tips
- Be honest and sincere in your feedback. Your input is valuable and will help the team improve.
- Try to be specific when providing feedback. The more detailed and descriptive your feedback, the more helpful it will be for the team.
- If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to include them in the **Comments** field.
- Make sure to save your progress and submit the form to share your feedback with the team.
