# Youth Ministries Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Ministries Application Form is designed to collect information from young people who want to participate in youth ministries. This form helps the staff members of the ministry to process the applications efficiently and follow up on any pending actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the User Information page with your details.
2. Proceed to the Contact Information page and fill out your contact details.
3. Go to the Event Information page and provide details about the event you are interested in.
4. On the Payment Information page, choose your preferred payment method and amount.
5. If you are interested in volunteering, please fill out the Volunteer Information page.
6. Write any additional comments or questions you have on the Additional Comments page.
7. Select the Event Date and Start Time of the event you want to participate in.
8. Choose the End Time of the event.
9. Provide Event Details about the event.
10. On the Payment Method page, select your preferred payment method.
11. If applicable, fill out the Amount Paid.
12. Select the Application Status of your application.
13. Enter your User ID (if applicable).
14. Enter your User Name.
15. Enter your User Email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (text, required)**: Enter your name, age, and any other relevant personal information.
* **Contact Information (text, required)**: Enter your contact details, such as phone number and address.
* **Event Information (text, required)**: Provide details about the event you are interested in participating in.
* **Payment Information (text, required)**: Choose your preferred payment method and amount.
* **Volunteer Information (text, optional)**: If you are interested in volunteering, provide your availability and any other relevant information.
* **Additional Comments (text, optional)**: Write any additional comments or questions you have.
* **Event Date (date, required)**: Select the date of the event.
* **Start Time (time, required)**: Select the start time of the event.
* **End Time (time, required)**: Select the end time of the event.
* **Event Details (text, required)**: Provide details about the event.
* **Payment Method (select_one, required)**: Choose your preferred payment method.
* **Amount Paid (number, optional)**: If applicable, fill out the amount you paid.
* **Application Status (select_one, required)**: Select the status of your application.
* **User ID (number, optional)**: Enter your User ID (if applicable).
* **User Name (text, optional)**: Enter your name (if not already entered in User Information).
* **User Email (email, optional)**: Enter your email (if not already entered in User Information).

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
* If you have any questions, please contact the staff member responsible for the youth ministries.
* You can contact us for any further guidance or support.
