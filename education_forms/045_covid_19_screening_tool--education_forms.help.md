# Covid 19 Screening Tool - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help healthcare professionals quickly assess patients for COVID-19 risks and symptoms. It will guide you through a series of questions to determine the patient's status and provide a summary of their screening results.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the first name and last name fields with the patient's details.
2. Enter the patient's date of birth in the `date_of_birth` field in YYYY-MM-DD format.
3. Enter the patient's age.
4. Enter the patient's contact number.
5. Enter the patient's email address.
6. Fill in the emergency contact's details in the `emergency_contact` field, if applicable.
7. Enter the patient's address, if applicable.
8. Enter the date and time of the screening in the `date_of_screening` and `time_of_screening` fields, respectively.
9. Report any symptoms the patient is experiencing in the `symptoms` field.
10. Provide travel history and exposure details in the `travel_history` and `exposure` fields, if applicable.
11. Report the patient's current travel status in the `travel_status` field.
12. Select the patient's current symptoms status in the `symptoms_status` field.
13. Indicate whether the patient has been exposed to COVID-19 in the `exposure_status` field.
14. Enter the date of the test, if performed.
15. Report the test result in the `test_result` field.
16. Indicate the patient's vaccination status in the `vaccination_status` field.
17. Select the type of test performed in the `test_type` field.
18. Report any additional test results in the `test_result_2` field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Enter the patient's first name.
* **Last name**: Enter the patient's last name.
* **Date of birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 1995-01-01).
* **Age**: Enter the patient's age.
* **Contact number**: Enter the patient's contact number.
* **Email**: Enter the patient's email address.
* **Emergency contact**: Enter the emergency contact's name and contact number, if applicable.
* **Address**: Enter the patient's address, if applicable.
* **Date of screening**: Enter the date of the screening in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2022-02-01).
* **Time of screening**: Enter the time of the screening in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:30).
* **Temperature**: Report the patient's current temperature in degrees Celsius (°C) or Fahrenheit (F).
* **Symptoms**: Report any symptoms the patient is experiencing.
* **Travel history**: Report any recent travel history, including destinations and dates of travel.
* **Exposure**: Report any exposure to COVID-19, including dates and details of exposure.
* **Travel status**: Select the patient's current travel status:
	+ None: The patient is not traveling.
	+ Local travel: The patient is traveling locally, within their city or region.
	+ Out of home: The patient is traveling outside their city or region.
	+ Returning from travel: The patient has recently returned from travel.
* **Symptoms status**: Select the patient's current symptoms status:
	+ Asymptomatic: The patient does not have any symptoms.
	+ Mild: The patient has mild symptoms.
	+ Moderate: The patient has moderate symptoms.
	+ Severe: The patient has severe symptoms.
* **Exposure status**: Indicate whether the patient has been exposed to COVID-19:
	+ False: The patient has not been exposed.
	+ True: The patient has been exposed.
	+ Suspected: The patient's exposure is suspected.
* **Test date**: Enter the date of the test, if performed.
* **Test result**: Report the result of the test:
	+ Negative: The test result is negative.
	+ Positive: The test result is positive.
* **Vaccination status**: Indicate the patient's vaccination status:
	+ Vaccinated: The patient is vaccinated.
	+ Not vaccinated: The patient is not vaccinated.
* **Test type**: Select the type of test performed:
	+ PCR: Polymerase chain reaction (PCR) test.
	+ Rapid Antigen Test: Rapid antigen test.
* **Test result 2**: Report any additional test results, if applicable.
