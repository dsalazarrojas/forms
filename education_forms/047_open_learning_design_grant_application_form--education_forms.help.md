## Open Learning Design Grant Application Form - Help Guide

### Purpose

The Open Learning Design Grant Application Form is designed to collect information from educators and institutions seeking funding to support their open learning design projects. This form gathers necessary details to evaluate and consider grant proposals for approval.

### How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and contact information, including email and phone number (fields 1 and 2).
2. Briefly describe your grant proposal, outlining the project's goals, objectives, and expected outcomes (field 3).
3. Specify the submission date and time for your application, as well as any relevant grant details (fields 4 and 5).
4. Optionally, select the type of grant you are applying for from the provided options (field 6).
5. If applicable, specify the grant amount you are seeking.
6. Review your application carefully before submitting it (field 7).

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (Field 1)**: Provide your name and contact information.
* **Contact Info (Field 2)**: Select the relevant contact information type from the provided options (e.g., "option 1" or "option 2").
* **Grant Proposal (Field 3)**: Write a brief description of your grant proposal, including the project's goals, objectives, and expected outcomes.
* **Submission Date (Field 4)**: Specify the date you plan to submit your application.
* **Submission Time (Field 5)**: Indicate the time of day you plan to submit your application.
* **Grant Details (Field 6)**: Select the type of grant you are applying for from the provided options. This will help us understand your project's focus.
* **Grant Amount (Field 7)**: If applicable, enter the grant amount you are seeking.
* **Email (Field 8)**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone (Field 9)**: Enter your phone number for further contact.

### Tips

* Make sure to answer all required fields carefully and accurately.
* Double-check your submission date and time to ensure it aligns with your project's timeline.
* Review your application before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed.
