# Automotive Preference And Knowledge Check Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Automotive Preference And Knowledge Check Form is designed to gather information about your vehicle preferences and knowledge to provide you with personalized vehicle recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Answer questions about your vehicle ownership experience, such as how many years you have owned a vehicle.
3. Select your preferred vehicle type, size, transmission, and fuel type.
4. Choose the features that are must-haves for your next vehicle.
5. Select your level of knowledge about engines and vehicle maintenance.
6. Identify your budget range and purchase timeline.
7. Choose your top priorities for your next vehicle.
8. Finally, indicate if you are interested in trade-in options and provide any additional comments or questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your valid email address so we can contact you with updates and information.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you with updates and information.
* **Years of Vehicle Ownership Experience**: Select the number of years you have owned a vehicle.
* **Preferred Vehicle Type**: Select the type of vehicle you are interested in (e.g. Sedan, SUV, Truck, etc.).
* **Preferred Vehicle Size**: Select the size of vehicle you prefer (e.g. Compact, Mid-Size, Full-Size, etc.).
* **Transmission Preference**: Select your preferred transmission type (e.g. Automatic, Manual, CVT, etc.).
* **Fuel Type Preference**: Select your preferred fuel type (e.g. Gasoline, Diesel, Hybrid, etc.).
* **Must-Have Features**: Select all the features that are must-haves for your next vehicle (e.g. Air Conditioning, Power Windows, etc.).
* **Knowledge of Engine Types**: Select your level of knowledge about engine types (e.g. Expert, Knowledgeable, Basic, etc.).
* **Vehicle Maintenance Knowledge**: Select your level of knowledge about vehicle maintenance (e.g. Expert, Knowledgeable, Basic, etc.).
* **Budget Range**: Select your budget range for your next vehicle (e.g. Under 15,000, 15,000-25,000, etc.).
* **Purchase Timeline**: Select your purchase timeline (e.g. Within 1 month, 1-3 months, etc.).
* **Top Priorities**: Select your top priorities for your next vehicle (e.g. Fuel Efficiency, Performance, Safety, etc.).
* **Interested in Trade-In Options**: Indicate if you are interested in trade-in options (e.g. Yes, No, Maybe).
* **Additional Questions or Notes**: Provide any additional comments or questions you have.
