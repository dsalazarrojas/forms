# Placement Host Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Placement Host Assessment Form is used to gather information about job placements and the contact person responsible for the host company. This form is used by the placement team to collect data for reporting and evaluation purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **First Contact Info** section with the name and any other relevant contact details of the person at the host company.
2. In the **Host Company** section, enter the name of the company.
3. In the **Job Placement Position** section, enter a brief description of the job placement.
4. Enter the contact person's details in the **Contact First Name**, **Contact Last Name**, **Contact Email**, and **Contact Phone** sections.
5. In the **Job Status**, **Job Type**, and **Type of Placement** sections, select the relevant options from the dropdown menus.
6. If required, enter the **Job Start Date** and **Job End Date**.
7. In the **Job Hours** section, enter the number of hours worked.
8. In the **Job Location** section, enter the location of the job.
9. In the **Job Description** section, enter a brief description of the job.
10. In the **Primary Language** and **Secondary Language** sections, enter the languages spoken.
11. In the **Industry** section, enter the industry related to the job.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Contact Info (First Contact Info)**: Enter the contact information of the person at the host company.
* **Host Company (Host Company)**: Enter the name of the host company.
* **Job Placement Position (Job Placement Position)**: Enter a brief description of the job placement.
* **Contact First Name (Contact First Name)**: Enter the first name of the contact person.
* **Contact Last Name (Contact Last Name)**: Enter the last name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email (Contact Email)**: Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone (Contact Phone)**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Job Title (Job Title)**: Enter the job title.
* **Job Start Date (Job Start Date)**: Enter the start date of the job (optional).
* **Job End Date (Job End Date)**: Enter the end date of the job (optional).
* **Job Status (Job Status)**: Select the status of the job (Active, Inactive, On Leave, Terminated).
* **Job Type (Job Type)**: Select the type of job (Internship, Co-op, Full-time, Part-time).
* **Job Hours (Job Hours)**: Enter the number of hours worked.
* **Job Location (Job Location)**: Enter the location of the job.
* **Job Description (Job Description)**: Enter a brief description of the job.
* **Primary Language (Contact First Language)**: Enter the primary language spoken by the contact person.
* **Secondary Language (Contact Second Language)**: Enter the secondary language spoken by the contact person.
* **Type of Placement (Type of Placement)**: Select the type of placement (Internship, Co-op, Full-time, Part-time, Short-term).
* **Industry (Job Industry)**: Enter the industry related to the job.

**Tips**

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information entered.
* If unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or support.
