# Educational Learning Service Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect information on students' attendance in educational learning services, allowing administrators to track attendance patterns.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing all required information.
- Review each field carefully to ensure that you are providing the correct information.
- If you have any questions or doubts, please reach out to your administrator for clarification or guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Class Name (`class_name`, Text, Optional)
* Enter the name of the class you attended.

### Student Name (`student_name`, Text, Optional)
* Enter your name as it appears on your official records.

### Date (`date`, Date, Optional)
* Enter the date you attended the class in the correct format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).

### Time (`time`, Time, Optional)
* Enter the time you attended the class in the correct format (e.g., HH:MM AM/PM).

### Attended (`attended`, Select One, Optional)
* Select the option that best describes your attendance:
	+ Option 1: Attended
	+ Option 2: Late
	+ Option 3: Absent

### Comments (`comments`, Text, Optional)
* Enter any additional comments or notes about your attendance.

### Email (`email`, Email, Optional)
* Enter your email address if you wish to be contacted for further information.

### Phone (`phone`, Text, Optional)
* Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted for further information.
