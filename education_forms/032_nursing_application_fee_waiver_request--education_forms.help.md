# Nursing Application Fee Waiver Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help nursing students apply for a fee waiver to reduce their financial burden when applying to a nursing program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please enter your contact information in the "Applicant Information" section.
2. Confirm your identity by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Applicant Confirmation" section.
3. If you need to attach any documents to support your fee waiver request, enter a description of the document in the "Document Upload" section. This field is optional.
4. Provide details about the document you uploaded in the "Document Details" section. This field is also optional.
5. Sign your application by entering your signature in the "Applicant Signature" section.
6. Select the reviewers who will review and verify your application, including Admissions and two other reviewers, by checking their names in the "Review and Verify" section.
7. If you have any additional notes or comments, you can enter them in the "Notes" section, which is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Information** (`applicant_information`, text, required): This is the section where you can enter your contact information, such as name, email address, and phone number.
* **Applicant Confirmation** (`applicant_confirmation`, select_one, required): Check "True" if you are the person applying for the nursing program, and "False" if you are not. This field is used to verify your identity.
* **Document Upload** (`document_upload`, text, optional): If you have a document that you want to attach to your application, enter a description of the document here. This field is only required if you upload a document.
* **Document Details** (`document_details`, text, optional): If you uploaded a document, provide details about the document, such as its title and date.
* **Applicant Signature** (`applicant_signature`, text, required): Sign your application by entering your name and date.
* **Review and Verify** (`review_and_verify`, select_multiple, required): Select the reviewers who will review and verify your application. Currently, Admissions and two other reviewers are required.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your application.
