# panhellenic_executive_board_application_2017 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for applicants to apply for a position on the Panhellenic Executive Board. The form collects personal information and supporting documents for review and evaluation by the selection committee.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name, email address, and contact number in the corresponding fields.
2. Upload your resume in the "Upload Resume" field.
3. Provide a brief description of the position you are applying for.
4. Click the "Submit" button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Application Form**: This is the main form where you can provide a brief description of your qualifications and experience.
* **Name**: Please fill in your full name as it should be displayed on your application and board materials.
* **Email**: Enter your active email address where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Cellular Number**: Provide your contact number where you can be reached in case of an emergency or further questions.
* **Number**: This field is likely for a specific position requirement (e.g. a specific phone extension), please fill in the required number as per the application guidelines.
* **Position Applied**: Describe the position you are applying for and why you are a good fit for it.
* **Upload Resume**: Attach a copy of your resume to support your application.
* **Signature**: This field is for digital signing of your application, please fill in your actual signature.

Note: Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely to ensure a successful submission.
