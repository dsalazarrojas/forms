# Education Magazine Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect requests for educational content from educators. By filling out this form, you can submit your proposal for consideration for publication in our education magazine.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Magaziner Request** (`magaziner_request`): Enter a brief title for your educational proposal.
2. **Educator Name** (`educator_name`): Enter your name as the submitter of the proposal.
3. **School Name** (`school_name`): If applicable, enter the name of the school or institution you are representing.
4. **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your proposal.
5. **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your proposal.
6. **Subject Area** (`subject_area`): Enter the subject area of your educational content, such as math, science, or language arts.
7. **Grade Level** (`grade_level`): Enter the grade level(s) that the educational content is intended for.
8. **Number of Students** (`number_of_students`): Enter the number of students that the content will be used with.
9. **Publication Frequency** (`publication_frequency`): Enter how often you would like to see the content published, such as weekly, bi-monthly, or one-time.
10. **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): If there's anything else you'd like to mention about your proposal, enter it here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Magaziner Request**: A brief title for your educational proposal.
* **Educator Name**: Your name as the submitter of the proposal.
* **School Name**: If applicable, enter the name of the school or institution you are representing. This field is not required, but if you are submitting on behalf of a school, please include it.
* **Email**: Your email address so we can contact you about your proposal.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number so we can contact you about your proposal.
* **Subject Area**: The subject area of your educational content, such as math, science, or language arts.
* **Grade Level**: The grade level(s) that the educational content is intended for.
* **Number of Students**: The number of students that the content will be used with.
* **Publication Frequency**: How often you would like to see the content published, such as weekly, bi-monthly, or one-time.
* **Additional Comments**: If there's anything else you'd like to mention about your proposal, enter it here.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* We will review your proposal and be in touch with you soon.
