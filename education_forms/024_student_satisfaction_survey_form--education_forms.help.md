This is a user-facing help guide for a student satisfaction survey form. The purpose of the form is to gather feedback from students about their experiences and perceptions of the school, its mission, and facilities. To complete this form, you will be guided through a series of questions that cover different aspects of the school's quality and overall environment. The form consists of several sections, each with a specific set of questions that help us understand student satisfaction.

## Student Satisfaction Survey Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from students about their experiences and perceptions of the school. Your input will help us understand areas of strength and weakness, and make informed decisions to improve the school's quality and overall student satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, please rate each of the following questions using the scale provided:
	* Mission and Vision: Rate how satisfied you are with the school's mission and vision.
	* Coursework: Rate the quality of the coursework you have experienced.
	* Education Quality: Rate the quality of education provided by the school.
	* Facilities: Rate the school's facilities.
	* Overall Feeling: Rate your overall feeling about the school.
2. If you have any suggestions or additional comments, please include them in the "Suggestions" text field.
3. Provide your email address for feedback and contact purposes.
4. Finally, if you have any other comments or feedback, please include them in the "Other Comments" text field.
5. Do not forget to enter your student ID for accurate record-keeping.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Mission and Vision**: Rate how satisfied you are with the school's mission and vision (Scale: 1-5).
- **Coursework: ** Rate the quality of the coursework you have experienced (Scale: 1-5).
- **Education Quality: ** Rate the quality of education provided by the school (Scale: 1-5).
- **Facilities: ** Rate the school's facilities (Scale: 1-5).
- **Overall Feeling: ** Rate your overall feeling about the school (Scale: 1-5).
- **Suggestions**: Include any suggestions you have for improving the school (text field).
- **Other Feedback**: Provide any other feedback or comments you have about the school (text field).
- **Email**: Please enter your email address for feedback and contact purposes (email field).
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number (text field).
- **Student Id**: Enter your student ID for accurate record-keeping (text field).
- **Other Comments**: Include any other comments or feedback you have about the school (text field).

## Tips
* Please rate each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any concerns or issues, please provide detailed feedback in the "Suggestions" and "Other Comments" fields.
* If you have any further questions or need clarification, please contact us through the email address you provided.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this form and provide your valuable feedback!
