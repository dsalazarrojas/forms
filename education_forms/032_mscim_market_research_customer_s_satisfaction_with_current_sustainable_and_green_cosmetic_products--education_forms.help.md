This form is designed to collect customer feedback on satisfaction with MscIM's sustainable and green cosmetic products.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  To complete this form, simply click on the "Submit" button at the end of the form.

3.  Make sure to provide honest and detailed feedback, as this will help us better understand your needs and improve our products and services.
4.  You can submit the form only once.
