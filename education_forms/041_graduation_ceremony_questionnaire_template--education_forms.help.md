# Graduation Ceremony Questionnaire Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Graduation Ceremony Questionnaire Template is a form designed to collect information from students about their graduation ceremony experience. This helps the university staff to gather feedback, identify patterns, and make data-driven decisions to improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Click on the first page, **Graduation Ceremony Details**.
2.  Fill out the form as completely as possible.
3.  Click on the next page, **Student Info**.
4.  Select the student's gender from the options provided.
5.  Click on the next page, **Graduation Year**.
6.  Enter the year the student graduated.
7.  Click on the next page, **Post Graduate Plan**.
8.  Fill out the student's post-graduation plan in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Graduation Ceremony Details**: Enter a brief description of the graduation ceremony experience.
* **Student Info**: Select the student's gender from the options provided. The options are Male, Female, and Other.
* **Graduation Year**: Enter the year the student graduated.
* **Post Graduate Plan**: Describe the student's post-graduation plan, including their plans for employment, further education, or other relevant information. 

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the **Graduation Ceremony Details** field.
* Make sure to select the correct gender for the **Student Info** field.
* Enter the exact year of graduation for the **Graduation Year** field.
* Be specific and clear when describing the **Post Graduate Plan** field.
