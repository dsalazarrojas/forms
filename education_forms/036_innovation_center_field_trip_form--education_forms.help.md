# Innovation Center Field Trip Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Innovation Center Field Trip Form is used to schedule and organize field trips for students to the Innovation Center. This form will be used by students, teachers, and parents to submit trip requests and receive confirmation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields (indicated by an asterisk*) with your information.
2. Select the grade level and class number for the student(s) attending the trip.
3. Confirm with your teacher that you are able to attend the trip.
4. Confirm with your parent/guardian that you are able to attend the trip.
5. Submit the form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Field Trip Form**: This field is used to describe the nature of the trip.
* **Innovation Center Name**: This field is used to identify the Innovation Center location.
* **School Name**: This field is used to identify the student's school.
* **Student's Name**: This field is used to identify the student's name.
* **Grade Level**: This field is used to select the grade level of the student(s) attending the trip.
* **Class Number**: This field is used to select the class number for the student(s) attending the trip.
* **Teacher's Name**: This field is used to identify the teacher's name who is accompanying the student(s) to the trip.
* **Teacher's Email**: This field is used to enter the teacher's email address.
* **Teacher's Phone**: This field is used to enter the teacher's phone number.
* **Date**: This field is used to select the date of the trip.
* **Time**: This field is used to select the time of the trip.
* **Note**: This field is used to add any additional notes about the trip.
* **Teacher's Notes**: This field is used to add any additional notes from the teacher.
* **Teacher Confirmation**: This field is used to confirm whether the teacher will be accompanying the student(s) to the trip.
* **Parent's Name**: This field is used to identify the parent's name who will be accompanying the student(s) to the trip.
* **Parent's Email**: This field is used to enter the parent's email address.
* **Parent's Phone**: This field is used to enter the parent's phone number.
* **Student Confirmation**: This field is used to confirm whether the student(s) will be attending the trip.

Note: The fields with an asterisk (*) are required fields that must be filled out in order to complete the form.</note>
