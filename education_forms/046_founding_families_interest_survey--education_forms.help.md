# Founding Families Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Founding Families Interest Survey is a form designed to collect information from school representatives about their interest in partnering with the Founding Families organization. This form helps the organization determine potential partnership opportunities and tailor their efforts to support these schools.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to understand the different sections and questions.
2. Choose the correct answer for each question based on your school's interest in partnering with the Founding Families organization.
3. Enter your full name, email, and contact phone number to help the organization contact you.
4. Provide your school's name, address, city, state, zip code, and country.
5. Share any additional involvement preferences or involvement details.
6. Review and agree to the consent terms.
7. Enter a message to the Founding Families organization, if desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Family Interest
* **Family Interest** (`family_interest`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose whether your school is **Interested** or **Not interested** in partnering with the Founding Families organization.

### Personal Information
* **Full name** (`full_name`, text, required: true): Enter your full name as a representative of your school.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: true): Enter your email address so the organization can contact you.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: true): Enter your contact phone number for easy communication.
* **School name** (`school_name`, text, required: true): Enter your school's name.
* **School email** (`school_email`, email, required: false): Optional field for your school's email address.
* **School phone** (`school_phone`, text, required: false): Optional field for your school's phone number.
* **School address** (`school_address`, text, required: false): Optional field for your school's address.
* **School city** (`school_city`, text, required: false): Optional field for your school's city.
* **School state** (`school_state`, text, required: false): Optional field for your school's state.
* **School zip** (`school_zip`, text, required: false): Optional field for your school's zip code.
* **School country** (`school_country`, text, required: false): Optional field for your school's country.
* **School region** (`school_region`, text, required: false): Optional field for your school's region.
* **School website** (`school_website`, text, required: false): Optional field for your school's website.
* **School facebook** (`school_facebook`, text, required: false): Optional field for your school's Facebook page.
* **School twitter** (`school_twitter`, text, required: false): Optional field for your school's Twitter page.
* **School youtube** (`school_youtube`, text, required: false): Optional field for your school's YouTube page.

### Additional Involvement Information
* **Involvement preferences** (`involvement_preferences`, select_one, required: false): Choose from **Parental involvement**, **Community involvement**, or **Other**.
* **Other involvement** (`involvement_other`, text, required: false): If you selected **Other**, describe your involvement preferences.
* **Student details** (`student_details`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose from **Student 1**, **Student 2**, or **Student 3**.
* **Guardian details** (`guardian_details`, text, required: false): Optional field for additional guardian information.
* **School message** (`school_message`, text, required: false): Optional field to share a message with the Founding Families organization.

### Consent
* **Consent** (`consent`, text, required: true): Agree to the terms of the consent by typing **Agree**.
* **Consent message** (`consent_message`, text, required: false): Optional field to share any additional consent information.
