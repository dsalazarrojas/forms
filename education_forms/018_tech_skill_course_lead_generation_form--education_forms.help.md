# Tech Skill Course Lead Generation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tech Skill Course Lead Generation Form is a tool used to gather information about courses in the technical skills category. It helps to generate leads and follow up with potential customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

* Fill out the form with the requested information.
* Make sure to provide all required fields.
* Double-check the fields before submission to avoid any errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: This is your first name.
* **Last Name**: This is your last name.
* **Email**: This is your email address.
* **Phone**: This is your phone number.
* **Course Title**: This is the title of the course you are offering.
* **Course Description**: Briefly describe the course you are offering.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: How would you like to be contacted (Phone Call, Email, or SMS)?
* **Preferred Language**: Choose your preferred language (English, Spanish, French, Chinese).
* **Location**: Enter your city, state, or zip code.
* **Interested in Follow-up**: If you want to be contacted for follow-up, choose 'True', otherwise choose 'False'.
* **Follow-up Frequency**: How often would you like to be contacted for follow-up (Once a week, Twice a week, Daily, Never).
* **Follow-up Message**: Leave a message to support faster follow-up (optional).
* **Follow-up Time**: Choose the time of day you would prefer to be contacted for follow-up.
* **Course Format**: Choose the format of the course (In-person, Online, Hybrid).
* **Course Level**: Choose the level of the course (Basic, Intermediate, Advanced).
* **Course Duration**: Choose the duration of the course (One week, Four weeks, Eight weeks, Longer than eight weeks).
* **Course Start Date**: Enter the start date of the course.
* **Course End Date**: Enter the end date of the course.
* **Course Start Time**: Choose the start time of the course.
* **Course End Time**: Choose the end time of the course.
* **Course Frequency**: How often the course will be run (Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly).
* **Course Time Zone**: Choose the time zone of the course (EST, PST, MST, CST).
* **Course Language**: Choose the language of the course (English, Spanish, French, Chinese).
* **Course Price**: Enter the price of the course.
* **Follow-up Method**: How would you like to be contacted for follow-up (Phone Call, Email, SMS)?

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check the form before submission.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, please contact our support team.
