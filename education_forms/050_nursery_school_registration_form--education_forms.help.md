# Nursery School Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information about a child's registration in a nursery school. It captures essential details such as the child's name, contact information, and educational background to ensure proper enrollment and subsequent progress monitoring. Please complete this form carefully to ensure accurate and complete information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (indicated by the asterisk * symbol).
2. Fill in any additional information as requested.
3. Review your submission for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Child Name** (child_name, text, *): Please enter the full name of the child registering for the nursery school.
* **Home Address** (home_address, text): Enter the child's current address.
* **Parent's Name** (parent_name, text): Enter the name of the parent or guardian.
* **Contact Number** (contact_number, text): Provide a contact number for the child's parent or guardian.
* **Date of Birth** (date_of_birth, date): Enter the child's date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2010-02-12).
* **Birthplace** (birthplace, text): Enter the city and state where the child was born.
* **School Name** (school_name, text): Enter the name of the school the child is enrolling in.
* **Grade Level** (grade_level, select_one, *): Select the child's grade level as per the given options. This will help in allocating them to the correct group.
* **Enrollment Date** (start_date, date): Enter the date the child is starting at the nursery school.
* **Graduation Date** (end_date, date): Enter the estimated date the child is graduating from the nursery school (if applicable).
* **Additional Comments** (notes, note): Enter any additional comments or information about the child's background or requirements.

## Tips
- Be accurate with the information you provide.
- If you are unsure about any information, please do not hesitate to ask for help.
- Review your submission carefully before submitting to ensure it is complete and accurate.

Please ensure you have filled in all required fields (marked * symbol) before submitting. If you have any concerns or need clarification on any field, please contact the relevant authorities.
