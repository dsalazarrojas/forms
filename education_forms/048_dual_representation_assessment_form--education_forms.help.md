# Dual Representation Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from participants about their experience with translation, including their ability to represent information correctly and their confidence level in their work. The form will help assess the participant's dual representation of information and provide data for research or feedback purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Select "Participant Information" page:** Click on the "Participant Information" button to access the participant information page.
2. Fill out the participant information fields: Please fill out the required fields as best you can. These fields are not optional.
3. **Select "Translation Format" field:** In the "Translation Format" section, select "Yes" if you translated information in the format required, or "No" otherwise.
4. **Select "Information Source" field:** In the "Information Source" section, select "Yes" if the information you translated came from the original source, or "No" otherwise.
5. **Select "Information Type" field:** In the "Information Type" section, select the types of information you translated (e.g., text, image, audio).
6. **Select "Information Translated" field:** In the "Information Translated" section, select "Yes" if you translated information, or "No" otherwise.
7. **Enter "Time Spent" field:** In the "Time Spent" section, enter the total time you spent on translating the information.
8. **Select "Difficulty Level" field:** In the "Difficulty Level" section, select the level of difficulty you experienced while translating the information (High or Low).
9. **Select "Correct Translations" field:** In the "Correct Translations" section, select "Yes" if the translations were correct, or "No" otherwise.
10. **Select "Incorrect Translations" field:** In the "Incorrect Translations" section, select "Yes" if there were any incorrect translations, or "No" otherwise.
11. **Enter "Total Time Spent" field:** In the "Total Time Spent" section, enter the total time you spent on translating the information.
12. **Select "Confidence Level" field:** In the "Confidence Level" section, select the level of confidence you have in your translations (High or Low).
13. **Select "Correct Time Spent" field:** In the "Correct Time Spent" section, select "Yes" if you spent the correct amount of time on translating the information, or "No" otherwise. (Note: This field is not required.)
14. **Add comments:** In the "Comments" section, you can add any additional comments about your experience with translation.
15. **Select "Comments Translations" field:** In the "Comments Translations" section, select "Yes" if you translated any comments, or "No" otherwise. (Note: This field is not required.)
16. **Add additional comments:** In the "Additional Comments" section, you can add any additional comments about your experience with translation. (Note: This field is not required.)
