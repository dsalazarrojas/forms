# Parent Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parent Perception Survey is a tool for parents to express their opinions and concerns about their school. Your input will help the school administration understand your views and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the option that best represents your opinion or experience for each question. You can select only one option for single-select questions and multiple options for multi-select questions. If a question is not applicable, please select "Not applicable" or leave it blank.
