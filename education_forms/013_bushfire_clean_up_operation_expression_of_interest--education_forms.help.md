# Bushfire Clean Up Operation Expression Of Interest - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for individuals who want to volunteer for a bushfire clean-up operation. The form collects information about your identity, availability, experience, and emergency contact information to ensure you are adequately prepared and qualified to participate in the operation.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Read the form carefully**: Before filling out the form, make sure you have enough time to complete it accurately.
2.  **Fill out the required fields**: All the fields marked as "true" (e.g. Full Name, Primary Phone Number, Email Address, etc.) are required and must be filled out.
3.  **Choose your availability**: Select your available days for the operation in the "Days Available for Operations" field.
4.  **Describe your experience**: If you have experience with disaster clean-up operations, describe it in the "Previous Experience with Disaster Clean-up" field.
5.  **Assess your fitness level**: Select your physical fitness level in the "Physical Fitness Level" field.
6.  **Check your PPE availability**: Select the PPE you have available in the "Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Available" field.
7.  **Provide emergency contact information**: Fill out the "Emergency Contact Name and Phone" field with your emergency contact's name and phone number.
8.  **Agree to undergo safety training**: Select "I Agree" in the "I agree to undergo mandatory on-site safety training" field.
9.  **Submit your form**: Once you have filled out the form accurately, submit it to express your interest in participating in the bushfire clean-up operation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name**: Your full name as it appears on your identification.
*   **Affiliate Organization**: If you are representing an organization, please fill out your affiliate organization (e.g. NGO, Corporate Team, Community Group).
*   **Primary Phone Number**: Your primary contact number.
*   **Email Address**: Your email address.
*   **Days Available for Operations**: Select all the days you are available to participate in the operation.
*   **Previous Experience with Disaster Clean-up**: Describe any relevant past experience with disaster clean-up operations.
*   **Physical Fitness Level**: Select your physical fitness level:
    *   High (Can lift 50lbs+, walk long distances)
    *   Moderate (Can lift 25lbs, moderate activity)
    *   Light (Administrative or light clearing only)
*   **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Available**: Select the PPE you have available:
    *   Steel-toed Boots
    *   Heavy Duty Gloves
    *   N95 or P2 Masks
    *   Safety Glasses/Goggles
    *   High-Visibility Vest
*   **Emergency Contact Name and Phone**: Your emergency contact's name and phone number.
*   **I agree to undergo mandatory on-site safety training**: Select "I Agree" to confirm you understand the importance of safety training.
*   **Date of Expression of Interest**: The date you are submitting this form to express your interest.
