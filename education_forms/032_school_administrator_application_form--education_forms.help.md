**school_administrator_application_form - Help Guide**
## Purpose
The school administrator application form is designed to collect information from potential school administrators who are interested in applying for a position at our institution. This form will help us understand your background, qualifications, and fit for the role.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your basic information, including your name and contact details.
2. Provide your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
3. Share your education information, including your qualifications and relevant experience.
4. List your work experience and relevant skills.
5. Select your relevant education background.
6. Enter your job title and description.
7. Provide any required documentation (e.g., resume, transcripts).
8. (Optional) List a second job title and description if applicable.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Basic Information**: 
  * **Name** (Text): This is the first field where you can enter your name.
  * **Email** (Text, Optional): Please enter your email address so we can contact you.
  * **Phone Number** (Text, Optional): This is where you enter your phone number.
* **Contact Information**: 
  * **Address** (Text, Optional): You can enter your home or office address.
  * **Phone Number** (Text, Optional): This is where you enter your phone number (if different from the previous field).
  * **Contact Preferences** (Text, Optional): You can specify how you prefer to be contacted (e.g., email, phone, or mail).
* **Education Information**: 
  * **Field of Study** (Select One, Optional): Please select the relevant field of study for your degree.
  * **Degree** (Select One, Optional): Choose your degree level (e.g., Bachelor, Master, Ph.D.).
  * **University Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of your university.
  * **Relevant Coursework** (Text, Optional): If applicable, share any relevant coursework or specializations you have.
* **Work Experience**: 
  * **Job Title** (Text, Optional): If you have a job title, enter it here.
  * **Job Description** (Text, Optional): Describe your job role and responsibilities.
  * **Job Duration** (Text, Optional): If applicable, specify the dates you worked in that role.
  * **Job Location** (Text, Optional): Enter the location of your workplace.
* **Skills**: 
  * **Select Multiple Skills** 
    + **Option 1** (Select): Check the relevant skills that apply to you.
    + **Option 2** (Select): Check another relevant skill.
    + **Option 3** (Select): Check another relevant skill.
* **Education**: 
  * **Select One Relevant Education Background** 
    + **Option 1** (Select): Choose the relevant education background for the role.
    + **Option 2** (Select): Choose another relevant education background.
    + **Option 3** (Select): Choose another relevant education background.
* **Job Title 1**: 
  * **Job Title** (Text, Optional): If you have multiple job titles, enter them here.
  * **Job Description** (Text, Optional): Describe your role and responsibilities.
* **Job Description 1**: 
  * **Job Description** (Text, Optional): Enter a description of your job responsibilities.
* **Required Documentation**: 
  * **Documentation** (Text, Optional): Attach or link any required documentation (e.g., resume, transcripts).

**Tips**

* Please fill out as much information as you can, but do not worry if you don't have all the details.
* If you need help with anything, do not hesitate to contact us.
* We will be in touch soon after you submit your form.
