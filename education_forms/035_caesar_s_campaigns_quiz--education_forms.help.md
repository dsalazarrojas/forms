# Caesar s Campaigns Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a quiz designed to assess your knowledge about Julius Caesar's military campaigns.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the "Student name" field with your full name.
2. If you're part of a class or group, enter the relevant identifier in the "Class or group" field (optional).
3. Choose the correct year for the "Key event year" question.
4. Select the primary opponent for the "Major opponent" question.
5. Select all the regions where Caesar's campaign took place for the "Campaign region" question.
6. You can optionally enter your score and feedback in the "Score" and "Feedback" fields.
7. Finally, estimate the time taken to complete the quiz in the "Completion time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Class or group** (`class`, text, optional): If you're part of a class or group, enter the corresponding identifier.
* **Key event year** (`q1`, select_one, required): Choose the year of the key event in Caesar's campaign.
	+ Select 49 BC to choose the year 49 BC.
	+ Select 45 BC to choose the year 45 BC.
	+ Select 44 BC to choose the year 44 BC.
* **Major opponent** (`q2`, select_one, required): Select the primary opponent of Caesar's campaign.
	+ Select Pompey to choose Pompey as the primary opponent.
	+ Select Cassius to choose Cassius as the primary opponent.
	+ Select Antony to choose Antony as the primary opponent.
* **Campaign region** (`q3`, select_multiple, required): Select all the regions where Caesar's campaign took place.
	+ Select Gaul to choose Gaul as one of the regions.
	+ Select Britain to choose Britain as one of the regions.
	+ Select Egypt to choose Egypt as one of the regions.
* **Score** (`score`, number, optional): Optionally enter your score.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Provide feedback or comments for the instructor.
* **Completion time** (`completion_time`, time, optional): Estimate the time taken to complete the quiz.
