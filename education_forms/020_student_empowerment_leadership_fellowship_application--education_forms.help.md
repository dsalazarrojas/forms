# Student Empowerment Leadership Fellowship Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Empowerment Leadership Fellowship Application form is designed to collect information from students applying for the Student Empowerment Leadership Fellowship program. This form will gather details about the students' background, interests, and qualifications for the fellowship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and understand the requirements for each field.
2. Fill in your student details accurately and completely.
3. Ensure you provide correct information for each field, paying attention to the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <label> Student Details (id: 1)
* **Student Details** (text): This field is an optional text field where you can provide any additional information about yourself that is not captured in other fields.

### <label> Student Name (id: 2)
* **Student Name** (text): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.

### <label> Student Email (id: 3)
* **Student Email** (email): Enter a valid email address that you regularly check. This is not a required field.

### <label> Student Phone (id: 4)
* **Student Phone** (text): Provide your contact phone number for any follow-up communication.

### <label> Student Address (id: 5)
* **Student Address** (note): Enter your current address. This field is optional as it will only be used for internal purposes.

### <label> Student Degree (id: 6)
* **Student Degree** (select_one): Select the degree you are currently pursuing or have completed. Options include:
	+ Bachelor's
	+ Master's
	+ Phd

### <label> Student Year (id: 7)
* **Student Year** (number): Enter the year you are currently in or have completed for your degree.

### <label> Student Program (id: 8)
* **Student Program** (select_multiple): Select the area of study you are enrolled in. Options include:
	+ Computer Science
	+ Engineering
	+ Arts

### <label> Student Interests (id: 9)
* **Student Interests** (note): Enter any relevant interests or activities that align with the fellowship program. This field is optional.

### <label> Application Date (id: 10)
* **Application Date** (date): Enter the date you submit this application. This field will be auto-filled with the current date.

### <label> Application Time (id: 11)
* **Application Time** (time): Enter the time you submit this application. This field will be auto-filled with the current time.

### <label> Student References (id: 12)
* **Student References** (select_multiple): Select the number of references you will be providing. Options include:
	+ 1
	+ 2
	+ 3

## Tips

* Ensure you review the form carefully before submitting.
* Double-check the accuracy of all fields before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to us.
