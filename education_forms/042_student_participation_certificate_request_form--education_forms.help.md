# Student Participation Certificate Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Participation Certificate Request Form is used by students to request a certificate that confirms their participation in a course, program, or event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your Student Name, Student ID, and Course information accurately.
2. If applicable, enter your Date of Participation, Time of Participation, and Certificate Type details.
3. Specify the Certificate Quantity and Format you require.
4. Choose your Certificate Delivery Method.
5. Enter your Student Email and Institution Email for contact purposes.
6. Provide your Student Phone and Institution Phone numbers for communication.
7. Enter any additional Certificate Request Details, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, Text, Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, Text, Required): Enter your student identification number or ID card number.
* **Course** (`course`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the course you participated in.
* **Institution** (`institution`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the institution where you participated.
* **Date of Participation** (`date_of_participation`, Date, Optional): If applicable, enter the date you participated in the course.
* **Time of Participation** (`time_of_participation`, Time, Optional): If applicable, enter the time you participated in the course.
* **Certificate Request Details** (`certificate_request_details`, Text, Optional): Provide any additional information about your participation or certificate request.
* **Certificate Type** (`certificate_type`, Text, Optional): Specify the type of certificate you require (e.g., academic, certification, etc.).
* **Certificate Quantity** (`certificate_quantity`, Number, Optional): Enter the number of certificates you require.
* **Certificate Format** (`certificate_format`, Text, Optional): Specify the format of the certificate (e.g., PDF, printed, etc.).
* **Certificate Delivery Method** (`certificate_delivery_method`, Text, Optional): Choose how you would like to receive your certificate (e.g., email, mail, etc.).
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, Email, Optional): Enter your student email address for contact purposes.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your student phone number for communication.
* **Institution Email** (`institution_email`, Email, Optional): Enter the email address of your institution for contact purposes.
* **Institution Phone** (`institution_phone`, Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of your institution for communication.
