# Dance School Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to enroll a student in dance classes at our school. Please provide the required information for the student, emergency contact, and any medical information we need.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start.
2. Fill in the student's information in the corresponding fields, following the instructions below for each field:
3. Make sure to provide all required information for the student and emergency contact fields.
4. Review your input carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, **text**, required): Enter the student's full name as it appears on their official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the student's email address if you have one.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the student's phone number if you have one.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of an emergency contact person for the student.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_one, optional): Select how the emergency contact person is related to the student (e.g. Parent, Guardian, Sibling).
* **Class Choice** (`class_choice`, select_one, required): Select the type of dance class the student will be enrolling in (e.g. ballet, jazz, contemporary, hip hop).
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the student will start attending classes.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the student will end attending classes.
* **Student Age** (`student_age`, number, optional): Enter the student's age.
* **Student Gender** (`student_gender`, select_one, required): Select the student's gender.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`emergency_contact_phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact person's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the emergency contact person's email address.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_one, required): Select how the emergency contact person is related to the student (e.g. Parent, Guardian, Sibling).
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): List any medical conditions the student may have.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): List any allergies the student may have.
* **Special Needs** (`special_needs`, text, optional): Provide any special needs information for the student.
* **Doctor's Name** (`doctor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the student's doctor.
* **Doctor's Phone Number** (`doctor_phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the doctor's phone number.
* **Doctor's Relationship** (`doctor_relationship`, select_one, required): Select how the doctor is related to the student (e.g. Parent, Emergency Contact).
* **Doctor's License Number** (`doctor_license_number`, text, optional): Enter the doctor's license number.
* **Doctor's Specialization** (`doctor_specialization`, text, optional): Enter the doctor's specialization.
* **Doctor's Clinic** (`doctor_clinic`, text, optional): Enter the doctor's clinic name.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact person's address.
* **Emergency Contact City** (`emergency_contact_city`, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact person's city.

## Tips
* Review your input carefully before submitting the form to ensure you have filled in all required fields and have accurate information.
* If you are unsure of any field, please contact the school administration for guidance.
