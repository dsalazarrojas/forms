# music_school_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from students applying to a music school.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields.
2. Provide accurate and valid information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it is written on your identification document.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your surname as it is written on your identification document.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format "dd/mm/yyyy".
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required=false): Enter your home or office address.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required=false): Enter your contact number (phone or cell number).
* **Music Instruments** (`music_instruments`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the music instruments you are skilled in.
* **Music Style** (`music_style`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred music style.
* **Message** (`message`, `text`, required=false): Enter any additional information or comments you have.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you agree to the terms and conditions of the music school.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
* Double-check that your email address is valid and your contact number is correct.
* Be honest in selecting your music style and instruments you are skilled in.
* If you have any additional information or comments, please enter it in the "Message" field.
* Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the music school.
