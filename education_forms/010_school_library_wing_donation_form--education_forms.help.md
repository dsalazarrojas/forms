# school_library_wing_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The school library wing donation form is used to collect information about school library wing donations. This form is used by school administrators or librarians to document and track donations received for a specific school project or library wing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `Donor Name`, `Title`, and `School Name` fields with the relevant details of the donor and school.
2. Enter the donor's `Email`, `Phone`, and `Address` for communication purposes.
3. Select the `Donor Recognition` and `Recognition Level` for the donation.
4. Enter the `Project Description` and `Project Status` details.
5. Optionally, add the `Project Update` and `Donation Amount` information.
6. Enter school address details, including `School Zip`, `School City`, `School District`, `School Country`, and `School Region`.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title**: Enter a brief description of the donation, e.g., "Library Wing Donation - [Donor Name]".
* **Donor Name**: Enter the name of the donor, e.g., "John Doe".
* **Title**: Enter a brief title of the donor, e.g., "Donor Title".
* **Donor Name**: Enter the name of the donor, e.g., "John Doe".
* **Email**: Enter the donor's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the donor's phone number.
* **Donor Address**: Enter the donor's address.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of the school where the donation is being made.
* **Project Description**: Enter a brief description of the project being funded.
* **Project Description**: Enter a detailed description of the project being funded.
* **Donor Recognition**: Select "Yes" if you want the donor to be recognized for their donation, and "No" otherwise.
* **Recognition Level**: Select the level of recognition you want to provide for the donor, e.g., "High" for prominent recognition and "Low" for less prominent recognition.
* **Project Update**: Enter any additional information about the project.
* **Project Status**: Select the status of the project, e.g., "Active" if the project is ongoing and "Inactive" if completed.
* **Donation Amount**: Enter the amount of the donation made.
* **School Zip**: Enter the zip code of the school's location.
* **School City**: Enter the city of the school's location.
* **School District**: Enter the school's district.
* **School Country**: Enter the country where the school is located.
* **School Region**: Enter the region where the school is located.
