# Teaching Aspirations Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Teaching Aspirations Survey is a tool for collecting data about education professionals' aspirations and goals. By answering this form, you will be helping to improve support and resources for professionals in the field of education.

## How To Complete This Form

* Take a few minutes to review the form questions carefully.
* Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
* You may skip questions that you don't have information about.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (<name>, <type>, required/optional):** Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (<name>, <type>, required/optional):** Enter your last name.
* **Job Satisfaction (<name>, <type>, required/optional):**: On a scale of 1-5, rate how satisfied you are with your job. (Required)
* **Career Goals (<name>, <type, required/optional)::** Describe your long-term career goals.
* **Educational Goals (<name>, <type, required/optional)::** Describe your educational goals and aspirations.
* **Job Satisfaction Level (<name>, <type, required/optional):** Select the level of job satisfaction:
  • Very Dissatisfied
  • Somewhat Dissatisfied
  • Neutral
  • Somewhat Satisfied
  • Very Satisfied
* **Job Satisfaction Frequency (<name>, <type, required/optional):** Select the frequency of your job satisfaction:
  • Daily
  • Weekly
  • Monthly
  • Rarely
* **Career Satisfaction (<name>, <type, required/optional):** On a scale of 1-5, rate your career satisfaction.
* **Career Goals Level (<name>, <type, required/optional)::** Describe your career goals level.
* **Educational Goals Level (<name>, <type, required/optional)::** Describe your educational goals level.
* **How Does Job Satisfaction Impact Career Goals (<name>, <type, required/optional):** Select a date for when you first started feeling job satisfaction.
* **Email (<name>, <type, required/optional):** Enter your email address.
* **Phone (<name>, <type, required/optional):** Enter your phone number.
* **Institution (<name>, <type, required/optional):** Enter the institution you work for.
* **Department (<name>, <type, required/optional)::** Enter the department you work in.
* **Job Title (<name>, <type, required/optional):** Enter your job title.
* **Job Role (<name>, <type, required/optional):** Enter your job role.
* **Job Satisfaction Impact Reason (<name>, <type, required/optional):** Explain how your job satisfaction impacts your career goals.
* **Educational Background (<name>, <type, required/optional):** Describe your educational background.
* **Years of Service (<name>, <type, required/optional)::** On a scale of 1-5, rate your years of service.
* **Years of Service Level (<name>, <type, required/optional):** Select the level of years of service:
  • Less than 1 year
  • 1-5 years
  • 5-10 years
  • More than 10 years
* **Years of Service Frequency (<name>, <type, required/optional):** Select the frequency of years of service:
  • Less than 1 year
  • 1-5 years
  • 5-10 years
* **Years of Service Why (<name>, <type, required/optional):** Explain why you've been in your job for the given number of years.
* **Institution Experience (<name>, <type, required/optional):** Describe your experience in the institution.
* **Department Experience (<name>, <type, required/optional):** Describe your experience in the department.
