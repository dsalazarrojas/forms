# educationalNeuroscienceExplorationRegistrationForm - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants of an educational neuroscience exploration program. It is intended to gather demographic details, preferences, and other relevant information to facilitate the program's administration and organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
2. Select the correct option for the Registration Form Submit field.
3. Review your input before submission to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (1)**: Enter your full name in the format "First Name Last Name". 
* **Email (2)**: Enter your email address. This will be used for correspondence and updates.
* **Phone (3)**: Enter your phone number. This will help us reach you if needed.
* **Date (4)**: Select your preferred date of participation. This is a required field.
* **Time (5)**: Select your preferred time of participation. This field is required.
* **Duration (6)**: Enter the expected duration of your participation. This field is required.
* **Location (7)**: Enter the location where you will be participating (optional).
* **Note (8)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your participation (optional).
* **Registration Form Submit (9)**: Choose how you would like to submit this form (e.g. Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any issues or questions while completing the form, contact the program organizers.
* Submission of this form will be considered as consent to participate in the educational neuroscience exploration program.
