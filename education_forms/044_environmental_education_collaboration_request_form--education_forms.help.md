# Environmental Education Collaboration Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used for schools to request environmental education collaborations. It is intended to provide information about their collaboration interests and needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your school's name
2. Enter your school's address
3. Enter your email address
4. Enter your phone number
5. Enter the contact person's name
6. Enter your school's organization
7. Select the type of collaboration you are interested in
8. Enter any specific interests or needs for the collaboration
9. Enter the partnership goals for the collaboration
10. Enter the expected duration of the partnership
11. Enter the start date of the partnership
12. Enter the end date of the partnership (if applicable)
13. Indicate if you want to be followed up on (yes/no)
14. Enter any additional comments
15. Enter your name
16. Enter your email address
17. Enter your phone number
18. Enter your department
19. Enter your position
20. Enter your organization
21. Enter any additional comments
22. Enter your school's email address
23. Enter your school's phone number
24. Enter your school's department
25. Enter any additional comments

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your school.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, text, required): Enter your school's address.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required): Enter your school's organization.
* **Collaboration Type** (`collaboration_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of collaboration you are interested in.
* **Specific Interests** (`specific_interests`, text, optional): Enter any specific interests or needs for the collaboration.
* **Partnership Goals** (`partnership_goals`, text, required): Enter the partnership goals for the collaboration.
* **Partnership Duration** (`partnership_duration`, time, required): Enter the expected duration of the partnership.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the partnership.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the partnership (if applicable).
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if you want to be followed up on (yes/no).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Submitter Department** (`submitter_department`, text, optional): Enter your department.
* **Submitter Position** (`submitter_position`, text, optional): Enter your position.
* **Submitter Organization** (`submitter_organization`, text, optional): Enter your organization.
* **Submitter Comments** (`submitter_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, email, optional): Enter your school's email address.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, text, optional): Enter your school's phone number.
* **School Department** (`school_department`, text, optional): Enter your school's department.
* **School Comments** (`school_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments.
