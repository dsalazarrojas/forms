# Vote Exam Date Flat Color - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on exam dates and color preferences. It is intended for educational surveys or assessments within the "education_forms" category.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Go through each page of the form, which consists of three fields.
* Fill in each field according to your preference or information:
  * **exam_date**: Enter the actual exam date.
  * **background_color**: Select a background color for the exam.
  * **selected_color**: Choose one or multiple preferred colors for the exam.
* Make sure to review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the exam date** (`exam_date`, `date`, not required): This field is used to enter the actual date of the exam. Enter a date in a standard format (e.g., DD/MM/YYYY or MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Flat color** (`background_color`, `select_one`, not required): Choose a flat background color for the exam. Select one of the provided options: White, Blue, Red, Green, Yellow, Black, Purple, or Pink.
* **Selected color** (`selected_color`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select one or multiple preferred colors for the exam. You can choose from the same options as the "Flat color" field.
 
## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields (although none are required in this form).
- Review your answers carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and consistency.
- If you experience any issues or have questions, contact form@gic.mx.
