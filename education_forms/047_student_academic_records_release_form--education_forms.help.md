# Student Academic Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about who you want to grant access to your academic records and what specific records you are willing to release. This helps maintain your data privacy while allowing authorized parties to access relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you want to submit this form.
2. Choose the person or organization you want to grant access to your academic records (Family Member, School, University, or Other).
3. Select the specific academic records you are willing to release (Transcript, Letter of Good Standing, or Other Academic Records).
4. Confirm your decision to release your academic records by selecting "True" or "False".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Submission** (`form_submission`, select multiple, optional): This section allows you to decide whether you want to submit this form at all. If you select "Yes", you can proceed with the next sections.
* **Who do you want to give access to your academic records to?** (`recipient_selection`, select multiple, optional): Here, you can choose the person or organization to grant access to your academic records (Family Member, School, University, or Other). Be cautious in your selection as this will determine who can view your academic records.
* **What information do you want to release?** (`release_scope`, select multiple, optional): Specify what academic records you are willing to share. This could be your transcript, letter of good standing, or other relevant records.
* **Confirm Release** (`form_submission`, select one, optional): Confirm your decision to release your academic records by selecting "True" to proceed or "False" to cancel the release. This is the final step to finalize your choice.
