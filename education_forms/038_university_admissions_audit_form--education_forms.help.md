# University Admissions Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Admissions Audit Form is used to track and review university applications, providing a structured way to collect information related to applicants' progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options for "Applicant Files" from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter the student's name in the "student_name" field.
3. Enter the program name the student applied for in the "program_name" field.
4. Select the date of application from the calendar or enter it manually in the "date_of_application" field.
5. Choose the current status of the application ("Active" or "Inactive") in the "application_status" field.
6. Enter any review notes related to the application in the "review_notes" field.
7. Enter the name of the reviewer who is reviewing the application in the "reviewer_name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Applicant Files
* **Applicant Files** (`applicant_files`, select_multiple, required: false)
    * Select all the files associated with the applicant, such as transcripts, letters of recommendation, and test scores.

### 2. Student Name
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required: false)
    * Enter the full name of the student, including their first and last name.

### 3. Program Name
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, required: false)
    * Enter the name of the academic program the student applied for, such as a specific major or degree.

### 4. Date of Application
* **Date of Application** (`date_of_application`, date, required: false)
    * Enter the date the student submitted their application.

### 5. Application Status
* **Application Status** (`application_status`, select_one, required: false)
    * Choose the current status of the application, either "Active" if the application is still being reviewed or "Inactive" if it has been closed.

### 6. Review Notes
* **Review Notes** (`review_notes`, text, required: false)
    * Enter any notes or comments related to the review process, such as additional information needed or concerns.

### 7. Reviewer Name
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, text, required: false)
    * Enter the name of the person reviewing the application.

### 8. Tips
* Make sure to review and update the application status regularly to reflect changes.
* Keep the review notes up-to-date and concise to facilitate efficient communication.
* Use the applicant files field to track all associated documents, such as transcripts and test scores.
