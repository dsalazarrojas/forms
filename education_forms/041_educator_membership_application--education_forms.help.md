# Educator Membership Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educator Membership Application form is designed for educators or educational institutions to apply for a membership with our organization. This form helps us understand your qualifications, background, and requirements to process your membership application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User Information: Enter your name, as the primary contact person for your membership application.
2. Provide your Contact Information: Enter your organization's name, address, and email address.
3. Fill in your Membership Information: Select your membership type (Individual, Organization, or Institutional), category (General, Premium, or Basic), level (Silver, Gold, or Platinum), and term (Monthly or Yearly).
4. Upload any required documents (if necessary): Attach relevant documents to support your membership application.
5. Review your membership status: This field will display the status of your membership application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (User)**: Enter your name as the primary contact person for your membership application.
* **Contact Information (Contact Information)**: Enter your organization's name, address, and email address.
* **Membership Information (Membership Type)**: Choose the type of membership you are applying for (Individual, Organization, or Institutional).
* **Membership Information (Membership Category)**: Select the category of membership you are interested in (General, Premium, or Basic).
* **Membership Information (Membership Level)**: Choose the level of membership you want to achieve (Silver, Gold, or Platinum).
* **Membership Information (Membership Term)**: Select the term of your membership (Monthly or Yearly).
* **Upload Documents (Upload Documents)**: Attach relevant documents to support your membership application.
* **Review Status (Review Status)**: This field will display the status of your membership application.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone (Phone)**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Address (Address)**: Enter your organization's address.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any supporting documents, please upload them to the "Upload Documents" field.
* Our team will review your membership application and contact you with any questions or concerns.
* You can contact us with any questions or clarifications by using the email address provided.
