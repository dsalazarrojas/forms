# soil_science_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to test your knowledge on soil science, covering various aspects of soil properties, processes, and conservation. The quiz is divided into different sections, each focusing on a specific topic related to soil science.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the introduction to understand the basics of soil science.
2. Complete each section by selecting the correct options or providing a short description in the text fields.
3. Make sure to review each question carefully and choose the most accurate answer.
4. When filling out the text fields, provide clear and concise descriptions.
5. For multiple-choice questions, select all the correct options that apply.
6. Once you have completed all the sections, review your answers and make any necessary corrections.
7. Finally, summarize your learning in the conclusion section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **introduction** (`introduction`, text, optional): This section is an introduction to the quiz, providing background information on soil science.
* **soil types** (`soil_types`, select_one, optional): Choose the correct type of soil from the available options: sand, silt, or clay.
* **soil composition** (`soil_composition`, text, optional): Describe the primary component of soil that affects its structure.
* **soil processes** (`soil_processes`, select_one, optional): Choose the process by which soil loses its nutrients: leaching, erosion, or weathering.
* **soil properties** (`soil_properties`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the relationships between pH and soil fertility: 
  - pH affects soil fertility
  - pH does not affect soil fertility
  - pH affects soil structure
  - pH does not affect soil structure
* **conservation** (`conservation`, select_one, optional): Choose the primary goal of soil conservation: 
  - prevent soil erosion
  - prevent soil erosion and improve fertility
  - improve soil fertility
* **fertility** (`fertility`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the factors that affect soil fertility: 
  - organic matter
  - pH
  - water content
  - temperature
* **microbiology** (`microbiology`, text, optional): Describe the role of microorganisms in soil formation.
* **erosion** (`erosion`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the causes of soil erosion: 
  - water erosion
  - wind erosion
  - tillage
  - deforestation
* **water** (`water`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the importance of water in soil formation: 
  - water affects soil formation
  - water does not affect soil formation
  - water affects soil fertility
  - water does not affect soil fertility
* **nutrients** (`nutrients`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the essential nutrients for plant growth: 
  - nitrogen
  - phosphorus
  - potassium
  - magnesium
* **conclusion** (`conclusion`, note, optional): Summarize what you have learned from this quiz.

## Tips
