# Poster Competition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Poster Competition Form is designed to collect data for a poster competition. This form is intended for students who wish to participate in the competition.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the required fields as accurately and correctly.
2.  Ensure that the student filling out the form is the correct person and not a duplicate submission.
3.  Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: The student's name who is submitting their poster.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: Yes
* **Institution**: The student's institution where they are studying.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: Yes
* **Field of Study**: The field of study that the student is pursuing.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: Yes
* **Title of Poster**: A brief title of the student's poster.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: Yes
* **Date Posted**: The date when the student's poster was posted.
	+ Type: Date
	+ Required: Yes
* **Time Submitted**: The time when the student's poster was submitted.
	+ Type: Time
	+ Required: Yes
* **Concept**: A brief description of the student's poster concept.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: Yes
* **Poster Content**: A description of the student's poster content.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: Yes
* **Uploaded Files**: This field is for uploading additional files related to the student's poster (optional).
	+ Type: Note
	+ Required: No
* **Email**: The student's email address.
	+ Type: Email
	+ Required: Yes
* **Phone**: The student's phone number (optional).
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
* **Submit**: Indicate that the form has been submitted.
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Required: Yes

Note: The form submission status will be indicated by the "Submit" button.
