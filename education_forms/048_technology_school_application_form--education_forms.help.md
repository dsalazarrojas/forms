# Technology School Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is meant for students to submit their applications to the Technology School. Please fill in the required information to complete your application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Read the form carefully and understand its purpose and fields.
2.  Fill in all the fields that are marked as "required" to ensure your application is complete.
3.  Double-check your email address for accuracy to ensure that the correct information is sent to the relevant authorities.
4.  Review your application before submitting for any errors or completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Application Form (Option 1)**: Select the relevant option from the list that best describes your application.
* **Student Name**: Fill in your real name as it should be on official records.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address to ensure that you receive any updates or communications related to your application.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number in the format of XXX-XXX-XXXX, for example, 123-456-7890.
* **Date**: Select the date in the format of MM/DD/YYYY, for example, 02/28/2022.
* **Time**: Select the time in the format of HH:MM, for example, 08:00.
* **Note**: Enter any additional information that you want to share with the relevant authorities.

Note: The "Application Form" field is a select_one field, meaning you can only choose one option from the list. Make sure to choose the option that best describes your application.
