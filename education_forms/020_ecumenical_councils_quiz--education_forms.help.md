# Ecumenical Councils Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about ecumenical councils quizzes, which are a crucial component of academic and professional development. The form is used to track and record the progress of quizzes conducted by instructors or facilitators.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter a brief introduction to the quiz (Field 1)
2.  Answer the first question (Field 2)
3.  Answer the second question (Field 3)
4.  Add any additional comments or remarks from the instructor (Field 5)
5.  Enter the participant's name (Field 6)
6.  Enter the participant's email address (Field 7)
7.  Enter the participant's phone number (Field 8)
8.  Enter the participant's location (Field 9)
9.  Add any additional notes about the participant (Field 10)
10. Enter the instructor's name (Field 11)
11. Enter the instructor's title (Field 12)
12. Enter the instructor's email address (Field 13)
13. Enter the instructor's phone number (Field 14)
14. Enter the instructor's location (Field 15)
15. Add any additional comments from the instructor (Field 16)
16. Enter the date (Field 17)
17. Enter the time (Field 18)
18. Select the type of quiz (Field 19)
19. Select the questions that were included in the quiz (Field 20)
20. Add any additional comments about the quiz (Field 21)
21. Enter any comments from the instructor (Field 22)
22. Mark the status of the quiz as completed or in progress (Fields 23 and 24)
23. Select the action to take with the quiz (Field 25)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Introduction** (Field 1):
    \*\* Enter a brief description of the quiz.
*   **Question 1** (Field 2): Answer the first question of the quiz.
*   **Question 2** (Field 3): Answer the second question of the quiz.
*   **Instructors Comments** (Field 5): Add any additional comments or remarks from the instructor.
*   **Participant Name** (Field 6): Enter the participant's name.
*   **Participant Email** (Field 7): Enter the participant's email address.
*   **Participant Phone** (Field 8): Enter the participant's phone number.
*   **Participant Location** (Field 9): Enter the participant's location.
*   **Participant Notes** (Field 10): Add any additional notes about the participant.
*   **Instructors Name** (Field 11): Enter the instructor's name.
*   **Instructors Title** (Field 12): Enter the instructor's title.
*   **Instructors Email** (Field 13): Enter the instructor's email address.
*   **Instructors Phone** (Field 14): Enter the instructor's phone number.
*   **Instructors Location** (Field 15): Enter the instructor's location.
*   **Instructors Comments** (Field 16): Add any additional comments from the instructor.
*   **Date** (Field 17): Enter the date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
*   **Time** (Field 18): Enter the time in 24-hour format (hh:mm).
*   **Quiz Type** (Field 19): Choose the type of quiz (Multiple Choice, True and False, Fill in the Blank, Essay, or other).
*   **Questions** (Field 20): Select the questions that were included in the quiz.
*   **Comments** (Field 21): Add any additional comments about the quiz.
*   **Instructors Comments** (Field 22): Add any comments from the instructor.
*   **Completed** (Field 23): Mark the status of the quiz as completed or in progress.
*   **Submission Status** (Field 24): Mark the submission status of the quiz as completed or in progress.
*   **Submit** (Field 25): Select the action to take with the quiz (Submit, Save as Draft, or Cancel).

## Tips
*   Please ensure that all required fields (marked as "required" in the field label) are completed before submitting the form.
*   If you're unsure about any field or step, contact your instructor or facilitator for guidance.
*   Double-check the accuracy of the information entered before submitting the form.
