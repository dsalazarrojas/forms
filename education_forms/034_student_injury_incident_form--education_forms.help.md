# Student Injury Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Injury Incident Form is a tool for schools to document and report incidents involving student injuries. This form helps schools track and manage student injuries, ensuring that students receive proper care and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the date of the incident using the date field.
2. Select the students involved in the incident using the "Student Injured" field.
3. Describe the injury in detail using the "Injury Description" field.
4. Specify the location of the injury using the "Injury Location" field.
5. Assess the severity of the injury using the "Injury Severity" field.
6. Choose who was notified of the incident using the "Incident Reported To" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date**: Enter the date of the incident.
* **Student Injured**: Select the students who were involved in the incident.
* **Injury Description**: Describe the injury in detail. For example, "Cut on the hand while using a sharp object."
* **Injury Location**: Choose the location where the injury occurred. For example, "Classroom 101" or "School Playground."
* **Injury Severity**: Assess the severity of the injury as:
	+ Minor (a minor cut or scrape)
	+ Moderate (a more serious injury requiring medical attention)
	+ Severe (a serious injury requiring immediate medical attention)
* **Incident Reported To**: Choose who was notified of the incident. For example:
	+ Parent (a parent or guardian)
	+ Teacher (a teacher or staff member)
	+ School Nurse (a school nurse or medical professional)

## Tips
* Make sure to complete the form thoroughly and accurately to ensure that students receive proper care and support.
* If unsure about any field, ask your school administration for guidance.
* Remember to submit the form promptly after an incident occurs.
