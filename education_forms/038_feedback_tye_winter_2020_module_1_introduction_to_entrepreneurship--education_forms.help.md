# feedback_tye_winter_2020_module_1_introduction_to_entrepreneurship - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from students on the "Introduction to Entrepreneurship" module of the Tye Winter 2020 course. Your feedback will help us evaluate the effectiveness of the module and identify areas that need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Provide detailed and constructive feedback in the text fields.
3. For the multiple-choice questions, select the options that best describe your experience with the module.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **feedback** (1, note, optional): This is a text field where you can provide any feedback about the module, including suggestions for improvement.
* **name** (2, text, optional): Enter your name, which will be used to identify you as the person providing feedback.
* **email** (3, email, optional): Enter your email address, which will be used to keep you informed about the feedback process and any follow-up questions we may have.
* **feedback** (4, text, optional): This is another text field where you can provide more feedback about the module.
* **module_1** (5, number, optional): Rate your understanding of the first module (on a scale of 1-10).
* **module_2** (6, number, optional): Rate your understanding of the second module (on a scale of 1-10).
* **submission_date** (7, date, optional): Enter the date you submitted this feedback form.
* **submission_time** (8, time, optional): Enter the time you submitted this feedback form.
* **module_1_feedback** (9, select_one, optional): Select one of the options (Good or Bad) to indicate your experience with the first module.
* **module_2_feedback** (10, select_multiple, optional): Select all the options (Yes or No) that apply to your experience with the second module.
* **email_sent** (11, text, optional): This field is not currently used, please do not enter anything in this field.

## Tips

* Please be constructive and respectful in your feedback.
* Provide specific examples or anecdotes to illustrate your points.
* If you have any suggestions for improvement, please provide them in the feedback fields.
* If you have any concerns or complaints, please let us know so we can address them promptly.
