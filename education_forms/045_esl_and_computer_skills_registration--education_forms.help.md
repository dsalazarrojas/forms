# ESL And Computer Skills Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ESL And Computer Skills Registration form is designed to help learners register their preferences and schedule for ESL and computer skills courses. This form is meant to gather necessary information to ensure proper course placement and to prevent duplicate registrations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your enrollment center (page 1).
2. Provide your learner preferences (page 2).
3. Choose your course preferences (page 3).
4. Select a date for your schedule (page 4).
5. Indicate your proficiency level (page 5).
6. Confirm your class roster status (page 6).
7. Enter your contact information (page 7).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enrollment Center** (page 1): Enter the name of the center where you would like to enroll in a course.
* **Learner Preferences** (page 2): Describe your preferred learning style, schedule, or any other special requests.
* **Course Preferences** (page 3): Choose the type of course you are interested in (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Schedule** (page 4): Select a date to start your course.
* **Proficiency Level** (page 5): Indicate your current level of proficiency in the subject area (e.g., 1, 2, 3).
* **Class Roster** (page 6): Confirm whether you will be enrolling in a class with others or alone.
* **Contact** (page 7): Enter your contact information so we can get in touch with you about your course placement.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate information to ensure proper course placement.
- Review your selections carefully before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
