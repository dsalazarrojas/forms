# Literary Selections Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for collecting feedback from users on literary selections they have read. It aims to gather a range of information, including ratings, comments, file uploads, and other relevant details to improve the selection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose your rating for the literary selection from the options provided.
2. Provide any comments or feedback you have on the selection.
3. Upload any relevant files related to the selection.
4. Estimate the amount of time you spent reading the selection.
5. Enter the publication date of the selection.
6. Choose your rating for other literary selections you have read recently.
7. Rate your overall experience with the selection.
8. Provide any additional comments or feedback.
9. Enter the amount of time you spent reading the selection.
10. Upload any additional files related to the selection.
11. Provide any other feedback or comments you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your rating for this literary selection?** (`Ratings`, `select_multiple`): Choose one of the provided ratings (Good, Fair) to rate your experience with the literary selection.
* **Do you have any comments?** (`Comments`, `text`): Enter any comments or feedback you have on the selection.
* **Can you upload any relevant files?** (`File Uploader`, `text`): Upload any files related to the selection that you think might be helpful.
* **How many hours do you estimate you spent reading this literary selection?** (`Time Spent`, `number`): Estimate the amount of time you spent reading the selection.
* **What is the publication date of this literary selection?** (`Publication Date`, `date`): Enter the publication date of the selection.
* **What time of day did you read this literary selection?** (`Time Of Day`, `time`): Choose the time of day you read the selection.
* **Which other literary selections have you read recently?** (`Other Ratings`, `select_multiple`): Choose any other literary selections you have read recently.
* **How would you rate your overall experience with this literary selection?** (`Overall Experience`, `select_multiple`): Choose one of the provided ratings (Good, Fair) to rate your overall experience with the selection.
* **Do you have any other comments or feedback?** (`Other Feedback`, `text`): Enter any additional comments or feedback you have on the selection.
* **Time Spent 2** (`Time Spent 2`, `number`): Estimate the amount of time you spent reading the selection (this field is repeated for consistency).
* **Can you upload any additional relevant files?** (`Additional Files`, `text`): Upload any additional files related to the selection that you think might be helpful.
* **Publication Date 2** (`Publication Date 2`, `date`): Enter the publication date of the selection (this field is repeated for consistency).
* **What time of day did you read this literary selection?** (`Time Of Day 2`, `time`): Choose the time of day you read the selection (this field is repeated for consistency).
* **Do you have any other feedback or comments?** (`More Feedback`, `text`): Enter any additional feedback or comments you have on the selection.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your feedback.
* Try to provide as much detail as possible.
* Use the ratings and comments to help improve the literary selection.
* If you have any additional files related to the selection, upload them for further reference.
