# Fashion Design Exam Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fashion Design Exam Form is used to document and evaluate student's performance in fashion design exams.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student name.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Select the fashion program you are enrolled in (e.g., Bachelor of Fine Arts, Fashion Merchandising, or Apparel Design).
4. Describe your design in the "Design Description" field.
5. Share your portfolio (if applicable).
6. Enter the date and time of your submission.
7. Add any additional submission notes.
8. Select the evaluation date (when you want to be evaluated).
9. Add any evaluation notes.
10. Leave any comments or feedback about the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your student ID or official records.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your university email address or any other contact information you'd like to be reached at.
* **Fashion Program** (`fashion_program`, select_one, optional): Choose the program you are enrolled in to help the instructor understand your background and context.
* **Design Description** (`design_description`, text, optional): Briefly describe your design project or submission.
* **Portfolio** (`portfolio`, text, optional): Share any relevant work or examples of your design project.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when you submitted your work.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of submission.
* **Submission Note** (`submission_note`, note, optional): Add any notes or comments about your submission.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, optional): Set the date for evaluation.
* **Evaluation Note** (`evaluation_note`, note, optional): Leave any feedback or comments for the instructor.
* **Fashion Design Exam Form Comments** (`fashion_design_exam_form_comments`, text, optional): Add any final comments or feedback about the form.

## Tips

* Make sure to be as concise as possible when filling out the form.
* If required fields are not filled out, please provide a justification or reason for not completing them.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact your instructor or course staff.
