# Algebraic Expressions Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information on students' understanding and preferences in algebraic expressions. It is intended to help educators identify areas where students may need extra support or reinforcement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and ID number.
2. Select your current grade level.
3. Enter the date you took this survey.
4. Answer the questions regarding your comfort with variables, understanding of algebraic expressions, and ability to simplify and evaluate expressions.
5. Choose the topics you find most challenging in algebra.
6. Indicate how you prefer to learn and receive help.
7. Rate your confidence in solving problems.
8. Answer questions regarding your extra help needs, preferred help format, math anxiety level, and enjoyment of algebra.
9. Indicate whether you see real-world applications of algebra.
10. Set your goals for this course, if any.
11. Provide any additional feedback.
