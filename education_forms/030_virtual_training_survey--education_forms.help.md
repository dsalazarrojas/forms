# Virtual Training Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Training Survey is a tool designed to gather feedback from users about their experience with virtual training. This feedback will help to improve the quality and effectiveness of the virtual training experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Answer the survey questions honestly and thoroughly.
2. Provide your suggestions and opinions about the virtual training course.
3. Submit the form when you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Virtual Training Survey**:
	+ Label: This is the first page of the survey.
	+ Type: This is the main page where you will provide your overall feedback about the virtual training experience.
	+ Required: No, you do not have to answer this question if you don't want to.
* **Survey Questions**:
	+ Label: This page is where you will answer specific questions about the virtual training course.
	+ Type: This is a note-taking page where you can write your thoughts and opinions about the course.
	+ Required: No, you do not have to answer this question if you don't want to.
* **Survey Questions**:
	+ Label: This is the second page of the survey, which is also where you will answer specific questions about the virtual training course.
	+ Type: This is a text input field where you can type your answers to the questions.
	+ Required: No, you do not have to answer this question if you don't want to.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your answers.
* Provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your feedback.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, please share them.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback.
