# Global Citizenship Education Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form gathers input from users on their level of familiarity and opinions on global citizenship education.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each field according to your best understanding and knowledge.
2. For select fields, choose one of the provided options.
3. For text fields, provide detailed and clear responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Context**: This field is a text box where you can provide any additional context or information that you think is relevant to your answers. 
* **Global Citizenship Education Familiarity**: Select one of the following options:
	+ High: You are familiar with global citizenship education.
	+ Low: You are not familiar with global citizenship education.
* **Feedback on Global Citizenship Education**: Provide any comments or suggestions on the current state of global citizenship education. 
* **Educators/Organizations/NGOs/Researchers Opinions**: Share any opinions or thoughts on global citizenship education from educators, organizations, NGOs, or researchers. 
* **Educators/Organizations/NGOs/Researchers Familiarity**: Select one of the following options:
	+ High: They are familiar with global citizenship education.
	+ Low: They are not familiar with global citizenship education.
* **Feedback on Global Citizenship Education for Improvement**: Provide any suggestions or comments on how to improve global citizenship education. 
* **Global Citizenship Education for Program Improvement**: Provide any feedback or suggestions on how to improve global citizenship education. 

## Tips
* Please answer this form carefully and accurately, as it will help us better understand your perspective on global citizenship education.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
