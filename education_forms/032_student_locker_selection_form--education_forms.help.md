# Student Locker Selection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Locker Selection Form is used to gather information from students for locker selection purposes. It helps the students access their designated lockers for classes or events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Select your school from the list provided.
2. Enter your student ID number.
3. Choose your locker number if you have one assigned.
4. Confirm whether you have a locker.
5. Optionally, provide your locker number and school location if applicable.
6. Enter your student name, email, phone number, and date of birth if you want to be reached in case of an emergency.
7. Provide the name and contact information of your emergency contact if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Choose Your Locker** (Yes/No, required): Select your school from the list to proceed with the locker selection process.
- **School ID** (Number, required): Enter your unique student ID number for identification purposes.
- **Student ID** (Number, required): Enter your unique student ID number for identification purposes.
- **Locker Number** (Yes/No, required): Confirm whether you have been assigned a locker. If you have, select "Yes" and enter the locker number.
- **Locker Number** (Number, optional): If you have a locker number assigned, enter it here.
- **School Name** (Text, optional): Enter your school's name for reference.
- **School Location** (Yes/No, optional): Select your school location if it's not the default location.
- **Student Name** (Text, optional): Enter your student name for reference.
- **Email** (Email, optional): Enter your email address for emergency contact purposes.
- **Phone Number** (Text, optional): Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
- **Date of Birth** (Date, optional): Enter your date of birth for age verification purposes.
- **Emergency Contact Name** (Text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
- **Phone Number of Emergency Contact** (optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact if needed.
- **Address of Emergency Contact** (optional): Enter the address of your emergency contact if needed.

Note: Please ensure you fill in all required fields to complete the form successfully.
