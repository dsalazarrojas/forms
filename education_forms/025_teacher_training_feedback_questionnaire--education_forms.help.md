# Teacher Training Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from teachers on their training sessions. Please fill out this form to help us evaluate the effectiveness and quality of our training programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the corresponding fields for each question and follow the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name as it is on your official identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name as it is on your official identification.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Please select a rating from 1 to 5, where 1 is Poor and 5 is Excellent.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter your work email address if you'd like to receive follow-up information or updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Please enter your work phone number if you'd like to receive follow-up information or updates.
* **Select Rating** (`select_rating`, select_one, optional): Please select the rating that best describes your experience with the training session.
* **Training Sessions** (`training_sessions`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the training sessions you attended.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Please enter the date you attended the training session.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Please enter the time you attended the training session.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Please enter any additional comments or notes about the training session.
* **Category** (`category`, text, optional): Please enter the category of the training session.
* **Training Program** (`training_program`, text, optional): Please enter the title of the training program you attended.

## Tips
Please fill out all the fields that are relevant to you and your experience. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us. Your feedback is greatly appreciated and will help us improve our training programs.
