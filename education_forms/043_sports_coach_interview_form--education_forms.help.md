# sports_coach_interview_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information from sports coaches to be considered for a position.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your general information, including any necessary contact details.
2. Select your highest level of education completed.
3. Enter the number of years of experience you have in the sport.
4. List any coaching licenses you hold.
5. Briefly describe your sport background.
6. List any relevant work experience you have.
7. Write a personal statement outlining your qualifications and experience.
8. Select any relevant references.
9. Click the "Submit" button to complete and submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`1`): Enter your name and any other general information as requested.
* **What is your highest level of education completed?** (`2`): Select one of the available options, such as High School or Master's.
* **Years of Experience** (`3`): Enter the number of years you have been working in the sport.
* **Coaching Licenses** (`4`): Select any relevant coaching licenses you hold, such as Coaching Certification 1 or Coaching Certification 2.
* **Sport Background** (`5`): Briefly describe your background in the sport, including any relevant experience or training.
* **Work Experience** (`6`): List any relevant work experience you have, including job titles and years of experience.
* **Personal Statement** (`7`): Write a personal statement outlining your qualifications and experience as a sports coach.
* **References** (`8`): Select any relevant references, such as colleagues or mentors.
* **Submit** (`9`): Click this button to submit your completed application.
* **Submit** (`10`): This is the final submit button to complete and submit your application.
