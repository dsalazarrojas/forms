# School Presentation Handout Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from presenters about their presentation, including their contact details, presentation schedule, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Presenter Applicant's details in the "Presenter Applicant" field.
2. Fill in the Presenter's name in the "Presenter Name" field.
3. Enter the contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.
4. Enter the presenter's phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
5. Select the type of phone number from the options provided in the "Contact Phone Type" field.
6. If the presentation is scheduled, select the presentation date in the "Presentation Date" field.
7. If the presentation is scheduled, select the presentation time in the "Presentation Time" field.
8. Enter any notes or comments in the "Agreement" field.
9. Provide the presentation location in the "Presentation Location" field.
10. Enter a brief description of the presentation topic in the "Presentation Topic" field.
11. Enter a brief description of the presentation type in the "Presentation Type" field.
12. Share any relevant experience or background information about the presenter in the "Presenter Experience" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Presenter Applicant** (`presenter_applicant`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who is the presenter applicant.
* **Presenter Name** (`presenter_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the presenter.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the contact email address of the presenter.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the presenter's phone number.
* **Contact Phone Type** (`contact_phone_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of phone number from the options provided (mobile or landline).
* **Presentation Date** (`presentation_date`, date, optional): If the presentation is scheduled, select the date of the presentation.
* **Presentation Time** (`presentation_time`, time, optional): If the presentation is scheduled, select the time of the presentation.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, note, optional): Enter any notes or comments about the agreement or terms of the presentation.
* **Presentation Location** (`presentation_location`, text, optional): Provide the location of the presentation.
* **Presentation Topic** (`presentation_topic`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the presentation topic.
* **Presentation Type** (`presentation_type`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the presentation type.
* **Presenter Experience** (`presenter_experience`, text, optional): Share any relevant experience or background information about the presenter.
