# Educational Support Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Support Assessment form is a tool for school staff or tutors to gather information about a student's challenges and create a support plan to help the student succeed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information about the student, such as their name, email, and school ID (if applicable).
2. Select the relevant challenges that the student is facing from the "Student Challenges" field.
3. Provide more details about the student's challenges in the "Challenge Details" field.
4. Choose the type of support the student needs (academic, wellbeing, or other) from the "Student Needs" field.
5. Enter a short-term and long-term support plan in the "Support Plan" field.
6. Specify follow-up actions in the "Follow-up" field.
7. Add any additional notes about the student's situation in the "Notes" field.
8. If applicable, assign a tutor from the "Assigned Tutor" field.
9. Enter the student's email and phone number in the "Student Email" and "Student Phone" fields, respectively.
10. Enter the school's email and phone number in the "School Email" and "School Phone" fields, respectively.
11. If applicable, assign a counselor from the "Assigned Counselor" field.
12. Ensure that the "School Id" field is accurate for the school the student attends.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Challenges** (`student_challenges`, text, required: false): Describe the specific challenges the student is facing.
* **School Id** (`school_id`, number, required: false): Enter the school ID for the student's school.
* **Student Needs** (`student_needs`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose from academic support, wellbeing support, or other needs.
* **Challenge Details** (`challenge_details`, text, required: false): Provide more information about the student's challenges.
* **Support Plan** (`support_plan`, text, required: false): Enter a short-term and long-term support plan for the student.
* **Communication Plan** (`communication_plan`, text, required: false): Describe the communication plan for the student.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, text, required: false): Specify follow-up actions for the student.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes about the student's situation.
* **Assigned Tutor** (`assigned_tutor`, select_one, required: false): Choose a tutor to be assigned to the student.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required: false): Enter the student's email address.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the student's phone number.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, email, required: false): Enter the school's email address.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the school's phone number.
* **Assigned Counselor** (`assigned_counselor`, select_one, required: false): Choose a counselor to be assigned to the student.
