# Educational Institution Financial Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an educational institution's financial audit, which is used to assess the institution's financial health and performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide the name of the institution being audited.
2. Enter the name of the auditor performing the audit.
3. Enter the date of the audit.
4. Select the title of the auditor (e.g. Certified Public Accountant).
5. Enter the name of the auditor's organization.
6. For the financial year, enter the fiscal year of the audit (e.g. 2022).
7. Select the audit results (e.g. Financially Healthy, Financially Unhealthy, Not Audited).
8. Indicate if supporting documents are required or not.
9. Provide the URL of the institution's website (if applicable).
10. Enter the institution's phone number (if applicable).
11. Enter the institution's email address (if applicable).
12. Provide any additional notes or comments about the audit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Institution name**: Enter the name of the institution being audited.
* **Auditor name**: Enter the name of the auditor performing the audit.
* **Audit date**: Enter the date of the audit.
* **Auditor title**: Select the title of the auditor (e.g. Certified Public Accountant).
* **Auditor organization**: Enter the name of the auditor's organization.
* **Financial year**: Enter the fiscal year of the audit (e.g. 2022).
* **Audit results**: Select the outcome of the audit (e.g. Financially Healthy, Financially Unhealthy, Not Audited).
* **Supporting documents**: Indicate if supporting documents are required or not.
* **Institution website**: Enter the URL of the institution's website (if applicable).
* **Institution phone**: Enter the institution's phone number (if applicable).
* **Institution email**: Enter the institution's email address (if applicable).
* **Notes**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the audit.
