**PTCAS Observation Hours Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The PTCAS Observation Hours Form is a tool used to record and report observation hours by students or observers. It is used to document hours observed, awarded, and completed for a student's academic progress and professional development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for each field.
2. Enter the student's name and relevant information in the "student name" field.
3. Enter the observer's name in the "observer name" field.
4. Select the relevant observation type in the "observation type" field.
5. Enter the institution name, code, phone number, and email address for the institution where the observation hours were completed.
6. Enter the relevant dates and times in the "observation date", "start time", "end time", and "time" fields.
7. Report the hours observed, awarded, and completed in the "hours observed", "hours awarded", and "hours completed" fields.
8. If necessary, provide comments or additional context in the "comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **student name**: Enter the name of the student who is completing observation hours. 
* **observer name**: Enter the name of the person performing the observation. 
* **observation date**: Enter the date on which observation hours were completed. 
* **start time**: Enter the start time of the observation period. 
* **end time**: Enter the end time of the observation period. 
* **hours observed**: Report the total number of hours observed for the student. 
* **hours awarded**: Report the number of hours awarded for the student. 
* **hours suggested**: Report the number of hours suggested for the student. 
* **hours completed**: Report the number of hours completed for the student. 
* **observation type**: Select the type of observation (e.g., clinical, practicum, etc.). 
* **hours averaged**: Report the average number of hours observed per week. 
* **comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about the observation hours. 
* **signature**: Enter your signature as the observer or student representative. 
* **institution**: Enter the name of the institution where the observation hours were completed. 
* **department**: Enter the department of the institution where the observation hours were completed. 
* **position**: Enter your position within the institution where the observation hours were completed. 
* **student id**: Enter the student ID number for the student completing observation hours. 
* **observation hours form version**: Enter the version of the form used (e.g., 1.0, 2.0, etc.). 
* **date**: Enter the date on which the form is being submitted. 
* **time**: Enter the time at which the form is being submitted. 
* **institution code**: Enter the institution's code or ID. 
* **institution name**: Enter the name of the institution where the observation hours were completed. 
* **institution phone**: Enter the phone number of the institution where the observation hours were completed. 
* **institution email**: Enter the email address of the institution where the observation hours were completed. 
* **comments_comments**: Select any additional comments or notes about the observation hours.

Please note that all fields with "required" as true are required to be filled before submitting the form.
