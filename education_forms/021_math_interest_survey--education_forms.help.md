# math_interest_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about users with math interests, including their opinions, preferences, and experiences related to math.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that best match your responses.
3. Provide any additional comments or notes as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **what_is_math** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): This is a text field where you can describe your interests and thoughts about math.
* **how_do_you_feel** (`2`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose how you feel about math, whether it's 'Yes' or 'No'. This will help us understand your attitude towards math.
* **what_do_you_like** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all the things you like about math from the provided options, or add your own in the additional comments field.
* **what_is_your_favorite** (`4`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose your favorite thing about math from the options provided.
* **what_is_your_grade** (`5`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your current grade level in the field provided.
* **what_is_your_interest** (`6`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose your level of interest in math from the options provided.
* **what_is_the_most_difficult** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the things that you find most difficult about math from the provided options.
* **what_is_the_most_easy** (`8`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the things that you find most easy about math from the provided options.
* **what_is_your_favorite_subject** (`9`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose your favorite math subject from the options provided.
* **what_is_your_grade_level** (`10`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your grade level in the field provided.
* **when_do_you_teach_math** (`11`, `time`, required/optional): Provide the time when you teach math, if applicable.
* **why_do_you_teach_math** (`12`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or reasons why you teach or have taught math, if applicable.
* **when_do_you_start_teaching_math** (`13`, `date`, required/optional): Provide the date you started teaching math, if applicable.
* **math_survey_comments** (`14`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about math-related topics.
* **math_survey_comments_two** (`15`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about math-related topics.
* **math_survey_comments** (`16`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about math-related topics.
* **math_survey_survey_comments** (`17`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any comments or notes about the math survey itself, if applicable.
* **survey_administrative_comments** (`18`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the survey, if applicable.
* **math_survey_organizers** (`19`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose if you have math survey organizers.
* **math_survey_organizer** (`20`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the math survey organizer, if applicable.
