# Teacher Loan Forgiveness Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teacher Loan Forgiveness Form is a document used to apply for loan forgiveness programs for teachers. This form collects information about your educational background, loan details, and school affiliation to help determine your eligibility for loan forgiveness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your School Name in the "School Name" field (id: 1).
2. Select your Education Level from the options provided in the "Education Level" field (id: 2).
3. Enter the amount of your loan in the "Loan Amount" field (id: 3).
4. Enter the name of your program in the "Program Name" field (id: 4).
5. Select the start date of your program in the "Program Start Date" field (id: 5).
6. Select the end date of your program in the "Program End Date" field (id: 6).
7. Enter your School District in the "School District" field (id: 7).
8. Enter your School Address 1 in the "School Address 1" field (id: 8).
9. Enter your School Address 2 in the "School Address 2" field (id: 9).
10. Enter your School City in the "School City" field (id: 10).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name** (id: 1, text, required/optional): Enter the official name of your school where you are currently employed or have taught.
* **Education Level** (id: 2, select_one, required/optional): Select the level of your education (e.g., High, Low).
* **Loan Amount** (id: 3, number, required/optional): Enter the total amount of the loan you wish to apply for forgiveness.
* **Program Name** (id: 4, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your program where you received your education or have participated (e.g., Master's, Bachelor's).
* **Program Start Date** (id: 5, date, required/optional): Select the start date of your program.
* **Program End Date** (id: 6, date, required/optional): Select the end date of your program.
* **School District** (id: 7, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the school district where you work or have taught.
* **School Address 1** (id: 8, text, required/optional): Enter the first line of your school's address.
* **School Address 2** (id: 9, text, required/optional): Enter the second line of your school's address.
* **School City** (id: 10, text, required/optional): Enter the city where your school is located.

## Tips
* Ensure you have all necessary documents and information before completing this form.
* Be accurate and truthful in your responses to ensure a smooth review process.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to your school's HR department or the loan forgiveness program's guidelines.
