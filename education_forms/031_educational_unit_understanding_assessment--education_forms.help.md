# Educational Unit Understanding Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help us better understand how you perceive and interact with educational units. Your feedback will help us identify areas of improvement in our teaching methods and materials.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the provided questions and answer them as honestly as you can.
2. For each question, provide as much detail as possible to help us understand your thoughts and experiences.
3. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please include them in the "Comments" field.
4. Make sure to answer all required questions and provide accurate information for all fields, especially if they are marked as "required".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How did you feel about the lesson?** (`lesson_summary`, `text`, required: false): Please describe your overall impression of the lesson, including any emotions or reactions you experienced.
* **What is your understanding of the unit?** (`unit_overview`, `text`, required: false): Explain your comprehension of the unit's content and how it relates to the lesson.
* **What were the most important concepts covered?** (`lesson_key_concepts`, `text`, required: false): Identify the key ideas or topics discussed during the lesson.
* **What would you do next with this information?** (`next_steps`, `text`, required: false): Describe how you plan to apply or use the knowledge you gained from the lesson.
* **How can we improve instruction for you?** (`teacher_support`, `text`, required: false): Share any suggestions or areas where you feel instruction could be improved.
* **Additional feedback** (`student_feedback`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or concerns you have about the lesson or unit.
* **Teacher feedback** (`teacher_feedback`, `text`, required: false): Share any feedback or comments you have for the teacher.
* **What is your score?** (`student_score`, `number`, required: false): Rate your understanding of the unit on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **Teacher score** (`teacher_score`, `number`, required: false): Rate your understanding of the unit on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **How much time did you spend on the lesson?** (`lesson_time`, `number`, required: false): Estimate the amount of time you devoted to the lesson.
* **How much time did you spend on the lesson?** (`lesson_time_range`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the time range you spent on the lesson (e.g., Less than 30 minutes, 30 minutes or more).
* **Educational unit** (`educational_unit`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the educational unit you are referring to (e.g., Unit 1, Unit 2, Unit 3).
* **Date of the lesson** (`lesson_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the lesson (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Time of the lesson** (`lesson_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the start and end times of the lesson (e.g., 08:00-10:00).
* **Comments** (`teacher_comments`, `note`, required: false): Provide any comments or feedback for the teacher.
* **Email** (`email_address`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Start time of the lesson** (`lesson_start_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the start time of the lesson (e.g., 08:00).
* **End time of the lesson** (`lesson_end_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the end time of the lesson (e.g., 10:00).
* **Lesson duration** (`lesson_duration`, `text`, required: false): Estimate the length of the lesson.
* **How long was the lesson?** (`lesson_length`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the length of the lesson (e.g., 1 hour, 2 hours, 3 hours, More than 3 hours).
* **Where was the lesson?** (`lesson_location`, `text`, required: false): Describe the location of the lesson.
* **What format was the lesson?** (`lesson_format`, `text`, required: false): Describe the format of the lesson (e.g., online, in-person, hybrid).
* **Teacher name** (`teacher_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the teacher.
* **Lesson topic** (`lesson_topic`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the lesson topic.
