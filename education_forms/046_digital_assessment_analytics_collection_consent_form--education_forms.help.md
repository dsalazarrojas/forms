## digital_assessment_analytics_collection_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect user information and consent for digital assessment analytics. The information collected will be used to improve the assessment experience and to contact users for follow-up purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer yes or no to the question of whether or not you consent to collect your analytics data.
2. If you give consent, please provide your contact information so we can get in touch with you.
3. Enter your email address and phone number if you want us to contact you.
4. Review your answers carefully and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **digital_assessment_analytics_collection** (`digital_assessment_analytics_collection`, text, optional): This field is used to ask the user if they consent to collect their digital assessment analytics data. Please answer yes or no.
* **consent_to_collect** (`consent_to_collect`, select one, optional): Please select "True" if you give consent to collect your data and "False" if you do not. 
* **contact_info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): This field is used to capture user contact information so we can get in touch with them. Please enter your name, company, and/or other relevant contact information.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): If you want us to contact you via email, please enter your email address here.
* **phone_number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): If you want us to contact you via phone, please enter your phone number here.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer all required fields, even if you give consent.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us.
- This form is designed to be completed once, so please review your answers carefully before submitting.
