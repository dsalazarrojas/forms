# parent_roles_and_responsibilities_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect information from parents or guardians to understand their roles and responsibilities related to their child's education.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Begin by reading the introduction.
- Fill out the "Student Name" field, which is where you will provide your child's name.
- Fill out the "Parent Name" field, which is your own name.
- Enter your parent's address in the "Parent Address" field.
- Add your parent's email address in the "Parent Email" field.
- Provide your parent's phone number in the "Parent Phone" field.
- Select your role(s) from the "Roles" field, which can be Teacher, Parent, Guardian, Coach, Leader, or Other.
- Select your responsibility(ies) from the "Responsibilities" field, which can also be Teacher, Parent, Guardian, Coach, Leader, or Other.
- Finally, enter any additional information about yourself and your role in your child's education in the "Additional Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, required: `false`): Read this section carefully before filling out the form.
* **Student Name** (`2`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your child's name.
* **Parent Name** (`3`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your own name.
* **Parent Address** (`4`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your address.
* **Parent Email** (`5`, `email`, required: `false`): Enter your email address.
* **Parent Phone** (`6`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your phone number.
* **Roles** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select one or more of the following roles: Teacher, Parent, Guardian, Coach, Leader, or Other.
* **Responsibilities** (`8`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select one or more of the following responsibilities: Teacher, Parent, Guardian, Coach, Leader, or Other.
* **Additional Info** (`9`, `text`, required: `false`): Add any additional information about yourself and your role.
