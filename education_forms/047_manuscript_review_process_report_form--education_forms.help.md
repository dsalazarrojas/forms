# Manuscript Review Process Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to document the review process of a manuscript, providing a structured format for reviewers to report their feedback, comments, and ratings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the manuscript carefully and provide your honest feedback on its quality and feasibility for publication.
2. Fill out the form with your comments, rating, and submission status.
3. Submit the completed form to ensure that the manuscript review process is thorough and transparent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Manuscript Review Process Form** (Page 1)
* **Manuscript Review Process Form** (<name>: `manuscript_review_process_form`, <type>: text, required: false): This field is for reviewers to enter a brief description of the manuscript review process they went through.
### **Reviewer Comments** (<name>: `reviewer_comments`, <type>: text, required: false): Use this field to provide detailed comments on the manuscript.
### **Submission Status** (<name>: `submission_status`, <type>: select_one, required: false): Select one of the following options:
	+ Accept: The manuscript is ready for publication.
	+ Reject: The manuscript is not ready for publication.
	+ Resubmit: The manuscript needs to be revised before publication.
	+ Revision: The manuscript is mostly ready for publication, but some revisions are needed.
### **Reviewer Score** (<name>: `reviewer_score`, <type>: number, required: false): Rate the manuscript on a scale (e.g., 1-5 or 0-10).
### **Reviewer Feedback** (<name>: `reviewer_feedback`, <type>: text, required: false): Provide any additional feedback on the manuscript.
### **Submission ID** (<name>: `submission_id`, <type>: number, required: false): Enter the ID of the manuscript being reviewed.
### **Reviewer Rating** (<name>: `reviewer_rating`, <type>: number, required: false): Rate the manuscript on a scale (e.g., 1-5 or 0-10).
### **Submission Type** (<name>: `submission_type`, <type>: select_one, required: false): Select the type of submission (e.g., Article, Book, Book chapter, Conference paper, or Other).

## Tips
* Be thorough and honest in your review, providing constructive feedback that will help the author improve their work.
* If you have any concerns or questions, don't hesitate to contact us.
* This form should be filled out only once the review process is complete.
