# online_learning_platform_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback and opinions from users of the online learning platform, to help us improve its functionality and user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Review each field carefully and answer each question honestly.
2. Fill out all required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
3. For fields with multiple options, select all that apply.
4. Enter any additional comments or feedback you have in the form's "Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How do you use the online learning platform?** (`platform_usage`, text, required: false): This field asks about your primary use of the platform.
* **What features do you find most valuable on the platform?** (`platform_features`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all features that you find most valuable on the platform.
* **Platform Features 2** (`platform_features_2`, select_multiple, required: false): Similar to the previous field, select all features that you find valuable on the platform.
* **How would you rate the quality of course content?** (`course_content`, select_one, required: false): Rate the quality of course content as "Good" or "Poor".
* **Course Content 2** (`course_content_2`, select_one, required: false): Another opportunity to rate the quality of course content.
* **How do you find navigating the platform?** (`navigation`, text, required: false): Share your thoughts on navigating the platform.
* **How would you rate the user support on the platform?** (`user_support`, select_one, required: false): Rate the user support as "Good" or "Fair".
* **User Support 2** (`user_support_2`, select_one, required: false): Another opportunity to rate the user support.
* **How would you rate your overall experience on the platform?** (`overall_experience`, select_one, required: false): Rate your overall experience as "Good" or "Fair".
* **Overall Experience 2** (`overall_experience_2`, select_one, required: false): Another opportunity to rate your overall experience.
* **Do you have any additional comments or feedback about your experience on the platform?** (`feedback`, note, required: false): Share any additional thoughts or suggestions you have.
* **What is your email address?** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can follow up with you.
* **What is your phone number?** (`phone`, text, required: false): Share your phone number if you'd like us to call you.
* **What is your name?** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your name so we can address you personally.
* **Which institution are you representing?** (`institution`, text, required: false): Share the name of your institution.
* **What is your role in the institution?** (`role`, text, required: false): Share your role in the institution.
* **Which department do you belong to?** (`department`, text, required: false): Share the name of your department.
* **What is your job function?** (`job_function`, text, required: false): Share your job function.
* **What is your job title?** (`job_title`, text, required: false): Share your job title.
* **Do you have any additional comments or feedback?** (`feedback_comments`, note, required: false): Share any final thoughts or suggestions.
* **What is the website of your institution?** (`institution_website`, text, required: false): Share the website of your institution.
* **What is the email of your institution?** (`institution_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of your institution.
* **What is the phone number of your institution?** (`institution_phone`, text, required: false): Share the phone number of your institution.
* **What is the job title of the contact person?** (`job_title_institution`, text, required: false): Share the job title of the contact person.
* **Who is the contact person?** (`contact_person`, text, required: false): Share the name of the contact person.
