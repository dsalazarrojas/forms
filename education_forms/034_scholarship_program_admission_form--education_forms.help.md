# Scholarship Program Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students applying for a scholarship program. It is intended to gather contact information, personal details, and other relevant data from applicants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand what information is required.
2. Fill in your contact information and personal details in the relevant fields.
3. Select the correct options for your student details and guardian information.
4. Enter your address and contact number (if applicable).
5. Provide your email address (if applicable).
6. Enter your educational institution (if applicable).
7. Double-check your answers for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Application Form** (`application_form`, text, optional): This is a text field where you can provide any additional information you'd like to be considered during the scholarship application process.
* **Contact Info** (`student_contact_info`, text, optional): This field is used to collect your contact information, including your name and any other relevant details.
* **Student Details** (`student_details`, select multiple, required): Here, you can select the fields that best describe your student status:
	+ Student Name
	+ Date of Birth
	+ Student ID
* **Guardian Info** (`guardian_info`, select one, required): Choose the correct guardian category that best describes the relationship between the student and their guardian:
	+ Mother
	+ Father
	+ Guardian
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, text, optional): This field is used to collect your current address, which may be required by the scholarship committee.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Provide your phone number or any other contact number you'd like to be reached at.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted by the scholarship committee.
* **Educational Institution** (`educational_institution`, text, optional): This field is used to collect information about the institution you are currently attending or plan to attend.
