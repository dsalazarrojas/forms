# Online Private Lesson Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from students and instructors about private lesson sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Provide your student name, email, and phone number.
- Choose a date and time for your lesson with your instructor.
- Select the frequency of your sessions (e.g., every week, bi-weekly, or monthly).
- Enter any additional notes about your lesson or session.
- If assigned a tool for your session, select it from the options provided.
- Fill out any other relevant fields that apply to your lesson.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears in your student ID or record.
- **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, optional): Enter your institutional email address.
- **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, optional): Enter your instructor's name.
- **Lesson Date** (`lesson_date`, date, optional): Choose a date for your lesson from the calendar.

- **Frequency** (`frequency`, select_one, optional): Select the frequency of your sessions (e.g., every week, bi-weekly, or monthly).

## Tips
- Fill out the form completely and accurately.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact your instructor or support team for clarification.
