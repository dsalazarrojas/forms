# Surgical Technology Clinical Slot Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about clinical slots and rotations for surgical technology students or technologists.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the days of the week you are available for clinical slots (Monday to Saturday).
2. Select the time slots you are available (09:00-12:00, 13:00-16:00, 17:00-20:00).
3. Choose the locations you prefer for clinical slots (Location A, Location B, Location C).
4. Select the rotation lengths you prefer (Week 1, Week 2, Week 3, Week 4).
5. Enter any available dates you are available for clinical slots.
6. Enter the start date of the clinical slot.
7. Enter the end date of the clinical slot.
8. Choose the frequency of rotations (Daily, Every Other Day, Every Third Day).
9. Select the type of rotation (Surgical Team, Clinic, Lab, Other).
10. Choose the details of the rotation (Surgical Team 1, Surgical Team 2, Surgical Team 3, Surgical Team 4, Surgical Team 5).
11. If you have a preferred start date for the rotation, enter it.
12. If you have a preferred end date for the rotation, enter it.
13. If you have a preferred time for the rotation, enter it.
14. Enter any comments or notes about the rotation.
14. Enter the name of the program you are participating in.
15. Enter the name of the instructor or supervisor.
