# Study Environment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Study Environment Survey is a form designed to gather feedback from students about their study environment. It aims to understand their overall satisfaction, the availability and satisfaction of resources, and their satisfaction with study settings.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Start at the "Study Settings" page, which is the first page of the form.
2. Answer the questions on this page to provide information about your study environment.
3. Proceed to the next page, "Satisfaction", to rate your satisfaction with different aspects of your study environment.
4. On the "Resources" page, rate your satisfaction with the resources available in your study environment.
5. On the "Study Satisfaction" page, rate your overall satisfaction with your study environment.
6. On the "Resources Satisfaction" page, rate the satisfaction with the resources you have access to.
7. Finally, proceed to the "Feedback" page to provide any comments or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Study Settings** (`Study Settings`, text, optional): This page is where you can provide information about your study environment.
* **Satisfaction** (`Satisfaction`, text, optional): This page is where you can rate your satisfaction with different aspects of your study environment.
* **Resources** (`Resources`, text, optional): This page is where you can rate your satisfaction with the resources available in your study environment.
* **Study Satisfaction** (`Study Satisfaction`, text, optional): This page is where you can rate your overall satisfaction with your study environment.
* **Resources Satisfaction** (`Resources Satisfaction`, text, optional): This page is where you can rate your satisfaction with the resources you have access to.
* **Feedback** (`Feedback`, text, optional): This page is where you can provide any comments or suggestions you may have about your study environment.
