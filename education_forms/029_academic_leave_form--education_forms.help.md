# Academic Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Leave Form is a tool used by students to request academic leave from the institution. This form is used to gather necessary information to process the leave request.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely, ensuring all required fields are filled.
2. Provide accurate and clear information in each field.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student name** (Student name, text, required): Fill in your full legal name as per your identification documents.
* **Student identification number** (Student identification number, text, required): Enter your university-issued identification number.
* **Program or major** (Program or major, text, optional): If applicable, select your degree program or major.
* **Type of leave requested** (Type of leave requested, select_one, required): Choose the type of leave you are requesting, such as Medical leave, Personal leave, Maternity or paternity leave, Study abroad leave, or Other.
* **Leave start date** (Leave start date, date, required): Enter the date when you will begin your leave.
* **Expected end date** (Expected end date, date, optional): If known, enter your expected return date.
* **Reason for leave** (Reason for leave, text, required): Provide a brief justification for your leave request.
* **Supporting documents** (Supporting documents, text, optional): Attach any relevant medical certificates or other documentation that supports your leave request.
* **Signature name** (Signature name, text, required): Sign the form with your full name.
* **Submission date** (Submission date, date, required): Date of form submission.

## Tips
* Be accurate and honest when filling out the form.
* Double-check your answers before submitting.
* Ensure you have included all required fields.
