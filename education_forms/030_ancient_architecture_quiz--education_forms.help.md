# Ancient Architecture Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help instructors evaluate and record the knowledge of students on ancient architecture.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's full name in the `Student name` field.
2. Answer the two questions:
   - For question 1, select the correct structure that is an example of Roman architecture.
   - For question 2, provide a short answer about the primary material used in Greek temples.
3. If you want to provide additional feedback, type it in the `Feedback from student` field.
4. Estimate the student's score for the quiz in the `Estimated score` field.
5. Check the box to agree to having your responses recorded in the `I agree to have my responses recorded` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the student.
* **Which structure is an example of Roman architecture?** (`question_1`, select_one, required): Select one of the options to answer this question.
* **Primary material used in Greek temples** (`question_2`, text, optional): Provide a short answer to this question.
* **Estimated score** (`estimated_score`, number, optional): Enter an estimated score for the student.
* **Feedback from student** (`feedback`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback about the quiz.
* **I agree to have my responses recorded** (`consent`, select_one, required): Check the box to agree to have your responses recorded.
