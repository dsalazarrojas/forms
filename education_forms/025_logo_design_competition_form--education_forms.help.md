# Logo Design Competition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for a logo design competition. It gathers data from participants, including their agency, school, submission details, and evaluation scores.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the "logo_design" page with a brief description of your design.
2.  Select the agency you represent on the "agency" page.
3.  Enter your school's name and other details on the "school" page.
4.  On the "submission" page, select your submission type and upload any required files.
5.  Enter your contact details, including email and phone number, on the "contact" page.
6.  Provide more submission details on the "submission_details" page.
7.  Evaluate your submission on the "evaluation" page, providing a score and comment.
