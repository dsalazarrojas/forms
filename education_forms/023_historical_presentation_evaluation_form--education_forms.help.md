# Historical Presentation Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Historical Presentation Evaluation Form is used to assess the effectiveness and quality of historical presentations. It helps reviewers provide feedback and suggestions to presenters, helping them improve their skills and delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Complete all required fields. The form is divided into sections, each with a clear label and description. Make sure to answer all questions thoroughly and provide specific feedback.
2.  For select-one questions, choose the option that best describes your opinion.
3.  For text fields, provide a clear and concise explanation of your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Score** (`score`, `number`, required): Rate the presentation's overall score, from 1 to 10, where 1 means the presentation was not engaging and 10 means it was highly engaging.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the presentation.
* **Content** (`content`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the four options that best describes your opinion about the content of the presentation:
	+ History is a subject with a rich and interesting past
	+ History is a subject that makes me sleepy
	+ History is a subject that is full of dates and facts
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Presentation Structure** (`structure`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the four options that best describes your opinion about the presentation's structure:
	+ The presentation starts strongly and ends strongly
	+ The presentation has some strengths and weaknesses
	+ The presentation was just average
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Engagement of Audience** (`engagement`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the four options that best describes your opinion about the audience's engagement:
	+ The audience was highly engaged
	+ The audience was somewhat engaged
	+ The audience was not engaged
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required): Provide any additional feedback or suggestions you have about the presentation.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, `text`, optional): Provide any suggestions or ideas you have for the presenter to improve their presentation.
* **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, required): Rate the presentation's overall quality, from 1 to 10, where 1 means the presentation was not good and 10 means it was excellent.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the presentation was given.

Note: The `rating` field is a numerical input for the overall rating, while the `date` field is a date input for the presentation date.
