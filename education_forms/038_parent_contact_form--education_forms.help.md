# Parent Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Parent Contact Form is a form used to collect contact information from parents or guardians of students. This information is essential for emergency contacts or other purposes related to student care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Parent Name** in the text field provided. This is the name of the person filling out the form.
2. Select a **Contact Method** from the drop-down list. Choose how you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency or other urgent matters.
3. In the **Contact Details** field, provide any additional information about your contact method, such as a phone number or email address.
4. If you have an **Emergency Contact Name**, enter their name in the text field.
5. For the **Relationship** field, select how you are related to the student (Parent, Guardian, Caregiver, or Authorized Contact).
6. If applicable, enter the **Emergency Contact Phone** number in the text field.
7. Enter the **Emergency Contact Email** address in the text field if applicable.
8. Finally, enter the **Student Name** in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Name**: Enter your name as the student's parent or guardian.
* **Contact Method**: Choose how you would like to be contacted (Contact Phone, Contact Email, Contact Address, Contact In Person).
* **Contact Details**: Provide any additional information about your contact method, such as a phone number or email address.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: If you have an emergency contact, enter their name here.
* **Relationship**: Select how you are related to the student (Parent, Guardian, Caregiver, or Authorized Contact).
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: If you have an emergency contact, enter their phone number here.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: If you have an emergency contact, enter their email address here.
* **Student Name**: Enter the name of the student this form is associated with.
