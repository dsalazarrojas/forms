# Virtual Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from participants for a virtual workshop registration. It's designed to gather details such as participant's name, email, and workshop preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Select the workshop type (Online or On Site) in the "Workshop Type" field.
4. Choose the date and time for the workshop in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
5. Enter the workshop location in the "Workshop Location" field.
6. Select whether you have attended the workshop before in the "Has Attended" field.
7. Confirm that you are a participant of the workshop by selecting "True" in the "Participant Status" field.
