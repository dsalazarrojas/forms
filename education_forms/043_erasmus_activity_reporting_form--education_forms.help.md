# Erasmus Activity Reporting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Erasmus Activity Reporting Form is designed to collect detailed information about the activities performed by program staff members. This form will be used to evaluate and report on these activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **General Information** section with your name, email address, and any other relevant details. 
2. Enter the name and contact information for the **Youth Exchange Participants** involved in the project.
3. Provide the name and contact information for the **Project Participants** involved in the project.
4. Enter the additional details for the **Project Participants** in the **Project Participants Additional** fields.
5. Fill in the **Activity** details, including the start and end dates, **Location**, and **Description** of the activity.
6. Enter the **Budget** and **Funding** details.
7. Enter the **Outcomes** and **Evaluation** details.
8. Upload any relevant **Attachments**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information**: Enter your name, email address, and other relevant details.
  * This field is required, and your information will be used to identify you as the reporter.
* **Youth Exchange Participants**: Enter the name and contact information for all the youth exchange participants involved in the project.
  * This field is not required, but it will help provide more details about the project.
* **Partner Organisation**: Enter the name and contact information for the partner organization involved in the project.
  * This field is not required, but it will provide more details about the project.
* **Project Participants**: Enter the name and contact information for all the project participants involved in the project.
  * This field is not required, but it will help provide more details about the project.
* **Activity**: Enter the details about the activity, including the start and end dates, **Location**, and **Description**.
  * This field is required, as it will provide the most crucial information about the activity.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the activity.
  * This field is required and will help determine when the activity began.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of the activity.
  * This field is required and will help determine when the activity ended.
* **Duration**: Enter the duration of the activity in numbers of hours or days.
  * This field is required to provide a clear picture of the activity's timeframe.
* **Location**: Enter the location where the activity took place.
  * This field is required to provide the location of the activity.
* **Description**: Enter a brief description of the activity.
  * This field is required and will provide more details about the activity.
* **Budget**: Enter the budget details for the activity.
  * This field is not required, but it will help provide financial information about the activity.
* **Funding**: Enter the funding details for the activity.
  * This field is not required, but it will help provide funding information about the activity.
* **Outcomes**: Enter the outcomes of the activity.
  * This field is not required, but it will help provide information about the project's results.
* **Evaluation**: Enter the evaluation details for the activity.
  * This field is not required, but it will help provide information about the activity's evaluation.
* **Attachments**: Upload any relevant attachments.
  * This field is not required, but it will help provide more details about the activity.
* **Attachments Additional**: Upload any additional attachments.
  * This field is not required, but it will help provide more details about the activity.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out completely and accurately.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid duplication of efforts and ensure consistency.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the relevant staff member for guidance.
