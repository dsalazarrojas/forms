# Parent Portal Account Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect important information from parents about their account details, ensuring we have the most up-to-date and accurate information about their account.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Parent Portal Account Information**: Enter the title of the parent portal account, if applicable.
2. **Parent Name**: Please provide the parent's name as it appears on their identification.
3. **Email**: Enter the parent's email address associated with their account.
4. **Phone**: Enter the parent's phone number associated with their account.
5. **Address**: Enter the parent's address associated with their account.
6. **Notes**: If you have any additional information to share about the parent's account, please add it here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Portal Account Information** (`parent_portal_account`, text, required): This is the title of the parent portal account. If you're unsure what to enter here, please ask for help.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Please enter the parent's name as it appears on their identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the parent's email address associated with their account. Please make sure to use the correct email format (e.g., [username]@domain.com).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the parent's phone number associated with their account.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter the parent's address associated with their account.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): If you have any additional information to share about the parent's account, please add it here.
