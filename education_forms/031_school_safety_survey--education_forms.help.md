# School Safety Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Safety Survey is designed to assess a school's safety and emergency response procedures, as well as its overall approach to school safety. The survey aims to collect data that will help identify areas of improvement in school safety, emergency response, and communication with parents and students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read each section carefully and answer truthfully.
* Choose the answers that best reflect your school's current safety and emergency response procedures.
* Be as specific as possible when answering questions that ask for details.
* If you are unsure about any question, ask for clarification or guidance from a school administrator.
* Fill out all required fields. While some fields are not required, providing more information will help to get a more accurate picture of your school's safety procedures.
