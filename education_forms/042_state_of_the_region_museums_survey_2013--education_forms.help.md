# state_of_the_region_museums_survey_2013 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The state_of_the_region_museums_survey_2013 form is designed to collect feedback and information from visitors to our museum. The purpose of this form is to gather insights on how we can improve our museum's facilities, exhibits, and services. Your input will help us understand what you value and what you expect from our museum.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the options that best answer each question.
2. Enter your comments and suggestions in the text fields.
3. Submit the form to share your feedback with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **About You** (`page_1`, text, required):
	+ Please enter a brief description of yourself.
* **How did you hear about our museum?** (`page_2`, select_one, required):
	+ Choose the option that best describes how you found out about our museum:
		- I saw a social media post.
		- I was referred by a friend.
		- I read an online review.
		- I saw a billboard.
		- Other (please specify).
* **Have you visited our museum before?** (`page_3`, select_one, required):
	+ Indicate if you have visited our museum before:
		- True.
		- False.
* **How many times have you visited our museum in the last year?** (`page_4`, number, required):
	+ Enter the number of times you visited our museum in the last year.
* **When was your last visit to our museum?** (`page_5`, date, required):
	+ Enter the date of your last visit.
* **How long did you stay at the museum during your last visit?** (`page_6`, number, required):
	+ Enter the length of time you stayed at the museum during your last visit.
* **What brought you to our museum last time you visited?** (`page_7`, select_multiple, required):
	+ Select all the options that apply:
		+ Exhibits.
		+ Programs.
		+ Events.
		- Other (please specify).
* **What is your current occupation?** (`page_8`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your current occupation or job title.
* **What is your current level of education?** (`page_9`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your current level of education.
* **How did you find our museum's facilities and exhibits?** (`page_10`, select_one, required):
	+ Rate your experience:
		- Excellent.
		- Good.
		- Fair.
		- Poor.
* **Would you recommend our museum to friends and family?** (`page_11`, select_one, required):
	+ Indicate if you would recommend our museum:
		- True.
		- False.
* **Why or why not?** (`page_12`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any reasons for or against recommending our museum.
* **Do you have any suggestions for improvement?** (`page_13`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any suggestions for how we can improve our museum.
* **How can we improve our museum's accessibility for visitors?** (`page_14`, text, optional):
	+ Suggest ways we can make our museum more accessible for visitors.
* **Would you like to be on our mailing list?** (`page_15`, select_one, required):
	+ Indicate if you would like to receive emails from us:
		- True.
		- False.
* **Your contact information** (`page_16`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your contact information so we can reach out to you.
* **Additional comments** (`page_17`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any additional comments or feedback.
* **Additional comments (2)** (`page_18`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any additional comments or feedback, if needed.
