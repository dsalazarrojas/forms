# work_study_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The work_study_application_form is designed to collect information from students applying for work or study programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Student Name.
2. Enter your Email address.
3. Provide your Phone number.
4. Enter your Date of Birth.
5. Select your Study Program choice (Yes or No).
6. Enter your Student ID (if applicable).
7. Enter your Address.
8. Upload your Resume.
9. Upload your Transcripts.
10. Upload your Letters of Recommendation.
11. Provide a brief description of your Work Experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): This field requests your full name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter a valid email address associated with your account or one you'd like to create for this application.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Study Program** (`study_program`, select_one, optional): Select Yes if you're applying for a work program, No if you're not.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, optional): Enter your student ID if you have one.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your current address.
* **Resume** (`resume`, note, optional): Upload your resume in a format that can be read by our system.
* **Transcripts** (`transcripts`, note, optional): Upload your transcripts in a format that can be read by our system.
* **Letters of Recommendation** (`letters_of_recommendation`, note, optional): Upload letters of recommendation in a format that can be read by our system.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, note, optional): Describe your previous work experience, including any relevant skills or qualifications.

## Tips
* Make sure your email address is accurate and valid.
* Be prepared to upload documents in formats that can be read by our system.
* Clearly describe your previous work experience.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the program administrator for assistance.
