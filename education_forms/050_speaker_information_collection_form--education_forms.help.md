# Speaker Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect essential information from speakers for various events, conferences, and presentations.

## How To Complete This Form

Before starting the form, consider the following:

* Gather all the necessary information beforehand to avoid errors.
* Ensure that your contact information and bio are accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are providing social media links, ensure that they are correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your business card or identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your business card or identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your professional email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): If you are associated with an organization, enter the name of the organization.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title or position within the organization.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the department or team you belong to within the organization.
* **Role** (`role`, text, optional): Enter your role in the event or organization.
* **Bio** (`bio`, note, optional): Enter a short description of yourself and your qualifications.
* **Profile Picture** (`image_url`, select_one, required): Choose whether you would like to display a profile picture or not.
* If you choose to display a profile picture, enter the URL of the picture.
* **URL** (`image_url_value`, text, optional): Enter the URL of your profile picture if you chose to display one.
* **Speaker URL** (`speaker_url`, text, optional): Enter the URL of your personal website or speaker page.
* **Website** (`website`, text, optional): Enter the URL of your organization's website.
* **Social Media** (`social_media`, select_one, optional): Choose the social media platforms you are active on.
* **Social Media URL** (`social_media_value`, text, optional): Enter the URL of your social media profile if you chose to display one.
* **Bio** (`bio_value`, text, optional): Enter a longer description of yourself and your qualifications if you did not provide one in the bio field.
* **Event** (`event`, date, required): Enter the title of the event where you are speaking.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of the event.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required): Enter the end time of the event.
* **Speaker Type** (`speaker_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of speaker you are (Keynote, Panel, Workshop, Breakout).
* **Speaker Type** (`speaker_type_value`, text, required): Enter the specific details of the speaker type you chose.

## Tips

* Be as accurate and concise as possible when filling out the form.
* If you are unsure about a specific field, contact the event organizers or administrators for clarification.
* Review your information carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and consistency.
