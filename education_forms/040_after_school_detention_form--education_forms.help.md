# After School Detention Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

<thinking>
This form is used to document and track student detentions, which are assigned as a disciplinary measure for misbehavior or misconduct. The form provides a structured way to collect necessary information about the student, the detention details, and the parent/guardian's notification and response. This information helps teachers, administrators, and parents/guardians understand the circumstances surrounding the detention and ensures that students are held accountable for their actions.
</thinking>

## How To Complete This Form

Before starting, please review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information. If you need assistance, don't hesitate to ask a colleague or supervisor. The form is divided into several sections, each covering a specific aspect of the detention process. Start by filling out the student information section, which includes the student's full name, ID number, grade level, and homeroom teacher. Then, proceed to the detention details section, which requires information about the detention date, time, location, and duration. Next, provide details about the incident that led to the detention, including the type of violation and any witnesses. You may also need to specify if the student has had previous disciplinary actions. Finally, provide contact information for the parent/guardian and information about the student's attendance status and behavior during the detention.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Full Name** (Required): Enter the student's complete legal name.
* **Student ID Number** (Required): Enter the student's identification number.
* **Grade Level** (Required): Select the student's current grade level.
* **Homeroom Teacher** (Required): Enter the name of the student's homeroom teacher.
* **School Name** (Required): Enter the name of the school the student attends.
* **Detention Date** (Required): Enter the date when the detention will take place.
* **Detention Time** (Required): Enter the time when the detention will occur.
* **Detention Location** (Required): Enter the location where the detention will take place.
* **Expected Duration** (Required): Enter the expected length of the detention period.
* **Type of Violation** (Required): Select the type of rule or policy that was violated. Options include:
	+ Disruptive behavior
	+ Cheating/plagiarism
	+ Dress code violation
	+ Late arrival
	+ Inappropriate language
	+ Bullying
	+ Fighting
	+ Vandalism
	+ Cell phone misuse
	+ Other
* **Specific Incident Details** (Required): Provide a detailed description of the incident that led to the detention.
* **Witnesses** (Optional): List any witnesses to the incident.
* **Previous Disciplinary Actions** (Required): Select whether the student has had previous disciplinary actions. Options include:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Details of Previous Actions** (Optional): If the student has had previous disciplinary actions, provide a brief description of them.
* **Parent/Guardian Name** (Required): Enter the name of the student's parent or guardian.
* **Parent Phone Number** (Required): Enter the phone number to contact the parent/guardian.
* **Parent Email Address** (Optional): Enter the email address for communication with the parent/guardian.
* **Notification Method** (Required): Select how the parent/guardian was notified about the detention. Options include:
	+ Phone call
	+ Email
	+ Written notice sent home
	+ In-person meeting
	+ Other
* **Notification Date** (Required): Enter the date when the parent/guardian was notified.
* **Student Attendance Status** (Required): Select the student's attendance status during the detention. Options include:
	+ Attended
	+ Did not attend
	+ Excused absence
* **Behavior During Detention** (Optional): Select how the student behaved during the detention period. Options include:
	+ Appropriate
	+ Somewhat disruptive
	+ Disruptive
	+ Refused to participate
* **Additional Comments** (Optional): Provide any additional comments about the detention or student behavior.
* **Administrator Signature** (Required): Enter your signature as the administrator assigning the detention.
* **Date of Signature** (Required): Enter the date when the form is completed.
