# Yoga Instructor Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from yoga instructors for certification, registration, or other official purposes within the yoga training context.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form title to describe the specific training or certification you are seeking.
2. Select the relevant category for your training (e.g., "Education Forms" or "Other Forms").
3. Provide a brief description of your training or certification.
4. Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
5. Enter your email address.
6. Enter your phone number.
7. Select the date you are seeking training or certification (if applicable).
8. Select the time you are available for training or evaluation (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the training or certification you are seeking.
* **Category** (`form_category`, select_one, required/optional): Select the category that best describes your training or certification (e.g., "Education Forms" or "Other Forms").
* **Description** (`description`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of your training or certification.
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Select the date you are seeking training or certification (if applicable).
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Select the time you are available for training or evaluation (if applicable).

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure efficient processing of your application.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact your training administrator or contact person for guidance.
- Double-check your entry for spelling, grammar, and formatting errors before submitting the form.
- If you have any supporting documents (e.g., certificates, diplomas), please attach them separately as required.
