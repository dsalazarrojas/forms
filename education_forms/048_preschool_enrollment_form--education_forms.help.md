# Preschool Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Preschool Enrollment Form is designed to collect information from parents or guardians to register their child for a preschool program. It is intended to gather basic contact information, educational details, and enrollment preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate information.
2. Review each field for any additional guidance or instructions provided.
3. If you're unsure about any field, do not hesitate to contact the preschool administration for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Parent Name (parent_name)
* **Parent Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* Type: text
* Required: false

### 2. Address (address)
* **Address**: Enter the address where you can be contacted and where the preschool will send important information.
* Type: text
* Required: false

### 3. Birth Date (birthdate)
* **Birth Date**: Enter your child's birth date in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* Type: date
* Required: false

### 4. Phone (phone)
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* Type: text
* Required: false

### 5. Email (email)
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* Type: email
* Required: false

### 6. Start Date (start_date)
* **Start Date**: Enter the date you would like your child to start the program.
* Type: date
* Required: false

### 7. End Date (end_date)
* **End Date**: Enter the date you would like your child to end the program.
* Type: date
* Required: false

### 8. Days Attended (days_attended)
* **Days Attended**: If your child will be attending the program, enter the number of days they will be present.
* Type: number
* Required: false

### 9. Days Available (days_available)
* **Days Available**: If your child will be absent for certain days, enter the number of available days.
* Type: number
* Required: false

### 10. Tuition Fees (tuition_fees)
* **Tuition Fees**: Please enter the amount of tuition fees applicable to your account.
* Type: number
* Required: false

### 11. Select One Program (select_one_program)
* **Select One Program**: Choose one of the available programs for your child.
* Type: select_one
* Options: Program A, Program B, Program C
* Required: false

### 12. Select Multiple Programs (select_multiple_programs)
* **Select Multiple Programs**: Choose multiple programs for your child.
* Type: select_multiple
* Options: Program A, Program B, Program C
* Required: false

### 13. Time of Day (time_of_day)
* **Time of Day**: If your child attends at a specific time, enter it in HH:MM AM/PM format.
* Type: time
* Required: false

### 14. Note (note)
* **Note**: Add any additional comments or information about your child.
* Type: note
* Required: false

### 15. Confirm Email (confirm_email)
* **Confirm Email**: Confirm your email address for communication purposes.
* Type: email
* Required: false

Please fill out this form carefully and accurately. If you have any questions or need further clarification, do not hesitate to contact the preschool administration.
