# Online SelfCare Course Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Online SelfCare Course Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback and evaluation from students about their experience with the self-care course. The form collects information about student satisfaction, course content, and evaluation of the course's effectiveness in providing self-care skills and knowledge.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your student name in the `Student Name` field.
2. Provide the title of the course you are evaluating in the `Course Title` field.
3. Choose your evaluation of the course on the `Evaluation Scale` using the dropdown menu.
4. Optionally, provide any additional feedback about your experience in the `Feedback` field.
5. Enter the date you completed the course in the `Date Taken` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official student record.
* **Course Title**: Provide the exact title of the course you are evaluating, including any additional information that can help identify the specific course instance or version.
* **Evaluation Scale**: Choose the number that best represents your evaluation of the course:
	+ 1: Very Dissatisfied
	+ 2: Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ 3: Neutral
	+ 4: Somewhat Satisfied
	+ 5: Very Satisfied
* **Feedback**: Share any additional thoughts, comments, or suggestions about your experience with the course. This can include positive or negative feedback.
* **Date Taken**: Enter the date you completed the course, in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
