# Dar Al Zahra Admission Form 2019 20 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dar Al Zahra Admission Form 2019 20 is a form designed to collect essential information for student admissions at Dar Al Zahra School. The form is used to gather details about the student and their parent/guardian, including contact information, identification, and demographic data.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student name.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Provide your phone number.
4. Enter your student ID (if applicable).
5. Enter your parent's name.
6. Enter your parent's phone number.
7. Enter your date of birth.
8. Enter the name of the school you are applying to.
9. Enter your home address.
10. Enter your parent's email address (if applicable).
11. Select your grade level.
12. Select your parent's phone type.
13. Enter the date of your admission.
14. Select the time of your admission.
15. Select your gender.
16. Select your nationality.
17. Select your religion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required): Enter your legal name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address that you own and check regularly.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, `text`, optional): If you have a student ID, please enter it here.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, required): Enter your parent's or guardian's name.
* **Parent Phone Number** (`parent_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your parent's phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`student_date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the school you are applying to.
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, `text`, required): Enter your home address.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your parent's email address (if applicable).
* **Student Grade Level** (`student_grade_level`, `select_one`, required): Select your current grade level.
* **Parent Phone Type** (`parent_phone_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Select how your parent can be reached (Home, Work, Mobile).
* **Student Admission Date** (`student_admission_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your admission.
* **Admission Time** (`admission_time`, `time`, required): Select the time of your admission.
* **Student Gender** (`student_gender`, `select_one`, required): Select your gender (Male, Female).
* **Nationality** (`student_nationality`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your nationality (Egyptian, Foreign).
* **Religion** (`student_religion`, `select_one`, required): Select your religion (Muslim, Christian, Jewish, Others).
