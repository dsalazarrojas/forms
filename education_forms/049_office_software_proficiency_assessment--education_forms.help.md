# Office Software Proficiency Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Office Software Proficiency Assessment form is used to evaluate the proficiency of users in various office software applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the software applications you are proficient in from the "software_used" field.
2. Choose your level of proficiency in each selected software from the "software_skills" field.
3. Enter any relevant dates for "first_question" and "second_question" fields, if applicable.
4. Describe your experience with the "first_task" and "second_task" fields.
5. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Software Used** (`software_used`, select_one): Choose all the software applications you are proficient in (e.g., Microsoft Office, Google Docs, LibreOffice).
* **Software Skills** (`software_skills`, select_multiple): Choose your level of proficiency in each selected software (e.g., Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
* **Proficiency Level** (`proficiency_level`, select_one): Choose your overall proficiency level (e.g., Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter your phone number.
* **First Question** (`first_question`, date): Enter any relevant date, if applicable.
* **Second Question** (`second_question`, time): Enter any relevant time, if applicable.
* **First Task** (`first_task`, text): Describe your experience with the selected software.
* **Second Task** (`second_task`, text): Describe any additional experience with the selected software.
* **First Page Question** (`first_page_question`, note): This field is not used, but if you have any additional comments, you can enter them here.
* **First Page Question 2** (`first_page_question2`, note): This field is not used, but if you have any additional comments, you can enter them here.

Note: The "First Page Question", "First Page Question 2", "First_question", "Second_question", "First_task", and "Second_task" fields are not required and can be left blank if not applicable.
