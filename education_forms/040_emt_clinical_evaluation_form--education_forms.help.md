# EMT Clinical Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The EMT Clinical Evaluation Form is used to gather information about a patient's medical history and condition. It's essential to complete the form accurately and thoroughly to provide the best care possible for the patient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the patient's medical history and condition before starting the form.
* Select the correct options for each field based on the patient's medical history.
* Ensure that you have answered all required fields.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (patient_info, text, optional): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and any other relevant information.
* **Medical History** (medical_history, text, optional): Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous illnesses, injuries, or conditions.
* **Medical History Conditions** (medical_history_conditions, select_multiple, optional):
	+ **Hypertension**: High blood pressure
	+ **Diabetes**: Diabetes
	+ **Asthma**: Respiratory condition
	+ **Cancer**: Cancer diagnosis
* **Vital Signs** (vital_signs, number, optional): Enter the patient's vital signs, such as blood pressure, pulse, and respiratory rate.
* **Medical History Allergies** (medical_history_allergies, select_multiple, optional):
	+ **Penicillin**: Allergy to penicillin
	+ **Erythromycin**: Allergy to erythromycin
	+ **Polypeptides**: Allergy to polypeptides
* **Medical History Surgeries** (medical_history_surgeries, select_multiple, optional):
	+ **Yes**: Has had a surgery
	+ **No**: Has not had a surgery
* **Medical History Falls** (medical_history_falls, select_multiple, optional):
	+ **Yes**: Has had a fall
	+ **No**: Has not had a fall
* **Medical History Injuries** (medical_history_injuries, select_multiple, optional):
	+ **Yes**: Has had an injury
	+ **No**: Has not had an injury
* **Medical History Other** (medical_history_other, text, optional): Enter any other medical history information that is not listed.
* **Medications** (medications, select_multiple, optional):
	+ **Yes**: Takes medication
	+ **No**: Does not take medication
* **Medical History Medical Device** (medical_history_medical_device, select_multiple, optional):
	+ **Yes**: Uses a medical device
	+ **No**: Does not use a medical device
* **Medical History Other Medical Device** (medical_history_other_medical_device, select_multiple, optional):
	+ **Yes**: Uses other medical devices
	+ **No**: Does not use other medical devices
* **Medical History Other Other** (medical_history_other_other, select_multiple, optional):
	+ **Yes**: Has other medical history
	+ **No**: Does not have other medical history

## Tips
- Always review the patient's medical history and condition before completing the form.
- Ensure that you have answered all required fields.
- Double-check your answers for accuracy.
- This form should not be used to create multiple evaluations for the same patient.
