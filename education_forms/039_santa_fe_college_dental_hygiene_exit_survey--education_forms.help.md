# Santa Fe College Dental Hygiene Exit Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students who have completed the dental hygiene program at Santa Fe College. Your input will help us understand your experience and suggest areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this survey honestly and thoroughly.
2. Select the option that best describes your answer for each question.
3. If you have comments or suggestions, please use the "Do you have any suggestions or comments?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your major?** (<code>text</code>, optional): This question asks about your major, which will help us track the demographics of our respondents.
* **How would you rate your experience with the dental hygiene program?** (<code>select_one</code>, optional with default: Excellent): Rate your overall experience with the program using the options provided.
	+ Excellent: Very satisfied with the program.
	+ Good: Satisfied with the program.
	+ Fair: Neutral or average experience.
	+ Bad: Dissatisfied with the program.
* **Do you have any suggestions or comments?** (<code>note</code>, optional): Share any thoughts or ideas you have about the program.
* **How likely are you to recommend the program?** (<code>select_one</code>, optional with default: Likely): Indicate how likely you are to recommend the program to others.
	+ Likely: Yes, I would definitely recommend the program.
	+ Not Likely: Not likely to recommend the program.
* **What can we improve on?** (<code>text</code>, optional): Provide any specific suggestions or areas that you think need improvement.

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough in your answers.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, use the "Do you have any suggestions or comments?" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact <form@gic.mx>.
