# mbr_online_parent_teacher_meeting_2020 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mbr_online_parent_teacher_meeting_2020 form is used to capture information about online parent-teacher meetings. It's designed to facilitate communication and updates between parents and teachers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief meeting request, stating the purpose of the meeting and any relevant details.
2. Enter the date of the meeting, using the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Enter the time of the meeting in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
4. Enter the duration of the meeting in minutes.
5. Provide the location of the meeting, e.g., online meeting link or classroom number.
6. Enter any additional details about the meeting.
7. Confirm if the meeting is booked or not.
8. Select the method of communication for meeting confirmation (e.g., Email, Call, SMS).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Request**: Enter a brief description of the meeting request.
* **Meeting Date**: Enter the date of the meeting in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Meeting Time**: Enter the time of the meeting in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Meeting Duration**: Enter the duration of the meeting in minutes.
* **Meeting Location**: Enter the location of the meeting, e.g., online meeting link or classroom number.
* **Meeting Details**: Enter any additional details about the meeting.
* **Meeting Booking**: Confirm if the meeting is booked or not.
* **Meeting Booking Confirmation**: Select the method of communication for meeting confirmation (e.g., Email, Call, SMS).

## Tips
* Ensure that the meeting date and time are accurate.
* Make sure to include any relevant details about the meeting in the "Meeting Details" field.
* Be mindful of the meeting duration and location to ensure smooth communication.
