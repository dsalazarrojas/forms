# CCDRA Member Site Test - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CCDRA Member Site Test is a comprehensive evaluation of CCDRA members' knowledge and skills in court reporting. This test is designed to ensure that members have a solid understanding of essential concepts and procedures in court reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Please complete each section within the 60-minute time limit.
2.  Answer all questions and questions in each section accurately and honestly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- CCDRA Professional Examination --** (`exam_intro_section`, `note`, required: false): This section serves as an introduction to the test and explains the time limit and rules for completing the test.
* **Member Full Name** (`member_full_name`, `text`, required: true): Enter your full name as it appears on your membership card.
* **CCDRA Member ID** (`ccdra_member_id`, `text`, required: true): Enter your CCDRA member ID found on your membership card.
* **Attempt Number** (`test_attempt_number`, `select_one`, required: true): Select your attempt number (First or Second) from the options provided.
* **-- Section 1 - Ethics and Professionalism --** (`exam_section_1`, `note`, required: false): This section tests your understanding of key concepts related to ethics and professionalism in court reporting.
* **Which of the following defines professional impartiality in court reporting?** (`ethics_question_1`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose the correct definition from the options provided.
* **How should a reporter handle an off-the-record request?** (`ethics_question_2`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the correct response from the options provided.
* **-- Section 2 - Technical Procedures --** (`exam_section_2`, `note`, required: false): This section evaluates your knowledge of key technical procedures in court reporting.
* **Define 'Real-time' transcription latency requirements** (`technical_question_1`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose the correct latency requirement from the options provided.
* **What is the procedure for a broken equipment emergency?** (`technical_question_2`, `text`, required: true): Enter the correct procedure for handling a broken equipment emergency.
* **-- Section 3 - Legal Terminology --** (`exam_section_3`, `note`, required: false): This section tests your understanding of essential legal terminology in court reporting.
* **Define 'In Camera' proceedings** (`legal_term_match_1`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the correct definition from the options provided.
* **Define 'Writ of Mandamus'** (`legal_term_match_2`, `text`, required: true): Enter the correct definition of a 'Writ of Mandamus'.
* **I certify that I have completed this test without outside assistance** (`integrity_pledge`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the correct response from the options provided.
* **Date and Time of Completion** (`test_submission_timestamp`, `date`, required: true): Enter the date and time you completed this test.

## Tips

* Please complete each section within the 60-minute time limit.
* Answer all questions accurately and honestly.
* If you are unsure of any question, use your best judgment and proceed with the available options.
* Ensure that your responses are legible and accurate.
* Complete the test submission timestamp accurately.
