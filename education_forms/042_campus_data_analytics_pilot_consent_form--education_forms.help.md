# Campus Data Analytics Pilot Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals who wish to participate in the campus data analytics pilot project. It is designed to gather information about the participant, project details, data usage and privacy, and consent from the participant.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and ensure you understand the project's purpose and data collection methods.
2. Fill in your participant information, including your first name, last name, email address, and student or employee ID.
3. Select your role on campus (Student, Faculty, Staff, or Administrator).
4. Provide your department or school name.
5. Specify the purpose of the pilot project.
6. Select the data collection methods used in the project.
7. Choose how long the pilot project will last.
8. Ensure you understand data usage and privacy concerns.
9. Confirm that you understand what data will be collected, how it will be used, and that your personal information will be protected.
10. Choose how you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone, or Text Message).
11. Sign and date your consent to participate in the pilot project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your university email address.
* **Student or Employee ID**: Enter your student or employee ID number.
* **Role on Campus**: Select your role on campus (Student, Faculty, Staff, or Administrator).
* **Department or School**: Enter your department or school name.
* **Analytics Pilot Project Name**: Enter the name of the pilot project you are participating in.
* **Purpose of the Pilot Project**: Enter a brief description of the project's purpose.
* **Data Collection Methods**: Select all the methods used to collect data for the project (Academic Performance Data, Campus System Usage, Survey Responses, Event Participation, Engagement Metrics, or Other).
* **Duration of Pilot Project**: Enter the length of time the pilot project will last.
* **I understand what data will be collected**: Select "Yes, I understand" to confirm you understand what data will be collected.
* **I understand how my data will be used**: Select "Yes, I understand" to confirm you understand how your data will be used.
* **My data will be protected and kept confidential**: Select "Yes, I agree" to confirm you understand data protection policies.
* **My personal information will be anonymized**: Select "Yes, will be anonymized" to confirm you understand data anonymization practices.
* **I understand participation is voluntary**: Select "Yes, I understand" to confirm you understand your right to withdraw from the project at any time.
* **I can withdraw from the pilot project**: Select "Yes, I understand" to confirm you understand your right to withdraw from the project.
* **I grant consent to participate in this pilot**: Select "Yes, I consent" to confirm you grant consent to participate in the pilot project.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for pilot project inquiries.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Select your preferred contact method (Email, Phone, or Text Message).
* **Date of Consent**: Select the date you are signing the consent form.

## Tips

* Please ensure you read the form carefully and understand the project's purpose, data collection methods, and data usage and privacy concerns before signing.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the project team.
* You can withdraw from the pilot project at any time by contacting the project team.
