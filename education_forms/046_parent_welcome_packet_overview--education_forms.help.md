# Parent Welcome Packet Overview - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Parent Welcome Packet Overview" form is used to collect information from parents or guardians about their child's enrollment in an educational program. This form helps the school gather contact preferences and other relevant details about the parents to stay in touch and keep them informed about their child's progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the welcome packet and indicate that you have reviewed it.
2. Select your contact preferences for various means of communication (e.g., phone, email, mail).
3. Enter your name, email address, phone number, and other demographic information.
4. Provide any questions or comments you may have.
5. Confirm your contact preferences for additional communication channels.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome Packet Reviewed** (`welcome_packet_reviewed`, text, required): Check this box to confirm that you have reviewed the welcome packet.
* **Contact Preferences** (`contact_preferences`, select_one, required): Select one or more ways in which you would like to be contacted (e.g., phone, email, mail).
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter your name as a parent or guardian.

* Please review the welcome packet carefully before submitting this form.
* Make sure to select all the contact preferences that apply to you.
