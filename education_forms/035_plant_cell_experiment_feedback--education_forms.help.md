# Plant Cell Experiment Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Plant Cell Experiment Feedback form is designed to gather students' opinions and experiences with a plant cell experiment, helping educators understand how to improve and refine the experiment for future use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and position (e.g., "Teacher" or "Lab Instructor") to identify yourself as the one completing the form.
2. Enter your student number if you are a student participating in the experiment.
3. Provide any comments or feedback on your experience with the experiment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher** (`teacher_name`, text, optional): This field is for identifying the teacher or lab instructor who conducted the experiment. 
* **Student** (`student_name`, text, optional): This field is for the student to enter their name, which is helpful for feedback purposes.
* **Lab Instructor** (`lab_instructor_name`, text, optional): This field is for lab instructors to enter their names.
* **Student Number** (`student_number`, text, optional): This field is for students to enter their student number for identification purposes.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): This field is for providing detailed feedback, comments, and suggestions on the experiment.

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when providing feedback to help us understand your experience and suggestions for improvement.
- Please ensure to fill out all required fields to ensure we receive complete and accurate feedback.
- If you have any concerns or questions, don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
