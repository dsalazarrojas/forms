# seo-training-enrollment-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SEO Training Enrollment Form is used to collect information from students who are interested in enrolling in the SEO training program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the course interest that best describes your goals for the SEO training program.
2. Provide your email address so that we can contact you with more information.
3. Enter your phone number (optional, but recommended for follow-up communication).
4. Select the enrollment date for the training program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Interest** (`course_interests`, select_one, required): Select one of the following course interests:
	+ SEO Basic
	+ SEO Advanced
	+ SEO Expert
* **Student Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address. Please use a valid email address so that we can contact you with more information.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for follow-up communication (optional).
* **Enrollment Date** (`enrollment_date`, date, optional): Select the date you would like to enroll in the training program.

## Tips
* Please make sure to select at least one course interest that accurately reflects your goals for the training program.
* If you have any questions or need more information, please don't hesitate to contact us at your email address provided.
