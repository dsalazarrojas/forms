# pre-class-survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This pre-class survey is a tool for teachers and students to share their expectations and feedback with each other before the start of the class. By completing this form, you will help ensure a clear understanding of what you and your peers are looking for during the class, which will lead to a more effective and enjoyable learning experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Answer all required fields (indicated by a *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### * Pre-Class Survey (1)
**Pre-Class Survey** (`pre_class_survey`, text, required*)
This field is a text input where you can share your thoughts and expectations about the upcoming class.

### Expected Lesson Outcomes (2)
**Expected Lesson Outcomes** (`expected_lesson_outcomes`, select_multiple, optional)
Choose from the options 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your expected outcomes from the class.

### Student Feedback (3)
**Student Feedback** (`student_feedback`, text, required*)
This field is for you to provide feedback about your expectations and concerns for the class.

### Teacher Expectations (4)
**Teacher Expectations** (`teacher_expectations`, text, optional)
Share your expectations from the teacher's side, such as what you hope the teacher will do or provide during the class.

### Teacher Comments (5)
**Teacher Comments** (`teacher_comments`, text, optional)
Provide any additional comments or concerns you have for the teacher about the class.

### Student Questions (6)
**Student Questions** (`student_questions`, text, optional)
This field is for you to ask any questions or seek clarification about the class.

### Pre-Class Survey Submitter (7)
**Pre-Class Survey Submitter** (`pre_class_survey_submitted`, text, optional)
Provide your name and any other information that may be useful for the teacher to contact you.

### Student Feedback Submitter (8)
**Student Feedback Submitter** (`student_feedback_submitted`, text, optional)
Provide your name and any other information that may be useful for the teacher to contact you.

### Teacher Feedback Submitter (9)
**Teacher Feedback Submitter** (`teacher_feedback_submitted`, text, optional)
Provide your name and any other information that may be useful for the teacher to contact you.

### Student Expectations (10)
**Student Expectations** (`student_expectations`, text, optional)
Share your expectations about your own learning and participation in the class.
