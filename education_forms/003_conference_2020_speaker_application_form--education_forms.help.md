# Conference 2020 Speaker Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential speakers for the Conference 2020. The purpose of this form is to gather essential details from speakers, including their contact information, professional experience, and presentation proposal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the -- Speaker Information -- section.
2. Provide a brief summary of your presentation in the Session Abstract field.
3. Choose the most relevant category from the Primary Conference Track options.
4. Select your preferred presentation format from the Preferred Presentation Format options.
5. List the key learning objectives of your session in the Key Learning Objectives field.
6. Choose any additional audio-visual and technical requirements for your presentation.
7. Indicate if you have previously presented at this conference.
8. Agree to provide your presentation slides by the deadline if your session is selected.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Speaker Full Name** (`speaker_full_name`, text, required): Fill in your full name, as it will be used to address you and for conference materials.
- **Professional Title** (`speaker_title`, text, required): Enter your professional title, such as Professor, Research Director, or Lead Consultant.
- **Organization or Institution Affiliation** (`organization_affiliation`, text, required): Specify your organization or institution you are affiliated with.
- **Contact Email Address** (`speaker_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for us to contact you.
- **Contact Phone Number** (`speaker_phone`, text, required): Provide your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
- **Professional Biography** (`speaker_bio`, text, required): Write a 150-word or less brief summary of your professional experience and background.
- **Title of Proposed Session** (`session_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the session you are proposing.
- **Session Abstract** (`session_abstract`, text, required): Provide a brief summary of your proposed session.
- **Primary Conference Track** (`session_track`, select_one, required): Select the most relevant category from the options provided.
- **Preferred Presentation Format** (`session_format`, select_one, required): Choose the format for your presentation, such as Keynote Address or Workshop.
- **Key Learning Objectives** (`learning_objectives`, text, required): List 2-3 key learning objectives that attendees will take away from your session.
- **Audio-Visual and Technical Requirements** (`av_requirements`, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional audio-visual or technical requirements you need for your presentation.
- **Have you presented at this conference before?** (`previous_speaking_experience`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have previously presented at this conference.
- **Agree to provide presentation slides by the deadline if selected** (`submission_agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm that you will provide your presentation slides on time if your session is selected.

## Tips
* Make sure you have all necessary information ready before starting the form.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
