# Immigration Continuing Education Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from attendees on their experience with an immigration continuing education session. The goal is to understand what aspects of the session were helpful, what topics they found engaging, and how the session impacted their work.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select one option for each question from the provided choices.
2. For the "What did you find most helpful or engaging?" field, please provide a brief description in the text box.
3. You can choose multiple options for "What topics would you like to see covered in future sessions?".
4. For rating questions, select one option that best reflects your assessment.
5. In the "Suggestions" field, feel free to provide any additional thoughts or comments you have on the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main page**: This is where you will select the main topic of the session (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **What was your overall impression of the session?** (`session_evaluation`): Please type a brief note about your overall impression of the session.
* **What did you find most helpful or engaging?** (`feedback`): This is the space to briefly describe what aspects of the session were most helpful or engaging for you.
* **What topics would you like to see covered in future sessions?** (`future_topics`): Choose all that apply:
	+ Topic 1
	+ Topic 2
	+ Topic 3
* **How will the knowledge you gained impact your work?** (`professional_impact`): Select one option that best reflects your assessment.
* **Do you have any suggestions or comments?** (`suggestions`): This is where you can provide any additional thoughts or comments on the session.
* **How would you rate the relevance of the session topics?** (`session_topics`): Select one option (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **How would you rate the presentation style of the session?** (`presentation`): Select one option (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **How would you rate the materials provided?** (`materials`): Select one option (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Overall, how would you rate the session?** (`overall_experience`): Select one option (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).

Please take a moment to complete this form, and we appreciate your feedback!
