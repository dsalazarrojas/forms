# Back To School Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from respondents about their readiness for the new school year. The purpose of this form is to help school leaders understand the needs and concerns of their students, parents, and staff, and to inform decisions about resource allocation and priority setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Select your role as a respondent from the options provided.
2. Enter your name, if you choose to provide it.
3. Rate your readiness for the new school year on a scale of 1-5.
4. Specify your primary concerns, if any.
5. Assess the effectiveness of school communications.
6. Indicate your level of access to necessary technology.
7. Identify the areas where you believe the school should prioritize its resources.
8. Suggest any improvements the school leaders could make.
9. Decide if you would be available for follow-up contact.
10. Complete the form by providing the date you finished filling it out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent role** (`respondent_role`, select_one, required): Please select your role in the school as a respondent. This could be Parent, Student, Teacher, or Staff.
* **Respondent name** (`respondent_name`, text, optional): If you choose to provide your name, please enter it here.
* **Readiness rating for reopening** (`readiness_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your level of readiness for the new school year on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is low and 5 is high.
* **Primary concerns** (`concerns`, text, optional): List your primary concerns about the new school year.
* **Communication effectiveness** (`communication_effectiveness`, select_one, optional): Assess how clear and effective the school's communications have been during the summer.
* **Access to necessary technology** (`technology_access`, select_one, optional): Indicate your level of access to necessary technology, such as devices or internet connectivity.
* **Support priorities** (`support_priorities`, select_multiple, optional): Identify the areas where you believe the school should focus its resources, such as Health and Safety, Academic Recovery, Social-Emotional Support, or Technology.
* **Suggested improvements** (`suggested_improvements`, text, optional): Provide any suggestions you have for school leaders to improve the school year.
* **Accept follow up contact** (`follow_up_acceptance`, select_one, optional): Decide if you would be available for follow-up contact from school leaders.
* **Submission date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date you completed this form.

This form is designed to be completed in under 10 minutes. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to your school leaders.
