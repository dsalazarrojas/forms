# Call Or Conference Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule a call or conference with a team member or other stakeholders to discuss specific topics or issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2.  Enter the name of your organization or school in the "Organization or School Name" field.
3.  Provide your email address in the "Email Address" field, as this is where the meeting invitation will be sent.
4.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field, which will be used for day-of-meeting contact.
5.  Describe the topic of discussion in the "Topic of Discussion" field.
6.  Select your preferred meeting type from the "Preferred Meeting Type" field.
7.  Choose a preferred date and time for the meeting in the "Preferred Date - Option 1" and "Preferred Time - Option 1" fields.
8.  If you have a second preferred date and time, enter it in the "Preferred Date - Option 2" and "Preferred Time - Option 2" fields.
9.  Specify the estimated duration of the meeting in the "Estimated Duration" field.
10.  If there are additional participants joining the call, list their names in the "Names of Additional Participants" field.
11.  Provide links to any relevant documents or background information in the "Links to Relevant Documents" field.
12.  Enter any special instructions or accessibility needs for the meeting in the "Special Instructions or Accessibility Needs" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (requester_name): Enter your full name, including both your first and last names.
* **Organization or School Name** (organization_school): Enter the name of your organization or school.
* **Email Address** (email_address): Enter the email address where you would like to receive the meeting invitation.
* **Phone Number** (phone_number): Enter your phone number for day-of-meeting contact.
* **Topic of Discussion** (meeting_topic): Enter a brief summary of what you would like to discuss.
* **Preferred Meeting Type** (meeting_type): Select the type of meeting you prefer:
	+ Phone Call
	+ Video Conference (Zoom/Teams)
	+ In-Person Meeting
* **Preferred Date - Option 1** (preferred_date_1): Choose a date for the meeting.
* **Preferred Time - Option 1** (preferred_time_1): Choose a time for the meeting, including the timezone if different from EST.
* **Preferred Date - Option 2** (preferred_date_2): If you have a second preferred date, choose it here.
* **Preferred Time - Option 2** (preferred_time_2): If you have a second preferred time, choose it here.
* **Estimated Duration** (estimated_duration): Select the estimated duration of the meeting:
	+ 15 Minutes
	+ 30 Minutes
	+ 45 Minutes
	+ 60 Minutes
* **Names of Additional Participants** (additional_participants): List the names of any additional participants joining the call.
* **Links to Relevant Documents** (relevant_documents): Provide links to any relevant documents or background information.
* **Special Instructions or Accessibility Needs** (special_instructions): Enter any special instructions or accessibility needs for the meeting.
