# Kindergarten Class Family Recipe Book Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kindergarten Class Family Recipe Book Form is designed to collect information from families with children enrolled in a Kindergarten class. This form aims to gather details about the class and its students to help the school administration organize and manage the class's records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the required fields: Class Number, Teacher Last Name, Student Count, Family Relationship Status, Student Names, Student DOB, Family Name, and Class Family ID.
2. Once you have filled in the required fields, you can proceed to fill in the optional fields, such as Class Recipe Book, Recipe Name, and Ingredients (up to 14).
3. Select the correct recipe type and family relationship status from the dropdown menus.
4. Ensure that you fill in all fields accurately and completely.
