# Scholarship Reference Submission - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect a reference submission from an academic referee. The referee provides information about the submitter's qualifications, achievements, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Determine whether you are the referee providing this submission or not. If you are the submitter, please do not fill out this form, as it is intended for the referee.
2. Select the correct option in the "Is the referee a submitter?" field.
3. Provide your name and title in the "Submitter Name" and "Referee Title" fields.
4. Select the correct option in the "Referee Institution" field.
5. Provide the referee's department name in the "Referee Department" field.
6. Rate the submitter's qualifications in the "Rating" field.
7. Indicate whether you are a reference for the submitter in the "Is the referee a reference for the submitter?" field.
8. Select the type of reference in the "Reference Type" field.
9. Indicate whether the length of the reference is sufficient in the "Reference Length" field.
10. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Referee Comment" field.
11. Attach any supporting letters or documents as required.
12. Complete the "Referee Name", "Referee Phone", "Referee Email", "Referee Address", "Referee Zip", and "Referee City" fields.
13. Select the correct option in the "Referee State" field.
14. Ensure all required fields are completed accurately and honestly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Is the referee a submitter?** (`is_submitter`, select_one, required: false): 
  Determine whether you are the submitter or not. This field is used to check for self-submission.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, required: false):
  This is the name of the submitter you are providing reference for.
* **Referee Name** (`referee_name`, text, required: false):
  This is your name as the referee providing this submission.
* **Referee Title** (`referee_title`, text, required: false):
  This is your title or position as the referee.
* **Referee Institution** (`referee_institution`, select_one, required: false):
  Select the institution where you are affiliated with.
* **Referee Department** (`referee_department`, text, required: false):
  This is the department where you are affiliated with in the institution.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required: false):
  Rate the submitter's qualifications using this field.
* **Is the referee a reference for the submitter?** (`is_reference`, select_one, required: false):
  Indicate whether you are a reference for the submitter.
* **Reference Type** (`reference_type`, select_one, required: false):
  Select the type of reference you are providing.
* **Reference Length** (`reference_length`, select_one, required: false):
  Indicate whether the length of the reference is sufficient.
* **Referee Comment** (`referee_comment`, text, required: false):
  Provide any additional comments or feedback about the submitter.
* **Referee Letter** (`referee_letter`, text, required: false):
  Attach a letter from you as the referee in support of the submitter.
* **Referee Signature** (`referee_signature`, text, required: false):
  Attach your signature as the referee.
* **Referee Letter Attachment** (`referee_letter_attachment`, text, required: false):
  Attach any other supporting letters or documents.
* **Other Referee Letter Attachment** (`referee_other_attachment`, text, required: false):
  Attach any other supporting letters or documents.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, required: false):
  This is the date you submitted this form.
* **Time Submitted** (`time_submitted`, time, required: false):
  This is the time you submitted this form.
* **Referee Phone** (`referee_phone`, text, required: false):
  Provide your phone number as the referee.
* **Referee Email** (`referee_email`, email, required: false):
  Provide your email as the referee.
* **Referee Institution 2** (`referee_institution_2`, select_one, required: false):
  Select another institution you are affiliated with.
* **Submitter Institution** (`submitter_institution`, select_one, required: false):
  Select the institution the submitter is affiliated with.
* **Referee Address** (`referee_address`, text, required: false):
  Provide your address as the referee.
* **Referee Zip** (`referee_zip`, text, required: false):
  Provide your zip code as the referee.
* **Referee City** (`referee_city`, text, required: false):
  Provide your city as the referee.
* **Referee State** (`referee_state`, select_one, required: false):
  Select your state as the referee.

## Tips

* Ensure you are not providing a reference for yourself or a third-party.
* Be honest and accurate in filling out this form.
* Ask the submitter or administrative team if you have any questions or doubts.
* Attach any supporting documents or letters as required.
* Complete all required fields accurately and honestly.
