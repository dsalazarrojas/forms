# Training Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from trainees about their training experience after a session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question thoroughly and honestly.
2. Select the correct rating for your session rating and content rating.
3. Provide any suggestions or comments about how to improve the session.
4. Add any notes about the presenter that you'd like to share.
5. Rate your overall satisfaction with the training session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Feedback** (`training_feedback_form`, text, optional): Please share your thoughts about the training session, including what you liked or disliked.
* **Session Rating** (`session_rating`, number, optional): Rate the session's content on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **Content Rating** (`content_rating`, number, optional): Rate the session's content on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **Session Improve** (`session_improve`, text, optional): Suggest ways to improve the session.
* **Presenter Notes** (`presenter_notes`, text, optional): Share any notes or comments about the presenter.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the training session on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very Dissatisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about the session.

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough in your responses.
- Please select the correct rating for each field.
- If you're unsure about any field, you can always ask your session facilitator for guidance.
- This form is meant to help us improve the training sessions, so your feedback is valuable to us.
