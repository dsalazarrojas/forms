# After School Programs Enrollment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to provide the necessary information to enroll your child in our after-school programs. It will be reviewed by our team to ensure that we have all required details to provide a safe and suitable environment for your child. Please provide accurate and complete information to help us understand your child's needs and provide a smooth transition into the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your child's personal details, such as their name, date of birth, and school attended.
2. Select the after-school program that you would like your child to join.
3. Choose the program schedule and time slot that works best for you.
4. Select the duration of your child's enrollment in the program.
5. Provide your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
6. If applicable, provide emergency contact information and any necessary medical details.
7. Confirm your agreement to our program policies, terms and conditions, and payment information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Full Name** (`student_full_name`, Text, Required): Enter your child's complete legal name.
* **Student Date of Birth** (`student_date_of_birth`, Date, Required): Enter your child's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Current Grade Level** (`student_grade_level`, Select One, Required): Select your child's current grade level.
* **School Attended** (`student_school`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the school your child currently attends.
* **Student ID Number** (`student_id_number`, Text, Optional): Enter your child's student ID number (if available).
* **Select After-School Program** (`selected_program`, Select One, Required): Choose the after-school program that you would like your child to join.
* **Program Schedule Days** (`program_schedule_days`, Select Multiple, Required): Select the days of the week that you would like your child to attend the program.
* **Program Time Slot** (`program_time_slot`, Select One, Required): Choose the time slot that works best for you.
* **Enrollment Duration** (`enrollment_duration`, Select One, Required): Select the duration of your child's enrollment in the program.
* **Parent/Guardian Name** (`parent_guardian_name`, Text, Required): Enter your complete legal name.
* **Relationship to Student** (`parent_guardian_relationship`, Select One, Required): Select your relationship to the student (e.g., Mother, Father, Guardian, etc.).
* **Parent/Guardian Email** (`parent_guardian_email`, Email, Required): Enter your email address for program updates and communication.
* **Parent/Guardian Phone** (`parent_guardian_phone`, Text, Required): Enter your primary phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, Text, Required): Select the relationship of the emergency contact to the student.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, Text, Required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, Text, Optional): If applicable, enter any medical conditions that your child has.
* **Medical Conditions Description** (`medical_conditions_description`, Text, Optional): Briefly describe any medical conditions that your child has.
* **Current Medications** (`medications`, Text, Optional): If applicable, list any medications that your child is currently taking.
* **Medical Authorization** (`medical_authorization`, Select One, Required): Confirm that you authorize program staff to seek medical attention for your child if needed.
* **Transportation Method** (`transportation_method`, Select One, Required): Choose the method of transportation for your child (e.g., parent/guardian, school bus, etc.).
* **Other Authorized Adults** (`authorized_adults`, Text, Optional): If applicable, list any other authorized adults that can pick up your child.
* **Pickup Instructions** (`pickup_instructions`, Text, Optional): If applicable, provide any special instructions for dismissing your child.
* **Program Fees** (`program_fee_agreement`, Select One, Required): Confirm that you understand and agree to pay the program fees as outlined in the program materials.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, Select One, Required): Choose the method of payment for the program.
* **Parent/Guardian Signature** (`signature`, Text, Required): Sign this form to confirm your enrollment in the program.
