# Playwriting Program Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Playwriting Program Application form is designed to collect information from applicants for the Playwriting Program. This form is intended to be filled out only once by each applicant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your details in the "User Details" section.
2. Provide a 3-5 page writing sample in the "Writing Sample" section. Please make sure it's in 12 point font.
3. Describe what motivates you to apply for the Playwriting Program in the "Motivation" section.
4. If you have any additional information you'd like to share, you can do so in the "Additional Information" section (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details**: (`user_input_1`, text, required): Please enter your name, email, and any other relevant contact information in this field. This will help us get in touch with you after reviewing your application.
* **Writing Sample**: (`writing_sample_1`, text, required): This is your chance to showcase your writing skills. Please submit a 3-5 page writing sample that demonstrates your writing style and technique. Make sure it's in 12 point font to ensure we can read it easily.
* **Motivation** (`motivation_1`, text, required): Describe why you're interested in joining the Playwriting Program. What drives you to pursue your writing goals? This will help us understand your goals and aspirations.
* **Additional Information**: (`user_input_2`, text, optional): If there's anything else you'd like to share or want us to know, feel free to add it here. This is an optional field, but it might be helpful to us in reviewing your application.
