# CoC Points Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CoC Points Submission form is used to submit points earned by members through various activities within the Society for Creative Anachronism (SCA). The form requires users to provide essential information about themselves and their submission, including their name, email address, branch or barony affiliation, and a description of their work. Submissions must be accurate and original to maintain the point system's integrity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct Branch or Barony from the options provided.
2. Enter your name as it appears on your membership card or registration documents (Legal Name).
3. Enter your email address as registered with the SCA.
4. Enter the number of points you have claimed according to the SCA handbook.
5. Provide a brief description of the work or research submitted.
6. Optionally, include a link to digital documentation or photos related to your submission.
7. Verify your submission by typing your name in the Signature and Verification field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **SCA Name** (`sca_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your membership card or registration documents.
* **Legal Name** (`legal_name`, `text`, required): Enter your legal first and last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address as registered with the SCA.
* **Branch or Barony** (`branch_barony`, `text`, required): Select the local group you belong to.
* **Category of Entry** (`category_of_entry`, `select_one`, required): Select the most relevant category from the provided options (e.g., Costuming, Cooking, Armoring, etc.).
* **Description of Work** (`description_of_work`, `text`, required): Briefly describe what you created or researched.
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, `date`, required): Enter the date the work was completed.
* **Points Claimed** (`points_claimed`, `number`, required): Enter the number of points according to the SCA handbook.
* **Documentation Link** (`documentation_link`, `text`, optional): Optionally provide a URL to digital documentation or photos related to your submission.
* **Signature and Verification** (`signature_verification`, `text`, required): Type your name to verify this submission.
