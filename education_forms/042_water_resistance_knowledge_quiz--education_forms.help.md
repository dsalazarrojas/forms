# Water Resistance Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Water Resistance Knowledge Quiz is a form designed to test users' knowledge about water resistance. The form is divided into several sections, each covering a different topic related to water resistance. Users are asked to provide their answers to questions and provide feedback at the end of the form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each section of the form carefully.
2. For each section, answer the questions presented in the text boxes, providing as much detail as possible.
3. In the "Confidence Rating" section, select the confidence level you have in your answers.
4. For the "Feedback" section, provide any additional comments or thoughts you may have about the form or its questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (text, optional): This is the first section of the form, which provides background information about water resistance.
* **What is Water Resistance?** (text, optional): Answer this question in as much detail as possible, providing your understanding of the concept of water resistance.
* **Examples of Water Resistance in Real Life** (text, optional): Share examples of situations where you have encountered water resistance in real life, such as a car driving through water or a boat navigating a river.
* **Confidence Rating** (select_one, optional): Rate your confidence level in your answers to the previous questions on a scale of 1 (not confident at all) to 3 (very confident).
* **How Did You Get Your Answer?** (text, optional): If you have any thoughts on how you arrived at your answers, share them here.
* **Feedback** (text, optional): Provide any feedback or suggestions on the form or its questions.

## Tips
* Please answer each question truthfully to the best of your knowledge.
* You can ask for help if you are unsure about any question.
* This form is designed to be completed in a few minutes, so please take your time and be thorough in your answers.
