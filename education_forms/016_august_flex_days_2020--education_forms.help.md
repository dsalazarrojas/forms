# August Flex Days 2020 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to gather information from teachers regarding their interest in attending professional development workshops during the upcoming August Flex Days.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the date you are submitting this form.
2. Fill out your participant information: enter your full name, email address, and contact phone number.
3. Choose your grade level and years of teaching experience.
4. Select all workshop topics that interest you from the provided list.
5. Choose your primary workshop preference.
6. Describe your professional development goals.
7. Indicate if you can attend full days or flexible days.
8. Choose your preferred days to attend.
9. Check if you need transportation help.
10. List any dietary restrictions or allergies.
11. Mention any special technology requirements.
12. Mention if you need accessibility accommodations.
13. Indicate if you are interested in collaboration sessions.
14. Provide feedback on previous PD workshops.
15. Suggest any new workshop topics you would like to see offered.
16. Add any additional comments or questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Date of Interest Submission** (`interest_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your school email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **School or District Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter your school or district name.
* **Grade Level Taught** (`grade_level`, select_one, required): Select your grade level (Elementary K-2, Elementary 3-5, Middle School, High School, Multi-Grade, Administration, or Support Staff).
* **Subject Area or Specialty** (`subject_area`, text, required): Describe the subject area or specialty you teach.
* **Years of Teaching Experience** (`years_of_experience`, number, optional): Enter the number of years you have been teaching.
* **Workshop Topics of Interest** (`workshop_interest`, select_multiple, required): Select all workshops you are interested in attending.
* **Primary Workshop Preference** (`primary_workshop`, text, required): Choose your top preferred workshop.
* **Professional Development Goals** (`learning_goals`, text, required): Describe your professional development goals.
* **Able to Attend Full Days** (`attendance_commitment`, select_one, required): Indicate if you can attend full days (Yes - All Days, Mostly All Days, Some Days, Flexible, or Not Sure Yet).
* **Preferred Days** (`preferred_days`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your preferred days to attend (Monday Aug 3, Tuesday Aug 4, Wednesday Aug 5, Thursday Aug 6, Friday Aug 7, All Days Acceptable, or TBD).
* **Transportation Needed** (`transportation_needed`, select_one, optional): Check if you need help with transportation.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** (`dietary_restrictions`, text, optional): List any dietary restrictions or allergies.
* **Technology Needs** (`technology_requirements`, text, optional): Mention any special technology requirements.
* **Accessibility Accommodations Needed** (`accommodation_needs`, text, optional): Mention any accessibility accommodations you need.
* **Interest in Collaboration Sessions** (`collaboration_interest`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you are very, somewhat, or not interested in collaboration sessions.
* **Feedback on Previous PD** (`feedback_previous_pd`, text, optional): Provide feedback on previous professional development workshops.
* **Suggested Workshop Topics** (`suggested_topics`, text, optional): Suggest any new workshop topics you would like to see offered.
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or questions.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and honestly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* We will review your form submission and will be in touch with you soon.
