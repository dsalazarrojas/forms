# Student Data Tracking Sheet - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended for tracking student data in a comprehensive and organized manner. It collects various aspects of a student's performance and experience in school, including their details, academic progress, absences, attendance, and notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the fields with accurate and relevant information for each student.
2. Start with the "student_details" page, where you can input basic information about the student.
3. Proceed to subsequent pages to input further details about the student's performance and attendance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **student_details** (`student_details`, text, optional): Enter the student's basic details, such as name, grade level, and contact information.
* **academic_progress** (`academic_progress`, text, optional): Describe the student's academic progress, including their strengths and weaknesses.
* **student_performance** (`student_performance`, text, optional): Record notes on the student's performance, such as their academic achievements or areas where they need improvement.
* **student_absences** (`student_absences`, text, optional): List any absences, tardiness, or other attendance-related issues for the student.
* **student_attendance** (`student_attendance`, text, optional): Note the student's attendance record, including dates, periods, and frequencies of absences.
* **student_disciplinary_actions** (`student_disciplinary_actions`, text, optional): Record any disciplinary actions taken against the student, such as warnings, detentions, or suspensions.
* **student_notes** (`student_notes`, text, optional): Keep any additional notes about the student's behavior, personality, or special needs.
* **student_additional_info** (`student_additional_info`, text, optional): Enter any relevant additional information about the student that doesn't fit in other categories.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete each field accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the data collected is comprehensive and relevant.
* Review and update the form regularly to maintain an accurate and up-to-date record of each student's performance and progress.
* Use this form as a tool for tracking and monitoring student data, but also for providing feedback and support to students to help them improve their performance and reach their full potential.
