# AI Policy For Schools - Help Guide
## Purpose
The AI Policy For Schools form is designed to help educational institutions document their Artificial Intelligence policies, guidelines, and procedures for implementing AI tools and ensuring compliance.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the name of your school or institution in the "School or institution name" field.
2.  Provide the district name if your school is part of a district (leave blank if independent).
3.  Specify the effective date of your AI policy.
4.  Enter the name of the person responsible for implementing the policy.
5.  Provide an email address for AI policy inquiries.
6.  Select the range of grades your institution serves.
7.  Choose permitted AI tools for educational use.
8.  Identify prohibited AI activities.
9.  Decide on the usage of student data for AI model training.
10.  Specify hours of AI ethics training required for teachers annually.
11.  Set the minimum grade level for mandatory AI literacy education.
12.  Indicate whether you have an AI oversight committee.
13.  Select the frequency of AI policy compliance reviews.
14.  Choose the mechanism for reporting AI policy violations.
15.  Enter the next scheduled policy review date.
16.  Include any additional comments or unique aspects of your institution's AI policy.
17.  Provide the date of policy approval by the board or administration.
18.  Enter the policy version number in format X.Y (e.g., 1.0, 2.1).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **School or institution name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the full legal name of your educational institution.
*   **District name (if applicable)** (`district_name`, text, optional): If your school is part of a district, enter the district name; otherwise, leave blank.
*   **Policy effective date** (`policy_effective_date`, date, required): Specify the date your AI policy came into effect.
*   **Principal or Head of Institution** (`principal_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the person responsible for implementing and enforcing the AI policy.
*   **Contact email for AI policy inquiries** (`contact_email`, email, required): Provide an email address for inquiries related to AI policy.
*   **Grade levels served** (`grade_levels_served`, text, required): Specify the range of grades your institution educates.
*   **Permitted AI tools for educational use** (`permitted_ai_tools`, select\_multiple, optional): Select all AI tools approved for use in the classroom.
*   **Prohibited AI activities** (`prohibited_ai_activities`, select\_multiple, required): Identify all prohibited AI activities.
*   **Is student data used for AI model training?** (`ai_training_allowed`, select\_one, required): Decide on the usage of student data for AI model training.
*   **Hours of AI ethics training required for teachers annually** (`teacher_training_hours`, integer, required): Specify the hours of AI ethics training required for teachers each year.
*   **Minimum grade level for mandatory AI literacy education** (`student_ai_literacy_grade`, integer, required): Set the minimum grade level for mandatory AI literacy education.
*   **Does your institution have an AI oversight committee?** (`oversight_committee_exists`, select\_one, required): Indicate if your institution has a committee for AI oversight.
*   **Compliance and monitoring frequency** (`monitoring_frequency`, select\_one, required): Choose the frequency of AI policy compliance reviews.
*   **Mechanism for reporting AI policy violations** (`violation_reporting_mechanism`, select\_one, required): Select the method for reporting AI policy violations.
*   **Next scheduled policy review date** (`policy_review_date`, date, required): Enter the next date for scheduled policy review.
*   **Additional comments or special considerations** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Include any unique aspects of your institution's AI policy.
*   **Date of policy approval by board/administration** (`policy_approval_date`, date, required): Provide the date of policy approval by the board or administration.
*   **Policy version number** (`policy_version`, decimal, required): Enter the policy version number in format X.Y (e.g., 1.0, 2.1).

## Tips

*   Always ensure your answers are accurate and up-to-date to maintain a valid and effective AI policy.
*   If you're unsure about any field, consult your institution's policies or seek guidance from your administration.
