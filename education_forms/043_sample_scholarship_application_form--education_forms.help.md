# Sample Scholarship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from applicants for a scholarship program. The form is designed to gather details about the applicant's scholarship details, contact information, academic history, personal information, and uploaded documents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Application Form with your name and any other relevant details.
2. Provide your Scholarship Details, including any relevant information about the scholarship you are applying for.
3. Enter your Contact Info, including your address, phone number, and email.
4. Describe your Academic History, including any relevant academic achievements or qualifications.
5. Enter your Personal Info, including any other relevant personal details.
6. Upload any required documents (e.g., transcripts, letters of recommendation).
7. Review and Agree to the terms and conditions.
8. Confirm your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Application Form** (text): Enter your name and any other relevant details that you would like to share with the scholarship committee.
- **Scholarship Details** (text): Provide information about the scholarship you are applying for, including its name, amount, and any specific requirements.
- **Contact Info** (text): Enter your contact information, including your address, phone number, and email.
- **Academic History** (text): Describe your academic achievements, including any relevant courses, GPA, or qualifications.
- **Personal Info** (text): Enter any other relevant personal details that you would like to share with the scholarship committee.
- **Upload Documents** (text): Upload any required documents, such as transcripts, letters of recommendation, or any other relevant documents.
- **Review and Agree** (text): Review the form carefully and agree to the terms and conditions.
- **Confirm Submission** (text): Confirm that you have completed and submitted the form accurately.
