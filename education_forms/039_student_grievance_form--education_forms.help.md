# Student Grievance Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for students to report incidents of grievance, which are concerns or complaints they may have about their academic experience, such as academic disputes, discrimination, harassment, unfair treatment, or any other issues that may affect their well-being. By filling out this form, students can report these incidents and help us identify and resolve the problems in a timely and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form

### To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your student details, including your name and any other relevant identification information (if applicable).
2. Select the type of grievance you are experiencing (e.g., Academic Disputes, Discrimination, Harassment, etc.).
3. If necessary, enter the date and time of the incident.
4. If applicable, enter the location where the incident occurred.
5. Please provide as much detail as possible about the incident, and any supporting evidence or documentation you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Details (Required: no)**: This is the section where you enter your student ID or name, so we can verify your identity and track your complaint.
* **Grievance Type (Required: no)**: Please select the type of grievance you are experiencing from the list provided (e.g., Academic Disputes, Discrimination, Harassment, etc.). This helps us to categorize and manage your complaint.
* **Date of Incident (Required: no)**: Enter the date of the incident, if you can remember it. This helps us to track the timing of the incident.
* **Time of Incident (Required: no)**: If you have a specific time of day or period of time, please enter it. This can help us to narrow down the incident.
* **Location of Incident (Required: no)**: Enter the location where the incident occurred, if you can remember it. This helps us to understand the context of the incident.
* **Any other information about the incident**: Please provide as much detail as possible about what happened, including any supporting evidence or documentation you may have. This helps us to thoroughly investigate and resolve your grievance.
