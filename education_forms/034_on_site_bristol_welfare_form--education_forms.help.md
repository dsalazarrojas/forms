# on_site_bristol_welfare_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request on-site welfare support for learners who are currently on-site.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your User ID in the first field. This is the unique identifier for your user account.
2. Enter your name in the second field.
3. Select the city of your course location from the third field. You can choose from Bristol, London, or Oxford.
4. Select the type of welfare support you require from the fourth field. You can choose from Type 1, Type 2, or Type 3.
5. Add any additional notes in the fifth field. This is a free text area where you can provide more information about your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User ID** (`user_id`, number, required: false): A unique identifier for your user account.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Your full name.
* **Course Location** (`course_id`, select_one, required: false): The city where your course is located. You can choose from Bristol, London, or Oxford.
* **Welfare Type** (`welfare_type`, select_one, required: false): The type of welfare support you require. You can choose from Type 1, Type 2, or Type 3.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): A free text area where you can provide more information about your needs.

## Tips
* Make sure to submit the form only if you are currently on-site.
* If you have already submitted a request for the same student, please don't resubmit.
