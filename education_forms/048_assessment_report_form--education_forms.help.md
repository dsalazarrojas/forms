# Assessment Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Assessment Report Form is used to assess the performance of students or trainees in a course or training program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the evaluator or instructor who is completing the form.
2. Enter the full name of the student or trainee being assessed.
3. Rate the student's or trainee's technical knowledge and skill level on a scale from 1 to 5.
4. Rate the student's or trainee's problem-solving and critical thinking skills on a scale from 1 to 5.
5. Rate the student's or trainee's professionalism and communication skills on a scale from 1 to 5.
6. Describe the student's or trainee's observed strengths.
7. Identify specific areas for development or improvement.
8. Choose the recommended assessment status (Pass, Fail, Incomplete, Pass with Honors).
9. Provide direct feedback for the student or trainee (optional).
10. Enter the date of the report.
11. Mark the supervisor approval status (Pending Review, Approved and Finalized, Revision Requested).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Evaluator or Instructor Name (1)**: Enter the name of the person completing this form.
* **Student or Trainee Full Name (2)**: Enter the full name of the student or trainee being assessed.
* **Technical Knowledge and Skill Rating (3)**: Rate the student's or trainee's technical knowledge and skill level on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Problem Solving and Critical Thinking (4)**: Rate the student's or trainee's problem-solving and critical thinking skills on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Professionalism and Communication (5)**: Rate the student's or trainee's professionalism and communication skills on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Observed Primary Strengths (6)**: Describe what the student or trainee did well.
* **Specific Areas for Development (7)**: Identify what the student or trainee needs to improve on.
* **Recommended Assessment Status (8)**: Choose one of the following options: Pass, Fail, Incomplete, Pass with Honors.
* **Direct Feedback for the Student (9)**: Provide actionable advice for the student or trainee (optional).
* **Date of Report (10)**: Enter the date of the report.
* **Supervisor Approval Status (11)**: Mark as Pending Review, Approved and Finalized, or Revision Requested.
