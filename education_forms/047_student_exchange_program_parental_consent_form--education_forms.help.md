# Student Exchange Program Parental Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Exchange Program Parental Consent Form is a document that requires parents or guardians to confirm their consent and provide additional information for their child's participation in the student exchange program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your child's name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide the address and phone number of an emergency contact person in case we need to reach out to them.
3. Choose your level of consent for your child's participation in the student exchange program.
4. Select your child's school year of study.
5. Add a note with your signature, if you wish to leave a personal message for school administrators.
6. Date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Enter your child's full name as per their school records.
* **Parent Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding the program. Please ensure it is your active email.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where we can reach you in case of emergency.
* **Address**: Enter your home or residential address, where we can contact you.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of an emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Address**: Enter the address of the emergency contact person.
* **Parent Consent**: Select one of the options for your consent level for your child's participation in the student exchange program.
* **Emergency Contact Consent**: Select all options that apply for your emergency contact's consent for your child's participation in the student exchange program.
* **School Year**: Enter your child's school year of study.
* **Student School Year**: Enter your child's current or next school year of study, if different from their school year.
* **Parent Signature**: This is a note section for you to add any personal messages or comments.
* **Date**: Date this form by filling in the date.
