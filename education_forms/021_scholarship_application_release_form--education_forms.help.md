# scholarship_application_release_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Scholarship Application Release Form is used to release information about a student's application for a scholarship. This form helps to gather essential details about the student's contact preferences, contact details, and school information to facilitate the scholarship application process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields as instructed.
2. Ensure you fill in all required fields.
3. Double-check your information for accuracy.
4. Submit the form once you have filled out all the necessary fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Details** (`application_details`): 
    * Enter a brief description of the scholarship application.
* **Student Info** (`student_info`): 
    * Enter information about the student, such as name, phone number, email, and address.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`): 
    * Enter information about the contact person, such as name, email, and phone number.
* **Contact Preferences** (`contact_preferences`): 
    * Enter your contact preferences, such as how to contact you and your preferred method of communication.
* **Upload Documents** (`upload_documents`): 
    * Upload any supporting documents required for the application.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`): 
    * Enter the student's email address.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`): 
    * Enter the student's name.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`): 
    * Enter the student's phone number.
* **Student Address** (`student_address`): 
    * Enter the student's address.
* **Student City** (`student_city`): 
    * Enter the student's city.
* **Student State** (`student_state`): 
    * Select the student's state from the dropdown menu.
* **Student Country** (`student_country`): 
    * Enter the student's country.
* **Student Zip** (`student_zip`): 
    * Enter the student's zip code.
* **Student School** (`student_school`): 
    * Select the student's school type from the dropdown menu.
* **School Name** (`school_name`): 
    * Enter the name of the school the student is attending.
* **Program** (`program`): 
    * Select the type of program the student is enrolled in.
* **Student Email 2** (`student_email_2`): 
    * Enter the student's email address.
* **Student Phone 2** (`student_phone_2`): 
    * Enter the student's phone number.
* **Student Address 2** (`student_address_2`): 
    * Enter the student's address.

* **Student School 2** (`student_school_2`):
