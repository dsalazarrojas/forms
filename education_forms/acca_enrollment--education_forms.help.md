# ACCA Enrollment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for enrollment in the ACCA program. 

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully read each field's label and description before filling out the form.
- Fill out the form online or on paper, depending on your preference.
- Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`full_name`, required): Provide your full name as it appears on an official ID.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, required): Enter your university or personal email address.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, optional): Include your country code (e.g., +44 ...).
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, optional): If you are unsure, please leave this field blank.
* **Age** (`age`, optional): If unknown, leave this field blank.
* **Mailing address** (`mailing_address`, optional): Enter your street address, city, and postal code.
* **Emergency contact name** (`emergency_contact`, optional): Include their relationship (e.g., parent, spouse).
* **Emergency contact phone** (`emergency_phone`, optional): Enter the best reachable number for the emergency contact.
* **Registration type** (`registration_type`, required): Choose the category that applies:
	+ New registrant
	+ Returning registrant
	+ Transfer from another program
	+ Conversion candidate
* **Accounting/finance experience level** (`experience_level`, optional): Select the option that best describes your prior experience:
	+ Beginner
	+ Intermediate
	+ Advanced
	+ Professional
* **Preferred study mode** (`preferred_study_mode`, required): Select how you intend to study for ACCA:
	+ Full-time
	+ Part-time
	+ Online
	+ Self-study
* **Institution or employer** (`institution_or_employer`, optional): Enter the name of your current employer or educational institution (if applicable), or 'N/A' if not applicable.
* **Job title or student level** (`position_or_role`, optional): Enter your job title or student level (e.g., Finance Assistant, Final-year student).
* **Highest academic qualification** (`highest_qualification`, optional): Select the highest completed qualification:
	+ High school diploma
	+ Bachelor's degree
	+ Postgraduate degree
	+ Professional qualification
	+ Other
* **Funding source for studies** (`funding_source`, optional): Select the source of funding for your studies:
	+ Self-funded
	+ Employer-sponsored
	+ Scholarship
	+ Government sponsorship
	+ Other
* **Supporting documents submitted** (`supporting_documents`, optional): Select all that apply:
	+ Official ID
	+ Academic transcript
	+ Passport photo
	+ Proof of address
	+ Reference letter
* **Consent to process enrollment and membership** (`consent_data_processing`, required): Select one option:
	+ I consent
	+ I do not consent
* **Additional comments or special requirements** (`additional_comments`, optional): Enter any additional comments or special requirements.
* **Program note: Free registration granted and membership fees covered by the program** (`acknowledgement_note`, note): This is a note explaining that registration fees are covered for the program.
 
## Tips
- Be thorough in filling out the form to ensure accurate and efficient processing of your registration.
- If you need assistance or have questions, contact the relevant authorities mentioned in the form's metadata.
