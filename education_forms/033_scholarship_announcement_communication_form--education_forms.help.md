# scholarship_announcement_communication_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The scholarship announcement communication form is used to share information about an upcoming scholarship opportunity with the university community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the title of the scholarship in the `scholarship_name` field.
2. Select the type of scholarship (yes or no) in the `scholarship_type` field.
3. Enter the eligibility criteria for the scholarship in the `scholarship_eligibility_criteria` field.
4. Enter the description of the scholarship in the `scholarship_description` field.
5. Enter the contact person's name in the `contact_person` field.
6. Enter the contact person's email address in the `contact_email` field.
7. Enter the contact person's phone number in the `contact_phone` field.
8. Enter the announcement link for the scholarship in the `announcement_link` field.
9. Upload any relevant files for the scholarship in the `upload_files` field (optional).
10. Enter the start date of the scholarship in the `scholarship_start_date` field.
11. Enter the end date of the scholarship in the `scholarship_end_date` field.
12. Select the status of the scholarship (active or inactive) in the `scholarship_status` field.
13. Enter the submitted date and time of the scholarship in the `submitted_date` and `submitted_time` fields, respectively.
14. Enter the submitter's email address in the `submitter` field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Scholarship Name** (`scholarship_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the scholarship being announced.
* **Scholarship Type** (`scholarship_type`, select_one, required): Select 'yes' or 'no' to indicate the type of scholarship.
* **Scholarship Description** (`scholarship_description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the scholarship.
* **Applicant Information** (`applicant_information`, note, optional): Enter any relevant information about the applicant(s) for the scholarship.
* **Scholarship Eligibility Criteria** (`scholarship_eligibility_criteria`, text, required): Enter the eligibility criteria for the scholarship.
* **How to Apply** (`how_to_apply`, text, optional): Enter any instructions on how to apply for the scholarship.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person for the scholarship.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Contact Email Alternate** (`contact_email_alternate`, email, optional): Enter an alternate email address for the contact person (optional).
* **Contact Phone Alternate** (`contact_phone_alternate`, text, optional): Enter an alternate phone number for the contact person (optional).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about the scholarship.
* **Scholarship Announcement** (`scholarship_announcement`, text, required): Enter the announcement text for the scholarship.
* **Scholarship Start Date** (`scholarship_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the scholarship.
* **Scholarship End Date** (`scholarship_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the scholarship.
* **Scholarship Status** (`scholarship_status`, select_one, required): Select 'active' or 'inactive' to indicate the status of the scholarship.
* **Announcement Link** (`announcement_link`, text, required): Enter the link to the scholarship announcement.
* **Upload Files** (`upload_files`, note, optional): Upload any relevant files for the scholarship (optional).
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person submitting the scholarship (optional).
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who submitted the scholarship (optional).
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, date, required): Enter the date the scholarship was submitted.
* **Submitted Time** (`submitted_time`, time, required): Enter the time the scholarship was submitted.
* **Submitted IP** (`submitted_ip`, text, optional): Enter the IP address of the person who submitted the scholarship (optional).
* **Submitted User** (`submitted_user`, text, optional): Enter the username of the person who submitted the scholarship (optional).
