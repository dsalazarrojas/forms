# Photoelectric Effect Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your understanding of the photoelectric effect, a fundamental principle of quantum mechanics. The photoelectric effect is the ability of light to eject electrons from a material, such as a metal, when the light hits it. This form will guide you through a series of questions to test your knowledge and understanding of this phenomenon.
