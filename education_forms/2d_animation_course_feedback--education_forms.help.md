# 2D Animation Course Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students on a 2D Animation Course. Your input will help instructors or course creators improve the course experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your student name and email
2. Provide the course title
3. Specify the course date or term end
4. Rate the course content clarity and pacing
5. Select the practical skills you learned (if any)
6. List the software tools used in the course
7. Rate the overall course (1-10)
8. Decide if you would recommend this course
9. Provide suggestions for improving the course
10. Add any additional comments or testimonials

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student name**: Please enter your name as a student who took the 2D Animation Course.
* **Student email**: Enter your student email address for contact purposes.
* **Course title**: Enter the title of the course you took, e.g., "Intro to 2D Animation".
* **Course date or term end**: Specify the date when the course ended or concluded.
* **How clear was the course content?**: Rate how clear the course content was to you, using the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Pacing of the lessons**: Rate how well paced the lessons were for you, using the options: Too fast, Just right, or Too slow.
* **Practical skills learned (select all that apply)**: Choose any practical skills you learned during the course, such as Line drawing and clean-up, Timing and spacing, etc.
* **Tools/software used in the course**: List the software tools or software used in the course, e.g., Toon Boom, Harmony, or Blender.
* **Overall course rating (1-10)**: Rate the overall quality of the course, with 10 being outstanding.
* **Would you recommend this course?**: Decide if you would recommend this course to others, using the options: Definitely, Probably, Not sure, Probably not, or Definitely not.
* **Suggestions for improving the course**: Provide specific suggestions on how to improve the course.
* **Additional comments or testimonials**: Add any additional comments or testimonials about your experience with the course.
