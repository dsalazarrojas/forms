# Student Consents 2019 2020 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect consents and information from students and their parents for the 2019-2020 school year.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Student ID**: Please enter your student ID number in this field.
2. **Parent Name**: Please enter your name as the parent or guardian of the student.
3. **School Years**: Please enter the school years you are currently enrolled in.
4. **Grade Level**: Please select your current grade level.
5. **Date Of Birth**: Please enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
6. **Phone Number**: Please enter your phone number.
7. **Email Address**: Please enter your email address.
8. **Parent Signature**: Please sign your name in this field to confirm your consent.
9. **Student Signature**: Please sign your name in this field to confirm your consent.
10. **School Name**: Please enter the name of your school.
11. **School Address**: Please enter the address of your school.
12. **School City**: Please enter the city of your school.
13. **School State**: Please enter the state of your school.
14. **School Zip**: Please enter the zip code of your school.
15. **Student Grade Level**: Please select your grade level.
16. **Parent Grade Level**: Please select your grade level.
17. **Student Attendance**: Please enter your attendance record.
18. **Student Absent Reason**: Please enter the reason for your absence.
19. **Student Absent Date**: Please enter the date of your absence.
20. **Student Absent Reason Note**: Please enter any additional notes about your absence.
21. **Teacher Name**: Please enter the name of your teacher.
22. **Teacher Email**: Please enter the email address of your teacher.
23. **Teacher Phone**: Please enter the phone number of your teacher.
24. **Student Absent Teacher**: Please enter the name of your absent teacher.
25. **Student Notes**: Please enter any additional notes about your student.
