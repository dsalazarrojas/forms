# Remote Learning Course Contract Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students about their remote learning course enrollment. It serves as a contract between the student and the course instructor, outlining the terms and conditions of the course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the enrollment date of the course.
2. Enter your name as the student.
3. Provide your email address for communication purposes.
4. Enter your phone number for contact and emergency purposes.
5. Enter your address for contact and delivery purposes.
6. Read and agree to the course terms and conditions.
7. Enter the name of the course instructor.
8. Describe the enrollment terms of the course.
9. Provide the course cancellation policy.
10. Describe the refund policy.
11. Provide the best way to contact the instructor.
12. Confirm that you have read and agreed to the course contract.
13. Fill in the start date of the course.
14. Fill in the end date of the course.
15. Fill in the start time of the course.
16. Describe the duration of the course.
17. Select the current status of the course (Active, Inactive, Pending, Completed, Cancelled).
18. Enter the location of the course.
19. Fill in the price of the course.
20. Enter the currency of the course.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enrollment Date**: Enter the date you plan to start the course.
* **Student Name**: Enter your name as the student.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact and emergency purposes.
* **Address**: Enter your address for contact and delivery purposes.
* **Course Terms Conditions**: Read and agree to the course terms and conditions.
* **Instructor**: Enter the name of the course instructor.
* **Enrollment Terms**: Describe the enrollment terms of the course.
* **Cancellation Policy**: Provide the course cancellation policy.
* **Refund Policy**: Describe the refund policy.
* **Contact Instructor**: Provide the best way to contact the instructor.
* **Student Confirmation**: Confirm that you have read and agreed to the course contract.
* **Start Date**: Fill in the start date of the course.
* **End Date**: Fill in the end date of the course.
* **Start Time**: Fill in the start time of the course.
* **Duration**: Describe the duration of the course.
* **Status**: Select the current status of the course (Active, Inactive, Pending, Completed, Cancelled).
* **Location**: Enter the location of the course.
* **Price**: Fill in the price of the course.
* **Currency**: Enter the currency of the course.
