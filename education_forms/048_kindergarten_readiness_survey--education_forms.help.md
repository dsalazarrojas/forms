# Kindergarten Readiness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kindergarten Readiness Survey is a tool to assess a child's preparedness and readiness to transition from pre-school to kindergarten.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Fill out the form for each child only once.
3. Select the options that best describe your child's skills and readiness level.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Child Information** (`Child Information`, Select One, required: false): This section is used to identify the child's gender. Please select Male, Female, or Other.
* **Parent Information** (`Parent Information`, Select One, required: false): This section is used to identify the marital status of the parent or guardian. Please select Single Parent, Married Couple, or Other.
* **Readiness Level** (`Readiness Level`, Number, required: false): This question assesses the child's overall readiness to transition to kindergarten on a scale of 1-5.
* **Communication Skills** (`Communication Skills`, Select Multiple, required: false): Please rate the child's communication skills as Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Social Skills** (`Social Skills`, Select Multiple, required: false): Please rate the child's social skills as Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Emotional Readiness** (`Emotional Readiness`, Select One, required: false): This question assesses the child's emotional readiness to transition to kindergarten on a scale of High, Medium, or Low.
* **Educational Readiness** (`Educational Readiness`, Number, required: false): This question assesses the child's educational readiness to transition to kindergarten on a scale of 1-5.
