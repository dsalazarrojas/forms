# Automata Theory Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to test your understanding of automata theory concepts, specifically finite automata, regular languages, pushdown automata, and Turing machines. It consists of three sections, each covering a different area of study. The form is intended to be completed by students or researchers interested in assessing their knowledge in these areas.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your student information, including your full name and university identifier (Student ID).
2. Provide your email address for quiz results.
3. Answer the questions in each section of the form. Each section is divided into specific fields with multiple-choice or text-based questions.
4. Confirm that the submitted work is your own by checking the "Submit for Grading?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Student Information --**: This section collects your student information, including your full name and university identifier (Student ID), which are required fields. This is for internal identification purposes.
* **Student Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Student ID**: Enter your university-issued student identifier.
* **Email Address**: Provide an email address where you can receive quiz results. This is for internal purposes only.
* **-- Quiz Section 1 - Finite Automata --**: 
  * **What is the formal definition of a DFA?**: Select the most accurate description of the formal definition of a DFA. Typically, a DFA is defined as a 5-tuple.
  * **DFA vs NFA Explanation**: Briefly explain the primary difference between a DFA and an NFA. This is a text-based field, allowing you to provide a concise explanation.
  * **Properties of Regular Languages**: Select all properties that apply to regular languages from the provided options (Closure under Union, Closure under Intersection, Closure under Complement, Closure under Concatenation).
* **-- Quiz Section 2 - Pushdown Automata and Context-Free Languages --**:
  * **Common PDA Stack Operations**: Select the core operation of a pushdown automata from the provided options (Push and Pop, Shift and Reduce, Enqueue and Dequeue).
  * **CFG Example for Palindrome**: Provide a simple production rule for a binary palindrome using Context-Free Grammar.
* **-- Quiz Section 3 - Turing Machines --**:
  * **The Turing Machine Halting Problem is**: Select the correct decidability status from the provided options (Decidable, Undecidable, Semi-decidable).
  * **Church-Turing Thesis Summary**: Briefly summarize the Church-Turing Thesis in your own words.
* **Submit for Grading?**: Check this field to confirm that the submitted work is your own. If you are unsure, select "False." If you are sure, select "True."
