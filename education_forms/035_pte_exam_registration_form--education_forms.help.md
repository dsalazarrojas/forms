# PTE Exam Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students who want to register for the PTE exam. Please fill out this form to provide us with your details and exam preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out each page of the form carefully and accurately.
- Make sure to complete all required fields (if any) as they are marked with an asterisk (\*). 
- You can skip optional fields if you do not have the information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Student Details

* **Student Name** (`student_details`, text, optional): This is where you enter your name as per your official government-issued ID or passport.
* **Exam Preferences** (`exam_preferences`, text, optional): In this field, you can specify any preferences you may have for your exam, such as exam date, time, or location.

### Page 2: E-Signature

* **E-Signature** (`e_signature`, note, optional): Please sign your name here to verify your identity. This is an electronic signature field for your official name as per your government-issued ID or passport.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to avoid any issues with your exam registration.
- If you have any issues or need help, contact us at form@gic.mx for assistance.
