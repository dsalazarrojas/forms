# Student Transcript Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help students request transcripts for academic records, financial aid, or other purposes. It collects necessary information to process the request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your student name in the "student name" field.
2. Enter your date of birth.
3. Provide your email address for contact and communication purposes.
4. Enter your student ID.
5. Enter the name of the institution where you studied.
6. Select the type of transcript you need (e.g., Academic Records, Academic Records for Financial Aid).
7. If needed, provide additional comments about your request.
8. Confirm or decline the student signature.
9. Confirm or decline the institution signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text): Enter your full name as it appears on your academic records.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, text): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, text): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted regarding your transcript request.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text): Enter your student ID number.
* **Institution Name** (`institution_name`, text): Enter the name of the institution where you studied.
* **Transcript Type** (`transcript_type`, select_one): Select the type of transcript you need (e.g., Academic Records, Academic Records for Financial Aid).
* **Date of Submission** (`date_of_submission`, date): Enter the date you submitted this form (automatically populated).
* **Time of Submission** (`time_of_submission`, time): Enter the time you submitted this form (automatically populated).
* **Comments** (`comments`, note): If you need to provide additional information or context about your request, enter it here.
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, select_multiple): Confirm or decline to sign the transcript request.
* **Institution Signature** (`institution_signature`, select_multiple): Confirm or decline to sign the transcript request.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure efficient processing of your transcript request.
* If you need help or have questions, contact the institution's support team for assistance.
