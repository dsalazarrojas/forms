# Fundamentals Learning Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information for the Fundamentals Learning Program, which provides students with a solid foundation in various subjects. By filling out this form, you will be able to enroll in the program and receive updates and notifications about program events and resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the program name to identify the specific program you are enrolling in.
2. Select the type of institution where you will be taking the program (e.g., School or University).
3. Enter the start date of the program.
4. Enter the start time of the program.
5. Enter the end date of the program.
6. Enter the duration of the program.
7. Enter your name as a student enrolled in the program.
8. Enter your email address for program-related notifications and updates.
9. Enter your phone number for program-related contact.
10. Provide any additional notes about yourself as a student.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **program_name** (text, required): Enter the name of the program you are enrolling in. This will help us identify the correct program and provide relevant information.
* **institution_type** (select_one, optional): Select the type of institution where you will be taking the program (e.g., School or University).
* **start_date** (date, optional): Enter the start date of the program. This will help us schedule events and updates for you.
* **start_time** (time, optional): Enter the start time of the program.
* **end_date** (date, optional): Enter the end date of the program.
* **end_time** (time, optional): Enter the end time of the program.
* **duration** (number, optional): Enter the duration of the program. This will help us estimate the program's length.
* **student_name** (text, required): Enter your name as a student enrolled in the program.
* **student_email** (email, optional): Enter your email address for program-related notifications and updates.
* **student_phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for program-related contact.
* **student_notes** (note, optional): Provide any additional notes about yourself as a student. This is a free-text field for any information you'd like to share with us.
* **student_email_2** (text, optional): This field is currently not clearly defined and should be removed or redefined for clarity and usability.
