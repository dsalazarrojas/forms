# Trainer Readiness Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help assess the readiness of trainers for a specific training program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the category of the training program you are assessing your readiness for.
2. Provide a brief description of the training program.
3. List your skills and qualifications related to the training program.
4. If applicable, provide feedback on your previous training experiences.
5. Add any additional comments you have about your training program.
6. Select the deliverables you are able to provide as part of the training program.
7. Assess your readiness for specific aspects of the training program using the provided drop-down menus.
8. Enter the date and time of the training program.
9. Enter your email address (if you have one).
10. Provide a brief summary of your phone number (if applicable).
11. Assess your readiness for specific aspects of the training program using the provided drop-down menus.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`): Select the category of the training program you are assessing your readiness for.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`): Provide a brief description of the training program.
* **Skills and Qualifications 1** (`skills_and_qualifications_1`, `number`): List your skills and qualifications related to the training program.
* **Skills and Qualifications 2** (`skills_and_qualifications_2`, `number`): List any additional skills and qualifications related to the training program (if applicable).
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`): Provide feedback on your previous training experiences (if applicable).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`): Add any additional comments you have about your training program.
* **Deliverables** (`training_deliverables`, `select_multiple`): Select the deliverables you are able to provide as part of the training program.
* **Trainer Readiness Assessment 1** (`trainer_readiness_assessment_1`, `select_one`): Assess your readiness for specific aspects of the training program using the provided drop-down menu.
* **Trainer Readiness Assessment 2** (`trainer_readiness_assessment_2`, `select_one`): Assess your readiness for specific aspects of the training program using the provided drop-down menu.
* **Trainer Readiness Assessment 3** (`trainer_readiness_assessment_3`, `select_one`): Assess your readiness for specific aspects of the training program using the provided drop-down menu.
* **Additional Training Programmes** (`additional_training_programmes`, `text`): Enter any additional training programs you are offering.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`): Enter the date of the training program.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`): Enter the time of the training program.
* **Trainer Readiness Assessment 4** (`trainer_readiness_assessment_4`, `select_one`): Assess your readiness for specific aspects of the training program using the provided drop-down menu.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`): Enter your email address (if you have one).
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`): Provide a brief summary of your phone number (if applicable).
* **Trainer Readiness Assessment 5** (`trainer_readiness_assessment_5`, `select_multiple`): Assess your readiness for specific aspects of the training program using the provided drop-down menu.

Please note that this form is meant to be completed by trainers to self-assess their readiness to provide training, and the results will be used by program managers or administrators to review and approve their readiness.
