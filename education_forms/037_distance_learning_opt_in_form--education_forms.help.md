# Distance Learning Opt In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information from students or parents interested in distance learning programs. Please fill out the form to express your interest and provide required information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out all the fields with your personal information and course preferences.
2. Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
3. Submit the form to express your interest in distance learning programs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Distance Learning Form** (`distance_learning_form`, text, optional): This field is for any additional comments or notes about your interest in distance learning programs.
* **School Id** (`school_id`, number, optional): This field is for your school ID, if applicable.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): This field is for your name, if you are a student interested in distance learning programs.
* **Course Code** (`course_code`, text, optional): This field is for any course codes you are interested in.
* **Distance Learning Start Date** (`distance_learning_start_date`, date, optional): This field is for the expected start date of your distance learning program.
* **Distance Learning End Date** (`distance_learning_end_date`, date, optional): This field is for the expected end date of your distance learning program.
* **Contact No** (`contact_no`, text, optional): This field is for your contact number, if you have any.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, optional): This field is for your email address, if you are a student interested in distance learning programs.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): This field is for your parent's name, if you are a student interested in distance learning programs.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all the required fields, if any.
* Be accurate and clear in your responses to ensure we can contact you properly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us after submitting the form.
