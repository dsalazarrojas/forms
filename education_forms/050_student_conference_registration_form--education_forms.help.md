# Student Conference Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students about their registration for a conference. The form includes fields for student selection of program, student details, and contact information, which will help the conference organizers plan and prepare for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your program of interest from the list of options.
2. Provide your student details, such as name, date of birth, grade level, and any other relevant information.
3. Enter your contact information, including email and phone number.
4. Review your selections before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Program Selection (`program_selection`)
*   **Program Selection** (`program_selection`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the program that interests you the most from the options provided.

### Student Info (`student_info`)
*   **Student Info** (`student_info`, `note`, required: false): This section is a text box for you to provide any additional student information that you feel is relevant.

### Student Phone (`student_phone`)
*   **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your student's phone number, or any other contact information that you would like to provide.

### Student Email (`student_email`)
*   **Student Email** (`student_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your student's email address, or any other contact information that you would like to provide.

### Student Date of Birth (`student_date_of_birth`)
*   **Student Date of Birth** (`student_date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): Enter your student's date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.

### Student Name (`student_name`)
*   **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your student's name.

### Student Grade Level (`student_grade_level`)
*   **Student Grade Level** (`student_grade_level`, `number`, required: false): Enter your student's grade level.

### Student Attendee (`student_attendee`)
*   **Student Attendee** (`student_attendee`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional information about the student that you would like to provide.

### Student Grade (`student_grade`)
*   **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, `number`, required: false): Enter your student's grade.

### Student Program (`student_program`)
*   **Student Program** (`student_program`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the student program that you are interested in attending.
