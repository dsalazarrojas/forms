Lincoln East Band Forms - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Lincoln East Band Forms is a collection of forms used to gather information from applicants for the Lincoln East Band. This form is used to collect personal and financial information from applicants to facilitate the payment and application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2.  Complete each field as follows:
    - **Application**: This is the applicant's name and contact information.
    - **Tshirts**: Enter the size and color of the t-shirt you are requesting.
    - **Other Materials**: Describe any additional materials you are requesting.
    - **Payment Method**: Select the payment method you would like to use, such as cash, check, or online payment.
    - **Consent**: Indicate your consent for the payment method selected.
    - **Payment Amount**: Enter the amount to be paid.
    - **Payment Date**: Enter the date of the payment.
    - **Other Materials Price**: Enter the price of the other materials you are requesting.
    - **Application Confirmation**: A note field for the applicant to confirm their application.
    - **Tshirt Size**: Select the size of the t-shirt you are requesting.
    - **Application Date**: Enter the date of the application.
    - **Other Payment Method**: Enter any additional payment method information, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Application**: The applicant's name and contact information.
*   **Tshirts**: Enter the size and color of the t-shirt you are requesting.
*   **Other Materials**: Describe any additional materials you are requesting.
*   **Payment Method**: The method of payment for the order, with options for cash, check, and online payment.
*   **Consent**: Indicate your agreement to the terms of the payment method selected.
*   **Payment Amount**: The amount to be paid for the order.
*   **Payment Date**: The date of the payment.
*   **Other Materials Price**: The price of the other materials being requested.
*   **Application Confirmation**: A note field for the applicant to confirm their application.
*   **Tshirt Size**: The size of the t-shirt being requested.
*   **Application Date**: The date of the application.
*   **Other Payment Method**: Enter any additional payment method information, if needed.
