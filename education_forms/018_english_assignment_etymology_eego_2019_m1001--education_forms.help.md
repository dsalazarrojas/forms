# English Assignment ETYMOLOGY EEgo 2019 M1001 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect demographic information from students for the English Assignment ETYMOLOGY course in 2019.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your information. Each field has been labeled to help guide you through the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Section** (`form_section`, text, optional): This is the main content section of the form where you can provide any additional information or comments.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification or records.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, optional): Enter your student ID number for verification purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date on which you are submitting this form.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time on which you are submitting this form.

## Tips
- Be accurate and honest when filling in your personal details.
- Ensure that all fields are filled in correctly to avoid errors.
- If you need help or have questions, contact your course administrator or instructor.
