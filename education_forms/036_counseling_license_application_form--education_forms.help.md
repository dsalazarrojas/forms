# Counseling License Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals seeking to obtain a counseling license. It is a critical document that will be reviewed internally to assess your eligibility for licensure.

## How To Complete This Form

* Ensure you have all the required information before filling out the form.
* Review each section carefully to provide accurate and complete information.
* Fill out the form in a clear and legible handwriting.
* Make sure to submit the form only once you have reviewed and verified the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Info (1)**: Provide your name and contact information.
* **Education (2)**: List your educational background, including the institution and degree earned.
* **License Type (3)**: Indicate whether you are applying for a Counseling License or an Associate Counselor License.
* **Experience (4)**: Briefly describe your relevant work experience in the field of counseling.
* **Education Level (5)**: Select your highest level of education earned, which could be High School, Bachelor's Degree, Master's Degree, or Doctoral Degree.
* **Work Experience (6)**: List your work experience in the field of counseling, including the dates of employment.
* **Work License (7)**: Indicate whether your work license is Active, Inactive, or Pending.
* **Education Institution (8)**: Provide the name of the institution where you earned your degree.
* **Submit (9)**: This is the submit button. Please review and verify your information before submitting the form.
* **License Status (10)**: Indicate the status of your license, which could be Active, Inactive, or Pending.
* **Education Degree (11)**: Select the degree you earned, which could be Bachelor's, Master's, or Doctoral.
* **Education Field (12)**: Select the field of education you are applying for, which could be Psychology, Counseling, or Social Work.
* **Education Start Date (13)**: Provide the start date of your education.
* **Education End Date (14)**: Provide the end date of your education.
* **License Number (15)**: Provide the number of your license, if applicable.
* **License Issuer (16)**: Provide the name of the institution or agency that issued your license.
* **Experience Start Date (17)**: Provide the start date of your work experience in the field of counseling.
* **Experience End Date (18)**: Provide the end date of your work experience in the field of counseling.
* **Work Start Date (19)**: Provide the start date of your current employment in the field of counseling.
* **Work End Date (20)**: Provide the end date of your current employment in the field of counseling.
* **License Years of Service (21)**: Indicate the number of years you have been serving as a counselor.
* **Work Years of Service (22)**: Indicate the number of years you have been working as a counselor.
* **Education Issuer (23)**: Provide the name of the institution where you earned your degree.
* **Work License Issuer (24)**: Provide the name of the institution or agency that issued your work license.
* **Submit (25)**: This is the submit button. Please review and verify your information before submitting the form.

## Tips

* Make sure to answer all the required fields.
* Double-check your information for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant authorities for assistance.
