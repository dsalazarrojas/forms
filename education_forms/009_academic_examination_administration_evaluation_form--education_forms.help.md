# Academic Examination Administration Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Examination Administration Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback on the administration and logistics of academic examinations. This form helps assess the effectiveness of the examination process, identify areas for improvement, and provide insights for future exams.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the exam details: Check the "Exam name or code" field to ensure you're evaluating the correct exam.
2.  Evaluate the exam logistics: Rate the "Organization and logistics rating" and "Security and integrity measures rating" fields based on your assessment.
3.  Provide feedback: Share any incidents or concerns in the "Incidents reported during exam" field, and suggest improvements in the "Suggestions for improvement" field.
4.  Add overall comments: Use the "Overall comments" field for any additional feedback or observations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Exam name or code** (`exam_name`, text, required): Enter the name or code of the exam being evaluated.
* **Exam date** (`exam_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the examination.
* **Exam location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the room or venue of the examination.
* **Proctor name** (`proctor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the proctor on duty.
* **Organization and logistics rating** (`organization_quality_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the logistical arrangements of the examination (Excellent, Good, Adequate, Poor).
* **Security and integrity measures rating** (`security_and_integrity_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the exam security and fairness (Excellent, Good, Adequate, Poor).
* **Incidents reported during exam** (`incidents_reported`, text, optional): Describe any breaches or concerns during the examination.
* **Suggestions for improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, text, optional): Provide recommendations for future exams.
* **Overall comments** (`overall_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional feedback or observations about the examination.
