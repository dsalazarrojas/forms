# Educational Leadership Workshop Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a leave of absence for a specific educational leadership workshop. It is typically used to document the workshop details and ensure that all necessary approvals are in place before proceeding with the leave.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the relevant details about the workshop, including the workshop details, team lead, team member, leave start and end dates, duration, type, leave days, reason, team and manager approvals.
* Select the correct status for the leave (Active or Inactive).
* Enter the submitter and team lead information, including their email and phone numbers.
* Optionally, enter the approver and their email and phone numbers.
* Add any additional approval notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form_header**: This is a header section to provide a title for the leave request.
* **workshop_details**: Select yes if the request is for a specific workshop, or no if not.
* **team_lead**: Enter the name of the team lead responsible for the workshop.
* **team_member**: Indicate if team member is part of the workshop. Select yes or no.
* **start_date** and **end_date**: Enter the leave start and end dates.
* **duration**: Enter the length of time for the leave.
* **leave_type**: Indicate the type of leave (e.g. workshop, training, etc.).
* **leave_days**: Enter the number of leave days.
* **reason**: Provide a brief reason for the leave.
* **team_approval**: Select yes if team approval is required, or no if not.
* **manager_approval**: Select yes if manager approval is required, or no if not.
* **submitter**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **submitter_email** and **submitter_phone**: Enter the email and phone number of the person submitting the form.
* **team_lead_email** and **team_lead_phone**: Enter the email and phone number of the team lead.
* **approver**: Enter the name of the person approving the leave.
* **approver_email** and **approver_phone**: Enter the email and phone number of the person approving the leave.
* **approval_note**: Add any additional notes for the approval process.
* **approval_date**: Enter the date of the approval.
* **status**: Select the status of the leave (Active or Inactive).

Note: All fields are required unless otherwise specified as optional.
