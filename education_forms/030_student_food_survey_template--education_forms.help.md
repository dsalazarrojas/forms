# student_food_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The student food survey template is a tool designed to gather information about students' eating habits, food preferences, and eating frequency. This form aims to collect data that can help in understanding student eating behaviors and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your school's name in the text field labeled "School Name".
2. Enter your name in the text field labeled "student_name". This is a required field.
3. Select your grade level from the options provided in the field labeled "grade_level".
4. Select your favorite foods from the list of options in the field labeled "favorite_food".
5. Provide your preferences for your favorite foods in the field labeled "favorite_food_preferences".
6. Indicate how often you eat outside school in the field labeled "eat_outside_school".
7. Choose when you eat during breaks or after school in the field labeled "eat_in_classroom".
8. Select whether you eat with your friends or not in the field labeled "do_you_eat_with_friends".
9. Choose how food satisfies your hunger in the field labeled "food_satisfies".
10. Select the quality of food you prefer in the field labeled "food_quality".
11. Enter when you usually eat in the classroom in the field labeled "when_do_you_eat_in_classroom".
12. Choose when you eat outside school in the field labeled "when_do_you_eat_outside_school".
13. Select whether you eat breakfast or not in the field labeled "eat_breakfast".
14. Select whether you eat lunch or not in the field labeled "eat_lunch".
15. Choose whether you eat snacks or not in the field labeled "eat_snacks".
16. Select whether you eat dinner or not in the field labeled "eat_dinner".
17. Select your favorite drink from the options in the field labeled "favorite_drink".
18. Choose whether your school offers a meal program in the field labeled "school_meal_program".
19. Select how often you eat a full meal in the field labeled "how_often_do_you_eat_a_full_meal".
20. Select how often you eat snacks in the field labeled "how_often_do_you_eat_a_snack".
21. Select your food preferences based on taste in the field labeled "food_preferences_taste".
22. Select your food preferences based on appearance in the field labeled "food_preferences_appearance".
23. Select your eating habits in the field labeled "eating_habits".
24. Select your food preferences based on price in the field labeled "food_preferences_price".
25. Select your favorite ingredients in the field labeled "favorite_ingredient".
