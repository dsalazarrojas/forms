# Graphic Design Exam Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by educators to evaluate student performance in graphic design exams. It is a comprehensive tool to track student progress and provide feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review the exam questions and ensure all necessary answers are provided.
2.  Enter the time allotted for the exam in the `Time Allotted` field.
3.  Enter the exam score in the `Exam Score` field.
4.  Enter the exam duration in the `Exam Duration` field.
5.  Select the student's performance in the `Student Performance` field.
6.  Indicate if any project details were provided in the `Project Details` field.
7.  Provide feedback comments in the `Educator Comments` field.
8.  Enter student feedback in the `Student Feedback` field.
9.  Enter the date and time of the exam in the `Exam Date` and `Exam Time` fields.
10.  Enter the student's name, email, and phone number in the `Student Name`, `Student Email`, and `Student Phone` fields.
11.  Enter the tool used during the exam in the `Assigned Tool` field.
12.  Enter the form ID and version in the `Form Id` and `Form Version` fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Exam Questions** (`exam_questions`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any questions or prompts asked during the exam.
* **Time Allotted** (`time_allotted`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the total time allotted for the exam in minutes.
* **Exam Score** (`exam_score`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the exam score out of the maximum score.
* **Exam Duration** (`exam_duration`, time, optional):
	+ Enter the total duration of the exam.
* **Student Performance** (`student_performance`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select the student's performance during the exam, either 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select any project details provided during the exam.
* **Educator Comments** (`educator_comments`, note, optional):
	+ Enter any comments or feedback from the educator.
* **Student Feedback** (`student_feedback`, note, optional):
	+ Enter any feedback from the student.
* **Exam Date** (`exam_date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date of the exam.
* **Exam Time** (`exam_time`, time, optional):
	+ Enter the time of the exam.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the student's name.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, optional):
	+ Enter the student's email address.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the student's phone number.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the tool used during the exam.
* **Form Id** (`form_id`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the form ID.
* **Form Version** (`form_version`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the form version.

This form should be submitted after all necessary fields have been filled out. Please ensure that all relevant information is provided and attached before submitting the form.
