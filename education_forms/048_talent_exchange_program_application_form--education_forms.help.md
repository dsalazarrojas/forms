# Talent Exchange Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Talent Exchange Program Application Form is designed to collect information from users for the purpose of the Talent Exchange Program. This program aims to facilitate the exchange of talents, skills, or expertise within an organization or community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the User Info section with your personal details.
2. Provide a brief description of your education history.
3. List your relevant professional skills.
4. Specify the rotation details if applicable.
5. If you have any relevant documents to support your application, please upload them in the Application Documents section.
6. Fill in the Exchange Program Info section with any additional information required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info (Text)**: Provide your personal details such as name, email, and contact number.
* **Education History (Text)**: Describe your relevant education background, including degrees, institutions, and dates attended.
* **Professional Skills (Text)**: List your relevant skills, such as programming languages, software proficiency, or soft skills.
* **Rotation Details (Text)**: If you are applying for a rotation, describe the details of the rotation, including dates, location, and roles involved.
* **Exchange Program Info (Text)**: Provide any additional information about the exchange program you are applying for, such as program dates, duration, and expectations.
* **Application Documents (Text)**: Upload any relevant documents, such as resumes, diplomas, or certifications, to support your application.
