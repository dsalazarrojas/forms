# university_housing_and_accommodation_questionnaire_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data about students' housing and accommodation status.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Select the relevant section for each accommodation type.
2. Fill in the required information for each section.
3. Ensure all required fields (marked as `required`) are filled.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Personal Data** (`student_personal_data`, text, required/optional): This section asks for basic student information. Please fill in your details accurately.
* **Student Housing Status** (`student_housing_status`, text, required/optional): Describe your current housing status.
* **Accommodation Type** (`accommodation_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of accommodation you are currently using or planning to use (Apartment, Flat, Other, Single room).
* **Accommodation Address** (`accommodation_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address of your accommodation.
* **Accommodation Postal Code** (`accommodation_postal_code`, number, required/optional): Enter the postal code of your accommodation.
* **Accommodation City** (`accommodation_city`, text, required/optional): Enter the city of your accommodation.
* **Accommodation Country** (`accommodation_country`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the country of your accommodation (England, Wales, Scotland, Ireland, France).
* **Accommodation Phone** (`accommodation_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of your accommodation.
* **Accommodation Email** (`accommodation_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of your accommodation.
* **Accommodation Note** (`accommodation_note`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes about your accommodation.
* **Other Accommodation** (`accommodation_other`, text, required/optional): If you have other accommodation, describe it here.
