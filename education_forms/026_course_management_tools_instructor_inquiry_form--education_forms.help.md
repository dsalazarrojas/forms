# Course Management Tools Instructor Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and inquiry from instructors about the course management tools used in their educational institutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Choose whether you have provided instructor feedback on the course management tools or not.
2. Enter the name of the tool you are inquiring about.
3. Enter the version of the tool you are using.
4. Provide a brief description of the tool and its purpose.
5. If applicable, specify the platform feature you are using.
6. Enter any additional comments about the tool.
7. Enter your name and email address.
8. Specify whether you are a contact person from your institution.
9. Choose whether you have implemented the tool in your institution.
10. Choose your level of satisfaction with the tool.
11. Choose whether you have implemented the tool on your platform.
12. Choose whether you are satisfied with your platform.
13. Enter the date for follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Instructor Feedback** (`instructor_feedback`): Choose whether you have provided feedback on the course management tool.
  - If you choose "Yes", provide more details in the **tool_request** field.
  - If you choose "No", provide more details in the **tool_evaluation** field.
- **Tool Name** (`tool_name`): Enter the name of the course management tool you are inquiring about.
- **Tool Version** (`tool_version`): Enter the version of the tool you are using.
- **Tool Description** (`tool_description`): Provide a brief description of the tool and its purpose.
- **Platform Feature** (`platform_feature`): If applicable, specify the platform feature you are using.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Enter any additional comments about the tool.
- **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): Enter your name.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address.
- **Contact Institution** (`contact_institution`): Choose whether you are a contact person from your institution.
- **Tool Implementation** (`tool_implementation`): Choose whether you have implemented the tool in your institution.
- **Tool Satisfaction** (`tool_satisfaction`): Choose your level of satisfaction with the tool.
- **Platform Satisfaction** (`platform_satisfaction`): Choose whether you are satisfied with your platform.
- **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`): Enter the date for follow-up.
- **Tool Evaluation** (`tool_evaluation`): If you chose "No" in **Instructor Feedback**, enter your evaluation of the tool.
- **Tool Request** (`tool_request`): If you chose "Yes" in **Instructor Feedback**, enter your request for the tool.
- **Other Tools** (`other_tools`): Choose whether you have used other tools.
- **Tool Suggest** (`tool_suggest`): Enter any other tools you would like to suggest.
- **Platform Implementation** (`platform_implementation`): Choose whether you have implemented the tool on your platform.
- **Tool Platform Satisfaction** (`platform_satisfaction`): Choose whether you are satisfied with your platform.
- **Tool Request** (`tool_request`): Enter any other tools you would like to request.
