# Educational Video Licensing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent for the use of educational videos. It is required for any individual or organization seeking to use the educational videos for a specific purpose. The information collected will be used to ensure that the use of the videos complies with copyright and licensing requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Form Title** to help identify the specific purpose of the request.
2. Provide your **Contact Name** and **Contact Email** to allow us to get in touch with you if we need more information.
3. Describe the **Purpose of Use** to help us understand why you want to use the educational video.
4. (Optional) If you are using the video for multiple purposes, list all **Prophets of Use**.
5. Provide a **Consent Text** to confirm that you have read and understood the terms of use.
6. Sign with your signature above the **Signature** field to confirm your consent.
7. Finally, enter the date of submission in the **Date** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Title** (1): The title of this request, which helps us identify the specific purpose of the use.
  - Type: text
  - Required: Yes
  - Hint: This will be used to help us identify the specific purpose of your request.
- **Contact Name** (2): Your name and title, or the name and title of the representative.
  - Type: text
  - Required: Yes
  - Hint: Please provide your name and title or the name and title of the representative.
- **Contact Email** (3): The email address where we can reach you for further information.
  - Type: email
  - Required: Yes
  - Hint: Please ensure it is a valid email address.
- **Purpose of Use** (4): A brief description of why you want to use the educational video.
  - Type: text
  - Required: No
  - Hint: Please provide a brief description of the intended use.
- **Prophets of Use** (5): If you are using the video for multiple purposes, list all the uses.
  - Type: text
  - Required: No
  - Hint: Please provide a comma-separated list of all the uses.
- **Consent Text** (6): A text confirming that you have read and understood the terms of use.
  - Type: text
  - Required: No
  - Hint: Please provide a text confirming that you have read and understood the terms of use.
- **Signature** (7): A handwritten or typed signature to confirm your consent.
  - Type: note
  - Required: No
  - Hint: Please sign above this field with your handwritten or typed signature.
- **Date** (8): The date of submission of this form.
  - Type: date
  - Required: No
  - Hint: The date will be automatically filled in upon submission.

## Tips
- If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
- Please ensure that the email address provided is valid and updatable.
- This form should only be completed once for each unique use case.
