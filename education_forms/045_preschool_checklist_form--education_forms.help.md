# Preschool Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Preschool Checklist Form is designed to collect information about a child's preschool experience. This form helps gather essential details about the child's school year, school supplies, student information, classroom location, classroom equipment, and other relevant aspects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the School Year field with the corresponding year of the child's preschool attendance.
2. Select the correct options for School Supplies, Student Information, Classroom Location, and Classroom Equipment by choosing "Yes" or "No" for multiple-choice fields.
3. Enter the Teacher Name and Parent Name fields with the relevant contact information.
4. Choose the correct options for Activity Planning and Material Request by selecting "Yes" or "No".
5. Add any additional notes or comments in the Other Notes field.
6. Finally, enter the Date and Time Submitted fields with the date and time the form was completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Year** (`school_year`, text, required): Enter the year of the child's preschool attendance.
* **School Supplies** (`school_supplies`, select_multiple, required): Check the boxes for the school supplies your child uses or needs, such as "Yes" for items like crayons, markers, or glue.
* **Student Information** (`student_information`, select_one, required): Select the correct student information category from the options provided.
* **Classroom Location** (`classroom_location`, select_one, required): Choose the correct classroom location from the available options.
* **Classroom Equipment** (`classroom_equipment`, select_multiple, required): Check the boxes for the classroom equipment your child uses or needs, such as "Yes" for items like chairs or desks.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of the teacher responsible for your child's education.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter your name and contact information, as a parent or guardian.
* **Activity Planning** (`activity_planning`, select_multiple, required): Select the activities you would like to schedule for your child, such as music, art, or physical education.
* **Material Request** (`material_request`, select_multiple, required): Check the boxes for the materials you request for your child's activities or lessons.
* **Other Notes** (`other_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your child's preschool experience.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, required): Enter the date the form was completed.
* **Time Submitted** (`time_submitted`, time, required): Enter the time the form was completed.

* If you have any doubts or questions, contact the relevant authorities or administrators for clarification.
* Make sure to review and submit the form before saving it to ensure all required fields are filled in.
