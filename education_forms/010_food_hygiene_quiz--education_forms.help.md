# Food Hygiene Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Hygiene Quiz is designed to assess your knowledge on food safety and hygiene practices. It is intended to help you evaluate your understanding of food handling, storage, and disposal principles. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. The form is divided into sections, each containing questions or fields that you need to complete.
2. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
3. For the `food_hygiene_quiz_name`, enter your name as it will be displayed on your score sheet.
4. For the `food_hygiene_quiz_date` and `food_hygiene_quiz_time` fields, enter the date and time you took the quiz.
5. In the `food_hygiene_quiz_email` field, you can enter your email address if you want to receive the results of your quiz.
6. The `food_hygiene_quiz_phone` field is optional and can be used to record your phone number for follow-up contact.
7. In the `food_hygiene_quiz_comments` field, you can add any additional comments about your experience with the quiz.
8. The `food_hygiene_quiz_score` field is not required but will display your quiz score.
9. The `food_hygiene_quiz_results` field is a dropdown menu with three options: `correct`, `incorrect`, and `pending`. Select the option that best describes your performance.
10. The `food_hygiene_quiz_results_comments` field is a text field for you to add comments about your performance.
11. The `food_hygiene_quiz_results_score` field is used to record your score.
12. The `food_hygiene_quiz_results_score_max` field is not required and should not be completed.
13. The `food_hygiene_quiz_results_score_min` field is not required and should not be completed.
14. The `food_hygiene_quiz_results_status` field is a dropdown menu with three options: `completed`, `pending`, and `cancelled`. Select the option that best describes your status.
15. The `food_hygiene_quiz_results_status_comment` field is a text field for you to add comments about your status.
16. The `food_hygiene_quiz_results_correct` field is a dropdown menu with three options: `correct`, `incorrect`, and `pending`. Select the option that best describes your performance.
17. The `food_hygiene_quiz_results_incorrect` field is a dropdown menu with three options: `correct`, `incorrect`, and `pending`. Select the option that best describes your performance.
18. The `food_hygiene_quiz_results_pending` field is a dropdown menu with three options: `correct`, `incorrect`, and `pending`. Select the option that best describes your performance.
19. The `food_hygiene_quiz_results_correct_comments` field and `food_hygiene_quiz_results_incorrect_comments` field are text fields for you to add comments about your performance.
20. The `food_hygiene_quiz_results_pending_comments` field is a text field for you to add comments about your performance.
