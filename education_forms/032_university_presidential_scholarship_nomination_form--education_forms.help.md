# university_presidential_scholarship_nomination_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to nominate a student for the university's presidential scholarship. The form collects information about the student's academic performance, nomination status, and faculty recommendation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the student's nominee information, including their last name and student ID (if applicable).
2.  Enter the student's GPA and GPA scale.
3.  Select the student's academic program (Undergraduate, Graduate, or Postdoctoral).
4.  Enter the date of the student's current GPA (if applicable).
5.  Enter any additional information about the faculty recommendation, if applicable.
6.  Enter the nominee's contact information, including email and phone number.
7.  Submit the form once all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Nominee information** (`Nominee Information`): Enter the student's name and any other contact information you'd like to include.
- **Nominees last name** (`Nominees Last Name`): Enter the student's last name.
- **GPA** (`GPA`): Enter the student's current GPA.
- **GPA Scale** (`GPA Scale`): Select the student's GPA scale (e.g., 4.0, 3.5, etc.).
- **Academic Program** (`Academic Program`): Select the student's academic program (Undergraduate, Graduate, or Postdoctoral).
- **GPA Year** (`GPA Year`): Enter the year the student's current GPA was achieved (if applicable).
- **Faculty Recommendation** (`Faculty Recommendation`): Enter any additional information about the faculty recommendation.
- **Student Statement** (`Student Statement`): Enter a statement about why the student is a strong candidate for the scholarship.
- **GPA Date** (`GPA Date`): Enter the date the student's current GPA was achieved (if applicable).
- **GPA** (`GPA`): Enter the student’s current GPA.
- **GPA Earned** (`GPA Earned`): Enter the student's current GPA earned.
- **GPA Total** (`GPA Total`): Enter the student’s total GPA.
- **Nominee Email** (`Nominee Email`): Enter the student's email address.
- **Nominee Phone** (`Nominee Phone`): Enter the student’s phone number.
- **Faculty Recommendation Name** (`Faculty Recommendation Name`): Enter the faculty member's name making the recommendation.
- **Faculty Recommendation Relationship** (`Faculty Recommendation Relationship`): Enter a brief description of the faculty member's relationship to the student.
- **Faculty Recommendation Email** (`Faculty Recommendation Email`): Enter the faculty member's email address.
- **Faculty Recommendation Phone** (`Faculty Recommendation Phone`): Enter the faculty member's phone number.
- **Faculty Recommendation Letter** (`Faculty Recommendation Letter`): Enter any additional information about the faculty recommendation letter.
- **Faculty Recommendation Letter Date** (`Faculty Recommendation Letter Date`): Enter the date the faculty recommendation letter was written.
- **Faculty Recommendation Letter Name** (`Faculty Recommendation Letter Name`): Enter the name of the person who wrote the faculty recommendation letter.
- **Faculty Recommendation Letter Title** (`Faculty Recommendation Letter Title`): Enter the title of the faculty member who wrote the recommendation letter.
- **Faculty Recommendation Letter From** (`Faculty Recommendation Letter From`): Enter the name of the institution the faculty member is from.
- **Nominee Supervisor** (`Nominee Supervisor`): Enter the student’s supervisor’s name (if applicable).

Please note that some fields may not be required, depending on the student's situation. Make sure to include all required fields and provide accurate information to ensure the nomination is properly reviewed.
