# Vertebral Column And Joint Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess your knowledge about the vertebral column and its joints. This quiz is designed to test your understanding of the different parts of the vertebral column, including its anatomy and functionality. The form consists of multiple-choice questions that cover various aspects of the vertebral column, such as its segments, joints, and disks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and choose the correct answer from the options provided.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select all the correct answers that apply.
3. Fill in the required information in the text fields, such as your user information and email address.
4. Once you have completed all the questions, click the "Submit" button to submit your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, required/optional): This is a note field where you can read a brief introduction to the quiz. You can read it once and then proceed to the questions.
* **Vertebral Column** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for providing a brief description of the vertebral column. Please provide a short answer in this field.
* **Anatomy** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose all the correct options for the anatomy of the vertebral column:
	+ Thoracic
	+ Lumbar
	+ Cervical
	+ Sacral
	+ Sacroiliac
	+ Sternum
* **Joints** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required/true): Choose all the correct options for the joints of the vertebral column:
	+ Ball and Socket Joint
	+ Plane Synovial Joint
	+ Gliding Joint
	+ Pivot Joint
	+ Saddle Joint
	+ Ball-and-socket Joint
* **Cervical Joints** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required/false): Choose all the correct options for the cervical joints:
	+ Atlanto-Axial Joint
	+ Deltoid Joint
	+ C1-C2 Joint
	+ C1-C7 Joint
	+ C2-C6 Joint
	+ C6-T1 Joint
* **Thoracic Joints** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required/false): Choose all the correct options for the thoracic joints:
	+ D2-D4 Joint
	+ D4-D12 Joint
	+ D12-T1 Joint
	+ D12-T12 Joint
	+ T1-T12 Joint
* **Lumbar Joints** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required/false): Choose all the correct options for the lumbar joints:
	+ L1-L5 Joint
	+ L1-S1 Joint
	+ L5-S1 Joint
	+ L5-S2 Joint
	+ L5-S4 Joint
* **Sacral Joints** (`8`, `select_multiple`, required/false): Choose all the correct options for the sacral joints:
	+ S1-S2 Joint
	+ S2-S3 Joint
	+ S2-S5 Joint
* **Vertebral Disks** (`9`, `select_multiple`, required/true): Choose all the correct options for the vertebral disks:
	+ Intervertebral Disk
	+ Intersegmental Disk
	+ Sacrum Disk
	+ Interlumbar Disk
* **Vertebrae** (`10`, `number`, required/false): Enter the number of vertebrae.
* **Joints Position** (`11`, `select_one`, required/false): Choose the correct position of the joints:
	+ Left
	+ Right
	+ Both
* **Vertebrae Segments** (`12`, `number`, required/false): Enter the number of vertebrae segments.
* **Joint Stability** (`13`, `text`, required/false): This field is for providing information about the joint stability.
* **Anatomy Information** (`14`, `text`, required/false): This field is for providing additional information about the anatomy.
* **Quiz Score** (`15`, `number`, required/false): This field is for displaying your quiz score.
* **Quiz Time** (`16`, `time`, required/false): This field is for displaying the time taken to complete the quiz.
* **User Information** (`17`, `text`, required/false): This field is for providing your user information.
* **Email Address** (`18`, `email`, required/false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`19`, `text`, required/false): Enter your phone number.
* **Submit** (`20`, `text`, required/false): Click this button to submit your answers.

Note: The fields marked as required/true are mandatory fields that must be filled in order to complete the form. The fields marked as required/false are optional fields that can be filled or left blank.
