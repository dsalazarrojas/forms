# Video Editor Job Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Video Editor Job Application Form is designed to collect information from applicants about their personal details, education background, experience, and video editing skills. The goal is to help the hiring team assess the qualifications and suitability of each applicant for the Video Editor position.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form with accurate and honest information about yourself.
2.  Please make sure to complete all required fields.
3.  If a field is not required, feel free to leave it blank, but make sure to be honest about your qualifications and experience.
4.  Review and proofread your form before submission to avoid any errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Personal Details** (`personal_details`): This field is for you to provide a brief introduction about yourself.
    *   Please include your name, address, and any other relevant personal information.
    *   Keep it concise and clear for the hiring team to understand your background.
*   **Contact Info** (`contact_info`): This field is for you to provide your contact information, including your email and phone number.
    *   Make sure to use a valid email address and phone number that we can use to get in touch with you.
    *   If you're a remote worker, please provide a valid address or a reliable way for us to contact you.
*   **Education** (`education`): This field is for you to provide information about your educational background, including your highest level of education completed.
    *   Please be honest about your educational qualifications and any relevant courses or training you've completed.
    *   If you're a recent graduate, please provide information about your academic achievements.
*   **Experience** (`experience`): This field is for you to provide information about your work experience, including your job title, company, and job duration.
    *   Be honest about your job responsibilities and achievements.
    *   If you have any relevant experience in video editing, please provide details about your experience.
*   **Video Editor Skills** (`video_editor_skills`): This field is for you to select your relevant video editing skills.
    *   Please choose all the skills that apply to you from the list provided.
    *   If you have any other relevant video editing skills, feel free to add them in the provided text field.
*   **Portfolio** (`portfolio`): This field is for you to provide a link to your portfolio or a brief description of your work.
    *   Please provide a link to your online portfolio or a brief description of your relevant work.
    *   If you're a student, you can provide any relevant projects you've worked on.

## Tips

*   Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
*   Make sure to review and proofread your form before submission.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
