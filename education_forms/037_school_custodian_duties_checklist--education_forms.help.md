# School Custodian Duties Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help school custodians report their duties and responsibilities to ensure the effective management of school maintenance and upkeep.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Custodian Name" field.
2. Type the location of your school in the "School Location" field.
3. Select all staff members you are supervising from the "Supervising Staff" field.
4. Choose the levels of supervision you have for each area of responsibility from the "Custodian Supervision" field.
5. Select all equipment you have access to from the "Custodian Equipment" field.
6. Choose how frequently you are supervising each area from the "Custodian Supervising Frequency" field.
7. Enter any safety concerns or issues in the "Custodian Safety" field.
8. Enter any security concerns or issues in the "Custodian Security" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Custodian Name** (`custodian_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **School Location** (`school_location`, text, required): Type the location of your school.
* **Supervising Staff** (`supervising_staff`, text, required): Select all staff members you are responsible for supervising.
* **Custodian Supervision** (`custodian_supervision`, select_multiple, required): Choose the levels of supervision you have for each area of responsibility.
* **Custodian Supervision Level** (`custodian_supervision_level`, select_one, required): Choose how frequently you are supervising each area.
* **Custodian Equipment** (`custodian_equipment`, select_multiple, required): Select all equipment you have access to.
* **Custodian Supervising** (`custodian_supervising`, select_multiple, required): Choose how you are supervising each area.
* **Custodian Supervising Frequency** (`custodian_supervising_frequency`, select_multiple, required): Choose how frequently you are supervising each area.
* **Custodian Safety** (`custodian_safety`, text, required): Enter any safety concerns or issues.
* **Custodian Security** (`custodian_security`, text, required): Enter any security concerns or issues.

Note: Please ensure to provide accurate information to ensure the effective management of your school's custodial services. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact me at form@gic.mx.
