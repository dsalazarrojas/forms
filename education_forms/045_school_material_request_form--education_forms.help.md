# School Material Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about school materials requested by teachers for their classes. This form helps school administrators keep track of material requests, ensuring they are reviewed and approved in a timely and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by selecting the type of school materials needed. Choose one or multiple options from the list, such as "School supplies", "Educational resources", or "Technology".
2.  Enter your name as the "Teacher Name" so that the school material request can be associated with you.
3.  Enter your school email address as "Teacher Email" so that we can get in touch with you about the request.
4.  Enter the name of your school as "School Name" to ensure your request is associated with the right institution.
5.  If you need to, enter the email address of your school by typing it in as "School Email".
6.  Enter the name of your class and the date you made the request as "Class Name" and "Request Date" respectively.
7.  If applicable, select "Requested By" to indicate whether the request was made by you, school admin, or other, so we know who is responsible for the request.
8.  Indicate the type of school materials you are requesting, such as books, materials, resources, or technology.
9.  Enter the quantity of materials you need as "Quantity".
10. Add any additional comments about the request in the "Additional Comments" field.
11. Enter any additional details about the request, including the type and any other relevant information, as "Supply Request Details".
12. Finally, select how you submitted this form, whether it was via email, JotForm, or other, and indicate when it was submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School materials** (`school_material_request_form_items`): This field allows you to select the type of school materials you are requesting. Choose one or multiple options from the list, such as "School supplies", "Educational resources", or "Technology".
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`): Enter your name as the teacher requesting the school materials.
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`): Enter your school email address so that we can get in touch with you about the request.
* **School Name** (`school_name`): Enter the name of your school to ensure the request is associated with the right institution.
* **School Email** (`school_email`): If you have a school email address, enter it here.
* **Class Name** (`class_name`): Enter the name of your class.
* **Request Date** (`supply_request_date`): Enter the date you made the request.
* **Requested By** (`supply_requested_by`): If applicable, select whether the request was made by you, school admin, or other.
* **Supply Type** (`school_supply_type`): Indicate the type of school materials you are requesting, such as books, materials, resources, or technology.
* **Quantity** (`school_material_quantity`): Enter the quantity of materials you need.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Add any additional comments or information about your request.
* **Supply Request Details** (`school_supply_request_details`): Enter any additional details about the request.
* **Submitted By** (`school_material_request_form_submitted_by`): Select who submitted the form, you, school admin, or other.
* **Submitted Via** (`school_material_request_form_submitted_via`): Indicate how you submitted this form, whether via email, JotForm, or other.
* **Submitted Date** (`school_material_request_form_submitted_date`): This field will be automatically populated with the date the form was submitted.
