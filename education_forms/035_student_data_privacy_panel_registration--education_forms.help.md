# Student Data Privacy Panel Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Data Privacy Panel Registration form is used to collect sensitive data from students, such as contact information and interests, and to obtain their consent for data processing. This data will be used to provide personalized services and support to the students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the registration form with your school district information (page 1).
2. Select your interests by choosing from the options provided (page 1).
3. Provide your consent for data processing (page 1).
4. Enter your contact information (page 2).
5. Provide any other relevant student data (page 2).
6. Choose your registration status (page 2).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form** (`page 1`, `text`, required: false): This is the main registration form, where you will provide your school district information.
  * Please enter your school district information to register with the student data privacy panel.
* **School District Info** (`page 1`, `text`, required: false): Enter your school district name, address, or any other relevant information.
* **Select Interests** (`page 1`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the interests that apply to you by selecting the corresponding options.
* **Yes** (`page 1`, `select_multiple`, option): Choose "Yes" if you are interested in this area.
* **No** (`page 1`, `select_multiple`, option): Choose "No" if you are not interested in this area.
* **Privacy Consent** (`page 1`, `text`, required: false): Provide your consent for data processing by typing "Yes" in this field.
* **Contact Info** (`page 2`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact information, such as phone number or email address, to be reached for any updates or support.
* **Student Data** (`page 2`, `text`, required: false): Provide any other relevant student data that you feel is essential for the support services.
* **Registration Status** (`page 2`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose your current registration status by selecting from the options: Active, Inactive, or Pending.
* **Active** (`page 2`, `select_one`, option): Choose this option if you are currently registered and active.
* **Inactive** (`page 2`, `select_one`, option): Choose this option if you are currently not registered or inactive.
* **Pending** (`page 2`, `select_one`, option): Choose this option if your registration is pending.
