# parent-teacher-engagement-monitoring-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to monitor parent-teacher engagement, collecting information from parents about their involvement and communication with teachers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your parent information in the first field.
2. Select the frequency of communication with teachers using the drop-down menu.
3. Indicate your level of involvement with teachers using the drop-down menu.
4. Choose the level of engagement you have with teachers using the drop-down menu.
5. Add any additional comments or notes about your interactions with teachers.
6. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
7. Add your phone number for contact purposes.
8. Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with teachers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Information**: Please enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Communication**: This field is optional, but if you select "very-often", "often", "rarely", or "never", you will be indicating how frequently you communicate with teachers.
* **Involvement**: Please select all options that apply to describe your level of involvement with teachers.
* **Engagement**: This field is required, and selecting "very-often", "often", "rarely", or "never" will indicate how frequently you engage with teachers.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or feedback about your experience with teachers. This field is optional.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Comments**: Please provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with teachers.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate information to help teachers understand your level of engagement.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please let us know in the notes field.
* This form is only to be used for monitoring purposes, and the data collected will not be used for reporting or evaluation.
