# Special Needs Education Training Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Special Needs Education Training Admission Form is designed to collect information from applicants who require special education training. This form will help assess the applicant's suitability for the training program and ensure that the necessary details are provided to make informed decisions about admission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the required fields.
2. Select the relevant programs and categories that apply to your situation.
3. Provide any additional notes or comments that will help us better understand your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address that we will use for correspondence.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, required: false): Enter your birth date (optional).
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter your current address.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, select_multiple, required: true): Select the programs that you are interested in applying for.
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, time, required: false): Enter the date you submitted your application (optional).
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes that you would like to share with us.
* **Additional Notes** (`note_2`, note, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes that you would like to share with us.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required: true): Select the category that best describes your situation.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required: false): Enter the department or team that will be responsible for your training.
* **Staff Name** (`staff_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the staff member who recommended you for the training.
* **Institution** (`institution`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the institutions that you are associated with.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required: false): Enter the contact person's name and details.
* **Position** (`position`, select_one, required: true): Select the position that you hold or are interested in.
* **Department Phone** (`department_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the department's phone number.
* **Department Email** (`department_email`, email, required: false): Enter the department's email address.
* **Department Address** (`department_address`, text, required: false): Enter the department's address.
* **Department Staff** (`department_staff_name`, text, required: false): Enter the department staff member's name.
* **Student Count** (`student_count`, text, required: false): Enter the number of students you are responsible for.
* **Student Level** (`student_level`, text, required: false): Enter the level of students you are responsible for (e.g., primary, secondary, tertiary).
* **Student Age** (`student_age`, text, required: false): Enter the age range of the students you are responsible for.
* **Student Gender** (`student_gender`, text, required: false): Enter the gender of the students you are responsible for.
* **Special Needs** (`student_special_needs`, text, required: false): Enter any special needs that the students you are responsible for may have.
