# Clone Of ONES GATE Coaching Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from applicants interested in the ONES GATE Coaching program. It will be used by the organization to assess their suitability for the coaching program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each field's purpose and requirements.
2. Fill in all the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Be specific and concise in your answers to provide a clear picture of your background and qualifications.
4. Proofread your application form before submitting it to avoid any errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant name**: A field to enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: true
* **Email address**: A field to enter your preferred contact email address.
	+ Type: email
	+ Required: true
* **Phone number**: A field to enter your mobile phone number with the country code.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
* **Coaching experience summary**: A field to provide a brief background and years of coaching experience.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: true
* **Availability**: A field to specify the days and times you are available for coaching.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: true
* **Qualifications or certifications**: A field to list any relevant credentials or certifications you hold.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
* **Preferred coaching area**: A field to select one area of coaching that interests you (Academic, Career, Life, Performance).
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: true
* **References**: A field to provide names and contact details of your professional or academic references.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
* **Additional information**: A field to provide any other relevant information to support your application.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
