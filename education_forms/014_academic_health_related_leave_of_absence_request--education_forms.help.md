# Academic Health Related Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information from students who need to take a leave of absence due to health reasons. It will be reviewed by the relevant university department or advisor to ensure a smooth and efficient process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have consulted with your university department or advisor before submitting this form.
2. Fill in all required fields (marked as required).
3. Be concise and clear in your answers to help the review process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student name** (`Student name`, `text`, required): Enter your full legal name.
* **Student identification number** (`Student identification number`, `text`, required): Enter your university ID or registration number.
* **Contact email** (`Contact email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for leave communication.
* **Program or major** (`Program or major`, `text`, optional): If applicable, select your enrollment program or major.
* **Leave start date** (`Leave start date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when you plan to start your leave.
* **Leave end date** (`Leave end date`, `date`, optional): Enter the expected return date if known.
* **Reason for leave** (`Reason for leave`, `text`, required): Describe your health-related reason for taking a leave of absence.
* **Supporting documents provided** (`Supporting documents provided`, `text`, optional): Indicate if you have attached any supporting medical certificates or documentation.
* **Contact method during leave** (`Contact method during leave`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your preferred method for urgent contact during leave (Email, Phone, or No contact).
* **Applicant signature name** (`Applicant signature name`, `text`, required): Type your full name to confirm your request.
* **Submission date** (`Submission date`, `date`, required): The date when you submit this form.

## Tips

* Ensure you have attached any necessary supporting documents with your submission.
* If you are unsure about any part of this form, please contact your university department or advisor for guidance.
* This form will be reviewed and processed in a timely manner to facilitate your leave of absence.
