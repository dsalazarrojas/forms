This form is designed to request an academic research visualization from a visualization expert, typically a data scientist or visual designer with expertise in creating data-driven visualizations. The purpose of this form is to gather information from the requester to understand their needs and requirements for the visualization. This form is not affiliated with or derived from any specific form platform, but rather an original adaptation created using public survey topic metadata.

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to request an academic research visualization that meets your specific needs and requirements. By filling out this form, you will be able to provide detailed information about your research project, visualization goals, and desired outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name, email address, and institutional affiliation.
2. Provide a brief description of your research project and its title.
3. Specify the purpose and type of visualization you are requesting.
4. Describe the data you wish to visualize.
5. Identify your target audience and the desired complexity level of the visualization.
6. Provide a deadline for completion of the visualization.
7. If required, select the file formats you need for the visualization.
8. Specify any color scheme preferences or branding requirements.
9. If applicable, upload any data files to be visualized.
10. Add any additional requirements or notes for the visualization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Requester Name** (`requester_name`, text, required)
Enter your full name as the person requesting the visualization.

### 2. **Email Address** (`requester_email`, email, required)
Provide your professional email address for communication about this request.

### 3. **Institution** (`requester_institution`, text, required)
List your university or organization affiliation.

### 4. **Department** (`requester_department`, text, required)
Specify your academic department or research unit.

### 5. **Research Project Title** (`research_project_title`, text, required)
Enter the title of the research project requiring visualization.

### 6. **Purpose of Visualization** (`visualization_purpose`, select_one, required)
Select the primary purpose of the visualization.

*   Academic publication
*   Conference presentation
*   Thesis/dissertation
*   Grant proposal
*   Classroom instruction
*   Public outreach
*   Other

### 7. **Type of Visualization Requested** (`visualization_type_requested`, select_one, required)
Select the type of visualization desired.

*   Bar chart
*   Line graph
*   Scatter plot
*   Heat map
*   Network diagram
*   Geospatial map
*   Infographic
*   3D visualization
*   Other

### 8. **Data Description** (`data_description`, text, required)
Provide a brief description of the data to be visualized.

### 9. **Target Audience** (`audience_type`, select_one, required)
Select the intended audience for the visualization.

*   Academic peers
*   General public
*   Students
*   Policy makers
*   Funding agencies
*   Industry professionals
*   Other

### 10. **Complexity Level** (`complexity_level`, select_one, required)
Choose the level of complexity for the visualization.

*   Simple and clear
*   Moderately complex
*   Highly detailed

*   Customizable

### 11. **Timeline Deadline** (`timeline_deadline`, date, required)
Enter the date by which you need the visualization completed.

### 12. **File Formats Needed** (`file_formats_needed`, select_multiple, optional)
Select the file formats required for the visualization.

*   PDF
*   PNG

*   Interactive HTML
*   Other

### 13. **Color Scheme Preference** (`color_scheme_preference`, text, optional)
Enter any color scheme preferences or requirements.

### 14. **Branding Requirements** (`branding_requirements`, select_one, required)
Select how you handle institutional branding requirements.

*   Yes, strict requirements

### 15. **Data File Upload** (`data_file_upload`, text, optional)
Upload data file(s) to be visualized (CSV, Excel, etc.).

### 16. **Additional Requirements** (`additional_requirements`, text, optional)
List any other specific requirements or notes for the visualization.
