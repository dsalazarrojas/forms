# Sample Podcast Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sample Podcast Release Form is designed for podcast creators and hosts to collect and manage metadata for each episode release. This form helps to gather essential details about the guest, release schedule, location, and audio recording for each episode.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the guest's name in the "Guest Name" field.
2. Select the release date for the episode in the "Release Date" field.
3. Enter the release location in the "Release Location" field.
4. Enter the recording location where the episode was recorded in the "Recording Location" field.
5. Enter the release time for the episode in the "Release Time" field.
6. (Optional) Provide the title of the audio recording in the "Audio Recording" field.
7. (Optional) Enter the details of the released audio recording in the "Release Audio Recording" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Name**: Enter the name of the guest who appeared in the episode. This will help in identifying the speaker and provide credit to the guest.
* **Release Date**: Select the date when the episode will be released. The format of this field is MM/DD/YY. For example, 01/22/2020.
* **Release Location**: Enter the location where the episode will be released. This could be the podcast's website, social media channels, or distribution platforms.
* **Recording Location**: Enter the location where the episode was recorded. For example, a studio, home, or office.
* **Release Time**: Select the time when the episode will be released. The format of this field is HH:MM. For example, 08:00.
* **Audio Recording**: (Optional) Enter a title for the audio recording.
* **Release Audio Recording**: (Optional) Describe the details of the released audio recording.

Note that some fields are optional and can be filled out only if necessary.
