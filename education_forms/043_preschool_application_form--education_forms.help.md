# Preschool Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Preschool Application Form is used to collect information from parents or guardians about their child's preschool enrollment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form in its entirety. All required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter your name and your child's name in the designated fields.
3. Provide your address and email address (if applicable) to ensure we can contact you regarding your child's preschool application.
4. Enter your child's birth date and other relevant information about your child.
5. Specify the preschool that you are applying to and the dates of the child's intended enrollment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name**: *Please enter your full name.*
* **Child Name**: *Please enter your child's full name.*
* **Birth Date**: *Enter your child's birth date.*
* **Address**: *Enter your home address for your child.*
* **Email**: *Enter your email address if you have one (optional but recommended for future contact).*
* **Phone**: *Enter a phone number where we can contact you (optional but recommended).*
* **Parent Contact Info**: *Please enter your contact information for follow-up questions or issues.*
* **Child Info**: *Please provide any relevant medical or special needs information about your child.*
* **Preschool Name**: *Please select the preschool that you are applying to.*
* **Preschool Address**: *Enter the address of the preschool that you are applying to.*
* **Preschool Start Date**: *Enter the start date of your child's enrollment.*
* **Preschool End Date**: *Enter the end date of your child's enrollment.*
* **Reason for Leaving**: *Optional field for stating the reason for leaving, if applicable.*
* **Additional Comments**: *Enter any additional comments or information about your child's preschool application.*
