# Proficiency Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Proficiency Evaluation Form is designed to collect information about a student's proficiency level and attendance at workshops. The form is used to assess the student's progress and performance in their field of study. It's essential to fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the student's skills and knowledge are evaluated correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Proficiency Evaluation Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter the student's email address in the "Student Email" field.
3. Select the student's proficiency level from the drop-down menu in the "Proficiency Level" field.
4. Fill in the student's address in the "Student Address" field.
5. Enter the student's phone number in the "Student Phone" field.
6. Fill in the course the student is studying in the "Student Course" field.
7. Enter the student's school name in the "Student School" field.
8. Fill in the student's date of birth in the "Student Date of Birth" field.
9. Enter the student's time of birth in the "Student Time of Birth" field.
10. Note any additional comments or remarks about the student in the "Student Note" field.
11. Fill in the student's proficiency in the "Student Proficiency" field.
12. Fill in the student's educational level in the "Student Proficiency Education Level" field.
13. Fill in the student's workshop level in the "Student Proficiency Workshop Level" field.
14. Enter the name of the workshop the student attended in the "Student Proficiency Workshop Name" field.
15. Fill in the date of the workshop the student attended in the "Student Proficiency Workshop Date" field.
16. Enter the time of the workshop the student attended in the "Student Proficiency Workshop Time" field.
17. Note any additional comments or remarks about the workshop in the "Student Proficiency Workshop Note" field.
18. Fill in the location of the workshop the student attended in the "Student Proficiency Workshop Location" field.
19. Select the attendance frequency of the workshop the student attended from the drop-down menu in the "Student Proficiency Workshop Attendance" field.
20. Fill in the attendance duration of the workshop the student attended in the "Student Proficiency Workshop Attendance Duration" field.
21. Enter the start date of the workshop attendance in the "Student Proficiency Workshop Attendance Start Date" field.
22. Enter the end date of the workshop attendance in the "Student Proficiency Workshop Attendance End Date" field.
23. Fill in the attendance frequency of the workshop the student attended in the "Student Proficiency Workshop Attendance Frequency" field.
24. Enter the attendance time of the workshop the student attended in the "Student Proficiency Workshop Attendance Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name (Student Name)** (`Student Name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the student, as mentioned in their records.
* **Student Email (Student Email)** (`Student Email`, Email, Optional): Enter the student's email address, which is used for communication purposes.
* **Proficiency Level (Proficiency Level)** (Proficiency Level, Select One, Required): This field allows you to select the student's proficiency level, which is crucial for evaluation purposes.
* **Student Address (Student Address)** (`Student Address`, Text, Optional): Fill in the student's physical address, which is used for communication and record-keeping purposes.
* **Student Phone (Student Phone)** (`Student Phone`, Text, Optional): Enter the student's phone number, which is used for contact purposes.
* **Student Course (Student Course)** (`Student Course`, Text, Optional): Enter the course the student is studying, which helps in understanding their progress.
* **Student School (Student School)** (`Student School`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the student's school, which helps in verifying the student's attendance.
* **Student Date of Birth (Student Date of Birth)** (`Student Date of Birth`, Date, Optional): Fill in the student's date of birth, which is used for record-keeping and verification purposes.
* **Student Time of Birth (Student Time of Birth)** (`Student Time of Birth`, Time, Optional): Enter the student's time of birth, which is used for record-keeping and verification purposes.
* **Student Note (Student Note)** (`Student Note`, Note, Optional): Note any additional comments or remarks about the student, which helps in understanding their progress.
* **Student Proficiency (Student Proficiency)** (`Student Proficiency`, Text, Optional): Fill in the student's proficiency, which is a measure of their skill level.
* **Student Proficiency Education Level (Student Proficiency Education Level)** (`Student Proficiency Education Level`, Text, Optional): Enter the student's educational level, which helps in understanding their background.
* **Student Proficiency Workshop Level (Student Proficiency Workshop Level)** (`Student Proficiency Workshop Level`, Text, Optional): Fill in the student's workshop level, which helps in understanding their progress.
* **Student Proficiency Workshop Name (Student Proficiency Workshop Name)** (`Student Proficiency Workshop Name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the workshop the student attended.
* **Student Proficiency Workshop Date (Student Proficiency Workshop Date)** (`Student Proficiency Workshop Date`, Date, Optional): Fill in the date of the workshop the student attended.
* **Student Proficiency Workshop Time (Student Proficiency Workshop Time)** (`Student Proficiency Workshop Time`, Time, Optional): Enter the time of the workshop the student attended.
* **Student Proficiency Workshop Note (Student Proficiency Workshop Note)** (`Student Proficiency Workshop Note`, Note, Optional): Note any additional comments or remarks about the workshop.
* **Student Proficiency Workshop Location (Student Proficiency Workshop Location)** (`Student Proficiency Workshop Location`, Text, Optional): Fill in the location of the workshop the student attended.
* **Student Proficiency Workshop Attendance (Student Proficiency Workshop Attendance)** (Proficiency Workshop Attendance, Select One, Required): Select the attendance frequency of the workshop the student attended.
* **Student Proficiency Workshop Attendance Frequency (Student Proficiency Workshop Attendance Frequency)** (`Student Proficiency Workshop Attendance Frequency`, Text, Optional): Fill in the attendance frequency of the workshop the student attended.
* **Student Proficiency Workshop Attendance Duration (Student Proficiency Workshop Attendance Duration)** (`Student Proficiency Workshop Attendance Duration`, Text, Optional): Enter the attendance duration of the workshop the student attended.
* **Student Proficiency Workshop Attendance Start Date (Student Proficiency Workshop Attendance Start Date)** (`Student Proficiency Workshop Attendance Start Date`, Date, Optional): Enter the start date of the workshop attendance.
* **Student Proficiency Workshop Attendance End Date (Student Proficiency Workshop Attendance End Date)** (`Student Proficiency Workshop Attendance End Date`, Date, Optional): Enter the end date of the workshop attendance.
* **Student Proficiency Workshop Attendance Time (Student Proficiency Workshop Attendance Time)** (`Student Proficiency Workshop Attendance Time`, Time, Optional): Enter the time of the workshop attendance.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the student's skills and knowledge are evaluated correctly.
* Make sure to select the correct attendance frequency of the workshop the student attended.
* If you're unsure about any field, please consult with your supervisor or colleague for clarification.
* Please note that some fields are optional, but it's recommended to fill them out for a more comprehensive evaluation.
* If you need help with any field, please contact the system administrator for assistance.
