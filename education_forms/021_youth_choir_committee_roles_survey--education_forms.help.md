# Youth Choir Committee Roles Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help youth choir committee members understand their own roles and responsibilities within the committee.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select all the roles that apply to you.
2. Provide your name and contact information if you are a committee member.
3. Answer the questions about your experience, role preferences, and other relevant aspects of being a committee member.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Name** (required): Enter your name as a committee member.
* **Yes/No: Role Preferences** (select multiple): Select all roles that you are interested in taking on within the committee.
* **Contact Details** (text): Provide any additional information about your contact details, such as your phone number or email.
* **Experience** (text): Share your relevant experience in music or leadership roles.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
* **Yes/No: Role Committee** (select multiple): Select all roles that you are interested in having on the committee.
* **Committee Size** (number): Enter the number of people you think the committee should have.
* **Yes/No: Role Experience** (select multiple): Select all roles that you have experience in.
* **Yes/No: Committee Leadership** (select multiple): Select all roles that you are interested in taking on leadership roles.
* **Yes/No: Music Formation** (select multiple): Select all roles that involve music formation.
* **Yes/No: Music Direction** (select multiple): Select all roles that involve music direction.
* **Yes/No: Communication** (select multiple): Select all roles that involve communication.
* **Yes/No: Conflict Resolution** (select multiple): Select all roles that involve conflict resolution.

## Tips
* Make sure to be honest about your role preferences and limitations.
* Be specific about your experience and qualifications.
* Select all roles that you are genuinely interested in taking on within the committee.
