# 2016 Recommendation Transcript Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The 2016 Recommendation Transcript Request Form is designed to collect information from applicants for official transcript requests. It is used to gather essential details about the applicant, their academic background, and preferences for transcript delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name as it appears on your academic records.
2. Enter your student ID or campus number, if applicable.
3. Provide your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Enter your email address, where you will receive confirmation and delivery updates.
5. Provide your phone number, including your country code (e.g., +1-555-555-5555).
6. Enter your current mailing address (street, city, state/province, postal code).
7. Select the degree or program you are requesting the transcript for.
8. Enter the year you graduated (YYYY format, e.g., 2016).
9. Enter the name of the recipient institution or individual receiving the transcript.
10. Provide the full address or secure email of the recipient.
11. Specify the number of official copies you require.
12. Choose your preferred delivery method (secure electronic, standard mail, expedited mail, or hold for pickup).
13. Select your preferred delivery date or leave blank for standard processing time.
14. Enter the payment confirmation or transaction ID.
15. Confirm your authorization to release your transcript and accuracy of the provided information.
16. Rate how likely you are to recommend this transcript request service (0-10).
17. Explain the primary reason for your rating.
18. (Optional) Select up to six important features that are crucial to you.
19. Enter any additional comments or special instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your academic records.
* **Student ID or campus number** (`student_id`, `text`, optional): Enter your student ID or campus number, including leading zeros if applicable.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Provide your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter the email address where you will receive confirmation and delivery updates.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, `phone`, optional): Enter your phone number, including your country code (e.g., +1-555-555-5555).
* **Current mailing address** (`current_address`, `text`, optional): Enter your current mailing address (street, city, state/province, postal code).
* **Degree or program** (`degree_program`, `select_one`, required): Select the degree or program you are requesting the transcript for.
* **Year of graduation** (`graduation_year`, `integer`, required): Enter the year you graduated (YYYY format, e.g., 2016).
* **Recipient institution or individual name** (`recipient_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the recipient institution or individual receiving the transcript.
* **Recipient address or delivery email** (`recipient_address`, `text`, required): Provide the full address or secure email of the recipient.
* **Number of official copies** (`number_of_copies`, `integer`, required): Specify the number of official copies you require.
* **Preferred delivery method** (`delivery_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred delivery method (secure electronic, standard mail, expedited mail, or hold for pickup).
* **Preferred delivery date** (`preferred_delivery_date`, `date`, optional): Select your preferred delivery date or leave blank for standard processing time.
* **Payment confirmation or transaction ID** (`payment_reference`, `text`, required): Enter the payment confirmation or transaction ID.
* **Authorization to release transcripts** (`authorization`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your authorization to release your transcript and accuracy of the provided information.
* **How likely to recommend** (`nps_score`, `integer`, required): Rate how likely you are to recommend this transcript request service (0-10).
* **Primary reason for rating** (`primary_reason`, `text`, required): Explain the primary reason for your rating.
* **Most important features** (`important_features`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select up to six features that are crucial to you.
* **Additional comments or special instructions** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or special instructions.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* If you have any special instructions or requirements, please enter them in the "Additional comments or special instructions" field.
