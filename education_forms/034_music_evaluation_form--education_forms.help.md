# Music Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Music Evaluation Form is used to gather information about a piece of music, such as its style, category, and ratings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Choose the correct music style from the options provided in the "Music Style" field.
2. Select all applicable music categories from the options provided in the "Music Category" field.
3. Rate the music by entering a number in the "Rating" field. A rating of 1-10 is required.
4. Enter any additional comments about the music in the "Comments" field.
5. If the music has a composer, select "Yes" in the "Composer" field.
6. If the music is from a specific music period, select "Yes" in the "Music Period" field.
7. If the music has a specific genre, select "Yes" in the "Music Genre" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Music Style** (`music_style`, select_one, required):
    The music style refers to the genre or classification of music, such as Classical, Pop, Rock, etc.
* **Music Category** (`music_category`, select_multiple, required):
    Select all applicable categories that apply to the music, such as Classical, Pop, Rock, Jazz, etc.
* **Musician** (`musician`, select_one, optional):
    This field is optional, but if selected, it indicates that the music is associated with a specific musician.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required):
    Rate the music on a scale of 1-10.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required):
    Provide any additional comments or notes about the music.
* **Composer** (`composer`, select_one, optional):
    This field is optional, but if selected, it indicates that the music has a composer.
* **Music Period** (`music_period`, select_one, optional):
    This field is optional, but if selected, it indicates that the music is from a specific music period.
* **Music Genre** (`music_genre`, select_one, optional):
    This field is optional, but if selected, it indicates that the music has a specific genre.

## Tips
* Be sure to select the correct music style and categories that apply to the music.
* Use a rating scale of 1-10 to rate the music.
* Provide any additional comments or notes that are relevant to the music.
* If the music has a composer or is from a specific music period or has a specific genre, select "Yes" in the corresponding field.
