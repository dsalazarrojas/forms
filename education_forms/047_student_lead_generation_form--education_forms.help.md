# Student Lead Generation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Lead Generation Form is a tool used by administrators to collect contact information and other relevant details from prospective students who may be interested in enrolling in the institution. This form helps to generate leads for student recruitment and enrollment processes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Student Name** (student_name)
   * Enter the student's name in this text field.
2. **Contact Number** (contact_number)
   * Enter the student's contact number in this text field.
3. **Email** (email)
   * Enter the student's email in this text field.
4. **Student Level** (student_level)
   * Select the student's academic level from the options: 10th, 11th, or 12th.
5. **Student Interests** (student_interests)
   * Enter the student's interests in this text field.
6. **Parent Name** (parent_name)
   * Enter the parent's name in this text field.
7. **Contact Method** (contact_method)
   * Select the preferred contact method from the options: phone, email, or in person.
8. **Student Address** (student_address)
   * Enter the student's address in this text field.
9. **Assigned Tool** (assigned_tool)
   * Enter the assigned tool in this text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required: false): Enter the student's name.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Enter the student's contact number.
* **Email** (`email`, text, required: false): Enter the student's email.
* **Student Level** (`student_level`, select_one, required: false): Select the student's academic level from the options: 10th, 11th, or 12th.
* **Student Interests** (`student_interests`, text, required: false): Enter the student's interests.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required: false): Enter the parent's name.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, required: false): Select the preferred contact method from the options: phone, email, or in person.
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, text, required: false): Enter the student's address.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required: false): Enter the assigned tool.
