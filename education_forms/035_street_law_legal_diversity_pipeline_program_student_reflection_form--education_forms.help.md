# street_law_legal_diversity_pipeline_program_student_reflection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Street Law Legal Diversity Pipeline Program Student Reflection Form is designed to gather student feedback on the program's effectiveness, impact, and areas for improvement. This form is typically used by program administrators to assess student engagement and gather insights that inform future program development.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly.
2. Ensure you provide enough detail in your responses to give program administrators a clear understanding of your thoughts and experiences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Reflection** (`student_reflection`, text, optional): Please share your thoughts and experiences about the Street Law Legal Diversity Pipeline Program. Describe what you found valuable or challenging about the program.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Please enter your name so that we can associate your response with your identity.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you'd like to be contacted about your submission, please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted about your submission.
* **Street Law Reflection** (`street_law_reflection`, text, optional): Please share any additional reflections on the program that you feel are important.
* **Diversity Pipeline Program** (`diversity_pipeline_program`, text, optional): Describe your understanding of the program's goals and objectives.
* **Why is This Important?** (`why_is_this_important`, text, optional): Share your thoughts on why you think this program is important or valuable.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your responses.
* If you have any concerns or suggestions, don't hesitate to share them.
* Program administrators are here to support you and your growth; your feedback is invaluable to them.
