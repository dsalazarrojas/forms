# SHS Semestral Clearance - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for users to provide personal data for Semestral Clearance. It is intended for internal administrative purposes within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter your general user information in the "User Input" field.
2. Provide your email address to ensure we can contact you.
3. If you have a phone number, please enter it to allow us to contact you if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (user_input, text, optional): Enter your general user information in this field.
* **Email** (email, email, required): Provide your email address so we can contact you. Please ensure it's a valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you have one, to allow us to contact you in case of any urgent matters.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields (in this case, your email address).
- If you need help or have questions, please contact the administrative office for assistance.
