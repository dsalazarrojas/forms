# Science Fair Project Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information for science fair projects, including experiment name, student details, project category, research question, methodology, results, conclusion, and references.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and detailed information about your science fair project.
2. Select the correct category for your project from the provided options.
3. Answer the research question and describe the methodology used in your project.
4. Provide a clear summary of your results and conclusion.
5. List any references used in your project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Experiment Name** (`experiment_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your science fair project.
	+ This field should be a brief and descriptive title of your project.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as the student responsible for the project.
	+ This field is where you should enter your name as the primary author of the project.
* **Project Category** (`project_category`, select_one, required/optional): Choose one of the following categories:
	+ Biology: Biological sciences, such as botany, zoology, or microbiology.
	+ Chemistry: Chemical sciences, such as chemistry, chemistry engineering, or materials science.
	+ Physics: Physical sciences, such as mechanics, thermodynamics, or electromagnetic theory.
	+ Mathematics: Mathematical sciences, such as algebra, geometry, or calculus.
	+ Other: If your project doesn't fit into the above categories, select "Other".
* **Research Question** (`research_question`, text, required/optional): Describe the question or problem you are trying to answer with your research.
	+ This field should be a concise statement of the research question or problem.
* **Methodology** (`methodology`, text, required/optional): Describe the methods and procedures used to conduct the research.
	+ This field should provide a detailed explanation of how you carried out the research.
* **Results** (`results`, text, required/optional): Describe the data collected and any significant findings.
	+ This field should summarize the key results and implications of your research.
* **Conclusion** (`conclusion`, text, required/optional): Summarize what was learned from the research and what the implications might be.
	+ This field should provide a clear summary of the research outcome and its implications.
* **References** (`references`, text, required/optional): List any sources or references used in the research.
	+ This field should include any relevant sources or references used to support your research.

## Tips

* Make sure to answer all required fields accurately and completely.
* Use clear and concise language in all fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the form creator at form@gic.mx.
