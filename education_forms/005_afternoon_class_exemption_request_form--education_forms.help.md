# Afternoon Class Exemption Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide students with a formal process to request exemptions from afternoon classes due to various reasons such as medical conditions, sports activities, or other legitimate reasons.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Student Name**: Enter your official student name exactly as it appears on your student identification card or student record.
2.  **Grade Level**: Enter your current grade level (e.g., 9, 10, 11, etc.).
3.  **Student Email**: Enter your valid student email address.
4.  **Parent or Guardian Name**: If you are under 18 years old, enter your parent or guardian's name.
5.  **Type of Exemption Requested**: Select the category of exemption you are requesting (Permanent, Temporary, or Recurring).
6.  **Course Name for Exemption**: Enter the name of the afternoon class you wish to be exempt from.
7.  **Duration of Exemption**: Select the duration you wish to be exempt for (One week, One month, Full term, or Custom range).
8.  **Effective Start Date**: Enter the date you wish the exemption to start from.
9.  **Effective End Date**: If applicable, enter the date you wish the exemption to end (optional).
10. **Reason for Request**: Provide a brief explanation for your request (e.g., medical, sports, work, etc.).
11.  **Supporting Documents**: Indicate which supporting documents you are submitting with your request (Medical Certificate, Employer Letter, Sports Academy Schedule, or Other legal document).

## Tips
*   Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure quick processing of your request.
*   Provide all required supporting documents to help staff assess your exemption request efficiently.
*   Ensure that you select the correct category for your exemption request.
*   If you are uncertain about any field, refer to your student handbook or consult with your teacher/advisor.

In this guide, each step is clearly outlined to ensure that you complete the form accurately and efficiently.
