# Private School Scholarship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal and academic information from students and their guardians to support private scholarship applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Applicant Information** section with your personal details (page 1).
2. Provide contact information for the applicant in the **Contact** section (page 1).
3. In the **Student Information** section, enter your education history (page 1).
4. In the **Guardian Information** section, enter the contact information of the applicant's guardian (page 1).
5. Describe your **Academic History** (page 1).
6. Enter your **Education Background** (page 1).
7. Fill in the **Scholarship Information** section with details about the scholarship you are applying for (page 1).
8. Finally, click the **Submit** button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Information (page 1)**: Enter your personal details such as name, address, phone number, and email.
* **Contact (page 1)**: Provide the contact information of the person to contact in case of any questions or concerns.
* **Student Information (page 1)**: Describe your education history, including schools you have attended, grades, and any relevant academic achievements.
* **Guardian Information (page 1)**: Enter the contact information of your guardian, including name, relationship, address, phone number, and email.
* **Academic History (page 1)**: Describe your academic history, including any relevant extracurricular activities, sports, or volunteer work.
* **Education Background (page 1)**: Enter details about your current education level, school, and any relevant education-specific achievements.
* **Scholarship Information (page 1)**: Fill in the details about the scholarship you are applying for, including the name and description of the scholarship.
* **Submit (page 1)**: Once you have filled in all the required fields, click the **Submit** button to complete the form.
