## Student Collaboration App Beta Testing Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Collaboration App Beta Testing Consent Form is a form designed to collect information from students, teachers, and school staff who participate in the beta testing program of the Student Collaboration App. This form will collect relevant information such as consent, contact details, role, and other relevant information to ensure a smooth beta testing experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Start by reading the Introduction section, which will explain the purpose of the beta testing program and the benefits of participating in it.
2. In the Consent Statement section, indicate your consent to participate in the beta testing program.
3. Fill out your contact information (Email and Name) in the Contact Information section.
4. Choose your role in the Role section (Student, Teacher, or School Staff).
5. If you choose "Other" as your role, provide a brief description in the role_other field.
6. If you have any additional comments, use the Comments field.
7. Read and agree to the terms of the beta testing program in the form_footer field.
8. Finally, review all the information you have entered and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header** (`form_header`, text, optional): This field is not required but is a header for the form.
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, text, optional): This field explains the purpose of the beta testing program.
* **Consent Statement** (`consent_statement`, text, required): Check the box to indicate your consent to participate in the beta testing program.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, email, optional): Enter your email address so that we can contact you.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your school.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your teacher.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, required): Choose your role as a Student, Teacher, or School Staff.
* **Other (please specify)** (`role_other`, text, optional): If you chose "Other" as your role, provide a brief description.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback you may have.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click this button to submit your form.
* **Form Footer** (`form_footer`, text, optional): Read and agree to the terms of the beta testing program.
* **Comments** (`form_comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback you may have.
* **Do you consent to participate in the beta testing program for the Student Collaboration App?** (`consent_1`, select_multiple, required): Check the box to indicate your consent.
* **Will you be using this beta version for educational purposes only?** (`consent_2`, select_multiple, required): Check the box to indicate your purpose.
* **School Administrator** (`school_admin`, text, optional): Enter the name of the school administrator.
* **Teacher Administrator** (`teacher_admin`, text, optional): Enter the name of the teacher administrator.
* **School** (`school`, text, optional): Enter the name of your school.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the name of your department.
* **Email (for beta testing)** (`email_2`, email, optional): Enter your email address for beta testing purposes.
* **Phone (for beta testing)** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for beta testing purposes.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of your school.
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, text, optional): Enter the email address of your teacher.

This form is designed to help us understand your expectations, provide a smooth experience, and ensure that you have a clear understanding of the beta testing program. If you have any questions or need further clarification, don't hesitate to contact us.
