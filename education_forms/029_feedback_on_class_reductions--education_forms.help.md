# Feedback On Class Reductions - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from instructors and administrators on class reductions. The goal is to gather information on the effectiveness of class size reduction policies and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by answering the initial question on the first page, "Welcome to the Feedback on Class Reductions form".
2. In the second page, enter the amount of reduction in numbers.
3. On the third page, select the date of the reduction.
4. In the fourth page, provide the reason for the reduction.
5. Finally, provide any additional feedback in the last page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome to the Feedback on Class Reductions form** (`introduction`, `text`, not required): This page is an introduction to the form and provides an overview of the purpose of the form.
* **How much reduction do you have to give?** (`class_reduction_amount`, `number`, not required): Enter the actual reduction amount as a number.
* **Date of reduction** (`class_reduction_date`, `date`, not required): Select the date when the reduction occurred or was announced.
* **What is the reason for reduction?** (`class_reduction_reason`, `text`, not required): Describe the reason or circumstances leading to the class reduction.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, not required): Provide any additional comments or suggestions related to the reduction.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when providing feedback
* Use clear and concise language to ensure that your feedback is easy to understand
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact the relevant department or authority.

This guide is intended to help you navigate the feedback on class reductions form efficiently and effectively. If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please don't hesitate to reach out.
