# Creative Play Idea Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Creative Play Idea Submission form is designed to collect innovative and engaging play ideas from educators and partners to enrich the learning experience of children. This form helps us to identify and develop new play ideas that align with our learning objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for your creative play idea in the "Form Title" field.
2. Provide a detailed description of your play idea in the "Description" field.
3. Indicate the related concept of your play idea in the "Related Concept" field.
4. Select the related subject of your play idea from the "Related Subject" field (Art, Music, Science, Math, Reading).
5. Choose the age range of the children for whom your play idea is suitable (Infant, Toddler, Preschooler, School-Age).
6. Indicate the type of play experience (Physical, Cognitive, Emotional).
7. Add any related media or materials that can be used to support your play idea.
8. Provide your contact information, including name, email, phone number, organization, title, and address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (1): Enter a brief title for your creative play idea.
* **Idea Title** (2): Provide a short and descriptive title for your play idea.
* **Description** (3): Write a detailed description of your play idea, including its objective, methodology, and expected outcomes.
* **Related Concept** (4): Describe the related concept or theory behind your play idea.
* **Related Subject** (5): Choose the subject area to which your play idea is most relevant (Art, Music, Science, Math, Reading).
* **Age Range** (6): Select the age range of children for whom your play idea is suitable (Infant, Toddler, Preschooler, School-Age).
* **Play Idea Type** (7): Indicate the type of play experience (Physical, Cognitive, Emotional).
* **Related Media** (8): Provide any relevant media or materials that can be used to support your play idea.
* **Submitter Information** (9): Enter your name, which will be used to identify your submission.
* **Submitter Name** (10): Enter your full name.
* **Submitter Email** (11): Enter your email address.
* **Submitter Phone** (12): Enter your phone number.
* **Submitter Organization** (13): Enter your organization or affiliation.
* **Submitter Title** (14): Enter your title or position.
* **Submitter Address** (15): Enter your physical address.
* **Submitter City** (16): Enter your city.
* **Submitter State/Province** (17): Enter your state or province.
* **Submitter Postal Code** (18): Enter your postal code or zip code.
* **Submitter Country** (19): Enter your country.
* **Submitter Timezone** (20): Enter your timezone.
* **Submitter Timezone Offset** (21): Enter the offset from UTC for your timezone.
* **Submitter Timezone Offset Hours** (22): Enter the hours offset from UTC for your timezone.

Note: The fields marked as "required" are mandatory and must be filled out in order to submit your form.
