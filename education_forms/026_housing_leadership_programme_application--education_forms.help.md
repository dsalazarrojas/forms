# Housing Leadership Programme Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Housing Leadership Programme Application Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in participating in the Housing Leadership Programme. This program aims to develop leadership skills in individuals working in the housing sector.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by ensuring you are eligible to apply for the Housing Leadership Programme.
2. Fill out each page of the form, providing your details and motivations for applying.
3. Submit your application by completing the final page with your name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1 - Personal Details
* **Personal Details**: This is where you will enter your basic personal details, such as your name and contact information.

### Page 2 - Motivation Statements
* **Motivation 1**: This is where you will provide your first motivation for applying to the Housing Leadership Programme.
* **Motivation 2**: This is where you will provide your second motivation for applying to the Housing Leadership Programme.
* **Motivation 3**: This is where you will provide your third motivation for applying to the Housing Leadership Programme.

### Page 3 - Candidate Details
* **Candidate Details**: This is where you will enter information about your background and qualifications relevant to the Housing Leadership Programme.

### Page 4 - Contact Information
* **Contact Information**: This is where you will enter your contact information, such as your email address and phone number.

### Page 5 - References
* **References**: This is where you will enter information about your professional references, such as their name and contact information.

### Page 6 - Education and Work Experience
* **Education and Work Experience**: This is where you will enter information about your education and work experience relevant to the Housing Leadership Programme.

### Page 7 - Additional Information
* **Additional Information**: This is where you will enter any additional information you feel is relevant to your application.

### Page 8 - Submit
* **Submit**: This is where you will confirm your application by submitting the form.

Note: Please ensure you have filled out all required fields before submitting your application.
