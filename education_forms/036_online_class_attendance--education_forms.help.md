# Online Class Attendance - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track the attendance of students in online classes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Enter the student's name under "Student Name".
3. Select the date of attendance under "Attendance Date".
4. Choose the attendance status (available, absent, late) under "Attendance Status".
5. Enter any comments under "Comments" (optional).
6. Enter the teacher's name under "Teacher Name".
7. Enter the class name under "Class Name".
8. Enter the student's ID under "Student ID".
9. Enter the class code under "Class Code".
10. Enter the attendance location under "Attendance Location".
11. Enter the teacher's email under "Teacher Email".
12. Enter the school name under "School Name".
13. Enter the student attendance under "Student Attendance".
14. Enter the attendance time under "Attendance Time".
15. Enter the teacher's phone number under "Teacher Phone".
16. Enter the school phone number under "School Phone".
17. Enter the attendance location (if necessary) under "Attendance Location 2".
18. Enter any attendance note under "Attendance Note" (optional).
19. Enter the student's email under "Student Email".
20. Enter the teacher comments under "Teacher Comments" (optional).
21. Enter the attendance status under "Attendance Status 2".
22. Enter the school website under "School Website" (optional).
23. Enter the teacher website under "Teacher Website" (optional).
24. Enter the class start date under "Class Start Date".
25. Enter the class end date under "Class End Date".
26. Enter the total attendance under "Total Attendance".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Enter the name of the student.
* **Attendance Date**: Select the date of attendance.
* **Attendance Status**: Choose the student's attendance status (available, absent, late).
* **Comments**: Enter any comments related to the attendance.
* **Teacher Name**: Enter the name of the teacher.
* **Class Name**: Enter the name of the class.
* **Student ID**: Enter the student's ID number.
* **Class Code**: Enter the class code.
* **Attendance Location**: Enter the location where the student attended the class.
* **Teacher Email**: Enter the teacher's email address.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of the school.
* **Student Attendance**: Enter the student's attendance count.
* **Attendance Time**: Select the time of attendance.
* **Teacher Phone**: Enter the teacher's phone number.
* **School Phone**: Enter the school phone number.
* **Attendance Location 2**: Enter any additional location information (if required).
* **Attendance Note**: Enter any additional notes related to the attendance.
* **Student Email**: Enter the student's email address.
* **Teacher Comments**: Enter the teacher's comments about the student's attendance.
* **Attendance Status 2**: Choose the status of the attendance.
* **School Website**: Enter the school's website (if required).
* **Teacher Website**: Enter the teacher's website (if required).
* **Class Start Date**: Enter the start date of the class.
* **Class End Date**: Enter the end date of the class.
* **Total Attendance**: Enter the total number of students attended the class.
