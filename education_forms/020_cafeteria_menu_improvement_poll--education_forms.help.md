# Cafeteria Menu Improvement Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from students and staff on the menu, service, and overall experience of the school's cafeteria. Your input will help us understand your needs and preferences, allowing us to make improvements to make the cafeteria a better place for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields: Your Name, I Am A, Meal Plan Status, Typical Meal Time, Food Quality Rating, Food Temperature, Portion Sizes, Price and Value, Service Speed, Staff Friendliness, Cleanliness Rating, and Overall Satisfaction.
2. Choose your responses based on your genuine experiences and opinions.
3. Provide additional comments and suggestions in the "Additional Comments" field.
4. Don't worry about the optional fields unless you have something specific to add.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: Your name is optional, but it will help us understand your identity and affiliation with the school.
* **I Am A**: Select the category that best describes you: Student, Faculty, Staff, Parent, or Visitor.
* **Grade Level or Department**: If you're a student, please select your grade level. If you're staff, please choose your department.
* **Meal Plan Status**: Let us know if you have a meal plan or not.
* **How Often Do You Eat in Cafeteria**: Choose how often you typically eat in the cafeteria.
* **Typical Meal Time**: Select the time of day you usually eat in the cafeteria.
* **Food Quality Rating**: Rate the quality of the food served in the cafeteria.
* **Food Temperature**: Let us know if the food is always served at proper temperature.
* **Portion Sizes**: Choose how you feel about the portion sizes.
* **Menu Variety**: Rate the variety of options available in the menu.
* **Healthy Options Availability**: Choose how many healthy options are available.
* **Dietary Accommodation**: Select if your dietary needs are met.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: Select any dietary restrictions you have (e.g., Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-free, etc.).
* **Favorite Menu Items**: Add your favorite menu items.
* **Least Favorite Items**: Add your least favorite menu items.
* **New Items You Would Like**: Add any new items you'd like to see on the menu.
* **Items That Should Be Removed**: Add items you'd like to see removed from the menu.
* **Price and Value**: Choose how you feel about the prices.
* **Service Speed**: Choose how fast the service is.
* **Staff Friendliness**: Rate the friendliness of the cafeteria staff.
* **Cleanliness Rating**: Rate the cleanliness of the cafeteria.
* **Seating Availability**: Choose if there's enough seating.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the cafeteria.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or suggestions you'd like to make.

## Tips

* Be honest and constructive with your feedback.
* Please keep your responses brief and clear.
* If you're unsure about any question, just choose the option that best represents your opinion.
* Your input will help us improve the cafeteria's menu and services, so thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
