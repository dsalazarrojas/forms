This form is designed to collect information from exhibitors participating in the 2020 Volusia County Fair Livestock Competition. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth experience for the exhibitor and fair organizers.

# 2020 Volusia County Fair Livestock Letter Of Intent - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from exhibitors participating in the 2020 Volusia County Fair Livestock Competition. This information will be used to verify the exhibitor's eligibility for participation and to ensure a smooth and successful experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Read the form carefully and fill out each field accurately and completely.
2.  Ensure you have all necessary documents and information ready before submitting the form.
3.  Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full name of exhibitor** (exhibitor_full_name): This is the name as it appears on your school records.
    *   Please enter your full name as it appears on your school records.
*   **FFA Member Number** (ffa_member_number): This is your 8-digit member identification number.
    *   Enter your 8-digit FFA member identification number.
*   **School affiliation** (school_affiliation): This is the name of your high school or college.
    *   Enter the name of your high school or college.
*   **Contact email address** (contact_email): This is a valid email for fair communications.
    *   Enter a valid email address that fair organizers can use to contact you.
*   **Contact phone number** (contact_phone): This is your 10-digit phone number.
    *   Enter your 10-digit phone number.
*   **Date of birth** (date_of_birth): This is for age verification purposes.
    *   Enter your date of birth for age verification purposes.
*   **Species of livestock to exhibit** (livestock_species): Select the type of animal you are exhibiting.
    *   Choose the species of livestock you are planning to exhibit.
*   **Breed of livestock** (breed of livestock): This is the exact breed of your animal.
    *   Enter the exact breed of your livestock.
*   **Name of animal (if applicable)** (animal_name): This is the name given to your animal.
    *   Enter the name given to your animal, if applicable.
*   **Age of animal in months** (animal_age_months): This is the age of your animal in months.
    *   Enter the age of your animal in months.
*   **Expected weight at time of fair (lbs)** (expected_weight): This is the estimated final weight of your animal.
    *   Enter the estimated final weight of your animal.
*   **Do you have a valid health certificate for your animal?** (health_certification): Select one of the options below.
    *   Choose whether you have a valid health certificate for your animal, and if so, select whether it is attached or will be provided before the fair.
*   **Primary goals for participating in fair** (project_goals): Select all that apply.
    *   Choose the primary reasons you are participating in the fair (Educational experience, Leadership development, Scholarship opportunity, Competition preparation, Community service, Career exploration).
*   **Previous fair participation** (previous_fair_experience): Select one of the options below.
    *   Choose your level of prior participation in fairs (First time participant, 1-2 years, 3-5 years, More than 5 years).
*   **Commitment statement** (commitment_statement): Select one of the options below.
    *   Choose whether you agree or disagree with the commitment statement.
*   **Electronic signature consent** (signature_consent): Type your full name to sign.
    *   Type your full name to signify your agreement to the terms.
*   **Date of submission** (submission_date): This is today's date.
    *   Enter today's date.
*   **Additional comments or special requests** (additional_comments): This is for any other information you want to provide.
    *   Enter any additional comments or special requests you may have for fair organizers.
