# Film Class Attendance Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to record attendance information and provide feedback on film classes. It's a tool for instructors to track student participation and assess the effectiveness of their classes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate date and time you attended the film class.
2. Enter your name as it appears on your official university ID or registration materials.
3. Choose how you felt about the session, providing feedback in the text box.
4. Optionally, provide a score for the session feedback.
5. Select the attendance status (e.g., present, absent, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Session Feedback
* **Session Feedback** (`session_feedback`, text, optional): Write a brief description of your experience during the session.

### Participant Name
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your official university ID or registration materials.

### Attendance Date
* **Attendance Date** (`attendance_date`, date, optional): Select the date you attended the film class.

### Attendance Time
* **Attendance Time** (`attendance_time`, time, optional): Select the time you attended the film class.

### Attendance Status
* **Attendance Status** (`attendance_status`, text, optional): Choose your attendance status (e.g., present, absent, etc.).

### Session Feedback Score
* **Session Feedback Score** (`session_feedback_score`, text, optional): Provide a score for the session feedback, if applicable.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and honest feedback to help instructors improve their classes.
* If you're unsure about any field, don't hesitate to ask your instructor for clarification.
* This form is designed to be completed by students, but feel free to use it for any relevant feedback purposes.
