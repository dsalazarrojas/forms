# University Recourse Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students for the university's resources. Students can use it to seek assistance with academic, support, or other services, and university officials will review the submitted data to address their requests.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Student Information** (Student Information): This is the first page of the form. It is a field where you can enter your student ID or other relevant information. Please fill in this page carefully.
2. **Academic Remarks** (Academic Remarks): In this field, you can provide any remarks or comments about your academic situation. This can help university officials better understand your needs.
3. **University Officials** (University Officials): Select the relevant university officials who can assist you with your request.
4. **Course Selection** (Course Selection): Choose the course or topic you need help with from the provided options.
5. **Date of Request** (Date of Request): Enter the date when you need assistance with.
6. **Time of Request** (Time of Request): Enter the time when you need assistance with.
7. **Additional Information** (Additional Information): If you have any other important information about your request, please add it here.
8. **Email** (Email): Enter your university-issued email address.
9. **Phone Number** (Phone Number): Enter your phone number to enable university officials to contact you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Information** (`student_information`, text, optional): Enter your student ID or any relevant student information.
* **Academic Remarks** (`academic_remarks`, text, optional): Provide any remarks or comments about your academic situation.
* **University Officials** (`university_officials`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the relevant university officials who can assist you with your request.
* **Course Selection** (`course_selection`, select_one, optional): Select the course or topic you need help with from the provided options.
* **Date of Request** (`date_of_request`, date, optional): Enter the date when you need assistance with.
* **Time of Request** (`time_of_request`, time, optional): Enter the time when you need assistance with.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, note, optional): If you have any other important information about your request, please add it here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your university-issued email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number to enable university officials to contact you.
