# MPH Concentration Change Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The MPH Concentration Change Survey form is used to submit a request to change your concentration or major at the university.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the required information before submitting.
2. Fill in the required fields:
	* First Name
	* Last Name
	* University Program
	* Proposed Concentration Name
	* Reason for Change (optional)
3. If applicable, select the concentration(s) you are interested in changing to:
	* Concentration(s) Name
4. You may also add comments or remarks in the text areas:
	* Comments
	* Advisor Comments (if applicable)
5. Finally, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Your first name as it appears on your official university documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Your last name as it appears on your official university documents.
* **University Program** (`university_program`, select_one, required): The current program you are enrolled in (e.g., Master of Public Health).
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, required): The name of the program you are changing to (if known).
* **Concentration Name** (`concentration_name`, select_multiple, optional): The concentrations you are interested in changing to (if applicable).
* **Reason for Change** (`reason_change_concentration`, text, optional): A brief explanation of why you are changing your concentration or major.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Any additional comments you would like to include with your request.
* **Advisor Comments** (`advisor_comments`, note, optional): Comments from your advisor on your request.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): The department or area of study for your proposed concentration or major.
* **Program** (`program`, text, optional): The proposed program for your concentration or major change.
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, text, required): Your electronic signature.
* **Advisor Comments** (`advisor_comments`, note, optional): Comments from your advisor on your request.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Your university email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Your contact phone number.
* **University Name** (`university_name`, text, optional): The name of your university.
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, date, optional): The date you submit the form.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): The person submitting the form.
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`, text, optional): The person approving the form.
* **Approved Date** (`approved_date`, date, optional): The date the form was approved.
* **Approved Time** (`approved_time`, time, optional): The time the form was approved.
* **Approved Comments** (`approved_comments`, note, optional): Comments on the approved form.
* **Rationale** (`rationale`, text, optional): Any additional comments or reasoning for the approval or denial of the form.
* **Advisor Comments** (`advisor_comments`, note, optional): Comments from your advisor on the form approval or denial.
