# Student Faculty Collaboration Highlight Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to obtain consent from students and faculty to collaborate and highlight specific projects. It's designed to ensure that all parties are aware of and agree to the terms and conditions of the collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the project details: 
    - Enter the project title.
    - Select the project type (e.g., Independent Project, Supervised Project, Group Project, Class Project, or Option 5).
    - Choose the start and end dates of the project.
2. Enter your information:
    - Enter your student name and signature (if you are a student).
    - Enter your faculty name and signature (if you are a faculty member).
3. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Title**: Enter a brief title of the project.
* **Project Type**: Select the type of project you are collaborating on (e.g., Independent Project, Supervised Project, Group Project, Class Project, or Option 5).
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the project.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of the project.
* **Student Name**: Enter your name if you are a student involved in the project.
* **Faculty Name**: Enter your name if you are a faculty member involved in the project.
* **Student Signature**: If you are a student, please sign your name to acknowledge that you have read and understood the project details. 
* **Faculty Signature**: If you are a faculty member, please sign your name to acknowledge that you have read and understood the project details.
