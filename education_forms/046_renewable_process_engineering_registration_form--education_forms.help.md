# renewable_process_engineering_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals interested in Renewable Process Engineering, aiming to gather information from those who wish to learn more about or pursue a career in this field.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide a valid email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Select your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
6. Choose the appropriate time of birth in the "Time of Birth" field.
7. Select one option for "What is your interest in Renewable Process Engineering?" from the dropdown menu.
8. Choose all applicable methods from the "What are your preferred methods of learning?" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): Select your date of birth from the calendar.
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, `time`, required: false): Choose your time of birth from the dropdown menu.
* **What is your interest in Renewable Process Engineering?** (`select_one`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one option that best describes your interest:
	+ Interest in learning new things
	+ Interest in new skills
	+ Interest in personal growth
* **What are your preferred methods of learning?** (`select_multiple`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all applicable methods from the dropdown menu:
	+ Online Courses
	+ Classroom Lectures
	+ Workshops
	+ Videos
	+ Podcasts
	+ Books
