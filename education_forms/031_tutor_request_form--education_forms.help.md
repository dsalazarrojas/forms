# Tutor Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tutor Request Form is a tool used to request a tutor for a specific subject and topic. It helps to match students with the most suitable tutor based on their needs and requirements. This form is used by students to provide detailed information about their needs, such as subject, topic, and available time, to enable tutors to offer their services and provide guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Tutor Request Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Student Name**.
2. Enter your **Email Address** so that we can contact you with updates and notifications.
3. Provide a brief **Description Of Homework Question** to help the tutor understand your needs.
4. Select your **Available Time** for the tutoring session.
5. Choose a **Topic** from the list of available options.
6. Select the type of **Tutor Type** you require, such as Online or Offline.
7. Choose the **Select Language** you want to communicate with your tutor.
8. Specify how much **Available Duration** you have for the session.
9. Set the required **Price** for the session.
10. If applicable, specify your **Student University** and **Tutor University**.
11. Select a **Payment Method** for the session.
12. Click the **Submit Button** to submit your request.
13. Optionally, select additional details such as **Select Subject**, **Select Level**, **Select Gender**, **Select Location**, **Select Language 2**, **Select Topic 2**, **Select Tutor Level**, **Select Tutor Qualification**, and **Submit Button 2**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your student ID or university records.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted for updates and notifications.
* **Description Of Homework Question**: Provide a brief description of the homework question or topic you need help with.
* **Available Time**: Select the time slots you are available for the session, e.g., Monday, Tuesday, etc.
* **Select Topic**: Choose the specific subject you require help with, such as Math or Physics.
* **Select Tutor Type**: Choose whether you want to meet your tutor Online or Offline.
* **Select Language**: Choose the language you are most comfortable communicating in.
* **Available Duration**: Specify how many hours you are willing to spend on the session.
* **Required Price**: Set the price you are willing to pay for the session.
* **Student University**: If you are currently studying at a university, provide the name of your institution.
* **Tutor University**: If you are requesting a tutor from a specific university, provide the name of the university.
* **Select Payment Method**: Choose how you would like to pay for the session, such as Online Payment or Offline Payment.
* **Submit Button**: Click this button to submit your request to our team.
* **Cancel**: Click this button if you change your mind or need to cancel your request.
* **Email To Tutor**: Enter the email address of the tutor you want to contact.
* **Select Subject**: Choose one or more subjects you require help with, such as Physics or Biology.
* **Select Level**: Choose your current level of study, such as High School or University.
* **Select Gender**: Choose whether you prefer a male or female tutor.
* **Select Location**: Choose where you would like to meet your tutor, such as On Campus or Online.
* **Select Language 2**: Choose one or more languages you are proficient in.
* **Select Topic 2**: Choose a specific topic you require help with, such as Math or Chemistry.
* **Select Tutor Level**: Choose the level of expertise you require from your tutor, such as Beginner or Advanced.
* **Select Tutor Qualification**: Choose the highest level of education the tutor should have, such as Bachelor or Master.
* **Submit Button 2**: This is a duplicate of the **Submit Button**. Please note that the **Submit Button 2** is not a required field.
