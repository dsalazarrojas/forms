# e_learning_course_order_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and manage information for e-learning course orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter the relevant information in each field.
* Review your input carefully before submitting the form.
* Ensure you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **E-learning course information (1)**: Enter the title of the course you wish to order.
* **User information (2)**: Provide your contact details so we can reach out to you.
* **Payment details (3)**: Enter the payment method and amount for the course.
* **E-signature (4)**: Add your digital signature.
* **Course information (5)**: Select the course you wish to enroll in.
* **Payment method (6)**: Choose the payment method to use.
* **Payment amount (7)**: Enter the payment amount.
* **Course name (8)**: Enter the course name.
* **Course price (9)**: Enter the course price.
* **Start date (10)**: Choose the start date for the course.
* **End date (11)**: Choose the end date for the course.
* **Course length (12)**: Select the course duration.
* **Course description (13)**: Enter a brief description of the course.
* **Email address (14)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone number (15)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Signature (16)**: Add a signature to confirm your order.
* **Order status (17)**: Select the order status.

## Tips
* Review the form carefully before submission.
* Ensure you have entered all necessary information.
* If you have any questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
