# Muet Results Update Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Muet Results Update Form is used to update Muet results for individual students.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's information in the "User Info" field.
2. Provide the student's Muet result in the "Student Muet Result" field.
3. Enter the subject-wise marks for each subject in the relevant fields (e.g., "Subject Wise Marks", "Subject Wise Mmarks", etc.).
4. Upload any supporting documents related to the update, if necessary.
5. Enter any additional questions or comments in the "Additional Question" field.
6. Review and submit the form to update the student's Muet results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info** (`user_info`, text, optional): Enter the student's name and other relevant information.
* **Student Muet Result** (`student_muet_result`, text, optional): Enter the student's Muet result.
* **Subject Wise Marks** (`subject_wise_marks`, text, optional): Enter the student's marks for each subject.
* **Subject Wise Mmarks** (`subject_wise_mmarks`, text, optional): Enter the student's marks for each subject.
* **Subject Wise Emarks** (`subject_wise_emarks`, text, optional): Enter the student's Emarks (whatever that means).
* **Subject Wise Kmks** (`subject_wise_kmks`, text, optional): Enter the student's Kmks (whatever that means).
* **Subject Wise Mks** (`subject_wise_mks`, text, optional): Enter the student's Mks (whatever that means).
* **Subject Wise Skks** (`subject_wise_skks`, text, optional): Enter the student's Skks (whatever that means).
* **Subject Wise Omks** (`subject_wise_omks`, text, optional): Enter the student's Omks (whatever that means).
* **Subject Wise Tmks** (`subject_wise_tmks`, text, optional): Enter the student's Tmks (whatever that means).
* **Subject Wise Amks** (`subject_wise_amks`, text, optional): Enter the student's Amks (whatever that means).
* **Subject Wise Omks 2** (`subject_wise_omks_2`, text, optional): Enter the student's Omks 2 (whatever that means).
* **Subject Wise Mks 2** (`subject_wise_mks_2`, text, optional): Enter the student's Mks 2 (whatever that means).
