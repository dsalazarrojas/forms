# Student Climate Literacy Materials Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Climate Literacy Materials Request Form is a tool used to gather information from students or educators about their requests for climate literacy materials.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your first name, last name, and email address.
2. Select your institution, request type, and school level.
3. Provide your shipping address, including city, state, zip code, and country.
4. Specify the quantity of materials you are requesting.
5. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address to receive updates on your materials request.
* **Institution** (`institution`, text, required): Select your institution from the provided options.
* **Request Type** (`request_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose from the options "Yes" or "No" to indicate the type of request.
* **Materials Requested** (`materials_requested`, text, required): Specify the climate literacy materials you are requesting.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the person to contact.
* **Institution Type** (`institution_type`, text, required): Select your institution type from the provided options.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter your department name.
* **School Level** (`school_level`, text, required): Select your school level from the provided options.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, text, required): Enter your grade level.
* **Grade Range** (`grade_range`, text, required): Enter the grade range you are interested in.
* **Materials Ordered** (`materials_ordered`, text, required): Specify the materials you would like to order.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, text, required): Enter the quantity of materials you would like to order.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required): Enter your shipping address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city of your shipping address.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter the state of your shipping address.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter the zip code of your shipping address.
* **Country** (`country`, text, required): Enter the country of your shipping address.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your request.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your school.
* **Email Sent** (`email_sent`, note, optional): This field is automatically populated once your request has been processed.
