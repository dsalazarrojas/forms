# Robotics Control Software Certification Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Robotics Control Software Certification Registration Form is designed to register individuals or entities for the Robotics Control Software Certification, a professional certification program that verifies expertise in robotics control software.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Candidate Name, ensuring it matches your official identity.
2. Enter a valid Email address for communication purposes.
3. Enter your Phone number for contact and verification.
4. Provide your current Address for record keeping.
5. Enter the name of the robot you are registering.
6. Select the type(s) of robot you are registering (e.g., Robotics, Industrial, Educational).
7. Choose the Certification Level (Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced) that you are applying for.
8. Enter the date you obtained your certification.
9. Enter the model of the robot you are registering.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Candidate Name** (`candidate_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
  * Please ensure it is spelled correctly to avoid any issues with your registration.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that you have access to and check regularly.
  * This will be used for communication and updates about your certification status.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact and verification purposes.
  * Ensure it is a valid phone number that can be used for communication.
- **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your current physical address for record keeping.
  * This will be kept confidential and used only for administrative purposes.
- **Robot Name** (`robot_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the robot you are registering.
  * This should be a distinct identifier for your robot.
- **Robot Type** (`robot_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type(s) of robot you are registering (e.g., Robotics, Industrial, Educational).
  * Choose all that apply, separated by commas.
- **Certification Level** (`certification_level`, select_one, required): Choose the level of certification you are applying for (Basic, Intermediate, Advanced).
  * Choose the level that corresponds to your expertise and experience.
- **Certification Date** (`certification_date`, date, required): Enter the date you obtained your certification.
  * Format should be mm/dd/yyyy.
- **Robot Model** (`robot_model`, text, required): Enter the model of the robot you are registering.
  * This should be the exact model of the robot you are registering.

## Tips
- Ensure the information you enter is accurate and complete to avoid any issues with your registration.
- Review your entries carefully before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact the certification authority.
