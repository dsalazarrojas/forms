# Final Assessment For Drawing Course - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Final Assessment For Drawing Course" form is designed to gather evaluations and feedback from students and instructors on a drawing course. It will help identify areas of improvement and ensure that the course meets its learning objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
- **Read each section carefully** to ensure you're providing relevant and detailed feedback.
- **Fill out each section** based on your experience or observation.
- **Provide as much detail as possible** to help the instructors identify areas that need improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Prophets** (`student_prophets`, text, required: false)
  This section is meant for students to provide feedback about the course, including their thoughts and opinions about their experience. Please provide your thoughts and feelings about the course.
* **Instructor Evaluations** (`instructor_evaluations`, text, required: false)
  This section is for instructors to evaluate their own teaching methods and identify areas of improvement. You can provide feedback on how you think the course should be modified or changed.
* **Final Artwork** (`final_artwork`, text, required: false)
  Share your final project or a sample of your work that represents your understanding of the course material. This will help instructors see your progress and assess your work.

## Tips
- **Be honest and detailed** in your feedback. The more information you provide, the better the course will be improved.
- **Use examples or samples** to illustrate your points, especially when providing feedback on the final artwork section.
- **Proofread your answers** before submitting to ensure they are accurate and clear.
