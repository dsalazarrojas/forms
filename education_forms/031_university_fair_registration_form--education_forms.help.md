# University Fair Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for university event registration. It collects information about an event, including its details and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide event information (Event Information)
	* Enter a brief description of the event.
2. Enter organizer information (Organizer Info)
	* Provide details about the person or organization hosting the event.
3. Enter event date and time (Event Date)
	* Choose the date and time of the event.
4. Enter event time (Event Time)
	* Select the time of the event.
5. Enter event venue (Venue)
	* Specify the location of the event.
6. Select event type (Event Type)
	* Choose whether the event has a specific type or is open to various types.
7. Enter event details (Event Details)
	* Provide more details about the event.
8. Enter contact person information (Contact Person)
	* Provide details about the person responsible for the event.
9. Enter contact email (Contact Email)
	* Enter the email address of the contact person.
10. Enter contact phone number (Contact Phone)
	* Specify the phone number of the contact person.
11. Enter contact note (Contact Note)
	* Leave any additional comments or notes about the contact person.
12. Confirm form submission (Form Submitted By)
	* Enter your name as a validation to ensure the form is submitted correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Information (event_info)**: Enter a brief description of the event.
* **Organizer Info (organizer_info)**: Provide details about the person or organization hosting the event.
* **Event Date (event_date)**: Choose the date of the event.
* **Event Time (event_time)**: Select the time of the event.
* **Venue (venue)**: Specify the location of the event.
* **Event Type (event_type)**: Choose whether the event has a specific type or is open to various types.
* **Event Details (event_details)**: Provide more details about the event.
* **Contact Person (contact_person)**: Provide details about the person responsible for the event.
* **Contact Email (contact_email)**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone (contact_phone)**: Specify the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Note (contact_note)**: Leave any additional comments or notes about the contact person.
* **Form Submitted By (form_submitted_by)**: Enter your name as a validation to ensure the form is submitted correctly.
