# Curriculum Innovation Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Curriculum Innovation Feedback Survey is designed to gather feedback from individuals on the quality and effectiveness of a new or existing curriculum initiative. This feedback will be used by educators and training providers to improve the curriculum and better meet the needs of their students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Select your role at the school or training provider.
2. Choose your role within the institution.
3. Enter the name of the new or existing curriculum initiative you are providing feedback on.
4. Rate the quality of support provided by the curriculum initiative from 1 to 5.
5. Select the factors that most influenced your response to the new curriculum initiative.
6. Indicate whether you are satisfied with the new or existing curriculum initiative.
7. Provide any feedback or suggestions for the new or existing curriculum initiative.
8. Add any additional comments you have about the new or existing curriculum initiative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your role at the school or training provider?** (`role`, select_one, required): Please select one of the following options to indicate your role at the school or training provider: Teacher, Student, Administrator, or Other.
* **What is your role within the institution?** (`role_institution`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the roles that apply to you within the institution. Options include School Principal, School Vice Principal, School Teacher, School Student, University Lecturer, University Professor, Training Provider, or Other.
* **What is the name of the new or existing curriculum initiative you are providing feedback on?** (`curriculum_initiative`, text, required): Enter the name of the new or existing curriculum initiative you are providing feedback on.
* **How would you rate the quality of support provided by the curriculum initiative?** (`feedback_question_1`, number, required): Rate your experience from 1 to 5, where 1 is very poor and 5 is very good.
* **What factors most influenced your response to the new curriculum initiative?** (`feedback_question_2`, select_multiple, required): Select all the factors that most influenced your response to the new curriculum initiative. Options include Curriculum design and structure, Resources and materials, Teaching methods and approaches, Learning outcomes and assessment, or Other.
* **Are you satisfied with the new or existing curriculum initiative?** (`curriculum_initiative_satisfied`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you are satisfied or not satisfied with the new or existing curriculum initiative.
* **Do you have any feedback or suggestions for the new or existing curriculum initiative?** (`feedback_suggestions`, text, required): Provide any feedback or suggestions you have for the new or existing curriculum initiative.
* **Do you have any additional comments?** (`additional_comments`, text, required): Add any additional comments you have about the new or existing curriculum initiative.
