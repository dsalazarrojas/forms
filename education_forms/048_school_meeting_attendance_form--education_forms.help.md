# School Meeting Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Meeting Attendance Form is used to report attendance for school meetings. It helps teachers, staff, or students to indicate their attendance status and provide additional information about their presence or absence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Submitter Information section with your name and email address.
2. Select your attendance status (Present, Absent, or Late) and provide a brief note if necessary.
3. Enter the date of the meeting you attended.
4. Choose your role (Student, Teacher, or Admin) and select the relevant groups (Class, Department, Floor, and Room).
5. Select the time you were present.
6. Enter the location of the meeting.
7. (Optional) Add any additional notes about your attendance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submitter Information** (Submitter Information, Text, Required): Enter your name and email address in this section. This information helps us to contact you if needed.
* **Attendance Status** (attendance_status, Select One, Required): Select whether you were Present, Absent, or Late from the dropdown menu. This helps us understand who attended the meeting.
* **Date** (date, Date, Required): Enter the date you attended the meeting.
* **Role** (submitter_type, Select One, Required): Choose whether you are a Student, Teacher, or Admin attending the meeting.
* **Class** (submitter_group, Select Multiple, Required): Select all the classes you belong to or are associated with.
* **Department** (submitter_dept, Select Multiple, Required): Select all the departments you are associated with.
* **Floor** (submitter_floor, Select Multiple, Required): Select the floors where you are present.
* **Room** (submitter_room, Select Multiple, Required): Select the rooms where you are present.
* **Section** (submitter_section, Select Multiple, Required): Select the sections where you are present.
* **Time** (submitter_time, Time, Required): Enter the time you arrived or left the meeting.
* **Location** (submitter_location, Text, Required): Enter the location of the meeting.
* **Note** (submitter_note, Note, Optional): Add any additional notes about your attendance. This is optional and not required.
* **Other** (submitter_other, Text, Optional): If you have any other information about your attendance, enter it here. This is optional and not required.
