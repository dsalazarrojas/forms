# guest_pass_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request a guest pass for a visitor accompanying a student, faculty, or staff member.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student or staff member's name, student or staff number, and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter the guest's name and email address in the respective fields.
3. Select the department that the student or staff member is affiliated with.
4. Optionally, enter a message to provide more information about the guest and the reason for their visit.
5. Choose a request date and time.
6. Once all fields are filled out, submit the form to initiate the guest pass request process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the student or staff member accompanying the guest.
* **Student Number** (`student_number`, number, optional): Enter the student or staff number of the accompanying person.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the student or staff member accompanying the guest.
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the guest.
* **Guest Email** (`guest_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the guest.
* **Message** (`message`, note, optional): Enter any additional information about the guest or the reason for their visit.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, optional): Select the department that the student or staff member is affiliated with.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, optional): Enter the date for which you are requesting the guest pass.
* **Request Time** (`request_time`, date, optional): Enter the time for which you are requesting the guest pass.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can contact the relevant department for clarification.
* The guest pass request process may take some time, so please be patient and allow for a reasonable processing time.
