# Learner Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and ratings from learners who have participated in a survey or program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Enter your Student ID
2. Enter the Name of the Survey or Program
3. Enter the Name of the Survey or Learners (or choose "Other" if applicable)
4. Enter your Email (if applicable)
5. Rate your experience on a scale of 0-15
6. Enter any feedback you may have
7. Select one of the available options or choose "Other" if applicable
8. Click the "Submit" button to submit your feedback

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student ID** (`student_id`, required): Enter your Student ID to help us identify you and your participation in the survey or program.
* **Name of Survey** (`name_of_survey`, required): Enter the name of the survey or program you participated in.
* **Name of Survey or Learners** (`name_of_survey_or_learners`, required): Enter the name of the survey or program you participated in (or choose "Other" if it's not listed).
* **Name of Survey or Program (if Other)** (`name_of_surveyor_program_other`, optional): Enter the name of the survey or program if it's not listed.
* **Email** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address to help us contact you (if applicable).
* **Rating 0-5** (`rating_0_5`, optional): Rate your experience on a scale of 0-5.
* **Rating 6-10** (`rating_6_10`, optional): Rate your experience on a scale of 6-10.
* **Rating 11-15** (`rating_11_15`, optional): Rate your experience on a scale of 11-15.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, optional): Enter any feedback or comments you may have.
* **Submit** (`submit`, required): Select one of the available options or choose "Other" if applicable.
* **Select Other** (`submit_other`, optional): Enter any other feedback or comments you may have.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you only fill out this form if you have actually participated in the survey or program being referred to.
* If you have any difficulty filling out this form, please contact us for assistance.
