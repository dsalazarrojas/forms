# scholarship_renewal_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The scholarship renewal form is designed for students who have been awarded a scholarship previously and are seeking renewal for an additional semester or year of funding. This form helps the scholarship committee evaluate the student's continued eligibility for the award based on their current academic and financial status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the scholarship renewal form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your student information, including your name, student ID, and academic institution.
2. Select your current academic status and financial status to help the scholarship committee assess your eligibility.
3. Provide a brief explanation for the reason you are seeking renewal.
4. Enter the expected completion date of your degree program, if applicable.
5. Enter the expected graduation date of your degree program, if applicable.
6. Select the current semester you are currently in (if applicable).
7. Enter the total number of credits you currently have.
8. Enter your current GPA (Grade Point Average).
9. Select the status of your current scholarship (Active, Expired, or Cancelled).
10. Enter the amount you are seeking to renew the scholarship for (if applicable).
11. Choose whether you are currently receiving full or partial scholarship funding.
12. Select whether you are currently being awarded the scholarship (True or False).
13. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Student Information
Student Information (`student_info`, Text, Optional)
The student information section is where you enter your name and student ID. This information is required to identify you as the student who is submitting the form.

### Academic Status
Academic Status (`academic_status`, Select One, Optional)
Select your current academic status to help the scholarship committee evaluate your eligibility for renewal. If you are unsure, select 'Unknown' or contact the scholarship committee for guidance.

### Financial Status
Financial Status (`financial_status`, Select One, Optional)
Indicate your current financial status to help the scholarship committee assess your need for renewed funding. If you are unsure, select 'Unknown' or contact the scholarship committee for guidance.

### Reason for Renewal
Reason for Renewal (`reason_for_renewal`, Text, Optional)
Provide a brief explanation for seeking renewal. This could be due to a change in your academic plan, financial circumstances, or other relevant reasons.

### Expected Completion Date
Expected Completion Date (`expected_completion_date`, Date, Optional)
If applicable, enter the expected completion date of your degree program.

### Expected Graduation Date
Expected Graduation Date (`expected_graduation_date`, Date, Optional)
If applicable, enter the expected graduation date of your degree program.

### Academic Institution
Academic Institution (`academic_institution`, Text, Optional)
Enter the name of the academic institution you are currently attending.

### Student ID
Student ID (`student_id`, Text, Optional)
Enter your student ID number.

### Academic Program
Academic Program (`academic_program`, Text, Optional)
Enter the name of your current academic program.

### Expected Degree Date
Expected Degree Date (`expected_degree_date`, Date, Optional)
If applicable, enter the expected completion date of your degree program.

### Current Semester
Current Semester (`current_semester`, Select One, Optional)
Select the current semester you are in (Fall, Spring, or Summer).

### Total Credits
Total Credits (`total_credits`, Number, Optional)
Enter the total number of credits you currently have.

### GPA
GPA (`gpa`, Number, Optional)
Enter your current GPA (Grade Point Average).

### Scholarship Status
Scholarship Status (`scholarship_status`, Select One, Optional)
Select the status of your current scholarship (Active, Expired, or Cancelled).

### Renewal Amount
Renewal Amount (`renewal_amount`, Number, Optional)
If applicable, enter the amount you are seeking to renew the scholarship for.

### Scholarship Funding
Scholarship Funding (`scholarship_funding`, Select Multiple, Optional)
Choose whether you are currently receiving full, partial, or no scholarship funding.

### Scholarship Award
Scholarship Award (`scholarship_award`, Select Multiple, Optional)
Select whether you are currently being awarded the scholarship (True or False).

## Tips
* Ensure you have not already submitted a scholarship renewal form for the same period or semester.
* If you are unsure about your eligibility or have already submitted a renewal form, please contact the scholarship committee for guidance.
* Provide accurate and truthful information to ensure a fair evaluation of your renewal request.
