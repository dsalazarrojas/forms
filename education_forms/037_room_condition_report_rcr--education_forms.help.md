# Room Condition Report Rcr - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Room Condition Report Rcr form is used to report the current condition of a residential room for maintenance or inspection purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out each field with the required information.
* Make sure to provide a clear and concise description of the current damages and room condition.
* If you have any additional comments or concerns, use the "Additional Comments" field to provide more context.
* Select the option to "Submit" the report to the Res Life Office, or "Cancel" if the report is not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Current Damages** (Text Field): Describe the current damages or issues with the room, such as broken fixtures, stains, or other noticeable problems.
* **Date Of Inspection** (Date Field): Enter the date of the inspection or the date when you first noticed the damages.
* **Resident Name** (Text Field): Enter your name, or the name of the resident or occupant who reported the issue.
* **Date Of Check Out** (Date Field): If you are reporting a issue on the day of check out, enter the date of check out. If not, leave blank.
* **Condition Of Room** (Text Field): Describe the current condition of the room, including any issues or concerns you have noticed.
* **Additional Comments** (Text Field): Provide any additional comments or concerns you may have about the room condition.
* **Res Life Office** (Text Field): Enter the name of the department or office responsible for handling maintenance or inspections.
* **Submit To Res Life Office** (Select One): Select "Submit" to send the report to the Res Life Office, or "Cancel" to discard the report.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide as much detail as possible to help the Res Life Office understand the issue.
* If you are not sure what to report, use the "Additional Comments" field to provide context.
* If you need to report multiple issues, consider using the "Additional Comments" field to provide a brief summary and list each issue separately.
