# Instruction Builder Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information from employees about their work habits and preferences regarding instructions and training. The responses will help the organization understand its employees' needs and tailor its training programs to better support them.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the select_one and select_multiple questions by choosing one or multiple options from the dropdown menus. If you select "Other (please specify)" for any question, please provide your answer in the text field below.
2. Fill in the text fields with your answer.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, you can type them in the "Other" text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your role in your organization?** (`title`, `select_one`, required)
	+ Choose the option that best describes your role in the organization.
* **What is your role?** (`role`, `text`, required)
	+ Type your role in the organization.
* **Which department do you work in?** (`department`, `select_multiple`, required)
	+ Choose all the departments that apply to your role.
* **What is your location?** (`location`, `select_one`, required)
	+ Choose your location.
* **What is your email?** (`email`, `email`, required)
	+ Type your email address.
* **What is your phone number?** (`phone`, `text`, required)
	+ Type your phone number.
* **How often do you receive instructions?** (`instructions`, `select_one`, required)
	+ Choose how often you receive instructions.
* **How often do you attend training programs?** (`training_programs`, `select_one`, required)
	+ Choose how often you attend training programs.
* **What factors influence the quality of your instructions?** (`quality`, `select_multiple`, required)
	+ Choose all the factors that influence the quality of your instructions.
* **What factors influence the effectiveness of your instructions?** (`effectiveness`, `select_multiple`, required)
	+ Choose all the factors that influence the effectiveness of your instructions.
* **How much time do you typically spend on instructions?** (`completion_time`, `time`, required)
	+ Type the amount of time you spend on instructions.
* **How often do you complete instructions?** (`completion_frequency`, `select_one`, required)
	+ Choose how often you complete instructions.
* **How do you evaluate training?** (`training_evaluation`, `select_multiple`, required)
	+ Choose all the ways you evaluate training.
* **Would you be interested in providing feedback on our training materials?** (`feedback`, `select_one`, required)
	+ Choose whether you would be interested in providing feedback.
* **Would you like to receive email notifications about new instruction materials?** (`email_notification`, `select_one`, optional)
	+ Choose whether you would like to receive email notifications.
* **Is there anything else you would like to share with us?** (`other`, `text`, optional)
	+ Type any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
