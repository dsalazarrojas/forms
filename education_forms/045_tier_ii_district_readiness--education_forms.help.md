# Tier II District Readiness - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help districts assess their readiness for Tier II implementation, ensuring they are on the right track and identifying areas that need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by reviewing the form sections and ensuring you have all necessary information.
2. Enter the name of your district in the **district name** field.
3. Select all relevant Tier II interests from the options listed.
4. Answer the **training attendance** question about your plans for attending Tier II training.
5. Choose the status of **implementation status** for your district.
6. Describe your district's **next steps** for Tier II implementation.
7. Provide any additional **comments** about your district's Tier II readiness.
8. Enter the contact information for your district in the **district contact** field.
9. Answer the **training facilitator** question about whether you plan to have a facilitator present for training.
10. Select the status of **technology use** for your district.
11. Select the status of **data collection** for your district.
12. Select the status of **data analysis** for your district.
13. Select the status of **data use** for your district.
14. Select the status of **progress monitoring** for your district.
15. Choose the **progress monitoring frequencies** for your district.
16. Enter any additional details about your district's **Tier 2 progress**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **district name** (text, required): Enter the name of your district.
* **tier ii interest** (select multiple, required): Choose all relevant interests for Tier II, such as Tier II Training, Tier II Certification, Tier II Implementation.
* **training attendance** (select one, required): Answer whether you will be attending Tier II training.
* **implementation status** (select one, required): Select the current status of Tier II implementation in your district.
* **next steps** (text, required): Describe your district's next steps for Tier II implementation.
* **comments** (note, optional): Provide any additional comments about your district's Tier II readiness.
* **district contact** (text, required): Enter the contact information for your district.
* **training facilitator** (select one, required): Answer whether you plan to have a facilitator present for training.
* **technology use** (select multiple, optional): Select the status of technology use for Tier II.
* **data collection** (select one, optional): Select the status of data collection for Tier II.
* **data analysis** (select one, optional): Select the status of data analysis for Tier II.
* **data use** (select one, optional): Select the status of data use for Tier II.
* **progress monitoring** (select one, optional): Select the status of progress monitoring for Tier II.
* **progress monitoring frequencies** (select one, optional): Choose the frequencies of progress monitoring for Tier II.
* **Tier 2 progress** (text, optional): Enter any additional details about your district's Tier 2 progress.
