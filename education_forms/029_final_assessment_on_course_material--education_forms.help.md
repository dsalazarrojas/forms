# Final Assessment On Course Material - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Final Assessment On Course Material form is designed to gather feedback from users about their experience with the course material. It helps course creators evaluate the effectiveness of the course material and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields: **Educator Name**, **Date**, and **Email**.
* If applicable, select the **List of Course Materials** options that best describe your experience with the course.
* Provide your **Overall Satisfaction** level, from 1 to 5, to express your opinion about the course material.
* You can also add any additional **Comments** or **Assessment Comments** to provide more detailed feedback.
* You can optionally provide your **Course Materials Title** and **Key Takeaways**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Educator Name**: Enter the name of the educator who taught the course.
* **Final Assessment On Course Material**: This is the title of the course material. Enter the title as it appears on the course website or syllabus.
* **List of Course Materials**: Check the boxes of the course materials that you used during the course.
	+ "Yes" indicates that you used the course material.
	+ "No" indicates that you did not use the course material.
* **Key Takeaways**: Enter any important points or key takeaways that you gained from the course.
* **Key Takeaways Comments**: If you have any additional comments about the key takeaways, you can add them here.
* **Course Effectiveness**: Rate the effectiveness of the course material from 1 to 5.
* **Course Evaluation**: Select the course evaluation that best describes your experience with the course material.
	+ "Evaluation of Course Material" indicates that you found the course material to be useful and applicable.
	+ "Not Applicable" indicates that you did not find the course material to be useful.
	+ "Very Good", "Good", "Fair", or "Very Poor" indicate your level of satisfaction with the course material.
* **Date**: Enter the date you completed the course.
* **Time**: Enter the time it took you to complete the course (optional).
* **Email**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you for any follow-up questions or feedback.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments about your experience with the course.
* **Assessment Comments**: Add any additional comments about your assessment of the course material.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the course material from 1 to 5.
