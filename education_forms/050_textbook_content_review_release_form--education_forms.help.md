# Textbook Content Review Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to officially release a textbook for use in the school district after it has been evaluated and deemed suitable.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the evaluation options that apply to the textbook.
2. Choose the release option that best reflects the decision made during the evaluation process.
3. Add any comments about the textbook, if needed.
4. Enter the date the textbook was released.
5. Provide the name of the publisher.
6. Enter the school district name.
7. Enter the contact email address.
8. Enter the school phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Evaluation** (textbook_evaluation): 
  This field allows you to select the evaluation options that apply to the textbook. Select all the options that are relevant to the book.
* **Release** (release_decision): 
  This field is used to officially release the textbook for use in the school district. Choose the option that best reflects the decision made during the evaluation process.
* **Comments** (comments): 
  This field is for any additional comments you may have about the textbook.
* **Date Released** (date_released): 
  Enter the date the textbook was released for use in the school district.
* **Publisher Name** (publisher_name): 
  Enter the name of the textbook's publisher.
* **School District** (school_district): 
  Enter the name of the school district where the textbook will be used.
* **Email** (email): 
  Enter the contact email address of the person responsible for the textbook.
* **School Phone** (school_phone): 
  Enter the phone number of the school where the textbook will be used.
