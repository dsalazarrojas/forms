# Child Play Activity Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Child Play Activity Assessment form is designed to evaluate a child's play behavior and developmental skills during a play session. This form helps professionals in the education and healthcare fields to assess a child's progress and provide recommendations for future play activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Child Full Name: Enter the child's full name as it is written on their birth certificate or ID.
	* Observer Name: Enter your name and title (e.g., Educator, Therapist, etc.).
	* Observation Date: Enter the date of the play session being observed.
	* Qualitative Summary and Feedback: Provide a detailed description of the child's play session, highlighting notable events, behaviors, and skills observed.
2. Select the relevant options:
	* Type of Play Observed: Choose from the options provided (Solitary, Parallel, Associative, or Cooperative Play).
	* Level of Engagement: Select from the options provided (High Engagement, Moderate Engagement, Low Engagement, or Disinterested or Detached).
	* Cooperation with Peers: Choose how often the child demonstrates cooperation with peers (Always, Sometimes, or Rarely).
	* Verbal Communication: Select the frequency of speech observed during play (Frequent and Clear, Occasional Communication, or Minimal or Non-verbal).
3. (Optional) Select any relevant skills demonstrated by the child:
	* Fine Motor Control
	* Problem Solving and Logic
	* Imagination and Roleplay
	* Empathy and Caring
4. Finally, include any recommendations for future play activities.

* **Child Full Name (required)**: Enter the child's full name as it is written on their birth certificate or ID.

* **Level of Engagement (required)**: Select from the options provided (High Engagement, Moderate Engagement, Low Engagement, or Disinterested or Detached).
* **Cooperation with Peers (required)**: Choose how often the child demonstrates cooperation with peers (Always, Sometimes, or Rarely).
* **Verbal Communication (required)**: Select the frequency of speech observed during play (Frequent and Clear, Occasional Communication, or Minimal or Non-verbal).
* **Skills Demonstrated (optional)**: Select any relevant skills demonstrated by the child.
* **Qualitative Summary and Feedback (required)**: Provide a detailed description of the child's play session, highlighting notable events, behaviors, and skills observed.
* **Recommendations for Future Play Activities (optional)**: Include any suggestions for future play activities to support the child's growth.
