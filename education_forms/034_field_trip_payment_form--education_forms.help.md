# Field Trip Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Field Trip Payment Form is used to collect payment details for educational field trips organized by schools.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the payment details section (**Payment Details**) with the required information.
2. Select the payment method (**Payment Method**) that applies to your payment.
3. Provide the trip details (**Trip Details**) in the text field.
4. Select the trip dates (**Trip Dates**) for which you are making the payment.
5. Enter the payment amount (**Payment Amount**).
6. If necessary, select the payment status (**Payment Status**) and add any additional notes (**Notes**) for the payment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Payment Details
* **Payment Details (Payment Details, text, required):**
This field is used to collect the payment information for the field trip. Enter the details of the payment, such as the payment type and amount.

### Payment Method (Payment Method, select_one, optional)
* **Payment Method (Payment Method, select_one, optional):**
Select the payment method for the field trip, such as Cash, Credit, or Other.

### Trip Details (Trip Details, text, required)
* **Trip Details (Trip Details, text, required):**
Enter a brief description of the trip, including any relevant details or requirements.

### Trip Dates (Trip Dates, select_multiple, optional)
* **Trip Dates (Trip Dates, select_multiple, optional):**
Select the dates for which you are making the payment. If the payment is for multiple dates, select all applicable dates.

### Payment Amount (Payment Amount, number, required)
* **Payment Amount (Payment Amount, number, required):**
Enter the total payment amount for the field trip.

### Payment Status (Payment Status, select_one, optional)
* **Payment Status (Payment Status, select_one, optional):**
Select the payment status, such as Approved, Denied, or Pending.

### Notes (Notes, text, optional)
* **Notes (Notes, text, optional):**
Add any additional comments or notes about the payment for the field trip.
