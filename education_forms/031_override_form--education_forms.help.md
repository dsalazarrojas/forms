# override_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The override_form is used to change the status of a previously submitted form. This form allows users to override a form status, provide additional comments, and request a re-review of a form submission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the correct authorization to override a form submission.
2. Click on the "Override Form" button on the corresponding form submission page.
3. Fill out the form with the necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. form_title (text, required: false)
• form_title: Enter a brief title for the override request.

### 2. form_description (text, required: false)
• form_description: Provide a detailed description of the override request.

### 3. category (select_one, required: false)
• category: Select the category for the override request. Currently, only "Education Forms" is an option.

### 4. category_id (number, required: false)
• category_id: This field is currently not being used.

### 5. override_id (text, required: false)
• override_id: Enter the ID of the form to be overridden.

### 6. status (select_multiple, required: false)
• status: Choose the new status for the overridden form. Options are "pending", "approved", and "denied".

### 7. submitter (select_multiple, required: false)
• submitter: Select the role of the person who submitted the original form. Options are "Student", "Instructor", and "Other".

### 8. student_id (text, required: false)
• student_id: This field is currently not being used.

### 9. override_type (select_one, required: false)
• override_type: Choose the type of override request. Options are "academic" and "other".

### 10. start_date (date, required: false)
• start_date: Enter the date the form should start from.

### 11. start_time (time, required: false)
• start_time: Enter the time the form should start from.

### 12. end_date (date, required: false)
• end_date: Enter the date the form should end by.

### 13. end_time (time, required: false)
• end_time: Enter the time the form should end by.

### 14. reason (text, required: false)
• reason: Provide a brief explanation for the override request.

### 15. override_status (select_one, required: false)
• override_status: Choose the status of the overridden form. Options are "pending", "approved", and "denied".

### 16. comments (text, required: false)
• comments: Add any additional comments for the override request.

### 17. override_request (text, required: false)
• override_request: Enter the reason for the override request.

### 18. additional_comments (text, required: false)
• additional_comments: Provide any additional comments for the override request.

### 19. instructor_approval (text, required: false)
• instructor_approval: Enter the instructor's comments.

### 20. override_request_date (date, required: false)
• override_request_date: Enter the date of the override request.

### 21. override_request_time (time, required: false)
• override_request_time: Enter the time of the override request.

### 22. override_requester (text, required: false)
• override_requester: Enter the name of the person making the override request.

### 23. instructor_comments (text, required: false)
• instructor_comments: Enter any comments from the instructor.

### 24. student_email (email, required: false)
• student_email: Enter the email address of the student.

### 25. student_phone (text, required: false)
• student_phone: Enter the phone number of the student.
