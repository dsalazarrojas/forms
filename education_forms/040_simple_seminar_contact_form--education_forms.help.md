# Simple Seminar Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from seminar attendees. It gathers details about the attendee, such as their name and contact information, as well as details about the seminar they are interested in or attending.

## How To Complete This Form

### To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First name** and **Last name** in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide your **Email** address and **Phone** number for contact purposes.
3. Write a brief **Message** if you have any questions or need more information.
4. Select the **Event Date** for the seminar you are interested in.
5. Select the **Event Time** for the seminar you are interested in.
6. Choose the **Seminar Type** you are interested in (e.g., Seminar 1, Seminar 2, Seminar 3).
7. Indicate whether you are an **Attendee** or not.
8. Confirm if you are **Registered** for the seminar.
9. Enter the **Seminar Title** and **Description** for the seminar you are attending.
10. Specify the **Seminar Location**.
11. Enter the **Event Time 2** and **Event Date** for the seminar you are attending.
12. Choose the **Seminar Type 2** for the seminar you are attending.
13. Select if you are an **Attendee 2** or not.
14. Enter the **Seminar Title 2** and **Description 2** for the seminar you are attending.
15. Specify the **Seminar Location 2**.
16. Enter the **Event Time 2 2** for the seminar you are attending.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (Required, Text): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (Required, Text): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Required, Email): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Required, Text): Enter your phone number.
* **Message** (Optional, Text): Write a brief message.
* **Event Date** (Required, Date): Select the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (Required, Time): Select the time of the event.
* **Seminar Type** (Required, Select Multiple): Choose the seminar type you are interested in (e.g., Seminar 1, Seminar 2, Seminar 3).
* **Attendees** (Required, Select One): Indicate if you are an attendee or not.
* **Registered** (Required, Select One): Confirm if you are registered for the seminar.
* **Seminar Title** (Required, Text): Enter the title of the seminar you are attending.
* **Seminar Description** (Required, Text): Enter a brief description of the seminar.
* **Seminar Location** (Required, Text): Specify the location of the seminar.
* **Event Time 2** (Required, Time): Select the time of the second event.
* **Seminar Date** (Required, Date): Select the date of the second event.
* **Seminar Type 2** (Required, Select One): Choose the seminar type for the second event.
* **Attendees 2** (Required, Select One): Indicate if you are an attendee for the second event.
* **Seminar Title 2** (Required, Text): Enter the title of the second seminar.
* **Seminar Description 2** (Required, Text): Enter a brief description of the second seminar.
* **Seminar Location 2** (Required, Text): Specify the location of the second seminar.
* **Event Time 2 2** (Required, Time): Select the time of the second event.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, use the **Message** field to ask.
* For the **Seminar Type** field, choose the type that best fits your interest.
* For the **Attendees** and **Attendees 2** fields, indicate if you are attending the seminar.
* For the **Registered** and **Registered 2** fields, confirm your attendance status.
* For the **Event Date**, **Event Time**, **Event Time 2**, and **Event Date** fields, select the correct dates and times.
* For the **Seminar Location**, **Seminar Location 2**, **Seminar Title**, and **Seminar Title 2** fields, enter the correct information.
