# School Intent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Intent Form is a crucial document used to capture the purpose and goals of a school or educational institution. It helps us understand the number of students, adults, and contact details for the proposed school or educational institution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Ensure you only fill out this form when you have a genuine intention to establish a new school or expand an existing one.
2. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
3. Provide all required information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Intent Form** (`title`): This is the main title of the form. Please enter the name of your school or educational institution.
* **Number of students anticipated** (`number_of_students_anticipated`): This field captures the number of students you anticipate enrolling in the school or institution.
* **Date of intent** (`date_of_intent`): This field is used to record the date when you plan to start or expand your school or institution.
* **Expected Number of Adults** (`expected_number_of_adults`): This field captures the number of adults (e.g., teachers, staff, or family members) associated with the school or institution.
* **Expected Number of Children** (`expected_number_of_children`): This field records the number of children associated with the school or institution.
* **Expected Number of Students** (`expected_number_of_students`): This field captures the total number of students expected to be enrolled in the school or institution.
* **Email** (`email`): This field is used to capture the contact email of the person filling out the form.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`): This field is used to capture the contact phone number of the person filling out the form.

## Tips
* Please ensure you only fill out this form when you have a genuine intention to establish a new school or expand an existing one.
* Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to help us better understand your intentions.
