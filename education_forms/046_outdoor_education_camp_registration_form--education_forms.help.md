# outdoor_education_camp_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for registration to an outdoor education camp. You will be asked to provide personal, guardian, and camper information, as well as emergency contact and medical information. You will also be able to select activities you are interested in participating in and choose a date for your activity. Finally, you will be required to provide payment information to secure your spot.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Provide your personal information.
2. Enter your guardian's information.
3. Enter your camper's information.
4. Provide any necessary emergency contact information.
5. List any relevant emergency medical information.
6. Select the activities you are interested in participating in.
7. Choose a date for your activity.
8. Enter your payment information to secure your spot.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Information** (Personal Information, text, required/optional): Please enter your personal details, including your name, address, and any other relevant information.
* **Guardian Information** (Guardian Information, text, required/optional): If you are under 18, please enter your guardian's information so we can contact them in case of an emergency.
* **Camper Information** (Camper Information, text, required/optional): Enter your camper's name, age, and any other relevant information.
* **Emergency Contact Information** (Emergency Contact Information, text, required/optional): Please enter the contact information for an emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Medical Information** (Emergency Medical Information, text, required/optional): Please provide any medical conditions or allergies that we should be aware of.
* **Activity Selection** (Activity Selection, select multiple, required/optional): Select all the activities you are interested in participating in, such as hiking, kayaking, swimming, and rock climbing.
* **Activity Date** (Activity Date, date, required/optional): Choose a date for your activity that you would like to participate in.
* **Payment Information** (Payment Information, text, required/optional): Enter your payment information to secure your spot.
* **Confirm Registration** (Confirm Registration, text, required/optional): Double-check your information to ensure it is accurate and complete before submitting your registration.
