# Ferpa Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ferpa Consent Form is used to obtain consent from students for educational activities or research projects that involve their personally identifiable information or data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the category that best describes the purpose of the form (Education Forms or Consent Forms).
2. Enter a brief description of the form's purpose (e.g., "Student data collection for research").
3. Provide a detailed description of the form (e.g., "This form is used to gather information about student demographics and academic performance for a study on educational outcomes").

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **ferpa_consent_form** (Title): Enter a title that clearly describes the form's purpose (e.g., "Student Data Consent Form for Research").
  - **Category** (Select multiple): Choose the category that best describes the form (Education Forms or Consent Forms).
  - **description** (Text): Enter a brief description of the form's purpose (e.g., "Student data collection for research").
  - **description2** (Text): Provide a detailed description of the form (e.g., "This form is used to gather information about student demographics and academic performance for a study on educational outcomes").
  - **Tips**: Be concise and clear in your descriptions, as they will be used to help understand the form's purpose and context.
