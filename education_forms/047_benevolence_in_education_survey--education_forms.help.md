# Benevolence In Education Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the respondent's experiences with kindness, empathy, and support within the school environment. It is meant to be completed by individuals who have had experiences within the school and is intended to provide insight into areas that may need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and date of the survey.
2. Select the role you represent within the school (Student, Teacher, Administrator, Parent, Staff, or Other).
3. Answer each question honestly, selecting the option that best reflects your perception of the school environment.
4. Provide examples of benevolence you have witnessed, if desired.
5. Suggest areas for improvement for the school, if desired.
6. Add any additional comments or suggestions.
