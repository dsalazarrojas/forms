# drama_academy_class_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students for registration in a drama academy class.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your Student Name and select "Drama", "Dance", "Music", or "Art" from the Class dropdown list.
2. Enter your Email and Phone Number (if applicable).
3. Choose your preferred Class Day and Class Start Date (if applicable).
4. Select your preferred Class Time.
5. Enter your Address (if applicable) and Parent Name (if applicable).
6. Click the Submit button to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (Student Name, text, required: false): Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification document, such as a passport or driver's license.
* **Email** (Email, email, required: false): Enter your official email address associated with your student identification or your personal email address.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, required: false): Enter your mobile or landline phone number to enable us to contact you.
* **Birthdate** (Birthdate, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format dd-mm-yyyy.
* **Class** (Class, select_one, required: false): Select your preferred class type from the list: Drama, Dance, Music, or Art.
* **Class Time** (Class Time, time, required: false): Select your preferred class time from the available options.
* **Class Day** (Class Day, select_multiple, required: false): Choose multiple days you are available for the class from the list: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, or Saturday.
* **Class Start Date** (Class Start Date, date, required: false): Enter the start date of your class. 
* **Address** (Address, text, required: false): Enter your home or school address for our records.
* **Parent Name** (Parent Name, text, required: false): Enter the name of your parent or guardian for our records.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact our support team for assistance.
* This form is for registration purposes only, and all fields are necessary for us to process your registration correctly.
