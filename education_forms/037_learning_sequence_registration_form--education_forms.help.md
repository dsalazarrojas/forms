# <string> - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect demographic and enrollment information from students or their parents/guardians for educational institutions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start, ensure you have all necessary information and documents ready, including student identification numbers and contact details.
2. Review each field carefully and fill it in accordingly:
   * **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter the student's first name.
   * **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter the student's last name.
   * **Email** (`email`, `text`, required): Enter the student's email address.
   * **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `text`, required): Enter the student's date of birth.
   * **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, `text`, required): Enter the student's grade level.
   * **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the school the student attends.
   * **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, required): Enter the parent's/guardian's name.
   * **Student ID Number** (`student_id_number`, `text`, required): Enter the student's identification number.
   * **Contact** (`contact`, `text`, required): Enter the student's contact details.
   * **Course ID** (`course_id`, `text`, required): Enter the course ID number.
   * **Course Status** (`course_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose from the options "In Progress," "Completed," or "Cancelled" to indicate the course status.
   * **School Status** (`school_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose from the options "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate the school status.
   * **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, `text`, required): Enter the date of registration.
   * **Student Type** (`student_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose from the options "Part-time" or "Full-time" to indicate the student's enrollment type.
   * **Last Attended School** (`last_attended_school`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the school the student last attended.
   * **Last Attended School Status** (`last_attended_school_status`, `text`, required): Enter the status of the school the student last attended.
   * **Last Attended School Time** (`last_attended_school_time`, `text`, required): Enter the time spent at the last attended school.
   * **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter the student's home address.
   * **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter the student's contact phone number.
   * **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, `text`, required): Enter the parent's/guardian's email address.
   * **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the parent's/guardian's phone number.
   * **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required): Enter the emergency contact details.
