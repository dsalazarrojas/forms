# Academic Peer Exchange Recording Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Peer Exchange Recording Consent Form is used to obtain consent from participants to record and share their contributions during academic peer exchange sessions. This form ensures that all participants are aware of the recording and sharing policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. **Fill out your name as the participant**: This will be your name as displayed in the recording and shared materials.
2. **Enter the session title**: Briefly describe the topic of the session being recorded.
3. **Consent to record**: Indicate whether you agree to be recorded during the session.
4. **Permission to share**: Specify if you allow your recording to be shared publicly or only within the group.
5. **Retention period**: Choose how long your recording will be kept on file.
6. **Specific restrictions**: If there are any specific limitations on how your recording can be used, please list them here.
7. **Signature**: Sign your full name to confirm your consent.
8. **Date of consent**: Date and time you provide consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as you would like it displayed in the recording and shared materials.
  *Example: John Doe*
- **Session title** (`session_title`, text, optional): Briefly describe the topic of the session being recorded.
  *Example: Peer Exchange Session 1*
- **Consent to record** (`consent_to_record`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you agree to be recorded during the session. 
  *Select 'True' to consent to recording*
- **Permission to share** (`permission_to_share`, select_one, required): Specify if you allow your recording to be shared publicly or only within the group.
  *Select 'Yes publicly' to share with public or 'Yes internally only' to share within group*
- **Retention period** (`retention_period`, select_one, optional): Choose how long your recording will be kept on file. 
  *Select '6 months' or '1 year' or 'Indefinitely'*
- **Specific restrictions** (`restrictions`, text, optional): If there are any specific limitations on how your recording can be used, list them here.
  *Examples: Only use for academic purposes, do not share with external parties*
- **Signature name** (`signature`, text, required): Sign your full name to confirm your consent.
  *Type your full name: John Doe*
- **Date of consent** (`date`, date, required): Date and time you provide consent.
  *Format: YYYY-MM-DD*
