# Student Council Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Council Registration Form is designed to collect relevant information from students to facilitate their participation in council activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required and optional fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Ensure all fields are filled out, as some fields are mandatory for a complete registration.
3. Review your submission before submitting it to ensure it is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (Text, Required): This field is used to capture the student's name. Please enter your full name, including your first name and last name.
* **School** (Text, Required): This field collects the name of the student's school or institution they are currently attending.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address for contact and communication purposes with the Student Council.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): If you have a phone number, please enter it for easier contact and communication.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Required): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Time of Birth** (Time, Required): Enter your time of birth in the format hh:mm.
* **Select One** (Select One, Optional): Choose one option that best represents your interest or preference. The options are:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Select Multiple** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose one or more options that best represent your interests or preferences. The options are:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Note** (Note, Optional): This field is for any additional comments or information you would like to share with the Student Council.
* **Student Name (Alias)** (Text, Optional): This field is for an alternate name format, if you prefer. (e.g., "John D.")
* **Student Name (Last)** (Text, Optional): This field is for your last name only.
* **Student Name (First)** (Text, Optional): This field is for your first name only.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and truthfully.
* Use the correct format for date and time fields (mm/dd/yyyy and hh:mm).
* If you need assistance or have questions, reach out to the Student Council staff for support.
