# High School Senior Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The High School Senior Survey is a form designed to collect information about high school students' experiences, plans, and perceptions. This form helps educators and administrators understand the student population's background, interests, and needs, informing decisions on resource allocation and support services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully to ensure you understand the questions and answers provided.
2. Complete each page, answering all questions thoroughly and accurately.
3. Use the provided options or text fields to answer each question.
4. Do not submit the form until you have reviewed and agreed to the submitted information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **High School Background** (`high_school_background`, `text`, required: false): This field is where you can provide any relevant information about your high school experience, such as notable achievements, accomplishments, or challenges you've faced.
* **Graduation Plans** (`graduation_plans`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select one of the following options that best describes your future plans after graduation:
	+ College
	+ University
	+ Community College
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Favorite Subjects** (`favorite_subjects`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the subjects that you enjoy most or are passionate about:
	+ Math
	+ Science
	+ English
	+ History
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Teacher Quality** (`teacher_quality`, `number`, required: false): Rate your teachers' quality on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being low and 10 being high. This will help us understand your perception of your teachers' effectiveness.
* **School Climate** (`school_climate`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select one of the following options that best describes your school climate:
	+ Positive
	+ Negative
	+ Neutral
* **Student Life** (`student_life`, `text`, required: false): Describe your overall student experience, including any positive or negative aspects of your life at school.
* **Graduation Commitment** (`graduation_commitment`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select one of the following options that best describes your level of commitment to graduating:
	+ Strong Commitment
	+ Somewhat Commitment
	+ No Commitment
* **Student Leadership** (`student_leadership`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check any leadership roles or activities you've been involved in:
	+ Class Officer
	+ School Organization
	+ Student Club
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Favorite Activities** (`favorite_activities`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the activities that you enjoy most:
	+ Sports
	+ Clubs
	+ Organizations
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Student Satisfaction** (`student_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select one of the following options that best describes your overall level of satisfaction with your student experience:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Not Satisfied

Note: This guide is for reference only and may be subject to changes based on your school's specific needs and policies.
