# Reaction Assignment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Reaction Assignment Form" is designed to gather participant information, assign tasks, and confirm participation in educational settings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Please provide your participant information**: Fill in the "Participant Info" section with your details.
2. **Select tasks for the participant**: Choose the tasks that the participant has been assigned to.
3. **Confirm participation**: Enter a confirmation message to confirm participation.
4. **Provide task details**: Enter any additional information about the task.
5. **Another confirmation**: Enter another confirmation message to confirm participation.
