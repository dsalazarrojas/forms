# Senior High School Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Senior High School Application Form is designed to collect information from students applying to a specific school. This form gathers data about the student's name, address, grade level, date of birth, expected graduation date, contact information, school choice, application type, and other relevant details. The form is used to streamline the application process and ensure accurate data collection.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the student's name in the "student_name" field.
2. Enter the student's address in the "student_address" field.
3. Select the student's grade level from the options provided in the "student_grade_level" field.
4. Enter the student's date of birth in the "date_of_birth" field in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
5. Enter the expected graduation date in the "expected_graduation_date" field in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
6. Fill in the student's phone number in the "student_phone" field.
7. Enter the student's email address in the "student_email" field.
8. Select the preferred school from the options provided in the "school_choice" field.
9. Choose the application type from the options provided in the "application_type" field.
10. Add any uploaded documents in the "uploaded_documents" field.
11. List any uploaded documents in the "uploaded_documents_list" field.
12. Enter the submitter's name in the "submitter" field.
13. Enter the submitter's title in the "submitter_title" field.
14. Enter the date and time of submission in the "submitted" field in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
15. Enter the name of the user who submitted the form in the "submitted_by_user" field.
16. Enter the title of the user who submitted the form in the "submitted_by_user_title" field.
17. Enter the date of submission in the "submitted_date" field in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
18. Enter the time of submission in the "submitted_time" field in the format HH:MM.
19. Fill in the name of the school in the "school_name" field.
20. Enter the address of the school in the "school_address" field.
21. Enter the phone number of the school in the "school_phone" field.
22. Enter the email address of the school in the "school_email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Enter the student's full name.
* **Student Address**: Enter the student's address.
* **Grade Level**: Select the student's grade level from the options provided.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the student's date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Expected Graduation Date**: Enter the expected graduation date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Student Phone**: Enter the student's phone number.
* **Student Email**: Enter the student's email address.
* **School Choice**: Select the preferred school from the options provided.
* **Application Type**: Choose the type of application from the options provided.
* **Uploaded Documents**: Upload any supporting documents required for the application.
* **List of Uploaded Documents**: List the documents uploaded, one per line.
* **Uploaded Documents Label**: Enter a label for the uploaded documents list.
* **Submitter**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Title**: Enter the title of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted**: Enter the date and time of submission in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Submitted By User**: Enter the name of the user who submitted the form.
* **Submitted By User Title**: Enter the title of the user who submitted the form.
* **Submitted Date**: Enter the date of submission in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Submitted Time**: Enter the time of submission in the format HH:MM.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of the school.
* **School Address**: Enter the address of the school.
* **School Phone**: Enter the phone number of the school.
* **School Email**: Enter the email address of the school.
