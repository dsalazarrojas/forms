# Digital Teaching Story Submission Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed information about digital teaching stories submitted by educators. It is intended to help create a centralized repository of these submissions for review, assessment, and publication purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out each field accurately and thoroughly. Each field is described in detail below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submission Registration Form** (`submission_registration_form`, text, not required): A brief title to describe the digital teaching story.
* **Educator Name** (`educator_name`, text, not required): The name of the educator who submitted the digital teaching story.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): The email address of the educator who submitted the digital teaching story.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, not required): The contact number of the educator who submitted the digital teaching story.
* **Institution** (`institution`, text, not required): The name of the institution where the digital teaching story was created.
* **Department** (`department`, text, not required): The name of the department where the digital teaching story was created.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, not required): The job title of the educator who submitted the digital teaching story.
* **Submission Type** (`submission_type`, select_one, not required): The type of submission, which can be 'Teaching Story', 'Other', 'Workshop/Project', etc.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, not required): The date when the digital teaching story was submitted.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, not required): The time when the digital teaching story was submitted.
* **Submission Note** (`submission_note`, note, not required): Any additional notes or comments about the submission.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_one, not required): The current status of the submission, which can be 'Draft', 'Submitted', 'Approved', 'Rejected', 'Published', etc.
* **Submission Comments** (`submission_comments`, text, not required): Any comments or feedback about the submission.
* **Institution Name** (`institution_name`, text, not required): The name of the institution where the digital teaching story was created.
* **Department Name** (`department_name`, text, not required): The name of the department where the digital teaching story was created.
* **Educator ID** (`educator_id`, text, not required): The ID of the educator who submitted the digital teaching story.
* **Submission URL** (`submission_url`, text, not required): The URL of the digital teaching story submission.
* **Educator Email** (`educator_email`, email, not required): The email address of the educator who submitted the digital teaching story.
* **Institution Address** (`institution_address`, text, not required): The address of the institution where the digital teaching story was created.
* **Department Address** (`department_address`, text, not required): The address of the department where the digital teaching story was created.
* **Job Title 2** (`job_title_2`, text, not required): The job title of the educator who submitted the digital teaching story (optional secondary job title).
* **Submission Date 2** (`submission_date_2`, date, not required): The date when the digital teaching story was submitted (optional secondary submission date).
* **Submission Time 2** (`submission_time_2`, time, not required): The time when the digital teaching story was submitted (optional secondary submission time).
* **Submission Status 2** (`submission_status_2`, select_one, not required): The current status of the second submission, which can be 'Draft', 'Submitted', 'Approved', 'Rejected', 'Published', etc.
* **Submission Comments 2** (`submission_comments_2`, text, not required): Any comments or feedback about the second submission.

Note: Each field is optional unless specified as 'required' in the form definition.
