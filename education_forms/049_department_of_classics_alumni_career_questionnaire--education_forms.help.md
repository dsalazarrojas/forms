# Department Of Classics Alumni Career Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the career paths of Department of Classics alumni. This will help us track the career outcomes of our graduates and provide valuable insights for future students and alumni.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with your information. You will need to provide the following information:
* Your first name
* Your last name
* Your email address
* Your phone number
* The department you graduated from
* Your current job title
* A brief description of your career
* The start and end dates of your career
* Any career achievements (awards, grants, publications, or patents)
* Your current job title and company
* Your current work location
* Whether you are currently employed
* The type of job you have (full-time, part-time, freelance, or intern)
* The status of your work (working, retired, looking for work, or on leave)
* The hours you work
* The current status of your job (working or not working)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Please enter your phone number.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required/optional): Please select the department you graduated from.
	+ Department of Art
	+ Department of Classics
	+ Department of Engineering
	+ Department of Music
* **Current Job Title** (`current_job`, select_multiple, required/optional): Please select the current job title that best describes your job.
	+ Software Engineer
	+ Data Scientist
	+ Artist
	+ Teacher
* **Graduation Year** (`graduation_year`, number, required/optional): Please enter the year you graduated.
* **Career Description** (`career_description`, text, required/optional): Please describe your career in a few words.
* **Start Date** (`career_start_date`, date, required/optional): Please enter the start date of your career.
* **End Date** (`career_end_date`, date, required/optional): Please enter the end date of your career.
* **Career Achievement** (`career_achievement`, select_one, required/optional): Please select any career achievements you have, such as awards or grants.
	+ Award
	+ Grant
	+ Publication
	+ Patent
* **Current Job Title** (`current_job_title`, text, required/optional): Please enter your current job title.
* **Current Company** (`current_company`, text, required/optional): Please enter the company you work for.
* **Education** (`career_education`, text, required/optional): Please describe your educational background.
* **Current Location** (`career_location`, text, required/optional): Please enter your current location.
* **Short Career Description** (`career_description_short`, text, required/optional): Please enter a short description of your career in a few words.
* **Start Date** (`career_start_date`, date, required/optional): Please enter the start date of your career.
* **End Date** (`career_end_date`, date, required/optional): Please enter the end date of your career.
* **Currently Employed** (`career_current_employment`, select_one, required/optional): Please select whether you are currently employed.
	+ True
	+ False
* **Job Type** (`career_current_job_type`, select_one, required/optional): Please select the type of job you have.
	+ Full-time
	+ Part-time
	+ Freelance
	+ Intern
* **Work Status** (`career_current_work_status`, select_one, required/optional): Please select the status of your work.
	+ Working
	+ Retired
	+ Looking for work
	+ On leave
* **Work Hours** (`career_current_work_hours`, time, required/optional): Please enter the hours you work.
* **Work Location** (`career_current_work_location`, text, required/optional): Please enter your current work location.
* **Current Job Type** (`career_current_job_type`, select_one, required/optional): Please select the type of job you have.
	+ Working
	+ Not working
* **Current Company** (`career_current_company`, text, required/optional): Please enter the company you work for.

## Tips
Please fill out the form as thoroughly as possible to provide accurate information about your career path. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
