# Orientation Information Collection - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect orientation information from attendees, which helps us tailor the session to best fit their preferences and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through the form carefully and answer all the questions that apply to you.
2. For each question, provide accurate and complete information.
3. Submit the form once you have finished answering all the questions.

* **orientation_details** (`orientation_details`, text, optional): Provide any special details about your orientation, such as any medical or physical conditions that may impact your participation.

* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you in case of any updates or changes.

* **time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time you plan to attend the session.
