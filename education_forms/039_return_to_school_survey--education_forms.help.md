# Return To School Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Return To School Survey is a form designed to gather information from parents or guardians about their child's educational status. It helps schools understand the reasons behind student absences and tailor their support and resources accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely, making sure to answer all required fields.
2. If you are unsure about any field, please contact your school or institution for guidance.
3. Please answer truthfully, as this information will help the school provide better support to your child.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as the parent or guardian of the student.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address for contact purposes. This will allow the school to reach out to you for any follow-up questions or concerns.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of your child's current school.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Please enter your child's full name.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_multiple, true): Please select the grade level your child was at when they left school:
	+ 1st Grade
	+ 2nd Grade
	+ 3rd Grade
* **Reason for Not Returning to School** (`reason_leaving`, select_one, true): Please select the reason why your child is not returning to school:
	+ Not Returning
	+ COVID-19 Concerns
	+ Health Concerns
* **Will the Student Return to School?** (`school_returning`, select_one, true): Please indicate whether your child will return to school or not:
	+ False
	+ True
	+ Maybe

## Tips

- Please answer truthfully and provide as much detail as possible.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact your school or institution for guidance.
- Remember to review and submit the form completely before hitting the submit button.
