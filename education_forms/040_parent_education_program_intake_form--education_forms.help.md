# Parent Education Program Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parent Education Program Intake Form is used to gather information from parents about their child's education program needs. This form helps us understand the family's situation and provide more tailored support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name and phone number.
2. Select all the programs that interest you and your child.
3. Provide the name of the school of interest (if applicable).
4. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Contact Information**: This section is used to gather your contact information, including your name and phone number. Please fill in this section accurately so we can get in touch with you.
* **Parent Name**: This field is used to collect your name as a parent.
* **Phone Number**: This field is used to collect your phone number, which we may use to contact you to discuss your child's program needs.
* **Programs of Interest**: In this field, please select all the programs that interest you and your child. This will help us tailor the support we provide to your family's specific needs.
* **School of Interest**: If your child is or will be attending a school, please fill in this field with the name of the school.

## Tips
* Please fill in all the required fields (though some fields are not required, filling them out will help us provide better support).
* If you have any questions or concerns while filling out this form, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will only create one instance of this form per family and update it as needed. If you have any changes, please let us know!
