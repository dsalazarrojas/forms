# Student Daily Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Daily Feedback Form is a tool used by educators to provide constructive feedback to students on their performance, behavior, or progress in a specific subject or project. This form helps gather information and track student engagement, understanding, or overall experience in the class or educational setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select one or more of the feedback options (very good, good, fair, or poor) in the "Feedback Form" section.
2. Enter the subject of the feedback in the "Subject" field.
3. Provide additional comments in the "Comments" field, if any.
4. Choose the type of feedback you are providing (Meeting, Lecture, Assignment, or Project) in the "Feedback Type" field.
5. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
6. Optionally, enter the student's ID number in the "Student ID" field.
7. Enter the educator's name in the "Educator Name" field.
8. If applicable, enter the educator's ID number in the "Educator ID" field.
9. Finally, select a date and time in the "Date" and "Time" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback Form** (`student_feedback`, select_multiple): Choose one or more of the options to rate the student's performance or behavior. This helps provide constructive feedback to the student.
* **Subject** (`subject`, text): Enter a brief description of the feedback topic or subject matter.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note): Provide additional information or context about the feedback, if necessary.
* **Date** (`date`, date): Enter the date on which the feedback is provided.
* **Time** (`time`, time): Enter the time at which the feedback is provided.
* **Feedback Type** (`feedback_type`, select_one): Choose the type of feedback (Meeting, Lecture, Assignment, or Project) to categorize and track feedback for the specific subject or project.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text): Enter the student's full name.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number): Enter the student's ID number, if applicable.
* **Educator Name** (`educator_name`, text): Enter your name or the name of the educator providing feedback.
* **Educator ID** (`educator_id`, number): Enter your ID number, if applicable.
