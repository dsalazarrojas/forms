# Online_Learning_Parental_Consent_Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather essential information from parents to facilitate online learning programs. It's likely used for initial or updated consents for their child's online learning activities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure your child's online learning information is up-to-date.
2. Enter your name and contact information in the fields provided, ensuring you can be reached regarding your child's online learning activities.
3. Confirm your consent for your child to participate in online learning programs.
4. Provide your child's details, including their name, email (if applicable), and other relevant information.
5. Complete the school-specific fields with your school's name, address, district, city, state, zip, and country details (if applicable).
6. Fill out your teacher's information, including their name, phone number (if applicable), and email (if applicable).
7. Optionally, provide your child's grade level, class, and other relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent_Name** (Text, Required): Enter your name as a parent or guardian.
* **Contact_Number** (Text, Required): Enter your contact number to be reachable for questions or updates about your child's online activities.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address to receive updates and information about your child's online learning activities.
* **Consen** (Select Multiple, Required): Confirm that you consent to your child participating in online learning activities.
* **Date_of_Birth** (Date, Optional): Enter your child's date of birth, if applicable.
* **Student_Name** (Text, Required): Enter your child's name.
* **Student_Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your child's email address, if applicable.
* **Student_Contact_Number** (Text, Optional): Enter your child's contact number, if applicable.
* **School_Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your school's name.
* **School_Address** (Text, Optional): Enter your school's address.
* **Teacher_Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your teacher's name.
* **Teacher_Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your teacher's phone number, if applicable.
* **Teacher_Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your teacher's email address, if applicable.
* **School_Website** (Text, Optional): Enter your school's website.
* **School_District** (Text, Optional): Enter your school's district.
* **School_City** (Text, Optional): Enter your school's city.
* **School_State** (Text, Optional): Enter your school's state.
* **School_Zip** (Text, Optional): Enter your school's zip code.
* **School_Country** (Text, Optional): Enter your school's country.
* **Student_Grade** (Text, Optional): Enter your child's grade level, if applicable.
* **Student_Grade_Level** (Text, Optional): Enter your child's grade level, if applicable.
* **Student_Class** (Text, Optional): Enter your child's class, if applicable.
* **Teacher_Title** (Text, Optional): Enter your teacher's title.
* **Signature** (Note, Optional): Enter your digital signature, if required.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information provided for your child and school.
