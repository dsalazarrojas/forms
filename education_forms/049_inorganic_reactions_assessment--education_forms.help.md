# Inorganic Reactions Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess students' understanding of inorganic reactions, a fundamental concept in chemistry. It consists of a series of questions that cater to various aspects of inorganic reactions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by reading each question carefully and thoroughly.
2. Select the correct answer or provide the required information for each question.
3. If a question has a date or time format, enter the correct date or time in the respective field.
4. For text fields, enter your thoughts or answers in the space provided.
5. Ensure you complete all required questions, indicated by an asterisk (\*) before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Page 1): This field is a text box where you can provide any additional information or context about yourself or the assessment.
* **Student Name** (Page 2): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Teacher** (Page 3): Enter the name of your instructor or teacher.
* **Question 1** (Page 4): Select the correct answer for this question from the options provided (A, B, C, D).
* **Question 2** (Page 5): Select all applicable answers for this question from the options provided (A, B, C, D).
* **Question 3** (Page 6): Enter the date in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Question 4** (Page 7): Enter the time in the 24-hour format (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Question 5** (Page 8): Provide any additional comments or notes about the assessment.
* **Question 6** (Page 9): Enter your email address where you can be contacted for further information.
* **Question 7** (Page 10): Enter your thoughts or answers in this text field.
* **Confidence** (Page 11): Enter a number value indicating your confidence level in the assessment (0-100).
* **Explanation** (Page 12): Enter a brief explanation of your answers or thoughts.
* **Assessment Notes** (Page 13): Enter any additional comments or observations about the assessment.

## Tips

* Please take your time to answer each question carefully and thoroughly.
* If you're unsure or need clarification about any question, consult with your instructor or teacher.
* Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the assessment.
* Review your answers before submission for accuracy and completeness.
