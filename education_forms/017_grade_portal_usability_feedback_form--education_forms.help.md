# Grade Portal Usability Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about the usability of the Grade Portal. Your input will help us identify areas of improvement and provide a better experience for our users.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide feedback, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your **First Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the second field. This is a required field, so please provide your full last name.
3. If you're providing feedback on behalf of an institution, please enter the name of the institution in the **Institution** field. This is not required, so you can leave this field blank if applicable.
4. Enter your valid **Email** in the fourth field. This will help us contact you if we need more information.
5. In the **Feedback** field, please provide your thoughts about the usability of the Grade Portal. Be as detailed as possible to help us improve.
6. If you'd like to add any additional comments, you can do so in the **Comments** field. This is not required, so feel free to leave it blank.
7. Choose a rating for your experience with the Grade Portal in the **Rating** field. You can select from **Very Good**, **Good**, **Fair**, or **Poor**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (First Name)** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name (Last Name)** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full last name in this field. This is a required field.
* **Institution (Institution)** (`institution`, `text`, optional): If you're providing feedback on behalf of an institution, enter its name here. This is not required.
* **Email (Email)** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you if needed.
* **Feedback (Feedback)** (`feedback`, `text`, required): Share your thoughts about the usability of the Grade Portal. Be as detailed as possible.
* **Comments (Comments)** (`comments`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments here. This field is not required.
* **Rating (Rating)** (`rating`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you rate your experience with the Grade Portal from **Very Good**, **Good**, **Fair**, or **Poor**.
* **Submit**: Click this button to send your feedback to us.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when providing feedback.
* Use a valid email address to ensure we can contact you if needed.
* Choose a rating that accurately reflects your experience with the Grade Portal.
