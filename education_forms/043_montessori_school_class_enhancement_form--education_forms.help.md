# montessori_school_class_enhancement_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about the enhancement needs and characteristics of Montessori schools.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each field description carefully to understand what information is being requested.
- Fill in the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
- If you are unsure about any field, refer to the "Field-by-Field Explanation" section below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **classroom_environment**: Enter a brief description of the current classroom environment (e.g., quiet, noisy, well-organized, etc.).
* **teacher_availability**: Enter the number of teachers available to provide support to students.
* **student_participation**: Indicate whether students are participating in class activities (Yes/No).
* **classroom_equipment**: Select whether the classroom has the necessary equipment for activities (Yes/No).
* **student_feedback**: Enter any feedback or comments from students about their experience in the class.
* **teacher_support**: Enter any feedback or comments from teachers about their support to students.
* **class_scheduling**: Enter the class schedule, including the start and end times, and any breaks.
* **student_interests**: Select the area of interest of the students (math, science, language, art, music).
* **staff_support**: Enter the email address of the staff member providing support.
* **parent_involvement**: Enter the number of parents involved in the class.
* **teacher_training**: Indicate whether teachers have received training on Montessori methods (Yes/No).
* **class_size**: Enter the number of students in the class.
* **student_needs**: Select any special needs of the students (special needs, other).
* **student_attendance**: Enter the date of the last student attendance.
* **class_safety**: Enter any safety concerns or incidents in the class.
* **student_conflict**: Indicate whether there have been any conflicts among students (Yes/No).
* **teacher_support_system**: Select the teacher support system used in the class (teacher support system, other).
* **parent_participation**: Enter the number of parents participating in the class.
* **class_environment**: Enter any additional information about the class environment.
* **class_resources**: Indicate whether the class has access to necessary resources (Yes/No).
* **class_goals**: Select the goals of the class (class goals, other).
* **teacher_training_support**: Enter any comments or feedback from teachers about their training support.
* **parent_participation_system**: Indicate whether parents are participating in a participation system (Yes/No).
* **parent_needs**: Indicate whether parents have any needs or concerns (Yes/No).
* **parent_needs_support**: Indicate whether parents receive support for their needs (Yes/No).

Note that some fields are optional and can be left blank if not applicable. 

This guide is designed to provide you with the information you need to complete the **montessori_school_class_enhancement_form** form accurately and effectively. If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out.
