# Discipline and Guidance Policy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Discipline and Guidance Policy Survey is used to collect information from school staff members about their awareness and perception of the school's discipline and guidance policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your official email address.
* **Position**: Select your position in the school (Administrator, Teacher, Student, Parent, or Staff).
* **Department**: Select your department (Administration, Teaching, Non-Teaching, Support).
* **Awareness Level**: Answer a number question about your level of awareness regarding the discipline and guidance policies.
* **Policy Effectiveness**: Answer another number question about the policy's effectiveness.
* **Improvement Ideas**: Provide any improvement ideas you may have.
* **Comments**: Add any comments or feedback you may have.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure that your submission is complete and accurate.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, please don't hesitate to reach out to your school's administration.
