# School Clothing Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Clothing Application Form is a form used by students and their parents or guardians to apply for school clothing. It captures essential information about the student and their parent or guardian, such as their names, dates of birth, contact numbers, and clothing requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
2. Ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
Each field is explained below:

* **Name (4)**: Please enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date of Birth (5)**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address (6)**: Enter your permanent address where you can be contacted.
* **Parent or Guardian Name (7)**: Enter the name of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Date of Birth (8)**: Enter your parent or guardian's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Relationship (9)**: Select "Parent" or "Guardian" to indicate your relationship with the student.
* **Student's School Attended (10)**: Select the type of school the student attends (Public, Private, or Other).
* **Level of Education (11)**: Select the student's current level of education (Primary 1-5, High School, or College).
* **Grade Level (12)**: Select the student's current grade level (Grade 1-10).
* **Contact Number (13)**: Enter your contact number where you can be reached.
* **Parent or Guardian Contact Number (14)**: Enter the contact number of your parent or guardian.
* **Total Allowance (15)**: Enter the total allowance you are eligible for.
* **Amount Requested (16)**: Enter the amount of clothing you are requesting.
* **Student Signature (17)**: Sign your name to confirm that you have filled in the form accurately.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature (18)**: Sign your name as a parent or guardian to confirm that the information is accurate.
* **Date (19)**: Enter the date the form was completed.

Note: Please review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors. If you have any issues or concerns, please contact the school administration.
