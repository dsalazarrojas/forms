# dietary_preferences_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dietary Preferences Survey Template is a form used to collect information about an individual's eating habits, dietary restrictions, and meal planning preferences. This form is designed to help you understand an individual's dietary needs and preferences, and can be used in various settings, such as healthcare, nutrition counseling, or social services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by reading the form's introduction, which provides context and background information.
2. Answer the questions on the "Eating Habits" page, which will help you understand your current eating habits.
3. On the "Restrictions" page, select the types of restrictions you have, such as food allergies or intolerances.
4. On the "Meal Planning" page, select how often you eat in a day, such as 1-2 meals or 5 or more meals.
5. On the "Meal Frequency" page, select the times of day you usually eat, such as breakfast, lunch, or dinner.
6. On the "Time of Day" page, select the time of day when you usually eat your meals.
7. On the "Frequency of Meals" page, select how often you eat meals in a day, such as daily or occasionally.
8. On the "Breakfast Time", "Lunch Time", and "Dinner Time" pages, select the specific times you eat your meals.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Eating Habits** (`Eating Habits`, `text`, optional): This field is a free-text area where you can describe your current eating habits. Be as detailed as possible to help us understand your eating patterns.
* **Restrictions** (`Restrictions`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the types of restrictions you have, such as food allergies or intolerances. You can also select "Other" if you have a different type of restriction not listed.
* **Food Allergies** (`Food Allergies`, `text`, optional): This field is used to list specific food allergies you have. Please be as detailed as possible, and separate each allergy with a comma.
* **Food Intolerances** (`Food Intolerances`, `text`, optional): This field is used to list specific food intolerances you have. Please be as detailed as possible, and separate each intolerance with a comma.
* **Other Restrictions** (`Other Restrictions`, `text`, optional): This field is used to list any other types of restrictions you have that are not listed above. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`Dietary Restrictions`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of dietary restriction you have, such as vegetarian or vegan.
* **Meal Planning** (`Meal Planning`, `select_one`, optional): Select how often you plan your meals, such as 1-2 meals a day or 5 or more meals a day.
* **Meal Frequency** (`Meal Frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Select how often you eat meals in a day, such as daily or occasionally.
* **Meal Times** (`Meal Times`, `time`, optional): This field is used to select specific times you usually eat your meals. Please use the format HH:MM or 12:00 PM/AM/PM.
* **Time of Day** (`Time of Day`, `select_one`, optional): Select the time of day you usually eat your meals, such as morning, afternoon, or evening.
* **Frequency of Meals** (`Frequency of Meals`, `select_one`, optional): Select how often you eat meals in a day, such as daily or occasionally.
* **Breakfast Time** (`Breakfast Time`, `time`, optional): Select the specific time you usually eat your breakfast.
* **Lunch Time** (`Lunch Time`, `time`, optional): Select the specific time you usually eat your lunch.
* **Dinner Time** (`Dinner Time`, `time`, optional): Select the specific time you usually eat your dinner.
