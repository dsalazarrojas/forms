# Sales Recruitment Workshop RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sales Recruitment Workshop RSVP form is designed to collect attendee information for a sales recruitment workshop. This guide will walk you through how to complete the form in a step-by-step manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Scroll down to each page to fill out the corresponding section.
2. For each section, enter the required information in the text field provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event Details
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, optional): This field is intended to collect the details of the event, such as the name or description of the workshop.

* **Company Details** (`company_details`, text, optional): This field is meant to collect information about the company the attendee is representing.

* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): This field is for collecting contact information for the person attending the event.

* **Submitter Details** (`submitter_details`, text, optional): This field is intended to collect information about who submitted the RSVP, in case the form is being completed on behalf of someone else.

## Tips
* Be sure to enter the required information accurately and completely to ensure your RSVP is processed correctly.
* If you are completing this form on behalf of someone else, please make sure to enter the correct submitter details.
