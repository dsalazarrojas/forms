# Who I Am Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Who I Am Form Template" is a general information collection form designed to gather details about a person's background, interests, skills, and career goals. This form is meant to be a starting point for users to provide a brief description of themselves.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Start by answering the questions on each page. You can type your answers in the text fields provided.
* Make sure to fill out all the fields, as some of them are required (although none are marked as required in this specific form, it is assumed that all fields should be filled out for a complete profile).
* Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure they accurately represent you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Details** (`personal_details`): This field is a general information section where you can provide any details you'd like to share about yourself. Please keep it concise and relevant.
* **Interests** (`interests`): Describe your interests, hobbies, or areas of passion. This could include anything from music to travel to sports.
* **Skills** (`skills`): List any relevant skills you possess, such as language proficiency, programming languages, or software proficiency.
* **Education** (`education`): Share your educational background, including any relevant degrees or certifications.
* **Career Goals** (`career_goals`): Describe your career aspirations or goals, such as job titles or industries you're interested in.
* **Personal Background** (`personal_background`): Provide any additional context or information about yourself that you feel is relevant to the purpose of this form.

## Tips
* Be as concise as possible while still accurately representing your identity.
* Use proper grammar and spelling to make your answers easy to read.
* Avoid sharing sensitive or personal information that you wouldn't typically share in a professional setting.
