# Teacher Administrative Support Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teacher Administrative Support Assessment Form is designed to collect feedback from teachers on the quality and frequency of administrative support they receive from the support team. This form will help administrators and support teams assess their support and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

* Please review the form carefully and only answer questions that you can provide an honest assessment on.
* For fields marked as "required", make sure to complete them as they are essential for a complete assessment.
* For fields that allow multiple selections (e.g., "select_multiple"), you can select multiple options that apply to your experience.
* For fields that ask for frequency or quality, please select the most accurate option that describes your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher Administrative Support Assessment** (`teacherAdministrativeSupportAssessment`, text, not required): This field is for general comments or feedback on the support provided by the administration team.
* **School ID** (`schoolId`, number, not required): Enter your school ID, if you are unsure of what to enter, please leave this field blank.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacherName`, text, not required): Enter your name, this will help the administrators know who provided the feedback.
* **Administrative Support** (`administrativeSupport`, text, not required): Provide feedback on the quality of administrative support you received. You can select "Good", "Fair", or "Poor" based on your experience.
* **Communication** (`communication`, select_one, not required): Rate the quality of communication from the support team. Select "Good", "Fair", or "Poor" based on your experience.
* **Collaboration** (`collaboration`, select_multiple, not required): Rate the collaboration level with the support team. Select "Good", "Fair", or "Poor" for each aspect that applies to your experience.
* **Communication Skills** (`communicationSkills`, text, not required): Provide feedback on the communication skills of the support team. This field can be a general comment or specific examples.
* **Communication Frequency** (`communicationFrequency`, time, not required): Select the frequency of communication from the support team. Please use the format "X times per week".
* **Feedback on Organizational Structure** (`feedbackOnOrganizationalStructure`, select_multiple, not required): Provide feedback on the organizational structure of the support team.
* **Administrative Support Frequency** (`administrativeSupportFrequency`, number, not required): Provide feedback on the frequency of administrative support you receive. Please select a number that best describes your experience.
* **Communication Channels** (`communicationChannels`, select_multiple, not required): Select all the communication channels used by the support team. Select "Email", "Phone", "In-person", "Text", or "Video Conference" that apply to your experience.
* **Feedback on Support Team** (`feedbackOnSupportTeam`, select_multiple, not required): Provide feedback on the support team's performance. Select "None", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor" based on your experience.
* **Communication Quality** (`communicationQuality`, select_multiple, not required): Rate the quality of communication from the support team. Select "Good", "Fair", "Poor" for each aspect that applies to your experience.
* **Administrative Support Team Size** (`administrativeSupportTeamSize`, number, not required): Select the size of the support team. Please select a number that best describes your experience.
* **Feedback on Communication** (`feedbackOnCommunication`, select_multiple, not required): Provide feedback on the communication performance of the support team.
* **Communication Channels Used 2** (`communicationChannelsUsed_2`, select_multiple, not required): Select all the communication channels used by the support team for this question.
* **Teacher Administrative Support Satisfaction** (`teacherAdministrativeSupportSatisfaction`, number, not required): Rate your satisfaction with the administrative support. Please select a number that best describes your experience.
* **Feedback on Support Team Size** (`feedbackOnSupportTeamSize`, select_multiple, not required): Provide feedback on the support team's size.
* **Communication Frequency 2** (`communicationFrequency_2`, time, not required): Select the communication frequency of the support team for this question.
* **Communication Style 2** (`communicationStyle_2`, select_multiple, not required): Rate the communication style of the support team. Select "Good", "Fair", "Poor" for each aspect that applies to your experience.
* **Teacher Satisfaction 2** (`teacherSatisfaction_2`, number, not required): Rate your satisfaction with the administrative support. Please select a number that best describes your experience.
* **Feedback on Administrative Support** (`feedbackOnAdministrativeSupport`, select_multiple, not required): Provide feedback on the administrative support performance. Select "None", "Good", "Fair", "Poor" based on your experience.
* **Communication Channels Used 2** (`communicationChannelsUsed_2`, select_multiple, not required): Select all the communication channels used by the support team for this question.

**Tips**

* Please be as specific as possible with your feedback.
* Select "None" if the question does not apply to your experience.
* If you are unsure of what to enter in any field, please leave it blank.
