# Hybrid Learning Program Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hybrid Learning Program Enrollment Form is designed for students or learners to enroll in a hybrid learning program, which combines both online and offline learning components.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Program Name in the designated field.
2. Fill out the required personal details, including First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone, Address, Date of Birth, and Student ID Number.
3. Choose the Program Type that best describes your enrollment purpose.
4. Select your Grade Level and Student Status.
5. Enter the School Year and Institution details, as applicable.
6. Choose your Student Group, and Course Name and ID, as applicable.
7. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Name**: Enter the name of the hybrid learning program you are enrolling in. This will help us identify your program.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name, as displayed on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name, as displayed on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your physical address for correspondence and delivery purposes.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Program Type**: Select the type of hybrid learning program you are enrolling in, e.g., High School Diploma or Equivalent, College Diploma or Equivalent, or Other.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date you plan to start the program.
* **End Date**: Enter the date you plan to finish the program.
* **Institution**: Enter the name of your school or institution.
* **School Year**: Enter the year you are currently studying or plan to study.
* **Student Status**: Choose your current student status, e.g., Active, Inactive, or Pending.
* **Grade Level**: Enter your current grade level, e.g., 9th, 10th, etc.
* **Student ID Number**: Enter your student ID number, as issued by your school or institution.
* **Program Code**: Enter the program code assigned to your hybrid learning program.
* **School Code**: Enter the school code assigned to your school or institution.
* **Student Code**: Enter the student code assigned to you.
* **User ID**: Enter your user ID, if applicable.
* **Program ID**: Enter the program ID assigned to your hybrid learning program.
* **School ID**: Enter the school ID assigned to your school or institution.
* **Grade**: Enter your current grade, e.g., A, B, C, D, E, etc.
* **Student Group**: Choose the student group that best describes you, e.g., Group1, Group2, Group3.
* **Course ID**: Enter the course ID, as applicable.
* **Course Name**: Enter the course name, as applicable.

## Tips
Please fill out the form carefully, as incomplete or inaccurate information may affect your program enrollment. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
