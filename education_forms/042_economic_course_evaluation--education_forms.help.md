# Economic Course Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback and evaluations from students on economic courses.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
*   Ensure that you have provided honest and accurate information.
*   Complete the form on the specific course you are evaluating.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your student ID.
*   **Course ID** (`course_id`, text, required): Enter the ID of the specific course you are evaluating.
*   **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the specific course you are evaluating.
*   **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, time, required): Enter the time you spent in the course (e.g., hours, days, weeks).
*   **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the course evaluation.
*   **Rating** (`rating`, number, required): Choose a rating of 1-5 for the course's overall performance.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Add any comments or feedback you have about the course.
*   **Course Outcome** (`course_outcome`, select_multiple, required): Choose all that apply from the list of outcomes.
*   **More outcomes** (`course_outcome2`, select_multiple, required): Choose all that apply from the list of outcomes.
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required): Add any additional comments or feedback you have about the course.
*   **Course Recommendation** (`course_recommendation`, select_one, required): Choose one of the following course recommendations: Very good, Satisfactory, or Unsatisfactory.
*   **More recommendations** (`course_recommendation2`, select_one, required): Choose one of the following course recommendations: Good, Not good.
*   **Even more** (`course_recommendation3`, select_one, required): Choose one of the following course recommendations: Yes, No.
*   **More and more** (`course_recommendation4`, select_one, required): Choose one of the following course recommendations: Yes, No.
*   **Even more and more** (`course_recommendation5`, select_one, required): Choose one of the following course recommendations: Yes, No.

## Tips

*   Please answer all questions to the best of your ability.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
*   Ensure that you have provided honest and accurate information.
*   This form is designed to help improve future economic courses, so your feedback is valuable.
