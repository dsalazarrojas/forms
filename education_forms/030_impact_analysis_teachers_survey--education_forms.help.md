# Impact Analysis Teachers Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from teachers about the impact of specific research areas on the university.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the University of Delhi from the dropdown menu.
2. Indicate whether you are aware of any undergraduate programmes at your institution.
3. Provide any additional information about research areas that you think are relevant to your institution.
4. Identify any teachers who are aware of research areas.
5. Enter a brief survey response in the text box.
6. Specify which research areas have a positive or negative impact on your institution.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **University of Delhi** (text): This field is where you select your institution. Please choose the University of Delhi from the dropdown menu.
* **UG Programmes** (select_multiple): This field asks if you are aware of any undergraduate programmes at your institution. Please select 'Yes' if you are aware of any undergraduate programmes, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Research Areas** (text): This field is where you can provide any additional information about research areas that you think are relevant to your institution.
* **Teachers** (select_multiple): This field asks if you are aware of any teachers who are aware of research areas. Please select 'Yes' if you are aware of any teachers who are aware of research areas, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Survey Response** (note): This field is where you can enter a brief survey response. Please provide any comments or feedback you have about the research areas.
* **Impact Analysis** (select_multiple): This field specifies which research areas have a positive or negative impact on your institution. Please select 'Yes' for positive impact, 'No' for negative impact, or leave this field blank if you are unsure.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have not already submitted an impact analysis for this semester.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please leave them blank and proceed with the other fields.
* If you have any feedback or suggestions, please enter it in the 'Survey Response' field.
