# Petition For Music Program - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Petition For Music Program" form is designed to gather support and signatures for a music program in schools. It collects information about students, teachers, and schools to provide a strong case for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Please enter your school's name in the "School name" field.
2.  Enter the student's name and grade level in the respective fields.
3.  Enter the teacher's name and email in the respective fields.
4.  Enter your school's department and email in the respective fields.
5.  Enter your school's phone number, address, and postal code in the respective fields.
6.  Provide a comment about the student in the "Student comments" field.
7.  Provide a comment from the teacher in the "Teacher's comment" field.
8.  Provide a comment about the school in the "School's comment" field.
9.  Choose how you want to show your support (Online petition, On site event, or Both) in the "How do you want to show your support?" field.
10. If you want to provide a signature, select the "Signature" options and enter your name and date in the respective fields.
11. Enter your country, province, and postal code in the "IP, Country, and Province" fields.
12. Enter your signature date, time, and IP in the "Date, Time, and IP" fields.
13. Finally, enter your school's comments about the student in the "School comments" field.
