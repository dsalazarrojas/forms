# Consumer Education Initiative Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather information from organizations interested in participating in the Consumer Education Initiative.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct organization type from the options provided.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
3. Provide a clear description of your education initiative.
4. Specify your target audience and expected number of participants.
5. State your initiative's goals and program duration.
6. Provide your proposed start date and any funding needed.
7. Describe your budget breakdown and any existing partners.
8. Share your previous experience and success metrics.
9. Indicate any additional resources you may need.
10. Submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (1, text, required): Enter the legal name of your organization.
* **Organization Type** (2, select_one, required): Select your organization type from the options provided.
* **Organization Address** (3, text, required): Enter your organization's street address.
* **City** (4, text, required): Enter your city location.
* **State or Province** (5, text, required): Enter your state or province.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (6, text, required): Enter your postal code.
* **Organization Website** (7, text, optional): Enter your organization's website URL.
* **Contact First Name** (8, text, required): Enter the first name of your primary contact.
* **Contact Last Name** (9, text, required): Enter the last name of your primary contact.
* **Contact Title** (10, text, required): Enter your primary contact's job title.
* **Contact Email** (11, email, required): Enter your primary contact's email address.
* **Contact Phone** (12, text, required): Enter your primary contact's phone number.
* **Initiative Name** (13, text, required): Enter the name of your education initiative.
* **Initiative Description** (14, text, required): Describe your education program.
* **Target Audience** (15, select_one, required): Select the target audience for your initiative.
* **Expected Number of Participants** (16, number, required): Estimate the number of participants your initiative will reach.
* **Initiative Goals** (17, text, required): Describe what you aim to achieve with your initiative.
* **Program Duration** (18, select_one, required): Select the duration of your program.
* **Proposed Start Date** (19, date, required): Enter the proposed start date of your initiative.
* **Funding Needed** (20, number, optional): If applicable, enter the amount of funding you require.
* **Budget Breakdown** (21, text, optional): Describe how you plan to use funds.
* **Existing Partners** (22, text, optional): List any existing partners.
* **Previous Experience** (23, text, required): Describe relevant past initiatives.
* **Success Metrics** (24, text, required): Specify how you will measure success.
* **Additional Resources Needed** (25, text, optional): Describe any additional resources you need.
* **Application Date** (26, date, required): Enter the date of submission.
