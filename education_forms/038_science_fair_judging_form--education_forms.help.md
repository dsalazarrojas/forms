## Science Fair Judging Form - Help Guide

### Purpose
The Science Fair Judging Form is used to provide an objective and transparent evaluation of student projects in a science fair. It helps judges to provide consistent feedback and scores for each project, allowing fair organizers to make informed decisions about awarding prizes and recognition.

### How To Complete This Form
To complete the Science Fair Judging Form, follow these steps:

1. Read through the overall rating and presentation sections to get an initial impression of the project.
2. Select the category that best fits the project from the drop-down menu.
3. Select any applicable science areas for the project.
4. Enter your name for the record of your judging evaluation.
5. Rate the project's overall quality using the numerical score input.
6. Provide any relevant comments or feedback about the project.
7. Make a recommendation for any awards or recognition.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Rating** (required, number): 
  Evaluate the project's overall quality and creativity on a scale from 0 to 10.

* **Presentation** (required, note): 
  Describe the project's presentation and how well it was displayed.

* **Project Name** (required, text): 
  Enter the project's title as displayed on the project board or exhibit board.

* **Category** (required, select_one): 
  Choose the subject area of the project, such as Physics, Biology, etc.

* **Science Area(s)** (required, select_multiple): 
  Select any specific science area(s) relevant to the project, such as Physics, Biology, etc.

* **Judge Name** (required, text): 
  Enter your name as the judge, for the record.

* **Score Given** (optional, number): 
  Enter a score between 0 and 10, or leave blank if you do not want to provide a score.

* **Comments** (optional, text): 
  Provide any additional comments or feedback to the project creator.

* **Recommendation** (required, select_one): 
  Indicate whether you recommend the project for any award or recognition.
