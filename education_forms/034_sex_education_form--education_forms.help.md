# sex_education_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sex_education_form is designed to collect information about students' educational activities. This form is meant to be filled out by students to report their progress, participation, or achievements in a course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the following fields:
	* Student Name
	* Student Number
	* Course Title
	* Course Code
	* Course Code Abbreviation
	* Student Email
	* Course Duration
	* Course Start Date
	* Course End Date
	* Course Hours
	* Course Start Time
	* Course End Time
	* Teacher

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, not required): Enter the student's name who is reporting their educational activity.
* **Student Number** (`student_number`, number, not required): Enter the student's number or ID.
* **Course Title** (`course_title`, text, not required): Enter the title of the course being reported.
* **Course Code** (`course_code`, text, not required): Enter the code of the course being reported.
* **Course Code Abbreviation** (`course_code_abbreviation`, text, not required): Enter the abbreviation of the course code.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, not required): Enter the student's email address.
* **Course Duration** (`course_duration`, number, not required): Enter the duration of the course in weeks or months.
* **Course Start Date** (`course_start_date`, date, not required): Enter the start date of the course.
* **Course End Date** (`course_end_date`, date, not required): Enter the end date of the course.
* **Course Hours** (`course_hours`, number, not required): Enter the total hours of the course.
* **Course Start Time** (`course_start_time`, time, not required): Enter the start time of the course.
* **Course End Time** (`course_end_time`, time, not required): Enter the end time of the course.
* **Teacher** (`teacher`, text, not required): Enter the name of the teacher or instructor.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check the information before submitting the form.
* If you need help or have any questions, contact your administrator or teacher.
