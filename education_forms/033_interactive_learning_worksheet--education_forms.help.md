# Interactive Learning Worksheet - Help Guide

## Purpose
The interactive learning worksheet is a tool used to support student learning and assessment in an educational setting. It is likely used to gather information from students and teachers to track student progress and provide feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully to understand the purpose and questions being asked.
* Fill in the required fields as indicated by the "required" flag in the form. These fields are:
  * None in this form, as all fields are optional.
* Fill in the remaining fields with relevant information as prompted.
* Submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`1`, `text`, required: false): This field is where you can enter a brief introduction or description of the learning worksheet.
* **student_name** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Enter your student's name here.
* **teacher_name** (`3`, `text`, required: false): Enter your teacher's name here.
* **lesson_plan** (`4`, `text`, required: false): Upload a lesson plan document here.
* **student_responses** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate whether the student has responded to the learning worksheet with "Yes" or "No".
* **submission_date** (`6`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you submitted this form.
* **submission_time** (`7`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you submitted this form. 

## Tips
* Be sure to fill in all relevant fields as accurately as possible.
* If you are unsure of a field's purpose or requirement, ask your teacher or educational administrator for clarification.
* Review your form carefully before submission to ensure it is complete and accurate.
