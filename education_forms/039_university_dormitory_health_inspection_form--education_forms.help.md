# University Dormitory Health Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Dormitory Health Inspection Form is used to inspect dormitories on campus and report any health and safety concerns. This form helps campus housing staff identify potential issues and take corrective action to ensure a safe and healthy living environment for students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select the "Residence Hall" field to choose the specific dormitory you are inspecting.
* Enter the date of the inspection in the "Health Inspection Date" field.
* Answer the question of whether you found any health and safety concerns during the inspection by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Findings" field.
* If you found concerns, list them in the "Recommendations" field.
* Indicate the follow-up status of the inspection by selecting "Not Required", "Required", or "Follow-up Completed" in the "Follow-up Status" field.
* Enter the name of the campus housing staff member who conducted the inspection in the "Campus Housing Team" field.
* Enter the name of the student residence in the "Student Residence Name" field.
* Enter the student residence ID in the "Student Residence ID" field.
* Enter the name of the dormitory in the "Dormitory Name" field.
* Answer the question of whether the inspection frequency is "Yes" or "No" in the "Inspection Frequency" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Residence Hall**: Choose the specific dormitory you are inspecting.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
* **Health Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the inspection.
	+ Type: date
	+ Required: true
* **Findings**: Answer whether you found any health and safety concerns during the inspection.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: true
* **Recommendations**: List any recommendations to improve health and safety conditions if you found concerns.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
* **Follow-up Status**: Indicate the follow-up status of the inspection.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: true
* **Campus Housing Team**: Enter the name of the campus housing staff member who conducted the inspection.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: true
* **Student Residence Name**: Enter the name of the student residence.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: true
* **Student Residence ID**: Enter the student residence ID.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: true
* **Dormitory Name**: Enter the name of the dormitory.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: true
* **Inspection Frequency**: Answer whether the inspection frequency is "Yes" or "No".
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: false
