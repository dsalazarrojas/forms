# After School Care Sign Out Sheet Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The After School Care Sign Out Sheet Form is used to ensure the safe and secure sign-out of students when they are not being supervised on school premises. This form helps staff members verify the identity of the person picking up the student and document the pickup details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Student Information Section**
	* Enter the student's complete legal name in the **Student Full Name** field.
	* Enter the student's identification number in the **Student ID** field.
2. **Pickup Details**
	* Select the **Grade Level** of the student from the dropdown list.
	* Select the name of the student's regular classroom teacher from the **Classroom Teacher** field.
	* Enter the date when the student is signing out in the **Sign Out Date** field.
	* Enter the time when the student is signing out in the **Sign Out Time** field.
	* Select the **Pickup Location** where the student was picked up from the dropdown list.
3. **Authorized Pickup Person**
	* Enter the full name of the person picking up the student in the **Pickup Person Full Name** field.
	* Select the **Relationship to Student** from the dropdown list (parent, guardian, grandparent, etc.).
	* Enter the phone number of the person picking up the student in the **Pickup Person Phone** field.
	* Enter the driver's license or ID number of the person picking up the student in the **Pickup Person ID** field.
4. **Pickup Authorization and Verification**
	* Indicate whether the person picking up the student is **Authorized to Pick up** by selecting the correct option.
	* Select the method of **Verification** used to confirm the person's identity from the dropdown list.
5. **Emergency Contact**
	* Enter the name of the emergency contact on file in the **Emergency Contact Name** field.
	* Enter the phone number of the emergency contact in the **Emergency Contact Phone** field.
6. **Pickup Confirmation and Student Release**
	* Confirm that the parent/guardian was **Notified** of the pickup.
	* Confirm that the student was **Released Safely** to the authorized person.
	* Confirm that all information entered is **Accurate**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Full Name** (`Student Full Name`, text, required): Enter the student's complete legal name.
* **Student ID** (`Student ID`, text, required): Enter the student's identification number.
* **Grade Level** (`Grade Level`, select_one, required): Select the current grade level of the student.
* **Classroom Teacher** (`Classroom Teacher`, text, required): Enter the name of the student's regular classroom teacher.
* **Sign Out Date** (`Sign Out Date`, date, required): Enter the date when the student is signing out.
* **Sign Out Time** (`Sign Out Time`, time, required): Enter the time when the student is signing out.
* **Pickup Person Full Name** (`Pickup Person Full Name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the person picking up the student.
* **Relationship to Student** (`Relationship to Student`, select_one, required): Select the relationship of the person picking up the student (parent, guardian, grandparent, etc.).
* **Pickup Person Phone** (`Pickup Person Phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the person picking up the student.
* **Pickup Person ID** (`Pickup Person ID`, text, required): Enter the driver's license or ID number of the person picking up the student.
* **Authorized to Pick up** (`Pickup Authorization`, select_one, required): Select whether the person picking up the student is authorized or not.
* **Verification Method** (`Verification Method`, select_one, required): Select the method used to confirm the person's identity.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`Emergency Contact Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact on file.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`Emergency Contact Phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Notified** (`Parent/Guardian Notification`, select_one, required): Confirm whether the parent/guardian was notified of the pickup.
* **Released Safely** (`Student Released Safely`, select_one, required): Confirm that the student was released safely to the authorized person.
* **Accurate** (`Information Accuracy`, select_one, required): Confirm that all information entered is accurate.

## Tips

* Always ensure that all information entered is accurate and up-to-date.
* Confirm that the student was released safely to the authorized person.
* Ensure that the parent/guardian was notified of the pickup.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all fields are completed correctly.
