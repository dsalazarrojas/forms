# Educational Leadership Trust Index Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Educational Leadership Trust Index Survey is designed to measure the levels of trust among educational leaders within the institution. This survey will help us understand the strengths and weaknesses of our leadership and services, and provide valuable insights to improve our institution.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Please select your role within the institution from the options provided in the "What is your role in the educational institution?" field.
2. Answer the questions regarding your position in the institution, the type of school you work in, and your years of experience.
3. Rate your levels of trust in the areas of leadership, teachers, staff, facilities, communication, decision-making, and resources using the scale provided.
4. Lastly, rate your overall satisfaction with the school leadership.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your role in the educational institution?** (`intro`, `text`, `required`: `true`)
	+ This field is used to determine your position within the institution. Please select your role from the provided options.
* **What is your position in the institution?** (`role`, `select_one`, `required`: `false`)
	+ If you are not sure of your position, you can select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you have a leadership position or not.
* **What type of school do you work in?** (`school_type`, `select_one`, `required`: `false`)
	+ Please select the type of school you work in from the provided options.
* **How many years of experience do you have?** (`years_of_experience`, `number`, `required`: `false`)
	+ Please enter the number of years you have been working in the educational institution.
* **How would you rate the school's leadership in terms of trust?** (`educational_leadership_scale_score`, `number`, `required`: `false`)
	+ Rate the level of trust you have in the institution's leadership on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is "Very poor" and 10 is "Excellent".
* **How would you rate the principal?** (`principal_survey`, `select_one`, `required`: `false`)
	+ Rate the principal's leadership on a scale of 1-4, where 1 is "Very poor" and 4 is "Excellent".
* **How would you rate the vice principal?** (`vice_principal_survey`, `select_one`, `required`: `false`)
	+ Rate the vice principal's leadership on a scale of 1-4, where 1 is "Very poor" and 4 is "Excellent".
* **How would you rate the teachers?** (`teachers_survey`, `select_one`, `required`: `false`)
	+ Rate the teachers' leadership on a scale of 1-4, where 1 is "Very poor" and 4 is "Excellent".
* **How would you rate the staff?** (`staff_survey`, `select_one`, `required`: `false`)
	+ Rate the staff's leadership on a scale of 1-4, where 1 is "Very poor" and 4 is "Excellent".
* **How would you rate the facilities?** (`facilities_survey`, `select_one`, `required`: `false`)
	+ Rate the facilities' leadership on a scale of 1-4, where 1 is "Very poor" and 4 is "Excellent".
* **How would you rate communication among teachers and staff?** (`communication_survey`, `select_one`, `required`: `false`)
	+ Rate the communication among teachers and staff on a scale of 1-4, where 1 is "Very poor" and 4 is "Excellent".
* **How would you rate decision making among teachers and staff?** (`decision_survey`, `select_one`, `required`: `false`)
	+ Rate the decision making among teachers and staff on a scale of 1-4, where 1 is "Very poor" and 4 is "Excellent".
* **How would you rate support among teachers and staff?** (`support_survey`, `select_one`, `required`: `false`)
	+ Rate the support among teachers and staff on a scale of 1-4, where 1 is "Very poor" and 4 is "Excellent".
* **How would you rate resources for teachers and staff?** (`resources_survey`, `select_one`, `required`: `false`)
	+ Rate the resources for teachers and staff on a scale of 1-4, where 1 is "Very poor" and 4 is "Excellent".
* **Overall, how satisfied are you with your school leadership?** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, `required`: `false`)
	+ Rate your overall satisfaction with the school leadership on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very dissatisfied" and 5 is "Extremely satisfied". 

## Tips

* Please answer all questions truthfully and accurately as they pertain to your role and experience in the institution.
* If you have any comments or suggestions, please use the "Comments" field to provide them.
