# Taylor FFA Chapter Officer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from individuals who are applying for officer positions within the Taylor FFA Chapter.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start, fill out your personal contact information such as your name, email, phone number, and chapter ID.
2. Select the officer positions you are applying for from the available options.
3. Provide your term start and end dates if applicable.
4. Enter your chapter's address, city, state, zip, and country.
5. Add any additional comments or attachments that you think are relevant to your application.
6. Review and confirm that you have filled out all required fields before submitting the form.
