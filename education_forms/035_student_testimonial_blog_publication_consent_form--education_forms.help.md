# Student Testimonial Blog Publication Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Student Testimonial Blog Publication Consent Form is a document that allows students to share their experiences and opinions about a particular institution or program. By completing this form, students are giving their consent for their testimonial and contact information to be published on a blog post.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and fill in the required information.
2. Provide a testimonial about your experience at the institution or program.
3. Select a publication date for your testimonial to be published.
4. Sign the form to confirm your consent.
5. Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user_info**: A text field where you can provide some general information about yourself (e.g., name, program, grade level).
* **testimonial**: A text area where you can write a short testimonial about your experience at the institution or program. This is the main content of the blog post that will be created from your submission.
* **publication_date**: A date field where you can select the date you are willing to have your testimonial published on the blog. Please note that this date will be used to determine when your testimonial will be posted.
* **student_signature**: A note field where you can sign your name, confirming that you are the author of the testimonial.
* **publication_date_signature**: A note field where you can sign the selected publication date, confirming that you are consenting to have your testimonial published on the specified date.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
* Be as detailed and honest as possible when writing your testimonial.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
