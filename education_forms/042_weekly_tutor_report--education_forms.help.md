# Weekly Tutor Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Weekly Tutor Report form is designed to collect data on student progress and feedback from tutors' lessons. It is intended to provide a structured template for tutors to report on their interactions with students, allowing them to track student development and provide valuable insights for future lessons.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's name
2. Enter the tutor's name
3. Select the date of the lesson
4. Record the duration of the lesson
5. Describe the student's progress
6. Provide any feedback for the student

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's name as it appears on their records.
* **Tutor Name** (`tutor_name`, text, required): Enter your own name as the tutor.
* **Lesson Date** (`lesson_date`, date, required): Select the date of the lesson.
* **Lesson Duration** (`lesson_duration`, text, required): Enter the length of the lesson in a descriptive format (e.g., "one hour").
* **Student Progress** (`student_progress`, text, required): Describe the student's progress during the lesson, using phrases such as "strong," "weak," or "mixed."
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Enter any feedback you would like to provide to the student, such as suggestions or areas for improvement.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when describing the student's progress and providing feedback.
* If the student has made significant progress, be sure to acknowledge and encourage them.
* If the student has shown weakness, provide constructive feedback and suggestions for improvement.
* Remember to review and adjust your feedback to ensure it is clear and actionable for the student.
