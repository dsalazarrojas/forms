# Aircraft Maintenance Engineering Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Aircraft Maintenance Engineering Application Form is a comprehensive document used to assess the qualifications of individuals applying for aircraft maintenance engineering positions. It is designed to gather detailed information about the applicant's background, experience, and skills to ensure a fair evaluation of their fit for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and efficiently, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your first name and last name in the corresponding fields.
2.  Enter your contact email address in the "Email Address" field.
3.  Enter your contact phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4.  Enter your street address in the "Street Address" field.
5.  Enter the city, state, and ZIP code in the respective fields.
6.  Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
7.  Select the position applied for from the list provided.
8.  Choose the date you are available to start work.
9.  Select your desired employment type from the options provided.
10. Select all relevant FAA certifications held.
11. Enter any certificate numbers in the "Certificate Numbers" field.
12. Enter any additional certifications or qualifications.
13. Select your level of experience from the list provided.
14. Choose the types of aircraft you have experience working on.
15. Select your highest level of education completed.
16. Enter the name of the school if attended for aviation maintenance.
17. Enter the year of graduation.
18. If applicable, enter your military experience and branch.
19. If applicable, enter your military specialty.
20. Enter the name of your most recent employer.
21. Enter the dates of employment with your most recent employer.
22. If applicable, enter the reason you left your previous employer.
23. Enter any specialized skills you possess.
24. Select the maintenance software you are proficient in.
25. Select the shifts you are available to work.
26. Indicate your willingness to travel for work.
27. If applicable, enter your expected salary range.
28. Indicate if you have references available.
29. Indicate your consent to a drug screen.
30. Indicate your consent to a background check.
31. Attach a brief statement of interest in the "Cover Letter" field.
32. Attach your resume.
33. Sign the form digitally.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`applicant_first_name`, Text, Required): Enter your legal first name.
*   **Last Name** (`applicant_last_name`, Text, Required): Enter your legal last name.
*   **Email Address** (`email`, Email, Required): Enter your contact email address.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone`, Text, Required): Enter your contact phone number.
*   **Street Address** (`address`, Text, Required): Enter your mailing address.
*   **City** (`city`, Text, Required): Enter the city where you live.
*   **State** (`state`, Text, Required): Enter the state or province where you live.
*   **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, Text, Required): Enter your postal code.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, Date, Required): Enter your birth date.
*   **Position Applied For** (`position_applied`, Select one, Required): Select the position you are applying for.
*   **Available Start Date** (`available_start_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date you are available to start work.
*   **Employment Type Desired** (`employment_type`, Select one, Required): Select your desired employment type (full-time, part-time, etc.).
*   **FAA Certifications** (`faa_certifications`, Select multiple, Required): Select all applicable certifications.
*   **Certificate Numbers** (`certificate_numbers`, Text, Optional): Enter any certificate numbers if applicable.
*   **Other Certifications** (`other_certifications`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional certifications if applicable.
*   **Years of Experience** (`years_experience`, Select one, Required): Select the level of experience you possess.
*   **Aircraft Experience** (`aircraft_experience`, Select multiple, Required): Select the types of aircraft you have experience with.
*   **Education Level** (`education_level`, Select one, Required): Select the highest level of education completed.
*   **Aviation School** (`aviation_school`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the school if attended for aviation maintenance.
*   **Graduation Year** (`graduation_year`, Number, Optional): Enter the year of graduation if applicable.
*   **Military Experience** (`military_experience`, Select one, Optional): Check if you served in the military.
*   **Military Branch** (`military_branch`, Text, Optional): Enter the branch of the military if applicable.
*   **Military Specialty** (`military_specialty`, Text, Optional): Enter the military specialty if applicable.
*   **Previous Employer** (`previous_employer`, Text, Required): Enter the name of your most recent employer.
*   **Dates of Employment** (`previous_employer_dates`, Text, Required): Enter the dates of employment with your most recent employer.
*   **Reason for Leaving** (`reason_for_leaving`, Text, Optional): Enter the reason for leaving your previous employer.
*   **Specialized Skills** (`specialized_skills`, Text, Optional): Enter any specialized skills you possess.
*   **Software Proficiency** (`software_proficiency`, Select multiple, Optional): Select the maintenance software you are proficient in.
*   **Shift Availability** (`shift_availability`, Select multiple, Optional): Select the shifts you can work.
*   **Will to Travel** (`travel_willingness`, Select one, Optional): Select your willingness to travel for work.
*   **Salary Expectations** (`salary_expectations`, Select one, Optional): Enter the expected salary range if applicable.
*   **References Available** (`references_available`, Select one, Optional): Check if you can provide references.
*   **Drug Screen Consent** (`drug_screen_consent`, Select one, Required): Check if you consent to a drug screen.
*   **Background Check Consent** (`background_check_consent`, Select one, Required): Check if you consent to a background check.
*   **Cover Letter** (`cover_letter`, Text, Optional): Attach a brief statement of interest if applicable.
*   **Resume Attached** (`resume_attached`, Select one, Optional): Check if you have attached your resume.
*   **Applicant Signature** (`applicant_signature`, Text, Required): Sign the form digitally.
*   **Application Date** (`application_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date you are submitting the application.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
*   Double-check the information you provide before submitting the form.
*   Attach any supporting documents or references as required.
*   Ensure that you are eligible for the position and that the form accurately reflects your qualifications.
