# University Preferences Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "University Preferences Survey" form is a non-mandatory survey that aims to gather your preferences and plans related to your university experience. The information collected will help us understand your needs and preferences, but it will not be stored with any sensitive or personal identifiable information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question carefully, but do not feel obligated to answer all of them.
2. You can choose as many or as few options as you like for each question.
3. Make sure to submit the form when you have finished answering all the questions.
