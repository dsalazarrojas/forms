# Exam Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Exam Leave Form is a document designed to collect information from students who choose to leave an examination or test.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have a valid reason for leaving before filling in the form.
2. Fill in your Student Name accurately and thoroughly.
3. Provide a clear Reason For Leaving, as specified under the "Reason For Leaving" field.
4. Enter the Date Of Leaving in the correct format (e.g., 2022-02-15).
5. Enter the Time Of Leaving in the correct format (e.g., 14:30).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): Please fill in your full name as it appears on your official student identification documents.
* **Reason For Leaving** (`reason_for_leaving`, text, required/optional): Please provide a valid reason for leaving, such as a medical emergency, family emergency, or personal reason. This field is mandatory to ensure accurate data collection.
* **Date Of Leaving** (`date_of_leaving`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of departure in the YYYY-MM-DD format, e.g., 2022-02-15.
* **Time Of Leaving** (`time_of_leaving`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of departure in the 24-hour clock format, e.g., 14:30.

## Tips
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure that all fields are filled in thoroughly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to your instructor or supervisor for guidance.
