# Classroom Budget Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from teachers for budget requests for classroom materials, equipment, and other educational needs. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information (Step 1-3)
	* Enter your **Teacher Name** in the text field.
	* Enter your **Teacher Email** in the email format (e.g., [username@school.edu](mailto:username@school.edu).
	* If you have a **Teacher Phone**, enter it in the text field.
2. Provide classroom details (Step 4-7)
	* Enter the **School Name** where you teach.
	* Enter the **School District** or region.
	* Select the **Grade Level** you are teaching (e.g., Kindergarten, Grade 1, etc.).
	* Select the **Subject Area** you are teaching.
3. Budget request (Step 8-12)
	* Enter the **Number of Students** in your classroom.
	* Select the **Total Amount Requested** for your budget.
	* Choose the **Purpose of Request** for your budget (e.g., Instructional Materials and Supplies, Technology and Equipment, etc.).
	* Explain the **Detailed Justification** for your request.
	* Describe how the **Expected Impact on Learning** will be for your students.
4. Itemize your budget request (Step 16-22)
	* If applicable, **Item 1 - Description**: describe what you plan to purchase.
	* If applicable, **Item 1 - Quantity**: enter the number of items.
	* If applicable, **Item 1 - Unit Cost**: enter the cost per item.
	* If applicable, **Item 2 - Description**: describe what you plan to purchase.
	* If applicable, **Item 2 - Quantity**: enter the number of items.
	* If applicable, **Item 2 - Unit Cost**: enter the cost per item.
5. Final steps (Step 23-25)
	* **Prior Budget Allocation**: if applicable, enter what was allocated last year.
	* **Request Submission Date**: this will be automatically populated with today's date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher Name**: Your full name as a teacher. 
* **Teacher Email**: Your email address where you can be reached for communication.
* **Teacher Phone**: Your contact telephone number.
* **School Name**: The name of the school where you teach.
* **School District**: The district or region of the school where you teach.
* **Grade Level**: Select your teaching grade level (e.g., Kindergarten, Grade 1, etc.).
* **Subject Area**: Select the primary subject you are teaching.
* **Number of Students**: Enter the number of students in your classroom.
* **Total Amount Requested**: Enter the total amount you are requesting for your budget.
* **Purpose of Request**: Choose the reason for your budget request.
* **Detailed Justification**: Explain why this budget is necessary.
* **Expected Impact on Learning**: Describe how this will improve student learning.
* **Prior Budget Allocation**: If applicable, enter what was allocated last year.
* **Request Submission Date**: This will be automatically populated with today's date.
* **Item 1 - Description**: Describe the first item you are requesting for purchase.
* **Item 1 - Quantity**: Enter the number of items you are requesting.
* **Item 1 - Unit Cost**: Enter the cost per unit of the first item.
* **Item 2 - Description**: Describe the second item you are requesting for purchase.
* **Item 2 - Quantity**: Enter the number of items you are requesting.
* **Item 2 - Unit Cost**: Enter the cost per unit of the second item.
* **Item 3 - Description**: Describe the third item you are requesting for purchase.
