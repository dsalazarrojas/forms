# Sunday School Teacher Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sunday School Teacher Evaluation Form is a tool used to assess the performance and effectiveness of Sunday School teachers. It provides a structured way to gather feedback on the teacher's name, contact information, availability, and teaching materials.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Sunday School teacher's name in the "Sunday School Teacher Name" field.
2. Enter the teacher's email address in the "Sunday School Teacher Email" field.
3. Enter the teacher's phone number in the "Sunday School Teacher Phone" field.
4. Select the teacher's availability from the options in the "Sunday School Teacher Availability" field.
5. Select the date of the evaluation in the "Sunday School Evaluation Date" field.
6. Rate the teacher's lesson effectiveness using the scale provided in the "Sunday School Lesson Effectiveness" field.
7. Select one or more of the options in the "Sunday School Lesson Materials" field to indicate the quality of the teacher's lesson materials.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Sunday School Teacher Name**
 sunday_school_teacher_name: text, required: false
Enter the name of the Sunday School teacher being evaluated.

### **Sunday School Teacher Email**
 sunday_school_teacher_email: email, required: false
Enter the email address of the Sunday School teacher being evaluated.

### **Sunday School Teacher Phone**
 sunday_school_teacher_phone: text, required: false
Enter the phone number of the Sunday School teacher being evaluated.

### **Sunday School Teacher Availability**
 sunday_school_teacher_availability: select_one, required: false
Select the teacher's availability for future evaluations.

* 1: Available for evaluations
* 2: Not available for evaluations
* 3: Available with notice

### **Sunday School Evaluation Date**
 sunday_school_evaluation_date: date, required: false
Select the date of the evaluation.

### **Sunday School Lesson Effectiveness**
 sunday_school_lesson_effectiveness: number, required: false
Rate the teacher's lesson effectiveness on a scale.

### **Sunday School Lesson Materials**
 sunday_school_lesson_materials: select_multiple, required: false
Select one or more of the options to indicate the quality of the teacher's lesson materials.

* Lesson materials are well prepared
* Lesson materials are partially prepared
* Lesson materials are not prepared

## Tips
* Ensure that you have reviewed the Sunday School Teacher Evaluation Form carefully before completing it.
* Be objective and unbiased when evaluating the teacher's performance.
* Use this form for its intended purpose only, and not for personal evaluations or reviews.
