# university_academic_journal_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "University Academic Journal Order Form" is designed to gather information for ordering academic journals. This form is used to collect details about the journal title, department, volume, edition, price, delivery address, and contact information. The form is typically used by universities to order academic journals for their libraries or research purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the journal title, department, and volume information in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the journal edition, year, and language as needed.
3. Enter the delivery address, contact name, email, and phone number.
4. Enter the journal price and quantity as needed.
5. Select the delivery instructions and order type.
6. Add any additional notes or order frequency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Journal Title (id: 2)**: Enter the title of the academic journal being ordered.
* **Journal Volume (id: 4)**: Enter the volume number of the academic journal.
* **Academic Department (id: 3)**: Enter the department or institution requesting the journal.
* **Journal Edition (id: 5)**: Select the edition of the journal being ordered.
* **Journal Year (id: 6)**: Select the year of the journal being ordered.
* **Journal Language (id: 7)**: Select the language of the journal being ordered.
* **Delivery Address (id: 8)**: Enter the address where the journal should be delivered.
* **Delivery City (id: 9)**: Enter the city where the journal should be delivered.
* **Delivery Province (id: 10)**: Enter the province where the journal should be delivered.
* **Delivery Postcode (id: 11)**: Enter the postal code where the journal should be delivered.
* **Delivery Country (id: 12)**: Enter the country where the journal should be delivered.
* **Contact Name (id: 13)**: Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email (id: 14)**: Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone (id: 15)**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Journal Price (id: 16)**: Enter the price of the journal being ordered.
* **Delivery Instructions (id: 17)**: Enter any special delivery instructions.
* **Notes (id: 18)**: Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Journal Quantity (id: 19)**: Enter the number of journals being ordered.
* **Order Date (id: 20)**: Enter the date the journal was ordered.
* **Order Type (id: 21)**: Select the type of order.
* **Order Frequency (id: 22)**: Select the frequency of the order.
* **Order Notes (id: 23)**: Add any additional order notes.
* **Journal Publisher (id: 24)**: Enter the publisher of the journal.
* **Assigned Tool (id: 25)**: Enter the assigned tool or equipment.
