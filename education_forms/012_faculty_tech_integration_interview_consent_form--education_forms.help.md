# Faculty Tech Integration Interview Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Faculty Tech Integration Interview Consent Form is used to collect consent from faculty members regarding the collection, recording, and use of audio or video from interviews. This form ensures that faculty members are informed and provide their consent for the use of their recorded materials in various ways.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and understand the purposes and conditions for which the interview will be recorded.
2. Provide your consent type (e.g., for research, educational purposes, etc.).
3. Enter a brief description of the interview in the "Interview Information" field.
4. Choose the audio or video format for the recording (e.g., audio only, video only, audio and video).
5. Select the recording method (e.g., in-person, remote, etc.).
6. Indicate whether you are informed about the recording and data collection.
7. Choose your faculty consent (e.g., agree, disagree).
8. Enter the date and time you gave your consent.
9. Enter the duration of your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consent Type**: Select the reason for the interview recording (e.g., research, educational purposes, etc.).
	+ Option 3: For research purposes.
	+ Option 4: For educational purposes.
* **Interview Information**: Enter a brief description of the interview, including the purpose, topic, and expected outcomes.
* **Audio or Video**: Choose the format for the recording (e.g., audio only, video only, audio and video).
	+ Option 1: Audio only.
	+ Option 2: Video only.
	+ Option 3: Audio and video.
* **Recording Method**: Select the method for recording the interview (e.g., in-person, remote, etc.).
	+ Option 1: In-person.
	+ Option 2: Remote.
	+ Option 3: Other (please specify).
* **Faculty Informed**: Indicate whether you are informed about the recording and data collection.
	+ Option 4: Informed.
	+ Option 5: Not informed.
* **Data Collection**: Choose whether you agree to data collection (e.g., yes, no).
	+ Option 6: Yes.
	+ Option 7: No.
* **Faculty Consent**: Indicate your consent for the use of the recorded material.
	+ Option 1: Agree.
	+ Option 2: Disagree.
	+ Option 8: Unable to consent.
* **Faculty Consent Date**: Enter the date you gave your consent.
* **Faculty Consent Time**: Enter the time you gave your consent.
* **Faculty Consent Duration**: Enter the duration of your consent.

## Tips

* Please review the form carefully before completing it to ensure you understand the purposes and conditions for which the interview will be recorded.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the interviewers or administrators.
* Your consent is required for the interview to proceed.
