# Education Platform Interview Question Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a candidate's education background, work experience, skills, and other relevant details for educational purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each section carefully and fill in the required information accurately.
- Select the appropriate options from the dropdown menus where applicable.
- Fill in the text fields with your current and desired job titles, education background, and any other relevant details.
- Make sure to include your current email and phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **candidate_name (Text, Optional)**: Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on the platform.
- **email (Email, Optional)**: Enter your professional email address.
- **phone_number (Text, Optional)**: Enter your contact phone number.
- **what_is_your_current_location (Select One, Optional)**: Select your current location.
- **what_skills_are_you_familiar_with (Select Multiple, Optional)**: Select the skills you are familiar with.
- **what_is_your_education_background (Text, Optional)**: Describe your education background.
- **date_of_birth (Date, Optional)**: Enter your date of birth.
- **time_of_day (Time, Optional)**: Enter your available time of day.
- **note (Note, Optional)**: Add any additional notes or comments about your experience.
- **what_is_your_level_of_experience (Select Multiple, Optional)**: Select your level of experience.
- **date_of_application (Date, Optional)**: Enter the date you are applying.
- **what_is_your_current_job_title (Select One, Optional)**: Select your current job title.
- **what_is_your_language (Select Multiple, Optional)**: Select your language proficiency.
- **note (Note, Optional)**: Add any additional notes or comments.
- **what_is_your_level_of_english_proficiency (Select One, Optional)**: Select your level of English proficiency.
- **what_is_your_area_of_expertise (Select Multiple, Optional)**: Select your areas of expertise.

Please note that some fields are optional, and you should only fill them in if you feel they are relevant to your application. If you have any doubts or questions, please reach out to the platform administrators.
