# educational-leadership-training-registration-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool for submitting requests for educational leadership training. It is intended to collect relevant details to facilitate the registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting, ensure you have all the necessary information to complete the form. The form is divided into several sections, each with its own requirements. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Form Submitter
* **Form Submitter** (`Form Submitter`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select 'Yes' if you are submitting this form on behalf of someone else.
* **Name of submittter** (`Name of submittter`, `text`, required): Enter your name to be associated with this training registration.
* **Email of submtter** (`Email of submter`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone of submter** (`Phone of submter`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Date of Training** (`Date of Training`, `date`, required): Select the date of the training event you are registering for.
* **Time of Training** (`Time of Training`, `time`, required): Select the time of the training event you are registering for.
* **Duration of Training** (`Duration of Training`, `number`, required): Choose the duration of the training session (e.g., 1, 2, 3, 4, 5).
* **Type of Training** (`Type of Training`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of training (Leadership Training, Management Training, Other).
* **Training Location** (`Training Location`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate the training location (Yes, No).
* **Training Instructor** (`Training Instructor`, `select_one`, required): Select the training instructor (John Smith, Jane Doe, Bob Johnson).
* **Comments** (`Comments`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional comments or information regarding the training.
* **Uploaded Documents** (`Uploaded Documents`, `text`, optional): Attach any relevant documents to support your registration request.
* **Training Availability** (`Training Availability`, `select_one`, required): Choose the training day (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday).
* **Training Duration in Hours** (`Training Duration in Hours`, `select_one`, required): Choose the duration of the training session in hours.
* **Training Status** (`Training Status`, `select_one`, required): Choose the status of the training (Upcoming, Active, Completed).
* **Training Start Date** (`Training Start Date`, `date`, required): Select the start date of the training event.
* **Training End Date** (`Training End Date`, `date`, required): Select the end date of the training event.
* **Training Start Time** (`Training Start Time`, `time`, required): Select the start time of the training event.
* **Training End Time** (`Training End Time`, `time`, required): Select the end time of the training event.
* **Training Start Hour** (`Training Start Hour`, `select_one`, required): Select the start hour (09:00 AM, 12:00 PM).
* **Training End Hour** (`Training End Hour`, `select_one`, required): Select the end hour (10:00 AM, 01:00 PM).
* **Training Start Minutes** (`Training Start Minutes`, `select_one`, required): Select the start minutes (00, 30).
* **Training End Minutes** (`Training End Minutes`, `select_one`, required): Select the end minutes (00, 30).
* **Training Remaining Minutes** (`Training Remaining Minutes`, `select_one`, required): Select the remaining minutes (00, 30).
* **Training Duration in Hours** (`Training Duration in Hours`, `select_one`, required): Choose the duration of the training session in hours (1, 2, 3, 4, 5).
