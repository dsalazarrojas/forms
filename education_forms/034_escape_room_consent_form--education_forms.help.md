# Escape Room Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Escape Room Consent Form is designed to collect information and consents from students and teachers participating in an escape room activity. This form aims to ensure that both parties are aware of their responsibilities and expectations, and that necessary information is gathered for the successful execution of the activity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for the student and teacher.
2. Select the correct consent options for both the student and teacher.
3. If applicable, provide the necessary phone numbers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Information (Student Information)** (`student_info`, text, optional): Enter the student's general information for the activity.
* **Student Name (Student Name)** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter the student's name who will be participating in the activity.
* **Team Members (Team Members)** (`team_members`, text, optional): List the team members who will be participating in the activity.
* **Student ID (Student ID)** (`student_id`, number, optional): Enter the student's ID number (if applicable).
* **Team ID (Team ID)** (`team_id`, number, optional): Enter the team ID number (if applicable).
* **Parent Consent (Parent Consent)** (`parent_consent`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the parent's consent option for the activity:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Student Attendance (Student Attendance)** (`student_attendance`, date, optional): If the student will attend the activity, choose the correct date.
* **Teacher Consent (Teacher Consent)** (`teacher_consent`, select_one, optional): Choose the teacher's consent option for the activity:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **School Information (School Information)** (`school_info`, text, optional): Enter the school's information (if applicable).
* **Student Phone (Student Phone)** (`student_phone`, text, optional): Enter the student's phone number (if applicable).
* **Teacher Phone (Teacher Phone)** (`teacher_phone`, text, optional): Enter the teacher's phone number (if applicable).
