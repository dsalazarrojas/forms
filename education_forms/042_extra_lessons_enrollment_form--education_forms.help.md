# extra_lessons_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The extra_lessons_enrollment_form is used to record and track the availability and status of additional lessons for students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Student ID to identify the student enrolling in the lesson.
2. Enter the student's full name as per the Student ID.
3. Enter the student's email address.
4. Enter the student's phone number.
5. Select the dates of the lesson that the student will be attending.
6. Select the time of the lesson that the student will be attending.
7. Check if the student will be attending the lesson on a weekday or weekend.
8. Enter the frequency of the lesson (e.g. once a week, twice a month, etc.).
9. Check if other students are also enrolled in the lesson.
10. Enter any additional notes about the lesson or student.
11. Check if the lesson is active or inactive.
12. Check if the lesson was submitted by the student or someone else.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student ID** (1): Enter the student's ID number to identify the student enrolling in the lesson.
* **Full Name** (2): Enter the student's full name as per the Student ID.
* **Email** (3): Enter the student's email address.
* **Phone** (4): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Lesson Dates** (5): Select the dates of the lesson that the student will be attending.
* **Lesson Time** (6): Select the time of the lesson that the student will be attending.
* **Lesson Days** (7): Check if the student will be attending the lesson on a weekday or weekend.
* **Lesson Frequency** (8): Enter the frequency of the lesson (e.g. once a week, twice a month, etc.).
* **Enrolled Students** (9): Check if other students are also enrolled in the lesson.
* **Notes** (10): Enter any additional notes about the lesson or student.
* **Status** (11): Check if the lesson is active or inactive.
* **Submitted By** (12): Check if the lesson was submitted by the student or someone else.
