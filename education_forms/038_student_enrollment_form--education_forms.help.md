# Student Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Enrollment Form is a tool for new students to provide information about themselves, their course preferences, and their fee payment details when enrolling in a course or program. It is designed to be easy to use and ensure that you provide all the necessary information to complete your enrollment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the "New Student" field, which is used to identify yourself as a new student.
2. In the "Course and Instructor" field, enter the name of the course and instructor you are interested in enrolling in.
3. In the "Student Details" field, provide your details such as name, contact information, and any other relevant information about yourself.
4. In the "Fee Payment" field, enter your payment information for the course fees.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **New Student**: Enter your name, email, and any other relevant information to identify yourself as a new student.
  - This field is optional but recommended to help us get in touch with you.
- **Course and Instructor**: Enter the name of the course and instructor you are interested in enrolling in.
  - This will help us ensure you are enrolling in the correct course.
- **Student Details**: Enter your details such as name, contact information, and any other relevant information about yourself.
  - This field is used to verify your identity and ensure you have the necessary information to complete your enrollment.
- **Fee Payment**: Enter your payment information for the course fees.
  - This field is required to secure your spot and complete your enrollment.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting.
- If you have any issues or questions, don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
- You can always update your information if you need to after submitting.
