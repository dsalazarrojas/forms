# Piano Festival Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Piano Festival Registration Form is a registration form for participants to sign up for the Piano Festival. This form will help us gather essential information from you, and we'll use it to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for everyone involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in your participant details.
2. Select your piano style and performance preferences.
3. Choose your performance date and time.
4. Add any additional notes or comments.
5. Confirm your registration by typing in your email address.
6. Verify your email address to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your name as you'd like it to appear on the festival program.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your festival participation.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required: false): Provide your phone number so we can reach you if needed.
* **Piano Style** (`piano_style`, select_one, required: false): Select your preferred piano style (e.g., classical, jazz, etc.).
* **Music Pieces** (`music_pieces`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the music pieces you'd like to perform (e.g., classical, jazz, etc.).
* **Performance Date** (`performance_date`, date, required: false): Select the date you'd like to perform on the festival schedule.
* **Performance Time** (`performance_time`, time, required: false): Choose the time slot you'd like to perform on the festival schedule.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): If you have any additional notes or comments, please enter them here.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, note, required: false): If you have any other information to share, please add it here.
* **Confirm** (`confirm`, text, required: false): Type in your email address to confirm your registration.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, email, required: false): Verify your email address to complete your registration.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your email address for accuracy.
* If you have any special requests or needs, please let us know in the "Note" or "Additional Notes" fields.
* If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to contact us.
