# Introduction To Control Systems Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants about the Introduction to Control Systems course or seminar they have attended.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please select the course or seminar you were participating in from the dropdown list (if provided).
2. Enter your rating of the course (on a scale of 1-5, if applicable).
3. Provide any additional feedback you have about the course in the text box.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What course or seminar were you participating in?** (`course_taken`, text, optional): This field allows you to enter the name of the course or seminar you recently attended. Please provide a brief name of the course or seminar, such as "Introduction to Control Systems" or "Control Systems 101."
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): If the course had a rating system, you can choose a number from 1 to 5 to reflect your opinion about the course. If there was no rating system, please enter a number that represents your overall satisfaction with the course.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): This is an open-ended text box where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about the course. You can mention what you liked or disliked about the course, what you found helpful or confusing, and any other thoughts or opinions you have.

## Tips

* Please be honest and concise in your answers.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas for improving the course, please mention them in the feedback field.
* If you have any concerns or questions, you can reach out to the instructor or course staff directly.
