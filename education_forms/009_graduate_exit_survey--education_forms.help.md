# Graduate Exit Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Graduate Exit Survey is designed to gather feedback from students who have completed their graduate program to assess the quality and effectiveness of the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select only one option for select_one type fields.
3. Select all that apply for select_multiple type fields.
4. Use the provided text fields to provide clear and concise answers.
5. Do not repeat any answers or provide unnecessary information.
6. If you have any comments or suggestions, please use the text field for comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Enrolled** (`course_enrolled`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the courses you have actually enrolled in during your graduate program.
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, optional): Please provide the name of your instructor(s) who taught you during your graduate program.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, text, optional): Please describe your overall experience in the graduate program, including any positive and negative aspects.
* **Course Satisfaction** (`course_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Please select your level of satisfaction with the course(s) you have taken.
* **Educational Values** (`educational_values`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the educational values you believe were effectively taught in the program.
* **Comments** (`teacher_comments`, note, optional): Please provide any comments or suggestions you may have about the program.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, optional): Please provide the name of the graduate program you have completed.
