# New Student Welcome Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register students for the New Student Welcome Event. It collects essential information from students to facilitate a smooth and enjoyable experience during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the provided text fields.
2. Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
3. Provide a valid email address that matches your official school email.
4. Select your school ID from the drop-down menu.
5. For the student ID, select the applicable option(s) from the drop-down menu.
6. Enter the event date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
7. Enter the event time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
8. Provide a brief description of the event in the text field.
9. If applicable, select the assigned tool from the provided options.
10. Choose the status of the event from the drop-down menu.
11. Select your school name from the drop-down menu.
12. Choose your student status from the drop-down menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth**: Provide your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Email**: Enter your school email address.
* **School ID**: Select your school ID from the drop-down menu.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Student ID**: Select the applicable student ID from the drop-down menu.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Event Venue**: Provide a brief description of the event venue.
* **Event Description**: Enter a brief description of the event.
* **Assigned Tool**: If applicable, select the assigned tool from the drop-down menu.
* **Event Status**: Select the status of the event from the drop-down menu.
* **School Name**: Enter your school name.
* **Student Status**: Choose your student status from the drop-down menu.
