# Learning Material Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Learning Material Requisition Form is a tool designed to facilitate the process of requesting learning materials from students. This form helps to gather essential information about the materials needed, the deadline for submission, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form title at the top of the page.
2. Enter your name as the teacher requesting the materials.
3. Specify the subject of the materials being requested.
4. Describe the specific materials you need.
5. Provide the deadline for when you need the materials by date and time.
6. Select your department from the options provided.
7. Fill in the student's name and any other details you need to collect from them.
8. Provide any relevant student identification information.
9. Choose the student's course and grade level.
10. Fill in any other necessary information about the submitter (you can select one or multiple options).
11. Submit the form by providing a date and time for when you need it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title**:
	+ This field is where you should input the title of your form submission for easy identification.
* **Teacher Name**:
	+ Enter your name as the teacher requesting the materials.
* **Subject**:
	+ Type in the subject of the materials you are requesting.
* **Material Requested**:
	+ Describe the specific learning materials you need.
* **Date Required**:
	+ Enter the date you need the materials by.
* **Teacher Department**:
	+ Select your department from the options provided.
* **Student Name**:
	+ Fill in the name of the student requesting the materials.
* **Student ID**:
	+ Enter any relevant student identification information.
* **Student Course**:
	+ Type in the student's course information.
* **Student Grade**:
	+ Enter the student's grade level.
* **Teacher ID**:
	+ Fill in any necessary teacher identification information.
* **Teacher Course**:
	+ Type in your course information.
* **Teacher Department**:
	+ Select your department from the options provided.
* **Submit Date**:
	+ Enter the date you submit the form.
* **Submit Time**:
	+ Enter the time you submit the form.
* **Submitter**:
	+ Choose one or multiple options for who is submitting the form (you can select one or multiple options).
