# seasons_and_axial_tilt_quiz--education_forms - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about the tilt of the Earth in relation to the seasons and the duration of specific seasonal events.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is the first day of spring?** (`season_start_date`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date of the first day of spring.
* **How long does the summer solstice last?** (`solstice_length`, `number`, `required`): Enter the number of days the summer solstice lasts.
* **What is the tilt angle of the Earth on the summer solstice?** (`tilt_angle_summer`, `number`, `required`): Enter the tilt angle of the Earth on the summer solstice.
* **How many days does the summer solstice last?** (`solstice_duration`, `number`, `required`): Enter the duration of the summer solstice in days.
* **What is the tilt angle of the Earth on the autumnal equinox?** (`tilt_angle_autumn`, `number`, `required`): Enter the tilt angle of the Earth on the autumnal equinox.
* **How many days does the autumnal equinox last?** (`equinox_duration`, `number`, `required`): Enter the duration of the autumnal equinox in days.
* **What is the tilt angle of the Earth on the winter solstice?** (`tilt_angle_winter`, `number`, `required`): Enter the tilt angle of the Earth on the winter solstice.
* **How long does the winter solstice last?** (`solstice_length_winter`, `number`, `required`): Enter the duration of the winter solstice in days.
## Tips
* Double-check your answers for accuracy.
* Make sure to enter dates in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, refer to your textbook or consult with a teacher.
