# Ancient Gaul Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ancient Gaul Quiz form is designed to educate students about ancient Gaul, gathering information about their understanding of the subject and providing a score-tracking feature for instructors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

* Start by reading the instructions on the first page.
* Enter your full name in the "Student name" field.
* Choose the correct answer for the first question, "Which tribe inhabited ancient Gaul" by selecting one of the options.
* Answer the short-answer question "Key characteristic of Gallic society" in the text field.
* If desired, enter your score in the "Score" field.
* Provide any additional feedback in the "Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your full name, which will be used to track your responses.
* **Which tribe inhabited ancient Gaul** (`question_1`, select_one, required): Choose the correct tribe from the options provided. This question is used to gauge your understanding of ancient Gaul's inhabitants.
* **Key characteristic of Gallic society** (`question_2`, text, required): Provide a short answer to this question to demonstrate your knowledge of Gallic society.
* **Score** (`score`, number, optional): If you are an instructor, enter your score in this field to track student progress.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the quiz or your experience taking it.

## Tips
* Be sure to read the instructions carefully to ensure you understand the questions and their requirements.
* Take your time to answer the short-answer question to ensure your response is accurate and thoughtful.
* If you are an instructor, use the score field to track student progress and provide feedback.
