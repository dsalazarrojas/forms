# Reverse Career Fair Student Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from students who want to register for a reverse career fair. This form captures basic contact information, education level, field of interest, and preferences for the career fair.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the first field.
2. Select your education level from the options provided.
3. Enter your major or field of study.
4. Enter your email address and phone number.
5. If you have a resume, upload it or provide a link to it.
6. Select the fields of interest that you are interested in.
7. Provide any additional comments you have.
8. Choose your registration option for the career fair.
9. Agree or disagree to attend the career fair.
10. If you agree to attend, provide a reason why you can't attend in the optional field.
11. Enter your signature on the form.
12. Enter the date and time of your signature.
13. Enter your time of attendance at the career fair.
14. Finally, confirm your attendance and select any optional activities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reverse Career Fair Student Name**: This is the field where you enter your name. Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Education Level**: This field allows you to select your current education level. Please choose from the options provided.
* **Major/Field of Study**: This is the field where you enter your major or field of study.
* **Email**: This is the field where you enter your email address. Please ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Phone Number**: This is the field where you enter your phone number.
* **Resume**: If you have a resume, you can upload it or provide a link to it. This is optional.
* **Fields of Interest**: This is where you select the fields of interest that you are interested in. Please choose all that apply.
* **Comments**: This is the field where you can provide any additional comments or information.
* **Registration Option**: This is where you choose your registration option for the career fair. Please select from the options provided.
* **Attend Career Fair**: This is where you agree or disagree to attend the career fair.
* **Why Can't Attend**: If you agree to attend but can't, please provide a reason why.
* **Signature**: This is where you enter your signature on the form.
* **Signature Date**: This is where you enter the date and time of your signature.
* **Attend Time**: This is where you enter the time of your attendance at the career fair.
* **Confirm Attendance**: This is where you confirm your attendance and select any optional activities.
* **Optional Activities**: This is where you select any optional activities you may be interested in. 

Please note that the form checks for duplication by user email address.
