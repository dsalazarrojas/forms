# Sustainability Leadership Course Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sustainability Leadership Course Registration Form is designed to collect information from users to provide a tailored course experience. This form will be used to gather information about you and your course preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your Last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Describe your Main goal for attending the course in the "Main goal" field.
6. Select your preferred format for receiving feedback in the "Preferred format for feedback" field.
7. Enter your Course Start Date in the "Course Start Date" field.
8. Enter your Course End Date in the "Course End Date" field.
9. Enter the Time of Day for your course in the "Time of Day" field.
10. Provide any Additional Notes about your course experience in the "Additional Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Enter your first name here. (Text input, not required)
* **Last name**: Enter your last name here. (Text input, not required)
* **Email**: Enter your email address here. (Email input, not required)
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number here. (Text input, not required)
* **Main goal**: Describe your main goal for attending this course. (Text input, not required)
* **Preferred format for feedback**: Select your preferred format for receiving feedback (Email, Phone, Text Message). (Select one option, not required)
* **Course Start Date**: Enter the date when your course will start. (Date input, not required)
* **Course End Date**: Enter the date when your course will end. (Date input, not required)
* **Time of Day**: Enter the time of day for your course. (Time input, not required)
* **Additional Notes**: Provide any additional notes about your course experience. (Text input, not required)

Note: Fields marked as "not required" can be left blank if you do not have that information.
