# training_enrolment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from individuals interested in enrolling in a training program. It helps to gather essential details for the training process, ensuring accuracy and efficiency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your Email address in the "Email" field in the correct format.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Indicate the Programme you are interested in in the "Programme" field.
6. Select the Training Type (Online, Offline, or In-house).
7. Choose the Training Location (Location 1, Location 2, or Location 3).
8. Provide any Additional Information if necessary.
9. Enter the Submission Date and Time.
10. Enter the name of the Training Provider.
11. Enter the details of the Trainee, as per their requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your First Name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your Last Name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your Email address in the correct format.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your Phone number.
* **Programme** (`programme`, text, required): Indicate the Programme you are interested in.
* **Training Type** (`training_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the Training Type (Online, Offline, or In-house).
* **Training Location** (`training_location`, select_one, required): Choose the Training Location (Location 1, Location 2, or Location 3).
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, note, optional): Provide any Additional Information if necessary.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the Submission Date.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, required): Enter the Submission Time.
* **Training Provider** (`training_provider`, text, required): Enter the name of the Training Provider.
* **Trainee Details** (`trainee_details`, text, required): Enter the Trainee Details.
