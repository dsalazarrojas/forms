# Herons Formula Math Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to test your understanding of Heron's formula for calculating the area of a triangle given the lengths of its sides. Heron's formula is a mathematical formula used to calculate the area of a triangle when the lengths of all three sides are known.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page in sequence.
2. Enter the lengths of sides a, b, and c in the respective fields.
3. Enter the result of Heron's formula for the area of the triangle in the "What is the area of the triangle using Heron's formula?" field.
4. Answer the following questions:
   * How does Heron's formula handle the case where two sides are equal?
   * What is the effect of increasing the length of one side on the area of the triangle?
   * Can you provide an example of a triangle with equal sides?
   * What is the minimum number of sides required to use Heron's formula?

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the formula for the area of a triangle?** (`herons_formula`, text, required): This is the starting point of the quiz, where you should enter the formula for the area of a triangle. The formula for the area of a triangle is a mathematical equation that relates the lengths of the sides of the triangle to its area.
* **What is the length of side a?** (`side_a`, number, required): This field is where you should enter the length of side a, one of the sides of the triangle.
* **What is the length of side b?** (`side_b`, number, required): Enter the length of side b, another side of the triangle.
* **What is the length of side c?** (`side_c`, number, required): Enter the length of side c, the third side of the triangle.
* **What is the area of the triangle using Heron's formula?** (`herons_result`, number, required): Enter the result of applying Heron's formula to the lengths of sides a, b, and c to get the area of the triangle.
* **How does Heron's formula handle the case where two sides are equal?** (`equal_sides_case`, text, required): Describe how Heron's formula handles the situation where two sides of the triangle are equal.
* **What is the effect of increasing the length of one side on the area of the triangle?** (`side_length_effect`, text, required): Describe the effect of increasing the length of one side on the area of the triangle.
* **Can you provide an example of a triangle with equal sides?** (`example_triangle`, text, required): Describe an example of a triangle where two or more sides are equal.
* **What is the minimum number of sides required to use Heron's formula?** (`min_required_sides`, number, required): Enter the minimum number of sides required to apply Heron's formula.
