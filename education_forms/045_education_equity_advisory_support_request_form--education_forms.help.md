# Education Equity Advisory Support Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information for support requests related to educational equity initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above. Ensure you fill in all required fields accurately and provide clear explanations for each section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **school_name** (text, required/optional): This field is used to enter your school's name.
* **email** (email, required): This field is used to enter your email address.
* **district_name** (text, optional): This field is used to enter your school's district ID, if applicable.
* **grade_level** (number, optional): This field is used to select your school's grade level.
* **subject_area** (text, optional): This field is used to provide the subject area you are requesting support for.
* **description** (note, optional): This field is used to provide a brief description of your support request.
* **support_request** (text, required): This field is used to describe the support request.
* **requested_by** (select_one, required): This field is used to select the person who made the request.
* **date_request** (date, optional): This field is used to select the date when you would like to make the request.
* **time_request** (time, optional): This field is used to select the time when you would like to make the request.
* **location_request** (text, optional): This field is used to provide the location where you would like to make the request.
* **contact_name** (text, optional): This field is used to enter your name.
* **contact_email** (email, required): This field is used to enter your email address.
* **contact_phone** (text, optional): This field is used to enter your phone number.
* **contact_extension** (text, optional): This field is used to enter the extension number you can be reached at.
* **school_phone** (text, optional): This field is used to enter your school's phone number.
* **school_fax** (text, optional): This field is used to enter your school's fax number.
* **school_email** (email, optional): This field is used to enter your school's email address.
* **school_address** (text, optional): This field is used to enter your school's address.
* **program_support** (text, optional): This field is used to describe any additional program support you require.
* **coaching_type** (select_multiple, required): This field is used to choose the type of coaching you require.
* **coaching_frequency** (select_multiple, required): This field is used to select the frequency of coaching you need.
* **support_request_comments** (text, optional): This field is used to enter any additional comments for the support request.
* **assigned_tool** (text, optional): This field is used to enter the tool assigned to you.

## Tips

*   Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately.
*   Provide clear explanations for each field to ensure accurate processing of your request.
*   Use the "coaching_type" and "coaching_frequency" fields to select your coaching needs accurately.
*   Use the "support_request" field to enter a clear description of your support request.

Please ensure you follow these guidelines carefully to complete the form accurately and ensure your support request is processed effectively.
