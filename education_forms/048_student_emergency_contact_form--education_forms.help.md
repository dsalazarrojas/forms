# Student Emergency Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Emergency Contact Form is used to collect emergency contact information from students. This form is typically used by school administrators or staff to record the names, numbers, and relationships of emergency contacts for each student.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's contact information on page 1.
2. Select the relationships between the student and their emergency contacts on page 2.
3. Enter any additional notes or details on page 3.
