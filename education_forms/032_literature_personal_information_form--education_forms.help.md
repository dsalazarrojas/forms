# Literature Personal Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal information from participants in literature-related programs or events. It will help the organizers of the program to gather essential details about the participants, including their names, contact information, and program-specific details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your name**: In the "participant_name" field, please enter your name as it appears on your official documents or ID.
2. **Provide your email address**: In the "participant_email" field, enter your valid email address. This will help us keep you updated on any important information related to the program.
3. **Choose your program type**: In the "participant_program_type" field, select the type of program you are participating in (Literature, Other, or Workshop).
4. **Enter your start date**: In the "participant_start_date" field, enter the date you began the program or event.
5. **Enter your start time**: In the "participant_start_time" field, enter the time you started the program or event.
6. **Enter your end date**: In the "participant_end_date" field, enter the date you completed the program or event.
7. **Enter your end time**: In the "participant_end_time" field, enter the time you completed the program or event.
8. **Add any notes**: In the "participant_notes" field, provide any additional information you think would be helpful for the program organizers to know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **participant_name**: The name that appears on your official documents or ID.
- **participant_email**: A valid email address to keep you updated on the program.
- **program_name**: The name of the program you are participating in (e.g., Literature Program).
- **participant_program_type**: Select the type of program you are participating in (Literature, Other, or Workshop).
- **participant_start_date**: The date you began the program or event.
- **participant_start_time**: The time you started the program or event.
- **participant_end_date**: The date you completed the program or event.
- **participant_end_time**: The time you completed the program or event.
- **participant_notes**: Any additional information about your participation in the program.
