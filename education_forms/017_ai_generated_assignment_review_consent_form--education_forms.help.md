# AI Generated Assignment Review Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The AI Generated Assignment Review Consent Form is a required document that ensures students understand and agree to the use of Artificial Intelligence tools in reviewing their assignments. This form is essential for the instructor to gather consent while maintaining the institution's commitment to student data privacy.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your Student Full Name, Student ID Number, Course Title / Number, and Instructor Name.
2.  Read the review process description to understand how AI tools are utilized.
3.  Choose whether you consent to having your coursework processed by AI review tools.
4.  Select the scope of consent: "All assignments for the duration of this course" or "Specific assignments only" (list below if necessary).
5.  If you choose "Specific assignments," please list them.
6.  Acknowledge that you understand AI feedback is supplementary to human faculty decisions.
7.  Confirm that you are under 18 (if applicable) and provide the parent/guardian name (if applicable).
8.  Enter your confirmation email address to receive a copy of the signed consent form.
9.  Select "I agree and sign this consent form" to digitally sign this document.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student Full Name** (`student_full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your student ID.
*   **Student ID Number** (`student_id_number`, text, required): Enter your student ID number as assigned by the institution.
*   **Course Title / Number** (`course_title`, text, required): Enter the title and number of the course you are enrolled in.
*   **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your instructor.
*   **Review Process Description** (`review_process_note`, note, required): Read this section carefully to understand how AI tools are used in the review process.
*   **Disclosure of AI Utilization** (`process_description`, note, required): Understand that AI tools are used for initial feedback generation and structural analysis, but final decisions are made by human faculty.
*   **Consent to AI Analysis** (`ai_tools_consent`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes, I consent" to having your coursework reviewed by AI tools.
*   **Data Privacy Acknowledgment** (`data_privacy_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Choose "I understand that my data will be processed according to the institution's privacy policy and will not be used to train external AI models."
*   **Scope of Consent** (`scope_of_consent`, select_one, required): Choose between "All assignments for the duration of this course" or "Specific assignments only."
*   **If "Specific", please list assignments** (`specific_assignments_list`, text, false): If you choose "Specific assignments," list them here.
*   **Human Oversight Agreement** (`human_oversight_agreement`, select_one, required): Acknowledge that AI feedback is supplementary to human faculty decisions.
*   **Is the student under 18 years of age?** (`minor_status`, select_one, required): If you are under 18, select "True" and provide the parent/guardian name.
*   **Parent / Guardian Name (If under 18)** (`guardian_name`, text, required): If you are under 18, enter the parent/guardian's name.
*   **Confirmation Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email to receive a copy of the signed consent form.
*   **Digital Signature** (`digital_signature`, select_one, required): Select "I agree and sign this consent form."
