# School Test Administration Plan - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Test Administration Plan form is designed to collect essential information for planning and executing school tests, ensuring a seamless experience for students, staff, and exam administrators.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully and answer the questions relevant to your test administration needs.
2. Fill out the form sections accordingly, providing accurate and complete information for each field.
3. Double-check your entries before submitting the form to avoid errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Test Logistics
* **Test Logistics** (`Test Logistics`, text, required: false): Please provide a brief description of the test logistics, including any specific requirements or procedures that need to be followed during the test administration.

### Page 2: School Information
* **School Information** (`School Information`, text, required: false): Enter the school's name, address, and other relevant details that will help us locate and contact the school.

### Page 3: Test Dates
* **Test Dates** (`Test Dates`, date, required: false): Choose the date(s) on which the test will be administered.

### Page 4: Test Times
* **Test Times** (`Test Times`, time, required: false): Select the time(s) of the test administration.

### Page 5: Room Setup
* **Room Setup** (`Room Setup`, text, required: false): Describe the room configuration and any special setup requirements for the test.

### Page 6: Exam Information
* **Exam Information** (`Exam Information`, text, required: false): Provide any specific exam details, such as exam type, duration, or special instructions.

### Page 7: Accommodations
* **Accommodations** (`Accommodations`, text, required: false): List any special accommodations needed for students, such as language support or physical assistance.

### Page 8: Test Coordinator
* **Test Coordinator** (`Test Coordinator`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the test coordinator who will be responsible for the test administration.

### Page 9: School Contact
* **School Contact** (`School Contact`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the school contact who will be responsible for general inquiries.

### Page 10: Exam Proctors
* **Exam Proctors** (`Exam Proctors`, select multiple, required: false): Indicate if proctors will be present during the test administration (Yes/No).

### Page 11: Exam Scribes
* **Exam Scribes** (`Exam Scribes`, select multiple, required: false): Indicate if scribes will be present during the test administration (Yes/No).

### Page 12: Test Equipment
* **Test Equipment** (`Test Equipment`, text, required: false): Specify any required test equipment, such as computers or scanners.
