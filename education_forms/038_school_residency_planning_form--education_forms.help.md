# School Residency Planning Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Residency Planning Form is used to plan and manage school residencies, capturing essential details such as location, dates, and duration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **Form Title** field to describe the purpose of the residency.
2. Enter the **Applicant Name** in the **Applicant Name** field.
3. Provide the **Email** address of the applicant.
4. Enter the **Phone Number** of the applicant.
5. Select the **Start Date** and **End Date** of the residency.
6. Choose the **Start Time** and **End Time** of the residency.
7. Enter the **Duration** of the residency.
8. Enter the **Application Fee** for the residency.
9. Specify the **Residency Location** where the residency will take place.
10. Select the **Program Type** (e.g., course, conference, etc.).
11. Enter the **Program Length** of the residency.
12. Enter the **Max Applications** allowed for the residency.
13. Enter the **Max Applicants** allowed for the residency.
14. Enter the **Max Programs** allowed for the residency.
15. Enter the **Max Residencies** allowed for the residency.
16. Enter any additional **Form Comments** about the residency.
17. Add any **Notes** about the residency.
18. Select who **Submitted** the form (Form Administrator or User).
19. Enter the **Submitted On** date and time.
20. Select the **Form Version** (e.g., 1.0, 2.0, etc.).
21. Enter the **Updated At** date and time of the last update.
22. Select who **Updated By** (Form Administrator or User).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title**: A brief description of the residency.
* **Applicant Name**: The name of the person applying for the residency.
* **Email**: The email address of the applicant.
* **Phone Number**: The phone number of the applicant.
* **Start Date**: The start date of the residency.
* **End Date**: The end date of the residency.
* **Start Time**: The start time of the residency.
* **End Time**: The end time of the residency.
* **Duration**: The length of the residency.
* **Application Fee**: The fee for applying to the residency.
* **Residency Location**: The location where the residency will take place.
* **Program Type**: The type of program or course being offered.
* **Program Length**: The length of the program or course.
* **Max Applications**: The maximum number of applications allowed for the residency.
* **Max Applicants**: The maximum number of applicants allowed for the residency.
* **Max Programs**: The maximum number of programs allowed for the residency.
* **Max Residencies**: The maximum number of residencies allowed for the program.
* **Form Comments**: Additional comments about the residency.
* **Notes**: Any additional notes about the residency.
* **Submitted By**: Who submitted the form (Form Administrator or User).
* **Submitted On**: The date and time the form was submitted.
* **Form Version**: The version of the form.
* **Updated At**: The date and time of the last update.
* **Updated By**: Who updated the form (Form Administrator or User).
