# Learner Suspension Notification - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Learner Suspension Notification form is a template used by schools to inform parents about a learner's suspension. This form provides a standardized way to communicate with parents, ensuring consistency in the information provided.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Learner Suspension Period** field with the date and duration of the suspension.
2. In the **Parent Notification** field, provide a brief message or note to be sent to the parent.
3. In the **Follow-up Steps** field, outline any additional actions or meetings that will take place after the suspension period.
4. In the **School Name**, enter the name of the school.
5. In the **School Email**, enter the school's email address.
6. In the **Learner Name**, enter the learner's name.
7. In the **Learner Email**, enter the learner's email address.
8. Select the **Reason for Suspension** from the provided options (Academic or Non-Academic).
9. Fill in the **Suspension Date** field with the actual date of the suspension.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Learner Suspension Period** (`learner_suspension_period`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date and duration of the suspension.
* **Parent Notification** (`parent_notification`, `text`, required: false): A brief message or note to be sent to the parent.
* **Follow-up Steps** (`follow_up_steps`, `text`, required: false): Outline any additional actions or meetings that will take place after the suspension period.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the school.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the school's email address.
* **Learner Name** (`learner_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the learner's name.
* **Learner Email** (`learner_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the learner's email address.
* **Reason for Suspension** (`reason_for_suspension`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the reason for the suspension from the provided options (Academic or Non-Academic).
* **Suspension Date** (`suspension_date`, `date`, required: false): Fill in the actual date of the suspension.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have all the necessary information before filling out the form.
* Proofread the form for any errors or inconsistencies before submitting it.
* Use clear and concise language in the **Parent Notification** field.
* Make sure to keep the **Follow-up Steps** field up-to-date and accurate.
* Use the correct format for dates in the **Learner Suspension Period** and **Suspension Date** fields.
