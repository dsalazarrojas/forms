# Curriculum Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Curriculum Verification Form is designed to collect information about a student's curriculum, including their student ID, school name, discipline, student name, grade level, enrollment and graduation dates, status, and verification status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's student ID.
2. Enter the school name where the student is enrolled.
3. Select the discipline of the student's curriculum.
4. Enter the student's name.
5. Select the student's grade level.
6. Enter the enrollment date.
7. Enter the graduation date.
8. Select the student's graduation status.
9. If the student is inactive, provide an explanation for their status.
10. Select the verification status of the student's curriculum.
11. If the student's curriculum is inactive, provide an explanation for their status.
12. Enter any additional notes about the verification status.
13. If you are a school administrator, indicate whether you are involved in verifying the student's curriculum.
14. If you are a school administrator and are not involved in verifying the student's curriculum, provide an explanation for your lack of involvement.
15. Enter any additional notes about the verification status.
16. Sign and date the form to confirm that it has been completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number): This is the student's unique ID number.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text): Enter the name of the school where the student is enrolled.
* **Discipline** (`discipline`, text): Select the discipline of the student's curriculum.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text): Enter the student's name.
* **Grade Level** (`student_grade_level`, select_one): Select the student's grade level.
* **Enrollment Date** (`enrollment_date`, date): Enter the date the student was enrolled.
* **Graduation Date** (`graduation_date`, date): Enter the date the student graduated.
* **Graduation Status** (`graduation_status`, select_one): Select the student's graduation status.
* **Graduation Status Other** (`graduation_status_other`, text): If the student is inactive, provide an explanation for their status.
* **Verification Status** (`verification_status`, select_one): Select the verification status of the student's curriculum.
* **Verification Status Other** (`verification_status_other`, text): If the student's curriculum is inactive, provide an explanation for their status.
* **Verification Notes** (`verification_status_notes`, text): Enter any additional notes about the verification status.
* **School Administrators** (`school_administrators`, select_multiple): If you are a school administrator, indicate whether you are involved in verifying the student's curriculum.
* **School Administrators Other** (`school_administrators_other`, text): If you are a school administrator and are not involved in verifying the student's curriculum, provide an explanation for your lack of involvement.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note): Sign and date the form to confirm that it has been completed.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date): Enter the date and time you signed the form.
* **Printed By** (`printed_by`, text): Enter the name of the person who printed the form.
* **Printed By Title** (`printed_by_title`, text): Enter the title of the person who printed the form.

Note: Some fields may require additional explanation or clarification, but this will provide a basic understanding of how to complete each field.
