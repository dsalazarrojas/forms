# Parking Pass - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to generate a parking pass for students. The form is designed to collect necessary information to issue a parking permit for students on campus. Please complete the form accurately to ensure you receive the correct parking pass.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Student ID number in the "Student ID" field.
2. Enter your full name in the "Name" field.
3. Select the date you wish to use the parking pass in the "Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student ID (Student ID, number, required: false)**: Enter your unique student ID number to identify you as a student.
* **Name (Name, text, required: false)**: Provide your full name as it appears on your student ID card.
* **Date (Date, date, required: false)**: Select the date you wish to use the parking pass for. Please note that parking passes are only valid on the selected date.
