# Elementary School Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students and their guardians for the purpose of assessing their eligibility for admission to an elementary school.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter the parent or guardian's name in the "Parent/Guardian Name" field.
3. Enter the student's date of birth in the "Student DOB" field.
4. Choose the student's grade level from the options provided in the "Student Grade" field.
5. Enter the student's email address (if applicable) in the "Student Email" field.
6. Enter the student's phone number in the "Student Phone" field.
7. Enter the student's home address in the "Student Address" field.
8. Enter the parent or guardian's phone number in the "Guardian Phone" field.
9. Enter the parent or guardian's email address (if applicable) in the "Guardian Email" field.
10. Choose the student's gender from the options provided in the "Student Gender" field.
11. Select the student's ethnicity from the options provided in the "Student Ethnicity" field.
12. Enter the parent or guardian's name (if different from the student's) in the "Guardian Name" field.
13. Enter the place where the student was born in the "Student Birthplace" field.
14. Describe the relationship between the student and the guardian in the "Guardian Relationship" field.
15. Choose the student's English language proficiency level from the options provided in the "Student English Level" field.
16. Choose the student's math proficiency level from the options provided in the "Student Math Level" field.
17. Choose the student's reading proficiency level from the options provided in the "Student Reading Level" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name (Text)**: Enter the student's name.
* **Parent/Guardian Name (Text)**: Enter the parent or guardian's name.
* **School Name (Text)**: Enter the name of the school the student is applying to.
* **Student DOB (Date)**: Enter the student's date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g. 1995-02-15).
* **Student Grade (Number)**: Choose the student's grade level from the options provided.
* **Student Email (Email)**: Enter the student's email address (if applicable).
* **Student Phone (Text)**: Enter the student's phone number.
* **Student Address (Text)**: Enter the student's home address.
* **Guardian Phone (Text)**: Enter the parent or guardian's phone number.
* **Guardian Email (Email)**: Enter the parent or guardian's email address (if applicable).
* **Student Gender (Select One)**: Choose the student's gender from the options provided.
* **Student Ethnicity (Select Multiple)**: Select the student's ethnicity from the options provided.
* **Guardian Name (Text)**: Enter the parent or guardian's name (if different from the student's).
* **Student Birthplace (Text)**: Enter the place where the student was born.
* **Guardian Relationship (Text)**: Describe the relationship between the student and the guardian.
* **Student English Level (Select One)**: Choose the student's English language proficiency level from the options provided.
* **Student Math Level (Select One)**: Choose the student's math proficiency level from the options provided.
* **Student Reading Level (Select One)**: Choose the student's reading proficiency level from the options provided.
* **Student Social Skills (Text)**: Enter any special social skills or requirements the student has.
* **Student Special Needs (Text)**: Enter any special needs or requirements the student has.
