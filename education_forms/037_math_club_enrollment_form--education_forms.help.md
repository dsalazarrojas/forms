# Math Club Enrollment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Math Club Enrollment Form is a simple form designed to gather information from students who are interested in joining the math club. This form is used to determine whether a student is a good fit for the math club and to plan events and activities around their interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the student information section with your name, grade, and email address.
2. Choose your math level: high, low, or select one of the provided options.
3. Indicate your interest in the math club by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' from the provided options.
4. Select your level of commitment to the math club by answering the time-related question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Information (student_info)**: This is the section where you enter your student information, including your name, grade, and email address.
* **Math Level (student_math_level)**: This is a selection field where you choose your math level: high, low, or one of the other provided options.
* **Math Club Interest (math_club_interest)**: This is a selection field where you indicate your interest in the math club. Select 'Yes' if you are interested in joining, 'No' if not.
* **Math Club Commitment (math_club_commitment)**: This is a time field where you answer how much time you are willing to commit to the math club, e.g. 1 hour, 2 hours, 3 hours, etc.

## Tips
- Be honest about your math level and interest in the math club. This will help us plan events and activities around your needs and abilities.
- Make sure to fill out your email address so we can contact you with club news and updates.
- If you are unsure about your math level or commitment, just answer as best you can. Our staff will be happy to help you figure it out.
