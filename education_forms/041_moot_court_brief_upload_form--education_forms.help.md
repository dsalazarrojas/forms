# Moot Court Brief Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to upload and submit a moot court brief and supporting documents for review or competition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Moot Court Brief Upload Form Title** field with the title of the brief you are uploading.
2. In the **Upload Brief** field, select and upload the brief as a PDF or Word document.
3. In the **Supporting Documents** field, select and upload any supporting documents as a PDF or Word document.
4. Select the **Category** of the brief, which can be Moot Court, Mock Trial, or Other.
5. In the **Description** field, provide a brief description of the brief and its contents.
6. Enter the **Date** and **Time** of the brief.
7. In the **Upload Brief File** field, select and upload the PDF or Word document containing the brief.
8. In the **Upload Supporting File** field, select and upload any supporting documents as a PDF or Word document.
9. Enter your **Email** and **Phone Number** for contact purposes.
10. In the **Comments** field, add any additional comments or notes about the brief.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Moot Court Brief Upload Form Title** (required): The title of the brief.
* **Upload Brief** (optional): Upload the brief as a PDF or Word document.
* **Supporting Documents** (optional): Upload supporting documents as a PDF or Word document.
* **Category** (optional): Select the category of the brief, which can be Moot Court, Mock Trial, or Other.
* **Description** (optional): A brief description of the brief and its contents.
* **Date** (optional): Enter the date of the brief.
* **Time** (optional): Enter the time of the brief.
* **Upload Brief File** (optional): Upload the PDF or Word document containing the brief.
* **Upload Supporting File** (optional): Upload supporting documents as a PDF or Word document.
* **Email** (optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Comments** (optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the brief.
