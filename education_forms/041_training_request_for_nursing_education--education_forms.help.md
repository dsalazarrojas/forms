# training_request_for_nursing_education - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from end-users about their training requests for nursing education.

## How To Complete This Form

This form is composed of several fields that need to be filled out. Please follow the instructions for each field carefully to ensure accurate and complete information is provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <label> Training Request (id: 1) - Required
* **Training Request** (`Training Request`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of your training request for nursing education.

### <label> Education Level (id: 2) - Optional
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select from:
	+ Certificate Level
	+ Diploma
	+ Associate
	+ Bachelor
	+ Master
	+ Doctoral

### <label> Staff Member (id: 3) - Required
* **Staff Member** (`Staff Member`, `text`, required): Enter the name and title of the staff member who requested training.

### <label> Education Field (id: 4) - Optional
* **Education Field** (`education_field`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select from:
	+ Nursing
	+ Medicine
	+ Healthcare
	+ Wellness
	+ Medical

### <label> Date (id: 5) - Required
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Select the date when training is requested for.

### <label> Time (id: 6) - Required
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Select the time when training is requested for.

### <label> Location (id: 7) - Required
* **Location** (`Location`, `text`, required): Enter the location of the training session.

### <label> Training Facility (id: 8) - Optional
* **Training Facility** (`Training Facility`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the training facility.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your training administrator.
* You can submit multiple entries for different training requests.
