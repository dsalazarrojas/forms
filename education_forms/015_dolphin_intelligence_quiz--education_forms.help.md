# Dolphin Intelligence Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dolphin Intelligence Quiz is a tool designed to evaluate your understanding of dolphins. It consists of a series of multiple-choice questions that assess your knowledge of various aspects of dolphins, such as their name, color, shape, and size.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Dolphin Intelligence Quiz, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Dolphin Intelligence" field.
2. Select the correct answers for each question, following the format presented on each section.
3. Ensure you fill in all required fields, as indicated by the "required" label for each section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dolphin Intelligence** (`page_one`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name in this field.
* **Section One** (`section_one`, `select_one`, required): Choose the correct answer from the options provided for the question "What is the name of the animal?"
* **Section Two** (`section_two`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all correct answers from the options provided for the questions in this section.
* **Section Three** (`section_three`, `select_one`, required): Choose the correct answer from the options provided for the question "What is the shape of the sea?"
* **Section Four** (`section_four`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all correct answers from the options provided for the questions in this section.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Review each section carefully before proceeding to ensure accuracy.
- If you're unsure about an answer, try to eliminate options that you know are incorrect to increase your chances of selecting the correct answer.
