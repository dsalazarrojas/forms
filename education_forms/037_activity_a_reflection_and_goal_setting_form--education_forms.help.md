# Activity A  Reflection And Goal Setting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Activity A  Reflection And Goal Setting Form is a self-assessment tool for students to reflect on their experiences and progress in Life Skills activities. It helps students identify areas of improvement and set goals for future development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your full name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter the name of the course this activity is part of in the "Course Name" field.
3. Enter the date of the activity in the "Activity Date" field.
4. Enter a brief title of the activity in the "Title of Life Skills Activity" field.
5. Describe the activity in detail in the "Description of the Activity" field.
6. Select the Life Skills practiced during the activity (e.g., Communication, Problem-solving, Time management).
7. Describe the positive outcomes you experienced from this activity in the "Positive Outcomes" field.
8. Describe any challenges you faced during this activity in the "Challenges Faced" field.
9. Write down important lessons you learned from this activity in the "Lessons Learned" field.
10. Rate your confidence in the practiced skills before and after the activity using the numbers 1-10 in the "Confidence Level Before Activity" and "Confidence Level After Activity" fields.
11. Enter your future Life Skills goals in the "Future Goals" field.
12. If needed, specify the support required to achieve your goals in the "Support Needed" field (this is optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Name** (text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your record.
- **Course Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the course this activity is part of.
- **Activity Date** (date, required): Enter the date when the Life Skills activity took place.
- **Title of Life Skills Activity** (text, required): Briefly title this activity.
- **Description of the Activity** (text, required): Describe what the activity involved in detail.
- **Life Skills Practiced** (select_multiple, required): Select which Life Skills you practiced during this activity (e.g., Communication, Problem-solving, Time management).
- **Positive Outcomes** (text, required): Describe the positive outcomes you experienced from this activity.
- **Challenges Faced** (text, required): Describe any challenges you faced during this activity.
- **Lessons Learned** (text, required): Write down important lessons you learned from this activity.
- **Confidence Level Before Activity** (number, required): Rate your confidence in the practiced skills before the activity using the numbers 1-10.
- **Confidence Level After Activity** (number, required): Rate your confidence in the practiced skills after the activity using the numbers 1-10.
- **Future Goals** (text, required): Enter your future Life Skills goals.
- **Support Needed** (text, optional): If needed, specify the support you require to achieve your goals.
