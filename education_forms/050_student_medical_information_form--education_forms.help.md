# Student Medical Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Student Medical Information Form is designed to collect important medical information about students. This form is used by school administration and staff to ensure students' safety and well-being while on campus. It is essential to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to help us provide the best possible care and support.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the **First name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **Last name** field.
3. Provide your date of birth in the **Date of birth** field.
4. If you have any medical history, please enter it in the **Medical history** field.
5. Select any allergies you have from the list in the **Allergies** field.
6. If you have any medical conditions, please enter them in the **Medical condition** field.
7. Enter any relevant medical notes or instructions in the **Medical notes** field.
8. Provide the **Emergency contact** person's name and phone number.
9. Enter your **Student ID** number.
10. Enter your email address (optional).
11. Enter your phone number (required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your given name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your surname.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Medical history** (`medical_history`, note, optional): If you have any pre-existing medical conditions or surgeries, please provide a brief description.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_multiple, required): Select any allergies you have from the list. Please note that this list is not exhaustive and if you have a different allergy, please contact school administration.
* **Medical condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions, please enter them here.
* **Medical notes** (`medical_notes`, note, required): Enter any relevant medical notes or instructions.
* **Emergency contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact person.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, required): Enter your student ID number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address (optional).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number. This will be used in case of an emergency.

# Tips

* Please make sure to complete this form accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact school administration.
* Remember to provide any additional medical information that may be relevant to your safety and well-being while on campus.
