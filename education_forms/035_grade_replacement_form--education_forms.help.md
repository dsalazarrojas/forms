# Grade Replacement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to replace an existing grade in a student's record with a new grade.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in its entirety.
2. Enter the student's input (user name or ID) in the "User Input" field.
3. Enter the grade you are replacing in the "grade_to_replace" field.
4. Enter a brief reason for replacing the grade in the "reason_for_replacement" field.
5. Enter the new grade in the "New Grade" field.
6. Select the confirmation options for the replacement in the "confirm_replacement" field.
7. If you have any additional comments, enter them in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Enter the student's input (user name or ID) to identify the student's record.
* **Grade to Replace** (`grade_to_replace`, text, optional): Enter the grade you want to replace in the student's record.
* **Reason for Replacement** (`reason_for_replacement`, text, optional): Enter a brief reason for replacing the grade.
* **New Grade** (`new_grade`, number, optional): Enter the new grade to replace the old grade with.
* **Confirm Replacement** (`confirm_replacement`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more options to confirm that you are replacing the grade correctly.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments about the replacement request.

## Tips
* Ensure you have a valid reason for replacement before submitting the form.
* Double-check that you are not duplicating an existing replacement request.
* Confirm that you are authorized to access and complete this form.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all fields are filled out correctly.
