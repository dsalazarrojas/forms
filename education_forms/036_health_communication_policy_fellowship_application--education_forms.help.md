# Health Communication Policy Fellowship Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Communication Policy Fellowship is a program that aims to bring together individuals from diverse backgrounds to discuss and develop health communication policies that can be used to improve public health outcomes. This application form collects information from potential applicants to assess their qualifications and eligibility for the fellowship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the "Application Details" section with your name and other basic contact information.
2. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Information" section.
3. In the "Education" section, specify your degrees earned or those you are currently pursuing.
4. Share your relevant experience in the "Experience" section.
5. Attach your resume in the "Resume" section.
6. Ensure that you attach your resume in the "Resume Attachment" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Details** (`Application Details`, text, required/optional): This section is where you can briefly describe your interest in the fellowship program and why you want to participate.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, text, required/optional): Please enter your contact details, including your name, email, and phone number. This will help the review panel get in touch with you if needed.
* **Education** (`Education`, text, required/optional): Please specify your highest degree earned or those you are currently pursuing. If you have multiple degrees, list them in order of relevance.
* **Experience** (`Experience`, text, required/optional): Share your relevant experience that aligns with the fellowship program's goals and objectives.
* **Resume** (`Resume`, text, required/optional): This section is not for uploading your resume directly. Please attach your resume file in the "Resume Attachment" section.
* **Resume Attachment** (`Resume Attachment`, text, required/optional): Attach your resume file here. Make sure it is in a supported format (PDF, DOCX, etc.).
