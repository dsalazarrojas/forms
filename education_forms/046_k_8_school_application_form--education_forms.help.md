# ประก K 8 School Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for students to apply to a school for grades 4-8. Please provide your information so our school can review your application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the requested information for yourself and your parent/guardian. Make sure to fill out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, required/optional): Enter your full name.
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, required/optional): Provide your current address to help us contact you.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, required/optional): Select your current grade level from the options provided (4th, 5th, 6th, 7th, 8th).
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, required/optional): Enter your parent's/guardian's name.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, required/optional): Enter your parent's/guardian's email address.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, required/optional): Enter your parent's/guardian's phone number.

## Tips
If you have any questions or need assistance, feel free to ask.
