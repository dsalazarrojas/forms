# Student Transportation Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book student transportation for educational activities. Please use this form to provide information about the student and any specific needs or requirements for the transportation booking.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*).
2. For fields with options (e.g., select_one, select_multiple), choose the relevant options that apply to the student's transportation needs.
3. Enter a valid email address and phone number for the student in the respective fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's name as it appears on their school record.
* **Student Transportation Date** (`student_transportation_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the student's transportation, using the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the student's school or educational institution.
* **Student Transportation Time** (`student_transportation_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the student's transportation, in 24-hour format (e.g., 12:00, 14:00, 15:30).
* **Student Transportation Mode** (`student_transportation_mode`, select_one, required): Choose the mode of transportation the student will use (bus, walk, bike).
* **Student Transportation Vehicle Type** (`student_transportation_vehicle_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the type(s) of vehicle the student will use (bus, car, taxi).
* **Student Transportation Notes** (`student_transportation_notes`, note, optional): Add any relevant notes about the student's transportation needs.
* **Student Transportation Email** (`student_transportation_email`, email, required): Enter the student's email address.
* **Student Transportation Phone** (`student_transportation_phone`, text, required): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Choose the tool assigned to the student (chatjimmy, other).
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, optional): Enter a unique identifier for the form entry (not required for most users).
* **Category** (`category`, text, optional): Enter the category for the form entry (not required for most users).
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Enter a description for the form entry (not required for most users).
* **Output File** (`output_file`, text, optional): Enter a file name for the output file generated by this form (not required for most users).
