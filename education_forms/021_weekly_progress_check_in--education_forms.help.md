# Weekly Progress Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Weekly Progress Check In" form is designed to provide learners with a structured way to report on their progress over the past week, allowing instructors to track and monitor their development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant fields for your progress report.
2. Answer each question or select an option that best describes your progress.
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback you feel are relevant to your progress.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Weekly Progress**: This section is a note field where you can provide a brief summary of your progress over the past week.
* **Goals Achieved**: Choose the goals you've achieved from the options provided (Coaching, Training, Course, or other relevant goals).
* **Participation**: Enter the number of sessions you've participated in over the past week.
* **Progress Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about your progress.
* **Progress Ratings**: Select multiple options to rate your progress (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Feedback**: Add any feedback you'd like to share with your instructor or facilitator.
* **Progress Status**: Choose the status of your progress (Active, Inactive, or Suspended).
* **Next Steps**: Describe the next steps you plan to take or need help with.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or observations you have about your progress.
* **Progress Date**: Date of your last progress update.
