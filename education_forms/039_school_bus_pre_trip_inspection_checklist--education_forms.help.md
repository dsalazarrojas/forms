# School Bus Pre-Trip Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be a quick and easy reference guide for pre-trip inspections of school buses. It helps drivers and administrators ensure that each bus is in good working condition and meets all necessary safety and regulatory requirements.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Review each section carefully and answer every question truthfully.
2. Check all boxes and enter relevant information as needed.
3. Make sure to sign and date the form when complete.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Bus Condition
* **Bus Condition** (required): Check the overall condition of the bus. This includes any dents, scratches, or damage to the body or wheels.
* **Tires and Wheels** (required): Check the condition and air pressure of the tires, as well as the wheel nuts and brakes.
* **Brakes and Suspension** (required): Check the condition and functionality of the brakes, including the brake pads and lines.
* **Electrical Systems** (required): Check the lights, signals, and electrical components to ensure they are all functioning properly.
* **Lights and Signals** (required): Check the condition and functionality of the lights, including headlights, taillights, and interior lights.
* **Driver and Passenger Seats** (required): Check the condition and comfort of the driver and passenger seats.
* **Mirrors and Wipers** (required): Check the condition and functionality of the mirrors and wipers.
* **First Aid Kit** (required): Check that a first aid kit is on board and easily accessible.
* **Emergency Equipment** (required): Check the condition and functionality of emergency equipment, such as fire extinguishers and emergency blankets.
* **Fire Extinguisher** (required): Check that a fire extinguisher is on board and easily accessible. Choose from: Fire Extinguisher, No Fire Extinguisher.
* **Emergency Phone Number** (required): Enter the emergency phone number of the school or transportation company.
* **Vehicle Id Number** (required): Enter the vehicle identification number of the bus.
* **Insurance Card** (required): Check that the insurance card is on board and easily accessible.
* **Vehicle Registration** (required): Check that the vehicle registration is on board and easily accessible.
* **Bus Driver License** (required): Check that the bus driver's license is on board and easily accessible.
* **Vehicle License Plate Number** (required): Enter the vehicle license plate number.
* **Vehicle Insurance Card** (required): Check that the vehicle insurance card is on board and easily accessible.
* **Vehicle Weight Capacity** (required): Enter the vehicle's weight capacity.
* **Bus Type** (required): Choose from: Small Bus, Large Bus, Minibus.
* **School Name** (required): Enter the name of the school or transportation company.
* **Emergency Phone Number** (required): Enter the emergency phone number of the school or transportation company.
