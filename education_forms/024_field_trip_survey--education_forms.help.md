# field_trip_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from students about their field trip experiences. It will help to evaluate the success of the event, identify areas of improvement, and suggest ideas for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form for each field trip separately.
2. For each field trip, provide your answers in the designated fields.
3. Answer all questions truthfully, and provide detailed comments where necessary.
4. Make sure to select a rating for each question, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Organizer** (Event Organizer): Enter the name of the person who organized the event.
* **Field Trip Date** (Field Trip Date): Enter the date of the field trip.
* **Student Name** (Student Name): Enter your name as a student.
* **What happened on the field trip?** (What happened on the field trip?): Describe the event that took place during the field trip.
* **How would you rate the field trip?** (How would you rate the field trip?): Rate the event on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Comments** (Comments): Add any additional comments about the event.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (Phone): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Event Location** (Event Location): Enter the location of the event.
* **Trip Cost** (Trip Cost): Enter the cost of the trip (if applicable).
* **Would you recommend this field trip?** (Would you recommend this field trip?): Rate the event on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Comments** (Comments): Add any additional comments about the event.
* **How likely are you to recommend this event to others?** (How likely are you to recommend this event to others?): Rate the event on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Any other comments about the event?** (Any other comments about the event?): Add any additional comments about the event.
* **Did you attend this event?** (Did you attend this event?): Select yes or no.
* **How long was the field trip?** (How long was the field trip?): Enter the duration of the event.
* **How long would you like the field trips to be?** (How long would you like the field trips to be?): Enter the desired duration of future field trips.
* **How often would you like to go on field trips like this?** (How often would you like to go on field trips like this?): Select how often you would like to attend field trips.
* **What type of field trip would you like to have next?** (What type of field trip would you like to have next?): Select the type of event.
* **Where would you like to have a field trip?** (Where would you like to have a field trip?): Select the location of the event.
* **Any other event that you think would make this field trip better?** (Any other event that you think would make this field trip better?): Add any suggestions for future events.
* **Other event ideas?** (Other event ideas?): Add any other ideas for future events.
* **Teacher Name** (Teacher Name): Enter the name of the teacher that organized the event.
* **Teacher Notes** (Teacher Notes): Add any notes about the teacher.
* **Comments** (Comments): Add any additional comments about the event.
