# Yes Or No Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Yes Or No Survey Template is a simple form for collecting user responses to a series of questions. It is intended for users to provide yes or no answers to the questions listed below.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title and description for the survey.
2. Answer the questions listed in the format of "yes" or "no".
3. Save the form by selecting "Submit" or "Cancel" depending on your preference.
4. You can save responses for later by selecting the save responses options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Title** (`survey_title`, text, required: false): Enter the title of the survey, such as "Survey for User Feedback".
* **Survey Description** (`survey_description`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the survey.
* **Survey Questions** (`survey_questions`, text, required: false): Enter yes or no responses to the questions listed below.
* **Save Response** (`save_response`, select_one, required: false): Select "Yes" or "No" to save the response.
* **Submit Button** (`submit_button`, select_one, required: false): Select "Submit" to complete the form.
* **Date of Survey** (`date_survey`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the survey.
* **Time of Survey** (`time_survey`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the survey.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required: false): Enter any contact information for follow-up.
* **Save Response 2** (`save_response_2`, select_one, required: false): Select "Yes" or "No" to save the response.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter a phone number for contact.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Save Response 3** (`save_response_3`, select_one, required: false): Select "Yes" or "No" to save the response.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields if you are saving a response.
* You can save responses for later by selecting "Yes" on the save response options.
* Use the form for collecting data on specific topics or events.
