# Education History Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and document a user's education history. This includes information about their education level, school attended, program of study, degree earned, graduate field, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by scrolling to the first page of the form, where you will find fields to input your education level, school attended, and program of study. Please select the correct education level and enter the name of the school you attended and the program of study you pursued.
2. On the second page, you will find fields for uploading documents and information. You can upload certificates, transcripts, and other relevant documents to support your education history. Please note that each field has a specific label indicating what type of document it corresponds to.
3. Ensure that you fill out all required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form. This will ensure that your data is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, required): Select the highest level of education you have completed (e.g., high school, bachelor's degree, master's degree).
* **Name** (`name`, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **School Attended** (`school_attended`, optional): Enter the name of the school you attended.
* **Program Of Study** (`program_of_study`, required): Enter the name of the program of study you pursued.
* **Degree Earned** (`degree_earned`, optional): Select the degree you earned (e.g., bachelor's, master's, Ph.D.).
* **Graduate Field** (`graduate_field`, optional): Select the field of graduate study you are in or interested in.
* **Upload Document** (`upload_document`, optional): Upload a document that supports your education history.
* **Upload Certificate** (`upload_certificate`, optional): Upload a certificate related to your education.
* **Upload Transcript** (`upload_transcript`, optional): Upload a transcript from your educational institution.
* **Upload Other Document** (`upload_other_document`, optional): Upload any other relevant document.
* **Upload Other Certificate** (`upload_other_certificate`, optional): Upload any other certificate related to your education.
* **Upload Other Transcript** (`upload_other_transcript`, optional): Upload any other transcript from your educational institution.
* **Upload Additional Document** (`upload_additional_document`, optional): Upload any additional document that supports your education history.
* **Upload Additional Certificate** (`upload_additional_certificate`, optional): Upload any additional certificate related to your education.
* **Upload Additional Transcript** (`upload_additional_transcript`, optional): Upload any additional transcript from your educational institution.
* **Upload More Info** (`upload_more_info`, optional): Upload any additional information about your education history.
* **Other School Attended** (`other_school_attended`, optional): Enter the name of another school you attended.
* **Other Program Of Study** (`other_program_of_study`, optional): Enter the name of another program of study you pursued.
* **Other Degree Earned** (`other_degree_earned`, optional): Enter the degree you earned from another institution.
* **Upload Other Document 2** (`upload_other_document_2`, optional): Upload any other relevant document.
* **Upload Other Certificate 2** (`upload_other_certificate_2`, optional): Upload any other certificate related to your education.
* **Upload Other Transcript 2** (`upload_other_transcript_2`, optional): Upload any other transcript from your educational institution.
* **Upload Other Info** (`upload_other_info`, optional): Upload any other additional information about your education history.
