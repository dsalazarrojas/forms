# digital_learning_platform_beta_application - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "digital learning platform beta application" form is used to gather information from applicants interested in participating in the beta version of our digital learning platform. This form is designed to collect contact and educational background information to help us tailor the platform to meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required information, and optionally, provide additional details as you see fit.
2. Make sure to review your submission carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your full name to help us get to know you better.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Provide your email address so we can contact you about the platform and any updates.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required: false): If you have a preferred contact number, please enter it here. This will help us reach you in case we need to discuss anything.
* **Education** (`education`, `text`, required: false): Share your educational background with us to help us better understand your needs.
* **Motivation** (`motivation`, `text`, required: false): Let us know what motivates you to use our digital learning platform and how it can benefit you.
* **Devices** (`devices`, `text`, required: false): If you have any devices that you use to access the platform, please let us know.
* **Consent** (`consent`, `note`, required: false): Check this box to indicate that you have read and understand the terms of service and that you give us permission to contact you about the platform.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information to help us better serve you.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We appreciate your interest in our digital learning platform and look forward to working with you.
