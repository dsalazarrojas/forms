# erasmus_plus_evaluation_questionnaire_youth_exchange_and_mobility_of_youth_workers - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and evaluate information about youth exchange and mobility programs. It helps program organizers track participant demographics, travel details, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the exchange type from the options provided in the "Exchange Type" field.
2. Fill in the participant's educational objective(s) from the options provided in the "Exchange Educational Objective" field.
3. Enter the participant's age in the "Participant Age" field.
4. Select the participant's country from the options provided in the "Participant Country" field.
5. Choose the participant's language from the options provided in the "Participant Language" field.
6. Enter any additional information about the participant in the "Participant Additional Info" field.
7. Fill in the exchange organizer's and exchange coordinator's information in the "Exchange Organizer" and "Exchange Coordinator" fields, respectively.
8. Enter the exchange location type(s) from the options provided in the "Exchange Location Type" field.
9. Provide travel information for the participant in the "Participant Travel Info" field.
10. Enter the participant's travel cost in the "Participant Travel Cost" field.
11. Choose the participant's travel arrangement from the options provided in the "Participant Travel Arrangement" field.
12. Enter the participant's housing information in the "Participant Housing" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Exchange Participants** (1) (`number`, not required): This field is not used in this form example.
