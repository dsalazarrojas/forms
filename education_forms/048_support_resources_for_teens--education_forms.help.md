# Support Resources For Teens - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from teenagers who need support resources. The form gathers personal details and support requirements to help identify the right resources for each individual.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the family member's name who is in need of support.
2. Enter the school name where the individual is or was enrolled.
3. Select the type of support resource required (e.g. Counseling, Mentoring, Job Training, Academic Support, Family Support).
4. Enter an email address and phone number for contact.
5. Select a date and time for the support session.
6. Provide any additional notes regarding the support session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Family Member Name** (text): Enter the name of the family member who needs support.
* **School Name** (text): Enter the name of the school where the individual is or was enrolled.
* **School 2** (text): This is an optional field to enter additional school information.
* **What kind of support resource do you need?** (select_one): Select one of the following options:
	+ Counseling
	+ Mentoring
	+ Job Training
	+ Academic Support
	+ Family Support
* **What kind of support resource do you need 2?** (select_one): Select one of the following options:
	+ Counseling
	+ Mentoring
	+ Job Training
	+ Academic Support
	+ Family Support
* **Email 1** (email): Enter a valid email address for contact.
* **Phone 1** (text): Enter a phone number for contact.
* **Email 2** (email): This is an optional field to enter additional email information.
* **Phone 2** (text): This is an optional field to enter a phone number.
* **Date** (date): Select the date for the support session.
* **Time** (time): Select the time for the support session.
* **Notes** (note): Provide any additional notes regarding the support session.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information entered is accurate and complete.
* If you need help with any field, ask a support staff member for assistance.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at the support email address.
