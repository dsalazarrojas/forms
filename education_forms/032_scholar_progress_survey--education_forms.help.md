# Scholar Progress Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form, "Scholar Progress Survey," is designed to gather information about a student's academic progress. It is intended for educational institutions to assess the strengths and areas of improvement for students, as well as gather recommendations from teachers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Select the Academic Period**: From the dropdown menu, choose the academic period that the student is currently in or has recently completed.
2. **Rate Academic Performance**: If applicable, select the overall rating of the student's academic performance using the "select_multiple" options provided.
3. **List Student Strengths**: In the text field, provide a list of the student's strengths or notable qualities.
4. **List Student Goals**: In the text field, list the student's current or short-term goals.
5. **List Teacher Recommendations**: In the text field, provide any recommendations or feedback from the student's teachers.
6. **List Additional Comments or Recommendations**: In the text field, provide any additional comments or recommendations for the student's improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **selected_academic_period** (select_one): This field allows you to select the academic period the student is currently in or has recently completed. Select one of the available options: January, February, March, April, May, or June.
* **overall_academic_rating** (select_multiple): If applicable, use this field to rate the student's overall academic performance. Select one or more of the options: Excellent, Satisfactory, or Needs Improvement.
* **student_strengths** (text): List the student's notable qualities or strengths in this field.
* **student_goals** (text): List the student's current or short-term goals in this field.
* **teacher_recommendations** (text): Provide any recommendations or feedback from the student's teachers in this field.
* **student_recommendations** (text): List any additional comments or recommendations for the student's improvement in this field.
