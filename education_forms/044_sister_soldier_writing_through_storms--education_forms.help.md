# Sister, Soldier: Writing Through Storms - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect user experiences and preferences on writing, particularly on lecturing and application methods.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, please follow the instructions on each page:

1. The form consists of three pages: introducing yourself, providing information on your lecturing method, and selecting applications.
2. Please fill out each page according to the questions asked.
3. The form is not strictly structured, allowing you to move between pages as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What makes you tick**: This field is a text-based question where you can describe what motivates and interests you in writing. 
* **How do you lecture**: Here, you can provide information about your approach to lecturing, such as your teaching methods, materials, or any specific strategies you use. 
* **Collect applications**: In this question, you can choose from three options: Online Application, Offline Application, or Both, depending on your experience and preferences. 

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have read the form carefully before filling it out.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the form creators for assistance.
- Remember that the form's purpose is to gather user insights and preferences on writing experiences, so provide honest and accurate information.
