# e-learning-subscription-refund-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The e-learning-subscription-refund-form is used to request a refund for your e-learning subscription. Please use this form to provide necessary details for your refund request.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Ensure that you have already tried to contact customer support before submitting the form.
- If you have any relevant documents or evidence supporting your refund request, attach them to this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`user_information`, text, required): Please enter your full name, email address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Subscription Information** (`subscription_details`, text, required): Please provide information about your subscription, including your subscription type and subscription start date.
* **Refund Amount** (`refund_amount`, number, required): Please enter the amount you are requesting for refund.
* **Reason for Refund** (`reason_for_refund`, text, required): Please clearly and concisely explain the reason for your refund request.
* **Refund Reason** (`refund_reason`, text, required): This field is meant to provide additional explanation for your refund request.
* **Date of Refund** (`date_of_refund`, date, required): Please enter the date you would like to receive your refund.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Ensure that you attach any supporting evidence or documents with your refund request.
- You will receive an email with further instructions once your refund request has been processed.
