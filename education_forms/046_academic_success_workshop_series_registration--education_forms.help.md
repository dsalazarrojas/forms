# Academic Success Workshop Series Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register for the Academic Success Workshop Series, a program designed to support students and faculty in achieving academic success. By completing this form, you will be able to choose from various workshop series, select your preferred format and time slot, and provide essential contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your **Participant Name** and **Email Address** to allow us to contact you for workshop communications.
2.  If applicable, enter your **Student ID** to help us track your registration.
3.  Select your **Academic Level**, which is your current level of study (e.g., Freshman undergraduate, Graduate student).
4.  Choose your **Academic Program or Department** to help us understand your affiliation.
5.  Select which **Workshop Series** you are interested in (e.g., Study Skills Mastery, Time Management).
6.  Specify the **Specific Topics of Interest** within the selected series.
7.  Choose your **Available Days** for attending workshops (Monday to Sunday).
8.  Select your preferred **Available Time Slot** (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Flexible, or Weekends only).
9.  Choose your preferred **Workshop Format** (In-person, Virtual, Hybrid, No preference, or Depends on topic).
10.  If applicable, indicate whether you have **Previous Workshop Participation** (Yes, multiple times, Yes, once or twice, No, but interested, or No, and not interested).
11.  Enter your **Learning Goals** for attending the workshops.
12.  If you require special **Accommodation Needs** or have **Accessibility Requirements**, please specify.
13.  Provide the **Emergency Contact Name** and **Emergency Contact Phone** for emergency purposes.
