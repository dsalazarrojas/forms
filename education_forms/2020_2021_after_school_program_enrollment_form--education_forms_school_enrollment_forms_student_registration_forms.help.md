# 2020 2021 After School Program Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to enroll your child in the 2020-2021 After School Program. Please complete this form to provide information necessary for the program staff to understand your child's needs and ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for them.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each section carefully and answer all questions to the best of your ability.
2. Ensure you provide complete and accurate information for each field.
3. Use the available help text in each field to guide you through the process.
4. Submit the completed form to the program staff.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student first name**: Enter the child's legal first name as it appears on school records.
* **Student last name**: Enter the child's legal last name as it appears on school records.
* **Date of birth**: Enter the child's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Current age**: Enter the child's age as of today.
* **Grade level**: Select the current grade level for the upcoming school year.
* **Current school name**: Enter the name of the child's current school.
* **Primary parent/guardian full name**: Enter the full name of the primary parent or guardian.
* **Relationship to student**: Select the relationship between the parent/guardian and the child.
* **Parent/guardian email address**: Enter a valid email address for the primary parent or guardian.
* **Primary contact phone number**: Enter a valid phone number for the primary parent or guardian.
* **Secondary parent/guardian name**: Enter the full name of the secondary parent or guardian, if applicable.
* **Secondary contact phone number**: Enter a valid phone number for the secondary parent or guardian, if applicable.
* **Emergency contact full name**: Enter the full name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency contact phone number**: Enter a valid phone number for the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship to student**: Select the relationship between the emergency contact person and the child.
* **Medical conditions, allergies, or special health needs**: List any medical conditions, allergies, or special health needs the child may have.
* **Current medications**: List any medications the child is currently taking, including dosage and timing instructions if applicable.
* **Days attending**: Select all the days of the week the child will participate in the program.
* **Areas of interest**: Select all the areas of interest for the child, such as homework help, arts and crafts, etc.
* **Transportation arrangement**: Select how the child will leave the program each day.
* **Adults authorized to pick up**: List the full names of all authorized adults who can pick up the child, besides the parents or guardians.
* **Photo release consent**: Select whether or not to allow the program to use photos of the child in newsletters and promotional materials.
* **Special accommodations or learning needs**: List any special accommodations or learning needs the child may have, such as an IEP or 504 plan.
* **Additional comments or information**: Enter any additional comments or information the program staff may need to know about the child.
