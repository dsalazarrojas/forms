# Teaching Reference Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and relevant details about users who need teaching references. It is meant to be a starting point for further discussion or action.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your contact information and relevant details about yourself. You can add any other information you feel is necessary in the "Other" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): A field where you can provide any information you feel is relevant to your situation.
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): A field where you can enter your contact information, such as your email address or phone number.
- **References** (`references`, text, optional): A field where you can list any academic, professional, or other references that are relevant to your situation.
- **User Education** (`user_education`, text, optional): A field where you can provide details about your educational background.
- **User Experience** (`user_experience`, text, optional): A field where you can provide details about your relevant work experience.
- **User Employment** (`user_employment`, text, optional): A field where you can provide information about your current or past employment.
- **Other** (`user_other`, text, optional): A field where you can provide any other relevant information.
- **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): A button to submit your form for review.

## Tips
Please fill in the required fields, and provide any other information you feel is necessary in the "Other" field. If you need any further assistance, please do not hesitate to contact us.
