# icssc_rcf_student - Help Guide
## Purpose
The icssc_rcf_student form is designed to collect and update student information for accurate and efficient processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
* Fill out each field as required, ensuring accurate and complete information.
* Review and ensure that all fields are filled in correctly before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **student_info** (`student_info`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the student, e.g., "Student ID: 123456".
* **name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter the student's name as it is officially registered.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the student's email address for official communication.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the student's phone number for direct contact.
* **birth_date** (`birth_date`, date, optional): Enter the student's birth date in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the student's mailing address for official correspondence.
* **address_2** (`address_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional address information, e.g., "apt or floor number" or "city, state, zip".

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check your entries for spelling and format errors.
* Submit the form only when you are certain that all information is correct and complete.
