# plant_based_innovation_lab_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The plant_based_innovation_lab_application_form is designed to capture essential details from potential participants in a plant-based innovation lab, aiding the evaluation and management of applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your relevant work or research experience in plant-based innovation, if applicable.
2. Clearly state your motivation for participating in the lab.
3. Indicate your availability and commitment to the project duration.
4. Fill in your contact information, including a valid phone number and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Experience** (Experience, text, required/optional): Describe your relevant work or research experience in plant-based innovation. If you have none, please explain how you think your skills may still be beneficial to the lab.
* **Motivation** (Motivation, text, required): State your reason for participating in the lab, such as interest in a specific area of plant-based research or desire to contribute to the lab's goals.
* **Availability** (Availability, text, required/optional): Indicate your availability and commitment to the project duration. If you have any conflicts or limitations, please explain how you plan to manage them.
* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, number, required): Enter a valid phone number and email address for communication purposes.
