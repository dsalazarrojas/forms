# Comprehensive Exam Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registering for comprehensive exams, which are designed to assess students' knowledge and skills in specific subjects. To register for an exam, fill out this form with accurate and complete information. Please note that this form is only for students who have been admitted to the exam.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all required information and documents ready before starting.
2. Fill out each field with accurate and complete information.
3. Review your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration ID** (`registration_id`): Unique registration identifier.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`): Date of registration.
* **Student Full Name** (`student_full_name`): Legal name as it appears on ID.
* **Student ID Number** (`student_id`): Student identification number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Your birth date.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`): Your email for exam notifications.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Best contact number.
* **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`): Complete mailing address.
* **City** (`city`): City.
* **State or Province** (`state_province`): State or province.
* **Postal Code** (`postal_code`): ZIP or postal code.
* **Country** (`country`): Country of residence.
* **Exam Name** (`exam_name`): Select the exam you are registering for.
* **Exam Level** (`exam_level`): Select exam level.
* **Preferred Exam Date** (`preferred_exam_date`): Select your preferred date.
* **Preferred Exam Time** (`preferred_exam_time`): Select preferred time.
* **Exam Location Preference** (`exam_location`): Where would you like to take the exam.
* **Special Accommodations Needed** (`special_accommodations`): Do you require any accommodations?
* **Accommodations Documentation** (`accommodations_documentation`): Have you submitted documentation for accommodations?
* **Previous Exam Attempts** (`previous_exam_attempts`): How many times have you taken this exam?
* **Preparation Course Completed** (`preparation_course_completed`): Did you complete a prep course?
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): How will you pay the exam fee.
* **Exam Fee Paid** (`exam_fee_paid`): Has the exam fee been paid?
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`): Name of emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`): Emergency contact phone number.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`): I agree to follow all exam rules and regulations.
* **Academic Integrity Pledge** (`academic_integrity_pledge`): I pledge to maintain academic integrity during the exam.
* **Registration Status** (`registration_status`): Current registration status.
