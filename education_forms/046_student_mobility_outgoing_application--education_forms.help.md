This form is used for students to submit an application for outgoing mobility, which is a process used to facilitate academic exchange between higher education institutions in the same or different countries. Outgoing mobility is essential for students to enhance their academic and professional experience, and this form is a crucial step in the process. To ensure that the form is completed correctly, it is essential to follow the instructions carefully.

## Student Mobility Outgoing Application - Help Guide
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information ready before starting the application process.
2. Fill in your Student Id (page 1).
3. Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents (page 2).
4. Provide your valid email address (page 3).
5. Enter your phone number (page 4).
6. Select the type of application you are submitting (page 5).
7. Enter the host institution's name (page 6).
8. Select the type of mobility you are applying for (page 7).
9. Choose the status of your application (page 8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Id** (`student_id`, text, required: false): Please enter your student ID number as it appears on your student card or student ID card.
* **full_name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **email** (`email`, text, required: false): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for further communication.
* **phone_number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number where we can contact you for further information.
* **application_type** (`application_type`, select_one, options: 1-8, required: false): Select the type of application you are submitting. Examples include: '1' for undergraduate, '2' for post-graduate, '3' for research, etc.
* **host_institution** (`host_institution`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the host institution where you will be studying or working.
* **mobility_type** (`mobility_type`, select_one, options: 1-4, required: false): Select the type of mobility you are applying for. Examples include: '1' for academic, '2' for professional, '3' for research, etc.
* **application_status** (`application_status`, select_one, options: 1-4, required: false): Choose the status of your application. Examples include: '1' for pending, '2' for approved, '3' for rejected, etc.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all information is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you encounter any issues while completing the form, please contact us for assistance.
* We will review your application carefully and respond to you within a reasonable timeframe.
* Please note that incomplete or incorrect applications may delay or even cancel the mobility process.
