# reading_recording_submission_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to record and store information about student's readings, including the student's ID, name, reading assignment, recording URL, and other relevant details. This form is used to track student's progress and provide feedback from teachers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's ID.
2. Enter the student's name.
3. Select the reading assignment ID.
4. Provide the recording URL.
5. If applicable, enter the teacher's name, comments, and rating.
6. Enter the reading level (beginner, intermediate, or advanced).
7. Enter the submission date, completion date, and review date.
8. Set the submission status to "submitted".
9. Provide additional comments and recording notes if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **student_id**: A unique identifier for the student. This field is required.
* **student_name**: The name of the student. This field is required.
* **reading_assignment_id**: The ID of the reading assignment. This field is required.
* **recording_url**: The URL of the recording. This field is required.
* **teacher_name**: The name of the teacher. This field is optional.
* **comments**: Additional comments from the teacher. This field is optional.
* **reading_level**: The level of the student's reading (beginner, intermediate, or advanced). This field is optional.
* **teacher_notes**: Notes from the teacher. This field is optional.
* **reading_date**: The date of the reading. This field is optional.
* **reading_time**: The time of the reading. This field is optional.
* **teacher_email**: The email of the teacher. This field is optional.
* **teacher_phone**: The phone number of the teacher. This field is optional.
* **student_email**: The email of the student. This field is optional.
* **form_submitted**: The date and time when the form was submitted. This field is required.
* **form_completed**: The date and time when the form was completed. This field is required.
* **form_reviewed**: The date and time when the form was reviewed. This field is required.
* **submission_status**: The status of the submission (submitted, reviewed). This field is required.
* **teacher_rating**: The rating given by the teacher (excellent, good, fair). This field is optional.
* **student_rating**: The rating given by the student (excellent, good, fair). This field is optional.
* **comments_from_teacher**: Comments from the teacher. This field is optional.
* **comments_from_student**: Comments from the student. This field is optional.
* **submission_comments**: Additional comments about the submission. This field is optional.
* **recording_notes**: Notes about the recording. This field is optional.
* **additional_comments**: Additional comments. This field is optional.
* **submission_date**: The date of the submission. This field is optional.

**Tips**

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Use the correct format for dates (DD/MM/YY).
* If applicable, provide feedback and comments for the teacher and student.
* Save the form to track student's progress and provide a record of submissions and reviews.
