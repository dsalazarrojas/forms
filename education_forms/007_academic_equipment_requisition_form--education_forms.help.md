# Academic Equipment Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Equipment Requisition Form is used to collect information about equipment requests made by department members. This form is designed to gather necessary details about the equipment being requested, such as its name, quantity, and justification for the request.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have a registered account to access this form.
2. Check if the equipment you're requesting is already available in the system or if a similar request has been made recently.
3. Fill in the form completely and accurately, making sure to provide all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester Information** (`Requester Information`, no, required no): This section is a placeholder, but it usually contains a brief introduction to the form.
* **Requester name** (Requester name, required, true): Enter the full name of the person making the request.
* **Requester email** (Requester email, required, true): Enter the email address associated with the person making the request, for correspondence and approval purposes.
* **Department or unit** (Department or unit, required, true): Enter the department or unit requesting the equipment.
* **Equipment name or description** (Equipment name or description, required, true): Enter a brief description or the name of the equipment being requested.
* **Quantity requested** (Quantity requested, required, true): Enter the number of units of the equipment being requested.
* **Justification for request** (Justification for request, required, true): Enter a detailed description of the purpose and expected use of the equipment.
* **Estimated cost** (Estimated cost, no, true): Enter the estimated cost of the equipment in local currency. If not required, you can leave this field blank.
* **Proposed funding source** (Proposed funding source, no, true): Select the proposed funding source (e.g., Department budget, Grant funding, External donation, or Other).
* **Approver name or signature** (Approver name or signature, no, true): If an approver has already approved this request, enter their name or a digital signature. If not required, you can leave this field blank.
* **Requested delivery date** (Requested delivery date, no, true): Enter the preferred delivery date for the equipment. If not required, you can leave this field blank.

## Tips

* Make sure to review your request carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed accurately.
* If you're unsure about any part of the form, don't hesitate to reach out to your departmental approver for assistance.
* Please note that the approver will review your request based on the provided information, so please ensure accuracy and completeness in your submission.
