# Camping Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about students who will be participating in a camping program, which may be a summer camp or a music camp for kids. This information will be used to manage and personalize the student's experience during the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your student's personal details, including their name, date of birth, and age.
2. Enter your relationship with the student (e.g., parent or guardian).
3. Provide your contact information, including name and phone number.
4. Enter your child's educational background, including their current grade level and academic performance.
5. If your child has musical talent, provide their musical instrument and years of experience, as well as their skill level.
6. Confirm your consent for your child's participation in the camping program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Student Information, text, required): Enter your child's first name.
* **Last Name** (Student Information, text, required): Enter your child's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (Student Information, date, required): Enter your child's date of birth.
* **Age** (Student Information, number, required): Enter your child's age.
* **Email Address** (Student Information, email, optional): Enter your child's email address (if applicable).
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (Parent or Guardian Information, text, required): Enter your name or the name of the parent or guardian.
* **Phone Number** (Parent or Guardian Information, text, required): Enter your phone number or the phone number of the parent or guardian.
* **Email Address** (Parent or Guardian Information, email, required): Enter your email address or the email address of the parent or guardian.
* **Current Grade Level** (Education Information, select_one, required): Choose the current grade level of your child.
* **School Name** (Education Information, text, required): Enter the name of your child's school.
* **Academic Performance** (Education Information, select_one, required): Choose the overall academic performance of your child.
* **Primary Musical Instrument** (Musical Talent Assessment, text, required): Enter the primary musical instrument your child is interested in or skilled in.
* **Years of Musical Experience** (Musical Talent Assessment, number, required): Enter the number of years your child has experience with music.
* **Music Skill Level** (Musical Talent Assessment, select_one, required): Choose the level of musical skill your child has.
* **Other Special Talents or Skills** (Musical Talent Assessment, text, optional): Enter any special talents or skills your child may have (e.g., dance, art, etc.).
* **Known Allergies** (Health and Safety, text, optional): Enter any known allergies your child may have.
* **Medical Conditions** (Health and Safety, text, optional): Enter any medical conditions your child may have.
* **Emergency Contact Name and Phone** (Health and Safety, text, required): Enter your name and phone number in case of an emergency.
* **Parental Consent and Terms** (Parental Consent and Terms, select_one, required): Confirm that you consent to your child's participation in the camping program.

Please take your time and answer each field accurately to provide us with the necessary information to ensure a safe and enjoyable camping experience for your child. 

**Note:** You can ask for help if you need it! Our team is here to assist you with any questions or concerns.
