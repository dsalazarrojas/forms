This form is designed to gather feedback from students on the quality of support resources provided by the institution. The purpose of this form is to help the institution understand what's working well and where improvements can be made. 

To ensure that your feedback is actionable, please only submit a single instance of this form per resource. Duplicate submissions may lead to unnecessary repetition and confusion in our analysis.

## Student Support Resource Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Support Resource Feedback Form is a tool for students to share their thoughts and opinions on the resources they've used for support. By completing this form, you'll help us understand how we can better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Fill in your User Information (page 1)
* Select the Service Name that you're providing feedback on (page 1)
* Write a brief description of your experience in the Feedback field (page 1)
* Rate the Service Quality (page 2)
* Indicate your level of satisfaction with the service (page 2)
* If you have any suggestions for improvement, share them in the Suggestions field (page 2)
* Provide any additional Contact Information or Email if you'd like to be contacted back (page 2)
* Optionally, specify when you submitted your feedback (page 3) and on what Date (page 3)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, `text`, required: false): This field is where you'll provide your basic details such as name and ID number.
* **Service Name** (`service_name`, `text`, required: false): Here, you'll select the service you'd like to provide feedback on.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `note`, required: false): Describe your experience with the service, including any challenges or issues you faced.
* **Service Quality** (`service_quality`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose an option to rate the service's quality - Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Student Satisfaction** (`student_satisfaction`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more of the options that best describe your level of satisfaction with the service - Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Not Satisfied.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, `text`, required: false): If you have any suggestions or improvements, share them here.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): If you'd like to be contacted for more information or follow-up, provide your contact information here.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted via email.
* **Submitted** (`submitted`, `time`, required: false): Optionally, specify the date and time when you submitted your feedback.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date on which you submitted your feedback.
