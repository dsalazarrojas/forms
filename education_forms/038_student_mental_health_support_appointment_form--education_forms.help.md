# Student Mental Health Support Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Mental Health Support Appointment Form is a form designed to collect information about a student's mental health support needs. This form helps students schedule appointments with the appropriate support team members and ensure they receive the necessary support to address their concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all the required fields (indicated by \*\*) to ensure that your appointment is scheduled accurately.
2. Use the provided options for the Support Type and Support Location fields to select the relevant choices that best suit your needs.
3. If you need to add any additional information or comments, please use the Additional Comments field.
4. Review your submission before clicking the submit button to ensure that all fields are filled out correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (Student Name, text, optional): Please enter your full name as it appears on your student ID.
* **Contact E-mail** (Contact E-mail, email, optional): Enter your institutional email address where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Appointment Date** (Appointment Date, date, optional): Select the date you would like to schedule the appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (Appointment Time, time, optional): Select the time slot you prefer for the appointment.
* **Support Type** (Support Type, select_one, optional): Choose the type of support you are seeking (Academic, Career, or Personal).
* **Support Duration** (Support Duration, number, optional): If applicable, select the duration of the support session (e.g., 30 minutes, 1 hour).
* **Support Location** (Support Location, select_multiple, optional): Choose the location where you would like to have the appointment (Counseling Office, Residence Hall, Campus Library, or Student Union).
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, note, optional): Add any additional information or comments you feel are relevant to your appointment.

## Tips
* Please review your submission carefully before submitting the form.
* Make sure to select the correct Support Type and Support Location to ensure you receive the right support. 
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact the support team.
