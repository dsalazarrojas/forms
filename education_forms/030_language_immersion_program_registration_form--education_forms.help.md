# Language Immersion Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in registering for a Language Immersion Program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read the form instructions and guidelines to ensure you provide accurate and truthful information.
2. Select your language ability: 
  * Beginner: You have little to no experience with the language.
  * Intermediate: You have some experience with the language, but it may not be perfect.
  * Advanced: You have a strong command of the language.
3. Choose the languages you want to learn or practice:
  - English
  - Spanish
  - French
4. Select the length of your language program:
  - 3 Months
  - 6 Months
  - 12 Months
5. Enter the desired start date for your program.
6. Enter the desired end date for your program.
7. Enter your name and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Language Ability** (`language_abilities`, select_one, optional): Please select the level of your language ability.
* **Participant Languages** (`participant_languages`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the languages you want to learn or practice.
* **Language Institution** (`language_institution`, text, optional): Please enter the name of the institution where you will be studying.
* **Program Duration** (`program_duration`, select_one, optional): Select the length of your language program.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the desired start date for your program.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the desired end date for your program.
* **Participant Institution** (`participant_institution`, text, optional): Please enter the name of your institution or school.
* **Participant Contact** (`participant_contact`, text, optional): Enter your contact information, including your name and email address.

## Tips
* Be honest about your language ability to ensure you are matched with a suitable language program.
* Choose a program duration that fits your needs and schedule.
* Provide accurate contact information to ensure we can communicate with you about your program.
