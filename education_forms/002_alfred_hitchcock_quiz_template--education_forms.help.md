# Alfred Hitchcock Quiz Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This quiz template is designed to assess the knowledge and understanding of Alfred Hitchcock fans or students about his life and work, specifically his transition from directing silent films to thrillers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name in the `Participant name` field. This is a required field to identify you as the respondent.
2.  Enter your email address in the `Participant email` field. This is an optional field, but it allows us to send you the results of your quiz.
3.  Select the correct answer to the first question: "Which film is known as a silent film director turning to thrillers." Choose from the options provided.
4.  Select the correct release year of the movie mentioned in the second question. Enter the year as a number.
5.  If you feel confident about your knowledge, you can answer the third question about a recurring theme in Hitchcock's films. If you're unsure, feel free to skip this question.
6.  Estimate your score out of 10, if you wish to provide feedback for instructors.
7.  Provide any feedback about the quiz in the `Feedback` field. This is an optional field for any comments you may have.
8.  Select the `Consent` checkbox to agree to have your responses used for teaching analysis. This is a required field to allow us to use your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant name (Required)
*   The `Participant name` field is used to identify the person taking the quiz.

### Participant email (Optional)
*   The `Participant email` field is used to send results of the quiz to the respondent.

### Which film is known as a silent film director turning to thrillers? (Required)
*   This question is designed to test knowledge of Hitchcock's early career as a silent film director and his transition to thrillers.

### What year was Psycho released? (Required)
*   This question is about one of Hitchcock's most famous films.

### Name a recurring theme in Hitchcock films (Optional)
*   This is a short-answer question that asks about a recurring theme in Hitchcock's films. You can provide any thoughts or observations you have about this theme.

### Estimated score (Optional)
*   This field is used to estimate your score based on the questions answered.

### Feedback about the quiz (Optional)
*   This is an optional field to provide any feedback or comments about the quiz.

### Consent to have responses used for teaching analysis
*   This is a required field that allows you to choose whether to allow your responses to be used in education.
