# Student Feedback On Learning Materials Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from students about the learning materials used in their courses. It helps instructors and course designers understand what works well and what needs improvement. Please complete this form to provide valuable insights that can enhance the learning experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Review the form and ensure you understand each field's purpose.
*   Fill in the required information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student Feedback On Learning Materials Evaluation Form** (`student_feedback_on_learning_materials_evaluation_form`, note, optional): 
    This is the title of the form, which is displayed at the top to let you know what you're about to complete. You can leave this field blank if you don't want to provide a title.
*   **Course Materials** (`course_materials`, select_multiple, optional): 
    Select all the materials that apply to your feedback. Please choose one or more of the options.
*   **Course Materials Important** (`course_materials_important`, select_one, optional): 
    Rate how important these materials were for your learning. Choose one option that best describes your experience.
*   **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, optional): 
    Rate your overall satisfaction with the learning materials. Please enter a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is not satisfied at all and 5 is very satisfied.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): 
    Provide any specific comments about the learning materials. Please be as detailed as possible to help us understand your feedback.
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): 
    Provide any additional comments or suggestions about the learning materials. Please be as detailed as possible.
*   **Student Feedback On Learning Materials Evaluation Form Additional Info** (`student_feedback_on_learning_materials_evaluation_form_additional_info`, text, optional): 
    If you have any additional information about your feedback, please share it with us here.
*   **Course Materials Rating** (`course_materials_rating`, number, optional): 
    Rate the learning materials used in your course. Please enter a number from 1 to 5.
*   **Learning Materials** (`learning_materials`, select_multiple, optional): 
    Select all the materials that apply to your feedback.
*   **Learning Materials Important** (`learning_materials_important`, select_one, optional):

*   **Course Materials Additional Info** (`course_materials_additional_info`, text, optional): 
    If you have any additional information about your feedback, please share it with us here.
