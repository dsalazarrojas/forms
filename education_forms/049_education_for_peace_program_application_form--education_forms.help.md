# Education For Peace Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Education For Peace Program Application Form is designed to gather information from individuals interested in participating in educational or professional development initiatives under the Education For Peace program. This form helps the program team assess applicants' qualifications and suitability for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter the name of your affiliated university in the "University Name" field.
6. Describe your motivation for applying to the Education For Peace program in the "Motivation" field.
7. Indicate your availability for the program by providing a date in the "Availability" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your given name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your surname as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter a valid email address that you use regularly.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **University Name** (`university_name`, `text`, optional): If you are affiliated with a university, enter the name of your institution.
* **Motivation** (`motivation`, `text`, optional): Describe your reason for applying to the Education For Peace program, including any relevant experiences or goals.
* **Availability** (`availability`, `date`, optional): Provide a date when you are available to participate in the program.

## Tips
