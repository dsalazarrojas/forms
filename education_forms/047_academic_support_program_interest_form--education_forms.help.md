# Academic Support Program Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Support Program Interest Form is a tool for students to express their interest in various academic support programs, such as tutoring, writing center, math lab, and library research support. This form helps support staff to understand the specific needs and preferences of students, ensuring that they provide relevant and effective support services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the `Student Name` field.
2. Provide your institutional or personal email address in the `Email Address` field.
3. Enter your official student identification number in the `Student ID` field.
4. Select your current academic program or major in the `Academic Program` field.
5. Indicate your current year in the program (e.g., freshman, sophomore, etc.) in the `Academic Year` field.
6. Select the academic support program you are interested in from the list provided in the `Academic Support Program Interest` field.
7. Specify the subject area you need support in the `Specific Subject Area` field (if applicable).
8. Choose how often you would like to receive support from the options in the `Support Frequency Need` field.
9. Select your preferred support format from the options in the `Preferred Support Format` field.
10. If you have availability time slots, provide them in the `Available Time Slots` field (if applicable).
11. Describe your specific academic challenges in the `Academic Challenges` field (if applicable).
12. Indicate whether you have used academic support services before and your level of involvement from the options in the `Previous Support Experience` field.
13. If you have any special accommodation needs, describe them in the `Accommodation Requirements` field (if applicable).
14. Indicate what you hope to achieve through these services in the `Expected Outcomes` field (if applicable).
15. Consent to being contacted by support staff and specify how you would like to be contacted in the `Consent to Contact` field.
16. Provide any additional comments or information about your support needs in the `Additional Comments` field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or official documents.
* **Email Address** (`student_email`, email, required): Provide your institutional or personal email address.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, required): Enter your official student identification number.
* **Academic Program** (`academic_program`, text, required): Select your current academic program or major.
* **Academic Year** (`academic_year`, select_one, required): Indicate your current year in the program (e.g., freshman, sophomore, etc.).
* **Academic Support Program Interest** (`support_program_interest`, select_one, required): Select the academic support program you are interested in (e.g., tutoring, writing center, etc.).
* **Specific Subject Area** (`specific_subject_area`, text, optional): Specify the subject area you need support in (if applicable).
* **Support Frequency Need** (`support_frequency_need`, select_one, required): Choose how often you would like to receive support (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, etc.).
* **Preferred Support Format** (`preferred_support_format`, select_one, required): Select your preferred support format (e.g., one-on-one, group sessions, online/virtual, etc.).
* **Available Time Slots** (`availability_time_slots`, text, optional): If you have specific availability time slots, provide them (if applicable).
* **Academic Challenges** (`academic_challenges`, text, optional): Describe your specific academic challenges (if applicable).
* **Previous Support Experience** (`previous_support_experience`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you have used academic support services before and your level of involvement (e.g., regularly, occasionally, etc.).
* **Accommodation Requirements** (`accommodation_requirements`, text, optional): If you have any special accommodation needs, describe them (if applicable).
* **Expected Outcomes** (`expected_outcomes`, text, optional): Indicate what you hope to achieve through these services (if applicable).
* **Consent to Contact** (`consent_to_contact`, select_one, required): Consent to being contacted by support staff and specify how you would like to be contacted.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or information about your support needs (if applicable).
