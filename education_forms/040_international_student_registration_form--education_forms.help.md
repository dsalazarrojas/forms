# international_student_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from international students for a specific purpose. Please ensure that you have already completed this form, if yes, do not proceed further to avoid duplicate processing of your information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that you have all necessary documents and information ready before proceeding.
2. Select each field carefully, following the instructions provided for each field.
3. Enter accurate and correct information for each field.
4. If a field has an "Other" option, you can select it and provide a brief description in the text field below it (if available).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Student Email**: (`student_email`, email, required): Enter your student email address.
* **Date of Birth**: (`student_dob`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **Country of Origin**: (`country_of_origin`, text, optional): Enter your country of origin if not already an international student.
* **Student Phone**: (`student_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Student Language**: (`student_language`, select_one, optional): If you can understand and communicate in English, select "Yes". Otherwise, select "No" and provide a brief description of the language you can understand and communicate in under `student_language_other`.
* **Language Other (if applicable)**: (`student_language_other`, text, optional): Enter the language you can understand and communicate in if different from English.
* **Language Other (Hint)**: (`student_language_other_hint`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your language skills.
* **Student Gpa**: (`student_gpa`, select_multiple, optional): If you have completed a GPA, select "Yes". If not, select "No" and provide a brief description of your academic background under `student_gpa_other`.
* **Gpa Other (if applicable)**: (`student_gpa_other`, text, optional): Enter your academic background if different from GPA.
* **Gpa Other (Hint)**: (`student_gpa_other_hint`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your academic background.
* **Student Program**: (`student_program`, select_multiple, optional): If you have registered or are interested in registering for a program, select "Yes". If not, select "No" and provide a brief description of your interests under `student_program_other`.
* **Program Other (if applicable)**: (`student_program_other`, text, optional): Enter your program of interest if different from the ones listed.
* **Program Other (Hint)**: (`student_program_other_hint`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your interests.
* **Tuition Fees**: (`student_tuition_fees`, number, required): Enter the tuition fees you are willing to pay or have already paid.
* **Tuition Fees Other (if applicable)**: (`student_tuition_fees_other`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about tuition fees if different from the listed options.
* **Tuition Fees Other (Hint)**: (`student_tuition_fees_other_hint`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your tuition fees information.
* **Student Address**: (`student_address`, time, required): Enter your current address.
* **Student Address Other (if applicable)**: (`student_address_other`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your address if different from the listed options.
* **Student Address Other (Hint)**: (`student_address_other_hint`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your address.
* **Student Country**: (`student_country`, select_multiple, optional): If you have traveled to another country, select "Yes". Otherwise, select "No" and provide a brief description of your travel experience under `student_country_other`.
* **Country Other (if applicable)**: (`student_country_other`, text, optional): Enter your country of origin if different from the ones listed.
* **Country Other (Hint)**: (`student_country_other_hint`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your travel experience.
* **Student Programs**: (`student_programs`, select_multiple, optional): If you have registered or are interested in registering for a program, select "Yes". If not, select "No" and provide a brief description of your interests under `student_programs_other`.
* **Programs Other (if applicable)**: (`student_programs_other`, text, optional): Enter your program of interest if different from the ones listed.
* **Programs Other (Hint)**: (`student_programs_other_hint`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your interests.
