# Digital Empowerment For Educators Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect information about educators who will participate in the program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your personal details.
2. Select your district and training provider.
3. Provide your sign-up date.
4. Share your motivation for participating in the program.
5. Select your skills program and teacher interests.
6. Enter your contact details and additional notes.
7. Enter school information and notes.
8. Enter your address and zip code.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address.
* **School name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter your school's name.
* **District** (`district`, select_one, required): Select your district from the provided options.
* **Training provider** (`training_provider`, select_multiple, required): Select the training provider for your school.
* **Sign-up date** (`sign_up_date`, date, optional): Enter your sign-up date.
* **Motivation** (`motivation`, text, optional): Share your reason for participating in the program.
* **Skills program** (`skills_program`, select_one, required): Select your skills program.
* **Teacher interests** (`teacher_interests`, select_multiple, required): Select your teacher interests.
* **Contact number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter a contact number.
* **Additional notes** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the teacher or school.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Teacher's phone** (`teacher_phone`, text, optional): Enter your teacher's phone number.
* **Teacher's email** (`teacher_email`, email, optional): Enter your teacher's email address.
* **School email** (`school_email`, email, optional): Enter your school's email address.
* **School phone** (`school_phone`, text, optional): Enter your school's phone number.
* **Teacher notes** (`teacher_notes`, note, optional): Add any notes about the teacher.
* **District notes** (`district_notes`, note, optional): Add any notes about the district.
* **School notes** (`school_notes`, note, optional): Add any notes about the school.
* **School address** (`school_address`, text, optional): Enter your school's address.
* **Teacher address** (`teacher_address`, text, optional): Enter your teacher's address.
* **Zip** (`teacher_zip`, text, optional): Enter your teacher's zip code.
* **School zip** (`school_zip`, text, optional): Enter your school's zip code.
* **Assigned tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter the assigned tool.
