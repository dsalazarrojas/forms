# Program Kit Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Program Kit Registration Form" is used to collect information about a participant's kits, shipping details, and other relevant details. It's essential to complete this form accurately to ensure that your kits are shipped correctly and on time.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.
3. Ensure that you've included all necessary fields, as indicated by the required label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details** (participant_details, text, required: false): Please enter your name.
* **Kit Selections** (kit_selections, select_multiple, required: false): Select whether you have one or more kits to register. This field is optional.
* **Shipping Information** (shipping_info, text, required: false): Enter your shipping details, including your address, city, state, zip, and country.
* **Participant Name** (participant_name, text, required: false): Enter your participant name.
* **Participant Email** (participant_email, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Participant Phone** (participant_phone, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Kit 1 Name** (kit_1_name, text, required: false): Enter the name of your first kit, if you selected to register it.
* **Kit 1 Type** (kit_1_type, select_one, required: false): Select the type of your first kit.
* **Kit 2 Name** (kit_2_name, text, required: false): Enter the name of your second kit, if you selected to register it.
* **Kit 2 Type** (kit_2_type, select_one, required: false): Select the type of your second kit.
* **Shipping Address** (shipping_address, text, required: false): Enter your shipping address.
* **Shipping City** (shipping_city, text, required: false): Enter the city for shipping.
* **Shipping State** (shipping_state, text, required: false): Enter the state for shipping.
* **Shipping Zip** (shipping_zip, number, required: false): Enter your zip code for shipping.
* **Shipping Country** (shipping_country, text, required: false): Enter your country for shipping.
* **Participant Note** (participant_note, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes about your participation.
* **Shipping Note** (shipping_note, note, required: false): Enter any additional shipping notes.
* **Participant Program** (participant_program, text, required: false): Enter the name of the program you're participating in.
* **Participant School** (participant_school, text, required: false): Enter the name of your school.
* **Participant Nonprofit** (participant_nonprofit, text, required: false): Enter the name of your non-profit organization.
* **Participant Training Program** (participant_training_program, text, required: false): Enter the name of your training program.
* **Shipping Date** (shipping_date, date, required: false): Enter the date for shipping.
* **Shipping Time** (shipping_time, time, required: false): Enter the time for shipping.
* **Assigned Tool** (assigned_tool, text, required: false): Enter the name of the tool assigned to you.
* **Output File** (output_file, text, required: false): Select the output file.

Tips: 

* Ensure that you've entered the correct shipping address and date to avoid delays in shipping.
* If you have any issues or questions while filling out this form, contact our support team for assistance.
* Please review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid errors.
