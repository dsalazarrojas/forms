# Technology Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to help you complete the Technology Survey form efficiently and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form at your own pace.
- Read each field carefully before selecting an option.
- Use the provided options for select_one fields. If your experience or interest doesn't match any of the options, choose the one that best describes your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. What is your highest level of education completed?** (`education_level`, `select_one`): This question helps us understand your educational background.
	+ Select your highest level of education completed from the provided options (high school, some college, bachelor degree, master degree, doctorate, or other).
* **2. How would you rate your experience with technology at work?** (`technology_experience_workplace`, `select_one`): This question helps us gauge your familiarity with technology in the workplace.
	+ Choose your level of experience from beginner, intermediate, advanced, or expert.
* **3. How would you rate your experience with technology in the classroom?** (`technology_experience_classroom`, `select_one`): This question assesses your level of experience with technology in educational settings.
	+ Select your level of experience from beginner, intermediate, advanced, or expert.
* **4. How important is technology to your job?** (`technology_importance`, `select_one`): This question helps us understand the significance of technology in your work.
	+ Choose how important technology is to your job from not at all, somewhat, neutral, very important.
* **5. What is your favorite technology-related hobby or activity?** (`technology_hobby`, `text`): This question encourages you to share your interests outside of work or class.
	+ Provide a brief description of your favorite technology-related hobby or activity.
* **6. How often do you use technology for personal projects or hobbies?** (`technology_use_personal`, `select_one`): This question helps us understand how you apply technology in your personal life.
	+ Select how often you use technology for personal projects or hobbies from never, occasionally, sometimes, often, or always.
* **7. What is your level of interest in learning more about technology?** (`technology_interest`, `select_one`): This question gauges your interest in expanding your technology knowledge.
	+ Choose your level of interest from not at all, somewhat, neutral, very important.
* **8. Do you prefer to learn through online resources or in-person tutorials?** (`online_resources`, `select_one`): This question helps us understand your learning preferences.
	+ Select your preferred method of learning from online or in-person tutorials.
* **9. How would you rate your experience with online learning platforms?** (`online_experience`, `select_one`): This question evaluates your comfort level with online learning platforms.
	+ Choose your level of experience from beginner, intermediate, advanced, or expert.
* **10. How often do you use online resources for learning?** (`online_use`, `select_one`): This question assesses your frequency of using online resources for learning.
	+ Select how often you use online resources from never, occasionally, sometimes, often, or always.
* **11. What is your preferred language for online learning (e.g., programming languages, languages)?** (`online_language`, `text`): This question helps us understand your language preferences for online learning.
	+ Provide a brief description of your preferred language for online learning.
* **12. Any additional comments or suggestions for improving technology education?** (`technology_comments`, `text`): This question invites you to share any further thoughts or suggestions for technology education.
	+ Share any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
