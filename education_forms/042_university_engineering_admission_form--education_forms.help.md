# University Engineering Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Engineering Admission Form is a comprehensive application form designed to gather essential information from potential students seeking admission into the engineering program. This form is a crucial step in the admission process, and providing accurate and complete information will help the admissions committee make an informed decision.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and ensure you have the necessary information ready.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. For fields with multiple options, select the correct choice that best describes your situation.
4. Double-check your responses for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Information** (`applicant_information`, text, required: false): Please provide a brief introduction about yourself.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required: false): Enter your contact information, including your name, address, and any other relevant contact methods.
* **Academic Background** (`academic_background`, text, required: false): Share your educational background, including any relevant courses or degrees completed.
* **Education Details** (`education_details`, text, required: false): Provide details about your education, including institutions attended and degrees earned.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, required: false): Describe your work experience, highlighting any relevant positions or skills acquired.
* **Upload Documents** (`upload_documents`, text, required: false): Upload any supporting documents required for the application process.
* **Submit Application** (`submit_application`, text, required: false): This is the final step in the process.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or information you'd like to share.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for correspondence.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Provide your phone number for contact.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth.
* **Date of Application** (`date_of_application`, date, required: false): Enter the date you submitted this application.
* **Select One Education** (`select_one_education`, select_one, required: false): Choose the education level you have completed.
* **Select Multiple Education** (`select_multiple_education`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the education levels you have completed.
* **Select One Work Experience** (`select_one_work_experience`, select_one, required: false): Choose your work experience level.
* **Select Multiple Work Experience** (`select_multiple_work_experience`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all your work experience levels.
* **Select One English Proficiency** (`select_one_english_proficiency`, select_one, required: false): Choose your English proficiency level.
* **Select Multiple English Proficiency** (`select_multiple_english_proficiency`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all your English proficiency levels.
* **Select One Reference** (`select_one_references`, select_one, required: false): Choose your reference type.
* **Select Multiple References** (`select_multiple_references`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all your reference types.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of a specific event.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of a specific event.
* **Integer** (`integer`, number, required: false): Enter a whole number value.
* **Decimal** (`decimal`, number, required: false): Enter a decimal value.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or information.
