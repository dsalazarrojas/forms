# Civil Procedure Study Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a study aid for students to assess their knowledge on Civil Procedure concepts, rules, and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Read each question carefully and choose the best answer based on your understanding of the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure (FRCP).
2. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required: true") with your answers.
3. Leave blank any optional fields (marked as "required: false") if you are unsure or do not want to answer them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (Student Name, text, required: true): Fill in your name as it appears on your student ID or official documents.
* **Course Number and Section** (Course ID, text, required: true): Enter the number and section of your course as it appears on your course schedule.
* **Under FRCP 4, how many days does a plaintiff have to serve the summons?** (q1_service_deadline, number, required: true): Choose the number of days a plaintiff has to serve the summons based on your understanding of FRCP 4.
* **What is the minimum amount in controversy for diversity jurisdiction?** (q2_diversity_jurisdiction, number, required: true): Enter the minimum amount in controversy required for diversity jurisdiction.
* **Proper venue is generally determined based on...** (q3_venue_proper, select_one, required: true): Choose where the plaintiff resides, where the defendant resides or events occurred, where the attorney's office is located, or where the nearest federal court is.
* **A motion under Rule 12(b)(6) is for...** (q4_rule_12b6, select_one, required: true): Choose the reason for a motion under Rule 12(b)(6).
* **Under Rule 15, a party may amend its pleading once as a matter of course within...** (q5_amended_pleadings, select_one, required: true): Choose the number of days a party may amend its pleading once as a matter of course.
* **Parties may obtain discovery regarding any nonprivileged matter that is...** (q6_discovery_scope, select_one, required: true): Choose what parties may discover regarding.
* **Unless otherwise stipulated, how many interrogatories are allowed per party?** (q7_interrogatory_limit, number, required: true): Enter the number of interrogatories allowed per party.
* **Rule 20 deals with which of the following?** (q8_joinder_parties, select_one, required: true): Choose the type of joinder that Rule 20 deals with.
* **Did the current study materials adequately cover these questions?** (study_materials_quality, select_one, required: false): Choose whether the current study materials adequately covered the questions.
* **Areas where you need more study help** (feedback_text, text, required: false): Enter any areas where you need more study help.
* **Date** (quiz_date, date, required: true): Enter the date you completed this form.

## Tips

* Please answer each question based on your understanding of the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure (FRCP).
* If you are unsure of an answer, leave the field blank or mark it as "Unknown".
* Review your answers carefully before submitting this form.
* This form is a study aid and not an assessment tool.
