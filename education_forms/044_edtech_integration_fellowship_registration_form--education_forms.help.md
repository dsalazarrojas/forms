# EdTech Integration Fellowship Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The EdTech Integration Fellowship Registration Form is a form designed to collect necessary information from individuals or schools for the EdTech Integration Fellowship program. This form will be reviewed and evaluated by the program's organizers to ensure that applicants meet the program's requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Please fill out the form with your accurate and truthful information.
2.  Ensure all required fields are filled out.
3.  If you are a school representative, please enter your school's information.
4.  Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name. If you are a school representative, enter your school's name instead.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name or your school's last name if you are a school representative.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number. This will be used for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address or your school's address if you are a school representative.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter your city or your school's city if you are a school representative.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter your state or your school's state if you are a school representative.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, optional): Enter your zip code or your school's zip code if you are a school representative.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter your school's name if you are a school representative.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, text, optional): Enter your school's address if you are a school representative.

## Tips

* Please ensure all required fields are filled out.
* If you are a school representative, please enter your school's information.
* Review your information carefully before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the program organizers for clarification.
