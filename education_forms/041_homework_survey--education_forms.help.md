# Homework Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Homework Survey form is designed to collect information from students regarding their homework assignments, enabling educators to track student progress and performance.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Student ID** in the corresponding field. If you don't have a Student ID, please contact your instructor or school administration for assistance.
2. Provide the name of the **Assignment Name** you are submitting.
3. If applicable, enter the grade you received for the assignment.
4. Choose the **Submission Date** when you completed the assignment.
5. If applicable, enter the **Completion Time** it took you to complete the assignment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student ID** (`student_id`, `number`, required: false): This field is used to identify you as a student. If you don't have a Student ID, please contact your instructor or school administration for assistance.
* **Assignment Name** (`assignment_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the assignment you are submitting.
* **Grade** (`grade`, `number`, required: false): If you received a grade for the assignment, enter it here.
* **Submission Date** (`submitted_date`, `date`, required: false): Choose the date you completed and submitted the assignment.
* **Completion Time** (`completion_time`, `time`, required: false): If you can estimate the time it took you to complete the assignment, enter it here.
