# Digital Inclusion In Higher Education Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on students' experiences with digital inclusion in higher education. The goal is to gather data on diverse aspects, including user characteristics and support needs, to inform the development of better digital inclusion programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your User Name.
2. Provide your Email address.
3. Choose your Student Type: Undergraduate, Graduate, or Other.
4. Select your Preferred Language(s): English, Spanish, French, German, or Other.
5. Check your Digital Access: Internet access, Laptop/Computer, Mobile device, or Other.
6. Check your Support Needs: Technical assistance, Software or application support, Online tutoring, or Other.
7. Select your Accommodations: Text-to-speech, Closed captions, Braille, Large print materials, or Other.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Provide your email address.
* **Student Type** (`student_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose your student status: Undergraduate, Graduate, or Other.
* **Preferred Language** (`preferred_language`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the languages you can understand or read. You can choose one or more languages.
* **Digital Access** (`digital_access`, select_multiple, required/optional): Check the digital devices you have access to: Internet, Laptop/Computer, Mobile device, or Other.
* **Support Needs** (`support_needs`, select_multiple, required/optional): Check the areas where you need support: Technical assistance, Software or application support, Online tutoring, or Other.
* **Accommodations** (`accommodations`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select any accommodations you require: Text-to-speech, Closed captions, Braille, Large print materials, or Other.
* **Submit** (`submit`, note, required/optional): This is the submit button, click it to complete the form.
