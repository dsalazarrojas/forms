# Neuroscience Training Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about a training program for neuroscience. Please fill out the form to submit your recommendation and training details.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out the form on a computer or mobile device.
- Select the "Training Coordinator" and provide their name and email address.
- Choose the training program that best fits your recommendation.
- If necessary, provide additional information or comments about the training program.
- Click "Submit" to send the completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Neuroscience Training Form**: Enter the title of the training program being recommended.
* **Training Coordinator**: Enter the name of the person who will be coordinating or managing the training program.
* **Training Coordinator Email**: Enter the email address of the training coordinator.
* **Training Start Date**: Enter the start date of the training program.
* **Training End Date**: Enter the end date of the training program.
* **Training Location**: Enter the location of the training program.
* **Training Program**: Select the training program that you are recommending.
* **Additional Info**: Provide any additional information about the training program, if necessary.
* **Submit**: Click this button to send the completed form.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your recommendation is sent successfully.
- If you have any questions or issues with the form, contact us at form@gic.mx.
