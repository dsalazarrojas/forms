# Principal Resume Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Principal Resume Submission Form is an online application form for educators interested in applying for Principal positions within our school system. This form will gather necessary information from applicants to review and evaluate their qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your School Name in the **School Name** field.
2. Select your position applied for from the **Position Applied For** dropdown list (Principal, Teacher, or Other).
3. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** fields.
4. Enter a valid **Email** address in the format "name@example.com".
5. Upload up to 5 **Resume Attachments** (if applicable).
6. Attach your **Resume** in the designated field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Name** (school_name): Enter the name of the school you are currently or previously affiliated with.
* **Position Applied For** (position_applied_for): Choose the position you are applying for (Principal, Teacher, or Other).
* **First Name** (first_name): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (last_name): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email): Enter a valid email address in the format "name@example.com".
* **Phone** (phone): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Resume Attachments** (resume_attachments): Upload up to 5 relevant attachments, such as a resume, cover letter, or references.
* **Resume** (resume): Attach your resume in a standard format (docx, pdf, etc.).
