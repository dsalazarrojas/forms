# university_valedictorian_selection_voting_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to collect votes for the university valedictorian selection, allowing students to choose one candidate among three options.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select one candidate from the list of options for Candidate 1.
2. Select one candidate from the list of options for Candidate 2.
3. Select one candidate from the list of options for Candidate 3.
4. Enter your email address in the Email field (optional).
5. Enter your Student ID in the Student ID field (optional).
6. Click Submit to cast your vote.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate 1** (`Select One`, required: false): Choose one candidate to vote for. Select one option from the list to indicate your preference.
* **Candidate 2** (`Select One`, required: false): Choose one candidate to vote for. Select one option from the list to indicate your preference.
* **Candidate 3** (`Select One`, required: false): Choose one candidate to vote for. Select one option from the list to indicate your preference.
* **Vote Count** (`Number`, required: false): Enter the number of votes for the selected candidate. This field is optional and should only be filled out if you are sure of the number of votes for your preferred candidate.
* **Vote Count 2** (`Number`, required: false): Enter the number of votes for the selected candidate. This field is optional and should only be filled out if you are sure of the number of votes for your preferred candidate.
* **Vote Count 3** (`Number`, required: false): Enter the number of votes for the selected candidate. This field is optional and should only be filled out if you are sure of the number of votes for your preferred candidate.
* **Email** (`Email`, required: false): Enter your university email address. This is optional but recommended as it helps the organizers contact you in case of any issues with your vote.
* **Student ID** (`Number`, required: false): Enter your Student ID. This is optional and should be filled out if you want to be contacted about your vote, but not required for the voting process.
