# Teacher Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track the attendance of teachers at workshops or events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the name of the workshop in the **Workshop Name** field.
2. Enter the date the workshop took place in the **Date** field, in the correct format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
3. Enter the time the workshop started in the **Time** field, in 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00).
4. Enter the location of the workshop in the **Location** field, with the exact address or name of the venue.
5. Select "Yes" or "No" in the **Attendees** field to indicate if you attended the workshop.
6. (Optional) Add any additional comments or remarks about the workshop in the **Notes** field.
7. Select "Yes" or "No" in the **Synced with App** field to indicate if the workshop was synced with the app.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Teacher's Attendance Form**: This is the form title.
* **Workshop Name**: A text field to enter the name of the workshop. This field is required and should be filled out to accurately record the workshop details.
* **Date**: A date field to enter the date the workshop took place. This field is required and should be filled out in the correct format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Time**: A time field to enter the time the workshop started. This field is required and should be filled out in 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00).
* **Location**: A text field to enter the location of the workshop. This field is required and should be filled out with the exact address or name of the venue.
* **Attendees**: A select multiple field to indicate if you attended the workshop. This field is required and should be filled out with either "Yes" or "No".
* **Notes**: A free-text field to enter any additional comments or remarks about the workshop. This field is optional and can be used to provide more context or information about the workshop.
* **Synced with App**: A select one field to indicate if the workshop was synced with the app. This field is not required but can be useful for tracking app usage.

## Tips
* Fill out each field carefully, as the information gathered will be used for reporting and analysis.
* Make sure to enter the correct format for the date and time fields.
* Be concise and clear when adding any additional comments or remarks in the **Notes** field.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask your supervisor or the event organizer for clarification.
