This form is designed to collect information and feedback from potential faculty members during the interview process. It aims to gather details about faculty information, contact details, evaluations, and feedback comments. The form is likely used for internal evaluation and improvement purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your faculty information in the first section.
2. Share your contact details in the second section.
3. In the third section, rate and evaluate your experience during the interview process.
4. Add any additional comments or feedback in the last section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Faculty Information (faculty_info)**: Enter your name, department, and any other relevant details about your faculty position.
- **Contact Information (contact_info)**: Provide your contact details, including your email and phone number, so that we can get in touch with you if needed.
- **Faculty Evaluations (faculty_evaluations)**: Rate your evaluation of the interview process from 1-5, and add any specific feedback or comments.
- **Interview Process (interview_process)**: Describe the interview process, including any notable events or discussions.
- **Candidate Evaluation (candidate_evaluation)**: Evaluate the candidate you interviewed and provide any feedback or comments.
- **Feedback Comments (feedback_comments)**: Add any additional comments or feedback about the interview experience.
- **Faculty Experience (faculty_experience)**: Share your thoughts about your experience during the interview process.
- **Additional Comments (additional_comments)**: Provide any other comments or suggestions that are not captured in the previous sections.
- **Faculty Interviewer (faculty_interviewer)**: Identify the person who conducted the interview.
