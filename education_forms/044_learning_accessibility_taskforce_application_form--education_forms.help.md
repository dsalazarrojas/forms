# Learning Accessibility Taskforce Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Learning Accessibility Taskforce Application Form is a tool used by the Learning Accessibility Taskforce to collect information from individuals interested in joining the taskforce.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the instructions and guidelines above carefully.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
3. Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and clearances required to work with sensitive information.
4. Submit the form and we will review your application and get back to you if you are selected for further evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **application form** (Application Form, 1, required): This is the main application form where you will be asked to provide your details.
* **name** (Name, 2, optional): Please provide your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **email** (Email, 3, optional): A valid email address is required for communication purposes.
* **phone** (Phone, 4, optional): Please provide your contact phone number.
* **highest level of education** (Highest Level of Education, 5, required): Please select your highest level of education from the options provided.
* **area of interest** (Area of Interest, 6, required): Please select your area of interest from the options provided.
* **years of experience** (Years of Experience, 7, optional): Please provide your years of experience in the field.
* **position** (Position, 8, optional): Please provide your current job position.
* **Years Of Experience 2** (Years Of Experience 2, 9, optional): Please provide your years of experience in the field (if different from above).
* **job title** (Job Title, 10, optional): Please provide your job title.
* **motivation** (Motivation, 11, optional): Please provide a brief statement of your motivation for joining the taskforce.
* **submission** (Submission, 12, optional): Please provide any relevant submission or publication.
* **Signature** (Signature, 13, optional): Please sign and date this form to confirm your application.
* **agreement** (Agreement, 14, optional): Please sign and date this form to confirm your agreement with the terms and conditions.
* **consent** (Consent, 15, optional): Please indicate your consent to participate in the taskforce.
* **date of application** (Date of Application, 16, optional): Please provide the date of application.
* **Name 2** (Name 2, 17, optional): Please provide your name for contact purposes.

* **Phone 2** (Phone 2, 19, optional): Please provide your contact phone number.
* **address** (Address, 20, optional): Please provide your address.
* **city** (City, 21, optional): Please provide your city.
* **state** (State, 22, optional): Please provide your state.
* **zip** (Zip, 23, optional): Please provide your zip code.
* **country** (Country, 24, optional): Please provide your country.
* **Name 2 2** (Name 2 2, 25, optional): Please provide your name for contact purposes.
