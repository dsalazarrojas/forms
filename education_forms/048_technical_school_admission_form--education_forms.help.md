# Technical School Admission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from students and parents for the enrollment process in a technical school.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your student's name, grade level, and contact information.
2. Provide your parents' contact information and their name.
3. List your student's interests and skills.
4. Sign the student declaration and agreement forms as required.
5. Sign the parent signature fields for verification.
6. Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and according to the provided instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter your student's current school name.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_one, optional): Select your student's current grade level.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your student's contact number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your student's email address.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your student's name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your student's date of birth.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, optional): Select your student's gender.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your student's address.
* **Parents Name** (`parents_name`, text, optional): Enter your parent's name.
* **Parents Occupation** (`parents_occupation`, text, optional): Enter your parent's occupation.
* **Student Interests** (`student_interests`, text, optional): List your student's interests.
* **Student Skills** (`student_skills`, text, optional): List your student's skills.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, note, optional): Sign and date the parent signature field for verification.
* **Teacher Signature** (`teacher_signature`, note, optional): Sign and date the teacher's signature field for verification.
* **Student Declaration** (`student_declaration`, text, optional): Sign and date the student's declaration form as required.
* **Student Agreement** (`student_agreement`, text, optional): Sign and date the student's agreement form as required.
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, note, optional): Sign and date the student's signature field for verification.
* **School Name 2** (`school_name2`, text, optional): Enter your student's previous school name.
* **Grade Level 2** (`grade_level2`, select_one, optional): Select your student's previous grade level.
* **Contact Number 2** (`contact_number2`, text, optional): Enter your student's contact number.
* **Email 2** (`email2`, email, optional): Enter your student's email address.
* **Student Name 2** (`student_name2`, text, optional): Enter your student's name.
* **Date of Birth 2** (`date_of_birth2`, date, optional): Enter your student's date of birth.
* **Student Interests 2** (`student_interests2`, text, optional): List your student's interests.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Submit the form after completing all required fields.

Please note that the fields with "2" are intended for listing additional schools or information that may be needed. Ensure that you provide accurate information according to your student's details.
