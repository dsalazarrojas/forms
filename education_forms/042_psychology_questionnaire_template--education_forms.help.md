# psychology_questionnaire_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals regarding their psychological and medical background.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each section of the form, starting with the "Introduction" page.
2. Provide your contact details in the "Contact Details" page.
3. Answer the medical history questions in the "Medical History" page.
4. Provide your mental status and symptoms in the "Mental Status" page.
5. Fill out the treatment history questions in the "Treatment History" page.
6. Answer the remaining questions in the following pages, making sure to choose the correct options for the "Family Medicine" field.
