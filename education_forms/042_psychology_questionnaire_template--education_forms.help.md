# psychology_questionnaire_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals regarding their psychological and medical background.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each section of the form, starting with the "Introduction" page.
2. Provide your contact details in the "Contact Details" page.
3. Answer the medical history questions in the "Medical History" page.
4. Provide your mental status and symptoms in the "Mental Status" page.
5. Fill out the treatment history questions in the "Treatment History" page.
6. Answer the remaining questions in the following pages, making sure to choose the correct options for the "Family Medicine" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is the first page of the form, which serves as an introduction to the questionnaire. You can read it carefully and proceed to the next page.
* **Contact Details**: Enter your contact details, such as your name and email address, in this page.
* **Family Medicine**: Please select "Yes" or "No" to answer this question.

* **Mental Symptoms** (`mental_symptoms_2`): Describe your mental symptoms in this field.
* **Treatment History** (`treatment_history`): Answer the treatment history questions in this page.

* **Medical Tests** (`medical_tests_2`, `medical_tests_3`, `medical_tests_4`, `medical_tests_5`): Describe the medical tests you have undergone in these fields.
