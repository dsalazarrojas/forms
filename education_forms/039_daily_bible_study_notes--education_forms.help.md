# Daily Bible Study Notes - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals record their daily Bible study notes. It includes spaces for recording the date of study, the specific verses or passages read, notes taken, and more.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the date of your study on the "Daily Date" field.
2. Enter the verse or passage you have read on the "Verse" field.
3. Write down any notes or insights you have from the study on the "Note" field.
4. Specify where the study took place on the "Location" field (optional).
5. Check off if you have completed the study on the "Completed" field.
6. If you are integrating with Google Sheets, select the "Integration Type" field.
7. Input your Google Sheet ID on the "Google Sheet ID" field.
8. Add any additional notes in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Daily Date** (date): Enter the date of your Bible study. This will help you keep track of your progress.
* **Verse** (text): Enter the specific verse or passage you have read during your study.
* **Note** (text): Write down any notes, thoughts, or insights from your study.
* **Location** (text): If you'd like, you can specify where you studied, e.g., home, church, etc.
* **Completed** (select_one): Check off if you have completed your study for the day.
* **Integration Type** (select_one): If you're integrating with Google Sheets, select the type of integration you're using.
* **Google Sheet ID** (text): Input your Google Sheet ID if you're integrating with Google Sheets.
* **Notes** (text): Add any additional notes or comments about your study.
* **Integration Type** (select_one): A dropdown to select the type of integration you are using, e.g., None, Google Sheets.
* **Google Sheet ID** (text): A field to input your Google Sheet ID, if applicable.
* **Notes** (text): A field to add any additional notes or comments.
