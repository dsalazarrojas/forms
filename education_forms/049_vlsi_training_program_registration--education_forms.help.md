# VLSI Training Program Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register for the VLSI Training Program. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that your registration is successful.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Choose the institute for which you wish to register.
2.  Select your session preferences (Yes/No) for the VLSI program. Note that this field may include multiple options.
3.  Enter your preferred dates for the session (if applicable).
4.  Enter your preferred times for the session (if applicable).
5.  Enter participant details, providing as much information as required for the session.
6.  Enter your motivation for participating in the VLSI program.
7.  Select your session preferences 2 (Yes/No) (if applicable).
8.  Enter participant details 2, providing as much information as required for the session.
9.  Select your session preferences 3 (Yes/No) (if applicable).
10. Enter participant details 3, providing as much information as required for the session.
11. Select your session preferences 4 (Yes/No) (if applicable).
12. Enter participant details 4, providing as much information as required for the session.
13. Select your session preferences 5 (Yes/No) (if applicable).
14. Enter participant details 5, providing as much information as required for the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Choose Institute** (`institute_select`, select_one, required): Choose the institute for which you wish to register.
*   **Session Preferences** (`session_preferences`, select_multiple, required): Select your session preferences (Yes/No) for the VLSI program.
*   **Session Dates** (`session_dates`, date, required): Enter preferred dates for the session (if applicable).
*   **Session Times** (`session_times`, time, required): Enter preferred times for the session (if applicable).
*   **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required): Enter participant details, providing as much information as required for the session.
*   **Motivations** (`motivations`, text, required): Enter your motivation for participating in the VLSI program.
*   **Session Preferences 2** (`session_preferences_2`, select_multiple, required): Select your session preferences 2 (Yes/No) (if applicable).
*   **Participant Details 2** (`participant_details_2`, text, required): Enter participant details 2, providing as much information as required for the session.
*   **Session Preferences 3** (`session_preferences_3`, select_multiple, required): Select your session preferences 3 (Yes/No) (if applicable).
*   **Participant Details 3** (`participant_details_3`, text, required): Enter participant details 3, providing as much information as required for the session.
*   **Session Preferences 4** (`session_preferences_4`, select_multiple, required): Select your session preferences 4 (Yes/No) (if applicable).
*   **Participant Details 4** (`participant_details_4`, text, required): Enter participant details 4, providing as much information as required for the session.
*   **Session Preferences 5** (`session_preferences_5`, select_multiple, required): Select your session preferences 5 (Yes/No) (if applicable).
*   **Participant Details 5** (`participant_details_5`, text, required): Enter participant details 5, providing as much information as required for the session.

## Tips

*   Ensure you fill out the form accurately and completely to avoid any delays in your registration.
*   If you have any issues or need assistance with the form, please contact the program administrators for support.
