# Form For Pairing French And American Students 2015 American Version - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to pair French and American students for the 2015 program. It gathers information about the American and French students, including their academic levels, language levels, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the First Name field with the American student's first name.
2. Provide the American student's email address.
3. Add the American student's parent's name, email, and phone number.
4. For the French student, provide the parent's name, email, and phone number.
5. Indicate whether the American and French students are proffessionals ("Yes" or "No").
6. Select the age range of both the American and French students ("Yes" or "No").
7. Choose the school year for the pairing.
8. Indicate the American and French students' language levels (High or Low).
9. Select their academic levels (High or Low).
10. Indicate whether both students prefer to be paired ("Yes" or "No").
11. Choose the gender of both American and French students ("Yes" or "No").
12. Select the language spoken by both students ("Yes" or "No").
13. Indicate whether both the American and French students are staying with a host family ("Yes" or "No").
14. Provide any other information about the pairing (if needed).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text): Enter the American student's first name.
* **email** (email): Enter the American student's email address.
* **American Student Information** (note): Add any other information about the American student.
* **French Student Information** (note): Add any other information about the French student.
* **American Parent Name** (text): Enter the American parent's name.
* **American Parent Email** (email): Enter the American parent's email address.
* **American Parent Phone** (text): Enter the American parent's phone number.
* **French Parent Name** (text): Enter the French parent's name.
* **French Parent Email** (email): Enter the French parent's email address.
* **French Parent Phone** (text): Enter the French parent's phone number.
* **French Prophets** (select_one): Indicate whether the French student is a prophet ("Yes" or "No").
* **American Prophets** (select_one): Indicate whether the American student is a prophet ("Yes" or "No").
* **French Student Age Range** (select_multiple): Select the age range of the French student ("Yes" or "No").
* **American Student Age Range** (select_multiple): Select the age range of the American student ("Yes" or "No").
* **School Year** (select_one): Choose the school year for the pairing.
* **American Language Level** (select_one): Indicate the American student's language level (High or Low).
* **French Language Level** (select_one): Indicate the French student's language level (High or Low).
* **American Academic Level** (select_one): Indicate the American student's academic level (High or Low).
* **French Academic Level** (select_one): Indicate the French student's academic level (High or Low).
* **Pairing Preference** (select_multiple): Indicate whether both students prefer to be paired ("Yes" or "No").
* **American Student Gender** (select_multiple): Indicate the American student's gender ("Yes" or "No").
* **French Student Gender** (select_multiple): Indicate the French student's gender ("Yes" or "No").
* **American Student Language** (select_multiple): Indicate the American student's language spoken ("Yes" or "No").
* **French Student Language** (select_multiple): Indicate the French student's language spoken ("Yes" or "No").
* **American Host Family** (select_multiple): Indicate whether the American student will stay with a host family ("Yes" or "No").
* **French Host Family** (select_multiple): Indicate whether the French student will stay with a host family ("Yes" or "No").
* **Other** (note): Add any other information about the pairing (if needed).
