# School Curriculum Relevance Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to evaluate the relevance and suitability of the school curriculum. It helps in making sure that the curriculum aligns with the educational goals and objectives of the institution and meets the needs of the students.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, you need to fill in the fields as described below:

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How would you rate the overall effectiveness of the curriculum?** (id: 1, `curriculum_effectiveness`): This field is used to rate the overall effectiveness of the curriculum. Rate it from 1 to 10, where 1 is low and 10 is high.
* **Alignment with educational goals** (id: 2, `alignment_goals`): Choose an option that best describes how well the curriculum aligns with the educational goals of the institution. This could be:
	+ Value 1: The curriculum is highly aligned with the educational goals.
	+ Value 2: The curriculum is moderately aligned with the educational goals.
	+ Value 3: The curriculum is not aligned with the educational goals.
* **Relevance to students' needs** (id: 3, `relevance_students_needs`): Select all the options that apply to the curriculum's relevance to students' needs. This could be:
	+ Option 1: The curriculum is highly relevant to students' needs.
	+ Option 2: The curriculum is moderately relevant to students' needs.
	+ Option 3: The curriculum is not relevant to students' needs.
* **Overall impact of the curriculum on students** (id: 4, `overall_impact`): This field is used to rate the overall impact of the curriculum on students. Rate it from 1 to 10, where 1 is low and 10 is high.
* **Student engagement and interest** (id: 5, `student_engagement`): This field is used to rate student engagement and interest. Rate it from 1 to 10, where 1 is low and 10 is high.
* **Teacher support for curriculum development** (id: 6, `teacher_support`): Choose an option that best describes the level of teacher support for curriculum development. This could be:
	+ Option 1: The teacher support is high.
	+ Option 2: The teacher support is moderate.
* **Assessment methods used in the curriculum** (id: 7, `assessment_methods`): Select all the options that apply to the assessment methods used in the curriculum. This could be:
	+ Option 1: The curriculum uses regular assessments.
	+ Option 2: The curriculum uses frequent and regular assessments.
* **Curriculum development process** (id: 8, `curriculum_development_process`): This field is used to describe the curriculum development process. Please enter a descriptive text.
* **Feedback and revision process for curriculum improvement** (id: 9, `feedback_and_revision_process`): Select all the options that apply to the feedback and revision process. This could be:
	+ Option 1: The process involves regular feedback from teachers.
	+ Option 2: The process involves feedback from students.
* **Evaluation process** (id: 10, `evaluation_process`): Choose an option that best describes the evaluation process. This could be:
	+ Option 1: The evaluation process is regular and ongoing.
	+ Option 2: The evaluation process is infrequent.
* **Curriculum relevance to institutional objectives** (id: 11, `curriculum_relevance_to_institutional_objectives`): This field is used to rate the curriculum's relevance to institutional objectives. Rate it from 1 to 10, where 1 is low and 10 is high.
* **Relevance to institutional goals** (id: 12, `relevance_to_institutional_goals`): Choose an option that best describes the curriculum's relevance to institutional goals. This could be:
	+ Option 1: The curriculum is highly relevant to institutional goals.
	+ Option 2: The curriculum is moderately relevant to institutional goals.
* **Teacher training and support for the curriculum** (id: 13, `teacher_training_support`): Select all the options that apply to teacher training and support. This could be:
	+ Option 1: The teacher receives regular training.
	+ Option 2: The teacher receives occasional training.
* **Resources available for curriculum development** (id: 14, `resources`): Select all the options that apply to the resources available for curriculum development. This could be:
	+ Option 1: The institution has sufficient resources.
	+ Option 2: The institution has limited resources.
* **Curriculum review cycle** (id: 15, `curriculum_review_cycle`): This field is used to describe the curriculum review cycle. Please enter a descriptive text.
* **Continuous curriculum improvement** (id: 16, `continuous_curriculum_improvement`): Choose an option that best describes the approach to continuous curriculum improvement. This could be:
	+ Option 1: The institution uses a structured approach.
	+ Option 2: The institution uses an unstructured approach.
* **Collaboration with external partners for curriculum development** (id: 17, `collaboration_with_external_partners`): Choose an option that best describes the level of collaboration with external partners. This could be:
	+ Option 1: The institution collaborates with external partners extensively.
	+ Option 2: The institution does not collaborate with external partners.
* **Student participation and engagement** (id: 18, `student_participation_and_engagement`): This field is used to rate student participation and engagement. Rate it from 1 to 10, where 1 is low and 10 is high.
* **Teacher participation and training** (id: 19, `teacher_participation_and_training`): Select all the options that apply to teacher participation and training. This could be:
	+ Option 1: The teacher receives regular participation and training.
	+ Option 2: The teacher receives occasional participation and training.
* **Evaluation frequency** (id: 20, `evaluation_frequency`): This field is used to describe the evaluation frequency. Please enter a descriptive text.
* **Curriculum alignment** (id: 21, `curriculum_alignment`): Select all the options that apply to the curriculum alignment. This could be:
	+ Option 1: The curriculum is highly aligned with other curricula.
	+ Option 2: The curriculum is moderately aligned with other curricula.
* **Curriculum sustainability** (id: 22, `curriculum_sustainability`): Choose an option that best describes the approach to curriculum sustainability. This could be:
	+ Option 1: The institution has a plan for long-term curriculum sustainability.
	+ Option 2: The institution does not have a plan for long-term curriculum sustainability.
* **Continuous curriculum evaluation** (id: 23, `continuous_curriculum_evaluation`): Select all the options that apply to the continuous curriculum evaluation. This could be:
	+ Option 1: The institution has a structured approach.
	+ Option 2: The institution has an unstructured approach.
* **Institutional resources** (id: 24, `institutional_resources`): Choose an option that best describes the institutional resources available for curriculum development. This could be:
	+ Option 1: The institution has sufficient resources.
	+ Option 2: The institution has limited resources.
* **Overall satisfaction** (id: 25, `overall_satisfaction`): This field is used to rate overall satisfaction. Rate it from 1 to 10, where 1 is low and 10 is high.

## Tips

- Be honest in your evaluation.
- Choose the options that best describe your answer.
- Describe your answers in the fields that require text.
- Rate the fields that require ratings with numbers.
