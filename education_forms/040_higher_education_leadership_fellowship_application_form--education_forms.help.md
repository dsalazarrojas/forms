# Higher Education Leadership Fellowship Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Higher Education Leadership Fellowship Application Form is designed to collect information about applicants' background, experience, and qualifications for the Higher Education Leadership Fellowship program.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling out the required fields, which are marked with the *Required* tag.
2.  Fill out all non-required fields with any relevant information you feel would support your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   *Application Form*: This is a brief introduction to your application, and is a required field. Please provide a short statement describing your interests and reasons for applying to the Higher Education Leadership Fellowship program.
*   *contact_person*: Provide the name and contact information of a person who can be reached for further information or to discuss this application.
*   *name_of_institution*: Please provide the name of the institution where you are working or studying.
*   *department*: This field should be filled out with the department you are working or studying in at your institution.
*   *email*: This is your email address for contact purposes.
*   *phone*: Provide a phone number that can be reached for further information.
*   *role_title*: This field should be filled out with your current role or title.
*   *years_of_experience*: This field is where you can indicate how many years of experience you have in the field.
*   *date_of_birth*: Provide your date of birth.
*   *time_of_birth*: Please note that this field is likely intended to indicate the time of birth, but it seems to be mislabeled. Please provide your time of birth or leave this field blank.
*   *address*: This is the address where you can be reached for further information.
*   *bio*: This is an optional field where you can provide a brief personal or professional bio.

## Tips

*   Make sure to proofread your application carefully before submitting.
*   If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to reach out.
*   Please be truthful and honest in your application.
*   The evaluation process is fair and equal for all applicants.
