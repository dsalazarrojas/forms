# Graduation Ceremony RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Graduation Ceremony RSVP Form is designed to collect information from guests about their attendance and preferences for the graduation ceremony. If you are not attending the ceremony, please do not complete this form. If you are unsure or need to check your status, contact the relevant team.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out this form to let us know if you will be attending the graduation ceremony.
- Enter your guest's name in the "guest_name" field.
- Enter your guest's email address in the "guest_email" field.
- Select the date and time you plan to attend in the "rsvp_date" and "rsvp_time" fields, respectively.
- If you have any dietary restrictions, choose them from the "dietary_restrictions" dropdown menu.
- If you have any special requests, enter them in the "special_requests" field.
- Choose how you would like to receive updates in the "send_update" field.
- Select how far in advance you would like to receive follow-up notifications in the "send_follow_up" field.
- Choose how early you would like to receive reminders in the "send_reminder" field.
- Enter the date of the graduation ceremony in the "graduation_date" field (this is pre-populated with the scheduled date).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Guest Name (`guest_name`, text, optional):
* This field is where you enter the guest's name.

### Guest Email (`guest_email`, text, optional):
* This field is used to store the guest's email address.

### RSVP Date (`rsvp_date`, date, optional):
* Select the date you plan to attend the graduation ceremony.

### RSVP Time (`rsvp_time`, time, optional):
* Choose the time you plan to attend the graduation ceremony.

### Number of Guests (`number_of_guests`, number, optional):
* If you're bringing guests, enter the number of people attending with you.

### Dietary Restrictions (`dietary_restrictions`, select_multiple, optional):
* Choose any dietary restrictions you have from the dropdown menu.

### Special Requests (`special_requests`, text, optional):
* Enter any special requests you have for the ceremony.

### Send Updates (`send_update`, select_one, optional):
* Choose how you'd like to receive updates:
  * Send updates to guest
  * No updates to guest
  * Send updates to both

### Send Follow-Up (`send_follow_up`, select_one, optional):
* Select how far in advance you'd like to receive follow-up notifications:
  * Send follow-up 1 hour before
  * Send follow-up 2 hours before
  * Send follow-up 3 hours before

### Send Reminder (`send_reminder`, select_one, optional):
* Choose how early you'd like to receive reminders:
  * Send reminder 1 hour before
  * Send reminder 2 hours before
  * No reminder

### Graduation Date (`graduation_date`, date, optional):
* This field is pre-populated with the scheduled graduation date.
