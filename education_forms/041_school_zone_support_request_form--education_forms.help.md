# School Zone Support Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Zone Support Request Form is used to report issues within a school zone. It helps administrators document and track problems that need to be addressed.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please select the issue you are experiencing in your school zone.
2.  Choose the specific location where the issue is occurring.
3.  Indicate if you are experiencing the issue (yes/no).
4.  You can attach photos or videos to help us understand the issue (optional).
5.  Add any additional comments about the issue.
6.  Provide your contact information so we can reach out to you.
7.  Specify the date of the issue.
8.  Indicate the time of the issue.
9.  Provide your phone number.
10.  Add any additional notes or details about the issue.
11.  Enter the name of the school where the issue is occurring.
12.  You can add any additional information about your school zone.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **What is the issue you are experiencing in your school zone?** (`school_zone_support_request_form_1`, text, required): This field is where you describe the problem you are experiencing in your school zone. It's essential to be as detailed as possible so that administrators can address your concern effectively.
*   **Briefly describe the location of the issue** (`school_zone_support_request_form_2`, select_one, required): Please select the specific location within the school zone where the issue is occurring.
*   **What type of issue are you experiencing in your school zone?** (`school_zone_support_request_form_3`, select_multiple, required): Indicate if you are experiencing the issue (yes/no) or select 'Other' if it's not listed.
*   **Please attach photos or videos to help us understand the issue** (`school_zone_support_request_form_4`, text, optional): You can attach supporting evidence to help us better understand the issue. This will help us provide more effective support.
*   **Additional comments or information about the issue** (`school_zone_support_request_form_5`, text, optional): Add any additional details about the issue to help us understand it better.
*   **How can we contact you?** (`school_zone_support_request_form_6`, email, required): This is your contact information so we can reach out to you to follow up on your issue.
*   **What is the date of the issue?** (`school_zone_support_request_form_7`, date, required): Please enter the date of the issue, so we can schedule follow-up visits or actions.
*   **What is the time of the issue?** (`school_zone_support_request_form_8`, time, required): Indicate the time of the issue, including the AM/PM designation.
*   **What is your phone number?** (`school_zone_support_request_form_9`, text, required): This is your contact information so we can reach out to you to follow up on your issue.
*   **Additional notes or details** (`school_zone_support_request_form_10`, note, optional): You can add any additional information about the issue that you think would be helpful.
*   **What is the name of the school?** (`school_zone_support_request_form_11`, text, optional): Enter the name of the school where the issue is occurring.
*   **Is there anything else you would like to add about your school zone?** (`school_zone_support_request_form_12`, text, optional): You can add any additional information about your school zone that you think would be helpful.

## Tips

*   Make sure to be as detailed as possible when describing the issue.
*   Attach photos or videos to support your request if possible.
*   Ensure your contact information is up-to-date.
*   Enter the correct date and time of the issue.
*   Be sure to add any additional information that might help us provide better support.
