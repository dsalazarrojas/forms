# Training Platform Usability Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the usability, content, and overall satisfaction of a training platform.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and answer each question as thoroughly as possible.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select one of the provided options.
3. For text fields, provide detailed and clear answers.
4. For comments sections, provide any additional information or suggestions you have for the platform.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Platform usability**: This field is where you can provide any comments or feedback on the platform's user interface, such as navigation, layout, or overall appearance.
* **Content relevance**: Please provide any comments or feedback on the relevance and accuracy of the content provided by the platform.
* **Overall satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the platform on a scale of 1-3.
	+ 1: Very dissatisfied
	+ 2: Neutral
	+ 3: Very satisfied
* **Learning experience**: This field is for any comments or feedback on the learning experience provided by the platform.
* **Platform improvement**: Select all the areas you think the platform could be improved in.
	+ Improved navigation
	+ Improved content organization
	+ Improved overall appearance
* **Platform usability comments**: Use this field to provide any additional comments or feedback on the platform's usability.
* **Content relevance comments**: Use this field to provide any additional comments or feedback on the content's relevance.
* **Overall satisfaction comments**: Use this field to provide any additional comments or feedback on your overall satisfaction with the platform.
* **Learning experience comments**: Use this field to provide any additional comments or feedback on your learning experience with the platform.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and thoroughly.
* If you have any questions or need help, feel free to contact the form creators at form@gic.mx.
