# extracurricular-activity-approval-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from parents or guardians about their child's extracurricular activities. It helps school administrators to make informed decisions about activity approvals and communicate with parents/guardians.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required and optional fields as instructed below.
2. Review all fields carefully before submitting the form.
3. Once you have filled out all the fields, click "Submit" to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Please enter your child's name.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your child's activity.
* **Date of Birth** (`student_date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your child's date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, text, optional): Enter your school's email address (if applicable).
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, number, optional): Enter your child's grade level (e.g. 1-12).
* **Student Activity Name** (`student_activity_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the activity your child is participating in.
* **Parent Relationship** (`parent_relationship`, select_one, optional):
	+ Approved: You are the parent/guardian of this child.
	+ Not Applicable: You are not the parent/guardian of this child.
	+ Unknown: You are unsure of your relationship to the child.
* **Activity Approval Status** (`student_activity_approval_status`, select_one, optional):
	+ Approved: The activity has been approved by the school.
	+ Not Approved: The activity has not been approved by the school.
	+ Pending: The activity's approval status is pending review.
* **School Approval Date** (`school_approval_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the school approved the activity (if applicable).
* **Comments** (`teacher_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or questions you have about the activity.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact your child's school or teacher for clarification.
* Once submitted, the school will review and respond to your submission accordingly.
