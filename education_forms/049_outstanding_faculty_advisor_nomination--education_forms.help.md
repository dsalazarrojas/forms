# outstanding faculty advisor nomination - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Outstanding Faculty Advisor Nomination form is designed to recognize and celebrate the exceptional work of faculty advisors who have made significant contributions to students' academic or personal growth. This form helps in identifying and acknowledging such individuals, providing them with the recognition they deserve.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Nomination Information

* **Nomination** (1): A brief description of the faculty member's outstanding work and contributions.
* **First Name** (2): The first name of the faculty member being nominated.
* **Last Name** (3): The last name of the faculty member being nominated.
* **Department** (4): The department of the faculty member being nominated.
* **Job Title** (5): The job title of the faculty member being nominated.

### Step 2: Nominee Information

* **Faculty Member Nominated** (6): A description of the faculty member's outstanding work and contributions.
* **Nomination Type** (7): Select the type of nomination (Outstanding Academic Advisor, Outstanding Research Mentor, or Outstanding Teaching Mentor).
* **Nomination Narrative** (8): A detailed description of the faculty member's outstanding work and contributions.
* **Nominator Name** (9): The name of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Nominator Title** (10): The title of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Nominator Department** (11): The department of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Nominator Institution** (12): The institution of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Nominator Email** (13): The email address of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Nominator Phone** (14): The phone number of the person submitting the nomination.

## Tips

* Ensure accuracy and thoroughness in filling out the form to provide a detailed and honest account of the faculty member's outstanding work.
* Check for existing nominations before submitting a new one to avoid duplication.
* Submit only nominations for faculty members who are currently employed at the institution.
* Proofread the form carefully before submission to avoid errors.
