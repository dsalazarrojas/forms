# European Criminal Law Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The European Criminal Law Quiz is a tool for assessing your knowledge and understanding of European Criminal Law. This form is designed to be a starting point for learning and not a comprehensive exam.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply navigate through the pages and fill in the information as required. Each page will present you with questions or related aspects of European Criminal Law. 

Please make sure to read the explanations below for each field to have a clear understanding of what is required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Criminal Law Principles** (`question_1`, `select_one`, required: false): This question is asking for your understanding of the principles that govern European Criminal Law.
* **EU Criminal Law** (`question_2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question is asking for your knowledge on the current state of EU Criminal Law, choose all that apply.
* **Legal Education** (`legal_education`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your experience or education in the field of law or related areas.
* **Universities** (`universities`, `select_one`, required: false): This question is asking for your affiliation with any of the listed universities.
* **Cooperation Tools** (`cooperation_tools`, `text`, required: false): This field is not clearly defined in the provided YAML, so this explanation will remain vague. Please provide any information related to cooperation tools you think are relevant.
* **European Criminal Law Quiz** (`european_criminal_law_quiz`, `text`, required: false): This is the title of the form, not a field, but it seems to be a required field for form submission. Please note that this field is not clearly defined in the YAML and might be a mistake.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions seriously and to the best of your knowledge.
* You may not know the answers to all questions, and that's okay. This form is designed to be a starting point for learning.
* If you're unsure about a question, please try to choose the best answer you know.
* Take your time to answer each question carefully, as it will affect the accuracy of your results.
