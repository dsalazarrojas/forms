# learning_motivation_study_group_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants interested in joining a learning motivation study group.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your Participant Information on the first page.
2. Enter your Contact Information in the second page.
3. Share your Session Details in the third page.
4. Complete the Participant Details page with any additional information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information**: A field to input your personal details (not required).
* **Contact Information**: A field to provide your contact information (not required).
* **Session Details**: A field to share your session-specific information (not required).
* **Participant Details**: A field for any additional participant details (not required).
