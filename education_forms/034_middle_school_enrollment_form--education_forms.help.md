# Middle School Enrollment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Middle School Enrollment Form is a document designed to collect information from students and their parents for the middle school enrollment process. This form aims to gather essential details for smooth and accurate processing of student enrollment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information about yourself as a student and your parent or guardian.
2. Enter your name in the "student_name" field.
3. Enter your parent's/guardian's name in the "parent_name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "student_email" field. Please ensure the email address is valid and in the correct format (e.g., [student_name]@example.com).
5. Enter your phone number in the "student_phone" field.
6. Select your current grade level from the "student_grade" dropdown menu. Please choose one of the available options: 7th Grade, 8th Grade, or 9th Grade.
7. Sign and date the "e_signature" field, which is a text field for electronic signatures. This field is not required for submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name (student_name)** (`text`, optional): Enter your name as a student.
* **Parent Name (parent_name)** (`text`, optional): Enter the name of your parent or guardian.
* **Student Email (student_email)** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Student Phone (student_phone)** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Grade Level (student_grade)** (`select_one`, required): Choose your current grade level from the options provided: 7th Grade, 8th Grade, or 9th Grade.
* **Electronic Signature (e_signature)** (`text`, optional): Enter a digital signature to confirm your identity. This field is not required for submission.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Double-check your email address and phone number for correct formatting.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, consult with your parent or school administration for guidance.

Note: This form is designed for middle school enrollment purposes only.
