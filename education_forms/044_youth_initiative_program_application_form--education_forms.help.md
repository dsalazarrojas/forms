# Youth Initiative Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Initiative Program Application Form is designed to collect information from students applying to the program. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and truthfully to ensure a smooth application process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. Ensure you provide all the required information.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Contact Information (id: 1)**: Enter your personal contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
* **Parent Information (id: 2)**: Provide your parent/guardian's contact information, if applicable.
* **Emergency Contact Information (id: 3)**: Enter an emergency contact person's name, phone number, and email address.
* **What are your talents? (id: 4)**: Select all the talents that you think you possess, such as leadership, teamwork, etc.
* **What are your interests? (id: 5)**: Choose all the interests that you are passionate about, such as reading, sports, etc.
* **What skills do you think are most important for your future career? (id: 6)**: Select the skills that you believe are essential for your future career, such as communication, problem-solving, etc.
* **What is your current level of education? (id: 7)**: Choose your current level of education, such as High School, College, etc.
* **What is your GPA? (id: 8)**: Select your GPA, Yes or No.
* **How many hours do you attend school? (id: 9)**: Enter the number of hours you attend school per week.
* **What languages do you speak fluently? (id: 10)**: Choose all the languages you speak fluently.
* **Please provide 2 references (id: 11)**: Select two references, such as teachers or mentors, who can vouch for your skills and character.
* **Please provide 2 references (id: 12)**: Select two more references, such as teachers or mentors, who can vouch for your skills and character.
* **Additional references (id: 13)**: Choose any additional references that you think are relevant to your application.
* **Additional Notes (id: 14)**: Provide any additional information that you think is essential to your application.
* **Attach Resume or File (id: 15)**: Upload a scanned copy of your resume or a relevant file.
* **I understand I am required to be honest about the information I enter (id: 16)**: Check this box to confirm that you will provide accurate information.
* **I understand that I will be contacted for an interview (id: 17)**: Check this box to confirm that you are aware of the interview process.
* **I understand that all information provided here will be kept confidential (id: 18)**: Check this box to confirm that you understand the confidentiality of your information.
* **I understand that all information provided here will be reviewed and verified (id: 19)**: Check this box to confirm that you understand the verification process.
* **I understand that failure to provide accurate information may result in disqualification from the program (id: 20)**: Check this box to confirm that you understand the consequences of providing inaccurate information.
* **I understand that all information provided here will be stored securely (id: 21)**: Check this box to confirm that you understand the data storage process.
* **Agreement 7 (id: 22)**: This is a placeholder for any additional agreement or term that needs to be confirmed.
* **Parent or Guardian's Agreement (id: 23)**: This is a placeholder for any agreement or term that needs to be confirmed by the parent or guardian.
* **Student Signature (id: 24)**: Sign your name to confirm that you have filled out the form accurately and truthfully.
* **Parent/Guardian Signature (id: 25)**: Sign your name or the guardian's name to confirm that you have reviewed and agreed to the information provided.
