# mock_exam_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mock_exam_form is designed to collect information from students for educational purposes, such as administering mock exams or assessments. It helps educators or administrators create a comprehensive record of student performance and progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the Student Information field with the relevant details about the student.
2.  Choose a Question Type from the options provided.
3.  Type in the Question field the question being assessed.
4.  Enter the Answer Key field with the correct answer to the question.
5.  Select the Question Type 2 from the options provided.
6.  Enter the Date field in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
7.  Enter the Time field in the format `HH:MM:SS`.
8.  Enter the Email field with the student's email address.
9.  Enter the Phone field with the student's phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Student Information** (`Student Information`, text, required/optional): Enter the student's demographic details such as name, ID number, or any other relevant information.
*   **Question Type** (`Question Type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of question being assessed, e.g., multiple choice, true/false.
*   **Question** (`Question`, text, optional): Type in the question being assessed.
*   **Answer Key** (`Answer Key`, text, optional): Enter the correct answer to the question.
*   **Question Type 2** (`Question Type 2`, select_one, optional): Select another type of question.
*   **Date** (`Date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the assessment in `YYYY-MM-DD` format.
*   **Time** (`Time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the assessment in `HH:MM:SS` format.
*   **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter the student's email address.
*   **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Enter the student's phone number.
