# Barrett West Experience - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Barrett West Experience is a form designed to collect information from students interested in our program. It helps us understand your interests and needs, ensuring a smooth and personalized experience for you and your guests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields marked as *required*.
2. Provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure we can cater to your needs.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Full Name** (Student Full Name, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
- **Student Email Address** (Student Email Address, email, required): Enter your official email address that we can use to communicate with you.
- **Student Phone Number** (Student Phone Number, text, required): Enter your current or primary phone number for us to reach you.
- **Current High School** (Current High School, text, required): Enter your current high school name.
- **High School Graduation Year** (High School Graduation Year, number, required): Enter the year you graduated or are expected to graduate from high school.
- **Intended Major or Area of Interest** (Intended Major or Area of Interest, text, optional): Enter your area of interest or intended major.
- **Preferred Visit Date** (Preferred Visit Date, date, required): Select a date that works best for you to visit us.
- **Preferred Session Time** (Preferred Session Time, select_one, required): Choose between Morning Session (9 AM - 12 PM), Afternoon Session (1 PM - 4 PM), or Full Day Experience for your visit.
- **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** (Dietary Restrictions or Allergies, text, optional): If you have any dietary restrictions or allergies, please let us know so we can provide specific meal options.
- **Number of Guests Attending** (Number of Guests Attending, number, optional): Enter the number of guests you plan to bring with you.
- **Do you have any specific questions for our admissions team?** (Do you have any specific questions for our admissions team?, text, optional): If you have any questions or concerns, please let us know so we can address them.
- **How did you hear about the Barrett West Experience?** (How did you hear about the Barrett West Experience?, select_one, optional): Let us know how you found out about our program, such as through a school counselor, social media, our website, a friend or family member, or an email invitation.
