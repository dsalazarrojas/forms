# high_school_scholarship_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "high_school_scholarship_application_form" is designed to collect information from high school students to support their application for scholarships. This form helps applicants demonstrate their academic achievements, extracurricular activities, and potential, increasing their chances of securing financial aid for their education.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Applicant Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Applicant Email** address in the second field.
3. Provide your **Address** in the third field (Address 1 and 2 are optional).
4. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
5. Select your **Grade Level Attending** from the dropdown menu.
6. Choose your **Academic Programs** of interest from the list.
7. Enter your **GPA** (Grade Point Average) for the current year.
8. Describe your **Academic Achievements**, such as AP courses, honor roll, or academic awards.
9. Select your **Extracurricular Activities**, like sports, music, or drama.
10. Choose your **Community Service** involvement from the list.
11. List any **Awards** you have received, such as academic or athletic awards.
12. Specify **Financial Aid** needed or available.
13. Provide up to 3 **References** with name, email, and relationship (Parent, Teacher, or Advisor).
14. Upload supporting documentation or materials as **Submission**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Name**: Enter your full name as it should be displayed on the scholarship application.
* **Applicant Email**: Provide a valid email address where the scholarship committee can contact you.
* **Address 1**: Enter your current address, including street address and city.
* **Address 2**: Enter any additional address information, such as apartment number or suite.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Grade Level Attending**: Select your current grade level in high school (Freshman, Sophomore, Junior, or Senior).
* **Academic Programs**: Choose the academic programs you are interested in or currently pursuing.
* **GPA**: Enter your current Grade Point Average for the current year.
* **Academic Achievements**: Describe your academic achievements, such as AP courses, honor roll, or academic awards.
* **Extracurricular Activities**: Select the activities you participate in outside of academic studies, such as sports, music, or drama.
* **Community Service**: Choose the community service activities you are involved in, such as volunteer work, community service, or fundraising.
* **Awards**: List any awards or recognition you have received in academics, sports, or art.
* **Financial Aid**: Specify if you need financial aid or if you have financial aid available.
* **References**: Provide up to 3 references with name, email, and relationship (Parent, Teacher, or Advisor).
* **Submission**: Upload supporting documentation or materials to support your application.
