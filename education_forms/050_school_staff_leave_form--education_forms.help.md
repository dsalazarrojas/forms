# School Staff Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request leave from school staff members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as a staff member in the **Staff Member** field.
2. Select the start date of your leave in the **Start Date** field.
3. Select the end date of your leave in the **Leave End Date** field.
4. Enter the reason for your leave in the **Reason for Leave** field.
5. Enter your expected return date to work in the **Return to Work Date** field (if known).
6. Provide any additional contact information in the **Contact Information** field.
7. Enter your contact number in the **Contact Number** field.
8. Enter your email in the **Email** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Staff Member**: This field is where you enter your name as a staff member.
* **Start Date**: This field is where you select the date you want to start your leave.
*   **Leave Start Date**: This field is where you select the date you actually start your leave.
*   **Leave End Date**: This field is where you select the date you want to end your leave.
*   **Reason for Leave**: This field is where you enter a brief explanation of why you are taking leave.
*   **Return to Work Date**: This field is where you enter the date you expect to return to work.
*   **Contact Information**: This field is where you enter any additional contact information that can be used to reach you while you are on leave.
*   **Contact Number**: This field is where you enter your contact number for communication while on leave.
*   **Email**: This field is where you enter your email address that can be used for communication while on leave.
