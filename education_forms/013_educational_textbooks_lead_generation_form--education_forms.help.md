# Educational Textbooks Lead Generation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture information about educational textbooks, including school details, contact person, and book specifics. The purpose of this form is to generate leads for textbook sales and related services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the school name, email, phone number, organization, department, and contact name.
2. Enter the contact's title and contact email.
3. Provide the publisher's name, website, and distributor information.
4. Describe the product type and provide a brief description of the product.
5. Mention the price range of the product.
6. Enter the sales representative's name (e.g., Jimmy) or choose "Other" if not applicable.
7. Choose the follow-up options (phone, email, text message, video conference, meeting, or in-person).
8. Select the follow-up time (if applicable).
9. Choose the lead generation option (New, Existing, or Both).
10. Select the lead source (Website, Social Media, Email, Trade Show, Print Media, or Event).
11. Select the follow-up channel (phone, email, text message, video conference, meeting, or in-person).
12. Enter the product lead ID and any additional notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Name** (`school_name`): Enter the name of the school that is interested in the textbook.
* **School Email** (`school_email`): Enter the school's official email address.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`): Enter the school's phone number.
* **School Organization** (`school_organization`): Enter the school's organization or department.
* **School Department** (`school_department`): Enter the school's department.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Title** (`contact_title`): Enter the title of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Publisher Name** (`publisher_name`): Enter the name of the textbook publisher.
* **Publisher Website** (`publisher_website`): Enter the publisher's website.
* **Distributor** (`distributor`): Enter the distributor of the textbook.
* **Product Type** (`product_type`): Enter the type of product (e.g., textbook, workbook, etc.).
* **Product Description** (`product_description`): Provide a brief description of the product.
* **Price Range** (`price_range`): Enter the price range of the product.
* **Sales Rep** (`sales_rep`): Choose the sales representative (e.g., Jimmy) or select "Other" if not applicable.
* **Follow-up Question** (`follow_up_question`): Ask any follow-up question about the lead.
* **Lead ID** (`lead_id`): Enter the product lead ID.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Add any additional notes about the lead.
* **Category** (`category`): Enter the category of the product.
* **Lead Generation** (`lead_generation`): Choose whether the lead is for a new or existing opportunity.
* **Lead Source** (`lead_source`): Choose the source of the lead (e.g., Website, Social Media).
* **Follow-up Channel** (`follow_up_channel`): Choose the channel for follow-up communication (e.g., phone, email).
* **Follow-up Time** (`follow_up_time`): Enter the time for follow-up communication (if applicable).
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`): Choose the person who submitted the form (e.g., Jimmy).
