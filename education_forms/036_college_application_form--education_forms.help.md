# College Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from college applicants about their personal details, academic background, and preferences for enrollment and housing. The form will help the admissions team to evaluate each applicant's eligibility and suitability for the college program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and truthful information.
2. Select the correct options for the select_one fields.
3. Use the provided hints as a guide to ensure that you are filling in the correct type of information.
4. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your given name.
* **Middle Name** (`middle_name`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your middle name (if applicable).
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your family name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, `required`): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, `required`): Select your gender from the provided options.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, `required`): Enter your primary email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, `required`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Street Address** (`address`, `text`, `required`): Enter your complete street address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name of your city.
* **State or Province** (`state`, `text`, `required`): Enter the state or province of your residence.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (`zip`, `text`, `required`): Enter your postal code.
* **Country** (`country`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the country of your residence from the provided options.
* **Citizenship Status** (`citizenship`, `select_one`, `required`): Select your citizenship status from the provided options.
* **High School Name** (`high_school_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name of your high school.
* **High School Address** (`high_school_address`, `text`, `required`): Enter the address of your high school.
* **Graduation Date** (`graduation_date`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date you graduated from high school.
* **Current GPA** (`gpa`, `number`, `required`): Enter your current unweighted GPA.
* **Class Rank** (`class_rank`, `text`, `optional`): If available, enter your class rank (e.g., valedictorian).
* **SAT Score** (`sat_score`, `number`, `optional`): Enter your total SAT score (if submitted).
* **ACT Score** (`act_score`, `number`, `optional`): Enter your composite ACT score (if submitted).
* **Intended Major** (`intended_major`, `text`, `required`): Enter the field of study you intend to pursue.
* **Intended Degree** (`intended_degree`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the type of degree you intend to earn.
* **Enrollment Term** (`enrollment_term`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the term you plan to enroll in from the provided options.
* **Enrollment Status** (`enrollment_status`, `select_one`, `required`): Select whether you plan to enroll full-time or part-time.
* **Housing Preference** (`housing_preference`, `select_one`, `required`): Select where you would like to live from the provided options.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Email** (`parent_email`, `email`, `required`): Enter the email of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone** (`parent_phone`, `text`, `required`): Enter the contact phone number of your parent or guardian.
* **Interest in Financial Aid** (`financial_aid_interest`, `select_one`, `required`): Select whether you are interested in applying for financial aid.
* **Have You Filed FAFSA** (`fafsa_filed`, `select_one`, `required`): Select whether you have filed the Free Application for Federal Student Aid.
* **Extracurricular Activities** (`extracurricular_activities`, `text`, `required`): List any extracurricular activities and leadership roles you have participated in.
* **Honors and Awards** (`honors_awards`, `text`, `optional`): Enter any academic or other honors and awards you have received.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, `text`, `optional`): Enter any relevant employment experience you have.
* **Personal Statement** (`personal_statement`, `text`, `required`): Enter a brief statement explaining why you are interested in attending the college.
* **Application Fee Payment** (`application_fee`, `select_one`, `required`): Select your status regarding payment of the application fee.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_accepted`, `select_one`, `required`): Select that you certify all information is accurate and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Tips
* Double-check your information for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you have filled in all required fields.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, contact the admissions team for clarification.

Please note that this help guide is a general guide and might not cover every possible scenario. If you need further clarification, please contact the admissions team directly.
