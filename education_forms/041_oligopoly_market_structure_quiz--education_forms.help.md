# oligopoly_market_structure_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose

The oligopoly_market_structure_quiz form is designed to collect data about market structures, specifically oligopolies. It will ask about market competition, number of firms, entry and exit barriers, and other factors.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form and ensure you have a clear understanding of the questions before starting to fill it out.
2. For each question, provide a brief answer in the required format (e.g., select from a list).
3. If you have any doubts or concerns, feel free to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Market Competition** (`market_competition`, text, optional): This field asks about the level of competition in the market. If you're unsure, consider the level of rivalry between firms in the market.
* **Number of Firms** (`firms`, select_one, optional): Choose the correct number of firms in the market. Please select one of the options: Firm 1, Firm 2, Firm 3, Firm 4, or Firm 5.
* **Entry Barriers** (`entry_barrier`, select_one, optional): Select the level of entry barriers in the market, which are obstacles that prevent new firms from entering the market. You can choose from High, Medium, or Low.
* **Exit Barriers** (`exit_barriers`, select_one, optional): Select the level of exit barriers in the market, which are obstacles that prevent firms from leaving the market. You can choose from High, Medium, or Low.
* **Barriers to Entry** (`barriers_to_entry`, select_one, optional): Select the level of barriers to entry in the market, which are obstacles that prevent new firms from entering the market. You can choose from High, Medium, or Low.
* **Barriers to Exit** (`barriers_to_exit`, select_one, optional): Select the level of barriers to exit in the market, which are obstacles that prevent firms from leaving the market. You can choose from High, Medium, or Low.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask.
* This form is designed to help you gather data about market structures, so please answer based on your knowledge and understanding of the market.
