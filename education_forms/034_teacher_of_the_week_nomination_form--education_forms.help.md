# Teacher Of The Week Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teacher Of The Week Nomination Form is designed to recognize and reward outstanding teachers who go above and beyond in their roles. This form allows you to nominate a teacher who deserves special recognition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: 
   - **Teacher Name**: The name of the teacher you are nominating.
   - **Department**: The department the teacher belongs to (e.g., Art, Music, etc.).
   - **Student Name**: The name of the student nominating the teacher.
   - **Nominator Name**: The name of the person submitting the nomination (e.g., department head, teacher, etc.).
   - **Nominator Email**: The email of the person submitting the nomination.
   - **School Admin Approval**: Choose "Approved" to confirm the nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Teacher Name** (`Teacher Name`, text, required)
Enter the name of the teacher you are nominating.

### 2. **Department** (`Department`, select_one, required)
Choose the department the teacher belongs to (e.g., Art, Music, etc.).

### 3. **Student Name** (`Student Name`, text, required)
Enter the name of the student nominating the teacher.

### 4. **Nominator Name** (`Nominator Name`, text, required)
Enter your name as the person submitting the nomination.

### 5. **Nominator Email** (`Nominator Email`, email, required)
Enter your email address.

### 6. **Nominator Phone** (`Nominator Phone`, text, optional)
Enter your phone number (optional).

### 7. **Nominator Message** (`Nominator Message`, text, optional)
Add any comments or reasons for nominating the teacher (optional).

### 8. **Teacher Comments** (`Teacher Comments`, text, optional)
Add any comments from the teacher (optional).

### 9. **Student Comments** (`Student Comments`, text, optional)
Add any comments from the student (optional).

### 10. **Nominator Signature** (`Nominator Signature`, note, optional)
Add your signature.

### 11. **School Admin Approval** (`School Admin Approval`, select_multiple, required)
Choose "Approved" to confirm the nomination.

### 12. **Approval Date** (`Approval Date`, date, optional)
The date the nomination was approved.

### 13. **Approval Time** (`Approval Time`, time, optional)
The time the nomination was approved.

### 14. **Form Submitter** (`Form Submitter`, text, optional)
The person who submitted the form.

### 15. **Date Completed** (`Date Completed`, date, optional)
The date the form was completed.

### 16. **Time Completed** (`Time Completed`, time, optional)
The time the form was completed.

### 17. **Teacher Email** (`Teacher Email`, email, optional)
The teacher's email address.

### 18. **Teacher Phone** (`Teacher Phone`, text, optional)
The teacher's phone number.

### 19. **Teacher Name** (`Teacher Name`, text, optional)
The teacher's name.

### 20. **Teacher Comments** (`Teacher Comments`, text, optional)
Add any comments from the teacher.

### 21. **Nominator Comments** (`Nominator Comments`, text, optional)
Add any comments from the nominator.

### 22. **Nomination Date** (`Nomination Date`, date, optional)
The date the nomination was made.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the teacher has not already been nominated.
- Make sure to enter all required fields.
- Review the fields carefully to provide accurate information.
- Only submit the form when you are certain the teacher deserves recognition.
