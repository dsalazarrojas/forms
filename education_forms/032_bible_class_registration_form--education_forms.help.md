# Bible Class Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals registering for a Bible class, aiming to understand their demographic, learning preferences, and logistical needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
- Fill in your personal information (First Name, Last Name, Email Address, and Phone Number)
- Select your age group (Youth, Young Adult, Adult, Mid-life, Senior)
- Choose your church affiliation (Church Name, Church City or Location, Member Status)
- Select the class you're registering for (Bible Class Title)
- Indicate your learning preferences (Class Level, Class Format, Preferred Class Time)
- Provide information about your Bible study experience (Familiarity with the Bible, Prior Bible Study Experience)
- Specify your learning goals (What Are Your Learning Goals)
- Choose how you like to learn (Preferred Study Method)
- Provide logistical information (Attendance Commitment, Do You Have Required Materials, Do You Need Transportation Assistance)
- Finally, provide any additional comments or questions

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name as you would like it to be known in class.
* **Last Name**: Your last name as you would like it to be known in class.
* **Email Address**: Your email address for class updates and communication.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact number for class reminders and updates.
* **Age Group**: Select your age group to help us tailor the class content.
	+ **Youth (13-17)**: For students under the age of 18.
	+ **Young Adult (18-25)**: For students in their 18-25 years old.
	+ **Adult (26-40)**: For students in their 26-40 years old.
	+ **Mid-life (41-60)**: For students in their 41-60 years old.
	+ **Senior (60+)**: For students aged 60 or above.
* **Church Name**: The name of your church or denomination.
* **Church City or Location**: The city or location of your church.
* **Church Member Status**: Your current status at your church.
	+ **Active member**: Currently an active member of your church.
	+ **Occasional attendee**: Attend church occasionally.
	+ **Visitor**: Not a member of your church.
	+ **New believer**: Recently accepted Christ as your Lord and Savior.
	+ **Non-member**: Not a member of any church.
* **Bible Class Title**: The title of the Bible class you're registering for.
* **Class Level**: Select the level of the class that suits your experience.
	+ **Beginner - new to Bible study**: For those new to Bible study.
	+ **Intermediate - some experience**: For those with some experience.
	+ **Advanced - extensive knowledge**: For those with extensive Bible knowledge.
	+ **No preference**: Not particular about the class level.
* **Class Format**: How would you like to learn in class.
	+ **In-person**: In-person classes.
	+ **Online/virtual**: Online or virtual classes.
	+ **Hybrid - both options**: A mix of in-person and online/virtual classes.
	+ **No preference**: Not particular about the class format.
* **Preferred Class Time**: Select the class time that suits your schedule.
	+ **Sunday morning**: Classes on Sundays in the morning.
	+ **Sunday afternoon**: Classes on Sundays in the afternoon.
	+ **Sunday evening**: Classes on Sundays in the evening.
	+ **Weekday morning**: Classes on weekdays in the morning.
	+ **Weekday afternoon**: Classes on weekdays in the afternoon.
	+ **Weekday evening**: Classes on weekdays in the evening.
	+ **Weekend**: Classes on weekends.
* **Familiarity with the Bible**: How familiar are you with the Bible?
	+ **Very familiar**: Familiar with the Bible.
	+ **Somewhat familiar**: Somewhat familiar with the Bible.
	+ **Familiar with some parts**: Familiar with some parts of the Bible.
	+ **New to Bible study**: New to Bible study.
	+ **No prior knowledge**: No prior knowledge of the Bible.
* **Prior Bible Study Experience**: How experienced are you with Bible study?
	+ **Extensive study**: Extensive experience with Bible study.
	+ **Regular participant**: Regular participant in Bible study.
	+ **Occasional participant**: Occasional participant in Bible study.
	+ **First time studying**: First time studying the Bible.
	+ **Never studied before**: Never studied the Bible before.
* **What Are Your Learning Goals**: Indicate why you're taking this class.
	+ **Deepen faith**: To deepen your faith.
	+ **Understand scripture better**: To better understand scripture.
	+ **Personal spiritual growth**: For personal spiritual growth.
	+ **Community connection**: To connect with others in the community.
	+ **Teach others**: To teach others about the Bible.
	+ **Answer specific questions**: To answer specific questions about the Bible.
	+ **General interest**: Just general interest in the Bible.
* **Preferred Study Method**: How do you like to learn?
	+ **Group discussion**: In a group discussion setting.
	+ **Lecture format**: In a lecture format.
	+ **Self-paced study**: At your own pace.
	+ **Mix of methods**: A mix of group discussion and lecture format.
	+ **No preference**: Not particular about the learning method.
* **Attendance Commitment**: How often can you attend class.
	+ **Every class**: Every class.
	+ **Most classes**: Most classes.
	+ **Occasional attendance**: Occasionally attend class.
	+ **Flexible schedule**: Flexible schedule to attend class.
	+ **Undecided**: Not committed to regular attendance.
* **Do You Have Required Materials**: Do you have the required materials?
	+ **Yes, I have everything**: All required materials.
	+ **Yes, most materials**: Most required materials.
	+ **Need assistance obtaining materials**: Need help obtaining the required materials.
	+ **Unsure what is needed**: Not sure what materials are needed.
* **Do You Need Transportation Assistance**: Do you need help with transportation?
	+ **Yes, please help arrange**: Need help with transportation.
	+ **No, I can arrange my own**: Can arrange my own transportation.
	+ **Not applicable**: Not applicable.
* **I Agree to Participate Respectfully**: Commit to participate in class respectfully.
	+ **I agree**: Commit to participate in class respectfully.
	+ **I do not agree**: Refuse to participate in class respectfully.
* **Additional Comments or Questions**: Any additional comments or questions.
## Tips
Please fill in the form carefully to ensure that you provide accurate and complete information. Review your answers before submitting the form to avoid duplication or errors. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
