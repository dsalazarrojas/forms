# Lesson Plan Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lesson Plan Form is a document used to plan and report on educational lessons. It is used to gather information about lessons and track progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form in a sequential manner, one page at a time.
2. Start with the Lesson Plan Form (Page 1), which has a brief description of the form.
3. Proceed to Page 2, where you will find fields for the topic, date, and time of the lesson.
4. On Page 3, select the subject of the lesson and provide any relevant information.
5. Move on to Page 4 to provide details about the teacher and student(s) involved.
6. Fill out the student contact information (Page 5) and teacher contact information (Page 6).
7. Leave comments and feedback about the lesson in the Teacher Comments and Student Comments sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Lesson Plan Form (Page 1)** (`lesson_plan_form_page_1`, text, optional): This is the introductory page of the form.
* **Topic (Page 2)** (`topic`, text, optional): Please describe the topic or subject of the lesson in a few words.
* **Date (Page 2)** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the lesson.
* **Time (Page 2)** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the lesson.
* **Subject (Page 3)** (`subject`, select one, optional): Choose the subject of the lesson from the options provided.
* **Teacher Name (Page 4)** (`teacher_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the teacher.
* **Teacher Email (Page 4)** (`teacher_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Teacher Phone (Page 4)** (`teacher_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Student Name (Page 5)** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter the name(s) of the student(s) involved.
* **Student Email (Page 5)** (`student_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the student(s).
* **Student Phone (Page 5)** (`student_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the student(s).
* **Teacher Comments (Page 6)** (`teacher_comments`, note, optional): Add any comments or feedback about the lesson.
* **Student Comments (Page 6)** (`student_comments`, note, optional): Add any comments or feedback from the student(s).
* **Lesson Planned (Page 6)** (`lesson_planned`, select one, optional): Check off if the lesson has been planned.
* **Lesson Actual (Page 6)** (`lesson_actual`, select one, optional): Check off if the lesson has been executed.
* **Lesson Attended (Page 6)** (`lesson_attended`, select one, optional): Check off if the student attended the lesson.

## Tips
- Make sure to check the "Lesson Planned", "Lesson Actual", and "Lesson Attended" fields after completing each section.
- Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
- Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy.
- This form is for your internal use. Do not share sensitive or confidential information.
