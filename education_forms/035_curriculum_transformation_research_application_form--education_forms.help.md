# Curriculum Transformation Research Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Curriculum Transformation Research Application Form is designed to collect information from applicants about their research projects, helping the education department to track and manage their applications.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your name and other contact information in the appropriate fields.
2. Provide details about your research, including the research question, methodology, objective, and outcome.
3. Upload any relevant files related to your research.
4. Choose the correct options for your research area, funding, duration, status, and submission status.
5. Confirm that you have submitted the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **applicant_info (Text)** (`applicant_info`, **Text**, Not Required): Enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **research_details (Text)** (`research_details`, **Text**, Not Required): Briefly describe your research project.
* **research_question (Text)** (`research_question`, **Text**, Not Required): Clearly state the research question you are trying to answer.
* **research_description (Text)** (`research_description`, **Text**, Not Required): Provide a detailed description of your research.
* **research_area (Select One)** (`research_area`, **Text**, Not Required): Choose the research area related to your project.
* **research_methodology (Text)** (`research_methodology`, **Text**, Not Required): Describe the methodology you will use for your research.
* **research_objective (Text)** (`research_objective`, **Text**, Not Required): State the objective of your research.
* **research_outcome (Select Multiple)** (`research_outcome`, **Text**, Not Required): Choose the outcomes you expect from your research.
* **research_funding (Number)** (`research_funding`, **Number**, Not Required): Enter the amount of funding you are seeking.
* **research_duration (Time)** (`research_duration`, **Text**, Not Required): Enter the duration of your research.
* **research_status (Select One)** (`research_status`, **Text**, Not Required): Choose the current status of your research.
* **research_notes (Note)** (`research_notes`, **Text**, Not Required): Add any additional notes about your research.
* **contact_info (Text)** (`contact_info`, **Text**, Not Required): Enter your contact information.
* **email (Email)** (`email`, **Email**, Not Required): Enter your email address.
* **phone (Text)** (`phone`, **Text**, Not Required): Enter your phone number.
* **research_file (Text)** (`research_file`, **Text**, Not Required): Upload any relevant files for your research.
* **research_attachment (Text)** (`research_attachment`, **Text**, Not Required): Attach any additional documents related to your research.
* **submission_status (Select One)** (`submission_status`, **Text**, Not Required): Choose the submission status of your research.
* **submitted_by (Select One)** (`submitted_by`, **Text**, Not Required): Confirm that you have submitted the form.
* **submitted_date (Date)** (`submitted_date`, **Date**, Not Required): Enter the date you submitted the form.
* **submitted_time (Time)** (`submitted_time`, **Time**, Not Required): Enter the time you submitted the form.
* **submitted_user (Select One)** (`submitted_user`, **Text**, Not Required): Confirm that you have submitted the form as the intended user.
* **submitted_ip (Text)** (`submitted_ip`, **Text**, Not Required): Enter the IP address of the user who submitted the form.
* **submitted_country (Select One)** (`submitted_country`, **Text**, Not Required): Choose the country of the user who submitted the form.

## Tips

* Make sure to review all fields before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* Enter your email address correctly to ensure that you can receive any relevant notifications.
* Upload any relevant files or attachments to support your research application.
* Clearly state the research question and objective to ensure that the education department understands your project.
* Choose the correct submission status to reflect the current state of your research application.
