The form purpose is to collect information about students who are attending an orientation day event. This form will help gather necessary details about the students, including their contact information, medical conditions, and special needs, to ensure their safety and provide the best possible experience for them during the event.

The form is structured into different sections, each with a specific focus, such as student information, guardian contact, medical information, and emergency contact.

Before starting, please review the form carefully and ensure that all required fields are completed.

## How To Complete This Form

Please complete the form in the following order:

1. Fill in the student's name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide the guardian's name, email, and phone number.
3. Enter the student's address and grade level.
4. Answer the question about emergency contact and relationship.
5. If necessary, add any medical conditions, allergies, or special needs information.
6. If the student takes medication, indicate whether they do or do not, and provide additional details about the medication.
7. Review and complete any additional fields as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Student Information

* **Student Name**: A required field to collect the student's name.
* **Email**: A required field to collect the student's email address.
* **Phone**: An optional field to collect the student's phone number.
* **Address**: An optional field to collect the student's address.
* **Grade**: An optional field to collect the student's grade level.
* **Grade Level**: An optional field to collect more detailed information about the student's grade level.
* **Parent Signature**: An optional field for the parent or guardian to provide a signature.
* **Emergency Contact**: A field to answer whether there is an emergency contact for the student, and if so, provide their name and relationship.

### Guardian Contact Information

* **Guardian Name**: An optional field to collect the guardian's name.
* **Email**: An optional field to collect the guardian's email address.
* **Phone**: An optional field to collect the guardian's phone number.

### Medical Information

* **Allergies**: An optional field to collect any allergies or special dietary restrictions.
* **Medical Condition**: An optional field to collect any medical conditions.
* **Special Needs**: An optional field to collect any special needs information.
* **Medications**: A field to answer whether the student takes any medications, and if so, provide additional details about the medication.
* **Medication Frequency**: An optional field to collect the frequency of medication administration.
* **Medication Time**: An optional field to collect the time of medication administration.
* **Medication Dosage**: An optional field to collect the dosage of medication.

### Additional Information

* **Attend**: A field to answer whether the student will attend, and if so, provide additional details.
* **Notes**: An optional field for any additional notes.
