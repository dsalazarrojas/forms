## Education System Reform Awareness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Education System Reform Awareness Survey is a form designed to gather information about individuals' awareness and perception of the current education system reforms. The data collected will help to identify areas where the reforms are well-understood and areas where there is a need for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below.

1.  Read each page carefully and answer the questions according to the instructions provided.
2.  Fill out all the required fields, which are marked as required.
3.  If you need help with any of the fields, refer to the field-by-field explanation section below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Demographic** (`Demographic`, `select_one`, required): This field asks you to select your gender. Please choose one of the options provided: Male, Female, or Other.
* **Education Level** (`Education Level`, `select_multiple`, required): This field asks you to select your current level of education. Please choose one or more of the options provided: Primary, Secondary, Tertiary, Postgraduate.
* **Education Field** (`Education Field`, `select_multiple`, required): This field asks you to select your area of education. Please choose one or more of the options provided: Arts, Humanities, Sciences, Technology, Engineering, Health and Social Sciences.
* **Awareness of the current education system reforms** (`Awareness of the current education system reforms`, `text`, required): This field is a text area where you can provide a brief description of your understanding of the current education system reforms. Please type in your response according to your knowledge and understanding.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, `note`, optional): This field is an opportunity for you to provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the education system reforms. Please use this field to share your thoughts.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): This field is where you enter your email address. Please provide a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required): This field is where you enter your phone number. Please provide a valid phone number that you check regularly.
* **Submission** (`Submission`, `time`, required): This field is for timestamping when you submit the form. It's automatically populated and does not require any action from the user.
