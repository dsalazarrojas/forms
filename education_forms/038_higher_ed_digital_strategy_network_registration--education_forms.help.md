# Higher Ed Digital Strategy Network Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for registering individuals with the Higher Ed Digital Strategy Network. The purpose of this form is to collect information about the user, their role and interests, and their preferred contact method. This form will help the network administrators understand who is interested in being part of the network and what areas of interest they have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide Your First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
2. **Provide Your Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
3. **Enter Your Email**: Enter your email address to be contacted by the network administrators.
4. **Select Your Role**: Choose one of the options from the dropdown menu to indicate your role in the education sector.
5. **Enter Your University**: Enter the name of your current or affiliated university.
6. **Enter Your Location**: Enter the city or region where you are currently located or work.
7. **Enter Your Department**: If you are a university employee, enter your department name.
8. **Select Your Interests**: Choose all that apply from the list of interests related to digital strategy in education.
9. **How Did You Hear About Us?**: Share how you came across the Higher Ed Digital Strategy Network.
10. **Add Comments**: If you have any additional comments or thoughts, you can add them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: A field to input your first name as it appears on official documents.
* **Last Name**: A field to input your last name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to be contacted by the network administrators.
* **Role**: Choose one of the options from the dropdown menu to indicate your role in the education sector.
	+ Digital Strategy: A professional who develops and implements digital strategies.
	+ Digital Marketing: A professional who manages and improves digital presence.
	+ Instructional Design: A professional who creates educational content and materials.
	+ Other: Any other role not listed above.
* **University**: Enter the name of your current or affiliated university.
* **Location**: Enter the city or region where you are currently located or work.
* **Department**: If you are a university employee, enter your department name.
* **Interests**: Select all that apply from the list of interests related to digital strategy in education.
	+ Digital Accessibility: A person interested in making educational resources accessible.
	+ Digital Analytics: A person interested in data analysis for education.
	+ Digital Content Strategy: A person who creates or curates content for education.
	+ Digital Engagement: A person who creates engaging educational experiences.
	+ Digital Marketing: A person who promotes educational programs or events.
	+ Instructional Design: A person who creates educational materials and experiences.
	+ Other: Any other interest not listed above.
* **How Did You Hear About Us?**: Share how you came across the Higher Ed Digital Strategy Network.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or thoughts you may have.
* **File**: Upload any supporting files or documents.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the network administrators at form@gic.mx.
