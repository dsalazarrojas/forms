# BCC Course Selection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The BCC Course Selection form is a form designed for students to select the courses they want to enroll in for the current or future semesters. It is intended for students who are currently enrolled or will be enrolled in the university.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student name.
2. Enter your student ID number.
3. Provide your official student email.
4. Select the semester you are enrolling in (e.g., Fall 2026).
5. List the course codes and titles you have selected.
6. Enter the total number of credits for the selected courses.
7. If applicable, indicate the status of your advisor approval (Approved, Pending, or Denied).
8. If applicable, enter any special scheduling requests (e.g., time conflicts, health needs).
9. If applicable, enter your contact phone number.
10. Finally, select the date of form submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your full name in this field.
* **Student ID number** (`student_id`, text, required): Enter your university student identifier in this field.
* **Student email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your official student email in this field.
* **Semester** (`semester`, text, required): Enter the semester you are enrolling in (e.g., Fall 2026).
* **Courses selected** (`courses_selected`, text, required): List the course codes and titles you have selected.
* **Total credits** (`total_credits`, number, required): Enter the sum of credits for the selected courses.
* **Advisor approval status** (`advisor_approval`, select_one, required): Select the status of your advisor approval (Approved, Pending, or Denied).
* **Special scheduling requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Enter any special scheduling requests (e.g., time conflicts, health needs).
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number (optional).
* **Submission date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Select the date of form submission.

Note: All fields marked "required" must be filled out. If a field is marked "optional", it can be left empty.
