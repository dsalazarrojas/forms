# School Climate Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gauge the school climate by asking questions about your experiences, opinions, and perceptions of the school environment. Your input will help us better understand your needs and concerns, and inform decisions to improve the school community.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer it according to your experiences and opinions.
2. For "Select one" and "Select multiple" questions, choose the option that best represents your answer.
3. For "Date" and "Time" fields, enter your answer in the expected format (mm/dd/yyyy and hh:mm am/pm, respectively).
4. Ensure you fill out as much of the form as you can, but don't worry if you can't answer every question.
5. If a question seems unclear or you are unsure of the answer, use your best judgment or skip it if possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School climate survey** (`school_climate_survey_1`, text, optional): A brief description of your current school climate and experience.
* **Select one** (`school_climate_survey_2`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the available options that best represents your current school climate.
	+ Option 1: You are very satisfied with the school climate.
	+ Option 2: You are somewhat satisfied with the school climate.
	+ Option 3: You are somewhat dissatisfied with the school climate.
* **Select multiple** (`school_climate_survey_3`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the options that apply to your current school climate.
	+ Option 1: You feel supported by your peers and teachers.
	+ Option 2: You feel challenged academically.
	+ Option 3: You feel connected to the school community.
* **School climate survey date** (`school_climate_survey_4`, date, optional): The date you are answering the survey, in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **School climate survey time** (`school_climate_survey_5`, time, optional): The time you are answering the survey, in the format hh:mm am/pm.
* **Email** (`school_climate_survey_6`, email, optional): Your email address, where you can be reached if we need to follow up with you about your answers.
* **School climate survey phone** (`school_climate_survey_7`, text, optional): A backup contact method, in case we need to reach out to you about your answers.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any concerns or feedback, you can contact us at [insert email or contact info].
