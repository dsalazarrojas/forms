# Evaluation Of Relevance And Reliability - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Evaluation Of Relevance And Reliability" form is designed to collect user feedback regarding the evaluation of the relevance and reliability of a particular item, topic, or concept.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and answer each field according to your thoughts and opinions.
2. Use the free-form text fields to provide your input.
3. For the "quality" field, select one of the options that best describes your assessment of the item's quality.
4. For the "reliability" field, select all the options that apply to your assessment of the item's reliability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input 1 (text)**: This field is a free-form text field where you can input your thoughts or opinions regarding the item.
* **Relevance (text)**: This field is also a free-form text field where you can input your thoughts or opinions regarding the relevance of the item.
* **Quality (select_one)**: This field asks you to rate the quality of the item. Select one of the options that best describes your assessment.
	+ Option 1: Highly reliable
	+ Option 2: Somewhat reliable
	+ Option 3: Not very reliable
* **Reliability (select_multiple)**: This field allows you to select all the options that apply to your assessment of the item's reliability.
	+ Option 1: Highly reliable
	+ Option 2: Somewhat reliable
	+ Option 3: Not very reliable
* **Date (date)**: This field asks for the date of the evaluation.
* **Time (time)**: This field asks for the time of the evaluation.
* **Note (text)**: This field is a free-form text field where you can input any additional notes or comments regarding the evaluation.
* **Email (email)**: This field asks for your email address.
* **Phone (text)**: This field is a free-form text field where you can input your phone number.
* **User Input 2-25 (text)**: These fields are all free-form text fields where you can input your thoughts or opinions regarding the item. Note that these fields are not used for the form's primary purpose and are likely meant to be used for additional comments or information.

## Tips
Please fill out the form carefully and accurately, and be sure to select all applicable options for the "reliability" field. If you have any additional comments or thoughts that don't fit in the provided fields, please use the "note" field to input them.
