# Distance Learning Feedback Form For Parents - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Distance Learning Feedback Form For Parents is a tool used to gather feedback from parents about their experience with distance learning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Please provide your honest and constructive feedback to help us improve the distance learning experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Feedback**: Please share your general thoughts and opinions about the distance learning experience. This is an opportunity to provide any positive or negative feedback about the course, instructor, or overall quality of the experience.
	+ **Required**: No
	+ **Type**: Note (open-ended text field)
* **Technical Support**: Describe any issues you encountered with the technology or platform used during the course, such as video or audio quality, lag, or connection problems.
	+ **Required**: No
	+ **Type**: Text field
* **Virtual Class Improvement**: Select one or more areas where the virtual class could be improved, such as video quality, audio quality, lag, or connection. You can also choose "Other (please specify)" and provide more detailed feedback.
	+ **Required**: Yes
	+ **Type**: Select one (options: Video quality, Audio quality, Lag, Connection, Other (please specify))
* **Communication**: Select the areas where the communication with the instructor or support team was most helpful or challenging. You can choose one or more options, such as Response time, Clarity of instructions, Availability of materials.
	+ **Required**: No
	+ **Type**: Select multiple (options: Response time, Clarity of instructions, Availability of materials)
* **Other Areas for Improvement**: Provide any additional feedback or suggestions that you think could improve the distance learning experience.
	+ **Required**: No
	+ **Type**: Text field
* **Suggestions**: Share any ideas or suggestions you have to enhance the distance learning experience.
	+ **Required**: No
	+ **Type**: Text field
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or thoughts about the distance learning experience.
	+ **Required**: No
	+ **Type**: Text field
* **Email**: Provide your email address so we can follow up with you if necessary.
	+ **Required**: No
	+ **Type**: Email field
* **Phone**: Provide your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
	+ **Required**: No
	+ **Type**: Text field
* **Other Contact Info**: Provide any additional contact information you think would be helpful for us to follow up with you.
	+ **Required**: No
	+ **Type**: Text field

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when providing feedback to help us better understand your experience.
- If you have a suggestion, please provide as much detail as you can.
- If you have any concerns or complaints, please let us know so we can address them promptly.
