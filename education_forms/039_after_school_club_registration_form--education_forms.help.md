# After School Club Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register your child for an after-school club program. It collects essential information about your child, your relationship to them, and your contact details. This guide will walk you through each section of the form to help you complete it accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling out the Student Information section, which includes:
	* Student Full Name: Enter your child's complete legal name.
	* Date of Birth: Enter your child's date of birth.
	* Current Grade Level: Select the grade level your child is currently in.
2.  In the Club Selection section, choose the after-school club your child would like to join:
	* Select After-School Club: Choose one of the available clubs.
	* Reason for Club Choice: Explain why you chose this particular club for your child.
3.  Move on to the Parent/Guardian Information section:
	* Parent/Guardian Full Name: Enter your complete legal name.
	* Relationship to Student: Choose your relationship to the student.
	* Email Address: Enter your email address for program updates and communication.
	* Phone Number: Enter your primary phone number for contact.
4.  Provide Emergency Contact Information:
	* Emergency Contact Name: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
	* Emergency Contact Relationship: Describe your relationship to the emergency contact.
	* Emergency Contact Phone: Enter the phone number for the emergency contact.
5.  If necessary, fill out the Medical Information section:
	* Medical Conditions: List any medical conditions your child has.
	* Current Medications: List any medications your child is taking.
	* Allergies: List any allergies your child has.
	* Medical Authorization: Confirm if you authorize program staff to seek medical attention if needed.
6.  In the Transportation Information section:
	* Transportation Method: Choose how your child will get home from the after-school program.
	* Other Authorized Adults: List any other adults authorized to pick up your child.
7.  Complete the Dismissal Instructions section:
	* Special Instructions: Enter any special instructions for dismissing your child.
8.  Finally, review and agree to the Registration Agreements:
	* Terms and Conditions Agreement: Confirm you agree to the terms and conditions of the program.
	* Photo Release Agreement: Confirm if you give permission for your child to be photographed.
9.  Sign and submit the form electronically.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Full Name** (`student_full_name`, text, **required**): Enter your child's complete legal name.

* **Student Grade Level** (`student_grade`, select_one, **required**): Choose the grade level your child is currently in.
* **School Attended** (`student_school`, text, **required**): Enter the name of the school your child attends.
* **Student ID Number** (`student_id_number`, text, **optional**): Enter your child's student identification number (if available).
* **Select After-School Club** (`selected_club`, select_one, **required**): Choose one of the available after-school clubs.
* **Reason for Club Choice** (`club_choice_reason`, text, **required**): Explain why you chose this particular club for your child.

* **Relationship to Student** (`parent_guardian_relationship`, select_one, **required**): Choose your relationship to the student.
* **Email Address** (`parent_guardian_email`, email, **required**): Enter your email address for program updates and communication.
* **Phone Number** (`parent_guardian_phone`, text, **required**): Enter your primary phone number for contact.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, **required**): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, **required**): Describe your relationship to the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, **required**): Enter the phone number for the emergency contact.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, **optional**): List any medical conditions your child has.
* **Current Medications** (`medications`, text, **optional**): List any medications your child is taking.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, **optional**): List any allergies your child has.
* **Medical Authorization** (`medical_authorizations`, select_one, **required**): Confirm if you authorize program staff to seek medical attention if needed.
