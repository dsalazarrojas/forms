The purpose of this form is to collect information about students' experiences with their instructor, which can help instructors evaluate their teaching quality, identify areas of improvement, and make necessary adjustments to provide better learning experiences for their students. This form should only be used by students to evaluate their own instructors, and not by instructors to evaluate their peers or students who are not currently enrolled in their courses.

Before filling out this form, please ensure that you have not already evaluated your instructor using this form previously. Duplicate evaluations can be time-consuming and may not provide valuable insights for the instructor.

To ensure the accuracy and usefulness of the evaluations, please provide honest and constructive feedback. Be as specific as possible when describing your experiences, and avoid making general statements or vague comments.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

## Purpose
The simple_instructor_evaluation_form_template is a tool for students to provide feedback on their instructor's teaching quality, which helps instructors understand their strengths and weaknesses and make improvements to their teaching methods.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Enter your name and email address in the fields provided.
3. Select the semester you took the course with the instructor.
4. Rate the instructor's performance using the provided options (Good, Fair).
5. Provide any comments or suggestions you have for the instructor.
6. If you wish to be contacted by the instructor or the department, please enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`Student Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your full name as it appears on your official records.
* **Instructor** (`instructor`, `number`, required: false): Enter the ID number of your instructor.
* **Course Name** (`Course Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the course you took with the instructor.
* **Semester** (`semester`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the semester you took the course with the instructor (e.g., Spring 2022).
* **Rating** (`Rating`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Rate the instructor's performance as Good or Fair.
* **Comment** (`Comment`, `note`, required: false): Provide any comments or suggestions you have for the instructor.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address so that the instructor or department can contact you.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted by the instructor or department.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, `note`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have for the instructor.
* **Submitted By** (`Submitted By`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name and email address so that the instructor or department can contact you.

## Tips
* Please be specific and honest in your feedback.
* Avoid making general statements or vague comments.
* If you have any concerns or complaints, try to focus on how the instructor can improve their teaching methods.
* Remember that this form is for your own evaluation, so please fill it out only once per instructor per semester.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can contact the department or instructor for guidance.
