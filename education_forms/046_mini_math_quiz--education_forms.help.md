# Mini Math Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users test their mathematical skills by solving a series of math problems. The form is divided into multiple pages, each containing a few fields. Users can submit their responses multiple times, and the form will keep track of their progress and performance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "submit" button on each page to move on to the next page.
2. Fill out the math problem on each page, making sure to enter a numerical answer.
3. If you want to provide an email address, name, and phone number, fill those fields out with the corresponding information.
4. Click on the "submit" button again to submit your response.
5. Review your progress and performance after submitting each page.
