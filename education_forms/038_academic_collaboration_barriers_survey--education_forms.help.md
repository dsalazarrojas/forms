# Academic Collaboration Barriers Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on academic collaboration barriers faced by individuals in academia. It will help researchers understand the challenges and difficulties that academics encounter when collaborating with others.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question as accurately and honestly as possible. You will be asked about your demographics (role, institution, and experience), your current collaboration status, frequency of collaboration, and the barriers you face when collaborating. You will also be asked about potential solutions and suggestions to overcome these barriers. Please select all that apply when multiple options are provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your current role** (`respondent_role`, `select_one`): This question asks about your current role in academia, such as faculty member, researcher, postdoctoral fellow, or graduate student.
* **Institution name** (`institution_name`, `text`): Enter the name of the institution where you are currently affiliated.
* **Department or discipline** (`department`, `text`): Enter the name of your department or discipline of study.
* **Years in academia** (`years_in_academia`, `select_one`): Select the number of years you have been in academia.
* **Are you currently engaged in any academic collaborations** (`current_collaborations`, `select_one`): This question asks about your current collaboration status, such as within your institution, with other institutions, or not at all.
* **How often do you engage in collaborative projects** (`collaboration_frequency`, `select_one`): Select how often you collaborate on projects, such as regularly, occasionally, rarely, or never.
* **What are the most significant barriers to academic collaboration you have experienced** (`top_barriers`, `select_multiple`): Select all the barriers you have experienced, such as lack of time, heavy teaching load, insufficient funding, etc.
* **Which single barrier has the greatest impact on your ability to collaborate** (`most_critical_barrier`, `select_one`): Select the single barrier that has the greatest impact on your collaboration.
* **How significant are communication challenges in your collaborations** (`communication_challenges`, `select_one`): Select the significance of communication challenges in your collaborations, such as not significant, somewhat significant, or very significant.
* **Do technology or infrastructure limitations hinder your collaborations** (`technology_barriers`, `select_one`): Select how significant technology or infrastructure limitations are to your collaborations.
* **What type of support would most help you collaborate more effectively** (`desired_support`, `select_multiple`): Select all the types of support that would help you collaborate more effectively, such as dedicated collaboration funding, reduced teaching load, etc.
* **What specific changes would you recommend to reduce collaboration barriers** (`open_suggestions`, `text`): Enter any specific changes or suggestions you have to reduce collaboration barriers.
* **Any additional comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`): Enter any additional comments or thoughts you have about academic collaboration barriers.
