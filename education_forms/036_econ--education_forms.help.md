# ECON - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form, "ECON", is for users to provide information about their educational background and research interests. It is not a requirement form, and all fields are optional.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required and optional fields according to your needs. You can skip any field you don't want to provide information for.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (user_input, text, optional): This field is for you to provide any additional information you want to share about yourself.
* **Education Level** (education_level, text, optional): Please enter your current education level (e.g., Bachelor's, Master's, Ph.D.).
* **Gender** (gender, select_one, optional): Select your gender to help us better understand your background.
* **Email** (email, email, optional): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Birthdate** (birthdate, date, optional): Enter your birthdate in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time** (time, time, optional): Enter the time you are available to be contacted.
* **Note** (note, note, optional): Add any additional information you'd like to share about yourself.
* **Education Background** (education_background, text, optional): Describe your educational background.
* **Research Goal** (research_goal, text, optional): Enter your research goal or area of interest.
* **Research Area** (research_area, text, optional): Describe the area of research you are interested in.
* **Research Question** (research_question, text, optional): Enter any specific research question you have.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when providing your information.
- If you're unsure about a field, skip it or contact us for clarification.
