This form is designed to collect information about incidents that occur in daycare settings. The purpose of this form is to gather details about the incident, including the description, date, location, and other relevant details. This form is typically used by daycare staff or administrators to report incidents that may have occurred during the care of children in their facility. It is not intended for general incident reporting, but rather for specific incidents related to daycare services.

Before filling out this form, please ensure that you are authorized to report incidents. If you are unsure, please consult your supervisor or HR department.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form carefully and ensure that you are reporting an incident that occurred in a daycare setting.
2.  Fill out the form with as much detail as possible, using the space provided for each field.
3.  Answer all required fields. If a field is not required but you have relevant information, you can still fill it out for further clarification.
4.  If you are unsure about any field or have any questions, please contact your supervisor or HR department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Daycare Incident Report** (`daycare_incident_report_form`): This is the title of the form and a brief description of its purpose.
*   **Incident Description** (`incident_description`): Describe the incident in as much detail as possible.
*   **Incident Date** (`incident_date`): Enter the date the incident occurred.
*   **Incident Time** (`incident_time`): Enter the time the incident occurred.
*   **Report By** (`report_by`): Enter the name and title of the person reporting the incident.
*   **Contact Person** (`contact_person`): Enter the name and contact information of the person who can be contacted for further information.
*   **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone_number`): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
*   **Daycare Name** (`daycare_name`): Enter the name of the daycare facility.
*   **Daycare Address** (`daycare_address`): Enter the address of the daycare facility.
*   **Incident Location** (`incident_location`): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
*   **Witness** (`witness`): Enter the name of any witnesses, if applicable.
*   **Witness Phone Number** (`witness_phone_number`): Enter the phone number of the witness.
*   **Witness Name** (`witness_name`): Enter the name of the witness.
*   **Action Taken** (`action_taken`): Enter the actions taken in response to the incident.
*   **Follow Up** (`follow_up`): Enter any follow-up information, if applicable.
*   **Incident Type** (`incident_type`): Select the type of incident from the options provided.
*   **Incident Description Type** (`incident_description_type`): Select the type of incident description from the options provided.
*   **Incident Description Detail** (`incident_description_detail`): Enter a detailed description of the incident.
*   **Witness Statement** (`witness_statement`): Enter a statement from the witness, if applicable.
*   **Staff Involved** (`staff_involved`): Indicate whether any staff members were involved in the incident.
*   **Staff Member Involved** (`staff_member`): Enter the name of the staff member involved.
*   **Parent Concerned** (`parent_concerned`): Indicate whether any parent was concerned about the incident.
*   **Staff Concerned** (`staff_concerned`): Indicate whether any staff member was concerned about the incident.
*   **Follow Up By** (`follow_up_by`): Enter the name of who will follow up on the incident.
*   **Follow Up Date** (`follow_up_date`): Enter the date the follow-up will occur.

Please note that all fields are optional unless otherwise specified as required.
