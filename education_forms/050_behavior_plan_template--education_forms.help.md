# Behavior Plan Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Behavior Plan Template is designed to support educators in creating a comprehensive plan to address a student's behavior, ensuring that the student's needs are understood and addressed in a consistent and effective manner.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the student's full name, which is a required field that should contain the student's legal name.
2. Enter the plan's effective date, which is the date that the behavior plan will be implemented.
3. Schedule a review date, which is a required field, typically 4-6 weeks after the start date, to review the student's progress and adjust the plan as needed.
4. In the section "Behavior Goals", provide a clear and concise description of the target behavior and the desired replacement behavior.
5. In the "Success Criteria" field, identify the specific criteria that will be used to measure the student's success, such as a percentage or specific behaviors.
6. In the "Intervention Strategies" section, outline the steps and approaches that will be taken to support the student's behavior, including proactive and preventive strategies.
7. In the "Positive Reinforcement" section, outline the rewards and incentives that will be used to reinforce desired behavior.
8. In the "Corrective Consequences" section, outline the steps that will be taken when the student exhibits problem behavior.
9. In the "Agreement and Sign-off" section, have the student and parent sign off on the plan, indicating their agreement to follow it.
10. Finally, in the "Plan Coordinator Name" field, enter the name of the person who created the plan, and in the "Date Signed" field, enter the date the plan was signed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter the student's full name, as it appears on their records.
* **Plan Effective Date** (`effective_date_plan`, `date`, required): Enter the date the behavior plan will be implemented.
* **Plan Review Date** (`review_date_plan`, `date`, required): Enter the date to review the student's progress and adjust the plan as needed.
* **Target Behavior Description** (`target_behavior_description`, `text`, required): Describe the student's target behavior in observable and measurable terms.
* **Desired Replacement Behavior** (`replacement_behavior_goal`, `text`, required): Describe the desired replacement behavior that the student will exhibit.
* **Success Criteria** (`success_criteria`, `text`, required): Identify the specific criteria that will be used to measure the student's success.
* **Intervention Strategies** (`proactive_strategies`, `text`, required): Outline the proactive and preventive strategies that will be taken to support the student's behavior.
* **Positive Reinforcement** (`positive_reinforcement_plan`, `text`, required): Outline the rewards and incentives that will be used to reinforce desired behavior.
* **Frequency of Reinforcement** (`reinforcement_frequency`, `text`, required): Specify the frequency at which reinforcement will be provided.
* **Tiered Consequences** (`tiered_consequences_description`, `text`, required): Outline the step-by-step response plan for problem behavior.
* **Agree to Try** (`student_agreement_check`, `select_one`, required): Check the box indicating that the student agrees to try their best to follow the plan.
* **Parent Agreement** (`parent_agreement_check`, `select_one`, required): Check the box indicating that the parent agrees to support the plan at home.
* **Plan Coordinator Name** (`lead_coordinator_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person who created the plan.
* **Date Signed** (`coordinator_signature_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the plan was signed.

**Tips**

* Make sure to tailor the plan to the individual student's needs and goals.
* Involve the student and parent in the planning process to ensure that all parties are on the same page.
* Regularly review and update the plan to ensure it remains effective and meets the student's changing needs.
* Use clear and concise language in all fields to ensure that the plan is easy to understand.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed before finalizing the plan.
