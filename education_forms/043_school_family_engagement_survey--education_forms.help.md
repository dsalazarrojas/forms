# School Family Engagement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from school families, teachers, and administrators to help the school administration understand their needs and concerns and make informed decisions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please read each field carefully and answer accordingly. The form consists of several sections, each with its own set of questions or rating scales. You can navigate between sections using the tabs at the top of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **How many students are enrolled?**
* **Student Enrollment Number** (`student_enrollment_number`, number, optional): This field asks for the number of students enrolled in the school. Please enter a numerical value.

### 2. **School Rating**
* **School Rating** (`school_rating`, number, optional): This field asks for a rating of the school's overall performance. Please choose a number between 1 and 5, with 5 being the highest rating.

### 3. **Student Survey**
* **Student Survey** (`student_survey`, select_one, optional): Please select one of the options that best describes your experience with the school.

### 4. **Student Comments**
* **Student Comments** (`student_comments`, note, optional): This field is for you to provide any additional comments about your experience with the school.

### 5. **Teacher Survey**
* **Teacher Survey** (`teacher_survey`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the options that apply to your experience with the school.

### 6. **Parent Survey**
* **Parent Survey** (`parent_survey`, select_one, optional): Please select one of the options that best describes your experience with the school.

### 7. **Teacher Rating**
* **Teacher Rating** (`teacher_rating`, number, optional): This field asks for a rating of the teachers' performance. Please choose a number between 1 and 5, with 5 being the highest rating.

### 8. **Teacher Comments**
* **Teacher Comments** (`teacher_comments`, note, optional): This field is for you to provide any additional comments about the teachers' performance.

### 9. **Parent Rating**
* **Parent Rating** (`parent_rating`, number, optional): This field asks for a rating of the parent-teacher relationship. Please choose a number between 1 and 5, with 5 being the highest rating.

### 10. **School Family Engagement Survey Comments**
* **School Family Engagement Survey Comments** (`school_family_survey_comments`, note, optional): This field is for you to provide any additional comments about your experience with the school.

### 11. **School Administrator**
* **School Administrator** (`school_administrator_rating`, number, optional): This field asks for a rating of the school's administration's performance. Please choose a number between 1 and 5, with 5 being the highest rating.

### 12. **Teacher Administrator**
* **Teacher Administrator** (`teacher_administrator_rating`, number, optional): This field asks for a rating of the teacher's administration's performance. Please choose a number between 1 and 5, with 5 being the highest rating.

### 13. **School Environment**
* **School Environment** (`school_environment`, text, optional): This field allows you to describe your experience with the school's physical and social environment.

### 14. **Minimalist Form**
* **Minimalist Form** (`minimalist_form`, select_one, optional): Please select if you prefer the current minimalist form of the school's surveys.
