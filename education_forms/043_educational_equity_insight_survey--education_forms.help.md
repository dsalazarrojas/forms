# Educational Equity Insight Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Equity Insight Survey is designed to gather information about your school's demographics. This information will help us gain a deeper understanding of the educational equity landscape, and inform our strategies to support your students and school.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps below:
1. Enter your school's name in the "school_name" field.
2. Select your district from the options provided in the "district_name" field.
3. Choose your role in the school from the options in the "role" field. You can select multiple roles.
4. Enter the number of students in your school in the "student_count" field.
5. Select the grade level of your students from the options in the grade_level field.
6. Choose your preferred language from the options in the preferred_language field.
7. You can add any contact information in the "contact_info" field if you wish to provide additional context.
8. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
9. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **school_name**: Enter your school's name.
* **district_name**: Select your school district from the options provided.
* **role**: Choose your role in the school from the options: Principal, Teacher, District Staff, or Other.
* **role**: You can select multiple roles if applicable.
* **school_year**: Enter the current school year (e.g., 2022).
* **student_count**: Enter the number of students in your school.
* **grade_level**: Select the grade level of your students (e.g., 9th, 10th, 11th, 12th).
* **preferred_language**: Choose your preferred language from English, Spanish, or Other.
* **contact_info**: You can add any additional contact information if necessary.
* **email**: Enter your email address.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Please fill out as much information as possible, but do not feel obligated to answer any questions that do not apply to you.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact the support team.
* You can save your work and come back later if needed, but please avoid duplicating your efforts.
