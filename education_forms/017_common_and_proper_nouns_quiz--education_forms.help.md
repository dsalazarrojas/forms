# Common And Proper Nouns Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Common And Proper Nouns Quiz" form is designed to test your understanding of common and proper nouns. It will guide you through a series of questions and exercises that will help you identify and use these linguistic elements correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, read each question carefully and choose the correct answer from the provided options. For open-ended fields, provide clear and concise answers. Make sure to double-check your answers before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Full Name** (`student_name_nouns_quiz`, `text`, required/optional): This field is where you'll enter your name to identify yourself. Please enter your name exactly as it appears on your identification.
* **Date** (`quiz_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you're submitting the quiz. This will help us track your progress and ensure you're on the right track.
* **Select the proper noun in this sentence** (`question_one`, `select_one`, required/optional): This question tests your ability to identify the proper noun in a given sentence. Choose the correct answer from the options provided.
* **Which of these is a common noun** (`question_two`, `select_one`, required/optional): This question checks your understanding of common nouns. Select the correct answer from the options provided.
* **Identify the proper noun - My dog, Max, loves to play fetch.** (`question_three`, `select_one`, required/optional): This question assesses your ability to identify proper nouns in a sentence. Choose the correct answer from the options provided.
* **Which word should always be capitalized** (`question_four`, `select_one`, required/optional): This question evaluates your understanding of capitalization rules for nouns. Select the correct answer from the options provided.
* **Grand Canyon is a...** (`question_five`, `select_one`, required/optional): This question tests your understanding of common and proper nouns in a sentence. Choose the correct answer from the options provided.
* **List three common nouns you can find in a classroom** (`write_common_noun_example`, `text`, required/optional): Provide three common nouns that you can find in a classroom, separated by commas.
* **Write a proper noun for each common noun - teacher, store, holiday.** (`write_proper_noun_example`, `text`, required/optional): For each common noun provided, come up with a proper noun that corresponds to it (e.g., teacher -> Mrs. Smith).
* **Write a complete sentence using at least one proper noun** (`sentence_writing_proper`, `text`, required/optional): Write a sentence using at least one proper noun, remembering to use correct capitalization.
* **How well do you think you understand common and proper nouns** (`nouns_quiz_self_reflection`, `select_one`, required/optional): This question invites you to reflect on your understanding of common and proper nouns. Select the answer that best describes your confidence level.
* **Any questions you have about nouns** (`student_reflection_notes`, `text`, required/optional): If you have any questions about common and proper nouns, type them here.
* **I have double-checked my answers before submitting** (`submission_acknowledgment_quiz`, `select_one`, required/optional): Confirm that you've reviewed your answers before submitting the form.

## Tips
- Read each question carefully before answering.
- For open-ended fields, provide clear and concise answers.
- Double-check your answers before submitting.
- If you're unsure about any question, type your query in the "Any questions you have about nouns" field.
- Remember to use correct capitalization rules when answering the "Write a complete sentence using at least one proper noun" question.
