# Academic Diversity And Equity Network Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and registration details from individuals interested in joining the Academic Diversity And Equity Network.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and fill in the required information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed before proceeding.
3. Double-check your details before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name**: This field collects your given and family name.
* **Email address**: This field requires your preferred contact email address.
* **Institution or organization**: This field is for the name of your employer or university.
* **Role or title**: This field is for your job title or position.
* **Areas of interest**: Select one or more topics that interest you from the provided options.
	+ **Policy and governance**
	+ **Curriculum and pedagogy**
	+ **Hiring and retention**
	+ **Community engagement**
	+ **Accessibility and accommodations**
* **Accessibility needs or accommodations**: Describe any accessibility requirements you have, if applicable.
* **Preferred participation type**: Choose how you plan to engage with the network:
	+ **Attendee**
	+ **Speaker or facilitator**
	+ **Contributor or volunteer**
	+ **Organizer**
* **Communication preferences**: Select the ways you prefer to receive updates:
	+ **Email**
	+ **Newsletter**
	+ **Slack or chat**
	+ **Webinars**
* **Additional comments**: This field is for any further information you would like to share with the network.

## Tips
- Ensure accuracy and completeness in your answers to each field.
- Review your submission carefully before submitting it.
- If you encounter any issues or questions while filling the form, feel free to contact the network administrators for assistance.
