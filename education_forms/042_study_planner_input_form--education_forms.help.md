# Study Planner Input Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you create a study plan tailored to your needs. It will guide you through a series of questions to collect relevant information about your study goals, schedule, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review each field carefully and answer the questions as accurately as possible.
2.  Fill out the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
3.  You can choose from the provided options for select fields (e.g., Schedule, Goal Type, Preferred Time Slots, Follow Up Time).
4.  For fields with a "hint" field, use the suggested guidance for your response.
5.  If you are unsure about any field or option, please ask your administrator or contact our support team for help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Study Goals** (`study_planner_input`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your study goals, such as "Improve math skills" or "Prepare for an exam."
* **Schedule** (`schedule`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose one or multiple schedule options that fit your study plan, such as "08:00 - 12:00" or "13:00 - 17:00."
* **Goal Type** (`goal_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of goal you aim to achieve, such as "Homework Help" or "Test Prep."
* **Preferred Time Slots** (`preferred_time_slots`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the time slots that best fit your schedule, such as "08:00 - 12:00" or "18:00 - 20:00."
* **Study Frequency** (`study_frequency`, text, required/optional): Enter the frequency of your study sessions, such as "Daily" or "Weekly."
* **Best Time** (`best_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the best time for your study sessions, such as "10:00 AM" or "3:00 PM."
* **Study Duration** (`study_duration`, number, required/optional): Enter the duration of your study sessions, such as "2 hours" or "1 hour."
* **Follow Up Reminder** (`follow_up_reminder`, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether you want a follow-up reminder, such as "True" or "False."
* **Follow Up Time** (`follow_up_time`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the follow-up time, such as "2 days" or "1 week."
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your study plan.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

Please note that while most fields are optional, it is essential to complete all required fields for the form to be valid.
