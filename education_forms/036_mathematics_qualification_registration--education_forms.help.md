# Mathematics Qualification Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for a mathematics qualification, such as a math exam or certification.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Candidate Details" page with your personal information, including your name, any relevant qualifications or experience.
2. Select your education level (Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced).
3. If necessary, provide your exam preferences.
4. Choose a date and time for your exam.
5. Enter your email address.
6. Provide your phone number.
7. If applicable, indicate your consent to receive communications or provide a reason for not consenting.
8. If you have any additional comments, enter them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Details** (`candidate_details`, text, required/optional): Please enter your name and any relevant qualifications or experience. This information will be used to identify you and tailor the qualification process to your needs.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, select_one, required/optional): Select your current education level (Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced).
* **Exam Preferences** (`exam_preferences`, text, required/optional): If you have specific preferences for your exam, such as a preferred format or location, enter them here.
* **Exam Date** (`exam_date`, date, required/optional): Choose a date for your exam. This will help us schedule your test.
* **Exam Time** (`exam_time`, time, required/optional): Choose a time for your exam. This will help us schedule your test.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate your consent to receive communications or provide a reason for not consenting.
* **No Consent Reason** (`no_consent_reason`, text, required/optional): If you decline consent, provide a brief explanation for your reason.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or requests you may have.
* **No Additional Comments** (`no_additional_comments`, select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether you have any additional comments or not.
