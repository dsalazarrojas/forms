# Academic Equity In Leadership Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Equity In Leadership Training Registration Form is designed for participants to provide their information and preferences for the Academic Equity In Leadership Training program. This form helps the program organizers with participant registration, contact, and accommodation arrangements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields (Participant name, Participant email, and Session selection) accurately and thoroughly.
2. Provide your preferred session date for the training program.
3. If necessary, indicate any dietary requirements or accessibility needs.
4. If you want to, specify your learning objectives for the program.
5. Provide the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
6. Review your information for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name for registration.
* **Participant email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email for program updates and information.
* **Institution or employer** (`institution`, text, optional): If you are affiliated with an institution, enter it here.
* **Role or title** (`role`, text, optional): If you have a job title or position, enter it here.
* **Session selection** (`session_selection`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred session date from the available options (Morning workshop, Afternoon workshop, Full day intensive).
* **Dietary requirements** (`dietary_requirements`, text, optional): Specify any dietary needs or restrictions you may have.
* **Accessibility needs** (`accessibility_needs`, text, optional): Request any accommodations you may require for the training program.
* **Learning objectives** (`learning_objectives`, text, optional): State what you hope to gain from the training program.
* **Emergency contact name and phone** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Provide the name and phone number of your emergency contact.

## Tips
* Ensure the participant name and participant email are accurate and up-to-date.
* Be specific about your dietary requirements and accessibility needs to help us arrange suitable arrangements.
* Be clear about your learning objectives to help us tailor the training to your needs.
* Double-check all information before submitting the form.
