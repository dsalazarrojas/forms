# University Graduation Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The University Graduation Checklist Form is designed to track student information and progression towards graduation. It ensures that students provide essential details about their academic and personal status, enabling the university administration to monitor and support their graduation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information ready, such as your graduation date and status.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for the following fields:
	* Student Program
	* Final Exam Schedule
	* Degree Earned
	* Graduation Status
	* Final Exam Status
3. Enter the required dates:
	* Graduation Date
	* Thesis Submission Date
	* Degree Awarded Date
	* GPA Required Date
	* Graduation Status Date
	* Degree Certified Date
	* Gpa Required Date 2
	* Gpa Status Date 2
	* Thesis Submission Date 2
	* Final Exam Schedule Date 2
	* Degree Earned Date 2
4. Enter the relevant text information:
	* Student Status
	* Degree Certified

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Information (student_info)**: Enter your student ID or other identifying information.
	* Type: text
	* Required: Optional
- **Student Program (student_program)**: Select "Yes" if you are currently enrolled in a program, "No" otherwise.
	* Type: select_one
	* Required: No
- **Student Status (student_status)**: Select "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate your current student status.
	* Type: select_multiple
	* Required: No
- **Graduation Date (graduation_date)**: Enter the date you expect to graduate.
	* Type: date
	* Required: No
- **GPA Required (gpa_required)**: Enter your expected GPA.
	* Type: number
	* Required: No
- **GPA Status (gpa_status)**: Enter a descriptive text regarding your GPA status.
	* Type: text
	* Required: No
- **Thesis Submission (thesis_submission)**: Enter the date you submitted your thesis.
	* Type: date
	* Required: No
- **Final Exam Schedule (final_exam_schedule)**: Select "Yes" if you have a final exam schedule, "No" otherwise.
	* Type: select_one
	* Required: No
- **Final Exam Status (final_exam_status)**: Select "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate your final exam status.
	* Type: select_multiple
	* Required: No
- **Degree Earned (degree_earned)**: Select "Yes" if you have earned your degree, "No" otherwise.
	* Type: select_one
	* Required: No
- **Degree Certified (degree_certified)**: Enter a descriptive text regarding your degree certification.
	* Type: text
	* Required: No
- **Graduation Status (graduation_status)**: Select "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate your graduation status.
	* Type: select_multiple
	* Required: No
- **Degree Awarded (degree_awarded)**: Enter the date your degree was awarded.
	* Type: date
	* Required: No
- **GPA Required Date (gpa_required_date)**: Enter the date your GPA is required.
	* Type: date
	* Required: No
- **GPA Status Date (gpa_status_date)**: Enter the date regarding your GPA status.
	* Type: date
	* Required: No
- **Thesis Submission Date (thesis_submission_date)**: Enter the date you submitted your thesis.
	* Type: date
	* Required: No
- **Final Exam Schedule Date (final_exam_schedule_date)**: Enter the date of your final exam schedule.
	* Type: date
	* Required: No
- **Degree Earned Date (degree_earned_date)**: Enter the date you earned your degree.
	* Type: date
	* Required: No
- **Graduation Status Date (graduation_status_date)**: Enter the date regarding your graduation status.
	* Type: date
	* Required: No
- **Degree Certified Date (degree_certified_date)**: Enter a descriptive text regarding your degree certification.
	* Type: date
	* Required: No
- **GPA Required Date 2 (gpa_required_date_2)**: Enter the date your GPA is required.
	* Type: date
	* Required: No
- **GPA Status Date 2 (gpa_status_date_2)**: Enter the date regarding your GPA status.
	* Type: date
	* Required: No
- **Thesis Submission Date 2 (thesis_submission_date_2)**: Enter the date you submitted your thesis.
	* Type: date
	* Required: No
- **Final Exam Schedule Date 2 (final_exam_schedule_date_2)**: Enter the date of your final exam schedule.
	* Type: date
	* Required: No
- **Degree Earned Date 2 (degree_earned_date_2)**: Enter the date you earned your degree.
	* Type: date
	* Required: No

## Tips
- Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
- Ensure you have all necessary documentation and dates correct.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the university administration.
