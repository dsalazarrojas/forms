# Research Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Research Request Form is used by researchers and staff to submit a request for research services. This form helps gather information about the research project, including its name, description, goals, and contact details. Please ensure that you only submit a single request per research project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief title for your research project in the "formName" field.
2. Provide a detailed description of your research project in the "researchDescription" field.
3. Specify your research goals in the "researchGoals" field.
4. Enter the name of the person to contact for this research project in the "contactPerson" field.
5. Enter the email address for contact purposes in the "contactEmail" field.
6. Provide the phone number for contact purposes in the "contactPhone" field.
7. Optionally, enter a unique ID for your research project in the "formID" field.
8. Enter the name of the institution or organization conducting the research in the "researchInstitution" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **formName** (Text, Optional): Enter a brief title for your research project.
* **researchQuestion** (Text, Optional): This field is not currently being used, please do not fill it out.
* **researchDescription** (Text, Optional): Provide a detailed description of your research project.
* **researchGoals** (Text, Optional): Specify your research goals and objectives.
* **contactPerson** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the person to contact for this research project.
* **contactEmail** (Email, Optional): Enter the email address for contact purposes.
* **contactPhone** (Text, Optional): Provide the phone number for contact purposes.
* **formID** (Number, Optional): Optionally, enter a unique ID for your research project.
* **researchInstitution** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the institution or organization conducting the research.
* **submit** (Text, Optional): This is a placeholder field and not intended for user input.
* **submit** (Text, Optional): This field is not currently being used and can be ignored.

## Tips
* Ensure you only submit one request per research project.
* If you need to submit multiple requests, please create separate forms for each project.
* If you are unsure about any information, please contact your department's research support team for assistance.
