# dual_enrollment_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The dual enrollment application form is used to collect information for the dual enrollment process. This form is not intended to be a substitute for other forms, such as student transcripts or other supporting documents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the student's high school from the available options (High School, Charter School, or Home Schooled).
2. Enter the student's details in the text field.
3. Choose the courses the student is interested in taking (Math, Science, English, Social Studies, or Physical Education).
4. Select the approval status (Approve or Deny).
5. Enter the student's name.
6. Enter the parent's name.
7. Enter the student's email address.
8. Enter the student's phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **High School** (`high_school`, required/optional): Select the student's high school from the available options (High School, Charter School, or Home Schooled).
* **Student Details** (`student_details`, required/optional): Enter any relevant information about the student, such as their name, grade level, or any special needs they may have.
* **Course Choices** (`course_choices`, required/optional): Choose the courses the student is interested in taking. Select one or more courses from the available options (Math, Science, English, Social Studies, or Physical Education).
* **Approvals** (`approvals`, required): Select the approval status for the student's dual enrollment application (Approve or Deny).
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, required/optional): Enter the student's name.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, required/optional): Enter the parent's name.
* **Email** (`email`, required/optional): Enter the student's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, required/optional): Enter the student's phone number.

## Tips
* Ensure that the student's high school information is accurate and up-to-date.
* Make sure to select the correct approval status to ensure the student's application is processed correctly.
* Enter the student's and parent's names correctly to ensure accurate contact information.
* Double-check the student's email address and phone number for accuracy.
