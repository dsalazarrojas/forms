# Engineering Program Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Engineering Program Enrollment Form is a form designed to collect information from prospective students interested in enrolling in an engineering program. This form helps us to determine your eligibility for the program and allows us to tailor our support to your specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply fill in the required fields and select any additional information you want to provide. You can submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Prospective Student Info** (prospective_student_info, text, optional): This field is meant to be used to provide a brief description of yourself, but it is not required to fill in.
* **Name** (name, text, optional): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (email, email, optional): Enter a valid email address that you can be reached at for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number for any additional communication.
* **Date of Birth** (date_of_birth, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Program Interest** (program_interest, text, optional): Describe which program you are interested in enrolling in.
* **Previous Education Background** (previous_education_background, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your previous educational background.
* **Resume** (resume, text, optional): Attach a copy of your resume if you have one. This will help us to review your previous work experience. 

## Tips
Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date. You can contact us if you have any questions or concerns.
