# Online Enrolment Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The online Enrolment Form Template is designed to collect essential information from students who wish to register for a course. This information will help the course administrators to verify the student's identity, provide access to the course materials, and ensure that the student is eligible for the course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in your details in the first page, including your first name, last name, email, and phone number.
* Move to the second page to enter course details, such as the course name, start date, and end date.
* On the third page, enter your contact details, including your address, post code, country, and state.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Details (Page 1)**
	+ **First Name** (`student_details_first_name`, text, Optional): Enter your first name.
	+ **Last Name** (`student_details_last_name`, text, Optional): Enter your last name.
	+ **Email** (`student_details_email`, email, Optional): Enter your email address.
	+ **Phone** (`student_details_phone`, text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
	+ **Date of Birth** (`student_details_date_of_birth`, date, Optional): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yy.
* **Course Details (Page 2)**
	+ **Course Name** (`course_details_course_name`, text, Optional): Enter the name of the course you wish to enroll in.
	+ **Start Date** (`course_details_start_date`, date, Optional): Enter the start date of the course.
	+ **End Date** (`course_details_end_date`, date, Optional): Enter the end date of the course.
	+ **Venue** (`course_details_venue`, text, Optional): Enter the venue where the course will be held.
* **Contact Details (Page 3)**
	+ **Address** (`contact_details_address`, text, Optional): Enter your address.
	+ **Post Code** (`contact_details_postal_code`, text, Optional): Enter your post code.
	+ **Country** (`contact_details_country`, text, Optional): Enter your country.
	+ **State** (`contact_details_state`, text, Optional): Enter your state.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, Optional): Click on this button to submit your application.

Note: Fields marked as "Optional" are not required for the form submission.
