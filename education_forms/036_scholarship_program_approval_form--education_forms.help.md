# Scholarship Program Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scholarship Program Approval Form is a document that approvers use to validate or deny a scholarship request. This form is meant to be used internally by approvers to review and approve or deny requests made by students or applicants.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the User Information section with details about the student or applicant.
2. In the Scholarship Request section, describe the specific request or award made by the student or applicant.
3. Provide contact details in the Contact Details section.
4. Add any necessary comments or notes in the Notes section.
5. Finally, sign and date the form in the Signature section to indicate the approval or denial of the request.
6. Submit the form to complete the approval process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information**: This section requires your name, student ID, or any other unique identifier to ensure that the request is properly linked to the student or applicant.
* **Scholarship Request**: Here, you will describe the specific scholarship award, grant, or request made by the student or applicant. Be as detailed as possible about the award or grant.
* **Contact Details**: This section should contain the contact information of the student or applicant, including their name, email, and phone number.
* **Signature**: Sign and date this section to indicate the approval or denial of the request. This is a legal document, so make sure to sign it carefully.
* **Approval Date**: Enter the date you approve or deny the request. This field is for tracking purposes.
* **Approver Information**: You may include your name, title, and any other relevant information as an approver.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the request or award.
* **Submit**: Click this button to finalize your approval or denial of the request.
