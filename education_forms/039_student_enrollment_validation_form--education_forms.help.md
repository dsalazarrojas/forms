# Student Enrollment Validation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from students for enrollment purposes, ensuring accurate and secure enrollment processes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Please answer all the fields in the form accurately**. Make sure to fill out each field as instructed, and review your answers carefully before submitting.
2. **Double-check your information**. Verify that your name, email, and date of birth are correct to avoid any errors.
3. **Submit the form once you've completed all the fields**. Make sure to review the form carefully before hitting the submit button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`Student Name`, text, required): This field requires your name, which will be used to identify you in our system.
* **Student Email** (`Student Email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address to ensure we can contact you about your enrollment process.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/2000). This is necessary for age verification and enrollment eligibility.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all the fields accurately and honestly.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to this help guide for clarification.
* If you have any issues with the form, contact our support team.
