# Job Posting Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect job posting information from users, allowing administrators or hiring managers to create or update job postings on their platform or website.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as "required".
2. Enter the job title and description in the "Job Title" and "Description" fields, respectively.
3. Select the job category, department, job status, job type, job level, and job location using the corresponding drop-down menus.
4. Enter the job posting URL, image, and any other relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Title** (`jobtitle`, text, required): Enter the title of the job posting, e.g., "Software Engineer".
* **Institution** (`institution`, text, required): Enter the name of the institution or company posting the job.
* **Job Category** (`jobcategory`, text, optional): Select the category of the job, e.g., "IT", "Finance", etc.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the job posting.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the department related to the job posting.
* **Job Status** (`jobstatus`, text, optional): Select the status of the job posting, e.g., "Open", "Closed", etc.
* **Job Type** (`jobtype`, text, optional): Select the type of job, e.g., "Full-time", "Part-time", etc.
* **Job Level** (`joblevel`, text, optional): Select the level of the job, e.g., "Entry Level", "Senior", etc.
* **Job Location** (`joblocation`, text, optional): Enter the location of the job posting.
* **Job URL** (`joburl`, text, optional): Enter the URL of the job posting.
* **Job Image** (`jobimage`, text, optional): Enter the image URL or link of the job posting.
* **Job Description** (`jobdescription`, text, optional): Enter a detailed description of the job posting.
* **Job Requirements** (`jobrequirements`, text, optional): Enter the requirements of the job posting.
* **Job Benefits** (`jobbenefits`, text, optional): Enter the benefits of the job posting.
* **Job Contact Info** (`jobcontactinfo`, text, optional): Enter the contact information of the job posting.
* **Job Posting URL** (`jobpostingurl`, text, optional): Enter the URL of the job posting.
* **Job Posting Status** (`jobpostingstatus`, text, optional): Select the status of the job posting, e.g., "Active", "Inactive", etc.
* **Job Posting Date** (`jobpostingdate`, date, optional): Enter the date of the job posting.
* **Job Posting Time** (`jobpostingtime`, time, optional): Enter the time of the job posting.
* **Job Posting Duration** (`jobpostingduration`, text, optional): Enter the duration of the job posting.
* **Job Posting Reason** (`jobpostingreason`, text, optional): Enter the reason for the job posting.
* **Job Posting Type** (`jobpostingtype`, text, optional): Select the type of job posting, e.g., "Internal", "External", etc.
* **Job Posting Location** (`jobpostinglocation`, text, optional): Enter the location of the job posting.
* **Job Posting URL** (`jobpostingurl`, text, optional): Enter the URL of the job posting.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Use accurate and relevant information to avoid errors.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the administrator or hiring manager for clarification.
* Review your submission before submitting to ensure it is complete and accurate.
