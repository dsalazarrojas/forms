# Daycare Cleaning Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daycare Cleaning Checklist is a form used to assess the cleanliness and organization of various areas within a daycare facility. It is used to identify areas that require improvement and to ensure the facility is safe and healthy for children.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review each section carefully, noting any areas that require improvement.
2. Check each field for accuracy and completeness.
3. Select the correct answer for each field.
4. Submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Facilities** (`daycare_facilities`, text, optional): Briefly describe the condition of the facilities at the daycare, including any issues or concerns.
* **Bathrooms** (`bathrooms`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate the current state of the bathrooms, choosing all that apply: `Yes`, `No`.
* **Kitchen** (`kitchen`, text, optional): Describe the cleanliness and organization of the kitchen, including any issues or concerns.
* **Toys and toys box** (`toys_and_toys_box`, text, optional): Note the condition of the toys and toy box, including any issues or concerns.
* **Trash** (`trash`, text, optional): Report the current state of the trash, including any issues or concerns.
* **Floors** (`floors`, text, optional): Describe the cleanliness and condition of the floors, including any issues or concerns.
* **Walls** (`walls`, text, optional): Report the current state of the walls, including any issues or concerns.
* **Windows** (`windows`, text, optional): Describe the cleanliness and condition of the windows, including any issues or concerns.
* **Doors** (`doors`, text, optional): Note the current state of the doors, including any issues or concerns.
* **Furniture** (`furniture`, text, optional): Report the condition of the furniture, including any issues or concerns.
* **Toys and activities** (`toys_and_activities`, text, optional): Describe the state of the toys and activities, including any issues or concerns.
* **Toys and games** (`toys_and_games`, text, optional): Note the condition of the toys and games, including any issues or concerns.
* **Storage** (`storage`, text, optional): Report the current state of the storage, including any issues or concerns.
* **Outdoor areas** (`outdoor_areas`, text, optional): Describe the cleanliness and condition of the outdoor areas, including any issues or concerns.
* **Playground** (`playground`, text, optional): Note the condition of the playground, including any issues or concerns.
* **Other** (`other`, text, optional): Report any other areas that require attention.
* **Completed** (`completed`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether the checklist has been completed.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about the facility.
* **Completed by** (`completed_by`, select_one, optional): Identify who completed the checklist.
* **Assigned to** (`assigned_to`, select_one, optional): Identify who is responsible for the facility.
* **Created at** (`created_at`, date, optional): Note the date and time the checklist was created.
* **Updated at** (`updated_at`, date, optional): Note the date and time the checklist was last updated.
* **Assigned tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Identify if a tool is assigned to the facility.
* **Created by** (`created_by`, select_one, optional): Identify who created the checklist.
* **Updated by** (`updated_by`, select_one, optional): Identify who last updated the checklist.
