# Virtual Course Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Course Registration Form is designed to collect information from students who are interested in registering for a virtual course. The form will help the course organizers to gather necessary details about the students and provide them with a smooth registration experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and understand what information is required.
2. Fill in your course details (if you're a course organizer) in the "Course Details" section.
3. Enter your student information in the "Student Info" section.
4. Provide your registration details in the "Registration Details" section.
5. Enter course-related information in the "Course Info" section.
6. Fill in your contact information in the "Contact Info" section.
7. Review all sections to ensure accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Details** (`course_details`, `text`, optional): This section is for course organizers to provide information about the course they are offering. Enter the title, description, and any other relevant details about the course.
* **Student Info** (`student_info`, `text`, optional): This section is for students to provide their personal information, such as name, email, and any other relevant details.
* **Registration Details** (`registration_details`, `text`, optional): This section is for students to provide details about their registration, such as payment method and registration date.
* **Course Info** (`course_info`, `text`, optional): This section is for students to provide any additional information about the course they are interested in, such as previous knowledge or experience.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, optional): This section is for students to provide their contact information, such as phone number and address.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, optional): This section is a placeholder for submitting the form. You can submit the form once you have filled in all the required information.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields to ensure accurate and complete registration.
* Double-check the information you enter to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
