# Student Administration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Administration Form is used to collect and manage student information for administrative purposes. This form is intended to be a comprehensive tool for gathering and tracking student data, which will help administrators in their daily tasks and decision-making processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant student information from the available fields.
2. Fill in the student's personal details such as Name, Date of Birth, Address, Email, and Phone number.
3. Choose the student's Grade Level, Status, and Group from the respective dropdown menus.
4. Select the School ID and Course ID for the student.
5. Finally, click the submit button to save the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the student's full name as it appears on their records.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the unique ID assigned to the student.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the student's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the student's current address.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the student's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the student's current grade level: High or Low.
* **Student Status** (`student_status`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the student's current status: Active, Inactive, or Awaiting.
* **School ID** (`school_id`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the school ID associated with the student.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the student's department: Yes or No.
* **Course ID** (`course_id`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the course ID associated with the student.
* **Student Group** (`student_group`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the student group: Yes or No.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the entered data is accurate and up-to-date.
* Double-check the form for any errors or missing fields before submission.
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the provided field label or contact the administrator for clarification.
