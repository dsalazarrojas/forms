# Paragraph Writing Guide - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Paragraph Writing Guide form is designed to provide feedback and reflections on paragraph writing exercises. It collects information about paragraph drafts, self-reflections, feedback requests, and other comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the following questions:
   - What was the subject of the paragraph draft (Category)?
   - Provide the date and time of the activity (Date, Time)?
   - Enter your email address and phone number (Email, Phone)?
   2. Type your comments and feedback in the Comments, Feedback, and Other Comments fields.
   - In the Self Reflection and Another Reflection fields, explain what you think about your writing.
   - Use the Feedback and Other F Request fields to ask for feedback or clarification.
   - If you have other comments or feedback that don't fit in the previous fields, type them in the Other Comments and Other Feedback fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Paragraph Draft** (1): Provide a draft of your paragraph writing.
* **Self Reflection** (2): Think about what you think about your writing and type your thoughts.
* **Feedback Request** (3): Ask for feedback from others or the instructor.
* **Category** (4): Choose the category of the writing exercise (Math, Science, Language Arts, or Other).
* **Date** (5): Enter the date of the activity.
* **Time** (6): Enter the time of the activity.
* **Comments** (7): Provide any comments or feedback you have.
* **Email** (8): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (9): Enter your phone number.
* **Note** (10): Provide any additional notes or comments.
* **Another Draft** (11): Provide another draft of your writing.
* **Another Reflection** (12): Think about what you think about another draft.
* **Another F** (13): Ask for feedback on another draft.
* **Feedback** (14): Provide feedback on a piece of writing.
* **F Request** (15): Ask for feedback on a piece of writing.
* **S** (16): Provide additional feedback.
* **R** (17): Think about what you think about feedback.
* **E** (18): Think about what you think about feedback.
* **L** (19): Provide additional feedback.
* **A** (20): Ask for feedback on a piece of writing.
* **Other Comments** (21): Provide any other comments or feedback.
* **Other Feedback** (22): Provide feedback on other comments or feedback.
* **Other F Request** (23): Ask for feedback on other comments or feedback.
* **Other S** (24): Provide additional feedback.
* **Other R** (25): Ask for feedback on other comments or feedback.

Note: This help guide is based on the provided YAML fields and the form's layout. Some field labels are unclear or seem to be duplicates; in those cases, I've provided the most logical explanation.
