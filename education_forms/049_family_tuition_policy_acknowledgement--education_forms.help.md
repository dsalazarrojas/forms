# Family Tuition Policy Acknowledgement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Tuition Policy Acknowledgement form is used to collect essential information about a student's tuition, payment terms, and agreements. This form helps educational institutions streamline their record-keeping and ensure accurate tracking of tuition-related data.

## How To Complete This Form
- Ensure you have all the necessary information about the student and their tuition before starting the form.
- Fill in the student's name, parent's name, and email address as prompted.
- Select the payment method for the tuition (Check, Card, or Bank Transfer).
- Enter the tuition amount and the date you signed the agreement.
- Choose the tuition term (Active or Inactive).
- If applicable, fill in the tuition start date and end date.
- Enter the student's date of birth and enrollment date.
- Add any remarks or comments about the tuition.
- Confirm you've read and understood the terms of payment and agreements.
- Sign the form as the student and their parent to acknowledge your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Name**: Enter the student's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
- **Parent Name**: Enter the name of the parent or guardian responsible for the student's tuition payments.
- **Email**: Enter the email address of the parent or guardian for communication purposes.
- **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact number of the parent or guardian.
- **Tuition Amount**: Enter the total amount of tuition due for the current term.
- **Payment Method**: Select how you intend to make the tuition payment (Check, Card, or Bank Transfer).
- **Agreement**: A text field for notes or comments regarding the tuition agreement.
- **Signature**: A date field for the student to sign the agreement.
- **Date Signed**: A date field for the student to record the date they signed the agreement.
- **School Name**: Enter the name of the school or institution the student is registered at.
- **Program Name**: Enter the name of the program the student is enrolled in.
- **Tuition Term**: Choose whether the student is Active (enrolled in the program) or Inactive (not currently enrolled).
- **Tuition Start Date**: If applicable, enter the start date of the tuition period.
- **Tuition End Date**: Enter the end date of the tuition period.
- **Student DOB**: Enter the student's date of birth.
- **Student Enrollment Date**: Enter the date the student was enrolled in the program.
- **Student Start Date**: Enter the date the student's tuition started (if not the same as the program start date).
- **Tuition Remarks**: A text field for any remarks or comments regarding the tuition.
- **Notes**: A text field for additional information or remarks.
- **Payment Terms**: A text field to explain the payment terms and conditions.
- **Terms Accepted**: Confirm whether the terms and conditions were accepted.
- **Student Signature**: A date field for the student to sign the agreement.
- **Parent Signature**: A date field for the parent or guardian to sign the agreement.

This guide provides a clear and concise explanation of each field, ensuring that end-users understand the form's purpose and how to complete it correctly.
