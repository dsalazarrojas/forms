# Academic Planning Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Planning Form is used to gather essential information from students about their academic goals, progress, and needs. It helps educators and administrators better understand each student's situation and provide tailored support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student information, including your name and university ID or registration number.
2. Specify your current program of study and short-term and long-term academic goals.
3. List the courses you plan to take in the next term (optional).
4. Indicate any support you need to meet your goals, such as academic advising, tutoring, or financial assistance.
5. Outline your expected timeline for achieving your goals and milestones.
6. Enter the name of your assigned academic advisor.
7. Add any additional notes or information about your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student name (Student name)**: Enter your full legal name.
* **Student ID (Student identification number)**: Enter your university ID or registration number.
* **Program or major (Program)**: Type your current program of study, e.g., Computer Science, English Literature.
* **Academic goals (Academic goals)**: Briefly describe your short-term and long-term academic goals.
* **Planned courses (Planned courses for next term)**: List the courses you plan to take in the next term, using course codes or titles (e.g., Math 101, History 202).
* **Support needed (Support needed to meet goals)**: Select any support services you require, such as:
	+ Academic advising
	+ Tutoring
	+ Financial assistance
	+ Mental health services
	+ Career counseling
* **Timeline (Timeline for goals)**: Describe your expected timeline for achieving your goals, including any milestones or deadlines.
* **Advisor name (Advisor name)**: Enter the name of your assigned academic advisor.
* **Additional notes (Additional notes)**: Add any other relevant information about your situation, such as any challenges or successes you've had.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* If you're unsure about any information, ask your academic advisor for guidance.
* Review your form carefully before submitting to ensure everything is complete and accurate.
