# University Interview Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Interview Booking Form is a form used to book an interview with a potential student or applicant to a university.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the booking date and time of the interview.
2. Enter the name of the applicant.
3. Select the interview location.
4. Enter the applicant's email address.
5. Enter the applicant's phone number.
6. (Optional) Enter the name of the interviewer.
7. Enter any additional notes about the booking.
8. (Optional) Enter any additional notes about the interview.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Booking Date** (`booking_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the interview.
* **Interview Time** (`interview_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the interview.
* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the applicant.
* **Interview Location** (`interview_location`, select one, required): Select the location of the interview.
* **Applicant Email** (`applicant_email`, email, required): Enter the email of the applicant.
* **Applicant Phone** (`applicant_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the applicant.
* **Interviewers Name** (`interviewers_name`, text, optional): (Optional) Enter the name of the interviewer.
* **Booking Note** (`booking_note`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about the booking.
* **Booking Note 2** (`booking_note_2`, note, optional): (Optional) Enter any additional notes about the interview.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter the correct booking date and time to avoid conflicts.
- Double-check the applicant's email and phone number for accurate communication.
- Select a suitable interview location to ensure a smooth interview process.
- Enter any necessary notes about the booking or interview to provide additional context.
