# input_for_iep - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect input for IEP purposes, specifically to document student progress, goals, and other relevant details for educators and school staff.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, start by selecting the relevant fields that apply to the student's current situation.
2. Fill in the **classroom_teacher** field with the name of the student's teacher or instructor.
3. Enter the **date** of the relevant event or observation in the corresponding field.
4. If applicable, record the **time** of the event or observation.
5. Provide any additional **remarks** or comments in the **remarks** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **classroom_teacher**: Enter the name of the student's teacher or instructor.
* **date**: Record the date of the relevant event or observation.
* **time**: If applicable, record the time of the event or observation.
* **remarks**: Provide any additional comments or remarks about the student's progress or behavior. 

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form to ensure accurate and comprehensive documentation.
* If you are unsure about any field or section, please consult with your institution's education staff or refer to their guidelines and policies.
* Remember to save and print the completed form for future reference and record-keeping purposes.
