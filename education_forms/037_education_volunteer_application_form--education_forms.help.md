# Education Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Education Volunteer Application Form is designed to collect information from potential volunteers who want to participate in educational activities or events. This form will help us understand their interests, availability, and skills to match them with suitable opportunities.

1. Fill in your **name** in the text field.
2. Enter your **email** address in the correct format (e.g., [username]@domain.com).
3. Provide your **contact_number** in the format of a phone number or a text message number.
4. Select your **availability** dates from the calendar.
5. Choose your **availability_end_date** from the calendar.
6. Briefly describe your **interest** in participating in educational activities.
7. Select your **skills** by ticking the relevant options (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
8. If you have any specific skills related to Option 1, please describe them in the text field **skills_option1**.
9. If you have any specific skills related to Option 2, please describe them in the text field **skills_option2**.
10. If you have any specific skills related to Option 3, please describe them in the text field **skills_option3**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **name**: Please fill in your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **contact_number**: Enter your phone number or text message number that we can use to contact you.
* **availability**: Select the date(s) you are available to participate in educational activities.
* **availability_end_date**: Choose the date when you are no longer available to participate in educational activities.
* **interest**: Briefly describe your interest in participating in educational activities.
* **skills**: Select the skills you have that match our educational activities.
* **skills_option1**, **skills_option2**, **skills_option3**: If you have specific skills related to the selected options, please describe them in these text fields.
* **submitted**: Choose how you would like to submit your application (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
