# School Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track student attendance in schools. It captures essential information about students' presence or absence from school, allowing school administrators to monitor attendance trends and patterns. This form is meant to be used for individual student attendance tracking.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully, ensuring all necessary fields are completed accurately.
2. Select the relevant options for each field based on the student's attendance status.
3. Enter the student's name, class name, and attendance status.
4. Choose the correct attendance status for each student.
5. Provide a reason for absence when the student is absent.
6. Enter the duration of absence for students who are currently absent.
7. Select the teacher's ID based on the class name.
8. Enter the school ID based on the student's school.
9. Choose the student's grade level and section.
10. Enter any comments or notes about the student's attendance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student ID**: Select the student's ID from the available options.
* **Student Name**: Enter the student's name as it appears on their official documents.
* **Class Name**: Select the student's class from the available options.
* **Attendance Date**: Choose the date the student was present or absent.
* **Start Time**: Enter the time the student started their class or period.
* **End Time**: Enter the time the student finished their class or period.
* **Duration**: Enter the duration of the student's absence (in minutes).
* **Reason Absence**: Provide a clear explanation for the student's absence.
* **Teacher ID**: Select the teacher's ID based on the class name.
* **Teacher Name**: Enter the teacher's name as it appears on their official documents.
* **School ID**: Select the school ID based on the student's school.
* **School Name**: Enter the school's name as it appears on official documents.
* **Attendance Status**: Choose the student's attendance status (Active or Inactive).
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the student's attendance.
* **Student Grade Level**: Select the student's grade level (High or Low).
* **Student Section**: Select the student's section (Yes or No).

## Tips

* Ensure all fields are completed accurately and consistently.
* If a student is absent, provide a clear reason for absence.
* Use the correct format for dates (dd/mm/yyyy).
* If a student's grade level or section changes, update the corresponding fields accordingly.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy.
