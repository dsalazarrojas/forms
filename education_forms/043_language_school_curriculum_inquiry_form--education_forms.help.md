# Language School Curriculum Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Language School Curriculum Inquiry Form is designed to collect information from students who are inquiring about language school programs. This form helps language school administrators to better understand the needs and interests of students and provide relevant information to help them make informed decisions about their language programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the language school name from the provided options.
2. Choose the level of language proficiency you are interested in (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Proficient).
3. Select the type of inquiry you are making (General Information, Admission, or Enrollment).
4. Choose the language programs you are interested in (e.g., French, Spanish, Mandarin Chinese, or English).
5. If applicable, provide your name, email, and phone number.
6. Choose the start and end dates of the program you are interested in.
7. Provide the address of the language school (if you have one).
8. Finally, leave any additional comments or messages you would like to include in the message field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Language School Name (text):** This field is where you can enter the name of the language school you are interested in.
* **Language Level (select_one):** Choose the level of language proficiency you are interested in, such as Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Proficient.
* **Language Inquiry Type (select_multiple):** Select the type of inquiry you are making, such as General Information, Admission, or Enrollment.
* **Language Programs of Interest (select_multiple):** Choose the language programs you are interested in, such as French, Spanish, Mandarin Chinese, or English.
* **Student Name (text):** This field is optional, but if you are a student, please enter your name here.
* **Email (email):** This field is optional, but if you have an email, please enter it here.
* **Phone (text):** This field is optional, but if you have a phone number, please enter it here.
* **Program Date Start (date):** If you know the start date of the program you are interested in, please enter it here.
* **Program Date End (date):** If you know the end date of the program you are interested in, please enter it here.
* **Language School Address (text):** This field is optional, but if you know the address of the language school, please enter it here.
* **Message (note):** Leave any additional comments or messages you would like to include in this field.
