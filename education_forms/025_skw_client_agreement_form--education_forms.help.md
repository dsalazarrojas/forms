# SKW Client Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SKW Client Agreement Form is a form used to collect information and agreements from SKW clients. It's essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure that we have the necessary details to provide the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field accurately and completely.
2. Review the "Agreement" field carefully and select the correct options.
3. Sign and date the "Signature" field.
4. Ensure that you've filled out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required: false): This is where you enter your name as a client of SKW.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): This is your address, where SKW can contact or send you information.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): This is your email address, which SKW will use to communicate with you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): This is your phone number, which SKW can use to contact you.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): This is your date of birth, which helps us understand your age.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required: false): This is where you sign and date the form, indicating your agreement with the information above.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, select_multiple, required: false): This is where you select your agreement with the provided information. Please choose "I have read and understand the above information" if you've read and understood everything. If not, select "Other" and add an explanation.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, required: false): This is the date you signed the form, which helps us track when you agreed to the terms.

## Tips

* Make sure to review the "Agreement" field carefully to ensure you understand and agree to the terms.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask our team for clarification.
* Please sign and date the "Signature" field to confirm your agreement.
* We recommend filling out all fields for the best possible service from SKW.
