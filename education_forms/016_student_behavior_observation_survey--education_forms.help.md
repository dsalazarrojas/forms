# Student Behavior Observation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Student Behavior Observation Survey is a tool designed to collect information on student behavior and progress. This form helps educators and teachers to gather data on individual students, which can aid in identifying areas of improvement, tracking progress, or making informed decisions about student support.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are filling out the correct form for the relevant student and observation date.
2. Fill in the "Student Name" field with the name of the student being observed.
3. Enter the "Teacher Name" field with your name, as the instructor or observer.
4. Select the "Observation Date" field to indicate the date of the observation.
5. In the "Behavior Notes" field, provide a brief description of the student's behavior or progress during the observation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the student being observed.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as the instructor or observer who conducted the observation.
* **Observation Date** (`observation_date`, date, required/optional): Select the date of the observation.
* **Behavior Notes** (`behavior_notes`, text, required/optional): Describe the student's behavior or progress during the observation. 

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions or authority to complete this form.
* Be as detailed and objective as possible when filling out the "Behavior Notes" field.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that all fields are accurate and complete.
