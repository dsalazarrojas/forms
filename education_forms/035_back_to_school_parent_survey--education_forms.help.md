# Back To School Parent Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey for parents to share their thoughts and concerns about the upcoming school year, their child's needs, and the school's services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

* Please fill out each section according to the instructions provided. 
* Be as detailed as possible in your answers, especially in the free-text fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the school for support.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Back-to-School Parent Feedback Survey --** (`parent_survey_intro`, note, required: false)
    * This is an introduction to the survey, explaining its purpose and the benefits of participating.
* **Parent/Guardian Name** (`parent_name_survey`, text, required: true)
    * This field is for your name, which is required information.
* **Child's Name and Grade Level** (`child_name_grade_survey`, text, required: true)
    * Please provide your child's name and their current grade level, which is required information.
* **Rate your experience with distance learning last semester** (`distance_learning_experience`, number, required: true)
    * This question asks you to rate your experience with distance learning last semester on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is "Very Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **What are your primary concerns about schools reopening?** (`reopening_concerns`, select_multiple, required: true)
    * Please select all the concerns that apply to you, which will help the school prioritize its resources and services.
* **Which learning model do you prefer for your child?** (`preferred_learning_model_parent`, select_one, required: true)
    * This question asks you to choose your preferred learning model for your child, which can help the school tailor its services.
* **Does your child have reliable internet and a device at home?** (`technology_access_home`, select_one, required: true)
    * Please select the correct answer to indicate your child's access to technology at home.
* **Does your family need assistance with school supplies or uniforms?** (`school_supply_assistance`, select_one, required: true)
    * This question helps the school determine if your family needs help with school supplies or uniforms.
* **How would you rate your child's current emotional well-being?** (`emotional_wellbeing_rating`, number, required: true)
    * This question asks you to rate your child's emotional well-being on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is "Struggling" and 5 is "Thriving".
* **What specific support could the school provide to help your family?** (`support_needed_from_school`, text, required: false)
    * This field is for any additional information you think would help the school better support your family.
* **Will you require after-school childcare this year?** (`childcare_needs_after_school`, select_one, required: true)
    * This question asks you to indicate if you will need childcare services after school hours.
* **Preferred way for the school to communicate with you** (`communication_preference_school`, select_one, required: true)
    * Please select the preferred way for the school to communicate with you.
* **Any other information we should know as we plan for the new year?** (`additional_thoughts_survey`, text, required: false)
    * This field is for any additional thoughts, comments, or concerns you might have as we begin the new school year.
