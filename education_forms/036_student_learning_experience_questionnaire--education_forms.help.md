# Student Learning Experience Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the student learning experience in an educational setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and any other demographic information about yourself as a student.
2. Provide information about your parents or guardians.
3. Share your thoughts and perceptions about your learning experience.
4. Describe your relationship with your teacher or instructor.
5. Share your feedback about your teacher or instructor.
6. Rate your experience in the classroom environment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information** (Page 1): Enter your name, age, and any other demographic information about yourself as a student.
* **Parent Information** (Page 1): Provide information about your parents or guardians, if applicable.
* **Teacher Information** (Page 1): Share your thoughts and perceptions about your teacher or instructor.
* **Student Perception** (Page 2): Share your thoughts and feelings about your learning experience.
* **Teacher Perception** (Page 2): Share your thoughts and perceptions about your teacher or instructor's teaching practices.
* **Teacher Support** (Page 3): Describe the level of support you receive from your teacher or instructor.
* **Teacher Teaching Practices** (Page 3): Rate the effectiveness of your teacher or instructor's teaching practices.
* **Class Environment** (Page 4): Rate the comfort, organization, and overall comfort of your classroom environment.
* **Student Feedback** (Page 4): Share your feedback about your learning experience.
* **Teacher Feedback** (Page 5): Share your teacher or instructor's feedback about you.
* **School Experience** (Page 5): Describe your overall experience at the school or educational institution.
