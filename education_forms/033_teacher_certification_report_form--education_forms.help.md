# Teacher Certification Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teacher Certification Report Form is a document used to collect and record a teacher's certification status. It is completed by the teacher or school administrators to provide accurate information about the teacher's certification.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter the teacher's name in the "Teacher Name" field.
3. Select the institution where the teacher is certified from the list provided in the "Institution" field.
4. Choose the appropriate certification level(s) from the list provided in the "Certification Level" field.
5. Enter the date and time of certification completion in the "Date of Completion" and "Time of Completion" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Teacher Name** (Text): Enter the teacher's name as it appears on their certification or identification card.
* **Institution** (Select One): Choose the institution that issued the teacher's certification.
* **Certification Level** (Select Multiple): Select all applicable certification levels (e.g., Level 1, Level 2, etc.).
* **Date of Completion** (Date): Enter the date the teacher completed their certification.
* **Time of Completion** (Time): Enter the time the teacher completed their certification.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please refer to this help guide for clarification.
* This form should not be completed by multiple users simultaneously.
