# global_academic_leadership_project_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Academic Leadership Project Application Form is designed to collect information from applicants to the Global Academic Leadership Project, a program that promotes academic excellence and leadership development among students. This form is used to gather personal details, academic background, contact information, project details, leadership qualifications, scholarship application, and file uploads.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your name and any other relevant information.
2. Provide your academic background, including any relevant education and experience.
3. Enter your contact details, including your address, phone number, and email.
4. Describe your project details, including the project's goals and objectives.
5. Select the leadership qualifications that you possess, such as Leadership, Project Management, Team Management, and Strategic Thinking.
6. If you have other leadership qualifications, provide a brief description in the "Project Leadership Qualifications - Other" field.
7. If you are applying for a scholarship, provide a brief justification for why you need it.
8. Select the sponsor of your scholarship application, such as Self, Parent/Guardian, Family, School, University, or Other.
9. Enter the amount of scholarship you are requesting.
10. Upload any relevant file, such as a resume or project proposal.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Details (Page 1)**:
	+ **Name** (text, required): Enter your full name.
	+ **Other** (text, optional): Enter any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Academic Background (Page 2)**:
	+ **Academic Background** (text, required): Describe your academic background, including your education and any relevant experience.
* **Contact Details (Page 3)**:
	+ **Contact Details** (text, required): Enter your address, phone number, and email.
* **Project Details (Page 4)**:
	+ **Project Details** (text, required): Describe your project, including its goals and objectives.
* **Project Leadership Qualifications (Page 5)**:
	+ **Project Leadership Qualifications** (select_multiple, optional): Select the leadership qualifications you possess, such as Leadership, Project Management, Team Management, and Strategic Thinking.
	+ **Other** (text, optional): If you have other leadership qualifications, provide a brief description.
* **Scholarship Application (Page 6)**:
	+ **Scholarship Application** (text, optional): Provide a brief justification for why you need a scholarship.
	+ **Scholarship Sponsor** (select_one, optional): Select the sponsor of your scholarship application.
	+ **Scholarship Amount** (number, optional): Enter the amount of scholarship you are requesting.
* **File Upload (Page 7)**:
	+ **File Upload** (text, optional): Upload any relevant file, such as a resume or project proposal.
