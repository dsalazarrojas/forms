# Student Individual Inventory - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and store information about individual students, including their basic contact details, educational background, and academic history. This data is used for internal purposes such as tracking student progress, reporting, and analytics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First_Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last_Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your student ID in the "Student_ID" field.
5. Enter your student number in the "Student_Number" field (if applicable).
6. Select your gender from the "Gender" field.
7. Enter your date of birth in the "Date_of_Birth" field in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
8. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
9. Enter your zip code in the "Zip" field.
10. Enter your city in the "City" field.
11. Enter your country in the "Country" field.
12. Enter your state or province in the "State" field (if applicable).
13. Enter your graduation date in the "Graduation_Date" field (if graduated).
14. Select your graduation status from the "Graduation_Status" field.
15. Select your course of study from the "Course" field (if applicable).
16. Select your degree level from the "Degree" field (if applicable).
17. Select your graduation level from the "Graduation_Level" field (if applicable).
18. Select your faculty of study from the "Faculty" field (if applicable).
19. Select your department of study from the "Department" field (if applicable).
20. Enter your major of study from the "Course_Major" field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First_Name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last_Name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Student_ID** (text, required): Enter your student ID number.
* **Student_Number** (text, optional): Enter your student number (if applicable).
* **Gender** (select_one, optional): Select your gender.
* **Date_of_Birth** (date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **Address** (text, optional): Enter your address.
* **Zip** (text, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **City** (text, optional): Enter your city.
* **Country** (text, optional): Enter your country.
* **State** (text, optional): Enter your state or province (if applicable).
* **Graduation_Date** (date, optional): Enter your graduation date (if applicable).
* **Graduation_Status** (select_one, optional): Select your graduation status.
* **Graduation_Level** (select_one, optional): Select your graduation level.
* **Course** (text, optional): Enter your course of study (if applicable).
* **Degree** (select_multiple, optional): Select your degree level (if applicable).
* **Major** (text, optional): Enter your major of study (if applicable).
* **Graduation_Year** (number, optional): Enter your graduation year (if applicable).
* **Faculty** (text, optional): Enter your faculty of study (if applicable).
* **Department** (text, optional): Enter your department of study (if applicable).
* **Graduation_Time** (time, optional): Enter your graduation time (if applicable).
