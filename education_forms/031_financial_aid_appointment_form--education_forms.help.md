# financial_aid_appointment_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to schedule an appointment with a financial aid representative. Please fill out the form to provide necessary information for the appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Determine if you have a schedule appointment (page 1, Schedule Appointment).
2. Enter your contact information (page 2, Contact Information).
3. Select a date and time for the appointment (page 3, Date and Time).
4. Indicate your availability for the appointment (page 4, Available Days).
5. Choose the type of appointment (page 5, Type of Appointment).
6. Select the reason for your visit (page 6, Reason for Visit).
7. Choose your preferred language (page 7, Preferred Language).
8. Select your preferred contact method (page 8, Preferred Contact Method).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Schedule Appointment** (page 1, Schedule Appointment, required: false): 
  This field allows you to indicate if you have a schedule appointment with a financial aid representative. Select 'Yes' if you have a scheduled appointment.
* **Contact Information** (page 2, Contact Information, required: false): 
  This field is for entering your contact information for the financial aid representative.
* **Date and Time** (page 3, Date and Time, required: false): 
  This field is for selecting the date and time of the appointment.
* **Available Days** (page 4, Available Days, required: false): 
  This field is for indicating your available days for the appointment.
* **Type of Appointment** (page 5, Type of Appointment, required: false): 
  This field is for selecting the type of appointment you would like to have.
* **Reason for Visit** (page 6, Reason for Visit, required: false): 
  This field is for entering the reason for your visit to the financial aid office.
* **Preferred Language** (page 7, Preferred Language, required: false): 
  This field is for selecting your preferred language for the appointment.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (page 8, Preferred Contact Method, required: false): 
  This field is for selecting your preferred contact method for the appointment.
