# University Admissions Process Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the university admissions process. It is meant to gather data that can help us improve the process and provide a better experience for users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Application Submission**: Enter a brief description of your application submission.
2. **Application Type**: Select one of the two options: "Yes" or "No", indicating whether you have submitted an application.
3. **Application Status**: Select one of the two options: "Active" or "Inactive", indicating the status of your application.
4. **Contact Details**: Enter your contact information, such as your name and email address.
5. **Application Date**: Enter the date you submitted your application.
6. **Communication Channel**: Select all the communication channels you use (Yes/No) for example: phone, email, etc.
7. **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person you would like to contact about your application.
8. **Feedback**: Provide any feedback or comments you would like to share.
9. **Follow up**: Schedule a follow-up meeting or call to discuss your application.
10. **Communication Channel 2**: Select all the communication channels you would like to use for follow-up (Yes/No).
11. **Contact Person 2**: Enter the name of the person you would like to contact about your application follow-up.
12. **Follow up 2**: Schedule a follow-up meeting or call to discuss your application follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Application Submission (Text)**: A free-text field for you to describe your application submission.
* **Application Type (Select One)**: Choose "Yes" if you have submitted an application, and "No" otherwise.
* **Application Status (Select One)**: Choose "Active" if your application is active, and "Inactive" otherwise.
* **Contact Details (Text)**: Enter your contact information, such as your name and email address. This is a required field.
* **Application Date (Date)**: Enter the date you submitted your application. This is a required field.
* **Communication Channel (Select Multiple)**: Select all the communication channels you use, such as phone, email, etc.
* **Contact Person (Text)**: Enter the name of the person you would like to contact about your application. This is a required field.
* **Feedback (Note)**: Provide any feedback or comments you would like to share.
* **Follow up (Time)**: Schedule a follow-up meeting or call to discuss your application. This is a required field.
* **Communication Channel 2 (Select Multiple)**: Select all the communication channels you would like to use for follow-up.
* **Contact Person 2 (Text)**: Enter the name of the person you would like to contact about your application follow-up.
* **Follow up 2 (Time)**: Schedule a follow-up meeting or call to discuss your application follow-up.

## Tips
* Please provide accurate and truthful information in all fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
* If you have any questions or need help, please contact the university's admissions team.
