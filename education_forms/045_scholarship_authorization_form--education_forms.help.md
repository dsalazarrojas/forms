# Scholarship Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Scholarship Authorization Form is used to collect information from students to verify their eligibility for a scholarship. This form helps the scholarship committee review and approve applicants for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field. Please note that this field is required.
3. Provide a valid email address in the "Email" field. This will be used for communication with the scholarship committee.
4. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone" field (optional).
5. Enter your Date Of Birth in the "Date Of Birth" field. This is a required field for verifying your age.
6. Enter the name of the school you attended in the "School Name" field. This is a required field.
7. Select the highest grade level you have attended in the "Grade Level" field. Choose from the options: Grade 9, Grade 10, Grade 11, Grade 12, or Postsecondary.
8. Select all applicable programs from the list in the "Academic Program" field. You can choose one or more programs from the options: Program 1, Program 2, Program 3.
9. Choose the type of authorization you are requesting in the "Authorization Type" field. Select from the options: Type A, Type B, Type C.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address to receive communication from the scholarship committee.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it here.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth to verify your age.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the school you attended.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_one, required): Select the highest grade level you have attended from the options.
* **Academic Program** (`academic_program`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable programs you are interested in from the options.
* **Authorization Type** (`authorization_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of authorization you are requesting from the options.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or need help, please contact the scholarship committee.
* This form is for verification purposes only and should only be filled out by students who are eligible for the scholarship program.
