# Educational Benefit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Benefit Form is designed to collect and document information about educational benefits received by students. This form helps ensure accuracy and completeness of the benefits' details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your name, as the student receiving the benefit.
2. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
3. List the name of the institution offering the benefit.
4. Select the type of financial aid received (if applicable).
5. Enter the amount of the benefit.
6. Enter the date of disbursement for the benefit.
7. Leave a note with any additional comments or details.
8. Sign the form to confirm your identity as a student.
9. Sign the form from the institution to confirm the benefit's details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Educational Benefit Form** (`form_title`, text, required):
	+ This field is the title of the form. Please ensure it's correctly filled out.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your name as the student receiving the benefit.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, optional):
	+ Provide your email address for contact purposes.
* **Institution Name** (`institution_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the institution offering the benefit.
* **Financial Aid Type** (`financial_aid_type`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select the type of financial aid received (if applicable). Options: AID TYPE 1, AID TYPE 2, AID TYPE 3.
* **Amount** (`amount`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the amount of the benefit received.
* **Date of Disbursement** (`date_of_disbursement`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date when the benefit was disbursed.
* **Additional Comments** (`signature`, note, optional):
	+ Leave a note with any additional comments or details.
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, text, optional):
	+ Sign the form to confirm your identity as a student.
* **Institution Signature** (`institution_signature`, text, optional):
	+ Sign the form from the institution to confirm the benefit's details.

## Tips

* Ensure the form is complete before submission.
* Double-check the accuracy of the provided information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant authority for assistance.
