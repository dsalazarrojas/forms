# E Form For Long Answer Questions - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect long answer questions from students, providing a platform for them to express their thoughts and opinions in a written format. It helps teachers to assess their understanding of the subject matter and provide feedback to students.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, start by reading and understanding the essay question provided.
2. Type your answer to the question in the "Student Answer" field.
3. Fill in the "Submission Status" field with any additional information about your submission, such as whether it's a draft or final answer.
4. Review and submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Essay Question** (`essay_question`, `text`, optional): This field displays the essay question you are asked to answer. Please read and understand the question before proceeding.
* **Question** (`question`, `text`, optional): This field is not used in this form, but it's a leftover from the original form schema. Please ignore it.
* **Student Answer** (`student_answer`, `text`, optional): This is the field where you type your answer to the essay question. Please provide a clear and detailed answer to the question.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, `text`, optional): Fill in this field with any additional information about your submission, such as whether it's a draft or final answer.

## Tips
* Make sure to read and understand the essay question carefully before typing your answer.
* Provide a clear and detailed answer to the question.
* Use proper spelling and grammar in your answer.
* Fill in the submission status field with accurate information about your submission.
