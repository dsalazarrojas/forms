# Book Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about your reading habits and preferences. The purpose of this survey is to collect data on how often you read books, what formats you prefer, and how you discover new books. This will help us understand your reading behavior and provide valuable insights for future book recommendations and library services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill out the following fields:

*   Fill in your name and email address in the "Your Name" and "Email Address" fields.
*   Choose your preferred book format (printed, e-book, or audiobook) in the "What format do you prefer" field.
*   Select how often you read books from the "How often do you read books" field.
*   Indicate how you typically discover new books from the "How do you discover new books" field.
*   Answer the other questions according to your reading habits and preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, text, optional): Enter your first and last name.
*   **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
*   **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, optional): Today's date is automatically filled in.
*   **How often do you read books** (`reading_frequency`, select_one, required): Select how often you read books, from "Daily", "Several times a week", "Weekly", "Monthly", "Rarely", or "Never".
*   **What is your favorite book genre** (`favorite_book_genre`, select_multiple, required): Select the genres you enjoy, such as "Fiction", "Mystery", "Romance", etc.
*   **Title of a book you recently read** (`recently_read_title`, text, optional): If you've recently read a book, please enter the title here.
*   **How would you rate the last book you read** (`recently_read_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate the last book you read from 1 (did not enjoy) to 5 (loved it).
*   **What format do you prefer** (`book_format_preference`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred book format, from "Printed books", "E-books", "Audiobooks", or "Mix of formats".
*   **How many books do you read per month** (`monthly_book_count`, number, optional): Approximate the number of books you read per month.
*   **How do you discover new books** (`book_discovery_method`, select_multiple, required): Select how you typically discover new books, such as "Recommendations from friends", "Online reviews", etc.
*   **Do you use the public library** (`library_usage`, select_one, required): Indicate how often you visit the public library, from "Frequently", "Sometimes", "Rarely", or "Never".
*   **Do you participate in a book club** (`book_club_participation`, select_one, required): Check if you've joined or are interested in joining a book club.
*   **What is your all-time favorite book** (`all_time_favorite_book`, text, optional): If you have an all-time favorite book, enter its title here.
*   **Would you recommend reading to others** (`book_recommendation`, text, optional): Explain why or why you would recommend or not recommend reading to others.
