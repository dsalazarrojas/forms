# Special Education Training Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed for registering individuals with special education training programs. Please fill out the form to provide necessary information for your training registration. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you are registering for a valid training program. 
2. Fill out the form completely and correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address where you can be contacted about your registration.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Please enter your contact phone number.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred payment method from the options provided. (Visa, MasterCard, Amex)
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, note, optional): You can add any additional payment details or instructions that may be necessary for your registration.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select the training date you will be attending.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select the start time of your training session.
* **Training Program** (`training_program`, select_one, optional): Select the special education training program you are registering for. (Special Ed Training 1, Special Ed Training 2, Special Ed Training 3)
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or requests you have for your registration.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): If a tool or resource was assigned to you, please list it here.

Please note that all fields are optional unless otherwise specified.
