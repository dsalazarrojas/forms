# Logbook Competency Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Logbook Competency Assessment form is used to evaluate a candidate's performance in a specific competency area. It captures information from both the candidate and the assessor, including contact details and ratings on performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the candidate's information, including their name and contact details (email and phone number).
2. Select the competency areas that the candidate demonstrates.
3. Choose the assessment date.
4. Rate the candidate's overall performance (Select "Rating 1", "Rating 2", or "Rating 3").
5. Rate the assessor's overall performance (Select "Rating 1", "Rating 2", or "Rating 3").
6. Add any comments about the assessment.
7. Enter the assessor's contact information (email and phone number).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Information (1)**: Enter the candidate's name and contact details.
* **Assessor Information (2)**: Enter the assessor's contact details.
* **Competency Areas (3)**: Select the areas where the candidate demonstrated competencies.
* **Assessment Date (4)**: Choose the date of the assessment.
* **Candidate Rating (5)**: Rate the candidate's overall performance.
* **Assessor Rating (6)**: Rate the assessor's overall performance.
* **Comments (7)**: Add any comments about the assessment.
* **Email (8)**: Enter the candidate's email.
* **Phone Number (9)**: Enter the candidate's phone number.
* **Assessor Phone Number (10)**: Enter the assessor's phone number.
