# Inorganic Industrial Analysis Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inorganic Industrial Analysis Quiz is a self-assessment tool designed to evaluate your knowledge and skills in inorganic industrial analysis. It consists of multiple pages with various fields that cover different topics, including inorganic chemistry concepts, industrial chemistry applications, and other relevant aspects.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Select the page to start with by clicking on the corresponding tab at the top of the page.
2.  Read and answer each question carefully, following the instructions provided.
3.  Enter your response in the text field or select an option from the drop-down list, depending on the field type.
4.  Move to the next page by clicking the "Next" button.
5.  When you have completed all pages, click the "Submit" button to review your results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Inorganic Chemistry Concept** (`inorganic_chemistry_concept`, text, optional)
This field allows you to enter your thoughts on inorganic chemistry concepts, such as the structure and properties of inorganic compounds, their uses, and applications.

### 2. **Industrial Chemistry Applications** (`industrial_chemistry_applications`, select_one, optional)
Please select "Yes" if you have experience with industrial chemistry applications or "No" if you do not.

### 3. **Chemistry Knowledge** (`chemistry_knowledge`, number, optional)
Rate your level of knowledge in chemistry on a scale from 0 to 10.

### 4. **Chemical Reactivity** (`chemical_reactivity`, select_multiple, optional)
Select all the options that apply to your understanding of chemical reactivity, such as "Yes" for each option.

### 5. **Process Economics** (`process_economics`, number, optional)
Enter a numerical value for your understanding of process economics, with higher values indicating greater understanding.

### 6. **Materials Science** (`materials_science`, text, optional)
Enter your thoughts on materials science, including their properties and applications.

### 7. **Production Processes** (`production_processes`, select_one, optional)
Please select "Yes" if you have experience with production processes or "No" if you do not.

### 8. **Chemical Engineering** (`chemical_engineering`, note, optional)
This field is intended for notes or comments on chemical engineering, but it is not required to be filled.

### 9. **Environmental Impact** (`environmental_impact`, select_multiple, optional)
Select all the options that apply to your understanding of environmental impact, such as "Yes" for each option.

### 10. **Industrial Applications** (`industrial_applications`, text, optional)
Enter your thoughts on industrial applications, including their uses and benefits.

### 11. **Inorganic Quiz Results** (`inorganic_quiz_results`, select_one, optional)
Please select "Yes" if you have completed the quiz and "No" if you do not.

Note: All fields are optional, except for the "Inorganic Quiz Results" field, which is required to submit the form.
