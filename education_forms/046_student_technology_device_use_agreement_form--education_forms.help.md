# Student Technology Device Use Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record students' agreement on the terms and conditions of technology device usage and return. It is a mandatory form that must be filled out by students before using institution-issued technology devices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Select the correct options for each field, such as the device type and condition.
3. Sign and date the form to acknowledge your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student name** (`student_name`, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your official records.
* **Student ID number** (`student_id_number`, required): Enter your student ID number issued by the institution.
* **Date signed** (`date_signed`, required): Enter the date you sign this agreement.
* **Device Use Policy** (`device_use_policy`, required): Select how you understand the institution's device usage policy:
	+ Accept: I understand and agree to the institution's device usage policy.
	+ Accept with some conditions: I understand and agree to the institution's device usage policy with some conditions.
	+ Decline: I do not agree to the institution's device usage policy.
* **Device type** (`device_type`, required): Select the type of device you will use:
	+ Laptop
	+ Tablet
	+ Desktop
	+ Smartphone
* **Device condition** (`device_condition`, required): Select the condition of the device:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Agreement duration** (`agreement_duration`, required): Enter the duration of the agreement in months.
* **Student signature** (`student_signature`, required): Sign your name to acknowledge your agreement.
* **Institution signature** (`institution_signature`, required): Sign your name as the institution representative.
* **Device return status** (`device_return_status`, optional): Check 'True' if you will return the device at the end of the agreement.
* **Device return note** (`device_return_note`, optional): Enter any notes about returning the device.
* **Student contact number** (`student_contact_number`, optional): Enter your contact number.
* **Student contact email** (`student_contact_email`, optional): Enter your contact email.
* **Student Contact Number 2** (`student_contact_number_2`, optional): Enter your secondary contact number.
* **Date Signed 2** (`date_signed_2`, optional): Enter the date you signed this agreement.

## Tips

* Fill out the required fields carefully and accurately.
* Read each field's label and options carefully before selecting an answer.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask for help from the institution representative.
* This form will be reviewed and verified by the institution before the device is issued or used.
