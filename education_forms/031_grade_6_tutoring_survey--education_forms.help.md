# Grade 6 Tutoring Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback and information from students who have received tutoring in Grade 6. Please provide honest and accurate answers to help us understand how our tutoring services are being perceived by our students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form with your honest answers to the questions asked.
2. Answer the questions in the order they appear on the page.
3. Make sure to answer all required questions, denoted by an asterisk (\*).
4. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "comments" field.
