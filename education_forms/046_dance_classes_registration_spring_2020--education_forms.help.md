# dance_classes_registration_spring_2020 - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information for the Dance Classes Spring 2020 registration process. It's meant to be filled out by students registering for dance classes to provide essential details about themselves and their dance class preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out your first name in the "first_name" field.
2.  Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
3.  Provide your email address in the "email" field.
4.  Enter your date of birth in the "date_of_birth" field (format: YYYY-MM-DD).
5.  Choose your preferred dance class type from the options provided in the "class_type" field.
6.  Select the start and end dates of your desired class period in the "start_date" and "end_date" fields respectively.
7.  Choose your available class duration from the options provided in the "class_duration" field.
8.  Select the frequencies you are available to attend in the "frequency" field.
9.  Choose the date for the reservation in the "reservation_date" field.
10.  Choose your preferred payment method from the options provided in the "payment_method" field.
11.  Select your current level of dance proficiency in the "class_level" field.
12.  Enter the expected number of students in your class in the "class_size" field.
13.  Provide any additional notes about yourself as a student in the "student_notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **first_name** (`text`, required: false): Enter your first name.
*   **last_name** (`text`, required: false): Enter your last name.
*   **email** (`email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
*   **date_of_birth** (`date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
*   **class_type** (`select_one`, required: false): Choose your preferred dance class type (Jazz, Ballet, or Modern).
*   **start_date** (`date`, required: false): Select the start date of your desired class period.
*   **end_date** (`date`, required: false): Select the end date of your desired class period.
*   **class_duration** (`time`, required: false): Choose your available class duration.
*   **frequency** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select the frequencies you are available to attend (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, or Sunday).
*   **reservation_date** (`date`, required: false): Choose the date for the reservation.
*   **payment_method** (`select_one`, required: false): Choose your preferred payment method (Cash, Bank, or Credit).
*   **class_level** (`select_one`, required: false): Select your current level of dance proficiency (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced).
*   **student_number** (`number`, required: false): Enter the expected number of students in your class.
*   **class_size** (`select_one`, required: false): Choose the size of your desired class (Small, Medium, or Large).
*   **instructor** (`text`, required: false): Provide any additional notes about your instructor.
*   **student_notes** (`text`, required: false): Enter any additional notes about yourself as a student.
