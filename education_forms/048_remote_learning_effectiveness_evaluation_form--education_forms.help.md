# Remote Learning Effectiveness Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from students and educators about the effectiveness of remote learning programs. It's crucial to fill out the form accurately and completely to help identify areas of improvement and provide recommendations for future development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you're providing specific, concrete, and relevant feedback.
2. Answer all required fields (marked as "true" in the form).
3. Provide clear and concise answers to each question.
4. Use the space provided for each question to elaborate on your thoughts and suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Remote Learning Program Title** (`remote_learning_program_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the remote learning program being evaluated. This will help us understand the context of your feedback.
* **Remote Learning Program Duration** (`remote_learning_program_duration`, `time`, optional): If you have information about the duration of the program, please provide it. If not, you can skip this field.
* **Feedback from Students** (`feedback_from_students`, `email`, optional): If you have received feedback from students about the program, you can provide their email addresses here.
* **Feedback from Educators** (`feedback_from_educators`, `email`, optional): If you have received feedback from educators about the program, you can provide their email addresses here.
* **Overall Effectiveness** (`overall_effectiveness`, `number`, optional): On a scale of 1-5, rate how effective you think the program was.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, `text`, required): Provide any suggestions or ideas for improvement for the program.
* **Benefits of Remote Learning** (`remote_learning_benefits`, `text`, required): Describe the benefits of the remote learning program.
* **Challenges** (`challenges`, `text`, optional): If you faced any challenges during the program, describe them here.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, `text`, required): Provide recommendations for educators and students.
* **Evaluation Form Conclusion** (`evaluation_form_conclusion`, `text`, required): Summarize your overall impression of the program.
* **Submit Option 1** (`submit_button`, `select_one`, required): Select the submission option that best suits your situation.
* **Submit Option 2** (`submit_button_2`, `select_multiple`, required): Select multiple submission options that best suit your situation.
* **Evaluation Form Conclusion 2** (`evaluation_form_conclusion_2`, `select_one`, required): Select the conclusion that best suits your situation.
* **Evaluation Form Conclusion 3** (`evaluation_form_conclusion_3`, `text`, required): Summarize your overall impression of the program.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, `time`, optional): If you're submitting your feedback, please indicate when you're doing so.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to identify yourself or your organization as the one submitting the feedback.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, `date`, required): Date and time of submission.
* **Form Submitted By** (`form_submitted_by`, `select_one`, required): Select who submitted the form.
* **Form Submitted At** (`form_submitted_at`, `date`, required): Date and time of form submission.
* **Submit Time** (`submit_time`, `time`, required): The time of submission.
* **Submitted User** (`submitted_user`, `text`, required): Identify yourself as the one submitting the form.

Note: Make sure to review the form carefully and answer all required fields to ensure accurate and complete feedback.
