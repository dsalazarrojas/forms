# university_committee_chancellor_voting_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect data on votes and comments from the University Committee in multiple voting rounds. It helps the committee to keep track of their decisions and discussions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the appropriate voting round from the first page.
2. Choose your vote for each round (Yes or No).
3. Provide any comments or feedback you have for each round.
4. Enter your name and ID for committee member information.
5. Submit the form to complete the voting process.
