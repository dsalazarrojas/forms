# After School Multimedia Class Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from parents or guardians to register their child for an after-school multimedia class. The form is used to gather essential details about the student, including their name, grade level, school attended, and medical information, as well as the parent's or guardian's contact details and transportation arrangements. This information is used to ensure the child's safety and successful participation in the class.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the Student Information section, which includes the student's full name, date of birth, current grade level, school attended, and student ID number (if available).
2. Select the multimedia class that the student would like to join.
3. Choose the class level that is most suitable for the student.
4. Specify the class schedule preference (days and time).
5. Provide the parent or guardian's contact information, including their full name, email address, and phone number.
6. Identify the emergency contact person and their relationship to the student.
7. Confirm the transportation arrangements for the student (picked up by parent/guardian, school bus, or walk).
8. Answer any additional questions about the student's technology access, medical conditions, and allergies.
9. Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the multimedia class program.
10. Sign the electronic signature to confirm the registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Full Name**: Enter the student's complete legal name.
* **Student Date of Birth**: Enter the student's date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Current Grade Level**: Select the student's current grade level from the options provided.
* **School Attended**: Enter the name of the school the student attends.
* **Student ID Number**: If available, enter the student's ID number for identification purposes.
* **Select Multimedia Class**: Choose the multimedia class the student would like to join.
* **Class Level**: Select the class level that is most suitable for the student (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced).
* **Class Schedule Preference**: Select the days and time for the class that work best for the student.
* **Class Time Preference**: Choose the time of day for the class (3:00 PM - 4:00 PM, 4:00 PM - 5:00 PM, 5:00 PM - 6:00 PM, or 6:00 PM - 7:00 PM).
* **Parent/Guardian Full Name**: Enter the parent's or guardian's complete legal name.
* **Relationship to Student**: Identify the relationship between the parent/guardian and the student (Mother, Father, Guardian, Grandparent, Aunt/Uncle, or Other).
* **Email Address**: Enter the parent's or guardian's email address for class updates and communication.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the parent's or guardian's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Identify the relationship between the emergency contact person and the student.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the emergency contact person's phone number.
* **Computer Access**: Confirm whether the student has access to a computer or laptop at home.
* **Internet Access**: Confirm whether reliable internet access is available at home.
* **Technology Experience**: Select the student's experience level with technology (Beginner, Some experience, Intermediate, or Advanced).
* **Software Experience**: If necessary, describe the student's experience with multimedia software.
* **Medical Conditions**: If necessary, describe any medical conditions the student has.
* **Medications**: If necessary, list any medications the student is currently taking.
* **Allergies**: If necessary, list any allergies the student has (food, medication, or environmental).
* **Transportation Method**: Choose how the student will get home from the class (Picked up by parent/guardian, School bus, Walk, or Other authorized adult).
* **Other Authorized Adults**: If necessary, list any other adults authorized to pick up the student.
* **Dismissal Instructions**: Provide any special instructions for dismissing the student.
* **Terms and Conditions Agreement**: Confirm that you have reviewed and agreed to the terms and conditions of the multimedia class program.
* **Photo Release Agreement**: Confirm that you give permission for your child to be photographed during class activities.
* **Technology Use Agreement**: Confirm that you agree to the technology use guidelines and that your child will use technology responsibly.
* **Parent/Guardian Signature**: Sign the electronic signature to confirm registration.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If you need help or have questions, contact the program staff or administrators.
* Review the terms and conditions carefully before agreeing to them.
* Make sure to sign the electronic signature to confirm registration.
