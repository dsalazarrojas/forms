# Class Ring Program Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Class Ring Program Application form is used to collect information about you, a graduating student, to create a customized ring that represents your academic achievements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your full name as it should appear on the ring.
2. Enter your student ID number.
3. Enter your graduation year.
4. Choose your grade level (e.g., sophomore, junior, senior).
5. Enter your email address and phone number.
6. Enter your mailing address, including city, state or province, and postal code.
7. Select your ring style preference (e.g., women's traditional, men's traditional, women's modern, men's modern, unisex contemporary).
8. Select your ring size.
9. Choose your metal type (e.g., yellow gold, white gold, platinum, silver).
10. Choose your stone preference (e.g., diamond, gemstone, school stone or birthstone, no stone).
11. If you have a specific engraving or initials, enter them in the "Engraving Text or Initials" field.
12. Check if you want to include your school seal or emblem on the ring.
13. If you have any special requests, enter them in the "Special Customization Requests" field.
14. Select your preferred ring price tier.
15. If you're not sure, you can choose "Not sure yet" for now.
16. If you'd like to make a deposit and pay the rest, or pay in monthly installments, please select your preferred payment plan.
17. Enter your preferred delivery date, if you've selected a specific date.
18. Finally, please confirm that all the information you provided is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (Student Information, required): Enter your full name as it should appear on the ring.
- **Student ID Number** (Student Information, required): Enter your student ID number.
- **Graduation Year** (Student Information, required): Enter your graduation year.
- **Grade Level** (Student Information, required): Choose your grade level (e.g., sophomore, junior, senior).
- **Email Address** (Contact Information, required): Enter your email address.
- **Phone Number** (Contact Information, required): Enter your phone number.
- **Mailing Address** (Contact Information, required): Enter your mailing address, including city, state or province, and postal code.
- **Ring Style Preference** (Ring Selection and Specifications, required): Select your preferred ring style (e.g., women's traditional, men's traditional, women's modern, men's modern, unisex contemporary).
- **Ring Size** (Ring Selection and Specifications, required): Select your ring size.
- **Metal Type** (Ring Selection and Specifications, required): Choose your metal type (e.g., yellow gold, white gold, platinum, silver).
- **Stone Preference** (Ring Selection and Specifications, required): Select your stone preference (e.g., diamond, gemstone, school stone or birthstone, no stone).
- **Engraving Text or Initials** (Customization Options, optional): Enter any specific engraving or initials you'd like on the ring.
- **Include School Seal or Emblem** (Customization Options, optional): Check if you'd like to include your school seal or emblem on the ring.
- **Special Customization Requests** (Customization Options, optional): Enter any special requests you have.
- **Ring Price Tier Selected** (Pricing and Payment, required): Select your preferred ring price tier.
- **Estimated Total Cost** (Pricing and Payment, optional): This will be calculated based on your selections.
- **Preferred Payment Plan** (Pricing and Payment, optional): Select your preferred payment plan (e.g., full payment now, deposit and final payment, monthly installments).
- **Preferred Delivery Date** (Delivery Information, optional): Enter your preferred delivery date.
- **I Certify That All Information Is Accurate** (Pricing and Payment, required): Confirm that all the information you provided is accurate.
