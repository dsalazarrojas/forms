# Student Envoy Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Envoy Application form is a tool for students to collect necessary information to apply for the Student Envoy role. This role represents and supports students in their academic journey, and we need your details to consider you for this position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the relevant fields.
* Enter your Date of Birth to verify your age and eligibility.
* Enter a valid Email address for communication purposes.
* Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* Select one or more Student Levels from the dropdown list to demonstrate your suitability for the role.
* Enter the name of your school to help us verify your attendance.
* Enter the Email address of your school for any further contact with them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Your first name. Please enter your full name here.
* **Last Name**: Your last name. Please enter your full name here.
* **Date of Birth**: The date of your birth to verify your age.
* **EMail**: A valid email address we can use to communicate with you.
* **Phone**: Your phone number so we can contact you with updates.
* **Student Level**: Choose one or more levels that describe you:
	+ Level 1: You are currently studying at an elementary or high school level.
	+ Level 2: You are currently studying at a college or university level.
	+ Level 3: You are a graduate or post-grad student.
* **School Name**: The name of your school to help us verify your attendance.
* **School EMail**: The email address of your school for any further contact with them.

## Tips
* Please ensure the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us at [support email].
* Remember to submit the completed form to complete your application.
