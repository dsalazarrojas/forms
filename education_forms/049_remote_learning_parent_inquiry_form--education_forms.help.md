# Remote Learning Parent Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for parents to submit feedback, suggestions, or questions related to the remote learning experience. Your input helps us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your school's code if you know it. This helps us direct your inquiry to the correct school or department.
2. Provide your name as the parent/guardian inquiring.
3. Optionally, provide feedback or comments about your experience.
4. Select a category for your feedback, such as 'General Feedback', 'Suggestion', or 'Report an Issue'.
5. Select one or more subcategories for your feedback, depending on the specific issue you're addressing.
6. Optionally, provide additional comments or notes.
7. Enter your email address or phone number for us to contact you back.
8. Enter any additional comments or remarks you'd like to share.
