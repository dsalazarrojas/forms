# General Knowledge Examination Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The General Knowledge Examination Registration Form is a tool used to collect information from users about their test-taking experience. It is essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure that your data is captured correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, start by entering your User ID.
2. Next, enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., user@example.com).
3. Select the date you took the General Knowledge Examination.
4. Choose the time you took the examination.
5. Enter the duration of the test, in numbers.
6. Choose the location where the examination was held.
7. Indicate whether a test instructor was present or not.
8. For fields 10-15, select the appropriate option regarding the presence or absence of each instructor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User ID** (`user_id`, `number`, required): Enter your unique User ID assigned to you for this examination.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., user@example.com).
* **Test Date** (`test_date`, `date`, required): Select the date you took the General Knowledge Examination.
* **Test Time** (`test_time`, `time`, required): Choose the time you took the examination.
* **Test Duration** (`test_duration`, `number`, required): Enter the duration of the test in numbers.
* **Test Location** (`test_location`, `select_one`, required): Choose the location where the examination was held.
* **Test Instructor** (`test_instructor`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate whether a test instructor was present or not.
* **Test Provisions** (`test_provisions`, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes or information you have about the examination.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your experience.
* **Test Instructor 1-6** (`test_instructor_1-6`, `select_one/select_multiple`, optional): Select the appropriate option for each test instructor present.

Note: Some fields are not required, so feel free to leave them blank if they do not apply to your experience.
