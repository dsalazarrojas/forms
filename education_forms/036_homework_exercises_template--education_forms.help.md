# Homework Exercises Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect student feedback and reflections on their homework exercises. It is intended to help teachers understand students' thoughts, opinions, and insights on various aspects of the class.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2. Provide your name and email address (if provided) in the required fields.
3. Choose the correct answer for each exercise from the dropdown list, if applicable.
4. Type your answers for each exercise in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **This is a template** (1): This field is a placeholder for the form description. You can leave this field blank.
- **What is your name** (2): This field requires your name to be filled in. Please enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
- **What is your email** (3): This field is optional, but if you provide your email address, it will be used for communication purposes only.
- **What is your phone** (4): This field is optional and not required for submission. You can leave this field blank if you don't want to provide your phone number.
- **What is your favorite subject in school** (5): This field is required and has three options to choose from. Select the subject that best represents your favorite subject.
- **What do you think will be the most interesting thing about this class** (6): This field is optional and allows you to express your thoughts and opinions about the class.
- **What is your biggest take away from this class** (7): This field is optional and allows you to share your insights and reflections on the class.
- **Date** (8): This field is optional and not required for submission. You can leave this field blank.
- **Time** (9): This field is optional and not required for submission. You can leave this field blank.
