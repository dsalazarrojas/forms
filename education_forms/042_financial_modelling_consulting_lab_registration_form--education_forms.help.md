# Financial Modelling Consulting Lab Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals interested in registering for the Financial Modelling Consulting Lab. It is meant to gather necessary information from users to tailor the lab to their needs and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your User Name.
2. Enter your Email address.
3. Enter your Phone number.
4. Choose your Department (e.g., Academic Affairs, Business and Finance, etc.).
5. Select the workshop you are interested in (e.g., Financial Modelling, Data Modelling, etc.).
6. Enter your preferred Date and Time for the lab.
7. Optionally, provide additional comments about your registration.
8. If you have already reviewed this form, select your level of satisfaction (High, Medium, Low).
9. Optionally, provide any comments about your review.
10. Finally, click on the "Submit Form" button to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **User Name** (`user_name`, text, optional)
Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.

### 2. **Email** (`email`, email, optional)
Enter your email address associated with your professional or personal account.

### 3. **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional)
Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

### 4. **Department** (`department`, select_one, optional)
Choose the department you belong to from the list:
* Academic Affairs
* Business and Finance
* Engineering
* Health and Human Sciences
* Information Technology
* Research and Development
* Other

### 5. **Workshop Interested In** (`workshop_interested_in`, select_one, required)
Select the workshop you are interested in:
* Financial Modelling
* Data Modelling
* Business Process Modelling
* Business and Financial Modelling
* Other

### 6. **Date and Time** (`date_and_time`, date, optional)
Enter your preferred date and time for the lab.

### 7. **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, date, optional)
Enter the date you plan to submit your work or comments about the lab.

### 8. **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional)
Provide any additional comments about your registration or the lab.

### 9. **Review** (`review`, select_one, optional)
Choose your level of satisfaction:
* High
* Medium
* Low

### 10. **Review Comments** (`review_comments`, text, optional)
Enter any comments about your review of the lab.

### 11. **Submit Form** (`submit_form`, text, optional)
Click on this button to submit your registration form.
