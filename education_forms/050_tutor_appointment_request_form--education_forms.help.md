# Tutor Appointment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tutor Appointment Request Form is a simple, one-page form designed to collect basic information from students to request a tutoring session. This form is intended to be used by schools, colleges, or universities to collect student information and schedule tutor appointments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the schedule date for the requested tutoring session using the date picker provided.
2. Choose the desired schedule time for the tutoring session.
3. Select the subject area for which you need tutoring (e.g., Mathematics, Physics, Computer Science).
4. Select the student information you want to provide for the session (e.g., Student Name, Student ID, Contact Number, Preferred Contact Method).
5. Provide a brief description of the subject you need help with (e.g., "Need help with calculus").

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Schedule Date
* **Schedule Date** (`schedule_date`): Choose the date you would like to schedule the tutoring session. This will help us determine when the tutor is available.

### Schedule Time
* **Schedule Time** (`schedule_time`): Choose the time slot you would like to schedule the tutoring session. This will help us determine the tutor's availability.

### Subject Area
* **Subject Area** (`subject_area`): Select the subject you need help with (e.g., Mathematics, Physics, Computer Science). This will help the tutor know what to focus on during the session.

### Student Information
* **Student Information** (`student_info`): Select the student information you want to provide for the session (e.g., Student Name, Student ID, Contact Number, Preferred Contact Method). This will help the tutor contact you or verify your identity.

### Subject
* **Subject** (`subject`): Provide a brief description of the subject you need help with. This will help the tutor understand the topic you're struggling with.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when selecting the subject area and providing student information to ensure the best possible match for the tutoring session.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification, feel free to ask the tutor or the institution administering the form.
