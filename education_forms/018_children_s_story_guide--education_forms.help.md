# Children Story Guide - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help educators create a comprehensive guide for storytelling sessions with children, tailored to their specific needs and goals. This guide will provide a framework for planning engaging and effective storytelling experiences that cater to the target age group and learning objectives.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your name as the educator or facilitator** (`educator_name`): Enter your name as the person who will be leading the storytelling session.
2. **Choose the target age group** (`target_age`): Select the recommended age range for the storytelling session using the dropdown menu. Please note that the age groups are `3 to 5`, `6 to 8`, and `9 to 11`.
3. **List the learning objectives** (`learning_objectives`): Type in the key goals for the storytelling session, ensuring they align with the selected age group.
4. **Suggest story titles or authors** (`story_titles`): Provide a list of recommended story titles or authors that are suitable for the target age group.
5. **List activity ideas** (`activity_ideas`): Share interactive activities that can complement the story, making the experience engaging and engaging.
6. **List materials needed** (`materials_needed`): Specify the props or materials required for the storytelling session.
7. **Estimate the session time** (`time_estimate`): Provide a suggested duration for the storytelling session in minutes.
8. **Propose assessment suggestions** (`assessment_suggestions`): Suggest ways to measure engagement or learning during the session.

* **Educator Name (`educator_name`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter your name as the educator or facilitator leading the storytelling session.

	+ Choose the recommended age range for the storytelling session.
