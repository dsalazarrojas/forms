# Class Project Preference Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Class Project Preference Poll is a tool for students to express their preferences for upcoming class projects. By completing this form, students can indicate their interests, skills, and needs for project selection, allowing teachers to tailor project assignments to their abilities and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide your basic information**: Fill in your name and student ID (if applicable).
2. **Select your preferred project topic**: Choose the project topic that interests you most from the options provided.
3. **Explain your choice**: Type a brief explanation of why you chose your preferred project.
4. **Choose your team preferences**: Indicate your preferred team size and choose students you work well with.
5. **Add any additional information**: If needed, specify any special accommodations or additional preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Name** (`Student Name`, required): Your full name as a student.
- **Student ID** (`Student ID`, optional): Your student ID number (if applicable).
- **Class Name or Section** (`Class Name or Section`, required): The class or section you are currently enrolled in.
- **Grade Level** (`Grade Level`, optional): Your grade level (e.g., 9th, 10th, etc.).
- **Preferred Project Topic** (`Which project topic interests you most`, required): Choose the project topic that interests you most.
- **Second Choice Project** (`Second Choice Project`, optional): If you have a second preferred project topic, choose it here.
- **Reason for Choice** (`Why did you choose your preferred project`, required): Type a brief explanation of why you chose your preferred project.
- **Preferred Team Size** (`Preferred Team Size`, required): Choose your preferred team size: Work alone, Partner with one other student, Small group (3-4 students), Larger group (5+ students), or No preference.
- **Preferred Team Members** (`Preferred Team Members`, optional): List the names of students you work well with.
- **Students to Avoid** (`Any students you prefer not to work with`, optional): List the names of students you prefer not to work with (for teacher reference).
- **Relevant Skills** (`Skills you can contribute to the project`, optional): Type any skills you can contribute to the project.
- **Learning Interests** (`What do you hope to learn from this project`, required): Type what you hope to learn from this project.
- **Strengths in Group Projects** (`Your strengths in group projects`, optional): Type any strengths you bring to group projects.
- **Presentation Format** (`Preferred Presentation Format`, optional): Choose the presentation format you prefer (e.g., Written report, Oral presentation, Video or multimedia, Poster display, Interactive demonstration, Website or digital project, Other).
- **Special Accommodations** (`Any special accommodations needed for this project`, optional): List any special accommodations you need for this project.
- **Additional Preferences** (`Additional preferences or comments`, optional): Type any additional preferences or comments you have.

## Tips
- Be honest about your interests and skills to help teachers create inclusive and engaging projects.
- Choose your preferred project topic and team size carefully to ensure a good fit for your abilities and needs.
- If you have any special needs or accommodations, please specify them to help teachers create a supportive environment.
