# Stipend Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stipend Request Form is a collaborative tool used by both supervisers and students to request and agree on stipends for student projects or research. It ensures clear communication and prevents duplication of requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in all required fields for both the superviser and student sections.
2.  Enter accurate and up-to-date contact information (email, phone numbers, and addresses) for both parties.
3.  Choose the correct dates for both the superviser and student.
4.  Enter any additional comments or requests, if necessary.
5.  Sign and agree to the stipend request details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Superviser Name** (`superviser_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the superviser.
- **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the student.
- **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required): Enter the student's email address.
- **Superviser E-mail** (`superviser_email`, email, required): Enter the superviser's email address.
- **Superviser Phone** (`superviser_phone`, text, required): Enter the superviser's phone number.
- **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, required): Enter the student's phone number.
- **Superviser Address** (`superviser_address`, text, required): Enter the superviser's address.
- **Superviser Date** (`superviser_date`, date, required): Enter the date for the superviser's part of the request.
- **Student Date** (`student_date`, date, required): Enter the date for the student's part of the request.
- **Superviser Note** (`superviser_note`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments from the superviser.
- **Student Note** (`student_note`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments from the student.
- **Superviser Request** (`superviser_request`, text, optional): Enter any additional requests or comments from the superviser.
- **Student Request** (`student_request`, text, optional): Enter any additional requests or comments from the student.
- **Superviser Amount** (`superviser_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount requested by the superviser.
- **Student Amount** (`student_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount requested by the student.
- **Superviser Frequency** (`superviser_frequency`, text, optional): Enter the frequency of the stipend payments (e.g., weekly, biweekly, etc.).
- **Student Frequency** (`student_frequency`, text, optional): Enter the frequency of the stipend payments (e.g., weekly, biweekly, etc.).
- **Superviser Comments** (`superviser_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments from the superviser.
- **Student Comments** (`student_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments from the student.
- **Superviser Agreed** (`superviser_agreed`, select_one, required): Choose whether the superviser agrees to the stipend request.
- **Student Agreed** (`student_agreed`, select_one, required): Choose whether the student agrees to the stipend request.
- **Superviser Additional Info** (`superviser_additional_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information from the superviser.
- **Student Additional Info** (`student_additional_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information from the student.
- **Superviser Signature** (`superviser_signature`, text, required): Enter the superviser's signature.
- **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, text, required): Enter the student's signature.
