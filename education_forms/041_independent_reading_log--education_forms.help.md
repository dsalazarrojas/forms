# Independent Reading Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Independent Reading Log form is a simple tool for users to record their reading experiences, including the title of the book, author, date finished, and any notes or reflections.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter a brief description of your reading experience in the **Reading Log Form** field.
2.  Enter the **Book Title** as it appears on the book cover or in your reading material.
3.  Enter the author's name in the **Author** field.
4.  Select the date you finished reading the book in the **Reading Date** field.
5.  Enter any notes or reflections on your reading experience in the **Reading Notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Reading Log Form** (`reading_log_form`, text, optional): A field to enter a brief description of your reading experience.
*   **Book Title** (`book_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the book you have read.
*   **Author** (`author`, text, optional): Enter the author's name.
*   **Reading Date** (`reading_date`, date, optional): Select the date you finished reading the book.
*   **Reading Notes** (`reading_notes`, note, optional): Enter any notes or reflections on your reading experience.
