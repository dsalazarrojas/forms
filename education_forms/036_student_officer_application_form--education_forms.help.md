# Student Officer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Officer Application Form is used to collect application information from students who are interested in taking up officer positions within the institution. This form ensures that the applicants provide necessary details such as contact information, position preferences, departmental affiliations, and other relevant qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, fill in your name and email address accurately.
2. Choose your position preference from the options provided.
3. Select all the departments you are interested in.
4. Attach your CV or resume, and a brief statement explaining why you want to take up this position.
5. Describe your additional qualifications that make you a suitable candidate for this position.
6. Indicate your availability for the position.
7. Confirm your departmental preferences by selecting the correct department.
8. Finally, attach any supporting documents or files and add any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title** (`title`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your official records.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your official email address.
* **Position** (`position`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred officer position.
* **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, required): Select all the departments you are interested in. 
* **CV** (`cv`, text, required): Attach your CV or resume.
* **Why Position** (`why_position`, text, required): Write a brief statement explaining why you want to take up this position.
* **Additional Qualifications** (`additional_qualifications`, text, required): Describe your additional qualifications that make you a suitable candidate.
* **Application Statement** (`application_statement`, text, required): Attach a statement explaining why you are interested in this position.
* **Position Availability** (`position_availability`, select_one, required): Indicate your availability for the position.
* **Department 1** (`department_1`, select_one, required): Confirm your departmental preferences.
* **Department 2** (`department_2`, select_one, required): Confirm your departmental preferences.
* **File** (`file`, text, optional): Attach any supporting documents or files.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or information.
