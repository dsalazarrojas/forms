# Training Material Quality Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to evaluate the quality of training materials used during a training session. It helps assess the relevance, accuracy, and overall effectiveness of the materials.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the training session and the materials used.
2. Fill out the form with your assessment of the materials. Make sure to select the correct options for each question.
3. Provide any additional feedback in the "Feedback Question" field.
4. Submit the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Materials ID** (`training_material_id`, text, optional): Enter the unique ID of the training materials being evaluated.
* **Trainer Rating** (`trainer_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate the trainer's performance during the session. Select from the following options:
	+ Not Rated
	+ Poor
	+ Good
	+ Excellent
* **Content Relevance** (`content_relevance`, select_multiple, optional): Evaluate the relevance of the content to the training objectives. Select all that apply:
	+ Highly Relevant
	+ Somewhat Relevant
	+ Not Relevant
	+ Not Applicable
* **Content Accuracy** (`content_accuracy`, select_one, optional): Assess the accuracy of the content. Select from the following options:
	+ High
	+ Moderate
	+ Low
* **Content Comprehension** (`content_comprehension`, select_one, optional): Evaluate the ease of understanding the content. Select from the following options:
	+ High
	+ Moderate
	+ Low
* **Feedback Question** (`feedback_question`, text, optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments on the training materials.
* **Rating Comment** (`rating_comment`, text, optional): Enter any comments on the overall rating.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate the overall quality of the training materials. Select from the following options:
	+ Not Rated
	+ Poor
	+ Good
	+ Excellent
* **Training Materials Used** (`training_materials_used`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if any other materials were used during the session. Select all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Not Applicable
* **Training Materials Quality** (`training_materials_quality`, select_multiple, optional): Evaluate the quality of the training materials. Select all that apply:
	+ Good
	+ Poor
	+ Fair
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the evaluation.
* **Trainer Name** (`trainer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the trainer.
* **Trainer Email** (`trainer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Trainer Phone** (`trainer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.

**Tips**

* Make sure to review the training session before filling out the form.
* Be honest and provide constructive feedback.
* Keep the feedback concise and to the point.
* Do not fill out this form multiple times for the same training material.
