# Preschool Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Preschool Registration form is designed for parents or guardians to provide essential information about their children and families. This form will help our preschool staff understand their needs, provide necessary contact information, and ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required")
2. Double-check your information for accuracy
3. If you have any doubts or questions, feel free to ask our staff for assistance

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Please fill in your child's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Please fill in your child's last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Please provide your email address so we can contact you in case of any emergencies or updates.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Please fill in your child's address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Please provide your phone number so we can contact you in case of any emergencies or updates.
* **Birthday** (`birthday`, `date`, required): Please select your child's birthday in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Enrollment Start Date** (`enrollment_start_date`, `date`, required): Please select the date when your child will start enrolling to our preschool.
* **Enrollment End Date** (`enrollment_end_date`, `date`, required): Please select the date when your child will end enrolling to our preschool.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Please fill in the name of the person we should contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Please provide the phone number of the emergency contact.

## Tips
* Please double-check all the information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any information, feel free to ask our staff for assistance.
