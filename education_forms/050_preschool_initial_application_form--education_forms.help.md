# preschool_initial_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The preschool_initial_application_form is a tool used to collect information from parents or guardians about their child's education and background. This form helps schools or educational institutions assess the child's qualifications for preschool admission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide accurate and honest information about your child's background, education, and contact details.
2. Fill out each field with the required information.
3. Make sure to review and edit your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Parent Information**:
  - Parent Information (`parent_info`, text, required: false): This field is for you, the parent or guardian, to provide your name and contact details.
  - Hint: Please enter your name and contact information accurately.
- **Child Information**:
  - Child Information (`child_info`, text, required: false): This field is for providing details about your child's background and education history.
  - Hint: Please provide accurate and honest information about your child's education and background.
- **Contact Information**:
  - Contact Information (`contact_info`, text, required: false): This field is for you to provide your contact details, such as your address and phone number.
  - Hint: Please enter a valid address and phone number.
- **Education Information**:
  - Education Information (`education_info`, text, required: false): This field is for you to provide information about your child's education history, such as previous schools attended.
  - Hint: Please be accurate and honest about your child's education history.
- **Parent Signature**:
  - Parent Signature (`parent_signature`, text, required: false): This field is for you, the parent or guardian, to sign and date the form.
  - Hint: Please sign and date the form accurately.
- **Date Completed**:
  - Date Completed (`date_completed`, date, required: false): This field is for you to enter the date and time you completed the form.
  - Hint: Please enter the correct date and time you completed the form.
- **Time Completed**:
  - Time Completed (`time_completed`, time, required: false): This field is for you to enter the time you completed the form.
  - Hint: Please enter the correct time you completed the form.
- **Email**:
  - Email (`email`, email, required: false): This field is for you to enter a valid email address.
  - Hint: Please enter a valid email address.

## Tips
- Make sure to review and edit your answers before submitting the form.
- If you need help or have questions, contact the school or educational institution administering this form.
