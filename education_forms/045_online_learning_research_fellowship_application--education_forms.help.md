# Online Learning Research Fellowship Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Learning Research Fellowship Application form is designed to help us get to know you better and understand your background and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to answer all questions truthfully.
3. Double-check your work before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Date** (`application_date`, date, required): This field is for the date you're applying to the fellowship program. Please enter the date you would like to apply.
* **Research Focus Area** (`research_focus_area`, select_one, required): This field is where you should select your area of research interest. Please choose the option that best describes your research focus area.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): This is where you should enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): This field is for your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): This is where you should enter your phone number.
* **Academic Background** (`academic_background`, note, required): This field is for a brief description of your academic background.
* **Motivation Statement** (`motivation_statement`, text, required): This is where you should enter your motivation statement.
* **Expected Dedication** (`expected_dedication`, select_one, required): This field is where you should select your expected level of dedication.
* **Available Start Date** (`available_start_date`, date, required): This field is for the date you can start the fellowship program.
* **Research Experiences** (`research_experiences`, text, required): This is where you should enter your research experiences.
* **Skills** (`skills`, text, required): This field is for your skills.
* **Research Interests** (`research_interests`, text, required): This is where you should enter your research interests.
* **Education History** (`education_history`, text, required): This field is for your education history.
* **Relevant Courses** (`relevant_courses`, text, required): This is where you should enter your relevant courses.
* **Research Goals** (`research_goals`, text, required): This field is for your research goals.
* **Research Areas** (`research_areas`, text, required): This is where you should enter your research areas.
* **Research Focus Areas** (`research_focus_areas`, select_one, required): This field is where you should select your research focus area.
* **Research Tools** (`research_tools`, text, required): This is where you should enter your research tools.
* **Collaboration Interest** (`collaboration_interest`, select_multiple, required): This field is where you should select your collaboration interests.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, note, required): This field is for a brief description of your project details.
* **Research Experience** (`research_experience`, text, required): This is where you should enter your research experience.
* **References** (`references`, text, required): This field is for any references you have.
* **Research Fellowship Application Form** (`research_fellowship_application_form`, text, required): This is where you should enter any additional information about the fellowship application form.
* **Research Fellowship** (`research_fellowship`, select_one, required): This field is where you should select the type of research fellowship you're applying for.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, required): This field is to submit your application form.

Note: If you're unsure about any of the fields or options, please don't hesitate to reach out to us for clarification.
