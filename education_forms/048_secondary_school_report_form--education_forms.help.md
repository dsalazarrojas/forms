# secondary_school_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about secondary school students' performance and progress, including their academic performance, attendance, and overall conduct. It is used to report on student progress to parents or guardians and for internal record-keeping within the school.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's information, including any relevant details that will help identify them in the system.
2. Report on the student's academic performance, including their grades and overall progress.
3. Provide an attendance record, including any absences or tardiness.
4. Add any comments or feedback from the teacher.
5. Sign and date the form to confirm completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Info (Page 1)**: Enter the student's name, grade level, and any other relevant details that will help identify them in the system.
* **Academic Performance (Page 2)**: Enter the student's current grade level, including any recent grades or test scores.
* **Attendance Record (Page 3)**: Record the student's attendance, including any absences or tardiness.
* **Teacher Comments (Page 4)**: Add any comments or feedback from the teacher, including any areas of improvement or strengths.
* **Teacher Signature (Page 5)**: Sign and date the form to confirm completion.
* **Date Completed (Page 6)**: Enter the date the form was completed.
* **Student Grade (Page 7)**: Select the student's current grade level.
* **Student Conduct (Page 8)**: Select the student's overall conduct, such as their behavior and attitude.
* **Overall Attendance (Page 9)**: Enter the student's overall attendance percentage.
* **Class Progress (Page 10)**: Enter the student's progress in their current class.
* **Subject 1 (Page 11)**: Select the student's strengths or areas of improvement in the first subject.
* **Subject 2 (Page 12)**: Select the student's strengths or areas of improvement in the second subject.
* **Subject 3 (Page 13)**: Select the student's strengths or areas of improvement in the third subject.
* **Subject 4 (Page 14)**: Select the student's strengths or areas of improvement in the fourth subject.
* **Subject 5 (Page 15)**: Select the student's strengths or areas of improvement in the fifth subject.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you are using the correct student information to avoid errors.
* Double-check your calculations and entries for accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the relevant teacher or school administrator.
* This form is for internal use only and should not be shared with anyone outside the school.
* Please sign and date the form to confirm completion.
