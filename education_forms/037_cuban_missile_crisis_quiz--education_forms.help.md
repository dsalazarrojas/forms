# Cuban Missile Crisis Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users about the Cuban Missile Crisis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "History Class Name" field with the name of your history class.
2. Enter the year of the Cuban Missile Crisis in the "Event Year" field.
3. Select the correct "Missile Site" from the options provided.
4. Choose the type of missile(s) from the "Missile Type" field.
5. Enter the date of the crisis in the "Crisis Date" field.
6. Provide your response to the question in the "Response" field (optional).
7. Choose the follow up information relevant to your response in the "Follow Up" field.
8. Select your relevant education background in the "Education Background" field.
9. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
10. (Optional) Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
11. Add any additional note about your response in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **History Class Name**: Enter the name of your history class.
* **Event Year**: Enter the year of the Cuban Missile Crisis (1962).
* **Missile Site**: Select the correct location of the missile from the options provided.
	+ Option 1: Select one of the locations where the missile was located.
	+ Option 2: Select another location of the missile.
	+ Option 3: Select the third location of the missile.
* **Missile Type**: Choose the type(s) of missile from the options provided.
	+ Option 1: Select one or more types of missiles.
	+ Option 2: Select another type of missile.
	+ Option 3: Select the third type of missile.
* **Crisis Date**: Enter the date of the Cuban Missile Crisis.
* **Response**: Provide your response to the question (optional).
* **Follow Up**: Choose the follow up information relevant to your response.
	+ Option 1: Select the follow up information.
	+ Option 2: Select the second follow up information.
	+ Option 3: Select the third follow up information.
* **Education Background**: Select your relevant education background from the options provided.
	+ Option 1: Select one or more education backgrounds.
	+ Option 2: Select another education background.
	+ Option 3: Select the third education background.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Note**: Add any additional note about your response.
