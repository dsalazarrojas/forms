# Summer School Completion Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the completion of a student's school session. It ensures that the student's academic journey is properly documented and reported.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the School Completion Date field with the date the student completed their school session.
2. Enter the Program ID of the student's program.
3. Enter the Student ID of the student.
4. Enter the student's Name.
5. Enter the name of the School the student attended.
6. Enter the name of the Instructor who supervised the student's program.
7. If applicable, enter the Re-Enrollment Date for the student.
8. Enter the Grade Level of the student.
9. Select the Student Group the student belongs to.
10. Select the Student Level the student represents.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Completion Date** (`school_completion_date`, date, false): Enter the date when the student completed their school session.
* **Program ID** (`program_id`, number, false): Please enter your school's program ID.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, false): Enter the student's ID.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, false): Enter the student's name.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, false): Enter the name of the school the student attended.
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, false): Enter the name of the instructor who supervised the student's program.
* **Re-Enrollment Date** (`re_enrollment_date`, date, false): If applicable, enter the date of the student's re-enrollment.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, number, false): Enter the grade level of the student.
* **Student Group** (`student_group`, select_one, false): Select the student group the student belongs to.
* **Student Level** (`student_level`, select_one, false): Select the student level the student represents.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions to complete this form.
- Double-check that the information you enter is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you are unsure about any field, consult with the relevant authorities or seek clarification before submitting the form.
