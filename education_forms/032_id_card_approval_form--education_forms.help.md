# id_card_approval_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The `id_card_approval_form` is used to gather information and verify employee details for ID card approval purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the requested information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are accurate and complete.
3. Review and submit the form for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Code** (`school_code`, text, optional): Enter the school code (if applicable).
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the school or institution.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the employee.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the date of birth of the employee in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **ID Number** (`id_number`, number, optional): Enter the ID number of the employee.
* **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more departments the employee belongs to (Finance, IT, HR, Operations, Marketing).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the job title of the employee.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the employee.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the employee.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, select_one, optional): Select the approval status of the ID card (Approved, Pending, Rejected).
* **Reason for Rejection** (`reason_for_rejection`, text, optional): If the approval status is Rejected, enter the reason for rejection.

## Tips
* Double-check the accuracy of all entered information before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you have the necessary authority to submit this form.
* Review the approval status and reason for rejection fields carefully before submitting the form.
