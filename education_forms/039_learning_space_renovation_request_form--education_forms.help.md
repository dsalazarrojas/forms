# Learning Space Renovation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Learning Space Renovation Request Form is designed to gather information from instructors and staff to facilitate the renovation of learning spaces in the educational institution. This form helps to collect details about the renovation project, including general information, contact details, space characteristics, project scope, and photos or attachments. The form will be reviewed and approved by relevant stakeholders to ensure that the learning space renovation meets the necessary requirements and is completed on time.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Provide general information about the renovation project, including the title, start date, and expected completion date.
2. Add your contact details, including your name, email address, and phone number.
3. Enter details about the space to be renovated, including the location, size, and condition.
4. Specify the project scope, including the type of renovation, budget, and timeline.
5. Upload photos or attachments related to the renovation project, such as floor plans or sketches.
6. Add any additional comments or information about the project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info** (`general_info`):
	+ Enter a brief description of the renovation project.
	+ This field is not required.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`):
	+ Enter your name, email address, and phone number.
	+ This field is not required, but it's recommended to ensure that we can contact you for follow-up questions or clarification.
* **Space Info** (`space_info`):
	+ Describe the location, size, and condition of the space to be renovated.
	+ This field is not required.
* **Project Info** (`project_info`):
	+ Specify the type of renovation, budget, and timeline.
	+ This field is not required.
* **Photos** (`photos`):
	+ Upload photos or images related to the renovation project.
	+ This field is not required.
* **Attachments** (`attachments`):
	+ Upload attachments such as floor plans, sketches, or other relevant documents.
	+ This field is not required.
* **Comments** (`comments`):
	+ Add any additional comments or information about the project.
	+ This field is not required.
* **Submit** (`submit`):
	+ Submit the form to initiate the renovation project.
	+ This field is required.
 
## Tips

- Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that your renovation project is properly reviewed and approved.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us using the contact information provided in the form.
- Please be aware that the form is reviewed and approved by relevant stakeholders before the project begins.
