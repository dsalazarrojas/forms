# Nursery Field Trip Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nursery Field Trip Registration form is used to collect information about upcoming field trips in the nursery setting. This guide will walk you through the steps to complete the form accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the relevant options for each field as required.
2. Ensure that you have permission to complete this form (Permissions field).
3. Provide accurate contact information (Contacts field).
4. Enter any relevant medical details (Medical Details field).
5. List the students and staff attending the trip (Student List and Staff List fields).
6. Provide trip details (Trip Details field).
7. Specify any medical conditions (Medical Conditions field).
8. List any medications that need to be administered during the trip (Medications field).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Permissions** (`permissions`, `text`, `False`): This field is used to confirm that you have permission to complete this form. Please select 'Yes' if you have the necessary permissions.
* **Contacts** (`contacts`, `text`, `False`): Enter the contact information for the trip, including the name, phone number, and email of the person responsible for the trip.
* **Medical Details** (`medical_details`, `text`, `False`): Provide any medical details relevant to the trip, such as allergies or specific medical conditions.
* **Student List** (`student_list`, `select_multiple`, `False`): List the students attending the trip, selecting all who are participating.
* **Staff List** (`staff_list`, `select_multiple`, `False`): List the staff members attending the trip, selecting all who are participating.
* **Trip Details** (`trip_details`, `text`, `False`): Enter the details about the trip, including the destination, date, and time.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `select_one`, `False`): Specify if any medical conditions exist during the trip.
* **Medications** (`medication_list`, `select_multiple`, `False`): List any medications that need to be administered during the trip, selecting all relevant medications.
