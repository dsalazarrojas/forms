# Online Course Instructor Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Course Instructor Feedback Form is designed to gather students' feedback on the quality and effectiveness of the online courses. This feedback is essential in helping course instructors improve their teaching skills and for the educational institution to evaluate the courses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the course instructor you are providing feedback for.
2.  Rate the overall quality of the course using the scale provided.
3.  Provide any comments you have about the course.
4.  Suggest any improvements that you think the course could have.
5.  Rate the effectiveness of the course in terms of its impact on your learning.
6.  Enter your email address and phone number.
7.  Finally, fill in the submitter information, including your name, email, and comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Course Instructor** (`course_instructor`, select_one): Select the course instructor who led your online course.
	+ This field allows you to choose the instructor who taught your course.
	+ You can only choose one instructor at a time.
- **Feedback Rating** (`feedback_rating`, select_one): Rate the overall quality of the course using the scale provided.
	+ The rating scale is from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
	+ Rate the course based on your experience and feedback.
- **Feedback Comments** (`feedback_comments`, text): Provide any comments you have about the course.
	+ This is a text box where you can type in any comments you have.
	+ You can provide as much or as little information as you need.
- **Suggestion for Improvement** (`suggestion_for_improvement`, text): Suggest any improvements that you think the course could have.
	+ This is also a text box where you can type in any suggestions you have.
	+ You can provide as much or as little information as you need.
- **Feedback on Quality** (`feedback_on_quality`, number): Rate the effectiveness of the course in terms of its quality.
	+ The rating scale is from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
	+ Rate the course based on its quality.
- **Feedback on Effectiveness** (`feedback_on_effectiveness`, number): Rate the effectiveness of the course in terms of its impact on your learning.
	+ The rating scale is from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
	+ Rate the course based on its effectiveness.
- **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address.
	+ This is where you can enter your email address.
	+ Make sure to enter it correctly to receive any follow-up communication.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter your phone number.
	+ This is where you can enter your phone number.
	+ Make sure to enter it correctly.
- **Submitter Info** (`submitter_info`, note): This is a note field where you can provide any additional information.
	+ You can type in any information you want to provide.
	+ This is a free text area.
- **Course Instructor Info** (`course_instructor_info`, select_multiple): Select the characteristics of the course instructor.
	+ You can choose one or more of the options provided.
	+ This field is meant to help the course instructors improve their teaching skills.
