# Data Literacy For Teachers Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to register new participants for Data Literacy For Teachers programs and track their progress.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Make sure to check the current program status and participant status before submitting the form.
2. Fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
3. Ensure that the program details, participant details, and progress are up-to-date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input**: Enter your name as the program administrator or coordinator.
* **School Email**: Enter your school email address to help us contact you about program updates.
* **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the participant(s) you are registering.
* **Course**: Enter the name of the course(s) the participant is enrolled in.
* **Program Start Date**: Enter the start date of the program.
* **Program End Date**: Enter the end date of the program.
* **Program Location**: Enter the location of the program.
* **Course Length**: Enter the length of the course in weeks.
* **Course Length Unit**: Select the unit of time (weeks or months).
* **Participant Start Date**: Enter the start date of the participant in the program.
* **Participant End Date**: Enter the end date of the participant in the program.
* **Participant Status**: Select the status of the participant (active, inactive, or completed).
* **Course Status**: Select the status of the course (active, inactive, or completed).
* **Program Status**: Select the status of the program (active, inactive, or completed).
* **Participant Notes**: Enter any notes or comments about the participant.
* **Course Notes**: Enter any notes or comments about the course.
* **Course Participants**: Select the participants enrolled in the course.
* **Participant Progress**: This field is not directly applicable to the form, as it is for internal use only.

Note: The form is subject to change, and new fields may be added or removed. This help guide will be updated accordingly. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
