# Educational Design Thinking Workshop Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture information for educational design thinking workshops. It helps workshop organizers understand your needs and preferences, allowing us to tailor the workshop to suit your requirements.


## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter your contact details, including your name and job title (if applicable).
2. Choose the correct dates and duration for the workshop.
3. Answer "Yes" or "No" for the goals, logistics, and workshop type fields based on your needs.
4. Provide additional details for goals (if applicable).
5. Select the workshop location (if applicable).
6. Choose the workshop type and frequency (if applicable).
7. Specify the workshop capacity and language (if applicable).
8. Provide any additional information about the workshop (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Details**: Enter your name, email, phone number, organization, and job title to ensure we can reach out to you.
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your business card or email signature.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number to ensure we can reach you if needed.
* **Organization**: Enter your institution name (if applicable).
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title (if applicable).
* **Department**: Enter your department name (if applicable).
* **Dates and Duration**: Enter the start and end dates of the workshop you are requesting, and the expected duration.
* **Goals**: Answer "Yes" if you have specific goals for the workshop, and "No" otherwise.
* **Goals Details**: If you answered "Yes" to the previous question, please provide more details about your goals.
* **Logistics**: Answer "Yes" if you need specific logistics support, and "No" otherwise.
* **Workshop Location**: Enter the location for the workshop (if applicable).
* **Workshop Type**: Choose "Yes" if you have a specific workshop type in mind, and "No" otherwise.
* **Workshop Frequency**: Answer "Yes" if you need the workshop on a regular schedule, and "No" otherwise.
* **Workshop Capacity**: Choose "Yes" if you need a specific capacity for the workshop, and "No" otherwise.
* **Workshop Language**: Choose "Yes" if you need a specific language for the workshop, and "No" otherwise.
* **Workshop Additional Information**: Enter any additional information about the workshop (if applicable).
* **Workshop Start Date**: Enter the start date of the workshop (if applicable).
* **Workshop End Date**: Enter the end date of the workshop (if applicable).

## Tips
* Please answer all fields that are marked as required (true).
* If you're unsure about any field, please feel free to ask us for clarification.
* If you have any additional information or questions, please enter it in the workshop additional information field.
* Please submit the form and we will review it to ensure we can tailor the workshop to your needs.
