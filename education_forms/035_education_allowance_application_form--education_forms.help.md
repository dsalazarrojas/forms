# Education Allowance Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Education Allowance Application Form is used to collect information from students or educational recipients for the purpose of evaluating and processing their applications for educational allowances. The form is designed to gather relevant details to determine the eligibility and suitability of applicants for educational benefits.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling out your first and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields, respectively.
2.  Enter your Date of Birth in the format "MM/DD/YYYY" in the "Date of Birth" field.
3.  Enter a valid email address in the "Email" field for contact purposes.
4.  Fill in your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5.  Select your current level of education from the "Level of Education" dropdown menu.
6.  Provide a clear reason for seeking educational allowance in the "Reason for Education Allowance" field.
7.  If applicable, upload any required documents in the "Upload Documents" field.
8.  Choose the type of educational allowance you are applying for from the "Allowance Type" dropdown menu.
9.  Enter the amount of financial support you are requesting in the "Amount of Allowance" field.
10. Choose how frequently you would like to receive the allowance payments from the "Frequency of Payment" dropdown menu.
11.  Select the interval at which you would like to receive payments from the "Payment Period" field, using the date format "MM/DD/YYYY".
12.  Choose your preferred method of payment from the "Payment Method" dropdown menu.
13. (Optional) Select additional recipients of the allowance, if applicable, from the "Allowance Recipient" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`education_allowance_application_form_1`, `text`, `true`): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Last Name** (`education_allowance_application_form_2`, `text`, `true`): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Date of Birth** (`education_allowance_application_form_3`, `date`, `true`): Enter the date of your birth in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
*   **Email** (`education_allowance_application_form_4`, `email`, `true`): Enter a valid email address where the organization can contact you.
*   **Phone** (`education_allowance_application_form_5`, `text`, `true`): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Level of Education** (`education_allowance_application_form_6`, `select_multiple`, `true`): Select your current educational level from the dropdown options.
*   **Reason for Education Allowance** (`education_allowance_application_form_7`, `text`, `true`): Provide a clear reason for seeking educational allowance.
*   **Upload Documents** (`education_allowance_application_form_8`, `note`, `false`): Upload any documents supporting your application (if required).
*   **Allowance Type** (`education_allowance_application_form_9`, `select_one`, `true`): Choose the type of educational allowance you are applying for.
*   **Amount of Allowance** (`education_allowance_application_form_10`, `number`, `true`): Enter the amount of financial support you are requesting.
*   **Frequency of Payment** (`education_allowance_application_form_11`, `select_multiple`, `true`): Select how frequently you would like to receive payments.
*   **Payment Period** (`education_allowance_application_form_13`, `date`, `true`): Select the interval at which you would like to receive payments.
*   **Payment Method** (`education_allowance_application_form_14`, `select_one`, `true`): Choose your preferred method of payment.
*   **Allowance Recipient** (`education_allowance_application_form_15`, `select_multiple`, `false`): (Optional) Select additional recipients of the allowance, if applicable.
