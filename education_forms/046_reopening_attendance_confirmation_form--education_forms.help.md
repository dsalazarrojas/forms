# Reopening Attendance Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reopening Attendance Confirmation Form is used to confirm the presence of a student or staff member at a school or workplace setting after an initial absence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the school or workplace from the provided options.
2. Enter the location where the student or staff member was present.
3. Choose the date they returned.
4. Select the time of their return.
5. Confirm if the student or staff member is physically present.
6. Enter any additional comments or notes.
7. If applicable, select the tool assigned to them and provide the tool's name and version.
8. Confirm if the form is true or false.
9. Enter the submitter's name or select from the provided options.
10. Enter any additional comments or submitter's information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School or Workplace**: Select the school or workplace the student or staff member attended. This field is required.
* **Location**: Enter the specific location within the school or workplace where the student or staff member was present. This field is optional.
* **Date**: Enter the date the student or staff member returned to the school or workplace. This field is optional.
* **Time**: Enter the time the student or staff member returned to the school or workplace. This field is optional.
* **Physical Attendance**: Confirm if the student or staff member is physically present at the school or workplace. This field is optional.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the attendance. This field is optional.
* **Email**: Enter the student's or staff member's email address. This field is optional.
* **Phone**: Enter the student's or staff member's phone number. This field is optional.
* **Confirmation**: Confirm if the form is true or false. This field is optional.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool assigned to the student or staff member. This field is optional.
* **Other Tool**: Enter the tool name and version if not listed in the assigned tools. This field is optional.
* **Other Tool Name**: Enter the tool name. This field is optional.
* **Tool Version**: Enter the tool version. This field is optional.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback. This field is optional.
* **Submitted at**: Enter the date the form was submitted. This field is optional.
* **Submitted by**: Select the submitter's name or choose from the provided options. This field is optional.
* **Submitter Info**: Enter the submitter's information. This field is optional.
* **Created at**: Enter the date the form was created. This field is optional.
* **Updated at**: Enter the date the form was last updated. This field is optional.
* **Deleted at**: Enter the date the form was deleted. This field is optional.
* **Deleted by**: Select the deleted user's name or choose from the provided options. This field is optional.
* **Deleted Reason**: Enter the reason for deletion. This field is optional.
* **Deleted user info**: Enter the deleted user's information. This field is optional.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback. This field is optional.
* **Confirmation**: Confirm if the form is true or false. This field is optional.
