# Pedagogical Research Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from educators or researchers about their pedagogical approaches, theories, and practices. The purpose of this survey is to collect insights into the effectiveness and applicability of different pedagogical research methods in real-world settings.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To start the survey, select the corresponding answers to each question. You can choose one or more options for select types and only one option for select one types.
*   Fill in the text areas as instructed.
*   Once you've selected your answers, submit the form to complete the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Teaching Approach (Teaching Approach)** (Select Multiple): This question asks about your current approach to teaching. You can choose one or more of the options 'Yes', 'No', or 'Other'.
*   **Pedagogical Theory (Pedagogical Theory)** (Text): Provide a short description of your current pedagogical theory or approach.
*   **Age Range (Age Range)** (Select One): This question is about the age range of your students. Choose one of the options 'Yes', 'No', or 'Other' as per your answer.
*   **Years of Service (Years of Service)** (Text): Please provide the number of years you have been working as an educator.
*   **Feedback (Feedback)** (Text): Share any additional feedback you have about your teaching experience.
*   **Form Data (Form Data)** (Text): This field is reserved for internal use and does not require an answer. Please leave it blank.
