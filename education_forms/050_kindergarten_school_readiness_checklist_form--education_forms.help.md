# Kindergarten School Readiness Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you assess your child's readiness for kindergarten by evaluating their language, physical, and social-emotional development skills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions on each page by selecting all the skills that your child has demonstrated. You can select multiple skills for each page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, required: false): This field is a brief introduction to the form and is not required to be filled out.
* **Language Skills** (`language_skills`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the language skills that your child has demonstrated:
	+ Able to communicate effectively
	+ Uses words to identify and describe objects
	+ Uses short sentences to communicate
	+ Listens for longer periods of time
	+ Identifies and says the names of all letters
	+ Identifies and writes numbers up to 20
	+ Counts up to 100
	+ Demonstrates an awareness of the concept of past, present, and future
	+ Uses vocabulary that is age appropriate
* **Physical Development** (`physical_development`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the physical skills that your child has demonstrated:
	+ Shows an awareness of right and left
	+ Shows an awareness of up and down
	+ Shows an awareness of front and back
	+ Walks up and down stairs
	+ Runs or walks with ease
	+ Kicks a ball forward
	+ Hops on one foot
	+ Kicks a ball back and forth
* **Social-Emotional Development** (`social_emotional_development`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the social-emotional skills that your child has demonstrated:
	+ Separates from others when appropriate
	+ Initiates play
	+ Shows an awareness of feelings in self and others
	+ Shows an ability to make friends
	+ Is willing to share toys
	+ Is not easily frustrated
	+ Is willing to try new things
	+ Is willing to follow rules

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and accurately to the best of your knowledge.
* You can select multiple skills for each page.
* If you're unsure about a particular skill, you can always consult with your child's preschool or kindergarten teacher.
