# Angles And Scales Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess students' understanding of angles and scales concepts. It is used to collect information from students about their ability to calculate angles and perform scale conversions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your full name in the **Student Name** field.
* Answer the **Calculate the angle given two lines** question in the **Calculate the angle given two lines** field. This is a short answer question that requires you to demonstrate your understanding of angle calculations.
* If you want to provide an answer to the **Scale conversion problem**, fill in the **Scale conversion problem** field with a numerical answer.
* If you want to provide feedback or comments to the teacher, type in the **Teacher notes** field.
* Finally, rate your confidence level in answering the questions from 1 to 5 in the **Confidence level** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student name**: This is where you enter your full name, as required.
* **Calculate the angle given two lines**: This question requires you to demonstrate your understanding of calculating angles using the information provided in the prompt.
* **Scale conversion problem**: If you're able to calculate a scale conversion, provide a numerical answer in this field.
* **Scale conversion problem**: This field is optional, but if you're able to provide a numerical answer, it will be recorded.
* **Confidence level**: This field allows you to rate your confidence level in answering the questions from 1 to 5. This will help the teacher understand your level of understanding.
* **Teacher notes**: This field is for the teacher's feedback and grading notes. It's not required, but it's a useful tool for the teacher to provide feedback to you.
 
## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to complete the assessment.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, you can ask for help from your teacher.
* If you have any feedback or comments for the teacher, use the **Teacher notes** field to provide them.
 
This guide will help you navigate the form and complete it efficiently and effectively.
