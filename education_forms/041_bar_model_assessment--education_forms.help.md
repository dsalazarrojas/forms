# Bar Model Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Bar Model Assessment" form is a tool to evaluate students' understanding and application of bar models in solving math problems, particularly in the context of addition, subtraction, comparison, multi-step problems, fractions, and ratios.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information, marked as required in the form.
2. Select the correct option from the dropdown menus where applicable.
3. Type your response in the text fields where specified.
4. Ensure you answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Assessment Date
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the assessment.

### Student Information
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, optional): If you have a student ID, enter it here.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_one, required): Select your current grade level.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required): Enter your teacher's name.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter your school name.

### Problem Information
* **Type of Bar Model Problem** (`problem_type`, select_one, required): Select the category of the problem.
* **Original Problem Statement** (`problem_statement`, text, required): Copy the word problem as given.
* **Bar Model Description** (`bar_model_description`, text, required): Describe the bar model you created.
* **Solution Method Used** (`solution_method`, select_one, required): Select the method you used to solve the problem.
* **Final Answer** (`answer_provided`, text, required): Enter your solution.
* **Work Shown** (`work_shown`, text, required): Describe your mathematical work.

### Self-Assessment
* **I Understand the Problem** (`understanding_check`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you understand the problem.
* **Bar Model Strategy Effectiveness** (`strategy_effectiveness`, select_one, required): Rate how helpful the bar model was.
* **Confidence in Your Solution** (`confidence_level`, select_one, required): Rate your confidence in your solution.
* **Difficulty Level of Problem** (`difficulty_level`, select_one, optional): Rate the difficulty level of the problem.
* **Areas Where You Need Help** (`areas_needing_help`, text, optional): Identify areas where you need improvement.
* **Time Spent on Problem (minutes)** (`time_spent`, number, optional): Estimate the time spent on the problem.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any other observations.
