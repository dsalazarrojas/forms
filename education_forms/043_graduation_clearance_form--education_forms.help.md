# Graduation Clearance Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Graduation Clearance Form is a document used to confirm a student's graduation status.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you are authorized to complete this form.
2.  Fill out all the required fields (marked as `required` in the form).
3.  Fill out any optional fields that you feel are relevant to your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (text): Enter the student's legal name.
* **Student ID** (number): Enter the student's identification number or ID.
* **Date of Birth** (date): Enter the student's date of birth.
* **Email** (email): Enter the student's email address.
* **Phone Number** (text): Enter the student's phone number (optional).
* **Address** (text): Enter the student's address (optional).
* **Graduation Date** (date): Enter the date the student graduated.
* **Graduation Year** (number): Enter the year the student graduated.
* **GPA** (number): Enter the student's cumulative GPA (optional).
* **Graduation Eligibility** (note): Enter any relevant graduation eligibility information (optional).
* **Comments** (text): Enter any additional comments regarding the student's graduation status (optional).
* **Signature** (select multiple): Select "Yes" if you are confirming the student's graduation status.
* **Clearance Officer Signature** (select one): Select the clearance officer's title (e.g., "Clearance Officer", "Dean", "Director").
* **Date** (date): Enter the current date for the clearance officer's signature.
* **Time** (time): Enter the current time for the clearance officer's signature.

Note: All fields marked as `required` must be filled out before submitting the form.
