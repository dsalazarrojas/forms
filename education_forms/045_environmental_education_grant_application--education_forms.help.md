# Environmental Education Grant Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for an Environmental Education Grant Application. It helps applicants provide details about themselves and their organization, which will be reviewed for potential funding opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your email address in the "User Email" field.
2. Enter your name in the "User Name" field.
3. Provide your organization's name in the "Organization" field.
4. Specify your contact title in the "Contact Title" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Email** (`user_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to contact you regarding the application.
- **User Name** (`user_name`, text, optional): Enter your name to be associated with the application.
- **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): Provide the name of your organization or institution.
- **Contact Title** (`contact_title`, text, optional): Specify your contact title within your organization.

## Tips
- Ensure you fill in all required and optional fields accurately to provide a complete application.
- Check your email address for correct formatting before submitting.
- If you have any issues or questions, reach out to form@gic.mx for assistance.
