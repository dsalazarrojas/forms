# Final Exam Preparation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Final Exam Preparation Checklist" form is a tool designed to help students prepare for their exams by gathering information on their exam dates, revision progress, and other relevant details. This form is intended to facilitate students' preparation, but it may also be used by educators or administrators to track student progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form's sections and understand what each field is asking for.
2. For each field, select the corresponding option that best describes your situation or answer the question as instructed.
3. Ensure that you answer all required fields (marked as "Required" in the form).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Exam Dates** (`exam_dates`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select all the exam dates that apply to you.
* **Revision Topics** (`revision_topics`, Text, Optional): List the topics you need to revise for the exam.
* **Study Progress** (`study_progress`, Select One, Optional): Select "Yes" if you feel you are making good progress, otherwise select "No".
* **Confidence Levels** (`confidence_levels`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select your current confidence level regarding the exam, such as "High" or "Low".
* **Revision Plan** (`revision_plan`, Text, Optional): Describe your plan for revising the material for the exam.
* **Exam Notes** (`exam_notes`, Text, Optional): Write any notes or observations you have about the exam.
* **Study Materials** (`study_materials`, Text, Optional): List any study materials you have used or plan to use for the exam.
* **Revision Schedule** (`revision_schedule`, Date, Optional): Select a date for your revision schedule.
* **Tips**: Remember to review and update this form regularly to ensure your progress and preparation are accurately reflected.
