# teacher_gift_certificate_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to order a teacher gift certificate for a specific teacher. It requires information about the teacher, the recipient, and the gift certificate details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details (first name, last name, gift certificate type, gift certificate value, and gift certificate message).
2. Provide the teacher's details (teacher name, teacher school name, teacher email, and teacher grade level).
3. Enter the recipient's address (address, city, state, and postal code).
4. Enter the recipient's phone number.
5. Select the order date and order time.
6. Enter any additional comments about the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: The first name of the person ordering the gift certificate.
* **Last Name**: The last name of the person ordering the gift certificate.
* **Teacher Name**: The name of the teacher for whom the gift certificate is being ordered.
* **Teacher School Name**: The name of the school where the teacher works.
* **Teacher Email**: The email address of the teacher.
* **Teacher Email Type**: The type of email address (e.g., school email, personal email).
* **Teacher Department**: The department where the teacher works.
* **Teacher Grade Level**: The grade level of the teacher.
* **Gift Certificate Type**: The type of gift certificate (e.g., physical certificate, digital certificate).
* **Gift Certificate Value**: The value of the gift certificate.
* **Recipient Address**: The address where the gift certificate will be delivered.
* **Recipient City**: The city where the gift certificate will be delivered.
* **Recipient State**: The state where the gift certificate will be delivered.
* **Recipient Postal Code**: The postal code where the gift certificate will be delivered.
* **Recipient Phone**: The phone number of the person receiving the gift certificate.
* **Order Date**: The date when the gift certificate order was placed.
* **Order Time**: The time when the gift certificate order was placed.
* **Gift Certificate Number**: A unique number for the gift certificate.
* **Gift Certificate Total Amount**: The total value of the gift certificate.
* **Gift Certificate Message**: A message to be printed on the gift certificate.
* **Order Comments**: Any additional comments about the order.
* **Submit**: To confirm and submit the order.

Please note that some fields are optional and may not be required for every order.
