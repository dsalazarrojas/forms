# Global Educator Collaboration Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Educator Collaboration Application is a tool designed to collect information from educators about their collaboration interests and preferences for global projects. This application helps us understand their needs and facilitates effective collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form carefully, following the instructions below.
2.  Select the "Application Type" field to indicate whether or not you are applying for a collaboration project.
3.  Enter your school's name in the "School Name" field.
4.  Fill out the "Contact Name" field with your name as it should be displayed in the collaboration platform.
5.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
6.  Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field (optional).
7.  Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field (optional).
8.  Specify your location in the "Location" field (optional).
9.  Select your collaboration interests in the "Collaboration Interest", "International Collaboration Interest", and "Other Collaboration Interest" fields.
10.  Indicate if you speak languages other than the default language in the "Language Spoken" field.
11.  Choose your preferred collaboration platform in the "Preferred Collaboration Platform" field.
12.  Select your collaboration frequency in the "Collaboration Frequency" field.
13.  Choose whether you are dominant or not in collaboration in the "Collaboration Dominance" field.
14.  Select the collaboration format in the "Collaboration Format" field.
15.  Specify the languages you are proficient in for collaboration in the "Collaboration Language" field.
16.  Select the status of your application in the "Application Status" field.
17.  Select the status of your submission in the "Submission Status" field.
18.  Enter the date and time of your submission in the "Submission Date" and "Submission Time" fields.
19.  Add any comments or notes in the "Comments" and "Notes" fields (optional).
20.  Click the "Submit" button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Type** (required): Select "Yes" if you are applying for a collaboration project, "No" otherwise.
* **School Name** (required): Enter your school's name as it should be displayed in the collaboration platform.
* **Contact Name** (required): Fill out this field with the name to be displayed in the collaboration platform.
* **Email** (required): Enter your email address to receive updates and communication about the collaboration project.
* **Phone** (optional): Provide your phone number to receive calls or messages from collaborators.
* **Job Title** (optional): Enter your job title to provide additional information about your role.
* **Location** (optional): Specify your location to facilitate communication and collaboration.
* **Collaboration Interest** (optional): Select the types of collaboration interests you have, such as "Yes" or "No".
* **International Collaboration Interest** (optional): Select the types of international collaboration interests you have, such as "Yes" or "No".
* **Other Collaboration Interest** (optional): Provide any other collaboration interests not listed in the above fields.
* **Language Spoken** (required): Indicate if you speak languages other than the default language.
* **Preferred Collaboration Platform** (required): Choose your preferred collaboration platform.
* **Collaboration Frequency** (required): Select your collaboration frequency, such as "Yes" or "No".
* **Collaboration Dominance** (required): Indicate whether you are dominant or not in collaboration, such as "Yes" or "No".
* **Collaboration Format** (required): Select the collaboration format you prefer, such as "Yes" or "No".
* **Collaboration Language** (required): Specify the languages you are proficient in for collaboration, such as "Yes" or "No".
* **Application Status** (required): Select the status of your application, such as "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Submission Status** (required): Select the status of your submission, such as "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Submission Date** (required): Enter the date of your submission.
* **Submission Time** (required): Enter the time of your submission.
* **Comments** (optional): Provide any comments about your application.
* **Notes** (optional): Add any additional notes about your collaboration interest.
* **Submit** (required): Click this button to submit your application.
* **Cancel** (optional): Click this button to cancel your application.
