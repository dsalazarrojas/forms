# recommendation-letter-generator - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help authorized individuals write a recommendation letter for a former student, employee, or colleague. The letter provides an opportunity to showcase an individual's qualifications, skills, and accomplishments, helping them stand out in their profession or academic pursuits.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and honest information.
2. Ensure that each field is filled out as requested, and the required fields are not left empty.
3. Use the "Submit" button to generate the letter once you have completed all necessary fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`user_information`, `text`, required: false): This field is optional and serves as a general information section for the user.
* **Name** (`user_name`, `text`, required: true): Enter the name of the individual being recommended.
* **Email** (`user_email`, `email`, required: true): Enter the individual's email address.
* **Phone** (`user_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the individual's phone number (optional).
* **Position** (`user_position`, `text`, required: true): Enter the individual's current or past position.
* **Recommendation Issuer** (`recommendation_issuer`, `text`, required: true): Enter the name and title of the person writing the recommendation letter.
* **Issuer Position** (`issuer_position`, `text`, required: true): Enter the writer's position or title.
* **Issuer Institution** (`issuer_institution`, `text`, required: true): Enter the name of the organization or institution affiliated with the writer.
* **Date Started** (`date_start`, `date`, required: true): Select the date when the individual started their position.
* **Date Ended** (`date_ended`, `date`, required: false): Select the date when the individual ended their position (optional).
* **Recommendation Period** (`recommendation_period`, `text`, required: true): Enter the length of time the individual worked or was associated with the organization.
* **Reason for Letter** (`reason_for_letter`, `text`, required: true): Briefly explain the purpose of the letter.
* **Recommendation Letter** (`recommendation_letter`, `text`, required: true): Enter the content of the letter.
* **Sign Name** (`sign_name`, `text`, required: true): Enter the name of the signatory.
* **Sign Title** (`sign_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter the title of the signatory (optional).
* **Sign Date** (`sign_date`, `date`, required: true): Select the date when the letter was signed.
* **Sign Phone** (`sign_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the signatory's phone number (optional).
* **Sign Email** (`sign_email`, `email`, required: true): Enter the signatory's email address.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, required: true): Enter the physical or digital signature.
* **Letter Header** (`letter_header`, `text`, required: false): Enter a header or introduction for the letter (optional).
* **Letter Body** (`letter_body`, `text`, required: true): Enter the main content of the letter.
* **Letter Footer** (`letter_footer`, `text`, required: false): Enter a closing or footer for the letter (optional).
* **File Upload** (`file_upload`, `text`, required: false): Upload a supporting file or attachment (optional).
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, `text`, required: false): Click this button to generate the letter.

## Tips
* Double-check the fields for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* Use the provided instructions and examples to ensure a well-structured and professional letter.
* If you're unsure about any field, please refer to the provided explanations or contact your supervisor for guidance.
