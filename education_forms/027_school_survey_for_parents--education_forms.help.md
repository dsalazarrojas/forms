# School Survey For Parents - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "School Survey For Parents" is designed to gather feedback from parents on their involvement and communication with teachers, methods used to track progress, and support services utilized for their child's education.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as instructed:

1. **First Name**: Enter your name as a parent or guardian.
2. **Last Name**: Enter your surname as a parent or guardian.
3. **what_do_you_do**: Describe your role or occupation related to the child's education (optional).
4. **parents_participation_level**: Choose how frequently you participate in your child's education (select one option).
5. **how_frequently_do_you_communicate_with_your_child_s_teacher**: Choose how often you communicate with your child's teacher (select multiple options if applicable).
6. **what_do_you_use_to_teach**: Choose the methods you use to teach your child (select multiple options if applicable).
7. **how_do_you_keep_tracked_of_your_child_s_progress**: Choose how you keep track of your child's progress (select one option).
8. **what_support_services_have_you_utilized**: Choose the support services you have utilized (select multiple options if applicable).
9. **additional_comments**: Enter any additional comments or remarks you'd like to share with us (optional).
10. **Submit**: Review and submit your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text): Enter your first name as a parent or guardian.
* **Last Name** (text): Enter your surname as a parent or guardian.
* **what_do_you_do** (text): Describe your role or occupation related to the child's education (optional).
* **parents_participation_level** (select one):
	+ Option 1: Frequently involved
	+ Option 2: Occasionally involved
	+ Option 3: Rarely involved
* **how_frequently_do_you_communicate_with_your_child_s_teacher** (select multiple):
	+ Option 1: Daily
	+ Option 2: Weekly
	+ Option 3: Rarely
* **what_do_you_use_to_teach** (select multiple):
	+ Option 1: Online resources
	+ Option 2: Books
	+ Option 3: Other methods
* **how_do_you_keep_tracked_of_your_child_s_progress** (select one):
	+ Option 1: Report card
	+ Option 2: Parent portal
	+ Option 3: Other methods
* **what_support_services_have_you_utilized** (select multiple):
	+ Option 1: Tutoring
	+ Option 2: Counseling
	+ Option 3: Other services
* **additional_comments** (text): Enter any additional comments or remarks you'd like to share with us (optional).

## Tips
* Take your time to answer the questions truthfully.
* Be as specific as possible when providing answers.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
