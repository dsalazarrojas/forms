# 2020 – 2021 Application For Membership - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect personal and program-related information from individuals interested in joining the 2020-2021 educational program. Please fill out the form carefully and thoroughly to ensure that your application is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and ensure that you have all required information ready.
2. Enter your personal details accurately, such as full name, email address, and phone number.
3. Select your preferred payment method and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name**: Enter your legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email address**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to communicate with you regarding your membership confirmation.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number, including country code.
* **Date of birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Age**: If you prefer not to provide your date of birth, you can enter your age.
* **Mailing address**: Enter your street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Emergency contact name**: Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency contact phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Experience level**: Select your current skill level: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Professional.
* **How did you hear about us?**: Choose how you became aware of the program.
* **Any special needs or accommodations?**: Describe any special needs or accommodations you require.
* **Additional comments or notes**: Provide any other information you would like to share with us.
* **Preferred start date**: Select the date you would like to begin the program.
* **Preferred courses**: Choose all courses you are interested in.
* **Payment method**: Select your preferred payment method.
* **Agree to terms and conditions**: Select "Yes" to confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Tips

* Ensure that you have all required information ready before starting the application.
* Review each field carefully to avoid errors.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask for help.
* Double-check that you have agreed to the terms and conditions before submitting the application.
