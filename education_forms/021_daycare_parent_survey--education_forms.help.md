# Daycare Parent Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daycare Parent Survey is designed to collect information from parents on their experiences and thoughts about the daycare service. This form is intended for parents who have been or are currently using the service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Make sure you have been or are currently using the daycare service.
2. If you have already provided feedback, do not complete this form again.
3. Fill out the form by providing the required information in the relevant fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your valid phone number.
* **Feedback**: Provide your honest thoughts and experiences about the daycare service. Be as specific as possible.
* **Rating**: Select one of the four rating options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or thoughts about your experience.
* **Rating and Feedback fields are required for us to understand your overall impression and specific issues you face while using our service. Please answer these fields truthfully.**

## Tips
- Be honest and specific in your feedback and rating to help us improve the service.
- Please note that your email address and phone number will only be used to contact you if we need more information or to confirm that we have received your feedback.
- We value your opinion and appreciate your time in completing this form.
