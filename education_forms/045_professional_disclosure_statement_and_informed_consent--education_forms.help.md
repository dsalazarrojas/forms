# Professional Disclosure Statement And Informed Consent - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a healthcare provider's professional relationship with a patient, including a professional disclosure statement, informed consent, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the professional disclosure statement, which describes the provider's relationship with the patient and their responsibilities.
2. Enter the informed consent, which indicates the patient's understanding and agreement to the terms of the relationship.
3. Enter the date of service, which is the date on which the professional service was provided.
4. Enter the date completed, which is the date on which the form was completed.
5. Sign and date the provider signature section, indicating that you have read and understand the form.
6. Have the patient sign and date their signature section, indicating that they have read and understand the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Professional Disclosure Statement And Informed Consent (1)
* **Professional Disclosure Statement And Informed Consent** (`professional disclosure statement and informed consent`, text, required): Enter a statement explaining the professional's relationship with the patient and their responsibilities.

### Patient Informed Consent (2)
* **Patient Informed Consent** (`patient informed consent`, text, required): Enter a statement indicating the patient's understanding and agreement to the terms of the relationship.

### Date Of Service (3)
* **Date Of Service** (`date of service`, date, required): Enter the date on which the professional service was provided.

### Date Completed (4)
* **Date Completed** (`date completed`, date, required): Enter the date on which the form was completed.

### Provider Signature (5)
* **Provider Signature** (`provider signature`, text, required): Sign and date your signature to indicate that you have read and understand the form.

### Patient Signature (6)
* **Patient Signature** (`patient signature`, text, required): Have the patient sign and date their signature to indicate that they have read and understand the form.

## Tips
* Be sure to enter accurate and clear information for each field.
* Ensure that the patient has read and understands the form before signing it.
* Store the form in a secure location, such as a patient's medical record, and keep it confidential.
* Consider having a separate document for each patient to ensure that each form is accurate and up-to-date.
