# Art Class Enrollment Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from students who want to enroll in an art class. It helps us understand your background, preferences, and commitments, ensuring a smooth and enjoyable learning experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your student information (section 1), including your full name, date of birth, and email address.
2. Select your art class level, preferred art medium, and class format in (sections 2-4).
3. Choose your schedule preference and indicate if you have art experience (section 5).
4. Answer questions about your past art experiences and learning goals (section 6).
5. Confirm your enrollment details, including the term, date, and payment method (section 7).
6. Review and agree to our code of conduct, liability waiver, and parental consent (section 8).
7. Finally, provide any additional information or questions you may have (section 9).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents. This is a required field.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY. This is a required field.
* **Email Address**: Enter your valid email address where you can be reached for class updates. This is a required field.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted for class-related matters. This is an optional field.
* **Student ID Number**: If you've taken art classes before, enter your student ID number. This is an optional field.
* **Art Class Level**: Choose your current skill level: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Mixed ability.
* **Preferred Art Medium**: Select all the art mediums you enjoy or want to learn about: Drawing, Painting, Sculpture, Printmaking, Digital art, Mixed media, or Other.
* **Preferred Class Format**: Select how you'd like your class to be conducted: In-person, Online, or Hybrid.
* **Schedule Preference**: Choose your preferred class schedule: Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Weekend, or Flexible.
* **Art Experience Level**: Indicate your level of art experience: No experience, Some experience, Moderate experience, or Advanced experience.
* **Have you taken art classes before**: If you have prior art class experience, select Yes - in school, Yes - private instruction, or Yes - other.
* **What are your learning goals**: Describe your goals for taking the art class, such as Hobby, Skill development, or Career prep.
* **Enrollment Term**: Select the term you'd like to enroll in: Spring semester, Fall semester, Summer session, Year-round, or Single session.
* **Enrollment Date**: Enter the date you'd like to start your art class. This is a required field.
* **Payment Method for Class Fees**: Choose how you'd like to pay for your class fees: Full payment, Payment plan, Financial aid, or Other.
* **Code of Conduct**: Agree to follow our class code of conduct.
* **Liability Waiver**: Understand and accept our liability waiver.
* **Parent or Guardian Consent**: If you're under 18, confirm you've obtained parental consent.
* **Additional Information or Questions**: Provide any additional details or questions you may have about the class or enrollment process.
