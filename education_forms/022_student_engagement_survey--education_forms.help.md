# Student Engagement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students about their engagement with their education. The survey aims to gather feedback and identify areas of improvement to enhance the learning experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the form title to start.
2. Provide your student information in the "Student Information" field.
3. Share your experiences and thoughts about your education in the "Student Experience" field.
4. If applicable, provide information about your teachers in the "Teacher" field.
5. Share any additional comments or feedback about your classroom environment in the "Comments" field.
6. If you have a preferred email address, please provide it in the "Email" field.
7. If applicable, enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
8. Click the "Submit" button to submit your survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `text`, optional): This is a text field where you can provide any information you'd like to share about yourself.
* **Student Information** (`student_info`, `text`, optional): Please enter your student information in this field.
* **Student Experience** (`student_experience`, `text`, optional): Share your thoughts and feelings about your education in this field.
* **Teacher** (`teacher_info`, `text`, optional): If applicable, provide information about your teachers here.
* **Classroom** (`classroom_info`, `text`, optional): Share any feedback or comments about your classroom environment.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): This is an open-ended field where you can provide any additional feedback or comments.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): If you have a preferred email address, please enter it here. This will help us contact you for any follow-up questions or actions.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): If you have a phone number, please enter it here. This is not required, but it will help us reach out to you if needed.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, optional): Click this button to submit your survey.
