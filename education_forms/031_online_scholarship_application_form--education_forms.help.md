# Online Scholarship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This online scholarship application form is designed to gather necessary information from students who wish to apply for scholarships. 
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the application date.
2. Provide a title for your application.
3. Enter your contact name.
4. Enter your address details.
5. Select your state of residence.
6. Enter your phone number and email.
7. Specify the scholarship sponsor (if applicable).
8. Select multiple scholarship types if applicable.
9. Enter any additional information.
10. Upload necessary documents.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Application Date** (`application_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are applying for the scholarship.
- **Application Title** (`application_title`, text, required): Enter a title for your application.
- **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Address Line 1** (`address_line_1`, text, required): Enter the street address or postal address where you can be reached.
- **Address Line 2** (`address_line_2`, text, required): Enter the apartment or suite number if applicable.
- **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city where you reside.
- **State** (`state`, select_one, required): Select the state where you reside from the provided options.
- **Zip** (`zip`, text, required): Enter your postal code.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
- **Scholarship Sponsor** (`scholarship_sponsor`, text, required): Enter the name of the scholarship sponsor (if applicable).
- **Scholarship Type** (`scholarship_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of scholarship you are applying for (e.g., Merit Based, Need Based, Both, Other).
- **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, required): Enter any additional information you wish to share with the scholarship committee.
- **Uploaded Documents** (`uploaded_documents`, note, required): Upload any necessary documents as required by the scholarship sponsor.
- **Uploaded Signature** (`uploaded_signature`, text, required): Upload a scanned copy of your signature.
- **Uploaded Transcript** (`uploaded_transcript`, text, required): Upload a scanned copy of your transcript.
- **Uploaded Resume** (`uploaded_resume`, text, required): Upload a scanned copy of your resume.
- **Uploaded Letter of Recommendation** (`uploaded_letter_of_recommendation`, text, required): Upload a scanned copy of your letter of recommendation.
- **Uploaded Proof of Enrollment** (`uploaded_proof_of_enrollment`, text, required): Upload a scanned copy of your proof of enrollment.
- **Uploaded Proof of Financial Aid** (`uploaded_proof_of_financial_aid`, text, required): Upload a scanned copy of your proof of financial aid.
- **Uploaded Copies of Certificates** (`uploaded_copies_of_certificates`, text, required): Upload a scanned copy of your copies of certificates.
- **Uploaded Additional Documents** (`uploaded_additional_documents`, text, required): Upload any other documents as required by the scholarship sponsor.
- **Uploaded Agreement** (`uploaded_agreement`, text, required): Upload a scanned copy of the agreement.
- **Uploaded Contract** (`uploaded_contract`, text, required): Upload a scanned copy of the contract.
- **Uploaded Other** (`uploaded_other`, text, required): Upload any other documents as required by the scholarship sponsor.

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are accurately filled out.
- Upload scanned copies of necessary documents.
- Review your application carefully before submitting.
