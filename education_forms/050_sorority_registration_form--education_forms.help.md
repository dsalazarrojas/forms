# Sorority Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sorority Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from interested students for the sorority's recruitment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Begin by providing your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2.  Proceed to the student information section, where you'll be asked to provide details about your student ID number, date of birth, and any other relevant student information.
3.  In the required documents section, ensure you upload any necessary documents or files that the sorority needs from you.
4.  For registration, provide any additional details requested by the sorority, which might include information about your academic background, interests, or other relevant factors.
5.  You may also be asked to upload a document in this section.
