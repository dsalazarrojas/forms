# Sorority Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sorority Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from interested students for the sorority's recruitment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Begin by providing your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2.  Proceed to the student information section, where you'll be asked to provide details about your student ID number, date of birth, and any other relevant student information.
3.  In the required documents section, ensure you upload any necessary documents or files that the sorority needs from you.
4.  For registration, provide any additional details requested by the sorority, which might include information about your academic background, interests, or other relevant factors.
5.  You may also be asked to upload a document in this section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required: false): This field asks for your name, email address, and phone number. Please ensure you enter accurate and up-to-date information so that the sorority can contact you with updates and information.
*   **Student Information** (`student_information`, text, required: false): In this field, you'll be asked to provide details about your student ID number, date of birth, and any other relevant student information. Make sure to double-check the accuracy of this information.
*   **Required Documents** (`required_documents`, text, required: false): Upload any necessary documents or files that the sorority needs from you. Please ensure that you provide all required documents to avoid delays in the recruitment process.
*   **Registration** (`registration`, text, required: false): This field is for any additional details you'd like to provide to the sorority about your background, interests, or other relevant factors. Be as detailed as possible to help the sorority get to know you better.
*   **Upload Document** (`upload_document`, text, required: false): You might be asked to upload a document in this section. Please follow the sorority's guidelines for the type of document they require.
*   **Signature** (`signature`, text, required: false): This field is likely for signing and confirming the accuracy of the information provided.
*   **Chapter Sorority Letters** (`chapter_sorority_letters`, text, required: false): In this field, please enter the correct letters associated with your chapter or sorority name. Double-check to ensure accuracy.
*   **Chapter Sorority Colors** (`chapter_sorority_colors`, text, required: false): Enter the correct colors associated with your chapter or sorority name. Be precise in your response.
*   **Recruitment Fees** (`recruitment_fees`, text, required: false): If required, you may be asked to pay a recruitment fee. Please follow the sorority's guidelines for payment and fees.
*   **Disclaimer** (`disclaimer`, text, required: false): Read and agree to the sorority's disclaimer carefully before proceeding. This might include terms and conditions of the sorority membership.

**Tips**

*   Double-check all fields for accuracy before submission.
*   Be detailed and thorough in your responses, especially in the registration and chapter sorority letters and colors fields.
*   Follow the sorority's guidelines for uploading documents and paying fees, if applicable.
