# Reading Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reading Report Form is a document used to gather information about a student's reading progress and habits. This form will help teachers and educators track student reading performance and provide insights to parents and educators about how a student is doing in reading.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Reading Report Form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form carefully, making sure to answer all required questions.
2. Select the correct options from the drop down lists where applicable.
3. Enter any comments or information that you feel will help in the text fields.
4. Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Reading Progress
* **Reading Progress (How is the student's reading progress?)**: A text field for teachers to describe the student's current reading progress. This field is optional.
### Grade Level (Grade Level)**: A select_one field for selecting the grade level of the student. The options are:
  * 1
  * 2
  * 3
  * 4
  * 5
### Reading Level (reading_level_label)**: A text field for entering the student's current reading level. This field is optional.
### Reading Frequency (Reading frequency)**: A text field for entering the student's reading frequency. This field is optional.
### Page 2: Additional Information
* **Student Attendance (Student Attendance)**: A text field for entering information about the student's attendance. This field is optional.
* **Parent Response (Parent Response)**: A text field for entering any comments or concerns from the parent. This field is optional.
* **Teacher Comments (Teacher Comments)**: A text field for any comments or concerns from the teacher. This field is optional.
* **School Year (School Year)**: A select_multiple field for selecting the current school year. The options are:
  * First
  * Second
  * Third
* **Student Name (Student Name)**: A text field for entering the student's name. This field is optional.
* **Teacher Name (Teacher Name)**: A text field for entering the teacher's name. This field is optional.
