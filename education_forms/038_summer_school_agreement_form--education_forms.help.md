# Summer School Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer School Agreement Form is used to capture information from parents or emergency contacts of students enrolled in a summer school program. The form aims to inform and obtain consent from the parents/guardians regarding the student's participation, behavior, and expectations while in the program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately, providing all required information.
2. Make sure to review each field carefully before submitting the form.
3. If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Summer School Information** (`summer_school_agreement_form_fields`, text, optional): This section is used to provide general information about the summer school program.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Please enter the student's name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the contact email of the parent or emergency contact.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact phone number of the parent or emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the emergency contact's email.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_one, optional): Select the relationship of the emergency contact to the student (Emergency Contact, Parent, Guardian).
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact's address.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter the student's name.
* **Date of Birth** (`student_date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the student's date of birth.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information for all fields.
* If the student's date of birth is not known, please leave the field blank.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all information is complete and accurate.
