# Additive Manufacturing Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess your knowledge of Additive Manufacturing (AM), a process that allows for the creation of complex shapes and structures by layering material, such as metals, plastics, and ceramics, using various technologies, including 3D printing, binding, and powder bed fusion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through each page, and fill out the corresponding fields according to your knowledge. You will be asked to select from multiple-choice options or enter your answer in a text field. Make sure to provide your email address to receive your results, which will be sent to you automatically after completing the quiz.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Name** (`participant_name`): Enter your name as it appears on your business card or identification.
- **How would you rate your current AM knowledge?** (`knowledge_level_self`): Select one of the four options:
  - Beginner (Curious)
  - Intermediate (Practitioner)
  - Advanced (Specialist)
  - Expert (Engineer/Researcher)
- **Which technology uses a laser to sinter powdered metal?** (`question_1`): Choose the correct answer from the options provided.
  - Fused Deposition Modeling (FDM) is not correct (it's a different technology altogether)
  - Selective Laser Sintering (SLS)
  - Direct Metal Laser Sintering (DMLS)
  - Stereolithography (SLA)
- **What does FDM stand for in additive manufacturing?** (`question_2`): Enter the correct abbreviation.
- **True or False - SLA technology uses a liquid resin that is cured by UV light** (`question_3`): Choose the correct answer.
  - True
  - False
- **Which of these materials are commonly used in industrial AM?** (`question_4`): Select all that apply:
  - Titanium Alloys
  - Nylon (Polyamide)
  - PLA (Polylactic Acid)
  - Inconel
  - PEEK
- **Identify a primary advantage of additive manufacturing over subtractive methods** (`question_5`): Choose the correct answer.
  - Faster mass production of simple parts
  - Ability to create complex internal geometries
  - Lower material cost for all parts
  - Better surface finish without post-processing
- **What is the purpose of 'support structures' in 3D printing?** (`question_6`): Enter a brief description of support structures in 3D printing.
- **Which file format is the industry standard for 3D printing?** (`question_7`): Choose the correct answer.
  - .JPG
  - .STL
  - .PDF
  - .DWG
  - .OBJ
- **Rate the importance of post-processing in metal additive manufacturing** (`question_8`): Choose a number from 1 to 5 to rate the importance.
- **Any comments on the quiz difficulty?** (`quiz_feedback`): Enter any comments or suggestions you may have about the quiz.
- **Email Address for Results** (`submission_email`): Enter your email address to receive your results automatically.
- **Date** (`date_taken`): This field will be automatically filled out with the current date and time.

Please ensure to complete all the questions honestly and thoroughly, and provide your email address to receive your results.
